465. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 6, 1942
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I received your letter yesterday. I had duly received all the
dhotis sent by you and I promptly began using them. Your
letter was received in Bardoli.
It is a good sign that Satyawati is keeping good health.
Mahadev is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
My blessings to all the companions.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2487
