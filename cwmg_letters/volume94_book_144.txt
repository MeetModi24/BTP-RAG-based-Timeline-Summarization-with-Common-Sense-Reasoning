144. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 18, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is a letter from you today. I shall abide by your wish
with regard to that girl. I am not in a position to know better. I cannot
write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
