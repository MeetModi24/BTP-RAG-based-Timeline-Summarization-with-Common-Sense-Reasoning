198. LETTER TO TEJWANTI DHIR
SEVAGRAM,
October 4, 1944
CHI. TEJWANTI,
I have your letter. It is all right that you went away. Come
when you feel like it. Let your elder son go his own way.
He will do as God prompts him. If you want you may work
among the tribals or render service by taking up residence in the
Bhangi Nivas. Do what you think best. Does this answer your
letter?
Nothing has yet been decided about the Ashram.
TEJWANTI DHIR
P. O. NAKODAR, DISTT. JULLUNDER
(PUNJAB)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
