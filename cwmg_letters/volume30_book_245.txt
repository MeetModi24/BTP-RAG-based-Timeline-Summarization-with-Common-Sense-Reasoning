245. LETTER TO SUDHANSHU KUMARI GHOSH
ASHRAM,
April 1, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is very sad to find that Sarat Babu is
fasting. Somehow or other I think that it is quite wrong and I
hope that long before this reaches you he has broken his fast.
Yours sincerely,
SHRIMATI SUDHANSHU KUMARI GHOSH
BARISAL
From a microfilm: S.N. 19887
