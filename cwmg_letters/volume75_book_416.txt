416. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
February 21, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I never lost sleep on account of your letter. Who told you
so? I had lost it with Jamnalalji's death.
When I heard about the utensils, I sent the girls to perform
their duty.
When each one brings the utensils they should be checked
then and there so that everybody knows how to clean them. Since
350
everybody cannot be supplied with a towel, it is better that one
person wipes the utensils with it as they come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4417
