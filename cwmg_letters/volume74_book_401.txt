401. LETTER TO JAKATDAR
August 21, 1941
DEAR JAKATDAR,
You have done so well at your time of life that you need
not re-offer C.D. If the Government choose to take you off for
any of your activities, it is a different matter.
I would like you to spread the gospel of non-violence by
doing the constructive work of the Congress in your district.
I hope you are keeping well.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
