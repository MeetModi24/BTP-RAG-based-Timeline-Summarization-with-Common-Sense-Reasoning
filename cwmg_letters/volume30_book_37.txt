37. LETTER TO A. T. GIDWANI
ASH RAM, SABARMATI,
February 19, 1926
MY DEAR GIDWANI,
Here is the copy of the reply from Purulia. I am keeping the
original for future use. I think it would be better to try the
2nd remedy. Mr. Sharpe's letter shows how little cause there is
for worry. I hope you are otherwise keeping quite fit.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1.
From a microfilm: S.N. 14111
