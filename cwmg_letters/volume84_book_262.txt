262. LETTER TO LABANYALATA CHANDA
NEW DELHI,
May 25, 1946
CHI. LABANYALATA,
I got your letter. I have talked to Satis Babu and Profulla
Babu regarding the hospital at Abhaya Ashram. Let us see what
happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
CAMP BALRAMPUR
