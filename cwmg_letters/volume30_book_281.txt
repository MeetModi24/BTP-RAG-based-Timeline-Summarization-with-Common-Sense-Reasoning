281. LETTER TO MILTON NEWBERRY FRANTZ
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 6, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am afraid it is not possible for me to
subscribe to the creed you have sent me. The subscriber is made
to believe that the highest manifestation of the unseen reality was
Jesus Christ. In spite of all my efforts, I have not been able to
feel the truth of that statement. I have not been able to move
beyond the belief that Jesus was one of the great teachers of man-
kind. Do you not think that religious unity is to be had not by
a mechanical subscription and a common creed but by all respect-
ing the creed of each other? In my opinion difference in creed
there must be so long as there are different brains. But what does
it matter if all these are. . .1 upon the common path of love and
mutual judgment.
I return the stamp kindly sent by you. It cannot be used in
India.
Yours sincerely,
MILTON NEWBERRY FRANTZ, ESQ.
COLLEGEVILLE
