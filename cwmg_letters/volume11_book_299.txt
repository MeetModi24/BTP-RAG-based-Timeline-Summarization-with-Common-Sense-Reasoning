299. LETTER TO JAMNADAS GANDHI
[November 17, 1912]
CHI. JAMNADAS,
I have your letters. I shall reply to them at leisure. At the
moment, I shall only say that since you have grasped the signi-
ficance [of human existence] you should order your life accordingly.
From the Gujarati original in pencil in Gandhiji's hand: C. W. 5639
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
