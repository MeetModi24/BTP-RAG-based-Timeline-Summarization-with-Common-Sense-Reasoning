127. LETTER TO SHRIKRISHNADAS JAJU
[After November 8, 1946]
BHAI JAJUJI,
Keep this letter. My advice is that you should have Sarala-
devi's name among the trustees. She is an extremely pure-
hearted and truthful lady. What I wrote in Harijan was re-
garding her. If you see anything wrong in this, write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
(Enclosure: Saralabehn's letter of November 8, 1946)
SHRI SHRIKRISHNADAS JAJU
WARDHA
