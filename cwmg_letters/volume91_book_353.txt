353. LETTER TO ABDUL KADIR BAWAZEER
DELHI,
February 2, 1925
BHAI IMAM SAHEB,
Chi. Chhaganlal writes that you are ill. Remember that we
have no right to fall ill. It is our duty to keep our body healthy.
I hope Amina and Qureshi are well and looking after you.
Here talks are going on, but one cannot say what the re-
sult will be. Tomorrow I may go to Rawalpindi. I hope to be
there on the 10th.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
BHAI IMAM SAHEB
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10784. Courtesy : Ghulam
Rasul Qureshi
