107. LETTER TO SUDARSHAN V. DESAI
September 29, 1934
CHI. MAVO1,
I got your letter. It would be good if you write your letters
with somebody by your side.
We must not use toys from Eng-
land or from any other country. We should be content with
whatever Indian toys we can get.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5740. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
