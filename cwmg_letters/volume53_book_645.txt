645. LETTER TO NARMADABEHN RANA
March 5, 1933
CHI. NARMADA,
You seem to have no regard for truth. You did not write
any letter yourself and you demand that I should write to you.
You did well in paying a visit to your home. Now devote
yourself completely to work.
Write to me regularly.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2773. Courtesy: Ramnarayan N.
Pathak
