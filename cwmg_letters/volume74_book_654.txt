654. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
October 10, 1941
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Read the enclosed letter and let me have your guidance.
Satyamurti has come today. He will state his case tomorrow.
I hope you are doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Biyani was here. There had been a reign of terror in Am-
raoti.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 261
