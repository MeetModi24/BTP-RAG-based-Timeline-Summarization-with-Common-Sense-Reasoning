408. LETTER TO RANI VIDYAVATI
January 18, 1931
CHI. VIDYAVATI (LUCKNOW JAIL),
I was very happy to have your letter and more so to learn
that Laxmi Devi is also there. When next you have the oppor-
tunity to write, let me know your routine there. Jang Bahadur
is also showing courage. What food do you get? Blessings to all
sisters. I am well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
