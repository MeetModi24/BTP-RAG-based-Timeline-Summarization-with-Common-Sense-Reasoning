234. LETTER TO V. G. SARAIYA
AHMEDABAD,
Posh Sud 10 [January 3, 1917]
I returned from Lucknow only last night. You should bear
whatever happens in patience and even submit to death while
maintaining moral principles. There is no other consolation I
can give.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
BHAI VRIJLAL GOVINDLAL SARAIYA
TODA POLE IN RAJA MEHTA POLE
AHMEDABAD
From the original post card in Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 2820.
Courtesy: Vinaychandra Govindlal Saraiya
