288. LETTER TO FATIMA SULTANA
[After January 28, 1920]
DEAR MADAM,
I have your papers forwarded to me from Ahmedabad. I have
read them and I feel that I am unable to help you in the matter.
I enclose the papers herewith registered post.
Yours sincerely,
From the pencilled original in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 7077
