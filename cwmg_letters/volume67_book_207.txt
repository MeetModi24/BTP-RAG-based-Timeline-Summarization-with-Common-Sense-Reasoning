207. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
July [4,] 1938
CHI. JAMNALAL,
The car was expected here today at 6 to take Balkrishna
to the hospital. If it has not left and if you can send it, please
do. A note is going to the hospital too. The car will be re-
quired only if there is still time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2994
