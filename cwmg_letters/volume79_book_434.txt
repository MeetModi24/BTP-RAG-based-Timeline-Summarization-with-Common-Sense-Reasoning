434. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
March 16, 1945
CHI. BRAJKISAN,
Do not worry about me. Do not believe the newspapers.
I have your letter. You must completely recover. Khurshed-
behn is with her sisters. My blessings to all the brothers.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2491
