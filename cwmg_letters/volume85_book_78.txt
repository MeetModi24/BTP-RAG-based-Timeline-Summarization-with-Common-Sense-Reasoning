78. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
POONA,
July 29, 1946
CHI. AMRITLAL,
SO you have completed 40 years. You must hope to live up
to 125. Keep your body fit in any case.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. AMRITLAL NANAVATI
KAKAWADI
OPP. MAHILASHRAM
WARDHA C. P.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 10811
