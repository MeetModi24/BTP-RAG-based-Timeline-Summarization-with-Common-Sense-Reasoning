582. LETTER TO PARIMAL SHOME
PANCHGANI,
June 18, 1945
MY DEAR PARIMAL,
I am sorry to hear from you that Sarat Babu is so ill.
But as you rightly say I have not much confidence in my abi-
lity to do good in such matters. Yet you may depend upon
my doing the best I am capable of in securing relief.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PARIMAL SHOME, B. COM.
12- HINDUSTAN ROAD
BALLYGUNGE, CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
