218. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEGAON,
September 19, 1939
CHI. NARANDAS,
My blessings on your birthday. You seem to be making
fairly rapid progress. The girls also seem to be doing good work.
If, however, the people are tempted to pay money only by the
sight of the girls, you should consider how far it is desirable to
send them out. You can know more by asking the girls them-
selves. This is only for you. Satish Babu will certainly come.
But not Ba. I will think about Mirabehn. Rajkumari is not
enthusiastic. I shall consider how the money should be used.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Is Shambhu Shankar still in prison? What did he do?
What is the state of affairs in Palitana? Dhebarbhai may be able
to tell you.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8562. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
