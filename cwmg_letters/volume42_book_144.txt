144. LETTER TO UNDER-SECRETARY, HINDUSTANI
SEVA DAL
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
With reference to your enquiry I am sorry I have not got
the repatriation rules regarding indentured labourers at the
Assam Tea Plantations but you are likely to have them either
from Pt. Banarsidas Chaturvedi, Vishal Bharat1, Calcutta, or Servants
of India Society, Poona.
Yours sincerely,
THE UNDER-SECRETARY
HINDUSTANI SEVA DAL
HUBLI
From a microfilm: S.N. 15758
1 A nationalist Hindi daily
