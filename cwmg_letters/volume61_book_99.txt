99. LETTER TO N. K. BOSE
May 16, 1935
DEAR NIRMALBABU,
I have been simply overworked. As soon as I finish revision
I shall return the manuscript. You may certainly send it to
The Modern Review.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI NIRMAL KUMAR BOSE
6/1A BRITISH INDIAN STREET
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 10520
