334. LETTER TO RAJA OF KANIKA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
January 8, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I was sorry that during my recent tour in Utkal I was not
able to visit your Raj and see for myself whether there was any-
thing in the allegations made to me about the oppression of the
ryots. These allegations were vehemently repeated by many people
during the tour. But I have said nothing about them before put-
ting myself in communication with you once more. Could you
entertain the idea of a representative being sent to find me the
truth about the allegations?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
THE RAJA OF KANIKA
