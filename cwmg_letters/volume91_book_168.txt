168. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
NADIAD,
Ashad Vad 11 [August 2, 1918]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Try to get Bhagirathkosh (Urdu-Hindi Dictionary) by Dina
Nath Kaul, Munshi Naval Kishore Press, Lucknow, price as. 12/-
and Complete Urdu Course by R. C. Bhushan, Longmans Green
& Co., price Re. 1/12/. If you are able to get these two books,
send them to Devdas by registered post. His address is : C/O
Ramji Kalyanji, Sowcarpeth, Madras. Take the money from
Chi. Narandas.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
[Ps.]
You will have read the other journal.
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
