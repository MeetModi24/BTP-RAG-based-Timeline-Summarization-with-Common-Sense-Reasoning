469. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
February 16, 1938
CHI. LILA,
The blood-pressure today has been, morning 194-108, after-
noon 160/100 and late afternoon 142-96. I have done a good deal
of work too.
Show this to all. I have taken serpina today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 397
