489. LETTER TO MANSHANKAR J. TRIVEDI
December 1, 1930
CHI. MANU (TRIVEDI),
From what Pyarelal reports after having seen you I gather
that you have recovered your strength. You must have talked
with Kakasaheb to your heart's content. Take care and do not
fall ill again. Don't be impatient to resume work. He whose
aspiration is noble and who is always eager to serve works even
when physically not working.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7772
