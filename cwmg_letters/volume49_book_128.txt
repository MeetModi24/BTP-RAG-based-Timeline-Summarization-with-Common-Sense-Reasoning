128. LETTER TO JAISHANKAR P. TRIVEDI
February 11, 1932
BHAI TRIVEDI,
I wished to write to Manu earlier but could not write all
these days. Whenever you wish, you may come and see me on
a day fixed for visitors. It will always be possible to include
you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1003
