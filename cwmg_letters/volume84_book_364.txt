364. LETTER TO KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
MUSSOORIE,
June 5, 1946
CHI. KAMALNAYAN,
The representation by the workers of the firm of Mukund
has just reached my hands. I am sending it to you. Is there
any truth in it? They have also sent me a wire. I have paid
no attention to it. How can I study all this? I don't have the
time either. But is there any truth in what they say in the notice?
Janakibehn should now be perfectly all right. It seems this
task has been well accomplished.
BACHHRAJ [&] Co.
MAHATMA GANDHI ROAD, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
