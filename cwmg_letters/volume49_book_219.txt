219. LETTER TO DURGA M. DESAI
March 11, 1932
CHI. DURGA,
Mahadev arrived yesterday unexpectedly.
It seems the
authorities thought that I must have one more companion be-
sides Sardar. There is no cause for worry about my leg. There
is a little inflammation at one spot.
Treatment is going on.
There is not the slightest [cause] for worry. You can always
write to me anything you wish to. And you may come whenever
you like.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
You will find it convenient if you come with the party of
visitors expected next week.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9484
