433. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
NEW DELHI,
September 20, 1946
CHI. KAKA,
I got your letter about Urdu.
We shall have to think
a little more about it and since I shall be there in a few days
I should like to meet you and discuss the matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 10976
