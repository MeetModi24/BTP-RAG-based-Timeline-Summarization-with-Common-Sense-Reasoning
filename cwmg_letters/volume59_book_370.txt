370. LETTER TO J. MACK WILLIAMS
November 13, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 15th ultimo. The rules that my co-
workers and myself seek to observe are eleven, not ten. They
are as under:
Truth, non-violence, continence, non-possession, non-stealing,
removal of untouchability, swadeshi, daily bread-labour, equality
of the principal religions of the world, restraint on the palate,
that is, eating to live-not living to eat, and fearlessness.
Yours sincerely,
J. MACK WILLIAMS, ESQ.
VASS, NORTH CAROLINA
U. S.A.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
