61. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
June 4, 1934
BHAI BALVANTSINHA,
I do not see any difference between 'Bhai' or 'Chi.' or
any other appellation as long as the sentiment is the same.
When I do not know a person well and do not know his age and
other particulars I usually address him as 'Bhai'. I would like
it if Surendra keeps you with him. Narandas is at Rajkot, you
should act as he advises.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1871
