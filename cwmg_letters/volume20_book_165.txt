165. LETTER TO KUNVARJI MEHTA
Saturday [July 9, 1921]
BHAISHRI KUNVARJI,
I could read your letter only just now. Please congratulate
Bhai Makanji and the people of Varad on my behalf. The former
showed real courage. It does not matter if, on the first impulse,
he asked the man to leave. I count it as a great thing that later
he checked his anger and behaved with forbearance and that
the residents of the village also remained peaceful.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original : G.N. 2671
