245. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
POONA,
March 4, 1946
CHI. ANAND,
I did write to you. My blessings are always there. Do what-
ever you like, but remain cheerful. Sushila has gone to Quetta.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANAND HINGORANI
C/O R. C. TANDON
10 SOUTH ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India,
and Anand T. Hingorani
