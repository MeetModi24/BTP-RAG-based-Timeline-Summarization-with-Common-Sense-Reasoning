2. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
October 16, 1929
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have your letters. For some time now I have not been able
to write at all. I got the article about Kaki only today. It is well
written. Let the critics say what they will.
The Gita proofs sent to Gorakhpur have not yet reached
here. I don't know when I shall get them. At Mussoorie I shall
have a good opportunity of disposing of them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11457
