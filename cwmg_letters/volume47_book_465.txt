465. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
September 6, 1931
CHI. PREMA,
You have still not written to me. I shall now get your letter
when I reach England only if you have posted one by air mail.
Or I may get one on the 19th.
You are causing me worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10263
