39. LETTER TO M. M. MALAVIYA
On THE TRAIN,
September 25, 1940
REVERED BHAI SAHEB,
I received your letter. I shall certainly strive to prevent
a clash and if a clash does occur, it will be in spite of myself.
What should I write about fasting? The matter is not really
in my hands. I hope you are well.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From the Hindi original: Padmakant Malaviya Papers. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
