80. LETTER TO COLLECTOR OF CHAMPARAN
SABARMATI,
[1918]
As Mr. Pundalik who is in charge of the school at Bhitiharwa
tells me that he is being often questioned about his representative
character and his activity, I write this to say that Mr. Pundalik
represents me and has been selected by me for the work he is do-
ing on the recommendation of Dr. Deva of the Servants of India
Society. He is a voluntary unpaid worker.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 5220
