40. LETTER TO M. N. DOCTOR
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 30, 1906
DEAR MR. DOCTOR,
I find that I am so very busy that I must cancel the appointment
given you; but if you can come on Sunday at 12 o'clock, we can walk
together and talk on my way to Mr. Polak's, with whom I have an
engagement. If you know London well, we will then separate somewhere
near Highbury.
Yours sincerely,
M. N. DOCTOR, ESQ.
102, WHARTON ROAD, W.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4420
1 Parmanand
