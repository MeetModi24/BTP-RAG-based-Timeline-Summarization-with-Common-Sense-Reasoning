509. LETTER TO ABDUL GHANI
December 7, 1934
MY DEAR GHANI,
I am posting a letter to you [in a] separate cover written
by Father. After he had written the letter, he was arrested in
my presence and taken to Bombay. There he would be tried
under Section 124-A. You would not be disturbed by the news.
Prisons are our second or rather real home.
I expect a long letter from you telling me how you are doing
there. Did you receive the letter I sent to you at Lucknow?
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
