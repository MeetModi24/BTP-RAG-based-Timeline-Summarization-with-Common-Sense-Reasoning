38. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
April 12, 1911
MY DEAR HENRY,
Herewith copy of Lane's letter. Ritch telegraphed saying Madras
wanted to know my views on the amendments to the Bill. I thought
I would ask Lane as to what he thought about the situation before
I cabled. 2 The letter was the result. I have now asked Ritch to cable
Madras that the Government, is considering the situation and that
another cable will be sent later. We may expect some definite news
next week. If Finot's Race Prejudice is there, please send it to me.
I want it for Canon Allmett.
I have your 2nd wire. It was difficult for me to advise. But I have
wired you what I thought was best in the circumstances.
Do you know anything about Max Nordeau? What kind of a man
is he ? I mean what is his private life? I am reading his book on
Degeneration.
Yours sincerely,
BHAI
From the original: Gandhi-Polak Correspondence. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
