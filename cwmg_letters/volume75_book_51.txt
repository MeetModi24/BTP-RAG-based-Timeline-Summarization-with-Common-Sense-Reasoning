51. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
SEVAGRAM,
October 20, 1941
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your letter. I have now got the exact figure. The
amount realized is 16,048-15-. I shall now see what can be done
with it. You need not now send Rs. 1,000 unless you want to in-
crease the donation. I would advise you not to at the cost of
your other work. I do hope your body will be quite well. You must
not worry. We have to resign ourselves to God's will.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI AMBUJAMMAL
96 MOWBRAY'S ROAD
ALWARPET, FORT
MADRAS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
