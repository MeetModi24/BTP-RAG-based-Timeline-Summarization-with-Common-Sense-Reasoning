161. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
JUHU,
June 5, 1944
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I read your anguish-filled letter. Constant efforts do result in
purity. Meditate upon chapters VI and XII. More when we
meet.
Shankaran is quite distressed and I feel that, if his father is
ill, he should be permitted to go.
What work was Parnerkar doing?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4441
1 By Manilal Nabhubhai Dwivedi, Gujarati poet and thinker
