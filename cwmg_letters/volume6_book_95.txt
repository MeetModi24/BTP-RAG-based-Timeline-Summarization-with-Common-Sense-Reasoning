95. LETTER TO SIR CHARLES DILKE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 3, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I am much obliged to you for your letter regarding the Indian
Deputation from the Transvaal. If you cannot possibly come, I venture
to hope that you will be good enough to send on Wednesday a letter
of sympathy which could be read to Lord Elgin.
I beg to remain,
Yours faithfully,
THE RIGHT HON'BLE SIR CHARLES DILKE, BART., M.P.
SLOANE STREET, W.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4476
