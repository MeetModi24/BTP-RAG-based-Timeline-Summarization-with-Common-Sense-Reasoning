303. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[Before August 27, 1936] 1
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your two letters. You ought to have faith in what Dr.
Bharadwaj tells you. Dr. Ansari would have done the same.
You are therefore going to clean your nose and carry out what-
ever else he has recommended.
I am enclosing Nilam's letter. I would certainly have
sent for you had it not been for Nilam's illness. I shall show
both your letters to Kanti and shall permit him to go if he wis-
hes. I also do not like the step he proposes to take, but I do not
wish to suppress him either.
I have no more time now, so I stop here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 603
