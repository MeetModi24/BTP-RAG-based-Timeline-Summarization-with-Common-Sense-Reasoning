430. LETTER TO SHAUKAT ALI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 18, 1929
DEAR BROTHER,
I have your note. I await your promised letter. But I still
more eagerly await your promised visit.
I have the invitation, but I am afraid I won't be able to
attend.
Mahadev is in Bardoli, helping Vallabhbhai about the
Inquiry.
Yours sincerely,
MAULANA SHAUKAT ALI
THE CENTRAL KHILAFAT COMMITTEE
THE SULTAN MANSION, DONGRI, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S. N. 15285
