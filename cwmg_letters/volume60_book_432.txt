432. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA B. ATHAVALE
[March 21, 1935]
Prabhashankar told me that I had given sufficient cause to
Nanalal Kavi to get angry with me. I do not know anything
about it. However, if I have given him any cause to get angry
with me, I would, being a devotee of ahimsa, apologize a thousand
times. If you have not so far found out the cause, please do
so and let me know.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
