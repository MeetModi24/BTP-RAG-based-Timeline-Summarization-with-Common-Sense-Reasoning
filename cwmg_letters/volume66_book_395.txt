395. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
December 15, 1937
CHI. SHARDA,
Got your letter. I do not wish to play a trick on you
by arranging classes for you. And is that possible in any case?
Are you such a simple soul that you can be so easily caught?
You have to do one thing. You must make your body strong
as steel. Looking after the rest is my responsibility. If you
have followed everything about Dinshawji's [treatment], start
it. It would not take as much time as you fear. But spare
whatever time may be necessary for it. Write to me regularly
about the result.
Why do you find the atmosphere dull? Why should your
mind be sad? It is your own fault.
So many people, the cows
and calves, the birds and bees, the shrubs and trees—one would
be in blithe spirits with so much of company.
They are all your
kith and kin—even the trees.
Why should you not play with
the local children?
Blessings from
BAPU
