340. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
WARDHA,
February 23, 1945
CHI. NARAHARI,
I understand about Anand Hingorani. I think he is a very
straightforward man. He will write anything you say. He will do what
we say. I have known him from his childhood. He has also worked
for Young India.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
