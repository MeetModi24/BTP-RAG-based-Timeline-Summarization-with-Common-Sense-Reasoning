289. LETTER TO PERCY W. BARTLETT
October 3, 1932
DEAR MR. BARTLETT1,
I thank you for your letter enclosing copies of your letter
to the Poet and the response from the Fellowship. I am quite sure
that the Fellowship is exerting itself in the cause of peace.
Yours sincerely,
PERCY W. BARTLETT, ESQ.
17
RED
LION SQUARE
LONDON, W.C. 1
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. III, p. 201
