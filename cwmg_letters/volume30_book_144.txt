144. LETTER TO DR. JOACHIM HENRY REINHOLD
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 18, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It pleases me to find that you have bene-
fited by my writings. You are at liberty to translate any of the
writings of Young India. There are German and French trans-
lations to be had in Europe and there is also an English edition
published by Messrs S. Ganesan, Pycrofts Road, Triplicane,
Madras.
Yours sincerely,
PROF. DR. JOACHIM HENRY REINHOLD
PROF. AT THE FREE UNIVERSITY LEAGUE
WARSAW, POLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 12446
