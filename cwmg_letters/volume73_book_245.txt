245. LETTER TO GHULAM RASUL QURESHI
December 4, 1940
CHI. QURESHI,
I have your letter. You should not be upset or unhappy over
Amtul Salaam's anger, nor be happy about her happiness. She
will be here tomorrow afternoon. What about Amina's piles?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10768. Courtesy: Ghulam Rasul
Qureshi
