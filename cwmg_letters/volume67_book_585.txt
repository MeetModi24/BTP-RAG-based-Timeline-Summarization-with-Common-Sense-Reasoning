585. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
October 4, 1938
CHI. BABUDI,
I have been receiving your letters regularly. Sushilabehn
says that you need not worry about the irregular monthly periods.
It can happen sometimes. Take more milk and cream if pos-
sible. How much do you take? What else do you take?
Remain calm. We are leaving today.
What is going on about your music? Now you will recite
the Gita properly, won't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9999. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokhawala
