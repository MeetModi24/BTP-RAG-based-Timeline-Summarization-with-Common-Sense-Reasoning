111. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
AHMEDABAD,
August 1 [1915?]
DEAR MR. SHASTRIAR,
This will be presented to you by Mr. Raghavendra who is
desirous of joining the Society in any capacity in which he may
be found fit. He seems to have had a varied experience. I have
known him for some time as a correspondent.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 6290
