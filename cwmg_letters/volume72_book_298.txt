298. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
ON THE TRAIN,
July 8, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
Here are two more translations corrected. Both are good.
The Masnavi captivated me.
It had the force of the original.
Of course it was easy. You have seen what happened at the
W.C.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3981. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7290
