396. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
December 25, 1932
BHAI MOOLCHANDJI,
Draw a circle and ask anyone to find its beginning and end.
None can indicate it if the circle is drawn correctly. This being true
of man's creation, what can we say of God's creation? I am
unable to answer your queries because no answer will be adequate.
Leave the thing alone.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 832
