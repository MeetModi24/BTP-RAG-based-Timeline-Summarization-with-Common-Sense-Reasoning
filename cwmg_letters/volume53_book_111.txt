111. LETTER TO FULCHAND SHAH
January 18, 1933
You are carrying on spinning whereas we are arguing with
the Shastris, some of whom are being sulky. They have been pour-
ing abuses on me. They discover weaknesses in me of which till now
I was unaware. My words are interpreted in strange ways and by
such means the victory of sanatana dharma is declared with beat
of drums. We laugh at all this, knowing that there is no real
strength behind it. If there were real strength behind it, and also
public opinion, then it would be not an occasion for laughter,
but one for weeping; then one could not help but weep. This
movement of the so-called sanatanists suggests that the founda-
tion of untouchability is tottering and the structure will shortly
tumble down.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 69
