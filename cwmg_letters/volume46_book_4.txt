4. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
April 18, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your letters. You seem to have made a mistake in
going there. However, don't feel unhappy and spoil your health.
I feel that, now that you have gone, you will have to stay there
for at least some days for the sake of form. If Harasbabu re-
covers, you should come away. Today I am in Bombay, and
will return to Ahmedabad tomorrow. It seems I may have to
remain there for some days. Let that not give you a fright. I
am all right.
Mirabehn is here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3398
