397. LETTER TO NIRMALA H. DESAI
April 18, 1932
CHI. NIRMALA,
Our spiritual progress will remain illusory till we learn to
regard every person as our own brother or sister. Why should
we feel any difference between relations and others? How should
people who have no relations behave? Or those who have lost
theirs through death? He who regards all human beings as his
relations is never bereft of them, for they are [count]1less. Such
relationship is the only true one, and all other relationships are
false and transitory. Can you understand this?
Blessings from
BAPU
