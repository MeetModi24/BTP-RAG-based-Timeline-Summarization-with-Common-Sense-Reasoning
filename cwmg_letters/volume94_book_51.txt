51. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
August 29, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you today. Kanaiyo is going to receive
Pyarelal. Prabhakar is having high fever today. He has been given
quinine injections. I am fine. Do not worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
C/O ASSTT. DEAN
K. E. M. HOSPITAL
PAREL, BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
