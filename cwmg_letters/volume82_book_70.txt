70. LETTER TO KASHI GANDHI
POONA,
November 8, 1945
CHI. KASHI,
I was glad to read your few words. I keep thinking of you
all and Kishorelalbhai and Gomatibehn. The latest news is that
what Chhaganlal is suffering from is malaria; so I expect the
fever will have come down and he will have regained his
strength.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
