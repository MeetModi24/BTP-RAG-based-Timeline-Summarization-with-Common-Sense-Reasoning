180. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
January 10, 1931
CHI. NIMU,
You just cannot leave Sumitra alone. It will suffice it you do whatever
work of public service you can, keeping her with you. It should be enough
for you that Ramdas has gone back to jail. I think all the requirements will
be satisfied if you stay on in Vijapur. The atmosphere there is good for Sumitra
and also you can do a lot of service from there.
If Sumitra's nose continues to run, it probably means that she is troubled
by the cold. I understand what you say about a baby girl having been born
to Hari-ichchha. She seems to be keeping well. As for my giving blessings
to Sumitra, I will have to think. For the present . . . .1
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
