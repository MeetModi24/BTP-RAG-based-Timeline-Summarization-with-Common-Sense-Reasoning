439. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
March 9, 1937
CHI. KANTI,
I got your letter. It would be best to write immediately
about things I ought to know. I will leave here for Delhi on
Sunday. I don't think I shall have much time to talk with
Devdas. The whole day I shall be busy meeting people. You
can certainly study in Mysore also. The climate is undoubtedly
good and Mysore has a university of its own.
I understand about the railway. Amtul Salaam has gone to
Indore.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7317. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
