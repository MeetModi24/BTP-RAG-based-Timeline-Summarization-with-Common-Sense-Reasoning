80. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
January 30, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
Why don't you write to me? How are you? In what form
are you? With whom have you made friends? Which of them
do you like most? Where do you live?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9420. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
