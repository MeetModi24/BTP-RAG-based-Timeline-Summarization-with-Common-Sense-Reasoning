572. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
November 6, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
I got Sharda's letter giving a beautiful description of how you
enjoyed the five holidays. I can give only a short reply to it. Hari-
jan work now takes up a great deal of my time, so that neither
much time nor enough strength is left to me to write a long letter
to you. You also should do whatever service you can to Harijans.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
