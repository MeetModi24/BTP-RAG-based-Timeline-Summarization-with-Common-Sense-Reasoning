109. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI G. PATEL
February 6, 1932
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
I got your letter. One must never forget prayer. As the
body craves for food when it is hungry and does not forget about
it, so the soul should yearn to pray. The prayer may consist of
nothing more than Ramanama, but one ought not to forget it in
any circumstances. To the extent that you forget it occasionally,
to that extent it is an external thing to you. Prayer must become
so intimately a part of one's being that at last one's every breath
is accompanied by Ramanama. As an eyelid goes on doing its
work, one will go on repeating Rama's name with every breath.
Blessings from
BAPU
