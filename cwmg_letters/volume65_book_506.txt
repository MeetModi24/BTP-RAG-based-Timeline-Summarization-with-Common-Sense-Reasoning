506. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
July 22, 1937
CHI. KAKA,
I read Shankar's letter in my present none too good health.
I send with this my reply to it and the copy sent by Bal. Send
the letter to Bal. You, of course, should read my letters.
I don't feel inclined to involve you in this matter. But if . . .1
is really what Shankar believes he is, it is necessary from every
point of view to know the truth.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7696
