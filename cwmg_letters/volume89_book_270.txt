270. LETTER TO HARISINGH GOUR
September 29, 1947
DEAR SIR HARISINGH4,
Many thanks for your letter.
I see that you can be carried away by your ideas. But I
cannot understand why you should disregard facts. No one has
displaced English from the great role belonging to it as an inter-
national medium. No Indian to my knowledge has ever desired
to banish English from India as a second language for study. But
some of us have undoubtedly desired to dethrone it from the
place it has usurped.
Again what a fund of misinformation in your note, a copy
of which you have been good enough to send me. But I have
neither time nor inclination to enter into a discussion with you.
Time will determine the issue.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR HARISINGH GOUR
SAGOR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
