428. LETTER TO BHULABHAI J. DESAI
December 31, 1932
BHAI BHULABHAI,
We were all happy to have your letter. I wish that nobody
should worry on account of the pain in my elbow. I am taking
the necessary treatment. The [authorities] will themselves call
for help from outside when there is need for it. I shall myself
ask for it if I feel the need. There is not much pain.
We are all glad to know that all of you are doing well.
Obviously one cannot study here as well as one can [outside].
Regards to all from all of us. Dhirubhai will come again.
MOHANDAS
ADVOCATE BHULABHAI DESAI
PRISONER
CENTRAL PRISON
NASIK ROAD
[From Gujarati]
