306. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
July 29, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I was very clear in my reply. But being confused in mind
you find it vague. My advice is that you should firmly decide
to stay here and be content with doing whatever work is given
you. This is the royal road and the royal yoga. If you cannot
do that, you may go on a tour and return if and when you feel
