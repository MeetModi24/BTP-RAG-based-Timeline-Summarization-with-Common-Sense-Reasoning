50. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
[February 7, 1927]
. . .3 serve us and others should attend to our comforts. Do we
really deserve all this? Please think over this. It will be long before
we really serve the world. At the moment we just accept what the
world offers us, claiming to be social workers. Let us live up to
the world's expectations of us. May I hope you will contribute
your full mite to achieve this?
Blessings from
BAPU
