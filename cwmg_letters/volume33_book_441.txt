441. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
NANDI DURG, MYSORE,
[Before June 5, 1927]
BHAI RAMESHWARJI,
Your letter to hand. I am steadily improving. There is no
cause for worry. You did right in informing me of the death of
Shankarrao's son. I have sent a letter of consolation to him and
his wife. What can I write to bring you peace of mind? Let us
believe that the one aim of our life is to control our senses; It is
the highest human achievement and therefore attainable with
difficulty. By constant effort we can conquer our passions. You
should be engaged in some noble work, mentally as well as
physically. Only when the mind is uncontrolled does it fall a
prey to passion. Why not make a painstaking study of the Gita?
And why not learn Sanskrit to understand the original work?
When you have not got to be at the shop or when you have to
be there and have nothing to do, why not ply the charkha or the
takli? Just as man cuts through solid mountains with persistent
effort, so can he cut through the barrier of passion. Therefore you
should always be diligent.
Blessings from
BAPU
