188. LETTER TO MESSRS A. BONNER
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 16, 1906
MESSRS A. BONNER
1 & 2, TOOK'S COURT
LONDON, E.C.
DEAR SIRS,
Herewith please find enclosed cheque for the sum of £2..
together with your bill, which kindly return duly receipted.
Yours faithfully,
2 enclosures
