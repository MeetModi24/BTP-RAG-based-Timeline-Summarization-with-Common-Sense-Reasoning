286. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 24, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
Your letter.
The weather is oppressive. I am not going to ask you to
come here before August or even later. If there is anything
serious I will tell you.
Khurshedbehn comes on 28th.
Horace Alexander and his friend Symonds are here for 3 days.
The train incident is startling. Has it any significance?
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
If Richardson writes, I shall let him come.
From the original: C.W. 4134. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7443
