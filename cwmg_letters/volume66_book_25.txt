25. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
SEGAON,
August 7, 1937
CHI. KANCHAN,
Do come over tomorrow (Sunday) morning and have lunch
here. If you can't come early, come after 1 p.m. But do
come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8293. Also C.W. 7020. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
