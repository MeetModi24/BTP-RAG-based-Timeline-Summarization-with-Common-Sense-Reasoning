96. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEVAGRAM,
January 22, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have read your letter. I am not at all disappointed. You
are giving me what you can. . . .3 You must certainly work in the
Sangh. Your account is good. Do whatever you can in the
Talimi Sangh. I think you must put in some reading and spare
some time for it. Do spare some time for weaving for yourself.
This is all that you should try to do. There is scope for improve-
ment in the quern, but that is beyond your province for the
present. What you are doing is enough. Keep writing to me.
Ask me whatever you want to.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4457
