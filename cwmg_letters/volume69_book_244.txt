244. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 7, 1939
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I received your letters. I also answered them. What can I
do if my letters do not reach you? Today I am sending you a
telegram as well. I pass through Ahmedabad on the 12th on
way to Rajkot. Sushilabehn is of course with me and Mahadev-
bhai also will be there.
You are keeping well, I hope. I also had talks with Mridula-
behn about you.
I hope something has been settled about Qureshi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 418
