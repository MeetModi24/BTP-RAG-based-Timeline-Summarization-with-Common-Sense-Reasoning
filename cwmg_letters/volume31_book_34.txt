34. LETTER TO TULSIDAS
THE ASHRAM,
Monday, June 21, 1926
BHAISHRI TULSIDAS,
Girdhari writes that he, too, has now been discharged [from
the hospital]. So I feel like writing something to you. Shall I
thank you?
I know that none of us deserved the affection you
have shown to my friends. How can I repay such affection? It
would be some sort of compensation if these youths and myself
devote our whole life to the service of the country. May God
bless you.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SIR HARKISHANDAS HOSPITAL
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19918
