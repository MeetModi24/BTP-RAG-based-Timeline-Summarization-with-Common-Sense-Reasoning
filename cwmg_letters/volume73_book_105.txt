105. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 22, 1940
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
Herewith I send you a copy of a wire received from Alwar.
Najim Thanedar publicly injured me seriously forcibly realizing war
funds. Paid 251 mortgaging grand-daughter's ornaments. Population
terrorized. Pray intervene. Jaharia Mahajan Padmara, Mundawar,
Alwar State.
I take it that it makes no difference in the charge of 'under
pressure' whether it is exercised in the British part of India or the
States part.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a microfilm: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
