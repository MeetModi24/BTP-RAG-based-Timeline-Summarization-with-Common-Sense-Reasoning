186. LETTER TO RANCHHODDAS PATWARI
June 17, 1944
BHAI RANCHHODDAS,
I like your letter, although I don't like your writing to me
in English. You certainly know Gujarati and so do I. Why
should not we write to each other in our mother tongue?
I understand the change in your thinking. I want you to
come and see me at Sevagram, after I am all right. I can take
quite a good amount of work from you. I had at heart nothing
but esteem for you even when you used to write and speak
against me because I always knew that you had no personal
grudge against me. You believed that I was doing harm to
the country, and hence you opposed me thinking it to be your
duty to do so.
Write to me frankly whenever you wish to.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
