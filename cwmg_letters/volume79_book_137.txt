137. LETTER TO GAURI
SEVAGRAM,
January 31, 1945
DEAR GAURI,
I know you only through your father. He informs me of
your forthcoming marriage. May you and yours be happy and
jointly serve the country.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
GAURIBEHN
81 VIVEKANAND ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
