325. LETTER TO RAMANLAL SHAH
May 28, 1945
CHI. RAMANLAL,
I got your letter only today. The description you have
given is good. I like your taking charge of the kitchen work.
Be steady. Gradually learn everything about the prayers, etc. I
have known you as a silent worker. You can shine wherever
you live. I am of the view, however, that for making one into
a true servant in spite of one's shortcomings there is no institu-
tion which can equal the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
