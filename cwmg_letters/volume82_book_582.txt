582. LETTER TO JEHANGIR P. PATEL
SODEPUR,
January 16, 1946
BHAI JEHANGIR,
I have your letter dictated in chaste Gujarati. I am delighted.
However, since we want to work for Dinshawji I shall certainly
write in English if that helps us better.
I shall try to reach Poona before 21st February.
I am sure you know that I have already sent the money.
All I am worried about, if at all, is that the clinic should benefit
the masses. Till now it has catered solely to the rich. But we
shall further discuss all this when we meet. For the time being
whatever you can do and get done is enough.
SJT. JEHANGIR P. PATEL
PATEL BROTHERS, PATEL HOUSE
10 CHURCHGATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
