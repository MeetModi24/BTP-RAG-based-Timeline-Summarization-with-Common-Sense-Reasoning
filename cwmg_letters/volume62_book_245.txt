245. LETTER TO TARA N. MASHRUWALA
February 20, 1936
CHI. TARA,
I keep thinking of you though I do not write to you. Follow
the treatment with faith, not giving it up unless and until Gau-
rishankarbhai admits defeat. Write to me. Write to me about
Narmada also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7525. Also C.W. 5001. Courtesy:
Kanubhai N. Mashruwala
