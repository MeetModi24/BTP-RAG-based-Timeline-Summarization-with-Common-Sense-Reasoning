441. LETTER TO CHAMPABEHN R. MEHTA
January 1, 1933
CHI. CHAMPA1,
Your name is included as one of the Trustees. The other names
are from among those which you had suggested. Narandas's
name is not included. Bhai Nanalal will be meeting you and
he will give you the details. I, therefore, don't write more here.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8756
