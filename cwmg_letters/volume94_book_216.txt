216. LETTER TO BHAYANKARANAND
JUHU,
June 13, 1944
DEAR BHAYANKARANAND,
Your letter. Much as I should like to see you I have no time left.
You may come and talk to Pyarelalji. Of course, I have read and heard
much about Bengal and other places. The question is how to deal with
the situation. We must not lose patience, faith or nerve.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
