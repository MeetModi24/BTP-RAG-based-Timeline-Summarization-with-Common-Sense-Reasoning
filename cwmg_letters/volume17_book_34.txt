34. LETTER TO NAOROJI KHAMBHATTA
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Tuesday [February 23, 1920]
BHAI NAOROJI KHAMBHATTA,
I have your letter. Convey the blessings of us both to Jal at the
time of his navjot.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI BEHRAM NAOROJI KHAMBHATTA
IBRAHIM NAOROJI KHAMBHATTA
No. 8, NAPIER ROAD
CAMP, POONA
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 5800. Courtesy: Tehmina Khambhatta
