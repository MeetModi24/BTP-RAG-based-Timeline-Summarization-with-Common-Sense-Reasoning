111. LETTER TO KHUSHALCHAND GANDHI
January 28, 1929
MU. BHAI,
I got your letter. I have not received Chhaganlal's. However, I am sending
a wire today. There is no cause for worry. It is just like a home there, too.
They will leave nothing undone to meet all his needs. Even so, if he does
not feel well there, I am sending him a wire asking him to come away.
Krishnadas left for Kashmir yesterday. He is sure to get well there. Chi.
Keshu is fine. I am starting on a tour of Sind on the 31st and expect to
return on the 13th.
Respectful prostration by
MOHANDAS
Mu. BHAI KHUSHAL JIVAN GANDHI
OPPOSITE MIDDLE SCHOOL
NEW SUBURB
RAJKOT
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 33102
