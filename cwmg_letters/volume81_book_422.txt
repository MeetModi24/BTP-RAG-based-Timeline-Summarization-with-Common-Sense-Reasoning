422. LETTER TO KANTA
September 10, 1945
CHI. KANTA,
I have your letter. I am very happy that the child has fully
recovered. It is good that you did not come. What could you
have gained by meeting me? If you are there, we shall meet
when I come over. I shall be happy if you are engrossed in
service but that as your dharma, not in order to please me or
anyone else.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
1 Narayan Desai, son of Mahadev Desai
