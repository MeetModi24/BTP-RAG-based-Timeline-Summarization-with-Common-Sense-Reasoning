162. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
POST ANDHERI,
March 16, 1924
MY DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
Bhansali has sent me the accompanying notes. I would
like you to go through them and add, amend or corroborate. It
will help me to supplement my own jail experiences so that I may
not give a mere rosy picture. Do also tell me when you write as
to how you are getting on and tell me all about Dr. Choithram's
health. He must get well very quickly.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
HYDERABAD (SIND)
From a photostat: S.N. 8507
