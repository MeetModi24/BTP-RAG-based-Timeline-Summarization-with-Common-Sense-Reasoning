330. LETTER TO M. NARAYANAN
January 26, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of 19th inst. You should see the two letters he has
written to Shri Sivashanmugam Pillai, copies of which are enclosed herewith.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
Encls : 2
SHRIM. NARAYANAN
NUNGAMBAKKAM UNITED CLUB
15 NUNGAMBAKKAM HIGH ROAD
MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
