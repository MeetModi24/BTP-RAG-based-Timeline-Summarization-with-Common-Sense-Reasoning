95. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Silence Day [May 20, 1929]
CHI. VASUMATI,
Your letters are scarce these days. Is it to spare me or is it due
to laziness or because nothing comes to your mind? It seems I shall
have the reply only in person now. I shall no doubt reach before the
28th. Four days in Bombay would be hard. But it appears that that
much time would be needed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S. N. 9313
