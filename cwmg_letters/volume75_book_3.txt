3. LETTER TO KANHAIYALAL VAIDYA
October 11, 1941
BHAI KANHAIYALAL,
Your letter. Are lawyers from outside given permission to
appear in Ratlam without let or hindrance? When will the appeal
be heard? How many days is it likely to last? If the High Court
exists only in name, what can we hope for by an appeal?
The matter about Gwalior is regrettable.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
