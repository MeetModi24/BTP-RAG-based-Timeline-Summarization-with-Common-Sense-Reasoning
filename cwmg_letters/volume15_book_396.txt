396. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO GOVERNOR
OF BOMBAY
[On or after July 8, 1919]
I thank you for your letter. I shall duly wait on H. E. on
Saturday the 12th at 3 p.m. I was sorry to hear from Mr. Griffith
that H. E. was indisposed. I do hope he has now been completely
restored [to health].
From a photostat: S.N. 6732
