114. LETTER TO NARAHARI PARIKH
Thursday [February 21, 1924]
BHAISHRI NARAHARI,
I am glad to know that your fast is over. Bhai Lakshmidas,
Ramji and Gangabehn are sitting here as I write this letter.
The excitement of the fast while it is in progress keeps up our
spirit, but the period following its end is a difficult time. Be
careful in your food. For the present take liquid food only. Start
taking chapatis and other things gradually. I am sure you
will be patient in your attitude towards others. However, I
caution you again since you have had difficulties in the past.
After the fast is over, the mind becomes restless and it is diffi-
cult to control it. More when you come.
Blessings from
BAPU
