153. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
January 21, 1926
I forgot to mention two things which would please you. One,
Ba says she would be too glad to go and nurse you. Again, last
night, she said, "Why not ask Mathuradas to come and stay here?"
I said you would not stand the heat here.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 84
