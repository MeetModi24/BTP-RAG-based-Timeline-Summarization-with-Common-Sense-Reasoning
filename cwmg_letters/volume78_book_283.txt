283. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL MAJMUDAR
SEVAGRAM,
October 23, 1944
CHI. PARIKSHITLAL,
As usual with me I am not well informed about the parents
and families of my co-workers. I learnt that your father was alive
only on hearing that he had [recently] passed away. If I had
known about it I have forgotten it. Do I have to condole with
you? Some day or other we are sure to lose our near and dear
ones and we too are bound to go the same way. There can be
no exception to this rule. I expect, therefore, that you have
resumed your work after performing the last rites.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
