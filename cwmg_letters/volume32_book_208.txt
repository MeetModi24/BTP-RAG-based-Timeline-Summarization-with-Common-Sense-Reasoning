208. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
[After January 2, 1927]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I find that you make mistakes in gender. In the Kutchhi
dialect there are only [ two] genders—feminine and masculine.
But in Gujarati there is a third, the neuter gender. You say
ghar kevo while in Gujarati we say ghar kevun you say tamaro sharir
while in Gujarati we would say tamarun sharir. You will find it
interesting to compare the two languages in this manner and
will be able to correct your mistakes more easily. Such mental
exercise will also develop your intellect.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8703. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
