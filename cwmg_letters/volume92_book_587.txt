587. LETTER TO NANDINI BALA
October 3, 1932
DEAR LITTLE NANDINI 1 ,
It was a perfect delight to receive your letter and garland.
I hope you did not feel your fast overmuch. I enjoyed mine,
did you? God bless you.
Love.
M. K. GANDHI
LITTLE NANDINI
UTTARAYAN
SANTINIKETAN
BOLPUR (E. I. R.)
BENGAL
From a photostat: C. W. 11017. Courtesy: Nandini Bala
