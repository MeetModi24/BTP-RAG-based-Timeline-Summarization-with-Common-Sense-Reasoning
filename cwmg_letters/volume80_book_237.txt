237. LETTER TO MUHAMMAD AHMAD SAID KHAN
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 15, 1945
DEAR NAWAB SAHEB,
I thank you for your kind note of 5th instant. I have been
besieged with letters and cuttings of newspapers from Hydera-
bad. Some of these make sad reading. I have heard from Shri
Vinayak Rao too. Their fear is that justice will be defeated.
Whatever may have happened in the past, I feel sure that you
will allow no such thing to happen.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. H. NAWAB SAHEB OF CHHATARI
HYDERABAD
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
