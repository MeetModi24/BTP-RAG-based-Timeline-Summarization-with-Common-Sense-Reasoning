303. LETTER TO ROHINI KANAIYALAL DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 12, 1930
CHI. ROHINI,
I have your beautiful letter. If Hamida learns Gujarati well,
surely the credit will go to you. It is because picketing of liquor-
booths is a difficult job that it has been entrusted to women. It
requires the utmost purity of character and the deepest faith.
Women possess both in greater measure than men.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2653
