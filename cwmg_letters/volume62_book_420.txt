420. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
May 2, 1936
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
We already have in our hands the work of Harijan Sevak;
other kinds of service of Harijans are carried on everywhere. Now
a third cause has come up. As desired by Jamnalalji the Hindi
Sammelan has entrusted the work of Hindi propagation to a spe-
cial committee which will have Wardha as its headquarters. We
all want you to be the secretary of that committee. We have Baba
Raghavdas too, in mind. Can he not leave Gorakhpur? Do you
like this work? Would you like to come over to Wardha? Can you
leave your work of Harijan education without any harm? If not,
do you know anyone who is a lover of Hindi and can handle the
work of a secretary and who is also willing to stay in Wardha ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1099
