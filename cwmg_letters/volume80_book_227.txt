227. LETTER TO VINODINI GANDHI
May 14, 1945
CHI. VINOD 2 ,
It is good that you wrote. Take Manu off to wherever you
can, whether Karachi or Porbandar. The only conditions are
that you must not allow her to work; and see that she sleeps
properly and teach her to masticate thoroughly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./III
