556. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
March 29, 1945
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I have read your statements. How can I endorse them?
How many naked women did you see? Are there no naked men?
How many wealthy men there are who are responsible for
misguiding women!
Those who are damaging the trains are not doing a good
thing. But this is my view today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 10398. Courtesy: Amrita Lal
Chatterjee
