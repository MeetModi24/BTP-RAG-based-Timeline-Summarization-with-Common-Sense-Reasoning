170. LETTER TO S. D. RAJAGOPALAN
October 6, 1934
MY DEAR RAJAGOPALAN,
I am glad you are conducting experiments with your machine.
The kind of cotton used should be noted. All the yarn turned
out by the machine should be classified according to the strength,
evenness and count. All the waste should be weighed. The names
of the spinners, their ages and sex should be given, and against
each spinner should be given the total hours of work per day and
information as to the continuous time given by each spinner per
day. If all these facts are given, they would certainly furnish valu-
able information. If any [part] of the machine breaks down or
requires repair while at work, that also should be noted.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI S. D. RAJAGOPALAN
BANGALORE CITY
