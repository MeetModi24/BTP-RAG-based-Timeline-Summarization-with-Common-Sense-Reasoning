263. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 22, 1938
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter only today and am replying immediately.
A letter for Jayaprakash is enclosed. If you want to resort to
satyagraha, you must not do so without consulting me. And in
any case you must not worry. Do not ruin your health. One's
duty is but to try. Worries weaken us in our effort.
Navin has come today.
I am keeping good health. My diet these days consists of
two pounds of milk, mosambis, as many as I can eat-at present
I eat about ten or eleven daily-and garlic water with jaggery
thrice a day. I feel better with this diet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3517
