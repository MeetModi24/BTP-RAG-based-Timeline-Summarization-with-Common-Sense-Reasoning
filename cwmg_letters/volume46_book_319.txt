319. LETTER TO V. B. HAROLIKAR
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 31, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
Here are the answers to your questions:
(1) Yes, if the purchase is made from certified producers.
But in every case it would be advisable to make large
purchases through the all-India agency.
(2) I should consider A.I.S.A. certificate as necessary in every
case.
(3) Congress Committees would relentlessly enforce the khad-
dar condition whilst it is part of the constitution.
(4) The relevancy of the fourth question, I do not understand,
because it is open to the Congress, if it is not obligatory,
to picket foreign-cloth shops provided of course that
picketing is absolutely peaceful and free of all coercion
direct or indirect.
I hope I have answered your questions clearly.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. V. B. HAROLIKAR
CITY CONGRESS COMMITTEE
28 SUKRAVAR PETH
POONA
