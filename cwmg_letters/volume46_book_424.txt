424. LETTER TO NANABHAI I. MASHRUWALA
BARDOLI,
June 6, 1931
BHAI NANABHAI,
I have your letter. How can I ever come there? Ba is no longer
fit to travel and visit any place. She gets inflammation of the mouth
repeatedly. But it may be possible to send Swami and Devdas.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6681. Also C.W. 4326. Courtesy:
Kanubhai Mashruwala
