506. LETTER TO KARKHANIS
PANCHGANI,
June 11, 1945
BHAI KARKHANIS,
I received your wire last evening. So Hanumantharao has
paid off whatever debt he owed to this world and has left. Please
convey my condolences to his relatives. What family has he left
behind? Who will look after his work?
Blessings from
BAPU
KAKA KARKHANIS
HARIJAN ASHRAM
BIJAPUR (KARNATAKA)
