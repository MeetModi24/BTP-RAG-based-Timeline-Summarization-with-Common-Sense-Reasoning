705. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
WARDHA,
Friday, December 14, 1928
CHI. MATHURADAS,
It is surprising that there was no letter from you until to-
day. I too feel tempted by your account of the place. But
where do I have such luck? Only yesterday Jamnalalji and I
were talking about you. He was thinking of sending his son to
you for some time. I am well. Have I written to you that
Mahadev is not with me? There is no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
EVERGREEN
MATHERAN
