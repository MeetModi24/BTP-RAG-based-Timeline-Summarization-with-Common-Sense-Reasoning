162. LETTER TO V. A. SUNDARAM
November 12, 1927
MY DEAR SUNDARAM,
This voyage has given me a little time to overtake correspon-
dence. I was thankful to hear of Savitri's safe delivery and her
presenting you with a daughter. May the baby prosper. I hope
Mother is quite well.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3177
