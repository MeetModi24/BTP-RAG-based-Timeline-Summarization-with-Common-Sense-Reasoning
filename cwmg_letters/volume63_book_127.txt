127. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
June 29, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
This is merely a love letter. Nothing to say except that
I am praying for your relief. Do not make any experiments is
the lesson to be learnt from this new experience.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6350. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9816
