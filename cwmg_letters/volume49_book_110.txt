110. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
February 6, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA,
If a boy or girl does not know what to write, he or she must
be stupid. Surely, you are not stupid, are you? If you even
describe the events of the day on which you write, you can fill
the whole letter. Try this when you reply to this. You will
have enough material to write about.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5763. Also C.W. 2986. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
