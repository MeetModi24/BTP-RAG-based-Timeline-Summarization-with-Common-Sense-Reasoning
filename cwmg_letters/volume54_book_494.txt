494. LETTER TO S. DHAVLE
April 18, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your postcard of the 13th April. I have had no
letter before this regarding your activity. Surely you need no
message from me. Your work must be your message. It is
wrong for workers to ask for messages for their new ventures.
They should seek to justify them by reason of substantial service.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. DHAVLE
134 HARRISON ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 20986
