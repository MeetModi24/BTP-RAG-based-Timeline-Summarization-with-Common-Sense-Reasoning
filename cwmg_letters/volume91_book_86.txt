86. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
AHMEDABAD,
Ashad Vad 10 [August 5, 1915]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Please send me two dozen copies of Kavyavinod.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
