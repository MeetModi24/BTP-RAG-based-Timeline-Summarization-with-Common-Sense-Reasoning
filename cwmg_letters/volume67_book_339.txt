339. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
August 11, 1938
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
If Premji is well enough he should be ready to leave today.
I shall write to Jhaverbhai about the piles, he will arrange about
the operation.
Consult Munnalal about Bankelal, I have already done so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1908
