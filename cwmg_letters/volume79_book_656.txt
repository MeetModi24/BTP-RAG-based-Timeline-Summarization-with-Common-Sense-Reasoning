656. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHASTRI
BOMBAY,
April 15, 1945
BHAI HIRALAL SHASTRI,
I have your letter. Keep on doing your work unperturbed.
Do not involve me. Do what your heart bids you-those are the
true blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HIRALAL SHASTRI
PRAJAMANDAL
KHEJADA KA RASTA
JAIPUR CITY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
