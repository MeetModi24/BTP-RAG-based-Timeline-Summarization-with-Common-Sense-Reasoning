76. LETTER TO PYARELAL
WARDHA,
November 2, 1935
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have your letter. I shall not press you to come. I would
be happy if you did. Mahadev and I are also hard-pressed. But
if you cannot remain cheerful after coming here, do go to
Calcutta. Your place is where you can be more at peace.
I am not able to understand this combination of faith and
despair in you. May God grant you peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
