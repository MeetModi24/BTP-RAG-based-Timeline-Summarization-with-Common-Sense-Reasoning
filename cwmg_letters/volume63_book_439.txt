439. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
October 3, 1936
BA,
There has been no letter from you lately. Have you been
ill? If so I hope you are recovered by now. Mirabehn is
better. She is of course weak. She has no fever. Nanavati,
who has some fever still, is likely to be free from it in a couple
of days.
I read your letter in the newspaper. What made you write
it? Your letter has prompted an Englishwoman to write
to you. Understand the letter with the help of Devdas and
ask him to write her a reply.
Lilavati comes here from time
to time with Mahadev. Things are going on fairly well.
All of you there, I expect, are now all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2038
