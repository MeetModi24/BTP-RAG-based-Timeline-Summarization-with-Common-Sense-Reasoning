429. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[December 1932]
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I have your letter. Do not worry about me. There is
nothing much the matter with my arm to cause anxiety. I feel no
discomfort in writing to you. I understand about the roza. By
all means observe it. I will not write any more today; there is
no time. It is very good you are serving the Harijans.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 263
