355. LETTER TO DILIP M. TRIKUMJI
SEVAGRAM,
November 6, 1944
CHI. DILIP,
I have your letter. Your duty is now to serve your father.
Reassure him on your side. His blessings which will be evoked
by the service you render will spiritually benefit you in all ways.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
