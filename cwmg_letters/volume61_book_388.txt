388. LETTER TO GOVINDLAL SAHA
July 20, 1935
BHAI GOVINDLALJI,
I have your letter after a long interval. As you have given
your word to the Municipality there is nothing more to be said.
Even otherwise the Harijan Sevak Sangh could not have taken
possession of the plot. It is much better to let the Municipality
have it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 10677. Also C.W. 9758. Courtesy:
Government of Uttar Pradesh
