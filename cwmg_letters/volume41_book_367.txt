367. LETTER TO M. S. ADHIKARI
CAMP AGRA,
September 17, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I suggest your showing your scheme to
someone better qualified to examine it and having more leisure
for it.
Yours sincerely ,
M. S. ADHIKARI
C/O MODERN HINDU HOTEL
FORT, BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 15545
