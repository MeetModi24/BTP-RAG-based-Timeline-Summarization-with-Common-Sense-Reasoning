571. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
SEVAGRAM,
March 31, 1941
CHI. MAGAN,
If you think the accompanying letter is worth replying to, send
the reply to me. In any case please return the letter.
Since you both are so keen, I will send Urmi back. I do not
feel like doing so as she is progressing satisfactorily. I will be on
the lookout for someone to send her with.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 1616. Courtesy: Manjulabehn M. Mehta
