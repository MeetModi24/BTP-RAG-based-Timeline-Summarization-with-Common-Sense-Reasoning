254. LETTER TO PYARELAL
May 21, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have sent off a postcard today after paying late fee. I got
your note after I reached here.
I am feeling quite well. I had my weight taken. It was
106 lbs. It is not bad.
It is quite hot here, though it is not unbearable. Do not be
slack in writing letters.
Blessings from
BAPU

and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
