183. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 5, 1940
BHAI PRITHVI SINGH,
I get your letters regularly. If you come, we shall discuss
the matter then and decide about the future. Just now you are
gaining good experience from your tours.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SWAMI RAO VYAYAM MANDIR
BHAVNAGAR
KATHIAWAR
From Gujarati: C.W. 2951. Courtesy: Prithvi Singh
