185. LETTER TO BALIBEHN M. ADALAJA
[On or after June 21,1941]
CHI. BALI 3 ,
You seem to have been born for the service of Harilal's
children. So what advice can I give you?
It would be surprising indeed if you weren't ill. What else
can you expect but to fall ill?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 1582.
Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
