533. LETTER TO JITU MASTER
GAUHATI,
January 11, 1946
CHI. JITU 2 ,
I have your letter. You must improve your handwriting.
I am glad you want to live in the Ashram. But it is a diffi-
cult life. You can lead Ashram life in your home and pursue
the crafts that are pursued in the Ashram. If you don't have
the strength to do it the Ashram cannot endow you with it.
Wanting to live in the Ashram merely for this is, I am afraid,
mere infatuation.
Blessings from
BAPU
