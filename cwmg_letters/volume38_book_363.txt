363. LETTER TO LADY R. L. RAMANATHAN
AS AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 30, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your touching letter. It revived all the memories of
my visit to your college It gave me joy to note that you are
not forgetting Daridranarayana . The handsome collection made by
you should be sent to Sabarmati which I reach about the 6th
of January.
Yours sincerely,
LADY RAMANATHAN
RAMANATHAN COLLEGE
CHUNNAKAM (CEYLON)
From a photostat: S.N. 13823
