40. LETTER TO SHAUKAT ALI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 6, 1928
DEAR BROTHER,
I got your letter after the Governor of Kabul had already
seen me. We had a very affectionate and cordial interview. I was
however sorry that I did not know of his coming beforehand.
I enclose herewith a copy of my letter to Dr. Ansari. It
speaks for itself. Dr. Zakir Husain has seen it and he agrees
with its terms.
Yours sincerely,
MAULANA SHAUKAT ALI
CENTRAL KHILAFAT COMMITTEE, DONGRI, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 13465
