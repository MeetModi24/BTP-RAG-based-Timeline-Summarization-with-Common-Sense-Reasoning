628. LETTER TO LAKSHMISWAR SINHA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 13, 1941
DEAR LAKSHMISWAR,
I have read your letter very carefully. It is difficult to give
you an independent organization. You should be able to work
in the existing organization and hope to carry your point with
your colleagues. I would like you also to tell me what your
requirements are.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
SHRI LAKSHMISWAR SINHA
13 LANSDOWNE TERRACE
KALIGHAT P.O.
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: C.W. 1474. Courtesy: A. K. Sen
