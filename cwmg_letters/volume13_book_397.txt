397. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
AHMEDABAD,
September 5, 1917
MY DEAR ESTHER,
I have your two letters really to answer, the last one is most
touching. The cause of the terrible pain I have suffered was with-
in myself. I twice ate when I ought not to have. The result was
dysentery in a most acute form. I am now much better and am
making daily progress. In four or five days, I shall be out of
bed.
With love,
BAPU
My Dear Child, p. 21
