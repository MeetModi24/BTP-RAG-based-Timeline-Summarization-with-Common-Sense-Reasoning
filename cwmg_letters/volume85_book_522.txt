522. LETTER TO DR. FOSS WESTCOTT
NEW DELHI,
October 2, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
I got your kind letter of 18th Sept[ember] only three or
four days ago. You know why it came into my hands so late.
You are more Sudhir's director than 1. He was wanted
by the Cabinet Mission and he went at their instance. My
part consisted in not dissuading him from going. In any case I
have never thought that his youth went against a man. But
let Sudhir's work tell its own tale. He is due on Saturday next.
You have given me interesting information about your acti-
vities. I am jealous of your youth.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. FOSS WESTCOTT
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
