371. LETTER TO K. ROY CHATTI
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
Your enquiry need not be answered through the columns of
Young India. The answer is so obvious. No Congress member
can bid for opium shops.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. K. Roy CHATTI
SEETHANAGARAM,
Via KOVVOOR, EAST GODAVARI DT.
From a microfilm: S.N. 17206
