248. LETTER TO AMEER ALI
[LONDON,]
September 6, 1909
DEAR MR. AMEER ALI,
I am much obliged to you for your prompt reply to my letter.
I am glad that His Highness the Aga Khan has sent Mr. Anglia's
letter to you.
We shall all be looking forward to your return, so that we may
receive the benefit of your guidance and advice. I entirely agree with
you that, whatever differences there may be between the Mussulmans and
the Hindoos in India, in this question of the grievances in South Africa
there can be none. In fact, my life is devoted to demonstrating that co-
operation between the two is an indispensable condition of the salva-
tion of India.
The Bombay Government have now apologized for having instructed
the Sheriff to recall his notice of public meeting, which has been re-
advertised for the 11 th instant.
The negotiations regarding the Transvaal matter are still progress-
ing, though slowly.
With regards from us all,
I remain, etc.,
