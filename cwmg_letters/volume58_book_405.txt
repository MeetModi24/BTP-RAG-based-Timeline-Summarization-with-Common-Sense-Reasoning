405. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
August 25, 1934
CHI. MANI,
I read the couple of lines in your hand. You are perfectly
right in not writing to me at present. Take good care of your
health and get completely all right. When necessary, do write to
me at length.
You need not now be very anxious to spare me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 117
