563. LETTER TO SARAT CHANDRA BOSE
POONA,
October 5, 1945
MY DEAR SARAT BABU,
Your letter. My instinct is to wait for the final reply. But
do as yours tells. After all the stake is very great.
The purpose of this, however, is to know from you why you
have launched a public attack on Jawaharlal. Did you not
first discuss the pros and cons with him? A public discussion
seems ugly. Do please enlighten me if you can.
I hope you are well and will give yourself some rest.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
