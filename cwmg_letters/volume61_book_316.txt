316. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
July 1, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
There is a letter from Calcutta announcing the ship's depar-
ture around August 10. It also says that you must reach there 10
or 12 days earlier. They will arrange for your visa for America.
Who sent you the newspaper cutting ? Anyway, we shall have
more specific news in four or five days' time.
Never mind the sea. You have to leave only from Calcutta.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
between pp. 172 and 173
