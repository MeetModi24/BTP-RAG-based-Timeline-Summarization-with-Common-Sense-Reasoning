745. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 14, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. It is all right. Let Devdas bring Ba
when he comes. About the same time you will be having
your examination. You will come as soon as the examination
is over.
Durgabehn is fine. What should I say about myself?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
