349. LETTER TO HOWARD HANLEHURST
As AT SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
December 27, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I would not take the responsibility of
advising you to come here. Though there is no movement in India
called by the name that your movement bears, the spirit of brother-
hood is there and works away silently. I fancy that your abilities
can be more economically and usefully employed in your natu-
ral environments.
Yours sincerely,
HOWARD HANLEHURST, ESQ.
CHESTER-LE-STREET, ENGLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 15101
