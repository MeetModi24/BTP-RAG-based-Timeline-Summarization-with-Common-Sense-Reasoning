403. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
August 20, 1932
CHI. LILAVATI,
You must have got my message. I hope you have calmed
down now. Remember the second chapter [of the Gita].
It seems best to me that you should entrust your money
to the care of Narandas and let him make any arrangement
about it which he thinks proper. You should know from him
what arrangement he makes. Obtain receipts, etc., and pre-
serve them, or give the original receipts to Narandas to preserve.
You should consult Narandas himself for further guidance in this.
I am writing to him. From here I cannot think of any other
suggestion to make. I hope your health is all right. Send me a
report of your work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9570. Also C.W. 6542. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
