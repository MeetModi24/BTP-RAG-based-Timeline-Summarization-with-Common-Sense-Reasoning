139. LETTER TO MAHESH DATT MISHRA
November 12, 1946
CHI. MAHESH,
I think of you every day. I can now say definitely that
your coming here will be useless. I am suggesting that each co-
worker should be put in a different village. What would be the
good of sending for you? In fact, they should all be Bengalis.
First I have to see about those who are with me. What shall
I do if I send for new workers? How does it help if the new
arrivals remain with me. Now do whatever you think is right.
What will happen to me, only God knows. Have you fitted
yourself for Hindustani Harijan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 6717. Also C. W. 4461. Courtesy:
Mahesh Datt Mishra
