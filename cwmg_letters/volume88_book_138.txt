138. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
NEW DELHI,
4.30 a. m., June 19, 1947
CHI. KANCHAN,
I have your letter. I hope you are now content. Munnalal
is in your hands, and so certainly are you in his. If you under-
stand this, it will be more than enough. If the Ashram does not
improve, how can Sevagram improve? If Sevagram does not,
how can India?
May all of you keep well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6251. Also C. W. 6975. Cour-
tesy: Munnalal G. Shah
