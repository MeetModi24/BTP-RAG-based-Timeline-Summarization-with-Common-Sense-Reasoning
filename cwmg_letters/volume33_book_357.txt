357. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
NANDI DURG,
Vaishakha Krishna 4 [May 20, 1927]
CHI. VASUMATI,
Your letter. Even if you have nothing to write, you will not
be excused from writing once a week. At least I can cer-
tainly hope to see the improvement in your handwriting. And if
you find nothing to write, just say what you did on that day (or
on the previous day).
Blessings from
BAPU
VASUMATI DHIMATRAM NAVALRAM PANDIT
KELAPITH
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 474. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
