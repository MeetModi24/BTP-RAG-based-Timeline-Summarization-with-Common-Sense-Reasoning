107. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
September 6, 1944
CHI. NARANDAS,
I sent you a wire yesterday saying that Janakibehn would
arrive there on the Rentia Baras Day. She will of course be accom-
panied by somebody.
About the fund, do what I have suggested in my letter.
I will, however, do whatever I can. Kanaiyo will write more.
I am likely to be in Bombay on the 9th.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
You have entered the sixtieth year of your life, haven't you?
We must all try to live up to a hundred. You have much work
to do yet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8612.
Courtesy : Narandas Gandhi
