262. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
December 9, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Your letter is clean. Yoga consists in doing your work skilfully.
But skill must include bhakti. The devotee chants the name of Rama
while eating and while sleeping. You should daily come and see
the figures of the three monkeys, which I keep by my side. Where
have you the time to see other things? Forget whatever you have
seen [before].
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4364
