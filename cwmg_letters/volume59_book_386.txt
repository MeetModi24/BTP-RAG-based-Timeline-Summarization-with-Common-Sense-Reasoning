386. LETTER TO BISWA MOHAN SANYAL
November 14, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. The Association that is in course
of formation will undoubtedly take whatever service you can give.
But I would not think of advising you to give up your present
occupation in becoming a whole-time worker for the Association.
I do not yet know how many and on what scale paid workers
would be taken.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI BISWA MOHAN SANYAL (KAVIRAJ)
5/ A MADHU GUPTO LANE
BOWBAZAR, CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
