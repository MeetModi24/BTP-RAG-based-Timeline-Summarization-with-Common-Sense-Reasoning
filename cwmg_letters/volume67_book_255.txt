255. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
July 20, 1938
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
There is only one solution. You must swallow the bitter pill
of yesterday and keep up the effort to overcome your anger.
Nothing is too much for the sake of goseva. No one can be angry
by himself. Anger can be mastered only where it is generated.
Do you not agree? We are servants. How can a servant raise
his hand against the master?
I know Ganpatrai.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am returning your letter.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1907
