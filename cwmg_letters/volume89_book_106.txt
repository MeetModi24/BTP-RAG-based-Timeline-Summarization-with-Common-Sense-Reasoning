106. LETTER TO DISTRICT MAGISTRATE, CHITTAGONG
CALCUTTA,
August 25, 1947
THE DISTRICT MAGISTRATE
CHITTAGONG
DEAR SIR,
A representative of the Humanity Association is soon pro-
ceeding with funds to render such aid as is possible to the flood-
stricken area. I have no doubt you will render such aid as is
possible.
Yours sincerely,
M. K . GANDHI
From a photostat: G. N. 9296
