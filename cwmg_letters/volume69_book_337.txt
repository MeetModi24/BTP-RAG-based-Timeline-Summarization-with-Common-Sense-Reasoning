337. LETTER TO AKBAR HYDARI
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
June 5, 1939
DEAR SIR AKBAR,
Many thanks for your letter of 30th ultimo and telegram.
I am pleading with the Aryasamajists to give up civil dis-
obedience and with you to grant their minimum request. It is
unfortunate that the talks with Mr. Hollins broke down. I
would still suggest your making a gesture. Of course new con-
structions should be subject to permission which should be gov-
erned by fixed rules and times. You alone can end the agony.
Why wait for the constitutional reform?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 6844
