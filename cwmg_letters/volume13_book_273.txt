273. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
MOTIHARI,
CHAMPARAN,
April 15, 1917
DEAR ESTHER,
Your sweet letter has followed me all the way here. I am
almost at the base of the Himalayas. I am studying the condition
of the people working under the indigo planters. My work is most
difficult. My trust is in God. We can but work and then be care-
ful for nothing.
Pray do not worry about Miss Graham. She has been fixed
up at Ooty.
You may address me as Bapu if you like. It means father.
In the Ashram it has become a term of endearment. I value
your affection very much indeed. You may continue to use the
Ahmedabad address.
With love,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
My Dear Child, p. 6
