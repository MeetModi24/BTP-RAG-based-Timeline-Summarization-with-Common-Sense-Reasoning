132. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 6, 1945
CHI. MRIDU,
You gave me a prompt and full reply. It is well and good
if you have not done it at the cost of your sleep. You must
have met Raihanabehn. She should be satisfied by your reply.
I will not write more as you will be seeing me in a few
days.
Blessings from
BAPU
MRIDULABEHN
C/O SHETH AMBALAL SARABHAI
MALABAR HILL
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
