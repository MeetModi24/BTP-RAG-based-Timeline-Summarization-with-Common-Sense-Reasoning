36. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
August 13, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. Your cold has persisted too long. Here I would
not have allowed it to continue so long.
How can I remove your anxiety about me ? I am sending herewith
the blood-pressure figures. Wherever you find them higher, it was
because of extreme cold or food or some similar cause. On one occasion
I went to bed late. There is nothing to complain of about my health.
I do not visit Tari daily.
Kanchan is well. Her fever can be considered to have gone. I
understand about you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
