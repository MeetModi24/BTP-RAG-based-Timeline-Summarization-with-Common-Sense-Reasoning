597. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
BIRLA HOUSE,
April 5, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
Nonsense! No reason for apology. I must simply have said
I might not be expected to write always. I forgot to tell you
yesterday that your letter paper with the edges unsmooth is bet-
ter than with edges smoothed. You must keep well. I must be
here for some time for work and medical attention by way of
precaution.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4154. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7789
