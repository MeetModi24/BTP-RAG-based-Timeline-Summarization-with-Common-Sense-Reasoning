467. LETTER TO M. S. ANEY
RANCHI,
April 30, 1934
DEAR BAPUJI ANEY,
I have gone through your letter to Nariman.
I agree with you entirely that the A.I.C.C. had to be held
at a place and date firstly convenient to the members at large and
then to me so long as my presence was necessary.
I also agree that the co-operation of Sjts. Kelkar, Jamnadas
and others should be invited.
Of course there can be no settled programme. It will vary
with the circumstances as they may arise from time to time.
I am quite clear that it will be wrong for the Congress to
abandon C. D. altogether. I would have the Congress an illegal
body so long as even the C. D. as restricted by me is objected
to by the Government.
But that is my personal view. If the majority do not want it
as restricted by me it should be definitely abandoned.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: C.W. 7955. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
