264. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
June 14, 1942
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter . You have given me good news about
Jayaprakash. I have to send him a book, which I will after getting
it from Acharya. You may come here when you can, and I will
set you right. Sushila is expected tomorrow. Ba's health is fine.
Sushila will stay here for ten days. She will be free in July.
I am sending this letter with Ramananda.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3577
