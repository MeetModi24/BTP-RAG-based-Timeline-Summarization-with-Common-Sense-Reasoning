289. LETTER TO LAVANYAPRABHA DUTT
August 27, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter about my visit to Bengal. I am con-
sidering what I should do.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI LAVANYAPRABHA DUTT
B. P. C. C.
10 SUBURBAN SCHOOL ROAD
BHAWANIPUR POST
CALCUTTA
