458. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
Unrevised
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 15, 1937
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Today is the election day. I am watching.
But this I write to tell you that I have begun to write on
the function of Congress Ministries and allied topics. I hesitated
but I saw that it was my duty to write, when I felt so keenly.
I wish I could send you an advance copy of my article for Hari-
Jan. Mahadev will see this. If he has a copy he will send it.
When you see it, you will please tell me if I may continue to
write so. I do not want to interfere with your handling of
the whole situation. For, I want the maximum from you for the
country. I would be doing distinct harm, if my writing disturbed you.
I hope you got my letter about the Maulana.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1937. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Also A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 235
