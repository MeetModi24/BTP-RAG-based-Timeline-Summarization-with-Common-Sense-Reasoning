68. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 3, 1931
CHI. CHHAGANLAL (JOSHI),
I just read the news of your having been released. Is it true
that you lost ten pounds of weight in jail? How did that happen?
Describe to me your experiences in jail.
I will certainly expect your weekly letter now. What a plea-
sant surprise it was to learn that Dhiru had stuck to the Ashram,
though left alone! But there have been no end of such pleasant
surprises. You are bound to hear everything about me and
Pyarelal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5497
