300. LETTER TO ELIZABETH F. HOWARD
February 3, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
It was good of you to send me the in memoriam card announc-
ing the death of your great and good mother, of the meeting
with whom I have pleasant memories. Please accept from both
Mahadev and myself our sympathies in your bereavement, though
I know that those who have faith in God have the conviction
that death is but a sleep and a forgetting. Do not harbour grief
over the departure from this earth of loved ones.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G. N. 865
