463. LETTER TO PIERRE CERESOLE
November 23, 1934
DEAR CERESOLE,
Enclosed please find Mrs. Emma Harker's letter. She is an
elderly widow. She is anxious to do some work. She has her own
means. Therefore she will pay her way. Can you take her in as
a worker? Of course, so far as I can judge, she won't be able
to take up the spade and work as a labourer pure and simple.
But you shall judge.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
PIERRE CERESOLE, ESQ.
PATNA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
