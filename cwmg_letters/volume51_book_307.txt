307. LETTER TO HORACE G. ALEXANDER
October 4, 1932
MY DEAR HORACE,
I have your letter. I have written so much to so many friends
about the fast that I do not feel like saying anything in this
letter. No doubt you will see some of the letters that are going
by this week's mail. This however I will say. God was never
nearer to me than during the fast, and I felt the love of you
all although I had not then any letter from England. My love to
you and Olive.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1415
