403. LETTER TO J. J. DOKE
[JOHANNESBURG,]
March 8, 1911
DEAR MR. DOKE,
The following telegram received from General Smuts throws per-
haps a little more light on his mental attitude:
7 March. Your letters of 2nd and 4th 2 March and telegram 4th 3 March
all duly received. The legal questions raised by your Counsel are being gone
into by Minister with legal advisers.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5255
