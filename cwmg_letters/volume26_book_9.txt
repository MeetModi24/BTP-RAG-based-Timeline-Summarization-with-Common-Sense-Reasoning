9. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SARBHON,
Posh Vad 9 [January 19, 1925]
I do not press anyone to observe brahmacharya; in fact, I am
indifferent. I cannot accept the position that it is the duty of
parents to arrange the marriages of their young ones. I am of
the opinion that parents should assist them in thus "settling
down".
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 66
