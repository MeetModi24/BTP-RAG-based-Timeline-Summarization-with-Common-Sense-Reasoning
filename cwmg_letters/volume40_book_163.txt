163. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
CALCUTTA,
Silence Day, March 25, 1929
SISTERS,
I write this just to show that I remember you, for I hope to
reach there practically at the same time as this letter.
Nowhere do I see such real education—that based on expe-
rience—being given to people as you women receive in the
Udyoga Mandir. But we have still much more to do. We should
reach a stage when we can admit any woman without fear.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro - 1: Ashramni Behnone, p. 59
