539. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
December 3, 1944
CHI. SHRIMAN,
I just now received your letter. It is full of the affection that
you both have for me. However, it does not seem necessary to
have a change of place right now. Let me see what happens
during the [work] fast. I would enjoy spending even a little time
with you, though. Hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 303
