52. LETTER TO SIR EVAN M. JENKINS
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD, POONA,
November 7, 1945
DEAR SIR EVAN,
I have to thank you for your letter of 1st instant regarding
Shri Sheel Bhadra Yajee. I anxiously await the result of further
examination ordered by His Excellency of certain points arising
out of the statement.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 60
