563. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 29, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
You have been as usual good in writing to me.
I am glad
you have been able to make all those acquaintances.
I am
waiting for detailed account.
Mahadev is steadily getting better. I am in first-class health.
Am going through a lot of work. Sushila is down with fever.
She is better today.
As the W. C. is sitting continuously, I am held up here. And
may be here when you arrive.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3636. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6445
