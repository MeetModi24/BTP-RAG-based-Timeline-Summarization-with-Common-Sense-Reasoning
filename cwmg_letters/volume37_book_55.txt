55. LETTER TO S. A. SAHASRABUDDHE
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 11, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have all your letters. I do not necessarily acknowledge all
the correspondence I receive, if only for want of time. I do not
notice in Young India all the functions that may take place with
reference to men dead or alive whom I may admire.
I have not altered my opinion that I have expressed before
about Shivaji and other heroes. But because they are not my
gurus nor my models, I am not blind to their heroism nor to the
important part they have played in the world drama.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. A. SAHASRABUDDHE
ZAVBA'S WADI, PAREKH BUILDING
GIRGAON , BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 13470
