374. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHASTRI
May 28, 1945
BHAI HIRALAL SHASTRI,
I have your letter.
I am not at all satisfied about Ratan Devi. She has only been
exploited. You yourself should have given the reply. Ratan Devi must
surely be regretting that she does not know English. What does it matter
if the editor brings out an English paper ?1
Do not involve me in the politics there. I wish to remain aloof.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
