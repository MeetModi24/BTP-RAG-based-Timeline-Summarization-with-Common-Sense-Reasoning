372. LETTER TO ASHRAM CHILDREN
Monday, May 23, 1927
Bal remembers that God was the first brahmachari. The idea
seems extremely beautiful to me. A perfect brahmachari must be
perfectly pure. Who else but God can be so? But we must try
to become pure like Him. All Shastras proclaim that that is
possible. There is nothing but joy in trying to become like
Him. It is my experience that we do not get even one-ten-
millionth part of that joy from the things of this world which are
supposed to give us pleasure. Hundreds of yogis have said the
same thing from their experience. Have faith in their experience
and try to observe brahmacharya to the best of your ability.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy; Narayan Desai
