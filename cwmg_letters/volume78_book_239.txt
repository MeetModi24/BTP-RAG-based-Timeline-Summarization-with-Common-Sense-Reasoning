239. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
Silence Day, October 16, 1944
CHI. SHRIMAN,
Herewith my foreword or whatever it may be called. Let
me know if you want something besides this. However much I
tried I could not read the whole book. It requires at least four
hours; how can I spare that?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 301
