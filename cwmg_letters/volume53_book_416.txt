416. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
February 15, 1933
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
I got your telegram. I did not mean Saruprani, Jawahar-
lal's mother, but meant Sarupbehn, his sister, wife of Ranjit Pan-
dit, who is in Bombay. As Pandit belongs to Rajkot, Sarupbehn
can be regarded as a Kathiawari. The name given to her by her
husband's family is Vijayalakshmi. She will reach Bhavnagar on
19th. Consult Ramjibhai and fix up your programme. Sarup-
behn's address is:
Meher Villa, Laburnum Road, Gamdevi,
Bombay. If you can, send a weekly report of the progress of
work in Gujarat for publication in Harijan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3962
