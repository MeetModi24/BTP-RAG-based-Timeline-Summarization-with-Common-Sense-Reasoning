103. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 6, 1931
Here is a letter from Chinnapavu of Coimbatore. He refers
to you in his letter. Please tell me all about him and his acti-
vity. I would like you to deal with this letter by writing to him.
Can it be true what he says about the treatment of Adi-Dravidas
in Singanallur?
Encl. 1
SJT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
TIRUCHENGODU (S. INDIA)
From a photostat: S.N. 17037
