70. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
CALCUTTA,
August 17, 1947
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letter. Is Gangi well? What foreword can I
write for my writings? Ask someone else.
If you want to go to America for your ear trouble, you may
go. Why do you want my permission? One can do as one
wishes. I am returning the Press cutting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi: Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
