122. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 25, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
I told you Mira had gone to Bundla. I have an enthusiastic
account from her.
Lalita Kumari is braving the heat quite cheerfully.
She
is quite happy being here.
Lilavati has no plans.
A[mtul] S[alaam] does not want to be happy. She thinks
everybody is at her.
Valjibhai's address is Harijan Ashram, Sabarmati.
Surely I have been writing to you fairly regularly. Why should
you feel depressed? The Allies seem to be losing ground everywhere.
These are the fortunes of war. You must [not] grieve over these
things. The slaughter is awful but it is part of the game. All
parties know what is what.
If it is the personal element that depresses you, it is worse than
childishness. Let us forget ourselves in this dance of death. And
then you have your day's work for you.
Love.
BAPU
