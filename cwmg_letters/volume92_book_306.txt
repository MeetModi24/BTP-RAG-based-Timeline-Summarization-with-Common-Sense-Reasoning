306. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
April 11, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
I have your frank letter. It has been arranged to hold a
small conference of Gujarati women at Dandi on Sunday. You,
Mridula and other women should attend it if possible. We shall
talk more about swadeshi. There is no more time left to meet.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11132. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
