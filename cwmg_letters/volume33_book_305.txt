305. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
May 7, 1927
CHI. MIRA,
I had your sweet wire and your letter. Fall it was. But I
was not ruffled. I knew that you fell but to rise. When moments
of exaltation become a permanent part of our lives, we need little
further. Therefore I was not unprepared for the fall in the
barometer. You will come when you must. Only you will do
nothing without fullest deliberation.
I now walk almost my usual pace. The round I took four
days ago has now been doubled. Progress is steady. You do not
expect me each time now to say I am getting on. You will know
when there is an interruption.
Pray never rely upon the newspapers. You get your informa-
tion first hand.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5225. Courtesy: Mirabehn
