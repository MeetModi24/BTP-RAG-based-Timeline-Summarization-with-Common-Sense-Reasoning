76. LETTER TO S. N. HAJI
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W . E . ,
September 30, 1931
MY DEAR HAJI,
I thank you for your letter. I am glad you sent me your note
on the definition of the word 'citizen'. It will prove useful
to me.
Yours sincerely,
S. N. HAJI, ESQ.
RANGOON
From a microfilm: S.N. 17679
