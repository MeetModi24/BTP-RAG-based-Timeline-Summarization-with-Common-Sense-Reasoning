597. LETTER TO ESTHER MENON
May 29, 1932
MY DEAR CHILD,
This is silence time. I have your longish letter—none too long
for me. I forgot last time to tell you I had received the book
you sent me. I shall read it as soon as I can. Every minute is
pre-mortgaged. Any new reading or other work that comes my
way has therefore to await its turn unless it is of such para-
mount importance as to warrant suspension of current work.
Feeling is of the heart. It may easily lead us astray unless
we would keep the heart pure. It is like keeping house and
everything in it clean. The heart is the source from which know-
ledge of God springs. If the source is contaminated, every other
remedy is useless. And if its purity is assured nothing else is
needed.
This is written with the right hand, for the left has become
worse than the right. There is nothing to worry about. Only, it
must have complete rest. I therefore spin on a wheel which has
a pedal and draw the thread with the right hand.
We are three — all well.
Love from us all. Kisses to the children.
BAPU
