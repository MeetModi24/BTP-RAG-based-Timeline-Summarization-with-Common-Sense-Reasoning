172. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
January 30, 1935
DEAR THAKKAR BAPA,
With reference to your question embodied in your letter of
21st instant, the only thing I can recommend is that the local re-
formers should zealously support persecuted Harijans as if they
were their own blood brothers and sisters. If even this much
cannot be guaranteed to them, we may not wonder that from
motives of self-protection they go over to some other religion
where they believe they can get some degree of protection.
Herewith a letter from Pratap Dialdas Inform Malkani
that I have written to him. If the contribution has not been
already used for Harijan work in Thar-Parkar District, please do
so now.
Encl. I
SHRI A. V. THAKKAR
GEN. SECRETARY
HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH, BIRLA MILLS, DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
