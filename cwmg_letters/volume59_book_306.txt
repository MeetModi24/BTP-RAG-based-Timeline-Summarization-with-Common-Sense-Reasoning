306. LETTER TO MAURICE FRYDMAN
November 4, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I have not yet seen your pam-
phlet. I am now asking for it if it was enclosed with your letter.
I will not trouble you to come to Wardha at this stage. When
I have drafted the rules of the Association, you shall have a
copy. You will be better able to give me constructive criticism
when you have seen the rules.
Yours sincerely,
MAURICE FRYDMAN, ESQ.
RACE VIEW
RACE COURSE ROAD, BANGALORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
