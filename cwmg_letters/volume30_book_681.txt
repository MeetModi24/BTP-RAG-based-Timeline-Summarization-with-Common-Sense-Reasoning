681. LETTER TO MULSHANKER KANJI BHATT
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, June 12, 1926
BHAISHRI MULSHANKER,
I could make out nothing from your letter. I can intervene
only if there is a debt due to you in law or in equity. I find neither
in this case.
SJT. MULSHANKER KANJI BHATT
KALBADEVI, NEw MARKET, ROOM No. 27
BOMBAy-
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19613
