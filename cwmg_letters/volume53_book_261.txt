261. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
January 29, 1933
CHI. PANDITJI,
The more you write to me about the Chharas, the more I
feel that we the Hindus are responsible for their evils. We do not
recognize our dharma. If we do, then we have not observed it.
Now do what you can. If we try we can definitely reform them.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 244. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
