130. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 3, 1937
BHAI BEHRAMJI,
I got both your letters, and also the bank-notes for Rs. 1,000,
being Rs. 500 from Behn Dinbai Khan and Rs. 500 from you. I
shall send Behn Dinbai Khan's amount to Verrier Elwin for con-
structing wells for the Gonds and use your amount for Harijans.
I will not publish your or Dinbai's name. Please thank Dinbai
on my behalf.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7560. Also C.W. 5035. Courtesy:
Tehmina Khambhatta
