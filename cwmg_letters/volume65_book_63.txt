63. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
April 5, 1937
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
It is not as if I did not understand what you said. I have
only given you the natural solutions. If marrying and money-
making seem more painful and you are not indulging in self-
delusion, you have to bear the mental conflict. Lasting peace
can be attained only by satsang. You should not live as a recluse.
There are two kinds of satsang: one the company of good men
and second the reading and study of good books and conduct-
ing oneself on the pattern enjoined therein.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2454
