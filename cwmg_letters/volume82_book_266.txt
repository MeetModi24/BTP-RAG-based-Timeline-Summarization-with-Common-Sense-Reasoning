266. LETTER TO SAMARENDRANATH ROY
SODEPUR,
December 9, 1945
DEAR SAMARENDRA,
It grieved me to learn from Satis Babu that your great
father was no more. You may know that your father and I
were friends. We often met and exchanged views. My condo-
lences to you all.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SAMARENDRANATH ROY
33/ BEADEN ST.
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
