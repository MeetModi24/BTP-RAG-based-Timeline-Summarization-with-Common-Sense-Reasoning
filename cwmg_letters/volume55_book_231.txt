231. LETTER TO BHULABHAI J. DESAI
'PARNAKUTI', POONA 6,
June 12, 1933
BHAISHRI BHULABHAI,
Only today I heard that you were quite ill. Get well soon. Do
write to me or get someone to write for you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bhulabhai Desai Papers, File No. 1. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
