194. LETTER TO V. G. DESAI
Ashadh Sud 8 [July 10, 1924]
BHAISHRI VALJI,
The time passed off even as I was wondering what I could
write about Abhechandbhai. The job that could be found for
him would, I think, have something to do with keeping accounts
or getting yarn produced. You know all about my activities; it
is for you to suggest [the job for him]. What would you do if
you were appointed manager for my activities (outside the
Congress)? Of the articles you mention, I have received the one
entitled "Simla under Swaraj". I shall inquire of Swami if he
has got the other.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 6014. Courtesy: V. G. Desai
