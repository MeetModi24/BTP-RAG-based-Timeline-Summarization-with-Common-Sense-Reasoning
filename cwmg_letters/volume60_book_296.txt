296. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
February 14, 1935
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Rajaji had come and has left. I had written to you about
Keshu. You appear to have forgotten to write about it. I am
still carrying on correspondence with him.
I am unperturbed about Krishnadas. A letter for Ramibai
is enclosed herewith. If you think it proper, pass it on to her.
I will be glad if you can reach here around the 20th. But come
only if the doctor permits you.
Your ear should be completely cured by now.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have forgotten to write about Ghani's expenses.
He had
asked for Rs. 60. Khan Saheb wanted him to manage with
Rs. 30. What is Rameshwar's opinion of him? Does he give him
(Ghani) any work? Is he friendly with him?
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2957
