610. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
ON A MOVING TRAIN,
June 23, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
Your letter of June 12, 1945 is before me. I approve of the
proposal to appoint Kalyanasundaram to help Thakkar Bapa on
a salary of Rs. 75 per month. It should be implemented if no
other formalities are necessary for this appointment.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
