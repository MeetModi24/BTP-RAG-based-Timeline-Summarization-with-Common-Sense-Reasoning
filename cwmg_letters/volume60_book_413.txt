413. LETTER TO N. G. APTE
March 16, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have now been able to trace the file you sent to me. I am
returning it to you by registered book post. I have already ex-
pressed my sorrow for the destruction of the two samples that you
had kindly sent me. If you would send them again, I shall try
them and report to you. I would, however, say that preparation
of these juices can hardly ever be part of any village industry.
They are not of any great value from the health point of view.
The latest researches show that these juices do not retain the vita-
mins of the fruits from which they are extracted.
If your pre-
paration retains the vitamins, that would undoubtedly be a gain.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
