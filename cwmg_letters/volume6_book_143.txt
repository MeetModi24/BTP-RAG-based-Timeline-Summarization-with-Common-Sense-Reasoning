143. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO LORD ELGIN
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 9, 1906
TO
LORD ELGIN'S PRIVATE SECRETARY
[SIR,]
As Lord Elgin told the Indian Deputation yesterday that official
notes would be taken of the proceedings of the Deputation, may I ask
you to let me have a copy of the official notes.
I remain,
Your obedient servant,
From a photostat of the typewritten draft: S.N. 4535
