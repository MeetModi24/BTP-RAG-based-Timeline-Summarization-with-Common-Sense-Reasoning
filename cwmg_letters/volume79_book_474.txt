474. LETTER TO SHRIKRISHNADAS JAJU
March 20, 1945
BHAI JAJUJI,
Those who spin should wear khadi and those who wear khadi
should spin. Spinning means picking the cotton bolls from the field,
separating the seeds with a rolling-pin, ginning the cotton, making
the slivers, spinning the yarn of the required count and doubling it.
It was good as it was. But since I was asked I have put down
my ideas. Have a look at it and then I will write down whatever
you want me to.
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
