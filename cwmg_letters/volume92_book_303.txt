303. LETTER TO TOTARAM HINGORANI
DANDI,
April 10, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
Anand is proving worthy of you. He is also keeping well.
Please do not worry about him.
If Vidya wishes to do public work I am sure you will en-
courage and bless her. And when she does such work, she cannot
observe the purdah. In any case the purdah is now gone.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a microfilm. Courtesy : National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
