7. LETTER TO PREMA KANTAK
July 19, 1945
CHI. PREMA,
I read your letter of the 11th today. With it is Rajkumari's
too. The post seems to have been received at Kalka. It is 4.30
just now. I am writing this after brushing my teeth. I am
sitting inside a mosquito-net and the light is outside. The prayer
bell is about to ring.
Today is your birthday. You will get this after two days.
You have to live many more years still. Spend them happily and in
service. Serving is in our own hands, and happiness, too, if we but
learn to be equiminded in happiness and sorrow. Isn't forgetting
Vishnu the only real suffering? Why should we forget Him?
I do not remember to have been annoyed with you. If I did
get annoyed, there must have been a reason. But is my annoy-
ance really annoyance? Shouldn't you understand this?
1 Kasturba Gandhi's brother
6
If you start your camp independently of us and ask for no
money, where would be the difficulty? Others will learn from
you, including myself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10436. Also C.W. 6875.
Courtesy: Prema Kantak
