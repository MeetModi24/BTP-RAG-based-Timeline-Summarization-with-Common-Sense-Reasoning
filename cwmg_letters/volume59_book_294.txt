294. LETTER TO DOROTHY HOGG
November 2, 1934
DEAR DOROTHY,
I have your letter as also copies of the articles. They were
quite serviceable. I suppose you are keeping yourself in touch
with Agatha. Therefore, I do not need to say much.
MRS. HOGG
DOVEMOUNT, DOVEDALE, ASHBOURNE
DERBYSHIRE, ENGLAND
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
