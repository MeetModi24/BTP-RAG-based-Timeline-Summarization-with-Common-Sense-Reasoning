243. LETTER TO FEROZE GANDHI
February 7, 1935
MY DEAR FEROZE,
Your postcard of the 2nd instant is somewhat disturbing.
Kamala has no weight to lose. Yet I suppose there will be these
ups and downs whilst she is still convalescing.
Are you getting the fruit and vegetables in the manner wanted?
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
