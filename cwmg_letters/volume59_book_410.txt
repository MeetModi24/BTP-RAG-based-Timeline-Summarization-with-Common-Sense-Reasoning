410. LETTER TO MANEKLAL AND RADHA KOTHARI
November 16, 1934
CHI. MANEKLAL AND RADHA,
I got the letters from both of you. May the New Year prove
prosperous for you. Ramdas has returned here. He can be said to
be a little better.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MANEKLAL
KHIRASTA, via RAJKOT
KATHIAWAR
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./III
