402. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
March 12, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
I think you gave me a list of the books belonging to the
Ahmedabad Municipality that are with you. I have no recollection
of the titles but The Earth and Colonial and Farm Sanitation, both
by Poore, should be among them. If they are with you, send them
on to me or bring them with you when you come.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha, p. 152
