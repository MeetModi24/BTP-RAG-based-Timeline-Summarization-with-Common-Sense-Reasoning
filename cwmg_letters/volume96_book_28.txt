28. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
Monday [After December 2, 1909)
MY DEAR HENRY,
I have not the time to give you a long letter today. Too much
disturbance at the office. At the invitation of Nagdee. I took the school
children to Warmbaths for the Sunday. We have just returned.
Kallenbach and Ritch were with me and so also Pragji .
You will have copy of my evidence before the Select Committee.
It is not meant for publication. Dr. Mehta is there. I wish you could
fix up now the essay on passive resistance on which you will remember
I drew up a syllabus.
Yours sincerely,
BHAI
