186. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
[July 16, 1936]
CHI. NARAHARI,
I have your letter. Nimu [is going] to Bombay. Ramdas
insists that she should now stay with him. He is now engaged
in the . . . business.
I follow your point about Ramji. Do not give up your firmness
in any case. Service of the cow we shall entrust to Vinoba.
. . .4
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9096
