545. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
April 6, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. I hope Devi is well. If he has to have
injections, the facility is available in almost all hospitals these days.
If it can't be done at Khurja, it certainly can be done in Delhi.
Remember that you have the right to come to live here.
As for ordering anyone, I have for the present no wish to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
facing p. 154
