65. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI M. PATEL
August 28, 1944
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I have your letter. Keep me informed about the calamity.
I have written to Dada and asked him to make inquiries and do
what is necessary. If my help is required, he may ask for it.
Kanjibhai will probably be there. If so, show him this letter.
We really miss Sardar. Now all of you must become sardars .
I am utterly useless.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAOJIBHAI
RAVINDRA & Co., PRARTHANA SAMAJ
CHARNI ROAD, BOMBAY 4
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10219. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
