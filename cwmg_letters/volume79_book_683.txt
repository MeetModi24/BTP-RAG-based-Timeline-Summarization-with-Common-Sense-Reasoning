683. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
BOMBAY,
April 19, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
Your letter is good. You have done right in writing a strong
letter about the boy. Now I will not ask you to let the children
go out during my absence. It is true that you are not a child
now. Your curriculum is good. I see that there is a fall in the
temperature there but it will rise again. Our country is hot.
Isn't it? Your handwriting has improved a little. Improve it
still further and also your language.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
