341. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
[SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA, (C.P.),]
October 12, 1944
CHI. NIMU,
How did Usha fall ill? If no milk or soup is given while the fever is on,
it will come down sooner. That is how we here begin the treatment in all
cases of fever. This applies to all, young or old. Nobody fears fever. Sometimes,
it may be necessary to give an enema. Give as much water as she can drink.
Warm water will be better. You may add salt and soda to it, also some lemon
juice. If Kanam really wants to come here, send him over. Or, bring him
along at Diwali time, that is, if you can come. Will not Ramdas also have
holidays then?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAMDAS GANDHI
TORNCO SALES DEPT.
KINGSWAY, NAGPUR, C.P.
