396. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
BARDOLI,
June 4, 1931
BHAI MOOLCHANDJI,
I have your letter.
(1) Village sanitation is the duty of the State.
(2) If the State neglects it, the villagers may take it up.
(3) Municipality means the village service society. Villagers can
themselves form such a society.
(4) Scavenging will cost nothing if performed by volunteers.
(5) In a small village even a few dedicated youths can do the
scavenging.
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 758
