107. LETTER TO SUNDER SWARUP
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 11, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You are at liberty to translate whatever
you like from Young India but you may not publish anything under
my authority because I cannot check your translations. What-
ever therefore you do you must do on your sole responsibility and
without mentioning my name in connection with your venture.
All I can do is to remove any legal hindrance from your way and
that is removed by this letter.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SUNDER SWARUP
LANDHANRA HOUSE
MEERUT CITY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19352
