33. LETTER TO SIKANDAR HYAT KHAN
ABBOTTABAD,
July 25, 1939
DEAR SIR SIKANDAR,
Pray accept my thanks for your very clear letter of 20th
instant.
I accept your suggestion that I may consult the Working
Committee and give you its opinion. The Committee is likely to
meet on the 9th proximo.
As to the Harijan electorates, I do hope that separate electo-
rates will not be countenanced. It is one thing to have separate
electorates for Muslims, but wholly different to have such divisions
among the same community.
Yours sincerely,
SIR SIKANDAR HYAT KHAN
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1939. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
