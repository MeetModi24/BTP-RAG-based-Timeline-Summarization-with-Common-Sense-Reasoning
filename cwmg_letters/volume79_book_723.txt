723. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 23, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
Consult Aryanayakumji or Ashabehn about the hair of the
Chimur children. Lice in the hair cannot be tolerated. Cut the
hair of girls and even grown-up women if they are agreeable. It
will save a lot of time. It does not spoil the face at all. After
a while, if one wants, hair can be grown again.
You have acted with great alertness with regard to Devendra.
Your health should improve if you do your own massage.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 9025. Also C.W. 9149. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
