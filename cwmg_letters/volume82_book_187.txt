187. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA P. KAMDAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 28, 1945
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA,
Though you have become the mother of so many children,
you are still as foolish as ever. If I cannot write to you owing
to pressure of work and ask somebody else to do so, what is
wrong in that? You did a good thing in giving me all the news
of the family. Do come when I settle down again in the Ashram
and stay there as long as you wish. Bring your son with you.
Perhaps you do not know that in Poona Valji used to come
every day to read books to me. I would quietly spin while he read
to me for half an hour or three quarters of an hour.
I hope your health is good. Now I am going on a tour of
Bengal and Madras and am not likely to return before February.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HARI-ICHCHHABEHN P. KAMDAR
KHADIA POLE
RAJMAHAL ROAD
BARODA
