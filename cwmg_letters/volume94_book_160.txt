160. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
BARDOLI,
December 15, 1941
Where are you going to put up here ? If you wish to come here,
you will not be a burden. Do not unnecessarily tax your body.
What is the news about Mummy ? What is Bharati doing ?
From the Gujarati original : C. W. 11222. Courtesy : Sarabhai Foundation
