341. LETTER TO NARGIS CAPTAIN
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 14, 1926
I was thankful to receive your letter and to hear that Perin
was better and more cheerful. I wish she could have stayed longer
with you. I am sure that fasting would be good for your headache.
It is a superstition to think that lean people cannot fast.
I do not want you in Mussoorie.
If you will only go to
Kashmir even for two months, I am sure you will benefit by the
visit. I am not likely to stay in Mussoorie beyond the middle of
June if so much. Is Dr. Bahadurji still spinning? When they do
come please remember me to him and to Manekbai.
Yours,
MRS. NARGIS CAPTAIN
PANCHGANI
From a microfilm: S.N. 19458
