444. LETTER TO MANSHANKAR J. TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 16, 1930
CHI. MANU,
I have your letter. As long as you continue to get fever
and the doctor forbids you to leave the bed, you need not your-
self write to me. It will be enough if you ask the person attend-
ing on you to write a few lines. Of course we were happy to
read your letter. There is no hurry about Almora at all. I
certainly would not insist that you should go. I had only made
a suggestion. It is for the doctors to consider it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7770
