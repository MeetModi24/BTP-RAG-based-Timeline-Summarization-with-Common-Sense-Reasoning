381. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
December 19, 1933
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
Your letters are coming infrequently now. I do not worry
as I have understood the reason. I hope that you at least have
not been hurt over my Bengal tour. How is Arun1? And how
are you keeping now?
So far my tour can be said to be proceeding very well.
Moreover, God is giving me the energy for this hectic travelling.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1705
