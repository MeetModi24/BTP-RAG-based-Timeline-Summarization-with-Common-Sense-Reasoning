374. LETTER TO KHAGENDRA PRIYA BARUANI
April 15, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for the khadi piece you have sent me. There
is no letter accompanying it. Please give me the history of the
piece.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. KHAGENDRA PRIYA BARUANI
NOWGONG
ASSAM
From a photostat: C.W. 9551
