94. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 3, 1940
CHI. LILA,
I had your letter. Do come after your work is over. One
must finish what one has undertaken. Mahadev is at Baramati.
He will return in about five days. Durga has gone to Surat.
Sharda has given birth to a son. Kanchan, who had gone out, has
returned. All the patients are improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. LILAVATIBEHN ASAR
NEW ERA SCHOOL
HUGHES ROAD, BOMBAY 7
From a copy of the Gujarati: C. W. 9934. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
