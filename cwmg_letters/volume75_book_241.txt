241. LETTER TO SUNDERLAL
December 16, 1941
BHAI SUNDERLAL,
I have your letter. Your optimism is terrific. I have answered
Sultana. She has not written back. I am ready. So far as I
am concerned there is no quarrel.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
