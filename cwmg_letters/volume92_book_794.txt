794. LETTER TO B. C. ROY
WARDHA,
September 14, 1934
DEAR DR. BIDHAN,
Here is a telegram from Assam. You will do the needful.
Do not be alarmed at the reported reopening of negotiations
with Malaviyaji. At the instance of Pt. Nekiram I wrote to him
that if he felt dissatisfied, I was prepared to go through his
list myself to examine the chances of success for his candidates.
Maulana was anxious I should make the position clear.
When is Satcowri Babu giving over charge of books, etc.,
to Satis Babu?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: B. C. Roy Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
