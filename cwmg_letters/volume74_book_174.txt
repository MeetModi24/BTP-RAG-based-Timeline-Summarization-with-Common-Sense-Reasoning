174. LETTER TO GANESHDUTT SINGH
June 16, 1941
BHAI GANESHDUTT SINGHJI,
I was very glad to have your letter. Rajendra Babu tells me
that despite your age you are taking considerable interest in public
activities.
Even if all satyagrahis are released I cannot say that I will
not send anyone to jail again. I am having consultations with
Rajendra Babu.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
