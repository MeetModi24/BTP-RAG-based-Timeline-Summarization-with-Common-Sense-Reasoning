120. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 5, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your postcard. I have faith in your treatment and that
is why I suggested that Madhavdas should be sent there. Give
him whatever treatment you find necessary without any hesitation.
I want him to be cured. While that will make me happy, what
is more important is that Ba's spirit will have peace. Chi. Manilal
will bear all the necessary expenses. You are not to do this work
free. Your agreeing to take this case is itself a great thing.
Tell Chi. Madhavdas on my behalf that he should resolve to be
there and get cured and that he should do whatever you tell him
to do. Let me know what the approximate expenditure will be.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. KRISHNAVARMA
NATURE CURE HOSPITAL
MALAD
