576. LETTER TO MAGANLAL AND MANJULA MEHTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 1, 1941
CHI. MAGAN AND MANJULA,
I talked to Urmi today. She would do whatever I ask her.
She does not like giving up the programme here nor does she
wish to disregard the wishes of you both. I suggest that you should
come over here in May and let Urmi stay on. But if that is too
much for you, send me a wire. I will do as you wish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1617. Courtesy: Manjula M. Mehta
