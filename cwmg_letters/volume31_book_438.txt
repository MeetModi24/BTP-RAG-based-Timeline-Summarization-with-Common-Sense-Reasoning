438. LETTER TO V. A. SUNDARAM
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
September 15, 1926
MY DEAR SUNDARAM,
I have your weekly gift again to acknowledge. Your offer
to send me simple Tamil sentences week by week is most tempting.
But I must resist the temptation as I have really no time left for
anything more than I have on hand.
With love to you all.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3194
