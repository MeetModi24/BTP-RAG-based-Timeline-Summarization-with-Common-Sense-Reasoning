173. LETTER TO RATILAL P. MEHTA
July 7, 1932
DEAR RATILAL,
Have you not completely forgotten me now? Do not remove
Shashi from the Sharda Mandir at all. He is being taught pro-
perly there. You must be happy.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 191
