121. LETTER TO MRS. R. SARDARKHAN
As AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 12, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You command sympathy as only recently
I lost a grandson of mine who was also a pet of the family. But
the lesson I learnt from his death was to practise resignation to
the will of God. After all death and birth are not death and
birth, but it is the face of the same com. If it is so, why grieve?
Yours sincerely,
MRS. R. SARDARKHAN
E.C.H.S.
LUDHIANA
