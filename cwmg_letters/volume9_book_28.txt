28. LETTER TO THE PRESS
JOHANNESBURG,
September 19, 1908
TO
THE EDITOR
SIR,
I beg to send you herewith, for publication, a communication
addressed by me to the Director of Prisons. The British Indian Association
has voluntarily undertaken to suffer, and to advise British Indians to
suffer; but I do not know whether treatment such as is described
in the letter herewith enclosed square with the humanity of the
Colonists. We do not ask to be treated as favoured prisoners, but we
do ask that British Indian prisoners be treated with some degree of
humanity in this enlightened country.
I am, etc.,
A. M. CACHALIA
CHAIRMAN,
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
Rand Daily Mail, 21-9-1908
