256. LETTER TO KUSUMBEHN DESAI
July 24, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I got your letter. You say that Pyarelal and others keep good
health, whereas I had a letter from someone else telling me that
Pyarelal's health has gone down. I forget who it was. I should like
you to go and see him again. I have had no letter from him at
all. I have written to him, but these days it is not smooth sailing
with my letters.
Have you been reading anything? Have you kept up your
effort to learn English?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1843
