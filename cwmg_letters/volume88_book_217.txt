217. LETTER TO SIYARAM SHARAN GUPTA
June 30, 1947
BHAIJI,
I have your letter. I do not think your flag-salutation
[song] can be popular in today's atmosphere. But if it has life,
some day it will.
Blessings from
BAPU
SIYARAM SHARAN
JHANSI
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
