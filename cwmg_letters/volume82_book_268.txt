268. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
SODEPUR,
December 9, 1945
CHI. SITA,
You seem to be demanding too much. How can you expect
a letter from me unless you write to me? I know that you are
keeping well and are working hard. Pass the examination.
Come to me when you can. I am glad to know that you do
rowing. It is a good exercise. Row the boat of India, too.
Take care of your health while studying.
Blessings to all.
Blessings from
BAPU
