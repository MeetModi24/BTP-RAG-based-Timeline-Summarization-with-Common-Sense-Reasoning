9. LETTER TO MOHMED HASAM CHAMAN
THE ASHRAM,
June 16, 1926
BHAISHRI MOHMED HASAM CHAMAN,
One who has a perfect understanding of ahimsa, who has
gained spiritual knowledge, and is filled to the brim with com-
passion can certainly shed the turbulent body by forsaking food,
drink, and so on while chanting the name of Rama. You have
done a very good thing indeed in giving away five fields for the
welfare of cattle.
Blessings from
BAPU
