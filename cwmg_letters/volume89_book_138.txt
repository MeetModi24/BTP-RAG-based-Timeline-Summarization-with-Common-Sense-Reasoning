138. LETTER TO DR. SYED MAHMUD
CALCUTTA,
August 29, 1947
BROTHER MAHMUD,
I have your letter. I do not know what happened but things
seem to be all right.
You were to come with me but stayed back. If the work there
goes on smoothly, it is just as well that you did.
Mehboob did meet me. Here I do not have a moment to
spare. The day began at 2.30 a.m. for me. I get very little
time for writing. If one wants to do public work one must
meet people. I hope Begum Saheba is well. It is good you
will be staying in Ranchi for some days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 5107
