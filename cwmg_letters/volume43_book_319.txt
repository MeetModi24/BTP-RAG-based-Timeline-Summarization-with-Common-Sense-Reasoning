319. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[After April 24, 1930]
I have your letter only just now with the post that brings me
the news about Mahadev.
Yes, you may take up the khadi work with Chhotelal. Ask
Balkoba to write to me. He must take milk and fruit.
I am conceiving the last move that must compel decisive
action. But it is all in God's hands.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5385. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 5385
