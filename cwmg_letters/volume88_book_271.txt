271. LETTER TO DR. D. P. GUPTA
NEW DELHI,
July 7, 1947
DEAR DR. GUPTA,
Your letter. Faith to be faith stands all trials and thanks
God. Are not the prayers of your Muslim neighbours sufficient
encouragement for you to persist in well-doings?
Yours sincerely,
M. K.
From a photostat: C. W. 10570
