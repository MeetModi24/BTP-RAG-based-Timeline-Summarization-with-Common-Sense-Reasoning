168. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Sunday, Chaitra Sud 8 [March 21, 1926]
CHI. DEVDAS,
I have your long awaited letter. You will be sorry to hear
that Hanumantharao died yesterday at Vizagapattanam. His
was, I think, a most noble death; he kept up his honour. But
we feel the loss when we remember his qualities. Only about ten
days back I had a long letter from him in which he gently rebuked
me for having quinine, arsenic and iron injections.
Kantilal has been sent to Amreli because of an insistent de-
mand for him. So I expect Ramdas to come here now. I hope
you meet Kishorelal quite often. Ask him if he got my letter
addressed to Nasik. I hope Taramati and Dilip are doing well.
