238. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJI
Kartika Vad 8 [November 8, 1925]
BHAI SHANTIKUMAR,
I had your two letters in Kutch. Give me the correct in-
formation, if indeed you have it, about Sholapur. Have you
come to know the main cause of the quarrel ?
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
I have used my left hand as the right hand is out of action.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4699. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarji
