199. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
WARDHA,
December 7, 1929
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I received your letter as soon as I reached here. I am very
happy at your decision. It is my wish that you should stay
there till June and if your health remains good, come down to
Bombay when it begins to get cooler there. In any case, even
there your time is not wasted. Dilip and Tara are getting
physical and mental education. Almora is the Himalayas. And in
the Himalayas countless sadhus have stayed. Many seekers have
found self-realization there. If Tara understands this, it will also
mean spiritual education for her. You are always busy doing
something or other. Hence it is only desirable that you do not
come down from there in a hurry.
1 An extract from this has been reproduced in Vol. XLII, p. 246.
My health is fine. Ba, Pyarelal, Kusum, Kamala and Bal
are with me. Mahadev is touring in Bihar with Vallabhbhai.
Ask Taramati to write to me. I am here till the 20th at least.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
TARA HOUSE
ALMORA
