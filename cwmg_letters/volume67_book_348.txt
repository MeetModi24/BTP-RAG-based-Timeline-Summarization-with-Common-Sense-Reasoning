348. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
August 13, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
Once you are on your feet, we shall discuss about the change
in your diet. I may say even now that your argument is
not correct. If Amritlal shares everybody's food, he will not remain
fit for your service. But we shall argue about it later on.
I will have a talk with Amritlal about ghee. Pyarelal's
fever has gone up to 104.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 10905
