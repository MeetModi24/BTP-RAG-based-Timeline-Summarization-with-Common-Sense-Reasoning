346. LETTER TO N. V. THADANI
December 22, 1932
MY DEAR THADANI,
I thank you for your letter enclosing copy of the resolution
passed by the staff and students of your college and the letter ad-
dressed to the Zamorin.
I am not treating your resolution and the letter to the Zamorin
as your final contribution to the cause of the Harijans, but I regard
them as an earnest of many services to come.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. V. THADANI
PRINCIPAL, RAMJAS COLLEGE, DELHI
