307. LETTER TO KRISHNA KRIPALANI
WARDHA,
February 15, 1935
MY DEAR KRIPALANI,
You are voracious. What is the use of your asking me to
contribute to the forthcoming quarterly? I have absolutely no
time to spare. All friends in Santiniketan have known this and
therefore have spared me. After all you will have no dearth of
first-class contributors.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. KRISHNA KRIPALANI
SANTINIKETAN
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
