256. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
Monday [April 14, 1930]
CHI. MAHADEV,
I wrote to you one letter in the morning. This is the
second. It is 10.30 p.m. Jawahar has also been arrested. Pandya,
Ghia and others have been arrested here. It is rumoured that they
also intend to arrest Jugatram. All these persons certainly needed
rest. They had been working ceaselessly. People seem to have
learnt now to work on their own.
I shall not have to go to Bombay this week. Swami writes
and tells me that he will invite me to go there next week after
more preparation.
Bring Ba fully prepared. She should bring with her whatever
things she may want. I have found some work for her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11480 and 11481
