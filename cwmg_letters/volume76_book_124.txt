124. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
BOMBAY,
May 10, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
Yesterday the train arrived after ten. Got seats comfortably.
Rangaswami had much to do with our getting the seats. We
arrived safe here at 1 p.m. Now I am writing this after begin-
ning my silence. It is 2.35 p.m. I hope it will not be necessary
to stay here for more than 8 days. Ba and other patients, I hope,
are well and Balvantsinha is quiet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4270. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N.
