77. LETTER TO KASIM ALI
May 11, 1935
BHAI KASIM A L I
The enquiry I had instituted about Shiva Bavani is continuing.
A reply from the Village Industries Association has been sent
to you. There is no obstacle to anyone doing any work for the
village industries but no salary is paid for it.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9751
