234. LETTER TO V. L. MEHTA
NEW DELHI,
December 22, 1947
BHAI VAIKUNTH,
I have your letter. I understand about Shamaldas. I am
sending the portion regarding grain to Harijan.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VAIKUNTH LALLUBHAI MEHTA
ANDHERI
BOMBAY
