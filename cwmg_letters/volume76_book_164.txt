164. LETTER TO RATHINDRANATH TAGORE
May 23, 1942
DEAR RATHI 3 ,
I finished the collection last Sunday. It was a sight to see
the subscribers coming in. There were some complaints which I
must discuss with you when I have a bit of time.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI RATHINDRA NATH TAGORE
SANTINIKETAN
EAST BENGAL
From C.W. 10352. Courtesy: Visvabharati, Santiniketan
