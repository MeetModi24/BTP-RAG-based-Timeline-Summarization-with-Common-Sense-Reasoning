202. LETTER TO S. SRINIVASA IYENGAR
October 11, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I prize your letter. All these days I have pined to write
to you about Ambujam. But a delicate sense of consideration
for you has restrained me.
Your letter gives me an opening and
much relief.
You know my affection towards Ambujam. If it is not a
presumption I may say I have felt towards her even as you have.
It has been a rare privilege of my life to be able to occupy a
place of confidence in several families. What little influence I
have had over Ambujam has been exerted to persuade her to do
nothing that could displease either of you. I think she has listened
to my advice.
I look forward to reviving old sweet memories when we meet.
With kind regards to both of you.
S. SRINIVASA IYENGAR
MADRAS
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
