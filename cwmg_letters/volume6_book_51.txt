51. LETTER TO F. H. BROWN
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 31, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for your letter. I shall await you at 3 o'clock
tomorrow, and then after having a chat together I shall avail myself
of your kind offer to take me to the reception.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
F. H. BROWN, ESQ.
"DILKUSHA "
WESTBOURNE ROAD
FOREST HILL, S.E.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4432
