376. LETTER TO TEHMINA KHAMBHATTA
AHMEDABAD, POST Box 26,
August 20, 1931
DEAR SISTER,
Though you of course have asked me not to take the trouble,
I must acknowledge your letter. You have bound me well enough.
However, one has got to see even the terrible form in which God
sometimes shows Himself. There are bound to be attacks on
our faith. It is necessary that it should be tested. Though I am
filled with the spirit of non-violence, there must of course be a
little violence lurking in me somewhere. Hence, there are hours
when I lose faith in myself. But I hope and pray that I shall not
lose it permanently. I should like you to join me in that hope
and that prayer!
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7546
