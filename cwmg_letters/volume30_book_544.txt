544. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
May 18, 1926
CHI. MIRA,
I had your letter. Not Main yeh (iss) vakt ka aisa upayog karenge
but Main iss vakt ka aisa upayog karungi. The verbal ending must
be feminine.
I am writing this at night just before retiring. Therefore I
must be brief. Nargis was here. She is looking better. The place
is no doubt cool but not cold.
With love,
BAPU
[PS.]
Please give the accompanying to Manibehn.
From the original: C.W. 5186. Courtesy: Mirabehn
