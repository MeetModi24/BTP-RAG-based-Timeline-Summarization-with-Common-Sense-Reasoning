453. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM AND ABHA GANDHI
January 12, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. Satis Babu related everything to me.
Re is pleased with your penance. If you cannot decide whether
or not to take anything through the rectum, you can take lemon
and salt. These two things you can take through the mouth
also. But if anything is to be taken through the rectum, then
why not glucose also? But whatever you decide about these things
I would consider it right. No one should stand in the way of
your deciding for yourself.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. ABHA,
Your letter is fine. Keep on writing like this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 560
