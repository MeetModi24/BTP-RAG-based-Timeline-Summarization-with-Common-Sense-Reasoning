315. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
March 31, 193[2]
CHI. VANAMALA,
I hope you have completely recovered from your illness. The
best subject of study for girls is how to live a pure life. As for
other subjects, each may learn the subject in which she has inte-
rest.
Persons of the same age are not necessarily similar in
nature.
The leaves of all plants do not show as if they felt shy in
our presence. I do not know why some of them do. If any of
the girls among you has an aptitude for research, let her discover
the reason.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5761. Also C.W. 2984. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
