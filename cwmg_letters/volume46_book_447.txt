447. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
June 8, 1931
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your letter, as also the letter to Schuster. I shall go
through it. The situation is already delicate and it will grow more
delicate still. Some good result can come out of it if we work for
the larger good.
Sen Gupta has agreed to arbitration. He has given up the
idea of postponing the election. Anyway, I am reaching Bombay
tomorrow and trust that both of them will come.
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 7889. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
