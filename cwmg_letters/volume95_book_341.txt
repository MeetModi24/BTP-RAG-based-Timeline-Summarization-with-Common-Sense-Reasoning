341. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
CHI. VASUMATI
You must be keeping good health. Has that stupid one fallen in
line? Are the studies proceeding? What is Shanta doing?
I shall not write more for the present.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Ba has returned from Rajkot.
From a copy of the Gujarati : S. N. 9311.
