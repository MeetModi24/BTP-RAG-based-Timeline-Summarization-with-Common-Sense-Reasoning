288. LETTER TO G. SITARAMA SASTRY
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
August 6, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
Mr. Banker tells me that he has been writing to you repeated-
ly for figures for the province and such other information as it
is necessary to complete the record of khadi work throughout
India. But he tells me that he is not able to get complete
figures from Andhra. I wish you could attend to this matter and
have the figures completed and sent.
You will see the table I have published in Young India. I
want to make that table exhaustive. But I cannot do it if principal
centres will not supply me the information. Will you please tell
me what difficulty there is in preparing and supplying the figures?
Yours sincerely,
G. SITARAMA SASTRY
GUNTUR
