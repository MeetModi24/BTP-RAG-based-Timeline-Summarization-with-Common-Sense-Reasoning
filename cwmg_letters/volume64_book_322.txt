322. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
January 27, 1937
MY DEAR HENRY,
Owing to the pilgrimage to Travancore, it has not been
possible for me to write to you earlier. Your question is whether
I retain the same opinion as I did at the R. T. C. of 1931. I said
then and repeat now that so far as I am concerned, if Dominion
Status were offered in terms of the Statute of Westminster, i.e.,
the right to secede at will, I would unhesitatingly accept it.
Yours sincerely,
BHAI
The Hindu, 1-2-1937
