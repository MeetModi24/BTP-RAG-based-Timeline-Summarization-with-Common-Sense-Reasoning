728. LETTER TO KASHI GANDHI
October 23, 1945
CHI. KASHI,
I am writing this letter for the sake of writing. Just to tell
you that I have not forgotten anyone of you while I am away.
Krishnachandra writes and says that you do not keep well
and intend going to Nagpur for a change of air. I have my
doubts if going there will be beneficial. Would not Madalasa's
house be better than that? Madalasa would like it, too. This
is merely a suggestion. Do as you think fit.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
