51. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
June 17, 1932
MY DEAR AMTUL,
I have your letter. I am glad you are staying with your
people. I have not yet been able to write to Dr. Sharma . But I
will do so early. If the door to interviews is again opened, you shall
certainly come and see me. Are you reading anything? Give me
your day's diary. Do write something always in Urdu.
Love.
BAPU
SHRIMATI AMTUL SALAAM
42 GHOD BUNDER ROAD
SANTA CRUZ
B. B. & C. I. RLY.
From a photostat: G.N. 251
