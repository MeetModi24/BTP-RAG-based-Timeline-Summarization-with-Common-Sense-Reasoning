97. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
March 23, 1936
CHI. LILAVATI,
Enclosed is Bhansalibhai's letter. Nanavati will have under-
stood the meaning of the telegram. I have got the import of
your letter. It will be enough for me if you bring credit to
your work.
This will be all for the present since I have lots of other
writing to do today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 Amritlal Nanavati
