455. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
September 16, 1945
CHI. KANAM,
I have your letter. I am glad to know that you are going
to Vinoba. Learn Sanskrit well and of course Urdu also. Don't
spoil your health by trying to go too fast.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KANU GANDHI
C/O SHRI RAMDAS GANDHI
KHALASI LINES
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
