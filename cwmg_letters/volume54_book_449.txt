449. LETTER TO DOROTHY E. NEWMAN
April 13, 1933
MY DEAR DOROTHY,
You have given me a long defence of Kamalani but it was
wholly unnecessary as I know Kamalani to be a very nice, steady,
sober, young worker.
I am glad that the Harijan is proving useful to so many
foreign friends. If you are not a blind reader, as you say you
are not, you will sometimes tell me as gently as you like or as
bluntly as you can wherein you differ. For it is the criticism
of friends which I treasure and by which I profit most.
Yours sincerely,
MISS DOROTHY E. NEWMAN
20 EVENDALE ROAD
STOCKWELL, S. W. 9
LONDON
From a photostat: S.N. 20922
