176. LETTER TO G. K. GOKHALE
7 MOGHUL STREET,
RANGOON,
February 2, 1902
DEAR PROFESSOR GOKHALE,
As there was no post for Calcutta before Monday I postponed
posting the letter written on board, which I enclose herewith.
I was fortunate in just catching Professor Kathawate . He
left for Madras yesterday morning. The Professor did not like
the Rangoon climate. It was too trying for him. He requires a
bracing climate which the Rangoon climate does not appear to be.
From a sanitary standpoint this is a very good place. The
streets are broad and well laid out. The drainage system too
appears to be fairly good.
I remain,
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
