446. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
SEGAON,
September 20, 1939
CHI. MRIDU,
I have your letter. Come whenever you want. I was under
the impression that I had explained the situation to you. I have
no money. Amtussalaam was to collect the money but she was
to use it in my name. Rs. 600/- belonging to her was lying with
me. Now that she has abandoned the work, she has also lost the
ability to collect funds. Now tell me what I should do. Surely,
it was not as though I trusted A. S. and did not trust you or
Lakshmidas? How could that be? But you should not give up
the work you have undertaken. Shankerlal is here. I will con-
sult him. I will discuss the matter with you if you come. I
was very happy to read about Mummy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11210. Courtesy: Sarabhai Founda-
tion
