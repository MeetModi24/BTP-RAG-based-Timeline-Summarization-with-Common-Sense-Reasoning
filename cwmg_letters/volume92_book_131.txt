131. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
BHOPAL,
September 8, 1929
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have reached Bhopal today. I kept thinking on the way
about Taramati. You will have received my letter. Devdas will
have reached there too. And Taramati will have been freed
from anxiety. If that is the case and if Devdas can be relieved,
please relieve him. I hope that Taramati's illness has not caused
you worry. My health is fine. You will get more news from
my letter to Devdas.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
