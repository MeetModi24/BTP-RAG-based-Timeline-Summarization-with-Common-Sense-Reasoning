59. LETTER TO VITHALBHAI J. PATEL
SABARMATI,
July 11, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your kind letter. I think it is impossible to prevent
our meeting from becoming public property. I suggest a way
out. If you take the metre-gauge from Delhi, you cannot avoid
Ahmedabad. I could join you at Kalol or Mehsana and we
could have an hour to ourselves unless you could break
journey at Ahmedabad. If however either of these courses is in-
convenient to you and if you think it absolutely necessary that
we should meet, I shall gladly come down to Bombay on 31st.
Yours,
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/70
