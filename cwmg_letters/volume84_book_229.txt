229. LETTER TO SIR STAFFORD CRIPPS
NEW DELHI,
May 21, 1946
DEAR SIR STAFFORD,
Enclosed you will find an advance copy of the article
which I wrote for Harijan today. If my analysis is wrong in
any way you will oblige me by sending your correction. Please
don't trouble to read it if you are tired or too busy. Of course
you can share it with Lord Pethick-Lawrence.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 192
