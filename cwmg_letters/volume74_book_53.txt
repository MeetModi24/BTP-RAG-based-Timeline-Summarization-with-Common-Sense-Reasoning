53. LETTER TO JAINARAYAN VYAS
May 7, 1941
BHAI JAINARAYAN,
Menon saw me. Now I have three letters from him. Is
there some bad blood between you? I have expressed my
view. You will have known about it. But now I feel that I
ought to hear what you have to say and then make up my
mind. Of course strictly speaking I have no right to interfere in
the matter. But since Panditji wants it I am giving my opinion
to Menon. But if there is friction between you my position will
become untenable.
Blessings from
BAPU
