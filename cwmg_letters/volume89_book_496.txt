496. LETTER TO HARJIVAN KOTAK
NEW DELHI,
November 9, 1947
CHI. HARJIVAN,
I was very glad to see your handwriting. I learn through
friends all about you from time to time. What is joy or grief
to one who knows that life and death are one and the same
thing? I am glad about your decision. Ramanama alone is the
unfailing remedy. All else is vain effort. Fasting is an aid to
Ramanama. Live on without worry or pass out free of care.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
