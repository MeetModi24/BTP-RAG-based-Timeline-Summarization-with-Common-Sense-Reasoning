615. LETTER TO RAMPRASAD
POONA,
October 11, 1945
CHI. RAMPRASAD,
I have your letter. I don't understand why you feel hesi-
tant about writing to me. But now I am about to come there.
Speak to me then.
I have suggested Jivanram as the boy's name. Jivanram
was a great scholar. At present I am reading his translation of
Bhartriharishatakam . Meanwhile, Chi. Kanta asked a name for
the child and this occurred to me. Moreover, he has just come
out of the jaws of death. That is another reason for suggesting
this name.
SEVAGRAM
WARDHA
