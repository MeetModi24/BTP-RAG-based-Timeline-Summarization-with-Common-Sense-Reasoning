382. LETTER TO HARIDAS T. MUZUMDAR
November 14, 1934
MY DEAR HARIDAS,
Please do not expect me to give you any messages. I have
simply run dry just now, and have no desire to send any messages
anywhere.
Yours sincerely,
HARIDAS T. MUZUMDAR, PH.D.
" INDIA TODAY AND TOMORROW"
20 VESEY STREET, NEW YORK, N. Y.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
