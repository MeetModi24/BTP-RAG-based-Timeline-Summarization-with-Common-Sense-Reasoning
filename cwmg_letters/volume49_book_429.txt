429. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
April 23, 1932
CHI. MANGALA (DARLING),
You deserve compliments for standing second. Did you
understand the idea that our bodies are things of little value?
If you have understood this, the next step in the argument is
easy. Is it not a great thing that God dwells even in such a
worthless thing?
Blessings from
BAPU
