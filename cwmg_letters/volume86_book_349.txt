349. LETTER TO GIRDHARI
SRIRAMPUR,
December 26, 1946
BHAI GIRDHARI,
I have your letter. The news is very vague. Something
can be done upon receiving more details. However, I am ar-
ranging to get further information.
Blessings from
BAPU
