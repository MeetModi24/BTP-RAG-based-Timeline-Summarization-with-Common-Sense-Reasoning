329. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
July 4, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
Here are two letters, one for Mother and the other for
Krishnaswami. To Mother I am writing at Father's wish. I have
received a sweet note from him today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the original: Ambujammal Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
