535. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
October 14, 1946
CHI. MANUDI,
You had left a letter here. I sent the reply the very next day.
You would not have got it. It went to Udaipur.
I have your letter today. I was sorry to learn about Shankarlal's
illness. It is good you two sisters went to Udaipur. Tell Shankarlal that
he should quickly get well. It does not behove a scout to fall ill. What-
ever the job he may be doing, he cannot cease being a scout.
Why is Umia feeling unhappy ? Bring her along with you. It is
another matter if you yourself do not come.
I am not calling you to me to make you unhappy. You are not
afraid of me, are you ? It can never be that I would make you do
306
anything against your wishes. I only wish you well. I wish to see you
healthy and well.
Now there is no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
