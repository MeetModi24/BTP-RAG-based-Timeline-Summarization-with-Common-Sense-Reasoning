25. LETTER TO KAMALA NEHRU
October 10, 1935
CHI. KAMALA,
What can I write to you except to say that we remember
you every day? May God give you peace whatever be the cir-
cumstances. Have no worry for anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
