35. LETTER TO SIR EVAN M. JENKINS
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD, POONA,
November 5, 1945
DEAR SIR EVAN,
I thank you for your letter of 1st instant informing me of
the action taken on my letter of 29th ultimo on khadi.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 70
