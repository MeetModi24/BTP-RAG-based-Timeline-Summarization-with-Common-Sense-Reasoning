145. LETTER TO J. KITCHIN
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 9, 1906
DEAR MR. KITCHIN,
I have purposely delayed replying to your kind note as my move-
ments were so uncertain.
I shall have much pleasure in dining with you on Wednesday next,
and I shall take the train at Charing Cross at 6.45 p.m.
If it is not inconvenient to you, we might meet at the station about
that time. I have not looked at a guide but I take it that it is the main
station from which I get my ticket.
Yours truly,
JOSEPH KITCHIN, ESQ.
"INGLENOOK "
BRACKLEY ROAD
BECKENHAM
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4532
