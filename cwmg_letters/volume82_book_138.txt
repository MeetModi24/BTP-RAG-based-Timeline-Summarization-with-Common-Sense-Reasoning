138. LETTER TO INAYATULLA KHAN
November 17, 1945
ALLAMA SAHEB,
I got your letter and two Khaksar officials also came and saw
me. I was very happy. I shall reach Bombay on the 19th but
that will be my silence day. The silence will end at about 7.30
p. m. At that time I have a meeting with a few Hindustani
teachers. On the 20th we have to catch a train for Wardha
but still I have told the Khaksar officials that you may come at
3 p. m. I shall wait for you then. If you want any change in
the time, please send a message to me at the Birla House.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
