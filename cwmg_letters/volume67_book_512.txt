512. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
September 16, 1938
CHI. PRABHA,
I assume that you have reached Malabar and that Jaya-
prakash's treatment has started. Prithuraj, Shamjibhai, etc., are
there. See them.
Ba, I and others will reach Delhi on the 20th. We shall be
there for about eight days. Thereafter I will go to the Frontier
Province. Ba will remain in Delhi. Ramdas will land in Bombay
tomorrow.
I am all right. Manu had fever but she is well now.
Pyarelal has fully recovered. I hope to return by the end of
October.
Do not be remiss in writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
