164. LETTER TO MOHANLAL RUPANI
SEVAGRAM,
August 13, 1946
BHAI MOHANLAL,
I got your letter and Rs. 51. The amount has been credit-
ed to the Harijan account.
Vrajlal's brother is bound to be
like him. I can therefore understand your not introducing him.
Still, if he had come to see me I would have spoken to him. I
infer from your letter-head that you are quite well off. Is there
some rule that letter-heads must only be printed in English?
Vrajlal has lived his life in such a way that we remember him
every moment.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MOHANLAL RUPANI
MANEKIA CHAMBER
1ST MARINE STREET
DHOBI TALAO, BOMBAY
