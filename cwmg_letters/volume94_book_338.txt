338. LETTER TO LAKSHMINARAYAN
SEVAGRAM,
February 16, 1945
BHAI LAKSHMI BABU,
We cannot accept even one of the three conditions even though
we concede that the speech was provocative. The constructive
programme of today has nothing to do with any movement such as
that of 1942. Even if we are opposed to the violent activities of Shri
Jayaprakash, we cannot accept any of the three conditions. In other
words, we shall not get those things done by coercion. If freedom of
India is common cause between us and the Government, why this
discrimination ? The truth of the matter is that the Bihar government
has committed a mistake in arresting people and instead of admitting
the mistake, it wants to throw the responsibility on us. We shall not
accept that responsibility even if they destroy us. Even if they seek to
prevent us from carrying on our peaceful work, we shall march ahead.
Blessings from
BAPU
