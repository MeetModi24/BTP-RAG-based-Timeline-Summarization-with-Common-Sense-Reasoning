155. LETTER TO B. G. HORNIMAN
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 15, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I was obliged to send you the telegram
if only because I am under a vow not to go out of Ahmedabad
or undertake any public function except for unforeseen events or
for reasons of health. You will appreciate my position and for-
give me.
Yours sincerely,
B. G. HORNIMAN, ESQ.
"THE INDIAN NATIONAL HERALD"
NAVSARI BUILDING
HORNBY ROAD
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 10962
