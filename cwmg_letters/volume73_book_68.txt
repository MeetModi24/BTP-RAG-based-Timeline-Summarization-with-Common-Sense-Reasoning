68. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 7, 1940
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
I enclose herewith Pandit Nehru's letter and its enclo-
sure. Though it relates to an individual British officer who
has risen against the tradition, I think the case merits sympathetic
treatment. The Pandit's letter I send in order that you may
know the inner working of the mind of one who will be the
future leader of all India. You will please tell me if I have
overstepped the limit in sending you these papers.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a microfilm: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
