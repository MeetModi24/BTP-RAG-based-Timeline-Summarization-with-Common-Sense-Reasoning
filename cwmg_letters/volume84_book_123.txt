123. LETTER TO PUSHPA N. NAIK
SIMLA,
May 5, 1946
CHI. PUSHPA,
Your letter. With God's grace, everything is working out
well. I personally liked your going to Chi. Mukta's wedding with
the consent of all the elders there. You are on test. You will
be pleasing your parents and other relations. Chi. Vrijlal also
will be happy. Return to the Ashram after satisfying them all
and then throw yourself heart and soul into service and see God
therethrough. Remember that God has no form or shape.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 9272
