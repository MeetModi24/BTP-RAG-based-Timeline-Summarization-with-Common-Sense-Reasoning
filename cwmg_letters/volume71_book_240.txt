240. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
February 8, 1940
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
If you are tired of this milk-drinking, it is another matter
but I shall not be tired. Nor am I going to shift you from
there. Stay here and do cheerfully whatever work I allot you. That
is sadhana for you as well as service to the cow.
The lady's letter is good. You may read out to me whatever
you want me to hear. I have written to Abhayadev.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1929
