411. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
SEGAON,
November 16, 1939
CHI. AMRITLAL,
You did well in remaining there for the sake of Kamath. I
shall discuss with Sushila about the purgative. What you have
written about Ashalatabehn is correct. Hers is a difficult problem.
I shall see. If grown-up persons cannot look after themselves,
what can we do?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10798
