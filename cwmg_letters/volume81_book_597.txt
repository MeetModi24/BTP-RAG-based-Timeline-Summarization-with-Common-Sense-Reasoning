597. LETTER TO CHAMPA R. MEHTA
October 9, 1945
CHI. CHAMPA,
I have your pathetic letter. What advice can I give you?
And how may I guide you?
I cling to your remark in yester-
day's letter and derive confidence from it and hope that you
will be able to face your difficulties.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRIMATI CHAMPABEHN R. MEHTA
CHANDRAKUNJ
JAGNATH PLOT
RAJKOT
From a photostat of the Gujarati ; G.N. 8758. Also C.W. 1044. Cour-
tesy ; Champa R. Mehta
