51. LETTER TO GOVIND DAS
April 28, 1945
BHAI GOVIND DAS,
I have your letter. You must get rid of the fits of giddiness.
Think over nature cure treatment. There is someone in Allahabad.
Otherwise there are doctors.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHETH GOVIND DAS
JABALPUR
