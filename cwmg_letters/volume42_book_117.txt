117. LETTER TO N. M. KHARE
ALIGARH,
November 5, 1929
BHAI PANDITJI,
Chhaganlal must have left by now. I shall therefore not
reply to his letters. I hope Manu is keeping up the steady reco-
very in health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 210. Courtesy: Lakshmibai Khare
