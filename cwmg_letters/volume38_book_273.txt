273. LETTER TO HARI KRISHAN DASS
AS AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 14, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Unless I see the text of the definite pro-
mise said to have been made by the British when your town
was founded, it is difficult for me to give you any advice. But of
course before adopting satyagraha, if it ever becomes advisable,
you will exhaust all the constitutional means at your disposal and
if you have a strong case, you will also approach good Mussalmans
and seek their intervention. By way of example I give you the
name of Dr. Ansari.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. HARI KRISHAN DASS
EDITOR, "THE BIJLI", FAZILKA
From a photostat: S.N. 13800
