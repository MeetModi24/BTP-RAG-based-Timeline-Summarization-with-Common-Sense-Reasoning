568. LETTER TO RANGANAYAKI DEVI
NEW DELHI,
October 10, 1946
CHI. RANGANAYAKI,
I have your letter. You may come wherever I may be.
God is in you. He will give you fortitude. You have ears
to listen to His voice, then why bother to hear anything else?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RANGANAYAKI DEVI
FIRST HOUSE
SRIRANGAM
