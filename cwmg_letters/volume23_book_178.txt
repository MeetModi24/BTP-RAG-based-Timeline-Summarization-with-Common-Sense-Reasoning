178. LETTER TO D. R. MAJLI
March 19, 1924
MY DEAR MAJLI,
My hand is a little steady now. How delightful to have your
postcard! Do write whenever you can. You will soon be calm, I
have no doubt. Do not fail to come here whenever you are per-
mitted and able.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
D. R. MAJLI
BELGAUM
From a copy: S.N. 8530
