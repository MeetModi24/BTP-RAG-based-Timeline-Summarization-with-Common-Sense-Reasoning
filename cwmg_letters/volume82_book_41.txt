41. LETTER TO LILAVATI PARIKH
November 6, 1945
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. You must tell your father everything and
then take his advice. If you do not have that much courage,
I am afraid you will not be able to do anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
LILAVATI PARIKH
[C/O] OCHCHHAVLAL NATHABHAI SHETH
KALYAN MOTI's CHAWL
ROOM No. 30/31
BOMBAY 4
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
