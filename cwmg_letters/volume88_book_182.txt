182. LETTER TO KULARANJAN MUKHERJEE
NEW DELHI,
June 27, 1947
DEAR KULARANJAN,
Your letter. Abha is there. It is now for you to convert
her to your method. I am anxious for her to gain complete
recovery. I am glad her brother is progressing.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
[PS.]
What about the phone?
SHRI KULARANJAN MUKHERJEE
114-255-2C HAZRA ROAD
KALIGHAT
CALCUTTA
