571. LETTER TO GATULAL J. DAVE
January 15, 1946
BHAI GATUBHAI,
I got your letter yesterday.
I don't know at all what in the first place I can do in
Maganbhai's case. I shall not be able to give him as much
time as he wants. Besides, it is very difficult for me to be in-
volved in this matter. I am therefore of the opinion that you
should seek the advice of some good lawyer and act strictly as
he directs.
GATULAL JATASHANKAR DAVE
KARANPARA, SHERI No. 23
RAJKOT
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
