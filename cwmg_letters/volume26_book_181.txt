181. LETTER TO KALYANJI V. MEHTA
Monday [March 16, 1925]
BHAI KALYANJI,
I felt at first like sending you a wire, but succumbed to my
tendency to thrift. Only today I read in Navajivan about your
release. Well and good. I shall reach the Ashram on the 27th.
You will no doubt come to see me there. I hope you are perfectly
all right now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of Gujarati: G.N. 2678
