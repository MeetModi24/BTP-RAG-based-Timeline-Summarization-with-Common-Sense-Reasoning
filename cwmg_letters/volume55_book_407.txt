407. LETTER TO HOME SECRETARY,
GOVERNMENT OF BOMBAY
August 10, 1933
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
HOME DEPARTMENT
POONA
DEAR SIR,
I regret to have to remind you of my letters regarding
Harijan work. Kakasaheb Kalelkar whom I met on Monday last
told me that there were urgent letters in the matter awaiting me
in the post. There are pressing Harijan problems demanding my
immediate attention. I do hope therefore that I shall be
favoured with the final decision before or on Monday next at the
latest.
I attach hereto a copy of the Government of India's orders
in the matter. In my humble opinion they are unequivocal.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI

ment, Special Branch, File No. 800(40) (14), Pt. V, p. 95
