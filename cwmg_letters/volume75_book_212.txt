212. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
December 5, 1941
BHAI MUNSHI,
Kanhaiyalal Vaidya will be seeing you. He will tell you about
Ratlam. Go there. Do what you can. Whether your efforts as a
lawyer succeed or not, the poor prisoners will feel comforted by
your going.
Meet the officials there and see that, outside your
sphere of duty too, compassion prevails.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7671. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
