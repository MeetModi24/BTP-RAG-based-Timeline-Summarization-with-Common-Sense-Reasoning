350. LETTER TO SHAMALDAS GANDHI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 29, 1945
CHI. SHAMALDAS,
I have received both of your letters. It is all right that
you wrote to me a long letter. If the hopes you entertain are
fulfilled, then mine also will be fulfilled.
Your father in his
last letter had entrusted all of you to me. He had especially
high hopes of Ranchhod but he wrote quite a lot about you
and Shanti. I never met Ranchhod. Let us see how Shanti
ultimately fares. For the present I have pinned my hopes on you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
