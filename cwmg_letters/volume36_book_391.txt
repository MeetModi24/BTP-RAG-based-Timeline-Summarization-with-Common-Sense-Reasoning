391. LETTER TO ZAKIR HUSAIN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
May 23, 1928
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter, which I prize for its absolute frankness.
I would personally have preferred a declaration of empha-
tic non-co-operation; but I am not prepared to advise you to
abandon the institution because you have a milder declaration.
After all, it is not the declaration that so much matters as action
when the testing time comes.
The fate of the institution will
depend ultimately not upon the trustees but upon the professors
who are giving their all to it.
I know your pecuniary difficulties. I am helpless. I discussed
the thing with Dr. Ansari in Bombay and he told me that he hoped
to send you some money from Bombay. I could not ask Jamnalalji
to send you further advance unless everything was in order.
I do not at all like the large body.
Dr. Ansari has promised to come to Sabarmati immediately
after Id. If he does, I shall re-discuss the thing with him.
Yours sincerely,
DR. ZAKIR HUSAIN
J A M I A MILLIA
KAROL BAGH
DELHI
