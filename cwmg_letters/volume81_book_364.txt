364. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
September 2, 1945
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your letter. I am sorry to learn that you are not keep-
ing well. Come over any time after you are all right. Let me
add that my staying anywhere has become a little indefinite.
It is very crowded here and I can't say where I will be. There
is some talk about my going to Bengal. Keep writing to me at
Poona how you are and when you want to come.
I hope Mother is all right. It is sad about Padma. It is as
God wills.
Blessings from
BAPU
S. AMBUJAMMAL
96 MOWBRAYS ROAD
TEYNAMPET, MADRAS
