12. LETTER TO PURATAN J. BUCH
April 21, 1941
CHI. PURATAN,
Vasumatibehn has given me your message. How can I forget
Anandi or you? But Anandi seems to have completely forgotten
me.
What is this that has happened in Ahmedabad? Have the
Pathans ruined your work?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9180
