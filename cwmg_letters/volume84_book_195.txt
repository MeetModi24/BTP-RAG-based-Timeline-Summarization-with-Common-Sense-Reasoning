195. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
SIMLA,
May 14, 1946
CHI. SHRIMAN,
Received your letter and some of the books of the Adabi
Board. I have glanced through the books but I can say nothing
about them. You will not send copies to all members, will you?
Can we not read them at the meeting? If the meeting is called
in August I may be able to attend it. You are not likely to
call it in Poona or Uruli, are you?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchaven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 309
