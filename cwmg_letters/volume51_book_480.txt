480. LETTER TO ABUL KALAM AZAD
October 24, 1932
DEAR MAULANA SAHEB,
I had your telegram and I sent a reply there and then. It has
been forwarded to the Government for approval.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MAULANA ABUL KALAM AZAD
CARE MUNICIPAL CORPORATION
CALCUTTA
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No. 800
(40)(3), Pt. III
