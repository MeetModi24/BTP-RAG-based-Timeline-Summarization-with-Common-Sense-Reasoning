186. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
SEVAGRAM,
June 24, 1942
BHAI GOPICHAND,
You are carrying on your struggle well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND
LAJPAT BHAWAN
LAHORE
PUNJAB
