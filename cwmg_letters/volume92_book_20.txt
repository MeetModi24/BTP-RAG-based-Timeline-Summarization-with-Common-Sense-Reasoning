20. LETTER TO PANNALAL JAIN
March 23, 1929
BHAI PANNALALJI,
Your November letter reached me only today. It is impos-
sible to increase the size of Navajivan at present.
Many things were deliberately left out and some could not
be included in the Autobiography. Only those things have been
mentioned which were relevant to the experiments with Truth.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI PANNALAL JAIN
KALYANMAL MILLS
INDORE
[From Hindi]
Madhya Pradesh aur Gandhiji, p. 151
