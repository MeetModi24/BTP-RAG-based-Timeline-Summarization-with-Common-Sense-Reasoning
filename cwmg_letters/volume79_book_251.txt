251. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
February 20, 1945
CHI. RAMDAS,
The enclosed letter is for all of you to see and then pass on
to Sumi. How well written it is! Sita is making progress.
I have a letter from Sumi but I regard it as one from
Nimu because although in Sumi's hand it was written at Nimu's
instance. Sumi is bound to me by a promise. But being a
child she has forgotten about it. I have not a minute to spare
or I should have written separately to her. I have Kanam's letter.
I don't have the time to write to him separately. Dr. Mahmud
has fever. Kanam should write to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
