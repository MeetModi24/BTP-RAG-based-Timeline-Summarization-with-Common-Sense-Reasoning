511. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Y. M.,
March 11, 1932
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Write to me everything about Shankerlal. God is our
Protector.
Who is this Nanakram in Majoor Hospital? What is wrong
with him? If necessary have him admitted to the Municipal
Hospital.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32785
