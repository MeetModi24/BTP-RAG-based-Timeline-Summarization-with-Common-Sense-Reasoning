503. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
SABARMATI,
January 31, 1929
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I have your letter. I do not mind your not being present at
the prayers. But your unhappiness at not being able to come
pleases me. Once we realize the significance of prayer, it becomes
more essential for us than food and water.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 6064. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
