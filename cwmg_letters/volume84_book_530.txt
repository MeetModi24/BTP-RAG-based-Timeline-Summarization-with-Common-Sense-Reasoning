530. LETTER TO SITA CHARAN DIKSHIT
PANCHGANI,
July 14, 1946
BHAI DIKSHIT,
I have a letter from Ratnamayibehn. It makes me happy
as well as unhappy. Unhappy because the fear which I had
and which I had expressed has proved true. And I am happy
at the frank admission of weakness. If you even settle down
in your present sphere of work and achieve success in it, I shall
be satisfied. To have confidence about something which is be-
yond our attainment is also a fault, it is a sign of pride.
"All creatures follow their nature; what then will constraint
avail ?"
Send this letter or a copy of it to Ratnamayi as I am not
writing to her separately.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SITA CHARAN DIKSHIT
C/O Nagpur Times
NAGPUR
