326. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
BARDOLI,
May 31, 1931
BHAI VITHALDAS,
Of course you are always in my thoughts. I have been inquir-
ing after your health too. How did you get this disease? Don't
insist on returning to work immediately. First get all right. If
necessary, go for a change to a place like Deolali.
Blessings from
BAPU
