30. LETTER TO EMIL RONIGER
POST ANDHERI,
May 15, 1924
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter. I have no copyright in connection with any
of the works you mention. They are not even published by me.
Strictly speaking, you should ask the various publishers for per-
mission. I do not think there will be any objection to the pro-
posed publication by you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
EMIL RONIGER, ESQ.
RHEIN FELDEN
(SWITZERLAND)
From a photostat : S.N. 8802
