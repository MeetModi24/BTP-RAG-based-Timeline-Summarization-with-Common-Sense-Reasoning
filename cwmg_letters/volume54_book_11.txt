11. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
March 7, 1933
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL2,
I have your letter. For the present you should give up the
idea of starting a school of the Sangh in Surat. If we can manage
well what we have already undertaken, we shall later think about
Surat.
I understand about Sarupbehn .
We are thinking of starting a Gujarati edition of the Harijan
to be published from here. The idea of publishing a leaflet is not
bad.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3994
