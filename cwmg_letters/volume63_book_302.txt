302. LETTER TO R. S. PANDIT
WARDHA,
August 26, 1936
CHI. RANJIT,
Why are you keeping indifferent health? Sarup writes that
now you are a little better. You must make your body as strong
as steel even if it requires some effort. One can certainly make a
boat to cross the Ganga. The real achievement would be to
make a boat to cross the waters of slavery. But you cannot do
this with a hollow chest. You should be strong enough not to
let the oar slip from your hands.
