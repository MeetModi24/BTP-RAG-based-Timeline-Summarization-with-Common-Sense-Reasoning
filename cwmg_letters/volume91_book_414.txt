414. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
November 14, 1925
MY DEAR PADMAJA,
Mother is here today to see how I look after the Cutch tour.
She tells me this should reach you on your birthday. This, there-
fore, comes to you charged with all the love I am capable of bear-
ing towards you. And you know what that love is. Are you
going to behave yourself and be strong? You have to help mother
in her labours next year.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
My right hand refuses to work. Hence this is written with the
left.
M.K.G.
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
