494. LETTER TO ABRAHIM RAHIMTOOLA
March 9, 1941
DEAR SIR,
I was delighted to see your firm signature. I am running a
close race with you but you can't be reached, I see. I shall make
time to glance through your pamphlets. I hope you are keeping
as fresh as your signature.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
