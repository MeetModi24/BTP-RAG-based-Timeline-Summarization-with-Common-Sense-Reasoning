509. LETTER TO LAKSHMANDAS KAPUR
April 2, 1935
BHAI KAPURJI,
I have been wanting to write to you all these days but could
not do so. Please forgive me. The new glasses sent by you fit very
well and now those are the ones I use every day. Thank you.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2216
