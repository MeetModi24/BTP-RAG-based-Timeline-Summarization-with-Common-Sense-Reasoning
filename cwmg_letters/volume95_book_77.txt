77. LETTER TO MANU H. GANDHI
Ashad Sud 5 [July 4, 1927]
CHI. MANU,
I have your letter. Keep on writing in that manner. We all think
of you but Bamisseseg you very much. She often says, 'When shall I
see Manu?' But that is in the hands of God. We shall meet when He
ordains. Do you learn bhajans, etc., there? Hari-ichchhabehn
Tarabehn and others are there. Do you ever meet them?
There is a Khadi Exhibition here today. Manibehn cards cotton
there. Kanti Parekh has also come. He also cards cotton. Keshubhai
spins cotton. There are so many others. It has a good collection of
khadi. If you were here, I would have shown you everything. But can
we poor travel such a distance [only for this purpose]?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 5053. Courtesy: Manu S.
Mashruwala
