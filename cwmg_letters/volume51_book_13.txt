13. LETTER TO YOGA V. KHARE
September 2, 1932
CHI. YOGA 2 ,
If you cannot read or follow this, request Panditji to read it
to you. I hope that you will continue to like the Ashram as much
as you do now and that your health will remain as good as it is
and even improve. Anybody who is ready to learn will find much
to learn in the Ashram. You should slowly learn it all. Write
to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 311. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
