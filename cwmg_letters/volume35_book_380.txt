380. LETTER TO F. W. STEINTHAL
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
January 27, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I should be delighted to see you on
Tuesday next between three and five in the afternoon.
Monday
you are welcome, but I shall be silent, as it is a day of silence
for me. And rather than that I should miss you altogether, if
you have to go away on Monday night, I would suggest your
coming on Monday. Although I cannot speak to you, you will
be able to say to me what you like.
Yours sincerely,
REV. F. W. STEINTHAL
C/O SALVATION ARMY SOLDIERS' HOME
DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 13051
1 Dated 23-1-1928; the addressee and his wife were missionaries having
spent about 30 years among Bengali students and Santhal villagers and were
leaving India for good. They had expressed a desire to see Gandhiji.
