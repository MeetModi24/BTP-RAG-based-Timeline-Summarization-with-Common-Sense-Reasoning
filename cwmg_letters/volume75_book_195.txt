195. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
November 30, 1941
MY DEAR AMRITLAL,
You should go to the Harijan Sevak Sangh and present the
ease. Don't rush to the Press.
As to my retiring from politics, I might as well wind up non-
violence as retire from politics. It would be like denying myself.
I hope you had my previous letter.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10329. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
