208. LETTER TO MANGALDAS PAKVASA
SEVAGRAM,
October 9, 1944
BHAI MANGALDAS,
Amritlal must declare how he got possession of the papers?
I have had a discussion with Bapa. Hope you are in excellent
health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4688. Courtesy: Mangaldas
Pakvasa
