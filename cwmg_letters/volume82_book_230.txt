230. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
SODEPUR,
December 6, 1945
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I have just been able to reach your letter. A letter to the
Aga Khan is enclosed. Read it. If you approve of it, send it
on to him. If you wish, you may show it to Sir Purushottamdas .
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 4806. Courtesy : Shantikumar
N. Morarjee
