82. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 14, 1940
CHI. SHARMA,
Retain them as long as they allow you. It is not neces-
sary to return them immediately.
It will be good to send Devi Prasad. But ask the school
first.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
between pp. 290 and 291
