401. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
April 27, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
If it is possible the boiling-pot will accompany the messenger.
If not, it will go with me. Ba is not quite well. Are there any
goats in Segaon? If there are, I do not want to bring any with me.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6329. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9795
