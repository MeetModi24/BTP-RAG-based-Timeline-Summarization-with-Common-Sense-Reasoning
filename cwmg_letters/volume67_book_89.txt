89. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
May 7, 1938
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
What is it in the new orientation of the Gandhi Seva Sangh
that has disturbed you? I must own that I am responsible for it.
I should like you to tell me unhesitatingly what has disturbed
you. If I have erred, you know that I shall retrace my steps
as soon as I discover the error.
As to the general deterioration, I agree with you, though we
may differ as to the weak spots.
More when we meet.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1938. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
