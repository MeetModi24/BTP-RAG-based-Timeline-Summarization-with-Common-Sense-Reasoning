112. LETTER TO SHALIGRAM VERMA
May 19, 1935
BHAI SHALIGRAM VERMA,
Whatever I said was wholly without prejudice. An argument
was advanced and I reacted to it. There was no intention to cast
any aspersion on anyone. I had already made it clear that the con-
sultation I had was only informal. I had later on understood that it
would not be proper to exclude the publishers from the office of
the secretary. There is no reason for you to sever connection. I
hope that you will give up the intention of resigning.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8219. Also C.W. 5634
