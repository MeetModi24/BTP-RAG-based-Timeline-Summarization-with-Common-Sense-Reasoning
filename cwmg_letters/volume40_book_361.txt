361. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
May 14, 1929
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter of 10th. I did receive all your letters but
too late to enable me to send any letters to Jeradai. I began
the Sadaquat Ashram address on your wire. I hope you have all
the letters sent there.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5376. Courtesy: Mirabehn
