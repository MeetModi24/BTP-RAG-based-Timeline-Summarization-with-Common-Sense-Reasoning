326. LETTER TO DILKHUSH DIWANJI
DELHI,
October 12, 1947
CHI. DILKHUSH DIWANJI,
I got the khadi woven from the yarn left by Mother. I
shall use it lovingly. Her blessings, I know, are ever with me.
What if her mortal frame is no more?
I have no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
