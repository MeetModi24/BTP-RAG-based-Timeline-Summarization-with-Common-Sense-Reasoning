264. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
September 27, 1947
CHI. NARANDAS,
One may say you are now approaching old age. You
certainly have my blessings. May you serve the people for many
more years. When you are too weak to serve I am not going
to wish you the same. Why should you ever become too weak?
Blessings from
BAPU
