671. LETTER TO NARAYAN SHRIKRISHNA HARKARE
SEVAGRAM,
February 17, 1941
BHAI HARKARE,
It is good you have been discharged. I hope you are well.
All that I did I did as a matter of duty. One does not always get
instantaneous result. The credit for its being so this time goes
to Sarojini Devi.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI NARAYAN SHRIKRISHNA HARKARE
KELI BAGH
NAGPUR CITY
From the Hindi original: W. S. Barlingay Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
