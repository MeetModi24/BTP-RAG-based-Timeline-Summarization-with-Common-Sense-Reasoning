107. LETTER TO TARA N. MASHRUWALA
WARDHA,
November 4, 1935
CHI. TARA,
I like your letter very much. I shall wait for you on the 7th.
I fully agree with your view. I do not want to tempt you in any
way. At present I only want to help you as much as I can in
improving your health. I am not writing separately to Nanabhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TARABEHN
C/O NANABHAI MASHRUWALA
AKOLA, BERAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6697. Also C.W. 4342. Courtesy:
Nanabhai I. Mashruwala
