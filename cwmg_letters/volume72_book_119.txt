119. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
[Before May 25, 1940]
CHI. VIJI,
I have your letter as also the beautiful handkerchiefs. I have
started using them. Do write to me about Father. I hope you are
calm. You must have got my letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7106. Also C.W. 4598. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
