84. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
NEW DELHI,
March 20, 1939
MY DEAR C. R.,
Read this from Satyamurti and do what you think is right.
The conscience clause does appeal to me as right. But my 'right'
may be your 'wrong' and vice versa.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10889. Courtesy: C. R. Narasimhan
