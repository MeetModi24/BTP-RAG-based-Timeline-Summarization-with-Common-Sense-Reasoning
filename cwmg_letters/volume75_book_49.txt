49. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
October 19, 1941
BHAI SATIS BABU,
Will you answer the enclosed? I had forgotten all about it.
I had been content with your statement that you had spent not
a penny of the Association's money except on khadi work.
Blessings from
BAPU
