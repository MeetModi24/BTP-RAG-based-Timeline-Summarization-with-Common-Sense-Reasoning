280. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
August 4, 1926
CHI. RADHAKRISHNA,
I have your letter. I can give only one reply in regard to
the Hindu-Muslim problem. Hindus should bear patiently every
form of suffering. This does not, of course, mean that they
should forsake their dharma. Employ all your spare time in spin-
ning.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
SIKAR
From a microfilm of the Hindi: S.N. 19940
