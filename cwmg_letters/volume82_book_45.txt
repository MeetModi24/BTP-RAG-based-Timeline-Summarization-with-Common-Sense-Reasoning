45. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
POONA,
November 6, 1945
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your postcard. Sushilabehn has explained my view in
her long letter. You must have received it by now. A short
note has been written to Khurshedbehn today. See that too. I
shall be in Bombay on the 19th and 20th, in Sevagram on the
21st and on the 1st December in Calcutta.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi ; G.N. 2488
