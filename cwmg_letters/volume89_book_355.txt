355. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
NEW DELHI,
October 19, 1947
CHI. KAKA,
I made all the arrangements I could on the basis of your
letter. I informed the Sardar and made prompt inquiries. Now
please send this very letter to Raihana. The papers enclosed
pertain to your side.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
