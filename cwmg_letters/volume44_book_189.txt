189. LETTER TO KASHINATH TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 8, 1930
CHI. KASHINATH,
One should not think too much about one's mental condi-
tion. We should keep ourselves absorbed in our appointed work
and be cheerful. When impure thoughts trouble the mind, we
should drive them out with good thoughts and remain peaceful. If
we remain firm in our decisions, we automatically acquire self-
confidence.
Blessings from
BAPU
