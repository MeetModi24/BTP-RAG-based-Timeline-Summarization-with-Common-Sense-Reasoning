370. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
MOTIHARI,
Shravan Sud 5, 1973 [July 24, 1917]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
Your birthday has come and gone. That means your body
has one year less to live. I have built on you hopes much like
Shaikhchalli's2, so that in wishing continuing growth of purity in
you, I serve my interest as well. You are all that I have and all
I desire. It is my prayer to God and my blessing to you that you
realize all your high aspirations, live long and grow healthier, that
you become an ideal for your family, for the country and the world
at large.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5721. Courtesy:
Radhabehn Choudhri
