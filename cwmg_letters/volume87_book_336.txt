336. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
PATNA,
April 26, 1947
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I have your letters. Now I know that Nirmal Babu will be
here in a day [or two]. I will have a clear picture of the
situation there when he comes.
I am afraid arrangements are being made for my going to
Delhi. Perhaps I may have to leave as early as the 30th. Let
me see what happens. I hope now there will be peace in
Noakhali.
I hope you are well. You must preserve your health. What
is the arrangement for water supply in this hot season?
Abha needs must improve her health. She is a very deli-
cate person.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have a telegram and shall therefore leave on the 30th.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9079
