366. LETTER TO DR. GIRDHARILAL BATRA
March 7, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
There is no one here who can be sent to your village in
Punjab. To train workers will be possible after a few months but
I would like you to correspond with Dr. Gopichand who is the
agent in general of the A . I . V . I . A . for the Punjab. He will be
better able to guide you than I.
Yours sincerely,
DR. GIRDHARILAL BATRA
CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
