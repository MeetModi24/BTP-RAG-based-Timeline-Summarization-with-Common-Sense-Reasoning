106. LETTER TO DINSHAW K. MEHTA
CALCUTTA,
November 5, 1946
CHI. DINSHAW,
Today is the seventh day of my restricted diet. I am fine.
The main reason for my going on this diet was the cough and
eruptions. Now it will continue and I may even go on a fast.
The reason is the current massacre in Bihar. Whatever will be,
will be. God is the doer as well as the undoer. There is no
need to rush to me. Serve your mother who is there. Place
your sanatorium on a sound footing. Write to me and ask me
anything you want to.
Gulbai, I hope, is well and so also are the children.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. DINSHAW MEHTA
POONA
