760. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 30, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Just a postcard will have to do for now. I have nothing
to say except that you should pass. There is no letter from
you today.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. COLLEGE
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
