318. LETTER TO RANJIT
May 28, 1939
BHAI RANJIT,
I have your letter. In the West, boys in many schools take
their bath completely naked. Perhaps in your school they are
imitating some of their ways. If that is so, it should not be
considered sinful. The whole question deserves careful thinking.
You should try to ascertain more facts.
Blessings from
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
