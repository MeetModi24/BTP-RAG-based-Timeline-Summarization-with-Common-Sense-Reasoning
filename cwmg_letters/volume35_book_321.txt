321. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
Monday [1927]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Here is the letter from Rajendrababu. I had written to him
that he might, if he liked, withdraw the case. But now it can-
not be done because Baijnathji had already been told that it
would be pursued. I am sorry about this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2881
1 Panchama Putrane Bapuna Ashirvad, (p. 42) also places this along with
