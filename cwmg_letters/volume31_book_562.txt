562. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL G. VORA
Asvina Sud 15, 1982 [October 21, 1926]
BHAI CHIMANLALJI,
I have your letter. Shad-darshan-samuchchayagranth is a discus-
sion of Buddhism, Vedant, etc. The original work is in Sanskrit.
Its Gujarati translation has been published. It may be available
with some bookseller selling Gujarati books. It is a difficult
work, and is purely an intellectual exercise.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRIYUT CHIMANLAL GULABCHAND VORA
SHRIMALI MOHALLA
RATLAM
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6301
