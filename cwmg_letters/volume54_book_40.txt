40. LETTER TO USHAKANTA MUKHOPADHYA
March 10, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your interesting letter which I have read
carefully. There is no difficulty about understanding your letter.
The prescription that you have suggested is being followed in all
the parts of the country. Sanskrit literature has been translated
in the vernaculars and is accessible to even the illiterate masses.
As far as the law is concerned, there is no question of compel-
ling anybody to regard any other as untouchable. The object of
the Bill is to withdraw the sanction of the law to a custom which
cannot be defended by any ordinary rule of morality. The law is
simply meant to give the reformer the scope of bringing the mes-
sage to those who will listen to it.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. USHAKANTA MUKHOPADHYA
12 MUKHERJIPARA LANE
KALIGHAT, CALCUTTA
