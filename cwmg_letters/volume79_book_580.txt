580. LETTER TO MANZAR ALI SOKHTA
BOMBAY,
April 1, 1945
BHAI MANZAR ALI,
What I have been told about women seems right. The
thing is that they should do something or other and they should
not lag behind men in doing any work for the country.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI MANAZR ALI SOKHTA
SEVAKUNJ
GANGAGHAT, UNAO, (U.P.)
