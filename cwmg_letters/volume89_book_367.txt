367. LETTER TO DAMODARDAS KHANDELWAL
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1947
BHAI DAMODARDAS,
I have your postcard. I don't know what work I should
suggest to you. All I can say is that you should take up any
work of service you like. About emoluments, who knows better
than you? Blessings to Chi. Krishna.
Blessings from
BAPU
DAMODARDAS KHANDELWAL
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
