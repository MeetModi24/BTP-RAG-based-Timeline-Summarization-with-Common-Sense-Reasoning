215. LETTER TO NARGIS CAPTAIN
SODEPUR, CALCUTTA,
December 3, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
Just a line to thank you for your note. Of course you
will point out the things you mention, when we meet. I have
to work double speed. But I am so far well. I did read Jh's
words. Love to you two.
BAPU
SMT. NARGISBEHN CAPTAIN
DUNLAVIN LODGE
POONA 5
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
