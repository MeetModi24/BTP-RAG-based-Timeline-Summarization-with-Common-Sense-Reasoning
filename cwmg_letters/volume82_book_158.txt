158. LETTER TO CHANDRASHANKAR SHUKLA
November 21, 1945
CHI. CHANDRASHANKAR,
Just now as I start writing, your letter is placed before me.
Of course I do get news of you.
And when the Sardar is
there what more can one want? I have secured the books you
recommended. I do want to glance through them but what am
I to do? Please send Joad's book when you come across it. Also
Hodge's. I understand about your health but I am glad that
you are able somehow to carry on your work. Reading your
letter for a moment I wondered if it was Mahadev's. You and
a few others have been copying his handwriting but you beat all
the rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHANDRASHANKAR SHUKLA
KRISHNA BHAVAN
BANGDI MATA'S ROAD
RAOPURA, BARODA
