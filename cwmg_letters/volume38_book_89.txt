89. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
November 18, 1928
BHAISHRI MUNSHI,
I got your telegram. I am not interested in expression of
regret. I have received several telegrams from the Punjab and
some friends are coming from there for consultations. Rest as-
sured that I shall do all that is necessary.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 7511. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
