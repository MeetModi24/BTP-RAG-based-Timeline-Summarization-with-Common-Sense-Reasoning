448. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
NEW DELHI,
April 8, 1946
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I think the hostel land, the well and the building should be
handed over. They may use it as they wish. You may retain
Rustom Bhuvan as long as R. stays there. He may vacate it,
if he can. It should not be given to any outsider. I understand
this is what Ashadevi has said. You cannot devote one day in a
week to prepare slivers for the other six days. Whatever the
difficulty, one should do oneself all the processing up to spinning.
Have I not written about it in Harijan?
I shall be here up to the 16th at any rate.
Blessings from
BAPU
