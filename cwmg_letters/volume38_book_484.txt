484. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 26, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is somewhat surprising. I thought you
wanted me not before the beginning of April. All my plans are
therefore being made so as to come to Andhra in April. March
30th and 31st I am bound to give to Kathiawar. My present
plan therefore is to go to Burma about the end of February,
return to Kathiawar and then come to Andhra in the beginning
of April. I hope to give one month to Andhra. Isn't that enough
for you? Isn't that what was agreed upon?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
GUNTUR
From a photostat: S.N. 15308
