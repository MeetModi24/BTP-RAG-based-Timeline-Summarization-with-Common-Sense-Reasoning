266. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
BORSAD,
July 30, 1931
CHI. KUSUM,
I got your letter. How distracted I must be! That postcard
was in reply to your last letter but I did not answer your ques-
tion. I don't remember what I told that gentleman.
I may
have told him, perhaps, that, if he had got any of my letters and
felt that some of them should be published, he could do so. If
you wish to give any letters to him and if you know him, you may
give them. I will go to Ahmedabad tomorrow morning, and will
leave it for Bombay on the 3rd. If you wish to come and see me,
you may do so. I shall be staying in the Vidyapith. If you wish
to come and see me in Bombay, you may do that. Tell Dahi-
behn that I got her letter. She should keep her promise after her
teeth have become clean. Nothing has been decided about my
going to England.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1825
