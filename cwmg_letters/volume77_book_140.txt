140. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI P. DESAI
JUHU,
May 27, 1944
CHI. MAGANBHAI,
I read your letter. Let them acquire it if they want to.
After all does it not belong to them? Remember what the poet
said in the bhajan "It is useless to sorrow over what God
ordains." Who could be the Divine Preceptor of our world?
He also has a Lord over him. But the mantra of worshipping
Him is altogether different. "You may enjoy things only after
renouncing them, etc."
MAGANBHAI DESAI
VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati
Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
