379. LETTER TO SUBODH CHANDRA ROY
October 22, 1947
DEAR PROF. ROY,
I thank you for your gift. I do not know when and where
I shall have time to read it. I shall certainly glance through it.
It is a great pity that you have missed me even though
you tried so often. There is no prospect of my ever going to
Sevagram. For the time being I am in Delhi. How long I shall
be here I do not know.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PROF. SUBODH CHANDRA ROY
TATA INSTITUTE OF SOCIAL SCIENCES
BOMBAY
From a photostat: C. W. 10577
1 Gandhiji gave this name to the addressee. The superscription is in
