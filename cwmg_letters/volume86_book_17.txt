17. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 22, 1946
CHI. AMRIT,
Your letter. Don't worry about work here. It is clear to
me that there was no sting. Something has come from within.
Be patient and let it take its course.
I have written to Valji G. Desai. What I sent you was for
your amusement. Mistakes will occur in the work that is being
done. We must be as accurate as possible when the book is
published.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 4181. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7817
