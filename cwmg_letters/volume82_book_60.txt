60. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
November 7, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
Nowadays you are so calm and so absorbed in various activities
that there should be no need for a letter from me. Hence I have
not worried.
Why do you not make Gajaraj write for you? It is good
that Uncle will be coming in a few days. If God wills it, we
shall all reach there on the 21st.
It is good indeed that you went and saw Vinobaji.
Blessings from
BAPU
