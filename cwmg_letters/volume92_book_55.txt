55. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
ON THE TRAIN,
July 6, 1929
DEAR MOTILALJI,
I have slept over your proposal. But I feel I must not shoulder
the burden. I am sure that Jawahar should preside. Let young
men have their innings. We must stand behind them. There are
a hundred reasons why I must not preside. There are five hun-
dred to show why Jawahar should preside. If you get this in
time and if you approve I would deal with the matter in the
next issue of Young India.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: Motilal Nehru Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
