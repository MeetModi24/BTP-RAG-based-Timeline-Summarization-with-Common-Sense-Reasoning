233. LETTER TO TEJ BAHADUR SAPRU
"DILKHUSHA",
PANCHGANI,
July 16, 1944
DEAR SIR TEJ,
Gandhiji has your letter of the 11 th inst. as also the enclosure. Your statement
had appeared only in part in the Bombay papers. He was, therefore, glad to get the
full text. You must have seen in the Press the statements he has issued including
the authorised texts of the two notes of his talks with Mr. Gelder. 2 For the present,
having thrown a 'bombshell' he is being inundated with 'shell-shocked' letters, protests,
abuse and what not. That was but to be expected. He is eager to have your considered
view.
He was thankful you remembered him. He reciprocates your kind sentiment.
Gandhiji felt very grateful to hear that your son was steadily improving. 3 He hopes
that he will be fully restored soon.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
