424. LETTER TO R. V. MARTIN
August 24, 1933
DEAR COL. MARTIN,
When you sent me to Sassoon Hospital I took with me my
clothes and certain other things, but not my books, spinning-
wheels, etc. Will you kindly hand these to Kakasaheb Kalelkar,
as also books that you might have received whilst I was under
your custody and which were not given to me?
Yours sincerely,
LT. COL. R. V. MARTIN, I.M.S.
SUPERINTENDENT, CENTRAL PRISON, YERAVDA
Government of Bombay, Home Department, I.G.P. File No. 20-XIII, 1933
