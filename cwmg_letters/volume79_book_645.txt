645. LETTER TO G. B. PANT
BOMBAY,
April 14, 1945
BHAI GOVIND BALLABH PANT,
I received your letter today. Dr. Jivraj told me only today.
The doctors are right. The body has to be made fit to undergo
the operation. I intend to leave Bombay on the 20th. Write to
me at Mahabaleshwar.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GOVIND BALLABH PANT
EX-PRIME MINISTER
NAINITAL
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
