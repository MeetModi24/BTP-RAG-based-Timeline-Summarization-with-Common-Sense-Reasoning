104. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 21, 1940
CHI. BRIJKISAN,
I got your letter. I am not planning the sort of satyagraha
that you imagine.
If I undertake a fast, then you can come
although your duty will be to remain engrossed in constructive
work. When, if at all, there will be a fast, I have not the least
idea.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2479
