58. LETTER TO SARLA
August 13, 1937
CHI. SARLA,
I got your letter. You are a wise girl. You have understood
why I don't send for you here. Persevere and complete the train-
ing under Nayee Talim. Become an expert in it. You must be
reading in the newspapers about things here. I have managed
to write this under great pressure of work just now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8769. Also C.W. 1042. Courtesy:
Champabehn R. Mehta
