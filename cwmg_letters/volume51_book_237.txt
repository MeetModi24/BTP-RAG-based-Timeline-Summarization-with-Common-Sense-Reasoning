237. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
September 30, 1932
DEAR MARY,
This is just to send you my love. I had your delicious oran-
ges. The extract you have sent from Bunyan is very good. I am
making steady progress. Every moment I mutter, God is great
and merciful.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 5984. Also C.W. 3312. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
