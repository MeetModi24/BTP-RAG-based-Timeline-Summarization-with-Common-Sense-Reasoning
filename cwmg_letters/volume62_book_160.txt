160. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 25, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
I have now two letters in front of me. Better a postcard than
no letter. I sent you a wire in reply to your first. I expect you
here any day in reply to this.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
JULLUNDER CITY
PUNJAB
From the original: C.W. 3719. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6875
