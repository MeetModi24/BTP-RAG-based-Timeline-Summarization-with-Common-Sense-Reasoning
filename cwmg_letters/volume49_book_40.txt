40. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
January 23, 1932
CHI. MANGALA,
I got your letter written in a beautiful hand. Yes, one can
get a [walking] stick even in England. Do you know by heart
all the chapters of the Gita?
How many does Pushpa know?
Why did she let herself fall ill? Has her nose grown a little?
Tell her that, when it is held high, she should not go and get it
snapped off. Do both of you rise early? Where is Kamala just
now? Does she ever write to you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4072. Also C.W. 36. Courtesy:
Mangalabehn B. Desai
