39. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[September 29, 1927]
CHI. MIRA,
This is merely to tell you I can't dismiss you from my mind.
Every surgeon has soothing ointment after a severe operation.
This is my ointment.
Tell Ramdas I have just got his letter. He must quickly
recover.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5279. Courtesy: Mirabehn
