383. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Silence day, Wardha
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I got your telegram regarding Keshavlal. I had expected to hear
from him today. But there has been no letter. So I take it everything
is all right. See to it that he resumes going to work only after
recovering fully.
Here the air is cool and good. How nice it would be if you were
here!
Blessings from
BAPU
