380. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
July 19, 1935
CHI. MARY,
I have no particular view on the reactions the treatment is
producing. I am quite clear that having gone there, if only to do
justice to the doctors there you should go through the full course.
After all we must trust them to know what they are doing. You
need not hurry. I hope you are otherwise well. The Marys are
here, gaining experience.
We are having gentle rains daily.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6054. Also C.W. 3384. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
