93. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
September 27, 1934
BA,
I have replied to your letter. It is not right to ask you both
to come over to Wardha just now. Let the treatment at the hos-
pital there also be completed. The climate there leaves nothing
to be desired. It is a different thing if Ramdas does not like
the place. You should, therefore, have patience. God will see
that everything ends well. Rajendrababu is leaving tomorrow,
and Sardar too.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Bane Patro, pp. 26-
