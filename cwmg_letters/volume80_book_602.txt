602. LETTER TO MANEKLAL GANDHI
ON THE TRAIN,
June 22, 1945
CHI. MANEKLAL,
You have entered into competition with me. Anybody who
devotes himself to service in a spirit of non-attachment is en-
titled to aspire to live up to 125. May you be so entitled.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANEKLAL
THANA DEVLI
KATHIAWAR
From a copy of the Gujarati : C.W. 892. Courtesy: Maneklal Gandhi
