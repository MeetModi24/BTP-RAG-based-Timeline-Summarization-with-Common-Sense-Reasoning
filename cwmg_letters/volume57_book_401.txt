401. LETTER TO SIR GEORGE SCHUSTER
AS AT PATNA,
April 14, 1934
DEAR SIR GEORGE SCHUSTER,
I thank you for your letter of 6th April just received during
my Harijan tour in Assam.
I am glad to learn that by reason of the re-starting of civil
disobedience no change was made in the Government decision of
1931 about salt. Am I free to advise workers accordingly? Will
you kindly let me know in which areas it was found necessary to
withdraw the concession and how the concession can be restored?
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: C.W. 9707. Courtesy: Andhra Pradesh Government. Also
Harijan, 30-11-1934
