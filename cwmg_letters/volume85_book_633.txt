633. LETTER TO HEMANT KUMAR NILKANTH
NEW DELHI,
October 18, 1946
CHI. HEMANTKUMAR,
Why talk about prejudice? We should not think of it. You
have made much improvement in your Gujarati. Continue to
show such good results.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. HEMANTKUMAR
HARIJAN ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
