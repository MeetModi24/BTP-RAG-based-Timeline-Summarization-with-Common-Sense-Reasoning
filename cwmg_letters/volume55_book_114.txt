114. LETTER TO BALIBEHN M. ADALJA
May 4, 1933
CHI. BALI,
I got your letter. I am writing this only to reassure you that
I cannot forget you. I have no time at all to write more. I
have written a letter to Kumi at Bombay. You are a brave
woman. Always remain so. I do hope you will get all right.
Manu's betrothal is out of the question just now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5056. Courtesy: Surendra Mashru-
wala
