13. LETTER TO C. C. DAS
CAMP MUSSOORIE,
October 18, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your note. I had a very happy time under
your hospitable roof. I do hope that the fatigue of those days
has left no trace upon your health now and I hope too that
domestic peace prevails in the home now. Please ask Mrs. Das
to write to me. She may do so in English or Hindi just as it
pleases her.
Yours sincerely,
MR. C. C. DAS
VILLA MAYA
GORAKHPUR
