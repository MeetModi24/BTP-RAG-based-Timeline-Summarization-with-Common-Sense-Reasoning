345. LETTER TO ABDUL KARIM SHEIKH
POONA,
March 20, 1946
BHAI ABDUL KARIM,
I have your letter. I hear that you are getting help and
that the Bombay [Provincial] Congress Committee has taken over
the task.
The death of both your sons must indeed be a great blow
to you. I too feel sorry. May God grant you peace.
ABDUL KARIM SHEIKH MAULANA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
