20. LETTER TO ABHYANKAR
March 9, 1933
MY DEAR ABHYANKAR,
Here is copy of my further correspondence with Sjt. Chhap-
khane. I suggest your seeking him and fixing up whatever is pos-
sible. He is so definite about what happened during my visit to
Sangli that it is difficult to contradict him without unimpeachable
evidence or to believe that he is guilty of saying what is not true.
Sjt. Joshi will perhaps show my letter to you.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20512
