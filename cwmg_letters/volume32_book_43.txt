43. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
Kartika Sud 11 [November 16, 1926]
BHAI BRIJKRISHNA,
It was only due to lack of time that I could not reply to your
letter earlier. Do come when you feel like coming and go when-
ever you wish. I want to offer you whatever consolation I can.
Devdas came here and went off to Panchgani.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2351
