159. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
June 10, 1938
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Women may well be there, but I must nevertheless have a
man by me. Whether I want support or not is not the question.
I am trying to do without it but how can I say when I may
have to have it?
I shall see about the massage.
Sushilabehn knows every
nerve and tissue of the body. That is why even she does not dare
to give it up too soon. You should learn how to massage by
practising it on Kanu.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4294; also S.G. 64
