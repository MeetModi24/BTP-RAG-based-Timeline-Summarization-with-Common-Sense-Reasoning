591. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
BOMBAY,
June 20, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have an impression that I have not replied to one of
your letters. I hope Kanchan is well and you are calm. I did
come here earlier than I had planned, but I do not hope to
arrive there also earlier. I may perhaps have to go to Simla.
If that does not become necessary, I may arrive there earlier.
Probably I shall know tomorrow. Let me see what happens. I
hope Chimanlal is keeping good health.
It is raining heavily here. It is cool. Let us hope the
streets will not be flooded with water. The rain is not so
heavy as yet.
A letter for Chi. A. D. is enclosed, which also contains the
reply to the postcard written by Prabhakar.
I am not writing to K. today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8447. Also C.W. 5583. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
