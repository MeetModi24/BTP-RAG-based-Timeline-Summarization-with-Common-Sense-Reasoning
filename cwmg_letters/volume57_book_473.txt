473. LETTER TO ROMAIN ROLLAND
RANCHI,
May 3, 1934
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have your two searching letters which Mira has translated
for me. Your letter about your personal affairs has touched me
deeply. Your utter frankness and your endeavour to let me
understand your action as fully as was possible endeared you all
the more.
Your exposition of the Soviet system I appreciate. I shall try
to find time to understand it more fully from Ceresole.
My love to you and yours.
BAPU
From a copy: C.W. 10585. Courtesy: Madeleine Rolland
