60. LETTER TO SHAW DESMOND
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W. 1,
September 29, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your very warm letter. If you could conve-

to see you.
Yours sincerely,
SHAW DESMOND, ESQ .
