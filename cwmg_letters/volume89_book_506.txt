506. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
NEW DELHI,
November 10, 1947
MY DEAR KU.1,
You mean meeting in New Delhi if I am alive till then.
In N. D., I cannot think of any other item than the policy of
the A. I. V. I. A. in view of the existing situation.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
