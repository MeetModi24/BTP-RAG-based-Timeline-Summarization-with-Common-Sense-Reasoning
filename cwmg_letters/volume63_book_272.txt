272. LETTER TO MANGIBAI
SEGAON,
August 13, 1936
CHI. MANGIBAI,
I have just heard of your husband's death. Why should we
grieve over the death of our loved ones. In the long run death
spares no one. This too should be regarded as one of God's
blessings. Now be calm and devote yourself to service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 172
