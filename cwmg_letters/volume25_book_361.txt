361. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI A. MODI
Magsar Vad 5 [December 16, 1924]
BHAI BHAGWANJI,
I have your letter. Good you wrote it. I shall try to follow
what I can from it.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAs GANDHI
BHAI BHAGWANJI ANUPCHAND MODI
RAJKOT
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 3031. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
