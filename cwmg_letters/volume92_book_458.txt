458. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
BORSAD,
July 4, 1931
DEAR ANAND,
If I am to write, for the time being you must be satisfied
with dictated letters.
I have yours of 23rd June. I met Jairamdas yesterday and
he told me that Vidya was worse and that milk was not
agreeing with her. That was quite likely and I had, I think,
in Karachi suggested that she might live on fruit alone for
some time. But the very best thing is for her to put herself
under the treatment of the nature-cure physician of Poona about
whom I talked to Jairamdas and about whom Jairamdas has
already written to you. I do not know him personally but I
have great faith in Dr. Khambatta whom the Poona physician
treated for suspected cancer. You must not lose time over Vidya's
treatment.
Love to you both.
BAPU
SJT. ANAND HINGORANI
C/O "HINDU" OFFICE
HYDERABAD SIND
