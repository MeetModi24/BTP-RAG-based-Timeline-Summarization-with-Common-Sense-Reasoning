228. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
WARDHA,
June 12, 1940
CHI. KAKA,
What you have sent about Hindustani is controversial. I feel
that nothing should be published just now. Go on doing quietly
whatever you wish to do. Have a brief discussion with me on this
matter when you come here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10988
