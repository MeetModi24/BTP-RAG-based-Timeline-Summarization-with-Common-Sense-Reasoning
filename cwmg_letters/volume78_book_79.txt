79. LETTER TO HOOTEN
August 31, 1944
DEAR COL. HOOTEN,
Many thanks for your kind letter. How can I forget you?
I recall our many cordial chats about medical work in villages.
About the part I have played during this terrible world crisis,
my conscience is absolutely clear.
With kind regards,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
COL. HOOTEN
EUROPE HOTEL, GERARDS CROSS
BUCKS, ENGLAND
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
