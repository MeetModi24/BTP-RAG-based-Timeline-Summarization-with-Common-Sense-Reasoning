138. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 18, 1931
BHAI FULCHAND,
I was eagerly waiting for a letter from you. I know your en-
thusiasm. I need not say anything from here whether or not picket-
ing would be proper. It will be enough, so far as I am concerned,
100
if people do not swerve from the path of truth and non-violence
even for the sovereignty of the three worlds.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2865. Courtesy: Shardabehn Shah
