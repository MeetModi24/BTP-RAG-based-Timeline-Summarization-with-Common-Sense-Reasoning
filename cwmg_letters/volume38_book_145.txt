145. LETTER TO J. KRISHNAMURTI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
November 29, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I hope that you
are completely restored. It will certainly give me much pleasure
to see you whenever you can find the time. I am in Wardha
up to the 20th December at least, then I shall be in Calcutta
for about a week and then I hope to find myself in Sabarmati.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. J . KRISHNAMURTI
C/O R. D . MORARJI, ESQ.
VASANT VIHAR, MOUNT PLEASANT ROAD, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 13006
