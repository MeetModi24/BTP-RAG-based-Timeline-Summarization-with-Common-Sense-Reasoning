485. LETTER TO TRANK LANBEAH
March 29, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter.
Please do not think that I undervalue literacy. I do not put
upon it the same emphasis that you do. And my field of opera-
tions is so different on the surface. At bottom we are one. You
cherish human happiness as much as I do.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. TRANK LANBEAH
MINDAN. (P.I.)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
