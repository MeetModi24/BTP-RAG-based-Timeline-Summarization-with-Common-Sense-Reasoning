188. LETTER TO MOHAMMED ABDULLA
October 1, 1944
DEAR SHEIKH SAHEB ,
I got your telegram at Bombay. I could not spare a minute.
I hope your function was a success. Mridulabehn has given me a
full report. She has also given me your book on Kashmir. I
shall read it when I get the time.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
