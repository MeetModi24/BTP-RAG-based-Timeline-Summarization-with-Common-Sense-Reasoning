323. LETTER TO A. K. CHANDA
November 1, 1944
DEAR ARUN KUMAR CHANDA,
I hope you are better. I note your advice about fasting. I am
in God's hands. Why do you think that I have not the Eastern
Provinces in mind?
BAPU
SHRI ARUN KUMAR CHANDA
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
