690. LETTER TO KASAMALI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Sunday, June 13, 1926
BHAISHRI KASAMALI,
Your letter. God is one. I do not understand your diffi-
culty in conceiving Him as formless. That which has a form
cannot be all-pervading, it needs must be the subtlest. It can
therefore be only formless. All must admit the need for a guru
but one may not hastily accept someone as a guru. In this age
to seek a guru is to believe in one, because to acquire a perfect
guide one must perfectly qualify oneself. Moreover if we regard
all faiths as true there is no need for us to relinquish our faith
or make others change theirs, since all can adopt whatever satis-
fies them from all the religions.
