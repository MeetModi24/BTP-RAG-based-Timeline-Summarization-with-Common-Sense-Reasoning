48. LETTER TO ABDUL HUK AND KAIKHUSHROO
[JOHANNESBURG,]
August 5, 1905
MY DEAR ABDUL HUK AND KAIKHUSHROO,
Your letter to hand. I am returning herewith Mr. Rustomji's letter.
I shall write to him. It is possible to interpret the matter concerning
rent the way you do. But you need not worry about it. It will suffice
if you take adequate care to see that the house does not remain vacant.
I have not yet done with Azam Moosa Hussain's power [of attorney]. You
had under-stamped the letter.
Salaams from
M. K. GANDHI
End: 1
MESSRS JALBHAI SORABJI BROS.
110 FIELD STREET
DURBAN
From the Gujarati m Gandhiji's hand: Letter Book (1905): No. 876
