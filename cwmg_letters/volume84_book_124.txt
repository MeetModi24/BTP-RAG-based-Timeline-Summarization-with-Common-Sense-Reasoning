124. LETTER TO PRAMOD MEHTA
SIMLA,
May 5, 1946
CHI. PRAMOD 1 ,
I have your letter. I am not writing a separate letter to
Chi. Champa. You did well in sending more money. It would
be good if you find Ratilal in Ahmedabad. You must see that
he does not starve for want of food.
I hope that all you brothers and sisters will keep good health,
study well and devote yourselves to service. Champa has suffered
so much that her only duty now is to shape you brothers and
sisters into dedicated workers.
May you all be happy there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8763. Also C. W. 1047. Cour-
tesy : Champa R. Mehta
