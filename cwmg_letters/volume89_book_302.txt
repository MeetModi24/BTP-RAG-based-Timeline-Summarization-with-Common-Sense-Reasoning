302. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
October 6, 1947
CHI. VALLABHBHAI,
Maulana Saheb came yesterday evening and was with me
for a short while. He wants the three of us to meet together.
The time for the meeting is to be fixed by you. He wants
one hour any time on Tuesday. Please let me know the time
and inform him also.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 375
