172. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
May 8, 1933
CHI. PREMA,
Need I write anything more to you? You should defy the
whole world and do what you think is for your good. According
to me, it will be easy for you to pursue your good in the Ashram.
However, that alone which seems right to you will be the best
course for you.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10345. Also C.W. 6784. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
