60. LETTER TO ABDUL ALIM
March 11, 1933
MY DEAR ABDUL ALIM,
I was delighted to see your postcard telling me that you had
safely arrived in Calcutta and housed in "The Refuge".
I had a letter from Sir Deva Prasad Sarvadhikari also.
You should not feel homesick. You are a wise young man. You
should therefore cultivate a brotherly spirit with all the helpless
inmates there and by your exemplary conduct endear yourself to
the officials. And if you will only believe that God is our constant
companion, guard and protector, you will not feel destitute or
lonely.
I hope you have got some paper to read.
I am writing to some friends to see you now and then.
Do keep me informed of how you are faring there.
Yours sincerely,
MR. ABDUL ALIM
THE REFUGE
125 Bow BAZAR
CALCUTTA
