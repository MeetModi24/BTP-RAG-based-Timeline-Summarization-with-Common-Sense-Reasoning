181. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Silence Day [December 3, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I get your letters regularly. I am writing to Babuji about
you.
Are the children regular in coming to Bal Mandir? Are they
attentive? Has Bimla fully recovered now?
I think it is time for you to leave for Dwarka.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3338
1 Dhairya means 'patience'.
2 From the reference to Prabhavati's proposed trip to Dwarka; vide also
"Letter to Prabhavati", 9-12-1928.
3 Brij Kishore Prasad
