198. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
May 15, 1946
MY DEAR AMRIT,
Well, I am having a holiday practically. The weather is
not very oppressive. We are in the Bhangi Colony. Of course I
miss you and do not. Only you must keep well. P[yarelal] will give
you what news there is to give. Sudhir had a long chat with me.
He will write to you. Agatha too had.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 4174. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7810
