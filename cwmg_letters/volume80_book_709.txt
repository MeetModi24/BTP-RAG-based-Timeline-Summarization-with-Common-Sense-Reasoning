709. LETTER TO SATYAVATI
SIMLA,
July 11, 1945
CHI. SATYAVATI,
I have your letter. Neither the country nor the body are
saved by taking the name of the country. Uttering the name
of Rama-not mechanically, but from the heart-saves both the
country and the body, if indeed this kind of work has use for
the body. Khurshedbehn and Prabhavati are here.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SATYAVATIDEVI
TUBERCULOSIS HOSPITAL
KINGSWAY [CAMP], DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
