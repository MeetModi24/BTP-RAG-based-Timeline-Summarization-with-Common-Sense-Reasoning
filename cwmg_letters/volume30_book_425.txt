425. LETTER TO ZAFARULMULK
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 24, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your lucid letter. The question you have put is really
difficult to answer. But this is one of those questions which every-
one must answer for himself. So far as the Congress is con-
cerned you have absolute liberty of action. But with those with
whom non-co-operation has almost become a creed, there is no
guidance but that of the inner voice. If you ask me what I
would do myself, I can only say that I could not work in a body
in which the Governor is ex-officio patron and in which the
Chairman and the Secretary are also officials as such.
Yours sincerely,
ZAFARULMULK, ESQ.
LUCKNOW
From a photostat: S.N. 19503
