345. LETTER TO HANUMANPRASAD PODDAR
April 8, 1932
I and 2. We must believe in God if we believe in ourselves. If
living beings have existence God is the sum total of all life and
this in my view is the strongest proof.
3. The denial of God is injurious in the same way as denial of
ourselves. That is to say, to deny God is like committing suicide.
The fact remains that it is one thing to believe in God and
quite another to realize God emotionally and act accordingly.
Truly, no one in the world is an atheist; atheism is merely a pose.
284
4. One can realize God only by ridding oneself totally of attach-
ment, aversion, etc., and in no other way. I hold that one who
claims to have realized God has not truly done so. Realization
can be experienced, but is beyond description. Of this I have
no doubt.
5. I can live only by having faith in God. My definition of God
must always be kept in mind. For me there is no other God than
Truth; Truth is God.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 82
