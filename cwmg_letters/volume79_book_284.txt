284. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
February 26, 1945
CHI. MIRA,
You can have Kisan Ashram. But if that, why not Mazdur
or its equivalent? A kisan can be a millionaire, not so a working
man, a labourer. But I do not mind kisan, if you prefer it.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABEHN
ASHRAM, MULDASPUR
P. O. BAHADRABAD
Via JWALAPUR, NEAR HARDWAR
From a photostat: C.W. 10518. Courtesy: Mirabehn
