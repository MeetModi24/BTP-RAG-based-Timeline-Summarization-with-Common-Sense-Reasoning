311. LETTER TO REVASHANKER JHAVERI
Monday [December 26, 1927]
RESPECTED REVASHANKERBHAI,
From Devdas's letter, received today, I gather that you, too,
have fallen ill and have returned to Bombay. I intend to leave this
place tomorrow and will arrive there on Thursday. If I don't
I shall arrive on Friday without fail. May God protect you.
Pranam from
MOHANDAS
RESPECTED REVASHANKERBHAI
MANIBHUVAN
7, LABURNUM ROAD
GAMDEVI, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1266
