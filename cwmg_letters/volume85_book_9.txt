9. LETTER TO DR. T. J. KEDAR
PANCHGANI,
July 17, 1946
BHAI KEDAR,
I have your letter of the 4th inst. No one dies or lives or
is born without God's grace. Then why rejoice at birth or grieve
over death? I have no interest in elections. If your name is
proposed for the Constituent Assembly, how can you escape it?
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. KEDAR
NAGPUR, C. P.
From a copy of the Hindi Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
