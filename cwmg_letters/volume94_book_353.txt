353. LETTER TO K. G. GOKHALE
[After April 2, 1945]
I had discussed your case with Baba Saheb Kher before your letter
came. He will write to you. As for khadi, can one of your relatives
not spin a little for you ? If no one is available, write to your branch.
Khadi should be made available in a case like this.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 This was in reply to the addressee's letter dated April 2, 1945.
