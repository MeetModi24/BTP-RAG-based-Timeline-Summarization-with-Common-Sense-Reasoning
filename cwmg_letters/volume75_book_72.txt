72. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
October 25, 1941
CHI. BABUDI,
Heaps of blessings to you both—be happy, live long and render
as much service as possible. We should not make comparisons
with other people who, though they do not take care of their health,
seem normal. No comparison is possible. You know from experience
that you would fall ill if you did not take care of your health
as you do. You at any rate should know the laws of health and
observe them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10039. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
