154. LETTER TO PYARELAL CHOPRA
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
You should not give up milk. You can use uncooked green
vegetables if you take them in very small quantities, not more
than 1 oz. per day to start with. You need not give up wheat.
It is possible to take wheat uncooked after soaking it in water
overnight in order to soften it.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. PYARELAL CHOPRA
HEAD CLERK, IMPERIAL BANK OF INDIA, DARBHANGA
From a microfilm: S.N. 13901
