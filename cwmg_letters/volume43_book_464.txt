464. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
June 29, 1930
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your letter. You must have gone to the Ashram by
now. Your first duty is to make your body strong. You have no
excuse now to neglect doing that. Now that you are married
and live with your husband's family, you ought not to fall ill. If
you do, will not the elders in the family lose face in society?
Write to me and tell me about your experiences in your
husband's family. Do not be restrained by a false sense of shame
when writing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9049
