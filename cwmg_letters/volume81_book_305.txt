305. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
August 29, 1945
CHI. KANCHAN,
How are you getting along? Do not lose heart. Make your
body strong.
Your work is different from that of the other women and,
therefore, requires a different approach.
Do you give any time to study?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8264. Also C.W. 6988. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
