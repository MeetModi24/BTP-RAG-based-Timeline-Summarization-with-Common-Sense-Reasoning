137. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI
Silence Day, Kartak Sud 6 [October 31, 1927]
CHI. GANGABEHN JHAVERI,
I got your letter about the girl and her mother. I am sure
Gangabehn will be able to apply the correct remedy for burns.
It is all to the good that one should lose faith in doctors, but the
cause should not be the negligence of one of them. Carefulness is
an independent quality of character. We may, therefore, place
ourselves in the hands of a doctor about whose carefulness we
have no doubt at all and in whom we have faith, and then
trust to God.
You should not be impatient to give up the Presidentship.
It is certain now that I shall go to the Ashram for a day. You
may tell me more then. Consider your position not as a privi-
lege but as a responsibility. One should never pass on responsi-
bility to someone else.
Blessings from
BAPU
