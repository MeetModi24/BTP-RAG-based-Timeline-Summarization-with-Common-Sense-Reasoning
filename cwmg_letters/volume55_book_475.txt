475. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
September 4, 1933
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
I got your letter. I had been waiting for one. Do write to
me occasionally. If you have any problem, let me know about it.
Don't worry about me. I am regaining my strength now. I don't
know how long I shall be out. I intend to go to Bombay on Friday,
and from there hope to go to Wardha after a week. Ba, Mirabehn
and Prabhavati are with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. DUDHIBEHN VALJI DESAI
C/O DAKSHINAMURTI, BHAVNAGAR, KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3134. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
