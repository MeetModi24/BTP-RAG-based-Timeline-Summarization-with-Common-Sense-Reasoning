401. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
WARDHA,
July 23, 1935
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
What is going on in Lahore? Can you form any idea about
it? Who is to blame? Insurance companies seem to have come up
like mushrooms. I don't like them at all. But what can we do? It
is too bad that they should cash in on the name of the Congress.
But what else can we do except look on helplessly?. . .1
. . .2 is a straightforward man. How can we take offence in
a matter like this?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 176
1 & 2 Omissions as in the source
