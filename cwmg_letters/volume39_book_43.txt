43. LETTER TO NANABHAI MASHRUWALA
[February 10, 1929]
BHAISHRI NANABHAI,
I have your letter. As for the summons, I shall wait for
more information. I have sent an alternative name to Sushila.
I have selected Sita. Her friends will like it and, in any case,
it is a sacred name. Sushila complains that there has been no
letter from Tara. At present I am in Sind. I shall be reaching
Delhi on the 17th. I may be there for two days. After that I
may proceed direct to Bezwada.
You have probably learnt that Harilal's son, Rasik, passed
away on the 8th at Delhi. I have thought much on the subject
of death and, therefore, I do not grieve over it. There is some suf-
fering, of course, because of selfishness, but that is only momentary.
Blessings from
BAPU
