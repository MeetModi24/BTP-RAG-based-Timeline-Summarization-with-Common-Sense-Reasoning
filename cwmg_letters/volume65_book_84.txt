84. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
April 12, 1937
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I shall see if I can give something to Manu on your be-
half. If I fail you may send something made of khadi of Punjab
make, nothing expensive.
The envelopes you left are still unexhausted. They will last
till your return. But the lot you have sent will be welcome.
Khan Saheb, his son Wali, Ba, Manu, little Kanu, big Kanu,
Mahadev, Durga Nirmala the bride, Bablo and Balvantsinha
will accompany me. Bal also. He is Kakasaheb's son.
The weather everywhere seems extraordinary, we had a fierce
storm here. The mango crop is all but ruined, and I am
afraid of the seasonal rains holding out.
Your Hindi letters are daily improving. If you continue the
practice, you will acquire good speed when you come.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3775. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6931
