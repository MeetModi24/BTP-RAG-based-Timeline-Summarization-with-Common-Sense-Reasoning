458. LETTER TO PYARELAL
October 24, 1939
CHI. PYARELAL,
I agree that it would have been better for me to have you
rather than Sushila to attend to my massage and bath. But
since you will be busy with the Harijan work on Sundays and
Mondays, Sushila can do these on those days. I must not waste
time over this argument. I shall be happy if you can do this
much cheerfully. I have discussed it with Sushila.
Blessings from
BAPU

and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
