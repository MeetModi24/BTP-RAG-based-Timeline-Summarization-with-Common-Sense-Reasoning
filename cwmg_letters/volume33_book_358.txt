358. LETTER TO GEORGES MIGNON
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 21, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you.
You may publish in your magazine the chapters of my writ-
ings called "My Experiments with Truth". Their rights of publi-
cation in book form in French have already been given to M. Emil
Roniger. You will please therefore confine your translation to its
publication in your magazine. I take it you will kindly send me
copies of your magazine that may contain the translation from
time to time.
Yours sincerely,
M. GEORGES MIGNON
EDITOR OF "EXTREME-ASIE"
SAIGON
From a photostat: S.N. 12505
