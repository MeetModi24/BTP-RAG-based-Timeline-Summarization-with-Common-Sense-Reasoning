156. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
November 6, 1940
MY DEAR ANAND,
Amtul Salaambehn is proceeding to Sind to give her life in
preventing the murders that are taking place in Sind. She is to
go to some Muslim friend and will appeal to Haji Sir Abdulla
Haroon to help her in the attempt. You will help her in every
way. Take her to Kikibehn also and do everything you can for
her.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 681
