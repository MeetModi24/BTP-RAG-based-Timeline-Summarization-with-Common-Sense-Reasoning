139. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA
SEGAON,
May 29, 1938
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
I have your letter. You don't mention how many years you
have completed. How can I say, therefore, how old you have
grown? I got your gift.
Today I am wearing the kuchchha
made from your khadi. There is still room for improvement in
your handwriting, though, of course, you have tried to write with
greater care.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9424
