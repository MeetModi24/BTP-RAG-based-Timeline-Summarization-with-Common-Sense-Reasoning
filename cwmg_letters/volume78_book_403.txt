403. LETTER TO SHRIPAD D. SATAVLEKAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
PANDITJI,
I have your letter. Herewith the reply from Appasaheb.
Please spare me of such responsibilities. I am no longer capable
of taking up such work. My strength is limited and my burden
heavy.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
PANDIT SATAVLEKAR
AUNDH
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
