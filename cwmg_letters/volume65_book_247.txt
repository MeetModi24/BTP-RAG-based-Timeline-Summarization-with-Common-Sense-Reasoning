247. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
May 24, 1937
BAPA,
If Jamnalalji agrees, you may hold the Harijan Sevak Sangh
meeting in Wardha in July.
I got just now the note about the financial condition of the
Sangh. Reading it today is, of course, out of the question. Re-
cently we have had a meeting of nationalist teachers here, and so
letters have piled up. I cannot promise to send you my comments
before the 28th.
Viyogi Hari writes and tells me that the Harijan Sevak
continues to remain short of funds and asks what should
now be done. I had a talk with Ghanshyamdas. He is unable
to decide. Can you? If nobody does anything and the shortage
of funds continues, then you know my opinion—close down.
BAPU
SHRI THAKKAR BAPA
HARIJAN NIVAS
KINGSWAY, DELHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1178
