388. LETTER TO FELLOW PRISONERS
August 15, 1932
For those who find it difficult to concentrate their mind on
their studies, it is a great help to forget the outside world alto-
gether. If a dying man has his heart in the world, he fails to
attain release, he is unhappy himself and is the cause of unhappiness
in others; the same is the case with a prisoner in jail who should
cease to think of the outside world; for imprisonment means civil
death. One who is civilly dead looks mad himself and maddens
others about him if he pokes his nose into the outside world. This
prescription of mine is no new discovery. [John] Bunyan could
not have produced his immortal work and Lokamanya Tilak
Gitarahasya if in prison they had continued to worry about the
outside world.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 363
