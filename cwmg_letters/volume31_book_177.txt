177. LETTER TO K. RAJAGOPALACHARI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 20, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I understand your difficulty. At the pre-
sent moment the Ashram is crowded. But if you can put up
with inconvenience, do please come whenever you like and re-
main as long as you like.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI K. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SECRETARY, CHITTOOR D.C.C.
TIRUPATI
From a photostat: G.N. 5669
