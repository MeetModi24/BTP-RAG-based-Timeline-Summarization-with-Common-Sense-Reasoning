376. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
May 2, 1930
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
I was in fact expecting a letter from you when I got one. If
you keep good health in the Ashram, you should stay either there
or in Vijapur. If you have overcome the fear of death, you
should be equally contented in all circumstances and go on doing
the work which may fall to your lot. I do not feel inclined at
