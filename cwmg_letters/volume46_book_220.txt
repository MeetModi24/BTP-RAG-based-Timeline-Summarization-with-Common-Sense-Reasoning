220. LETTER TO MAHARAJ KUMAR OF VIZIANAGRAM
As AT SABARMATI,
May 21, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. As I expect to deal with the
subject discussed by you in the pages of Young India I need not
send you a detailed reply.
Yours sincerely,
MAHARAJ KUMAR SAHIB OF VIZIANAGRAM
VIZIANAGRAM PALACE, BENARES
From a microfilm: S.N. 17108-a
