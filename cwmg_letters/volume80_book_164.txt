164. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 8, 1945
CHI. SHRIMAN,
Your suggestion is good. How we can get away has to be
considered. I will send for you if necessary.
Madalasa was not right in giving up hip-bath. She can fill
the tub with river water and use it.
Blessings to all of you. A sweet kiss for Rasagulla.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, pp. 305-
