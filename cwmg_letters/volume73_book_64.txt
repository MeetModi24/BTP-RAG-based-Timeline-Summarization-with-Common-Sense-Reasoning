64. LETTER TO DILKHUSH B. DIWANJI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 6, 1940
BHAI DILKHUSH,
I duly got your loving gift. You are progressing day by
day. You have to progress a great deal more still.
I got
Harshadabehn's gift too.
She has given an incomplete address.
Please thank her yourself on my behalf.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DILKHUSH DIWANJI
GANDHI KUTIR
KARADI, via NAVSARI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2647
