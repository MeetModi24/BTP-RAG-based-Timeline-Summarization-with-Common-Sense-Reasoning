101. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
March 15, 1933
CHI. NARANDAS,
Kakasaheb has removed Anandi today to Prof. Trivedi's
house. Since the facilities in the hospital were not very satisfactory
and since both the operation and the post-operation dressing were
quite simple, the doctor himself suggested that she should be re-
moved to some private hospital or house where she could be pro-
perly nursed. The doctor will visit Prof. Trivedi's place every
morning and evening to dress the wound. And there is no dearth
of nurses-women volunteers.
Two or three Parsi ladies who do
Harijan work and a daughter of Lilavati Munshi take turns.
Premlilabehn and Lilabehn, Lakshmidas's sister, are with
Anandi all the time. She gets a little temperature during day-
time, but the doctor says that is common. She drinks fruit juice and
milk and is quite happy and cheerful. There is no cause for worry
at all. But she was saying that she would be happy if Velabehn
was with her. Premlilabehn, therefore, seems to have sent a wire.
If Velabehn cannot resist her desire, she may certainly come. She
may have even started yesterday. Or she will arrive tomorrow
morning with Devdas.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI NARANDAS GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
