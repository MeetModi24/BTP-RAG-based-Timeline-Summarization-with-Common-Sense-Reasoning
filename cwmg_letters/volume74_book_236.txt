236. LETTER TO LAKSHMI BHARATI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
July 7, 1941
DEAR SISTER,
Regard being had to your poor health and other circum-
stances, you should not re-offer civil disobedience.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRIMATI LAKSHMI BHARATI, M.L.A.
C/O SHRI L. K. BHARATI
PASUMALAI
MADURA (S. INDIA)
From the original: C.W. 9269. Courtesy: L. Krishnaswami Bharati
