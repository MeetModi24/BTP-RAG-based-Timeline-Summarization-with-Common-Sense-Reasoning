416. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
JORHAT,
April 17, 1934
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I got your letter. Have you regained full strength? You
have not sent me your comments on my decision. Do send them.
You have done right in deciding to stay on where you are for the
present. Study the problems of khadi and Harijan work.
See
what Ramjibhai and Jivanlal are doing. Give them whatever help
you can.
Also see how the children fare there.
How are
Dudhibehn and Kusum? If you add up all these things, you will
have sufficient work for the present.
I shall be in Bihar up to
the 3rd. I shall reach there on the 24th. As you will under-
stand, I cannot spare more time to write about other things.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5519
