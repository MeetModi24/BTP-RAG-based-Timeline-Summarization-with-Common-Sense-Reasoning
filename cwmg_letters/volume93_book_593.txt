593. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 9, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
You must be getting my letters regularly. Have I written
to you that I have sent A. S. to Sind? The purpose is that
she should stop the murders of the Hindus even if she has to
fast for it. There is a telegram from her today saying that she
has reached Karachi.
The fast has been postponed for the time being. Soundaram
is still here. Pyarelal is doing well. Prithvi Singh is still at
Wardha. He will be here tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Prithvi Singh has arrived to stay here.
Brijkrishna's brother has been ailing for a long time. I
have instructed that he should be examined by you. Examine
him if he wants you to, and treat him if you can diagnose the
case.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
