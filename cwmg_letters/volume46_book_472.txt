472. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
BORSAD,
June 14, 1931
DEAR AMTUL,
Your letter. You must not brood over your weakness or the
fact that you have to receive service from the Ashram. Everyone
knows that you are eager to serve. If you will have patience
God will give you the necessary strength for service. You must
not worry.
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
Let Gangabehn treat you.
From a photostat: G.N. 241
