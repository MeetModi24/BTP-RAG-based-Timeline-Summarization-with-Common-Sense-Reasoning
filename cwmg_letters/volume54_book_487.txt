487. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
April 16, 1933
BOYS AND GIRLS,
You have of late become irregular in writing to me.
You must by now have learnt everything or something about
. . .1. You must know something. Did you learn any lesson from
it?
All the persons living in the Ashram are like blood brothers
and sisters. If anyone feels even slightly lustful they should imme-
diately confess before their elders. They should take all neces-
sary steps to get rid of the passion.
No one should have any secrets. No one should live in
privacy. No one should dress himself up. One should be simple
in body and mind as far as possible. No one should cross the
bounds in any way.
Only limited liberties should be taken
with each other.
I would be glad if you would all write to me your opinions
on these things. There is no harm if I have already written this
before. It is good to have the memory of such things kept fresh.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
