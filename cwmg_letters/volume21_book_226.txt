226. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Tuesday, December 6, 1921
After hearing about what is going on there, I think you should
now be brave. This means that, whenever necessary, you should
speak out, calmly but firmly, and put up opposition. If there is
no bitterness, and no anger under any circumstances, even when
you oppose, you will be listened to by one and all. On the other
side, you should keep your electors well-informed and see that
your constituency becomes strong. If in this way the truth comes
out, the trouble which broke out for a while will calm down.
Those of you who insist that everything should be done in a clean
way should meet together and decide what ought to be done.
You should have no anger in your heart, but only love and com-
passion towards those who appear to be committing a mistake. That
is bound to influence them. This is the path of Rajayoga which I
have shown to you. You are fit for that path. I want you not
to give way to despair.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 41
