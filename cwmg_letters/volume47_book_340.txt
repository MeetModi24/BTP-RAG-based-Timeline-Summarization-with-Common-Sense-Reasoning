340. LETTER TO K. B. BHADRAPUR
AHMEDABAD,
August 16, 1931
DEAR MR. BHADRAPUR,
I thank you for your two letters about the fines imposed in
21 villages by the Panch and the rents in Ras. Please excuse me
for the delay in acknowledging them. I shall be for some time
in Ahmedabad now. I hope to write to you more fully as soon
as I have finished my enquiries.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 17489
