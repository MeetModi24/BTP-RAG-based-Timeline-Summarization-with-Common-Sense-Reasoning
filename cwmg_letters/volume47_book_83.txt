83. LETTER TO H. T. SORLEY
BORSAD,
June 28, 1931
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for your exhaustive letter of 23rd inst. No. 43-
14/301. It will be impossible for me to get hold of the purchaser of the
muchwa and induce him to part with the thing he has got admittedly
so cheap. The sale of the muchwa being after the Settlement, was
clearly in breach of it and was due to the negligence of the official
at Surat. The only proper course in my opinion is to pay the
owner the market price of the muchwa, which, I understand, is
Rs. 200. But I have no evidence in support of my statement
beyond the one made by the owner. There should be no difficulty
in ascertaining the market price. I hope that the course suggested
by me would be adopted without delay. The poor owner naturally
has to suffer loss whilst he remains without a muchwa.
Yours sincerely,
H. T. SORLEY, ESQ., M.M.I.C.S.
COLLECTOR OF SALT REVENUE, BOMBAY
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-C, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
