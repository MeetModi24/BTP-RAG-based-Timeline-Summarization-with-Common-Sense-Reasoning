347. LETTER TO U. GOPALA MENON
December 22, 1932
MY DEAR GOPALA MENON,
Since dictating my letter of the 20th I have read a copy of
the manifesto or notice issued by Sjt. M. K. Acharya. He has
attached to it a copy of the leaflet said to have been issued by
you. Will you please send me your comments on it?
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18780
