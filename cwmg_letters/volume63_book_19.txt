19. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
June 5, 1936
BHAI RAJENDRA PRASAD,
Pass on the enclosed letter to Pierre Ceresole. I saw your
letter. You must have received a copy of Jawaharlal's letter.
This time we shall have to further clarify many things.
I hope you are keeping well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 9875. Courtesy: Rajendra Prasad
