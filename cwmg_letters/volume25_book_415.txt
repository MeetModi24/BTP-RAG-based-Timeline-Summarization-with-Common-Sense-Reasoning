415. LETTER TO KAPIL THAKKAR
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Posh Sud 11, 1981 [January 5, 1925]
BHAISHRI KAPIL
I have your letter. I am sorry I shall not be able to go to
Botad this time as I have no time.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
BHAISHRI KAPIL THAKKAR
BHAVNAGAR
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2896. Courtesy: Kapil Thakkar
