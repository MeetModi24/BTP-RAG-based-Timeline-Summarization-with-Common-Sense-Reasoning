339. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
[1927]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have received your letter. You should certainly not spoil your
health. You must do only as much work as you can do without
straining yourself. Keep sending your diary. I get a lot even from
that.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9360
