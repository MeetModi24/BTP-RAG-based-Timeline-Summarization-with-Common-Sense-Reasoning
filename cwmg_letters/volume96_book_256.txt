256. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
FROM M. K. GANDHI
April 16 [1917]
MY DEAR HENRY,
The enclosed will tell you its own tale. I have drawn up [a]
memorandum of instructions for the workers here. You will get that
also. I may get time to write another letter. I have sent copies to
Shastriar, Andrews and Natrajan. Am sending one to the Ashram.
With love,
Yours,
BHAI
From the original: Gandhi-Polak Correspondence. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
