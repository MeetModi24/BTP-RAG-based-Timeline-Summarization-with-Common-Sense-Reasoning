427. LETTER TO CHANDRASHANKAR SHUKLA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
May 25, 1930
DEAR CHANDRASHANKAR,
The Jail Superintendent has shown me your letter. For the
present I will not ask for any book. The books already here are
lying [unread]. The whole of the spare time is spent in spinning
and carding. Some time is devoted to reading.
I hope your health is all right. If it is not so, improve it.
You must have forgotten the pang [of separation]. You must be
seeing Kaka. Where is Kamalnayan? If he is there, ask him
to write.
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in
India, Vol. III, part III, P. 573
