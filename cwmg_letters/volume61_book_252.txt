252. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
June 18, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
My whole heart goes out to you in your trial. God is merci-
ful. He will give you strength. It was good you did not inform
Father and Mother and had the courage to have the operation
performed at once. Why not take Kichi too to Kodai, if the
doctor will allow it?
I sent you a letter at Bangalore. I hope you will receive it
in due course, if it has not already come to you.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: Ambujammal Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
