173. LETTER TO E. C. GIBSON
RAJKOT,
April 16, 1939
DEAR MR. GIBSON,
As I know that you were in possession of my letter of yes-
terday to the Thakore Saheb, I gave myself time to write to you
informing you of my having sent the letter. It has been a most
anxious and taxing time for me. I have been experiencing difficulties
I had little thought of. But of this when the proper time comes.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: C.W. 10177. Courtesy: D. B. Kalelkar
