453. LETTER TO RATANLAL MALAVIYA
BORSAD,
June 19, 1931
DEAR RATANLAL,
I have your letter. I saw the Government notice. It is no
doubt improper. If the students have courage and the spirit of
sacrifice they should not attend college till the flag goes up again.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI RATANLAL MALAVIYA
LAW STUDENT
NEW HOSTEL
COLONELGANJ
ALLAHABAD - U. P.
From the Hindi original. Courtesy : Gandhi National Museum and
Library
