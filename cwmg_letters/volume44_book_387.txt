387. LETTER TO JAMNA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 3, 1930
CHI. JAMNA,
You did very well in going to Bombay. If you keep good
health there, don't be in a hurry to return. My blessings to
Kalyandas, Dharamdas, Premkunvar and other men and women
whom I met but whose names I forget.
What occupations do
Kalyandas and Dharamdas follow at present?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 545. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
