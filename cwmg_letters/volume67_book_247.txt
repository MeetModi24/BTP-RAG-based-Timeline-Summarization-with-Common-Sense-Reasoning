247. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 19, 1938
CHI. KANTI,
I hope you reached there safely and are not worrying. It
was very good indeed that you came. There was a long and fine
letter from Saraswati.
I knew only this time that she could
write. She seems to feel quite at home with me. She feels the
separation from you a good deal but is trying to bear it. Sushila
and Arun have arrived. Do you not write to Prabhavati?
Do write. She is in Patna.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7341. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
