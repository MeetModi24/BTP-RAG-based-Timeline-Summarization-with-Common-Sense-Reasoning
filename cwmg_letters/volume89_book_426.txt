426. LETTER TO RAJA OF WANKANER
NEW DELHI,
October 29, 1947
YOUR HIGHNESS RAJA SAHEB,
Chi. Chhaganlal Joshi of the Harijan Sevak Sangh has
forwarded to me a draft for Rs. 2,000 instead of the usual
Rs. 1,000 for Harijan welfare, and has mentioned your indifferent
health. May God grant you a long life and inspire you to serve
your subjects.
I have returned the cheque to Joshi to encash it for service
of Kathiawar Harijans.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
