13. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
April 6, 1942
CHI. VANUDI,
I got your slip. After you get well you will be coming
here for a few days to appear in the examination, won't you? If
Mahadevbhai has arrived, tell him that I have not sent his two
short articles. I will explain the reasons when he comes here, if he
wants me to do so and if I still remember the matter.
Today everybody is well. What it would be like tomorrow,
God alone knows. The weather is curious. It rains, too, occasion-
ally.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5790. Also C.W. 3013. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
