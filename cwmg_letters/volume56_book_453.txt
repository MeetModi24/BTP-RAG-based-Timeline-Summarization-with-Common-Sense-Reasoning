453. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
TUMKUR,
January 4, 1934
CHI. MARY,
No letter from you for a long time. Duncan got on very well.
He was with us all the way and I saw him just for a second even
in Adyar, for I happened to visit Adyar at the instance of Mrs.
Wood. Do tell me how you are getting on. I hope Chandra is
flourishing, and causes you no bother.
I am sending this to
your village address though you should now be in Wardha. I
hope your eyes are behaving better.
Love.
BAPU
SHRIMATI MARY BARR
C/O SHETH DEEPCHANDJI
BETUL, C.P.
