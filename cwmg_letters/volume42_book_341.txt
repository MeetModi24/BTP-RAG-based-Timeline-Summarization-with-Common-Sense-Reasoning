341. LETTER TO RAMNIKLAL MODI
LAHORE,
December 30, 1929
CHI. RAMNIKLAL,
Though it is my Silence Day, Jawaharlal has dragged me to
the Subjects Committee. Sitting in the meeting, I am writing
letters. There is nothing special to write about to you. But where-
ver I happen to be on the Silence Day, I remember you all.
That is why I write this.
I think we shall be able to start from here on the 3rd. A
situation has arisen which does not permit me to go away.
It is very cold. Everyone feels it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Everyone must have been introduced to Dr. Mehta. He must
have been carefully looked after.
