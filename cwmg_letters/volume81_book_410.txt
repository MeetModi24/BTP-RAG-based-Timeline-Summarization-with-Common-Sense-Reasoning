410. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
September 8, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I got your letter. There is no need for you to write any-
thing to Ramnarayanji.
I advise you to go on serving silently, speaking only when
you must.
Kanchan's problem is a difficult one. It can be made easy
if you come to a firm decision and act upon it.
I understand about Hiramani. You should write nothing to
Ambegaonkar. Let him go to court. If he comes to me, I will see.
Forget about Vina.
Sushilabehn is going there and will do what is necessary
about the hospital.
I am very hard pressed for time and, therefore, content myself
with this short letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8435. Also C.W. 5596. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
