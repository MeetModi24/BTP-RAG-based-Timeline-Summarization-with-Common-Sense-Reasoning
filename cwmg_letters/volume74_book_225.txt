225. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
July 4, 1941
BHAI MUNSHI,
I had written to the Rao Bahadur even before he got your letter.
I felt your reply to Dr. Khare was lacking in spirit. How
dare he compare himself with you? There is a lot of difference
between you two.
He violated all the principles of the Con-
gress, whereas you observed them in an ideal way and left the
Congress in obedience to them. Nothing that anybody says or
wishes can create a rift between us two.
I hope you are all fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7664. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
