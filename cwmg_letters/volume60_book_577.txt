577. LETTER TO VITHAL L. PHADKE
April 12, 1935
CHI. MAMA,
I have written to Parikshitlal, but have received no reply so
far. I am sure that the leaf-plates made by you must be excellent.
Show me a specimen some time. About bhaji, what you say is
correct.
You certainly need not select a village in a hurry, but you
should not delay through lethargy, either.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3830
