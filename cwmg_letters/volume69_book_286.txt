286. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA
RAJKOT,
May 18, 1939
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
I got your loving gift quite a few days ago, but have not
been able to write till now. May you live long and prove yourself
a good woman. I wear the kachha sent by you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9425
