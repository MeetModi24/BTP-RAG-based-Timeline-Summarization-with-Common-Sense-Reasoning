6. LETTER TO BANARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
Kartika Sud 3, 1983 [November 8, 1926]
BHAI BANARASIDASJI,
I have your letter. I like the idea of your going to S. A. but
the purpose of it does not seem very justifiable to me. If you
want to earn your livelihood by going there and writing for the
newspapers, the purpose will not be served by your going to
South Africa. It will be better if you write a book or take up a
job in order to earn money.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2574
