414. LETTER TO K. SANTANAM
February 2, 1931
MY DEAR SANTANAM,
I have just heard from Rajaji the awful news of your wife's
death. But I know that you are god-fearing and brave-for
those who believe in Him nothing is awful. He gives and has
the right to take away. If you are permitted let me have a line
that you are at peace with yourself.
Yours,
BAPu
SJT. K. SANTANAM
B. BLOCK
CENTRAL PRISON
VELLORE
S. INDIA
From the original: Courtesy: K. S. Ramanujam
