436. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
April 20, 1934
MY DEAR PREMI,
Your letter.
The provisional date fixed for Hyderabad is
7th July. Only 3 days have been allowed for Sind. There is
ample time for you to prepare.
We are a party of 12 to 16.
By that time Father should be out. I do hope Dr. Choithram is
better. Give my love to Father and Dr. Choithram.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 9246. Courtesy: Jairamdas Doulatram
