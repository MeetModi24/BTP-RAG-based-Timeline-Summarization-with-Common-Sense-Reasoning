128. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 3, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is nothing from you today. I forgot to write yesterday
that my weight had increased by 1 lb. It was 103 lbs. The blood-
pressure is fully under control. Ba does not heed the advice that she
should take rest. She is a difficult person. Balkrishna arrived yesterday.
His room looks quite good with the extension of the verandah. Travelling
has not harmed him in any way.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
