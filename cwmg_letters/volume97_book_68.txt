68. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
[Before November 18, 1926]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
It will not do if you remain lenient. To be lenient with Ratilal is to be
cruel to him. To be strict with him is kindness. What would have happened
if the doctor had made an incision on my stomach lightly? Or, how effective
would be a person who pierces the earlobes if he pricked the needle lightly.
Even with Ramachandra, be strict as the situation demands and be frank.

LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI

After you have shown your generosity to the full, he should realize what his
shortcomings are.
Remember that the article I have sent about the lift, has to be sent straight
to Navajivan if it is all right. If you want to give it in the coming issue of
Young India, get it translated into English by Valji or send a copy of it here
in time.
Blessings from
BAPU
