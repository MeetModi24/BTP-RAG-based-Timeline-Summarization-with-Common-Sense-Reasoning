51. LETTER TO V. A. SUNDARAM
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 23, 1926
MY DEAR SUNDARAM,
It was sweet of you to have sent me those Tamil hymns so
as to reach me on my silence day and the date; it was quite easy
reading for me and your translation was a great help. It is like
paraphrase of verses from the Bhagavad Gita or from the Bible.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3192
