252. LETTER TO EMILY HOBHOUSE
SABARMATI,
July 27, 1924
DEAR MISS HOBHOUSE
Friends had preserved your letter of 8th December, '22. It
was like meeting you to receive that letter. I never met Miss
Adams.
I was quite happy in the Prison. I had as many books as I
wanted so long as they were non-political.
I wonder if you ever read Young India. It would be a privi-
lege to send it to you, if you would care to read it. How are
you keeping body?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
MISS EMILY HOBHOUSE
THE WARREN HOUSE ST. INES
CORNWALL
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
