370. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
July 15, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
Forget Harilal completely now. I have almost forgotten
him.
Enclosed is a lesson for Lilavati.
Vajubhai has sent one more letter as a reminder. I shall send
it to you, if necessary, after I get your reply.
Blessings from
BAPU
PS.
What is your advice regarding Dhiru's request. I suppose you
know that he wants to go to Santiniketan.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8464. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
