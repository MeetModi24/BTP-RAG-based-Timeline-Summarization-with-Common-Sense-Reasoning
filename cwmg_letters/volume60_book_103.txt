103. LETTER TO TANGAI MENON
January 17, 1935
MY DEAR TANGAI,
You are a very brave girl cheerfully going through the ill-
ness you get from time to time. You must ask Daddy this
question: 'Why do I get these sores when you are yourself a
doctor? You should find a way of helping me.' I hope you are
much better now.
Love and kisses.
BAPU
My Dear Child, p. 121
