348. LETTER TO DR. M. A. ANSARI
[Before March 31, 1934]
My emphatic opinion is that the paralysis of the intelligentsia
must be removed.
However much, therefore, I may differ as to
the Council-entry programme, I would welcome a party of Con-
gressmen pursuing that programme rather than [that they] should
be made sullen, discontented and utterly inactive.
I still retain
the view that the Congress cannot, without committing suicide,
give up the Poona resolution but, if the majority of the Con-
gressmen do not feel like carrying on this, I would welcome a meet-
ing of the A.I.C.C. at which it should express its opinion and
withdraw the Poona resolution.
I am sure that the country
would agree with their courageous behaviour.
The Hindustan Times, 5-4-1934
