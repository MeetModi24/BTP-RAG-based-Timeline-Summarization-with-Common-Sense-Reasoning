437. LETTER TO KANAM GANDHI
[January 10, 1947]
CHI. KANU,
I have your nice letter. I am keen on writing to you but I
must restrain myself. It will soon be 4 o'clock and I must
brush my teeth and wash. Get rid of the cold. Did you not
learn that from Vinoba? Learn to do pranayama. Learn a few
yogic exercises also and find out the right diet for you. The
result of your examination will surely be in your favour as you
are so energetic and also growing in wisdom. May God grant
you long life.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
