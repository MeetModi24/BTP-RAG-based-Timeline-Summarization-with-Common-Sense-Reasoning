243. LETTER TO S. V. VENKATANARASAYYAN
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 1, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I do not think that it is necessary to forget
one's own faith in order to be tolerant towards the rest. In fact,
tolerance loses its value when one's own faith is forgotten. In my
opinion, tolerance requires the same respect to be paid to another's
faith as we claim for our own.
I am of opinion that God can be reached without a mediator.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. V. VENKATANARASAYYAN
7, MILLER ROAD
KILPAUK, MADRAS
From a photostat: S.N. 19407
