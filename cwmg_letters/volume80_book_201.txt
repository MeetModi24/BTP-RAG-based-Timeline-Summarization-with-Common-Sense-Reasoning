201. LETTER TO VINAYAKRAO KORATKAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 12, 1945
BHAI VINAYAKRAOJI,
I have your letter. I am pained to hear about all that has
happened. I am proceeding after my own style. Two activities
cannot be carried on at one and the same time.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI VINAYAK K. KORATKAR, BAR-AT-LAW
HYDERABAD (DECCAN)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
