390. LETTER TO P. SUBBAROYAN
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
DEAR SUBBAROYAN,
I am listening to every argument advanced by friends like
you but the final arbiter will be the inner voice.
I do hope Rajaji won't stir out unless he is completely re-
stored.
I shall be glad if Radhabai breaks her journey.
Please warn her however that I am reaching the end of my
bodily resources.
Yours,
BAPU
DR. SUBBAROYAN
MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
