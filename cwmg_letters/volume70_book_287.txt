287. LETTER TO MAHOMED YUNUS
[October 14, 1939]
DEAR FRIEND,
You will never tire me.
Only I have no powers of an
autocrat, whatever may be said to the contrary. The question
is no one man's job. I am forwarding your letter to Maulana
A. K. Azad.
From a copy: Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1939. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
