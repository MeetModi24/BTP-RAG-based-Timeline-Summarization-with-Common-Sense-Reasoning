109. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 11, 1926
Here is another cable from South Africa. I wonder if you or
Sorabji sent any reply to Hajee's first cable referred to in the
accompanying. If nothing was sent do please send a satisfactory
reply now.
The reply I have sent to the enclosed cable is as follows:
"Sent opinion Delhi Committee week ago."
Yours,
Encl. 1
From a photostat: S.N. 19354
