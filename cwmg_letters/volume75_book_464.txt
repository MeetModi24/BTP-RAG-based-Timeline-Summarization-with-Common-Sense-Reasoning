464. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
March 6, 1942
CHI. PRABHA,
I had your letter. Everybody is well here. Ba is fairly well.
A letter from Jayaprakash is enclosed. I am also sending a
copy of my reply1, so that if my letter gets lost on the way he
may get the copy sent to you. And if you have anything to
ask, you may also be able to write immediately.
Mahadevbhai also is better. Janakibehn has come to stay
here. Kamala and her children also have come with her.
I did not invite you for the meeting in connection with
Jamnalalji because I did not think it proper to do so. I did
not mean anything more by "courage".
Does not dharma-
consciousness make a man timid? My feelings would always be
in favour of inviting you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Khurshedbehn and Lohia have arrived for talks.
The
Working Committee may perhaps meet here on the 17th.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3572
