605. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
ON THE TRAIN,
June 22, 1945
CHI. SATIS CHANDRA,
I read your postcard of June 16 only today. I should be
sorry if your heart is still weak. But if that be the case, do
not insist on working. [You] have not said about the letter to
the apiaries. Now you have got to write to them. I am going
to Simla. God alone knows what He will make me do.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
SODEPUR, via CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1644
