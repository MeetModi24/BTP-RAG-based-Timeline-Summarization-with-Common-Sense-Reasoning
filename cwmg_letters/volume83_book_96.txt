96. LETTER TO JATINDAS M. AMIN
SEVAGRAM,
February 7, 1946
CHI. AMIN JATIN,
I would only say this much that you should stay where you
are and render such service as you can. "Action alone is thy
province, never the fruits thereof."
The best way of undoing injustice is to persuade ourselves
that no one can [really] do an injustice.
Blessings from
BAPU
JATINDAS M. AMIN
VITHTHAL KANYA VIDYALAYA
NADIAD
