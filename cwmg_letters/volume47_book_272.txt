272. LETTER TO H. P. MODY
AHMEDABAD,
August 1, 1931
DEAR MR. MODY,
I thank you for your prompt reply to mine of 28th ult.
I shall fall back upon you for any further information that I
may need.
Yours sincerely,
H. P. MODY, ESQ.
MILL-OWNERS' ASSOCIATION
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 17417
