197. LETTER TO UDIT MISHRA
WARDHA,
December 7, 1935
BHAI UDIT MISHRA,
I have your letter about Sharma. I am not satisfied. In
your earlier letter you had praised Sharma. It now becomes
your duty to give me a correct picture of what Sharma said. Please
let me have it. I have not received Brijmohanji's letter.
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
