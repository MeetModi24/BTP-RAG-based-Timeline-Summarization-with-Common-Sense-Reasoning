487. LETTER TO SAVAL L. IDLANI
SODEPUR,
January 5, 1946
BHAI IDLANI,
How is it that your letter to me is neither in Urdu nor in
Hindi?
You should refer to the Congress President the question you
ask me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SAVAL L. IDLANI, JOURNALIST
LALCHAND NANMAL BUILDING
JAMSHED ROAD
KARACHI
