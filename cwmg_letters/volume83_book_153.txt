153. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
February 16, 1946
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
You cannot detain me there till April 3. I have already
written to you that you may keep me there for 15 days at the
most. I must see through what I have started. I have even
accepted engagements outside Bardoli after the 19th. You can
take all the work you want from me during my fifteen days
in Bardoli. I have discussed everything with Bhai Kher. Of
that more when we meet. I am quite busy at present.
I am sorry to hear about Bhulabhai's illness. I should
like to go and see him before you take me home from the rail-
way station. My silence will not come in the way. I think
Mathuradas will be able to come and see me at Birla Bhavan.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- : Sardar Vallabhbhaine, pp. 295-
