137. LETTER TO S. D. SATAVLEKAR
July 1, 1932
BHAI SHRI SATAVLEKARJI,
Perhaps you know that Sardar Vallabhbhai and Mahadev
are here with me. Sardar has a desire to learn Sanskrit, Maha-
dev will guide him. Please send a set of your Sanskrit Readers
(Parts 1-24). I hope you are all right. We three are doing well.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SHRI SHRIPAD DAMODAR SATAVLEKAR
SWADHYAYA MANDAL
AUNDH, DIST. SATARA
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 4762. Courtesy: S. D. Satavlekar
