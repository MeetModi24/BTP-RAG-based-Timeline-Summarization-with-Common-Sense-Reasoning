526. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
[Before July 28, 1937]
CHI. KANTI,
Read this letter and preserve it. I have accepted Devdas's
demand. If now any additional expenses are incurred for you,
I will see about them. Send the account also to me. Write a
pleasant letter to Devdas. He must be relieved of worry. I
have not decided where I will get the money from.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 10230. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
