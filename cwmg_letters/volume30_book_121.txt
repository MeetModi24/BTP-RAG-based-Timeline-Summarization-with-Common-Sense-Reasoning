121. LETTER TO ANANDAPRIYA
SABARMATI,
March 13, 1926
BHAI ANANDAPRIYAJI,
I have your letter. I have looked at the handbill; certainly
it is obscene. But we had better take no notice of it. Such matters
acquire some importance only when we give them any attention.
And many people indulge in this kind of activity just to come into
the limelight. Later, if necessary, I may explain a point or two.
KARELIBAG
BARODA
From a microfilm of the Hindi: S.N. 19861
