79. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
March 12, 1933
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
You are very well occupied. I hope you will not give up this
work. Do not lose faith. Mix with the Harijan families. When you
have won their love, teach them not to eat left-overs. Learn from
them the names and addresses of the families where they work, so
that you can explain to those families also not to give them left-
overs. Keep on writing to me about your experiences. You must
have seen that I have made use of your first letter in the English
Harijan.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 353. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
