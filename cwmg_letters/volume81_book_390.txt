390. LETTER TO PRABODH RANJAN GHOSH
September 3, 1945
BHAI PRABODH RANJAN,
I feel sorry for you. If you don't have money, ask Dr. Roy.
Only he can help.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI PRABODH RANJAN GHOSH
P. O. MADANIPUR
DISTRICT FARIDPUR (BENGAL)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
