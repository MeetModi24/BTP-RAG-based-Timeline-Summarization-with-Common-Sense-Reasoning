299. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
WARDHA,
Silence Day, December 16, 1929
SISTERS,
Having written to you a sufficiently long letter last time, I
wish to be brief today. I have many other letters to write, and
the time at my disposal is up. I may write about all manner
of things, but you may accept from my letters only that which
you can digest. You can leave the rest. Strive hard to act upon
what you understand and accept.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3713
