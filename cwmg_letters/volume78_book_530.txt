530. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM,
December 3, 1944
CHI. MIRA,
So you have the land of your choice. May all your dreams
be fulfilled. If I go to Delhi and get the time of course I should
love to drive to your place.
Do not be anxious about me. All I need is rest from the
routine work, even love-letters. So till the end of the year this is
my last letter. This is the final day of writing letters.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6501. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9896
