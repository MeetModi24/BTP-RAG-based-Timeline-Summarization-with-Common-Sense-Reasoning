334. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 5, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your note. I sent you the wire at once. I am quite sure he
must not speak for some time. His speech will be all the better for
the restraint. You will make out all the reasons.
You were right also in vetoing his going to Hardwar. He must
have uninterrupted stay there whatever it is to be. And you can
truthfully say that he can't have equal quiet, equal facility and
equally good and clean arrangement for food. May all these con-
veniences result in permanent benefit to his body and mind.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
What you say about Sailen is correct.
