651. LETTER TO PARACHURE SHASTRI
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
April 15, 1945
SHASTRIJI,
You have fallen ill! It is not good if it is from worry. But if
it is death calling, there is no harm. "You must go with a smile
on your lips." And that too from a Lepers' House . Whatever
it may be, remain calm and sing Tukaram's abhangas or Vedic
mantras.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 10668. Also Pyarelal Papers. Cour-
tesy : Pyarelal
