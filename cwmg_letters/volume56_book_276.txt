276. LETTER TO KOTWAL
November 20, 1933
BHAI KOTWAL,
The work which has come to us unsought is swadharma.
Anybody who is wedded to an institution should not, as a general
rule, join the present battle. One's dharma cannot be pointed
out to one by somebody else. He who has seen it adheres to it
despite the opposition of the whole world.
We should not sit in judgment over anybody. Every case
should ultimately be judged on its own merits. It is therefore
dangerous to be guided by solutions of hypothetical cases.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3604
