417. LETTER TO R. V. MARTIN
August 19, 1933
DEAR COL. MARTIN,
For the last three or four days you have been sending me
mutilated copies of The Times of India. I have surmised that the
mutilations are due to references to my fast. If my surmise is
correct, I venture to think the mutilation is not fair to me. If
anything has been said by or on behalf of Government, I should
at least know what it is, so that I could make my submission to the
Government if there was anything that I might consider unfair in
the statements appearing in the Press. In fairness to all concerned,
I would suggest that the full text of the correspondence beginning
from my letter of the 29th September 1932 addressed to Major
Bhandari, with reference to my request for doing Harijan work,
should be published, if it has not already been done.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
