135. LETTER TO GIRIRAJ KISHORE BHATNAGAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 6, 1945
CHI. GIRIRAJ,
It is good I got your postcard. It is not a matter for worry
that you are not able to meet me. It is enough for me that you
are doing the work which is dear to me. Advance your knowledge of
Hindi and Urdu so that they become one. It does not matter if it
takes time. You did well to send me news of Hari and Vimala.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 8773
