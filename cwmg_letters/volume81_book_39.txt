39. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
July 23, 1945
BHAI RAJENDRA BABU,
I have sent a telegram about Mahendra. I am enclosing
herewith the Viceroy's letter and my reply to him. Mahendra
will have gone, but what now? The full details of the case should
come out.
I hope the fever has gone.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
