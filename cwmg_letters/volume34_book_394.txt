394. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
GUDIATHAM,
September 1, 1927
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I enclose herewith Jamini Babu's letter which please destroy
after perusal. It gave me great joy to find all the good coming out
of Jamini Babu. I know you will handle him in the gentlest man-
ner possible.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1575
