294. LETTER TO DR. H. S. DEVA
BETTIAH,
CHAMPARAN,
April 29 [1917]
DEAR DR. DEVA,
Your Geography is at fault. This is not Assam. This is
North Bihar. This is the land of Janak to which Vishwamitra
took those two youngsters, Rama and Lakshman. Nature's bounty
has been lavished as unstintingly as man has abused his power.
The position is so bad that I dare not leave here even for a day. I
have cancelled all appointments. I cannot be with you in Nasik,
much as I should like to be.
We are taking the statements of men who come to us as fast
as we can. In a few days, I may have something to report. I have
kept Mr. Shastriar supplied with all the information to date.
Although I am not in gaol, I shall still look up to you to
finish the programme about the translation of Mr. Gokhale's
speeches.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5797
