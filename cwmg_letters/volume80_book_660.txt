660. LETTER TO MAHESH DUTT MISHRA
SIMLA,
July 1, 1945
CHI. MAHESH,
I have your letter. Now I am stuck here. Maybe, I shall
not be able to leave before the 15th of July. Hope you are
well. Meet me somewhere on the way if you can, otherwise at
Sevagram.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MAHESH DUTT
HARDA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
