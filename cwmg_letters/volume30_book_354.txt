354. LETTER TO DHAN GOPAL MUKERJEE
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 16, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I wrote to you at the address that was
given by you. You are right in surmising that I do not encourage
your coming specially for the purpose of gathering materials for
writing the proposed biography.
I am writing to the Manager, Young India, to give him your
instructions. Thank you. I am quite well. And the rest from
constant travelling that I am giving myself is doing me a great
deal of good.
Yours sincerely,
DHAN G. MUKERJEE
1904, TIMES BUILDING
TIMES SQUARE
NEW YORK CITY
From a photostat: S.N. 12465
