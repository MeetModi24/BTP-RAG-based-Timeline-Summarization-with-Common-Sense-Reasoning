286. LETTER TO RUKMINIDEVI BAZAJ
July 27, 1932
CHI. RUKMINI 2 ,
I got a letter from you after many days. I did not know that
you were having fever. Naturally in that condition you cannot
write. And if you cannot write, why should Benarsi write? It
seems that you have lost the strength you had gained at Deolali.
You should regain it. I hope the child is thriving. It was a good
thing that all of you went to see Devdas.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
SHRIMATI RUKMINI
C/O SJT. BENARSIDAS
PANCH GANGA, BENARES CITY, U.P.
From Gujarati: C.W. 9451. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
