25. LETTER TO JETHALAL L. GANDHI
MADRAS,
January 24, 194[6]
CHI. KAKU,
I have your very long letter. We should be charitable.
We would go crazy looking for other people's faults, and we
would never see our own. Hence remain silent and render such
service as you can.
I should consider it a matter of shame that you should rush
about in the car to do my work. All the same I am trying to
be vigilant.
I shall not be able to look up Hemchandbhai and the
labourers. Sorry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
