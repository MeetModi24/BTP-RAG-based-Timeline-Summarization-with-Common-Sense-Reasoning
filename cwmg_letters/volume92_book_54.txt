54. LETTER TO C. SATYANARAYAN
KASHIPUR,
July 4, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You may not interfere with your sister.
It is no business of yours to keep watch over her. God alone
can guide her course, if she will let Him. Your business is to
keep a strict watch over yourself. If your conduct is correct, it
will react on your surroundings.
Yours,
C. SATYANARAYAN
C/O G. VENKAT RAo
NARSAPUR
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/68
