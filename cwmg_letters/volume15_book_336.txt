336. LETTER TO E. W. FRITCHLEY
LABURNUM ROAD,
BOMBAY,
June 13, 1919
DEAR MR. FRITCHLEY3,
I enclose herewith a portion of Young India containing a
reference to Babu Kalinath Roy's case and copy of a general letter
addressed by me in the matter. I wish I could interest you in
this case. I think you will agree with me that this is a case of
manifest injustice.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 6650
