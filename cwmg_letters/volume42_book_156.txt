156. LETTER TO REGINALD REYNOLDS
SHAHJAHANPUR,
November 11, 1929
MY DEAR REYNOLDS,
I do think of you on my silence day. You are charitable
about your being debarred from temple-entry and it is right for
us all to be so towards one another. But the hideous truth is that
this bar is a variety of the curse of untouchability which in its worst
form we are making a mighty effort to remove.
Pray do not be greedy about doing many things at once.
I would have you do some things at least well. We shall not be
long meeting now. If all goes as expected I reach there the night
of 25th.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
