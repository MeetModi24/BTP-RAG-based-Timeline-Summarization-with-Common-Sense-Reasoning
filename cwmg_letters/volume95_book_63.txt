63. LETTER TO AMY JACQUES GARVEY
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 12, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I had the 2nd volume of "The Philosophy and Opinions of
Marcus Garvey" or ''Africa for Africans" for which I thank you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
AMY JACQUES GARVEY
P. O. Box No: 22, STATION E
NEW YORK CITY, U.S.A.
From a copy: Gandhi Museum (Vol. 136) : Courtesy: Gandhi Memorial
Museum, New York
