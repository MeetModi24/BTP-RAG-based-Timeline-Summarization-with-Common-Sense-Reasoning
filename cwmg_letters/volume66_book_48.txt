48. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI A. MEHTA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 12, 1937
BHAI BHAGWANJI,
What a sad description of your family affairs you have given!
In spite of this, if you alone can practise perfect self-sacrifice I am
sure you will be able to repair the broken ship.
You need never apologize to me. It will always remain my
wish that your family, that is, the whole Karsanji family, should
prosper and regain the illustrious name that it once had, and that
you should be the means of bringing it about.
Vandemataram from
M . K. GANDHI
BHAGWANJI ANUPCHAND, VAKIL, B.A., LL.B.
RAJKOT SADAR
KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5836. Also C.W. 3059. Courtesy:
