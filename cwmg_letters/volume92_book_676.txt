676. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
May 2, 1933
CHI. NANIBEHN JHAVERI,
Because I am not writing 'blessings', do not think you do
not have my blessings. I said once that the inmates of the
Ashram should take my blessings for granted. Hence, they should
not mind if I do not put it in writing. If I avoid writing that
in some letters, it is merely because it saves me the effort.
Do not be frightened by the fast.
There is nothing small or great about life or death. What-
ever happens is only for the good. You must become more
enlightened.
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari - Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 28
