156. LETTER TO RAMA DEVI CHOWDHARY
October 24, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. If your son and daughter are keen on
studies it would be the right thing to arrange for that. It would
be excellent if they learn some vocation. It is impossible to have
your son touring with me. The present tour is rather [diffi-
cult].
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. RAMA DEVI CHOWDHARY
CHANDNI CHOWK
CUTTACK, ORISSA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2782
