97. LETTER TO MARGARET SANGER
WARDHA,
November 27, 1936
DEAR SISTER,
It was very thoughtful of you to send me an assortment
of your choice dried fruit. Agatha Harrison is here just now
and I remarked to her, "Whilst Mrs. Sanger went away from me
without taking anything, you see how much I am taking from
her even though she is so many miles away." You may judge
who is the better of us two, of course apart from the natural
superiority of the sex to which you belong. I have gone
through your reproduction of the interview in Asia. My
hurried reading left on me the impression that it was a faithful
reproduction.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
MRS. MARGARET SANGER
BIRTH CONTROL CLINICAL RESEARCH BUREAU
17 WEST 16TH STREET
NEW YORK CITY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
