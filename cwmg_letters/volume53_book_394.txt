394. LETTER TO M. S. ANEY
February 13, 1933
I am no more a general. I am as good as dead. I have
ceased to be a general as I am dead in the civil sense. Not only
that, but I am not even a pawn. All your generals and pawns
are out. In reply to those who had doubts I quoted a well-known
Sanskrit saying. Who could make it clearer? The Government
has correctly interpreted my words.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 141
