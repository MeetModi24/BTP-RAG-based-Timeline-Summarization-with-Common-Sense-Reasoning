59. LETTER TO BHUJANGILAL K. CHHAYA
May 8, 1941
CHI. BHUJANGILAL,
What will you do here with me? I am always engrossed in
my work and do not get even a minute to look at anyone. You
should, therefore, stay there and, availing yourself of the com-
pany of Narandas and others, shape the course of your life as you
wish. How much longer will my presence be available? What
trust can one put in it?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2594
