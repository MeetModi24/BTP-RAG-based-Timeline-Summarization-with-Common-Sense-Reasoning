374. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
February 12, 1933
MY DEAR SATISBABU,
You will have got on Sunday a parcel of 500 Harijans. I do not know
how you will distribute them, but I suppose you can think of better ways
than I can suggest from here. From among the friends in the mofussil you
will please have as many subscribers as possible and get newsboys to do
the street sales. I do not know if you will want the Khadi Pratishthan to
act as agents. But if you feel that the Pratishthan cannot undertake the
work, please try to get as many subscribers as possible and collect the
money and send a cheque for the amount and save so much money for the
Harijan. In case you would have the Pratishthan to take up the agency please
let the Manager know direct the maximum number of copies you will want.
With pranams to Hemprabhadevi,
Yours sincerely,
MAHADEV
From a photostat: G.N. 1625b
