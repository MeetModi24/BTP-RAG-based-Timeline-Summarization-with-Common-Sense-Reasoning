541. LETTER TO MAGANLAL AND MANJULA MEHTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 23, 1941
CHI. MAGANLAL AND MANJULA,
The portion in pencil in the accompanying letter was returned
because Urmi had written the wrong address. I did not like the
pencil handwriting and so she re-wrote the portion in ink. Urmi is
keeping fine and is becoming mature.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 1614. Courtesy: Manjulabehn M. Mehta
