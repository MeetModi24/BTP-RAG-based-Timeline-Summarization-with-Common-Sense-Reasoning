200. LETTER TO INDRA VIDYAVACHASPATI
Ashadh Sud 14 [July 15, 1924]
BHAI INDRA,
I have your letter. It reached me after I had already written
something [on the subject]. But I wrote nothing to hurt any-
body. I hope none will go to court and the matter has now
calmed down.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
SHRI INDRA VIDYAVACHASPATI
"ARJUN" OFFICE
DELHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4857. Courtesy: Chandragupta Vidyalankar
1 Endogamy prevailed among the Patidars of only these villages.
