357. LETTER TO VIJAY KUMAR
POONA,
March 22, 1946
DEAR VIJAY,
I have your letter of 10th instant. Of course Andhra is
entitled to separation. As to that I have no doubt. But since
independence is coming, why agitate and why threaten satyagraha ?
Is Indian opinion divided on this question?
I hope you are quite well. It distresses me to think that an
athlete like you can suffer from any illness. There must be some-
thing wrong in athletics which results in illness or early death.
Yours,
BAPU
MAHARAJAKUMAR OF VIZIANAGARAM
BENARES
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
