148. LETTER TO PREMLILA THACKERSEY
January 20, 1931
DEAR SISTER,
You must have received my last postcard in which I had ac-
knowledged receipt of the dates and requested you to send
cotton. I got the tin box of cotton on Sunday. It contained
1¾ pounds. That will last ten days. At present I require exactly
four pounds every month. Please, therefore, send the balance before
the end of this week. You need not put the cotton in a tin box.
It will be enough if you pack it in paper which has not been used.
Here I store the cotton in a bag.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4820. Courtesy: Premlila Thackersey
