342. LETTER TO CHANDABHAI
NEW DELHI,
July 15, 1947
BHAI CHANDABHAI,
If you had given me instances of my negligence, it would have
helped me. I am inquiring into what you have said. Don't give
up your resolve.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
