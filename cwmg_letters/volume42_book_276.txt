276. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
WARDHA,
December 11, 1929
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. I wonder why you have not received my
letters. I replied to everyone of your letters. I have informed you
of the date of my leaving here. At the earliest I may leave on
the 20th. I have received all your letters and I was quite satisfied.
With one letter I wrote, I had posted one to Jayaprakash too.
Was that also lost in transit? Come as early as you can.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-10: Shri Prabhavatibehnne, pp. 25-
