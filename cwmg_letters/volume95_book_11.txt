11. LETTER TO ELIZABETH MARI MOLTENO
7 BUITENSINGLE,
CAPE TOWN,
February 23, 1914
DEAR MISS MOLTENO,
My impression is that I said we would call on you tomorrow
(Tuesday), but Dr. Gool does not remember. Not to make any
mistake we shall be coming there between 3 and 4 tomorrow and take
our chance.
The visit to Miss Hobhouse was entirely successful. It was a
perfect pilgrimage for me. Mrs. Botha was all you described her.
She was most kind to both of us and most loving towards Mrs.
Gandhi. Thank you for all this. Incidentally, we met lady Gladstone
too & ?? Are you not pleased?
With our regards to you and Miss Greene,
I remain,
Your sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Molteno Murray Family Papers. Courtesy: University of
Capetown Libraries
