309. LETTER TO PALLATH RAMAN
February 4, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I have no doubt that when the so-called
higher classes or castes have forgotten the distinctions between high
and low and given up untouchability, Harijans will give up un-
touchability as among themselves.
The origin that you give of Nayadis is very interesting and in-
structive if it is historically true.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. PALLATH RAMAN
"SAHITHU SADANA"
PALGHAT
From a microfilm: S.N. 19294
