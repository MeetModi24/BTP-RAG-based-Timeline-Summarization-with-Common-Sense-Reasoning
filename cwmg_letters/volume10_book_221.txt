221. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
Jeth Vadi 2 [Samvat 1966]
[June 29, 1910]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
I write nothing more about Thaker, as I have sent you his long
letter.
I think I do have with me the dates of the Boer War in newspaper
cuttings or somewhere else. I have no time to find them out just now.
This also I write from the Farm. I shall arrange to get them for you
if you particularly want them. I only remember that this Corps was
formed in the November of 1899.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the original Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 4924
Courtesy: Radhabehn Choudhri
