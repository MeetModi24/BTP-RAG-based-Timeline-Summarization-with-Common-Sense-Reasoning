293. LETTER TO G. B. GAJBHIYE
November 2, 1934
MY DEAR GAJBHIYE,
I have now heard from Thakkar Bapa, and he says that it
is not possible for him to help you at this stage. All the scholar-
ships were considered and the allotment to the C. P. was exhaus-
ted. A stronger case than yours was rejected. Therefore, you
must, for the time being, wait and try again.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI G. B. GAJBHIYE
MORRIS COLLEGE
NAGPUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers: Courtesy: Pyarelal
