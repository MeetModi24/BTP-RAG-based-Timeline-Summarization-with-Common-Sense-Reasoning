673. LETTER TO PRANSHANKAR JOSHI
BOMBAY,
April 18, 1945
BHAI PRANSHANKAR,
I have your letter. I shall be glad even if I get a single
book. I do not have time to spare for you. Still you may
write to me after ten days, when I have reached Mahabaleshwar.
M. K. GANDHI
PRANSHANKAR JOSHI
PHOOLWADI
JETPUR, KATHIAWAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
