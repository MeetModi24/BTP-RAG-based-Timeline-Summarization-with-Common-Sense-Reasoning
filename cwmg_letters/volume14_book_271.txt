271. LETTER TO G. A. NATESAN
SABARMATI,
May 12 [1918 1]
DEAR MR. NATESAN,
Here is my speech.
I consider myself honoured to find my name among
the supporters of this resolution. I realize fully its meaning
and I tender my support to it with all my heart.
I had your note with the Rs. 100. You do not now need any
reply to the note!
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: G. N. 2225
