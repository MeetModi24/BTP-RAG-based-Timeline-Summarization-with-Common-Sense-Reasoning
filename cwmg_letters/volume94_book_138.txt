138. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 11, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Dr. Subbarayan's wife, Radhabai, has arrived today. Gosibehn 1
and Khurshedbehn are of course here. Sultana is here. The group
from Gujarat is also here. That is the situation. You must be doing
well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 Gosibehn Captain, grand-daughter of Dadabhai Naoroji
76
