437. LETTER TO KRISHNA IYER
SEVAGRAM,
March 16, 1945
CHI. KRISHNA,
Complete your studies. Even after that if you want to come,
write to me. How can you be tired of studies? You can do a
great deal of my constructive work while studying.
Blessings from
BAPU
KRISHNA IYER
HANSRAJ MAHILA MAHAVIDYALAYA
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
