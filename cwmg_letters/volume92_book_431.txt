431. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
BORSAD,
April 30, 1931
CHI. MANGALA,
I have your letter. I would certainly like to write to every-
one if I had even a moment's leisure. True, I get a little time
here. I wrote to Durga because Anandi had written that she
wanted a letter from me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11093. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
