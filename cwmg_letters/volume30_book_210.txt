210. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, Chaitra Sud 13 [March 27, 1926]
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I saw your letter to Kashi. I can understand your eagerness
to have her back. You had written to her about the cold which
made her hesitate. Now she can leave as soon as you are ready.
But no accommodation has been arranged yet, as I learned on
enquiry today. They have yet to fill in the application form and
other things. To whom is the application to be sent? We shall
act promptly if you let us know the name and address of the per-
son who is authorized to allot the accommodation. I had an im-
pression that Swami had taken everything upon himself and all
we have to do was to leave this place. You are not to bother your-
self regarding this; I mean not at the cost of your health. Let us
have as much information as you can send from there and the
rest of it we shall manage here.
You cannot at present leave
Lonavala; that's quite right. How you came to have fever I shall
know only when I get your letter. You should not panic when
you get some temperature; I see signs of it in your letter.
