95. LETTER TO LILAVATI MUNSHI
September 27, 1934
CHI. LILAVATI1,
There will be no room for the danger that you envisage. I
am planning from right now. As you know, I don't rely on votes
in my favour for doing anything. It is enough that I get a
measure of what the politically minded desire. Please have no
fear.
My being out [of the Congress] does not mean that I shall
be idle. I will continue to ply my charkha, perhaps with increased
speed. But surely one hand is not enough for clapping. Hence I
shall certainly need whatever help people can give.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy:
Narayan
Desai
