343. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Silence day
CHI. VASUMATI,
I would expect to hear the description of how you are carrying
out your new responsibility. Let me know the whereabouts of
everyone. Are there noises at night? Even a moment's peace should
not be allowed to be disturbed. Then I would not worry about how
much and which responsibility you are shouldering. Do not think
about what Bapu would like. Think about 'what I can do'.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: S.N. 9381.
