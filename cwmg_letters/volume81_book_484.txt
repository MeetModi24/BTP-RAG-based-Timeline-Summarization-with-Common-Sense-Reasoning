484. LETTER TO E. M. JENKINS
BIRLA HOUSE,
MOUNT PLEASANT ROAD, BOMBAY,
September 24, 1945
DEAR SIR EVAN,
I have to thank you for your letter of 18th instant in reply
to my letter about the prisoner Shri Haridas Mitra. I shall
await further reply about the case.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR EVAN M. JENKINS, K.C.S.I.
THE VICEROY'S HOUSE
NEW DELHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 48
1 He was arrested in 1942 and released in January 1945.
2 Vide pp. 253-.
