276. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
June 19, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Your unhappiness is understandable, but there is no cause
to be disheartened. If you make the effort success will be yours.
Yes, there is a lesson of humility, which you should learn. There
is no need to give up work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4434; also S.N. 24483
