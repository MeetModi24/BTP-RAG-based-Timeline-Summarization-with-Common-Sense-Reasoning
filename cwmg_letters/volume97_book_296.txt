296. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
SEGAON,
April 18, 1938
CHI. SUMI,
You write to me only rarely. Are you trying to pay me back in the same
coin? Write to me a long letter in Hindi. Kanam is fine. He thinks of you
sometimes.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
