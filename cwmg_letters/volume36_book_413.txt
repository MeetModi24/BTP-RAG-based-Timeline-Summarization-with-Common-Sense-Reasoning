413. LETTER TO KARNAD SADASHIVA RAO
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
May 27, 1928
MY DEAR SADASHIVA RAO,
I hope you are now completely free from the after-effects of
malaria. I hope to be at the Ashram practically throughout the
year. But I never know when I might have to move out owing
to unforeseen circumstances. When therefore you propose to bring
your daughters here, you will ascertain my movements beforehand.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SADASHIVA RAO
KODAIBAIL
MANGALORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 13229
