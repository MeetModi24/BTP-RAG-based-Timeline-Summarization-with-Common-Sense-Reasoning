90. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 30, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. I have also read the letter to Pyarelal. This
letter will be going with the late fee. Today there was a Goseva meeting
in Nalwadi which took three hours. I returned from there at 5.30 p.m.
After that I had my meal. I am writing this after prayer.
Pyarelal will be offering satyagraha tomorrow. Today he has worked
like mad. I did not think it was proper to postpone it after his arrival
here. His work will always be there. So, I decided on tomorrow. Now
I shall prepare the statement 1 for him.
Blessings form
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
