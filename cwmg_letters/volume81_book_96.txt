96. LETTER TO JYOTILAL MEHTA
SEVAGRAM,
July 31, 1945
CHI. JYOTI,
I understood your letter. Champa has written a long letter
but has not given her address. I do not know whether she is
there or somewhere else. You yourself can convey the message
that she may come here any time she likes. She should not waste
my time and should not set up a separate kitchen. Everything
has changed here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
