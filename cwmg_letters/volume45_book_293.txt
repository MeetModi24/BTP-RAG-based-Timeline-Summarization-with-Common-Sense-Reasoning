293. LETTER TO SURESH CHANDRA BANERJI
CAMP DELHI,
February 23, 1931
MY DEAR SURESHBABU,
For the present I will not write anything about the business
part of your letter. Let me ask you just now to follow the
treatment as a doctor should [advise] and restore your body to
its original healthy condition. Annada Babu may come any day
he likes and take his chance. I say this because my movement
is so uncertain. I have already opened discussion with Mr.
Banker.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI SURESH CHANDRA BANERJI
P-39 FERN ROAD, BALLYGUNJ
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 16935
