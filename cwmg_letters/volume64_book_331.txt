331. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 31, 1937
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I cannot write with the right hand. I will therefore write
only a few lines. How could I get the time to write during the
journey?
Why do you send your letters by express delivery?
It was such a long letter and yet there was no substance
to it. When will you learn to write letters?
The trip to Belgaum will probably be fixed in the middle
of April. Let it be when it will. I should advise you to come
over here and tell me whatever you wish to. If you have
nothing to do for your brothers there, do come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 388
