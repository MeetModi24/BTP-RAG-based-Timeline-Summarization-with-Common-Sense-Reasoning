346. LETTER TO PADAMPAT SINGHANIA
SEVAGRAM,
July 16, 1942
BHAI PADAMPATJI,
I deliberately postponed answering your letters.
Tandonji
was to come here. I wanted to see him first, since he is the
spirit behind the Hindi Sahitya Sammelan. In regard to ques-
tions pertaining to Hindi I always try to take him along with
me. I placed all your letters before him with a few introductory
remarks. He thought the burden of your letters was that your
donation to the Rashtrabhasha [Prachar] Samiti was for the spread
of Hindi alone. I showed the letters to other members also.
They too were of the same opinion. And later when Tandonji,
having examined the Samiti's records, showed me Jamnalalji's note
no doubt remained in my mind. It was only out of stupidity
that I troubled you. Please excuse me. I am certainly obliged to
Tandonji who opened my eyes to my own foolishness. My folly
lay in my wanting to know Jamnalalji's wishes, which made me
place on your letters an arbitrary construction. I am now credit-
ing all the money to the account of the Rashtrabhasha Prachar
[Samiti].

The second point concerns the inclusion of your name on
the Hindustani Prachar Sabha. I had not read the letter you
wrote to the Secretary. Tandonji pointed out that the letter
showed that you did not want to be a member and had agreed
to become one only at my insistence. I have no right to pre-
vail upon you in this manner. Hence if you so wish I shall
have your name struck off.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
