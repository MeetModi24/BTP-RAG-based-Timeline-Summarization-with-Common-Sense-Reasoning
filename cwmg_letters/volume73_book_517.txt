517. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
March 18, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I approve of your resolution. See that your health does not
suffer. Make a trial. If you are not ready this time, then try
again the next time. In this matter, of course, whatever you have
learnt will prove useful. That you will have to strive so hard shows
how weak we are.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8522. Also C.W. 7122. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
