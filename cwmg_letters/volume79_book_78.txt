78. LETTER TO MANGALDAS
SEVAGRAM,
January 19, 1945
BHAI MANGALDAS,
I have your letter and the cheque. It is good that your
blood-pressure is cured. How did it happen? Hereafter try to
avoid it.
Blessings from
BAPU
MESSRS MANGALDAS & SONS, BOOKSELLERS
BHAGA TALAO
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
45
