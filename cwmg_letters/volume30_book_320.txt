320. LETTER TO GULABDAS
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, Chaitra Vad 1[3] April [10, 1926]
BHAI GULABDAS,
I have your letter. Brahmacharya can be observed by keeping
good company, reading good books and repeating Ramanama. The
mind and the body should not be idle for a single moment. You
can certainly stick to the spinning-wheel if you want to. Your
father can be persuaded by your humility. There is in Calcutta
a college of indigenous medicine that has no connection with the
Government, but it is quite expensive. Likewise there is Tibbia
College in Delhi, also equally expensive.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 10869
