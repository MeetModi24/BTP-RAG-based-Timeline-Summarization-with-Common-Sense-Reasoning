116. LETTER TO LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
SEVAGRAM,
August 4, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
May I send you my congratulations on your appointment ? If
the India Office is to receive a decent burial and a nobler monu-
ment is to rise from its ashes, who can be a fitter person than
you for the work?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
THE RT. HON'BLE LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 173
