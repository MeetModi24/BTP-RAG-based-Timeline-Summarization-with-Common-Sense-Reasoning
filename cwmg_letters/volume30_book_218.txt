218. LETTER TO PHOOKEN
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 29, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
Mr. Banker now tells me that for a long time a sum of
Rs. 4,000 is due from you to the Khadi Board, now the All-India
Spinners' Association. Every pie is at the present moment requir-
ed to go through the Budget. Could you not now make this
payment?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. PHOOKAN
ASSAM
Copy to the A.I.S.A. Office for information.
From a microfilm S. N. 11158
