504. LETTER TO T. S. CHOCKALINGAM
September 11, 1941
DEAR CHOCKALINGAM 2 ,
I would not trouble you to come here as Dr. Subbaroyan
will be coming himself.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI T. S. CHOCKALINGAM
"DINAMANI"
100 MOUNT ROAD
MADRAS
From the original: C.W. 2976. Courtesy: T. S. Chockalingam
