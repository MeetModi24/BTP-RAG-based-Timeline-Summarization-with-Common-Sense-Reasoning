366. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 10, 1930
CHI. MANGALA,
Your handwriting is gradually improving. I can see from
your letters that you are trying. Learn well by heart the chapters
of the Gita. Be particular about the pronunciation. Is Pushpa
learning anything by heart?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C.W. 11090. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
