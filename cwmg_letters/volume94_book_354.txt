354. LETTER TO KERUM NATH BHATT
April 7, 1945
You should follow in your own practice as many of the 15 points
of the Constructive Programme as you can and inspire others to do
the same. In business you should give first place to service. Give up
profiteering. Never abandon truth and rectitude. Promote the charkha,
service of the Harijans, use of items of village industries and show
love, respect and generosity towards votaries of other faiths.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
