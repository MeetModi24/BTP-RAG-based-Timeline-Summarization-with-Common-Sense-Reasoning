123. LETTER TO PYARELAL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 21, 1936
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have your telegram and letter.
I had to wait for your
reply to my last letter. But I am writing this because I have
read your articles in the mean time. The articles are good. I
shall publish them in the Harijan in four parts. More could be
written about the remedies. You can still do so if anything fur-
ther occurs to you.
I hope your health is in perfect shape. If you decide not to
come here, it must be after careful thought. You should not
need any time to decide to come. If there is any doubt the
benefit of it should be in favour of coming.
Let me know if you had any talk with Haribhau about
which I should know.
Blessings from
BAPU
