475. LETTER TO MOHANLAL
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
BHAI MOHANLAL,
I was glad to have your letter. You have done good work
for the Harijans of Dukhaidi. You must work hard and remove
all their difficulties.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MOHANLAL
LAJPATRAI BHAWAN
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
