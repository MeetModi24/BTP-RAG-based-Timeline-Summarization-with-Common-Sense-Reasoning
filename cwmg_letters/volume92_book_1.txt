1. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
January 13, 1929
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I have your letter. It would have been better if you could
have personally come to Allahabad. But now it will do even if
a responsible representative of yours comes. A satyagrahi must
be absolutely faultless. Then alone can he be invincible. If
necessary, we will talk further about this when we meet.
I presume that you will definitely come to Wardha if you have
not come to the Sabarmati Ashram before that.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Haribhau Upadhyaya Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
