348. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
[November/December 1938]
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
How are things there? Is everybody well? What about
the cows? How much milk is left? How is Bhansali? Is
Parnerkar happy? Does Shakaribehn feel lonely? Here the
cold is not much to speak of. Sharada is fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
