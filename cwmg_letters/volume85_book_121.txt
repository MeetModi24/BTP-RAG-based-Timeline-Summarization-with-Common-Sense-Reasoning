121. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
URULI,
August 3, 1946
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have your letter. If you see no risk in it, what is there
for me to say? Do by all means settle with Bhimarao. I have
nothing further to say in the matter.
I note what you say about your coming here. I shall
await you on the 7th. Forward Rameshwardas's letter to him.
So Chimanlal has passed away. I hear there is again
trouble from the postmen.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 323
