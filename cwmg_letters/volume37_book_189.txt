189. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
August 9, 1928
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your long letter today. You don't seem to have become
completely free from your illness. If you want anyone to be sent
from here, let me know. It appears that I shall be able to leave
Bardoli on Sunday.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
If you find your hair to be a source of trouble to you, have
no hesitation in having it cut off.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 493. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
