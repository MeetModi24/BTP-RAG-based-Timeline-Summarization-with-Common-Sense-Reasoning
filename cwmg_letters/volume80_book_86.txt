86. LETTER TO BARBARA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 1, 1945
MY DEAR BARBARA,
I like your cheery letter. I hope you will be better still by
the change and return hale and hearty.
You know Hindustani. Try to write to me and to those who
understand it in Hindustani.
Yes, the climate here seems to suit me.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
