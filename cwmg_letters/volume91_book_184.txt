184. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Maha Sud 13 [February 12, 1919]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
My health had suffered some upset even while I was there.
I had started having some swelling, which persists. I must say
it is somewhat reduced.
I hope you are getting on with the work for the Hindi
Sahitya Sammelan. There is no reply from Prof. Karve yet. I
have even sent him a telegram. It is surprising that he does not
answer. I am now doubtful about the struggle continuing.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers Nehru Memorial Museum
