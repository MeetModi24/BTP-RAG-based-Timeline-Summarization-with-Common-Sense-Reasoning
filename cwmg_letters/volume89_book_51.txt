51. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
HYDARI MANSION,
August 13, 1947
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I have just come to Beliaghata. Kshitishbabu is with
me, and so is Arun. What I want is that both should go to
Sodepur and see to the work there. Hemprabha wanted to
look after me. I have detained her. Shaheed Saheb will be
with me. Let me see what happens. I have taken many risks,
perhaps this is the greatest of all. Who knows what will hap-
pen? We have to live as God wills and be content. I came
for Noakhali but have stayed on in Calcutta. It is all right. If
things go wrong here, then they will go wrong everywhere. If
things improve here, then perhaps they will improve everywhere.
Tell everyone that here also I am working for Noakhali. All
Muslims say that it is good that I have stayed on in Calcutta
and that too where Muslims feel unsafe and moreover in a
Muslim's house.
Blessings from
BAPU
