426. LETTER TO SARASWATI
March 4, 1937
CHI. SARASWATI,
I had your letter after a long interval. What subjects are
you studying? I want all the details, such as how far you have
progressed in mathematics. By now that subject, too, you must
be finding interesting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6155. Also C.W. 3424. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
