400. LETTER TO MANGHARAM SANTDAS
August 25, 1934
MY DEAR SANTDAS,
I have read your little note to Jairamdas at the back of Will
Hayes' letter. Though I have not been able to write to you I
have been closely following the course that Kamlani's derange-
ment is taking. I have no doubt that he will be himself again,
and when he does regain his balance, subject to your approval,
I would ask him, if it is only for a time, to come back to India
before he resumes his work. Life in England, especially for a
man with a tender conscience like Kamlani, is a very difficult job.
I hope you are not worrying. After all Kamlani and all of us are
in God's hands. We can but work and pray.
SHRI M. SANTDAS
HYDERABAD
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
