13. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
July 18, 1939
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I have read your entire letter. What can I do if you do not
get your post? I am writing regularly these days. It is difficult
to find room in Harijan Ashram for those who come forward to
work. Why insist on the Lal Bungalow ? There are other build-
ings too nearby. If the work grows, we can build houses. But
what could we do if you keep falling ill? You are the cause of
your illness. Ba is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI AMTUL SALAAMBEHN 1
HARIJAN ASHRAM 2
SABARMATI 3
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 425
