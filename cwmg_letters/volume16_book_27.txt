27. LETTER TO INDRA VIDYALANKAR
BOMBAY,
Shravan Sud 6 [August 18, 1919]
DEAR INDRA,
I am cleaning up my office and I see there is a letter from
you. I think I have replied to it. In case, however, you have
not received the reply, please let me know and I shall try to write
to you.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi original in Gandhiji's hand: C. W. 4856.
Courtesy: Chandragupta Vidyalankar
