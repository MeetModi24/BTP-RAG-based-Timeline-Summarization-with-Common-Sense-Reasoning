239. LETTER TO TARAMATI M. TRIKUMJI
December 15, 1941
If Mathuradas is destined to render still more service, he will
come to no harm. I have no doubt in my mind that if he observes
complete silence and fixes his thoughts exclusively on God, that
would be the best medicine.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, pp. 181-
