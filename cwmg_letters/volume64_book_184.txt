184. LETTER TO SHRIMANNARAYAN
December 20, 1936
CHI. SHRIMAN,
I have gone through your article. It cannot be published
in Harijan, it is not worth publishing. Make your scheme pub-
lic. Your resolution is acceptable to all but how are we to
define 'literacy'? The question is very controversial.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 299
