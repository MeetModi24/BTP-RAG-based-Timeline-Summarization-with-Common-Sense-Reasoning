220. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
June 11, 1935
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your letter. It is not good that your constipation per-
sists. Eat neem leaves and tamarind. You get plenty of tamarind
there. Clean it before eating it.
Narandas is very eager that you should go to Rajkot. If you
are not needed there, it would certainly be good that you should
go to Rajkot. If you do not find the place agreeable you may
come away.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S. N. 9400. Also C.W. 646. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
