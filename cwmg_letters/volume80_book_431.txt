431. LETTER TO JAMNADAS DWARKADAS
PANCHGANI,
June 4, 1945
CHI. JAMNADAS,
I received your letter at 5 o'clock. You should get this
tomorrow. I will be able to see Panditji if he comes at 3 o'clock
on the 6th. Of course I shall be observing silence. If afterwards
you want to talk to me it will be all right. Or I shall find some
other time if you want. At 4 o'clock Bhole, a Harijan, is coming.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JAMNADAS DWARKADAS
GLENDALE
MAHABALESHWAR
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
