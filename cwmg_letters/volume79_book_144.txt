144. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
SEVAGRAM,
February 1, 1945
MY DEAR DESHBHAKTA,
I do not appreciate your appeal. There is no hurry. It
should be revised. Give the assets of the ashramam, the liabilities,
yearly donations, the concrete activities, the income therefrom.
Then give the prospective expenditure in detail.
Has the ashramam enough workers for the different tasks?
Who is the doctor for general work, who is the veterinary surgeon,
who is the matron? Are all these experts to be brought in? The
thing reads visionary. It must be businesslike. If the burden cannot
be borne, the appeal should wait. You will get the funds. Let
them not be a curse. Money won't produce men. The latter will
produce as much as you need.
Love.
BAPU
DESHBHAKTA KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
GUNTUR
