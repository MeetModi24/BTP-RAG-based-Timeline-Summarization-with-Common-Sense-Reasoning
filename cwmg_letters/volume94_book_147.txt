147. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA (C.P.),
November 22, 1941
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
With reference to the Sales Act, please do as Shri Jajuji 2 may
advise. I have not applied my mind to it in all its bearings.
Yours,
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
LAJPATRAI BHAVAN
LAHORE
From the original: Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
1 The letter is written at the back of Kasturba Gandhi's letter of November 21,
1941.
2 Shrikrishnadas Jaju, Secretary, All-India Spinners' Association
80
