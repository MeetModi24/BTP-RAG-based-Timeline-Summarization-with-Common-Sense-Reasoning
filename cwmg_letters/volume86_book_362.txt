362. LETTER TO T. PRAKASAM
SRIRAMPUR,
December 28, 1946
MY DEAR PRAKASAM 1 ,
I have now your letter enclosing papers. I hope you have
my reply to your telegram, copy of which is enclosed here-
with. I have nothing to add to what I have said. I regard
Central Government's reply as a challenge to your faith in your-
self, the charkha and the dumb masses of Madras. If the latter
are really with you, as I hope they are, you can accept the
challenge and you will benefit not only Madras but the whole
of Indian humanity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
