57. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI
Magsar Vad 13 [December 13, 1925]
BHAI PUNJABHAI,
I regularly get your nice letters. I hope you had the letter
I wrote after reaching here. With persistent efforts you will be
able to clear your eye [of evil]. Always be on the alert. The way
to escape violence in speech is to talk as little as possible and
not to speak at all without weighing your words.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI PUNJABHAI (NANA)
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 186. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
