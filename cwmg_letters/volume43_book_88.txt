88. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
ANAND,
Silence Day [March 17, 1930]
O PARSI LADY,
There is no limit to your cunning. You seem to be prepar-
ing an alibi in advance. If parents lift you up, haven't they
the right to throw you down? And why do you believe that
I have lifted you up in order that I may afterwards throw you
down? Why did I have Chapter XII [of the Gita] read out on
the day of your marriage? If you believe that you do not possess
the virtues I attribute to you, you should pray to God to grant
them to you. But your uncle calls you Dhairyamata . What
do you say to that?
Blessings from
BAPU
