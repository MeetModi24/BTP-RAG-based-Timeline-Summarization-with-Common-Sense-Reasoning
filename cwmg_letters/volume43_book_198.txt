198. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
Tuesday, April 8, 1930
CHI. MAHADEV,
Bhai Amritlal has been arrested. And on the Dholera side
the authorities seem to be acting in a novel fashion. I think
you should pay a visit to that part. From today I have started
visiting the neighbouring villages. I don't intend this week to go
outside the limits of this taluka or its neighbourhood. If, however,
it becomes necessary I will be ready to go to Dholera or any place
near it. I suppose you have heard about the arrests of Jamnalal,
Ishvarlal, etc.
Blessings from
BAPU
