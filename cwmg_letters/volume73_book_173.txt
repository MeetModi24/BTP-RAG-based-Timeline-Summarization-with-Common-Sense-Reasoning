173. LETTER TO MANUBHAI PANCHOLI
SEVAGRAM,
November 11, 1940
CHI. MANUBHAI,
I got your beautiful letter.
I had got Vijaya's letter also.
I can understand your pain. This should be sufficient to calm
both of you: if I go on a fast, it won't be on my own but as
prompted by the inner voice. Like Mirabai, I too, am pierced
with the dagger of love.
Show this to Nanabhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7133. Also C.W. 4625. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
