77. LETTER TO JAYA
September 24, 1934
CHI. JAYA,
Bhanu is gradually improving. I have not taken the trouble
to know what that welcome gift of a hundi is. If you sisters don't
like it, by all means don't accept it. Our aim is somehow to
propagate khadi by pure means.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. JAYABEHN
NENSHI MORARJEE'S BUNGALOW
SEA VIEW, GHODBUNDER ROAD
VILLE PARLE, B.B.&.C.I. RLY.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXIII
1& Kasturba Gandhi's brother and his wife
