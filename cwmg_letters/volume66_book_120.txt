120. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
SEGAON,
September 1, 1937
MY DEAR KU.,
Of course nothing is required to be done before the meeting
of the Association about the President.
Have you considered Shankar Lal's suggestion that the meet-
ing be held on the 23rd instead of the 16th inst.
I understand what you say about Rao. If missing Chhote-
lal is not found when you get this letter, apply your ingenuity
and see whether you can find a way of tracing him.
If you have not done so already I suggest your collecting
specimens of all hand-made paper that is produced in India
and their present prices.
If Joshi can come here on Saturday at 3 p.m., it will be con-
venient for me but if the morning is more convenient you can
bring him at 8 a.m.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10132
