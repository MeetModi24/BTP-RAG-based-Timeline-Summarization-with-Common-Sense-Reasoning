38. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
SABARMATI,
Tuesday [November 29, 1921]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
You will have received my reply sent yesterday. It is good that
you met Sir Prabhashankar and others. The important thing is that
we should love our colleagues while adhering to our own programme.
If our faith is in peace there is no other way.
It will be good if I too have a talk with Umar. This great task
can be accomplished only when all his doubts are dispelled and he is
absorbed in work.
I hope to reach Bardoli on Thursday evening. From there I
expect to reach Bombay on Sunday. I shall stay there till Wednesday
if I think it is necessary, but I do wish to leave earlier if I can. Will
it be all right if I decide only when I am there whether I should take
the chair on Wednesday?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
