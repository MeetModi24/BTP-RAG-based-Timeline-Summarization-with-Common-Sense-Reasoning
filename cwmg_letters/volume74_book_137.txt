137. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
[May 30, 1941]
CHI. AMRIT,
Your note.
I am sorry the jaw is still giving you pam. You will carry
out instructions.
I hope you got my letter of yesterday.
Your resolution is not up to the mark. We will discuss it
when we meet.
Here everything goes well.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4024. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7333
