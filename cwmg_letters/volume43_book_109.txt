109. LETTER TO GANGADEVI SANADHYA
KARELI,
Thursday [March 20, 1930]
CHI. GANGADEVI,
I received your letter. Take good care of your health and
render what service you can. Whatever the doctors may say, you
must not even think of including in your diet anything except milk
and fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2533
