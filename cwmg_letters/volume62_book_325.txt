325. LETTER TO TARA N. MASHRUWALA
March 21, 1936
CHI. TARA,
After all, you didn't write to me. Anxiety or thinking
about you—call it what you like—persists every day. I regard
it as wrong that you should ignore your ailment. You have
many excellent qualities, but this disregard of the body is hard
to reconcile with them. You ought to make a "manly effort"
to overcome your ailment. It does not matter if you have given up
the treatment of Gaurishankerbhai. Try the prescription of any
other in whom you have faith. I am certainly prepared to keep
you under my care. You had, if I have correctly followed your
words, no troubles when you were living with me. Try one
thing for a week: take in two instalments, mixing it with your
food, one tola of green garlic very finely crushed. It indeed had
excellent effect on me. It helps greatly in eliminating viruses
in the body—in relieving one of gas. This was the remedy
prescribed by Dr. Ansari for Navin. Green garlic, which is to
be consumed along with the bulb and the stems and the
leaves, should be washed clean before it is crushed. It is meant
not for imparting taste, but as medicine. Write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
