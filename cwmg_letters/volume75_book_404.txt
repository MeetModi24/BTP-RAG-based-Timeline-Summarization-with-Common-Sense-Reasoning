404. LETTER TO GOVINDLAL, SHIVLAL AND MOTILAL
February 17, 1942
GOVINDLAL, SHIVLAL, MOTILAL,
Your letter. It is no more as clear to me as you think. You
know of course that I am not responsible for the present policy of
the Congress, nor have I any influence over it.
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
