134. LETTER TO AVADHESH DUTT AVASTHI
[November 14, 1935]
CHI. AVADHESH,
I have your letter after a long time. Why have you not yet
taken up some definite work? In order to be fearless we should
love all and adhere to the path of truth. The assertion that
goodness is easier to come by is based on the fact that all wish to
be known as good people and no one wants to be known as a
sinner. Therefore it can be said that to be a sinner is difficult!
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3214
