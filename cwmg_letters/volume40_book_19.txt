19. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 23, 1929
BHAISHRI KHAMBHATTA,
I have your letter. On March 9, I shall be in Burma,
April 5 seems feasible. When, at the earliest, do you have to be
informed of the decision? On March 1, I shall leave Ahmedabad
for Delhi en route to Calcutta and leave for Burma from there.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
275 HORNBY ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6591
