49. LETTER TO R. S. NIMBKAR
[Before August 13, 1937]
So far as I am concerned, I would not create deadlocks but
would cheerfully face them when they are forced upon me. I
do not work the Act, when I work so as to end it. It would be
foolish of me, having entered the Legislature, not to take all ad-
vantage I can of it, consolidate my position and strengthen it.
Mr. Gandhi informs Mr. Nimbkar that the points raised by him are
for the Working Committee to deal with and he takes it they are already
before the Committee.
The Hindu, 13-8-1937
