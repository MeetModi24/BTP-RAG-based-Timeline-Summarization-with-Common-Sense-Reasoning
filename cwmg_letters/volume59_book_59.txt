59. LETTER TO RAM SARAN VIDYARTHI
September 22, 1934
DEAR VIDYARTHI,
Khan Saheb is most disinclined to accept public appoint-
ments. He does only when there is no escaping from it. He has
been turning down proposal after proposal. I would ask you
to secure someone else, preferably some local man. We should
by this time learn to depend upon intrinsic merit than on ex-
trinsic aid.
Yours sincerely,
MR. RAM SARAN VIDYARTHI, B.A.
PRESIDENT, KHADI AND SWADESHI EXHIBITION
STUDENTS' KHADI LEAGUE, MEERUT
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
