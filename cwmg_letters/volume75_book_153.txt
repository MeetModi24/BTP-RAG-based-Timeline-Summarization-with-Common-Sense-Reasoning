153. LETTER TO S. SATYAMURTI
SEVAGRAM,
November 15, 1941
MY DEAR SATYAMURTI,
I have your letter. You should have published the whole of my
letter. It did not admit of abbreviation. It has given rise to con-
siderable misunderstanding. I would ask you please to send the
whole for publication.
I have no objection to the whole correspondence being pub-
lished. But it may take time. Hence the necessity for publishing
the particular letter separately.
I am glad you propose earnestly to rebuild your broken
body . . . .
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Bombay Chronicle, 19-11-1941
