338. LETTER TO BAL D. KALELKAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 3, 1944
CHI. BAL 3 ,
I have your very beautiful letter. I can understand that
Western music has claimed you. Does it not mean that you have
such a sensitive ear as to appreciate this music? All I wish is
that you should have all that is to be gained there and come
here when your time is up, and be worthy of your country. I
don't know whether I shall survive to see you. And what does
it avail to know about it beforehand? Aren't we all in God's keep-
ing? I like your letter so much that I would love to reply to it
at length but it cannot be. This too I have written out soon after
the morning prayer.
Blessings from
BAPU
