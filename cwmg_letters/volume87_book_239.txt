239. LETTER TO NIRMAL KUMAR BOSE
ON THE TRAIN,
April 13, 1947
CHI. NIRMAL BABU,
Your letter is frank. It does you credit on that account,
but it makes me sad.
On your own showing, you were less than truthful. Had
you shown the requisite courage and spoken out, I would not
have let you go so abruptly as you did.
I see that I have lost caste with you. I must not defend
myself. If we ever meet and if you would discuss what I consi-
der to be your hasty judgment, we shall talk.
My Bengali continues, though slowly.
Love.
BAPU
My Days with Gandhi, p. 188
