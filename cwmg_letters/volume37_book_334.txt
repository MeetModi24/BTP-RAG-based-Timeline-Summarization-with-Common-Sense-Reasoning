334. LETTER TO DHANWANTRI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
September 21, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letters. The message that I can send to the
Students' Union is:
"Do not fear the Government or any other power that may
come in your way. Go forward and build a strong link between
yourselves and the toiling millions who do not even know the mean-
ing of the word education."
Yours sincerely,
SJT. DHANWANTRI
SECRETARY, LAHORE STUDENTS' UNION, LAHORE
From a photostat: S.N. 13531
