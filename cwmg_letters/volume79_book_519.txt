519. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 25, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Why the despair? If you cannot find work here where
there is so much work, where else will you find it? One may
not succeed in finding a needle in a haystack, but does one have
to search for the hay? There is work right before your eyes.
It is another matter that you should ignore it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4505
