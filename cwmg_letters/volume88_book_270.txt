270. LETTER TO WANDA DYNOWSKA
NEW DELHI,
July 7, 1947
MY DEAR UMA,
Your letter. You are suspicious. Sardar is not so bad as you
imagine. He has no anti-European prejudice. Don't be sentimental
but deal with cold facts and you will succeed.
My movement is uncertain. You will come when I am fixed
up somewhere.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
