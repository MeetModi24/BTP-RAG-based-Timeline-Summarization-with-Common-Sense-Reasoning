103. LETTER TO J. M. SARKAR
CAMP KHURJA,
November 3, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Engaged as I am in continuous touring
I do not get many newspapers to read. Even as it was I was
not in touch with the newspaper world but now I am even less
in such touch. I therefore did not even know that the differences
between the two leaders had become so much accentuated as to
lead to a public discussion about them. I share fully your grief
over them but I do not know what I can do, circumstanced as
I am. I can therefore only satisfy myself with the hope that all
will be well and that local pressure will be sufficient to put an
end to the differences.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. JNANENDRA MOHAN SARKAR
VICE-PRESIDENT
MURSHIDABAD DT. CONGRESS COMMITTEE
BERHAMPORE (BENGAL)
From a photostat: S.N. 15721
