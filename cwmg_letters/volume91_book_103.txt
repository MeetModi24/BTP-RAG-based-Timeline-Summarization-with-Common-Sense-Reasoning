103. LETTER TO PARAMANAND K. KAPADIA
AHMEDABAD,
Aso Sud 12 [October 8, 1916]
DEAR PARAMANAND,
I have received your letter. You and your friends may come
with pleasure and stay in the Ashram. It would be more conven-
ient if you could bring your bedding, plate and bowl and a jug.
Bhai Virchand has also written to me about you.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
PARAMANAND KUNVERJI KAPADIA
MANHAR BUILDING
PRINCESS STREET
BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original : G.N. 11583
