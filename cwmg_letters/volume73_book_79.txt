79. LETTER TO CHANDEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 13, 1940
CHI. CHANDEL,
I have your beautiful letter.
You are doing good work
there. I am looking forward to seeing you both if you will
come here after Diwali.
Blessings from
BAPU
Bapu-Conversations and Correspondence with Mahatma Gandhi, p. 189
