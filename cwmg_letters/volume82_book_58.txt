58. LETTER TO NARENDRA DEV
November 7, 1945
BHAI NARENDRA DEV,
I have your letter. There are occasions when one has to
write about those with whom one is very close. The rule to follow
is that people closely associated should not eulogize each other. I
can write much in support of this but what shall I write to a per-
son like you? Better to spare me from all such things. I am sorry
I have not even seen the daily called Samsar. Even if it comes
here, it is not shown to me because so many editors are kind
enough to send me their dailies. I can say that these days I do
not read newspapers at all. Clippings from newspapers believed
to be useful to me are placed before me. If I send a message
to one, why not to the others? Others also do write and ask for
messages.
Blessings from
BAPU
