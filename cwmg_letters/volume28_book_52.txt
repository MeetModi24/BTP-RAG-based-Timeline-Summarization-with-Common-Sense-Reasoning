52. LETTER TO MATHURADAS PURUSHOTTAM
August 20, 1925
BHAI MATHURADAS,
I was very pleased on reading your letter in which you own
up your mistake. This weakness is common. Decisive speech is
for him only who never speaks without thinking and who speaks
only when it is absolutely necessary. One should use language as
sparingly as a miser. Everything will be well since you are firm
about it. Today I am in Cuttack and have some leisure, so I am
disposing of all pending letters such as yours.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3725
