426. LETTER TO MADHAV BIRLA
FATEHPUR,
January 8, 1947
CHI. MADHAV,
Don't spoil me. Hariram is devoted to me. He has given
me much service. But I am being well looked after. I am eating
well. Therefore I am sending both back. You may send me
what I ask for; anything more will be an excess. Write to
Ghanshyamdasji. Don't worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
