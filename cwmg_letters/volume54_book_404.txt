404. LETTER TO NANALAL K. JASANI
April 9, 1933
BHAI NANALAL,
I have your letter. Even if Maganlal does not join you,
you two should divide the property and put the suggested divisions
before Chhaganlal. We should hope that Maganlal will accept
whatever you do. You should make the divisions with due re-
gard for the sums earmarked by Doctor for specific purposes-
that is, the total value of the property should be reduced by that
much.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9631
