31. LETTER TO DEVA PRASAD SARVADHIKARI
March 9, 1933
DEAR SIR DEVA PRASAD,
I thank you for your letter of 3rd March and was grateful to
learn that Abdul Alim of Tipperah had already been admitted to
the institution and that you were having him examined by a
doctor and making arrangements for special attention being paid
to him.
Let us hope that he will prove worthy of all the kindness that
is being shown to him.
Yours sincerely,
SIR DEVA PRASAD SARVADHIKARI
125 Bow BAZAR ST.
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm : S.N. 20506
