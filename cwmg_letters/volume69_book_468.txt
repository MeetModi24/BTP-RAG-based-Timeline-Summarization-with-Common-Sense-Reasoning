468. LETTER TO UMA DEVI
ABBOTTABAD,
July 14, 1939
MY DEAR UMA,
You can come here whenever you like. You won't be a bur-
den on anybody. But it is fairly hot here. And my stay is uncer-
tain. I am likely to come to Kashmir after 23rd if not earlier.
Now do as you like.
You need not go to Poland. Security can be given regarding
your mother by transferring your cash to me. Other ways also
may be devised. These will be thought out when we meet.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1202 and 8056.
Also C.W. 5097.
Courtesy:
Uma Devi
