272. LETTER TO JAMNADAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 7, 1939
CHI. JAMNADAS,
What have you been doing? Why don't you make enough
effort to build up your body? How can you afford to feel old at this
age? Now stay there and take rest and leave Rajkot only after
you have fully recovered your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
