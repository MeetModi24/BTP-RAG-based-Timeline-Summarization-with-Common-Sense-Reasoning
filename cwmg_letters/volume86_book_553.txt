553. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
AMISHAPARA,
February [1/2] 1947
CHI. VALLABHBHAI,
Please go through this letter.
Frydman is the same as Bharatanand; please see if you can
grant him Indian citizenship.
*
*
*
I want you not to be unhappy. Please leave me in the
hands of God.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 347
