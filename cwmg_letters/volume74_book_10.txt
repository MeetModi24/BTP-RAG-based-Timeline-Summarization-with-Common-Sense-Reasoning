10. LETTER TO SACHINDRA NATH MITRA
April 21, 1941
DEAR SACHINDRA BABU,
Your letter. I gave you the minimum. But a satyagrahi
who has the call may go from one end of the earth to the other.
So far as your forming a sevadal is concerned please see the
Bengal Provincial Congress Committee people.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SACHINDRA NATH MITRA
5/ KANTAPUKUR LANE
BAGHABAZAR, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 7186
