224. LETTER TO GHULAM RASUL QURESHI
November 25, 1940
CHI. QURESHI,
I am glad your letter is quite frank. I will not send either you
or Gulzar Khan. You cannot go leaving the work there unfinished.
I shall explain things to Amtul Salaam.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10767. Courtesy: Ghulam Rasul
Qureshi
