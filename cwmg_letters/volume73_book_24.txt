24. LETTER TO SHARDABEHN G. CHOKHAWALA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
September 22, 1940
CHI. BABUDI,
SO you did not come. I wanted to talk to you in person.
Why do you lose heart? I will resolve your doubts when you come.
Come soon. I am going to Simla. I will return in October.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10030. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
