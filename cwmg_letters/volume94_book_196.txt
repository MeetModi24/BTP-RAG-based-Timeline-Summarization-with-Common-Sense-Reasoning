196. LETTER TO SIR RICHARD TOTTENHAM
DETENTION CAMP,
January 13, 1944
SIR,
I beg to thank the Government for their reply to my letter of 22nd
ultimo regarding Shri Mirabai and for the arrangements made to send
Capt. Simcox from time to time for the completion of the treatment
begun by him.
I am, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
THE ADDITIONAL SECRETARY
To THE GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
(HOME DEPARTMENT)
NEW DELHI
From a photostat : India Office Library and Records. Courtesy : The British
Library, London
