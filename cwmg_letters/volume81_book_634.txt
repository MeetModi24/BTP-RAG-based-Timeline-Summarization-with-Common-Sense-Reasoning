634. LETTER TO VIMLARANI GURBUXANI
October 14, 1945
CHI. VIMLA1,
Gurbuxani writes to say that you have gone to your parents
and your confinement is drawing near. I hope you will have
an easy delivery. Let me know when it is over.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VIMLARANI GURBUXANI, M.A.
2A CONVENT ROAD
DEHRA DUN
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
