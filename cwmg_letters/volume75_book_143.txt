143. LETTER TO MADALASA
November 12, 1941
CHI. MADALASA,
This is just to cheer you up. I do get news about you. You
must be getting my messages. Do you move about a little now?
You must go out for walks, provided the doctor agrees.
The fewer the tonic foods you eat the better.
Is the baby growing well? Dr. Das was to come today.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 323
