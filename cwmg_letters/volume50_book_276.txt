276. LETTER TO LALITA
July 26, 1932
DAUGHTER LALITA,
From Subbiah's letter I have learnt of your grief and Subbiah
writes that you have been weeping much. But you should know
that religion forbids weeping. The God who gave the daughter
has the right to take her away. And in the end we all are to go
there. What grief can there be for that! So forget the grief and
plunge yourself in service (of others). Certainly write to me. May
God give you peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. 11, p. 491
