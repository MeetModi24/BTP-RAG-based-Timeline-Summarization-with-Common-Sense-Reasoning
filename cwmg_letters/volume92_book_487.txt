487. LETTER TO FRANCISCA STANDENATH
December 21, 1931
DEAR SAVITRI 1 ,
Our meeting this time was not quite happy. You jarred on
me by your harsh judgments of people and your pride in your
righteousness. This latter defect is a terrible handicap on progress.
I do not want you to brood over what I am writing. I would
like you just to think over what I am writing and forget all
about it if you think that I am misjudging you. Even so, it is
better that I tell you what is in my mind rather than that I
should harbour a judgment about you. I would be untrue to
you if I did not tell you what I thought about particular actions
of yours.
Never mind whether I can write to you or not. Both you
and Satyavan should continue to write to me. It does happen
nowadays that I do not even get the time to read the letters
I receive. All the same I should be anxious if I did not hear
from those who like you are in close contact.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
