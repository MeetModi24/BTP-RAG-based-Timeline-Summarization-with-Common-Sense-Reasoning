36. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR AND SUMATI MORARJEE
August 15, 1944
CHI.
SHANTIKUMAR AND
SUMATI,
We are all arriving there on Saturday. We shall be staying
at Birla House. You should both call there, as you are aware
what comforts I require at present. The rest when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 848
