304. LETTER TO VIMALABEHN A. PATEL
March 28, 1932
CHI. VIMALA,
Narasinhbhai had prepared me for your letter and the books.
Perhaps you know that I was able to see him. He was in good
health and quite cheerful. He is now kept with other prisoners.
I will find time to read the books which you have sent. I hope
your work is going on very well.
Vandemataram to Mother from both of us, and blessings to you.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3275
