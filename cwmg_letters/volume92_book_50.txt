50. LETTER TO M. P. SRINIVASAN
KAUSANI,
July 1, 1929
MY DEAR SRINIVASAN,
Your letter. I think a bridegroom has a perfect right to say
what the bride shall wear as the bride has regarding the bride-
groom. They are not yet husband and wife. Therefore there is no
question of pressure. Every young man has a right to say what
qualities his future partner shall have. So has every young girl.
Pomp and paraphernalia are a curse.
Yours,
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/63
