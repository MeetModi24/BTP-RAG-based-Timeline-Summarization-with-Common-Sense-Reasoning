3. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
[September 16, 1934]
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I got your letter. The Kaka affair has become a painful one.
But you should laugh away the thing. It will all subside by and
by. I think there is no malice behind the outcry. I am trying
to calm it and hope to succeed. There is nothing but simple
misunderstanding behind the whole affair. I will not let Kaka go
in this manner. I have sent you a copy of the letter I have written
to Mavalankar.
Kaka has been laid up with fever from the very next day
after he arrived here. The temperature has not come down even
once. It was above 100° this morning. It rises to 102°. There
are no other symptoms, except cold and a little cough. There is
some fear that it may be typhoid. Write a brief note to him.
Jones is better but not quite well. Write a few lines to him
also. Dr. Khan Sahebs examines him.
Both the brothers went to Akola yesterday. They were dragged
to Khamgaon from there. They were to return today, but now
they will return tomorrow.
I am sending a letter from Lala Sham Lal for you to read.
1 The address is supplied from the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's
Diary.
2 From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary
3 Vide Vol. LVIII, pp. 452-.
4 Stanley Jones, an American missionary
5 Elder brother of Abdul Ghaffar Khan
It seems there has been some haste in regard to Dunichand.
There was another wire from him.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
89 WARDEN ROAD
BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
