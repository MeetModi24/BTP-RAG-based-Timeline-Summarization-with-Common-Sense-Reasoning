343. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM,
January 8, 1941
BHAI SATIS BABU,
I have your letter. Jajuji has written something. I have
not read it. Whenever Annadaji wants to see it, show it to him
without hesitation.
I am sending these two slips of paper for
perusal. They are not to be returned.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2738
