114. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
November 27, 1932
CHI. MANI,
Today's report is better even than yesterday's. [Dahyabhai's]
temperature had gone down to 97.5° and did not rise above
101.5°. He sleeps well. You should devote all your attention
to your work.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
All the friends jointly bear the expenditure on account of
Dahyabhai.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro‒4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 94
