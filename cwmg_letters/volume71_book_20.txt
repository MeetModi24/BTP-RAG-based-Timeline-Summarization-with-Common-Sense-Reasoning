20. LETTER TO MANUBEHN MASHRUWALA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 8, 1939
CHI. MANUDI,
At last you have become a mother. Give the child whatever
name you wish. I did not like at all the name you gave Rami's
daughter. It is much too long. The girl still does not come near
me. Manojna's Shobhan took to me immediately. Kunvarji is
quite well now. The girl also is growing very well. Ba has gone to
Delhi. Lakshmi is ill there. Write to them at Delhi.
I hope all three of you are keeping fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2674. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
