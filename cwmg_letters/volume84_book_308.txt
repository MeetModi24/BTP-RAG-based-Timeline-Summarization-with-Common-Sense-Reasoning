308. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL VIDYARTHI
MUSSOORIE,
May 31, 1946
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
Why does your health suffer so often? I have had a lot
of correspondence in connection with your case and have also
thought at length over it. Devdas feels that something bor-
dering on untruth has crept into your reply. It is not enough to
say that they may proceed against you if they want. Is there
any proof that you were in the Fort at Delhi?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
