440. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
August 24, 1932
CHI. MANUDI,
I got your letter. Why do you still cry sometimes for Hari-
lal? We should pray to God for his welfare. You must write to me
regularly. I hope you do not display temper now, and pay pro-
per attention to study. This time your handwriting was bad. I
hope all the children of Ramibehn are in good health now.
Blessings from
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1515. Courtesy: Manubehn Mashru-
wala
