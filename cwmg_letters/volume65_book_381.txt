381. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
June 25, 1937
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Just received your statement on the Frontier policy. Khan
Saheb and I have read it. I like it very well. I wonder if the Spanish
bombing and the British are exactly alike. How has the extent
of the British damage been known? What has been the ostensible
reason given for the British bombing? Don't smile or be angry
that I do not know these things so well as you do. I can learn
very little from the little I see of the newspapers. But don't
trouble to answer my questions. I shall follow the reactions to
your statement. Maybe these will throw some light. And in any
case you will fill in the gaps when we meet. I hope the
Maulana will come.
But even if he cannot I would
like you to hold on to the date. Let us have the three quiet
days.
Hope Indu is well.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Khan Saheb wants you to see the enclosed.
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1937. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
