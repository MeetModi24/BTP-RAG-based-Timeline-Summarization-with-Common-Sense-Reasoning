134. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[DATTAPARA,]
November 11, 1946
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I have your letter. You will send the khadi here.
I shall certainly come but I cannot say when. If I am
late, Bapa will come. The work is difficult. But whatever the
situation, we have to do our duty. What more shall I write?
I have now come back from Noakhali and Sonachaka. Prayer
is over and I am writing this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 538
