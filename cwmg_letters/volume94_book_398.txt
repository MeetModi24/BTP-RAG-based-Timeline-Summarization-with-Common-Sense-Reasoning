398. LETTER TO PYARELAL
SEVAGRAM,
August 3, 1945
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have your note.
Kishorelal and Rajaji are writing to you. What then shall I write ?
Not writing to you is as good as writing.
Blessings from
BAPU
