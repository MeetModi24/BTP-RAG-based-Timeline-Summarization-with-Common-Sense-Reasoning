381. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 2, 1930
CHI. RAMACHANDRA1,
I was glad to have your letter. Boys of your age spin with
proficiency, recite the Gita and understand the Ramayana. What
do you study? How much do you spin in an hour and what is the
count?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5259
