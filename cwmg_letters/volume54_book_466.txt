466. LETTER TO SUDHIR MITRA
April 14, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 10th instant. I doubt very much if
a monthly magazine exclusively devoted to untouchability is called
for. You would therefore forgive me for not blessing your ven-
ture in anticipation.
Yours sincerely,
SF. SUDHIR MITRA
MITRA COTTAGE
2 KALI LANE
KALIGHAT
