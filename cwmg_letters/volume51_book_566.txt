566. LETTER TO V. RAMJEE RAO
November 5, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of the 1st inst. Go on with the work with
Rama's name on your lips and all obstacles will be removed. You
will see the statements on anti-untouchability work that I am issu-
ing from day to day. Please read them carefully and digest them.
They should allow enough guidance to the anti-untouchability
worker.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 4579
