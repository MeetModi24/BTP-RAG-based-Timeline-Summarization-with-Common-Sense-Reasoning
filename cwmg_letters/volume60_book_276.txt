276. LETTER TO RAM KRISHNA BHARDWAJ
February 11, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I am glad that the institution which you are having will
be under the aegis of the Sanatan Dharam Educational Board
and that it has been decided to have Harijan girls side by side
with the others.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI RAM KRISHNA BHARDWAJ
GENERAL SECRETARY
SANATAN DHARAM SABHA
QLLA GUJJAR SINGH, LAHORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
