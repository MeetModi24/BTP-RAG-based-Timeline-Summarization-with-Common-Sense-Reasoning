142. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
[JOHANNESBURG,]
May 21, 1908
CHI. MAGANLAL,
I have received your letter. You need not worry about me. I
think I shall have to sacrifice myself. I do not believe that Smuts can
play foul to the end. But it gives an opportunity to those who have
reached the limits of their patience and are ready to strike at me. If
that should happen, we need not be unhappy. If I have to give my
life for a cause which I consider to be good, what better death can there
be?
If God found it fit to take away Gokaldas, why should the idea
of death make us sorrowful? This world is transient. If, therefore,
I leave this world, why should one be worried on that account? It
should be enough to wish that nothing improper is done by me as long
as I live. We should of course be careful that we do nothing improper
even by mistake. True, I have not yet reached the stage when I can
attain liberation but I do believe that if I leave this body while treading
the path along which my thoughts are nowadays running, I shall be
reborn and speedily attain to moksha at the end of that life.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati: Mahatma Gandhijina Patro, ed. D. M. Patel, Sevak Karya-
laya, Ahmedabad; 1921
