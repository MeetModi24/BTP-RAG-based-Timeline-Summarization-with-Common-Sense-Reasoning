343. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
WARDHA,
July 7, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
Where is the need for you to reason any further with Harilal?
Can we compel him to be virtuous? It, therefore, seems to me
that you need do nothing except pass his letters to me and mine
to him. You may see what you can do when he repents again.
Kanu recovered from fever long ago. Please rest assured
that he is perfectly at ease here. Neither you nor Jamna nor Puru-
shottam need worry in the least about him. He is working con-
stantly under my eyes.
Blessings from
BAPU
