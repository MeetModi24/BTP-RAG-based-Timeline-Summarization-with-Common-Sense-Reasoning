100. LETTER TO H. G. JAGIRDAR
September 28, 1934
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter. It was with difficulty that I gave a mes-
sage to the Columbia Company. I am sorry that I could not
undertake to give any more messages.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI H. G. JAGIRDAR
RUBY RECORD Co., CHURCHGATE HOUSE
CHURCHGATE STREET, FORT, BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
