296. LETTER TO UMIA AGRAWAL
SEVAGRAM,
October 22, 1944
CHI. UMIA,
Why do you reprimand me ? I wanted to give you a good husband.
Have I not given you one ? I can arrange for divorce if you do not
like him. There are always ups and downs in life. They temper us.
You will make me feel ashamed by feeling defeated like that. You
will hurt the spirit of Jamnalalji and also make Jaya feel ashamed. Why
are you becoming a coward ? I have imagined you to be brave. May
the New Year be fruitful to both of you.
Blessings from
BAPU
