356. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
April 20, 1945
CHI. MRIDULA,
I did write the preface for Tandon. Later there was a complaint
about his behaviour at Swaraj Bhavan, and that dampened me
somewhat. But I have not abandoned the thing. Now you will throw
more light on the matter.
You will certainly come to Mahabaleshwar.
197
Look after your health. It would be good if you do not work beyond
your capacity.
Blessings from
BAPU
