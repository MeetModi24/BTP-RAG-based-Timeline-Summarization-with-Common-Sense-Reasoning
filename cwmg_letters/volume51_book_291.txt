291. LETTER TO SAMUEL FRANCIS
October 3, 1932
MY DEAR SAMUEL,
I was glad to hear from you. I do not know what I, a
prisoner, can do for you. But if your children will take to the
Indian life, it is possible that they may be admitted to the
Ashram at Sabarmati.
Yours sincerely,
M. K . GANDHI
SAMUEL FRANCIS, ESQ.
C/O J. T. CHRISTIAN, ESQ.
8 MART LANE, EAST LONDON
SOUTH AFRICA
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. III, p. 201
