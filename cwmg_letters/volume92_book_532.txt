532. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
May 16, 1932
CHI. PUSHPA,
Premabehn would never refuse to reply. You must ask
your question politely.
You must learn Hindi, because that is the language spoken
in the major part of India.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11079. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
