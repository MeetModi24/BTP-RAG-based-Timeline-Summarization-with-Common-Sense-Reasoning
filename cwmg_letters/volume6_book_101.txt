101. LETTER TO AMEER ALI
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 5, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I have your note of the 3rd instant. Mr. Ally I expect here today
from Bromley, and he and I will be pleased to wait on you at the
Reform Club to-morrow at 4 p.m.
I am,
Yours faithfully,
AMEER ALI, ESQ., C.I.E.
THE LAMBDENS
BEENHAM
NEAR READING
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4485
