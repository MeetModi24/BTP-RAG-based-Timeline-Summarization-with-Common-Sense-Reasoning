142. LETTER TO DR. B. C. ROY
AS AT THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
December 13, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
The enclosed has remained with me for a long time, buried
among the papers which I was then unable to attend to. As I
have a few moments to spare to go through arrears, I have come
across the enclosed which I send you for such remarks as you may
wish to make.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19750
