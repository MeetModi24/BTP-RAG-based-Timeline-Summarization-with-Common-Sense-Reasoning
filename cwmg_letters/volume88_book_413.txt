413. LETTER TO SWAMI SIVANANDA
NEW DELHI,
July 21, 1947
SHRI SWAMIJI,
I have your letter. There was violence in the name of
non-violence and we are now tasting its bitter fruit. What more
can I write?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SWAMI SIVANANDA
RISHIKESH
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
