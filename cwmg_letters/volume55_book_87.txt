87. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
May 2, 1933
MY DEAR BROTHER,
Dare I ask for your blessings for the coming yajna2? It is now
nearing 2 a.m. I have left my bed among other things to write
this begging letter. If the fast does not meet with your appro-
val, I know you are too true a brother to grant my request
merely to please me.
With love,
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
Letters of Srinivasa Sastri, p. 252
