14. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
December 19, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I got your letter. Is Kripalani all right? Kanti and others
will meet me in a few days. Pyarelal tells me that his books about
the rules governing sandhi and samas in Sanskrit are with you, or
that you know where they are. Send them here. He requires them
for a systematic study of the Gita. Both of us keep good health. So
far, rotlas of jowar and bajra seem to have agreed with me.
I give detailed news about my health in the letter meant for
all, and so I do not write about it separately in each letter.
You can send letters to Pyarelal through Trivedi .
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1814
