91. LETTER TO PADMA
WARDHA,
October 11, 1933
CHI. PADMA,
I got your letter. I hope all of you are all right now. Give
me a description of the accommodation. My weight has now
gone up to 103 pounds. My diet includes milk, fruit and vege-
tables such as gourd, snakegourd, tomatoes, etc.
After a few days Anandi, Babu and Nirmala will leave for
Ahmedabad. At present there are about 40 girls here. The
girls under 12 willingly got their hair cut yesterday. I have
been trying to persuade the rest.
You cannot be blamed for the doctor having given you an
Injection of brandy. You did not drink the thing for pleasure.
Moreover, an injection of brandy is not as objectionable as a
vaccine. I don't know any remedy for your complaint of the
ear-drum. You should show it to a doctor, or a vaid who knows
the remedy that can help you.
My tour will commence on November 8. Till then I shall
remain here. I hope Sheela is enjoying her time.
Blessings from
BAPU
