389. LETTER TO SHAMBHUSHANKAR TRIVEDI
November 7, 1939
Your letter called for no reply. Keep your pledge. One
must certainly put up hedges round oneself to be able to keep
a pledge, but how long one should cling to them, even if one breaks
down, should be decided by each one for himself. What one
should do about others who fail to keep their pledges depends on
each individual case. Is politics [at your place] improving?
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
