305. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
BARDOLI,
May 30, 1931
CHI. GANGABEHN,
Why do you feel so unhappy? After all, no great tragedy has
struck you down. There is no law that no one shall die young.
Moreover, you and I have not one child, but countless children.
Some of them will die and others will be born to take their place.
Why, then, keep count of who dies and who lives? We should
do our best to serve those who happen to be near us. It will then
be the same to us that someone lives and another dies.
If the secretary has made the necessary arrangements, we need
not think about the matter any further.
Cultivate non-attachment.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
