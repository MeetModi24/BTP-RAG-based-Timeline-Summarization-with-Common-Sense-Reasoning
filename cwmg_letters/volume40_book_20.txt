20. LETTER TO BECHAR PARMAR
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 23, 1929
BHAISHRI BECHAR,
I have your letter. As for the Antyaja teacher you mention,
if he puts off marriage and is a man of character, he can be
taken in the Vidyapith and a scholarship too may be given to
him. If he is agreeable, an application stating all the facts may
be forwarded to Kakasaheb along with your covering letter.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5577
