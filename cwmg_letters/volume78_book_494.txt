494. LETTER TO SHIVLAL GUPTA
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1944
BHAI SHIVLAL GUPTAJI,
It is from the Provincial Congress Committee that you should
ask for help for Bhai Sant Ram. I am helpless.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHIVLAL GUPTA
JAIPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
