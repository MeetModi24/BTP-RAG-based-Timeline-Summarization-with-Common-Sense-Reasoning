7. LETTER TO SHAVAKSHA
October 12, 1941
BHAI SHAVAKSHA,
I envy you. Where is 84 years and where 72? And you run
the mill in full steam and a young man like me remains idle! Let
God's will be done and let us be thankful to Him. Received both
your books. The beautifully printed one must indeed be costly. I
will make full use of it. Because while you may run one or two
mills I ply thousands of mills and plan to ply millions.
If a junior may bless a senior here are
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
