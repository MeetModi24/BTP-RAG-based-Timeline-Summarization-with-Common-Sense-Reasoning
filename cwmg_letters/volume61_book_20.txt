20. LETTER TO VIJAYA GANDHI
April 28, 1935
CHI. VIJAYA 1 ,
I learnt from Jamna's letter, received yesterday, that you had
survived a serious illness. You should now learn the art of keeping
healthy. You should eat food as medicine and not for the pleasure
of the palate. Never eat factory-made sugar. Eat daily with your
food fresh leaves of a bhaji like fenugreek and juicy fruit like or-
ange or grapes. Learn to breathe properly. And you must take
some exercise. Keep in open air as take as possible.
Write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. VIJAYABEHN
C/O SHRI HARAKHCHAND MOTICHAND
CHORWAD
SONGADH STATE
KATHIAWAR
