457. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Sunday [January 31, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
None of you should worry about me. I have no fever now
and I am gradually gaining strength. I shall not write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
WINDY HALL
NASIK ROAD
DEOLALI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
