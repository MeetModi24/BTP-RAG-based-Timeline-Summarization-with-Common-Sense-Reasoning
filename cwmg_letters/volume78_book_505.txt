505. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN PATHAK
SEVAGRAM,
November 29, 1944
BHAI RAMNARAYAN,
I had asked you to write again regarding the Hindustani
Prachar Sabha. There were other letters too, on the basis of
which I have issued a statement which both of you should see,
and write to me if you have anything to say. You ought to get
some guidance.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAMNARAYAN PATHAK
ELLISBRIDGE
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
