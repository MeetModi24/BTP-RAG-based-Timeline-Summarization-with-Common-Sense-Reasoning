242. LETTER TO VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 16, 1940
CHI. VALLABHRAM,
Your settling down in Bombay means that you cannot pay
your way in Ahmedabad. If it is so what a sad state of affairs!
You must have gone to Poona and examined Valjibhai. I
have advised him that when you are ready to call him to Bom-
bay he should go.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VALLABHRAM VAID
1756 JUGAL BHUVAN
GANDHI ROAD
AHMEDABAD
From Gujarati: C.W. 2910. Courtesy: Vallabhram Vaidya
