406. LETTER TO LADY ABBAS ALI BAIG
'DILKHUSHA' ,
June 2, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
Many thanks for your greetings and mangoes to which I
hope to do justice. I shall see to the correction of the name of
the place.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
LADY ABBAS ALI BAIG
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
