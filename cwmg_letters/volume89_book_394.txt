394. LETTER TO JAL PAVRY
NEW DELHI,
October 24, 1947
BHAI JAL PAVRY,
I have your letter. You cannot imagine the burden I am
carrying. I did not know about your father's death. It was
good that you informed me. He lives even though he is no
more. Why grieve then? Let us who are left behind continue
his mission of service.
Yours,
M. K . GANDHI
DR. JAL PAVRY, M.A.,PH.D.
TAJ MAHAL HOTEL
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10826. Courtesy: Bapsybanoo
Pavry
