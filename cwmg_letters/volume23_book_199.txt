199. LETTER TO DWIJENDRANATH TAGORE
ANDHERI,
March 22, 1924
DEAR BORODADA,
It was good of you to send me two copies of your essays on
the Bhagavad Gita. I note with gratitude your affectionate inscrip-
tion in one copy. I shall prize it and endeavour at the earliest
opportunity to understand your interpretation of the message of
the Gita.
Mr. Andrews always gives me your kind messages. His pre-
sence is a great comfort to me. It was so good of you to have
let him come to me.
With much respect,
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 8564
