64. LETTER TO MANUBEHN S. MASHRUWALA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 9, 1940
CHI. MANUDI,
I got your letter. Why need you give your address, since the
whole world knows you? If, therefore, I merely write Bombay, will
not the letter reach you? Or do you perhaps think that I must have
your address by heart? Ba is all right. Do you wish to come here
in Ashadh? You know that you will be welcome whenever you wish
to come. Kunvarji is keeping good health. Sharda is still here. The
boy is fine. His eczema persists but he is growing. It is very hot.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2676. Courtesy: Kanubhai N. Mashruwala
