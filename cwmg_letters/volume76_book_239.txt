239. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
June 8, 1942
CHI. KAKA,
I got your letter. Since there is already an agreement be-
tween us made long ago that you should consult me before taking
up anything new, where is the need of a fresh one? You have
assumed heavy responsibilities, and the work about Hindustani
is not an easy one either. However, you must fulfil this respon-
sibility. Do it as well as you can. You must categorically
refuse to go anywhere else, whether it is to Vanasthali or Ved-
chhi or Ambala. If you cannot remember the decisions taken by
you, you should ask either Amritlal or Saroj to remind you and
to get them implemented.
Blessings from
BAPU
