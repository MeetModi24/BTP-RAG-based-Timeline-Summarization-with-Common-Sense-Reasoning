239. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
September 22, 1947
CHI. JIVANJI,
I have revised the accompanying in great hurry. All helpers
are new and inexperienced and I have, therefore, to put up
with considerable inconvenience. But I will live as He wills.
The original English by Valjibhai is also enclosed. Check
the translation carefully with it. I think it would be better to
ask Valjibhai himself to supply the Gujarati translation of his
English articles.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 6958. Courtesy: Jivanji D. Desai
