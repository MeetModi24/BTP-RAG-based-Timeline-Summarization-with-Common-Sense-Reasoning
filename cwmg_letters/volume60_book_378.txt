378. LETTER TO JAYARAMDAS JAYAWARDHANE
March 8, 1935
MY DEAR JAYARAMDAS,
I have your letter of the 3rd instant. You have now given
me what I can call definite information in as brief a form as
possible. I have already put myself in touch with a medical
man. I shall see what is possible to do. Meantime send me full
particulars. How many volunteers are working in your Mandal?
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
