126. LETTER TO M.
October 2, 1934
MY DEAR M.,
I have your letters. I hope you duly received mine which
has . . .2
I see from one of your letters that you have again lost the
conviction of guilt. You seem to doubt your untruthfulness. Do
you not realize that you were regarded by the responsible per-
sons as almost a saint, in purity! But who am I to judge you,
if you cannot yourself? God alone is the infallible Judge. May
He show the way. I can but pray that you and we may see the
Light and follow it.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
