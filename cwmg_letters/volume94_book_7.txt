7. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
July 20, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no post from you today. Taribehn must have arrived there.
Examine her and let me know.
You had mentioned something about breaking a glass. How crazy
you are ! If you go on paying every time something breaks, all your
funds will be exhausted. Hence, give up that idea.
Kanchan and Kusum Desai have come today.
Blessings from
BAPD
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
