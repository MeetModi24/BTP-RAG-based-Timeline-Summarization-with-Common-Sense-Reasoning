552. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
September 24, 1938
CHI. BABUDI,
I have your letter. If you write 'Personal' no one will read
your letter. Do write whatever you have in mind. Are you
going on with the Gita and music? You have not said anything
about your health. Give me full details.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9997. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokhawala
