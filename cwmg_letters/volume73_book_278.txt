278. LETTER TO PREMNATH BAJAJ
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 13, 1940
DEAR PREMNATH,
I have your letter. On the face of it the order about Hindi
and Urdu seems quite harmless. But I can give no final opinion
without knowing all sides of the question. And if at all possible
you will spare me the trouble of studying it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
