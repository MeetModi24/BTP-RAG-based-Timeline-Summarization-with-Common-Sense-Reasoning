541. LETTER TO MANILAL B. DESAI
January 31, 1947
CHI. MANILAL,
I have your letter. You must have received my long letter.
I believe that Maganbhai should be included among the Trustees.
Show this to Dinshawji and Jehangirji. If possible, I shall write
to them separately. Otherwise use this letter. I am writing to
Maganbhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 2733. Courtesy: Manilal B. Desai
