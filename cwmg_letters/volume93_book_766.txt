766. LETTER TO DR. ABRAHAM PAUL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, (C. P.),
June 29, 1941
DEAR ABRAHAM,
Rajkumariji has gone to Simla. I have seen your letter
to her. I would like you personally to visit Sevagram and pass
about a week and see things. I could then decide if I can
make use of your services. And you would also be better able
to judge whether you would like the work here.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. ABRAHAM PAUL
THOPPAL HOUSE
P. O. PARUR
TRAVANCORE
From a photostat: C. W. 10980. Courtesy: Dr. Abraham Paul
