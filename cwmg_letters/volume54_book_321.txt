321. LETTER TO PARASHURAM MEHROTRA
April 2, 1933
CHI. PARASHURAM,
Teach Rajbhoj well and see that he becomes proficient.
Marybehn and Duncanbhai missed you very much.
You should not hesitate to write to me anything that comes
to your mind. I wish to get plenty of work out of you, if you are
ready to give it. And for that I must know your mind completely.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7513. Also C.W. 4990. Courtesy:
Parashuram Mehrotra
