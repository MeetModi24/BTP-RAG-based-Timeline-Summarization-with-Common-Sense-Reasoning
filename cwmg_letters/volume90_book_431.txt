431. LETTER TO KHURSHED NAOROJEE
NEW DELHI,
January 21, 1948
DEAR SISTER 3 ,
So your hope has been fulfilled. I am drinking not merely
water which I was [taking] when on fast, [but also] sweet
fruit juices, clear vegetable soup and milk.
Though or because
I am taking substantial things, the zone of calm has been left
for that of storm.
I hope now you have got the work for which you are most
fitted.
Love.
BAPU
KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJEE
KANYA MAHAVIDYALAYA
JULLUNDER
