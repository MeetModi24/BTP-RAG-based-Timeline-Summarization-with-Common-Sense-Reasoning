406. LETTER TO NARMADABEHN RANA
April 9, 1933
CHI. NARMADA,
You should not write in a scrawl. Write neatly in big
letters.
I note that you had taken away the carrion from them with
their consent. They eat carrion even though there are plenty of
other things to eat because they have come to relish it.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 2776. Courtesy: Ramnarayana N. Pathak
