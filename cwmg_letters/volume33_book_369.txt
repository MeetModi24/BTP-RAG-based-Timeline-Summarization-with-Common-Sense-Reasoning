369. LETTER TO NARMADA
NANDI DURG,
Vaishakha Krishna 6 [May 22, 1927]
CHI. NARMADA,
I had replied to your letter but I had sent it to your school
address because I did not know your full name and address. You
must have received that letter.
I did not know you were to be married so soon. I hope
you would continue your studies and that you would not give
up the noble patriotic feelings that you had expressed to me and
also conveyed through your letter and that your husband would
let you cherish them. Never give up khadi and the spinning-
wheel. . . .1
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4755
1 Illegible
