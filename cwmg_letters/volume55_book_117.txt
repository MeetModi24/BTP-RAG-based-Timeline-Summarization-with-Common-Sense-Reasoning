117. LETTER TO E. E. DOYLE
May 5, 1933
DEAR COL. DOYLE,
I would like, if you can give permission today, to write
daily to and hear from Mrs. Gandhi, Shrimati Mirabehn (Slade,
Sabarmati), Shrimati Manibehn Patel, (Belgaum), Shrimati
Vasumatibehn Pandit (Thana), Sjt. Pyarelal (Nasik) and Sjt.
Surendraji (Visapur), as I used to during the September fast.
I have restrained myself up to now. But I know how anxious
friends must be as Monday approaches.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Government of Bombay, Home Department, I.G.P. File No. 10, 1933
