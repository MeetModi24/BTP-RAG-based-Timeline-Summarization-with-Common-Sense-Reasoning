54. LETTER TO MADALASA BAJAJ
November 22, 1932
CHI. MADALASA1,
I must say that your handwriting is improving very much.
Your plan of studies is also very good. See that you do not
work beyond your strength. If you neglect your health for the
sake of study, both will suffer. Since you know that anger is bad,
you are bound to become free from it in the course of time. And
the same about pride. It is a sign of weakness that you start crying
at every little thing. If you start playing games, your crying will
stop. Begin reciting the Gita loudly whenever you feel that you are
about to begin crying; you will then immediately forget about
crying. Try this method.
Why do you say that no one lives in a temple the whole day
and night? The priest certainly does so.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 471
