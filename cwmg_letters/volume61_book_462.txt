462. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
August 9, 1935
CHI. LILAVATI,
You should improve your handwriting still further. It is not
proper that you are not keeping the rule.
You have certainly done well to reduce your expenses. If you
have patience, everything will be well. Don't reduce the expenses
to a level which you may not be able to keep up. Take every
step after careful thought.
If you do not follow any of the explanations I give in your
exercises, ask me again. If you use sheets of the same quality and
size and keep a margin for comments, the exercises will be easier
to preserve.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 10103. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
