246. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
FROM M. K. GANDHI
AHMEDABAD,
November 6 [1916]
MY DEAR FRIEND,
Today is the Hindu year's last day. We are therefore all busy as
you may imagine. I can only therefore send you our greetings and wish
that we may soon join.
With love from all,
Yours,
OLD FRIEND
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy : National
Archives of India
1 The quotation from John Milton's Paradise Lost, I, 253, reads thus in full :
"The mind is its own place and in itself can make a heaven of hell, a hell of heaven."
2 The letter has been placed here serial-wise among those of 1916.
