13. LETTER TO DR. PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
December [19]4, 1934
DEAR DR. PATTABHI,
This is village paper. The ink is village-made, and the pen
is made of village reed. Do the villagers manufacture paper there?
If so, at what price?
I have your full letter. Yes, we must meet. Since you have
time, you have to be humble enough to ask for the responsibility
you can shoulder — work of the highest order, with or without
office, whichever is better for your work. In this service of the
destitute, there is no room for ceremony.
And this business of rice, flour, gur, oil, ghee, etc., is a vast
business. You have to revive your knowledge of medicine.
There are two ways of doing the thing
by compulsion
—
through State organization, or voluntary effort, i.e., organized
honesty or non-violence . . .1.
Yours sincerefy,
M. K. GANDHI
Incidents of Gandhiji's Life, p. 224
