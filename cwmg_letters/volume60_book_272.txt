272. LETTER TO GIRIJA BHUSHAN DUTT
February 11, 1935
DEAR GIRIJABABU,
I have your letter. I have never asked Harijans to give
up their poultry or anything else but carrion, beef-eating and
intoxicants. They are certainly at liberty to take poultry, goats,
sheep and whatever else they like and whatever other Hindus
can lawfully take.
It will certainly be a great thing if you succeed in getting
Harijan weavers to do their own spinning amongst their families.
Everyone of such families will always be able to keep the wolf
from the door if they will adopt this method of combining
spinning with weaving. I suggested it to nearly 10,000 weavers
during the march near Sakhigopal.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI GIRIJA BHUSHAN DUTT
ANGUL HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH
ANGUL (ORISSA)
