65. LETTER TO BAHLOL KHAN
[Before April 6, 1937]
Do not fear I can ever oppose Urdu. Of course I know
not how and in what other way I can help or work for its prog-
ress. But I think the very fact that I am not opposing it is
enough—I don't think I could do anything more beyond that.
The Bombay Chronicle, 6-4-1937
