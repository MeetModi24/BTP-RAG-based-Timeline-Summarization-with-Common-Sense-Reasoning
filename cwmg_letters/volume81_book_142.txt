142. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
[On or after August 7, 1945]
CHI. A.
You must get well quick and if you can get well more quickly
living with me, you should follow me to Poona.
I do not
know how long I shall be there. I have written to Atulanand
and that at length. Probably you will have a copy of my letter
to him.
Love.
BAPU
SHRIMATI RAJKUMARI
SIMLA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
