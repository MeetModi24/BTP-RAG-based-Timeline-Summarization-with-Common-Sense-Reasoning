234. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
August 30, 1909
MY LORD,
In order not to unnecessarily worry you, I have refrained from
acknowledging your last two letters.
May I invite your attention to the statement made yesterday to
Reuter's Agent on the Indian question by General Smuts? What can
be the meaning of the statement? Does it mean that the General will
decide after he has reached Pretoria, and if so, what is our duty?
I remain, etc.,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5034
