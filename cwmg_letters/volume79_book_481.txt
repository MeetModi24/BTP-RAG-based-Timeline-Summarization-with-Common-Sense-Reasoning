481. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
SEVAGRAM,
March 21, 1945
MY DEAR ICE,
Your letter. Never mind Aundh.
I have wired you 2 to
come at once as I am in Bombay in April. I hope to present
you with some patients. Give me satisfaction, the rest will follow
as day follows night.
Yours,
BAPU
DR. M. S. KELKAR
R. J. PATKAR
MANORAMAGANJ
INDORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
