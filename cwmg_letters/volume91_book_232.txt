232. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
[April 1, 1920]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Although a translation of the Punjab report is carried by
Gujarati, it will be better to have a translation of our own.
Send six copies of the report and of the evidence to Vaman-
rao, the headman. He will pay for the same. Send him the
bill. I am finding much peace here. So I spend all the time
writing letters. You must have got back your harmonium from
Revashankarbhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Why have you given your address as 93 Bazar Gate Street?
