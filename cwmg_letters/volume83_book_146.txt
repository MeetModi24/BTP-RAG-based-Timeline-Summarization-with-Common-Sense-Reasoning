146. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
February 15, 1946
BHAISHRI,
In reply to your letter I should say that your ideal can be
reached through a knowledge of nature cure. I am of the opinion
that nature cure is at present in a very imperfect state.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
PURUSHOTTAMBHAI PATEL
69 BARODA COLLEGE HOSTEL
BARODA
