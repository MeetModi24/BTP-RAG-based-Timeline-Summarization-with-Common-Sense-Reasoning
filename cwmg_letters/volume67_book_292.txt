292. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
July 30, 1938
CHI. JAMNALAL,
You have told somebody here that you wish to come and
stay here. If you wish to come, everything is of course ready.
But if you are not coming, I should like to keep Kishorelal here
for some time. But this does not mean that you should drop the
idea of coming. I will ask Kishorelal only if you are not coming.
I wish you to visit Ramana Maharshi as early as possible.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2995
