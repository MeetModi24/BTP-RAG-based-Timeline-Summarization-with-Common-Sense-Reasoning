482. LETTER TO RAMKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday [May 1, 1926]
BHAI RAMKRISHNAJI 2 ,
Brijkrishna gave me news of the passing of your elder daugh-
ter. I was grieved. May God grant you and your mother peace.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SHRI RAMKRISHNAJI
C/O SHRI BRIJKRISHNA
KATRA KHUSHALRAI
SHRI NIVAS
DELHI
From the Hindi original: Brijkrishna Chandiwala Papers. Courtesy:
Gandhi National Museum and Library
