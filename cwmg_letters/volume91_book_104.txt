104. LETTER TO SAM HIGGINBOTTOM
AHMEDABAD,
January 4, 1917
DEAR MR. HIGGINBOTTOM,
I have just returned to find the book so kindly sent by you.
I thank you for it. Can you now pay the promised visit to the
Ashram ? If you can come, will you address a public meeting?
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: Higginbottom Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
