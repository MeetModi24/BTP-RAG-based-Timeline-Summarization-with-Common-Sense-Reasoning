392. LETTER TO M. D. SHAHANE
November 14, 1934
MY DEAR SHAHANE,
I was delighted to receive your offer on behalf of the So-
ciety's C. P. Branch. You may know that I am already in corres-
pondence with Dravid. He will see me at the time that will
most suit him. And so far as I am concerned, all the members
can join him when he comes. The earlier the better. Meanwhile,
I am passing your letter on to Sjt. Kumarappa.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI M. D. SHAHANE, B.A.
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
CRADDOCK TOWN, NAGPUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
