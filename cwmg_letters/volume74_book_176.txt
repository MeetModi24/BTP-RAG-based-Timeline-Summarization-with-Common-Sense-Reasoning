176. LETTER TO JIVKRISHNA SHARMA
June 18, 1941
BHAI JIVKRISHNA SHARMA,
Your intention to hold an exhibition of the products of
khadi and village industries and to give away the total profit
to the Kamala Nehru Hospital is indeed praiseworthy. I wish
you success in the effort.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 89
