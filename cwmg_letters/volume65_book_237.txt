237. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 22, 1937
CHI. NARANDAS,
Please pay Chi. Kamu Rs. 30 from this month, i.e., from
the 1st. If you can debit part of the sum to the school, do so.
Debit the rest to my account.
Kamalabai doesn't seem to have recovered completely. She
has expressed a desire to stay on till I return to Segaon and I
have agreed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8522. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
