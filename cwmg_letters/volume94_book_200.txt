200. LETTER TO TEJ BAHADUR SAPRU
PALM BUN, JUHU,
May 14, 1944
DEAR SIR TEJ,
I showed your letter to Gandhiji. He was sorry to learn about your son's
serious illness and he hopes that he will be completely restored to health in the
bracing climate of Kashmere. I shall let you know as soon as he is declared free
to undertake public work.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SIR TEJ BAHADUR SAPRU
19 ALBERT ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 The addressee's third son, Anand, was suffering from paralysis of the whole
body excepting the head and the neck after taking anti-rabies injections under medical
advice.
