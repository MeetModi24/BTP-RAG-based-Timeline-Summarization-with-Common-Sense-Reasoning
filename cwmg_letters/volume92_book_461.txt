461. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
SIMLA,
July 20, 1931
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Lady Willingdon has reminded me of the saree she has
been promised. I have told her that it is being got ready. If
it is ready, send it on to her directly. Write to her as follows:
"I am sending you the saree promised by Mrs. Gandhi. I
hope you will like it. Please excuse delay."
My stay here has been extended. I may have to stay over
till Wednesday. The situation is certainly serious. Even if it
improves it will remain a patchwork effort. Everywhere we see
weakness. But then that is the way of the world. The present
situation is the result of his and our actions and intentions.
Shankerlal should keep himself in readiness in case I have
to go.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32791
