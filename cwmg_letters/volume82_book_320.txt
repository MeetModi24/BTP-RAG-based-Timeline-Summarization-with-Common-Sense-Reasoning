320. LETTER TO ARUN GANDHI
SODEPUR,
December 17, 1945
CHI. ARUN5,
I think of you every day, but especially today during silence.
Do you spin carefully at least 160 rounds daily? Is the yarn
even? Do you yourself fix the spinning-wheel? Do you keep a
daily account? If you keep this one promise, you will learn a lot.
Are you all right? How is Ila ? Has she become a little
wiser?
Blessings to all of you from
BAPU
