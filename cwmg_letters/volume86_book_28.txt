28. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
NEW DELHI,
October 23, 1946
CHI. KUSUM,
I do not know whether I shall be attending the Congress this
time. So forget me. If you wish to go there, make your own
arrangements. At present, I am preparing for a tour of Bengal.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KUSUMBEHN DESAI
MEHTA POLE
BARODA
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 1852
