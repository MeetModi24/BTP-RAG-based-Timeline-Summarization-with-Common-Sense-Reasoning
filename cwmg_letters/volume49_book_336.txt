336. LETTER TO CHAND TYAGI
April 5, 1932
BHAI TYAGIJI,
I was glad to get your letter and gladder still to learn that
you are nursing the plague patients. I am not taking milk these
days. Here I can do with bread, almonds, dates, vegetables and
lemons. My views are the same as before. I am awaiting a letter
from Devsharmaji My right hand pains only when writing, but
it has been like this for one year and there is nothing special.
Balvir's handwriting is good. Has he got rid of his former
faults?
Mahadev is with me. This card is in his hand.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3259
