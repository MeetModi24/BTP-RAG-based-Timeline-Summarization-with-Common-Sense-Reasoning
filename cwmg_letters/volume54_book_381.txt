381. LETTER TO MUNNALAL SHAH
April 7, 1933
BHAI MUNNALAL,
We were all happy to read your letter. Your impression is
correct. Describe those of your experiences in Pondicherry which
you found particularly interesting. I hope you have settled down
now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8647
