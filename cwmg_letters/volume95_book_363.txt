363. LETTER TO LALITA PATEL
CHI. LALITA,
I would certainly write letters to the girls who write to me. It
was good to receive your letter. Continue to write like that. Many
boys were caught telling lies. Have you also been caught along with
them? Write to me if you are given to telling lies. There is nothing
to hide in that.
LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI N. PATEL
247
Tell Kamala that I had expected much from her. But right now
I see very little of it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : G.N. 9219
