304. LETTER TO MANGALSINGH
August 10, 1924
DEAR SARDAR MANGALSINGHJI,
This will introduce to you Mr. Valji Desai who has been a
co-worker for a long time. He went to jail soon after me on the
same charge. Please help Mr. Desai in any manner that may
enable him to stay in the Punjab and study things.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 5996. Courtesy: V. G. Desai
