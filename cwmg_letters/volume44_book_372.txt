372. LETTER TO UPTON SINCLAIR
YERAVDA CENTRAL PRISON,
October 30, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
I read your Mammonart 1 with absorbing interest and Mental
Radio with curiosity. The former has given me much to think,
the latter did not interest me. Nobody in India would, I think,
doubt the possibility of telepathy but most would doubt the wis-
dom of its material use.
I will now avail myself of your kind offer and ask you to
send me your other volumes or such as you think I should read.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
UPTON SINCLAIR, ESQ.
STATIONA P., PASADENA
CALIFORNIA
From a photostat: G.N. 2552
