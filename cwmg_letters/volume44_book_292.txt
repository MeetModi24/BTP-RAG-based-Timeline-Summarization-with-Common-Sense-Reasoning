292. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 10, 1930
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA,
I have your letter. My congratulations to Chandan. You
also should give your name for the competition. If you get the
prize, you may use the money for helping somebody. Those who
do not require any incentive such as a competition provides, should
none the less join one for the sake of other people. My blessings
to Chandan, Tara and Vasant.
Blessings from
BAPU
