241. LETTER TO KANTA R. VYAS
February 19, 1945
CHI. KANTA,
I think of you often. Only today I have come to know that
you have not been keeping good health. I did not like letting
you leave this place but I was helpless. May God protect you
and your child. Write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
KANTA RAMPRASAD VYAS
DOHAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
