303. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
SABARMATI,
January 25, 1922
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I still think that the Conference has
done much good. It has cleared the air and defined our posi-
tion as never before. Sir Sankaran's action will be forgotten.
But the attitude of non-co-operators will, I think, be remem-
bered to their credit.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: C. Vijayaraghavachariar Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
