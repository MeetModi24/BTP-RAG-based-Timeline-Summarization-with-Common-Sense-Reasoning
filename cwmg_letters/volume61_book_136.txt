136. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
May 24, 1935
CHI. MARY,
I had a talk about you with Khurshed. Her whole house
will be practically vacated. If therefore you would rather go to
Miraj, do by all means go and that as early as you can. Of
course there is no difficulty about your stay elsewhere in Bombay
if you go simply for examination. You will do whatever is best
for your health.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6042. Also C.W. 3371. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
