561. LETTER TO JAYANTI N. PAREKH
September 5, 1935
CHI. JAYANTI,
I am surprised that you have not been able to understand
my step. What I have said is that all my activities have swaraj as
their goal. Every breath I take is dedicated to it. To reach that
goal sooner I left Yeravda Mandir and took up Harijan work
and have now taken up village industries work. Worship of any
God is worship of Lord Krishna.
It is now your duty to help Kanti. You two brothers should
take up some honest business and free Nathubhai from debts.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 6262
