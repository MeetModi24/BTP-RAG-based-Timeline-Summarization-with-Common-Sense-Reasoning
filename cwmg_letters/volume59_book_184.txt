184. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
October 7, 1934
I have your letter enclosing a letter from Shri Krishnamoor-
thy Iyer and [a] cheque for Rs. 2,000. I am sending him a
receipt directly, and sending the cheque to Thakkar Bapa with
instructions to send it to you to dispose of it in accordance with
the suggestion contained in your letter. That seems to be the best
way of giving full effect to the intention of the donor.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
