411. LETTER TO KEDARNATH CHATTERJEE
SEVAGRAM,
March 13, 1945
BHAI KEDARBABU,
Will you on my behalf place flowers on Deenabandhu's tomb
on April 5? Benarsidas has reminded me. Please tell him that
I have acted on his suggestion.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
KEDARNATH CHATTERJEE
"VISHAL BHARAT"
CALCUTTA
