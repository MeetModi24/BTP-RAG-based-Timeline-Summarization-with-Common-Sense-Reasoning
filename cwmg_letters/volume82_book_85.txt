85. LETTER TO SUBROTO ROY CHOWDHARY
November 10, 1945
BHAI SUBRATA ROY 2 ,
I received your letter of October 24 yesterday. I hope you
will forgive me for replying in the national language. Yours is a
big programme. It is good. What can be added to it if I say
something? May you succeed and may all of you serve India in
the right way.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
