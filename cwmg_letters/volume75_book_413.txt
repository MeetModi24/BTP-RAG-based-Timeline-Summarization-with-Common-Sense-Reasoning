413. LETTER TO KULSUM SAYANI
February 21, 1942
DEAR KULSUM 3 ,
I inadvertently began the letter in English.
I shall convey
your feelings to Janakibehn.
My congratulations on your doing the work of teaching.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Begum Kulsum Sayani Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
