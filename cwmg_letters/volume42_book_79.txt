79. LETTER TO B. L. RALLIA RAM
October 30, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
You have asked the wrong man for naming the best book he
might have read during the year. The book I get the time to read
is the book of Nature which is not to be found on any book-stall.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. B. L. RALLIA RAM
NATIONAL COUNCIL, Y.M.C.A.
5 RUSSELL STREET, CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 15706
