461. LETTER TO SUDHIR CHANDRA CHAKRAVARTY
April 14, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I can do nothing. But you should
approach first of all the Provincial Untouchability Board and then,
if you like, the Central Board.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SUDHIR CHANDRA CHAKRAVARTY
10/ NEPAL BHATTACHERJI ST.
KALIGHAT
From a microfilm: S.N. 20936
