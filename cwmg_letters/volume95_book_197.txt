197. LETTER TO MOHAMMED HUSAIN KHAN
[After July 5, 1947]
I have your two letters. As I have intimated to you, I sent your
letter, written in English, to the Bihar Government the moment it
came. I shall write to you when I get the reply. If it is found
necessary to see you, I shall trouble you.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
