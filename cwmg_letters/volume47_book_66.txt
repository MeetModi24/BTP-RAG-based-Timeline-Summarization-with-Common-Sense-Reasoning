66. LETTER TO J. J. SINGH
BORSAD,
June 24, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your lucidly written letter of June 5th. I
agree in the main with all you say. The rumour about my wear-
ing European costume if I went to London is as ill-founded as
my rumoured visit to America. All the same I appreciate the
motive lying behind your letter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Hindustan Times, 27-4-1969
