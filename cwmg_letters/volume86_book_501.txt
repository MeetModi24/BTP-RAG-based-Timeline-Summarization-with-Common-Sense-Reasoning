501. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 22, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. I understand about Sushilabehn. The
copy which you want is enclosed herewith.
Amritlalji wants to call Abha back after she is relieved of
attendance on you. She may remain as long as she is with you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 570
