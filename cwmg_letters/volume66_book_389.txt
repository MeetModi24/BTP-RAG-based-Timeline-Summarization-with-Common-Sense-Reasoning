389. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
December 8, 1937
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I should like you to tell me why you have no peace of
mind. What could be the cause? Whatever it may be, you
must change. If you wish to go out for some time, when is
it to be and for how long? Let me know your weight. Write
about whatever other things you have omitted.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10742
