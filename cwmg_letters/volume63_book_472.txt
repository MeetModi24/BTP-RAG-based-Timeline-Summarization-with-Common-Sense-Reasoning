472. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
SEGAON,
October 13, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
When you have already placed your head at my feet, where
is the question of forgetting?
I think that the article should go as it is. We had better
let him proclaim his dissatisfaction rather than anticipate his
argument. There is no harm in letting the article go as it stands,
for it is, according to me, a fine thing that the same old argument
should have occurred to me involuntarily even today though
I had forgotten the subject. Haven't I said before that I have
no matter with me? I am certainly not sorry that I can spare
no time from amidst the patients. All is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
You should derive full satisfaction from the services assigned
to you.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11496
