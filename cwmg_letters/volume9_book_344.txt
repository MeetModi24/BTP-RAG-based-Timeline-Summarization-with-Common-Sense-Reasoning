344. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
November 10, 1909
MY LORD,
I am much obliged to Your Lordship for your letter of today. As
you propose to make use of the information about the interview at the
forthcoming discussion in the House of Lords, I quite agree that the
minutes should be sent to Lord Crewe for confirmation or . . .
I am delighted to learn that your son is making good progress.
I remain, etc.,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5172
