381. LETTER TO SOHANLAL DWIVEDI
SEVAGRAM,
November 11, 1944
BHAI SOHANLAL,
I have had a talk with Ghanshyamdasji. I hope you have
calmed down. The incident has shown me a new way. May I
hope the same has happened to you? A few friends, if not many,
have understood my point.
Blessings from
BAPU
SOHANLAL DWIVEDI
