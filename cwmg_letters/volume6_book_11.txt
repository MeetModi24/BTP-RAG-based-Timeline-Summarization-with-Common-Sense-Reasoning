11. LETTER TO A. H. GOOL
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 25, 1906
DEAR MR. GOOL,
Your father has asked me particularly to see you before I return
to Johannesburg. My arrangements do not allow of my paying friendly
calls at present, and lest I may be too much engaged during the whole
of my stay, may I ask you to give me a call at the above address
between 9 and 9.30 in the morning, any day. The day is occupied with
paying visits, and I am never sure when I am in. I hope you are
getting on well.
Yours truly,
A. H. GOOL, ESQ.
27, PECKHAM ROAD, S.E.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4394
