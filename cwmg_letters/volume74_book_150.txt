150. LETTER TO NATVARLAL J. VEPARI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 1, 1941
BHAI VEPARI,
When is the inspection of Harijan accounts likely to be finished?
If your health permits, I should like you to finish it as soon as
possible.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
June 2, 1941
The above was written yesterday, after the time for sending
the post. I got your letter today. I certainly feel that, if you sent
an abstract of the [account] books, I would attend to the rest, for
in any case I will have finally to go through the accounts myself.
BAPU
NATVARLAL J. VEPARI
361 HORNBY ROAD
BOMBAY 1
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10119
