264. LETTER TO S. P. PATWARDHAN
SEVAGRAM,
October 20, 1944
CHI. APPA,
I have your letter. Let us not gloat over the pleasant nor
brood over the unpleasant. It is therefore well and good that
you experienced pleasure and pain simultaneously. There is no
need to quit the Congress, or to give up the office that might
come your way as a result of the election. Maybe, it is your
dharma to cling to the office. Dharma to quit can arise if there
is risk of being left behind. We must not frantically pursue an
elective office so as to lose our senses. You have to decide for
yourself which of the two above-mentioned dharmas holds in your
case.
Blessings from
BAPU
APPA PATWARDHAN
RATNAGIRI
