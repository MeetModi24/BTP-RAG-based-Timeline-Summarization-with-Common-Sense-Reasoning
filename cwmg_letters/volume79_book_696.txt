696. LETTER TO AMBALAL SARABHAI
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 21, 1945
SUJNYA BHAISHREE,
This is just to acknowledge your letter. I shall write out
whatever is necessary on the basis of that. I have reached here
today.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHETH AMBALAL SARABHAI
THE CALICO MILLS
P. O. Box 12, AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
