529. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
December 11, 1934
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got both your letters together. I will probably leave on the
19th. I had thought that you would arrive here on Sunday. What
can my influence avail? You alone can judge how much your
presence is necessary there. Is it your job, or mine from this dis-
tance, to please Mummy? It is not as easy to send for you here
now as it was to relieve you. For myself, I am eagerly awaiting
your arrival, and your work also remains unattended to. I shall
write to Mummy today again.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3436
