9. LETTER TO SAROJINI
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 25, 1945
SISTER SAROJINI 2 ,
I have your letter. I advise you to do what Chimanlalji
says. It will not be proper for me to say anything from here.
Keep fit, keep calm and mix with others as sugar mixes with milk.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
