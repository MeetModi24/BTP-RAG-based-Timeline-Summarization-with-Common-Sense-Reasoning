571. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
PANCHGANI,
June 16, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I approve of your demand for money up to Rs. 1,000 for
three months. However, you must have Bapa's and Mridula-
behn's concurrence.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SHYAMLAL
KASTURBA SMARAK NIDHI
SCINDIA HOUSE
BALLARD ESTATE, BOMBAY-
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
