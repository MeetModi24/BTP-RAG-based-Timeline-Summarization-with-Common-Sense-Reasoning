128. LETTER TO VALJI AND DUDHIBEHN DESAI
September 19, 1932
CHI. VALJI AND DUDHIBEHN,
You know my boundless faith in you. May God grant you
the strength to justify it in full. Take as much care as you can
of your health so that you may be able to join in the great
yajna.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7437. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
