440. LETTER TO SECRETARY, HOME DEPARTMENT,
GOVERNMENT OF BOMBAY
September 26 1942
THE SECRETARY
GOVERNMENT OF BOMBAY
HOME DEPARTMENT
SIR,
With reference to your letter of 22nd September, I beg to
say that I cannot exercise the privilege extended by the Govern-
ment since I may not refer in my letters even to non-political
matters mentioned in my letter of 27th August, 1942.
I am,
Yours, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat; C.W. 10366; also Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Govern-
ment, 1942-44, p. 5
