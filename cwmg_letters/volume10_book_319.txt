319. LETTER TO "THE STAR"
JOHANNESBURG,
November 18 [1910]
SIR,
I beg to request that you will be so good as to publish the follow-
ing letter which I have addressed to those gentlemen who were members
of the Asiatic Conference, that was held in August, 1908.
I am, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
The Star, 19-11-1910
