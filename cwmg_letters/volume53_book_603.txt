603. LETTER TO K. RAMACHANDRA
March 3, 1933
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRA,
I thank you for your wire. But I did not reply as I would
not trouble you to come just now. I would like you however
to write out whatever you know about N. It should not be diffi-
cult for you to do so although you consider the case to be intri-
cate. Then, if necessary, I might ask you to come.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20440
