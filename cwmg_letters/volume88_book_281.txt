281. LETTER TO GOPALRAO KALE
NEW DELHI,
July 7, 1947
CHI. GOPALRAO,
The Hasan chapter is not yet closed. I have a long letter
from him. Let me know if he is clean enough.
Herewith I am enclosing a copy of my Gujarati article.
When should the whole matter reach you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
