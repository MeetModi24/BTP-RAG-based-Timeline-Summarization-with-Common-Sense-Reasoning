405. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
January 11, 1931
CHI. SUMANGAL PRAKASH,
I have your letter. It is difficult for me to remember what
I wrote. Was the letter long? I do remember having said that
you had done no wrong in going back home. I had answered all
your questions. I hope you have now fully regained your health.
What work are you doing there?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
