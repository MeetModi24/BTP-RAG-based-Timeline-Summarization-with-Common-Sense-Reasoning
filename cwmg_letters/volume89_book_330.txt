330. LETTER TO VIJAYA M. PANCHOLI
NEW DELHI,
October 14, 1947
CHI. VIJAYA,
I have your letter. If we are unable even to pour a bucket
of water over a raging fire, can we aspire to live up to a hundred
and twenty-five? To me this is clear enough. Why are you
afraid? Nobody can harm one whom Rama protects.
I hope you are all well.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. VIJAYABEHN
GRAM DAKSHINAMURTI
AMBLA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 7152. Also C. W. 4644. Courtesy:
Vijaya M. Pancholi
