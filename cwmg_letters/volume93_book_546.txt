546. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 8, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
I am enclosing two more articles with this. Two have
been posted at Nagpur. Thus, you will be receiving four
articles together.
You must be at ease. It is not raining here right now. Every-
one is in good health. Shakaribehn did come but is going
back today. Anand has again fallen ill. Sharada cannot look
after her children alone.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 Shakaribehn C. Shah
