815. LETTER TO P. V. KRISHNA REDDI
WARDHA,
December 21, 1934
DEAR REDDI,
Your letter to hand. Please tell me what you are getting
there and what are your needs. If you leave the business, who
will look after it?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
P. V. KRISHNA REDDY
C/O KHADI BHANDAR
SADAR BAZAR
RAIPUR, C. P.
From the original: C. W. 10831. Courtesy: Gandhi Darshan, Rajghat,
New Delhi
