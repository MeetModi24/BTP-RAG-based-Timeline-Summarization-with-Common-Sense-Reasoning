158. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
June 1, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I shall write to Bharatananda. Let us see what happens.
What he writes is silly. Yes, it is true that I have said that the
eleven vows do not apply to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
We have to think about Appu.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4345
