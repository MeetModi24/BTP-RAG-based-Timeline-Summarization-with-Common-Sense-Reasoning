435. LETTER TO R. N. BANERJEE
NEW DELHI,
June 21, 1946
DEAR SHRI BANERJEE,
Thank you for your D.O. No. 459-46/PA. of the 18th June which I
read out to Gandhiji. I shall be grateful if you will be kind enough to let me
know what the monthly quota of hessian and gunny bags exported to South
Africa is and when it is to be stopped. In spite of the economic sanctions
adopted by the Indian Government do arrears have to be made good?
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 91
