705. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
SIMLA,
July 11, 1945
CHI. MRIDU,
I have your letter. We shall discuss everything when we meet at
Sevagram, and get things cleared. You, Bapa and I shall sit together.
A person's originality or creative faculties should never die.
However, my experience is that one who has the zeal can culti-
vate them in whatever situation he or she may be.
What shall we do if you get tired? But, if you really do,
you must take some rest.
I wish to leave here on the 15th and go straight to Seva-
gram. However, you, me and everyone else are in the hands of
the Almighty. We are relieved of all burdens if we surrender
even our wishes to Him.
Blessings from
BAPU
