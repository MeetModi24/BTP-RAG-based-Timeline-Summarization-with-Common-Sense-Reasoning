200. LETTER TO BUDHABHAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
March 21, 1932
CHI. BUDHABHAI,

suffer from involuntary discharges. Stop thinking of yourself as Babu's father
and regard yourself as his trustee only. Small children always live with their
