61. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 29, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
If Chi. Ramdas wants to accompany Yashodhara Devi and
Nanavati to Mysore, let him. We shall take care of him when he
returns. How are you getting on with doing your own massage.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9026. Also C.W. 9150. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
