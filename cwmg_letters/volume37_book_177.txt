177. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
August 6, 1928
CHI. RAIHANA,
Your first Gujarati letter was very good indeed. If you write
thus often and improve your handwriting, your Gujarati will
become really very good. Since there is a settlement now, what
work can I give you here?
Vandemataram to everyone.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9608
