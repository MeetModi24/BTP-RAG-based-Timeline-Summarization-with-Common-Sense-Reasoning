244. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
December 18, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter. Of course, you should now have your cor-
respondence and everything. You must lead your own natural life
without let or hindrance and grow to your fullest stature. The
heat here continues but I bear it well. The place is full but Sar-
dar's arrangements are perfect.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 10874. Courtesy: Prithvi Singh
