582. LETTER TO JATINDAS M. AMIN
October 7, 1945
CHI. AMIN,
I have already written to you. It is everyone's duty to
destroy sin. But that is for one's own sin. With regard to
another's sin our attitude should be one of non-interference, i. e., in
that case nothing but non-violence is our duty. My mother used
to go to temples. I did not. I did not take away or destroy the
idols she used to worship. Let us not sit in judgment on others.
Of course I admire your spirit of service. But you are rash and
hot-tempered. Get rid of these two shortcomings and you will
double your capacity for service.
AMINBHAI
ANTI-CHOLERA CAMP
SINDI KHANDALA
WARDHA
