683. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
WARDHA,
July 19, 1933
CHI. AMBUJAM 1 ,
Your letters cannot tire me, for I am interested in your
true happiness.
You may send the little bigger-size cooker through someone
coming. There is no immediate hurry.
I am glad you have a good daughter-in-law.
I shall be relieved when I hear that Kichi's finger is
completely healed.
I hope Father is keeping good health.
Love.
BAPU
From the original : Ambujammal Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
