48. LETTER TO DR. SATYAPAL
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
August 28, 1919
DEAR MR. SATYAPAL 3 ,
I was delighted to see your letter. I shall certainly attend to
the matter as fast as I can. Will you please convey to all the
leaders that at the present moment the situation in the Punjab
occupies a predominant position in my thoughts and actions, and
assure them, especially the ladies whose husbands are at present
undergoing unmerited incarceration, that I shall leave no stone
unturned to secure justice. It is no joke for me to be outside
the prison walls when so many leaders of the Punjab are suffering
imprisonment for no fault save that of daring to serve their country
to the best of their ability.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy ; S.N. 6829
