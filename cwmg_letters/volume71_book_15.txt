15. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 7, 1939
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I have not read the Muttra resolutions or your speech I
should like to have both.
I note your gentle complaint in Mahadev's letter. What can I
do? You have to take me as I am. I know you do. I know
also how gentle you are with me.
You may bring Cripps when you like.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1937. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
