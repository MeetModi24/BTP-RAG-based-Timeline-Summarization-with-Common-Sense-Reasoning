361. LETTER TO LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
BIRLA HOUSE, MUSSOORIE,
June 5, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your kind letter of the 3rd instant.
In accordance with your advice I am staying in Mussoorie
until the afternoon of the 8th, reaching Delhi about 11 p. m.
that night. This enables me to have the next morning's treat-
ment without bustle and hurry so as to be ready for the Working
Committee meeting the same afternoon.
This will be given to you by our mutual friend, Horace
Alexander.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 201
