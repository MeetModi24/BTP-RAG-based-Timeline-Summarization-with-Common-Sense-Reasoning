118. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
July 9, 1927
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter after the Wire. You must have received
my usual weekly letter. I sent also a postcard to Wardha but
that was merely to tell you that I had sent the main letter to
Sabarmati. I am glad you decided to stay on and to get the doc-
tor's report. If we knew all the laws of nature or having known,
had the power to obey them in thought, word and deed, we
would be God Himself and not need to do anything at all. As it
is, we hardly know the laws and have little power to obey them.
Hence disease and all its effects. It is, therefore, enough for us to
realize that every illness is but a breach of some unknown law of
nature and to strive to know the laws and pray for power to
obey. Heart prayer, therefore, whilst we are ill, is both work
and medicine.
I went through another day's strain yesterday and stood it
extremely well, better even than last Sunday. I am in no hurry
to have your Hindi letters.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5247. Courtesy: Mirabehn
