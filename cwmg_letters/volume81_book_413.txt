413. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
September 8, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
Aminbhai may go to Karachi or do hospital work at Seva-
gram itself. I think it is worth giving him the necessary help
for our work.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PRABHAKAR
ASHRAM, SEVAGRAM
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
