270. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
December 16, 1927
BHAISHRI KHAMBHATTA,
Since Shri Govindji Cheda is under your charge, I do not
worry about him. Kindly write from time to time, and tell me
what Dr. Jivraj Mehta says, who is to perform the operation, etc.
You must have recovered by now.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
[PS.]
18-21 Cuttack
23 Madras
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 5011. Courtesy: Tehmina Khambhatta
