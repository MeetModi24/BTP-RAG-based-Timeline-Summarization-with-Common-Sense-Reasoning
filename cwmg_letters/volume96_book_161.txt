161. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
7 BUITENSINGLE,
March 8 [1914]
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
If you are sufficiently composed why not re-commence your
Gujarati. It would be such a gain if you knew something of it. And
you will easily pick it up by getting the household words and commencing
to talk straightaway.

2 For the Valliamma Memorial
3 From the contents
Eric has written to me about Polak. Of course if Ritch comes and
wants to practise, Polak will not worry about it. But I know that Ritch
will not resume practice. He is hopelessly insolvent. He has broken
his vow. He had gone away without notice. He is mad. I have to
[tell] Eric about it. I want to give him about £150 for the pressing
debts. I am wiring Phamix to send you cheque for £200 so that you
may have enough for disbursements.
With love,
UPPER HOUSE
