182. LETTER TO ANANDLAL
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Wednesday, First Chaitra Sud 11 [March 24, 1926]
CHI. ANANDLAL,
Why should you write to me at all? I hear about you from
stray visitors, and for a moment I am sad. It seems you make
no payment towards the maintenance of Chi. Kashi and her
children. And you do not, I hear, let her have even the rent
which is due to her from the house which forms part of her own
share. If so, it is a shame and regret. Let me know if you have
anything to say to justify your stand.
