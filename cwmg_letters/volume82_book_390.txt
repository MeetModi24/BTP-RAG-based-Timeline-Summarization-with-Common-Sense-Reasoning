390. LETTER TO SADHU CHARANDAS
MAHISHADAL,
December 26, 1945
BHAI SADHU CHARANDAS,
I have your letter. I have written quite a lot on students.
You should read it and do whatever you feel is right. What
can be there in it which makes you sleepless?
SADHU CHARANDAS
HEAD MASTER
BOARD HIGH SCHOOL
SURADA (GANJAM)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
