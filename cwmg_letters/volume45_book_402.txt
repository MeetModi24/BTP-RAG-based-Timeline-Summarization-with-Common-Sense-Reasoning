402. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
KARACHI,
March 27, 1931
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your letter . You should not expect a reply from me
every time. You yourself, however, should write regularly. As
for my programme, I intend to reach Delhi on the 4th. My health
is fairly good. Everyone is all right. Radha keeps well on the whole.
Keshu has come here.
PRABHAVATI FOR BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 9293. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
