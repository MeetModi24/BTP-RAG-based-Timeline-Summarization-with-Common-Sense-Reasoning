329. LETTER TO A. IRBE
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 11, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I hardly think I shall have to go
to Finland. But if I do, and if I have to pass through Latvia, I
would certainly like to make the acquaintance of your father. You
will watch the papers and if I do go, you will perhaps send me the
necessary letter.
Yours sincerely,
MRs. A. IRBE
WEAVING SCHOOL
C.S.M.
MAYAVARAM
From a photostat: S.N. 19448
