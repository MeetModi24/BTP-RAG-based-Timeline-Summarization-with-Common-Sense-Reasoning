473. LETTER TO SHOORJI VALLABHDAS
September 4, 1933
BHAI SHOORJI,
I was thinking of writing to you, when I got your letter. I
hope to reach Bombay on Friday. I will not, therefore, give you
trouble by asking you to come here. I am certainly eager to see
you, but I will have patience for a few days. I hope the girl's
health is improving, and that you are well, too.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4095
