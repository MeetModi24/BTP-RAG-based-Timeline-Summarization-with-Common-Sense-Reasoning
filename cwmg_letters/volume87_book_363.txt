363. LETTER TO SYED JAFAR IMAM
[After April 29, 1947]
Our conversation was so cordial that I referred to it in my
post-prayer speech3, of which a brief account was issued to English
newspapers. I do not read what newspapers write on their own
account. . . . If there is any mistake kindly point it out.
Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase, Vol. I, Bk. II, p. 325
1& The addressee in his letter dated April 29, 1947, had written that
the report of his conversation with Gandhiji on April 15, vide pp. 286-7, had
been "twisted and misinterpreted in some sections of the Press". Gandhiji asked
his secretary, who had taken rough notes, to prepare an account of the
interview and send it to the addressee with a copy of the rough notes, along
with a report of his speech which contained the reference to the interview.
3 Vide p. 289.
4 Omission as in the source
