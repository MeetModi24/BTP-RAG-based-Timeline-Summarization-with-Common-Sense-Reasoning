160. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
August 29, 1939
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
I thank you for your letter of 26th instant. I reciprocate
your wish that the world will be spared the calamity of war.
But if it comes and you think my presence necessary in Simla,
of course I shall come.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a microfilm: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
