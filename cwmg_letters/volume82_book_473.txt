473. LETTER TO FATEHCHAND NAHATA
SODEPUR,
January 4, 1946
BHAI FATEHCHAND,
Consider what will be my fate if everyone asks me for 10
minutes of my time as you do. You are not going to gain anything
by meeting me. You will unnecessarily waste your time. Read
whatever I have written and devote yourself to service.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI FATEHCHAND NAHATA
PRESIDENT KUSHTIA MAHAKMA CONGRESS COMMITTEE
KUSHTIA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
