130. LETTER TO C. C. GANGULI
August 5, 1945
BHAI GANGULI,
I have your letter. My blessings to Chi. Malavika and
Chi. Ruplekha. I have no zest for writing the preface.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
C. C. GANGULI
ASST. SESSIONS JUDGE
KHULNA (BENGAL)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
