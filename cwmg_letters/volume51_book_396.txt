396. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. DESAI
October 12, 1932
CHI. NARAYANRAO ALIAS BABLO,
I did receive a complaint against you. All children play, but
they should play when it is time for play and should work when it is
time for work. In any case, they should not give trouble to others.
I am told that these days you play all the time and also harass
people. Is this true? If it is, you should not behave in that manner.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9480
