161. LETTER TO LADY CHHOTURAM
SEVAGRAM,
February 3, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I am grieved that Sir Chhoturam has passed away. Only a
few months back he had written to me. He had love for me.
What consolation can I offer you? Let us live as God ordains.
Tours,
M. K. GANDHI
LADY CHHOTURAM
C/O DR. GOPI CHAND
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
