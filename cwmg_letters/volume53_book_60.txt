60. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS GANDHI
January 15, 1933
CHI. KRISHNADAS 1 ,
I have read your letter to Prabhudas. It is ignorance, and
therefore an error, to go on working without regard to one's
health till the body breaks down. You should take rest even for
being able to serve, as you wish to do. I hope that Prabhudas
did not drag you as far as Bombay. He could have easily gone
there by himself and also seen Vinoba. I will suggest this to
him if it is not too late-let me know if it is.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3084
