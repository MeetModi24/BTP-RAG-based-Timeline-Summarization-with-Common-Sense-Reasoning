656. LETTER TO H. K. VEERANNA GOWDH
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 8, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
No permission is necessary for dedicating your work to me.
But if you do dedicate it to me please do not say that you have
obtained my permission. I cannot give it without reading the
book and if you send it to me, I should have no time to read it.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. H. K. VEERANNA GOWDH
CHANNAPATNA
From a microfilm: S.N. 19606
