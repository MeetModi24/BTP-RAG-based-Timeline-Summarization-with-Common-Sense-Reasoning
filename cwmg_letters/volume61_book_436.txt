436. LETTER TO APRAKASH CHANDRA MEHTA
WARDHA,
August 2, 1935
MY DEAR APRAKASH,
Why should it be galling to you to be on trial at your age.
Age is rather against you in a novel experiment. A stranger
would be on trial no matter how able he is. Much more so one
who embarks like you on a new venture. Why should we assume
that the world knows us as we think we are? Remember the
recital of the eleven vows which we have at each prayer daily.
The verse ends with the exhortation that the observance should be
with due humility. Humility is the key to peace and real joy.
I await your first letter of experiences with a little impatience.
Look out for Amala there and befriend her.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
