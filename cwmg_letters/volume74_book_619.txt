619. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
October 3, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
You will be at the bend tomorrow Saturday at 5 p.m. when a
tonga will be waiting for you to take you to Madalasa.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Better come here at 5 p.m. and you will go with Rajen Babu.
From the original: C.W. 6489. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9884
