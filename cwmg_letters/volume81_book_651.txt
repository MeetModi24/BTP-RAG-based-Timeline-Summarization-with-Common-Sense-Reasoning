651. LETTER TO CHHOTUBHAI K. MEHTA
October 16, 1945
CHI. NAPOLEON1,
I got your letter written in a beautiful band. I was glad to
read about Mama. Why do you want blessings for your Associa-
tion? You should assume that you have everybody's blessings
for good work and go on with it silently.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. NAPOLEON
ADARSH DUGDHALAYA
MALAD, via BOMBAY
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./XXII
