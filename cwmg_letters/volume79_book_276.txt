276. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
February 24, 1945
CHI. SITA,
I have your letter. You draw your matras too long, as I
illustrate with ~ here. Draw them as I do or if you must have
a knot at the end make it very small. Consult your teacher. It
will be very hot by 20th April and hotter still in May. I don't
know where I shall be then. I should like you to spend the hot
season in a cool place. I am very happy that you are getting
along nicely.
I have come to Madalasabehn's to spend five days. Blessings
to all.
Blessings from
BAPU
SITA GANDHI
AKOLA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
