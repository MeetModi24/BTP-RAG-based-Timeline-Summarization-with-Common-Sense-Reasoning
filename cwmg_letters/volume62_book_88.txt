88. LETTER TO ABHIMANYU
MAGANWADI, WARDHA,
October 30, 1935
CHI. ABHIMANYU,
Your handwriting cannot be described as very good but
it is fairly good. The letters in a word are also pictures, and a
good picture is one that is an exact copy. If you drew such well-
formed letters as looked like pictures, I would say your hand-
writing was good. According to me, learning to form letters
is a step in the process of learning to draw. If you are eager
to learn how to draw such letters, you may succeed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5746
