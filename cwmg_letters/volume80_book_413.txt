413. LETTER TO SHANTABAI KALE
PANCHGANI,
June 2, 1945
CHI. SHANTABAI,
I was happy to receive your detailed letter.
Gopalrao will
get well. He has faith and courage and he is simple-hearted.
Keep on writing to me. Convey my blessings to Gopalrao.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHANTABAI KALE
VICTORIA HOSPITAL
JABALPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
