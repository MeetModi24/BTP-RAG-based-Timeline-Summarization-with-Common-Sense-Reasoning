206. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM,
[Before September 21, 1919]
DEAR MATHURADAS,
You must give thought to your health. Help Young India as
long as you are there. I am trying to shift Young India here soon.
I have sent some matter to Bhai Nandlal only today.
Blessings from
BAPU
