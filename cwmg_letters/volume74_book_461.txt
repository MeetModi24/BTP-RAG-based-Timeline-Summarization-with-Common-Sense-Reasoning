461. LETTER TO F. MART BARR
September 2, 1941
CHI. MARY,
At last you have come. And now that you have arrived it seems
you had never left India. I shall patiently await your arrival at
Sevagram. I hope you have derived much physical benefit from
your visit to S[outh] A[frica].
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Please tell Kamala that I forgot to tell her that her standpoint
about Red Cross, etc., was correct. You are right about her health.
Fault is wholly hers. She can easily procure the food she needs.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6082. Also C.W. 3412. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
