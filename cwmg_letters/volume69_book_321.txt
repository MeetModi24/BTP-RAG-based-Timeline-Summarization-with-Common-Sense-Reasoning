321. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
ANANDKUNJ, RAJKOT,
May 29, 1939
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
It is perfectly true that you do not get justice. I cannot
myself manage to write. I have asked Kanu.
I have tried nu-
merous arrangements, but when I myself am lax how can I blame
anybody else? I will see if I can be regular now.
I have been trying to do what I can about the problem
there. It will be good if I get regular reports from there. Do
you think the movement will go on well?
What does the fact that you didn't get permission for even
one assistant indicate? I had thought that it would be child's
play for Manilal to secure such permission.
I am all right. We leave this place on June 1 and go to
Bombay. After four or five days there, we go to the Frontier
Province.

Blessings from
BAPU
