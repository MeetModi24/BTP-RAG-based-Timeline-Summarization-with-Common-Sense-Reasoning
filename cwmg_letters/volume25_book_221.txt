221. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
October 25, 1924
MY DEAREST CHARLIE,
Your loving messages continue to come daily. Mine you must
take for granted. I am quite well. The Youngs are by me as I
am writing this. I am to stop in this bungalow up to even the
first few days of November if necessary. Rughbir Singh has
been very kind.
With love,
Yours,
MOHAN
From a photostat: G.N. 2620
