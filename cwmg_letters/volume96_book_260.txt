260. LETTER TO MILLIE GRAHAM POLAK
BANKIPORE,
May 11 [1917]
MY DEAR MILLIE,
Just a line before leaving for Bettiah. Your telegram of yesterday
has frightened me. Immediately on receipt of your wire, I wrote to
you a long letter about Henry and Ceilia. I do still hope that [you]
have received it. The postal hours in Bettiah are most inconvenient.
I have to trust to chance friends to post the letters and it is possible
that the one who takes the letters might have missed the post for which
my letter to you was intended.
It alarms me to find that fever has not yet left Henry and I have
telegraphed to you to wire or write to me at Bettiah.
I came in yesterday on the invitation of the Government to see
Hon'ble Mr. Maude . I had two hours' interview. I shall write more
about this later. The matter is critical. I dare not leave the scene of
activity at the present moment or I would have gone down to you.
Please use the wire freely if necessary. I shall expect at least a postcard
from you daily.
With love,
Yours,
BHAI
