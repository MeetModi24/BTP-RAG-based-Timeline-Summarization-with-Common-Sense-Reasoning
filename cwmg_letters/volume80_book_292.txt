292. LETTER TO AMIYA NATH BOSE
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 23, 1945
BHAI AMIYA,
Your letter suggests that the power scheme according to my
calculations is useless. How can I therefore form a committee?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI AMIYA
1 WOODBURN PARK
CALCUTTA
