807. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL SHAH
October 31, 1945
BHAI CHHAGANLAL,
Go home from there and live where and as God ordains.
I can't say anything more than this.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
CHHAGANLAL SHAH
DEVJI LADHA
DAFTARI ROAD
MALAD EAST
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
