182. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
June 1, 1935
BHAI KHAMBHATTA,
I do get news about your illness from time to time. Please
let me know how you are now. The Rajkumari has given me some
news and that has increased my curiosity. You must get well.
What treatment are you trying there? I hope your mind is per-
fectly clear irrespective of the state of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7556. Also C.W. 5031. Courtesy:
Tehmina Khambhatta
