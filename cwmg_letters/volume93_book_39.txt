39. LETTER TO RAJARAM R. BHOLE
WARDHA,
May 11, 1935
MY DEAR RAJARAM,
Why should you be torn to pieces. You should simply
do what your body allows. See friends there and find out
what is possible.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI RAJARAM BHOLE
C/O MINERVA TALKIES
POONA
From the original: R. R. Bhole Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
