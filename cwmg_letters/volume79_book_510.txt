510. LETTER TO KHURSHED NAOROJI
SEVAGRAM,
March 24, 1945
VAHALAN BEHN,
I am so glad you are going for a change to Panchgani. Work
there but don't descend till you are really well. I treasure
your caution to think of nothing but the constructive programme.
You have also done well to tell me that I must meet the co-
ordinating committee.
BAPU
SMT. KHURSHEDBEHN
DUNLAVIN LODGE
POONA 5
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
