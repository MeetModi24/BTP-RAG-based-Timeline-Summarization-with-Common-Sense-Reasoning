48. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 31, 1934
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
Your letter. How is it that you reached the hospital and yet
the operation was not performed? I reach Bombay on June 14.
How nice it would be if the operation is performed! As for your
accompanying me, I have already written to you. Why are you
scared of the operation? Don't you have enough faith in God?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 307
