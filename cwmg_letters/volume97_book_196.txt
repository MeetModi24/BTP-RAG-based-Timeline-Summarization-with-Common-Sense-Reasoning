196. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 31, 1932
CHI. NIMU,
Why are you tardy in writing to me. Do you feel lost because of Ramdas
having gone to jail? How do you spend your time? How is the child? Does
he give trouble? Does he also suffer from constipation? How do you keep?
Do you go out for walks?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
