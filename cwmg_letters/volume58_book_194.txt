194. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
July 12, 1934
CHI. MIRA,
This is being written in a moving train.
I hope you duly received a note of authority on his banker.
I do not know for how much it was. You will let me know your
requirement in due time.
For the whole of August at least I am free. The way before
me after is not at all clear and I do not worry. He will show
the next step. Andrews is due on 25th August.
You will keep your health. Please know that I shall not mind
if you show no visible result from your visit to England. The
experience you will gain is adequate result for me.
Therefore
you will not be impatient with yourself or your surroundings.
I am keeping quite well. The fatigue is there. But that is
inevitable.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6191. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9757
