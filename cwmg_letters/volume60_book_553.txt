553. LETTER TO DR. PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
WARDHA,
April 8, 1935
DEAR DR. PATTABHI,
The young men are not going back unless the life here dis-
quiets them. Strange food, strange climate. If their friends can
find money, I should like the return fares in case of need and
some for their bedding. They are under Mirabehn's charge.
Yours,
BAPU
Incidents of Gandhiji's Life, p. 225
