581. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 19, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
Do not expect a long letter from me. Do not be distressed
by my illness; instead rejoice. God will grant you happiness.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
