166. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI P. DESAI
NEW DELHI,
June 24, 1947
CHI. MAGANBHAI,
I just got your letter and am writing the reply.
Mahadev's work will not be carried on by associating his
name with an educational institution. Then why bother about
the name? If the teachers and students imbibe his spirit, all
will be well. Be careful in your work, and know that it will
immortalize his name.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
