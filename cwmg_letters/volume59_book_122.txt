122. LETTER TO M. TAYABULLA
October 1, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. It is a most diffi-
cult thing for me to interfere in the matter you mention. It is
essentially a matter for you, local men, to settle. In any case, I
must not intervene.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 61
