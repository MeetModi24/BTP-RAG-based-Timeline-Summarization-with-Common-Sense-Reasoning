571. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
May 22, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
Even silence for five minutes can serve many purposes. It
also means rest. If the Ashram cat makes a mess all over the place,
you must take it that it has acquired the bad habit from us. No
man has influenced the cat here.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9947. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
