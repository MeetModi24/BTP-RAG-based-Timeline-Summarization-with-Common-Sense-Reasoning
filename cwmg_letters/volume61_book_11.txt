11. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
April 27, 1935
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have certainly no plans for a tour. Perhaps I may have
to go to Borsad. In the beginning of May at any rate I shall
be here.
You will stay with me in the orchard here. There is no room.
We are living in the verandah. There is a room but only large
enough to keep our things in. Bear these facts in mind before
you decide to come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9681
