38. LETTER TO L. W. RITCH
[CAPE TOWN,]
April 20, 1911
MY DEAR RITCH,
The enclosed has been sent to me by Chhaganlal with a
request to give it to you. The extract is good for reproduction in
Indian Opinion, but can we?
I hope you were successful in your case. When you want
any cash, I assume you have been taking it from Miss Schlesin.
Anyhow, the extract should go to Henry to read.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 5487
