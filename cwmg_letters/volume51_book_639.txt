639. LETTER TO M. M. ANANTA RAU
November 13, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Your argument seems to be this: Bhaga-
vad Gita refers the devotee to shastravidhi and since Shastras sup-
port untouchability, Bhagavad Gita must be said to support it. The
question then is what are Shastras? And I have answered that
question by the answer that whatever is contrary to the main
theme of the Gita must be rejected as being no Shastra. And
since the main theme is oneness and therefore equality of all life,
there is no warrant for untouchability in the Gita.
Yours sincerely,
