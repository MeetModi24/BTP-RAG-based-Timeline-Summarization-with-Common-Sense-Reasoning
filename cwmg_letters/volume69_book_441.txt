441. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
ABBOTTABAD,
July 10, 1939
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I hope Chandan is improving. I have had a letter from her.
Shankar was improving rapidly. Aren't they both, Shankar and
Chandan, fortunate? Shankar should be arriving there soon now.
You had asked for Kaka's opinion, but he has left the deci-
sion to me. If Chandan does not require your presence now and
you also feel the same, please return to Wardha.
Ba has fully recovered. But, as a small incision was made
under Pyarelal's tongue, Sushila and Ba had to stay back. They
will be leaving in a day or two.
Blessings from
BAPU
