729. LETTER TO GHANSHYAM MIRCHANDANI
MAHABALESHWAR,
[Before April 25, 1945]
In your place I would not submit even if I were to die.
But there is no one law for everyone. You should act solely
according to your capacity. In any event seeing that you did
not accept the notice, you should inform the authorities that you
would not accept restrictions save of your own conscience and
that you fully believed in non-violence and truth.
The Bombay Chronicle, 26-4-1945
