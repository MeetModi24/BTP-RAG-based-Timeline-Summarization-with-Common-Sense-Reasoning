540. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 23, 1941
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I gave my opinion to Kishorelalbhai yesterday. I don't think
there is any need to send for all family members. If the lady doc-
tor also is of the same opinion, get the operation done at once.
There is no danger at all in opening the abdomen. I think it
absolutely necessary to find out about the tumour. Even if all of
them come over, they will not be of much help. Why, then, incur
expenses on travelling and so on? It is necessary to observe cer-
tain limits in every matter. Show this letter to everybody.
Blessings from
BAPU
