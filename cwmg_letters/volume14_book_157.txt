157. LETTER TO G. K. DEODHAR
[ AHMEDABAD,]
Wednesday, March 6, 1918
Do by all means come and we shall discuss. Meanwhile, we
must agree to differ. I have come in close touch with both
Messrs Pratt and Ghosal and I think I know them. I suppose
we shall have to [be] content with half your usefulness. He who
remains sick half the time of his life is only half useful. Is he not?
You will not do the one thing needful to regain health.
Very sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
