128. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
August 1, 1928
CHI. NARANDAS,
Mirabehn has made slivers of the finest quality out of five
tolas of cotton and given them to me. You credit them under
whichever head you may decide and debit them in today's ac-
count against Behn Rajkishori. I have given them to her.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11806
