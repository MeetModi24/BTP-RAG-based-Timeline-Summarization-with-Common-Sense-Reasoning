145. LETTER TO KASHINATH TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 23, 1930
CHI. KASHINATH,
I have your letter. After Shanta and Kalavati have tried the
medicine given by the doctor, write to me and tell me what effect
it has. If the latter has taken a pledge to join the struggle, I think
she cannot leave. But you have already written to Father. You
should both do as your hearts bid you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5249
