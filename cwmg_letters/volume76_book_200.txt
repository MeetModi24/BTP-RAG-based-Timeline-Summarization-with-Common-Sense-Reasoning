200. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
May 30, 1942
CHI. SUSHILA AND MANILAL,
You two seem to have been left alone there. That is good.
It is enough if you are happy in that state. Frequently the
joy we find in following a lonely path is not to be found in
travelling in the company of many, because in the latter case
there is the danger of one's being dragged in a certain direction
against one's wishes. I shall be content if all of you keep good
health and are happy.
All are well here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4930
