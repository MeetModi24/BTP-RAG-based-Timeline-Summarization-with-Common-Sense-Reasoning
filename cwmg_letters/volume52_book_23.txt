23. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI PATEL
November 19, 1932
CHI. DAHYABHAI,
We get daily reports about your health. Such illness comes
to us as a test. Bhai Karamchand says that you are bearing it
patiently. That is just what one expects of you. Don't worry
about Manibehn.
God will surely protect you.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DAHYABHAI PATEL
RAM NIVAS, PAREKH STREET
BOMBAY-
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro‒ : Manibehn Patelne, p. 149
