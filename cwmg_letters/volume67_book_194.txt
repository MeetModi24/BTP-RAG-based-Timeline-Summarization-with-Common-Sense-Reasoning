194. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
Silence Day [After June 29, 1938]
CHI. VIJAYA,
You must be all right. Manilalbhai will bring you here in the
evening, if the doctor permits, that is.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7109. Also C.W. 4601. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
