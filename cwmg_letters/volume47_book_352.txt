352. LETTER TO R. G. PRADHAN
AHMEDABAD,
August 18, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter enclosing your appeal to the
Hindu members of the R.T.C. It does not any longer affect me
but I may say that the appeal is not likely to be of much use
because in my opinion the Government want to keep the question
for themselves to arbitrate upon. I hope I am wrong. But the
conviction is not baseless.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. R. G. PRADHAN
NASIK
