248. LETTER TO Y. M. PARNERKAR
August 22, 1945
CHI. PARNERKAR,
I believe that you understand Gujarati and can read it
with ease. Let me know if it is not so. Have this read out to
you. Do something about the wolf. The manure heap should go.
The Goshala should become an ideal goshala. Keep the place per-
fectly clean. Our water should be drinkable. Mosquitoes and
flies should easily go. The roads should be in good condition.
The hill is in our possession. Those who go for walks there should
bring stones from there. Do you know that in this way I had
made a mountain of stones in the Mahila Ashram? All of it
came in useful there.
Blessings from
BAPU
