304. LETTER TO V. R. KULKARNI
November 4, 1934
DEAR KULKARNI,
I have your letter. I return herewith your cuttings. The
certificates are interesting. Before I can recommend you any-
where I would like you to give me a detailed scheme which any
manager of a dairy could understand.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI V. R. KULKARNI
HANUMAN GADA
WARDHA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
