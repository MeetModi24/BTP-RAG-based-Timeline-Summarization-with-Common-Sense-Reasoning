213. LETTER TO SIR PHILIP HARTOG
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, S.W. 1,
November 19, 1931
DEAR SIR PHILIP,
I am much obliged to you for your letter of the 17th inst.
I do not propose just now to withdraw the statement I made
at the meeting at Chatham House. At the present moment I have
not got any time for searching the records to which you are mak-
ing reference. I, however, promise not to forget the matter, and
if I find that I cannot support the statement made by me at
Chatham House, I will give my retraction much wider publicity
than the Chatham House speech could ever attain.
Meanwhile I am endeavouring to find out the references you
want.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR PHILIP HARTOG, K.B.E.
5 INVERNESS GARDENS, W.
From a photostat: C.W. 9403-a. Courtesy: India Office Library
