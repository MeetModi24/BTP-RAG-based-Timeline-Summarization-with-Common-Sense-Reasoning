267. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
March 21, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA,
I have explained about plays in my previous letter. Did you
receive any letter from Narahari from Belgaum? If you did, what
does he write? Are all [the prisoners] kept together?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5769. Also C.W. 2992. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
