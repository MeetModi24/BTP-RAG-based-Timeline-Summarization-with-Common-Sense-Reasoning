304. LETTER TO CHANDAN PAREKH
RAJKOT,
May 24, 1939
CHI. CHANDAN,
Why did you fall ill? Take the medicine suggested in the
letter which Sushila is writing to you. But make this change in
it. What you are suffering from is not dysentery but an infec-
tion which you have contracted here. To cure it you should
live for some time only on fruit juices and glucose. The fruits
should include pineapples, pomegranates, musambis, oranges and
grapes. Take about 50 ounces of juice and 2 to 4 ounces glucose.
If you do this you will be able to keep up your strength. If you
cannot drink this quantity of juice, you may drink less. If you
think that the quantity of glucose also is too much, take less.
Put a mud-pack of clean earth on the abdomen at night, cover
it up with a piece of dry cloth and then wrap a piece of khadi
round it. Remove the khadi piece when you wake up at night.
Keep me informed. Get well soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
