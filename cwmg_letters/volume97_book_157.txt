157. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
May 18, 1930
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
Hope you are keeping well. What have you ultimately decided about
Vijapur? Write to me in detail. Do the children now pay attention to the
prayers? Has the heat affected you adversely?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32947
