229. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
July 30, 1936
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM1,
I have with me two of your letters to answer. There is no
harm in your being operated upon there. I shall do through
Devdas whatever is possible. Moreover, there will be Brajkisan
when he comes. Give the accompanying letter to Devdas.
I have understood your point about Rukmini. And now
she has after all gone. It was just the proper thing that Mal-
kani should have gone to leave her at Hyderabad.
I shall ask you to come after your operation. Meanwhile
look after your health carefully. Do send for anything that you
need. You should freely ask Devdas for anything that you need
if you truly regard him as your brother.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 342
