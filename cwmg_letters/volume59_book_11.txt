11. LETTER TO VIDYA PARKASH
September 17, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of the 25th ultimo. I well remember your
previous letter. But I have been under the impression that I
had already sent you my reply enclosing a letter for your father.
It is just likely that the letter like many of my letters written
during the hurricane tour has gone astray. Here is a letter
addressed to your father. You will please send it to him. I take
it that you will treat the letter as confidential.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
SHRI VIDYA PARKASH
LATH BUILDINGS
RISHINAGAR, LAHORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
