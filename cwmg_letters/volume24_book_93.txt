93. LETTER TO PARASRAM MEHROTRA
Jyeshtha Sud 1 [June 3, 1924]
CHI. PARASRAM,
I have your postcard. Study the Ramayana very carefully.
It won't do to read it only once. I am sure the Ramayana will
give you peace of mind. Are all the patients improving?
Blessings from
BAPU
PARASRAM MEHROTRA
U. P. KHADDAR BOARD
CAWNPORE
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4960. Courtesy: Parasram Mehrotra
