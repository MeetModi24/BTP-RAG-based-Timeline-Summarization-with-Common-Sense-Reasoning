75. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
September 24, 1934
CHI. KAKA,
You may either keep this girl with you or go and live wher-
ever she wants you to live. She is sticking here like a fly impor-
tuning me to bring you here for treatment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10890
