170. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
December 18, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
Babla's handwriting, one can say, has improved. But he
has forgotten quite a lot of what he had learnt. I hope you
have received and despatched the telegram to Kasturbhai. I
have received a fair number of copies of Ishopanishad.
I expect to reach there at about 7.30 tomorrow. Navin will
come with Ba. So for the present three of us and Mehrtaj will
go. Bablo will join us on the 23rd.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11511
