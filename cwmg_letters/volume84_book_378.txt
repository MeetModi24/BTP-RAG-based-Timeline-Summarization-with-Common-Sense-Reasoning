378. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
MUSSOORIE,
June 7, 1946
CHI. MANILAL,
I have your letter. I will not reply at length. You may
ask what you please when we meet. I assume that Sita's prob-
lem will be solved.
I think I shall leave Delhi before the end of this month.
Everything may be over even before the 15th.
To you all,
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANILAL GANDHI
KHIMJI JIVA SANATORIUM, BLOCK No.
6
BANGANGA, WALKESHWAR ROAD
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 4982
