506. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 15, 1941
CHI. KANCHAN,
Tari is of course lazy, but aren't you also like her? Why is
there no letter of acknowledgement from you? How is Tari?
What about your diet? How are you? Do you cook everything
yourself? Help as much as you can in the Ashram. You can
even take your meals in the Ashram kitchen. Take part in the
prayers, etc. Teach them to make bread. How are you keeping
there? Ask Tari to write to me, I am all right. It seems Shastriji's
wounds are healing up. Mahadev has gone to Bombay. I still
give Amtul Salaam only bhaji soup. She does take honey, of course.
Munnalal is fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8275. Also C.W. 7127. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
