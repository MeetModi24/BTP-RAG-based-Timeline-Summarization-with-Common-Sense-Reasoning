494. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
BOMBAY,
January 2, 1932
DEAR MR. JAYAKAR,
I was grieved to find from your wire that you were suffering
from insomnia. Why not take longer rest and get rid of the
disease altogether? In your present state I am not going to
worry you with my troubles.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
