15. LETTER TO J. H. GARRETT
GUJARAT VIDYAPITH,
AHMEDABAD,
April 21, 1931
DEAR MR. GARRETT,
I have to thank you for your exhaustive reply.
The chief point of the moment is with reference to the status
of the Congress in the Settlement. If you agree that the Settle-
ment is between the Congress and the Government, and if it is the
Congress that has to implement its terms so far as they are appli-
cable to the people, it follows that the Congress must be recogniz-
ed as the intermediary between the Government and the people
whom the Congress represents. If such was not the case, I suppose
that I should have no right to see you or to correspond with you
or to receive your replies in the several matters arising out of the
Settlement. Your letter raises a question of the first magnitude,
and on your reply to it will depend the action to be taken by me
in connection with the Settlement. Meanwhile, I refrain from deal-
ing with the details covered by your letter.
Yours sincerely,
J. H. GARRETT, ESQUIRE
AHMEDABAD
