72. LETTER TO G. J. ADAM
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 2, 1906
DEAR SIR,
Lord Elgin has appointed Thursday the 8th instant for receiving
the Deputation. The Delegates from the Transvaal will be assisted by
the gentlemen as per list enclosed. Sir Lepel Griffin will head the deputa-
tion. The list is subject to addition.
Yours truly,
G. J. ADAM, ESQ.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4454
