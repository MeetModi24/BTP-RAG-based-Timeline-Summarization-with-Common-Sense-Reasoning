270. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 21, 1938
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
The Maulana has flatly refused, and it does not, therefore,
seem proper to press him further. I think it will be best to think
of Pattabhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 230
