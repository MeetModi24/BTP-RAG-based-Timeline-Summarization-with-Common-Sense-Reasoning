298. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 11, 1930
BHAI VITHALDAS,
I have received your letter, along with the figures sent by
you. I know that stocks of khadi are again accumulating. Do
not lose heart. According to me, the flood has not reached full
tide yet. If we have faith, it is bound to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9774
