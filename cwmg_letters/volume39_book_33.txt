33. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
Saturday [February 9, 1929]
BA,
You have acquired true knowledge. You showed great pat-
ience when you went to Delhi. I, therefore, hope that you are
not grieving excessively. These children were brought up by you
and, therefore, you are bound to suffer. But all of us will follow
Rasik one day. Why should we, then, grieve when someone goes
before the others? If you happen to stay on at Delhi, we shall
meet on Sunday, the 17th.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2156
