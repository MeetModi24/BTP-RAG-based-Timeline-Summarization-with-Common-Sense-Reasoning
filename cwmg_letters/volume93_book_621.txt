621. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 23, 1940
DEAR SLAVE,
Your two love letters. I see you are filling your place well.
Only I hope your body is obeying you or are you its slave
instead of being its driver?
In a way I am glad all the three members of the family
are having a bit of rest.
Love.
SLAVE-DRIVER
LOTUS-BORN
HYDERABAD
From
the original: Padmaja Naidu
Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
