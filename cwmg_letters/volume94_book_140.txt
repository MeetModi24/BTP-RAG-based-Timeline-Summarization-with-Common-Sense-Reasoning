140. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 13, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you today. I am all right. But again
I took three drops of sarpagandha last night. The backache has
subsided. I am having to do a lot of work. Prabha will lodge the
complaint.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
