213. LETTER TO DR. JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
NEW DELHI,
September 16, 1947
DEAR DR. HOLMES,
Devdas has handed me your kind letter.
I do not remember having received Mr. Ording's invitation.
In any event I should be at sea serving on the committee. My
way seems to be different. We must discuss this when we meet
and if you and I find the time for it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C. W. 10970. Courtesy: Roger W. Holmes. Also Pyare-
lal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
