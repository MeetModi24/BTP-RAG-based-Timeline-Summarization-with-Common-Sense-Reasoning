214. LETTER TO PRAFULLA CHANDRA GHOSH
October 13, 1934
MY DEAR PRAFULLA,
I have read your long letter to Mahadev.
In the circum-
stances narrated by you, of course you were quite right in doing
what you did, and arranging the tour for Khan Brothers. It is
also well that the balance of the tour has been cancelled, and that
they will be with me about the 16th. Then too, there will be
only two days left. They must reach Bombay on the 19th.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
