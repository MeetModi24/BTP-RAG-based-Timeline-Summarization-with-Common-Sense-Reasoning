355. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Y.M.,
September 18, 1930
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
If I were free you would be the first visitor I would be
seeing in the morning tomorrow. It has been many days since
I last saw your handwriting. Do keep writing to me. Is your
health all right? Tell Nirmalabehn that I have not forgotten her.
Her innocent face often swims in my ken. What is Indu doing?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: G. N. 11553
