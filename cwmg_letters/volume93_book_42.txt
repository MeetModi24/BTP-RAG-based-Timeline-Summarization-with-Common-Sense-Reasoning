42. LETTER TO RAJARAM R. BHOLE
AS AT WARDHA,
May 25, 1935
DEAR RAJARAM,
It is most difficult to guide you. My own general advice to
all has been to stop all college education as soon as they have
had in their own opinion enough of it and enter business.
Those who have love of education truly begin it after the
college course. I would like you to succeed as an honest, able
businessman who would earn not for himself but for all. And
in every case you must consider your body.
Yours,
BAPU
From the original : R. R. Bhole Papers. Courtesy : Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
