649. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
April 15, 1945
CHI. JIVANJI,
I send you with this for your perusal Anand's letter regard-
ing the books. I am sending also the English material which
he has sent. Perhaps you will have that with you. Read both
and return them with your comments. I have not yet written
anything to Anand.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati ; G.N. 9954. Also C.W. 6928. Courtesy;
Jivanji D. Desai
