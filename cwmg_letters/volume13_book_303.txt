303. LETTER TO W. MAUDE
BETTIAH,
May 14, 1917
MY DEAR MR. MAUDE,
As promised by me, I have sent today my representation to
the Chief Secretary. I do hope that it will receive urgent considera-
tion.
I am making arrangements as fast as possible to alter the
method of enquiry also in terms of my undertaking.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Select Documents on Mahatma Gandhi's Movement in Champaran, No. 73. p. 131
