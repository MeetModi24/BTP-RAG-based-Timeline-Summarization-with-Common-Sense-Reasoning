281. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
October 30, 1934
BHAI RAJENDRABABU,
In accordance with my decision I hereby dissociate myself
from the All-India Congress Committee. My name may please
be removed from the list in the office of the Committee.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RAJENDRA PRASAD
PRESIDENT, [INDIAN] NATIONAL CONGRESS
C/O BIRLA MILLS, DELHI
[From Hindi]
A.I.C.C. File No. 463, 1934. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
