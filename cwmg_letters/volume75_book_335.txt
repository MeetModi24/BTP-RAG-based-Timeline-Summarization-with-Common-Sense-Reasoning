335. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
February 1, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Your question is right.
Ordinarily such things cannot be
passed. But it is reasonable to give this much to Lakshmidas.
He should be paid every month, if he asks for it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4411
