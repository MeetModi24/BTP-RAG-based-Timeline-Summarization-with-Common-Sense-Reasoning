372. LETTER TO JETHALAL G. SAMPAT
SABARMATI,
Chaitra Vad 10 [April 18, 1925]
BHAI JETHALAL,
You will have informed Lakshmidas of your decision. Only
if he agrees should you leave Ramesara. I am going to Bengal
in a few days, so I cannot say when we shall meet. I do not even
know how long I shall be able to stay at the Ashram.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9840. Courtesy: Narayan Jethalal
Sampat
