452. LETTER TO PRABHU DAYAL VIDYARTHI
February 19, 1941
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
Whatever you do you must do it with Kakasaheb's consent and
blessings. You may live anywhere you like but so long as you do
not earn your bread through work, I shall not be satisfied.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 11702
