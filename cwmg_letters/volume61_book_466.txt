466. LETTER TO BHUJANGILAL CHHAYA
WARDHA,
August 10, 1935
CHI. BHUJANGILAL,
Do what your conscience tells you and your strength permits
you to do. What others may advise is not one's dharma. Dharma
is what a man himself believes to be such. How can another
man know your heart? You should, therefore, pray to God to help
you to recognize your path. He is the only true guide.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2607
