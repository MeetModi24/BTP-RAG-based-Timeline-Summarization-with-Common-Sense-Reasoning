201. LETTER TO DEVRAJ
August 14, 1945
BHAI DEVRAJ,
I have your letter. I am sorry you felt hurt.
But I am
sorrier that you cannot see what is so obvious.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI DEVRAJJI
YOGIC PHYSICAL CULTURE INSTITUTE
PLEADER STREET
LYALLPUR, PUNJAB
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
