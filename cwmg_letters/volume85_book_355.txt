355. LETTER TO SHIVAJI
NEW DELHI,
September 9, 1946
BHAI SHIVAJI,
I have your letter. I was indeed sorry that even though
Kakodkar was consuming fruit and milk he was considered to
be fasting. At least you could have said that he was fasting
on milk and fruit. When we utter what is untruth or very
near to it it is our duty to correct ourselves even though this
should cause us temporary harm. My experience is that this
never causes harm. I am confessing my error through the Press
and to the Goan Government.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
