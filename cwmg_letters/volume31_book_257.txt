257. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Saturday, Asadha Vad 7 [July 31, 1926]
BHAISHRI BEHRAMJI,
I got your letter. I was very happy indeed to read about
the improvement in your health. Please write to me once again
before you leave for Bombay.
Blessings to both of you from
BAPU
SHRIYUT BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
8, NAPIER ROAD
CAMP
POONA
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 4365. Courtesy: Tehmina Khambhatta;
also G.N. 6586
