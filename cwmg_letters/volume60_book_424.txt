424. LETTER TO L. K. KIRLOSKAR
March 19, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your exhaustive letter in reply to mine of the
10th instant. Sjt. Banker happened to be here when it came and
I handed him a copy of your letter. I have now forwarded to
him my opinion of which I enclose herewith a copy.
I was tempted to add a paragraph about the lower use which
in my discussion with you I showed could be made of the machine.
I avoided mention of it for I remembered your objection to it.
But if you want me to give my opinion to the judges about the
lower use, I will gladly send it to them.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1 Banker's letter
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
