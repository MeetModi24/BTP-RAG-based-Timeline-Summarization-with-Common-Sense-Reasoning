107. LETTER TO JAGDISH SHASTRI
January 19, 1935
BHAI JAGDISH SHASTRI,
A list of the chapters of the essay on cow-protection is en-
closed herewith. You can add to it, you may even change the order.
1. Origin of cow-protection.
2. The position in the Vedic Age.
3. The position in the Age of the Smritis.
4. The position in the Age of the Epics.
5. The attitude of other religions.
6. Origin of dispute with the Muslims.
7. Is cow-protection an essential feature of Hinduism? If
it is, then what does it signify? Does the tradition date back to
the ancient times?
Difference between the attitudes of the
Arya Samajists and sanatanists and its critical appreciation.
8. The present position—census of goshalas. Difference
between Jain pinjarapoles and Marwari goshalas and feasibility of
cow-protection through them.
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
