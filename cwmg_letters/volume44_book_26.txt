26. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 13, 1930
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your letter. What does work mean? Is not spinning
and carding work? All work done sincerely has equal value. True
soldiership or devotion to God or spiritual living consists in being
content with the duty which falls to our lot. Absorption in the work
of service which has come to us unasked is the only true samadhi.
It is quite true that attaining this state of mind is a slow pro-
cess. All that one can do, therefore, is to go on striving. The result
lies in God's hands. We are both in good health. I will give more
information on this subject in the common letter for the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
