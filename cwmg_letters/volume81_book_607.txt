607. LETTER TO K. T. SHAH
October 10, 1945
BHAI SHAH,
Only today I read a copy of the article Chi. Kishorelal
had sent to [The Bombay] Chronicle around June 15. It is a
short one which I am enclosing herewith. It will not take you
long to glance through it.
If you find his suggestions worth while, you yourself can
do further research. If what he says is right and if decimal
coinage is going to be forced on the people, you can play an
important part in opposing it.
K. T. SHAH
8 LABURNUM ROAD
GAMDEVI, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
