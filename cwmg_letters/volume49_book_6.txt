6. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
January 5, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
I have been writing to you regularly. I am not sure if some-
times I forget about it. But one who follows the path of non-violence
would not pay a person in the same coin. He would do a good
turn to someone who has done nothing for him or even to one
who has done him harm. Hence, even if I am lazy, you should
not be. If your yarn snapped 12 times in 47 turns, you could
have done 36 more turns if it had not snapped at all. It is my
experience that if the yarn snaps once, re-uniting it takes the time
required for spinning three turns.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9943. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokhawala
