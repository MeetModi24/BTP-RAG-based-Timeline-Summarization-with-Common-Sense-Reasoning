228. LETTER TO UTTIMCHAND GANGARAM
SEVAGRAM,
August 23, 1946
BHAI UTTIMCHAND,
I have your letter. I was delighted to see your Hindu-
stani. I read the Urdu also. I am sending the cheque to Sar-
dar. I like the analogy from geometry and I got the answer too
by drawing the figure.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI UTTIMCHAND GANGARAM
BOMBAY BAKERY
HYDERABAD (SIND)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
