110. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL AND
KASHI GANDHI
September 18, 1932
CHI. CHHAGANLAL AND KASHI,
It is night and the time is much too short for all the letters
which I wish to write. What shall I write to you? Please look
upon the days of my fast as a time of rejoicing. Prabhudas I
forbid you to get agitated. Use all your wisdom to cultivate the
utmost devotion to service. God will surely help you.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 47
