432. LETTER TO GOPINATH BARDOLOI
CONTAI,
December 31, 1945
BHAI BARDOLOI,
With great difficulty I can spare five days for Assam. You
can have me freed for Assam but do not be tempted to keep me
there too many days or take me to too many places. I had ex-
plained this to the man who had come from there. He too was
convinced. The truth is that I am no longer fit for travelling.
I had to visit Bengal, so I included Assam also. What else can
I do? It does not look nice to go on refusing. I am receiving
wires. Please save me.
Blessings from
BAPU
BARDOLOI
