365. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
December 29, 1946
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. Why do you write yourself? An anti-
phlogistine tin is being sent. Ma will send you someone to
work for you according to your instructions. Dictate your letters.
Drink plenty of water.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 549
