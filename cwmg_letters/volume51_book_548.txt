548. LETTER TO PRABODHKUMAR AND OTHERS
November 2, 1932
CHI. PRABODHKUMAR, BHUPENDRAKUMAR AND NARENDRAKUMAR,
The names of all of you seem very short. Hence think how
to make them longer. A potter would never be satisfied with a
badly shaped pot. He will mix up the clay again and again with
the unused lot till he gets a well-shaped pot. Similarly, Prabodh
should not write to me till his handwriting has improved. He
should first learn to draw straight lines, triangles, circles, etc.
Sardar, Mahadevbhai and I, all three of us, are well. May all of
you grow up and be good men.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12152
