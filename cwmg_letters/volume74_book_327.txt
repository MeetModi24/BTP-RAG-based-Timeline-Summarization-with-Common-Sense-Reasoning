327. LETTER TO RATANMONI CHATTERJI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
August 3, 1941
DEAR RATANMONI,
You have done well in giving me details about Dr. Das.
His death is a distinct loss. We can but intensify our devotion to
the country's cause. Please convey my condolences to his aged
mother.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
SHRI RATANMONI CHATTERJI
DISTRICT CONGRESS COMMITTEE
G. T. ROAD
P.O. SERAMPORE, DIST. HOOGHLY
BENGAL
From a photostat: G.N. 8026. Also C.W. 10342. Courtesy: Ratanmoni
Chatterji
