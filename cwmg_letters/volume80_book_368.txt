368. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 30, 1945
CHI. VASUMATI,
I could take up your letter only today. What you write is
correct. Everyone works according to his ability. You and I
cannot be exceptions. Take care of your health and keep on
doing whatever you have to do. We should never lose faith
that God looks after everyone. Ask Akbar.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VASUMATIBEHN
C/O DAULATRAM KASHIRAM
KARELWADI
THAKURDWAR ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
