427. LETTER TO HAROLD F. BING
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 18, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your letter, but I am able to reach it only today. I
see that the date you have given me for my message to reach you
is the 15th instant, and I am dictating this on the 18th. I am
therefore writing to express to you my regret that I was unable
to send you the message.
Yours sincerely,
HAROLD F. BING, ESQ.
ORGANIZING SECRETARY
THE BRITISH FEDERATION OF YOUTH
LONDON W.C. I.
From a photostat: S.N. 14988
