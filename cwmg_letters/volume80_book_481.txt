481. LETTER TO BHARATAN KUMARAPPA
PANCHGANI,
June 9, 1945
MY DEAR BHARATAN,
So far as possible, I want to give you something for every issue
of the Patrika . You do well therefore to remind me in time and to
give me a hint as to the subject on which you will like me to write.
Love.
[PS.]
Of course J. C. [Kumarappa] will be quite well.
SHRI BHARATAN KUMARAPPA
A. I. V. I. A.
MAGANWADI, WARDHA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
