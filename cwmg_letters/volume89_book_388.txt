388. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
October 23, 1947
CHI. SITA,
I got your postcard. You must have received the letter
I wrote to you. May you live long and be a sincere worker.
It is a matter for joy that Vasant has given birth to a son
and that both mother and son are well.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
CHI. SITA GANDHI
MASHRUWALA BHAVAN
AKOLA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 5017
1 A constructive worker devoted to Harijan uplift and propagation of khadi
2 Vide pp. 377-.
3 Wife of Kanti Mashruwala, addressee's maternal uncle
