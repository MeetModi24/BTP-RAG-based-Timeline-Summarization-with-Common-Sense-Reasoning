638. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Y. M.,
January 15, 1933
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Muldas's account is excellent. I had learnt about it from
someone. Such tours may prove very effective. They are an
object-lesson and real propaganda.
I saw your signature after many days. I do think of you
constantly of course. I am certainly hungry for silent devotion.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: G. N. 11559
