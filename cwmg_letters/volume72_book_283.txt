283. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
DELHI,
July 3, 1940
You must have seen in the Press my public appeal to every
Briton.
Nevertheless, for the sake of courtesy, I enclose here-
with a copy.
You will note the last sentence of my appeal.
Will you please convey the contents of it to the proper quarters?
The appeal and the offer at the end represent my personal and
humble contribution to Britain's cause. It is the best I could
give.
Harijan 21-7-1940
