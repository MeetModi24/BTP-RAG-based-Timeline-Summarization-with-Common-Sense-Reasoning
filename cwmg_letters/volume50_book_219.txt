219. LETTER TO MOHAN N. PARIKH
July 17, 1932
DEAR MOHAN,
I was glad that you wrote to me. Tell me in your next
letter how you spent the ekadashi day. Uncle Mahadev sends his
blessings to you, and Sardar also would send his if you give him
your respects in your letter. I forget, I see that you have done
that. So accept his blessings too.
Improve your handwriting. There should be no blots.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9181
