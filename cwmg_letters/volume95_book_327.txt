327. LETTER TO JIVRAJ MEHTA
Wednesday, Pausa Sud
DEAR JIVRAJ,
Both of us seem to be in good health so far. The pain in my ribs
has neither increased nor decreased. The weather has been very
inclement. There should be some improvement tomorrow. My diet
consists of fresh chapatis and fruits. I have not felt the need to have
anything else. I would not forget your affection. You must have
passed the examination. Keep writing to me.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: Jivraj Mehta Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
