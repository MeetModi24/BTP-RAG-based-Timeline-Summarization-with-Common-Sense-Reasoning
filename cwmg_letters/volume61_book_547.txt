547. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
August 30, 1935
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Hand over the respective letters to Ishwarbhai and Kanti.
I had your letters and saw the account. I suppose you have
enough funds. Tell me when you need any; your account is satis-
factory. It is good that you made a study of the vegetables, etc.
My apology was only for my own spiritual good but I am happy
to learn of the deep impression it has made on you. You already
possess a good capacity for work and Savli will help you attain
equanimity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1880
