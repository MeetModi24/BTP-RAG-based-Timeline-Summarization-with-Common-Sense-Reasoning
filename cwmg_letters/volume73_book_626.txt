626. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
April 12, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
I have your postcard. I am glad you like the place. I am
sure you will have the solitude you desire. Harakhchand bhai
is a pearl. His left hand knoweth not what his right gives. Give
him my love.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6477. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9872
