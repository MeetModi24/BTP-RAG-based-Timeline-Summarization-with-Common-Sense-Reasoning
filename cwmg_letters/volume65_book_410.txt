410. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
June 30, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
The doctor's explanation seems satisfactory. What weight
need we attach to Shankar's allegations? He has always been
like that. You do not seem to have read the letter I wrote to
him. I have severely reprimanded him, though I have not written
all that you said. I will write that, too. Write to Shantikumar
and tell him that I will decide now . You can send for a flask
from Bombay. How can you get an exactly similar one? The
goat seems to be proving a costly affair. I am in a fix. I am
sending the letters.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Since you also would be writing to Kakalbhai, I am not clos-
ing the envelope. Received Gokulbhai's postcard. I forgot to
tell you. Write to him and tell him that he can certainly fix for
the 10th.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11526
