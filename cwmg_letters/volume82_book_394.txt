394. LETTER TO MUKHERJEE
MAHISHADAL,
December 26, 1945
BHAI MUKHERJEE,
I have your letter dated 23rd December. I have had a talk
with your son. I hope your wife is now well. I have gone
through all the papers from Nepal that you have sent. I do not
see what help they can be. This work perhaps can be done
only by people like you who are familiar with it. It cannot be
done with the help of a certificate from the Government of
Nepal or anyone else.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
