554. LETTER TO HAQQI
February 25, 1933
DEAR PROFESSOR,
As I have already told you, I wrote to Baboo Bhagwandas
of Benares. I hope he has written to you, but if he has not, you
should put yourself in touch with him. His address is as follows:
"Seva Ashram, Sigra, Benares Cantt".
I hope to find another friend very shortly. As soon as I
have met him, I shall see that he writes to you.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20384
