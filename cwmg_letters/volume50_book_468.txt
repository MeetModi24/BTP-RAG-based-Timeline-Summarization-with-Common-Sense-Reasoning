468. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
August 28, 1932
BHAI KHAMBHATTA,
I got your letter and the ointment sent by you. I have
started using the ointment. I will let you know the result.
Blessings to you both from
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6603. Also C.W. 4389. Courtesy:
Tehmina Khambhatta
