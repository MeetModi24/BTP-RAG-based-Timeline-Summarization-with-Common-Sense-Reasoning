67. LETTER TO RAMI PAREKH
Thursday [November 18, 1926]
CHI. RAMI,
I have your letter after a long time. Everybody is happy here.
There is no special illness nowadays. Chi. Ramdas was here for a
couple of days. He is in good health. He is touring in Kathiawar and
selling khadi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S. N. 9702
1 Vide Vol. XXXII, p. 64.
2 Son of Gandhiji
