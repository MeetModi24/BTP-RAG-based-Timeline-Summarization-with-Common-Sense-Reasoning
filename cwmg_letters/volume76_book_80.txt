80. LETTER TO PADAMPAT SINGHANIA
SEVAGRAM,
April 21, 1942
BHAI PADAMPATJI,
I deliberately withheld the reply to your letter. Even now I
have not met the trustees. I have however sent you the con-
stitution of the Hindustani Prachar Sabha. It would facilitate
my arriving at a decision if you could let me have your opinion
on it. But if you would rather not, I will not insist.
Blessings from
BAPU
