51. LETTER TO SURENDRARAI B. MEDH
April 14, 1942
CHI. SURENDRA MEDH1,
I have your letter. This time you gave me no news about
happenings there. They do not allow Indian Opinion into India.
We hardly get any letters. If Bhai Hamid comes to me, I will
do what I can. I do not have much say in such matters. Still
I will try.
At present there is a scarcity of khadi and woollen material
here. We cannot get enough to send to anybody outside.
Ba is quite well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4929
