588. LETTER TO GAJANAN NAIK
October 8, 1945
CHI. GAJANAN (NAIK),
I have your letter. Just think what my loyalty demands.
Either I should pay no attention to your complaints or place
them before Kumarappa and hear his side also and then ask for
an explanation from you and then give the decision. Your own
loyalty also demands the same.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
