511. LETTER TO BASSIC
[September 29, 1945]
DEAR BASSIC,
Many thanks for your letter and draft. You have correctly
guessed my age. I did not know your good father-in-law was
dead. So shall we all one day. I hope you are all keeping well.
Amtul Salaam is working for khadi in Bengal. What work of
service are you doing? You have not given me your full name.
Nevertheless I hope this will reach you.
P. O. DEHENNU via KHANNA
LUDHIANA DISTRICT
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
