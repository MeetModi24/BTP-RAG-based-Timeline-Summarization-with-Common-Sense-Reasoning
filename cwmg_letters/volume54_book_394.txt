394. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
April 8, 1933
CHI. KAKA,
I got your letter. I sent my reply to Keshav three days ago.
It was a brief one. He must have seen you by now.
These friends may start using honey at the Eucharist. But
other Christians, and particularly the Roman Catholics, will never
agree to do that, since the word used in the service is "wine".
It is more likely, however, that they might use grape juice which
has no intoxicating effect. Some scholars do interpret "wine"
in the original as "unfermented grape juice". So in any case
there must be grape juice. Such blameless juice is used in some
churches, and nowadays hundreds of thousands of bottles of
such grape juice are available. In Trivedi's books also which
you have sent, different methods of obtaining such grape juice
are described. Hence the suggestions to use it can be imme-
diately implemented.
It could be very good if that gentleman undertakes to pub-
lish a Marathi edition of Harijan.
Give me detailed information about you from time to time.
Send me that American book about eye treatment. Do get
your eyes treated there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9496. Courtesy: D. B. Kalelkar
