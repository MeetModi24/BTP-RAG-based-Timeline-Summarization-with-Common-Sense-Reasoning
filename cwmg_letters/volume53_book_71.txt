71. LETTER TO A YOUTH
January 15, 1933
A great many such things are being written about me. The
article is such a palpable lie that I hope nobody will put his
trust in it. And if there are any people who put their trust in
it, my reply to it will have no effect on them.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 55
