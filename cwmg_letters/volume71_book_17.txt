17. LETTER TO SURENDRA
December 7, 1939
CHI. SURENDRA,

ferable to have several wives rather than secretly indulge in immo-
ral practices. This truth may seem bitter but its fruits would be
very sweet. The relative praise of polygamy implicit in it is fully
justified. The more I think the more clearly I see that the false
