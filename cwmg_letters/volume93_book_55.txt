55. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
WARDHA,
August 16, 1935
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
As I have now begun to take a direct part in the adminis-
tration of the A.I.S.A. affairs, I am expected to deal with various
standing complaints. One of these is about the Punjab branch.
I would like you to give me your brief replies . . . Khadi
Bhandar opened by H. Kotak is required in Lahore. There are
complaints about the management of the Kashmir branch. I
would like to have your views about that branch.
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
Please return the enclosed when replying.
From the original: Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
