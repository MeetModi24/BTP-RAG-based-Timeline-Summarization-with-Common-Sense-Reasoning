282. LETTER TO DR. HASAN
NEW DELHI,
July 7, 1947
BHAI HASAN,
I have your letter. Who wrote it? Neither the handwriting
nor the language of the letter appears to be yours.
What do Bhai Gopalrao and Dada Dharmadhikari say?
I am having another complaint. I desire that at least a few
Muslims should prove perfect in every way. I want to see you
become such a one. The time is extremely delicate. I am sending
a copy of your letter to Shuklaji.
Blessings from
BAPU
