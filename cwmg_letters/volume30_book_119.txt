119. LETTER TO LALLUBHAI B. PATEL
ASHRAM,
Friday, March 12, 1926
BHAI LALLUBHAI,
I have your letter. I think it is barbarous to pierce a girl's
ears or nose [for ornaments].
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI LALLUBHAI BAKORBHAI PATEL
NAPAD
TALUK ANAND
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19858
