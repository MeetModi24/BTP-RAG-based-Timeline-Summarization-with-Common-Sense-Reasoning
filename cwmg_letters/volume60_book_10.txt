10. LETTER TO KANTI GANDHI
December 18, 1934
CHI. KANTI,
I have had no letter from you for some time. I should like
you to observe some rule. I for my part did reply to your last
letter. How are things with you? Ramdas is here at present. He
is fairly well. Devdas came and stayed for two days. Lakshmi
has gone with Rajaji. Kakasaheb is in Madras. He has gone
there to help in the Hindi work. Ba is all right. For the rest,
read Harijan.
Most probably I shall have to go to Delhi at the end of
this month.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7291. Courtesy: Kanti Gandhi
