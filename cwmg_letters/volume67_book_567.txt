567. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
DELHI,
September 29, 1938
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You did well in giving up jaggery. Do not worry about
the weakness. The chhilta water will bring back strength.
You shall certainly continue to serve me. Do not expect
letters from me. I hope Balkrishna is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
