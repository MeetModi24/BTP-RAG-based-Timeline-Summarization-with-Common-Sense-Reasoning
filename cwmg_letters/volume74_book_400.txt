400. LETTER TO UTIMCHAND GANGARAM
SEVAGRAM,
August 21, 1941
DEAR UTIMCHAND,
I have Government papers containing Rs. 500. I am hand-
ing the 3½ p.c. ones (Rs. 200) to Harijan Sevak Sangh and the
other Rs. 300 to the Deenabandhu Memorial, since Gurudev
would have donated the amount to the Memorial which, as you
know, is to be wholly used for Santiniketan.
My interest in Geometry never gets stale and I shall be your
first 'school-boy'. Whether I shall succeed in cutting off the two
triangles or not I do not know.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
