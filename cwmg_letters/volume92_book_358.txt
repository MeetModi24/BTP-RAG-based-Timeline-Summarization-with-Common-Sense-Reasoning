358. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
September 21, 1930
CHI. KANTA,
I have your letter. It is well that you have sought out Kama-
labehn's company. Try to understand her. Do learn from her the
art of nursing. You may if you wish add salt to the curd, but
do not forget that adding salt only changes the sour taste of curd,
it does not neutralize the acidity. I therefore suggest that when
the curd tastes sour you should add soda to it. Soda neutralizes
the acid in the curd. The acid combines with the soda and
evaporates in the form of carbolic acid gas, leaving the curd
sweet.
Blessings from
BAPU
