371. LETTER TO STEPHEN HOBHOUSE
NEW DELHI,
October 21, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
Many thanks for your cheering letter. The look of things
here is certainly ugly. Let us see what God has in store for
the whole of humanity. I wonder if really man's duty is to
be the helpless witness of tragedies being enacted in front of him.
Your special task is very difficult indeed.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
STEPHEN HOBHOUSE, ESQ.
20 ST. CATHERINE'S
BROX BON RUE
HERTS, ENGLAND
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
