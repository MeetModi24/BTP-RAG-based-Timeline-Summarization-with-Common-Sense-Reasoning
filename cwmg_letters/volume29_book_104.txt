104. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
Monday [January 4, 1926]
CHI. NARANDAS,
Chi. Maganlal is getting along well with his fast. There are
no complications except general weakness. Ramdas is here in
connection with [khadi work at] Amreli.
If you will come over
here you can guide me in this matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. NARANDAS KHUSHALCHAND GANDHI
OPPOSITE MIDDLE SCHOOL
NAVU PARU, RAJKOT
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7706. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
