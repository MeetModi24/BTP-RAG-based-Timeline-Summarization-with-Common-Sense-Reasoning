65. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
Thursday [Before June 1, 1926]
CHI. RAMDAS,
I have your postcard. I never got Manilal's letter. I do not know to what
address you forwarded it. Do come over here when your khadi work there is
over. Since you have given your address as C/o Post-Office I have addressed
the letter accordingly. It is likely that I may go to Finland. If I go, I will
have to start on July 1. I am thinking of taking Mahadev and Devdas with
me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
