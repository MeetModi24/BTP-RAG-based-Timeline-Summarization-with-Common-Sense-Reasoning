661. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SABARMATI,
August 20, 1928
MY DEAR LOTUSBORN 3 ,
You may not fade. You must bloom. Though I never write
you are constantly in my mind.
Love.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRIMATI PADMAJA NAIDU
TUBERCULOSIS SANATORIUM
AROGYAVARAM
CHITTOOR DIST.
S. INDIA
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
