468. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 25, 1930
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
I have your letter. I am including your name in the list but
how can I ask you to give up the responsibility which you have
undertaken there and call you here?
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
KHADI KARYALAYA
CHALALA (KATHIAWAR)
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: MMU/III/75
