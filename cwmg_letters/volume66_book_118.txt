118. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 31, 1937
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I got your letter last evening.
I am sending a wire today
about your coming here or sending Nrisinhprasad. If you can,
please do come. You do not seem to have improved in spite of
such a long tour. There is no cause for worry about me. The
hand and the brain need plenty of rest, which I am giving
them. How can your coming here or sending someone be any
trouble to me? Can a friend ever cause inconvenience? I used
to receive your cables. They didn't call for a reply.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 5952. Also C.W. 3269. Court-
esy: Mahesh Pattani
