115. LETTER TO SHUAIB QURESHI
January 21, 1935
Give my kind regards to the Nawab Saheb and tell him
that I am still paying the penalty of having said that when I
found you in your surroundings I was reminded of the great
Omar.
There is talk of communal peace, but I fear there is no
time yet. It can come only out of a heart unity and we must
wait for it. No patched-up peace can last.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary.
Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
