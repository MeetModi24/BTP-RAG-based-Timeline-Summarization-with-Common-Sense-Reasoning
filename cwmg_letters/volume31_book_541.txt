541. LETTER TO NORMAN LEYS
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 14, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the statement made before
the Court by Mr. Tarini P. Sinha. I have a copy of your book
called Kenya. But I shall value an autograph[ed] copy if you
have one to spare.
Yours sincerely,
DR. NORMAN LEYS
BRAILSFORD
NEAR DERBY
From a photostat: S.N. 12173
