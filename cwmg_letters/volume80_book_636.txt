636. LETTER TO MUNSHI
SIMLA WEST,
June 27, 1945
BHAI MUNSHIJI,
I am no doubt keeping in mind the name you have sent.
I do not know what will be feasible. Let us see what happens. I
hope the Ashram is going on well.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
