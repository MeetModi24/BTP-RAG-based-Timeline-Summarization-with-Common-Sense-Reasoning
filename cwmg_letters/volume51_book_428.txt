428. LETTER TO HENDERSON
October 17, 1932
Argument is useless when you talk of your God and my God.
Hitherto I have thought there was only one God for the wise
and the foolish, saints and sinners. Instead of arguing with
me, I suggest your praying for me, so that 'your' God may open
the eyes of my understanding and let me see what in your opi-
nion is my error.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 143. Also G.N. 4797
