200. LETTER TO G. V. MAVALANKAR
POONA,
June 21, 1944
BHAI MAVALANKAR1,
Pyarelal could not deal with the papers you had sent. He
was not attending to our discussion with sufficient care for that.
Besides, he has never concerned himself with that subject. So I
myself have done what I thought proper in this matter. I could
have written more, but I think this much will be sufficient for you.
If you think any points need further clarification, please ask me
and I will immediately explain. Your two questions regarding the
Kasturba Fund could have been drafted differently. But I have not
touched them. I wanted to save time. I have made only a slight
change regarding copyright, and it is self-explanatory.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Maganbhai's letter is enclosed. According to our under-
standing, I assume that you yourself will explain the matter to
him, won't you?
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 1249
