171. LETTER TO CHUGH
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 9, 1945
BHAI CHUGH,
I have your Wire. I have written to you that you should
treat me when I come down, haven't I? The main treatment
will be for shivering, blood-pressure and hook-worm. The climate
here is congenial. So if there is an improvement it will be
difficult to say whether it was due to the climate or the treat-
ment. If the climate here does not do any good I shall see
what your treatment does. Besides, in Sevagram you will also
treat other patients.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. CHUGH
RAVAL BUILDING
LAMINGTON ROAD (SOUTH)
BOMBAY 4
