624. LETTER TO R. ACHYUTHAN
October 4, 1941
DEAR ACHYUTHAN,
I was glad to receive your frank letter. Much misunder-
standing arises from ignorance pure and simple. Give my regard
to all.
Yours,
BAPU
(M. K. GANDHI)
SHRI R. ACHYUTHAN
STUDENT DETENU, CENTRAL JAIL
RAJAHMUNDRY
ANDHRA
