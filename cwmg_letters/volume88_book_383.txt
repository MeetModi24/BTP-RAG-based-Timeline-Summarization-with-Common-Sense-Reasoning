383. LETTER TO LORD MOUNTBATTEN
BHANGI COLONY, NEW DELHI,
July 19, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
I sent your kind letter of yesterday to Pandit Nehru and
he at once wrote saying that regard being had to your advice,
my going to Kashmir should be postponed till after Pandit
Kak's arrival in New Delhi. So I shall await Pandit Kak's arrival
and further developments.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. E. THE VICEROY
NEW DELHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, pp. 274-
