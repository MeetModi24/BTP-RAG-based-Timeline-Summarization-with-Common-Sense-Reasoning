177. LETTER TO P. R. SATHE
March 22, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I do not quite see what you are
driving at. I see nothing wrong in preaching temperance and
habits of cleanliness so long as they are not made a condition
of removal of untouchability.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. R. SATHE
PLEADER
GONDIA, C. P.
