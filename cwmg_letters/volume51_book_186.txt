186. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
September 21, 1932
CHI. MANI,
Need I try to give you courage? I forbid you to shed a sin-
gle tear. It is only rarely that a man is blessed with such good
fortune as has come to me. You should, therefore, rejoice and
not shed tears. You and others like you should not start fasting,
but should devote yourselves heart and soul to your work. I
have obtained permission for everybody to write to me whenever
they wish. Therefore do write to me.
I hope you will get this letter without delay.
Blessings from
BAPU
MANIBEHN PATEL
PRISONER
CENTRAL PRISON, BELGAUM
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 87
