486. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
November 30, 1930
BHAI MOOLCHANDJI,
Your letter to hand. You must resolutely get up at 4 a.m.
It does not matter if you do not have any motions. Cleanse your
mouth and say the prayers; drink a bowlful of cold water after
cleansing the mouth. Go out for a walk after the prayers. Walk
briskly. This will induce motions. You will feel warm instead of
cold. Don't worry if your bowels do not move. After the walk,
you can occupy yourself in whatever task you choose. Go to the
lavatory when you feel the urge. You will be cured by doing so
for some days.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
RINGAS (RAJ.)
