199. LETTER TO BODHRAJ
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 19, 1931
DEAR LALA BODHRAJ2,
I have your letter about Lala Lok Nath. Could you send
me a copy of the full record of his case. I shall then see what
can be done.
Yours sincerely,
