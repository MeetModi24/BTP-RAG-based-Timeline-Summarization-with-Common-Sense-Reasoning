206. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI G. PATEL
WARDHA,
February 3, 1935
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
I do not see any objection to your wanting to join a school.
You alone can say whether you will be able to shoulder the
responsibility of that work or not.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9514
