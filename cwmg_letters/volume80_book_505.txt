505. LETTER TO BENARSIDAS CHATURVEDI
PANCHGANI,
June 11, 1945
BHAI BENARSIDAS,
I could read Dr. Zafar Saheb's essay only yesterday. I am so
busy that I could write to him only today. The essay is interest-
ing and good. Let me have a few copies if it has been published
in pamphlet form. I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2519
