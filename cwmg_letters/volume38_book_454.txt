454. LETTER TO DR. PARASHURAM
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 23, 1929
DEAR DR. PARASHURAM,
I am glad you have given me a full letter. I note your res-
trictions which I shall bear in mind. I shall make careful use
of your important letter. It is a terrible business.
Yours sincerely,
DR. PARASHURAM
PUNJAB PROVINCIAL CONGRESS COMMITTEE, LAHORE
From a photostat: S.N. 14894
