337. LETTER TO BANARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 19, 1930
BHAI BANARASIDAS,
Bhai Kashinath has given me the news of your wife's death.
A great calamity has befallen you. We have given up the fear
of death, but we grieve out of selfishness. I understand you have
children of tender age. But why grieve over that too? Such things
do happen in the world. All such events are moments of trial to
us. Only such an occasion can prove if all our assiduously acquired
knowledge is truly assimilated. May God grant you peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2525
