216. LETTER TO SYED MAHMUD HAD
[September 27, 1947]
BHAI SYED MAHMUD HAD,
Your letter has been redirected to me by the Navajivan
Office. It is not necessary to publish it. I had said enough about it
at Calcutta.
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
