98. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 4, 1930
CHI. RUKMINI,
How is it that though you have come to the Ashram, you have
not been able to find time to write two lines to me? Do you have
peace of mind and are you contented and happy? I shall be satis-
fied if you tell me this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9312. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
