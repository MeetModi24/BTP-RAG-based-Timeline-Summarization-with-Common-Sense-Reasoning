97. LETTER TO ABBAS
February 5, 1932
CHI. ABBAS,
I got your letter. Your description of a bamboo spinning-
wheel is very fine. The wheel seems very cheap. Personally, I
find the portable Gandiva very convenient and easy to work. Has
your speed of spinning improved appreciably? I understand
what you say about carding. Has any improvement been effected
in the ginning machine? What is the highest count of the yarn
from which we can weave khadi now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6306
