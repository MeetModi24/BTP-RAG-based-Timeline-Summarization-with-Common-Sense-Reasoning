158. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 31, 1930
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
Your letter. Do go to Almora if there is an opportunity.
How is your mental state? Convey my blessings to Krishna Nair.
Continue writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2384
