412. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEGAON,
July 2, 1937
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
The doubt you have raised is extremely pertinent. I cannot
say now how I could dictate such a meaningless sentence. Read it
as follows: "But it should never be taken that the ancestors of an
adharmaja were all dharmaja themselves or that his future progeny
will all be dharmaja."
There is no harm in learning and practising hatha yoga for the
sake of physical health, but all the instructors are not fully well-versed
in it. It has also been my experience that those who do not know
the science and techniques of hatha yoga can even cause harm.
Moreover, everyone is not capable of performing each and every
asana. All this simply means that you must use your discretion
in the matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4283. Also S.G. 59
