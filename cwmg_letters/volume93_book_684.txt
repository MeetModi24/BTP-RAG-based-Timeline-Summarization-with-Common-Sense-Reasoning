684. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
Via WARDHA,
March 8, 1941
CHI. LILA,
I have your note. Do not feel defeated. You will pass.
Mahadev is at Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
LILAVATI UDESHI
KANJI KHETSI CHHATRALAYA
65 MINT ROAD
BOMBAY FORT
