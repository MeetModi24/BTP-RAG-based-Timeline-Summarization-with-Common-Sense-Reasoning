421. LETTER TO V. RAGHAVIAH
August 25, 1941
DEAR RAGHAVIAH 1 ,
You are right. Your preoccupation need not be any rea-
son for exemption, you having once taken the plunge. I could
still give you the exemption, if you insisted. But for one like
you, it is better to withdraw from the satyagraha list at least for
the time being. There would be no slur on you. But I would
leave the choice to you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SRI V. RAGHAVIAH, B.A., B.L.
NELLORE
From a copy: C.W. 10447. Courtesy: K. Linga Raju
