87. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 24, 1942
DEAR SISTER,
Bapa wrote about Father's injury. I can understand your
grief. It does not matter if you cannot come. Your resolutions
will be taken up. But I hope Raja Saheb will recover.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8003. Also C.W. 3101. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
