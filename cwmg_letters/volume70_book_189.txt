189. LETTER TO DR. JIVRAJ N. MEHTA
SIMLA,
September 5, 1939
BHAI JIVRAJ,
You are keeping good watch over my health from all that
distance. Ramjibhai showed me your telegram. I was aware
that I should not climb any heights and had therefore intended
to swallow the bitter draught of riding in a rickshaw. Your
wire put the seal on it.
BAPU
[PS.]
I am returning to Segaon today.
DR. JIVRAJ MEHTA
KHAMBHATTA HALL
ALTAMOUNT ROAD
BOMBAY
