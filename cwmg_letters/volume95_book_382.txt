382. LETTER TO AMBALAL SARABHAI
Wednesday, SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI
DEAR BROTHER,
I have received your letter. Since you have left no scope for me
to refuse, please send the car. Ultimately, one man has won against
two women, because poor Indu had also excused me.
I have written this with the left hand.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
Your Gujarati is becoming sweet.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11130. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
