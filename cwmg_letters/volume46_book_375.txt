375. LETTER TO MOHANLAL VIDYARTHI
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. There is no occasion for you to lose heart.
You should take gentle exercise, increasing in quantity as you gain
in strength. It should consist in a daily walk in the fresh air
twice, in the morning and in the evening. You should eat as
much milk as you can easily digest and fresh fruit. Omit every
other food for the time being. And you should take a hip-bath at
about noon every day sitting in the tub about 10 minutes going
upto 30 minutes if you feel comfortable. If you do not know what
hip-baths are you will find them described in my Guide to Health.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. MOHAN LAL VIDYARTHI
CAWNPORE CITY
From a photostat: S.N. 17211
