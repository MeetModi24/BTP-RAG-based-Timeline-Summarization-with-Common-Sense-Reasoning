250. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
TITHAL, BULSAR,
May 25, 1937
CHI. VIJAYA,
You have thinned down nowadays. Don't let that happen.
Why are you lazy in writing letters?
Manubhai has started writing to me. Nanabhai also was
here. I had a talk with him also. I think Nanabhai approves of
your self-restraint. He will, therefore, pacify Manubhai.
I was sorry that my return there had been postponed by
eleven days. Wherever I am, there is always some work to do
But my real work is there.
Please tell Balvantsinha that I got his letter. There is
nothing special in it which calls for reply. I am, therefore, sav-
ing my time to that extent today.
Blessings from
BAPU
