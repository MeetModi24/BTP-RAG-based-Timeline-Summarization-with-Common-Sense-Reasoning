99. LETTER TO CHAMPA R. MEHTA
NEW DELHI,
4.15 a.m., June 11, 1947
CHI. CHAMPA 2 ,
I have your letter. My advice is that, as the girls are there,
you should stay on there and return to Sevagram when it is
cooler. Let the girls study whatever they can there. You do the
same. Nothing is definite about me. Your place is ultimately
in Sevagram. More in my next letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. CHAMPABEHN MEHTA
SATISHKUNJ
PANCHGANI, DIST. SATARA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 8761. Also C. W. 1053. Cour-
tesy: Champa Mehta
