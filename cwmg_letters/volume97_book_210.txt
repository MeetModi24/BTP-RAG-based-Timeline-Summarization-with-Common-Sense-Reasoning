210. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
September 7, 1932
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I have your letter. What you want cannot be sent for Rentia Baras. How
do you forget that we are prisoners? But you may imagine that our yarn is
there. Mahadev draws yam of about hundred counts from Keshu's slivers. If
there is a proper spindle, it would not take much to draw a thinner yam. I
spin a yarn of 40 counts on Magan spinning-wheel. It takes about three hours
to spin 250 rounds of yarn. It takes much of my time. But as long as my hand
gives work, I will stick to it. This time, I do not have with me the right
mechanic. Otherwise, I would have got the alterations made. Whatever changes
you make, do not sacrifice the simplicity of the spinning-wheel. In order to
draw fine yarn, I feel the distance between the spindle and the tip should be
reduced. There are more chances of fine yarn getting snapped if drawn long.
The spindle should be shorter and thinner than it is. This is only my thinking.
That you have set up a limit [of work] is more than enough. Do not be
impatient. Do not build castles in the air. Keep writing to me regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
