89. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
Wednesday [February 1, 1928]
CHI. RAMDAS,
Bravo! Now that you have become a family man, you must needs stop
writing to me! That will not do. Things are going on quite well here. The
mail is going just now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
