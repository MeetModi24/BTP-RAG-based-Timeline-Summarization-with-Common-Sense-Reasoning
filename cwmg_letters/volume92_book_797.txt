797. LETTER TO RANI VIDYAVATI
WARDHA,
September 20, 1934
CHI. VIDYA,
I had received your letter.
You will have seen my statement. Remain free from arro-
gance and do only so much by way of service as you can. I do
not see the need of your coming to the Bombay Congress. There
will be no chance at all of my being able to see anyone. Those
who come will not be coming out of any desire to serve. In
such a situation, those who wish to follow the path of service
would do well to serve wherever they may be stationed.
If I am not in prison in December and if I am at Wardha,
you may certainly spend a couple of months with me. Let us
see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
