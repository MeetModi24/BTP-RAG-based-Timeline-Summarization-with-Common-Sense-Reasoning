93. LETTER TO SOLOMON ALEXANDER
PATNA,
March 16, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
Of course I knew your brother very well indeed. But I
plead with you to spare me at the present moment. I must not
divide my attention for things great or small. It will be time for
me to consider others if I come out safe from the fire which I am
trying to quench. The odds are so great that the fire may quench
me, instead of my quenching it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SOLOMON ALEXANDER, ESQ.
BARRISTER-AT-LAW
25 RUE TALAAT H A R B
ALEXANDRIA
(EGYPT)
From a photostat: G. N. 8037
