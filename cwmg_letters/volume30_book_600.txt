600. LETTER TO S. G. VAZE
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 30, 1926
MY DEAR VAZE,
I have your circular letter about the Society's publications.
I have just heard that Sastri is there. But as the matter contained
in my letter to him is urgent lest he might not be there I send
you herewith a copy. And if my offer is at all considered possible
of acceptance you will be at liberty yourself to come and take
up your abode in the Ashram and bring with you as many as
you like and edit the paper from the Ashram till the press is
re-established in Poona.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 10912
