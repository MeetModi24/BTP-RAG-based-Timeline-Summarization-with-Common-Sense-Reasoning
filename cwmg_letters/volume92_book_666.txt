666. LETTER TO AMBALAL SARABHAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
April 4, 1933
DEAR BROTHER,
I am fascinated by the love both of you show for your
children. But I think your anxiety for Mridula is unnecessary
and harmful. Her sacrifice and her courage should delight you
so that she can feel reassured. But your anxiety cannot but
affect her mind. I have been impelled to write this because all
your actions are guided by reason and you do nothing without
proper thought. I know that reason cannot control our feelings.
But this is not wholly true. Man can succeed to a certain extent
if he tries to fight back feelings before they assail him. Leave Mridu
under God's protection. Her strength of character is her real
shield. She will surmount all difficulties. It is the earnest wish of
us all that you should have this much faith and save yourself
from anxiety.
All of you will of course go to Mussoorie.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
