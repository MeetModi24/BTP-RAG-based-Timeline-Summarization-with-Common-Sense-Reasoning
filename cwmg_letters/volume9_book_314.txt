314. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
October 19, 1909
MY LORD,
I have to thank you very much for your letter of the 18th instant,
and for your very kind and excellent advice.
I have now sent a letter to Lord Crewe, copy of which I enclose
herewith, and which I think embodies all the points of your letter. I
hope it will meet with Your Lordship's approval.
I remain, etc.,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5137
