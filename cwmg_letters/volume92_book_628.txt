628. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day [1932]
CHI. MANGALA,
How can I not remember you? What is the news about
Pushpa with the pointed nose? We shall meet when God wants
us to meet. But we meet in the spirit every day, do we not?
As for the body it is here today and gone tomorrow. What does
it matter whether physically we meet or not? Tell Premabehn
that she should tell you the story of Lucy and her seven
brothers.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C. W. 11105. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
