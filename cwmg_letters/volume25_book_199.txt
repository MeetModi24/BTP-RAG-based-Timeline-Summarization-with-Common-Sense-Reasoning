199. LETTER TO ANNIE BESANT
DELHI,
October 18, 1924
DEAR DR. BESANT,
Dr. Ansari gave me your letter with the papers today. You
may put me down as a convener. I have no choice as to the place.
If it is the third week of November I should make a strenuous
effort to attend. I think that you should be the Provisional
Secretary.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary: Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
