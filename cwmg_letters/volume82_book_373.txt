373. LETTER TO SANTOSH KUMAR BASU
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR,
December 24, 1945
DEAR SANTOSH BABU,
Many thanks for your letter and the papers. I have read your
statement with painful interest. I propose to make full use of the
facts vouchsafed by you. I return the papers as suggested by you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SANTOSH KUMAR BASU
65 DIAMOND HARBOUR ROAD, KIDDERPORE
From a photostat: C.W. 10554
