81. LETTER TO ARTHUR J. DAVIS
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W. 1,
October 1, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
Thank you for your letter. Here is my message:
"It was a brave step, worthy of America, to have undertaken
the most difficult task for her of total prohibition. It would be
a shame and a rude shock to reformers throughout the world if for
any cause America abandoned the policy and returned to the
drink evil."
Yours sincerely,
MR. ARTHUR J. DAVIS
THE MASSACHUSETTS ANTI-SALOON LEAGUE
345 TREMONT BUILDING
73 TREMONT STREET
BOSTON, MASS.
From a photostat: S.N. 17808
