50. LETTER TO KHWAJA NAZIMUDDIN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
April 21, 1938
DEAR SIR NAZIMUDDIN,
I thank you for your letter. Of course, I understand that
everything has to go to the Cabinet before the final decision is
taken. My suggestions were, therefore, intended to be indepen-
dent and for examination on their merits.
Only, I had felt that
the policy so far as the detenus are concerned was a reiteration
of that adopted by the Cabinet. I am anxiously awaiting your
final decision. But before it is taken, if my presence is considered
necessary you are only to send me a line-even a wire if
necessary.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Hindustan Times, 4-10-1938; also C.W. 9921
