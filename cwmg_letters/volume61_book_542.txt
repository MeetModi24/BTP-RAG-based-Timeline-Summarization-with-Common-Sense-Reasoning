542. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM L. BAVISHI
August 29, 1935
BHAI PURUSHOTTAM,
I got your letters and the seed. Mirabehn has relinquished
charge of farming for the present. It is in Dahyabhai's hands. It is
enough that you are doing your best. I understand about cactus.
Will you please enumerate its uses? Have you had experience
about them?
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 128. Also C.W. 4749. Courtesy:
Purushottam L. Bavishi
