144. LETTER TO HASAN ALI
SIMLA,
May 7, 1946
BHAI HASAN ALI,
It is beyond me to answer your letter. Yours is a typical
letter illustrating that even things done with the best of motives
can be perversely interpreted.
M. K. GANDHI
KARACHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
