262. LETTER TO DR. P. SUBBAROYAN
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 21, 1945
MY DEAR SUBBAROYAN,
I was glad to have your letter. Regard me as partner in
your joint joy on the restoration of your son to liberty. May
he soon join you! Mohan ought to see me this week. I like
him. The para about Rajaji was redundant as you say. He
left on 19th.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
You will come when I return to Wardha.
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
