114. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
[January] 12, 1931
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your postcard. I did not get your earlier letter. When
were you released? Who else were released with you? How is
your health? How is Dr. Ansari? What are you doing at pre-
sent?
We are both well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2387
