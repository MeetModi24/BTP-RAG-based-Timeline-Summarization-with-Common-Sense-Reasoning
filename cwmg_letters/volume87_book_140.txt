140. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
PATNA,
March 23, 1947
CHI. AMRIT,
I sent a love note yesterday thro[ugh] Mridula. I have
yours of 21st.
When I get the time to write, your letters are not before me.
Hence the omissions.
Tell me whether you have the dictionaries you wanted.
I fancy that I wrote to you twice about paying me a brief
visit but never at the sacrifice of your duty undertaken there.
Mine may be regarded as a selfish wish. We shall exchange
thoughts about it when the 4th April is on us.
I was satisfied with your answer about Aryanayakum.
I think now I have answered all your questions, your letter
was in front of me.
I have taken the Monday silence. It is now 6.10 [p. m.]
when I finish this.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 4192. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7828
