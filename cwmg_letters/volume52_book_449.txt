449. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
January 1, 1933
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
Your letter. Sun-bathing should do you good. It would be
excellent if you took some rest too. All our actions must be
performed with detachment.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HEMPRABHA DEVI
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
COLLEGE SQUARE, CALCUTTA
