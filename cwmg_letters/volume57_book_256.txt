256. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
March 5, 1934
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
The tour this time is proving so demanding that I am unable
to write letters to my satisfaction.
So you have entered your
thirty-fifth year ! My blessings are ever with you. God will see
to your well-being and take further services from you. As for the
treatment, you must implicitly follow Dr. Ansari's directions.
Mother, I trust, has recovered.
I reach Patna on the 11th instant.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2409
