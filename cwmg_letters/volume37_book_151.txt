151. LETTER TO D. C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
If you have the strength of mind you will certainly wear
khadi even if you incur the displeasure of your employers. Our
duty is to so act as not to cause injury to others. Duty is a debt
and discharge of a debt does not carry any reward with it unless
of satisfaction with oneself.
We pray to feel strong and purified.
Yours sincerely,
D.C. RAJAGOPALACHARI 3
L/78/ CORAL MERCHANT STREET, MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 13903
