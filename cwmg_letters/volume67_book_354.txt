354. LETTER TO C. P. RAMASWAMI IYER
[August 15, 1938]
DEAR FRIEND,
In order better to guide R. and through him the State Con-
gress and if at all possible to avoid friction between the State
and the people, I am sending Shri Rajkumari Amrit Kaur to
study the situation and to offer friendly assistance. She expects
to leave here tomorrow and to proceed to Trivandrum after a
day's stay in Madras. The Rajkumari will not address public meet-
ings or do any public propaganda.
I hope you will make full
use of her services.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: C.W. 4217. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7853
