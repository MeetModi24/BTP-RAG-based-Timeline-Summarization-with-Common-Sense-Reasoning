477. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
October 31, 1928
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I have your letter. I can understand Bipin Babu's attitude.
But I stand unmoved. The position is becoming clearer to me
day by day, and we must learn to forget that life is everything
and that death is nothing. Indeed, we must learn to regard death
as a thing of joy.
If the Exhibition is going to be what you suggest, we are well
out of it.
You say nothing about Tarini's health in this letter of yours.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR
From a photostat: S.N. 13578
