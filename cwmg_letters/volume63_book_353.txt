353. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEGAON,
September 14, 1936
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have from Nanavati the painful news that you have been
weeping like mad. You will postpone your coming to Segaon
so long as, while there, you do not do as I say. I had been
expecting a letter in your beautiful handwriting but what I have is
only bad news! What is this? Be reasonable and do as I tell
you. I am helpless if you must persist in hurting me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9344. Also C.W. 6619. Court-
esy: Lilavati Asar
