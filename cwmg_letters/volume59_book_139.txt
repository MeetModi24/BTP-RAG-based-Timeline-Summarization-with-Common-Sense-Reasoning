139. LETTER TO VITTHAL L. PHADKE
October 3, 1934
CHI. MAMA,
I got your clean letter. I have already written to you that
I will believe what you say. I, therefore, welcome your assurance.
But it does not change my view about . . .1. If he cannot
behave as a soldier, he certainly cannot continue to work in the
Sangh.
MAMA PHADKE
GODHRA
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
