172. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 5, 1930
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
Narandas tells me in his letter that I have not written to
you. But I do remember that I wrote to you. Do you remember
any letter of yours to which I did not reply? Send me a report
of the activities on that side. How is Kasumba? Why did Yukti
come away? If she can write, she should write to me. How is
your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./III
