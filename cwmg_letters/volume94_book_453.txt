453. LETTER TO SHIV NARAYAN TANDON
URULI,
March 29, 1946
BHAI SHIV NARAYAN,
I am firmly of the opinion that it will be more becoming on the
part of you all to find the money for the building, etc. And it will be
good for the Kasturba Memorial Trust too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
