334. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
SEGAON,
October 22, 1939
CHI. MUNNALAL,
What a confusion reigns in your mind! Read your note
again.
In one place you say that you would go to Ramana
Maharshi since I advise you to do so but later on say that you
do not know where to go.
I suggest only one thing. You
should go straight from here to Ramana Maharshi's Ashram,
stay there for seven days and if you feel no benefit by the end
of that period either go to Pondicherry or return here straight.
Did you understand what I told you yesterday about
Shankaran? If you did not, reflect over it deeply till you
understand it. It was no ordinary mistake.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 8557. Also C.W. 7059. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
