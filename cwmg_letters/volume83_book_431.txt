431. LETTER TO SIR STAFFORD CRIPPS
HARIJAN MANDIR,
April 5, 1946
DEAR SIR STAFFORD,
Many thanks for your affectionate letter. Sudhir has given
me your message too. I am here at least till 16th instant at
Maulana Saheb's behest.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 181
