326. LETTER TO P. H. CADRE
POONA,
August 31, 1945
DEAR GADRE,
Why should you feel sore if you have done your duty? Duty
is its own reward! If the Committee do not want your services,
you will serve where you are wanted. The field of service is
as wide as the earth. The donors should not mind the removal
of the [status]. The question is whether you have become
yourself a Harijan? If you have, all is well.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI P. H. GADRE
PLEADER
HINDU COLONY, NASIK 2
