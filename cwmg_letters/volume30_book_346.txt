346. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Wednesday, April 14, 1926
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
The Parishad's [Working] Committee have resolved to take
over the Amreli Centre, and they have further resolved to convert
it into a trust. There was much discussion. Credit to the account
of the Conference whatever commission falls due to Gariyadhar
and Panch Talawadi. Do not clear any dues in cash.
