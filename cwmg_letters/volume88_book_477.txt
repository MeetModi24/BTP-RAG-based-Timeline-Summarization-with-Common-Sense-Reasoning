477. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
July 26, 1947
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
I have your letter. At the moment it seems there is some
improvement in Manu's condition.
She gets clear motions.
There is no pain and no fever. She eats well. But I have to
force her to take complete rest. She is fidgeting to resume
her service of me. But I do not let her. She is being given no
medicine. She is under Sushilabehn's treatment, and the latter
believes, as I do, that the only medicine she requires is com-
plete rest. Manu has now begun to realize this.
Blessings from
BAPU
