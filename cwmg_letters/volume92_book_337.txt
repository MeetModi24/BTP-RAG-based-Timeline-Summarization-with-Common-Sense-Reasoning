337. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
Y. M.,
August 2, 1930
CHI. KANTA,
All right, here is a letter in Devanagari. You will have fully
recovered. I can understand Mother's continuing to worry. You
should write to her politely but firmly. If you do so, she will
calm down. Have faith that all will be well with you.
I hope Brother is now keeping good health. How many persons
are there with him in the Ashram? How many women? My
blessings to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy :
Gandhi National Museum and Library
