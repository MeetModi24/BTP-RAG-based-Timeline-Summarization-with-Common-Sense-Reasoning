243. LETTER TO CHARUPRABHA SENGUPTA
SODEPUR,
December 7, 1945
CHI. CHARUPRABHA,
These days I try to write to all Indians in the national
language. How long shall we go on writing to each other in
English?
I am occupied with many things up to the 10th. I don't
know how long I shall be here after that. Even then I am
writing to Aruna.
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 8710. Also C.W. 1496. Courtesy:
A. K. Sen
