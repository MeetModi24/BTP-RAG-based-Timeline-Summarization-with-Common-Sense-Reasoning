165. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
September 2, 1947
CHI. MANI,
Arrange to [despatch] the accompanying papers. You will
have found no difficulty in understanding the reason for my
fast. Rajaji strove hard with me, but the more he argued the
more I was confirmed in my own decision. Was the amity of a
fortnight a mere sham?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 143
