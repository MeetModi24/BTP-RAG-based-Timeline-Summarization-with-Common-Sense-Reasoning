304. LETTER TO KODI NARASIMHAN
SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA,
November 1, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of 24th ult. for which he desires me to thank
you.
He has never said to anyone that Communists as such should not
be admitted to the Congress. So far as he knows no one who signs the Congress
creed and pays 4 as. can be prevented from becoming a member.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI KODI NARASIMHAN
NANDYAL
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
