302. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
July 28, 1941
CHI. NARANDAS,
Herewith a postcard from Jethalalbhai. I have not replied.
If you wish to give any, please do so directly. If you think
I should write, let me know.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
If you have any suggestions to make about Khadi Jagat, do
send them.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8587. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
