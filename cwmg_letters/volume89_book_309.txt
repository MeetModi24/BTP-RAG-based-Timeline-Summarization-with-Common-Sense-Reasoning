309. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
DELHI,
October 7, 1947
MY DEAR SINGER,
Your letter.
Who says you are old?
Dr. Bidhan is coming but he has not. What matters when
[he] comes? One and the same thing to you.
Love to the whole family.
Yours,
SPINNER 2
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
