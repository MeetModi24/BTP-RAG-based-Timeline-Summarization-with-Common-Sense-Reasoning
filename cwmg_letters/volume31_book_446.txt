446. LETTER TO SHAUKAT ALI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
September 16, 1926
DEAR BIG BROTHER,
I have your letter. I am glad you have liked my reply to
the appeal.
Start if you like by all means on the 17th but I shall have
to leave for Bombay on the same day you arrive here. I have
to do so in connection with the South African Deputation. I leave
Bombay the same night, take silence also in the train. You shall
have plenty of curds, bran bread and green vegetables when you
come. I would like you to come next week. But if you are coming
according to your programme, please send a wire to Anasuya-
behn and she will inform me. The special bread will be prepared
only after you are here. It won't take much time as I shall
keep the ingredients ready.
Yours sincerely,
MAULANA SHAUKAT ALI
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 19693
