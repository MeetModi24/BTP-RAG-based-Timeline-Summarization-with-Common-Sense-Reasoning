27. LETTER TO RENUKA RAY
NEW DELHI,
October 23, 1946
DEAR RENUKA 2 ,
I am doing all I can about your question.
I am sorry about the illness of your brother's child. Will
you need to stay on whilst there is work in Bengal? Of course,
you know where your duty lies for the moment.
Rajkumari is no better. The boil is obstinate. The sur-
geon in Simla says it cannot yet be opened.
Yours,
BAPU
SMT. RENUKA RAY
2 SAFDARJANG LANE
NEW DELHI
