26. LETTER TO YUK LIN LEW
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 26, 1906
DEAR MR. LEW,
You promised to send me a representation addressed by the Chinese
community to the Chinese Ambassador (or is it Minister?), so as to
enable me to draft the letter you want me to.
I am quite ready to draft the representation as soon as I get the
Chinese petition, but you will recognize that it is necessary for me to
have it, if only for the date and description.
Yours truly,
YUK LIN LEW, ESQ.
CHINESE LEGATION
PORTLAND PLACE, W.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4409
