150. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
SABARMATI,
Thursday [February 27, 1930]
CHI. RAMDAS,
I have your letter. I understand your desire not to leave the Ashram this
time. I do wish to frame some rules. How is the girl? And Nimu?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
