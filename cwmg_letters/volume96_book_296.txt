296. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
SIMLA,
July 17, 1931
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
If Manilal thinks that Indian Opinion cannot be managed, he may
close the Press down. But the trustees must decide.
Do write to me now and then.
Love,
Yours sincerely,
UPPER HOUSE

Archives of India
