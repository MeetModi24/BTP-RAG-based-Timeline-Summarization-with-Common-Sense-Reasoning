219. LETTER TO E. HEWITT
BIRLA HOUSE,
NEW DELHI,
October 26, 1947
DEAR CAPT. HEWITT,
I thank you very much for your considerate letter of 23rd
instant making a free offer of your stock of blankets which may be
condemned for reissue, but not unacceptable for purposes of protec-
tion. I am sending to a friend in Bombay the authority you have
enclosed for use.
CAPT. E. HEWITT
DIVISIONAL SEA TRANSPORT OFFICER
SEA TRANSPORT HEADQUARTERS
NAVAL HEADQUARTERS
NEW DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
