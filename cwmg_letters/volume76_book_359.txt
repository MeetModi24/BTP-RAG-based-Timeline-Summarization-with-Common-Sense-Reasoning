359. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 20, 1942
MY DEAR C. R.,
I was about to write to you when your letter came.
Of course I understand and appreciate the exquisite consider-
ation running through your letter. I invite you all four to come
here and pour out your love and argument to wean me from
what appears to be an error. Anyway your monthly visit is due.
You can come any day you like.
What I wanted to write to you about was this.
Why don't
you form a league with Muslim friends to propagate your idea of
settlement? Have you seen Q A.'s reply to my note ? Do you
accept his definition of Pakistan?
What is the common idea about independence. Surely you
should have a common understanding over fundamentals before
you come to an agreement.
Let not your fear of the Japs betray you into a worse state
of things.
But more of all this when you come.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From a copy: C.W. 10922. Courtesy: C. R. Narasimhan. Also The Indian
