107. LETTER TO MILLIE GRAHAM POLAK
Box 6522,
Thursday [July 10, 1913]
MY DEAR MILLIE
So Henry is on the waters on his way to London. Such is the
uncertainty of the life of those who are associated with me. It was
very brave of you to have permitted Henry without a murmur to separate
from you. I need hardly say that you will as before unreservedly fall
back upon me for your wants. You will not leave me to anticipate
your wants, will you ?
There is no reply yet from General Smuts. I am expecting one
every day. The strike has dislocated many things.
How are you all getting on ? I am not likely to leave here, if at
all, before Monday next.
With love to you all,
Yours,
BROTHER
[PS.]
The Bazaar was a social success but not a financial success that
it might have been.
From the original: Gandhi-Polak Correspondence. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
1 H. S. L. Polak left for London on July 10, 1913 to assist Gokhale in
representing South African Indians' case. From the contents it is evident that the
letter was written on this day, a Thursday.
