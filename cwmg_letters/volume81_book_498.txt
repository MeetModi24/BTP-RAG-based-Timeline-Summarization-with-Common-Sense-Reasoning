498. LETTER TO V. A. SUNDARAM
POONA,
September 27, 1945
CHI. SUNDARAM,
What a good thing your sister has passed away painlessly!
We must all join her, some soon, some late.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI SUNDARAM
KRISHNAKUTIR
P. O. BENARES HINDU UNIVERSITY
From the original: C.W. 10523. Courtesy: S. R. Venkataraman
