103. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 3, 1945
CHI. MRIDU,
I am sending herewith the original as revised and a copy
of it for your convenience. If it is too late, you can discard it.
I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
KASTURBA GANDHI NATIONAL MEMORIAL FUND
SCINDIA HOUSE
BALLARD ESTATE
FORT, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
