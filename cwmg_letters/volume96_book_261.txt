261. LETTER TO MILLIE GRAHAM POLAK
BETTIAH,
May 12 [1917]
MY DEAR MILLIE,
On my return yesterday I found your telegram but not of yesterday's
date. I do hope you received my wire from Bankipore and I am sending
another one just now. How I wish I could be by Henry's side. But
it cannot be. The position here is so serious that I dare not leave this
place. I had an important interview with a member of the Government.
I am preparing a general letter of which you shall have a copy.
Do you want anybody to come from Ahmedabad? Do please give
me the fullest information.
In the hope that you might have received my long letter, I am not
repeating what I have said in it.
With love,
Yours,
BHAI
From the original: Gandhi-Polak Correspondence. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
