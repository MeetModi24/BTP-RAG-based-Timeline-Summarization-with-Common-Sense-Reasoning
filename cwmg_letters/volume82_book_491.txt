491. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
SODEPUR,
January 6, 1946
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Chi. Mridula writes to say that you are now growing old
and are often bed-ridden!!! What is this? And why? If you
grow old, what about me? 125 years is still far away for you as
well as for me. And then 125 years is not for me alone. It is
for all of us.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
