412. LETTER TO MAULANA SHAUKAT ALI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 23, 1926
DEAR FRIEND AND BROTHER,
I do hope you will forgive me for not having come to Delhi.
But I felt and both Panditji and Mrs. Naidu agreed that I should
not be made to leave Ahmedabad contrary to the resolution of
Cawnpore.
You have seen my letter to Hakimji. That was the best
advice I could give. I hope everything has gone well.
Your charkha was repaired. It went yesterday with Yeshwant
Prasad to Bombay and will be delivered to you.
Yours,
MAULANA SHAUKAT ALI
DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 19488
