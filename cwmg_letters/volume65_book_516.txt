516. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 25, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
I got the post at 3.15. Janba had come earlier but had
not brought the post. Now do what you can. I will comply
with your request that I should observe silence. I feel much
better.
Read my letter to Shanta. Think over it and cancel the
passage if you wish. Personally that is what I desire. I would
of course agree . . .
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11538
