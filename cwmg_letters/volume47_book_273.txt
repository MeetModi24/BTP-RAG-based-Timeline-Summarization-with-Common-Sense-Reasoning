273. LETTER TO GIRIRAJ KISHORE
AHMEDABAD,
August 1, 1931
MY DEAR GIRIRAJ,
I did have your first letter but I did not know where you
would be and so I simply took a mental note of what you were
doing. I am glad you were able to see the children and that
they were well. You will keep me informed of your own progress.
Nothing is decided about my going to London.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. GIRIRAJ
C/O DR. SHRI RAM
SCOUT ORGANIZER
SRINAGAR (KASHMIR)
From a microfilm: S.N. 17418
