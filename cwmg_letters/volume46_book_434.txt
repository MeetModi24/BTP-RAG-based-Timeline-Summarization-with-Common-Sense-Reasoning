434. LETTER TO THAPPAN NAIR
As AT SABARMATI,
June 7, 1931
MY DEAR THAPPAN,
I have your letter. You should diligently follow the columns
of Young India. They might help you. So far as your immediate
question is concerned, if you feel that there is no work for you
there you are at liberty to return to the Ashram. Many have done
so.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. THAPPAN NAIR
CONGRESS CAMP
PALGHAT (MALABAR)
