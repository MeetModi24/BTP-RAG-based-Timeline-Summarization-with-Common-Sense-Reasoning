703. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
SIMLA,
July 11, 1945
CHI. KAKA,
It was good you sent the wire.
I hope you are keeping good health. You have with you
the work about Hindustani. I attended to it as best I could,
with the utmost difficulty. But you are the expert.
Take care of your health in everything you do.
Bal is expected now.
Sardar is with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10963
