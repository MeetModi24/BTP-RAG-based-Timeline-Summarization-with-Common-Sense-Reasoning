309. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
December 18, 1946
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I am not prepared to say that Amtussalaam's fast is not
justified from any point of view. Full information is also not
available with me to enable me to give any verdict. It would
be good if the two of you could give your considered decision.
I hope Hemprabha is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Enclosed is a letter to Amtussalaam. Send it to her.
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 545
