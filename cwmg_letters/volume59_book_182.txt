182. LETTER TO PRAFULLA CHANDRA CHOSH
October 7, 1934
MY DEAR PRAFULLA,
I have your letter. Khan Saheb is all that you describe him to
be. You are keeping the Brothers beyond their time. They were
to have returned here by the 8th. They have to reach Bombay
without fail on the 19th, and they expect to [visit] two places
on their way back to Wardha. The result would be that they
would be with me only for two days after that. I do not know
that you are wise in making Khan Saheb work in that debilitating
atmosphere when he himself is so much run down. If it is at
all possible to cut down the tour, please do so. We must not run
a willing horse to death.
SHRI PRAFULLA GHOSH
79/13 B LOWER CIRCULAR ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
