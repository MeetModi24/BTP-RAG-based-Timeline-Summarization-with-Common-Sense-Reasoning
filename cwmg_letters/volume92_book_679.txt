679. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
May 7, 1933
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have received two letters from you. Do not be afraid. Find
strength from my fast. This fast will be for the welfare of all of
you. It is my wish that all of you may qualify yourselves for this fast.
Steady your mind and do whatever you can.
BAPU
