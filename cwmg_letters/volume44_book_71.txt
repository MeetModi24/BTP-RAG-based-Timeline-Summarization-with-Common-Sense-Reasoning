71. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 27, 1930
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. How many women live with you? Have
they learnt the prayers? Do you get time to read the Anasakti-
yoga? Is your mind at peace? I am all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9277
