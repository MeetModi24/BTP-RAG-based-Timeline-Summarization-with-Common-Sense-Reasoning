107. LETTER TO SANT SINGH
November 3, 1941
DEAR SANT SINGH,
There is no analogy between my selling blankets to a murderer
and your offering services on the field. Selling blankets is my
calling and I may sell to whomsoever requires them. To offer
your services on the field is no part of your duty. My answer
therefore is 'no'.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 861
