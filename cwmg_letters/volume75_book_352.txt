352. LETTER TO PUSHPA SUNDARAM
February 7, 1942
CHI. PUSHPA,
Father has to give another twenty-five years to the Kashi Vish-
wavidyalaya. Who is going to be satisfied with twenty-five years?
Why do you write in English?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3189
1 V. A. Sundaram
