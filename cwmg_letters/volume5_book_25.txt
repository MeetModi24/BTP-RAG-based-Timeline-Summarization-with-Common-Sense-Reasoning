25. LETTER TO OMAR HAJI AMOD ZAVERI
[JOHANNESBURG,]
July 17, 1905
MY DEAR SHETH OMAR HAJI AMOD ZAVERI,
Your letter to hand. I am returning herewith both the letters of
Sheth Haji Ismail. I do not at all like the way he has written them.
I can see from it that it will be difficult to exercise control over his ex-
penditure. If the expenses there come to as much as the rent, you have
to consider what is to be done about it.
If the business here does not yield profit enough to meet the expen-
diture at Porbandar, it will amount to consuming the capital. I believe
it will be necessary to remit £100 a month for the present to Porbandar
in order to obviate further bitterness. I am writing to Sheth Haji Ismail
today.
Salaams from
M. K. GANDHI
From the Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: Letter Book (1905): No. 678
