409. LETTER TO KUNVARJI MEHTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 10, 1930
BHAI KUNVARJI,
I got your cheerful letter. I am very happy to know that
you have regained health. I have often observed that people who
enthusiastically respond to a call for service and plunge into the
work improve in health.
Write to Pragji and tell him that his living in exile is also a
form of service. When the Ruler of our destinies requires him in
India, He will certainly bring him here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2688
