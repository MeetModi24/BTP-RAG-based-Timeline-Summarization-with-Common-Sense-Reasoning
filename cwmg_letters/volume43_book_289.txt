289. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
April 20, 1930
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have sent Chhotelal to Ahmedabad today, as I sent away
Pyarelal. Retaining both of them in my service encouraged them
and me in indulgence and resulted in their services being not fully
utilized. I have, therefore, sent Pyarelal to Wadhwan and other
places and Chhotelal to Ahmedabad to stay in the Vidyapith and
elsewhere and propagate the takli, the spinning-wheel, etc. Let
me know which women ought to remain there.
The sum of Rs. 250 which I have sent with Chhotelal was
received from a Marwari gentleman. Credit it to the Satyagraha
Fund.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C. W. 8102. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
