178. LETTER TO SOPHIA WADIA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 11, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your usual gift for the White Lotus Day to be used
for any of my various constructive public activities.
Love to you both.
BAPU
SHRI SOPHIA WADIA
ARYA SANGHA
22 NARAYAN DABHOLKAR ROAD
MALABAR HILL, BOMBAY 6
