466. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
September 2, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You can imagine my condition twenty-five years ago. Now I
stay in bed and accept service. At that time mostly I used to
do physical work and took part in all activities. There was this
difference, that when I worked almost everyone used to come
and join me.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4401
