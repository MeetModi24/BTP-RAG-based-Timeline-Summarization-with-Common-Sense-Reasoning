431. LETTER TO ASHABHAI
SEVAGRAM,
November 18, 1944
BHAI ASHABHAI,
Since I had passed on your original letter to Bapa I did not
reply to it. Ravishankar Maharaj gave me a copy of it and we
also had a talk. You certainly have my blessings. Your work is
fine. And it was good that all of you participated in [raising]
the contributions. I suggest you should utilize the fresh amount
you might have for whatever work you wish to do and not add it
to the Memorial [Fund]. This will not create any difficulty and
you will be able to utilize the entire sum locally. I am sure you
have a local committee.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
