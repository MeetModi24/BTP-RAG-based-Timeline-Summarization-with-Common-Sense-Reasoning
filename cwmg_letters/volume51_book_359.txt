359. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
October 8, 1932
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
Your letter has come. Kudsia's brother has sent a letter. He
too will come to the Ashram this month. Kudsia must be all right
now. I am getting back my strength. I was glad to have seen you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 258
