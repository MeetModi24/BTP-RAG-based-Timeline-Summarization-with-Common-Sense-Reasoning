221. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
Ashadh Vad 6 [July 22, 1924]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I have your letter. You may come whenever you wish. God
will do only what is good. I would advise you not to bring your
son's daughter with you. Let the husband and wife do what-
ever they deem proper. The father is welcome to bring the
daughter and leave her here. If you bring her now there is a possi-
bility of the estrangement widening.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 6017. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
