391. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
Y.M.,
December 5, 1930
MY DEAR ANAND,
Depression overtakes you because you think too much about
things other than the task before you. This is a sign of un-
faithfulness. You will cease to worry about any other thing the
moment you realize that you are to devote yourself body, mind
and soul to the duty undertaken by you. Truth demands this.
Love.
BAPU
From a microfilm. Courtesy : National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
