327. LETTER TO EMILY KINNAIRD
SEVAGRAM,
November 2, 1944
DEAR MOTHER,
What is a Christian luncheon table? What distinguishes it
from say a Hindu or a Muslim luncheon table?
You won't fret about my fast, if it comes. For if it does,
it will come from God and He will be responsible for the result
that may follow.
How can I pay even a pie towards the expenses of a room
named after my wife? You may pay an anna yourself.
Your loving son,
BAPU
MRS. EMILY KINNAIRD
Y. W. C. A.
57 CANTONMENT ROAD
LUCKNOW
