142. LETTER TO ABDUL KHALIL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 18, 1940
BHAI ABDUL KHALIL,
I could read your letter only today. If what you write is true,
it is regrettable. I shall make inquiries.
M. K. GANDHI
BHAI ABDUL KHALIL
C/O MUNSHI ABDUL WAHAB SAHEB
VAIJANATH PARA
RAIPUR (C. P.)
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6861
