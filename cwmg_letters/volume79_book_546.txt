546. LETTER TO THE METROPOLITAN OF CALCUTTA
SEVAGRAM,
March 28, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Sudhir has given me your sweet note. I agree with you when
you say that insistence should be not on rights but on duties and
that we should love our neighbour as ourselves.
With love,
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
METROPOLITAN
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
