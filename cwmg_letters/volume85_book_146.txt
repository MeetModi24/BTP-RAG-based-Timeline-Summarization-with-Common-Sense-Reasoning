146. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA, C.P.,
August 9, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
I am acknowledging your letter to Bapuji on his behalf. He hopes
it will not be necessary for Shri Shriramulu to fast again.
I hope Shrimati Parvati will have an interesting and successful time
in Switzerland.
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
From a photostat: G. N. 3231
