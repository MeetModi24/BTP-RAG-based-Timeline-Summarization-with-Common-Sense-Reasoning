272. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 2, 1930
CHI. SHARDA (BABU),
Your letter. The beauty of trees and plants surpasses that
of electric lights as the beauty of real tomatoes surpasses that of
clay ones. Have you ever heard anyone's hunger being satisfied
by clay tomatoes?
Blessings from
BAPU
