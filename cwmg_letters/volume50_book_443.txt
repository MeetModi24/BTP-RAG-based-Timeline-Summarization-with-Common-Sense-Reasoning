443. LETTER TO MANILAL JHAVERI
August 24, 1932
CHI. MANILAL,
I had to wait for quite some time for your letter, but at last
it came. At present at least I get letters from all. Chhaganlal's
letters are perfectly satisfactory. But Jeki1, Manekbai and Ratilal
Desai have expressed their dissatisfaction about him.
From
Chhaganlal's letters, however, I can see no reason for distrusting
him. All the same, I have put him some direct questions. If it
becomes absolutely necessary, you will have to go to Rangoon. As
far as possible I will not involve you in this. I have received
Nanalal's letter. If you have heard or if you hear anything or
if you have any suggestion to make, please do not hesitate to
write to me about it. The whole business is rather delicate. Let us
1 Jayakunvar, elder daughter of Dr. Pranjivan Mehta and wife of Manilal
Doctor
2 Wife of Dr. Pranjivan Mehta
hope that Doctor's punya will work and everything will be all right.
I am not writing to Manjula, thinking that she must have
returned by now. I wrote one letter to her at Jetpur. Letters for
Gulab, Maganlal and Tilak are enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
