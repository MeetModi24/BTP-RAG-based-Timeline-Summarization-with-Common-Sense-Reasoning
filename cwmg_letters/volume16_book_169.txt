169. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
LAHORE,
Monday [October 27] 1919
MY DEAR CHILD,
I have your letter.
I am having a precious time here. I may not be able to come
in the early part of November after all.
Mr. Andrews is here and we often talk of you.
Keep good health please.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat of the original in N.A.I.; also My Dear Child, p. 43
