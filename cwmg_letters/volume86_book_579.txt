579. LETTER TO TULSIRAM
February 6, 1947
BHAI TULSIRAMJI,
I have your letter, and also the two books. They are very
good but too long for a beginner.
Send me a small book in
Sanskrit if you can find one. If you cannot, I shall manage.
Sharmaji met me. Why does he keep indifferent health? He
ought to regain his health through nature cure.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
