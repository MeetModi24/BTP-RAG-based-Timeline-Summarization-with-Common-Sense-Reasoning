676. LETTER TO KANAIYALAL N. DESAI
SIMLA,
July 4, 1945
BHAI KANJIBHAI,
So you lost your long-ailing daughter-in-law. She was bound
to go. In my view it is good that she herself has been freed
from the suffering and has relieved those who looked after her.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KANAIYALAL NANUBHAI DESAI
GOPIPURA
SURAT
