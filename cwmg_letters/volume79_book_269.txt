269. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
WARDHA,
February 23, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I find even five days away from the Ashram too much.
Gurbuxani and Vimalabehn seem to me to be real jewels.
The latter has resigned her post in the school where she was
teaching. She is ready to take up everything gladly. Only we
should know how to deal with her gently, i.e., in the spirit of
ahimsa.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8460. Also C.W. 5570.
Courtesy : Munnalal G. Shah
