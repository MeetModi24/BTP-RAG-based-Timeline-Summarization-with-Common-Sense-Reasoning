464. LETTER TO RAMASWAMI
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
CHI. RAMASWAMI,
Let me at any rate reply in Hindi to your English letter.
I did not even know that Lady Tata was still alive. Where does
she figure in this? Where does she live? Please write to me.
You can come to the Ashram in July.
Blessings from
BAPU
