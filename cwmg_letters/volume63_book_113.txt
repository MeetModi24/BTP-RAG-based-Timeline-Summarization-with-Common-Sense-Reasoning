113. LETTER TO NAN MENON
June 26, 1936
MY DEAR NAN,
I had hoped to send you a long chatty letter but I never
got the time. Now Esther sends me a reminder. So here is
this note to tell you that though I cannot write, I can never
forget you. I hope both of you are happier than before.
Love and kisses to both of you.
BAPU
My Dear Child, p. 122
