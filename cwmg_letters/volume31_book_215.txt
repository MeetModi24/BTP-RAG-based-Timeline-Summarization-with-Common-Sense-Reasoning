215. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Asadha Vad 2, 1982 [July 27, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Please remember that the 15th is the last date for paying the
money to Girjashankar Joshi. I should get the sum before that date.
Yesterday, Lala Shyamlal of Hissar arrived with his wife. At
the moment, there is no room in the Ashram in which the
husband and the wife could be put up. The latter, therefore, has
been accommodated in the room occupied by Janakidevi. The
Lala seems to be well acquainted with you. Om took ill, and so
she came down here. She is quite well now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2870
