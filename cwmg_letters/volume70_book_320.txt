320. LETTER TO TARA JASANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 19, 1939
CHI. TARA,
You have made no mistake. Being dubbed a 'mahatma',
I have two birthdays. Greater ones still may have three birth-
days. You must find out and let me know how I have two and
others may have three or more birthdays.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TARABEHN JASANI
ANANDKUNJ
RAJKOT, KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9833.
Courtesy: Tarabehn
