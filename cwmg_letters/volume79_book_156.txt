156. LETTER TO SHRIKRISHNA SINHA
SEVAGRAM,
February 2, 1945
BHAI SHRIKRISHNA SINHA,
You ought not to need comforting. Death inevitably follows
birth. Why grieve over it? Your wife is released from suffering.
Now you have only service to devote yourself to. So do it.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRIKRISHNA SINHA
PREMIER
PATNA
