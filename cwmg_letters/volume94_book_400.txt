400. LETTER TO ACHYUT PATWARDHAN
SEVAGRAM,
August 12, 1945
BHAI ACHYUT,
All your papers are being sent. If something has been left out, do
ask. It will be sent.
I trust you are in good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
