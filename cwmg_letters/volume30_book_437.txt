437. LETTER TO NAGINDAS
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Sunday, April 25, 1926
BHAISHRI NAGINDAS,
Read the enclosed postcard and let me know what you wrote
to him. If this gentleman had ordered books as per the announce-
ment in Navajivan, I think you should have sent them. . . Let
me also know how many orders you have received to date on
cash payment.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19506
