94. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
February 2, 1934
DEAR SATISBABU,
I have your letters and wire. You will have seen my long
letter to Dr. Bidhan. I would then like your revised opinion.
Your acceptance of my argument has produced a revulsion in me.
But I must not reargue the point. If we will imbibe the Gita
spirit, we must have detachment even in the midst of a raging
fire. Who knows what God intends by things that baffle us.
You will be well rid of Harijan1, if there is not a natural
demand for it. You may issue leaflets now and then. In closing
down you should say firmly why you are closing.
You will study my letters to Hemprabha. The letter has
become a serious thing without my meaning to write so seriously.
It shows how full I am just now of these things.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1625
I Bengali Harijan, which the addressee had been editing
