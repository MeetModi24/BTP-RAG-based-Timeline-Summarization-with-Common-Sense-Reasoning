476. LETTER TO PARASHURAM MEHROTRA
February 19, 1933
CHI. PARASRAM,
I got your letter. You should acquire equal facility in both
styles, so that you can use either as the occasion may require.
We will use both also when we have an opportunity to revive
Navajivan.
Work hard to help Duncan Greenlees as you have been
doing to help Marybehn. It is desirable that he should learn
Hindi as soon as possible. Let us pray to God to give peace to
Father.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 7514
