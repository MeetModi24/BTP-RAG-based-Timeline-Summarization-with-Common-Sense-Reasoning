387. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
December 8, 1937
CHI. VIJAYA,
I understand your suffering.
Have patience. Write to
me. I will get your letter. Give me all the details. Don't
worry. Learn everything carefully.
Take full advantage of
Vinoba's satsang.
The rest on the back.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7075. Also C.W. 4567. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
