249. LETTER TO J. M. WILSON
May 3, 1919
I have read the Bihar Planters' Association Memorial to the
Government. Your Association has done itself and me a cruel
injustice. But I will not answer the charge brought by your Asso-
ciation. Time is on my side and it will show you the error of a
judgment hastily pronounced.
M. K. G.
From a photostat: S.N. 6578
