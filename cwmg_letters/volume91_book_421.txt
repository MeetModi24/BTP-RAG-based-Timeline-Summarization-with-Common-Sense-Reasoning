421. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Magshar Sud 12 [November 27, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I am kept posted with news of you. I have letters from
Swami and Nargisbehn. As soon as you are fit enough to leave
the house you must have a long spell at Nasik or Deolali or any
other place considered good and improve your health. You must
exercise more. Mahadev did not get fever again. But I am not
sending him because Swami is already there. I have received the
telegram about Taramati. Let mother and son live in peace.
Anand's soul must be pleased because she valued this a lot.
You do not have to worry about me at all. Three [days] out of the
seven have already passed. I am still carrying on with my work.
Only moving about is stopped. Lying in bed, I am doing everything.
Blessings from
BAPU
