489. LETTER TO M. A. JINNAH
SEVAGRAM,
March 22, 1945
BHAI JINNAH,
Having read that you are ill, I am writing this. I trust the
illness is a minor one and that you have recovered by now.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
Q. A. JINNAH SAHEB
PRESIDENT, MUSLIM LEAGUE
NEW DELHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
