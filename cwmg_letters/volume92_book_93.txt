93. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
August 12, 1929
DEAR MOTILALJI,
I had your letter about Jawaharlal. I hope the election
will go through all right. The more I think over it, the more
convinced I feel of the correctness of the step I have taken.
But this is just to tell you I have seen Mr. Jinnah. He
explained the 14 points of demands framed at Delhi. The
chief however is the demand for one-third representation in the
C[entral] L[egislature] and separate electorate if the other 12
demands are not clearly accepted. How that can be done or whe-
ther it should be done, you know best. My mind is in a whirl in
this matter. The atmosphere is too foggy for me to see clearly.
I hope Kamala will go through the operation bravely.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
I saw the Ali Brothers. They had a fairly heavy list of
complaints against me. But I could make no impression on them
as they distrust the whole (practically) of my associates. But I
was glad of the interviews. They may do good in the long run.
