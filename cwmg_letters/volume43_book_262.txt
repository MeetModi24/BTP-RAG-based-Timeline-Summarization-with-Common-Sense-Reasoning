262. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI MADHAVJI THAKKAR
April 16, 1930
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
I received your beautiful letter. I am replying rather late. I
have no time. I got up today at a quarter past two in the morning.
I will give you sufficient work to do. I have understood the
natures of you both. Your resolves are noble.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6795
