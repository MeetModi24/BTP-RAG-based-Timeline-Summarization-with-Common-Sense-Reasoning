369. LETTER TO FARID ANSARI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 15, 1945
MY DEAR FARID,
I was glad to see your beautiful handwriting after a long time. It
at once reminded me of the late Dr. Ansari.
You were bound to think of Ba and Mahadev. They have won.
We have to plod. I am delighted that you are keeping well. My love
to all.
Yours,
BAPU
FARID ANSARI SAHEB
PRISONER
FEROZEPUR JAIL
PUNJAB
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
