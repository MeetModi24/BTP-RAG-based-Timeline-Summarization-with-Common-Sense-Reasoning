395. LETTER TO NARADEV SHASTRI
December 25, 1932
All the doubts will be resolved if you bear in mind the fact
that I am working for Harijans while confined in jail. To write
more would be to violate the restrictions. No Congressman is
under any compulsion to join this work. No one need give up
his dharma for the sake of this work.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 370
