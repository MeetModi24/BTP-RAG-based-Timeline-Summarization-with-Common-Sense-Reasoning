239. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
May 15, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have not fully answered one of your letters. It does not
seem proper to me to send for the Santiniketan gentleman now.
We have said "No" to him, and we should stick to it. Jajuji
is a king. He may relent in response to a request. How can we
take advantage of that?
Do not mind if Sarojini has gone. She is bound to return.
We cannot control her mind.
I have already suggested one remedy for Shakaribehn. An-
other is to take the treatment of Dr. Ice. He can easily deal
with such a complaint.
There is no harm in your having detained Saroj. You may
detain anyone whom you wish to and can. Do not detain any-
body just to please me and that too in my absence.
Blessings from
BAPU
