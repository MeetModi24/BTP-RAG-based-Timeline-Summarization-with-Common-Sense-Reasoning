54. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
July 25, 1945
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have your letter. If you have to take treatment now, I
would strongly advise you to go to Dinshaw at Poona for it. I
will be ready to go there, and so you will have the benefit of
my quackery, too. In any case you cannot get worse than you
are and it is likely Dinshaw will win glory.
I had a talk with Pardiwala. I will write today itself. That
mail goes by the morning post. A copy cannot be sent with
this. Such things will go on happening. You are not the man
to worry about them.
I have no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- : Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 281
