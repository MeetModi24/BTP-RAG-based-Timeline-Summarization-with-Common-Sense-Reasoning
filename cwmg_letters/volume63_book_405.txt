405. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
September 26, 1936
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I have your letter. The Bhangis' problem seems to have
been solved satisfactorily.
I am returning the letter which
you want.
I am surprised about Chandulal. I have sent him the rel-
evant extract from your letter and asked for his reply.
I hope your work is going on smoothly now. It must.
Very few workers are left now.
I am hoping to meet you all. I only wish to pay a visit
of duty. There should be, therefore, no publicity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5540
1 & 2 Faded in the source
3 Chandulal Becharbhai Patel who was then Education Officer of the
