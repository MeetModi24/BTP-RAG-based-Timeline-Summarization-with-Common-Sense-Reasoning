273. LETTER TO RAGHUBIR SINGH
As AT SABARMATI,
May 26, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. My answer to your first question is that
if your mind really hankers after the degree or the knowledge
that the college course gives you in physics you should rejoin the
college.
As to the help to your friends I think that you are entitled
to and even bound to help them if they are starving but not to
further their aims. In no case [may] even you disclose their names
to the police.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. RAGHUBIR SINGH
C/O BENGALI SAREM
COLLECTOR'S OFFICE
MEERUT
