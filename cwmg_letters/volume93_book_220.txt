220. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
December 15, 1937
CHI. MRIDU,
I will not object if you can attract Vijaya and I will not
interfere. Otherwise she gives good news about Mummy first at
Calcutta and then on her way there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11206. Courtesy : Sarabhai
