299. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI H. SHAH
October 3, 1932
I tried to race with you, but for the present I have lost. I
don't mind, however. "Life or death, I value neither more than
the other." This is a new life for me. God's will be done. He
has preserved my honour. He has let me off with a light test.
I feel His grace every moment of my life.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 102
