340. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 25, 1939
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I have gone through the American thing. It is most ex-
pensive. In other respects, too, it does not attract me.
I hope you have good news about Indu.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1939. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
