169. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
January 25, 1931
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I got your letter. "This time, too, I cannot think of any-
thing to write about" -this is how you begin practically every
one of your letters. Should I laugh at this or weep over it?
Answer the question for yourself.
What reason is there now for you to worry about my health?
Every time there was a set-back, however slight, I informed
people about it, took immediate measures and recovered. My
strength has remained unchanged. Why, then, should you worry?
Shanta must have arrived there by now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1818
