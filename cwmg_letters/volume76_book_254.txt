254. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
[June] 12, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
I am writing this in Gujarati. I got your letter. The dis-
cussions with the Maulana and Jawaharlal take up a good deal
of my time. They will continue for one or two days more.
They are carried on peacefully. The weather seems to have
changed somewhat today. A hot wind is still blowing.
I am keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7899. Also C.W. 4267. Cour-
tesy: Amrit Kaur
