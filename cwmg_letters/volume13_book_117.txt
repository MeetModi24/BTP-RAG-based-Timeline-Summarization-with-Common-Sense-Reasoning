117. LETTER TO MADHURIPRASAD
AHMEDABAD,
Bhadrapad Shukla 1 [September 10, 1915]
DEAR SHRI MADHURIPRASAD,
I have received the letter written by Totaramji and you. I am
sorry I have no time at present to visit Ferozabad. The Ashram
work here will not allow me to go.
Yours sincerely,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI MADHURIPRASAD
BHARATIBHUVAN KARYALAYA
FEROZABAD
From a photostat of the Hindi original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 2764
