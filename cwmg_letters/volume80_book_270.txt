270. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
May 21, 1945
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I have gone through your two lines. You will certainly find
work wherever you go.
Blessings from
BAPU
[C/O] SHRIMATI YASHODHARA DASAPPA
VONTICOPPAL
MYSORE
