686. LETTER TO SURESH CHANDRA DAS
SIMLA,
July 9, 1945
DEAR SURESH,
You should not believe Press reports. There was no question
of anger; it was one of necessity. But I have been angry and
made myself an ass. So you see on what a broken reed you rely!
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SURESH CHANDRA DAS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
