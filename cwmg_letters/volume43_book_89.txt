89. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
ANAND,
Silence Day [March 17, 1930]
CHI. KUSUM,
I have your letter. What you say about a house is quite
correct. But your duty was to move into the Hostel, and it was
good that you did so. We should love what is good for us. Work
as much as you like, but preserve your health. And be sure that
you write to me from time to time.
The secretaryship still clings to you. When you get time,
clear up everything. Don't worry about me. I know that I have
