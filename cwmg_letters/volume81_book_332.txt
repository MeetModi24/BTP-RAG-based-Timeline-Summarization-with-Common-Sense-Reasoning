332. LETTER TO DHIRENDRANATH MUKHERJEE
August 31, 1945
BHAI DHIRENDRANATHJI,
I have your letter. There are some things men achieve by
talking, some by observing silence and some by working.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI DHIRENDRA N. MUKHERJEE
SENHATI P. O., KHULNA DIST.
BENGAL
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
