487. LETTER TO VICHITRA NARAYAN SHARMA
PANCHGANI,
June 9, 1945
CHI. VICHITRA1,
I have your letter. It is a difficult problem. You should
fearlessly write to Gadodiaji and ask him what he wants. Let
him go out if he wants. Ask Jajuji also. Find out whom Dr.
Sharma wants to be appointed. I am managing with one letter
to save time. Otherwise I would have had to write three.
Blessings from
BAPU
Copy to Dr. Sharma
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen, p. 330
