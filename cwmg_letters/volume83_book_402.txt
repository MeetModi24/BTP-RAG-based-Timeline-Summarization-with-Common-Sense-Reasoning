402. LETTER TO SEVAGRAM CAMP INMATES
ON THE TRAIN,
March 31, 1946
BROTHERS AND SISTERS IN SEVAGRAM CAMP,
Chi. Kanu Gandhi gives me all the news. I do hope that
all inmates of the Camp will produce plenty of foodstuff in this
period of emergency.
And I hope they will all learn other things as well.
Blessings from
BAPU
THE BROTHERS AND SISTERS IN THE SEVAGRAM CAMP
