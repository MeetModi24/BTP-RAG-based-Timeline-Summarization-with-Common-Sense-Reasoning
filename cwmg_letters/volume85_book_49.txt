49. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
PANCHGANI,
July 25, 1946
CHI. ANAND AND GANGI,
I have the letters of you both. About your falling down
and breaking the toe, what shall I call it, the grace of God or
your carelessness! If you had come here we would surely have
found some time to talk. What will happen in Poona I do not
know. I shall reach there on the 28th or 29th.
Why should you cry when you have Gangi? Why should
you not take it that in Gangi Vidya has returned to you? Now
it is not a question of forgetting Vidya. Gangi is Vidya's image.
She does as much work of service as Vidya did. She has married
you at the instance of Vidya. What more do you want?
Surrender all your worries to God and become carefree.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
