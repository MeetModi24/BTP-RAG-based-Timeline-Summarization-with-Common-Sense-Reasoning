178. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 6, 1942
CHI. MRIDU,
I read your letter and immediately destroyed it.
You have no reason at all to worry. I had sent Mirabehn merely
to give you the letter and to gain some experience. She was free to
discuss non-violence with anyone she chose. Had Sardar left on that
very day, I would have sent the letter through him. More when we
meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11226. Courtesy : Sarabhai Foundation
