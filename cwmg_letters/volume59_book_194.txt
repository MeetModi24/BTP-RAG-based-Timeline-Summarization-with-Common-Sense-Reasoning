194. LETTER TO MITHUBEHN PETIT
October 9, 1934
CHI. MITHUBEHN,
I see that you have been enlarging your field of service and
that you will have the happiness now of naming your weaving
school Sevashram. May your labour and that of your men and
women helpers bear fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Improve your health and see that it is perfectly all right.
SMT. MITHUBEHN PETIT
PARK HOUSE
COLABA, BOMBAY
