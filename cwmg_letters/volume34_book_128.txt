128. LETTER TO NAJUKLAL N. CHOKSI
BANGALORE,
Monday, July 11, 1927
BHAISHRI NAJUKLAL,
I got your postcard. God provides man with a means to
learn from his fall. You two have it through the birth of a son,
but Moti, if she wills, can so use it as to fulfil the true end of
her life. You are, moreover, a teacher by vocation. I shall wait
and see how you bring up the child born to you. Let Moti read
this.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Preserve this postcard.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12141
