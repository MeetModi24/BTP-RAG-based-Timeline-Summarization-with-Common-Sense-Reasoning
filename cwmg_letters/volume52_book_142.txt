142. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
December 2, 1932
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have not written to you recently because you are receiving
all the news as Satisbabu is here. I continue to receive news
of you. I have explained to Satisbabu regarding those localities.
Your work is very much to my liking.
You have raised a pertinent question about the Ashram
and the answer is a simple one. If we wish to run the Ashram
only through disciplined men and women we must admit only
those who are willing to exercise self-control. They will come with
full understanding of the rules and willingness to abide by them.
We cannot impose our rules on those who come simply for earn-
ing a living. Their status is only that of employees. But we
must not treat them as employees. We must regard them as our
companions and keep them as long as they perform their duties
conscientiously. The problem is that even those who come with
the intention of observing regulations turn slack. Discrimination
is essential on such occasions. In the name of God and by His
Grace we must do all we can.
Do you follow all this? If not, ask me again.
No time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
