323. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
August 7, 1938
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I think that the women alone will not be able to cope with
the vegetables. Two of them are guests. And the sick Ba should
be left out of account. Mirabehn also is not there. You should,
therefore, put the responsibility on a man. For the present let it
be Navin. He should carry on with whatever help he may
get from the women.
A bucket for everybody is too much. Everybody must have
a lota of course. A lantern for everybody is also a luxury. We
should always think of what the poor do and try as far as possible
to live as they do.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8571. Also C.W. 7069. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
