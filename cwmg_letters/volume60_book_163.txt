163. LETTER TO J. AWASTHI
WARDHA,
January 30, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I like your plain words. Of course, if it is not the same plate
that was auctioned by me, you are entirely absolved. I am now
asking for the return of the plate to you.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI J. AWASTHI
BRIJ NIVAs
NEW GANESHGANJ, LUCKNOW
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
