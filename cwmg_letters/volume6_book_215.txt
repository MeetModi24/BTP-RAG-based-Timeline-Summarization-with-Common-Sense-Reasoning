215. LETTER TO HEAD MASTER, COUNTY SCHOOL
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 20, 1906
THE HEAD MASTER
COUNTY SCHOOL
BEDFORD
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter of the 19th instant for which I thank you. I
am afraid I cannot leave London. My friend Mr. L. W. Ritch will there-
fore present the young man to you and you can then examine him.
Mr. Ritch will show you the certificate also. Mr. Ritch will be there
on Friday leaving St. Pancras by the 2. train.
Yours faithfully,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4603
