461. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
February 25, 1941
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I got Sushila's beautiful letter. The description of the tour was
good. I hope the collection also was good.
Here, at present, I am busy with the affairs of the struggle.
By God's grace, I keep good health.
CHI. SITA1,
I got your letter. Your English is still weak. But it will im-
prove gradually. The handwriting is not bad. Do no forget
Gujarati.
Taribehn's health has improved. She is in Delhi and will
stay there for the present. I have forwarded your letters to her.
Manu is all right. Nanabhai is thinking of settling down
somewhere in Surat.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4920
