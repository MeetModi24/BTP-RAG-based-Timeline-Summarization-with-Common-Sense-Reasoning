489. LETTER TO GANGA RAM
January 5, 1933
DEAR LALA GANGARAM,
I thank you for your letter of the 30th ultimo and a copy
of the report you have kindly sent me. I shall try to find time
to go through your report.
Yours sincerely,
LALA GANGA RAM, B.A.
ADVOCATE, SIALKOT CITY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19174
