344. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
April 27, 1930
CHI. LILAVATI,
I got your letter. I was glad to read it. If you continue to
exercise such intelligent self-control, your restlessness will dis-
appear. I do not think there will be any harm in applying the
plaster. If, however, you take hip-baths, menses will certainly start.
It would be better if you did not join the drill. If you keep your-
self calm and do not work too hard, and if you sleep outside in the
open, your fainting fits will stop.
If you do not pass stools easily, you must take enemas. The
bowels should not remain uncleared for more than 24 hours.
Blessings from
BAPU
