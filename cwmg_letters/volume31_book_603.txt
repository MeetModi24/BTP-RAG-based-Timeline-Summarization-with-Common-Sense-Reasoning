603. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
Thursday [November 4, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Your letter. I received Baijnathji's hundi and have sent him
the receipt. Soniramji is here; he is not in good health.
Kamala changed her mind at the last minute and has decided
to go with me to Wardha and I am happy about it. My health
is now good. I had been to Bombay for a day. I had talks
with Sir Ganga Ram, Kamath, Ganguli and Sir Chunilal. Let's
see the result.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2875
