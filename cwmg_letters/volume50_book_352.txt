352. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
August 8, 1932
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
It is surprising that you received no letter of mine although
I wrote many. The last one I deliberately wrote in Hindi.
I shall write at length if you receive this. We are all quite com-
fortable. My weight is 105 lb. and I am taking milk. There
is no cause for anxiety as regards my arm. Blessings to Sarup,
Krishna and Kanta. Your letters do reach me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3421
