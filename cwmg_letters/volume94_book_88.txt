88. LETTER TO PANNALAL JHAVERI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
September 28, 1941
CHI. PANNALAL,
You will have received my letter. If you are not already on your
way here, both you and Gangabehn should be right away. You must
lighten your heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari - Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 33
