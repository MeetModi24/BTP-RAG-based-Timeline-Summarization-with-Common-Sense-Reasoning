492. LETTER TO NANALAL K. JASANI
February 20, 1933
BHAI NANALAL,
I had received your invitation. I had sent my blessings to
Indira directly. She claimed an old connection with me. How-
ever, convey my blessings to both the brides and both the bride-
grooms, and also my hope that they will lead a pure life and con-
tinually grow in the spirit of service.
You must have heard everything about the divisions. Chha-
gan is there. I have discussed the matter with Ratubhai. Do
meet him. If Chhagan agrees, we may proceed further. Lila-
vati also has promised.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : C.W. 9629
