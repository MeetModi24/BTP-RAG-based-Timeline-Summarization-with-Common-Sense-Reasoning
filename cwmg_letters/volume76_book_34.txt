34. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
April 12, 1942
CHI. VALJI,
I got your article. I have passed it. I have been forget-
ting to inform you that you will not get anything more, that
is at the [higher] rate of Rs. 25. The trustees did not pass the
increase. There was no point in insisting on it. I will, therefore,
find the additional sum from some other source. Do you need it
urgently? Ba is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
PROF. V. G. DESAI
DEVAGIRI
POONA 4
From Gujarati: C.W. 7497. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
