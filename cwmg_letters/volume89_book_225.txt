225. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
September 19, 1947
CHI. RAMESHWARI,
I got your letter yesterday.
This much is clear that if nothing can be done here, I
shall not be able to do anything there either. Do what you can
surrendering everything to God. All will turn out right.
Jawaharlal told me that you would be coming here. Bapa
also said the same. My view is that you should do what you can
there with the utmost peace of mind. It is another matter if
you can do nothing and can have no peace of mind. In the
end, follow the promptings of your heart. Keep well both of
you.
The task here is a difficult one. Suhrawardy came yesterday.
He is staying with me.
I feel happy whenever I hear that Brijlal helps you a
great deal. This is as it should be. I have his letters. I showed
them to Jawaharlal. I am not writing separately to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 8012. Also C. W. 3112. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
