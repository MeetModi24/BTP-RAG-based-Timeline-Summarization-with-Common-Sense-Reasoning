497. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 9, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Amrita Lalji says whoever wants fruits and vegetables takes
them away. The key is not in the custody of one particular person.
How is this?
Satisfy Amrita Lalji and hand over the key
to him.
Give this to Chhajuramji. Arrange his bed outside the
verandah in the open.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4376
