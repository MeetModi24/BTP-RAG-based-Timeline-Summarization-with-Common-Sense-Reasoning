304. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
TUNI,
May 2, 1929
CHI. MIRA,
I have not been able recently to write to you as I should like
to. The spare time at my disposal I have been utilizing for
overtaking arrears.
I enclose herewith my letter to Rolland. Please translate and
send, unless you want me to revise the original.
I hope you have now my detailed programme and you know
exactly where I am from day to day up to the 28th instant. I
am anxiously awaiting something from you from Muzaffarpur. I
want to make sure that you are definitely on the road to re-
covering your strength.
Love.
BAPU
Encl. 1
SHRIMATI MIRABEHN
CARE KHADI DEPOT
MUZAFFARPUR
From the original: C.W. 5369. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G. N. 9425
