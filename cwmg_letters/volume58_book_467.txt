467. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI A. MEHTA
September 7, 1934
BHAI BHAGWANJI,
I got both your letters. I have been doing Indian States' work
according to my lights and ability. The principle of ahimsa is
founded on the assumption that every human being is capable
of reform. Since I cannot give up this faith, some difference of
opinion between you and me will always remain.
I understand about Prabhashankar. He will be meeting me
next week, and I will, then, talk with him about that matter too.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5824. Also C.W. 3047. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
