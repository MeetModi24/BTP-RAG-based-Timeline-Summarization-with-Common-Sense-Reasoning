326. LETTER TO MADAN MOHAN MALAVIYA
January 26, 1942
BHAI SAHEB,
How can I express the joy I experienced on meeting you? I hope
you have started what you spoke of. Please arrange to send a wire.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI 5 MADANMOHAN MALAVIYAJI
P. O. BENARES HINDU UNIVERSITY
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2202
