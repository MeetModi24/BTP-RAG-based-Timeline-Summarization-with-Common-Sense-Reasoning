414. LETTER TO BHAILALBHAI D. PATEL
February 21, 1942
BHAI BHAILALBHAI,
Narahari described to me the help you gave him. God him-
self rewards such services. Sardar also had talked to me about you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Dr. Bhailalbhai Patel 75mi Varshaganth Abhinandan Granth, p. 21
