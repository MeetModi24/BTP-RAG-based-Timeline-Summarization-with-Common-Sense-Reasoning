474. LETTER TO S. D. SATAVLEKAR
August 12, 1935
BHAI SATAVLEKAR,
I am grateful for your loving letter. I think the snake was not
venomous at all. Even Panchlegaonkar said that it was not parti-
cularly venomous. It did not bite anyone even though deliberately
provoked. Still, your warning is quite proper.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 4777. Courtesy: S. D. Satavlekar
