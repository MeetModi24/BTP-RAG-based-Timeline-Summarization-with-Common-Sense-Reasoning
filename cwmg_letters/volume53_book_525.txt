525. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA
February 24, 1933
DEAR RAMACHANDRA,
N. is in Poona as you may know.
She tells me you were
for some time in the Ashram at Sabarmati. What year could
that be?
She further tells me that latterly you have not felt quite
happy about her. Please tell me quite frankly all you know about
her or may have against her.
KINGHERI ASHRAM
BANGALORE CITY
From a microfilm: S.N. 20354
