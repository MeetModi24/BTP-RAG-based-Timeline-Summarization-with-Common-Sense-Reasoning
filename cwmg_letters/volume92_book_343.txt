343. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 23, 1930
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. Look upon the Ashram as your home.
Try to improve your Hindi handwriting still further.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
