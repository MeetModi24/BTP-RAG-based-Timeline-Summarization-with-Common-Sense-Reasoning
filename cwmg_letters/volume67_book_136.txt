136. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 28, 1938
CHI. A. S.,
I am comfortable. Vijaya has to stay there as long as the
doctor does not allow her to move. If this is more than six
days, I shall call you back.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
There is no reply from Jinnah.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 659
