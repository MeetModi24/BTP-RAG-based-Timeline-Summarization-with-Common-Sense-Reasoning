589. LETTER TO CHAMPA R. MEHTA
October 8, 1945
CHI. CHAMPA,
I had sent a reply to Shashi. If Keshavlal does not pay
the rent, you can certainly take the necessary steps. Do I have
to tell you that you are not a simple woman? Chi. Sarala should
get well soon.
CHAMPABEHN MEHTA
CHANDRAKUNJ
JAGNATH PLOT
RAJKOT
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
