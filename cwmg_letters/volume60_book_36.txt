36. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
December 24, 1934
DEAR DAUGHTER,
You have kept me in great anxiety by not writing earlier.
But you will not be you if you did otherwise. Thank God however
that I have your card and letter received yesterday. All well
here. Lali is playing in Bombay. Mehr is here studying a little
and playing. She seems to be happy. We leave for Delhi on
28th.
Hope you are eating well. No more today.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 312
