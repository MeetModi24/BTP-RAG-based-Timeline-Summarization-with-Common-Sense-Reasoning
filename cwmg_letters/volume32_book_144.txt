144. LETTER TO S. K. JAIN
AS AT THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
December 13, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter of 3rd October last has lain with me without at-
tention for want of time. If the young man will correspond with
me giving me all the particulars about his life and tell me whe-
ther he will be prepared to go through the discipline at the Satya-
graha Ashram for six months before he is put to any work, I
shall see what can be done. As a matter of fact, there is no diffi-
culty about accommodating any number of young men of cha-
racter and ability.
I am in Wardha up to the 20th instant, after which date, it
would be better to address your letter to my permanent address.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. K. JAIN
15, NAGJI BUNGALOWS
MATUNGA, G. I. P.
BOMBAY
