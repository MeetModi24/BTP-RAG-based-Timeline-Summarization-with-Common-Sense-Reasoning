392. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
DELHI,
March 23, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
I thank you for your letter of 22nd instant regarding Con-
gress action about foreign cloth. The draft questions and answers
suggested by you seem to me to be in order.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Home Department, Political, File No. 33/6, 1931. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
