325. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
May 31, 1931
CHI. PREMA,
Why do Lakshmi and Padma keep falling ill? Do you think
that they are careless about taking medicines, etc.? If Padma
continues to get fever, she will lose her health. Who is specially
responsible for looking after her? No child in the Ashram should
feel the absence of his or her parents. How is Krishnakumari?
Write to me about the others too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10255. Also C.W. 6703. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
