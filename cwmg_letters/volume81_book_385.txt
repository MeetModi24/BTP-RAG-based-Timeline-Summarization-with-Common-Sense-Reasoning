385. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
September 3, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have your two letters. One regarding resolution No. 209
and the other about Krishna District.
Both suggestions are
accepted.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SHYAMLALJI
KASTURBA SMARAK
BAJAJWADI, WARDHA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
