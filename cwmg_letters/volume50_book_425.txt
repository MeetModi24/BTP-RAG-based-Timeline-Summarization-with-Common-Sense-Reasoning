425. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
August 21, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
The word for 'learned' in the shloka you have quoted includes
the meaning 'virtuous'. In the olden times the word for a learned
man, viz., vidvan was synonymous with vedavid, meaning 'the
knower of the Vedas', and it was believed that anyone who knew
the Vedas was bound to be virtuous. What I meant to say
was that Doctor was respected because of his virtue. This does not
mean that scholarship may never be honoured. But we know
very well that there are many highly learned people who are
utterly ignored.
BAPU
