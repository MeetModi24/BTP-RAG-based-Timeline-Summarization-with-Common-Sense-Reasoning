638. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 15, 1945
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Why did you fall ill? You must not exert yourself to do
anything. It is one's duty to keep the body radiant as copper.
Eat only what suits you. Do only what you are able to. A
its Joint Secretaryship. Moreover, you should not forget that
you have resigned from Secretaryship, not from trusteeship.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Your decision to publish the resignation letter is well con-
ceived. That will scotch all rumours and speculation either in
private or in public. You are already on leave. Does it [resig-
nation] take effect from now itself?
