359. LETTER TO ALI HASAN
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
August 26, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Of course, I remember you well and
your hospitality. I must, however, confess that I do not at all
like your manifesto. You have made communalism as the pivot
of your appeal and you are looking to the Hindu voters to vote
for you not because of your superior ability and other qualifica-
tions but solely on the ground of your being a Mussalman. I
feel that you have struck the wrong note. If you had, on the
contrary, recited your better qualifications, if you claim them, and
added the hope that your being a Mussalman will not be regarded
as a disqualification by the Hindu voters, from my point of view,
your manifesto would have been unexceptionable. As it is, on the
merits, I am obliged to give you a hostile opinion. But, in any
case, even if your manifesto had been unexceptionable, being an
avowed non-co-operator, I cannot have given you anything
whatsoever by way of endorsement for publication.
Yours sincerely,
ALI HASSAN , ESQ.
BARRISTER-AT-LAW
BANK ROAD
PATNA
