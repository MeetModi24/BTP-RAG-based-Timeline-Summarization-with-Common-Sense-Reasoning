321. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 10, 1938
CHI. SUSHILA,
What must I do to please you? I gave you the cutting to
make you laugh. It only hurt you. I showed you Mahadev's
letter to make you see what I feel. Even that hurt you.
Why are you so sensitive? If Mother does not see your
good the way I see it, how can I keep you? Even you agree
that Mother should be kept happy.
Now be calm. Whatever has to happen will happen. If
you cannot have peace by staying with me, then leave me. If
you think it is in your interest to stay with me, what does it
matter to you. . .1
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
