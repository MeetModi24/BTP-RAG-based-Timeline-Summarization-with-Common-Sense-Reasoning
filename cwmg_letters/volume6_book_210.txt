210. LETTER TO MESSRS WOOLGAR & ROBERTS
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 20, 1906
MESSRS WOOLGAR & ROBERTS
58, FLEET STREET, E.C.
DEAR SIRS,
I now enclose herewith cheque for £1.10. for press cuttings.
From and after the 28th instant, will you please address all the
cuttings to L. W. Ritch, Esq., Secretary of the South Africa British
Indian Committee, No. 28, Queen Anne's Chambers, Westminster.
Yours faithfully,
Enclosure
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4597
