365. LETTER TO SATYA DEVA
March 7, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It does not show that you have any knowl-
edge of things going around you. From your writing I hardly
think that you will be able to render any service to the Associa-
tion as an accountant. But you can become a good village worker
in your own district and if you will be that, I should like to ask
you to correspond with Babu Brijkishor Prasad, Durbhanga.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SATYA DEVA
C/O B. HARIBHAU SAHAY
SEGAULI SUGAR WORKS LTD.
SEGAULI, (CHAMPARAN)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
