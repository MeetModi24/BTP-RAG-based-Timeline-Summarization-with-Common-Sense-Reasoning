478. LETTER TO SUMATIBAI RAI
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
KUMARI SUMATIBAI,
I have your letter. If you have the strength, the knowledge
and the will for it, do work for the Kasturba Trust.
Please tell Ramkrishna Jaju that I am not replying to him
separately. May he get well soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SUMATIBAI RAI
JAIN SEVIKASHRAM
SHOLAPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
