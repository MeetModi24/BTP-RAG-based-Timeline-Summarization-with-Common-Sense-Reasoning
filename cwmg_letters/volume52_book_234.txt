234. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
December 11, 1932
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
Your letter. Give up all worries; leave everything to God. It
is good if Dr. Sharma comes to the Ashram. We must have
only nature cure but up to now we did not find anyone willing to
observe the Ashram rules too. These days long letters should
not be expected from me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 267
