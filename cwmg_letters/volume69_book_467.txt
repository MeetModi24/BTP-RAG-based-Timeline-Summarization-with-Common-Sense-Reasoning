467. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
ABBOTTABAD,
July 14, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I forgot to answer one question yesterday. Yes, your Hindi is
very good. The letters are perfectly formed. Why don't you con-
tinue ? If you don't care to write wholly in Hindi, do it half and half.
The reply from Sir Mirza is an utter disappointment. How-
ever we must continue to appeal to the best in him. There is no
doubt that the State Congress people have not behaved well with
the Dewan.
The other letter is characteristic. I have not yet studied
Sir S[ikandar Hyat Khan]'s scheme. No time left after dealing
with the day's work. I shall have to make time.
Rest from Mahadev.
Love.
TYRANT
[PS.]
Sir Gokul Chand just waiting to come in. I go to the 'lib-
rary' and then see him. Herewith Sardar Datar Singh's letter for
you to read and keep for me.
From the original: C.W. 3929. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7238
