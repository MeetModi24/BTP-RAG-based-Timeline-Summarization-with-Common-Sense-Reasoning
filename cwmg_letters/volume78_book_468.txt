468. LETTER TO NATHTHUBHAI PAREKH
SEVAGRAM,
November 23, 1944
BHAI NATHTHUBHAI,
I have your letter full of affection. This is only to acknow-
ledge receipt of it. I just don't have the time to write more.
Writing even this little thing is burdensome, but how can I
forget you?
Blessings from
BAPU
NATHTHUBHAI PAREKH
C/O KISHORE BROTHERS
BANGALORE
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
