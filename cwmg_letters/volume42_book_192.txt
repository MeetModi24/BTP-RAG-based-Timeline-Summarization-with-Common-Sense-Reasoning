192. LETTER TO SANTOK GANDHI
November 16, 1929
CHI. SANTOK,
I have now Mahadev's letter from which I learn the details.
It is very good that you underwent the operation. I hope you
are resting now. Ask Radha or Rukhi to write to me a detailed
account.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 8678. Courtesy: Radhabehn Choudhri
