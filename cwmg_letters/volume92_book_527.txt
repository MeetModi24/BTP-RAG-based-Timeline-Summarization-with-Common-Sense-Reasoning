527. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
YERAVDA MANDIR,
May 5, 1932
CHI. SHANKERLAL,
I have your letter. Take care of your health in every way
possible. If you are in good health, other things can be taken
care of. What advice can I give you at the moment? What
can I even suggest? May God protect you.
I am writing a separate letter to Lilavati.
We have to consider about Gulzarilal. But I am not
worrying since Jamnalal is there.
Things here are proceeding well. I have not yet felt the need
to take milk. Almonds seem to be helping me quite a bit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32747
1 Wife of Dahyabhai, Vallabhbhai Patel's son
