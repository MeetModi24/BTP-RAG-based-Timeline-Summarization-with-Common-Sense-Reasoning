208. LETTER TO HARIHAR VYAS
PALM BUN, JUHU,
June 1, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
I have read out your letter to Gandhiji. He says he is unable to advise
on the use of a purely communal donation. He suggests that the scope may be made
general. Whoever benefits by the donation, the benefit will go to India. If the advice
is accepted, he will make suggestions.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI HARIHAR VYAS
JAWAHAR GUNJ
JUBBELPORE
