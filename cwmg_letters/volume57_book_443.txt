443. LETTER TO KARUNA BAI
April 22, 1934
Shri Shukdevji has given me the invitation for your wedding.
I hope you and Narmada Prasad will lead a happy married life
devoted to service.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI KARUNA BAI
C/O
SHRI SHUKDEV PRASAD TIWARI
SUHAGPUR
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8681
