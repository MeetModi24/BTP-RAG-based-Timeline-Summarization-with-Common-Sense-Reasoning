21. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day, July 9, 1930
CHI. SHARDA (BABU)1,
Is it any wonder that we who are housed in the Mandir
are in fine spirit? But why should you be suffering from asthma?
Is it not due to their own fault that children or grown-ups fall
ill? If this is true, you must find out where your fault lay and
should not repeat it. Once you have recovered, your asthma
would not visit you again if you follow a regular course of deep-
breathing exercises, sun-bathing, massage, etc., and if you observe
restraint in food, etc.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9885. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
