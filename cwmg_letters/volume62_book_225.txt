225. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[February 8, 1936]
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter relieved me of all anxiety. I hope you will be
able to keep the promise not to brood over the past mistakes
but look hopefully to the future not to repeat them. I am
sure you will be quite all right if in coming to decisions [you] never
think of what others will say. Consult the Dweller within and
He will never fail you. I am making steady progress. Doctors
come tomorrow. You shall have their opinion.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6309. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9775
