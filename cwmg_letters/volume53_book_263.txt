263. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
January 29, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
I had a letter from Purushottam. Bombay will probably
suit him. He should try it, and then come and see me whether
it suits him or not. How happy I would be if both of you comp-
letely recover your health.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 871. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
