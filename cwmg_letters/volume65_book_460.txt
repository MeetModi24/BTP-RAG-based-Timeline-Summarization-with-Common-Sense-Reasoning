460. LETTER TO N. C. KELKAR
July 15, 1937
DEAR MR. KELKAR,
Deo has sent me the enclosed cutting containing a report
of your speech. He wants me to reply to certain allegations
about me in that report. Before I do so I should like to
have your confirmation of the report. For, I know, how often
public speeches are misreported, sometimes consciously, some-
times unconsciously.
I hope you are in possession of the best of health.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 3121. Courtesy: Kashinath N. Kelkar
