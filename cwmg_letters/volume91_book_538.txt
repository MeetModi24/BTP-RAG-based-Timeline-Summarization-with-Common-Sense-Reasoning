538. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 5, 1926
DEAR MOTIBABU,
I have your usual balance-sheet for which I thank you.
Our accountant here tells me that if in your next balance-sheet
you will send the amount of capital investment in the khadi
work and the management expenses, he will be able to appre-
ciate the figures better.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
BABU MOTILAL ROY
PRABARTAK SANGHA
CHANDERNAGORE
(BENGAL)
From a photostat: G.N. 11031
