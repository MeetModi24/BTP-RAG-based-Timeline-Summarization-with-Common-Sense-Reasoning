144. LETTER TO S. D. SAKLATWALA
BHAVNAGAR,
July 1, 1934
DEAR MR. SAKLATWALA,
I thank you for your prompt reply dated 19th ultimo.
Constant pressure of work has delayed acknowledgment. In the
circumstances you mention, I shall not trouble you for further
information at present.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI S. D. SAKLATWALA
C/O MESSRS TATA AND SONS
BOMBAY HOUSE
BOMBAY FORT
Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
DURING HARIJAN TOUR, GUJARAT, JUNE 1934
WITH HARIJAN CHILDREN, BHAVNAGAR, JULY 1934
