324. LETTER TO SUBHAS CHANDRA BOSE
AHMEDABAD,
August 15, 1931
DEAR SUBHAS,
I had your wire. As there was no hurry I did not wire back.
In view of it we all came to the conclusion that it would be
better for Mr. Aney to go on with his investigations so that at
least a proper finding on the facts would be available.
I hope that Dinesh's resolution was rescinded.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. SUBHAS CHANDRA BOSE
From a photostat: S.N. 17472
