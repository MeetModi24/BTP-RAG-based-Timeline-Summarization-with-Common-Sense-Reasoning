73. LETTER TO SHAMAL R. RAVAL
June 19, 1932
CHI. SHAMAL,
It will be well if the food which you eat suits you. The fact
that you suffer from constipation indicates that you should eat
boiled vegetables in good quantity.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3142. Also C.W. 2881. Courtesy:
Shamal R. Raval
