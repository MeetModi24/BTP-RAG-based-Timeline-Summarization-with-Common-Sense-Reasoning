43. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
ON WAY TO RANGOON,
Phagan Vad 14 [March 14, 1915]
CHI. NARANDAS,
We are having a very bitter experience of the deck, which is
but common. Those going are Ba, Ramdas and myself. We
hope to return to Shantiniketan by the end of this month.
I see that there has been a misunderstanding between Harilal
and me. He has parted from me completely. He will receive no
monetary help from me. [I] gave him Rs. 45/- and he parted at
Calcutta. There was no bitterness. Let him take any books or
clothes of mine he may want. Hand over the key to him. He may
take out anything he likes and then return the key. You must have
heard that the key supposed to have been lost was found. It was
with Revashankerbhai. Get it from him, if you have not got it
already.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original in pencil in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5668.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
