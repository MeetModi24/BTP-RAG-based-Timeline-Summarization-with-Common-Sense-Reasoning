80. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
March 12, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
You may certainly try to learn the Gita verses by heart while
working in the field, but you must not do so if your pronunciation
is bad. Pronunciation can be learned only from the teachers.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 9364. Courtesy: Ravindra R.
Patel
