228. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
[On or after October 15, 1934]
CHI. VASUMATI,
You also seem to have become very busy like me. For a
few days you wrote daily, and now you don't write at all. How
is that? This time I am not bringing any girls with me. Ba also
is not coming. I would advise you, too, to remain where you
are. These days there is no cooking to be done for me. I shall
use the ink.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9389. Also C.W. 634. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
