481. LETTER TO JACOB SORIS
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 31, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I understand that you collected some fund during my visit to
Ceylon. These funds went to Allahabad instead of Ahmedabad and
the Allahabad office returned them to you and since then the funds
are held up there. As your subscriptions were earmarked for khadi,
I hope that you will not divert the funds but send them to
me for khadi.
Yours sincerely,
JACOB SORIS, ESQ.
MESSRS PAUL SORIS & Co., BADULLA (CEYLON)
