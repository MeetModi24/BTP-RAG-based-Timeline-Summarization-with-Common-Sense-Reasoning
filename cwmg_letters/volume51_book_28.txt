28. LETTER TO TILAKAM
September 4, 1932
MY DEAR TILAK,
How extraordinary! I did not know that you had left Mani
Bhavan. Where are you staying now and what are you doing?
Do give me a full account of your doings please. I hope you are
keeping very good health now. Did you get my previous letter?
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9553
