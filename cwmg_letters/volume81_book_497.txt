497. LETTER TO ANAND SUNDARAM
POONA,
September 26, 1945
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letter. It would not be right to argue that just
because I went to England all others should. Should others
repeat all mistakes that I have committed? I don't believe that
you can serve India better by studying abroad. To think this
is sheer ignorance. To feel that education abroad is the best
betrays ignorance.
I do not give my blessings to those who wish to study abroad.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANAND SUNDARAM
KRISHNAKUTIR
BENARES HINDU UNIVERSITY
