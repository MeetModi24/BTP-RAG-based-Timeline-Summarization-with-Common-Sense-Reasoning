149. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 30, 1940
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I do not know if Mahadev wrote to you about Suresh's
visit. He himself is now more inclined to our side. He wants
to draw Subhas also but he is not likely to succeed. I have
told him that he [Subhas] can come and see me whenever he wants.
He knows my position. His publicly expressed views clearly indi-
cate that he will not be able to come. He [Suresh] believes
that those views have now changed. It doesn't seem so to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 240
