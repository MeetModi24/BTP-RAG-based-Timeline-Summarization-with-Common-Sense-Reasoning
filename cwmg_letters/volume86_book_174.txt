174. LETTER TO MALATI CHOWDHURI
November 20, 1946
CHI. MALATIBEHN 2 ,
Bapa has shown me your letter.
Send me the judgment. But now that Mahtab Babu is the
Premier, why can't he do something? Since there is a popular
ministry, a man like me is redundant.
It will not be right for you or anyone else to come here
at present. There is darkness all around which will not be
dispelled but only deepen with the arrival of outsiders. The best
thing is to remain where you are and do your duty.
BAPU
SMT. MALATI CHOWDHURI
CUTTACK
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
