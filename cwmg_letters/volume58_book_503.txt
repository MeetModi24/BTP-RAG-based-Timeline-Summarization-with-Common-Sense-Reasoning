503. LETTER TO ATMARAM
September 14, 1934
BHAI ATMARAM,
This is my reply to your letter. The deeper I go into the
matter the more clearly I see Balvantrai's sincerity. Your sus-
pecting your own friend doesn't seem proper. If you still harbour
suspicion, you may certainly see Bhai Balvantrai. I will not write
to him any more and give him pain. I have seen that you are
by nature inclined to suspicion and exaggeration.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
