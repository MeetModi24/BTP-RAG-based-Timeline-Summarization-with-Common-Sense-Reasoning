494. LETTER TO K. T. BHASHYAM
SODEPUR,
January 6, 1946
BHAI BHASHYAM,
I have your long letter. I may not be regarded as a guide
in any way any more. The days are gone when I could act as
one. My advice is that you may take up whatever appeals to
you from among the activities I have already indicated. Hence
you should come together and do whatever you can regardless of
whether the outcome is good or bad. I am now of no use
whatever for such matters.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI K. T. BHASHYAM
COTTONPET
BANGALORE CITY
