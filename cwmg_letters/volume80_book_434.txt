434. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
June 4, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
Your reply to Majid was good.
What else could you have
said? Your body should be strong as steel. It can be made so
only with effort. Let Zohra and Vina write to me. How is your
health? What has Vina been doing?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 9029. Also C.W. 9153. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
