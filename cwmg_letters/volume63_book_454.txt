454. LETTER TO GLADYS OWEN
October 6, 1936
MY DEAR GLADYS1,
I was glad to receive your good wishes.
You are a bad woman to have free days just when I am to
be away. I leave here on 22nd and do not return till 5th Nov.
I am in Kashi on 24th and 25th [October]. Tell me when you
can come.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6193
