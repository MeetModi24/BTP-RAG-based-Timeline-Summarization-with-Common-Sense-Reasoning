531. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
DELHI,
October 4, 1946
CHI. SUMI,
I am glad you are comfortable there. You will do as much
as you can, with due regard for your health, and go forward.
That is what I hope.
I had certainly hoped to be in Sevagram this time but I
am held up here till the 20th on account of the work. Let us
see when I can get away.
Let me have news from there.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUM. SUMITRA R. GANDHI
KHALASI LINE
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
