66. LETTER TO MRS. G. HAINES
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
September 29, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
It was good of you to have written to me and shown so
much care for my health.
You will be glad to know that, though I do not take orange
juice and honey mixed together, I do take three oranges per
day and, early in the morning, honey and a little lemon juice
added to it.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. G. HAINES
ABBOTSFORD
10 MARKET PLACE
WARWICK
From a photostat: S.N. 17883
