457. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA
March 2, 1942
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
You are foolish. You have not at all fallen by marrying.
How can you conquer your nature? But you could surely be
said to have fallen if you led a life of luxury and forgot your
duty. Marriage can become a means of rising high if it can be
utilized for practising restraint.
You would be committing a
great mistake if you think you have fallen because of marriage,
for then it would slacken your effort to rise. Hence have the
faith that by marrying you are sure to rise higher.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANNAPURNA DEVI
GANDHI ASHRAM
P.O. BARI
CUTTACK
ORISSA 1
