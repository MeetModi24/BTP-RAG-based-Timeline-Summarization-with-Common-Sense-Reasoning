629. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SIMLA,
June 27, 1945
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter. Beware of overworking yourself or acting against
the weather for which you are not made. Do come to Simla
whether I am here or not. I may leave any day. I am glad you
have two helpers. I wish Balvantsinha can come but I doubt.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABAI
KISAN ASHRAM
BAHADRABAD P.O., via JWALAPUR
(NEAR HARDWAR)
From the original: C.W. 6508. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9903
