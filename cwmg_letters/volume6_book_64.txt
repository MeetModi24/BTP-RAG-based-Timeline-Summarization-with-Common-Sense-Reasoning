64. LETTER TO AMEER ALI
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 1, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I have your postcard. My letter informing you, however, of the
date of the Deputation must have crossed the postcard. Mr. Ally, I am
sorry to say, has not yet returned from the Hospital, though he is get-
ting much better. Both he and I are anxious to pay our respects to you
and to place the position before you before the Deputation waits on Lord
Elgin. If, therefore, you can give an appointment some time before
Thursday Mr. Ally will come down from Bromley specially and we will
wait on you.
I am,
Yours faithfully,
AMEER ALI, ESQ., C.I.E.
THE LAMBDENS,
BEENHAM
NEAR READING
