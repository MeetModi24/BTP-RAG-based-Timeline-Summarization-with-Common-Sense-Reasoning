251. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
May 25, 1937
CHI. MUNNALAL,
God's will be done. Sardar will not permit me to leave this
place till the 10th June. My heart is there. The heat there
seems pleasant to me and the cool breeze here seems hot, for I
know that my right place is there. Today also I am not replying
to your [earlier] letter, because there is no time and the pressure of
work is heavy. I cannot be very happy that you are going to start
eating wheat. You should pass two to three months without
eating wheat, i.e., without starch. Not eating wheat is likely to
do you no harm, and the benefit is obvious.
I have sent a wire today. It must have been sent to you
by Chhotelalji. I, therefore, don't write about that here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8582. Also C.W. 7015. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
