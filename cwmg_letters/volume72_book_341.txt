341. LETTER TO PUSHPA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 17, 1940
Bravo! May God bless you in your University career. Don't
damage your eyes or other parts of your body in the mere pursuit
of studies.
Do collect for the Andrews Memorial and use this as your autho-
rity. And congratulate the two brothers on their having finished
12 chapters of the Gita.
Love.
BAPU
KUMARI PUSHPA
C/O SHRI V. A. SUNDARAM
KRISHNAKUTIR
BENARES HINDU UNIVERSITY
From a photostat: G.N. 3193
