100. LETTER TO ANNIE BESANT
C/O "COMRADE" OFFICE,
DELHI,
September 14, 1924
DEAR DR. BESANT,
I am eternally grateful for your letter and your note on our
conversation. I wired to you on receipt of your letter that
Young India was already published. I am therefore now handing
the note to the Associated Press. I have not yet heard from the
Swarajists. It is therefore not possible to say what the A.I.C.C.
is likely to say. I shall however keep you informed of any deve-
lopment that may take place.
My son will go to Adyar as soon as you want him to. Indeed
he will regard it as a privilege to teach you spinning.
Yours sincerely,
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
