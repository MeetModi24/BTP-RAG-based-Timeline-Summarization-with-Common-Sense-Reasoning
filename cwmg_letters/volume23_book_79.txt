79. LETTER TO JAIL SUPERINTENDENT, YERAVDA
YERAVDA CENTRAL PRISON,
April 16, 1923
THE SUPERINTENDENT
YERAVDA C.P.
SIR,
As my youngest son has come to see me today, I should like if
possible to see the Government reply to my letter of the 23rd
February last regarding the regulation of interviews with me.
The reply will enable me to find out whether consistently with my
said letter I should see my son or not, as you know today is my
silent day. The silence breaks at 2 p.m. today.
I remain,
Yours obediently,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 8025; also Young India, 6-3-1924
