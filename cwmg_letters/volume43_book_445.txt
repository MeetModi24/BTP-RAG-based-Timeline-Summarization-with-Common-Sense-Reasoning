445. LETTER TO KALAVATI TRIVEDI
Silence Day [May 26, 1930]
DEAR KALAVATI,
I await a letter from you. How is the state of your mind at
present? What are you doing? Write to me your account.
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 575
