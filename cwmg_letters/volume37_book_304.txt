304. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
September 12, 1928
CHI. BRIJKISHORE 2 ,
I have your letter. If your nephew has grown so overbearing
it will do him good if one just has nothing to do with him. If
you think it necessary, let him be paid a monthly allowance.
Build up your body. For this, the mind too has to be healthy!
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2359
