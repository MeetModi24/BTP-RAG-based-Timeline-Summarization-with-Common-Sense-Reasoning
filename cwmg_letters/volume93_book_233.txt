233. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
[Before April 28, 1938]
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I am getting restive about the talks with J. Are you hasten-
ing the matter? I am holding up some of my actions for that
purpose.
I hope good news continue to come about Indu.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy : Gandhi-Nehru Papers. Courtesy : Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
