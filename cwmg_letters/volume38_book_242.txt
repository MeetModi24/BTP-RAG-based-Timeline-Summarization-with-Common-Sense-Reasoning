242. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
December 10, 1928
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your letter. The experiment I am conducting was
necessary for my mental peace; I am careful [about it].
On the 20th I leave this place for Sambalpur. I shall reach
Calcutta on the 23rd. I hope to reach the Ashram in January.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
What shall I do about the Fund?
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2361
