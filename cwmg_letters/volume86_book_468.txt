468. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM AND ABHA GANDHI
January 14, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. Forget about Kafikhan. If he wants to
come, he will come. Just think of God alone. You will have
taken an enema with lemon and salt. Remain cheerful.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. ABHA,
God will reward you for your dedication.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 562
