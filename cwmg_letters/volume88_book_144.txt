144. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
NEW DELHI,
June 20, 1947
CHI. AMRIT BABU,
Your two letters. I am sorry about Ramen and Abha .
How I wish both will be healed under Kularanjan Babu.
Shanti Kumar wants Abha in Bombay. I have sent his com-
munication to Kanu. My movement is unsettled. Otherwise I
would have her with me. My own inclination is that she
should give the fullest trial to Kularanjan Babu.
About Paniala you should discuss with Satis Babu and
act as he decides. It grieves me that I am not in Noakhali. I
do not know when I shall return. The poor widows should be
supported. You can show this to Satis Babu.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C. W. 10567. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
