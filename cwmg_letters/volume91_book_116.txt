116. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI ANOOPCHAND MEHTA
AHMEDABAD,
Bhadarva Vad 4 [September 5, 1917]
BHAISHRI BHAGWANJI,
I have your letter. Many thanks. The decision about
Viramgam has been well taken.
The question of small princely states is always present in my
mind. For the present it does not seem to advance any further.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
BHAGWANJI ANOOPCHAND VAKIL
RAJKOT
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5805. Also C.W. 3028. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
