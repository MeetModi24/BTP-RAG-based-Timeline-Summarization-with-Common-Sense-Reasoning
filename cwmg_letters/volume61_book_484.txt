484. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
WARDHA,
August 15, 1935
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Four days back Jethalal left for Anantpur. He needed some
bhakharis prepared from ghee-softened dough as he buys nothing
from the wayside stations. I sanctioned the preparation when
Amtussalaam referred it to me. I was reminded of the occasion
when I had berated you for the same matter; the recollection
was painful. Although I know it turned to your ultimate good,
it does not justify my own error. Notwithstanding the purity
of motive I am not absolved. Do forgive me, your Bapu is so
very imperfect! The other details have been conveyed by K.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 1879
