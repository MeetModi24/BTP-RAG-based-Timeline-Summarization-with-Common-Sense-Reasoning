517. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
January 7, 1933
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter and Prema's, and also those of the others.
My reply to Prema is enclosed. I hope that she has realized her
foolishness.
What food do you eat now? What was the effect of Jowar
porridge and rotlas of Jowar and bajri? From where do you get the
ghee?
Is there any relief in the harassment by the Chharas? Does
anybody visit them?
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I
