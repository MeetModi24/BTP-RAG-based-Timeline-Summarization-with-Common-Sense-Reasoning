642. LETTER TO R. ACHUTHAN
October 15, 1945
BHAI ACHUTHAN1,
I have written a great deal about constructive work. Read
it all, over and over again. Do not listen to criticism by others.
If you hear criticism, you must have the strength to answer it.
How long can I go on replying to people? Students should deve-
lop the ability to understand and reply to such things.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 10853
