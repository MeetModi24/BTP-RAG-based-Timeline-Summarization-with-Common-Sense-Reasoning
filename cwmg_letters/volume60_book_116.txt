116. LETTER TO KHALIQ
January 21, 1935
There seems to be nothing in the coming interview be-
tween Jinnah and Rajenbabu. Let us have real unity, even
if we have to wait.
In the Assembly much will depend upon
the correct behaviour of Congressmen on every occasion.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
