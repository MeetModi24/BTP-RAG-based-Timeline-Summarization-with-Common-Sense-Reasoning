221. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
October 14, 1934
DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
I hope everything is fixed up for Anand.
If you know anything about the Karachi murder I should
like you to write to me.
BAPU
SHRI JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
C/O CONGRESS HOUSE
WARDEN ROAD, BOMBAY
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
