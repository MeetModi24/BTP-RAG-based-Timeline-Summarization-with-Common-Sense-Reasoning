425. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
March 19, 1935
CHI. PANDITJI,
I got your letter. I was waiting for it. Do whatever you can
for Rambhau. Do not be angry. Conciliate Mridulabehn. I had a
very good letter from her. I do appreciate that you have started
cooking separately. Teach Rambhau to cook also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9073
