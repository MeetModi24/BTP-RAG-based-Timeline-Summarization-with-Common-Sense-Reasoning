183. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 11, 1945
CHI. BABUDI,
You seem to be very dear to God. Something or the other
always befalls you. So a heavy weight fell on Anand ? He was
lucky to survive. Was the accident caused through any of his
pranks? Learn to train him. God is testing you; see that you
pass.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W.
10057. Courtesy: Sharda G.
Chokhawala
