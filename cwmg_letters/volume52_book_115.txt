115. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
November 27, 1932
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
I have your long letter. I can write only a short one. As
regards going to Delhi and all other matters, do whatever Naran-
das suggests. You must be convinced that sick persons too can
serve. I have mentioned two individuals in the letter to Kusum.
Read it. Give up thinking of too many matters. May God keep
you safe and fulfil all your wishes to render service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 265
