288. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
Saturday [April 30, 1927]
CHI. MIRA,
Well, I have nothing to say today. I only write so that you
may know that nowadays I am rather worried about you. God
will take care of you. Do you understand what I say?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 5222. Courtesy: Mirabehn
