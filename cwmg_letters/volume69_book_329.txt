329. LETTER TO NRISINHPRASAD K. BHATT
ON THE TRAIN,
June 1, 1939
BHAI NANABHAI,
I wanted to write to you as soon as I got your wire, but
could not do so. Now I am writing this in the train. The path
of those who worship ahimsa under all circumstances is straight.
Such a person will not indulge in fighting even in self-defence. But
he who does not have that strength has but to run away—that is,
play the coward—if he does not attack the attacker. Cowardice is
worse than violence because cowards can never be non-violent. So
such people should learn to defend themselves. Under its con-
stitution members of the Parishad can be said to be wedded to
non-violence. But here such ahimsa is of no use. A person who
has full faith in non-violence should be a thousand times more
fearless than an armed man. Such fearlessness is not gained by
joining some organization. Hence every member of the Parishad
should find an independent way for himself. It is the duty of every
believer in ahimsa to see that cowardice is not propagated in the
name of non-violence. So we must frankly tell the common people
that we are unable to guide them. It is not proper that they
should look to us. If they can independently think of non-violent
ways they will not ask us and if they do ask us we should give
them only one guidance that they should not be weak under
any circumstances. They should learn to deal with the attacker.
It is possible that if they observe restraint and if a handful of
us can manifest true ahimsa, the attacker too may turn to
ahimsa.
Please explain all this to all our workers there. The talk
of secret settlement that is going on in Bhavnagar is harmful.
Settlement can be with straightforward people. Here there is
nothing of the sort.
Blessings from
BAPU
