204. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Bhadarva Vad 7 [September 16, 1919]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. If you have not sent the raisins do not
send them by post. I shall take them when I go there. I am
afraid I shall have to return on Friday night itself. Right now
Navajivan takes up the whole of my time. I am sure I shall be
able to regain my health. Bhai Shankerlal fully endorses the
idea of bringing out Young India from here. You must learn the
trick of improving your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
