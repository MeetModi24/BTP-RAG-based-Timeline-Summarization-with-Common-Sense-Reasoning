558. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
September 22, 1941
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
You are still not on the rails again. If you cannot judge
definitely in fifteen days, I should like you to come here. If you
are well enough to travel, I think it might even be desirable
that you should come and spend a few days here. Do what
suits you. Rajendra Babu is daily improving. He comes every
day now.
Mahadev's letter is enclosed. From there you may send [it]
whenever you like.
Prema Kantak must have seen you. Her work is going on well.
What is the position about Allah Bux? I am convinced that
the Congress must withdraw. Rajendra Babu suspects something.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 255
