300. LETTER TO HARIBHAI DESAI
[After January 5, 1922]
It is good that Mahadev is jailed. He will get rest thereby.
The work outside is so heavy that he would have fallen ill.
Although there is hardship in jail now, I am sure that condi-
tions will improve very soon. Mahadev has the knack of win-
ning over people wherever he goes. I am sure that by speaking
sweetly and modestly he will have any undue hardship in jail
life removed. You should neither lose patience nor worry.
Mahadev Desai's Early Life, p. 64
