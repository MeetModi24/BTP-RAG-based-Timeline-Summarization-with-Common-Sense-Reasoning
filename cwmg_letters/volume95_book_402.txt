402. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
Silence day, SODEPUR,
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Your letter.
I like it that you are absorbed in work. I have, of course, replied
to your earlier letter.
Keep your mind, body and speech pure while you are there. I
am glad that you are there. Its good too that your mother-in-law has
come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8372
