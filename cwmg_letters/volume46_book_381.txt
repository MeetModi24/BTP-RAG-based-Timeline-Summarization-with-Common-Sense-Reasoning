381. LETTER TO PADAMLAL
June 3, 1931
BHAI PADAMLALJI,
I have your letter. The subjects of States have the right to
take any steps including satyagraha in respect of the hardships
they have to face. But the name of the Congress should not be
associated with this because it will not be able to help them in
their struggle; the States' people should rely on their own strength
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[From a facsimile of the Hindi]
Madhya Pradesh aur Gandhiji, p. 136
