387. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
April 18, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
Since we ourselves are a democratic people, it is natural that
our temperament should draw us towards a democracy. But as
lovers of truth it is our duty to see justice and support the party
which has justice on its side.
Blessings from
BAPU

Narandas Gandhi
