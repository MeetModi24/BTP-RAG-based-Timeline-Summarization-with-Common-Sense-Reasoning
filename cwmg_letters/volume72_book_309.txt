309. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
July 10, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
Your 2 letters. I won't keep you quite letterless. Wired to-
day to say you can join the States C[onference] Executive. In
fact I had a hand in your nomination. The matter was referred
to me. And I approved. I forgot to tell you about it.
Even when I write in Gujarati, its Hindi has to go. And
263
now that I wrote that Appeal something will appear in English
too from my pen.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3982. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7291
