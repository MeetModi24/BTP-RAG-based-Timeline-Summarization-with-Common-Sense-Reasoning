348. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
SRIRAMPUR,
December 26, 1946
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Lying in bed I am unable to write to you myself.
Those who were doing such work for me have been sent
to different places. Manu alone is with me out of attachment
and does all work for me. I won't be able to dictate a full
reply to your letter. I do not even remember all your queries.
As for your coming here, if I have not already written to
you I want you to stay on there for the present. That alone
is your duty. Be composed, restrain your anger and live like a
sthitaprajna.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 1968
