140. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
[Before March 4, 1927]
Besides these I want a takli with a case to be presented to the
bride and the groom. Manilal does not spin on the takli; so
Sushila alone is to be given one. Ask Manilal if he would ply
one so that I may give one to him also. Please send two.
Manilal should himself bring along these articles when he comes.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Please look after Professor Jugalkishore.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7765. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
