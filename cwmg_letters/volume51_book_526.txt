526. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
October 31, 1932
I do not want to have dysentery, but if I get it I should go
through it. But at the slightest sign of it I shall stop all food.
The non-co-operation has to be progressive. This course I have
adopted to cause the Government the least possible inconve-
nience. I cannot live and not work for the removal of untouchabi-
lity. But if the Government would that I died rather than I lived
to work for removal of untouchability I cannot help it.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 189. Also Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home
Department, Special Branch, File No. 800(40) (4), Pt. I, p. 249
