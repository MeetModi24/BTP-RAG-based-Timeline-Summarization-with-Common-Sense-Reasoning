397. LETTER TO MERCHANT
PANCHGANI,
June 1, 1945
BHAI MERCHANT,
Do come if you really must see me. I cannot even so much
as lift up my head from work. Please do not tell me to go any-
where with you. One who is supposed to be ill can go even for
prayer and the like only with great difficulty.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
