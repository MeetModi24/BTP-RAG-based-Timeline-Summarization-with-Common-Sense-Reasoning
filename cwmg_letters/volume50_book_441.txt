441. LETTER TO BALIBEHN VORA
August 24, 1932
CHI. BALI,
I got your letter after a long time. Please overcome your
laziness in this matter. You must refuse to pay bills in Harilal's
name which you may receive. I hear that he is somewhere in
Ahmedabad. My hope of his reforming is becoming dim day by
day. All of us, he and we, are in God's hands.
The pain in my elbow is not much to worry about.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1516. Courtesy: Manubehn
Mashruwala
