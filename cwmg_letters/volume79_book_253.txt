253. LETTER TO P. C. GHOSH
SEVAGRAM,
February 20, 1945
CHI. PROFULLA,
I have your letter. Get well. I shall not send for Bibi A[m-
tul] S[alaam] unless you all relieve her. It is your duty not to
detain her longer than it is necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
79-10
