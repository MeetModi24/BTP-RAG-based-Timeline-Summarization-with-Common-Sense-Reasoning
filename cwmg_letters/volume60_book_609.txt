609. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
April 19, 1935
CHI. AMALA,
I am in good condition and order. Broke silence today.
The qualifications you have described are perfect. The shy
manner in which you have announced the giving up of khaddar
is characteristic. You are making progress with a vengeance.
Mahadev has gone to Ajmer. He and I return on 25th.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
