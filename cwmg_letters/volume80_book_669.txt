669. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
SIMLA,
July 3, 1945
BAPA,
I have your two letters before me-that of 28th and 29th.
You may call meetings of Harijan Sevak Sangh and the
Executive Committee of the Kasturba Trust in August as you
suggest. Leave out Mondays.
I approve the sum of Rs. 975 to meet the expenses of Mridula-
behn's office for three months.
Yours,
BAPU
SECRETARY,
KASTURBA SMARAK NIDHI
BAJAJWADI, WARDHA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
