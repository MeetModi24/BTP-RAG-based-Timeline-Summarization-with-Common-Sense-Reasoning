420. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
November 17, 1934
CHI. SHARMA,
You may give your answer tomorrow evening or even later.
Where is the figure '8' that you want to be changed to indicate
the month '11'? Whatever it is, you may take it as done.
I have added a little more to the letter to your brother and
you may do the rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
facing p. 118
