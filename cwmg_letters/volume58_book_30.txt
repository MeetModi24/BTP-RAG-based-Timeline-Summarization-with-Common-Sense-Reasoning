30. LETTER TO KSHIRODE CHANDRA MAITY
May 27, [1934]
If I feel confident that I could do useful work in the direction
indicated by you, I should set aside all other activities and
tour in Bengal for several months. But I have no present hope.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI KSHIRODE CHANDRA MAITY
BYBATTARHAT, MIDNAPUR
From a photostat: G.N. 5058
