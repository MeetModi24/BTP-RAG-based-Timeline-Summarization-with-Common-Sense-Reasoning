161. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
SHAHJAHANPUR,
November 11, 1929
CHI. GANGABEHN,
Nathji must have come now. He will give you consolation.
I can see that you are firm in your decision but not calm.
For the sake of health, take the necessary fruits as medicine.
It is not a sin to take care of the body, treating it as a temple, but
certainly it is a great sin to consider the body to be an abode of
pleasure and pamper it. But you have freed yourself from it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G.S. Gangabehnne, p. 29
