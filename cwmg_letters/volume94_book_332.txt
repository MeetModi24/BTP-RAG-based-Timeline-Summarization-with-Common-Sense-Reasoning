332. LETTER TO J. SIVASHANMUGAM PILLAI
SEVAGRAM,
January 29, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Some Harijan M.L.A.s in Bihar have asked Gandhiji for copies of the
correspondence you had with him. Gandhiji would like to know whether you have
any objection to the copies in question being supplied to them. It was necessary
to ask for this formal permission because one of your letters is marked personal.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
J. SIVASHANMUGAM PILLAI
MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
