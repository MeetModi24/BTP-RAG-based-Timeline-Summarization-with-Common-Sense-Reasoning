532. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
December 13, 1934
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I got your letter. Manilal's and . . .1's problem seems to
have been solved satisfactorily. I feel unhappy about Karna-
taka. But what can we say to a leader like Gangadharrao? Do
what you can.
I am now wholly occupied with the Village Industries As-
sociation. Rajaji is here. He came the day before yesterday
evening and wants to leave today. Jamnalal will go there in a
few days.
The rest you will know from Mahadev.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 145
