427. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
P. O. WARDHA,
May 4, 1936
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
Is there in your State a cool place having Gopnath or
some such name? I am sure you came across Vinoba's brother,
Balkrishna, at the Ashram. He is a learned man, well versed in
music, too, but his health has run down. He loves solitude and
may not be able to stand noise. I wish he could use a quiet
room in Gopnath or whatever the place is called. I have written
to Bhai Jivanlal to see if accommodation can be found for him
at Chorwad.
I am in Segaon and Mahadev [will be] in Bombay. I am
taking Sardar to Nandi Durg on the 8th. May I, therefore, ask
for a reply by wire if this reaches you in time?
I hope you are quite well.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
Please address the mail to Wardha.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5947. Also C.W. 3264. Courtesy:
Mahesh P. Pattani
