279. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
October 12, 1937
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I have your letter. I am trying to come to Calcutta, leaving
here on the 25th. You will then tell me all about the minis-
terial deeds in the Congress Provinces. I do hope that the sore
throat and cold were only temporary things, and that you were
able to stand the strain in the Punjab. The climate in the Fron-
tier must be very delightful. How I wish you would take things
easy for a time at least!
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1937. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
