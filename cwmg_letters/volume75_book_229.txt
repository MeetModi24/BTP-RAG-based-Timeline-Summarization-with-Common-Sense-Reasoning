229. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
BARDOLI,
December 13, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
This is just to tell you that you are never out of my mind.
I was immersed in writing work which is just finished. I hope you
are getting stronger and having greater inner peace.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
I am in excellent form.
From the original: C.W. 6491. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9886
