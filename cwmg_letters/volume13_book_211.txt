211. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
AHMEDABAD,
August 8, 1916
DEAR MR. POLAK,
This will introduce to you Mr. Kunverji V. Mehta whom I
have known as a public worker chiefly devoting his time to the
Patidar community. He is going to S. A. to collect [funds] for his
Society among the Patidars there. Please help him to enter the
Union.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. S. L. POLAK, ESQ.
Box 6522
JOHANNESBURG
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N.2664
