354. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
You will remember we discussed the question of students who
had suspended their studies. From everywhere complaints continue
to pour in. I think that it is highly necessary to take back these
students unconditionally. It is terrible to think that they alone
should be singled out for punishment.
Yours sincerely,
H. W. EMERSON, ESQ.
SIMLA
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
