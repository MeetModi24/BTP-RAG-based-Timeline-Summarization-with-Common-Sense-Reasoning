313. LETTER TO LILAVATI
October 16, 1930
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. I was glad to know that you have re-
mained calm. What was the cause of your fasting for three
days? Don't forget that you are only a child. A child should
not take upon itself the burden of the whole world. Really
speaking, all of us are children. There is only one grown up be-
ing, God. He shoulders the burden of all. Why, then, need we
worry? All that we should do is to serve Him as His slaves.
Blessings from
BAPU
