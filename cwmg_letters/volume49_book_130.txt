130. LETTER TO MANIBEHN N. PARIKH
February 12, 1932
CHI. MANIBEHN (PARIKH),
If the pain in the ear has increased so much, did you consult
anybody? I think you should get the ear examined once. If
Haribhai cannot understand the cause after examining it, you
should pay a visit to Bombay. There are some good ear special-
ists there. Dr. Jivraj will take you to one.
Blessings from
BAPU
