153. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 4, 1940
CHI. SHARMA,
I understand you. Everything is in the hands of God. We,
too, are all in His hands. He will make me do what He likes.
You have to concentrate on your work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
between pp. 290 and 291
