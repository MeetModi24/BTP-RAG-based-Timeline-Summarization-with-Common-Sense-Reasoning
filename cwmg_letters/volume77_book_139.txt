139. LETTER TO E. W. ARYANAYAKAM
JUHU,
May 27, 1944
MY DEAR ARYANAYAKAM,
I thank God and His instrument Asha that you are alive.
She has been your Savitri to deliver you from the jaws of death.
God bless you both. Don't do it again. Even a Savitri has her
limits. I see you are in harness again. I am wrong. You were in
harness even in your sick-bed.
Don't waste your time in answering this.
Love to you.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
