281. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
April 19, 1930
CHI. GANGABEHN,
Tell all the women that no one should suppose that I will not
call them here. I will call them here soon, but write to me
immediately and tell me who must remain there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8745. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
