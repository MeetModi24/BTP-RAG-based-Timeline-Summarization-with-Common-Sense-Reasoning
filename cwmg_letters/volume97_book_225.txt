225. LETTER TO KASHI GANDHI
January 31,1933
CHI. KASHI,
I have your letter. Do not at all worry about Prabhudas. He is indeed
Prabhu's das. 1 God will look after him better than we can. We may do the
best that we can.
He must now go to Almora as early as possible. It seems necessary that
he should again settle down there and be free from all worries. On the plains,
the days will get hotter by and by. All of us will look for a suitable girl for
him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 33071
