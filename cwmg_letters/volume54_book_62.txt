62. LETTER TO BALMUKUND
March 11, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I hope that your Conference will devote
itself to internal reforms, such as the observance of the rule of
hygiene, giving up of carrion-eating if it is prevalent amongst the
Harijans in the Punjab at all, total abstinence from drink, etc.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. BALMUKUND
GENERAL SECRETARY
THE BALMIK ACHHUT MANDAL
LAHORE CITY
