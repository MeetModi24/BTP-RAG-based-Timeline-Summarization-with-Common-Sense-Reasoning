445. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
October 20, 1932
DEAR MAJOR BHANDARI,
With reference to the telegram from Maulana Abul Kalam
Azad I send herewith the original and my reply to it. To be of
any use the reply should go promptly or not at all.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Government of Bombay, Home Department, I.G.P. File No. 9
