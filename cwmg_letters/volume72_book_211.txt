211. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
June 8, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have your letter. My advice is that at present you should
stay there. Let your health also improve. When you yourself
feel bored, you will of course run away from there. Both of you
are ill, i.e., in mind. Mental illness should not be neglected.
Blessings from
BAPU
MUNNALAL SHAH
VADILAL AROGYA BHAVAN
PANCHGANI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8535. Also C.W. 7094. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
