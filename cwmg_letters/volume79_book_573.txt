573. LETTER TO CHANDRAPRAKASH
ON THE TRAIN,
March 31, 1945
CHI. CHANDRAPRAKASH,
Take part in all the activities of the Ashram, including the
administration. Improve your knowledge of the language. If it is
necessary to go to Bhimavaram for health reasons, do go.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
