428. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Posh Vad 2 [January 12, 1925]
I remembered you and Anand during my Kathiawar tour.
Wished you were with me, and good health for Anand. I shall
await your letter on reaching the Ashram. Tell Anand that I
think of her daily.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 79
