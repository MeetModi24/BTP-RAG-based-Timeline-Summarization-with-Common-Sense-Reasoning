355. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 14, 1939
DEAR PRITHVI SINGH,
Your letter of 27th May is very good. It shows that you
have no difficulty in understanding the working of ahimsa. There
are many who swear by ahimsa [but] have not understood my
step.
I have not given up the attempt to secure possession of your
book.
Attempts continue to be made about the release of the politi-
cal prisoners. There are difficulties [in] the way. But for those
who only know the non-violent way, success is contained in the
effort itself. And prisoners who believe in non-violence regard
prison-life and the outside with indifference. They serve whether
in prison or outside.
I hope to go to the Frontier Province early July when both
Mahadev and Pyarelal will be with me. One of them will surely
see you.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 5635. Also C.W. 2946. Courtesy: Prithvi Singh
