576. LETTER TO G. ANNASAMY
SODEPUR,
January 15, 1946
BHAI ANNASAMY,
I got your English letter. I am afraid that much as I
would wish it, I shall not be able to visit Pondicherry. I hope
you will all be able to understand this much Hindi.
G. ANNASAMY
PONDICRERRY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
