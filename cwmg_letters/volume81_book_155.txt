155. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
August 8, 1945
BHAI KELKAR,
I have your letter. Would not your having to take quinine
indicate that there was something lacking in your treatment?
Do take Rs. 25 per month and improve your health. My
only point is that if you spend the entire sum of Rs. 25 on
food and improve your health, but then have a relapse and are
again obliged to take quinine, what would one have to say of
your treatment? I do not doubt your goodness but I have the
gravest doubts concerning your treatment. That I may be wrong
is a different thing.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KELKAR (NALWADI)
