92. LETTER TO PURSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
AHMEDABAD,
Vaishakh Vad 11 [June 8, 1915]
DEAR SHRI PURSHOTTAMDASJI 3 ,
I saw your letter on Revashankerbhai. Thanks for your
views.
You will know something about my activities if you go through
the accompanying draft [constitution of the Ashram]. I shall be
obliged to have your comments.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5700. Courtesy:
Ramanlal Saraiya
