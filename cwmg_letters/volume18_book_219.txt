219. LETTER TO ROBERTSON
[October 28, 1920]
DEAR MR. ROBERTSON 3 ,
I have just returned from my tour to find your kind note. I
shall certainly peruse the pamphlet sent by you and I shall have
pleasure in discussing the subject with you in December if I am
in Ahmedabad during that month.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 7308
