56. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
April 15, 1942
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your letter. You are right. Such severe criticism of
the British is not appropriate in a biography of Jamnalalji. This
criticism has a place but not in a book of this kind.
Jamnalal would never have entered politics if he had not
been impelled by moral principles. I feel that ill-will against
the British had little part in his life. Be that as it may, criti-
cism of the British appears improper in this article of yours and
will obstruct your future work.
Blessings from
BAPU
