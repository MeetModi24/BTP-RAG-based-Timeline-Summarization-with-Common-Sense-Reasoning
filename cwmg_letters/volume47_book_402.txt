402. LETTER TO J. B. KRIPALANI
AHMEDABAD,
August 22, 1931
MY DEAR PROFESSOR,
I forgot to write to you about one matter arising out of your
letter of yesterday, i.e., the egregious speech reported to have
been made by Pandit Sunderlal. I have never said to anybody
or at the All-India Congress Committee meeting that, even though
the Government carried out the terms of the Truce, I would not
go because of the Hindu-Muslim dissensions. But let us hope that
Pandit Sunderlal never said what is imputed to him. All the
same I am writing to him.
Yours sincerely,
PROF. KRIPALANI
GANDHI ASHRAM
MEERUT
