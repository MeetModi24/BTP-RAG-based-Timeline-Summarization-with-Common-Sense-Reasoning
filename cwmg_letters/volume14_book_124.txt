124. LETTER TO G. S. ARUNDALE
[SABARMATI,]
February 21, 1918
I have your letter. Just now I am immersed in one or two
difficult matters. Thoughts do not come to me for the asking.
I have to make my mind play upon a subject before I can write
anything readable on it. I can merely say that I shall bear your
letter in mind and try to send you something. The odds are that
I shall fail unless the things I am handling are finished before
the expected time.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
