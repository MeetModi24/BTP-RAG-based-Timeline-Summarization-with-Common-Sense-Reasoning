197. LETTER TO GANGADHARRAO DESHPANDE 2
[Before April 29, 1916)
Nothing but death can prevent me from going to Belgaum and
attending the Conference.
Majhi Jivan Katha
1 Vide Bhagvad Gita, III.12.
2 A political worker of Karnatak, popularly known as "Lion of Karnatak"
3 Gandhiji arrived in Belgaum on April 29 to attend the Bombay Provincial
Conference.
4 Gandhiji had accepted the invitation to attend the Conference but later
on there were rumours that he might not attend it. On an enquiry from the
addressee, Gandhiji wrote this reply.
