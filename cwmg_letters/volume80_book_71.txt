71. LETTER TO MUHAMMAD AHMAD SAID KHAN
Personal
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 30, 1945
DEAR NAWAB SAHEB1,
I cannot do better than send the accompanying to you. If
the facts are correctly set forth, they are terrible.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
THE NAWAB SAHEB OF CHHATARI
HYDERABAD DECCAN
From a copy ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
