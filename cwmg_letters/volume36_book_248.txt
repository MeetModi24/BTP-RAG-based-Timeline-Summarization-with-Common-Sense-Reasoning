248. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
[Before April 10, 1928]
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I got your letters. I do not think that I have missed any
mail recently. It is good that Sushila is progressing in her studies,
but I am worried about her health. I should like her to make
every effort to acquire good health.
Nimu is here at present. Ramdas is on a tour hawking khadi.
Afterwards he will go to Jamnadas's school. Both of them will
work there. Devdas has gone to Delhi.
A proposal is being discussed about my going to Europe. I
cannot make up my mind. The matter will be decided within a
week.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Tell Pragji that I got his letter. I have no time to write to
him a separate letter. It appears that the cases of both have been
decided now.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4722
