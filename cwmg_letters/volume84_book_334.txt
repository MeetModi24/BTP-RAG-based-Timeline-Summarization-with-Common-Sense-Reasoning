334. LETTER TO NARDEV SHASTRI
MUSSOORIE,
June 2, 1946
BHAI NARDEV SHASTRI,
I was glad to have your letter. I am sorry to know that
your health is poor. I am likely to be here for six days. Can
you come so soon? Why take the trouble?
Blessings from
BAPU
