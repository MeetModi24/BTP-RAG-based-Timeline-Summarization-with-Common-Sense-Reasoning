572. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
Saturday [On or after April 16, 1927]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
Herewith a letter from Bhai Damodardas. It is a good one.
Think and decide about the children now.
I am keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8830. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya.
Also G.N. 11386
