48. LETTER TO NORALMAL PHULOMAL
November 6, 1945
BHAI NORALMAL,
I have your letter. I was distressed to hear about your
health. May God keep you. Get well soon. Write to me either
in Urdu or Devanagari. Sindhi and Urdu scripts are almost
the same. Only the languages are a little different. Learn
Hindustani after you get well. I have the Rs. 20 sent by you.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI NORALMAL PHULOMAL
SUB-DIVISIONAL CLERK, P. W. D.
NARA SICHAI, SUKKUR
SINDH
