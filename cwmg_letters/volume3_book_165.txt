165. LETTER TO COLONIAL SECRETARY, NATAL
14 MERCURY LANE,
DURBAN,
October 18, 1901
TO
THE HONOURABLE THE COLONIAL SECRETARY
PIETERMARITZBURG
SIR,
I had the honour on behalf of the representative Indians to
telegraph this evening as follows :
Indian community Durban wish present respectful address to Lord
Milner. Will His Lordship accept same?
In anticipation of His Excellency's consent, I am authorized
to submit for His Excellency's approval copy of the proposed humble
address .
I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
M. K. GANDHI
Pietermaritzburg Archives: C.S.O. 9038/1901
