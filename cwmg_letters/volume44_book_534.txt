534. LETTER TO SHANTA
December 14, 1930
CHI. SHANTA,
Your letter. Looking after Jiji is also a kind of national
service. It should be performed without attachment. Jiji needs
to be served and there is nobody else who can do it. We have
no self-interest in this service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5268
