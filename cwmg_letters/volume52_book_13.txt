13. LETTER TO S. D. SATAVLEKAR
November 17, 1932
BHAI SATAVLEKAR,
You have sent me a most encouraging reply. But I hope
you did not think that I was unaware of your past activities in
regard to the removal of untouchability. Earlier I had read your
article too. I only wanted to ascertain what your contribution
is to be now in this powerful movement. I am immensely reas-
sured now on this count. Please convey my deepest thanks to
Shrimant Maharaj and Rani Saheba. I shall at the right time
make good use of your beautiful account of the work going on
there.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SHRI S. D. SATAVLEKAR
SVADHYAYA MANDAL
AUNDH
(DIST. SATARA)
From Hindi : C.W. 4769. Courtesy: S. D. Satavlekar
