530. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 10, 1926
MY DEAR KRISHNADAS,
It was from Guruji that I learnt something about you. How
is it that you have not kept me in touch with you for some
time now? Let me know all about your health.
Here at the present moment about 30 people are bed-ridden
with malaria. Shanker, Kakasaheb's son, has a mild attack of
typhoid and Kishorelal is down with his old friend asthma. Dev-
das is still at Mussoorie.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19721
