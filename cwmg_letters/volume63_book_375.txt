375. LETTER TO G. S. N. ACHARYA
[Before September 21, 1936]
I never knew that there was a movement in Andhra Desa
against Hindi prachar . But I would resist any attempt to dis-
place the rich vernaculars of the different provinces. Hindi is
not intended to take their place, but it is intended to supplement
them as a medium of inter-provincial intercourse. Therefore, Hindi
propaganda should not only not interfere with the progress of
vernaculars, but it must enrich them.
The Hindu, 22-9-1936
