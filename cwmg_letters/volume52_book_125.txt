125. LETTER TO VASANTRAM SHASTRI
November 29, 1932
Somebody sent me a copy of your pamphlet. I went through
parts of it. I never thought even in my dreams that you could
write and tell such lies. What you are doing will not harm me,
but don't you think it harms Vaishnava dharma?
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 291
