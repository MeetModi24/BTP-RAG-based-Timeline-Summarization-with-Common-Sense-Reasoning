151. LETTER TO ISHWARLAL JOSHI
BADAYUN,
November 9, 1929
CHI. ISHWARLAL,
As long as you were at the Ashram it was not necessary for
you to write to me except when there was some reason. But
now it is necessary that you should. You may write to me when-
ever you have any difficulty.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9280. Courtesy: Ishwarlal Joshi
