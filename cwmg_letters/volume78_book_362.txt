362. LETTER TO RISHABHDAS RANKA
SEVAGRAM,
November 7, 1944
CHI. RISHABHDAS,
I have heard Guneji's reputation. He should send me the
names of the medicines and their properties. I shall make use
of the information. It would be best if he prepares a booklet for
me. I shall have it printed if I like it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
