718. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA (C. P.),
April 18, 1941
MY DEAR SINGER,
You are a finished diplomat. I am sending a wire of con-
dolences. Herewith love letter for Lilamani. Sarup coming here
on Sunday.
Hot winds blowing here all day. Hope you are better off.
Love to you all.
SPINNER
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
