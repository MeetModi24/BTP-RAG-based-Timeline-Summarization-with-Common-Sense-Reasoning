199. LETTER TO LAKSHMI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
[1926]
CHI. LAKSHMI,
This time, it may be said, your letter was late. Your hand-
writing has improved. There should not be two kinds of writing
in one letter. There is nothing to worry if you are inclined to
play. Children are bound to be playful. But they should have
the same heart for work too. Those children whose mind is al-
ways engrossed in play can't help lying some time or other. Now
you will be entering your 13th year. It is not so young an age.
You should get from here new clothes if those you are using
wear out. If they can be stitched there, that is, if Danibehn knows
how to sew, khadi can be sent from here; but if she is unable
to do so, the clothes can be made here and sent to you. Take
a vow to do some good deed on your birthday.
Rukhi and Anandi are ill. Both have fever. I hope they will
be all right in two or three days.
