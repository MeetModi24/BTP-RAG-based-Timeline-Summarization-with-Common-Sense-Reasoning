396. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
August 23, 1938
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have entrusted to Mahadev the task of writing to you or
sending anything to you. I assume that you will be ready to
start as soon as you get the call. If there is any change in the
programme inform Mahadev.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10592
