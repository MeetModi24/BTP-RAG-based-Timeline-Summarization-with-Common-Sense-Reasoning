39. LETTER TO E. C. GIBSON
RAJKOT,
March 7, 1939
DEAR MR. GIBSON,
I broke the fast, gave a message to the Press and now send
you my thanks for your warm letter. Let me hope this contact
is a foundation for lasting friendship.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: C.W. 10143. Courtesy: D. B. Kalelkar
