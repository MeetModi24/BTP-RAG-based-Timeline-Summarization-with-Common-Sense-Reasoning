35. LETTER TO L. N. HARDAS
March 10, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I am sorry that being preoccupied I have not been able to
reach your letter of the 10th February earlier than now. Even if
I had reached your letter earlier I could not have sent you any-
thing, but you are at liberty to copy anything you like from my
writings in the Harijan.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. L. N. HARDAS
C/O THE "MAHARATTA"
KAMPTEE (C.P.)
From a microfilm : S.N. 20536
