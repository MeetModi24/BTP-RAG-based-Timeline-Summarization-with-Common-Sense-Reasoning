358. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
CHI. NARANDAS,
I hope the atmosphere there will have improved by now, but even if it
has worsened, you need not worry over it. When a river is in flood, its water
is muddier than at any other time, but when the flood has subsided, it becomes
clearer than it was before. You need not lose courage. Express your own views
freely. When an occasion offers itself, invite someone. Rajendra Babu or Satis
Babu may come. Invite Kaka once a fortnight. It is desirable that you should
come into more intimate contact with him. You should not undervalue his
knowledge. Believe it for a certainty that his heart is straightforward, the
mind is pure and he is striving to make it purer still. But the mind is weak
because he woke up rather late. He cannot immediately put into practice what
