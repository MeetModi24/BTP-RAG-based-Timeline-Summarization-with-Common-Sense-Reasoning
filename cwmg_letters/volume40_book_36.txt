36. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
Monday [February 25, 1929]
CHI. MIRA,
I daily receive your instructive letters. Your work bears the
promise of a big future. It is good that you are working along
the line of least resistance. The introduction of Yogendrababu's
wife makes your miniature ashram a good model to work upon.
I leave here on 1st March and go through Delhi. Probably
Patna is on the way. The Delhi route is the quickest and the
cheapest. I reach Calcutta Sunday night, 3rd March. 4th March
is the silence day. I leave Calcutta Tuesday morning, 5th March.
Balkrishna came here yesterday. He will attend to the weaving
department. Fresh effort is being made to put the Gita reading
on a firm footing.
Chhotelal is still an uncertain factor but he is slaving away
as usual. Mahadev will accompany me.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5347. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9403
