271. LETTER TO LAKSHMI D. DAFDA
October 2, 1932
CHI. LAKSHMI,
What did you do in Bombay? How do you spend the day
now? Did I write and tell you that my fast has increased your
responsibility? I hope you understand how that is so.
BAPU
