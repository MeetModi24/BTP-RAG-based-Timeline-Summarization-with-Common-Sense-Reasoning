325. LETTER TO N. P. COWIE
[After June 9, 1919]
DEAR MR. COWIE,
In continuation of my letter regarding Mr. Manilal Vyas, I
learnt from him that he has already forwarded his petition regard-
ing his case. I do hope that it will receive H.E.'s early and favour-
able consideration.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat : S.N. 6655
