341. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
August 7, 1932
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
I certainly compliment you on your good gesture if you have
given the yarn willingly. I can understand that you would like
to go to Jetpur if Valji has to stay there for some time to come.
The position will be clearer after he is released. See that Manu
improves his handwriting.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7434. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
