318. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
May 18, 1940
CHI. NIMU,
I got your letter. Ba will not go there. It is hot, no doubt, but she keeps
quite well. It will be good if you do well there and suffer from no shortages.
R[aj]kumari has gone to Simla. Amtussalaam is here. There are a number of
1 Rajkumari Amrit Kaur

visitors coming and going. Sharda is still here. Her boy is growing fast. His
eczema persists and that is rather vexing. Radha is here these days.
Blessings from
BAPU
