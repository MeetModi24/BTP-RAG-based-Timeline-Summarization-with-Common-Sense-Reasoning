75. LETTER TO K. L. RALLIA RAM
August 29, 1944
BHAI RALLIA RAMJI,
Although my reply is delayed, this much I can say with cer-
tainty that neither of us will take any step to the detriment of
the interests of others.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
REV. K. L . RALLIA RAM
OFFICE OF THE HONORARY SECRETARY
C/O Y. M. C. A., LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
