368. LETTER TO JAYAKUNWAR M. DOCTOR
SEVAGRAM,
March 8, 1945
CHI. JEKI,
I have written to him about the jewellery. 1 Try to find
out why he gave up his job. It will not take him long to find
another. He is clever.
Blessings from
BAPU
JEKIBEHN
35 JUHU LANE
ANDHERI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
