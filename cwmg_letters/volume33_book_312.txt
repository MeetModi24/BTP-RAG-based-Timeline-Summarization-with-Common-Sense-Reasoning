312. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
NANDI,
May 8, 1927
It is all to the good that you propose to do without him,
provided it does not affect your health.
Let us thank God for all
this improvement. But do not hesitate to write to me when you
need any help.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 91
