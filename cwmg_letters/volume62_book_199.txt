199. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
WARDHA,
December 11, 1935
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I am writing to you after many days. I don't know if I am
disobeying doctors' instructions in doing so. Jamnalalji has got
alarmed, but I don't want you to be so. Come only when you
would ordinarily have done. I am all right. The strings of
your life, mine and everybody else's, are held by Mira's Lord.
Let Him pull them as He wills. Does He ever let anybody have
his way? Pyarelal is fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 193
