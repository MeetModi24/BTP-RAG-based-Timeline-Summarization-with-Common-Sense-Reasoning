44. LETTER TO ATMA S. KAMALANI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
September 30, 1933
MY DEAR KAMALANI,
I have your letter.
You have not given me any alternative to fasts. But you need
not be agitated over my prospective fasts. They are not lightly
undertaken and for the most part they are inspired and there-
fore I may not be held responsible for them. The inspiration may
be a hallucination. Whilst the hallucination lasts, reason is sus-
pended and all appeal to it becomes vain. Do you not see this
insurmountable difficulty in the way of myself or friends dissuad-
ing me from a fast so undertaken?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. ATMA S. KAMALANI
JT. HON. SECRETARY
'FRIENDS OF INDIA'
46 LANCASTER GATE
LONDON W. 2
From a photostat: G.N. 1531
