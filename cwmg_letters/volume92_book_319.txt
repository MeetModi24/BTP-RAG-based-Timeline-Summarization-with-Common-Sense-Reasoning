319. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
KARADI,
April 30, 1930
BHAI HARIBHAUJI,
Bapu has received your letter. He has asked me to write that Kashinathji
may well join you if Ramnarayanji can conduct the Hindi Navajivan by
himself. So please write to him. It is very likely that Ramnarayanji may
have to share some work of Young India and Navajivan on account of Maha-
devbhai's arrest. Maybe he will be unable to manage all the work by himself.
Respects from
KANTI
