305. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
DELHI,
February 25, 1931
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
Your letter and a portion of the translated Gita-praveshika
have arrived. I shall read the translation when I have time. I have
had some news from Shankerlal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1686
