568. LETTER TO GUNOTTAM HUTHEESING
October 5, 1945
CHI. RAJA,
Why do you write in English? In any case I am of no use
so far as elections are concerned.
I just do not know anything
about them. Why should I be angry? Of course, I do expect
that "one may give up one's life, not one's word". Effort will
help to make this a habit.
RAJA HUTHEESING
20 CARMICHAEL ROAD, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
