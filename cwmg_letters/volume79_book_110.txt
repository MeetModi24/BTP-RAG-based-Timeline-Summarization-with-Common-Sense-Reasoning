110. LETTER TO WILLIAM Q. LASH
SEVAGRAM,
January 25, 1945
MY DEAR FATHER LASH,
I am so glad you came. Please tell all our English friends
that we all do our duty regardless of result. Has not an English
divine said that "duty will be merit when debt becomes a dona-
tion"? Non-violence, translated 'love', is the supreme law for
human beings. It knows no exception. I have tried all these
years to live by that law and hope to die in that state.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 41
