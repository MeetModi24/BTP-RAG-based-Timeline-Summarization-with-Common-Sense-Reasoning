226. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
March 26, 1933
CHI. JANAKIMAIYA,
What is this? You do not even reply to my letter. Are you so
much afraid of me ? If you feel unhappy about giving money for
Harijans, tell me so. Is it that you find the purse-strings easy to
loosen when you wish to send me oranges, but too tight when you
have to give money for Harijan work?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Jamnalal went to Bombay yesterday. He will be examined
there by Dr. Modi. His health is perfectly all right. He has gone
only for your and his own satisfaction.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2911
