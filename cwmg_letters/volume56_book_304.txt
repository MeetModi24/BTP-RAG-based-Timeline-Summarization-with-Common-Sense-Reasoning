304. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
November 26, 1933
CHI. KAKA,
I never get time to write a sufficiently long letter to you.
I was shocked to read . . . ' S1 letter. Is he really mad? I
hesitate to believe that the lady yielded to force, though it is
possible that she was unwilling. Even if, however, it was a
case of rape, she ought not to resort to abortion. She should
rear the child with love. However, she must live away from her
husband.
*
*
*2
What happened about your teeth!
Do you still suffer from backache?
I received the three books on astronomy.
I suppose Chandrashankar writes to you and gives you other
news.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9475. Courtesy: D. B. Kalelkar
