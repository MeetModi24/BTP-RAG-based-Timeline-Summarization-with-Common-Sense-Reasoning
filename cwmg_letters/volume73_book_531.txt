531. LETTER TO CHAMANLAL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 22, 1941
DEAR CHAMANLAL,
In spite of my best effort, I have not been able to read your
book. But a cursory glance shows that some of your statements
are too startling to be true. If they are true, you have made no
mean contribution to research work. If your thesis is accepted by
Americans, there must be established a cultural bond between
America and India.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
