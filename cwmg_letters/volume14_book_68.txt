68. LETTER TO A PUBLIC WORKER
[After January 11, 1918]
I have your letter and telegram. I was fully reassured by
them. Do not back out of the task you have undertaken. In
fact you don't need me or anyone else. Those who are unable to
pay the land revenue will remain so, whether or no the Govern-
ment admits their inability. Why should they pay it, then? This
is all you have to explain to the people. Even if only one person
remains firm, he will have won the battle. From this, we shall
be able to raise a new crop. Go ahead fearlessly.
[From Gujarati]
Kheda Satyagraha
