41. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 4, 1940
CHI. MANI,
The figures you have sent are excellent.
You had better
spend the time in spinning rather than in writing to me.
Ask Father whether I should send that amount to him or
to Prithvi Singh direct. How is his health?
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MANIBEHN
C/O SARDAR PATEL
68 MARINE DRIVE
BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 126
