155. LETTER TO CHANDAN S. KALELKAR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 16, 1941
CHI. CHANDAN 1 ,
I have your letter. All of us liked it. May you both be happy
and do as much service as you can. I am not writing separately to
[Satish]. Take this as addressed to both of you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6267
