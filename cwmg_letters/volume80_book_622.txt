622. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
SIMLA,
June 26, 1945
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have your letter of the 12th. Whatever you are doing,
you are doing with deliberation. I shall read the circular when
it comes. I hope Satis Babu is well-he should be.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
