26. LETTER TO JAMNA GANDHI
[April 26, 1940]
CHI. JAMNA,
I have your letter. We should use the strength God has given
us and be content. Kanaiya's work is progressing very well. Now
he has decided to have a dark-room.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8573. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
