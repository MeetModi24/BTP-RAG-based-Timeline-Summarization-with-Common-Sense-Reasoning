219. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SODEPUR,
April 29, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I hope you do not feel neglected. I have been sending you
wires which you must have received.
I have carefully gone through your notes of the interview
with G. It is a good account. We must now allow things to
run their course. You should not mind my being discredited or
misunderstood. That is no novel experience for me.
Since sending you the telegram I find that my route may
be changed. And I see that you could get a direct train to
Bettiah from Lucknow. It is worth considering. I may send
you another wire.
Sushila returns tonight. She must tell you all about my
health.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3913. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7222
