401. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
February 23, 1937
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
You must have got my letter of yesterday and your fever
must have abated by now. No need to get panicky. Patience
will make everything come right. Yes, the bhajan is certainly
worth pondering over. A mosquito-net should be used if the
mosquitoes are proving bothersome.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1898
