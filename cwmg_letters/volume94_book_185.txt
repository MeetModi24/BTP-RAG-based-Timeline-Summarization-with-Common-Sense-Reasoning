185. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 24, 1942
CHI. LILA,
I had been anxiously awaiting your letter. Something somewhere
will be arranged. Do not worry. You must be attending college regularly.
If a separate room is the only remedy, try that. Do not lose courage.
Vasumati has arrived. The Ashram is full. Sushila has gone to Delhi
today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
