374. LETTER TO T. B. SAPRU
SEVAGRAM,
March 9, 1945
DEAR DR. SAPRU,
P. is in Simla today, probably with Rajkumari. This letter
of greetings will be presented to you by Shri Narahari Parikh,
one of the oldest Ashramites. You may trust him with any mes-
sage you like. I hope you are keeping fit.
Please do not forget your promise about Hindustani, though
you said you could not become a member.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhi-Sapru Papers. Courtesy: National Library, Calcutta. Also G.N. 7569
