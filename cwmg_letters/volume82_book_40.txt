40. LETTER TO TARA MODAK
November 6, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. Keep on sending Shikshan Patrika. If
you have anything to ask, by all means do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
TARABEHN MODAK
996 HINDU COLONY
DADAR, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
