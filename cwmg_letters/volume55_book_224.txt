224. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
June 9, 1933
MY DEAR AMALA,
I have your letters. Do become steady. It is not good to
be constantly thinking of being with me. On the contrary you
will be constantly thinking of the duty in front of you.
Do be-
come a wise woman of 35 instead of being a child of 5 years.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library. Also
from a microfilm: S.N. 19094
