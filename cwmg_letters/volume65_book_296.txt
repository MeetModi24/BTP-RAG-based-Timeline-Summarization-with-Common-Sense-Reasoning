296. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
June 4, 1937
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I had your letter. In this condition what can you do by
staying there1? How much can you do even for the spread of the
charkha? One wonders whether your living there is the right
thing from the point of view of service. Think carefully about
this and let me know.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6550
