561. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Asvina Sud 15,1982 [October 21,1926]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I have your letter. This is not the time to start an Ashram
such as you have described. The atmosphere is very foul. Workers
have neither intelligence nor character.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRIJUT GHANSHYAMDAS BIRLA
C/O HINDU SABHA
GORAKHPUR
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6137. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
