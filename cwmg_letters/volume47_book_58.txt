58. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
BORSAD,
June 24, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
Here is a copy of a precis of an important case in Meerut.
The precis is so admirably prepared that it makes easy reading.
I do suggest that it is a terrible thing that a cultured man like
Syt. Shital Prasad Tayal should not be reinstated. I do hope that
you will intervene in the matter. You will observe that the in-
cumbent alleged to be permanent did not take up the post and
