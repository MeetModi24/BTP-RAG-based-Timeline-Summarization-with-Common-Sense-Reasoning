279. LETTER TO LAKSHMIBEHN KHARE
BARDOLI,
May 26, 1931
CHI. LAKSHMIBEHN,
I suppose Rambhau has returned there. Don't get angry with
him or beat him. Let him follow his own inclinations. If he does
not like to study, he may do some manual work or learn some
craft. There will be no harm either if he concentrates on cultivat-
ing his gift for music. Try to discover the bent of his mind and
let him do what he chooses. No one else will be able to look
after him or reform him better than you can. Be patient with
him. Let me know what he decides to do.
Blessings from
BAPU
