401. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN VALJI DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 8, 1930
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
I have had no letter from you recently. I am writing this be-
cause Valji has gone back to the temple. Do you feel nervous?
We shall win true swaraj only through the sacrifices of men of his
sincerity and purity. We should, therefore, rejoice at his self-sacri-
fice, and not at all feel unhappy. God protects everyone. Write
to me about all your circumstances.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7409. Courtesy: V. G. Desai
