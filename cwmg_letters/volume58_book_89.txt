89. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
BOMBAY1,
June 14, 1934
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I got both your letters.
Your convenience and wish will
be my wish. I understand your problems.
Leave all the ar-
rangements to the Reception Committee and be content with
what you may have to do to facilitate its work. This will not
show that your love for me has diminished.
One is not always
in a position to demonstrate one's love.
More when we meet.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5933. Also C.W. 3249. Courtesy:
Mahesh P. Pattani
