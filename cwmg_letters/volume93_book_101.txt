101. LETTER TO NEKI RAM SHARMA
NANDI DURG,
May 17, 1936
BHAI NEKI RAM SHARMA,
Please convey my blessings to Chi. Chandrakala and Chi.
Omprakash. May their married life be happy and devoted to
service.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI NEKI RAM SHARMA
BHIWANI
(PUNJAB)
From the Hindi original: Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
