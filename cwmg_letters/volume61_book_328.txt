328. LETTER TO S. SRINIVASA IYENGAR
July 4, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I was glad to have your letter. I do hope that you will not
leave Kodaikanal till you are thoroughly restored and enjoying
your normal health.
I sent my blessings through Ambujam two days ago, as I
learnt through her the date of the forthcoming marriage. I have
written today to Mrs. Iyengar as also to Krishnaswami. Thank you
for your good wishes.
Yes, I am keeping quite well and quite cheerful. Both I
count as God's gifts. a
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. S. SRINIVASA IYENGAR
"SRINIKETAN"
KODAIKANAL
From a photostat: C.W. 10752. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
