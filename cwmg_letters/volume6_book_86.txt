86. LETTER TO YUK LIN LEW
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 2, 1906
DEAR MR. LEW,
I hope you have received the draft of the letter for the Chinese
Minister to send to the Foreign Secretary.
Yours truly,
HIS EXCELLENCY YUK LIN LEW
CHINESE CONSUL-GENERAL IN THE TRANSVAAL
RICHMOND HOUSE
49, PORTLAND PLACE, W.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4468
