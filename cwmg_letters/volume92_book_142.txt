142. LETTER TO ASHRAM CHILDREN
AGRA,
September 16, 1929
BIRDS OF THE BAL MANDIR,
If you do not dictate any letters to me, why should I go on
writing to you? You should dictate to respected Gangabehn what-
ever kind of letter you may like for me. There are beautiful
buildings here in Agra. I remember you when I look at those
buildings. Learn from your teacher where Agra is and all about
its history.
From a copy of Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32578/16
