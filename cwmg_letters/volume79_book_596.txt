596. LETTER TO LADORANI ZUTSHI
BOMBAY,
April 4, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
Haven't I told you I have not interfered in this work? I
wouldn't know anything about it.
M. K. GANDHI
LADORANI ZUTSHI
C/O MANMOHINI SANYAL
ALCAZER, 3RD FLOOR
GAMDEVI ROAD, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
