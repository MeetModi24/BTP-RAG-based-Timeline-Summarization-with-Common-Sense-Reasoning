334. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
March 3, 1945
You or Sushila should have said it-either to yourselves or
aloud-and then calmed down, because you two and others like
you are and should be more to each other than brother and
sister. I myself should have supplied the quotation marks. But
surely I cannot attend to all that. The best thing would be for
you to come-either at 5.30 or after 3.30-and remove them.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8640
