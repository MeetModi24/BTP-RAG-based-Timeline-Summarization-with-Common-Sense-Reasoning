262. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
July 22, 1938
CHI. LILA,
If you think that by not writing to me you save my time,
you are making a mistake. I would be satisfied if you wrote once
in a while. If it is necessary for you to take tea for the bowels
to move, you may take just enough for that purpose as medicine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9590. Also C.W. 6562. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
