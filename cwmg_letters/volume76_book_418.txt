418. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA BHAVE
BOMBAY,
August 6, 1942
CHI. BALKRISHNA,
I got your postcard. There is still time to rope you in.
Have patience. When the time comes, you will certainly get
the call some way or another. Your sadhana lies in improving
your health, for the sake of this yajna at any rate. But that also
without worrying. We may try every remedy we know, and
remain unconcerned with the result. I wrote out this letter in
the early morning, having got a few moments to spare.
I hope everybody is well there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 808. Courtesy: Balkrishna Bhave
