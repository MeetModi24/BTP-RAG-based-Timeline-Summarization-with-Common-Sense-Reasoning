248. LETTER TO PREMNATH BAZAZ
As AT SABARMATI,
May 24, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. What young men can do is to make up
their minds to marry only widows and if they cannot secure
widows in the Pandit caste, they should travel outside if only by
way of satyagraha. But if they are desirous of restricting them-
selves to their caste they should reason with the caste elders and
give them notice that they would go outside the caste if they did
not merely permit but encourage widows to remarry.
Yours sincerely,
PT. PREM NATH BAZAZ
CHONDHPORA
SRINAGAR (KASHMIR)
From a photostat: S.N. 17129
