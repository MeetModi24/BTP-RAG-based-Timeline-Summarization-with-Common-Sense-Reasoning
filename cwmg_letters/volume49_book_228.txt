228. LETTER TO MAHENDRA V. DESAI
March 13, 1932
CHI. MANU,
Your description of Ajmer is good. But the handwriting is
bad. Try and make it better in your future letters. I will look
forward to descriptions of Jaipur and other places. Ask Dudhi-
behn to write to me. What is your weight?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7422. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
