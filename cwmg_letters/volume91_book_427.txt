427. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Monday [December 14, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your telegram. On Friday itself I had sent a telegram
to Chi. Devdas asking him to go to you. As yet I have not
received his reply. I shall make some other arrangement if he
cannot go.
I must have news about your health every day. Even
here the weather is very lovely and at present there is a good
deal of company too. Jamnalalji even suggested that you should
be asked to come here. But right now you have to see to things
there.
I am writing to Taramati. I shall send the letter to your
address, even though she is not staying there.
Do not put yourself to any inconvenience there. Let me know
what facilities you are having. If there is need to send a tele-
gram do not hesitate. If Devdas has not gone there and if you
need somebody urgently, send me a telegram. In that case,
Mahadev will go.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Telegrams may reach early if they are sent to Wardha-
ganj. I received your letter only today at 10 o'clock.
