481. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
February 23, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Your intention was not honest, nor was it correct. All the same,
it is good that you did not come, since you also needed sleep.
Send the following telegram to Gosibehn.
"Health good. Hope Jalbhai makes steady progress. Love.
Bapu."
Who sent the wire to A. P. saying that I had not seen the
statement? But it does not matter now.
The rest when you come in the evening.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Sushila brought a car when I had walked a little distance
beyond the level crossing, and I got in.
