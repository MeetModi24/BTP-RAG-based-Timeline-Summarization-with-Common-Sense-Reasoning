639. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
YERAVDA,
January 16, 1933
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I shall expect you on Friday at noon. Bring along the
Court decision if you have no need of it. I am cognizant of all
that you write about Malabar; I have been there three or four
times. The ground has also been well prepared. Work has been
going on since my visit in 1925 in connection with the Vykom
[Satyagraha]. Yet, much remains to be done. But we will talk
more about it when you come. Do bring along Taramati and
the children.
If you have preserved my letters which you received in the
jail bring along with you such of them as have passages scored
out or otherwise tampered with by the authorities.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
21 MINT ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
