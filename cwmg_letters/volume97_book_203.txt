203. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
April 24, 1932
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I would not be able to think anything about Dr. Ambedkar from here. It
is enough if we can retain our right in the land. If anything has to be done,
please do it yourself.
The news about Prabhudas is good. He gets milk. His health is good. He
has gained two pounds.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32866
