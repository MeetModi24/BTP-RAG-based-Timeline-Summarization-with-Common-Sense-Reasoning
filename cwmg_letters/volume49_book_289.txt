289. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI M. PATEL
March 25, 1932
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I got your letter. This time we are really being tested. We
should not mind it. Write to me from time to time.
I suppose you know that Mahadev has been brought here.
We three are practically enjoying ourselves.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8994
