441. LETTER TO A. KALESHWARA RAO
SEVAGRAM,
November 20, 1944
DEAR KALESHWARA RAO,
I see that I cannot bear the burden you want me to shoul-
der about Rajagopalarao. You should have no difficulty in
bearing it.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI KALESHWARA RAO
NANDIGRAM
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
