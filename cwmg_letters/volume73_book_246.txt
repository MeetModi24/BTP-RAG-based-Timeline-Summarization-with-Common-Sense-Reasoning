246. LETTER TO S. C. CHATTERJEE
SEVAGRAM,
December 5, 1940
DEAR SIR,
The contents of the enclosed speak for themselves. I send you
a copy in order that you might correct the bare statement of facts
regarding your circular referred to in the letter. You will now
understand why I have prevented publication of my letter until I
have heard from you. I am anxious not to be instrumental in
doing an unconscious injustice to you.
From a photostat: C.W. 10504. Courtesy: India Office Library
