268. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
August 24, 1945
BHAI KELKAR,
Yours of the 22nd arrived today. About the bicycle it is
difficult. If some friend sends you one you may take it. You
can charge to the Ashram the expense you have mentioned on
the two patients. The decision you have taken to go to Akola
now is right.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. KELKAR
C/O SHRI VINOBA
NALWADI, WARDHA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
