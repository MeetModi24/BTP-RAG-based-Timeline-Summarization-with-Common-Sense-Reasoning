104. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
June 23, 1936
BHAI RAJENDRA BABU,
I delayed replying to your letter till Ceresole arrived.
He
tells me that all the difficulties have been resolved. That is why I
did not hurry. If there are still some loose ends you can tell me
on coming here. I shall leave here on the 27th. I am now using
my left hand, having overworked the right one.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 9875. Courtesy: Rajendra Prasad
