372. LETTER TO HARGOVIND GUPTA
NEW DELHI,
September 11, 1946
BHAI HARGOVIND GUPTA,
I had and still have a great regard for Ganesh Shankarji.
Since Maithilisharanji is associated with the work it will be a
literary accomplishment. But the true memorial to him will be
if we generate in us a spirit of self-sacrifice even to the extent
of laying down our lives.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HARGOVIND GUPTA
GANESH SHANKAR HRIDAYATIRTH
CHIRGAON (JHANSI)
