376. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
BANGALORE,
August 28, 1927
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I have no time today to write myself. I am dictating this
letter while I am having my meal. After I sent the cable to
you, I have not missed a single mail. Mr. Andrews is here. I
shall write more by the next mail.
Sushila's health must improve.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4738
