223. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
May 20, 1937
CHI. LILA,
You may be 30 years old but to me you are still a child
and will remain so. If you have patience everything will be
all right. Everything would be all right if only you settled down.
Nothing else is needed. Do you understand that one who wants
to become orderly must have no pride in oneself? The orderli-
ness that I want is both external and internal.
I have written about not sending The Times [if India]. I
understand regarding your silence.
Radhakrishna ought not to return the ghee. He may buy
some more, but should not return what he has.
Improve your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9361. Also C.W. 6636. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
