468. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
August 10, 1935
CHI. MANUDI,
I got your letter after I had finished writing the letters. I had
already got the news about Ba's illness yesterday and, therefore,
wrote today. You must be fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1546. Courtesy: Manubehn
S. Mashruwala
