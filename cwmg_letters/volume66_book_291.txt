291. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
October 15, 1937
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have made use of your figures. You are doing admirable
service to the cause. But remember you have to advance a great
deal further. Think over the question whether real intellectual
development can be brought about through crafts.
Enclosed is a letter from Kumi. What do you think about
it? If you cannot afford to keep her, certainly don't. She must
not be an obstacle in your efforts for progress. But it seems that
I shall have to meet her expenses in any case.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8540.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
