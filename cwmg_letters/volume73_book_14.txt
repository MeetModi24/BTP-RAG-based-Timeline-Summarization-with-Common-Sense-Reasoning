14. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
September 20, 1940
CHI. KAKA,
You may certainly publish the matter about the Kanpur report.
I have read the portion relating to Dr. Mahmud. There
is nothing in it worth including. I am returning it. I will send
Bal's letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
