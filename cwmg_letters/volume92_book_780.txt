780. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
SABARMATI,
June 30, 1934
DEAR HARIBHAUJI,
Bapuji has received your letter.
He wants me to write: 'Shri
Chaitanya Muni will not be able to change his guise before me.'
Yours,
CHANDRASHANKER
From the Hindi original: Haribhau Upadhyaya Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
