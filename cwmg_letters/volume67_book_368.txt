368. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
August 18, 1938
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I am glad the operation is successfully over. I understood
about Shankaran. How long will you have to remain there?
Pyarelal's fever will go only when it has run its course.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10589
