387. LETTER TO KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
June 25, 1937
CHI. KAMALNAYAN,
Mr. Kallenbach has been bothering me to let him send you
a gift at the time of your marriage. He wants to spend more than
Rs. 100, and spoke of £25. I would not hear of it. He then
asked me what he should give. I suggested books. "Which
books?", he asked. I couldn't decide. Tell me what books
you would like to have?
Reply by return of post.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3056
