102. LETTER TO MANEKLAL AND RADHA GANDHI
November 2, 1935
CHI. MANEKLAL AND RADHA,
I have the New Year day letters from both of you and was
glad to read them. May the new year bring you happiness and
prosperity.
Ba is here. Devdas recovered recently from his illness. He will
come over here for rest. Ramdas is in Bombay, doing nothing
there. One cannot quite say that he has settled down. Manu and
Kanti are here. Manu is betrothed to Surendra, Kishorelalbhai's
nephew.
Blessings from
BAPU 1
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXIII
