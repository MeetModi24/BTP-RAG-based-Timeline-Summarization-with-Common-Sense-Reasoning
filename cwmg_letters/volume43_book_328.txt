328. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
April 26, 1930
CHI. NARANDAS,
Moulvi Syed Rauf Pasha is a professor in Colombo. He is com-
ing to see the Ashram and may stay there for a few days. Look
after him and introduce him to Imam Saheb and Mirabehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 8107. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
