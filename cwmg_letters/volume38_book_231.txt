231. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Sunday [December 9, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
Your letter. Mrityunjaya's letter mentioned Friday as the
probable day of your leaving for Dwarka. But having waited for
a day and knowing well your nature, I have written.
Do only as much as you can. Do not fall ill through your
desire to work to excess. Half the period is over now. I hope to
come in January. Learn the rest from the letter to Kusum.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3323
