460. LETTER TO D. C. MALCOLM
JOHANNESBURG,
September 26, 1906
TO
D. C. MALCOLM, ESQ.
GOVERNOR'S OFFICE
JOHANNESBURG
DEAR SIR,
With reference to your letter of the 24th instant addressed to the
Association, will you be good enough to let me know whether it means
that Royal assent has been given by cable to the Asiatic Ordinance?
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
ABDOOL GANIE
CHAIRMAN,
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
Indian Opinion, 6-10-1906
