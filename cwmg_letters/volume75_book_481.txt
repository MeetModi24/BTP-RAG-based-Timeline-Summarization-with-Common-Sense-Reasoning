481. LETTER TO KANTI GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
March 10, 1942
CHI. KANTI,
Got your letter. I shall certainly try to detain Saraswati. I
shall train her in whatever I can. She will certainly be useful in
the dispensary. Shanti will get many companions.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Chhaganlal Gandhi Papers. Courtesy:
Gandhi Smarak Sangrahalaya, Ahmedabad
