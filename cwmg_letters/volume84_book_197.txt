197. LETTER TO SUNDERLAL
SIMLA,
May 14, 1946
BHAI SUNDERLAL,
I have read what you sent me regarding the name 'Hindi'.
I shall watch what you do.
I leave for Delhi today.
Blessings from
BAPU
PANDIT SUNDERLALJI
37 BAI KA BAGH
ALLAHABAD, U. P.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
