192. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
September 22, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
We can form any habit about eating and sleeping that we
desire. If you rise early and do not sleep during the day time,
you are bound to form the habit of going to bed early.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9433. Courtesy: Ravindra R.
Patel
