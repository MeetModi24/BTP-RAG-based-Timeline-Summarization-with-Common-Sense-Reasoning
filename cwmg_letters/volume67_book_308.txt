308. LETTER TO SARASWATI
August 5, 1938
CHI. SARU,
If I am not to call you crazy shall I call you wise? Have
you really grown wise? Why would Kanti be uneasy if that
were true? Kanti's letters reveal that you are only crazy. Tell
me now what I am to believe. Give me all the details: what
your diet is, how many hours you sleep, what you study and
whether you play the vina. Do you go to school or study at
home? Do you keep cheerful?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Manu has arrived. Kakasaheb has had cholera but I hope
he will survive.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6170. Also C.W. 3444. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
