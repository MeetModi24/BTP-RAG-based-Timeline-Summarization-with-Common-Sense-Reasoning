469. LETTER TO DR. SYED MAHMUD
BIRLA HOUSE, NEW DELHI,
January 28, 1948
RESPECTED DOCTOR SAHEB,
Jai Hind.
Gandhiji has received your letter. He says that it is not possible for
him to go there for rest. Where is any rest for him in this life? If it is
possible, he wishes to go to Sevagram for a few days. From there he will
return straight here.
Hope you are well.
Yours,
BRIJKRISHNA
From a photostat of the Urdu: G. N. 5097
