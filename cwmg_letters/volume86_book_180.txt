180. LETTER TO SEVAGRAM ASHRAM INMATES
[On or after November 20, 1946]
I am afraid you must give up all hope of my returning
early or returning at all to the Ashram. The same applies to
my companions. It is a Herculean task that faces me. I am
being tested. Is the satyagraha of my conception a weapon of
the weak or really that of the strong? I must either realize the
latter or lay down my life in the attempt to attain it. That
is my quest. In pursuit of it I have come to bury myself in
this devastated village. His will be done.
Harijan, 8-12-1946
