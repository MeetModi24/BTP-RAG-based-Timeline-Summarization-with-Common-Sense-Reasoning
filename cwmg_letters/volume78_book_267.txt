267. LETTER TO SHUAIB QURESHI
SEVAGRAM,
October 21, 1944
MY DEAR SHWAIB,
Your letter in your usual style. I must await your arrival
in patience. The Nawab Saheb's telegram quite becomes him.
Let us see what happens.
I hope all is well on your side.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
