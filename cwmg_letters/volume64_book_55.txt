55. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
November 16, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter. Consider this as a footnote to Lila-
vati's letter. Kanaiyo is happy here. He has taken upon him-
self a big job. If he sticks to it his life will certainly be re-
warded. Jamna should not pester him for coming there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8512. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
