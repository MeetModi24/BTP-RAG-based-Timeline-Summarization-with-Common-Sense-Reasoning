396. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 12, 1939
MY DEAR AMRIT,
You have been true to your word. Though your telegrams
have made bad reading they have consoled me, being so full and
accurate. I hope that in spite of all the trouble, you will come
well out of your illness and reach Simla in a fairly fit condition.
I must have wires while you are ailing.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3941. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7250
