227. LETTER TO URMILA DEVI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 18, 1928
DEAR SISTER,
You must have seen in the papers that I have returned to the
Ashram. You may now come whenever you like with Dhiren. I
can understand and even appreciate Dhiren's disappointment.
Everything however comes to those who wait, and if Dhiren has
patience and qualifies himself, he may hope to take part in the
final struggle which must come sooner or later if the time of its
advent can be accelerated by those who wish to take part in it.
I hope that Sudhir is now thoroughly restored.
Yours sincerely,
SHRIMATI URMILA DEVI
4-A NAFAR KUNDU ROAD, KALIGHAT, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 13493
