71. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKER PATTANI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 14, 1928
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I got your letter. Were it not that Mahadev is ill and con-
fined to bed, I would probably have sent him to the station for
his own satisfaction. But now I am sending none. However,
Chi. Kusum has to go to the station to meet her friends and so
I take the opportunity to tell you through this note that all of
us are looking forward to your arrival.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 3223. Courtesy: Mahesh Pattani
