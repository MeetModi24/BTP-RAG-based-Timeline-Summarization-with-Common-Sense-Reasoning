267. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
As AT SABARMATI,
May 26, 1931
MY DEAR SHANKERLAL,
With reference to the suggestion of Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramayya
advising purchase of the site and building at Masulipatam I agree
to the proposal if the purchase price is anywhere between five
and six thousand rupees.
SYT. SHANKERLAL BANKER
MIRZAPUR, AHMEDABAD
From a microfilm: S.N. 17149
