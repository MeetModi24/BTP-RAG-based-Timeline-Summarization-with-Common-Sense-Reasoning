190. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
RAJKOT,
April 18, 1939
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I get your letters; but how can you know my predicament?
How do you expect me to send you a telegram, send you some
[note], when I cannot even go to sleep till 1 o'clock at night.
Others do not expect such things from me.
Why should I have to worry over your fast? You began it in
the name of God. He was watching over you. Was that not
enough?
I am reasonably well. Some discomfort is there but I keep
cheerful. The rest from Sushila's letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 417
