684. LETTER TO SARASWATI GANDHI
BOMBAY,
April 19, 1945
CHI. SURU,
I was very happy to receive your letter. God will grant you
success. The victory over Harilal, which was denied me, has
come to you two. You are correct in saying that if he can get
rid of the two vices he can be the best of all brothers. Let us see
what you people can do. Kanti is very confident. Faith is a
great thing. I am going to Mahabaleshwar tomorrow. I shall
be there for a month and then one more month at Panchgani.
Madhavdas had taken poison but has survived. Manilal, Sushila
and the children have arrived.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 6185. Also C.W. 3459. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
