428. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
[PANCHGANI] 1,
June 4, 1945
CHI. MANUDI,
I have not seen anyone so foolish as you. You are to come
here in response to this letter. Bablo must accompany you, so
that you may not have any difficulty on the way.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Ba Bapuni Shili Chhayaman, p. 231
