281. LETTER TO SUDHIR GHOSH
BIRLA HOUSE,
BOMBAY,
September 9, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji desires me to thank you for your letter of the 29th August enclosing
a letter from Mr. Graham White.
You certainly can come to Sevagram with Mrs. Ghosh on Gandhiji's return after
the meeting with Quaid-e-Azam Jinnah.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI SUDHIR GHOSH
TISCO HOTEL
JAMSHEDPUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
