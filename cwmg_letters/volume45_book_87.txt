87. LETTER TO KALAVATI TRIVEDI
January 7, 1931
CHI. KALAVATI,
Yes, purdah is observed here and there in Gu[jarat] too.
After all Gu[jarat] is a part of India! Hirabehn is in a pitiable
plight. Teach the people of Ras to spin on the takli and make
bamboo taklis locally. I don't understand about Samalbhai.
Do write again. I shall enquire into the dispute. Keep your-
self fit. Learn shirshasana or sarvangasana for your feet. Learn the
method. Kashinath can read from the book available at the
Ashram and write to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5272
