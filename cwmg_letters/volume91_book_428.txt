428. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
Monday [December 14, 1925]
CHI. TARAMATI,
There is a telegram from Mathuradas saying that your health
is not good and you look pale and sad. You certainly do not
have to worry about Mathuradas. I am there to worry about
him. In truth it is God who worries about us. You must look
after your health and that of the child. Therein lies your service.
Write to me. I have wired to Devdas to go to Mathuradas.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
