414. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
February 15, 1933
CHI. NARANDAS,
I send with this letters for Jethabhai, Jivaram and Budha-
bhai. I also send the letter written by Jivaram. It should be
shown to all the inmates. It reveals his sincerity and the devo-
tion with which he is working in Orissa. The letter need not be
returned.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I
