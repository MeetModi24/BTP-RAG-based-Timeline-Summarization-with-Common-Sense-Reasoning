614. LETTER TO GULAM RASUL QURESHI
December 5, 1932
CHI. QURESHI,
I have your letter. You must get yourself cured of the piles
completely. I will take care of Amina. There are many places
to which she can be sent. I will see about it after 2nd January.
Meanwhile she should get ready. I am not writing separately
to her.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10814. Courtesy: Gulam
Rasul Qureshi
