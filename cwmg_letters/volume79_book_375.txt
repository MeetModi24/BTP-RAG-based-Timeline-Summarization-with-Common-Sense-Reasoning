375. LETTER TO V. VENKATASUBBAIAH
SEVAGRAM,
March 9, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your estimate. Now let me have your qualifications
in detail and references. Is Rs. 60 per month meant for you?
Is it to be devoted merely to women and children?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
VENKATASUBBAIAH, M.L.A.
ASHRAM
NELLORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
