157. LETTER TO DEVRATNA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 15, 1926
BHAI DEVRATNAJI,
Your letter to hand. I think a lot about the Hindu-Muslim
problem but I know that at this moment, it is useless for me to say
anything. Rest assured that when I see I can do something
I shall certainly place the remedy before the people. This I do
know that Hinduism shall never be saved without tapascharya. I
am giving it due thought and taking necessary action.
From a photostat of the Hindi: S.N. 12200
