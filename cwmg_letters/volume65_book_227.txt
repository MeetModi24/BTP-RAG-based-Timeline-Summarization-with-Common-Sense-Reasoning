227. LETTER TO KAPILRAI H. PAREKH
May 20, 1937
BHAI KAPILRAI,
It is good that you wrote. I am sending the letter itself to
Jamnalalji. I will forward to you the reply that I receive from
him. Perhaps he may even write to you direct.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KAPILRAI P A R E K H
SUNNY SIDE
BLOCK No. 1, PLOT 253
MATUNGA, G. I. P., BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9730. Courtesy: Kapilrai H. Parekh
