138. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
NEW DELHI,
April 5, 1939
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
You know I do not write letters these days. Do come for
the Gandhi Seva Sangh. I learn today that it has been post-
poned on account of the plague. The date will be announced
later.
I understand about the dictionary. You should not mind
such things. It is true that I was trying to avoid giving Shan-
karan a separate dictionary but one must get the things one
needs. Study well. It will be good to learn Urdu as well. The
health, of course, has to be improved. I have no idea when I
shall be arriving.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1920
