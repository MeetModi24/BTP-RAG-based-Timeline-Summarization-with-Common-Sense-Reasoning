153. LETTER TO MANSHANKAR J. TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 29, 1930
CHI. MANU (TRIVEDI),
Gangabehn writes and tells me that you have become im-
patient to go out [for work]. This was before you got my letter.
I hope that my letter has overcome your restlessness. Can a
soldier ever ask why? He goes on doing, silently and cheerfully,
the work assigned to him. Let Kakasaheb's condition be fulfilled.
It is not long now before he will be released. If you are still not
satisfied let me know.
Blessings from
BAPU
