305. LETTER TO PARASRAM
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Sunday, Asadha 11, August 8, 1926
BHAI PARASRAMJI,
I got your letter, which made rather painful reading. It is
true indeed that the programme of Council-entry has resulted in
bitter quarrelling. But why should those who take no interest
whatsoever in Councils feel unhappy about the matter? If we
go on doing our best, truth is bound to triumph ultimately. The
present is a difficult time, but we should not make ourselves un-
happy about it if we are to retain our peace of mind.
SHRI PARASRAMJI
C/O CONGRESS COMMITTEE
BRADLAUGH HALL
LAHORE
