353. LETTER TO S. THURAI RAJA SINGAM
AHMEDABAD,
August 18, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the golden studs. They will
be used for khadi fund.
You may make what use you like of my writings about
Dinbandhu Andrews.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. S. THURAI RAJA SINGAM
THE SCHOOL
PEKAN
[MALAYA]
From a microfilm: S.N. 17508
