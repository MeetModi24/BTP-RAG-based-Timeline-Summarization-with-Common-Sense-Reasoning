68. LETTER TO RAMNARESH TRIPATHI
MEERUT,
October 28, 1929
BHAI RAMNARESHJI,
What I have been able to see of your collection of folk songs
has enabled me to have some idea of the extent of your labour
and your love for the national language. It is a fine collection
and I would like every Hindi-lover to study it. The book deserves
a place in educational institutions. Your translation of the folk
songs has further enhanced its worth. Congratulations on your
effort.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From the Hindi original: C.W. 9240. Courtesy: Municipal Museum,
Allahabad
1 Gramya Geet, published in four parts
