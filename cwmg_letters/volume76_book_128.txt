128. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
BOMBAY,
May 11, 1942
CHI. NARANDAS,
I had just taken up the pen to write a letter to Jamna
when I saw your article. I do not see any point in publishing
it. So I am not doing so. Write to me.
Kanaiyo is quite well.
Since I have no time, I stop here.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
We are returning on Sunday.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8604. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
