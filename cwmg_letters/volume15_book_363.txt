363. LETTER TO MUKERJI
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
June 27, 1919
DEAR MR. MUKERJI,
Many thanks for your letter of the 24th instant, enclosing the
petition to H.E. the Viceroy. I am publishing the petition in
extenso in Young India. I am also publishing the bit of information
about Mr. Kalinath Roy's health contained [in] your letter. I
am hoping that our friend will regain his liberty at an early date.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 6694
