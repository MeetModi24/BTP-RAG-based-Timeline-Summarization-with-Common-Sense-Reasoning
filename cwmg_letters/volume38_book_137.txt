137. LETTER TO PRESIDENT, LATENT LIGHT CULTURE
As AT SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 28, 1928
THE PRESIDENT
LATENT LIGHT CULTURE, TINNEVELLY
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
I am sorry that I have not yet been
able to reach your book and now that I am again on the move
I do not know when I shall be able to look at it. I would
really not trouble you to send me the lessons that you have
kindly offered to send. I shall have no time to read them. And I
do not think that the other members of the Ashram, busy as they
are, will have much time at their disposal to tackle new things.
Yours sincerely,
