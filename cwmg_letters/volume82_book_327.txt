327. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
CAMP: KHADI PRATISHTHAN,
SODEPUR (NEAR CALCUTTA),
December 18, 1945
DEAR MR. ABELL,
In continuation of the correspondence about the alleged
ill-treatment of Dr. Rammanohar Lohia, I am now able to send
as requested a copy of the statement made by Dr. Lohia to his
legal adviser.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 66
