201. LETTER TO KUNVERJI KHETSHI PAREKH
Ashadh Sud 14 [July 15, 1924]
CHI. KUNVERJI,
I was sorry to read about the demise of your revered maternal
uncle. I know he was a great support to you. But knowing that
birth and death are our inescapable companions, we should nei-
ther rejoice at the one nor mourn for the other.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
CHI. KUNVERJI KHETSHI
C/O PAREKH GOKULDAS TRIBHUVAN
MORVI
From a copy of the Gujarati original: C.W. 676. Courtesy: Navajivan Trust
