530. LETTER TO KUNVARJI MEHTA
December 14, 1930
BHAI KUNVARJI,
I got your letter full of information. Ask Jugatram too to
write. I am all right wherever I am, whether in or out of jail.
God holds the string and I dance as He pulls it.
Kanjibhai's self-sacrifice is great, and his courage greater
still. Convey my compliments to him.
I have written to Napoleon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2689
1 Chhotabhai Kalyanji Mehta
