252. LETTER TO DR. JAWAHARLAL
February 8, 1935
DEAR DR. JAWAHARLAL,
I have your letter enclosing copies of correspondence about
Angnu Chamar. I shall await advice of further developments.
Yours sincerely,
DR. JAWAHARLAL
CIVIL LINES
CAWNPORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
