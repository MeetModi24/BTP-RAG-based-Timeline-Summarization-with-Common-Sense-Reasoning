279. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
March 30, 1933
CHI. NARANDAS,
I forgot to write to you about Rajbhoj. Look after him. Ask
him and find out his needs. He will have to observe all the
Ashram rules and take part in all its activities.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8345. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
