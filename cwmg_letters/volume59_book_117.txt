117. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
September 30, 1934
BHAI SHRI VITHALDAS,
Enclosed is a khadi hundi for Rs. 10. Buy with it khadi
which may be useful to Harijans and distribute it among Harijans
selected by you. Let me know what you do. If you can't decide,
consult Nurgisbehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9786-a
