285. LETTER TO SIR DINSHAW MANECKJI PETIT
POST ANDHERI,
April 5, 1924
DEAR SIR DINSHAW PETIT,
I thank you for your letter of the 31st ultimo and appreciate
the reasons you give for your decisions against Mrs. Sorabjee.
I thank you too for your kind inquiry. I am making slow
but steady progress.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR DINSHAW MANECKJI PETIT
41, NICOL ROAD
BALLARD ESTATE
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 8676
