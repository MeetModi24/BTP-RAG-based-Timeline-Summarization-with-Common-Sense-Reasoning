38. LETTER TO J. H. GARRETT
BORSAD,
April 26, 1931
I have your letter of 24th instant.
It is hardly fair to tear from its context a sentence from my
letter so as to give it a meaning wider than what it would bear
if the sentence was read in its context. If you accept the repre-
sentative character of the Congress, the party to the Settlement,
is it right to issue over the heads of the representatives the notice
that the Mamlatdars have issued in the place of the one about
which I complained?
I must confess that I miss the friendly response which I had
hoped our interview would evoke. I still ask you to approach
the Settlement in the spirit that actuated Lord Irwin. The way
you are going is the way of war. I assure you that I want to
work the Settlement in the friendliest spirit possible. Will you not
reciprocate?
Young India, 20-8-1931
