173. LETTER TO KOTWAL
May 31, 1935
BHAI KOTWAL,
I got your letter. I have absolved nobody. I merely said that
if Haribhau and you had misled the members of the Committee,
they would be absolved from blame. But even though you might
have misled them, how can those who accompanied you be ab-
solved? As a matter of fact, therefore, nobody is absolved. Your
atonement lies, first, in explaining to the people there what their
dharma is, and, secondly, in refusing in future to assume any
responsibility beyond your capacity. You have no gift for collect-
ing money. Since you did not have money, you should not have
involved yourself in money matters. Except this there is no other
atonement. It will not matter if you can collect nothing. But
even if you are offered contributions of a hundred or two hundred
rupees, you may accept them. Drop by drop the lake fills.
Blessings from
BAPU
