443. LETTER TO GORDHANDAS CHOKHAWALA
[June 23, 1946]
CHI. GORDHANDAS,
Chi. Chimanlal writes and tells me that you are having pro-
longed fever. If so, you should rest and go for a change of air.
Sevagram may perhaps suit you. I hope to reach there on or
before the 7th [August]. I will go to Poona on the 28th.
I hope Sharda and Anand are fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 10085. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokhawala
