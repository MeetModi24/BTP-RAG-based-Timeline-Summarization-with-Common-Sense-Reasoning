49. LETTER TO B. R. AMBEDKAR
April 27, 1933
DEAR DR. AMBEDKAR,
In accordance with my promise I send you herewith my opi-
nion on your proposal. I hope you do not mind my having dealt
with the matter publicly. I thought that the issue raised by you
was of such momentous importance that if I discussed it at all,
I should do so publicly.
Yours sincerely,
Enclosure
From a photostat: S.N. 21074
