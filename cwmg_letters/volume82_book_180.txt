180. LETTER TO ZOHRA CHAWDA
November 27, 1945
CHI. ZOHRA,
I have your letter. I did not know you had such a sense of
humour. I was very happy to learn that you had made one of
the rooms shine like a mirror. Do the same with the other
rooms and all of you then use them as mirrors. This will save
the expense on a mirror.
You slept a lot. If you keep sleeping like that, you will
recover quickly. A sick person should sleep as much as he can.
I have Vanu's letter but I am not writing to her today. I
am very busy.
Blessings from
BAPU
ZOHRABEHN
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
