429. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
October 2, 1936
CHI. LILA,
You have seen how from being Lilavati you have become
Lila. Now before you become Lili I have to be worthy of it.
If I were, I would not have to argue with you. It is difficult
to become a kinsman without being a blood relation. About
paper you may do as you think fit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9582. Also C.W. 6554. Court-
esy: Lilavati Asar
