387. LETTER TO DR. B. PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
SEGAON,
August 21, 1938
DEAR PATTABHI,
Please make a searching inquiry into this complaint and let
me know the result. I do not want to publish the letter before
knowing your verdict.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
