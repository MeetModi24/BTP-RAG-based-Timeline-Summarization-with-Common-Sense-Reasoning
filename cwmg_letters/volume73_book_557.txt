557. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 26, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
You will care to read the enclosed. You may destroy it.
The weather is getting warmer though I remain unaffected by
it.
Manu Trivedi's father has come. He is one of the rare men
I have met. He is a good man. He is suffering from blood-pressure
and is under my treatment though living in Wardha.
Janakibehn is getting on. Madalasa is still causing worry.
Amtul Salaam is keeping fairly well. All else is as when you were
here. Do drop a postcard to Ba. She is to have an injection today.
This is being written before 4 a.m.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4007. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7316
