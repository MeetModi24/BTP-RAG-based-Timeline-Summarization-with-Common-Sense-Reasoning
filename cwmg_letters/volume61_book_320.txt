320. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
July 2, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
I forgot to reply to one question of yours.
Is it necessary to reply to what Vajubhai has written?
I do want your reply when you get the time. I have received
another still longer statement. I will send it to you after reading
it. Read both and send me your remarks.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8458. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
