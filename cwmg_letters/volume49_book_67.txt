67. LETTER TO SHAKARIBEHN C. SHAH
January 28, 1932
CHI. SHAKARIBEHN1,
No woman need spare me while I am here. When I am
outside, anybody writing to me may increase my burden. But
while I am here you can make up for your previous [self-restraint].
Do, therefore, write to me. Is your mind at peace now? Why
is it that Babu remains weak?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.G. 29
