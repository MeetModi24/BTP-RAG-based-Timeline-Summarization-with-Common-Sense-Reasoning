238. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
August 20, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
You will have recovered by now. Both of you should drink
plenty of pure water. Read a lot. Write neatly in ink every day.
Show it to me when I come. Preserve all that you write. The
boy should also write. He should also work and spin.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
