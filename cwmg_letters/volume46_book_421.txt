421. LETTER TO URMILA DEVI
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 6, 1931
DEAR SISTER,
I had your telegram and I have now your letter. Things in
Bengal are certainly most deplorable but I do not know what can
be done from here. I thought that arbitration was the only thing
that can relieve the tension. What else could be done either by
the Working Committee or me personally? Subhasbabu came
here and gave Sardar Vallabhbhai a long indictment against Syt.
Sen Gupta. Vallabhbhai has filed it hoping to be able to investi-
gate the complaints when both the parties were present. Can
you suggest any other way out of the difficulty?
SM. URMILA DEVI
40/ RUP CHAND MUKHERJEE'S LANE
BHOWANIPUR, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 17240
