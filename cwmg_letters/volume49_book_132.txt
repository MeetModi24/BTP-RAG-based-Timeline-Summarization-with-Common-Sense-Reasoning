132. LETTER TO MATHURI N. KHARE
February 12, 1932
CHI. MATHURI,
I will forgive you everything except writing a bad hand. You
should write each letter [in a word] as neatly as you draw a pic-
ture. Gajanan is there to help you. If you ask him he will
teach you how to do so. It is a sort of game. You need not
spend much time in practising. You should practise only for a
short time every day. Tripathibhai's Manu used to practise while
lying ill in bed. He was in bed for one whole year.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 263. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
