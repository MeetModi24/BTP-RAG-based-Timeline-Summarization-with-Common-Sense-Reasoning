260. LETTER TO MADHAVJI V. THAKKAR
February 9, 1935
CHI. MADHAVJI,
I was feeling uneasy as there was no letter at all either from
you or from Mahalakshmi. But then I received your letter. I hope
that both of you bear in mind that your present sadhana is for
gaining greater ability for service. Sadhana can never be service.
How are the children?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MADHAVJI
15 TANGAR, ENTALY
CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6827
