411. LETTER TO LALJI PARMAR
June 5, 1931
CHI. LALJI,
Is Mamasaheb displeased with you? Do you write to him?
Have you been looking for a wife? Are you in a hurry to get mar-
ried? Write to me and tell me frankly what your wish is.
I hope you are working hard there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3292
