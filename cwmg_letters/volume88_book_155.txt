155. LETTER TO PARASURAM
NEW DELHI,
June 23, 1947
MY DEAR PARASURAM,
Your letter. I am sorry you feel cramped in Ahmedabad.
Of course you will go to the Socialist Party in Bombay where
you will get greater scope for service according to your notion.
Keep well wherever you are.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G. N. 5859. Also C. W. 3073
