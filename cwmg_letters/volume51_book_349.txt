349. LETTER TO MOHANLAL
October 8, 1932
BHAISHRI MOHANLAL,
You are right in your view that inter-dining and inter-
marrying with Antyajas are not a necessary part of the movement
for the eradication of untouchability. Hence there can be no
coercion brought to bear on anybody in regard to these. If,
however, anybody wishes of his own free will to have such re-
lations with Antyajas, he should not be prevented from doing so,
just as members of "touchable" communities are not prevented
from having such relations among themselves. Eradication of
untouchability means permitting Harijans, and persuading others
to permit them, to enjoy the rights which as a human being
every Hindu enjoys.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 232
