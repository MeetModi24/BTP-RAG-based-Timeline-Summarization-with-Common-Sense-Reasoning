45. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
May 5, 1941
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
Today I finished reading your article on the economic condition
of India. I liked it very much. To do justice to it, a gist should
be given in the beginning. There should be more of such articles,
and they should be printed in pamphlet form and translated.
If it is necessary to go to Bihar, I am fully prepared. I read
again about Schuster, etc. I do not feel there is any need for sugges-
tions from here. We should put our own house in order and
clean it. Time is on our side. They have to move forward. We
must secure control over things. At least freedom of speech and
the Press should be allowed and the detenus should be released.
Even communists should not be detained without trial.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8042. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
