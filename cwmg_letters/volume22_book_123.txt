123. LETTER TO V. A. SUNDARAM
[Before February 1, 1922]
MY DEAR SUNDARAM,
My heart goes out to you. I do not want to hurt you with
a harsh word or a kind word harshly uttered. I want your week of
silence to be a week of privilege. Do not think of helping Ba during
the week. But use the wheel. It is a real companion. Study
Hindi and think deep. Write what you like. Do not read much
just now.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3201
