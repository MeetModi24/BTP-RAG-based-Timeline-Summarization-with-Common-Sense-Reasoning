104. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL VIDYARTHI
NANDI DURG,
May 18, 1936
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
If your nose bleeds, clean it with cold water every morning
and also inhale water through the nose. Keep a wet cloth
on your head throughout the day as I am doing. Otherwise,
consult a doctor. There is no need to go to Sind. Tell
Nanavatiji that I have given instructions about ghee. Tell Bhanu
Bapa that I have sent a letter to Nasik. Pursue your studies
diligently.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PRABHUDAYAL
MAGANWADI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 11670
