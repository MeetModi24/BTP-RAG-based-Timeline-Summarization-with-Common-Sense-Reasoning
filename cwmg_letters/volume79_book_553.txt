553. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
March 28, 1945
CHI. RAMESHWARI,
I have your beautiful letter. So Father won ultimately. He
was short of temper but he had a heart of gold. He was equally
generous. I have already received a cheque for Rs. 1,000. I shall
see what can be done with it.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAMESHWARI NEHRU
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
