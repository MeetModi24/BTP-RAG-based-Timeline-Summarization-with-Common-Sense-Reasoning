546. LETTER TO CHAMPA R. MEHTA
NEW DELHI,
October 7, 1946
CHI. CHAMPA,
I have your letter. If only you are patient, the clouds of
misfortune that have gathered over you will disperse.
It is surprising that Shanti has still not recovered. Write
to Sheth Bachharaj Tribhuvandas at Panchgani. He will at
once make some arrangements. Medical and other facilities are
available there. And go there when he writes. There is no
need to go to Miraj just now. Besides, the place may have
no facilities. It must be getting a little cold in Panchgani now,
but that is nothing to worry about. Let me know what happens.
Bachhrajbhai is known to us and you need not, therefore, go to
another place.
Has Pramod stayed on there? I hope he has got well.
I shall have to stay on here for the present.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am not writing a separate letter to Gatulal. Let this be
for him also.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 1049. Courtesy : Champa
R. Mehta
