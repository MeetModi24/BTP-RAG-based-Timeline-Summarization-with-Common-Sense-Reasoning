235. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
March 27, 1933
DEAR MAJOR BHANDARI,
With reference to Seth Jamnalalji's transfer to Arthur Road
Prison for medical examination, will you please ascertain from the
Government whether I may correspond with him freely and rece-
ive letters from him freely in view of the daily interviews I was
permitted to have whilst he was here, of course subject to the limi-
tation of the correspondence strictly to his health and untouch-
ability matters ?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. Gandhi
From a photostat: G.N. 3884; also Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home
Department, Special Branch, File No. 800(40) (3), Pt. IV, p. 261
