12. LETTER TO THE HEAD MISTRESS,
BALIKA VIDYALAYA, PILANI
SEVAGRAM,
January 4, 1945
HEAD MISTRESS
PILANI SCHOOL
THE HEAD MISTRESS,
Please find enclosed a letter for Sumitra Gandhi. Please see
its contents and pass it on to her.
Since her eyes are weak her
diet needs some attention.
Please tell Lakshmibehn and Chi. Mathuri to write to me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
