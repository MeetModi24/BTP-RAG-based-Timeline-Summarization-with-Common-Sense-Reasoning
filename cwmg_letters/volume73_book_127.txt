127. LETTER TO ABDULLA HAROON RASHEED
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 28, 1940
DEAR HAJEE SAHEB,
I see you have published only an extract from my letter.
It gives rise to a misunderstanding. Will it not be better for
you to publish the full text of my letter?
HAJI ABDULLA HAROON
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
