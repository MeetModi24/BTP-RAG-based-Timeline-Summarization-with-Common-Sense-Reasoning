74. LETTER TO LAKSHMI GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 25, 1939
CHI. LAKSHMI,
What sort of a person you are! You go on falling ill and mak-
ing us all anxious. Now take plenty of rest and get well. The
weather is very good here if you would come here now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2135
