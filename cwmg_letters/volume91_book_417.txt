417. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Monday [November 23, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I am under pressure, so I am writing with the right hand.
You must have received my express telegram earlier. But when
do things happen as man would wish? It is now five o'clock.
As I eat, Mahadev says he is shivering. He is getting fever. It
is already 100° F., so how can I send him? It seems hardly any
use sending Devdas. You do not need nursing; you need Maha-
dev's company. If you need Devdas, send me a telegram.
God will always do good. Do not worry. Do your best and
concentrate on the Gita.
Going by Nargisbehn's letter and Chhaganlal's talk I had
decided to send Mahadev. Do not try to write to me yourself.
But somebody should always write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
