411. LETTER TO VIPIN BIHARI
October 15, 1928
BHAI VIPIN,
I have your letter. It is almost impossible for me to go or
send someone. I congratulate you all on being able to persuade
Sadhu Vaswani to come over to Motihari. It is needless to tell
the students of Bihar that true learning means increasing purity
of life, i.e., being simple and straightforward.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI VIPIN BIHARI
RECEPTION COMMITTEE, STUDENTS' CONFERENCE
MOTIHARI, BIHAR
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9128.
