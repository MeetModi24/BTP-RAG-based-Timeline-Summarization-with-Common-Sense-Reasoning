213. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
October 2, 1932
CHI. CHHAGANLAL (VIJAPUR),
I had your letter. I am gaining strength and therefore today. I am writing
letters to the Ashram. I no doubt have faith in you. By the grace of God,

Classes. The fast was, however, broken on September 26, after the terms of a settlement
were accepted by the British Government.
