495. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 25, 1945
CHI. SUSHILA,
As I feel stronger after dinner, I am writing this. I hope
you will return soon after completing your work there. Except
for weakness, there is nothing.
Blessings from
BAPU
