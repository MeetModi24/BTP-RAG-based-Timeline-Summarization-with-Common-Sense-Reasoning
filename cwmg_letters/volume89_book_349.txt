349. LETTER TO DEV PRAKASH NAYYAR
October 18, 1947
CHI. DEV,
Enclosed is a cheque for Hunar.
I too had a letter regarding Chand's illness. Chand will prob-
ably come here in a few days. I had thought that Sushila must have
written to you. She has gone to Kurukshetra today. There is no
cause for worry about Chand. I do not see any necessity of your
going to Sevagram. I shall write on receiving further news of Chand.
Blessings from
BAPU
