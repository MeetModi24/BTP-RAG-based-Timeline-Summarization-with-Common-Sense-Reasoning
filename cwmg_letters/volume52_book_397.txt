397. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
December 25, 1932
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I got your letter. I was sorry to read about the piles. You
should avoid bajra roti in this condition. Take bread with butter,
milk and fruit. It may help to consult the doctor. Whatever
Narandas advises should be done. I would approve of your go-
ing away if that is considered necessary for your health. In this
matter too follow Narandas's advice. The German lady met
me and gave me all news. Kudsia seems to be doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 268
