489. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
December 2, 1934
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
Your telegrams to Vallabhbhai and to me have been received.
Even this minor operation is proving troublesome. I have a
letter from Dr. Bidhan too which says that you are improving,
though I do not gather the same impression from your wires. I
am awaiting a further telegram.
Writing to the Viceroy for an interview does not seem proper
just now. I have anyway written to him a second time and
asked what he meant by 'not at the present time'. I have also
asked for the reason of the refusal. Let us see what happens.
Whatsoever happens will be for the good.
If you are well and can go to Delhi I shall try to leave for
Delhi by the 20th. I am preparing for it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 8002. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
