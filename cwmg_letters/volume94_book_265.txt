265. LETTER TO V. V. NENE
August 20, 1944
BHAI NENE,
I have your letter. I had no knowledge that I would be arrested
so soon or arrested at all. I did have a plan but was arrested before
it could be announced. And there were to be negotiations first. I cannot
say whether or not I would have met Jinnah Saheb.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 The reference presumably is to Gandhiji's impending meeting with Jinnah.
