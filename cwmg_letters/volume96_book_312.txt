312. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 28, 1938
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
Your absence which was wholly unexpected caused me deepest
disappointment. I expected you even up to the last day. But it was
not to be. I have Ramdas's letter giving me your message. I must
not force the pace. You must come in your own good time. I should
be satisfied if you will religiously decline to take new obligations and
simply set your heart on fulfilling the existing ones. And it may be
useless your coming here during the hot months. The best month is
November. From then to February it is cool enough. But it is cool
also during the rains. Now make your choice.
I am quite well.
Love.
UPPER HOUSE
