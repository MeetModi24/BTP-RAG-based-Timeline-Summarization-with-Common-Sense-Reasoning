420. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEVAGRAM,
August 7, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
There should be no eating in the kitchen. I shall advise the
women.
Clothes must be washed, slivers made. Grinding, sweeping
and dish-washing should be compulsory. If there is any confu-
sion, I shall see about it.
Bharatanandji's dog should be sent away. Dhiren's has been.
I shall see.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4350
