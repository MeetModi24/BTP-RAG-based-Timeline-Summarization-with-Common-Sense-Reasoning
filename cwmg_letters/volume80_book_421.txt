421. LETTER TO GHANSHYAM JETHANAND
PANCHGANI,
June 3, 1945
BHAI GHANSHYAM,
I have your letter. I would not take any money while in
jail and not more than Rs. 75 when out of jail. However, others
will act according to their lights. No one can borrow dharma
from others.
My health I should say is good.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI GHANSHYAM JETHANAND
29 AMIL COLONY No. 1
KARACHI
