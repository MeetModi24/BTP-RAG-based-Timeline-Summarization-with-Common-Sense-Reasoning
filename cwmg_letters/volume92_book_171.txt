171. LETTER TO VENILAL
MUSSOORIE,
October 23, 1929
BHAISHRI VENILAL,
It would be desirable for the members of the Satyagraha
Volunteer Corps to form the habit of doing every day some work
fixed in advance rather than a lot of it all together.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32578/66
