87. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
October 28, 1941
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter. I have suggested that you should be ap-
pointed Vice-President. I think you should let the Committee be
formed. There have to be such bodies. Only thus can we progress.
What is approximately the total figure now?
Kanaiyo is expected on Saturday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8595. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
