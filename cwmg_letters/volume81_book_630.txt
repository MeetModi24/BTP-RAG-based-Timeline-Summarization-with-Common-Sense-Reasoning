630. LETTER TO K. T. SHAH
October 14, 1945
BHAI SHAH,
I have your letter. You have replied promptly. I approve
of all your arguments. If you start an agitation on those lines,
perhaps the changes recently introduced can be withdrawn.
Blessings from
BAPU
PROF. K. T. SHAH
GAMDEVI
BOMBAY-
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
