429. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
[May] 5, 1936
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Herewith Akarte's letter. It is difficult to make out what
the truth is. I find it necessary to exercise restraint in giving
publicity to Buva. It is all right to cultivate his acquaintance.
I consider it improper to make use of him for public service
without closer experience of him. I shall go deep into the matter
when I get the time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2979
