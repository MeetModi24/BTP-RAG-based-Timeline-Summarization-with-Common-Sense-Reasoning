342. LETTER TO VITHAL L. PHADKE
March 30, 1936
CHI. MAMA,
I don't know whether Sardar has replied to you. You should
do as he says. For my part, I would say that you should not
incur the trouble of having a house, unless you are required to
stay there rather long.
You must have read in Harijan what I have written about
an ideal temple. Read it if you have not. While writing it
I had you in mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3834
