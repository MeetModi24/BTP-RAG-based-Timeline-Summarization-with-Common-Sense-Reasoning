252. LETTER TO R. R. AITHEN
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 11, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I fear that I am unable to give you any
useful guidance. I have not even been able to visualize your
organization. But if you are desirous of coming to Ahmedabad,
I shall be able to see you any day next week except Monday at
4 p.m.
Yours sincerely,
R. R. AITHEN, ESQ.
GENERAL SECRETARY,
INTERNATIONAL PEACE CAMPAIGN
150 WATSON HOTEL
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 13185
