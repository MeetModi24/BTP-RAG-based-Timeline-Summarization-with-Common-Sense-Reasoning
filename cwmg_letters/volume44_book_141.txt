141. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 22, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (DESAI),
I have your letter. You see that I am never behind in reply-
ing to any of your letters. Learn from Sushila all that you can.
But do you get any time for reading? Do you write the diary?
Have you kept up prayers? I keep good health.
How many women work there? Is there any news from
Kapadwanj?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1802
