295. LETTER TO TEJWANTI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 23, 1945
CHI. TEJWANTI,
Do not ask me hypothetical questions. You may ask me if
there is something concrete.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI TEJWANTI
CHARKHA SANGH (PUNJAB BRANCH)
ADAMPUR DOAB
PUNJAB
