469. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
CHI. SATIS BABU,
May I send a copy of your letter to the Madhukosh people
if you have not sent one already? Please let me know. What
you write has certainly startled me. I have received both the
samples of honey.
I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1643
