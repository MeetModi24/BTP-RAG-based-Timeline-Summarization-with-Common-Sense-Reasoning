275. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[After December 12, 1940]
I am not able to make out anything from what was narrated
to me. As for money, I had already issued an appeal. Let us be
content with whatever we have received. Now you have to deal
directly with Maulana and Jairamdas. You should write to
them everything. I have put in my letter to him everything that I
could. The Sindis received it direct from here.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 688
