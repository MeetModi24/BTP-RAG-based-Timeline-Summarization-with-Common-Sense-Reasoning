37. LETTER TO GIRIRAJKISHORE
CAMP MUSSOORIE,
October 22, 1929
MY DEAR GIRIRAJ1,
I have your letter. I have heard from Pratap Pandit also.
He says that he is taking a personal interest in you and that he will
certainly watch what you are doing. At present he wants you to
read and understand what is written in the light of the experi-
ence that you are having. I suppose that is much the best thing to
do. You should also try to procure all the literature on the eco-
nomics of the leather trade. You will find therein much to
strengthen the method that we are pursuing. There must be in
Pandit's library literature of this type too. If there is not, Kishore-
lal will be of assistance in finding out such books. You should col-
lect all the information about the various tanneries in India, the
condition of the leather trade, the requirements in India, the im-
port of leather goods from outside, the history of the failure of the
British Government to capture the leather trade from Germany.
All this is interesting reading. You should avoid fever and when
you do get any such thing deal with it at once. How are you do-
ing otherwise? I hope you write to the Ashram regularly.
