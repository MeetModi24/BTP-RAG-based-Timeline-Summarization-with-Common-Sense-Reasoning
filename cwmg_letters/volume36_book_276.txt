276. LETTER TO U. RAJAGOPALA KRISHNAYYA
April 16, 1928
BHAI RAJAGOPALA,
God being Almighty can bring about anything.
Violence should not be answered with violence.
One cannot gain knowledge of dharma from a historical inter-
pretation of the Mahabharata. And the Mahabharata is certainly
not history.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 9238. Courtesy: U. Rajagopala Krishnayya
