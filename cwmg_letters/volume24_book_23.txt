23. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
Wednesday [May 14, 1924]
CHI. DEVDAS 2 ,
There is a heart-rending letter from Ba. I do not know what
my duty is. If the children are there and if you think that they
ought to be brought here, do bring them. I hope you have com-
pletely recovered.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: S.N. 8814
