397. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
MAHISHADAL,
December 28, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
The papers report your safe arrival. This is earlier than
anything you could have sent. I hope all will go well there.
Here I am in the midst of peaceful surroundings on the
canal bank. This is saved children's home. No mosquitos. Is
it not 'es'? Fancy meetings absolutely silent though attended by
perhaps one lakh of people.
No more.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
