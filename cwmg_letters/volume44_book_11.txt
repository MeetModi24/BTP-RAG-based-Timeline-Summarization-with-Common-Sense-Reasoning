11. LETTER TO DAHIBEHN PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 6, 1930
CHI. DAHIBEHN (RAOJIBHAI),
You should write to me. I often remember you two Dahibehns.
I hope you never get fainting fits now. My blessings to all the
women who may be with you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9204
