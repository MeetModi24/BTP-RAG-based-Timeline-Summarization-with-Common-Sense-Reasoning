592. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 5, 1941
DEAR IDIOT,
Mahadev in Bombay, Mira in Nasik and Anand Hingorani
in front of me pulling the punka. He will be here a few days.
It is now 10.35 a.m.
Here are three letters. Haskell's I have read carefully. It
is a good letter and requires a reasoned reply. Discuss the letter
or the argument with Datta or any other learned Christian and
send your answer. Even if you do not feel like sending it without
discussing it with me, do have the discussion. See if you find a
supporter for our position.
I hope you got Pyarelal's pamphlet.
The weather has become less unbearable. The nights are cool.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Your letter. We shall discuss what should be done about
the Punjab when you return. Did not Mahadev tell you that I had
sent to Rameshwari my draft reply to Lady Hartog's wire? You
must have seen it. Your draft too is good but mine is probably
better.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4014. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7323
