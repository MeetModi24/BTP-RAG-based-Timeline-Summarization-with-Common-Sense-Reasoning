415. LETTER TO SANTOSH KUMAR BASU
[Before April 17, 1934]
DEAR SANTOSHBABU,
I congratulate you and your Corporation upon the passing
of the Harijan budget. But that is merely a beginning.
To pass
the budget is one thing, to actually spend the money is another.
I hope that you will personally see to it that, however poor your
purse may be, the Harijan will be a first charge on it. Please do
not forget water taps in the bastis.
Yours sincerely,
Amrita Bazar Patrika, 17-4-1934
