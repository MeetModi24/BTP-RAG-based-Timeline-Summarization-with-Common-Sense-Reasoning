540. LETTER TO DR. B. S. MOONJE
March 29, 1942
DEAR DR. MOONJE,
I had your two letters. Want of time is my excuse for the
lateness of reply.
I am out of all this thing. The Working Committee has the
matter in hand.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
B. S. Moonje Papers, File No. 40/1945-48. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
