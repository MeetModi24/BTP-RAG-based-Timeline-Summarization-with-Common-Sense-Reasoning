469. LETTER TO MITHUBEHN PETIT
February 26, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
I was eagerly awaiting your letter. Come on the 1st.
Jamnabehn, Perinbehn, Khurshedbehn, Jawaharlal, etc., will also
have arrived on that day.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MITHUBEHN PETIT
SWARAJ ASHRAM
SURAT
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2685
