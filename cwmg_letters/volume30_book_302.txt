302. LETTER TO A STUDENT
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday, April 7, 1926
BHAI . . . 1 ,
I have your letter from which I learn that you have not at all
lived with your wife. You were never intimate with her and yet,
you are afraid, she is pregnant. And this worries you. But I see
no cause for worry. If your wife is pregnant, you can put her
aside not in hatred but with pity. She may, if she can, live with
the man with whom she misbehaved and if that man is already
married she may stay with her parents. To them you should con-
vey the news gently but firmly.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 10853-A
