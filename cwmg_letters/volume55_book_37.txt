37. LETTER TO GANGABEHN B. JHAVERI
April 26, 1933
CHI. GANGABEHN JHAVERI,
I have your letter. You should experiment under Gauri-
shankar's guidance. You are taking a long time to get well. You
should experiment under some expert's guidance. If not Gauri-
shankar, let it be somebody else.
I will not forgive you if you do not write to me. An error
committed unknowingly can be pardoned. Should negligence or
lethargy be forgiven?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3953
