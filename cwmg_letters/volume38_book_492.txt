492. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
January 28, 1929
MY DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
Malkani advises me to abide by your programme and no
other. He evidently does not know me still. But I knew you
better before I began to know him as well. Hence his mistake
is pardoned. Tell him. Ba, Pyarelal and Subbiah accompany
me as far as I know at present. Narayandas has a programme
for Karachi which needs overhauling. Govindanand wants me
to visit Kotri. I am referring him to you. Kikibehn wants me
to stay with her in Karachi. You will do what you can.
BAPU
SJT. JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
MARKET ROAD, HYDERABAD, SIND
From the original: C.W. 9252. Courtesy: Jairamdas Doulatram
