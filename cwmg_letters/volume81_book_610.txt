610. LETTER TO SATYADEVI
October 10, 1945
CHI. SATYADEVI 1 ,
I am glad to have your letter. The letters are quite well-
formed. I am glad to learn that none of you have given up the
spinning-wheel. Ply it and also master all the attendant processes.
I hope Durga and Maitreyi are all right. So also Krishna-
maiya . What is Mahavir doing these days? You have not
given any news of Dharmakumar. Do you all stay together?
SATYADEVI
BORIVLI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
