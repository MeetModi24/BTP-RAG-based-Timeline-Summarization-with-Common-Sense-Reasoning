507. LETTER TO AKBAR HYDARI
[Before September 12, 1940]
In the course of a letter written to Sir Akbar Hydari, Gandhiji says
that satyagraha has been suspended by the State Congress under his advice.
However, he thinks that the time is come for some action to be taken and
he has picked up only four satyagrahis 2 out of a long list to offer satya-
graha at Aurangabad tomorrow. After they are arrested, none else will offer
satyagraha, but if they are released, they will again offer satyagraha.
Those four satyagrahis left Wardha last night for Aurangabad.
The Hindu, 12-9-1940
1 The news item in the source is dated September 12.
2 These were Achyuthrao Deshpande, Motilal Manthiri, Devram Nanji
Chohan and Hiralal Kotecha.
