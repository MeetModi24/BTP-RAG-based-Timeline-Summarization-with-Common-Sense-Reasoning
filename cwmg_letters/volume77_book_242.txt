242. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR H. PAREKH
PANCHGANI,
July 9, 1944
BHAI PRABHASHANKAR,
I have your letter. You are in great distress but you are
enduring it with courage. From Champa's letters, I learn that
you have been freed [from attending to Dr. Mehta's finances.]
For what work am I appointed an arbitrator? I am not even
aware of it. My appointment and continuance as an arbitrator
depend only upon the goodwill of friends. May I know what kind
of guidance you expect from me? I have no idea what I should
do. Without going deep into the matter I should only advise
you to give Champa whatever she wants. What is there left for
you now? Repeat the Lord's name in solitude and render as
much service as you can.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI PRABHASHANKAR HARCHANDBHAI PAREKH
DERA SHERI
RAJKOT CITY
KATHIAWAR
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XX
