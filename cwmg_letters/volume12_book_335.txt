335. LETTER TO KUNVARJI MEHTA
[CAPE TOWN,]
Jeth Vad 8 [June 15, 1914]
DEAR SHRI KUNVARJI1,
I have your letter. Thanks. I hope to meet the students
in Surat when I return to India.
With due regards
MOHANDAS KARAMCHAND GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: S. N. 2660
