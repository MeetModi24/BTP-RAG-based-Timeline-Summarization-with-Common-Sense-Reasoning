72. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
DELHI,
April 24, 1946
CHI. K. C.,
Rajkumari will write about the tents.
I do not like what you say about Bhansalibhai.
As for Anna he may look after things.
It is good that Shakaribehn and Champabehn have taken
up ginning.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 4553
