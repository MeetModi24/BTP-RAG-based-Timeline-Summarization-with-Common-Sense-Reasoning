272. LETTER TO RUP NARAYAN SHRIVASTAVA
As AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 14, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. The calf incident was an isolated
case with which I was called upon to deal personally. The rats
question is too big a question for me to handle. You will there-
fore excuse me for not dealing with it in the pages of Young India.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R U P NARAYAN SHRIVASTAVA
C/O SETH JAMNADAS, M.L.A.
JUBBULPORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 13799
