131. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
January 26, 1935
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have your letter. Khan Saheb is here today. I read
out your telegram to him. You must have received the long
telegram of blessings which he sent after reading it. You appear
to be specializing in arranging marriages. This marriage will
be remembered as a historical event. Poor Sophia could never
have thought that she would marry a Pathan.
Nor could Saa-
dulla have imagined that he would marry a Khoja girl. I like
your selection very much. Both will be happy and Sophia will
be able to do public work as much as she wishes. All of us will
reach Wardha on Tuesday.
There will be no new face in our
party. A nice girl engaged to Chand Tyagi's Balbir is with us.
Both Marys will get down at Betul.
It seems that Sardar, Rajaji and Rajenbabu will have to stay
on till February 8. The discussion on the Bill will be over by
then.
Kamalnayan has become impatient to go to Ceylon but he
should wait for some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
