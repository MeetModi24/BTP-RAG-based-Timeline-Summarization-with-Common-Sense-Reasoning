16. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
April 28, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
Titus must have handed over charge to you. If he has not
done so, send me a wire. I will then wire to him again. I hope
he gave you my letter.
If you can manage the dairy and make it a success, I think
an important task will have been accomplished.
Get over your nose trouble. I think it can be done with
simple remedies.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Why have you asked for sans and khadi to be sent from
here? Your request doesn't seem proper to me. Mahadev will
write more.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9076
