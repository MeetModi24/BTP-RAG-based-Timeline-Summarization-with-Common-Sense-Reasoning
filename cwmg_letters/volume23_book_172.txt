172. LETTER TO N. K. BEHARE
POST ANDHERI,
March 18, 1924
DEAR MR. BEHARE,
I have the text of the resolutions passed by the Depressed
Classes Conference held at Koteshwar. You may depend upon
my doing all that is possible for me to do in the common cause.
Yours sincerely,
N. K. BEHARE, ESQ.
NORMAL SCHOOL
WARDHA
From a photostat: S.N. 8522; also C.W. 5121
