143. LETTER TO KAPILRAI MEHTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 22, 1930
CHI. KAPIL,
I don't mind your staying at Vile Parle. Who else are there?
How is Abdulla Sheth? Improve your health and make it robust.
Kakasaheb keeps good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Kakasaheb sends his blessings.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3974
