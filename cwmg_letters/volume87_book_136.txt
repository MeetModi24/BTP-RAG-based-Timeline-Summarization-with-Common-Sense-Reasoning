136. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
PATNA,
March 23, 1947
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I read both your letters and gave them to Khan Saheb for
reading. He says he is watchful and will go when it is neces-
sary. If I ask him to go he is ready to leave today. But I do
not feel the need for it. Your anxiety is meaningless. You must
have received my previous letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 2504
