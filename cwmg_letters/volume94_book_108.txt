108. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA, C.P.,
October 13, 1941
CHI. SHANKERLAL,
I have your letter. You must recover soon. I shall meet Bhai
Rangaswami if he comes.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHANKERLAL BANKER
19TH CROSS ROAD
MALLESWARAM
BANGALORE
From the Gujarati original : S. N. 32742
