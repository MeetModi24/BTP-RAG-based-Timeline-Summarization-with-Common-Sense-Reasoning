98. LETTER TO G. FINDLAY SHIRRAS
BORSAD,
June 30, 1931
DEAR PRINCIPAL SHIRRAS,
I thank you for your letter of 25th instant, in reply to mine.
I understand that you have admitted all students but seven.
There is therefore, I presume, no question of want of accommoda-
tion. So far as the internal discipline is concerned I suppose that
what you regard as indiscipline was more or less common to the
majority of students. But what perhaps you have in mind is that
the seven students were ring-leaders. I suggest that, if it was
right to take the other students, it would be wrong to keep out the
ring-leaders. The Delhi Settlement makes no such distinction bet-
ween the leaders and the rank and file. I would urge you, there-
fore, to take the remaining students and avoid a possible crisis.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
G. FINDLAY SHIRRAS, ESQ.
PRINCIPAL, GUJARAT COLLEGE
AHMEDABAD
