383. LETTER TO V. A. SUNDARAM
October 8, 1928
MY DEAR SUNDARAM,
Though no one likes to lose his mother, I do not propose to
send you my condolences. Your mother died happy in the thought
of having brought up good and dutiful children and having lived
a godly life. A death such as hers is to be envied. I hope both of
you are keeping well.
With love,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3208
