412. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
August 27, 1938
You should have no worry while you are building your body.
In this we may well copy Englishmen. Out of office they for-
get the office cares and every day come fresh to work. If they
get ill, they know that they must rest and trust that without
them the nation's business will go on as usual. This is true Gita
living. It is the soundest economy.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
