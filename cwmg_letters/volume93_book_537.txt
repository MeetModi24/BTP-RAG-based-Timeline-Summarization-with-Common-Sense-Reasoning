537. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
SEVAGRAM,
Via WARDHA (C. P.),
July 24, 1940
MY DEAR PREMI,
What are you doing in Hyderabad? . . .1 that Father is quite
restored now. He is not working, is he? Do write to me
something about the situation. Love to you all.
BAPU
SHRI PREMIBEHN JAIRAMDAS
BHAI MULCHAND LANE
HYDERABAD, SIND
From a photostat: C. W. 10160. Courtesy: Arjun Jairamdas
