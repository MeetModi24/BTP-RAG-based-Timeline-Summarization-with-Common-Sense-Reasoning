533. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
PANCHGANI,
July 15, 1946
CHI. VALJI,
I have gone through your draft. It is a good summary.
You have given better treatment to the vows than to the con-
structive programme. The latter lacks life. Maybe the original
itself lacks it.
It would be quite wrong to give to this writing the title
"Mohan Samhita". I do not see the quality of a Samhita in it.
A simpler title would be more becoming. Truly speaking what
Manu Subedar wants is something much better. It has to be
something original. He is not likely to want a mere summary.
Probably what he has in mind is a booklet or two based on
all my writings. You should show him your draft and find out.
It is the title that I strongly object to. 'Mangalprabhat' and
'A Summary of Constructive Programme' would be a better
description of what you have written.
I hope you are well. It would be morally wrong to neglect
your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Should I send the draft and the books with somebody or
bring them with me when I come there?
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 7500. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
