362. LETTER TO DR. SATYAPAL
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
August 23, 1927
DEAR DR. SATYAPAL,
I have your letter. I understand what you say, but I do not
appreciate your attitude towards Lalaji. There are many matters
on which I do not agree with him, but his sincerity, his love of
the country are undeniable. His self-sacrifice and unbroken re-
cord of service entitle him to our respect and affection and make
him indispensable for any public work in the Punjab. You may
know that even in the heyday of Non-co-operation, I used to say
this very thing and used to tell co-workers that without Lalaji,
I could and would do nothing in the Punjab and that my busi-
ness was to convert Lalaji and that the Punjabis had a right to
defer to Lalaji rather than accept the views of one like myself who
was comparatively a stranger to them. If I were you therefore,
I should go again and again to him and strive with him but not
strive against him.
Yours sincerely,
