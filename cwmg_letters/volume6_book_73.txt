73. LETTER TO H. COX
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 2, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter and your postcard. I shall be pleased to wait on
you on Monday at 4.30.
I am,
Yours truly,
HAROLD Cox, ESQ.
6, RAYMOND'S BUILDINGS
GRAY'S INN, W.C.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4455
1 Parmanand
