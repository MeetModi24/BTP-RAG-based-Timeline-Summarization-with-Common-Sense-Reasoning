271. LETTER TO KHWAJA
August 2, 1926
KHWAJA,
I have your letter. I had written to you specially to tell
you that if you thought that the book you wrote did no good to
Islam, you had better withdraw it—not for my sake or for the
sake of anyone else. I do not know if the Arya Samajists are
trying to convert some prominent Muslims. One nowadays hears
all sorts of things, which have neither head nor tail.
Yours,
GANDHI
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary, p. 257. Courtesy:
Narayan Desai
