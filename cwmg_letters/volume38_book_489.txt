489. LETTER TO PERIN CAPTAIN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 27, 1929
I have your letter. I am sorry about Jamnabehn. Are you
sending the parcel directly to Karachi or are you sending it to
me? If it reaches me at Karachi the same time that I reach there,
it would be of course ideal. I reach there on the 1st February,
leaving Ahmedabad on the morning of the 31st instant.
About the message to be sent to Queen Souriya, what can
poor Ba really write out. She doesn't follow the controversy.
Whatever, therefore, she sends would be not hers but somebody
else's. In all such matters, therefore, I should leave her severely
alone. Nor am I sure that the step you propose is without
question the proper step to take.
If I pass through Bombay on any account, I should love to
give you the time you need.
MRS. PERIN CAPTAIN
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 15000
