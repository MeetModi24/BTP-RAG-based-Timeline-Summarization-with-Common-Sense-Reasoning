214. LETTER TO DR. SHUMSHERE SINGH
WARDHA,
January 15, 1936
MY DEAR SHUMMY,
You have I know forgiven me for keeping Amrit here so
long. Her presence was a great comfort to me. The discovery
of her great affection was a treasure. I hope you have found
her in possession of good health. I am faring well. Amrit passed
on to me all your carefully thought out suggestions. Thank you.
Love to you both.
BAPU
[PS.]
The accompanying from Prabhavati for Amrit.
From the original: C.W. 3560. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6369
