7. LETTER TO NARASINHRAO B. DIVATIA
November 16, 1932
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I hope I did not worry you by writing to you. If a beggar
hides his begging bowl, his cause would be lost. Though you are
advanced in age, I can put no limit to your capacity. Will you
kindly send me the names of other persons whom you think I can
approach for help?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Narasinhraoni Rojnishi, p. 548
