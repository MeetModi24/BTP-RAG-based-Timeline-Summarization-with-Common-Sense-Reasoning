117. LETTER TO AMULYA DHAN RAY
January 19, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I have read as you have about the
movement in Bengal for the revision of the Yeravda Pact. I can
assure you that I have found no reason whatsoever to revise my
own attitude. You need not be anxious, for I am convinced that
that Pact cannot be revised without the unanimous consent of all
the parties concerned.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. AMULYA DHAN RAY, M.L.C.
JESSORE TOWN
BENGAL
