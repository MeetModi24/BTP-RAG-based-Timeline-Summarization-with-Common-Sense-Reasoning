100. LETTER TO PARASURAM
NEW DELHI,
June 11, 1947
CHI. PARASURAM 1 ,
Shri J. P. Narayan tells me that you would like to serve the
Socialist Party if I agreed. I could have no objection what-
ever to your bettering your prospects. I was told that you
were in the Harijan office till you felt you could join me. You
know that I have not filled your place and I have no intention
of doing so. You will return when you like.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G. N. 5858. Also C. W. 3072
