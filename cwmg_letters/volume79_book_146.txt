146. LETTER TO NRISINHPRASAD K. BHATT
SEVAGRAM,
February 1, 1945
CHI. NANABHAI,
I have your letter. I understand what you say regarding
Bachu. The sea is close to Ghogha and to Bhavnagar. Will not
its cooling effect be enough?
I shall consider how much I should leave over for you after
Manubhai and Vijaya arrive. I shall do my utmost to lighten
your burden.
Blessings from
BAPU
NANABHAI BHATT
AMBALA, KATHIAWAR
