449. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[April 8, 1946]
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
Don't you go to the patients? I have sent over whatever
spinning equipment of yours I could find. Let me know what
still remains so that I can send for it. Even these things I
found only with difficulty.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 507
