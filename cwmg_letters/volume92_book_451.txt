451. LETTER TO N. P. RAGHAVAN
June 7, 1931
MY DEAR RAGHAVAN,
I was delighted to hear from you after so long. I hope
Titus is better. Thappan says you are all weary there and not
wanted. If such is the case you may return to the Ashram.
Yours,
BAPU
SJT. N.P. RAGHAVAN
KHADI VASTRALAYA
PAYYANUR
N. MALABAR
From a photostat: C. W. 10865. Courtesy: N. P. Raghavan
