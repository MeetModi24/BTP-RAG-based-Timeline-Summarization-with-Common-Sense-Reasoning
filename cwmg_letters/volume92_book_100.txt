100. LETTER TO CHUNILAL
August 14, 1929
BHAI CHUNILAL,
Reading can be made popular by putting into practice what
we read. Children can be trained to be good by our setting be-
fore them the example of our own firm conduct. The beginning
can be made by wearing khadi and plying the spinning-wheel
in public. You may learn my views about school education from
what I write in Navajivan. In trying to live a truthful life, one
should learn to endure even seemingly unendurable hardships. In
order to purify the poisonous atmosphere, one should make oneself
like nectar. In order to keep the village clean, one should become
a scavenger.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/114
