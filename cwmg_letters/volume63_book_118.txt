118. LETTER TO DEVNANDAN AND OTHERS
June 26, 1936
CHI. DEVNANDAN, RAMEHAR, RAMSWARUP,
SHRIRAM
AND
RAM-
VRIKSHA,
I have your letters. You did well in writing. Out of the
four the best handwriting is Devnandan's, the worst Ramvrik-
sha's. The least the learners can do is to perfect their hand-
writing. If the handwriting does not improve now, it will be
extremely difficult to improve it later. I write a wretched hand
and it can hardly be improved in my old age.
And just as we should maintain a neat and tidy hand, so
should we keep our heart good and pure. This is the easiest
thing to attain.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1084
