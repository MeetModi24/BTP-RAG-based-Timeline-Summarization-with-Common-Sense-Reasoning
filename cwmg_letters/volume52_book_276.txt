276. LETTER TO MANGALDAS M. PAKVASA
December 16, 1932
BHAISHRI PAKVASA,
Dhirubhai told me that you had to get yourself operated
upon for hernia. All of us felt worried on hearing this.
Dhirubhai said that you had become quite weak. Write and
give full details. May God restore your strength soon.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4677. Courtesy: Mangaldas Pak-
vasa
