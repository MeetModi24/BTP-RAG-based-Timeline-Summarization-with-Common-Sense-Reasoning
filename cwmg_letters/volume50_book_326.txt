326. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
[August 4, 1932]
CHI. NARANDAS,
You will find with this a letter for Ratubhai. The clearing of
the outgoing mail seems to have become regular once again. This
has happened at the right time. For some time you may write
to me daily if you find it necessary to do so. Inquire and try
to get as much information as you can.
Doctor had been thinking of coming over in November, but
we know that God does not always fulfil our wishes.
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro -9: Shri Narandas Gandhine, Pt. I, p. 447. Also C.W. 8244.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
