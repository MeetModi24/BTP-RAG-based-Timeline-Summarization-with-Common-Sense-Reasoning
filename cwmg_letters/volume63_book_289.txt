289. LETTER TO MEHERCHAND AHLUWALIA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 22, 1936
DEAR FRIEND,
My agreement is general. I have never advocated nor do I
now advocate class war or class hatred. I do not believe the for-
mer to be inevitable. But I have never said nor believed that
capitalist help is necessary for the Congress cause.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI MEHERCHAND AHLUWALIA
RAJPURA
N.W.R.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
