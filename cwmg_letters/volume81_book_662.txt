662. LETTER TO RATNADEVI
October 17, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
Now don't ask for a message from me. As far as possible
I want to do silent service. If you want to see me, come over
when I have settled down.
RATNADEVI
VANASTHALI VIDYAPITH
JAIPUR STATE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
