70. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 17, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I just could not write yesterday. It was a full court to the very
end. There should have been a letter from you today but it has not
come. There is no letter from your brother either ! I am getting on
well. Ram Saran Das is in good shape. Nirmal Singh continues to have
fever. His swelling has subsided.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
