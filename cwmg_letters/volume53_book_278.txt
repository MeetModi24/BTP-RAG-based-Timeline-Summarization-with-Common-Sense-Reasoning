278. LETTER TO KALI MOHAN GHOSH
January 31, 1933
DEAR KALI BABOO,
It was a matter of great joy to me to receive your letter des-
cribing the activities of Santiniketan and Sriniketan in the service
of the Harijans. My heart-felt congratulations to you for the solid
work you are doing.
I note that you are presently holding a district conference of
the Harijans of Birbhum. I hope that that would result in a great
wave of purification both amongst the Harijans and the caste Hin-
dus followed by really solid work.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. KALI MOHAN GHOSH
HON. SECRETARY
SERVANTS OF UNTOUCHABLES SOCIETY
BIRBHUM, BENGAL
