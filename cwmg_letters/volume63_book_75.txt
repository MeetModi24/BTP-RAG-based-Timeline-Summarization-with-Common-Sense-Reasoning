75. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 18, 1936
MY DEAR AGATHA,
This is from my new abode-a proper village which may
be defined as a place with no post-office, no store for food-
stuffs of quality, no medical comforts and difficult of access in
the rainy season. I could add many more adjectives but these
should be enough for the time being. This is not to say that
I am suffering any discomfort. I have told you this to let you
understand the nature of [the] task before me.
I appreciate all you say about news. Jawaharlal is trying
to cope with the problem. Let us see what is in store for us
during the next six months.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1495
