333. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH AZAD
August 31, 1945
BHAI PRITHVI SINGH,
I have your letter. About your quoting me I have often
told you to have me put down in writing what I say. Be it
as it may, I know this much that caste Hindus do not abide
by their dharma. In this connection please read the discussion I
had with Murthy as also about what Harijans should do.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PRITHVI SINGH AZAD
HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH
LAJPATRAI BHAVAN, LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
