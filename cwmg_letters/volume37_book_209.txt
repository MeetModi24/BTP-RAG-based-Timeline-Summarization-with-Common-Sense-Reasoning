209. LETTER TO DR. S. C. BANERJEE
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 14, 1928
DEAR SURESH BABU,
I got your two letters in Bardoli from where I have just
returned. I shall certainly deal with your report in the pages
of Young India.
With reference to the Exhibition I am in touch with Sjt.
Subhas Babu. I enclose herewith a copy of my latest letter to
him. I must confess that I do not like the idea surrounding the
forthcoming Exhibition. If I had my way I should not exhibit
not only British goods but also foreign goods unless they are so
valuable and yet so unknown as to require a special advertise-

not exhibit mill-cloth; for, mills neither need nor deserve any ad-

Yours sincerely,
DR. SURESH C. BANERJEE
