250. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
POONA,
March 5, 1946
CHI. SITA,
I got your postcard. I like your confidence that you will
pass. It will bear fruit. Keep up your courage and peace of
mind at the time of the examination, too. You may come when-
ever you wish to. Your place will be reserved.
I wrote to you one letter yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 4973
