140. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI N. PATEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 24, 1939
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I see that your yajna is becoming a very great one. We will
be able to solve the problem of disposing of khadi only if we go
deep into the matter and study it carefully. This will require
collection of figures.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9010
