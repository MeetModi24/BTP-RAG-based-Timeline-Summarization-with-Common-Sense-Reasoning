102. LETTER TO PUSHPA K. DESAI
CALCUTTA,
August 24, 1947
CHI. PUSHPA,
I have your letter. I was glad that you wrote frankly. All
I said was that I would not object if you decided to go to
Jugatram. Your duty is to live in the Ashram and overcome
all your ignorance. The Ashram, such as it is, is your refuge.
Your good lies in doing as I advise, since you have faith in me
and have made yourself my daughter. The final responsibility
should rest with me. You should welcome anything that Chimanlal
or Balvantsinha may say. But if you do not like it, you can refer
it to me.
Bhansali is a respected elder. But in my estimate he is
unworldly, an avadhuta. He cannot be an example for you to follow.
It would do you no good to let you accompany me on my
tours.
Blessings from
BAPU
