324. LETTER TO INDRAVADAN N. DIVYENDRA
August 2, 1941
BHAI INDRAVADAN,
I got your letter. My opinion about milk has not changed.
But what is the value of opinions which I myself have not
been able to act upon? Please, therefore, forget my opinion for
the present and do take as much cow's milk and ghee as you
can digest.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
