80. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
May 1, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
You have completely relieved me by your excellent letter.
After your giving a certificate to Narandas, I don't require
one from anybody else. But I hope you know who can give a
certificate. Can a person who does not know how to cook give a
certificate to a cook? Looking at the matter from that point of
view, you should have a higher degree of yoga, equanimity and
concentration, than Narandas. If it is so, it means I have two
persons instead of one who may be described as sthitaprajnas.
If Dr. Purushottam has left, haven't I sent you Dr. Sharma?
He seems to have filled you with colours indeed. Even if you do
not benefit from his treatment, it will certainly do no harm. Conti-
nue it with complete faith. Try whatever changes in diet he may
suggest. If the result is not satisfactory, he himself will ask you
to give them up. Try to win over Keshu if you can.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 878. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
