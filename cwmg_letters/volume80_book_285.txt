285. LETTER TO S. SALEMNA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 23, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
The case you relate is very sad. You should do all you can
locally. My capacity is too limited to cope with things I used
to.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI S. SALEMNA
DEPRESSED CLASSES LEAGUE
VANIVILAS
MANGALORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
