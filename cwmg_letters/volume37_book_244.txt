244. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
August 25, 1928
BHAISHRI CHHAGANLAL,
Chi. Navin is to be sent to Jamia Millia to help Devdas.
Therefore, release him for that institution. If more time is re-
quired for the purpose, we shall think about it when I return
there. Chi. Rasik is already free. He, too, has to go to Delhi. It
is necessary for both to get trained in carding, etc., so that they
may be proficient in these processes.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 14762
1 Of the Ralston Purina Company, Missouri, which manufactured food
products
2 Phlegm; the Gujarati word kaph is from Sanskrit.
