309. LETTER TO G. K. V. DEVAR
February 15, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
You must not believe all that may be reported of me. But I
can say this much that the women of our villages are not slaves.
They are mistresses of their households and I do say also that
they are not compelled in the matter of child-bearing by their
husbands.
SJT. G. K. V. DEVAR, B.A.,B.L.
GUNTUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
