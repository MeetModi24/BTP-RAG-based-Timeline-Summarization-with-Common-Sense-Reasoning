489. LETTER TO DHIRUBHAI B. DESAI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
September 7, 1941
CHI. DHIRUBHAI,
Received your letter. It is frank. I am satisfied. I don't
attach importance to the other things I have heard. More about
that when I have time.
You have not written about Bhulabhai's health.
I hope
he is in good spirits.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
