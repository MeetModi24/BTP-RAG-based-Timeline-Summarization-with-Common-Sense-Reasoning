311. LETTER TO MANGALDAS PAKVASA
SEGAON,
August 6, 1938
BHAI MANGALDAS,
I got your letter too late to be able to send you my blessings
on the 3rd. You did not give either your son's or his bride's
name. The marriage celebration must have passed off without
any trouble. Please convey my blessings to both. May both of
them devote themselves to service and may God grant them long
lives.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4680. Courtesy: Mangaldas
Pakvasa
