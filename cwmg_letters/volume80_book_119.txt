119. LETTER TO PARMANAND DESAI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 5, 1945
CHI. PARMANAND,
I have your letter. Come whenever you want to. The place
will be full when there is a meeting of the Kasturba Smarak Nidhi
from the 15th. You are not concerned with that.
Blessings from
BAPU
PARMANAND DESAI
C/O RAMAN BHATIA
170 BARA IMAM ROAD
NULL BAZAAR, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
