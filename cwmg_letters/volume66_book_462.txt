462. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
February 13, 1938
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I am happy. My letter to Lila will give you the blood-
pressure count. Zakir Saheb arrived today and had his meal with
me. Nothing is known about the persons who are coming from
the Frontier. It is very dusty here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 401
