595. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
October 9, 1945
CHI. KANTI,
I have your letter. I think it is a good thing that both the
Desais have gone there.
Valjibhai has told me all that you omitted from your letter.
He gave me a description, too, of all the people he had met.
I did not tell you, but now I do, that Valjibhai readily
agreed when I suggested it to him that he should go there. He
did not even ask for any time.
If Sardar permits, I hope to reach Sevagram on the 21st.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 7378. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
