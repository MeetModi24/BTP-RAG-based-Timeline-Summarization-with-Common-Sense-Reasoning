23. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
April 24, 1940
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I knew one day you would see the truth. I wish to explain
things at length, but I have no time. With patience, everything
will become clear.
I should be informed of whatever loss is
observed. I have read the letter from B.A. Ascertain his needs
before permitting him to come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1932
