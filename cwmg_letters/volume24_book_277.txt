277. LETTER TO INDRA VIDYAVACHASPATI
Sravana Sud 4 [August 4, 1924]
CHI. INDRA,
Received your letter. Do you wish to drag me out of my
sphere of work? What can I write about Shivaji Maharaj? I
feel ashamed to say that I know nothing more than what I read
about him in my student days.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI INDRA VIDYAVACHASPATI
"ARJUN"
DELHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4859. Courtesy: Chandragupta Vidya-
lankar
