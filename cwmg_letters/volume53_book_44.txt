44. LETTER TO DHANNU LALL SHARMA
January 13, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I will not refer you to any of my statements this time, but
I will ask you to re-read your own letter. I suggest to you that
that letter is libellous, insulting and unworthy of one who holds
the position of General Secretary of a Sanatan Dharma Sabha.
If you will re-read your letter calmly and dispassionately, you
will at once apologize to the pandits whom you have libelled,
and, in any event, till you apologize, your letters will neither
be read nor replied to. I am sorry for it.
Your sincere friend,
SF. DHANNU LALL SHARMA
GENERAL SECRETARY
SHREE SANATAN DHARMA SABHA
220 HARRISON ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 18917
