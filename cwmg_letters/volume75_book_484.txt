484. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
March 11, 1942
BHAISHRI JIVANJI,
You should keep here a stock of English copies of Constructive
Programme Either give an agency to somebody or send the copies
here. I will try to sell them. A note may be published in Harijan, too.
Regarding Bhajanavali, I will have to study the question care-
fully. How is that possible? Please, therefore, be satisfied with what
we have. If I am imprisoned any time, I may undertake this and
other such tasks.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9949. Also C.W. 6924. Courtesy:
Jivanji D. Desai
