467. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SHAHPUR,
January 14, 1947
CHI. VALLABHBHAI,
. . .1 Now about the proposed Bihar commission of inquiry.
It was some gentleman from Bihar who gave me the infor-
mation. I did not note down his name. Is it not a fact that you,
the Governor and the Viceroy are against the appointment of a
Commission and that this is sufficient to stop the Chief Minister
from appointing one? In spite of all this, I am strongly of opin-
ion that if no commission is appointed, the League's report will
be accepted as true. I alone know what pressure is being put
on me.
This is my view regarding Sudhir. If the League Ministers
and the Viceroy also agree, I do not see any harm in appointing
him. If he is to work under the High Commissioner, the post
also will have to be approved by you three. Moreover, it is
the members of the Cabinet who are suggesting Sudhir's appoint-
ment. If so, they should invite him publicly. If this point is not
made clear beyond any possibility of doubt, Sudhir will lose
whatever value he now has. You may now do what all of you
think fit. I saw just now that Sudhir's name has appeared in
the statement.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, pp. 344-
