237. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
March 14, 1932
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your letter.
I would prefer 'Madhavdas' to 'Madhavlal'. But these days
a good many people do not like the suffix 'das'. Did Santok
like my suggestion? You should also discuss the matter with
Benarsi and then decide. Please do not think that the name
selected by me should be approved. Do not neglect to write to
me after going to Kashi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9061
