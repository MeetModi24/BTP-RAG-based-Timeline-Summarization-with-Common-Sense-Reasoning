260. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
PANCHGANI,
July 14, 1944
DEAR DR. JAYAKAR,
Many thanks for your kind letter.
Publication of the interview was premature as I have ex-
plained in my public statement . Having given the fullest weight
to your advice I could not say to the Viceroy more than I did.
I am not going to put you to the trouble of coming to Panch-
gani. If Sir Tej comes as he had said he would, I would like
you to join him. In any case now I know your mind.
I have the lawyers' opinion. I am not making public use of it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
THE R T . HON'BLE, DR. M. R. JAYAKAR
MALABAR HILL
BOMBAY 8
Gandhi-Jayakar Papers, File No. 826, p. 15. Courtesy : National Archives
