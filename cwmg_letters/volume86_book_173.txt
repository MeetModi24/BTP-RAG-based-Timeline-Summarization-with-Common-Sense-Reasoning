173. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
AND SAROJ NANAVATI
KAZIRKHIL,
Wednesday, November 20, 1946
DEAR DAUGHTERS RAIHANA AND SAROJ,
I had two letters from you. Yesterday I started taking milk
and shall take whatever diet agrees with me. Rajendra Babu
says that the Bihar affair is settled now. Let us see what God
now prompts us to do. My proposal to fast was absolutely right
but there is no time to argue about it. I shall argue it out if
we meet some day, and I hope that you will agree that my
action was right, or you will point out my error. I shall be
satisfied either way.
It will not be right to invite you two here. The weather
here is also not good.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAIHANA TYABJI
WARDHA
