222. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
July 11, 1938
CHI. LILA,
What a letter from you! What is the point of your staying
on there in such a condition? If you do not like to remain
there, come over here. I do not want to keep you there against
your wishes. If I have killed your individuality, what good has
your staying with me done you? I did not at all like your
letter. Why do you not understand that you are free to do what
you like?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9374. Also C.W. 6649.
Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
