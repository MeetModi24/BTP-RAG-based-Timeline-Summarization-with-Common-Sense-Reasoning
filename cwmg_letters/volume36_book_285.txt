285. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 19, 1928
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I have your letter. You tell me nothing about your own
health. I hope you are keeping well. Does this tour mean more
khadi sales? Is there a great response from the people addressed,
or, are the collections from individuals?
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1589
