443. LETTER TO SHANTA
Silence Day [May 26, 1930]
DEAR SHANTA,
How do you fare? Is your mind absolutely calm? Perhaps
there might be a letter from you in the post which I have not
received. If you have not written to me any letter, write to
me.
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 578
