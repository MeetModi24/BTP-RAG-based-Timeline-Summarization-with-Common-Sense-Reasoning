146. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
October 22, 1933
CHI. MANI,
I got your postcard. I will wait for you there on Wednes-
day. I do hope Baba will come. I hope you are improving.
Swami arrived today. The rest on Wednesday.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 109
