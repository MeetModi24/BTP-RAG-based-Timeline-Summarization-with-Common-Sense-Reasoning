46. LETTER TO VIDYAVATI
April 13, 1942
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. My blessings and Ba's to the newly-wed
couple. May Jogendra 2 be well soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
RANI VIDYAVATI
T. R. N. SANYAL BUILDING
11 BLOCK, JOGENDRA PATHAK ROAD
LUCKNOW
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers.
Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
