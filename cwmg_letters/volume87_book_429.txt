429. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
PATNA,
May 15, 1947
CHI. MIRA,
Your two letters. I do not reach there before 25th so far
as I can see. I do not know what you should do now. No
more time.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABAI
P. O. RISHIKESH
DIST. DEHRADUN
From the original: C. W. 6526. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G. N. 9921
