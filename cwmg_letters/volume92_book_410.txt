410. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
January 24, 1931
CHI. SUMANGAL PRAKASH,
I see nothing wrong in your having had to go home because
of illness.
I have not read Kropotkin's book. My views concerning
India are well known. In a general way on economics I liked
Ruskin's Unto This Last. Write to me and tell me about your
present activities and whether you have fully recovered.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
