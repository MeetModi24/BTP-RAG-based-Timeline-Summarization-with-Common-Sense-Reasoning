270. LETTER TO S. ALI HUSAIN
2, MOZANG ROAD,
LAHORE,
January 24, 1920
DEAR MR. HUSAIN,
I was glad to receive your letter. The information I made use
of in the meeting was given to me at Meerut and in the motor
by the friends who brought me to Muzaffarnagar. If you dispute
their version, I would like you to let me have your own.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat of the handwritten copy: S.N. 7068
