681. LETTER TO G. L. CROSS
[October 19, 1945]
DEAR FRIEND CROSS,
It gave me joy to have your letter through Sudhir who will
tell you all about my movement.
Of course you and your wife must see me when I come to
Bengal. About attending Friends' meeting, you have proposed a
difficult task. They will have to excuse me. But if they could
come to Sodepur, I shall be delighted to meet them.
FRIEND G. L. CROSS
INDIAN RED CROSS SOCIETY
D-
CLIVE BUILDINGS, CLIVE STREET
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
