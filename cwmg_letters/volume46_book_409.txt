409. LETTER TO DR. JOSIAH OLDFIELD
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 5, 1931
MY DEAR OLDFIELD,
You have revived sweet memories of old. I am not going
to England this summer. Whether I shall have to go in Septem-
ber I do not know. If I do and if I get ill, I might have to seek
shelter in your hospital. Anyway it was good of you to think of
me.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. JOSIAH OLDFIELD
LADY MARGARET HOSPITAL
DODDINGTON, KENT (ENGLAND)
From a photostat: C.W. 4517. Courtesy: Dr. Josiah Oldfield
