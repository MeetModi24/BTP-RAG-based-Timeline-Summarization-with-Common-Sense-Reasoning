400. LETTER TO TARABEHN N. MASHRUWALA
February 23, 1937
CHI. TARI,
I got your letter after many days. I was pleased. I am
glad that you have gone to take a vaid's treatment. I want
your body to get strong somehow. I find it simply intoler-
able that you should be so unsteady in mind and practically
useless for anything. I am hoping to take much work from you.
Fulfil that hope of mine. I give rest to my right hand on days
other than Mondays, and dictate most of the Gujarati letters to
Manu or Vijaya.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6700. Also C.W. 4345. Courtesy:
Kanubhai N. Mashruwala
