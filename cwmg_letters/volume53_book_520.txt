520. LETTER TO S. GANESAN
February 24, 1933
MY DEAR GANESAN,
I have your letter of 20th instant, but as I have anticipated
you and told you that advance copies can be sent to you on Fri-
days and not before, I need not say anything more.
Mahadev just reminds me that sending you copies on Fridays
gives you no advantage because Madras copies are all sent on
Fridays. My previous letter tells you how utterly impossible it
is to send you anything earlier.
I return the letter from Dr. Rajan.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20347
1 Of Harijan
2 Vide p. 369.
