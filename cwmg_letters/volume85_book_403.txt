403. LETTER TO SYED RAZA ALI
NEW DELHI,
September 15, 1946
BHAI SAHEB,
I got your letter. Thanks. It was a misunderstanding on
your part. It was wrongly reported in the papers. I shall not
bother you with arguments. And I don't have the time.
RAZA ALI
RAZA LODGE
MORADABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
