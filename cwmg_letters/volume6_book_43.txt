43. LETTER TO J. H. POLAK
HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,
October 30, 1906
DEAR MR. POLAK,
I told you that I should pass the whole of Sunday with you, but
I see that I shall have to attend to an important matter in the evening.
The Pandit of whom I spoke to you and I have not yet finished the
whole of our discussion, and as it is rather important I am afraid
that I must deprive myself of the pleasure, I was looking forward to,
of passing the whole of next Sunday with you. I fear therefore that I
1 Shyamji Krishnavarma
will have to leave you next Sunday also at about 4 o'clock.
My regards to all.
Yours truly,
J. H. POLAK, ESQ.
28, GROSVENOR ROAD
CANONBURY
