377. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
July 18, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
This is just to wish you a happy time in Poona.
Of course you will do about the balance Rs. 400 what you
would have done about 400 should a similar occasion arise. I
wanted you to wait for me to give the word so long as you were
in doubt.
I shall look forward to your arrival on 1st or 2nd August.
Herewith a note for Charlie.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3717. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6873
