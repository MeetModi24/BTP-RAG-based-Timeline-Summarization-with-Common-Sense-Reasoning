267. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
January 29, 1933
BOYS AND GIRLS,
Your letter can be said to be beautiful. I cannot send you
such a beautiful one. All of you should try and increase your
knowledge of farming. The more you know of farming, the more
you will be interested in it. Take it from me it is a good training.
All of you going out to serve the Harijans-I regard it a better
training still. If you work conscientiously and do not tire of it,
you will be able to win them over and wean them from their bad
habits.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
