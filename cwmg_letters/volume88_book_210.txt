210. LETTER TO HOOSENBHAI LALJEE
June 30, 1947
BHAI HOOSENBHAI,
I have your letter. These days it is no use consulting me; I
don't know what is happening. I am buried in my own affairs.
I shall now show your letter to friends.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
HOOSENBHAI LALJI
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
