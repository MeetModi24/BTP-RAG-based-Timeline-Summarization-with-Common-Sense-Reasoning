150. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 30, 1940
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I am enclosing Bal's letter. He intended to send it direct.
I said if he insisted on sending it I would rather send it myself.
But attach no special significance to my sending it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8037. Courtesy: G.D. Birla
