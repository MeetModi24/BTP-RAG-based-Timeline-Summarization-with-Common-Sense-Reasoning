20. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
NEW DELHI,
October 22, 1946
CHI. A. S.,
I have your letter. I cannot always be sending you tele-
grams. I have said what I had to say. It is strange your
dragging Reddy and Anna with you. I have said 'No' to
them. What can they do? I cannot see what even you will do.
I merely told Jajuji that if he felt so inclined, he might give his
consent. Everyone is free, and may go anywhere in exercise of
that freedom. But not on my behalf or the Ashram's behalf.
Blessings from
BAPU
