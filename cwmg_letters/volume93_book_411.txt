411. LETTER TO HANNA LAZAR
[AS AT] WARDHA,
July 24, 1939
MY DEAR HANNA,
I was delighted to have your letter after so many months.
I have gone through your old letter of which you have sent me
a copy.
Just at present you need no guidance. Your story is incom-
plete. But you will soon complete it. Whatever may be the story,
the remedy is the same. As in the body so in the spirit though
the diseases are various the cause is one and so is the remedy
one, so in the spirit world, the cause of distemper is one and
therefore the remedy also is one. Every spiritual distemper is a
fleeing away from God. The surest remedy therefore is to seek the
presence of God so much so that we see Him face to face. In the
presence of God, there can be no sin, no sorrow, no anger, no
malice, no falsehood. In His presence there is no fear, no exter-
nal affliction can put us out. If this lesson of lessons is learnt
there is no question, no doubt left.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy:
National Archives of India
