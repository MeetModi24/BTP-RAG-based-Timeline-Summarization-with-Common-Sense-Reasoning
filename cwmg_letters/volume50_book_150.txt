150. LETTER TO RADHA GANDHI
July 2, 1932
DEAR RADHIKA 2 ,
Your letter to hand. As Premkuver is involved in great diffi-
culties, there is little possibility of her going there. Hence, are
you, mother and daughter only there? Who does the shopping,
etc.? It does not seem that any of the male members are there.
Looking to the conveniences of that place, there may not be
any need of them. How much do you spend? Have you kept any
account? Is there pipe water? Is there any news about Rupi
again? It seems that you have made good progress.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRIMATI RADHABEHN GANDHI
BHATIA SANATORIUM
DEOLALI
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. II, p. 111
