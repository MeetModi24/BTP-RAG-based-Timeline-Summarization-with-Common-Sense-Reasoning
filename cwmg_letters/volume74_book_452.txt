452. LETTER TO FARID ANSARI
SEVAGRAM,
August 31, 1941
DEAR FARID,
I have your letter with Brijkrishna's footnote.
I have written to Satyawati inviting her to come to Sevagram.
No reply.
Jamnalalji went to Rajkumari's home for health. He is now
in Anandamayi Devi's Ashram near Dehra [Dun]. He will be
returning about 21st September. He is well.
Radhabehn has passed, is well and gaining experience as a
pathologist.
Sardar is out of immediate danger but not out of the wood.
No cause for anxiety.
The rest you know.
My regards to all.
Love.
BAPU
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
