301. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
August 2, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
Kanu has frightened me. He says you flatly refuse to take
medicine. This is not right. Sushila and Amritlal are going there.
Having heard that Mahodaya needs help, I am sending Sushila.
Take whatever medicine she gives, either oral or for external
application. I have told her that she must give no medicine
containing non-vegetarian or alcoholic ingredients.
Blessings from
BAPU
