492. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 22, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
There is a Sanskrit party and a translation party. Both
equally command my allegiance. Because we are social ani-
mals we ought to find joy in collective recitation. Ultimately an
individual should choose whichever way helps him to rise higher.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4488
