343. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
September 12, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
Prema's letter accompanies this. You must be getting news
of my illness from time to time. Neither Sardar nor Jamnalalji
will be able to go there. Whom else do you expect? I would
advise you not to bank upon anyone but be satisfied with whatever
you can do there. You should look upon my effort as absolutely
independent.
The contributions raised for the spinning fund you may use
for famine relief if a state of famine prevails. But then this is
only a suggestion.
Today I put my signature in the copy of the Gita required
for Purushottam and Vijaya's use.
I returned from the hospital to Segaon today. Ba and
Lilavati stayed back at Maganwadi.
Blessings from
BAPU
