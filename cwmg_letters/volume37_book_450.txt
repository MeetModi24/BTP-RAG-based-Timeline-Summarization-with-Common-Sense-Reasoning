450. LETTER TO SVENSKA KYRKANS
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 26, 1928
MESSRS SVENSKA KYRKANS
DIAKONISTYRELSES, HOKFORLAG, STOCKHOLM 7 (SWEDEN)
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am surprised that you have not yet
received my letter. The purport of my letter was that you were at
liberty to translate the Autobiography in the Swedish language
and that whatever you sent me would be utilized for some public
purpose.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 12783
