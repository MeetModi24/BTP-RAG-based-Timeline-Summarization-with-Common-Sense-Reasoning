246. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
POONA,
March 4, 1946
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Yesterday Rajkumari did reply to you. Today, however, I
am observing silence. I believe we should consume only cow's
ghee. Even Champabehn may not keep buffalo's ghee. The
Ashram does not exist for providing all facilities; we ought to
put up with difficulties. Then cow's ghee can be made available.
Vinoba should not be troubled for this. Yesterday Rajkumari must
have written to you about visiting him there from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 4547
