226. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
August 23, 1946
CHI. LILY,
I have your letter. One too much crushed by debts feels
not debts; one too much crushed by suffering feels not suffering.
What are you afraid of? I have told you that you have nei-
ther relatives nor friends till you have passed your examina-
tion. Your studies are your relatives. I leave for Delhi on
Sunday.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. LILAVATIBEHN
G. S. MEDICAL COLLEGE
LADIES' HOSTEL
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 10242. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
