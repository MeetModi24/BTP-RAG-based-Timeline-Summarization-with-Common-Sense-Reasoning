278. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
BARDOLI,
May 26, 1931
CHI. VASUMATI,
I had your letter. Let me know about the effect of the vaid's
medicine. What does he give you by way of medicine? For what
is Maitri being treated? Tell Mahavir and Maitri to write to
me. When is Gangabehn expected to come? For the present I
shall have to stay here. Ramdas and Nimu have reached Almora.
Rambhau has returned to the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9324. Also C.W. 570. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
