99. LETTER TO K. S. NATARAJAN
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 5, 1927
DEAR MR. NATARAJAN,
I enclose herewith a copy of my letter to Mr. Petit. I do not
need to add anything except to ask you please to expedite the
despatch of funds, if you agree with the substance of my letter
to Mr. Petit.
Yours sincerely,
K. S. NATARAJAN, ESQ.
"INDIAN SOCIAL REFORMER"
FORT
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 12365
