487. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1944
CHI. SITA,
I have your letter. The handwriting is beautiful. You should
make your letters slightly bigger. It has now grown pretty cold
here. Build up your body with the same devotion with which
you are pursuing your studies. Do not be lax about anything.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
SITA
C/O NANABHAI MASHRUWALA
AKOLA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
