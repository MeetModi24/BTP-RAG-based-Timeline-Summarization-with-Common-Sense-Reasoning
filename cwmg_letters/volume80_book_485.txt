485. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
June 9, 1945
CHI. A. S.,
I have your letter. I left Mahabaleshwar long ago. I shall
leave Panchgani too by the end of this month. I may leave even
earlier. I shall reach Sevagram in July. Do not think about
the six months. Think about your work. Finish it and come back.
Send Kanchan to Sodepur.
Sushilabehn has written. You do not remember and then
become impatient.
What should I do about Khan Saheb?
Blessings from
BAPU
