104. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
January 11, 1931
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. You did well in going round and meet-
ing everyone. Surendra was not keeping good health; has it
improved now? Since you don't write anything about Sita, I
believe that she is improving satisfactorily. Don't be parsimo-
nious in supplying her fruit. She must not become ill.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4782
