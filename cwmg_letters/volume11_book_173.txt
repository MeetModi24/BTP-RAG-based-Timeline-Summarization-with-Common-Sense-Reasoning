173. LETTER TO G. K. GOKHALE
TOLSTOY FARM,
LAWLEY STATION,
TRANSVAAL,
January 12, 1912
DEAR MR. GOKHALE,
It is difficult to give you an idea as to how delighted we all
were to read the announcement that you were coming here very
shortly.
I hope that you will give ample notice of the date of your
coming!
If it is at all possible, do you not think that it would be better
if you could come here on your way to London? You could then
study our question on the spot and materially assist us in London.
And I am vain enough to think that your stay here will result
in your taking with you to London much better health than you
would otherwise.
The Union Parliament will now be sitting and the promised
draft & bill may be published any day.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: G. N. 3771
