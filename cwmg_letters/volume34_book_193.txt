193. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 24, 1927
MY DEAR RAIHANA,
I have your letter. As you think that the matters between
you and Mother are fixed up, I am not writing to her or to Father,
but look to you to smooth your way, which you will do if you
know your own mind and adhere to it with firmness though
with greatest gentleness.
We are not bound to patronize any industry whatsoever. But
when in satisfying our vital wants we are bound to patronize
some industry, we naturally patronize that which supports our
neighbours who need our help most. This answers your question.
With love,
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a photostat: S.N. 9605
