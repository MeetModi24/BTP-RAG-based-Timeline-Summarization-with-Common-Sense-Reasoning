518. LETTER TO KALKA PRASAD
October 1, 1946
BHAI KALKA PRASAD,
Please sell khadi worth Rs. 50 to Tulsiram. You may
assume that he has tendered the necessary yarn. However,
please charge him the price of the yarn.
M. K. GANDHI
GANDHI ASHRAM
CHANDNI CHOWK
DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
