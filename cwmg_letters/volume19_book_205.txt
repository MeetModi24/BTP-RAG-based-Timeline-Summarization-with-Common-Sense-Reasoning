205. LETTER TO VARMA
MULTAN,
March 5 [1921]
DEAR MR. VARMA,
Your letter has followed me in my travels.
I am doing all I can to overtake the unruly tendency in the
boys. I am hoping that the unseemly exuberance will pass and
things will settle down and take their proper shape. Are we not all
in the melting-pot today? Perhaps we are not able to appreciate
merits and demerits of actions or their proper value. However
I must do what little I can to avoid a repetition of the disgraceful
scenes of Kashi. I am asking Pundit Jawaharlal Nehru to attend
to the matter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K.
From a photostat : S.N. 7978
