54. LETTER TO Y. BHASKARE
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
February 25, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am very sorry I am under strict medical
orders not to take up any new burdens however slight they may be
and even to reduce the existing responsibilities to the lowest possible
minimum. I do not therefore in this circumstance comply with
your request.
Yours sincerely,
MISS Y. BHASKARE
WOMEN'S CHRISTIAN TEMPERANCE UNION
742 NEAR PETIT HALL
POONA
From a microfilm: S.N. 13087
