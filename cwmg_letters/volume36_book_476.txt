476. LETTER TO PREMLILA THACKERSEY
June 11, 1928
DEAR SISTER,
I received Rs. 100 from you two days ago. I got your letter
only today. If we can still see to it that your name is not pub-
lished, we shall do so. This will be possible only if it has not
yet been published.
I am keeping well. When will you come again?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4812. Courtesy: Premlila
Thackersey
