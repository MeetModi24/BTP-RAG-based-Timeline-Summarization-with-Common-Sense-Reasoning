54. LETTER TO JAMNADAS GANDHI
BORSAD,
June 23, 1931
CHI. JAMNADAS,
When I asked whether the construction could not be post-
poned, I meant the proposed extension. I suggest that it may
be postponed till the end of this year, if possible.
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 9326. Courtesy: Jamnadas Gandhi
