97. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Wednesday [March 10, 1926]
BHAI TULSI MAHER,
I have been getting your letters. Since your whereabouts
were uncertain I did not write to you. I am keeping well. They
are planning to go to the hills in April. Are rice and vegetables
alone adequate for keeping up your bodily strength? You must
not ruin your health. Nowadays the Ashram is full.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6525
