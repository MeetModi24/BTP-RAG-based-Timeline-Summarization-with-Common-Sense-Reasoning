497. LETTER TO SHEELA
NEW DELHI,
September 28, 1946
CHI. SHEELA,
Your letter. Everyone did well. Do you ever observe
silence during daytime while you are awake? You should do it.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHEELABEHN
NAWABHARATI
MEERUT
