110. LETTER TO SIR CHARLES DILKE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 6, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I am extremely obliged to you for your letter of the 5th instant.
As suggested therein, I will not make use of the letter before Lord
Elgin.
I beg to remain,
Yours faithfully,
THE RIGHT HON'BLE SIR CHARLES DILKE, BART., M.P.
76, SLOANE STREET, W.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4493
