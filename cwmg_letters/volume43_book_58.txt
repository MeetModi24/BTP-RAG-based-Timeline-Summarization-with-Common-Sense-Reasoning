58. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
DURING DANDI MARCH,
[After March 12, 1930]
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letters. Find some work for Ranchhodbhai. It
can be manual labour. If you can find even some teaching work
discuss the matter with him and fix it up.
In this weather Punjabhai can sleep outside. If he does that,
mosquitoes at any rate will not bite him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8091. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
