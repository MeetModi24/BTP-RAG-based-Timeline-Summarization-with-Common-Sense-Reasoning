416. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEGAON,
July 29, 1939
STUPID GIRL,
I wrote to you twice during the train journey. Shankaran is
well. He is not allowed to go up and down. He eats well.
Mathew is at Balkrishna's place. He appears well. He came
walking all the way here.
You will have met Devdas. I am getting on well. Kanu
gave me massage and I dozed off. It is difficult to stop Ba from
working. You were able to stop her. Ba also was free from
care. Now she has to tend me. Let us see what happens. There
is no rain here. People are distressed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
