445. LETTER TO SHAMLAL
ABBOTTABAD,
July 11, 1939
DEAR LALA SHAMLAL,
I kept your letter of 6th April till today. I was glad you
were able to make up your mind. I hope the vow is being kept
and that the keeping of it is doing you good.
Yours,
BAPU
LALA SHAMLAL, M. L. A. (CENTRAL)
From a photostat: G.N. 1288
