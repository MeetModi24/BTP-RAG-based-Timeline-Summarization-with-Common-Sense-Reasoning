515. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
SEVAGRAM,
June 5, 1940
DEAR PREMI,
What a bad girl you are? You have not written to me for
ages. What was your promise? How is Father?
Love.
BAPU
SHRI PREMIBEHN
C/O SHRI JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
HYDERABAD, SIND
From the original: C. W. 11053. Courtesy: Arjun Jairamdas
SV:III. 21
