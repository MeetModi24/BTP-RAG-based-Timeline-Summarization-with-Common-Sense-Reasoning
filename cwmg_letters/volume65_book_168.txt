168. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
May 8, 1937
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your letter. I am now going to Gujarat and shall
reach Tithal on the 12th. The best thing would be to obtain
a full account from the gentleman and forward it to me. After
reading it, I shall, if I think it necessary, send for him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2453
