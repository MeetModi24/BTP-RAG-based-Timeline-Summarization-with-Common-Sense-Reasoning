160. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
October 5, 1934
CHI. PREMA,
I have an impression that I have not replied to your last
letter.
I was pleased to learn that you had fully understood my
statement. Your work seems to be progressing very well. Don't
widen its field. See that what you have started strikes deep roots.
In this poor land, people sow grass seeds and live on what grows
from them. Wheat and other grains are grass seeds. Since we
don't have enough patience to plant fruit trees, the poor never
get any fruit to eat.
And the rich don't eat enough of them.
They eat fruit only as dessert. Being similarly poor in the field of
service we are content to sow and reap grass. If even a few of us
save ourselves from committing this mistake, the fruit-trees which
grow will give shade and generation after generation will eat the
fruits. This will do for today.
Blessings from
BAPU
