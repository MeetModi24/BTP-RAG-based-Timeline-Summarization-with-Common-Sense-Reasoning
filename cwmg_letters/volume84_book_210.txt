210. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
NEW DELHI,
May 19, 1946
BETI A. S.,
I have your two letters. I am writing this at 6 o'clock in
the morning in the midst of other work.
My advice is, do whatever you think is your duty. You are
away from me, so how can I know what is right and what is
not? Try and persuade your sister-in-law. Your brother is dead;
now do whatever you can about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 517
