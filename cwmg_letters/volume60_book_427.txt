427. LETTER TO SECRETARY, POLITICAL DEPARTMENT,
GOVERNMENT OF BENGAL
[On or after March 19, 1935]
THE SECRETARY To THE GOVERNMENT
OF BENGAL
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for your letter (No. 8043x) of the 19th instant
regarding my letter asking for permission to Shri Mahadev Desai
to interview detenu Shri Dhirendra Chandra Mukherjea at present
detained in Deoli Jail.
Yours truly,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
