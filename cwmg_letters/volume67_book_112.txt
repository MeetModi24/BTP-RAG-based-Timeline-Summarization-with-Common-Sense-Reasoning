112. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
VISHRAM VATIKA,
JUHU (P.O. SANTACRUZ),
BOMBAY,
May 16, 1938
MY DEAR C. R.,
I have gone through the salty note. If you mean that I
should take notice of the flagrant breach, I shall do so. If you
mean anything more, please be clear. Are we breaking up by
the sheer weight of our own folly or worse? You must give
yourself a moment's leisure and think out where we are drift-
ing to. Don't say you have no time. I want to go away to-
morrow or as early as I can consistently with the business here.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2069
