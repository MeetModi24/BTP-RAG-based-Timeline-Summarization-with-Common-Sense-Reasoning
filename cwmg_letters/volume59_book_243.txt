243. LETTER TO MEHERBAI F. S. TALYARKHAN
October 18, 1934
DEAR SISTER,
I had your touching letter. I can thoroughly understand your
grief. But you must now bravely turn that grief into energy for
serving the nation. I know that you have in abundant measure
the spirit of service in you.
Yet that measure should now be
increased, and you will then be able to merge your sorrow in the
larger sorrow of the suffering humanity. Of course, my prayers
are with you for the departed soul.
MRS. MEHERBAI F. S. TALYARKHAN
29 NEW MARINE LINES
BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
