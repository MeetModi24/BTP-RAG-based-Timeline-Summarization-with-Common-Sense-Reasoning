507. LETTER TO H. V. GLASENAPP
February 23, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have just got your letter addressed to the Secretary of the
Ashram at Sabarmati. I have no knowledge of the book called
India by Luciano Magrini. I have not read the book. I do not
know Italian and I have never given any opinion on the book
to anybody, and so far as my knowledge goes, I have seen no
notice of the book in the Indian newspapers.
Yours sincerely,
H. V. GLASENAPP, ESQ.
PROFESSOR OF SANSKRIT
KOENIGSBERG UNIVERSITY (GERMANY)
