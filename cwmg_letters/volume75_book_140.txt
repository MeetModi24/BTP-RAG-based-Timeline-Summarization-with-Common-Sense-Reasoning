140. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
November 11, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I hope you received my letter.
Yes, undhi[y]u will be available. I have been forgetting to write
to you about it.
Radhabai Subbaroyan is here. She will leave for Madras
tomorrow.
Here is a letter for you. I think it is from Mridula She has
forgotten to sign the letter.
I am glad you will be bringing someone. Don't make up your
mind to send him away at once. But you shall decide.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4116. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7425
