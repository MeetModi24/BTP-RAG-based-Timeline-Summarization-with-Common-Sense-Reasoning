177. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
BARDOLI,
May 10, 1937
CHI. NARANDAS,
Do you still receive every three months or six months the
remittance on Mirabehn's account? If so, what is the amount?
How much in all must you have received?
You must have got my letter regarding Kamalabai. Manu
has joined me from Akola. I, therefore, have a big enough crowd
accompanying me. One or two more may still join. Kanaiyo
of course is in the party, together with his dilruba. Manu has
brought her sitar.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
We reach Tithal on the 12th. Address your reply there.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8520. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
