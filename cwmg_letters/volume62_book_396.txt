396. LETTER TO CARL BUTTO
April 23, 1936
I have just received your interesting letter. Yes, I am fol-
lowing the events in Germany and the other parts of Europe.
It is all confusion worse confounded. It is a mere trial of phy-
sical strength, it is also a trial of diplomacy of the worst kind
ever experienced in history. As you know my attempt is
wholly on a different plane. I can show nothing and yet my
faith is growing stronger—more so for the events of Europe.
Love.
BAPU
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy:
Narayan
Desai
