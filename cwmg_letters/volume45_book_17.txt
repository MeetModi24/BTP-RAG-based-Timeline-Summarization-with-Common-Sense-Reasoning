17. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
December 21, 1930
CHI. MAHAVIR,
You may ask me any question that may arise in your mind.
I have plenty of time here to reply to people's questions. If we
cannot overcome all our shortcomings at a time, we may first over-
come those which we can easily remove. If we proceed thus, we
shall gradually be able to overcome all of them. Your handwriting
cannot be described as bad, but you can improve it and make it
better still.
Blessings from
BAPU
