400. LETTER TO PURATAN J. BUCH
BOMBAY,
June 28, 1939
CHI. PVRATAN,
Amtulbehn has been working among the Muslim women.
Both of you should help her as much as you can without allowing
your Harijan work to suffer.
I did not like it at all that Anandi did not come along
beyond the Sabarmati station. I very much wanted to enjoy a few
minutes' fun with her and was disappointed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9173
