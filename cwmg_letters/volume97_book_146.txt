146. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
ASHRAM SABARMATI,
January 23, 1930
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I have your letter. Chhaganlal and Ramniklal are immediately leaving
the Ashram because of the new rules. I do not want to go deep into the
reasons for their leaving as I do not have the time. I have received your
letter regarding Chandradutt. He is not at all ready to go to Vijapur. He started
crying when I talked to him about it. What was the use of sending him against
his wishes? So I am keeping him here. I will see what I can do. I am unable
to look after him. From what you write, it seems that the situation is difficult.
I hope you do know that Bhagirathji is here.
Keep making efforts to improve your health. Do not worry about us. I
will go through the Ramayana. Ba sends her blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32946
