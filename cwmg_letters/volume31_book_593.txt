593. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SABARMATI,
November 1, 1926
MY DEAR C.R.,
I have your note. I understand how your boys are shaping.
When boys grow old, they must be allowed to go their way.
If I were you and Narasinharao could not help running into the
trap, I should let him have his way, only, I would not support
him in the trap.
These elections are an awful business. You are well out of
them.
I am not religiously against quinine. I have been using it
freely in the Ashram. I should be chary of using it for myself.
Heaven only knows whether I am going to Gauhati or not.
But I suppose I will have to go. I have no heart in going there.
My work lies in the Ashram and I see my use here. However,
a month hence is too long a time for one to speculate about.
Are you keeping all right? Can you walk 50 miles at a
stretch? Can you wrestle with Mahadev or even me? A fair test!
Sickness is now clearing. Am off to Bombay today for a
day to see Sir Ganga Ram and others and preach the wheel.
BAPU
From a photostat: S.N. 19727
