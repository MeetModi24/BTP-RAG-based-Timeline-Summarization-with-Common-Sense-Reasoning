132. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 21, 1939
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
Now you will have no occasion to complain about the ab-
sence of letters from me. But that will be because of the strug-
gle. Remember that both of you have to sacrifice your all
there. Once the struggle starts it won't end soon. What will
you do about the children? You must have thought about
every contingency. If you cannot keep them there, then Sushila
will perhaps have to keep out of the struggle and bring over the
children here. Or, if she has the ability, she may single-handed
run Indian Opinion. Thus think out your plans beforehand.
Here everything is all right. And, besides, what time can
you get now to wonder about what is happening here?
Blessings from
BAPU
