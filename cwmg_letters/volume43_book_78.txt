78. LETTER TO DURGA GIRI
[On or after March 15, 1930]
CHI. DURGA,
I have your beautiful letter written in a beautiful hand. What
you write about Mahavir is correct. He who regards all as equals
wins. Kaka's Shankar too has joined the caravan. Immerse your-
self fully in works of service. Do not be lazy about anything. We
are all well here. Tell Maitri not to be indolent in the least. Does
she dip her hands in potassium permanganate solution? She must
observe restraint in her diet.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Virat Vatsalta, pp. 30-
