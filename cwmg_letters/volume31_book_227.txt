227. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 28, 1926
I am glad you agree with me about your South African
article. I did not want to weary you with your opium article,
because there was nothing wrong with it as the South African.
I am sure this rest from constant mental toil of writing will
do you much good. We shall all be looking forward to your
coming here in September. Devdas is still in Mussoorie where
he is doing well and is also helping Punditji.
Yours,
REV. C. F. ANDREWS
SHANTINIKETAN
From a photostat: S.N. 19675
