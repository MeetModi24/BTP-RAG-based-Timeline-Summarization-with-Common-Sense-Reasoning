259. LETTER TO SUHASINI NAMBIAR
As AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 13, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
You have given me a big commission. Whatever answer I
may give will be incomplete unless I enter into details for which
I have no time. I am sorry. But if you will refer to the pages
of Young India, you will certainly find the majority of these ques-
tions answered and for the rest you will be able to frame your
own answers after having read the Young India articles. They are
to be had in book-form, except the current year numbers, from
Sjt. S. Ganesan, 18 Pycrofts' Road, Triplicane, Madras.
Yours sincerely,
SHRIMATI SUHASINI NAMBIAR
