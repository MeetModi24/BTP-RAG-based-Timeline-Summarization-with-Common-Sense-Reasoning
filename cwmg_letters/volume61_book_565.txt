565. LETTER TO GOWAMAL
September 6, 1935
BHAI GOWAMAL,
I have had no letter from Padmavati. I don't remember
at all about the blessings. If she got them, I can't tell in what
circumstances she did. You should not waste my time about such
matters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Gujarati: Mahatma Gandhi aur Jabalpur, p. 47
