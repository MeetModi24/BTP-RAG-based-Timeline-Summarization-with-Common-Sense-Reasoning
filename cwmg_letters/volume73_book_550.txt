550. LETTER TO ADVAITA KUMAR GOSWAMI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
March 24, 1941
BHAI ADVAITA KUMAR,
I am replying to your letter belatedly as I could get no time.
What can I write about the condition there? I cannot write to
Baba Raghavdas, Mohanlalji and others. So try to do your best.
Yours,
M. K.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 151
