430. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
February 26, 1942
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
Very many thanks for your kind letter and all the trouble
you took over the matter about the A. I. S. A. referred to you by
me. Your decision tides over my present difficulty.
Your postscript breaks the pervading gloom. I wish the general
public had the privilege of knowing that your cheerfulness never
forsakes you. May God be with you always.
I am,
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
The Transfer of Power, Vol. I, p. 254
