492. LETTER TO J. FRIEND LODGE
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 15, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I send you the
autograph herewith. The name of the photographer is Mahulikar,
Ritchie Road, Ahmedabad.
Yours sincerely,
J. FRIEND LODGE, ESQ.
'SUNSET'
BUSTLETON
PHILA.
From a photostat: S.N. 12520
