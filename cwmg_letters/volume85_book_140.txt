140. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
August 8, 1946
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have read your letter and copies of the correspondence
with . . . .1 I shall know more when I have further information.
If you can take care of your health I shall consider it as part
of the service rendered by you. But you being the Sardar,
who may tell you to do anything?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 324
