319. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 18, 1940
CHI. RAMDAS,
How are you keeping now? Has the load on your mind lightened? Are
you able to absorb yourself in your work? I hope you are not short of money,
are you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
