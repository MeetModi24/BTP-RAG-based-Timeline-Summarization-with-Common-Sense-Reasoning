275. LETTER TO THAKORE
LAHORE,
January 25, 1920
DEAR MR. THAKORE,
I cannot say anything about your work in England beyond
saying that you should be accurate in your information and
moderate but firm in your demand; extravagance in either case
weakens the cause. I am unable to send you any papers because
I have nothing with me here. I take it that you will see
Mr. Polak and be guided by him. I wish you all success.
Yours sincerely,
From the handwritten pencil draft: S.N. 7027(e)
