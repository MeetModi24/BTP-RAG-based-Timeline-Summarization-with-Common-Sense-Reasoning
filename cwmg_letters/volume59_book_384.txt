384. LETTER TO HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
November 14, 1934
MY DEAR MAHTAB,
I have your letter. I think you have to put up with your
wife. You have to take her with you.
With reference to your proposed gift I would like you to
think it over well before you do the thing. But if both you and
your wife have decided to make the gift, I shall thankfully accept
it provided that you would work at the place in conjunction with
a committee.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
KARMA-MANDIR, AGARAPARA
BARHAT-TIRLOCHANPUR P. O., via BHADRAK (ORISSA)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
