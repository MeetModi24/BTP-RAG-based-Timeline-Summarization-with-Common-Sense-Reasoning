117. LETTER TO CHARU PROBHA SEN GUPTA
June 24, 1934
DEAR CHARU PROBHA,
You should live where you think best. Your son has come
to a wise decision. Living honourably is to live on one's own
body labour. That is or should be our ideal. We can only go
near it as much as we can.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI CHARU PROBHA SEN
RAJBARI, BENGAL
From a photostat: G.N. 8707. Also C.W. 1493. Courtesy: A. K. Sen
