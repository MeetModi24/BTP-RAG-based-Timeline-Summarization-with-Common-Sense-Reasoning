201. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
June 7, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Yes, when you suspect a dear one, it is your duty to speak
out. Suspicion is imaginary. It indicates the evil in you. It
is one's duty to suppress it. But when it grows too strong and
looks as if it might have consequences, it is necessary to give
expression to it. My pain arises out of the fact that a suspicion
should arise in my heart. There have been such occasions
earlier and my suspicions proved right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4349; also S.G. 84
