62. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
WARDHA,
May 9, 1935
CHI. MARY,
Here is a letter from Dr. Tandy. Her bill came to Rs. 87.
I have sent word to discharge it.
What about her proposal for you to go to Miraj? If you give
yourself full rest and take proper nourishment, nothing more may
be necessary. Do write to me fully please.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Did I tell you Chhotelal had gone to Coimbatore to learn
bee-keeping? I see that the money order has been sent to you. I
am sorry. You have to send the money to Dr. Tandy.
From a photostat: G.N. 6040. Also C.W. 3372. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
