1. LETTER TO VISHWANATH
WARDHA,
October 1, 1935
MY DEAR VISHWANATH,
I have your note. I shall await your arrival on the 5th
instant. If you do not see anybody to receive you at the station,
you will ask your way to Maganwadi which is about under ten
minutes' walk from the station. Someone will be there, however,
to receive you.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
I am not writing to Satis Babu.
SJT. VISHWANATH
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
15 COLLEGE SQUARE
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 2731
