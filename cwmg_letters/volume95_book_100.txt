100. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
[About March, 1930]
CHI. NARANDAS,
I don't worry at all, since you are not likely to feel any burden.
I see that guests will continue. Please look after all those who come
properly. Dissuade anyone who may wish to follow me.
Shri. Mullick is the Municipal Engineer. He will go there in a
day or two and advise you on how to increase the supply of water. See
the Chandrabhaga also. I have talked to Shri Mullick about Parnerkar.
Get acquainted with him and introduce Parnerkar also to him.
What is Purushottam doing? Will he go for a change of air? If
he had keen well, I would certainly have taken him with me. If you
think it is necessary to consult me about anything please do so
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro - Narandas Gandhine, Part I, p.273

101 LETTER TO TRILOKCHANDRAJI
Wednesday [April 2, 1930]
MUNISHRI TRILOKCHANDRAJI,
I feel that you need not join the satyagraha but should propa-
gate its teaching to all in clear terms.
It will not be necessary to keep a watch as you suggest when the
satyagraha becomes widespread. The latent power of truth and non-
violence will act like electric current and keep the people under
control.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Jainprakash, Margashirsha, 1987
