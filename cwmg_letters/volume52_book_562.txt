562. LETTER TO K. MADHAVAN NAIR
January 10, 1933
MY DEAR MADHAVAN,
I enclose herewith a copy of a letter from Mrs. Ponnammal
and a copy of my letter to her. Do please try to see her, and if
you feel that she is a deserving woman, she ought to be relieved
of the burden that she has, and her very simple wants must be
assured to her. She seems to me to be a worker of a rare type, but
from this distance I may be exaggerating her merits. You will
please guide me.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19207
