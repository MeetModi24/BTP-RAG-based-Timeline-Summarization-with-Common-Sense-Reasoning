149. LETTER TO G. RAMACHANDRAN
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 4, 1928
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRAN,
I had your letter dated 8th ultimo. You must give me a
regular fortnightly letter. It is only because Vallabhbhai has
called me to Bardoli that I am able to overtake arrears.
Devdas is in Delhi. Surendra is now trying to become an
expert tanner. The common kitchen has now 150 diners. Ba,
Mahadev and Pyarelal are in Bardoli, apart from others.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13905
1 In reply to his letter (S.N. 13879) dated July 9, 1928, suggesting that
the Satyagraha Ashram should be self-contained and not maintained by
contributions from friends
