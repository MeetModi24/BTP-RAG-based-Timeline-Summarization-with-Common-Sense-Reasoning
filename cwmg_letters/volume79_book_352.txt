352. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
March 6, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
What do you mean when you say, "Leave aside the question
of sleeping together"? I do not know how to solve your difficulty.
Why don't you explain it to me more clearly? There is no need
now for Balvantsinha to go to Nathji. But if there is, let him go
with such other persons as you choose to send. Come over at 3.30
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10618
