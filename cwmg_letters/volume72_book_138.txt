138. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 28, 1940
CHI. MIRA,
I do not think the stay at Oel was a waste. You are
gaining valuable experience and keeping well in body and
mind. I don't mind the expense. Do as the spirit moves you.
It will be good if you can persuade Punditji to join you. Fright-
ful things are happening in the West. God's will be done.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6454. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10049
