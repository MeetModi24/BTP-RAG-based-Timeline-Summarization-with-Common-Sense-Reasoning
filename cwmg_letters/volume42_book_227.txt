227. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
BANDA,
November 21, 1929
CHI. VASUMATI,
Jamnalalji informed me about your having arrived at Wardha.
During your stay there regain your health completely and have
no hesitation at all in asking for whatever things you may find
necessary for the purpose. While you are there, finish your study
of the Gita, etc., which you may have planned to do.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9271
