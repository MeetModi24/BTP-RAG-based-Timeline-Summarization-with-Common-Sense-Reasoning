474. LETTER TO MOTWANI
July 26, 1947
DEAR MOTWANI,
I have your letter of the 18th inst. I have not mixed up
socialism with sociology. I must confess I have read very little
if anything at all about sociology.
Socialism as conceived in the West may go against Indian
culture. Then it is faulty but socialism as defined by me in the
articles you have read, is a living thing and true. It is un-
doubtedly an economic doctrine but not based on class warfare.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
