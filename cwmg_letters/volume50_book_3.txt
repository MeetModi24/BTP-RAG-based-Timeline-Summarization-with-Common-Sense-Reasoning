3. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
June 1, 1932
BHAI RAMESHWAR,
I got both your postcard and your letter. The conclusion
which you have drawn from my letter is correct. If, instead of
attempting a big leap, we take small steps according to our capa-
city, our enthusiasm for the work will increase and with that our
self-confidence. I am not worried about Jamnalalji's loss of
weight, as he otherwise keeps good health. And, as for Vinoba,
let him refrain from milk and ghee so long as he can maintain
his health and strength. I am satisfied with the message which
he has sent.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 179
