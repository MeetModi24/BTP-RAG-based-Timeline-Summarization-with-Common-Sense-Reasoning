282. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
June 25, 1935
CHI. MARY,
I am glad you are daily improving. Therefore if once in a
while they induce fever in order to complete their test, I suppose
we may not grudge them.
Chhotelal has not yet turned up. Hakim's medicine did no
good. I didn't expect any.
Love.
Blessings from
BAPU 2
From a photostat: G.N. 6048. Also C.W. 3378. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
