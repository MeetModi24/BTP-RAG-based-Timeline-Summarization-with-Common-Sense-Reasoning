61. LETTER TO G. A. NATESAN
YERAVDA CENTRAL PRISON,
July 22, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
I have been given your letter with enclosures. Many thanks.
You may depend upon my doing my best. I had a cable from
Shastriar. I am praying for light, but as yet I do not see a
ray in this impenetrable darkness.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. G. A. NATESAN
"INDIAN REVIEW"
GEORGE TOWN, MADRAS
From a photostat: G.N. 2236
