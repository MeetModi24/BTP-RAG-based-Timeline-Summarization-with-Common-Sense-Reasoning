257. LETTER TO GHANSHYAM SINGH GUPTA
August 23, 1945
BHAI GHANSHYAM SINGH,
I received your letter here. I have gone through it. How
long can I go on pointing out the mistakes? I am of the
opinion that you should do whatever you can. Now Deshbandhu
and Brijlal have gone there. Let us see what they do. After
that you can come to me if you want to. I would advise you
to do on your own what you feel is proper.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
After writing the above I got your letter. I have made some
corrections. You may make them if you think they are proper
and send it.
BAPU
SHRI GHANSHYAM SINGH GUPTA
SPEAKER, DRUG (C. P.)
