566. LETTER TO SIR STAFFORD CRIPPS
NEW DELHI,
October 10, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
Three letters I am writing after the morning prayer. The
other two are to the Lawrences.
Many thanks to you two for your wishes. I am here only
for the work you expect me to do. Heaven help us all.
Hope you are really well and strong.
With love,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR STAFFORD CRIPPS
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 218
