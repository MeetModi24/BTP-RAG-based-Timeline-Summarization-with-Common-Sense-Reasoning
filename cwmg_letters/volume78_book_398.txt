398. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
November 13, 1944
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
You should go on the 18th. Things will be managed somehow
here. The kitchen here must not come in the way of other work.
I have seen Satis Babu's letter and I have written to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1948
