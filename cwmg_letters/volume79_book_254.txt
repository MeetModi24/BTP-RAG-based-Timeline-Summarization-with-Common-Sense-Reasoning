254. LETTER TO MAHADEV A. HINGORANI
February 20, 1945
CHI. MAHADEV,
I was glad to read your letter. There are some mistakes in
it. Never mind. It is not S11~T but q\l~TI and not fu&,(i but ~2.
It is good to read Jap Saheb at night. I have seen your photo-
graph. I wrote my blessing on it too.
Blessings from
BAPU
[Ps.]
Your legs have no muscles. Do some exercise. You seem to
be weak.
