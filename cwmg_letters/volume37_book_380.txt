380. LETTER TO ROLAND J. WILD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 7, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to receive your letter. I am sorry Mr. Wilson
has not been able to visit Sabarmati. I am sending you a tele-
gram tomorrow, today being Sunday, as follows:
"Your letter. You will be welcome any time. Advisable
your coming Ahmedabad before going Bardoli. Letter fol-
lows."
I expect to be in the Ashram during the whole of the month.
You will be therefore welcome whenever you arrive. On Mon-
days I take my silence as you may be aware.
I wonder if you will stay with us at the Ashram. Ours is you
may know a meagre board and extremely simple life as it may
appear to you. But if you can accommodate yourself to the life
of the Ashram, of course we should all be delighted to have you
in our midst.
I quite agree with you that it will be better for you to visit
the Ashram before going to Bardoli.
Yours sincerely,
ROLAND J. WILD, ESQ.
ASSISTANT EDITOR, "THE PIONEER", ALLAHABAD
