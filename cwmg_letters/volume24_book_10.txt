10. LETTER TO V. G. DESAI
ANDHERI,
Thursday [May 8, 1924]
BHAISHRI VALJI4,
I have received the other material sent by you. The proofs
will certainly be sent to you. We, not you, are at the feet of the
Himalayas. You could describe only yourself as being "half
way to the Himalayas". You should know better than I about

Conference
2 The postmark bears this date.

editorial staff of Young India

to carry anything with me to the prison?
Vandemataram from
