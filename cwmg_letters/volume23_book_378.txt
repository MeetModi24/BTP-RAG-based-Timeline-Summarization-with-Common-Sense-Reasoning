378. LETTER TO OTANE JAKATA
[After April 30, 1924]
DEAR SIR,
Thank you for your letter as well as the book.
I have absolutely no time to give you the details you need,
even if I was minded to do so. I do not keep any portraits of
me nor do I give sittings. The recent photographs are all snap-
shots. The best books of reference are collections of my writings
in Y.I. by Mr. Ganesan of Madras (give address) and my speech-
es by Mr. G. A. Natesan also of Madras (address). The latter
contains rules of the Satyagraha Ashram.
Yours truly,
[OTANE JAKATA
45, KODA MACHO 4 CHOME
TAIHOKU, FORMUSA, JAPAN]
From a photostat: S.N. 8759
