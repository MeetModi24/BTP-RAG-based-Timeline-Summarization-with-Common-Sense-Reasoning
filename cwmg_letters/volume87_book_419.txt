419. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SODEPUR,
May 13, 1947
CHI. SATIS BABU,
Charu is leaving earlier as you are not well. As it is, his
presence was not necessary [here]. He makes himself useful in
many ways. But this is my home. My convenience is looked after
well enough. The real centre is there . No one should go away
from there. That is why Ajit did not come. Now have complete
rest and get well. I have no time to write anything else.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9081
