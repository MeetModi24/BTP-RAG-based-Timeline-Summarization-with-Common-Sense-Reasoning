286. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 6, 1926
DEAR RAJENDRA BABU,
Please go through the portion marked about untouchability
in the enclosed letter and let me know what the truth is.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1: That of Sjt. Rakhal Chandra Maity, Sadakat Ashram,
Dighaghat P.O., Patna
BABU RAJENDRA PRASAD
MURAD PUR
PATNA
From a microfilm: S.N. 19425
