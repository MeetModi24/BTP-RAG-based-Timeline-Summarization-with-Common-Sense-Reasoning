54. LETTER TO ANUPAM N. KAVI
SEVAGRAM,
August 22, 1944
BHAISHRI ANUPAM,
It is good that you opened my eyes. If you continue to
show me my errors in this way I shall correct them. At the
moment I feel ashamed neither on account of the Rajkot episode
nor the present one. My nature will make me act only in the
manner I do.
ANUPAM NANALAL KAVI
129 GREAT WESTERN BUILDINGS
BAKEHOUSE LANE
FORT, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
