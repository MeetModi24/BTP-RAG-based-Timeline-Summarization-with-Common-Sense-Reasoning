295. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
August 28, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have given you quite a lot of work. Take care of your
health in whatever you do. See that Shakaribehn also does not
lose her health through overwork. See about Anna and Kamala
If the two work sincerely, they can do very good work.
Tell Govinda Reddi that I had no time for talk with him.
I cannot tell you definitely when I shall return.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10641
