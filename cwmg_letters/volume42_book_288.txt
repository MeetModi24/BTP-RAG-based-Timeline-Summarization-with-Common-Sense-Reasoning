288. LETTER TO RAGHUNATH
[After December 14, 1929]
BHAI RAGHUNATH,
I wanted to have a talk with both of you but I had not a
moment to spare. And neither of you asked for it.
Do not worry over the future. The present duty well perform-
ed gives an indication of the future.
There are no objective tests of Truth. What our heart assents
to is Truth. In most cases we recognize Truth; but purity of heart
is essential for the correct perception of Truth. That is where
the yama niyama and other disciplines come in.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4215
