19. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
April 28, 1935
CHI. MANUDI 1 ,
All right, you may now even stop writing to me. If you
insist on my permission to go to Bombay, you have it provided
Aunt is agreeable.
I shall be content if you become happy and good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1541. Courtesy: Manubehn
S. Mashruwala
