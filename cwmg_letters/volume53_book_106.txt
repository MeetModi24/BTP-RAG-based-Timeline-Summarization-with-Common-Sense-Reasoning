106. LETTER TO HRIDAY NATH KUNZRU
January 18, 1933
MY DEAR HARIJI,
Here is a letter for you to read and deal with. I have
simply asked the writer to see you. Is there any truth in the
charge that all the Harijan candidates were unsuccessful at the
municipal election?
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18964
