269. LETTER TO A SISTER
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, Chaitra Vad [5] [April 3, 1926]
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. I share your grief. You may come here
with your husband or, if you send your husband, I shall surely
talk to him, try to bring him peace. He cannot stay here for long;
within a few days I myself have to go to Mussoorie. So if both
of you come here or your husband does, it should be immediately.
Do not lose your faith and forbearance. Seek happiness in the
midst of misery. You should not start from the wrong premise
that you can never attain the strength of Savitri .
