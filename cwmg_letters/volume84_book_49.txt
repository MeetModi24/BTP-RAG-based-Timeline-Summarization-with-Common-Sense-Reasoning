49. LETTER TO ALAGAPPA CHETTIAR
NEW DELHI,
April 21, 1946
BHAI ALAGAPPA CHETTIAR
Bapa tells me that you have been ailing for some time.
Why is it so? Do you recite Ramanama? Ramanama will bring
you peace if nothing more. Bapa is staying here. He had been
rather unwell. He is all right now.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
