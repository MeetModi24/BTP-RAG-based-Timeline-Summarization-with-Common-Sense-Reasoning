322. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
SEGAON,
[On or after] June [11]1, 1937
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I got your letter and the fruit too. Some apples had been
pilfered during transit in the railway.
The basket should be
packed properly. Next time you happen to send me fruit do
send sour limes. Good ones are not available here. The
Wardha sunshine is hot and strong.
I have already written about Gomati.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9610. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal.
