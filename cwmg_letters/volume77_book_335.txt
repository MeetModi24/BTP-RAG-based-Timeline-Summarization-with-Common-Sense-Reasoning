335. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
PANCHGANI,
July 28, 1944
CHI. KUSUM,
I have your letter. I hope to reach Sevagram on the 3rd.
I will not go to Bombay. I shall catch the train at Kalyan.
You may travel by that train. If you do, meet Shantikumar. I
myself am keen, of course.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KUSUMBEHN DESAI
MEHTA POLE
BARODA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1851
