437. LETTER TO VAIKUNTHLAL L. MEHTA
HARIJAN MANDIR,
April 6, 1946
BHAI VAIKUNTH,
I have your note. Balasaheb also saw me. Join the
[Bombay] Ministry for the sake of service. If you can afford
to forgo the salary, you may not accept it. Do what you
think best.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 1366
