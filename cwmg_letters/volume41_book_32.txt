32. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
Tuesday, June [11]1, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL JOSHI,
Vallabhbhai told me that the cheque for Rs. 900 which I
brought with me from Bardoli was not acknowledged in Young
India, and that when he made an inquiry and again after an
investigation into the matter, you wrote to him that you knew
nothing about it. Please let me know what the facts of the case
are. Now I sleep all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5421
