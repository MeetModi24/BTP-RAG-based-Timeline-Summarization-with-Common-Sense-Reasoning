607. LETTER TO VIJAYA
Aso Vad 8, October [18 2,] 1927
CHI. VIJAYA,
I was happy to read your letter. So was Ba. It is good
news that both of you are now well. Chi. Mathuradas and
Taramati are also well. Stay there for now. Devdas is gradually
recovering.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Chhaganlal Gandhi Papers. Courtesy : Sabar-
mati Sangrahalaya
