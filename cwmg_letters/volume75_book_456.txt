456. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
March 2, 1942
MY DEAR C. R.,
What you say is not brutal. Fear is writ large on our faces.
Fear of doing wrong, fear of running into the imaginary enemy's
trap, is all a species of fear and dangerous at that. However, you
have to submit till you patiently convert your companions. They
are the best material we have. And it is from that that we have
to weave the national fabric. What about the Quaid-e-Azam ?
I am counting the days against 20th.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2084
