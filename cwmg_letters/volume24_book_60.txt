60. LETTER TO ALI HASSAN
ANDHERI,
May 24, 1924
DEAR MR. HASSAN,
I thank you for your letter. I do not share your opinion that
Mussalmans have lost by Non-co-operation work. I share[the]
view that Mussalmans are [not] superior to Hindus in admini-
strative ability. On the general question you will find my views
developed from time to time in my writings.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a microfilm of a newspaper cutting: S.N. 10469
