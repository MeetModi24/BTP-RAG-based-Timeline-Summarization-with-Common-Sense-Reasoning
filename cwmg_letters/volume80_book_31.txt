31. LETTER TO GANGI A. HINGORANI
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 26, 1945
DAUGHTER GANGI,
Your letter is very good. It was good that Anand wrote a
letter. The doctor's treatment should continue. This is the
meaning of the letter. Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy : National Archives of India,
and Anand T. Hingorani
