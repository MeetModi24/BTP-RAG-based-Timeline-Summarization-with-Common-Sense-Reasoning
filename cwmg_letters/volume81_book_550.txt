550. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL SETALVAD
October 3, 1945
SIR CHIMANLAL,
Thanks for your good wishes.
For years I have not been taking any part in elections.
Hence I am entrusting your letter to Sardar. I seldom talk
with him about this.
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
