206. LETTER TO LILAVATI P. ASAR
PATNA,
August 8, 1947
CHI. LILI,
I received your letter yesterday on the train. I arrived here this
morning. I shall be off to Calcutta in the evening. You seem to be
facing many hardships. I have left Sushila at the Wah camp. She will
be free after the 17th. You will be really free from anxiety when
Dwarkadas has fully recovered. You are in the situation of a person
under heavy debts for whom a little additional debt makes no
difference.
Your ears will have been completely cured by now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
