390. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
March 9, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
See what happened to the wire I sent you. You had said
just "Sharma" would suffice and so I thought I would save an
anna. The telegram said: "Do come. No misunderstanding."
My decision was not the result of a misapprehension. In
view of your circumstances it seemed the correct thing. But do
come if that is your wish. I have written to Dr. Bhaskar about
the books. They should be arriving in a day or two.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
between pp. 130 and 131
