123. LETTER TO GAJANAN NAIK
SEVAGRAM,
January 26, 1945
CHI. GAJANAN,
Are you concerned with ownership or use? What if we could
have the use of the building for a certain rent? I hope you
have not assumed that I can do or get done anything that I fancy.
Blessings from
BAPU
GAJANAN NAIK
GULWADI
SEVAGRAM
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
