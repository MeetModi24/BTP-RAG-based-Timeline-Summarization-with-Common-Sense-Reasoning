226. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
May 20, 1937
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I continue to receive complaints about your health. Vijaya
writes and tells me that you have been losing weight. Vasumati
writes and says that you are not keeping good health.
You
yourself must find out the cause. You are not nursing any trou-
ble in your heart, are you? Write to me in detail.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10732
