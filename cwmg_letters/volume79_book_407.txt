407. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
March 12, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
We have had enough of this. You may not do anything for
Sushila but you should do everything for the hospital. If we have
one, we must put up with a good many things. Shushila cannot
see any other point of view, but ignore that. You should endure
what you can easily endure and leave what you cannot. It is a

condition for a peaceful life. Gita which at one place says "what
then will restraint avail" teaches restraint elsewhere. If you
understand this, your path will be easy.
BAPU
