330. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
POST ANDHERI,
April 12, 1924
MY DEAR RAJAGOPALACHARI,
Here is a copy of my letter to the Secretaries of the Kerala
Provincial Conference.
I sent you yesterday a copy of the draft about Council-entry
which I have prepared. I have not revised it, nor is it the final,
even so far as the matter is concerned. It is prepared in order to
enable the Swarajist friends to understand where I stand today.
Do try to attend the Working Committee and therefore come
earlier if it is at all possible.
Yours sincerely,
Encl.
SJT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
EXTENSION
SALEM
From a photostat : S.N. 8721
