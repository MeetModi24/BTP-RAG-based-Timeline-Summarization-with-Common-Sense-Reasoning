223. LETTER TO KRISHNA DAS
As AT SABARMATI,
May 21, 1931
MY DEAR KRISHNA DAS,
Your old familiar handwriting shows that you have made
remarkable progress. You will not be in a hurry to regain original
strength. You must have no temperature at all. At the end of
this dreadful sickness you should be stronger than you were before.
Continue to write to me regularly. We all leave here for Borsad
at the latest on Sunday.
SYT. KRISHNA DAS
SHAKTI ASHRAM
P.O. RAJPUR
(DT. DEHRA DUN),
