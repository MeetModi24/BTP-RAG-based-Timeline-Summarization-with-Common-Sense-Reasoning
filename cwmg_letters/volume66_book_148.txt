148. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
SEGAON,
September 8, 1937
CHI. BHANSALI,
Don't even dream of going away. You must not leave.
Your cave is here and so is your cremation-ground. Most pro-
bably Lilavati will return today. If not today, certainly within a
few days. Please write a note to her and give it to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8357. Also C.W. 7021. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
