13. LETTER TO GANGADEVI SANADHYA
January 12, 1932
CHI. GANGADEVI,
You and Panditji were in my mind often enough, but there
was no time for letter-writing. How are both of you and how is
your health now? Can Panditji still work hard? How is Hari-
prasad keeping?
What is the progress on Panditji's farm?
What fruits and
vegetables are being grown?
Is any produce being sent into the
city for sale? Can the farm stand on its feet?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2545
