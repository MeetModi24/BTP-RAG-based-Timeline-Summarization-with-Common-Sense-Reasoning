305. LETTER TO GOVIND DAS
PATNA,
April 22, 1947
BHAI GOVINDDASJI,
I have your letter. Congratulations on getting a grandson.
May your grandson have a long life and may he become a true
worker.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GOVIND DAs
RAJA GOKULDAS MAHAL
JUBBULPORE, C.P.
From a photostat of the Hindi: C. W. 10422. Courtesy: Seth Govind Das
