100. LETTER TO CHAND TYAGI
January 17, 1933
BHAI CHAND TYAGI,
I have your letter. I spend almost the whole day under the
sky and in the sun. The finger is all right. My diet consists of
milk, dates, oranges and, when available, papaya. Mirabehn is in
the Bombay jail. The observance of physical as well as mental
brahmacharya is made possible by keeping both body and mind
under control.
Chhaganlal Joshi too is with me. We are all doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3261
