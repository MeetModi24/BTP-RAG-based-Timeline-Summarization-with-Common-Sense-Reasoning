190. LETTER TO BAIJNATH MAHODAYA
SEVAGRAM,
October 2, 1944
BHAI BAIJNATH,
I read your long letter. Do whatever you think right in the
matter. It is beyond my understanding.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI BAIJNATH MAHODAYA
179 RAMBAGH
INDORE (C. I.)
