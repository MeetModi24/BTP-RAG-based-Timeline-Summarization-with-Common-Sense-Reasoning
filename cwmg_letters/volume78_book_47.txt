47. LETTER TO ANUGRAHA NARAYAN SINHA
SEVAGRAM,
August 19, 1944
BHAI ANUGRAHA BABU,
In a worthy cause the effort carries its own blessing. Your
work is noble, no doubt, still, if you need my blessings, you
have them. I hope Shrikrishna Babu and you are in good
health. Write to me about Rajendra Babu.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
