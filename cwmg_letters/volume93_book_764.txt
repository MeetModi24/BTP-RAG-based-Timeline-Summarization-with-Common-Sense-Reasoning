764. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
June 23, 1941
CHI. LILI,
You will have reached safely. Concentrate fully on your
studies. I do miss you. Had I permitted M., he would have
come at 8.15 and wasted valuable time.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI LILAVATI UDESHI
KANJI KHETSI CHHATRALAYA
65 MINT ROAD, BOMBAY FORT
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
