168. LETTER TO S. V. KAMAT
November 28, 1935
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am sending it to Sjt. Gangadharrao
for his reply. On receipt of it I shall write to you further.
I cannot recall all the conversations that take place with
friends.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
