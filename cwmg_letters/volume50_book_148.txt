148. LETTER TO VIDYA
July 2, 1932
DEAR VIDYA1,
I feel gratified that you have reached Quetta. Take the ful-
lest advantage of the weather there and walk as far as you can.
Give me an account of the scenery and climate. Who are the
people living there?
What you write is indeed true. If the intention is good the
mind is pleased. May your pleasure go on increasing and remain
permanent.
Please let me know if you have any information about
Anand. How do you pass your time there?
Blessings from
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. 11, p. 73
