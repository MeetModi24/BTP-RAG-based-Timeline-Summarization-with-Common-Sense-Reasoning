18. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
November 7, 1936
CHI. SHANKERLAL,
I went through the notice issued on behalf of the Majoor
Mahajan. It is all right. Be very watchful of every step you
take and having taken it you should not waver.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
