338. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
Bhadrapad Vad 14 [September 27, 1924]
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
The letter from the Ashram is being sent through you, that
is, through the students. I am the leader of the Ashram, that
is, its teacher. I know at least how difficult it is to be a teacher.
When I remember my student days, I realize that the life of
a student is a thousand times easier. In the life of a student
action and worship, that is, devotion, are predominant and they
shall be predominant.
1 The date, corresponding to Bhadrapad Vad 14, has been supplied in the
source.
Impatience and lack of faith are at the root of the desire to
solve too many problems at the same time. My advice to stu-
dents is that they should engage themselves in action with pa-
tience and faith and believe that they will get everything out of
it. Consider how devotees manage to get everything they desire
from their worship of clay idols.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Ashram Bhajanono Swadhyay, p. 14
