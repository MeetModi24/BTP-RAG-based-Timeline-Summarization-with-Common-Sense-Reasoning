177. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 28, 1933
Nobody has complained so much against your nature that
I should think you to be an unworthy man. Is it likely that I
would think well or ill of you on the basis of what somebody
might tell me?
Can I know you only through what others say
about you? Wake up from your slumber.
Come here and
lighten your heart. It makes me unhappy to think that you are
unhappy.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, pp. 138-
