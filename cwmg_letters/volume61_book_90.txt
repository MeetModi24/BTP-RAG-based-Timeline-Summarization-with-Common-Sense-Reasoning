90. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
May 14, 1935
BHAI MOOLCHAND,
It is a mistaken notion that every wrong can be righted
if it appears in the pages of Harijan. Great effort will be
required to eliminate the evil custom of child-marriage.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 756
