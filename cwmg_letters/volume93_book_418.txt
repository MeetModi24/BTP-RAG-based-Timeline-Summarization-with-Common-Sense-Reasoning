418. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEGAON,
July 31, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have written you three letters. I could not write yester-
day. I have received your letter. It does not acknowledge
receipt of my letters. I wrote from Mathura, Betul and Segaon.
You must stay there and give your whole heart to the work. It
is good that you are finding some novelty in the atmosphere
there. Kanu gives me massage every day. I also bathe regu-
larly. The blood-pressure is not checked every day. Today
Mahodaya checked it. The reading was 155/90.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
