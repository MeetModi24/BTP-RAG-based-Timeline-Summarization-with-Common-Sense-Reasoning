176. LETTER TO VEERSUT
ASHRAM,
March 23, 1926
BHAI VEERSUT,
I have your letter. You ask for details. It goes without say-
ing that every boy should undertake a one-year vow. If this is
not clear it may be added to the vow in so many words. Vows
for grown-ups are also for the same duration. I think it quite
proper for a person to preach and practise something that strikes
him as a duty. We need teachers to keep the children on the right
path. I do not see any impropriety in what Bhai Gopalrao is
teaching the children.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
Dakshinamoorti
Bhavnagar
