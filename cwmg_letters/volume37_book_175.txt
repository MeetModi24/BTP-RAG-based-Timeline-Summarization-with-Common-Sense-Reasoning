175. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Monday [August 6, 1928]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got three postcards together today. They report fairly
satisfactory progress. I had already sent a wire earlier. I know
that there you should follow the doctor's instructions. May God
restore you to health very soon.
A settlement about Bardoli is almost certain. But I shall have
to stay on for a few days longer.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. VASUMATIBEHN
KANYA GURUKULA
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 491. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
