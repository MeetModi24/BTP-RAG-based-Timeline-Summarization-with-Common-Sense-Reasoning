39. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 27, 1945
CHI. KANTI,
I have your letter. I remember you had come to see me in
Bombay. It is true that I was very busy. This is the only message:
Everyone in South Africa should live in harmony and bring
credit to India. Everyone should be dedicated to service.
Manilal and Sushila have not come so far. They may come.
Convey my blessings to all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
