368. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
October 30, 1939
CHI. KANAIYO,
I do not have the courage to take you along. I am leaving
behind Sushilabehn. Get well soon. Afterwards if you wish you
may go to Rajkot to regain your strength. For getting the tonsils
removed you will have to go to Bombay. I hope to return on
Thursday. Is that all right?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
