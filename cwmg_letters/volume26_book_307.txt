307. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
Vaishakh Sud 1 [April 23, 1925]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
Chi. Chhotelal is not likely to have peace here. He wishes
immediately to return to the Ashram. He says he is ready to do
as you wish. But he wants to have some work all the time. I
feel that Chi. Chhotelal should be put either on carding or on
weaving. Let him card or spin the whole day. We do need
such workers also. Under the arrangement Chi. Chhotelal will
not have to come frequently into contact with others and will be
able to remain at peace. Nevertheless, if you can think of any
other work more suitable for him, you may certainly give him
that.
Blessings from
BAPU
