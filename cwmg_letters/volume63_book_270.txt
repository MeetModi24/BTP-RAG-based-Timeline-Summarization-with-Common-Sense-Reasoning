270. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
August 10, 1936
CHI. SHARMA,
Your letter. I feel ashamed that Babbitt's book has not
yet been found though I am sure it will be found. The yarn
got mixed up with the other yarns. You should accept the
khadi from me . . . . 1
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
facing p. 259
