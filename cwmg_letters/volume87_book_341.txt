341. LETTER TO TARALIKA
PATNA,
April 27, 1947
CHI. DIDIMONY,
I do not know how best to address you.
I have been daily expecting your letter. It comes today.
You will come whenever you wish. I am off to Delhi on
30th for a few days.
Hope the girls are composed.
Am glad Charu is with you. You must even from now
apply yourself to some service of man and through him of God.
Love to you all.
BAPU
SHRI DIDIMONY
3 ALIPORE PARK AVENUE
ALIPORE
BENGAL
From a photostat: G. N. 8728
