482. LETTER TO P. W. SEBASTIAN
SEVAGRAM,
March 21, 1945
DEAR SEBASTIAN,
Sorry for the death of your wife. But there should be no
grief. You, I and all of us have to go where she has gone.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
P. W. SEBASTIAN
PARIS HALL
TRICHUR
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
