328. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
PATNA,
March 26, 1934
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
After careful thinking and for more reasons than one, I have
come to the conclusion that it is best that Valji should tour with
me at present, that it is his dharma to do so. I know that you
and the children will feel the separation from him. But you
should derive joy from such separation. If Valji did not keep
good health with me, I would not let him accompany me. He
certainly keeps good health. And he is also useful to me. You
need not, therefore, think more about him. The present programme
is up to the end of July. After that, God will lead us as He
wills. I hope that you and the children will take the same view
as I do and remain happy.
I hope the weather there has now improved.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7465. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
