107. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
WARDHA,
Magsar Sud 1, 1983 [December 6, 1926]
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
You are welcome. I got your letter at Wardha. You give no
news about your health. Write about it. I shall have to go to
Kathiawar in March. We shall certainly meet then. I will write
if I have to come earlier. I also got a letter from you written
from England.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: C.W. 3217. Courtesy:
Mahesh Pattani
