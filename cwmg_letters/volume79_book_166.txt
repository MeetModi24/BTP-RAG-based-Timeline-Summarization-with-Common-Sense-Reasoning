166. LETTER TO ARUN CHANDRA GUHA
February 4, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Yours of 26th ultimo 2 to Gandhiji. He was very much pleased to read
that even in jail you were able to renew the pledge and the oath of allegiance.
As regards his going to Bengal, though he would very much like to go
there, the circumstances are such that he does not think it advisable at pre-
sent to venture to go to Bengal.
Yours sincerely,
NARAHARI PARIKH
SHRI ARUN CHANDRA GUHA, SECURITY PRISONER,
SPECIAL RESERVE JAIL
P. O. BAKSADUAR (DT. JALPAIGURI)
From a photostat: G.N. 8671
