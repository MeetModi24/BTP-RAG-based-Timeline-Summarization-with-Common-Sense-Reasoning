397. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
February 21, 1937
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
Your letter.
Although I do not see any reason why honey or dates should
cause boils you may consult the doctor. The reasons were the
lack or insufficiency of milk and green vegetables, excess of
wheat and, above all, your fiery temper.
Knowledge of English is not required of village workers
though knowledge of any language is of course good; but I do
not think this was the idea behind your question.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1896
