432. LETTER TO PURATAN BUCH
April 23, 1932
CHI. PURATAN,
Whatever spiritual growth has taken place in me is wholly
the result of my worship of Truth. From that I have gained all
other things. For the present, you should completely give up
the thought of going to jail. Improve your health first.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9166
