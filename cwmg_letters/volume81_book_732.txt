732. LETTER TO KAMAAL KHAN
October 23, 1945
BHAI SAHEB,
I have your letter.
If you have anything to ask me on the occasion of your
restoration to the rulership, do write.
I hope you are all right.
THAKORESAHEB KAMAALKHANJI
MARKS FARM
PARDI
DISTRICT SURAT
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
