415. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
November 16, 1944
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
You have not acted properly in resuming the experiment of
ground-nuts and dates. It is wrong to make such an experiment. To
persist in living on uncooked food is a kind of obstinacy. One
must know how to carry out such experiments. There is no diffi-
culty in cooking ordinary food, and that is what you should do.
Or I may send back Manu there. She herself will not like to
remain here after hearing about your experiment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./XXIV
