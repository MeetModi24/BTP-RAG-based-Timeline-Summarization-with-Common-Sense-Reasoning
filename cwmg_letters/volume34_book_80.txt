80. LETTER TO P. R. SUBRAMANIA SASTRI
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 30, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
Sjt. Rajagopalachariar has shown me your letter of the 29th
instant. I am not yet fit enough to meet people for holding sus-
tained discourse. Whilst therefore if you desire to come, you will
be welcome any day between 4 and 5 p.m. I shall not be able to
engage in any long conversation with you. If you have anything
original to say, I would suggest your writing it as briefly as pos-
sible on one side of an ordinary note paper and sending it to
me.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. R. SUBRAMANIA SASTRI
55, MALLESWARAM
BANGALORE
From a photostat: S.N. 14177
