92. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
POONA,
November 11, 1945
BAPA,
I got the copy of your letter to Dharmadevji. I was pained
and also surprised. We shall discuss this further when we meet.
If I said anything about the money for his ashram I do not
recollect it. This again shows that mere word of mouth has
no value.
I can't trace Suchetabehn's letter. Let us see what happens.
We shall discuss that matter also.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
