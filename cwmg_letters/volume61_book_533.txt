533. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
August 26, 1935
CRI. DEVDAS,
I have sent a wire today. I was expecting a telegram
from you but none has come. What can you do? And who
would think of sending a telegram from there? It is good that
I have a detailed letter from Ba. She writes and says that you
have become panicky. But why should you be alarmed because
of the illness? When we know the ultimate result and are pre-
pared for it, why should you feel frightened? But there is still
plenty of time before you take leave. You have to render a lot
of service through your body. So resolve and get well. I can
only advise you as regards diet and so on. May God protect you.
Blessings from
BAPU
