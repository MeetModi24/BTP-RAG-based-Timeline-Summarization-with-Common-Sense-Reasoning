362. LETTER TO GILLESPIE
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
June 27, 1919
DEAR MR. GILLESPIE,
I thank you for your letter. I know it will be a great trouble
for you to come to the Ashram on a Sunday, and yet I am so anxious
to have a chat with you that I have not the heart to ask you not
to come. I shall discuss with you the points you raised about swa-
deshi. I never knew that you were born in a place where father
served as Diwan for many years and where he passed the last days
of his life.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 6693
