458. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
DELHI,
April 9, 1946
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. How can one be called a naturopath,
if one acts with so much impatience and forgets one's dharma?
Why don't you give up the land and house since you are
resting at home?
Your statement of complaint is not as it ought to be, but I
have sent it over to Gadodiaji for his reply. The letter of
authority for Munshiji is enclosed. Put your signature on it and
return it. Your signature should be accompanied by the date,
place and the signature of a witness.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi : Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
facing p. 358
