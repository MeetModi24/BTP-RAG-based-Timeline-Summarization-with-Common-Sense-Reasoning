416. LETTER TO HARI GOVIND GOVIL
July 27, 1935
BHAI GOVIL,
This letter will be handed over by Shri Hiralal Sharma. He
is proceeding to America to gain further knowledge of nature
cure. He wants to live in poverty and if possible even earn his
living. He may be supplied with the names and other details of
the sanatoria [there] as also some introductions if possible. Shri
Sharma has never been out of India.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HARI GOVIND GOVIL
54 CHOWRINGHEE
CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1024
