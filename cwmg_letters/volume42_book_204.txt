204. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
November 18, 1929
MY DEAR JAWAHAR,
Here is my draft. I want you to consider it carefully and
take your full share in the discussion tonight. I do not want
you to suppress yourself in any way whatsoever except where you
feel that self-suppression is better than self-expression on particular
occasions. After all we must each serve according to our lights,
not borrowed.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1929. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
