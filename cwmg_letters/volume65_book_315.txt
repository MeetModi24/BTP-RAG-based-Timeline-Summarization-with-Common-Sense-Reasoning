315. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
June 9, 1937
CHI. KANTI,
Though you didn't get a first, you got very near it. Your
preparation was very inadequate and hence I think that your
achievement is creditable.
Khushalbhai's death was such as fully became him. He
passed away with his mind fixed on God till the end. Since today
is the last day here, I will write no more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7323. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
