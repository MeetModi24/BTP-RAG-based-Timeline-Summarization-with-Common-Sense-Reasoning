509. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
February 2, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I got your wires at Siddhapur. I got one at Marwar junction
too. There is very little hope. Ba and Kanti got down to take
a train to Delhi. May such occasions make us gentle and more
devoted to duty. Swami and Yehabhai met me at Siddhapur sta-
tion. Ramniklal should go there, tour the area and report about
the women there who are so poor that they would be ready to
card and spin. The intending donor is a friend of Punjabhai and
wants khadi work to be started in Siddhapur itself.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro - 7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 40
