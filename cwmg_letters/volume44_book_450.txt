450. LETTER TO JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 16, 1930
CHI. JAYAPRAKASH,
You must have got my letter. That Prabhavati is daily
suffering from gastric trouble is intolerable. A change of climate
and congenial company can cure it. I would request you either
to escort or arrange to send Prabhavati to the Ashram. She can
return when cured.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3380
