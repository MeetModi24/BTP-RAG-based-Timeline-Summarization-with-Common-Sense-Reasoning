331. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
SEGAON,
October 23, 1937
CHI. KAKA,
I am returning herewith the letters that Kabit should read.
He should give in writing whatever reply he wishes to give.
You can see that he has attacked Ananda Babu very strongly. If
he admits that there has been exaggeration in his attack, he should
withdraw the charge and give the same publicity to his with-
drawal as he gave to the charge. And if he does not withdraw it,
he should be prepared to prove it. Why did he not make these
complaints to me? This also should be found out.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7709
