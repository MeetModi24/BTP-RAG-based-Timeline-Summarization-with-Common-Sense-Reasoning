394. LETTER TO AMBALAL SARABHAI
RAJKOT,
May 14, 1939
DEAR BROTHER,
I have your letter. I have known Juthabhai for many years.
He is industrious. He is honest. If you want him to handle
large amounts, I would suggest that you start entrusting him
with responsibility little by little. I have no idea about his
ability to handle large amounts.
I fully endorse Juthabhai's charitable activities. But I do
not personally collect money for all my colleagues. In many
cases, I give them the responsibility of collecting money. This
has brought good results. They get the training. They gain
respect independently, and my burden of raising funds becomes
lighter.
Saralabehn met me in Bombay.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
