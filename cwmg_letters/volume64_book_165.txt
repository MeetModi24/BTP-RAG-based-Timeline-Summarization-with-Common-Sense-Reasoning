165. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 16, 1936
BHAI MOOLCHAND,
I am replying to your letter after a long time. I am enquir-
ing into the matter instead of writing about it in Harijan. Please
let me have the names and addresses of the prominent workers of
the Arya Samaj in Ajmer.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 759
