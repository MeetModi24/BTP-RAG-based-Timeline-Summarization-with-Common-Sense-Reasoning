393. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
BARDOLI,
June 4, 1931
CHI. PREMA,
I got your letter. I, too, shall start on Monday. So both of
us will reach Bombay on Tuesday. But I shall be reaching there
a little earlier than you. Drop in on the same day when you get
time. I will then decide if I can spare time for a talk with you.
Your letter was quite full of information. Gangabehn has
indeed many aspirations. You should have long talks with her
and should also help her. Her capacity for love is boundless
and her desire for service intense.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10256. Also C.W. 6704. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
