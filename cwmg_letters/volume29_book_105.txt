105. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
January 4, 1926
If you want, I shall send Ba and no one else; she will nurse
you all right; besides she will soon feel one with you.
Complete rest is absolutely necessary; no doubt about that.
You may have no other medicine. It is advisable not to engage
in exciting activities for the present. What you need at present
is a quiet, peaceful yet pleasant time.
You should continue to be a member of the Municipal Corpo-
ration of Bombay. This is just the correct attitude. And it is a
good sign if the voting takes place in your absence.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, pp. 84, 85, 86
