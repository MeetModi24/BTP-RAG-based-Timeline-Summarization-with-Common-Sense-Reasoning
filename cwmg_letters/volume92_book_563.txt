563. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 27, 1932
CHI. MANGALA,
Lately you have stopped writing. Why? How do you
propose to repay those from whom you received presents?
Indu writes to me that you, Nirmala and Kanu are not
on talking terms with him. What can be the reason for this?
Write to me in detail. Start talking to him if possible. We
cannot stop talking to anyone.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11101. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
1 Major M. G. Bhandari
2 Indu N. Parekh
3 Nirmala Desai, step-sister of Mahadev Desai
