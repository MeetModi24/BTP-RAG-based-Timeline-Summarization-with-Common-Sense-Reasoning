378. LETTER TO V. L. MEHTA
September 3, 1945
BHAI VAIKUNTH,
I have your letter. I am happy about your joining the sub-
committee. You are bound to be useful in some ways.
I think it is good that you left Bharat Bank. Why should
you join a new concern?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI V . L. MEHTA
91 BACK HOUSE LANE, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
