42. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
Thursday [After July 18, 1920]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
The more I think about the spinning-wheel, the more I fall
in love with Kale's handiwork. Take care of his health, and also
get from him [designs for] machines to prepare slivers, etc.
Inform Chhotalal that Vitthaldas will soon ask for all the
khadi to be sent to him. Keep back the samples in which the warp
and the woof are both of hand-spun yarn.
Attend to Ramjibhai. He is a fine man. Keep these 18 rupees
with you and credit the sum to his account. Send him yarn against
it and ask him to weave pieces of greater width. He will do so.
We should now have khadi of such width, in which both the warp
and the woof are of hand-spun yarn. If you do not get spinning
done in Ahmedabad, the expenses on the carder will be wasted.
Be quick about this.
The Trust is in my mind. I don't keep idle for a single minute,
but I find myself helpless. This whole day I have been writing
for Young India. I am now tired. I will finish what remains, though.
Look after Ba.
Blessings from
BAPU
