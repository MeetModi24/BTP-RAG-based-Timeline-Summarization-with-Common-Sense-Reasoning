289. LETTER TO T. B. SAPRU
October 25, 1944
DEAR SIR TEJ,
The Press cuttings on the breakdown of the talks between
Quaid-e-Azam Jinnah and me show that for the most part the
criticism is unhelpful and in some cases even regrettable. In the
circumstances if a representative conference is held it might prove
profitable. Anyway it can do no harm. No one is better able than
your good self to take the lead in this matter. This conference
should confine itself to an examination of the Pakistan issue
in the light of the correspondence mentioned above.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR TEJ BAHADUR SAPRU
19 ALBERT ROAD
ALLAHABAD
