105. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
AHMEDABAD,
July 16 [1915]
DEAR MR. SHASTRIAR,
Herewith copy of Mr. Andrews' letter for your perusal. I
think that the Society can inaugurate a big movement for seeking
total prohibition .
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Are you trying to secure me a Tamil teacher?
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 6293
