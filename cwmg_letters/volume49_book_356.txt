356. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
April 10, 1932
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
You should drink through the nostrils every day tepid water
in which soda and salt have been dissolved. The nasal passage,
then, will not get blocked. This is a useful habit to form.
Blessings from
BAPU
