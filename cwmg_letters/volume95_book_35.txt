35. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Monday [April 15, 1921]
RESPECTED ANASUYABEHN,
You will now have been restored to health. I consider it a
failing in men and women doing public work to fall ill. We are the
tools in the hands of India and so even as tools we must ever be clean
and sharp-edged. You must have resumed weaving. It will be
sufficient if you understand about stretching. If you keep up the
practice of walking, some day you will be able to walk for [the
purpose of] stretching too. You are eager to learn it in a day; but how
is that possible?
I will be going to Godhra on Monday. I am not able to decide
whether I should pass through your place or go direct from here.
I could not meet anyone except Panditji and Rangaswami.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: G.N. 11576
