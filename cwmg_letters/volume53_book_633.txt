633. LETTER TO CHARU CHANDRA MITRA
March 5, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I am now sending your letter
to Dr. Bidhan Roy for such investigation as may be possible for
him to undertake.
With reference to your pamphlet, I have read it. You will
permit me to say that I regard it as unbalanced. Such argument
in it as is relevant has already been dealt with in the pages of the
Harijan which you can easily secure, because there is a local sale
of the paper. Sjt. Satis Chandra Das Gupta, 15 College Square,
is the Agent.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. CHARU CHANDRA MITRA, ATTORNEY-AT-LAW
5 HASTINGS STREET
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 20475
