287. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 29, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your letter and wire. This is just to tell you all is well
here. Too busy to say more. I am in the midst of friends and
work.
Hanna still on glucose and oranges.
Love there to the Tyabjis and for yourself.
Yours,
ROBBER
