364. LETTER TO SHIV SHARMA
SEVAGRAM,
March 7, 1945
CHI. SHIV SHARMA,
The news of your ill-health makes me unhappy. Under such
circumstances I will not give you the trouble of coming here. I
hope Ramsarandasji is well. You on your part must become per-
fectly healthy. Hari-ichchha is in mid-sea. She is a girl to be
pitied. She does not want to go anywhere to have her rib taken
out.
Blessings from
BAPU
PANDIT SHIV SHARMA
CURZON ROAD
NEW DELHI
