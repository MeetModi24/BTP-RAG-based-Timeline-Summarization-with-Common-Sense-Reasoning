440. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PARIKH
May 26, 1930
DEAR MANIBEHN,
Narahari did after all go to jail. He received beating also. He
is doubly fortunate. Are you completely courageous? Take care
of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 577
