143. LETTER TO E. E. DOYLE
January 19, 1931
DEAR MAJOR DOYLE,
Sjt. A. Patwardhan is an old associate of mine in political, edu-
cational, social and religious work. He is at present in R. prison.
I read in the papers that he is fasting because he is not permitted
to spin during leisure hours. I hope the report is not true. The
prohibition is unthinkable when A and B class prisoners are per-
mitted. I may say that with many of us the duty of spinning comes
before the pleasure of eating. Will you be so good as to let me know
whether there is any truth in the report? I am sure you will ap-
preciate my anxiety.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 19987
