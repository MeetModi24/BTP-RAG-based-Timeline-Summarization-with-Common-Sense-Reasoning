547. LETTER TO H. S. SUHRAWARDY
SRIRAMPUR (EAST BENGAL),
December 4, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
Sardar Bahadur Sir Datar Singh of the Department of Agriculture,
Government of India, New Delhi, writes to me to say that difficulty
is being experienced in their land being restored to the Calcutta
Pinjrapole Society. This was seized for military purposes evidently during
the war period. The reason given for the refusal is that" the Provincial
Government have under consideration a scheme for the develop-
ment of the Kanchrapara area". I fancy that the decision about the

development scheme must necessarily take a long time to make. When
and if it is made there should be no difficulty in the Government
appropriating the restored land. Meanwhile, it could be utilised for a
benevolent purpose, namely, increase in the supply of milk for the city
of Calcutta. Of course, the stipulation for restoration may well be made
that the reverted land should be used only for the above purpose.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
THE HON'BLE THE PRIME MINISTER
(BENGAL)
CALCUTTA
