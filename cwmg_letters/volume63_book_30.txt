30. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
June 7, 1936
MY DEAR REBEL,
Do not be alarmed at the reduced prices of the articles sent
to you. I asked for it because you were to sell them as swa-
deshi propaganda and might have to lose on them, too, if you
are unable to dispose of them. Let not your princely pride be
wounded. You should charge the full selling price plus postage,
etc. No idiocy allowed in business matters. For these things you
are no rajkumari but a simple sevika and trustee. I have a copy
of the letter and bill sent to you.
Hope you are quite restored.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3578. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6387
