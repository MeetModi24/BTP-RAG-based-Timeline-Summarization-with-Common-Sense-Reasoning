5. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 3, 1930
CHI. MANUDI,
So Balibehn has turned you away. Never mind. Only learn
now to behave like a grown-up girl. Do household work. Spin
and card as much as you can, and write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1502. Courtesy: Manubehn
Mashruwala
