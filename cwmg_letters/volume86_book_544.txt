544. LETTER TO ATINDRA MOHAN ROY
February 1, 1947
DEAR ATINBABU 2 ,
I have no such trainer you ask for. My work lies in the
opposite direction. Non-violent defence is the supreme self-
defence, being infallible. No trainer is required for the purpose.
And in this part of the country, self-defence through training in
some kind of arms is suicidal. Anyway I am the wrong person
to look to for the purpose.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat : G. N. 10530
