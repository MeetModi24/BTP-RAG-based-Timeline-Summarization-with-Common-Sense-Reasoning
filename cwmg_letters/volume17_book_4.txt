4. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
LAHORE,
Sunday [February 1, 1920]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
There is a letter from Esther and hence I write this second
letter to you. I have written to her as well. She made a
great mistake in going. I can see her weakness. This is how
a holy soul falls. She claims too much independence. She feels
humiliated to have to ask anyone. She is like a ship without a
rudder. She has a large heart, but does not know what to do
with it.
I have advised her in my letter that she should look upon
you as an elder brother and obey you, discuss matters with you.
You should see her and find out her needs. If necessary, she may
stay with you. It is possible that, by herself, Ba will not be able
to look after her. I so much want to be in the Ashram at this
time. My heart is bleeding. It does not seem that Esther has
done anything sinful. But I also believe that she may do such a
thing in no time. This feeling may be only an expression of my
fear.
Blessings from
BAPU
