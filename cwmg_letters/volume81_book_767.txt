767. LETTER TO HARJIVAN KOTAK
October 27, 1945
CHI. HARJIVAN KOTAK,
I have your letter. I understand about the expenses. I
don't remember the decision I had taken but I think if they
agree to give you more, a way can be found.
I can say more if I know who is the hakim and what his
opinion is. I want you to settle down.
HARIJAN ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
