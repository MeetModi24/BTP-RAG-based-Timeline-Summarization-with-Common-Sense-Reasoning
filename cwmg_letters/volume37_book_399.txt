399. LETTER TO ISAAC SANTRA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 12, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I shall certainly
go through the pamphlet when it comes to me. But I prefer
doing this kind of work in my own way. At the present moment
I am averse to mixing up with any Empire thing because I distrust
the Empire.
Yours sincerely,
ISAAC SANTRA, ESQ.
OFFICER-IN-CHARGE, LEPROSY SURVEY
C/O POST MASTER, JAMNER, EAST KHANDESH
From a microfilm: S.N. 13553
