391. LETTER TO SYED MUSTAFA
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
DEAR SYED SAHEB,
I thank you for your kind note. You have my assurance that
I shall do nothing without a call from God.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SYED MUSTAFA, BAR-AT-LAW
LUCKNOW
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
