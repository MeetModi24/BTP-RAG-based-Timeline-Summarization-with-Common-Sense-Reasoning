410. LETTER TO SHANTILAL J. MEHTA
April 16, 1934
CHI. SHANTI,
I have received your letter, the hundi and Rs.15. I am using
all the amounts exclusively for Bihar.
Inform Mohanlal and Umiashankar.
If you live worthily in every way, your aspirations are bound
to be fulfilled.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
