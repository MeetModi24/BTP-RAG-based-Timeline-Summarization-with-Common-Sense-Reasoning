377. LETTER TO NAUTAMLAL BHAGWANJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 12, 1930
BHAISHRI NAUTAMLAL,
I got your letter. I showed it to Doctor. He was happy.
I hope you will succeed in your effort.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SJT. NAUTAMLAL BHAGWANJI
JETPUR, KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2582
