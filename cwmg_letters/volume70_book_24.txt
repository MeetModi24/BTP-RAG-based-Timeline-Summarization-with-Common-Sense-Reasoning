24. LETTER TO S. K. BOLE
ABBOTTABAD,
July 23, 1939
DEAR RAO BAHADUR,
I thank you for your letter of 20th instant. I understand your
explanation. I had to deal with the memorial as it was drawn
up. The last paragraph was incapable of any other meaning. In
the circumstances I imagine it is unnecessary to say anything fur-
ther on my article. But if you think otherwise, I shall insert any
explanation you may think fit to send. It should be brief and
to the point.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 4880
