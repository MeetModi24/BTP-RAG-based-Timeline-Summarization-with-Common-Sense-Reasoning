470. LETTER TO G. V. GURJALE
ABBOTTABAD,
July 14, 1939
DEAR GURJALE,
I was glad to hear from you. You must not give in. If the
medical work progresses, well and good. Otherwise you must live
on what the goodness of neighbours brings you. Have no hedge
and no gardening. Let the people know that you do not resent
their depredations.
Yours,
BAPU
SWAMI NIRMALANANDA BHIKSHU
GANDHIKUPPAM
TIRUVENANALLUR P . O . , INDIA
From a photostat: G.N. 1387
