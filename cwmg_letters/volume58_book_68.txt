68. LETTER TO KANTI GANDHI
June 5, 1934
CHI. KANTI,
Is it proper that you should never write to me? How are
you?
What do you read?
How do you live?
Will you not
write to me and tell me all these things? From there you must
write to me a beautiful letter every week.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7285. Courtesy: Kanti Gandhi
