2. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Jufy 1, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
How is your state of mind now? And your health? When
did you see Satis Babu last? How is he? Who are all at Sode-
pur? What do you study and what is your daily routine? Keep
this in mind:
Who rejoices not, neither frets nor grieves, who covets not, who
abandons both good and evil—that devotee of Mine is dear to Me. 4
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Write to me care of the Ashram.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1667
