474. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day [June 30, 1930]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
All of us are far away from our ideal and will remain so. Our
duty lies in striving to reach it. Man's ideal grows from day to
day and that is why it ever recedes from him. You are ever striving
and that is all that you can do. Our effort should be pure and
unremitting.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I do not write separately about Kaka, because the long letter
contains all the news.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8751. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
