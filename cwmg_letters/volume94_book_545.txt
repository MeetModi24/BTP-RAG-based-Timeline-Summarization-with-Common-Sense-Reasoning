545. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
December 2, 1946
CHI. SARALABEHN,
I am able to attend to your letter only today. I have written to
Jajuji. 1 He will write if there is anything more to be done. He will write
to me if there is anything to be said. I am very happy by your
acceptance. May your good act bear fruit.
I am glad to hear that Nirmalabehn's health is beginning to
improve. You must recover fully.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11151. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
