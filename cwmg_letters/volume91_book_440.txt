440. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Friday [December 25, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I get your letters regularly. Do not be in a hurry to start
walking. Read whatever you feel like reading. And eat as much
as you can easily digest. I think it would be good to try fruit
after consulting the doctor at Deolali. Sweet-lime and grapes
are entirely sweet.
Blessings from
BAPU
