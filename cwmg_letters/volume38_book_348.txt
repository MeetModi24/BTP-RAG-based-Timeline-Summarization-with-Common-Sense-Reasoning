348. LETTER TO RAJA OF KANIKA
8 PRETORIA STREET,
CALCUTTA,
December 27, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Much as I should like to send you an
appointment, I am so much pressed for time that I hardly
know whether I could give you an appointment before the Con-
gress meets. I would therefore ask you to tell me briefly in
writing what you want to say.
Yours sincerely,
THE RAJA OF KANIKA
