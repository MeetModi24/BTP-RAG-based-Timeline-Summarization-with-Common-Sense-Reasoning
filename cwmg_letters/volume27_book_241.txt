241. LETTER TO MANIBERN PATEL
Thursday [July 16, 1925]
CHI. MANI,
I have your letter. If you want the other bangles just now,
let me know and I shall send them by post. Will Dahyabhai like
to study in the National Medical College of Calcutta? It seems
to be working well. Or what is Dahyabhai's real desire in the
matter? I am so busy that I cannot possibly write long letters.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne, p. 27
