442. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 13, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
Ba is becoming impatient. Everybody is asking: "Is Sushila-
behn coming on the 16th?" I therefore advise you to come
within the stipulated time. But if Mother feels unhappy you
should do what you consider to be your duty. Maybe you can
make your final decision after coming here. I have great need
of you for Krishnadas.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
