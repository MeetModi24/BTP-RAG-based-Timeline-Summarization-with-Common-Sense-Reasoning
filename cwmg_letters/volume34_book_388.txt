388. LETTER TO T. R. MAHADEVA IYER
CAMP VELLORE,
August 31, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I can only repeat what I have said, that
in my opinion it is better for you to give up possession. You
will be morally in the right in giving up possession right, if those
who you feel are of your opinion are duly informed before you
give up possession. Not to give notice and remain in possession
is, in my opinion, indefensible. So far as I have been able to
understand the intentions of the Committee, they are averse to
going to arbitration on matters about which they have no moral
qualms and no legal difficulty.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 12941
