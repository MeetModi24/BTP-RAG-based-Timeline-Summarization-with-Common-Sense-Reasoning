71. LETTER TO AN AMERICAN WOMAN
November 24, 1932
I am not enamoured of the idea of using divine healing for
the purpose of proving the existence of divinity or efficiency of
prayer. If Jesus came back to us, it is difficult to know what
he would say to some of the uses that are being made of his
powers of healing and other miracles attributed to him.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 275
