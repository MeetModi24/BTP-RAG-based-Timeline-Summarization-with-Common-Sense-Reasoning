12. LETTER TO ELIZABETH MARI MOLTENO
7 BUITENSINGLE,
February 24, 1914
DEAR MISS MOLTENO,
I am sorry to have to inform you that Mrs. Gandhi has had a
relapse and she is at the time of writing lying in bed. She wants me
therefore to say that whilst she would try her best to keep the
appointment for tomorrow, she might not be able to go out at all. I
thought that I should let you know this. In any case I shall expect you
