410. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
MAHISHADAL,
December 28, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have your letter.
Haven't I written about Lala Ramswarup that even if we
want to delete a word we have to invite the opinion of the
members as far as possible? Consult Pakvasa and Dada Mavalankar.
They are there. We must have the opinion of the persons who
have moved for the amendment of the resolution. There is no
doubt that it can be secured. Only it will take some time but
you have to put up with that.
The second question you have raised is good. I would not
like to be bound down to any particular use of the building.
I would like that Lala Ramswarup should either donate the
building on the understanding that we may make what use we
like of it or he should not donate it at all. The donor should
make the donation on our condition and not his.
BHAI SHYAMLAL
WARDHA
