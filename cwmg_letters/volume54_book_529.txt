529. LETTER TO S. SWAMINATHAN
April 20, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. All I can do is to commend to your
attention the weekly Harijan and you will find in the issues my
position clearly stated. There is no question whatsoever of com-
pulsion in connection with temple-entry.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. SWAMINATHAN
1146 ANDIAPILLAI AGRAHARAM
KARUNATTAMKUDI
TANJORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 20996
