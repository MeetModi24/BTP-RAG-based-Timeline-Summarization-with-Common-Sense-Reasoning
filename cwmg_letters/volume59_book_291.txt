291. LETTER TO HIRALAL M. GARHWAL
November 2, 1934
MY DEAR HIRALAL,
I thank you for your letter of the 30th ultimo giving me
figures of the Hindi-speaking population in the district of Ahmeda-
bad.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI HIRALAL M. GARHWAL
KOSHTI SAMAJ MANDAL
BOMBAY 11
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
