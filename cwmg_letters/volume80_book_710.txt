710. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
SIMLA,
July 11, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have your letter of the 6th. I have already sent my con-
sent for Dharmadev Shastri's scheme. I have more informa-
tion about Prof. Jagadisan than Bapa has. Much depends on
a person's goodness, experience and skill. Therefore, if Bapa
approves and if I have the power, I agree to the doctor being
retained on Rs. 125 or Rs. 150, and to the selection of the
members of the committee.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHYAMLALJI
KASTURBA SMARAK NIDHI
BAJAJWADI, WARDHA
