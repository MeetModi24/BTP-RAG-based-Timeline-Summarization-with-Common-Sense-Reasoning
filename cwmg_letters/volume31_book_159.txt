159. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 15, 1926
BHAI HARIBHAU,
Herewith a letter from Pyarelal, a teacher. Please find out
how far what he says is true and let me know.
I had received your letter from Ujjain. I had written to the
Dewan of Mysore for his approval, which he has accorded. Shall
I send the report to you? Or to Pustakeji?
Chi. Martand must have written to you yesterday. Do not
worry on his account.
