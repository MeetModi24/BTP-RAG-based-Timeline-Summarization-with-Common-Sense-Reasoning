164. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
December 1, 1928
CHI. KUSUM,
May I not say that you are a little fool? Why should you
feel hurt because I asked you? If you feel hurt like this every
time, I can ask you nothing.
I want to see you what I have imagined you are. I have
no time today to write more. I can have no doubt that you would
look after Manu properly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1761
