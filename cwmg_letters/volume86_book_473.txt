473. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM AND ABHA GANDHI
January 15, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. Keep up your strength by reciting
Ramanama. Pass as many days as possible in peace. That way
God will be pleased and the work will progress. There is no
need to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. ABHA,
Remain cheerful.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 563
