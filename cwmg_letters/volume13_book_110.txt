110. LETTER TO CHHOTALAL TEJPAL
[AHMEDABAD,]
Ashadh Vad 5 [July 31, 1915]
The following is found in the issue of February 2, 1910 of
The Times [of India] :
We could wish that His Excellency's visit had synchronised with the
removal of the Viramgam customs line, which is a blot upon the ad-
ministration of India and very imperfectly serves the purpose for
which it was designed.
1 This letter appears to have been written in 1915, for Gandhiji had two
interviews with Tilak on July 11 at Poona.
2 From the postmark
Please excuse me for the delay in searching this out.
MOHANDAS KARAMCHAND
SHRI CHHOTALAL TEJPAL
RAMCHANDRA MANSION
SANDHURST ROAD
GIRGAUM
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 2586
