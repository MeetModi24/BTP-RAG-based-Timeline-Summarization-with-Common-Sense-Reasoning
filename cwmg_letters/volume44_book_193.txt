193. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
[About September 10, 1930]
CHI. MOTIBEHN,
I hope you have overcome your grief now. The Gita is the
medicine for your grief. Read the Gujarati translation every day
and read it over and over again. Get Shantu examined by Dr.
Haribhai and take the necessary measures about his teeth. Read
a little every day.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3738
