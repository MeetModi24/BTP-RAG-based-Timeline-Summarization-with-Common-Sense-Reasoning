88. LETTER TO MURIEL LESTER
SEVAGRAM,
January 21, 1945
MY DEAR MURIEL,
I have your chatty letter filled with news. Most of those you
mention are in prison and beyond me even through letters. But
of course such is the lot of satyagrahis.
Of course Doris is a tower of strength to you. I can well
picture her standing to her post in all weathers.
I am fairly well.
The rest from Pyarelal.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
