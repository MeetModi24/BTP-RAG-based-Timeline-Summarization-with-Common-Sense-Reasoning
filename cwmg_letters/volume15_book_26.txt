26. LETTER TO GOKULDAS PAREKH
August 17, 1918
DEAR FRIEND,
As your letter written in the month of June was addressed
to me c/o the War Conference2, I got it only two days ago. You
will therefore understand why you did not receive even so much
as an acknowledgement from me. Though I got the letter so late,
its value is no less to me on that account. I am, naturally, always
eager to see you pleased. I see that you are, and think myself
fortunate. I have realized from experience that the people of
Kheda are strong enough for a good many tasks. I have learnt,
and have been learning, much by my public contacts with these
men and women. I trust you are well.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. IV
