310. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
July 10, 1933
CHI. AMALA,
Do not be in a hurry to know all your limitations. I want
your letters giving me your natural state. When I get the time
I shall analyse some of them. Meanwhile know that all my
criticism is charged with love. And I am hoping that you have
sense of humour.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
