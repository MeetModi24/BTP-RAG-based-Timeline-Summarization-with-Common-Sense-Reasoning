243. LETTER TO RAFI AHMED KIDWAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 1, 1936
MY DEAR KIDWAI,
To ask for a message from me is like drawing . . .1 live
tooth and then too for a meeting such as you are describing.
Surely at a meeting at which Mr. Jinnah presides and which
Jawaharlal opens any message would be superfluous.
Yours sincerely,
MOULVI RAFI AHMED KIDWAI
4A RUTTLEDGE ROAD
LUCKNOW
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
