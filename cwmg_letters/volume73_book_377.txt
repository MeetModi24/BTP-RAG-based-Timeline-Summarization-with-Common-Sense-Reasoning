377. LETTER TO RAMBHAU B. TILAK
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
January 26, 1941
DEAR RAMBHAU,
I did send you a message asking you to give up the fast. I
have read your papers and I am of opinion that you should not
resist the trustees. And in any case there is no case for a fast.
I hope you will break the fast.
Yours sincerely,
M. K.
SHRI RAMBHAU B. TILAK
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
