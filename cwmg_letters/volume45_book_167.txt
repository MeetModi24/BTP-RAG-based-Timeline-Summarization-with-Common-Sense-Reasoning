167. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 25, 1931
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your letter only now. Do not allow your good health
to be impaired. I have not so far received the Conversations by
Aurobindo. Even the copy sent by you has not reached me yet.
Where is Krishnan Nair? We are both well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2386
