450. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
April 23, 1934
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
Your letter.
It is not good for you to accompany me on
the tour or to come here all the way for a couple of days.
Travelling with me is a tough proposition.
Almost all fall ill.
You cannot even take the trip to Badrinath and Kedarnath but,
if you can, you may go to Mussoorie or Dalhousie. There is
also not any more that urgency about going to the Ashram as
existed before my decision.
Now Krishna Nair will be able to
stay on. I hope you have understood my decision.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2411
