252. LETTER TO SATYANAND
SODEPUR,
December 8, 1945
DEAR SATYANAND BABU,
It was a delight to receive your postcard. I am doing all
I am capable of for the alleviation of distress. I do not know
what God will do through the effort.
I hope you are keeping well.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
