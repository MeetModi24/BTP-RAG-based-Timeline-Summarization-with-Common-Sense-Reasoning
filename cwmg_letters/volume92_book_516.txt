516. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
March 28, 1932
CHI. TARAMATI,
There is no news at all from Mathuradas. Give me news of
all of you in detail. Do you know that Mahadev is with me?
How do you spend your time?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI TARABEHN MATHURADAS
C/O MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
57 MINT ROAD
BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
