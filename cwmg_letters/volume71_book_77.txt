77. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 26, 1939
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. What is the point in my writing to you,
since this is how you behave every time? I even wired as desired
by you. Somebody will come to receive you at the station on the
2nd. Take care of your health. Ba is still in Delhi. Everything
else is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3530
