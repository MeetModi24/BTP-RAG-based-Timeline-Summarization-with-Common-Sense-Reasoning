324. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
November 1, 1940
CHI. SUMI,
Your letters are fine. You have made good progress. When shall I be
able to see you all? When will you let me hear your music? Motiba 2 told me
about the thing. You may go in for an operation if the doctor advises. But
you should do so only if the doctor assures you of its success.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
1 Of the Congress Working Committee, at Wardha
2 Literally, the elder Ba, that is, grandmother Kasturba
