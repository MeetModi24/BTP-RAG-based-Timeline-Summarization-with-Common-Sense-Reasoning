228. LETTER TO LAJPAT RAI
Continuation
October 28, 1924
DEAR LALAJI,
Since writing the first letter, I have heard from the P.S.V.
saying that in the present state of things in Kohat my visit cannot
be countenanced. I hope soon to publish the whole of the cor-
respondence. Now what am I to do? I suppose, I am of no use
in Rawalpindi. I can bring no comfort to the poor refugees.
The question now before me is whether I should go to the Punjab
now or later regarding the Hindu-Muslim question. This you
alone shall decide.
What a situation in Bengal? I wonder whether the Swaraj
Council meeting is coming off here on the 30th. I have as yet
heard nothing from Motilalji. Mr. Das went to Calcutta yester-
day.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 15938
