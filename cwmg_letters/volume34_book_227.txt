227. LETTER TO RADHA SUNDER DAS
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 29, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am sorry that your application has not
been favourably received. But you must be neither despondent nor
enraged against those who have rejected your application. These
things must always happen. And it would be wrong to suppose
that because our applications are not accepted injustice has been
done. Please keep me informed of your progress and movements.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. RADHA SUNDER DAS
SURI P.O.
BIRBHOOM DISTRICT
From a microfilm: S.N 19792
