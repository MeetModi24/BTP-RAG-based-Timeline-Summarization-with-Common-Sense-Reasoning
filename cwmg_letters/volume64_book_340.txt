340. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
February 3, 1937
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You have been well saved. If self-control does not come
easily to you, you may marry. You must practise restraint over
food.
It is very necessary to make slivers with your own hands.
You should do it.
What can you gain by further studies? You can readily obtain
a teaching job provided you do not require a high salary. Will
you be satisfied with Rs.75 a month?
You must now compose yourself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4278; also S.G. 55
