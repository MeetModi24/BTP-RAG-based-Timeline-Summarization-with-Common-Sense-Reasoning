344. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
August 7, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
It was good that you two girls were not frightened of Harilal
and gave him bold replies.
Even though Chimanlal has come, if you like to stay in the
hostel and if your health remains good, why should you not con-
tinue to stay there?
BAPU
