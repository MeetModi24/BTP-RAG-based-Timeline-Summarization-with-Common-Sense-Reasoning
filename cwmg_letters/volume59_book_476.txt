476. LETTER TO V. V. GIRI
November 27, 1934
DEAR GIRI,
Your wire. When almost all win1, who should receive con-
gratulations? The policy of the Congress has been vindicated. Let
us deserve the great trust.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
V. V. GIRI
VIZAGAPATNAM
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
