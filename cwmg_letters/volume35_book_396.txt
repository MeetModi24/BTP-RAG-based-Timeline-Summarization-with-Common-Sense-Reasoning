396. LETTER TO ELISABETH KNUDSEN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
January 31, 1928
DEAR MISS KNUDSEN,
I have your letter. I am conducting no hydropathic esta-
blishment. I give Kuhne's baths to some people in the Ashram
and that is all. If you can come at all, come to the Ashram.
You will be welcome guest and you will teach massage to some
of the sisters here as also men. You will of course have to pay
nothing for your boarding and lodging, and you will stay as long
as you like. Life here is very simple, perhaps even harder, but
I know that that matters little to you.
Yours sincerely,
MISS ELISABETH KNUDSEN
ADYAR
MADRAS
From a photostat: S.N. 13059
