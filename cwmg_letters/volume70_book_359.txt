359. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 29, 1939
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I wrote to you yesterday. This is to send you a complaint
from Meerut. Please inquire and write directly to the corres-
pondent. I have told him I have passed the letter on to you.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1939.
Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
