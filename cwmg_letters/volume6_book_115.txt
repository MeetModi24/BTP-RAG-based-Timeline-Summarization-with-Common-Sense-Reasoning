115. LETTER TO MISS E. ROSENBERG
HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,
November 6, 1906
DEAR MADAM,
You have been massaging Mr. Ally at the Lady Margaret Hospital.
Mr. Ally is now staying with me at the Hotel. Do you mind calling
tomorrow and giving a massage to Mr. Ally at 3.30 p.m. precisely,
and as it takes some time before your card will arrive through the page
at the Hotel, if you will be at the Hotel at 3.15 you will be able to
commence the massage at 3.30. Mr. Ally has to fulfil an important
engagement at half past five, if not a little earlier.
Yours faithfully,
MISS EBBA ROSENBERG
5, CHESTNUT ROAD
ENFIELD
WASH
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4499
