179. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
POONA,
February 21, 1946
CHI. CHAND,
I have your letter. That you keep longing to meet me is as
good as meeting me. Get well and be ready [for service]. That
is much more important than meeting me. I also like it that you
consider Satyavati your ideal. God will take care of the rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : Chandrani Papers. Courtesy; Gandhi
National Museum and Library
