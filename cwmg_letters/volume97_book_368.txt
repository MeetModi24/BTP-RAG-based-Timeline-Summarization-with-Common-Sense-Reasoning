368. LETTER TO RAMDAS AND NIRMALA GANDHI
CHI. RAMDAS AND NIMU,
Ramdas has got a good place. If he keeps good health, everything else
will be all right. Nimu should think of sitting for the examination only after
she has improved her health. That she has passed the Madhyama 2 means one
burden less. Kanam thinks of Motiba and of Sumi and then forgets both. He
cheerfully eats in my company and sleeps by my side at night. 3
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
