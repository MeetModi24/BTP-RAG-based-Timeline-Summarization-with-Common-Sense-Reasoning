148. LETTER TO BHUPENDRANATH GHOSH
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter, enclosing suggestions for amend-
ing the Ashram Rules published in Young India.
They will
be carefully considered when revision is taken up.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13907
