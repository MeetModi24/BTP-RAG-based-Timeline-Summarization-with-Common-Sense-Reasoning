722. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
April 22, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. I have made it a rule to have someone
visit Pyarelal. Send me the address of P. Gupta. I shall give
him notice when the next visit falls due.
I have understood about your examination. I do not worry
at all. It may be held any time. If it is held in May, you
will be able to give me time without any worry. From that
point of view, I would rather that it was over in May.
Ba is keeping well and so I shall not be anxious about
her at all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
