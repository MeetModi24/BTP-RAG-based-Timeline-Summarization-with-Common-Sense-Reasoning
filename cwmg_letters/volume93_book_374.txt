374. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
BARDOLI,
January 21, 1939
CHI. MATHURADAS,
On second thoughts I feel that you should give the enclosed
letter to Jairamdas to read. Then let me know what he thinks.
I am quite worried about him. He is very shy. So he will
not ask for help. His letter is enclosed. You will understand
his situation from it. There is no question at all of taking his
time. It will be good if Jivaraj, Bharucha, etc., also go.
Blessings from
BAPU
