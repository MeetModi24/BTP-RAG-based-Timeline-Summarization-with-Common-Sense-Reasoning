121. LETTER TO KUSUMBEHN DESAI
July 1, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
Some of your letters are no letters at all. For instance the
last letter. If you cannot think of anything to write about, it
would be better not to write at all. It is a weakness in you
that you cannot think of anything to write about, but writing a
letter which is almost a blank sheet of paper does not make up
for that weakness but confirms it.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1841
