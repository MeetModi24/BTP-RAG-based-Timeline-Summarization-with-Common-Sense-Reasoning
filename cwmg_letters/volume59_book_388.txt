388. LETTER TO C. HANUMANTHA RAO
November 14, 1934
MY DEAR HANUMANTHA RAO,
I have your letter. You cannot use mill-yarn even for making
tapes. Those who want to make tapes may well devote a little
time to spinning the yarn required for tape.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI C. HANUMANTHA RAo
GOUTAMI SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SITANAGARAM, via KOVVUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
