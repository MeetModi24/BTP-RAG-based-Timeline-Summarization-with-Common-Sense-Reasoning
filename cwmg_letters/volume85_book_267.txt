267. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
NEW DELHI,
August 30, 1946
CHI. KANCHAN,
Be composed. Therein lies your good. Remember what I
have told you. Your physical condition depends entirely on
your mental condition. I do feel pity for you, and that is why
I also feel irritated with you. Why do you make yourself an
object of pity? I look upon you as a child. But considering
your age, you ought not to be a child in mind. You should
learn to think systematically. You should find your own path in
life. Your day should be spent in service of some kind; your
night should be given to sleep.
Blessings from
BAPU
