258. LETTER TO PYARELAL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
May 24, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
I wrote to you at considerable length yesterday. You have
asked in your letter to Kanu as to what is being decided about
you. He will no doubt write. I can only say that all that we
are thinking of is your return. When will you be coming?
And what is there to think about? You had said three weeks
at least. I had thought it would be three weeks at the most.
Deep within me I had felt that you yourself would not be able
to stay away for three weeks. If you do not fix another date, I
shall await your arrival on the 30th. Counting from the 9th,
would not three weeks be over on the 30th?
Blessings from
BAPU
