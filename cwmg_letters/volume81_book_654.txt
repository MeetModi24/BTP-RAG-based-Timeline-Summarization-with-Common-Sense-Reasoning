654. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
October 16, 1945
CHI. ANAND,
It is very good that your faith has increased and also your
peace. I am writing the daily thought. I hope to complete
what is left. After that I want to be quiet.
Chi. Mahadev will be well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy : National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
