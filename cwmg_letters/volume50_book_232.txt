232. LETTER TO INDU N. PAREKH
July 17, 1932
DEAR INDU,
If you are well, certainly attend the prayers. Sitting here I
cannot know (anything) about your demand. Do as Narandas
asks you to do. Has your life been made regular? Can you
concentrate your mind on study? Has the confusion of your mind
lessened? Has (it) begun to understand its responsibility?
Think about all these things.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 309
