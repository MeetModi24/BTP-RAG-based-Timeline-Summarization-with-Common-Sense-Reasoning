465. LETTER TO H. N. KUNZRU
April 14, 1933
MY DEAR HARIJI,
I have a letter from a correspondent in Bulandshahr who
says that in common with the reduction in the wages of the gene-
ral staff by its Municipal Board, the scavengers also have come
in for the reduction. This latter have struck against the reduc-
tion. As it is, their wages are, the correspondent contends, low
enough. The Chairman of the Board does not listen to the sca-
vengers' complaint at all. On the face of it, the correspondent's
letter appears to me to be reasonable. Will you please enquire
and do whatever is possible?
I enclose the letter received by me.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
