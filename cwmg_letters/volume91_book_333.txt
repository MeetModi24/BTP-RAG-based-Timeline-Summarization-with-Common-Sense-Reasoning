333. LETTER TO BEGUM KHURSHED ABDUL MAJID
SABARMATI,
September 10, 1924
DEAR SISTER,
Hakim Saheb and Khwaja Saheb came to see me. Khwaja
Saheb stayed over for a day. Please write to me about your
mother's health. I hope she is better now. If you have stopped
plying the charkha, you should start it again and send 2,000
yards of yarn every month.
Yours,
GANDHI
BEGUM KHWAJA SAHEB
SOLAN, KALKA-SIMLA RLY.
From the Urdu original: A. M. Khwaja Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
