110. LETTER TO SATYADEVI GIRI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 9, 1930
CHI. SATYADEVI,
I have your letter. You have drawn the trees well. Now you
should have real trees as your models so that your drawing may
give an impression of reality. You must practise your letters well
before you start giving them curves. You like learning good things
that is why I ask you to learn correct spelling even from now. Do
not slacken about spinning.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Viral Vatsalata, pp. 33-
