12. LETTER TO PADAMPAT SINGHANIA
NEW DELHI,
October 21, 1946
BHAI PADAMPAT,
Mirabehn has forwarded to me the letters you wrote to
Hari. In my opinion it is not right to postpone collecting the
money till land has been acquired. Do send the money and
collect from other donors. The amounts will be returned in case
the project does not materialize. I have collected money on
many occasions; work always follows. Isn't this the history of
all activities?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
