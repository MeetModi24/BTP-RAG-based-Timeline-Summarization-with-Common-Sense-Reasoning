277. LETTER TO MULK RAJ
POST ANDHERI,
April 5, 1924
DEAR LALA MULK RAJ,
I have your letter of the 1st instant, for which I thank you.
Deshbandhu Das has not yet come here. Pandit Malaviyaji
had to leave for Benares before finishing the conversations. He
returns again at the end of the month. Pandit Motilalji is here,
and I hope to discuss the Jallianwala Bagh Memorial as soon
as the present deliberations are finished. I shall take care of the
original plans and return them after use.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
LALA MULK RAJ
AMRITSAR
From a photostat: S.N. 8668
