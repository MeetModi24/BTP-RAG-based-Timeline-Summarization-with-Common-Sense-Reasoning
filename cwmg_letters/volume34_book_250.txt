250. LETTER TO SECRETARY, JUBILEE COMMITTEE
HASSAN,
August 3, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I am much obliged to you for the invitation to the Maharaja
Silver Jubilee Commemoration. I hope that the function will
be worthy of the occasion.
Yours faithfully,
THE SECRETARY
JUBILEE COMMITTEE
BANGALORE CITY
