227. LETTER TO INDU N. PAREKH
January 27, 1933
CHI. INDU,
I got your letter. How did you become ill? I hope you
are careful about eating. You may use a bicycle, but you should
also walk daily. Send me your time-table for the day's work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6254
