96. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
September 27, 1934
Whatever is happening is for the good. One gains all
this by submitting to God's will.
Whatever happens, the mind
that has offered up all the acts to the Lord never feels discontented.
One who is conscious of the "I" is satisfied with nothing. Know
that this contains all that is worth knowing.
It is true, of
course, that it is difficult to cultivate the attitude of offering up
all the acts to the Lord. However, if after knowing the truth we
ceaselessly strive to follow it in practice our contentment will go
on increasing.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
