214. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
February 27, 1946
CHI. MUNNALAL,
You must learn to exercise control over your language.
Even when thinking about individuals one should not pay too
much attention to their shortcomings. You yourself should decide
whether or not you should remain here. If you cannot decide,
it would mean you should not stay here; for the work is so diffi-
cult that, if you cannot take full interest in it, you would not be
able to do it. This is an important sphere of work for learning
to acquire control over one's temper.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8420. Also C. W. 5606. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
