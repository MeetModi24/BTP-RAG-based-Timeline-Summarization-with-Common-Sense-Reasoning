517. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
NEW DELHI,
October 1, 1946
CHI. HOSHIARI,
I have your letter. I am glad that you have taken up
weaving. If you learn all the the processes relating to cotton
everything will be all right.
My stay here is getting prolonged.
Blessings from
BAPU
HOSHIARIBEHN
ASHRAM, SEVAGRAM
