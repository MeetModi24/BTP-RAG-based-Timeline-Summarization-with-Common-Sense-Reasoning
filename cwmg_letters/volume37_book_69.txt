69. LETTER TO S. G. VAZE
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 14, 1928
MY DEAR VAZE,
Here is a copy of a letter from Oza with my reply. I do
not feel safe about guiding and I have told him as much. But
you have studied the situation locally. Can you guide him?
I am writing to Benarsidas also. I have cabled Oza asking
him to await reply.
I was greatly pleased with your Bardoli report .
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. G . VAZE
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY, POONA
From a microfilm: S.N. 12861
