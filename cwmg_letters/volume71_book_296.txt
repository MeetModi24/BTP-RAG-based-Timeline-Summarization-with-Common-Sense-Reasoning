296. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
ON THE TRAIN,
March 3, 1940
CHI. NIMU,
I have your letters. You will have the other news from the
letter to Ra[mdas]. The train has stopped as if for you! Kanam
will not surrender to force. Let us be satisfied by getting him to do
as much as is possible by love. He certainly will progress.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Nirmala Gandhi Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
