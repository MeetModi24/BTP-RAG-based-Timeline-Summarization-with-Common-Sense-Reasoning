116. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
AHMEDABAD,
August 23 [1915]
DEAR MR. SHASTRIAR,
Here is Manilal my second son. He will tell you why he
finds himself in Madras. He is to complete his training in Tamil
and handloom weaving there. Kindly guide him. Hanumanthrao
can try his social service training on Manilal.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 6292
