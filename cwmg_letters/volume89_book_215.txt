215. LETTER TO CHOITHRAM GIDWANI
NEW DELHI,
September 16, 1947
BHAI CHOITHRAM,
What is this that you are doing? If people must flee why
must they all flock to Bombay? There is a way of fighting too.
Why are you ruining the case of Sind? Ponder well. The time
is extremely delicate.
'Do or die' is not a mere slogan to be
spoken but to be acted upon.
Blessings from
BAPU
