77. LETTER TO T. N. SHARMA
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 30, 1945
BHAI SHARMA,
I have your letter. I do not like it. The circumstances keep
changing. If Chakrayya wants to do nature-cure work, why
does he insist on that particular place? Why not Bhimavaram?
Anand Niketan should be given up. I do not like to spend
money on the building. The money should be returned.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI T. N. SHARMA
C/O CHEMICALS LTD.
NIDADAVOLU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
