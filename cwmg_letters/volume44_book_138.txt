138. LETTER TO RADHABEHN GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 22, 1930
CHI. RADHIKA,
I shall wait for your letter. I can't bear the thought of
Keshu's remaining ill all the time. You should take special care
of his health. How did he again get fever? What food does he eat?
Give me all details.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have just received your letter. I understand.
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 8685. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
