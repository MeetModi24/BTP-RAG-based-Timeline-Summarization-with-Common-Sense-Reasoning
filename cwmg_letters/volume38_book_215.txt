215. LETTER TO KLAAS STORM
December 7, 1928
MY DEAR STORM1,
Every letter that comes from you gives me delight and pleasure.
I am so glad that you are getting on so well. I was delighted to
have the card containing a portrait of your teacher and his family.
Do continue to write to me from time to time.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 15107
