290. LETTER TO MARIAM
February 12, 1935
MY DEAR MARIAM,
I was glad to see the few words from you written at the
end of Father's letter to me. I hope you are getting on well.
You should write to me regularly and tell me all about your
doings. Do you hear from Sadullah and from Sofia?
SHRI MARIAM
13 WINDSOR PLACE
NEW DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
