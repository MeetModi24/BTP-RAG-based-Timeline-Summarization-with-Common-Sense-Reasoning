3. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEGAON,
August 1, 1937
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Now I can send you a wire only tomorrow, is it not? If possible
Mahadev will send it [today]' I can't issue my statement
immediately. It will be issued only at the appropriate time. Read
my letter of yesterday. I can't decide whether it would be pro-
per to publish the whole correspondence. It is not a question of
permission, but one of propriety from our point of view.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
PURUSHOTTAM BUILDING
OPP. OPERA HOUSE
BOMBAY 4
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 209
