629. LETTER TO DHARMAKUMAR GIRI
October 14, 1945
CHI. DHARMAKUMAR,
I am glad to have your letter. Let me know as soon as
Satyadevi is operated upon. Appear for the examinations again
since you have failed. It is always better to complete what one
has undertaken. I have got the yarn.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DHARMAKUMAR GIRI
BHIMJI KARA'S BUNGALOW
BORIVLI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
