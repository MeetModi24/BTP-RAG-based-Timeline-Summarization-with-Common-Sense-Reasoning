305. LETTER TO S. RADHAKRISHNAN
SRIRAMPUR,
December 17, 1946
DEAR DR. RADHAKRISHNAN 3 ,
Om Prakash gave me your letter yesterday. My congratu-
lations on your decision. I had expected nothing less of you.
You will be in charge as long as you are needed there.
I never dreamt of any of the brothers being Pro-Vice-
Chancellor or holding any of the high offices in the University
or even any office whatsoever except it be required in its inter-
est. They should all be mute servants. Perhaps you have seen
my article on the subject in Harijan.
Dr. Shyamaprasad is an ideal man for the post. Only I
wish he was as sober a Hindu Sabha man as he is an able and
