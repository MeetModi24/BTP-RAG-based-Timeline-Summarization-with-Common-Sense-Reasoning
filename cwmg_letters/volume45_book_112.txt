112. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 12, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your last letter, but not the one which you wrote before
that. I am making inquiries. If you think it necessary to live in
the U.P., by all means do so. How was Brijkishorebabu's
health? What arrangements have been made about his meals?
Give me a detailed report. Tell Swarupraniji that she should keep
up courage. My blessings to Swarup and Krishna. Where is
Jayaprakash at present?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3394
