300. LETTER TO D. D. SATHYE
SEVAGRAM,
October 27, 1944
BHAI SATHYE,
I have carefully gone through your articles on village recon-
struction. I find that they show lack of experience. I don't find in
this work the attention to detail that is evident in the book on
eyes. Yet the work of rural uplift calls for much greater persever-
ance and thoroughness than is required for the care of the eyes.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
D. D. SATHYE
39 PEDDAR ROAD
CUMBALLA HILL
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
78-16
