150. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
CALCUTTA,
August 31, 1947
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I
got both your letters.
Radhakisan may be given
Rs. 75,000. I don't understand why my letter could not reach
you. God knows if something went wrong at this end. I cannot
do everything promptly.
Which Vallabhbhai? It is all right if Bhansali has left. It
was bound to have its effect. He is rather crazy but that does
not affect his nobility.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
