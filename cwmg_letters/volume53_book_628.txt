628. LETTER TO ANNAPURNANAND
March 4, 1933
Tell Shivaprasad to give up reading the newspapers. He can
read the Gita or Yogavasishtha or the Ramayana-Balkanda or Uttar-
kanda-or the dialogue of Socrates on death. He should leave
worldly affairs solely to God.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 172
