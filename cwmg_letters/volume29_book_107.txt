107. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
[Before January 6, 1926]
CHI. MANI,
Jamnalalji writes that you have reached there (Wardha).
Write to me regularly. Take good care of Kamala and Mada-
lasa . Do I have to tell you anything regarding the rest of the
class? Did you write to Devadhar a letter of thanks? If not, write
one in Marathi.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I called on Nandubehn the day I came. She is bearing it
very well.
SMT. MANIBEHN
C/O SHETH JAMNALALJI
WARDHA (C. P.)
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-Manibehn Patelne, p. 33
