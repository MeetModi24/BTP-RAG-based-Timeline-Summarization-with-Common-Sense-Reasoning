590. LETTER TO VIRBHANU
October 8, 1945
BHAI VIRBHANU,
I have your letter. Congratulations to those who have taken
a vow of making cotton hanks. It is good indeed that hanks
are not sent by post. Spinners should learn to save every pie
because it is not theirs but belongs to the poor. It is only
proper that the hanks are handed over to the bhandar. And it
will be all the better if khadi also is sold when it is woven.
DEPUTY SADAN
ATHAVA LINES
SURAT
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
