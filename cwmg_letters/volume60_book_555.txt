555. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
April 8, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
Who told you that I intended to go there? We know no-
thing about the matter here. Please banish the thought from
mind.
Do write and let me have your views about the dairy.
Tell Puratan that there is considerable substance in what
he says. I still need to get more experience about the tannery,
and I am doing so. If I live, I will certainly guide him.
I know the reason for the failure of Sabarmati.
Blessings from
BAPU
