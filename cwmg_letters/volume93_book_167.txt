167. LETTER TO RANI VIDTAVATI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
March 7, 1937
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. Since Dr. Roy knows you now, why do
you need a letter from me at all? Write to him introducing
yourself and go when he is agreeable. Write to me if there is no
reply from Dr. Roy. I am confident that he will answer your
letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy : Gandhi
