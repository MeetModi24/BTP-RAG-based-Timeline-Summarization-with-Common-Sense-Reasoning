728. LETTER TO JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
DELHI,
December 12, 1933
CHI. JAYAPRAKASH,
I have your letter. Your debt has thrown a new light on
the situation and I have tried to explain to you your duty.
Borrowing from another source does not solve the main problem.
How will you repay the loan? What has been arranged about
your expenses? I am hoping that it will not be difficult to
arrange for Rs. 50 to be paid to Rajeshwar every month. I
shall have a talk about it and let you know more definitely.
I hope you are now better. Father too will be well. I have
written in detail to Prabhavati. So I stop here. Also I do not
have the time for more.
Blessings from
BAPU
