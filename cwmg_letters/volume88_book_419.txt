419. LETTER TO KULSUM SAYANI
NEW DELHI,
July 22, 1947
Heaven knows what is in store for us. The old order
changeth giving place to new.
Nothing is settled. Whatever is decided by the C. A., Hindu-
stani with the two scripts remains for you and me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat: Kulsum Sayani Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Also G. N . 8042
