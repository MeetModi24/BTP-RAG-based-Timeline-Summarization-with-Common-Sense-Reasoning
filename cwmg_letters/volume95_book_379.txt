379. LETTER TO PYARELAL
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have written to you after great thought. Keep my last post-
card with you. We shall consider further in Delhi. So, keep yourself
ready. About the mail you can consult Sushila and then decide. It
seems to me we shall have to come here from Delhi. The rest is up to
God.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
