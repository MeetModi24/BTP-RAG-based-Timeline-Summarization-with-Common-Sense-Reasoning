257. LETTER TO DRAUPADI DEVI SHARMA
BOMBAY,
October 22, 1934
CHI. DRAUPADI,
I had your telegram but no letter followed. Krishna must
be all right. Sharma is somewhat disturbed. He has gone to
see Surendraji during my absence and will return to Wardha by
the time I reach there. You must be getting letters from him.
The letter you wrote about him was excellent.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I may reach Wardha on the 30th instant.
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah
Varsha, facing p. 109
