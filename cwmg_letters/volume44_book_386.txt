386. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 3, 1930
CHI. PREMA,
If you have symptoms of jaundice and get acid eructations, I
am sure what you should do is to fast for at least seven days.
While you are fasting, you should drink every day at least four
pounds of water, mixing soda bicarb and/or salt with it. You
should break the fast with fruit juice and then start whey and
rice in required quantities. During the fast, see that you take an
enema and Kuhne bath daily. I am sure that you will not be-
come bed-ridden with a seven-day fast. You may even be able
to do a little work every day. In any case the fast will do you no
harm.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 6689. Courtesy: Premabehn
