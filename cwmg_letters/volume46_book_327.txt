327. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
BARDOLI,
May 31, 1931
CHI. NARANDAS,
Some effective measures should be taken about Lakshmi and
Padma. Request Dr. Kanuga to examine them both. If they do
not recover even under his treatment, we shall have to think
further what to do.
The vaid's treatment does not seem to have helped Ba much.
She seems to benefit rather by the water and the soda bicarb which she
takes. She believes that the medicine merely suppresses the cough.
Both the Gangabehns and Vasumati are here for a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I wrote to Mathew yesterday to tell him that if he wished to
come and if he could be spared from the Ashram, he might come.
Read my letter to Amtulbehn and explain to her the position
regarding her money.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I
