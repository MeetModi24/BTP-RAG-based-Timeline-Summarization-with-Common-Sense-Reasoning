607. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
September 16, 1935
I do not have to go anywhere till the end of this year. Hence I
shall be here in October. Your complaint regarding pencil hand-
writing is correct. I will now make the corrections in ink.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9254. Also C.W. 10104. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
