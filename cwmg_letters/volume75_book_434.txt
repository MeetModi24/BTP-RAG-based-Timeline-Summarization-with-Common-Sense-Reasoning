434. LETTER TO MANGALDAS PAKWASA
February 26, 1942
BHAI MANGALDAS,
I saw your letter of the 16th only just now. Mahadev is ill
and in bed. Does load of work ever leave a man unharmed? May
your labours for adult education bear fruit, and let nobody in
Bombay remain illiterate.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Why do you write in English?
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 4686. Courtesy: Mangaldas Pakwasa
