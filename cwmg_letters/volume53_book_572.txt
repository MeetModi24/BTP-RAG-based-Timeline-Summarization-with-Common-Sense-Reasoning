572. LETTER TO BEGUM MOHAMMAD ALAM
February 26, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
Your letter should serve as an Urdu lesson for me. How-
ever short the letter may be, the alphabets must be well formed.
Your earlier letters were beautiful. I was extremely glad to learn
that both of you were now maintaining good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
BEGUM ALAM
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 29
