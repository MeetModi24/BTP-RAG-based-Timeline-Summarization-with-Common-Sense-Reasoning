255. LETTER TO VARADACHARI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 28, 1928
MY DEAR VARADACHARI,
The enclosed letter and my reply will speak for themselves.
Subbiah fortifies the complaint made by the correspondent and
says that the Tamil Nad Khadi Depot gives no encouragement
whatsoever to voluntary spinners. This should not be.
Yours sincerely,
Enclosure: 1
From a microfilm: S.N. 13674
1 Dated June 23, 1928 (S.N. 13628)
2 Giving year-wise the quantity of khadi produced in India from 1925 to
1928
