243. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA BHAVE
NEW DELHI,
May 23, 1946
CHI. BALKRISHNA,
How are you getting along? It would be good if the
workers there could manage the land, the buildings and the cows.
My feeling is that we cannot fully develop our activity in the
absence of the land and buildings. From the point of view of the
climate it would seem the place is undoubtedly good.
