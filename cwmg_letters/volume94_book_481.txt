481. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
MUSSOORIE,
June 5, 1946
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have received your telegram. I can see that you are not at all
willing to leave Bombay. It would become a little cooler there during
the monsoon but I do not think it can be of much benefit to one in
your situation. There are hill-stations like Kasauli, Dharampur, etc., in
the mountain ranges of Simla. If you spend two years there, even that
would help. And those hill-stations provide fairly good facilities. Of
course, there are many inconveniences and so one has to consider it
carefully. But if you are calm and cheerful within, I have nothing to
say.
I shall leave here on the 8th so that I can be ready for the 9th.
Let us see what happens.
