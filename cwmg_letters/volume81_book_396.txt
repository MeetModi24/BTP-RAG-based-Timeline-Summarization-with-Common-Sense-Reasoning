396. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
September 5, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have your letter about Assam. Pass the budget for Rs. 810.
As for the rest, wait till you get the Committee's report.
See if Gopalaswami has replied about Miss Petersen.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SHYAMLALJI
SECRETARY, KASTURBA MEMORIAL
BAJAJWADI
WARDHA
