149. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
May 4, 1937
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your letters are before me. It is hot here but I do not
feel it much. The nights are cool.
I quite agree with you that Lionel should give up the pre-
sent job and do what he can in England. I wish you could per-
suade him to give up the job and go back to England.
Shummy is right about his fear of infection. You may not
expose your dear ones to risks which they and their parents will
not willingly run.
I am glad you have resumed Sanskrit study. You won't
give up Hindi on any account.
I have already told you about my movements. Departure
9th 10 p.m., Bardoli, 10-11, Tithal, Bulsar, 12 to 30 May.
Mira has been down again with fever. She can't live with-
out me. So she accompanies me this time and I suppose will
insist everytime I go out. It is no use my striving against it any
more than I did against her coming back to Segaon.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3780. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6936
