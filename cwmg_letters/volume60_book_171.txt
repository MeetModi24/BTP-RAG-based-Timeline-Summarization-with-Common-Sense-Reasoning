171. LETTER TO T. T. SHARMAN
January 30, 1935
MY DEAR SHARMAN,
I have forwarded your letter to Kakasaheb Kalelkar who
will go into the matter. He is just now in the South principally
for the purpose of helping Hindi Prachar work. I would ask
you to put yourself in touch with him. His address is: Kaka-
saheb Kalelkar, C/o Hindi Prachar Sabha, 107 Armenian Street,
G. T., Madras.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI T. T. SHARMAN,
EDITOR
"VISWAKARNATAKA DAILY"
BANGALORE CITY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
