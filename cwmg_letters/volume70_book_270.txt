270. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
October 7, 1939
CHI. NARANDAS,
I hope the Rentia Baras programme will pass off well under
Rajkumari Amrit Kaurbehn's chairmanship. I very much like
your decision to make the spinning yajna a permanent feature, and I
hope large numbers of men and women will welcome it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8567. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
