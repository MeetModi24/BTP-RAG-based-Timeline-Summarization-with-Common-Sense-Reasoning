196. LETTER TO DURGA PRASANNA CHATTERJEE
NAINITAL,
May 19, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I have no power to help you in the
matter referred to by you. But let me point out to you that
to gain representation is surely not the way to deal with the
undoubtedly critical economic situation in Bengal.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 17071
