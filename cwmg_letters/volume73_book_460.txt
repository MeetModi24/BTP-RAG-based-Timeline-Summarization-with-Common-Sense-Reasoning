460. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
February 24, 1941
CHI. AMRITLAL,
Those who have observed fast on Shivaratri night, should
remember Siva and decide to become more pure and restrained.
Make this clear to all the children.
Blessings from
BAPU
