251. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
December 14, 1932
DEAR MOTI BABOO,
I have your letter, and now your telegram. I hope you
received my telegram. I can trace through everything you write
and wire your enthusiasm and optimism. It is a good thing
you have decided to see me and to bring Pandit Panchanan
Tarkaratna.
But I want you to be quite sure of your foundation. The
trouble is one for making Hinduism a living faith. There is
therefore no room for compromise on fundamentals. It does not
matter, even if at the present moment, we cannot see the revival
with our physical eyes, but in our impatience we may not com-
promise with untouchability as we know it today. Untouchability
of a type is universal. Our quarrel is against the monstrosity as
we see it today.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. MOTILAL ROY
PRABARTAK SAMGHA, CHANDERNAGORE
From a photostat: G.N. 11040
