294. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM L. BAVISHI
June 26, 1935
BHAI PURUSHOTTAM,
I got your letter.
I do indeed see a miracle in the spinning-wheel.
May your agricultural experiment succeed. Please write to me
from time to time about everything you may think worth wri-
ting about in connection with it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 129. Also C.W. 4748. Courtesy:
Purushottam L. Bavishi
