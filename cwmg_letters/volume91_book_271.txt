271. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ON WAY TO LUCKNOW,
Sunday, August 7, 1921
CHI. MATHURADAS,
These questions have just lain with me. I have gone through
them today and made corrections. Now you can make any use
of them you want. I hope your health is all right now. I tried
hard to come but how could I ? Write to me. My travel plan is
as follows:
8th Lucknow
9th Kanpur
10th Prayagji
11th to 15th Patna-Address: Rajendra Prasad Vakil
16th to 20th Gauhati, Assam Congress Committee
Write to me about Durga's health also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
