623. LETTER TO D. D. SATHYE
October 4, 1941
DEAR DR. SATHYE,
Many thanks for your letter.
I have in mind your suggestions.
I am considering what
is possible. I can only take what people give.
Chhotubhai's suggestion cannot be worked. We need no groups
as suggested. Everyone has to satisfy the lowest test. Nor is Diw-
akar's practicable. Some districts will give more some less. This
is a voluntary effort. I should be wholly satisfied, if only true
men and women went, no matter how few they are.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: D. D. Sathye Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
