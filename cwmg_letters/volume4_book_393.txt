393. LETTER TO KAIKHUSHROO AND ABDUL HUK
[JOHANNESBURG,]
May 25, 1905
MESSRS. JALBHAl SORABJI BROS.
84, FIELD STREET
DURBAN
MY DEAR MESSRS. KAIKHUSHROO AND ABDUL HUK,
Your letter to hand. In the matter of Nuruddin, you should act
strictly in accordance with the instructions given by Rustomjee Sheth.
I have asked him to write to Rustomjee Sheth directly.
Make an advance of up to £7 against his salary to Hussein Issop
if he is trustworthy and his work satisfactory.
Salaams from
M. K. GANDHI
From the Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: Letter Book (1905) : No. 153
