373. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Sunday [April 19, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. I am returning the papers about C.P.
The thing about the educational institution is correct. Please
settle the matter.
Anand can take service as long as she wishes.
My health is all right. I did not get fever again.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers.
