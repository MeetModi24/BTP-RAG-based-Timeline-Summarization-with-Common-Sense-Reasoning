234. LETTER TO SARDUL SINGH CAVEESHAR
POONA,
March 2, 1946
BHAI CAVEESHAR4,
So at long last you have been released ! You have done a
brave deed too. But is there any gain? And why make allegations?
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
SARDAR SARDUL SINGH CAVEESHAR
LAHORE, PUNJAB
