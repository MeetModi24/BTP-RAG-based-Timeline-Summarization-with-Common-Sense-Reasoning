331. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
March 13, 1940
CHI. BABUDI,
Anand must be fine. You can safely carry him and go out.
Sushila may help you to carry him. The child's feet and stom-
ach should be properly covered. You should go out for walks
twice a day. Shakaribehn also must go for walks. Give Ba as
much of your company as possible, sing bhajans to her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10027. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
