468. LETTER TO PERIN CAPTAIN
February 19, 1933
I must dictate if I am to overtake my correspondence. I
was delighted to see the familiar writing and signature. You and
Gosibehn and Kamalabehn shall come on Friday next at 2 p.m.
Mathuradas told me that you were none the worse for your life
1 Vide the preceding item.
in Bijapur Mandir except that you had left there a few pounds
of your weight.
With love to you all from us all.
PERIN CAPTAIN
ORIENT CLUB BUILDING
CHOWPATTY, BOMBAY
