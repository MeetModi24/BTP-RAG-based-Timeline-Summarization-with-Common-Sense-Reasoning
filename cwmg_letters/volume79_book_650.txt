650. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
April 15, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
I have your letter. It is good. Shripat Baba should go to
Bhimavaram. It may be possible to get coconuts here but the
other thing also is needed.
Shastriji should not set his heart on dying in the Ashram.
Manahar's institution is the only proper place and Shastriji should
have the discernment to see it.
Set apart two minutes before <t ~qT. I was under the impres-
sion that we recite the ~1Tf<G prayer after <t ~qT. Let it be halted for
two minutes. But you should do only what you have suggested.
Blessings from
BAPU
