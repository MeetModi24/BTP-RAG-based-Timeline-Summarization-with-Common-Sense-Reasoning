117. LETTER TO KANAIYALAL N. DESAI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 5, 1945
BHAI KANJIBHAI,
You are drowned in a sea of miseries. After all it is Mani-
behn who gives me the news! A man is tempered through adver-
sity, in adversity. So do not consider your miseries as miseries.
Forgetting God is the real unhappiness. So keep Him in your
heart and remain cheerful.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANIBEHN PATEL
C/O DAHYABHAI PATEL
68 MARINE DRIVE BOMBAY
