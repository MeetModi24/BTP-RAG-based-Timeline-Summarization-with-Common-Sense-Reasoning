152. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
[April 8, 1939]
CHI. AMRITLAL,
How is your health? I hear from Krishnachandra that Raj-
bhushan is not making much progress either in Hindi or in spin-
ning. Is there any letter from Vijaya ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7973
