395. LETTER TO URMILADEVI
February 13, 1933
No one has a right to physical rest. Spiritual rest is always
possible. One must have the will power. This will be anasaktiyoga.
Those who work with non-attachment, never tire physically; if
they do, they will soon go to sleep and have all the rest. In the
absence of attachment the atman will surely be at peace.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 141
