443. LETTER TO KOTWAL
March 22, 1935
BHAI KOTWAL 2 ,
I have your letter. I hope the collection goes on daily.
It will become difficult if it is not done regularly. I must have
an account every day. . . .4 Send me the literature.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Veena, Tributes Issue, April-May 1969
