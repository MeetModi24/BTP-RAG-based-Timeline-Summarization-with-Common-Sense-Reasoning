251. LETTER TO DIRENDRANATH DAS GUPTA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 2, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter with a report of khadi work. Satis Babu
was here recently, and I had a talk about you with him. He told
me that he was anxious to help you all he could. I have really
no money apart from the Charkha Sangh fund that I can send
you and the Charkha Sangh fund can only be utilized in the ordi-
nary manner. I do hope therefore that you will write to Satis
Babu and secure help you want through him.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. DIRENDRANATH DAS GUPTA
VIDYASHRAM
BEAMIBAZAR P. O.
SYLHET
From a microfilm: S.N. 19409
