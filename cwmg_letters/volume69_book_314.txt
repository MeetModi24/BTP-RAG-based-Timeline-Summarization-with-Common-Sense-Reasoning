314. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
May 27, 1939
BAPA,
I have read the cutting sent by you. Will they let me set
right the affairs of the Comilla Ashram? Bengal politics are in a
curious state. They wouldn't eat themselves nor let others eat.
In such circumstances what can one do?
The Talcher chapter has become complicated. Let me see.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1183
