97. LETTER TO JAYANT E. PATEL
June 22, 1936
BHAI JAYANTBHAI,
If you cannot leave your wife and if you are truly free from
lust, you can be a true teacher to her and make of her an
accomplished woman. If your brahmacharya is only theoretical,
then it is better given up.
I cannot give you more guidance than this.
JAYANTBHAI E. PATEL
CHAMPAKLAL BROS.
143 PRINCESS STREET
SAI BHUVAN, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
