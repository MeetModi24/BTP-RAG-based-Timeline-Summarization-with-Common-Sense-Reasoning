259. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
Silence Day, April 22, 1929
SISTERS,
Today we are in a village which is without any facilities and,
therefore, I shall have to get ready with the post early. Even after
that, the letters will have to be carried to the post office which
is at a distance of eight miles. There is a lot of discomfort here,
but with it I get plenty of new experiences. Contributions to the
fund continue to come in.
You know that some of the women here are very good at
spinning. Khadi is very much more in use among women here
than in Gujarat. There is no purdah and no custom of keeping
the face veiled, so that the women are strong in body. They also
work very hard.
They have filled my collection bag with jewellery. Many
of them give away their rings, some their bangles and a few
their necklaces. I must have collected about one lakh rupees till
now.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-1: Ashramni Behnone
