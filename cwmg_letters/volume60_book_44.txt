44. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
BIRLA MILLS, DELHI,
December 29, 1934
CHI. SHARMA,
Your fast should be completed today. Give me a full
account soon. I reached here this morning.
Ramdas has gone to Deolali with Swami and will proceed
from there to Bombay.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
facing p. 140
