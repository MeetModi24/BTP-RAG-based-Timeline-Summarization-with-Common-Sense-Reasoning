446. LETTER TO GOPALDAS A. DESAI
PATNA,
May 17, 1947
BHAI . . . ,
I have received your telegram. You had never ceased to
be a Prince. I was quite sure that you were going to regain
the title which had been snatched away from you. This has
appeared in the newspapers. I was not at all surprised. Your
telegram was received today. When it came to Calcutta I had
already left. Now that you have got back your title I hope
and expect you would be humbler, purer and more devoted to
service. With the restoration of the title, your field of service
has not narrowed; rather it has widened. Your responsibility
has increased. You should add glory to the title. We are all
passing through critical times. It is not possible to predict to-
day who amongst us would pass the test.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Biharni Komi Agman, pp. 375-
