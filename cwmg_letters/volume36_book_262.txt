262. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 13, 1928
DEAR SATIS BABU,
You will see how I have used your letter to sustain the case
for khadi. I am anxious to receive your account of the tour that
is now going on and more so to hear from you how it is affecting
your health.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 1588
1 The reference presumably is to "Place of Khadi", 12-4-1928.
