674. LETTER TO KANTI PAREKH
Sunday [On or before September 30, 1928]
CHI. KANTI,
I did not worry at all on receiving the wire about your
having been wounded. But I prayed to God that you should
have been wounded an innocent man. Many of us will have to
be wounded in that manner. It is better that we should be
wounded rather than other innocent people. Wish well even of
the assailant. You have learnt that lesson here. I have received
today Bhai Kalyanji's letter giving all the details. Your wound
is serious but not dangerous. I have forbidden Jayanti and Indu
to be upset. We are learning in the Ashram not to grieve when
our dear ones are wounded or die. Many lovers of God have
strengthened their love during such periods of compulsory rest.
You should do the same.
Blessings from
BAPU
