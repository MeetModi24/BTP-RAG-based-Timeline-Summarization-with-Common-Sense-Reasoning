438. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 3, 1928
BHAISHRI VALLABHBHAI,
I am enclosing herewith a draft of the reply to the Gover-
nor. [The struggle] is developing well. May you live long.
Write or wire when you need me. There are constant reports that
you may be arrested. You will get some rest if you are. If you
are not, haven't we taken a vow never to rest?
BAPU
VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SWARAJ ASHRAM
BARDOLI
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 13
