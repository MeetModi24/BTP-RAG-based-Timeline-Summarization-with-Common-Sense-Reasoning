366. LETTER TO ANANTRAM
MUSSOORIE,
June 5, 1946
CHI. ANANTRAM,
I have your letter. A farmer has to face Nature's vagaries.
Even then he is the mainstay of the world. So I am glad that
you both are working together. Nobody can compete with
Balvantsinha in hard work. I have often seen it.
For your peace of mind now I can think of no other remedy
except Ramanama. It has been proved by experience. There
are two conditions to it.
(1) It should proceed from the heart.
(2) The rules that I have enunciated for its repetition
should be observed.
They are so simple that they can be observed easily. It
is all given in a Harijan issue.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 132
