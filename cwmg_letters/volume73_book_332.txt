332. LETTER TO K. SURYAPRAKASA RAO
January 2, 1941
You need not join the Society referred to in your letter. You
can remain independent and do some constructive work without
joining any society.
With blessings.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 10384. Courtesy: K. Suryaprakasa Rao
