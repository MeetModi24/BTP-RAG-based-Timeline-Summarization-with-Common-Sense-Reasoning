452. LETTER TO REGINALD REYNOLDS
AS AT SEVAGRAM,
January 1, [1946]
DEAR ANGAD 2 ,
I have your letter received by me early morning on New
Year's day. I am penning these lines in the evening before re-
tiring.
I fancy that I wrote to you some time ago. Your letter
just represents you as I have known you. Of course it is good
you have returned to your old love. We should all be glad to
greet you back, even if it be for a short time, if you do succeed
in coming this side.
Agatha is in India. I hope to see her soon.
With love and all good wishes.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 4546. Courtesy: Swarthmore College Peace
Collection
