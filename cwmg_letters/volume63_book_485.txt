485. LETTER TO BALVANTRAI K. THAKORE
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 19, 1936
BHAISHRI BALVANTRAI2,
I am at present busy nursing patients. I finished your letter
little by little yesterday evening. I have no idea what I would
be able to do in Ahmedabad. I regard myself quite unfit to
preside over the Conference. Being a Mahatma, perhaps, I am
supposed to know all things. Would you send your suggestions
in the form of an amendment? I should be glad to meet you,
but how could there be such an occasion? After all I am not
visiting Bombay. I shall be wandering into Ahmedabad.
I expect you are in good health.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
Your letter shall remain confidential. I have destroyed it.
