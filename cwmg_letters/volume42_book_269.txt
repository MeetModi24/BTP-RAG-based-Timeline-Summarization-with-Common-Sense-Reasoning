269. LETTER TO RAMNIKLAL MODI
WARDHA,
December 8, 1929
CHI. RAMNIKLAL,
I have your letter. Anyone who has kept a regular diary for
the whole year may, if he or she wishes, get that book. For the
present, it will be enough if all the people start using a small note-
book like the one I use. Keeping a good diary is an art, and it
can be of much benefit to the person keeping it and to the Ashram.
One can record one's work for the day in the fewest possible words
and give a brief description of the things done.
I had sent a letter for you from Bardoli itself in regard to
Tara. I had enclosed it with Apte's.
Blessings from
BAPU
