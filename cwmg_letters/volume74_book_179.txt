179. LETTER TO VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
June 19, 1941
BHAI VALLABHRAM,
I got your letter. You are doing great tapashcharya indeed.
May you succeed. Come whenever you wish to. I am all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
SHANTISADAN
MOUNT ABU
From Gujarati: C.W. 2914. Courtesy: Vallabhram Vaidya
