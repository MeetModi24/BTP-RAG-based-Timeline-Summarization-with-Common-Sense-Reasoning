384. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 3, 1930
CHI. NARANDAS,
Bhai Sundaram was formerly an inmate of the Ashram. He
has the virtues suggested by his name. He has left the Benares
University and come to us. He will stay there for a few days.
Cultivate his acquaintance and introduce him to the others. I
shall write about other things after I get the mail.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 8109. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
