247. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
POONA,
March 4, 1946
CHI. SHARMA,
I write what I feel after listening to what you had to say
yesterday. I find that you have a lot of family responsibility which
might entail a monthly expense of about Rs. 200. In the circum-
stances, you would not be able to serve the poor, and it is difficult
for you to work here . Therefore you may do whatever you can
independently. You had better give up the trust land which is
in your custody, though there is room for further consideration of
the matter. The other things are going on as they are. If there
is any misunderstanding on my part, clear it.
Blessings from
BAPU
