292. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
Friday [January 30, 1920]
MY DEAR CHILD,
No letter from you today. I am surrounded by too many
people who want me, to be able to write to you a love-letter. I
therefore send you my prayers and all love.
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
Do give me a cheering and cheerful letter if you can.
M. K. G.
From a photostat of the handwritten original in N.A.I.; also My Dear
Child, p. 54
