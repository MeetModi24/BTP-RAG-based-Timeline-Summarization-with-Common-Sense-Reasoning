41. LETTER TO N. S. VARADACHARI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
November 16, 1926
MY DEAR VARADACHARI,
Here is the preface. It should be quite in time. If you have
to suggest any improvement, do not hesitate to make the suggestion.
You may write your own preface and call it foreword or call this
foreword and call yours a preface if you like writing one. You
will see that the essay is free from error.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: S.N. 11246
