88. LETTER TO G. SITARAMA SASTRY
[May 14, 1935]
DEAR SITARAM SASTRIJI,
I approve of the proposal to invite an essay on the barter
system. The prize does not seem to be attractive. Who should be the
judges? They must be [men] with special [qualifi]3cation. [I]
cannot just now think [of] them.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
SHRI G. SITARAMA SASTRY
VINAYA ASHRAM
GUNTUR DIST.
CHANDOLE P. O.
From the original: C W. 9175. Courtesy: Government of Andhra Pradesh
