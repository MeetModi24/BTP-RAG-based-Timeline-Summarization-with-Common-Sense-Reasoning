19. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
DELHI,
April 17, 1946
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
I have your letter. I have not deliberately omitted to an-
swer any [ question]. Tell Viramma to be cautious. I will write
to her if I get the time.
You may engage a music teacher if it is right. Chimanlal
should decide it.
Do not undertake a three-day fast. You should observe
silence, talking when work demands.
It seems that Kambleji has stayed on with Dastaneji . Ask
me if anything has been left out.
You can give this to Viramma to read. I had proposed to
write more letters, but I find I cannot.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 91034. Also C. W. 9158. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
