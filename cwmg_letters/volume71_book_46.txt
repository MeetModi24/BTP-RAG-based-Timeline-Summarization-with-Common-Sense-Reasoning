46. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 14, 1939
CHI. KANCHAN,
I have your letter. What are you doing there? Return com-
pletely restored. Munnalal has not yet returned. The kitchen is
being looked after by A[mtul] S[alaam]. Zohra helps her. Ba
has gone to Delhi. Kanu has gone to Rajkot.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. KANCHANBEHN
C/O SUDHABAI
LADY BUTLER HOSPITAL, KHANDWA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8287. Also C.W. 7065. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
