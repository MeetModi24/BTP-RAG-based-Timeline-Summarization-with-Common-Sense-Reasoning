2. LETTER TO DHIRENDRA MAZUMDAR
PANCHGANI,
July 16, 1946
BHAI DHIREN,
I read Krantikari Charkha from cover to cover. I like it.
None of the arguments irk me. You have correctly understood
the place of the charkha. I want all lovers of the spinning-wheel
to read your booklet and demonstrate its importance in the
villages through their own work.
Blessings from
BAPU
