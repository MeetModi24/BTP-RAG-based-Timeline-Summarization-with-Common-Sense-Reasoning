241. LETTER TO MURIEL LESTER
September 30, 1932
All is over and the much advertised fast is a thing of the
past. It was an experience well worth having, if only for the sake
of the love it evoked from all parts of the world and the wave of
reform that passed from one end of India to the other.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 92
