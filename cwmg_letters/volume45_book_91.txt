91. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
January 9, 1931
CHI. NARANDAS,
I think I am very well today. I am writing this after the
midday meal. I had a normal motion.
In the morning I
took almond paste mixed with water and dates. Just now, I had
fenugreek leaves, liquid curds and dates. The diet tomorrow,
God will decide. There is no cause for worry. The weight is
bound to go down after four days of fasting. It came to 96. If
things go well tomorrow, I shall recover some of the lost weight
during the next week. Tell Mirabehn that I got yesterday the
card she posted on Tuesday. I got your packet only today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I
