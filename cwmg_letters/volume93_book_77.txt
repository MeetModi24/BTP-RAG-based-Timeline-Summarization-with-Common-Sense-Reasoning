77. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
MAGANWADI, WARDHA,
November 8, 1935
CHI. SUMANGAL,
Your letters are received regularly. I destroy them after
reading. I do not see any need to give you any advice at the
moment. Do what you consider best. "None who works for
self-redemption meets with an evil destiny."
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
