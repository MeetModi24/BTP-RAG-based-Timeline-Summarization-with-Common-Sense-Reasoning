32. LETTER TO KESHAV GANDHI
November 20, 1932
CHI. KESHU 1 ,
Other people also get unwanted thoughts as you do. You
should go on repeating Ramanama. The thoughts will cease
then. A teacher should be a man of pure character and should
know his subject well. A student should be humble and hard-
working.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3284
