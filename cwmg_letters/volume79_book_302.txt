302. LETTER TO GAJANAN NAIK
SEVAGRAM,
February 28, 1945
CHI. GAJANAN,
I got your letter today. It does not matter if Kumarappa
does not provide you the money. But should you not have his
consent to whatever you do? If you carry on your activities on
the strength of money when you don't have the consent of the
institution to which you belong, it is sure to cause them some
embarrassment. If you cannot have Kumarappa's consent you have
only to have patience and try to persuade him. Read my reply
again.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
In reply to yours of February 15, 1945. It is irrelevant
in whose name the jaggery complex stands. There should be no
problem for you since you have the custody. You should make
any arrangement you want after consulting everybody. Kuma-
rappa ought to be informed. Obtain his consent.
Blessings from
BAPU
GAJANAN NAIK
SEVAGRAM ASHRAM
