14. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
CALCUTTA,
April 7, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
You won't be disturbed over the news that S. may give you
today. Blood-pressure may help doctors.
It is no help to
patients. When they feel bad, they are bad. Such was my
case last night. The same kind of pain in the back of the head.
But gentle massage by S. brought me round in a few minutes
and there is no return. B.P. was 154/98 at 12 noon. It rose
again later to 170/98 or something like that. So you are not to
worry. When the real alarm comes, you will hear after the
event and then there must be no idle sorrow but greater dedi-
cation to service.
Love.
ROBBER
[PS.]
Did you observe 6th April?
From the original: C.W. 3626. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6435
