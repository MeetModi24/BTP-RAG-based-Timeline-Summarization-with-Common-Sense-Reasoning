175. LETTER TO DR. T. S. S. RAJAN
March 22, 1933
DEAR DR. RAJAN,
Here are some papers in connection with Kannappar Free
Reading Room.
Please tell me what can be done by you and what need be
done by me. You should return the papers when you reply to me.
Yours sincerely,
Encl.
From a microfilm: S.N. 20661
