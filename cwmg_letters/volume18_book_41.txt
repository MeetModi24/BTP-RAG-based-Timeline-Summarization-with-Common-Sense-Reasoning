41. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI 1
[After July 18, 1920]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
I am simply in love with the spinning-wheel and with Kale.
You, too, should keep thinking about this, and this alone, all the
time. You may now entrust the making of the block of the spin-
ning-wheel and of Kale's photograph to Anandanand. Get to
know him personally, too. Collect some facts about Kale's life.
What have been his studies? Take out the patent of the spin-
ning-wheel on your name. If Kale agrees, name the spinning-
wheel "Gangabai Spinning-wheel". He wants it to be named
after himself. If that is still his wish, name it accordingly. Ins-
cribe the name and other particulars on the spinning-wheel in
Devnagari and Urdu scripts. Do not delay the application for the
patent. The Trust has not gone out of my mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 5793. Courtesy: Radhabehn Choudhri
