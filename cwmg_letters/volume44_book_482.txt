482. LETTER TO AMINA QURESHI
November 28, 1930
CHI. AMINA,
I have your letter. I hear from Imam Saheb that you have
become impatient to go to jail. That is not proper, however.
When God wills that you should go to jail, you will get an op-
portunity without seeking it. It is enough that you yourself are
ready. Till that time comes, look after the children. Preserve
good health and do whatever service you can from home. It is
possible in this struggle to serve even from home. It is enough
that you have shed fear.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
How far have you progressed in your study of Urdu?
