12. LETTER TO DR. JOSEPH NUNAN
February 5, 1920
DEAR DR. NUNAN,
I enclose herewith an extract from the Daily Argosy sent by Mr.
Polak. You will see from it that there seems to be a party in British
Guiana opposed to your scheme. They evidently consider it to be
not a Colonization Scheme but one intended to find labour. Will
you please let me know who this party is and what exact position
[it] is that you wish to reach. I have written for Young India an
article setting forth my views; you will have a copy as soon as it is
published. This extract from the Argosy has certainly unsettled me
a bit and it makes me revise ever over what I have written for
Young India. As soon as I get a moment's leisure I propose to put
down my own thoughts of what any Colonization Scheme should
be if it is to give any satisfaction at all and to receive any coun-
tenance even from those [of] you [who] believe in emigration.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: S. N. 7393
