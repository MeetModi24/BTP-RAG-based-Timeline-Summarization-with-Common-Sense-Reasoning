132. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
May 8, 1931
CHI. KUSUM,
I got your two letters. If you were not satisfied with those
letters, neither was I. I did not understand what you wished to
say. But I will not harp on the subject. I understood some-
thing and I will content myself with that.
See that your picketing does not become mechanical. If you
are convinced of what I said, act upon it. Through your picket-
ing, enter the homes of addicts. How long will you go on working
like an automaton?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I shall be leaving this place on Monday.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1821
