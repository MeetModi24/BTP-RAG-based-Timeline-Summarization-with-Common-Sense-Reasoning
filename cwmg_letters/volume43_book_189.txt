189. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
April 7, 1930
CHI. NARANDAS,
I send with this a cheque received from Bhai Shantikumar.
Treat it as an anonymous contribution.
Gangabehn will start from here tomorrow morning.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have permitted Jayantiprasad to go to the U.P.
From Gujarati: C.W. 8097. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
