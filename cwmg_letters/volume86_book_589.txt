589. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
February 9, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
It is now 6.30 a. m. I understand about Abha. It is true
that I have no time at all. Then why do you ask for letters
from me? I am there with you, if you can understand.
Don't be in a hurry. Work only according to your capa-
city. It is enough if you can sit and spin and continue to
double the threads.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Manu sleeps with me. If you want to say anything about
it, you may.
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 578
