459. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
[September 7, 1938]
DEAR HARIBHAU,
Ba and Bapuji have received your letters of the 2nd. Bapuji says that Ba
is now no longer fit for such work and therefore should be spared.
We shall certainly meet in Delhi.
Yours,
MAHADEV
From the Hindi original. Haribhau Upadhyaya Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
