113. LETTER TO H. L. PAUL
August 7, 1911
DEAR MR. PAUL1,
I shall watch the career of your institution with anxious
interest. Pray congratulate Angie for me.
Hoping you are all well,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 4901. Courtesy:
Dr. Cooppan
