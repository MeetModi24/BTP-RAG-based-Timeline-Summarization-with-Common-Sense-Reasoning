611. LETTER TO PYARELAL
February 8, 1947
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have your letter. The diary is not ready today. I do not want
to detain the courier. I also want to read your letter carefully. Hence,
both the diary and the reply will be sent tomorrow. Be completely at
peace and let us stop discussing this subject altogether. Let us only
talk about work.
Blessings from
BAPD
