294. LETTER TO VINAYAK RAO
August 27, 1945
BHAI VINAYAK RAO,
I have your letter. Whatever I do or have done has been
impelled by a feeling of dharma. There is no room for gratitude
in it. Please let me know whatever may be the outcome.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI VINAYAK RAOJI
BAR-AT-LAW
JAMBAGH, HYDERABAD (DN.)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
