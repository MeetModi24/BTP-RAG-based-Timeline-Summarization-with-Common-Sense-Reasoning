334. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
DEAR HENRY,
This will be presented to you by Kamalnayan Bajaj, the eldest son
of Jamnalalji. However much we may fight Great Britain, London is
increasingly becoming out ancient Kashi. Kamalnayan is no exception.
I have advised him to take up a course in the London School of
Economics. Perhaps. . .1
From the original: Gandhi-Polak Correspondence. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
1 Incomplete in the source
