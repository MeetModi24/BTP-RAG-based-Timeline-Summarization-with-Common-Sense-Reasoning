522. LETTER TO E. E. DOYLE
May 12, 1932
DEAR COLONEL DOYLE,
I am deeply grateful to you for the prompt reply you have
sent in respect of my inquiry contained in my letter of the 4th
instant. The addition of three ounces of ghee to Kaka Kalelkar's
diet certainly meets, if I may dare to say so, the physical needs
of his constitution. The assurances contained in your letter do
relieve me of considerable anxiety on the score of these friends
of mine.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Government of Bombay, Home Department, I.G.P. File No. 9
