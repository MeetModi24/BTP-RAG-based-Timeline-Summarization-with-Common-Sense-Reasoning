78. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
ON THE TRAIN,
October 28, 1946
CHI. JAIRAMDAS,
I have your letter. We cannot give up the flag. A pro-
cession or drums may not be necessary but it is our duty to hoist
the flag in a peaceful manner. More can be said only after
observation.
I am going to Bengal. I do not know when I shall return.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
