123. LETTER TO J. H. POLAK
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 7, 1906
DEAR MR. POLAK,
This introduces to you Mr. Ruthnam. You can make your
appointment with him for taking him to the City of London College,
as also for putting him into the boarding house, and you may enter into
a long chat with him to enable you to see his capabilities.
Yours sincerely,
J. H. POLAK, ESQ.
28, GROSVENOR ROAD
CANONBURY, N.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4508
