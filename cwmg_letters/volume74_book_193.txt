193. LETTER TO KANHAIYALAL
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
June 26, 1941
BHAI KANHAIYALAL,
I hope Chi. Kumari Vidyavati's wedding will pass off
without any hitch. May both of them have a long life and serve
the country. Mirabai has returned from Chorwad. She is happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 10052. Also C.W. 6457
