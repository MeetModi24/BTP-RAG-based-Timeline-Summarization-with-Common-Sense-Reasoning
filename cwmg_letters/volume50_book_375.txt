375. LETTER TO MAHENDRA V. DESAI
August 14, 1932
CHI. MANU,
Exercise books are meant for doing lessons. If you use loose
sheets of paper for writing letters, they will cost you less. For
learning good handwriting you should have a reed-pen. If you
carefully practise writing in a copy-book, your handwriting will
certainly improve. It was a little better this time.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7435. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
