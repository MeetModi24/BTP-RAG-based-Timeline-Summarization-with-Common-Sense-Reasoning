564. LETTER TO BHAU PANSE
February 26, 1933
CHI. BHAU,
If Rajkot agrees with you, you should stay there for a longer
time. While there, you may help Jamnadas in any way you can.
If you are completely cured there, the improvement will remain
even after you have left Rajkot. Let me know your daily pro-
gramme of work and also your diet.
Those who have money ought to help others. Those who
cannot meet their needs in any other way, like totally disabled
people and sannyasis (genuine), etc., deserve to be helped.
The mendicant may be deserving if he gives all his time to
social service and accepts no other return.
The woman certainly deserves help.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6750
