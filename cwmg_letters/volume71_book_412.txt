412. LETTER TO AKBAR HYDARI
April 14, 1940
DEAR SIR AKBAR,
You have given me stone when I had asked for bread.
I fail to see the slightest connection between a simple perfor-
mance of a promise and the big question of communal unity. And
how can unity be brought about in the face of events like Bidar,
if the papers are to be believed? As you know I have put a gag
on myself regarding Hyderabad but I feel that I must not do so
now, if I am to do my duty by the people of Hyderabad. This
treatment of the State Congress and the reported happenings in
Bidar have filled me with misgivings.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 10253
