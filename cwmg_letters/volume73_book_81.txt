81. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 14, 1940
CHI. MIRA,
I am glad you are in your new abode. There is a ring of
sadness about the few lines you have sent me. I want you to
feel inner joy and strength. God be with you.
Love.
BAPU
[Ps.]
Your khadi has now come.
If you gave me instructions
about it, I have forgotten. Shall I keep it for you?
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6461. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10056
