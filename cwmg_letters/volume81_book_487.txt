487. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
September 24, 1945
It hurts me that I shall be leaving this time without see-
ing you. But in my present weak condition it is best not to
go anywhere. I was very happy to learn that your health is
better now. I think I will soon regain strength.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 207
