38. LETTER TO CHAKRAYYA
October 16, 1941
CHI. CHAKRAYYA,
I have your letter. What you write is clear. It is good. Convey
promptly to me whatever comes to your mind. I do not like
your persistent fever. Let us see what the doctor does and says.
Will it not be good to buy a case for the watch? I shall arrange
to get one for you from there.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI CHAKRAYYA,
"AROGYA BHAVAN"
AVADI, MADRAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9113
