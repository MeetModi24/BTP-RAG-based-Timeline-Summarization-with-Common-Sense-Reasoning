463. LETTER TO VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
September 2, 1941
BHAI VALLABHRAM,
You are having good experience. Let it result in your render-
ing truly great service of the people.
SHRI VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
BIRLA HOUSE
P.O. UTTARKASHI
DISTT. TEHRI GARHWAL
From Gujarati: C.W. 2915. Courtesy: Vallabhram Vaidya
