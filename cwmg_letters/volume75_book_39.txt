39. LETTER TO KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
[After October 16, 1941]
DEAR SISTER,
You are bound to wait for a reply. If it does not come during
the next few days, you will write a brief note saying that you will
regard the silence as consent and move out as stated in your letter.
Wait for a while before writing to Iftikhar.
Farid says he may bring Satyawati here.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
