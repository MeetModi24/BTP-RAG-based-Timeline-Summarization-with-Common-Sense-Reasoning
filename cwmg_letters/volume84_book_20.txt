20. LETTER TO RAMMANOHAR LOHIA
DELHI,
April 17, 1946
BHAI RAMMANOHAR 4 ,
Bhai Humayun is bringing this letter. I had thought that
you would proceed further after meeting me in Delhi. And when
you went to Calcutta I thought that you did so because you
regarded it your foremost duty to perform your father's shraddha.
I am sure that we shall meet somewhere at some mutually
convenient time. Does anyone attend to the social service
programme of your father?
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. RAMMANOHAR LOHIA
