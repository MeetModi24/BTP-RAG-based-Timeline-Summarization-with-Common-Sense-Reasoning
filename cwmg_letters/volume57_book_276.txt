276. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
PATNA,
March 12, 1934
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I got your letter. I am enclosing a letter to Gangabehn.
You may use it if necessary. Let me know if there is anybody
else who can persuade her. Would you say that we can pay for
weaving a higher rate of wage than that fixed by Shankerlal?
I hear from Prabhudas from time to time. I reached Patna last
night. For the present I have decided to stay in Bihar. I shall
be able to arrive at a definite decision after I have been here for
some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9582.
Courtesy: Chhaganlal
Gandhi
