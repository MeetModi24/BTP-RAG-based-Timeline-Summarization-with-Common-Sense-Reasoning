349. LETTER TO DR. LAXMIPATHI
SEVAGRAM,
November 6, 1944
DEAR LAXMIPATHI,
Mine is a most unenviable position. You remember how
helpless you were when Ashadevi's son died. I do not want
allopathic medicine and yet I do not get out of its coil. You are
very good but you refuse to see things as they are. I am now
trying Pandit Shiv Sharma. I do not know how I shall fare.
Yours,
BAPU
DR. LAXMIPATHI
BEZWADA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
