146. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Jeth Vad 5 [June 21, 1924]
There should be no difficulty in your coming here at the
time of the A.I.C.C. session, but you should give up the idea of
attending the meetings. Maulana Mahomed Ali alone, I think,
is issuing passes. The less of them are issued the better.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 71
