538. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
December 3, 1944
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Your letter. I showed it to Satis Babu. He says that you
have to stay in the village itself and do what is required. The
problem of water, etc., can be met; he has told you something
about it and will talk to you more. You should take it that
until the 31st you will not hear from me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1951
