727. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
April 27, 1941
BA,
Sushila will tell you everything about my ending the fast.
Now there is no reason at all to worry. Durga's condition may
be considered satisfactory. Trivediji is in midstream. I am not
writing separately to Devdas and Lakshmi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati
original: Pyarelal Papers.
Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
