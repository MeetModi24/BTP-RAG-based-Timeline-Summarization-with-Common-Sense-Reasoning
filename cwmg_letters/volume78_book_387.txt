387. LETTER TO ERNEST F. PATON
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
MY DEAR ERNEST,
I have your heartening letter. You will not mind if I find it
helpful to publish your letter. I take it you are also a doctor.
Have we ever met?
Yours,
BAPU
ERNEST F. PATON
CHRISTU KULA ASHRAM
TIRUPATTUR
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
