470. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 2/3, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I have now begun to send you business. You know that Rat-
lam has passed unconscionable sentences on certain members of the
Praja Parishad. The men are important people. Do you know
anybody there? If you want any literature, it can be supplied. If
you do not know, you need not worry.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Your note in Hindi. The apples received from [you] were not
each wrapped in paper. Should they not have been?
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4065. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7374
