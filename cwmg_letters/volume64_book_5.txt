5. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
November 4, 1936
CHI. NARAHARI,
The haste this time, and the crowds, were terrible. So, too,
the number of accidents.
I could not even have a look at the Ashram. How could
I when my mind was in Segaon?
I keep on receiving complaints from Rajaji. Give him the
enclosed letter. I have asked Joshi of Bhavnagar to discuss his
scheme with you. It will be very nice if there is something
worth while in it. Let me know.
Deliver the accompanying letters.
It still pricks me that I was not able to spend some time
with the girls.
Blessings from
BAPU
