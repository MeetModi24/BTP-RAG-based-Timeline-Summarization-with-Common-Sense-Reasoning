76. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI PATEL
May 1, 1933
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
The fast which commences from Monday is intended as
punishment for those also who believe that they are too weak to
fulfil the vow they have taken—not to reproach them but to help
them.
Lakshmibehn has complained against you that you have been
talking ill about the Ashram. I am sure there must be some error
in this.
BAPU
