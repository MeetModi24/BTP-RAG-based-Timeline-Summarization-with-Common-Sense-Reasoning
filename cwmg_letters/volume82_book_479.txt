479. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
SODEPUR,
January 5, 1946
CHI. JIVANJI,
The booklet on the constructive programme was received
only yesterday. It has taken a rather long time. Why this
delay? It took me some time to find the price. What was the
intention in giving it on the back-page? Or is there merit in
novelty as such? Generally the reader's habit is to see the cover
page and look for the price, etc. There is no signature at the
end of the preface. I wonder whether you thought that, since
the preface was by the author himself, there was no need for
his signature. But that does not seem likely as Chi. Kanaiya
also reminds me. Moreover, I had drawn your attention speci-
fically to this point in my letter of December 7, 1945. The
copies are very few. A lot of them could have been sold here.
What happened about Hindi, Gujarati, Urdu, etc.?
Blessings from
BAPU
JIVANJI DESAI
P. B. 105
AHMEDABAD
