650. LETTER TO MANORANJAN CHAUDHURI
September 30, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I am making use of the information you have given me
about tree cotton grown by Shri Kiranprova Devi. I would
like you to send me a sample of her cotton and the seeds, if availa-
ble. I would like to spin it myself.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI MANORANJAN CHAUDHURI
B. P. HINDUNISA
211 BOWBAZAR STREET, CALCUTTA
From a copy: C. W. 9769
