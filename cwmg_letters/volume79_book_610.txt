610. LETTER TO GOPE GURBUXANI
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
April 7, 1945
CHI. GURBUXANI,
I have the letter from you two. I remember both of you at
least while spinning. I am here till the 14th, maybe till the
20th. Then Mahabaleshwar. Let us see what God makes me do.
It is good that you have met Mother.
Blessings from
BAPU
