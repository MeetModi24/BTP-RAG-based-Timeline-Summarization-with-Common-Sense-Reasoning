423. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
WARDHA,
May 3, 1936
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
My right hand needs rest. You will perhaps like to read
the enclosed. It need not be returned.
Khurshed has written to me about her new suggestion regar-
ding the scope of Kamala Memorial. If it is a substitute for the
hospital, it is in my opinion inadmissible. And it cannot be
carried out for 3 lacs.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1936. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
