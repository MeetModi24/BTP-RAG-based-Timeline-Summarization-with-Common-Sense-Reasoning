302. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
November 26, 1933
CHI. RAMA,
You may write to me. How do you feel there now, both
physically and mentally?
Is there any difficulty about the arm
now? How is Vimu ? Does Dhiru write to you? I hope you
got Nanabhai's letter. There is no cause for anxiety.
What is Nirmala Joshi's address?
Did you get a letter from Chhaganlal? I am eager to know
whether he got my long letter. Ask Vimu to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5358
