677. LETTER TO MOTIBHAI
ASHRAM,
October 9, 1928
BHAI MOTIBHAI,
You have raised a relevant question. The feeling of high
and low has become deeply ingrained in us. The use of the sing-
ular in speaking to persons is its least expression. The feeling
is the least in the Ashram, but I see that it is there. Continue,
both of you, to exercise self-restraint to the best of your ability.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/12
