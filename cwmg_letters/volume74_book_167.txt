167. LETTER TO WALCHAND HIRACHAND
June 13, 1941
BHAI WALCHAND 1 ,
I am in receipt of your letter. As Rajendra Babu is go-
ing [to Vishakhapatnam] there should be no necessity of a message
from me. In view, however, of my old association with the late
Sheth Narottam I can quite understand that you would expect
my blessings on this occasion. May your enterprise succeed and
may it benefit the whole country.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 120
