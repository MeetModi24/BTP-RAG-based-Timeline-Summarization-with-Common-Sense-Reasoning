432. LETTER TO STUDENTS OF SEVA SADAN
HIGH SCHOOL
February 17, 1933
DEAR YOUNG FRIENDS,
I have your letter. I shall be glad to see you all on Monday
next, the 20th instant, at 2.30 p.m. You will not take more than
30 minutes and if you will come with questions prepared before-
hand, we should be able to go through a lot of work in 30 minutes.
Yours sincerely,
THE SECRETARY
STANDARD VI
SEVA SADAN HIGH SCHOOL
POONA CITY
From a microfilm: S.N. 20271
