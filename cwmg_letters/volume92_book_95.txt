95. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
SABARMATI,
August 12, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your two letters. The League of Nations business I
do not understand. It cannot unite hearts.
What I want is
a union of hearts.
I am young at 60 for the work for which I have confidence.
I should feel old at 16 for that about which I have no confidence.
You must get well quick and feel young like Mrs. Besant
who is nearly 90.
Yours,
C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
SALEM
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/111
