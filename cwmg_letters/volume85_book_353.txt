353. LETTER TO RAMANAND TIRTH
NEW DELHI,
September 9, 1946
SWAMIJI,
I have your letter. I am carrying on correspondence. Now
you are coming on the 15th. I shall be here. We shall have
a talk.
Blessings from
BAPU
SWAMI RAMANAND TIRTH
