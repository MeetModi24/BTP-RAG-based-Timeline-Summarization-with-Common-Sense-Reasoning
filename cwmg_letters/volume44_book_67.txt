67. LETTER TO RAMI GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 26, 1930
CHI. RAMI,
I got your letter. Ba had written to me about you. How
did it happen that Kusum fell ill so seriously? Ask Kunvarji
to write to me. I don't write a separate letter to him. Write
to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5061. Courtesy: Surendra
Mashruwala
