483. LETTER TO M. A. JINNAH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 24, 1938
DEAR MR. JINNAH,
I thank you for your letter. I have read your letter to Jawa-
harlal also. I observe that both the letters invite, not written re-
plies but a personal discussion. I do not know whether it will take
place in the first instance between you and Jawaharlal or, now
that Subhas Bose succeeds him, between you and the latter. If
you desire that before this there should be a talk between you
and me, I would be delighted to see you in Segaon any time
which is convenient to you before the 10th of March after which,
if health permits, I might have to go to Bengal. So far as I am
concerned, just as on the Hindu-Muslim question I was guided by
Dr. Ansari, now that he is no more in our midst, I have accept-
ed Maulana Abul Kalam Azad as my guide. My suggestion,
therefore, to you is that conversation should be opened in the
first instance as between you and Maulana Saheb. But in every
case regard me as at your disposal.
Yours sincerely,
M . K . GANDHI
The Bombay Chronicle, 16-6-1938
