174. LETTER TO RAVI SHANKAR SHUKLA
NEW DELHI,
June 26, 1947
BHAI SHUKLAJI,
I have your letter and all the documents. I have read
the ones in Hindi and English. I do not like these opinions.
I have expressed my views through the newspapers.
Yes, Hassan's conduct was not at all correct but it will
not be proper to ask for his resignation. However, if there is
some other complaint, then that can be a reason for demand-
ing his resignation.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am writing to Dr. Hassan.
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
