232. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
September 21, 1947
CHI. SATISBABU,
I have your telegram. When Hemprabhadevi sent for me, I
had to go to Calcutta. She has written to Bisen. I learnt from
her letter that Babua is still not well and now Kshitish Babu
is ill. Who will look after Sodepur in these circumstances? We
have to think of all this.
How is your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9287
