324. LETTER TO BALWANTRAI MEHTA
January 25, 1942
BHAI BALWANTRAI,
I was able to read your letter only today. You should certainly
be relieved. Talk to Prof. Dantawala. Let me know his require-
ments. So long as alternate arrangements are not made and your
visits are necessary, please do the needful. If Himmatlal takes over,
what else can we hope for?
Send him immediately to the office. Dr. Menon will certainly
look after the general work in your absence. R. K. will supervise
it. Jamnalalji has decided to keep the office at Sevagram for the
present. The other staff is freshly recruited, isn't it? You will
have to go there once.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
