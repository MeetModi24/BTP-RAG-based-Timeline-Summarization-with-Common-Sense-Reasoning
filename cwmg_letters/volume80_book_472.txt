472. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS TANDON
June 8, 1945
BHAI PURUSHOTTAMDAS TANDONJI,
Please see the enclosed. Do what you think proper. I have
written to the lady advising her to see you. I am awaiting your
reply to my letter about Hindustani.
BAPU
PURUSHOTTAMDAS TANDONJI
10 CROSSWATH ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
