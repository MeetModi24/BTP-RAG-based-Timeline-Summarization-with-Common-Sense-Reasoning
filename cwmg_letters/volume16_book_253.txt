253. LETTER TO LUCHMAYA
MAURITIUS 1
January 13, 1920
DEAR MR. LUCHMAYA,
I have your letter. I hope to be in Bombay on the 16th. Will
you be able to see me at Mr. Revashankar's bungalow? I have
gone through Mr. Wilberforce's letter, but I shall learn from you
the position more fully. I shall be so busy in Bombay that I would
like you not to take up much time. You will pardon my saying
this, but I am doing so in order that you may collect your
thoughts and so arrange them that you may be able to tell me
all you wish in a few minutes' time.
From a photostat of a handwritten copy: S.N. 7051
