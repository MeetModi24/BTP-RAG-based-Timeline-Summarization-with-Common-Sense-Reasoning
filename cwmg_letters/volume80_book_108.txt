108. LETTER TO GOVIND REDDY
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 3, 1945
CHI. GOVIND,
I have heard that you have become very naughty. It is shame-
ful, if true. You have been kept for a special purpose. You
have to be an ideal child. You will become one, won't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 5873. Courtesy: Munnalal G.
Shah
