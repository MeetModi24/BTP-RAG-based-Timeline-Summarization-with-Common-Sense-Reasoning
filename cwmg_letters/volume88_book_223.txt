223. LETTER TO KULSUM SAYANI
NEW DELHI,
July 1, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER KULSUM,
I have received your letter. Convey my blessings to Hamid
and Zarina. May they serve the country. There is no mention
of the daughter-in-law in the card!
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Begum Kulsum Sayani Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
