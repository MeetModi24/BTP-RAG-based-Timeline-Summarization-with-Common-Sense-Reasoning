176. LETTER TO DURGASHANKAR
SIMLA,
May 12, 1946
BHAI DURGASHANKAR,
Your letter is good. I am sorry that I cannot take your
help from Jaora. I know the shortcomings. You will help me if
you check the issues and continue to point out the errors to me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
LALA DURGASHANKAR VAKIL
BAR ASSOCIATION
JEEVAN BASERA
JAORA, MADHAYA BHARAT
