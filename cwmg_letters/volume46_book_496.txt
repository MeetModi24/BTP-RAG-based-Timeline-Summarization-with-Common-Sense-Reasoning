496. LETTER TO K. F. NARIMAN
BORSAD,
June 17, 1931
MY DEAR NARIMAN,
I have your letters about the Pathan Abdul Ghaffar Khan
and his son. I shall begin working on them as soon as possible.
With reference to the National Flag Film I would like you to
go into the regulations governing the censorship of films and
enter first into correspondence with the Board of Censors and find
their reason. Don't you think that this is the first step to take?
Yours sincerely,
SYT. K. F. NARIMAN
CONGRESS HOUSE, BOMBAY
