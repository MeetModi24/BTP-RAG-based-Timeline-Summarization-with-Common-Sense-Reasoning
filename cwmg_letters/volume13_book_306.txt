306. LETTER TO W. B. HEYCOCK
May 14, 1917
DEAR MR. HEYCOCK,
I beg to refer you to the enclosed. All kinds of rumours have
come before me. Pressure is being put upon me to make a state-
ment but I don't want to make any unauthorised statement. Will
you kindly let me know for purposes of publication the damage
caused by the fire, the nature of the outwork burnt, whether it was
inhabited or otherwise protected and whether any connection has
been shown between my presence in Champaran and the fire. I
am sending a special messenger who will await answer.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
Select Documents on Mahatma Gandhi's Movement in Champaran, No. 79 (A),
p. 136
