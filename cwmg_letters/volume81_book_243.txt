243. LETTER TO PRAYAG DUTT SHUKLA
August 21, 1945
BHAI PRAYAG DUTT,
Pandit Hrishikeshji did not come or see me. His wife saw
me. Please write to me. I am going to Poona today. I shall
be staying in Dr. Dinshaw Mehta's Arogya Bhavan.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
PRAYAG DUTT SHUKLA
SITABURDI
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
