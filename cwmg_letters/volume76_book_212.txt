212. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 2, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
Today seems to be dies non for you. No letter for the first
time.
This is to tell you that Sushila has passed her examination.
Thank God! She deserved to pass.
Mridula and Dhebarbhai are here. So.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4126. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7435
