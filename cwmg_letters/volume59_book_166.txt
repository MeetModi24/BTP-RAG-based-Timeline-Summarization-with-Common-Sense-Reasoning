166. LETTER TO KAKALBHAI KOTHARI
October 5, 1934
BHAI KAKALBHAI,
I have your letter.
The mess about the Indian States proves
the truth of the ancient saying : Man is his own friend or foe.
No outside power can harm him. What is happening is all wrong.
Each one of us is labouring under his own delusion.
Shall we
all pray, "O God! save us from our friends-we will offer
thanks even if you save us only from ourselves?"
Blessings from
BAPU
KAKALBHAI
SONGADH
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
