27. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
NADIAD,
Wednesday [February 20, 1918]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
Anasuyabehn needs a man badly. So I have decided to put you there.
Render her all help. Stay only with her, get acquainted with all the labourers
and keep them peaceful. See to all. . . . 3 etc. Keep me informed.
I will see the Collector tomorrow.
I expect to meet you on the 25th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32869
