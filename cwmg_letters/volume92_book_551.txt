551. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 14, 1932
CHI. MANGALA,
May you live for many years and grow into a fine sevika.
May God sustain you in your vow. It is very hard to be non-
violent at heart, but it is easy for one who has faith in God.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11100. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
