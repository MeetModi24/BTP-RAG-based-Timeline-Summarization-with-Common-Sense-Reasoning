639. LETTER TO RAMDASIA MAZHABI SIKH FEDERATION
KAZIRKHIL,
RAMGANJ P.S.,
NOAKHALI DISTT.,
February 28, 1947
DEAR FRIENDS,
I have your letter signed by eleven members of your group. The
names are typed instead of there being original signatures. I must
confess my ignorance of the existence of this group. I do not know
what I can do to remedy the difficulty you complain of. I feel helpless.
Yours sincerely,
THE RAMDASIA MAZHABI SIKH FEDERATION
THROUGH THE PRESIDENT
MISTRI HARNAM SINGH
P.B. SAMRALA
DISTT. LUDHIANA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
