690. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
October 19, 1945
BAPA,
I hope the climate of Wardha suits you. See the enclosed
letter.
Do what you think proper after reading it. She had come
to me earlier about it and I remember having given some opinion.
KASTURBA SMARAK NIDHI
BAJAJWADI
WARDHA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
