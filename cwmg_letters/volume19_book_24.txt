24. LETTER TO DR. MUHAMMAD IQBAL
[Before November 27, 1920]
DEAR DR. IQBAL 2 ,
The Muslim National University calls you. If you could
but take charge of it, I am sure that it will prosper under your
cultured leadership. Hakimji Ajmal Khan and Dr. Ansari and
of course the Ali Bro[ther]s desire it. I wish you could see your
way to respond. Your expenses on a scale suited to the new
awakening can be easily guaranteed. Please reply Allahabad
care Pundit Nehru.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 7361a
