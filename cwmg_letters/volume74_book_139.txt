139. LETTER TO VINA CHATTERJEE AND
ABHA CHATTERJEE
May 30, 1941
CHI. VINA AND ABHA,
Your letter is full of lies. Father was with you and you did
not at all ask him. Can't you be truthful enough to say that
you liked the thing, so you took it?
Blessings from
BAPU

terjee
