110. LETTER TO LAKSHMISWAR SINHA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 15, 1939
MY DEAR LAKSHMISWAR4,
1 hope you will not take the final step without consultation
with Aryanayakam. 1 would like to see you with him, if that is
at all possible.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: C.W. 1473. Courtesy: A. K. Sen
