10. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
September 18, 1940
You must have seen the resolution passed by the All-India
Congress Committee at its session on 16th instant. But, for
ready reference, I send a copy of it, as also a newspaper cutting
containing a fairly accurate summary of my speech in Hindustani
and English.
I now request an interview at your early convenience. I seek
it both as guide of the Congress and your friend which you have
allowed me to call myself.
From a photostat: C.W. 10163. Courtesy: India Office
