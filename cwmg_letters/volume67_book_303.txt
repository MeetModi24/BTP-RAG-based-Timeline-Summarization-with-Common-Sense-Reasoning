303. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
August 3, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
Pandurang and Dabke have gone where all of us will go
sooner or later. I kept back this fact from you. But why keep
it a secret? It is necessary that you should not let the news af-
fect you. Is not our study of the Gita tested in digesting such
news calmly?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10904
