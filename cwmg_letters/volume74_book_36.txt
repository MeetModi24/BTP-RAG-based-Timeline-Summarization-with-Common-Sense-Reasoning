36. LETTER TO MARGARET JONES
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 3, 1941
DEAR KAMALA,
Yes, I had all your letters. I should have written earlier, but
I could not do so. I was glad you were doing so well, and were
keeping to the simple life. Do continue to write.
Love.
BAPU
Bapu—Conversations and Correspondence with Mahatma Gandhi, p. 191
