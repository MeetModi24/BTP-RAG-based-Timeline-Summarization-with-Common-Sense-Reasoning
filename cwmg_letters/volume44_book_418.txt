418. LETTER TO SHANTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 10, 1930
CHI. SHANTA,
Your letter. Kashinath writes that you have fever again.
How is it? You must be careful in everything. It is well you
have gone with Gangabehn. You can come back if the climate
there does not suit you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5260
