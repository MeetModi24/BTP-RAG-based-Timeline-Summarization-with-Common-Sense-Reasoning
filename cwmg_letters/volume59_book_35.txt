35. LETTER TO SANTDAS MANGHARAM
September 20, 1934
MY DEAR SANTDAS,
I have your letter. I had your telegram also. Atma's
illness would prove a blessing in disguise if he actually comes
here, and that with Mirabehn. There is no doubt that he will
profit greatly by her company.
Yes, prayer is a mighty power. We do not realize it be-
cause we do not see it through our senses.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI SANTDAS MANGHARAM
ADVOCATE, HYDERABAD (SIND)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
