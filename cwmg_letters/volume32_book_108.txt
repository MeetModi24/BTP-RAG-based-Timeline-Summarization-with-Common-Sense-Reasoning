108. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
Monday [December 6, 1926]
BHAI DEVCHANDBHAI,
I send the accompanying for you to read and preserve. I have
sent a suitable reply. I have written to tell him that he need
say nothing on the political situation, [and also that] we shall
not ask him often to go to Kathiawar.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5722
