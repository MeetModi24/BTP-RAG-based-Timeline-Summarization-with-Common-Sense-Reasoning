452. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
[After April 23, 1934]
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I was wondering why there was no letter from you so far, but
I got your letter today. You should not have the diet you are
taking at all the four meals. There is no gain from having too
great a variety in your food.
It would be better to have plain
roti. Going out for walks and the hip-bath are absolutely essen-
tial in my opinion. You must in consultation with your elder
brother chalk out a regular routine for yourself. I do not see
any harm in taking the trip to Badrinath and Kedarnath if your
strength is up to it. But I do wish you to get well somehow
and have peace of mind.
Tell your mother that I shall have a standing complaint
against her. There can be no dharma in refusing to take food
at the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2390
1 The text suggests that this letter was written some time after the letter
of April 23, 1934; vide p. 429.
