455. LETTER TO RADHA
PANCHGANI,
June 7, 1945
CHI. RADHABEHN,
Read this. I intend to write to you only in the national
language. Dr. Mahmud gave me your English letter. It is beauti-
ful. But we want to see beauty in the national language. Your
son will have come by now. Convey my blessings to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
