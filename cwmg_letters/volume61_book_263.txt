263. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
June 20, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
Let Panditji start a music class if he wishes. The expenses
seem all right. There must be a regular curriculum for inclusion
in the prospectus. The progress which the pupil will make during
the whole period and annually must be indicated in detail.
Even if Vanamala's fever has subsided, it will benefit her
if she sticks to the diet I have suggested. You have certainly done
well in imposing on yourself that restriction. It doesn't matter if
you cannot observe it outside. Vanamala must take regular walks
and the baths, too, of course.
How many boys and girls have joined the new school? Who
are the teachers? What will the expenses come to every month?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Is Jayabehn there at present? What does she teach?
