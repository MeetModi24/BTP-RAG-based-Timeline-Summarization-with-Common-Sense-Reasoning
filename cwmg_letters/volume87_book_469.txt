469. LETTER TO A MINISTER
PATNA,
May 19, 1947
. . . Got your letter today. The meeting must have been held
yesterday. I hope it was a success. I know that we have
taken an important step by changing the name. How wonder-
ful if, by our work, we became worthy of our name!
[From Hindi]
Biharni Komi Agman, p. 385
