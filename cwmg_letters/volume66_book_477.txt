477. LETTER TO SHAM LAL
HARIPURA,
February 20, 1938
DEAR LALA SHAM LAL1,
Please tell the hunger-strikers that I feel deeply grieved that
they have disregarded my appeal. I wish they would still
listen and give up the strike. I would try for their release even
by going to the Punjab as soon as doctors give me the permission
and I have fulfilled previous obligations. The Congress Reso-
lution too should induce abandonment of the strike. I do hope
this appeal will not be in vain.
Yours sincerely,
M . K . GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 1284
