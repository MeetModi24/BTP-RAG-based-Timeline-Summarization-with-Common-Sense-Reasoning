479. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
Thursday [September 23, 1926]
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
How naughty? But it is quite right to have complete rest.
You must not think of going tomorrow nor the day after unless
you are quite ready. I would come to you if you want me in
body. If not, the body remains here and the spirit watches by
your bedside.
With love,
Yours,
MOHAN
From a photostat: G.N. 2634
