287. LETTER TO DRAUPADI SHARMA
March 9, 1936
CHI. DRAUPADI,
I have a letter from Sharma from which I learn that you
are not keeping well. What is this? I am writing this, for I am
now permitted to do some writing. As I am in Delhi it would be
fine if you could come up to see me. Amtussalaam is of course
here already.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
