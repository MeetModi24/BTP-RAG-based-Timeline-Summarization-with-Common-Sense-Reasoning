201. LETTER TO UNDER SECRETARY FOR COLONIES
[LONDON,]
August 6, 1909
SIR,
I beg to acknowledge your letter of the 4th instant, in which you
say that Lord Crewe will be willing to see my colleague and me on Tues-
day the 10th instant at 3.30 p.m., in reference to the position of British
Indians in the Transvaal. My colleague and I will wait upon His Lord-
ship at the time.
I remain, etc.,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4984
