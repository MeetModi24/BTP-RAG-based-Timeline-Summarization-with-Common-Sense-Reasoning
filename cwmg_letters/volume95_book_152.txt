152. LETTER TO KASHINATH TRIVEDI
Tuesday [ 1940 ]1
BHAI KASHINATH,
Received your letter. I am sending this much today. The
booklet is all right but the absence of the table of contents is a great
fault. Let Ramnarayan look after the Hindi Navajivan and you join
the Ajmer struggle. I would like that arrangement. You should not
go till it becomes absolutely necessary. At present men are required
there also. Mahadev has gone.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : G.N. 5287
