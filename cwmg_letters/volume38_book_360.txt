360. LETTER TO TARA SHANKER
As AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 30, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Personally I do think that you should
remain firm and cut off all contact even if there is the slightest
danger of her dying. In seeking to do the right often we have
to risk the death of dearest ones. I am quite clear in my mind
that all contact must cease.
I return the Hindi letter.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. TARA SHANKER
50 BUTLER HOSTEL
BADSHAHBAGH, LUCKNOW
From a microfilm: S.N. 13826
