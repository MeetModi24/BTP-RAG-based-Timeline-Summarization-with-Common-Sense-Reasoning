438. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
SEGAON,
January 30, 1938
CHI. SHARDA,
I passed two days without writing to you.
But today I have
got to write. There should have been a letter from you today.
You must be happy. You must be keeping well. I hope you
are not finding it too cold.
I must have your letters regularly
and in detail. Did Devdas, Brajkisan and Pyarelal's mother
meet you? Get well soon and come over. I am fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Blessings to Saraswatibehn.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9981. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Cho-
