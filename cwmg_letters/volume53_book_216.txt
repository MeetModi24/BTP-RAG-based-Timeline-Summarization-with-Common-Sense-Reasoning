216. LETTER TO BILL LASH
January 27, 1933
DEAR BROTHER LASH,
I have your letter and the questions which are very good. En-
closed you will find my brief answers. If you find them to be
too brief to be clear, you will not hesitate to tackle me further, and
if it will serve the purpose better by coming, please do not hesi-
tate to come.
I am glad the ointment has cured you of the poisonous sting.
You should now give me a receipt for that ointment.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
BRO. BILL LASH
CHRISTA SEVA SANGHA ASHRAM
POONA
From a photostat: G.N. 39
