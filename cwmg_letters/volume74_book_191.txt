191. LETTER TO D. P. KARMARKAR
June 25, 1941
DEAR KARMARKAR 3 ,
As I know so little of municipal affairs they are all refer-
red to Rajendra Babu and Acharya Kripalani. Both are just now
away.
Either follow the existing rule or write to Rajendra
Babu at Patna.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: A.I.C.C. File No. 1293-A, 1940-41. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
