55. LETTER TO NANABHAI BHATT
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
Jeth Sud 13, 1982, June 23, 1926
BHAISHRI NANABHAI,
I send herewith Bhai Gokulbhai's letter. I understand nothing
in it. When Vallabhbhai comes, I will talk about it. Let me
know what is your opinion. Can we, even if we want to, give
our permission without a meeting of the committee?
C/O RASHTRIYA SHALA
BOMBAY
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19635
