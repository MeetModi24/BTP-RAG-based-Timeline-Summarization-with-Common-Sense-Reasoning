589. LETTER TO DR. MANEKBAI BAHADURJI
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 28, 1926
I was pleased to receive your letter. I shall ask the Khadi
Pratishthan to send you a charkha like mine. Where do you want
it sent, Panchgani or Bombay? I shall write to the Pratishthan
after hearing from you. And if you are in a hurry you will write
yourself. The address of the Khadi Pratishthan is 170, Bow Bazar
Street, Calcutta.
When you mention the fruit that will grow in your ground at
Panchgani you make my mouth water. Of course during this visit
of mine I was not able to contemplate all the beautiful views around
Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani. That one can see only when one
has leisure.
With love to you all,
Yours,
DR. MRS. BAHADURJI
OOMRA HALL
PANCHGANI
