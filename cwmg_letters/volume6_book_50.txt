50. LETTER TO LORD STANLEY
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 31, 1906
TO
THE RIGHT HON'BLE LORD STANLEY OF ALDERLEY
18, MANSFIELD STREET
MY LORD,
I am obliged for the appointment given by you. I shall avail
myself of it at 10 a.m. tomorrow (Thursday).
I remain,
Your humble servant,
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4431
1 "Representation to Lord Elgin", pp. 45-54
2 (1839-1925); educationist and Member of Parliament
