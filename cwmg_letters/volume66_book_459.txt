459. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
HARIPURA,
February 13, 1938
CHI. SHARDA,
There has been no letter from you for two days. You must
write at least a postcard. I constantly think of you. Did
Mohanlal visit you? There is no letter from him here. There
are crowds of people today, and also a lot of dust.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9987. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
