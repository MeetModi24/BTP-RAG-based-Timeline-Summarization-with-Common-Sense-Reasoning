347. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
June 17, 1937
CHI. KANAIYO,
It seems you have taken the account book [by mistake]. I
suppose the information as to the amounts given by different
people will be there in that book. If so, please send the book
to Mahadev by registered post. Or send a note showing the
amounts credited into the accounts of the persons concerned.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
How is the heat there? It is quite hot over here.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
