54. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
April 29, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
If Chi. Ramdas wishes to go to Mysore for a few months,
let him go. I have already dictated a letter regarding a store-
room for jaggery. It should belong to the Ashram. I assume that
you will incur the expenditure only if Parnerkar desires. You
may also put up in the Ashram the building you speak of.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10627
