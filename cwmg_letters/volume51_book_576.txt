576. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
November 6, 1932
CHI. RAMA,
I hope you got the letter which I wrote to you after the
last post and have started acting as I advised. Not to show anger
against anger or speak ill of one who speaks ill of us, but to meet
anger with patience, to speak well of one who speaks ill of us and
to do good in return for evil-this is dharma and this is the ideal
which guides our relations in the Ashram. See that you do not
stray from it.
Give me news about Chhaganlal if you have any.
BAPU
