215. LETTER TO M. C. RAJAH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 26, 1936
DEAR RAO BAHADUR,
I have no difficulty about giving general endorsement to
your letter to Dr. Moonje. I do not at all understand Dr.
Moonje's or Dr. Ambedkar's position. For me removal of un-
touchability stands on a footing all its own. It is to me a
deeply religious question. The very existence of our religion
depends on its voluntary removal by savarna Hindus in the spirit
of repentance. It can never be a question of barter for me.
And I am glad you take nearly the same position that I do.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 7787. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
