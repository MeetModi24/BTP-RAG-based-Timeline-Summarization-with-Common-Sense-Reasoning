292. LETTER TO H. S. L. AND MILLIE GRAHAM POLAK
November 15, 1926
MY DEAR HENRY AND MILLIE,
I have both your letters. I can read your grief and fortitude. Such
events humble our pride and make us weary of the world's tinsel. They
make us realize the meaning of 'to be in the world and yet not of
it'. Your account of Ceilia and Agnes gave me much joy. Agnes's life
was shaped when I last saw her, Ceilia's was still in the making. Hers
is wonderful sacrifice. If Leon at all remembers me, let him write to
me.
With love,
Yours,
BHAT
From the original: Gandhi-Polak Correspondence. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
