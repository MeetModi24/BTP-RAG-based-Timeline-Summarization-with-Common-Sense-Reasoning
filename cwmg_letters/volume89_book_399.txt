399. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
NEW DELHI,
October 25, 1947
SHRI BALVANTSINHA,
Your letter to Manu was read out to Bapu. He says: Why are you
disheartened like this? The dairy has not really come to an end. It has
merely expanded. Try to improve the plight of the village cattle, increase
the milk yield, improve the breed, and educate the people to be honest and
sell only pure milk. Devise a scientific test for the detection of water mixed
with milk and so on. All this you can do and should do. This would be
real service to the cow.
SUSHILA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 1989
