166. LETTER TO PARAMANAND
July 6, 1932
BROTHER,
I have today learnt from a newspaper the news of your wife's
death. When you were imprisoned in the Andamans I had
come to know her and was much impressed by her simplicity.
May God grant you peace and patience.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 163
