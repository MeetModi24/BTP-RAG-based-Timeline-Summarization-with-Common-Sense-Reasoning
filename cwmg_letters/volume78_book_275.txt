275. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHASTRI
October 22, 1944
BHAI HIRALAL SHASTRI,
Why should you need anyone's blessings for your function?
Even then you have mine. For people like you, however, your
work itself ought to be the blessing. I have heard a lot in
praise of your work. It would have been fine if you could have
come. But why should anyone, immersed in his own work, waste
his time going places?
Blessings from
BAPU
BANASTHALI (JAIPUR)
