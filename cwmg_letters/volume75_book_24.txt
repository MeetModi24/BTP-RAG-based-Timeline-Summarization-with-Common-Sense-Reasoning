24. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
October 15, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
You are right. The extensions to the cottage are so elaborate
that there is every danger of our being crowded. The cosiness will
be gone. Let us see.
I have suggested full fortnight to test yourself. And otherwise
too this is no time for you to come. Nights are good but the days
are oppressive. So every way it is good you are not here.
Asaf Ali is in Wardha. He will be coming presently.
So
good-bye.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4099. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7408
