126. LETTER TO SAROJINI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 5, 1945
CHI. SAROJINI DEVI,
I have your letter. Your handwriting is worse than mine.
I can hardly read it. If you want to learn Hindustani and im-
prove your handwriting, you have got all the time at your disposal.
You must meet all your expenses and follow Chimanlal-
bhai's instructions or leave the Ashram. I cannot build quarters
for you. Live in a rented house.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 5878. Courtesy: Munnalal G. Shah
