181. LETTER TO D. G. VELANKAR
January 24, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You can come and bring your two or
three friends at 2 p.m. on 27th instant. I hope 30 minutes will be
enough for you.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. D. G. VELANKAR
866 SADASHIV PETH
POONA CITY
From a microfilm: S.N. 20083
