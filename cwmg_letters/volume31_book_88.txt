88. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 30, 1926
I have your postcard. It is surprising how you managed to
get ill somehow or other. Instead of having a good and quiet
time at Kotgarh, you must have blood-poisoning.
I wrote a long letter some days ago to the Kotgarh address.
Do please let me know fully how you are keeping. Do come here
if you at all feel like it. And you know you will have all the
nursing that you can possibly desire or need.
Yours,
From a photostat: S.N. 19647
