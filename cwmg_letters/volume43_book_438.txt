438. LETTER TO DR. PRANJIVANDAS MEHTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
May 26, 1930
DEAR BROTHER PRANJIVAN,
I remember you many times in the jail. You must be in good
health. Do not worry about me.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 577
