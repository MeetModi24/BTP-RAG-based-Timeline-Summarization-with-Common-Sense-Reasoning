181. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
January 31, 1935
CHI. BRIJKRISRNA,
You are pardoned—though such errors could have been easily
avoided. We are susceptible to subtle faults such as anger and
the like from which it is not easy to escape. But everything will
turn out well as you are ever on the alert.
Blessings from
BAPU
