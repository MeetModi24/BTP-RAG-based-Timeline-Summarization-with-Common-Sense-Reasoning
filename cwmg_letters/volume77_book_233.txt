233. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
PANCHGANI,
July 6, 1944
CHI. MANUDI,
Your letter is good. The work that you have undertaken
is fine but it will come in the way of your study. But never mind
that. It will save your eyes from harm. Study as much as you
can with due care for your eyesight. God has endowed you
with capacity for service and, therefore, you get such work un-
asked. Overcome your habit of thoughtless spending. Take care
of everything and use it as a poor person would.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
To Jaisukhlal later.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./XXIV
