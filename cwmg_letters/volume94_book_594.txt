594. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
January 25, 1947
CHI. SATIS BABU,
This is surprising. Yes, do file (the case), but did I not say that
we should pass on the case to Suo and say that we on our part wanted
to do everything but some letters from Chowdhury Saheb have
not come ? Consider what is appropriate.
Blessings from
BAPU
