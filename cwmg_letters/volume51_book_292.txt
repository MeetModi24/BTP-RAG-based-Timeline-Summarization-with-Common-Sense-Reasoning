292. LETTER TO S. K. GEORGE
October 3, 1932
MY DEAR GEORGE 1 ,
I was glad you were in the Ashram. I hope your fever has
left you. For the time being only this note. Yes, Ramarajya is
possible even with this mixture, if the workers are true. This
does not exclude me. If I am true, there must be true co-work-
ers; if false, co-workers also false. Do write whenever you feel
like it.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi's Challenge to Christianity, p. 89
1 Teacher in charge of Deenabandhu Bhavan, Santiniketan
179
