453. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
WARDHA,
August 6, 1935
MY DEAR MALKANI,
As to Hariji all's well that ends well. He suggests decentrali-
zation. Please send me list of members of the Executive and tell
me who are likely to come. Send an invitation to Hariji, if he is
not on the Board .
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 916
