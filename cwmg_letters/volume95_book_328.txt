328. LETTER TO KUNVARJI V MEHTA
BHAI KUNVARJI,
I have your letter. Sitting here and not knowing your ailment,
I cannot suggest a remedy. There is a general remedy of course. We
should become absorbed in some activity beyond our capacity.
Weakness of the brain can be cured by this means. One who is thus
conscious of his duty will not neglect his work. Doing his work he will
gain in virtues such as truthfulness, firmness, fearlessness and so on.
My health is all right. There is still some weakness, but it will
go.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 2666
