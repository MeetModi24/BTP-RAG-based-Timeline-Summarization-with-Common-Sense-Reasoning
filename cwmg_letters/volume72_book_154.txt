154. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEVAGRAM,
May 31, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You can certainly tell the guests that extra milk is not
served except under doctor's advice.
Talk to Bharatananda about Appu and about himself too.
You may also talk about . . . 4 Consider first how it will affect
Appu's heart. If Appu looks upon him as father and stays and
works with him, we should have nothing to say.
Surendra's denial is partly true and partly untrue. Does not
Sarladevi's man wash her clothes? Ask her. Leave out Parner-
karji now. The girls at any rate do their jobs, don't they?
Whatever we do should be inspired by ahimsa. Whomso-
ever you speak to, speak with affection. I shall tell you what
your duty is. It is for you to act accordingly. Consult me about
rules. Ask me to make new rules. But relieve me of the rest.
My sadhana will be disturbed if I am saddled with day-to-day
management.
Having said all this, I am still there if there is any difficulty
in the management. I shall try to escape as much as I can.
I suppose those who are getting extra milk get it only on
doctor's advice.
Blessings from
BAPU
