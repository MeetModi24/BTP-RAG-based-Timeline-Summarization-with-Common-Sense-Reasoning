183. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS
TRIKUMJI
September 7, 1930
I hope Mathuradas's imprisonment has not upset you. Where
do you live at present? How is your health and Dilip's? What
does Mathuradas say in his letters? Does he feel any hardship?
How do you spend your time? Do you take any part in public
activities? Do you get any visitors? Do you visit any friends?
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 102
