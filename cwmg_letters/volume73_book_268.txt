268. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 10, 1940
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
You are greedy. You were not satisfied with the elegant mes-
sage that I sent with Bhai Vaidya. Here is my signature. I like
the issue of your Khadi Patrika very much. If the three things that
are mentioned there are carried on well, the Bombay [bhandar]
will not remain a mere khadi bhandar but will become a beautiful
production centre also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10850. Courtesy: Purushottam
K. Jerajani
