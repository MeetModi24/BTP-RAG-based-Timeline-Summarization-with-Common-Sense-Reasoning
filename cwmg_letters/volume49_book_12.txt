12. LETTER TO KASHINATH AND KALAVATI TRIVEDI
January 12, 1932
CHI. KASHINATH,
I got your letter. For the present, remain where you are and
learn through patience. This is enough today.
CHI. KALAVATI,
I got your letter. I have a faint impression that you did take
the pledge. Whatever that be, do what Narandas asks you to do.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati and Hindi: G.N. 5284
