4. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
PANCHGANI,
July 17, 1946
CHI. NARAHARI,
I did get your letter. The riots are a shameful affair from
beginning to end. I do get reports from all sources. It is a
matter for concern that your sickness does not leave you. Sushila
will soon be arriving there. See if she can do anything. My
preference is for nature cure. I would consider extraction of
teeth as part of the nature cure treatment. Jesus advised long
ago that a limb which has become rotten should be cut off.
I know a great deal about V. now. We shall discuss the
problem when I am there.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI NARAHARI PARIKH
ACHARYA, KHADI VIDYALAYA
A[KHIL] BHA[RATIYA] CHARKHA SANGH
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA, C. P.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S. N. 9143
