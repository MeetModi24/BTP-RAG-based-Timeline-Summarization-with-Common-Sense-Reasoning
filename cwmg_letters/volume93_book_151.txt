151. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 11, 1936
CHI. LILA,
You seem to have gone far.
I have written a letter to
Dwarkadas which is enclosed.
It is difficult to give any
guidance from here. Dinshaw visits Bombay from time to time.
Bring him along. Find out about his date of coming from his
man. His office is in Fort, Bombay. Maybe Ramdas knows
about it. Ba's blessings to all of you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Navin has arrived today.
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
