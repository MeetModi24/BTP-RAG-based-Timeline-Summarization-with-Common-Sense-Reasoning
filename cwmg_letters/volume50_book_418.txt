418. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
August 21, 1932
CHI. JAMNA,
You wrote to me after a long interval. It is certainly good
that you get an opportunity of staying in Rajkot from time to
time. Khushalbhai's recovery to this extent is indeed a miracle.
Tell Jamnadas that I should be happy if he stops being lazy in
writing to me.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 858. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
