179. LETTER TO KISHORELAL
BULANDSHAHAR,
[November 3, 1929]
BROTHER KISHORELAL,
I have your letter. The uplift of the aborigines can be justi-
fied solely so that that caste is made to sacrifice itself for the
whole of India, not that it may forget the people in furtherance
of its own selfish interests.
Love from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal Papers.
