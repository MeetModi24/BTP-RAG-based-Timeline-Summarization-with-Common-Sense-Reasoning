126. LETTER TO JITENDRA CHAKRAVARTI
CHAUMUHANI,
November 8, 1946
BHAI JITEN1,
In the present condition of Noakhali and Tippera you can
sell khadi without insisting on yarn in exchange. Keep sending
an account of the khadi thus sold.
Blessings from
BAPU
