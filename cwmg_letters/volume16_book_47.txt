47. LETTER TO MRS. CLAYTON
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
August 28, 1919
DEAR MRS. CLAYTON1,
I hope you received the precis of my address in Godhra as I
had promised. A lady teacher has been sent to Godhra to open
a spinning class. Will you not encourage the class by taking up
spinning yourself? The lady, Mrs. Desai, who is at present in
Godhra, has been teaching Lady Dinshaw Petit. I do not want
you to give much time to spinning, but even if you spun for half
an hour a day, it would be your free offering to the poor and the
needy.
Will you please remember me to Mr. Clayton and tell him how
much I appreciate his prompt issue of notice regarding veth
Yours sincerely,
