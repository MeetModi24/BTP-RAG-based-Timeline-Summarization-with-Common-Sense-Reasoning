146. LETTER TO MIR MUSHTAK AHMED
[After May 29, 1940]
BHAI MIR AHMED,
It is good that you have given up writing and speaking in
English for as long as you can.
The answer to your question is covered in the opinion I have
given. I feel that such a responsibility should not be undertaken
as long as the Congress remains outlawed. Congress Assemblies
are a different matter. In this each province is free to act in the
manner it likes.
From Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
