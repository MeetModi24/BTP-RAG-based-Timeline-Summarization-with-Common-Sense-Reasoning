721. LETTER TO DEV PRAKASH NAYYAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 23, 1945
CHI. DEV,
Prabhakar writes that you are losing weight and you get head-
aches. You must take more ghee. Have a mud-pack on the
abdomen at night and sit in a tub filled with cold water for an
hour as I used to do. Fill the water yourself. It will be very good
if you find a solitary place near the well. You must keep a wet
cloth on the head.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. National Archives of India
