567. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
September 24, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
What you write is worth thinking over. Put up the sug-
gestion to me.
Lakshmibai has given me a description of Surgaon. What-
ever we do, we shall do after careful thinking.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8484. Also C.W. 7157. Cour-
tesy: Munnalal G. Shah
