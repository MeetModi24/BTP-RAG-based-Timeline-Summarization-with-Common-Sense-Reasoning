148. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
October 23, 1933
CHI. MARY,
Great minds think alike. I was wondering why you would
not take your meal here last evening. Of course you can take
all your meals here. The liquidness has nothing to do with the
indigestion. It points to the necessity of less protein. But you
should take some time from me when you come.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
If the bowels have been sluggish and you feel any indiges-
tion now, you may mix one portion of milk. Yes, three meals
will be perhaps quite enough.
From a photostat: G.N. 6012. Also C.W. 3338. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
