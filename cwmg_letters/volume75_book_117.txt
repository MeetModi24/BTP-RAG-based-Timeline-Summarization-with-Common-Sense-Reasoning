117. LETTER TO PRAN KRISHNA PADHIARY
[Before November 6, 1941]
Your letter makes sad reading. I can only say even if the
Congress contains only a few men who are true to it, it will be well.
Those who secede will do so, but if there are a few true and brave
Congressmen, people will follow them and not the seceders, no
matter whether they are Ministers or not.
The Hindu, 8-11-1941
