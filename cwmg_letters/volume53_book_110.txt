110. LETTER TO BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
January 18, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of the 12th instant. I shall thank
you if you will enlighten me about the facts and conditions
prevailing in the Province of Bengal which require a re-consider-
ation of the Yeravda Pact.
Yours sincerely,
THE JOINT HONY. SECRETARY
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
18 BRITISH INDIAN ST.
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 18967
