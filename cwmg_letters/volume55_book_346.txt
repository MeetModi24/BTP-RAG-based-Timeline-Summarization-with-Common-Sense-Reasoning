346. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
July 24, 1933
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I got your letter. I have no time to answer letters. I have
regained considerable strength but not enough to permit me to
do unlimited work. Whatever be my lot, yours is the khadi
work. I have explained everything in the letter to Satisbabu
and so I am not repeating that here. I trust Arun is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1703
