222. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 16, 1928
DEAR MOTILALJI,
Your report is a great document. I am hoping that the Con-
ference that is to meet at Lucknow will give it all the serious consi-
deration it deserves and not light-heartedly begin to tear it to
pieces. The intrinsic merit of the report is so great as to ensure
full attendance at Lucknow.
Before I got your warning I began to think out what could
be done for the next year. But I must confess that I have not yet
been able to hit upon anything to my liking. Lucknow might
give me the inspiration.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 13660
