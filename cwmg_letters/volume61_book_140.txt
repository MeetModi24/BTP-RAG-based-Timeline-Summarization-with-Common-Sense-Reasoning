140. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
May 24, 1935
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I got your letter. Do not let the Indore matter weigh on
your mind. Do please go there after you come down.
You must be getting news about Gangadevi.
Everything is all right at Borsad.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2970
