415. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
December 25, 1933
CHI. RAMA,
I got your letter. It is certainly good that you try to work
with the arm only little by little. There is plenty of other kind
of work for you to do.
You should not mind if Dhiru feels unhappy at being separat-
ed from you. If his health remains good, he will in time become
self-reliant in Bhavnagar. Dudhibehn's company is bound to bene-
fit him. Moreover, Kusum also is there. Let him, therefore, stay
on there permanently. It will be enough if you continue to write
to him regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5361
