334. LETTER TO MRS. LINDSAY
October 6, 1932
Thanks for your sweet letter . Yes, it would be tragic if
God was accessible only to the learned. My feeling coincides
with your washerwoman's. There was once a scientific expedi-
tion in search of God. The scientists are reported to have come
to India and to have found God not in the homes of Brahmins,
nor in the palaces of kings, but they found Him in a hut of an
untouchable. Hence my cry to God to admit me to the untouch-
able fold. After fifty years of trial they found me worthy to be
so admitted. And I rejoice over the event.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 112
