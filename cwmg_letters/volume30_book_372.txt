372. LETTER TO MANU
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, April 17, 1926
CHI. MANU1,
I learnt that you were very ill. Since then I have been keep-
ing myself informed about your health through visitors. I was
recently told by Swami Anand that you are now almost normal,
and have learnt it again today from Shri Chandrakant's letter.
I have not forgotten your many services to me when I was in
hospital nor your majestic gait, nor your ever-smiling face. May
God restore you to complete health and grant you a long life
dedicated to the service of the country.
