464. LETTER TO SURENDRA SINGH
As AT SABARMATI,
June 13, 1931
DEAR SARDAR SURENDRA SINGH,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I am doing all
I can and in the manner I know to spread the gospel of love all
round but it makes very slow progress in the midst of growing hate
and distrust between Hindus and Mussalmans. As to your solu-
tion of the communal question my observation is that the parties
concerned want a solution for all the provinces or none at all. I
agree with you that it would be a great advance if some provinces
at least found out their own solution.
Yours sincerely,
SARDAR SURENDRA SINGH
DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE
PUNJAB GOVERNMENT
SIMLA EAST
From a photostat: S.N. 17273
