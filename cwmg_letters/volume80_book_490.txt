490. LETTER TO KASHINATH VAIDYA
PANCHGANI,
June 9, 1945
BHAI KASHINATH VAIDYA,
I have your letter of June 6. I am doing all that I can.
Let us see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KASHINATH VAIDYA
1307 SULTAN BAZAAR
HYDERABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
