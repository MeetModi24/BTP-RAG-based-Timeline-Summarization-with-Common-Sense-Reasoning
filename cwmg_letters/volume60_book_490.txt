490. LETTER TO MITHUBEHN PETIT
March 30, 1935
CHI MITHUBEHN,
I see that your work is making good progress. It is a good
thing that you will soon be producing honey. You must do some-
thing about hides and also grow cotton.
My visit to Gujarat is not certain. Sardar did ask me.
I am still considering. If at all I go to Gujarat, I shall confine
myself to one place only.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 24320
