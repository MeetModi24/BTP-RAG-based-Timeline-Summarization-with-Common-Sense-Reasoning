458. LETTER TO MAUDE
SEVAGRAM,
March 19, 1945
MY DEAR MAUDE,
Of course I have had all about your operation and your
bravery. Purposely I refrained from writing to you. I now learn
that you will appreciate a line from me. Well, all I have heard
fills me with admiration for you and joy that you have come out
of the ordeal so splendidly. Let us hope you will have no
more to go through.
Love.
BAPU
SMT. MAUDE
C/O RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
