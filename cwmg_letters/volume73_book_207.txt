207. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 18, 1940
DEAR SISTER,
I got your letter. The fast should cause no anxiety. If I
undertake it, God alone will make me do so. Hence the fruit is
bound to be wholesome. At present the question does not arise.
We shall think about your courting imprisonment. The con-
ference work has got to be done. The struggle is likely to be a
long one. Hence it would be better if you join late.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7992. Also C.W. 3089. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
