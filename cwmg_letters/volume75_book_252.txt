252. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
December 21, 1941
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Get the work done through the All-India Spinners' Association
as suggested by Bhai Jugalkishore in his letter. We will certainly
spend as much as possible in Kangra as also in Pilani.
I think it would be best to call a meeting of the A. I. C. C.
in Wardha. If you agree, send invitations by wire. The meeting
should start on the day after my arrival and be over before the
19th.
Indu has come here.
I hope Madalasa is all right. The baby must be growing.
I missed you very much at the All-India Spinners' Associa-
tion and will miss you again at the Working Committee. But I
think your good lies in my not pressing you.
I am keeping good health. I hope you too are all right.
182
You can fix a meeting of the Goseva Sangh on any date after
January 27. Has Janakimaiya returned? I hope she has not
spoiled her health.
Blessings from
BAPU
