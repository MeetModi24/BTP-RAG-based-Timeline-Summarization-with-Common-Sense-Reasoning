254. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 26, 1937
CHI. NARANDAS,
Kamalabai returned there rather suddenly. Give the accom-
panying letter to her. You may take from her whatever work
you like, within her capacity. If she serves as she has promised
to do, she will certainly be able to do much work. You need not
hesitate to ask her any question.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I Shall be staying here up to the 10th. Shouldn't, therefore,
Kanu also leave on the 10th?
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8523. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
