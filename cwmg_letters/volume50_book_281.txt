281. LETTER TO RAMNIKLAL V. SHAH
July 26, 1932
DEAR RAMNIKLAL,
I have received your letter. I do not know your elders, there-
fore, I dare not write to them. You can show (them) this letter.
It is our religion to touch or to dine with those whom we erroneous-
ly call untouchables or of low caste. Not to touch them is a sin.
I have not the slightest doubt about this. This is not a reform which
has come from the West, but it is the duty of the Hindus.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
RAMNIKLAL VIMBASHI SHAH
CHINTAMANI BUILDING
2ND BHOIWADA, BOMBAY 2
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. II, p. 451
