223. LETTER TO PRAGNANESWAR YATI
December 11, 1932
I thank you for your frank letter. I would ask you not to
feel so nervous about myself. Through a period of active service
covering more than forty years you will not recall more than
perhaps twelve occasions of vicarious fasting.
It came into my
life, as I conceive it, after I had become a fit instrument for
doing it. No one can do it in a hurry and my claim you know.
I do not act on my own, but I act in obedience to the inner
prompting. Whether it is the voice of the divine or the devil,
it is not always easy to say. Nevertheless in each case the claim
to the inner prompting may be held to be justified. So far as
Mr. Mate's reproduction of his conversation between him and me
is concerned, your deduction is too sweeping. In order to have
a proper clearance it would be better perhaps if we meet, and I
shall await you on . . . at . . . "
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 318
