491. LETTER TO AGNIBHOJ
POONA,
July 2, 1946
BHAI AGNIBHOJ,
I have your letter. You did well in writing to me. I think the
C.W.C. is seized of the matter. I would say this much that either
untouchability will be eradicated through a change of heart in the caste
Hindus or the sin that is being perpetrated in the name of religion will
destroy Hindu society.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
