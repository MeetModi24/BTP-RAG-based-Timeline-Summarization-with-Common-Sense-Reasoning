248. LETTER TO NATHUBHAI N. PAREKH
May 24, 1937
BHAI NATHUBHAI,
Kanti has given me your letter and it was only after reading
it that I came to know about your vanapravesh. When I wander,
the letters also wander after me. Your letter could not come
into my hands till now. All the letters cannot reach me on the
very day they arrive. It is good that you have completed 50
years. Take everything that one may from this stage of life
and march on. Jayanti also came and saw me. Indu is stay-
ing with me at Segaon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6249
