278. LETTER TO PARASRAM MEHROTRA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 3, 1930
CHI. PARASRAM,
Your letter. Do as Shankerlalji says. You must go on with
your task despite the world's ridicule. Do not picket the dealers
in mill-made slivers, but persuade them with love. On no ac-
count give up truth and non-violence. You will then automatically
acquire mental strength.
Continue to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 4965. Courtesy: Parasram Mehrotra
