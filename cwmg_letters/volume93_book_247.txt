247. LETTER TO PYARELAL
May 17, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
How is it there is no letter from you today? Not a day
should pass without a letter from you. At present, I am working
sometimes for ten hours a day. Let us see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PYARELAL NAYYAR
19 RAJA BAZAR SQUARE
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
