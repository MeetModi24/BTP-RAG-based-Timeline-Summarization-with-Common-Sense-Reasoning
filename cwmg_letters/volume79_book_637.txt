637. LETTER TO KUSUM NAIR
BOMBAY,
April 12, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I find your Hindi very sweet. The handwriting too is good.
Read the reply I have given. I did not say I would give the
article soon. However if you keep on reminding me you will
get it.
Blessings from
BAPU
