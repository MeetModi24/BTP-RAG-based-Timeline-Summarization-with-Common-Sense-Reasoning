471. LETTER TO ABDUL QAIYUM
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 3, 1941
DEAR ABDUL QAIYUM,
It is good you have written to me. I was firmly of opinion that
the report should be published. But somehow or other it was not.
I do not know that it will be wise now to publish it. But I shall
consult Rajen Babu and Prof. Kripalani as also Sarojini Devi and
see what should be done.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
