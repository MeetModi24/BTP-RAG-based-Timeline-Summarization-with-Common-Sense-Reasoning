236. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
PANCHGANI,
July 7, 1944
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
Indeed we have suffered a great loss in the passing away
of your father . He is relieved of the burden. Although I knew
very little about him, I had heard a lot about his love of khadi.
The straight and noble way to fill the void created by his passing
away is to emulate that love of his.
Blessings from
BAPU
KHADI BHANDAR
BOMBAY
