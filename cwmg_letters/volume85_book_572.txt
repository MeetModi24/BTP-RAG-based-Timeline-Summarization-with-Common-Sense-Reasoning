572. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
October 11, 1946
CHI. MANUDI,
I have gone through your letter. I gave it to Sushila Pai,
Kanu, Sushila (Dr.) and Pyarelal to read. Here I shall tell
you only this, that I liked your letter.
Further, I shall be happy if you come over and have
a talk with me. I do not wish to put any pressure on you.
It is my earnest desire that you should remain a pure virgin
till the end of your life and spend your life in service.
I hope Umiya's son is doing well.
Blessings to all from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./XXIV
