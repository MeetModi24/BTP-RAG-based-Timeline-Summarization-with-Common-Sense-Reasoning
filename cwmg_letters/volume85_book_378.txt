378. LETTER TO MOHAMMED YUNUS
NEW DELHI,
September 12, 1946
BHAI SAHEB,
I got your letter. There was nothing the matter with me
except for a mild cold, which too has now gone. Do not worry.
Who can harm a person as long as he is protected by God or
keep him when He wants to take him away?
All that you have written about Calcutta is shameful. Joint
electorates are a noble solution, I agree, but how to bring it
about remains to be seen.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
JANAB MOHAMMED YUNUS
(OF BIHAR)
