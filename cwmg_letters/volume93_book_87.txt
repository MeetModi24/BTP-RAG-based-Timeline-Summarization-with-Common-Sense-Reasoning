87. LETTER TO PYARELAL
December 1, 1935
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have received your letter. Now do not leave anything
wanting with regard to food and get well soon. There is no
need to put up with pain of any kind. Your present duty is to
build your health properly.
You have not written anything about Mother. Where is she
staying? Who is treating her?
Rajkumari arrived yesterday. Her health is indifferent. She
has cough. She was making enquiries about you.
Blessings from
BAPU
