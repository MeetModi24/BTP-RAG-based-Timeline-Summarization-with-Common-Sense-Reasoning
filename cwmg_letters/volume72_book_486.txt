486. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
September 4, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Your letter becomes your ahimsa. I did not wish to ex-
amine the entire case but only wanted to reply to the grave alle-
gations of the Bengal Premier. It is one thing for the C.P. re-
presentative to take interest and another altogether that he
should bring forward false witnesses. The fact that the Hindus
in Biswa had suffered greatly was relevant, and the ill-treatment
of the prisoners subsequently, though a fact, was irrelevant.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8528. Also C.W. 7101. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
