202. LETTER TO HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
SEVAGRAM,
August 14, 1945
BHAI MAHTAB,
I had sent your letter to Shri Jajuji. He has written to you.
From it you will see that the new [scheme] is good from all
points of view.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
CONGRESS HOUSE
CUTTACK, ORISSA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
