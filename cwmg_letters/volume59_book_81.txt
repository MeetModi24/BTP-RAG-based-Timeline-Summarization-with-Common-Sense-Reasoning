81. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
September 25, 1934
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
By all means keep that postcard with you. I don't need it.
I pray that Kumarsaheb will soon recover.
As for myself, God helps me to keep working.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5938. Also C.W. 3254. Courtesy:
Mahesh P. Pattani
