214. LETTER TO KANTILAL AND SARASWATI GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
October 10, 1944
CHI. KANTI AND SARASWATI,
One letter from Kanti is lying before me. It is full of infor-
mation. You are doing right. I will not write more. There is
a good family gathering just now. It will disperse soon. I keep
good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 7369. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
