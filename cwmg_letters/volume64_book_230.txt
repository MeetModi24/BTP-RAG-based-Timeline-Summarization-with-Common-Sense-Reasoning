230. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 3, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
Enclosed are letters to Madgavkar and Kasturbhai. Send
a copy of the translation to each of them. Keep one copy for
me. Send one to Shankerlal. A letter to him is also enclosed.
Agatha and Polak have not arrived till now, that is, 4 o'clock.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I read the letters; but personally I feel that we should not go.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11515
