513. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
September 29, 1945
CHI. SUMI,
I have your letter. I am not happy at your falling ill so
often. Is it not an essential part of education to learn not to
fall ill?
SUMITRA GANDHI
BIRLA HIGH SCHOOL
PILANI, RAJPUTANA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
