382. LETTER TO SATYAVATI DEVI
SEVAGRAM,
November 11, 1944
CHI. SATYAVATI,
I got your letter. Send over Chandrani. If you do not
get permission to go to Lahore even for reasons of health, write
to me before taking any step.
Only this much today. Do not worry about my fast. I am
in God's hands.
Blessings from
BAPU
SATYAVATI DEVI
JUHAR, via SIMLA HILLS
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
