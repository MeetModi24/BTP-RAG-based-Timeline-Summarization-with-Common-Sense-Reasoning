173. LETTER TO TARADEVI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
April 12, 1937
CHI. TARA,
Let us see when you come with Mother. What is the result
of your examination? In how many more examinations do you
have to appear? I receive letters from Mother.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI TARA KUMARI
MAHILA VIDYALAYA
LUCKNOW, U. P.
From the Hindi original : Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy : Gandhi
National Museum and Library
