257. LETTER TO NRISIMHAPRASAD K. BHATT
Kartak Sud 1, 2001 [October 18, 1944]
BHAI NANABHAI,
I am sure your son's operation will be smooth and success-
ful. Normally this kind of surgery does not involve any risk.
You must pay me a visit before you return. Even if you have
no questions to ask I have some. You may send Manubhai
and Vijaya whenever you can. They may stay on as long as
they like. In the mean while who will look after the work at
Ambla? Blessings to Hiralal and Ponchibehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
