243. LETTER TO VIDYA A. HINGORANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 6, 1937
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. Got one after a long time. Extend your
stay at Nainital and be fully restored. Your first duty is to make
your body strong.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India and
Anand T. Hingorani
