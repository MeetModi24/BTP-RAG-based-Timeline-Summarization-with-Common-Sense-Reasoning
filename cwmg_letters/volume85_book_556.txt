556. LETTER TO DR. ATMARAM K. BHAGAWAT
NEW DELHI,
October 7, 1946
BHAI BHAGAWAT,
Appasaheb should leave the place only when he has re-
covered fully and regained his strength. If the cure is not
within your reach and you need help from someone do take it.
It is a human virtue to know one's own limitations.
I hope everything else is going on well. How is Balkrishna?
Is milk available?
My blessings to all.
URULI KANCHAN
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
