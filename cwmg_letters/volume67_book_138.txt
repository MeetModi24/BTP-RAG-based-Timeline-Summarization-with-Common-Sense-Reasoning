138. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
May 29, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
I hope you are better today.
I intend to replace A. S.
tomorrow. I am thinking of sending Lilavati. If you have any
suggestion to make, let me know.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7086. Also C.W. 4578. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
