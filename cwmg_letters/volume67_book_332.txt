332. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
August 9, 1938
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I read your letter to Sharda. Do not take all the freedom
about food that the doctors may give. Eat only as much as the
body demands. If you do that you will be able to return early
and there will be no complication.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10584
