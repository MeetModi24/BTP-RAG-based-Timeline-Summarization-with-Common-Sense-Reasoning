442. LETTER TO AMINA QURESHI
May 26, 1930
DEAR AMINA,
At last Imam Saheb has after all gone to the jail palace.
Perhaps Qureshi may have also gone. Is your health good?
What arrangements have been made about confinement? How
do children fare?
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 578
