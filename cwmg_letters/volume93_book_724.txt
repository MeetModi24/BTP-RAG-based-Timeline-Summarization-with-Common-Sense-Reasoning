724. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
April 24, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I could not write to you yesterday. And today I am writing
this at bed-time. The whole day was spent in meeting people.
Durgabehn is well. I am of course fine. Pyarelal is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
