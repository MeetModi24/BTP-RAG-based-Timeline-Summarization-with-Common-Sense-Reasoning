284. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
SEGAON,
July 27, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
I got your letter. Balkoba has gone to Varoda. He is fine.
Get rid of your fever. Try to find out what medicine the
vaid is giving you. It is no doubt an advantage to you that the
milk there is thin. It means that there is less fat in it.
Khan Saheb has arrived in Wardha. When the work of the
[Working] Committee is over, he will come to Segaon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7091. Also C.W. 4583. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
