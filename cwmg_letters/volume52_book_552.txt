552. LETTER TO GIRDHAR SHASTRI
January 9, 1933
I have your letter. I do accept the Shastras as authoritative.
But no one gives me a list of the works and my observation so
far does not even suggest the possibility of such a list. That is
why the Gita is my refuge. I try to be courteous in whatever I
undertake. But I try harder to make courtesy consistent with
truth. What more can I say?
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 30
