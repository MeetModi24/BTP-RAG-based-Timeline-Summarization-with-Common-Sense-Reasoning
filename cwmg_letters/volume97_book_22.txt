22. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
BETTIAH,
Jeth Vad 3 [June 8, 1917]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
All of us arrived here today. The indigo planters are out to make trouble
and therefore my return might be delayed. I will see.
Herewith a postcard. If, as stated in it, the man comes there, take him
in. If he knows Hindi, entrust him with teaching work. Otherwise, any other
work you think him fit for. I have asked him to go there directly.
As for your going to Rajkot, do what you think is proper. 4
1 That is, June 2, 1917
2 Where Gandhiji was to hold discussions with the Lt. Governor from 4th to 6th
June
3 From the combination of place-name, Gujarati date-line and contents
4 Also vide Suppl. Vol. I, p. 114.
LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
21
Prabhudas's body is getting quite strong. Though he still suffers from
headaches, that does not matter.
Very fine work was done at Ranchi. Devdas has given you a full report.
I myself am not in a position to write more. The reports which Devdas sends
also should not appear in the newspapers.
Blessings from
BAPU
[Ps.]
Bhai Pragji has asked for money. I have told him that volunteers must not
be enlisted by paying them. Boarding and lodging is all that may be offered
to them. The travelling expenses also should be borne by them. I see no harm
in the matter relating to the Doctor being printed in Rajkot. But the cost
must not exceed what it would be in Ahmedabad.
