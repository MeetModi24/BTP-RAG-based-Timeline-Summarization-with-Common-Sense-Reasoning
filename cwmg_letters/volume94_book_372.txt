372. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
"MORARJI CASTLE",
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 25, 1945
DEAR AMMAJAN,
I am herewith enclosing copy of an extract from Harry H. Field's book After
Mother India which a correspondent 1 has sent to Bapu. On the face of it the whole
thing seems to be a malicious invention. Bapu knows nothing about it. All the same,
since you are mentioned in the extract he has asked me to refer the same to you.
Hoping you are well.
With regards,
Yours sincerely,
SMT. SAROJINI NAIDU
HYDERABAD (DN.)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library. Courtesy:
Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
