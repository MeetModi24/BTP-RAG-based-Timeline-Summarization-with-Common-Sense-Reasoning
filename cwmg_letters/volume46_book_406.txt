406. LETTER TO P. A. WADIA
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 5, 1931
DEAR PROF. WADIA,
I enclose herewith a copy of a letter from an English friend.
I have sent him a reply but I know that my reply cannot carry
the same weight that a trained economist['s] can. Will you kindly
therefore send me your considered reply supported by facts and
figures?
Yours sincerely ,
Encl. 1
PROF. P. A. WADIA
WILSON COLLEGE
BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 17233
