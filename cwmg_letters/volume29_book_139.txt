139. LETTER TO PHULSIMHA
Maha Sud 2 [January 16, 1926]
BHAI PHULSIMHAJI,
I have your letter. I can very well see your point of view, but
I think no purpose will be served by discussing the subject per-
sonally.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
BHAI PHULSIMHAJI
CHAROTAR KELAVANI MANDAL
ANAND
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 1294
