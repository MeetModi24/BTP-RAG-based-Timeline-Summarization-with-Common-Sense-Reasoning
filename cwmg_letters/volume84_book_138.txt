138. LETTER TO CHIMANBHAI
SIMLA,
May 6, 1946
BHAI CHIMANBHAI,
I have your bank draft for Rs. 1,000. I shall credit it to the
public funds account that stands in my name. Congratulations
to all the friends.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
