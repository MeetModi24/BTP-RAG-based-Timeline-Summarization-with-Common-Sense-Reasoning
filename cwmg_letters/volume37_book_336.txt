336. LETTER TO AMY TURTORE
September 21, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter as also the beautiful piece of linen.
Here is a sample of what is being done here.
I thank you also for your prayers. I am forwarding our cor-
respondence to Mr. Andrews.
Yours sincerely,
M. AMY TURTORE
CAMOLLIA, 47 SIENA, ITALY
From a microfilm: S.N. 14398
