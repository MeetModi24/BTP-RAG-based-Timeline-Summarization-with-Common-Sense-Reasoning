535. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
March 27, 1945
CHI. SITA,
Only this much for today. Why are you afraid of dreams?
You must not cry. We get dreams owing to indigestion. Some-
times dreams are due to our impatience. Many a time we worry
unconsciously and then we get dreams. In such cases we should
keep repeating Ramanama. Remember that it is an unfailing
remedy.
Teach me how to row.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4947
