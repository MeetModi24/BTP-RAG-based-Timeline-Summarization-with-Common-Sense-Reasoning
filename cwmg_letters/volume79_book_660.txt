660. LETTER TO BISWANATH DAS
BOMBAY,
April 15/17, 1945
MY DEAR DAS,
I like your letter. I agree that those who are so inclined
should follow the parliamentary activity. But the decision has
to be taken by the W. C.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI B. DAS, M.L.A. (CENTRAL)
CHANDNI CHOWK
CUTTACK, ORISSA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
