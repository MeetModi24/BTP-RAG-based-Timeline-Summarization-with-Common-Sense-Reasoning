675. LETTER TO MOHANLAL VERMA
October 18, 1945
BHAI MOHANLAL VERMA,
Just now I came to know about Kumar Chintaman Vinayak's
death. If it is true that Congressmen were responsible for it and
that too for unworthy reasons, it is a matter of shame, more so
378
for Congressmen. Ever since I heard about this death, I have
been trying to find out the truth.
K. MOHANLAL VERMA
GENERAL SECRETARY
ANTI-PAKISTAN FRONT
GIRGAUM, OPP. MANGALWADI
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
