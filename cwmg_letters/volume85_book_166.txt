166. LETTER TO SHAFAAT AHMED KHAN
SEVAGRAM,
August 13, 1946
BHAI SHAFAAT AHMED KHAN SAHEB,
I was very glad to have your letter. I see that you still
take a keen interest in the affairs of our people in South Africa.
You have made many useful suggestions. Can't they be
made public with the permission of the Viceroy?
You may
be aware that from 1890 till the Boer War the British Govern-
ment had an Agent-General in the Transvaal, and the corres-
pondence he carried on with the Transvaal Government was
published. That is the general practice.
May I make public use of any part of your letter?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
