350. LETTER TO NIRMALA H. DESAI
April 9, 1932
CHI.,
People worship the sacred tulsi because the plant is believed
to possess certain virtues. One should be angry with anger itself.
Because the other person forgets himself or herself, why should we
do the same? Grown-up people also do wrong in getting angry.
Even when they become angry, one should cling to what one
[believes] to be the truth. Did you get the letter which your
elder brother wrote to you?
Blessings from
BAPU
