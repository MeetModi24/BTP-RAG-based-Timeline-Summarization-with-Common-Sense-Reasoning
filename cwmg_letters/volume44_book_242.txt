242. LETTER TO P. G. MATHEW
September 26, 1930
MY DEAR MATHEW,
The things you write about are ultimately decided by faith.
Reason can take us only a little distance. Man is a person, God
is not in the same sense. Man sins because he has the sense of right
and wrong. Our difficulty arises through our effort to measure
God by our little selves. And He eludes all measure.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1553
