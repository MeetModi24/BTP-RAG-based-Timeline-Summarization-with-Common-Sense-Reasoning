347. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
LUCKNOW,
April 3, 1936
MY DEAR REBEL,
Though you could have stayed here till today, it was well
you went on Tuesday. Your wire was good and the letter quite
in keeping with the past performances. Yes, we had the rain
here, too, but not much damage was done at the Exhibition for
the dust storm and the cold wind gave ample warning. The
attendance is improving. Of course it was good to have told
your sister-in-law to purchase khadi for you. I understand she
went yesterday and bought a fair quantity, I hope, not all for
you. I was tempted to buy an exquisite mat for Rs. 35 for
you. But I resisted the temptation. Mahadev was inclined to
purchase it.
I am glad Nabi Bux told you why he would not eat at
Harijan mess. I shall talk to Amtul Salaam about it. This is
being written before the morning prayer. Don't be alarmed.
I got up only at 3.50.
I miss you during my walks more than at other times. At
other times, I saw very little of you.
Expect you in July at the latest.
Hope Shummy has got rid of his cold, etc., and that Beryl
is thoroughly restored.
Love to you both.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3566. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6375
