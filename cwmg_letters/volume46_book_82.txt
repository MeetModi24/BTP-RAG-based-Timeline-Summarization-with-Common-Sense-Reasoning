82. LETTER TO DALAI LAMA
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 4, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your gift. I am sorry I cannot understand
your language. My desire and hope is that Tibetans should
understand and follow the secret of the message of ahimsa given
by Lord Buddha.
Your friend,
M. K. GANDHI
HIS HIGHNESS THE LAMA
TIBET
From a photostat: G.N. 9674. Also C.W. 6208. Courtesy: Mirabehn
