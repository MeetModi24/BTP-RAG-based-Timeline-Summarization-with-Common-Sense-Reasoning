397. LETTER TO B. SUBBA RAO
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 21, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Will you please answer the following
questions? Are you married? If so, have you children? Do you
want to live here alone? Can you do bodily labour? Are you
keeping good health? Apart from your medical diploma, do you
regard yourself as an all-round good physician? What is the
meaning of describing yourself as 'Ophthalmic Surgeon' ? Have you
special qualifications in that direction?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. B. SUBBA RAO
OPHTHALMIC SURGEON
86, PILLAYAR KOIL ST.
TRIPLICANE, MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 19485
