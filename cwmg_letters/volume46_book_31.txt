31. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
BARDOLI,
April 24, 1931
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your letter. Take proper care of your health while
working. Be patient in everything you do. Let service be its
own reward, no matter what the results of your work. Who are
the others with you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9322
