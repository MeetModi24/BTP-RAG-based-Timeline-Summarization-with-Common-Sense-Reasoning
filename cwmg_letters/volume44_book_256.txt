256. LETTER TO KALAVATI TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 27, 1930
CHI. KALAVATI,
I was glad to get your letter. Be firm as you are in observing
the rules. Ignore those who criticize. Do not even be angry
with them, but love them.
There is much scope for improvement in your handwriting.
With some effort you can improve it.
Blessings from
BAPU
