548. LETTER TO AVANTIKABAI GOKHALE
SEVAGRAM,
March 28, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
Gauribai's passing away must have grieved you more than
everyone else.
But where is the need to console anyone? One can say that
Gauribai went after rendering much service.
Blessings from
BAPU
AVANTIKABAI GOKHALE
KUTCH CASTLE
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
