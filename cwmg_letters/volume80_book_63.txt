63. LETTER TO KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 29, 1945
CHI. KAMALNAYAN1,
I have your letter. I remember Mother had given me
Varma's letter. Now I can't find it. Perhaps I left it behind
in Sevagram. Do you remember what it was about?
Come whenever you and Satyanarayan want to. I am get-
ting along well. The treatment has not begun yet.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
51 MAHATMA GANDHI ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
