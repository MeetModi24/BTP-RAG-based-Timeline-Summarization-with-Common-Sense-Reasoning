286. LETTER TO SATYANARAYAN SINHA
WARDHA,
February 26, 1945
BHAI SATYANARAYAN SINHA,
I have all your letters. I have gone through Prajapati Mish-
ra's letter. There was nothing in the speech to alarm the Govern-
ment. In discussing the constructive programme such a speech
should be considered irrelevant or inopportune. But what was to be
done about it? It is a different matter if the Government wants
to ban even constructive work.
Blessings from
BAPU
