671. LETTER TO GOKULCHAND NARANG
SIMLA,
July 3, 1945
BHAI GOKULCHANDJI1,
I have your letter of June 29,1945. It is so full of rage that you
could have written it only in English. If Rajkumari wrote in English,
she made a great mistake. But what could the poor woman do?
Her education began with English. The ideas were mine.
You and I have different views regarding victory and defeat,
justice and injustice. But what is the harm? A tree is known from
its fruit. Let us see what happens.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI GOKULCHAND NARANG
SAVOY HOTEL
MUSSOORIE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
