11. LETTER TO SARASWATI GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 4, 1939
CHI. SARU,
I have your letter. I hope you are keeping up your courage
for going through your delivery. Have faith in God. Kanti does
not write to me. I am satisfied that I get news about him.
What else do I desire than the welfare of you both? Ba has gone
to Delhi because of Lakshmi's illness. Kanu is leaving for Rajkot
today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6178. Also C.W. 3452. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
