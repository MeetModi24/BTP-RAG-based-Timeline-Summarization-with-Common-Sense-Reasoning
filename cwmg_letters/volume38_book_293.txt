293. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJI
Sunday [December 16, 1928]
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I have your letter. I have received the book about snakes.
If I want another copy, I shall write to you. What was the
accident which befell grandmother, and how? Tell her that she
has many more years still to live.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Tell Gokibehn that I got her letter.
SHRI SHANTIKUMAR
SHANTI BHUVAN, PEDDER ROAD, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4710. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarji
