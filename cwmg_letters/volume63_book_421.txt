421. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
September 29, 1936
CHI. BHANSALI,
It is very good indeed that following Mahadev's advice you
gave up the idea of coming to me and hiding yourself as in a
cave. Your cave is there. Listen to another bit of advice. For
the boil in your arm-pit, perhaps plain earth will not suffice. You
should let ordinary poultice be applied to it. I see no reason
why it should not maturate.
Blessings from
BAPU
