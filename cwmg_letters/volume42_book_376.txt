376. LETTER TO RAMI PAREKH
SABARMATI,
January 12, 1930
CHI. RAMI 2 ,
I suppose I should not expect any letter from you. Ba yearns
to see you. I, on the other hand, get no time even to think about
you all. But I too wish that you should come and pay a visit,
if Kunvarji permits you and you feel inclined. Just now Ba has
gone to Vijapur. She will return in a few days. Manilal is here,
and Sushila too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9713
