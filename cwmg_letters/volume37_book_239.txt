239. LETTER TO B. DE LIGT
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 24, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter enclosing an English translation which you
have very kindly made for me of your open letter. I am so busy
that I have not had the time to go through the open letter, but I
hope to go through the whole of it and let you have my reply as
early as I can. I may find it necessary to give you the reply through
the pages of Young India. If I do, I take it you will not mind it.
Yours sincerely,
B. DE LIGT, ESQ.
ONEX (GENEVA), SWITZERLAND
