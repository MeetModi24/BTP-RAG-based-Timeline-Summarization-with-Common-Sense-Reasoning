368. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
February 14, 1937
BHAI BALWANTSINHA,
There is no reason for you to be overwrought. It is good
you have placed yourself under the doctor's care. You are bound
to be cured. Only do not give up patience. Doctors, vaidyas
and hakims all make mistakes. The system precluding any pos-
sibility of mistake is nature cure. But very few people have
the faith to carry it on and there are very few experienced
practitioners.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1892
