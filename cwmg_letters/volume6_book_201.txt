201. LETTER TO EMPIRE TYPEWRITING COMPANY
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 17, 1906
TO
THE MANAGER
THE EMPIRE TYPEWRITING COMPANY
77, QUEEN VICTORIA STREET
DEAR SIR,
With reference to the "Empire" hired by me, I shall keep it for a
month as from the 12th instant. I understand that the monthly terms are
15/-. You have already received 7/ and I now enclose cheque for the
balance. I shall thank you to let me have the receipt.
Yours faithfully,
[Enclosure]
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4589
