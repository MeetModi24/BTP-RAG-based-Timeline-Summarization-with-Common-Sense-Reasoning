190. LETTER TO SATYADEV
SEVAGRAM,
November 28, 1945
CHI. SATYADEV,
I was glad to have your letter. I would like to have a copy
of the order you have mentioned. I just cannot agree to what
you say. I think there is some misunderstanding. You had
better ask Jawaharlal also and write to me at Calcutta. There I
shall be staying with Satis Babu at Khadi Pratishthan, Sodepur.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
