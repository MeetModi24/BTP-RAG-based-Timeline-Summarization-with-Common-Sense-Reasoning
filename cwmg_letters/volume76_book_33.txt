33. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
SEGAON, WARDHA,
April 12, 1942
MY DEAR AMRITLAL,
I hope you are doing well. I would like to send Abha to
Rajkot. Vina is doing extraordinarily well. Abha too would
do better there than here.
But she perhaps should not go to
Rajkot till she is finally engaged to Kanu Please consult your
wife and let me know.
I am convinced that Abha's happi-
ness depends on her being tied to Kanu.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10337. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
