149. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
[February 26, 1930]
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I have your postcard. You ought to complete the course of injections which
you have commenced. If necessary, therefore, you should stay either in the
Vidyapeeth or the Ashram for that purpose. I believe the Ashram air to be
