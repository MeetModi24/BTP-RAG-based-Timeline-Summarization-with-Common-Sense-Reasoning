91. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
February 3, 1932
BHAI VALJI,
No one in the Ashram can keep a parrot in a cage.
If someone happens to do it, the manager will free the
parrot which is bound to fly away once it gets an opportunity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 7418. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
