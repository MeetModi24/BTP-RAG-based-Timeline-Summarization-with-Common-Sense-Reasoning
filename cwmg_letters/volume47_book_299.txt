299. LETTER TO PADMA
August 8, 1931
CHI. PADMA,
I have got your letter. You should stay where you are and
improve your health. We shall see what to do when Valjibhai
and others go that side. You should always remain contented
wherever you are.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6124; also C.W. 3476. Courtesy:
Prabhudas Gandhi
