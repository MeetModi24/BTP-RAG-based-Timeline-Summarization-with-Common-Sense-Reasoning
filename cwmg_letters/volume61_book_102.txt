102. LETTER TO R. B. GREGG
WARDHA,
May 17, 1935
MY DEAR GOVIND,
Of course I know that absence of letters does not mean
decrease of love. I know what you are doing to spread the
gospel of love.
It must tax your energy to the full.
If America will take to an equivalent of khadi, it will be a
great thing.
Andrews, Pierre Ceresole and Joe Wilkinson, the former's
companion, are here just now. Andrews will be in India for some
[months] with the Poet. Ceresole leaves for Europe on 23rd.
The Village Industries work is going on.
H. L. Sharma is a nature-cure man. He wants to go to
Battle Creek to perfect his knowledge. He wants to use it for
purely humanitarian purposes. I am getting a scholarship for him.

2 Rabindranath Tagore
If he can earn his board and lodging by part-time work it will be
a good thing. Have you any suggestion for him?
Love to you and Radha.
BAPU
