640. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
September 27, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have nothing particular to write about.
You must have received my letter to Mathew. A spinning-
wheel is kept going here continuously for 16 hours.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8477. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
