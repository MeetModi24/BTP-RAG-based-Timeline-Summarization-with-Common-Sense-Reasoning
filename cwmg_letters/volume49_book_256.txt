256. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
March 20, 1932
CHI. PUSHPA,
Would a Sardar write letters? He would act as Sardar and
ask others to write on his behalf.
This time your handwriting was good.
If you pay careful
attention, it is bound to improve. Was there any letter from
Kamala?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3984. Also C.W. 30. Courtesy:
Pushpabehn N. Naik
