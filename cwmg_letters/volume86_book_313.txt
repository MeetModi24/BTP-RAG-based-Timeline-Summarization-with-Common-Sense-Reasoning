313. LETTER TO POTTI SRIRAMULU
NOAKHALI,
December 19, 1946
DEAR RAMULU 3 ,
From Narasimuloo's letter I learn that your fast persists and
that you have not heard from me. I have written to you to
desist from your fast. What is this news I get? I have seen
nothing in the papers.
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
As this was going to the post I received your welcome post-
card of breaking the fast.
From a photostat: G. N. 117
