444. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN CHOWDHARY
PANCHGANI,
June 6, 1945
CHI. RAMNARAYAN,
I have your postcard. I won't call you here. Your idea of
going to Sevagram is good. I shall try to go through the book
when I get it. I did not know that Jugalkishore was your
brother. Hope you are keeping well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAMNARAYAN CHOWDHARY
ADARSH PRESS
AJMER
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
