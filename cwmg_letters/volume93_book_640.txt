640. LETTER TO TARA MASHRUWALA
[January 19, 1941]
CHI. TARI,
You would never write to me, is it not so? If not, let your
friend write.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original:
Pyarelal Papers.
Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
