406. LETTER TO AYAZ PEERBHOY
NEW DELHI,
July 21, 1947
BHAI AYAZ PEERBHOY1,
I have gone through your letter. You are right. I have already
said that the struggle we carried on for the last thirty years was
non-violent in name only but was not non-violent in spirit. The
violence which we harboured in our minds is now coming out.
How can the ignorance of those thirty years be dispelled all at
once? Those of us who understand this must do the best we can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Gujarati: Weekly Observer, 6-3-1948. Also from a
copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
