322. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Your recitation of the Gita is defective. You ought to read it
absolutely correctly and sweetly. Strive to do so. You may seek
guidance from anyone among us who knows the right style. This
should be done quickly. I have spoken to Munnalal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4543
1 The word here in the original appears to be of ambiguous meaning.
