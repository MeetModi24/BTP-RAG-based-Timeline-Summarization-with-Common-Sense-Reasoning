267. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
SODEPUR,
December 9, 1945
CHI. SUSHILA,
I got your letter. It is enough that your aspiration is pure.
I don't think there is anything special in staying with me.
When you serve people wherever you are, I shall take it that
you are with me. I do not feel that you are losing anything
by not being with me. The air is humid and it is cold, too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4967
