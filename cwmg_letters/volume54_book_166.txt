166. LETTER TO MOHANLAL
March 21, 1933
MY DEAR MOHANLAL,
I am much touched by your letter for which you need not
have apologized at all; nor were you an accused person in my
estimation. I knew that you were a member of Lalajee's Society.
But workers have got to answer all kinds of charges in a detached
spirit. You do not perhaps know what charges Lalajee himself
had to answer. I had long discussions with him about these. I
have referred to the charges against Dadabhai in my autobiogra-
phy. Therefore you have been in quite good company. You have
given me however the letter that I desired and expected from
you.
Yours sincerely,
LALA MOHANLAL
C/O SERVANTS OF THE UNTOUCHABLES SOCIETY
LAJPAT RAI BHAVAN
LAHORE
