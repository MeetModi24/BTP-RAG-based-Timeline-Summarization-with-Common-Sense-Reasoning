244. LETTER TO ABHA
March 28, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Your questions are very queer. If I
knew exactly who I was it would be a great feat. My mission is
service of all.
I think that the legend about Radha and Krishna is celestial.
I would advise you not to study me but study God which is
Truth.
Yours sincerely,
SHREEMATI ABHA
18-B HARTAKI BAGAN LANE
P. O. BEADON ST.
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 20728
