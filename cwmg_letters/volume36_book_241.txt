241. LETTER TO S. GANESAN
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 7, 1928
MY DEAR GANESAN,
I have your letter with Dr. Marie Stopes's review. I do not
propose to publish it in Young India as it seems to me to be more
an advertisement of her books and her methods than a serious
review of the chapters seriously written.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1 file
SJT. S. GANESAN
18 PYCROFTS ROAD
TRIPLICANE, MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 13174
