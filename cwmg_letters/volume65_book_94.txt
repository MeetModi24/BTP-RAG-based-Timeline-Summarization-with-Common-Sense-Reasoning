94. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
POONA CITY,
April 15, 1937
MY DEAR REBEL,
I got your last letter yesterday as I was leaving Wardha.
I am glad you were able to go to the villages. It is certainly
good news that the surrounding villages are voluntarily taking
village reform. No good work but spreads like the perfume of
a rose.
You will take the additional man carefully. Please do not
take more work than you can without ruining your body.
Love.
ROBBER
[Ps.]
We leave tonight for Belgaum.
From the original: C.W. 3601. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6401
