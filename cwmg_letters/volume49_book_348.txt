348. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
April 9, 1932
CHI. MANGALA,
If we treat an enemy as a friend, he will certainly come ulti-
mately to trust us.
People who are ill, feel tempted to eat all kinds of things.
If one does not succumb to such temptations, one would get all
right sooner. Your weight will increase slowly.
If you pay sufficient attention, you will surely be able to un-
derstand the Gita.
Blessings from
BAPU
