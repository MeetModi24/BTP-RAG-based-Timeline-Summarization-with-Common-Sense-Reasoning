169. LETTER TO HARDAYAL NAG
POST ANDHERI,
March 18, 1924
DEAR MR. NAG1,
I have your letter of the 9th instant.
Thanks for your inquiry about my health. I am making
steady progress and am able to attend to my correspondence. Do
please therefore write whatever you wish to.
Yours sincerely,
BABU HARDAYAL NAG
CHANDPUR
DIST. TIPPERAH (BENGAL)
From a photostat: S.N. 8519; also C.W. 5122
