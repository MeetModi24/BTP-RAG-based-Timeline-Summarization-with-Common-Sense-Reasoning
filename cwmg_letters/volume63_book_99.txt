99. LETTER TO SHOBHALAL GUPTA
June 22, 1936
BHAI SHOBHALAL,
The test through which Hinduism is passing . . . 1 They alone
can be true servants who have faith in dharma and genuine
love for Harijans and who are prepared to sacrifice themselves
in the service of Harijans.
SHOBHALAL GUPTA
C/O HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH
AJMER
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
