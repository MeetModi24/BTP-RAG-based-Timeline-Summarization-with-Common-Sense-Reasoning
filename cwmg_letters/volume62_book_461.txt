461. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
NANDI DURG,
May 14, 1936
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
Mirabehn tells me that you have reached Segaon. Good.
Now help her and be cheerful. I hope you will not even want
to go anywhere till I return. Train Govind and Dashrath well.
Preserve your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1884
