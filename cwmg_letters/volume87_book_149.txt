149. LETTER TO SIR HUGH DOW
PATNA,
March 25, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
Many thanks for your letter of the 22nd instant. I have
to deny myself the pleasure of writing in my own hand for the
sake of sparing you the infliction of bad handwriting, of which
I am really ashamed. Whenever you feel that you want to discuss
anything with me please do not hesitate to tell me so and I shall
be at your disposal.
I am going on my third tour tomorrow morning. I return
on the 28th instant.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. E. SIR HUGH Dow
GOVERNMENT HOUSE
PATNA
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, pp. 234-
