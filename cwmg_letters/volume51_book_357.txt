357. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI M. THAKKAR
October 8, 1932
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
I hope you got the letter which I wrote to you last week.
You have done right in giving up cereals and pulses for some
time. If you restrict your food to milk and curds, raisins, juice of
sour lemon with soda bicarb, oranges, mosambis, cucumber, toma-
toes, radishes and salad, your weight will certainly go down. Do not
eat almonds or other nuts. If you wish to chew something, you may
eat one ounce of coconut. You may eat less but not more than one
ounce. If it does not agree with you, you can stop it.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6822
