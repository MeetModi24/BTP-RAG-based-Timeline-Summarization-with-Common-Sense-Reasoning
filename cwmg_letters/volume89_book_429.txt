429. LETTER TO YVONNE PRIVAT
October 30, 1947
MY DEAR BHAKTI1,
I have just read your consoling letter. I understand and
appreciate your view-point. Leave me in the care of the great
Unseen and Unknown. I must not be deceived into the belief
that all is well when it is not.
Psychoanalysis has been often . . .2 I have never been able to
find time to study it. You should go your way without caring
to know what I think about these new modes of thought.
"Seek ye first the Kingdom of God and everything will be
added unto you."
I am glad you suffered and got well together in the same
room. Do I not know how you used to bathe together in the
same bathroom as if you were utterly innocent brother and
sister!
My love to you both.
BAPU
MADAME EDMOND PRIVAT
AVENUE DE LA GARE
NEUCHATEL
SWITZERLAND
From a photostat: G. N. 8804
