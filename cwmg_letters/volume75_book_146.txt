146. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 13, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
Today also I shall make do with a postcard. I am all right,
and so you are, I hope. I understand what you say in your letter.
I have to go to Wardha in connection with the States' People's
[Conference]. Hence this much should be enough. Beryl has sent
a shawl. Tell her it is good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4258. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also
G.N. 7890
