504. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
February 6, 1932
CHI. NANIBEHN JHAVERI,
1 am writing letters. Sardar is going through the newspapers
and reading out important items to me. 1 have just learnt about
you and other ladies having been arrested and later released. I
would not write to you thinking you would be in jail and the
next thing I learn from the papers is that you are free and far
away. Now, if you get this letter, do let me have all your
experiences.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari-Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 26
