527. LETTER TO C. K. NAMBIAR
October 31, 1932
MY DEAR NAMBIAR,
I have read your letter from top to bottom. I think that
you are needlessly suspicious and therefore harsh in your judg-
ment. Whilst Press propaganda has a value, substantial results
are only attainable through solid, silent, persistent work. Those
who realize this will not deplore absence of notice of their work
in the Press.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 148
1 The addressee's name is supplied from the G.N. register.
