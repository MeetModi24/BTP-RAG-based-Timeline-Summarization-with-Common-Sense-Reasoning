375. LETTER TO JAMES A. H. MILLS
MAGANWADI,
WARDHA (C. P.),
BARDOLI,
January 26, 1939
DEAR MILLS,
Gandhiji has your letter of 18th inst.
Since you disown all responsibility for what appeared in the Press there
is nothing more to be said about it, though he is still unable to understand
how such a wholesale misrepresentation of what you reported could take
place. He is sorry he has not got that cutting with him to send to you
but surely, you will be able to procure it for yourself and have a look at
it. Anyway, Gandhiji desires me to say that if he is pure he will outlive
all propaganda that ignorance or malice may set on foot against him.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
JAMES A. H . MILLS, ESQ.
ASSOCIATED PRESS OF AMERICA
DJIBUTI
