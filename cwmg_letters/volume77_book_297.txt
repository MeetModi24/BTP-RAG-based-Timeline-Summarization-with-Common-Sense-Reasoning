297. LETTER TO R. R. DIWAKAR
PANCHGANI,
July 19, 1944
BHAI DIWAKAR,
I have not been able to read the entire material. You al-
ready know my views.
I would like all to court imprisonment on or before the 9th
while doing something or other. But that something should be a
normal activity of the Congress, not satyagraha. Do you get the
difference?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am disgusted at our people writing in English.
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Mahatma, Vol. VI, between pp. 336 and
337
