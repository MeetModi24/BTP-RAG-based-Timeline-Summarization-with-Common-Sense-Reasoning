257. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
November 19, 1934
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I got your letter. The pipal tree will get cut down when its time comes.
You should go on with your work. Vinoba has carefully gone through the
article you sent me. I of course had read it. All of us feel that your figures
are exaggerated. You state that foreign cloth costs four annas a yard, swadeshi
three annas a yard and that khadi is even cheaper than that. How can that
be? Think over this. Moreover, you say that khadi is more durable. Is this
true? Also, is it true that foreign cloth is less durable than the swadeshi mill-
cloth? Think over all this.
Blessings from
BAPU
