456. LETTER TO SUNDERLAL
NEW DELHI,
July 25, 1947
BHAI SUNDERLAL,
I have your letter and the enclosed papers. Let me see when
I can go through them. I am writing this at 4.50 in the morning.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
What a pity! Is there no other language than English for
our work? Oh! what culture, what convenience !3
