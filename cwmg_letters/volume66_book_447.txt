447. LETTER TO N. S. HARDIKAR
WARDHA,
February 5, 1938
DEAR MR. HARDIKAR1,
Gandhiji has your letter of the 26th January. As we are likely to meet
at Haripura very soon, Gandhiji says you had better meet him and have a
few minutes from him to discuss the future of the Seva Dal.
Yours sincerely,
MAHADEV DESAI
From the original: N. S. Hardikar Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
