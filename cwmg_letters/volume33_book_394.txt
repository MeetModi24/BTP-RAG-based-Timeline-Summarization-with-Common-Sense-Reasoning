394. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
NANDI DURG,
Vaishakha Krishna 12 [May 28, 1927]
CHI. VASUMATI,
Your letter to hand. It may well be that they are working
without getting results. But what I got from your diary you
could not have given even through many letters. Therefore even
if it relates only to a day prior to that on which you write to
me, do let me have your diary. It is good that you regularly
write to Ramdas.
My health is improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 471. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
