151. LETTER TO F. G. PRATT
NADIAD,
April 23, 1918
DEAR MR. PRATT,
I thank you for your note.
Naturally I accept your assurance that you did not deli-
berately misrepresent the strikers.
I have been summoned to attend the War Conference The
first object is stated to be to sink domestic differences. This
Kheda business falls under that category. I am not ashamed to
approach you again and appeal to you in the name of the ob-
ject of the Conference to recede from the position you have
taken up. What a great relief it would be to the Viceroy if I
could tell His Excellency that we had settled our domestic
quarrel.
I am leaving tonight for Bombay to attend the Begar Con-
ference.
From a copy: C.W. 10684. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
