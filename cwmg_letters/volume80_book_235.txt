235. LETTER TO OMKARSINGH SENGAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 14, 1945
BHAI OMKARSINGHJI,
Your daughter Premlata is distressed. She has chosen a
husband belonging to a different caste and you are preventing
her from marrying him. He is a deserving man. This talk
about castes has become outmoded. Ability alone should count
today. Premlata wants to go on a fast. Show her this post-
card and stop her from undertaking the fast. Write to me if
there is anything else.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
THAKUR OMKARSINGH SENGAR
MADAN KHADI KUTIR
KAROLI STATE, RAJPUTANA
