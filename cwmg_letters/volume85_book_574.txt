574. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
NEW DELHI,
October 11, 1946
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
Your letter is no doubt long. But it does not matter. If
your heart prompts you to go to Borkamata, do go. I do not
know what order to give you. When we meet we shall talk
more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi G. N. 532
