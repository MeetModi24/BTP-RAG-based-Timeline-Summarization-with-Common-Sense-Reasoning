150. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEVAGRAM,
August 8, 1945
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
Your letter is beautiful. Come back fully recovered. Indu
has done a bold thing. I have written to Menon. I enclose a
copy. That is all the time I can spare.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
