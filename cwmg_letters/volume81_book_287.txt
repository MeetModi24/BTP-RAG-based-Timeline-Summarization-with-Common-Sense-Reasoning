287. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
August 27, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
I am happy to know that you and Gajraj are keeping good
health. You will certainly get rid of constipation if you drink
plenty of water.
Everything is all right here.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
