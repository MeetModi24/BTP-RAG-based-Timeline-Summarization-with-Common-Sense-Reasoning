132. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
May 24, 1944
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
When an inadvertent mistake can wipe out even our own
lives, it is no surprise if some pictures fade out. We should derive
not only a lesson from this but some consolation also. The rest
from Pyarelal's letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4440
