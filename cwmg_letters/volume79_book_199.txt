199. LETTER TO DR. SYED MAHMUD
SEVAGRAM,
February 8, 1945
BHAI MAHMUD,
Why did you write to me in English? From now on write
in Hindustani. Why do you have to apologize? We are all mem-
bers of the same family. Do not be unhappy; be cheerful. Re-
gain your health.
Yours,
GANDHI
From a photostat of the Urdu : G.N. 5093
