176. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
December 3, 1928
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I have all your letters. The whole affair is bad. But we must
not resist. Therefore I have wired you. Please inform the other
centres. You should now do whatever is possible. I have sent a
wire to Shankerlal too and issued a brief Press message. Here
are copies of correspondence.
No more today but love of which you will need now much.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 8921
