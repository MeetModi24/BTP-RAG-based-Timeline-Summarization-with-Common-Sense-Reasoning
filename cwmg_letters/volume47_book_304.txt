304. LETTER TO LILAVATI SAVARDEKAR
BOMBAY,
August 9, 1931
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. My advice is, you must stick to your re-
solve, no matter what happens.
You may not know that I tried to do all I could for the
Devdasis of the South.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Bombay Chronicle, 29-8-1931
