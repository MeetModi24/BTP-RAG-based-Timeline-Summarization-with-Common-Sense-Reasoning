366. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
Silence Day
CHI. RAMDAS,
These days, I get no time at all. I rise at three o'clock. That does no
harm to my health. Have you written to Nimu to come over? I get no letters
from her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
