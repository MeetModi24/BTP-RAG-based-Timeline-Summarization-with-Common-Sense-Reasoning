295. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
May 4, 1927
I have been wanting to write to you for many days past, but
I could do it only today. Hardly a day passes without your name
coming up or without your being remembered.
You wanted me to stay at Panchgani, but it could not be
done. It was essential for my work that I stayed in this province.
I will certainly finish my work here when I get well, though I
might have to revise my methods.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, pp. 88 & 90
