473. LETTER TO M. MANICKAM
January 3, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter and I am glad that you have drawn my
attention to the fact of non-Hindus offering obstruction, mis-
called satyagraha, in connection with a temple in Colombo. I
considered it to be so important that I have dealt with it in a
Press message which, no doubt, you have seen. I do hope that
there has been no repetition of interference by non-Hindus.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. M. MANICKAM
PRESIDENT, THE NEHRU SANGHAM
165 SEA STREET, COLOMBO
From a microfilm: S.N. 19151
