87. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 7, 1939
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I got your complaint. I have been writing regularly. You
should complain to the postmaster. I have already written to
you saying that you can do as you wish.
Consult Shankerlal-
bhai. That is why I am not sending a telegram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 431
