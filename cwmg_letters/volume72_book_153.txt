153. LETTER TO SHOBHALAL GUPTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 31, 1940
BHAI SHOBHALAL 1 ,
I have a faint recollection that I had written to Durga-
prasad about the poem It is indeed provocative. But it is also

Blessings from
