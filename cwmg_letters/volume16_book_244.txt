244. LETTER TO JUSTICE RANKIN
[SABARMATI ASHRAM,]
January 11, 1920
DEAR JUSTICE RANKIN,
Here are the extracts I promised regarding my declarations
in connection with civil disobedience. I regret to say that I
have not been able to trace Swami Shraddhanandji's telegrams
or his letters received just after the 30th March. If you consider
them to be very material evidence in connection with any point
I suppose you could get copies of the telegrams through the
Telegraph Department and possibly a copy of Dr. Satyapal's
letter through the C.I.D. in the Punjab. The Superintendent
who took me to Bombay from Sawai Madhopur told me that he
had seen Dr. Satyapal's letter to me.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat of the pencilled draft in Mahadev Desai's hand:
S.N. 6988
