324. LETTER TO DR. SYED MAHMUD
SODEPUR,
December 17, 1945
BHAI MAHMUD,
How was it that you came and went away? I kept on
waiting? It was much easier for you to come to Sodepur. How
can any arrangement be made now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 5092. Also Pyarelal Papers.
Courtesy : Pyarelal
