325. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
October 28, 1909
MY LORD,
I beg to enclose herewith copy of a letter to the Private Secretary
to Lord Crewe with reference to meetings held all over the Presidency
of Madras.
I may also add that cablegrams have been received from Johannes-
burg saying that active operations have commenced against passive resis-
ters again in the Transvaal. Twenty-one have been arrested and im-
prisoned for three months, including the Acting Chairman of the
