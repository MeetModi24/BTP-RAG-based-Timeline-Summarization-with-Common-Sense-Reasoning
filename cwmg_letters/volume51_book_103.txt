103. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
[On or before September 18, 1932]
MY DEAR MARY,
I am working against time. Hence this card only. The
story in the Mysore magazine seems to me to be unbelievable.
If I survive the ordeal, you must remind me and I shall inquire.
My introduction to the Gita appears in an issue of Young India.
I hope you are able to understand the ordeal. For me it is a
matter of pure joy. You shall read Ch. IX and XII of the
Gita. But the Guide to Health you must ask again.
Love.
BAPU
MISS F. MARY BARR
KARIMNAGAR
H.E.H. NIZAM'S DOMINIONS
From a photostat: G.N. 5983. Also C.W. 3311. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
