151. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
WARDHA,
November 21, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
I had your letter. The Hindustan Times will continue to be
available to you as before as Devdas who is here has agreed to it.
I have a letter from Draupadi saying that she is happy and would
now write regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, pp. 210-
1 A Harijan leader of Ahmedabad
2 The letter appeared under the date-line "Ahmedabad, November
21, 1935".
