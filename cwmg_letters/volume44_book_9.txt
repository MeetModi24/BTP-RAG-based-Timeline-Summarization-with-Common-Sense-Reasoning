9. LETTER TO AMINA QURESHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 6, 1930
CHI. AMINA,
I was very happy to read your letter. I was glad to know
that you had a baby. May God grant him long life and may he
be as brave as Qureshi and you. Do the girls remember me?
I trust you keep good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6656
