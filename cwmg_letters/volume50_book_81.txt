81. LETTER TO ROHINIBEHN DESAI
June 20, 1932
CHI. ROHINI 1 ,
I got your and Surbala's letters. What a coincidence that I
got your and Hamida's letters almost at the same time! Next
time you feel inspired to write, tell me how you spent your time in
jail.
I hope uncle's health is all right now. Our blessings to both
you sisters.
BAPU
SMT. ROHINIBEHN
C/O SJT. KANJIBHAI DESAI
GOPIPURA, SURAT
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2657
