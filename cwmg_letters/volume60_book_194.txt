194. LETTER TO AVINASILINGAM CHETTIYAR
February 1, 1935
MY DEAR AVINASILINGAM,
I enclose herewith a letter received by me from Subra-
maniam and copy of my reply. If the facts set forth in the
letter are correct, I hope you will take energetic steps to overtake
the evil.
SHRI AVINASILINGAM CHETTIYAR, M.L.A.
NEW DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
