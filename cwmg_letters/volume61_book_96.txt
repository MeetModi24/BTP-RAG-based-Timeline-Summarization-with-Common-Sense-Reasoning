96. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
WARDHA,
May 16, 1935
CHI. MARY,
Your letter. Wherever you are you need complete rest. Why
not take it before it becomes too late? Instead of Miraj, why not
undergo examination in Bombay? You almost touch Bombay
going to Miraj. But doing it in Bombay will serve the same pur-
pose without any cost. There are most competent doctors in
Bombay. Anyway you must be healed quickly.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6041. Also C.W. 3370. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
