9. LETTER TO R. L. SUR
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 11, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of the 28th ultimo. I have sent the
message to Mrs. Fisher which I have incorporated in my letter to
Bishop Fisher. I thank you however for reminding me of my
promise and the offer to send the message to Mrs. Fisher.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. L. SUR
SECRETARY TO BISHOP FISHER
METHODIST EPISCOPAL CHURCH
3, MIDDLE TON STREET
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 14094
