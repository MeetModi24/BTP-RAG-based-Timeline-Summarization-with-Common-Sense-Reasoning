218. LETTER TO J. R. GLORNEY BOLTON
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, S.W. 1,
November 21, 1931
DEAR MR. BOLTON 1 ,
I was much touched by your letter. I had already seen your
letter in the Times, and thought it was just the thing. So far as I
can see nothing is going to come out of the Conference, when there-
fore, the struggle revives, many Englishmen and women will be
faced with the question whether they can put right before coun-
try. I am still making a desperate effort to secure a settlement,
and thus avoid conflict which is likely to be more bitter than that
of last year.
Yours sincerely,
J. R. GLORNEY BOLTON, ESQ.
9 KING's BENCH WALK
E.C. 4
From a photostat: S.N. 18329
