337. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
August 5, 1941
CHI. VALJI,
Once I have agreed, where is the need for you to decide
anything? But it would be extremely painful for me to give the
decision you want. I wish to settle everything at the same time.
Durga is in Bulsar with her sister's husband at present for a
change of air.
Blessings from
BAPU
PROF. V. G. DESAI
DEVAGIRI
POONA 4
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7496. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
