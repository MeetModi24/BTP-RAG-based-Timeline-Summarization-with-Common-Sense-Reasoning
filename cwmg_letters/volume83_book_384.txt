384. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
URULI [-KANCHAN],
March 27, 1946
MY DEAR C. R.,
You are giving me good lessons in Tamil and incidentally in
Sanskrit. From which root is Lakshmi derived? I never knew
that etymologically 'Lakshmi' was (ocean-born). Why not rombu
but romba for 'much'? I remember having learnt rombu.
The news you give me about the temple-entry case is interest-
ing and disturbing. You will tell me what happens.
Yes, I am off to Delhi (D. V.) on 1st April. Heaven help
them that a fool should go on All Fools Day!
So your son-in-law is off to America with Goenka. He has
become a great man.
But nothing interests me so much as your stomach trouble.
It may be beyond me but not nature cure. If it is beyond
nature cure, it is beyond repair. Yours is not that. Rest and
be thankful. I wanted to give you rest. But now I can't till
after my return from Delhi.
Romba anbudan
BAPU
From a photostat : G. N. 2120
