146. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
January 19, 1940
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. Vijaya's address is: Ambla via Songarh,
Kathiawar.
Your work will not suffer in the least if you stay here for a
month. What would happen if you were bed-ridden, or if you had
to go to help Father? It will be better to come here now and improve
your health than to be forced to come here after it has already bro-
ken down. If you ask Rajen Babu, he too will give you the same
advice. As to your question how you should work there, the answer
is simple enough. If you get even one or two women who will spin
willingly and sincerely, you should labour for them as best as you
can. You should also go from door to door selling as much khadi
as possible. Get a few women together and form a bhajan party,
and teach them how to read and write. Go among the poor women
also and spread literacy among them. Go to the Harijan quarters
and meet the women there. Thus you can widen the sphere of your
work as much as you wish provided your health remains good
enough. By and by other women also will join you. But the fact is
that your health is not strong enough. Nor can you make adequate
preparation. It appears to you that you have no work because you
have not chalked out a programme.
We had a lot of rain here.
Sushila is in Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
