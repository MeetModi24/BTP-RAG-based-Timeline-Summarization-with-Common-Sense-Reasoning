106. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 21, 1940
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your letter. I have also written to Madhav. All of you
must naturally be very sad at Sumitra's death. Still, do not such
occasions serve to test our wisdom and faith? I trust you will all
pass the test.
Europe is now an arena of bloody strife. Be that as it may.
I have hardened my heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8046. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
