81. LETTER TO PANNALAL JHAVERI
[After September 23, 1941]
CHI. PANNALAL,
How could anyone have imagined that Nanibehn would leave us
so suddenly ? I cannot forget her ever-smiling face, yet I must. The
soul that dwelt in her body is of course immortal. Our concern was
with that soul. That remains. Let us emulate her good qualities and
character.
1 The period is inferred from the contents. Nanibehn, the addressee's wife, passed
away on September 23, 1941.
46
There is no need to preach anything to you. Gangabehn too is
competent.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari - Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 33
