167. LETTER TO RATANBEHN
SEVAGRAM,
August 14, 1946
CHI. RATANBEHN,
I have your letter to Chhaganlal. I have received Rs. 51
which you sent for distributing food-grains among the Harijans.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. RATANBEHN
C/O HASMUKHLAL FAUJDAR
15 LILY COURT
CHURCHGATE, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
