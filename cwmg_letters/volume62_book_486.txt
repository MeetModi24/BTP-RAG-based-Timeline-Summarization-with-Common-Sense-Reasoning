486. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
May 18, 1936
CHI. PRABHA,
You have been causing some anxiety. It is over 12 days
since I got your little note. There was a wire from you, to
which also I have replied. How are you keeping now? Have
you started taking milk? How much milk do you take? What
is your decision?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3469
