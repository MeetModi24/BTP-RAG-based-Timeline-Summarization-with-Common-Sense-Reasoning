283. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
October 13, 1937
CHI. JANAKIBEHN,
I have a letter from Acharya Ramdev saying that you
must accept the invitation to go to Dehra Dun. I don't have the
date with me. Shriman will get all right, you may rest assured.
If you cannot go, send him a wire. It will be better of course
if you can go. Is it necessary to consult your lord and master?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2989
