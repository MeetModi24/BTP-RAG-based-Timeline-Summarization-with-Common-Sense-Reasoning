114. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 19, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There have been no letters from you for two days. Today I am
saving time. Yesterday I just could not write. We really miss you when
there is some illness here. Two persons are ill. The homoeopath could
not cope with them. Both are running high fever. It is of course malaria.
Ramdas, Nimu and the children are coming today.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
