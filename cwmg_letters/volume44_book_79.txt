79. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day [July 28, 1930]
CHI. GANGABEHN (SENIOR),
I am glad to see that you are completely at peace. In be-
ing content with opportunities of service which come to us un-
sought lies the growth of the soul; that is the only way in which
we can realize it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- 6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 40; also C.W. 8754. Courtesy: Ganga-
behn Vaidya
