71. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
June 7, 1934
The rains have started here, making touring on foot im-
possible. I am, therefore, spending three days in Bhadrak. I
will leave tomorrow (Friday) for Wardha. I will stay there for
four days and arrive in Bombay on the date fixed.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 146
