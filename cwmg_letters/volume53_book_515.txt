515. LETTER TO DEVIDUTT SHUKLA
February 23, 1933
BHAI DEVIDUTTJI1,
I have your letter. I am aware of the service rendered by
Pandit Mahavir Prasadji to the Hindi language. Sarasvati was
known to me even in South Africa. I may also be allowed to join
in congratulating him on the occasion of his 70th birthday and
I hope that we shall have the benefit of his literary services for
many more years and that Sarasvati will continue to progress.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From Hindi: C.W. 9666. Courtesy: Hindi Sahitya Sammelan, Prayag
