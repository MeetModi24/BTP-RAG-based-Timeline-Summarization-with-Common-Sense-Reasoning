73. LETTER TO KASHINATH TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 27, 1930
CHI. KASHINATH,
I have your letter. What I said in my last letter regarding
prayers must have partly answered the questions which you ask in
your letter. Regarding image-worship, we don't forbid anyone
to use an Image. But we cannot have one in our common
prayer.
I have suggested that Shantabehn should be examined by a
doctor. But I can offer no proper guidance from this distance.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I get no time to attend to the Hindi translation.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5243
