371. LETTER TO KAKALBHAI KOTHARI
June 18, 1939
BHAI KAKALBHAI,
I got your misty vapourings about Rajkot. I glanced through
the thing. I saw nothing in it but rhetoric. But isn't rhetoric
your life? How then can you help yourself? If you have patience,
one day you will realize the truth of these remarks of mine.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary.
Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
