32. LETTER TO E. C. DEWICK
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 6, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your cordial letter. As owing to the death of Maganlal
Gandhi all my plans have become upset, I am not sure whether
I would be able to attend the Federation meeting in Mysore.
But I will not finally make up my mind against the idea of go-
ing to Mysore.
Yours sincerely,
REV. E. C. DEWICK
2 INFANTRY ROAD, BANGALORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 13455
