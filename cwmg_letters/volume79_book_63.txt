63. LETTER TO INDU MASHRUWALA
[January 16, 1945]
CHI. INDU 2 ,
Are you asking my blessing? I have already refused it to
Gomati. She understood. What is so novel about your marry-
ing within your caste? Still, if you want my blessing you have
it. Never mind if you both belong to the same caste; both of you
should break the barriers of caste. Make your own rules. Observe
them and do work of service. Be good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
