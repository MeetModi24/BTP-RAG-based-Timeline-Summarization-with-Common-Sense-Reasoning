340. LETTER TO NARMADABEHN RANA
August 7, 1932
CHI. NARMADA,
I got your letter. Personally, I do wish that you could stay
in the Ashram and make great progress. Work hard and im-
prove your handwriting, etc. Your resolution is noble. God
will certainly help you in it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Write to me from time to time.
From Gujarati: C.W. 2758. Courtesy: Ramnarayan N. Pathak
