36. LETTER TO JAYANTI N. PAREKH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 9, 1937
CHI. JAYANTI,
I got your letter. Give the accompanying letter to Dinkar if
he is feeling better. Public workers ought not to fall ill.
I don't understand what you say regarding the reform of the
Provincial Committee. Send me a copy of the rules. I am
making inquiries, of course.
I am very glad indeed that you three brothers have come
together.
How I wish Indu would settle down to something.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6264
