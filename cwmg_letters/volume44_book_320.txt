320. LETTER TO GOKIBEHN
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 17, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
I was very glad to have your letter. Death is a highway
which all of us must tread, whether we wish or no. And though
countless travellers tread it, it ever seems deserted. Hence this is
also the path of supreme peace. He who is afraid to take it, feels
agitated; he who remains fearless enjoys the bliss of peace.
Jai Shrikrishna from
MOHANDAS
SMT. GOKIBEHN
