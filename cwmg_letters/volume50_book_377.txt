377. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
August 14, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
If the same thing is mentioned in somebody else's letter,
why should you not read it?
It is good that you have put on three pounds. Do not re-
duce because Shakaribehn has come.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9914. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
wala
