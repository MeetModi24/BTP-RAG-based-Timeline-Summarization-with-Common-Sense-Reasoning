236. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
May 13, 1920
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Read the enclosed letter and pass it on to Bhai Shaukat
Ali in an (open) envelope. That letter explains everything.
Hence I am not writing to you anything more. I shall send
you another copy which you should give to Dr. Mehta. If I
have to go1, how I go has to be considered. Everything de-
pends on what the Muslim brethren desire.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
