43. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
[February 12, 1922]
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I have embarked on a fast from today for Gorakhpur's sin. It will end on
Friday. This was the least I ought to have done. The civil disobedience has
been suspended for the present. I have your letter. What you did was well
enough. I will not enter into further discussion on the matter just now.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. PRABHUDAS
C/O KHUSHAL GANDHI
NEAR THE MIDDLE SCHOOL
RAJKOT SUBURB
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33053
1 The letter is incomplete.
2 That is, Kartik Sud 1, observed in Gujarat as the New Year Day
3 From the reference to Gandhiji's fast; he started the five-day fast on this date.
