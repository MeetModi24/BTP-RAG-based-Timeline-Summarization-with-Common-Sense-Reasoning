61. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
March 13, 1930
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I hope you received what might have been my last letter
The news given to me of my impending arrest was said to be
absolutely authentic. But we have reached the second stage
safely. We take the third tonight. I am sending you the pro-
gramme . All the friends are most particular that I should not go
to Ahmedabad for the Working Committee. There is considerable
force in the suggestion. The Working Committee may, therefore,
come to the place where we may be for the day or you alone
may come. The sentiment that we won't voluntarily go back
without finishing the struggle is being well cultivated. My going
back will mar it a bit. Jamnalalji told me he had written to you
about it. I hope Kamla is keeping well.
I asked yesterday that full wires should be sent to you.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 84
