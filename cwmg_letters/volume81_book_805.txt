805. LETTER TO JETHALAL GANDHI
October 31, 1945
CHI. KAKU,
I have your letter. What you say is right. Yesterday I read
it out to your father's sister also. I appreciate your writing
so frankly about service-mindedness but I wish to caution you.
By following tradition you will feel the burden of the family all
the more and you must know that sometimes one gets entangled
in it. So you will have to cultivate the spirit of service.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JETHALAL K. GANDHI
C/O JEEVANLAL (1929) LTD.
127 MINT STREET
G. T. MADRAS
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
