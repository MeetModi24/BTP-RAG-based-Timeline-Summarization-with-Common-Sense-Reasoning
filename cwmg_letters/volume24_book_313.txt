313. LETTER TO V. G. DESAI
Sravana Sud 11 [August 11,1924]
BHAISHRI VALJI,
I have not been able to follow clearly what Ganesan has to
say and your question. But, in a general way, I would advise you
to leave the matter to Ganesan's discretion.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
The replies to other matters have been given yesterday.
V. G. DESAI
STERLING CASTLE
SIMLA
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: C.W. 6026. Courtesy: V. G.
Desai
