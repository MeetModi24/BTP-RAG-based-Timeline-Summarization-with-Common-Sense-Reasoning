239. LETTER TO VERRIER ELWIN
September 30, 1932
It required my fast, among many other things also, to bring
me in physical touch with members of the Sangh. I had loving
chats with Father Winslow. I was glad to know these brethren.
Shyamrao was also with them.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 92
