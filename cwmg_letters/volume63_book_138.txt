138. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
WARDHA,
July 3, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
I hope you found everything in order in Segaon. I am
making a desperate effort to reach Segaon on Sunday.
Yes, I
read Mrs. Sanger's speech. Most leaders have gone. It was
an anxious time.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6354. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9820
