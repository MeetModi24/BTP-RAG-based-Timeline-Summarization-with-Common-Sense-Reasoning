360. LETTER TO G. V. MAVALANKAR
March 5, 1935
BHAI MAVALANKAR,
I have your letter. Thakkar Bapa's terms were exactly similar.
I can very well visualize from here the load on you. There are
only a few workers, so they will have to bear the burden.
Do whatever you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1243
