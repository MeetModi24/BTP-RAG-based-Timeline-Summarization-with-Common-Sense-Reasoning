369. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
August 18, 1938
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Today if the rain stops, otherwise tomorrow under any cir-
cumstances. You yourself should go to see Gajanan . . .1 having
fallen ill . . . If he does not feel comfortable there, he may stay at
the Harijan Ashram. Let him not . . . endanger his health . . .
Ask him and find out if he wants anything. A co-worker of his
has got cholera. Who is he? Where does he come from?
Here all . . . carefully . . .
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8570
