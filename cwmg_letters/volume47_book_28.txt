28. LETTER TO PADMA
BORSAD,
June 20, 1931
CHI. PADMA,
I have your letter. I cannot understand why Vasumatibehn
should try to get your private notebook. Nobody is entitled to
take anything belonging to somebody else without the latter's
knowledge. I am inquiring about the matter. Do not, however,
take it very much to heart. You should be generous. Your chief
aim should be to improve your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6121
