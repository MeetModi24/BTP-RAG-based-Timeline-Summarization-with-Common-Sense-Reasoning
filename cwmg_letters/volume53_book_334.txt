334. LETTER TO N. R. KSHIRSAGAR
February 7, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I understand what you say. If
Harijans were not Hindus, samskara would be necessary. None is
necessary for Hindus before they can enter temples. Of course
they have to observe the laws of ceremonial cleanliness and the
rest.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. R. KSHIRSAGAR
15 NABHIKAM STREET
VELLORE (N.A.)
From a microfilm: S.N. 19309
