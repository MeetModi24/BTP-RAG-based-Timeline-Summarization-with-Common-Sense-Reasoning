85. LETTER TO K. G. SAIYIDAIN
SEVAGRAM,
January 21, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your note. I missed you at the Conference which was
a good success. I know the worth of your word.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. K. G. SAIYIDAIN
HEAD, ED. DEPT.
SHRINAGAR
KASHMIR
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
