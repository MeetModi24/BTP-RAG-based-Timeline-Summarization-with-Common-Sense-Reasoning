470. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
May 1, 1932
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
It is good that you wrote to me. I am going to write about
the subject1; so I do not expatiate on it here. You drew the atten-
tion of a responsible person to what you felt was a slip. Now
you have only to watch what he would do. As for you, you have
done your duty.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 346. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
