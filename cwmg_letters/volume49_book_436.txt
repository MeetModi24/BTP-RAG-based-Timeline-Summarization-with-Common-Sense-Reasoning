436. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
April 24, 1932
CHI.,
Mother India should possess the virtues of patience, capa-
city for endurance, forbearance, courage, non-violence, fearless-
ness, etc. One can cultivate them in the Ashram.
Do we remember every event in our present life? If we did,
we would become mad. After we have learnt the proper lesson
from anything which we would like to remember, what harm is
there if we forget it? On the contrary, it would be for our good.
Blessings from
BAPU
