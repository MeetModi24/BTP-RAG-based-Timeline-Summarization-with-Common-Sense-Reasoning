320. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
May 2, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
I was very happy to see your signature on the letters about
the meetings. You know how much I value your signature.
Shankerlal told me about your activities and I was glad to
hear it.
Ask Mridu to give me a report of her work.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original : C. W. 11133. Courtesy : Sarabhai
Foundation
