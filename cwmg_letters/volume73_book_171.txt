171. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
November 11, 1940
CHI. PREMA,
Do as Shankarrao advises you. But he will do nothing
without consulting me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PREMABEHN KANTAK
ASHRAM
SASVAD
POONA DISTRICT
From a photostat of the Gujarati. G.N. 10414. Also C.W. 6853. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
