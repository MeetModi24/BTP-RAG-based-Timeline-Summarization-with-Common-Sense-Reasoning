105. LETTER TO S. SATYAMURTHI
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
October 9, 1931
DEAR SATYAMURTHI,
I have your letter giving me your views about the scope of
our demands—about Indian States' Subjects, Defence, External
Affairs and Finance. I do not know when the subjects will be
reached, but of course I shall bear in mind all you say on the
four heads.
Yours sincerely,
S. SATYAMURTHI, ESQ.
2/18 CAR STREET
TRIPLICANE
MADRAS
From a photostat: S.N. 18047
