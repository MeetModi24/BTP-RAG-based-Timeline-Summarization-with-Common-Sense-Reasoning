506. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Thursday, July 15, 1926
BHAI MOOLCHANDJI,
I got your letter. I very much appreciate your resolve to
make khadi the centre of your activities and to impart educa-
tion too through it. If I receive from you any information about
khadi, which may be of general use, I will certainly publish it
in Navajivan.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI MOOLCHAND, TEACHER
MONPUR (CENTRAL INDIA)
