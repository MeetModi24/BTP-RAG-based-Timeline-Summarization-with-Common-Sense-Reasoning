129. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 4, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. There is no reason to worry about me. At
the moment the blood-pressure is ideal. Last night it was 136/88 and
today it is 146/96. In the morning it was 172/98. I am also able to
eat well. Ba is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
