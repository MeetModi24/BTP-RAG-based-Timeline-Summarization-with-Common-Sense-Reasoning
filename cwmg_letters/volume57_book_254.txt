254. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
March 5, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
I have your letter. My weight is 107. Food as usual.
B. P. not taken for some time. Health quite up to the mark.
Yes I had your Gujarati letter. It was quite good. By all
means learn Sanskrit.
I wish I knew German grammar. I am sorry I was never
able to learn German.
I can suggest no reason for the change of 'ci' into 'qt' when it
is joined on to '.-.t( \-:i' except that it is easier to pronounce.
I am glad you went to Sharda Mandir.
I cannot believe R. approving of the persecution of the Jews
in Germany.
Of course I want you to attain perfection. Nothing like
trying for it.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
