28. LETTER TO ANANDSHANKAR B. DHRUVA
September 19, 1934
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
God has saved you and me from a catastrophe. I had re-
commended . . .2 to you. For eight or nine years all of us thought
him to be a good man. He hardly talked with anybody. But he
has been found to be a man given to lust and a fraud. He has
suddenly left the Ashram. I rarely recommend anybody. When
even one like . . . is found to be a man of straw, whom can I
recommend?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
