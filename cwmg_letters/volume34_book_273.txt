273. LETTER TO WILLIAM SMITH
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
August 7, 1927
DEAR MR. SMITH,
I thank you for your letter about the Italian method of castra-
tion enclosing a letter from the Superintendent, Imperial Cattle-
breeding Farm, Karnal.
I shall see if I can prepare something for you that may be
fit for publication. But I hope you will not mind delay; for, as
you know I have not much time at my disposal. But I am ask-
ing the manager to let me have accurate details and figures.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 12921
