269. LETTER TO REGISTRAR, HIGH COURT, BOMBAY
LAHORE,
January 24, 1920
SIR,
I beg to acknowledge your letter of the
instant.
The 3rd March next will suit me for the hearing of the rule
against Mr. Mahadev Desai and myself.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat of the draft in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 7063
