384. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 11, 1939
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have your letter. Quite a few of us have gathered in the
room: Gilder1, Jivaraj2, Agatha3, . . .4, Pattabhi5, Kishorelal Fryd-
man etc. Others are sitting outside. The doctors have warned
me that I should take care of my health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
