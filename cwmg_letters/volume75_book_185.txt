185. LETTER TO M. S. ANEY
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1941
DEAR BAPUJEE,
I have your letter. Three names come to my mind. H. N.
Kunzru, Hamid Ali, ex-Collector, Satara, K. A. D. Naoroji (Lieut.)
of the Tatas. I have given you enough to choose from.
I have your other letter just now which I have not yet read.
Of course all your letters are treated as confidential.
Hope you are not freezing there.
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
