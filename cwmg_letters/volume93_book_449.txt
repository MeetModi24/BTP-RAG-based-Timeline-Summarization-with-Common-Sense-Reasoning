449. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 27, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
I arrived this morning. I am leaving for Wardha this evening.
You will come, won't you? The car is coming to take you.
Come with your luggage. You can keep the car waiting for a
while. You will have the opportunity to see Mother and others,
won't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
