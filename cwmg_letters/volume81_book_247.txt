247. LETTER TO TRIBHUVANDAS SHAH
August 22, 1945
BHAI TRIBHUVANDAS1,
I have received your books. I have gone through your in-
structions. The big question is when I shall be able to read those
books. I very much wish to but a man can never fulfil all his
wishes. Isn't it baffling?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. TRIBHUVANDAS SHAH
BARODA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
