386. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
June 25, 1937
BELOVED DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
I got your note. Mahadev tells me that the doctor would
get nervous if I were to go there. So I will not go. I am not
worried. Be brave. If necessary I will go over and see you after
the operation. Please send me a report yourself. The letter from
Kanti is being dispatched today. Read it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 382
