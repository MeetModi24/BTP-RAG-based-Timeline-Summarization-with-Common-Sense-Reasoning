14. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
[Before April 28, 1935]
CHI. NARAHARI,
I got your letter. I have sent a wire to Titus asking him to
hand over charge to you immediately and to wait there till the
arrival of Narandas and then come over here.
I have also written to him. Do you have any suspicion regard-
ing his integrity? If possible I wish to keep him with us. Please,
therefore, guide me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
How is Vanamala1? How are you?
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9077
