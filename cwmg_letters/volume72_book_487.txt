487. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEVAGRAM,
September 4, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
All of them do not take the same interest in collecting my
articles as you do. Therefore I do not find anything wrong in
your taking Harijan Sevak.
I understand the rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4360
