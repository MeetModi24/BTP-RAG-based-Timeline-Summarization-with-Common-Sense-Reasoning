205. LETTER TO HARILAL GANDHI
October 11, 1934
CHI. HARILAL,
I liked your letter very much. I hope the changes in you
will endure. You should have patience.
Since you have come
to trust me, there will be no difficulty.
We shall think before asking Nanalalbhai for help.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
