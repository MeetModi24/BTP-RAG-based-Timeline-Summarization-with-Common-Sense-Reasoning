59. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
May 8, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Send the accompanying letter to Panchgani. I welcome the
idea that you should set up house with her. We shall think over
the matter when Kanchan returns. If on getting Kanchan's reply
you think it necessary to go to Panchgani you may do so. I have
no objection if you stay there and Kanchan comes here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8543. Also C.W. 7085. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
