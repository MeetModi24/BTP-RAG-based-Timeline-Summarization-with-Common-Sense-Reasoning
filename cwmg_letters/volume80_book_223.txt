223. LETTER TO KUSUM NAIR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 13, 1945
CHI. KUSUM,
I have received your letter written in beautiful Hindustani.
I shall be a little free after the 20th. You may come then. I
will try to answer your questions. Do not go about shouting
that you are coming to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KUSUMBEHN NAIR
N. I. P. & I. BUREAU
74 LAKSHMI BUILDING
SIR PHEROZESHAH MEHTA ROAD
BOMBAY 1
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
