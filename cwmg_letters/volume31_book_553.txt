553. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
Asvina Sud 12, 1982 [October 18, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
You must have got my letter written yesterday. If you can
find time, go and visit Pratap Pandit's tannery, and ask him when
he will send his man.
Kamala was thoroughly examined by Dr. Rajabali. There is
no cause for anxiety. It has been decided to put her under his
treatment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2874
