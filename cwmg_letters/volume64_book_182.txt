182. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
December 20, 1936
CHI. A. SALAAM,
You have indeed started writing a good deal in Hindi.
What is the use of sending a telegram to you? There is no
limit to your foolishness. If you are not patient with the treat-
ment, then please come to Segaon. I will give you the treat-
ment I desire. I will reach Segaon on the 29th, or, at the
latest, on the 31st.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 368
