167. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
SEVAGRAM,
March 4, 1942
BHAI GOPICHAND,
I have gone through all your articles. They are good. I shall not
suggest any emendations. Do you wish to get them published ? If so,
you must give them a close scrutiny. Would they be in the three Punjabi
scripts and not in Hindustani ?
I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
1 Jamnalal Bajaj had died of cerebral haemorrhage on February 11, 1942.
