301. LETTER TO NIRMALA B. MASHRUWALA
October 3, 1932
CHI. NIRMALA,
I had got your letter of the 13th. I am slowly regaining
strength. I had got the proofs of Gitamanthan. I had also received
Babukaka's letter.
The pain in the elbow seems to have disappeared since I
undertook the fast. But I don't know if it will come back when
there is more flesh on the body. Ask Surendra to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI NIRMALABEHN
C/O SHRI BALUBHAI MASHRUWALA
TOPIWALA'S BUNGALOW
SANDHURST ROAD
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2887. Courtesy: Nirmalabehn
Shroff
