546. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
DELHI,
March 31, 194[2]
DEAR SISTER,
Read your letter of March 21, 1942. The resolutions you have
sent are no doubt good. They should be placed before the Board.
Let us see what happens. Both the things are important.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAMESHWARI NEHRU
2 WARIS ROAD
LAHORE
PUNJAB
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7993. Also C.W. 3091. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
