282. LETTER TO G. RAMACHANDRA RAO
SEVAGRAM,
February 25, 1945
BHAI RAMACHANDRA RAO,
The enclosed is a rough draft of the scheme along with details
as to its estimated budget. Give it shape and return it to me.
It will be placed before the Committee.
Blessings from
BAPU
SEVAGRAM ASHRAM
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
