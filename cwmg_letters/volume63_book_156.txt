156. LETTER TO KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
July 7, 1936
Your statement contains a statement of eternal truth. But
your previous letter contained a definite complaint which you had a
perfect right to make. Indeed it was your duty to do so. Do you not
owe it to me [that you should tell me] who were the culprits and
what they had done? After all we approach eternal verities only
by tackling details in terms thereof. Anyway, whatever glimpses
I may have of truth I owe principally, if not entirely, to attention
to the minutest details, always keeping the supreme objective in
view. Do please therefore help.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
