148. LETTER TO BHAGWANLAL R. SHAH
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 7, 1945
CHI. BHAGWANLAL,
I have received your draft for Rs. 303 from Chi. Jaisukhlal.
I hope you are getting on well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHAH BHAGWANLAL RANCHHODDAS
MEHTA MILL STORES, KARACHI
C/O JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
SIND MARKET, MAHATMA GANDHI ROAD
KARACHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
