94. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Friday [March 8, 1929]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
You must be well by now. I want to be free of anxiety on
your account. That can be only when you become brave and
self-reliant.
I have reached Rangoon today and am disposing of the mail.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3335
