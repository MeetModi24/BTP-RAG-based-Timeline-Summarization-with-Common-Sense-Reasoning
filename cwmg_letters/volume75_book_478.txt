478. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 9, 1942
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
I have your letter. I had promptly replied to your earlier
letter. I hope you got it.
Mukerji is a good and honest worker. He has some land. I
had asked him. He said he did not seek charity. I have no doubt
he will return the entire amount. He was prepared even to pay
interest. We have given much aid to other workers. I am clearly
of the view that we should lend Rs. 3,000 to Bhai Mukerji for six
months.
Blessings from
BAPU
