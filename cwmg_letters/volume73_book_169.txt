169. LETTER TO VASUKAKA JOSHI
November 11, 1940
DEAR VASUKAKA,
Of course, I feel honoured to have your name on my list.
I know your energy is superior to mine and your love of the
country as great.
Will you give me the exact details of your
diet? How do you take the lemon juice—not undiluted?
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 7915
