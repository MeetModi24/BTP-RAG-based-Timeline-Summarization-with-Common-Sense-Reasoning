75. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI H. SHAH
June 19, 1932
You write well indeed. You have made the best use of your
life. One whose aim in life is service and who remains in that
state of mind till death may be said to have made the best use
of one's life. Narandas informs me that you fainted once again.
One day you will fall into eternal sleep as a result of such an
attack. Welcome it when it comes.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 233
