448. LETTER TO N. S. HARDIKER
SEGAON (WARDHA),
July 13, 1937
DEAR DR. HARDIKER,
My own opinion is that now that there will be a Con-
gress Ministry in the Bombay Presidency it may be well not to
publish anything. But in this, too, it will be better for you to
be guided by what Jawaharlal may say.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. N. S. HARDIKER
HUBLI
(KARNATAK)
From the original: N. S. Hardiker Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
