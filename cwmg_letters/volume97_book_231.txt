231. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
March 14, 1933
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I see from Prabhudas's letter that nobody is left with you now. Why is
176

that so? And what is to be done about it? I can understand your inducing
someone from the Ashram to join you. But how could anyone be sent there
by some other person ? Either train somebody from that place or attract
somebody from the Ashram or, if you can get anybody from Dhrangadhra,
send for him. All that I ask is that you should not feel helpless.
Blessings from
BAPU
