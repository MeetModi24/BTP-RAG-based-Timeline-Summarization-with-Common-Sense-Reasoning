95. LETTER TO SATCOWRIPATI ROY
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 2, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your telegram, but such news as you have given me
always travels fast. I got the news of Bhomble's death twenty-
four hours before your telegram. I telegraphed to Sudhir and
wrote also to Basanti Devi giving such comfort as was possible.
I hope you are keeping well and that things in Bengal are
shaping themselves right.
Yours sincerely,
BA[B]U SATCOWRIPATI ROY
BHAWANIPUR
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 19649
