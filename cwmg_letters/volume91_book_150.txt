150. LETTER TO G. CARMICHAEL
NADIAD,
April 19, 1918
DEAR MR. CARMICHAEL,
I thank you for your note. I will not make use of the
purport unless I get the true version. Will you please help me
if you think that I may allow public use to be made of the
interview? If however you think that I may not make use of
what was a purely friendly interview I shall entirely respect
your wish. I merely thought of making use of it as we discussed
large principles and found we had honest but insurmountable
difference of opinion.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
