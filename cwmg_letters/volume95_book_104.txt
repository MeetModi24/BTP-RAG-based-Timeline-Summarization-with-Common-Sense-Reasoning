104. LETTER TO RAMI GANDHI AND MANU GANDHI
[After July 26, 1930]
CHI. RAMI AND MANU,
It is not nice that both of you have written the letter as Bali Masi
dictated. I can understand if Manudi does not realize this. But what
should I say about Rami? I have not the faintest memory of having
turned back Rami from Amreli. Of course, I was not in favour of
giving more money for meeting Manilal. I had asked Manilal to spend
the money if he had something on him. But surely he did not have.
Kunvarji is a poor man and how can he be made to spend? And what
money can I have? After all, public money cannot be used indiscrimi-
nately. I would certainly wish that Rami should remember this much
teaching. Bali Masi is an elder, her ideas are great, her capacity is
great. You cannot imitate her.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Manu should have taken my permission before she had her nose
and ears pierced.
