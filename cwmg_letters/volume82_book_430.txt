430. LETTER TO BAL D. KALELKAR
CONTAI,
December 31, 1945
CHI. BAL,
I have your letter. It is worthy of you that you wish to
serve Kaka or me; however, at present your dharma is to keep
up what you are doing. If you do credit to what you have learnt
you will have rendered service to both of us.
We might
have to consider what your dharma would be if there had been
no one else to serve us; but all that is irrelevant in the present
context. For the time being your knowledge is to be utilized for
Birlaji's work. In the end you have to let the masses utilize
your knowledge without any thought of fame or fortune. I shall
certainly like you to come and stay with me for some time at
your own leisure.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. BAL KALELKAR
