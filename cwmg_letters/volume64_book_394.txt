394. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
February 20, 1937
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
I sent a few lines in the morning. I could have written
more had I detained Kumarappa's car which did not seem proper
and writing with the left hand is an extremely slow business.
Impatience will only delay the cure, it is possible only through
patience. The civil surgeon says that impurity in your blood
is not recent but of a long standing, which makes treatment
prolonged. What are you doing there? How do you pass
your time and what is the diet? A cheerful spirit is a great
contributing factor towards recovery. A student of the Gita
should be content whatever be his lot. This is said in Chapter
XII.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1895
