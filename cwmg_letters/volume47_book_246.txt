246. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
BARDOLI,
July 26, 1931
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
Your letter. I know it will be good if I can go to England.
But then the atmosphere here too should be conducive. At pre-
sent it is extremely adverse. I have written a letter in the nature
of an ultimatum to the Government and am awaiting the reply.
I cannot write much due to lack of time. But if you possibly can, do
come to Bombay soon. I shall reach there on August 4. We shall
see later whether or not you should stay in England in case I go.
BAPU
[PS.]
I shall be in Borsad on Tuesday.
From Hindi: C.W. 7893. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
