79. LETTER TO BASANT KUMAR CHATTERJEE
January 16, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter by registered post. Of late I have missed
the balanced judgment which characterized all your letters which
it was my pleasure to receive. The latest one in front of me is full
of hot spices, reckless statements and innuendoes. If your letter
had not betrayed anger, I would have pleaded with you and
shown you the mis-statements and misrepresentations with which
your letter bristles. Some day, and I am sure that day is not
very far, when passions have subsided, you will admit, if you have
patience enough to keep this letter, that what I have said in it
was all true.
Yours sincerely,
BASANT KUMAR CHATTERJEE
CALCUTTA
