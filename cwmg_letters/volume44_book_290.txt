290. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 7, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (DESAI),
I was able to see Pyarelal last week. They had permitted
very little time for the meeting. He had certainly become weak-
er, but is better now. He gets milk, etc. He is well looked after.
I hope that I shall be able to see him several times again.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1806
