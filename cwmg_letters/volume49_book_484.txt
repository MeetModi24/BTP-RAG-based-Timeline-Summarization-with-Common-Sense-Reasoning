484. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
May 3, 1932
DEAR AGATHA,
I have your letter of the 15th ultimo. Yes, Mahadev is with
us. You have in English the saying that two is company, three
is none. Somehow or other we three are belying that saying and
are getting on quite well. You must be feeling considerably re-
lieved now that you have Deenabandhu with you. Whether you
call Mahadev's little hope—or shall we say big hope?—Master
Desai or some other it is the same thing to him, because he is
utterly innocent of English. But the book you have sent I have
no doubt contains many pictures. These he will enjoy without
stopping to inquire how you have addressed him.
With love from us both.
BAPU
[PS.]
Love to Charlie.
From a photostat: G.N. 1453
