685. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
July 31, 1933
CHI. PREMA,
I do not get letters from you these days. Tell your father
that if Shri Aney goes to jail and he becomes the president and
if he accepts my views about dictators, he should abolish the
post of Dictator and those of Provincial Dictators.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C. W. 11041. Courtesy: Arjun Jairamdas
