348. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 27, 1939
CHI. VALJI,
It is very good indeed that you have decided to come here.
We shall see what is to be done. You must have received Rs. 25
sent for Chitre. I have asked Kanu to send today another Rs. 30.
I would like Chitre staying with you. I would then have to worry
less about you. If he was needed there, why won't he be needed
here? However, do whatever you and Chitre wish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7489. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
