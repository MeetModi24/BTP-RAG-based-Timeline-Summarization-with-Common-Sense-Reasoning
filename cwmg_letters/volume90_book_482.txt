482. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
NEW DELHI,
January 30, 1948
SHRI ANANDBHAIYA AND GANGIBEHN,
Bapu had your letter. Bapu is going to Sevagram but only for ten
days. So Bapu says that there is no need for Gangi to come. Yes, when
he goes there for a long stay she may come. . . .
Yours,
BISEN
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
