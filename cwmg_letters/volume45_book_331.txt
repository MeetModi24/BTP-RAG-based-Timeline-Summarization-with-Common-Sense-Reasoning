331. LETTER TO G. CUNNINGHAM
1 DARYAGANJ, DELHI,
March 6, 1931
DEAR MR. CUNNINGHAM,
I thank you for your two letters. Pray convey my thanks to
His Excellency for the prompt action he has taken on my letter of
the 4th instant regarding the sold lands.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 9337. Courtesy: India Office Library; also A.I.C.C.
File No. 16-C, 1931
