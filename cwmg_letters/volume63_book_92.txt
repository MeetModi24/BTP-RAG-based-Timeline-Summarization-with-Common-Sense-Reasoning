92. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 22, 1936
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
This is another batch of cuttings1, of more importance per-
haps to you than to me. My right hand is resting.
No more, since we meet soon.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1936. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
1 These are not traceable.
