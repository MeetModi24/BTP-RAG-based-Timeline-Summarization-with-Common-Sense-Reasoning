218. LETTER TO TARABEHN N. MASHRUWALA
SEVAGRAM,
November 23, 1940
CHI. TARA 2 ,
I vote for Sevagram. I have not accepted defeat. You
know my attachment. I have good facilities here to cure you.
I will send for you when you are ready.
I am ready and all the
arrangements made.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7530. Also C.W. 5006. Courtesy:
Kanubhai N. Mashruwala
