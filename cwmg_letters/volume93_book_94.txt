94. LETTER TO PYARELAL
WARDHA,
February 28, 1936
CHI. PYARELAL,
I heard all from Jamnalalji. I was much pained. You must
now wake up, get over the stupor and dedicate yourself to
work. Come over if you wish. Let me know your programme.
It may be as well if you come with me to Savli.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
