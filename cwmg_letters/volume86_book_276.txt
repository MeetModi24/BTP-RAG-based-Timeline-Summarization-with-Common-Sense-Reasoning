276. LETTER TO MANU S. MASHRUWALA
December 11, 1946
CHI. MANUDI,
I got your letter after many days. I think I have already
replied to it. But these days I cannot keep a complete list
for I am practically alone. You need not worry about me at
all. I am quite well. I do not make my own khakharis. I
made them only once. Parasuram is with me.
I hope all three of you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 2682. Courtesy: Manu S.
Mashruwala
