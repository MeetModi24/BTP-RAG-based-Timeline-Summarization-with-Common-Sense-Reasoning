294. LETTER TO R. R. BAKHALE
CAMP 1 DARYAGANJ, DELHI
February 23, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter enclosing six copies of your report. Having
had some minutes at my disposal, I have gone through the report
as carefully as was necessary and I have no hesitation in saying
that it is well written and it is free from exaggeration and decla-
mation. It is a report such as I would expect from anyone con-
nected with the Society.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI R. R. BAKHALE
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
BOMBAY-
