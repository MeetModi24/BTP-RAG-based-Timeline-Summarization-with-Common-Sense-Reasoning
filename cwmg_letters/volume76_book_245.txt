245. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
June 10, 1942
CHI. KAKA,
I got your note yesterday evening after prayer. I feel that
sending for Krishna Varma here will not help. It would be
better to give him fifteen days. That will pay. You will soon
be able to judge whether there was any improvement. If no
improvement is observed, you need not stay. But it does appear
that you will not be able to judge before fifteen days. The
earlier you go the better. Go today if you can. You will have
returned by the time Shriman arrives.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
A letter for Varma is enclosed.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10959
