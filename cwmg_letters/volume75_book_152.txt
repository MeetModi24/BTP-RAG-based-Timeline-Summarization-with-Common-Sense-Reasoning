152. LETTER TO SAM HIGGINBOTTOM
November 15, 1941
DEAR PROFESSOR,
I am in need. I want a good, sound man who can guide Seth
Jamnalal in the matter of cows and dairying. He must be an ex-
pert. Sethji has taken up cow-protection as his life's mission. He has

need of an adviser to guide him. I have advised him to correspond
with you in such matters and assured him that you will give him
guidance.
I need, too, one who can be director of an agricultural and
dairying institute. He can get a good salary. If you have one in
view, will you please put me in touch with him?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PROF. SAM HIGGINBOTTOM
AGRICULTURAL INSTITUTE
NAINI, ALLAHABAD
