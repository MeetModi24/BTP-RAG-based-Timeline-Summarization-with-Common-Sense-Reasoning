79. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
November 25, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Did I not say in my first letter that I
am not sure of attending the Congress this year for the simple
reason that I do not know whether I can [be] of any service? At
the same time, I shall not certainly avoid going there if my ab-
sence is at all likely to be misunderstood. But I shall consult
friends when this wretched election fever has subsided.
My attitude towards Hindu-Muslim unity is a fixed thing. I
want it without the slightest bargain and I shall strain every nerve
to secure it.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 12087
