135. LETTER TO BARNES
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 8, 1906
MY DEAR BARNES,
I wonder if you are still in Victoria Street. If you are, please let
me know, and I shall look you up. I am here only for a very short
time. My regards to all, if you receive this letter.
Yours sincerely,
BARNES, ESQ.
C/O MESSRS TROUTBECK & BARNES
SOLICITORS
VICTORIA STREET, S.W.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4520
