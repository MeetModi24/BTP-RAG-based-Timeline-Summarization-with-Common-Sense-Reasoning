225. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
July 13, 1938
CHI. LILA,
I got your letter. You have not at all forfeited your right to
come to me. You may certainly come whenever you are free.
I will be perfectly content if you can preserve peace of mind
there. Ultimately of course you will have to come here. Build
up your health there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9589. Also C.W. 6561. Court-
esy: Lilavati Asar
