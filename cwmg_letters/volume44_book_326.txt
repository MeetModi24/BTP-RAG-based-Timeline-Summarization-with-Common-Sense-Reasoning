326. LETTER TO DURGA GIRI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 17, 1930
CHI. DURGA,
I am not satisfied with your letter so far. Tell me your daily
routine. When you start writing regularly, you may merit a dif-
ferent adjective. Your handwriting is good this time. I was glad
to see Maitri's handwriting at the end of your letter. I shall
await her full letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Viral Vatsalata, p. 32
