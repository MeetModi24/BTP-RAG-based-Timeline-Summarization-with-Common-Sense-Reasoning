218. LETTER TO CHANCHALDAS
October 14, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
You sent me a telegram about the murder of Maharaj Nathu-
ram. Though it had cut me to the quick, I have not contem-
plated any fast. Nevertheless I was looking forward to the full
details promised in your telegram. The telegram is dated the 7th
instant. I have not heard from you as yet.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI CHANCHALDAS
PRESIDENT, ARYA SAMAJ
HYDERABAD (SIND)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
