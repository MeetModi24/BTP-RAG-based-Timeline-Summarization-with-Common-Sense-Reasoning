315. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 11, 1940
CHI. PRABHA,
How lazy you are! And you want an immediate reply from
me! If you have no work in Patna for the time being and if
Jayaprakash also wishes it, then take Rajen Babu's permission
and come here and improve your health. What prevents you
from coming? If you are not going to Hazaribagh either, we shall
plan about the future if you come.
Sushila is working in her own college Hospital in Delhi at
present.
Vijaya's father has passed away. She will perhaps come
here for a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
