814. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
WARDHA,
December 17, 1934
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
Is it not time you gave your decision about yourself and
Jagannath? I want the names of the others whom you would
recommend as agents.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
VACHWADI
LAHORE
From the original: Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
