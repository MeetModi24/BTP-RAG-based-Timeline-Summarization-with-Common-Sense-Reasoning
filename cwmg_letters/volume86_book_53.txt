53. LETTER TO P. G. MATHEW
October 26, 1946
BHAI MATHEW,
Rajkumari is at Simla. She is giving herself a rest. No
letters are sent there. Why grieve over the passing of your
mother? She has been freed from pain. I am going to Bengal.
Blessings from
BAPU
PROF. P. G. MATHEW
SACRED HEART'S COLLEGE
THEVARA
ERNAKULAM
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 1546
