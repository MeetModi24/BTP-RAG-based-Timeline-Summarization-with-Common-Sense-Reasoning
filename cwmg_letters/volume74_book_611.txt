611. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH
October 2, 1941
BHAI PRITHVI SINGH,
Your letter. Ahimsa was born along with man. Hitler too does
not kill his own people. This is ahimsa though in a very limited
measure. As ahimsa is the very nature of the atman, either man comes
to it out of weariness or accepts it willingly, as we are trying to do.
