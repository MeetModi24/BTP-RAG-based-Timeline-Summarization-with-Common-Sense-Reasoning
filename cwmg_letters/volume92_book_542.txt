542. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
Y. M.,
July 2, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
I am very glad that you have gone to Quetta. Derive the
fullest benefit from the air there. Walk as much as you can.
Describe to me the scenery and the climate of the place. Who
are the settlers there?
What you write is absolutely correct. An honest heart is a
happy heart. May your happiness grow and stabilize.
Give me any news you may have of Anand. How do you
spend your time there?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
