227. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
WARDHA,
September 22, 1939
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
See the telegram about Rajkot. Please forward it. I think
you should stay here, so that your burden may be lightened
and we may be able to meet every day and exchange views.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
BIRLA HOUSE
5 ALBUQUERQUE ROAD, NEW DELHI
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 235
