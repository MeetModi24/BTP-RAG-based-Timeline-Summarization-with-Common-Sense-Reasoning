192. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
SEVAGRAM,
July 31, 1942
BHAI GOPICHAND,
I have only now been able to reach your letter of July 24. The
case is sad. Your reply is very good. But who will listen ? There is
nothing for me to do in the matter, is there? And what after all can
one do ? Please reassure Dukhiya's relatives on my behalf.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
C/O THE POSTMASTER, BATOTE
(JAMMU & KASHMIR STATE)
From the Hindi original: Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
