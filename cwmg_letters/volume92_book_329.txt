329. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
Y.M.,
July 12, 1930
CHI. KANTA,
I have your letter. Only the woman who controls her desires
and finds joy only in doing her duty can truly serve. There is
always a conflict going on in our minds between the good and
the desirable. To be truly human one must choose the good and
find it desirable. We are not always certain what should be
regarded as good. Hence, in the course of one's striving it is
necessary to have faith in one's superiors and in the received
word and to act accordingly. Of course, it will always be well
with you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
What is Brother doing? Give him my blessings.
