85. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
NASIK,
February 16, 1927
CHI. MIRA,
I had first thought of asking the Khadi Pratishthan people to
debit the Ashram with the price of the travelling charkha that
will be sent to you. But I reconsidered my decision and I saw
that it would be cheaper to have the charkha by value payable
parcel. If therefore you receive a parcel from Calcutta by value
payable post, please discharge the bill there.
On the 6th and 7th of March, I shall be at Akola for Mani-
lal's marriage. From the 8th to 14th March I am at the Ashram,
15th to 17th Bardoli. I entrain at Surat on the evening of the
17th for the Gurukul.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
Better a dictated letter than none.
SHRIMATI MIRABEHN
GURUKUL KANGRI
From the original: C.W. 5205. Courtesy: Mirabehn.
