251. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Silence Day [August 27, 1928]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. Pay up the bills of the doctor and the
hakim. You should know from time to time the amounts as they
become due. If your health does not improve there at all, it would
be better for you to come away here. Tell Vidyavatiji that, rather
than stay there and be a burden on her, you had better return to
the Ashram. After your health has completely recovered, you
may return there if necessary. Or, they may send a student from
there who will learn the work in the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9251
