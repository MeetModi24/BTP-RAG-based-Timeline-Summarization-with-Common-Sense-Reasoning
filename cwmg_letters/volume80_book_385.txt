385. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
[MAHABALESHWAR] 1,
May 31, 1945
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I received your letter just now. Tell me everything when
you meet me. I have written to Tandonji about my intention
to leave the Sammelan. I have already written to Ramdas. He
should get well now. I note what you say about Mr. Sen. I
know he can render very good service and he is service-minded.
Have as much experience as you can have there. We shall go
to Panchgani today.
Blessings to all of you from
BAPU
SHRI AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
C/O SMT. YASHODHARA DASAPPA
VONTICOPPAL, MYSORE
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
