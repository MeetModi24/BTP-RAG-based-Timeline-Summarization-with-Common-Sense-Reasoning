375. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
NEW DELHI,
September 12, 1946
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. I am inclined to think that the cost of
the stamp was borne by M[unshi]. He is a lawyer of the first
rank and at present he earns maybe 15 to 20 thousand rupees
a month. Be that as it may, he is of a generous nature.
Perhaps Gadodiaji will remit the cost of the stamp. I have
not met him. You are at liberty to feel that the award is
against you. But I do not feel so. I had written to you that
all that should have been in your petition was not there.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, pp. 368-
