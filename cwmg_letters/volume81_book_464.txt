464. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
September 17, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
I hope you and Gajraj are well. I am glad you are doing
good work. Does Gajraj know how to write? He should do
every day some writing, reading and spinning. And whatever he
does, he should do well.
Blessings from
BAPU
ASHRAM SEVAGRAM
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
