641. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
June 28, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I got your wire. Hari-ichchha had been a daughter to me.
But why need we grieve over her death? Her soul is certainly
living. Let nobody, therefore, grieve.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8445. Also C.W. 5586. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
