309. LETTER TO DUNICHAND
SEVAGRAM,
November 10, 1944
DEAR
FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of 31st ult. and desires me to thank you for the same.
He has decided nothing about the fast. He has only taken the public into
confidence as to the working of his mind.
Could you kindly send the address of Maulvi Abdul Ghani though I doubt
whether anything effective can be done from this end ?
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI DUNICHAND
7-A BEGUM ROAD
LAHORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
