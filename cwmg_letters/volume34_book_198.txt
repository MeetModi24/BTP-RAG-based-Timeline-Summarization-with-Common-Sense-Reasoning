198. LETTER TO SUNDER LAL MATHUR
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 24, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Any clean earth, black or red, but clay
will do. The mud poultice should be taken at least for seven
days continuously and if it does good, you may continue it till
you feel that you have had enough. For dyspepsia, a complete
fast with copious draughts of water is a fine thing even while
earth bandages continue. And if you have the fast, you should
clean the bowels once a day by means of enema.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SUNDER LAL MATHUR, B.A., LL.B.
Vakil
PIRGHAIB
MORADABAD (U.P.)
From a photostat: S.N. 14197
