136. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI H. SHAH
July 1, 1932
DEAR PUNJABHAI,
Received your letter. Go on renouncing the liking for the
body so that when it falls, you will experience complete peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. II, p. 59
