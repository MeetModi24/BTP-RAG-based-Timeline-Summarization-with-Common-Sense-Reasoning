464. LETTER TO KARNAD SADASHIVA RAO
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 24, 1929
MY DEAR SADASHIVA RAO,
Here is the report about the closing of Nileshwar centre. Sjt.
Chhotelal himself agrees that it should be closed.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1 in 5 sheets
SJT. KARNAD SADASHIVA RAO
KODAILBAIL, MANGALORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 14898
