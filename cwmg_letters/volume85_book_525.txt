525. LETTER TO PANDURANG PATWARDHAN
NEW DELHI,
October 2, 1946
BHAI PANDURANG,
I have your letter. I do hope that Appasaheb will recover.
You did well in writing to me. I shall be more careful. Keep
writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PANDURANG PURUSHOTTAM PATWARDHAN
SAHITYA MANDIR
RATNAGIRI
