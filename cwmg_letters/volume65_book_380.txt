380. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
June 25, 1937
MY DEAR REBEL,
I return Sultan Ahmed's letter. It is good. I do not
think it is possible to change the name of the Sammelan . I
got the definition put right with difficulty. It is the spirit that
needs to be changed and not the name which is ancient. I hope
you will not commit yourself to anything that may embarrass you
or the officials of the Sammelan. Have you read any of the
annual addresses of the Sammelan Presidents? Do, if you get the
time.
No more today as this is being written just after the morning
prayer for Parnerker to take it to Wardha.
Love.
ROBBER
From the original: C.W. 3791. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6947
