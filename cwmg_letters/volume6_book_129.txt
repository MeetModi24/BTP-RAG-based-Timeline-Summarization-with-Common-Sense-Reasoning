129. LETTER TO S. HOLLICK
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 8, 1906
DEAR MR. HOLLICK,
Many thanks for your note. I am sorry, you were ill yesterday.
I enclose herewith Memorial for Lord Elgin. If you think that any
change is necessary, you may make it and I will have the Memorial
re-typed: otherwise it can be circulated as an original copy.
Yours sincerely,
Enclosure
S. HOLLICK, ESQ.
62, LONDON WALL, E.C.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4526
