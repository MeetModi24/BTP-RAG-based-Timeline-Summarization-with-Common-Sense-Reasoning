182. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
TITHAL, BULSAR,
May 21, 1937
CHI. ANAND,
At last I have a letter from you in Hindi. Your writing is
good. It can be improved.
I feel sorry for Vidya. But we have to be patient in every-
thing. I have sent you a telegram. Do take Vidya to a hill-
station.
What is the matter with Jairamdas? Why does he not write?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: C. W. 11066. Courtesy: Anand T.
Hingorani
