721. LETTER TO SAMPURNANAND
SIMLA,
July 14, 1945
BHAI SAMPURNANAND,
I have your letter. You have not given enough thought
to the matter. If khadi workers are pure, khadi work will not
become static. Today we have very little khadi. Make a
thorough study of the theory and practice of khadi.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SAMPURNANAND
JALPADEVI
KASHI
BANARAS
