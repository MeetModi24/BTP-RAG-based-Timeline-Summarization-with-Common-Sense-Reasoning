198. LETTER TO LILAVATI P. ASAR
NEW DELHI,
July 10, 1947
CHI. LILA,
I have read your letter to Manu. You are not stopping your
foolishness. Why should you feel remorse about knowing? Why
should one remember words of flattery? How many are there as
fortunate as you? You must complete your studies properly. We can
analyse later whether or not there has been any benefit.
I am doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 This was in reply to the addressee's letter dated July 5, 1947.
