153. LETTER TO DEPUTY COMMISSIONER, WARDHA
January 16, 1941
Shri Vinoba Bhave, who was discharged yesterday from the
Central Jail, Nagpur will offer Civil Disobedience again from
Sevagram Chowk at 9 A.M. tomorrow by addressing a meeting on
the duty of carrying on anti-war propaganda within the limits pre-
scribed by the law of non-violence as I understand it.
India Office Library and Records: IOR R/3/1/341, p.12
