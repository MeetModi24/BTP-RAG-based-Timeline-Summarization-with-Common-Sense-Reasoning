176. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
November 30, 1935
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
How much do you spend daily on vegetables? How much
on fruit? What is the expenditure on food per head per month
at the present rate of quantity served? Do you keep an ac-
count of the quantity consumed daily? Do you note down the
names of those who come for meals from day to day? How
are you keeping? In what other activities besides this are
you engaged?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 383. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
