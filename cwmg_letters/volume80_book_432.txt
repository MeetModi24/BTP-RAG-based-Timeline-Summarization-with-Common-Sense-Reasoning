432. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM PATEL
PANCHGANI,
June 4, 1945
BHAI PURUSHOTTAM PATEL,
I received your letter today. Why is it in English? You should
not involve me in your candidature. Do whatever you can on
your own. After all you are staying there.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
