539. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
October 2, 1945
CHI. BABUDI,
I was very pleased to see your letter. I had received one
from Gordhandas earlier. All of you seem to have stood the
heavy rains well enough. Keep Shakaribehn there till you have
fully recovered.
Come to Sevagram after I return and settle down there
for some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10061.
Courtesy: Sharda G.
Chokhawala
