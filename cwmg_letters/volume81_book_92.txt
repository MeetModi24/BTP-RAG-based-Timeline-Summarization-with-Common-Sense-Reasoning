92. LETTER TO DEVRAJ
July 30, 1945
BHAI DEVRAJ,
I can do something for your brother only after he puts in
an application. To do anything before that is impossible. My
stay here is uncertain. Write to me about your coming after
I settle down.
Blessings from
BAPU
LAJPAT BHAVAN
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
