163. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHARMA
February 15, 1934
BHAI SHARMA,
I have your telegram. I am writing this in a moving train.
There does not seem to be any hitch now in your leaving
for Wardha.
Amtussalaam has well and truly fallen sick. I shall meet her
on Monday.
Could you possibly come to Madras before going
to Wardha if she has not recovered by then? Send me a wire if
you can come. I do not wish to inconvenience you for nothing.
She has an amazing faith in you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
