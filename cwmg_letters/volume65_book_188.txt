188. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
TITHAL, BULSAR,
May 14, 1937
DEAR MOTI BABU,
Arun Chandra Dutt has my blessings and so his partner. I
assume that this celibacy means freedom from every form of
sexual contact, mental or physical, and that they are husband
and wife only in name and are truly partners in service.
I hope you are keeping fit.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 11049
