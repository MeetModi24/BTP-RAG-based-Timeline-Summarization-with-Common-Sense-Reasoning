89. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
November 25, 1936
CHI. AMALA,
You have not given your address. So don't blame me if
you get this late. I had your previous letter but I had no time.
I do hope your mother and your pet have come. I do
hope you won't have to shift from your present quarters.
Your earnings are good. You must not starve yourself. Take
plenty of milk, fruit and greens.
All's well here.
Love.
Blessings from
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
