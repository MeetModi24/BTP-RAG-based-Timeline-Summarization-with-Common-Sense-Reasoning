9. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
March 17, 1937
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have written to Ba, but it is you who will have to prepare
the thing. You will have always to give to Kanu the bread
which he will take with him and eat in the evening. If it is
necessary to prepare something else also, do it yourself. Keep
an account of each minute of yours. If possible, form the habit
of getting up at 4 o'clock without fail. Then in the afternoon, you
must have an hour's nap. Attend to your studies carefully.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9584. Also C.W. 6556. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
