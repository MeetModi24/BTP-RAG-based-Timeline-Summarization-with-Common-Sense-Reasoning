54. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 16, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
I am enclosing Prema's letter. Keshu has sent me your
letter to him. Do you believe that Lakshmidas is unjust to
him?
Can Keshu acquire a patent in his individual capacity?
Isn't what Lakshmidas writes of Arjun correct? Is Lakshmidas
jealous of you or Keshu? Is he partial? Which are the special
points about Keshu's machine? Has he stolen any points from
the machine which Lakshmidas has made? I have come to
believe that Keshu is prejudiced against Lakshmidas.
Please throw whatever light you can on the matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
