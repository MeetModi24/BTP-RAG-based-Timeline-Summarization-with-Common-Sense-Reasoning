372. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
[NADIAD,]
July 29, 1918
DEAR MR. SHASTRIAR,
Thakor has just arrived. He tells me you were again down
with illness. You need a cruel doctor who would mercilessly order
a complete fast and water treatment. But you can expect nothing
but licensed murders from that most empirical of professions. When-
ever I hear of your illness, I feel like shooting some doctor or other
but my ahimsa comes in the way. Happily for you and India I
have no parliamentary ambition. Otherwise I should introduce a
Bill disqualifying people getting continuously ill from membership.
Here is a cable 1 from Polak. I cannot understand its full
significance. But I suppose there is no danger of the scheme be-
ing rejected by the country.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
