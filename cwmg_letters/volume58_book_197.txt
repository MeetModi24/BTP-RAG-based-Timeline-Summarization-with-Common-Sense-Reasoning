197. LETTER TO UTTAMCHAND SHAH
July 13, 1934
CHI. UTTAMCHAND,
I got your letter. Just now I don't feel like criticizing you or
other co-workers for what you or they do. Maybe through the pres-
ent separation itself the sincere ones will come together. Anybody
who earns an honest pie will certainly be doing service thus. Please
remain where you are without feeling any regret and see that both
of you improve your health. When the time comes, you may
return. Why do you worry about my fast?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9442
