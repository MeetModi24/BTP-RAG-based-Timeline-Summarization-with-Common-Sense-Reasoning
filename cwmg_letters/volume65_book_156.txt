156. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
May 6, 1937
MY DEAR C. R.,
I have gone through your statement. It is quite good and
effective. We might let the thing ripen. I have sent a state-
ment for publication. You will see it if it is published. I hope
you are organizing constructive work. I hope you are keeping
well.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2062
