161. LETTER TO SARALADEVI CHOWDHARANI
July 5, 1932
DEAR SISTER,
I was grieved to note from the papers that your mother was
no more. My sympathies go out to you and Deepak in your
sorrow. Sardar and Mahadev join me in sending you condo-
lences.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SMT. SARALADEVI CHOWDHARANI 2
CALCUTTA
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. II, p. 159
