282. LETTER TO VIJAYA M. PANCHOLI
January 7, 1942
CHI. VIJAYA,
I have your postcard. I see that you will not be able to recover
completely. Why does Nanabhai fall ill so often? Would he not
come to Sevagram for a few days?
Vasumati has reached Sevagram. We leave the day after
tomorrow. Ba is expected to return from Maroli today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7144. Also C.W. 4636. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
