366. LETTER TO THE SECRETARY,
INDIAN MERCHANTS' CHAMBER
As AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
THE SECRETARY
INDIAN MERCHANTS' CHAMBER
BOMBAY
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for your letter of the 19th ultimo advising me that
I was unanimously elected an honorary member of your Chamber.
Please convey my thanks to the Chamber for conferring on me
this honour.
Yours faithfully,
From a photostat: S.N. 17201
