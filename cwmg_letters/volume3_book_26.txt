26. LETTER TO COLONIAL SECRETARY, NATAL
14 MERCURY LANE,
DURBAN,
February 20, 1899
TO
THE HONOURABLE THE COLONIAL SECRETARY
PIETERMARITZBURG
SIR,
Messrs Amod Suliman, Ismail Mahomed Khota and Issa
Hajee Soomar intend to visit the Transvaal. The first two hold
return tickets, having come from the Transvaal on business. The
last one has a large business in Standerton and he intends to go
to that place to inspect his business. The first two are connected
with a business in Heidelberg.
I shall be obliged if you will be good enough to secure
for these gentlemen permits enabling them to go to the
Transvaal.
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
M. K. GANDHI
Pietermaritzburg Archives : C.S.O. 1584/99
