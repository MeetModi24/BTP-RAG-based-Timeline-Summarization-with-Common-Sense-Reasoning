405. LETTER TO METROPOLITAN OF CALCUTTA
SEVAGRAM,
November 14, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Many thanks for your letter just received. I wonder if you
saw my statement. If the fast comes, it will have nothing to do
with the Government. For ready reference I send you a copy of
my statement.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
METROPOLITAN
BISHOP'S HOUSE
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
