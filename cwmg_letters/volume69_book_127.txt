127. LETTER TO GOPINATH BARDOLOI
NEW DELHI,
April 3, 1939
DEAR BARDOLOI1,
You are embarking on a great experiment perhaps the
greatest for Assam. You have my prayers for the success of
your experiment. If Assam can really get rid of the opium habit,
the face of its people will be changed. But before that happens
you will have to add to prohibition much constructive effort.
You will require skilled medical assistance to enable you to find
out a substitute for opium which will kill the craving and rebuild
the shattered bodies of the opium-eaters. May you be blessed
with all the strength and wisdom you would need.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal. Also The Statesman,
24-4-1939
