558. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
Y. M.,
August 21, 1932
CHI. NIMU,
If the children are being bitten by mosquitoes, rubbing a
little kerosene on the body would keep the mosquitoes away.
This has been the experience with many.
You must have started taking ghee and oil now.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Nirmala Gandhi Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
