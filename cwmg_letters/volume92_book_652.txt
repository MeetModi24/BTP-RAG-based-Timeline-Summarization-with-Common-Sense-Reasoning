652. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
February 17, 1933
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. You will see from Harijan that I do not
see any particular need to go to Panditji now. It is all to the
good that the secret is out.
I can meet Ambedkar any number of times. But nothing can
be done against his will. His nature also should be understood.
In most cases, there is no cause to get frightened by
children's fever.
You must get rid of your cold. Coughing is also the result
of cold, is it not? Come whenever you feel like it except on
Wednesday and Thursday.
Send your comments on Harijan.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
53 MINT ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
