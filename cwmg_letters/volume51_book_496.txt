496. LETTER TO RAMNATH 'SUMAN'
October 26, 1932
Individual prayer alone can be the basis of congregational
prayer. My emphasis on the latter does not at all mean that I attach
greater importance to it. Since we are not used to congregational
prayer, I have attempted to show the need for it. What you can expe-
rience in seclusion is certainly difficult, if not impossible, to experience
in a group. I have also noticed that some people cannot pray except
in a group. For such people individual prayer is essential. I would
also admit that one can do without congregational prayer but
certainly not without individual prayer.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 178
