321. LETTER TO R. V. PATWARDHAN
December 20, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I can only say in reply to one
part of it that I have signed letters asking me to anticipate the
2nd and thus end my earthly life. I do not mind these letters.
I merely state the fact.
For the rest of your letter, I can only give you my assurance
that behind every step I take, and every word that I utter, there
is great and prayerful deliberation. I ask sanatani friends to give
me the same liberty of thought and action as they claim for them-
selves. Without such mutual toleration, no reform is possible.
I may also tell you that I am leaving no stone unturned
to reach some workable compromise. But I must regretfully confess
that I have failed hitherto.
Yours sincerely,
