231. LETTER TO VIDYAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 13, 1940
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. It is God's grace that Chi. Virendra has
recovered.
You have shown much patience.
Blessings from
BAPU
RANI VIDYAVATIJI
KOROKALA
BENIGUNJ
HARDOI, U.P.
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi National
