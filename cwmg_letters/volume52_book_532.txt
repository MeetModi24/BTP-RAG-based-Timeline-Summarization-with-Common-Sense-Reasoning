532. LETTER TO G. V. MAVALANKAR
January 8, 1933
BHAISHRI MAVALANKAR,
I got both your letters. I certainly did not want you to spend
so much of your time in replying. I had merely forwarded to you
the letter which I had received. But your letter has certainly
thrown more light on the problem.
I have nothing to write about the Chharas. Inform me by and
by whether the step you have taken proves sufficient.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1235
