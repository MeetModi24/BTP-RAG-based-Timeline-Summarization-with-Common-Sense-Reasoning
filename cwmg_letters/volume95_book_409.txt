409. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
CHI. MUNNALAL,
You have given a good summary. It can be improved upon. But
for the time being it will do for you. Do not be impetuous or
impatient. Lethargy developed over the years cannot go in a moment.
The lethargy primarily was from my side in overlooking so many
things. I intend not doing so any more.
Rest when I receive your next [summary]. It is possible to solve
Dhirendra's problem without bothering Jajuji.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8374
