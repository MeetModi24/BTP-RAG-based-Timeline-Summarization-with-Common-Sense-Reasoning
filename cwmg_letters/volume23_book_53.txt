53. LETTER TO KISHORELAL MASHRUWALA
SABARMATI JAIL,
Friday [March 17, 1922]
BHAI SHREE KISHORELAL,
I have always longed to see you. It would have been enough
to have met you. Well, now your letter will do. You did the
right thing in giving up the idea of coming over to see me. Your
coming would not have served any special purpose; besides, the
interruption in your practice on account of it would have been an
obvious loss.
Since your effort is sincere, it will certainly bear fruit. No
honest endeavour ever goes in vain.
Sentence has not yet been passed on me. Possibly it will be
known only tomorrow. At present I am an under-trial prisoner,
and perfectly at peace. Shankerlal Banker, too, is with me.
My blessings are ever with you. Do not be in a hurry to leave
the place. But you may certainly leave it when your inner voice
tells you to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Shreyarthini Sadhana, pp. 139-40
