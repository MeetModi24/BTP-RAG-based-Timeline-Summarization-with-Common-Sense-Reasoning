130. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
BOMBAY,
May 11, 1942
It was a pity I could not see you, though I passed through
Nasik But such is my condition. I am now arranging to send
someone there. I read your letter. I had thought that you
had already crossed the Sahara, but it seems you have not.
Who knows how many deserts you will still have to cross. Do
not lose heart. Many others like you have risen from the sick-
bed. Am I also not one such? I am not, therefore, giving up
hope. The only thing which makes me unhappy is that I cannot
keep you with me at this time. But why should I be unhappy
even for that reason ? You have given a great deal, and if God
wills, you will give more.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 182
