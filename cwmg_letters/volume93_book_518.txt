518. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
[June 11, 1940]
I am convinced that your good lies in your joining college.
God will provide the funds for completing the medical course.
Otherwise neither you nor I shall find any peace of mind.
You do have that capability and you have the will. There is
certainly nothing wrong about it. Hence, you should start
tomorrow. Ba's trouble is only temporary.
From a copy of the Gujarati: C. W. 11285
