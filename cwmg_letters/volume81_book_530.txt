530. LETTER TO KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
October 1, 1945
VAHALAN BEHN 3 ,
I understand your letter. Of course you must go where your
inner voice takes you. You will give a good account of yourself
wherever you are. But I would like you to settle down some day
to some definite work. That is a characteristic of dordi .
Nargisbehn saw your letter. She comes often.
Love.
SHRI KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
ORIENT CLUB, CHOWPATI
BOMBAY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
