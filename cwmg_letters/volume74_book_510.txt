510. LETTER TO SARASWATI GANDHI
September 11, 1941
CHI. SURU1,
I have your letter. May you live long, and be a pure
worker. These are Ba's blessings and mine to you. There is
always some trouble with the body, that has to be borne. We
shall meet some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. SURU
[C/O] SHRI KANTI GANDHI
DEVRAJ MOHALLA
MYSORE 2
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6181. Also C.W. 3455. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
