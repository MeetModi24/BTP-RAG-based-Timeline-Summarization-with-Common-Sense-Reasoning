405. LETTER TO S. S. PANDE
November 16, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Scheme or no scheme, if you have under-
stood the seriousness of the resolution and appreciate the method
of approach to the work, I would like you to tackle the four
things that are already before the public: pounding of rice by the
hand, grinding of flour through the village chakkis purification
and popularization of gur and restoration of better processes of
tanning of hides in the villages.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI S. S. PANDE, B.A.,LL.B., PLEADER
KHANDWA (C. P.)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
