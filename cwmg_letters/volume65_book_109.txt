109. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
KUMRI ASHRAM, BELGAUM DISTRICT,
April 19, 1937
CHI. MIRA,
I hope you got my letter[s] from Poona and Hudli. From
the night of 17th we have been having continuous rain. The
camp was not constructed for rains. We have been obliged to
shift where there is cover. Therefore we are overcrowded. And
yet there is no sign of rain stopping.
It is a pity Munnalal should have fever again. I hope you
are having the papers and the post regularly.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
The wedding went off well.
From the original: C.W. 6378. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9844
