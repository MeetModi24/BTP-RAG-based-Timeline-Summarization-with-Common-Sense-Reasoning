423. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
October 16, 1932
I do not consider that your total expenditure of Rs. 125
for three months is excessive. I wished to know the details be-
cause they tell me a number of things. Everyone must keep an
account of every pie spent, even though the money might be
one's own. For the truth is that nothing in this world is our own
but all belongs to God, as we realize from experience every day.
Hence, we should not be attached to whatever we enjoy or
spend. A person who does so will gladly keep an account of
every pie for his own satisfaction. If you have not kept a de-
tailed account of the expenditure of Rs. 125 in this manner, you
should from now on form the habit of keeping such account.
I remember that Devbhabhi used to keep a full account of her
expenses mentally.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 141
