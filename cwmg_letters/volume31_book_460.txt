460. LETTER TO NARAHARI PARIKH
Saturday, September 18, 1926
BHAISHRI NARAHARI,
You have not asked for a reply to your letter, but I wished
to write a long one. In my view the step which you have taken
is correct. I understand Nanabhai's point of view, but to me
wearing khadi and spinning are as essential a part of education as
learning Gujarati. This is not being a missionary. I shall be
patient in regard to your decision. Sometimes problems solve
themselves.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI N. D. PARIKH
LOKAMANYA R. V. MANDIR
SURAT
