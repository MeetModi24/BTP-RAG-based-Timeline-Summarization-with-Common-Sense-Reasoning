712. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
October 20, 1945
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I have the copy of the letter you have written to Rajkumari.
Today she must have reached Delhi from Simla and will fly to
London tomorrow or the day after. You did well in writing to
her. Why keep it in your heart? Since you have written so
frankly, I also have an opportunity of saying something. Your
sense of detachment should be sufficiently strong. Explain your
doubts to Prafulla Babu and listen to what he says and if he
disagrees, accept his advice. Haven't I made it clear in the
letter that I have sent through Sudhir that so long as you all
do not decide unanimously and not by majority, it should not
be considered as finally decided? Still, I shall go there and
decide which places I shall visit and which I may not. I have
stopped anything being sent to the newspapers. I think this
solves all the problems. How many people can be accommodated
in Sodepur?
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
SODEPUR
