64. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 29, 1945
DR. ICE,
I have your letter. One can stay for a month in the Birla
Dharmashala. I can't say anything about Wardha. In Sevagram
a hut can be built. I will be more than happy, if Hari-ichchha
is cured. Arrangements for ice can be made. The leper has to
be kept somewhere in the village. It is difficult to keep him in
the Ashram. My advice is that in this connection you should
do whatever Manoharji says. He has devoted his whole life to
this work and therefore we must have his permission.
Blessings from
BAPU
