420. LETTER TO PREMA KANTAK
October 28, 1947
CHI. PREMA,
I got your letter. Come when you can and spend three or
four days with me. We shall then talk sentiment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 10458. Also C. W. 6896. Cour-
tesy: Prema Kantak
1& The superscription and subscription are in Gujarati.
