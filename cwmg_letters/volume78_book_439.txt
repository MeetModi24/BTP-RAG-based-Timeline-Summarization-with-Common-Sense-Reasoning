439. LETTER TO GOPINATH BARDOLOI
SEVAGRAM,
November 20, 1944
DEAR BARDOLOI1,
I have your letter with enclosures. Concentrate on producing
a solid scheme for villages and the names and qualifications of
those who would work it. If you can do that there might be no
difficulty about funds even if you exceed your allotment. But you
must be prepared for a stiff test. Don't take a full bite at once
and face disaster.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI GOPINATH BARDOLOI
GAUHATI
ASSAM
From a photostat: C.W. 10493. Courtesy: Omeyo Kumar Das
