359. LETTER TO BAPUBHAI N. VASHI
POONA,
March 22, 1946
BHAI BAPUBHAI,
How does it happen that you have contracted tuberculosis?
If you could repeat Ramanama from the depth of your heart, it
could prove an unfailing remedy for tuberculosis. The others who
do not observe the rules of nature do not repeat Ramanama but
rather bring a bad name to it.
Blessings from
BAPU
BAPUBHAI NARANJI VASHI
UMER MANZIL
GHODBUNDER ROAD
KHAR
