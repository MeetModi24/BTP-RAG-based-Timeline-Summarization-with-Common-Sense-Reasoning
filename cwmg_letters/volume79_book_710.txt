710. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 22, 1945
BHAI AMRITA LAL,
I forgot to mention one thing in my letter yesterday. I am
aware of most of the complaints in Manu Bhimani's letter. There
is no substance in them. There are other things too. If she
can send me some convincing proof I am willing to investigate.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 10401. Courtesy: Amrita Lal
Chatterjee
