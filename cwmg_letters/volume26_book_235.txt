235. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Chaitra Sud 3 [March 28, 1925]
Why do you say that my report has become lame ? What
is it that I have left out? Did you not see my first one? But
even if I seem to be obtuse, I shall be satisfied if all of you who
have understood the position will retain the spirit of justice and
non-violence while remaining firm in your convictions.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 76
