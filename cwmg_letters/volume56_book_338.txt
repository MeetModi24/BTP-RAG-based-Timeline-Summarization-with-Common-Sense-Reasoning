338. LETTER TO RUKMINIDEVI BAZAJ
December 9, 1933
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your postcard. During a tour how can one get time to
write letters? Even today I have got only a few minutes and am
using them to write this. It is God's kindness that Devendra is
growing so well. I hear occasionally from Radha. I have just
heard that Santok's mother passed away.
I hope you get the Harijan.
Blessings from
BAPU
