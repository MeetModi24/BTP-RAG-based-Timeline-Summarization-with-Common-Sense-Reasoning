357. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Shivratri
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have received your letter. You have done well to start
(playing) the sitar. If your health becomes all right, everything can
be afforded. I like your resolve to adhere to whatever you have taken
up. In my opinion, a person who cannot sing, i.e., who cannot
recognize the tune, cannot play an instrument. God alone knows how
far this is right. Or Panditji knows.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N.9378
1
Narayan Moreshwar Khare
