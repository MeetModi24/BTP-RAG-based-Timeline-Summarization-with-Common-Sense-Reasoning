477. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
September 4, 1941
CHI. AMRITLAL,
You are a fortunate man. Father's falling into sleep eternal
without having to be nursed by anybody is no occasion for
mourning. I vividly see his face before me. He was a very holy
man. Console Mother and return soon after finishing your
work there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10802
