99. LETTER TO DINSHAW K. MEHTA
SEVAGRAM,
August 1, 1945
CHI. DINSHAW,
There are still [two] other girls whom I would like to send
there. Is there room for them? Neither of the two is an invalid
but both need treatment. One of them is Manilal's wife Sushila.
She has two children. She would not like to leave them any-
where else. Do not hesitate to say no if there is no room or if
you feel that the case is not worth taking.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
