5. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
March 15, 1937
CHI. VIJAYA,
You wish to win the heart of Mirabehn. Take her company
as virtuous, good and saintly. Overlook other people's short-
comings and see their virtues. Here is a verse from Tulsidas:
God has made the world full of
living and non-living things, of virtue and vices,
The good, like the swan, take the milk of virtue
and leave out the water of vice.
If you do not understand the meaning, look it up in "Bal-
kand" or ask Nanavati.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7063. Also C.W. 4555. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
