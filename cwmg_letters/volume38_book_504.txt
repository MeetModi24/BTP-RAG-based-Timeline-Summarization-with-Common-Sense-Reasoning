504. LETTER TO K. T. PAUL
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 1, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You need not apologize. I know how
helpless a busy man often becomes as to his movements.
I am leaving for Sind tomorrow and I shall be back on the
15th February. I shall be at the Ashram at least up to the 23rd
or 24th instant.
Yours sincerely,
K. T. PAUL, ESQ.
MAITLAND HOUSE, DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 15003
