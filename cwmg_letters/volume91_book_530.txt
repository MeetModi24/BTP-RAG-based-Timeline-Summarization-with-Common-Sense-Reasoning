530. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
SABARMATI,
Ashwin Shukla 4, 1982 [October 10, 1926]
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I have your letter. I am glad to know that Martanda is
improving. If you can find out why he was not recovering here,
please let me know. What treatment is he taking? May you
succeed in observing the vows. The work on the occasion of
the Charkha-dwadashi was well done. Keep me informed of the
results of your dietetic experiments.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
RAJASTHAN CHARKHA SANGH
AJMER
From the Hindi original: Haribhau Upadhyaya Papers. Courtesy:
