47. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 19, 1930
CHI. PREMA,
I got your humorous letter crammed with news. Continue
to write in this manner. I do hope that I shall not fall ill here.
How can I have here Prema and Vasumati who would remain by
my side whenever I seemed to be passing through a difficult time,
fearing that something must have happened to me? Don't believe
the rumour about my having lost weight. My health is indeed good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10226; also C.W. 6674. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
