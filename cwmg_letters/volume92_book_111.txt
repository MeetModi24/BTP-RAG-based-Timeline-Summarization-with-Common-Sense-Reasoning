111. LETTER TO RUDRANATH
[August 21/22, 1929]
BHAISHRI RUDRANATH,
I have your letter. You do not seem to have understood
the life of Zaghloul and Lenin. Both of them worked very hard
at first and only then were they able to achieve what they did.
I have only the spade, the [carding-]bow, the spindle and the
basket fit for youthful blood. You are not ready to accept with
faith any of these four. Had you really learnt to spin on the takli
with courage, there would have been no need for me to write
anything.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/129
