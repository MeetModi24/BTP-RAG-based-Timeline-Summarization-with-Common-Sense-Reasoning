413. LETTER TO FULCHAND SHAH
Posh Sud 11, 1981 [January 5, 1925]
BHAISHRI FULCHAND,
I shall take up another programme only after I reach
Bhavnagar and feel like doing so. Do not bind me from today.
I am very tired and I have yet to attend many functions.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAISHRI FULCHAND KASTURCHAND
KELAVANI MANDAL KARYALAYA
WADHWAN CITY
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2824. Courtesy: Sharadabehn Shah
