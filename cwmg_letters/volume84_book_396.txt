396. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
NEW DELHI,
June 10, 1946
MY DEAR MR. ABELL,
I am desired by Gandhiji to send you the enclosed copy of a cable
just received from South Africa.
Gandhiji hopes that the information is wholly unfounded. 2
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 89
