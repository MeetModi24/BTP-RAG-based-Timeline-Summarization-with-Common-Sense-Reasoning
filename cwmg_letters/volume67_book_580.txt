580. LETTER TO GLADYS OWEN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 4, 1938
MY DEAR GLADYS,
It is with difficulty I can cope with the correspondence. I
hope you have profited by the treatment there.
Peace has been preserved but at the cost of honour.
Muriel should soon be in India. Mahadev is decidedly on
the mend.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6194
