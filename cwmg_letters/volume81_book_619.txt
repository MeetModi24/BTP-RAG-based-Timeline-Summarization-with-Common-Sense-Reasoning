619. LETTER TO UMA AGRAWAL
POONA,
October 12, 1945
CHI. OM 2 ,
I have your letter. After writing such a slovenly hand, how
can you ask for pardon? One must never write a bad hand.
I received the baby's silent message. Whom do you mean by
"his"? Is it not the limit of helplessness that you feel embarrassed
in taking your [husband's] name? If you send me some names,
I would select one.
Sushilabehn has returned. She did excellent work.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 345
