18. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
January 15, 1932
CHI. PANDITJI,
You have given a fine description of your visit to Kashi. The
fact that the Master's pupils are carrying on his work in a worthy
manner should make all of us happy. You should now resume
your visits among Chharas . If you can cultivate close contacts with
them and influence them, it will be a great achievement.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 220. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
