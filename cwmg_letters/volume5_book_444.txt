444. LETTER TO LT.-GOVERNOR OF THE TRANSVAAL
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
P. O. Box 6522,
JOHANNESBURG,
September 12, 1906
TO
HIS EXCELLENCY THE LIEUTENANT-GOVERNOR
OF THE TRANSVAAL AND JOHANNESBURG
SIR,
In accordance with a resolution passed by the British Indian Mass
Meeting held at the Empire Theatre, Johannesburg, I have the honour
to enclose herewith Resolutions 2, 3, 4 and 51 for Your Excellency's in-
formation.
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
ABDOOL GANI
CHAIRMAN,
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
Pretoria Archives: L. G. Files: 1902-1906
