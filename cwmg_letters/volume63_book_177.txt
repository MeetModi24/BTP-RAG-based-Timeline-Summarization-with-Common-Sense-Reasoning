177. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN AGRAWAL
SEGAON,
July 13, 1936
BHAI SHRIMAN,
Your letter I could read only just now. I am unable to
read the whole mail as soon as it arrives.
I am sending Roti ka Raga. Return it to me after Kaka-
saheb does his writing on it. In fact I had understood that my
opinion was required solely for your own satisfaction. But now
I cannot find anything to write which can go into print. All
the same let us see what can be done. You may come over
whenever you wish to. You are not going to take my time, are
you? Do whatever work Mahadev asks you to do for Harijan.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 299
