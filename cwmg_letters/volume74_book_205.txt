205. LETTER TO BHULABHAI J. DESAI
June 30, 1941
BHAI BHULABHAI,
Bhai Munshi had given me the substance of your talk with
him. You must have seen from my statement that the Congress
policy is quite clear. The clarity was achieved after a long discus-
sion.
Hence those who do not accept that particular portion
of the Poona resolution have no option but to leave the Congress,
and that is why I persuaded Bhai Munshi to take the step he
has taken. Now if what I understand about your views is
correct, your path is clear. You should come out openly with

your view and try to cultivate public opinion in accordance with
it. This is an age of action. All of us will be judged by our
actions. A heavy responsibility rests on the Congress. It can be
discharged only if there is perfect correspondence between our
views and our actions. We claim to represent the 35 crores. I
am addressing this to you personally, but I should like all of
you to think over it.
It is going to be a long and a hard
struggle.
What is going on at present is but a preparation.
Blessings from
BAPU
