129. LETTER TO KHUSHALCHAND GANDHI
May 23, 1935
RESPECTED BROTHER,
Chi. Narandas informs me that, though you have read and
reflected a great deal, all that seems to be of little help now
and your mind has become weak. I am not surprised, for this
happens to everybody. I must say, however, that I have cherished
a different hope about you. For one whose mind has turned God-
ward, there is no old age. The body becomes weak but in that it
but follows its law. The mind need not become weak. It may go
on rising till at last it merges in God. Isn't this the meaning of
the verse:
This is the state, O Partha, of the man who rests in Brahman; having
attained to it, he is not deluded. He who abides in this state even at
the hour of death passes into oneness with Brahman
Isn't the true brahmi state that which endures up to the end?
What should a mind that has become weak do to attain such a
state? The only reply is, constant repetition of the name. I,
therefore, pray that you should make a firm resolution and, with-
drawing your mind from everything, merely go on repeating, after
taking the Lord's name, the following verse:
I desire neither earthly kingdom nor paradise no, not even release from
birth and death. I desire only release of the afflicted from misery.
If you cannot do this, repetition of the name will suffice. If
you fill your mind completely with strength and light, you will
cease feeling old and feel young instead. If you still feel attach-
ment to any being or object, I entreat you to shed it completely.
Humblest respects from
MOHANDAS
