654. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 21, 1928
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. Why do you let yourself be so affected by
the injustice prevailing in the world? Does not the world itself
mean selfishness?
How would the world go on without
selfishness? That is why the Gita teaches detachment. How would
selfishness avail one suffering from tuberculosis or some such disease?
But should I talk such wisdom to you? What is surprising is that
you were upset by the behaviour of the owner of the bungalow
towards you. Write to me when things are settled. I have not
detained Shankar. You have done well to resist the temptation
of coming here. It will be very good if you come in winter.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
18 BANGANGA ROAD
WALKESHWAR, BOMBAY
