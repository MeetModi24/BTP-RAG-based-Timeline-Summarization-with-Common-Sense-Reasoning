203. LETTER TO SHARAD KUMARI
August 14, 1945
CHI. SHARAD KUMARI,
I have your letter. Why should you feel nervous now that
you have gone there? Whatever the other girls do, you must
maintain simplicity. Spin and learn other crafts. Do not indulge
in luxuries but at the same time do not be jealous of others.
Love everyone. In the end you will make an impression.
Blessings from
BAPU
C/O THE HEADMISTRESS
CENTRAL HINDU GIRLS SCHOOL
KASHI, U. P.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
