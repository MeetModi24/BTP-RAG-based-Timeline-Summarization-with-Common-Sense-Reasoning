508. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEGAON,
July 23, 1937
BELOVED DAUGHTER,
I got your letter. For the present stay on there. Complete
one month. We will think afterwards. You can stitch clothes for
Harijans. There is enough to do there. But do only as much
as you can stand.
I[ Saraswati really wishes to come and Ramachandran also
approves of it, I should certainly like her to come. She can
gain practical experience here. If Ramachandran agrees, let
me know what she wishes.
I have written to Bari about the Rs. 1,000 and told him that
if he intends to give the sum he should give it unconditionally.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 386
