10. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 6, 1930
CHI. PREMA,
They gave me your letter of July 1. It is good that you get
fruit with your meals.
I took in Dhurandhar because I saw that he was scrupulous
in observing rules. I also liked his frankness. This should not
be published at present.
Give my love to the flower plants and trees. Here, too, I see
their brothers and sisters. Shouldn't I be satisfied with that?
In all, I got only two letters from you. I didn't get the one
in English.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10224; also C.W. 6672. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
