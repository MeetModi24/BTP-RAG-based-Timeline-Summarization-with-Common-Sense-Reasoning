462. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 22, 1930
BHAI TULSI MAHER,
I received your letter.
You are carrying on your work quite well and I hope it will
continue so. Do not be perturbed by the news of the struggle
here. Your duty lies in sticking to your post.
These days smallpox is rampant here. We are being tested
in this way too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6537
