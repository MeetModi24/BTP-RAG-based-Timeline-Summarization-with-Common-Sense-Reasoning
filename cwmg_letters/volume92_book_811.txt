811. LETTER TO S. SRINIVASA IYENGAR
WARDHA,
December 11, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I am thankful for your letter. It is a matter of great joy to
me that in the opinion of both of you, Ambujam has benefited
by her stay with me. Her only ambition in life seems to be to
make herself a useful instrument of service. I regard it a privi-
lege to be able to help Ambujam in any way I can. And I
shall always welcome any suggestion you may make in further-
ance of my effort to guide her.
With my regards to both of you,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Builders of Modern India, p. 53
