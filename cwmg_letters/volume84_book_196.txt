196. LETTER TO MUNSHI ISHWARSARAN
SIMLA,
May 14, 1946
BHAI MUNSHIJI,
If a change of name can bring about change in work, you
may change the name. Work comes first; and then why fuss
about the name?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
MUNSHI ISHWARSARAN
HARIJAN ASHRAM
ALLAHABAD, U. P.
