412. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
December 25, 1933
CHI. AMALA,
I have your letters. Whatever I did was done out of pure
love. That you did not feel the love is my misfortune. Anyway
you need not fear a repetition of the performance. You shall
develop in your own way. You know what is wanted. You will
do it after your style not mine. Only keep your mind and body
fit. Do your Hindi in your own fashion. Take care of yourself
and your things. Keep your room and yourself tidy. You should
select good and easy Hindi books.
Do write fully and freely. Your letters have not hurt me.
I am first class.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
