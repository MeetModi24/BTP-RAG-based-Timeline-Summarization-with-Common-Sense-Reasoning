290. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
July 26, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Yours.
You must have heard that the Agra strike is off. So you have
become Jamnalal's outfitter!
As Tara was due here today I vacated this house for her
and transferred myself to the dispensary. Dr. Das has gone to
Janakibehn with his patients. So I am literally aniketa1, house-
less. I do not mind.
Sushila has gone to see Pyarelal with Prabhavati and
Kusum.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4249. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7881
