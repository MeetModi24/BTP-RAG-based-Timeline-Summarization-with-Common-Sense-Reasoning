422. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 11, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
Are the Gita recitations at Pratishthan conducted on the same
lines as at Sabarmati? In that case, you may note that we have
decided now to complete the recitation within seven days instead
of fourteen. Write what you and Satis Babu think about it. If
the period in which you complete the recitation is different, you
need take no notice of this letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
