360. LETTER TO KAPILRAI PAREKH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 15, 1939
BHAI KAPILRAI,
I have your letter. Let us hope that there will be no partiality
in Bihar. But I cannot interfere. I think you should personally
go and see Munshiji in Bombay.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KAPILRAI PAREKH
GORJI'S BUNGALOW
RAIKHAD, AHMEDABAD
From Gujarati: C.W. 9828. Courtesy: Kapilrai Parekh
