93. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
Between 1- at Night, May 2, 1933
If your betrothal with S. has not been broken off, you
may certainly marry. I will approve of your doing so even
during the fast. You will continue to serve even after you get
married. After marriage try to observe as much self-control as
you can. Continue to write to me. You should not grieve if I
die. The 'I' will not die. What will it matter if the body perishes?
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 9540. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Joshi
