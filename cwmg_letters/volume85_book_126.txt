126. LETTER TO F. MART BARR
POONA,
August 4, 1946
DEAR MARY,
Bapu got your letter duly and was glad to have it. He hopes you
will continue to give him such news as is worth giving from time to time.
We follow it all in the papers with very deep interest. You can imagine how
Bapu's heart is with them all there. He is wonderfully well considering the
heavy burden he carries. We are off to Sevagram tomorrow. I do not think
we have been more than a month in anyone place since last October ! Dr.
Sushila is engaged to be married to Kaka Kalelkar's younger son. Kanu and
his wife are setting up house as soon as we return to Sevagram.
It will be
nice to get there again after so long. My love to you and Bapu sends
his too.
Yours,
AMRIT
[PS.]
Mira is in her Ashram in the U. P.
From a photostat: G. N. 8086. Also C. W. 3416. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
