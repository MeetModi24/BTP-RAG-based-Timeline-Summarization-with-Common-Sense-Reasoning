41. LETTER TO NIRMALA H. DESAI
January 23, 1932
CHI. NIRMALA (AUNT),
Yes. If I had not seen you in Bombay, I would not have
thought of asking you to write to me. But having seen you
there, I did not write a separate letter to you but said in my
letter to Anandi that she should ask you to write. You have im-
proved your handwriting.
Write to me from time to time.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9462
Blessings from
BAPU
