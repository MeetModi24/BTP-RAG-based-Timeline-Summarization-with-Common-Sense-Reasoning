203. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
GUJRANWALA,
[November 22] 1919
BHAISHRI MAHADEV,
I have your telegram about Lala Lajpat Rai. I do not
understand how he can have asked for money. He has plenty
with him. All the same, I shall see his son and make sure. You
must have received all my articles.
There was a letter from Narahari today, from which I learnt
about your visit to Sojitra. I do not know what took you there.
Narahari writes about the progress of the construction work.
I was indeed very happy to read about it. If a few buildings get
ready, we shall have some relief. I am leaving for Delhi today
and shall reach Lahore on Tuesday morning. You will be able
to see my itinerary in Navajivan itself.
Blessing from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9856
1 He was later released in 1920.
2 Gandhiji left Gujranwala for Delhi on this date and arrived in Lahore
on November 25, which was a Tuesday.
