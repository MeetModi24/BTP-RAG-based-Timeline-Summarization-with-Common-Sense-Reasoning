234. LETTER TO MANAGER, "YOUNG INDIA"
February 9, 1931
BHAISHRI,
After reading in the papers about the attack on you, I stopped
sending articles to you for the journal. The loss of the cyclostyling
equipment is, from our point of view, certainly a sort of defeat
for us. It has had no effect on me. For we have no right at
all to own property. In a tyrannous state, the tyrant claims every
pie as his property and, through that claim, he is able to oppress
the subjects. If, in such a state, an individual or group of in-
dividuals becomes indifferent to property, the tyrant's power of
oppression ends.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Navajivan, 22-2-1931
