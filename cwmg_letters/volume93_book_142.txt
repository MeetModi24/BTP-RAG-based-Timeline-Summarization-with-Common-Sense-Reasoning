142. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL VIDYARTHI
SEGAON,
October 17, 1936
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
You must not eat rice and dal. Take only milk and fruit.
I am sending six mosambis from here. Eat them. Take milk.
It is your duty to get well quickly. Do not take cereals.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 11701
