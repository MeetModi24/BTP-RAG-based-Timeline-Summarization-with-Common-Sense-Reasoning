533. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 14, 1930
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
Your letter. That 1,000 copies of the Bengali edition of
Anasaktiyoga have been sold augurs well. In winter mornings it
is good to expose as much of the body as possible to the sun
rays between 9 and 10. It is also beneficial to take some raw
onion with roti by way of medicine during winter.
Onion has
many qualities, though some drawbacks too. The main drawback
is that of smell, but that can be avoided by taking a small quan-
tity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1679
