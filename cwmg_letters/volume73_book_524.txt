524. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 20, 1941
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter. It is good indeed that you went. Remember,
you have to come here when your work there is over. The packet
to Jawaharlal is being sent by book-post. Things are all right
here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3556
