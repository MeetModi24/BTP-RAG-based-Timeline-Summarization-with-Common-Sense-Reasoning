379. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
[February] 12, 1933
CHI. DEVDAS,
I got your letter. Write to me when you get time and let
me know your reasons for thinking that the reforms suggested by
you in Hindi are possible. There is Dr. Sharma's Sun-ray Hospi-
tal in Karol Bagh. Amtussalaam has been admitted there. She
has great faith in Dr. Sharma. Find some time and get acquainted
with him, and learn from him whatever appeals to you. Give me
some details about the hospital. See Amtussalaam and inquire
after her health.
I hope you remember about the small Urdu
dictionary. I will remember what you have written about Rajaji.
Do not be lazy in writing to me. Send me Rajaji's and your
comments on Harijan. Request him to write to me a few lines.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1993
