307. LETTER TO SYED JAMIL WASTI
June 30, 1942
DEAR SYED SAHEB,
Many thanks for your letter with enclosure. I have read the
letter with interest. Whether social reform has connection with
political liberty in the manner you state or not, I agree with
you that it must come. I am doing all I can in that direction.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
