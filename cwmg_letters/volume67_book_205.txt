205. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
July 2, 1938
CHI. KANAIYA,
I hurt you in the morning, didn't I? But did you realize
or not that there was love behind my anger?
I could not bear
even that mistake on your part which I knew you would make.
It is a good sign. I want to see you perfect. If you have patience,
you will certainly become so.
Are you not Narandas's son? Emulate his patience and faith.
You should never yield to despair.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
