594. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
October 9, 1945
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter. I am not going to omit you when I come to
that side which as at present arranged I do not do before Jan-
uary. But nothing is fixed unless I move from Sevagram. I hope
you are keeping well. Tell Balvantsinha I am not writing to
him for want of time.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6511. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9906
