393. LETTER TO RAMJI
February 13, 1933
BHAISHRI RAMJI,
I got your wire. Swarupbehn's name in her husband's house
is Vijayalakshmi. Her address at Bombay is Meher Mansion,
Laburnum Road, Gamdevi. I have sent her your wire and your
suggestions. See that after coming there, she does not have to
bear the expense. Arrange interviews with the Raja and Ranis.
Introduce her to some courteous sanatanist, if there is such a one.
Do not let her run up and down too much.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XX
