309. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
WARDHA,
June 29, 1935
CHI. MARY,
I quite see that you are going to take long there. But you
should give as much time as may be necessary.
What about the climate there? How many are you in your
ward?
What is the distance between bed and bed?
What food do they serve you?
Love.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6051. Also C.W. 3381. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
