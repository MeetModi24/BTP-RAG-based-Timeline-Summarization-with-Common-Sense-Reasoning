540. LETTER TO T. P. JOSHI
October 2, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter as well as the cheque. I would like to
come over to Mahabaleshwar just to hear your poems. But it
will be, won't it, as God wills.
T. P. JOSHI
40A RIDGE ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
