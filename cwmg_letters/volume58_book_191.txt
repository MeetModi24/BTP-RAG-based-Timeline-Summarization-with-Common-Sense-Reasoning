191. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
July 11, 1934
BHAISHRI MUNSHI,
I got your letter. I went through your article with as much
attention as I could command in this excitement. I found the
last paragraph against the spirit of peace. I have, therefore, omit-
ted it and concluded the article with two new sentences. [The
new sentences are:]
Whether this consummation can be reached through the
Parliamentary effort remains to be seen. Its success will
largely depend on the verdict of the voters.
If you approve of these changes, you may adopt them and use
the article.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7546. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi. Also Pilgrimage to
Freedom, p. 376
