251. LETTER TO DR. CHIMANDAS J. JAGTIANI
July 27, 1924
MY DEAR CHIMANDAS,
I have your letter. You must have seen my argument in
Young India showing why it is necessary to send half an hour's
output to the Congress for the nation. You may spin for yourself.
But you must spin for the nation too.
Pray do not worry about my health.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. CHIMANDAS
NIHARJUNGO PIR
HYDERABAD (SIND)
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
