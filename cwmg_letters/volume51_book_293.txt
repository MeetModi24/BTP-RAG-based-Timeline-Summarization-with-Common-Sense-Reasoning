293. LETTER TO J. S. HOYLAND
October 3, 1932
MY DEAR HOYLAND,
I am glad you have given me a brief description of your visits
to Tolstoy's home. I wish you had also told me something of your
impressions of modern Russia.
I am quickly regaining lost strength of body. I need say no-
thing more about the fast as it is now past history. This I may
say. God was most merciful to me during those days.
Love from us all.
BAPU
From a photostat of the original: C.W. 4506. Courtesy: Woodbrooke
College, Birmingham, and Mrs. Jessie Hoyland
