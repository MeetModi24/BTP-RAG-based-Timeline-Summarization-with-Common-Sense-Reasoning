91. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
September 16, 1932
I can see from here the misery on your faces-Shankerlal's
and yours-and hear it in your voices. But please regard it as the
result of ignorant human attachment. You ought to feel unmixed
joy. It is a holy opportunity which God has sent me unasked.
The duty of you all is to cultivate greater devotion to your work
and become purer.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 28
