483. LETTER TO PARAMANAND K. KAPADIA
Wednesday [May 5, 1926]
BHAISHREE PARAMANAND,
I have read all your letters carefully. I shall try to deal
with some of your questions in Navajivan. I want to write much
more but I have to control myself.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
BHAISHREE PARAMANAND KUNVERJI
BHAVNAGAR
From the Gujarati original: G.N. 11586
