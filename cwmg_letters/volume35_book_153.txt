153. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
Monday [November 7, 1927]
CHI. GANGABEHN (SENIOR),
Ramibehn accidentally met me in the train. She travelled
with me from Mehmedabad to Nadiad. She talked about you
all the time. She feels unhappy because you do not look after
the children. I told her that, if the children were entrusted to
you on your conditions and if no one interfered with you after-
wards, you would certainly agree to take charge of them. She had
nothing to say to this. I am sure you write to her from time to
time. Her ideals are good. At present, she devotes all her time
to acquiring knowledge of the letters.
See that you do not flee from the responsibility which has
come upon you. It is now that the knowledge and experience
you have gained are being tested. With patience, good temper and
generosity of heart, you will be able to overcome all diffi-
culties. Just as the sea accepts the water of all rivers within
itself, purifies it and gives it back again, so you too, if you make
yourself as the sea, will be able to accept all people. As the sea
makes no distinction between good rivers and bad, but purifies
all, so one person, whose heart is purified and enlarged with non-
violence and truth, can contain everything in that heart and it
will not overflow or lose its serenity. Remember that you aim
at being such a person.
Blessings from
BAPU
