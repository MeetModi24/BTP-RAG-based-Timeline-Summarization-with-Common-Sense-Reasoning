210. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 14, 1928
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I have your letter.
Here is a copy of my letter to Sjt. Subhas Bose in reply to
his letter.
I have just returned from Bardoli.
SJT. SATIS CHANDRA DAs GUPTA
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR
From a photostat: S.N. 13655
