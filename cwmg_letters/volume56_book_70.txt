70. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
WARDHA,
October 7, 1933
CHI. MANI,
I have your letter. See all the people whom you wish to
see and then come.
But please do not take this to mean that
you may come in the next millennium. Do bring along Baba .
He will like this place. I am improving, that is, regaining my
strength. I shall be here till November 7.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRIMATI MANIBEHN PATEL
RAMNIVAS, PAREKH STREET
BOMBAY 4
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- : Manibehn Patelne, p. 109
