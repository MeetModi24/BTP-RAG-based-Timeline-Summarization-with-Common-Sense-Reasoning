293. LETTER TO AMINA G. QURESHI
March 15, 1934
CHI. AMINA,
I had your letter. Aren't you yourself also to blame for
your persistent ill health? You just don't take care of your
diet. You must have spices, you cannot do without rice and
you also want this and that. You ought to live on milk and
fruit. Your body too is bulging out of shape. Hamid must be
allowed to stay in the hospital and get rid of his eczema. You
and the children must regain your health somehow.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Write to me at my Patna address.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10624. Courtesy: Amina G. Qureshi
