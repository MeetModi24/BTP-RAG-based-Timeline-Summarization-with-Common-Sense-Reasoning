394. LETTER TO PADMA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 7, 1930
CHI. PADMA,
I got your letter. The tumour must be cured. Do not neg-
lect it. If Gangabehn were not with you, I would have certain-
ly felt worried because of your having gone out of the Ashram.
But since she is with you, I feel easy in my mind. Write to me
regularly. You should write your letters in a bold and clear
hand.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6114
