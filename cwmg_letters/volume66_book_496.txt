496. LETTER TO LAKSHMI NARAYAN GADODIA
March 8, 1938
BHAI LAKSHMI NARAYANJI,
I have your letter. I shall recall Sharda only when you
two relieve her. I certainly have faith in nature cure but it
is not always that we come by the proper cures. I have not
yet full faith in Hakimji's knowledge. Of course, I am keep-
ing well.
How is the dairy going on?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5626
