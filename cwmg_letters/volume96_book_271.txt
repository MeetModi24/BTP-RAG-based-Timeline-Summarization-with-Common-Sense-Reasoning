271. LETTER TO MILLIE GRAHAM POLAK
NADIAD,
July 22 [1918]
DEAR MILLIE,
The enclosed is just to cheer you. I have been corresponding with
Dr. Mehta too. Of course, I do not expect Henry will have to do war
work. And if he has, you know that you have no cause for anxiety.
I am undergoing a revolution in my outlook upon life. As it seems to
me some old cobwebs are falling away. But of these when I have
more time.
With love,
Yours,
BHAI
[PS.]
Do write to me please. Devdas is in Madras, do you know? Write
to him a line care Natesan. He is teaching Hindi.
