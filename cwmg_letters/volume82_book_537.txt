537. LETTER TO ADVAITKUMAR GOSWAMI
[January 11, 1946]
BHAI ADVAITKUMAR,
I have your letter. I am trying to find out. Let us see
what happens.
I have the Raja Saheb's letter. I have not yet read it.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI ADVAITKUMAR GOSWAMI
C/O MUNICIPAL COMMISSIONER
BRINDABAN
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
