457. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
April 27, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
I do think it would be harmful if boys and girls play hututu
together. In this game, the players catch and throw down one
another. Boys and girls cannot play such a game together. We
should not knowingly do something which may rouse Impure
feelings in us.
Blessings from
BAPU
