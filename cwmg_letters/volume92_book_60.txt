60. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 13, 1929
CHI. MATHURADAS,
How is it that Dilip has not yet recovered? He used to be
so healthy. Some lapse on his part must be the cause of his falling
ill. When you are fit enough for the journey and go to Almora,
I think he will regain his health. Our relations with people at
Almora are such that when you go there you will feel at home.
I shall have to go to Allahabad on the 24th. I shall be
back here by the 29th. I shall go via Agra.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
121 FORT STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
