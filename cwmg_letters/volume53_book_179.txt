179. LETTER TO T. K. S. RAJAN
January 24, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I do hope that stu-
dents will not be satisfied with merely having passed a resolution
but that they will engage in some concrete service of Harijans.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. T. K. S. RAJAN
51 I AGRAHARAM
SALEM
From a microfilm: S.N. 19001
