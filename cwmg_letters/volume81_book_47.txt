47. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
July 24, 1945
CHI. SHRIMAN,
It seems you have sent the letter already. I had thought you
would show me the draft. Anyway in my view you should have
given only one reason for resigning . Use of the term 'Hindustani'
is of secondary importance. Implications of the term 'Rashtra-
bhasha' are far-reaching. It would be better also to send the
resignation after revising it. If you want to do this you should
show me the draft before sending it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 307
