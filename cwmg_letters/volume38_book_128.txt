128. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
November 27, 1928
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have both your letters. Do go to Dwarka if you wish to.
Do not at all take to heart my absence. Some day this body is
bound to leave for ever. Why be distressed over separation from
it? We should find happiness in devotion to a cause for which
our passing away would have a meaning.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3341
