377. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
May 2, 1930
BHAISHRI VITHALDAS,
I got your letter. You have asked for someone to help you.
Maganbhai is going there. He is a very exact man in all matters.
He will prove very useful to you. I wish you all success.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9773
