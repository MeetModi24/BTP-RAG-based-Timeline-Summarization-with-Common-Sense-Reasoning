48. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
Monday [June 19, 1911]
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
Yes, I knew about the disturbance at the Farm. None of us is
fit to live on a Tolstoy Farm. We can but qualify by making continuous
endeavour.
I am having no rest here this time.
It will be a fine thing if the picnic comes off on Thursday. You
will have a tough job to tackle.
With love,
Yours,
UPPER HOUSE
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy : National
Archives of India
