346. LETTER TO GANGADEVI SANADHYA
NANDI DURG,
Vaisakha 15 [May 16, 1927]
DEAR SISTER,
You have not recovered as yet. How is it? Quite some time
has gone by. Do you not wish even to change the treatment?
Totaram should write to me all the details. You need not bother
to write.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2548
