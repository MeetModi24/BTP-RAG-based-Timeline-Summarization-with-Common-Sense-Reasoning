247. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
CAMP: KHADI PRATISHTHAN,
SODEPUR (NEAR CALCUTTA),
December 8, 1945
DEAR MR. ABELL,
I have your letter of 26th November about Shri Yajee.
I am thankful for the considered reply. I am now trying to
put myself in touch with the gentleman and see what he has
to say.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
G. E. B. ABELL, ESQ.
PRIVATE SECRETARY TO THE VICEROY
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 61
