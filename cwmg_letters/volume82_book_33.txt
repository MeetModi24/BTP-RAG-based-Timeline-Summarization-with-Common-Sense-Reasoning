33. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
POONA,
November 4, 1945
CHI. GHANSHYAMDAS,
Dinshaw has had a talk with your brothers. As a result he is
not keen any more about going to Nasik. So you may proceed
on the assumption that the Nasik idea has been given up. About
the building we may continue along the present lines. If I find
that Dinshaw still wishes to go to Nasik, I shall have a talk
with him. We shall see if any building or land is available then.
We shall all reach Bombay on the 19th. I shall leave Bom-
bay for Wardha on the 20th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8074. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
