529. LETTER TO PARASRAM MEHROTRA
May 15, 1932
CHI. PARASRAM,
You should write in your diary anything which occurs to
you. You can write even your most secret thoughts. Nothing
concerning ourselves need be kept secret from other people, and
you should not, therefore, worry who will read your diary. For
that reason, however, you cannot record others' short-comings or
what anybody may have told you in confidence. Only the secre-
tary of the Ashram or a person authorized by him has the right
to read anybody's diary. But, on our part, we should not wish
our diary to be kept secret from any person.
You should not in any circumstances stop spinning on the
takli and be out of practice, no matter even if you can give to
it only half an hour every day. Have you become methodical
now?
BAPU
