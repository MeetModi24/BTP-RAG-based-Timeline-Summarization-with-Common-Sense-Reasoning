232. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
[CALCUTTA]
Ashad Vad 4 [July 13, 1925]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. You say you don't know what to write.
Does it mean that you are worried? If so, please stop worrying.
There is no cause whatever for it. One should not be upset be-
cause of the loss of money. If there is any other reason for worry,
let me know. You should not feel nervous to go to the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
PS.
My programme is uncertain. I shall have to spend the whole
of this month here.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9216
