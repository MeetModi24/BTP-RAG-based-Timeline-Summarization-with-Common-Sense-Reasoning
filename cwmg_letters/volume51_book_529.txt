529. LETTER TO HARJIVAN KOTAK
October 31, 1932
It is your moral duty to tell him about your private life too.
Surely you do believe that impurity even in private life does
harm to one's work. Don't argue that others who are impure in
their private lives have succeeded in business, etc. It is not for us
to judge whether they have succeeded or not. We make no
distinction between private life, public life or business activities.
All our activities are for self-purification and hence any impurity
in us our will obstruct progress at every step.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 190
