318. LETTER TO KRISHNASWAMI
'PARNAKUTI', POONA,
July 16, 1933
BHAI KRISHNASWAMI,
I have your letter. I have also learnt from Rajaji of your ac-
quaintance with him. My blessings on your marriage.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SJT. KRISHNASWAMI
DAKSHINA BHARAT HINDI PRACHAR
TRIPLICANE
MADRAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6580; also C.W. 4405
