610. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
[After September 19, 1935]
CHI. LILAVATI,
Just as you were delayed, I too was delayed by a couple
of days. I have already sent you your previous lesson. You have
all taken enough pains.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9254. Also C.W. 1050. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
