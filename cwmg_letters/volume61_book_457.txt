457. LETTER TO A STUDENT
August 8, 1935
No matter how weak a person is in body, if it is a shame to
flee he will stand his ground and die at his post. This would be
non-violence and bravery. No matter how weak he is, he will use
what strength he has in inflicting injury on his opponent and die
in the attempt. This is bravery but not non-violence. If when his
duty is to face danger, he flees, it is cowardice. In the first case
man will have love or charity in him. In the second and third
cases there would be dislike or distrust and fear.
Yours,
M. K. G.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai. Also Harijan, 17-8-1935
