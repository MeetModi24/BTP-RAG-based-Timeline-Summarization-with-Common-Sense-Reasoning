143. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
November 3, 1940
CHI. KANTI 1 ,
I thought you did not write to me out of shyness. Now I
see that you do not write out of anger. If that is true, it does
not befit you. Have you forgotten what nice letters you used to
write? Harilal's letter is enclosed. Write to him. He had a fall
but now he is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7361. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
