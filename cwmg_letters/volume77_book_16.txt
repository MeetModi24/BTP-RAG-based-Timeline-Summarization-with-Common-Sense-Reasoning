16. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
DETENTION CAMP,
March 2, 1943
DEAR COL. BHANDARI,
You were good enough yesterday, my day of silence, to tell
me that the Government had restricted to my two sons the
admission of outsiders at the breaking of the fast tomorrow.
Whilst I am thankful for the concession, I am unable to avail
myself of it. For, as the Government know, I make no distinc-
tion between sons born to me and numerous others who are as
dear to me even as they are. I told you three or four days
ago that, if the Government allowed any outsiders to be pre-
sent at the breaking of the fast, they should allow all-nearly
fifty-who are at present in Poona, and who have been allowed
to visit me during the fast. I see that that was not to be.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, p. 49
