158. LETTER TO GLADYS OWEN
July 8, 1936
DEAR GLADYS,
I am glad you have accepted a year's appointment from the
Rajghat Theosophical School. Anyway it will enable you to
gain some experience of Indian students and you will certainly
be able to give something to the boys or girls who may be under
your charge.
Yes, Abbas Tyabji is the one of Baroda whom you had inten-
ded to see and who, alas, is no more. He was one of the noblest
of men I had the good fortune to meet, and you should still nurse
the desire of meeting the members of his family. They are all
chips of the old block, and especially Raihana, one of the daught-
ers of the deceased. She is not only most accomplished but she
is also deeply spiritual.
Love.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
