234. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Ashadh Krishna 8 [July 24 1924]
BHAISHRI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I have your letter.
I have not yet been able to visualize how the spirit of non-
violence can lead to the commission of violence. I have thought
about this deeply. I also believe that, so long as we do not ourselves
transcend the [three] gunas2, we cannot think about this thing to our
full satisfaction in all its bearings.
Swami Anand has sent you the bill for Young India, etc.
I intend to go to Delhi, but it will take some time yet. I very
much wish I could go right now, but I am not yet ready to under-
take physical exertion.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6018. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
