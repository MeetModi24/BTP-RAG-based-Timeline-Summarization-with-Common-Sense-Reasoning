523. LETTER TO BHUJANGILAL CHHAYA
April 4, 1935
CHI. BHUJANGILAL,
If you are obliged to write to me without your father's knowl-
edge, it is the duty of us both not to write to each other.
Anybody who is unwilling to perform a modest duty facing
him in the immediate present in the hope of being able to per-
form a more important one in future is sunk in moha. "Make
the best of today, for who has foreseen tomorrow?" But even
if you decide to take up the smaller duty, you should do so af-
ter consulting your father.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of Gujarati: S. N. 24225
