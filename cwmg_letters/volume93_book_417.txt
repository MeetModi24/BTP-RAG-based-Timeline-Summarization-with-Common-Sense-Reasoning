417. LETTER TO PYARELAL
July 31, 1939
CHI. PYARELAL,
You have not done the correct thing if you have not eaten
today. What adjective should be used for you for your not
replying to Ba at all? It would be good if you ate even now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
