426. LETTER TO K. RAMACHANDRA
[December] 31, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am sorry for the complication that has
arisen there with reference to the temple-entry question. I have
no doubt whatsoever that whilst a non-Hindu may sympathize
