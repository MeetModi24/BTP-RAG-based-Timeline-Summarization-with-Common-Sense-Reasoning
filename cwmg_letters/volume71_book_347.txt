347. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
ON THE TRAIN,
March 21, 1940
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I got your letter just now. I am glad that you feel at home
there. Write to me regularly. I wrote to Rami. She has not
answered.
Do you get any newspapers there?
Teach Kanchan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9740. Also C.W. 720. Courtesy:
Navajivan Trust
