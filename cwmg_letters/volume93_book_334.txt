334. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
October 23, 1938
CHI. LILA,
I have your letter. You will feel uneasy at Segaon in my
absence. It would be better if you waited till I returned. Is
there not enough work of service in Rajkot? What about the
struggle there? Let me have your experiences.
Blessings from
BAPU
