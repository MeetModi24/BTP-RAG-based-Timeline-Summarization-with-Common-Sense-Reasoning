466. LETTER TO V. L. MEHTA
October 10, 1936
BHAI VAIKUNTH,
I heard only yesterday that Lallubhai has fallen ill again.
Things are bound to be all right where workers like you are
available to look after him. I hope he is better now.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I spoke to Shankerlal about the Agriculture Department.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1363
