12. LETTER TO MIRZA M. ISMAIL
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 16, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I esteem your letter enclosing copy of your address to the
Mysore Representative Assembly. I shall go through the whole
of it as soon as I get a few moments. But I have already gone
through the portion kindly marked by you referring to the un-
touchables. It gave me great joy.
I had also your letter about the birthday greetings.
I hope to be able to visit Mysore before the 15th of next
month and pay my respects to the Maharaja Saheb.
Yours sincerely,
MIRZA M. ISMAIL, ESQ.
'LAKE VIEW'
MYSORE
[PS.]
I now learn that the marking was done here.
M. K. G.
From a photostat: S.N. 14162
