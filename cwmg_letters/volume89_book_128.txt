128. LETTER TO TARADEVI NAYYAR
[On or after August 28, 1947]
Why do you worry about Sushila1? God is the Lord and
Master of all. Let us not cease to trust in Him. Rajkumari
has brought the news that Sushila is all right. I hope you are
keeping well. Baby must be delightfully naughty!
Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase, Vol. II, p. 380
