137. LETTER TO UMA AGRAWAL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 1, 1940
CHI. OM 1 ALIAS SLEEPING BEAUTY,
Did you do me a great favour by writing? You should
bow to Nanda Devi, etc., on my behalf also. Now at last you
are a resident of the mountains. It is no small thing that you
remember us. Be happy, all of you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 344
