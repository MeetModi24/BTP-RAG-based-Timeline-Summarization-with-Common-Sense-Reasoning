386. LETTER TO POONAMCHAND RANKA
September 3, 1945
BHAI POONAMCHAND,
Your letter is interesting. Get well. Engross yourself in
constructive work. Learn and also teach all the processes of
[making] yarn.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHETH POONAMCHAND RANKA
RANKA KUTI
SHANKER COLONY, NAGPUR
