16. LETTER TO JALBHOY & SORABJEE BROS.
[JOHANNESBURG,]
July 13, 1905
MESSRS JALBHOY & SORABJEE BROS.
110 FIELD STREET
DURBAN
DEAR SIRS,
You have forgotten to send me an extract of the Press account against
me. Please let me have it at your convenience. I hope you will be good
enough to execute with promptness orders that may be given in connec-
tion with the Press, as things are not yet quite in order at Phoenix.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
I have your letter of the 11th instant. I am glad you have got the
credit from Mr. Laughton, which I return. I thank you [for] having
paid Chhaganlal £100; I note you have sent a draft for £80 to Mr. Rus-
tomji.
Letter Book (1905): No. 633
1 F. A. Laughton, a leading advocate. Vide Vol. I, p. 383.
2 Parsi Rustomji, Indian merchant and co-worker of Gandhiji. Vide Vol. I, p. 385.
