604. LETTER TO HILDA PETIT
ON THE TRAIN,
June 22, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. I personally feel that it is good that
Mother has gone. I had seen her suffering. Of course we do
feel unhappy because of our selfishness. I hope all of you will
be calm.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI HILDA PETIT
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
