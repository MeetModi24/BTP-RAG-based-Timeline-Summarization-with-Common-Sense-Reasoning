225. LETTER TO A. CARTWRIGHT
[JOHANNESBURG,]
July 20, 1908
DEAR MR. CARTWRIGHT,
This is just to tell you that prosecutions have now started.
Mr. Sorabji was sentenced to one month's imprisonment with hard
labour virtually for non-submission to the Asiatic Act. Several hawkers
have been arrested for hawking without licences. They will, so far as
I am aware, elect to go to gaol.
I hope you will not resent my troubling you with my letters.
Yours sincerely,
ALBERT CARTWRIGHT, ESQ.
PRETORIA CLUB
PRETORIA
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4846
