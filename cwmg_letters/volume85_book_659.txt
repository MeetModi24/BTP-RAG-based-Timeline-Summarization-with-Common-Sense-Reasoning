659. LETTER TO SANKARAN
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
CHI. SANKARAN,
Your mental state does not appear to be good. What is
it that has appeared in Jeevan-Sakha? It contains something
about you which is not at all good. It does not suggest humi-
lity. Now, what are you going to do? It is not good to hang
around here aimlessly. I allowed you to come here because I
wanted to know your wishes. It is Sushilabehn who has made
you what you are, and if you cannot get on with her how are
you going to do the hospital work at Sevagram? Think for
yourself and make a determined effort. I cannot talk. It is better
for me to observe silence today.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SANKARAN
