313. LETTER TO KAZI NIZAMUDDIN AHMED
CAMP KARADI,
April 25, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter giving me full information about the
progress of the campaign in Meerut. I hope all the prisoners
are doing well. Please keep me informed from time to time.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
KAZI NIZAMUDDIN AHMED
PRESIDENT
DIST. CONGRESS COMMITTEE
MEERUT
From a photostat: G.N. 10855
