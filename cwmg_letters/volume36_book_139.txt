139. LETTER TO M. R. MADHAVA WARRIER
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 20, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is difficult to guide you from this dis-
tance. But I suggest your going as slow as possible but steadily.
If you will launch out on an ambitious scheme, you will find that
it would prove to be embarrassing in the long run.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. M. R. MADHAVA WARRIER, B.A., LL.B.
PRESIDENT, MUNICIPAL COUNCIL
QUILON
TRAVANCORE
From a photostat: S.N. 13115
