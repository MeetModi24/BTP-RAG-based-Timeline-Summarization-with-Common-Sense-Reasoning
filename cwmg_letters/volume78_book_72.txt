72. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM,
August 29, 1944
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
In my letter of yesterday I forgot to answer a question of
yours. Bhagirathji writes to me that the work will not be held
up for lack of funds.
Everything is fine. Parnerkar is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 482
