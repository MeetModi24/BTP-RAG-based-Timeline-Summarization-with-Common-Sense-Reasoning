213. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
April 9, 1947
CHI. JIVANJI5,
Bhai Shivabhai Amin in East Africa wishes to write and
publish a book about me in the Swahili language. Please do the
needful as quickly as possible to give him the necessary permission
to translate, etc.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 9975. Also C. W. 6949. Cour-
tesy: Jivanji D. Desai
