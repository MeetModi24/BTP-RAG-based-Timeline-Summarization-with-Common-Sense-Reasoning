14. LETTER TO Y. R. DATE
March 8, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
This is my message for the Gaekwar Birthday Commemora-
tion Volume:
"There can be no doubt that His Highness the Maharajah
Saheb Gaekwar of Baroda deserves the warmest congratulations
of us all for his treatment of Harijans and his withdrawing all
State recognition of untouchability."
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. Y. R. DATE
C/O SJT. HARIBHAU PHATAK
From a photostat: S.N. 20497; also Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home
Department, Special Branch, File No. 800(40)(3), Pt. IV, p. 127
