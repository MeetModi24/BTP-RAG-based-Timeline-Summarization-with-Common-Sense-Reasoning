8. LETTER TO AMIYA
WARDHA,
February 16, 1935
DEAR AMIYA,
Father tells me you are about to enter a new state. May
you and yours lead a happy life of useful service to the country.
Remember that marriage is a sacrament. You are therefore
presently to take up a new and sacred trust in life. May God
give you and yours strength to discharge it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
