85. LETTER TO GOPAL GURUBAXANI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 1, 1945
DEAR GOPAL GURUBAXANI,
Your letter makes strange reading. I do not suppose you or
your father has ever met me. Are you in any way related to
Gope Gurubaxani who with his wife was the other day in the
Ashram and is now in Sind? In any case, both of you have my
blessings for a happy life of service to the country in the place of
a life given to pleasure and self-gratification. I have your cheque
of Rs. 200 for any public service of my choice.
Yours,
BAPU
[C/O] PRINCIPAL N. D. GURUBAXANI
DHARAMDAS BHOJ RAJ LANE
SANTINIKETAN
JACOB ROAD
HYDERABAD (SIND)
