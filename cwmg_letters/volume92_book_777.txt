777. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
June 7, 1934
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have said the correct thing to Nariman. We do not need
a hotel that will give shade instead of sunshine. The Reception
Committee should not encroach upon the prayer time without
consulting me. They must take me to the Harijan colony
unannounced.
I am returning Nariman's letter. If you are in the Recep-
tion Committee, the outside programmes for me should be kept
to the minimum. Keep this in mind.
The rains have started here. So, the walking tour has
become difficult, and I have had to spend three days in Bhadrak.
I shall leave for Wardha tomorrow (Friday). I shall be there
for four days after which I shall be in Bombay on the
appointed date.
Blessings from
BAPU
