195. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
June 28, 1941
DEAR AMRITLAL,
It is clear to me that I can do nothing for you. You must
now come down to mother earth and do as other men do—labour
to earn an honest cowrie and subsist on it. You will then be
able to learn what self-respect is.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10314. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
