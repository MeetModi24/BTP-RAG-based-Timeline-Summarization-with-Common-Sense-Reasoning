159. LETTER TO RAMNIKLAL MODI
November 30, 1928
BHAISHRI RAMNIKLAL,
I send the accompanying cutting for you to read. Will the
average Jain accept the views expressed in it? Do you know
the editor of Jain Jagat? He has also written a pleasant letter
to me.
Write to me and give me news about the health of you both.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4143
