129. LETTER TO INDRA VIDYAVACHASPATI
JUHU,
May 23, 1944
CHI. INDRA,
Come over when you wish to. But why should you take the
trouble just to see me, when travelling is so difficult these days?
I am observing silence which will end on the 29th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7206. Also C.W. 4864. Courtesy:
Chandragupta Vidyalankar
