315. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
PATNA,
April 24, 1947
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I have your letter. You have done the right thing. It is
no use trying to hide what has happened.
I have been summoned to Delhi again. I shall have to
leave on May 2. I hope that now there will be no need for my
presence there. Even here I am doing what needs to be done
there and I hope I shall be doing the same in Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9077
