88. LETTER TO SHANTABEHN G. PATEL
June 22, 1932
CHI. SHANTA,
I hear from Bhaktibehn that you intend to come to see
Narasinhbhai in the beginning of July. Whenever you come, see
Bhaktibehn too. When you see Narasinhbhai, give him my re-
gards. I don't ask him just now to see me, and he controls his
desire to come and see me as an example to others. I have read
most of the books sent by you. Now they are being read by the
women.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. SHANTABEHN NARASINHBHAI
C/O GORDHANDAS P. PATEL, ESQ.
POONA CITY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5229
