10. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 4, 1939
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I would certainly like it if Chhaganlal Joshi is given control of
the verandah for khadi work. Chhaganlal is an old inmate of the
Ashram.
The indifference of the State authorities is a painful affair.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4730. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
