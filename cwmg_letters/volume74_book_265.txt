265. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
July 18, 1941
DEAR MOTI BABU,
I was delighted to have your prompt reply. The legal
advisers of the Sangha insist that a promissory note is necessary.
You will therefore please send the promissory note.
I am telling Shri Jajuji that you are and have been willing
to be certified by the A.I.S.A. and to that end to conform to
the usual conditions.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
I hope your eyes may be restored. You are so identified with
the Sangha that your nominal withdrawal will mean no change.
