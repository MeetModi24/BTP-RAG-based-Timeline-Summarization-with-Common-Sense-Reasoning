301. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 24, 1945
CHI. KISHORELAL,
I have your letter. I understand about Surendraji. I should
like it if he pays me a brief visit. But in a religious or spiritual
sense, what is there in such a visit? Why should we spend even
one cowrie or one minute on the journey?
How nice it would be if some treatment could do a little
good to Gomati. No one has worked for twenty hours at a
meeting of the trustees or elsewhere, then why should I do so?
Do all the legends spread in this way?
No doubt, I did plenty
of work. I sat at a stretch for four hours at a meeting of the
trustees. But that was enough for me. There is such a difference
between four hours and twenty hours. Doctors or no doctors,
I should find it impossible to sit that long. Those days are gone.
Blessings from
BAPU
