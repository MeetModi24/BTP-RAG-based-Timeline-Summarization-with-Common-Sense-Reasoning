374. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR,
December 24, 1945
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I got your letter only today. I am leaving for Midnapore.
Write to me at the address given above. I have got a telegram
from the Aga Khan acknowledging [receipt of my letter1]. If a
letter follows, I will send you a copy of it. In the telegram he has
suggested an interview. What you say in your letter is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 4807. Courtesy: Shantikumar
N. Morarjee
