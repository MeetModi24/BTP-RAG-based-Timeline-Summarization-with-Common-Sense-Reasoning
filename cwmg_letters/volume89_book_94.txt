94. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
CALCUTTA,
August 22, 1947
CHI. BRAJKRISHNA,
What a letter? You must have a frank talk with Jaya-
prakash. Nothing can be settled through correspondence. If it is
all true, then the socialists cannot do anything.
Nandalal's watch seems useless. The back of the casing
comes off. I will see what can be done about it.
I have sent Saheb's papers to Panditji.
Has Sushila come? Tell her that I have intentionally not
written to her. I had thought that she would be coming here.
But if she feels that it is her duty to stay there for a few days,
she may do so.
I have sent your watch with Aruna.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 2499
