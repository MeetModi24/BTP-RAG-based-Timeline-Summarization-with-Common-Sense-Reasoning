587. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[After September 12, 1935]
There is hardly anything I can suggest in this matter. You
can win him [Kanam] over only through love and this love will
grow of its own accord. Anyway, it is an old story.
There is nothing to write down. Do on your own whatever
you can do.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 608
