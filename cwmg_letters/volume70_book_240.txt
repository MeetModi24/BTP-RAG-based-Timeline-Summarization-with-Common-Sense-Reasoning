240. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
ON THE TRAIN,
September 25, 1939
CHI. KAKA,
I have read the accompanying. We should get help. I have
assured Margarete. You should write in detail. Remind me
when we meet. Shankar must have met you. Wake up Chandan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7970
