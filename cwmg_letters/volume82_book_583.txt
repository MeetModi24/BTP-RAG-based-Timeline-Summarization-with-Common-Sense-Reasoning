583. LETTER TO DR. N. B. KHARE
SODEPUR,
January 16, 1946
BHAI KHARE,
I have your letter. I was delighted to see that you wrote in
Hindustani and signed in Urdu.
I am carefully watching what you are doing and what
you can do in Malaya-Burma. And I wish that everything is for
the good of the people there. What is the harm in Dr. Bidhan's
Medical Mission going to Malaya-Burma?
I understand what you have written about the Congress. I
do not want to write anything more about it.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 4871. Courtesy: Dr. N. B. Khare
