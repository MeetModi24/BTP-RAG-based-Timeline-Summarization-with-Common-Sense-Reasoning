466. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 14, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter. Here are my blessings to the bride and
the bridegroom. I am also enclosing yarn garlands for them
both. Other letters that I intend writing will also be enclosed
in this packet.
Kanu is enjoying himself and remains absorbed in his work.
He is full of idealism and will have, I feel, a bright future.
Kusum, too, is well enough. I hope you won't mind if she
cannot reach there before the 20th or 22nd, as we shall be in
Mysore till the 15th [of June] at any rate.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8489. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
