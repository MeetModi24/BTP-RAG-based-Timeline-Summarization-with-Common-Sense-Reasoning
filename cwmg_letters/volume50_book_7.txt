7. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
June 2, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA 2 ,
As you are left-handed, you should also try to perform every
function with your right hand. The human race increases
through births. Do you know how you came into being? Other
men and women came into being in the same way as you did.
Human beings owe their birth to their parents.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5772. Also C.W. 2996. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
