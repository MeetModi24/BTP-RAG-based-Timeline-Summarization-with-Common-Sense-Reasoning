516. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
September 12, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
We should tolerate other people's shortcomings, just as they
do ours. This should not be interpreted to mean that we may
let anyone intentionally break the rules.
Mark the difference
between the two!
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4404
