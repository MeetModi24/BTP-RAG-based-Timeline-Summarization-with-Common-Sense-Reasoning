316. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
June 9, 1937
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your letter. There is no need to bother much
about Giriraj.
I shall reach Segaon the day after tomorrow and plan at
any rate to stay there till the Congress session.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2449
