509. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
SEVAGRAM,
November 30, 1944
BHAI MUNSHI,
I have had a talk with Rajaji. The position is as I have
explained, namely, that the Princes can preserve their indepen-
dence and Pakistan can exist only if British power remains. How
can I tolerate this? It means that a part of India can be indepen-
dent, and the British rule will continue in the States ruled by the
Princes and in the Muslim majority Provinces. I can never be a
willing witness to this. I cannot understand how you can be.
You may, if you wish, meet Rajaji and seek clarification. I have
stated here the position as I have understood it. See that in
trying to secure something you do not lose everything. Go thor-
oughly into the details.
Sarala must be doing fine.
Blessings from
BAPU 2
