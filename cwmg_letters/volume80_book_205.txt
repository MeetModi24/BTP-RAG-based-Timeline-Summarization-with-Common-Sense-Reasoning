205. LETTER TO SRIKRISHNADAS JAJU
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 12, 1945
BHAI JAJUJI,
I have your letter. I approve of Dr. Rajan and Gopala-
swami (of Harijan Sevak Sangh). Let me know if there are
any others.
Blessings from
BAPU
SRIKRISHNADAS JAJU
AKHIL BHARATIYA CHARKHA SANGH
SEVAGRAM P. O., via WARDHA
