132. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
May 23, 1935
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I was distressed to see you so upset on the 21st. But it was
well that I learnt the extent of your weakness. You should now be
calm in mind and understand yourself. Talk it over with Kishore-
lal and Kakasaheb.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1877
