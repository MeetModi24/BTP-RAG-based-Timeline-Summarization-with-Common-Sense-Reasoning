321. LETTER TO MRS. S. SRINIVASA IYENGAR
July 3, 1935
DEAR SISTER,
As my right hand is resting I must dictate, for the left hand
can work but slowly. The forthcoming Sunday is a great event
for you. May God bless you and yours, and may He bless the
bride and the bridegroom. May He give him a long life of health,
strength and service.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: Ambujammal Papers.

Museum and Library
