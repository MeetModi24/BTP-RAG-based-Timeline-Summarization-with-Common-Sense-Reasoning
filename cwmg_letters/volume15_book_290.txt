290. LETTER TO F. C. GRIFFITH
BOMBAY,
May 23 [1919]
DEAR MR. GRIFFITH,
Could you please lend me or procure for me a loan of the Regu-
lations named under the Defence of India Act today? I will return
them towards evening.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original in Mahadev Desai's hand signed by Gandhiji : Bombay
Government Records: Bombay Commissioner of Police File No. 3001/H/19, p. 129
