397. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
[After May 5, 1924]
CHI. MANI,
It is a great relief to me that your letters now come regularly.
Have patience and self-confidence. Self-confidence will do more
good than medicines. Prabhudas has dropped the idea of going
to Panchgani. Chi. Radha is all right. She attends the evening
prayer. Kikibehn does not show any improvement. Chi. Girdhari
left for Ahmedabad yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MANIBEHN VALLABHBHAI PATEL
HAJIRA, via SURAT
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne, p. 15
