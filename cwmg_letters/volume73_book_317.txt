317. LETTER TO MAGANLAL AND MANJULA MEHTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 27, 1940
CHI. MAGAN AND MANJULA,
Everyday I mean to write to you but cannot do so. Urmila
is happy.
You should not mourn over Mother's death. Instead, you
should be grateful to God that she has found peace at last.
I myself cut Urmila's hair today. She looks much better
now. Her hair used to fall off a good deal.
It will take some time to get your house ready. But whenever
you decide to come, I will arrange for a separate kitchen for you.
Let me know in advance.
May your vow bear fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 1611.
Courtesy: Manjulabehn
M. Mehta
