416. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
August 24, 1941
CHI. BABUDI,
I do know, of course, that the happiness and security to be
found in living together are not to be found in staying alone.
That is why it is said that even the overcrowding of the dear
ones is to be welcomed. For Anand's diarrhoea, add one tea-
spoonful of lime-water or dill-water or the two together to the
milk. You can make lime-water even at home. If you add water
to pieces of limestone, there will be effervescence and you will
get clean, sky-blue water at the top. Decant and store it in a
bottle. Add a teaspoonful from this to the milk. Boil one tea-
spoonful of dill seeds in eight ounces of water, strain the water
and add one teaspoonful from this to the milk. Thus mix lime-
water and dill-water with the milk by turns. He is bound to
benefit. Do not give him any rotli for a day or two.
Blessings from
BAPU
