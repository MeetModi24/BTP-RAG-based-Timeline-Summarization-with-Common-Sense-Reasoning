111. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
[On or after June 18, 1921]
DEAR MR. ACHARIAR,
I have your telegram. The Committee has informed the
Punjab friends that they should continue to obey orders till the
All-India Congress Committee has decided upon the matter. The
A.I.C.C. meets at Lucknow on the 22nd July in the morning.
The question of formulating a scheme may be considered then. In
my opinion the time has not arrived for it as yet.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 7548
