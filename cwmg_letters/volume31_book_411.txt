411. LETTER TO A. T. GIDWANI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
September 9, 1926
MY DEAR GIDWANI,
I have your letter. Mr. Basu shall be suitably received on
his arrival. He has given me no intimation as yet.
I have forgotten all about Jugal Kishore. My apologies
to him. I think it is quite possible to take him in if he does
not restrict me to anything in particular. That is to say, does he
believe in khadi? And will he be prepared to work in the Khadi
Department? What would be his requirements? Is he married?
Yours sincerely,
ACHARYA A. T. GIDWANI
PREM MAHA VIDYALAYA
BRINDABAN
