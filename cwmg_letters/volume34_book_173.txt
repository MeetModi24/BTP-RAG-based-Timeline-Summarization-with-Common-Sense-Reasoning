173. LETTER TO LIES BURZAS
As AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 20, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I was pleased to receive your note through Sparrow.
The more I live the more real the doctrine of oneness of life
becomes to me. But the difficulty of practising the doctrine is also
growing with me instead of diminishing. Realization of the doctrine
seems to be impossible unless we could reduce the 'I' in us to
a complete cipher.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 12525
