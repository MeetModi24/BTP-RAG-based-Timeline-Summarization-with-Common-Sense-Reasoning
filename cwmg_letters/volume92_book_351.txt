351. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
Y.M.,
September 12, 1930
CHI. KANTA,
I got your letter. I will of course write to you regularly. It
will be another matter if I have to stop writing letters altogether.
You too may go on writing as you please. By all means eat
boiled vegetables instead of raw. When the vegetable for the day
is radish, gourd, pumpkin, tomato, etc., make a habit of eating a
little of it raw. If you cannot digest it, you may discontinue it.
Make only such changes as suit your body. When you get time,
improve your arithmetic. Is there no letter from Krishna at all?
Blessings from
BAPU
