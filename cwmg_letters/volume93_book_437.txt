437. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
SEGAON,
August 30, 1939
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I received your gift. It is not good that you are not getting
well. Write to me what Dr. Erulkar says. Bhabhi's fever should
abate now. Is that doctor still there?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANASUYABEHN
MOUNT PLEASANT ROAD
BOMBAY
[PS.]
If Shankerlal is there, tell him that I have sent a message
for Tirpur paper.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S. N. 3282
