703. LETTER TO MADAN MOHAN MALAVIYA
WARDHA,
December 10, 1928
BHAI SAHEB,
I was pained to learn from Ghanshyamdasji that you are
being indifferent to your health. You have vowed to live long.
You are not going before the attainment of freedom. Then, how is
it that you are not taking the necessary care of your health?
I shall meet you in Calcutta.
Your younger brother,
MOHANDAS
From a copy of the Hindi: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/39
