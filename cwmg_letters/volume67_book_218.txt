218. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
SEGAON,
July 9, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
I will read the Hindi book. It is not going to be easy. The
whole policy will have to be reconsidered. If even one false
step is taken, then there is serious danger of the whole structure
which is being built falling to pieces. If you come on Tuesday
we will think over it. Come in the afternoon if the time suits
you. Write to me and let me know, when you get the time, what
you thought after your talk with Tandonji. That will help me
and also help you to clarify your thoughts.
Ponder carefully over what I have written on education.
Blessings from
BAPU
