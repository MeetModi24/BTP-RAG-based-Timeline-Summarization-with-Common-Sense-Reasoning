313. LETTER TO KARNAD SADASHIVA RAO
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 24, 1928
MY DEAR SADASHIVA RAO,
I have your letter which I like very much. Let your daughters
put themselves in touch with me. They have got to be brave,
if they will not become worse than purdanusheens . For those who
will have the butterfly existence are, in my opinion, worse than
purdanusheens. And those who will become real servants of the nation
have to accept voluntarily poverty as a blessing and not a mere
tolerable position.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SADASHIVA RAO KARNAD
KODAIBAIL
MANGALORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 13202
