398. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
August 19, 1932
CHI. MIRA,
So you are again in your den. I hope you will regain your
lost strength and vitality. And you will be anxious about nothing.
Simply concentrate on your spinning and such studies as you can
easily undertake. We are all doing well. Love from us all. God
[be] with you.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6233. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9699
