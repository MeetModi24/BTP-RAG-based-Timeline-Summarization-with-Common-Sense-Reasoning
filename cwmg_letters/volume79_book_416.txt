416. LETTER TO ALLADI K. KRISHNASWAMY IYER
SEVAGRAM,
March 14, 1945
DEAR SIR ALLADI,
I thank you for the pains you have taken. May God cure
you quickly and spare you for many years to come. Yes, Vaidya-
nath Iyer has a heart of gold. He is a rare servant of the
people.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR ALLADI KRISHNASWAMY IYER
ADVOCATE
MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
