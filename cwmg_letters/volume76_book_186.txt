186. LETTER TO HANUMANTHA RAO
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 26, 1942
BHAI HANUMANTHA RAO,
I have only now been able to reach your letter of May 2.
I feel you ought to have written with greater frankness. If
your heart is fully cleansed, I think you should clearly say some-
thing like "For a lapse in the performance of public duty I have
decided to . . ." I think it is your duty to make some such state-
ment. You can still make amends even by issuing a simple
statement.
I hope you will be able to read my handwriting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
