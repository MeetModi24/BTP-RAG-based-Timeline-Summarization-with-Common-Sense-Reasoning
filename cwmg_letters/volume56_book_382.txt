382. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA AND RAMAKRISHNA
CHANDIWALA
December 19, 1933
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
How is it that I have received so far no letter or wire?
I
hope that a suitable house has been found and your health is
improving. Send me all the news. It will do if I get a post-
card daily and a wire when necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI RAMAKRISHNA OR SHRIKRISHNA,
Discussions should be avoided in the presence of Brijkrishna.
Stick to Doctors Ansari and Sen.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2404
1 Addressee's son
