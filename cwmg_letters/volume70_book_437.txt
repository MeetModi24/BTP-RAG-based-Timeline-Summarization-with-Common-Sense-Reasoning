437. LETTER TO BARIN GHOSH
November 24, 1939
BARIN GHOSH,
The difference about the charkha is not immaterial. My
whole life is wound up with it. If you cannot support it, you
cannot whole-heartedly support non-violence. And of what use
am I without non-violence?
Yours sincerely,
M. K.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
