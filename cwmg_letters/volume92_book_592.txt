592. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 20, 1932
CHI. TARAMATI,
I have your letter. When you write to Mathuradas tell him
that I have sent him a letter which he must have received.
Ba has come there. She is staying at Mani Bhavan. Manilal
and Devdas are also there. Go and meet all of them. I do not
know if you have met Manilal's wife Sushila. She is also in
Bombay. At present she is in Dosibai's hospital.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI TARAMATI MATHURADAS
21 MINT ROAD, 2ND FLOOR
FORT, BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
