79. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
January 31, 1934
BHAI JIVANJI,
I got your letter. Please give the accompanying letter to
Kakasaheb if he is there.
I understand what you say about Durga. I shall know in a
day or two whether I shall be going to Belgaum or not. Even
if I go, it will be around February 25 or 26 or even later. Will
Durga wait till then? I will certainly try to secure permission
if I do go to Belgaum. But it is possible that I may not get
it. Such risks have to be taken. If they do give me the per-
mission, probably they will do so even if Durga has seen him in
the mean while. But one can't be sure. I stop here as some
visitors are coming.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9936. Also C.W. 6911. Courtesy:
Jivanji D. Desai
