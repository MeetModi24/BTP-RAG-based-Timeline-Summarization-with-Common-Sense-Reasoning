64. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
August 3, 1939
CHI. KAKA,
The letter for Maganbhai was posted promptly.
Come over at 3 o'clock today for giving me your report about
Meher Ashram.
A bill for a fairly large sum has been sent by Wanless [Sana-
torium] regarding that gentleman. Ask for it from me.
I have written to Jivanji inquiring about the Autobiography.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7971
