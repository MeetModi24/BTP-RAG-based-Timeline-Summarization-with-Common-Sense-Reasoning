180. LETTER TO NATHURAM TRIKAMDAS
July 8, 1932
CHI. NATHURAM,
One who does not share in the work cannot be called a
partner.
There is certainly himsa in castrating a bull.
The sacred thread worn by one cannot be used as belt for the
spinning-wheel.
One who uses authority over others without consulting them
is an autocrat.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
