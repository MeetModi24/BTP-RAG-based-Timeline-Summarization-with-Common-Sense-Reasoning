31. LETTER TO P. C. GHOSH
October 16, 1941
MY DEAR PRAFULLA,
You had no business to lose weight and develop fever. Anyway
I hope you are out of the wood now. I had a letter from Suresh
and I replied. Sardar and others are due about 20th. All well.
Love.
BAPU
(M. K. GANDHI)
[Ps.]
Your record in fine spinning is splendid.
From a photostat: G.N. 3784
