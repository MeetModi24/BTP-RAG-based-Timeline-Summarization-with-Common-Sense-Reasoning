284. LETTER TO SOMABHAI PATEL
March 24, 1932
CHI. SOMABHAI,
Never mind if Narandas has made a prisoner of you. Improve
your health. Narrate your experiences to me. How did you
spend the time? Is there any news about Bhansali? How much
weight did you lose?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9220
