782. LETTER TO AMBUJAMMAL
July 9, 1934
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your letter. Today is my silence-day. We are all in
Karachi, in a house facing the sea. The air is very good.
Bombs and other things are only apparent causes of death.
Death must arrive at the appointed hour. And I am convinced
that no man will ever die until he has played his allotted part
in the world. Why then should we be distressed over a bomb-
throwing? Yes, we should have compassion for the person who
throws the bomb and we should try to reform him.
I hope your health is perfedy normal now. Harijan welfare,
Hindi and khadi are the three activities open to you and nobody
can have any objection to that.
Blessings from
BAPU
