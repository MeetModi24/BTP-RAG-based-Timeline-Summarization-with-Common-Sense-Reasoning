478. LETTER TO H. CLAYTON
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 11, 1927
DEAR MR. CLAYTON,
I thank you for your letter of the 6th June.
I note the care with which you seem to have gone into the
allegations. I hope to write further as soon as I have heard from
Mr. Thakkar.
I shall take care to prevent publication of any portion of the
minutes of the Committee meeting referred to in your letter and
of which you have favoured me with a copy.
Yours sincerely,
H. CLAYTON, ESQ.
COMMISSIONER,
MUNICIPAL CORPORATION
BOMBAY
