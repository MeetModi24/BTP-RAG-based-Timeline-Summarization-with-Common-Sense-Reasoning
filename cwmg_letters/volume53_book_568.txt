568. LETTER TO KALICHARAN
February 26, 1933
BHAI KALICHARANJI,
I have your letter. I know all that is written about me. I
do not think there is any remedy for it except remaining unper-
turbed. I hope that nobody believes it at all. And how can my
denial have any effect on those who believe it? Anyway I have
written something in this connection in the current issue of Harijan.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8039
