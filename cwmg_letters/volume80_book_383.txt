383. LETTER TO MANGALDAS
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 31, 1945
BHAI MANGALDAS,
This is my acknowledgment. I have received your draft
and it will be officially credited to the account.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHAH MANGALDAS'S FIRM
PUBLISHER AND DISTRIBUTOR
BHAGATALAV
SURAT
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
