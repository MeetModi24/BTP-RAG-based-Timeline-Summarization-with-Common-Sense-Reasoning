308. LETTER TO MANU H. GANDHI
Silence day
CHI. MANUDI,
Do you ever forget eating as you do writing to me? Do you
write regularly to Ba? I hope you know that because of Ba's constant
nursing, you had got up from your death-bed. So never forget Ba.
1
From the contents it appears that this and the following letters were
written in 1933; vide Vol. LIII, pp. 123- and Vol. LV, p. 108.
