699. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
March 25, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your postcard. Accommodation has been found for
Tari at Panchgani. She can go there by the end of this month
or any time she wants.
I understand about Ba. I have placed her in your hands.
It is a shocking thing about your papers. But why feel dis-
heartened by it? Examination is only a pretext. It will most
probably be held in October.
Harijan will be published from Ahmedabad from the 5th of April.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the
Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
