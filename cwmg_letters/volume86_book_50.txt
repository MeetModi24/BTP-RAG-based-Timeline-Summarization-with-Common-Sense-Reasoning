50. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
October 26, 1946
CHI. AMRIT,
You do not acknowledge my letter in yours of today. I
have never missed a day.
I do hope your cut will heal by the time you are ready to
fly and that you will be able to leave without any harm to the
body.
We leave for Calcutta on Monday.
Herewith a letter from Shanta.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 4183. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7819
