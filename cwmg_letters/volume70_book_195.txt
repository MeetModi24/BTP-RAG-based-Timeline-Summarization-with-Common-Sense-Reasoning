195. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 8, 1939
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
You must have got my letter.
You can go to the Delhi
dairy in October. Ishwardas has written to me. If you want
to try him for ten days or longer, please do so. He is a capable
man. I met Sardarji at Simla. Mahadev has gone to Ranchi
to see Rajendra Babu. He may come here tomorrow. We are
all well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1924
