374. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
August 12, 1941
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. I am not under a misapprehension. You
have yourself said that what I decide will be the right thing.
This is not enough. If you are not clear in your intention my
decision should be regarded as invalid. If Draupadi also does not
fully agree, this gift should be considered tainted. No sacrifice
can endure unless backed by renunciation. I have only raised a
moral issue.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
between pp. 302 and 303
