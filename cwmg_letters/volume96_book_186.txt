186. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
July 9, 1914
MY DEAR FRIEND,
No letters from you for the last 2 days. Manilal says there should
be with you my shoes and neckties, etc. I have not a collar left nor
more than one tie. Please search and if the shoes are there you may
get them stretched.
Yesterday's function was good - I acted for you and against you.
I am writing this in the Durban train. Am paying the passage here
and sending all my luggage here so that there may be only a little
to carry.
With love,
Yours,
DAS
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
