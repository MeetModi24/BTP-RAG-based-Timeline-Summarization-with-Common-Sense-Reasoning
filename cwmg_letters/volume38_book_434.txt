434. LETTER TO V. L. PHADKE
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, January 19, 1929
BHAI MAMA,
I got your letter and that of Maneklal Gandhi. I approve
of Jaishukhlal Mehta's name. We can add Lakshmidas's. But it
would be good if Jaishukhlal joins. I cannot think of any other
name.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3823
