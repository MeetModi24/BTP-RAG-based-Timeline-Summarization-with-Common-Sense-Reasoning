351. LETTER TO RUKMINIDEVI BAZAJ
December 22, 1932
CHI. RUKMINI,
I had got your letter in due time. How nice it would be if
you maintain the slow improvement in your health and by and
by become completely all right? Do you read any newspapers?
Or is it that you get no time at all to read anything? If you read
newspapers, which ones do you read? I suppose you have to speak
in Hindi there most of the time. Do you read anything else? Do
you visit any people? Have you made any friends?
All of us are well. These days I spend most of my time in
Harijan work.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9149
