47. LETTER TO V. P. LIMAYE
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 28, 1945
BHAI LIMAYE,
I have your letter. My blessings are certainly with you in
your work of Nayee Talim. The success of this venture is full
of significance.
Blessings from
BAPU
PROF. V. P. LIMAYE
PIPANE
SASWAD P. O.
POONA DISTRICT
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
