261. LETTER TO U. GOPALA MENON
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 26, 1931
MY DEAR GOPALA MENON,
I have your letter. I know all about the case of M.P. Nara-
yana Menon. I had a letter addressed to Lord Irwin by some
missionary friend given to me to be delivered to Lord Irwin which
I gladly did and asked him to endeavour to secure Narayana
Menon's release. I have no doubt about the justice of the case.
Unfortunately the talk with Lord Irwin took place only two days
before he left Delhi. It is highly likely therefore that he was able
to do nothing. Now as you say Sir. C. P. Ramaswami Iyer knows
the case well you have to knock at his door continually.
I cannot give you any definite opinion upon the proposed
Temple Entry Satyagraha unless I know what the other side has
to say. The objection you have raised is certainly worthy of con-
sideration. Beyond this I do not feel able to go.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. U. GOPALA MENON, B.A., B.L.
CALICUT
