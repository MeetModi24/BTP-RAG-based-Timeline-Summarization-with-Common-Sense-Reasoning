409. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
December 28, 1932
MY DEAR GHANSHYAMDAS,
The Secretary of the Friends of India Society, London, writes
to me saying that she has sent you a cheque or draft for £42-0-
being the takings during the Fast Week. You will please tell
me whether the amount has been received.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
C.W. 7911. Courtesy: G. D. Birla. Also S.N. 18832
