696. LETTER TO OM PRAKASH GUPTA
SIMLA,
July 9, 1945
CHI. OM PRAKASH,
I hear that you are again depressed. Only he is a man who
adjusts himself to changing situations and remains uninvolved and
detached. I do not know when God will take me to the Ashram.
I hope of course that I shall be coming soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
