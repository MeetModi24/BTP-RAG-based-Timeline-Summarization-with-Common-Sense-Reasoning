177. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1945
CHI. BABUDI,
I have your letter. I shall be very happy if you get well
soon. I am leaving on the 30th. I hope to hear about you
even during my tour. Chimanlal is accompanying me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10064. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokha-
wala
