80. LETTER TO G. A. NATESAN
21-24, COURT CHAMBERS,
CORNER RISSIK & ANDERSON STREETS,
JOHANNESBURG,
June 2, 19111
DEAR MR. NATESAN,
This is to introduce to you Mr. R. M. Sodha. As you know
Mr. Sodha is one of the staunchest passive resisters. If he travels
towards your side, may I ask you please to introduce Mr. Sodha
to our leaders there? Mr. Sodha is the husband of the celebrated
Mrs. Sodha who was persecuted by the Transvaal Government.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original in Gandhiji's hand: C. W. 3422. Courtesy: Reva-
shanker Sodha
