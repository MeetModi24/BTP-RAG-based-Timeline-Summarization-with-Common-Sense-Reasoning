184. LETTER TO J. B. PENNINGTON
CAMP KALAKANKAR,
November 14, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
However much we may differ, your letters are always welcome.
They serve as a tonic. I am reproducing your letter in full in
Young India with a short reply of which I send you an advance
copy herewith so as not to keep you waiting for the reply for a
fortnight which it would take before it is printed as I am
sending it to Young India from an out-of-the-way place in my U.P.
tour. May you live long enough to send me many more letters
still. There is no reason why you should not live to see India
reach her cherished goal.
Yours sincerely,
J. B. PENNINGTON, ESQ.
NATIONAL HOMECROFT ASSOCIATION LIMITED
CARDIFF
From a photostat: S.N. 15249
