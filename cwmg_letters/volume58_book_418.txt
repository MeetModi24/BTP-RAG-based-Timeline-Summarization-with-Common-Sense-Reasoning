418. LETTER TO VIMALA JOSHI
August 30, 1934
CHI. VIMU,
Why do you write with a pencil? Write with ink and make
each letter as beautiful as a carefully drawn picture. Surely you
can be in no hurry to finish your letter. It is good that all of
you have settled down there for the present.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 302
