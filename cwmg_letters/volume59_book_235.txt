235. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
October 17, 1934
CHI. MANUDI,
You have now become Bhai's instructor, it seems. Teach
him spinning on the takli, etc., properly. Did you learn the new
method of spinning on the takli which is being followed here?
I am picking it up. I can now spin 40 rounds in half an hour.
I hope to be able to spin more. If you know this new method,
teach the same to Bhai. Write to me about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1534. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
