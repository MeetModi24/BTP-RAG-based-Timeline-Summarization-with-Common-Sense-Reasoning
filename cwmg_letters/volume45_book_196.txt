196. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
February 2, 1931
CHI. PREMA,
This is just to drop a line to you. I have before me only one
sheet of your letter. The rest seem to have got mixed up with other
papers. I shall get them by and by.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10253
