494. LETTER TO BHAGWANDIN
March 30, 1935
BHAI BHAGWANDIN,
I saw your letter to Avadhesh. Many young men commit
the error that he did. Avadhesh has realized his mistake and
repented, so we should not lay it against him any more. I shall
be writing so in Harijan.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 736
