98. LETTER TO RAMANAND CHATTERJEE
BORSAD,
March 18, 1930
DEAR RAMANAND BABU,
Under great stress I was able today to scribble the en-
closed You may do what you like with it. I hope you are giving
the movement your unreserved support and blessing.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: C.W. 9287. Courtesy: Sita Devi
