74. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
WARDHA,
October 29, 1935
CHI. MRIDULA,
I should not really forgive you. But when many undeserving
people escape from me, what is surprising about your escaping?
Do come over.
Why should Gautam and Vikram also not spend a day
here? Tempt them to come if you can give the place a good
certificate after your visit.
You can have the reply to the second part of your letter
after coming here.
Khurshedbehn will be here.
1 & 2 Addressee's daughter and son
3 Addressee's brother
4 Khurshed Naoroji, grand-daughter of Dadabhai Naoroji
43
I had received the telegram of Diwali greetings from your
parents.
I am glad to read that Mother's health is improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
