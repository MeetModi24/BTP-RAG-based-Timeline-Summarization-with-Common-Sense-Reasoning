199. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
March 3, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I got your postcard and letter. You say that you have no-
thing to write about, in the same way as children do. This is not
right. You will be able to fill pages even if you describe your expe-
riences. Think, and write.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1829
