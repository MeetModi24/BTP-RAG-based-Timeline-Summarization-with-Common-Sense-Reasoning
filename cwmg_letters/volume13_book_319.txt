319. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
BETTIAH,
[After May 22, 1917]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
There is a letter from Revashankar in which he asks for
permission to send Chhotalal and also for some monetary help. I
have told him that he should write to Chi. Maganlal about money
and that he should send Chhotalal only if he is himself willing to
come.
The accompanying papers will show that the situation here
may take a serious turn any moment. Dr. Hariprasad and others
who are ready to come here must give some time to learning Hindi.
I think I have already written about this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 6358
