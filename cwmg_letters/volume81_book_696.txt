696. LETTER TO HUMAYUN KABIR
October 19, 1945
BHAI HUMAYUN ,
I had your book Men and Rivers with me. Khurshedbehn
took it for reading. She especially recommended it to me. I
read it with great interest. I recognize your ability to write
novels.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
