251. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 20, 1940
CHI. SHARMA,
I got your letter; also the book. I had asked Su[shila]behn to
send an acknowledgment. The book was needed for Shankaran
who is working in the dispensary. I shall expect one letter from you
every month.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 286
