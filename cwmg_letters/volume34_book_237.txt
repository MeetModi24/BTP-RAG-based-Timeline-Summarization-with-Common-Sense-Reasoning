237. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
August 1 [1927]
CHI. MIRA,
I have your beautifully written Hindi letter. Your time-
table seems to be crowded. There is no breathing time between
different periods. I suppose it is the same with all. It is quite
capable of execution, if one concentrates and does not fuss or
waste in idle thought or talk.
You know the plight of Gujarat. Rain has done very great
damage and the Ashram too has come in for its due share. I
have a telegram too that Kantilal who was keeping accounts was
drowned in the river. It appears to be a case of suicide. I have
no definite particulars yet.
I shall be absent from Bangalore for four days from tomorrow,
returning Friday evening.
No more this time.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5256. Courtesy: Mirabehn
