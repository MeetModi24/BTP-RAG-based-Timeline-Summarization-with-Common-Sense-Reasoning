430. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI AND SAROJ NANAVATI
June 4, 1945
CHI. RAIHANA AND CHI. SAROJ,
I got today the postcard written by you two. I am glad that
Grandmother has passed away. She has settled her accounts, but
suffered much while doing so. The fact that she exacted service
from you both earned you merit. But wasn't that at her cost?
One should not wish to earn such merit. I, therefore, regard
Grandmother's passing away a very good thing indeed from every
point of view.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9684
