45. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
January 25, 1934
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have not heard from you recently. I feel worried because
of the terrible earthquake. Where is Jayaprakash? How are
you both? Following the earthquake I had expected a detailed
letter from you. I take it both of you must be busy with relief
work just now. Is Rajeshwar at Kashi? I have asked you in a
previous letter to send me his address, etc. I have also inquired
from which month I should send him the amounts. I will ar-
range to send them after I get your reply.
I have also asked you to let me know what you have done
regarding other expenses.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3442
