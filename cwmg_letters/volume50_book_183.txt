183. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI H. SHAH
July 8, 1932
DEAR PUNJABHAI,
To one who is sinless, death is a bed of happiness. When we
have taken to heart the lesson that birth and death are one and
the same thing, we should know that we have freed ourselves
from the cycle of birth and death.
Blessings from
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 201
