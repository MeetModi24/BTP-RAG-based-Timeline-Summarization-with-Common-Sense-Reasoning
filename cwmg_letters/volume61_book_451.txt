451. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
August 5, 1935
CHI. AMALA,
You are unnecessarily suspicious. And now that you are
grandmother of five pupils, you can afford to ignore Mahadev,
me and a whole host of lovers. Slivers, I have asked Satisbabu
to send you at once. I hope you have got them by this time.
I am glad you are feeling settled there.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI AMALABEHN SPIEGEL
SANTINIKETAN, via BOLPUR
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
