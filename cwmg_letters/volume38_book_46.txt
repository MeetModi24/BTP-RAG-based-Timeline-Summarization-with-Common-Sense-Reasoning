46. LETTER TO VIOLET
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 10, 1928
MY DEAR VIOLET,
I have your letter. Hinduism should certainly be purged of
all the evil and superstition that have crept into it.
Nothing
then need be a substitute for it. It is, in my opinion, an all-
embracing and all-sufficing religion.
I hope your aunt is quite well now.
Yours sincerely,
[MRS. VIOLET
C/O] MISS BABA GUNASEKERA
55 HAMPDEN LANE, WELLAWATTE, COLOMBO
From a photostat: S.N. 12994
