89. LETTER TO RAGHUVANSH GAUR
October 29, 1941
BHAI RAGHUVANSH,
What Prabhavatibehn wrote to you was not correct. I never
got your letter. I had asked Prabhavati to reply on behalf of Raj-
kumari that she herself would not be able to offer help. And you
should not go abegging like this. If you do not get money from
friends, then leave Patna and do some work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 142
