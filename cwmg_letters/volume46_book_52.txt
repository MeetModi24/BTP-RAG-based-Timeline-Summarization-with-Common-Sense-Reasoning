52. LETTER TO JAMNADAS GANDHI
BORSAD,
April 29, 1931
CHI. JAMNADAS,
How is it that you do not write to me at all? I cannot write
to you, but I do think about you. What is your present state of
mind? How are Father and Mother? How is the school? Write
to me and describe some of your experiences in jail. What kind
of person is the new ruler? Ask Purshottam to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9291. Courtesy: Jamnadas Gandhi
