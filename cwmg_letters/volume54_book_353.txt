353. LETTER TO AMULYADHAN ROY
April 5, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you.
My position is quite clear. The Yeravda Pact is a solemn
document. It cannot be altered except with the consent of all the
parties interested in it.
So far as I am concerned, I have seen nothing warranting
a change in my opinion.
Of course you are at liberty to publish my reply, if you
wish it.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. AMULYADHAN Roy
GIRISH GHOSH LANE
GHUSURI
P. O. BELURMATH (HOWRAH)
