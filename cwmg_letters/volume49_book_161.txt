161. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
February 20, 1932
BHAI VITHALDAS,
I got your letter. Take rest till your body has completely
recovered its strength. Do not be in the least impatient. The
reason why khadi sales have gone down this time is as clear
as daylight. But do not worry over that. "It is useless to sorrow
over what God ordains."
Sardar is very well.
Blessings from
BAPU
