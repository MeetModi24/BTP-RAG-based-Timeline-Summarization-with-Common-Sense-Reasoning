420. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 1, 1930
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
Your letter. Don't you lose heart like this. If the climate
of Vijapur suits you, most certainly go there; or go to Bardoli
if that suits you. And during summer you can stay at Tadikhet.
Your health must improve. If you are drawn towards Vijapur,
come here and we shall discuss about Morvi too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2374
