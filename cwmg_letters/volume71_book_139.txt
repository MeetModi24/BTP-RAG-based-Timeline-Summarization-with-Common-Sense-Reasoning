139. LETTER TO DR. CHOITHRAM GIDWANI
[Before January 18, 1940]
Mahatma Gandhi in a communication to Dr. Choithram P. Gidwani
has contradicted the statement of the Secretary of the Sind Hindu
Mahasabha that he (Mahatmaji) had called Mr. Virumal Begraj and Mr.
Bhojraj Ajwani, President and Secretary of the Hindu Mahasabha, Sukkur,
respectively, to Wardha to hear from them the Hindu viewpoint on the
Sukkur riots. Mahatma Gandhi states that he had invited nobody from Sind
to meet him.
The Bombay Chronicle, 19-1-1940
