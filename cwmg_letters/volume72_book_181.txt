181. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 4, 1940
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I understand about Bal.
You may bring the children whenever you like. The days
are hot but the nights have become pleasant.
Blessings from
BAPU
SETH GHANSHYAMDAS BIRLA
BIRLA HOUSE
MOUNT PLEASANT ROAD
BOMBAY
From Hindi: C.W. 8038. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
1 Kunvarji Parekh, husband of Rami, Harilal Gandhi's daughter
