423. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
December 26, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER RAIHANA,
I had no idea that you were confined to bed. I thought:
'Raihana has now forgotten Bapu.' As for me I had no time
when I could write. It is God's grace that you are now all
right.
I had a long letter from Hamida. I have replied to her.
Kamaladevi and I talked about many things. I have often
thought about you during the tour. I yearned to hear you sing:
"Arise, awake, O Traveller", but how could I hope for such
good fortune? I even felt the wish to join your band singing
bhajans. My party includes Kisan and Jamnalalji's Om. Where
has Father gone? Give my sincere greetings, vandemataram,
love, etc., etc., to him and to Mother. Blessings to Saroj and
you, and a kiss to Kamalmian.
BAPU
[PS.]
I hope you read Harijan regularly.
