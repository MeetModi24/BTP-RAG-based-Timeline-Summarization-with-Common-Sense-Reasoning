496. LETTER TO J. W. PETAVEL
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 15, 1927
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I see with much pleasure you will not give in, and if you
will continue to hammer away, it is likely that some day I shall
be able to see eye to eye with you and then do what you want
me to.
I have now got your book. I shall go through the portions
referred to by you.
Yours sincerely,
J. W. PETAVEL, ESQ.
BAGBAZAR, CALCUTTA
