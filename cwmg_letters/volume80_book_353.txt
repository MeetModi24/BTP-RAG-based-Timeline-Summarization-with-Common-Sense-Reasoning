353. LETTER TO SUSHILA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 29, 1945
CHI. SUSHILA,
You must firmly refuse to marry the person whom your
parents want you to marry.
Marry the man with whom you are so much in love after
informing your parents. Do not seek my blessings. My advice
and hope would be that both of you should devote yourselves
to service and forget each other. I know it is difficult.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
[C/O] SHRI GURUPRASAD SRIVASTAVA
R. M. S. OFFICE
CHARBAGH JUNCTION
LUCKNOW
