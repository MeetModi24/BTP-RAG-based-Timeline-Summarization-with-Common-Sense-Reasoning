98. LETTER TO BHAGWATICHARAN SHUKLA
POONA,
November 11, 1945
CHI. BHAGWATI,
Now marriages cannot be performed in the Ashram. I can
arrange at some other place in Wardha outside the Ashram.
I
shall not be able to attend the function. The ceremony will be
performed by a Harijan, as was done in the case of Tendulkarji.
It will be better if it is done according to the Civil Marriage Act.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAGWATICHARAN SHUKLA
"NAGPUR TIMES" OFFICE
NAGPUR
