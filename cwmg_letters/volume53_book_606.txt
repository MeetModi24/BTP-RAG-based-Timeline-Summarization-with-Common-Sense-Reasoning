606. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
March 3, 1933
MY DEAR ANAND,
I have your letter.
They say dreams go by contraries.
Naturally, therefore, there was nobody and no prisoner and no
trial.
The more we merely think of the duty in front of us the
greater is the satisfaction from it. Absorption in the immediate
duty is like brahmacharya, peace-giving and invigorating.
Love.
BAPU
