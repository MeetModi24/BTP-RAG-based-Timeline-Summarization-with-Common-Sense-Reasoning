122. LETTER TO H. W. B. MORENO
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 7, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I shall certainly deal with the subject
in the pages of Young India as you desire and therefore need not
give you a detailed reply. The original of which you have en-
closed a copy was never received.
Yours sincerely,
H. W. B. MORENO, ESQ.
GENERAL PRESIDENT
THE ANGLO-INDIAN LEAGUE
9 MARSDEN STREET, CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 17060
