33. LETTER TO LAJPAT RAI
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 21, 1927
DEAR LALAJI,
I do not know whether this letter will reach you. I simply
passed on to you about the trust what I felt. I know that you will
do whatever is proper. I surely do not need any explanation.
I hope you are gaining strength day by day and taking rest
without being concerned with the events that may be going on
about you. I would like you to do what Asquith did in the midst
of the War. He took, when his health threatened to break down,
a fortnight's cruise in the Mediterranean and cut himself off from
all communications. We may not afford to take any such cruise,
but we can afford to retire unto ourselves untouched by the en-
vironment.
Yours sincerely,
LALA LAJPAT RAI
NATIONAL LIBERAL CLUB
WHITEHALL PLACE
LONDON S.W.I
From a photostat: S.N. 14166
