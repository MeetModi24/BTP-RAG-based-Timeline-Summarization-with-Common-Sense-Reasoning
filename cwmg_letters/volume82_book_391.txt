391. LETTER TO DHARMA DEV SHASTRI
MAHISHADAL,
December 26, 1945
SHRI DHARMA DEV SHASTRI,
Is it true that you have fallen ill? Get well soon. Why
did you fall ill?
SHRI DHARMA DEV SHASTRI
DARSHAN KESARI
ASHOK ASHRAM
KALSI, DISTT. DEHRA DUN
