407. LETTER TO JUGATRAM DAVE
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 9, 1930
BHAI JUGATRAM,
Your letter is full of information. So far, everything seems to
be going as it should. We are being tested well in every respect.
As long as you are free, write to me regularly. The fact that all
the activities are going on normally is a sign of great efficiency.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2686
