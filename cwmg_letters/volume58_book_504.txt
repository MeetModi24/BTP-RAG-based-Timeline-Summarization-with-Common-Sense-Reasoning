504. LETTER TO . . . 1
September 14, 1934
CHI. . . . ,
I have your letter. You must not give way to despair.
You
must resolve to exercise self-control till a suitable partner is found.
You must not fail.
. . .
CALCUTTA
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
