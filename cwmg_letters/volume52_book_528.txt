528. LETTER TO P. V. SESHU AIYAR
January 8, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. Whilst your proposals are good
in themselves, the propaganda for temple-entry has to go on side
by side with the work suggested by you.
I am glad you wrote to Sjt. Kelappan.
It was a pleasure to see your daughter with you. Please give
her my regards.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. V. SESHU AIYER
PERUVAMBA
PALGHAT
