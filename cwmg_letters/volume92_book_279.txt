279. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
February 22, 1930
CHI. DEVDAS,
You will have received my reply to your wire. Now I am
awaiting your letter. A letter to the Viceroy will go from here
on the 2nd. You will see everything if you are here at the
time. It will not be surprising at all if they arrest me
any day after the 4th. Let those who wish to join you in the
march have patience for the present. My desire, if I myself lead
the march, is to keep you with me. Alternatively, I may send
you in the first batch and try to follow after all of you have
been arrested. They are sure to arrest me to prevent all this.
In that case you should court arrest. And after that most of
the Ashram inmates should get themselves arrested. I should
like you to be here at least to enable me to give you a comp-
lete picture of this plan. Manilal also will be here by about the
27th. Motilal and Jawaharlal are coming.
You may now do what you think best.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Chhaganlal Gandhi Papers. Courtesy:
Sabarmati Sangrahalaya
