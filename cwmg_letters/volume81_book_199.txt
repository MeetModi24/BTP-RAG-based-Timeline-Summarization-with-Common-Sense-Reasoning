199. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
August 14, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your letter. You (and Mama, if you think he is fit
and can be brought) should come to Bombay on Monday. I will
be observing silence but do not worry about it. I shall spare a
few minutes somehow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
