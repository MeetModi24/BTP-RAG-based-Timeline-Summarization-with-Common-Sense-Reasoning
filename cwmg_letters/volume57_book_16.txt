16. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
January 19, 1934
CHI. PRABHAVATI ,
You do not receive my letters at all. What does that mean?
I wrote three letters to you which you have not acknowledged.
I have written to Jayaprakash also. I shall now be going there
in a short time. We shall meet then. I am well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3441
