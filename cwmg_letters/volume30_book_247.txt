247. LETTER TO P. S. VARIER
[On or after April 1, 1926]
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter enclosing a copy of your Ashtangashareer
for which I thank you. I should inform you that Young India is
not a reviewing paper. Occasionally it notices books that are
of superlative value and concern the subjects with which the
writings in Young India are generally occupied.
Yours,
P. S. VARIER
KOTTAKKAL
