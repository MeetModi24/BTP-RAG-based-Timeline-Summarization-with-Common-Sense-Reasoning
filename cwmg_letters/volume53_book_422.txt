422. LETTER TO B. R. AMBEDKAR
February 16, 1933
DEAR DR. AMBEDKAR,
I thank you for your letter of the 12th instant enclosing your
statement. I did not receive anything from the Associated Press,
but I saw it in the daily Press. I hope you saw my reply. I
wish that you could appreciate my viewpoint.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20265
