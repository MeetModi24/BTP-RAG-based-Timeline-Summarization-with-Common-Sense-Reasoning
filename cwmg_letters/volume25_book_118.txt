118. LETTER TO RUKMINI GANDHI
Wednesday [September 17, 1924]
CHI. RUKHI,
I had been waiting for your letter all this while. Whatever
you may do take care of your health. You should build up your
health nicely while you are there. Do not neglect Sanskrit. I
am glad to know that Moti Ba is now quite well. Give her my best
respects.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. RUKMINI GANDHI
C/O SHRI KHUSHALBHAI GANDHI
OPPOSITE MIDDLE SCHOOL
NAVU PARU
RAJKOT
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 6095. Courtesy: Radhabehn Choudhri
