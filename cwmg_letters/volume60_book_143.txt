143. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
January 28, 1935
CHI. VASUMATI,
My letters appear to trail behind you. I am writing about
the ink directly to Chandubhai.
You must get ready by the 10th February to go to Madras.
You should earmark five years for Bochasan. I only wish you
would settle somewhere.
Today we are leaving for Wardha.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. VASUMATIBEHN
C/O RASIKLAL BHOGILAL
VISNAGAR, via MEHSANA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9394. Also C.W. 639. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
