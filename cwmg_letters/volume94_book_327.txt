327. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
January 18, 1945
MY DEAR HENRY,
I have an unfinished part of your letter of November 4, 1944. The
other part cannot be had.
From your letter I gather that you are correctly reported. And that
is, in my opinion, damaging.
I do not recall your previous letter or letters. But there was no
intentional negligence. In any event I never said anything against you
publicly. On the contrary, on suspicion arising, my first approach was

invited him to spend the summer in Mysore State.
