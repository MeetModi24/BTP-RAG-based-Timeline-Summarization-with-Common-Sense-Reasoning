146. LETTER TO D. B. BARVE
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 8, 1937
I thank you for your letter and your suggestions. I shall think
[of] all of them in my talks with the Ministers in charge of Vil-
lage Industries.
M . K . GANDHI
From a copy: B. G. Kher Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
