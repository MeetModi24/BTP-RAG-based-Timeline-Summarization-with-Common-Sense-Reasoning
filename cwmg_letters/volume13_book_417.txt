417. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI VAKIL
RANCHI,
[Adhik] Aso Vad 3 [October 3,1917]
BHAISHRI,
I have received the papers you sent. I shall read them and do
what I can. I don't think I can go to Kathiawar at present. I
just cannot get away from Champaran.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
BHAISHRI BHAGWANJI ANOOPCHAND
RAJKOT CITY
KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the original postcard in Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand:
