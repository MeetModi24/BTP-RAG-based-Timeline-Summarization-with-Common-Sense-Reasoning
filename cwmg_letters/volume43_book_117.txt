117. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
AMOD,
Before prayer, March 23, 1930
CHI. KUSUM,
I have your letter.
If Narandas and Gangabehn permit you, come and spend a
day. I hope you know that we reach Broach on Wednesday. You
should get this letter on Monday. You would have got it today,
but I had no time at all to write.
It is foolish of you to feel sorry that you could not get up at
three. How can you help if your body is not strong enough? Be-
sides, everything depends on God's will. It is enough if you do
not remain complacent. I know that you are striving hard. I
have no time to write more.
I have of course written to Dudhibehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1796
