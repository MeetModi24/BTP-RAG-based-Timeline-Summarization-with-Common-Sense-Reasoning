425. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 13, 1930
CHI. SHARDA,
Your letter. Continue to have sun-bath and oil-massage.
While spinning on the takli, the thread should never snap. I
could not understand your saying that there is little waste on the
spinning-wheel. If you tell me the amount of waste in a given
number of rounds of a particular count I would understand it.
Calculate it this way. There is another way also. Find out how
often you pulled out the yarn and how often it snapped in the
course of half an hour. Is it clear to you that there is a differ-
ence between calculating the waste and [the frequency of snap-
ping]? If you have understood this, write to me what this dif-
ference is.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9894. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
