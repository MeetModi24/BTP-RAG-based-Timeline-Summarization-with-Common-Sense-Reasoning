510. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 23, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
Rajkumari is arriving on Monday by the 11.35 train. Read
the accompanying letter. I believe Jamnalalji will not be there
at the time. You will, of course, go to receive her. Do what is
needed. She will stay for two days.
I have talked to Anna about Devraj. He will write to
him. I am not very hopeful.
Shankerlal may come tomorrow at 8, or whenever he wishes
to come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11537
