171. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 3, 1930
CHI. NIMU,
I had a letter from you after so many days. It seems nowadays you have
become Ba's secretary. How is your health? Is Sumitra all right? Does she
still suffer from constipation? So long as one can get ink, one must never
write with a pencil.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
