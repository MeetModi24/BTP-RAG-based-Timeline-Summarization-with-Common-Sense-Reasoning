52. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
THE ASHRAM,
Thursday, November 18, 1926
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. You may come to Wardha. You have not
given me the news that I had specially asked for about your health.
Ramdas left today after staying for two days. Haribhai has come
today. He and Kusum will go to Broach tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9221
