348. LETTER TO BINODE KANUNGO
[On or after June 9, 1939]
What can I do about the undoubtedly improper appoint-
ments? If a proper complaint is made before the Working Commit-
tee something may be possible. Where public opinion is unsound
and non-existent the things you describe will happen.
Yours,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
