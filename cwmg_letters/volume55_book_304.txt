304. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
July 7, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I have received your letter after a long interval; it gave me
immense pleasure. If the word 'dear' has been missed in some
letters, it does not mean that you are no longer dear to me.
I was very happy to learn that all is well with you. No
other letter from you has reached me. You are, of course, getting
all the news about me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 287
