52. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
January 13, 1933
CHI. NARANDAS,
Dr. Kanuga writes and tells me that Radha does suffer from
tuberculosis. She should be sent away to a place like Deolali.
She will be restored to health only if she takes complete rest
there. You should discuss this with Santok and Radha. This is
a difficult problem, but we must solve it.
Do you get any letter from Ramabehn ? Chhaganlal has
received no letter from her at all.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I
