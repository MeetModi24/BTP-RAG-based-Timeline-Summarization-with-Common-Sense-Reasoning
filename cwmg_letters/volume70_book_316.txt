316. LETTER TO B. P. RUSTOGI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 19, 1939
DEAR SIR,
Please give me the Dewan's name, his salary, etc. What is
your occupation? Are the previous dictators in jail? If they are,
what is their sentence? How many are in prison?
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
