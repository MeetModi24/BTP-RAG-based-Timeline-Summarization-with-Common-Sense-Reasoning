475. LETTER TO MADAN MOHAN MALAVIYA
BHAI SAHEB,
SEVAGRAM,
August 31, 1940
I was happy to see your handwriting. The very word convoca-
tion scares me. Where do I have knowledge worthy of a scholar?
How can I face the boys? And certainly the lack of time is an
important consideration. Therefore please excuse me. I know it is
your love and Radhakrishnan's that calls me, but I am helpless.
Hope you are well.
Your younger brother,
From Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
