40. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
June 9, 1936
CHI. AMRITLAL,
We expect to reach there on the 14th morning. Attend
to the enclosed letters. I hope you are in good health and
Bhansali is practising his penance within limits. Of course I
remember others, too, but don't write about them to save time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10716
