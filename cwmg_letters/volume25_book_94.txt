94. LETTER TO RADHA GANDHI
Bhadarva Sud 15 [September 13, 1924]
CHI. RADHA,
I write this on my way to Delhi. I shall perhaps be there for
about fifteen days. Manibehn has pain in the stomach. Her ad-
dress is: C/o Vallabhbhai Patel, Barrister. My address in Delhi
is: C/o Maulana Mahomed Ali, Comrade Office, Delhi.
Devdas has stayed on at Ahmedabad. Ramdas is going to
Bhavnagar. Kristodas, Pyarelal and Mahadev are with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. RADHA
C/O VORA SHIVLAL KARSANJI
NEAR LAL DARWAJA
RAJKOT
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 6034. Courtesy: Radhabehn Choudhri
