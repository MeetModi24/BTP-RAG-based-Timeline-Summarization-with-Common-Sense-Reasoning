231. LETTER TO K. RAMACHANDRA
January 28, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is very difficult for me to give you any
further advice. I am quite convinced, however, that the inter-
ference on the part of non-Hindus cannot be tolerated in a matter

mined to champion the cause of temple-entry as their own, to collect funds
in its name and to start direct action.
3 For Gandhiji's previous letter to the addressee, vide Vol. LII, pp. 312-.
172
which is a purely religious issue and which is one essentially for Hin-
dus to settle among themselves. I am surprised that non-Hindus
should persist in their interference.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. RAMACHANDRA
SRI WICKRAMA ROAD
WELLAWATTE
