63. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 9, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
Enclosed please find a letter from Bhagwanji. Go to help him
or send someone else. There is nothing great in going to nearby
villages. Go and see him. He is a simple man and wants to do a
lot. If he volunteers to devote some time to the school, you may
accept his services. However, if your experience is to the contrary,
then leave it.
A letter for Lilavati is enclosed. What is the news about
Keshu?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8443.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
