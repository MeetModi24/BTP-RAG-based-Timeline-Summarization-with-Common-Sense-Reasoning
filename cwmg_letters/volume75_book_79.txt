79. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 27, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I must hurry again. I began a serious study of your rendering
of K's writing. It has begun ill. English is fairly good but the
thought does not run smooth. The whole must read logical. You
will see on your return what I mean. It is a most difficult task
and if you are found to have failed it won't be accounted as your
fault.
Of course I shall deal with your chest. You will stay in Jul-
lunder as long as necessary.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4107. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7416
