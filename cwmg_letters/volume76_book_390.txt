390. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
July 30, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You are responsible for the conduct of the Ashram inmates
all the twenty-four hours. How you can discharge that respons-
ibility is a different thing. This can be possible only when they
willingly submit to observation. You should act as if I was not
there. Do what you can. See how far your ahimsa goes. If things
are thrust on you of which you do not even have any know-
ledge you should put up with it. By all means take my help
whenever it is needed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4437; also S.N. 24486
