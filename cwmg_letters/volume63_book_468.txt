468. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
5 a. m., October 11, 1936
CHI. LILA,
I warn you lest you should prove all my fears right. Do
you think you are free from all restraints since you are released
from the obligation of coming here? Have you stopped copying
out the slokas ? You do not mean that you will never be able
to come here, do you? He who used to play on the dilruba is
prostrate. Shall I send it to you? Let me have a list also of
your other belongings which are here so that I may send them to
you, or you may come and collect them. Do as you think fit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9351. Also C.W. 6626. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
