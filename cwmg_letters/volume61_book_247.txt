247. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
June 17, 1935
CHI. VASUMATI,
If Shivabhai has really some work for you and wishes you
to remain there, you have already given a promise to stay there
for a year and you should keep it and live there in perfect peace
and joy and carefully do the work entrusted to you. Try to cure
your constipation.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Lakshmi and Manu have arrived here.
SHRI VASUMATIBEHN
VALLABH VIDYALAYA
BOCHASAN
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9401. Also C.W. 647. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
