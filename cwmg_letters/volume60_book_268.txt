268. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI N. PATEL
February 10, 1935
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I have received your postcard. I am yet to receive the
paddy. I see that paddy is first husked. Let me know what kind
of mill-stones are used for it? We are not concerned with any
other processes except husking. You are in a position to make
the best experiments as you are in the land of rice. How many
days does it take for paddy to dry for husking after it is harvest-
ed? Or can it be husked immediately after it has been har-
vested? How much is the husk? How much paddy can be husked
in an hour? What is its cost? Over and above this give me as
much information as you can. There was a letter from Natha-
bhai about Lalitabehn. You must have read it.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAOJIBHAI NATHABHAI
LIMBASI
(MATAR TALUKA)
