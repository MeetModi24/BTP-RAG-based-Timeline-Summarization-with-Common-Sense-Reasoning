432. LETTER TO MADELEINE ROLLAND
August 25, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
Just a word to send my love to you and to the Rishi.
Mira will tell you all about the latest astonishing event. I hope
that you are both well. Andrews too is here now.
Ever yours,
BAPU
From a copy: C.W. 10582. Courtesy: Madeleine Rolland
