166. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
December 5, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
You should rejoice, and not get upset, whenever I fast. You
should not speak without thinking and without reason, let alone
gossip. In other words, when eating or working you should speak
only if it is necessary. To speak loudly is a sign of immodesty.
All of you should acquire complete mastery over the takli. Know
that the skill which Bhau possesses is very useful.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
