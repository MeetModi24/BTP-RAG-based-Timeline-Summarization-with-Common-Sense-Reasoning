61. LETTER TO SATYAVATI
August 24, 1944
CHI. SATYAVATI 2 ,
A question has arisen whether I have written in respect of
the two points. I think I have. If I am mistaken, here I write
again. I don't believe you can gain anything by violating [the
restrictions] imposed upon you. If you can, do come over in
the cold season wherever I might be. Let us see what happens.
I see your letters to Khurshedbehn.
Sushilabehn arrived here today. Today she told me what
was written about you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
