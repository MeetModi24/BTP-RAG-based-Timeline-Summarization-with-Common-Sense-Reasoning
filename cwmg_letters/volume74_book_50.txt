50. LETTER TO VICHITRA NARAYAN SHARMA
SEVAGRAM,
May 6, 1941
BHAI VICHITRA,
I have your letters. I do not fully understand the situ-
ation, but I have faith in your capacity and your devotion to
duty. So all will be well. Our work goes on, doesn't it?
Blessings from
BAPU
