422. LETTER TO YAJNESHWAR PRASAD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 18, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you.
With reference to the boy who is suffering from tuberculosis,
if he is old enough to judge for himself, I think he should use his
own discretion and take whatever he considers is not objectionable
from the religious standpoint. If, however, he is too young to
decide for himself, it is proper for him to submit to his father's
wishes.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. YAJNESHWAR PRASAD
790 NAI BASTI, QUEEN'S ROAD, DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 13561
