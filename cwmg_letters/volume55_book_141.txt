141. LETTER TO ESTHER MENON
May 7, 1933
MY DEAR CHILD,
I know what you are passing through. I look upon the
coming fast as the richest gift God has yet blessed me with. I
must not write more. You should know that it is well what-
ever the result.
Love for you both and kisses for the children.
BAPU
From a photostat: No. 122. Courtesy: National Archives of India. Also
My Dear Child, p. 101
