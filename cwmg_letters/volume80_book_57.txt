57. LETTER TO DAULATRAI DAVE
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 29, 1945
BHAI DAULATRAI,
Who are my attendants? Without knowing my plight you
get angry with those who look after me. It is because of them
that I am able to do some work. If you want to be angry, let it be
with me because I am not able to cope with the work and am
not able to satisfy everyone. I have done whatever I could for
you but there is no limit to your needs that I could never meet
them. That is no doubt my fault; but I am helpless.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DAULATRAI DAVE
MADHUKUNJ, CHITTARANJAN ROAD
EAST VILE PARLE
