161. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
June 23, 1947
CHI. NARANDAS,
Show the accompanying letter to Aunt. What is her com-
plaint? Who is treating her? Ramanama is compatible only
with the use of the five elements: water, earth, etc.
How are your affairs? How about khadi, the school, music,
Harijan work, etc.?
I didn't like Abha's falling ill. I write to her regularly.
If I could stay at one place, I would keep her with me and
hope for her complete recovery.
Jaisukhlal's Manu is fine. The cut has healed. I heard
that Jamna and others paid a visit to Bombay. I hope she is
all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II/526. Also C. W. 8638.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
