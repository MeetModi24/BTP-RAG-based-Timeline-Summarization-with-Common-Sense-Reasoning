586. LETTER TO DUNCAN GREENLEES
March 1, 1933
Newcomers do find the routine a stiff business. But we are
trying to live the life of the billions of the earth. They are toilers
all the day long. They have to do their thinking whilst their
bodies are working. When the routine becomes natural, it becomes
pleasant and does not interfere with hard thinking. All thinking
