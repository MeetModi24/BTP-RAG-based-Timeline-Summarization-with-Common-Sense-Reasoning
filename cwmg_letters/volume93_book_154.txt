154. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
[1936]
BHAI RAJENDRA BABU,
I had a little discussion with Khan Saheb last night.
The result is the enclosed resolution Read it. If you like
it, tell Khan Saheb. I had told him that I would draft and
send something. If any changes are thought necessary they
should be made.
I have instructed Khan Saheb that he should not make
public speeches. Please instruct Jawaharlal too accordingly.
Blessings from
BAPU

Archives of India
