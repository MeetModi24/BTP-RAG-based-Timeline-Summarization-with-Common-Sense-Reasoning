246. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
July 12, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
The enclosed is the last. It is very interesting. The gods
and men share the same virtues and vices and are familiar
friends who often quarrel among themselves. The one invisible
Power stands supreme and unbound.
You are cutting your way through difficulties. You can easily
make chapatis of juari flour. Try and you will succeed.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6485. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9880
