142. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
MY DEAR SHANKERLAL,
Here is a letter I received from Lala Shankarlal. I would
like you to reply to it in detail and send it to me. And if
Manilal Kothari is there I would like you to get his reply also
but even if he is not there you may send a copy of this letter to
him and get him to send a reply as early as possible.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 2
SF. SHANKERLAL BANKER
ALL-INDIA SPINNERS' ASSOCIATION
MIRZAPUR, AHMEDABAD
From a microfilm: S.N. 15753
