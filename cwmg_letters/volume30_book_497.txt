497. LETTER TO KUSUM AND DHIRU
ASH RAM, SABARMATI,
May 8, 1926
CHI. KUSUM AND DHIRU,
I have your letter. I do not want to apportion blame for what
has happened. I only wish that you should now take full advantage
of the free time you will get there, and in this manner you should
make your influence felt in the neighbourhood by being more
regular in your daily work of spinning, etc., and strengthen your
resolves. Keep writing to me. I see your handwriting still needs
much improvement. Dhiru's especially is extremely poor. I shall
forget my grief over Dhiru's going away to Bombay if he reli-
giously follows there all his vows. Tell Bhanumati that, if she starts
spinning regularly and forms the habit of getting up at four, I
will think that letting both of you go to Bombay has been of great
value. Devdas is there; he is to be operated upon, and is along
with Ba and Mahadev. You must have heard about all this.
