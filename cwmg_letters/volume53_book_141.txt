141. LETTER TO A. D. APPADURAI
January 21, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. In my opinion, khaddar has virtues which
nothing else has. Khaddar means help to the most needy, and
amongst them are to be counted thousands of Harijans, for their
women eke out a living by spinning and several thousands of
Harijans are weavers of nothing but khaddar.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. A. D. APPADURAI
ST. JOHN'S COLLEGE
PALAMCOTTAH
(TINNEVELLY DIST.)
From a microfilm: S.N. 18993
