428. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
October 19, 1928
CHI. MIRA,
I have been having your perfect letters. They please me and
relieve me of all anxiety. You will not mind my not sending
you anything long or regular just now.
About Prabhudas,
Chhaganlal will be wiring you.
Love.
BAPU
SHRIMATI MIRABAI
JAMIA MILLIA, KAROL BAGH, DELHI
From the original: C.W. 5314. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 8204
