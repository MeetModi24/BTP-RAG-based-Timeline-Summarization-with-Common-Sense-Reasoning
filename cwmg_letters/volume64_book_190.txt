190. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
December 21, 1936
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
I gave Ramdas your letter. He thought you might have
come here since you had no work on hand. I told him about
your having taken up service of the cow and he was silent. I
hope you are keeping well.
Blessings from
BAPU
