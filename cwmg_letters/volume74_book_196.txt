196. LETTER TO MANUBEHN S. MASHRUWALA
June 28, 1941
CHI. MANUDI,
There have been no letters from you recently. But I learn
that you keep good health. Write to me. Grandmother is all
right. I am also quite well. Manojna and Krishnadas have ar-
rived. Prabhudas also is here at present. He will soon be
leaving.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1581. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
