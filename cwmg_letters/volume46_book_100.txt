100. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
BORSAD,
May 6, 1931
MY DEAR BHRRR1,
I have not received your letter sent to the Vidyapith. I
suppose it will come to me in due course. I am delighted that
you have had a successful operation. I hope this will be the last.
How long will they keep you in the hospital? I am in Borsad up
to Monday at least. I might then have to go to Simla.
I have now got your Vidyapith letter. I am writing to the
Gaikwar. I wonder if it will be too late. I must not go to
Baroda just yet.
Yours,
BAPU
SJT. ABBAS TYABJI
K.E.M. HOSPITAL
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 9575
