380. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
August 20, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
There must be a wire awaiting me at Maganwadi. I heard
yesterday that you were to be State Guest. I do hope my
letter went through all right (or alright?).
Pyarelal is decidedly better today. The temperature is only
101 against 103 at this time yesterday (8 a.m). If he is restored
it would be a great and good thing.
I have no doubt that you will be able to fix up everything
nicely.
I had a visit yesterday from the President of the
T[ravancore] National Congress. Of course I was silent. He is
an able man. He spoke with sweet reasonableness.
I hope you will keep well and drive to the Cape if you
have time. Would like you to drop in at Tiruvannamalai for
a day to see the Maharshi on your return. My b.p. was 160/
100 this morning.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3635. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6444
