177. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
December 21, 1930
CHI. NIMU,
I got your letter. Are you not pleased that Ramdas has once again been
honoured? Or, do you repent having married him? If not, you too, should be
ready. How many teeth has Sumitra cut? What all has she started eating now?
Does she suffer from constipation?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
