310. LETTER TO SIR ROBERT MCCARRISON
November 5, 1934
DEAR MAJOR-GENERAL,
I thank you for your letter and the books on food.
Yours sincerely,
MAJOR-GENERAL SIR ROBERT MCCARRISON
KT.C.I.E., K.H.P., I.M.S.
DIRECTOR, NUTRITION RESEARCH
INDIAN RESEARCH FUND ASSOCIATION, COONOOR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
