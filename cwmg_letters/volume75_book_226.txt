226. LETTER TO AMARNATH JHA
BARDOLI,
December 12, 1941
BHAI AMARNATHJI,
I have your letter. I did not answer your previous letter
because Kakasaheb and Shrimanji were going to visit you.
Kakasaheb has given me a full report. Let us see what Tan-
donji has to say. We must at all cost avoid friction.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 10261
