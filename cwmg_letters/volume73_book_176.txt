176. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 11, 1940
DAUGHTER,
I hope you are getting letters every day. Your news has ap-
peared in the Press. Akbar is well. I have not been able to do
anything about Niyamat's daughter. I shall do something. The
rest from Pyarelal. I think of you very often. Bari's letter has
been received.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 437
