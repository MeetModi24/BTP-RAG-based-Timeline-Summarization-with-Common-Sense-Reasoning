379. LETTER TO V. L. PHADKE
Sunday, January 6, 1929
BHAI MAMA,
I have your postcard. I arrived here last night. Bring along
Jaggu and his wife. We shall lodge her in the women's quarters.
Do you still require help for the house? If so, how much?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3822
