417. LETTER TO SURENDRA
Silence day
CHI. SURENDRA,
I have received your letter. I have written about the tannery in
my letter to Ramniklal. It irks me also that we could not talk. But
there was no alternative. You have taken my worry about the tannery
off my mind. Hence I was not impatient to talk.
I do not think you have to permanently remain at Ranchi. But
I do think it necessary that a couple of responsible persons should
learn that art.
Encourage the students who can take consolation from you. Do
not avoid ladies altogether. Be guided by Nathji in the matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : G.N. 3133
