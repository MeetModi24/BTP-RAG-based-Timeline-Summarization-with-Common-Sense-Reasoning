419. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
August 30, 1934
CHI. MANUDI,
I did get your letter. Are you not attending Sushilabehn's
school now? I am content with what you are able to study. I
am very glad indeed that you have shed fear of Bhai. Take care
of your health. Overcome anger, serve aunt and live happily.
Ramdas is here now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1533. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
