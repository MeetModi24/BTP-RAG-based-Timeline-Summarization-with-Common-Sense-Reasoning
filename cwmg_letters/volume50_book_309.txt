309. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
July 31, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
I may say your letters are beautiful. With regard to protec-
tion read the letter to Nirmala. One whose mind is pure should
have the faith that God would protect her purity. It is wrong
to depend on weapons. What if the weapon is snatched away?
One who follows the path of non-violence may have no faith in
weapons. Non-violence and love are his weapons.
There is no violence if someone's feelings are hurt when you
speak the truth. There is no violence if someone is hurt without
our wanting to hurt. Supposing I ask for cow's milk, but knowing
about my vow, if you do not give it to me you are not commit-
ting violence, but doing your duty.
BAPU
