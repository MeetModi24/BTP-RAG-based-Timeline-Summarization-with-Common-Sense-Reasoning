274. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
NEW DELHI,
August 31, 1946
CHI. SHARMA,
I have received Shri M[unshi]'s award.
From what you
told me I had thought it was in your favour. Now I find that
it is not in your favour but against you.
When I receive fair
copies of the document I shall send one to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 364
