182. LETTER TO R. N. MANDLIK
POST ANDHERI,
March 20, 1924
DEAR MR. MANDLIK,
I have your letter.
I have not seen the Nava Kal. I must not therefore express
any opinion whatsoever. I have too great regard for Mr. Khadil-
kar to venture any opinion on what he might have written without
knowing it and without seeing him personally about it, if I was
not satisfied with it. You will therefore please excuse me for not
giving any opinion on the question raised by you at the present
moment. Will you please send me a marked copy of the Nava
Kal referred to by you.
Yours sincerely,
R. N. MANDLIK, ESQ.
"LOKAMANYA" OFFICE
207, RASTIBAI BUILDING, GIRGAUM
BOMBAY, No. 4
From a photostat: S.N. 8544; also C.W. 5129
