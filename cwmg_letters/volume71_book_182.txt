182. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 27, 1940
BHAI PRABHAKAR,
Your questions are such as would take me long to answer.
Some time when you come here you may raise them. Meanwhile
keep reading Harijan. That may incidentally provide you all
the answers.
Blessings from
BAPU
