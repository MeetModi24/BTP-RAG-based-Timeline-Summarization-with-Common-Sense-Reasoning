159. LETTER TO SAILENDRANATH CHATTERJEE
JUHU,
June 5, 1944
MY DEAR SAILEN 1 ,
I was glad to have your letter. Abha will be with me whilst
I am free. She has become very weak but there is no cause for
anxiety.
About your difficulty, we shall talk when I am in Sevagram.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat : C.W. 10494. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
