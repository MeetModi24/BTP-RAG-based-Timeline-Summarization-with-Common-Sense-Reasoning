107. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 8, 1930
CHI. RUKMINI,
At last I have a letter from you. Take immediate steps to
make your body strong. Let there be no weight on your mind.
Occupy yourself with any work or reading you can take up. I
shall be happy if you write to me and open your heart. What does
the doctor say now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 9313. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
