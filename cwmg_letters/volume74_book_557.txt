557. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
September 22, 1941
MY DEAR AMRITLAL,
I am glad you are free from fever. I hope you will remain
so. I hope too that your wife and son will also be entirely free.
So far as Vidyashram is concerned, I am in correspondence
with Annada. I know Dhirendra's worth. He won't do any-
thing in opposition to the A. I. S. A. Of course he is free to come
whenever he wishes.
As to Abha I am prepared to have her whenever she comes.
If she comes, I shall prepare her for her marriage if she proves
her worthiness. I won't draw a line between her and Kanu. If
she comes, I can find an escort for her.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10323. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
