23. LETTER TO V. M. NAWLE
January 12, 1933
DEAR DR. NAWLE,
I have your letter after many days, but you have not ack-
nowledged my letter regarding Sjt. C. V. Vaidya. I therefore
do not know whether you have appreciated the force of my criti-
cism.
Please tell me in as few words as possible what "the new and
unique path" to the temple-entry is; then I shall decide whether
an interview is necessary. I have just now no time to spare for
anything but the most important matters.
Yours sincerely,
DR. V. M. NAWLE
EDITOR, "DINBANDHOO"
444 RASTA PETH, POONA
From a microfilm: S.N. 18907
