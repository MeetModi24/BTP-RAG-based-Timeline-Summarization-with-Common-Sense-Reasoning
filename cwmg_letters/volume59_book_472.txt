472. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
November 25, 1934
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I have both your letters, for Ba and for me. This time again
you have been very late. You should write at least once a month.
What is the cost of the Nepali Paper? We are all well. Does
Harijan reach you intact?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6546
