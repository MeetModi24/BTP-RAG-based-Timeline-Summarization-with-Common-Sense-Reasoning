374. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
August 19, 1938
CHI. AMRITLAL,
The mistake regarding plums should not be repeated. Nana
Dharmadhikari should go only after the work for which he had
come is over. He need not stay on because of Kaka. If the
latter wants him, however, by all means let him stay. In fact
what I said was just off hand, so there was no question of your
being guided by it.
I will tie the rakhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
