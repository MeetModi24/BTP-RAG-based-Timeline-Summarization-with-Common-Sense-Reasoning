25. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 26, 1940
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter. I hardly get time to read Harijanbandhu.
Only today it chanced to fall into my hands and I read the very
sentence you refer to.
It irked me, too. This happened in the
morning. In the afternoon I got your letter. How strange, or
rather what a coincidence!
I had a talk with Vallabhbhai about your work.
He said
he would send some help.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
You yourself may write to Shamaldas and Kaku and let
me know the result. How much used to be given?
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II.
Also C.W. 8573. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
