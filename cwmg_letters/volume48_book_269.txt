269. LETTER TO DEVI WEST
S. S. "PILSNA",
December 15, 1931
MY DEAR DEVI 2 ,
I often wanted to write to you but never could get the time.
We are today on the Red Sea. I am slowly overtaking the arrears
of sleep and correspondence in between. I was delighted to hear
from Muriel that you had decided to join her. You will be a great
acquisition to Kingsley Hall and I know you will be happy there.
You must write to me regularly.
We are a party of nine all travelling deck. The weather is
still cold but pleasant. I had your farewell letter.
Love.
BHAI
From a photostat: C.W. 4437. Courtesy: A. H. West
