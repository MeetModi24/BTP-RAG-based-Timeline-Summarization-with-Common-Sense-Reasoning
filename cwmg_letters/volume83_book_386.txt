386. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
URULI-KANCHAN,
March 27, 1946
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. I could not reply to it earlier. I do not
like the letter. Your old impatience is still there.
You have not to fight for money, do you have to? You have
to fight for truth. I am in correspondence with Gadodiaji. He
had suggested Bhulabhai's name. When he was told about Bhula-
bhai's illness, he accepted M[unshi],s name. I am now writing
to M. You have to send me your brief. It should be to the
point. I will ask Gadodiaji to reply to it. And you will have
to send in your rejoinder. I shall send all this correspondence to
M. Before that there should be signatures of both of you on
the award.
I have today your letter of the 22nd.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
between pages 356 and 357
