153. LETTER TO JAMNA N. GANDHI
January 22, 1931
CHI. JAMNA,
I advise you to put yourself under the treatment of the
Punjabi vaid who had treated Chandrashankar. Chandrashankar
says that he practises in a spirit of service. You and Kusum ought
to get strong.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 843. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
