368. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
Y.M.,
October 16, 1930
MY DEAR ANAND,
The authorities have given me your greetings wire. It shows
that Vidya is now with you. I am glad. I hope she is keeping
well.
Love to you both.
BAPU
[PS.]
I hope you got my previous letter.
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
