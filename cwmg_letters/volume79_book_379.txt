379. LETTER TO JANAKDHARI PRASAD
March 9, 1945
BHAI JANAKDHARI BABU,
I read your letter to Pyarelal. He has gone to Simla. My
health is all right. You can ask me whatever you want to. I
will answer as best I can. I trust you are in good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 57
