413. LETTER TO DILKHUSH B. DIWANJI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 16, 1939
BHAI DILKHUSH,
I have your letter. Your work is no doubt always solid. Has
your problem been solved? Your weaving is done there, isn't
it? Can you weave fine yarn? Try to attain perfection.
Blessings from
BAPU
DILKHUSH DIWANJI
GANDHI KUTIR
KARADI, via NAVSARI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2643
