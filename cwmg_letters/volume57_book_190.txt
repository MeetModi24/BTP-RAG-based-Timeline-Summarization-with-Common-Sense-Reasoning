190. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
February 19, 1934
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have been unable of late to write to you. I have now
reached Madras on the morning of the silence day. I got your
postcard.
It would appear you are progressing.
Do what Dr.
Ansari says. I am going on well. Now it will be the Karnataka
tour. After that probably Bihar.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2408
