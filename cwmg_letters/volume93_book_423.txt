423. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 6, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. I have given to Ba your letter to her.
Shirin checks my blood-pressure quite frequently. It is going a
little high. It was 160/99 this afternoon. She herself must be
writing. My weight is 103½ lb. It seems to have gone down a
bit. I feel full of energy. I get good sleep.
Shankaran has been taken ill. He has fever. It was 102°
yesterday. Shirin is looking after him. She is doing all the work
with keen interest. She is even learning to spin.
I hope you are being careful about your food. Do you
have to work at night?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
