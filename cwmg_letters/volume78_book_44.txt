44. LETTER TO MRS. K. L. RALLIA RAM
SEVAGRAM,
August 18, 1944
DEAR SISTER,
Gandhiji had your letter of 4th August and the enclosure.
He desires
me to thank you for both. He is glad that you wrote. I regret that owing
to heavy pressure of work he cannot write himself.
Of course all genuine
attempts to establish unity have his blessings.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
MRS. K. L. RALLIA RAM
5 MASON ROAD
LAHORE, N.-W. RLY.
From the original : Gandhi Papers, File No. 84. Courtesy : National
Archives of India
