238. LETTER TO JAGDISH CHANDRA BOSE
POST ANDHERI,
March 28, 1924
DEAR SIR JAGDISH CHANDRA BOSE1,
I thank you very much for your letter of the 5th instant.
I would be delighted indeed to see you and Lady Bose
on your return if you could find time to motor down to Juhu.
It is a pleasant retreat near Andheri.
Yours sincerely,
SIR JAGDISH CHANDRA BOSE
C/O V. N. CHANDAVARKAR, ESQ.
PEDDER RD., CUMBALLA HILL
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 8619; also C.W. 5162
