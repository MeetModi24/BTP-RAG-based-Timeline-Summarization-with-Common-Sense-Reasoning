241. LETTER TO VIDYA ANAND HINGORANI
March 1, 1934
CHI. VIDYA,
You have spoilt your handwriting. Don't be lazy in writing
to me.
I have written to Anand about Mahadev. If Anand goes
to Patna, you will have to stay in Karachi or Multan. No time
to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India and
Anand T. Hingorani
