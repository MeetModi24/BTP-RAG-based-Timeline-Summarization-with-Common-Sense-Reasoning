495. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
July 20, 1937
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
I ought to have talked to you about Bhagwandas of Kalpi.
I told him not to return unless I wrote to him. Do you want
him? He seems to be no good as a teacher. Please tell me
what you will have me to do. Meanwhile I am telling him not
to come in answer to a letter just received.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10125
