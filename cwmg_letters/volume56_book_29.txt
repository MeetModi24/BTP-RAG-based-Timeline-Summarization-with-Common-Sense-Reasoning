29. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI A. MEHTA
WARDHA,
September 28, 1933
BHAI BHAGWANJI,
I got your letter. . . .2 May your efforts succeed. Leave no-
thing undone. . . .3 If they cannot live together in perfect har-
mony, do not hesitate to separate the shares.
I am not writing a separate letter to Ratubhai.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5820. Also C.W. 3043. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
1 The superscription in this and other letters to the addressee is in
Devanagari script.
2& The source is damaged here.
4 This was regarding the property of Pranjivandas Mehta.
5 Ratilal Sheth, father-in-law of Chhaganlal Mehta
