518. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 14, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Sushila came yesterday and gone today. Prabha has [been]
to her mother for two or three days just to feed her superstition.
Her brother had a child born and she has to attend some cere-
mony. She returns on 23rd. Annapurna has gone with Sushila
for treatment. There was suspicion of tuberculosis. Dr. David
however says there is no trace. But she had slow fever and has
grown weak.
I have two deputations-Alla Bux and Sidhwa and the
other from Burma.
Munshi's daughter Sarala who is here for a few days has
taken Prabha's place. She is a very fine girl. She is one of the
three Indian women solicitors in all India. But she is exceedingly
simple in her tastes and has made friends with everybody. She
has come to pass a few days with me.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Your letter. Mr. Hancock, 27th September, 4 p.m. Glad
you are better.
From the original: C.W. 4073. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7382
