408. LETTER TO MADHAVANAR
As AT SABARMATI,
June 5, 1931
MY DEAR MADHAVANAR,
I have your letter about picketing. At the present moment I
cannot give you better advice than to ask you to discuss the whole
thing with Syt. Rajagopalachari and act in consultation with
him. I am sure that if orders under 144 are passed, for the time
being at any rate you should obey them.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. MADHAVANAR
MATHRUBHUMI BUILDINGS
CALICUT (MALABAR)
From a microfilm: S.N. 17236
