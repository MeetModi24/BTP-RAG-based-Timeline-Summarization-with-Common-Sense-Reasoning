221. LETTER TO SAADULLAH KHAN AND SOFIA SOMJI
[January 26, 1936]
If Bapu had been well, he should have written himself what I am
writing on his behalf. May the auspicious day of the 26th of January begin
for you both a happy wedded life of service and dedication, made all the
stronger and richer by the indissoluble tie that binds you.
Yours sincerely,
MAHADEV DESAI
The Bombay Chronicle, 27-1-1936
