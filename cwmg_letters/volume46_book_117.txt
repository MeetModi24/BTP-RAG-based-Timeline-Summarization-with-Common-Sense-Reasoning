117. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 7, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
I thank you for your letter of the 30th. I can fully
understand what Sir Malcolm Hailey says and nothing would
please me better than to find that my visit to him is rendered
unnecessary by the cultivators getting the relief which they
need.
I thank you too for your enquiry about my health. There is
now nothing wrong with it. I still need some rest which I am
taking as far as possible in the circumstances.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. W. EMERSON, ESQ.
HOME SECRETARY TO THE GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
SIMLA
Home Department, Political, File No. 33/XI & K.Ws 1931 p. 170. Courtesy:
National Archives of India
