253. LETTER TO AKBAR CHAVDA
[On or before May 25, 1946]
CHI. AKBAR,
I have your letter. I am forwarding your letter to Khan
Saheb. He left the day before yesterday. You should do just as
he says–—that will be the right thing. I understand what you
say about your not reading the newspapers. It would be better
if some time you sent me your full day's time-table so that I
could see from it that you really cannot spare any time at all to
read the newspapers.
Are you keeping well? I cannot say how long I shall be
staying here. Zohra is in a village called [Uruli] Kanchan.
Kanchan also is there. Munnalal is with me. If Vasumati is
there who is in the Ashram to do the work? Don't you read
even Harijan Sevak? Or is it that no one sends it to you?
Blessings from
BAPU
GRAMSEVAK
SAMAU
