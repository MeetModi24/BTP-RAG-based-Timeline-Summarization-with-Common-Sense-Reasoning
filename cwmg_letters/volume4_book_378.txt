378. LETTER TO S. V. PATEL
[JOHANNESBURG,]
May 19, 1905
MR. S. V. PATEL
P. O. Box 208
KLERKSDORP
DEAR SIR,
Probably the best medical education is to be obtained in Germany,
but then a knowledge of German would be necessary. Ordinarily, a
medical degree obtained in Glasgow is considered very good, and no
matter what degree may be held in Bombay, the course takes, as a rule,
5 years. A Glasgow degree would be considered quite sufficient for practice
in any part of South Africa.
The address of the paper India is 84-85, Palace Chambers, West-
minster, London.
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
Letter Book (1905) : No. 93.
