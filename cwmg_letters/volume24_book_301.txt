301. LETTER TO PAUL F. CRESSEY
SABARMATI,
August 10, 1924
DEAR FRIEND,
We are taught from childhood discipline in self-denial.
Though, therefore, in the East, we ever fail to live up to it, we
know that life is not for indulgence but essentially for self-denial.
Would that the students of America could imbibe that one lesson.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PAUL F. CRESSEY, ESQ.
GRANVILLE, OHIO
U.S.A.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy; Narayan Desai
