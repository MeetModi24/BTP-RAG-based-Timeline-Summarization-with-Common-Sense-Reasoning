100. LETTER TO NATHURAM TRIKAMDAS
June 25, 1932
BHAI NATHURAM,
The use of temples and choras is well known. The people
would assemble in these places, sing bhajans, hold meetings, etc.,
and that was the purpose which they were intended to serve.
The question whether image-worship is necessary does not
arise, since it has always existed and will continue to exist.
Every human being is not an image-worshipper, of course.
It may be desirable to make changes in the Vaishnava rituals
of worship.
God exists everywhere, and, therefore, He exists in the image
too. I believe that destruction of image-worship is impossible.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
