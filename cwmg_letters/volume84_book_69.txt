69. LETTER TO JAYAKRISHNA P. BHANSALI
DELHI,
April 24, 1946
CHI. BHANSALI,
You will admit that it is alarming that you fell ill critically.
Man ought not to strain his powers to the utmost. It is indeed
desirable that your whole life should be disciplined. It is better
if you yourself decide to live such a life, not because I say it.
Ultimately we have to live as God wills.
Blessings from
BAPU
