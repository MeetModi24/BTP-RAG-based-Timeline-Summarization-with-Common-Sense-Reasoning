585. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 3, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your two letters came in today. How is this? This is the third
time it has so happened.
I could not write yesterday.
Your letter to Zuberi is all right and so are your suggestions
and replies to Lala Dunichand. Let us see what happens.
If Mridula has her limitations, she has very great qualities.
Her gate-keeper in her Ashram was murdered. She has taken no
fright. She is handling the situation in a cool and brave manner.
You have to nurse such girls. She is a rare woman.
Mahadev has written to you fully about Harijan.
I hope Shummy is better, if not fully restored.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4012. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7321
