176. LETTER TO BHUPENDRA NARAYAN SEN
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 17, 1931
MY DEAR BHUPEN,
I have your letter. You will see the use I have made of
your reference to Habu's mother. Why have you not got her
name? You should have known her age also and had a snap-
shot. Just now my movement has again become uncertain. I do
not know when I shall go to Bardoli. But as soon as I am fixed
up somewhere for some time, both of you may certainly come
and stay with me.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. BHUPENDRA NARAYAN SEN
KHADI MANDAL
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 17087
