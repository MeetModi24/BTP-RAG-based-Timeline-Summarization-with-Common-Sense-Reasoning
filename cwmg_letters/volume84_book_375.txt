375. LETTER TO SAMBHAJI
MUSSOORIE,
June 6, 1946
BHAI SAMBHAJI1,
Manjulabai and others whose land Government has acquired
for the main road to the Ashram ought to be exempted from
land-tax pertaining to that portion and the amount inadvertently
paid ought to be refunded. There can be no doubt about it.
If you will see to it, I need not spend my time on it. If the
job is beyond your power, do write to me and also tell me if
what I believe is correct.
Blessings from
BAPU
SEVAGRAM
