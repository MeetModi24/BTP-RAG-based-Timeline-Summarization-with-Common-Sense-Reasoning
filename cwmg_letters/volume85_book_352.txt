352. LETTER TO G. B. PANT
NEW DELHI,
September 9, 1946
BHAIJI,
I have your letter. You did well in writing to Mirabehn.
I am well. There is nothing to worry about. We are all in
God's hands.
Blessings from
BAPU
GOVIND BALLABH PANT
