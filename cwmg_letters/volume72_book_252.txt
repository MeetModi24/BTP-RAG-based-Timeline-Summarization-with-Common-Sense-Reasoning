252. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
June 21, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
Just one line of love in the midst of distraction. I suppose
you have abstained knowing that I would hardly have time to see
the post. If so, you were right.
Well, you have seen what has happened. I am both unhappy
and happy.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3976. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7285
