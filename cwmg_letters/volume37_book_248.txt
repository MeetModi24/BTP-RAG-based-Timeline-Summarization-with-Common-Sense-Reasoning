248. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 26, 1928
Keshu is anxious to make up his English as early as possible.
He knows a fair amount. My idea is that if he can be kept with the
Bjerrums, he should do well. Will you please advise? If you think
