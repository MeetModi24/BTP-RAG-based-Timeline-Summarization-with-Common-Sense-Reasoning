311. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
ABBOTTABAD,
July 23, 1939
CHI. NIMU,
You left and I got this wire in today's mail. So there is no cause to
worry about Ramdas any more. I was not happy that you felt vexed here. But
such things will happen. You should put up with them and not feel hurt.
You should not in the least take them to heart. Kanam must be flourishing.
I had a comrade in him. I take it that you will meet me in Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
