315. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
PATNA,
March 22, 1934
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
You may certainly go to Wardha. It is no use coming here.
I am going to Assam in a few days. I shall write to you a
Wardha.
Sharma should be there by now. You have only to
build up your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 298
