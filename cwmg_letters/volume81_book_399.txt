399. LETTER TO AHMED DASTAGIR
September 6, 1945
AHMED DASTAGIR SAHEB,
As promised I am sending a letter to you for Allama Saheb.
I had your letter. You can come over whenever you wish to.
Today I have Allama Saheb's letter and a copy of his earlier
letter. As desired by him, I have written to him also.
MOHAN K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Urdu : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
