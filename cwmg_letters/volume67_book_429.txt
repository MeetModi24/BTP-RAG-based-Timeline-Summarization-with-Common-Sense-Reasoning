429. LETTER TO BHARATAN KUMARAPPA
August 31, 1938
MY DEAR BHARATAN,
Ramdas Gulati saw the museum for the spinning models.
His engineer eyes saw defects in the building. I asked him to
reduce them to writing. Here is his report. You should study
it and if the defects pointed out are really defects you should
find who is responsible and, where possible, they should be
remedied. How much progress in Hindi?
Love.
BAPU
Encl. I
From a photostat: G.N. 3595
