203. LETTER TO MRS. EMMA HARKER
POST ANDHERI,
March 22, 1924
DEAR MRS. HARKER,
I have your letter. The sad note about it grieves me. The
progress continues. Could you come on Tuesday next at 5 p.m.?
Yours sincerely,
MRS. E. HARKER
C 3, DATUBHOY MANSIONS
MAYO ROAD
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 8563; also C.W. 5138
