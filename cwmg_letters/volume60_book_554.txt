554. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
April 8, 1935
CHI. MANUDI,
How can you say that I have stopped writing to you? Have
I failed to reply to any letter of yours? How can you charge me
when you yourself do not write? I got your letter today and
here I am replying to it. I am glad to learn that you have passed.
Instead of trying to speed up progress from form to form and save
years, it would be better to try to master what you learn in every
form. Write to me regularly from now on. About other things Ba
or somebody else will write. I hope you yourself do not feel that
you are already nineteen. Write to me frankly.
Blessings to aunties.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2665. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
