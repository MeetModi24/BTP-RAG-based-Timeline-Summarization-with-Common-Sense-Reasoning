27. LETTER TO SANTISUDHA GHOSH
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 19, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Surely it is not too late for a girl of 19
to change her mode of life and to obtain self-control. As a
matter of fact, it is never too late to do either of these two things.
The only thing needful is perfect faith in God and waiting upon
Him to make the necessary change.
I do believe that mind has much to do with the body. If
you have got any constitutional diseases, you should medically
treat them, and if you are merely weak in body because of the
weakness of mind, faith in God and His power to make us better
should restore you.
Yours sincerely,
SRIMATI SANTISUDHA GHOSH
C/O MR. K . N . GHOSH, M.A.
ALIKONDA (BARISAL)
From a photostat: S.N. 19624
