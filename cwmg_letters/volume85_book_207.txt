207. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM,
August 19, 1946
CHI. MIRA,
This is merely to ask you how you would arrange the la-
trines for the Ashram and the village. Subsoil water being so
near the surface, medical men who gathered here yesterday
voted for septic tanks. I know you are averse to the idea.
Send me your own opinion and a description of the prepara-
tion of compost. I forget if you include night-soil also as they
do in Indore. Anyway give me an accurate description for me
to print or show to medical friends.
Don't recommence work unless you are quite fit. You have
ample to do in Mussoorie.
It is raining as I write.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
R. K. reminds me that I have already published your
writings on the thing. If they are enough don't trouble.
From the original: C. W. 6518. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G. N. 9913
