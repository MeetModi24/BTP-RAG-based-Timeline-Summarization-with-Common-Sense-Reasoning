518. LETTER TO V. L. MEHTA
[December 1, 1944]
BHAI VAIKUNTH,
I saw your letter to Pyarelal. I think you should bring along
the Maharaja's money. Maybe, we will not be able to take up
the responsibility. He should entrust the amount either to the
Talimi Sangh or to someone in Bihar who might take up the burden.
It would be all right if he could accomplish the job through
Badrinarayan who is in Bihar.
Blessings from
BAPU
PS.
Show this to Bapa if you can. Don't hesitate to write to me
if you differ from me.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
