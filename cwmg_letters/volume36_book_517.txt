517. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 22, 1928
I have decided to help Ganesan to the extent of Rs. 8,500
by way of loan against the security of four publications: 1. Satya-
graha in South Africa, 2. Gandhiji in Ceylon, 3. Seven Months with
Gandhiji 4. Economics of Khaddar. But I would like you to help
him and guide him. He was inclined to throw up the sponge
and retire from the publishing business entirely. I thought that
was unmanly and I have advised him to brave all difficulties
and survive the storm. I have advised him to secure your
guidance. I also suggested to him that he might get Natesan's
help. But I leave all that to you. If you will think that he
should do so, you will introduce him to Natesan, whom you know
so well.
The books are to be stored with Harihar Sharma. If you
have any other advice on the point, you will tell me.
