80. LETTER TO V. N. BARWE
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 13, 1940
DEAR BARWE,
I do not want to deal publicly with your question. As a
matter of ethics, however, I do maintain that to do Red Cross
work is an indirect encouragement to war effort. But I know
that is a very delicate question and admits of two conflicting
opinions.
Yours sincerely,
M. K.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
