504. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
May 8, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I am thinking whether or no I should continue to write to
you. But since you wrote a postcard to me, I write this. I
should like you to pay a visit to Dhulia without waiting for a
reply. But you know best whether you can spare time now.
BAPU
[PS.]
Write the essay with the utmost care, and not simply as a
task.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1836
