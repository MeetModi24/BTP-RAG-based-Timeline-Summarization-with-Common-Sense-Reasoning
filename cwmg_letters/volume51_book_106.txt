106. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
September 18, 1932
DEAR MAJOR BHANDARI,
I am sorry. I took silence at 1 p.m. so as to be free earlier
tomorrow. Now the only thing possible is to see those who are
coming and listen to what they say. I can reply by writing my
answers.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 5126
