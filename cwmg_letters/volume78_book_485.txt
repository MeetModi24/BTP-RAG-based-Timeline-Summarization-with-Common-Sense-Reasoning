485. LETTER TO BHAGWANDAS
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1944
BABOOJI,
I have treasured your letter of 28-9-44 all these days in the hope
of answering it. I came across it today while overtaking arrears.
You lay upon my shoulders a burden they are ill able to
carry. I am ill-equipped for the task you call me to.
I hope you are keeping quite well.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. BHAGWANDAS
BENARES CANT.
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
