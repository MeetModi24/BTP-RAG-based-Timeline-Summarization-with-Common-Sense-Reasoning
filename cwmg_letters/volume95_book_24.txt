24. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
LAHORE,
Monday [October 27, 1919]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I have received your letter. Of course, you have my blessings.
I think your straightforwardness will take you far. I wish all your
great aspirations are fulfilled. Take care of your health.
It seems I will not be able to come there for the present. There
is a lot of work to do. I have met the Governor. But right now there
is no time to write in detail. I shall leave for Delhi tomorrow. See the
letter regarding the application that has been sent to Chandulal.
Make the necessary changes in it.
I wish you would carry on all the work to be done there. Relieve
me from the anxiety about things there.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 32710
