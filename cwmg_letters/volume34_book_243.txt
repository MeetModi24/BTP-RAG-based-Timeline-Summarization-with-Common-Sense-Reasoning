243. LETTER TO C. V. VAIDYA
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
August 3, 1927
DEAR MR. VAIDYA,
I highly appreciate your letter. I know that you have the
instinct of the judge and had been a judge in Gwalior for a long
time. But your letter has whetted my curiosity, and I must make
time to study your volumes. Are they in Marathi or in English?
You must pardon my gross ignorance even of our good authors of
whom I have long known you to be one. But fate has never left
me any time for doing this kind of reading.
Are the authorities in favour of your conclusion—that our fore-
fathers in the Vedic times ate beef—collected in those volumes?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. C. V. VAIDYA
SADASHIV PETH
POONA CITY
From a photostat: S.N. 12629
