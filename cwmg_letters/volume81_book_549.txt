549. LETTER TO PREMA KANTAK
October 3, 1945
CHI. PREMA,
I have given a long reply to your letter. You must have
received it by now. You have acted up to what you wrote.
Send me a copy of the article you have written in Nava Kal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10438. Also C.W. 6877. Cour-
tesy : Prema Kantak
1 Vide p. 296.
2 Entitled Amhi Kothen Ahont? meaning "Where are we?" The addressee
had severely criticized the proceedings of the A.I.C.C. meeting which failed
to provide any guidelines for the workers and for making no reference to
Gandhiji's absence.
