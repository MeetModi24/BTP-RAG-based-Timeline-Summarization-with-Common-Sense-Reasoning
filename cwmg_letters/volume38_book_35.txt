35. LETTER TO JEHANGIR B. PETIT
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 8, 1928
DEAR MR. PETIT,
I thank you for your very prompt reply. I am sending you
now the blind boy. His name is Daya Arjun. He belongs to
Junagarh. He is a blacksmith, not an untouchable.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
JEHANGIR B. PETIT, ESQ.
PETIT BUILDING, 359 HORNBY ROAD, FORT, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 12984
