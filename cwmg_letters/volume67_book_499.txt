499. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
September 15, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
You must have duly received all the material sent yesterday.
Prabhu Dayal's report for today is good.
Manu had high fever yesterday. Today she is better. The
ups and downs thus continue. I am sending as many letters as
are ready.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11653
