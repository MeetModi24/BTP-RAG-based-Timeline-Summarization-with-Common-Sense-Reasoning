251. LETTER TO DR. S. K. VAIDYA
July 14, 1941
BHAI VAIDYA,
Your certificate is fine indeed. Did it have any effect on
your mind? Did the charkha give you any peace of mind in the
midst of the prevailing atmosphere of violence? I suppose you
have kept up your practice.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5751
