465. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 17, 1939
CHI. SUMANGAL,
I have your letter. I am glad your marriage has been
according to your wishes. May you both be happy and serve
the country.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SUMANGAL PRAKASH
NAVASHAKTI KARVALAYA
PATNA
From a photostat of the Hindi: Sumangal Prakash Papers.
