84. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
SEVAGRAM,
October 28, 1941
CHI. AMBUJAM,
Your letter has "award". What is it?
Of course you can pray for definite objects so long as they
are worthy. The fact is that God is within everyone of us. In-
tense prayer is intense concentration of the right type.
I hope things will go well with you.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
