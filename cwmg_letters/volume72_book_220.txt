220. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 10, 1940
CHI. PREMA,
I got your letter. Everything is in a mess. We have to find
our way through it. We are in God's hands. He will do as He
wills.
As regards the organization do as your heart bids you. I have
no objection but I shall not encourage you either.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10407. Also C.W. 6846. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
