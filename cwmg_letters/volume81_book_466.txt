466. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
September 17, 1945
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have your letter. I have time only to acknowledge it. I
continue to get letters from Hoshiari. She is fine. You must
be well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI BALVANTSINHA
KISAN ASHRAM
P. O. BAHADARABAD, via JWALAPUR
U. P.
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1967
