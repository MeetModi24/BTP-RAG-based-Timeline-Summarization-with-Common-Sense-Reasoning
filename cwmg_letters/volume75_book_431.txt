431. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
February 26, 1942
DEAR MOTI BABU,
I have a complaint from Barisal that the P. Sangh is to open
an uncertified khadi shop and that propaganda is being carried on
against the A. I. S. A. for maintaining the system of certificates;
what is this?
I understand too that the promissory notes have yet
not been sent. All this is surely against the spirit of your
teaching.
I hope you are keeping well.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 11056
