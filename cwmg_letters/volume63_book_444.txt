444. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
October 4, 1936
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I have today a very painful letter from Kanti. He says
that you do not leave him alone and waste his time talking for
hours about useless things though he has not a minute to spare.
I had told you specifically that you might see Kanti only if he
wished it. I have forbidden Kanti to see you, and I am forbid-
ding you also to see him. Kanti's future will take care of itself;
you may not bother about him. Just get well as early as possible.
Tell me in your letter that you will not see him any more.
Write to me whatever you want to say to him. You must have
got my letter of yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 356
