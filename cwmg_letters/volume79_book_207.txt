207. LETTER TO KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
February 10, 1945
CHI. KAMALNAYAN,
Read the enclosed and act upon it. Already there has been
inordinate delay. Return the paper to me after you have acted
on it. I have to send a reply.
Blessings from
BAPU
KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
WARDHA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
