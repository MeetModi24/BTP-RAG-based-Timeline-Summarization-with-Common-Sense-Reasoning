86. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
March 17, 1930
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter and the flowers that were struggling to
find me out. My fatigue so far seems to be health-giving. For
it enables me to take milk twice instead of once and plenty of
fruit. Today the fatigue of the past five days made me sleep
five times during the day. I hope to find myself thoroughly fit to
undertake the ensuing week's march or whatever else may be in
store for me. So you will not worry about me.
I see you are now finding yourself there. The struggle has
been a veritable godsend for all of us. It is, as it should be,
a process of cleansing. Let us never be slack.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5427. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9661
