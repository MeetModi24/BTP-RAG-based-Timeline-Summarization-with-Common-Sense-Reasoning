226. LETTER TO SUDARSHAN V. DESAI
February 26, 1934
CHI. SUDARSHAN,
You have ceased to be Mavo and have now become Sudar-
shan. I hope you will forgive me for not writing to you in
time for your birthday on your completing six years. May you
live long and serve the country well. Your handwriting cannot
still be said to have improved.
It is too early yet to take you
with me on my tours.
I can't say whether you can stand the
strain of touring with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5742. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
