202. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
March 5, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
Since you have demanded a long letter, I have taken the
double sheet. Now that you have entered upon your sixteenth
year, you have become a responsible person. Improve your health
and dedicate yourself to the service of others for life. And for
this you should, of your own free will, observe the rules of
Ashram life.
Now to your questions.
In the Ashram we keep no idol or image because there is
before us God's image in the form of the world and we should
know God through it. If we look at the sky, there are innumerable
images there too, and if we wish we may meditate on any of
them and think that God dwells there also. In this connection,
we should remember that persons like Imam Saheb and Amina
did not find it necessary to have what people call an idol.
I have rivers and valleys to be crossed, which means that I
have a long distance to go before I can realize God.
There can be no absolution from the sin of telling a lie
knowingly, no matter how severe the prayashchitta one undergoes
for it. Prayashchitta wins forgiveness only for one who has told
a lie in ignorance. Do you understand this? If not, ask me
again.
Blessings from
BAPU
