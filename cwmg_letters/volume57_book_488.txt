488. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 8, 1934
CHI. AMTUSSALAAM,
I hope you will stop worrying now. You are free to go to
Bombay. You should get the piles removed. If you wish, I would
send a note of recommendation to the doctor. Follow Sharma's
advice. Don't attempt anything beyond your strength. Write to
me.
Blessings from
BAPU
