372. LETTER TO JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
BARDOLI,
January 18, 1939
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the newspaper cutting. The
heading is a misrepresentation of the purpose of my article.
This is clear even from the cabled summary. But when you
read the original you will discover what a cruel misrepresentation
it is.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MINISTER
JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
NEW YORK CITY
From a photostat: C. W. 11074. Courtesy: Roger W. Holmes and
Francis L. Brown
