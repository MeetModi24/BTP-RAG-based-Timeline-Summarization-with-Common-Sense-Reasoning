463. LETTER TO KALYANJI MEHTA
Saturday [October 27, 1928]
BHAISHRI KALYANJI,
It is said that the Secretary of Hindu is hand-cuffed and ill-
treated in other ways. Write to me to tell me what you know
about this.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Kanti must have left the bed now.
BHAISHRI KALYANJI
PATIDAR ASHRAM, SURAT
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2680
