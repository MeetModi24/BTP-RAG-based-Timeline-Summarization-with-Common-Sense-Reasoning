44. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN DESAI
July 19, 1930
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
I have your letter. You must have returned to the Ashram
now. Do you go and see Valji? How is he? When you meet him
next, tell him that I often think of him. Take care of your
health.
How are Hari-ichchha and the others?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7404. Courtesy: V. G. Desai
