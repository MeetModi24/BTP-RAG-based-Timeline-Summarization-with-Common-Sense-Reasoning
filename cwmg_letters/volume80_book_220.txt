220. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 13, 1945
BHAI GOPICHAND1,
You did a good job in the matter of the Dukhaidi Hari-
jans. They should have full relief. You must persuade the Raj-
puts. The courts alone cannot do the thing. A sad thing has
happened. I was under the impression that the Dunichand who
died was the Dunichand we know. How is the deceased related
to him? I couldn't have written to him. Please find out. If
there is anything I have to do, I will do it.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
LAJPAT BHAVAN
LAHORE (PUNJAB)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
