188. LETTER TO SECRETARY, KHALSA DIWAN
SOCIETY, VANCOUVER
As AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 4, 1928
THE SECRETARY, KHALSA DIWAN SOCIETY
SIKH TEMPLE, VANCOUVER B. C.
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. We were all won-
dering what this money was for and from whom.' I now enclose
receipt for the amount which will be utilized as desired by you.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 15116
