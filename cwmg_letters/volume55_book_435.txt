435. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
August 26, 1933
CHI. RAMA,
I got your letter, as also Nirmala's. I am not writing a
separate letter to Nirmala. I was happy to learn that you did
not worry. Vinabehn gave me news about your health. Dhiru
wrote to me from Bhavnagar. He seems to be quite happy. I
could not see Chhaganlal this time at all. This time the condi-
tions were completely different. I was not permitted to write
to anybody. My health is improving rapidly. Gradually I am
recovering strength. Chhaganlal has been removed to Thana. If
you have received any letter from him from there, please give me
news about him. Also give me news about Vimu if you have any.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5356
