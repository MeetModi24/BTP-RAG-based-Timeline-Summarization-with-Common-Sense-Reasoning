23. LETTER TO SUBHADRA KUMARI CHAUHAN
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 26, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
Convey my blessings to the newly married couple. May they
render great service to the country.
Blessings from
BAPU
SUBHADRA KUMARI CHAUHAN
569 RIGHT TOWN
JABALPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
