172. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
May 24, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
We have so arranged it here that anyone who needs any-
thing should ask for it.
The designation may be Assistant
Manager and the sphere of work may be defined. My suggestion
did not mean that you were to make the announcement. That
will be done by Chimanlal or me. I merely suggested the remedy.
The next step can be taken after I have received your
suggestion. I shall see about it now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4428
