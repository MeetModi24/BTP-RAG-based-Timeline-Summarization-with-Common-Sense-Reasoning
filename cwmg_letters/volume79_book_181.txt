181. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
SEVAGRAM,
February 6, 1945
CHI. BABUDI,
The doctor's advice is not correct. You can give Anand
mosambi juice in warm water. Or you can warm it by putting the
vessel containing it in hot water and give it to him. You can
give him honey mixed in hot water, or pure jaggery. Honey
would be better. You can give him vegetable soup. This will
clear the bowels and normalize motions and then he may drink
milk with relish. The cough and the cold must disappear. There
was no harm in giving him Sanatogen. You will soon observe its
effect. All children fall ill. You should not worry. You should
not give him just any medicine. All children are endowed with
the power of self-recovery. Watch what he eats.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10052. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokhawala
