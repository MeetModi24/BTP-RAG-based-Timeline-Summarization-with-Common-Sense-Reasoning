186. LETTER TO FRANCESCA STANDENATH
PATNA,
May 16, 1947
CHI. SAVITRI 2 ,
I have your letter of 23rd February received by me only this
month. I was delighted to see your handwriting. I never expected
that I should hear from you. I had almost given you up for dead in
the frightful bloodbath through which Europe has gone. As your
letter came as an agreeable surprise I had it copied and distributed
among friends who knew you at the Ashram. If you receive this
letter I would like you to continue writing and telling me all about the

2 This is in the Devanagari script. 'Savitri' was the Indian name given to the
