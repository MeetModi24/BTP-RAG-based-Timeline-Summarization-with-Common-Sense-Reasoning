173. LETTER TO MRS. G. BLAIR
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 13, 1906
DEAR MADAM,
I am very much obliged to you for your note of the 12th instant.
Mr. Ally and I will be pleased to come over to Croydon, and to pay
our respects to Mrs. Bonnerji and to make your acquaintance, some
time in the afternoon of Thursday next. We hope to be there between
4 and 5 o'clock.
I am,
Yours truly,
MRS. BLAIR
C/O MRS. W. C. BONNERJI
"KIDDERPORE"
BEDFORD PARK
CROYDON
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4564
1 Widow of W. C. Bonnerji. He died in England in July, 1906.
