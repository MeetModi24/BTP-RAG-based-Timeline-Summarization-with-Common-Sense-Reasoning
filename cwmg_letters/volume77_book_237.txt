237. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
PANCHGANI,
July 8, 1944
BHAI MUNSHI,
I got your opinion. In the circumstances stated by you, it
is not desirable to publish it. I will preserve it with me. Your
labour will not go in vain; I am already using it for my own
purpose. I am enclosing a note for Sarla .
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : C.W. 7676. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
