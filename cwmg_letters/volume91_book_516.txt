516. LETTER TO JAGJIVANDAS NARANDAS MEHTA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Shravan Vad 4, Friday [August 27, 1926]
BHAISHRI JAGJIVANDAS,
I have your letter. I am enclosing herewith three drafts for
the sums of Rs. 400/-, Rs. 300/-, and Rs. 300/-, totalling
Rs. 1000/-. It seems to me that Trivenibehn has got to put up
with the loss of interest. The following books can be useful to
some extent in the matter of the municipality. . . .2
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 10606
