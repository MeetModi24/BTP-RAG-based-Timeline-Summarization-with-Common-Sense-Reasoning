149. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
July 6, 1936
DEAR HENRY,
This will be presented to you by Kamalnayan Bajaj, the
eldest son of Jamnalalji. However much we may fight Great
Britain, London is increasingly becoming our Mecca or Kashi.
Kamalnayan is no exception. I have advised him to take up a
course in the London School of Economics. Perhaps you will put
him in touch with Prof. Laski who may not mind guiding young
Bajaj. Muriel has undertaken to mother him.
Please treat this also as acknowledgment of your letter recei-
ved some time ago. I am trying to become a villager. The
place where I am writing this has a population of about 600-
no roads, no post-office, no shop.
Love to you all.
BHAI
From a photostat: G.N. 3053
