392. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
CENTRAL PRISON,
SABARMATI,
August 1, 1933
CHI. AMALA,
You must shed your strange and embarrassing manners.
Effusiveness is no mark of true devotion. True devotion works
stealthily. You will not be fit for Harijan work if you do not
concentrate and learn to use your hands well. Your spinning is
most clumsy. I hope you do not mind my speaking or writing
as I feel. Pure love has to feel harsh at times.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
