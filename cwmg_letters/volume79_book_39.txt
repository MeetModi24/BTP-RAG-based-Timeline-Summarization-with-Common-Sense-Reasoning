39. LETTER TO INDUBHUSHAN BHINGARE
SEVAGRAM,
January 11, 1945
BHAI BHINGARE,
I send herewith the manuscript of my foreword and a
picture. Please send me the proofs of my foreword.
I have other pictures. If you want to publish them at ap-
propriate places write to me. I shall send them.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. BHINGARE
MAHAL
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
