364. LETTER TO HAKIM AJMAL KHAN
March 26, 1925
BHAI SAHEB,
I have received your telegram. Does the money have to be
sent in lump sum? I did not say so to the friend who has pro-
mised to send it; but there should be no harm if it is paid well
in time every month. Please send the reply to the Ashram ad-
dress. When are you going to Europe? Has there been another
riot in Delhi? Who was at fault? I know nothing about it. I
heard something only on reaching Madras. I hope you are keep-
ing well.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
HAKIM SAHEB AJMAL KHAN
DELHI
From the Urdu original: A. M. Khwaja Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
