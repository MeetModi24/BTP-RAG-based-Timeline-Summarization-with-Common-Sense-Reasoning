218. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
DANDI,
April 10, 1930
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your two letters. I have no time to write a long letter.
I am happy to know that you are happy. I suppose you do get
Navajivan, etc., there. Ramdas has been jailed. Devdas has been
arrested. Jamnalalji and Kishorelal have also been sentenced
to imprisonment. Indeed hundreds have been jailed. There is
no limit to people's enthusiasm.
I am sure that you will mix with the people there as natural-
ly as sugar mixes with milk.
Blessings from
BAPU
