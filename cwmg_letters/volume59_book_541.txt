541. LETTER TO H. P. MODY
December 15, 1934
DEAR MR. MODY,
The Assam Relief Committee write to say that they have not
received any blankets from you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI H. P. MODY
MILL OWNERS' ASSOCIATION
BOMBAY FORT
From the original: H. P. Mody Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
