208. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
AHMEDABAD,
June 30 [1916]
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
It is no use having Tota Ram there. I have met him. He
will not help. Indenture system has to go because it is bad in
itself and is a remnant of slavery. If it can be done away with
today, it should be without considering any interest whatsoever.
With love
MOHAN
From the original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5734. Courtesy: Rajmohini
Rudra, Allahabad
