124. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
January 20, 1933
DEAR SATISBABOO,
Have you at all interested yourself in the opposition that is
being raised in Bengal against the Yeravda Pact, and can you
tell me what is at the back of it?
I hope you received my previous letter and that you are
keeping well.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18980
