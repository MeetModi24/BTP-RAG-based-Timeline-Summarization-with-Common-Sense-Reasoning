395. LETTER TO CHAMAN KAVI
October 12, 1932
It does not seem likely that I shall get the permission to write
what you suggest. If anybody suspects my motives, God alone
can remove his suspicion. Personally I feel that I have served all
religions. Many friends have even realized this. If my claim is
true, the fact will not remain hidden for ever. God, who inspired
me to undertake the fast, will also make all people realize its
significance.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 132
