93. LETTER TO RANCHHODLAL PATWARI
AHMEDABAD,
Vaishakh Vad 12 [June 9, 1915]
RESPECTED SHRI RANCHHODBHAI,
I have your letter. Thanks for your criticism. The portion
concerning parents is written with the best intentions but I shall
make changes in it all the same. As for the other points, instead
of carrying on a discussion in letters, I hope to have a full talk
about them when we meet. I will sacrifice this life itself to
uphold the sanatana dharma as I understand it.
I want coarse cloth as well as fine hand-woven cloth. I shall
be glad, therefore, if you send it from there as well as from
Palanpur. Please send the loom and the man who will teach how
to operate it, as soon as they can possibly arrive.
Respectful greetings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 2798; also
G. N. 4114
