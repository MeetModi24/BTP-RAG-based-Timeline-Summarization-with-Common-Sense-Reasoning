385. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
August 20, 1938
CHI. AMRITLAL,
Most probably the Nagpur students will come. If Mahadevi
wants to come, let her come.
But what will she do?
I of course continue to observe silence. Who knows what I
shall think it right to do when the students arrive?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10765
