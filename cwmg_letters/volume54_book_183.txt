183. LETTER TO S. C. GHOSH
March 23, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
What is the use of asking me questions which you have asked?
What matters is not what I believe but what you believe and your
belief will come to you through prayer and service of God's crea-
tures.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. C. GHOSH
96 BELTALA ROAD
KALIGHAT
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 20673
