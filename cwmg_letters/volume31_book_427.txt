427. LETTER TO BANARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Saturday, Bhadra Sud 4 [September 11, 1926]
BHAI BANARASIDASJI,
Here is my message.
I am much pained to hear that Indians in Fiji are sunk
deep in the drink-evil. May God save them from it!
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SHRI BANARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
FEROZABAD (U.P.)
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2565
