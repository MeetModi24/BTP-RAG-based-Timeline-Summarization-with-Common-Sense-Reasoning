457. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
NANDI HILL,
May 13, 1936
DEAR BROTHER,
Yes, I had need of sympathy on Ansari's death. Your
letter was therefore most welcome. Few deaths make me discon-
solate. This one has made me so. Not only were our hearts one
but we had so much in common. We had some joint plans also
in view. All these have been dashed to pieces at a stroke. Your
recalling the old days in Delhi adds to the poignancy of the grief.
I do hope you are well. We must meet before Sardar and
I leave this place.
Love.
M. K. GANDHI
Letters of Srinivasa Sastri, p. 286
