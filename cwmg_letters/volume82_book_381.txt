381. LETTER TO DHOLKE
ON THE STEAMER; MIDNAPORE,
December 25, 1945
BHAI DHOLKE,
I have your letter and also the newspaper cutting. I per-
sonally do not like all these things. Even a well-meaning report
can do harm. I had never thought that any part of the report
was going to be published. In parts it is not even true. What
can I say about the comment appearing in Navabharat and The
Nagpur Times?
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
