232. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
December 13, 1941
CHI. PRABHA,
How is it that there is not a single letter from you? Ba makes
inquiries every day. How are you and what are you doing? And is
your work now properly arranged? My health is fine. There is
no sign of winter yet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3563
