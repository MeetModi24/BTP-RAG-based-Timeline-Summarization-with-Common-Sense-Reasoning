123. LETTER TO D. V. GOKHALE
SASSOON HOSPITAL,
POONA,
February 29, 1924
DEAR MR. GOKHALE,
I was pained to see a paragraph in the Kesari referring to what
little I am doing to understand the dispute between the Moslem
trustees and the Hindus concerned. I would like you, if you could,
to prevent further reference to my work in connection with it. Any
such publicity, in my opinion, lessens my usefulness for truce.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SH. D. V. GOKHALE
POONA
From a photostat: G. N. 5213
