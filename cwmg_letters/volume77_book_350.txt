350. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
[July 31, 1944]
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
What about Amritlal? Do bring Prahlad here if you wish
to. How is the new arrangement working? If there is much
work in the field, some of us should give to it as many hours
as we can spare. The work of weeding and so on is easy. Stock
the grass, etc., in the godown.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10614
