148. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
WARDHA,
November 19, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your letter. You have done well in sending me a
small quantity of ragi. I simply wanted to have a specimen. I
shall now try it. Is it used only for making chapatis or is it used
like rice also? What is the price of it? I inquire in order to com-
pare prices, not to send you the money. I understand your diffi-
culty about the A. I. V. I. A. I quite see that you should not be-
come an ordinary member. I shall look up the rules and find out
whether you can come in cheaply under any classification. Proba-
bly Bharatan or Kumarappa will write to you.
When I have tried the ragi you have sent me, I shall tell
you whether you have to send more.
Next time when you come you should come to stay at least
for a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI AMBUJAM
MADRAS
