412. LETTER TO TEJWANTI DHIR
SEVAGRAM,
November 15, 1944
CHI. TEJWANTI,
I have your letter. If you want you may stay in Lalaji's
house. If you are not perfectly all right within three months,
take more time. Go to the village only when you are fully re-
covered.
Blessings from
BAPU
TEJWANTIBEHN
ADAMPUR
