13. LETTER TO G. A. NATESAN
BOMBAY,
August 9 [1919]
DEAR MR. NATESAN,
May I thank you for looking after Devdas during his illness
and will you please convey my thanks to Dr. Krishnasamy for his
great attention to Devdas.
You will not hesitate to criticize my writings and doings when
you feel the necessity.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: G. N. 2931
