363. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
December 28, 1946
DAUGHTER,
I have your letters. Jawaharlal is here with me. What
you say is right. Fast and be happy. Some attendant, male
or female, ought to be with you. After all He, the Master of
us all, is omnipotent. More some other time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 548
1 Premier, Madras province
2 The reference is to the Madras Government's khadi scheme and the
Premier's announcement that no new textile mills would be started in the
Madras province. The scheme had met with criticism from many quarters.
