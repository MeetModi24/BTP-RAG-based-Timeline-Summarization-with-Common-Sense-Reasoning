121. LETTER TO ABDUL DADAR BEG
May 25, 1940
DEAR MIRZA SAHEB,
Herewith Shri Garg's reply to your indictment. I would like to
have your reaction to it. Political differences there will be. But all
bitterness between parties should be avoided.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
