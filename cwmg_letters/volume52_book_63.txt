63. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
November 24, 1932
DEAR MAJOR BHANDARI,
With reference to my letter to you of the 14th instant sub-
mitting certain names for approval by Government as visitors in
connection with the late Dr. P. J. Mehta's estate, as the widow
and the heirs of the estate are anxious for an early settlement of
affairs of the deceased, may I request that the matter may be
treated by the Government as urgent?
Yours sincerely,
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(2), Pt. I, p. 347
