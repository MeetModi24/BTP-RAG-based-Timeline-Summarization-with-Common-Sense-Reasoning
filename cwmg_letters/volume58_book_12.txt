12. LETTER TO LALJI K. PARMAR
May 22, 1934
CHI. LALJI,
I got your letter. I was pleased.
My effort will have suc-
ceeded if you now remain vigilant and go on improving daily.
Write to me from time to time. Do you get any pay? How do
you live?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3296
