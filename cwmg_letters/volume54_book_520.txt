520. LETTER TO P. H. GADRE
April 20, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter and your notes on the Harijan quarters in
Nasik.
What help do you want from Seth Mathuradas Vasanji?
If I knew definitely, I might be able to do something.
I note what you say about the quotations from the other saints.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. H. GADRE
PLEADER
NASIK
From a microfilm: S.N. 20997
