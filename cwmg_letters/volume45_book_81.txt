81. LETTER TO VANAMALA PARIKH
January 5, 1931
CHI. VANAMALA,
I get your letters. Your speed on the spinning-wheel can be
described as good, but you should increase it still further. Can a
mistake committed through carelessness be forgiven? After writing
a letter, you should go through it once.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I saw your slip after writing this letter.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5758
