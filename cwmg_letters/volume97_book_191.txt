191. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 15, 1932
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
You may now write long letters to me. Do write. What progress have
you made with regard to the spinning-wheel? Have you completed the writing
of the Ramayana? Are you writing on any other subject? Has the body been
built up? Has your mind become clear and tension free? Has the land been
