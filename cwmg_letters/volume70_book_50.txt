50. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 31, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I had no time to write earlier. I was immersed in Harijan
work. The heap of correspondence still lies before me.
We had an exacting journey—crowds unusual everywhere.
There was no rest till reaching Gwalior, i. e., 2 a. m. I have not
got at the bottom of this sudden manifestation.
Mahadev is still in Calcutta.
Mira came in yesterday without notice. I knew that she was
to return but did not know when. The place is filling up.
Aryasamajists occupy much of my attention.
We are all keeping well. Mira has a bad cough and consti-
pation.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3935. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7244
