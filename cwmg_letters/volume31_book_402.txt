402. LETTER TO PYARELAL NAYYAR
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday, Bhadra Sud 1 [September 8, 1926]
CHI. PYARELAL,
I got your long letter. I understand your dilemma. It had
all along been my desire to see that there was no excess of any-
thing. Even excess of hesitation may sometimes appear as a sign
of incivility. Now that you have adopted a particular manner,
I don't see any need for you to change it. If you try, that may
even give a shock to Mathuradas . You should, therefore, go on
as at present. Write to me when you run short of money. I
hope you got the khadi. I suppose that the article is ready now.
See that you improve your health. Keep writing to me.
PYARELALJI
HOMI VILLA
PANCHGANI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12274
