595. LETTER TO ANNE MARIE PETERSEN
March 2, 1933
MY DEAR MARIA,
I enclose herewith letter from Esther which I have no doubt
you will like and prize. We shall all look forward to their com-
ing in autumn and it will be a great relief to me.
Mary Barr wrote to me that she was likely to accompany
you during your visit to Kashmir. I hope it is coming off and
that it will do you good and give you a little bit of bracing air
and rest to your tired limbs and mind.
I hope you are getting your copy of the Harijan regularly.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 20431
