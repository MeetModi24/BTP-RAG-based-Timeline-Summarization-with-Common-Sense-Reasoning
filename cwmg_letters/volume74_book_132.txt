132. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 29, 1941
DEAR IDIOT,
YourS. Strange no letter from you today. I do hope you have
no more bad jaw. If it is not good, you must consult Jivraj.
Mani coming here tomorrow.
Ba is keeping well.
I [am] first class.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Can you remember where Pakistan literature could be? Kanu
searched and found nothing. If you have any notion, wire and
tell me also how your jaw is behaving.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4023. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7332
