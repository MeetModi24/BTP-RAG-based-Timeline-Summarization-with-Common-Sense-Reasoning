77. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
POONA,
November 9, 1945
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
I have your letter. It was, and is indeed good that you
did not come. To cling to service is more important than seeing
me. You must not start talking about having already grown old.
Service when done for the love of God is not burdensome.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DAHYABHAI MANORDAS PATEL
DHOLKA
PRANTIJ RAILWAY
From Gujarati : C.W. 2715. Courtesy: Dahyabhai M. Patel
