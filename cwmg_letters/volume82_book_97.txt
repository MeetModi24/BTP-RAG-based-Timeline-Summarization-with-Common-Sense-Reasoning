97. LETTER TO SHRIPATICHARAN SAHU
POONA,
November 11, 1945
BHAI SHRIPATI BABU,
I have your letter. I don't know how much I shall be able
to do in Bengal. Please keep all the literature ready. I don't
believe that there is any grief that cannot be overcome.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SHRIPATICHARAN SAHU
C/O CALCUTTA INS. LTD.
15 CLIVE STREET
CALCUTTA
