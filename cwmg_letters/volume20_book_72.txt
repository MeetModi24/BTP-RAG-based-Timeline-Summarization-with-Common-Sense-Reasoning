72. LETTER TO HASSAN IMAM
[After May 27, 1921]
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and enclosure. You may depend
upon my doing all I can to allay excitement and prevent trouble.
It seems difficult for me to leave this part of India for this month
at any rate.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat of the draft in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 7537
