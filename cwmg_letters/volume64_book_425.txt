425. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
March 4, 1937
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
You must have reached there safely. I do feel a little wor-
ried about your health. If you carry out my suggestions, the
fits of giddiness will certainly disappear. Do put mud-packs on
the stomach and the head.
They are bound to benefit you.
Don't feel the slightest hesitation or shame about eating onions
and garlic. There is a letter from Saraswati addressed to you
which I am sending with this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3488
