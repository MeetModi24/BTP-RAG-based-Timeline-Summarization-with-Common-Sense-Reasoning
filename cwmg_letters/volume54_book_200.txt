200. LETTER TO DR. HARRY J. EHRLICH
March 24, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. The name of one book is
Keener Vision Without Glasses by Benjamin Gayelord Hauser, and
the address is Tempo Books Inc., 580, Fifth Avenue, New York.
Yours sincerely,
DR. HARRY J. EHRLICH
84 CHURCH ST.
NEW BRUNSWICK, N. J.
