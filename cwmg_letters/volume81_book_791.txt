791. LETTER TO J. BARUA
October 29, 1945
SIR,
What you say is quite right. The word "easily" was used by
me through ignorance. It was not deleted from the later edition
through oversight. I have now written to the Press. Forgive me
for this lapse. You can publish this letter.
J. BARUA
C/O G. N. TAGORE
BAJAJ BHAVAN
TILAK NAGAR, KANPUR
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
