462. LETTER TO V. L. PHADKE
January 5, 1934
BHAI MAMA,
I have your letter. I have suggested not that the manage-
ment of the institutions should be handed over to the Harijan
Sevak Sangh but that the Harijan Sevak Sangh should provide
their expenses. From now on, therefore, it will be necessary to
satisfy the Sangh. When somebody provides us funds it is only
fair that we satisfy them about the way we spend the funds and
the way we run the institutions.
Sardar did not mean by the words used in his letter 'the
other world'.
He meant 'in jail' or 'outside jail'. Is not that,
too, 'the other world'? We can call it 'the other world' on the
earth. But you need not get alarmed or start worrying because of
that. For the present, you are expected to devote yourself ex-
clusively to your [work]. Those who wish to go to 'the other
world' on the earth will certainly get their turn. It is a matter
of a few days only. You must be getting the news about me
from Harijanbandhu.
Blessings from
BAPU
