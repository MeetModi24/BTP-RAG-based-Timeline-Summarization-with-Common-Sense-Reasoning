183. LETTER TO URMILA DEVI
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1945
CHI. URMILA,
I hope you will understand my Hindi. I shall certainly talk
to Jawaharlal.
Arrangements will be made for you here. You will get a
room with a lavatory near by. You will be able to cook for
yourself.
I shall be busy with my work in Sodepur but you can
come whenever you wish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
