185. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
BOMBAY,
September 30, 1944
CHI. ANAND,
I am leaving for Wardha today. I have your letter. You
may come over to Wardha when you want. Be at peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANAND HINGORANI
SHARDA VILLA
FYZABAD ROAD
LUCKNOW, U. P.
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India and
Anand T. Hingorani
