71. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
October 8, 1927
CHI. MIRA,
I am not going to write to you every day. For I fancy you do
not need any soothing ointment. The wound must be healed by
this time. And your letter from the Ashram reassures me.
Yes, you may take up the dairy work or whatever you like.
How about your feeds ? Chhotelal's message is unacceptable.
He must write and that fully.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5285. Courtesy: Mirabehn
