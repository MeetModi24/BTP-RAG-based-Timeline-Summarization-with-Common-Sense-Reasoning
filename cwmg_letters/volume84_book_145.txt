145. LETTER TO BHAGAVADACHARYA
SIMLA,
May 7, 1946
BHAI BHAGAVANDAS,
I have your letter. You were right to point out my error,
although I do not quite see it as such.
Ramanama is very dear to me. I can easily give it up if I
feel that it is an infatuation.
What you write about Harijan is correct. The fault ought to be
removed. I am sending on to Jivanji the relevant portion of your letter.
It should not be a subject for complaint that I cannot see
the friends from Kathiawar. I am perpetually faced with the
question whom I should see and whom I need not.
Blessings from
BAPU
SWAMI BHAGAVADACHARYA
RAJNAGAR SOCIETY ROAD
AHMEDABAD 7
