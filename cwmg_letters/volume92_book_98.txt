98. LETTER TO RAMANAND CHATTERJI
SABARMATI,
August 14, 1929
DEAR RAMANAND BABU,
I have your private letter. The information you give me
does not surprise me. Personal jealousy has been the bane of our
public life. I have destroyed your letter.
In the absence of instructions from Dr. Sunderland the step
you took was inevitable.
I thank you for copies you were sending me of this pro-
ceeding.
Yours,
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/118
