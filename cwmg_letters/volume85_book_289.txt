289. LETTER TO DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
NEW DELHI,
September 2, 1946
CHI. SUSHILA,
Mataji wished you were here today. For no particular
reason. She just expressed a fond wish since today a popular
government is assuming office.
I have no doubt that your place is there till Vijaya ar-
rives. I shall be here certainly till the 10th. Maybe I shall have
to spend the whole month here. Let us see what God wills.
Bal must have gone to Calcutta.
I hope you are well and the work of the clinic is progressing
well. There is no letter from you. Chand has gone to Amritsar.
The weather cannot be said to be too warm.
SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM
