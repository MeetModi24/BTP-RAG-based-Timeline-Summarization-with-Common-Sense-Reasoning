219. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
April 12, 1929
CHI. MIRA,
I constantly think of you. This leanness of body won't do.
You must have enough flesh on you to support your big frame.
But of course there is no hurry. Do insist on having a room to
yourself where you can shut yourself in if you like.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5363. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9419
