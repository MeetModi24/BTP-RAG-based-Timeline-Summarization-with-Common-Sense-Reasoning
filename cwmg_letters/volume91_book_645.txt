645. LETTER TO CHHOTALAL MANSINHA
ASHRAM,
Jeth Vad 3, Tuesday [June 5, 1928]
BHAI CHHOTALAL,
I have your letter. We cannot discuss the suicide of the
late Acharya Bhatt in Navajivan. Hindu society can be reformed
by everyone adopting the necessary changes in his family.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 27588
