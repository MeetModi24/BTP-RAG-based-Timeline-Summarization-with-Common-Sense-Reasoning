676. LETTER TO SHANKARBHAI MANEKLAL DESAI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 5, 1928
BHAI SHANKARBHAI 1 ,
I have your letter. Give this message from me to all who
may come on the Rentia Baras day2, that if they want to per-
petuate Haribhai's memory in Kapadwanj they should perpetuate
the work that he did. Whatever the difficulties none of the
activities started by him should be abandoned or neglected.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-3: Kusumbehn Desaine, p. 82
