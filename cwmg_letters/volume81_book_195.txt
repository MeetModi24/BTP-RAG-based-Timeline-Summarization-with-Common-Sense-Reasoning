195. LETTER TO GOVERNOR OF BENGAL
SEVAGRAM,
August 14, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Many thanks for your letter of 8th instant received yesterday
afternoon.
I shall gladly avail myself of your kind offer of assistance,
if I need it. Of course I know Mr. Symonds very well.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
HIS EXCELLENCY THE GOVERNOR OF BENGAL
CALCUTTA
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 104
