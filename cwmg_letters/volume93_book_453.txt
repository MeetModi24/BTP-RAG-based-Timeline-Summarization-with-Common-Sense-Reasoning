453. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
Tuesday [October 10, 1939]
CHI. MRIDU,
I am observing silence today. I may break it at 3 o'clock.
But it is impossible to find time today. Tomorrow is the meeting
of the Working Committee. It would depend on how much time
it takes. Mahadev will find out and tell you. Bharati can cer-
tainly meet me on Thursday. You will of course stay on till we
can meet.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11209. Courtesy: Sarabhai Founda-
tion
