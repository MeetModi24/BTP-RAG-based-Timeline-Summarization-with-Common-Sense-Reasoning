706. LETTER TO NAVNIT SHAH
October 20, 1945
BHAI NAVNIT,
I have your letter. You do not need a message from me.
It has become a mania to ask for messages. Where is the need
for messages while one does good work? Good work itself is the
message. If young people understand this much they would be
free of many worldly problems.
I have read your description. The money collected by you
will be used for Harijan work.
NAVNIT SHAH
SHREE YUVAK SANGH
P. B. 726 KAMPALA (UGANDA)
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
