135. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
November 17, 1945
CHI. SUMI,
I have your letter. The less you adorn the letters, the better
they look. I realize more and more each day that handwriting
can be made into an art. What sort of animal is this "study"1?
I hope the water shortage is over.
We shall all leave here on the 19th and reach Sevagram on
the 21st.
Blessings from
BAPU
SUMITRA GANDHI
PILANI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
