380. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
April 18, 1936
CHI. VALJI,
Do go to Nainital. It would be good if Jivram gives up his
insistence on attaching his mother-in-law's name to the donation.
If he does not, we shall have the name.
There is little likelihood of getting an extensive grazing
ground for Rs. 500. He might entrust it to Gopabandhu Chow-
dhary to utilize the sum for the service of the cow.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Your letter was delivered only yesterday as it did not bear
the correct address.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7477 Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
