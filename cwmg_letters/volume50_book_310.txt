310. LETTER TO A GIRL
July 31, 1932
Everything written in books should not be accepted as sacred
truth. We should not believe anything which violates our moral
sense or justifies cruelty, no matter where we find it. So long as
we have not acquired the capacity to judge between right and
wrong, we should, in regard to what we read in books, accept the
opinion of elders whom we trust.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 335
