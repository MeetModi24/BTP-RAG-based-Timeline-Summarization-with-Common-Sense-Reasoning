134. LETTER TO MRS. JINNAH
April 30, 1920
Please remember me to Mr. Jinnah and do coax him to learn
Hindustani or Gujarati. If I were you, I should begin to talk to
him in Gujarati or Hindustani. There is not much danger of
you forgetting your English or your misunderstanding each other.
Is there?
Will you do it? Yes, I would ask this even for the love you
bear me.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
