118. LETTER TO WANDA DYNOWSKA
BOMBAY,
September 10, 1944
DEAR UMA 1 ,
All I can say about the affliction through which Poland is
passing is that no small nation of Europe is to expect any real
help from the Allied Powers in spite of their professions to the
contrary. You know I proposed a solution. It was summarily
rejected. Let us rely upon God, the Rock of Ages.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1206. Also C.W. 5101. Courtesy: Wanda
Dynowska
