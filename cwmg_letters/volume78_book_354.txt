354. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEVAGRAM,
November 6, 1944
CHI. MATHURADAS,
What shall I write to you? Give up all worrying, whether
on my account or Dilip's or any other. You will then get well
sooner. You must not write to me yourself but content yourself by
dictating to Dilip. I am tied up with so much work. My health,
however, is co-operating with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
MATHURADAS, BOMBAY
