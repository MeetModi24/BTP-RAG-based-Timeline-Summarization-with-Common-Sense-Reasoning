82. LETTER TO N. S. HARDIKAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 28, 1939
DEAR DR. HARDIKAR3,
I think you have taken the right step. But I do not like your
language. Read the marked passages. They betray anger and
ill will. Much of the grace has gone out of the resignation.
I hope the Mysore friends have been keeping you informed of
the events there.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: N. S. Hardikar Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
