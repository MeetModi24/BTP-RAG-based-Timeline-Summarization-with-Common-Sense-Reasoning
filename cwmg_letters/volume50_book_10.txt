10. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
June 3, 1932
CHI. PREMA,
I write this slip merely because I think I must write some-
thing to you. Don't forget to send the Urdu books. If visits
stop now, send them by registered book post.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10288. Also C.W. 6736. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
