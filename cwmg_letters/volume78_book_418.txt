418. LETTER TO JAYENDRA
November 16, 1944
BHAI JAYENDRA,
I have read your letter carefully. I have no time to set
out my arguments in reply. I shall do as God bids me. Just
now there is no call from Him.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI JAYENDRA, SAHITYARATNA
HINDI PRACHARAK
AMADAL PALLEE
From the Hindi original : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
