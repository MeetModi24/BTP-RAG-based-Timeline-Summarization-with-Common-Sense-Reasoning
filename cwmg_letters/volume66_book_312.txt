312. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
October 18, 1937
CHI. KAKA,
Do send for all the three. We shall admit them. I also
like the suggestion regarding Gopalrao. But what you have
written is all right. I am writing to Nayakam.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Accept Vora's suggestion.
I have read about Armstrong
earlier. I shall make use of it.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7707
