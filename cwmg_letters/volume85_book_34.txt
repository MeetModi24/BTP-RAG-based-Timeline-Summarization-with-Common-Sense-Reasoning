34. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
PANCHGANI,
July 22, 1946
BHAI RAJENDRA BABU,
I have your letter. A short preface is enclosed herewith.
You may send it if you like it.
I hope you are fully recovered by now.
I shall be in Poona on the 28th and expect to reach Seva-
gram by the 6th or the 7th August.
What happened to the Urdu book?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
