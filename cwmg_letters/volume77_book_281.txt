281. LETTER TO ASHFAQ HUSSAIN
July 17, 1944
MY DEAR ASHFAQ1,
I never knew that you were a careless reader. Do you not
see that if the thing was to be put on the League Committee
without the President sponsoring it, it should be before the public
for discussion? The League and others can now pronounce upon it.
Expect to see you at Sevagram.
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
