191. LETTER TO DAS
NEW DELHI,
June 28, 1947
DEAR DAS,
I wrote out a note the moment I got Mrs. Sen Gupta's letter.
I am averse to multiplying newspapers which crop up like
mushrooms.
Mrs. Sen Gupta should not be dragged into this thing.
Despriya's memory would be better served by doing his
work silently and selflessly.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
