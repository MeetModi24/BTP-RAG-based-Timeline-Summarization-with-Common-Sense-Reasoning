477. LETTER TO GULZARILAL NANDA
September 8, 1934
MY DEAR GULZARILAL,
I am glad you are keeping me informed of what is going
on there. It is a fine thing that the proceedings are being con-
ducted in a perfectly orderly manner. That means real solid
work.
I am keeping quite well.
SHRI GULZARILAL NANDA
45 CHOWPATTY
BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
