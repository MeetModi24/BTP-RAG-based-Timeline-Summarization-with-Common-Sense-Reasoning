158. LETTER TO KASHI GANDHI
June 18, 1930
CHI. KASHI,
I hope you are keeping well. I take it that you are at Vijapur. I will not
write separately to Krishnadas. He should write to me a detailed letter. Who
are the other persons with you there?
Blessings from
BAPU
