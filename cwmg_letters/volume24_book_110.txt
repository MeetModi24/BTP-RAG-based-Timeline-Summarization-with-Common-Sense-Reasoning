110. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
Jeth Sud 6 [June 8, 1924]
BHAISHRI DEVCHANDBHAI,
Someone has sent me the enclosed extract. Please see the
portion underlined by me. Is the statement true? If true, who
did the thing?
Vallabhbhai, Devdas, Ba-all will leave together, on the 10th
evening most probably.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: G.N. 5732
