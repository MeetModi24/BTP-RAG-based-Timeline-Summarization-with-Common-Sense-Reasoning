193. LETTER TO DR. D. S. SARDESAI
October 9, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
Swami Anand has sent me your report upon my grand-
daughter's bad eyes.
I thank you for all the attention you are
giving to the little child.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. D. S. SARDESAI, L.R.C.P & S.
SANDHURST RD.
GIRGAON, BOMBAY
From a photostat: G.N. 8827
