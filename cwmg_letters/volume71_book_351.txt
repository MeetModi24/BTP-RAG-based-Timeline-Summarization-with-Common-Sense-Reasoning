351. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 23, 1940
CHI. MANILAL,
Read the accompanying letter. I have written to Haji Ismail
Bhabha and asked him to see you and discuss the matter with
you. You are brothers, not enemies. I have also informed him
that I do not interfere with you in your policy. You yourself
should try to meet him.
You know about things here through newspapers. I will ask
Pyarelal to give you some news.
Ba is fine, and so am I.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4911
