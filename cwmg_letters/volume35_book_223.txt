223. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
JAFFNA,
November 28, 1927
MY DEAR BHRRR1,
Have read the story of Shireen and Farhad? Have you ever
known lovers tired of hearing from or about the loved ones? Now
do you wonder why I was thirsting for your letter? Similar is
the story of how I discovered Raihana.
Now love.
Yours,
BHRRR
From a photostat: S.N. 9560
