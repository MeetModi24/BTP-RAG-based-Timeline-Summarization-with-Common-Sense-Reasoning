135. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
BOMBAY,
[May 12, 1942]
CHI. AMRIT,
Ashadevi has arrived, also the post. It is difficult to write
today. I am collecting money. Everything is going on well.
Arrange about Balkrishna in Delhi. You must leave on the
28th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4262. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N.
7894
