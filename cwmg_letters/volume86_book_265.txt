265. LETTER TO MANU S. MASHRUWALA
December 8, 1946
CHI. MANUDI,
I was very glad to see your handwriting. I am of course
engrossed in work, but I would welcome letters from you all.
Do not worry on my account. I made khakhari only once. Who
will let me work? With the great God above to protect me,
why need you worry? I get plenty of dry fruits, etc.
I hope all of you are well. Do not spoil your health. It
is 8.35 p.m. now and I must, therefore, stop.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 1583. Courtesy: Manu S.
Mashruwala
