249. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
February 15, 1931
BHAI VALJI,
I got your letter. Do stay there as long as necessary. Do
[not] forget mother khadi and mother cow. The Government
is acting like one who is doomed to destruction and whose reason,
therefore, is perverted, for it has been issuing all sorts of arbitrary
ordinances. I am hanging in mid-air. It seems likely that I
shall have to go to Delhi. How long it will be necessary for
me to stay there will depend on the Viceroy's attitude. From
Delhi, it seems I shall have to go to Bombay. I very much wish
that you would learn the art of preserving your health. As for
your questions, you must wait till we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7413. Courtesy: Valji Desai
