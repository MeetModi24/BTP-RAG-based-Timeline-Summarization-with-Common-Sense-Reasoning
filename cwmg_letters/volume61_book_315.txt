315. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
July 1, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
Read my letter to Harilal. It will perhaps help you to see
what you should do. I am not yet ready to order you, but I think
Bali Kumi and Manu are right. Please warn me beforehand if
you think they are wrong. If they appear right to you and if
Harilal does not confess his faults, then, you cannot keep him at
all. In no circumstances can anything be given to him in the shape
of help from public funds. He may certainly earn something by
doing work. This sums up my advice. Think over it and act
according to the promptings of your heart.
Enclosed please find Mathew's letter. Prema's letters are such
that they promptly elicit fitting replies.
What about the workshop?
Blessings from
BAPU
