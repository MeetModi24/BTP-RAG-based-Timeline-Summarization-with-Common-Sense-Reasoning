84. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 21, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
Where is the book that Prema writes about? I don't re-
member to have received it. I have entered silence at 6 this
evening. Silence always suits me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Send over Verrier's letter.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11553
