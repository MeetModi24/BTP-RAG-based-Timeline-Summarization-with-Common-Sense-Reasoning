37. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
July 23, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Your catching malaria is a matter of concern. Maybe, you
would have been spared if you had properly used the mosquito-
net. In my opinion the principle of not taking any treatment
cannot hold good for everybody.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4519
