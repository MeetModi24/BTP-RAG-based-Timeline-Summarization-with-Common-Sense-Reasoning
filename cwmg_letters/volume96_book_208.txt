208. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
April 29 [1915]
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I can only send you goodwill by the mail that leaves tomorrow.
I am just going to a meeting and from there to a place further away
so that if I do not send you this line now I cannot write at all this
week.
With apologies and love,
Yours,
OLD FRIEND
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
