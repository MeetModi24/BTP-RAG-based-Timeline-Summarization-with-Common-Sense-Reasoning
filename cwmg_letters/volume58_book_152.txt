152. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
July 2, 1934
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. I will inquire about the ink. I have not
been able to get it so far. Anyone can make ink, but there are
many difficulties in making ink of good quality. Hardly anybody
succeeds. You may go to the Harijan Ashram whenever you like
after discussing the matter with Parikshitlal. Remember Octo-
ber, however. Don't go even during the last week of that month.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI V A S U M A T I B E H N
DAULATRAM KASHIRAM CO.
KHAN MAHOMAD KASAMBHAI BUILDING
KARLEWADI, THAKURDWAR, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9388. Also C.W. 633. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
