605. LETTER TO DIWAKAR SINGH
March 3, 1933
MY DEAR DIWAKAR SINGH,
I am glad you wrote to me. I had already seen a notice
in the papers about your work. If you will stick to it, I am
sure that the work will prosper. The quieter it is, the better it
will be. Do please continue to report progress. N. is here just
now and I have shown your letter to her. She is happy over
the beginning made by you and she agrees with me about the
necessity of avoiding showy demonstration and she adds that this
she can say from bitter experience.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
KUNWAR DIWAKAR SINGH
3 CANNING ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From the original: C.W. 9655. Courtesy: Municipal Museum, Allahabad.
