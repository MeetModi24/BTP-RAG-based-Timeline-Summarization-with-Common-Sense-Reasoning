158. LETTER TO E. RAGHAVENDRA RAO
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 10, 1938
DEAR RAGHAVENDRA RAO,
I have your letter of 6th inst.
Mahadev has already asked
you to send a copy of your first letter. I want to work at it
without in any way disclosing your name. All I wish to say at
this stage is that you should send facts and figures to support
your conclusions.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a facsimile: Madhya Pradesh aur Gandhiji, p. 130
