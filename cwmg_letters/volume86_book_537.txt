537. LETTER TO MADALASA
January 30, 1947
CHI. MADALASA,
I have your letter. I would like you to go to Gulbehn be-
cause her company is what I call satsang. Moreover the climate
of Poona is very good for you and the children. If you go to
Poona, do visit Uruli-Kanchan also. You must have got my
earlier letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 331
