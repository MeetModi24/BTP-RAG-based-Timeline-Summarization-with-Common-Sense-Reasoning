269. LETTER TO M. L. GUPTA
August 1, 1926
BHAISHRI,
I got your questions. I think it unnecessary to discuss such
questions in Navajivan. There is certainly life in every plant.
Since plants are for man's use, we may use twigs for brushing
teeth so long as we also eat vegetable foods. When we commit
so much needless violence, why should we raise such fine issues
and muddle our minds?
M. L. GUPTA
AJMER
From a microfilm of the Hindi: S.N. 19939
