212. LETTER TO SIR WALTER LAWRENCE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 20, 1906
DEAR SIR,
Mr. Ally and I have arrived from South Africa as a Deputation
on behalf of the British Indians in the Transvaal. We would be obliged
if you would be good enough to give us an opportunity to place the
position before you.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
SIR WALTER LAWRENCE, K.C.I.E.
SLOANE STREET, S.W.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4599
