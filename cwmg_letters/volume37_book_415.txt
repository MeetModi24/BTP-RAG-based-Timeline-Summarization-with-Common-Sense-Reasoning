415. LETTER TO DR. C. MUTHU
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 16, 1928
DEAR DR. MUTHU,
I have your letter. You won't ask me to sign any appeal
please. You won't find my name in any appeal save those which
1 Vide "The Fiery Ordeal", 30-9-1928. The article appeared in Hindi
Navajivan, 4-10-1928.
2 The typewritten office copy (S.N. 13556) of this letter bears the note:

I have myself inaugurated, for the simple reason that if I sign one
I should have to sign many, and I do not like the idea of having
to do so. I hope however that you will be able soon to finish
the sanatorium and realize your ambition.
Yours sincerely,
DR. C. MUTHU
9 MONTEITH ROAD, EGMORE, MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 13555
