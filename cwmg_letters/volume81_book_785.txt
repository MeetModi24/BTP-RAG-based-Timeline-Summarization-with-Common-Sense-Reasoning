785. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
[October 29, 1945]
MY DEAR C. R.,
I have just received your letter. I do not attach any impor-
tance to unscrupulous propaganda. However, what statement do
you think I should issue? Today trees can only be tapped for
gur not for making intoxicants.
How are you? What is the atmosphere there?
As I cannot yet leave Sardar, my tour has been put off by
one month.
Love.
[PS.]
Madhavan's letter returned.
SHRI C. RAJAGOPALACHARIAR
MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
