643. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
NEW DELHI,
October 19, 1946
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
I have wired to you. I have no information. What can I
tell you? I do not consider myself fit to guide you. Go to Bengal
if you want to.
I am myself getting ready to go there. I have said so in
the Press also. When God will take me there I do not know.
Tell everybody to give up any hope of my returning to Sevagram
for the present. I want to return there but God won't let me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 533
