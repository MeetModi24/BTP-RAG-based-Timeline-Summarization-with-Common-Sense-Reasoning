361. LETTER TO SACHINDRA NATH MAITI
AS AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 30, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I would suggest your studying the consti-
tution of the Ashram published in the pages of Young India and I
would further ask you to live in accordance with its vows at least
for one year and then write to me what progress you have made.
It will be then time enough to take stock and consider whether
you should join the Ashram or not.
Yours sincerely,
SACHINDRA NATH MAITI, ESQ.
MIDNAPORE, BENGAL
From a photostat: S.N. 13827
