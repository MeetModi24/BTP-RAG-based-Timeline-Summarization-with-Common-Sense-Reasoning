492. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
December 4, 1930
CHI. MIRA,
The Ashram post received last evening brings your disturbing
letter. It does not cause anxiety but it furnishes food for reflec-
tion. What is the cause of this breakdown in health? In any
case you must give yourself complete rest. You must relax both
mind and body. Therefore please go gently. Read the 6th chap-
ter [of the] Gita. Yoga should be undertaken gently. What work
we are doing is yoga. Please drop me a card daily.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5423. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9657
