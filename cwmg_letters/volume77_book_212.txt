212. LETTER TO V. P. LIMAYE
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
June 25, 1944
DEAR ACHARYA LIMAYE,
I have seen a copy of the notice. It reads awful. It gives
the occasion a public character. Have you seen it? And it ad-
vertises the time as 4 to 6. I see that I shall be unequal to it.
Not more than half an hour should be given in all. If the ques-
tions are the fewest possible, I can wind up the whole thing in a
few minutes. Let there be an exhaustive report of the work done
sent to me and the questions well beforehand. Let me have also
a list of those invited with their addresses and status in the or-
ganization. I trust that Prof. Javdekar and Bhagwat are included
in the list.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 999. Courtesy: V. P. Limaye
