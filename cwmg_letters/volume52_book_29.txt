29. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
November 20, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
You should not expect any letter from me these days. You
will then be satisfied with a few lines. You, however, should
continue to write to me.
Do not get tired of the food which is similar to that in jail.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./II
