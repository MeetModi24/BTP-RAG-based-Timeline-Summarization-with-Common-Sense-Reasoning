134. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 8, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
The pain in the shoulder does not seem to be wholly due to
fatigue. Massage does give some relief. But it may well be rheumatism.
I am observing.
The blood-pressure is quite satisfactory. It is 150/88. I am able
to eat a lot. Still, it may be necessary to remain on a diet of fruit
for a couple of days.
Sometimes I do feel it would be good if you were by my side.
But I quickly drive the thought from my mind. I consider it is a thought
best avoided till after your examination.
Navin came for two days. Lakshmibehn and Madhuri have
come. Madhuri sang us a bhajan. I have instructed Prabha to write
it down and send it to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
