242. LETTER TO R. P. KARANDIKAR
October 18, 1934
DEAR DADASAHEB,
I was delighted to receive your interesting letter. Much more
so to think that you were applying yourself, at your time of life,
to the Indian problem.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI R. P. KARANDIKAR
SATARA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
