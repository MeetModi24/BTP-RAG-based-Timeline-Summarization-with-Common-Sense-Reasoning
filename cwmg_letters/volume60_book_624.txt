624. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
April 22, 1935
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I wrote a letter to you this morning. After that I had to
write so much that I cannot write with the right hand any more
today.
If you think it necessary to make Munshi the secretary of
the Board you may consider doing so. Now that Ansari has
resigned, will Bhulabhai become the President? If you can
persuade Raja by some means, do so. Has Bidhan resigned too?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 164
