454. LETTER TO TULSIRAM
January 12, 1947
BHAI TULSIRAMJI,
I have your two letters and also the three books. But Sanskrit
Shikshika is not among them. A Hindi, English or Bengali edition
ought to be available in Calcutta. The Bengali must be available
there. Bapa has written that you looked after him extremely well.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
345
