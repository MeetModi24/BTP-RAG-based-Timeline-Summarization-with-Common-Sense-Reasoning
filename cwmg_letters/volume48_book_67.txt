67. LETTER TO S. S. ZAHEER
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W. 1,
September 29, 1931
MY DEAR ZAHEER,
Why do you want me to give you a separate appointment?
Will you not be meeting me at one of the students' meetings?
I want these meetings to be meetings where you ask me all the
questions you like. This arrangement will serve a useful purpose
and save me so very much time.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 17874
