109. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
June 29, 1932
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter here just now. This is just to entrust you with two
letters for Damodardas and his wife. They might feel comforted by
the letters.
I am well. The milk, bread and fruit are progressing.
More later.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6227. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9693
