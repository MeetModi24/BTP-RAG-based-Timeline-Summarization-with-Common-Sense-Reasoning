74. LETTER TO DR. JAYADEV KULKARNI
August 29, 1944
BHAI KULKARNI,
The same question has been raised by others too. I have
answered it. It is impossible for me to be acquainted with all
the circumstances. Therefore it is almost impossible for me
to answer questions individually. I have, of course, laid down the
general rule.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. JAYADEV KULKARNI
577 MATHGALI
BELGAUM
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
