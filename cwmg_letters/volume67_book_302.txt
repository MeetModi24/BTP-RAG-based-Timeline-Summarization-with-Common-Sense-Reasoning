302. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
August 3, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
I have deliberately hardened my heart and am not coming
there to you. You need not reply to this letter. Get well
soon. Do send for me whenever you consider my presence
necessary.
218
My vandemataram to Bhai Daftari.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10903
