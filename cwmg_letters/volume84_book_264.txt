264. LETTER TO NARAYANAMURTI
[May 25, 1946]
BHAI NARAYANAMURTI,
I have talked to Dr. Pattabhi . You should approach the
Provincial Congress Committee. If the book is good, why should
they not publish it ?
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
ANAKAPALLE
VIZ[AGAPATTAM]
