111. LETTER TO KIRON SHANKAR ROY
SODEPUR,
November 6, 1946
DEAR KIRON SHANKAR1,
Please see Sarat Babu to whom I talked last night about
my interview with Muslim friends. My opinion is that you
should all join the proposed advisory committee if it comes into
being.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
