608. LETTER TO V. L. MEHTA
October 10, 1945
BHAI VAIKUNTH,
Only today I read a copy of the article Chi. Kishorelal
had sent to [The Bombay] Chronicle around June 15. It is a short
one which I am enclosing herewith. It will not take you long
to glance through it.
Write to me if you find his suggestions useful and take
further steps if you can. I am writing a similar letter to
Gaganvihari and Shah. Perhaps the cloud (if it is a cloud)
that is hovering over the people will easily be averted if some-
thing is done in time.
VAIKUNTH MEHTA
BOMBAY PROV. CO-OPERATIVE BANK
BACK HOUSE LANE, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
