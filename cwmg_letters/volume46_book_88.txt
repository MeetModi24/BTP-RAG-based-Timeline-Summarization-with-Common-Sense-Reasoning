88. LETTER TO FLORENCE ROSENBLATT
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 5, 1931
DEAR YOUNG FRIEND,
I have your good letter.
You should possess your soul in
patience. Whatever good you might have seen in my life you can
adopt even being there and it would be as good as seeing me,
indeed much better. What you can do effectively for India is to
put in a good word whenever anybody talks uncharitably about
India and her people and in order to be able to put in that good
word with confidence, you have to find time to study the Indian
struggle. You can then speak with authority.
Yours sincerely,
FLORENCE ROSENBLATT
BROOKLYN, NEW YORK (U.S.A)
From a photostat: S.N. 17029
