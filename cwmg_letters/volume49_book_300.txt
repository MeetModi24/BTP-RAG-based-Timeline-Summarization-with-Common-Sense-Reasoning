300. LETTER TO TILAKAN
March 28, 1932
Vanity is emptiness: Self-respect is substance. No one's self-
respect is ever hurt except by self, vanity is always hurt from
outside.
In the phrase 'Seeing God face to face', 'face to face' is not
to be taken literally. It is a matter of decided feeling. God is
formless. He can, therefore, only be seen by spiritual sight-vision.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 52
