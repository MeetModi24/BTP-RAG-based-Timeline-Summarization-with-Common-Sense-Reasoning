266. LETTER TO ANANDSHANKAR B. DHRUVA
January 29, 1933
I have always needed you; and do that more so now, as I have
given you and Malaviyaji the rank of my guru. Therefore you
must act your role.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 100
1 Gandhiji is jestingly alluding to the Bombay sanatanists' charge that
he had come to look upon them as his gurus.
