704. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
October 20, 1945
CHI. KISHORELAL,
The argument that you have put forward has also been
shared by Shah and Kumarappa. It is clear that only independent
India can make such changes. The best of reforms can be im-
plemented only if approved by the people. I am also sending
Gagan Mehta's letter to you. Why should you do typing work?
I believe the Talimi Sangh should do it.
Regarding your health I would like you to come here for
treatment while I am here. But that requires enthusiasm on
your part or detachment like Sardar's. He has no faith in this
system but still he is giving it a trial.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
