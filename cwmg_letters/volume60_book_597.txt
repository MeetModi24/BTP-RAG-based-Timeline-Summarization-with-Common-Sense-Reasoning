597. LETTER TO NARASINHARAO DIVATIA
WARDHA,
April 17, 1935
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I have just heard that you are at present bed-ridden. How
can you afford to be so? You have to live for many more years
still and serve. Haven't you a right to live up to a hundred at
least?
You need not even reply to this letter.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[From Gujarati]
Narasinharaoni Rojnishi, p. 617
