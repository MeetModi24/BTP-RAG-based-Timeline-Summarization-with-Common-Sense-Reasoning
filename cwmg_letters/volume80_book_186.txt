186. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
May 11, 1945
BHAI ICE,
I have your letter. How nice it will be if Hari-ichchha re-
covers completely. The entire credit will go to you. More than
that, flowers will be showered on you from above.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Do something to make the heat there bearable.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
