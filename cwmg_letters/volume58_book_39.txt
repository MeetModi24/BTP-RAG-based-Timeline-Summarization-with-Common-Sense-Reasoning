39. LETTER TO M. R. MASANI
(CAMP) KENDRAPARA,
May 29, 1934
MY DEAR MASANI,
Here is my answer to the questions you left with me. You
will see my answer covers all the questions. With reference to the
resolutions adopted at the First All-India Congress Socialist
Conference I should have very little to say against them if your
programme was sound which for the reasons I have given does
not appear to me to be. The resolution is perhaps bombastic; it
is certainly prolix. Do not hesitate to write to me again when
necessary.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI M. R. MASANI
C/O "THE SUN"
139 MEDOWS STREET
POST Box No. 62, FORT, BOMBAY
From a photostat: C.W. 4883. Courtesy: M. R. Masani. Also G.N. 4125
