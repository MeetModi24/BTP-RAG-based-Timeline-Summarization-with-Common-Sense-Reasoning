113. LETTER TO MADHAVDAS AND
KRISHNABEHN KAPADIA
September 18, 1932
CHI. MADHAVDAS AND KRISHNA,
I got the letters from you both. Do not be perturbed in the
slightest degree by my decision to fast. It is really a matter
for rejoicing. Such an opportunity comes rarely to anyone. I
hope that the effect of the fast on you both will be to increase
your self-control and your devotion to service. Do not let your-
self be made unhappy by your financial difficulties. Resign your-
self to the misfortune. God will protect my body if He wants
to take more work from it, otherwise He will let it perish.
Either way it will be good. Fix the thought in your mind that
not a blade of grass moves without God's will.
I have written this letter after commencing the silence.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
