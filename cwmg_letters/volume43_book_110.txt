110. LETTER TO ABDUL KADIR BAWAZEER
GAJERA,
March 21, 1930
BHAI IMAM SAHEB,
I think about you every day. How shall I describe your love?
I feel extremely happy to see that you have risen very high. The
Ashram is yours and I need not entrust it to your care. Take care
of your health. Qureshi has been doing fine work. I observe that
everyone is pleased with him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6645
