701. LETTER TO PANNALAL JHAVERI
WARDHA,
October 15, 1933
CHI. PANNALAL,
Chimanlal says that you have given up civil disobedience
and now wish to start a dairy. If your faith in civil disobedience
is shaken, you can certainly do something else. But I certainly
expect that before taking any new step you will consult me.
All the three of you will be doing well.
Prabhudas will be married on the 17th. The bride is from
the North.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari-Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 31
