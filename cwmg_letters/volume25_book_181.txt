181. LETTER TO MAHOMED ALI
DELHI,
October 8, 1924
MY DEAR BROTHER,
You are more than a brother to me. I have seen the cow.
My bed was lifted to enable me to see her. What love that has
prompted the act! May the bond between you twins and me
fructify into an indissoluble bond between Hindus and Mussalmans
for the good of our respective faiths, for the good of our country
and for the good of humanity. Yes, God is great. He can work
wonders.
Yours ever,
M. K. GANDHI 2
The Bombay Chronicle, 11-10-1924
