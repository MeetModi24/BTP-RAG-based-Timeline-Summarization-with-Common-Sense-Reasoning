160. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
7 BUITENSINGLE,
March 7 [1914]
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
Here is the inscription in 3 languages. The Tamil one you should
procure there. The dates have to be filled in, in each case.
Yes, it would be sinful to desire death in cowardice but not when
one saddened by grief outside desires it. If properly desired, I think
it uplifts one and fits one better to meet difficulties involving danger
to one's life.
Mrs. Gandhi's state still remains uncertain. Mrs. Alexander called
in to see her and was inquiring about you.
With love,
UPPER HOUSE
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy : National
Archives of India
