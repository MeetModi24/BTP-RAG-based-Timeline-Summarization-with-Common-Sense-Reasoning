205. LETTER TO SIR HENRY COTTON
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 17, 1906
DEAR SIR HENRY,
With reference to your question put to Mr. Churchill, I enclose
herewith cutting from South Africa. I have also written to The Times
and the two brothers of Dr. Godfrey, who are studying for the Bar here,
have also written.
I am,
Yours truly,
Enclosure
SIR HENRY COTTON, M.P.
45, ST. JOHN'S WOOD PARK, N.W.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4593
