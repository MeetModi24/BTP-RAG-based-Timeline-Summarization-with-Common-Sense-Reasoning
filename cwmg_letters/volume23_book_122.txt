122. LETTER TO G. N. KANITKAR
POONA,
February 29, 1924
MY DEAR KANITKAR,
You are hatching before the hen has come to the roost. I have
no idea when the autobiography will be commenced. If, however,
it ever sees the light of day, as far as I am concerned, you shall have
the right of translation. But I am afraid Kaka or Anandaswami
will have the final say in the matter.
If you, therefore, want to
take premature precaution, please write to one of them, or both.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
GAJANAN N. KANITKAR
MANAGING TRUSTEE, S. R. PATHSHALA
CHINCHWAD
From the original: C.W. 956. Courtesy: G. N. Kanitkar
