460. LETTER TO GANGABEHN AND NANIBEHN JHAVERI
September 5, 1931
CHI. GANGABEHN AND NANIBEHN,
I got the letters of you both. I could not see either of you,
but that does not matter. After all, I am not going away for a
long time. I shall be in England maybe two weeks, or at the
most two months. That time will pass quickly.
As for the work to be done by you, you may do what you
choose after consulting Kakasaheb. If Pannalal has gone to
Peshawar and Khurshedbehn wants you, I should like you to go
and join her.
I was very happy to learn that Nanibehn's health is steadily
improving. By the time this letter reaches you, I hope she will
have completely recovered.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3118
