384. LETTER TO JANE HOWARD
ASHRAM, SABARMATI, 1
May 26, 1927
DEAR MISS HOWARD,
I was delighted to receive your letter giving me details about
the late Mr. Howard and yourself. Of your connection with
him and your selfless devotion, I knew nothing. It does one's soul
good to read the account you have sent me, for which I am most
grateful to you.
I hope you are now getting your Young India regularly; if not,
you will please let me know. Do please write to me from time
to time. I am obliged to dictate this letter as I am just now on
the sick-list.
With loving regards from Mrs. Gandhi and myself,
Yours sincerely,
MISS JANE HOWARD
'ROSEMARY'
50, PANDORA ROAD
MALVERN
JOHANNESBURG
From a photostat: S.N. 12354
