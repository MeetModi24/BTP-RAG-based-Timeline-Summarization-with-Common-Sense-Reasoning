458. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
NEW DELHI,
September 23, 1946
CHI. CHAND,
I have your letter. How did you get malaria? Do you
use a mosquito-net? It is good that your blood is improving.
Don't be in a hurry. Come to Sevagram when you have com-
pletely recovered. I plan to leave here for Wardha the day
after tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. CHANDRANI
C/O SARDAR KARMA SINGH
RTD. S.D.O.
KATRA JALLIANWALA
AMRITSAR, PUNJAB
