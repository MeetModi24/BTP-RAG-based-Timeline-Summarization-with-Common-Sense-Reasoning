81. LETTER TO KRISHNALAL T. MANIAR
November 9, 1945
CHI. BACHU,
I am glad you wrote a postcard to me. I saw Kanu's hand-
writing also. May the New Year bring happiness to all of you
and may all of you start rendering some service to the country. At
the present day that is the dharma for all in India. I am glad
you have settled down at the Bank of Jaipur. Become an expert
at your job and stick to the bank so that in the field of your
work you can earn a name for yourself. I know of two persons
who, just like you, began even lower than clerks and yet reached
the top and became famous. Both of them are no more. They
were Gujaratis but it is immaterial whether they were Gujaratis
or not. One is bound to make progress if one sincerely sticks to
something and wishes to acquire mastery in it. May you make
progress in your work and serve the country through it.
Blessings from
BAPU
KRISHNALAL TULSIDAS MANIAR
C/O BANK OF JAIPUR LTD.
RITCHIE ROAD
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
