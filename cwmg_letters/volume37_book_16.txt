16. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN PATHAK
July 2, 1928
BHAI RAMNARAYAN,
I have preserved your letter of April 18 till this day with
the idea of writing to you when I got a little time. I certainly
felt unhappy at your leaving the Vidyapith. I do believe, how-
ever, that though you have given up direct connection with it,
you will always help as much as you can an institution which you
have served. I trust you are keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
