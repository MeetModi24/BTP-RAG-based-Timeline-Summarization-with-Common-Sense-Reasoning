396. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
Monday, ANDHERI
DEAR SISTER,
Radha has just informed me that you are feeling very weak and
so would be going to Simla. I was very sorry to hear about your
illness. I pray to God that you may regain your health quickly. I have
never forgotten your priceless love. I have always thought of you and
the children. I was looking forward to meeting you here, but now I
shall hope to see you only after you recover and come back. In the
meantime, I shall remember your love and shall try to qualify myself
for such true affection.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11156. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
