633. LETTER TO PREMNATH BAJAJ
April 15, 1941
DEAR PREMNATH,
Your complaint is just. I have not replied because I could
not apply my mind to the question and so I shirked a reply as
I must do even now. I would not like to let my perfunctory
note to go forth as my opinion. I fear you should do without
it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
