464. LETTER TO POTHAN JOSEPH
September 8, 1938
MY DEAR POTHAN,
I am on my back just now for the abdominal bandage. Hence
the pencil hand.
I shall read your article.
You may fail ten times out of ten, and you will come to
me as often as a penitent. What my word may have done for
a moment it may also do permanently. There is nothing in me.
But God may speak to you through me.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
