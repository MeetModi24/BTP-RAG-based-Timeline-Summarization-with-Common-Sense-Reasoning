122. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEVAGRAM,
January 26, 1945
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. Why do you have so much attachment?
How nice it would be if you entrusted everything to Harkishan
and stopped worrying! Why should you take it upon yourself to
translate the Shakuntala? What will Jyotsna do with it? And
how? After all it is but a play. A lot of it is very sweet.
Plays are of no use for the kind of life we are living these days.
This life is the real thing. At the most you should ask her to
read the relevant portion from the available translations. But you
have taken pains over it, so now I shall only say that it may
bear fruit. And there can be no doubt that I should like
Jyotsna to learn from it. And that is my blessing for her. Get
well. I am all right. How can Pyarelal or Sushila attend the
wedding? Both of them are immersed in work. We are having a
camp.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
