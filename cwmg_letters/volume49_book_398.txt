398. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
April 18, 1932
CHI. PUSHPA,
If swaraj were to mean Gandhi's rule, it would be a curse.
Swaraj means the rule of all, and that would include Pushpa too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3986. Also C.W. 32. Courtesy:
Pushpabehn N. Naik
