468. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
April 25, 1946
MY DEAR LOTUS-BORN,
Your congratulations wanted for my exemplary patience in deciphering
your indecipherable love-note. You robbed me of my precious 15
minutes. Have I done sufficient penance for my not knowing in time
that you were behind Indu last Sunday ?
Of course Lotus will have some time from me on 29th although
it is my silence-day.
Love.
BAPU
LOTUS-BORN
NEW DELHI
From the original
Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy
Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
