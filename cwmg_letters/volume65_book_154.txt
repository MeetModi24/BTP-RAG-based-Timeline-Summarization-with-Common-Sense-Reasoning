154. LETTER TO BENARSIDAS CHATURVEDI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
May 5, 1937
BHAI BENARSIDAS,
The purpose of the tour of Mauritius, etc., is quite different
from the purpose behind the tour of Sumatra and other countries.
Burma, Sumatra, Java, Siam, etc., have some connection with
Eastern culture and it seems natural enough for them to have
links with Indian languages. It does not of course imply that
they will all learn Hindi, yet one need not be surprised if some
of them do study it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2559
