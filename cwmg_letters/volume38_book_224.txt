224. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
WARDHA,
Saturday, December 8, 1928
CHI. KUSUM,
You never get well-how is that? If your only wish is to come
to me and if you think you will get well then, please do come. I
have written to Bhai Chhaganlal [Joshi] about it. But think about
Prabhavati. At present, however, your first duty is to take care
of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1764
