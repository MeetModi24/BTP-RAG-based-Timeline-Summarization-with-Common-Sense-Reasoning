280. LETTER TO JUGATRAM DAVE
March 24, 1932
BHAI,
You have filled up pages all right. Our gharry is not driven
by human beings. It is driven by God. It will certainly con-
tinue to run as long as we, the passengers, have faith in Him.
The moment we lose faith, be sure that the train will stop.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 8983. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi. Also Mahadev-
bhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 34
