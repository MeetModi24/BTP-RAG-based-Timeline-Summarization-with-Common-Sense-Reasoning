171. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
May 24, 1942
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I feel that it would be desirable to give some formal posi-
tion to Krishnachandra. We may call him Assistant Manager
and define his field of work. If you approve, you may, if you
like, put the suggestion before the Executive Committee. You
are the best judge of what would be the proper thing to do. I
do not remember the rules.
Blessings from
BAPU
