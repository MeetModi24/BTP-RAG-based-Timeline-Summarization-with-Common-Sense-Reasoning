299. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
August 31, 1938
CHI. NIMU,
I got your letter. You must have received the money. I can say nothing
about my vow of silence. I feel quite comfortable with it. It will be given up
when God wills so. My silence does not inconvenience either me or anybody
else. The cholera epidemic has almost subsided. Pyarelal' s fever has left him.
Tell Ramdevji that he will get a letter from me soon.
Do not worry about passing. It will be enough if you study as best as
you can. What came of your plan of going to Hardwar?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
