189. LETTER TO S. T. MISRA
March 23, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
The opinion of M. Sreedhar Shastri and his friend was pub-
lished in the supplement of the Aaj. A copy was handed to Sjt.
Haribhau Pathak to be sent to the Mahamahopadhyaya. This
was some time ago.
Yours sincerely,
PT. S. T. MISRA
MOGLAI BAZAR
DHULIA
From a microfilm: S.N. 20677
