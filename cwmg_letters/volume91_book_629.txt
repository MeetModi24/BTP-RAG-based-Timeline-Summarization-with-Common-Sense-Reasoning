629. LETTER TO RAMAKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
ASHRAM,
Magh Krishna 2 [February 7, 1928]
DEAR RAMAKRISHNA,
I have your letter. I could read it only today. Convey
my blessings to the couple. May they live long and may both
devote parts of their lives to some service or other.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
RAMAKRISHNAJI
KRISHNA NIVAS
KATRA KHUSHALRAI
DELHI
From the Hindi original : Brijkrishna Chandiwala Papers. Courtesy:
Gandhi National Museum and Library
