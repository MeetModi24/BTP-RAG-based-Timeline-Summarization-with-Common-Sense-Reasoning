350. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
SEVAGRAM,
July 20, 1940
MY DEAR BROTHER,
I have your long letter2—but not long enough for me. You
do me less than justice when you say neither R. nor I are
too ready to defer to others' judgment. This can never be true
of you. But there are some differences between us which our
mutual love and regard cannot get over. I have much to say
about the letter, but I know you don't want me to argue.
Please believe me; no word of yours to me is without its effect.
I fancy I am in God's good hands. Mahadev Desai will write.
Love.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
Letters of Srinivasa Sastri, p. 326
