409. LETTER TO UMADEVI BAJAJ
August 20, 1932
CHI. OM,
I got your letter. Your handwriting has improved greatly.
If your present weight is due to a strong build, why do you wish
to reduce it? You will be better equipped for service if you
grow up to be big and strong in body, provided of course that your
mind also becomes strong along with the body. If the body has
become merely fat because of some disease, you should certain-
ly try to reduce your weight. Do you suffer from any disease?
Write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 335
