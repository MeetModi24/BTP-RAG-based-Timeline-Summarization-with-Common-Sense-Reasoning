164. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
KAZIRKHIL,
November 18, 1946
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
I have your wire. I have sent a wire saying that we are
both glad. You must have received my other letter as well.
Now have an amicable discussion with colleagues and mend mat-
ters where necessary.
You deserve to be President of the Sammelan. Render
whatever service you can. My blessings you have but I wish to
say nothing about the Sammelan. So my name must not be
brought in on any account.
Blessings from
BAPU
