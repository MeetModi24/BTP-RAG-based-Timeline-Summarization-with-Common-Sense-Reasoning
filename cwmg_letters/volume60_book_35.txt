35. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
December 24, 1934
DEAR SISTER,
I have your several letters. My prayers are certainly with
you in your noble endeavour.
C. F. Andrews should be here on 26th.
Here is the message duly signed.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3518. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6327
