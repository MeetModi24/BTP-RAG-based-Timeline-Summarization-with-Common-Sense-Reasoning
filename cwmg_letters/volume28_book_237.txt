237. LETTER TO P. A. NARIELWALA
SABARMATI,
November 7, 1925
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and donation. If you will send
it directly to the Secretary, A.I.S.A., Satyagraha Ashram, Sabar-
mati, it will be more convenient.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[P. A.] NARIELWALA, ESQ.
"ROSE LEA"
ALTAMONT ROAD
CUMBALLA HILL
BOMBAY
From the original: C. W. 9275. Courtesy: P. A. Narielwala
