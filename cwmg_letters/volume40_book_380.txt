380. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA BHAVE
[About May 19, 1929]
I have been unhappy at your going away. Unhappy because
I have founded the Ashram for self-realization; service is its outer
manifestation, while the observance of vows is the inner one.
The basic purpose of the Ashram is the realization of God. I
cannot understand why in these circumstances you went away
elsewhere to seek God.
[From Hindi]
Bapuna Patro -7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 110
