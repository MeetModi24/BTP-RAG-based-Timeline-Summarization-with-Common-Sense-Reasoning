363. LETTER TO K. PANDAR CHETTY
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
With reference to your letter it does not appear that your gun
licence was confiscated because of civil disobedience. Cases not
arising out of civil disobedience are not covered by the Settlement.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. K. PANDAR CHETTY
CARDAMOM PLANTER
BODINAYAKANUR (S. INDIA)
From a photostat: S.N. 17197
