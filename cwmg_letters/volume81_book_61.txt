61. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
SEVAGRAM,
July 26, 1945
MY DEAR KU.,
This is to introduce Shri Shyamlal to you. He is the Secre-
tary of the K. G. Memorial Trust. He will plead with you for
accommodating the prospective women's camp for four months
from after the rains are over, i. e., not later than 2nd October.
I support the pleading, if it is feasible. More from the bearer.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10175
