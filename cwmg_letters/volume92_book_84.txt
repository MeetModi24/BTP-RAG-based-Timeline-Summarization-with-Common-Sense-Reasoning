84. LETTER TO K. V. SUBRAMANIA IYER
SABARMATI,
August 5, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I am not publishing your letter as I still hold that your
fast was wrong. You should read my article again. I have said
'you should fast against wrong done by friend.' The court was
no friend of yours. My advice to you is to refuse to have your
child vaccinated and you should go to jail as often as they would
take you. You should carry on a quiet, dignified and patient
agitation against compulsion. There is no case for fasting.
Tours loving,
K. V. SUBRAMANIA IYER
SECY., ANTI-VACCINATION LEAGUE
PALGHAT
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/94
