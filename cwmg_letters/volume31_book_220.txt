220. LETTER TO ANANDANAND
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 27, 1926
BHAISHREE ANANDANAND,
Enclosed is a letter from Venilal. Fix up an appointment
with him on Saturday or Sunday, according to your convenience,
so that the issue may be settled once for all. His suggestion
that a copy of all entries in his account with us should be kept
ready seems to me quite proper. Sunday 3 p.m. would be
more convenient for me.
SHRI SWAMI
NAVAJIVAN KARYALAYA
AHMEDABAD
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 12222
