399. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
June 21, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
You will have received the letter I sent you yesterday from
the station. I shall expect your letter tomorrow. Come soon.
Manju has returned from the hospital. You will be well. Lila-
vati has not met me yet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
