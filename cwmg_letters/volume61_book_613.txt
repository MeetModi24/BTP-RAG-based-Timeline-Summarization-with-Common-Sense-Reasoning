613. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
September 20, 1935
MY DEAR ANAND,
I must dictate this if I am to reach you at all today. It is
bad thing—these continuous attacks for Vidya. I hope that the
special treatment at Delhi did good. It would certainly be good
if you could accept Valji's invitation. I am glad, too, that you
have hope from your Harijan work in Karachi. Personally I am
opposed to taking cost of clothing for Harijans. I refuse to treat
them as forlorn paupers. And when I have to provide anything in
the shape of clothing, I always resort to khadi [but] it is not
possible to lay down a law for all. You must, therefore, use your
own judgment in every case of this kind and do what appears to
you to be proper or refer to Jairamdas. And when in doubt
always err on the right side.
Love to you both.
BAPU
SJT. ANAND HINGORANI
FEROZEPORE
