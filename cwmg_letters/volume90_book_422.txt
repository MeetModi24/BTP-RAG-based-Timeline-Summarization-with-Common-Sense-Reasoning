422. LETTER TO JUGATRAM DAVE
NEW DELHI,
January 19, 1948
CHI. JUGATRAM,
How could I write to you during the fast? Yet I used to
dictate for Harijan and attend to some very important matters too.
Right now everything seems to be very fine; God knows what
happens in the end. The whole world has turned topsyturvy.
Everyone has to stand face to face with God, else there is perdition.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
