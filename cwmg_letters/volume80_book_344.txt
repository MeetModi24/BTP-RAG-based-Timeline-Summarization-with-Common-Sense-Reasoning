344. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
May 29, 1945
CHI. SITA,
Your handwriting is good. Write to everybody in the same
way. Equally good handwriting for everyone. Shivir agni seems
all right. You can say that on that day we had lighted shivir
agni all over the place.
Blessings to all from
BAPU
CHI. SITA GANDHI
NANABHAI MASHRUWALA'S BUNGALOW
AKOLA, BERAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4951
