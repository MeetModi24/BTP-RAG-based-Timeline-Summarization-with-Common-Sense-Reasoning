116. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[Before March 23, 1930]
CHI. MIRA,
I have your two letters. No time to say anything. I notice
that everywhere they say you have taken charge of the Ashram.
This needs contradiction. I am trying to think out something.
I hope those [who] were stung have now recovered.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5387. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9621
