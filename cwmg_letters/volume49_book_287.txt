287. LETTER TO ANANDI ASAR
March 25, 1932
CHI.,
It was bad of you to have given a curt reply to the visitor.
We should always be courteous to any visitor, no matter how he
is dressed, and we must reply to a visitor's question.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 8984. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
