538. LETTER TO PARVATI
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 30, 1928
CHI. PARVATI,
I have received a cablegram from South Africa to the effect
that Pragji has won the case. I hope you are keeping well.
Write to me sometimes. Though I may not be able to share your
joys and sorrows, I would like to hear from you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5032
