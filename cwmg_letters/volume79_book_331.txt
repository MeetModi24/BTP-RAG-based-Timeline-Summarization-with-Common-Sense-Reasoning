331. LETTER TO B. G. KHER
SEVAGRAM,
March 3, 1945
BHAI BALASAHEB,
I am sure you will at any rate read the enclosed. If you
can go, please do give that much time to Kathiawar. It will
bear fruit. But if you really cannot go can you suggest some
other name?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 2774
