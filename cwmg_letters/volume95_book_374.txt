374. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
BARDOLI
This is just to say that H[industani] music and Ramayana
would be all right. I hope you and Govindrao are keeping good health.
This letter may be taken to have been addressed to all to whom I have
not written.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi in Gujarati script: G.N. 9019
