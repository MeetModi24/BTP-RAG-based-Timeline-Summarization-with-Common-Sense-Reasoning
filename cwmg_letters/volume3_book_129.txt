129. LETTER TO COLONIAL SECRETARY, NATAL
14 MERCURY LANE,
DURBAN,
April 18, 1901
TO
THE COLONIAL SECRETARY
PIETERMARITZBURG
SIR,
I have the honour to acknowledge your letter of the 16th
instant in reply to my letter of the 30th ultimo with reference to
the special mention in General Buller's dispatch of officers of the
locally raised Volunteer Indian Corps, and I beg to thank you
for the same.
I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
M. K. GANDHI
Pietermaritzburg Archives: C.S.O. 1901/2888
