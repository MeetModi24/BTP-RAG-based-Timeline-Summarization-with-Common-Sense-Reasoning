179. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
BOMBAY,
May 10, 1942
I had a letter from Jawaharlal only yesterday. He writes that he
will be reaching Allahabad on the 22nd and will then decide about
coming to Wardha. The Maulana writes that he too will be coming
around the same time. I do not know about his coming to Bombay.
He also writes that his health is bad.
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11227. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
