149. LETTER TO JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
WARDHA,
November 16, 1936
CHI. JAYAPRAKASH,
I saw Prabha in Benares. It gave me much pain to see that
she had become very weak.
She appeared to be in a much
worse condition than before. If it is possible, send her here for
a few months.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Jayaprakash Narayan Papers. Courtesy:
