267. LETTER TO KUNDAR DIWAN
ON THE TRAIN,
October 6, 1939
BHAI KUNDAR,
I do not write to you because Krishnachandra has been writ-
ing. Balkrishna will certainly gain strength. Rest and diet are
the normal prescription. He complains about lack of sleep. I
am thinking of doing something about it. I may ask him to
come over to Segaon during the cold season. I shall consult the
doctor.
What suggestions can I give you regarding the takli? We are
reaching Wardha this evening.
Blessings from
BAPU
