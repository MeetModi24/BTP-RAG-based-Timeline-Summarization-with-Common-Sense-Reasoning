370. LETTER TO ARUN CHANDRA GUHA
August 12, 1941
DEAR ARUN BABU,
I have your interesting letter. I have no difficulty in accept-
ing your limitation of non-violence. When it is honestly worked
it automatically expands. But your acceptance of non-violence
in communal conflicts and the like is enough for my purpose.
Your interpretation of the present struggle is accurate.
With regards to all the friends,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 8669
