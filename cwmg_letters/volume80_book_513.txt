513. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
June 12, 1945
CHI. NARANDAS,
I had your lotter of the 6th. Since I had acted upon it, I
did not reply to it assuming that Kanaiyo would certainly write.
I am also constantly thinking about him. As long as necessary, he
will go on doing that work. Isn't that my work as well?
I liked your becoming President of the Khadi Board. I am
sure you will bring credit to that office. I am enclosing a letter
for Savita. Give it to her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8625.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
