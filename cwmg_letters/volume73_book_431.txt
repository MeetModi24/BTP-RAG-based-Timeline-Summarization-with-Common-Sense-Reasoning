431. LETTER TO PATWARDHAN
February 12, 1941
BHAI PATWARDHAN,
I had already advised you to put the money in the name of
a panch even though Sahasrabuddhe may not approve of it. I had
understood that Sahasrabuddhe was prepared for this much. Now I
331
don't know what can be done. I don't even know in whose name
the money has been kept. I shall tell you if I can see more clearly.
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
