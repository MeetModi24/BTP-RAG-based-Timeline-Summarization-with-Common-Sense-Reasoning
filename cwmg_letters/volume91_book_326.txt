326. LETTER TO SUMER SINGH
SABARMATI,
August 2, 1924
DEAR FRIEND 2 ,
I have your letter. I am in constant touch with the A. S.
Bureau and Sardar Mangal Singh. I do publish or comment
on whatever seems to me to be important. I shall however thank
you to draw prompt attention to any omission you may notice.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIKH LANE
SINDH
From a copy: Mohammed Ali Papers. Courtesy: Jamia Millia Islamia
Library
