342. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
BARDOLI,
June 2, 1931
CHI. NANIBEHN,
I got your letter. In staying in the Vidyapith you have done
nothing for which you need apologize. It is not a crime to live
there. You will certainly learn there, and should, therefore, live
there contented. Improve your health and cultivate the mind.
Write to me from time to time. Most probably, Gangabehn also
will go and live there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3115
