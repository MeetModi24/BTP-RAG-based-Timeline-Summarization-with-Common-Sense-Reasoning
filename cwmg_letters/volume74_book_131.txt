131. LETTER TO PRABHULAL
May 28, 1941
BHAI PRABHULAL,
Of course you have my blessings. If you think they can be
a source of inspiration for you, use them for the purpose. If you
publish them in papers, you may take it that they will lose the
power, if any, of inspiring you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4136
