569. LETTER TO ELSE GIESE
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 24, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to receive your letter through my friend
Mr. Deshpande of Baroda. I feel thankful to God that my writings
give solace to friends all over the world.
Yours sincerely,
MADAM ELSE GIESE
BERLIN SW 11,
PRINZ ULBRECHT-STRASSE 5
From a photostat: S.N. 19722
