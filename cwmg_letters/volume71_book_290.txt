290. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
CALCUTTA,
February 26, 1940
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I have not been quite as forgetful of you as you think. I wrote
a letter at Santiniketan which P[yarelal] forgot to post till we
reached Malikanda! I wrote another at M[alikanda]. But I have
wanted to write every day. The thing has been impossible. Mira
has been writing. She is better. P. is still on the brain. Her address
is care Pandit Jagat Ram, Hariana. She has been moving about
under his directions.
Charlie is better. I have not seen him yet. I shall tomorrow.
Ba is laid up. Fever and severe cough. I shall have to leave her
here. You will tell Sushila.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3963. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7272
