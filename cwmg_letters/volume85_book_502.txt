502. LETTER TO PHEROZE
NEW DELHI,
September 29, 1946
BHAI PHEROZEBHAI,
I have your letter. Why in English? Have you forgotten
your Gujarati? And why are you staying in bed? Why are
you subsisting on your friend's sympathies? What did you do
all these years?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
