6. LETTER TO BARINDRA KUMAR GHOSH
December 17, 1934
I have glanced through your book. It has proved a severe
disappointment.
You have lost yourself in the exuberance of
your own language. You have missed the spirit of non-co-opera-
tion and civil resistance. You have glorified slavery; our vice has
become virtue in your estimation.
I may not argue with you.
Time will show us the true way. What does it matter so long as
we pursue the path that seems to us to be right?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
