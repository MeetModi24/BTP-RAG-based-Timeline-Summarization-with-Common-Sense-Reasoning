106. LETTER TO IDA S. SCUDDER
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 10, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I am thankful to you for remembering Mrs. Gandhi and me
in connection with the opening ceremony of your building. You
will be interested to learn that there is no hospital and no institu-
tion where there is a ward endowed in my name. If there was, it
would be a fraud. For, how can a ward be endowed in my name
when I have not one farthing to pay for it. If I can induce
friends to endow wards or beds, the endowment should be in their
names. But I can think of none to whom I can speak about en-
dowing a hospital ward. All influence is exhausted in asking them
to endow spinning-wheels and institutions for the so-called un-
touchables or for a member of the dumb creation, the cow.
Yours sincerely,
MISS IDA S. SCUDDER
VELLORE
From a photostat: S.N. 13093
