227. LETTER TO MANI
July 17, 1932
DEAR MANI1,
I thought you would never fall ill, then how is it that you
fell ill again? You do not write letters now. Do you like to stay
at the Ashram? Why did you come away from Mother? Write to
me everything in detail. Write also openheartedly what your
desires are.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. 11 p. 311
