51. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
[August 20, 1925]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. Stay in peace at the Ashram. Let me know
where you are staying. I have not seen Navibunder but I can
draw a mental picture of it. Today I am in Cuttack. I have
come to see the tannery. Mahadev and Satis Babu are with me.
You must make a complete recovery. I am certain if you stay at
Hajira you will be completely all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Now we shall meet soon. I shall reach there on the 5th.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9217
