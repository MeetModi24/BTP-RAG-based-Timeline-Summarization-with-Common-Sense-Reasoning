573. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
October 5, 1945
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I am sorry about Prafulla Babu. Anyway I shall be staying
at Sodepur. Sudhir Ghosh will have a major share in my tour.
I will not be happy if all the parties do not unite. Only this
much today.
SATIS BABU
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
SODEPUR (24 PARAGANAS)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
