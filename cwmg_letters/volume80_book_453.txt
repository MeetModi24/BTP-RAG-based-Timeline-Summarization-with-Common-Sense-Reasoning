453. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
PANCHGANI,
June 7, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
I have your telegram. As soon as I received Balvantsinha's
letter, I sent a wire and also wrote to him. I have decided that
you should stay on till I return. Convey this humbly but firmly
to Father. Your humility will set everything right. True humi-
lity is filled with ahimsa. Learn everything thoroughly. You must
have known from Balvantsinha's letter what advice I have given
to Father.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
