382. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
August 14, 1941
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Your health seems to be improving there. It seems from
Dr. Menkel's report that the knee-trouble will remain for ever.
If there is no other complication, I see no harm. Do not leave
that place, as long as you get peace of mind there.
Do not be very keen on seeing [Sir Francis] Wylie. There
will be no harm if you can see him without any effort. It would
not be good if you have to make an effort to see him.
I was very pleased to see Ramakrishna. He is taking full
advantage of his stay in prison.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3020
