80. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
WARDHA,
October 28, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
What do you say to this postcard? Choudhury has made
vast strides. You will see the works when you come.
I do hope you are having full rest in Simla. I dread your
habit of making work and then fretting that the day's work is
not done.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3718. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6874
