446. LETTER TO MANILAL T. DOSHI
JAGATPUR,
January 11, 1947
CHI. MANILAL1,
I have your letter. Bulsaria from Panchgani had promptly
given me the news about Vachharaj. Panchgani has sustained a
great loss. Can you bring lustre to the work left behind by
Vachharaj?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
