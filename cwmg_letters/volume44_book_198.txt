198. LETTER TO BALBHADRA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 11, 1930
CHI. BALBHADRA (OR BUDDHICHAKRA),
Narandas is full of praise for you. You have done quite a good
deal of carding. How nice it would be if you thus worked regular-
ly! Why don't you write to me?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9210
