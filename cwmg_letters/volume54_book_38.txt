38. LETTER TO MICHAEL LINDSAY
March 10, 1933
MY DEAR MICHAEL,
I was so glad you sent your little note with Mother's letter.
We have often thought of you.
The two sayings you have sent me of Confucius are very beauti-
ful and very seasonable for me.
Please remember us to Father, and accept Mahadev's and my
regards for yourself.
Yours sincerely,
MICHAEL LINDSAY, ESQ.
