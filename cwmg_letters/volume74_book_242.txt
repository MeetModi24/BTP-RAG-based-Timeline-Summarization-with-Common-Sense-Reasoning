242. LETTER TO C. A. TULPULE
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
July 11, 1941
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of the 9th. You ought not to think of going
to jail until you are completely free from the complaint. 2 In the mean while,
you will do what constructive work comes your way.
Yours sincerely,
MAHADEV DESAI
SF. C. A. TULPULE, ADVOCATE
TILAK ROAD
POONA
From the original: C.W. 2902. Courtesy: C. A. Tulpule
