167. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
SIMLA,
May 11, 1946
CHI. KANAM,
I have your letter. Your handwriting is bad. Can't you at
all write your Gujarati characters?
It is not good that you have no interest in spinning. If you
read some literature on the subject you will come to feel that
you may not neglect this dharma.
I am glad that you clean the latrines and do other scav-
enging work. What do you read?
God alone knows when I shall be able to get away from
here. No one else does.
I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
