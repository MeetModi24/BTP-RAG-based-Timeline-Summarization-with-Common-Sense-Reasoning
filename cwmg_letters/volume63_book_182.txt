182. LETTER TO HORACE ALEXANDER
July 16, 1936
DEAR HORACE,
Your letter was most welcome. You will get a note from
me through Kamalnayan Bajaj. But that is a mere introduc-
tion. Only it was written before the receipt of your letter.
I anticipated you about Agatha. I cabled approval of her
of God and use it accordingly. Take care of your body. Take
up no more burden than it can carry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10718
