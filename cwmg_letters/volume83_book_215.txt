215. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
February 27, 1946
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I understand all that you say. I do value your services.
What other job would you like, if you are not kept here [in
Poona]? Do you wish to return to the Ashram? What about
Kanchan and Hiramani? Whom should I post here in your
place? Have a talk with me so that we can come to a final
decision.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8422. Also C. W. 5607. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
