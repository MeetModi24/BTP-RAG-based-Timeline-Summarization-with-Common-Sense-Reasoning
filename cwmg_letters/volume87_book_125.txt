125. LETTER TO H. S. SUHRAWARDY
March 21, 1947
DEAR SUHRAWARDY,
Pardon the somewhat familiar style. I hope you got my
wire [of] yesterday and that there was no justification for the
fears expressed to me. I have a right to expect you to act on
the square. The heart is too full to enable me to say more.
I am just now in a little village in the devastated area.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G. N. 8968
