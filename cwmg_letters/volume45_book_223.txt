223. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
February 7, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
Only today I got your letter of the 3rd. Why do you go
on worrying even there? If by the time I reach Bombay you
have recovered sufficient strength, do come there. I hope you
got the letter in which I had asked you some questions. What
do you eat? And how much? Do you sleep well? Have you
gained any strength there? Can you sit up or leave the bed?
Let me have as many details about the state of your health as
you can give. Till the 14th at any rate, I shall be in Prayag.
Write to me every day.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3406
