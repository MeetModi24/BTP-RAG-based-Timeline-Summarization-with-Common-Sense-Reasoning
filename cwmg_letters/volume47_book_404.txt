404. LETTER TO DAVID POLLOCK
AHMEDABAD,
August 22, 1931
DEAR MR. POLLOCK,
I was delighted to see your letter.
There is no prospect whatsoever of my going to London at
the present moment. But if I did go I would of course be de-
lighted to renew our old acquaintance and old relations. How far
I shall be able to give you satisfaction I do not know.
Yours sincerely,
DAVID POLLOCK, ESQ.
29 ONSLOW GARDENS
LONDON, S.W. 7
