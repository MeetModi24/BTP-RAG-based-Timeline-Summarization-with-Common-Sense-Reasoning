179. LETTER TO JEHANGIR P. PATEL
November 27, 1945
BHAI JEHANGIR,
It is good you have reached there. I am considering sending
someone from here for keeping the accounts and so I have been
doing a lot of thinking. We would like to have all possible help
from you.
I hope Mother is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
JEHANGIR PATEL
PATEL BROTHERS
10 CHURCHGATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
