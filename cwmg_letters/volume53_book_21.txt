21. LETTER TO EDMOND AND YVONNE PRIVAT
January 12, 1933
MY DEAR ANAND AND BHAKTI,
We were all delighted to receive your letter after many months.
I know that you are both spreading the gospel of love and also
trying to live it. I often think of those very happy times we were
together on the Pilsna. I am never tired of recalling your utter
indifference to possession of riches, and your losing yourselves in
one another.
May this New Year make your life richer, happier and more
fruitful in the service of humanity.
I had to dictate if I was to write to you at all.
Love from us all.
Did you get Mahadev's Christmas letter?
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 8794
