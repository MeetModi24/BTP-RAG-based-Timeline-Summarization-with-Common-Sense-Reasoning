320. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
October 5, 1932
MY DEAR AGATHA,
I have your letter. I know I gave you all a most anxious
time. It was inevitable. It was all God's doing. I could trace
His hand in everything that happened during those days.
Love in which Mahadev joins.
BAPU
[PS.]
Herewith a letter for Charlie.
From a photostat: G.N. 1459
