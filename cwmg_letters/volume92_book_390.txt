390. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
December 4, 1930
CHI. KANTA,
There is no letter from you this time. I think you must
have written the letter but it may not have reached the Ashram
in time. I am used to having your letter every week and so
when it does not come I wonder.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
