459. LETTER TO LIMAYE
SEVAGRAM,
November 22, 1944
BHAI LIMAYE,
It is disturbing that both of you keep indifferent health. The
only consolation is that you have two doctors with you. Please
come when you are free.
I feel that we cannot let those who sell uncertified khadi also
have Congress agency. It is not a matter of rules but of morals.
You however have to work as the local committee directs.
It is good that you have taken up the work of Nayee Talim.
About this when you come.
Chi. Balmohan must be progressing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
ACHARYA LIMAYE
925 SADASHIVPETH
POONA 2
