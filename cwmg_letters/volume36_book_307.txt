307. LETTER TO SHRINATH SINGH
Vaisakha Shukla 4 [April 23, 1928]
SHRINATH SINGHJI,
I have your letter. I am acquainted with the Birla boys;
hence I sent a message to them. It would take up all my time
if I started sending messages to every newspaper and every editor
that asked for them even though they may not be known to me.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SHRINATH SINGHJI
EDITOR, "BAL SAKHA"
INDIAN PRESS LTD.
ALLAHABAD
From Hindi: C.W. 2973. Courtesy: Shrinath Singh
