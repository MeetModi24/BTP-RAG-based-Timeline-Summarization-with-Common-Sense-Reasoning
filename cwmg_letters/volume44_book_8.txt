8. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 5, 1930
CHI. MOTIBEHN,
The letter which you wrote to me long ago was handed to
me just now. You must have received the letters which I wrote
to you. Read Chapter XII over and over again and reflect on it.
I am sure you get news about the children regularly from Cochin.
Don't grieve about Meghji at all. He rests in peace. He who
dies uttering Ramanama with devotion, what evil can he meet
with?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3737
