318. LETTER TO SYED MAHMUD
December 20, 1946
BHAI SYED MAHMUD,
I cannot dictate this in the Urdu script because Manu-
behn does not know Urdu. If you are not required to stay
there, I should like you to come and stay here and Harijan will
be despatched from here. Have a talk with Pandit Sunderlal
and take whatever decision you wish to.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
