14. LETTER TO LT.-GOVERNOR'S SECRETARY
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
25 & 26 RISSIK STREET,
JOHANNESBURG,
OCTOBER 19, 1903
TO
THE PRIVATE SECRETARY
TO HIS EXCELLENCY THE LIEUTENANT-GOVERNOR
PRETORIA
SIR,
With reference to your letter of the 1st instant, I beg now to remind
you thereof, and to request on behalf of the Association the appointment
of a day for the British Indian Deputation to wait on His Excellency.
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
ABDOOL GANIE 2
CHAIRMAN
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION.
Pretoria Archives: L. G. 2132, Asiatics 1902-1906.
