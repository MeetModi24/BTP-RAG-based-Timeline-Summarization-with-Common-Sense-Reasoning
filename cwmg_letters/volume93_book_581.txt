581. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 14, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
I wrote you a long letter today but I forgot one thing.
You may give liver injections or any other injections you want.
I do not think Kanchan on her own will have any objection.
If she can be saved by such measures do try to save her.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
LADY HARDINGE MEDICAL COLLEGE
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
