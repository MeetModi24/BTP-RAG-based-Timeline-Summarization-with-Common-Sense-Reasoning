581. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
September 26, 1941
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I had sent a copy of my statement to the Chief Minister
of Burma and conveyed my views to him. He has sent me a
cordial reply, accompanied by copies of his two statements before
the Indian Delegation. You have presumably seen these letters.
I think we can get something done if we have people working
there too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4737. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
