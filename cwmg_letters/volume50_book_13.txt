13. LETTER TO BHAU PANSE
June 3, 1932
CHI. BHAU 2 ,
I have already answered your letter. Write to me if you
have any fresh doubt.
Both my hands are disabled for plying the takli. The fingers
have grown so weak that for the present I am unable to use
them for manipulating the takli. The future rests with God.
Thus I have to content myself with the reports of the progress
made by others.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6730. Also C.W. 4473. Courtesy: Bhau
Panse
1 Daughter of Raojibhai M. Patel
2 He was an Ashram inmate.
