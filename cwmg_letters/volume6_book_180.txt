180. LETTER TO SIR RICHARD SOLOMON
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 15, 1906
SIR,
As you were good enough to say on board that you would, if time
permitted, give me a few minutes during your stay in London, may I
ask you to give me an appointment?
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
SIR RICHARD SOLOMON
THE REFORM CLUB
PALL MALL
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4571
