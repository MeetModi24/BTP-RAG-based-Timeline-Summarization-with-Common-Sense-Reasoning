652. LETTER TO LORD WAVELL
'MANOR VILLE', SIMLA WEST,
June 30, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Many thanks for your two letters-one of yesterday's date
about the condemned prisoners and the other of today about
my presence in Simla till the conference finally disperses.
About the first letter I need say nothing beyond thanking
you for your prompt and hopeful reply.
As to the second, I will not leave Simla without knowing
that you no longer want me. Whenever you need me, you
have only to send me a message.
I hope you have noticed that Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru is
reaching Simla tomorrow and that you will invite him to meet
you and Lady Wavell.
Yours very sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. E. THE VICEROY
SIMLA
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 27
