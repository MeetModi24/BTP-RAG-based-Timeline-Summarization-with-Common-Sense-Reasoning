250. LETTER TO LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
NEW DELHI,
May 24, 1946
DEAR LORD,
The Working Committee passed its resolution this evening.
I call it a good resolution. So far as the Congress is concerned,
I am free now to leave Delhi. What would you have me to
do?
Hoping you are not finding your labours too exacting,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 197
