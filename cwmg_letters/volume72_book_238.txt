238. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
June 15, 1940
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
Balvantsinha has asked for Apu's help in farm work. I
feel that is reasonable. Apu is strong and it is not right to
use him in the kitchen, though of course I do believe that the
kitchen should be entrusted to a man. He feels that if you are
agreeable Apu should be transferred to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
