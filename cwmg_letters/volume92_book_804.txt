804. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
WARDHA,
November 10, 1934
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I have your sweet letter.
How can it be acknowledged or
answered? It is just by chance that this letter is going by the
Mussoorie post. I am aware of your faith. My blessings to all
the children.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32778
