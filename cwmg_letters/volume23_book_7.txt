7. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
Silence Day [March 6, 1922]
CHI. DEVDAS,
These are the wire and letter received here. The letter is from
Satish Babu. Reply to him immediately.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I replied to you yesterday. Do go and see Mr. Joseph,
the Headmaster, when you get time.
From a photostat of the Gujarati original : S.N. 7980
