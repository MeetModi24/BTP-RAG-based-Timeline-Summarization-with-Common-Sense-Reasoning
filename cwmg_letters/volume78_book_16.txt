16. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
August 7, 1944
CHI. AMRIT,
I have your letters. I was glad S. was able to go to
Simla and give you some time. She is still in Poona looking after
Manjula . Did I tell you that Dr. Jivraj Mehta was with me?
I must ask him to write to you. He helps in a variety of ways
and looks after my body without the slightest fuss. I do not even
know that he is doing it. He is supposed to have come merely
to keep company.
You must not worry.
Love to all.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4146. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7781
