746. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 15, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. You have given very good news. Here
also everything is fine. But Balkrishna's health is not good.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
