364. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
December 30, 1928
CHI. KUSUM,
I get your letters regularly. Prabhavati will have returned
by the time you get this.
It has reassured me on your account to know that you have
been serving everyone. Tell Sarojini Devi that I did not write
to her because I had nothing particular to say. And now we
shall be meeting in four or five days. I intend to reach there on
the 6th. Letters are now being taken away to be posted, and so
I shall not write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1774
