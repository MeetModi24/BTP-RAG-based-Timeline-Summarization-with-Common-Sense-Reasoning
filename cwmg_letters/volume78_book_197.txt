197. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEVAGRAM,
October 4, 1944
CHI. SHARMA,
I wanted to write to you. In the mean time Gadodiaji
arrived. He is not at all satisfied with your work. He says that
nothing gets done. I told him it couldn't be so. What is the
truth? Come here for a few days if you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
H. L. SHARMA
KHURJA ROAD, U. P.
From a facsimile of the Hindi ; Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
between pp. 324 and 325
