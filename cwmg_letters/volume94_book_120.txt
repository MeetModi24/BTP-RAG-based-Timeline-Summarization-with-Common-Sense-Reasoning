120. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 26, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you even today. Do not be so irregular.
If you cannot write every day, tell me on which days you will write.
The whole world is governed by rules. My blood-pressure remains a
little high these days.
Blessings from
BAPU
[P.S.]
Today the weight was 102 lbs. That means it has gone up
by 1 lb.
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
