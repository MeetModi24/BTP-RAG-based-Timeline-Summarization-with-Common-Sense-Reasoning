611. LETTER TO LORD WAVELL
'MANOR VILLE', SIMLA,
June 25, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I was too tired to acknowledge your note there and then.
Nor was there any hurry. I shall remain in Simla during the
conference.
Yours very sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. E. THE VICEROY
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 26
