131. LETTER TO CHANDRANI SACHAR
April 24, 1937
DEAR SISTER,
Thanks for your cheque for Rs. 500.
A well can be con-
structed for Rs. 500 and even Rs. 1,500. We have to bore a well
in the Thana district, which will cost Rs. 1,200. If you can,
please send the balance, too, otherwise your money can of course
be utilized for some other place.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRIMATI CHANDRANI SACHAR
C/O SHRI J. S. SACHAR
NAYA BAZAR, DELHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4090
