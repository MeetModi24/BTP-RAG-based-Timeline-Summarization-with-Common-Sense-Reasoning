288. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
[On or before December 9, 1921]
DEAR MOTILALJI,
This time the unexpected has happened. It is all for the
good. I envy you and Jawaharlal. If we go at this rate swaraj
may burst upon us even without warning.
I think that Godbole should continue the routine work. I
would like you to send your instructions, views and wishes as to
the present and the future. I wrote today [to] Sarup and Ranjit
saying, unless they had been advised by you otherwise and if
they could, they should go to Allahabad.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original : Motilal Nehru Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
