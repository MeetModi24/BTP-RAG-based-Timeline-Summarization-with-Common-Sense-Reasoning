593. LETTER TO GULAM RASUL QURESHI
October 20, 1932
CHI. QURESHI,
Why are you not getting well? If necessary consult Hari-
bhai. He does many operations for piles. Do you get clear
motions? If there is pain you should eat only leafy vegetables,
fruit and a little milk, or only leafy vegetables and fruit. To
reduce her obesity Amina should eat only bread and leafy vege-
tables. If she does that, the fat cannot but get reduced and she
will get well. She gets tired because of the excessive fat. Do
write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10811. Courtesy: Gulam Rasul
Qureshi
