484. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
February 25, 1938
CHI. SHARDA,
I had your letter. Do stay there if you can remain peaceful.
You have my permission to come over. Saraswatibehn is very keen
that you should stay on for three months. You should definitely
stay on if your health can really improve. I am likely to go that
side in April. I have talked to Devdas about your music. He
will get in touch with you. When you return you can surely
come via Ahmedabad. I have already written to you that I
had a long chat with Shakaribehn. The whole of last night I
dreamt about you. With great impatience and restlessness you
said: 'Now please do get me married.' You even showed me
the man. I did not know him, nor his name. You did not want
to act against my wish but your insistence was tremendous. How
is that?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9989. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
