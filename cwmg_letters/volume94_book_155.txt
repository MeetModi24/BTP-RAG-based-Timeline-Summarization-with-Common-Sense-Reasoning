155. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 29, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Ba and Mahadev have gone to Nagpur. Mahadev may write to
you from Nagpur. The blood-pressure had gone up last night. It came
down to 154/96 in the morning. In
the afternoon also it was
154/92. The climate is good. Sardar is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
