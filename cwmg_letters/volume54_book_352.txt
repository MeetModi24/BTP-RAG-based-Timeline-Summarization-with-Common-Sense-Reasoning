352. LETTER TO DIWAKAR SINGH
April 5, 1933
MY DEAR DIWAKAR,
I have your letter. I cannot possibly undertake to judge
you, and if, on closer introspection, you have found the present
method of serving better than the one you started with, you
should stick to it. You have understood my point and that is
enough for me.
Yours sincerely,
