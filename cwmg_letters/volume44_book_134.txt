134. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 21, 1930
BHAI PANDITJI,
Both of us felt happy after reading your letter. I had heard
that your health had suffered. Since you have brought no disease
with you, you will recover your former strength by and by. Take
care of your health. Rambhau and Mathuri do write to me oc-
casionally. I read in one or two letters that Lakshmibehn had
displayed great courage and fortitude. Her face itself reveals her
courage. Your voice as you used to sing at the time of prayers
haunts me every day. Neither of us can sing bhajans, as you know.
We content ourselves with Ramadhun.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 231. Courtesy: Lakshmibai Khare
