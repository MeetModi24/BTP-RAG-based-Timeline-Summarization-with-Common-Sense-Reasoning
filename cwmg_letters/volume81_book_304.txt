304. LETTER TO V. L. MEHTA
POONA,
August 29, 1945
BHAI VAIKUNTH,
Is Bharat Bank good? I am scared of a new bank. How-
ever, if you have trust in it you should obtain other signatures.
I am signing this because of my faith in you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
