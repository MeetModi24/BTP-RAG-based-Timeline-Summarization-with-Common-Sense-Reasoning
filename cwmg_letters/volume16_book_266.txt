266. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
LAHORE,
Tuesday [After January 23, 1920]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
How could I manage to write to you? All the same, I look
for letters from you. Let me know how matters stand there with
regard to spinning and weaving. Did you promise to send Kantilal
for Sarladevi? Can he be spared? If he can be, send him to teach
the boys. Ask Deepak to write to Sarladevi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5779. Courtesy:
Radhabehn Choudhri
