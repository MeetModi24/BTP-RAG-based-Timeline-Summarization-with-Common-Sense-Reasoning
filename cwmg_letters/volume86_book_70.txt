70. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
October 28, 1946
CHI. SUMI 2 ,
I had your note. Didn't you go to Kashi a little early? I am
today taking the train to Calcutta. I don't know how long I shall
have to be there or what is going to happen there. But then,
what have we to do with that? Let us live as God wills and go
on doing His bidding. He has already shown us what we should
do ! You are studying, aren't you? Tell me.
The rest in my letter to Sita. Both the letters are for both of you.
Blessings from
BAPU
SUMITRA GANDHI
BANARAS
