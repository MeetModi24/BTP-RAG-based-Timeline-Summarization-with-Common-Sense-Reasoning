99. LETTER TO EKAROZ
ON TOUR,
February 20, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I espoused the Khilafat cause not for
the sake of tyranny but for the sake of humanity. This insidious
attempt that, I think, was made to destroy the Khilafat had to
be resisted. My resistance being non-violent prevented in my
opinion, violence which would but for the movement have un-
doubtedly broken out. The Khilafat movement was not decided
upon to bolster any Turkish tyranny. Wherever tyranny exists, it
must be resisted by non-violent means.
Yours sincerely,
REV. FR. EKAROZ
HAIFA, GERMAN HOSPISEE OF ST. CHARLES
PALESTINE
From a photostat: S.N. 11785
