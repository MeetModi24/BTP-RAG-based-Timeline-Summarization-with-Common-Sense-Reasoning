26. LETTER TO ESTHER MENON
January 21, 1932
MY DEAR CHILD,
Your account of the meeting with the ailing sister is touch-
ing. Give my love to her when you visit her again.
I hope you are at peace with yourself now. The children
must be quite well. Kiss them for me if they will let me kiss them.
Maria was with me in Bombay. But I had hardly time to
talk to her.
I hope Menon is doing well. My love to him and to all
at Woodbrooke settlement . Send my love to Miss Harrison and
tell me where she is staying. Do you write to Maud Cheeseman?
Love.
BAPU
1 Narahari Parikh, addressee's husband
2 The Menon family were in England, at Selly Oak, Birmingham.
3 An English girl, poor and bed-ridden, who was a great admirer
of Gandhiji
4 Addressee's daughters, Nan and Tangai
5 Anne Marie Petersen
6 The Quaker Centre, near Birmingham, in whose activities the addressee
and her husband took part

Sardar Vallabhbhai is with me. Both of us are doing quite
