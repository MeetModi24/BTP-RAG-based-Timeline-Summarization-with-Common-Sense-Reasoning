75. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
[March 15, 1930]
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
It pained me to have to use harsh words with you, but it
had to be done, as I wished to remove your pusillanimity. Unless
you are determined and firm in your purpose, your energies can-
not be fully utilized. Tenderness of heart is essential, but for true
tenderness determination is even more essential. Thereby family
ties are purified, and delusion is overcome. You must also give
up your desire to meet me. Whatever I could give you I have
already given. A time will come when you too will have to go to
jail like so many of us, but for the present your work is your prison.
Therefore do not leave Vijapur unless it is on important business. Im-
prove your health, and continue to render whatever help you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
