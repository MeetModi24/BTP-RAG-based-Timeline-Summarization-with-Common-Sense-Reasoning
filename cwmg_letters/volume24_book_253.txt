253. LETTER TO KHUSHI RAM DARYANOMAL
July 27, 1924
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I endeavour to uphold the cause of truth
only. As for the Mussalman, I should advise the Hindus to ignore
the thing entirely.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SETH KHUSHI RAM DARYANOMAL
"ZAMINDAR"
JACO[BA]BAD (SIND)
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
1 An Englishwoman of liberal views; vide An Autobiography, Pt. IV, Ch. XL.
