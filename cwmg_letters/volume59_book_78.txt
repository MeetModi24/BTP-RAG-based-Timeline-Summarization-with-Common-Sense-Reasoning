78. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
September 24, 1934
CHI. RAMDAS,
I don't like your idea of paying Sharma. His services cannot
be rewarded with money. You two have become friends. There
can be no giving and receiving money between friends.
I think
he will feel offended if you offer to pay him. If he does accept,
I would be surprised. His needs are being met and I have also
been doing whatever else is necessary for him.
You need not,
therefore, worry about his economic condition. In fact I see from
his letters that he would be fully satisfied if you are completely
cured physically, mentally and spiritually and if you can testify
that you were cured mainly through his instrumentality- and that
is as it should be. I would, therefore, advise you to give up the
idea of paying him that sum. Having said this, let me repeat
that you are a completely free agent and that the money belongs
to you. If you think that giving it to him would be the best way
of using it, by all means give it. You may rest assured that what
pleases you will please me.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
