104. LETTER TO J. D. JENKINS
May 3, 1933
DEAR MR. JENKINS,
I thank you for your kind letter.
It requires a political argument to show you why I cannot
take the step you advise and, as a prisoner, I may not enter into
any political discussion. You will, therefore, forgive me.
I hope you received my previous letter.
Thanking you for the book you sent me,
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 19074
