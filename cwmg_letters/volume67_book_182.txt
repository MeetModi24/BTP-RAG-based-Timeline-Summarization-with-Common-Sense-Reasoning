182. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
June 23, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
My idea of boycott is that it can be a perfectly non-violent
measure. When it is that, it becomes a duty. Hence boycott of
Japanese goods does become a duty for those who consider
Japan to be wholly in the wrong. And when it becomes a duty,
it takes no account of consequences. And duty has to be performed
whether one has company or not. Have you seen Stanley Jones's
thesis on the subject?
No more today.
Love.
TYRANT
