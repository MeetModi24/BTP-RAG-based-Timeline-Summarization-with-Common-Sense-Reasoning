229. LETTER TO HEMAPRABHADEVI DAS GUPTA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday Asadha Krishna 4 [July 28, 1926]
DEAR SISTER,
I got your letter four days ago. I was much concerned to
read that you were having fever and had an attack of chicken-
pox. I hope you are all right now.
I love teaching people to spin, but I fear that, if you go on
working all the time beyond your capacity, you will continue to
lose strength. My condition with you, therefore, is that you should
work only as much as you can while taking due care of your
health. The substance of what I have been saying on the Gita
in my discourses is likely to be published in Hindi, sooner or later.
But that will take some time. We should never forget that it is
our dharma to take proper care of our body.
BAPU
SMT. HEMAPRABHADEVI
CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1648; also S.N. 12224
