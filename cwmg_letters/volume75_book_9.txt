9. LETTER TO PRABHU DAYAL VIDYARTHI
October 12, 1941
CHI. PRABHU DAYAL,
I have gone through the articles about which you have written.
Lately I have not written on this subject for any newspaper nor
are these my writings. And there is nothing to indicate the source
from which they have been reproduced.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 11695
