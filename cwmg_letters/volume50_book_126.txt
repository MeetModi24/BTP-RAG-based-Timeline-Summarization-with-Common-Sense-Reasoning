126. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
July 1, 1932
. . . As you follow whole-heartedly the remedy I have suggested,
you will get more and more peace. What you have already
read will have an invisible effect on you and it will be amazing.
Live as if you had never read anything. Whatever you have
digested and assimilated will of itself bear fruit as action.
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, pp. 270- and Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home
Department, Special Branch, File No. 800(40) (3), Pt. 11, p. 57
