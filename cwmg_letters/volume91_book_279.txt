279. LETTER TO MANAGER, EASTERN BANK
TRICHY, MADRAS PRESIDENCY,
September 19, 1921
DEAR SIR,
Will you please hand to bearer cheque for £90 received by
you on my behalf from Mombasa?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
THE MANAGER
THE EASTERN BANK
BOMBAY
From the original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
