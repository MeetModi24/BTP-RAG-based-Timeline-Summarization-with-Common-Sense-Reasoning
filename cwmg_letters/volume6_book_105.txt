105. LETTER TO EDITOR, "THE TIMES"
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 5, 1906
TO
THE EDITOR
THE TIMES
PRINTING HOUSE SQUARE, E.C.
DEAR SIR,
I beg to enclose herewith for your information [and] publication
the signed letter addressed by some of the Members of the House of
Commons.
I remain,
[Enclosure]
Yours faithfully,
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4489
1 "Circular for Meeting at House of Commons", p. 87.
