518. LETTER TO DHARMADEV
January 7, 1933
I like your letter immensely. You are quite right in saying
that no one can find what my decisive opinion on varnashrama
dharma is from all my articles published so far on the subject. The
reason is that in each article I express the opinion I had reached
at the time.
Now I have certainly arrived at a more definite opinion and
possibly the picture seems clearer. I am writing thus vaguely be-
cause until I have put into writing my views up to date, things
will not be clear to me. I intend to write an article on varnashrama
in connection with this agitation when I have some time.
[From Hindi]
Vishvajyoti, October 1959
