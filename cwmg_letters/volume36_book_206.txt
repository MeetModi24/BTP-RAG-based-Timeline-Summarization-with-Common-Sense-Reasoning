206. LETTER TO REMINGTON TYPEWRITER CO.
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 1, 1928
MESSRS REMINGTON TYPEWRITER Co., LTD.
YUSUF BUILDING
CORNER OF CHURCHGATE STREET AND ESPLANADE
BOMBAY
DEAR SIR,
I am in receipt of Rem. Portable No. 61625 which I had
sent you for slight repairs and adjustments.
I am exceedingly glad to say that the machine is working to
my entire satisfaction.
Thanking you,
Yours faithfully,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13146
