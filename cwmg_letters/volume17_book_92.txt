92. LETTER TO "THE TIMES OF INDIA"
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
April 3, 1920
SIR,
I submit for adoption the following three resolutions during
the Satyagraha Week, i.e., for the 6th, 9th and 13th. I take it that
there will be no two opinions about the first and the third resolu-
tions. But I have heard criticism about the Caliphate resolution.
I suggest that without the non-co-operation clause the Caliphate
resolution will be inane. The country requires some definite ac-
tion. And nothing can be better for the country than non-co-
operation as some definite action. The forces of violence cannot
be checked otherwise.
M. K. GANDHI
The Times of India, 4-4-1920
