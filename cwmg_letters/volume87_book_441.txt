441. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
PATNA,
May 16, 1947
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Your letter, written in the early hours before prayers. It
is a pleasure to read all that you write about Kosambi. I am
enclosing a letter for him. You may hand it over or read it out
to him if he is still alive when this reaches you.
I have no doubt whatsoever that his stay in the Ashram
has sanctified it.
Sankaran's letter is enclosed. It would be good if something
could be done.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 1977
