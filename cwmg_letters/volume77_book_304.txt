304. LETTER TO R. R. DIWAKAR
PANCHGANI,
July 20, 1944
BHAI DIWAKAR,
I wrote to you yesterday. Pundalik is still here, hence the
second letter. This shows how these things occupy my thoughts.
I think those who are underground should discover themselves
and then do whatever they like. It would be better if no mili-
tant step is taken as long as I am out.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi : Mahatma, Vol. VI, between pp. 336 and
337
