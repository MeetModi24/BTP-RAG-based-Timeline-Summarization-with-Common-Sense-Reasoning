309. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
ANANDKUNJ, RAJKOT,
May 26, 1939
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. Sushila is having fever. Nothing more
remains to be done for that student. However please ask or
write to Rajendra Babu about the matter. Tell him that it is
not your job to collect money for them.
It is certainly good that you are having medical treatment.
Sushila thinks that you must get your private parts examined by
a woman doctor. Without such examination it will not be possible
to prescribe the right treatment. Get yourself examined, therefore.
Do continue milk and fruit. It would be very nice if you come to
Segaon when I return there. We shall be starting from here on
the 1st. After staying there for three or four days, we shall go to
the Frontier Province. Kanti is here, and of course Saraswati too.
Amtul Salaam is in Ahmedabad. Ba's health is all right and so
is mine.
What have you done about your plan for studying?
Blessings from
BAPU
