603. LETTER TO SHANTI GHOSH
February 14, 1947
CHI. SHANTI,
Sudhir has told me everything about you.
Of course he
had also written to me.
I congratulate you and Sudhir on having
taken the decision independently. It is a different thing that
I do not approve of your decision. I have written this letter in
order to congratulate you, not to stress our difference of opinion.
Sushila is going to Delhi for two days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Sudhir Ghosh Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
