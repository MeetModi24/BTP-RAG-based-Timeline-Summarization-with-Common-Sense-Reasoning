322. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
August 7, 1938
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I got your letter. I was much relieved.
I am sending milk
from here. Sushila, Pyarelal and I think that it would be better
if you refrained from taking milk today. Fruit and lime juice will
be enough. Do not worry about the weakness, but there should be
no injury to the piles.
For their quick shrinking it is better to
avoid food.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10582
