623. LETTER TO F. M. PINTO
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD,
POONA,
October 13, 1945
MY DEAR PINTO,
I have your letter. I take little or no interest in the elec-
tions. Sometimes, however, I have to tender advice. Your letter
I like and also dislike. I like its frankness but the argument is
bad. I want every minority to be robust and independent in the
full sense of the term. A Christian remains a full Indian because
he cannot be otherwise and expects or wants no reward for
remaining an Indian. Then the word 'minority' loses all its
significance. I have put myself in the minorities' position. I can,
therefore, speak with frankness and even some authority. What
does it matter, if older Christians cling to the Government and
look up to it for crumbs? That will be the testing time for the
younger ones. For time runs against the former and in favour of
the latter.
If you have understood and appreciated my argument, in
your private talks too you will bear down the opposition of the
older ones by being and remaining true to yourselves and the
nation to which you belong.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI F. M. PINTO
NATIONALIST CHRISTIAN PARTY
C/O ANGLO LUSITANO
15 BANK STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
