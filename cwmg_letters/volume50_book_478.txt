478. LETTER TO HIRALAL
August 29, 1932
I look upon myself as slow-witted. There are many things
which I take more time to understand than other people. But
that does not trouble me. There is always a limit to the growth
of the intellect but none to that of the heart.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 389
