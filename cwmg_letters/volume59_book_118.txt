118. LETTER TO SIDDHIMATI
September 30, 1934
CHI. SIDDHIMATI,
I got your letter. Your handwriting is poor. The lines are
not straight. Improve this. If you wish to observe brahmacharya,
you should live separately from Mahadev, never be alone with
any other man or touch him without reason.
You should be
absorbed in your work and keep Ramanama inscribed in your
heart all the twenty-four hours of the day.
SIDDHIMATI
HUBLI
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
