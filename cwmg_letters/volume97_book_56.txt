56. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
[July 18, 1925]
CHI. RAMDAS,
This postcard has been lying with me. I had thought of using it for
writing to you, and that too not on the day fixed for writing to you but
whenever I happened to get two minutes.
BAPU
CHI. RAMDAS GANDHI
KHADI KARYALAYA
AMRELI
KATHIAWAD
From the Gujarati original: Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
