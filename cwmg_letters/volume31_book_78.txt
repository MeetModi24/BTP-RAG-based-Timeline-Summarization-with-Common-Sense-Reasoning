78. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI MEHTA
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
Jeth Vad 2, 1982 [June 27, 1926]
BHAISHRI BHAGWANJI,
I have your letter. Why do you suppose that I am prejudiced
against you? Because I cannot accept your argument it does not
mean that I am prejudiced against you. It is not my experience
that I could refrain from sensual pleasures because I am married.
How can I then accept this position? Among my friends there
are a number of life-long celibates, men as well as women. Then
how can I entertain any doubt?
About God I shall write in Navajivan when I have the time.
I say when I have the time because the question you ask has
been asked often and there will be nothing new in my reply; but
I shall try to take it up for your sake. I have never had any
doubts about your honesty.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI BHAGWANJI ANUPCHAND MEHTA
SADAR
RAJKOT
