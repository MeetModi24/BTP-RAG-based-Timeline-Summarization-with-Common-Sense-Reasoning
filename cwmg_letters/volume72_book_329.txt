329. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 15, 1940
BHAI PRITHVI SINGH,
I won't interfere with your programme, but I must remind
you that you have altered the schedule you had planned. Your
decision was that after finishing your book in about eight days in
Bombay, you would come here, begin the work and impart your
knowledge to the people here. You will keep to the dates you
have sent if you must. If it is not necessary then give them up
and come soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR PRITHVI SINGH
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5641. Also C.W. 2952. Cour-
tesy: Prithvi Singh
