143. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
[June 20, 1924]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. Your hand should attain the perfection
of print. You have not said anything about the copy-book. Ram-
das and Ba returned from Surat yesterday. Pragji's case has been
postponed. Stay on there without worrying and recover your
health. Radha is pulling on as well as she might, Mani is progres-
sing rapidly. There has been no rain yet, only some advance
showers.
Blessings from
BAPU
SISTER VASUMATI &C.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 447. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
