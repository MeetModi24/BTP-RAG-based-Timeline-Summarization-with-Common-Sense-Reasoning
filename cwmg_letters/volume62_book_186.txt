186. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
WARDHA,
December 4, 1935
CHI. MANUDI,
I sent you yesterday the letters from Natal. But though I
spent money on postage, I didn't reply to you and so am writing
again. I am glad you wrote to me. Do write to me from time
to time. You did very well in going there. Serve your mother's
sisters devotedly and, if they permit you, return soon. Keep your-
self well occupied all the time. Describe your daily programme
to me. We had quite a crowd of guests here. The number has
come down today, but will go up again.
Take proper care of your health. If you are careful about
your food, you are bound to keep well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MANUBEHN GANDHI
C/O BALIBEHN VORA
OPP. HIGH SCHOOL
RAJKOT SADAR (KATHIAWAR)
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2667. Courtesy: Manubehn
