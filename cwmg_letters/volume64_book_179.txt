179. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
December 19, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
As decided earlier, I am sending the letter herewith. The
bearer's name is Mahadev. Give him whatever he is able to
carry. Lilavati is of course coming there in a cart.
I want six pomegranates and 12 bananas.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11512
