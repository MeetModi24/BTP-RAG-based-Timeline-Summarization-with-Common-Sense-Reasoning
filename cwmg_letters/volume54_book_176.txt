176. LETTER TO SECRETARY, KANNAPPAR
READING ROOM
March 22, 1933
THE SECRETARY
KANNAPPAR FREE READING ROOM
ROYAPURAM, MADRAS
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter of 24th January last was received by me yesterday
through the kind offices of Rao Bahadur M. C. Rajah.
I am taking immediate steps to find out what is possible for
me to do in the matter.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20659
