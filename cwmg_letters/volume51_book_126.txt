126. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. DESAI
September 19, 1932
There was only one Arjuna to put questions to Krishna. No
wonder, then, that he could reply to all sorts of questions. More-
over, Krishna had spiritual knowledge.
My knowledge is
limited; and what a great number of Arjunas who ask me
questions! Try and count them. If I give a little of my know-
ledge to everyone of them, how long would that Gita be?
Arjuna put questions to Krishna on only one occasion, whereas
so many Arjunas ask me questions every week.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 54
