365. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
WARDHA,
July 14, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
Enclosed is the letter from Mr. Gregg giving an idea of the
situation there. But have no worry. The fees if required will be
paid. Someone had come here. He says that Kellogg is not
now held in such high esteem as some others. I shall find out their
names, addresses and other particulars.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3151
