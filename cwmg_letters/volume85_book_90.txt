90. LETTER TO ABIDALI JAFARBHAI
POONA,
July 30, 1946
BHAI ABIDALI,
I have your letter. It makes me very happy. Now, get
well at Jassawala's and come to me whenever you like. I can
take a lot of work from you.
I am not writing about the other matters just now.
It is 9.15 p. m.
Blessings from
BAPU
ABIDALI JAFARBHAI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
