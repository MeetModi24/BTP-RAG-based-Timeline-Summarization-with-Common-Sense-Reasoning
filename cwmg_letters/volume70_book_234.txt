234. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
NAGPUR,
September 24, 1939
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Don't ask Lilavati or Hansabehn yet about Rajkot. I
have written to Perinbehn and asked her to go. I felt I must
write to her. Wadia has not refused so far.
I have written
to Perinbehn that if Wadia refuses I should like her to go. I
