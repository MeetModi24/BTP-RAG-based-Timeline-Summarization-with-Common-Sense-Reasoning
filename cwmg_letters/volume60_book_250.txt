250. LETTER TO HARIBHAU PHATAK
February 8, 1935
MY DEAR HARIBHAU,
Herewith correspondence between the New Industrial and
Commercial Education Society and myself. If the Secretary
writes to you, you will do the needful.
I have your letter enclosing Dr. Sahasrabuddhe's letter and
elaborate opinion on comparative study of cow's milk and buffalo's
milk by Dr. Apte. I shall make use of both. I am writing to
Dr. Apte.
I hope you had my letter giving you full explanation about
rice. Shankerlal Banker, who has already commenced experi-
menting in unpolished rice, has sent me a valuable record of his
experiments which bears out what I have stated to you and which
you will see in due course in Harijan.
SHRI HARIBHAU G. PHATAK
POONA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
