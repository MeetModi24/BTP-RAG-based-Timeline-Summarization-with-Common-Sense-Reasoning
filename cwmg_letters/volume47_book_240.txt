240. LETTER TO SHANTA S. PATEL
BARDOLI,
July 26, 1931
CHI. SHANTA,
I hear your thunder after many days. All these days you
had no time even to write to me. But at last you have written,
no matter what kind of a letter it is. I must thank you for your
kindness.
I will inquire into your complaint. I have written to Naran-
das and will write to Premabehn too. How can I form any
judgment unless I know what Premabehn has to say? Much of
what you write is news to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 4061
