118. LETTER TO ARCHBISHOP OF CANTERBURY
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
October 15, 1931
DEAR ARCHBISHOP,
It was a pleasure to receive your letter for which Mr.
Andrews had prepared me this morning. I shall present myself at
the Palace at 5.30 p.m. on Tuesday, the 20th. I was most anxious
to make your acquaintance and discuss with you the mission that
has brought me here.
I quite agree with you that our conversation should be con-
fined to us two only.
I remain,
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 18108
