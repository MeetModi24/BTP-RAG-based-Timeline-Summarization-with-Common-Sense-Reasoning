20. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 4, 1928
MY DEAR MALKANI,
I have your letter. What are these two parties over the
separation of Sind? I mean Jethmal and others on the one
hand and Jairamdas and company on the other? Which
are the newspapers you refer to?
You think that it is enough, if the dowry is limited instead
¹ Jethmal Parasram
2 Jairamdas Daulatram
of being unlimited. And what about the poor parents who can't
pay Rs. 3,000, the lowest figure mentioned by you?
Please congratulate on my behalf the girls who are helping
you in khadi-hawking. It certainly requires special bravery on
the part of a Sindhi girl to wear khadi in Hyderabad and then
to hawk it.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 887
