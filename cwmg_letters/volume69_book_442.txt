442. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
ABBOTTABAD,
July 10, 1939
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I am worried for there has been no news from you since
Wednesday. If you have not written anything by the time you
get this letter, send a telegram. I hope Balkrishna is well, and
your worry has ended. At the moment your duty is to stay put.
We shall see what can be done when I come down.
Sushila, Pyarelal, Ba are still in Bombay.
I hope you have found a good help.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4322
