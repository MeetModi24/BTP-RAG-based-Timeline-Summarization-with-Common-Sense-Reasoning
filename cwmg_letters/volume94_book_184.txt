184. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
June 21, 1942
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have not been writing to you because I believe that you are out
of danger. Your postcard today has startled me. Whatever is to happen,
let it happen. Even while striving to live, does not man keep himself
ready for death ?
What is the advantage of going to Poona ? I for one feel that
you should go to Bombay. You can certainly get medical treatment
there. In Poona there was only Trivedi who could have given you any
help you needed. But he is no more.
Blessings from
BAPU
