537. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
DEOLALI,
May 15, 1926
CHI. MANI,
I could persuade Ba [to go]. However, she refuses to go there
before Tuesday and will reach there on Wednesday. Tell Surajbehn
about it. I hope your wards give you satisfaction. Learn to be
a good mixer. Do try to bring Nandubehn to the Ashram.
Krishnadas must have told you about the change in the pro-
gramme.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro —4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 37
