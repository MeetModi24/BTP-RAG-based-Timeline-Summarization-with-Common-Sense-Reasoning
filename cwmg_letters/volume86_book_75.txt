75. LETTER TO KANAM GANDHI
ON THE TRAIN,
October 28, 1946
CHI. KANAM 1 ,
I am writing this on a moving train. I am going to Bengal.
I do not consider your handwriting too bad. Still you can
improve it. You are engrossed in your studies. I do not know
when I shall be going that way. Ask Ushi to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 9523. Courtesy: Kanam Gandhi
