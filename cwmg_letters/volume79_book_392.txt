392. LETTER TO CHIMANDAS ISARDAS
SEVAGRAM,
March 10, 1945
BHAI CHIMANDAS,
I hope you will be able to read this. What is there to write
in English? I heard about Jairamdas. God will guard him. Hope
your mother is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. CHIMANDAS ISARDAS
10 AMIL COLONY
HYDERABAD—SIND
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
