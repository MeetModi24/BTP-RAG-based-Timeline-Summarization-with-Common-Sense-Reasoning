81. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 28, 1930
CHI. GANGABEHN (SENIOR),
I have your letter. The family seems to be increasing. That
is as it should be. So long as we have the spirit of service in us,
people will continue to come.
How serious was Nath's eczema?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- 6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 40; also C.W. 8755. Courtesy: Ganga-
behn Vaidya
