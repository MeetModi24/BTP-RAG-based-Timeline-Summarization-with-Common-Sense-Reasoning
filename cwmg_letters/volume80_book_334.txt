334. LETTER TO GHANSHYAMSINH GUPTA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 28, 1945
BHAI GHANSHYAMSINH,
I like your letter. I am also going on with the work.
The important thing is whether the local people can hold out
or not.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GHANSHYAMSINH GUPTA
SPEAKER
DRUG
