227. LETTER TO G. E. CHATFIELD
April 21, 1919
DEAR MR. CHATFIELD,
A friend has just drawn my attention to the fact that the
mill-hands will be doubly hit by the levy. They have already
been made to pay from their wages. Now, as I understand, they
will be made to pay through the water rate. For every little tene-
ment is liable to pay if the occupant draws more than Rs. 7 per
month. Thus, almost all the mill-hands will have to contribute
to the levy twice over. Probably you have not considered this
point. You have the power of exemption. Could you not exempt
the mill-hands from the double levy if the view submitted by me
is correct?
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat
S.N. 6555
