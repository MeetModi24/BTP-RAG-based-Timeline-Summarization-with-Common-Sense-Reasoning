384. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
August 20, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
I got your message. I have been writing as much as I
can. I remember your suggestion. If God wills it, I will write
before the incense burns out. But who permitted you to resume
work? If you exert your mind and body so soon, the weakness
will be prolonged. Pyarelal is better.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10906
