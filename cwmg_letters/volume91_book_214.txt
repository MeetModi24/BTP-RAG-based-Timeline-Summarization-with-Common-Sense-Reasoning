214. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Diwali, [October 23, 1919]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
May the New Year be propitious to you. May you be healthy
and have good character.
Now you go to Lahore only after the Surat Conference, for
I shall try to go there at that time. My address will be c/o
The Tribune.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
