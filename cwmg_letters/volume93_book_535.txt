535. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM,
July 20, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
How careless you are! Three mistakes in your letter to
Kumarappa? How many times have I corrected "answere"?
There should be no 'e' at the end. "Alltogether" is 'altogether'.
There has to be only one '1'. One 'c' in "reccord". You must
make full use of the dictionary. I cannot tolerate wrong English
spelling and bad handwriting. You can write correct spelling
if you are careful. You can improve your handwriting if you
are careful.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original:
