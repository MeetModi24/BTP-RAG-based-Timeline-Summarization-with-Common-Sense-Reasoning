120. LETTER TO CHAND TYAGI
Dipavali [October 25, 1927]
BHAI CHAND,
Received your letter. For some time now don't fuss about
the Chandrayana vow .
I am happy to learn that you have arrived at the Ashram.
What work have you taken up?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3269
