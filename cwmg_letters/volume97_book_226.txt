226. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
February 12, 1933
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I cannot write a long letter. It does not matter whether you stay in Khali
or outside but it seems proper to me that you should so live that you spend
only what is necessary for you alone. It will be more convenient if you live
in the Ashram. Gomatibehn has in view a girl of twenty-two. But the fact
that she belongs to the bania caste, does present a difficulty. However if the
girl is agreeable, all the conditions stipulated by you will be fulfilled. If,
therefore, you think you can marry her, I would advise you to get married
immediately. Everybody will be pleased. If you wish, you may come and see
me. Have you calmed down now ?
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32987
