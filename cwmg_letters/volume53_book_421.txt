421. LETTER TO DUNCAN GREENLEES
February 16, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I was wondering what had happened to my letter when I
received yours of the 10th instant. Of course there can be no
pledge till you know those with whom you have to work and
they know you. I hope you have got your box. I shall await
your arrival.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20263
