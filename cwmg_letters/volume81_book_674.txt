674. LETTER TO GULZAR SINGH
October 18, 1945
SARDAR GULZAR SINGH,
I have your letter. I don't know which places I shall be
able to visit during my Bengal tour. My request is that all
sevaks should spare me. Only then shall I be able to do what
I want to.
SARDAR GULZAR SINGHJI
SHRI GURU SINGH SABHA
31 RASBEHARI AVENUE, KALIGHAT
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
