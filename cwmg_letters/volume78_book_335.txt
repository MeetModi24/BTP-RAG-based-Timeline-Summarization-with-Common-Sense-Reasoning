335. LETTER TO LAKSHMAN PRASAD TIWARI
SEVAGRAM,
November 2, 1944
SHRI PANDIT LAKSHMANSINGHJI TIWARI,
I have before me your daughter's heart-rending letter. She
wants that either you should bless her marriage to the boy of
her choice, or, if that is not possible, you should never force her
to marry anybody else. I think you can have no objection to
this. How can we now coerce our daughters?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
LAKSHMAN PRASAD TIWARI
NARASINGHPUR
HOSHANGABAD
