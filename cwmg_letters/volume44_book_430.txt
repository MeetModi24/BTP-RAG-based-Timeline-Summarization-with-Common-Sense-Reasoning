430. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 14, 1930
BHURRR TO WHITE-BEARDED YOUNG MAN, THREE WEEKS' GUEST,
If I write in Gujarati to the daughter, who, though a mere
girl, tries to look old, why should I not write in the mother
tongue to the father who, though old, is getting younger day by
day? In the West people communicate by means of wires, and
transmit images too. I have the gift of the East. I see before me
a youthful face, white-bearded but bursting with hearty laughter,
and I hear the man telling the reporter: "I shall be back in the
guest-house within three weeks." I have in my pocket the like-
ness of his face notwithstanding that I don't have a pocket. The
old man has played his part marvellously well. The whole family
is like him! May God protect him. Khuda Hafiz.
Bhurrr from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9573
