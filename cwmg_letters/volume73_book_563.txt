563. LETTER TO PRABHU DAYAL VIDYARTHI
March 28, 1941
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
I have your letter. Give up all needless talk and do only what
Kakasaheb says. Consider it your dharma to do whatever work he
gives and be fully engrossed in it.
Blessings from
BAPU
