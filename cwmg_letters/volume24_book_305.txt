305. LETTER TO ALI HASAN
SABARMATI,
August 10, 1924
DEAR FRIEND,
I did have your letter. A Hindu is one who believes in the
Vedas, Upanishads, Puranas, &c., and Varnashrama dharma. I
am unable to agree with you that we may reject the claim of those
who say they belong to particular religions. I claim to be the best
judge of what I believe. Don't you?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MR. ALI HASAN, BAR-AT-LAW
PATNA
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
