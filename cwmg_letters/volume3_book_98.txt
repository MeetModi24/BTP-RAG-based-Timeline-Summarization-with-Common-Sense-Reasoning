98. LETTER TO COLONIAL SECRETARY, NATAL
14 MERCURY LANE,
DURBAN,
August 13, 1900
TO
THE HONOURABLE THE COLONIAL SECRETARY
PIETERMARITZBURG
SIR,
I beg to acknowledge your letter of the 11th instant con-
veying the information that His Excellency the Governor
has received from the Secretary of State a telegram stating
that Her Majesty desires that her thanks may be conveyed
185
to the Natal British Indians for their message of condolence.
I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
M. K. GANDHI
Pietermaritzburg Archives: C.S.O. 6142/1900
