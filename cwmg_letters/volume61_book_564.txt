564. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
September 6, 1935
BHAI MUNSHI,
Study this case. If you think we can win it, you may go
to Jalgaon or send someone else. These friends cannot afford
to pay that person anything more than the railway fare.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 7589. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
