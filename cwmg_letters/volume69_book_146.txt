146. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
April 7, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Yours. I am off to Rajkot. Mahadev will tell you all about
it. Of course there is no Brindavan now. The Talimi Sangh
will meet on 12th, 13th, 14th. I can't attend. I shall try to go
to Subhas if I can from Rajkot. I am quite fit.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3908. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7217
