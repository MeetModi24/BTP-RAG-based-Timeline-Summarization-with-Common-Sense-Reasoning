6. LETTER TO GHULAM RASOOL QURESHI
PANCHGANI,
July 17, 1946
CHI. QURESHI,
I got your letter giving me the details. It is most painful.
It seems they haven't learnt any lesson from the death of Vasant-
rao and others. Send me further details.
I hope both of you as well as the children are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
GHULAM RASOOL QURESHI
SABARMATI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
