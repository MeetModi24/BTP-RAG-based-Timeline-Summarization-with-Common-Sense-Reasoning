270. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
WARDHA,
February 23, 1945
CHI. KISHORELAL,
I have been worrying ever since you fell ill. Both of you
should think over the matter. I think both of you can be cured
with treatment. The first thing is sufficient rest, some nature-
cure treatment and proper yogic exercises. Hip-bath plays a
very important role in treatment. Try it.
Blessings from
BAPU
K. MASHRUWALA
ASHRAM
SEVAGRAM
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
