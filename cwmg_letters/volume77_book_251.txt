251. LETTER TO P. G. MATHEW
PANCHGANI,
July 11, 1944
MY DEAR MATHEW,
I am glad you are fixed up at last. Of course, I have not
forgotten you but I have not written to the people I have not
forgotten. I write only when I must.
Love.
BAPU
PROF. P. G. MATHEW
S. H. COLLEGE
THEVARA, via ERNAKULAM
From a photostat: G.N. 1544
