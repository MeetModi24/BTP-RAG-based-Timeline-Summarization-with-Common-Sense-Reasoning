182. LETTER TO SECRETARY, SUMMERFORD
ORCHARD
August 12, 1945
THE MANAGER,
I have received the apples you have sent on behalf of Shri
Padampatji. They are good. Thanks.
M. K. GANDHI
SECRETARY, SUMMERFORD ORCHARD
RAMGARH P. O.
DIST. NAINITAL, U. P.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
