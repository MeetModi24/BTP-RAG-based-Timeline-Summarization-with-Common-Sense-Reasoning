515. LETTER TO JAYA
SEVAGRAM,
December 1, 1944
CHI. JAYA1,
Didn't my sending the yarn by itself mean my blessings?
Since, however, you are not satisfied with that, tell Chi. Bindu
and Chi. Chandrakant that I bless them and wish that both of
them should understand the significance of marriage which I have
explained, act accordingly, lead their lives in a spirit of service
and be happy.
If you can persuade Manu to go with you, she may certainly
do so. I have not held her back. You should, however, under-
stand that on an occasion like this one should entertain no expect-
ation from anybody who may have been entrusted to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
