511. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
July 29, 1947
DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I have your letter. Your apprehension about freedom turning
into ruin is justified. If that happens, whose fault will it be?
Now we have to think what to do.
I want to reach there before the 15th. Let us see what
God wills. Abha is better but one cannot say the improvement
is very great. She moves about. I think she will be with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
How do you manage during the Ramzan? Don't you get
goat's milk?
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 587
