79. LETTER TO D. J. REID
MOTIHARI,
January 17, 1918
DEAR MR. REml,
I did not know whilst I had the privilege of working with
you, what it meant for you to be on that Committee. I know
now what risks you ran. I do not offer you my sympathy for I
know that you are unaffected by the campaign of calumny Messrs
Irwin and Jameson are leading. Public men who wish to work
honestly can only rely upon the approbation of their own conscience.
No other certificate is worth anything for them. May you have
strength to bear the fire through which you are passing.
I hope you had a nice time in Ceylon.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 4447
