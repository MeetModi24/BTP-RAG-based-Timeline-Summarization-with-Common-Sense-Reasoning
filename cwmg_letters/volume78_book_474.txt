474. LETTER TO KAILAS HAKOOR
SEVAGRAM,
November 25, 1944
DEAR SIR KAILAS,
I am late in acknowledging your kind letter of 28th ultimo.
I am so much pressed for time. I send these lines to thank you
for your letter.
Pray don't be anxious about the contemplated fast. It won't
come except in answer to a call from God.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR KAILAS HAKOOR
SIMLA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
