148. LETTER TO ANNIE BESANT
2, CATHEDRAL STREET,
[MADRAS,]
March 23, 1919
DEAR MRS. BESANT,
There appeared in yesterday's New India a letter signed by
The correspondent has given some infor-
mation about the proceedings of a private meeting of satya-
grahis. May I say, for future guidance, that the proceedings of the
Sabha or its Committee are to be regarded as private unless when
publication is officially authorized? I am sure that you will res-
pect the Committee's wish.
