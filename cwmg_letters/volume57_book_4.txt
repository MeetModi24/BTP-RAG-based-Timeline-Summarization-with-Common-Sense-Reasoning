4. LETTER TO GOVINDDAS
January 17, 1934
BHAI GOVINDDAS,
I have your letter. I was delighted.
I too wish to meet
you. The itinerary is enclosed. The dates for U.P. are not
fixed. Utkal is followed by Bengal, Assam and Bihar.
God sustains me.
I trust you are keeping well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 9709. Courtesy: Bharat Kala Bhavan,
Varanasi
