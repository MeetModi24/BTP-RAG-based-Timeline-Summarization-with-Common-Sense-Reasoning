156. LETTER TO VIDYA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 7, 1945
CHI. VIDYA,
Your husband went and then your son too. But what does
it matter? All of us are destined to go the same way. Everyone
pays his debt and is gone. Some go early, some late. Let the
rest of your life be a life of service. Then happiness or sorrow is
all the same. May God prosper you.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VIDYABEHN
RAMJAS BUILDING
4 PARK ROAD
LUCKNOW
