292. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
March 26, 1932
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
What is your wish regarding the bamboo charkha? I need
not see the model. I should like to see models which would
help us to increase the output. If this model is strong and good
otherwise, people will use it because of its low cost. Do you
think it necessary that it should be used in the Ashram? I can-
not judge about that from here.
Let me know what the condition of your nose is.
If Sharda does not have massage or take sun-bath, is it be-
cause she dislikes them or is it through lethargy?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.G. 12
