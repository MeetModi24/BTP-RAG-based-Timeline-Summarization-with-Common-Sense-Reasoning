548. LETTER TO JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
AS AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 9, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter enclosing the draft agreement prepared
by the Company. You will see that I have made 3 additions,
all of which are, in my opinion, necessary. I have signed the
agreement before two witnesses and initialled the additions whe-
ther made by me or the Company.
The two parts I contemplate publishing as soon as possible.
It is open to the Macmillan Company to publish the volumes
as they get completed. But if they do not feel inclined to do so,
they must not mind the sale of Indian edition outside India.
Hence the inscription of the addition to clause 121.
Yours sincerely,
DR. JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
12 PARK AVENUE
NEW YORK CITY
(U.S.A.)
From a copy: S.N. 32231
