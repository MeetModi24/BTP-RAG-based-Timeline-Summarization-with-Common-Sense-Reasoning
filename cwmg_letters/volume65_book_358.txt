358. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
June 19, 1937
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Despatch this wire: "Khan Saheb not enthusiastic on his own.
If need his presence urgent come and discuss with him.
Gandhi."
Send this reply if it seems proper to you. I do not wish
to send it as an order.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2987
