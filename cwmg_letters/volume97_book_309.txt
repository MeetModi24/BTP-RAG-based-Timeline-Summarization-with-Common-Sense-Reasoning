309. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI AND SUMITRA GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 17, 1939
CHI. NIMU-SUMI,
I got your letter. Kanam is here sitting with me. He keeps me talking to
him. So how am I to complete this letter? For the present, you should continue
with your studies there. Do not worry about Kanam. We will think about the
matter after I return from the Frontier Province. Ba will go to live in the
Harijan Ashram for the present. Kanam will study there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
1 Gandhiji returned from a tour of the Frontier Province on July 27, reaching
Segaon on the 28th.
