142. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 7, 1945
DEAR AGATHA,
This is a full letter. I simply add my love and say I am
flourishing as well as circumstances permit.
BAPU
MISS AGATHA HARRISON
2 CRANBOURNE COURT
ALBERT BRIDGE ROAD
LONDON S. W. 1 1
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
