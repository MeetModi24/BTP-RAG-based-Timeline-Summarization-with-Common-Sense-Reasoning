420. LETTER TO MITHIBEHN
October 15, 1932
Eradication of untouchability does not imply inter-dining
and intermarriage with Antyajas. But I don't believe that those
who inter-dine with the so-called untouchables, Harijans, vio-
late dharma. Restrictions on inter-dining and intermarriage
among communities are not an inseparable part of Hinduism.
They are only a matter of custom. We cannot treat Harijans dif-
ferently from other communities. Since there will be no coer-
cion in this matter, there is no reason for anybody to feel hurt.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 138
