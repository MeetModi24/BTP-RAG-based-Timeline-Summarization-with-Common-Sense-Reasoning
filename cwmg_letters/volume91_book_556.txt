556. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
PATNA,
Silence Day [January 31, 1927]
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Your present was at last received here. On arriving here
yesterday, I found the box of biscuits waiting. For the last two
days I have been eating them. They are well made. The soda
is a bit much but there is no harm in it.
What has happened to Kokila? What was it she quarrelled
with Kripalani over? It is as well she is with you. We are leav-
ing for Maharashtra tonight.
2nd - Gondia
3rd - Nagpur-Wardha
4th - Wun
5th - Yeotmal
6th & 7th - Akola
The detailed programme for the tour thenceforward has not
been received.
It may be said that good work has been done here. A sum
of Rs. 50,000 has been collected and the khadi sales were really
good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 32820
