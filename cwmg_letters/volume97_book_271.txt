271. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
WARDHA,
April 29, 1935
CHI. RAMDAS,
I got both your letters. Even if you were to fail in following your own
inclination, I would count that too, as success. If you do what I desire and
succeed in that, it would be my success. But that would be of no profit to
you. What you need just now is self-confidence. If you acquire that, all will
be well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
