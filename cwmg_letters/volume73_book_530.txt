530. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 21, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
It was not proper to burden Mahadevbhai. What is to be
done now? I will see to that. To burden Kishorelalbhai for such
matters should be considered a sin. I understand about Akbar. Let
me see what can be done.
If I am told what Chimanlal said, I shall be able to say some-
thing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4378
