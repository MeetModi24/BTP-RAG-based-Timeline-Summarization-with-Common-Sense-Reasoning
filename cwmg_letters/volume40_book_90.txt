90. LETTER TO T. NAGESH RAO
S.S. "ARONDA",
March 7, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Heart-felt prayer comes as a result of
persistent effort without weariness.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. T. NAGESH RAO
TEACHER, BOARD HIGH SCHOOL, PUTTUR
S. CANARA (S. INDIA)
From a photostat: G.N. 9240
