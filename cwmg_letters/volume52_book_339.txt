339. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
December 21, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA,
You have become careless about your handwriting recently.
Write the next letter with your right hand. You should write with
both hands alternately.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5780. Also C.W. 3003. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
