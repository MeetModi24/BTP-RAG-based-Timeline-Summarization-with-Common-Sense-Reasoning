540. LETTER TO DR. MOHAMMAD ALAM
February 25, 1933
DEAR DR. ALAM,
I was delighted to have your letter and to observe that you
were making steady progress.
If she continues to be as careless as she has been in her Urdu
writing in her latest letter, please tell Begum Alam that I shall
have to tone down my compliments. At one time I thought that
she was competing with Zora in beautiful writing, but in her pre-
sent letter I fancy that I can easily beat her after half a dozen
letters from Zora. This won't do. I do not want to break my
idols. I had begun to think that Muslim women beat their Hindu
sisters hollow in perfect writing, for every Muslim sister who has
written to me has a beautiful hand. Begum Alam must keep
up that reputation. I hope to enclose with this a letter for Be-
gum Alam.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Encls.
From a photostat: G.N. 28
