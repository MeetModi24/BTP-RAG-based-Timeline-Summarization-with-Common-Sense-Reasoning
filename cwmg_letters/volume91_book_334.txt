334. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Bhadrapad Sud 13 [September 11, 1924]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I had good news about Taramati today. Now she must re-
cover. I am worried only about the future. In my view a good,
open and clean place is absolutely necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
