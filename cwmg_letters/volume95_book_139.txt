139. LETTER TO Y.M. DADOO
SEGAON, WARDHA
August 19, 1939
MY DEAR DADOO,
It has stirred me to find you leading the Satyagraha band.
Manilal and Medh have together given me a good account of you. It
makes me glad to know that you are the son of a valued client of
mine.
You know that I am watching your movement as closely as I
can. You have done well in sending me the relevant literature. There
is just a ray of hope that we shall reach a settlement. But you will
