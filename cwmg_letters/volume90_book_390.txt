390. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
January 13, 1948
CHI. MANI,
I had a talk with the Sardar today. Therefore not again
just now. I wish to see the men from Bahawalpur. I will send
for you after that. I can't judge how I misunderstood the matter.
I will rectify the mistake.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
