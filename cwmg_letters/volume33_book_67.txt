67. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
Unrevised
DONDAICHE,
Sunday [February 13, 1927]
CHI. MIRA,
Today is Sunday morning before prayer whilst the others
are getting up.
Here is the further itinerary.
I have your latest letter.
I forgot to tell you how glad I was that Father and others
were coming nearer to you. It must be the natural result as you have
for them nothing but pure affection daily growing purer. Know-
ing affection grows purer if also more detached.
I understand the changes in your vow. They are quite all
right.
No more today.
Yours,
BAPU
