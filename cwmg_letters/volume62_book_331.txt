331. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
March 25, 1936
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
I read both your letters. If you find no peace in Savli
or your health suffers there, you have my permission and bless-
ings to go wherever you deem fit. Kishorelalbhai will write
in detail. I have written to him at length.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1883
