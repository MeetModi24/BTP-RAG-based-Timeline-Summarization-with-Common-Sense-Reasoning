132. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
July 1, 1932
DEAR PUSHPA,
Received your letter. Sultan means a king. He can do much.
But if he does not worship God his whole life and begins to do so
in old age, that cannot be done. There is no reason why you
should [not] write a letter in Hindi. It is proper that you do.
But my Hindi may not be correct, and so if I write in Hindi it
may be a wrong lesson.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No. 800
(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 53
