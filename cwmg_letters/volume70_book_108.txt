108. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 14, 1939
BHAI JIVANJI,
What sort of an article do you expect from me? Hadn't
you better leave me out? You should give me a reward even if
I am able to do all the other work you have given.
I understand about the Autobiography.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI JIVANJI
P. O. Box 105, AHMEDABAD
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9947. Also C.W. 6921. Court-
esy: Jivanji D. Desai
