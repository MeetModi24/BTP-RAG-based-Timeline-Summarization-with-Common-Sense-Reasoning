21. LETTER TO MOHAN AND VANAMALA PARIKH
April 24, 1933
CHI. MOHAN AND VANAMALA,
I don't remember who is the elder of you two. If Vana-
mala is elder, then she should excuse me for putting her name
second. We do not follow the rule of the West. There, even if
the girl is younger, her name would be put before that of her
elder brother. We always respect seniority in age.
I wanted to write about your health but I have written
something else. Never mind. Write to me, both of you, how
you keep.
Van am ala has done very good spinning indeed.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5781. Also C.W. 3004. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
