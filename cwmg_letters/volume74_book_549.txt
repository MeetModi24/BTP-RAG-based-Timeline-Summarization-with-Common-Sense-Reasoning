549. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 20, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
I dare not give you more than this today. For the first time
after his arrival I was able to give some time to R[ajendra]
Babu. The patients here are getting on. The Ashram is still
filling.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4079. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7388
