540. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
May 16, 1932
CHI. JAMNA,
If your health does improve there, what more can we desire?
Kusum was guilty of a serious lapse in not asking for permis-
sion. Did you draw her attention to her error? Sometimes the
girls don't mean anything and forget common courtesy only
through their innocence. I admit this short-coming in our edu-
cation.
If the grown-up women treat all the children as their own, the
latter would develop very well.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 853. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
