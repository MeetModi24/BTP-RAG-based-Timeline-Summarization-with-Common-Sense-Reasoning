459. LETTER TO MRS. RACHEL M. RUTTER
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 8, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. What you say about Miss Mayo
is only too true. There are people who will not stop at anything
however untruthful it may be.
I am taking the liberty of quoting in Young India that part of
your letter which refers to Miss Mayo. I am not making use
of your name.
Mr. Andrews is just now in Colombo, nursing the Poet who
on his way to Europe had to disembark owing to sudden illness.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. RACHEL M. RUTTER
IRESON LANE
WINCANTON
SOMERSET, ENGLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 14323
