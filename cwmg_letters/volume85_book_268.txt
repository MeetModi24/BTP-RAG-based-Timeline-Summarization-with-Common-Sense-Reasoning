268. LETTER TO JEHANGIR PATEL
NEW DELHI,
August 30, 1946
BHAI JEHANGIRJI,
I have your letter. I understand what you say. I shall
write in English whenever I have anything special to say. But
I do want you to get used to reading and writing Gujarati.
I have wired Ramprasad to see you on the 3rd. I have
also written to Bhai Bhise. Your committee can now do as
they like. I hope Khurshedbehn is well. It will be more than
enough if she maintains her health. I am glad that Dinshaw
will be going with you. Please comfort him.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JEHANGIR PATEL
