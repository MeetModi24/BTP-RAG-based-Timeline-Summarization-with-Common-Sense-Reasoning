109. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI G. PATEL
October 22, 1940
CHI.
SHIVABHAI,
I got your letter and the roll of cloth. I have gone through
the figures. They are promising. Hard work always pays. Give
my congratulations to all those who have contributed to the work.
Gangabehn has explained everything to me in detail. Use
whatever yarn and money you have collected for me for the khadi
work there. Suryachandra found everything strange here for a
day or two. Now he is happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHIVABHAI G. PATEL
VALLABH VIDYALAYA
BOCHASAN
BORSAD TALUKA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9519. Also C.W. 436. Courtesy:
Shivabhai G. Patel
