162. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
March 25, 1929
CHI. MIRA,
I posted a letter yesterday written on the boat. On reach-
ing here I found three letters from you. There should have been
one today but nothing up to 4 p.m. It looks as if there would
be no difficulty about my leaving tomorrow.
Rajenbabu is here. He has told me all about your activity.
You leave little for anybody else to tell me of your doings.
The heat here during the day is fairly strong. The morning
was quite cool and there was a breeze throughout the night.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5354. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9410
