193. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Friday [August 10, 1928]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your long letter giving all the details. It is very well
written. I know now all that I wanted to know. I wish to
make no suggestion. The doctor is a good man; why should I
interfere with his treatment? Personally, though, I would not
approve of gram soup, and that too spiced.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 494. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
telegram was sent with your knowledge and approval. I would also be pleased
to receive any comment that you may care to make in regard to this matter."
Vide also "Letter to Dr. B. C. Roy", 3-8-1928.
