341. LETTER TO KAJI
NEW DELHI,
July 15, 1947
DEAR KAJI,
I have your full letter which is deeply interesting.
I can give no opinion. All I can say generally is that you
should avoid parties within our own ranks.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
