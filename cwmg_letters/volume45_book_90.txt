90. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 8, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. It is a good sign that there are some
days in between when you don't suffer from your usual complaint.
You must have received my previous letter. I am very eager
indeed that you should go over to the Ashram without delay.
Why doesn't Jayaprakash write to me? And what is the position
about him now? Is it now fixed that he is joining that college?
Is he waiting for any letter from Ghanshyamdasji?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3393
