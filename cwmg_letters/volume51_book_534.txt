534. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
November 1, 1932
MY DEAR PLAYMATE AND UNWILLING SLAVE,
Wanting you to be a willing slave, though a driver of slaves,
I slavishly carry out your wish that out of philanthropy I should
write to you with the left hand. I had no notion of my being
a slave-driver till mates like you from personal experience made
the discovery. So I had thought that they must have willingly
submitted to the yoke. But I see that your pride comes in the way
of your making a frank admission. I do not want more accidents
to humble your pride. . . .
About reading the books you have sent me, I shall follow
the course suggested by you. I am fast increasing the number of
my teachers. Raihana was the first, then Zohra was appointed.
And now you have sought the honour. Let this serve as the letter
of appointment. But in order to retain the honour you will have
to be good and not ill and bed-ridden.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 194
