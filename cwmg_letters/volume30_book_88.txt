88. LETTER TO J. V. BETHMANN
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 5, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. There have been many applications for
translation of the autobiography I am writing but I have not yet
given to anybody the exclusive right.
The lady from Copenhagen is free to translate it if she is
content to do so without claiming an exclusive right.
I thank you and Mrs. Bethmann for your kind enquiry about
my health which is progressing.
With regards to yourself and Mrs. Bethmann.
Yours sincerely,
REV. J. V. BETHMANN
20 MILLER ROAD, KILPAUK
MADRAS
