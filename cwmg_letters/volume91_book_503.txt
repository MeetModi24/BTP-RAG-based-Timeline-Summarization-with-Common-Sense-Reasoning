503. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday [July 9, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. I am very happy that your temperature
is now under control. I hope you have made it a rule to keep
to your bed when it is raining. Even a slight pain in the sto-
mach is not good. Have you not come across a good doctor
there? If not, send all details to Dr. Mehta and have him pres-
cribe treatment. What is the situation about fruit there? Do

you get good milk? Persuade Taramati to write to me. She
should go for a walk even when it is raining a little. She can
carry an umbrella. I shall consider it sinful if she does not gain
in physical strength in that climate.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
HOMI VILLA
PANCHGANI
