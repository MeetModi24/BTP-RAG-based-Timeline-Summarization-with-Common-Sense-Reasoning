198. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
November 3, 1933
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
My own opinion is that the atrocities such as are referred to
in the Frontier Province note should first be dealt with privately,
and all the means that we can employ to induce the authorities
to adopt less cruel measures should be employed. I am asking
Andrews to deal with the Frontier Province note. And if you
have no objection I would like you to show the note to Sir Tej
Bahadur and see what he has got to say and if he is at all
inclined to move.
I enclose herewith a copy of my letter to the Governor of
Bengal about what is going on in Hijli.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1933. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
