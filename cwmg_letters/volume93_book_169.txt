169. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
DELHI,
March 22, 1937
DEAR SISTER,
It seems you are having your share of sorrows. Mridula
usually gives me the news, happy or sad, of the family. Whenever
she meets me she generally informs me of things at that end. It
is my hope that whatever happens, you will not lose your peace
of mind and watch with patience whatever happens and do
whatever you can with determination.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
