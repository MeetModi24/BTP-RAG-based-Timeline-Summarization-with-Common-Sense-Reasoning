113. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
[After February 6, 1932]
I got yours today. I was wondering what had happened to
you. I did not receive your letter in reply to mine. Do write
again and repeat your questions. I think I shall receive it safely.
We are all doing well, engaged in various studies and spinning.
We have more books than we can cope with. Love from us all.
BAPU
Bapu -Conversations and Correspondence with Mahatma Gandhi, p. 15
