309. LETTER TO M. R. MASANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 18, 1939
MY DEAR MASANI,
I want the co-operation of everyone. But the question is
whether our agreement is superficial or solid. The Viceregal
declaration will soon show where we are.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 4131. Also C.W. 4889. Courtesy: M. R. Masani
