61. LETTER TO KATHERINE MAYO
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 4, 1926
DEAR FRIEND1,
I have your letter of the 28th ultimo enclosing a letter from the
Bishop of Calcutta.
17th March will be a suitable day for me to receive you at 4 p.m.
at the above address. The Ashram is about four miles from Ahmedabad.
Most of the gadiwallas at the Station know the place.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MISS KATHERINE MAYO
C/O THOMAS COOK & SONS
BOMBAY
From a copy: Katherine Mayo Papers. Courtesy: Yale University Library,
New Haven, Connecticut, U.S.A.
