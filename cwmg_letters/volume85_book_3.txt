3. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
PANCHGANI,
July 16, 1946
CHI. HOSHIARI,
I have your letter. Certainly I was a little annoyed. There
was no reason for your coming to Delhi. If you found Gajraj
in poor condition you alone were responsible for it. I had
thought that you would be firm in your resolve but I find that
you were not. I believed that you had given up your attach-
ment to home but that too I did not find. Anyway, how does
it matter? It is good that I now see you as you really are. It
may be said, and rightly, that I have no reason to be unhappy
about it. You may now realize the limit of your strength and do
what you can. In that alone lies your well-being. Do not spoil
Gajraj. Leave him alone but gradually, otherwise he will go
completely out of hand.
I have your slivers.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. HOSHIARIBEHN
SEVAGRAM ASHRAM
