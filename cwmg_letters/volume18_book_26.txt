26. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
Monday [Before July 12, 1920]
CHI. MAGANLAL 2 ,
I have today asked Anandanand to write to you and tell
you that Mr. Kale of Dharwar will be leaving for Ahmedabad
by mail and that you should receive him at the station. I have
asked him to write to you in detail.
This letter I write for the sake of Ba. I observe that she is not
quite happy. She has been ill and works beyond her strength.
As Nirmala came away with me, I asked Devdas to stay behind.
Consult Gokibehn and Ba, with Devdas present, and do whatever
is necessary. Perhaps it will do if you reduce the burden of work
in regard to clothes and utensils. I do not understand why there
should be any difficulty on account of rotlis . If the work in the
kitchen is too heavy and if it is possible to make any changes,
make them. Are there at present 15 persons who take their meals?
I shall return there most probably on Monday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 5787. Courtesy: Radhabehn Choudhri
