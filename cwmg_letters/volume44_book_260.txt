260. LETTER TO KASUMBA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 28, 1930
CHI. KASUMBA,
You did a very good thing in going over to Chalala. You
should live as you agreed to do during our talk. Give up the
idea of being polluted by anyone's touch. I trust Umiya is happy.
Take interest in all the activities of Jaisukhlal. I was pleased by
the virtue of firmness which I observed in you. I should like
you to use it in serving others.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./III
