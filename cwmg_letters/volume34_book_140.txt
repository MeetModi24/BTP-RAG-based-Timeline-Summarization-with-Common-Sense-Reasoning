140. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 13, 1927
DEAR MOTILALJI,
Here is a characteristic letter from "Comrade" Saklatvala.
The facsimile of the stamped letter he sends seems to make out a
very strong moral claim for him. I do not need to say any-
thing more; for, I know that whatever language he uses you will
press his claim for all its worth.
I have another letter from Jawaharlal. There is nothing
new in it.
Yours sincerely,
PANDIT MOTILAL NEHRU
'ANAND BHAWAN'
ALLAHABAD
From a photostat: S.N. 14183
