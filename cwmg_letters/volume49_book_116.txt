116. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
February 8, 1932
MY DEAR AGATHA,
I have your letter. I was wondering how you were doing.
I was therefore glad to hear from you. I may not say more
beyond sending my love. Sardar Vallabhbhai is with me and we
are both keeping well.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
MISS AGATHA HARRISON
2 CRANBOURNE COURT
ALBERT BRIDGE ROAD
LONDON S.W. 11
From a photostat: G.N. 1450
