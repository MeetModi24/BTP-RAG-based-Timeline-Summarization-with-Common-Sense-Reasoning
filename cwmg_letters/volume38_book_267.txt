267. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Thursday [December 13, 1928]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I have your letter about Lalaji. I am happy to learn that
khadi work is progressing. In this connection there is a letter from
Satis Babu. I send it to you for perusal. You need not return it.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From Hindi: C.W. 6164. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
