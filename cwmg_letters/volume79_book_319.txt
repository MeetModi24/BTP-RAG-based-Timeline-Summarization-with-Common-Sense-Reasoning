319. LETTER TO K. N. KATJU
SEVAGRAM,
March 1, 1945
BHAI KATJU,
I saw your letter about Father's illness. Your wife has gone
and so will Father, some day if not today. Let us do our duty.
Everything rests on that.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. KATJU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
