35. LETTER TO MANCHERSHA AVARI
July 23, 1945
BHAI MANCHERSHA AVARI,
I have your letter. I am very happy to know that you are
immersed in constructive work and your wife is also with you.
It is difficult to attend to everyone-and then there are so many
things to see to. It will be so much the better if you could carry
further what has since been accomplished. Let us see what happens
about the death-sentence convicts. I am doing all that I can.
Blessings to both of you from
BAPU
GENERAL MANCHERSHA AVARI
SIRAS PETH
NAGPUR CITY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
1 Several freedom fighters who had retaliated against British repression
in Chimur and Ashti had been sentenced to death. Vide also Vol. LXXIX,
