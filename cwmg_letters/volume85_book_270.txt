270. LETTER TO VICHITRA NARAYAN SHARMA
NEW DELHI,
August 30, 1946
BHAI VICHITRA,
Your resolve to present yarn on the occasion of Charkha
Jayanti is indeed commendable and I hope it will succeed.
My faith has ever been increasing that if we all take to spin-
ning with the right understanding we can make greater head-
way along the path of freedom.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VICHITRA NARAYAN SHARMA
GANDHI ASHRAM
MEERUT
