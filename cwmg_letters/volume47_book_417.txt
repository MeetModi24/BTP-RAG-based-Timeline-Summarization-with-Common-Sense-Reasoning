417. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
ASHRAM,
August 23, 1931
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
Jamna believes that you now wish to get married or are willing
to consider a proposal. If this is true, let me know and also tell
me whether you have already chosen the girl. If I am to choose
for you, tell me whether you wish to respect the restrictions of
caste or Province. You know my own views in the matter. We
wish to do away with such restrictions but in a matter like mar-
riage I would certainly not insist on my own ideas being followed.
The inclination of the person who wishes to marry should prevail.
Write to me frankly and tell me what you wish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 902. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
