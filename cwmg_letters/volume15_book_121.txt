121. LETTER TO SIR STANLEY REED
February 25, 1919
It is not without some hesitation that I am sending the en-
closed papers to you. But I feel that the right course for me to
adopt is not to withhold them from you. Probably you will totally
disagree with me as to my opinion of the Bills as also the method
proposed to be adopted for securing redress. I will not argue
about the matter because I can carry the argument no further
than I have done in my telegram to the Viceroy.
All the papers are confidential.
I shall value your frank opinion in the matter.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
