294. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
July 26, 1941
CHI. VALJI,
I got your wire. I have already written and suggested that a
report of the investigation as far as it has proceeded will suffice.
I shall let you know as soon as I receive the report and will
also fix the date.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7495. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
