302. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
SEVAGRAM,
October 28, 1944
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
You must have received my wire. Bhai Amrita Lal, his daughter
and his younger son have arrived. The whole family is pleased.
Amrita Lal is perfectly willing that you should give away the bride.
This simplifies everything. I will accommodate you, Jehangirji
and any other people whom you may bring along in this very
place. Grandmother should not be put to any trouble. Get her
operated upon. Her blessings will be enough.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 4804. Courtesy: Shantikumar N.
Morarjee
