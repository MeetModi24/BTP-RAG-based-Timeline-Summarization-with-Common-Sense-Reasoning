314. LETTER TO D.E. KALELKAR
CHI. KAKA,
Herewith a beautiful letter from Bal.
You should be given a certificate as an expert in making
dentures.
Shriman has written and asked for exemption from having to
come on the 2nd. I have replied that he need not come. We shall do
without him. We shall think about selecting the six some other time.
Why send for him in this weather?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 10955
