173. LETTER TO RAMANAND TEERTH
SEVAGRAM,
November 26, 1945
BHAI RAMANAND SWAMI,
I have your letter. As I have said earlier, you should not
approach me for advice. Pandit Jawaharlalji is the President.
You should ask him and do as he advises.
I hope you have recovered by now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
