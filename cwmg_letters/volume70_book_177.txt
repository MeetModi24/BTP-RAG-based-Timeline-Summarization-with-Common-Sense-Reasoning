177. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
SEGAON ASHRAM, WARDHA,
August 31, 1939
BAPA,
Before you write to me about your doings, I mostly get to
know about them. Whatever may be the case with others, we
have benefited by the Congress Raj. Harijan work has progressed
fairly well. If they mean to, they can do a lot more. Shantilal
had sent me Barve's letter. Instead of writing in Harijan, I am
directly dealing with Kher in order that the work may be done
more expeditiously. Barve will meet me on the 7th. Won't you
take some rest?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1184
