46. LETTER TO PARASHURAM MEHROTRA
November 21, 1932
CHI. PARASHURAM,
I am unable now to write a long letter. Don't mind if some
of the classes have had to be closed. Carry on those which are
running. You will be able to keep up the atmosphere even by
talking with everybody in Hindi.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 7512. Also C.W. 4989. Courtesy:
Parashuram Mehrotra
