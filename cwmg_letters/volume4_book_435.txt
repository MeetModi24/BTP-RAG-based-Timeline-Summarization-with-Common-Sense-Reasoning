435. LETTER TO MAX NATHAN
[JOHANNESBURG,]
June 29, 1905
TO
MAX NATHAN, ESQ.
KEMPSEY BLDGS.
JOHANNESBURG
DEAR MR. NATHAN,
Re : Meer Allam & Leve
My clerk, Mr. Polak, tells me that each time he has called on you
regarding this matter, you have insulted him. This is painfully surprising
as this would be the last thing I should expect from you. He came to
you for a simple inquiry, and he tells me, in a rough tone you declined
to see him or to give any information. Why so ?
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
Letter Book (1905) : No. 470.
