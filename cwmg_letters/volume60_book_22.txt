22. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
December 21, 1934
BHAI MUNSHI,
Do take proper rest. In my view Khan Saheb is a real bhakta.
I have nearly finished your last chapter. The other chapters
I will read now. For whose benefit did you write all this in
English? I do not know if you have explained your aim in the
earlier chapters.
Please do not expect a long foreword from me.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
ADVOCATE K. M. MUNSHI
PANCHGANI
From Gujarati: C.W. 7565. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
