234. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
March 14, 1932
BA,
For some days past I had been thinking of writing to you;
and I read today that you were still out. And so I had a mind
to write. Mahadev also is with us now. Thus our company has
become larger. All three of us are quite well. Ramdas came and
saw me. He was all right. Write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
