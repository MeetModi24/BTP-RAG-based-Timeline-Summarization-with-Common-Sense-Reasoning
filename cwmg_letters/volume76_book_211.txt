211. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
June 1, 1942
CHI. PRABHA,
Here is a letter from an unhappy woman. I have advised
her to see you. If she comes to you, please guide her. Ask for
the letter which I have given to her. Mridula is coming tomorrow.
Khurshedbehn is here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3576
