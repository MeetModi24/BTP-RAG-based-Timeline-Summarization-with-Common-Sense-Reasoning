507. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
May 8, 1932
CHI. PUSHPA,
You should try and understand the virtues of Mother India,
and then cultivate them in yourself by degrees.
If we think over everything which happens in the course of
even one day, we feel giddy. If, then, we try to recollect every-
thing that happened during a number of years, would we not
become mad?
BAPU
