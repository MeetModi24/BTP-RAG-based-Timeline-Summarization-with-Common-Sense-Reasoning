305. LETTER TO RAM NARESH TRIPATHI
NEW DELHI,
September 3, 1946
BHAI RAM NARESH,
I was sorry to learn of your illness. You must be getting
the Harijan Sevak. I have started a new feature in it. You
may contribute to it whatever you can. You may suggest any
additions or alterations to the terms already included. You
may include as many village expressions as you can. If you can
suggest any idiomatic expressions, well, let me have a whole lot
of them.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAM NARESH TRIPATHI
VASANT NIWAS
SULTANPUR
