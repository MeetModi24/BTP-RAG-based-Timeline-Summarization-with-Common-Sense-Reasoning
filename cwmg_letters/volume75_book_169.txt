169. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
November 19, 1941
CHI. KAKA,
Herewith the draft. Show it to Rajendra Babu and Jamnalalji
also. If absolutely necessary, you may come over here, but it
will be better if you revise the draft and send me the final version
so that I need do nothing more than sign it. I suppose you know
that this afternoon I have no time at all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10954
