456. LETTER TO RENUKA RAY
NEW DELHI,
September 23, 1946
CHI. RENUKA,
Your long letter written after so many years revived old
and pleasant memories. You and your husband seem to have
risen to the occasion during the terrible crisis thro' which Cal-
cutta passed on and after the fateful 16th. Who knows what
is in store for Bengal and the rest of the country in the near
future? We have not gone through the worst yet. My views I
have set forth as accurately as was possible in my article in
Harijan of 15th inst. Read it again and again and follow one
of the two ways described therein, never the third.
Do write again when you feel like it.
Blessings from
BAPU
RENUKA RAY
