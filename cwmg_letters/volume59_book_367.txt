367. LETTER TO N. S. BENGALI
November 13, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I can see no use in your meeting me,
because you seem to hold views wholly different from mine. I
would, therefore, invite you to view the village movement with
sufficient detachment, and then if you find that you have come
to the wrong conclusions, you will naturally write to me, and I
would be glad to take from you such service as you are willing
to render.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI N. S. BENGALI, B.SC., L.T.
KARANDIKAR'S BUNGALOW
DHANTOLI, NAGPUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
