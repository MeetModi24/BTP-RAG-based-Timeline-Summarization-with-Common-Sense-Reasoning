156. LETTER TO LAKSHMIBEHN N. KHARE
February 19, 1932
CHI. LAKSHMIBEHN,
I have again been writing letters to the women, but who
is in charge of the work now? Why don't you write [to me]? The
class which was started ought not to be given up. The women
who are still there should decide to continue it and you should
then let me know. It is you who should take up this burden.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 279-a. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
