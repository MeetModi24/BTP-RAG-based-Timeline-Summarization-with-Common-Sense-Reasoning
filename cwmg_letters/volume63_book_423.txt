423. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
September 30, 1936
CHI. LILA,
There is no end to your lila. Yesterday you wrote, "We shall
talk it out when we meet," and today you write to me that there
is no letter from me. And what a bad hand you have written!
It is all right if you do not go to Rajkot just now. Go there
when we go to Kashi and from Rajkot join us at Ahmedabad when
we leave the place. Isn't that what you want?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11487
