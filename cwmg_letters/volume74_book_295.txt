295. LETTER TO NATVARLAL J. VEPARI
[July 26, 1941]
BHAI NATVARLAL,
I got your letter.
I am writing to Bhai Mahadev. My
own view is that you should draw up the report on the basis
of the material in your possession. But Mahadev has studied
this problem and if he holds a different view I might think
again.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10131
