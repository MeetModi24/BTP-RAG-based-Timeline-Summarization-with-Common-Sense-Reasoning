69. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI PANDYA
July 26, 1930
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
I have your letter. What you did in the case of Manibehn
is quite correct. In the case of a lady whom we have accom-
modated we would have done our duty after having pointed out
her faults dispassionately. In this case too you should do the
same. You should explain to her that your suspicion will not be
removed by [her] undertaking a fast. Why should she undertake
a fast if she has committed no fault? If she has, fasting is no
cure. She should confess it and resolve never to do it again. If
she refuses to be persuaded in spite of all this, let events take
their own course. But tell Manibehn on my behalf that fasting
without my permission is forbidden. She should therefore give
up her fast if she hasn't already done so.
Blessings from
BAPU
