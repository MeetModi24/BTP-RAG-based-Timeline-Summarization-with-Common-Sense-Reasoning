134. LETTER TO G. V. SUBBA RAO
SABARMATI,
October 19, 1921
DEAR FRIEND,
Agriculture is no doubt better than weaving as an occupa-
tion, but it requires a greater outlay of capital expenditure and an
amount of patience, perseverance and outdoor labour, which is not
likely to be suited to a Government servant fresh from his desk-
work, whose means, again, generally speaking may be so limited
as to make it impossible to begin farming even on a modest scale.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 3622
