246. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
January 9, 1937
CHI. MIRA,
I must rest the right hand. No time left here for much
writing. I hope you are now quite well and happy in mind.
There was a basket of fruit lying in Maganwadi when I reached
there. You must have received it. Anyway you will see to it
that you, Balkrishna and others who need fruit have a regular
supply.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Has Kanu taken up spinning?
From the original: C.W. 6372. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9838
1 &2 Ramachandran and Khan Saheb; vide p. 212.
