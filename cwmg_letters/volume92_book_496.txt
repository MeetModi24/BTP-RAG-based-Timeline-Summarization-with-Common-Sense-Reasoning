496. LETTER TO DR. SCARPA
BOMBAY,
January 3, 1932
DEAR DR. SCARPA 3 ,
Just a line, whilst I am yet free to thank you for your
kindness during my all too brief stay in beautiful and historic
Rome. I wish I had two months instead of only two days.
Please tell the Triestano Agent with my thanks that the
Commander and the officers of S. S. Pilsna made me and my
party thoroughly comfortable.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
