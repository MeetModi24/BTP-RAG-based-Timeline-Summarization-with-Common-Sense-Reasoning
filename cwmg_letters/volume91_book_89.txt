89. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI H. PATEL
BHAVNAGAR,
Tuesday [December 7, 1915]
BHAISHRI MAGANBHAI,
You have asked good questions. Krishnasamy cannot thus
ask for fresh rotis, nor send registered letters. He must read
Gujarati. And if he has already decided to leave after one year,
he cannot even stay there. You handle him as best as you can.
Or leave the matter to await my return. He ought to be told to
do grinding.
I do not think it is proper to remind Sundaram about his
studies as long as he is ill.
You have not sent me the rules of the Ashram. I have to
be at Bhavnagar till Thursday. I shall be at Amreli on Friday.
You must have received my other letters.
There is good progress everywhere. The collection at Wank-
aner will be Rs. 1,000. How are Shanti and Bhaiya?
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 11006. Courtesy: Suryakant C.
Patel
