284. LETTER TO JAIJI PETIT
March 9, 1936
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. God has been putting you to a severe test
and you are going through it very well, which also is due to His
grace. There is a verse which we have been reciting here during
the daily prayers. It says, that which goes by the name of adver-
sity is not such; nor is that prosperity which goes by that name.
To forget God is adversity; ever to think of Him is prosperity. It
is good that Hira is with you.
Do write a few lines to me whenever you think it fit. Are
you at all likely to pay a visit to the country?
I am quite well, though perhaps I may not now be able
to put in the amount of work I used to formerly. I, of course,
do need rest. At present we are in Delhi. Ba is with me.
Probably we shall be back in Wardha in a fortnight.
Blessings from
BAPU
