243. LETTER TO BHAGWANDAS
SEVAGRAM,
June 9, 1942
BABUJI,
I was delighted to have your letter. I had also received
your book. I have even glanced through it.
I think what I am doing these days is the thing you
want. Be it as it may, I feel overwhelmed by your great effort.
May God keep you with us for many many years for the sake
of India, for the sake of the world.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
BABUJI DR. BHAGWANDAS
SEVASHRAM
BANARAS
