325. LETTER TO KAMAL KISHORE MEHROTRA
April 2, 1933
CHI. KAMAL KISHORE ALIAS HANUMAN1,
You have introduced yourself well. Remain a Hanuman
even now. Hanuman as a child was very rampageous but on
growing up he displayed his powers of brahmacharya and became a
great devotee of Rama, so much so that when we sing praises of
Rama we remember Hanuman first. You should become like him
and be worthy of the name 'Hanuman'. Let us see what you do.
Blessings from
BAPU
