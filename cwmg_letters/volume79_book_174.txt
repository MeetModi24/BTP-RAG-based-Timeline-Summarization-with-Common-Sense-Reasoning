174. LETTER TO L. KAMESWARARAO SARMA
SEVAGRAM,
February 5, 1945
BHAI KAMESHWAR SHARMA,
I have your letter of January 31, 1945.
My complaint remains. Your letter has, if anything, aggra-
vated it. 1
All your office-bearers are your own men. So must be the
members. This is not the way to unite people.
All your work will be carried on in English. What has this
got to do with nature? You have dug up the very foundations
of nature. Why inflict English on poor Tandonji who is an
advocate of the Rashtrabhasha? If you did not want the
Rashtrabhasha it would have been better if you had chosen one
of the South Indian languages.
Father's letter is full of wisdom. What could Dr. Sapru do?
There is nothing to obstruct a nature-cure practitioner in India.
Yours,
KAMESWARA SARMA
PUDUKKOTTAI
TRICHY
