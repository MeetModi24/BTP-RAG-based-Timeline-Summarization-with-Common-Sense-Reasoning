135. LETTER TO RAMPRASAD VYAS
CALCUTTA,
August 29, 1947
CHI. RAMPRASAD,
I have your letter. I shall be very glad if Jivram gets well.
I understand how the expenditure on food is met at Pilani. The
information is given clearly.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
