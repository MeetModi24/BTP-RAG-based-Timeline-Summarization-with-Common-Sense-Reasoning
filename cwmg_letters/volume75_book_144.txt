144. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 12, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
Your letter. You will get this on the 15th. From tomorrow
I shall write to your Jullundur address. Today there are Kher
Miyan, Ismet, Radhabai, Sultana, etc. All are accommodated in
the new hut. Dr. Das and Nimai have come today, and Kanaiyo also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4257. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N.
7889
