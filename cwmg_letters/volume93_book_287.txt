287. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SEGAON,
July 8, 1938
DEAR LOTUS-BORN,
Hope you have been behaving yourself and that Bhopal air
has done you good. But tell me if you are ever going to be
fit enough to do active work. Don't tell me that you did
excellent work about Hyderabad famine and the like.
LOVE.
PLAYMATE
From the original : Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy : Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
