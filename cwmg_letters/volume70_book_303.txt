303. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 16, 1939
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have sent your article to Harijanbandhu. I have written as
much as I could in Harijan. I have had no time for further
embellishment. What is your suggestion regarding how the money
collected should be used? I understand your difficulty. What
cannot be cured must be endured.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8567. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
