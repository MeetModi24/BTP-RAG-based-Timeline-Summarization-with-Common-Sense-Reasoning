264. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
[About February 14, 1935]
CHI. RAMDAS,
I got your letter. Personally I like your taking your meals in Kevalram's
eating-house. I should like to know what you eat, but I am not particular
about it. All that I wish is that you may eat whatever pleases you and agrees
with you and build up your body.
Of Dhiru, it is as you write. You may certainly go whenever you wish
to. You have kept your bedding at Kalyandas's place, but for sleeping you
surely go to Manibhuvan, don't you? How much distance do you cover in
your walks every day?
If you like to help Kevalram in his work in the eating-house, do so. I
