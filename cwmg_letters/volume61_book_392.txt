392. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
July 21, 1935
BHAI KHAMBHATTA,
Let us now forget the hospital. Just remember that you had
recovered your health there and be grateful to God for that, and
serve every living creature in whatever way you can. My hand
only needed some rest.
Blessings to both of you from
BAPU
