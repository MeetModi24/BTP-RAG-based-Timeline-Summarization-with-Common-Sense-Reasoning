160. LETTER TO KAPILRAI MEHTA
Midnight [April 3, 1930]
CHI. KAPILRAI,
I got the news about you. There is no hurry. Come on
Saturday, but only if you have no temperature. They will send
you back from Navsari. Do not apply the wet-sheet pack any
longer. Do take milk. There is no harm in taking hot-water
bath. As for the cough, consult the doctor. He says that you
should remain in Surat for at least ten days. I also see no harm
in your coming on Saturday if you have no temperature on that
day.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Show this to the doctor.
CHI. KAPILRAI SAINIK
ANAVIL ASHRAM, SURAT
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 1594. Courtesy: Kapilrai Mehta; also
G.N.3972
