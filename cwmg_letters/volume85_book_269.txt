269. LETTER TO RAMPRASAD VYAS
NEW DELHI,
August 30, 1946
CHI. RAMPRASAD,
You have to go and see Jehangirji Patel on the 3rd. I
think you will benefit by going to him. At present he has
called you only for an interview. I should like you to see him
and have a frank talk with him and try to join him if you
find the work good.
Enclosed is a letter from Jehangirji.
Blessings from
BAPU
