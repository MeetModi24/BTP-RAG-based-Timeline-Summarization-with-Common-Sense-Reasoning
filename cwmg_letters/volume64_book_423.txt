423. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
March 4, 1937
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I hope you had a comfortable journey and reached there
safely. Throw off all burden from your mind and get better
quickly. I will not let anybody do any washing in your tub.
At present it is kept in my room and I have reserved it for my
use. If, therefore, I don't need the bigger one, it will remain in
my room. There was a letter from Saraswati addressed to you.
It is enclosed.
Blessings from 1
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 374
