542. LETTER TO HARIBHAU G. PHATAK
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 17, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I hope to be in Poona Wednesday morning and drive straight
to Sinhgarh to see Kaka. I return in the evening and we should
meet at Professor Trivedi's house where I want to go to see his
son. I leave the same night for Bombay.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. HARIBHAU GANESH PHATAK
341, SADASHIV PETH
POONA CITY
From the original: C.W. 2800. Courtesy: Haribhau Phatak
