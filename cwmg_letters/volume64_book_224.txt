224. LETTER TO KASTURBHAI LALBHAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 1, 1937
BHAISHRI KASTURBHAI,
I have your letter and a copy of your Award. Gulzarilal
writes that Motilal Hirabhai Mill has come to an agreement
with the Labour Association. Shouldn't we, therefore, withdraw
its case from the Umpire? Please do so. If you send me the
English translation of my Award, I will go through it. Mean-
while, if I get time, I myself will do the translation and send
it. We will send whichever is ready earlier. If possible I will
send it today, for while I am writing this it occurs to me that
the translation should be sent to him without delay.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4201
