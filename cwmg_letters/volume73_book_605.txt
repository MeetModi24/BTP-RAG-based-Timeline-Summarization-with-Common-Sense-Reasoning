605. LETTER TO VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 7, 1941
BHAI VALLABHRAM,
It is good that you went there. You will gain good experi-
ence. After the tour is over, if necessary, we will think again about
your problem. Jivanlalbhai has not yet asked me anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2916. Courtesy: Vallabhram Vaidya
