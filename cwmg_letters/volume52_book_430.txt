430. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
1932
CHI. RAMA,
I am a little disappointed about you. You know that I long
for letters from everybody. If somebody does not write to me in
order to save my time and because he or she is all right, that is
another matter. But in your case I think the reason is in-
