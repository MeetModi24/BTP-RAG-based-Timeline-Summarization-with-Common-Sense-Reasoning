194. LETTER TO E. C. GIBSON
ANANDKUNJ, RAJKOT,
April 19, 1939
DEAR MR. GIBSON,
Thank you for your prompt reply. My fever is still rising.
Therefore I must not rebel against the good doctor. The fever
went up to 10 1. at half past twelve. I shall therefore hope to
call on you tomorrow at 2 p.m., if that hour is convenient.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: C.W. 10179. Courtesy: D. B. Kalelkar
