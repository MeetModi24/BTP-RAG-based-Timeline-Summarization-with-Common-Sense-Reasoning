219. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
September 20, 1930
CHI. SHARDA (BABU),
You seem to have grown really wise. From now on, so
long as I receive your letters, you will receive my letters too.
So, you have now become a store-keeperess . You must become
very strong in body. Punjabhai is older than I am but he con-
siders me his elder. Even if a nephew is older than his uncle, it
is the custom, is it not, that only the uncle can bless the nephew.
Do you understand me now?
If Anandi is missing me, all of you must keep her in good
cheer.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9890. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
