319. LETTER TO B. M. CHAUNDE
July 4, 1942
It is good you have reminded me. I shall correct the error.
You say that you do not refuse to use hides of dead cattle. You
should have said that you object to hides of slaughtered animals
and use leather got only from dead cattle.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI GORAKSHA SAMSTHA
455 SADASHIV PETH
POONA CITY
