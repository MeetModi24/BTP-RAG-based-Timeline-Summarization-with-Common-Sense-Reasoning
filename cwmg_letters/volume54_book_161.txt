161. LETTER TO SHANTILAL J. MEHTA
March 20, 1933
CHI. SHANTI,
Is it too much to expect a letter from you?
Either you should leave Phoenix just now, or, if you decide
to stay there, you should give up doing odd jobs and also your
desire to make money. Everyone who has tried to serve and to
pursue his personal aims at the same time has failed in both. I
expect better from you.
I hope your wife agrees with you. Encourage her to study.
Do not live extravagantly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
