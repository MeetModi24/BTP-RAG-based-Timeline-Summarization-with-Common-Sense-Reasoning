104. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
May 21, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
By all means stay on there. You are making yourself fully
useful and are getting some peace of mind. Kanchan also gets it.
Return only when you clearly feel that there is no further need
for you to stay on. Ask Kanchan to write to me some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8540
