285. LETTER TO N. S. VARADACHARI
[December 17, 1932]
I have your letter. Whoever is responsible for the so-called
blessings, I am not. Yours is the first intimation that I have of
any swadeshi exhibition in Kumbakonam. I do not and cannot
under the limitations of jail life send messages on anything except
untouchability. You may therefore make whatever use you like
of this letter.
The Hindu, 21-12-1932
