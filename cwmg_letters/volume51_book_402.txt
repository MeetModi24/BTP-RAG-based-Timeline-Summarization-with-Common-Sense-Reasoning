402. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
October 13, 1932
BHAI THAKKAR BAPA,
I got your letter written in English, as also the printed arti-
cle. But I have still not got permission to write even about untouch-
ability. Personally, I am indeed very eager to write. I have also
written to the Government for necessary permission. Let us see
what happens. All of us are happy here. Please take care and see
that your constant travelling does not affect your health.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1103
