412. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
[On or after May 12, 1930]
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
Does your being senior mean that you are counterfeit or ge-
nuine? You have left the Ashram but do not forsake the path
of service. You can write to me. May God bless you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1799
