66. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
September 22, 1934
CHI. SHARMA,
Your letter. . . .1 I do not recollect what you had written about
M. but those days I had little respect for your ability to judge
people. Not that I have much even now. I have no dearth of
instances when you formed too hasty a judgment, but that
does not matter. I read your article on malaria. . . . 2
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha, pp. 106-
