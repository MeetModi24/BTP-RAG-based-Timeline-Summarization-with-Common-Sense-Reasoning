441. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
SEGAON,
September 3, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
I did not even remember anything about Bombay.
You
may now do what you think proper.
Has Amritlal decided anything? I like what you say about
the Smriti. If you can work with the others and select extracts,
do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10908
