575. LETTER TO MARWARI RELIEF SOCIETY, RAMGANJ
CAMP LAMCHAR,
NOAKHALI,
January 11, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
Mr. Hashmar Ullah Khan came to see Gandhiji with a request to expedite
certain payments which are due to him on account of rice supplied to your
relief camp at Chatkhil. His letter in original as well as enclosures are being
sent to you. Gandhiji wishes you to enquire into the matter and say how far
the case is true. After receiving your reply, he will pursue the matter further
if necessary.
Yours sincerely,
NIRMAL KUMAR BOSE
THE OFFICER-IN-CHARGE
MARWARI RELIEF SOCIETY
RAMGANJ CAMP
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
