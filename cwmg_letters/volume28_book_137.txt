137. LETTER TO BISHAN NATH
September 27, 1925
DEAR FRIEND,
You have not told me why you are leaving the board nor why
your interest or faith in khadi is on the wane. I see nothing
wrong in accepting wages for national service faithfully rendered.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
LALA BISHAN NATH
PUNJAB KHADDAR BOARD
PURI
LAHORE
From a photostat: G.N. 7942
