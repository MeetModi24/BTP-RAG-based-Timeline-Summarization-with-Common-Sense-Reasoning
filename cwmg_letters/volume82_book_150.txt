150. LETTER TO R. ACHUTHAN
ON THE WAY TO SEVAGRAM,
November 19, 1945
BHAI ACHUTHAN,
I have your letter. But why is it in English? Why not in
the national language? Your arguments are correct. Remember
that only he who helps himself achieves anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI R. ACHUTHAN
CONSTRUCTIVE SECTION
P. O. BANARAS HINDU UNIVERSITY
