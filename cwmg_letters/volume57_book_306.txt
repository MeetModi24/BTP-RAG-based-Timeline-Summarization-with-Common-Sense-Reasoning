306. LETTER TO GANGAMA
PATNA,
March 20, 1934
DEAR GANGAMA,
Thanks for your Rs. 5 note for Bihar. No apology needed
for what you call a little . . .1 from your heart.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRIMATHY GANGAMA
C/O SHRI B. B. MURNAD
COORG
Gandhiji's papers received from outside Agencies, File No. 1. Courtesy:
National Archives of India
