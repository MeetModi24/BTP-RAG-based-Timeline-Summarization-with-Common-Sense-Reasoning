316. LETTER TO DHIRENDRA NATH CHATTERJEE
SEVAGRAM,
October 30, 1944
CHI. DHIREN,
I like your letter very much. You have got a fine job to do.
Serve all and devote yourself to your work. Write to me from
time to time. Improve your health, use a mosquito-net.
DHIREN CHATTERJEE
SODEPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
