369. LETTER TO DR. SATHAYE
November 13, 1934
DEAR DR. SATHAYE,
I had your telegram. Since my retirement from the Congress
I have refrained from sending special messages. I could not make
any exception. I hope, therefore, that you will forgive me for
not sending you one.
Yours sincerely,
DR. SATHAYE
GIRGAUM
BOMBAY 4
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
