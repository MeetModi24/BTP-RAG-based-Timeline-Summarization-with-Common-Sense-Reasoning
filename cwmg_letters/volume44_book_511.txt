511. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 7, 1930
CHI. SUSHILA,
I knew two or three persons of the name 'Stuart'. One was a
magistrate and afterwards became an officer in the army at the
time of the Zulu rebellion. Another was a lawyer. I had become
fairly intimate with the former. Is it that same Stuart who is
there? Do you also wish to follow Manilal?
Will you carry
with you or leave behind Sita alias Dhairyabala alias whatever other
names you have given her?
How many women work there?
To all of you,
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4778
