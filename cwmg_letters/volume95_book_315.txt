315. LETTER TO D.E. KALELKAR
CHI. KAKA,
1.
How much fuel is consumed in the oven? Has it gone up or
down after the use of the cooker? Do they do all the cooking at one
time or does the kitchen fire burn in the afternoon also? Examine all
this minutely. You have to finish the job while you are at the place.
This can be arranged in a day or two.
2.
How long are Gangabehn and Gomati going to stay? What is
their purpose? What does Gangabehn want? What services can be
taken from them? I could not ascertain all this as I had been
observing my silence. Try to know about it and guide them.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 7701
1
Addressee's son
2
Shriman Narayan Agrawal, son-in-law of Jamnalal Bajaj
