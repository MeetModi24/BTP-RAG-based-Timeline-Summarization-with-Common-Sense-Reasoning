4. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
January 11, 1933
MY DEAR GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your doleful letter of the 7th January, but you are
not going to be disappointed or discouraged. What you describe is
the common lot of most organizations. The best in a man, as also
the worst, is drawn out when he is in charge of such organizations.
The best is drawn out when he works with sufficient detachment.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a copy: C.W. 7919. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
