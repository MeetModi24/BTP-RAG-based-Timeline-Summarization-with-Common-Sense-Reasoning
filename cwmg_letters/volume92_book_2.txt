2. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 19, 1929
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I enclose Shankaran's letter. It does not appear that he
has spoken to you. Can you spare him for a month or two? If
you can spare him, and if you need a substitute, would you like
someone of your choice to be sent from here, or would anyone
I can send do? If Shankaran goes, give him the money he is
asking for. I shall ask Revashankarbhai to reimburse the amount
by draft.
Chhotalal has given me news about the health of all of you.
My visit to Europe is likely to be put off even this year.
Blessings from
BAPU
