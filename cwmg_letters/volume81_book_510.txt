510. LETTER TO DUNCAN GREENLEES
POONA,
September 29, 1945
MY DEAR DUNCAN,
I was delighted to have your letter through Dr. Raju. Of
course I will see him.
There is a ring of despair running
through your note. I do not like it. After all, you are a man
of God and three can be no such thing as a note of despair
for such.
Do come to me as soon as you find me finally settled down.
Just now I am in a sanatorium, as you see. Then, if all goes
well, I go to Madras in December and from there probably
to the Frontier Province. After that I shall settle down in
Sevagram.
Send me your manuscript. I shall see it and if it appeals
to me, I shall get it published.
Love.
BAPU
PROF. DUNCAN GREENLEES
BHIMLIPATAM, VIZAG DISTRICT
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
81-19
