370. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 8, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Do not give up the quern. You can give up talking. Keep
your body and mind engaged in good deeds so that you can
observe brahmacharya in the Ashram. Do not indulge in idle
talk with any man or woman and do not touch anyone without
reason. Consider every woman as your mother or sister.
Blessings from
BAPU
