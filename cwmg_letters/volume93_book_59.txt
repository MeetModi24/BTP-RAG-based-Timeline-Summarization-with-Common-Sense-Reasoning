59. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
WARDHA,
August 25, 1935
DEAR SISTER,
You are passing through considerable ordeals. Only yester-
day I heard that Vikram had been seriously ill. You yourself
have not recovered yet. I see much sickness around. I strongly
feel that nature cure, simple food, pure air, is all that a man
needs. How far can we improve the body which is more deli-
cate than a glass bangle? My blessings to all the children.
There is no need to reply to this letter.
The enclosed letter is for Deenabandhu.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
