478. LETTER TO W. LUTOSTAWSKI
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 25, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
Though I know Mr. Dhangopal
Mukerjee's name by correspondence, I have never had the pleasure
of meeting him personally whether in India or elsewhere. Nor do
I pass my time in contemplation as distinguished from active work.
You have rightly guessed that my active work is my contemplation.
I thank you for your anxiety about my health. But there is
nothing radically wrong with it. I am not ultra strong nor am
I ultra weak.
Do please send your book on Pre-existence and Reincarnation.
Current Thought ceased publication many months ago.
Yours sincerely,
PROF. W. LUTOSTAWSKI
JAGIELLONSKA, 7M. 2
WILNO, POLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 15132
