448. LETTER TO NAWAB OF BHOPAL
(By hand through Chandu Shah)
POONA,
September 16, 1945
DEAR NAWAB SAHEB,
Chandu has just given me your kind note. My sympathies
with you in your loss.
I shall expect to hear from you whenever you think the time
is opportune.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. H. NAWAB OF BHOPAL
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
