360. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
December 23, 1932
I am being visited by a great number of Shastris these days.
Their plight is pitiful. It has become difficult to learn anything
from them. They lack the capacity even to impart what they
possess. And so I see them full of prejudices and hatreds. But
I don't wish to narrate this painful story.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, pp. 367-
