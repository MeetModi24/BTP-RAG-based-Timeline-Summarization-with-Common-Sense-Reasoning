22. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
[On or after March 26, 1929]
BHAI CHHAGANLAL,
I have your letter. I am signing the letter for Sharadabehn
and returning it. We have now got to think about her. I would
not be able to send the letters today. I have not been able to
go through all the letters.
As for Chalala, Narandas's opinion is necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5482
