55. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
May 5, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
Yesterday I sent you a letter. I have now received the reply
regarding the steamer. It is on the back side of this. Isn't it all
right?. . .2 Travelling by cargo boat is in no way uncomfortable.
I travelled from Bombay to Ceylon in a cargo boat. I found it
better, it provided more seclusion.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha, p. 158
