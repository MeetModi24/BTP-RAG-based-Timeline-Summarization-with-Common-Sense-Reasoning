347. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
NEW DELHI,
September 9, 1946
CHI. SHARMA,
M[unshi] is here today. He has sent you a copy of his
award. The stamped one he has sent to Gadodiaji. He has
also made a deposition. If you want I can send you a copy,
but it is not necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 368
