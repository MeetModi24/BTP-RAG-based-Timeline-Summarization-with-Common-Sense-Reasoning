263. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 9, 1936
CHI. KANTI,
How you have hurt me like a thorn! Since I had this news,
your face has been present before my mind's eye, and I have been
brooding constantly. What is this that has happened and why did
you do it? But how could you help it? Who could overcome
his nature? And it is quite proper that you should not curb your
nature, as this would only lead to hypocrisy. It is thus I console
myself. I am considering what I can do, within my limits, to
help you fulfil your ambition.
I shall send you a dhoti if you write to me the length and the
width you want. Or, you may yourself cut it out when you come
here. I have three rolls with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7304. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
