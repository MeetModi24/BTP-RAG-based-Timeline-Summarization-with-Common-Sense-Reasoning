658. LETTER TO KALYAN CHOUDHARI
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
SISTER,
I have your letter.
I have nothing to give.
You should
remain there and serve.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SMT. KALYAN CHOUDHARI
C/O SHRI JAGDISH CHANDRA DUTT CHOUDHARI
MOHALLA MALIGUNJ
LUDHIANA, PUNJAB
