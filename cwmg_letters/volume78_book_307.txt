307. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA BHAVE
October 29, 1944
CHI. BALKRISHNA,
It is raining today. Detain [him]. Shriram may come any
day after the 10th, excepting Monday.
Rameshwardas may accept the agency for ghee, on condition
that there will not be the slightest adulteration and that the ghee
is not sold as ghee from cow and buffalos' milk and is not used
for adulteration of such ghee.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 213
