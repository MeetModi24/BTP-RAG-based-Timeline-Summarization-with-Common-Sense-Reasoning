83. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
April 22, 1942
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have your letter. Maulana's wire would seem to leave
you no choice but to go, though it does not seem to me advis-
able for you to do so. Be firm. If they do not adopt an un-
ambiguous resolution of non-violent non-co-operation, your duty
will be to resign. You must also oppose the scorched-earth
policy and any suggestion to invite foreign troops. They are
pressing me to attend but I have categorically refused. I have
already fixed three or four meetings here at about the same
time. The main meeting was fixed long ago. It cannot be
shifted now.
Drop in here on your way back from Prayag even if it be
only for a day or two. It is a hundred times better here than
in Prayag. Bring along Rajendra Babu, too, and Deo also.
You have given me no reply to my question regarding
Patil,
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 274
