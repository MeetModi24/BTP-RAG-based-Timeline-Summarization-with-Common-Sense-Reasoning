330. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
[August 4, 1941]
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your letter. Here is a letter for you. You will remember
the girl.
Nothing need be done by you for the time being.
If Jamnalal leaves in the middle of the month you will be
without work! !
Yes, you can get a 1st class certificate for packing and gene-
ral neatness. You will have to get [one] for general fitness of
body. Just now the family regard [you] as too delicate to be
handled anyhow.
Ask Shummy how to deal with flies. They are a pest at the
present moment. If the rains keep out long, they will devour
us. I would not like you to take part in the battle unless you
come with some special remedy which admits of no failure.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Veeramma and Cariappa gone to Karnataka.
From the original: C.W. 4043. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7352
