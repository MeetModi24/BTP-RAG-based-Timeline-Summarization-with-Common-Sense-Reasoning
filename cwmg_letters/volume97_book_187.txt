187. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
BORSAD,
May 5, 1931
CHI. RAMDAS,
I got your letter. You may by all means go to Almora. But you will get
three hundred rupees only. Ask Manibhai to give the amount to you and debit
it to my account. Show this letter to him. If, afterwards, you find the amount
not enough, write to me. Do not spend much on warm clothes. There also, it
is not so cold in this season. Moreover, you will get some warm clothing
even there. Some warm thing to cover yourself with will suffice. I may have
to go to Simla next week.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
