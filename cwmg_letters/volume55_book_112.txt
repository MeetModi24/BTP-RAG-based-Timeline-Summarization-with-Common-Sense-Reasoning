112. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
May 4, 1933
CHI. MANUDI,
I don't mind your having left school. Since you survived
a serious illness, you have lost your power of memory. I don't
set much store by your studying. I shall be satisfied if you re-
main good and pure and maintain good health.
Nobody should be upset by my fast.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1521. Courtesy: Manubehn Mashru-
wala
