129. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
July 1, 1932
DEAR MANGALA,
A letter should not be written on a page torn from a note-
said, it was wrong. Thereby, even the widow has been wronged.
One should not tell a lie even to relieve someone from misery.
That surely is no way of ending misery.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9952. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
