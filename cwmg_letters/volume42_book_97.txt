97. LETTER TO DR. H. W. B. MORENO
CAMP KHURJA,
November 3, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I would like you not to worry over our
different points of view. I assure you that I am no less a friend
of the Anglo-Indian cause than you can be. Proof of the pudding
is in the eating. I hope that whenever the occasion arises I shall
not be found wanting but I must act naturally and not artificially.
Yours sincerely,
DR. H. W. B. MORENO
GENERAL PRESIDENT, THE ANGLO-INDIAN LEAGUE
2 WELLESLY SQUARE
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 15633
