148. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 30, 1940
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter.
Do you take enough milk? What work have you taken up?
Just now this place is fairly crowded. Sarladevi Chowdhrani has
come. Lalita Kumari is still here. You know she was in Ram-
garh. There are others also. Krishnakumari has come here for
a few days. Radha too is here. R[aj] K[umari] is still in Simla.
It is a little cooler now. I am keeping excellent health. Ba's
is fairly good. A[mtul] S[alaam] is rather unwell.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3542
