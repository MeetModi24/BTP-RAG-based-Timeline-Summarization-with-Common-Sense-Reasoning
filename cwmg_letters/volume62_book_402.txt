402. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
April 28, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
I shall bring the necessary things with me. I cannot come
tomorrow. The W. C. is still sitting and I cannot leave so long
as the members are here. In no case shall I have uninterrup-
ted 10 days there. I must be here on Sunday and also for 1st
and 2nd May, if Dr. Ambedkar comes. Then I have to be
absent from 5th or 6th. I expect to leave for Bangalore on 8th.
Therefore there will be no returning after I leave on 5th or 6th.
So the ten days will be reduced to 30th, 31st and 4th and 5th
May, the latter only possibly. I shall be thankful even if I can
be there even to that extent. Ba is still not well.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6330. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9796
