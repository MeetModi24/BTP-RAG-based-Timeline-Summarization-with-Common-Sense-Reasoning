151. LETTER TO MARGARET JONES
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 2, 1941
MY DEAR KAMALA,
Having heard about your accident I wrote to you at your
hospital, but I see you are at your old place. Yes, come when
you like and you will stay as long as you can.
You will have
all the help you may need.
Love.
BAPU
Bapu—Conversations and Correspondence with Mahatma Gandhi, p. 194
