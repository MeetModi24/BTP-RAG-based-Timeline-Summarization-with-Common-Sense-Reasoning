359. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 17, 1941
CHI. SHARMA,
I got both your letters. You are invariably forgiven. If you
insist I will allow you to go. You had better be patient since
you are already engaged in work there. But still if you are
restless, I am prepared to send you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
p. 295
