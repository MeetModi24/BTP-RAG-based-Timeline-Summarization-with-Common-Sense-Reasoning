543. LETTER TO GULAM RASUL QURESHI
July 31, 1932
CHI. QURESHI,
Write to me in detail. I believe you remember the books I want.
It seems Premabehn has sent Khulfa-e-Rashidin Sarat-Part II,
the biography by Dr. Mohammed Ali, and other books.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10807. Courtesy: Gulam Rasul
Qureshi
