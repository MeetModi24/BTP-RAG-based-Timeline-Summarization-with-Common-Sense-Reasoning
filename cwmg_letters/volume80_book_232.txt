232. LETTER TO SAILENDRA NATH CHATTERJEE
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 14, 1945
CHI. SAILEN,
I have your letter.
I hope you have a place to stay in
Bombay. Go to Bombay. There is a Nature Cure Clinic in
Malad. It is owned by Dr. Krishnavarma. He will admit
you. I am writing to him. Follow his instructions. You will
be cured. You must also learn everything. Give whatever help
you can, if your health permits. Keep me informed. Leave
Wardha only with Rishabhdas's permission. Show this postcard
to Dr. Krishnavarma.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. SAILEN CHATTERJEE
INSURANCE COMPANY
BAJAJWADI
WARDHA
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 10391a. Courtesy: Amrita Lal
Chatterjee
