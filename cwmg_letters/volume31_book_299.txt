299. LETTER TO RAMESHWAR
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Saturday, Asadha Krishnapaksha 14 [August 7, 1926]
BHAI RAMESHWARJI,
I have your letter. I did not decide on my own to teach the
Bible to the boys. They themselves wanted it. Those who claim
to follow the sanatana dharma should not be ignorant of other
religions. By studying other faiths, we only serve the cause of
religion. We should not be afraid of such study.
As far as possible, you should avoid going to court. If you
have no charkha, then use a takli.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 165
