286. LETTER TO R. D. PRABHU
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
September 8, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. If bath conciliates the Inamdar, you will
take it, not because you believe in the necessity of it but for
the sake of the Inamdar, and therefore for the sake of the untouch-
able brethren whom you want to serve.
If the Mahratta boys leave the school because the Mahar
boys are also learning there, you will not mind the former
leaving the school, but you will persist in teaching the Mahar
boys at any cost.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. D. PRABHU
VINZANE, P.O. HALKARNI, MAHAL CHANDGAD, DT. BELGAUM
