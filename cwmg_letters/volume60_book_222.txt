222. LETTER TO M. SUCOSE
February 5, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is not possible for me to send you
Rs. 30 per month. I have no such funds to fall back upon. You
have to make what effort you can there, or write to the Central
office sending all your qualifications.
Yours sincerely,
M. SUCOSE, ESQ.
SAMADANAPURAM
PALAMCOTTAH P.O., TINNEVELLEY DT.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
