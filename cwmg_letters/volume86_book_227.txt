227. LETTER TO SHRIKRISHNADAS JAJU
December 2, 1946
BHAI JAJUJI,
If Prakasam remains firm about the mill at Madras no one
will or can compel him. It would be a good thing if all the
provinces followed an identical policy but probably it will not
be possible. Prakasam had mentioned absolutely nothing about
the difficulty. Of course, I am going to do all that is possible.
But there is certainly some difficulty in acting from here.
How is your health?
1 Umiya's son
2 Addressee's sister
Let me see what I can do about the Charkha Sangh in
Bengal.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
What is being done in Madras about the Charkha Scheme?
