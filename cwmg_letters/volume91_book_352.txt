352. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Maha Sud 9 [February 2, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Please read the enclosed letter carefully and watch out for
the steamer mentioned in it. Meet the person whose name has
been given in the letter. You have only to tell him that his
mother has written to me. If he intends to see me, he may
come to the Ashram. Buy his ticket if he has no money. It
is possible that being a sailor he may not be relieved. In this
regard you have to accept whatever happens. Maybe it is neces-
sary for him to obtain permission for living in India. Please see
to all this. You can consult me if you want to ask anything
more. I shall reach the Ashram on the 10th. I am very glad to
read that Mother is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
We are going to Rawalpindi today. Preserve this letter and
send it back to me.
