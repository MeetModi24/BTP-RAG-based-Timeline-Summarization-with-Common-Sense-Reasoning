23. LETTER TO A. S. DAVID
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 19, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I understand what you mean. But I
must confess that I still less like your latest letter. But I don't
propose to argue. I repeat my advice that it will be better for
you to come here and see things for yourself before you take any
further step whatsoever.
Yours sincerely,
A. S. DAVID, ESQ.
71, DILKUSHA
LUCKNOW
From a photostat: S.N. 10944
