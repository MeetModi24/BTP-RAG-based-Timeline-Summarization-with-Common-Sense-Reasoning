676. LETTER TO FENNER BROCKWAY
DEAR FRIEND,
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 12, 1926
I have your cable. I am so sorry you had to go to the ex-
pense of cabling me. Newspaper correspondents will publish
things without verification. There was a talk about my going to
Finland. But it was announced in the newspapers that everything
was arranged, whereas, nothing was certain. The final decision
come to was that I should not go to Finland. Had I gone, I
would certainly have accepted your invitation. As it was, I had
to send you the following cablegram which I hope you duly
received.
"Thanks, not going Europe."
Yours sincerely,
FENNER BROCKWAY, ESQ.
INDEPENDENT LABOUR PARTY
13, GREAT GEORGE STREET
LONDON S.W. 1
From a photostat: S.N. 11361
