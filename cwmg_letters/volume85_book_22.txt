22. LETTER TO RAMA SHETTY
PANCHGANI,
July 20, 1946
SIR,
I got your wire. You did well in sending the wire. I have
written to Kalyanidevi. I remember the Doctor very well. I had
stayed at his house when I went to Trichy the first time.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RAMA SHETTY
PRESIDENT, TOWN CONGRESS
TRICHINOPOLY
