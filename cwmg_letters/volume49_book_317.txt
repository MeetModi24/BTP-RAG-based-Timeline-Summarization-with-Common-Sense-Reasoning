317. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
April 2, 1932
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
There is no particular trouble in my hand. I am not using
the right hand for writing merely as a measure of precaution. You
must not let your weight increase. Fast for a few days or live
only on fruit. All this weight is superfluous. It may help you
even if you live for some time on buttermilk.
Blessings from
BAPU
