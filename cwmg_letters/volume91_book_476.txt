476. LETTER TO S. NAGASUNDARAM
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 15, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You may make what use you like of my
previous letter to you.
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. S. NAGASUNDARAM
LAKSHMINIVAS BUILDING
MATUNGA
From the original: S. Nagasundaram Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
