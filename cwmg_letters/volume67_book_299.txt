299. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
August 1, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
I am in a very great hurry. It is good that you sent the
names of the medicines. Observe the rule about writing on a
fixed day in the week.
Hanna is leaving tomorrow. Khan Saheb has left. Kishore-
bhai and Manu are coming tomorrow. And so the number will
remain the same.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7092. Also C.W. 4584. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
