40. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
October 13, 1935
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I got the letters of you both. You may arrange for Sita's
education as you like best.
We are considering a proposal for Manu's betrothal with
Surendra. It was Kishorelal's wish.
Devdas is still in Simla. Pyarelal is with him. He seems to
be improving. But he still needs very careful nursing. There is
no cause for worry.
Ramdas is battling with fate in Bombay. He enjoys the game.
I am therefore content.
I have already written to you that Ba has returned here.
Mirabehn has recovered completely. In a few days' time she
will go to a village to live there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4845
