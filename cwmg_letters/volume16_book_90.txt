90. LETTER TO CHHOTALAL TEJPAL
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday [September 17, 1919]
BHAISHRI CHHOTALAL,
I have your letter. If you send me a factual statement which
will take up one column in Navajivan, I will publish it. What
you have sent is too long. I have no time at present to shorten it.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
CHHOTALAL TEJPAL
ARTIST
RAJKOT
From a photostat of the postcard in Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: S. N. 2588
