258. LETTER TO SYED MAHMUD
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 9, 1940
DEAR DR. MAHMUD,
Thank you for your long letter which I shared with Bapu. We both
had a few minutes' relaxation because of it. Such relaxation is a welcome
thing when one is hard pressed for time as one is these days.
Bapu said, he was glad to know that you had been able to write such a
long letter in your own hand; it shows your health must be better. I said,
the handwriting was not yours and the letter was dictated.
We had a dis-
pute! You may enlighten me as to which is right!
Kindest regards and hoping your health will gradually improve,
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
From the original: Dr. Syed Mahmud Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memo-
