519. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
SEVAGRAM,
December 1, 1944
CHI. SHRIMAN,
Your letter is very frank and good. We will all discuss it
after my [work] fast is over. I quite understand the importance of
your college work. Organization of the students and the responsi-
bility of the Mahila Ashram work will take up all your time.
Hence I shall try to relieve you, as much as possible, from the work
of the Hindustani Prachar [Sabha]. I will see what can be done.
You must keep yourself in good health. I hope you will not
neglect your duty to preserve your health for the sake of service.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 302
