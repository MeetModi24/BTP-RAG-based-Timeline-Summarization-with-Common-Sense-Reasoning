454. LETTER TO FIROZA P. S. TALEYARKHAN
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 7, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to receive your letter. I came down here
on Sunday. Do please come any day you like except Mondays
when I am silent. Four o'clock is the best time for me. I hope
you are feeling better.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
FIROZA P. S. TALEYARKHAN
3, RESIDENCY ROAD
BANGALORE
From the original: C.W. 9170
