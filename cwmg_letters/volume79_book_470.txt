470. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
March 20, 1945
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. What a narrow escape you have had!
I hope Lavanyalata is doing well. Kanchan's postcard to Munna-
lal came today. I see from it that she is very ill. Why? I am
asking Munnalal to go there. Let us see what happens. I am
all right. I am working regularly. I shall have to go to Bombay
towards the end of this month. I am not writing separately to
Kanchan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 493
