526. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Bhadrapad Vad 3, Friday [September 24, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. The new place should certainly suit you
better. Is there some extra accommodation?
I have already
written to Lady Pattani that we shall keep the bungalow up to
February. By the end of February you will have become fit
enough to go to Almora, etc., and so the change may even be
agreeable to you. But we can think about all that in February.
Find out from the agent the rent of Pattani Saheb's bungalow.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
PANCHGANI CASTLE
PANCHGANI
