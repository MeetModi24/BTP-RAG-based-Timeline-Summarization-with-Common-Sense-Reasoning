204. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
February 25, 1946
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have started the treatment of Chi. Kishorelal yesterday.
Let us see what happens.
You have to do your duty by meditating over navadha bhakti .
Scavenging and kitchen work are the key to our life. If these
two are done, the rest follow.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen, p. 374
