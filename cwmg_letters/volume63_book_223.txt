223. LETTER TO LAKSHMI NARAYAN GADODIA
July 28, 1936
BHAI LAKSHMI NARAYAN,
I am happy to see the signatures of you both. My hip-
baths still continue. There is no need of steam-baths as I pers-
pire enough as it is. I am not using the mud-pack at night.
I am not used to a sitz-bath. I shall now see about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5625
