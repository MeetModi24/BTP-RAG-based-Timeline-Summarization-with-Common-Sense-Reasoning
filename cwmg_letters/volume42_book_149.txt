149. LETTER TO RAMNIKLAL MODI
HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
CHI. RAMNIKLAL,
I have not received any mail from you today. A letter on the
Bank is enclosed with this, which you may use if necessary.
Ba and Devdas have gone to Shahabad today to attend a
women's meeting. Others have gone to Etah ahead of me.
Maintain your health in excellent condition.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Chhaganlal has separated from the party today and left for
the Punjab.
