199. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
March 5, 1932
CHI. NIMU,
Having been trained in the Ashram, why need you feel false embarrassment?
If you take the lead, other women also will go out for walks. Who else, if not
a mother, will go out for a walk carrying her child in her arms? Do you not
have any friend in Lakhtar? Induce her to go out with you. You, mother and
daughter, should go out. Take Navin along. We should effect some carefully
thought out reforms in such matters.
Blessings from
BAPU
