268. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
ASHRAM,
April 3, 1926
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
You have searched your heart pretty well. You can
devote yourself to the charkha; this can be a great social service.
You will attain contentment, I am sure, if you have this faith,
and ultimately you will also see the good results. But perhaps
you will set a limit to your patience. It is no patience which has
an end. May you succeed in your resolve.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DAHYABHAI MANORDAS PATEL
DHOLKA
