281. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
June 30, 1933
CHI. PREMA,
Why is there no letter from you? How is your health? And
the condition of your mind? How is your throat?
What is the news about Sushila?
Dhurandhar had come again to see me.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 6785. Courtesy: Premabehn Kantak
