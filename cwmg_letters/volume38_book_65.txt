65. LETTER TO EVELYN C. GEDGE
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 15, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I wish I could do something for the friend
you mention. But it is difficult for me to suit him. All the work
we have at the Ashram is principally physical labour such as
spinning, weaving, farming, dairying and payments we make are
also very small.
Yours sincerely,
MISS EVELYN C. GEDGE
UNIVERSITY SETTLEMENT
VACCHAGANDHI ROAD, P.O. 7, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 12999
