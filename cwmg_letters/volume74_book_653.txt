653. LETTER TO HARIKRISHNA BHANJI
October 10, 1941
BHAI H. B.,
Received your letter. Do come and meet me in December.
Meanwhile you should start taking interest in constructive work.
You should acquire skill in spinning and weaving and other
processes of the yarn. You should serve the Harijans. You
should serve the Muslims, Christians, Parsis. If you do all these
things, then only will you become a true worker and then only
will you become fit for civil disobedience. In fact you need not
come to me to learn all this.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
