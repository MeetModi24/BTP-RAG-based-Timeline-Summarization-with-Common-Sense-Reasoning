87. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
Silence Day [January-February, 1932]
CHI. JANAKIBEHN,
How are you? Have you kept up your courage? How is
Madalasa? Do not worry about Kamalnayan. Haven't you learnt
from Vinoba's Talks on the Gita that one should not worry about
anything?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 83
