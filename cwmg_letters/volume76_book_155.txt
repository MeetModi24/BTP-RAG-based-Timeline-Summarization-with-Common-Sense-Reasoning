155. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHASTRI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 21, 1942
BHAI HIRALAL SHASTRI,
Your lamenting is needless. Let us be vigilant. Everyone
makes mistakes. Sohanlalji is well. I expect.
Blessings from
BAPU
