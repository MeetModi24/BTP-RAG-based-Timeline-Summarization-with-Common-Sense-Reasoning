142. LETTER TO ESTHER MENON
June 30, 1934
MY DEAR CHILD,
Though I often get up at 2 a.m. I do not overtake my
correspondence. There is just time to send you a card of love on
your birthday. I know I owe the box spinning-wheel to the
children. My love and kisses to them. Mira felt the impulse
all of a sudden and went.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI ESTHER MENON
BOURNE END
KODAIKANAL
S. INDIA
From a photostat: No. 129. Courtesy: National Archives of India. Also
My Dear Child, p. 106
