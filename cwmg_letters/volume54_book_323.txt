323. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
April 2, 1933
BHAI HARIBHAU,
Indefinite fasts certainly have their place in the circum-
stances which you have described. But they require the utmost
fitness on the part of those who fast. It would be enough even
if we get only one thousand such persons. What God has or-
dained will happen.
What you write about Gaurishanker is correct. Keep me
informed about yourself. You claim to have gained experience
only after you have fully recovered. What does Modi say?
I
have not heard from him as yet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 6077. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
