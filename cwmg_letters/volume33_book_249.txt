249. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA
NANDI HILLS, MYSORE,
Chaitra Purnima [April 16, 1927]
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA,
I have the letter written by all of you, sisters. I am steadi-
ly improving. I often think of you all. I on my part tried hard
to bring you all to the Ashram. You may go if you still wish to.
You can all live where you like, but spin a lot, study a lot and
move about a lot. The body and the mind ought to be fit. Write
to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA DESAI
C/O SUNDERJI GOVINDJI DESAI
IN THE OLD HOUSE OF RATILAL MANIAR
RAJKOT
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 4906. Courtesy: Hari-ichchhabehn
Kamdar
