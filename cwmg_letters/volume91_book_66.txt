66. LETTER TO T. D. PATHER
JOHANNESBURG,
May 25, 1910
DEAR MR. PATHER,
I have your letter giving me a full explanation of the two
cases referred to in your letter. I am glad that you are not
concerned in any injustice being done to our countrymen. Indeed,
interpreters should be a source of comfort and help to Indians
who come in touch with them. I am glad you have been
helping Mr. Royeppen and others.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MR. T. D. PATHER
P.O. BOX 1256
DURBAN, NATAL
From a photostat: G.N. 777
1 Addressee's brother
2 Joseph Royeppen
