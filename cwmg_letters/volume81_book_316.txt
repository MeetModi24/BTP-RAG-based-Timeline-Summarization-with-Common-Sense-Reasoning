316. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
POONA,
August 30, 1945
CHI. LILI,
I do not remember having answered your letter. Just now,
after the prayer, I have taken out the old letters. This is just
to tell you that I think of you. Continue your studies and pass.
Do not lose courage. Do not spoil your health. There is no
time to write more. Sardar's treatment is going on. I am well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10206. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
