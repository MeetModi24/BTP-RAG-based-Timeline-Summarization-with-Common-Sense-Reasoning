132. LETTER TO NIRMALABEHN GANDHI
September 19, 1932
Please do not get agitated in the slightest degree. I have
entrusted a brave and good man like Ramdas to your charge.
Why, then, should you be agitated? How long would you keep
me? And if you would I am always with you all. The body is
but inert matter. What will you do with it? I spent two hours
with Ramdas on Friday. He did not show the slightest agitation.
I felt proud of him as both his father and teacher. You also
should be like him. Look after the children properly. Keep up
ghee and milk in your diet.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I I , p. 51
