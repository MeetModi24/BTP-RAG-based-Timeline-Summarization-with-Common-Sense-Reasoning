214. LETTER TO YUSUF MEHERALLY
September 29, 1937
BHAI MEHERALLY,
It is only right that the Shop Assistants' Conference should
be founded by Seth Jamnalalji who has turned many a shop-
assistant into masters of firms. There is no doubt that they have
to work long hours. All they should remember is that they
would not attain their objectives by following the war-path but
by remaining calm, truthful and firm.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
