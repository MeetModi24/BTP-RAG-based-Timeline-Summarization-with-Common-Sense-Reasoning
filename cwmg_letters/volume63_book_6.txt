6. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
June 1, 1936
CHI. NARAHARI,
I hope you have all returned, and in much better health.
How did Manibehn fare at the place? Is she now quite well?
Did Vanamala and Mohan gain in health? You used to take
long walks, didn't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9094
