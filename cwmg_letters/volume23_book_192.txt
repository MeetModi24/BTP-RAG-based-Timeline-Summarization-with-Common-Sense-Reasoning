192. LETTER TO M. REINER
POST ANDHERI,
March 21, 1924
DEAR MR. REINER,
I have your letter of the 20th instant.
I shall be pleased to see you on the 26th instant at 5 p.m.
Yours sincerely,
M. REINER, ESQ.
ROOM 23, GRAND HOTEL
BALLARD ESTATE
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 8551; also C.W. 5133
