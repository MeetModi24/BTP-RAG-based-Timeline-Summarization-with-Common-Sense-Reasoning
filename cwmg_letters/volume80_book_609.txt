609. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
ON A MOVING TRAIN,
June 23, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have before me your letter of June 11, 1945. Your inter-
pretation of the rules, a copy of which you have sent, seems
correct to me. The office should shift to Wardha or some-
where around it.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
From the Hindi original: Kasturba Gandhi National Memorial Trust
Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
