133. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
CALCUTTA,
August 29, 1947
CHI. AMRIT,
Your note even when you were tired.
You must not lose faith in humanity. Humanity is an ocean.
If a few drops are dirty, the ocean does not become dirty. And
do not let your body suffer. You have to take much work from it.
I am glad Sushila is keeping well. Of course her place was
in the Wah Camp so long as the men needed her services.
The work here goes on. I cannot yet go to Noakhali. They
want me there, as also in Bihar and Sylhet. Now J. wires I
should be in Delhi.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 3709. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 6518
