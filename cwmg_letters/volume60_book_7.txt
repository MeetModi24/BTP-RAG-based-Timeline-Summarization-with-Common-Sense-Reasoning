7. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
December 17, 1934
BHAI THAKKAR BAPA,
I got your letter.
In my wire I only asked you to send
Rs. 5,000 for research. Didn't I say in it that the budget itself
would be passed in Delhi?
Nrisinhaprasad's example must be said to be an excellent one.
How many sanatanists like him did you come across in Kathiawar?
I have no time to write about other things.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1149
