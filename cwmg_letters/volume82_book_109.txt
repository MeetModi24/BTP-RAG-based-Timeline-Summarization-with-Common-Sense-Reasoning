109. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
POONA,
November 13, 1945
CHI. BABU,
I have your letter. You have not said what your "one
last" attempt was. See that it does not become too late for
improvement.
I know that Chokhawala is busy. I do not expect from
him the courtesy of a letter, because it is wrong to cherish any
hope at all.
Why does Shakaribehn get migraine?
Blessings to all of you from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10063. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokha-
wala
