304. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
[On or after March 5, 1940]
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I have Manilal's letter. Herewith a letter to Mrs. Fagson.
I am glad I met her. She is a very good woman. How is West
doing? How is Sorabji? Give me all the news from there. Things
are going on all right here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4873
