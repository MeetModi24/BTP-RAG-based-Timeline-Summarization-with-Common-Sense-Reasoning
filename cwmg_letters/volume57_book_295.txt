295. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
March 15, 1934
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
I got your letter.
You have done well in buying a clipper
and scissors. One must become one's own barber.
Galiharilal will not come.
I understand about Manibehn. Forget her if you can. . . .1
Blessings from
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 367. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
