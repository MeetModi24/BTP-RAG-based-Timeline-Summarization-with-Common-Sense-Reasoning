125. LETTER TO G. FINDLAY SHIRRAS
BORSAD,
July 4, 1931
DEAR PRINCIPAL SHIRRAS,
I thank you for your further letter of 3rd instant. It was good
of you to have taken in Syt. C. H. Desai and I would like you
to extend the same liberal treatment to the other students.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G. N. 824
