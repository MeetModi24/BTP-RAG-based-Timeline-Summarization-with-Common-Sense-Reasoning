429. LETTER TO P. PANDURANGA SHENAI
April 11, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I hope when I get the time to deal with one portion of your
letter in the pages of the Harijan without in any way mention-
ing your name.
As to the last question in your letter, the Brahmin who dis-
coursed upon untouchability was in my opinion utterly wrong.
Untouchability in the case of womenfolk is only in respect of a
temporary condition and if there was untouchability observed
about people in similar cases, there would be nothing to be said.
But here it is a permanent untouchability with heartless conduct
attached to it. Between the two conditions there is no resem-
blance.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. PANDURANGA SHENAI
20 ANDIAPPA GRAMANI ST.
ROYAPURAM, MADRAS
