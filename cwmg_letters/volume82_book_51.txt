51. LETTER TO R. R. DIWAKAR
November 6, 1945
BHAI DIWAKAR,
You did come but you did not ask for time to see me. I
had thought you would come and have a talk with me. That is
why I did not write anything on what you had sent.
What you say about income tax will not work. The rest
seems to be all right. I am sending a copy of what I have
drafted. It will give you some idea of what I think. If you
wish to say something about it, do so. I shall leave here on the
19th morning.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
