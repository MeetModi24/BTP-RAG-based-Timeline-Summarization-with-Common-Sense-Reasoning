107. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
June 18, 1934
CHI. VIDYA,
I hope you are keeping your promise. You should never
cry but should always be smiling, should work hard and not
worry about marrying. You should tell yourself that you will
marry when God sends a suitable young man. One who is al-
ways absorbed in work is never troubled by other thoughts.
Write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9586. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
