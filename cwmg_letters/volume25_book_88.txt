88. LETTER TO INDRA VIDYAVACHASPATI
SABARMATI,
Bhadarva Sud 13 [September 11, 1924]
CHI. INDRA,
I have your letter. I wish to have full details about Bhopal.
If I get a list of the atrocities, I shall immediately do whatever
is possible.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
Received Rai Sahib's letter. I am awaiting Maulana Sahib's
letter or telegram.
PROF. INDRA
"ARJUNA" OFFICE
DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 4861. Courtesy: Chandragupta Vidya-
lankar
