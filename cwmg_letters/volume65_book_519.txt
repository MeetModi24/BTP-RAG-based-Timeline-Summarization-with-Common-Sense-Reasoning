519. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
July 27, 1937
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your kind letter.
I had for some time intended to ask for an appointment
to discuss the possibility of lifting the ban on Khan Saheb Abdul
Ghaffar Khan's entry into the Frontier Province and of my
visiting that Province. Of course there is no bar against me
but I had no intention of going there except with the approval
of the authorities.
Your letter is therefore doubly welcome. I assume that
there would be no objection to my discussing these two points
at our meeting. I shall gladly report myself at Viceroy's House,
New Delhi, on 4th August next at 11.30 a.m.
I remain,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
