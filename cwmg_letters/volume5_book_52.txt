52. LETTER TO HAJI HABIB
[JOHANNESBURG,]
August 9, 1905
DEAR SHETH SHRI HAJI HABIB,
I have received your letter regarding Karodia. I have sent the notice.
Salaams from
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
I went to Pretoria last night on business. Since I returned by the
7.30 train in the morning, I could not see you, for which please excuse
me. I have sent word through Mr. Kallenbach.
From the Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: Letter Book (1905): No. 907
1 Relates to war losses.
2 Secretary, British Indian Committee, Pretoria
3 Herman Kallenbach, a wealthy German architect introduced to Gandhiji by Mr. Khan
who had discovered in him a vein of other-worldliness; became a friend of Gandhiji and an
associate in his experiments in simple living; courted arrest during the Passive Resistance
Movement in South Africa. Vide Satyagraha in South Africa, Chapters XXIII, XXXIII to XXXV.
