143. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
December 11, 1936
CHI. BRAJKISAN,
Mother must have recovered by now. Why do you wish to
go to the Congress? I shall probably reach there on the 20th.
At present I have no idea where I shall be lodged or how I
shall go. My idea is to walk from the station. If I have room
enough and if you have to come you can certainly stay with me.
But if it is not really necessary for you to come, you should re-
main there and do some work of service.
Blessings from
BAPU
