331. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
May 23, 1941
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I got your letter. According to me, Kashi is not well enough to go to
jail. I will be satisfied if she goes on helping you and Chhaganlal in your
work without harming her health. I would also be unhappy if Chhaganlal
cannot stay at Vijapur. However, if he does not at all feel comfortable there,
he should join you and help you in your work as much as he can. Kashi,
too, should remain there. When she is stronger and feels that it is her dharma
to go to jail, she may do so. There is no need at all for her to do that as
early as possible. If Chhaganlal and Kashi wish to stay here with Krishnadas,
they may do so. All of you may think over this. Durgabehn is all right. She
has gone to Bombay. Ba will arrive here tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
