172. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Sunday, Chaitra Sud 8 [March 21,1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
A letter from you after many days, which I was waiting for
all the time. All my time is now taken up, so I have little to write,
which has made me lax. But I get a little anxious if I do not have
any letter either from you or from Devdas. It is now clear that
your health will improve only gradually. You should take rest
there and not worry. It has been decided that I go to Mussoorie
in April. There will be room for a few people there. Won't you come
over if you are permitted? That might be better. Boarding faci-
lities will, of course, be there.
