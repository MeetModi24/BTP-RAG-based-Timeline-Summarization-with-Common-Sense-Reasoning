67. LETTER TO DWARKANATH
[June] 5, 1934
CHI. DWARKANATH,
The rains have set in here making it impossible to continue
the tour on foot. It has therefore been decided to spend three
days in Bhadrak before going over to your place. We hope to
leave Bhadrak on Friday and reach there on Saturday. We shall
leave from there on Wednesday.
The party would comprise
about ten people. I am not writing separately to Jamnalalji and
Ramdas. Please tell Vidya I have her letter; it is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3087
