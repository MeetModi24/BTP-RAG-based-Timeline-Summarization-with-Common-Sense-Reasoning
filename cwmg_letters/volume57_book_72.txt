72. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
January 30, 1934
DEAR DAUGHTER RAIHANA,
I have your letter through the sisters Schill and Boari. Of
course you are absolved from the promise. What I said was the
general experience of mankind. There was no question whatso-
ever of your honesty being doubted. But I understand what is
weighing on you. That weight must be lifted. I know that you
will grow in any event.
I wish I could join your prayer meeting. You are doing
better than what I have been able to achieve.
It is quite good news you give me about Gopi's diary.
Father tells me about some yogi treating you with good
results and a promise of better. Who is he?
Tell Father I have his long letter. I cannot help him in
supplementing his recollections of our first meeting. I have but a
faint recollection of meetings prior to the meetings in Godhra.
I have a fair recollection of our meetings since then. And the
brief meeting at Baroda station when you and Hamida were sit-
ting in my lap I shall never forget. It was all as if I had met
members of the same family!
Love to you all. I take it you
have been collecting for Bihar.
BAPU
From a photostat: S.N. 9654
