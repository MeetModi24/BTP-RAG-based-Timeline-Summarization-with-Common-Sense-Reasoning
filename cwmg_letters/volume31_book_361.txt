361. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Friday, Sravana Krishna 4, August 27, 1926
BHAI TULSI MAHER,
I feel happy whenever I get a letter from you. You are do-
ing very good work. Send me a few pieces by way of samples
from the varieties of cloth being woven there. What does a
charkha cost there? From where do you get the wood? Is the
spindle made locally? If yes, what does it cost? How many
charkhas are plying? What is the rate of payment for spinning?
1 Vide "Letter to R. A. Adams", 18-8-1926.
What is the count of the yarn? If you have not introduced the
practice of testing the strength of the yarn, you should do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
