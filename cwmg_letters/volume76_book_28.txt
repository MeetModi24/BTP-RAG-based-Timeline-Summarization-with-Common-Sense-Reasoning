28. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEGAON,
April 11, 1942
CHI. MIRA,
I wrote to you yesterday. You will, I know, do the best you
can for the camp. As to other work I have nothing in view just
now and may not have any at any time. But no one knows what
will happen the next moment.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: C.W. 6495. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9890
