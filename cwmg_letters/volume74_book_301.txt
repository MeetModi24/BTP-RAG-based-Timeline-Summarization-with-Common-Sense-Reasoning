301. LETTER TO SIR ROBERT E. HOLLAND
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
July 28, 1941
DEAR FRIEND,
Apologies for the delayed reply.
I have not asked for suspension of respective activities. I
have only asked for eschewing goondaism for enforcing claims.
Thus the Muslim League may pursue their claim to Pakistan and
the antis their objection so long as each party does so non-violently.
For the time being I have not aimed at agreement on issues.
I have aimed at agreement on non-violence, i.e., calm discussion.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 10452. Courtesy: Sir Robert E. Holland
