359. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
March 4, 1935
It will not be necessary for you to attend any function that
may be arranged in connection with the Silver Jubilee celebrations.
If it is clear, however, that your not attending will harm the
Corporation, you ought to go. The same rule applies to social
functions. You should remember that we are not practising non-
co-operation these days. That part of the programme has been
suspended. A municipal councillor, therefore, may serve the city
in whatever manner he can consistently with his freedom, self-respect
and popularity. Having said this, I need not give illustrations.
If you want, however, I shall think up some. When you feel that
you are not in a position to serve but on the contrary are
likely to endanger your self-respect, it will be your duty to resign
and give reasons for your action.
Do whatever you can in the fields of Harijan work, prohibi-
tion, khadi, village industries, etc. The Government it is true has
not left much scope for such work. But we also are a good deal to
blame for that.
As you see, we are able to do nothing about
Hindu-Muslim unity.
However, this fact is not a reason for
yielding to despair but for intensifying our efforts.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 157
