193. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[May 12, 1933]
CHI. MIRA,
You will be brave to the end. No joke to be my daughter.
Being there you have to pass through a more searching ordeal than
[I.] But then my children to be worthy have to do better than
I. Have they not? God be with you.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9691. Courtesy: Mirabehn
