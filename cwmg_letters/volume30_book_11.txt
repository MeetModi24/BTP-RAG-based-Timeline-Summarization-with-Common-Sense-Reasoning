11. LETTER TO P. S. VARIER
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 12, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. The parcel I had four or five days before
the receipt of the letter. I thank you for both.
I fear that for myself personally your medicine will be useless
because I can take only one or two ingredients in any 24 hours,
as I can take only five ingredients during that period whether in
the shape of medicine or food. Thus if your pill contains more
than one ingredient, I must not take it because I should then
have no food.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. S. VARIER
ARYA VAIDYA SALA
KOTTAKAL
S. MALABAR
From a photostat: S.N. 14097
