182. LETTER TO DHIRUBHAI DIKSHIT
SEVAGRAM,
August 16, 1946
CHI. DHIRU,
Can we say that your problem has been solved? Dr. Bha-
gavat is a saintly man. Each time [I see him] he impresses me
more and more. One can gain a lot from him provided one has
the faith. I have no doubt about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. DHIRUBHAI
URULI KANCHAN
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
