301. LETTER TO DRAUPADI SHARMA
March 14, 1936
CHI. DRAUPADI,
I have your letter which I am sending on to Sharma. My
[stay] in Delhi is certain till the [23rd]. I would like it if you
could visit me in the mean while. I am keeping well but cannot
tackle much work. I have strength but there is the doctor's
veto.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
between pp. 192 and 193
