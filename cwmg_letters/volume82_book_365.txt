365. LETTER TO DR. N. B. KHARE
SODEPUR,
December 23, 1945
BHAI KRARE,
I have your letter. Thanks for the same. I have read it
through. Let us see what happens now.
I am enclosing a newspaper cutting which seems to me quite
wrong. Is not what happened quite the opposite?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 4870. Courtesy : N. B. Khare
