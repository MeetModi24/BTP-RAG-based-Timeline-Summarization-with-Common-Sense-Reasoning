440. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
August 28, 1931
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your two letters. They give no news about Ratilal.
Send Mumbai Samachar to Prabhavati.
I have already sent a note to you about Lalji. Send him
goods worth up to Rs. 50 when Mama writes about them. Deduct
from that amount the sum of Rs. 20 which you have already sent.
That is, you have now to send Rs. 30. Three Yeravda wheels
being manufactured in Bardoli are to be sent to Mama just now.
Nine more may be supplied later when he writes for them.
Now that I am going, I am leaving a heavy burden on you.
But you are capable of shouldering it. Mahadev must have sent
you my address. Write to me from time to time.
I believe I
shall return in six weeks at the shortest and twelve weeks at the
longest. It is nearly ten now. I feel sleepy and, therefore, don't
write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
To the others I will write in the morning or from the ship.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I
