27. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
July 22, 1945
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
I have received all your letters. They have all been duly
answered. Bear in mind that anyone who spins will have to see
to all the previous processes connected with cotton. Without it
the work will be incomplete. Among these the process of card-
ing works wonders. Let them ply the takli instead of the spinning-
wheel.
I have not quite understood what took place with Kanu
Gandhi. Narandas's suggestion appears to be quite acceptable.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10855. Courtesy: Purushottam
K. Jerajani
