237. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 30, 1940
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I got your letter. If the persons whose names have been sub-
mitted are willing to fulfil the conditions, they may offer civil
disobedience.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Tell Raghunandanji that he may well stay out for a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2477
