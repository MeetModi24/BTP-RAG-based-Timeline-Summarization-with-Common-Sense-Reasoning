29. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
April 13, 1938
CHI. PRABHA,
I had your letter. You must have received mine. I do not
like your worrying all the time. You must regain perfect health.
I hope Jayaprakash is well. Write to me fully. Don't
postpone writing till I write to you. I am going to Delhi for a
day. I will leave there on the 15th and reach Wardha on the
16th. I am fairly well. The blood-pressure at present is 154/
100. I observed a fast today1, but didn't feel it.
The work here has remained unfinished. Perhaps I shall
have to come back. I do hope that everything will be all right.
But why need we concern ourselves with the result?
Write to
me regularly. Write to Ba also. And don't forget A. S.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3511
