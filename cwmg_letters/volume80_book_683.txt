683. LETTER TO DHARMADEV SHASTRI
SIMLA,
July 8, 1945
BHAI DHARMADEV SHASTRI,
You enter your fourth year on the 10th. You are serving
the hill tribes among whom very few workers work. I like that.
May your work progress steadily.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DHARMADEV SHASTRI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
