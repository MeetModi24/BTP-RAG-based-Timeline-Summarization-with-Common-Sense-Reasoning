484. LETTER TO SHANTA S. PATEL
August 31, 1932
CHI. SHANTA (PATEL),
I had received the note which you wrote before you were
released. I will wait for your promised letter. With whom do
you stay? Give a detailed report about yourself. Let me know
about the condition of both your mind and body. What did
you read? Who were your friends? Write about all these things.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4065. Also C.W. 16. Courtesy:
Shantabehn Patel
