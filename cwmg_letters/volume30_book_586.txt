586. LETTER TO SYED RAZA ALI
THE ASH RAM, SABARMATI,
May 28, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I write this to thank you for the letter that you wrote to me
from. South Africa enclosing a copy of the Memorandum of your
evidence. I congratulate you and your deputation upon the
success that attained your efforts.
Without mentioning your name I made use of your tribute to
Mr. Paddison in my weekly notes in Young India.
I hope you enjoyed your South African visit.
Yours sincerely,
RON. SYED RAZA ALI
AT INVERARM
SIMLA
From a microfilm: S.N. 11959
