656. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
Via WARDHA,
February 4, 1941
CHI. LILA,
You are a limpet. Mahadev has written many letters. Even
I have written at least two or three. But if you cannot be sati-
ated what to do?
Do not spoil your health by keeping awake
at night. It is enough that you are determined to do my work.
Sitting there, are you not doing only my work?
Manu is now
better. Her fever is going down.
R. K. and Mahadev are at Lucknow and Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI LILAVATIBEHN UDESHI
KANJI KHETSI CHHATRALAYA
65 MINT ROAD
BOMBAY FORT
