626. LETTER TO SHYAMJI SUNDERDAS
PESHAWAR,
October 12, 1938
BHAI SHYAMJI,
Prabhavati's husband, Jayaprakash, is taking treatment from
a vaidya. He says that his fee is Rs. 200. Find out if you
know him, otherwise give Prabhavati a sum of up to Rs. 200.
Let me know what you withdraw. I will try to have it sent
from Wardha.
I hope Prithviraj and Vali are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 10867. Courtesy: Shyamji Sunderdas
1 Jayaprakash Narayan was taking treatment at Arya Vaidya Shala,
Kottakkal.
