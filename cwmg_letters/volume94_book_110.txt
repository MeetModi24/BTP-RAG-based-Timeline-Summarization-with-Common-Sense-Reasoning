110. LETTER TO PANNALAL JHAVERI
October 14, 1941
CHI. PANNALAL,
I hope you are allowing time to take its course.
Come quickly with Gangabehn and any children you wish to bring.
Letters for the children are enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari - Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 34
