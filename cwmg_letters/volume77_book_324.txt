324. LETTER TO MANJULA M. MEHTA
PANCHGANI,
July 26, 1944
CHI. MANJULA1,
I have your letter. I feel that whatever is due to Ratilal
should be paid up. He will use it as he is destined to. How
long can we keep a watch over a person who has come of age?
I regularly get from Champa letters which are quite sensible.
I intend to start from here on the 1st for Sevagram. Do
come and see me there when you can.
I do not remember having received your letter in jail. Very
few letters were delivered to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MAGANLAL PRANJIVANDAS MEHTA, BARRISTER
8 2 GHODBUNDER ROAD
ANDHERI
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 1029. Courtesy: Manjula M.
Mehta
