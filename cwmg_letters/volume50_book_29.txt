29. LETTER TO NANABHAI I. MASHRUWALA
June 11, 1932
BHAI NANABHAI1,
I got your postcard. I have not yet received Manilal's letter.
I have completely stopped seeing visitors. Even if this had not
been the case, Medh could not have seen me without special per-
mission. Nevertheless, when I receive Manilal's letter, I will
form some opinion from it if I can. From the fact that there has
been no cable from Phoenix, I had concluded that Manilal and
Sita were quite well. About the latter, I came to know only
from your postcard. I had heard from Surendra that he was
well in Visapur.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6684. Also C.W. 4329. Courtesy:
Kanubhai Mashruwala
