688. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
POONA,
October 19, 1945
CHI. KISHORELAL,
I have your letter. We cannot have Premabehn on the Dhu-
lia Trust. Nor can we have Sushila. I would like to suggest
Tara's name. Have you written to Rameshwardas? If not, do
so. I do not see the need to suggest a woman's name just for the
sake of having one. Is what Kumarappa says true? He points
out an error and then raises an issue.
I hope you are all right.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
