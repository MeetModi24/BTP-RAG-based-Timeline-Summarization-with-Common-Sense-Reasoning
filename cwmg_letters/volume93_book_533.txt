533. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM,
July 18, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. You must have got my letter of yesterday.
The jackal bite patients are well. There were seven vaccinations
today. Kumarappa has had a little relapse. His vision was
blurred and then lost altogether. This lasted for about an hour.
He remembers you. He will write to you. You should treat the
talk about my going that side as mere gossip.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
LADY HARDINGE MEDICAL COLLEGE FOR WOMEN
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original:
Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
