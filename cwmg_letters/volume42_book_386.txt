386. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
January 16, 1930
BHAI GHANSHAYMDASJI,
I have both your letters. I am so busy these days that there
is no time to answer letters. I shall make my comments after
going through the speech. I too had a talk with Malaviyaji
Maharaj. Things can improve much if he inculcates tolerance in
the other party. Do whatever you can in this direction.
We shall discuss your health when we meet.
405
I have no anxiety on Keshu's account.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From Hindi: C.W. 6181. Courtesy: G.D. Birla
