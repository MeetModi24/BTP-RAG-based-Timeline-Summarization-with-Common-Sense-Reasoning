37. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
September 24, 1931
CHI. PREMA,
Narandas writes to tell me that you are calm now. But I am
unhappy that you have still not started writing to me. You can
certainly help to banish my worry on your account.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10264. Also C.W. 6713. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
