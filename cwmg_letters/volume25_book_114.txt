114. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
DELHI,
September 17, 1924
MY DEAREST CHARLIE,
Don't you fret over my decision. It has been taken after deep
prayer and clearest possible indication. This fast of 21 days is
the least I can do. Oh the agony of it all! Every day has been
a day of torture. But I shall soon be at peace. I was longing to
see my duty clearly. The light has come like a flash. Can a man
do more than give his life?
With dearest love,
Yours,
MOHAN
C. F. ANDREWS, ESQ.
SHANTINIKETAN
Via BOLPUR
E. I. RLY.
From a photostat: G.N. 2613
