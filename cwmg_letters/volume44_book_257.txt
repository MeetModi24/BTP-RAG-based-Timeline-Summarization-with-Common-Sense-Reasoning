257. LETTER TO KASHINATH TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 27, 1930
CHI. KASHINATH,
I have both your letters. Kalavati is showing very good
progress.
You have done well in deciding to keep to the policy of
self-sufficiency in khaddar. Spare diet, physical and mental labour
are the remedies for nocturnal emissions. Benefits of concentration
while doing physical work are twofold: the performance improves
and passions automatically remain under control.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5253
