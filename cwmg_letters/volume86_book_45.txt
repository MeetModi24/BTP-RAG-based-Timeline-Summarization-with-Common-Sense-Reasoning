45. LETTER TO G. D. TAPASE
NEW DELHI,
October 25, 1946
BHAI TAPASE,
I have your letter of October 4, 1946. Thanks. I have
been able to reach it only today. I am giving the relevant
parts in the Harijan papers.
It should be properly implemented.
Blessings from
M. K.
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
