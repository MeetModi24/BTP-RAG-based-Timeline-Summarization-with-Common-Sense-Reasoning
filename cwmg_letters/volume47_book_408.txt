408. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
August 22, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your letter. I am still giving rest to my right hand. Write
to me all the time at the following address: P.B. 26, Ahmedabad.
I am here till September 8. It is being discussed whether I should
go to England. But I don't think it probable that anything will
come of it.
I had written to Jayaprakash the very next day. I hope he
got my letter. What did you do?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3397
