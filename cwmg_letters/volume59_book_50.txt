50. LETTER TO NIRMAL K. BOSE
September 21, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
Khan Saheb was good enough to give me a copy of Selections
from Gandhi for which I thank you. I have now your essay on
"caste". If time permits, I shall gladly go through it and let
you have my opinion, if there is anything worth saying.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. NIRMAL K. BOSE
KHADI SANGH
BOLPUR P.O.
BIRBHUM 1
From a photostat: G.N. 10523
