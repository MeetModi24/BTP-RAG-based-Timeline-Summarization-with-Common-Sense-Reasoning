450. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
SABARMATI,
January 12, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to have your ink and yarn. The receipt you
will get from the office. I wish every member of the Sangh will
join the A.I.S.A.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. MOTILAL ROY
PRABARTAK SANGH
CHANDERNAGORE
From a photostat: G.N. 11024
1 The addressee received this letter on January 5, 1926.
