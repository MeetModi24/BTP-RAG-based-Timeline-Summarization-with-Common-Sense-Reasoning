234. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
BRINDABAN,
May 4, 1939
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Take Mathew to Nagpur and get him admitted to David's
dispensary if possible. Why need you stay in Nagpur for three
months? Can't he be treated in Wardha? If [you think] it is
absolutely necessary to stay, think how . . . and send me . . . at
this address up to the 8th . . . and after that wire at Rajkot. . .
[Blessings from]
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8561.
Also C.W. 7050.
Courtesy: Munnalal G. Shah
