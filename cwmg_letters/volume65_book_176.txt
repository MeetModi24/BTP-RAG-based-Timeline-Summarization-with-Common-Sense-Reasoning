176. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
May 10, 1937
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
We had a good talk together. You have to learn the art
of living with others. Look to the good qualities of Lilavati
and others and overlook the bad ones. I hope you have started
your yajna of goseva.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1899
