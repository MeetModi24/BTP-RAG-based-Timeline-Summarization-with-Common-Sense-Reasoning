350. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
April 5, 1933
DEAR SATISBABU,
I have your letter of the 1st instant enclosing a copy of your
letter of the 19th March.
The Sanatani I have been getting regularly. You need not
therefore send it to me. Who is the editor? He wrote to me
saying that he had met me before. I cannot recall his features.
What was or is his occupation?
Ramanandababu's note is indeed interesting; but that is
his style whenever he is displeased with anything.
I have given you my views fully about our duty concerning
the Pact. You will now do exactly what you think is called
for by the local situation. I have the Bengal schedule prepared
by the Bengal Government.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20839
