390. LETTER TO GILBERT KERLIN
AHMEDABAD,
August 21, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of 16th July.
The only ability that a people, in my opinion, requires to gov-
ern itself is to resist alien encroachment. This does not necessarily
mean good or pure government.
I cannot think just now of any book to recommend to you.
Yours sincerely,
GILBERT KERLIN, ESQ.
CAMBRIDGE, MASS., U.S.A.
From a photostat: S.N. 17556
