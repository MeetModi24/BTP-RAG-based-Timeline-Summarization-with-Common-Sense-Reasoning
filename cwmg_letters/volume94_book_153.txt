153. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 27, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
You will have received the letter I wrote you yesterday. Today
the blood-pressure was 142/86. In the morning it was 152/96. It is not
always as good as this. But you will see that it is all right. Sardar's
quota of food has been increased. He takes 4 oz. of separata.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
