343. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 31, 1937
MY DEAR UNTOUCHABLE,
Today I took silence at 12.10 to be ready for the W. C. to-
morrow.
As I write this I have your letter of 29th. I have
not a moment's rest to even write a word under Mahadev's
letter. I have been generally at the W. C. meetings then[sic].
I have kept as well as possible under the peculiar circum-
stances. Yes, I have seen the prisoners. I had two hours
with them. The release is not going to be a simple or easy
affair. I shall leave no stone unturned to secure it. But 'God
disposes'.
I leave for Segaon tomorrow and return here on 11th
November to see the Governor and all whom it may be neces-
sary to see.
No more for the day.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3826. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6982
