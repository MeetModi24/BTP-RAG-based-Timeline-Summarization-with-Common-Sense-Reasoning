275. LETTER TO J. D. ATRE
AS AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 14, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is clear that you have not read my arti-
cles carefully. B's life cannot be taken by A, because A must be
credited with sufficient intelligence to know that B when he seeks
to commit suicide is temporarily insane. A must arrive at his own
judgment and not rely upon another's, and certainly not upon the
one who may be suffering from insanity.
Yours sincerely,
J . D. ATRE, ESQ.
