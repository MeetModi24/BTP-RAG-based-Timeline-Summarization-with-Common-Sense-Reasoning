490. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
February 20, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
Purushottam came and saw me. He has acquired fairly good
knowledge on the subject. I have asked him to stay there for
some time longer still and learn more. He now hopes, and so
do I, that he will be all right. I hope you are progressing satis-
factorily.
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 872. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
