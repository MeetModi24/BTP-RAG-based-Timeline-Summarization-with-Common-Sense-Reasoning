110. LETTER TO KASHI GANDHI
Silence Day [December 17, 1928]
CHI. KASHI,
Do something about Nimu's and Bachu's constipation. Bachu is likely to
benefit by gentle massage of his abdomen. Similar exercise will benefit Nimu
too.
Did you send that chain to Talwalkar ? I think we shall be leaving this
place (Wardha) on the 20th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S. N. 33092
1 Place-name and date as supplied in the source
2 Gandhiji here says he would leave Wardha on 20th. In 1928, he left Wardha for
Sambalpur on December 20. Monday, i.e., Gandhiji's silence day, prior to that date,
fell on December 17.
