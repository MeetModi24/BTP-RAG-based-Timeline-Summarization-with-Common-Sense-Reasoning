386. LETTER TO DR. P. SUBBAROYAN
PANCHGANI,
May 31, 1945
MY DEAR SUBBAROYAN,
I have your letter. I understand and appreciate all you
say. I thought I had told you that Mohan had come and
gone, again leaving a good impression about himself. I have not
even attempted to convert him to my way. I simply let him
perceive the affection I have for him.
Love.
BAPU
DR. SUBBAROYAN
WHILE'S GARDEN
ROYAPETTAH
MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
