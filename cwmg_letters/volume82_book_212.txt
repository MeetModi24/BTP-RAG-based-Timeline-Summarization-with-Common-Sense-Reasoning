212. LETTER TO NIRMAL KUMAR BOSE
SODEPUR,
December 3, 1945
DEAR NIRMAL BABU1,
Your sweet letter . You will do exactly as you please.
In order to interpret my writings, you should be for some
time in Wardha when it is fairly cool.
Your amoebiasis may not be removed but can certainly be
brought under control by nature cure methods. I suggest your
coming to the clinic in Poona when I am there. From 1st January
it will be for the poor.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10511
