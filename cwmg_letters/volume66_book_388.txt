388. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
December 8, 1937
CHI. BHANSALI,
Spare some time for Sharda for teaching her English. She
is such a girl that anything you give her will bear fruit.
Though a child, she is mature and wise.
You have realized that all that I told you about spinning
is essential for a votary of Truth.
Blessings from
BAPU
