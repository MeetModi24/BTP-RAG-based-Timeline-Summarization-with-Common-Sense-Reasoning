590. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
November 7, 1932
CHI. MANUDI,
You seem to be very lazy and forgetful. After a long interval,
you write a letter. You promise that you will not be lazy again,
and then forget and break your promise. This is bad. One should
always keep one's promise, and should never make a promise which
one cannot keep. You should write a letter every Sunday and post
it to the Ashram. You should give in it a report of the work done
by you during the preceding week, what you studied, what other
work you did and how much yarn you spun. I hope you know
that we should wear khadi even at home.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 1517. Courtesy: Manubehn Mashruwala
