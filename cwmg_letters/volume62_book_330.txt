330. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
DELHI,
March 25, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter. Just a line to say that I have to stay for the
Congress. I shall not be there therefore before 15th April. I
am sorry but it was inevitable. I am just off to see the Poet.
Dr. Ansari's diagnosis remains hopeful. The manometer showed
a rise of 8 points but he is not disturbed. He insists on my
taking garlic. I wrote to you yesterday.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6321. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9787
