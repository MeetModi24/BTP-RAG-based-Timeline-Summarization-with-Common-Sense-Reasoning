331. LETTER TO ANASUYABAI KALE
SODEPUR,
December 18, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I was happy to read your letter. Your Hindi is not in any
way inferior to mine.
Everyone should do some constructive work over and above
parliamentary work. And the aim of parliamentary work should
also be to advance constructive work.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI ANASUYABAI KALE
ANAND BHAVAN
DHANTOLI
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
