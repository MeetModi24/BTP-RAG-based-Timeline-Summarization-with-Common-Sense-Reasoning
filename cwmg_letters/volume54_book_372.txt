372. LETTER TO LAKSHMIBEHN M. SHARMA
[April 6, 1933]
CHI. LAKSHMI,
I get several complaints against you. You spent Rs.40 on
saris and have bought so many bangles that you would not
be able to wear them even in your whole lifetime. You forget
that you are married to a poor man and are a poor father's da-
ughter. I am poorer than even Dudhabhai. You should know
that my proposed fast will be for you also. I will be fasting for all
who are connected with me. Moreover, you are a Harijan girl.
You ought to understand your duty. You will cause me much pain
if you tell lies or learn extravagance. Wake up. Do write to me.
From Gujarati: C.W. 9532. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Joshi
