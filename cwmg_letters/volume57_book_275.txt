275. LETTER TO GANGABEHN MAJMUDAR
March 12, 1934
CHI. GANGABEHN,
You have many times promised to Chhaganlal that you will
go to Vijapur, but you have never kept the promise. I wouldn't
have felt it if the land at Vijapur were mine. But it belongs to
the poor and your failure to do your duty of ensuring its proper
management hurts me. You look upon me as your father. I
didn't seek that honour. Now do your duty as a daughter and
do the work for the sake of which you adopted me as a father
and end my pain. I hope you and Kiki are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9583. Courtesy: Chhaganlal
Gandhi
