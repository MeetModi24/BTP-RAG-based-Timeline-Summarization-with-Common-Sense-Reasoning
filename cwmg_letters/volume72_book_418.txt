418. LETTER TO MANJULABEHN M. MEHTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 7, 1940
CHI. MANJULA,
I have your letter. Here you will not get a Gujarati teacher.
You should bring one from there. My advice to you is that you
should come here once, stay for a month or so and then decide
which place will suit you. If you come I will of course like it.
If you come, perhaps Magan also may come. I do not think
that letters will have much influence on him. I did expect that he
would turn out as you have described him to be. But you are
brave and sensible and so I keep patience.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 1604. Courtesy: Manjulabehn M. Mehta
