309. LETTER TO S. MOHAN KUMARAMANGALAM
PANCHGANI,
July 23, 1944
DEAR MOHAN 2 ,
Do come with your friend at 4 p.m. on 28th instant. Your
friend may take two snap-shots without any notice to me.
I am glad Joshi wants my reply. I have not been idle. I
hope to be able to send a reply at an early date.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
M. KUMARAMANGALAM SUBBAROYAN
COMMUNIST PARTY
190 B KHETWADI MAIN ROAD
BOMBAY 4
Chief Commissioner's Office, Bombay, File No. 3001/H/, p. 123. Cour-
tesy: Government of Maharashtra
