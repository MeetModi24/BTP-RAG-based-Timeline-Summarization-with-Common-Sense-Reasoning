616. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 11, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. I hope you are now well. Let me know
your daily routine. Tell Anand that I remember him very much.
There is no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
