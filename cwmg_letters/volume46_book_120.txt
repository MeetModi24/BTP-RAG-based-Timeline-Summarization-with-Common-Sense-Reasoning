120. LETTER TO KHAN CHAND DEV
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 7, 1931
DEAR LALA KHAN CHAND,
Here is copy of a complaint received by me from the Central
Government. Will you please make a detailed enquiry and let
me know the result as early as you can?
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
LALA KHAN CHAND DEV
BRADLAUGH HALL, LAHORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 17058
