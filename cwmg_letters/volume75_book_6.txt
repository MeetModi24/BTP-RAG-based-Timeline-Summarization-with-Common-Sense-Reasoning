6. LETTER TO A. AZIZ
SEVAGRAM,
October 12, 1941
DEAR FRIEND,
There is nothing offensive in your letter. It is well argued.
But you seem to me to have missed my point. I would ask you to
reread my argument if you have got it in full.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SH. A. AZIZ
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
