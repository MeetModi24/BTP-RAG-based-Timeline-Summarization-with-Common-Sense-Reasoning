320. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
October 20, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
Pattani has been most wonderful. I am not sending any-
thing today. Tandy was here. We finished [the job] in half
an hour. He seemed to be a very good man. No, I am not
sending anything today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11582
