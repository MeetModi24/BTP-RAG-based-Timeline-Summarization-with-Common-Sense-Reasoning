101. LETTER TO DRIVER
February 2, 1934
BHAI DRIVER,
I got your letter all right. I like your sincerity. Many young
men have gone through what you have.
You need not feel un-
happy about it at all.
My feeling after reading your letter is that your duty as yet
is neither to come to me nor to go to Father Elwin, but to re-
main where you are and learn to live in poverty.
You should
save as much as you can and use the money for helping people.
Try to teach students whatever moral values you can. So long
as you do not feel vairagyal you should continue in your present
way of life. Renunciation of your present way of life before you
have come to feel strong aversion to it will not be proper, nor
will it endure.
When intense vairagya has developed in you, no-
body will be able to stop you.
Do write to me whenever you wish
to. I hope you find no difficulty in deciphering or understanding
my Gujarati.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8045
