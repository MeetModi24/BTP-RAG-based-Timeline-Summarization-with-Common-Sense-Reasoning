472. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[Before July 17, 1937]
Radhakrishna should have been removed altogether, shouldn't
he? But I don't know much in this matter. You know him
[better].
This time I have removed from your article the portion
in which you have replied to Syed. For the present let them
say what they wish. We should go on writing what we ourselves
think
proper.
Please, therefore, go through the extract I
have given from Tandonji's letter. I have also supplied an
extract for Harijan from Pattabhi's speech.
I have inserted
that, too, in your article. If you do not like it, you may
remove it. It can be said that I have written a political
article for Harijan this time. Go through it. If you don't
approve of it, you can wait. And if you find it all right, an
advance copy may be supplied to the Press. I cannot make
up my mind.
How can I possibly get time to talk to Kanu
today?
Both Shambhudayal and Janba should take plenty of quinine.
They should not eat chapatis. At the most they may take milk,
jaggery and fruit if available. I will attend to the receipts, etc.,
tomorrow. I can't detain Kanu today.
I don't think Nariman's reply calls for a rejoinder. There is
nothing in it to suggest that he is on the war path. He seems to
have calmed down. But who knows?
Blessings from
BAPU
