68. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
SABARMATI,
Thursday, Jeth Sud 14 [June 24, 1926]
CHI. DEVDAS,
Your letter. I saw [your] letter to Mahadev. My rebuke had
been issued by then. From my reference to compound interest you
would have guessed that I had got your letter on the way. Gir-
dhari has been discharged from the hospital, but his health does
not appear very good. He is expected here now. Jamnalalji
would also arrive at about the same time. After that I would
think things over and, if there is any need, I would send him
there. I wrote letters to Dr. Dalal and Bhai Tulsidas soon after
Girdhari's discharge. I wrote to them that it was only by dedi-
cating ourselves to the country's service that we could discharge
to some extent the debt we owe them. Dr. Dalal has replied.
Herewith I send you his letter.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 19638
