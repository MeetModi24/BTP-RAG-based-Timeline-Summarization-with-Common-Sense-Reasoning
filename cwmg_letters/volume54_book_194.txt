194. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
March 23, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. The letter I wrote to you ought to reach
you. You must not lose hope. Whatever He wills will happen.
You should take the treatment and then keep silent. I have
written a long letter to Dr. Sharma regarding his going to the
Ashram, which should reach him. Your letter was enclosed with
that. You must have heard of Lakshmi's marriage. She has gone
away to Bardoli.
Many Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 280
