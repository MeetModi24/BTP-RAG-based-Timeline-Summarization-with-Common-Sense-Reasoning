460. LETTER TO BHUKANSHARAN
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday, April 30, 1926
BHAI BHUKANSHARANJI,
I have your letter and the hundred rupee currency note sent
with it. I intend to utilize the amount on charkha and khadi
work, for by that means we can serve the largest number of the
poor.
