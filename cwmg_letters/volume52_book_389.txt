389. LETTER TO GANGABEHN B. JHAVERI
December 25, 1932
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I got your letter. Please don't stop writing to me. It is good
news that your health is improving.
Nanibehn had written to me about Navin and Mahesh
having gone there.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3951
