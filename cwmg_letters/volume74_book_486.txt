486. LETTER TO PURATAN J. BUCH
September 7, 1941
CHI. PURATAN,
You may come over whenever you wish.
Tell Vasumati-
behn that I got her letter. Let her by all means complete her
programme.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I got your letter. I am glad.
PURATAN BUCH
HARIJAN ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9184
