70. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
February 29, 1928
MY DEAR BHRRR 3 ,
You are a good young man of twenty-five to brave the Govern-
ment and the heat of Bardoli. No defeat if you please.
Yours,
BHRRR
From a photostat: S.N. 9562
1 Manilal Doctor, husband of Jayakunwar
2 Jayakunwar, daughter of Dr. Pranjivan Mehta
3 This was a manner of greeting between Gandhiji and the addressee.
