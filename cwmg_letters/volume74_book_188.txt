188. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
June 23, 1941
CHI. MAHAVIR,
How is it your fever still does not leave you? Are you
sufficiently careful about your diet? Such fever cannot be the
result of simple fatigue.
Whatever the cause, get rid of it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6243
