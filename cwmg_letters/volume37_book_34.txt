34. LETTER TO M. P. SRINIVASAN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 6, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. In my opinion, you are not bound to
marry against your will, even to please your parents. But you are
bound to vacate the house if your father is so desirous. Obedience
to parents has well-defined limits. Disobedience becomes a virtue
when the orders are repugnant to the moral code.
I do not know of any book on fasting that can be of any use
to you.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. M. P. SRINIVASAN
SUB-EDITOR, TAMIL "SWARAJYA"
2 VALLABHA AGRAHARAM, TIRUVATEESVARANPET
TRIPLICANE, MADRAS
