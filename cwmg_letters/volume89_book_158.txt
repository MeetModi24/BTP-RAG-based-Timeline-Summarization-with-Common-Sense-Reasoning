158. LETTER TO DURGA M. DESAI
September 1, 1947
CHI. DURGA 2 ,
I got the letter about Babla's marriage yesterday. Since it
is an alliance of mutual choice, what could my blessings add?
Both are competent and public-spirited. I think anything that
is auspicious needs no blessings; and blessings never come true
when the thing is inauspicious. However, I certainly wish them
both a long life; may they ever serve the people.
Since Bablo and his wife will be there, you are sure to be
settled comfortably. Could you live separately from them?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
