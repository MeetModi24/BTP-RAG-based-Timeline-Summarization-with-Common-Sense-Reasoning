370. LETTER TO S. E. STOKES
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
September 1, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I quite appreciate the difficulty in your
taking Miss Haussding. She has now gone to Mussoorie. She
will be there for some time with Devdas.
Of course, I am longing to be with you and pass even if
it is only for a few days. When that time will come I do not
know. I am watching with considerable interest your experiment
amongst the hill folks.
With love to you all.
Yours sincerely,
S. E. STOKES, ESQ.
KOTGARH
SIMLA HILLS
From a photostat: S.N. 19679
