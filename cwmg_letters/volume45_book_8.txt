8. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI MADHAVJI THAKKAR
December 18, 1930
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
I got your letter. I understand what you say about the chil-
dren. They ought to gain strength. If their present diet is kept up
and they get regular exercise, they certainly will. You may write
and make whatever suggestions you think necessary regarding
them to the people at Calcutta and then stop worrying about
the matter. For you cannot do more than that at present.
Blessings from
BAPU
