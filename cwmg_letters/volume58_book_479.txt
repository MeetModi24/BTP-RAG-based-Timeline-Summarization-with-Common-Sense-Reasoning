479. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA
September 8, 1934
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
I have your letter. My congratulations to Father on leading
everyone in speed. Your note does not indicate the count and
uniformity of yarn. You must find it. I suppose you know the
way of finding it. The method used for villages is very simple.
Give me your opinion about the yarn spun on the bamboo spin-
ning-wheel. What is the circumference of the wheel?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2792
