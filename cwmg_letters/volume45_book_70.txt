70. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS
TRIKUMJI
January 4, 1931
I was very happy that all of you got an opportunity to visit
the Ashram. I would have been happier still if you could have
spent a few days there. I get news about Mathuradas from time
to time. I am sure his days will pass peacefully. This is a good expe-
rience he is getting. Pyarelal is quite well. My love to children.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 103
