546. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
November 2, 1932
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
Your letter and my postcard seem to have crossed. You are
being tested fairly severely, and I am sure that you will prove your
perfect worth.
Give my respectful greetings to Grandmother. I hope she
preserves her peace of mind.
My blessings to Sumati.
All three of us are quite well and often think of you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4799. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
