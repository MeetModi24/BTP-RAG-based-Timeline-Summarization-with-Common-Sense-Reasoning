473. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
[1939]
CHI. SUSHILA,
What is this I hear? You have fallen ill because of care-
lessness about food and insufficient sleep. Why was this and how?
We had agreed that you would return from there after putting
on some weight and gaining some energy. Why should you
succumb if people press you to eat this and that? Do you not
have the strength to refuse?
Your article in The Statesman was good. Do you realize that
you have tied yourself down in that article?
293
The dairy, the cows, etc., have to be taken nearer to the
well. The dairy has a pucca building. There is a proposal to
set up a hospital for you there. Enclosed is Mirabehn's plan.
Have a look at it and give it thought. Whatever has to be
done will be done only after you return.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
