224. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 29, 1936
BHAI SATIS BABU,
There is little likelihood of Chandranath following your
advice if he does not wholly believe in truth and non-violence.
Why does Hemprabha keep worrying?
How are matters with the Harijan Sevak Sangh? And
how is Arun1?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1627
