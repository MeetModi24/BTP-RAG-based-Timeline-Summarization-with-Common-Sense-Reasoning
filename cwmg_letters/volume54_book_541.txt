541. LETTER TO E. HILLIARD
April 21, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of the 18th instant. I have glanced
through the interesting article you have sent me. The quotations
given in it are very appropriate.
Yours sincerely,
E. HILLIARD, ESQ
4 HARRIS ROAD
BENSON TOWN, BANGALORE
