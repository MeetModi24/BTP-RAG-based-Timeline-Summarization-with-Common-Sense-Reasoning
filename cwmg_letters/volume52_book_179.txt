179. LETTER TO K. MADHAVAN NAIR
December 6, 1932
MY DEAR MADHAVAN,
I have your letter. You do not need to apologize, and
under the stress you are going through, let only one person
write, and that too for giving me the necessary report. Urmila
Devi, of course, writes and she won't be happy until she has
written something practically every day.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18661
