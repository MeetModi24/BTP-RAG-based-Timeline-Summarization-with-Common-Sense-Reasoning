148. LETTER TO N. G. CHANDAVARKAR
NADIAD,
April 15, 1918
DEAR SIR NARAYAN,
Here is Mr. Pratt's speech. The version I am sending is a
full translation of the verbatim report taken by my reporter. I send
also a copy of my reply. I send you also the Wadthal annawari
report prepared by Mr. Ghosal and the correspondence thereon.
If the latter is not understandable please let me know. The
Wadthal correspondence shows clearly how much room there is
for honest difference of opinion. Only an impartial inquiry can
bring about a satisfactory settlement.
Please let me know if you need further information.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 10703. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
