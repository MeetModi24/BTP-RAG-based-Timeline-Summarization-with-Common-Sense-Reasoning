211. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
June 30, 1947
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have already written to you regarding Kamala. Now I am
also sending with this the letter I received from her today. Decide
as you like and let me know, and write directly to her too.
Abha is really ill and is in Calcutta.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C. W. 8640. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
