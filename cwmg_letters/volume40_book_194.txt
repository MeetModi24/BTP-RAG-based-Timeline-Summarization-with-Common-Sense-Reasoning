194. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
April 7, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
You must have got the letter which I wrote to you from
Bombay. I am writing this from Hyderabad. Kishorelal has
advised H. to see S. He will, therefore, do so. He asked my
permission and I have given it. You should not feel nervous
about this affair. Remain calm. We should be careful and
see that no injustice is done by us. There will be no harm if we
are deceived, but it will certainly be wrong if we do injustice to
anyone. If some people persist in their sins despite our vigilance,
we may remain unconcerned by that because we shall not be res-
ponsible for their sins. I have no time to elaborate this, however.
I must run now. Waman Naik is sitting before me.
Blessings from
BAPU
