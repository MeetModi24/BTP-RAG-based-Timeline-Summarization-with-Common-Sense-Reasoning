436. LETTER TO MITHUBEHN PETIT
August 26, 1933
CHI. MITHUBEHN,
I have your letter. Please do not worry about me. I am
steadily regaining strength. I can digest milk.
I trust you are in good health.
I need not enquire about
your work; it must be going on as usual.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRIMATI MITHUBEHN PETIT
COLABA, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2705
