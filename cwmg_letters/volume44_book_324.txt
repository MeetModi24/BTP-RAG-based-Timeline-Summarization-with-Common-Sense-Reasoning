324. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 17, 1930
CHI. PUNJABHAI,
I have your letter. I am sure you will not be left free for
many days. When you are back in jail, try not to fall ill. If
you have discovered the cause of your illness, remove it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4017
