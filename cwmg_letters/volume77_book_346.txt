346. LETTER TO SHWAIB QURESHI
PANCHGANI,
July 31, 1944
MY DEAR SHWAIB,
Your description of the boy brings tears to the eyes. I have
a vivid recollection of my struggle with the child. God's peace on
him and you, the parents.
So, you see, the Qaid-e-Azam and I are to meet. If the
publication of the C. R. Formula was blameworthy, I must share
the blame equally with him. Anyway you will work away, I know.
Love to both.
BAPU
SHWAIB SAHEB QURESHI
BHOPAL
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
