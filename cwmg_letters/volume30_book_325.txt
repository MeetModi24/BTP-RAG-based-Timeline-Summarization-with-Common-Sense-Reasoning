325. LETTER TO S. NAGASUNDARAM
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 11, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I must not deal with the matter referred
to by you in the pages of Young India. I said all that had to be
said on the incident in 1921. I am never in an uncompromising
mood. Viceroy never made a single offer that could be accepted
by any self-respecting person. When I advised the Ali Brothers
to sign that famous document called apology2, I went nearest
the edge of weakness. But I do not regret it. That 'apology' did
the Brothers and the nation much good. When their trial came
it was an issue that was as honourable to them as it was dis-
honourable for the Government.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. NAGASUNDARAM
FIRST FLOOR
LAKSHMINIVAS BUILDING
NEAR KING'S CIRCLE
MATUNGA, BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19444
