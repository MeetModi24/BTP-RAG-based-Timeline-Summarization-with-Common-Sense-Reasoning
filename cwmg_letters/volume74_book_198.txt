198. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
June 29, 1941
DEAR MOTI BABU,
Shri Jajuji has sent me the file regarding Prabartak Sangha.
I was distressed on reading it. The A.I.S.A. is a huge concern
which has the poorest of the land as its only concern. It must
put all its transactions on a sound legal footing.
Then only
can it be morally sound. I therefore do not understand the hesi-
tation to give the required promissory note. Indeed, the debt
should have been paid off long ago. And why this hesitation to
get the certificate? If you cannot accept the requirements, surely
the least you should do is to stop khadi work altogether.
I am sure I have but to draw your attention to things to
set them right.
I hope your eyes no longer trouble you and that otherwise
too you are well.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 11054
