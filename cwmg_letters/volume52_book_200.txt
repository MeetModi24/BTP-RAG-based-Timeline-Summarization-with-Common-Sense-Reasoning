200. LETTER TO REGINALD REYNOLDS
December [8]1, 1932
MY DEAR ANGADA 2 ,
You do not [know] how glad we all are when we hear
from you. So we were delighted to have your letter of 10th
November. This is your 3rd letter. As my companions I have
only the Sardar and Mahadev. Ba and Pyarelal were naturally
taken away soon after the fast was over. But Ramdas, Surendra
have been in this jail for some months and now there is Jamnalalji
-the last having been transferred here for the sake of his health.
I hope you are well in every way.
With love from us all.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 4544. Courtesy: Swarthmore College, Philadelphia
