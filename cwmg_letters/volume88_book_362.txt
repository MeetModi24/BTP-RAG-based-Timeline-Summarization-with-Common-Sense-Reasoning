362. LETTER TO PYARELAL
July 17, 1947
CHI. PYA.,
I indeed feel delighted to read about your work and I long
to return there. The Kashmir problem is threatening to become
serious. After going to Kashmir, if I do go, I intend to return
there visiting Bihar on the way. I want to stay with you. But I
do not know what God will prompt me to do.
If you have no workers with you, cannot you do with Kanu
and others? I discussed your schemes with Satis Babu.
You seem to be on your feet for long hours till late in the
evening. I must confess that I would not have had such courage.
I am sure you can speak Bengali well enough by now.
If you send some toffee again, I shall have a look at it and
may even be able to sell it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Gujarati: Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase,
Vol. II, between pp. 496 and 497
