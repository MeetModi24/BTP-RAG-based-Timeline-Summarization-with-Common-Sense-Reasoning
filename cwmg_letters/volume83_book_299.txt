299. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
BOMBAY,
March 14, 1946
DEAR MR. ABELL,
Many thanks for your kind note. The reason for my
putting in writing my impression of our talk was only to guard
against any misunderstanding about the position.
I would not
like to put you or me in any false position and certainly not
H. E. As it is you will agree with me that it was good I write
out my own understanding of the message you had brought me.
Of course I have no intention of publishing anything.
I have
to say something to the Working Committee in a general way,
nothing to the Press.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 151
