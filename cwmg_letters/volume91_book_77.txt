77. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI H. PATEL
Chaitra Sud 6 [March 21, 1915]
BHAISHRI MAGANBHAI,
I have your letter. I shall be here till Monday night. I
shall reach Calcutta on Thursday and hope to be at Santiniketan
on Saturday. It is very good that the work there is proceeding
well. We should continue to help in it to the best of our ability.
Look after Messrs. Andrews and Pearson very carefully. You
have not given any news about Pal Baba's health. Maganlal shall
have reached there before my arrival.
I had told Manilal to send the dried fruit immediately.
You must have received it soon after your letter.
It is extremely hot here. I am now tired of all the honour
and respect being showered on me, I do not see any meaning
in it.
All the three of us are well.
Regards from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
Look after your health. Professor Balwantrai Thakore will
have arrived there or will be arriving. Look after him. Give him
my regards if he has already come, and give the necessary news.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10999. Courtesy: Suryakant C.
Patel
