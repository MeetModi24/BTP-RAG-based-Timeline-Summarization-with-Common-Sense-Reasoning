487. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
July 18, 1937
CHI. KANU,
If it is necessary for you to cook your meals there, you may
certainly do so. However, if you can arrange your programme
as before, that will not be necessary.
In any case don't be
careless about food. We will talk further when you go over
here. Learn typing as quickly as possible.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
