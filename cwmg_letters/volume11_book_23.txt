23. LETTER TO L. W. RITCH
7, BUITENCINGLE [STREET,
CAPE TOWN,]
April 11, 1911
MY DEAR RITCH,
I have nothing to report today. I have your wire about
Madras. I am just now going to Lane and [will] then decide
upon the answer to be given.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Nothing to be known before next week. That is Smuts'
answer. More tomorrow.
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 5450
