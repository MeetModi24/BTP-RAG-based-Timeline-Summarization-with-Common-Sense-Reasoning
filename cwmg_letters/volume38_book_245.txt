245. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
WARDHA,
Tuesday [December 11, 1928]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I got your letter about Rajagopalachari. I like the suggestion.
But it is difficult to say whether Rajaji's constitution will stand
the strain of this work. Anyway I shall write to him.
How is your health now?
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SHRIYUT GHANSHYAMDAS BIRLA
8 ROYAL EXCHANGE PLACE, CALCUTTA
From Hindi: C. W. 6163. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
