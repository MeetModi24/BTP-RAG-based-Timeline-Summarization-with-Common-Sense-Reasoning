446. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 22, 1929
I have your letter. You will see from current Young India
what I have done about the boycott of foreign cloth. You would
do some such thing like that. I want you to throw yourself into
that work.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARIAR
GANDHI ASHRAM, TIRUCHENGODU, S. INDIA
From a photostat: S.N. 14892
