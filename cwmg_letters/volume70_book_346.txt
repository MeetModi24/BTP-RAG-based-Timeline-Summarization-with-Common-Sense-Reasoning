346. LETTER TO DIGVIJAYSINH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 26, 1939
MEHERBAN MAHARAJA 2 ,
I thank you for sending me a prompt reply.
I understand
what you write about water. Nevertheless I am requesting you
to go deep into the matter and resolve to rid Kathiawar of this
scarcity of water.
It is my humble opinion that very little has
been done to increase the number of trees.
When I get an opportunity to meet you, I shall certainly
state my views about the doubt you have raised. It is my firm
belief that the path you have adopted is not right.
This much
I have written as a friend.
Yours,
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
