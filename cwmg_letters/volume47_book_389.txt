389. LETTER TO KANHIALAL
AHMEDABAD,
August 21, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
The only thing I can suggest for your daughter is that you
should put her in a widows' home. As you know, the late Sir
Ganga Ram ran one in Lahore.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. KANHIALAL
RETIRED RECORD KEEPER OF E. I. Ry.
ASHRAFABAD, LUCKNOW
From a microfilm: S.N. 17555
