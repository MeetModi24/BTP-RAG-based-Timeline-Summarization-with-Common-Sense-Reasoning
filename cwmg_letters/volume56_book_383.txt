383. LETTER TO TOTARAM SANADHYA
December 19, 1933
BHAI TOTARAMJI,
I got your letter. I like the scheme. It must be implemented
as far as possible.
I trust the children from Utkal give you satisfaction.
Hariprasad must be engrossed in service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2537
