119. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
ASHRAM,
Paush Krishna 10 [January 9, 1926]
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I have the letter sent with Chi. Martand. Everything possible
will be done for Martand. A proper diet alone will restore your
health. You should not mind a little delay in starting your work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 6057. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
