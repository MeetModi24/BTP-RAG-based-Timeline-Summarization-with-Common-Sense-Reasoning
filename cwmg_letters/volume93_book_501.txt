501. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
SEVAGRAM,
March 31, 1940
CHI. PREMI,
Our letters crossed. I hope Father is none the worse for the
journey or the climate there. My letter contained the mala for
you. I hope you got it safe. You will give me a detailed letter
and guard Father against unwanted interviews.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 11052. Courtesy: Arjun Jairamdas
1 This is in the Devanagari script.
