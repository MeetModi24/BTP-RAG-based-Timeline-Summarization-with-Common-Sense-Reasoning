539. LETTER TO K. ISWARA DUTTA
April 21, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Apart from the fact that I have not a
moment to spare for the article you will want me to write for
you, I may not contribute such articles under the restrictions
governing my incarceration.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. ISWARA DUTT
"THE SWADESH BHAGTA"
ALLAHABAD
From a microfilm: S.N. 21027
