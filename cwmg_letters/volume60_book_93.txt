93. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
January 14, 1935
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. You are economizing very well. You
must no more fall ill. Accept Narandas's advice in matters of
food, studies, etc.
I obviously cannot guide you from this dis-
tance. Always remember that fruits of patience are sweet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9330. Also C.W. 6605. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
