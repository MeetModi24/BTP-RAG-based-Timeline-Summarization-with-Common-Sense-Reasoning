63. LETTER TO DWIJENDRANATH TAGORE
ON WAY TO BHAGALPUR,
December 11, 1920
MY DEAR BARODADA,
Your letter was a consolation to me. Your approval I count
as a blessing. I am in Calcutta on the 13th and in Dacca on
the 14th. May you live to see swaraj established in India.
Yours,
M. K. G.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
