343. LETTER TO MANKAR
MUSSOORIE,
June 3, 1946
BHAI MANKAR,
I got the news of Sheth Lallubhai's passing away from your
letter. He did much work of compassion for animals. Sooner
or later we shall all pass away. Let us hope God will send
someone worthy to fill his place. In the meanwhile you for your
part should go on pursuing your dharma and add lustre to the
work.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
JIVADAYA MANDAL
148 SARAF BAZAR
BOMBAY 2
