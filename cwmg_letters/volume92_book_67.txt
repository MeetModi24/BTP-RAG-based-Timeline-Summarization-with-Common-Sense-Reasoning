67. LETTER TO SURENDRARAI B. MEDH
[July 21] 1929
BHAISHRI MEDH,
What advice can I give about the law from here? I write
to the Press what I think. I write about the Agent too, on
occasion. I can give more practical advice only if I am present
there and see things for myself. But I see no possibility of that
in the present life. The dissensions among you there are indeed
unfortunate. Do whatever you can.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/78
