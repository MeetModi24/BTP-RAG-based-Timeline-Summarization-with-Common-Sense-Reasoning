185. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
December 21, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
We did not have the rain you had but we had full-strength
gale and lightning. Here there has been nothing so far. They
are working full speed but much remains to be done yet. Nature
has been unkind. Her unkindness will be forgotten if she does
not repeat it.
It will be a great thing if Prahlad's boy pulls through. I am
glad you will have half an hour with Balkoba every evening.
I have given strict instructions about fruit. I hope the two
B.s are getting it without any hitch.
Do not overwork.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Tilak Nagar P. O. No further address required.
[PPS.]
Have talked to P. He will write.
From the original: C.W. 6368. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9834
