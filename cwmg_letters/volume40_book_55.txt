55. LETTER TO V. L. PHADKE
February 28, 1929
BHAI MAMA,
I have your letter. Here is the hangman come to put the
noose round your neck. Wear it if you have the courage. You
have not proved a failure at the Antyaja Ashram. You will not
fail even as Secretary. Restrict your work to constructive acti-
vities. Don't do even this work if it is likely to interfere with
your work for the Antyajas. Write about Jagu.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3824
