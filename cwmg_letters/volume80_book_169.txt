169. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
May 9, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your letter. I take it that Madhavdas is getting on
well. I do wish that you should at least accept the amount you
actually spend on him. You may not take anything when I
send someone who really can't pay. I did not pay for Ba's
80-
treatment, did I? If you do not accept anything I shall have
hesitation in sending you other cases.
If you cannot write yourself, you should not hesitate to ask
others to write to me. The only thing is that all sections of
the Nature Cure Clinic should be functioning well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
