109. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
May 19, 1935
CHI. KUNVARJI 1 ,
I got your letter. Here we didn't see anything to criticize in
Harilal's behaviour. I shall be pained if your suspicion proves
true. There is no question of handing over Manu to his care. I
hope all of you are quite well there.
Ba and I shall be in Bombay on Wednesday. You can come.
I will give you a few minutes.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9721
