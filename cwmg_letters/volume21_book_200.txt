200. LETTER TO GIRDHARILAL DAYAL
SABARMATI,
Kartak Vad 4 [November 19, 1921]
SHA. GIRDHARILAL,
Received your letter. I am sorry that your brother-in-law has
been injured. What other remedy than patience can I offer?
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHA. GIRDHARILAL DAYAL
2ND FLOOR
THAKKAR KANJI KESHAVJINO MALO
SATRISHI GALI
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 7680
