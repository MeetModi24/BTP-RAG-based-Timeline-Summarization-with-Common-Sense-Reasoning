487. LETTER TO HUSSEINBHAI
NEW DELHI,
September 27, 1946
BHAI HUSSEINBHAI,
I have your letter. I understand that you had to rush back
owing to the demise of your aunt who was also your mother-in-
law. Is there any need for condolence? Birth and death are
companions. One follows the other. Then what is there to
grieve over? Let us live as God ordains.
Blessings from
BAPU
