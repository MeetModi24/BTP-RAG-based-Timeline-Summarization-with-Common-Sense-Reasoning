247. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 27, 1930
CHI. MANI (PATEL),
You ask me to write to you every week. But will the prison
authorities allow you so many letters? I also doubt whether you
will be able to write to me. Preserve your health. Make good
use of every minute and keep a record of your work.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 74
