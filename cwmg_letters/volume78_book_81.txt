81. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
August 31, 1944
CHI. NARANDAS,
A fund of Rs. 75 lakhs is being collected. Let its fate be what
it will. I do not know how much from it will be made avail-
able to you. You who are at the root of this collection should
however carry on with your work in your own manner. This
means that you should be able to collect money according to
your plan. Those who have been always helping you for the
Rentia Baras purse should understand this. And if they do, they
should send you their contributions this time also as they have
been doing in the past. Whenever you feel that such donors re-
quire a word from me, you may use this letter. It would certainly
pain me if the purpose of the Kasturba Fund is misunderstood
and in consequence further expansion of your work is obstructed.
I will regard the Fund as having been put to good use only if
its work, like your work, spreads throughout the country.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8610. Cour-
