453. LETTER TO MAUDE ROYDEN
April 26, 1932
I thank you for your letter enclosing the correspondence bet-
ween yourself and Sir Erric Drummond and Sir John Simon.
When I read about your movement I did not think that you
were in any way showing preference to China over India. I
then felt that you were quite right in concentrating your energy
over a situation that threatened to involve bloodshed on
a vast scale and that too by the adoption of the method of
satyagraha.
The Diary of Mahadev Desai, Vol. I, p. 90
