10. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Silence Day [February 18, 1929]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letters. Yesterday there was a telegram saying
you need not go back home. So you can stay on with a light
heart. There is nothing more to write since we shall be meet-
ing on Wednesday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3333
