171. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
July 29, 1929
MY DEAR MALKANI,
I have just read your Tamil Nad report on the train taking
me back to the Ashram. It is good. I like the frankness about
it. I am sending it to Varadachari for report.
What about the flood there?
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 893
