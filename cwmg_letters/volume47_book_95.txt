95. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
BORSAD,
June 30, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
In the enclosure to my letter of the 24th inst. regarding the
picketing in Cawnpore the Secretary now writes correcting the
date on which the photograph was taken as 5th of May instead
of middle of March.
Yours sincerely,
A.I.C.C. File No. 387, 1931. Courtesy; Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
