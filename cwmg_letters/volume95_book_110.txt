110. LETTER TO PARASRAM MEHROTRA
[About March 1932]
PARASRAM,
Questions were being left unanswered. The thing which is seen
settling down in urine is the natural salt. Everyone has some of it
more or less.
It is good if a new entrant is medically examined. The question
remains whether we can cope with it or no. Neem stick, like babul
stick, gives good service.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 7480; also C. W. 4955.
Courtesy : Parasram Mehrotra
