335. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
November 4, 1909
MY LORD,
I have now received a letter from Lord Crewe, of which I enclose
a copy1, which shows clearly where we stand. The last paragraph I do
not understand at all. I beg to enclose herewith draft of the proposed
reply to Lord Crewe. I await Your Lordship's advice before sending it.
I remain, etc.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5159
