348. LETTER TO NANALAL I. MASHRUWALA
SEVAGRAM,
July 19, 1940
BHAI NANALAL,
I have your letter. I had got your wire also. I have
written to Phoenix. About violence and non-violence I will write
in Harijanbandhu. Hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6693. Also C.W. 4338. Courtesy:
Kanubhai Mashruwala
