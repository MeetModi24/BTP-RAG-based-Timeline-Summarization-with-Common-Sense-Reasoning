226. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
AHMEDABAD,
February 9, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
I wrote to you yesterday. Today I have your letter telling
me of your conversation with Jamnalalji. It is a great thing
to be able to bear with joy being humbled. You should do
the work in front of you without thinking of the future. Jamna-
lal Bajaj is here. I have not met him yet. I shall surely see
him and talk to him about you.
No anxiety of any sort.
Prabha is writing to you about the medical examination.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6310. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9776
