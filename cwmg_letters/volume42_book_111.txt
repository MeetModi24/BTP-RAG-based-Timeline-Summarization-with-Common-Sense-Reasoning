111. LETTER TO PANNALAL JHAVERI
ALIGARH,
November 4, 1929
BHAI PANNALAL,
I was glad to have your letter. I see no error in your reasoning.
If your experiment succeeds, many young men will certainly
benefit. You have my blessings for its success and I also trust that
it will succeed, for I have such faith in you. I am sure you are
keeping a record of the experiment and proper accounts.
You are bound to succeed in your effort to observe celibacy
because both of you are Sincere, both are persevering and both
possess a good measure of self-control. Success is certain for him who
perseveres. That is the assurance the Gita gives and it cannot fail.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3098
