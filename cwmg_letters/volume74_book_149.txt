149. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
June 1, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
If you wish to have a talk with me, you can do so to-
morrow. I am observing silence today. But I can explain with-
out speaking. Make a dhanush takli with a bamboo stick, and
make the spindle from an umbrella spoke which I will send for.
Make as many such taklis as you can. It is an easy job. You
will also learn something as you do it. You seem just now to
have forgotten altogether about the examination. Remember that
you have to appear at both simultaneously.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8499. Also C.W. 7140. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
