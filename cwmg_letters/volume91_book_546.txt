546. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
[December 4, 1926]
BHAISHRI PURUSHOTTAMDAS,
It is all right that you wrote in English. Allow me to con-
tinue to write in Gujarati. I have not been able to read your
articles. If you have any off-prints please send me. I have in-
vited Jaisukhlal Mehta to Wardha to gather more information.
I am writing this on my way to Wardha. I shall be at Wardha
till the 20th.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original ; Purushottamdas Thakurdas Papers. Courtesy ;
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
