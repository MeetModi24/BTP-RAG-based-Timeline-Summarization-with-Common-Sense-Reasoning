83. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
June 19, 1936
CHI. LILAVATI,
Don't you be in a hurry. Mahadev has personally seen how
difficult it is to accommodate anyone here just now. Should
we not at least have a bathroom and a latrine? These just
do not exist. The rains continue, workmen do not turn up as
required, and even the mason does not appear so that the
culvert is half built. Now I doubt whether we shall have
things done within the next month or two. Besides, you have
not cured your cough so that you can think of coming over. This
is not good.
Are you indulging your taste for chillies and oil?
I have heard a lot about your fondness for tasty foods. Even
here, you missed your spicy things, didn't you? Conquer this
weakness. Things are difficult here. In such matters you should
not go by what Ba does. That, however, is another thing. Get
over your cough by taking salt in warm water and other things.
Blessings from
BAPU
