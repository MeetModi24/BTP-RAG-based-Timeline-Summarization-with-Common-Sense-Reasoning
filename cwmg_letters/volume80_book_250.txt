250. LETTER TO GOPE GURUBAXANI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 17, 1945
CHI. GURUBAXANI,
One of two things has happened; either it was my im-
pression that I had written to you or you had written saying
that it was not necessary for me to reply. I sent your letter on
to Sevagram because I thought it was good. Now it appears
that I did not write at all. But now it is to be understood

be well. It is not right that she does not write. I must know

Blessings from
BAPU
