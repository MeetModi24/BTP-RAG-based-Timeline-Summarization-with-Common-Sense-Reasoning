75. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
March 12, 1933
CHI. RAMA,
I have your postcard. Do not lose your peace of mind.
Narandas will certainly come and see you. It is my duty to ask
him to go and it is his duty to go. Your duty is not to ask him
to come and, if he wishes to come, to tell him that he need not
do so. This is how sweetness is preserved in social relations. But
now you may take that further step only after you have tested
him. Meanwhile, you should not worry. I hope your arm is all
right now.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5343
