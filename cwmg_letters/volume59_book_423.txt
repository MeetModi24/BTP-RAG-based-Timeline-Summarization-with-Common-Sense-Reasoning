423. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
November 18, 1934
CHI. NARANDAS,
Read the accompanying letters and give them to Harilal.
Give him from time to time what you think proper.
If you have any comments to offer on the new scheme for
village industries, please do so. How are things there?
Blessings from
BAPU
