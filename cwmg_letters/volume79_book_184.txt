184. LETTER TO ABHAY KUMAR
SEVAGRAM,
February 6, 1945
BHAI ABHAY,
I have heard enough of such wise talk. I do accept its gen-
eral purpose. But it does not mean that we may have no dif-
ference of opinion or see no faults in anyone. I have learnt to
love people in spite of their faults. I do it and I feel satisfied.
Is it not enough that I dwell on truth? I follow truth as I
see it. We do not all see truth the same way. Outlooks differ.
Blessings from
BAPU
ABHAY KUMAR
P. Box 85
NEW DELHI
