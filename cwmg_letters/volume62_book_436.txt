436. LETTER TO RUKMINI MALKANI
May 7, 1936
CHI. RUKMINI,
What is all this? What could a nice girl like Amtussalaam
have done to you? It is not good of you to harass a husband
like Malkani. Do you remember your promise to me? Be calm,
assist Malkani in his work and live amicably with all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 920
