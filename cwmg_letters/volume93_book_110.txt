110. LETTER TO DILIP KUMAR ROY
WARDHA,
June 17, 1936
MY DEAR DILIP,
Mahadev Desai gave me your letter and the correspondence
only yesterday. I glanced through the very interesting corres-
pondence between you and Raihana ! I have perhaps a theory
of my own about Krishna. I need not discuss it here as it is of
no consequence. I hope with you that we shall meet some day.
Then we can usefully discuss the Krishna legends and many
other things of common interest. And of course I should listen
once more to your music.
Love.
M. K. GANDHI
Golden Book of Dilip Kumar Roy, p. 121
