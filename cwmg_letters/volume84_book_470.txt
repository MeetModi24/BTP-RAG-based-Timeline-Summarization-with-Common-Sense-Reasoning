470. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
June 27, 1946
CHI. ANAND,
I got your letter. I intend to leave here tomorrow evening.
I reach Poona some time on Saturday. A telegram will be sent
to Dinshaw. Enquire at his place. I hope you are both happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy : National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
