100. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
January 2, 1926
Just received your two letters to Mahadev, in which I read
your despair. But why despair at all? After all an illness is cured
sooner or later.
It is only good that you have gone over there.
Now you should leave aside all worries, even on your own account.
If you cannot do this, what is the use of all your knowledge?
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 84
