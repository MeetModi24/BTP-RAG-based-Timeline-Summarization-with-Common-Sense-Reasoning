384. LETTER TO RATHINDRANATH TAGORE
March 10, 1945
MY DEAR RATHI,
I was glad to have your letter and learn that you were put-
ting up a home for destitute women.
Love.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From C.W. 10521. Courtesy: Vishvabharati, Santiniketan
1 This is in Tamil script.
2 Addressee's daughter
