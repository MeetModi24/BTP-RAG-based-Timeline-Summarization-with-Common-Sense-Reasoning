536. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
March 31, 1938
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
How are you? Would you not regard Sushila's letter as
coming from me? You yourself wrote that I should not write
[myself], that a letter from Sushila or anyone else would suffice.
If you steadied your mind you must soon get well. I am all
right. The rest from Sushila's letter. You did well to have
your tonsils removed.
Keep on writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 398
