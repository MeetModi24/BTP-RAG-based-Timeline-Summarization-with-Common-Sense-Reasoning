168. LETTER TO M. PIGGOTT
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 27, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You say Rs. 3,000 is so little to me, and
they are much to the widow and her boy. You little know that I
am poorer than the widow. For I do not possess any property
over which I can go to any court of law much less to Privy Council.
I have no money of my own. I am a humble trustee holding some
funds for well-defined trusts. I may not deviate the funds without
exposing myself to the charge of breach of trust. You should
approach a monied man.
Yours sincerely,
M. PIGGOTT, ESQ.
HYDERABAD (SIND)
