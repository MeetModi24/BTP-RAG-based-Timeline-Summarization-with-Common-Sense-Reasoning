758. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 28, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you today. You will have received
the one I sent yesterday. It is your third day today. You
will receive this letter on the 31st morning. That will be your
last day. Send a telegram when the examination is over. When
will the results be known?
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
SOHANLAL COLLEGE
16 LAKE ROAD
LAHORE (PUNJAB)
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
