599. LETTER TO PYARELAL
January 29, 1947
CHI. PYARELAL,
I forgot to reply to your note concerning Shahpur. I am having
to work in such rush.
Join Dr. Gupta's committee. But Gupta himself should be told that
the local Muslim doctor should not be boycotted. Why should the local
doctor not be on the committee ? You may also consult the Muslims.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
When should I send the diary ?
