455. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
November 22, 1930
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
You should write to me in order that the burden of giving
me the news about everyone's health and about the activities there
may not rest on Narandas. When you write, you should give me
news about Jamnadas too. Convey my respectful greetings to
Grandfather and Grandmother. I think about both of them
every day and pray for their blessings. Have you completely
recovered now? Give me a sample account of your activities on
anyone day.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 900. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
