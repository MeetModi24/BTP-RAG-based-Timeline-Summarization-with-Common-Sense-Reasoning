561. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 25, 1932
CHI. TARAMATI,
I have your letter and that of Dilip. I replied to your
previous letter yesterday. You must have received it. Hinkalam
means the Belgaum jail, just as Yeravda means the Poona jail.
Mathuradas has not been transferred. I too saw it in the news-
papers. Explain thoroughly to Dilip the meaning of the verse in
Chapter II of the Gita. He should not merely cram it. I am
not writing separately to him today.
Blessingsfrom
BAPU
