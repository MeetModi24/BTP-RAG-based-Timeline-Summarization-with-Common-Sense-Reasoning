55. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
October 1, 1933
CHI. KAKA,
I got your letter. You kept me waiting for a long time. I
understand the reason now.
You need not get Jamnalalji's permission, since you are an
inmate of the Ashram. . .
I have dispatched today to Ghanshyamdas the letter re-
garding the handing over of the Ashram. Personally I liked your
letter to Vallabhbhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9479. Courtesy: D. B. Kalelkar
