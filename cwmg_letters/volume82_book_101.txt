101. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD, POONA,
November 12, 1945
DEAR MR. ABELL,
I have to thank you for your letter of 6th instant in reply
to mine of 2nd instant regarding certain Press cuttings to which
I had drawn His Excellency's attention.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 43
