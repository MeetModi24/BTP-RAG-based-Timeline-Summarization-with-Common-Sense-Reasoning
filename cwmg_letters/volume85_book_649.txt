649. LETTER TO HARJIVAN KOTAK
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
CHI. HARJIVAN,
You have fallen ill again. Now get well soon.
I am held up here for the time being.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. HARJIVAN KOTAK
SHRI LALLUBHAI GORDHANDAS HOSPITAL
MANINAGAR
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
