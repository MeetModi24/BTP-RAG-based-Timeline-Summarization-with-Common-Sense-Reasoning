98. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJI
ASHRAM,
Friday [January 1, 1926]
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
Devdas is just now at Deolali nursing Mathuradas, but he
has left with me a letter of yours to him containing a question
to be answered. If a person who wears khadi exclusively and on
all occasions is once in a way compelled, by force of circumstances,
to use some other cloth, would he still be regarded a "habitual
wearer of khadi"2? My answer is that he should undoubtedly be
regarded a "habitual wearer". I have an impression, however,
that I had already answered this question for you.
Was a settlement reached in the Sholapur dispute, or is it still
hanging fire?
I am keeping well, and have almost regained the weight lost
during the fast.
Someone on your behalf gave me a basket of fruit on my way
to Kanpur.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4698. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarji
