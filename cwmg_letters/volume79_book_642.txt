642. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
BOMBAY,
April 13, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have read this letter. Speak to him about his habits and
irregularities and warn him. If he does not listen, then it is only
right that he goes. Nevertheless tell him frankly if you all feel that
he is incorrigible and relieve him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
