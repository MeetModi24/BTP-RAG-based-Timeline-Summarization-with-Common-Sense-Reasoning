407. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 23, 1930
BHAI RAMESHWARDAS,
I got your letter. You need not worry. I will now set the
matter right. You have not violated truth in any way. I can
be blamed for an oversight, though I do not know what mistake
I made. I distinctly remember that I told Pyarelal or Kusumbehn
what should be done. If either of them made a mistake, it should
be regarded as mine. This is not formal justice but real justice.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 202
