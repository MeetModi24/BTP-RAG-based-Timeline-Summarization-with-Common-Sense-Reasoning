254. LETTER TO S. A. BRELVI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 9, 1940
MY DEAR BRELVI,
Your fairly full letter is very helpful to me in understanding the
situation.
Yours,
BAPU
SYED ABDULLAH BRELVI SAHEB
ZAMIAH MANZIL
CHURCHGATE RECLAMATION
BOMBAY
