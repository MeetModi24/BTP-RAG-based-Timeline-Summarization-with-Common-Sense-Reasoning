240. LETTER TO NARMADABEHN RANA
March 27, 1933
CHI. NARMADA,
I got your letter. You did well in burying the piece of meat
with your own hand. I hope you took it away from those people
without hurting their feelings. Remember that nothing should
be done forcibly.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 2775. Courtesy: Ramnarayan N. Pathak
