113. LETTER TO MISS KATHERINE MAYO
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 7, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your second letter. The notes were duly corrected,
amplified and posted to you. I hope you have received them. If
they have gone astray, please let me know. I believe that the
original is not yet destroyed. If they have not been received by
you, let me inform you that they were amplified as they were
sent by you. You will therefore kindly wait for the amplified copy
if you have not received it already.
Yours sincerely,
MISS KATHERINE MAYO
BEDFORD HILLS
NEW YORK (U. S. A.)
From a photostat: S.N. 10778
