527. LETTER TO T. RAMACHANDRA
[Before December 11, 1934]
Mahatma Gandhi has addressed a letter to Brahmachari T. Rama-
chandra 2 asking him to suggest names for occupying the position of agents
for pursuing the activities of the All-India Village Industries Association in
Mysore for each of the several districts in the State. Gandhiji has hinted
that the workers so selected should be "whole-hoggers".
The Hindu, 12-12-1934
