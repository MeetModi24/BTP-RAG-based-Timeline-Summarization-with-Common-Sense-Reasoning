188. LETTER TO NAJUKLAL N. CHOKSI
[July 22, 1927]
BHAISHRI NAJUKLAL,
I got your letter. Before that, I had received Dr. Chandu-
lal's telegram and had sent a reply to it. It must have been
received there.
Please hand over the enclosed letter to Chi. Kusum. Let me
know all the facts about her. I hope Chi. Moti and the child
keep well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12140-A
