361. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 30, 1945
CHI. MIRA,
This is just to ask you not to worry about help. Bapa
says he won't find the man to suit you. Balvantsinha can come
211
only after I reach Sevagram. Ramprasad won't leave you till
B. can join you. Mean[ while do as you sing "Rock of ages,
cleft for me, let me hide myself in Thee".
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABEHN
KISAN ASHRAM
BAHADARABAD P. O.
JWALAPUR
NEAR HARDWAR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
