109. LETTER TO SARUPRANI NEHRU
October 15, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
You are always in our thoughts though I may not write to
you. We receive news of you from Jawaharlal. I hope you will
be well enough on the 20th. I regret my inability to be there on
that day but my heart will certainly be there. May God grant
you quick recovery.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
[From Hindi]
Gandhi-Indira Gandhi Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Also G.N. 11406
