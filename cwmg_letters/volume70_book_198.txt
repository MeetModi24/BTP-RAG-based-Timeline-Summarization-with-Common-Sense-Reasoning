198. LETTER TO HARIVANSH SINGH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 10, 1939
SIR,
I have your letter. I do not find anything alarming in
the magazines that you have sent. I am trying my level best
to see that there is no harassment by the kisans. But the land-
lords should be prepared to render full justice. I shall try to
write on this in Harijan.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI HARIVANSH SINGH
TIRWA
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
