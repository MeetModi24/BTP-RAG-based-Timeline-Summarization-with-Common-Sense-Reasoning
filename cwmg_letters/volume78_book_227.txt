227. LETTER TO P. T. RAJAN
SEVAGRAM,
October 13, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
It was an act of self-denial for the students to have given
out [of] their pocket money Rs. 133 to the K. B. M. Fund.
Thank you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI P. T. RAJAN
INDIAN STUDENTS' HOSTEL
KANDY, CEYLON
From a photostat: G.N. 797
