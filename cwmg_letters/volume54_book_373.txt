373. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
April 6, 1933
BHAI MOOLCHANDJI,
You have to bear with your wife's misguided fondness.
If your son-in-law loves khadi your daughter is bound to be
influenced.
You should leave your younger brother to himself.
In order to overcome your anger you should daily recite
Ramanama and remove yourself from the spot the moment you
feel angry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 773
