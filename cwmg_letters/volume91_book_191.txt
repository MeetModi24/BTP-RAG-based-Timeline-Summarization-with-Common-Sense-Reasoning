191. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[Before June 7, 1919]
DEAR MAHADEV,
I have sent with the peon the lead article about Horni-
man which will please you. He came with the newspapers. He
must have seen you. It is good you took notes. See that there
are no mistakes and the article is well composed.
I have received a letter from Simla that I should send the
reply at my earliest. Its language raises hopes.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9855
