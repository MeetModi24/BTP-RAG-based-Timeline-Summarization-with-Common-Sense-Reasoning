237. LETTER TO INDU PAREKH
BARDOLI,
July 26, 1931
CHI. INDU,
I got your brief letter. You will get no boils if, while bath-
ing, you rub the body properly and then clean it thoroughly with
a dry towel. If the water is not very clean, heat it before using.
What progress have you made in your studies?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6253
