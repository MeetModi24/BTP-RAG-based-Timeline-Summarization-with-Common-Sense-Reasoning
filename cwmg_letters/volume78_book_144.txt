144. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
September 18, 1944
CHI. MARY,
I have seen your letter to Manilal. You seem to be flou-
rishing there . I suppose you are not to be expected to be back
in the near future. I do not mind; you are doing good work
there as you were doing here. Kamala was with me for a few
days in Sevagram. She seems to be quite happy in Khedi and
loves her work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6084. Also C.W. 3414. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
