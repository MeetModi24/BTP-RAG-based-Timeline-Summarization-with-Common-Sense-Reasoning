361. LETTER TO PIROJ SHAH
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1947
BHAI PIROJ SHAH,
I am helpless if you don't get the reply I have written
or if it fails to satisfy you. I cannot have all the time that you
have. Cheer up.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
