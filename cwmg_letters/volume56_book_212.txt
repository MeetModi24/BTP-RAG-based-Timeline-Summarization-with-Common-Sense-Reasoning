212. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
November 7, 1933
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
Dhiru seems to be keeping bad health. Ramabehn-who is
here-feels unhappy because of that.
Give me a report about
him, and write separately to Ramabehn also. I hope you have
taken Dhiru and others under your special care.
You must have received the letter I wrote to you at Rajkot.
Write to me from time to time. Whenever you get any news
about Valji, let me have it. Ask Manu and Mavo to write to
me.
How is Kusum?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3137. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
