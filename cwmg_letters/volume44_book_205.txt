205. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 12, 1930
CHI. RUKMINI,
I have your letter. I was in fact waiting for a letter from
you. How is your health now? Didn't Vijapur agree with you?
I hope you do not worry at all about Benarsi. He is a careful man.
And ultimately it is only God who protects us.
Blessings from
BAPU
