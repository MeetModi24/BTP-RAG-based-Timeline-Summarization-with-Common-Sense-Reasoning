792. LETTER TO DEVPRAKASH NAYYAR
October 29, 1945
CHI. DEV,
I have your letter. You will be able to render a lot of service
if you remain healthy. I know that though the Ashram is
far from perfect there is something in it which is not found
elsewhere. We have deliberately given up certain things which
are seen at other places.
SEVAGRAM ASHRAM
SEVAGRAM
