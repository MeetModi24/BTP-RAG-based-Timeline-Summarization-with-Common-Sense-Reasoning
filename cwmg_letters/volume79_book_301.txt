301. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEVAGRAM,
February 28, 1945
MY DEAR C. R.,
Ramchandran read your p. c. to him. It makes bad reading.
This night fever, whatever its cause, must go. I suggest your com-
ing here as soon as possible. If s o m e o n e should go there to fetch
you that can be done. Weather is warm and dry and is likely
to be so till the middle of May. Warmer it will be day by day.
You do not mind the heat I hope.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2102
