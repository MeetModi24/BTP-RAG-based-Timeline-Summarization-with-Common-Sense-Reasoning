135. LETTER TO NARAYAN SWAMI
July 2, 1936
DEAR FRIEND,
Pressure of work has prevented me all these days from
thanking you for all the care with which you attended to the
arrangements of our stay in Nandi and Bangalore. While I
am thankful to the State for the generous hospitality extended
to me and my party, I know that our comfortable stay had not
a little to do with your careful stewardship.
Yours sincerely,
NARAYAN SWAMI
BANGALORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
