108. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
May 18, 1935
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have not a shadow of doubt that it is best for you to
settle down in some village. You should live by your body labour
and serve the villagers by thought, word and deed and to that
end observe the eleven vows. A timepiece is not a necessity; but
if you think it is, you can construct a device using sand or water.
That would involve no expense whatsoever. Yes, you have my per-
mission to visit me whenever you like, only remember that we
have no money to waste on railway journeys.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1876
