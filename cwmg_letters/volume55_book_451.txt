451. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
September 1, 1933
MY DEAR AGATHA,
C. F. Andrews has shared with me your letters to him. This
is just to press upon you the necessity of taking fair rest for the
sake of putting in more work. 'A stitch in time saves nine' has
much truth in it.
I need say nothing about my body or the situation created
by my unexpected discharge. Deenabandhu has, I know, written
to you fully about it.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Poor Mahadev! He is alone now In Belgaum prison. But
this isolation will do him good.
From a photostat: G.N. 1467
