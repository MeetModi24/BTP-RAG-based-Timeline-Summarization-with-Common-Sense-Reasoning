479. LETTER TO P. K. ELAYADAM
PATNA,
May 21, 1947
DEAR ELAYADAM,
Your letter. I have not received the money mentioned
by you. If you give me more particulars I shall make further
inquiry. You cannot live with me during my present very un-
certain life. You must shed all misgivings.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C. W. 10563
