691. LETTER TO TOTARAM SANADHYA
RANCHHOD NIVAS, AHMEDABAD,
[September 21, 1933]
DEAR TOTARAMJI,
I have your letter. Do go to Wardha and before that
anywhere else you think necessary. We shall be meeting at
Wardha this month. There is no cause to worry about
Hariprasad 2 •
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Banarsidas Chaturvedi Papers. Courtesy:
