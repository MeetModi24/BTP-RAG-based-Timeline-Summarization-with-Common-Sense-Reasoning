653. LETTER TO RATILAL SHETH
September 30, 1935
BHAI RATIBHAI,
I got your letter. May the new year be happy for you and
may your capacity for service increase. I was glad to read the
news about your family. If one can earn enough for a livelihood
through honest means, why should one covet more? If Kantilal
does his work to your satisfaction, you will be free from your
worry about him. I understand about Chhagan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 7175. Also C.W. 4671. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
