189. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
Ashadh Vad 10 [July 23, 1927]
BHAI RAMESHWARDAS,
Your letter. I am writing to Nanasaheb Deo. The venture
just undertaken is highly commendable. Nanasaheb will write
to me if any help from Vinoba or Kakasaheb is needed for this
work.
Keep Ramanama firm in your heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 184
