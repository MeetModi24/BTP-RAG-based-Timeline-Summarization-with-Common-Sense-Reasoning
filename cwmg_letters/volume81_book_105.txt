105. LETTER TO GOVERNOR OF BENGAL
SEVAGRAM,
August 2, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Shri Sudhir Ghosh kindly gave me copies of your two speeches,
one of which I finished yesterday during moments snatched from
my daily work.
I write this to draw attention to two things for the moment.
Cloth shortage you can deal with without delay by following the
policy laid down by the All-India Spinners' Association which
is represented in Bengal too. In one sentence the scheme is to
ask every home practically to spin its cotton and every village
to weave its own cloth. It is the largest co-operative effort that
can be conceived in the world.
The second is the cattle question. For that you should meet
Shri Satis Chandra Das Gupta of Khadi Pratishthan. He is ailing
and may not be available just now. He has just published a
monumental work on the question.
Shri Sudhir Ghosh has given me your message about my
visiting Bengal. I thank you for it. I am anxious to come as
early as the rains of Bengal will permit. When I come my first
business will be to give myself the privilege of meeting you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
HIS EXCELLENCY THE GOVERNOR OF BENGAL
CALCUTTA
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 103
