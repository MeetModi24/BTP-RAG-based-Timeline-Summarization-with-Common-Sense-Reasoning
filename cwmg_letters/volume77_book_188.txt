188. LETTER TO INAYATULLAH KHAN MASHRIQUI
June 18, 1944
I may say at once that nothing has been issued from this office to
say that you were coming to see Gandhiji. He certainly endorses your
view that Qaid-e-Azam is the man he should try to see. He does not re-
member the warning you gave him through the late Dr. Ansari. He has
no objection to your publishing your letter under reply. But he very much
doubts the wisdom of carrying on public correspondence of the nature of
your letter. Premature publicity robs such correspondence of its intrinsic
value and use, and lays the publisher open to the charge of self-advertise-
ment.
From File No. 51/4/44. Courtesy : National Archives of India
