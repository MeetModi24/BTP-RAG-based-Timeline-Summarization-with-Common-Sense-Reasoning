20. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
Friday, Jeth Sud 8 [June 18, 1926]
CHI. DEVDAS,
It would appear that you have taken a vow not to write.
In Bombay the rule was well observed, but from Mussoorie one
letter has been received for the whole Ashram. Eschew laziness.
If Jamnadasji cannot come here on the 26th, he should not worry.
His health is also somewhat indifferent. It is therefore imperative
that he should get perfectly fit. Aren't Lalji's [wounds] completely
healed yet?
Mathuradas has now got used to Panchgani.
