188. LETTER TO KASTURBHAI LALBHAI
BARDOLI,
May 30, 1931
BHAISHRI KASTURBHAI,
The delay in implementing the resolution of the Swadeshi Sabha to take
over the foreign goods lying with the merchants of Ahmedabad seems to me
dangerous. Cannot the matter be settled soon? I am writing a letter to some-
what the same effect to Chimanlal also.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33145
