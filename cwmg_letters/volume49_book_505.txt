505. LETTER TO MANIBEHN N. PARIKH
May 8, 1932
CHI. MANI (PARIKH),
I was glad that you came and saw me. It is true that I
could not talk with the children. But you know that this is a
prison. How long could we go on talking? You should give up
your weakness. You should not let tears be seen in your eyes.
Write to Shankarlal and tell him that I was unhappy that I
could not see him. But that could not be helped. I was very
glad that Babu also came and saw me. I could have no fun
with him either.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5968. Also C.W. 3285. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
