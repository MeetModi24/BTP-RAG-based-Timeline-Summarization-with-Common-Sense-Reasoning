118. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 5, 1945
CHI. MANI,
You did well to write. No one could have given the news
which you gave. Go and deliver the enclosed letter to Kanjibhai.
I will not say anything more as you are coming here. Narahari,
Manilal, Kamalnayan and Satyanarayan arrived yesterday.
Munshi is coming today. Kamalnayan and Munshi are here only
on a flying visit.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
SHRI MANIBEHN PATEL
C/O SHRI DAHYABHAI PATEL
68 MARINE DRIVE, BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- : Manibehn Patelne, p. 136
