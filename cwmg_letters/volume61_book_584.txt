584. LETTER TO VITHAL. L. PHADKE
September 12, 1935
CHI. MAMA,
I have thrown your article into the waste-paper basket.
There is no ban against efforts by the right type of men or
women. We cannot, however, get such workers by advertising
for them in Harijanbandhu, nor do they grow in the [Harijan
Sevak] Sangh's Office. They emerge in answer to tapascharya.
Why don't you become one?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3833
