137. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 6, 1945
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have your letter. It was good that you went to Dr. Shar-
ma's centre. My relations (financial) with him are terminated.
Chi. Hoshiari arrived here last night along with the child. Both
are well. Go to Mirabehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1966
