532. LETTER TO ZOHRA A. CHAVDA
NEW DELHI,
October 4, 1946
CHI. ZOHRA,
I have your letter. You have my letter with you. You
can make use of it in my absence so that the apparent con-
tradiction will disappear. You should therefore quietly go on
doing your work and improve your health.
Manu is at present here. Jaisukhlal too was here; he left
last night. Manu will stay on for some more time.
Write to me what you decide.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
