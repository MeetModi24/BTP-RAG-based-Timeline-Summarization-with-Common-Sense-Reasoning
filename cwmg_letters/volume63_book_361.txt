361. LETTER TO CHAND TYAGI
September 15, 1936
BHAI CHAND TYAGI,
Your letter has been lying in front of me for many days but
I could not attend to it till now. You have given me good news
about Balvir. I cannot send for him as I am striving hard to
live a village life. I do not have enough accommodation in this
village nor do I want to add to the family here. I have now
neither the strength nor the inclination to do what I could do in
places like Sabarmati, etc. My aspiration of leading a village
life can be realized only if I maintain a limited family. God
alone knows the future, assuming that the thread of my life is to
stretch farther. Rajkishori seems to have completely forgotten me.
She does not even write to me. What does she do? How much
does she spend?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6633. Also C.W. 4281. Courtesy:
