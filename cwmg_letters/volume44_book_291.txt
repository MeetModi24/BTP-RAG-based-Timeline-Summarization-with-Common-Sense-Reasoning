291. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 10, 1930
CHI. SHARDA (BABU),
I have your letter. One reason why you do not like to spin
is that you are not fully aware of the fact that through spinning
you can serve millions, or, maybe you are not as much interested
in serving them. If that is the case you should correct your atti-
tude. Another reason may be that you have still not mastered
the art of spinning. Interest is bound to develop if you could
spin fine yarn, the thread does not snap and the spinning-
wheel hums along without a jarring sound. Have you ever seen
the Andhra women spinning at an exhibition? Who would not
be interested in spinning if he could spin like them. The same
applies in the case of the Gita. You will like it if you realize its
worth.
Blessings from
BAPU
