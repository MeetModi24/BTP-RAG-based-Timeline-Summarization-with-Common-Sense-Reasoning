462. LETTER TO P. G. MATHEW
ABBOTTABAD,
July 13, 1939
MY DEAR MATHEW,
I am sorry your recovery is not so rapid as you had expect-
ed. Let this sickness teach you patience. I am here till the end
of the month at least.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
Pyarelal, Ba and Sushila join me here tomorrow.
SHRI P . G. MATHEW
GRAM UDYOG LODGE
NATH MAHAL, NAGPUR
From a photostat: G.N. 1541
