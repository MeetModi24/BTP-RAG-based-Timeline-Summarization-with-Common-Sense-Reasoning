142. LETTER TO SURESH CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
January 31, 1945
DEAR SURESH BABU,
I received your letter in three languages. Hindi would have
been sufficient. May Chi. Annapurna and her husband be happy
and may they continue to render sincere service to the country.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 8019
