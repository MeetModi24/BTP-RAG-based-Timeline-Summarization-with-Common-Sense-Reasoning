159. LETTER TO JUGLAL CHOWDHARY
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 28, 1939
MY DEAR JUGLAL,
I have a copy of your letter to Kripalani. Three years is the
limit. If Ministers can bring about prohibition in a year, they
are expected to do so. Conversely, if in spite of great effort they
cannot finish the programme within the prescribed period, they
will not be blamed. Three years should be counted from the
date of the Congress resolution. But of course the President's
ruling should be your guide.
Yours sincerely,
RON. JUGLAL CHOWDHARY
MINISTER
PATNA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
