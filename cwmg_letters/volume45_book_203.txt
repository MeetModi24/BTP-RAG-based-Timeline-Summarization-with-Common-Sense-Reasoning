203. LETTER TO SHANTA S. PATEL
February 2, 1931
CHI. SHANTA (SHANKARBHAI),
You have taken a good vow. May God give you the strength
to keep it. You have given me no news about Shankarbhai. On
hearing about his fast in connection with his demand for a takli, I
had taken steps while in jail and had heard that orders had been
issued that a takli should be supplied to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4058
