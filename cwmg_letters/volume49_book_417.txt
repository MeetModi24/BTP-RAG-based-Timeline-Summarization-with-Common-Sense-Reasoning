417. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
April 22, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
It is good to form the habit of having a bath before entering
the kitchen. But the rule cannot be applied to a person who goes
there to have a meal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9425. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
