50. LETTER TO DILKHUSH DIWANJI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 15, 1939
BHAI DILKHUSH,
Your work seems so good that I should very much like to re-
lieve you of all worry with regard to finances. But I feel that per-
haps it would be better that you progress under the strain of such
worries. In any case Lakshmidas is there by your side.
I do not have the least doubt that your weaving should be
done there only. Don't mind if it takes time to train the men.
It is necessary for the final success of khadi that all the processes
should be carried out in the same village.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DILKHUSH DIWANJI
GANDHI KUTIR
KARADI via NAVASARI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2644
