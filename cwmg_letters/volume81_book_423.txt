423. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 10, 1945
CHI. SUSHILA,
I hope you reached there comfortably. Just now there is
a telegram from Kishorelal saying that you need not go. But it
is good that you are there. Enclosed is a letter from Krishna-
varma. It is about cholera. Go through it. He has sent
some medicine. I am not forwarding the newspaper [cutting]
he has sent.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
