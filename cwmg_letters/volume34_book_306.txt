306. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
DAVANGERE,
Shravan Sud 13 [August 11, 1927]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your letter only yesterday—that is, after seven days. I
am glad that you sent me information about Chi. Kusum. If you
come to know anything more, let me hear. I have of course
written a letter to Kusum. I have not so far received a reply to
it. All of you must have been put on your mettle at the time
of the floods. I was glad to hear this time that you maintain good
health. Keep it up.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 586. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
