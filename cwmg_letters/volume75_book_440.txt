440. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
February 28, 1942
CHI. NARANDAS,
Why is Jamna getting fever? Do you need Kanaiyo? I have
sent him to learn the art of training a stronger and more powerful
body than Prithvi Singh has. He has gone for two weeks. If,
however, you need him to look after Jamna, send for him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II/489. Also C.W. 8600.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
