381. LETTER TO SWAMI RAGHAVANANDA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
September 3, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I suppose by conquest of sleep you
mean its regulation and not complete giving up of sleep. For
myself, I take and need at least six hours' sleep during 24 hours.
It is true that I take my sleep very lightly. But, if I do not take
this minimum quantity, both body and mind suffer. Complete
eradication of sex desire I hold to be a possibility and an ad-
vantage. Complete cessation of sleep I do not regard as pos-
sible or desirable. Control over sleep is attained by light diet
and freedom from exhausting physical exertion.
Yours sincerely,
SWAMI RAGHAVANANDA
VEDANTA SOCIETY
24 WEST 71ST STREET
NEW YORK CITY
(U. S. A.)
