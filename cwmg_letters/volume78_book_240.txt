240. LETTER TO P. H. GADRE
SEVAGRAM,
October 16, 1944
MY DEAR GADRE,
Incidents like what you describe will happen. You must not
mind them. You have to continue to invite visitors. The Manag-
ing Committee members may be told to be careful and tactful.
If you must come, you may come any Sunday. It is Divali today.
For financial strain, I would like you to keep yourself in touch
with Bapa.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI P. H. GADRE
NASIK
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
