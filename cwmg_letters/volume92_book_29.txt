29. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI
April 10, 1929
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I expect a lot from you. It is good that you are looking
after the nursery school. Give good thought to it. Bathe the
children in love. Then their intelligence will automatically
grow. They are your own children. Why should you not feel
love for them? Then jealousy and envy will automatically vanish.
Lust and wanton thoughts will not come anywhere near us.
Surely this is simple enough to understand?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari - Pannalal Jhaveri p. 41
