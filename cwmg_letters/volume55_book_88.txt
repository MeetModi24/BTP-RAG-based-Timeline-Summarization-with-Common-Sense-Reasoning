88. LETTER TO MADAN MOHAN MALAVIYA
May 2, 1933
BHAI SAHEB,
It is getting on to 2 in the morning. Do send me your
blessings for the coming yajna if you can. I am only trying to
do what I learnt from my parents since childhood. Mother
spent almost half her life in fasting. What can I do? I am
unable to serve the cause of the Harijans through intellect alone.
Your younger brother,
MOHANDAS
From Hindi: C.W. 9668
