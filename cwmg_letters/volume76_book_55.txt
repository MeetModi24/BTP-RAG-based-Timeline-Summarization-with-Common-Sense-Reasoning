55. LETTER TO A. S. PATWARDHAN
SEVAGRAM,
April 15, 1942
BHAI PATWARDHAN,
I am definitely of the opinion that Bhayya should form a
trust for the money as early as possible and an arbitrator should
be appointed to advise on its utilization.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
