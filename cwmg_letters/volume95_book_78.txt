78. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Silence day [After August 20, 1927]
CHI. VASUMATI,
Received your letter. It is good that you have started teaching.
I did not mean that you should teach Gujarati only to the extent that
you know it. I want much greater knowledge of perfect Gujarati. I
expect a deeper study of grammar. But let that be. Teaching too is
