37. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
June 14, 1932
CHI. NARANDAS,
After I had dispatched the post yesterday, I got a letter from
Mahavir in which he says that he has not received the money.
He has estimated the expenditure at Rs. 15 per head. You may
decide what it should be. Maitri informs me that she has not
the slightest wish to marry just now.
Personally, I think we should let them stay in Darjeeling as
long as they wish.
BAPU
CHI. NARANDAS GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C. W. 8233. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
