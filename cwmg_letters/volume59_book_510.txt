510. LETTER TO CHOITHRAM GIDWANI
December 8, 1934
MY DEAR CHOITHRAM,
I have your letter. I am not sure that any propaganda
will be of any service to you.
In any case you cannot have
Khan Saheb now. He is under arrest. Remember we do not
want to court arrest just now. We have to become speechless
manual labourers working in the villages. Speech-making must
be reserved for the few in the Assembly and therefore it must
be confined to a few. I hope you are keeping yourself fit.
Yours,
BAPU
