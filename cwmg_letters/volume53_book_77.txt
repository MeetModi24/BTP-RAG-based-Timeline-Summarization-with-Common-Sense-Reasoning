77. LETTER TO K. PARAMESWARAN NAMBOODIRI
January 16, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and offer of the sacred oil. Believ-
ing as I do firmly in true Shastras not only tolerating but per-
mitting Harijans of today to enter temples, I could not avail
myself of the offer you kindly made. If you will investigate
the Shastras, you will fail to discover a single text that would
apply to those whose entry today is debarred.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. PARAMESWARAN NAMBOODIRI
OTHIKKEN
C/O TEMPLE SUPERINTENDENT
GURUVAYUR
From a microfilm: S.N. 18937
