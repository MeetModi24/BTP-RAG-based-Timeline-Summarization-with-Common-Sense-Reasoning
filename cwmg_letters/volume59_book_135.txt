135. LETTER TO B. SUBBANNA
October 3, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I am grateful to you for the beautiful casket which I have
just received. I shall try to get the best price possible for it and
use the proceeds for the khadi and Harijan service.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI B. SUBBANNA, RETIRED JUDGE
BASAVANGUDI
BANGALORE CITY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
