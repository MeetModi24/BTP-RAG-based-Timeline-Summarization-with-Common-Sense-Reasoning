155. LETTER TO CHOITHRAM GIDWANI
[Before November 6, 1940]
The sole concern of Congressmen in Sind should be to rid the
province of the terror that is raging there.
If the Congressmen
of Sind cannot do that, they had better retire from public life.
The Hindu, 9-11-1940
1 & 2 The addressee was President, Sind Provincial Congress Committee.
The letter was reported under the date-line "Sukkur, November 6".
