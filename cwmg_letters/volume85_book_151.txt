151. LETTER TO ADVAIT KUMAR GOSWAMI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C. P.,
August 10, 1946
BHAI ADVAIT KUMAR GOSWAMI,
Gandhiji has received both your letters. He does not send any mes-
sages. It is good that Raja Mahendra Pratap is coming back. You will all
be pleased.
About South Africa Gandhiji feels that none of us should go there.
They won't allow the party to land at all.
Yours,
AMRIT KAUR
From a photostat of the Hindi
G. N. 805
