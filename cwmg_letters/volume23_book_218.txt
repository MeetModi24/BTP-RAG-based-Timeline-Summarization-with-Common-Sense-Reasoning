218. LETTER TO DR. SATYAPAL
POST ANDHERI,
March 25, 1924
DEAR DR. SATYAPAL,
I was glad to receive your letter giving me so much information
about tension between Hindus and Muslims. I await the second
instalment regarding Sikhs and Hindus with interest. I know
that the problem before the country is very serious and very
complex, and in our ability to solve it satisfactorily and perma-
nently lies the road to swaraj. I have been thinking over it day
and night ever since my release, and as soon as I have seen the
leaders, I shall begin to write upon it.
I thank you for your inquiry about my health. I am making
steady progress. I observe you are not now in Amritsar, but in
Lahore. Why the change?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. SATYAPAL
LAHORE
