473. LETTER TO SATYAVATI
SEVAGRAM,
March 20, 1945
CHI. SATYAVATI1,
I do have news of you. Pyarelal also gave me some. It is
good that you are in Harijan Nivas. You must get well. Chand
is somewhat agitated. She has received so much from you. Must
she not now serve you? The thought is noble. If you need
Chand's services, I will send her immediately. She is learning
to nurse the sick here. But nursing you will be the proper train-
ing. However, if you do not need Chand's services, then I do
not see any point in sending her merely so that you can look
at each other. Let me know your mind.
Khurshedbehn left a few days ago. She is in Poona with
Nargisbehn.
The heat has started here. I do not therefore have the
courage to send for you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
