338. LETTER TO VINA CHATTERJEE
August 31, 1945
CHI. VINA,
I have your letter after a long time. I think you should
notify the court. I have written to Naraharibhai. It is better
to get married soon. Be satisfied with the registration in the
court. Show this also to Naraharibhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Be very good and render plenty of service.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
