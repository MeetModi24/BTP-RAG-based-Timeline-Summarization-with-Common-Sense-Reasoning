530. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
September 16, 1941
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I am positive that the Indians living in Burma should pro-
test strongly against the Agreement signed by the two Govern-
ments. They should have no fear in this matter. We can have
no quarrel with the people of Burma. We may live among them
only with their goodwill.
But who is to ascertain their view?
And also who is to decide how it should be ascertained?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4736. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
