133. LETTER TO DHIRU
SIMLA,
May 6, 1946
CHI. DHIRU,
I got your letter. I am glad that you are doing a lot of
work. If Dr. Bhagwat dispenses pills and so on, one cannot
call it nature cure. It would be more polite if you settled the
matter in consultation with Dr. Bhagwat himself. For my part
I am prepared to write to him. But where work proceeds harmo-
niously it is better to have mutual discussions.
I shall be here for another few days. Then we shall see.
Our entire party has left for Delhi. It was necessary for the work
I have to do here.
Blessings from
BAPU
DHIRU
URULI KANCHAN
