550. LETTER TO PURNIMA BANERJEE
PANCHGANI,
June 14, 1945
CHI. PURNIMA,
What you say is entirely correct. But in the present circum-
stances, how can Aruna stay back to nurse Asaf Ali? It is very
difficult to reconcile service to the country and service to an indi-
vidual. Very often the two are contradictory. They have both
dedicated their lives to the country. I therefore hope that they
will be able to bear the present separation cheerfully. I am
keeping in touch with Asaf Ali. I hope that he will get well.
Hope your work is getting on well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. PURNIMA BANERJEE
41 GEORGE TOWN
ALLAHABAD
