285. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL G. BANKER
ON ARRIVAL AT WADI
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I keep worrying about you. Though unwittingly, I have hurt you
no doubt. Its remedy lies in fortitude. You must contain your anger.
Anandanand is not so bad as you seem to think. But even if he is bad,

you cannot abandon him altogether as long as he continues to be my
colleague. You must agree to this much. You can be modest because
you have that power. The rich can always be humble and modest.
They have nothing at all to lose thereby. But right now, all I ask is
that you should be calm. I feel sad when you feel hurt.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a copy of the Gujarati : S. N. 32687
