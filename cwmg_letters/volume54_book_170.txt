170. LETTER TO SUDARSHAN V. DESAI
March 21, 1933
CHI. MAVO,
I have your letter. You should learn to draw the letters.
You can do that only after you have learnt to draw straight lines,
triangles and circles since drawing letters is more difficult than
drawing those three figures. How can I explain this to you con-
vincingly?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5764. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
