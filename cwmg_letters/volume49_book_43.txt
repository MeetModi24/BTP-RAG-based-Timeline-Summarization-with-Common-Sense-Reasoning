43. LETTER TO VASUDEV
January 23, 1932
BHAI VASUDEV,
I have your letter. The soul is ennobled by self-control.
The true nature of the soul consists in a passionless state. That is
why control over the emotions is imperative.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 8903-a. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
