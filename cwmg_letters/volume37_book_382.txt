382. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
October 8, 1928
CHI. MIRA,
After I wrote to you at Almora, I got your wire. I am how-
ever letting that letter go to its destination. It is fairly long but
there is nothing much in it.
Malarial fever is still with us. Chhotelal's bakery is ready.
He has been making experiments.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5311. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 8201
