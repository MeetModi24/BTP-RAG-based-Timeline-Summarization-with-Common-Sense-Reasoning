318. LETTER TO RAMESHWARLAL BAZAJ
October 4, 1932
BHAI RAMESRWARDAS,
Chi. Purnachandra has sent envelopes with your address. My
fast is an old story now. My strength is returning, you need have
no anxiety.
Have you got any returns from the new enterprise? Do write
to me all details.
My earlier letter must have reached you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 9448. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
