367. LETTER TO PREMA KANTAK
July 23, 1942
CHI. PREMA,
I have your letter. May your aspirations be fulfilled.
Everything is covered by this.
Meet me in Bombay and, if you are not satisfied with
that you may accompany me, if at all I come back. "Enjoy
yourself today, for who has seen tomorrow?"
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10429. Also C.W. 6868. Cour-
tesy: Prema Kantak
