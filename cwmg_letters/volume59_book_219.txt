219. LETTER TO J. N. SAHNI
October 14, 1934
MY DEAR SAHNI 1 ,
If you can draw blood out of stone, you can draw messages
out of me. As for encouragement for yourself, if my life provides
none to your liking, then nothing that comes through the pen
will be of any use.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI SAHNI
"NATIONAL CALL"
DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
