62. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 2, 1931
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
I have your letter. I had also got your letter describing your
difficulties at Pij, and replied to it. I hope you received my
reply.
You have done well in confessing everything. In my opinion,
you should certainly not have physical relations with your wife.
You should make some arrangement for her education so that
her intellect may develop. If you are firm in your decision to
have no physical relations with her, you should plainly tell her
about that so that she may not remain discontented and may
not go on hoping. You should also let her marry again if she
wishes to do so. You should not do that if deep in your heart
you crave for physical gratification. In any case, you should make
it clear to her that you cannot satisfy her as long as the present
movement lasts. Since she is poor, you should bear the burden of
maintaining her. You should ask her to go and live in the
Ashram. The remedy against attacks of lustful thoughts is to de-
vote oneself to work. How can a person who is always absorbed
in work ever get time for such thoughts?
Blessings from
BAPU
