505. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 11, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
SO you are not yet out of the wood. What is this rheuma-
tism of the mouth? Why should you have it at all? There
must be deeper cause. How I wish you allowed yourself to be
overhauled in Delhi in Hardinge College Hospital. But I sup-
pose Shummy won't listen to it. I would suggest Bombay as
an alternative. Sardar is now under a homoeopath and is bet-
ter. The chief thing is to get well.
Here are two letters from your pupils.
Did I tell you Sailen had returned? I have put him on
Hindi and charkha. He has not wasted his time in Nagpur.
He has become more amenable to discipline.
I must inquire about the apples.
Annapurna has gone to Nagpur to be examined by Dr. David.
Rajendra Babu has not yet come to Sevagram.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W.4070. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7379
