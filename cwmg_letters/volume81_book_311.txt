311. LETTER TO PARACHURE SHASTRI
August 29, 1945
SHASTRIJI,
I have your letter. Do not go anywhere. Stay in Dattapur
and end your life there. You are rendering service there.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PARACHURE SHASTRI
DATTAPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
