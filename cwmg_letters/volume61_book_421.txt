421. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
July 29, 1935
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
If you have booked for 17th, you have to leave Santiniketan
fairly soon. Or else there will be a rush at the last moment. A day
with Verrier is quite right but you have hardly time to go to
Kodai. Esther's is a tough problem. My opinion is that Menon
should stick to India but not to Tanjore. Real skill has its price
everywhere.
. . .1ember Dina Babu . . .2ay give my . . .3d condolences
to Gurudev.
Mrs. Ambalal is in Ahmedabad. They returned only the other
day. She is well.
It would be a good thing if you can write something sub-
stantial about the Congress for the European reader. But then it
would have to be independent and authorship exclusively yours.
You can't tack yourself on to Girija or anybody else.
[PS.]
Did I tell you I wrote to Sudhir at once?
What about Aprakash?
From a photostat: G.N. 991
