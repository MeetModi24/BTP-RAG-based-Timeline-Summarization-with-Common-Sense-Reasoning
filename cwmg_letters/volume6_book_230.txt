230. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO CHURCHILL
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 21, 1906
G. C. WILLIAMS, ESQ.
THE PRIVATE SECRETARY TO
THE UNDER-SECRETARY OF STATE FOR THE COLONIES
THE COLONIAL OFFICE
DOWNING STREET
DEAR SIR,
I shall be obliged if you will kindly convey to Mr. Winston Churchill
the thanks of Mr. Ally and myself for consenting to receive us at the
Colonial Office. We shall wait 1 on Mr. Churchill at 12 noon on the
27th instant.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4619
