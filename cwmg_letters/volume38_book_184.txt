184. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
December 4, 1928
MY DEAR PADMAJA,
You must not mind this dictated letter. It is better that I
dictate than that I delay writing to you. What on earth are you
doing with your health? Is it not more your mind that is at
fault? Why can't you make up your mind to be and remain
healthy? This set-back in your health is bound to trouble the
poor old songstress in America. You must become a good
daughter.
SHRIMATI PADMAJA NAIDU
HYDERABAD
From a photostat: S.N. 13013
