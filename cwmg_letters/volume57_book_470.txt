470. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
April 30, 1934
BHAI MOOLCHAND,
I have found Swami Dayanand quite different from his writing.
The work he did has great value for me. I stick to my views
about Satyarthaprakash.
I have read it from a religious view-
point. It shows an imperfect knowledge of other religions, which
have not been seen with the eyes of their votaries. But I do
not wish to enter into a discussion on this. I hold the Arya Samaj
in great esteem.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 840
