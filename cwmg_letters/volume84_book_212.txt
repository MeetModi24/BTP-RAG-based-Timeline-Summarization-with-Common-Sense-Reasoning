212. LETTER TO SIR STAFFORD CRIPPS
VALMIKI MANDIR,
READING ROAD,
NEW DELHI,
May 19, 1946
DEAR SIR STAFFORD,
I promised to send you a copy of the summary of my speech
to the prayer gathering last evening. This I do now herewith.
I enclose herewith a translation of the hymn of the previous
evening. Of course you will share it with Lord Pethick-Lawrence
or whomsoever you like.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 191
