272. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL P. NANAVATI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Tuesday, Asadha Vad 10, August 3, 1926
BHAISHRI CHHAGANLAL,
I have your letter. For the present, I am having your
amount credited to the scholarship account. I am sure we shall
get someone with those qualifications.
SHRI CHHAGANLAL P. NANAVATI
NAVASARI BUILDING
HORNBY ROAD
FORT
BOMBAY
