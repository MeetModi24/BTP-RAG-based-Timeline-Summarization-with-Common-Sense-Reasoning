265. LETTER TO ANNAPOORNA C. MEHTA
May 21, 1945
CHI. ANNAPOORNA,
I got your postcard yesterday. How can I believe that you
are 25? I saw you as a mere child. You have yet to live for
many years and do much work.
I will wear the khadi dhoti when I get it. There is no
hurry. Is the word prandai or prandayi?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9437
