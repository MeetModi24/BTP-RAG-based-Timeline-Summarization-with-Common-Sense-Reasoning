31. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
DELHI,
Maha Sud 8 [February 1, 1925]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I have your letter. I am trying to get a better charkha for
you. While at the charkha, one can repeat Ramanama as well.
There is the case of two learned men who saved themselves
from [an attack of] madness only by repeating Ramanama while
plying the charkha. Ultimately, things happen to us according
to our aspirations.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6103. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
