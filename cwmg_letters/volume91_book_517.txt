517. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Friday [August 27, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your telegram and letter. There is no question of my
being hurt because you have said no. My duty was to ask you
and your duty was to say no under the conditions obtaining
there.
Pattani Saheb came yesterday. I have requested him to make
his bungalow available. If no other bungalow is available we
shall take his. I shall see whether or not he will take rent.
I told him that in case no other bungalow was available we
intended to have his bungalow as there was no other alter-
native. I asked him frankly whether or not he would take rent.
He said he would like to take rent as otherwise the place could
not be maintained.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
HOMI VILLA
PANCHGANI
