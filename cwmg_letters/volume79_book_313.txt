313. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 1, 1945
CHI. NARANDAS,
If you cannot honourably take the money for Chi. Purushot-
tam's monthly expenses from there, you may obtain it from me.
Let me know what it will come to per month. Take care of your
health. Chi. Kanaiyo and Abha may be going that way shortly.
If they do, Kanaiyo will bring you another letter from me.
Letters from him will continue as of now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II/508. Also C.W. 8620.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
1 Addressee's son
2 Kanu Gandhi's wife
