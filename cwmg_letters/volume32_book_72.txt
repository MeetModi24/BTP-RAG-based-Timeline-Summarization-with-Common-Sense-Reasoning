72. LETTER TO F. W. PETHICK-LAWRENCE
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
November 24, 1926
DEAR MR. LAWRENCE,
I was delighted to receive your letter. Friends had really
prepared me for receiving you and Mrs. Lawrence at the Ashram
and I was looking forward to your visit here. I am sorry you
were not able to come. I shall certainly be delighted to meet you
at Gauhati if I come there. There is just a likelihood of my being
unable to attend the forthcoming Congress. I shall know more
fully during the second week of December.
I shall be in Wardha near Nagpur between 4th and 20th
December.
I, indeed, remember having had the privilege of lunching with
you in London when I was there on the South African Deputation.
Yours sincerely,
F . W . PETHICK-LAWRENCE, ESQ.
C/O GREAT EASTERN HOTEL
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 19747
