420. LETTER TO KALYANJI MEHTA
BOMBAY,
July 26, 1919
BHAISHRI KALYANJI,
I clean forgot to write to you. I will start tomorrow (Sunday)
and be definitely there. I shall travel by the passenger train
which arrives there at 6 or thereabouts. If it is not necessary, don't
keep me for two days. You may, though, if absolutely necessary.
For the moment, I leave the matter at that.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 2670
