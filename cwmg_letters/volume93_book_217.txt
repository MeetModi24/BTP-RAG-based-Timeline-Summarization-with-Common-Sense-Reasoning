217. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
CALCUTTA,
November 14, 1937
MY DEAR LOTUS-BORN,
I was not going to write to you just yet. I have been only
dictating the most necessary correspondence. But the imperious
singer won't rest till I promised to let you have a line. For I
am told you are visibly growing in years and let us hope in
wisdom too. Your birthday is nearing! May it find you full of
health, fun and wisdom.
Love.
SLAVE-DRIVER
SHRI PADMAJA NAIDU
GoLDEN THRESHOLD
HYDERABAD, DECCAN
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
