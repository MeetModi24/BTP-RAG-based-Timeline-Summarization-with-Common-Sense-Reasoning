104. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 10, 1941
CHI. TARAMATI,
I have received your letter. Mathuradas is a pious man. God will
do good in every way. Patients with much worse sickness have
recovered. Mathuradas has taken a lot of physical strain. Hence, he
may take longer to recover. But there will be nothing to worry about
once his health takes a turn for the better. I shall be happy even if
he has to spend the winter at Wardha. But all that depends on the
will of God. Let us be content to live in God's keeping.
Blessings from
BAPU
