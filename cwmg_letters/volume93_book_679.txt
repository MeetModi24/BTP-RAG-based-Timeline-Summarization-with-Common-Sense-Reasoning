679. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 3, 1941
DEAR SLAVE (BY CHOICE),
You deserve the name for you have slaved as only you
could have to make the whole function the success it was. You
have been a friend indeed for you have been that in need.
God bless you. You must not grow stouter.
What about Jawaharlal?
Love.
BAPU
SHRI PADMAJA NAIDU
ANAND BHAWAN
ALLAHABAD (U. P.)
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
