459. LETTER TO EASTERN STEAM NAVIGATION CO. LTD.
November 23, 1934
GENTLEMEN,
I am now able to tell you that the forty-two packages of dates
sent by goods train were safely received day before yesterday.
Yours sincerely,
THE EASTERN STEAM NAVIGATION CO., LTD.
24 MANGALORE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
