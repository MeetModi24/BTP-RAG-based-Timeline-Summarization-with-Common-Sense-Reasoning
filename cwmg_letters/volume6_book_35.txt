35. LETTER TO S. KRISHNAVARMA
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 29, 1906
DEAR PANDIT SHYAMJI KRISHNAVARMA,
I enclose herewith stamps for 1s 6d which you were good enough
to advance last evening.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
Enclosure
PANDIT SHYAMJI KRISHNAVARMA
9, QUEEN'S WOOD AVENUE
HIGHGATE
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4415
