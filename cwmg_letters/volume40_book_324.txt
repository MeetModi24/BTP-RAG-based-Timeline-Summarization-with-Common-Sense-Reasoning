324. LETTER TO GANGADEVI SANADHYA
Silence Day [On or before May 6, 1929]
CHI. GANGADEVI,
How is your health now? Take nothing but milk and fruit.
You must not at all exert yourself. Write to me. Tell Totaram
too to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
