147. LETTER TO TRIVENI J. MEHTA
February 18, 1932
CHI. TRIVENI,
I got your letter. There is no fixed mould for education. It
consists in the purity of one's love. A mother spoils her child if
she regards it as hers. If, instead, she brings it up as a trust
received from God, the child soon learns to behave well. I am
writing on this subject in today's letter for the women. Read it
and think over it. Instead of paying attention to the weaknesses
of others, we should try to become better ourselves.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TRIVENI JAGJIVANDAS MEHTA
SABARMATI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 70. Also C.W. 8941. Courtesy:
