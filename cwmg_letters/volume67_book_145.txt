145. LETTER TO DR. SYED MAHMUD
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 2, 1938
DEAR SYED MAHMUD,
You are a long time coming. If you come now, I won't
be able to provide for you even the comforts you would need
for your health. You must now let the rainy season pass.
Hope your drive against illiteracy is making rapid pro-
gress.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: Syed Mahmud Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Also G.N. 5111
