274. LETTER TO Y. M. PARNERKAR
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
August 7, 1927
MY DEAR PARNERKAR,
I enclose herewith two letters for your file and note. When
you can get the time I would like you to give me notes contain-
ing the information enabling me to prepare the article of the kind
Mr. William Smith wants.
I hope that you are now thoroughly acclimatized and that
both of you are keeping well and fit.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. PARNERKAR
DAIRY
ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a microfilm: S.N. 12922
