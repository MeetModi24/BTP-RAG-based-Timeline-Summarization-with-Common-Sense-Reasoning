442. LETTER TO PADMA
June 8, 1931
CHI. PADMA,
I have your letter. It is good news indeed to hear that your
health is improving steadily. Have you now given up the idea of
going to U.P. ? Take plenty of rest and get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6120. Also C.W. 3472. Courtesy:
Prabhudas Gandhi
