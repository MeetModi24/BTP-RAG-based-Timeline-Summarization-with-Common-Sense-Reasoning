76. LETTER TO JAMES T. RUTNAM
BORSAD,
May 3, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I am publishing your letter and a brief reply to it in the
forthcoming issue of Young India . I hope that the reply will
satisfy you and those friends who had any misgivings about my
attitude.
Yours sincerely,
JAMES T. RUTNAM, ESQ.
ST. XAVIER's
NUWARA ELIYA (CEYLON)
From a microfilm: S.N. 17028
