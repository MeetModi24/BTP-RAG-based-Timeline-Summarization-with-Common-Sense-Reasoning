59. LETTER TO WILFRED WELLOCK
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
February 26, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I am sending you an autographed volume of the so-called
autobiography. You will be interested to know that all the bound
volumes are bound in khaddar and every rupee invested in khad-
dar means at least twelve annas into the pockets directly of the
poorest people.
Yours sincerely,
WILFRED WELLOCK, ESQ.
VICTORIA AVENUE, QUINTON
BIRMINGHAM
From a photostat: S.N. 14250
