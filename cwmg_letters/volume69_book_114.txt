114. LETTER TO NARAYANI DEVI
NEW DELHI,
March 31, 1939
DEAR SISTER,
Keeping in view the present situation I think the satya-
graha, or the preparations for it, going on in the Indian States
should be suspended. It would be good, therefore, if in Mewar
too the satyagraha was suspended. Constructive work must of
course go on and what I write these days should be studied.
Yours,
M. K . GANDHI
SHRIMATI NARAYANI DEVI 2
MEWAR
