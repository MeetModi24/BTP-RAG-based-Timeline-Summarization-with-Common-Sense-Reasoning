277. LETTER TO VIDYA A. HINGORANI
November 20, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
Why don't you write? What sentence did Anand get? Are
your studies progressing well? And your cooking? Don't be
disheartened. Know that there is virtue in eating what one has
cooked with one's own hands. Have you secured a sewing
machine? Is the [sewing] class going on? How is Mahadev?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Ask Kanta as also the other girls to write.
From a microfilm of the Hindi: Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
