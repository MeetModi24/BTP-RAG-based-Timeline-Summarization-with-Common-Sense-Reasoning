339. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
August 7, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
The story relating to the significance of the ekadashi is interest-
ing. But we observe the ekadashi in a different manner.
For us, the ekadashi should be a voluntary vow observed both
physically and mentally, and its aim should be self-purification.
When a person who is ill does not eat, his aim is to cure his ill-
ness and not to observe a fast. Similarly, a poor man also who
does not eat because he has no food does not observe a fast thereby.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
