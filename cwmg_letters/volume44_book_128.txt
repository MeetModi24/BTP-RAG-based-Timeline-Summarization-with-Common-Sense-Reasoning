128. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 18, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. With the conditions prevailing in the
country I do not feel like taking dry fruit unless necessary. I have
no time to write more today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3427
