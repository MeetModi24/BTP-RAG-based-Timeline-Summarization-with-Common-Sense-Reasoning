609. LETTER TO JAGANNATH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 8, 1941
MY DEAR JAGANNATH,
You have told me a sorry tale. Why don't you see the Editor?
I cannot issue any statement. Of course, if Harijan was published,
I could deal with all such things.
Yes, Mahadev told me everything. When Rajkumari returns,
I shall see what can be done.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10349. Courtesy: Jagannath
