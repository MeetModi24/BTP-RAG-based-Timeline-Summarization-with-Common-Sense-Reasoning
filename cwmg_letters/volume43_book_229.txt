229. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
DURING DANDI MARCH,
[April 11, 1930]
CHI. NARANDAS,
I cannot choose the names. Only those women who wish
to take up these two activities and whom you can spare need come.
You alone can think and decide in this matter. Prompted by
love, everyone may wish to come. That is a different matter.
We have no choice but to exercise self-control in this matter.
Brijkrishna and Krishnadas have come here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 8099. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
