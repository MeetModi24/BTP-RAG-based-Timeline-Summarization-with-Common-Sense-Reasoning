74. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 13, 1940
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have already written to you about this matter. I have
written to him too. I suppose you know that Nanabhai is
connected with this. Just now we shall have to hand him
Rs. 2,000. We shall manage it. I am writing to him in detail;
you should too.
I have not yet read the note about Chandrashanker. I will
do something if I can. What happened at Rajkot?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 240
