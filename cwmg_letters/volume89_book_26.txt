26. LETTER TO SIR FREDERICK BURROWS
SODEPUR,
August 11, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
What you could not do, a big Muslim deputation was able
to do yesterday. And so I am here at least till tomorrow. Man
is veritably clay in the hands of the great Potter.
All my good wishes with you and yours.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR FREDERICK BURROWS
H. E. THE GOVERNOR
CALCUTTA
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 277
