464. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
February 15, 1938
CHI. LILA,
I write to you every day. But how is it that there is no-
thing from you? The blood-pressure was 174-106 in the morn-
ing and 160-96 in the afternoon. I have started taking garlic
from today. How is your health, mental as well as physical?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 396
