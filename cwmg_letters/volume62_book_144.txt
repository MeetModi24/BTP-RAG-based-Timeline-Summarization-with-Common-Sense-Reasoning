144. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
WARDHA,
November 17, 1935
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
Send the accompanying letter to Kavitha if you approve of
it. If you receive any names of persons intending to migrate from
the place, see Gulzarilal and fix them up in some mill for the
present. Write to me if you encounter any difficulty.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4034. Also C.W. 133. Courtesy:
Parikshitlal L. Majmudar
