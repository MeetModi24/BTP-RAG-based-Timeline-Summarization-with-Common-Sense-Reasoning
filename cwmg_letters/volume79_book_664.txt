664. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
April 17, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I got your letter. Ramprasad is to be sent to Mirabehn.
You already have work in hand. One person at a time should
look after the kitchen by turns. Do not go on discussing things.
The President after listening to everything should decide either
through consensus or a majority vote or at his own discretion.
Everything is imperfect as long as the arrangement does not
function smoothly.
What you say about the sum of Rs. 25 is correct. It is
not for you to see under which head it is accounted. Keeping it
in the Hospital account seems all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 5944. Courtesy: Munnalal G.
Shah
