484. LETTER TO ATULANAND CHAKRAVARTY
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1944
DEAR ATULANAND,
Do be merciful to me. Don't ask me to read anything and
don't seek my opinion. Of course you must pursue your mission,
come what will.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
