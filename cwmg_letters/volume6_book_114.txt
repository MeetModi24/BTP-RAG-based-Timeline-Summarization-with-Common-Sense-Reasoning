114. LETTER TO DR. J. OLDFIELD
HOTEL CECIL,
[LONDON,]
November 6, 1906
MY DEAR OLDFIELD,
Will you kindly take the bearer's case in hand. His name is
A. Tanzi. He is working as a waiter in this Hotel. He has evidently
been suffering from rheumatism for three months in the left hand. I
shall thank you to charge him the poor fee, and let me know it.
Yours sincerely,
DR. JOSIAH OLDFIELD
2A, HARLEY STREET
PORTLAND PLACE
CAVENDISH SQUARE, W.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4498
