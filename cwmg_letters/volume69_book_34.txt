34. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
March 6, 1939
CHI. NARANDAS,
Please examine Kanaiyo's method of keeping accounts and
give your opinion on it as an expert; if you find it necessary to
teach him account-keeping, please do so. The walls and doors
in the school are dirty and there are cobwebs. The walls must
be cleaned and the cobwebs, etc., removed.
The walls should be whitewashed up to four or five feet.
Start the work immediately from tomorrow.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8556. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
