76. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 4, 1931
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
It is surprising that there is no letter from you for the last
two weeks. This must not happen. Even if there is nothing
worth writing it will do if you send a few words about your
welfare. How are Charu and Arun? When are Satisbabu and
Kshitishbabu to be released?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1680
