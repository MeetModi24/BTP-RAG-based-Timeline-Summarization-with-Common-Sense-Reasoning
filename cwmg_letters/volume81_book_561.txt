561. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
October 4, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have your letter, also Kanchan's. One way for you is to
become completely passionless, like Shiva-and win over Kanchan
to your view but not with an effort. But this is beyond your
capacity. You have, therefore, no other option but to start a
separate establishment. You must satisfy Kanchan. This is your
dharma. I regard it as your first duty. Doing it will answer all
your questions. You must have read the letter that Kanchan wrote
to me. If not, ask her. I tore it up.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8362 and 8430
