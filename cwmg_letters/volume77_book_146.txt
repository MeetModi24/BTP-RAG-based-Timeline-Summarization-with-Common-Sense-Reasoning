146. LETTER TO GULZARILAL NANDA
[After May 27, 1944]
CHI. GULZARILAL,
I read your letter to Pyarelal. Dr. Das visits me from time
to time. Dr. Chugh has not impressed me, Dr. Das has. He
does not propose to administer any medicine to me. By all means
bring over anyone you like from among the names that you sug-
gest. I wish I could have faith in homoeopathy and biochemic
medicines, but I don't. Now I am thinking of taking allopathic
drugs to get rid of the hook-worms and the amœbae. Your work
seems to be going on well. Can one say that you have completely
recovered?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
