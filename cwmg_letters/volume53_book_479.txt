479. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA N. KHARE
February 19, 1933
CHI. RAMACHANDRA,
I got your letter. You should always write what you do
after careful thought. Anybody who wants to be scrupulously
truthful would not utter or write even one word without care-
fully weighing it. I had got confused by what you wrote. Now
everything is clear.
You write Jai Jai to Sardar and Pranams to Mahadevbhai
and Chhaganbhai. Explain the difference between the two. What
does Jai Jai mean? Does it mean the same thing as Jaya Jaya?
A person like me can afford to make a mistake in spelling a word.
You boys and girls should always write correct Gujarati.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati; C.W. 305. Courtesy; Lakshmibehn
N. Khare
