244. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
September 23, 1947
CHI. NARANDAS,
This statement seems all right. May your desire be fulfilled.
It should, however, be remembered that this yajna must not be
felt as a hardship by anybody. Its importance lies in the largest
number joining it. But till that time comes, all those who join
it at present should put all their strength behind it, provided of
course that nobody works so hard that he or she gets exhausted
by the effort.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 8645. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
