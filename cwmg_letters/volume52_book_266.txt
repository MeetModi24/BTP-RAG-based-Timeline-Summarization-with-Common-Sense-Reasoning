266. LETTER TO A. RANGASWAMI IYENGAR
December 15, 1932
MY DEAR RANGASWAMI,
I have your letter enclosing a Session Judge's commentary
on the present agitation. I read it through. It gave me pain to
find that a judge could lose balance of mind over reform carried
on as it is with the greatest restraint and many limitations. The
letter is one more proof to show that the reform was overdue.
In some shape or form the agitation for temple-entry and comp-
lete removal of untouchability as It is being practised today has
been on for years, and I am glad that the proposed fast has put
life into it.
I hope that your wife is now thoroughly restored.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. A. RANGASWAMI IYENGAR
THE HINDU OFFICE
MOUNT ROAD, MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 18712
