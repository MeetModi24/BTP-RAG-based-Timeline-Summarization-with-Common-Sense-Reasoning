368. LETTER TO SHANTA S. PATEL
Monday Night, WARDHA
CHI. SHANTA,
Since you have written me two letters, I too must write. Now
that you are studying and spinning, you will have to be called a good
girl. Be such a good girl always. Write letters to me. You must
always write with good spacing and clearly.
Do not take chillies.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9418
