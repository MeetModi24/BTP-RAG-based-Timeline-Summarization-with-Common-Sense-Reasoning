518. LETTER TO UTTAMCHAND GANGARAM
SEVAGRAM,
March 25, 1945
MY DEAR UTTAMCHAND,
Your letter. Many thanks for not insisting on re-conversion.
The capital will be left untouched. Do please supplement the
interest from time to time. The interest will not be allowed to
fall below 3½ p. c. If it does, you shall be consulted. Unless things
go topsyturvy, it shall not go below 3½%.
I do hope you will keep better. Have you consulted any
naturopath? If Mahadev were alive and you had sent him
money, he would have instructed me and done according to my
wish. I shall see if anybody can make anything of the puzzle.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
UTTAMCHAND GANGARAM
BOMBAY BAKERY
HYDERABAD, SIND
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
