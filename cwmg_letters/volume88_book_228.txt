228. LETTER TO K. KELAPPAN
[Before July 2, 1947]
It is very good that all temples have been opened. I agree
that opening of temples in Cochin also is now necessary. Now
there ought to be reform of temples, as in every way the
condition of Harijans will be exactly like that of savarna Hindus.
For this work why should Pracharakas be sent from this side?
They may be prepared there only. Why not you yourself?
BAPU
The Hindu, 4-7-1947
