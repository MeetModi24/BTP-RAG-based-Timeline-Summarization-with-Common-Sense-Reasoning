302. LETTER TO M
December 16, 1929
CHI. M,
It is not wrong to refuse to undertake a task, but it is alto-
gether wrong to leave off a task once begun. Now that you have
vowed to write the diary, it should not be discontinued. As spin-
ning is a sacrificial activity for us, we ought to learn it properly.
Write to me from time to time.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4217
