103. LETTER TO M. S. ANEY
June 18, 1934
DEAR BAPUJI ANEY2,
You dare not resign. And in any case not without a pro-
per fight with me.
I would undertake to prove to you that
by remaining in both the Committees you will do no violence
to your principle.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Reminiscences of Gandhiji, p. 209
