372. LETTER TO MOHAMMAD ALI
AHMEDABAD,
August 20, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
You will pardon me for not having written to you before
now. I have been working against time but I have been attend-
ing to your matter. I have not yet attained any success. If the
Settlement is continued I shall certainly make further effort. If
it is broken off, then, of course, I shall be of no service in cases
325
such as yours. There was a friend enquiring about your matter.
I sent you a message through him which I hope you duly received.
Yours sincerely,
MAULVI MOHAMMAD ALI
DHARAVI, BOMBAY 17
