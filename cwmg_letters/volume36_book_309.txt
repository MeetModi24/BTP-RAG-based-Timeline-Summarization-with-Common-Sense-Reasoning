309. LETTER TO SANTOK GANDHI
[After April 23, 1928]
CHI. SANTOK,
Seth Ghanshyamdas Birla is willing to employ Keshu, so you
need not worry now about him. God will certainly ensure that he
prospers. All of you should get absorbed in work. Take care of
your health.
Write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 8672. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
