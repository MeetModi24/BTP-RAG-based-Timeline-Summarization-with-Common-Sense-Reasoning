306. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
July 8, 1933
DEAR "GRAND MOTHER",
Your old age does not permit you to master the simple Hindi
alphabet.
For your sake I have looked up the word 'petticoat' in the
Oxford Dictionary and it was properly used by me. The Indian
woman's skirt is part of her sari which hangs from the waist
and covers the petticoat.
The word ballast was properly used. Look up the dictionary.
I have not the time just now to give you instances of what I
mean. It would be better if you will discover them yourself.
The necklace sent to you is strong enough to last for months.
Another can be easily made. Do you want me to send you a
spare one?
Love.
BAPU
AMALABEHN
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
