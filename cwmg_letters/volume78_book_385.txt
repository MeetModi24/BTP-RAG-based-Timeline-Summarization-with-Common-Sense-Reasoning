385. LETTER TO CARL HEATH
November 13, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Your welcome letter came into my hands today. I am in
the midst of a raging storm and often hum to myself:
Rock of Ages cleft for me, let me hide myself in Thee.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
CARL HEATH
WHITEWINGS, 57 MANOR WAY
GUILDFORD, SURREY (ENGLAND)
From a photostat: G.N. 1051. Also C.W. 4441. Courtesy: F. H. Chopping
