547. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
NEW DELHI,
October 7, 1946
CHI. LILY,
You did well in writing to me. Your letter is painful. What
is His will He alone knows. We have gone completely mad.
Thoughtful men and women should pray to God to save us.
By "we" I do not mean you and I but everyone. You and I
should ask that we may sacrifice ourselves in this conflagration.
But we must also make ourselves fit for that, shouldn't we?
Go on doing service.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. LILAVATIBEHN UDESHI
G. S. MEDICAL COLLEGE
LADIES' HOSTEL
PAREL
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 10247. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
