17. LETTER TO G. J. ADAM
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 26, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I am very sorry I missed you when you gave me a call at the
above address. I shall be pleased to see you between ten and half-past
tomorrow morning, and to give you all the information you may require.
My co-Delegate Mr. Ally, I am sorry to say, is at present at the Lady
Margaret Hospital undergoing treatment for rheumatism.
Yours faithfully,
G. J. ADAM, ESQ.
82, SHAFTESBURY AVENUE, W.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4400
