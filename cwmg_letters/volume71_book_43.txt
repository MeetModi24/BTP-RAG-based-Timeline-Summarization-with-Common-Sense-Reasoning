43. LETTER TO G. V. GURJALE
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 14, 1939
MY DEAR BHIKSHU,
I wonder if you ever got the wire I sent you in reply to yours.
In going through arrears I came across your letter. Don't think
of disengaging yourself from vows.
Go on doing the work in
hand. How is the temple-entry work going?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 1390
