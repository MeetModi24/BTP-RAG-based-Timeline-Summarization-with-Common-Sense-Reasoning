353. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
July 11, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
For the present Harilal may be considered as lost to us. . . .1
I get Lilavati's letters. Enclosed please find one for her. We
may pay her out of the school funds if that is possible. If this
is the only cause of her discontent, remove it.
Do give Mathew physical labour. What is the duration of
each class?
Kanu is keeping very well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8463. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
