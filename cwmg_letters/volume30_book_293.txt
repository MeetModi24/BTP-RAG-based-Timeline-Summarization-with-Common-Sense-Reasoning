293. LETTER TO AMRITLAL NANAVATI AND OTHERS
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Wednesday, April 7, 1926
BHAISHRI AMRITLAL AND OTHERS,
I have your letter. I never take up an activity of my own ac-
cord, nor do I think it proper to concern myself with any odd
activity. Regarding the Palitana affair I know the Sangh leaders
are making some move; how can I interfere with it? In my opi-
nion if you too have anything to say you had better say it through
the leaders. This is not a movement wherein any shravak may
start on a satyagraha on his own. Even if you think it has reached
the stage for satyagraha, you should start it through the Sangh.
Some time back some people had come to consult me about it. I
explained all this to them.
