473. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
SEGAON,
October 14, 1936
CHI. PREMA,
I got your letter. You are flying high. All right fly. But
see that you don't get exhausted and fall.
To my right and left are Mira's and Nanavati's sick-beds.
Both are suffering from typhoid.
I have practically stopped attending to correspondence. But
you certainly have my blessings for your camp which you have
asked for. I hope that being the servant you are, you will go
on serving silently and without ostentation and regard service as
its own reward.
I don't know anything about my having to go to Bombay.
Even the visit to Ahmedabad has become uncertain now. I
cannot leave Mira in her present condition. Nanavati, one may
say, is on the road to recovery.
Blessings from
BAPU
