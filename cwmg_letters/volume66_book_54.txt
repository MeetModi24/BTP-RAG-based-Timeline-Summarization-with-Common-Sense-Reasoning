54. LETTER TO M. MARGUERITE WIT
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 13, 1937
DEAR MARGARET,
I was delighted to hear from you.
Here is a letter for
Maria Sevenich which please send to her.
Love.
BAPU
M. MARGUERITE WIY
LAGER OESCHINENSEE B.
SWITZERLAND
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
42
