241. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
ON THE TRAIN,
April 13, 1947
CHI. SITA,
I will be reaching Patna today. Come and join me as soon
as you can get relieved there. Do not worry about anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. SITAKUMARI GANDHI
KUMARIKA MANDIR
BENARES HINDU UNIVERSITY P.O.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 4999
