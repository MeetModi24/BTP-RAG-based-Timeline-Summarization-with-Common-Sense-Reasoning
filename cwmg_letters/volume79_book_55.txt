55. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
January 15, 1945
CHI. SITA,
I like your letter. It is clear and to the point. Your presump-
tion is correct. In that case Sushila should arrive soon.
I am glad, too, that you are making good progress in your
studies. Make as much progress as you can, but see that your
body is strong.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4945
