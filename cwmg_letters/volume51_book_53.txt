53. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
September 11, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
I have your letter. As you could easily save many hours in
doing the work in the store-room and cleaning, so also, with some
thought, you can save time in other work as well. For that,
sincerity and co-operation on the part of everybody are required.
If all of you work as a team, each doing his best, a lot more work
can be done.
This holds true for all living creatures. If you watch the
ants and other insects, you will learn the same lesson from their
activities.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
