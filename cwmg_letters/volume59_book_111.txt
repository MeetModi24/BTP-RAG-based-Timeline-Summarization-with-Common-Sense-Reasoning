111. LETTER TO SURESH SINGH
September 29, 1934
CHI. SURESH 1 ,
I had your letter. Ranisaheba must have received my tele-
gram. Birth and death are a part of our destiny. Why should
we rejoice or grieve? You have to carry on the banner of Dada's
good work and comfort Ranisaheba.
I hope that the Management
will remain as before. Do keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8688. Also C.W. 2892. Courtesy:
Kumar Suresh Singh
