195. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEGAON,
August 2, 1937
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Does haste ever pay? I am doing whatever is possible. Letters
are being sent to Nariman. When the time comes, I will issue a
statement if it is necessary. I am also in correspondence with
Jawaharlal.
Blessings from
BAPU
