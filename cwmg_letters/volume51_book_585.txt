585. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
November 6, 1932
BELOVED DAUGHTER RAIHANA,
Have you now really decided not to write to me?
I must
have at least one letter in a week from you. When I do not get
it, I fear you may be ill. Did I tell you that I have found a new
teacher for me? Her name is Zohra Ansari. She teaches me with
great enthusiasm. She writes the Urdu letters very neatly.
Teach me to write a letter [in Urdu], and tell me whom we
can address as Janab or as Maulvi and when. How should I
address you? Have I correctly written your address? And mind
you do not fall ill. If, however, you do fall ill, then ask Hamida
to write to me. Convey my regards to both Father and Mother.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
BIBI RAIHANA
C/O ABBAS SAHEB
CAMP
BARODA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9667
