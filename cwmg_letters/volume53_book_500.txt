500. LETTER TO S. D. SATAVLEKAR
February 21, 1933
BHAI SATAVLEKAR,
I did not answer your postcard expecting to see Lakshman-
shastri any day. Now I do not know when I shall see him.
But I shall write more when I do see him. I am aware that
Hindi or English are not useful for Maharashtra.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From Hindi: C.W. 4773. Courtesy: S. D. Satavlekar
1 Omission as in the source
2 The reference here is to the possibility of bringing out a journal in
Marathi on the subject of untouchability; vide also p. 221.
