133. LETTER TO RAJKISHORI TYAGI
May 23, 1935
CHI. RAJKISHORI1,
I was sorry I did not take you with me. But your good
lay in my keeping you there.
However, you will have opportu-
nities of travelling with me in future. I hope you are happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6636. Also C.W. 4284
