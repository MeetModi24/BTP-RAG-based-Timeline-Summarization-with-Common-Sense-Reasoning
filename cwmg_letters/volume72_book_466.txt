466. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 27, 1940
MY DEAR C. R.,
Here is the draft prepared by the jury. The addition in
my hand in the last paragraph was suggested by me and ver-
bally accepted. I have no time to describe to you the back-
ground but you will have no difficulty in filling it in. Mah-
mud, R. Babu, Jamnalal and the Nightingale were also present.
What will happen on 13th4, I do not know. If all goes well
I may have to go to Bombay also. I am not too happy over
what is happening but I rejoice in my faith in God.
Love.
BAPU
[Ps.]
Can you bring Papa this time?
From a photostat: G.N. 2080
