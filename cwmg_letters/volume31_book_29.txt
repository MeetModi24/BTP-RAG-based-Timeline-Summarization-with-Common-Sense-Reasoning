29. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Jyaistha Shukla 9 [June 19, 1926]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
Of course you know about the loss incurred by the Servants
of India Society. In this connection Srinivasa Sastri has asked me
also to go round with a begging bowl. He has a right to ask
me. I have already written in Young India, but Sastriji desires
that I should write to my friends also. Although I do not ap-
prove of the political activities of the Society, I cannot forget the
honesty, patriotism and sacrifice of its members and therefore I
look upon it as a duty of every patriot to support and sustain it.
If you hold the same opinion, do send some donation and, if possi-
ble, ask your other friends also to give something.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6129. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
