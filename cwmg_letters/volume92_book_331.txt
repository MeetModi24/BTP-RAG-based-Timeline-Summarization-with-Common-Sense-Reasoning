331. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Y. M.,
July 13, 1930
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I was pleased to read your letter. The weaving school students
are doing credit to the name and the work of their Ashram.
I am getting along well. Take care of your health.
What more need I write?
Blessings from
BAPU
