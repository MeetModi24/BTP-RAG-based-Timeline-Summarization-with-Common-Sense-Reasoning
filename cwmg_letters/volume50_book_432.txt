432. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
August 23, 1932
CHI. VASUMATI,
Now I can write a separate letter to you. You could not meet
Mahadev after all. I had expected your letter immediately. It
is possible that I may get it before you get this. Describe all your
experiences, what you learnt from other women, the thoughts
which came to you, the books which you read, etc., etc. How was
your health [in jail]? Did you learn any Marathi? Could you
keep up your energy of body and mind on the food that you
were getting? I have had no letter from Nanibehn. Why is that
so ? We are all very well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9331. Also C.W. 5076. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
1 In 1930-31
