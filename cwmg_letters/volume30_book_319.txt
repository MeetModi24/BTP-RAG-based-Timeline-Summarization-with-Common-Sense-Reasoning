319. LETTER TO JAGJIVANDAS
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, Chaitra Vad 1[3] [April 10, 1926]
BHAISHRI JAGJIVANDAS,
The delay was deliberate because I knew you would still be
on your pilgrimage and would not reach Amreli for some time.
Today I am sending a hundi for Rs. 500 to the address given by
you.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 10865
