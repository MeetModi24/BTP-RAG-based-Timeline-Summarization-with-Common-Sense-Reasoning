443. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
August 29, 1941
CHI. NARANDAS,
May you live long and render much service. I shall send
Jajuji there. As you know, he is a holy man. He is the soul of
the Spinners' Association. He was a great lawyer, but today he
lives like a fakir.
He will let you have one week. See if you can manage
with less. Write to me and let me know the date and other
details.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8591. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
