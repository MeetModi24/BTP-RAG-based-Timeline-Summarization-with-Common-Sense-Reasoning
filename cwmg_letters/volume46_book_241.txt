241. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 24, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
I have your three letters forwarding me extracts from reports
received by you from Surat, Assam and the U.P. They are having
attention.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. W. EMERSON, ESQ.
Home Department, Political, File No. 33/9, 1931. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
