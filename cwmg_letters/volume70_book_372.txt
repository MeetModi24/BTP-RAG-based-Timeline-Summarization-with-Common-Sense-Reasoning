372. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 31, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
This has just come. You will do what is proper.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3938. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7274
1 Vide pp. 265- and 315-.
2 The reference is to a telegram to the addressee, at the back of which
Gandhiji had written this letter. The telegram dated "Dehra Dun, October 30,
1939" from Ramdev, a leader of Arya Samaj, read: "If possible, arrive one
day earlier."
