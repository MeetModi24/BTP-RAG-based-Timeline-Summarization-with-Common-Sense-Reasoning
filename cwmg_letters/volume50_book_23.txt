23. LETTER TO NATHURAM TRIKAMDAS
June 9, 1932
BHAI NATHURAM,
I felt happy on reading your detailed letter. I am glad that
you have gone there to learn khadi work. Learn it well and pro-
perly. If you get mere superficial knowledge, on your return you
will not be able to teach it to others. You will be able to do
that only if you acquire mastery over the smallest details. Observe
the Ashram rules carefully.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
