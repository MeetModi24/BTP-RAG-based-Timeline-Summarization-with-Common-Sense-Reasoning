436. LETTER TO CHAUNDE MAHARAJ
SEVAGRAM,
March 16, 1945
CHAUNDE MAHARAJ,
I have your letter. I do advise you. What is the point then
in including me in the Mandal? Take work from me. Keep my
name out.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
CHAUNDE BUA
C/O GOVARDHAN SANSTHA
SADASHIV PETH
POONA
