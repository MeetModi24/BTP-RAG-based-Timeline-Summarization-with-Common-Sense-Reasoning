141. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL VIDYARTHI
SEGAON,
October 10, 1936
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
I have your letter. I was all the time having news of you.
Never eat peanuts. When one is weak it is usual to have
dreams. You need not worry about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 11703
