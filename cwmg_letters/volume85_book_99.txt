99. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
POONA,
August 1, 1946
MY DEAR MR. ABELL,
I am writing on behalf of Gandhiji to thank you much for yours of
the 22nd July.
I am sending a copy of it to the Polish friend at whose instance
Gandhiji wrote to you. 3
With kind regards,
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
G. E. B. ABELL, ESQ.
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 89
