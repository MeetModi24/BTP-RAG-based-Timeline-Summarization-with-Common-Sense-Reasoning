512. LETTER TO SAVITA
June 12, 1945
CHI. SAVITA,
I received your letter yesterday. The invitation still stands.
I intend to reach Sevagram in July. Write to me then and come
when you have my reply. There are some changes in the Ashram.
If I did not tell you about them when we last met, I am telling you
now, that at the Ashram the work is principally physical labour
of every kind including the cleaning of lavatories. If you do not
like such work, Sevagram will not suit you.
Blessings from
BAPU
