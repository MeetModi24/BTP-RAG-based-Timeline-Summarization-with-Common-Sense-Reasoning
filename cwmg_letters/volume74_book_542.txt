542. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
September 18, 1941
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have your letter. It seems best now to go through the
homoeopathic treatment. Don't mind if that means some loss of
time. You must keep patient till you are sure whether or no
it is benefiting you.
I am glad you met . . .1 He does not at all seem clear in
his mind. About Valji also it is difficult to judge. He has got
very much prejudiced. I think he also will come round.
I understand about Lilavati. When you have taken her case
in hand, why need I worry about her? I did not wish to worry
you with her problem, though. She is hard working and smart.
I hope she will pass. But she has got a little tired. I hope
Bhanumati is all right. So it now seems certain that the girl
will not survive.
Has Bhulabhai been released? He seems to be quite ill.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, pp. 253-
