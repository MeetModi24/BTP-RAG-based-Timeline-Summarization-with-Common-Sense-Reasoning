234. LETTER TO PRINCESS EFY ARISTARCHI
July 18, 1932
DEAR SISTER,
I have to thank you once more for your lovely gifts. I have
got the beautiful book The Way of the Cross with your markings
and the crucifixion cards with helpful descriptions and thoughtful
quotations made by you at the back.
I hope all your troubles are over and if they are not, God
has blessed you with sufficient wisdom to regard them, in the
language of one of your quotations, as "blessings in disguise".
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PRINCESS ARISTARCHI
HOTEL SCHOTZKY
FREIBURG I.B., GERMANY
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 253
