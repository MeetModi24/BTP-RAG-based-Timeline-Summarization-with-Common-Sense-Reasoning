283. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
October 2, 1932
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have your letter. Now I have regained proper strength.
I am taking milk, fruit and one vegetable. My weight has risen
to 100 lb. There is, therefore, no cause for worry. Your health must
improve. How is Kshitishbabu? Give me news of Arun.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HEMPRABHA DEVI
KHADI PRATISTHAN
SODEPUR
VIA CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1689
