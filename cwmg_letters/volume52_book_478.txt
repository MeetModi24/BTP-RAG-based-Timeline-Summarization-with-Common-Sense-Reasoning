478. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
January 3, 1933
MY DEAR C.R.,
It was stupid of me not to have discussed with you my pro-
posal to Pandit Panchanan Tarkaratna. Now I have given it
to the Press; you will therefore see it earlier than you receive this.
I would like to have your opinion on it.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 19170
