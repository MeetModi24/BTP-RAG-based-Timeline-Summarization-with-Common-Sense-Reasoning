492. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Thursday [June 3, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have not been able to write to you at all of late. But I
have been doing what needs to be done. Your health will of
course improve there gradually. But it would be best to go to
Panchgani. Pattani has written me a very kind letter saying
that I should have no hesitation at all in using his bungalow in
July. Still I feel that we should not use his bungalow for too
long. Hence, I have declined his offer.
I have decided not to go to Finland. I have kept only one
option open, that is, I will go if by my not going Mr. Paul2,
who sent the invitation, is embarrassed in any way. But I see
no reason why he should be. Therefore, you can take it that I
shall not be going. I do wish this to be my last letter to you
at Deolali. Swami is a fast worker and so I do not think the
matter will take too long.
Blessings from
BAPU
