285. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
March 9, 1936
CHI. VANAMALA,
You must have learnt from Anandi why I could not speak
to you. I had very much wished to hear from you all the news
of Sumitra, but does God always let all our wishes be fulfilled?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5785.
Also C.W. 3008.
Courtesy: Vanamala M. Desai
