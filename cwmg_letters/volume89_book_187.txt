187. LETTER TO SHYAMA PRASAD BANDOPADHYAYA
CALCUTTA,
September 6, 1947
BROTHER SHYAMA PRASAD BANDOPADHYAYA,
I congratulate you on the heroic death of Smritish as he
went to save the Muslim brothers with his friends. I don't grieve
over such a death. I hope you and others also do not grieve
over it. I wish we have thousands of Smritishes amongst us.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 6567
