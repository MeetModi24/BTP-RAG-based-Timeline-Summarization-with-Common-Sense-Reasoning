78. LETTER TO DR. MAUDE ROYDEN
LONDON,
September 30, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. It was a privilege to meet you
and speak to your congregation . Your people did give the
donation to me and if I transfer the collection to you, why
should you be troubled? I could not have made better use of
the gift.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Guildhouse, November 1931
