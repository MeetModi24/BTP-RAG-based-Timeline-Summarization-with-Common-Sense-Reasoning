662. LETTER TO BHUPENDRA NARAYAN SENGUPTA
BOMBAY,
April 17, 1945
MY DEAR BHUPEN,
I have sent the condolence message.
Do send me details about Dhirendra's activities. And let
Dr. Indra see Profulla about medical work.
Yours,
BAPU
SJT. BHUPENDRA NARAYAN SENGUPTA
99/ BALLYGUNJ PLACE
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 10065
