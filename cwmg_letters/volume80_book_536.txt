536. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
June 13, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
I did not at all delay replying to you. It is good that you
have been firm and have stayed on. I have written to Father
at the Khurja address. More you will tell me when I come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
