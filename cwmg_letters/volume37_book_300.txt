300. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
September 10, 1928
MY DEAR KRISHNADAS,
Your letter about Ram Binod gives me much relief. I am
sending copies to Jamnalalji and others. You do not say anything
about reported purchases by Ram Binod. Is there any truth in
those allegations?
I received the Bengali edition of Seven Months. Is it selling
well? The English edition badly lacks an index. There are mis-
prints too.
How are you keeping in health and how is Guruji?
The Ashram is undergoing many drastic changes of which
perhaps Giriraj writes to you.
With love,
BAPU
From a microfilm: S.N. 13654
