740. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 11, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from anybody today. You may have
gone to Lahore. Still I am sending this letter. The enclosed
cutting may be useful to you. It had been crossed, but when
I noticed it, I cut it out for you. Ba must be well.
Things are going on well here.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I hope you are regularly guiding Tari. She has chronic in-
digestion. She says she keeps writing to you.
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
