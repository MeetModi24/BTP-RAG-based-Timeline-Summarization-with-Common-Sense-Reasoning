422. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
Monday
CHI. GANGABEHN,
There was no letter from you this week. I hope your health is
all right.
It will still be some time before I visit the Ashram. My spirit
of course is there. We also have daily prayers and use the same hymns
that are used for morning and evening prayers there. In the morning
the time also is the same. We spin here, too, just as you do there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C.W. 8829. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya.
Also G.N. 11385
