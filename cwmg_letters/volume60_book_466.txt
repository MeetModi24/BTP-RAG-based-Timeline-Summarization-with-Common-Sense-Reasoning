466. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
March 27, 1935
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
I read your letter to Mahadev. We have to devote four
columns to the Charkha Sangh. I have written to Shankerlal
about this, saying that he should either secure a certain number
of subscribers or pay a certain sum. We should guarantee the
columns but must not immediately raise the number of columns
in the paper. Many items we publish are much too lengthy.
The expansion should come about as we go along.
Do write to me regarding the Sammelan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1077
