131. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
May 27, 1938
CHI. PRABHA,
Kanti, Kanu and I wrote to you around the 15th. How
are we to blame if you didn't get the letters?
Please continue to write to me. I am all right. A meeting
is going on here just now. Kanti and Saraswati left for Trivan-
drum yesterday. Ba arrived from Delhi yesterday. The rest,
after I hear from you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3514
