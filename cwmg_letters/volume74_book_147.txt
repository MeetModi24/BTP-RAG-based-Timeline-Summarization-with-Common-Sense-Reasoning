147. LETTER TO ABRAHAM
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
[May/June 1941]
DEAR DR. ABRAHAM,
Here is a copy of the report on your letter. It will be a
pity if there is difference of opinion on facts. It is likely that
the riots had economic causes rather than religious. But you will
give me your considered opinion.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
