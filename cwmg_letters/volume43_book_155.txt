155. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
April 2, 1930
CHI. PREMA,
I have your perfect letter. You do not mention in it your
having received my letters, but I presume that you have received
them.
I have not received the pansy, but I shall take it as received.
Your rearing the plant with love is as good as presenting the
flower to me. Giving it physically is mere ceremony.
Is it true that you beat children? Mirabehn has gently com-
plained to me that you do.
I hope you take care of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 6667. Courtesy: Premabehn
Kantak
