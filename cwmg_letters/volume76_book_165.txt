165. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
May 23, 1942
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Since Prithvi Singh has lost faith in me, our relations have
come to an end. Gopalrao will leave that work. I think Nathji
and Kishorelal will end their connection with the Sangh. What
Prithvi Singh does we shall know by and by.
Give me news from there. Something must happen soon.
I have suggested to Prithvi Singh that he will himself have
to announce his loss of faith in me. If he does nothing, I shall
have to say something ultimately. You may tell our people about
the break in our relationship. I suppose it would be best to keep
silent about Limdi for the present.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
68 MARINE DRIVE
BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 275
