420. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
Silence Day [On or after August 24, 1931]
CHI. PREMA,
Even if you stop finally writing to me, I must write to you.
But you are not doing right in not writing to me. Will you write
if I order you to write?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10262; also C.W. 6711. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
