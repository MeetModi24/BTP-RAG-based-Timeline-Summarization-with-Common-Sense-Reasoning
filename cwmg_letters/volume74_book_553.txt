553. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA C. MEHTA
September 21, 1941
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
I have your chits. Do drop at least a postcard to Vedchhi
from time to time. You must come back fully restored. Since
you have normal appetite, you will be all right soon. We are
plying here a spinning-wheel round the clock which will continue
till October 2. Jamnalalji has arrived today. He seems to have
gone a little thinner. Fulchandbhai's widow, Shardabehn, and
Shivanand have arrived.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 4929
