21. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
August 23, 1924
BHAI SAHIB,
Bhur-r-r-r
I am glad to have your letter. You will not find it so
troublesome after you get used to the spinning-wheel. Try to
avoid looking that side.
You have made a good collection for the Muslim Boarding.
Convey my Vandemataram, or salaam—whatever they wish—
to Mrs. Abbas and the girls.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9548
