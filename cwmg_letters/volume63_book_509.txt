509. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
ON THE TRAIN, PALANPUR,
October 28, 1936
CHI. KANAIYO,
Of all your fellow-students you alone seem to have remained
firm so far. I believe that you have thereby lost nothing what-
ever. May God grant you the strength to remain firm for ever.
May you live long.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
