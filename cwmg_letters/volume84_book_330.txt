330. LETTER TO ASHABHAI
MUSSOORIE,
June 2, 1946
BHAI ASHABHAI,
It is good that you wrote. Discuss the matter also with
Sardar. I have already written about it. I can think of no-
thing more that I should do. Datar Singhji once told me that the
roze could be domesticated. Even if it were true, I don't think
one can keep whole herds of them. Give the matter some more
thought.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
