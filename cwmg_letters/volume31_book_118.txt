118. LETTER TO P. R. LELE
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 8, 1926
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I understand all you say, appreciate it
too, but, you cannot realize my helplessness. Just for the present
moment, I am not despondent. I feel certain that things will be
better, but, at the present moment, the fury, I fear, must be al-
lowed to spend itself. Anyway, the remedies that I have for the
disease are useless for the time being. I know that evil-doers are
having full scope and that the poison is saturating even young
minds. It all seems inevitable. Of course, do not think that be-
cause I do not write or speak, I am doing nothing.
I hope Delhi agrees both with you and Mrs. Lele. I am glad
you wrote to me.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. PURUSHOTTAM RAMCHANDRA LELE
3008, BURN BASTION ROAD
DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 11076
