326. LETTER TO D.
July 6, 1942
MY DEAR D.,
Your letter does not surprise me. But you have to smile
over these trifles. You should come here and assist. You know
there is always a place for you with me. I am writing this in
the midst of work. Come in when you can. Drop a line or a
wire in advance.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
