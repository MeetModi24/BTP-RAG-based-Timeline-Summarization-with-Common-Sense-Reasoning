305. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
Tuesday [December 18, 1928]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I wanted to write about many things today, but have no time.
I do not know what Santok wants. If she wishes to stay on
and lives contentedly, it would be very good indeed. You and
Narandas should decide about this. It will be best if you leave this
decision to Narandas. I will write about Devdas later. I had read
Mirabehn's letters. I will write to the Gurukul in regard to Balbir.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 32
