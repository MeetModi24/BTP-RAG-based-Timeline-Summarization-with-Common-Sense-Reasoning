163. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 27, 1935
DEAR DAUGHTER,
Though you forbade me to write I feel my letter will gladden
you. I do have some leisure for writing. It is pleasing to learn that
everything is proceeding smoothly there. I hope the Japanese
monk is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 328
