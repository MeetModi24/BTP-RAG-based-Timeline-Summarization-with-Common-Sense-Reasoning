380. LETTER TO MAITRI GIRI
July 30, 1933
CHI. MAITRI,
I was pleased to get your letter. Work well now and bring
great credit to the Ashram. Bachubhai is a selfless worker. There
is very much indeed which you can learn from him. Learn as
much as you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6241
