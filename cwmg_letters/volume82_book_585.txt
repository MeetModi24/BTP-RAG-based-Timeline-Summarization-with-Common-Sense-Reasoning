585. LETTER TO SECRETARY, MADRAS LABOUR UNION
FOR TEXTILE WORKERS
SODEPUR,
January 16, 1946
DEAR MR. SECRETARY,
I propose to address no meetings. I have neither the desire
nor the strength to undertake more work than I have already
accepted. You must realize that the days are gone when I could
go anywhere and address any number of meetings. Therefore,
please excuse me. You may consult Shri Satyanarayana about
an appointment with me.
M. K. GANDHI
SECRETARY
MADRAS LABOUR UNION FOR TEXTILE WORKERS
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
82-28
