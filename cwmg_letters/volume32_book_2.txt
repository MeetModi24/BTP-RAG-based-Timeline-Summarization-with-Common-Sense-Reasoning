2. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Diwali [November 5, 1926]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I was very much pleased to have your long letter. I had
heard slanderous remarks made about you. I did not believe
them, but your letter has completely satisfied me. They said that
you took work by giving Rs. 500 instead of a hundred. From
what you have written, I have nothing to say.
As for Geneva, I must advise you to be patient. I see no great
benefit in your going there. If the experience of the West is
necessary, go on your own. You will have many occasions to
go. But my inner self says it is not today. In the end you
should do what your conscience dictates.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 6137. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
