161. LETTER TO V. A. SUNDARAM
November 18, 1946
CHI. SUNDARAM,
So Panditji is gone and yet he is not gone! How can
he, of so many unrivalled services? His ailing body is delivered
from torture. But he is immortal.
Now is your testing time. You have to do all you can to
make his monumental work a success. I do not mean merely
financially. I am trying to write an article which you will see,
if I succeed in finishing it as I hope to.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G. N. 3197
