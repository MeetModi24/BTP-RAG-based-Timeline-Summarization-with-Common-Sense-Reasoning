389. LETTER TO PURNA CHANDRA SARMAH
November 14, 1934
MY DEAR PURNA CHANDRA SARMAH,
I have your letter which you did well in writing to me.
I would like you to give me your accounts up to date.
I hope that Annadababu has been to you to guide you regard-
ing spinning.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI PURNA CHANDRA SARMAH
SECRETARY
THE CONGRESS RELIEF COMMITTEE
NOWGONG (ASSAM)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
