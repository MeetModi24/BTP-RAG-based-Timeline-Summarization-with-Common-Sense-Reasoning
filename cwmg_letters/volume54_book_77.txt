77. LETTER TO LAKSHMIBEHN N. KHARE
March 12, 1933
CHI. LAKSHMIBEHN,
I was really very happy to get your letter. Who can find fault
with your handwriting? It is much better than mine. Do, there-
fore, write to me from time to time. Your letter this time seems
to have been written merely to please me.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 282. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn
N. Khare
