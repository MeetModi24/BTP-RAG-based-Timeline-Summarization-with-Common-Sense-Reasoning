134. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
Thursday [August 2] 1928
BHAISHRI VALJI,
I had believed that what Parnerkar had written regarding the
demand of that cow-protection worker, the one with the books of
accounts, was among my papers. But I do not find it among
the papers here. Please inquire about it. I do not know if it is
lying somewhere in my room. Please look for it and let me know.
Have you drafted the constitution of the Cow Service
Society? Please keep it ready when I return there.
I trust you take good care of your health.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7395. Courtesy: V.G. Desai
