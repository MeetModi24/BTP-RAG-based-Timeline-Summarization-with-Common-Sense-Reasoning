464. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
BARDOLI,
July 26, 1931
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
You must have got my letter written from Simla. What
happened to the sari? Lady Willingdon has reminded me thrice.
I shall be in Borsad on Tuesday. My going to England is
still uncertain.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 32779
