483. LETTER TO ESTHER MENON
May 18, 1936
MY DEAR CHILD,
You are being tested. Why should you have 'flu even in a
cool place like Kodai? But I know you will keep your peace
even in the midst of tortures and live up to your motto. You
will tell me when you are free. My prayers and my love are
with you.
BAPU
From a photostat: No. 140. Courtesy: National Archives of India
