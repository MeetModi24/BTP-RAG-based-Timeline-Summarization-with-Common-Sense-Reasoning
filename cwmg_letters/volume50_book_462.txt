462. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
August 27, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
Your letters are always nice. The summary of Rajaji's dis-
course which you have sent me is good.
I know why you are having pain in the back. Find it out
from my letter to Anandi and then write more about it.
Why are the slivers not up to the mark? Why don't you tell
Narandasbhai about it?
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9916. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
wala
