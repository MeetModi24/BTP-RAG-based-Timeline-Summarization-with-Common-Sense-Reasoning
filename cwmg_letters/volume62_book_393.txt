393. LETTER TO KRISHNALAL
April 22, 1936
CHI. KRISHNALAL,
You are on the contrary finding fault with me. You your-
self would not write and yet expect me to write. How is that?
Shall I say that you did not even argue out my case properly?
It is good that you have passed your examination. Become a
very intelligent and equally nice boy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1558. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
