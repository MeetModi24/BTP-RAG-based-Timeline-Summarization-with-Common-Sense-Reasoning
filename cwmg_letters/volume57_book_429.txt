429. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
April 19, 1934
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
I don't remember having left any letter of yours unanswered.
I did make use of the book you sent. For the present, go on
doing whatever service you think proper. I don't remember any
request for volunteers either. Chandrashankar may know. He is
not here at the moment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 2702. Courtesy: Dahyabhai M. Patel
