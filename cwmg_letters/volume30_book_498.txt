498. LETTER TO JAYA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, May 8, 1926
CHI. JAYA,
One benefit has certainly followed from Kusum and Dhiru
going there; I have had a letter from you. I did not know that
your handwriting was so laboured; it can certainly be improved.
Please see that the children keep all the rules. I shall then forget
my grief over their going from here. Please let me know how
Dr. Prabhudas is keeping. You will know the rest from the
letter to Kusum and Dhiru.
