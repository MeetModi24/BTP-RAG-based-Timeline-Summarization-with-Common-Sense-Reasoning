109. LETTER TO KUSUMBEHN DESAI
September 18, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I have had no letter at all from you for some time. I hope
you have not become panicky because of my indefinite fast. If
I pass away, fulfil my hopes. If you can give an emphatic reply
to this soon, do so.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1847
