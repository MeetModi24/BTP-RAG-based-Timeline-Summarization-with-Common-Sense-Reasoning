241. LETTER TO G. S. NARASIMHACHARI
February 7, 1935
DEAR NARASIMHACHARI,
I have your letter of 30th ultimo. I can give you very little
help in the matter .
You should write to the member yourself.
Prof. Ranga did see me in the matter. He appreciated my difficulty.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI G. S. NARASIMHACHARI
C/O VICE-CHANCELLOR
ANDHRA UNIVERSITY, WALTAIR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
