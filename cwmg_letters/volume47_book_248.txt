248. LETTER TO KISANSINH CHAVDA
ON THE TRAIN,
July 27, 1931
BHAI KISANSINH,
I have your letter. Come to Borsad on Wednesday. Stay
on if I have had to leave the place, though I don't expect to
have to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9328. Courtesy: Kisansinh Chavda
