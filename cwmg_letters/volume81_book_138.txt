138. LETTER TO ALFRED FRENSCH
SEVAGRAM,
August 7, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am no master. I am unable to take
charge of your children. My place is designed otherwise than
you seem to think. You should keep your children in France.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MON. ALFRED FRENSCH
CRENSPRE. LIEPVRE (HANT RHIN) FRANCE
C. P. POSTAL STRASBURG 191-31
FRANCE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
