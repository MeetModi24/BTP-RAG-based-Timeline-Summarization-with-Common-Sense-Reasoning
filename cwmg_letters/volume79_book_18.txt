18. LETTER TO RAGHAVDAS
SEVAGRAM,
January 5, 1945
BHAI RAGHAVDAS 2 ,
What is this? The golden remedy is Ramanama. Recite it
from the heart. Let me know what medicine the local vaidya
prescribes. I also have some good vaidyas. Get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
