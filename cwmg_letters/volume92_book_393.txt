393. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
December 11, 1930
MY DEAR ANAND,
I have left not one single letter of yours unanswered. I
hardly think they have been intercepted. I have answered all
your questions. I do hope you will get them all.
For avoiding constipation try green leafy vegetables and toma-
toes only without any protein food for two or three days. This
plan has worked well with me. Alternatively try stewed French
plums without anything else.
Love to you both.
BAPU
[PS.]
I am well but am not taking fresh fruit.
