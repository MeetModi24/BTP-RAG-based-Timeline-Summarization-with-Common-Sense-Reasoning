229. LETTER TO JAGANNATH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C. P.,
June 6, 1942
BHAI JAGANNATH,
I have your letter. I can understand your condition. May
God give you such strength that the sentiments you have cher-
ished these twenty years do not disappear.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 996. Courtesy: Jagannath
