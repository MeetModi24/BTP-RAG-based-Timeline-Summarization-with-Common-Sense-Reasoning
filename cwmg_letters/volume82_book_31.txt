31. LETTER TO GAJANAN NAIK
POONA,
November 4, 1945
CHI. GAJANAN,
I have your letter. I am glad to read it. You will be able
to do real service only if you assimilate the lesson I imparted
to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
GAJANAN NAIK
AKHIL BHARATIYA GRAMODYOG SANGH
MAGANWADI, WARDHA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
