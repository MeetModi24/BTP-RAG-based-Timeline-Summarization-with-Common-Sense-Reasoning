84. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
June 10, 1934
BHAI MUNSHI,
Let us see how much so many of you lawyers collect.
I hope the meeting for swadeshi will not clash with the time
for the meeting of the Parliamentary Board. I will keep myself
ready to answer all the arguments and to explain my plan. But
I don't know whether I shall be able to convince the people.
Earn as much as you wish without sacrificing truth, but
spend on yourself as if you had nothing of your own.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI K. M. MUNSHI, ADVOCATE
RIDGE ROAD, MALABAR HILL
BOMBAY
From Gujarati: C.W. 7543. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
