339. LETTER TO N. G. JOSHI
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 2, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter giving me facts of [the] criminal case of
Nipani. Where there is any allegation of violence the matter
rests with the local government. I am not likely to succeed in any
representation I may make. I would therefore advise that a proper
systematic defence is put up.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. N. G. JOSHI
PRESIDENT, DISTRICT CONGRESS COMMITTEE
BELGAUM
From a photostat: S.N. 17189
