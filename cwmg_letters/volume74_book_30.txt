30. LETTER TO URMILA M. MEHTA
[April 30, 1941]
CHI. URMI 3 ,
I got your slips. I kept silent because there was no need
for a reply. What are you reading at present? I miss you very
much. All of you should come at the earliest in May. What do
you read and what is your diet? Let me know your weight. Do
you go out for walks?
Use ink for writing. In what form are you studying? It is
quite hot here.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI URMILABEHN
C/O SHRI M. P. MEHTA
TOP FLOOR, 71 DARYAGANJ
DELHI
From Gujarati: C.W. 10116. Courtesy: Manjula M. Mehta
