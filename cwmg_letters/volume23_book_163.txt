163. LETTER TO A. D. SKENE CATLING
POST ANDHERI,
March 16, 1924
DEAR MR. CATLING,
I thank you for your note sent through Mr. Panikkar.
I shall be delighted to see you and Mr. Iyer on Wednesday
at the time stated by you.
Yours sincerely,
A. D. SKENE CATLING, ESQ.
MESSRS REUTERS LIMITED
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 8509
