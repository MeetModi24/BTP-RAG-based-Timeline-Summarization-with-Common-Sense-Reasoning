608. LETTER TO N. VENKATA KRISHNAIYA
April 19, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
In one of my letters I pointed out the difficulty of the bar-
ter system. There should be a common measure of value. As
to your second point, undoubtedly there should be division of
labour.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI N. VENKATA KRISHNAIYA
KHADDAR SANSTHANAM
BEZWADA
From a photostat: G.N. 9242
