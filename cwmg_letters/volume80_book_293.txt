293. LETTER TO HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 23, 1945
Your letter of the 17th. Bapuji has seen it. He says that if you are
not in a hurry, you may come after he has settled down in Panchgani. We
have to leave Mahabaleshwar on the 31st at the latest. We shall have to
leave earlier if it rains. So you may take it that your coming to Maha-
baleshwar will be useless.
Yours,
PYARELAL
SHRI HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
CUTTACK, ORISSA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
