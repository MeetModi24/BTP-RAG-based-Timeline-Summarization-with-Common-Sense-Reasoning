408. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
April 16, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
I have your letter. Mira has been keeping you in touch with
things concerning me.
You may take the vows, if you feel the confidence. Your
things will be disposed of. Why do you think I doubt you or
that you are less than Mira? Mira has come in for rebukes and
reproaches to which you are an utter stranger. But that is no-
thing. You should simply lose yourself in your work and you will
find me truly.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
