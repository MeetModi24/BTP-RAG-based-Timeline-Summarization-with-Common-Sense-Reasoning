120. LETTER TO SYED ABDUL LATIF
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 26, 1940
DEAR DR. LATIF,
I have read with attention your outline which you kindly
sent me. I can offer no useful criticism as my objection holds
good the same way as when I raised it to your first scheme.
The underlying idea is repugnant to me. For me India is one
indivisible. I believe in a blending of cultures, not in their being
kept in water-tight compartments. It should be enough for you
to know that I am open to conviction and that you are free to
convert me to your view.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
