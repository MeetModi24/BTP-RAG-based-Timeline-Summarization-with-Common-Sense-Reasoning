219. LETTER TO PURATAN BUCH
POONA,
[February 28, 1946]
CHI. PURATAN,
You write to say that engine-driven flour-mills are to be
found in thousands of villages. Thousands would mean that their
number could be greater than ten thousand. You cannot de-
scribe them as thousands, if they were a thousand and one. If you
have the statistics, give me the exact figures. You must also have
the names of the villages, only then you can arrive at the [right]
figure. If the number of the villages runs into thousands, it is a
very alarming situation. What would be the cost of the equip-
ment and the engines? Calculate the cost, if you haven't already
done so, and let me have the figures.
Blessings from
BAPU
