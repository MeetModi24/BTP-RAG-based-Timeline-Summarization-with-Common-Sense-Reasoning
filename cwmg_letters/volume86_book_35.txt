35. LETTER TO RAM NARAYAN DUBE
NEW DELHI,
October 24, 1946
BHAI RAM NARAYAN DUBE,
I read your book on nature cure. It is packed with infor-
mation but there is much exaggeration. There is a "must" with
every instruction.
I passed on the book to a well-known vaidya to read the
chapter on herbal science. He says that your claim cannot be
proved.
If it is so, the book needs to be drastically revised.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RAM NARAYAN DUBE
PRAKRITIK CHIKITSALAYA
BENARES
