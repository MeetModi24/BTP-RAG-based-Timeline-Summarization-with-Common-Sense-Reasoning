489. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
September 4, 1940
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I shall write about the members of the Prajamandal, etc.,
in the next issue of Harijan Sevak.
We have to see how the language of Harijan Sevak shapes up
now. Pyarelal has been charged with the responsibility. All the
articles will pass through him. Pyarelal will write to you. If you
can send some factual articles, not just essays, do so. And of
course your comment on the language.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: Haribhau Upadhyaya Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
