193. LETTER TO C. SUBRAMANIAM
February 1, 1935
MY DEAR SUBRAMANIAM,
If the teachers are truly repentant, I suppose they must be
allowed to remain. It is difficult for me to give you infallible
guidance without knowing the parties and knowing the method
under which the institution is being run. There can never be
too much purity in teachers. And the problem of getting the right
type of teachers and managers for institutions where boys or
girls receive training is proving more and more baffling.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI C. SUBRAMANIAM
C/O SHRI L. K. MUTHUSWAMI
161 VICTORIA HOSTEL, TRIPLICANE (MADRAS)
