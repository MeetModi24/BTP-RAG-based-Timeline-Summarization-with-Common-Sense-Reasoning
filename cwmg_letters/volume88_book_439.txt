439. LETTER TO VITHALDAS
NEW DELHI,
July 23, 1947
BHAI VITHALDAS,
Received your letter and the monthly Arogya. I do not know
if you can render any service through the monthly. I have found
the naturopaths to be full of hypocrisy and arrogance. If your
monthly contains nothing but the truth you might possibly be able
to render some service.
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
