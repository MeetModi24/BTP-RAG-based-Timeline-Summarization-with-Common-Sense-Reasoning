529. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
May 11, 1932
CHI. MANGALA,
I can say that your speed in spinning on the takli is quite
good. You will stand first.
Aminabehn's children have taught [ the lesson] that parents
should observe self-restraint. They were only children. Men and
women who observe celibacy can have no children. If there are
no births, there will be fewer to die. When you grow up, won't
it be good if you practise self-restraint?
BAPU
