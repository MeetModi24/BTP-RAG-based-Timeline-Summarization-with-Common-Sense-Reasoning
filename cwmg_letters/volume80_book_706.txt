706. LETTER TO VINOBA BHAVE
SIMLA,
July 11, 1945
CHI. VINOBA,
Kaka sent me a telegram about your release. Hope you are
well. The work for you is ready. Baba and Gokhale are also
in it. Do what you feel is right.
I may leave here on the 15th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
