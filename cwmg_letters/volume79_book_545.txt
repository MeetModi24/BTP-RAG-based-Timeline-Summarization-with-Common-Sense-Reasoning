545. LETTER TO SHAMDAS P. GIDWANI
SEVAGRAM,
March 28, 1945
DEAR SHAMDAS,
I have your wire and two letters. I cannot interfere. Con-
gressmen must decide for themselves.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHAMDAS P. GIDWANI
NEW TOWN, KARACHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
1 Wife of Brajkrishna Chandiwala
