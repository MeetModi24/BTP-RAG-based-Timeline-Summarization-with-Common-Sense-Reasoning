336. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
August 7, 1932
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I got your letter. My letter to you went to the Camp. What
is the trouble in your leg? Ramdas and Hargovind told me
about it when they met me this time. See that you do not let
your health suffer outside jail.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5505
