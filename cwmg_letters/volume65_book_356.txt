356. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
June 19, 1937
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter has just come. I hope the mountain air will set
you right. Here is note for Dr. Dharmavir. My love to Subhas
Babu to whom I have no time to write separately.
You will be grieved to know that yesterday the rain set in
but destroyed the earth-work near the creek bridge, nearly destroy-
ed the houses on either side. If the rain had continued for five
minutes longer the houses would have gone. Now I am considering
what to do.
Khan Saheb and Mehrtaj came in today.
No more time to say more.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6383. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9849
