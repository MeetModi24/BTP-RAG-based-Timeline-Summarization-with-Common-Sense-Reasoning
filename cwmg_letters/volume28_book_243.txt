243. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
Monday [November 16, 1925]
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
Your commandments are being carried out. The wire went
to Sarojini this morning. I am giving a leading article to your
mission.
I hope you are quite well. God keep you well on the
voyage.
With deepest love,
Yours,
MOHAN
From a photostat: G.N. 2632
