527. LETTER TO T. PRAKASAM
April 20, 1933
MY DEAR PRAKASAM,
I sent the enclosed papers to the Provincial Board of the
S.U.S. They have returned the papers saying that the matter is
outside their jurisdiction and that I should forward them to you,
and so you have the papers and you will tell me what you can
do in the matter.
I hope you are keeping well.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 21004
