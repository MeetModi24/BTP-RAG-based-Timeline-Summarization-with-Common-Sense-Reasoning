94. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 19, 1940
CHI. SHARMA,
There is a vast difference between the present civil disobe-
dience and the earlier ones. It may hardly be necessary to call
any other volunteer. Your name is already with me. But you
need not make any special preparation. You may carry on as
if no one was to be called. Everyone should engage himself in
constructive work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
between pp. 290 and 291
