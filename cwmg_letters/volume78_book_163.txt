163. LETTER TO M. A. JINNAH
Id [September 23, 1944]
BHAI JINNAH,
I was wondering what I shall send you today. It should
be fair on my part to let you and your sister share equally
the crisp chapatis they make for me. Here is your share. Please
regard it as a token of my love and do please help yourself to it.
Id greetings from
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
