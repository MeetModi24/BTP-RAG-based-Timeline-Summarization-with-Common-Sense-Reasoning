216. LETTER TO HOSA RASHID KHAN
NEW DELHI,
May 19, 1946
BETI HOSA,
I have sent you wires in reply to yours. Amtul Salaam says
you are brave. I think you cannot add glory to Rashid Khan's
name by shutting yourself in; but you can, by rendering service.
Do write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
