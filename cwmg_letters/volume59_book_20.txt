20. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM G. PANSE
September 18, 1934
CHI. BHAU,
I like the name Rentia Baras. We can eradicate poverty and
unemployment with the minimum effort if this sudarshana chakra
of the modern age is plied in every home. And this is possible
only if all the villagers spin and wear clothes made out of the
yarn they spin.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6753. Also C.W. 4496. Courtesy:
Purushottam G. Panse
