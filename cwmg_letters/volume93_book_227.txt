227. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
[After April 7, 1938]
CHI. SUSHILA,
What shall I write? You will have received my letter of
yesterday. After I wrote it, I saw Pyarelal's letter. May God
grant you courage and peace. If you cannot find peace there,
you have always got my bosom such as it is.
From the Hindi original : Pyarelal Papers.
Nehru
Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
