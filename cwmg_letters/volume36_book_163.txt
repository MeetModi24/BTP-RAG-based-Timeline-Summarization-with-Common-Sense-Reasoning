163. LETTER TO N. RAMA RAO
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 26, 1928
N. RAMA RAO, ESQ.
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
DEVELOPMENT DEPARTMENT
SECRETARIAT, BANGALORE
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for your letter enclosing a copy of Sjt. Pujari's
report on the Badnaval Spinning Centre. This work was noticed
the pages of Young India.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13130
