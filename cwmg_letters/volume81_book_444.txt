444. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
POONA,
September 14, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your letter. It is well that Munnalal and Prabhakar
have gone 12 miles away. I hope they have not gone walking!
It is good that you are giving lessons to Hoshiaribehn. Babaji
must be well. It is too bad about Mahars. If the condition
worsens let me know.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
We have to meet Pushpa's expenses. Whatever money is spent
by her on that account should be paid back into her account.
We shall see about Kamle. Balkrishna had fever. He is
better now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4527
