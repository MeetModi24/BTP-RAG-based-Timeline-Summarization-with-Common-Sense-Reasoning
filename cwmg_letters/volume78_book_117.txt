117. LETTER TO VICEROY
CAMP "BIRLA HOUSE", BOMBAY,
September 10, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
I wrote to you on July 17, 1944, asking you kindly to send
a letter of same date addressed to the Prime Minister. May
I know whether that letter was sent as requested? I am asking
because so far I have had no acknowledgement of my letter.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. E. THE VICEROY
VICEROY'S HOUSE
NEW DELHI
From a copy: C.W. 10500. Courtesy: India Office Library, London
