388. LETTER TO SANKARAN
September 3, 1945
CHI. SANKARAN,
I am glad that the cholera has subsided to some extent.
Try to find its root. Do you cremate or bury the dead? From
what I hear, it seems people are running away.
All of you should be engaged in serving the patients. Do
you get any help from the Ashram? Have you asked for it or
have no need for it?
Blessings from
BAPU
