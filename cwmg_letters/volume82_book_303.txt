303. LETTER TO KHWAJA NAZIMUDDIN
KHADI PRATISHTHAN,
SODEPUR, CALCUTTA,
December 15, 1945
BHAI SAHEB,
Amtussalaam has given me your letter inviting me to your
place on the 4th. How nice it would have been if I could come
to your place. But circumstances do not permit me to do so.
Does it mean that we shall not be able to meet? If that happ-
ens I shall be very sorry.
SIR NAZIMUDDIN
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Urdu : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
