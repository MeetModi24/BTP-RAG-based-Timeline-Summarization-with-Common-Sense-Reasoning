389. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
AHMEDABAD,
Bhadrapad Shukla 4 [August 21, 1917]
DEAR SHRI JAMNALAL,
I have your letter. I have come here for a few days. There is
no need for you to go to Champaran. The Committee's work is
almost over now.
Yours
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi original in Gandhiji's hand: G. N. 2834
