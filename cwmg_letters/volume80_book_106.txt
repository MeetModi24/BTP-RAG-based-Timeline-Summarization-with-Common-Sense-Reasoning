106. LETTER TO ANANT RAM
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 3, 1945
CHI. ANANT RAM,
Your Urdu writing is very good. Continue to write like this.
If Ramanama can do so much how much more can you? If
things will continue like this, I shall be very happy indeed. I do
not desire any change in your time-table.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : S.G. 131
