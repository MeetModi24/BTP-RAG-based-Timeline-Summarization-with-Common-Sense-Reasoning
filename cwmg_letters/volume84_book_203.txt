203. LETTER TO MANIBHAI DESAI
NEW DELHI,
May 16, 1946
CHI. MANILAL,
I have already written and given instructions for sending you
the money.
Even if Dhirubhai has to attend to his own affairs from
time to time and Paramanand has not settled down to work,
[as] his wife remains ill, I hope you will still cling to the place
and have confidence that you will be able to manage somehow.
Persuade Balkrishna also to consider this.
1. You may give the Trust any name you like. Follow
Dinshaw's wishes in the matter.
2. The main activity will remain what it is just now.
3. You need not bother about the future at all.
The budget seems all right. I do not have time to go deeper
into it. I have barely been able to find time to write even this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : C. W. 2727. Courtesy: Manibhai Desai
