308. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI AND SUMITRA GANDHI
BOMBAY,
June 5, 1939
CHI. NIMU,
I received your letter. I am not sending over Kanam to you. You need
not therefore wait for the day I leave. You should complete your studies there.
The figure of expenditure you have put down for your books is correct. You
may take charge of Kanam after you are free from there. Do not worry about
him. You may send for Usha.
CHI. SUMI,
I got your letter. Improve your handwriting, but however indifferent it
may be, do keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI NIRMALABEHN GANDHI
KANYA GURUKULA
DEHRA DUN
U.P.
