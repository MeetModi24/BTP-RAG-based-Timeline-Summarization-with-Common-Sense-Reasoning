181. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
January 31, 1931
CHI. RUKMINI,
I have just heard about your illness. I read your letter to
Jamnalalji. What should I write to you? Get all right quickly. I
had hoped to be able to see you both. But this time that could
not be.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9057
