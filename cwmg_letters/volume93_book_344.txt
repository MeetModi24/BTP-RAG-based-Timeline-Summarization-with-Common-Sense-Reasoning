344. LETTER TO HANNA LAZAR
November 26, 1938
MY DEAR HANNA,
I had your letter from Kimberley but not the letter you
want to write.
You must not only keep well but be robust.
I am not writing to your mother or young Isele.
But my love to all of you.
BAPU
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy:
National Archives of India
