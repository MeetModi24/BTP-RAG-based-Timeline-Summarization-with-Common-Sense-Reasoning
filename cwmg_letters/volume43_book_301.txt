301. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
April 22, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
There has been no letter from you for several days. I have
no idea how long I shall be allowed to remain free. But you
must keep writing to me. After I am arrested we shall see.
How is your health now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1666
