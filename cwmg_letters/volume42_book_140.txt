140. LETTER TO A. A. PAUL
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
MY DEAR RAJAN,
I have your letter as also the minutes . I went through the
minutes perfunctorily. I have hardly time for more. Of course
I have studies on the Gita in mind but I have almost des-
paired of getting the time for them. Let me however hope and
pray. I am keeping well. Thank you.
Yours sincerely,
A. A. PAUL, ESQ.
FEDERATION OF INTERNATIONAL FELLOWSHIPS, MADRAS
From a photostat: S.N. 15728
