44. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
April 13, 1942
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I do not at all feel enthusiastic about offering a plot to
Prabhashankarl. But if he is prepared to spend money, we may
build a house for him without conceding to him any rights
over it. It would be convenient to get the alterations asked for
by Champa carried out at her expense. It has become my
duty and that of the Ashram too, to accommodate Champa and
her children. We will not, therefore, make this case a precedent
for a general rule.
The general rule should be not to give
accommodation to anyone.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10608
