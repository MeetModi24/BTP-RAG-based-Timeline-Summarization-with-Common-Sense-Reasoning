486. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
July 18, 1937
DEAR KUMARAPPA,
Sankaran Nair was in the Maganwadi school and he was
ailing. Expenses were incurred for medical attendance, medicine
and food.
For these there is a bill outstanding. He is now
in the tannery. He has got Rs. 10 from Ramachandran. He
says he should be excused the rest of the payment. What do
you say?
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10124
