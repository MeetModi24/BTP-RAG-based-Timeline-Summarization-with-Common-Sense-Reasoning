479. LETTER TO UMA AGRAWAL
June 8, 1945
CHI. OM,
I have your letter. If I have to take a decision about you, I
can do that when I reach there in July.
I follow what you say about Hindustani. There should of
course be a dictionary of the words spoken in the villages. No
language can survive without a dictionary. Dr. Tarachand and
Sunderlalji alone will not complete the dictionary. We shall
have a little discussion when we meet. The committee formed for
this purpose has to evolve a composite language.
Blessings from
BAPU
