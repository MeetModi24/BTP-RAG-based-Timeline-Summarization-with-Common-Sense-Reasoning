505. LETTER TO N. VYASTIRTH
September 27, 1945
BHAI VYASTIRTH,
I have your letter. Your spinning activity is laudable. Go
on increasing it, learn all the processes preceding spinning.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI N. VYASTIRTH
840 SULTAN BAZAAR
HYDERABAD DECCAN
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
