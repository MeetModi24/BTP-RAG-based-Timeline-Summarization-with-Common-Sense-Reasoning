101. LETTER TO KAKALBHAI KOTHARI
August 16, 1929
BHAISHRI KAKALBHAI,
Whatever you want to say against Gondal today, send to me
in the form of a summary of the points without taking me into
the evidence. But you should not include in the charges any
which you cannot fully substantiate. I have not written this letter
for publication, nor for you to use it, directly or indirectly, for
agitation.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/116
