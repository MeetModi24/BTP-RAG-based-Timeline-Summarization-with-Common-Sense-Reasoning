113. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Jeth Sud 10 [June 11, 1924]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your second letter. Please expect only as many letters
as you write to me. It has worked out only thus so far. I re-
plied to you the very day I received your letter. I hope it has
reached you. Ramdas and others have returned from Abu. I see
that the stay at Abu has done them much good. How nice it
would have been if you could have gone with them! Now do stay
there long enough and recover your health completely. I am quite
well. Prabhudas has not yet returned from Abu. Devdas and Ba
have left for Bhavnagar today.
Blessings from
BAPU
SISTER VASUMATI
LEELAVATI AROGYABHUVAN
DEOLALI
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 444. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
