240. LETTER TO MANORANJAN CHATTERJEE
SODEPUR,
December 7, 1945
BHAI MANORANJANBABU,
I have your wire. I am writing to Dr. Shyamaprasadji
and I shall send either Dr. Sushila Nayyar or Pyarelalji there.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI MANORANJAN CHATTERJEE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
