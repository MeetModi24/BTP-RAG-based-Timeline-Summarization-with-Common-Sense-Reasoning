220. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
July 10, 1938
CHI. LILA,
Do you expect a note from me every day? You should not
be over-impatient to come here. You may come here whenever
you get an opportunity in the normal course. I would also like
your coming here, but you should not come at the cost of your
work or health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 9373. Also C. W. 6648.
Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
