47. LETTER TO CHAMANLAL VAISHNAV
Maha Vad 1 [February 9, 1925]
BHAI CHAMANLAL4,
I write this on the train. I got your letter. It is impossible
for me to go there before the 16th. I am afraid I shall be able to
do so only about the 20th or 21st, or it may well be the visit
is cancelled.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2869. Courtesy: Shardabehn Shah
