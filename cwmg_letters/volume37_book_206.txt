206. LETTER TO KISHORELAL MASHRUWALA
Monday [August 13, 1928]
CHI. KISHORELAL 2 ,
What shall I say to comfort Nanabhai or you? Both of you
look upon death as a friend. Our grief can only be due to our
selfishness. Before his death, Balubhai wrote to me a sweet
letter. It was about Bardoli. In fact he wished to go there, but,
since the doctor did not permit it, he contented himself with a letter.
You will do a wrong if you do not ask for any service which the
Ashram can render. We can even look after the children.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2659. Courtesy: Kanubhai Nanalal
Mashruwala
