464. LETTER TO NAGINDAS
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday, April 30, 1926
BHAISHRI NAGINDAS,
I had sent your letter to Bhai Darshansingh. I am sending
his reply for your information along with the stamps received with
it. I have advised him that, if he thinks the books are useful,
he should order them and remit the money for them.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19517
