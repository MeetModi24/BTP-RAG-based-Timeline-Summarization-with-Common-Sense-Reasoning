20. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
October 14, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your letter. Of course you should not worry Shummy until
you are uniformly well for at least a fortnight. There need be no
hurry to leave the place so long as the weather there agrees with you.
We have here a homoeopath. He is a crank like me. I have
entrusted Prabhakar to his care. He took charge yesterday and
P. is distinctly better today. As you know I have little faith
h'pathy, though I would like to for its comparative simplicity.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4098. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7407
