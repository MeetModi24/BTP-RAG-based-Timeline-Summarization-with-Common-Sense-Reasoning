71. LETTER TO GOKALDAS H. THAKKAR
THE ASHRAM,
June 26, 1926
BHAISHRI GOKALDAS,
Your letter. I have nothing to write on except the charkha
and allied subjects. I have never seen your paper. And I do not
like to write for a paper I know nothing about. Please therefore
excuse me.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI GOKALDAS HIRAJI THAKKAR
SECRETARY, "SEVAK MANDAL"
SEVAK MANDAL KARYALAYA
OPPOSITE POST OFFICE
JAMNAGAR
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19921
