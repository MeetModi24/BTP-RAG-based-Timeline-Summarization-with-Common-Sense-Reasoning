443. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
ABBOTTABAD,
July 10, 193[9]
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I have been writing regularly. How about you? I hope your
health is all right. We are all well.
Ba and others have not arrived yet. A telegram intimating
their departure is expected today or tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 237
