539. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
January 8, 1933
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I got your letter and the leaflet. Your interpretation of the
dream is all right. We have so many things which are real and
true that we need little help from anything seen in a dream.
I am waiting for news of your marriage. Chhaganbhai is
with us at present. Jamnalalji also is in this jail, though not
with me. He is in excellent health.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6543
