464. LETTER TO DR. BHAGWAT
April 11, 1946
BHAI BHAGWAT,
I have your letter. Stay where you are. Do not worry, if
nobody comes up. We should visit people in their homes and
instruct them about the rules of hygiene. We should go into the
schools and undertake to teach there. For every moment there
is some work to be done. Write to me regularly. How is the
heat there?
See Zohrabehn and also Amtul.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C. W. 2756. Courtesy: Manilal Patel
