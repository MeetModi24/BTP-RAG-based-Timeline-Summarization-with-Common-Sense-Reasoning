294. LETTER TO KUSUM GANDHI
December 17, 1932
Every person who is ill holds the key to his life in his own
hands as long as recovery is possible. If he or she gives way to
despair, no medicine will help, and, on the contrary, if he or
she does not lose heart, even a simple powder will be a priceless
remedy. You should, therefore, remember three rules: Do not lose
courage; follow the advice of the person in whose hands you have
placed yourself; and, however much you suffer, go on repeating
Ramanama, remain cheerful and never cry.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, pp. 346-
