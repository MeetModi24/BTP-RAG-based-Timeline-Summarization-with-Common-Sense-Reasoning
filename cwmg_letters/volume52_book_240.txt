240. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
December 12, 1932
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter of the 10th. Radha's case is rather alarming.
As she has been running continuous temperature going up to 103
degrees, I think she must have become very thin and weak.
Kusum seems to be improving slowly. If you have now got more
information about Jethalal who died in the Sabarmati jail, to
what term he was sentenced and of what he died, for what offence
he was arrested, etc., please let me know.
I got Mithubehn's, Bhansali's and Kanti's letters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8280. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
