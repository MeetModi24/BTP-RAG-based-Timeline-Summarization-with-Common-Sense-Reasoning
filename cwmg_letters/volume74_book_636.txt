636. LETTER TO KHWAJA KHURSHED ALAM
October 5, 1941
BHAI KHWAJA SAHEB,
I have your letter. Salesmen in khadi stores work on very
meagre wages.
There are very few Muslims of this class willing
to work for so little. It is something that cannot be helped.
Muslims are also there where they are available. I do not like
your threat. You could have asked this question through the
Press. The question is pertinent. The answer is clear.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
