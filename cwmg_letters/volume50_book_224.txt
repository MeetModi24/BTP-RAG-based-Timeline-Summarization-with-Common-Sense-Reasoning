224. LETTER TO LAKSHMI
July 17, 1932
DAUGHTER LAKSHMI,
I have received your letter. Devdas had sent a wire to me
in respect of Varadachari and so I wrote a letter to you which you
must have received (by now). Along with it a letter (each) was
sent for Anna and Papa. What further consolation can I give?
All (our) sufferings vanish when we entertain no fear of death and
consider it as our greatest friend. Why should we grieve since the
soul is deathless? This idea should be strengthened. Well, I
hope Narasimhan has not hastened to you. If he has, tell Anna
that he should go back.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. LAKSHMI
GANDHI ASHRAM
TIRUCHENGODU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 339
