333. LETTER TO J. B. KRIPALANI
AHMEDABAD,
August 16, 1931
DEAR PROFESSOR,
Someone has to be sent to the Frontier Province for doing
khaddar work there, that is to say, teaching spinning, carding,
weaving, etc. Have you anybody in view whom you could send?
Khurshedbehn would like you to handle this work.
How do you like the latest developments?
ACHARYA KRIPALANI
GANDHI ASHRAM
MEERUT
