503. LETTER TO A. B. M. ENAYAT HUSSAIN
SODEPUR,
January 7, 1946
BHAI SAHEB,
I got your letter last evening. Today is my silence day.
Tomorrow I leave for Assam. You and your friends may come
on the 16th at 2.30 p. m. I shall manage to give you half an
hour.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
JANAB A. B. M. ENAYAT HUSSAIN
GENERAL SECRETARY
MUSLIM UNEMPLOYED RELIEF ASSOCIATION
18 MIRZAPUR STREET
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Urdu : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
