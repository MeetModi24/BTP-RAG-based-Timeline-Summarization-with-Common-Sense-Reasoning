147. LETTER TO G. N. KANITKAR
December 3, 1932
You say Harijans should be allowed to enter temples, because
all have broken the Shastras. This is an immoral attitude. If in
99 cases we have broken the Shastras that is no reason for break-
ing them in the 100th case. That is no reform but deform. My
point is that not to allow Harijans to enter temples is wrong and
against religion. This is a religious movement for elevation of religion.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 302
