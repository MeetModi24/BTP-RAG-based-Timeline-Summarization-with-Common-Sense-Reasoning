331. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI MADHAVJI THAKKAR
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 18, 1930
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
I got your letter. From others' letters I see that you had fever.
How is that? I hope no one there will fall ill. The time for real
work seems to be approaching now. Even when working the hard-
est, we should never feel worried within. It is God who does
everything and we are only instruments in His hands. If this truth
sinks into our heart, we shall never feel worried.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6801
