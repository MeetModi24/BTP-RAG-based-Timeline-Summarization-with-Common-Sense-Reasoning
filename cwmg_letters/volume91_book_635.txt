635. LETTER TO DHIRU JHAVERI
Monday [February 27, 1928]
CHI. DHIRU 2 ,
It is good that you came. Be true to your name and have
patience God will protect you. Dr. Muthu has a reputation
for succeeding and if he comes he might be instrumental in
your getting cured. But whether you are cured or not, learn to
bear suffering in patience.
It is good that Mother has joined you there. Give my pra-
nams to her. You need not reply. I get and will continue to get
news about your health from your father or Manibhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 1279
