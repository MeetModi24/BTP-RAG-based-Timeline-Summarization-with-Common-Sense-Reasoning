338. LETTER TO SIR EDWARD JENKINS
AS AT SEVAGRAM,
CAMP PANCHGANI,
July 29, 1944
DEAR SIR EDWARD,
I thank you for your letter of 16th July. I am thankful for
the enclosure of the letter to Miss Agatha Harrison.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR EDWARD JENKINS
THE VICEROY'S HOUSE
NEW DELHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, p. 35
