341. LETTER TO DR. S. K. VAIDYA
[After January 6, 1941]
I am in no hurry at all. If you can preserve your peace
of mind, by all means stay. You may go and get well and come
back after some time with the whole family. But this is only
by way of suggestion. Your wish will be my wish.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5743
