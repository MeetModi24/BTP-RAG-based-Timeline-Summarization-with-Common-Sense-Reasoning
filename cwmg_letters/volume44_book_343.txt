343. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM D. SARAIYA
October 23, 1930
CHI. KAKU,
You wrote in a really beautiful handwriting. But your letter
was a blank sheet of paper, so to say. You didn't even tell me what
work you had in Bombay. Where do you live? Kakasaheb sends
his blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Do you pray? Do you understand the Gita correctly?
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2806. Courtesy: Purushottam
D. Saraiya
