383. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS BIRLA
June 25, 1937
BHAI RAMESHWARDAS,
I got your letter. As regards the money I have had the
intimation from Bachharaj and Co. Almost a lakh will go to the
Village Industries Association. What you give for my personal
expenses is of course additional.
I used to obtain through Braj Mohan passages in cargo-boats for
the European workers bound for England. He is not there at present.
Whom should I write to in Calcutta? Or will you yourself enquire if
an Englishwoman can be accommodated in one of the cargo-boats?
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 8032. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
