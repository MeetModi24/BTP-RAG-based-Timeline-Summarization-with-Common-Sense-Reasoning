83. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
March 16, 1930
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. Restrain your desire to come and see
me. If, despite your best efforts, you cannot do that there is
no harm in coming. Keep in mind the condition that you should
take proper care of your health while working. You will not
get such an opportunity again. You should, therefore, preserve
your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9282
