347. LETTER TO S. K. BELVALKAR
NEW DELHI,
July 15, 1947
BHAI BELVALKARJI 2 ,
I have your letter.
I am confident that you will have all success in what you
are beginning with regard to the Shanti Parva on Akhi-panchami.
I am also of the view that you should receive sufficient finan-
cial help in your venture.
Thank you for proposing to send me a copy of the Bhishma
Parva when it is ready.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
