52. LETTER TO K. B. MENON
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 7, 1941
MY DEAR MENON,
I had both your letters. The first did not call for a reply.
I note what you say about the meeting.
As Seth Jamnalalji had a talk with Shri Vaze and as the
latter is understood to have undertaken even the editing of the
paper I have written to him and am awaiting his reply. I would
therefore suggest your holding the meeting after I have had the
reply.
I think your reply to Shri Jainarainji was correct.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PS.
I have your further letter today. If the Standing Commit-
tee ceases to function, J.N. also does likewise. But hand the
enclosed to J.N. and show this letter too to him. I shall
give you further instructions after I hear from J.N.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
