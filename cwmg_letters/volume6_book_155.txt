155. LETTER TO W.J. WEST
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 10, 1906
DEAR MR. WEST,
Please send a copy of Indian Opinion to Abdul Qadir, Esq., c/o
Thomas Cook & Son, Ludgate Circus, London, as exchange copy for
a monthly magazine which he will send.
Mr. Qadir is a graduate of Punjab University and the proprietor
of the magazine Urdo. He may also become our honorary contri-
butor.
Yours sincerely,
W. J. WEST, ESQ.
PHOENIX
DURBAN
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4541
