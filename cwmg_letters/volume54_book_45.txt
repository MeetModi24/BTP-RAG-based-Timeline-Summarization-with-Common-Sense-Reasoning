45. LETTER TO S. V. SONAVANE
March 10, 1933
MY DEAR SONAVANE 1 ,
I have your letter. I would like you to see Jajuji at Sheth
Jamnalalji's residence in Wardha and let him investigate your case
and report to me. Show him this letter. You will give him all the
particulars about yourself, your age, the members of your family
and any other information that he may need.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. S. V. SONAVANE
PULFILE
WARDHA
From a microfilm: S.N. 20520
