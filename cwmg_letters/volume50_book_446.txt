446. LETTER TO BEGUM MOHAMMAD ALAM
August 24, 1932
DEAR SISTER,
I was very glad to get your letter. I did get the letter from
Doctor Saheb. It was only yesterday I learnt from it your ad-
dress. I hope that Doctor Saheb is better. Keep me informed.
Please convey to him vandemataram from Sardar, Mahadev and
myself. We are all doing well by the grace of God. I hope you
will find no difficulty in deciphering this letter.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 25
