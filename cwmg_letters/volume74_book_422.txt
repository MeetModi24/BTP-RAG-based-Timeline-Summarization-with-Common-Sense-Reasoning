422. LETTER TO S. RADHAKRISHNAN
August 25, 1941
DEAR SIR R[ADHAKRISHNAN],
You do make yourself irresistible. You may book me provi-
sionally for 21st January next. I am not certain myself as to
what will happen between now and 21st January. Please defer
advertisement as long as you can.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
