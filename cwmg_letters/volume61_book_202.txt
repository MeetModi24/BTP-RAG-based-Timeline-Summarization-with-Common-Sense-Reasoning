202. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
June 6, 1935
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your letter. If I find the letter about neem, I shall send
it. Neem never causes heat. Even people who are taking a fast
eat neem. But I wish you to take as much milk as you can. Cow's
milk is the best. Give up all worry. I have already said that
you should spend this one year of abstention from studies with
me here. Come whenever you like.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 4272
