214. LETTER TO BHAU PANSE
January 26, 1933
CHI. BHAU,
Even if I am very busy, you must write to me. You should
go to Rajkot full of faith. It will not matter if you do not im-
prove. We shall think about fasting after you have tried Rajkot.
Describe your other experiences at Vijapur.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 4491. Courtesy: Bhau Panse
