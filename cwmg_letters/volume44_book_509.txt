509. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
December 7, 1930
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got both your letters. You have not been arrested so far,
though of course it would be good if you were. You should remain
unconcerned whether or not you are arrested. Go on patiently
doing all your work. I see that you have been having very useful
experiences there. I have Pyarelal with me now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9295
