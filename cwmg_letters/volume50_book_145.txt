145. LETTER TO LAKSHMI JERAJANI
July 2, 1932
DEAR LAKSHMI 1 ,
My hand pains while doing certain work. It is not paralysis.
There is no cause for anxiety. Rest is the chief remedy.
Vithaldas had written to me as regards eczema. Radium
must be regarded to have been efficacious.
Do send me your daily work.
Blessings from
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. 11, p. 63
