494. LETTER TO M. C. RAJAH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 14, 1938
DEAR FRIEND,
I must apologize for the delay in replying to your letter. I
have been overwhelmed with work. Now I have your wire.
I wish you would trust C. R. to do his best. He should
be allowed to do the thing in his own way. If you cannot trust,
naturally you will take the course which commends itself to you.
All I know is that Harijans have no better friend than him. Go
to him, reason with him and if you cannot persuade him, bear
with him. That is my advice.
Yours sincerely,
M.
K. GANDHI
