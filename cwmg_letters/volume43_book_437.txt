437. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN
Silence Day [May 26, 1930]
DEAR MOTIBEHN 5 ,
Ba told me that you were melancholy. How is that? One
who studies Gita cannot have melancholia. How can one be melan-
choly who is daily in meditation with God and believes that He
resides in the heart? Drive out melancholia.
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 577
