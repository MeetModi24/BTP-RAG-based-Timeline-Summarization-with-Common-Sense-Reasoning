393. LETTER TO P. C. GHOSH
SEVAGRAM,
March 10, 1945
CHI. PROFULLA,
I have your beautiful letter. If you can conveniently reach
Bombay on April 2, then do come. It is Amtul Salaam's duty to
leave in good shape what she has begun. Retain her. Relieve her
only when you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. P. C. GHOSH
14/ GARIAHUT ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
