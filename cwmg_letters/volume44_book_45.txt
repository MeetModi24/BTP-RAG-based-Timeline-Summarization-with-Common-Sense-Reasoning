45. LETTER TO AMINA QURESHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 19, 1930
CHI. AMINA,
I have your letter. Father and Qureshi are in jail. You wish
to be there and, moreover, you live in the Ashram. You should,
therefore, believe that you are in the struggle too. It is more
than enough if we are content to live where and in the condi-
tion that God wills. Do not give up your study of Urdu. Giriraj
will be able to teach you. Tell Father or Qureshi, whoever it is
you see, that I cannot forget either of them.
Blessings from
BAPU
