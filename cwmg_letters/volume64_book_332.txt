332. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 31, 1937
CHI. NARAHARI,
I got your letter. I will discuss Vinoba's opinion with him. I
have not been able to understand it.
The Goseva Sangh can take over the Goshala of Dhulia
without assuming financial responsibility for it. But the present
trustees of Dhulia would not like it.
When are you leaving for the Kathiawar tour?
I think the opinion that you have expressed to Rameshwar
regarding ghee is perfectly correct.
Blessings from
BAPU
