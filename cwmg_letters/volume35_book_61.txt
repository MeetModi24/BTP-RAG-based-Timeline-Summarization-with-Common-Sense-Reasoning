61. LETTER TO SURENDRA
[About October 4, 1927]
CHI. SURENDRA,
I have your letter. About Sharadabehn it was just a slip of
memory. Chi. Maganlal writes that she recovered long ago. I
intend to write to her tomorrow.
You should certainly make any change that needs to be
done about rising early in the morning; I should certainly not
insist on this point. There is no doubt that the first require-
ment is that everyone keeps good health. Those who naturally
wake up at 4 o'clock may do so and the others when the bell strikes.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9413
