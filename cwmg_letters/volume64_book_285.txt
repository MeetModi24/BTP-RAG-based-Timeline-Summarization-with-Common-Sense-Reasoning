285. LETTER TO LAKSHMIDAS ASAR
TRAVANCORE,
January 18, 1937
CHI. LAKSHMIDAS,
Hand over Anandi's and Puratan's letters to them. I believe
that everything will be all right. Send Anandi to reach Wardha
on the 24th. It will not matter if I reach there a day late.
Durga is there in Maganwadi. A letter for Velanbehn also is
enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5069.
Courtesy: Anandibehn
P. Buch
