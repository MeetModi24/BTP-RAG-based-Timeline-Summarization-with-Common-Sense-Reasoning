295. LETTER TO KALYANJI MEHTA
Chaitra Vad 12 [April 20, 1925]
BHAI KALYANJI,
Herewith Pragji's letter addressed to Parvati and to me.
Send Parvati's reply to me and return my letter, too, so that
I can send an acknowledgement to Pragji. If there is anything
else to be communicated to Pragji, let me have that also. Are you
going to see him?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2675
