36. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
[May 19, 1920]
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I find that there is a marked improvement in your health. You can get
even more benefit by keeping yourself cheerful. Do not worry about your work,
nor even about your health. Remain cheerful as is the wont of human nature.
Doing so, you will be able to take the full advantage of the pure air there. I
will go to Kashi in a few days. Therefore, coming there is just not possible.
I feel sorry about that.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 32956
