398. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO LIEUTENANT-
GOVERNOR
[July 12, 1919]
TO
THE PRIVATE SECRETARY TO
HIS HONOUR THE LIEUTENANT-GOVERNOR
LAHORE
DEAR SIR,
I enclose herewith copy of Young India dealing with the case of
Lala Radha Krishna, the Editor of the Pratap, for submission to His
Honour. I understand that the case is under His Honour's con-
sideration at the present moment. May I hope that Lala Radha
Krishna's sentence will be remitted?
I am,
Yours faithfully,
From a photostat: S.N. 6765
