266. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Sunday, Asadha Vad 8, August 1, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I send with this a telegram for you received here. For a
moment I thought of opening the envelope and relaying the
message by telegram, but then I felt that a telegram from a
person who did not know that you had already left this place
was not likely to contain anything very important.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SIR PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
PORBANDAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3204. Courtesy: Mahesh Pattani;
also S.N. 12231 and G.N. 5890
