381. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
[Before May 20, 1929]
CHI. KUSUM,
Indeed you were embarrassed. Nevertheless you were asked
to do what you pleased. Prabhavati is exhausted and is now
sound asleep near me. Throughout the night the din in the train
continued. One may say the Mahatma too has sometimes to
suffer the congestion in the third class. It is to be seen if
Prabhavati can maintain her health.
Whatever happens I shall take you along on my next tour.
We shall see how you stand it.
I hope Sulochanabehn is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1791
