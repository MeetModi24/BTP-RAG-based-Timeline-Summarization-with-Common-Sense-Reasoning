168. LETTER TO W. H. ARATHOON
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 13, 1906
DEAR MR. ARATHOON,
I am obliged to you for your note of even date. If you will kindly
let me have as many invitation cards as you can spare, I shall
distribute them amongst the M.P's.
I have received a copy of the Minutes of the meeting with Lord
Elgin. I am taking copies for distribution. I shall have much pleasure
in sending you one.
With many thanks for all the trouble you are taking,
Yours sincerely,
W. H. ARATHOON, ESQ.
3, VICTORIA STREET, S.W.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4559
