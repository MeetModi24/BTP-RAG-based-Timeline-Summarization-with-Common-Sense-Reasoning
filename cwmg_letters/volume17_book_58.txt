58. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
March 10, 1920
I sent you the balance from Amritsar and the bulk from
Lahore. I have endeavoured to go through everything as care-
fully as possible. I have considered all the suggestions you made
on the margin and incorporated those that I thought fit could be
so treated. Please let me know what progress is being made.
Shall we be ready for the 16th of April?
The Story of My Life, Vol. I, p. 332
