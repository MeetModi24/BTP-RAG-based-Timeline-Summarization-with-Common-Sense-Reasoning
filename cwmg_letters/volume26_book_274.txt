274. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
Sunday Chaitra Vad 4 [April 12, 1925]
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM 2 ,
I got your letter at Manavadar. May you live long and may
all your pious wishes be fulfilled.
I am in Bombay today. Chi. Jamnadas is with me. Chi.
Prabhudas met me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
On Tuesday I shall tour the Jalalpur taluk.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 894. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
