346. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 29, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your letter of the 20th. You can certainly let uncle
read my letters. We all wish that he should get well. For that
he should live under your care till he recovers completely and
should do whatever you ask him to do. I hope his health is
fast improving.
Sailen should come to you in a few days. He will write
directly to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. KRISHNAVARMA
NATURE CURE CLINIC
MALAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
