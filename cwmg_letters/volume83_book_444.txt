444. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
DELHI,
April 8, 1946
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
I have your letter. My advice is that you should continue
to do there whatever you can. Grow new food crops, vegetables,
etc. Stimulate the sympathy of the people.
I am glad that your activities are going on well.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. BHAGWANJI PURUSHOTTAM
HARIJAN ASHRAM
WADHWAN CITY
KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 403. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P.
Pandya
