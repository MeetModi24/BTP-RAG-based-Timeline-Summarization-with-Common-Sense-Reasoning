623. LETTER TO S. M. CHITRE
NEW DELHI,
October 17, 1946
BHAI CHITRE,
I have your letter.
Do what you can by your own effort. Why do you write
in English?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI S. M. CHITRE
HARDIKAR HOUSE
BHANDARA, C. P.
