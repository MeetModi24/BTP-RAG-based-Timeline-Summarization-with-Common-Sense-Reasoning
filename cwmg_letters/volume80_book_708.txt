708. LETTER TO S. K. PATIL
SIMLA,
July 11, 1945
BHAI PATIL,
I have your letter. What message can I send about Sir
Pherozeshah Mehta that can be published? Where is the time?
Bharucha wrote to me in this connection. I gave him a similar
reply. You should forget me for such things. For the rest, I
know the virtues of Sir Pherozeshah Mehta and the services he
had rendered, and I am his ardent admirer.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI S. K. PATIL
HEERA HOUSE
381 SANDHURST ROAD
BOMBAY-
