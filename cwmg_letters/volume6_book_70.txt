70. LETTER TO W.J. MACINTYRE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 2, 1906
DEAR MR. MACINTYRE,
I have your nice, chatty, and humorous letter. Your pun is good.
It is funny that you should not have been aware to the extent you now
are of the spirit of toleration I possess. However, we shall know each
other better when the mists have rolled away. By the time this reaches
you you will be nearing your examination. Mr. Ritch has passed, and
your sanguine letter inspires me with the hope that you will do likewise.
I expect tomorrow Mrs. Freeth's address.
Yours sincerely,
W. J. MACINTYRE, ESQ.
Box 6522
JOHANNESBURG
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4452
