37. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 20, 1934
DEAR SISTER 3 ,
I am grateful to you for the delicious apples and raisins
you sent me through Deenabandhu C. F. Andrews.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SMT. RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
SIMLA W.
From the original: C.W. 3511. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6320

2 The letter is not available.
