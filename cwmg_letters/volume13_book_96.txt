96. LETTER TO KOTWAL
AHMEDABAD,
Jeth Sud 1 [June 13, 1915]
BHAISHRI KOTWAL,
You seem to have forgotten me. I send herewith a draft of
the constitution for the Ashram. Please go through it and let me
have your considered comments. Anna has arrived at Ahmedabad.
You may also come when you have time and take a look at the
Ashram.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 3607
