87. LETTER TO N. C. KELKAR
May 25, 1925
DEAR MR. KELKAR,
I have your note. Of course we must not enter upon arbi-
tration unless we have a proper deed of reference. The parties
know that I have insisted upon that from the first. I have also
stipulated that they must not bind me to time.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 3114. Courtesy: Kashinath N. Kelkar
