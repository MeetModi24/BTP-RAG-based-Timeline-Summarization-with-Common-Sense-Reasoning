368. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
[After April 5, 1925]
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM4,
I received your letter yesterday. I am writing this reply after
the morning prayers. How years pass! I had not realized you
were already sixteen. I too wish that you should become devout
and brilliant as Hanuman. I am giving you better blessings than
you have asked for. I bless you that you may always try to be
like Hanuman. Therein lies achievement. We can then feel
that we are making whole-hearted effort and for one who feels
this there is only joy. Hanuman is an ideal character. Whether
we can attain to the ideal depends on many factors. The res-
ponsibility of making efforts, however, rests solely on us, and in
that rests our striving.
Radha told me that you thought that I had forgotten you.
I am forgetful, no doubt, but I have seen you and listened to
your singing so many times that I could not have easily forgotten
you. I often think of you and of others whom I can remember.
Hopes of a bright future rest only on young people like you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original; C.W. 914. Courtesy; Narandas Gandhi
