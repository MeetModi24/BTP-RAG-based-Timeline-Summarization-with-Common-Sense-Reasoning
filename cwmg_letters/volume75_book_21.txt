21. LETTER TO C. K. NARAYANASWAMY
SEVAGRAM,
October 14, 1941
DEAR NARAYANASWAMY,
I was glad to have your letter and to note that in the end
you began to like the Ashram and its people.
You will come with your wife whenever you like.
There is very little difference between your socialism and mine
as you subscribe so whole-heartedly to non-violence and the con-
structive programme.
I am organizing villages along the constructive line but I don't
mix with it the political programme so called. The two will not
blend, though those working both are Congressmen. The construc-
tive programme is complete in itself.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
SHRI C. K. NARAYANASWAMY
C/O "BOMBAY CHRONICLE"
BOMBAY
