180. LETTER TO TEGH RAM
November 24, 1941
BHAI TEGH RAMJI,
I have your letter. I am in correspondence with Shri Amar-
nathji. It will be enough to tell you that in Tandonji's absence
nothing will be done that might affect his policy.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
