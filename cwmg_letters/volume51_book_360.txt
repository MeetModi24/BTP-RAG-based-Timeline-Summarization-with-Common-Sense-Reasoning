360. LETTER TO SECRETARIES, FRIENDS OF INDIA
October 9, 1932
JOINT HON. SECRETARIES
FRIENDS OF INDIA
46 LANCASTER GATE
LONDON, W. 2
DEAR FRIEND [S],
I thank you for your letter of sympathy. The funds can be
sent to the anti-untouchability association formed under the chair-
manship of Sjt. G.D. Birla for the furtherance of its objects.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
