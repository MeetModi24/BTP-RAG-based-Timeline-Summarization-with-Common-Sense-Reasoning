452. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
March 24, 1935
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. You seem to be doing very good work. If
you sow plenty of leafy vegetables, you will have enough for all
the twelve months.
My weight is 109 lb.
Everything is being cooked by steam for the last two days, so
plenty of time is saved.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VASUMATIBEHN
UDYOG MANDIR
BOCHASAN (NEAR BORSAD)
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9396. Also C.W. 642. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
