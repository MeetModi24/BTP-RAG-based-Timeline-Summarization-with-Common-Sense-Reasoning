450. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON,
March 13, 1937
REBEL DEAR,
Your two letters were destroyed as soon as I finished reading
them. I am glad you have kept well. If you have that tape
measure by any chance, you will send it to Delhi.
You will tell me all about Shummy after you reach Jullun-
dur.
Of course I shall see Fielden. R. is with me.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3766. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6922
