367. LETTER TO SECRETARY, VAIDIK DHARMAVARDHINI
SABHA
December 24, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter supplying me with the population
figures for Ponnani Taluk. They will help me when I receive the
result of the referendum.
Yours sincerely,
THE SECRETARY
VAIDIK DHARMA VARDHINI SABHA, BEZWADA
From a microfilm: S.N. 18794
