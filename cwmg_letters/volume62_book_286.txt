286. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
March 9, 1936
CHI. SUMITRA,
How is it that you do not write to me? You must be enjoy-
ing yourself. Do play well and do study well, but understand
that all work or all play is of no real use. Can you read this
letter? Grandmother is here with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5786. Also C.W. 3009. Cour-
tesy: Vanamala M. Desai
