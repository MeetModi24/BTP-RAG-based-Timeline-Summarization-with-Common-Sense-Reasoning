50. LETTER TO PYARELAL
August 29, 1941
CHI. PYARELAL,
Kanaiyo is coming to take you. He will tell you everything. I have
also written to Mohammed. Sushila has gone to Bombay to acquire
more knowledge. The rest when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
