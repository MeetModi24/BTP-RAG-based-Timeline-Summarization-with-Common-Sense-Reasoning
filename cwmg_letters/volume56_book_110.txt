110. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
October 15, 1933
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Here is a letter for Mother. Let us all hope that she will be
well enough to take part in the ceremony.
Have you introduced the Roman character for writing Hindi?
A typed letter is going to you [today] with a copy of my
letter to Malaviyaji.
Love.
BAPU
From a facsimile: C.W. 10108
