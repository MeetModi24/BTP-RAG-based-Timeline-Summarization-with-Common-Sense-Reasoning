230. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
February 13, 1936
CHI. PRABHA,
You forgot to see that I wrote a letter to Jayaprakash. I
am now sending it with this. I hope you had no difficulty
on the way. Kanti and Kanu have properly settled down to their
work and the time-table is strictly followed. Manibehn is gui-
ding them. I had the support of her shoulder during the mor-
ning constitutional today. Anandi does the massaging with oil
in the afternoon, and Kanti and Kanu in the evening. You
should not worry about me at all. I felt better for not taking
ghee yesterday. I didn't take it today also, though I took all
the other things correctly. I had excellent sleep. The weather
is not cold at all. Be regular in writing to me, and do please
avoid grief on my account.
Blessings from
BAPU
