74. LETTER TO L. FRENCH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 20, 1920
L. FRENCH, ESQ.
CHIEF SECRETARY TO
THE PUNJAB Gov[ERNMEN]T
LAHORE
DEAR MR. FRENCH,
I hope you will please pardon me for not sending you the
Sargodha statements earlier. I had not brought them with me
to Ahmedabad, and more were being taken by a local worker.
And then, I have been travelling. I now send you a selection
out of the numerous statements I have received. I shall hope to
hear from you in due course. Will you please let me know whether
the punitive police has been withdrawn?
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S. N. 7126
