600. LETTER TO SHASHIBHUSHAN SINGH
BOMBAY,
April 6, 1945
THAKUR SAHEB,
I have your letter informing me of the death of your elder
brother. May God give fortitude and peace of mind to the sons
347
of your widowed sister-in-law. The late Thakursaheb has won
the battle of life.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
THAKUR SHRI SHASHIBHUSHANJI
ZAMINDAR, MANEGAON
P. O. MEKH, TEHSIL NARASINGHPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
