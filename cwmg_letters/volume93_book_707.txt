707. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
April 2, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I should have received your letter today. I have a letter
from Ba in which she writes about her pain. For the present,
try if you can to write to me a couple of lines every day. If
you think that Ba needs to be brought here do not hesitate to
do so. We shall take Ba to Bombay if it is necessary. I shall
accompany you. Being far away, I tend to take a serious view
of the disease. We have not fully understood your medical
language. I have not tried to understand it by consulting
anyone. I shall feel happy if you can send even a telegram.
Otherwise I am not worried since you are there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
