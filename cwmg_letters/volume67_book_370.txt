370. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[About August 18, 1938]
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have been thinking about Khare. Do give up the idea of
writing the article. About Pyarelal, I will let God guide me.
Herewith is a bottle containing Balkrishna's sputum. If the
bottle can be returned, he wants it back. We shall pay no
attention to his fever just now. Let them send any medicine
they think proper.
You need not worry about me. I have been forewarned.
So there will be no trouble. Ultimately God's will must prevail.
Read the letter addressed to Shanta and give it to her.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
If Shantikumar can pay the money, let him do so. Let him
not do so against his will at all.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11536
