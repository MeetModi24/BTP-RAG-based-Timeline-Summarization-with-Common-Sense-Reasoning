391. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
October 12, 1932
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
Today I will not write in Urdu.
You do deserve shabash for curing yourself quickly. You will
get well quicker if you won't worry about meeting me. When
God wills it, we shall meet. Meanwhile let us be thankful that
we can write to each other.
Yes, I will write to the other girls about the baths.
Let us hope Kudsia will find her peace during her walks
with Narandas. She will, if she keeps good health.
As for your womb it must not be removed unless it is diseas-
ed. For that you should consult Mrs. Lazarus and do as she
advises. There should be no hurry about this operation. Opera-
tions are not always a sure cure. Natural treatment is the most
efficacious in the long run and never harmful. Operations have
often been harmful and at times even proved fatal.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 259
