55. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Kartika Sud 14, 1983 [November 18, 1926]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your letter. Regarding Geneva I have given my opi-
nion a few days ago at your Kashi address.
It appears from Deviprasadji's letter that you are bound by
promise. If such is the case the question of going or not going
does not arise. As it is, you ought to go.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 6138. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
