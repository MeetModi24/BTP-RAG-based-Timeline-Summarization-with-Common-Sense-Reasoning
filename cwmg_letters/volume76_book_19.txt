19. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
April 8, 1942
BAPA,
I have read your note. About Bhai Jagannath, it is somewhat
complicated. I shall see. I have done nothing much about your
fever. The old must protect themselves against fever. Take com-
plete rest.
I hope Balkrishna has started Gadodiyaji's treatment. I have
had no news about him.
BAPU
SHRI BAPA
HARIJAN NIVAS
KINGSWAY
7
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1191
