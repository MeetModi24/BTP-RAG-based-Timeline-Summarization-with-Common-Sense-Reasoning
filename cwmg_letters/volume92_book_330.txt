330. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
Y.M.,
July 13, 1930
CHI. SHANKERLAL,
I have your letter. One who understands the meaning of
ahimsa must realize the importance of the takli and the charkha.
It becomes clearer each day that they are visible manifestations
of ahimsa.
Take care of your health. Did Gulzarilal receive my letter?
Ask him to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: G. N. 11549
