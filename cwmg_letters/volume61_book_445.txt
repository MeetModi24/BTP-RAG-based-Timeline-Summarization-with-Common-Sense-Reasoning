445. LETTER TO ISHWARDAS
August 3, 1935
BHAI ISHWARDAS,
I have both your letters. You seem to have a mania for ask-
ing questions. So I will not answer them. Rest content with what-
ever you can gain from Harijan and Harijan Sevak and try to
put into practice as much as you like in it to the best of your
ability.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
