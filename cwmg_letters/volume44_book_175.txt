175. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 6, 1930
CHI. LILAVATI (ASAR),
I got your letter. Narandas tells me in his letter that your
fainting fits have become more frequent. In such a case you re-
quire rest. Spend a few days in the Ashram or at any other
place where you think you will feel happy. But you can do this
only with Khurshedbehn's permission. You should write pure
truth in your diary. Don't mind who reads it. It is to our
good to appear to the world exactly as we are. There is in this
no humiliation at all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9564
