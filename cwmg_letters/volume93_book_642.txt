642. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
January [20]1, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I received your letter of the 13th today on the 21st.
Your keeping awake at night is terrible. It will be enough
if it does not spoil your health.
As for Tari's health, you must instruct her friend to write
so that at least for the present I am posted with her condition
every day — not for myself, but for the sake of Gomatibehn and
others. In the midst of your heavy work, I cannot expect from
you daily letters or detailed information.
You will have received in time the telegram I sent concern-
ing Amtul Salaam. That is indeed an addition to your burden
of work. But her health was so bad that the telegram could
not be avoided. Of course, it is true that I could not have a
correct idea of your burden of work.
Here, Ramnarayan and Manu have been ill. Both are
better.
I had written about diet only for you to think over.
Brother is doing well. That is what those who visited Jamnalalji
told me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I had to pay a charge of one anna on your letter. You
should use thin paper.
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
