207. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
SEVAGRAM,
October 9, 1944
BHAI MUNSHI,
I was glad you sent your reply so soon. It has served the
purpose. But can that prohibitory order be defended from the
point of view of the right of freedom of expression? Do you agree
with the opinion I expressed to Homi1?
I read your statement made in Jaipur. I did not like it. Is
it not opposed to the Congress policy? Do you think the policy
enunciated at Jaipur is contrary to the policy that we had
jointly adopted at Nagpur ? I would not think it wrong if it should
be so. I want to know only for my information. Has a difference
of opinion arisen between us in this matter too?
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 7683. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
