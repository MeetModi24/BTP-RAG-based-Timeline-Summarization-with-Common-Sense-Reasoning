74. LETTER TO TARA R. MODI
ON RETURN FROM VEDCHHI,
Thursday [March 17, 1927]
CHI. TARA,
It was very good that you came to the Ashram. The training the
women get at the Ashram at present is not to be had anywhere in the
world. Do whatever work you can while taking care of your health.
Look after the children well, as if they were your own children. Keep
Vimu with you if she agrees. I do not know where you have decided
to stay. If you are going to stay in the hostel Vimu will have to live
away from you. Take particular care of her even if she lives away. It
would be great if she could by some means come into your grip.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : G. N. 4186
1 Inferred from the fact that Gandhiji returned to Bardoli from Vedchhi
on March 17, 1927 which was a Thursday.
