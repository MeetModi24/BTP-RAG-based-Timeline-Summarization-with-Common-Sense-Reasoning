97. LETTER TO KASTURBHAI LALBHAI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 26, 1928
BHAISHRI KASTURBHAI,
I have your letter. I will be ready on Tuesday at 6.15 and wait for your
friends in order that I may reach there and present myself.
Greetings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33138
