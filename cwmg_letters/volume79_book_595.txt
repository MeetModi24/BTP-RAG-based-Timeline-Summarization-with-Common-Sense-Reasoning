595. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
BOMBAY,
April 4, 1945
CHI. KISHORELAL,
If Kelkar treats you even for a few days and the treatment
is effective, you can continue it wherever you go, can't you?
What I told you about silence was from my experience.
You will practise it and can practise it only when it grows from
within the heart.
Tell Durga that both the girls and Mahadev's mother came
to see me yesterday. Paramanand was with them.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
[PS.]
Pushpa came to see me yesterday. I have sent her home.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
