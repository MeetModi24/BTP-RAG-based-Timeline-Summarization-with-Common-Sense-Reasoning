37. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
[October 16, 1941]
CHI. RUKHI 3 ,
I have your hundi. The money will be spent as you say. I
hope you are keeping perfect health. Who gets the letter-papers
for you printed in English? How many letters do you have occa-
sion to write in English?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9162. Also C.W. 10130. Cour-
tesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
