396. LETTER TO NRISIMHAPRASAD K. BHATT
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
BHAI NANABHAI,
I have your letter. I cannot come to such a quick decision;
I can say something only after we meet. Let us hope Bachu's
affairs are settled without any hitch.
Blessings from
BAPU
NANABHAI BHATT
HINDUSTAN STORES
CHOWPATTY
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
