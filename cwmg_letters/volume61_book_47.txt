47. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
May 3, 1935
I am writing to Dr. Gopichand. Your account here shows
a balance of Rs. 53. Rs. 42 is your railway fare. But I don't
remember the details. My advice is that you may leave Rs. 100
as a standing account.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha, pp. 157-
