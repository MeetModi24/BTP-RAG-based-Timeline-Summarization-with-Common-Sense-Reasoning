344. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
[After April 15, 1924]
I am deeply thankful to you for the attention you are giving
Ramdas. I think that the regular training he is having at present
from you is likely to do him good and steady him.
I hope your mother is making satisfactory progress after her
operation. Give her my very kind regards.
The Story of My Life, Vol. II, p. 265
