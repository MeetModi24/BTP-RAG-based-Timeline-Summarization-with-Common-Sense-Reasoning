77. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
ON THE TRAIN,
October 28, 1946
CHI. RAMDAS,
I am writing this on my way to Bengal. I got your note.
You had better preserve your health.
I had a postcard from Sumi. She seems to be deeply im-
mersed in her studies. I don't know when I shall be able to
return from Bengal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
