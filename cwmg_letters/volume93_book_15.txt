15. LETTER TO N. S. HARDIKAR
WARDHA,
March 13, 1935
DEAR DR. HARDIKAR,
On inquiring of Kamala Devi about you, she told me that
you were suffering from T. B. I was distressed. Please let me
know how you are faring.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. HARDIKAR
HOOBLI
KARNATAKA
From
the original: N. S. Hardikar Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
