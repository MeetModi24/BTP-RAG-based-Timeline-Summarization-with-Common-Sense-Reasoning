8. LETTER TO PURATAN J. BUCH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 20, 1941
CHI. PURATAN,
I got your letter. The news that you give does not seem cor-
rect; but even if it is true, we must keep ourselves ready. I hope
both of you are quite prepared. You may write whatever you
wish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9179
