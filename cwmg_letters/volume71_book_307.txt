307. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 7, 1940
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
Here is a letter from Lilian I have sent her a consoling
reply. I have your note.
You need have no worry about the
sisters. I have told you about Kallenbach's money. But you will
not use it for any other purpose no matter what requests for aid
you might have.
I hope you are making steady progress.
Love from us all.
MOHAN
From a photostat: G.N. 993
