254. LETTER TO MADALASA
SEGAON,
February 13, 1940
CHI. MADALASA,
So you are again down with fever, are you? Do not feel dis-
pirited. Get well soon. What we can gain from illness is to increase
our faith in God and learn to control our nature. That also helps
one to get well soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 320
