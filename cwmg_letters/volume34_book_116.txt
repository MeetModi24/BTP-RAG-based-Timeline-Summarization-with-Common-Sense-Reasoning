116. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
July 8, 1927
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letters. There seems to be one missing still.
Absence of further telegraphic news from you I take to mean
good news.
Do stay as long as you wish and certainly till you are quite
restored to health. Health must be kept.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5246. Courtesy: Mirabehn
