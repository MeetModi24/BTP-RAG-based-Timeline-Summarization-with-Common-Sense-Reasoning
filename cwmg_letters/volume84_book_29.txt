29. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
DELHI,
April 19, 1946
CHI. JIVANJI1,
I saw the sample slips printed with blocks for the Urdu edi-
tion of Harijan Sevak. They are all right. I shall have nothing
to say regarding the different sizes, for you will be using them
according to your requirements. It is true that you had shown
them to me before proceeding further.
How bad it would be if we could not publish in book form
the writings received from Ashadevi ! I think we must if neces-
sary, make arrangements with some other printer and publish
them. If even that is not possible, let me know.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 9966. Also C. W. 6940. Cour-
tesy : Jivanji D. Desai
