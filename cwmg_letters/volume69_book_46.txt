46. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
RAJKOT,
March 8, 1939
CHI. PREMA,
Sushila is sitting by my side. She does her work absent-
mindedly. I was in fact in perfect bliss. As for the rest of the
news, Sushila has written to you. To write more would mean
disobeying doctors.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10398. Also C.W. 6837. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
