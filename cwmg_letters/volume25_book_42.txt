42. LETTER TO SHWAIB QURESHI
September 1, 1924
MY DEAR SHWAIB 1 ,
I had hoped to have a long chat with you yesterday but that
was not to be.
Take care of Kristodas. God take care of both of you.
You must not pay the railway fare. Please let the account at
my disposal pay for it.
Of course you will not resist Hyderabad authorities in any way.
Ours is not a public inquiry. Indeed not an inquiry at all in
the proper sense of the term. It is merely gathering information
for my sake.
You will see all parties who will permit themselves to be seen
by you including officials and vakeels for both parties. If you can
bring photographs please bring.
After you have seen everything, I suggest each of you writing
out your own impressions and then comparing them and evolving
an agreed report or statement.
Kristodas tells me you are sad and despondent. How can that
be? You have abundant faith in God. He cares for the least of
His creatures. Why then should they care at all? Is it not enough
that we do His will, as we know it, and be cheerful?
Yours,
M. K. G.
