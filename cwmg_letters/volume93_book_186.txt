186. LETTER TO GLADYS OWEN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 15, 1937
DEAR GLADYS1,
Your letter is touching. I know that like the good people
at Sat-Tal many are praying unknown to me.
I am glad Celia also is having a little bit of cool air with
you. My love to both of you and regards to all at the Ashram.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
MISS GLADYS OWEN
THE ASHRAM
SAT-TAL
Via BHIM TAL
From a photostat: C. W. 11280: Courtesy: Dr. Priyamvada Mathur
