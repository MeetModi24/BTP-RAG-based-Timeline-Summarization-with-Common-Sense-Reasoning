185. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 8, 1930
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
Have not yet got the pamphlet sent by you. There is a thrill
that is sattvik and one that is rajasik. What category would your
writings fall in? We expect to hear more about the American
lady. I hope the Ashram life will not prove too trying for her. Our
joint congratulations to Mrs. Appasamy on her introduction of
the wheel and the takli in her school.
Love.
BAPU
