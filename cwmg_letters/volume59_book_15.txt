15. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
September 17, 1934
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I got your letter. I hope you are fully recovered now.
You have solved all the problems well.
It is best that the cart should move with its natural speed.
Can we not say now that it is no more the motor-car age but
it is the aeroplane age? But all this rushing about is only for a
while. Our legs, however, will remain as long as mankind
survives. Those who are content with the natural speed of their
legs remaining unimpaired will win in the end.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. SHANTIKUMAR NAROTTAM MORARJEE
SUDAMA HOUSE
BALLARD ESTATE
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4724. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
