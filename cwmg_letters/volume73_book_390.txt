390. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 31, 1941
BHAI SATIS BABU,
Get from here the money for your expenses. Or, shall I send
you Rs. 15? It is suggested that Khadi Pratishthan should get cer-
tification. It is good for us to have it. I believe there would
be no difficulty in getting it. Jajuji has sent me Hem Prabha's
letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2735
