563. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
WARDHA,
April 10, 1935
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I got your letter. What you have done seems perfectly all
right. By all means go and stay for some time in that part. I
will talk to Janakidevi about the engagement.
When Chandrakant's father writes to me, I will do what is
necessary. I hope your ear does not give you any trouble.
This is enough for now.
Blessings to Kamala.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2962
