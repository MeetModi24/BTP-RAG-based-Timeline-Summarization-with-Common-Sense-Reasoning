540. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
March 27, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
The mind must be concentrated during these two minutes
and in the second half of the prayer. This does not mean that
we cannot concentrate on some spiritual thing. That is bound to
happen.
What is the meaning of concentrating on Ramanama or Aum?
Yes, let us not deceive the mind with regard to spiritual things.
That is why I asked why you had not familiarized yourself with
the Gita. About the hospital, some other time.
Blessings from
BAPU

Prabhakar
