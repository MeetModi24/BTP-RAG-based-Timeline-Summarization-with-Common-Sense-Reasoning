390. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 1, 1940
CHI. PRABHA,
It is very difficult to suggest anything about Father. My ad-
vice is that he should give up medical treatment. Let him go on
repeating Ramanama. He should live on milk and fruit only.
He may give up even milk, and take only fruit juice. He may
take milk of course if he can digest it. Let him rest peacefully and
wait for the end. You may show him this letter. Do what you
think best. Come when you can. Take care of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3546
