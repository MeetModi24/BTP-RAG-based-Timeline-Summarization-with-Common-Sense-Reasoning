443. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
WARDHA,
August 3, 1935
CHI. JAMNALAL,
You are proceeding to Indore to collect funds for the purse.
In this connection you want to be clear on these three points:
1. How will this money be spent?
2. Should any earmarked donation be accepted?
3. Whether I wish to form a trust or a committee to deal
with the expenditure or if I have thought of any alternative
arrangement.
Here is my clarification on these points: Although my main
requirement is for the propagation of Hindi in South India, I
would like to utilize the funds when occasion arises for provinces
like Bengal, Assam, Sind, Gujarat and Punjab where Hindi is
not known well or has not been propagated. There should be
no objection to accepting for the purse earmarked contributions
if a donor wishes to contribute for the work in any of these pro-
vinces or for the training of necessary propagandists.
Now about a trust or a committee. I intend to form a trust
or a committee or a registered institution when the whole sum is
collected and disburse the funds through it under my own super-
vision.
Blessings from
BAPU
