365. LETTER TO O. M. THOMAS
"MORARJI CASTLE",
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 13, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Your registered letter of the 8th inst. to Gandhiji has been received today. Your
previous letter also was received. Here are answers to your questions.
1. Gandhiji has no recollection of having read the book referred to by you.
2. Impossible to say anything at this distance of time.
1 After Mother India by Harry H. Field
201
3. The reply is in the negative so far as my recollection goes, but you can look
up the files of Young India.
4. Please send an authentic copy of Mrs. Annette Helen Doharty's affidavit
referred to by you. On the face of it, it seems to be a malicious invention.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI O. M. THOMAS
20 ELGIN ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
