203. LETTER TO ISHVARBHAI PATEL
March 5, 1932
CHI. ISHVAR,
I got your letter. Both of us were pleased. Persuade Napo-
leon also to write sometimes. And you should continue to write.
I hope all of you are well.
178
Blessings from us both.
BAPU
CHI. ISHVARBHAI PATEL
C/O GANGABEHN KUNVARJI
VANZ
Via SACHIN
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2696
