95. LETTER TO RAM MANOHAR LOHIA
August 22, 1947
CHI. RAM MANOHAR,
I will write tomorrow about Nepal.
Smoking can be given up all at once. Those who smoke
moderately cannot keep to a limit. If we workers do not have
such will power, what work can we expect to do?
It is now
that we have to do real work.
Have you understood the Goa problem fully?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Mahatma, Vol. VIII, between pp. 120
and 121
