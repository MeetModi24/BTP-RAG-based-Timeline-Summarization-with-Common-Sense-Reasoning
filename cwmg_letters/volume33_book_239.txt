239. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
Sunday [April 10, 1927]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I am not unhappy at Chi. Mama's passing away. The poor
girl was in agony. And it was good that you were with her at
the last hour. I have written to [your] son-in-law. We are left
with the problem of what we should do about the children. The
question now is whether or not you should accept custody of the
children if he leaves them in your care and offers their main-
tenance. Please think over this. I am sure you have not been upset.
I have been getting your letters. I may say I am keeping
well. During the last two days I have been walking a little.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8827. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
