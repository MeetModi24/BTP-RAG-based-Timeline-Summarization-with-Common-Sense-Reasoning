709. LETTER TO PRINCIPAL, KANYA GURUKUL
October 20, 1945
PRINCIPAL,
I have your letter of 4th September.
I cannot involve myself in the activities you have men-
tioned because I don't have the time.
PRINCIPAL
RAMDEV SMARAK NIDHI KANYA GURUKUL
60 RAJPUR ROAD
DEHRA DUN
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
