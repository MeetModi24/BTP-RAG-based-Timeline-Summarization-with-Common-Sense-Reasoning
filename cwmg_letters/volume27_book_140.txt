140. LETTER TO RAJA MAHENDRA PRATAP
[On or before June 15, 1925]
I continue to hear from you from time to time. I know
that we differ in our outlook upon life. I know there are as
many minds as there are men. But as cold and heat cannot co-
exist in the same place at the same time and in the same circum-
stances, so can violence not coexist with non-violence at the same
time and place and in the same circumstances.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
