473. LETTER TO DRAUPADI SHARMA
August 12, 1935
CHI. DRAUPADI,
Sharma is to leave today. While he was yet in Calcutta
I had no worry regarding you. Henceforth it becomes my duty
to keep myself informed regarding you all. Give me the news
about yourself and the children. Write about your way of living
and also give me your daily routine.
Tell me who are all the people who help you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Are there any books belonging to the Kanya Ashram?
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah
Varsha, facing p. 184
