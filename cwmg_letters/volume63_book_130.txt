130. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
June 30, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
If there is no satisfactory clearance, why not castor oil or
Epsom salt. If you need any other opening medicine, I can
send it. Shall I send any vegetable from here?
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6351. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9817
