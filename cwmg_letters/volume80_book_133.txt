133. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
May 6, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
Instead of tearing this up, I am sending it for all of you to
read.
I hope you yourself are well. Prabhakar should take all the
necessary treatment and get well. If he wishes to go to Bhima-
varam he may do so. But I am quite sure that he can get well
there if he is regular in food and other habits.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 9108. Also C.W. 9179. Cour-
tesy : Chimanlal N. Shah
