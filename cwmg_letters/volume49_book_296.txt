296. LETTER TO NIRMALA H. DESAI
March 26, 1932
CHI. NIRMALA,
Your elder brother's weight has increased and the leg has
improved so much that he can walk now. There is not the
slightest cause for worry. If brother and sister are of the same
age, the sister can ask the brother and learn things from him.
It is a sister's duty to do what will be for the brother's good,
regardless of their age.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9468
