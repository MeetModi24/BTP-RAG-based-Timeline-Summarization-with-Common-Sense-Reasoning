272. LETTER TO BENNET
Monday [August 8, 1921]
DEAR MR. BENNET,
I thank you for drawing my attention to the evil of prostitu-
tion in Lucknow. I have known of it and have talked about it too.
I shall do so again before leaving. But I shall write about it too in
Young India. Thanks for your offer of help in the matter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 11668
