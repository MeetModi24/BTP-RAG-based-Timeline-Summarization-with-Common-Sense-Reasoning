459. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
September 1, 1941
CHI. NARAHARI,
Read the accompanying papers when you are free and see if it
is necessary to do anything. Return Bhagwanji's letter to him after
reading it. If you think it necessary, meet any of the people whose
names he has mentioned.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9124
