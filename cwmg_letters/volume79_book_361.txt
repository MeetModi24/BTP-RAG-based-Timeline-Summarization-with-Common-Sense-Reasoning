361. LETTER TO JAYAKUNWAR M. DOCTOR
SEVAGRAM,
March 7, 1945
CHI. JEKI,
I have your letter. Where should I look for Ratubhai?
Sometimes we have found him after six months. We can get a
photograph of him published in the newspapers and offer a re-
ward. However, I do not feel like doing that. What can we
do if he has left? It will certainly be good if he is found.
I am making inquiries about the jewellery. I had forgotten
about it. I am all right. I just heard that Ratilal has safely
arrived in Ahmedabad.
Blessings from
BAPU
JEKIBEHN
35 JUHU LANE
ANDHERI
