297. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
Silence Day [About March, 1930]
CHI. KANTA,
I do think of you often. I have built great hopes on you.
Are you getting letters from Brother? Where is he? What are
you doing? Has Krishna gone home? Write all details.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
