135. LETTER TO SHUAIB QURESHI
February 27, 1918
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I am ashamed of myself. I am most anxious to be there. Yet
the facts seem to have conspired against me. The strike is still on
and it is of such a delicate nature that I dare not leave it. The
Kaira affair, too, involving as it does the rights of several lacs
of people, demands my attention. I know that delay about Ali
brothers is dangerous. I therefore stay where I am till I feel free.
I know you will not have me do otherwise. Will you please apolo-
gize to Maulana Saheb? Do please keep me informed of what goes
on there.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
