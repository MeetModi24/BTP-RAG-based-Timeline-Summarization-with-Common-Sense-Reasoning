46. LETTER TO KANTI GANDHI
Bhadarva Sud 3 [September 2, 1924]
CHI. KANTI 2 ,
I have not asked you to write to me. You have become very
wise. I had therefore nothing to say to you. But you should cer-
tainly write to me in a clear and good hand and let me know your
ideas. Take care of Rasik and ask him to write to me. We have
had good rains here. Nothing has been decided yet about Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. KANTI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 10149
