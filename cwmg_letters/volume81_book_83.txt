83. LETTER TO NAIDU
July 29, 1945
BHAI NAIDU,
Who can stop it if conversion takes place in the manner
you describe? Read what I have written on the subject. If we
want purification every Hindu will have to become an Atishudra.
I hope you will be able to read my Hindustani.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 94
