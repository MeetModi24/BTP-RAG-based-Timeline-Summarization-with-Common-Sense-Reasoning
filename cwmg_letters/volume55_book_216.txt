216. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
June 6, 1933
CHI. RAMA,
You may write to me now. How is the condition of the arm?
Is the pain still as bad as it has been? How is your study going
on? What is the condition of your mind?
How are the children? How is Dhiru's health?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5351
