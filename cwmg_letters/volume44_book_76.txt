76. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 27, 1930
BHAI VITHALDAS,
I got the figures for April, May and June. I see from them
that no one yet gives yarn in sufficiently large quantity. You can
write to me a detailed letter on this subject.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9775
