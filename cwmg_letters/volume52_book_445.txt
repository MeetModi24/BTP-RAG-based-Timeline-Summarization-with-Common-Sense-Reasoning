445. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
January 1, 1933
BOYS AND GIRLS,
You are doing very good work. Keep it up. Once you
learn farming you will find it more interesting than any other
work. And when one gets familiar with plants, one feels they
are part of one's family.
And service of Harijans itself is dharma.
Concentrate on whatever work you are doing.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
