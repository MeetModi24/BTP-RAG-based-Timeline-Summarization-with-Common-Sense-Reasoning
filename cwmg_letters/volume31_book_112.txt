112. LETTER TO BHUPENDRA NARAYAN SEN
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 7, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I now understand somewhat better
your plans. But tell me wherein you differ from Khadi Pratish-
than and Abhoy Ashram in your plans to develop some of the
items of constructive programme on your own lines.
The only suggestion I have about malaria is its prevention.
There is no certainty about it. But I feel that if a person keeps
his body pure and unlogged and takes the ordinary precautions
about pure water and pure food, he is not likely to be troubled
by malaria.
You ask me to tell you all about my health. There is, however,
nothing to tell because it seems to be quite all right for the
moment.
Yours sincerely,
