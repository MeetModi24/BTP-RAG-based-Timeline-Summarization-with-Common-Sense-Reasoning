221. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
August 18, 1945
CHI. SHRIMAN,
I did take up your book to read but could not complete it.
You are leaving tomorrow morning. I shall not be able to send
it before that. I shall send it from either Poona or Bombay.
It will be good if you can come to Poona at an early date.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 307
