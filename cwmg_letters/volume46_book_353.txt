353. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
There is still the fire-arms question to be decided. I do not
know whether you obtained legal advice in the matter. Whatever
the legal advice, I am quite certain of the conversation about this
very matter at the time I suggested the expression "illegal possession".
If you will look up the original draft, you will perhaps recall the
conversation because the original draft excluded fire-arms.
May I remind you about Mathura and Ludhiana incidents.
These are both matters deserving close scrutiny.
Yours sincerely,
H. W. EMERSON, ESQ.
SIMLA
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
