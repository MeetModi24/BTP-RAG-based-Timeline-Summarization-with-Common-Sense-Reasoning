378. LETTER TO RADHA GANDHI
May 2, 1930
CHI. RADHA,
Mithubehn told me that you were eager to work with her.
She intends to entrust you with a responsible task. If you really
wish to work with her, I will not dissuade you. But Khurshedbehn
also told me that you wished to work with her. If you gave her
your word first you should work with her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8682. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
