255. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
May 20, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have a letter from Amtul Salaam. She has sent a mes-
sage for you to the effect that you should go and bring back
Kanchan. She has become very weak. In these circumstances,
you must either go yourself or send someone. If A. S. herself
accompanies her from Borkamata, nothing like it. But I cannot
decide about all this from here. You yourself should decide.
Kanchan also is tired of the place. She must, therefore, return.
A. S.'s letter is enclosed. I am writing to her as above as also
to Kanchan. If she has already left, you need not worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8453. Also C.W. 5578. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
