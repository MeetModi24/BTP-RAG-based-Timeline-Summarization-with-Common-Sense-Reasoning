178. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
February 25, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
Received your letter. The inmates of the Ashram can never
abuse one another. But quite often, what is not meant to be abuse
sounds like abuse. Hence, let me know what you consider to be
abuse and also inform me who uses those words. Anyone who
stays in the Ashram must abandon fear. Whom need we fear?
We should boldly tell even the elders what we regard as the
proper thing.
Blessings from
BAPU
