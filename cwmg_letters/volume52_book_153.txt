153. LETTER TO E. E. DOYLE
December 4, 1932
DEAR COL. DOYLE,
Many thanks for your note.
I did say during the morning conversation that the present
practice of employing the so-called low-caste prisoners could not
be suddenly changed and that therefore there was all the greater
reason why real volunteers should be encouraged for conservancy
work whenever they make the offer spontaneously. I recognize too
that the experiment can only be made cautiously till the barrier
between high and low is broken down.
After I made the statement about the fast, the committee
released me from the task of a discussion on untouchability.
Thanks for sending the letter to Appasaheb.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
