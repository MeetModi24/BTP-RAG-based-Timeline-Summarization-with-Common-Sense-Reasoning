394. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA SHARMA
SEVAGRAM,
March 10, 1945
BHAI BALKRISHNA,
I have your wire. Today I have received your letter too. It
is good that you have been released. Kumari Sarala always has
my blessings for her health. There is no reason why she should
not recover after sufficient rest and proper diet.
Blessings from
BAPU
PANDIT BALKRISHNA
"PRATAP"
CAWNPORE, U. P.
