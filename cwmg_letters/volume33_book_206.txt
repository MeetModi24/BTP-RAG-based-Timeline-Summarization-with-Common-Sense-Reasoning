206. LETTER TO JAMNALAL
March 28, 1927
[CHI.] JAMNALALJI,
You ought not to have been upset. The light is bound to
go out one day; now it has only dimmed. It is all the same to
us whether it dims or dies away. That which gives light must
grow faint and go out.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
