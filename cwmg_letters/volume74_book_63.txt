63. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 9, 1941
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter. We have a number of different methods.
One is yours, the second Lakshmidas's1, the third Vinoba's, the
fourth (perhaps) Jajuji's2, the fifth that of the Meerut Ashram
(that is, Kripalaniji's), the sixth Mathuradas's3, the seventh Diwan-
ji's. Still more names can be added. None of these methods is per-
fect. If any of them was, it would have been universally adop-
ted. But only a creation of God can be perfect. Since I have
faith in you, I did not permit the formation of the committee
which you could not join. Now that you are ready, you may
certainly form one along the lines you have suggested and
intensify your work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8582. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
