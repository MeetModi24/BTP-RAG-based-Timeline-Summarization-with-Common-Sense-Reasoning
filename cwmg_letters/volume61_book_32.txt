32. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
May 1, 1935
MY DEAR AGATHA,
If it was possible, for your sake, to send somebody to London
I would do so. But I see that it cannot be done at present.
Rajaji will not look at the proposal nor would anyone else.
There never has been within living memory such unbending atti-
tude on the part of the Government one sees now. It is the
naked sword that is being dangled before India at the present
moment. I regard it as a trial from above. If we have real love,
i.e., ahimsa in us, all will be well. If we have not and we have
used it merely as a cloak, the naked sword is well deserved. Any-
way the mentality being as I have described it, no one of the
front rank think of going to England, so long as the policy persists.
I did read a paragraph about Sir Samuel's illness. But the
reports did not give it any serious importance. I therefore re-
frained from writing or cabling.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1490
