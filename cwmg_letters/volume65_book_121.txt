121. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
April 23, 1937
MY DEAR REBEL,
Your letters before me. Returned this morning. This will
go to Simla.
I have the prospectus, etc. That letter was over-weight
and carried a fine of two annas.
It is so hot that we are melting. I am in no mood to write
at length.
Yes, I leave for Allahabad on 25th and return on 29th.
The weather this time has been cruel in so many parts of
India.
What is this eczema again? Do you mean the same patches?
Love.
Yours,
ROBBER
From the original: C.W. 3776. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6932
