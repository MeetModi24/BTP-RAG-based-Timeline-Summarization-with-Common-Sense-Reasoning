449. LETTER TO INAYATULLAH KHAN
CONTAI,
January 1, 1946
BHAI SAHEB,
It is good you wrote in Urdu. I received the letter only
today in Contai. I have written to you that I am not a member
of the Congress. I cannot write anything on behalf of the
Congress. Only Maulana Saheb can do so. I have already
expressed my opinion that no one is going to accept your con-
stitution. I personally do not accept it.
Write to me at the Sodepur address.
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Urdu : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
