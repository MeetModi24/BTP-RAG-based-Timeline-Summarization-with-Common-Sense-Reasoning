55. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
NAINITAL,
Silence Day, June 17, 1929
SISTERS,
Your responsibilities are increasing fairly rapidly. I send
with this a letter from Kishorelal about "An Ideal Bal Mandir".
Read it and show it to the teachers. I should like those of you
who take interest in the work to get trained up for it. Do so
even if that means putting Narandas to a lot of trouble. It is pos-
sible to get a more intelligent guide than he, but we shall get
everything we want if we cling, as the phrase goes, to the trunk
of one tree.
Do your best to make the kitchen a success.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3715
