287. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
March 7, 1930
CHI. MRIDULA,
You can come tomorrow (Saturday) at 9.30 a.m. if it is
convenient. If this time does not suit you, come any time and
we shall meet. If you can make it at lunch time, it will be good.
We shall lunch together.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C. W.
11108. Courtesy: Sarabhai
Foundation
