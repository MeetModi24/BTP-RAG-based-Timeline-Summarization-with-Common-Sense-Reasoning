74. LETTER TO KUSUM
November 8, 1945
CHI. KUSUM,
Kusum is written in quotes : that means it is not your real
name. Why are you so distressed over your widowhood? A true
widow does not believe that because the body of her husband has
perished, the soul has also perished. A woman is married not
to a human frame but to the soul which resides in it. That is
precisely the reason why widow remarriage is taboo. That is why
your lamenting, thinking of committing suicide and so on are
contrary to dharma and justice. Your duty is to serve, giving the
first place to spinning. Learn the processes preceding and following
spinning. Also study the science of the charkha so that you can
understand properly the significance of spinning. You have not
stated your age and your education.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUSUMBEHN
C/O SHRI RAJENDRA KUMAR AGRAWAL
PANNALAL ROAD
ALLAHABAD
