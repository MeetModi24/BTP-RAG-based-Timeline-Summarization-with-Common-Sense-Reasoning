116. LETTER TO FULCHAND SHAH
Phagan Sud 6, 1981 [February 28, 1925]
BHAI FULCHAND,
I have your letter. I have forwarded it to Pattani Saheb.
I got your second letter too. I shall reply to it publicly with-
out mentioning any names. I fail to understand the propriety
of the rule that we ought not to dine at a drunkard's house.
Don't I go to people who do not wear khadi? The cause of
prohibition would gain nothing directly by my doing as you
suggest. Our not drinking despite going to his place does help it.
If we would have no such relations, we should leave society
altogether.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2870. Courtesy: Sharadabehn Shah
