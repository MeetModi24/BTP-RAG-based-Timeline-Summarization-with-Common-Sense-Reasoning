281. LETTER TO MADHAVDAS AND KRISHNA KAPADIA
January 31, 1933
BHAI MADHAVDAS,
I got your letter. May God protect you. I shall tell Devdas
to wire to Medh. He is here. But if Medh sends something, isn't
that enough hope?
CHI. KRISHNA,
You should keep on writing to me. There is no need to
worry about my elbow.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MADHAVDAS GOKALDAS
MANORDAS STREET
BOMBAY, FORT
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
