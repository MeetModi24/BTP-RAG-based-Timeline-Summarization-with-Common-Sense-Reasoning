157. LETTER TO PARACHURE SHASTRI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 7, 1945
BHAI PARACHURE SHASTRI,
I have your beautiful letter in Sanskrit. You have risen
from the death-bed. Recover fully. Achieve complete victory
over the mind. I am well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PARACHURE SHASTRI
MAHAROGI SEVA MANDAL
DATTAPUR
NALVADI P. O., WARDHA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
