448. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
BARDOLI,
May 29, 1931
CHI. KANTA,
I have your letter. I have written about you to Brother
and so I will not repeat it here. You should never worry about
1 Omission as in the source
my health. It is all right. Keep writing to me. How is your
health?
Blessings from
BAPU
