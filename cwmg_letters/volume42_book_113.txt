113. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
ALIGARH,
November 4, 1929
BHAISHRI FULCHAND,
Vallabhbhai met me in Delhi. I see some difference between
the account he gave and the one given by you. As Vallabhbhai
puts it, since the officials gave an apology the Thakore Saheb
suggested to the President to discontinue the inquiry and because
Manilal did not do so, an order was issued to stop the inquiry.
If this is true, I think your struggle was unjustified. Fight on if
the struggle is for a pure cause.
Blessings from
BAPU
