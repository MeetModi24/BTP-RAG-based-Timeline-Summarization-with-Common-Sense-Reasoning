624. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR SAKHALKAR
BOMBAY,
April 10, 1945
BHAI PRABHAKAR,
You cannot resort to satyagraha for everything. A thing that
rests on truth will succeed on its own. In any case therefore bless-
ings are unnecessary. Harijans should have the permission.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI PRABHAKAR SAKHALKAR
SAKHALKARWADI
RAJAPUR
DISTT. RATNAGIRI
