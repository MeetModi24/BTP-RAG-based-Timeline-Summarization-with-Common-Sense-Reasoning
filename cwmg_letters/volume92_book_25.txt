25. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
BOMBAY,
[Friday]1, April 5, 1929
CHI. KUSUM,
You will know more about Sharada from the other letters.
Give all help in the matter. Serve Sulochanabehn. I am sure
you will preserve your peace of mind. I will certainly take you
with me on the next tour. Radha's health is very delicate.
Therefore lighten her burden if you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1783
