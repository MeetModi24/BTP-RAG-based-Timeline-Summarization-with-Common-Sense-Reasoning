622. LETTER TO KUNVERJI K. PAREKH
Tuesday [January 10, 1928]
CHI. KUNVERJI,
I have your letter. Rami will reach here today. Let me
know what work is being done there and how it is progressing.
Tell me how you found Rami this time and also make what-
ever suggestions you wish to.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9705

2 Kantiprasad Chandrashankar Antani, a political worker of Cutch
