390. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
April 16, 1939
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have your letter. I learnt a great deal from my talk with
Laithwaite. It is a difficult matter. I do not remember ever
having gone through anything like what I am now experiencing.
I am in the midst of a forest fire. I am being tested. It is
good that Sardar is with me. I cannot say where I stand now
and where I shall end up. The article in the Statesman has had
a tremendous effect. You are going to join me in Calcutta.
Why don't Durga and Bablo go to Simla? Will they come to
Brindaban?
Blessings from
BAPU
