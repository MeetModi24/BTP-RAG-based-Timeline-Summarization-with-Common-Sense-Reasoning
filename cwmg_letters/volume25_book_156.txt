156. LETTER TO SATISH CHANDRA MUKERJEE
DELHI,
September 23, 1924
DEAR SATISH BABU,
This is just to tell you that I am taking the fast extraordi-
narily well. I know you are inwardly rejoicing that God has
given me the strength to go through the ordeal. If you at all feel
like coming, do come during the last week of the fast. You will
no more worry about Kristodas.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Kristodas just informs me you are having fever. In that
case you must not travel.
M. K. G.
From a photostat: G.N. 5596
