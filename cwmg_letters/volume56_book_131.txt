131. LETTER TO KOTWAL
October 21, 1933
BHAI KOTWAL,
As a general rule it can be stated that a person who has
faith in civil disobedience and has joined it cannot take up any
other work. But, on the other hand, a person who has faith in it
but has already taken up some responsibility should not join it
at the risk of failing in that responsibility. "Better one's duty,
bereft of merit. . . " 1
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3605
