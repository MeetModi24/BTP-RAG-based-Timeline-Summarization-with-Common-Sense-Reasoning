46. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
Magsar Vad 10 [December 10, 1925]
BHAI PUNJABHAI1,
I had your letter in Bombay but read it at Wardha. Do not
be miserable on account of the discharge. You should retire at
night after reciting either Ramanama or the navakara mantra . Slowly
and gradually you will recover. Maybe it will suit you better to
take only milk at night. Meditate on the bhajans that are recited.
God Himself is the Lord of all rasa . Derive all your rasa from
this meditation.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI PUNJABHAI NANA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 185. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
