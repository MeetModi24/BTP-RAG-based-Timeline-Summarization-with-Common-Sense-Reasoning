439. LETTER TO P. C. GHOSH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 20, 1929
DEAR PROFULLABABU,
I have your letter. I am sorry to know that you were ill.
I am not yet sure what part I shall have to play during this year
in connection with the Congress programme. I am supposed to
advise the Working Committee in connection with the foreign
cloth boycott and I am preparing a scheme . I am in corres-
pondence with Pandit Motilalji. If I have to do anything, natu-
rally I expect you to play your part in it. I know that you will
realize the expectations I have of you.
Yours sincerely,
DR. PROFULLA GHOSH
ABHOY ASHRAM, COMILLA
From a photostat: S.N. 15283
