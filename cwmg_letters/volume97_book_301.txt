301. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
PESHAWAR,
October 7, 1938
CHI. NIMU,
We have arrived at Peshawar. Now that Ramdas has returned, do you
think you need not write to me? He has complained about your health. Do
not do anything at the cost of your health. Do you not get the money regularly?
That responsibility is entrusted to Mahadev. He has been left behind in Delhi.
You may therefore write to him. Ba and Kanam also are in Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
