481. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
October 18, 1936
CHI. RAMESHWARDAS,
I like the scheme sent by Parnerkar for the Goseva section
at the Exhibition. I think it would be proper for you to shoul-
der its expense around Rs. 1,300 to Rs. 1,500 if it is not too
much for you. Not more than Rs. 5,000 is to be taken from
the Congress Committee for the entire exhibition.
Blessings from
BAPU
