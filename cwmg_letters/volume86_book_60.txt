60. LETTER TO SHANTILAL A. THAKKAR
NEW DELHI,
October 27, 1946
BHAI SHANTILAL,
I have your pathetic letter. If your repentance is genuine,
you have started life anew. Seek satsang if you can. If you
cannot have satsang of people, read good books and meditate.
You should give up pleasures and pastimes, but not exercise,
asanas, etc., which are necessary for building up the body.
I am not available now. Nor am I useful any more for
such work.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHANTILAL AMRITLAL THAKKAR
DOSSA JIVAN'S CHAWL
NEW HANUMAN LANE
BOMBAY
