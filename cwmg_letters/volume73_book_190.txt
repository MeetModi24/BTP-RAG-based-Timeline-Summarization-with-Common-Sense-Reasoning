190. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
SEVAGRAM
November 14, 1940
CHI. KANCHAN,
I had your letter. I hope you are making progress as expect-
ed. Has the itching stopped? Write to me in detail.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8277. Also C.W. 7110. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
