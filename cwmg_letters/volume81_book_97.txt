97. LETTER TO CHANDRAKALA AND KRISHNAKUMAR
July 31, 1945
CHI. CHANDRAKALA AND CHI. KRISHNAKUMAR,
It is only natural that you both, brother and sister, should
grieve over the passing of Mother. When I was as young as you
are, I too used to grieve when a relative died. But now I real-
ize that it is vain to grieve over a death. Death goes with birth.
Some die today; others will die tomorrow. What is there to
grieve about it? You must become wise.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Give my blessings to Father.
SHRI SITARAMJI KHEMKA
J. C. MILLS
GWALIOR
