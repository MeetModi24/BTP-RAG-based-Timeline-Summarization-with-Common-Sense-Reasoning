383. LETTER TO SYED MAHMOOD
July 28, 1942
BHAI MAHMOOD,
I read the book from the first page to the last. It reflects
your mind. It is good but in my opinion it is not worth publish-
ing. All your information does not seem correct. It should be
342
such that there is not any scope for two opinions. In the last three
chapters there are many things which are not desirable. Your
information should be such as both Hindus and Muslims can
accept. Let their conclusions be different.
Your duty is to bring the two together. I do not wish
that your book should start a controversy.
My last advice is: do what Maulana Saheb says.
I hope you have started learning Hindi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5116
