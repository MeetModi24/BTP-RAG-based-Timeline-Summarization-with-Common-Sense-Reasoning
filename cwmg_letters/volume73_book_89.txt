89. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
October 17, 1940
CHI. AMALA,
I got a letter from you after a long time. Why do you
keep indifferent health? The asthma must be cured. Do you
take any treatment?
Blessings from
BAPU
MARGARETE SPIEGEL
COLABA
BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
