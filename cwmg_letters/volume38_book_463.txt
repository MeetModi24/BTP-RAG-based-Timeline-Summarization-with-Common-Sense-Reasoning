463. LETTER TO S. K. SARMA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 24, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter and your book Towards Swaraj. If you are
a reader of Young India, you must have noticed that Young India
is not a reviewing paper in any sense of the term, and I have
so little time to read the books that friends kindly send me.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. K. SARMA
PLEADER, TEPPAKULAM, TRICHINOPOLY
From a microfilm: S.N. 14897
