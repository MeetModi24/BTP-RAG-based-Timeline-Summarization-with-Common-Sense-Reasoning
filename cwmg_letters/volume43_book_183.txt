183. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
April 6, 1930
CHI. MIRA,
No time to send you a long love letter. It is now nearing
11 p.m. How much yarn have you now left to be paid for the
two Sanghs, to what dates have payments been already made?
More when time permits. Manilal has gone to replace Ram-
das.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5388. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9622
