130. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 28, 1941
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter written from Champaran. You seem to have
stuck to the place. Ba has returned. She is better. Lakshmi-
behn has left for Madras, and Mahadev and Devdas for Simla.
Rajkumari has gone to Bombay for four days to attend a
women's meeting there. It must be as hot there as it is here.
How can it possibly be hotter than this?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3560
