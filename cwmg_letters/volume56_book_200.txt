200. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
November 4, 1933
CHI. MANI,
I got your letter. Dahyabhai is putting up a good fight.
Let him fight wherever he sees unworthy motives and insincerity.
I hope you are getting proper treatment. Do write to me regu-
larly. I was very glad that Babo came here. Ba will [court arrest]
after I go. Anyway we must keep ourselves ready for that.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 110
