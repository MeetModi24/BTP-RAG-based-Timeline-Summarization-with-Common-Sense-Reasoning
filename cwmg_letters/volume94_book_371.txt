371. LETTER TO K. G. GOKHALE
May 23, 1945
I had your letter. Gandhiji says that you should do what Kher says. G. would
not be able to collect the money. G. would not be able also to say whether Mr.
Polak should or should not be detained. The whole matter should be left to Kher.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
