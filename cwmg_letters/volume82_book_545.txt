545. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
As FROM KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR,
CAMP : GAUHATI,
January 12, 1946
DEAR MR. ABELL,
I am writing to you on behalf of Gandhiji. A couple of days ago he
received a letter from Raja Mahendra Pratap dated October 19th, 1945,
from Yokohama Prison. The letter came through the American Red Cross.
He has also received a letter from a friend of Raja Mahendra Pratap to
say that rumour has it that he is now in prison somewhere in India. Is this
true? If so, could you let Gandhiji have any particulars about him?
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 78
