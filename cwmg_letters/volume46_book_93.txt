93. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
BORSAD,
May 5, 1931
CHI. NANIBEHN1,
I got your letter. We should be satisfied if you somehow
recover your health. Where is Gangabehn now? How is she?
You have selected a good number of books. Amritlal Mani-
ar's books are worth reading.
Pannalal may write to me again and again if his questions
have not been satisfactorily answered.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3112
1 Wife of Pannalal Jhaveri, manager of the dairy in the Ashram
