278. LETTER TO DHIRU C. JOSHI
November 21, 1933
CHI. DHIRU,
I hope your anger with Rama has now left you. Take care
of your health. Continue to write to me. Whether you are
writing to Rama or to me or to somebody else, you must see
to it that your handwriting is good. Ask Balabhadra to write to
me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5316
