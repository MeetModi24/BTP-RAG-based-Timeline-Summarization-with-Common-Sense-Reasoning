349. LETTER TO SANTRAM AGRAWAL
September 1, 1945
BHAI SANTRAM,
You have mixed up the two things. In working out the
marriage rite in Prakrit I have only followed Tulsidas, Surdas
and others. Sanskrit has survived because Prakrit developed. What
I have done has only promoted dharma. The Hindu-Muslim
question does not arise here. It is a different question altogether.
I do not wish to go into it. You are running a divine life insti-
tution. Think a little.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SANTRAM AGRAWAL
DIVINE LIFE SOCIETY
JARAULI, AMRITSAR
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 775
