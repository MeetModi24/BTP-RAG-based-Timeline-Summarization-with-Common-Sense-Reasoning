421. LETTER TO JAGANNATH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 17, 1929
DEAR LALA JAGANNATH,
This is what Dr. Ansari writes in his letter to me signifying
his assent to the notice about the Memorial which I sent to him
and which referred to the allocation of the Fund for the Society.
I would like you to put Dr. Ansari at ease altogether on this score.
Please show this letter to Purushottamdas Tandon and it will
be better perhaps for him to write to Dr. Ansari. If there is
any member whom you think Dr. Ansari has in view, he should
also write.
The Sind programme, of which I wrote to you the other day,
stands and I leave here on the 31st instant.
Yours sincerely,
Enclosure: Extract from Dr. Ansari's letter
