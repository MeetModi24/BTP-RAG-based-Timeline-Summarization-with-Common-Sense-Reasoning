18. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
BOMBAY,
Friday [July 9, 1920]
BHAISHRI VALLABHBHAI,
I shall go there on Monday and return the same day. Please
see my letter to Indulal for the line of action the Rajakiya
Mandal should follow. I hope it will resolve for non-co-operation.
Total boycott of the councils is the only thing which will help us.
Inform Mavlankar and others.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
BHAISHRI VALLABHBHAI PATEL
BARRISTER
BHADRA
AHMEDABAD
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-Sardar Vallabhbhai Patelne
