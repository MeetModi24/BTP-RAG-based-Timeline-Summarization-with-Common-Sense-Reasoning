476. LETTER TO PURATAN J. BUCH
September 4, 1941
CHI. PURATAN,
Bhagwanji has sent extracts from your diary casting aspersions
on Dada. He has made very serious allegations, or has allowed
himself to be used as a tool in making them. Let me know what
you know and believe concerning this. You can show this letter
to Bhai Bhagwanji.
Both of you must be keeping well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9183
