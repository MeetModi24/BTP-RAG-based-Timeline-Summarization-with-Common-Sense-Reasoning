641. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
November 13, 1932
CHI. JAMNA,
I have done my duty in regard to the use of the primus
stove and don't think about the matter further. You should do,
courageously and without regard to my views, only what seems
right to you.
There is no cause at all for worrying about my elbow.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 862. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
