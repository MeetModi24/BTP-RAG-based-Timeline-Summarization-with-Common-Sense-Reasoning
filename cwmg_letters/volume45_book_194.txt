194. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
February 2, 1931
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
I got your letter. I see that it is still very necessary to be care-
ful. You should not exert yourself too much. Naturally, therefore,
your food also should be light. Let us see-I think we shall now be
able to meet somewhere.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7412. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
