290. LETTER TO JAMNADAS GANDHI
April 20, 1930
CHI. JAMNADAS,
I had been expecting your letter. You seem to be doing good
work. Is your throat all right? It would be very good indeed
if Khushalbhai joined the movement. According to me, it is pure
dharma to join it. Write to me from time to time and give all
the details.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. JAMNADAS GANDHI
OPP. MIDDLE SCHOOL
NAVA PARA, RAJKOT
KATHIAWAR
From the original in Gujarati: C.W. 9307. Courtesy: Jamnadas Gandhi
