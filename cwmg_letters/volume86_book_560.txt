560. LETTER TO CHANDRAGUPTA VIDYALANKAR
February 3, 1947
BHAI CHANDRAGUPTA,
I do not remember if I have already written to you re-
garding your scheme. I am sending you Kakasaheb's opinion
which I found lying in my papers here. At present I am un-
able to do anything more than this.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Return Kakasaheb's opinion after reading it.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 7210. Also C. W. 4866. Courtesy :
Chandragupta Vidyalankar
