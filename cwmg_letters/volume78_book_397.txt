397. LETTER TO ADWAIT KUMAR GOSWAMI
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
BHAI ADWAIT KUMAR,
In my opinion a Congressman may, as an individual, do
anything that is not opposed to the publicly stated policy of the
Congress. However, you should remember that I am not a
registered member of the Congress.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 804
