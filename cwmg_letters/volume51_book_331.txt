331. LETTER TO BADRIDUTT PANDE
October 5, 1932
Your grief is beyond words. But joy and sorrow are alike
gifts of God. So we must accept both with tranquillity and in
the same spirit. And why the fear of death? That is in store for
all. Those who are gone are not truly gone, those living are
not truly with us. Both exist but at different places. So much
for philosophy. May God grant both of you peace and fortitude.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 109
