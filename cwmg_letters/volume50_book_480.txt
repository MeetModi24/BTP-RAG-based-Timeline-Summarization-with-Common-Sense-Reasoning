480. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
August 30, 1932
CHI. BRAJKISAN,
Well, why didn't you mention it earlier? I got your letters
but it appears as if you have not received mine. One letter in
particular was quite long as it contained the replies to your queries.
How is your health? How did you find your companions? Where
is Krishnan Nair? Sardar and Mahadev remember you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2393
