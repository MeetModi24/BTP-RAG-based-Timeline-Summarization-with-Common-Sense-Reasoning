304. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
April 1, 1933
I was sorry that you had to run away so abruptly, but I do
hope that there is nothing serious with Father. I hope too that you
will behave yourself and not get worse than you were here.
You will render me an account of what you may do without
much exertion for the Harijan cause.
PADMAJA NAIDU
From a microfilm: S.N. 20805
