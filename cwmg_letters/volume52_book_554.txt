554. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
January 9, 1933 1
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I am told that the telegram I sent about you has reached
you. I was expecting a letter from you to follow it but nothing
has come. My letters of course do not reach you. From a letter
from Krishna received only today I learn that you are having
fits. I am very sorry. I shall enquire about your condition from
the Superintendent too. You must be getting letters from Jaya-
prakash. He is always cheerful. Chhaganlal Joshi has joined
me now and we are all doing well. My weight is 103 lb. and the
diet consists of 2 lb. milk, mosambis, oranges, dates and papaya.
And of course there is honey. I maintain good health on this.
The pain in the elbow is there but that is an old story. There
is no need at all to worry on that account.
I have to devote a great deal of time to Harijan work and
the government have sent Chhaganlal Joshi to assist me in this
work.
How is Kanta? Who else is with you? What is your diet
these days?
Blessings from
BAPU
