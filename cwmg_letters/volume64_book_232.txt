232. LETTER TO G. V. GURJALE
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 4, 1937
MY DEAR GURJALE,
I strongly advise you to take up some occupation and earn
your bread and doing so keep one or two Harijan boys. Give
up all ambitious schemes. Would you care to go to the Kodam-
bakam Harijan Institute?
Yours,
BAPU
