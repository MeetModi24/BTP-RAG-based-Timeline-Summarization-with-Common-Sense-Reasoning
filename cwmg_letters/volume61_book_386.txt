386. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
July 20, 1935
CHI. VASUMATI,
Join Gangabehn whole-heartedly in the work of sweeping and
cleaning. Even the pupils who join can be asked to do this work
after some time. This work is neither laborious nor time-con-
suming.
You seem now to have recovered your peace of mind.
Prabha has fallen ill after going to Bombay and has not,
therefore, returned as yet.
Lakshmi is still here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9404. Also C.W. 650. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
