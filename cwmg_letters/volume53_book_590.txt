590. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
March 2, 1933
MY DEAR MARGARETE,
You are sending me letters regularly. But you are telling
me nothing except about myself. You must now begin to tell
me something about your children, and the many things that you
teach them and how you teach them. You don't think that those
things will not interest me. They will, because they might be of
use for the Ashram children. You should tell me also as a
teacher what you would do to and for the Ashram children if
you had them under your charge.
Love from us both.
BAPU
