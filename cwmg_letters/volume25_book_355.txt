355. LETTER TO A. VARADAN
December 14, 1924
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for the apology accompanying your letter and
signed by Mr. Subramaniam. I have never thought of the matter.
I do hope he will not be made to retire by reason of letters
written by him.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
A. VARADAN, ESQ.
HEADMASTER
From a photostat: G.N. 5676
