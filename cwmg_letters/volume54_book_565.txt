565. LETTER TO K. C. SURYANARAYANA
April 22, 1933
MY DEAR SURYANARAYANA,
I have your letter, but I am wholly unable to help you.
You should write to the Secretary, A.I.S.A., and put all your
arguments before him.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. C. SURYANARAYANA
KHADDAR MERCHANT
PITHAPURAM
