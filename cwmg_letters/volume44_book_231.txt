231. LETTER TO SATYADEVI GIRI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 21, 1930
CHI. SATYADEVI,
I have your letter. You must get your Gujarati corrected by
someone. Are you keeping up the drawing? Don't you find any
improvement now and then? Is Dharmakumar naughty?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Virat Vatsalata, p. 34
