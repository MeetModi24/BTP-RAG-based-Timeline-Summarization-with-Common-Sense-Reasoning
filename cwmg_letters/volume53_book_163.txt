163. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
January 23, 1933
SUJNA BHAI,
You have returned at the right time. I hope you have come
back with improved health. May I hope that your contribution
to the Harijan cause will surpass that of anybody else? You may
make it in your way, but that way should be such that the progress
made by others will seem insignificant in comparison. If you
decide, I am sure you will be able to do much. Do try. Pay im-
mediate attention to the problem of bham. It is necessary to
introduce a great change in this practice if we wish to make the
Dheds and Chamars give up eating carrion. All of us are well.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5923
