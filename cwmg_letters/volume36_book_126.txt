126. LETTER TO A. T. GIDWANI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 16, 1928
MY DEAR GIDWANI,
I see you are already in harness. Brij Krishna, who was here
when I received your letter, has promised to send you name and
address of a good man after he reaches Delhi. He went today and
expects to be in Delhi in two days' time.
You must get Gangabehn now to write to me. I hope you will
all keep much better health than you did at Brindaban.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. A. T. GIDWANI
6 QUEENS ROAD
KARACHI
From a photostat: S.N. 13107
