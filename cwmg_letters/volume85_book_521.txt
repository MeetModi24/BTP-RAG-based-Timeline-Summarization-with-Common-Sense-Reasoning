521. LETTER TO M. W. H. DE SILVA
NEW DELHI,
October 2, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
I shall treasure your cheque for the cause of untouchability.
May your work in India result in bringing the two countries
together as never before.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
M. W. H. DE SILVA
CEYLON REPRESENTATIVE IN INDIA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
1 Dr. Sushila Nayyar
