187. LETTER TO SIR MAHOMED HABIBULLAH
WARDHA,
December 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. You will note that
I have not said one word as yet about the appointment and I
propose to retain my silence as long as I can.
Yours sincerely,
SIR MAHOMED HABIBULLAH KHAN BAHADUR, C.I.E.
MEMBER, VICEROY'S COUNCIL, NEW DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 15094
