632. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
October 13, 1938
BA,
I get news about you. Devdas is even complaining against
you that, though you have become very weak, you frequently get
up. Please don't do so. You should obey the doctor's instruc-
tions. Get well quickly so that all of us may be free from
worry.
I keep excellent health. Khan Saheb lets me have plenty
of rest. This also enables me to attend to my work properly.
Sushila, too, has got well. You know about Amtul Salaam.
She is drudging the whole day.
Keshu is betrothed to the daughter of Mathuradas's sister-
in-law.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2214
