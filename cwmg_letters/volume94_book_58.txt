58. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 3, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
It is surprising that there is no letter from you for three days.
Pyarelal will have arrived. He does not eat well. See to it there. There
is a book called Princes and Chiefs and Notable Persons in
Kathiawad. Barrister Chudgar writes that he had sent the book. If
Pyarelal remembers let him write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
C/O ASSTT. DEAN
K. E. M. H.
PAREL, BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
