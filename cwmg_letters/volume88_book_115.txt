115. LETTER TO M. A. JINNAH
NEW DELHI,
June 14, 1947
DEAR QAID-E-AZAM,
Yours of the 13th instant was received when I was at a
meeting.
I had hoped that H. E. had not clearly understood your
meaning. I now see that I was mistaken. I cannot ask the
Congress to commit hara-kiri.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 375
