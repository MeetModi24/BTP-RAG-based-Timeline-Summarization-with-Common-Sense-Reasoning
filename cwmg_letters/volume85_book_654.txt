654. LETTER TO DHIRENDRA
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
BHAI DHIRENDRA BABU,
I had your letter. Please forgive me for the delay in re-
plying. You can now take charge of the purse presented to me
and spend the amount in accordance with the scheme you have
sent to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DHIRENDRA
VIDYA ASHRAM
