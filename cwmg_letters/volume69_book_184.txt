184. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
RAJKOT,
April 17, 1939
CHI. NARAHARI,
I don't get a single minute free. I am writing this in the
'library'.
Ramji's letter is enclosed.
Qureshi's salary may be raised. It would not be right for
Sardar to pay his debt. That will not be to his good either.
Let him give up other activities and mind the store only and pay
off his debt. According to me he will be doing public service and
will be contributing to Hindu-Muslim unity even by doing that.
It wouldn't be desirable that he should become altogether depend-
ent on others. You may show all this to him. If, however, you
yourself hold a different view, please let me know.
You must take whatever freedom may be necessary for your
health. Why not consult the vaid who is treating Vasumati?
Sometimes the remedies of such vaids succeed. Consult Dinshaw
also. Don't be negligent about regaining your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
