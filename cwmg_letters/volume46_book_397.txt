397. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
BARDOLI,
June 4, 1931
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
Here is a copy of the letter from Sir Darcy Lindsay. Send
a reply with facts and figures. I have already sent one1, but a more
informed reply is called for.
I got the wire about the dispute in Bengal. I have wired
to Sen Gupta unconditionally to accept arbitration.
BAPU
[PS.]
I shall be in Bombay from 9th to 11 th.
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 7887. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
