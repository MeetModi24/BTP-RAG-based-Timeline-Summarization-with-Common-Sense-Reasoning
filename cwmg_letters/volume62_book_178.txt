178. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
November 30, 1935
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
You should immediately approach the four persons whose
names you have given me and show them your budget. For this
you need not await my arrival. Let me know the outcome.
Mother must have recovered by now.
Soya beans are available in Bombay at two rates. The
variety from Sind costs 12 annas a lb. and the one from Manchu-
ria 8 annas a lb. Both the varieties can be obtained through Gosi-
behn.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Gosibehn's address: Shrimati Gosibehn Captain, Nair Buil-
ding, Gandhi Seva Sena, Sandhurst Road, Bombay.
