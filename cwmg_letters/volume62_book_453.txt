453. LETTER TO NUR M. MALIK
May 12, 1936
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to receive your letter of 2nd ultimo. Much as I
would like you to return to India I must not encourage you. I
think you are better off as you are. It is a most uphill task to
run a new hospital in India.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
NUR M. MALIK, M.D., ESQ.
7407 TWELFTH AVE.
DETROIT, MICHIGAN
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
