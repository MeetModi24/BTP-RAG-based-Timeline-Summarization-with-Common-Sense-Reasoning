107. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
February 6, 1932
CHI. MANGALA,
I suppose the broken point of the thorn has come out now.
Try and get over the mistakes which you make when reciting the
Gita verses. Do you know the meaning of the verses? Have you
learnt any Sanskrit?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4074. Also C.W. 38. Courtesy:
Mangalabehn B. Desai
