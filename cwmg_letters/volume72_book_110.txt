110. LETTER TO G. A. NATESAN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 23, 1940
DEAR NATESAN,
You had a perfect right to send me your letter.
What more can India as a subject country do than it is made
to do? You do not suppose for one moment that they hesitate to
take all they want from this country. The Congress has nothing
but moral help to give. They have disabled India from doing more.
India as a subject country cannot save Britain. India as a free
country may. There is no want of will on my part. It is sheer
want of ability. Hope your wife is better.
Love.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 2238
