372. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
BARDOLI,
January 23, 1939
DEAR SISTER,
I have both your letters. The articles will be published in
Harijan. I had myself asked for them-hadn't I?
I was only
awaiting the second. I hope your daughter-in-law is well. Ba
is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAMESHWARI NEHRU
PAKPATTAM
PUNJAB
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7987. Also C.W. 3083. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
