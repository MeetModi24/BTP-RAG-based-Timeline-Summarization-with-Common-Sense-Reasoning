127. LETTER TO NIRMALA H. DESAI
September 19, 1932
CHI. NIRMALA (AUNT),
Yes, worship of a photograph also is image-worship. How-
ever, there is certainly a difference between meditating on a
photograph on special days and building a temple and instal-
ling an image in it. We should not introduce image-worship in
the Ashram. The Ashram should have place equally for all
religions.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9465
