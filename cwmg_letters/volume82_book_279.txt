279. LETTER TO SHITIKANTH JHA
SODEPUR,
December 10, 1945
BHAI SHITIKANTH,
I have your letter. If you want to sit by my side, come
over some day. But what is so special in sitting near me? Doing
the work suggested by me amounts to sitting near me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHITIKANTH JHA
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
GRAM SEVA KENDRA
THAKURDANDI
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 7477. Also C.W. 4923
