59. LETTER TO PADMA
BORSAD,
April 30, 1931
CHI. PADMA,
I got your letter. I had certainly not forgotten you. But I
was helpless. I am glad that you are trying to learn verses from
the Gita by heart. How many rounds do you spin in an hour
on the Magan spinning-wheel? For how many hours at a stretch
can you spin?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: G.N. 6118
