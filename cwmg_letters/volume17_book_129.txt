129. LETTER TO SARLADEVI CHOWDHRANI
April 29, 1920
As I write this I hear Dipak singing to Balkrishna's sweet sitar.
Balkrishna is a great gift of the gods to me. He [is] innocent as
a flower. He looks after me like a mother.
Did you read my message on the Khilafat to the A.P.2? Lest
you may not have got a copy of Y.I., I am sending you a copy.
Do please read the article on khaddar.
