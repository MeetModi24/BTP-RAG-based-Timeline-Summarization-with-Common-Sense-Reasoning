604. LETTER TO LADY PETHICK-LAWRENCE
NEW DELHI,
October 16, 1946
DEAR SISTER,
So you are two years ahead of me. You should try as we
all should to live the full span of life-125 years. I seem to have
lost the equilibrium I thought I would have to entitle me to
entertain the wish. May you have what I seem for the moment
to miss.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
LADY PETHICK-LAWRENCE
11 OLD SQUARE
LINCOLN'S INN
LONDON, W. C. 2
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
