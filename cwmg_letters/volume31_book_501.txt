501. LETTER TO MISSES ANDREWS
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 1, 1926
MY DEAR FRIENDS,
Though I cannot recall your features, I have a vivid recollec-
tion of having met you in Birmingham in 1914. Your brother
Charlie has been more and more endearing himself to the people
here. In fact, he has become as much Indian as he is English.
And he is now on his way to fulfil his self-imposed mission in
South Africa. I had a few happy days with him before he sailed
for South Africa. Do please give me a line in acknowledgment
when you have leisure and tell me how you are faring.
Yours sincerely,
THE MISSES ANDREWS
ARDLEIGH
BRAY'S LANE, COVENTRY
ENGLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 19710
