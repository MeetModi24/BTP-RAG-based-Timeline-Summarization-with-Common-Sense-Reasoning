543. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA C. MEHTA
September 18, 1941
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
I have your letter. Grow strong and healthy there. I knew
you would get friendly with all. You are of course not likely to
allow yourself to become a burden on anybody.
I know you
would do nothing but serve others there. I am sure the cli-
mate there will suit you. Write to Vedchhi from time to time.
In any case you will write to me regularly. Read something.
Learn Hindi. Learn Urdu also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9428
