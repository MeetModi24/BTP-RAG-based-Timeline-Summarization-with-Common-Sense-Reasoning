137. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
PATNA,
March 23, 1947
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
After sending a letter today I received your letter of the
20th and also the paper cuttings.
If you have stopped Dubeji's treatment, my advice is that
you should go to the Nature Cure clinic in Calcutta. You can
come over here to see me. You can come wherever I am or come
after my third tour.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 2503
