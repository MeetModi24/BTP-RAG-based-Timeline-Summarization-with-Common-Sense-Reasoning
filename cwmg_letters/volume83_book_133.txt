133. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
February 13, 1946
BHAI SAHEB,
I have your long letter. It is good, you have given [the
details]. I already knew some of the things, but some are news to
me. I am cautious and will remain so. To keep me on my
guard please do write to me as much as you can with ease.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI M. R. JAYAKAR
[From Hindi]
