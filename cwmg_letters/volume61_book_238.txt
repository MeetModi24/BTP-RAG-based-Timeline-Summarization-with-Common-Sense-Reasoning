238. LETTER TO G. SITARAMA SASTRY
June 16, 1935
DEAR SITARAMA SASTRI,
I shall try the gur syrup and report the result to you. Only
I shall know nothing about its capacity to keep for a long time.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
Sjt. SITARAMA SASTRY
VINAYA ASHRAM
P. O. CHANDOLE, DIST. GUNTUR
From a photostat: C.W. 9174. Courtesy: G. Sitarama Sastry
