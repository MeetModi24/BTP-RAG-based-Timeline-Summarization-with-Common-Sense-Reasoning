146. LETTER TO SIR WILLIAM WEDDERBURN
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 9, 1906
DEAR SIR,
Both Mr. Ally and I are obliged for the tickets for the complimentary
breakfast to be given to Mr. Naoroji on Tuesday, the 20th instant at
9.30 a.m.
Both Mr. Ally and I will consider it an honour to be present at the
banquet.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
SIR W. WEDDERBURN, BART.
84, PALACE CHAMBERS
WESTMINSTER
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4533
