216. LETTER TO RAGINI DEVI
POST ANDHERI,
March 25, 1924
DEAR SRIMATI RAGINI DEVI,
I thank you for your kind letter of the 11th February and the
interesting cutting containing your article on Indian music.
I thank you for your kind inquiry about my health, and I am
glad to be able to tell you and other friends who are interested in
it that I am making steady progress towards complete recovery.
Yours sincerely,
SRIMATI RAGINI DEVI
1240, UNION STREET
BROOKLYN
NEW YORK
From a photostat: S.N. 8586; also C.W. 5148
