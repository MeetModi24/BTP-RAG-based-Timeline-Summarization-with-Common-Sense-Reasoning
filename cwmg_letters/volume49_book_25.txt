25. LETTER TO MANIBEHN N. PARIKH
January 20, 1932
CHI. MANIBEHN,
I am glad that you teach the children in the kindergarten.
You should write to me from time to time and tell me your
experience, which children are well-behaved and which are un-
disciplined, what difficulties you have to face, etc. Narahari teaches
in Nasik and you teach in the Ashram. This is very good co-
operation between you two.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5963
