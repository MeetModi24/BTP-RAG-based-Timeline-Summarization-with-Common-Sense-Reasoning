288. LETTER TO O. V. ALAGESAN
June 26, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your letter. I wish my pen or my word had the in-
fluence that you attribute to them.
What you say about the puzzles competition appearing in the
daily Press is largely true. But it is an evil much subtler than the
gambling on which I have been writing. Though fashionable, the
latter is still regarded an evil. The puzzles competition is not. I
have not, therefore, the courage to deal with this evil.
Yours sincerely,
The Hindu, 6-7-1935
