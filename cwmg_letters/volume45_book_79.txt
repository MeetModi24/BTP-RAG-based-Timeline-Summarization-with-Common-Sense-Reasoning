79. LETTER TO S. D. SATAVALEKAR
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 5, 1931
BHAI SATAVALEKARJI,
I am grateful for your promptitude in sending me Gita-
shlokardhasoochi and other books. Are handmade slivers strong
enough to yield 19,000 yards of yarn in eight hours? What is
the count? Will this charkha be entered in the prize-winning
charkha competition?
If possible, send me the pocket-size
charkha.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 4761. Courtesy: S. D. Satavalekar
