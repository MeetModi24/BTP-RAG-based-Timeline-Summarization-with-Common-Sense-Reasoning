483. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
February 19, 1933
CHI. VALJI,
I had got your letter. I did not reply immediately because
I was waiting for the book. I have got the copies now. I am
distributing them among friends. I request everyone to whom
I send a copy to let me have his comments. How many copies
have been sold? I hope you get copies of the Harijan. Send me
your comments. If you feel inspired to write something, do so. I
hope your health is improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 7447. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
