76. LETTER TO MANEKLAL
October 26, 1941
CHI. MANEKLAL,
It was from your letter that I first learnt about Chi. Mridu's
engagement. Afterwards I got Maneklal Gandhi's letter. May you
all prosper during the new year and may you always be the instru-
ment of some service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXIII
