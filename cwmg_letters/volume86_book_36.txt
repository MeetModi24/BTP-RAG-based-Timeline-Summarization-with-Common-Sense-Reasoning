36. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
October 24, 1946
CHI. AMRIT,
I am so glad the boil has been opened and it promises to
heal within time.
Jawaharlal was here when I got your letter. I showed it
to him.
Just about to go to the prayer.
It was good to have attended to the dictionary, so like
you.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 4182. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7818
