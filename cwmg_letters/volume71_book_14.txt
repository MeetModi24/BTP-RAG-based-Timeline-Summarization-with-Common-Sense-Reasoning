14. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
December 6, 1939
MY DEAR AMRIT,
Your letter today is consoling. Poor Nayakam has lost his
son Anand Rup otherwise Tongawala. He had convulsions yester-
day. The parents have borne the grief bravely. No more time for
more.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3953. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7262
