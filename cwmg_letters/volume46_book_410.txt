410. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
June 5, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your letter. It is intolerable that, despite such illness, you
should compulsorily have to stay there. You should show courage
and find some way. I have written to you a long and detailed letter.
I hope you got it. I need not, therefore, write much in this. I shall
be in Bombay only during 9-11. The address there will be: Labur-
num Road, Bombay. I shall have to stay there for three days only.
Blessings from
BAPU
