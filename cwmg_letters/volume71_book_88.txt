88. LETTER TO MADALASA
December 29, 1939
CHI. MADALASA,
What tricks are you up to? Let what will be, be. Don't worry.
Make proper use of all the abhangas you have learnt and all the
knowledge that you have imbibed from Vinoba. Follow the doc-
tors' instructions.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 319
