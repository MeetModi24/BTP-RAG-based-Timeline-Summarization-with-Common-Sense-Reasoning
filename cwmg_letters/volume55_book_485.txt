485. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
September 9, 1933
CHI. MAGANLAL4,
I had hoped to get a letter from you and Manjula She
must have got my previous letter. I had written it to the Mogul
Street address.
1 & 2 Addressee's father and mother
3 The reference is to the dispute among the sons of Dr. Pranjivan Mehta
over their ancestral property; vide also the following item.
4 Youngest son of Dr. Pranjivan J. Mehta
5 Addressee's wife
414
I enclose Jekibehn's letter, which I received today. Think
over it and reply to her.
Write to me a detailed letter. I can work well enough now.
How is your business? Do you continue your study? Do you go
out for walks? Urmila must have grown quite a bit now. I would
be very happy indeed if an understanding is reached.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5819. Also C.W. 3042. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
