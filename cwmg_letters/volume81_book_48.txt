48. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
July 24, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I congratulate you on your accepting a cut of Rs. 100
voluntarily. If you can conveniently give up something more
please do so. Only good can come of it but nothing should be
done on my bidding. All sacrifice must be voluntary.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
