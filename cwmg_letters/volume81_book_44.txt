44. LETTER TO BENDRE
SEVAGRAM,
July 24, 1945
BHAI BENDRE,
I have your letter. How shall I console you? Your bond
was with your Bachi's soul. What does it matter whether the
body is buried or cremated? You of course know that the soul
does not perish. Why, then, should you grieve? But this is mere
philosophy. Experience of the world tells us that no one goes
mad over the death of his children. You should, therefore, feel
ashamed of yourself over your excessive grieving and shower
love on Nalini. Please calm yourself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10246
