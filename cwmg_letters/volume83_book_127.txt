127. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA BHAVE
SEVAGRAM,
February 12, 1946
CHI. BAL,
You do not seem to have fully recovered yet.
I met Vinoba yesterday. But I got a report from him only
about carding and making slivers.
Regarding sleep, when we meet. I hope to arrive there on
the 18th.
Paper which resembles mill-made paper in appearance cannot
be hundred per cent hand-made paper. Explain this to me further
when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 814. Courtesy : Balkrishna Bhave
