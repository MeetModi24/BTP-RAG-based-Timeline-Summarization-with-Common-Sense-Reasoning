129. LETTER TO JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 18, 1930
CHI. JAYAPRAKASH,
Your letter. I am glad to note that you are calm. These days
Jawaharlal is with me. I hope you are both keeping good health.
Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
