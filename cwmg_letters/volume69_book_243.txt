243. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
May 7, 1939
CHI. VALJI,
If Chitre spins yarn worth Rs. 10, he will certainly get that
much. If he engrosses himself in the work, his problems will be
solved. The Wardha rates are the highest. I am ready to assess
the value of his yarn at those rates.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VALJI GOVINDJI DESAI
GONDAL
KATHIAWAR
From Gujarati: C.W. 7484. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
