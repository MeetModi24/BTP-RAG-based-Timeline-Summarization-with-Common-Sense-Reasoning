700. LETTER TO MADALSA AGRAWAL
SIMLA WEST,
July 10, 1945
CHI. MADALSA,
How are you? Why don't you write to me? No matter how
I was occupied otherwise, I would certainly read a letter from
you. Has Om gone to Mussoorie?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 325
