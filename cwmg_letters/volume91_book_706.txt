706. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
[After December 15, 1928]
CHI. KUSUM,
I do not have the time today to write a long letter. Your
health is now all right. Maintain the improvement in your health.
There is no letter about Padma yet. But I shall have no objection
if she comes to stay there and behaves well. You would know
the real state of things.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1770
