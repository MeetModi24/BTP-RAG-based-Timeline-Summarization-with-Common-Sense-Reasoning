241. LETTER TO CHARU CHANDRA BHANDARY
October 18, 1934
DEAR CHARU BABU,
I have just heard about you from Birendra Nath, and then
from Sitaramji who happens to be here. It fills me with pleasure
and pride to know of your selfless services. I would like to know
still more about your own life and requirements as also of those
who are working in co-operation with you.
SHRI CHARU CHANDRA BHANDARY
DIAMOND HARBOUR
24 PARGANAS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
