276. LETTER TO ACHREKAR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 13, 1940
DEAR ACHREKAR,
I am sorry for your troubles. You may certainly postpone
offering civil disobedience till you are out of the wood. I hope,
however, that you are doing regular spinning and taking
in constructive work. I hope you know also that spinning includes
carding.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
