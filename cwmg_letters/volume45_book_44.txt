44. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI AND
NANIBEHN JHAVERI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 29, 1930
CHI. GANGABEHN AND NANIBEHN,
I got your full letter. I cannot guide you from here in
detail. Is it not our principle that a prisoner should not take
interest in problems outside nor guide anyone? I have merely
drawn your attention to the principle which we have accepted.
Think over it and then unhesitatingly follow what seems to you
your dharma. If you cannot decide where your dharma lies,
you may consult Pannalal or Narandas. Now Kakasaheb also is
there. Discuss the matter with him and then do what your con-
science tells you. Write to me from time to time. Both of you,
take care of your health. Has Pannalal completely recovered now?
Blessings from
BAPU
