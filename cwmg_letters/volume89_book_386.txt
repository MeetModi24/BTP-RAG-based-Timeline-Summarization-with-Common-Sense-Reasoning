386. LETTER TO LORD MOUNTBATTEN
NEW DELHI,
October 23, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
I have spoken to Pandit Nehru. But he is adamant. He is
firmly of opinion that no change should be made until the
weather has cleared. If it does, it may take two or three
months. In this estimate I agree with him.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. E. LORD MOUNTBATTEN OF BURMA
GOVERNMENT HOUSE
NEW DELHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, pp. 280-
