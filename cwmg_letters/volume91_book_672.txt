672. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
September 19, 1928
DEAR SISTER,
I have read the Khan Bahadur's letter. You should send
him the following reply:
'Gandhi says that although he takes the deepest interest in
the subject, he does not think that any useful purpose can be
served by his expressing an opinion on the questions asked.'
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11131. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
