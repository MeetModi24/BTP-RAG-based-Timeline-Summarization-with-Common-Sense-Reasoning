196. LETTER TO MAN/BERN PATEL
Silence Day [1926]
CHI. MANI,
I got your letter. I heard the news from father too. I do not
write more about [your] illness, for I hope to meet you on
Saturday at the latest. But you should get well and strong im-
mediately.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne, p. 39
