33. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
May 2, 1941
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter. I don't know what can be done about Jaya-
prakash. We have got to endure it. Continue to make inquiries
from time to time. If I think of anything I will write. In any
case go on doing your work. Do not worry about other things.
Rajendra Babu had a talk with me. He will write about it
himself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3565
