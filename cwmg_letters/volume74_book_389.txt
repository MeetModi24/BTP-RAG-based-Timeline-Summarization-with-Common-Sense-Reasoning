389. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
SEVAGRAM,
August 17, 1941
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I got your letter. First come here. We shall think about
Sikar, etc., afterwards. Today I am busy with the shraddha.
Mridu has come.
I will not, therefore, write more. My
blessings to Om and her husband
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3021
