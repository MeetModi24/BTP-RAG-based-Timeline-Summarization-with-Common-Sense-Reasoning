378. LETTER TO FRANCISCA STANDENATH
NEW DELHI,
October 22, 1947
CHI. SAVITRI,
I have your short note. I have sent you replies to all your
letters. I do hope they are all in your hands.
We are passing through most anxious times here. Heaven
knows what will happen in the end. For the present I am fixed
up here.
I am glad you are free from your illness.
You there have to pass through a time of scarcity food
and clothing. I wonder how the people face [it].
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
