84. LETTER TO N. G. ABHYANKAR
November 9, 1945
BHAI ABHYANKAR,
I have your Marathi letter written in a beautiful hand. I
had no difficulty in understanding it. About Ashtekar I am
doing what I consider it my dharma to do. What more shall I
54
say? I have expressed my grief. I try to understand as much
as I can. I have the lines that have appeared in Lokshakti.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
N. G. ABHYANKAR
130 BUDHWAR
OPPOSITE JOGESHWAR
POONA 2
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
