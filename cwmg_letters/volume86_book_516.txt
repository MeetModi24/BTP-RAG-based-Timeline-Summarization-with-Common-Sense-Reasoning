516. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 25, 1947
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
Read this telegram . Your letter shows you are making very
good progress. If the cough is cured and there is no fever you
will do a lot of work. You will write, if you need anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 572
