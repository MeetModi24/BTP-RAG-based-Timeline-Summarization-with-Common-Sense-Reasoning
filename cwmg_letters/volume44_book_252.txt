252. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI AND
NANIBEHN JHAVERI
September 27, 1930
CHI. GANGABEHN AND NANIBEHN,
I have letters from you both. I do remember you once every
week, but afterwards give up the thought of writing to you.
In a sense I look upon you as an ideal pair. You don't seem to
be mother-in-law and daughter-in-law, but are more than sisters
to each other. It is true that Pannalal has had a share in bring-
ing this about; but what could he have done if you two did not
have such a quality in your nature? We have to rise much
higher still. You three do have the capacity so to rise. Tell
Bhai Panachand that I got his wire.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3104
