500. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday [July 2, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. You are bound to gain weight in that
climate. The stomach trouble should not persist now. What is
your body temperature now? There is quite a crowd at the
Ashram at present. A lady who was to come from Germany
has already arrived.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
HOMI VILLA
PANCHGANI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
