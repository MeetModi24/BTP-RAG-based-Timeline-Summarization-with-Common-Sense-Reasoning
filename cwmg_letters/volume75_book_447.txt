447. LETTER TO VITHALDAS V. JERAJANI
March 1, 1942
BHAI JERAJANI,
I got your letter and the copies enclosed with it. I cannot
think of any modifications in your suggestion. The time may
come, of course, when it may be necessary to stop all our activ-
ities in cities.
The better course would be for the people to
buy up now all the cloth they might need.
I can see no harm
in advancing money to the bhandars. Let us see what Jajuji
has to say about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JERAJANI
AKHIL BHARATIYA CHARKHA BHANDAR
396 KALBADEVI ROAD
BOMBAY 2
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9804
