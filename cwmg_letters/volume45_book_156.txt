156. LETTER TO KALAVATI TRIVEDI
January 23, 1931
CHI. KALAVATI,
Be patient with people. The lethargic will become indus-
trious by observing your scrupulous diligence. We become forget-
ful by thinking about too many things. Therefore we must con-
centrate only on the task on hand and nothing else. The memory
is thus sharpened.
Blessings from
BAPU
[Ps.]
You are now entering the seventeenth year. Keep growing in
character as well as in age.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5278
