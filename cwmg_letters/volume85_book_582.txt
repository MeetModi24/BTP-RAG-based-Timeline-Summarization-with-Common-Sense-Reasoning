582. LETTER TO KALKA PRASAD
NEW DELHI,
October 12, 1946
BHAI KALKA PRASAD,
Shridharani has just arrived form America. He needs khadi.
Give him as much as he wants. Charge the actual price.
Assume that the yarn is with me. Charge the price of the
yarn also.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi : C. W. 10418
