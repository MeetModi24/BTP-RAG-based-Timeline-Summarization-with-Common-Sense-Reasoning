604. LETTER TO ATINDRA MOHAN ROY
SATGHARIA,
February 2, 1947
DEAR ATINBABU,
I have your note. I think you are unnecessarily agitated. I refuse
to tax my mind even as to the proposed section until it becomes part
of the constitution. And in any case there is no harm in your
corresponding with the President of the Congress.
I am destroying your note.
ATINDRA MOHAN Roy
SECRETARY
TIPPERAH DISTRICT CENTRAL RELIEF COMMITTEE
COMILLA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 This was written on a telegram postmarked January 31, 1947.
