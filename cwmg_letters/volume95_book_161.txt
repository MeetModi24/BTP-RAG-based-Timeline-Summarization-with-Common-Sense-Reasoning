161. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
[1941]
STUPID DAUGHTER,
How foolish you are! Where can you go to escape from me?
Wherever you go, you will find me. It is very difficult to abandon me.
1
It appears from the contents that this and the following item belong to
the year 1941.
2
J.C. Kumarappa
TELEGRAM TO M.A. JINNAH
125
On Sunday you have to sleep near me.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
