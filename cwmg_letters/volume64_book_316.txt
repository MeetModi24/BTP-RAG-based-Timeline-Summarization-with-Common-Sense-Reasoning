316. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[January 24]1, 1937
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
We reached here today. Kanti went to Poona taking Saras-
wati with him. He will come to Bombay with her. Paparamma
had been to Bangalore but could not come here.
Sharma has met you. If you wish to come and see Saras-
wati you may.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 597
