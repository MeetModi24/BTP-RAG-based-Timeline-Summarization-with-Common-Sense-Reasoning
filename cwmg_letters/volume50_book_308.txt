308. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI
July 31, 1932
There is a mantra in Ishopanishad, which can also be interpret-
ed to mean, "concentrate on the work before you". If one does
so, one will certainly realize God. God is Omnipresent. He is
present in "my" work too. What I think of as "my work" is in
truth His. If I concentrate on it, I shall regard it as His. Anybody
who does the Lord's work attains Him.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 335
