30. LETTER TO VANAMALA PARIKH
December 27, 1930
CHI. VANAMALA,
You have written your letter in a beautiful handwriting. It
is not enough that your speed on the takli is the same as mine.
Can an old man and a child have equal capacity? Have you
heard with what speed they spin in Wardha? Even a girl of
your age spins 80-85 rounds in half an hour.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5757
