466. LETTER TO DR. M. A. ANSARI
BORSAD,
June 13, 1931
DEAR DR. ANSARI,
I forgot and I had not the time to talk to you about the local
quarrels in Delhi. A letter from Mrs. Asaf Ali reminds me that the
quarrels are not yet over and that they have extended to the ladies
also. Can you not put these quarrels down?
I hope you found your brother better. I wonder what Dr.
Rehman was able to do.
DR. M. A. ANSARI
1 DARYAGANJ, DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 17275
