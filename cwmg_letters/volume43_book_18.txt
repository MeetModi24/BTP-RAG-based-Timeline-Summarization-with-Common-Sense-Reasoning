18. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 6, 1930
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
I have your letter. I do not think it advisable to ask you to
leave that place just now. I will start on the march on the
12th, accompanied by most of the men in the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U. III
