388. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
February 12, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I have your letters. You did right in going straight to
Delhi. Now you must remain there until you recover fully. You
should come to see me on your recovery before you go to the
Ashram.
That Dr. Sharma is preparing to go to the Ashram is com-
forting news to me.
You can write to me whenever you wish. It will cause me
no discomfort to write to you, but your not writing will certainly
grieve me.
Inform Kudsia that she may keep writing to me. How is
she doing at Patiala?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G. N. 276
