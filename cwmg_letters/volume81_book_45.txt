45. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
July 24, 1945
CHI. RAMDAS,
You may consider this first for Usha. If you are still ill,
then why don't you go to Dr. Dinshaw and get cured? Why
should you be lethargic in this matter? Kanam had also
become weak.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
