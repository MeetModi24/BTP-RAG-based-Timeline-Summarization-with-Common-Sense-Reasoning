303. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
July 7, 1933
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
Your letter. I hope you are all right by now. God is
testing you from all sides. That your mind is at peace is a great
thing. What solace can I offer you? Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1702
1 The name is not legible in the source.
