267. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
[June 15, 1942]
CHI. PRABHA,
I am sending your report with this letter, but by separate
book post. I read it. It is good. My note is enclosed. You
will like it.
Heavy rains have started here. No more today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3571
1 This was Atulanand's letter to Amrit Kaur, on which Gandhiji was
writing the note.
2 From Bapuna Patro-10: Shri Prabhavatibehnne
