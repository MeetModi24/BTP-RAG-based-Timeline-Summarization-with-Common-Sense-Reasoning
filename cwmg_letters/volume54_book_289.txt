289. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA
March 31, 1933
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRA,
I have your postcard. What you say is quite correct. I am
writing to N. Devi. Do please tell me something more about
Rudramani. Who is he, what is his education, what is his occu-
pation? Did he refund you the Rs. 10? Was he to pay for the
fare, or was he not? Has he sufficient means?
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20778
54-17
