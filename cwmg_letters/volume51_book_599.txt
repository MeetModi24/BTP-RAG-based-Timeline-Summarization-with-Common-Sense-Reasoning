599. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
November 8, 1932
DEAR MAJOR BHANDARI,
You have been receiving on my behalf the Vedic Magazine
-a Hindi monthly, Purushartha-a Marathi monthly, both reli-
gious publications, Indian Review of Madras-Hon'ble Mr. Natesan's
monthly and possibly other magazines.
Now that I am permitted to do propaganda work regard-
ing untouchability, I shall want these and other publications in
order to enable me to keep myself in touch with public opinion
as expressed in the Press and to combat, where necessary, criticism
that may appear therein.
You seem to doubt whether these papers and magazines
that are already coming and which may come in answer to my
appeal may under the recent decision of the Government of India
be given to me. Will you please obtain the Government inter-
pretation in the matter as early as possible?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
