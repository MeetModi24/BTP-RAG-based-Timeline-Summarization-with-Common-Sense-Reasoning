240. LETTER TO CHAMPA R. MEHTA
NEW DELHI,
May 23, 1946
CHI. CHAMPA,
I went through the letters you sent. Your reply seems to be
correct. I am returning the letters. Who is Naginbhai? What is
this you write about Shashikant ?
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
