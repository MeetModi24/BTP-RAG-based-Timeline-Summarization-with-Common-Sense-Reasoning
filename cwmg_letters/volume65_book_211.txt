211. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
May 17, 1937
CHI. VIJAYA,
I had a very long letter from Manubhai. He has given the
whole history. For you there is nothing new in it. You must
have got my letters.
Please let me know your [daily] programme.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7068. Also C.W. 4560. Court-
esy: Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
