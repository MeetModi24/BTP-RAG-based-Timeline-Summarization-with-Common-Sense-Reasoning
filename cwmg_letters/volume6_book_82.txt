82. LETTER TO MESSRS A. BONNER
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 2, 1906
DEAR SIRS,
I enclose herewith cheque for £6.17. together with your bill in
settlement of your account. I shall thank you to receipt the bill and
return it.
Yours faithfully,
[2] enclosure [s]
Cheque, £6.17.
Account
MESSRS A. BONNER
1 & 2, TOOK'S COURT
LONDON, E.C.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4464
