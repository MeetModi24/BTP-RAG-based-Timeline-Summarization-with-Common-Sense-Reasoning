471. LETTER TO MECMANAGE
July 26, 1947
DEAR MECMANAGE,
Your letter. My advice is: you should settle down in Ceylon
and quietly serve the Ceylonese. The way of ahimsa is narrow.
If I know you, you will not be happy in the Ashram.
Marry by all means. But marry a simple Ceylonese girl and
see how you and she feel.
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
