17. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
August 6, 1937
MY DEAR C. R.,
What nonsense! Why should you feel sorry or disappointed
because I hold certain views about salaries? I do not at all
resent your not enforcing them.
I have said, my views need
not be accepted if found unworkable. We all marvel at the way
you are managing things there. You have approached your task
with faith and religious zeal. You must not feel the slightest
disappointment. You know my deepest feeling. Then why
should you worry? I hope you will be able to spare yourself
for 17th. My prayers and best wishes are with you always.
The talk with the Viceroy was formal though quite friendly.
Devdas was looking well.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2066
