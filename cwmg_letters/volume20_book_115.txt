115. LETTER TO KUNVARJI ANANDJI
BOMBAY,
June 21, 1921
BHAISHRI KUNVARJI ANANDJI,
Bhavnagar has so far done nothing for the Tilak Swaraj Fund,
which means that you have done nothing. I am pained by your
slackness. You understand everything and have money . You
know the importance of this work, its bearing on national rege-
neration, and still refuse to contribute your full share to the Fund.
I want you to give your intelligence, your heart, your time and
your money, all the four things. Such an opportunity will not
come again. I should like you to help.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
