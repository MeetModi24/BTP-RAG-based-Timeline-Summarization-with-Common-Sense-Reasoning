208. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
August 9, 1909
DEAR LORD AMPTHILL,
I have now received the somewhat delayed proof of the
Rev. Mr. Doke's book1, which I am very anxious to see published as
early as possible. I might mention in passing that I have received a
number of subscriptions from subscribers in advance.
I know you are very busy and I have hesitated to burden you further
with the perusal of this proof and with the writing of the introduction,
which you were good enough to promise, if the proof should meet with
your approval. Nevertheless you will, I hope, find time—as I am sure
you have the desire—to give this matter your very kind attention.
I am forwarding the proof under separate cover.
I remain, etc.,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4989
