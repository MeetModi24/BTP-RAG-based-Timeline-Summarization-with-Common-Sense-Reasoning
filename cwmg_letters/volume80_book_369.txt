369. LETTER TO RAMPRASAD VYAS
May 30, 1945
CHI. RAMPRASAD,
I hope you are well. Do not move from there until I send
Balvantsinha or someone else. It is all right even if you have to
stay there for two months. It would be a different thing if you
wished to do no more than what I had asked.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
