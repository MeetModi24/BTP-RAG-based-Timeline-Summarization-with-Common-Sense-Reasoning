115. LETTER TO J. S. M. JOSEPH AND HELEN KONARD
March 17, 1933
DEAR FRIENDS,
I thank you for your letter and the book accompanying it.
You will be glad to hear that I had already read it and that
was with much interest and profit. This was years ago.
Yours sincerely,
DR. J. S. M. JOSEPH & HELEN KONARD
158 HARRISON STREET
PASSAIC, N. J. (U.S.A.)
From a photostat: S.N. 20601
