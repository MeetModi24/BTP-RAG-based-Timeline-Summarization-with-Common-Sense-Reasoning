229. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 12, 1940
CHI. VIJAYA,
At last you have gone back to Ambala. You could not peep in
here! I did acknowledge the handkerchiefs. Only those are used
these days. What news may I give of Ba? I can write about her
if she is not well. When I give no news, you should understand
that all are well. Lilavati has passed and is mad with joy. She
will now go to Bombay for further study.
Blessings to both of you from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7129. Also C.W. 4621. Cour-
tesy: Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
