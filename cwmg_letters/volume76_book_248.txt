248. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEVAGRAM,
June 10, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Why do you feel tired? You are right in saying that you
can have no cause for enmity or malice towards Chi. or any-
one else. You should therefore cheerfully do what work you
have agreed to do. It should not be hampered. You have my
co-operation and my blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4431
