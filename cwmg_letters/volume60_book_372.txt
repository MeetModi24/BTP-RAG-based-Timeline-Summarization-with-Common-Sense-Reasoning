372. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
March 7, 1935
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
Having taken silence for a short while, I reply to your letter
myself.
You may write to him that such is my opinion. You will be
going to England in a few months. It would be proper then to
talk to Barlow and others. But it cannot possibly result in any
rights being granted. At the same time it is also necessary to
make clear that anything workable can be negotiated only with
the political leaders. In the prevailing atmosphere no agreement
unrelated to politics is possible.
Writing this will not lead to any misunderstanding.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 8006. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
