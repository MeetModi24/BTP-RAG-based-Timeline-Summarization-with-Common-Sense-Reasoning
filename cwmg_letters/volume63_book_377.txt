377. LETTER TO BHULABHAI J. DESAI
September 21, 1936
BHAISHRI BHULABHAI,
Shoulder the whole burden of the parliamentary election,
financial as well as organizational. You will yourself no doubt
contribute the largest amount but also persuade others to
do likewise. This task is primarily yours; others will join
afterwards.
I hope Dhiru and Madhuri are fine. Blessings to them both.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Bhulabhai Desai Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
