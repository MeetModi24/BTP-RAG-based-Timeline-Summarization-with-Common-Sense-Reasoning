46. LETTER TO INDU N. PAREKH
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 28, 1945
CHI. INDU,
I was glad to have your letter. Chi. Kanti had given me
the news. Now stick on and be successful. What machinery
are you using? Do you only cure the hides? Do you use only
the hides of dead animals or whatever you can get? Do you
make anything from the leather?
Blessings from
BAPU
INDU PAREKH
DEENABANDHU ENGINEERING
ITOLA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
