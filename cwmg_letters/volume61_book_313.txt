313. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
WARDHA,
July 1, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
May the marriage ceremony go off well and may Krishna-
swami and his wife have a long life of happiness and service. I
hope that Kichi's finger will be quite healed by Saturday.
There is no hurry about the Wells Fund.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9602. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
