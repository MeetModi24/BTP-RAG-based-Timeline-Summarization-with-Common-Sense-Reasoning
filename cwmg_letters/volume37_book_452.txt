452. LETTER TO HARRIETTE ASHBROOK
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 26, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I have also the book
by Mrs. Williams. As soon as I can get the time, I shall read the
book and let you have my opinion. But I am so overwhelmed
with work that I do not know when I shall get the time to read
the book.
Yours sincerely,
HARRIETTE ASHBROOK
COWARD MCCANN, 425 FOURTH AVENUE, NEW YORK
From a photostat: S.N. 14390
1 In reply to his letter dated September 7, which read: "Mother India
has created a terrible sensation in America. It has been difficult to know
just how to meet the situation. . . . There is now coming off from the
press a book by Gertrude Marvin Williams called Understanding India,
which I believe will help in many ways to correct the wrong impressions which
Miss Mayo has given. . . ."
