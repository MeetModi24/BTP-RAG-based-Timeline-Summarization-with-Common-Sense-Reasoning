516. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
May 24, 1936
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I think you did well in going there. You should look upon
Mirabehn's company as a veritable satsang . Serve her as much
as you can and put your heart in doing what she bids you.
Don't you be a burden to her in any way. Learn from Balwant-
sinha the entire processing of cotton.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8604. Also C.W. 6992. Courtesy:
