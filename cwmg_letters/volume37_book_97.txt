97. LETTER TO G. V. SUBBA RAO
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 21, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am sorry that I shall not be able to
write anything useful about the late Gopalakrishnayya. I could
not write anything about him without having to criticize his
conduct in several matters. I have therefore considered it wise
not to say anything about the deceased.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 3625
