64. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
June 18, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
You should not be afraid of anything you may see in a
dream. Nor should you feel unhappy about it. If kitten sit in
my lap, so do children. A cat has no reason, whereas human
beings have it. Hence there is nothing in a cat's life for which one
may wish to be born a cat.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9435. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
