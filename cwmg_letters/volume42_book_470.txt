470. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA DESAI
February 26, 1930
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA,
I got your letter. May you live long and render much service.
I would have been happy if you could have come. Janakibehn
will come in a day or two.
I got Chandan's letter too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7463
