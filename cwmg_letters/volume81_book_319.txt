319. LETTER TO PRIYAMVADA NANDKEOLYAR
August 30, 1945
CHI. PRIYAMVADA 1 ,
I have your letter. I have written to Prabha that she
should be on your Committee but she should not work till she
gets well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PRIYAMVADABEHN
NAND VILAS
GAYA (BIHAR)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
