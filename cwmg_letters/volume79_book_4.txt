4. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
January 1, 1945
You should not worry. I have fallen ill through my own sins.
I indulged a little too much in Ayurveda and am paying the
price. Now I am bed-ridden because according to the law of
nature I am throwing off the poison. I am all right. Get well
soon.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 205
