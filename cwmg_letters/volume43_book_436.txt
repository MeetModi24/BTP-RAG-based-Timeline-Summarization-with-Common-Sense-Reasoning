436. LETTER TO GOMATI MASHRUWALA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day [May 26, 1930]
DEAR GOMATI,
I hear that Kishorelal is keeping good health. Write to me
in detail. How is your health? Where is Tari? If she is there
ask her to write. Where is Nathu? How is he? Does he go
to the Ashram? Which sisters are there?
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 575
