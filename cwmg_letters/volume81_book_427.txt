427. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
September 10, 1945
BAPA,
I have two letters before me. Do get Rajkumari's pamphlet
published in other languages. The Santiniketan people are doing
It in Bengali. Most probably Sucheta will not go there. I am
thinking about it. We shall get someone. More when we meet.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
