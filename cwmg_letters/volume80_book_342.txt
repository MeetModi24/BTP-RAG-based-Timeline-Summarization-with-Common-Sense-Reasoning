342. LETTER TO POTTI SRIRAMULU
May 29, 1945
DEAR SRIRAMULU 2 ,
It is more honest to sell your yarn for the required quan-
tity of khadi and take your food from your beggings and de-
vote the rest to the Harijan cause. Let the people know your
worth.
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
Temple entry is not the only way to remove untouchabi-
lity; it is one of the many ways all of which have to be taken.
From a photostat : G.N. 106
