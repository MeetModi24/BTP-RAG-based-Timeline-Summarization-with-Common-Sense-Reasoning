236. LETTER TO S. SATYAMURTI
As AT THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
January 3, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
It would be wrong of me to conceal from you the grief
which I have expressed to others over your highly ungentlemanly,
unpatriotic and uncalled-for conduct on the 1st instant at the
A.I.C.C. meeting. I do not mind opposition even though it may
be merely destructive, but I do mind want of manners, which I
am afraid you were betrayed into on the very day when you
would be expected to be restrained and helpful, even though you
did not believe in the programme or even the procedure.
Yours sincerely,
S. SATYAMURTI
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S. N. 32579/39
