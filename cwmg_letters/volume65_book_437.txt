437. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
July 10, 1937
CHI. MIRA,
I have not been able to write to you lately. Your letters
and sketches have been coming in regularly. I have sent them
on to Nandalal Babu for his opinion. You shall know it when it
comes.
I am glad Dr. Sen is coming there. You will discuss your
health with him and invite him to come to Segaon if he ever
wishes to do so.
Rameshwari Nehru is here and is likely to stay for the month.
I do not need to say anything about the Working Committee
meeting.
I am glad the doctor has allowed you to go to the simple
diet. The nuts are not for you.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6391. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9857
