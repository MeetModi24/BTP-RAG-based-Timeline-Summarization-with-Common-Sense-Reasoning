146. LETTER TO G. CARMICHAEL
NADIAD,
April 14, 1918
DEAR MR. CARMICHAEL 1 ,
I met yesterday after the interview with you a small com-
mittee consisting of Sir Narayan Chandavarkar and others. I
gave them the purport of the patient and prolonged interview
you and Sir James Duboulay were good enough to grant me.
That committee asked me to give them the main purport in
writing for publication.
But I feel that I should ask for your
1 Head of the Revenue Department
2 Sir Narayan Ganesh Chandavarkar, judge of Bombay High Court and
social reformer; President of the Congress session at Lahore in 1900
confirmation of the purport and permission for its publication. I
enclose herewith the purport. Will you kindly endorse it if it
represents your views correctly and let me know whether I may
allow it to be published? May I ask you to favour me with
a telegraphic reply?
From a copy: C.W. 10675. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
