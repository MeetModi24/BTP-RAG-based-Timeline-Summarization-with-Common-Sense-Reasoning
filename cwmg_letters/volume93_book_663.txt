663. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
February 9, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
You will have received my letters of yesterday and the day
before. There is nothing from you even today. I want to
write about Kanchan today. She again has the itch. She has
some pain in the throat. Should she be given milk injection?
Sankaran has suggested it. Kanchan has consulted me. Now
I shall do as you say.
Shastriji's health appears to be deteriorating. There is the
danger of his having ulcers all over. His skin which was begin-
ning to have a healthy appearance is again looking unhealthy.
Bharatanand is no doubt working very hard. Now let us see
what happens.
I have sent the weights with Raghunandan. You will have
received them before you get this letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 Raghunandan Saran, a Congress leader from Delhi
