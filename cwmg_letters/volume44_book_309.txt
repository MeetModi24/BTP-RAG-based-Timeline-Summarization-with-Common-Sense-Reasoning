309. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 13, 1930
CHI. RAMDAS,
As long as you are free, I must get a letter from you every
week. How is your health? Do you digest food properly? Do
you still take medicine? What work have you taken up? How
is Nimu? And Sumitra? What happened about the khadi im-
plements centre? The real test will be now. But there is nothing
which we did not expect.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6860
