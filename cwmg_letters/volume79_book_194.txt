194. LETTER TO T. B. SAPRU
SEVAGRAM,
February 8, 1945
DEAR DR. SAPRU,
Though I am supposed to be resting and silent between 8.15
a.m. to 8.15 p.m. I am working intensely. Hence the delay in
sending you a reply.
If it is no great trouble I should like you to put me specific
questions about my talks with Q[uid-e] A[zam]. I would like to
confine myself to that question alone.
I see you have entered upon an ambitious programme.
I wish you all success and full strength for the work.
I hope the patient is quite out of the wood.
Yours sincerely,
M. K.
Gandhi-Sapru Papers. Courtesy: National Library, Calcutta. Also G.N. 7571
