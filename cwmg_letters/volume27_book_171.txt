171. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Ashad Sud 1 [June 22, 1925]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got both your letters at almost the same time. At present
I will have to stay in Calcutta itself for one month. Then I
shall start touring. I wish you to be settled both in mind and in
your place of residence. As to your being fit for work, it is for
me—is it not?—to consider.
But we shall think of this later.
Though I am at present settled in one place, I am very much
engrossed in work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: C.W. 464. Courtesy: Vasumati
Pandit
