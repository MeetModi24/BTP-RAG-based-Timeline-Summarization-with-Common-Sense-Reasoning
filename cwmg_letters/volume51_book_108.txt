108. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
September 18, 1932
MY DEAR C. R.,
My heart goes out to you in your distress. I have no doubt
about the truth of the Inner Voice. Nor have I any doubt that
you will soon see the light out of the darkness.
Love and yet more love.
BAPU
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 44
