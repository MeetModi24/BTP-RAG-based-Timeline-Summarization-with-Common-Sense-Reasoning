414. LETTER TO MANIBEHN N. PARIKH
August 21, 1932
CHI. MANIBEHN,
I have your letter. I hope you do not worry about Narahari
now. Why should we worry when we have left it to God to worry
for us? And what do we gain by worrying?
I will write to Vanamala some other time.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5971. Also C.W. 3288. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
