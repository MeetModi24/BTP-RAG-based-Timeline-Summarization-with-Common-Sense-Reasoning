512. LETTER TO PUSHPA K. DESAI
POONA,
September 29, 1945
CHI. PUSHPA,
I have had no letter from you recently. I am sending with
this a letter from your father. Just now he is not likely to trust
you. But that should not hurt you. When you have proved
your freedom from worldly desires his present unhappiness will
turn into happiness and you will be a credit to everyone.
I like your going for a couple of days to Vinobaji. Please
remember that that alone is a true vision of Lord Krishna which is
attained through pure service. Take care of your health. Take
every step calmly and after careful thought. Use your intelli-
gence in whatever public work you undertake. Write to your
father from time to time. If you write to anybody else, do so
either through him or through me.
Blessings from
BAPU
