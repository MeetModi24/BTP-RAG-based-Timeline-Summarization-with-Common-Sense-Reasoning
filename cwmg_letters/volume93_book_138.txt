138. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 29, 1936
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
You have given me good news. Samples of the exercise
books are good. It will be all to the good if people do not
forget khadi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 10838. Courtesy : Purushottam
K. Jerajani
