237. LETTER TO GANANATH SEN
February 7, 1935
DEAR KAVIRAJ,
Can you answer the enclosed questionnaire in the light of
Ayurveda and interpret it in terms of modern medicine?
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
KAVIRAJ GANANATH SEN
AYURVEDIC HOSPITAL
CALCUTTA
