285. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
LAHORE,
January 26, 1920
MY DEAR CHILD,
I have your brief note. I hope you have been receiving my
letters regularly. I have hardly missed a day. I cannot send you
a love-letter today as I have not many minutes for the post.
How is Deepak shaping? Please tell Deepak he has not written
to Sarladevi for the last 4 days. He must be regular in writing.
Please see that he writes at least a p[ost]c[ard] every day.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat of the handwritten original in N.A.I.; also, My Dear
Child, p. 49
