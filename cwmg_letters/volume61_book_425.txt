425. LETTER TO AMBA GANDHI
July 29, 1935
CHI. AMBA,
Your letter is good even though it smacks of pessimism. But
we must not lose heart. Whatever the circumstances, we have to
render service and that cheerfully and devotedly. It is all right if
people care to listen to us, but even if they do not [we have to
go on serving].
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
