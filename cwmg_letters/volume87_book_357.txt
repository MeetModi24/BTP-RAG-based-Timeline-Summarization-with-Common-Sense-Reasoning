357. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
PATNA,
April 29, 1947
CHI. SUSHILA,
I got your letter of the 26th, together with Manilal's. I
have enclosed a reply to Manilal, which please forward to him.
You will learn about yourself also in that letter.
I think I shall know about Arun's result in the next letter.
Why don't you ask them both to write to me? Do not let
them be lazy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 5003
