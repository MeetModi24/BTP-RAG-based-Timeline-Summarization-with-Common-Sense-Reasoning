77. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
May 12, 1941
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter. I have not yet received Jayaprakash's.
Have you seen the resolution passed by the Socialists? Who were
they? Why do you worry? Whatever is to happen will happen.
We can only do our best. I am all right. The fast has no
after-effect. Ba is better. She is still in Delhi. I have sent
Lilavati there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3559
