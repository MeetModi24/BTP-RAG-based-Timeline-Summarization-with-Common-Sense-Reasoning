459. LETTER TO PERINBEHN CAPTAIN
NEW DELHI,
July 25, 1947
CHI. PERINBEHN,
Your letter. You will see what I spoke yesterday on Hin-
dustani. You and I must work hard, even unto death for the
purpose. Let us not lose heart.
Make an effort to speak and write Hindustani in both the
scripts.
The agony is postponed for two months. Much can be done
by earnest people during the breathing time.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
