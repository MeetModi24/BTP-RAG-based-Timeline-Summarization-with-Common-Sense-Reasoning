479. LETTER TO AMINA AND GULAM RASUL QURESHI
November 27, 1931
MY DEAR QURESHI AND AMINA,
You have been well warned by Providence. Do not grieve
over the children's death. All who are born must die. I hope
Amina is keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10806. Courtesy: Gulam Rasul
Qureshi
