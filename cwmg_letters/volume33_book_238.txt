238. LETTER TO DAMODAR LAKSHMIDAS
AMBOLI, via BELGAUM,
Chaitra Sud 9 [April 10, 1927]
BHAI DAMODAR LAKSHMIDAS,
Bhai Kishorelal conveyed to me the news of Chi. Mamabai's
demise. We cannot help grieving owing to the bonds of flesh.
But when looking at it from Mamabai's standpoint, I realize that
she has been released from pain.
I am not well enough acquainted with you but I would, if
I may, advise you to cherish and remain faithful to her memory,
abstaining from another alliance.
You have the great responsibility of the children. Make
arrangements for them in consultation with pujya Gangabehn and
do what you can to advance their interests. Ask any help you
want of me and, needless to say, I shall do whatever I can.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2817. Courtesy: Purushottam D. Saraiya
