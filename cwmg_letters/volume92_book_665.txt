665. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
Y.M.,
April 3, 1933
CHI. MRIDULA,
I have received your letter. Have you received Sardar's
letter? It was written before mine. The purpose of writing this
is to say that Manibehn does not seem to be getting Sardar's
letters, though Sardar has been writing regularly and I too have
been scribbling a few words at the bottom. I may not have
one it in a few cases. It is a mystery why Mani has not been
receiving the letters. We are making enquiries. Meanwhile let
Mani know this.
Blessings from
BAPU
