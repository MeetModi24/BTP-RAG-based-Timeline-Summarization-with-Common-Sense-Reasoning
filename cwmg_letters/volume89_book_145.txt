145. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
August 30, 1947
CHI. MANI,
All the letters are enclosed. Send them to the addressees.
I hope I am not burdening you with too much work. This is
the only way I can get the letters delivered in time. Show to
Sardar the letter addressed to Jawaharlal and then have it
delivered as soon as you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 142
