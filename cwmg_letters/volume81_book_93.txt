93. LETTER TO DEVRAJ VORA
July 30, 1945
BHAI DEVRAJJI,
I have your letter. Why is it in English? I can understand
Hindustani. The experience I had of you was bitter. I did not
find in you devotion to truth, but I did find vanity in a large
measure. You will therefore gain nothing here nor will the
people here gain anything from you. I therefore feel that your
coming here is not proper.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI DEVRAJ VORA
C/O SETH N. L. SEHGAL
INDIAN TIMBER WORKS
P. O. GULZAR BAG
PATNA, BIHAR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
