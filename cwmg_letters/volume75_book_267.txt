267. LETTER TO KHWAJA SAHEB
[1941]
KHWAJA SAHEB,
I am much obliged to you for your kindness in sending me a
Hindi version of the Koran.
I hold Urdu in the greatest respect. I am also a student of
Urdu and I desire its progress. In my opinion the Hindus who
want to serve both should know Urdu and read Urdu books and
newspapers. Similarly the Muslims who wish to serve both should
know Hindi and keep in touch with Hindi books and newspapers.
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
