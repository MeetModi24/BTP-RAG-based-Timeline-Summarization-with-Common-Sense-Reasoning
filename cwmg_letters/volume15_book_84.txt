84. LETTER TO DR. PRANJIVAN MEHTA
[BOMBAY,]
January 9, 1919
My health, like the moon, has its phases; it waxes and it
wanes; only it jumps the new moon day. The pain caused by piles
has disappeared completely, but I have no appetite and feel weak
in the body and to that extent the illness persists.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. IV
