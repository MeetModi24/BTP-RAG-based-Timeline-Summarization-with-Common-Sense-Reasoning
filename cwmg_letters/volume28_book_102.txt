102. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
Bhadra Vad 13 [September 15, 1925]
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have your two letters. I hope you received the wire I sent
yesterday. I wish you not to come if it makes Durga unhappy.
I shall manage somehow in regard to the Gujarati writing. Dalal
has told me that for writing I should use the right hand, a little
at any rate. So I shall use it for Navajivan at least. The English
I shall dictate to Kristodas and Pyarelal. Mr. Sen has sent word
that he is not coming.
I hope you have understood what I said about Ramdas in the
telegram. Satis Babu is with me. His brother, Hemprabhadevi
and Prafulla Ghosh also were with me in Purulia. Urmila Devi
and Mona have not come. My programme is as follows:
17 Ranchi
18 Hazaribagh
19 Gaya, and Patna at night
19-24 Patna
I do not know further.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11433
