490. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
March 22, 1945
CHI. SITA,
I am very happy to have your letter. May you go on mak-
ing progress. Manilal and Sushila will come when God wills.
You should not worry. Look after your health and engross your-
self in work. Sumi writes to me every week. I will send you—
all of you—her next letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
SITA GANDHI
AKOLA
