140. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS TANDON
September 17, 1944
BHAI PURUSHOTTAMDASJI,
Today I have some leisure, so I am going through the file of
old letters. Your letter is there. You must have tried a truss
for your hernia. How could a man like you get hernia? Or does
anyone develop hernia without provocation?
I did not mean what you interpret from my telegram. I
had written only about the matter of health.
However, the
opinion you have sent will prove helpful to me. Our talks are
going on. I cannot say what the outcome will be.
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
