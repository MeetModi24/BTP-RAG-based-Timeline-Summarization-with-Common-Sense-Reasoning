503. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 14, 1941
MY DEAR AMRIT,
These partings must come. They must be cheerfully borne.
Your gadi is occupied by K3. Don't worry. Keep your times
there as far as possible.
The library room is undergoing innovation.
Amtul Salaam is the same as when you left her. Sita has
fever.
My [blood-pressure] reading was 156/98. Heat has commenced
today. I have a wet wrapper on the head.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3998. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7307
