291. LETTER TO W. B. HEYCOCK
MOTIHARI,
April 22, 1917
DEAR MR. HEYCOCK,
I thank you for your kind note of yesterday. I am writing to
Mr. Cox today.
I leave for Bettiah this afternoon.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original in Gandhiji's hand in the National Archives of India;
also Select Documents on Mahatma Gandhi's Movement in Champaran, No. 45, p. 83
