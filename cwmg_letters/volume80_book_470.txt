470. LETTER TO DEVRAJ SETHI
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
BHAI DEVRAJ,
I would certainly like to have Vidyavati for the Nayee Talim
work. However, I am afraid that that work demands hard
labour and perhaps Vidyavati will not be up to it. I would
advise her to write to Ashadevi.
How can I meet the Acharya in Panchgani, now that I shall
be getting down from here by the end of the month?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DEVRAJ SETHI, M.A.,LL.B.
JHANG CITY (PUNJAB)
