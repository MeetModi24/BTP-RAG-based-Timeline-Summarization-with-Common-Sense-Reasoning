102. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
[Before February 21, 1927]
Haste is waste. Let me do a few places thoroughly rather
than many places indifferently. You will have to remember that
I have to do continuous travelling throughout the year. My capa-
city and energy are limited. I must therefore have three forty-five
minutes for three meals, one forty-five minutes for bath, etc., and
two forty-five minutes for a quiet walk in the morning and eve-
ning. I must be made to retire at 9 p.m., and I must have
three hours for attending to correspondence and editing, and I
must have half an hour for sleep during day. If you can give me
these hours, I fancy that I shall not only bear the strain of travel-
ling but go on almost indefinitely and shall improve in health.
The Bombay Chronicle, 22-2-1927
