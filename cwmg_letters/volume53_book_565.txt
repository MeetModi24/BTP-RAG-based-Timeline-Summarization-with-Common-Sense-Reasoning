565. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
February 26, 1933
I got your letter only today. I would certainly advise you to
go to Banaras. It is our duty to plead with Malaviyaji. Whether
we shall succeed or fail does not depend on us. The interpreta-
tion which you put upon his wire is correct. From the wire it
seems that he has an open mind on the subject.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 121
