445. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C.P.,
August 18, 1940
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
I thank you for your letter of the 15th instant.
I gave all the particulars I had from Nagla Nawabad. But I
admit that enquiry into the particular cases will be difficult without
the details you ask for. I am at once writing to my correspondent.
It may be difficult for him to produce the required details. Direct
proof of these cases may be impossible save in a case like Hingan-
ghat. But I shall try to get what details I can. You may expect a
further letter from me containing information about other cases.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a printed copy: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
