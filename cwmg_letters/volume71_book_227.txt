227. LETTER TO G. RAMACHANDRAN
February 5, 1940
We leave here tomorrow morning for Wardha. I have just got
your letter. I am biding my time. But do not pin your faith on
negotiations. If you are exhausted you should say so and sus-
pend the struggle without any weakening or yielding but recuperate
yourselves in the midst of stormy weather. This is a possible
feat in satyagraha. If on the other hand you can go on endlessly
without money from outside, you have nothing to fear. I am at
your back always as a purely moral force.
Is this enough for the time being?
Love.
BAPU
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
