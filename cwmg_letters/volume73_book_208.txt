208. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 20, 1940
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter. Yes, all the Puranas are worth reading.
They are real history of all time. I am glad you are having more
and more peace.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABEHN
PALAMPUR
KANGRA DISTRICT
PUNJAB
From the original: C.W. 6465. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10060
1 Presumably, the reference is to the All-India Women's Conference which
was to be held at Bangalore from December 27 under the presidency of the
addressee.
