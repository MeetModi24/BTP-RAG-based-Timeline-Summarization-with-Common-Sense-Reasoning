449. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
ABBOTTABAD,
July 12, 1939
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
I am much obliged to you for your letter of 1st instant
and the wires about Talcher.
I thank you, too, for your inquiry about my health. It
continues to be good.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a microfilm: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
