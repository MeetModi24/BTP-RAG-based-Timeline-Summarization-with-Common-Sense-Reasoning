217. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO A.J. BALFOUR
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 20, 1906
TO
THE PRIVATE SECRETARY OF
THE RT. HON. A.J. BALFOUR
4, CARLTON GARDENS
PALL MALL
DEAR SIR,
I am obliged to Mr. Balfour for your letter of the 19th instant.
I venture to state that the Delegates have already approached
Mr. Lyttelton who has kindly granted an appointment.
As the leader of the Conservative Party and ex-Prime Minister,
we would deem it an honour if the Right Hon'ble gentleman will
allow us to pay our respects to him.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4605
