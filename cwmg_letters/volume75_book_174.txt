174. LETTER TO MADALASA
November 21, 1941
CHI. MADU,
Will you always remain as crazy as you are? Come here
at the earliest opportunity, if not to stay here, at least to meet me.
And then you may pour out all that is stored up in your heart
and cry yourself out to your heart's content. Since I am giving you
such a fine opportunity of crying here, stop crying there. For the
rest, if you follow the rules I have suggested you will forever be
happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 323
