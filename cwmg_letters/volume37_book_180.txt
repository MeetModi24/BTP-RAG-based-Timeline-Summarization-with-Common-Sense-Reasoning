180. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
Silence Day [August 6, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I get your nice letters regularly. Your handwriting and
expression are both good. Do not fall ill by struggling to reach
the kitchen at five. It will do if you go at half past five. I shall
now return in a few days. How is Vidyavati's health? How is
the Bal Mandir Hindi class getting on? Maintain a regular daily
diary.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3331
1 Addressee's sister
