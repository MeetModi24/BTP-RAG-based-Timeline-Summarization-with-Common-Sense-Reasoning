363. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
LUCKNOW,
April 10, 1936
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
I have your letter. You always have my blessings wherever
you go. May you live long. I regard you as a bhakta. No
harm will ever come to you. If your constipation persists, take
with curds or milk half a tola of crushed garlic, green or dry,
with your meals. It would be good to do so one hour before
you begin your meal. Continue it if you find relief after three
days.
Blessings from
BAPU
PURUSHOTTAM NARANDAS GANDHI
OPP. MIDDLE SCHOOL, NAVUN PARUN
RAJKOT, KATHIAWAR
