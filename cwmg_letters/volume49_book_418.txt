418. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
April 22, 1932
BHAISHRI KHAMBHATTA,
I got your letter. I was glad that both of you could meet
Andrews. I will trouble you and request you to come here if I
can secure permission for you to see me, and then also only if
the condition has not improved by that time. Both of us will go
through the books on massage when we get them. I find that
Lakshadi oil is excellent.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6600. Also C.W. 4386. Courtesy:
Tehmina Khambhatta
