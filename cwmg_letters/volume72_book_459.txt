459. LETTER TO G. V. MAVALANKAR
August 24, 1940
BHAI MAVALANKAR 2 ,
Bhai Prabhashankar has come here. He told me his tale of
woe. I was glad to hear that this work had been entrusted to
you. I know that you will do whatever you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1248
