204. LETTER TO KUNVARJI PAREKH
Silence Day [July 25, 1927]
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I write this postcard only to tell you that I think of you
every day. I get no time to [write]. Ramdas gives me news
about you. I hope your mind is at peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati S.N. 9703
