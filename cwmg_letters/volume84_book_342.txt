342. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
MUSSOORIE,
June 3, 1946
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. I am repeating what I have said. Make
yourself fit in body and mind. Know that Ramanama is the
supreme remedy for this.
Blessings from
BAPU
[C/O] SJT. SURESHWAR ACHARYA
SHRI NRISINHA ASHRAM
BHOOTADINO JHANPO
VADODARA
