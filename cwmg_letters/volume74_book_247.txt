247. LETTER TO DHIRUBHAI B. DESAI
July 12, 1941
CHI. DHIRUBHAI,
Appasaheb writes and tells me that he is restraining himself
because you do not let him move out in Bombay.
I feel,
however, that you should let him move freely. There are very
few persons like him and it is not proper that we should hold
him back. Never mind if he is killed. He is gaining invaluable
experience, which he will not get in any other way. If this rea-
soning appeals to you, permit him. You must have received my
letter of yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Bhulabhai Desai Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
