27. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
April 9, 1929
CHI. KUSUM,
I have your letter. The view which you have advanced can
also be justified. But what is happening is all right. Gossiping
should not only be discouraged, but not indulging in gossip should
become second nature with us. We are making a novel experiment
in the Ashram. Till we have assimilated its spirit, it may have
seemingly contrary results. But we need not be afraid of that.
Only by continuing in this manner shall we stop hiding sins.
One of the beauties of Mahabharata is that Vyasa has made no
attempt to hide sins. Think over this.
Blessings from
BAPU
