139. LETTER TO NAUTAMLAL M. KHANDERIA
THE ASHRAM,
July 11, 1926
Your letter. If you find the Gujarati [original] interesting
enough, take it from me that Mahadev's English rendering is
highly spoken of by many who know English well. Hence I see
no need to make any change.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 10941
