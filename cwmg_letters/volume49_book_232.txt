232. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
March 13, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA,
If a girl's letter does not contain anything which requires a
reply, she should not expect a letter by return of post. Your last
letter was of that kind. But since you have asked me to write to
you every week, I have written this letter. However, in future
bear in mind what I have said.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Is there any news about Narahari?
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5768. Also C.W. 2991. Courtesy:
Vanamala Desai
