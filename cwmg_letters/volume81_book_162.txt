162. LETTER TO VENKATAKRISHNAIYYA
SEVAGRAM,
August 9, 1945
BHAI VENKATAKRISHNAIYYA,
I have gone through your letter. The argument given in it
does not appeal to me. Your thoughts are confused. Maybe,
I am too old to appreciate your point. I personally feel that my
mind is able to grasp new things. However, you may go ahead
according to your ideas.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI U. VENKATAKRISHNAIYYA
KHADDAR SANSTHANAM
BEZWADA
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 9244. Also Pyarelal Papers.
