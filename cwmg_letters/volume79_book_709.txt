709. LETTER TO GOPE GURBUXANI
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 22, 1945
CHI. GURBUXANI,
I have your letter. Keep in touch with Rajkumariji and do
as she says. Vimala must have fully recovered by now. Let her
write to me. Let her attend to the household work but in such
a way that she continues to do public service. I consider it im-
portant that both of you should be well acquainted with the
science of khadi and learn all the processes. We arrived here
yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1314
