345. LETTER TO JAYAKUNVAR V. DESAI
May 29, 1945
CHI. JAYA 2 ,
I have your letter.
You may, by all means, marry away Chi. Chandu. Both
seem to be worthy of each other. I cannot, however, welcome
this marriage. I feel for that abandoned lady. How will she
take it? Manilal is in Bombay.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./23
