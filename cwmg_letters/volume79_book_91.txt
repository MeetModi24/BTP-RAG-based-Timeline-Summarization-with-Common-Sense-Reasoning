91. LETTER TO MAHENDRA B. DAVE
SEVAGRAM,
January 21, 1945
CHI. MAHENDRA1,
I have your letter. Send me Bhogibhai's full name and ad-
dress so that I can write to him. What do you do? How many
brothers and sisters are you? You should not rush to a court of
law. I will do what I can. How far have you studied ?2
Blessings from
BAPU
MAHENDRA BHOGIBHAI
KEVALRAM MAVJI'S BUILDING
RAJKOT
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
