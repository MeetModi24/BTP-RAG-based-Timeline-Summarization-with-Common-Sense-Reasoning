46. LETTER TO ARUNA ASAF ALI
ON THE TRAIN TO PATNA,
March 5, 1947
CHI. ARUNA 1 ,
I have your letter.
I am glad. What was there to mind in your not accompany-
ing Asaf Ali2? Go on doing what you regard as true service.
Here I am learning the lesson of absolute non-violence. I do
not know how far I shall succeed. Let us see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Brijkrishna Chandiwala Papers. Courtesy :
