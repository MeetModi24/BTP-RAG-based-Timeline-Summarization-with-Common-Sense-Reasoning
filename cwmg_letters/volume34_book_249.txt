249. LETTER TO SHANKER
HASSAN,
August 3, 1927
MY DEAR SHANKER,
I have your postcard. I see you have already made yourself
quite at home there. You will cover yourself well if you find it
very cold there. The climate however is very bracing and is
bound to agree with you.
Do write to me regularly.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SHANKER
C/O MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
PANCHGANI CASTLE
PANCHGANI
From a microfilm: S.N. 14207
