211. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[Before November 21, 1940]
DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. I do not approve of your roza. But
I shall not interfere from this distance. You will not undertake
a fast without my permission. You must be guided by Maulana
Saheb as long as he is there. While you are still busy meeting
people, there is no occasion for undertaking a fast. The fact
that you suffer from asthma indicates that God does not approve
of your rozas.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 445
