328. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
ON THE TRAIN,
December 2, 1924
BHAI SAHIB,
I have your letter. I alone have the privilege to fall ill.
Other workers may not. I hope your leg is completely cured. I
understand your complaint. I shall not give you the trouble of
going to the Council. My Vandemataram to all.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9550
