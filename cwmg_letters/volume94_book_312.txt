312. LETTER TO R. S. DESHPANDE
SEVAGRAM,
November 16, 1944
DEAR PROF. DESHPANDE,
Apart from the fact that I have no time, the books have been
transferred to the late Jamnalalji's daughter for whom Prof. Kalelkar
had meant them.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI R. S. DESHPANDE
POONA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
