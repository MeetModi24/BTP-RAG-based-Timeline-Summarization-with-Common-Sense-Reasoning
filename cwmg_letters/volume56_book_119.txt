119. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
October 18, 1933
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Herewith two garlands made out of yarn specially spun by
me today for the bride and the bridegroom charged with my
blessings. Will you please put them round their necks on my
behalf! I hope they will reach you in time.
I cannot help being sorry that the ceremony has been vetoed
by Mrs. Hutheesing. But I suppose in these matters I am a back
number.
I understand what you say about Dipak. I would write
to Sarala Devi in as gentle a manner as I can.
1 & 2 The source is illegible here.
Love to you all.
BAPU
[PS.]
I would like you to wire to me when all is over that
Mother was able to stand the strain.
A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 112
