453. LETTER TO B. J. B. GALVIN
YERAVDA CENTRAL PRISON,
November 18, 1930
DEAR MAJOR GALVIN,
(1) Here is the usual post I send on Tuesdays. Will you
please order its despatch today?
(2) There should be a spinning-wheel for me there. Could
I have it please?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 4504. Courtesy: Prof. B. J. B. Galvin
