134. LETTER TO SHIV BALAK BISEN
March 23, 1947
CHI. BISEN,
Your letter was frank. That your views about the Ashram
inmates are not correct does not matter. As for your desire to
stay with me, we shall think about it when I return there. I
cannot decide just now. Normally I would have welcomed your
services, but at present I have to consider what [my] dharma is.
We are going to meet before long, aren't we?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 7726
