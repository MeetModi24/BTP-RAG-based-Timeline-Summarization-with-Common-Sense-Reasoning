432. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
March 16, 1945
CHI. ANAND,
Beware of the boy who is with you. Get some translations
done so long as you do not receive something from Bhai Jivanji.
How are you?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANAND HINGORANI
UPPER SIND COLONY
KARACHI, SIND
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
