112. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
[On or after August 21, 1929]
DEAR MOTILALJI,
I have your second wire. I do not take the view you do
about Jawahar. Jawaharlal would have been elected had I not
been in the way. If the Congressmen concerned can be induced
to think that I shall be of greater service without the chair they
would surely have Jawahar. You may depend upon my not being
unmindful of Jawahar's self-respect. I would not on any account
thrust him on the country. But let us see how things shape.
I shall take no hasty step.
I expect more news about Kamala. I hope she will now be
entirely free from recurring pains and that this operation was
all that was necessary to put her on her feet.
Yours,
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/133
