150. LETTER TO ALFRED C. MEYER
POST ANDHERI,
March 15, 1924
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 2-2-1924.
There is at present a firm of booksellers in Madras known
as S. Ganesan who sell a collection of most of my articles in a
weekly called Young India and edited by me. It will give you
all the information that you are likely to desire.
Yours sincerely,
ALFRED C. MEYER, ESQ.
181, VINE AVENUE
HIGHLAND PARK
ILLINOIS, U.S.A.
From a photostat: S.N. 8496
