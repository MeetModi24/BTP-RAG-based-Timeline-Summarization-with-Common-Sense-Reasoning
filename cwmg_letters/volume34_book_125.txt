125. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[July 10, 1927]
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter. You will follow the doctor's directions ac-
curately. There need be no hurry about Wardha. You can do
your Hindi just as well there. No Wardha until you feel quite
free and secure. Dr. Harilal Desai is quite a sound man and
very careful.
Do not strain yourself to write to me in Hindi. By all means
do, whenever you feel like it. You will use the mosquito-net with-
out any hesitation. Do not be in a hurry to move about quickly.
I had another meeting today, this time for Hindi. I stood
it quite well.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5245. Courtesy: Mirabehn
