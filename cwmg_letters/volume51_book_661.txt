661. LETTER TO P. N. RAJBHOJ
November 14, 1932
MY DEAR RAJBHOJ,
I have your letter. Here is the corrected copy of my answers.
I have not made any corrections in your questions. Some questions
being repetitious may be dropped.
I hope you got my previous letter which contains the message
you want.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 796
