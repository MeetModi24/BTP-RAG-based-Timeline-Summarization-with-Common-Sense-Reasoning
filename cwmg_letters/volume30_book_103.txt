103. LETTER TO P. S. R. CHOWDHURY
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 11, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter with enclosures. You have related in a
telling manner a painful story. I am afraid not much will come
out of the agitation contemplated by you. We have to develop
real strength before relief can be had. However, I propose to
deal with the matter in the pages of Young India.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. S. R. CHOWDHURY
HON. SECRETARY
GLASGOW INDIAN UNION
C/O GLASGOW UNIVERSITY
GLASGOW
From a photostat: S.N. 12440
