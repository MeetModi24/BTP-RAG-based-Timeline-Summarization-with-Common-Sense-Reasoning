268. LETTER TO BARI KHAN
SEVAGRAM,
August 21, 1944
MY DEAR BARI1,
You have done well in writing to me. I have advice from all quarters.
But I have chosen the one Infallible Adviser. I feel secure in His bosom.
You should come after I have seen the Q. A.
Love.
BAPU
BARI KHAN SAHIB
21 ALKAPURI
BARODA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
