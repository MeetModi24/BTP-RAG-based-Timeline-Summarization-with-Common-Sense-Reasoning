666. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI P. DESAI
BOMBAY,
April 17, 1945
CHI. MAGANBHAI,
Narahari has sent his opinion regarding your scheme. Have
a look at it. Discuss it with him and write to me about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MAGANBHAI DESAI
RASHTRIYA GUJARAT VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
