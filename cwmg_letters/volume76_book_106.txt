106. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHASTRI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 2, 1942
BHAI HIRALAL SHASTRI,
I have your letter. It will not be hypocritical if the parents,
knowing that the couple will not be wearing khadi after marriage,
still dress them for the wedding only in khadi.
Herewith Dugarji's letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
