114. LETTER TO K. MADHAVAN NAIR
March 17, 1933
MY DEAR MADHAVAN,
I have your letter advising me that Sjt. Kelappan has been
appointed the sole dictator and that the provisional committee
has been dissolved. I hope that the change will justify itself. I
have not been quite able to understand it.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20599
