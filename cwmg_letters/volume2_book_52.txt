52. LETTER TO NATAL COLONIAL SECRETARY
[DURBAN],
June 2, 1897
TO
THE HONOURABLE
THE COLONIAL SECRETARY
PIETERMARITZBURG
SIR,
As it is the intention of the representatives of the Indian
community in Natal to address a petition to the Rt. Honourable
1 Honorary Secretary of the Natal Indian Congress from June 1896,
when Gandhiji had to leave for India, to June 1897
2 Vide the preceding item.
256
the Colonial Secretary with reference to the Indian Bills of
the last session, the last batch of which was published in
yesterday's Gazette, I have to request you to hold over sending
the Despatch with reference to them to the Colonial Secretary
till the receipt of the petition which now is in the course of
preparation.
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
M. K. GANDHI
