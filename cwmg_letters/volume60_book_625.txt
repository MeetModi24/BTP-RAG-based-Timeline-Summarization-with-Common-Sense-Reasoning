625. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
April 23, 1935
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have had a fairly long talk with Kamalnayan. If. . .1
can come here before the engagement takes place, I think I
may also examine her a little. Kamalnayan also has approved
of this. I have, therefore, given him a letter to that effect
addressed to . . .2.
I had already sent Radhakrishna to Sikar before I got
your wire, and so I didn't wire to you.
How is the ear?
How is Madalasa?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2964
