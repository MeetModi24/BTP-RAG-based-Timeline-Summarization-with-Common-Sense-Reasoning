286. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
June 2, 1937
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
Perhaps you know Giriraj. He was in the Sabarmati Ashram
and has two children. His address is as follows:
Vidyashram, 157 Cloth Market, Delhi.
He has a teaching certificate and is willing to do any work
like teaching, office work, sales or anything else. His requirement
is Rs. 35 per month. Meet him and send me your impression.
See if you need him for any kind of job.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2450
