211. LETTER TO SUBHAS CHANDRA BOSE
WARDHA,
December 20, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
You attribute to me powers I know I do not possess. There
are no-changers and pro-changers nowadays. It would be wrong
on my part to interfere with the discretion of co-workers in
matters outside the operations which keep them in touch with me.
If there is any specific thing against any of them, I shall gladly
go into it.
But whether I can be of any service or not I wish you could
find a way out of this unseemly wrangling.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
SUBHAS BOSE
From a copy : Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32579/32
