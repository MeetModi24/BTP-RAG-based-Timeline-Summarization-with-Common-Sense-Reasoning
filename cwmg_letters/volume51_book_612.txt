612. LETTER TO SECRETARY, ANTI-UNTOUCHABILITY
LEAGUE, AHMEDABAD
[Before November 10, 1932]
We should not place the Depressed Classes in a position where
they might be insulted or injured. We should place ourselves in
such a position for their sake. . . .
This proposal is excellent but if public opinion is opposed
to it, I see the risk involved in this experiment.
The Hindu, 10-11-1932
