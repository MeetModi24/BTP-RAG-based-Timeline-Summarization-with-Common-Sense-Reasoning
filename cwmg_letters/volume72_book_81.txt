81. LETTER TO PURATAN J. BUCH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 15, 1940
CHI. PURATAN,
You are doing excellent work. But do you want me to
notice all that work in Harijan?
If it is likely to do any good,
I will certainly write. Anand and you must be fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PURATAN BUCH
HARIJAN ASHRAM
SABARMATI
B.B. & C.I.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N.9175
