356. LETTER TO MOHAN LAL
December 23, 1932
DEAR LALA MOHAN LAL1,
I was glad to have your letter after such a long time. What-
ever the remnant of the untouchability in the Punjab, it has got
to be fought against. Therefore, no province should say: "We are
not as the others." Every province has to show an absolutely clean
slate.
Yours sincerely,
LALA MOHAN LAL, ADVOCATE
"FIRGROVE", SIMLA
From a microfilm: S.N. 18787
