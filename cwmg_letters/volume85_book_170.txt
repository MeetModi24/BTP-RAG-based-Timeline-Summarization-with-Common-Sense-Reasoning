170. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SEVAGRAM,
August 15, 1946
DEAR LOTUS-BORN,
Your sweet letter. Your report is a good and great docu-
ment. And I am making of it the wisest use I know. Have
already written to Sir Mirza. Hope to make still better use if
it is necessary.
You should take care of the obstinate old (with apologies)
Singer.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
