463. LETTER TO NAN AND TANGAI MENON
May 14, 1936
DEAR CHILDREN,
Do you remember that there was a time when you used
to write to me? Should you not—now that you have grown in
years and wisdom? I was sorry to hear of Tangai's illness.
The hat for delicate children like you is a necessary article of
wear.
Love and kisses from
BAPU
[PS.]
Esther will tell you all about where I am at present.
My Dear Child, p. 122
