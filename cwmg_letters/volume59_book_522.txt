522. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
December 10, 1934
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Pass on the accompanying letter to Khan Saheb. The rest
Mahadev will write. After receiving Rajendrababu's letter, I
was left with no option. I have received no wire from Delhi so far.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 143
