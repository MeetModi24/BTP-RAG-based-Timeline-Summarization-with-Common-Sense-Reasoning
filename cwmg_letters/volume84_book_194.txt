194. LETTER TO BALWANTRAI K. THAKORE
SIMLA,
May 14, 1946
BHAI BALWANTRAI,
After wandering from place to place Panchoterme reached me
yesterday. I am glad you remembered me. Now that the book
will travel with me I hope to look into it.
I expect you are well.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
ACHARYA BALWANTRAI THAKORE
34 CHOWPATTY ROAD
BOMBAY 7
