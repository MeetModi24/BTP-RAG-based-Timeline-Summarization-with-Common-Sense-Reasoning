505. LETTER TO MRS. HUNTER
January 6, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
I thank you for your letter of the 8th December last, and
for telling me that you had sent to the Anti-untouchability
Committee a cheque for £42-0-.
Please convey my thanks to all the English and other friends.
I have just heard from the Committee that they duly received
your cheque. I hope you have had a proper receipt from them.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 1532
