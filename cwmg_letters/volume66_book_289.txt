289. LETTER TO DR. N. B. KHARE
October 15, 1937
DEAR DR. KHARE1,
I think that General Awari should be warned by the P. C. C.
and if he does not listen I have no doubt that there should be
a legal prosecution. But if you have any doubt yourself I am
no authoritative guide. The only authority is the President
or the Working Committee.
Yours,
M . K . GANDHI
The Hitavada, 2-4-1939
