329. LETTER TO RADHIKADEVI
[After July 26, 1944]
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. What else can one do but resort to a fast
when one is treated as less than human? I do not dare to inter-
fere. Have faith in God and put up with whatever happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. RADHIKADEVI
VISHWANATH PRASAD CHAUBE
GOPALPUR
BALLIA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
