420. LETTER TO JAINARAYAN VYAS
SEVAGRAM,
March 14, 1945
BHAI JAINARAYAN VYAS,
If you must see me then let me know the date before you
come. Make it before the 23rd. Do not call the conference.
Blessings from
BAPU
JAINARAYAN VYAS
ALL-INDIA STATES PEOPLES' CONFERENCE
JODHPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
