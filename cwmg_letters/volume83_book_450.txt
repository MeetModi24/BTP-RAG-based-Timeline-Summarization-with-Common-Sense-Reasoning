450. LETTER TO CHAKRAYYA
HARIJAN MANDIR, NEW DELHI,
April 8, 1946
CHI. CHAKRAYYA,
I have your letters. I am very busy. Stay on where you
are. Learn a lot. Learn the art of being well at any place. It
is good if Rajuji learns Hindi. He may come to Sevagram when-
ever he wishes.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 9117. Also C. W. 9186
