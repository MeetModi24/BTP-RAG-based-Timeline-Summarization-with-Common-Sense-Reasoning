672. LETTER TO AMINA QURESHI
April 16, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER AMINA,
I must have your letters by every post. How are you? What
do you eat? How do the bowels work? How are the cuts left
from the piles operation? How is Qureshi? Give all such details.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 10799. Courtesy: Gulam
Rasul Qureshi
