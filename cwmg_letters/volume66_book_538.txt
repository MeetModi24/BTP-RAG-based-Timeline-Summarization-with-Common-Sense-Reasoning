538. LETTER TO SARASWATI
March 31, 1938
CHI. SARASWATI,
Ba and I were very sorry that you could not come, but
what could be done? We have to live as God disposes, shouldn't
we? I hope to send for you soon enough. Can you stand the
heat? Will you keep up your studies? Reply promptly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6166. Also C.W. 3439. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
