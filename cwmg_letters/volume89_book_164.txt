164. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 2, 1947
CHI. AMRIT,
Of course, you will not only not deplore my fast but wel-
come it as the only act so far as I am concerned. Had it not
been for the flare-up in C[alcutta] I would have started for
Delhi this evening on my way to the Punjab. Jawaharlal
thinks it is time now for me to go there. If the disturbance
continues, the so-called friendship was some accident. The fast
will show where I am.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
