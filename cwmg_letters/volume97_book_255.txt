255. LETTER TO AMBA GANDHI
November 2, 1934
CHI. AMBA,
I received your letter. There was nothing wrong in that you both came
to Kashi afterwards and lived the way you did.
Make some effort and learn Gujarati. I hope you will have a safe delivery.
Why does Prabhudas's weight not increase? With you by his side, Prabhudas
should have no cause for worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : S.N. 33063
