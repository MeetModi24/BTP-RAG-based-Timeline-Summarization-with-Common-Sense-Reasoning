655. LETTER TO HARJIVAN KOTAK
September 30, 1935
CHI. HARJIVAN,
You are being obstinate. Nobody can convince a person
who is being stubborn. Your behaviour shows that you cannot
observe rules. Either things should work out as you wish or
else you would be furious. Is this the result of staying so many
years with me? You are angry and I am pained. God knows
whether it is a folly to be angry or to feel pained. Pain is a
sign of attachment. Attachment and anger are signs of rajas. My
pain will not be for long. How nice it would be if your anger
also subsides after showing up for a moment.
Blessings from
