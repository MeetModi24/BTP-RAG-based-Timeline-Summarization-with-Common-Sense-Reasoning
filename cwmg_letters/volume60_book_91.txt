91. LETTER TO NIRMAL KUMAR BOSE
January 14, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
Your article has been simply waiting for my note on it.
Your postcard has therefore come in time. I am glad you have
reconsidered your position. You can publish your notes of the
interview if you send it to me first for revision.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Delhi till 23rd inst.
SHRI NIRMAL K. BOSE
6/1A BR. INDIAN STREET
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. l10518 and 10521
