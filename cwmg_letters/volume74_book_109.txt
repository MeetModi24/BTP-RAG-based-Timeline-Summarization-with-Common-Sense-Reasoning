109. LETTER TO BHARATAN KUMARAPPA
SEVAGRAM,
May 22, 1941
DEAR BHARATAN1,
Yes, Kumarappa may take the salts—mild doses. He must
not leave Coonoor till the yellowness is entirely gone. If necessary
you will wire.
As to the Bombay report, I agree with your suggestion.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10154
