18. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 8, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I am saying I cannot sponsor the fund. Of course you should.
I am saying no in gentle terms.
I may not be ready with your cupboard. I am trying to pro-
cure one ready-made.
Did I tell you Ba had gone to Delhi for Lakshmi ? You will
look them up. Kashi and Kanu leaving today. Rajaji went to
Delhi today only for Lakshmi. You should build up your strength.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3955. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7264
