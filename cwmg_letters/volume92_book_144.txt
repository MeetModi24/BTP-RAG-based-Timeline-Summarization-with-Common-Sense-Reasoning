144. LETTER TO SURAJBEHN
September 19, 1929
CHI. SURAJBEHN,
I have your letter. You should now improve your hand-
writing. Surely you are not going to turn round and tell me
that you cannot improve your handwriting at your age? What-
ever the age, nothing is difficult for one who has determination.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32578/23
