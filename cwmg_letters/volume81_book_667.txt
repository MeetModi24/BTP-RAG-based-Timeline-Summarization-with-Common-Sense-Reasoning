667. LETTER TO ZAKIR HUSAIN
October 18, 1945
BHAI ZAKIR,
I have sent a wire today. I can't believe you could have
said such a thing. Whatever it is, you had better make a state-
ment about what you had said.
I hope you are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. ZAKIR HUSAIN
JAMIA MILLIA
DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
