346. LETTER TO JHAVERBHAI PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
March 5, 1945
CHI. JHAVERBHAI,
On the reverse is the Foreword. I wrote it yesterday. I
remember having written something like it before. Are you sure
it is not in your note book?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1368. Also Pyarelal Papers.
Courtesy : Pyarelal
