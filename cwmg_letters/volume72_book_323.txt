323. LETTER TO BHOLANATH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 12, 1940
BHAI BHOLANATH,
I could reach your letter only yesterday. I had made up my
mind to write. How it got left I do not know, but it was all to
the good. It should be enough if your work is not interfered
with.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1379
