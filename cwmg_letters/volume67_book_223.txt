223. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 11, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Have you read Lilavati's letter?
If you have not, you will
know about it from my reply. Kanti it seems could give you
fairly good work. Read carefully what I have written today.
I feel like writing about something else too, but I will restrain
myself. Mirabehn is down with malaria.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am sending that draft for Jawaharlal. I have kept with
me a copy each of the articles.
