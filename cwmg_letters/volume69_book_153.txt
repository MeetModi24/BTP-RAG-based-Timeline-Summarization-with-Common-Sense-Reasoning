153. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
[April 8, 1939]
CHI. BRAJKISAN,
Why such a long letter? I admit my mistake. My nature
is responsible for it. Only those who demand insistently get
anything from me. I have not the ability to judge everyone.
Nevertheless, my confessing my error does not in any way reduce
your own. Your lack of method is terrible though you are not at
all careless. Your attachment, too, is equally terrible. That is why
you have not finished any work. Let the brothers alone even now.
Where do you have any money to give? Do not take even a pie
from the family. Beg for Jugatramji. Take up a job and live
on the salary. You will thus set an example for the brothers.
Forget me, and surrendering yourself to God make an effort.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
How much did you spend on having the watch repaired?
