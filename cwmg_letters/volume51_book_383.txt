383. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
October 10, 1932
BHAI KRISHNACHANDRA,
Your letter reached me only today. Destiny is certainly there
but so is effort too. Destiny simply means that in the absence
of effort we have only the fruits of the actions of our past lives.
Effort can change destiny. Therefore brahmacharya is necessary for
those who wish to realize the Brahman. See the fifteenth chapter of
the Gita. Anyway brahmacharya is the essence of the Gita. One
who wants to lose himself in the Brahman, one who wants to be
ever immersed in service has no time for sensual pleasures. This
answers all your doubts.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
