299. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
December 16, 1946
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
Your letter of November 17 came into my hands on December
14. Such is the postal arrangement here!!! You have done right.
Now the work for Harijans will shine forth. The times are such
that everyone must do the greatest penance. And of course you
have the capacity for it. You have done right in becoming the
President of the Hindi Sahitya Sammelan. You should serve to
the best of your ability.
This work should not hinder your
Harijan service. You must have got my letter to this effect.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi : Badonke Preranadayak Kuchh Patra, front
cover
