463. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
SEVAGRAM,
March 19, 1945
CHI. MRIDU,
I have your letter. It is good that you met Mathuradas. I
have gone through the speech for the Association. It is good.
If all the three think it proper, you too may write. I have done
what I had to do. Look after your health. There is a letter
from Mummy also. I do not write to her separately.
Blessings from
BAPU
MRIDULA SARABHAI
KASHMIR HOUSE
94 NAPEAN SEA ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
