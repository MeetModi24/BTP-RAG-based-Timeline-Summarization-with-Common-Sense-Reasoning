249. LETTER TO BHAGWANDAS
July 27, 1924
DEAR BABU BHAGWANDAS,
I thank you for your letter. I assure you I am continually
taxing myself as to how to end this wrangling. I know that
there is room for both the policies. But, as you very properly say,
they can only work like submarines and aeroplanes. Fields of
action must be different. They will not then clash but help. I
am seeking means of exit from the Congress without a fireworks
display. In Mr. Tilak's own time, I had no difficulties in
working along my own lines. And I know that I venerated
him and that he did not dislike me and helped me whenever he
could.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
BABU BHAGWANDASJI
SEVASHRAM, SIGRA
BENARES CANTT.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
