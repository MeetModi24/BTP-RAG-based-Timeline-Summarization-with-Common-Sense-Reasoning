402. LETTER TO THE PRINCIPAL, KANYA GURUKUL,
DEHRA DUN
MAHISHADAL,
December 28, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. I was a little surprised. I had always
regarded the Gurukul as Dharma Devji's institution.
I have forwarded your letter to Thakkar Bapa and have
written to him to write to you.
THE PRINCIPAL
KANYA GURUKUL
DEHRA DUN
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
82-19
