167. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
SEVAGRAM,
February 4, 1945
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I have your letter . You are scared for nothing. Let all the
people come together. I do not think it proper to change the pro-
gramme after everything has been decided. On my part I have
no misgivings. After all we only want to do our work. You
and Maganbhai should come over. Hope you have completely
recovered.
Blessings from
BAPU
AMRITLAL NANAVATI
GUJARAT VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
