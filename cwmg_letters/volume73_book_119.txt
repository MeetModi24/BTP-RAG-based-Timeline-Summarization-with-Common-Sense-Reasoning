119. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
October 25, 1940
BHAI VITHALDAS,
I have your letter about the Meerut Ashram. I am trying
to stabilize the Ashram.
I hope I shall succeed. Tell Vaid
not to give up his trade entirely. Let him master the science
of spinning and desist from unnecessary suppression of the body.
His body should be vigorous.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VITHALDAS JERAJANI
A.I.S.A. KHADI BHANDAR
396 KALBADEVI
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9797
