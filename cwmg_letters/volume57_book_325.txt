325. LETTER TO TOTARAM SANADHYA
March 24 [1934]
BHAI TOTARAMJI,
I received your letter. You may certainly go to Wardha
but you can come here on your way if you feel it is necessary. We
met at Wardha during this month. There is no reason to worry
about Hariprasad.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2527
