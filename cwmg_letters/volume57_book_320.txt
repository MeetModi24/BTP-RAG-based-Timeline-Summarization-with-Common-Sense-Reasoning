320. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
[March 23, 1934]
CHI. LILAVATI,
I got your letter. What shall I write to you? How can it
be that you don't get my letters?
You should not harass Prema
in that manner.
You may try to copy her self-sacrifice and her
hard work. If she wants any personal service from you, by all
means render it.
But how can you insist on giving such service
if she doesn't want it?
Now that Narandas is there, follow
his advice. Get well anyhow and become steady in mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9328. Also C.W. 6603. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
