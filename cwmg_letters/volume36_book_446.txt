446. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
June 6, 1928
BHAISHRI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I got your letter. I too hold that asanas are beneficial. But
my experience is that specific knowledge is needed to choose the
asanas.
It now seems that I shall remain in the Ashram during
August. Do come.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SHRIYUT GHANSHYAMDAS BIRLA
BIRLA PARK
BALLYGUNGE
CALCUTTA
From Hindi: C.W. 6159. Courtesy: G.D. Birla
