347. LETTER TO DR. H. W. B. MORENO
8 PRETORIA STREET,
CALCUTTA,
December 27, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your kind letter. I am so hemmed in that in this
place of long distances, much as I should like to, it is not pos-
sible for me to go to your house. But if you could take the
chance of finding me at the above address, please come tomorrow,
the 28th instant at 8 p.m.
Yours sincerely,
H. W. B. MORENO, ESQ.
PRESIDENT, THE ANGLO-INDIAN LEAGUE
2 WELLESLEY SQUARE, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 13024
