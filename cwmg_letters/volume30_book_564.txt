564. LETTER TO A. L. NAIR
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 23, 1926
DEAR DR. NAIR,
I thank you for your letter of the 19th instant. For the rea-
sons explained to you I am unable to stay in Bombay up to the
26th instant for the ceremony.
I hope the function will be
successful and that the original intention of the founders will be
realized.
Yours sincerely,
DR. A. L. NAIR
MESSRS N. POWELL & Co.
BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19565
