51. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
November 15, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
Send fruit with Ganpat. I take it that it includes apples.
The stock of apples is exhausted. I have of course spoken to
Kanu for the oranges, as that man Satyanarayan fleeces us. His
bill was exorbitant. I have paid it. Do the oranges cost four
rupees a hundred and bananas eight annas for fifty? In spite of
having been told not to, he has sent the fruit again. I have
returned it. This is just for your information.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11503
