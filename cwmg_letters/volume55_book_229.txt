229. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
June 10, 1933
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
I had put your letter under my mattress. I could reach it
only today. If you can come away and if Narandas permits to
do so, do come, so that we can talk and understand each other's
point of view. If you cannot come, you may ask me whatever
questions you may wish. But assuming that you will come, I
don't write anything in this letter.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 358. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
