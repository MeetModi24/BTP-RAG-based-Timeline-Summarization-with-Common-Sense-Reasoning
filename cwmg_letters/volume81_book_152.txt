152. LETTER TO GOPE GURBUXANI
August 8, 1945
CHI. GURBUXANI,
You are mistaken. It is not right that the world has an eye
only for the wealth of people. It looks more for the heart than
for wealth.
You must have received the money. Your intention to give
all the money is commendable.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1318
