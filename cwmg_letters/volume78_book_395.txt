395. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI G. PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
What guidance can I offer? See if you can deduce something
from what I am writing. I have had a talk with Ravishankar
Maharaj3, who will offer some [advice]. All of you should do as
much [work] as you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHIVABHAI PATEL
BOCHASAN
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
