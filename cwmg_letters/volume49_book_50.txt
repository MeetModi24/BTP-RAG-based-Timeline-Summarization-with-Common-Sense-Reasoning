50. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
January 24, 1932
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I have your two letters. What can I write to you? The
progress of your work is as regular as the rising and setting of
the sun. Keep it up. I am being provided with datuns. These
days Sardar prepares them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6539
