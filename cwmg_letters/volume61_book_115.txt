115. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
May 20, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
I got your letter. I have already written to you and given
you complete freedom whether or not to give a cow to Ramjibhai.
Follow your own inclination in the matter. I attach no importance
to my wish. Where is the sense in my expressing a wish when it
is you who are doing the work?
You may sell to Parnerkar as many of the cattle as you
think necessary. In other words, you have complete freedom in
this matter. I assume that you will inform me of whatever you do.
How Vanu's illness is being prolonged? I have to reach
Borsad on the 23rd. And what a bother also that you have to
go to Broach for Mani's molar?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9079
