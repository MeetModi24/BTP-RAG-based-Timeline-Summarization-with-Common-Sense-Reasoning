505. LETTER TO HENRY S. SALT
October 28, 1932
I thank you for your letter. May I say in all humility that
one rarely finds people outside India recognizing non-human be-
ings as fellow beings. Millennium will have come when mankind
generally recognizes and acts up to this grand truth.
Thank you.
With my best regards to you and Mrs. Salt,
Yours sincerely,
M.K.G.
From a photostat: S.N. 18579
