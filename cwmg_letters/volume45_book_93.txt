93. LETTER TO ROHINI K. DESAI
January 10, 1931
CHI. ROHINI,
I got your letter. The handwriting was beautiful, but the
letter was written in pencil. As far as possible, you should not
write a letter in pencil. I have also understood that Kanajibhai
71
will be released. When he is released, ask him whether the
people outside have done him any harm that, having given up
chewing pan during his stay in the rest-house, he should resume
the practice outside and deprive the poor of the money spent on it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2656
