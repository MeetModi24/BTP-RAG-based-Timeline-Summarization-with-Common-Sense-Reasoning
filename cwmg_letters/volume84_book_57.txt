57. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
NEW DELHI,
April 22, 1946
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Bhansali can do what he likes. It is not necessary to obtain
my permission for giving up ginning.
Did the thunderstorm cause any damage? Or did it do any
good?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 4552
