283. LETTER TO DIRECTOR, PUSA INSTITUTE
WARDHA,
December 15, 1928
THE DIRECTOR
PUSA INSTITUTE
DEAR SIR,
Could you please let me know whether you have bee-keeping
on the Pusa Farm, and if so, whether instruction is given there in
bee-keeping and whether there is any literature about bee-keeping
in India?
Yours faithfully,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13810
