318. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
August 7, 1938
CHI. VALJI,
I have kept back both your articles.
The one about Shan-
karacharya seems unnecessary. The argument does not sound
convincing. The other about the Manusmriti is rather imaginative.
It makes pleasant reading but it does not help much in understand-
ing the meaning of the Smritis. What would you suggest?
How is Dudhibehn? How is the children's education going
on? How is their health? And yours? Here Kaka and a few
others have had cholera. Two workers have passed away.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7480. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
