434. LETTER TO MAITRI GIRI
Silence Day [May 26, 1930]
DEAR MAITRI,
Have you become wise? Be a credit to the name of your father.
How is Krishnamaiya Devi? Write to me a letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 578
