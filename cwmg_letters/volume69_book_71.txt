71. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
DELHI,
March 16, 1939
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have your letter. I deliberately do not want to write
much. It is my firm belief that we should not increase our de-
mands. We may withdraw civil disobedience if the Praja Mandal
is recognized unconditionally and civil liberties are granted. Of
course the prisoners must be released.
I hope you are keeping well and mentally also you are at
your best. Do you do any reading? Do you spin? What is
