714. LETTER TO NRISINHAPRASAD K. BHATT
SIMLA,
July 12, 1945
CHI. NANABHAI,
I am sending herewith a copy of a letter addressed to
Narahari. Now, if you can send me the names, I shall consider
them in consultation with Bapa and then send them on to you.
Write to me at Sevagram. I hope to leave on the 15th.
More later.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
SHRI NANABHAI BHATT
DAKSHINAMURTI
AMBLA (BHAVNAGAR STATE)
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
