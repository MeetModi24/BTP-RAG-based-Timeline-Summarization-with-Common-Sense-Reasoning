241. LETTER TO BHOLANATH SINGH
April 17, 1929
BHAI BHOLANATH,
It is only today that I am able to reply to your letter received
long ago. Regarding girls of tender age I know of no other way
than to publicize each case and get in touch with the parents and
persuade them not to commit such "murder" of their children.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
BHAI BHOLANATH SINGH
RASHTRIYA VIDYALAYA
P.O. HAVELI KHADAGPUR
DIST. MONGHYR, BIHAR
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7778
