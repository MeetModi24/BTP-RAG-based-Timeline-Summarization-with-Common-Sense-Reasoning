201. LETTER TO H. V. KAMATH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 30, 1940
MY DEAR KAMATH,
Have just seen the printed correspondence. I see Rajkumari
said "daily" instead of "regularly". It was purely a slip for which
please accept my apology. R. K. is just now not here or she would
have apologized.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a facsimile: Mahatma, Vol. V, between pp. 280 and 281
