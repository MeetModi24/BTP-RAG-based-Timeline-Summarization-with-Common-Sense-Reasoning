162. LETTER TO MANINDRA NATH DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM,
November 17, 1941
DEAR MANINDRA,
I have your letter. If you are brave and have no fear of
death, you should fearlessly move among Muslims and show them
affection and wish them well. You should never feel cowed down.
I know this is difficult but not impossible.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. MANINDRA NATH DAS GUPTA
HINDU HOSTEL
MANIPUR AGRICULTURAL SCHOOL
DACCA (BENGAL)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
