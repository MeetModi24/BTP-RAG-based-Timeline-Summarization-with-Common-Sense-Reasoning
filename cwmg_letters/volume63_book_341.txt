341. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
September 12, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
Puri has sprung a surprise on me. He says you do not want
him, his presence will be a tax on you. You want to be left
alone. If so, P. must go. He too says he won't come up to
your expectations. Balwantsinha will follow with milk. You will
take what service you like from him. Who will sleep there?
You must not put any strain on yourself. I wish I could come
over to you and see you face to face. But that cannot be just yet.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6363. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9829
