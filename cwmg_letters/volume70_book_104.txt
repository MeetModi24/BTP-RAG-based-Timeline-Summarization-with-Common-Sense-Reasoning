104. LETTER TO GHULAM RASUL QURESHI
SEGAON,
August 11, 1939
CHI. QURESHI
I have your letter. I take it that the instalment of Rs. 100
has started. I am glad you got out of Sugandhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
If Amtul Salaam is still there, tell her that I have not
written to her thinking she must have left.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10773. Courtesy: Ghulam Rasul
Qureshi
