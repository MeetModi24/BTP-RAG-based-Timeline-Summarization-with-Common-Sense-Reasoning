166. LETTER TO A. VAIDYANATHA IYER
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 30, 1939
DEAR VAIDYANATHA IYER,
Under separate cover I send you the papers received from
Shri Prasada Rao. I would like you to give me your reply to
his allegations. He seems to be a seasoned co-worker. Why is it
difficult to win him over if his allegations are untrue? If they
are true, there is something wrong about the whole affair. Have
the Brahmins and the orthodox non-Brahmins boycotted the
temple1?
Yours,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
