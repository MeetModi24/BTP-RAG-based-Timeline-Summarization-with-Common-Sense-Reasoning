354. LETTER TO S. SEN
December 23, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 17th instant enclosing resolution of the
students and staff of your college, for which please convey my
thanks to them and accept them for yourself. You will not ask
me to regard this resolution as the final contribution by your col-
lege. I take it by way of promise of tangible services to the cause
of Harijans.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. SEN
PRINCIPAL, COMMERCIAL COLLEGE
8 DARYAGANJ, DELHI
From a microfilm: S.N. 18788
