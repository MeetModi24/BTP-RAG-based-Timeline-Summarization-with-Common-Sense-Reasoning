90. LETTER TO SECRETARY FOR INTERIOR
[JOHANNESBURG,]
July [4], 1913
DEAR MR. GORGES,
I thank you for your note of the 3rd instant, and in view of
my conversation with you regarding Mr. Polak, may I ask you to
'phone to me as early as you can tomorrow, Saturday? I shall be
practically up to half-past-two at my office, telephone No. 1635.
I promised to let you have the Natal case regarding the in-
dentured Indians who are paying the £3 tax. The case is Subrayen
versus the Principal Immigration Officer, Natal Reports, page 638.
This is the substance telegraphed to me of the case:
