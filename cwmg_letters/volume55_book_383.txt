383. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
July 30, 1933
CHI. MAHAVIR,
You seem to be working well. Go on improving your effi-
ciency in the same manner and produce a fine impression on
everybody with your sincere work. Bring credit to the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
