477. LETTER TO K. NAGESWAR RAO
November 27, 1934
MY DEAR NAGESWAR RAO,
You have outdone everybody in the race for votes. But even
you had to spend money? What a tragedy! How much were you
out of pocket? Without his opposition the Congress could not have
shown its hold over the people.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
K. NAGESWAR RAO
MADRAS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
