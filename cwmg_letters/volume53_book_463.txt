463. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
February 18, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I have your letter. Think no more of Kudsia. After all,
only her father has the right to decide her future. Our duty is to
perform with heart and soul whatever job falls to our lot. The
result rests with God. I hope Dr. Sharma's daughter is all right.
What was the treatment? I am very happy to learn that you are
making all-round progress. What treatment is given to you? What
do you eat? Tell me everything. I have written quite a few
letters to you. I am keeping count of them. I shall certainly
keep on writing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 277
