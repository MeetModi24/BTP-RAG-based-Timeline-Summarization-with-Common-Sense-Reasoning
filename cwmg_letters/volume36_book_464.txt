464. LETTER TO KEDARNATH BANNERJEE
[After June 9, 1928]
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
I have no doubt that even if it should cause temporary pain
to your father you should leave him and go where you can earn
a decent livelihood so as to help your people.
Yours sincerely,
KEDARNATH BANNERJEE
NAYA GANJ
CAWNPORE
