107. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
SABARMATI,
August 17, 1929
BHAI RAJENDRABABU,
I have a distressing letter from Krishnadas. I enclose a copy
of it. I have written fully to Ram Binod. I am also enclosing
a copy of my letter. If he does not want to compromise then
the matter will have to be taken to court. I believe you will be
coming here on the 21st. We shall then talk more. I shall inform
you when I get Ram Binod's letter.
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/121
