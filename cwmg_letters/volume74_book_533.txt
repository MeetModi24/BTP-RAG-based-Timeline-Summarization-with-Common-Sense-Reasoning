533. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
September 17, 1941
CHI. BABUDI,
I got your letter. Lotus seed and cardamom are well-known
tonics.
If they solve your problem, so much the better.
The
chief thing is attention to diet. There is no illness here worth
mentioning. Everything is all right. There is considerable crowding.
Nobody here is now afraid of typhoid, since good nursing has
saved all cases. You are having good experience there. Mix with
all as sugar mixes in milk. Prabhavati has left. She will return
on the 23rd.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10037. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokhawala
