286. LETTER TO YUVARAJA OF PITHAPURAM
SEVAGRAM,
October 6, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of the 3rd inst. and the enclosed statement.
He does not feel like putting you to the inconvenience of coming here. Moreover,
at present every moment of his is pre-mortgaged.
With regards,
Yours sincerely,
THE YUVARAJA OF PITHAPURAM
VASANTASOBHA
TEYNAMPET P. O.
MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
