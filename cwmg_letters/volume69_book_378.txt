378. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 20, 1939
CHI. KAKA,
Please find enclosed a letter from Amritlal. Return it to me
today along with the prescription.
The very next day after your visit I gave instructions to
Jivanji to print the Autobiography in the Nagari script and sell it
at one anna less.
There is no reply from Sardar. I will insist on getting one.
I will be going to the Mahila Ashram at 4 and to Jamnalalji's
bungalow at 4.30. Vinoba will be there. Have you agreed to
keep Prabhu Dayal?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10922
