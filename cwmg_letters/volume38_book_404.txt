404. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
January 13, 1929
DEAR SATISBABU,
Can you prepare the tricolour national flag with the charkha
in it? The question is how to have the three colours nicely on
one piece. If you can prepare it, let me have the price also.
You will keep me informed of how the new Khadi Bhandar
is going on.
I wish I could have given more days to you. Hemprabha
Devi must not overwork herself. What is the cost there per head
of feeding and how is it worked out?
Love.
BAPU
SJT. SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 1601
