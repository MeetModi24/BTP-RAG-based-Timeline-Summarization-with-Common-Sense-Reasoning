94. LETTER TO SYED HUSSAIN
January 30, 1919
In wishing you success in your new enterprise, I would like to
say how I hope your writings would be worthy of the title you have
chosen for your journal; and may I further hope that to a robust
independence you will add an equal measure of self-restraint
and the strictest adherence to truth? Too often in our journals
as in others do we get fiction instead of fact and declamation in
place of sober reasoning. You would make The Independent a power
in the land and a means of education for the people by avoiding
the errors I have drawn attention to.
Yours sincerely,
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
