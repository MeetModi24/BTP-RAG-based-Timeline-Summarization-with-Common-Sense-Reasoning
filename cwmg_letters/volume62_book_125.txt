125. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
November 12, 1935
The sight of your handwriting has become rare these days.
But I am content that you let me have it every time there is some
crisis.
I am cherishing the hope that for the present I will at least
get a postcard from you from time to time. The Sardar suffers more
because he delayed [his operation]. But there is no fear, since he
knows how to bear pain and can laugh even while in pain.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, pp. 159-60
