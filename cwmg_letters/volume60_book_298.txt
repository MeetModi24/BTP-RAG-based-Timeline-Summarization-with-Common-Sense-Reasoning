298. LETTER TO HARILAL GANDHI
February 14, 1935
CHI. HARILAL,
I have received your letter. It is my earnest desire to have
you with me if you can come willingly. I think of you every day.
If you have come to have the same faith about the truthfulness
of my life, your place from now on is with me. What you hope
to get in Amreli can be had here and in the neighbourhood. And if
you are in a position to do some work, I have plenty of it.
Moreover you will have an unlimited number of good compa-
nions here. The most weighty argument is that there is no
certainty about me. One cannot say when I may get the invita-
tion to go to the temple. I would therefore like you to be with
me. Now you do whatever you deem proper. Kanti will write
to you about the other part of the letter. Maybe we shall meet
very soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
