336. LETTER TO N. C. KELKAR
POST ANDHERI,
April 13, 1924
DEAR MR. KELKAR,
I have your letter. I have written to Mr. Sheriff Devji Kanji.
He has written to me in reply raising difficulties as to the terms of
reference. I see Mr. Potdar does likewise. I can only suggest
that, if you send me the terms of reference, I shall submit them to
him and, if he suggests any, I shall submit them to you. I have
asked Mr. Sheriff Devji Kanji to meet me on Thursday next.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
N. C. KELKAR, ESQ.
THE "KESARI" AND THE "MAHRATTA" OFFICE
POONA CITY
From a photostat : S.N. 8727
