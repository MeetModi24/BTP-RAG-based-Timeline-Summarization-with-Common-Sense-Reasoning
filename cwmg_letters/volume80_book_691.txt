691. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA AND HOSHIARI
SIMLA,
July 9, 1945
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have your letter. I had a talk with Mirabehn. She is
still here. If you are still in the Ashram, then do not leave be-
fore I come. We shall see after I arrive there.
CHI. HOSHIARI,
I am so much engrossed in work that I hardly get any time.
Now I hope to reach the Ashram soon. Everything is in God's
hands.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
