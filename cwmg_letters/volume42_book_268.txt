268. LETTER TO MATHURADAS PURUSHOTTAM
WARDHA,
December 8, 1929
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I hope you are carrying out my suggestions to you regarding
daily attendance, etc. Keep me informed from time to time how
the work is going on.
Inform me about Motibehn's daily work. See that she writes
her diary.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3734
