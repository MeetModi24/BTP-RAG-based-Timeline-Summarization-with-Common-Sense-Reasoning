242. LETTER TO BALBHADRA
SEVAGRAM,
February 19, 1945
BHAI BALBHADRA,
I have your postcard. It is good that you wrote. Of course
Satyavatibehn has my blessings. Let us see what happens now.
Keep me informed. Chand is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
BALBHADRA
6 PYARELAL BUILDING
KASHMIRI GATE
DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
