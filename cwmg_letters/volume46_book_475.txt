475. LETTER TO P. G. MATHEW
BORSAD,
June 14, 1931
MY DEAR MATHEW,
How is it you funked it again. I had fully expected you at
Bardoli. You could have passed a clear week with me. I do not
mind your not coming if you are in love with your work. Give
me an idea of your day's work.
SYT. MATHEW
THE ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a microfilm: S.N. 17282
