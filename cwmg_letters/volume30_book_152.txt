152. LETTER TO PARASRAM MEHROTRA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday [March 20, 1926]
BHAI PARASRAM,
Your letter. Never mind if the post at the Vidyapith has been
filled up. Even if you are late the other post will not be filled
up. You can come even after finishing the work there. It is good
that you are practising typewriting.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PARASRAM
'STREEDARPAN' KARYALAYA
CAWNPORE (U. P.)
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4961. Courtesy: Parasram Mehrotra
