34. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
MUSSOORIE,
Silence Day [October 21, 1929]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
There is nothing special today to write to you about. Read
the letter to Raghunath Prasad and also Chhaganlal's which
accompanies it.
Bhansali must be his normal self again. Request everyone to
refrain from talking to him about the events during and following
the fast.
Let me know your final decision about Budhabhai 1.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5565
