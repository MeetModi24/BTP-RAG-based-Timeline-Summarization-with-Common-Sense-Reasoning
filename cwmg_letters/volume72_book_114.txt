114. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 24, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
I have your translations. I must make time to read them. V.
Hari says he does not want any now. All the same you must prac-
tise them. One never knows when the need will arise.
Your corrections must be in ink for my eyes' sake—not mine!
This week's articles will go in today I hope. I have not got
them from Kanu yet.
No time for more just now. Nanabhai Bhatt has come in.
I am silent indefinitely from 7.30 a.m. today.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3668. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6477
