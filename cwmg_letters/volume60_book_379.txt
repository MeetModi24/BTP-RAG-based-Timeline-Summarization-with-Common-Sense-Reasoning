379. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM BAVISHI
March 8, 1935
BHAI SHRI PURUSHOTTAM,
I have your letter. The custom prevalent among the Kanabis
of consuming large quantities of sugar on such occasions as death
is certainly bad. But who will pay heed to my writings? The
local people should work to that end.
It is better if they use
jaggery instead of sugar, but why should even jaggery be put to
unnecessary use? Send me the details of your village industry.
What work do you do in the agriculture department? In what
subject have you gained specialized knowledge?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 125. Also C.W. 4744
