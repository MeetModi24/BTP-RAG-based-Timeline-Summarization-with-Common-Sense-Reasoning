60. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA
April 28, 1933
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRA,
I have your letter of the 25th instant.
You are asking me to do something which I cannot do at
the moment. I would ask you, therefore, to study carefully the
pages of the Harijan and I think you will understand from them
my idea of 'Reformed Hinduism'. I always believe in a concrete
programme. We have this in the big untouchability work. If we
succeed in tackling that root of all evil in Hinduism, the rest will
be incredibly simple and easy. The work of reformation will pro-
ceed apace as soon as many of us realize that we must concen-
trate our energy on that single reform. You will see how I have
dealt with your question about a model temple.
Yours sincerely,
