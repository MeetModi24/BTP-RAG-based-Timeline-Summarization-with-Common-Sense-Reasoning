13. LETTER TO PARSEE COWASJI
[JOHANNESBURG,]
July 8, 1905
DEAR SHRI PARSEE COWASJI,
Your letter to hand. I am not in a position to give you monetary
help.
M. K. GANDHI
MR. PARSEE COWASJI,
115 FIELD STREET
DURBAN
From the Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: Letter Book (1905): No. 584
1 Vide Vol. IV, pp. 348, 357- and 367.
2 Gandhiji has spelt the name here as "Kavasji".
