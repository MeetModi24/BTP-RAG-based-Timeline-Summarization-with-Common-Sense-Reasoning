595. LETTER TO LALA GIRDHARILAL
October 5, 1938
DEAR LALA GIRDHARILAL,
You are needlessly suspicious. There is no change in me
or my form.
I still advise you to resign. It is surely wrong to refuse
when your colleagues want you to. They can't take the work
from you that they want.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. LALA GIRDHARILAL
C/O SJT. A. HOON, BAR-AT-LAW
55 CANTONMENT
CAWNPORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
