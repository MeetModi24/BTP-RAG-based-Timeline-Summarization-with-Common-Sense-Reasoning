162. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
BARDOLI,
January 7, 1942
CHI. TARAMATI,
Dr. Bhaskar's letter to Sardar has arrived today. It reports great
improvement in Mathuradas's health. Observing silence has been very
beneficial. Let Mathuradas take good care and observe silence without
being impatient at all to talk. Tell Dilip to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TARAMATIBEHN MATHURADAS
WINDI HALL
DEOLALI - G. I. RLY.
