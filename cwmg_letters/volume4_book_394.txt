394. LETTER TO OMAR HAJI AMOD ZAVERI
[JOHANNESBURG,]
May 26, 1905
OMAR HAJI AMOD ZAVERI
Box 441
DURBAN
DEAR SHRI OMAR HAJI AMOD ZAVERI,
Received your letter as well as copies of Sheth Haji Mahomed's
letter. I am very much surprised and sorry to read the latter and to
know that even mature and wise men [sometimes] forget themselves.
I think it would have been good if you had sent a short reply as soon
as the letter was received. But since no reply has been sent so far, I see
no need to do so now. I shall write to you if I receive a letter.
Salaams from
M. K. GANDHI
From the Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: Letter Book (1905) : No. 157
