211. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
[End of March 1927]
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I got the letter you wrote before you left. I hope you have
arrived safe.
I had sent a radio set for you, but before it could reach
you your steamer had left the wharf. I had it sent within the
time fixed by you.
One who has failed to keep one's word should become more
resolute. You could not keep your promise, so now you have
resolved not to make any. Remember this is not the way to rise;
it only leads to one's fall. May God help you . . .1
From the Gujarati original: G.N. 4715. Courtesy: Sushilabehn Gandhi
