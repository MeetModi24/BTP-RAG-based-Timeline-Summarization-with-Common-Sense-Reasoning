281. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI A. MEHTA
August 27, 1945
BHAI BHAGWANJI,
I went through your 20 points today. You have apologized
thrice. But you were pardoned even without your asking. Re-
member the lawyer's maxim: Do not give judgment till you have
examined all the evidence. Your words have no effect on me
as you have formed a habit of jumping to conclusions without
examining things. The same is true of your last letter. This
postcard needs no reply.
I am sure, God is.
Remembrance of God from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI BHAGWANJI ANUPCHAND, VAKIL
RAJKOT
