36. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
September 24, 1931
CHI. GANGABEHN (SENIOR),
I hope your mind is at peace and you are keeping good health.
I do not immediately get all the letters to read, so I do not know
whether there is one from you. Write to me every week and pour
out your heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 61. Also C.W. 8786. Courtesy:
Gangabehn Vaidya
