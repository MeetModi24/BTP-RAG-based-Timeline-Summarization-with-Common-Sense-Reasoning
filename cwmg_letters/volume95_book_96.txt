96. LETTER TO DAHIBEHN R. PATEL
Tuesday [About October 1929]
CHI. DAHIBEHN 3 ,
Why do you faint so often? Steady the mind. Rid the mind of
anything that disturbs it. Eat very simple food. Don't let the stomach
get heavy. Sit naked in the sun; but take care that the head does not
get heated. If you wish to do any work, do it sitting. Take a hip-bath
and walk a little every day. Take someone as an escort, when you
wish to go anywhere. Take blue-ray treatment. Purushottam will
guide you. Take it on the face. Don't allow depression to weigh your
heart down.
Blessings from
BAPU
