395. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 10, 1940
CHI. PRITHVI SINGH,
I have your letter. Must you write in English? I merely passed
on the telegram I had received from Guntur. I did not suggest that
you should go. But that is over now.
You need not feel concerned if anybody distorts what I write.
What certificate need I give you? Need I give one to Mahadev?
I do not intend to write anything. Get on with your job and every-
thing will be all right. Do not move about much.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5637. Also C.W. 2948. Cour-
tesy: Prithvi Singh
