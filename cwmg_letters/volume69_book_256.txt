256. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
May 8, 1939
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You will have got my letters. I had sent a letter for Mother too.
Do help Lilavati to the extent you can but do not reduce
the time devoted to carding.
Why not get Hari's wife treated at the leprosy hospital there?
I have written about Mathew. It was by mistake that
Mahadev directed that no post should be sent there. Now a
telegram has been sent. The newspapers should come to you,
so also the mail and you can then forward the things you think
worth forwarding. Social activity is as necessary as individual
activity. Each one of us is unique as an individual but we are
also social beings. If there is no society we too are nothing.
That is why it is our dharma to take part in community prayers
and community yajnas.
Today we leave for Kashi, then on to Bombay and from
there to Rajkot. Jayabehn will go straight on to Wardha with
someone.
Blessings from
BAPU
