523. LETTER TO GOSIBEHN CAPTAIN
SEVAGRAM,
March 26, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I was glad you stayed away for Manekji's sake. I do hope he
will pull through his illness and be up and doing. You need not
worry about coming to me in Bombay if you cannot. Just
write and I shall attend. I had not fainted. I was simply starved
for the moment.
Love to you both.
BAPU
GOSIBEHN CAPTAIN
122 MARZBAN BAD
ANDHERI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
