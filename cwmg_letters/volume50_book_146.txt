146. LETTER TO LILADHAR
July 2, 1932
DEAR LILADHAR,
The girls know about the privies. I had only played a joke.
Narandas had written about Shanti. Let her remain with you
for the present. Remove her stupidity with love. Do not be angry.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. 11, p. 63
