44. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
January 8, 1907
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I saw the Secretary of the Hamidia [Islamic Society] again
today in connection with its regulations. He lays the blame on
you, saying that the proofs were sent after two months! Is this
right? He has now promised to return the proofs and the report
on Friday.
Don't forget that you are to send me a list of simple books
in Hindi and Tamil.
Approach the men from whom no advertisements have been
received, and some others as well. Let me know what happens.
I am also sending some matter.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 4681
