435. LETTER TO H. V. HODSON
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 7, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. If I do come to London I hope
we shall meet and if I do not succeed in coming, you will please
believe me when I tell you that the failure will be due not to any
want of will or effort on my part but to circumstances wholly
beyond my control.
Yours sincerely,
H. V. HODSON, ESQ.
10 WOOD LANE
HIGHGATE, LONDON, N . 6
From a photostat: S.N. 17251
