258. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
July 16, 1941
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I will keep worrying about you. If you get the expected
benefit there, I will feel greatly relieved. Much will depend
on Rajkumari's selfless love.
But your firmness of mind also
will have a share. If you wish to make any change in diet or
anything else, please write or send a wire to me.
Madalasa has stayed on with Mirabehn today. Her as-
pirations are very high. If her health improves and if she de-
livers without any complication, I think she will make good.
Vinoba's training is bound to bear fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Khan Saheb's teeth have been removed.
