333. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
[NADIAD,]
July 5, 1918
I feel worried. You know our rule. One must not fall sick.
Self-restraint is all that is necessary to ensure that one does not.
Sufficient exercise and only as much food as one needs : if one
keeps in mind these two things, one will never suffer in health.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. IV
