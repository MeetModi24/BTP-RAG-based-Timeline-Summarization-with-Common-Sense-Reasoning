137. LETTER TO STANLEY REED
NADIAD,
April 1, 1918
DEAR MR. STANLEY REED,
I am anxious to enlist your active sympathy in the Kaira
trouble. I have not embarked upon it without due considera-
bitterness. Both will have gained in the end if none but the
fairest means be adopted by the parties to gain their respective
ends.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 10658. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
