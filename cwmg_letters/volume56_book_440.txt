440. LETTER TO LAKSHMI GANDHI
January 1, 1934
CHI. LAKSHMI,
Though we could not talk much, your three days' stay brought
me satisfaction. Your depression ought to disappear. I am en-
closing a copy of the letter received from Ba. Read it if you can
and send it on to Devdas.
Ask Papa to write to me. A letter for Anna accompanies
this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2003
