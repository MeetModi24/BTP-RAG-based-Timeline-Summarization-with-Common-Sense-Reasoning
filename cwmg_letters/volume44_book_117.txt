117. LETTER TO V. G. DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 11, 1930
BHAISHRI VALJI,
This letter is meant for the whole batch. Write to me and
tell me how many of you have been released and how everyone
is. What did you all do [while in jail]? How did you keep
there? Have you improved your health and become stronger?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Anyone who wishes to write to me may do so.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7405. Courtesy: V. G. Desai
