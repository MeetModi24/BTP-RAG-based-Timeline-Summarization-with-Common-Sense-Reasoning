438. LETTER TO HARSHADA D. DIWANJI
August 28, 1941
CHI. HARSHADA,
I have your letter and the ten-rupee note. The manner in
which you are observing the rules is excellent. There is not only
no need to send the yarn to me, but you need not send it to Kaku-
bhai either. You can certainly buy it. It will, therefore, suffice if
you send the amount which you think the yarn is worth.
Chi. Abhimanyu's skein is excellent. The yarn seems to be
fine and even.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9955
