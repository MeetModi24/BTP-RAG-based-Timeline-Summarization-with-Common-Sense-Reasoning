425. LETTER TO THE NETHERLANDS TRADING SOCIETY
November 17, 1944
DEAR SIRS,
With reference to your draft No. nil, dated 14-11-1944 for
£. 1,000 in my favour, I have to inform you that I have authorized
the Bank of Nagpur Ltd., Wardha, to collect the amount from
you.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
MESSRS THE NETHERLANDS TRADING SOCIETY
BOMBAY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
