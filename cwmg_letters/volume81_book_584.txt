584. LETTER TO ABUL KALAM AZAD
October 7, 1945
I am not happy to read in the newspapers about your ill
health. It is not good that you should get temperature so often.
Now the weather in the Punjab should be good. Perhaps the
weather in the Frontier Province would be better than in
Lahore. I think you need some rest.
You seem to have done good work in the Punjab.
MAULANA SAHEB ABUL KALAM AZAD
FALETTI'S HOTEL
LAHORE
