260. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
NEW DELHI,
May 25, 1946
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM, 3
I have your letter. I have already written everything. Do
everything you can for Roshan . It will certainly be better if
you go to settle in the countryside along with Hosa.
Again you are spoiling your health. This is not good. I
have written to Hosa5; I hope she got the letter.
Here the work is going on very slow. God alone knows
what will happen in the end.
Whatever you do, take it as though it were a command
from me.
To you all,
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi : G. N. 579
