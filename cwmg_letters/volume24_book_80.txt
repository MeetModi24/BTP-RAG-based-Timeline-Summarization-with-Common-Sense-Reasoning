80. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
[After May 29, 1924]
CHI. MANI,
Wasn't it nice of you to have come yesterday and gone away?
And now you send me a message! A patient has the right to
change her mind as often as she pleases; she cannot be bound
by any promises. So you are free not to come. And you are
also free to come if you feel like it. I only want you to get well
somehow or other.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MANIBEHN PATEL
KHAMASA CHOWKI
AHMEDABAD
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne, p. 20
