532. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
June 13, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
Ten or twelve persons sleeping in a small room can be
wrong from the point of view of brahmacharya, it can also be
proper. But it is bad from the point of view of health and
other things and it would be desirable to find a way out. Vina
must recover.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 9031. Also C.W. 9155. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
