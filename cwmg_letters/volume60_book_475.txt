475. LETTER TO BHUJANGILAL CHHAYA
March 28, 1935
CHI. BHUJANGILAL,
The reply to your second letter is pending.
There was
nothing needing a reply in the first letter.
Since you have time to spare, meet Narandas Gandhi and
ask him to give you some light social work.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI BHUJANGILAL CHHAYA
AT PORBUNDER STATE VAKIL'S
RAJKOT (C. S. KATHIAWAR)
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 24218
