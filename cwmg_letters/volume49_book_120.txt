120. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
February 10, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA,
You get five marks for your handwriting. You will get eight
if you leave space between the words and use a good pen. Why
does man have no root? A man carries his root wherever he
goes. A tree has its root under the ground because it comes out
of the ground. Man is born from his mother's body, and his
root also comes out with him at the time of his birth. One may
say that the navel is his root. If this is not clear to you, show
this letter to Premabehn and ask her to explain its meaning to
you.
Blessings from
BAPU
