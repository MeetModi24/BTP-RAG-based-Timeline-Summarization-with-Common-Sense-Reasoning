181. LETTER TO DR. M. S. KELKAR
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
October 30, 1933
DEAR DR. KELKAR,
Will you put down as briefly as you can the reasons for your
asking patients under milk treatment to drink as much water as
milk you prescribe for them? I hope your experiments are pros-
pering there.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 6108. Also C.W. 3301. Courtesy: M. S. Kelkar
