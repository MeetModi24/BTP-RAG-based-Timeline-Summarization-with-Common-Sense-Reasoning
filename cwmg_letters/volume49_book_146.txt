146. LETTER TO RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
February 16, 1932
DEAR RAMANANDA BABU,
I have duly received the Golden Book. What treasures of
love have you poured into it? I gave two hours to it straight-
way. Thank you for thinking of sending it to me here. Had I
got it outside, I would not have been able to go beyond open-
ing it and laying it down with a sigh.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: C.W. 9502. Courtesy: Santa Devi
