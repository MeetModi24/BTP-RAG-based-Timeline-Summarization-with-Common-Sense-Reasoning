442. LETTER TO MANUBEHN S. MASHRUWALA
SEVAGRAM,
February 16, 1941
CHI. MANUDI,
Let Ba get angry. But we must do our duty. So ultimately you
lost the purse containing eight rupees? Or did you find it after-
wards? There was absolutely no need to give the pen to Vina. She
handed it over to me. I have told her to keep it. Recover fully.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1578. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
