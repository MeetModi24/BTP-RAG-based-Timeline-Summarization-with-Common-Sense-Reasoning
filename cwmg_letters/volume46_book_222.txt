222. LETTER TO S. G. VAZE
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 21, 1931
MY DEAR VAZE,
You never failed to send me the usual anniversary notice and
I have ever failed to attend the function. The fact however that
I have never been able to attend the function, I am sure, will not
be interpreted by any of the members to mean that I have
ceased to be one of you. Though our views may be as poles
asunder, though we may not seem to be working together on the
same platform, I have always felt that we are at heart one, be-
ing disciples of the same guru.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. S. G. VAZE
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
POONA
From a photostat: S.N. 17111
