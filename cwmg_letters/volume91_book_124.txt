124. LETTER TO F. G. PRATT
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
February 12, 1918
DEAR MR. PRATT,
I am much obliged to you for your note which I received
this morning. As I am asking for an appointment I do not
wish to deal with the various matters referred to in your note
beyond saying that I have already taken precautions against any
departure from the strict instructions given regarding dairies and
that I shall gladly adopt your suggestion regarding public re-
pudiation in writing. Kindly send me an appointment.
M. K.
From a copy: C.W. 10627. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
