261. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
AHMEDABAD,
Wednesday [Before March 7, 1917]
CHI. MANILAL,
It was more painful for me to let you go than it was, perhaps,
for you to go. But I have often to make my heart harder than
steel, for I think that to be in your interest. It will be all to the
good if you should get into proper shape there. I want you to
learn to think for yourself and, when you find it necessary to resist
me, to do so with courage. Make yourself altogether a labourer.
That is, I believe, the way to our welfare.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 111. Courtesy:
Sushilabehn Gandhi
