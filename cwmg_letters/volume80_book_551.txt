551. LETTER TO LAKSHMI GANDHI
June 14, 1945
CHI. LAKSHMI,
You have been to Simla. I hope both of you have benefited
by the visit. It would have been better if you could have stayed
there longer. This much only because I must write.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
