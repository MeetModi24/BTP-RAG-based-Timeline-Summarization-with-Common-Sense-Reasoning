314. LETTER TO ANASUYA BAJAJ
March 19, 1936
CHI. GODAVARI 1 ,
Your letter. You do not tell me the time you get up.
Sugar is unnecessary and can be even harmful if taken in
large quantities. To chew sugar-cane when in season is prefera-
ble to sugar and so is jaggery.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9134
