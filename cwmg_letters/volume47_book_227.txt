227. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
BARDOLI,
July 25, 1931
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I have your letter. You had every right to write what you
did. Much of what you say is indeed true. I will do what I
can in the matter. Your letter is so carefully written that I am
sending it, like the earlier one, to Shri Fulchand. Though I find
him at present in an angry mood, I believe that he is a good
man at heart.
I am not quite convinced about the Jamnagar matter. Perhaps
I have not understood all the implications.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[Ps.]
On Tuesday at Borsad.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5918. Also C.W. 3233. Courtesy:
Mahesh Pattani
