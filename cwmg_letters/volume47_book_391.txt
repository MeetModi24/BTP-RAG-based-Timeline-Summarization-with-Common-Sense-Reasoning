391. LETTER TO R. S. HUKERIKAR
AHMEDABAD,
August 21, 1931
MY DEAR HUKERIKAR,
I have your letter enclosing copy of a confidential circular.
It is very valuable information. It is better for you to leave the
time of publication to me. You need not therefore say anything
about it just now.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. R. S. HUKERIKAR
KARNATAK PROVINCIAL CONGRESS COMMITTEE
DHARWAR
From a microfilm: S.N. 17557
