704. LETTER TO MAGAN AND MANJULA MEHTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
[After March 31, 1941]
CHI. MAGAN AND MANJULA,
I had got Manjula's letter. I gave her permission to come
here long ago. I will arrange for a separate kitchen for you
too. The house will be definitely arranged for you in April.
About Urmi, I will leave the decision to her. If she wishes to
go with you I will let her. She is definitely making progress.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 1037. Courtesy: Manjula Mehta
