326. LETTER TO RAMABEHN PATTANI
SABARMATI,
Magsar Sud 5 [December 1, 1924]
DEAR SISTER,
I have your affectionate letter. I shall be at the Conference
on 8th and 9th January. After that you may take me to some quiet
place for a few days. I will have to be back here on the 14th
at the latest. Everything will be arranged after I go there. I
have been persuaded to visit Kathiawar and preside over the Con-
ference in the hope that women like you may help me in the
propagation of khadi. If this wish of mine is fulfilled, that will
give me greater peace than the peace I can get in a quiet place.
I hope you are well.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
LADY RAMABAI PATTANI
BHAVNAGAR
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 3183. Courtesy: Mahesh Pattani
