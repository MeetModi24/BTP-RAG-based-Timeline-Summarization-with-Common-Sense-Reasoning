458. LETTER TO SURESH CHANDRA BANERJI
September 6, 1934
MY DEAR SURESH,
I have your letter. I was not able to reply to it earlier.
I had a chat with Birlaji. He is agreeable to your spending
from Rs. 2,000 to 3,000 on land and buildings, provided of
course that they remain his property. But you will be at liberty
to inhabit the house free of rent or any other charge so long as
your health needs such residence. He does not intend to make
private use of such property. He will give it for a charitable
purpose, most probably for a Harijan hostel. But that is a thing
with which neither you nor I are concerned. I think that then
you won't need Rs. 200, but only Rs. 60 per month. Observe
too that from October to December you will need not Rs. 200 but
ranging from Rs. 125 to 150. You will let me know in time
what you would require. And I hope that you will make steady
improvement. You must not encourage the thought that the
disease is incurable and that you have got to leave within a year
or two.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI SURESH CHANDRA BANERJI
11/ DILKUSHA STREET
PARK CIRCUS, CALCUTTA
