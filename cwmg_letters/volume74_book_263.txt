263. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 18, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
As far as possible I want to send you a daily line. But at
times it becomes physically impossible.
I have your wire about Jamnalal's safe arrival. I do hope
it will be all well.
1 Vide "Rentia Baras", p. 136.
2 Uncle of the Raja of Kalakankar
I am 99½, blood-pressure 160-94. It has a tendency to rise
at night. But I am quite well.
The rest from Mahadev.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4245. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7877
