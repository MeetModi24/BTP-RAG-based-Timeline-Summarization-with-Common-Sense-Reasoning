648. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
October 15, 1945
CHI. RAMESHWARI 1 ,
I have your letter. Your sister has passed away leaving be-
hind a large family. Your mother must be feeling it the most.
These days I read Bhartriharishatak for a few minutes daily.
What it says on moral wisdom and detachment is worth pon-
dering over at such times. Why grieve over the inevitable?
It is good indeed that the fast over the Garhwal [issue] was
withdrawn.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAMESHWARIBEHN NEHRU
SRINAGAR
KASHMIR
