81. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
WARDHA,
November 18, 1935
CHI. MRIDULA,
I wish that you would show yourself here every three
months.
I have written about Roshni.
As for staying on the only consideration should be what you
can do from there.
Blessings from
BAPU
