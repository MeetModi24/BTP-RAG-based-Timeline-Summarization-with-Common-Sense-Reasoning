365. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
August 17, 1938
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have learnt everything about the operation from Sushila.
After the remaining complaint is completely cured, there should
be no difficulty in building up the body. I hope you get milk
regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Pyarelal is not quite well today. There is no cause for
worry, though.
Tell Shankaran that I got his note. He wants a change.
Ask him about his food, etc. Ask him to write to me agam.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10588
