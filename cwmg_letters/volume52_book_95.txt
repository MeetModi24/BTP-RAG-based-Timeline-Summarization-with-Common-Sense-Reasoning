95. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
November 26, 1932
CHI. JAMNALALJI,
I have just been given the happy news of your having been
brought here. I hope you did not feel the strain of the journey.
Now that you are here, eat all the fruits which doctors recommend
for you. How is your cough? I am trying to secure permis-
sion to see you. We are all happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2905
