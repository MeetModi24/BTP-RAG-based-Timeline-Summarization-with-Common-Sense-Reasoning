412. LETTER TO JOSEPH BAPTISTA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
September 9, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 6th September. I do not think there
is sufficient sincerity in the atmosphere to warrant a day for pub-
lic prayer in connection with Hindu-Muslim tension. The prayer
must proceed from the heart. There must be a sincere desire for
reconciliation. I think the more becoming thing is for each one
of us to pray in his own closet.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
JOSEPH BAPTISTA, ESQ.
MATHAPPACADY
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 12380
