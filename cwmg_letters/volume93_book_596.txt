596. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
I send you a letter every day. My letter or Rajkumari's
letter or Pyarelal's letter will be sent today. Radha's was sent
to you yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
SV: III. 25
