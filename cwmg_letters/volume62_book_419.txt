419. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
May 2, 1936
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your letter from Madras. I hope to leave for Banga-
lore via Madras on 8th instant. I pass through on 9th evening. If
you can you will find me out at the station. Ba, Mahadev, the
same boys1, Kumarappa, will certainly be with me. I am going
to Nandi Hill for the sake of Sardar.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9606. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
