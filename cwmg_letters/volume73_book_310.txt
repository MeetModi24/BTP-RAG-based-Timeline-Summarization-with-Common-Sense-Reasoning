310. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
December 24, 1940
BHAI PRITHVI SINGH,
Do go to Bhavnagar. Everything will be all right at Poona.
I am writing to Nalini.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR PRITHVI SINGH
C/O SHRI VAISHAMPAYANJI
"VANDEMATARAM"
787 SADASHIV PETH
POONA CITY
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5644. Also C.W. 2955. Courtesy:
Prithvi Singh
