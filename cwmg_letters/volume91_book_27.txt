27. LETTER TO P. S. TO LT.-GOVERNOR, TRANSVAAL
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION,
P.O. BOX 6522,
JOHANNESBURG,
September 25, 1903
TO
THE PRIVATE SECRETARY
TO HIS EXCELLENCY THE LIEUTENANT-GOVERNOR
PRETORIA
SIR,
As the end of this year is drawing near and the position of
the British Indians is undefined, and as the existing licences held
by old bonafide residents of the Transvaal are in danger in view
of Notice 356 of this year, and as the working of that Notice has
already caused serious inconvenience to the community represent-
ed by my Association, I have been desired by my committee to
approach His Excellency with a view to inquire whether he will
be pleased to receive a small deputation and grant a hearing
38
on the general question in so far as immediate relief is
required.
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
ABDOOL GANI
CHAIRMAN, BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
