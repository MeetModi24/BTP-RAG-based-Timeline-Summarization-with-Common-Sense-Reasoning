28. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
SEVAGRAM,
April 26, 1940
CHI. HARIBHAU,
I have your letter. I am awaiting news from Ajmer. We
should be in a position to give a full reply to the Government's
note. I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Gandhiji aur Rajasthan, p. 252. Also C.W.
6089. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
