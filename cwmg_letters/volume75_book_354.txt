354. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
5.45 a.m., February 8, 1942
MY DEAR KU[MARAPPA],
I have decided to drink the cup to the full. I become Presi-
dent of the A. I. V. I. A.
I would like Jairamdas to come on the Executive if it is at all
possible.
You may occupy a column of Harijan every week.
