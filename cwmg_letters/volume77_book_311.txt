311. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
July 23, 1944
Do not get in the least impatient. Observe with detachment
whatever happens. . . . Doctors have examined me. There has
been good improvement. The climate has agreed with me. But
I do not wish to stay on much longer now. Nor is it necessary
to do so. I am pining to return to Sevagram.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, pp. 201-
414
