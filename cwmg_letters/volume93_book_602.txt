602. LETTER TO PYARELAL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 21, 1940
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have your letter sent from Nagpur. I am sending the
telegram today. I shall keep the notice ready. Do not worry.
We shall certainly meet. It is enough that you do not stay
longer than necessary and get back on Monday.
There is nothing particular for any copies to be
sent to
you. There was a telegram from A. S. She is happy. She
has also written a letter. Sushila must be calm.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
