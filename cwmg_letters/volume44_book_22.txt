22. LETTER TO WILFRED WELLOCK
YERAVDA CENTRAL PRISON,
July 11, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. From a prison cell it would not
be proper for me to enter into any argument. But you may de-
pend upon my not missing a single real opportunity for co-opera-
tion. But I confess that I see no sign as yet in the midst of fraud,
falsehood and force that appear to me to surround so many of the
acts of the powers that be.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Bombay Secret Abstracts, 750(34), p. 117
