98. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 6, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is nothing from you today. There is nothing in particular to
write from here. I am doing very well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
