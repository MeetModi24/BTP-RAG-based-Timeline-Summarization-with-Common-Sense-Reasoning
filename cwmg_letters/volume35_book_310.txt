310. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
CENTRAL STATION, MADRAS,
Silence Day [December 26, 1927]
BHAISHRI FULCHAND,
I got your letter. It is worthy of you. I had no doubt at
all, and did not worry about the thing.
I hope to reach the Ashram on Saturday at the latest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2832. Courtesy: Sharadabehn Shah
