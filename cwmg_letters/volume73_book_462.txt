462. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
February 25, 1941
CHI. VIJAYA,
You do not write. How then do you expect me to write?
Mahadevbhai will write to you about Manubhai. I am very glad
that Nanabhai reads the Bhagavata regularly. I will procure
the book about the marriage ceremony and send it to you. Shas-
triji had a copy of the very same book published by Kaka. Anna-
poorna has not yet come here. Shastriji is again suffering these
days. I am going to Allahabad tomorrow to open the Kamala
[Nehru] Hospital. We shall return on Sunday.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. VIJAYABEHN
DAKSHINAMURTI GRAMBHAVAN
AMBLA, via SONGARH, KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7137. Also C.W. 4629. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
