108. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
Silence Day [After May 29, 1931]
CHI. MANGALA,
I have your letter. Your handwriting is passably good. But
there are too many blotches. Do you remember Ramdas Swami's song
I sent you in this connection?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : C. W. 11103. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
