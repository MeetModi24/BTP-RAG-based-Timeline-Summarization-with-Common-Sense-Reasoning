176. LETTER TO SUNDERLAL
[November 22, 1941]
BHAI SUNDERLAL,
Only today could I reach your telegram of the 18th. So I am
not replying by wire. May what you have begun meet with
brilliant success.
Blessings from
BAPU
PANDIT SUNDERLALJI
C/O DR. DAVAR
NEW DELHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 10263. Courtesy: Purushottam Prasad
