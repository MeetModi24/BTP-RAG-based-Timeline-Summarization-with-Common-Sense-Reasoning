102. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
August 1, 1945
CHI. BRAJKRISHNA,
I have your letter. It will be better if you do not come at
present. You can ask me when I go to Poona. There are so
many people eager to serve me that I do not think you will get
a chance. I cannot write more now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2490
