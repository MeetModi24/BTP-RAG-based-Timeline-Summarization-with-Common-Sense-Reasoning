394. LETTER TO BHUPENDRA NATH SENGUPTA
POONA,
September 5, 1945
MY DEAR BHUPEN,
I have your letter. I must not tax myself to give you a
detailed answer. You must revert to it when we meet or remind
me later, if time fails to solve the puzzles. When I go to Bengal
I won't have a large party with me. Who will be there, I do
not know.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI B. N. SENGUPTA
99/ BALLYGUNJE PLACE
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 10066
