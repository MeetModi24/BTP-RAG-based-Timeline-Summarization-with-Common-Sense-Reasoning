514. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 24, 1937
BHAI MUNSHI,
I got your letter. How can you expect any mercy from
me? I can't approve of your princely salaries and additional
princely house-rent allowances and conveyance allowances. More-
over, you will draw your salary at one rate and your secretary
at another and lower rate, though both of you are guests of the
same family. Why such distinctions under the Congress flag?
When Vijayaraghavachari was President, Motilalji was secretary.
If we were paying salaries, would we have paid less to Motilalji?
To me this is like a fly in the very first morsel.
Take care of your health. Don't let there be unpleasantness
among yourselves. Overcome co-workers' anger with forbearance.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7618. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
