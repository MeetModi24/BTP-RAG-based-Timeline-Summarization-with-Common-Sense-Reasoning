730. LETTER TO RANI VIDYAVATI
December 26, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
Your letter has been lying with me for so long, but how
to reply? I have to attend to heavy correspondence during
travel. There is no other remedy to improve your health.
The work of Harijan service is not difficult for you. Try
to improve the conditions of the Harijans right where you
are living.
Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy : Gandhi
National Museum and Library
