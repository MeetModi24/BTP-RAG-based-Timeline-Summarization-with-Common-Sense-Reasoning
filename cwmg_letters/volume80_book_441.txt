441. LETTER TO PREMLILA THACKERSEY
June 6, 1945
CHI. PREMLILABEHN,
I got your letter.
Is it I who should thank you for looking after me and my
large family or is it you who should thank me? You have always
showered love on me, but this time you surpassed yourself. You
stayed all these days in Mahabaleshwar along with your own peo-
ple and let us suffer no inconvenience, and on top of that gave
us money when bidding us farewell. I think it would be improper
for me to offer you thanks for so much love. Silence is the best
course.
We are all right here. Shantabehn has put me in a prison.
I have left one jail and got into another.
My blessings to Vashi and the girls, which includes Damayanti.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 80. Also C.W. 4835. Cour-
tesy : Premlila Thackersey
