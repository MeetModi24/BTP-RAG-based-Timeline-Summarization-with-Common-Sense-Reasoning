366. LETTER TO TEJ BAHADUR SAPRU
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 14, 1945
DEAR SIR TEJ,
I was going to write to you in Hindustani but for this letter at
least I restrain myself. For the future I shall do as you will bid.
I had thought that Dr. Jayakar and Kumar Saheb had come to
me on your behalf and at your instance. Rajaji told me otherwise. Hence
this note, though belated.
I considered the whole of your report as sketched to me to be
an able document. But I did not interest myself in the whole of it.
My interest was naturally confined to the communal question and it
was wholly satisfactory. I hope you do not want me to express any
opinion on the rest.
You have, and that at your age, laboured at the report and laboured
with your colleagues as few men would have done. Rajaji has told
me much about this part of your valuable work. And in Santanam you
have an able and painstaking secretary.
Hope you are keeping well and that your ailing son has recovered
completely.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
THE RT. HON. SIR TEJ BAHADUR SAPRU
