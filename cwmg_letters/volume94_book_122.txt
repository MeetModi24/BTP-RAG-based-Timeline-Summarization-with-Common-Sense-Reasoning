122. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 28, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
My blood-pressure continues to remain high. Hence, I took three
drops of sarpagandha yesterday morning and evening. This morning
it came down to 166/98. There is no cause for worry. In the morning
I talked while walking and when I took the blood-pressure it was
196/112. When it was taken in the afternoon, it was 150/90. My food
intake is good.
Sardar is quite well. Vasumati's breasts are very tender. The
slightest pressure causes her pain. Let me know if you understand why
this is so.
There is no letter from you even today. I take it that this is
because you are very busy.
Blessings from
BAPU
