75. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 20, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I cannot write at length today. There is a pile ofletters. The visitors
are of course there. The widow of our late colleague Fulchandbhai
has come. There are others too. Rajen Babu has also come. The patients
are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
