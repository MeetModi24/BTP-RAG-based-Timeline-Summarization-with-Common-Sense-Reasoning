194. LETTER TO JEHANGIR PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
August 17, 1946
BHAI JEHANGIR,
I have your English letter. Do you have my letters read
out to you? If you could tell me whether you read my letters
yourself and can follow them fully or have to have someone
explain them to you, I could do something about it. If
Ramprasad's case is likely to take long I shall have to think
about the matter a little. I thought you needed a man urgently,
and that obtaining sanction was only a formality. But what
you say is right. Consider it only after having consulted your
association. It would be better if you could tell me how much
time it will take. But this you ought to bear in mind that
Ramprasad is not unoccupied here. All I wish to convey is
that I can spare him without inconvenience to me.
You will find in Harijan the answer to the question you
have raised regarding Ramanama. Nevertheless you will please
ask me again if you do not understand it or are not satisfied
with it. I think it is another matter whether you put it into
practice or not. However, as a trustee you ought to have a
correct understanding of it. And if I am making a mistake
you should correct me. I note that Dinshaw has been seeing you.
Write to me if you have anything to say regarding the views
I have expressed.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. JEHANGIR PATEL
PATEL HOUSE
10 CHURCHGATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
