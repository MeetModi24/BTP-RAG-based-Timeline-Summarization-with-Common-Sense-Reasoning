182. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
Tuesday [February 9, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Your letter. We shall decide about Manibehn when you come
here.
Surely, my weight has gone up a little. I hope it will go up
more this week. There is no cause for anxiety.
I have been receiving the oranges sent by you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2857
