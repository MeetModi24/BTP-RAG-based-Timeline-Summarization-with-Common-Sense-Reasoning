339. LETTER TO OM PRAKASH
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR,
ON THE TRAIN,
December 18, 1945
CHI. OM PRAKASH,
I have your letter. If you have to go to your brother and
do the work there, you should spare some time and come back
after finishing the work. Take care that you do not devote too
much time to it.
It is necessary to have a good knowledge of Urdu but why
should you crave for a degree?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
