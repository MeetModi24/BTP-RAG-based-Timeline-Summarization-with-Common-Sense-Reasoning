463. LETTER TO DUDABHAI DAFDA
HARIPURA,
February 15, 1938
BHAI DUDABHAI,
I got your letter just now. It is natural that you should
grieve over Dani's death. But let us remember that everybody
passes away only at the appointed time and that all of us have
to go sooner or later. This should console us. It is indeed nice
that Dani passed away with Ramanama on her lips. That you
did not observe the superstitious customs does you great honour.
If you can remain pure, do not marry again. God has blessed
you with enough children.
I hope Lakshmi does not grieve too much.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3248
