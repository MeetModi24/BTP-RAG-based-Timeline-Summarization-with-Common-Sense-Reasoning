296. LETTER TO PADMA
BARDOLI,
May 29, 1931
CHI. PADMA,
I met your father in Nainital. Do you write to him? How
is your health now? Write to me and give me all the news
about yourself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6119. Also C.W.3471. Courtesy:
Prabhudas Gandhi
