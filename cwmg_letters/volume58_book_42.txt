42. LETTER TO CHARU PROBHA SEN GUPTA
May 30, 1934
MY DEAR CHARU PROBHA,
I have your letter. Do by all means join me at any stage
you like. You know my programme, don't you? On 6th I reach
Bhadrak.
Love.
BAPU
