705. LETTER TO RANI VIDYAVATI
WARDHA,
October 23, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
Have you not received my letters at all? There is no
mention of them in your letter which I received today.
Why did you go to Hardoi in spite of the pain? One can
be attached even to service. You can render true service only
by giving up attachment altogether. Can the cripple not worship?
One can also serve through one's mind. Take a couple of
Harijan girls into your house and bring them up.
Lakshmi has not been writing at all. How is that? Write to
her that I shall be satisfied if I have a postcard from her. Do
you receive Harijan Sevak?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers: Courtesy: Gandhi
