250. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
SEVAGRAM,
August 7, 1944
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I was wondering why I had not received the customary rakhadi
from you. Well, I got it today. Has Shankerlal also returned ? What
are your plans ?
Blessings to all of you. I am all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANASUYABEHN
MARINE DRIVE
BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original : S. N. 32769
1 Addressee's daughter
