242. LETTER TO D. N. SARMAH
February 7, 1935
MY DEAR SARMAH,
I have your letters enclosing opinions on the Modino's treat-
ment. I shall make such use as is possible of these opinions.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI D. N. SARMAH
SECRETARY
HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH, JORAHAT (AssAM)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
