401. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
CHANDIPUR,
Saturday, January 4, 1947
What you say is correct, but I have got so deeply involved
in this problem that I cannot say when I shall be able to
come out of it. Kanchan should take proper care of her health.
Never mind what expense has to be incurred for that purpose.
It has got to be incurred. You may draw it from Kanchan's
account. We shall adjust all that afterwards. In the last resort
there is the Ashram. Do not, therefore, worry on that account.
Keep me informed about Kanchan's health.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 5619. Courtesy: Munnalal
G. Shah
