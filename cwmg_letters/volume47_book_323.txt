323. LETTER TO MATHEW KOHOSOFF
AHMEDABAD,
August 15, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for the gift of Midstream by Helen Keller. Though
I have not been able to go through the whole of the volume a
glance through its pages shows that it is a remarkable story.
Yours sincerely,
MATHEW KOHOSOFF, ESQ.
574 WEST 192D STREET
NEW YORK (U.S.A.)
From a photostat: S.N. 17471
