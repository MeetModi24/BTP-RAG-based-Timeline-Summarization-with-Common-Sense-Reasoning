403. LETTER TO THE MERCANTILE BANK OF INDIA, LTD.
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD,
POONA,
October 8, 1945
MESSRS. THE MERCANTILE BANK
OF INDIA, LTD.
P.O. BOX 128, BOMBAY
Ref : Your letter No. Acctt, Mis. M/158 dt. 13-10-'45
DEAR SIRS,
With reference to your above-mentioned letter enclosing a receipt
in duplicate for Rs. 5305-11-2, I have to inform you that I have
authorised Messrs. Bachhraj & Co. Ltd., Bombay, to collect the amount
from you.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
