107. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 22, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have your letter. I could not reply by today's post. There is
no need to send you a wire. Ask Kanchan to write to me about the
matter and ask Balkrishna and Kunvarji also to write. I cannot also
understand why Bhojubhau and Kanchan are not on speaking
terms. Is Balkrishna's work suffering in any way because of this
tiff? He had some such fear, and if it is so they should let him alone.
I can give my decision about you only after taking into account all
these factors.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8539. Also C.W. 7090. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
