489. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
SABARMATI,
May 24, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your second letter. Thank you. Devdas is quite all
right. He had his father's fashionable disease—appendicitis. He
expects to be discharged in a few days.
Do please give me your suggestions for a solution of the
H.M. problem. I must own I have despaired for the time
being. Hakimji never came here.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Nothing of importance at Mahabaleshwar. Spinning-wheel
of course.
From the original: C. Vijayaraghavachariar Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
