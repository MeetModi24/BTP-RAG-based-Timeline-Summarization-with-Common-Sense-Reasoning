69. LETTER TO MADALASA
October 25, 1941
CHI. MADALASA,
Having conveyed my message through Radhakrishna1, this is
not necessary. I am writing this merely for your entertainment.
Shall I send some more papad ?
Why do you cry? Do you know that even your crying has an
effect on the baby?
When are you coming over here?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 322
