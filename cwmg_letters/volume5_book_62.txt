62. LETTER TO ABDUL REHMAN
[ JOHANNESBURG,]
August 16, 1905
TO
MR. ABDUL REHMAN
P. O. Box 12
POTCHEFSTROOM
DEAR SIR,
I have to thank you very much for assisting Mr. Kalyandas in con-
nection with the subscriptions for Indian Opinion. You mentioned to me
about insurance of stock in Potchefstroom. There is a Company who
would insure such stock provided the buildings are nice and suitable, I
think at £7-. If there are any who want their stock to be insured, please
let me know.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
Letter Book (1905): No. 981
