186. LETTER TO G. V. GURJALE
January 25, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your very long letter. I am afraid that I cannot guide
you definitely. There is much to be said in favour of your
method and as much to be said in favour of the method of living
in the midst of the Harijans. Therefore you alone can be the final
judge. You should serve in the manner that appeals most to you,
after consultation with those who know you well and know the
local circumstances.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. G. V. GURJALE
KRIPA ASRAM
TIRUVENNAINALLUR (S. INDIA)
From a photostat: G.N. 1398. Also S.N. 20092
