145. LETTER TO KANHAIYALAL
September 19, 1932
A poor man is one who has not a drop of true love. And
a rich man is one who has enough love to encompass an insect
as well as a mad elephant. The atheist does not recognize the
all-pervasive spirit present beyond his own physical self. The be-
liever sees everywhere nothing but the spirit.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I I , p. 54
