31. LETTER TO L. B. NAYAK
January 12, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Attractive as your suggestion may appear
to be, it savours of dishonesty, if I have understood it correctly,
and it also seems to be impracticable in the existing circumstances.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. L. B. NAYAK
341 THAKURDWAR
BOMBAY No. 2
From a microfilm: S.N. 19233
