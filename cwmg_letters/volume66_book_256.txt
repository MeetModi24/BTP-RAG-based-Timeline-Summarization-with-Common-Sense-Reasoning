256. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 8, 1937
CHI. SHARMA,
I am watching your experiments with interest and I wish
you success. It seems almost impossible that I may be able to
witness them personally but God makes even the impossible
possible.
You have given me no facts regarding the Khurja Con-
gress; what can I do in these circumstances?
If you send
me the facts with the name and address I shall certainly for-
ward the letter to the right quarters.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 268
