48. LETTER TO SHAH MOHAMMED QASIM
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 11, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
With further reference to your letter of 9th ultimo, I en-
close herewith a copy of a letter received from the Jodhpur
Darbar in reply to my enquiry.
Yours sincerely,
Enclosure: 2 sheets
SHAH MOHAMMED QASIM
C/O SYED MOHAMMED HUSSAIN
P.O. NURHUT, GAYA
