18. LETTER TO VIDYAGAURI R. NILKANTH
AHMEDABAD,
Bhadarva Sud 6 [September 15, 1915]
DEAR SISTER,
I am grateful to you as well as to Shri Ramanbhai for the
translation you have sent. What I require, however, is the meaning
of the bhajan. I want to have its spirit conveyed in our language.
We do not have an expression corresponding to ~ Ii'litftt
('Kindly Light'). ~ Q4[ct[;:{) 3ITm 3 does not convey the same sense as
in 'one step enough for me'. It should not be difficult for us to find
from among our literary works an expression parallel to Newman's
thought.
Do not charge me while giving an inch with asking for an ell.
Please do not take what I have said as criticism.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS KARAM CHAND
[From Gujarati]
Vidyabehn Nilkanth Jeevan Jhanki
