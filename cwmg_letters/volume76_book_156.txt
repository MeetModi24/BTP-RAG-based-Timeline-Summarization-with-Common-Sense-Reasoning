156. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEVAGRAM,
May 21, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I seem to recollect that I had answered your previous letter.
The present one is easy to answer. Your wishes should prevail
in the work that has been entrusted to you.
But in a non-violent constitution law as law vanishes and
we never feel its stress. So when someone violates a law we are
charitable towards him. Have you understood this?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4426; also S.N. 24480
