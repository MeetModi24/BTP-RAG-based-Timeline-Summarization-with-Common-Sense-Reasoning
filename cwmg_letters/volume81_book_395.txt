395. LETTER TO ANNE MARIE PETERSEN
September 5, 1945
MY DEAR MARIA,
I have got all your papers now. I see that it is nobody's
fault at the Centre. Anyway I am on the track now.
Love.
SMT. MISS MARIA PETERSEN
SEVA MANDIR, PORTO NOVO
SOUTH INDIA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
