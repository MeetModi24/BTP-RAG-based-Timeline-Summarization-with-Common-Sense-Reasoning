17. LETTER TO B. G. HORNIMAN
[NADIAD,]
August 12, 1918
I have your peremptory letter. Though it was written on the
8th, it was received yesterday. I suppose we have to put up with
these vagaries of the Postal Department during this time of stress.
Really I am recruiting mad. I do nothing else, think of nothing
else, talk of nothing else and therefore feel ill-fitted to discharge
any presidential function, save one on recruiting. Will you not
therefore excuse me?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
