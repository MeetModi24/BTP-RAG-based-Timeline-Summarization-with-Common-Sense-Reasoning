236. LETTER TO HORACE G. ALEXANDER
September 30, 1932
MY DEAR HORACE,
I am expending my energy today in sending love letters to
friends in England. I have written so much already that I am
not going to say anything to you beyond sending my love to you,
Olive and all the brothers and sisters of the Settlement. During
the fast the English friends were ever near my heart.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1414
