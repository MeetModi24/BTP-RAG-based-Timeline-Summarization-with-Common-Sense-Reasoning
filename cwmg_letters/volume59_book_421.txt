421. LETTER TO H. P. MODY
November 18, 1934
DEAR MR. MODY,
Here is another appeal for help which only mills can render.
This time it is from afflicted Assam. The enclosed speaks for itself.
You will find a summary of the contents attached to the full
statement from Assam. I do hope that blankets will be sent on
behalf of the Association.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
H. P. MODY, ESQ.
MILL-OWNERS' ASSOCIATION
BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
