633. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
September 25, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
Please find enclosed a letter for Jamnadas. I am also writing
one to Kusum. Your purse is being filled up fairly well. Some
work is being done here under Amtussalaam's inspiration. One
charkha will be kept working for sixteen hours daily till the 2nd.
Yesterday, it was kept working 24 hours just by chance. Kanti
started spinning late in the evening and continued till 4.45 a.m.
He rose at the time of morning prayer, because only he and
Mahadev have all the eighteen chapters by heart. Kanaiyo cannot
but absorb himself in such work and Navin also has done the
same. It looks as if they really like it all. Does Mathew take
part in any of the activities?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8486. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
