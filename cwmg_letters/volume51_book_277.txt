277. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
October 2, 1932
CHI. PANDITJI,
You should not expect a long letter from me today. I feel
that I have had a new life. God's will be done. Lakshmibehn
should take care of her teeth and have them fully attended to.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 235. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
