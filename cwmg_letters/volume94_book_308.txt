308. LETTER TO DIWANCHAND OBHERAI
SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA, C. P.,
November 10, 1944
DEAR OBHERAIJI,
I read out your letter of the 28th to Gandhiji.
He has decided nothing about the fast yet but has simply put himself
in God's hands. He won't undertake the fast unless he has a clear cal!. He
cannot rule out fasting altogether as he holds it to be the ultimate and most
potent weapon in the armoury of non-violence.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI DIWANCHAND OBHERAI
SENIOR ADVOCATE
PESHAWAR
