133. LETTER TO VIJAYA M. PANCHOLI
November 9, 1941
CHI. VIJAYA,
I have your letter. You are greedy. But I will see what I can
send you. Take care of your health. Today I have sent Vasumati-
behn to Bombay for treatment. We have a large number of guests
just now—Khurshedbehn, Sultana1, Gosibehn and so on. They
will leave after two or three days.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. VIJAYABEHN
GRAM DAKSHINAMURTI
AMBLA, via SONGARH
KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7142. Also C.W. 4634. Cour-
tesy: Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
