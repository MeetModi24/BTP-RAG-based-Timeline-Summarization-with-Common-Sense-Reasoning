65. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN M. ASAR
Sunday [July-October, 1926]
CHI. MOTIBEHN,
I was very glad to receive your letter. Start the study of
Gujarati. The handwriting is good of course.
You must get rid of slow fever. It is necessary to have hi-bath
for that purpose. It will be good if you remain on milk and fruits for
a while. Raw vegetables can be regarded as fruits. You must chew
them well or make them into pulp. You have got to cure constipation.
Take enema if you do not pass stool. In the morning as soon as you
get up, you should drink hot water as soon as you have cleaned your
teeth. Many people have benefited from it. Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : G. N. 3762
