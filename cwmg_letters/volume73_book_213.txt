213. LETTER TO SYED MAHMUD
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 22, 1940
MY DEAR MAHMUD,
Your letter. Don't think of civil disobedience. The first thing
is to get well. You must have a radical cure. Shaukat showed me
a scheme which bore your name. But your denial is quite enough
for me. He showed me also copy of the draft I gave you. Brelvi
mentioned the fast and so your name was connected with it as you
were with him. But why worry about these things?
Love.
BAPU
DR. SYED MAHMUD, M.L.A.
CHHAPRA
BIHAR
From a photostat: G.N. 5081
