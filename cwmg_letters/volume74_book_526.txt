526. LETTER TO HARILAL M. RANGOONWALA
September 15, 1941
BHAI HARILAL,
I had your letter written from jail. I have already replied
to it. I have now the letter written after your release. I
am glad that you are not worrying about the boycott In
Rangoon. Let it go on for the present. You may go to jail again
when the Bombay office invites you to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 143
