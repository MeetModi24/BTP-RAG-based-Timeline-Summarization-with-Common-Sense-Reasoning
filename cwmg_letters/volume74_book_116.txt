116. LETTER TO ANNADA BABU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 24, 1941
DEAR ANNADA,
I have your letter. My sending Mahadev there would be
of no use. He can do no effective work there. He cannot se-
cure the co-operation of all Congressmen. So far as the reports
are concerned I have ample material to judge for myself the
extent of the damage done. How to combat the disease is for
Congressmen to find. If you can tackle that proposition it will
be worth while.
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
