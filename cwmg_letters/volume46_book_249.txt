249. LETTER TO WALTER B. FOLEr
As AT SABARMATI,
May 24, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the two articles.
I would indeed be delighted to meet you if it is at all con-
venient for you to go to Surat or Anand. Generally I am to be
found between Borsad near Anand and Bardoli near Surat. Just
at present I am on my way to Bardoli. I received your letter
while waiting for the Surat train at Muttra Junction. I am not
likely to be anywhere between Lahore and Calcutta in the course
of next month.
Yours sincerely,
WALTER B . FOLEY, ESQ.
3 MIDDLETON STREET
CALCUTTA
