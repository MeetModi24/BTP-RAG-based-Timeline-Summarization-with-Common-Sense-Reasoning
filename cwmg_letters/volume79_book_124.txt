124. LETTER TO SHEELA
SEVAGRAM,
January 26, 1945
CHI. SHEELA,
I have your postcard. Your description is good. Today is
Independence Day, do you remember? Do you write Urdu? If
not, learn it. You will not have letters from me for some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHEELA
C/O NIRMAL SWARUP
PREMPURI, RAILWAY ROAD
MEERUT, U. P.
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
