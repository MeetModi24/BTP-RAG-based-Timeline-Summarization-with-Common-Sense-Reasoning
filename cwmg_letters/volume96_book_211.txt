211. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
AHMEDABAD,
June 4 [1915]
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I feel like crying out to you 'Do come and help me!' Mrs. Gandhi
is again down with her swellings. She has lost all power of resistance.
She weeps like a child, is ever angry with me as if I was the party
responsible for her swellings. I am over head and ears in work. This
institution costs me much trouble. I wish I had the time to describe
to you the troubles I am passing through. I am not dejected but I feel
lonely. You know what I mean. Heaven knows what will happen. There
are so many sick people on the Farm. I want hours of solitude and
have not a minute of it. Do 'buck up' and prepare for the struggle
of the spirit when you are able to come here.
I know nothing about some honour that has been conferred upon
me. I have just received a letter of congratulations. More in my next.
With love,
OLD FRIEND
