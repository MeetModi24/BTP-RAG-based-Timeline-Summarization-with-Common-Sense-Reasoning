462. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH AZAD
September 17, 1945
BHAI PRITHVI SINGH,
I have read your letter from beginning to end. Even though
the signature is yours the language is not yours. Nor is the hand-
writing yours. I have already written to Joshiji I cannot go
into the facts. I shall do as God prompts me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
You say you have showed the letter to Nathji . Let him
write to Kishorelalbhai if he can testify to the facts. He would
probably investigate and if Nathji wants it, I feel he would
certainly do it.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 5655. Also C.W. 2966. Courtesy:
Prithvi Singh Azad
