658. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Tuesday, June 8, 1926
BHAI SHANTIKUMAR,
I have read your letter to Mahadev. When you sent me
mangoes on your birthday, you should have asked for blessings,
at any rate, though as a matter of fact you have them always.
Do tell me why you have given up mangoes for the last seven
years. May God grant you a long life.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 4702. Courtesy: Shantikumar Morarji
