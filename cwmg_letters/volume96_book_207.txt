207. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
April 23 [1915]
MY DEAR FRIEND,
In the hope of writing to you at length I deferred writing to you
earlier and now I find I would have almost missed the mail. But I
suddenly woke [up] with a fright that I had not yet written to you.
Here am I, therefore, writing this in the small hours of the morning
by candle light. Your diary still remains interesting for what it omits.
My dear friend, I know you want to assist me, you could do so even
now if you were here. But you could do so from there by realizing
our common ideals. You have no notion of how from that distance
even you can affect the lives of men here. The powers of a soul purified
are illimitable. May you be able to carry out your resolves. More later.
With love,
Yours,
OLD FRIEND
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
1 & 2 Vide Vol. XIII, pp. 63 and 164, and An Autobiography, Part V, Ch. VII,
p. 310.
