23. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
MADRAS,
January 24, 1946
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Two of your letters are lying with me. I am writing this
during a few moments of free time. Do not worry in the least
about the samadhi . Go on doing only the task in hand. Nothing
is done there (in the Spinners' Association) without my permission.
Do what is possible regarding the clinic . Now I will be
there in a few days.
Kanchan is with me. Her cough has not disappeared. But
otherwise she is all right. She is quite cheerful. According to me
she is being looked after better than a queen. I have not asked
her whether she would agree.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8609. Also C. W. 7209. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
