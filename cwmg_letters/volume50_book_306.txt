306. LETTER TO NIRMALA B. MASHRUWALA
July 31, 1932
CHI. NIRMALA, (TOPIWALA BUNGALOW)
I was waiting for your letter. I received the proofs of Gita-
manthan [just now], but I have not received your previous letter
till the moment of writing. There was a problem about letters
written to me, which had created some confusion. Things have
now settled down somewhat.
It was very good that you wrote to me. You have many
things to write about. I would certainly be happy if you give me
news about everyone.
I had received Babukaka's letter, and I had also written a
long letter in reply. He must have received it now. Give my
blessings to Gomati. All three of us are well. Blessings to every-
body there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2885. Courtesy: Nirmalabehn
Shroff
