305. LETTER TO VIJAYALAKSHMI PANDIT
October 28, 1944
CHI. SARUP,
I got your letter. I again went through my article. Personally
I was satisfied with it. But if you send me a sample I shall know
243
what you want. This is what I had asked Jawaharlal to do.
You might have heard about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
2 MUKHERJI ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
