196. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
Monday, November 21 [1927]
SISTERS,
I have as yet received no letter from you. I have to wander
about so much in Ceylon that it is difficult to get my mail direct
from Colombo.
When I look at the Ceylonese women, I think of our Ashram
women. I wrote to you upon the simplicity of the dress of ordi-
nary women. On the other hand, women of higher social standing
have grown so fashionable that they put on nothing but silk and
brocades. In my eyes, it does not suit them at all. I always ask
myself, "Whom do these women want to please by putting on
such clothes?" There is no purdah system here. Why women
adorn themselves, you can tell better than I. But seeing all this, I
felt that it was good that we had established the tradition in the
Ashram of wearing the fewest possible ornaments. I cannot of
course say that in the Ashram we put on no ornaments at all.
Write and tell me if you don't agree.
Blessings from
BAPU
