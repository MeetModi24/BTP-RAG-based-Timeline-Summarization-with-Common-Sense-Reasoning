178. LETTER TO FULCHAND SHAH
October 12, 1925
BHAISHRI FULCHAND,
I have your letter. I am writing to Chi. Chhaganlal about
the Rs. 1,000. Tell Devchandbhai to ask me when I am in Kutch
about the meeting of the Parishad Committee.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Is your mother all right?
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2871. Courtesy: Fulchand Shah
