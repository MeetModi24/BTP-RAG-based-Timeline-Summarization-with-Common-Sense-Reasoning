116. LETTER TO KASHI GANDHI
Monday [Before April, 1929]
CHI. KASHI,
I received your letter. Do not in the least worry about Prabhudas. I have
written in detail to Kaka about him. If it is necessary to send him to Almora,
I will do so. I have already settled the matter with Hargovind Pant. I am
hopeful that hip-baths will set him right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S. N. 33076
