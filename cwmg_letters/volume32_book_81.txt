81. LETTER TO REVASHANKER J. MEHTA
Thursday [November 25, 1926]
RESPECTED REVASHANKERBHAI,
Received your letter. The matter about the [Ramachandran]
water-lift is clear to me. I feel that we ought to keep an eye
on Dhiru's diet.
I shall leave this place for Wardha on the 2nd. I have chanced
upon the postcard Chi. Ratilal wrote to a diamond merchant
asking for diamonds. He has not much judgment. He admitted
[having done that] when I spoke to him. Both have returned
from Dhoraji.
Pranams from
MOHANDAS
SHRIYUT REVASHANKER JAGJIVAN MEHTA
ROSEDALE
DEOLALI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1265
