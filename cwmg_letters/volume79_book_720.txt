720. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 23, 1945
CHI. SHRIMAN NARAYAN,
Enclosed is a letter from Dr. Tara Chand. Read it and send
me your opinion.
I feel that the expenditure is too much on the Western style.
If it is to be done in Wardha we have provision for every-
thing. The printing can be done by the Navajivan Press itself.
I have no authority to do anything on my own. Don't we
have to place the matter before our executive?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 305
