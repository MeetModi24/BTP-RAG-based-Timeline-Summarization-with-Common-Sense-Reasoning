208. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
POONA,
February 26, 1946
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have your letter. What are you going to do about Rajaji ?
He wants to withdraw1; let him do so.
If you are keen on taking me to Bardoli, I am ready. I
have already suggested that in a difficult situation like this
your place is in Bombay. But you know best. In making the
suggestion I did not have my convenience in view, but only what
the circumstances of the nation demanded of us.
You will see what I have again said about Aruna.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, pp. 300-
