257. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 7, 1936
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I read both the letters. Other things later. Parnerkar has
not come to me so far.
The interview
was all right but I see nothing hopeful
in it; he will not be able to do a thing. Their policy and
ours are poles apart. I firmly hold that no further overtures
should be made to him. It is not correct to say that I had made
any promise. Whatever I did was all worth doing. This is the
reason why it was done and not because of any promise of mine.
It was not in the public interest to proceed any further. I write
all this in order to make things clear for the future.
What can I do in connection with the elections? Yes, I can
try to avoid friction within the Congress; in fact I am already
engaged in this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 8021. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
