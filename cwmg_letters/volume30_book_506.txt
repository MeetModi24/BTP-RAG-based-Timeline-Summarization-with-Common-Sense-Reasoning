506. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 9, 1926
DEAR MOTIBABU,
I hope you received my letter with reference to the loan.
Please make the following corrections in the agreement to be signed
by you:
The interest should not be one per cent per annum but one
per thousand per annum.
The interest is intended to be purely nominal. And, where
the following sentence occurs —"the selling price of khadi . . .
up to weaving plus 6¼% for the expenses of management"—please
say "the sale price of khaddar shall not be above the rates fixed
by the Association from time to time".
This improvement is intended to make the position of the
khadi organizations more elastic than it otherwise would be.
These are the conditions embodied in the loan agreements. When
I prepared the draft for you Mr. Banker was away and I had
not the Association model before me. On his return he drew my
attention to these discrepancies. Kindly excuse me.
Yours sincerely,
