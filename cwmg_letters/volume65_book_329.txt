329. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 13, 1937
CHI. MIRA,
I hope you had no difficulty in reaching Dalhousie and that
you were met at the respective stations. How I wish you would
be thoroughly restored and return with the body thoroughly re-
newed. You won't overstrain yourself in your anxiety to get
well soon.
No more today.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6380. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9846
