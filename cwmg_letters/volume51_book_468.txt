468. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
October 22, 1932
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
Your letter and the report. The report is good. It is a
matter of regret that not much khadi was sold during the Puja
season. Consult Ghanshyamdasji about this and see if he can
do something. Arun must have got my letter. All this touring
should not affect your health. How are Satisbabu and Kshitish-
babu?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1691
