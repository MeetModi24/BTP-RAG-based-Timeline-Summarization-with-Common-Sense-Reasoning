599. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
Sunday [On or before September 11, 1927]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I have your letter. This time your handwriting may be said
to have improved a little. Good handwriting and correct spell-
ing are not important in themselves. But if they are not impor-
tant, they also do not require much time to improve. Try, there-
fore, to improve them both.
I understand about Damodardas. Now you don't have to do
anything in the matter. You should now devote yourself wholly
to your studies and be satisfied.
My health is improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8821. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya.
Also G.N. 11377
