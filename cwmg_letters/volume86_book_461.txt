461. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 13, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
What shall I do? Yesterday I sent a letter to you. Satis
Babu who worries so much took it with him. But I got the letter
back. Now I am sending it again. I did not complain of your
obstinacy. I do not know what Sushilabehn said. It is enough
for me that you have faith. I shall be happy whether you die
reciting Ramanama after getting rid of your ailments, or are saved.
You yourself told me to inform Kafikhan and that is why I
did so. I had not thought of it. Now I do not want to do
anything. You can send a telegram asking him not to come.
It will be all right even if you don't.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 561
