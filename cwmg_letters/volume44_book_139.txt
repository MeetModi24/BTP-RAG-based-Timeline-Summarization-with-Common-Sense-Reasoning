139. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 22, 1930
CHI. MANI (PATEL),
You have given a good report of your experiences. I now
know you met Father. But he has not met me [since then]. Write
to me regularly. When in Bombay, meet Perinbehn [Captain]
and Lilavati [Munshi].
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro - 4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 73
