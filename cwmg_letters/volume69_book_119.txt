119. LETTER TO AKBAR HYDARI
NEW DELHI,
April 2, 1939
DEAR SIR AKBAR,
I hope you had my letter. I have been conferring with
Aryasamaj leaders about their satyagraha. Their demands seem
to be so small that they should lend themselves to easy adjustment.
Is not this possible?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 6843
