273. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
CHI. A. SALAAM,
Your folly has no limit. Your fast was intended for two days
only. The period will be over today. You should take orange juice
tomorrow with a light heart. Afterwards I shall tell you why I have
torn up your letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 665
