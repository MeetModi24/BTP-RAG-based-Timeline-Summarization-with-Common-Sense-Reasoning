34. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
August 21 [1919]
DEAR DEWAN BAHADUR,
During the suspension of civil resistance, I feel that there
should be sustained agitation to remove the Rowlatt Act. I
suggest that a reasoned memorial may be sent by leaders either
to the Viceroy or to Mr. Montagu. I am moving the leaders here.
But some of them think that even to send a memorial may jeopardize
reforms! Will Madras lead?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
