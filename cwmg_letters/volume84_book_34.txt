34. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
GANDHI CAMP, NEW DELHI,
April 19, 1946
DEAR BROTHER ANAND,
Pujya Bapu has your telegram. He wishes happiness to you both and
hopes you will serve the country. He hopes that marriage 3 might improve your
hearing. Bapu did not think it necessary to send a wire. Hope you are both
well.
Yours,
AMRIT KAUR
From a microfilm of the Hindi : Courtesy: National Archives of India and
Anand T. Hingorani
