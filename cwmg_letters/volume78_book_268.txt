268. LETTER TO V. VENKATAKRISHNAIAH
SEVAGRAM,
October 21, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
I would like you to make your experiment in Sevagram but
I lack the faith you want to inspire in me. Your intention is
wholly good, your execution is defective. Nevertheless you shall
come to see me, if I feel settled down. You will know when I
am.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI V. VENKATAKRISHNAIAH
KHADDAR SAMSTHANAM
BEZWADA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
