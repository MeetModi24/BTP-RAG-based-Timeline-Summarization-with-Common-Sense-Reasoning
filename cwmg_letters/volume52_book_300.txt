300. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
December 18, 1932
CHI. NARANDAS,
So at last you also have fallen ill. All of us do. You and all
others should carry out the new experiment only to the extent
that it agrees with you. For some time, you should only drink
milk and eat fruit. You may also eat vegetables. If you feel the
need for rice, you should eat it unhesitatingly. I have suggested
its exclusion purely for health reasons. Hence anybody who needs
it for maintaining good health may certainly eat it. Nobody
should feel ashamed to eat it. If the results do not seem bene-
ficial, you may give up the whole experiment.
I cling to the suggestion which I have made concerning Jamna.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8284. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
