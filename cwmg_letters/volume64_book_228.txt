228. LETTER TO SARASWATI
January 3, 1937
CHI. SARASWATI,
Your letter. It seems I have not to come to Tr. Just yet
at any rate. Kanti could hardly come even if I have to go there.
He was with me for three days at Faizpur.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6158. Also C.W. 3431. Courtesy: Kantilal
Gandhi
