192. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 29, 1940
MY DEAR AMRIT,
Nothing from you for the past two days. Here it is all well.
Hope you are free from cough and cold.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
My love to Sarup and Co.
SMT. RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
ANAND BHAVAN
ALLAHABAD, U.P.
From the original: C.W. 3960. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7269
