551. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
DELHI,
September 24, 1938
CHI. MIRA,
Here it is all dry, there all wet. This prolongation of wet
weather is a ruinous thing. And yet we may not grumble. It is
all God's doing. Only we don't know Him. To grumble is
also a f9q1j. It won't abate till we know Him face to face.
Both Mahadev and I are keeping ideal health. No strain is
put upon me. My silence has done the trick.
You need not worry about the wires. I hope to enclose with
this a note to the P. M.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6403. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9998
