245. LETTER TO R. D. BIRLA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Chaitra Shukla 6 [March 30, 1925]
BHAISHRI RAMESHWARDASJI,
I have your letter. When I get the Rs. 5,000, I shall spend
the amount on the untouchables' welfare, as desired by you. No
letter from Jamnalalji as yet. At present he is touring Rajpu-
tana for khadi propaganda.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SJT. RAMESHWARDAS BIRLA
BIRLA HOUSE
RANCHI
From the Hindi original: C. W. 6122. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
