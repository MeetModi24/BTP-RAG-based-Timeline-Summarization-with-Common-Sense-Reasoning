151. LETTER TO V. K. SALVEKAR
POST ANDHERI,
March 15, 1924
DEAR MR. SALVEKAR 2 ,
I thank you for your letter offering me the use of your bunga-
low in Nasik. I know that the climate of Nasik is very salubrious,
but at the present moment I am having my convalescence at a
pleasant seaside retreat near Andheri. If this place does not suit
me and I am advised to go to a dry place, I shall bear in mind
your kind offer.
Yours sincerely,
V. K. SALVEKAR, ESQ.
HATTIKHANA ROAD
NASIK CITY
From a photostat: S.N. 8501
