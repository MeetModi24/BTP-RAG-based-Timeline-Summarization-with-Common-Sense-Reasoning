142. LETTER TO JATINDRA DAS
POONA,
November 18, 1945
CHI. PARAM OR JATINDRA DAS,
I wanted to write to you yesterday, but could not find the
time. And today I have your letter. Even now I cannot write
at length. You are doing good work. If you try to come over
in a hurry, all your efforts will be wasted.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
