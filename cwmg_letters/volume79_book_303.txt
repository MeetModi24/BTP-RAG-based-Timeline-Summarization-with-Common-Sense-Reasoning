303. LETTER TO DR. JIVRAJ MEHTA
SEVAGRAM,
February 28, 1945
BHAI JIVRAJ MEHTA,
Chi. Indu writes to say that the Kamala [Nehru Memorial]
Hospital committee does not meet at all. The local committee
too seems to care little. They have run short of funds. If you
can, throw some light on all this.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. JIVRAJ MEHTA
ALTAMAN ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
