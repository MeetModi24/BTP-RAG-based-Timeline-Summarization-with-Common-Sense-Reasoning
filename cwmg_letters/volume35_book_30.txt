30. LETTER TO HEMAPRABHA DEVI DAS GUPTA
KARAIKUDI,
Ashvin Shukla 1 [September 26, 1927]
DEAR SISTER,
Your letter has reached me. How long will you grieve
over Anil's death? It does no good, either for the departed soul or
for us, to brood over his qualities. Why should we not look at
the matter from this angle? Anil's soul is immortal. We were
concerned only with his soul, not with his body. Had it been
with the body we could have embalmed the corpse and preserv-
ed it for years. But we cremated the body upon the soul's depar-
ture. In order to realize this and put it into practice, we need no
yogi nor anyone else. Yes, we needs must have faith in God
and also in the immortality of the soul. Now let us forget Anil's
body and try to emulate him.
Nikhil too seems to be a wonderful child. Do not let him
overwork his body.
May God grant you wisdom and peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
