42. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 7, 1928
BHAI HARIBHAU,
Read the accompanying letter and let me know what truth
it contains. The correspondent has told the story about Bihar,
but it concerns Rajputana. Probably, therefore, you will be able
to say more about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 6060. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
