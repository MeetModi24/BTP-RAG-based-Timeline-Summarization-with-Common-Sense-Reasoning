18. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
April 8, 1942
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
Vidya must be sent away.
It is not dharma to put
up with misbehaviour.
About Babu and Saryu also we must
think. However, I am continuing my efforts. I will try again
after I return from Bombay. I am not very hopeful. Babu
Mahatma seems to have cast a powerful spell. As a last resort,
we can adopt the remedy I have suggested. If they don't listen
to us, we should boycott them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10607
