494. LETTER TO ISHWARDAS
August 17, 1935
BHAI ISHWARDAS,
The couplets composed by you concern a guru and his disciple
and that too when they are residing at the same place. Neither
am I a guru nor are you a disciple. I have never made anyone my
disciple. I hope you know this. I find a sort of listlessness in your
questions. They are such that they have already been covered in
my writings. But you don't study and ponder over them.
Blessings from
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
