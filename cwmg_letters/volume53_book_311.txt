311. LETTER TO L. L. YELIGAR
February 4, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of 30th ultimo enclosing an expla-
natory article on one of sister Mahadevi's sayings. As soon as I
get the time I shall go through the article and write to you.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. L. L. YELIGAR
C/O K. B. MUMMIGATTI
SCHOOL MASTER, HOSAYELLAPUR
DHARWAR
From a microfilm: S.N. 19296
