140. LETTER TO JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
DATTAPARA,
November 12, 1946
CHI. JAYAPRAKASH,
I feel that today you are the God in Bihar. Will Bihar really
become calm? We have committed a grievous error. Write to
me frankly what is likely to happen now. Give me your un-
reserved opinion. I have a feeling that there should be no Con-
gress [session] this time. Leaders of all the provinces should re-
main in their own provinces. You may convey my opinion to
all. I may not be able to do it as I have little free time.
Where is Prabha1? What does she do?
I hope you are keeping well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
PATNA
