301. LETTER TO PYARELAL
August 15, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
Why should I feel bad if you enquired about my blood-
pressure? But what reply could I give? What is the way when
anxiety is eating me? I continue my efforts. But what about my
responsibility for whatever is happening? My constant thought is
only that you should soon be well and leave your bed. Did I not
write to you that your illness would be unbearable to me? But
I did not know how unbearable it would be. What can I do?
Blessings from
BAPU
[P.S.]
There is no need to reply to this. It will be sufficient
if you have peace.
