206. LETTER TO K. S. NARAYANA IYER
WARDHA,
December 16, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
In wishing the Conference success I do hope that Travancore
will wipe out the curse of untouchability and unapproachability
and the like.
Yours,
K. S. NARAYANA IYER
THE ALL-INDIA HUMANITARIAN CONF[ERENCE]
TRIVANDRUM
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S. N. 32579/27
