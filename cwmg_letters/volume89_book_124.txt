124. LETTER TO DHIRENDRA NATH CHATTERJEE
CALCUTTA,
August 28, 1947
CHI. DHIREN1,
I have your letter. Write to me after your examination.
Then I will tell you what to do. There is nothing to be said
now.
It is good that you have passed the third level in Hindu-
stani without much effort and with distinction.
It is sad that you are not keeping well. It has to do a
good deal with your mind. I find that your mind is not stable.
This in turn affects the body.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 5107
