19. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
March 3, 1939
The longer words should be more widely spaced. Anyway
the improvement is considerable and it will be good if it is sus-
tained. If I have my way you shall turn out into a true and
expert gosevak.
This letter reached me here today, having been forwarded
from Bardoli.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1919
