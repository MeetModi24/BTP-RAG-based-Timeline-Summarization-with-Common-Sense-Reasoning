569. LETTER TO KULSUM SAYANI
PANCHGANI,
June 16, 1945
BETI KULSUM,
To whom should I write? Where is the time? And yet how
can I say 'No' to you? This is my message :
I like the mission of Rahbar to unite Hindi and Urdu. May
it succeed. -M. K. Gandhi
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KULSUM SAYANI
RUPA VILA
CUMBALLA HILL, BOMBAY
