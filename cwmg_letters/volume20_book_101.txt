101. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
AHMEDABAD,
June 13, 1921
DEAR FRIEND,
Correspondents from Bhavnagar tell me that you are not op-
posed to all of my activities. I certainly expect help from you, and
from everyone else, for the spinning-wheel movement and educa-
tional work. I should like you to contribute to the Swaraj Fund,
and persuade others also to do so, the contributions being ear-
marked for these causes.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3177; also S.N. 27769 and G.N.
