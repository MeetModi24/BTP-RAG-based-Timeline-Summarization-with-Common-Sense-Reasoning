180. LETTER TO MULJIBHAI T. SHARMA
May 25, 1942
BHAI MULJIBHAI,
You are unduly sensitive. We should not resent harmless
popular sayings. Why should a Baniya take the saying, 'I see
you are after all a Baniya', to be a reflection on himself? What
if cowardice is attributed to a Baniya? Or if a Brahmin is be-
lieved to get wise after the event? As for me, 1 look upon the
barber's occupation as an honourable one, for barbers have some
knowledge of home remedies too.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI MULJIBHAI TULSIDAS SHARMA
NAYEE HIND SABHA
DANDIA BAZAAR
BARODA, B.B.& C.I. RLY.
