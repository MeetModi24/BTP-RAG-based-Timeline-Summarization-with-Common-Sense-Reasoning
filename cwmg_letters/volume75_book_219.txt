219. LETTER TO R. M. SANYAL
SEVAGRAM,
December 8, 1941
DEAR PROFESSOR,
As you know I am doing all I can. I suppose your brother
will be transferred in due course to U. P. The release is a different
and difficult matter. You will have seen my statement on it.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
