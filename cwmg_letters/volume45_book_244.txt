244. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
After Prayers, February 11, 1931
CHI. CHHAGANLAL (JOSHI),
I do not know whether you will get this letter. This time, be
satisfied with a postcard. I do wish to go to Kaira District at
the earliest opportunity. Whatever settlement is reached,-I have
little hope that any will be reached-I will certainly try to safe-
guard fully the interests of the cultivators who had joined the
struggle. I have not received the note sent by you.
Write to me from time to time and ask others to write too.
I shall be here up to the 15th at any rate. After that, wherever God
takes me. If I can carry out my plan, I wish to go to Kaira via
Ahmedabad. I need not write anything in this letter about the
events here.
Blessings from
BAPU
