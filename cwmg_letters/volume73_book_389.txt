389. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 31, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
Mahadev has left.
I am reminded of you today because you are going on a
difficult task. Of course God is with you. Will remember the
second. Ultimately Subhas Babu could not be arrested. Manu
has fever today also. I am quite all right. It is pretty cold,
so I slept under the root Kishorelal has come. Blessings to
Maharani and Maharaj Kumar.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7873. Also C.W. 4240. Courtesy:
Amrit Kaur
