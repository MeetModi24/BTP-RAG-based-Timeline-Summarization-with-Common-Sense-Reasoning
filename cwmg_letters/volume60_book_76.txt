76. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
January 8, 1935
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I have your separate letters. I understand what you write
about Ramdas. I did want a permit issued from there. But
never mind if we cannot have it. I shall myself see about it
here if Ramdas is willing.
I had a tersely worded letter from Mama about Medh
which I am enclosing along with one from Harilal. If you
wish, you may let Medh read the letter. There is a great differ-
ence between what you recommended and what I find in this
letter. Now Pragji alone remains to be consulted.
The new Agent came to see me.
He has visited South
Africa once. You will surely meet him. Keep me informed about
your experience. I have of course asked him to bring about a
reconciliation.
I am in Delhi these days. Ba, etc., are with me. We shall
be here for a few more days. At any rate we must return by the
end of this month. It is pretty cold here.
I hope you are all well.
Sushila seems to have acquired wide knowledge.
What Kallenbach says is on the whole true. Good Jews do
not oppose us. Narandas's Kanu is with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Tell Medh I am not writing a separate letter to him.
