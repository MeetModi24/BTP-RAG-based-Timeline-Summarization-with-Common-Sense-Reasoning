731. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
December 31, 1933
CHI. KANTA,
I had expected your letter. Write regularly. Give me news
about Vidya, Shanti, etc. Om and Kisan are quite happy. Kisan
probably does not remember you. What did you do at Banaras?
I am sure you will have met Sumangal.
Blessings from
BAPU
