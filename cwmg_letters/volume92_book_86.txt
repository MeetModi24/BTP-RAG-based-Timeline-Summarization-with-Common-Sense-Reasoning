86. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
August 7, 1929
MY DEAR PEACE-MAKER,
I have your letter giving me all the information about dogs
and daughters. I suppose you put the dogs first because they are
less troublesome.
I shall be in Bombay on 11 th by the Gujarat Mail, not the
Kathiawar Mail which comes an hour later. I dare not stay at
the Taj. I must go to Laburnum Road. Nothing will be required
at Mr. Jinnah's house as I shall have taken horse's food at
Laburnum Road.
You will please send me back the same day.
Lovingly yours,
MATTER-OF-FACT
(NOT MYSTIC)
SPINNER
