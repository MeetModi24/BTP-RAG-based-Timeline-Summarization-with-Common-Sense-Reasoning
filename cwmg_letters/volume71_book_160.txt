160. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
January 23, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I was brief in order to save time. Try again. Don't lose
heart. I know that there is a lot of [suppressed] anger in A.S.
All of us are imperfect and should, therefore, bear with one an-
other. My own smallness has been exposed by my dreams. But
just as all of you tolerate me as I am, so also should you tolerate
one another. Otherwise all of us will be put to shame.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8553. Also C.W. 7073. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
