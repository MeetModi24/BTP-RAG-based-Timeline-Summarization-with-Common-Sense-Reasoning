33. LETTER TO LALLUBHAI SAMALDAS MEHTA
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
Shravan Vad 10, 1975 [August 20, 1919]
DEAR FRIEND,
I have not troubled you any time, but today I cannot help
it. Perhaps you are not altogether a stranger to the case of Manilal
Jadavji Vyas. He is a subject of the Rajkot State. He had a
business in Karachi. He signed the satyagraha pledge in March
or April. In May, the Commissioner at Karachi deported him from
British India under the Act of 1864. The man wrote to me and
appealed to the Bombay Government. The latter has confirmed
the order. You will find this action of the Government commented
upon in Young India. I take it that you read it. If you don't
get a copy, let me know and I shall see to it that you do. If
you choose, you can do much in this matter. I should like you to.
From a handwritten copy of the Gujarati original: S. N. 6810
