208. LETTER TO GANGADHARRAO B. DESHPANDE
POST ANDHERI,
March 23, 1924
MY DEAR GANGADHARRAO,
I was thinking this morning very early what I could do to
help Majli. The result was a letter of which I send you a copy.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. GANGADHARRAO B. DESHPANDE
BELGAUM
From a photostat: S.N. 8568; also C.W. 5143
