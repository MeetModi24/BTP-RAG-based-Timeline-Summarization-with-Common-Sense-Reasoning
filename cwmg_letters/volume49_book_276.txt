276. LETTER TO A PENSIONER
March 22, 1932
You should write to Dr. Muthu. My own knowledge, which,
though not scientific, but based on experience suggests that you
should fast for three days and break the fast with milk and
orange juice. If you try this, it will help you.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 30
