529. LETTER TO VICEROY
SEVAGRAM,
December 3, 1944
DEAR FRIEND1,
This is with reference to your letter of 2nd November last.
My letter of 17th July to Mr. Churchill was, in my estima-
tion, of a sacred character, not meant for the public eye. I now
contemplate an occasion or time when it might need publication
without losing the sacred character. Even so I do not wish to publish
it without the Prime Minister's permission. May I have it, in
case I need to publish it?
I should tell you that I have shown the contents to a few
friends.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. E. THE VICEROY
VICEROY'S CAMP
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, p. 15
1 Lord Wavell
2 Vide Vol. LXXVII, pp. 391-.
3 In his letter dated December 21, 1944, E. M. Jenkins conveyed the
Prime Minister's consent.
