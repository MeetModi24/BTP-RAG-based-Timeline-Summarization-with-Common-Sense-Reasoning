492. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
POONA,
September 25, 1945
CHI. BABUDI,
On reading about the rains there, my thoughts flew to you
as if I did not care for the others. Despite every effort to culti-
vate non-attachment, such things do happen sometimes. I hope
you are well. You need not write. It will suffice if Chi. Gordhan-
das drops a postcard. The poor must have been rendered
homeless.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: 10060. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokhawala
