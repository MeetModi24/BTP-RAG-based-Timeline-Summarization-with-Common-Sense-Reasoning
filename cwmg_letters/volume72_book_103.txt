103. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 21, 1940
BHAI VITHALDAS,
I think we should not ask for contributions just yet. I have
even written to Shankerlal [Banker] to this effect. We shall know
in a month or so. Meanwhile we should think over the matter.
How to improve the internal state of affairs?
What should we do if we get no contributions?
Blessings from
BAPU
