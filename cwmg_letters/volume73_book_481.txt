481. LETTER TO ISWAR SARAN
March 6, 1941
DEAR MUNSHIJI,
Your letter. It was not trouble but joy to visit the Ashram
which you have built up with patient effort. I should repeat
the visit whenever possible. Your institution is worthy of full
public support. May you live long to serve this essential cause of
humanity.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI MUNSHI ISWAR SARAN
HARIJAN ASHRAM
ALLAHABAD
From a copy: C.W. 10289. Courtesy: Allahabad Municipal Museum
