488. LETTER TO J. R. BHALA
SEVAGRAM,
March 22, 1945
BHAI BHALA,
I read carefully your letter to Pyarelal and your article. I
hope to reach Bombay on the 31st. Make an appointment and
meet me there. I will make further inquiries so that I can guide
your Association.
M. K. GANDHI
BHAI J. R. BHALA
OVERSEAS STUDENTS' ASSOCIATION
9 FORJETT HILL, BOMBAY 26
