28. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
September 28, 1933
CHI. MARY1,
I am delighted you are normal today. I think that it is
better for you to take the orthodox medical treatment and get
well. Injections are the order of the day nowadays. You had
better take them. And take what food they permit. If you do not
need N.'s help, let her come away. I know that the introduc-
tion of strangers disturbs doctors in a well-managed hospital.
May God be with you.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6007. Also C.W. 3333. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
