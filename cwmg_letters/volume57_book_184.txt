184. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
February 19, 1934
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL
,
Ramji's letter is enclosed. You can get work from him only
with the utmost gentleness coupled with equal firmness. Read
my letters to him before passing them on to him.
If you experience any difficulty on account of Amalabehn, let
me know.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4021
