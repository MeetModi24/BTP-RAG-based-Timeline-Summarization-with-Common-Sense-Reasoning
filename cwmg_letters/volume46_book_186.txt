186. LETTER TO DUDABHAI
NAINITAL,
May 18, 1931
BHAI DUDABHAI,
I have decided to give Lakshmi in marriage to a non-Antyaja.
It seems necessary to me to do that. Let me know what you
think in the matter. It will do if you send your reply to the
Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3243
