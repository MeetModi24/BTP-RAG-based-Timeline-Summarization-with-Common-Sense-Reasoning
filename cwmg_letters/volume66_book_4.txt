4. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 1, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
The Simla-dweller and you have praised the reed with a
fountain-pen and a typewriter! I have added a paragraph to your
article.
If Shanta can fit herself into the set-up, I don't think we
shall need Devraj, provided you take work from Chhotelal also.
But now we shall think over all this on the train on Tuesday.
Now Kanu is not going there today. He will go tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
PS.
You may send a wire to Vallabhbhai as follows: "Statement
not yet. Am considering propriety publishing correspondence."
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11542
