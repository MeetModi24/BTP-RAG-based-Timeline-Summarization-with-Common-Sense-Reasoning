377. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
February 14, 1942
CHI. AMALA,
It seems I have not written to you. If I have not, it is not
through any negligence on my part but because I forgot about
it owing to pressure of work.
You may come whenever you wish.
Come when you wish.
Love.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
