168. LETTER TO KHAN CHAND DEV
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 16, 1931
DEAR DR. KHAN CHAND,
I was glad to receive your exhaustive note and it came
just in time. There will be difficulty about the release of the
remaining prisoners. I must now ask you to give me copy of
judgment and evidence in each case if it is at all possible or
have a note prepared there on each case showing why you con-
sider that the prisoners concerned fall within the Settlement.
You must give me also a full description about the cases still
pending.
Yours sincerely,
DR. KHAN CHAND DEV
BRADLAUGH HALL
LAHORE
