84. LETTER TO J. L. MAFFEY
MOTIHARI,
January 21, 1918
DEAR MR. MAFFEY,
I thank you for your two letters.
I fear that probably I have failed to convey my full meaning
in my letter. Matters so delicate as the one regarding Messrs Ali
brothers are least satisfactorily handled by correspondence. It
would perhaps be better, if you think it advisable, that I should
run down to Delhi and first have a chat with you and then, if
it is considered necessary, I should wait on His Excellency. Will
1 Shri Krishna

you please consider my suggestion and let me know what you
think about it?
Yours sincerely,
