537. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
WARDHA,
April 6, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
I understand what you say about gur and shakkar.
I am returning straight from Indore. The report about the
tour was quite wrong. I might have to go to Gujarat later.
I hope to be here in July to receive you.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3712. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6868
