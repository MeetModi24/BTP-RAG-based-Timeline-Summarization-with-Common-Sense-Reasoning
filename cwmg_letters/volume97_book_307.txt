307. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
May 18, 1939
CHI. SUMI,
I got your letter. You have deformed your handwriting. Write to Kanam.
Will the sores caused by small-pox remain? Write to me regularly. Segaon is
almost deserted these days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
