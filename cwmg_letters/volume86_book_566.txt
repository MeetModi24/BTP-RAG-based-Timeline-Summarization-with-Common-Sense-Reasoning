566. LETTER TO C. P. RAMASWAMY IYER
February 5, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
I have very hastily glanced through the Press communique
which you have been good enough to send me. Is there not
a discrepancy between para 2 and para 45?
The reservation powers seem to me to be so great as vir-
tually to neutralize the liberality of the promised constitution.
Further than this I dare not go. Naturally I would like yours
to be the most progressive State in the whole of India.
C. P. RAMASWAMY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
