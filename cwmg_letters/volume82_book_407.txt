407. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
CAMP : MAHISHADAL,
POST SODEPUR,
December 28, 1945
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
I am enclosing a letter, this as desired by the writer. He
might have met me in South Africa, but I do not remember. I
have written to him that he has made a tall claim in his address.
He seems to be a crazy man.
I could find time only now to read what you said before the
students in Bihar. I liked it very much.
You need a little rest. It would indeed be good if you could
snatch it.
I had told Rajkumari to write to you about the Commu-
nists. Today I find a totally different thing in the newspapers.
A clipping is attached herewith. What is this? Can you throw
some light?
Blessings from
BAPU
