489. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
March 15, 1942
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter. I am glad you have seen the untruth. Dan-
ger is not yet over. But God is your sure guide and friend. Trust
Him through and through. Your body is a good index for you.
The Ashram is overcrowded but it goes on.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6494. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9889
