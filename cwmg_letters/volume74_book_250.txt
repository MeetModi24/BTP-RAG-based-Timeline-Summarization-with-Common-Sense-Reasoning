250. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 14, 1941
MY DEAR AMALA,
You are foolish. Mahadev is in Delhi. He has to wander
about just now. Why this hankering? True friendship does not
require meeting or even letter-writing. Why not just treasure
silent love? Or must you for ever remain mad?
Love.
BAPU
From the original: Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
