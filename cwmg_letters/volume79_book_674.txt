674. LETTER TO SHEELA
BOMBAY,
April 18, 1945
CHI. SHEELA,
Your letters are prolix and thoughtless. The briefer a letter,
the sweeter it is. That I would be writing to you did not mean
that I would be writing to you daily. If I write once in a year
it may be said I keep writing. There was no question of writing
promptly.
A friend is one who never expects a letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHEELABEHN
SHIKSHASADAN
MEERUT
