114. LETTER TO ANJANADEVI CHOWDHURY
August 22, 1929
DEAR SISTER,
Jamnalalji has just informed me that Shri Ram Narayanji
has been taken seriously ill. Jamnalalji is coming that way. I
know you to be a devoted wife and a devoted worker. It will be
a good thing if Ram Narayanji gets well because of your nur-
sing him. There is no death, however, for the soul residing in
that body. Then why should we grieve over the body? Tell Ram
Narayanji that he should remain calm and recite God's name.
From a copy of the Hindi: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/131
