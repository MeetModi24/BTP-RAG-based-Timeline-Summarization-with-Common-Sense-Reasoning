214. LETTER TO NAGJIBHAI
SEVAGRAM,
June 2, 1942
BHAI NAGJI,
You have made a handsome gift. The Sangh was in need
of such a building. But I want a much greater contribution
from you. My appetite, therefore, is not likely to be satisfied
with this building. I hope your business is flourishing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6252
