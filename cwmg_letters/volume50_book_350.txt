350. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PAREKH
August 8, 1932
BHAISHRI PRABHASHANKAR 3 ,
I got your letter. It is natural that we should feel grieved
at the death of Doctor. For himself, it was a release from suffer-
ing. I have expressed my opinion and told Ratilal and Champa
that it is not necessary for them to go to Rangoon. In any case
I will write to Rangoon. I do not know whether there is a will.
I think that safety and good sense lie in trusting Chhaganlal.
All the same, you may take the advice of Nanalal or anybody else
in whose judgment you have confidence. My views will probably
seem impractical to you, but to me they appear most practical.
I believe that the interests of Champa and Ratilal are safe.
I hope Mother has recovered. My respectful greetings to her.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
