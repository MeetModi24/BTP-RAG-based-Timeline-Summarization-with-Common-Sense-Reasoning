79. LETTER TO LAKSHMI GANDHI
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 30, 1945
CHI. LAKSHMI,
You must have received my telegram. You and the child
will be well. It is quite some time now. Will Rajaji and Dev-
das be coming together or will Rajaji come a little earlier? Is
Papa also there?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2000
