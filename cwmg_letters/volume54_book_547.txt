547. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
April 21, 1933
MY DEAR THAKKAR BAPA,
I have your letter of 17th instant enclosing the bill from
Kodanda Rao. You can check the bill far better than I can.
I can only say that he came promptly whenever I sent him a
message to come during the days when I used to issue statements,
and I suppose that is what you want me to state. The dates
given by him I have no doubt are in perfect order.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 21023
