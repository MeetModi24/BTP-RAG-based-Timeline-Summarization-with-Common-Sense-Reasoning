271. LETTER TO LORD STANLEY
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 28, 1906
MY LORD,
The Delegates have not received your reply to the invitation card
they have ventured to send to Your Lordship for breakfast at 10.30
tomorrow. The Delegates venture to hope that Your Lordship will
honour them by your presence.
I beg to remain,
Your Lordship's obedient servant,
THE RT. HON. LORD STANLEY OF ALDERLEY
18, MANSFIELD STREET, W.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4658/a
