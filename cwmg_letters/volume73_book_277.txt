277. LETTER TO J. M. DATTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 13, 1940
DEAR FRIEND,
Nothing that I can do will alter the situation but I think
there will be no difficulty as there is no boycott declared on behalf
of the Congress.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
JATINDRA MOHAN DATTA
45 BARRACKPORE TRUNK ROAD
COSSIPORE P. O.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
