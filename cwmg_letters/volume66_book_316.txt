316. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 19, 1937
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
The money collected for the Gidwani Memorial was of
course well spent. And I consider it a good omen for the
students' hostel that the inauguration ceremony of the Bhangi
Students' Hostel will be performed by Principal Anandshankar-
you not to worry. There is absolutely no need. Let him follow
his own inclination.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10739
