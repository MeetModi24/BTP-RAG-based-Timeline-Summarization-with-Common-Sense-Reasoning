426. LETTER TO E. E. MACK
WARDHA,
February 12, 1941
DEAR MR. MACK,
I thank you for your letter. Of course I accept your word.
I never published anything.
I must not be held responsible for
things said to be [mine].
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
