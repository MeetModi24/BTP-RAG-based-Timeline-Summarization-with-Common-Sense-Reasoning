78. LETTER TO MADHAVDAS G. KAPADIA
April 21, 1942
CHI. MADHAVDAS,
You must have got the letter I wrote to you yesterday. To-
day I got your second letter. You have lost a companion, but
Krishna is released from suffering. Rest assured she has attained
peace. Have patience. Keep calm. Try to payoff the debts
if you owe any, and spend your days in devotion to God. Ba
wishes the same.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
