398. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
NEW DELHI,
September 15, 1946
CHI. JIVANJI,
I have your letter. I approve of the idea of giving eight
columns of the dictionary every fortnight. You may, therefore,
do whatever is convenient for you. The readers will probably
find eight columns more convenient because it will be easier to
preserve and bind them. It is for you to consider and decide
what to do.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9971. Also C.W. 6945.
Courtesy: Jivanji D. Desai
