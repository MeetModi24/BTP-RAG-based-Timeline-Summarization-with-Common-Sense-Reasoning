284. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN CHAUDHARI
February 25, 1940
CHI. RAMNARAYAN,
Your time is fully occupied. Do not take up any more work.
Your main job is to attain proficiency in khadi work. It is not
at all difficult to do the carding according to the Andhra method.
Consult Krishnadas.
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapu: Maine Kya Dekha, Kya Samjha, p. 133
