255. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
June 12, 1942
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
You have volunteered to shoulder the responsibility for
Lilavati Her college reopens on the 20th. I am trying to spare
you. But if I fail, will you please see, or have someone see, about
her boarding and lodging?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4739. Courtesy: Shantikumar
N. Morarjee
