63. LETTER TO LAKSHMINARAYAN GADODIA
BORSAD,
May 1, 1931
BHAI LAKSHMINARAYANJI2,
I have your letter. I am grateful to you for the help you
extended to Ramanandji. Have you seen his work?
Give me the news about the foreign-cloth trade.
What is the dispute in the Congress Committee about?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5617
