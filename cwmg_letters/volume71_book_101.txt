101. LETTER TO MADALASA
SEGAON,
January 5, 1940
CHI. MADALASA,
How silly you are! Now that Shriman is coming, get well soon.
Keep Ramanama engraved in your heart. He will see that every-
thing ends well. Don't lose heart. You needn't reply. Shriman
will do that.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 319
