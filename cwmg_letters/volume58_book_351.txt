351. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
August 19, 1934
DEAR MOTIBABU,
I thank you for your letter. I am slowly regaining lost
strength. I would like you to send me a detailed report of your
activities, and then if I have any suggestions to make of course I
shall do so.
With love.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI MOTILAL ROY
PRABARTAK SANGH
CHANDRANAGORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
