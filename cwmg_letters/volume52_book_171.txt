171. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
December 5, 1932
BELOVED DAUGHTER RAIHANA,
I got your postcard. I am very glad that you have come to
Poona. Your health should improve now. You can come when-
ever you feel like. A judge's daughter can come when she wants
to. Inform me in advance when you intend to come.
Come
about 1 p.m. except on a Sunday. I had a letter from Father. I
had got your previous letter and I had also replied to it.
As my right hand pains, I use the left hand and so the Urdu
letters are very badly written. Come soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: S.N. 9664
