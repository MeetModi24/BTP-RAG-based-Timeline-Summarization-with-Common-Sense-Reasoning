301. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
Silence day
BHAI SHRI VALJI,
Herewith the preface you had asked for.
It seems to me the proper thing to hand over the money
collected for the students to the Go-raksha fund with the consent of
the donors. If there is no need to consult the donors or anyone else,
your way is clear.
The answers to your questions have been sent to the Navajivan.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 11174. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
