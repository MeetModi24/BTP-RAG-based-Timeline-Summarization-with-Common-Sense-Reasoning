159. LETTER TO SYED AZMATULLAH
May 14, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I can only give you my assur-
ance that I shall do my best to promote Hindu-Muslim unity.
What I am striving for is a heart unity. The solution of the con-
stitutional question rests in many other hands than mine.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 17034
