354. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 29, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have received your letter about the office. I agree. I
understand Wardha to mean Wardha proper or places around
it. The reason is clear.
M. K. GANDHI
SHYAMLAL
KASTURBA TRUST OFFICE
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
