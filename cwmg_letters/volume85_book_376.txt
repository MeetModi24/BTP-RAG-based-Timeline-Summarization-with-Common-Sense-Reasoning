376. LETTER TO MORARJI DESAI
NEW DELHI,
September 12, 1946
BHAISHRI MORARJI,
Nagadi Sheth of South Africa brings you this note. This
gentleman was an active participant in the struggle that I
carried on in South Africa. Please see him.
BAPU
SJT. MORARJI DESAI
MINISTER
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
