487. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
October 19, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
I can say that I have done a fair amount of writing today.
Baba has done a great thing. Mira's temperature today has been
98° and it has not risen so far. She however continues to be
restless. There is no release of saliva in her mouth. It remains
sticky and she has no wish to take glucose or even fruit. There
is something else too. I want no oranges or lemons. Munnalal
made a mistake in sending for the lemons.
Blessings from
BAPU
