370. LETTER TO LADY MOUNTBATTEN
NEW DELHI,
July 18, 1947
DEAR SISTER,
SO you are celebrating the silver jubilee of your wedding
amid a shower of congratulations and good wishes. Let me add
mine to them and hope that your joint career here will blos-
som into citizenship of the world.
I hope your daughter is fully restored.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
LADY MOUNTBATTEN OF BURMA
From a photostat: G.N. 10831; also Life of the Countess Mountbatten of
Burma, p. 131
1 What follows is from Harijan.
