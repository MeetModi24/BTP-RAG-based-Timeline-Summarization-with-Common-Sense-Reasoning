39. LETTER TO R. S. KADKIA
July 6, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is not possible to give you a detailed
reply to all the questions you have put, but it is possible to give
you a general reply, namely, that wherever you can curtail your
requirements so as to bring yourself to a level with the poor you
should do so, and, in considering your requirements, you should
as a rule decide against yourself. And if you do so, you are likely
to reach the real minimum.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. S. KADKIA
CONGRESS OFFICE, HYDERABAD (SIND)
From a photostat: S.N. 13460
