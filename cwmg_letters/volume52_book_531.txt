531. LETTER TO PROMODE BIHARI MATHUR
January 8, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. The practice you mention is by no means
common all over India, but the remedy is surely in the hands
of the householders. If they have got rid of the false notions of un-
touchability, they would dismiss a careless sweeper and do sweep-
ing themselves. He can interfere with a new man coming, but he
dare not prevent the householder himself from doing his own clean-
ing. The evil is of our own creation and therefore we can deal with
it without any difficulty.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. PROMODE BIHARI MATHUR
KANUNGOYAN STREET, ALIGARH (U.P.)
