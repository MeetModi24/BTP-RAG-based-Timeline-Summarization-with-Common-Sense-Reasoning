67. LETTER TO GHANSHYAM SINGH GUPTA
July 27, 1945
BHAI GHANSHYAM SINGH,
There is no discourtesy in publishing the letter from the
Governor of Sind. I have made emendations in both the drafts.
You will understand them.
I finished it today but it could not be registered in the post
today. It will go tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
