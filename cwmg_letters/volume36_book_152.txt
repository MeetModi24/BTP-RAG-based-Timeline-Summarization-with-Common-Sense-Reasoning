152. LETTER TO T. DE MANZIARLY
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 21, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
It was a pleasure to receive your letter after such a long time.
I am sending you the two books you mention and I am adding a
third-Hand- spinning Essay, the Guide to Health and Takli Teacher.
Now about the 2nd paragraph. I would just like to say that
whilst I am a passionate devotee of simplicity in life, I have also
discovered that it is worthless unless the echo of simplicity comes
from within. The modern organized artificiality of so-called civi-
lized life cannot have any accord with true simplicity of heart.
Where the two do not correspond, there is always either gross self-
deception or hypocrisy.
Yours sincerely,
T. DE MANZIARLY
From a photostat: S.N. 14267
1 The addressee in his letter dated December 27, 1927 had written:
" . . . You know how I would like to see mankind realizing the necessity of
becoming more simple, to have more time and more energy to be spent on
truer things. . . ."
