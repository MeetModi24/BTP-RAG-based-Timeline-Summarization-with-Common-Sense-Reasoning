211. LETTER TO R. B. GREGG
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 14, 1928
MY DEAR GOVIND,
I have your letter. As soon as I receive the manuscript of
your Science notes I shall certainly go through them and then
hand them to Kaka for his opinion too.
With reference to the publication, whilst I appreciate your
arguments somehow or other my inner being dislikes the idea.
However I shall be able to judge better after I see the notes.
I hope you will not make yourself sick with overwork before
you leave for America. I would like you to leave in a perfectly
healthy condition.
The index and everything else can wait if you cannot easily
find time for them.
I never thought that you did not know that Andrews was in
England. He had the intention even whilst he was here of going
to America. Of course you will meet him there. He is going
there in September.
Yes, Bardoli is a great lesson. It has revived faith in non-
violent methods and in power of the masses.
My love to the Stokes.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 13489
