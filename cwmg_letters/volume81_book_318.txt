318. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
August 30, 1945
CHI. PRABHA,
A letter from Priyamvada is enclosed. I think you should
join it . Get yourself enrolled. About the work, we shall see
after you have rested in the Ashram.
I discovered after writing the postcard this morning that I
had in fact dictated a letter to you. However, I write the post-
card lest I should forget it.
I hope you are keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 3580
