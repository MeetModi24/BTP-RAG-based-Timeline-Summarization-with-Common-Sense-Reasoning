194. LETTER TO RAGHAVDAS
POONA,
February 23, 1946
BABA RAGHAVDAS,
I got your letter and was pleased to get it. I shall surely
do all I can. Do you get Harijan Sevak? What newspapers are
you allowed? I hope you are keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
[RAGHAVDAS]
FAIZABAD JAIL
