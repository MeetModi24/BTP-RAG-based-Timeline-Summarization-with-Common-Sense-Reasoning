506. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
April 1, 1935
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
The problem of conducting the official business of the Char-
kha Sangh, Udyog Sangh and Harijan Sangh in Hindi is a diffi-
cult one. For all the three offices the only capable men we have
found are English-knowing. Where are efficient Hindi-knowing
men to be found? What can one do? There is no need for me to
expound upon it. If there were right men to be found, we could
bring about the change in all the three offices today. Let me know
if you have any suggestion. You are coming to Indore, I suppose?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1095
