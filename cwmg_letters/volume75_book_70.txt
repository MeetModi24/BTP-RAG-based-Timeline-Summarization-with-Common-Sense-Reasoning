70. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
October 25, 1941
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
Send me Mohansingh's statement. I have read your letter.
According to my practice I shall inquire into the matter at my
convenience.
You must have fixed up with Chimanlal about yourself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 397. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
