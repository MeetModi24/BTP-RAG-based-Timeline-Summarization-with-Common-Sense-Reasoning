213. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 18, 1939
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Here is my letter to Chiang Kai-shek. The letter became
longer than I had wanted it to be. Perhaps it will be well to
send a typed copy with the original.
Mahadev went to Madras yesterday.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1939. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Also A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 380
