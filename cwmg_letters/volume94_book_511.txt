511. LETTER TO ARUNA ASAF ALI
PANCHGANI,
July 21, 1946
CHI. ARUNA,
I was not pained so much by your speech that day as by your
visit later and the inquiry. You appeared to be putting up an act. You
are not a goddess, nor do you want to become one. You should become
an instrument of service, pure and simple. You are not an actress either.
Beware !
1 In this, the addressee had informed Gandhiji of his advice to Nehru to
defer his visit to Kashmir.
292
This letter is a sigh of sorrow. Brajkisan will tell you more.
Blessings from
BAPU
