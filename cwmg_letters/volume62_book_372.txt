372. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
April 13, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
I have now two letters of yours to deal with. Not much to
say. If I do not leave tomorrow, I leave for certain on 15th.
Segaon is on the brain. Jamnalalji is half converted.
It is a great joy to me that in Sejila you have found a faith-
ful and intelligent companion. I dread your nightly adventures.
I know it is wrong to do so. We are all in His keeping. But I
am anxious for you to avoid all mishaps which can be antici-
pated.
I expect to find you hale, hearty and joyful.
Yes, Ku. has put his whole soul into making my rooms as
attractive as possible and that after my style.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
We are all fasting today. I have just broken mine.
From the original: C.W. 6325. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9791
