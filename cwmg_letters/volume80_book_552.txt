552. LETTER TO RAMCHANDRA AND RAJMOHAN GANDHI
June 14, 1945
CHI. RAMU 1 AND MOHAN 2 ,
I shall say I have letters from you both. In fact a letter written
in pencil should be considered as not having been written at all.
You have now looked at the Himalayas and seen the golden
snow on the mountains. Did you get any gold out of that?
Tara is quite grown up now. Does she not get time to write?
Have all of you become very strong?
Blessings from
BAPU
