474. LETTER TO DR. VARIAVA
August 31, 1940
BHAI VARIAVA,
Chi. Kunvarji is the husband of my grand-daughter, Ramibehn.
He was suffering from tuberculosis. Now the T. B. specialist Dr.
David says that he is all right. I shall be grateful if you will examine
him after a fortnight or so and send me your report.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 221
Blessings from
BAPU
