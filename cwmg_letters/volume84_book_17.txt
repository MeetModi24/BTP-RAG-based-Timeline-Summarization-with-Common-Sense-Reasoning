17. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
DELHI,
April 17, 1946
CHI. A. S.,
I have your letter. I do not wish to criticize you. I accept
what you have done. I plead guilty to your complaint that I
stopped [you] from going. I also understand your accepting
money from Brother. I want to tell you only one thing. Build
up excellent health and live as you wish to.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 510
