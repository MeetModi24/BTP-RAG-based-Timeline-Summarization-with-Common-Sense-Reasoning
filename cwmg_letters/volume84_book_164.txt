164. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SIMLA,
May 11, 1946
CHI. A. S.,
I got your letter. Herewith a letter from Badshah Khan.
I had sent two telegrams to Begum Rashid. In my letters
to you I referred to her as Amtul. I had an impression that
her name was Amtul. What is her name?
I have written to Akbar and advised him that he may go
to the Frontier Province when he can free himself completely
1 Dated May 4 and May 9; vide pp. 100 and 131.
135
from Samau and if he really feels eager to go there. To start
with, he should go alone. Afterwards, when he has settled down,
he may send for Zohra.
You must have received my letter containing advice about
[Begum] Rashid. I hope you are in good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
