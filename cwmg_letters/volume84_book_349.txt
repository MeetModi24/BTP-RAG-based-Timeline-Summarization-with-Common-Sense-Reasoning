349. LETTER TO ALLADI KRISHNASWAMI AIYER
MUSSOORIE,
June 4, 1946
DEAR SIR ALLADI,
Rajaji has sent me your opinion which I have read with
the attention it demands.
Hope you are gaining by your stay In Bangalore.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR ALLADI KRISHNASWAMI
TATA SILK FARM AREA
NEAR SOUTH END ROAD
BASAVANGUDI, BANGALORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
