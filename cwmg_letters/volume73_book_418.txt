418. LETTER TO ISWAR SARAN
February 10, 1941
DEAR MUNSHIJI,
If I come there, I must try to visit the Ashram. Please make
no announcement or fuss.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI MUNSHI ISWAR SARAN
HARIJAN ASHRAM
ALLAHABAD
From the original: C.W. 10282. Courtesy: Allahabad Municipal Museum
