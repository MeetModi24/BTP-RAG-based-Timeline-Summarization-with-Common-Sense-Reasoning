182. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
May 18, 1931
CHI. SHARDA,
I have your letter. Do not spoil your handwriting. Read the
translation of a poem on handwriting by Ramdas Swami which I had
sent. Send me a copy of it. I shall publish it in Navajivan some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati: C. W. 9901. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokhawala
