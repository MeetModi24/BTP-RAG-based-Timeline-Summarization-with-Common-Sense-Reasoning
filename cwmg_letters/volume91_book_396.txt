396. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
July 13, 1925
DEAR MOTI BABU1,
I have just got your letter. I am off again tomorrow morn-
ing. The whole of the week I shall be very busy. Could you
please come or send Nirmal Babu Tuesday next week early
morning, say, 7 a.m.?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 11018
