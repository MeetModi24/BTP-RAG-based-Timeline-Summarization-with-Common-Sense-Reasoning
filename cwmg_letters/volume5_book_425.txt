425. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
P. O. Box 6522,
JOHANNESBURG,
August 27, 1906
MY DEAR CHHAGANLAL,
I am sending you three leading articles to-night; that about Dadabhai
will of course occupy the first place, the second should be that about Johan-
nesburg and the third, the Colonial-born Indians, Note . I am too ex-
hausted to give you more although I have a lot to write upon, nor have
I the time, but I may be able to send an article or two to-morrow. They
will reach you on Thursday. It is now nearly five o'clock; I shall try

to give you some Gujarati, at least an article about Dadabhai. If possible,
give Dadabhai's photograph as a supplement next week. Brian Gabriel
has got the negative; he should do it free of charge. The block must be
well printed. I was almost about to telegraph to you about the supplement
but I think we do not want to hurry. It will come in very nicely next
week. I am giving another article on the Cape Act herewith, so that
you have 4.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
C. K. GANDHI, ESQ.
