385. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
Unrevised
5.25 Local Time, January 1, 1947
CHI. AMRIT,
I have been sending you messages. Sent you a wire through
J. Though I did not write because I could not, you were never
out of my mind.
Hope you found Shummy, Maud and Beryl quite well and
the old servants our friends. Of course I had all your letters.
I have studied your programme. You will go through it.
Now you need not come to me to discuss your programme.
The journey is long and tedious. You may get a special plane
if you will waste a lot of money. Even so you cannot avoid a
wretched motor journey of a few hours. It is not worth it. So
you must not come except when you must.
I suppose you know all about the accident to Renuka. She
was so anxious to do substantial service. But cruel fate would
not let her. Such is life. She has come nearer to me.
My trek will take a few days yet. I change my abode
tomorrow. You may address letters at Noakhali. They will send
my letters wherever I may be.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 4188. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7824
