467. LETTER TO M. K. SYED AHMED
[March 7, 1942]
DEAR SYED SAHEB,
Your undated letter before me. My blessings on your mar-
riage. May your union result in happiness to you and greater
service to the nation.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
JANAB M. K. SYED AHMED
MEMBER DISTRICT BOARD
DEEVO STREET
KAYALPORTNAM, S. INDIA
From a photostat: G.N. 8036
