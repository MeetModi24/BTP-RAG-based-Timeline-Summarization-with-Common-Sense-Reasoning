274. LETTER TO MEGHADEVI
SEVAGRAM,
February 23, 1945
CHI. MEGHADEVI,
I heard about your illness. Shri Ishwar D. has written to
me. You must not lose heart. Even incurable diseases are
cured. If you are destined to live, your illness is bound to go.
And even if it does not, what does it matter? Life and death
are not in our hands, are they? Be cheerful. Reassure your
elders too.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUMARI MEGHADEVI
C/O SETH RAMKISHORE
DEHRA DUN, U. P.
