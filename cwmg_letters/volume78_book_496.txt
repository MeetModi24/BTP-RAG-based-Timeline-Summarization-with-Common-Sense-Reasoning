496. LETTER TO RADHAKANT MALAVIYA
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1944
BHAI RADHAKANT1,
What you say is correct. No doubt I promised but now I
plead helplessness.
I feel exhausted and the burden of work is
quite heavy. I know what you want to tell me. Spare me, if
you can. Come if you must but after December 5.
M. K. GANDHI
RADHAKANT MALAVIYA
ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
