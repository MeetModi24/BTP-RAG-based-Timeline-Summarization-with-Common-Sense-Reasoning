260. LETTER TO KHUSHIRAM
SODEPUR,
December 8, 1945
BHAI KHUSHIRAM,
Bhai Jairamdas has told me how generous you are. It is
good to give whatever you want to give without laying down
unnecessary conditions. And the trust is being made. All this
makes me very happy.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
