52. LETTER TO PUSHPA K. DESAI
July 25, 1945
CHI. PUSHPA 1,
I have your letter. If you have really realized God, you will
see Him everywhere, even in your father. Still, if you must come
here you may. But you will have to find solitude in multi-
tude. You will have to do everything right from cleaning the
latrines and you will have to find God in that. One cannot
serve God by singing bhajans. You may therefore be escaping
from one difficulty to land into another. I advise you to see
Manibehn and then arrive at a decision. Consider it a sin to
write in pencil.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. PUSHPABEHN
C/O SHRI MANILAL POPATLAL DOSHI
SHARDA'S CHAWL
SECOND FLOOR, ROOM No. 12
BOMBAY
