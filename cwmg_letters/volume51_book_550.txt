550. LETTER TO SULOCHANA
November 2, 1932
CHI. SULOCHANA,
I got your letter. If you spin with better slivers, you will
spin still finer yarn. You should feel no shame whatever in asking
Premabehn any questions. If you cannot ask a teacher, whom
else can you ask? Do, therefore, ask her and let me know what
replies she gives. If, even after this, you feel ashamed to ask
her, write down the questions and give them to her.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1744
