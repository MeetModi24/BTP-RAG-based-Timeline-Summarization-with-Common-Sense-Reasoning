491. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
October 21, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
You may send a wire to Shivprasad also stating who are
coming with me. Take it for certain that you will be there. The
accompanying is for Raja.
Write to S. saying that I have
written to Raja.
I can't see how I may write on Premchandji . If we sug-
gest it to Viyogi Hari, he might draft a brief note. We take no
editorial notice of men of letters. Lallubhai's case, however,
deserves to be considered. For my part, I propose to leave here
at six o'clock. No arrangements need be made from there.
The luggage will be carried in the cart from here. I shall
walk as much as I can; I am feeling strong enough. I shall
climb onto the cart if I feel it necessary.
On my way I shall
stop to look at Kamala's son. I shall peep into Maganwadi
if there is time. You may if you wish suggest any change in
this. The patients are fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
