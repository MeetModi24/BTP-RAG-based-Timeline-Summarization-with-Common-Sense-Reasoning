206. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
July 14, 1934
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your letter.
I see no need for your coming to Wardha,
but you may come if you cannot control your desire. Even if you
don't feel inclined to stay in the Harijan Ashram, I think it will
be worthwhile to have some experience there. But I don't insist
on your adopting any particular course. All I desire is that you
should do what appeals to you and that, having made your choice,
you should stick to it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9403. Also C.W. 649. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
