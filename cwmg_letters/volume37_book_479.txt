479. LETTER TO E. C. DEWICK
October 31, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I had fully intended to be with you all
at the time of the Federation in December. But I see that it is
not to be. I am thoroughly washed out and I have accepted
the invitation of Jamnalalji to be in Wardha for a month just for
rest before proceeding to Calcutta during the Congress week.
Will you please forgive me?
Yours sincerely,
REV. E. C. DEWICK
5 RUSSELL STREET, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 13580
