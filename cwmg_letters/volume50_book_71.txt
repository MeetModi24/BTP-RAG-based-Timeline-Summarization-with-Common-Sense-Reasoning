71. LETTER TO LAURI SAWYER
June 19, 1932
MY DEAR LAURI,
Prof. Horace Alexander reminds me of your existence and tells
me how weak you are. Of course I remember you perfectly. Weak
in body you may be, but the very first time I met you I saw how
strong you were in will. And if God wants more service from you
in your present existence, He will give you sufficient strength of
body. For those who have faith in God, life and death are alike.
Ours is to serve till the last breath. Do write to me when you
can. Love from Mahadeo.
Yours,
BAPU
PS.
I write nothing about ourselves as you must know all there
is to know.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 232
