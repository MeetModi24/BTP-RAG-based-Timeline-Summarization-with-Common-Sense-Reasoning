57. LETTER TO ROLAND J. WILD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 14, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter enclosing the cuttings. You perhaps have
seen my comment upon them. And many things you have put
into my mouth and views you have ascribed to me I fail to recog-
nize myself, and some of them are so palpably absurd that I can-
not imagine how you came to attribute them to me. Do you
not think you owed it to me to show me the proof?
Yours sincerely,
ROLAND J. WILD, ESQ.
"THE CIVIL AND MILITARY GAZETTE"
P.O. Box No. 36, LAHORE
From a photostat: S.N. 12988
