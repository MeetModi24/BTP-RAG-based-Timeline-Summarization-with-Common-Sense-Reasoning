439. LETTER TO SHIVANAND
August 28, 1941
CHI. SHIVANAND,
You have written in good detail. Tell Fulchand not to lose
heart. Those on the death-bed have risen whereas others, [full
of life] like a juicy berry, have passed away in the space of a
yawn. We dance as the Master makes us dance and so, if in
spite of our reasonable precautions we have to depart, we should
do so with a smile on our face.
Fulchand has great work to his credit. As I was writing
this sentence, some visitors arrived.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W.2872. Courtesy: Sharda F. Shah
