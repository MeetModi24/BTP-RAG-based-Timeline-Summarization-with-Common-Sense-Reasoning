376. LETTER TO RAMANLAL SONI
October 10, 1932
On the whole I liked the poems. But I did find the language
of some of them bitter. . . . I am no good judge of poetry.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 125
1 (1897-1947); Gujarati poet and writer
2 The addressee had sent to Gandhiji a poem "Chhelli Salaam".
3 A Gujarati writer and translator
