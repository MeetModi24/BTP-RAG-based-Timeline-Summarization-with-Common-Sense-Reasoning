422. LETTER TO CHARUPRABHA SENGUPTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
February 11, 1941
DEAR SISTER,
Gandhiji has your letter. You must not offer satyagraha, and put in
your resignation also-unless the Provincial Committee people insist on your
remaining in office. If people are arrested for prosecuting the constructive
programme, they may defend themselves.
Yours sincerely,
MAHADEV DESAI
SHRIMATI CHARUPRABHA SENGUPTA
32/ BEADON STREET
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 8712
