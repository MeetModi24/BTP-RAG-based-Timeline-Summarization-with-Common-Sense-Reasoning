178. LETTER TO PURATAN J. BUCH
May 25, 1942
CHI. PURATAN,
I have your letter. You should tell him what you have told
me. If you wish me to tell him on your behalf, I can do so.
Constructive work should not produce such effect. All our activities
have swaraj as their aim. We may give them up whenever it
becomes necessary to do so. It would be better for you to discuss
your views with Naraharibhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9185
