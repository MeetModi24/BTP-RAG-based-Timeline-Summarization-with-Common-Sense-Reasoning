672. LETTER TO RAGHUVIR
SIMLA,
July 3, 1945
BHAI RAGHUVIR,
I have your letter. Thanks. It is not Hindi Prachar; it is
Hindustani Bhasha Prachar Sangh. Are not Dr. Tarachand, Dr.
Hasan and others philologists? But if you can help me, in any
way, I want your help. I am taking your dictionary with me.
I like it.
Please let me know in what way you will be able to help.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. RAGHUVIR
SARASWATI VIHAR, LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
