8. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
SEVAGRAM,
April 21, 1940
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
What Vithaldas says is correct. Even so there is no harm in
your having yourself registered as an "active" member, since only
those whom I call will have to join the struggle. I do not want
to bring out persons like you at the moment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10844. Courtesy: Purushottam K.
Jerajani
