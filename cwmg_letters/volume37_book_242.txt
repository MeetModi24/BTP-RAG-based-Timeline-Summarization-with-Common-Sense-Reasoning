242. LETTER TO WILLIAM H. DANFORTH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 24, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter as also for the parcel of your food
products sent through Mr. B. N. Birla. He sent me also a copy
of the book you have written on your Indian experience.
As my menu is limited and as I do not eat things of whose
composition I have no knowledge, I have not been able to try any
of the delicacies you have kindly sent me. But I have distributed
your biscuits amongst the Ashram inmates. If it is not a secret,
I would like to know how the corn flakes are prepared and whether
anything beyond wheat is used in preparing the flakes. Is it not the
same thing as the wheat flakes prepared at Dr. Kellogg's Battle
Creek Sanatorium?
Yours sincerely,
W. H. DANFORTH1, ESQ.
From a photostat: S.N. 14384
