35. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
June 8, 1936
CHI. MARY,
I am in no hurry. Do finish your slivers and other urgent
work before tackling Tara's box. I wrote yesterday or the day
before returning your letter to Sumitrabehn.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6063. Also C.W. 3393. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
36.
