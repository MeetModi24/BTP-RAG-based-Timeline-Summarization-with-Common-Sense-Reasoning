631. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
October 18, 1946
CHI. AMRIT,
The expected wire came after 5 p. m. yesterday. Everybody
is anxious to know the result of medical examination there.
I have been incessantly writing for Harijan. Have practically
or wholly finished. It is now 3 o'clock.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From the original : C. W. 4179. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7815
