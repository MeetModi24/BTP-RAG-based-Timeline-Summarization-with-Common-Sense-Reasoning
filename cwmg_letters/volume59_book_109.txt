109. LETTER TO JAIKRISHNA P. BHANSALI
September 29, 1934
CHI. BHANSALI,
I get your letters. I like them. I have not replied to them
because you didn't give your address. I am writing this because
now you have given it. I am very glad to learn that you
have removed the stitches from your lips. The natural thing
is to speak or remain silent as one feels an urge from within.
External means produce only a temporary effect.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
