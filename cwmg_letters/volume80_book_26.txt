26. LETTER TO HARIPRASAD
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 26, 1945
CHI. HARIPRASAD,
Panditji has given me a vivid description of you. It is
better that you go to Sabarmati to meet him. You need not
come to Sevagram. It is far off, and the fare is also more. I
will spend two months away from Sevagram. That is my plan
at the moment.
Blessings from
BAPU
