545. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
August 3, 1932
CHI. PUSHPA,
Here is the first letter of the day to you. But would not
everyone wish to have the first letter? So, we must be satisfied
with what we get. And, if we have to choose, we should choose
the worst for ourselves.
One who follows this rule will always be content.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11081. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
