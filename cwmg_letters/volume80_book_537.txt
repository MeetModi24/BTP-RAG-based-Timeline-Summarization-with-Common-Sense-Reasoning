537. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
June 13, 1945
BHAI ICE,
Chi. Hari-ichchha has taken malati. Where do you get it
from? I will pay the expenses if her father does not pay. Life
and death are not in anyone's hands. How can I therefore
complain against you? I do realize the value of your services.
I want to be sure of your knowledge. Do what you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
