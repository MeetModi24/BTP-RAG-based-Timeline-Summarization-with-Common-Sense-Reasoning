680. LETTER TO KESHAV DEVDHAR
BOMBAY,
April 19, 1945
CHI. DEVDHAR,
Your letter is very good. It is God's grace that Chi. Devendra-
nath has survived.
Prabhakar is industrious and intelligent.
Narayan has come to this world only to help others. May God
give him a long life. I do not know the other brothers. Please
convey my thanks to all of them. The civil surgeon is of course
very kind to us. I hope Devendra has fully recovered now.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KESHAV DEVDHAR
KHADI VIDYALAYA
SEVAGRAM
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
