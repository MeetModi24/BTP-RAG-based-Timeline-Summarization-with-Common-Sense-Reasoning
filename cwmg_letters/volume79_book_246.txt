246. LETTER TO LAJ RALLIA RAM
SEVAGRAM,
February 20, 1945
MY DEAR LAJ,
I was pleased to have your letter. Are you the daughter of the
Rallia Ram I know? You have made a fine choice or has Yunus?
I know about Mehrtaj.
I am glad Yunus is with Badshah Khan . I am writing to
Yunus.
Love.
BAPU
KUMARI LAJ RALLIA RAM
5 MASSON RD.
LAHORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
