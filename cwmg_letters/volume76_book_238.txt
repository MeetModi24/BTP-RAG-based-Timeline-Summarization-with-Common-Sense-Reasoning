238. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 8, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
Your letter.
Sushila comes here on 18th for a week. I think she has
come first-nothing much among five candidates. Sushila says
nothing about the place she has got.
I hope there is nothing serious with S.'s eyes.
J. L. came in yesterday. He slept in Sevagram. Maulana
comes tomorrow.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4130. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7439
