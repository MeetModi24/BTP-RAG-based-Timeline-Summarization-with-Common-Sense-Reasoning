280. LETTER TO BHATNAGAR
POONA,
August 27, 1945
DEAR BHATNAGAR,
As you see I am in Poona and shall be for some time. You
can go to Sevagram and meet Shri Ramachandran of Talimi
Sangh and show him this. If the work there pleases you and
if you suit him, he will take you. Show him this.
You must not hate the world.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI BHATNAGAR
C/O POST MASTER, LAHORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
