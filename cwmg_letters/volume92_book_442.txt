442. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
NAINITAL,
May 18, 1931
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. Khurshedbehn will certainly want to have
you, but I think it is better that you remain at the Ashram for
the present and get trained. Your mind will become steady only
when you find some quiet atmosphere at the Ashram. Take care
SV: II. 17
of your health. Profit from Premabehn's company. Abide by the
instructions of Narandas. Be sure that it will be to your benefit.
Blessings from
BAPU
