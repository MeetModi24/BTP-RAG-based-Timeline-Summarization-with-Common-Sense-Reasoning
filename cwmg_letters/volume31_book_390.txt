390. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Tuesday, Sravana Amava.rya, September 7, 1926
BHAI RAJENDRABABU,
It is only today I could read the enclosed letter. I have
also written to the student and asked him to see you.
BABU RAJENDRA PRASAD
CONGRESS OFFICE
MURADPORE
PATNA
From a photostat of the Hindi: S.N. 12272
