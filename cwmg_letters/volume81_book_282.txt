282. LETTER TO ADAM ALI
August 27, 1945
BHAI ADAM ALI,
I have your letter as also the pictures. Thanks. The
picture of the charkha is an advertisement, but that of the
Buddha is good.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
HASAN ALI DAUDBHAI
21 PERIYA MISTRI STREET
MADRAS, G.T.
