440. LETTER TO RAMADEVI CHOWDHARY
February 17, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. You have not introduced yourself nor
given your proper address. Give me a weekly account of your
work. Who are the other ladies working there and what exactly
are they doing? To what extent do the townspeople co-operate
with you?
Blessings from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SMT. RAMADEVI 1
HARIJAN SEVIKA
C/O HARIJAN SEVA SANGH
CUTTACK
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2789
