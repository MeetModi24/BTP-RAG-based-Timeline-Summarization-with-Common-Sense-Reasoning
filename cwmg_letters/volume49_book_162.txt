162. LETTER TO LAKSHMI JERAJANI
February 20, 1932
CHI. LAKSHMI,
Your handwriting is certainly good. Why don't you write
in ink? The service you are giving to Jerajani [in his illness] is
the best preparation for your future life.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. VITHALDAS JERAJANI
SASAVANE
DISTRICT ALIBAG
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9778. Also C.W. 2809. Courtesy:
Purushottam D. Saraiya
