392. LETTER TO G. N. KANITKAR
September 4, 1945
BHAI BALUKAKA,
How can anyone feel insulted if I do not see him? How
can you feel insulted?
I know my duty with regard to Satara. I am following it.
What I am doing is not for the satisfaction of anyone.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
BHAI BALUKAKA KANITKAR
341 SADASHIV
POONA
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 974. Courtesy : Gajanan Kanitkar
