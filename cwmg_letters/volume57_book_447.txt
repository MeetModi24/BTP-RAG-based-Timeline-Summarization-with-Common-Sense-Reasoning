447. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
April 23, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
I hope you have the letter I wrote to you. Your Gujarati
is indeed good. It would be excellent progress if you could make
out my letters. It is only for you that I am trying to improve
my handwriting.
It is good that you liked my decision. There is nothing more
you could do. What you have done should be enough. Why
do you divorce politics from religion?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
