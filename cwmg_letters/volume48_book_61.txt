61. LETTER TO A. FENNER BROCKWAY
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
September 29, 1931
DEAR MR. FENNER BROCKWAY,
I have your letter of the 18th inst. I already gave one message
to the Irish Free Press.
I had also the pleasure of seeing Mr. Valera's Secretary.
Will your friend Mr. Fox want a message? I am practically drain-
ed dry.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 17880
