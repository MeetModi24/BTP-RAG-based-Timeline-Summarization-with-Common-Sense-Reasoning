76. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Saturday, March 20, 1920
I should tell you that I slept only half an hour last night
and I am sure, therefore, that you will forgive me for not writ-
ing to you. I ask your forgiveness because, though I always
remember you, and wanted to write to you to share your dilemma1,
I could not do so. I shall arrive there on Tuesday morning.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi
