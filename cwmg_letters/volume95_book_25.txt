25. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
LAHORE,
[After November 5, 1919]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I have your letter. Umar has left today. At the moment I am tied
down here and cannot say when I shall be able to come. We shall
attend to the question of Hindi only when I come. The struggle may
be an intense one. Do you sometimes go to the Ashram? Chhotalal has
written a long letter. I wish you to read it.
1 From the reference to the meeting with the Governor, which took place
on October 27, 1919. Gandhiji left Lahore for Delhi on October 28, a Tuesday.
2 From the contents. Gandhiji was in Lahore form November 5 to
November 16 and then again from December 12 to December 22, 1919.
