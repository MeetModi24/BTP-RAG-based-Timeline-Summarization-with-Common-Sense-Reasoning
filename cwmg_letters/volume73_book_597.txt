597. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
April 6, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
I was afraid you will suffer in the train. Yes, you can go to
Chorwad. Harakhchandbhai's letter has been received. He is
quite glad, you could go. I am not wiring because it is Sunday
and a wire would reach you no earlier than this.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABAI
C/O SHETH JIVANLAL MOTICHAND, NASIK
From the original: C.W. 6475. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9870
