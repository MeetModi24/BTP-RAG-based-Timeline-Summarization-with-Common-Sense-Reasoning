239. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
January 28, 1933
CHI. RADHAKISAN,
I wrote one letter to you about the Mahilashram. I hope
you got it. I frequently see Jamnalal. He keeps good health.
I heard yesterday that the number of visitors to the Lakshmi-
narayan temple had gone down. Is this true? Is any record
maintained of the number of daily visitors? Find out also the
position regarding the other temples which have been thrown open
to Harijans.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3038
