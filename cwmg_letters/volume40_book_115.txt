115. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
MOULMEIN (BURMA),
March 12, 1929
I have your letter. Yes. It is quite a good idea your staying
out the summer in England watching events and holding yourself
in readiness for anything that may crop Up.
About the stirring things happening here you have the pages
of Young India which I hope you are getting regularly.
If I collapse as I did in Kolhapur be it so. I am trying to
conserve myself as much as possible, but I feel that I may not
shirk the work that has come upon me.
I am dictating this under great pressure from Moulmein.
C. F. ANDREWS, ESQ.
C/O MRS. ELMHURST
1172 PARK AVENUE
NEW YORK CITY
From a photostat: S.N. 13372
