27. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
MUSSOORIE,
October 19, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
Today's mail has most probably arrived, but I have not been
able to see it. The postal hours here are rather awkward, so that
I cannot wait till I have looked into the mail which has been
received. I return the letter received from Utkal. Kanti is leaving
today for Ahmedabad. You may learn everything from him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5463
