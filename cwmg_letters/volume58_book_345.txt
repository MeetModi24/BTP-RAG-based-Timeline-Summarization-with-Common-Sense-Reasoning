345. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHARMA
August 18, 1934
CHI. SHARMA,
Jamnalalji has been operated upon and he is in a Bombay
hospital. About Ramdas, I have wired that the proper thing may
be done. I do not feel it would be your dharma to come away
leaving Devi in this condition but I cannot insist on my view in
this matter. Every man forms his own code of conduct as a
father. What more can I write? We can continue the discussion
in person or through letters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
1932-48, facing p. 89
