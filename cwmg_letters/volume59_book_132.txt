132. LETTER TO DR. S. SUBBARAO
October 3, 1934
DEAR DR. SUBBARAO,
It was very kind of you to have sent me a letter showing
that you had issued prompt instructions to admit Shri Bhole.
I have now heard from the Medical Officer in charge of the Sana-
torium saying that he was ready to admit Shri Bhole and that
he would be treated free of charge.
Yours sincerely,
DR. S. SUBBARAO, B.A., M.B., M.R.C.S.
VISVESVARAPURAM
BANGALORE CITY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
