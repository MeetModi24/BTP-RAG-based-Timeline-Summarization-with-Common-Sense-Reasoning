326. LETTER TO FRANCISCA STANDENATH
October 5, 1932
My saying that in Europe people could not live without some
compromise was surely not meant to apply to a man like Satya-
van. But you and I must not judge him. After all even the
strongest have to compromise to an extent. No one can lay down
the law for the other.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 107
