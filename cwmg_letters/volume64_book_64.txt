64. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI N. PATEL
November 19, 1936
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I have your letter. I wish your Conference success. The
yardstick of success is not resolutions however grand but firm
follow-up action on the part of the youth. They should not
pass any resolution which they are not prepared to implement.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAOJIBHAI NATHABHAI PATEL
PATIDAR YUVAK MANDAL
SEVA MANDIR
NADIAD, B . B . & C.I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9009
