307. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
Sravana Sud 10 [August 10, 1924]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Please see the letter I have written to Motilalji. I have asked
Krishnadas to send a copy of it to you. Govind Babu is working
in Orissa. You may inspect his work and, if you approve of it,
give him monetary assistance from the Gandhi Seva Sangh funds.
His ability is very limited, though his demand is great, viz.,
Rs. 200/- a month. Of course, this much is not to be given. If
he passes your test, you may give him Rs. 50/- a month. Examine
his case carefully.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: G.N. 2848
