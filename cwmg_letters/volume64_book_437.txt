437. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
March 8, 1937
CHI. PRABHA,
I duly received your letter yesterday. Why have you given
no description of the journey? If you don't worry, your health
is bound to remain good. Take hip-baths. Eat garlic. Procure
fresh milk. Take ghee and butter in sufficient quantity. And
eat whatever fruit you can get. Rajkumari left this morning. She
was reluctant to go. She will stay for four days in Delhi. Amtul
Salaam has gone to Indore. She left on Tuesday. The house has
become empty now. Khan Saheb also is not here. And there is
plenty of milk. Everybody is fine. Bhansali is still in hospital.
He is better now.
Blessings from
BAPU
