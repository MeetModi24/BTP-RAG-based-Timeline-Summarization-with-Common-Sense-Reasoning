440. LETTER TO HARIBHAU PHATAK
March 22, 1935
MY DEAR HARIBHAU,
Your letter of 10th came into my hands only yesterday.
Mahadev had gone to Calcutta and some correspondence remained
unattended to.
What is the date of the exhibition? In any case, if anybody
there wants to attain the speed on the takli, he should be sent
here for a week. Mere demonstration for a day or two in Poona
won't serve the purpose you have in mind.
What about rice? We are preparing unpolished rice ourselves.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1370
