373. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
September 3, 1945
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
The letter to Bapa could not be written earlier. I wrote it
this morning and enclose it herewith. You will pass it on to him,
if you approve of the contents. I showed it to Sardar. He has
approved of it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 4805. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
