70. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
ON THE TRAIN,
Monday night [Before January 10, 1927]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have received your letter. Manilal informs me that you are not
keeping as well as you should. Why is it so? If the weather there does
not suit you, go to Wardha and stay there. You may go to Bihar also.
It would be better if you got the question of the emerald solved
through Jamnabehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9312
