433. LETTER TO RUKMINIDEVI BAZAJ
December 31, 1933
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your postcard. You do not seem to have received the
postcard I wrote to you on my way here. I have had no
letters from Santok for some time. You did well in meeting
Jamnalalji. Have you learnt the Marwari speech? I am sure
you have acquired perfect command over Hindi now. Besides
Om, Kisanbehn is accompanying me. Has Om written to you
about her? She is a friend of Premabehn's. I have caught cold.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
