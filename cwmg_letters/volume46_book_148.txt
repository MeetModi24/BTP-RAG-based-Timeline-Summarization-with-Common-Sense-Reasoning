148. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 11, 1931
CHI. NARANDAS,
I am writing this letter at midnight at Baroda station. Champa
came and saw me here. I think Ratilal will not come at present.
I shall be in Simla for about five days; and then in Nainital for
five days. I expect to return to Borsad round about the 25th.
Buy the land at Bidaj. I was sorry to hear about Hanuman-
singh. Can you tell me how his condition became what it is?
Keep him mostly on fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U/I
