428. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 19, 1939
DEAR SISTER,
I have already given you the blessing that your operation
may be successful. I satisfy myself by seeking news of you from
anyone who comes and knows you. You know how much I respect
you. But this letter is to convey my good wishes to Ambalal-
bhai. Your operation will certainly go off without any trouble.
I am even praying that both of you should always be happy
and go on doing public service. There is no need to answer this
letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11148. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foun-
dation
