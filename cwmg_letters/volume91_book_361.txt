361. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Phagun Sud 6 [February 28, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I shall be reaching there on Thursday morning by the Mail.
I shall leave for Madras on the same day. Tell Shuaib that I
shall reach there on Thursday. I do not think anything can be
done about the Hindu-Muslim question for the present. I sense
from your letter that you wish to talk about a lot of things. But
it seems that I can have time for that only if you accompany me
on one of my tours. This time Mahadev and Ramdas are with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Tell Mother that I constantly think of her. I am going to
the Ashram tomorrow morning. I shall leave the Ashram on
Wednesday by the Mail.
CHI. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
93 BAZAR GATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
