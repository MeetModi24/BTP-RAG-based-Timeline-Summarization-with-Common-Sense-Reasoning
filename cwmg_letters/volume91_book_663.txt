663. LETTER TO AMBALAL SARABHAI
August 21, 1928
DEAR BROTHER,
Your letter is beautiful. Your sentiments are even more
beautiful. I had been waiting for the doctor and the injection,
having abandoned the idea of using a gun. Your letter has made
me check myself. I will not now make use of your gun. I do
not share your fear. The question is deeper than the one you
have raised. I shall not discuss it here beyond saying that it is
not merely one of relieving pain. What is our duty towards
animals and other living creatures that are disabled and suffering
pain? Where does compassion which underlies Hinduism take
us? But to me that is not a matter for discussion. It concerns
my deepest feelings. I shall take no step without proper thought.
The act of killing has to be postponed at least for today.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
