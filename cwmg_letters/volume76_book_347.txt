347. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 17, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
Your letter.
The A. I. C. C. takes place in Bombay. You may come if
you wish. I hope to be there on 4th or may be even 3rd.
P. is down with fever—103. Something wrong with the
bladder.
Asaf Ali went because he was indisposed.
But he is against
the resolution. The Maulana's statement you must have seen.
The resolution is my draft. Changes have been made for
J. L.'s satisfaction, and of course Maulana's.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3691. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6500
