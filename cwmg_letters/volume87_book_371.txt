371. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
NEW DELHI,
May 2, 1947
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I have all your letters. I am unable to write but the work
I am doing is the same.
Amtul Salaam is unhappy. Please satisfy her if you can.
I have written a letter to her. Please read it and pass it on
to her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9080
