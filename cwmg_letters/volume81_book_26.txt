26. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
July 22, 1945
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Chi. Sushila (Nayyar) is leaving today. If an operation
becomes absolutely necessary, get it done. If it is decided to
keep you under observation for two or three months, I am in
favour of your staying at Dinshaw's. If you decide to go, I also
will be ready to go there. You may write, or dictate, whatever
else you wish to.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
68 MARINE DRIVE
BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- : Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 280
