305. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
April 10, 1930
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. If you have self-confidence, come out
for some voluntary service. They need not hinder you. I have
written to Father. He will never oppose this. It will be good if
you can gain some experience in the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy : National Archives of
India and Anand T. Hingorani
