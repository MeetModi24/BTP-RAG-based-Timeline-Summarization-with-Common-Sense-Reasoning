103. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 28, 1936
DEAR CHARLIE,
I am glad you were able to carry Isudas with you to a cer-
tain extent. Of course there can't be any question of shuddhi.
You must get rid of this tiredness. It means that you need
both mental and physical rest.
If it can be had by delaying
departure by a week and taking rest at Wardha or Ahmedabad,
you must take it unless you will be too late for your first lecture.
You won't put an undue strain on yourself. Love to Gurudev.
Love.
MOHAN
From a photostat: G.N. 992
