14. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
SEVAGRAM,
April 21, 1941
CHI. RUKMINI 1 ,
I got your letter just now.
Indubehn is sitting beside me.
She knew him His ship had sunk, but he was in a small boat for
a few days and finally reached Scotland. From there he took
another ship and Miss Harrison had heard that he had reached
South Africa. After that Indubehn does not know anything. Let
us hope he will arrive safely. Remain calm, all of you.
Ba is still in Delhi.
Blessings to both 4 from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 10129. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
