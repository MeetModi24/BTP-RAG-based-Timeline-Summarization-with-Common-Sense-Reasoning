434. LETTER TO V. J. PATEL
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
B. B. C. I. RLY.,
June 1, 1928
BHAISHRI VITHALBHAI,
I have your letter. I have not yet actually received that
letter of yours but I read it in the newspapers. It will produce a
good impression. What shall we say or do about Harilal Desai?
I am writing to Mahadev at Bardoli to send you a copy of
Vallabhbhai's letter. We must keep on doing our work. You are
doing your part very well. Is not God Himself Karta-harta.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 14436
