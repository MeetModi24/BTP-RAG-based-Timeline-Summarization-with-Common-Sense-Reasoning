332. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
August 16, 1934
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have just received the wire about the operation. A heavy
load of anxiety is off Janakimaiya's head. Do not worry about
me. I am quite well. I can eat. I won't run up there in undue
haste. I won't go anywhere else either, till I have regained full
strength. Don't worry, therefore, and get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati; G.N. 2938
