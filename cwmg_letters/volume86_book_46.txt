46. LETTER TO DEVPRAKASH NAYYAR
October 25, 1946
CHI. DEV,
It is not required of you to go to Bengal just now. I did
wish you to be with me during the holidays. My visit to Ben-
gal has not yet been finalized. Of course, my heart is there.
Here also there has been enough work. There is no need to
decide anything in haste. Enough if you know that the work
you have taken up there is not quite simple.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy; National Archives of India
