241. LETTER TO JANG BAHADUR SINGH
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 1, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter and the cutting. In my opinion it would
be much better for you to see Lalaji and Pandit Santanam and
make a public appeal in co-operation with them. I am quite
certain that the institution should live but how to do it depends
upon local circumstances. People would undoubtedly want to
know what Lalaji and Pandit Santanam have to say.
Yours sincerely,
JANG BAHADUR SINGH, ESQ.
EDITOR, "NATION"
RAILWAY ROAD
LAHORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 19405
