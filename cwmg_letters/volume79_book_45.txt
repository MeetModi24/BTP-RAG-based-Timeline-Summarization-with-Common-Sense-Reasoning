45. LETTER TO ABDUL MAJID KHAN
SEVAGRAM,
January 12, 1945
MY DEAR PROFESSOR,
Fortunately you have sent me a copy of my previous message.
I cannot improve upon it. You should boldly stand and
win. They will choose you for your work as a man.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PROF. ABDUL MAJID KHAN
LAHORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
