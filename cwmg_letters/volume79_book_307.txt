307. LETTER TO SITARAM
SEVAGRAM,
February 28, 1945
BHAI SITARAM,
I have received your money. Your sending the money in
itself carries my blessings, does it not? If you still require them
you have them. I had hoped that you would come and see me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
