51. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
WARDHA,
July 15, 1935
DEAR PLAYMATE,
How stupid of you not to have broken your journey because
I was silent. You did not need to hear my voice. And you could
have made sure yourself that I was not ill. I expect better be-
haviour next time.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
How is the Shuaib family?
MISS PADMAJA NAIDU
C/O SHUAIB QURESHI
PRIME MINISTER
BHOPAL
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
