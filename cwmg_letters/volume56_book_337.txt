337. LETTER TO KISAN GHUMATKAR
December 9, 1933
MY DEAR KISAN,
I had your letter today in the train. Unless you have already
left, I suggest your joining the Madras Mail at Wardha on 15th
instant. I take the Grand Trunk Mail at Delhi on 14th inst. I
am glad you are strong both in body and mind.
Love.
BAPU
SHRIMATI KISANBEHN
GANESH TERRACE
GIRGAUM BACK RD.
BOMBAY
C.W. 9689
