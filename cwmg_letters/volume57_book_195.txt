195. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
February 20, 1934
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
What is this? Why only a few words on a postcard? Why
have you not given me all the news? How much loss has been in-
curred there? What is the State doing in the matter? Give me
all details. I shall be leaving for Patna in a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6545
