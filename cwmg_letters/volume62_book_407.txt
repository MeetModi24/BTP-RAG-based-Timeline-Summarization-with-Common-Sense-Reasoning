407. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
April 30, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter. Sardar is taking a different route.
Arrange to send Kusum to Wardha. She should
be
here
before the 8th as we are leaving on that day. She should bring
warm clothing and blankets, but not superfluous luggage.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am spending the day in Segaon, where I hope to settle
down.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8488. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
