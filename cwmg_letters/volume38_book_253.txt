253. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
Wednesday, December 12, 1928
CHI. MAHADEV,
I understand your difficulty. We cannot suspend publication
of the papers for a week. I am preparing from now on. I, there-
fore, don't think that it will be difficult to publish them. I
count on three columns by you. May you win success after
success there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11437
