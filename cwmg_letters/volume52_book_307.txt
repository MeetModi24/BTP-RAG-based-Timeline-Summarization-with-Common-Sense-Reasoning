307. LETTER TO SECRETARY, ARYA SAMAJ, BOMBAY
December 19, 1932
I was pained to see the published report of our interview. I
do not carry a copy of the Satyarthaprakash with me. I only said
that there were several copies of it in the Ashram library. I did
not ask for the books, but Mahadev did.
When you offered your help to the temple-entry movement,
I advised you not to interfere with it and you also accepted my
advice. But the report suggests that I desired your help. The
cause will suffer through such misrepresentations. I, therefore,
consider it necessary that for the sake of truth and the cause you
should immediately publish a correction to the report. I should
like you to publish it as soon as you can. A false report can
never help a cause, and it cannot but harm dharma. A correc-
tion, therefore, is desirable from every point of view.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, pp. 354-
