109. LETTER TO S. P. KAMAT
November 6, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I know that some khadi bhandars have been having their
khadi dyed and bleached through mills, if not with the permission,
certainly with the connivance of the A. I. S. A. I do not propose
to interfere with them all of a sudden, but your objection is
sound. I shall see what is practicable in view of the fact that the
custom of having khadi dyed and bleached through mills has been
going on for some years.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. S. P. KAMAT
ANKOLA (NORTH KANARA DIST.)
From a photostat: G.N. 5680
