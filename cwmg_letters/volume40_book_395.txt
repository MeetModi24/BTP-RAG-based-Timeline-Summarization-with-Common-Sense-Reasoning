395. LETTER TO D.
May 27, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I am extremely dissatisfied with your letters. You are hyste-
rical, easily excited, unstable and revengeful. You should cease
to think about the girl. It would be improper for you to advertise
the connection to the world. It must be enough for you to plead
your suit before the girl's father. After all, you should recognize
your own limitations. Would a father willingly give his daughter
to a cripple? The girl has a perfect right to choose you as her part-
ner if she wishes. But a girl who does so must be far above the
average, and if this girl is, she will overcome her fears and all other
difficulties. She is in no need of your protection. It is you who
stand in need of protection from her. You are forgetting your limi-
tations and denying your philosophy.
From the original: C.W. 6169. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
