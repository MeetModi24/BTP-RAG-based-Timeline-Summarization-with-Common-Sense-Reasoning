449. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
SEVAGRAM,
March 18, 1945
BHAI MUNSHI,
I read the whole of your book on the deadlock too. I
liked it. My basic opposition remains. But you have given the
reader quite a lot of material to think about.
I hope Sarala and her daughter are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 7686. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
