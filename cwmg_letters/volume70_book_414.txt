414. LETTER TO KANCHANBEHN M. SHAH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 16, 1939
CHI. KANCHAN,
I got your two letters. They did not call for any reply. Go
to Ramana Ashram only if you feel like going. Recently there
has been no news from Munnalal. But there is nothing to worry.
Improve your health. I am going to Allahabad for some three
days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8288. Also C.W. 7064. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
