57. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
July 25, 1945
BHAI (DR.) KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your letter. Chi. Sailen has come here. He is well.
More about him later. Do whatever you can for Mama. The
result is in God's hands. Please tell Mama that it will be good
if he observes the rules of the place where he stays.
Blessings from
BAPU
MALAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
