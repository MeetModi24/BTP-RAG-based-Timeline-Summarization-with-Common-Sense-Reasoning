241. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
February 8, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Your letter is good. Yes, body-labour constitutes one of our
VOWS 2 .
Its significance cannot be over-emphasized. Give to Chi-
manlal whatever time he needs. Hindi teaching should come only
after that. Massage, etc., do constitute body-labour. It may be
undertaken.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4337
1 A member of the Gandhi Seva Sangh
2 Namely, non-violence, truth, non-stealing, brahmacharya, non-possession,
body-labour, control of the palate, fearlessness, equality of religions, swadeshi
and 'touchability'
