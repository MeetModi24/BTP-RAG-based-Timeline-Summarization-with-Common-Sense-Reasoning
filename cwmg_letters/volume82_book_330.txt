330. LETTER TO GULBAI D. MEHTA
SODEPUR,
December 18, 1945
CHI. GULBAI,
You must now be due for confinement. How are you? You
are calm, aren't you? Write to me whatever you like. You are
not going to fight shy of me. How is Mother?
How is Ardeshir1?
Blessings from
BAPU
