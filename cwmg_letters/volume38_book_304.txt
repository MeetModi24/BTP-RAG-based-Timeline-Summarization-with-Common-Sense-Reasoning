304. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
[Before December 18, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
Your letter. Your presence there spares me any anxiety on
Kusum's account. Rajendra Babu came yesterday. I hope a
telegram has been sent cancelling the Dwarka trip. There is no
time now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3315
