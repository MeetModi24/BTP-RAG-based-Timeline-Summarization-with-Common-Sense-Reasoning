224. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
ON WAY BACK FROM SIRAJGANJ,
Friday [July 10, 1925]
CHI. MAHADEV,
The train is jolting very much.
I am writing this with nothing particular to say. I may have
to despatch directly from here the matter for Young India. It is
not yet ready. It will, therefore, be sent perhaps by the Assam
Mail. Take the Trust-deed and keep it with you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
The collection here will be about three thousand.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 11431
