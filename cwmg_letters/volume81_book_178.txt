178. LETTER TO HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
August 12, 1945
BHAI MAHTAB 2 ,
How are you? You did not even mention in your letter
where you were writing from. It is not that the khadi produced
in Orissa cannot go out of the province. However, it is true that
only a small quantity will be sent out. Be assured that true well-
being of Orissa lies only in that. It should not be necessary to
write down everything that I have written to you. Understand
the new scheme and carry it out.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI H . MAHTAB, M.L.A.
CUTTACK (ORISSA)
