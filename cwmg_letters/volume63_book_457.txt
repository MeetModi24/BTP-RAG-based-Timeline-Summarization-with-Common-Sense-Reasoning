457. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
October 6, 1936
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
I am enclosing with this the reply to your complaint. For
my part, I fully believe Chhaganlal's denial, and my impression
is that Narandas too believes it. Anyway he does not support
you. Now it remains for you either to prove your charges
against him or cleanse your own mind in the matter. Chhaganlal
has his faults but, I trust, he is free from the ones you attribute
to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./III
