89. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
ON THE TRAIN,
March 30, 1920
MY DEAR CHILD,
I have just received your letter for which I thanked God. I am
from a resting place going to the Ashram. After leaving Delhi, I
have not been able to write to you. I had four days' good rest. I hope
to go back after the 13th April. When are you going to Denmark?
With love,
BAPU
From a photostat of the original in N.A.I.; also My Dear Child, p. 68
