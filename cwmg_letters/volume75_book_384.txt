384. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
February 15, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
Your letter. I expect you have the letter I wrote yester-
day. Everything is all right. Ba is well. I am not taking on
any great burden.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
C/O PRINCIPAL SECRETARY
INDORE
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4260. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N.
7892
