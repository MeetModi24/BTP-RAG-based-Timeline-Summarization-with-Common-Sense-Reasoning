35. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 15, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
The last two packets from the Ashram contained no letter
from you. Since as a rule you write regularly, the absence of a
letter from you worries me. How is Father? What is Jayaprakash
engaged in at present? I am all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3390
