100. LETTER TO J. KITCHIN
HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON, W.C.,
November 5, 1906
DEAR SIR,
Your brother and my friend, Mr. H. Kitchin, has written to me
sending your address. He is anxious, and so am I, that I should make
your acquaintance during my brief stay in London, and I shall thank
you if you will kindly make an appointment.
I shall be exceedingly busy this week with reference to the
Deputation that is to wait upon Lord Elgin. Will you therefore kindly
make an appointment for some time next week?
Yours truly,
JOSEPH KITCHIN, ESQ.
"INGLENOOK"
BRACKLEY ROAD
BECKENHAM
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4484
