376. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
March 9, 1945
CHI. SUMI,
Here are a couple of lines for your perseverance. It is good
you passed. If the bangles fit and you want to wear them, you may
do so. Don't leave anything from fear of me. Sacrifice without
renunciation cannot endure. You should only give up things for
which you no longer feel attachment.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. SUMITRA GANDHI
C/O HEAD MISTRESS
BIRLA HIGH SCHOOL
PILANI, RAJPUTANA
From the Gujarati original: Sumitra Gandhi Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
