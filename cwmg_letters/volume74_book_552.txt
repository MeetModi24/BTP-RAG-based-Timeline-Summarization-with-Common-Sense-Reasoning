552. LETTER TO VIJAYA M. PANCHOLI
September 21, 1941
CHI. VIJAYA,
You are very greedy. You yourself write only a couple of
lines and ask for an "immediate" reply. Is not this one immedi-
ate enough? I got your postcard only just now. I had your
reply in which you had given a full description.
I remember
to have replied to it also. I hope you will come over soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7141. Also C.W. 4633. Courtesy:
Vijaya M. Pancholi
