393. LETTER TO BHOLE
March 10, 1935
MY DEAR BHOLE,
Here is a copy of Dr. Jayaram's letter just received. I have
had your letters regularly but I was waiting for Dr. Jayaram's
letter before writing to you. It is quite clear now that you should
withdraw and proceed to Poona or remain for some time with Sjt.
Ramachandran at Bangalore.
SJT. BHOLE
PRINCESS KRISHNAJAMMANNI
TUBERCULOSIS SANATORIUM, MYSORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
