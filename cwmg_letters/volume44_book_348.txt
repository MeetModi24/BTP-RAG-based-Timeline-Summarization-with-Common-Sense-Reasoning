348. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI PANDYA
October 24, 1930
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
You ought to understand that one whose [own] mind is not
integrated cannot weigh the merit or demerit of others. I for one
know Giriraj well. He has lofty ideas but his strength to live
up to them is very inadequate. Yet I have hopes for him because
he is always striving. The aim of the Ashram is to insist on truth
and on conduct conforming to truth. Everything is organized with
Truth as the focal point. There are not many ideals in the
world and there ought not to be. The apparent manifold is only
a golden lid hiding the Truth. When it is removed we will see the
One alone.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 328. Courtesy: Bhagwanji Purushottam
Pandya
