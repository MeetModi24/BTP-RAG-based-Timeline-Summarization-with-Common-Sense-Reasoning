180. LETTER TO PARACHURE SHASTRI
August 12, 1945
BHAI PARACHURE SHASTRI,
I have your letter. Give the song to Prabhakar. It is a well
known fact that the word chakra is to be found in the Vedas and
other texts; also that the word charkha is derived from chakra.
I have understood the second point.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
