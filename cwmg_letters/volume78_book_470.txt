470. LETTER TO KHWAJA A. HAMID
SEVAGRAM,
November 23, 1944
BHAI HAMID,
Why in English? I can read Urdu written in a clear hand.
I can also write it but with some difficulty. I am writing to you
in Hindi hoping that you can read the language. I have gone
through your letter thoroughly. What have I to discuss with the
nationalist [Muslims]? I can meet the nationalists only if I can
bring something from Jinnah Saheb. I did not go to him to
surrender the rights of the nationalists. Our duty is to reach,
if possible, an agreement with the League, is it not?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
KHWAJA A. HAMID
BYCULLA
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
