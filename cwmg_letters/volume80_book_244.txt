244. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 15, 1945
CHI. AMRITA LAL,
Dhiren has arrived today with Satis Babu. It appears from
your letter of the 8th that Bhai Bhimani wanted to send informa-
tion about the women. If so, let me say that I do not regard
him as a reliable person to give such information. He can send
me written evidence even from there. I have not forgotten what
you said. But of what use can it be if it is not fully supported?
I have arranged to send Sailen to Malad. I hope he will
do as Romen has written.
Blessings from
BAPU

Chatterjee
