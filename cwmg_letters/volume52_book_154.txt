154. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
December 4, 1932
CHI. MANILAL,
You will find with this a letter from Pragji. I hope you were
not upset by my short fast. A fast by me has become a common
occurrence and so nobody should get upset by it. I hope you
have adhered to your decision to leave.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4802
