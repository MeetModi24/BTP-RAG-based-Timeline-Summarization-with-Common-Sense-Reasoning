284. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
NEW DELHI,
July 7, 1947
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
I have your letter. I know I have not been able to write to
you often. How can it be anger? I am buried in work.
Pakistan has come into being. But our duty remains the same.
Those who are there have to do or die.
I want to reach there early. When it will be only God
knows.
Whatever rice we get we should accept. We should not carry
on trade in it. I consider it immoral. I cannot go into greater
details.
How are you?
Abha came here yesterday. She does not appear to be
well. Today is my silence day.
Blessings from
BAPU
