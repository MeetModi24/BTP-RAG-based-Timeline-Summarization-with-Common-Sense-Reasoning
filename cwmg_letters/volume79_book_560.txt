560. LETTER TO MADHAVENDRAPRASAD SINGH
SEVAGRAM,
March 29, 1945
CHI. KUMAR MADHAVENDRA,
My blessings are always with you in your good work. I may
say I am well enough.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUNWAR SHRI MADHAVENDRAPRASAD SINGH
VARANVA, KARAGHANA
DIST. ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
