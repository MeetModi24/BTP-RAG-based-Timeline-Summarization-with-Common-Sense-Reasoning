332. LETTER TO KHAGENDRA PRIYA BARUANI
October 6, 1932
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter and khadi piece and socks. I thank you
for them. I shall use them as may be required. The fine piece
you sent in April was also received. It still covers me daily. I
did acknowledge it on 15th April. I am surprised you did not
get my postcard.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SMT. KHAGENDRA PRIYA BARUA
NOWGONG
ASSAM
From a photostat: C.W. 9552
