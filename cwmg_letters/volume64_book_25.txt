25. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
November 9, 1936
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I just got your card at 7.15. I also had the note sent from
Maganwadi. I shall see what to do about the honey and the
bottle. I should have given you khakharis for the journey. You
should increase your diet gradually. I think your weight is all
right and for the present you should go up by at least 2 lb. per
week. I hope you are getting fairly good fruit there.
Things here go on well. I allowed Mirabehn to have four
khakharis this evening.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI AMRITLAL NANAVATI
BHADARAN BHAVAN
MALAD, B.B. & C.I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10722
