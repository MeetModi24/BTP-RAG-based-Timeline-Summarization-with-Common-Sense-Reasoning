106. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 18, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
What is the news about Dhiru ? Who meets his expenses?
How is he progressing? When will he complete his study? Do
you have what he gave in writing?
How is Harilal?
Everything has been settled about the school. Everybody
agrees that your scheme is all right.
How is Purushottam ? Does Chorwad suit him?
Did I write to Jamna that I had written a letter to Vijaya ?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I shall be in Borsad from 23rd to 31st May.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II Also C.W. 8444. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
