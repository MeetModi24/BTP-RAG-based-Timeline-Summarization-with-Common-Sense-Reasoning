41. LETTER TO LALJI PARMAR
BORSAD,
June 22, 1931
CHI. LALJI,
So I have a letter from you at last. Do not be so lazy
again. Write to your father and tell him plainly that you do not
wish even your engagement to take place just now. Improve
your handwriting. Write to me regularly every week. How much
do you weave daily now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3293
