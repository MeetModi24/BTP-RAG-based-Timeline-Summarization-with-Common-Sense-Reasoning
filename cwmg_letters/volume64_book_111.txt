111. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
November 29, 1936
BHAI BAPA,
I have got your letter regarding Govind Babu. I have brought
him up as my son. You do know of his previous misadventure. I
asked Baba Raghavdas to make an inquiry. He too gave an un-
satisfactory report.
I did not know at all that he wanted to
become a member of Parliament. All those to whom he had gone
know him as my man. I took action when the complaint
came from Bombay. For my part I do desire that he should
hand over possession of the Ashram. As you share the unhappi-
ness of everybody around, I must write to you.
Mustn't I?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI THAKKAR BAPA
HARIJAN NIVAS
KINGSWAY, DELHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1172
