501. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 20, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
I am sending today a good deal of material for Harijan.
If you have not sent a wire to Kher, send one as follows—and
if you have already sent, send a revision: "Received yours and
Gulzarilal's letter. His objection seems unsurmountable. Take all
assistance from him. Suggest keeping office vacant taking labour
portfolio yourself. Bapu."
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Prepare an envelope for Kallenbach there. I don't remember
the address.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11534
