522. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
September 30, 1945
CHI. BHANSALI,
How much money will be required for the boys you refer
to? Will the Talimi Sangh admit them? How old are they? I do
not think there will be any difficulty in meeting their expenses.
You must have inquired at the Mahila Ashram about the
girl.
I hope you eat regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8358. Also C.W. 7190. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
