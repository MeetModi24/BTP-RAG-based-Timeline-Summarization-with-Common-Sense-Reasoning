451. LETTER TO DR. N. R. DHARMAVIR
September 5, 1934
DEAR DR. DHARMAVIR 2 ,
I thank you for your letter. If I could only allow myself
lemon-juice, I know there would be no difficulty in drinking
sufficient quantity of water with lemon-juice added. But my
fasts do not admit of the introduction of lemon-juice, for however
low it may be it has a food value apart from such value as water
and salts have. The problem before me, therefore, is somewhat
difficult-how to overcome the disagreeableness of water without
taking anything with it except soda or salt ?
Yours sincerely,
DR. N. R. DHARMAVIR
PADIHAM GROVE
DAVIS ROAD, LAHORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
