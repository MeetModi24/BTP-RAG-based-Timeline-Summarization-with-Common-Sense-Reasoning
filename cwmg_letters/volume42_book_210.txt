210. LETTER TO NANDKISHORI
PRAYAG,
Silence Day [November 18, 1929]
CHI. NANDKISHORI,
You have asked a pertinent question. Whether one's love
is pure or not can be judged only by its manifestation. The love
of a mother indulging her offspring with luxuries proceeds from
ignorance. That wife's love is selfish who is reluctant to stay
away from her husband even in the interest of his dharma. We
can note innumerable such instances.
Those people who do not wish to observe the rules have no
place in the Ashram. But the others, who are making honest
though unsuccessful efforts, do have and should have a place.
In the Ashram we do not follow the policy of punishment for
transgression of the rules or for other lapses.
May you ever pass your life in such a thoughtful manner.
Blessings from
BAPU
