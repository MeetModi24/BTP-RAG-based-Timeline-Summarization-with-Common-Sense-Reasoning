95. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
Silence Day [July 4, 1927]
CHI. MIRA,
I have sent the Monday letter to Sabarmati. But if you leave
for Wardha before getting it, you should not miss your Mon-
day mail at Wardha either; hence this epistle.
I had expected your letter today but it has not come; it must
tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Don't worry. The letter addressed to Sabarmati is in Eng-
lish.
[PPS.]
I have just had your Hindi letter. It is beautiful.
From the Hindi original: C.W. 5242. Courtesy: Mirabehn
