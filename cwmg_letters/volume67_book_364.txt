364. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
August 17, 1938
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I take it that you are daily writing to Bal. I was careless
about writing to him regularly. Tell Kaka not to start thinking
about the future from now. We shall think about everything
after he has completely recovered.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Pyarelal is not quite well today. However, there is no cause
for worry.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10760
