354. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Maha Sud 11 [February 4, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Vithalbhai is not giving the lunch. I had asked you to fol-
low the instructions of the Party. You need not go for the lunch.
I shall explain further later.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
93 BAZAR GATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
