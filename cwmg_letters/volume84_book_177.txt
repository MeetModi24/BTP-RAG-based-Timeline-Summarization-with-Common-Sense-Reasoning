177. LETTER TO M. S. ANEY
SIMLA,
May 12, 1946
BHAI BAPUJI ANEY1,
I have read your pamphlet with great relish and am glad
to note your keen love for Sanskrit. Things are getting along
fairly well here.
BAPU
SHRI M. S. ANEY, AGENT, GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
11 STANMORE CRESELEY
HAVELOCK TOWN
COLOMBO, CEYLON
