619. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
[After April 10, 1941]
CHI. KAKA,
Follow Koyaji's advice in regard to diet. Otherwise you will
not be fair to him. If you get a secretary, you may go out slowly.
It is good that Raihana is there now.
I understand about Sarvodaya.
Get well soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10948
1 The letter was in reply to the one dated April 10 from the addressee.
2 & 3 The letter is written on the one dated April 10 from the addressee.
4 Dr. Koyaji, a physician of Bombay
