318. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 17, 1930
CHI. SHARDA,
Your letter. But how did you fall ill? Was there any
mistake about your diet? As far as your asthma is concerned
you must treat it so ruthlessly that it does not visit you again.
You will succeed if you are firm. Do you take sun-bath? Do
you massage your chest? Do you keep your bowels clean? After
the morning prayers you must do deep-breathing on an empty
stomach. This would clear the lungs. We must take deep breaths
so that the oxygen may clear our lungs. You must understand
this point from Chimanlal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9892. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
wala
