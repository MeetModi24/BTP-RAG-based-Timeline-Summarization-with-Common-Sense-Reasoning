422. LETTER TO R. R. CHAKRAVARTTI
April 11, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the cutting you have sent
me, and it made me glad to find that you were already doing
all you could to remove the curse of untouchability.
I would not have you to infer from my defence of Hardayal-
babu that I would write exactly as he did.
I hope you saw my
article in the Harijan on Brahmins. That explains my own
personal position. I am wholly averse to a crusade against Brah-
mins or any particular class. I fear that we are all tarred with
the same brush so far as this monster of untouchability is con-
cerned.
Yours sincerely,
