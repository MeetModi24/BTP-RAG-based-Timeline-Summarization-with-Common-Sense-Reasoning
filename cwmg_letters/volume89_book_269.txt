269. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
September 29, 1947
CHI. MANI,
Herewith a letter from Narandas Gandhi. I have wired to
him not to take action till he hears from me. But ask Sardar
and let me know what should be done.
I also enclose Pattani's wire. You may have received an
identical wire there. What should be done about it? I am
under the impression that whatever Shamaldas does is done with
the Sardar's consent. Please ask the Sardar what reply I should
send and let me know.
Send back both the enclosures.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 144
