413. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 14, 1940
CHI. NARANDAS,
What is the correct position regarding this1? Behcharbhai was
saying that Gokibehn used to get the amount regularly. If there
has been any change please let me know. The amount should of
course be sent to her. Show her this letter. Since when has
the remittance been discontinued?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W.8572. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
