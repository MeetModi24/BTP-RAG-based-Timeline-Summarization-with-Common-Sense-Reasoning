6. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
SEVAGRAM,
April 19, 1940
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
If you want to bind all letters into a book, use paper of uni-
form size.
You did well in writing the letter. It was your duty. After
writing it, your duty is over and mine begins. I have started
investigating what you mention. There seems to be a difference
between what is actually happening and your understanding of
it. I put up with many things. Yes, it is true that I trust Parner-
kar's scientific knowledge. I also trust your practical knowledge.
You will gain more strength from what you are doing now.
I shall see about Krishnachandra.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1933
