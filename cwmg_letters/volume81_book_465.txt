465. LETTER TO ANUGRAH NARAYAN SINGH
September 17, 1945
BHAI ANUGRAHBABU,
I have your letter. I will use the medicine when it comes.
If the medicine is so effective you must say how it is made.
It is dharma not to make a business out of it.
Blessings from
BAPU
ANUGRAH NARAYAN SINGH
KADAM KUAN
PATNA, BIHAR
