12. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI THAKKAR
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 6, 1930
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
I got your letter. I was happy to learn that all the women
were doing their work well. Please tell Ramabehn that I got her
letter. I am not writing to her this time. The women to whom
I don't write must not think that I have forgotten them. I write
to as many as I can. But when I write to one, the letter should be
regarded as meant for all. I note what you say about the chil-
dren. I was pained by their going away. I get news about
Madhavji from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
