458. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
October 6, 1936
CHI. SHARMA,
I have two patients on my own hands. They are both having
fever. The temperature of one of them has now been below 99°
for the last eight days and the other patient is at Simla. What
will you do about such patients? Anyway there are many
other patients as well. I hope everyone at home is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
facing p. 260
