587. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
April 3, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I heard about the meeting held in Sevagram. My criticism is
well-founded. We cannot ignore anything.
Yes, if you find any suitable village, you may stay there. But
just now you should fulfil at least one resolution. You will then
have taken one step. Your latest resolution was good. Take all the
khadi examinations.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8510. Also C.W. 7124. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
