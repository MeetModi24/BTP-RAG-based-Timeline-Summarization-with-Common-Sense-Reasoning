5. LETTER TO MANGALDAS PAKVASA
NEW DELHI,
October 21, 1946
BHAI MANGALDAS PAKVASA 3 ,
I have your letter. I do not like your falling ill again and
agam. There is no hurry about the Trust work. Do it at your
leisure.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 4693. Courtesy: Mangaldas
Pakvasa
