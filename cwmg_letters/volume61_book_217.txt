217. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS
THAKURDAS
WARDHA,
June 10, 1935
BHAI PURUSHOTTAMDAS,
I got your letter. From which expression of mine did you con-
clude that I was offended?
As regards the Village Industries Association, I merely reas-
sured you and took the opportunity to have a little dig at you.
However, I was very much pleased that you wrote to me.
Do write to me often. I was never so thin-skinned as to take
offence quickly, and even if I was I have been assailed by so
many that I am no longer so. Have no fear about me, therefore.
My question was not about what kind of relief we should ask
for, but about what we should do to prevent harm. My difficulty
is that nobody is likely to listen to us just now. What steps
should we take to ensure that no harm will be done?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From Gujarati: Purushottamdas Thakurdas Papers, File No. 159/1935.
