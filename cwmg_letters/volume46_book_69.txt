69. LETTER TO MARTIN C. MILLER
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 2, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. In reply to your
question I can say that I have profited much by a prayerful read-
ing of the Sermon on the Mount. I regard Jesus to have been
one of the greatest teachers of the world. I do not believe in his
exclusive divinity.
Yours sincerely,
MARTIN C. MILLER, ESQ.
THE CLEVELAND GRAPHITE BRONZE CO.
CLEVELAND (U.S.A.)
From a photostat: S.N. 17047
