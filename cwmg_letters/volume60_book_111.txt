111. LETTER TO ANAND SWARUP GUPTA
DELHI,
January 20, 1935
BHAI ANAND SWARUP1,
Do come, except on Monday. You will be shown the new
way of plying the takli. I shall give you a few minutes if possi-
ble. It takes half an hour to teach the new way to those who
bring their own takli. Afterwards it is only a matter of practice.
You can come on or before the 27th.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI ANAND SWARUP
VAISHYA SCHOOL
MEERUT, U. P.
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9744. Courtesy: Anand Swarup
Gupta
