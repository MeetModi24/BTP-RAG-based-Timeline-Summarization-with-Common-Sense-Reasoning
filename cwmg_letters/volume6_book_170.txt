170. LETTER TO SIR GEORGE BIRDWOOD
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 13, 1906
DEAR SIR GEORGE,
Thank you very much for your letter of even date. I return here-
with your previous letter referred to in your letter under reply. I shall
thank you to let me have an amended letter, as suggested by you. I
entirely agree that Sir Muncherjee has made this question his own.
I am,
Yours truly,
Enclosure
SIR GEORGE BIRDWOOD
119, THE AVENUE
WEST EALING
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4561
