477. LETTER TO C. R. KRISHNASWAMI
January 3, 1933
MY DEAR KRISHNASWAMI,
I am so glad you have sent me Low's cartoon. I had read
about it. I am sorry it came too late for Daddy to see it. I know
he would have enjoyed it.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. C. R. KRISHNASWAMI
C/O "THE HINDU", MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 19154
