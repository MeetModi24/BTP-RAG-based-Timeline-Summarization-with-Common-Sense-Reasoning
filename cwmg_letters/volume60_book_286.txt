286. LETTER TO G. SITARAMA SASTRY
February 12, 1935
DEAR SITARAMA SASTRY,
I have your letter giving me a full answer to all my points,
and enclosing your report on the Khaddar Samsthanam.
From the description I am getting of the treatment of rice
from the first husking to the last polishing, I imagine that your
rice also does undergo one polish. In order to set my doubt at
rest, please tell me how your first process of removing the husk
is done.
SHRI G. SITARAMA SASTRY
VINAYA ASHRAM
CHANDOLE P.O., GUNTUR DT.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
