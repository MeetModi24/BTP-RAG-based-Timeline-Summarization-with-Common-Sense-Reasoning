534. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
DELHI,
September 20, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Better a pencil letter than none.
I have sent you a wire. Mahadev took the journey well. Poor
Shraddha was bad.
The train reached Wardha 4 hours late.
I hope you are having a good and useful time there. You
will insist on keeping well.
No more for the day.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3880. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7036
