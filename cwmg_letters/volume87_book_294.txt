294. LETTER TO MADALASA
PATNA,
April 21, 1947
CHI. MADU,
I have your letter. Radhakisan and Jajuji are here. You
know there was a meeting of the Charkha Sangh.
Get rid of your unsteadiness and your stupidity. If you sub-
merge yourself in Shriman your own self will be revealed to
you. For you there is no other way but this. Have you under-
stood this man who signed a blank sheet for you?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, pp. 332-
