338. LETTER TO KHUSHALCHAND GANDHI
February 3, 1937
RESPECTED BROTHER,
I am sure you could not be grieving over the death of my
sister-in-law. Only one who has earned great merit meets such
a death. I only felt happy when I got the telegram.
Salutations of
MOHANDAS
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
