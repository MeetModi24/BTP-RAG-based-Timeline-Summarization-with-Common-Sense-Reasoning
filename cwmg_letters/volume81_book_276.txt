276. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
POONA,
August 26, 1945
MY DEAR C. R.,
There is no other person as well-informed and able as you
are to answer Dr. Ambedkar's indictment of the Congress on the
question. Please therefore make time and send your answer to
Bapa as early as you can.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2109
