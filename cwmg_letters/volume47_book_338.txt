338. LETTER TO SHRI RAM SHARMA
AHMEDABAD,
August 16, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter about repression in Rohtak. If relief is
not forthcoming, you will presently have the needed permission to
adopt defensive measures. As I am not going out of India you
will please continue to keep me informed of the situation there.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI RAM SHARMA
GENERAL SECRETARY, DT. CONGRESS COMMITTEE
ROHTAK (PUNJAB)
