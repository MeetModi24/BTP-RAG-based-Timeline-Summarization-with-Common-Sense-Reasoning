187. LETTER TO JAYA
July 8, 1932
DEAR JAYA,
When we meet a thief, we should request him not to steal, if
we have courage and love for him. We must endure it if he beats us.
Sleep must be without dreams. If you are at fault and the
elders do not punish you, this is mercy.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II. p. 193
