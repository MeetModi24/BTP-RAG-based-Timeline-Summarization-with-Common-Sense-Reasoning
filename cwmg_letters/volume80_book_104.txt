104. LETTER TO GAJANAN NAIK
May 3, 1945
CHI. GAJANAN,
Kumarappa and others will be glad if Dhiren Mazmudar
goes there. But how can he go? Everyone is busy in his own
work. Do bring him if you are sure that he will come. I am
of the opinion that only a new person should be brought in.
What does Jhaverbhai say? He has managed to bring in two
persons. Will not one of them do?
Blessings from
BAPU
