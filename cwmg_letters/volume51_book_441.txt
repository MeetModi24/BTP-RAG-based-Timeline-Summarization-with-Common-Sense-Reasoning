441. LETTER TO HARIJANS OF VALVA
October 19, 1932
I have your beautiful letter written in a beautiful hand. I
can understand your hardships. I have had a long discussion
with Babasaheb Ambedkar. I can do little service from here. All
I can do is to advise you to acquaint the newly formed association
with your tale of woes. And of course keep writing to me.
That you remain Hindu is no favour to others. Hence how
can I advise you to forsake Hinduism because of the hardships? It
is in tribulation that one's dharma is put to test. But I can certain-
ly assure you that I have staked my life for the amelioration of your
lot. If other Hindus do not treat you justly, I shall atone for it
by resuming my suspended fast. May God give me the strength to
do this.
I remain,
Your servant,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 154
