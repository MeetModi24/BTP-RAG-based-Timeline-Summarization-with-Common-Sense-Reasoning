276. LETTER TO V. N. KHANOLKAR
As AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 14, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am now corresponding with the khadi
bhandar. I quite agree with you that if slivers are supplied, they
must be good and workable.
You tell me in your letter that you cannot card, but in the
concluding portion you ask for one pound of cotton. Is it carded
cotton that you want, or cotton for carding?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. V. N. KHANOLKAR
GANESH BHUWAN, KHAR, DISTRICT THANA
From a microfilm: S.N. 13804
