207. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
June 24, 1932
CHI. NIMU,
If you boil a clove of garlic in the oil which you drop into Babu's ears,
probably it will be more efficacious. Tell Jethalal that the method of making
lime-water is given in Moore's book called Family Medicine. He will look it
up for you. The important thing is to keep a watch over diet. I do not find
your name at all in the account I have received of the work done by the

June 25, 1932.
