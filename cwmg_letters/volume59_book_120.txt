120. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
September 30, 1934
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
Your letter.
"The slanderer is our brother." We have no reason to avoid
the people who talk ill of us, we have to beware of those who
praise us.
I have not seen the articles on Hindi, I shall see them now.
The Hindi article is enclosed.
I did have a letter from Ranisaheba.
All that was possible
was done. So why need we worry further?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Prithuraj says that he has some dues from Harijan Sevak the
bill for which has been submitted. Please look into the matter.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1076
