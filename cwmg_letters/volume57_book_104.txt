104. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
February 2, 1934
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have both your letters. I could not reply sooner due to
lack of time but you are always in my mind.
It is a very good thing that you are reading the Ramayana,
and that you are doing Harijan service is also to the good.
Did you collect any funds for the Bihar calamity? Did Father
give anything?
Continue writing to me; I do attempt to write a legible hand.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9595. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
