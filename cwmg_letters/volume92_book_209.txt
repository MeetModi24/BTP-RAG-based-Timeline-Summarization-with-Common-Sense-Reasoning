209. LETTER TO VITHALBHAI PATEL
Monday [December 16, 1929]
CHI. VITHALBHAI,
Many telegrams have been exchanged since your letter and
there is therefore no need to write anything. I am now only
waiting for the wire from Delhi. I shall leave for Delhi after I
get it. If I do start I will reach there on the evening of the 21st
You are of course dragging me there, but I do not know what
I shall be able to do at present.
I am returning the letter you had asked for. I have not
shown your letters to anyone to read; but I had to give the
substance to my associates. It is for you to decide whether by
doing that I have disobeyed your order.
So far everybody has been acquitting himself creditably.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[PS.] I have just received the wire from the Viceroy. I will
reach Delhi on the 21st.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: Vithalbhai Patel Papers.
