94. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
Jeth Sud 7 [May 29, 1925]
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I have your letter. I am sorry to know that you will not
be able to take up the treasurership of the Gauraksha Mandal.
May I hope you will view it with sympathy.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SIR PURUSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
NAVSARI CHAMBERS
OUTRAM ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 6196. Courtesy: Purushottamdas
Thakurdas
1 The postmark is "Santiniketan, May 30, '25". Jeth Sud 7 fell on
May 29.
