305. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA N. KHARE
July 31, 1932
CHI. RAMBHAU,
You boys and girls are now old enough to have a sense of
responsibility, and it is, therefore, right that you take up such
work. What do you write in English? Send me a specimen of
your English handwriting. Write a short essay.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 294. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
