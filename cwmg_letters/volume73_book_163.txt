163. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 9, 1940
CHI. MAHADEV,
I got your letter. I have sent copies of the correspondence.
You carried my blessings, when you left. Devdas had asked for a
message for the meeting, but I could not send it. These days
I do not like to send any messages and so in this case also I did
not send any.
I believe I will be arrested and that too soon. I am ready
of course.
I understand about Brahmadutt. He is at the right place.
I see, however, that the experiment cannot be repeated.
I finished Charkha Sangh work today.
Brijkrishna will show you the instructions.
Go through
them.
If you wish to suggest any changes, let me know. I
could not write the letter to the Viceroy. Now I will write it
tomorrow. I do not wish to keep awake all night.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
