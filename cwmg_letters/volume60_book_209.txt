209. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
February 4, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
Being tired I am writing with the left hand. You must have
received the dates sent to you. Amtul writes that you are both
miserable. It is a matter of regret if it is true. Dr. Ansari
had sent a list of Urdu books and some Hindi books too, which
I rejected. Now he has promised to send the English. I do not
worry except on your account. It is well if you are putting your
time to good use. Send me the time table.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
facing p. 144
