132. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
October 31, 1940
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter. It is not good your being ill. What work
can you do there if you are ill? Get well soon and go away.
Jawaharlal left yesterday. He will now court imprisonment.
There was nothing special in Jayaprakash's letter. I have
replied to him.
Here someone or the other is always ill. There is always
one sick-bed. At the moment it is Lilavati's brother, Lakshmidas.
It seems he has typhoid. He will get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3549
