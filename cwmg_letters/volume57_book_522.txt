522. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
May 15, 1934
CHI. VASUMATI,
Have you resolved never to write to me? I asked Valjibhai
to write to you some time ago, but you have not replied even
to that letter.
Are you happy there? How do you use your time? What
do you do about fruit and other requirements?
I hope there
are no bickerings among you.
I often remember you all during this tour on foot. All of
you will have to undertake such tours by and by.
I am writing this in a village at 3.15 a.m. So far we have
experienced no difficulty in touring on foot.
You should write to me regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9385. Also C.W. 630. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
