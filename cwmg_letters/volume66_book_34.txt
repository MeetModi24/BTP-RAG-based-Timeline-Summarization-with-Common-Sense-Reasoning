34. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 9, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
I am sending with this an article by Kishorelal which
has been abridged. It was abridged by Rajkumari. She seems
to have done a fine job of it. However, have a glance at it. And
if anything of importance is left out, insert it. I think it will be
better if you return the original to Kishorelal. A quotation
remains to be copied from it. Please see that it is done. For this
at least you will require the original. I will send my article with
Janba or whoever comes. I shall be ready at 2.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I hope the ulcer is better now.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11546
