410. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI M. PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
November 15, 1944
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I have your letter. I suggest no one should rush down here
at the moment. All of you should immerse yourselves in your
own work. Maybe it will avert the fast.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAOJIBHAI MANIBHAI PATEL
VITHAL KANYA VIDYALAYA
NADIAD
