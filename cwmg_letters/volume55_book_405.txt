405. LETTER TO RABINDRANATH TAGORE
August 7, 1933
DEAR GURUDEV,
Your letter of the 28th July enclosing copy of your cable to
Sir Nripen Sarkar on the Yeravda Pact was handed to me here
on the 4th instant. Evidently your letter crossed mine which I
wrote whilst I was in Ahmedabad. For the time being I am un-
able however to send you anything but this bare acknowledgment.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 4640
