634. LETTER TO NAMUDURY VENKATRAO
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 4, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am sorry to hear of your loss. I am
quite clear in my mind that you should not think of remarrying
in any circumstance whatsoever. And, if you remain firm in your
resolve, God will give you strength to resist any temptation that
might come in your way to remarry.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. NAMUDURY VENKATRAO
BHATNAVILLE
AMALAPURAM TALUQ
GODAVARI DT.
From a microfilm: S.N. 19595
