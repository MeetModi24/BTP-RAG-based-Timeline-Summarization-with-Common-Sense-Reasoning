149. LETTER TO SIR S. SUBRAMANIA IYER
March 23, 1919
I am extremely grateful for your candid note . I shall cer-
tainly respect your wishes. I can't misunderstand and I am sure
that the friends who are associated with me in this work will not
do so either.
Will you please tell Mrs. Besant, this movement is not a party
movement, and those who belong to particular parties after join-
ing the movement cease to be party men? She will find, as the
movement progresses, that satyagrahis will endeavour to purge
themselves of acrimony and other such delinquencies. I entirely
agree with you that however much we may differ from her, no
Indian can help feeling grateful to her for her wonderful services
to India.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 6466
