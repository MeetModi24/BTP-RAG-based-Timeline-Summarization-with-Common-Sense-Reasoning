523. LETTER TO P. C. GHOSH
September 15, 1941
DEAR PRAFULLA,
Sardar is now under homoeopathic treatment. He feels better.
But he has to take care of himself. He has intestinal spasms.
Rajen Babu is here. He is better though still weak. He is free
from fever.
I am sorry about Suresh. I do hope he will get quite well.
He must take care of himself.
Give my love to all.
Love.
BAPU
(M. K. GANDHI)
From a photostat: G.N. 3783
74-21
