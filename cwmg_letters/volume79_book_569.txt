569. LETTER TO KANAM GANDHI
March 31, 1945
CHI. KANAM,
I have your letter. I am writing this on the train. I shall
certainly be in Bombay for about a week. I cannot say any-
thing about the programme later. However, when it is finalized
you will see it in the papers. Nanavati tells me that Ramdas is
ill. It is good if he has taken leave and is going to Poona. I am
all right.
Blessings to all of you from
BAPU
