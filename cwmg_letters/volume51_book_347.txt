347. LETTER TO SARUPRANI NEHRU
October 7, 1932
DEAR SISTER,
Your letter.
You should not hesitate to send for a doctor when your health
warrants it. You are taking sufficient fruit, I hope. May God
make you well soon.
I am delighted with your decision to remain here till I have
given a final answer. The children will, of course, be pleased at
it. Give my blessings to them.
Your gift has been received but do we need this kind of
courtesy between us?
Premlilabehn and Ramanarayan's wife
anyway send me fruits in plenty.
Write to Sarup and Krishna and tell them that I know their
feelings even without a wire from them.
I can see what services you are rendering to the Harijans here.
Ba narrates everything to me.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From the Hindi original: Gandhiji-Indira Gandhi Correspondence File.
