367. LETTER TO LAKSHMI JERAJANI
August 13, 1932
CHI. LAKSHMI (JERAJANI),
I got your letter.
There was some problem about my letter4, and that is why
you got it late by some days.
It was good that you sent me your daily programme. What
subjects do you study? I will make one suggestion. I see no time
fixed for prayer in your programme. Immediately after rising in
the morning and brushing your teeth, you must sit down quietly
and pray with complete concentration.
And you should do the
same when retiring. Prayer is to the soul what food is to the
body. The body can live for some days without food. A few days'
fast may even benefit it. There can be no fast in regard to prayer.
One who believes in the existence of the soul cannot do without
prayer even for one day. Write to me occasionally and give me
news about Vithaldas.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 2813. Courtesy: Purushottam D. Saraiya
