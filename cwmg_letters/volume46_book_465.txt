465. LETTER TO SECRETARY, CENTRAL SIKH LEAGUE,
AMRITSAR
As AT SABARMATI,
June 13, 1931
THE SECRETARY
CENTRAL SIKH LEAGUE
AMRITSAR
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter forwarded to me in my capacity as editor
of Young India. So far as the recommendations about the flag are
concerned I would advise you to send them to the Secretary of the
Flag Committee appointed by the Working Committee of the
Congress. The convener and the Secretary of the Flag Commit-
tee is Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramayya, Masulipatam, S. India.
Yours sincerely,
