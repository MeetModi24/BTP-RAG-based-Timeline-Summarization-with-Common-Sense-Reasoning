16. LETTER TO A. SUBBIAH
CAMP MUSSOORIE,
October 18, 1929
MY DEAR SUBBIAH,
I have your letter. I was wondering why you had not writ-
ten for so long. It does not matter whether I reply at once or
not. I would like to have a weekly budget from you. I never
expected the prohibition work to go faster than it is. Nevertheless
regular plodding is bound to bear fruit. I am glad Vimochanam is
doing so well. You must send me a copy. I am glad too that
you and Lalita are together and flourishing. Many happy returns
of the day to the year-old baby Krishnamurti. Ba and I send
our joint blessings. I hope Seshan is not over-worrying you.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SUBBIAH
C/O SJT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
RAJAPALAYAM (S. INDIA)
