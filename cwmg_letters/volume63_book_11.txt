11. LETTER TO ABUL KALAM AZAD
[After June 2, 1936]
DEAR MAULANA SAHEB,
If you have seen the enclosed I would like your considered
opinion on the points raised therein. Is such conversion valid in
Islam? Is the method adopted lawful? Is the way the thing is
being advertised lawful or permissible? Will you publish your
opinion or permit me to publish it?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
