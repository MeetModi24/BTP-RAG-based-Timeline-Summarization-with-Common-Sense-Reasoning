405. LETTER TO YOGA R. SOMAN
JUHU,
December 29, 1937
CHI. YOGA,
You will learn what is what, now that you are living
with your husband. Tea for such a young girl like you! Why
do you spoil milk in that manner? If you cannot digest milk,
you can add some water to it, or palm-jaggery. And be sure
you become proficient in music.
You will be able to learn much from Somanji if you mean
to. You are not yet too old to learn. Build up a strong physique.
Serve your mother-in-law as well as you can. I shall not be able
to write a separate letter to Soman. I am not writing to Panditji
either. You will show this to them, won't you? Has Rambhau
reformed himself completely?
Blessings from
BAPU
