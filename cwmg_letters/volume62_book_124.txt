124. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
November 12, 1935
MY DEAR MALKANI,
I have your letter. There are many reasons for issuing Harijan
Sevak from here. Ghanshyamdas had approved of all the sugges-
tions as also Viyogi Hari's coming here. But I understand your
difficulties. You shall certainly keep him. I shall manage somehow.
He also likes the idea of staying there. More when we meet.
Herewith a letter for Viyogi Hari.
Love.
BAPU
PROF. N. R. MALKANI
DELHI
From a photostat: G.N. 1168
