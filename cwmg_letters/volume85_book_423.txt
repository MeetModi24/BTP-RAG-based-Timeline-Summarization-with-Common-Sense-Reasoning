423. LETTER TO MAHADEV A. HINGORANI
NEW DELHI,
September 17, 1946
CHI. MAHADEV,
You should not have written when you were so weak.
Write when you are fully recovered. Quietly, slowly get strong
and then write.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
