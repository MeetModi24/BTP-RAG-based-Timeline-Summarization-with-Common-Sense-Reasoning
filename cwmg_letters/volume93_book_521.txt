521. LETTER TO PYARELAL
June 16, 1940
CHI. PYARELAL,
A. S. says that you are still not careful about your food.
This is not good. Relieve me of this anxiety. It will end the
trouble if you eat whatever A. S. cooks and as often as she
cooks.
Blessings from
BAPU
