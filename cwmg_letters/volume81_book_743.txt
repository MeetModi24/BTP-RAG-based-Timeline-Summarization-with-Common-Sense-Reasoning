743. LETTER TO NILKANTH MASHRUWALA
October 24, 1945
CHI. NILKANTH 1 ,
I have your postcard. Everyone asked Chi. Arun and so did I.
But he wants to leave this place only along with me. I don't
want to send him away forcibly. Please go yourself on the
date fixed.
MASHRUWALA
SOUTH AVENUE ROAD
SANTACRUZ
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
