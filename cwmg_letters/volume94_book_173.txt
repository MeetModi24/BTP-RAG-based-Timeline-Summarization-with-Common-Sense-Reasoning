173. LETTER TO ABUL KALAM AZAD
April 19, 1942
MAULANA SAHEB,
I have your letter. I will write to you only in Urdu. This time you
will forgive me. I had already told you in Delhi that now my health
does not permit me to move around. Moreover I do not have any work
there and I have convened three meetings here which I must attend.
People from faraway places have been invited. You will understand
my position and excuse me for my absence.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
