482. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
February 28, 1930
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I have your letter. I have reproduced your speech at length.
Whatever was done is all to the good. I have practised my
'gift' well by this time. I now see that they have no answer to
it. They merely exploit our ignorance and cowardice.
The sooner the Assembly dissolves the better. I have little
hope of remaining out of jail by the end of March.
I have a query. Keshu and his mother were there, so were
Radhabehn and Devdas. Tell me what you think of them? How
did Keshu behave during his illness?
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From Hindi: C.W. 6182. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
