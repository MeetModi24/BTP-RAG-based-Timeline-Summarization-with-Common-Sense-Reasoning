199. LETTER TO POTHAN JOSEPH
PALM BUN, JUHU,
May 13, 1944
DEAR POTHAN JOSEPH,
As you probably know Gandhiji asked the Government to provide him with
Dawn while he was in detention. And whenever it came - which was fairly regularly
- he perused it carefully. Since our coming here, however, he has been missing it.
For, naturally, the Government copy has ceased to come. He has, therefore, asked
me to request you to send him a copy of the Dawn regularly. In case it is not the
practice of your management to send it free to anybody, he says the subscription
should be paid by you out of your capacious pocket. You know well enough that
Gandhiji has no money to buy newspapers with.
Please send the back-numbers too as from the 1st of May, 1944. The address
should be as above until further notice.
With regards,
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
POTHAN JOSEPH, ESQ.
NEW DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
