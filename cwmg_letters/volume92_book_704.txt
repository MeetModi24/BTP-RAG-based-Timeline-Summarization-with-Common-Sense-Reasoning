704. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
WARDHA,
October 21, 1933
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Blessings for the new year. Anandi, Babu and Bachu will
have arrived there. As Vimu has not come so far, all the girls
will go to Sharda Mandir. So there is nothing to say about
their studies.
I find that the girls do not get proper food. They cannot
be fed differently from the Harijan girls. Talk to them and if
necessary make changes in the menu without increasing the
expenditure. I believe that the Harijan girls should get the same
food as we normally eat. I know it is difficult. We can think
about it only when we meet. I have just told you of the girls'
attitude. We should do whatever is possible without doing harm
to the Harijan Ashram.
New Year's blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32824
