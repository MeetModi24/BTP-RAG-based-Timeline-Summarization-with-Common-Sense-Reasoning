81. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
May 13, 1941
DEAR SISTER,
Why do you fall ill again and again? Those who want to serve
should learn the art of keeping healthy. I hope with the grace of
God the tour would be completed without any hitch.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7995. Also C.W. 3092. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
