451. LETTER TO F. B. FISHER
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 26, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your letter from Hingham. I have got the book also,
called Understanding India. I do not know when I shall get the time
but as soon as I do, I shall read Mrs. William's book.
I reciprocate the hope that we shall meet one another some
time next year.
Yours sincerely,
REV. F. B. FISHER
METHODIST EPISCOPAL CHURCH, 3 MIDDLETON STREET, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 13509
