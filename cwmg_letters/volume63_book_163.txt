163. LETTER TO NARASINHRAO B. DIVATIA
July 10, 1936
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
If Gokulbhai were not to give me the news of the passing
of your life companion, then who else would? He is one of your
many admirers. Where is the need for offering condolence to you? I
had been hearing about your illness. I did not know anything
about Sushilabehn. Truly God tests his devotees. You have passed
