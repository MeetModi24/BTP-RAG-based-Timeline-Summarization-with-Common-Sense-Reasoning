429. LETTER TO K. S. VENKATARAMANI
November 19, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Of course, I had your book Kandan—
the Patriot in Yeravda. But there was no copy here. All the books
received in Yeravda went to the Ahmedabad Municipality. I re-
member having read several chapters of the book. Is it not the
proper thing for you to translate your own convincing argument
into action?
Yours sincerely,
SHRI K. S. VENKATARAMANI, M.A., B.L.
ADVOCATE, SANKARAPURAM
MYLAPORE, MADRAS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
