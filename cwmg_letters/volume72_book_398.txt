398. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
August 4, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
The reply is good. You may add: 'Instead of inviting me, it
would be better if Mother comes here and sees for herself how hap-
pily I live. It will give her the satisfaction of meeting her son and
I shall get the pleasure of seeing her.'
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4355
