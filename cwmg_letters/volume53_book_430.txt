430. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
February 17, 1933
MY DEAR MARGARETE,
I have your letter. I hope you have been receiving all my
letters.
You should not think of coming here in the hope of getting
a professorship or something of that kind so as to enable you to
support your mother. You will only come when the way is per-
fectly clear for you. Surely, it is possible for you to love India
even from where you are and to do many acts of service. You
have plenty of years before you. Go through the necessary train-
ing, keep India your goal, and some day you will gravitate here.
Of course you are like Lakshmi to me or Mira. But you must
also realize that it is a hard yoke to bear.
Love.
BAPU
