2. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
September 16, 1933
CHI. AMALA1,
I have your several letters. You must shed this wretched fear
of me. Whatever I said and did at the Ashram was said and
done in pure love and for your good. Why should you fear me,
if you believe what I say? You have therefore no need to avoid
me or fear me.
I am keeping well in spite of blood-pressure.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
