435. LETTER TO SHARDABEHN C. CHOKHAWALA
ABBOTTABAD,
July 9, 1939
CHI. BABUDI,
I am glad that you are keeping good health.
We are likely to spend at the most one month in this district.
As a small incision was made under Pyarelal's tongue, he had
to stay behind. Hence Ba and Sushila also stayed on. They are
likely to return in about three days. Ba is not going to Sabar-
mati. I am in excellent health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10013. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
