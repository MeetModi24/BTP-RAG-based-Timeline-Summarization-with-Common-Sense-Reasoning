42. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
January 23, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA 1 ,
I was glad to get your letter. You have improved your hand-
writing. Do your lessons there with attention. Do you ever re-
member the Vidyapith? How is Mohan ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5762. Also C.W. 2985. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
