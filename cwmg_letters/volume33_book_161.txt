161. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
MADHI,
[March 15, 1927]
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I have your letter. I get news about your health regularly.
Now you have been released. That's good. If you cannot recoup
enough strength you had better drop your trip to Bharatpur.
Ghanshyamdasji's letter shows his candour. It is enclosed
herewith.
How is Janakibehn now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6142. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
