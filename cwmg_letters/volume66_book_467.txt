467. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
HARIPURA,
February 16, 193[8]
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I talked with Shakaribehn for one hour. I will tell you
more when we meet. At the moment I feel that I have been
able to satisfy her. For the present she will stay on in the
Harijan Ashram. I have told her that if she wishes to see me
she may certainly come once.
I am getting on all right. Sharma, Dahyalal and Parnerkar
must be engrossed in their work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10581
