346. LETTER TO OLIVE DOKE
[JOHANNESBURG,]
Monday [On or after December 19, 1910]
MY DEAR OLIVE,
Ramdas and Devdas just now tell me that father is ill. I was
sorry to hear this. I dare not leave the office just now. I am going
back to the Farm. Please let me know there how father is getting on and
what the illness is. You know the address: Tolstoy Farm, Lawley
Station.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photo copy of the original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 4928
Courtesy: C. M. Doke
