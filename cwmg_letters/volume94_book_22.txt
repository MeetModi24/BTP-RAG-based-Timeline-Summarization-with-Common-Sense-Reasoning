22. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA (C.P.),
August 4, 1941
MY DEAR BULBUL,
Your love letter which is also business letter. Of course you are
working there and wearing yourself out. Take care that you don't
disappear before me. So you go to Hyderabad, and do the diplomatic
work.
As usual you have come out with your wise suggestion. I am writing
to Chhatari.
I know you will come to my view that it is not time yet for me
to move out. I am doing better work remaining in Sevagram.
Love.
SPINNER
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
1 Muhammad Ahmad Said Khan, Nawab of Chhatari, Member of the National
Defence Council
