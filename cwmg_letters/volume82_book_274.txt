274. LETTER TO RADHAKANT MALAVIYA
SODEPUR,
December 9, 1945
BHAI RADHAKANT,
I have gone through your letter. I do not take any interest
in the elections. I know that the Congress must win.
Babuji has formed a separate association. I did not like
it. But who was I to prevent him from doing so? I think it is
improper for you to run it. It is a different thing if Babuji does
so. Have a talk with the Working Committee about the role of
the Hindu Mahasabha. I personally do not like it. I am returning
the two letters.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
