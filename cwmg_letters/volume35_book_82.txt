82. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
ON TOUR,
October 12, 1927
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
I have the two letters promised by you and the letter contain-
ing your reply to Nadkarni. The reply will be duly published.
I am anxious to know what you thought of the Spinning Essay
and what you said to the Viceroy.
I do hope that your having sent in Sir Visvesvarayya's name
will not be considered too late.
I hope you received my letter about Pragji and Medh. I had
your telegram about Orissa.
I do not mind your spending twelve annas to tell me when
your hand is completely restored.
With love,
MOHAN
From a photostat: G.N. 2622
