19. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
April 9, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
If you can, stay on till I return. If you think it is your
duty to leave, do so. If this is still not clear, ask Amritlal and
do as he advises.
I hope to return in a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
