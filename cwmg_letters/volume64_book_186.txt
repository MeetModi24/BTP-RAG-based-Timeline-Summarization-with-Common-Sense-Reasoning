186. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
TILAKNAGAR,
December 21, 1936
CHI. LILA,
How is it you have not received my letter? I was glad to
learn that your brother is now recovering.
Tell Damayanti I had thought she was brave. A brave
woman will not weep or be restless. Life and death are not in
your hands, nor in the hands of anyone else. Why should one
grieve then? We have done everything when we have served
the sick.
Only Mahadev, Bablo, Pyarelal and Radhakishan are
here with me. Of course Khan Saheb and Mehrtaj are here.
They lodge separately. Mehr is with Prema.
Ba, Manu, Nimu's Kano and Navin will arrive on the
24th.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
This is enough for Mehr and Tara.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9353. Also C.W. 6628. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
