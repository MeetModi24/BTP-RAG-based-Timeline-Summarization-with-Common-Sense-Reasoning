622. LETTER TO DATAR
NEW DELHI,
October 17, 1946
BHAI DATAR,
When will your work begin? When we work for some-
one on payment we have to observe a time-limit; the same
applies to altruistic work.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DATAR
NIJADHAM
URULI KANCHAN
