811. LETTER TO KALICHARAN GHOSH
October 31, 1945
BHAI KALICHARAN GHOSH,
I have your letter. I have the book about famine in Bengal
but I have not read it. Send Economic Resources of India. Carefully
go through what I have said about prayers. Go and see Satis
Babu of Khadi Pratishthan and also Babu Nirmal Kumar. If
even then you are not satisfied, write to me again.
It is good that you care for work and are not after name.
If you do care for name it should be only for Ramanama, for
that includes all work.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI KALICHARAN GHOSH
6 RAJA BASANTRAI ROAD
KALIGHAT, CALCUTTA
