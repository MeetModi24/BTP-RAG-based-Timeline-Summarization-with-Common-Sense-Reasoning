55. LETTER TO APTE
July 25, 1945
BHAI APTE,
He who had given you Shambhu has taken him back. We
must also meet the same fate. Why should you then grieve?
Why should Sharaja lament? All the children belong to you both.
All this is implied in the khadi spirit. Cultivate that spirit and
go on doing your duty. Everything will be all right if you
exercise self-control. Moreover, you have already rendered what-
ever service you could.
Blessings from
BAPU
APTE
279- SADASHIV PETH
POONA CITY
