4. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 12, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
I can ill afford even to send a postcard. The apples have
come. Hancock came yesterday. Can't say whether there was any
change in K. The heat for the last two days has been scorching and
I felt glad that you were not here to go through the experience.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4096. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7405
