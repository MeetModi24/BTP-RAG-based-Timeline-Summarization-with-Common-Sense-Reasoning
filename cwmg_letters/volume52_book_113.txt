113. LETTER TO SULOCHANA A. SHAH
November 27, 1932
CHI. SULOCHANA,
You gave a good reply to Mother. If the new food does
not agree with you, you should immediately complain. A thief
gets into the habit of stealing. Some thieves steal because they
see others doing that, and some make stealing their profession.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1746
