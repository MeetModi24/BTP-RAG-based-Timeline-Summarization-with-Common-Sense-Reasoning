253. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
BORSAD,
July 29, 1931
MY DEAR AMTUL,
I have your letter.
You are very impatient. God willing, I expect to be in
Ahmedabad on the 1st of next month.
You must not mind your illness after having taken all the
precautions. It will come all right if you won't be in a hurry.
You should be satisfied with whatever little God enables you to
do. If we all have the readiness of mind for any service that
comes to us it is enough.
BAPU
AMTULBEHN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a photostat: G.N. 243
