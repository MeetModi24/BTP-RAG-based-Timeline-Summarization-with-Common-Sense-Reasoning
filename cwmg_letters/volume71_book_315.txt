315. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 10, 1940
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter. May your faith bear fruit. Write to Sardar
from time to time. I am sure you keep Nanalal informed, too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8571. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
