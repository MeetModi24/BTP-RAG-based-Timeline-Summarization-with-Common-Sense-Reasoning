319. LETTER TO IFTIKHAR-UD-DIN
SEVAGRAM,
November 28, 1944
DEAR MIAN SAHIB,
Bapu was glad to have yours and Mrs. Iftikhar's letters. He is too tired to write
to all those whom he would like to write. He desires me to thank you both for your
kind sentiments.
So far as the fast is concerned, he says he won't undertake it unless there is
a clear call.
How is your health now ? I hope this will find you fully restored.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
MIAN IFTIKHAR-UD-DIN
21 AIKMAN ROAD
LAHORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
