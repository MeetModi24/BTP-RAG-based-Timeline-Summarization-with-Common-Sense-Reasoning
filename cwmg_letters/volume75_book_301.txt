301. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
January 15, 1942
CHI. NARANDAS,
Champa tells me that Ratilal's condition is very bad. She is
staying here. A letter from Atmasarupanand is enclosed. Can you
suggest or do anything in this matter?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am leaving for Kashi on the 19th, and shall return on the
24/25 th.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8597. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
