378. LETTER TO DR. HIRALAL SHARMA
February 12, 1933
DEAR DR. SHARMA,
I was glad to have your letter. Amtussalaam being with you
puts me at ease. She has such great faith in your ability to cure
her. I do hope that she will be thoroughly restored to health. I
hope that your troubles will be soon over. I am glad you are pre-
paring yourself for the Ashram life.
It will please me greatly
if you could go to the Ashram.
Yours,
BAPU
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 18
