184. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
September 30, 1944
BHAI MUNSHI,
See the enclosed and, if you can throw light on it, do
so. Homi Taleyarkhan had asked me about this and my reply
was published in the Jame [Jamshed]
I am writing this in a hurry. Take care of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 7682. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
