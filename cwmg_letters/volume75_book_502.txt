502. LETTER TO NATWARLAL M. PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
March 20, 1942
BHAI NATWARLAL,
I have your letters. Considering everything I feel that the
Spinners' Association cannot sanction another store. The existing
store is almost under the Spinners' Association. It is a result of
considerable efforts. I am afraid, sanctioning the two stores will
be harming both of them. If you really love khadi, build up
the existing store as much as you can.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
NATWARLAL MOTILAL PATEL
JOGIDAS VITTHALNI POLE, BARODA
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
