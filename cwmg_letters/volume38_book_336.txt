336. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[ CALCUTTA,]
December 24, 1928
CHI. MIRA,
I have your note. This is Xmas eve. If Xmas has a special
memory and special meaning for you, may you have on that day
a purer and greater grasp of the realities of life. You have a sound
heart and, therefore, all will be well with you. I knew you were
happy and at peace in Wardha and to see you so made me
happy.
Motilalji had work cut out for me as soon as I reached the
station. So I was able to spin only at night.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5327. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 8217
