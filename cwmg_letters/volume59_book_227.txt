227. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
October 15, 1934
BA,
I fear you are becoming irregular in your letters. It would
be better if you keep writing regularly. Khan Saheb has come
over.
Other people have also turned up because the Charkha
Sangh meets today. Rajendrababu was expected but he has fallen
ill. Let us see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Bane Patro, p. 28
