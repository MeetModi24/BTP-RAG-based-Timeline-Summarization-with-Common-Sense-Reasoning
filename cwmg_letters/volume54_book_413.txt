413. LETTER TO HOME SECRETARY,
GOVERNMENT OF BOMBAY
April 10, 1933
SECRETARY TO GOVERNMENT
HOME DEPARTMENT, POONA
DEAR SIR,
I regret to have to remind you of my letter regarding Hari-
Jan work. Kakasaheb Kalelkar whom I met on Monday last
told me that there were urgent matters in the letters awaiting me
in the post. There are pressing Harijan problems demanding
my immediate attention.
I do hope therefore that I shall be
favoured with the final decision before or on Monday next at the
latest.
I attach hereto a copy of the Government of India's orders
in the matter. In my humble opinion they are unequivocal.
Yours sincerely,
Home Department, Government of Bombay, I.G.P. File No. 10
