366. LETTER TO JETHALAL G. SAMPAT
July 24, 1940
CHI. JETHALAL,
Am I not doing exactly what you have suggested? I am
pouring into this work all the strength and energy I and my
co-workers have. I have based all my plans on the success of
this. Let us see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9871. Courtesy: Narayan J. Sampat
