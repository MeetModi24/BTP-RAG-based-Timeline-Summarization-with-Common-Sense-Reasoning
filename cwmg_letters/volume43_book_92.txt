92. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
Silence Day [March 17, 1930]
CHI. GANGABEHN (SENIOR),
I have your letter. You will always be doing good, doing
service. Lakshmibehn came and told me something. As you
know, I could not spare time to ask her for more details. Take
care of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8743. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
