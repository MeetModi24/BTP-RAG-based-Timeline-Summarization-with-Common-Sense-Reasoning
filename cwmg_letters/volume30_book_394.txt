394. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Tuesday, April 20, 1926
BHAISHRI DEVCHANDBHAI,
Here is the letter from Diwan Saheb. It is now for him to
decide when he will come. I suggest that you should yourself go
over to Porbandar. You may tell him that you have read the ac-
companying letter. And since you will see him personally, I do
not write to acknowledge the enclosed letter. Your going there
in person will be as good as my doing so.
