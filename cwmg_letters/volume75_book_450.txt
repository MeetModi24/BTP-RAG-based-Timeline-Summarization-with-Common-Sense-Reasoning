450. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
March 1, 1942
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
Please don't worry about Mahadev. He is all right. He
must have rest. Mental peace is absolutely essential for him.
I do not wish to send him out for the time being.
I hope your experiment is going on well. How about [your]
weight and strength? What did you do about what I wrote to you
regarding Jamnalalji?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8053. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
