45. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
September 28, 1940
CHI. A. SALAAM,
You also should learn to read Gujarati. I hope everything is
going on well, and you are happy. I will have to be in Simla
a little longer. Work should be over by Monday at the latest.
Recover your health fully.
Remember what I have told you.
Master Hindi spelling.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 434
