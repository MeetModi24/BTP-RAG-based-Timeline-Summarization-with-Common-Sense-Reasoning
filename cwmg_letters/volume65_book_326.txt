326. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 12, 1937
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. I want to give you all the consolation I
can. Do come over in July. We both shall talk over the matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7981. Also C.W. 3080. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
