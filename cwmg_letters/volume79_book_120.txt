120. LETTER TO JAIL SUPERINTENDENT, GORAKHPUR
SEVAGRAM,
January 26, 1945
SUPERINTENDENT
JAIL, GORAKHPUR (U. P.)
DEAR SIR,
Persistent letters are being received that Baba Raghavdas is
in your custody and is suffering from insanity. I shall be obliged
if you will kindly inform me of the true situation and if I may
be allowed to send a physician or a vaidya.
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
