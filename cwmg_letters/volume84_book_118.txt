118. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SIMLA,
May 4, 1946
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
Rashid has passed away. All of us have to go the same
way. I hope, therefore, that you are not grieving. I have sent
a wire to Amtul.
Whether or not to go to Indore rests entirely with you.
I have no courage at all to guide you. I cannot judge in what
your good lies. You must not feel any hesitation in doing what
you like.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 503
