487. LETTER TO HELENE ALPERS HAHN
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 12, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of the 19th April last. I have not yet
decided what is to be done to the Autobiography for its publica-
tion in book form in European languages after its completion.
At the present moment it is being published in several maga-
zines for which I have given free permission. But beyond that I
am unable to go at present.
Yours sincerely,
HELENE ALPERS HAHN
HEILBRONN AM NECKAR
GERMANY
From a copy: S.N. 32250
