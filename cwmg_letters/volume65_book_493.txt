493. LETTER TO MAURICE FRYDMAN
July 20, 1937
DEAR FRYDMAN 1 ,
So you have taken sannyasa. Were you not a sannyasi
even when you came to Segaon? But I understand what you
mean. May God fulfil your aim of utterly selfless service of the
most downtrodden. Come whenever the spirit moves you.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1199. Also C.W. 5095. Courtesy : Wanda
Dynowska
