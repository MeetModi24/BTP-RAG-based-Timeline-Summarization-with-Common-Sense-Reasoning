440. LETTER TO RAMIBEHN PAREKH
April 24, 1932
CHI. RAMI,
A letter from you would always be a surprise for me. You
said that your birthday was approaching, but forgot to mention
which. As you know, I never remember. Many happy returns
of the day to you. But you should also take up some work of
service. Come and see me when you feel inclined.
Blessings from
BAPU
