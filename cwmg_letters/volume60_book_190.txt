190. LETTER TO MOHANLAL
February 1, 1935
MY DEAR MOHANLAL,
I like your very thorough report on the allegations of the
Meghs of the Arya Nagar Colony. Can you now follow it up
by seeing the members of the Sabha finding out
how the
negotiations go and do whatever is possible on behalf of the poor
Meghs? I suppose the corresponding Salvation Army Colony
is flourishing, well managed and that the Christian settlers or
tenants, whatever they be, are quite happy.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI MOHANLAL
HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH
LAJPAT RAI BHAWAN, LAHORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
