322. LETTER TO SURAJBHAN AGRAWAL
KARADI,
May 4, 1930
BHAI SURAJBHAN,
I have your letter. You all have my blessings of course.
Keep writing to me. Where is Yashoda Devi? What work are
the ladies doing? No doubt there are difficulties. But endeavour
and God's grace will overcome them.
It does not surprise me that Lala Dunichand's wife is
engaged in work.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. SURAJBHAN AGRAWAL
SATYAGRAHA CAMP
ROHTAK (PUNJAB)
From a photostat of the Hindi: Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
