483. LETTER TO KULSUM SAYANI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C.P,
September 4, 1940
MY DEAR KULSUM,
I was delighted to hear from you. I shall see what I can
do with your report. But you have my blessings in your work
and also for your dear ones, here and in England.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: K. Sayani Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
