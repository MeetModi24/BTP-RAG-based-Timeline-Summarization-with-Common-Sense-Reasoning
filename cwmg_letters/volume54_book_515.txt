515. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 19, 1933
DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
It is good that you have returned to the Ashram. As regards
coming here you should do only what Dr. Sharma permits. No
one has any business to say anything about you now that he is
there. The doctor writes that you are to take complete rest. If
he allows, you may come on Saturday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 284
