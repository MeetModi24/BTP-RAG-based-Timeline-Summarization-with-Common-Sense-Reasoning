119. LETTER TO DEVPRAKASH NAYYAR
SIMLA,
May 4, 1946
CHI. DEV,
Daily I think of you. Now all would be reaching there.
They have realized that it is better to leave me alone at Simla.
When I come down from Simla I shall be with you all. Pyare-
lal will give you all the news.
Hope Satya is all right and her operations have been suc-
cessful.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Prakash must be keeping fit.
From a microfilm of the Hindi : Documents relating to Gandhiji. Cour-
tesy : National Archives of India
