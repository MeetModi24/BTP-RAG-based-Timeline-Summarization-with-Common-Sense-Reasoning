604. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 24, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
There should have been a letter from you or Pyarelal today.
Pyarelal should be coming today. I am writing this to relieve
you of anxiety. I am not suffering from anything except
fatigue. You must have received yesterday's telegram. I do
all the work after prayers.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
