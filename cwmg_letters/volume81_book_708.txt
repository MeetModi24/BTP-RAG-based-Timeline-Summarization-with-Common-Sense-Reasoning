708. LETTER TO VINA CHATTERJEE
October 20, 1945
CHI. VINA,
I have your letter and also Sailen's. My views are firm.
I don't believe in this sort of family attachments. Do what both
of you deem fit. You won't gain anything by coming with me.
My going has also been postponed. Go if you think it is your
