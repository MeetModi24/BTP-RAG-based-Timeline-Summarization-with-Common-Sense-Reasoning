496. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Tuesday [June 22, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. I had no doubt at all that the climate
there would suit you. All of you will enjoy it during the rains.
On my inquiring many people told me that water evaporates there
as fast as it rains. Where there is moisture, there is danger.
Girdhari was discharged from the hospital the day before yes-
terday.
Blessings from
BAPU
SF. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
HOMI VILLA
PANCHGANI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
