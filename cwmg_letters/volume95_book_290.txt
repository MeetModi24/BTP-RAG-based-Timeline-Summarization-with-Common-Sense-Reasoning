290. LETTER TO THE CHILDREN OF BAL MANDIR
LITTLE BIRDIES OF BAL MANDIR,
I have got no letter from you during this journey. The fault is
not yours. It is the fault of my nature of travelling. I am so far away
that post can reach here only three times a week.
Be it so. Now more when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8656. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chowdhari
