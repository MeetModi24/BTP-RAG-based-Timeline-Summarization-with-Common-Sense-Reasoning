236. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SABARMATI,
November 26, 1929
CHI. MIRA,
I have your three letters. My weight was found to be 100t lb.
Your last entry shows 89[1b.] 15 tolas. The increase is therefore
11 ½ lb. net. Not a bad bargain! Otherwise too all well.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
I did not know that there was an address already on the p.c.
I hope this reaches you all right.
From the original: C.W. 5381. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9437
