488. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
November 29/December 1, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (DESAI),
Though you had resolved to write every week, there has
been no letter from you this week. I look upon this as a serious
lapse. One may say that there is nothing as grave as violating
a solemn resolution. This is such a common weakness that we
don't realize its gravity. But you should know that it is a grave
weakness indeed and guard yourself against it. When you have
nothing to write, you may sign on a blank sheet, as Chhotelal
does. But it is not likely that a child will have nothing to say to
its father or mother.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
December 1, 1930
Pyarelal took Kakasaheb's place on the 29th.
