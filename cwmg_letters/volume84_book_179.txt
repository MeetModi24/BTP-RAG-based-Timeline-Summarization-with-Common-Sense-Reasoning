179. LETTER TO CARIAPPA
SIMLA,
May 12, 1946
BHAI CARIAPPA,
I have your letter. I am not definite where I shall be stay-
ing. You will tell me everything whenever you happen to meet
me. I hope your health is all right. I hope Veeramma too is
all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
