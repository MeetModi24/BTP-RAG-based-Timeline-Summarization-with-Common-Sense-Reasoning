646. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
March 5, 1933
BHAISHRI MUNSHI,
I got your postcard. It seems that you are being weighed
down by misfortunes. But probably man cannot be tested except
through suffering. I had already given a 'quack doctor's' advice
to let Jagdish stay where he was if his health had deteriorated very
much. Your hand does not seem to have improved yet. Take
proper treatment and see that it does.
We all share your sorrows.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7527. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
