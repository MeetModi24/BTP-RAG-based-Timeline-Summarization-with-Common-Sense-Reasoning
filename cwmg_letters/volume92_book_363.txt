363. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
October 3, 1930
DEAR MOTI BABU,
I have replied to your wire. I must not exert myself to give
you a long reply to your loving letter of 15th instant. I appre-
ciate your deep love and know that the Sangh is with me in all
acts of love.
Sardar and Mahadev join me in sending you love.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G. N. 11037
