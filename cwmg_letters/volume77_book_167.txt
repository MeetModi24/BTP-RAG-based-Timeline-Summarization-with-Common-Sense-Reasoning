167. LETTER TO T. B. SAPRU
June 9, 1944
DEAR SIR TEJ,
I have your kind letter. I am glad your son is making rapid
progress. May the residence in Kashmir bring about complete
recovery.
The publication of my letter to Dr. Jayakar had become in-
evitable. You need fear no hasty statement from me till I am
declared passable by the doctors. I shall look forward to meeting
you in Sevagram. I shall be for a fortnight from 15th instant
in Dr. Dinshaw's sanatorium and may have to go to Panchgani
thereafter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
