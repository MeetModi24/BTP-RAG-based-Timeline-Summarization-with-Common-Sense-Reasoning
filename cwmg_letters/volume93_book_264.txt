264. LETTER TO PYARELAL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 27, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
There has been no post from you yesterday and today.
There are only three days before the 30th. I gather from Ba's
broken sentences that you are planning to run away somewhere.
I cannot believe it. But if you want to throw pepper into a
raw wound you will do that too. Think of it if you can. Is not
my prestige in your hands?
You will have received my letter of yesterday. My mind at
present is inclined in that direction. I am back in the same
state of mind as I was in on the 14th. If that conclusion is
correct, have I not been like the guilty person arraigning the
policemen? Can you be of some help in this as well? I shall
feel greatly relieved if Sushila's agitation subsides.
Blessings from
BAPU
