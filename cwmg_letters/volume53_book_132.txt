132. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI A. MEHTA
January 20, 1933
BHAI BHAGWANJI,
You are a pessimist and an atheist. I am a great optimist
and a devotee of God. I, therefore, see no possibility of our
agreeing about anything. Both of us, therefore, should go our
different ways and learn in the school of experience. He who sur-
vives will know the truth a little.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
