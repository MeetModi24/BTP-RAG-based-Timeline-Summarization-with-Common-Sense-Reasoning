102. LETTER TO LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
POONA,
November 12, 1945
DEAR LORD LAWRENCE1,
How nice that your wedding day should coincide with my
birthday? Thanks for your congratulations. May I reciprocate
them? Is not wedding a new birth?
Did I not know you first through Lady Lawrence during
those suffragette days? I was then a novice in the art of
civil resistance and had friendly differences with the late Mrs.
Pankhurst and her daughter but not with your wife and the late
Mrs. Despard.
With kind regards to both of you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
THE RT. HON'BLE LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
11 OLD SQUARE
LINCOLN'S INN, LONDON W. C. 2
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 174
