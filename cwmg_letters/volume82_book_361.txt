361. LETTER TO RANGANAYAKI DEVI
SODEPUR,
December 23, 1945
CHI. RANGANAYAKI,
I have your letter. I have shown your letter and cutting
to Dr. Sushila Nayyar. She says that surgically the thing is sub-
stantial. But I would not advise you to pay for your doctor
friend for going to America. If you feel like it you may go to
America. But that is a matter solely for you to decide.
Personally I think that you should turn your deafness into a
blessing and listen to the voice from within. What is worth hear-
ing will be written down for you. But this can be done only
if you feel like it, not otherwise.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RANGANAYAKIDEVI
FIRST HOUSE
SRIRANGAM
S. INDIA
