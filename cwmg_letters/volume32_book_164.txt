164. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
GONDIA,
[December 21, 1926]
CHI. MANI,
I am aware of your affection for me. But surely you do not
expect to stay with me all your life. But you can live with my
work. That means that you should prepare yourself for it. Do
not waste a single minute there. Write to me regularly. I too
shall write as often as I can.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne, p. 39
