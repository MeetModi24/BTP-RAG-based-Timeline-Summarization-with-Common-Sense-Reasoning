561. LETTER TO RANGANAYAKI
SEVAGRAM,
March 29, 1945
CHI. RANGANAYAKI,
I have your postcard. Both of you may come. How long
I have to be in Bombay I shall know only when I reach there.
Keep me informed.
Blessings to both of you from
BAPU
RANGANAYAKI
SRIRANGAM
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
