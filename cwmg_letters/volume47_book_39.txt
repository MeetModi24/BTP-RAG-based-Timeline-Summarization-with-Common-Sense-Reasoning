39. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI N. PATEL
BORSAD,
June 21, 1931
BHAI RAOJIBHAI,
I have your letter. If the superior officer does not listen to
you and redress the injustice, I would advise you to put up with
it for some time. Perhaps after a few days I may be able to
advise you to take further steps in the matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8992
