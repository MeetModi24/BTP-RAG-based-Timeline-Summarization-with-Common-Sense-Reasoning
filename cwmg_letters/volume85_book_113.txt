113. LETTER TO GENERAL MANAGER,
G. I. P. RAILWAY
URULI KANCHAN, DIST. POONA,
August 2, 1946
GENERAL MANAGER
G. I. P. RLY.
DEAR SIR,
A friend in Cochin has sent me a cheque for Rs. 100/8/-
to be presented to driver Mr. Pereira who was driving the third
class special that brought me on 9th ultimo to Poona from
New Delhi, and by his skill avoided what might have proved
a serious accident. I enclose the cheque herewith and request
you to send it to Mr. Pereira if your rules permit the presen-
tation of such gifts to your employees.
I am here till 4th instant after which date, letter may be
addressed to me Sevagram, via Wardha, C. P.
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C. W. 10529
