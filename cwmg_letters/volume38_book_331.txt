331. LETTER TO K. T. PAUL
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 22, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I shall be in Calcutta from tomorrow
probably for a week. I shall be staying with Sjt. Jiwanlal,
8 Pretoria Street.
I have the summary of the proceedings of the Conference as
also the text of your speech. The speech I have not yet been
able to read. As soon as I get the time, I shall certainly read
it and if there is anything I have to say on it I shall gladly do
so.
Mr. Dewick wrote to me before you about Dr. Mott. I
would also like to meet him. My movements during January and
February will be determined in Calcutta.
Yours sincerely,
K. T. PAUL 3 , ESQ.
5 RUSSEL STREET
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 13818
