397. LETTER TO DEVCHAND U. PAREKH
January 21, 1930
BHAISHRI DEVCHANDBHAI,
I have your letter. I do believe that, if the Thakore Saheb
of Morvi offers to use his good offices to settle the dispute, they
should slacken the satyagraha. I don't understand why Manilal
is so impatient. Moreover, Fulchand, too, is there to help them.
Write to me from time to time. If possible, you should even run
down to Malia.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5724
