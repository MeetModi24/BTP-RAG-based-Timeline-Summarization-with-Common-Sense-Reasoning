341. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
July 6, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
Read the accompanying letters and do what is necessary.
What has happened to Lilavati? She is extremely agitated. She is
a good girl, but one can never be sure when she will let one
down. Still I am at ease since you are by her side.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II Also C.W. 8460. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
