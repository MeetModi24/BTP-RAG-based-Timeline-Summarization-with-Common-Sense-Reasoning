164. LETTER TO VALJI DESAI
ON THE TRAIN,
Wednesday [November 2, 1921]
Please ask the Press to send the proofs of Young India for this
time only at the address of the Rajasthan Seva Sang, Ajmer. They
should be posted on Wednesday evening or early morning on
Thursday so that they may reach Ajmer by the morning mail.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
BHAISHRI VALJI DESAI
YOUNG INDIA
AHMEDABAD
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 6040. Courtesy: Valjibhai
Govindji Desai
