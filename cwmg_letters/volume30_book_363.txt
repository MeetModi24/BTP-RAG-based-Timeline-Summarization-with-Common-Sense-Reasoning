363. LETTER TO PRANJIVAN MEHTA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday, April 16, 1926
BHAISHRI PRANJIVAN,
I hope you got my earlier letter regarding Jeki. Herewith
another. I have also had a letter from Shri Manilal to the same
effect. Please decide soon about this matter. I have been await-
ing your letter for a long time. I got detailed reports about
your health. I will most probably leave for Mussoorie on the 22nd.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 19464
