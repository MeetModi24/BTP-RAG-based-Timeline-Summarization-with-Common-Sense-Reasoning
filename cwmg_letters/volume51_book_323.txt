323. LETTER TO DR. SYED MAHMUD
October 5, 1932
DEAR DR. MAHMUD,
Many thanks for the basket of fruit. I have no letter for
you. I hope you are better and that your domestic troubles are
eased if not over. How I wish that the untouchables solution
could lead to further unity! May your efforts fructify. My
salaams to Maulana Saheb.
Love.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 5073
