This is the last verse in the Ayodhyakanda 3 . Please ponder over it.
It is still ringing in my ears. In these hard times bhakti has acquired
the highest place. But even for the practice of that bhakti we needs must
have self-discipline. That discipline is at the root of our education. I
am realizing every moment that without it all our intelligence is of no
avail. What other blessing shall I wish for you?
It is only when I think of it specifically that I feel some sorrow over
the death of Chi. Anandlal's son. Otherwise my feelings are dead.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
