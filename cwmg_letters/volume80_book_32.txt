32. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
April 26, 1945
CHI. ANAND,
How can I forget? Do I not have to write for you daily?
I do not like your illness. There is danger in treating oneself.
Do as I have written to Gangi. Now, when you go to Dr. Raju
take his treatment. Recover now with the doctor's treatment.
What about Mahadev .
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India,
and Anand T. Hingorani
