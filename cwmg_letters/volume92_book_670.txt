670. LETTER TO AMINA QURESHI
April 7, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER AMINA,
You seem to be progressing well. Write to me every day,
slowly and in clear handwriting as you do an exercise.
Try to discover why Qureshi suffers from piles. What is his
food? After ending a fast one always feels weak because the
nerves are put to greater strain.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10798. Courtesy: Gulam
Rasul Qureshi
