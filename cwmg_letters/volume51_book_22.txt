22. LETTER TO SECRETARY, NAGARI PRACHARINI SABHA,
BANARAS
September 3, 1932
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter. Although I am not personally acquain-
ted with Acharya Mahavir Prasadji I am not unaware of his
services to Hindi literature.
I consider it most fitting that he
should be honoured by the lovers of Hindi on his entering his
seventieth year.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI PRADHAN MANTRIJI
NAGARI PRACHARINI SABHA
BANARAS CITY
From Hindi: C.W. 9663. Courtesy: Bharat Kala Bhavan
