257. LETTER TO B. G. HORNIMAN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 28, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
To get an article from me is like drawing a live tooth. I can
only therefore send you a message, and here it is!
The way to constitutional swaraj may lie through Lucknow, the
way to organic swaraj, which is synonymous with Ramarajya lies
through Bardoli.
Yours sincerely,
B. G. HORNIMAN, ESQ:
"THE INDIAN NATIONAL HERALD"
POST Box No. 800, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 13497
