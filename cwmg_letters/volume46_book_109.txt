109. LETTER TO K. KELAPPAN NAIR
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 6, 1931
MY DEAR KELAPPAN,
I have your letter. Please continue to send me full report
of whatever irregularities you may notice. I might have to go
to Simla to discuss all these things. Meanwhile you must use all
the tact you are capable of using.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. KELAPPAN NAIR
KERALA PROVINCIAL CONGRESS COMMITTEE
MATHRUBHUMI BUILDINGS
CALICUT
From a microfilm: S.N. 17043
