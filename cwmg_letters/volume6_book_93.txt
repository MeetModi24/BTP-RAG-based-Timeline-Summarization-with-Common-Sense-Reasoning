93. LETTER TO NATAL BANK MANAGER
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 3, 1906
TO
THE MANAGER
NATAL BANK
LONDON
DEAR SIR,
Will you kindly give the bearer Mr. Ritch a draft on Johannesburg
for £100 filled in. I shall then sign it and return it to you to be placed
to my credit.
Yours faithfully,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4474
