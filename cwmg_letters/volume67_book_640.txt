640. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
PESHAWAR,
October 14, 1938
CHI. VALJI,
I understand your reason for staying there. You are commit-
ting violence by writing articles in pencil. It is difficult to read
them. How is the children's education progressing? We are fixed
up here up to November 8 at any rate. So far the climate has
been pleasant.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7483. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
