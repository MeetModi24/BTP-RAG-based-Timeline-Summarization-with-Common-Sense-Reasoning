427. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
February 12, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
I had fully expected to see you yesterday and today. I would
then have fixed up the day with you. You can have Wednesdays
and Saturdays at 8.15 p.m., if that would suit you. You can start
from tonight.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6471. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9866
