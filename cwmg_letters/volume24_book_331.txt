331. LETTER TO RAJA OF KANIKA
SABARMATI,
August 15, 1924
DEAR RAJA SAHEB,
I must apologize for not having acknowledged your two
letters earlier. I wanted to study the papers on my file before
replying. I am so pressed for time that I have not yet been able
to study them. I hope to do so soon and write to you further.
Thanking you for promise of prompt attention,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
RAJA OF KANIKA
ORISSA
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
