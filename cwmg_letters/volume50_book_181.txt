181. LETTER TO GOKIBEHN
July 8, 1932
DEAR SISTER,
I have been wanting to write to you since a month ago and
had, therefore, asked for your address. I have just got it. I hope
you enjoy peace of mind. Does anybody stay with you? Do
write to me from time to time.
Jai Shri Krishna from
BAPU
SMT. GOKIBEHN
KARSANDAS DHARAMSINH'S BUNGALOW
KRISHNA BHUWAN
BORIVLI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9813
