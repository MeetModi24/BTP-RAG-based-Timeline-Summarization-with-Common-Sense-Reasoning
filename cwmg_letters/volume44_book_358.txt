358. LETTER TO LAKSHMIBEHN KHARE
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 26, 1930
CHI. LAKSHMIBEHN,
Though the letter is from Panditji I am writing to you. I
always wish to write to anyone who is unwell. Why should you
fall ill while serving? But illness always comes like a thief, how-
ever watchful one may be. I hope you have driven out the
intruder. Do not neglect your health. Does Mathuri still feel
weak? Some children become sturdier after an attack of small-
pox. This happened in Manilal's case. Write to me in detail.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 277. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn Khare
