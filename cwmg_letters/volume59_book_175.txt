175. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
October 6, 1934
BA,
It is not worth while at all for you to come to this year's
Congress session. I shall be forced to go, though I do not at all
like to go. There will be no free tickets this time. The lowest charge
is Rs. 25. Who will pay that much? And how can we permit
anybody to pay? I have been urging everybody not to go.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
