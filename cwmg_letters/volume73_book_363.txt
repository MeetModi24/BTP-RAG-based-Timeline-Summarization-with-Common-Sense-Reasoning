363. LETTER TO DR. S. K. VAIDYA
January 21, 1941
BHAI VAIDYA,
How is it that there is no letter from you? Is it a case of 'out
of sight, out of mind'? I will not, of course, take it in that light.
I know that you are not writing because you do not want to waste
even a single minute of my time. But I cannot forget you. How
are you? Please write and tell me what you are doing. What
about the laboratory3? How is Ambu ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5744
