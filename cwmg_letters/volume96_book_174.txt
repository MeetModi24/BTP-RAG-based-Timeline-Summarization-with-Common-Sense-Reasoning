174. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
[PHOENIX]
Monday [May 4, 1914]
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
You must excuse my not writing to you regularly. I have not a
minute to spare and I have no heart to write just now except when
I am obliged. I am unable to get up early and unless I can do so
I cannot write. The new discipline taxes me to the utmost.
Your telegrams I have misunderstood. I still do not know their
meaning. I do not know whether you have to give me some news
of further distress or what. However, your letter will say something.
I have, I think, answered all the points you summarised in your
last letter. The Nagappan inscription, Manilal can write from Valliamma's.
I have not before me the exact dates.
On your diet scale I should like to write more fully when I have
the time. Mrs. Gandhi continues to be better.
With love,
Yours,
UPPER HOUSE
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
