72. LETTER TO T. PRAKASAM
WARDHA,
May 11, 1935
MY DEAR PRAKASAM,
I have gone through your note. I believe in the system of barter.
It can apply only to village communities. In its very nature it can
work among small groups of men. The village movement [if it]
succeeds, must result in villagers exchanging their goods. It won't
spread by preaching but it will by practising it wherever possible.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 9245
