433. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
March 21, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. I understand about the wire. If my
advice is not in conformity with your experience, you need not
act upon it. I have issued no command to you, simply left you
free. You should come here only if you feel that for your study
or for the sake of Draupadi or the children it would be best for
you to come here. That is, I am not particular as to where
you live.
The two books by Poore have been received. Whose bio-
graphies do you want? And should they be in English or in Hindi ?
I did have quite a number of books on Indian drugs but
now I have none as they have all been donated to the Ahme-
dabad Municipality.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha, pp. 152-
