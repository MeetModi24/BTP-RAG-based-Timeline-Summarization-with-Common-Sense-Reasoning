321. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD, POONA,
March 17, 1946
DEAR MR. ABELL,
I thank you for your letter of 12th instant.
My son tells me that the Home Member has come to the
conclusion that Shri Vidyarthi's case is all false regarding Delhi.
Is my son's interpretation of the Home Member's finding correct?
You will please answer my question when you tell me of his find-
ing after investigations in Nagpur are complete.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 65
