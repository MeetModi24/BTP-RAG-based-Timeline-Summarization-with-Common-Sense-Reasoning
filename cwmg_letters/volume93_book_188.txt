188. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 27, 1937
CHI. ANAND, VIDYA,
I have your letter.
Vidya must quietly stay there, whether she gets well or not.
You cannot find a better place than Bhowali. It is good for the
child also. Both of you should study the Ramayana and under-
stand it properly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C. W. 11067. Courtesy: Anand T.
Hingorani
