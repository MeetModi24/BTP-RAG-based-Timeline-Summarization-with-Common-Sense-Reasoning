784. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
POONA,
October 29, 1945
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter just now and I reply before going for
massage.
I am not leaving Sevagram, must not, having founded home
for so many institutions. I must now contradict and correct the
report. I could not desert Sardar. I must come to you after
doing the Frontier or [before J going there. Everything is post-
poned by a month.
Let us trust to God guiding us. He may so compass that
ultimately I may have to live with you. Wish nothing except to
do His will.
Sorry about Balvantsinha. No more time.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6512. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9907
