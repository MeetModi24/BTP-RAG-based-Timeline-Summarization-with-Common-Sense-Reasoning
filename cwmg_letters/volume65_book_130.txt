130. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
April 24, 1937
CHI. SHARDA1,
Received your letter. Nothing is permanent. Stick to the
treatment so long as you feel that it is doing you no harm. Both
of you must take onions and garlic. It is my opinion
that
nature-cure, if intelligently taken up, is bound to benefit. Write
to me in detail about the treatment you are taking and about
your diet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9975. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
