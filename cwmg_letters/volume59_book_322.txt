322. LETTER TO AMALI SAID ABDUL HADI
November 6, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. India is a very big place and unless I
have much fuller particulars about your father than you have
given it is impossible to trace him.
Yours sincerely,
AMALI SAID ABDUL HADI, ESQ.
C/O HAJ BAHA ABDUL HADI
NABLUS, PALESTINE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
