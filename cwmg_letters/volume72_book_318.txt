318. LETTER TO RADHA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 12, 1940
CHI. RADHA,
I hope Mother's passing away has not filled you with des-
pair. She lived quite long, and God fulfilled many aspirations of
her. She has left you all in a happy state. Of course however
long a mother lives the children are bound to miss her when she
dies. One must bear the loss patiently.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXIII
