135. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
November 1, 1940
CHI. NARAHARI,
Vasumatibehn says that as I am contemplating a fast, she is
not inclined to go there. I tell her that she ought not to neglect
her duty and stay on here, to which she replies that she does
not have there any such responsible work to attend to. I told
her that you would decide about that. She has agreed to that.
You have to decide only from the point of view of dharma. If
she stays here, it won't be to serve me. There are many others
who will do that.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI NARAHARI PARIKH/VASUMATIBEHN
HARIJAN ASHRAM, SABARMATI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9120
