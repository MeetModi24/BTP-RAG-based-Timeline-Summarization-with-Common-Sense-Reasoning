82. LETTER TO MATHURADAS PURUSHOTTAM
January 5, 1931
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. I will now find some time and read
about the carding-bow. This time I got the post two days
late. You have got a good band of workers with you. You
should only think about the work and not feel worried about it.
Never doubt that you are doing God's work, provided, of course,
you do it in the spirit of yajna. I have read what you have written
about carding. It is good. When you gain further experience,
you will be able to make more definite statements.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3751
