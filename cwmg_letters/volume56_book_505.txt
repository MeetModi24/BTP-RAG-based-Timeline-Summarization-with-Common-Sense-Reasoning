505. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
January 15, 1934
CHI. PREMA,
I am writing this only to tell you that I will await the long
letter which you propose to write.
Kisan is quite well. I cannot give her as much attention as
I would like to.
Go through all the issues of Harijan, both Gujarati and Eng-
lish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10353. Also C.W. 6792. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
