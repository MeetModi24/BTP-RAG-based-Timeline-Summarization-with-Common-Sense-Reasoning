297. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 11, 1930
CHI. MAHAVIR,
I got your letter. Who else is there with you in Siddhapur?
Wherever you go, write to me regularly and give me all news,
whether important or unimportant.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6219
