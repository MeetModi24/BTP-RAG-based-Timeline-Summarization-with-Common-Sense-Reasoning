381. LETTER TO ATMARAM RAMCHANDANI
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
BHAI ATMARAM,
Jairamdas has given me a cheque for Rs. 500/-. I shall use the
amount for public work of some kind.
I hope that you will devote your time to constructive work as
suggested by me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Atmaram Ramchandani Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
