43. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
November 6, 1945
BAPA,
I have your letter as also the cheque for Lila Jog.
I shall
of course forward your letter to Mridulabehn. Still it is cer-
tainly right to send the cheque.
What you say about Sushila Pai is right. It was never my
intention to make one of the two permanent. But as long as
Sucheta stays there, whether as a candidate or for examinations,
she will be considered a candidate for the post of organizing
secretary. Won't she? So the same should hold good in the
case of Sushila Pai also. She also wanted it that way. But I
saw that Sucheta did not like it. So if Sushila Pai goes there it
will be just to observe the work and to see if she can adjust her-
self in the office. Sucheta is going to let me know her decision
only after seeing you.
BAPU
THAKKAR BAPA
BAJAJ WADI, WARDHA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
