131. LETTER TO KANTI AND KANU GANDHI
May 23, 1935
CHI. KANTI AND KANU,
Please understand that I was very eager to take you with me
but I had to restrain my desire. I have no doubt that it is for the
best. But there is no doubt, either, that you are wanting to accom-
pany me. I hope everything is going on all right. Take as much
interest in prayers as you can. Kanu must learn the chapters of
the Gita by heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7297. Courtesy: Kantilal
Gandhi
