430. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day [May 26, 1930]
DEAR JANAKIBEHN,
How are you? I hope you have not lost your courage. How
is Madalasa? Do not be anxious about Kamalnayan. Have you
not learnt this much by hearing Gita from Vinoba that we need
not be anxious about anything?
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in
India, Vol. III, Part III, p. 575
