159. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
June 30, 1930
CHI. NIMU,
I got your letter. Nobody will object to your experimenting with juwar in
order to improve your health. Try it once and if it does not agree with you,
give it up. Moreover, when our dear ones are in jail, we may certainly eat
what they do, provided it is possible for us to do so. You may stay wherever
you like. Blessings to Ba. You seem to be quite engrossed in your work.
'Sumitra' is a good enough name for the baby.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
What is Navanit doing?
