318. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
August 1, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
No time. No letter from you today. Kanu is going to
Rajkot. His mother has lost one eye through glaucoma and is
in danger of losing the other.
Poor boy!
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Prabha has returned.
SHRI RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
MANORVILLE
SIMLA
From the original: C.W. 4040. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7349
