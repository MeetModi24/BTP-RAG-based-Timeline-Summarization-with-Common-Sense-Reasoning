350. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 14, 1938
BHAI JIVANJI,
What kind of an article do you expect from me? Should
you not leave me out of it? You should give me a reward if I
can carry out even your other assignments.
I understand about the Autobiography.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9946. Also C.W. 6921. Courtesy:
Jivanji D. Desai
