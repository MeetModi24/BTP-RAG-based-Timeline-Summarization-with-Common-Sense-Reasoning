307. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Tuesday, Sravana Sud 2 [August 10, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have your letter, and also Ghanshyamdas's. And your
telegram too. It was good that you went to Sikar. Please see
that now you do not change your mind about coming here
direct from there. Ghanshyamdas tells me that your health, too,
is none too good. I was alarmed by the news.
More when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2872
