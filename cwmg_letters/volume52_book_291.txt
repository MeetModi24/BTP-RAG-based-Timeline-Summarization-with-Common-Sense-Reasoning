291. LETTER TO PYARELAL NAYYAR
December 17, 1932
You seem to have taken a vow not to write to me. Well,
I must live as God wills, or as Pyarelal wills. If your health is
good and you have nothing to write to me about, I am quite
content.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, pp. 345-
