632. LETTER TO NANDLAL PATEL
SIMLA,
June 27, 1945
CHI. NANDLAL,
Prabhakar has sent a wire saying that Hari-ichchha is dying
a slow death. I hope that Hari-ichchha is keeping up her spirit.
Of course you two will be showing great courage too. If we fear
death it shows our faith in God is shaken or we lack it. Life and
death are in His hands. We should live in full realization of this.
I have not taken it for granted that Hari-ichchha is going to
die. The saying "As long as there is breath, there is hope" has
so often come true.
I cannot say when I shall be able to come there. But I hope
it will be soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
