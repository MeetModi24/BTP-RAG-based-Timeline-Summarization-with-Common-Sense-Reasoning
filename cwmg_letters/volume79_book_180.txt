180. LETTER TO P. SRIRAMULU
February 6, 1945
DEAR RAMULU,
You are right. By mistake your letters were not shown to
me. I am glad of the reformed remarriage. I hope both are
happy and doing well. Do report about your progress. I know
you have difficulties to contend with.
Yours,
BAPU
