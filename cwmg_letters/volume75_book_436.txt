436. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
February 27, 1942
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
I am stupid. I forgot to tell you yesterday that the A. I. S. A.
had tried to keep within and under the law. But I will not weary
you with the technicalities. You know how the law often floors
the lawyers. This case is one such. But no more at the present
juncture.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Transfer of Power, Vol. I, p. 255
