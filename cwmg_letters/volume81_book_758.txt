758. LETTER TO P. N. KAUL
October 26, 1945
BHAI KAUL,
Do barristers forget their mother tongue?
Maulana Saheb is doing something about Caveeshar. I shall
certainly take necessary action when required. There is no
question of forgetting. He will certainly be released.
P. N. KAUL, ADVOCATE
83 EXPRESS ROAD
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
