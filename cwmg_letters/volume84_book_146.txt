146. LETTER TO SHRIKRISHNADAS JAJU
SIMLA,
May 7, 1946
BHAI JAJUJI 1 ,
Your letter. I am still under the impression that there is no
[sales tax] on khadi. But whom shall we fight? Now the work
is in the hands of the ministers. Whom should I write to? It
would be better to ask Annada. He may do as he wishes. Can
they not sell Borkamta khadi in Borkamta itself? Many questions
arise. Ask Satis Babu as well. And then leave it to them.
I don't want to claim that I have pondered fully over it but I
have helped others to think about it.
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
