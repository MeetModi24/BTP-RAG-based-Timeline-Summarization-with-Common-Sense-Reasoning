26. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
SABARMATI,
[On or before March 10, 1922]
CHI. DEVDAS,
You are making separation from you more and more unbear-
able to me every day. I feel it, however much I wish that I did
not. However, at a time like this, separation is the proper thing.
I have already given you whatever advice I wanted to. The best
thing for you now is to go to jail in an innocent manner, that is,
without thinking of your safety, you may plunge yourself in any
danger that comes your way. And if riots break out anywhere, I
