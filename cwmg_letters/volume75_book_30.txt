30. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
October 16, 1941
MY DEAR C. R.,
Your brother is on his trial. May God give him peace. I
had a sweet little note from him.
I shall expect you on 21 st. V[allabhbhai] will be here by then.
I fear I cannot accept the compliment for knowing slang better
than you. I did not even know that 'O. K.' and 'A 1' were slang. So
you see the poverty of my language. Anyway it won't do for you to
be worse than you were in jail. And you know the consequence!!
Asaf Ali leaves for Calcutta tomorrow. All eyes are on you,
including mine!
Your son-in-law has now become a distinguished journalist.
What about Papa ?
Love.
BAPU
