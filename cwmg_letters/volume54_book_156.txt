156. LETTER TO NARMADABEHN RANA
March 19, 1933
CHI. NARMADA,
I got your letter. If you are really eager to learn the Gita and
music, you will certainly succeed in learning them. But what-
ever you learn you should learn thoroughly.
My compliments to all if they feel interested in working in the
fields.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 2774. Courtesy: Ramnarayan N. Pathak
