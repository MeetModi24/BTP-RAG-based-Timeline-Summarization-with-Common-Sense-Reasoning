300. LETTER TO MANJULA M. MEHTA
January 15, 1942
CHI. MANJULA,
I had your letter. There was one from Magan, too. Can't you
stay in the room next to Champa's? If you cannot do that, will you
stay in the same block? If you cannot do even that, you may stay
in my room. You will not be a burden to me. You will get there
privacy, too. I will not mind. . .2 In short, I will make whatever
arrangements you wish.
I am going to Benares on the 19th and will return on the 24th
or the 25th. Come after that. You may come even during my
absence.
Ratilal is in a miserable plight in Rajkot. I would still advise
Magan to go there. He will be able to find an attendant. I cannot
find one from here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 1615. Courtesy: Manjulabehn M. Mehta
