440. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 15, 1930
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
All right, I drop 'Dear Sister'. However I may have addres-
sed you, I tried to be like a father. Daily I pray to God to make
me worthy of your love and confidence. The thought of women
like you makes me feel the hand of God in the yajna of self-
purification. Your Hindi is so lucid that I don't find any trouble
in understanding it. The truth is that language is inadequate for
heartfelt emotions. They just manifest themselves. Krishnadas is
now released. Welcome him as one of your own. I have a
letter from him. Anyway I have written to him to see you.
My letter about the Gita must have reached you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1676
