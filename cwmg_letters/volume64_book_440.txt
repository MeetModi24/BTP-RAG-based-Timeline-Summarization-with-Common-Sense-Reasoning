440. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON,
March 10, 1937
MY DEAR REBEL,
Herewith Bishop Moore's letter and copy of my reply.
Please return after perusal. If you have read the appeal and you
do not agree with the Bishop's reading, you should pass on to him
your own interpretation.
Your wire gave me great joy.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3765. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6921
