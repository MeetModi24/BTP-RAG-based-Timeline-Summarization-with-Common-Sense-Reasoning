182. LETTER TO SULTANA RAZIA
SEVAGRAM,
November 25, 1941
MY DEAR SULTANA,
I was delighted to have your letter. The four propositions
you have laid down with the approval of the Maulvi Sahib are
sound. But how to secure universal acceptance for them is the
question. They form a basis for joint action. If you have been
able to assure Maulvi Sahib of my bona fides the next step for us
is to meet and discuss the ways and means. If he can take the
trouble to come here, this is a good time. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
too is here. You will ascertain his wish and let me know.
As to the fifth proposition I have doubts. It demands clarifica-
tion and mutual discussions.
I hope you are keeping well. When are you returning? We
all miss you.
Did you not take with you that book on women? I miss it on
the shelf. I have an idea that you borrowed it.
Love.
BAPU
BIBI SULTANA RAZIA
AUNDER KOT
MEERUT
