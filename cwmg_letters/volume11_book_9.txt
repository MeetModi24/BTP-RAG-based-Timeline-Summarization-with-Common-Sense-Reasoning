9. LETTER TO L. W. RITCH
[CAPE TOWN,]
Wednesday [April 5, 1911]
MY DEAR RITCH,
Your wire. I shall not run away hastily. I wrote a short
note to Lane saying I was staying for some time to see the other
members. He sent a note in reply asking me to see him at once.
On going to him he gave me the message from J. C. S. to the
effect that I should reduce to writing my two proposals. He
added that Smuts did not want me to go away empty-handed
and said that the matter must be settled during this session. Our
friends may derive what hope they can from this.
Have been lobbying the whole afternoon. More tomorrow.
I have to submit my proposal tomorrow after 10-30 a.m.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 5417
