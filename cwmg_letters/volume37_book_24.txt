24. LETTER TO MASOOD JUNG BAHADUR
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for a copy of your Convocation Address at the
Karve University. I had the cutting made for me from the
papers which contained extracts from it and you might have
noticed in Young India I have already dealt with the subject-
matter of your address. I shall now give myself the pleasure of
reading it through as soon as I find a few spare moments.
Yours sincerely,
NAWAB MASOOD JUNG BAHADUR
DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC INSTRUCTION
H.E.H. NIZAM'S GOVERNMENT, HYDERABAD, DECCAN
From a photostat: S.N. 13449
