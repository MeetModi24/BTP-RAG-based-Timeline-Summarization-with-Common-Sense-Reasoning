323. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Sravana Shukla 4 [August 12, 1926]
BHAISHRI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your letter . Well do I know that no one except
Malaviyaji and Shraddhanandji can bring about Hindu-Muslim
unity. I wish to be no more than an advisor and help, if I can,
to settle small points of dispute. My work is a Bhangi's, to clean
and try to keep clean things. When the time for a settlement
comes, the approval of Malaviyaji and others will certainly be
essential.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6131. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
