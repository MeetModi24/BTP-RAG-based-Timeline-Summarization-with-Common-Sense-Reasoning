223. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 5, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
I have your English letter.
J.'s letter was received today. He says he is ready to come
when Maulana comes. There is nothing from Maulana.
I have told Rajaji I am not going to engage in a public
controversy with him.
Hot wind is blowing.
I had expected the weather to
cool down by this time. Having had the expectation, it is
disconcerting to find the expectation unfulfilled. "Blessed are
they that expect nothing."
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4128. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7437
