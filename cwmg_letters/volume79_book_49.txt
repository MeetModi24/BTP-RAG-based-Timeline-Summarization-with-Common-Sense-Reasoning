49. LETTER TO ANATH NATH BASU
SEVAGRAM,
January 14, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have gone through your paper. It reads well on paper but
it appears to me to be largely unpractical. I feel that the new
type is made of sterner stuff and is revolutionary in the true sense
of the term. I should like you to think deeper and in terms of
the starving millions of the villages.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI ANATH NATH BASU
C/O SHRI ARYANAYAKUM
