368. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
August 12, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
You have given me good news. I hope the improvement
will be steady.
You will see the message I have issued regarding Gurudev.
Appa of Aundh is coming here for a week. The Burma depu-
tation on 15th. And so the day fills in. Dr. Shyama Prasad wants
me to preside at a Gurudev meeting in Calcutta. I have wired
'No' for the simple reason that I must finish Andrews Memorial
first.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4050. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7359
