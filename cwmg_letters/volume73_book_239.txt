239. LETTER TO PATWARDHAN
November 30, 1940
BHAI PATWARDHAN,
I have your letter. I got the earlier one also. I could not reply
promptly as I was busy with other things. I am still very weak.
I have not been able to ask Dada Dharmadhikari. I shall do
so now. Clarification is necessary because Sahasrabuddhe's doubts
should be cleared. The money will be put to good use only when
the school runs smoothly and the money is easily available. I will
not be useful as a Panch but if I can convince you and Sahasrabud-
dhe, let me know the correct position on 1st December. When I
can deal with the whole thing and if I feel the need of sending
for all of you, I shall do so.
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
