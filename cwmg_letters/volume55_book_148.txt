148. LETTER TO NIRMALABEHN B. MASHRUWALA
May 7, 1933
I know that countless men and women are unhappy. But
can there ever be a birth without agony? We are struggling for
a new birth. I, therefore, expect nothing but good from this great
agony. Remain patient and do whatever service you can.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, pp. 294-
