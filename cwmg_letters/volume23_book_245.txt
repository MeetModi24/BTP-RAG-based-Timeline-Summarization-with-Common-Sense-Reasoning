245. LETTER TO A. G. ADVANI
POST ANDHERI,
March 29, 1924
DEAR MR. ADVANI1,
I have your letter.
I knew nothing of what you referred to, but I am immediately
doing all I can to ascertain the truth. I would like you to send
me all the proof you have in support of your statement. I take it
that you do not want me to regard your letter as confidential, be-
cause I must use it if I am to know the truth. I do not wish to
publish it in the Press without it being absolutely necessary, and
certainly not before I hear from you.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. A. G. ADVANI
S. J. CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETY
ELPHINSTONE STREET
CAMP KARACHI
From a photostat: S.N. 8626
