129. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
November 12, 1935
CHI. MANIBEHN,
Show the remarks on the reverse to Father. I hear that
everybody was extremely pleased with the way Jawaharlal conduc-
ted himself.
Father must be in excellent spirit and making the doctors
laugh with his jokes. Don't neglect your own health.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 118
