82. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 28, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. Where does Mrityunjaya live? What are
his present activities? Does Mataji feel the separation from
Rajendra Babu? How is Vidyavati's health?
My diet continues to be the same. The weight remains
steady at 103-104. That is not bad.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3391
