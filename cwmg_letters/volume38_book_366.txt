366. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
CALCUTTA,
Silence Day, December 31, 1928
DEAR SISTERS,
I expect this to be my last letter to you. According to pre-
sent indications I should be there on Sunday morning.
Today I have only enough time to warn you that on coming
there I shall ask all of you to render an account of your activities.
Where is the need now to write anything new? If you have settled
down, if you have been able to bring quiet in the kitchen, and
if you are regular in attending prayers, I should think that you
have done a great deal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3687
