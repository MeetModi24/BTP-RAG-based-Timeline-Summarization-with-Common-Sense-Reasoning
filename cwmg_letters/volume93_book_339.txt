339. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEGAON,
November 14, 1938
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I should be happy if both the scripts were made compul-
sory. There is no question of you or anyone else coming to
replace Mahadev. In a way, those who do their duty in their
respective positions are also helping.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHETH MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
74 WALKESHWAR ROAD
BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
