455. LETTER TO M. B. GODBOLE
May 12, 1936
DEAR DR. GODBOLE,
Please forgive me for my inability to acknowledge your letter
of 15th March last earlier. It was impossible as I was moving
to and fro. I was able to read it only the other day. I am sorry
to have to disappoint you for going through Dr. Lindlahr's volu-
mes. It did not strike me that reprinting of the volumes would
be of much use. Some of the conclusions of the author are dog-
matic and some others are based on insufficient observation.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
DR. M. B. GODBOLE
P.B. 19
POONA CITY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
