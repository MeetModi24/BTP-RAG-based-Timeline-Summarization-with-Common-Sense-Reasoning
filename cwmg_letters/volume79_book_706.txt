706. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 22, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
The two things mentioned in today's letter which I have
not scratched out deserve attention. Have you done anything
about them?
It shows that all of us, new members and old, men, women
and children, should learn Hindustani. The necessary literature
for that purpose should be collected.
There is no truth as regards the bullocks. I think It is per-
fectly right about the dogs. Torturing is worse than killing.
In my absence, the administration must be improved.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10625
