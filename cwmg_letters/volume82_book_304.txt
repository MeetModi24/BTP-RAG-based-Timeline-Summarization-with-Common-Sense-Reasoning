304. LETTER TO R. G. CASEY
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR,
December 16, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have insistent complaints about goondaism and favouritism
at the elections. We had a talk about these. But they seem
to persist. I know you do not want these things. Can something
be done in the matter?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, pp. 113-
