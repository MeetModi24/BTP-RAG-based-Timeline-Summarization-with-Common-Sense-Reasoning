338. LETTER TO PYARELAL
[After November 9, 1938]
CHI. PYARELAL,
I had given you two items to be condensed for Harijan.
I shall do the condensing myself if you pass them on to me.
I can see that you are overburdened.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
