277. LETTER TO JAIKRISHNA P. BHANSALI
PANCHGANI,
July 16, 1944
CHI. BHANSALI,
Discipline demands that you should ask me. If you do not
observe it, who else will? But what guidance can I give you in
this matter? If you have faith in yourself, by all means go and
relieve the suffering of the people. I must admit, however, that
you have excelled me and I derive comfort from the thought
that my teaching has proved fruitful. Isn't he a true teacher who
is surpassed by his own pupil? May God increase your strength
still further.
I keep on reminding myself daily that I must go there as
early as possible in August. The climate here does suit me, of
course. Inform everybody. Manilal and Sita are arriving today.
Blessings from
BAPU
