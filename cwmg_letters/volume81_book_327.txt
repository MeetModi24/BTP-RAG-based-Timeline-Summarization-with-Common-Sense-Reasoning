327. LETTER TO D. PARIMALA
POONA,
August 31, 194[5]
DEAR SISTER,
The Gita does answer your doubts, if you will search diligent-
ly. If you do not doubt the fleeting present, how can you doubt
the future? Let the old lady suffer. We may not judge what is
good for her.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SMT. D . PARIMALA
2681 V. V. MINALLA
MYSORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
