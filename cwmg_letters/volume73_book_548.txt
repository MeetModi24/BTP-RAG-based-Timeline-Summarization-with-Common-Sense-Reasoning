548. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 24, 1941
CHI. KANTI,
I got your letter. May Shanti and you live long. May you
bring credit to the family and society and be true servants.
Ba has gone to Delhi to have treatment from Sushila. She
will stay there for a month or so. The piles had become painful.
Manu has a tumour and she may have to be operated upon. Saras-
wati must be fine.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
From the photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7363. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
