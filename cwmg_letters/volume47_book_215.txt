215. LETTER TO P. G. MATHEW
SURAT,
July 24, 1931
MY DEAR MATHEW,
Your letter. You will stay with me to your heart's content
when the real time comes. It is no use forcing the pace. But the
real staying with me is the working out of the ideal I stand for.
The other the physical contact may easily produce false satisfaction.
But this is not to wean you from the desire. This is to give you
patience during separation. What are you teaching at Vidyapith
and whom? Does it give you some satisfaction?
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1547
