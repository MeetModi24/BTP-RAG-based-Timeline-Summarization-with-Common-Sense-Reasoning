689. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
March 11, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letters and Pyarelal's. I have sent Kanchan
today. Sushila has to be sent back. I do hope it will be con-
venient. Tell Pyarelal that I am not writing to him since he is
coming on Friday. I am deeply immersed in work.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers.
Nehru
Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
