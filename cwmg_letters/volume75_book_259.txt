259. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
December 28, 1941
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
Babudi has gone to Surat. Anand had cough and felt uncom-
fortable in the crowded atmosphere here. They will come here on
Tuesday to get Anand's stitches removed. I shall try and detain
them.
Shakaribehn has gone to Ahmedabad. Ba has gone to Maroli
for four days.
I hope you are doing well.
What did you do about the book and the money with Suren-
dra? Does Lakshmidas wish now to work under Balvantsinha?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10606
