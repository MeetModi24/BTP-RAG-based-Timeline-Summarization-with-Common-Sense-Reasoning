550. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
DELHI,
September 24, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Narandas is a worker, not a writer. Therefore the descrip-
tion of things has to come from you and no one else. Chh.
does write but he won't have the time to give me details. And
they are all afraid to burden me with descriptions.
Well, if your suggestion had been received by me it would
have helped me. However I accept your apology. Only don't
do it again.
The weather for me is ideal. The blood-pressure is ideal,
almost always 160-164/100-104.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3881. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7037
