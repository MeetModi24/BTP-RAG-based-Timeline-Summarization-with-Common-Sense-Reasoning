280. LETTER TO SECRETARY, KASTURBA MEMORIAL FUND
BIRLA HOUSE,
BOMBAY,
September 9, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
I am enclosing herewith a scheme outlining the curriculum for the
education of women for your consideration.
Yours sincerely,
SECRETARY
KASTURBA NATIONAL MEMORIAL FUND COMMITTEE
SCINDIA HOUSE
BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
