369. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 15, 1937
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I feel ashamed that I am not able to write to you. I do
remember that I have to write but can't because of the pressure of
work. I will be more careful hereafter, even if it is only two
lines that I can write, as is the case today. But please be
content since there are long letters from Ba and Manu.
Krause has arrived. Probably he will be going back without
seeing me because of two day's delay on my part. He got my letter
late. I have a long letter from him. I have suggested that he
should see Devdas.
Blessings from
BAPU
