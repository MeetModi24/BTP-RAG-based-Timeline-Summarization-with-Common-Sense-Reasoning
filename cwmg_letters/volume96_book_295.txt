295. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
October 20, 1930
MY DEAR HENRY,
I had your and Millie's loving message. You are never absent from
my mind. How is Leon ?
My love to you all,
BHAI
PROPERTY OF H. S. L. POLAK
265 STRAND
LONDON, W. C. R.
From the original: Gandhi-Polak Correspondence. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
