194. LETTER TO SHUAIB QURESHI
SEVAGRAM,
October 4, 1944
MY DEAR SHWAIB,
I have done the best I could. Now you have to take up
the thread, unless you think I have made a mess of the whole
thing. If I have, I know you will be frank enough to tell me so.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
