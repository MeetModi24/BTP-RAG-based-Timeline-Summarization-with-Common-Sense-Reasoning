197. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
May 12, 1945
CHI. RAMDAS,
Are both of you too lazy or only showing pity on me?
This is for you to read. I am well. Manilal is here. Sushila
and the children have not come. How are you? How is Nimu ?
I receive letters from Kanam. He may come here.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
How is Usha 3 ?
SHRI RAMDAS GANDHI
TOMCO SALES DEPARTMENT
KHALASI LINES, NAGPUR
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
1 Son of C. Rajagopalachari
2 & 3 Nirmala (wife), and daughter of the addressee
