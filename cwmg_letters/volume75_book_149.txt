149. LETTER TO MAGANLAL AND MANJULA MEHTA
November 14, 1941
CHI. MAGAN AND MANJULA,
I have letters of you both.
I understand about you. Khersaheb and I were so busy, each
with his own affairs, that we could meet only for a few minutes. I
therefore forgot to talk to him about you. I will now write to
him and inquire.
The plan for the building was sent by Chimanlalbhai at the
Mani Bhuvan address. If you have received it, let him know your
decision about it.
About Ratubhai I believe that you should go and fetch him
and keep him with you. Let him have the taste of brotherly love.
When you come here, you will of course bring him along with you.
If Champa arrives in December we shall see what can be done. I
agree with Manju's analysis.
I am glad that Munno often mentions Sevagram. Only if the
other children too feel the same way, will they enjoy staying
here. If they cannot forget their attachment to city life, they will
never be happy in Sevagram.
Blessings from
BAPU
