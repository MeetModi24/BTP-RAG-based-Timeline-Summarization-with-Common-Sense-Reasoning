253. LETTER TO MANUBEHN AND SURENDRA
MASHRUWALA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 29, 1939
CHI. MANUDI, SURENDRA,
Ba gave me the news about Surendra's illness. How is he
now? I could not reply to your letter. I am enclosing a letter
to Kunvarji. You will forward it to him, won't you? I will
be going again to Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1575. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
