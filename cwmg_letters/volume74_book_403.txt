403. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
August 21, 1941
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I was indeed afraid that you would be released. What else
could they do? Now you must get completely well before re-
suming work. There is, of course, a lot to be done. I will not
be easy in my mind till the operation is over. Keep me regularly
informed. Did they give you my letters?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 250
