49. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
CALCUTTA,
August 13, 1947
CHI. RADHAKRISHNA,
I am entrapped here. Well, we have to live as God wills.
I got your letter regarding the goshala. I have only to say
that you must keep Sevagram supplied with milk. It is impos-
sible for me to go into details. I am writing under great pres-
sure of work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
