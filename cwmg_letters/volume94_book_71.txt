71. LETTER TO PYARELAL
September 18, 1941
CHI. PYARELAL,
What a letter from you ! A tonga will be arranged if you let me
know when you are coming.
You are right about the teeth. Only Bareto should do it.
How will it be a burden to me if Mother comes here ? Ba gets
along well with her. She cannot be a burden to anyone here. She can
stay on freely if her health remains satisfactory. If she suffers in health
we shall see. It would be a matter of satisfaction to her that if she
was here she could see you oftener.
Your dejection must go. It should be remembered that there is no
reason for it at all.
Blessings from
BAPU
[P.S.]
Mira could have gone with Devdas for a day.
