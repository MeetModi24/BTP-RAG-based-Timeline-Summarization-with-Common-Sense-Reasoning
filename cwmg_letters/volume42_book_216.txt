216. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
MIRZAPUR,
November 19, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I have your letter. I myself find it difficult to understand
my telegram of which you have sent me a copy. The message
does not seem to have been transmitted correctly. Be that as
it may. The Bombay Women's Council workers use English
thread for embroidery work on khadi, but their articles are not
boycotted for that reason. This practice has been going on for
many years. Hence follow it this time too. I have no time to
write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5470
