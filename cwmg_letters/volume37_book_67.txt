67. LETTER TO ABID ALI JAFFERBHAI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 13, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
Here is the message for your Annual:
Those who live in places outside India such as Singapore
should bear in mind that they are the trustees for the honour of
the nation to which they belong and therefore their conduct
should be above board and absolutely honest with strangers in
whose midst they are living. They should also retain the bond
with the poorest in the motherland by the use of khadi.
Yours sincerely,
ABID ALI JAFFERBHAI
YOSUF BUILDING-C, MOUNT ROAD, MAZGAON, BOMBAY 10
From a photostat: S.N. 14568
