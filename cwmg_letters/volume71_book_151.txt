151. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 22, 1940
MY DEAR DESHABHAKTA,
I have your letter. Your decision is correct. I think you
should withdraw from all organizations and devote yourself to such
service as you can render from your seat. May God keep you for
many years.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3224
