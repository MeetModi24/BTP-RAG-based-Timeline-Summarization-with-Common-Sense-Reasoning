210. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
SEVAGRAM,
August 19, 1946
CHI. RADHAKRISHNA,
I learn from Prabhudayal's diary that in Nalwadi, the place
from which milk is distributed is very dirty. How far is the
complaint true? If there is truth in it, study the rules of dairy
hygiene and follow them. Our goseva must be ideal.
Blessings from
BAPU
RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
