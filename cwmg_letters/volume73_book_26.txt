26. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
[After September 22, 1940]
What is the sense in my sending messages every now and
then?
Even if I enumerate nine hundred ninety-nine virtues of
khadi, those who lack faith in it will not acquire any.
It can
come only through effort and God's grace.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8681. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
