30. LETTER TO PAUL RICHARD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 10, 1922
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
If I am free I expect to be in Surat on my way to Bardoli on
Sunday morning. Do please come to Bardoli that day. I would
like to have a long chat with you.
I am publishing your statement .
Yours sincerely,
M. PAUL RICHARD
BHARATIYA BUNGALOW
ATHVA LINES
SURAT
From a photostat: S.N. 7982; also G.N. 869
