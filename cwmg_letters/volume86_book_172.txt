172. LETTER TO VIJAYA
November 20, 1946
CHI. VIJAYA,
You have been married for quite some time but I am able
to write to you only today. I intended and wished to write to
you earlier. I trust your married life is happy and the capa-
city of both of you to render service has further increased as
both of you are inclined to serve. You will have recovered. I
hope the climate of Sevagram is suiting Apteji.
Sushila must be writing to you about the conditions pre-
vailing here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 3161
