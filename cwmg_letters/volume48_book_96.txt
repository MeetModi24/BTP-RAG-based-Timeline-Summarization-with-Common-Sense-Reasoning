96. LETTER TO E. DOLBY SHELTON
October 6, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to receive your letter and to recall the
pleasant days I spent under your roof at Ventnor.
I have not seen the Vegetarian News. I was a born vegetarian,
but I had lapsed from my vegetarianism owing to foolish compa-
nionship in youth. On coming to London I became a convinced
vegetarian, through having read Mr. Salt's essay.
Is this quite clear?
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 17889
