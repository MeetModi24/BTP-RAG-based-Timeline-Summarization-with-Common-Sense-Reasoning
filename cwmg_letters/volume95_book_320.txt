320. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
CHI. K.C.,
It is our duty to do as Shakaribehn says. If after Sharadabehn
comes Shakaribehn wants to cook separately, we must bear the
expense. If Shakaribehn wants some fruit we must lovingly satisfy
her. Shakaribehn has suffered much. Whether Shardabehn pays
anything or not is a separate question. We cannot insist on it. Her
