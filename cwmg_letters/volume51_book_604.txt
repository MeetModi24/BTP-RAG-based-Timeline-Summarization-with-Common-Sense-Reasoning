604. LETTER TO KIKI LALWANI
November 8, 1932
It is true that my companions know no such thing as rest.
What can one do? God himself has said in the Gita that He
does not relax even for a moment, that He needs neither sleep nor
food nor drink. How then can any rest be granted to us?
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 218
