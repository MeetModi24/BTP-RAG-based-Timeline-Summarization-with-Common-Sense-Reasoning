395. LETTER TO JETHALAL G. SAMPAT
July 21, 1935
BHAI JETHALAL,
May the child get well soon.
I shall wait for an account of your change of heart.
I understand your suggestions. I shall not do anything in a
hurry. Since you are so near, would it not be better if you went
to Anantpur via Wardha?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 9853. Courtesy: Narayan J. Sampat
