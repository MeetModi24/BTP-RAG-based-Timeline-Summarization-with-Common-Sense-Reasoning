204. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
July 14, 1934
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
Have Budhabhai [and] Juthabhai taken up some additional
work now? Try and remove Amalabehn's difficulties if you can.
Let me know if she is harassing you. I suppose you must have
asked that girl to leave the Ashram. I hope Ramabehn is staying
on there for the present.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4024
