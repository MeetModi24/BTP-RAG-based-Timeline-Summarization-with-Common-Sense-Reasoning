235. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 7, 1928
MY DEAR RAIHANA,
Better a dictated letter than none. My congratulations to
Sohaila and many kisses on both the cheeks, on the lips, on the
forehead and in the centre of the head of the baby.
I wish I had time to hear more of your songs.
Yours sincerely,
MISS RAIHANA TYABJI
CAMP BARODA
From a photostat: S.N. 13169
