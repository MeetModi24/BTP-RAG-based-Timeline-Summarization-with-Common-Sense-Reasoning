474. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
October 23, 1932
CHI. RAMA,
I hope Chhaganlal's arrest has not made you nervous. Do
you get letters from him? Do you visit him any time? Where
is he lodged? I put up with your negligence in writing to me
till now. I will not do so any longer. You should write to me
regularly. Have you recovered strength now? How do Dhiru
and Vimu behave?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5337
