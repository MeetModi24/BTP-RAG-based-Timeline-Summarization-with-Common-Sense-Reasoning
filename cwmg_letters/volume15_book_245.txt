245. LETTER TO KER
[April, 1919]
DEAR MR. KER2,
I had hoped to be able to be in Nadiad on Monday, but the
critical situation here has detained me in Bombay. Some of the
friends from Nadiad have been here and they tell me that those
who were involved in the cutting of telegraph wires are ready
to make a full confession but they want me to be in Nadiad at the
time they do so. I do not know when I shall be free to go over
there. I hope, however, that there will be no difficulty about
complying with the wishes of the people concerned.
From a photostat : G.N. 8227
