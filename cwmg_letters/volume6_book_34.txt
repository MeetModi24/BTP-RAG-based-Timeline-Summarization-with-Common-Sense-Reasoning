34. LETTER TO F. MACKARNESS
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 27, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I am much obliged to you for your letter of the 25th instant. I
have placed this suggestion before Sir William Wedderburn and he agrees
that there should be a meeting like the one mentioned by you. I ven-
ture to think that, unless local circumstances prevent it, it need not neces-
sarily be a meeting of Liberal Members only, as there is no division of
opinion on the question of the treatment of British Indians in South
Africa.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
F. MACKARNESS, ESQ., M.P.
6, CROWN OFFICE Row
TEMPLE
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4414
