401. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
July 20, 1947
CHI. KANAIYO,
The enclosed letter is for you to see. Also the copy of my
reply. Give me detailed news about your health.
Abha is all right. The doctors say that hers is not a deep-
rooted malady; Manu's is. At present both of them are taking rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
