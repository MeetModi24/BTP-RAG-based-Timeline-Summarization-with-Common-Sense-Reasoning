50. LETTER TO SIR PRABHASHANKER PATTANI
[Before February 10, 1925]
I have a letter charging you with adultery. I had heard the
charge even at Bhavnagar when we were there. But I did not
believe it. Now it has come from a man whom I cannot ignore.
Can this be true? I was highly pleased at your straightforwardness
and courage. But what to do if this is true?
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
