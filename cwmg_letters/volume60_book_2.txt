2. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI G. PATEL
December 16, 1934
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
I got your letter. Give your wife the freedom that you have
reserved for yourself. In matters in which both do not agree, each
must be free to go his or her own way. She should, therefore,
get training for using her freedom well. I see the good of you
both in that.
Take the fullest interest in village industries work. And do
not forget that khadi is the centre of such work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9513. Also C.W. 429. Courtesy:
Shivabhai G. Patel
