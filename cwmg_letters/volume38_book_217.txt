217. LETTER TO V. G. TCHERKOFF
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
December 7, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I hope in the
near future to deal with the objections so gently raised by you.
Even if I don't convince you in the reply that I may attempt,
you will please believe me that expediency3, as I understand the
word, has no place in my scheme of life. Whatever I have done
in connection with war I have done believing it to have been my
duty for the moment.
Yours sincerely,
V. G. TCHERKOFF, ESQ.
PRESIDENT, Moscow VEGETARIAN SOCIETY
OULITZA OGAREVA 12, Moscow 9, U.S.S.R.
From a photostat: S.N. 15109
