452. LETTER TO P. A. WADIA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI
January 23, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Dr. Atkinson did not mention to me that
I should join the Committee It was perhaps because I did
not give him much encouragement. For I told him that I did
not have much faith in conferences, [for] promoting real goodwill
and brotherhood, something far more tangible than conferences was
necessary. And I must say that, on the whole, Dr. Atkinson did
not leave a good impression upon me.
Yours sincerely,
PROFESSOR P. A. WADIA
HORMUZD VILLA, MALABAR HILL, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 14888
1 Dated January 21, 1928 (S.N. 14887)
2 Central Committee of the Universal Religions Peace Conference for
India
