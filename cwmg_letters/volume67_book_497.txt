497. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[After September 14, 1938]
If you have faith in me, it does not become you to be
obstinate. Khan Saheb was only joking. However, you will
not be able to stay in the Frontier Province alone. I therefore
advise you to go to Bombay; improve your health there and come
back here fully restored when I return. You cannot always accom-
pany me on my tours. Can one obey orders and be obstinate at
the same time?
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 679
