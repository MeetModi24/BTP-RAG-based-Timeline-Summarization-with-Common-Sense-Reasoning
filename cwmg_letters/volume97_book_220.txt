220. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 18, 1932
CHI. NIMU,
So, after a long time you have condescended to write to me. Should I
thank you or twit your ear? Ramdas leaves it to you to decide about Kanu
Sheth's name. If you let me have a few names, I will help you make the
choice. Ramdas has now started taking milk and bread.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
