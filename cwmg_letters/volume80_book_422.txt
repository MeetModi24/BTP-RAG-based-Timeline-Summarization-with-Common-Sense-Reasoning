422. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
PANCHGANI,
June 3, 1945
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have your letter. Satis Babu will have fully recovered by now.
Do give khadi to the poor without yarn. Do you take money
from them and, if you do, do you charge the market rate or
less? After I know this, I shall write to you if I have anything
to say. I have the book about the cow read out to me when I
spin each day.
Blessings from
BAPU
HEMPRABHADEVI
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
SODEPUR, via CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
