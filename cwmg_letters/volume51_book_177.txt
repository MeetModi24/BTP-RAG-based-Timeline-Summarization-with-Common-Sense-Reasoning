177. LETTER TO JAISHANKAR P. TRIVEDI
September 20, 1932
I got your lines full of love. I know your love. God does not
live somewhere in the sky. For me pure love like yours is God,
and it is such love that gives me the strength to undertake yajnas
like the present one.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I I , p. 62
