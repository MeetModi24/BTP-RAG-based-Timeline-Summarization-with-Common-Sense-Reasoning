207. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 12, 1945
CHI. CHAND,
I have your letter. Your decision is good. I had written
to Viyogiji before I received your letter. Do what Satyavati
says. She should get well. If Satyavati agrees, you can come
back earlier. Show this letter to her. I had received her post-
card. Satyavati is always in my thoughts. If she is a soldier,
she is a great one. Khurshedbehn is in Panchgani.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
