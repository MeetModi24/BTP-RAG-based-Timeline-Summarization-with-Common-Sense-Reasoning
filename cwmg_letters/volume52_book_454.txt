454. LETTER TO A. KALESWAR RAO
January 2, 1933
MY DEAR KALESWAR RAO,
I was delighted to receive your letter and to have the greet-
ing and prayers from fellow-prisoners.
I have seen your contribution to the Press. Of course there
is no lack of precedent for introduction of Bills of a religious
character.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. A. KALESWAR RAO
PLEADER, BEZWADA
From a microfilm: S.N. 19137
