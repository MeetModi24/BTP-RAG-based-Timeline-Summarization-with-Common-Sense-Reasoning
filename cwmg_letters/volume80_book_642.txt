642. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
June 28, 1945
CHI. CHAND,
You seem to be extremely sensitive. No one has accused
you of telling a lie. You may understand a thing in one way,
another person in another way, while the truth may be a third
thing. But this matter is now over. Now the pleurisy has flared
up again. How can you study in this condition?
How did
you come to get pleurisy? This needs to be considered. How-
ever, there should be [no] hurry. Sushilabehn will be writing
to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
