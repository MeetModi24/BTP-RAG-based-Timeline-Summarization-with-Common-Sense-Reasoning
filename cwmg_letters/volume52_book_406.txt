406. LETTER TO M. R. RAMASWAMI
December 27, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I shall certainly
deal with the subject-matter of your letter publicly, but that
may take a little time. I may therefore give you this assurance,
that it is not open to those who have no faith in temple wor-
ship to join the movement for temple-entry, and I should be very
sorry indeed if any person who does not believe in temple wor-
ship joined the movement simply in order to destroy the vene-
ration for temples, and I should be very sorry to find reformers
resorting to abuse of sanatanists. I would like you to give me specific
instances in which case I would be able more effectively to deal
with cases of abuse.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. M. R. RAMASWAMI, B.A.,B.L.
VIVEKODAYAM, TRICHUR (COCHIN STATE)
