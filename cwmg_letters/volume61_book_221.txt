221. LETTER TO NIRUPAMA PANGALKAR
June 11, 1935
CHI. NIRUPAMA,
I have got your letter. There is a way of putting an end to
your weeping. You are still a child. You should not speak in
public for three or four years. Concentrate on studies. When you
153
grow up, you will start speaking on your own, and your self-
restraint will have strengthened you.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI NIRUPAMA PANGALKAR
SUDAMA KUTIR
UDIPI, KATAPADI, P. O.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9226
