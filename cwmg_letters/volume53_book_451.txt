451. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
February 18, 1933
MY DEAR GHANSHYAMDAS,
Lest you might have forgotten all about the contribution to
the Aaj through Baboo Bhagwandas, on account of his publishing
the learned opinions by way of supplement to the Aaj, if you
have not already sent anything, please do so now.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a copy: C.W. 7928. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
