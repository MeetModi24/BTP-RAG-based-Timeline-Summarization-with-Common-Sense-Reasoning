159. LETTER TO ATULANANDA CHAKRABARTY
July 8, 1936
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your letter. I wish you would appreciate my own diffi-
culty. My method of approach to the question is unfortunately
different from yours. I would therefore at least for the time
being suggest that you serve according to your own line. If your
method ever commends itself to me I should have no difficulty in
accepting it and for any organized efforts I would not do other-
wise than to refer you to the Congress. Do not expect any para-
graph from me. You will presently find that if I am to do the
village work on which I am bent, I should have to cease corres-
pondence at least for the time being.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 1475. Courtesy: A. K. Sen
