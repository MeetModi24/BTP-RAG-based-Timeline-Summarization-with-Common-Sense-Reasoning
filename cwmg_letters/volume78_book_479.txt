479. LETTER TO SATYACHARAN
SEVAGRAM,
November 25, 1944
BHAI SATYACHARANJI,
I have your letter. I am considering what I ought to do. I
am also having a talk with Ghanshyam Singhji.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SATYACHARANJI
D. A. V. HIGH SCHOOL
ALLAHABAD
