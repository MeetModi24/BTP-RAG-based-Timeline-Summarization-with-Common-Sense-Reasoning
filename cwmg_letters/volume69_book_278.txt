278. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
RAJKOT,
May 15, 1939
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
I sent you a letter from here. You saw what happened in
Bhavnagar? The lesson it has for you is that you must stay in
Ahmedabad and do the work of service. That will result one
day in Hindu-Muslim understanding.
I am all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 420
