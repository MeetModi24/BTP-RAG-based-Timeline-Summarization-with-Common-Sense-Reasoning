164. LETTER TO KANU R. GANDHI
[After May 1, 1945]
CHI. KANAM,
My congratulations on your standing first in the half-yearly
examination. And there are indications that you may stand first in my
examination, too. Your obstinacy seems to have disappeared. You
seem to have become wiser. But I think you are making one mis-
take. You seem to be resolved to become a learned man even at the
cost of your health. True education develops body, intellect and soul
in equal measure. Besides, don't you know the great saying 'The
body is the primary means for the performance of all dharmas'? If
my memory is deceiving me, ask Vinoba about it. For a human being
the soul never exists without the body. We can of course conceive
of a disembodied soul, but do not, cannot, see one. Hence the great
text : "Neti, neti". Therefore, take care of your health while
studying, even if that means your taking more time to cover the same
ground. It will be useless for you to come to me just now. My plans
are uncertain. And it is very hot. By all means go to Dharmadev
Shastri. Sumi writes to say you would like to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
