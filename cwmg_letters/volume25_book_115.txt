115. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
DELHI,
September 17, 1924
DEAR MOTILALJI,
I have your wire. I am in Delhi for sometime at any rate. I
shall therefore be delighted to see you and Mr. Das whenever you
come. I have taken what might be the final plunge. My fast of
21 days commences from today. That is how I have learnt to
understand religion.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
