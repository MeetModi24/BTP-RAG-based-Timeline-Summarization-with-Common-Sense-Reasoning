77. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 20, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
Haven't I invited all this heavy labour myself? How shall I
find time to read these essays on Nariman and education? Would
it not be proper to send the accompanying statement to the
Associated Press by wire? Do as you please. I understand what
you say about the fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11552
