101. LETTER TO A. K. BHAGWAT
NEW DELHI,
April 29, 1946
BROTHER BHAGWAT,
I have your letter. Amtulbehn has come over. I have
already written that you should go to Appa if you can. Yes, it
is true, he may not be able to send for you often.
Your work there is going on well and it is very important.
At present it is only a beginning. For making progress you will
have to confine yourself to it.
Blessings from
BAPU
