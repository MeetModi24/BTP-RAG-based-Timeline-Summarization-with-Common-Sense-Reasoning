477. LETTER TO AMRABAPA
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
September 21, 1945
DARBARSHRI,
I got your letter. I also received the sum of Rs. 51 (Rupees
fifty-one only). I will give it to Dr. Dinshaw to use it for a
charitable purpose.
I have a faint recollection of having met your father. I
wish you complete recovery.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
From the Gujarati original : C.W. 10228. Courtesy: Gajanan Joshi
