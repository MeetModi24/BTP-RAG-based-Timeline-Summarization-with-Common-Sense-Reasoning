301. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[After May 30, 1946]
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I shall be happy if you get well completely. After that you may
engage yourself in any service you like. My eagerness to press my
advice on you has left me, but my love remains the same as it ever
was. I am trying to overcome my moha. You can deduce the rest
from the above.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 599
