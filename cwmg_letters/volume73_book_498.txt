498. LETTER TO RAGHUVANSH GAUR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
March 9, 1941
BHAI RAGHUVANSH 1 ,
Rajkumaribehn went over to you on my instructions, and
conveyed only my views. The letter written to . . .2 behn is all
right. Now you should go home. Whatever can be done from
here will be done. Railway fare will be given to you, which
you will send back on reaching Kanpur.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 139
