208. LETTER TO GORUR RAMASWAMI IYENGAR
NEW DELHI,
[After September 14, 1947]
DEAR RAMASWAMI,
Your son has become a martyr. Brave boy! Do not grieve.
Such sacrifices are inevitable in our country. Let us emulate him.
He only saves his life who loses it. Console your wife.
Yes. I remember you very well.
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy: C. W. 10580. Courtesy: Gorur Ramaswami Iyengar
