299. LETTER TO AMINA G. QURESHI
July 31, 1932
CHI. AMINA,
I expect to get a long letter from you by and by. How is your
health? How is your study of Urdu? Write a few sentences if you
can. How do you find the children? How did everybody behave
towards you?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6661
