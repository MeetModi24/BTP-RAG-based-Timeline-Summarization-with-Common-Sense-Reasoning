60. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Thursday [February 25, 1926]
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
I have gone through your articles on election of a chamar
to the municipal body. I am afraid their publication is likely
to do more harm than good to the cause of the untouchables. I
have therefore dropped the idea of publishing them. In my opi-
nion the best remedy is to let the public fury subside gradually
through silence and peace on your part. Please let me know if you
have anything more to say in this regard.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI DAHYABHAI MANOHARDAS PATEL
DHOLKA
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2694. Courtesy: Dahyabhai Patel
