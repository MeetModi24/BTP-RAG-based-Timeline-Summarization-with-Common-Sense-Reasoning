202. LETTER TO MADHAVJI V. THAKKAR
April 9, 1929
BHAISHRI MADHAVJI,
I was about to send you a wire asking you to give up your
fast when I got your telegram. It is well that you have given it
up. You must have received my wire in which I have explained
the matter in detail. I hope you are taking complete rest. I have
asked you to send me a wire every day. If you patiently carry
out my instructions, you will regain your strength and weight.
I wrote to you yesterday giving detailed instructions. The address
for the present should be Bezwada.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6769
