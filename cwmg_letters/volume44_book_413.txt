413. LETTER TO ABBAS
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 10, 1930
CHI. ABBAS,
I got your letter. I have hit upon a simple method of ascer-
taining the count of yarn, and it is this: the number of rounds
in the quantity of yarn equal to the weight of one anna should
be taken as its count. I understand what you say about the rest.
I hope you keep good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6304
