74. LETTER TO MRS. S. WALTON
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 2, 1906
DEAR MRS. SPENCER WALTON,
I have your letter of the 30th ultimo. I am now preparing for the
interview with Lord Elgin, which comes off on Thursday next. I shall
therefore endeavour to call on you either on Friday next, or early some
time week after. I shall however drop you a note if I can at all spare
the time.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. SPENCER WALTON
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4456
