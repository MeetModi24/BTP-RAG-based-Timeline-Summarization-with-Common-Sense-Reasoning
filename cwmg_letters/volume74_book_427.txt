427. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
August 26, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
The Rigveda should be here tomorrow.
You don't say anything about your illness. How did the
medicine act? Here is isabgol. If you have not taken the cas-
cara take half a spoonful of the seed as it is. Drink water after
to swallow it.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6488. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9883
