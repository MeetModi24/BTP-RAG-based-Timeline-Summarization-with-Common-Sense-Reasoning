770. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
July 12, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I had been expecting your letter. Prabhavati has come
today. Both your rosaries are with me. I hope you have
settled down properly.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
C/O ASSTT. DOCTOR
K. E. M. HOSPITAL
BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
