255. LETTER TO RATUBHAI DESAI
June 18, 1935
BHAI RATUBHAI,
I have already drafted my award. Bhai Vithaldas will give
you a copy of it. Go and collect it yourself in order to save time.
The purport of the message is that if you, Ganatra and others
do not send in your apologies, you are doomed. I have given the
message in the hope that all of you will live as members of a
family and will work with greater devotion than before and serve
the cause of khadi through the bhandar.
Write to me as soon as possible what you have to say regar-
ding the management of the bhandar. I shall take up the matter
immediately. The bhandar does not belong to anybody. It is for
us only a means of service. I have as much right as you to give
suggestions regarding its management.
Blessings from
