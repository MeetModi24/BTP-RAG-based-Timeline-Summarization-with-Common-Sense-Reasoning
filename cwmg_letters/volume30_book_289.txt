289. LETTER TO LALLU MORAR
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Tuesday, April 6, 1926
BHAI LALLU MORAR,
I have your letter. I am sorry to learn about your disunity.
If only one of you will humble himself, follow the path of truth
and engage in the service of others, the rest will readily gather
round him. At the moment we are in no position to send anyone.
However, if you wish to consult me please do so. Do you subscribe
to Navajivan? If not, it is desirable that you become a subscriber.
The subscription is sh. 10 a year.
