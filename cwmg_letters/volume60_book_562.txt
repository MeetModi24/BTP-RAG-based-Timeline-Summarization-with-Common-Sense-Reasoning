562. LETTER TO MADAN MOHAN MALAVIYA
April 9, 1935
BHAI SAHEB,
Your telegram relieved me of my anxiety. You have resol-
ved to live to a hundred. In order to do that you will have to
make an effort to protect the body.
I am setting out to draft the speech for the Hindi Sahitya
Sammelan. Before I do that I must ask your blessings. I shall be
greatly encouraged if I can receive them before I leave here on
the 19th.
The presidentship was to be yours but your refusal has
thrown the burden on me which only your blessings can lighten.
Your younger brother,
MOHANDAS
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
