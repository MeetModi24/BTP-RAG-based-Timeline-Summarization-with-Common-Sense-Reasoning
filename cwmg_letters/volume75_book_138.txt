138. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
November 10, 1941
CHI. LILI,
You must have reached there safe. Ba misses you very much.
Who in your absence will go on talking silly things? Get engrossed
in your study. Make up your mind to pass. I shall be satisfied
if you drop me a postcard every week.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. LILAVATIBEHN UDESHI
KANJI KHETSI CHHATRALAYA
MINT ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10112. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
