259. LETTER TO F. C. GRIFFITH
BOMBAY,
May 6, 1919
DEAR MR. GRIFFITH,
I hope you have been glancing at the leaflets being published
from day to day. I want particularly however to draw your
attention to yesterday's leaflet declaring hartal for next Sunday.
I am hoping that all will go well.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat : S.N. 6592
