248. LETTER TO NIRMALA H. DESAI
March 20, 1932
CHI. NIRMALA,
Even if we Spin to meet our own requirements, the country
gains since it means so much addition to its wealth. And, more-
over, we shall have set an example of spending one's time use-
fully and of self-reliance.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9467. Also C.W. 8972. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
