401. LETTER TO DHARMADEV SHASTRI
NEW DELHI,
September 15, 1946
BHAI DHARMADEV SHASTRI,
We do not lend glitter to gold; similarly the name gurukul
also does not need any embellishment. Forget about the name
and such other things. All noble deeds carry their own bless-
ings. Remember this and stop begging for blessings from others.
ASHOK ASHRAM
P. O. KALSI (DIST. DEHRA DUN)
