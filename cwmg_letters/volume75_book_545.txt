545. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
DELHI,
March 30, 1942
DEAR SISTER,
I got your letter. The statement is good. Very good work
has been done in Multan. It is surely a very good thing to meet
our Muslim brethren in this manner.
Chandwani had come to Wardha and had a long talk. Raj-
kumari must have written all about it, or even Chandwani might
have. I do not have the time to write a long letter.
I hope I shall be able to leave for Sevagram tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8002. Also C.W. 3100. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
