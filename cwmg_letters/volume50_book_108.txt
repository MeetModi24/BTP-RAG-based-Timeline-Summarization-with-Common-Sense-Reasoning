108. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
June 28, 1932
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have received the khaddar, but no letter has been received.
Please tell Behenji (sister) that I shall lovingly be using the khad-
dar. Since I wrote I have been studying books on economy. I
have read your two pamphlets. I can understand (the subject)
better than before. But as yet the picture of the whole subject
(lit. all things) does not come before me. But I am pursuing it
and so I hope to be sufficiently acquainted with the subject.
So far I have been reading (Prof.) Shah's book on Banking and
Exchange. In this connection any books which may be considered
worth reading may be sent. It would be very well if the Reports
of the Herschel Committee, Fowler Committee, Babington Smith,
Chamberlain and Hilton Young Committees are printed in book
form. If they are not so printed they may be sent as they are
available. Dissenting minutes should also be with them. I also
wish to have these Reports over and above the literature you have
in mind. If you send also the books our experts have written, there
will be sufficient acquaintance with the subject.
Blessings from
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. I, p. 319
