21. LETTER TO CHIEF SECRETARY, C. P. GOVERNMENT
January 7, 1945
THE CHIEF SECRETARY TO THE C. P. GOVERNMENT
NAGPUR
Re : Nalwadi and Paunar Ashrams
SIR,
As my work-fast is now over I write this to remind you of
my letter of 20-11-1944, and ask for a reply thereto. The pro-
perty in question is not only going to increasing waste but
so much useful and creative labour is being lost for what may
be termed want of human consideration.
I am,
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
