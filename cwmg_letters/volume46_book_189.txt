189. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
May 18, 1931
CHI. VASUMATI,
Though there is no letter from you, I am writing this letter
as today is my silence day. Have you started working among
the people of Nagarwada and other places? I suppose you are
now required to report yourself only once to the police station.
I hope to reach Borsad about the 25th instant.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9323. Also C.W. 539. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
