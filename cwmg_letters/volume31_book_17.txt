17. LETTER TO PARASRAM MEHROTRA
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
June 18, 1926
CHI. PARASRAM,
I would now like to know when you can come. Wire me
the final date. I want to utilize you for Hindi Navajivan and
work should be done with expedition.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PARASRAMJI
THE "MALAVA MAYUR" OFFICE
AJMER
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 6100. Courtesy: Paras ram Mehrotra
