363. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
August 11, 1941
BHAI MUNSHI,
I got your letter. It is quite clear. It will now be easy to
write to Chandravadan. I didn't know anything before I got his
letter. I read your speech in Social Welfare after I had his letter.
Come whenever you wish to.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7665. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
