270. LETTER TO A. H. GOOL
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 28, 1906
DEAR MR. GOOL,
I hope you have received the invitation card. Be sure and come
at 10.30 tomorrow and find your way to the banqueting hall.
I am,
Yours truly,
A. H. GOOL, ESQ.
27, PECKHAM ROAD, S.E.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4657
