389. LETTER TO RUPAVANTI
SEVAGRAM,
March 10, 1945
CHI. RUPAVANTI,
Why should you grieve over the death of your husband?
Why should you cry? If you do so, the departed soul will be
unhappy. Your duty is to imbibe your husband's virtues and
do as much good work as you can, as he did, so that he lives
on in you.
Blessings from
BAPU
C/O JAYANT MULJI CHAVDA
MUTHEGANJ
ALLAHABAD, U. P.
