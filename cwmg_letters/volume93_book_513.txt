513. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
[May 1940]
CHI. NARANDAS,
I had got your letter. Why need you be unhappy that you
lost five hundred rupees? There would have been cause for sorrow
if the loss had been due to any mistake of ours. In the present
case I see no mistake on your part. Your faith will bear fruit.
You will get the help you need. It is better that whatever
help you may get should be through Sardar.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 8574
