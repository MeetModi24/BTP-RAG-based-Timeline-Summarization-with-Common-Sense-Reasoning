172. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
November 11, 1940
BHAI MUNSHI,
I am aware of the love you both 2 have for me. If there is
a fast, it will only be at the prompting of the inner voice. If
you believe this, you will rejoice instead of being pained.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 7655. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
