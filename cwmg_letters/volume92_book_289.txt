289. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
Sunday [March 16, 1930]
CHI. CHANDRAKANTA,
I hope your work is proceeding well. I have pinned great
hopes on you. May God grant you the strength to fulfil these
hopes. Sumangal had a slight touch of fever. There is no cause
for anxiety. He is being nursed well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Keep writing to your parents.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
