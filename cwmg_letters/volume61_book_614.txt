614. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI A. MEHTA
September 20, 1935
BHAI BHAGWANJI,
I shall send on to Jamnalalji your letter to him. Why do
you deliberately select rich partners for your daughters? I got
your letter regarding Devchandbhai. If I send that letter to him,
he wouldn't meet you even if he otherwise had intended to do so.
I, therefore, wrote to him a separate letter. I will let you know
the result.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5827. Also C.W. 3050. Courtesy:
Bhagwanji A. Mehta
