454. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 12, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
You are not unhappy about what you said about massage
at the time of bathing? Would you feel happy? Did you like
the telegram that was sent to Delhi? Are you calm?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
