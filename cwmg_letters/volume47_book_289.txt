289. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
August 6, 1931
CHI. PREMA,
Is it right that you should not write to me at all? I had
expected a long letter from you. Write one now. I was forced
to talk about you today for nearly one hour with Dhurandhar and
Kisan. What a shame!
I was glad to read the news that you embraced Maitri.
But I shall not be satisfied till I get a full description.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10260; also C.W. 6709. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
