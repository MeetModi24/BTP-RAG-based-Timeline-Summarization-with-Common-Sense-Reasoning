644. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
NEW DELHI,
October 19, 1946
DEAR C. R.,
Here is something on salt for you, Matthai and Greenfield
to see. There seems to be much in what the writer says. It
has no bearing on the tax which I hope is soon going.
Love.
BAPU
RAJAJI
NEW DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
