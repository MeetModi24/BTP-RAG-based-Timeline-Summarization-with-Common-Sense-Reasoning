194. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
January 29, 1940
CHI. PREMA,
Prabha would have come there immediately if Ba had not
specially wanted to see her. If you take care of her health, she will
give you the work you want. Not that you don't know this.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10404. Also C.W. 6843. Cour-
tesy: Premabehn Kantak
