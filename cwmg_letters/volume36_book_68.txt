68. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN CHOUDHRI
February 27, 1928
BHAI RAMNARAYAN,
Your letter to hand. I had no idea of what was being
written about me in Shraddhanand. I glance at one or two news-
papers for a few minutes only. I do not want that anyone should
defend me. Moreover, it hurts me that someone should be at-
tacked on my account. You can use this letter as you like. I am
writing to Pratap.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
[From Hindi]
Bapu: Maine Kya Dekha Kya Samjha?, p. 161
