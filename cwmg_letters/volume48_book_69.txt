69. LETTER TO J. THEODORE HARRIS
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
September 29, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
Mr. Alexander has handed your letter to me. I am looking
forward to meeting Dr. Montessori on Wednesday next. I shall
wait for her if she is not here at 10 o'clock punctually.
Yours sincerely,
J. THEODORE HARRIS, ESQ.
4 GRAHAM ROAD, E. 8
From a photostat: S.N. 17873
