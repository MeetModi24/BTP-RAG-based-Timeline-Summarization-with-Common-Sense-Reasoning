457. LETTER TO SULOCHANA
August 27, 1932
CHI. SULOCHANA,
I got your letter. You seem to have made good progress.
Learn carding as soon as you can. You make mistakes in your
letters. You should form the habit of going through a letter
after writing it. Get your letters revised by somebody. With a
little effort, you will also be able to improve your handwriting.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1737
