77. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
Wednesday [After April 30, 1927]
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I have your letter. I have got your point for not coming to Nandi. My
objection to your going to Almora was . . .2 for want of a man. Now Jethalal
is coming and if he keeps good health, you can go without any compunction.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original S.N. 32973
