152. LETTER TO VIDYA A. HINGORANI
March 19, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
Now that you have Mahadev why should you write to me?
Be frank and say that because of the appearance of Mahadev in
the house, you have got behindhand in writing and therefore
need not apologize.
It is necessary to keep children free of constipation. You can
give him castor oil even daily. Just ten drops will be more than
enough. Give my blessings to Anand whenever you write to him.
I have not met Dr. Mehta again.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
