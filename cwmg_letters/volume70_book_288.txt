288. LETTER TO N. S. HARDIKAR
SEGAON, WARDHA (C. P.),
October 14, 1939
DEAR DR. HARDIKER,
I have seen the Mysore friends. I am in correspondence with
the Dewan. If anything comes out of it, I shall let you know.

(2.) Now that the total number of Muslim population has increased to almost
one-third in the whole of India, a representation of one-third in all Central
Legislatures and allotment of similar proportion of seats under the Government
of India.
These are some of the suggestions which I am making, and I am
requesting you to kindly take up this question now in all seriousness for imme-
diate solution.
I have been writing to you for nearly two years and I hope
you will not think it impatient on my part if I earnestly beseech you to kindly
expedite matters specially in the present situation." Vide also the following
item.
Please tell Diwakar not to go to Mysore at the present
moment. I hope you are better.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
