177. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
SEVAGRAM,
November 11, 1940
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Your letter is good. I have made proper arrangements about
land, etc. I shall do more if I remain free. Yourself, Parnerkar
Chimanlal, Sukhabhai etc., are, of course, remaining out.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1938
