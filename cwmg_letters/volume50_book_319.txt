319. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
August 4, 1932
Dr. Mehta is no more. I have lost a lifelong faithful friend.
But for me he lives more intensely by his death than before, for
I treasure his many virtues now more than ever. That treasure
becomes a sacred trust. Here is a letter for Maganlal. I expect
you to do all you can to make him a worthy son of his father. I
have advised him not to worry but continue his studies. Broken
down though Dr. Mehta had become of late, I expect he had
preserved his original circumspection to make suitable financial
arrangements for Maganlal's studies. Maganlal will know. I feel
that I am not by his people's side at the present moment. But not
my will, let His be done, now and for ever.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 344
