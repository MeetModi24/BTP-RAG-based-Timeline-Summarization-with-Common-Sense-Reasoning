432. LETTER TO SHANKARAN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 19, 1929
MY DEAR SHANKARAN,
I have your letter. I am forwarding it to Mathuradas. I
want you to talk to him and you may go as soon as you are
disengaged. On no account will you leave abruptly. Mathuradas
will give you what you need. You ought not to have kept this
thing from me for such a long time. I expect you to return as
early as you can. And don't spend more than is absolutely
necessary.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SHANKARAN
EVERGREEN, MATHERAN
From a microfilm: S.N. 14985
