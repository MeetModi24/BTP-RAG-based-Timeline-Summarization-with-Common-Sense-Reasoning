422. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
NEW DELHI,
September 17, 1946
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letter. I also got the notebook you have sent
for the daily thoughts. This I am writing in the morning at 3
o'clock. Your toe must be all right now. However many
troubles you may have, you must be happy.
What can I write about Mahadev? God will give him
back his strength. I am writing to him. Also to Gangi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
