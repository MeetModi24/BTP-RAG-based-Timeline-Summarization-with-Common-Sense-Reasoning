201. LETTER TO CHANDRAMANI VIDYALANKAR
November 26, 1946
BHAI CHANDRAMANI VIDYALANKAR,
I have your letter. Thanks. I am myself in the dark. Under
the circumstances I do not regard it as proper to send for any-
one. You should do your duty remaining at your own place.
Blessings from
BAPU
PANDIT CHANDRAMANI VIDYALANKAR
BHASKAR PRESS
DEHRA DUN
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
