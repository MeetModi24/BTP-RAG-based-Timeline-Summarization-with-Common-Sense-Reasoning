60. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
January 27, 1932
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter. Mirabehn and others came and saw me.
As requested by Mahavir in his letter, send the family to Darjeeling
and give them money for the expenses there. Tell Valjibhai
that he can send the translations to me. I will see if they per-
mit me to return them revised. My compliments to Surendra.
He does not seem to have been hurt much.
The rest next week. You must have received the letter
which I posted today. I sent with it the talk on Chapter XIV
which I had omitted.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. NARANDAS GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8205. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
