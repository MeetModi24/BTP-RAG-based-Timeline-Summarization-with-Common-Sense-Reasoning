300. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
July 31, 1932
CHI. PANDITJI,
Since Tai has come to stay with you, it is our duty to see that
she improves her health while she is there. Discover the art
of building up good health. Ask Tai to write to me. She may write
in Marathi.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 241. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn
N. Khare
