560. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
October 8, 1946
CHI. SITA,
I have your letter. I hope you received the message I
sent in Behn's letter. When does New Year commence for us?
Is not time changing ceaselessly? If we remain steadfast in all
these changes, why need we care for a New Year?
But you may have the good wishes.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
And aspire to live for 125 years, doing service till the end.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4992
