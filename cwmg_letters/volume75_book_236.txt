236. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
December 14, 1941
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I keep thinking about your health. You must bring it round.
Sankaran wrote and told me that he was planning something.
Write to me and give me the news. Babu is expected in a day
or two.
I am all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10605
