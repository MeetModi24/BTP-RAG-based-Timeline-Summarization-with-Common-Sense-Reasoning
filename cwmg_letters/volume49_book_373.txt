373. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
April 15, 1932
DEAR MR. BHANDARI,
I would like the accompanying letter to be delivered to
at once, if you approve of the contents. They are nothing
but re-exhortation to break his fast and take ordinary food.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
If. . .3 accepts the advice tendered in my letter to him and
breaks the fast, I hope you will issue him milk for one or two
days, for it is my experience as a fasting expert that the break-
ing of fasts on solid food often results in great harm to the body.
M. K. GANDHI
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 99
