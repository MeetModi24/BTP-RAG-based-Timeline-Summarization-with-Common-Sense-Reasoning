103. LETTER TO G. WALPOLE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 5, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter of the 3rd inst. for which I thank you.
I shall not need your services with regard to the Deputation as
I have permanently engaged the services of an expert shorthand writer.
Yours faithfully,
GEORGE WALPOLE, ESQ.
1, NEW COURT
LINCOLN'S INN
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4487
