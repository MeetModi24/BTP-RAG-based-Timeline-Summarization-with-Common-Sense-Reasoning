520. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
September 5, 1926
DEAR MOTI BABU,
I have your letter and report.
It is surprising how troubles after troubles overcome you.
I am asking the manager to book the Sangha for a com-
plimentary copy of Young India.
I do hope that your yarn will improve in quality.
You
know that we have now introduced the practice of testing the
strength and the count of yarn.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 11029
