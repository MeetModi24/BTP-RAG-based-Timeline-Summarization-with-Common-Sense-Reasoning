429. LETTER TO H. S. SUHRAWARDY
NEW DELHI,
Early morning, January 21, 1948
MY DEAR SHAHEED,
I was much concerned about your financial obligations. This
letter is the measure of my concern. I have seen many Muslim
friends go under because of their extravagant habits. Do not say
you are no better than Hindus, etc. The remedies you suggest are
worse than the disease. I would like you to do what I have induced
my Muslim clients to do to their credit. Thereby they gained in
self-esteem and in the esteem of their friends. Honourable insol-
vency is the straight line. Of this more when you return from
Sind and if you would then discuss the thing with me. In politics
you have much to correct, if I am right. The Sardar is not bad.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase, Vol. II, p. 485
