240. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SRIRAMPUR,
December 4, 1946
CHI. VALLABHBHAI,
Herewith a statement of my views about the Constituent
Assembly. Please go through it and do what you think fit.
Jawaharlal's absence will be a handicap. I hold very strong
views in the matter. There is certainly no weakness on our part
in giving up the plan. Doing what the situation demands is no
weakness. But maybe I am quite wrong.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 328
