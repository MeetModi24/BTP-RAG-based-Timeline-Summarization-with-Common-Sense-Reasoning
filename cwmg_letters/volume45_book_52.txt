52. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
December 31, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I get your letters. But I put off writing to you thinking
that you will soon be in the Ashram. The more you stay on
there, the more I feel worried. Why don't you go away im-
mediately and leave matters to take care of themselves?
If
Jayaprakash cannot go immediately, cannot he ask Harasubabu?
I see that the longer you delay in going to the Ashram, the
weaker you are becoming. What further can I now write even
to Jayaprakash?
What is your diet in your present condition of health? Who
watches over you when you are in a swoon? What did Father
tell you when you met him? I feel afraid even to ask you
questions, for it may be a burden on your mind to think and reply
to them. I dismiss my worries in the faith that God has been
protecting you and will continue to do so. I am quite well.
Blessings from
BAPU
