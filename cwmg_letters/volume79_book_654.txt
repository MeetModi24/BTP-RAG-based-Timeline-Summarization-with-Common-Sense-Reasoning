654. LETTER TO UMA AGRAWAL
BOMBAY,
April 15, 1945
CHI. OM1,
If you keep your clothes in the room on getting up, does it
mean the room is yours? The ideal thing would be that we con-
sider it more than enough if we have a corner to keep our things
in. Do you throw your clothes about anyhow or do you fold
them and keep them neatly arranged? Do you wash your mouth
and face before going to the morning prayer? You have de-
scribed the schedule from lunch to 3.15 p.m. as cleaning and
ginning of cotton, newspaper-reading and writing. You ought to
mention specifically the time each activity takes. Working eight
hours is compulsory. In an institution given to non-violence,
taking work from others is coercion. Why should we exact work?
Everything we do is work. We should not keep our diaries secret.
It is our ideal not to keep anything secret. It is better to get
the diary signed by the manager.
Any work given to us is our study, or should be. Prof.
Shriman Narayan's school should not have English. It will be dis-
continued. I do not know the price. You must find out. Do not
send the diary to me. Show it to me when I come.
Blessings from
BAPU
