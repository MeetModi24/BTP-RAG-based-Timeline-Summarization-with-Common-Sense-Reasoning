105. LETTER TO DILIP MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 10, 1941
CHI. DILIP,
I like what you have written. I also like your handwriting. It can
be further improved. It is good that you are keeping up courage. Yes,
I keep getting Hakubhai's letters. They are very helpful.
Blessings from
BAPU
