71. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
January 4, 1931
CHI. MAHAVIR,
I got your letter. It seems you are getting daily new expe-
riences. Wherever you go, live with the utmost self-restraint. If
the experiment of starching yarn succeeds, that will undoubtedly
help us very much.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6224
