56. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
DELHI,
June 6, 1947
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letter. I had it read out at 4.15 a.m. after the
prayers and now I am dictating this. I cannot answer all the
questions. I can offer no solace to you. One who has lost faith
in oneself has only Ramanama to fall back upon. So great is
the value of repeating God's name that one may die with it
on one's lips and yet stand to gain. So my advice to you and
Gangi is that you should do whatever you can reciting His name
all the time.
Everything about me is uncertain.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANAND HINGORANI
GURPURI BUNGALOWS
DECCAN GYMKHANA
POONA
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
