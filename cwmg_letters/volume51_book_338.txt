338. LETTER TO V. RAMJEE RAO
October 6, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I do hope that Andhradesh
will not lag behind the rest of the country in the matter of re-
moval of untouchability.
Yours sincerely,
M. K . GANDHI
SHRI V. RAMJEE RAO
"DEENABANDHU" OFFICE
MASULIPATAM
From a photostat: G.N. 4578
