93. LETTER TO BHIMARAO
September 17, 1932
I have your touching wire. The fast is to be undertaken
in God's name, at His will. It would be wrong and cowardly
now to desist. Let us hope that He will give me strength to go
through it. After all no life can live without His permitting.
If He has any further use for it He knows best how to keep it.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 34
