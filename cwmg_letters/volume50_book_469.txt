469. LETTER TO PYARELAL NAYAR
August 28, 1932
Vallabhbhai is running with the speed of an Arab horse. He
is never found without a book about Sanskrit in his hands. I
had not hoped for this. Nobody can equal him in the art of
making envelopes. He makes them without using measurements
and is guided only by his eyes in cutting the paper, and still he
does not seem to take much time. His orderliness is simply
wonderful. If there is anything which he has to do, he does not
leave it to his memory. He does it immediately. From the time
that he started spinning, he always spins at the fixed time, and
so the quantity of yarn spun and the speed of spinning are In-
creasing daily. He rarely forgets anything which he takes up.
With such orderliness, there can be no confusion any time.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 386
