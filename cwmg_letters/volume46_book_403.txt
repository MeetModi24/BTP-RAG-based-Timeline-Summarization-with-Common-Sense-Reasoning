403. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 5, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
This liquor business is causing a great deal of trouble. The
following extracts from a letter from Calicut will show you what
is to be said on the Congress side. I have given you already facts
about picketing in Ahmedabad. It is high time that this matter
is settled finally.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
H. W. EMERSON, ESQ.
SIMLA
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
