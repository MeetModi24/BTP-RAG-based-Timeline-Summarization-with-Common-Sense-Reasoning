343. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
November 20, 1938
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Send Rs. 251/- to Thakkar Bapa for the Harijan Sevak
Sangh. His address: Harijan Nivas, Kingsway, Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
74 WALKESHWAR ROAD
BOMBAY
