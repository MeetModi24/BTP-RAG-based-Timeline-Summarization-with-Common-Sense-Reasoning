425. LETTER TO M. S. ANEY
NEW DELHI,
January 19, 1948
BHAI BAPUJI ANEY1,
I have your beautiful letter. Keep up the practice of writing
in Hindi.
God is always present in all good work. All success to you!
There is a lot of work to do in Bihar.
My fast is now a thing of the past. Who knows what God
wills us to do!
Do keep writing to me whenever you feel like it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
