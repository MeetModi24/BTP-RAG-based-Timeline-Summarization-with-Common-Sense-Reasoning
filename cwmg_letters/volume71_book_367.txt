367. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEVAGRAM,
March 31, 1940
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your wire and letter, also the one from Jayaprakash.
I had expected a longer sentence. In a way I welcome this
imprisonment. It will surely give him rest. See that he takes
proper care about his food. As for you, you should engross yourself in
the work there. Know the names and addresses of all your women
friends and arrange for their spinning-wheels, etc. See that they
get lessons in reading and writing in their homes. Send Harijan
Sevak and other literature to those who can read. Train them in
sanitation. Create in them a desire to discard the purdah. But
don't be in a hurry about this.
I am all right.
Pass on the letter to Jayaprakash.
Blessings from
BAPU
