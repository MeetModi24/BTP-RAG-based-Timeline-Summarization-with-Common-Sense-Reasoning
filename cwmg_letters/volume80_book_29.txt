29. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 26, 1945
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
There is no reason to feel unhappy over having brought
Om Prakash with you. What can one do if such persons get in?
You did not bring him deliberately. I have written to him.
Let us hope he will understand.
Hoshiari is getting along well.
Dr. Kelkar has written about teaching you English. I have
dissuaded him. We can acquire all knowledge through our own
language. But if you are particular, I shall not stop you, nor
shall I encourage you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1959
