328. LETTER TO K. P. CHAKRAVARTY
SEVAGRAM,
November 2, 1944
DEAR CHAKRAVARTY,
Your touching letter. Treasure your wife's memory and devote
yourself to the service of afflicted humanity round you. No one
really dies. Bodies are destined to perish; some today, some
tomorrow.
BAPU
SHRI K. P. CHAKRAVARTY
TOLLYGUNGE
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
