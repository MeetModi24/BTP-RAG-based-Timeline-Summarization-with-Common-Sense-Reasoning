232. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
September 22, 1930
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
Let me point out a flaw in your calculation. If you "return
the compliment", you should find out the love notes I send every
week. So if love may be measured arithmetically your notes must
be as many times long as all my notes put together. But thank
God! Love ignores and falsifies both arithmetic and geometry.
Yes, Kamalabehn is doing very well indeed.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10091
