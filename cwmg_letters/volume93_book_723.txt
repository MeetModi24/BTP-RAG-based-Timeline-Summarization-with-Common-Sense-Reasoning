723. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
April 23, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you today. You will have received
mine of yesterday.
Durgabehn is much better today.
I have two suggestions about Ba. She should have hip-baths
and apply mud-packs to the lower abdomen at night. Both
these things should have an effect on the rectum.
Today almost everyone is on fast.
How is Satya's life going on? How is Mother's health?
Write a letter to Pyarelal and send it here. I shall try to have
it delivered to him.
Urmila has gone there. I have told her that she should meet you.
My health is fine. What would you say if Anand were
unable to pass stool without enema?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
