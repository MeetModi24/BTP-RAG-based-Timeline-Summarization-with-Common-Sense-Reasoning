265. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 15, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
I was unable to send you anything yesterday. M. left this
morning and J. L. will leave for Bombay this evening.
He is
passing the day here and dining with Mahadeo. In one sentence, M.
is not satisfied with my demand or the manner of enforcement;
he needs time for thought. J. L. not quite so dissatisfied as M.
but not quite convinced either. The W. C. will meet early July.
The weather is better but still oppressive. No sign of a downpour.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
I have just got today's post. Your knee has got to be right soon.
From the original: C.W. 3688. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6497
