236. LETTER TO L. N. GOPALASWAMI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 15, 1945
MY DEAR GOPALASWAMI,
I have your generous letter . You will do as Bapa bids you.
I must not abuse your good nature. Push forward good women
for all you are worth.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI L. N. G OPALASWAMI
KASTURBA GANDHI NATIONAL MEMORIAL FUND
S. 13 BUILDINGS
ROYAPETTAH
MADRAS
From a photostat: C.W. 10549. Courtesy: Government of Tamil Nadu
