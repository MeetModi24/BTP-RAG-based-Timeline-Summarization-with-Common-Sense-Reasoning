168. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 25, 1940
MY DEAR C.R.,
The above is a translation of the letter received from J2. Shan-
kerlal feels keenly in the matter. Nevertheless so far as I am con-
cerned yours should be the final word. The Viceroy has fix[ed]
5th, 10 a.m., for the meeting. There is talk of having the Sangh
meeting in Delhi earlier than 13th for the sake of. . .2 elections. I
shall wire from Delhi if I want you.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10885. Courtesy: C. R. Narasimhan
