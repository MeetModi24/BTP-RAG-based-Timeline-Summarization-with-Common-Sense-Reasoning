456. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
PANCHGANI,
June 7, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have your letter. You have my approval for appointing
Manohar Dhotre to look after the accounts, etc., for 1½ months.
If possible copy letters in ink so that the original does not
have to be written in pencil.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHYAMLALJI
KASTURBA TRUST OFFICE
SCINDIA HOUSE
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
