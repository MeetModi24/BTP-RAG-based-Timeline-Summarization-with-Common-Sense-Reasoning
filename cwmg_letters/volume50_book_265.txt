265. LETTER TO KUSUM GANDHI
July 24, 1932
DEAR KUSUM (JUNIOR),
You must be thoughtlessly speaking out too much. My
post did not reach there and so you stopped writing, though
you have written to me so often that you like to write to me
every week. Do not speak anything hastily or thoughtlessly. Do
not take a vow before thinking fully (about it). If (once) it is
taken, remember it and observe it. Has your fever gone? What
amount of milk can you take? If you want to be a nurse re-
cover (from illness) soon and pass (your) life in meditation. By
searching for the cause of everything any activity which we
undertake becomes interesting and it does not appear to be bur-
densome.
BAPU
Government of Bombay, Home Department, I.G.P. File No. 20-IX
