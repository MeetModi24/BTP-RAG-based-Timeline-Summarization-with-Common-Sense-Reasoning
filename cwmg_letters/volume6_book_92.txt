92. LETTER TO F. H. BROWN
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 3, 1906
DEAR MR. BROWN,
I venture to enclose herewith two copies of the Representation
forwarded to Lord Elgin for your perusal. The Representation will
be used as the basis for discussion at the interview that comes off on
the 8th instant.
Yours truly,
2 enclosures
F. H. BROWN, ESQ.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4473
