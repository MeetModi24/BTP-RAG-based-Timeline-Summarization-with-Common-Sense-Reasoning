325. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
[May-June, 1930]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I get your letters regularly now. I have already written to
you about my health. Mirabehn wrote saying that she would
give you some work where you are. If she has not done so, and
if Jayaprakash is willing and Kamalabehn permits you may
certainly go and join Ba. There is much to learn there these
days. My weight is normal. You can write to me about anything
you wish to do.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
What is Mrityunjai doing? How is Grandmother3? How does
Vidyavati keep? Write to Father and tell him that I often
think of him.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3307
