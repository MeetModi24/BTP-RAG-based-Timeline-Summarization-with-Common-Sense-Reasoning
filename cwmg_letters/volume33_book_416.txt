416. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
NANDI HILLS,
June 1, 1927
DEAR BROTHER,
In a few days' time you will be on the waters and at the
end of the month you will be in South Africa. My thoughts
and my prayers will be with you throughout your stay in that sub-
continent. This appointment is to me a unique event. I must not
let myself say one word more. May God keep you and bless you.
Do let me have a line whenever you can. I hope you got
the important letter I addressed you at Madras just after we last met.
Yours sincerely,
THE RT. HON'BLE V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
CARE OF SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
SANDHURST ROAD, GIRGAUM
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 12356
