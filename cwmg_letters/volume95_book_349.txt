349. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Silence day
CHI. VASUMATI,
How are things? Are you two sisters together or have you made
some other arrangement? You can write to me. Look after your
health. Do not be perplexed. Do not worry. Tell Kamalabehn that
she should also write.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: S. N. 9361.
