376. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
MUSSOORIE,
June 6, 1946
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have your letter. What you say is quite correct. We
should not underrate the gravity of the situation. He who ab-
stains from doing a thing merely because it does not seem im-
portant enough eventually fails to accomplish anything at all.
You have to fulfil the promise you have given. I am here-
with making a beginning to do what needs to be done. Enclosed
you will find a letter to Sambhaji . Read it and if you find it
all right send it on to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen p. 378
