446. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
November 22, 1934
MY DEAR AGATHA,
It was quite like you to have put your whole soul into
sending little Mehrtaj to Brindisi. It was a fine contribution
to Indo-British understanding. The Khan Brothers are a rare
type of humanity.
The more I see them, the more I love
them. And inasmuch [as] you have done this little and yet
great thing for them, you have done it for all India. That simple
bit of service is its own reward. Thanks or any outward expres-
sion is a poor substitute. Mira is working well and so is her
ward.
Well, the preliminary acknowledgement from Delhi has
come. If there is any development I shall use the cable.
About the J[oint] Committee's report, you must have seen
my opinion. One who has retired from Congress can have no
opinion for public consumption. You do not need my private
opinion.
I am coining closer to Amrit Kaur.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1481
