85. LETTER TO CHANDAN PAREKH
April 12, 1937
CHI. CHANDAN,
Chi. Shankar has sent me your letter to him. I see that
the . . . affair still troubles you. The one per cent that I
have reserved is not to be dismissed as of no consequence. Open
your heart and write frankly about whatever you are harbouring
in it. If you mean to, you can do much. I wish that either
you should be proved completely truthful, or that you should
purify your heart thoroughly. You are perfectly right in saying
that one who admits one's faults rises. I wish you well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 942. Courtesy: Satish D. Kalelkar
