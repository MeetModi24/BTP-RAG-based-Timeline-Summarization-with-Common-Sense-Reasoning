532. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
[March 22, 1941]
CHI. AMRIT,
You will know this letter. I forget all about it.
Herewith Kamala's very interesting letter. Ten rupees are
being sent to Chandel.
I understand what you say about the Law Committee.
Don't worry. You will go to Simla if you are at all pressed.
Even a week there would do you good.
I have your wire.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4002. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7311
