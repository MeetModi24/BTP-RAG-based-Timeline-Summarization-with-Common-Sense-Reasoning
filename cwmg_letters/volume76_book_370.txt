370. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
July 24, 1942
CHI. MUNNALAL,
There is nothing in Kanchan's letter which would require a
telegram to be sent. Your letter is enough. Let us await her reply.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8472. Also C.W. 7173. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
