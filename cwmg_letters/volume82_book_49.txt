49. LETTER TO M. R. BONDRE
November 6, 1945
BHAI BONDRE,
Come whenever you wish before the 11th. I will spare a
few minutes for you at 5.30 p.m. when you come. I hope
Nalini is all right.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
M. R. BONDRE, B.A.,B.T.
11 -C HARI NIVAS, SHIVAJI PARK
LADY JAMSHEDJI ROAD
BOMBAY 28
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
