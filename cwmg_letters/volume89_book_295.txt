295. LETTER TO KAILAS NATH KATJU
NEW DELHI,
October 4, 1947
BHAI KAILAS NATH KATJU 1 ,
I heard attentively both your articles read out to me, but
as I am their subject how can I reproduce them in Harijan?
Nowadays even praise of any kind hurts me like poison. We
have become like beasts.
I asked Dr. Jivraj about your blood-pressure. He suggests
that you should take sarpagandha. The sarpagandha extract is
available at the Tropical Medicine School in Calcutta. Would
you like me to send it from my stock? Is it not a sevak's
dharma to keep his body healthy?
Blessings from
BAPU
