247. LETTER TO T. PRAKASAM
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 26, 1928
MY DEAR PRAKASAM,
I have your letter. If you can get Justice Venkatasubba Rao
to be the sole arbitrator in the matter between you and the All-
India Spinners' Association, it will be quite good. You may there-
fore please try to get his consent and let me know the result.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. T. PRAKASAM
"SWARAJYA", MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 13672
