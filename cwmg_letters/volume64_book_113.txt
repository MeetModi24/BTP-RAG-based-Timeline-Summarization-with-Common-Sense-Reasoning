113. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
November 30, 1936
CHI. KAKA,
I just read your article on eroticism. We shall have to
discuss it.
It is not necessary to make any change in your diet. At
any rate I approve of what you take at present. This should
suffice for the present.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7695
