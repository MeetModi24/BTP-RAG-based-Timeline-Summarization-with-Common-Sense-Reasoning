295. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI
May 29, 1931
CHI. GANGABEHN,
Chhaganlal informs me that you have come to Nadiad. I
also hear that you wish to see me. You may come whenever you
wish. I shall not be required to go to Borsad for some time.
Send the accompanying letter to Navin wherever he is.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3114
