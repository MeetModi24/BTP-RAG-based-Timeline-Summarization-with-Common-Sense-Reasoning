217. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
SODEPUR,
December 4, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your letter. The charge against you is that you are
hypocritical and given to falsehood. My own experience with
you is the opposite, but nevertheless you are thoughtless and
impatient and your thinking is not consistent. That is why your
work does not shine. I have already told you that if you cannot
accommodate more people in a hygienic way have only one.
If I send someone from here, my fear is you will not be able to
put up with him. So I feel you should do what I say. If you
insist I may think of sending someone but if you do you may
have to repent it later.
DR. KRISHNAVARMA
NATURE CURE HOSPITAL
MALAD, BOMBAY
