108. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
October 15, 1933
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I had your letter enclosing a copy of your letter to Krishna-
murti of Madura.
Here is a copy of my letter to Hales, in reply to his letter
which you must have seen in the Press. And here is also a copy
of my letter to Malaviyaji. It speaks for itself.
All my good wishes for the wedding. I shall be with you in
spirit on that day.
Love.
BAPU
Encl. 2
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1933. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
