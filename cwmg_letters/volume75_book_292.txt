292. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
January 11, 1942
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I have just learnt that both you and Maulana Saheb have
arrived. I had told M. S. that I would take silence at 2. When I
said so I had forgotten that I had given an appointment to Prof.
Copeland for 4.30 p.m. I could not cancel it. I took silence, there-
fore, at 5.25 p.m. I should be at your and his disposal after that
time. I am sorry but I was helpless.
Please read this to M. S.
Indu should be coming tomorrow.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1941. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
