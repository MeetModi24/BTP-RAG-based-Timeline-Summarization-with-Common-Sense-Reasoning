87. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
DELHI,
June 9, 1947
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
I am enclosing Sankaran's letter. How far is this true? He
also writes about Bhai Ranade. What is all this? However much
we may work, if our minds are not pure or if our conduct is
not sincere, we are good for nothing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9037. Also C. W. 9161. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
