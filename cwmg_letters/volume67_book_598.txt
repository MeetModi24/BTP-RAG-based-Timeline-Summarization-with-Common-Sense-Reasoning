598. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
PESHAWAR,
October 6, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
We reached quite well. The pressure behaved well in the
train also.
Better address letters Peshawar.
There is no cold here as yet. The nights are very good.
My silence continues giving me great peace and saving so
much time and energy.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3637. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6446
