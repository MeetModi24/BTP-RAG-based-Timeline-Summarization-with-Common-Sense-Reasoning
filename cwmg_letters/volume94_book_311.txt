311. LETTER TO GENERAL SECRETARY, KURNOOL DISTRICT
CONGRESS COMMITTEE
SEVAGRAM.
November 16, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of 24th ult.
You should be guided by Shri A. Kaleswara Rao who is at present here and
will be soon returning to Andhra Pradesh after his talks with Gandhiji.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
THE GENERAL SECRETARY
KURNOOL DISTRICT CONGRESS COMMITTEE
NANDYAL
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
