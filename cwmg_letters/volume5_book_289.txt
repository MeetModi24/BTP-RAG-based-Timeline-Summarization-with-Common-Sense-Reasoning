289. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
JOHANNESBURG,
April 10, 1906
MY DEAR CHHAGANLAL,
I return Hoosen Khan's letter. It will be dealt with in the English
columns. You may say in the Gujarati columns that the matter is being
dealt with in the English columns.
I half expect a letter from you to-morrow. I may leave by the
Friday morning train.
I am still discussing the situation with Mr. Kitchin. He may rejoin.
I hope Maganlal is now much better.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
1 Enclosure
C. K. GANDHI, ESQ.
c/o Indian Opinion
PHOENIX
From a photostat of the original : S.N. 4349
