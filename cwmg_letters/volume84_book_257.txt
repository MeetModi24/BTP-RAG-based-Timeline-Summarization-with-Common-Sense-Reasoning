257. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
May 25, 1946
CHI. KANAM1,
I have your postcard. I was pleased. You are learning a
great deal from Bhansalibhai and Vinoba. Keep up the progress.
I am very glad that you also take part in the Ashram work
now and observe the rules.
It will soon be decided now when I shall be leaving Delhi.
If I go to Panchgani come there.
When will you learn the Gujarati script? If you but make
up your mind, you will be able to learn it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 9521. Courtesy: Kanu Gandhi
