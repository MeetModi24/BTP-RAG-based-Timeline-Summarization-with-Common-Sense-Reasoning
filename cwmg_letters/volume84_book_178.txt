178. LETTER TO P. N. BAJPAYEE
SIMLA,
May 12, 1946
BHAI BAJPAYEE,
I have your letter. How can the Boy Scouts work for the
Shanti Sangh if they do not have complete faith in non-violence?
If we can meet somewhere when I am a little free, we can have
a further talk.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI P. N. BAJPAYEE
SCOUTS ASSOCIATION
WANLESS WADI
Via MIRAJ
