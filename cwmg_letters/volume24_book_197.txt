197. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Ashadh Sud 11 [July 12, 1924]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your postcard. Rain is tantalizing us by not arriving.
Signs of its coming are always visible, but it does not come. From
the water flowing in the river, it is evident that there has been rain
higher up. Radha is still weak; Perinbehn and Nargisbehn had
been here; they returned [to Bombay] with Jamnabehn. Only
the lady who was superintendent at the Seva Sadan is now here.
Try to stay on there as long as you can. I hope you have received
my letter giving instructions.
Blessings from
BAPU
SISTER VASUMATI
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 449. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
