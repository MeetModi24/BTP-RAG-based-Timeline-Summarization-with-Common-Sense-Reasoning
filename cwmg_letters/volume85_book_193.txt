193. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
SEVAGRAM,
August 17, 1946
MY DEAR MR. ABELL,
I am instructed by Gandhiji to send you a copy of a wire just re-
ceived by him. It needs no comment. Things in Goa seem to be in a bad
way.
Kind regards,
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
G. E. B. ABELL, ESQ.
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, pp. 101-
