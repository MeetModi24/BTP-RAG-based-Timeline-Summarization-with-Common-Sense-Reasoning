37. LETTER TO S. SATYAMURTI
May 19, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have just received your letter. I have not followed the office
controversy. I shall form no hasty decision. My formula is what
I had framed at Sabarmati.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. S. SATYAMURTI, M.L.C.
SINGARACHARI STREET
TRIPLICANE
MADRAS
From the original: S. Satyamurti Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
