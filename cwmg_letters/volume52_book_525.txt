525. LETTER TO N. NARAYAN MURTHI
January 8, 1933
MY DEAR NARAYAN MURTHI 2 ,
I thank you for your letter. I am glad that the Sree Raghu-
nath Swami Temple of Berhampore is now really open to Harijans.
I take it that you are carrying on an agitation amongst Hari-
jans for self-reform and for conforming to the common requisites
of temple-entry-avoidance of beef and carrion, the taking of
daily ablutions and the wearing of clean clothes.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. NARAYAN MURTHI
THE BHARATI MANDALI
BERHAMPORE
