99. LETTER TO C. P. MATHEW
CAMP KHURJA,
November 3, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I have intimation from the Ashram of
the receipts of the gifts from the students. My message is this:
Real education consists in character-building. Let the students
therefore cultivate the taste for character-building side by side
with their literary pursuits.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. C. P . MATHEW
LECTURER, UNION CHRISTIAN COLLEGE
ALWAYE (S. INDIA)
