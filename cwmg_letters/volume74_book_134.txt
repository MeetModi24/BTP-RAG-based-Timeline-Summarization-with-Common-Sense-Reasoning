134. LETTER TO DR. S. K. VAIDYA
May 29, 1941
BHAI VAIDYA,
You give me useful information regularly. Whether one
adopts the method of non-violence or of violence, one can suc-
ceed only if one has patience. Can we ever imagine what pa-
tience and self-control Hitler and others must be obliged to
exercise? Stabbing cannot be stopped by stabbing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5750
