4. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
HARIJAN NIWAS,
KINGSWAY, DELHI,
March 15, 1937
CHI. MIRA,
The weather here is superb just now. It always is during
March and part of April.
Hope you will win Vijiya's heart. I shall never be able
to give you a better girl. And you must not pamper Kandu and
the other boys. They are done for if they develop the tender
skin that you and I have the misfortune to possess. The water
incident stuck me in the throat.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
This was written on the date given but could not be posted
before. Your letter is in. May not leave before Sunday.
From the original: C.W. 6377. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9843
