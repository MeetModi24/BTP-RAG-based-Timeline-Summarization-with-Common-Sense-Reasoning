38. LETTER TO A. KALESWARA RAO
July 23, 1945
BHAI KALESWARRAO,
I have your telegram. I showed it to Vinoba. He is so
much involved in the work here that he needs some free time.
He will therefore not be able to do any work for the exhibition.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
