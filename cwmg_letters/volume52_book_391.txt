391. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
December 25, 1932
CHI. PANDITJI,
Do write to me and let me know your views about the changes
in food.
I have received Mavalankar's letter about the problem of
the Chharas. I don't think it is necessary for me to do anything
from here.
I liked the description of cleaning work. I do remember about
the Bhajanavali, but I am helpless.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 239. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
