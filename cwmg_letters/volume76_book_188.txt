188. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 27, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
J. L. has passed the whole day here. We have had a heart
to heart talk. It is all to the good. We shall meet again. He
has got to go for the Lucknow meeting.
Your letter. I told J. L. about your anxiety to meet him,
but Shummy's boil prevented you. He thought it was your
visit to the V. I hope he is better for the lancet cut.
The heat is on the increase. It increases but to decrease.
I don't want you to witness the process.
Ba is flourishing. Did I tell you I had gone up to 101
lb.? I think I have.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
MANORVILLE
SIMLA W.
From the original: C.W. 4124. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7433
