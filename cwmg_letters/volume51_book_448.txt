448. LETTER TO DOROTHY
October 20, 1932
MY DEAR DOROTHY,
I prize your love letter. Muriel was right when she told you
'pray' in reply to your question. Real praying from the heart brings
the real work behind it. For in the end work itself becomes prayer.
I like your simple affirmation 'I am clean now'. God will keep
you clean. Do not look back to the past. You have had your lesson
from it. Look forward to the future in hope and confidence.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, pp. 161-
