156. LETTER TO MADHAVJI V. THAKKAR
July 13, 1931
CHI. MADHAVJI,
I have been getting your postcards. These days I get no time
at all for writing letters. But you should go on writing regularly.
I am glad that Darbari has come over there. Tell him that he
should now stay on.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am in Surat today and will entrain for Simla at night.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6817
