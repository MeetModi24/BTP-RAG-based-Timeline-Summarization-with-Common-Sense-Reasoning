66. LETTER TO MADELEINE R. HARDING
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 15, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to receive your letter enclosing the introduction
from Rev. F. B. Meyor. I should have been delighted to meet
personally one bringing a note from Mr. Meyor, and I hope that
it will still be possible for us to meet some time. When you write
to Mr. Meyor, please send him my kind regards and tell him that
the meeting in Johannesburg to which he refers in his note is still
fresh in my memory.
Yours sincerely,
MISS MADELEINE R. HARDING
JOSHI VILLA, NAINITAL
From a photostat: S.N. 13000
