236. LETTER TO B. N. SASMAL
January 28, 1933
MY DEAR SASMAL,
Here is a postcard for you to digest.
Please enquire and
let me know what truth, if any, there is in the charge made by
the Kaviraj. I have written to him saying that you are incapable
of inciting people to shoot others.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1: p. c.
SJT. B. N. SASMAL
BAR-AT-LAW
MIDNAPORE
