437. LETTER TO NARSINHRAO B. DIVETIA
April 11, 1931
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I did not know that you had gone to live in another house. I
got your gift. Many thanks for it. The Bhajanavali contains one
more English hymn. Take up that, too, for translation, when you
have free time.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Narasinhraoni Rojnishi, p. 513
