444. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
November 22, 1934
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I sent you a letter some days ago simply inquiring about
your health. Mummy who came in yesterday tells me you do
not get letters except what is sent in Kamala's packet. I should
like to know the rules regulating your correspondence. Please
let me know how you are doing and how you are passing your
time.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1934. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Also A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 119
