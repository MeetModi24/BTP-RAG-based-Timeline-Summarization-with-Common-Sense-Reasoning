523. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
GAUHATI,
January 10, 1946
CHI. AMTULSALAAM,
Since you have been following my suggestions without
arguing about them, you are bound to get well. I should be
very happy if both of you sisters recover enough to be able to
come to Madras. But it will be as God wills. I may not write
to anybody else today. Blessings to all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 503
