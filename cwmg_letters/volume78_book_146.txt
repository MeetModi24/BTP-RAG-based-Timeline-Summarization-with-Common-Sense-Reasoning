146. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
September 18, 1944
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter about Kamalnayan. What you say is
correct.
Are you still suffering from any after-effects of your fall?
I would be happy if you thought of paying a visit to Sevagram
some time.
What is your opinion regarding the suggestion in Chhagan-
lal's letter?
I am enclosing a letter from Mirabehn also. Send me your
reply to her. I will forward it to her. Follow the suggestion
made by her.
I am tied up here. I don't know when I shall be able
to get away.
Blessings from
BAPU
