110. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 29, 1936
MY DEAR REBEL,
I gave you a hurried note this afternoon.
V.'s letter herewith. You will talk to me about it when we meet.
Yes, I read J.'s letter as you did. The meaning has been
wilfully perverted by the Press.
If you do not meet me at Faizpur, your stopping at Wardha
would not mean any loss of time. And coming to Wardha and
Segaon is certainly easier than Bhusaval and Faizpur. And living
at Faizpur is none too comfortable. Let us [see] what is in store
for you.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3756. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6912
