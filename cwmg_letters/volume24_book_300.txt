300. LETTER TO A. W. BAKER
SABARMATI,
August 10, 1924
DEAR MR. BAKER 2 ,
I have received two letters from you. The first I acknowledged.
I have the book too. Do write always please. But I feel that
I must go my way. We are all searchers. It is well with us so
long as we do not interpose the'!' between God and ourselves.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
A. W. BAKER, ESQ.
P.O. PORT SHIPSTONE
NATAL
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
