92. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
SEVAGRAM,
October 18, 1940
CHI. PREMA,
I have your letter. Women certainly do have a place. But
I do not know where this struggle will take me and the country.
Everything is in God's hands.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10412. Also C.W. 6851.
Courtesy: Premabehn Kantak
