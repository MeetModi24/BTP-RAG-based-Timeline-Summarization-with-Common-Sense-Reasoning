695. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
SIMLA,
July 9, 1945
CHI. RADHAKISAN 1 ,
I am not enthused by your or anyone else's release. How is
Ramakrishna ? Write to me in detail. I know your release has
made some people happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
