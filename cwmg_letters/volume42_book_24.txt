24. LETTER TO K. SRINIVASAN
CAMP MUSSOORIE,
October 19, 1929
MY DEAR SRINIVASAN,
I thank you for your letter and the enclosures. You are quite
right in being indifferent about After Mother India. However, if I
get some leisure I shall go through the chapters you have sent
and if I find anything that I can usefully deal with I shall do so.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
K. SRINIVASAN, ESQ.
FREE PRESS OF INDIA, LTD.
24 BRIDE LANE, LONDON E.C.
From a photostat: S.N. 15827
