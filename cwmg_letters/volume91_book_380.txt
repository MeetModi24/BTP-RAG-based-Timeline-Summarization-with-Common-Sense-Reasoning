380. LETTER TO KHWAJA ABDUL MAJID
148 RUSSA ROAD, CALCUTTA,
May 5, 1925
DEAR KHWAJA SAHEB,
I wrote to you some time ago to ask you whether you had the
money that I was to collect and what decision had ultimately
been arrived at. I have to enquire this of you again. I under-
stand that the money has been paid over at Hakimji's. Pray
remember me to Khurshed Begum.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
KHWAJA SAHEB ABDUL MAJID
NATIONAL MUSLIM UNIVERSITY
ALIGARH
From the original : A. M. Khwaja Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
