486. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
September 24, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
What you have reported is a very serious matter. We can-
not steal an idol, nor can we hide the fact. Chi. Barin must
understand this. We should humbly admit the fact before the
village people. This is my [opinion. . . . Do] as Chi. Kishorelal
advises. From here I can only express my opinion.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8429. Also C.W. 5599. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
