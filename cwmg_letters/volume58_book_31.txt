31. LETTER TO MATHURADAS SHETH
May 27, 1934
Most probably I will arrive there on the 14th and leave for
Poona on the 19th. I will decide finally in a few days. You may
arrange whatever is possible within that time.
Please do not
take me to too many places. We should be content with what
is possible in one meeting. Personally I should like to keep at
one place and explain my views to workers and listen to what
they have to say.
Untouchability cannot be eradicated merely
with the help of money.
It will disappear only if the hearts of
large numbers can be melted. I would ask you to think about
this problem.
From Gujarati: C.W. 7540. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
