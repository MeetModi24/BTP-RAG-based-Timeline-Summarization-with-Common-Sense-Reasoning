165. LETTER TO SURENDRANATH MAHEY
January 30, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am for the abolition of all castes as they
exist today. I am in favour of abolition of all distinctions of high and
low, but I am not in favour of abolition of varnashramadharma
which to me is the antithesis of caste.
I do believe that Hindu
Marriage Law requires amending. If you can succeed in inducing
legislators to interest themselves in the cause, it will be good. I
cannot, because I believe that I am attending to things which are
of greater moment at present than marriage reforms through legis-
lation.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI SURENDRANATH MAHEY
10 TEMPLE ROAD
LAHORE
