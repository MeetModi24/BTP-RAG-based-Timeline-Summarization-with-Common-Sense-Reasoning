145. LETTER TO HAZARIPRASAD DWIVEDI
November 12, 1941
BHAI HAZARIPRASADJI,
I very much like the idea of publishing a quarterly from Visva-
bharati. This venture has my full approval.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HAZARIPRASAD DWIVEDI
VISVABHARATI, SANTINIKETAN
BENGAL
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 10260. Courtesy: Ravindra Sadan
Visvabharati
