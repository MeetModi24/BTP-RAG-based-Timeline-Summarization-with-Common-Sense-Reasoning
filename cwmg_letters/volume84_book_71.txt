71. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
DELHI,
April 24, 1946
CHI. A. S.,
I have your letter. Write to Jiten Babu what you think
proper and do what you like.
I shall return Jiten Babu's letter after showing it to Pra[fulla]
Babu.
It will be very good if your health improves.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 512
