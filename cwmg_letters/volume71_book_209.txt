209. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
SEGAON,
February 1, 1940
CHI. KAKA,
I have read Beli's article which I am enclosing. There is no-
thing new in it. You must have noticed that the Hindustani he has
suggested is nothing but Urdu. It is not worth adopting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6761
