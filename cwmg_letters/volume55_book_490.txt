490. LETTER TO BIPIN BIHARI VARMA
September 9, 1933
BHAI BIPIN,
I have your letter. I am pained to learn of your illness. May
God grant you quick recovery. As for the money, talk over with
Brijkishorebabu. I have not yet made up my mind. Write to
me again if I am still out. I am sending your letter to Brijkishore-
babu.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. BIPIN BIHARI VARMA, BAR-AT-LAW
SHAKARPUR, P.O. NARKATIA GANJ
CHAMPARAN
