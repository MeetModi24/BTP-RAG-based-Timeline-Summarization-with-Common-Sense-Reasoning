182. LETTER TO SUJATA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 20, 1926
MY DEAR SUJATA,
Urmila Devi tells me that you are bearing your grief bravely.
I know you are a good girl. Do give me a line to tell me how
you are feeling. May God be with you.
Yours,
SRIMATI SUJATA
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 19669
