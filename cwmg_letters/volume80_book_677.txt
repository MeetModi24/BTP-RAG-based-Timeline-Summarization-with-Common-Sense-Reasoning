677. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
July 6, 1945
CHI. VANU (OR VANUDI?),
Whatever you please. As you are there I will expect you to
make Manudi as cheerful and witty as you are. See that both
of you return completely cured of all your complaints. You are
not to be idle there. A worker learns wherever she is. And
there are lots of things to be learnt at that place. You should
not, therefore, feel unhappy at having to stay there. This letter
is meant for both of you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 5793. Also C.W. 3016. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
