263. LETTER TO RADHABEHN GANDHI
September 28, 1930
CHI. RADHIKA,
I have separate letters from you all. Since you post your letters
separately they do give them to me. But the understanding is
that ordinarily all letters should be included in one packet.
Your leg seems to be taking a long time to improve. Probably
the general weakness of your body is partly responsible for the
slow recovery. Narandas complains that none of you join in
prayers, etc. Is this true? Tell me clearly what the position is.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8686. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
