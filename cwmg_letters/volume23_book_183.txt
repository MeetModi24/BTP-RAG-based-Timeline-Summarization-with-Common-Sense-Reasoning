183. LETTER TO MANGAL SINGH
POST ANDHERI,
March 20, 1924
DEAR SARDAR MANGAL SINGH,
I was delighted to receive your letter.
I hope my telegram of congratulations was duly received.
I have hitherto refrained from saying anything publicly because I
do not know what our friends there would wish me to do in the
matter, and on receipt of your letter, I was inclined to make use
of it in order to enable me to make appropriate reference to the
splendid behaviour of the Jatha. But doubting whether you would
approve of such a step, I have framed an independent message of
which I enclose a copy herewith. Please keep me informed of
further developments.
Please remember me to the other friends.
Yours sincerely,
SARDAR MANGAL SINGH
" AKALI-TE-PARDESI"
AMRITSAR
From a photostat: S.N. 8541; also C.W. 5127
