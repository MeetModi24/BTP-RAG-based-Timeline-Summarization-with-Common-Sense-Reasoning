124. LETTER TO AMMU SWAMINATHAN
November 15, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. I take no interest and no part in elec-
tions. Please, therefore, keep me out of it.
Write to me in the national language or in your mother
tongue Tamil. Keep English for English people. At least make
a beginning.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI AMMU SWAMINATHAN
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
