475. LETTER TO KRISHNABAI NIMBKAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 25, 1944
DEAR KRISHNABAI,
I am late in answering your letter. I see you are incorrigible.
Obstinacy is both a virtue and a vice. Which it is depends on cir-
cumstances. In your case I would fain believe that it is a virtue.
You ask for permission to do certain things. Who am I to give
or withhold permission? I can only advise where advice is sought.
I have given you my answers.
Your attack on Rajaji is ill-founded.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SMT. KRISHNABAI NIMBKAR
192 POONAMALLEE HIGH ROAD
VEPERY, MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
