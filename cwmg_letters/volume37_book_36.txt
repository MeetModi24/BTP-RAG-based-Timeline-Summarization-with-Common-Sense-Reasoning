36. LETTER TO M. M. ASLAM KHAN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 6, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You will please excuse me for not being
able to give you anything. Without knowing a magazine, it is
very difficult for me to frame anything be it ever so short.
Yours sincerely,
M. M. ASLAM KHAN
SUFI MANZIL, PINDI BAHAUDDIN, PUNJAB
From a microfilm: S.N. 13462
