267. LETTER TO EDITOR-IN-CHARGE, "FREE PRESS
JOURNAL"
'DILKHUSHA', PANCHGANI,
July 15, 1944
DEAR EDITOR-IN-CHARGE,
I have your wire. My letter to Shri Sadanand is a public
reply to a public question and is meant for publication. The
proper thing was to have waited for my reply before publishing
the complaint against me. Delay appears to me to be sus-
picious.
If Shri Sadanand is away, and if direction is considered
necessary, in a matter of ordinary course, you have means of
taking directions by telephone.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
This Was Bapu, pp. 154-
