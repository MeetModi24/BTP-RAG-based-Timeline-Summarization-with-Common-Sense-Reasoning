123. LETTER TO A MAHARAJA
August 28, 1947
MAHARAJA SAHEB,
I have already sent you an acknowledgement of the sum of
Rs. 100,000 sent by you. I find that I have with me Rs. 150,000
earmarked for use in Noakhali. I am thus able to carry on
my work for the present. Now I have on hand relief work in
Tripura, Chittagong near Noakhali, Calcutta and Bihar, for which
I have a small sum. It will facilitate my work, if in addition
to it, I can use for the relief of both Hindu and Muslim
victims in these parts the sum received from you.
Kindly let me have your reply.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXIII
