406. LETTER TO SUDHIR CHANDRA GHOSH
November 16, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
Did I know anything about your Ashram when I was in
Orissa? How long have you been working? Are you in touch
with Gopabandhubabu? Who are your workers?
Yours sincerely,
SHRI SUDHIR CHANDRA GHOSH
ARILo SEVASHRAM, VILL. USUMA
FULNAKHARA P.O., CUTTACK DT.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
