577. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI JAKATDAR
September 25, 1941
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
To Jakatdarji I write in English but to you I shall write in
Hindi. I was pained to hear of Father's condition. I congratulate
you on your bravery.
You may make what use you think proper of the money
you have collected. Whether any money can be spared from
here we shall discuss when you come. I hope your work will
prosper and you will be able to bring order to the work in the
office.
Blessings from
BAPU
SAU. PRABHAVATIBAI JAKATDAR
CHAIRMAN, LOCAL BOARD
BHANDARA
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
