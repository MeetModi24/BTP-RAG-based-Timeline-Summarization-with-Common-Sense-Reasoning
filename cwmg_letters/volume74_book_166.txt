166. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 12, 1941
MY DEAR AMALA,
Your letter. Mahadev is in Ahmedabad. He has not been
ill. Durga is, and is in the Bhatia Hospital. I am glad you are
110
to have a new companion in Sati.
What is this about the
college? You will let me know more about it.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
