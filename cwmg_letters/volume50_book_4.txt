4. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
June 2, 1932
CHI. NARANDAS,
There is no reply yet regarding Mirabehn. I, therefore,
think that I should stop seeing visitors pending the reply. So
do not send any names for the next week. I will send you a
wire if a favourable reply is received. Mahadev desires that
Durga should be informed about this.
BAPU
SJT. NARANDAS GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
B.B.&.C.I. RLY.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C. W.8231. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
