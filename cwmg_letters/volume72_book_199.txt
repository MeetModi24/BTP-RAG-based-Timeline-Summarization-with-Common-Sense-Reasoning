199. LETTER TO SHIVA RAO
June 7, 1940
You are wonderful. I have read your letter to Mahadev.
The draft requires drastic amendments. In any case I won't
make them. I would advise you to send it to Maulana Saheb
and to Jawaharlal. My own opinion is that the time for a settle-
ment has not arrived. It will come but not without agony. I
have not lost hope but am prepared for the worst.
But you persevere along your own line.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
