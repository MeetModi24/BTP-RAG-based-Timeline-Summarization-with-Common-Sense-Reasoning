169. LETTER TO CHANDRAPRAKASH
SEVAGRAM,
February 4, 1945
CHI. CHANDRAPRAKASH,
I have your two postcards. Although Father is still not out
of danger, it is good that he has survived the crisis. May God
cure him completely.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
CHANDRAPRAKASH
PILLARS
SIALKOT
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
