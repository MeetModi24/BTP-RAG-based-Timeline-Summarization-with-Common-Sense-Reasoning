413. LETTER TO NARAYAN MORESHWAR KHARE
June 5, 1931
CHI. PANDITJI,
I did not require such a detailed explanation about the gather-
ing of Bhangis. You may arrange Rambhau's programme if he lets
you do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
