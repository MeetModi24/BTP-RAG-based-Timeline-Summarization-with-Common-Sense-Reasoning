454. LETTER TO CHATURBHUJ
April 24, 1934
BHAI CHATURBHUJ,
I wish success to your Youth Conference. Let the young
men remember one thing, namely, that service can be done through
self-control. A person of self-restraint does not consider anybody
lower than himself and should, therefore, free himself from all
sense of high and low. Let the young realize clearly that a prac-
tice like untouchability can never be part of religion.
Blessings from
BAPU
