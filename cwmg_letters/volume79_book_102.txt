102. LETTER TO A. N. SHARMA
SEVAGRAM,
January 24, 1945
MY DEAR SHARMA,
You are going to pick up Hindi soon I hope.
I understand what you mean. I want our friend to treat all
patients equally in the sense that each should be treated accord-
ing to his wants. None can have special care because of his status
Therefore our friend ought not to go beyond his capacity even
in the matter of assistants and attendants. I want some nature-
cure man to show that perfection which is humanly possible.
Describe to me at your leisure the whole of his treatment.
My own nature cure has proved quite efficacious for me.
Love.
Yours,
A. N. SHARMA
BHIMAVARAM
