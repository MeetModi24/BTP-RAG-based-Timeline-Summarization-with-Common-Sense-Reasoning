419. LETTER TO NIRANJAN SINGH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 17, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Your idea commends itself to me and
you are at liberty to translate my other works. I hope that all
your translations will be full and not of parts of the books you
may translate. I am obliged to write this because some transla-
tors have taken such liberty with my writings and that too in a
manner so as to give almost different meaning to them.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. NIRANJAN SINGH
PROFESSOR OF CHEMISTRY
KHALSA COLLEGE
AMRITSAR
From a microfilm: S.N. 15289
