75. LETTER TO ABHIMANYU
October 26, 1935
CHI. ABHIMANYU,
I am glad you wrote to me the moment you reached there.
When you write next, please improve your handwriting. Why
didn't you like this place? Which item of food did you not like?
A hungry person would relish any food. Such likes and dislikes
in food are for the sick and the aged. Children should be satisfied
with whatever things the elders give them to eat. May you all
prosper in the new year.
I am not writing a separate letter to Mother.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ABHIMANYU
C/O SHRI HEMENDRA B. DIWANJI
KHAR, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5744
