413. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
September 27, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
I am sending with this Gadodia's cheque for Rs. 200, which
for the present is to be credited to the Segaon account. You
will be sending it to Jamnalalji's, I suppose?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11485
