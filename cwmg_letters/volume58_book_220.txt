220. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
July 16, 1934
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
I had got your article about impolite names. I have been
wanting to write on the subject. At present I don't get even a
single moment. I cannot write anything for Harijan or Harijan-
bandhu. But I will certainly write about impolite names. I hope
you do spin.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. DAHYABHAI MANORDAS PATEL
DHOLKA
From Gujarati: C.W. 2704. Courtesy: Dahyabhai M. Patel
