38. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Phagan Sud 8 [February 27, 1920]
I got your letter yesterday. I hope to arrive there on Mon-
day. Hence I do not write myself, but just dictate a brief reply.
I feel completely exhausted today and so am lying down. With
some rest, however, I think I shall feel somewhat better. When
you served me like a good son, how could it ever be that I felt
dissatisfied? Because of you, I moved everywhere with no anxiety
about my correspondence. Had you not been there, Devdas
would have fallen ill and I also would have had to abandon
so many of the things I could do. More on Monday.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi
