188. LETTER TO ANANDI
July 8, 1932
DEAR ANANDI,
I shall see how long you remember your irritability. It is
good that you take milk regularly. How can I forget the stick
while taking a walk in the evening? Girls like you should read
books selected by the teacher. Then you will learn to select the
books.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 195
