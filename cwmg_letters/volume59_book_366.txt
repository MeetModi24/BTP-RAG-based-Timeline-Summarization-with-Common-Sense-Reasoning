366. LETTER TO C. G. RAMAN
November 13, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I must not trouble you to come to Wardha,
for I am not much interested in the invention to which Sjt.
Mehta refers in his letter.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI C. G. RAMAN
LINOTYPE AND MACHINERY, LTD.
21 GRAHAM ROAD, BALLARD ESTATE, BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
