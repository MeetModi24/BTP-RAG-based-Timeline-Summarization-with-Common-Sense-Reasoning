555. LETTER TO SATISH D. KALELKAR
NEW DELHI,
October 7, 1946
CHI. SATISH,
I have your letter. The story about the Chinese is very
interesting. I might use it in Harijanbandhu.
I was very glad to read that Chandan has acquired faith
in Ramanama and following in her footsteps so have you.
How nice if [the baby] possesses qualities worthy of the name
it has been given and what a great delight it would be for
all of you! If Rama came to dwell in Chandan's heart all
illness would disappear from her.
To you all,
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. SATISH KALELKAR
