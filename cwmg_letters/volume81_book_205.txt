205. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
August 14, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
Your two covers. Of course you will come to Poona when
you are easily free from all work there.
Can Vaidya Nanak Chand work in Sevagram? What will
he need? Has he a family? There may be very little Ayurvedic
work for him unless he is really well up in his work.
Sushila is in Nagpur.
Harijan meeting just on. Rameshwari is here.
Love.
BAPU
From the original : C.W.4164. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7800
