349. LETTER TO HARI GOVIND GOVIL
July 10, 1935
BHAI HARI GOVIL,
The book you sent has been received. My thanks to your
wife. She can write whenever she pleases.
What you say about a dictionary and a Hindi primer is
quite right. I shall do what is possible.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1022
