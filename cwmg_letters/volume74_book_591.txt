591. LETTER TO JIVARAJ
September 29, 1941
So far as I am personally concerned, the feeling grows upon
me that the possession of land and property is a nuisance and a
burden. Those who wish to travel should have as little luggage
as possible and I want to get rid of my superfluous baggage.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
