138. LETTER TO BHAGWANDAS
January 21, 1933
DEAR BABOO BHAGWANDAS,
I enclose herewith a Hindi translation of Sjt. C. V. Vaidya's
contribution, which is in Marathi, and a copy of Shridhar
Shastri Pathak's which is in Hindi.
Both the translations are likely to contain grammatical errors,
which you will please have corrected.
The translation of Sjt. C. V. Vaidya's contribution was done
by Sjt. Mahadev.
When these are published, will you kindly send me ten copies
of that supplement and ten of the first supplement?
Please tell Sri Prakasa to write to me.
Here are the promised lines for the Aaj.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18990
