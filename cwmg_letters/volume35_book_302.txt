302. LETTER TO CHERUKANDY KUTTAN
AS AT THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
December 23, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
Here is a copy of the letter from the Commissioner for Immi-
gration and Asiatic Affairs, Pretoria, in reply to my enquiry. If
you can give me fuller information, I shall communicate with the
Natal authorities.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. CHERUKANDY KUTTAN
ENGLISH COMPOSITOR
"MALABAR SPECTATOR" PRESS
CALICUT (S. MALABAR)
From a microfilm: S.N. 12665
