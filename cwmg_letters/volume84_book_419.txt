419. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
DELHI,
June 16, 1946
CHI. LILI,
I have your postcard of the 11th. You did well in not
coming just for a couple of days.
I am glad that you have passed your examination. I have
no doubt that if you work with the same confidence you will
succeed right through your finals. You can then serve to
your heart's content.
Blessings from
BAPU
PAREL
BOMBAY
