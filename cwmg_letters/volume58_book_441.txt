441. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
September 3, 1934
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
Mr. Mung of Burma came and saw me two days ago. He said
that at Haji's request he plunged into the Burmese question
and carried on such a powerful agitation that it even told on
his health. He has incurred, he says, an expense of Rs. 2,000.
Since there has been no result, neither the company nor Haji
would now accept his demand.
They deny any understanding
with him. He also expressed a desire to see you. I have replied
to him that everybody takes up such work of his own free will
and for the good of his people. If somebody refuses to keep
his promise, one should blame one's own stupidity. All the same,
I have advised him to write to you. If Haji gave a moral under-
taking of any kind, I am of the view that Mr. Mung should be
reimbursed the expense which he has incurred. Please let me
know what the facts of the case are.
I hope you are doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4723. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
