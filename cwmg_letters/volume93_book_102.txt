102. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
NANDI HILL,
May 18, 1936
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
The people in Maganwadi report that they have not yet
got cow-milk ghee. They will be presently out of stock. Please
see to the supply.
Yours,
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
LAHORE, PUNJAB
From a copy: Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
