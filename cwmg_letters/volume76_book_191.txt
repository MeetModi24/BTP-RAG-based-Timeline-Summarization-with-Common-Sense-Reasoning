191. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 28, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
I hope you have been having my letters regularly. They are
posted all right.
Maulana Saheb is definitely leaving tomorrow and reaching
here the day after. Khurshed is coming with him. Mira is still
in Orissa.
I am really keeping very well. What I am eating is enough.
I hope Shummy is better and that you are putting on weight.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Here is a letter from Sarup. You should attend after you
descend.
From the original: C.W. 4125. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7434
