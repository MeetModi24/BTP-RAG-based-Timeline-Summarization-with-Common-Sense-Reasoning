129. LETTER TO H. S. SUHRAWARDY
March 22, 1947
I have seen your Press note. . . .2 I must confess that it
does not give me much satisfaction. May we hold Pakistan Day
celebration meetings in parts of Bengal where Section 144,
Criminal Procedure Code, is not in action or where there is no
other prohibitory order? And if meetings can be held indoors
are they not likely to be far more dangerous than public meetings?
Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase, Vol. II, p. 38
