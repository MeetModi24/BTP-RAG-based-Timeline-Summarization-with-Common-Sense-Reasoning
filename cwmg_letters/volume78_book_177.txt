177. LETTER TO M. S. SUBBULAKSHMI
BOMBAY,
September 28, 1944
DEAR SUBBULAKSHMI,
Rajaji has told me everything about your good work in con-
nection with Kasturba Memorial Fund by using your musical
gifts. May God bless you.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI 2
From a photostat: C.W. 10600. Courtesy: T. Sadasivam
