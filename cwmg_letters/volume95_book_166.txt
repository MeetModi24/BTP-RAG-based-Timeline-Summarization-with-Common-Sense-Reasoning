166. LETTER TO LILAVATI P. ASAR
[1945]
CHI. LILI,
Here is the reply. How can you say that you are not getting all
the news from here? I am writing. You must not thus accept defeat.
You have got to pass. If you get disheartened, it will be a matter of
shame for me. You were born to carry out difficult things.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 9615; also C. W. 6587.
Courtesy: Lilavati P. Asar
