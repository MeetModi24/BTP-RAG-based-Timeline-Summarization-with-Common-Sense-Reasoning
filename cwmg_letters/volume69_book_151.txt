151. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
ON THE TRAIN,
April 8, 1939
CHI. KAKA,
I got your letter. I am on my way to Rajkot. Only God
knows when it will all end. I have a fear that you yourself tore
up the papers relating to the budget. Prepare it afresh and send
it. It is only in May that we shall now be able to attend to the
Gandhi Seva Sangh affairs. It will be excellent if the Hindi pro-
pagation work in Assam is properly organized. We should meet
their request for free teachers. The money for that can be raised
in Calcutta. Why not ask all the Provinces to give an account
of the Hindi work done? I don't think you will get any.
How can they send any when there is no account to give? All
the same you may try. Do you take proper care of your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
