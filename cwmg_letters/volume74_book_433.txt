433. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
August 27, 1941
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Remember that if your penance is pure, you will return here.
Wherever you may be study of Urdu must not be given up, and
you should improve your Hindi letters. Learn more and more
of the science of farming and cow-breeding.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen, p. 289
