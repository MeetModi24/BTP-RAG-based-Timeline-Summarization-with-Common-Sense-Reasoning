475. LETTER TO S. K. DATTA
January 3, 1933
DEAR DR. DATTA,
I had your letter. The way your students can help is by doing
regular, steady service to the Harijans as by conducting night
schools, by organizing not expensive but indigenous sports for
Harijan children in which the students would play side by side
with the children, by penetrating Harijan homes and finding out
their difficulties and solving them where they are able to do
so. These are only some of the ways. They will no doubt discover
many others.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19149
