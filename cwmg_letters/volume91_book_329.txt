329. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Shravan Vad 12 [August 27, 1924]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I shall reach there on Friday morning by the Mail. I shall
be accompanied by three or four persons. I shall stay there till
Wednesday and leave for Poona on Wednesday night.
The rest when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
