232. LETTER TO DADABHAI NAOROJI
Box 299,
JOHANNESBURG,
March 30, 1903
DEAR SIR,
I have to thank you for your letter. I now enclose a state-
ment up to date just to keep friends informed of the terrible
position here.
At the request of the people in East London, I am send-
ing today to Sir William a draft for £20-0- in connection with
their matter. The state there is just the same, though I under-
stand that the police, after the representations from the people,
are not enforcing the regulation about foot-paths strictly.
I remain,
Yours obediently,
M. K. GANDHI
THE HONOURABLE DADABHAI NAOROJI
[LONDON]
