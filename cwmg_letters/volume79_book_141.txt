141. LETTER TO BHULABHAI J. DESAI
SEVAGRAM,
January 31, 1945
BHAI BHULABHAI,
You must have seen the report of Liaquat Saheb's speech at
Tinnevelli. How can we reach an understanding with him? In
public he speaks as he likes and then talks to you in a different
tone altogether. And you are obliged to hold your tongue. This
is just to inform you and caution you. Do what you think
best. I may only let you know what I observe from a distance.
I can see nothing else, and what I see frightens me.
Blessings from
BAPU
