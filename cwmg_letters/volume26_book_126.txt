126. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
March 2, 1925
What is this decision about closing the national schools even?
I can understand somewhat the closing of the college. Is it neces-
sary to close the schools also?
With love,
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
