132. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
November 14, 1935
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
I shall be reaching there on the 12th. It is, therefore,
necessary to think and decide right now in what way I can be
useful for collecting money for the Harijan Fund. Thakkar Bapa
will be arriving here on the 23rd instant. If you wish you may
come then.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4003. Also C.W. 132. Courtesy:
Parikshitlal L. Majmudar
