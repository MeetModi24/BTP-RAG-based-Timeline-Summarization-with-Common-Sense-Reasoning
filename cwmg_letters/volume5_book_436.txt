436. LETTER TO COLONIAL SECRETARY
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
Urgent
[JOHANNESBURG,]
September 8, 1906
SIR,
I have the honour to request Your Excellency to forward the enclosed
telegrams to the Right Honourable the Secretary of State for India and
His Excellency the Viceroy of India.
You will notice that the text of the cable to His Excellency the Viceroy
of India is different from that of the other two My Association has autho-
rised me to disburse the expense of the cablegrams and on hearing from
you, I shall forward cheque. As the matter is one of great urgency, may
I request that the cablegrams be sent today.
I am, etc.,
ABDUL GANI
Pretoria Archives : L. G. File No. 93 : Asiatics
