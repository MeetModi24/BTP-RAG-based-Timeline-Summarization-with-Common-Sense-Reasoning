515. LETTER TO SARALADEVI A. SARABHAI
NEW DELHI,
October 1, 1946
CHI. SARALABEHN,
I am at present held up here. The meetings will be held
here. So, even though the meeting of the Agents may not take
place it will be good if you come over about that time. We
can then have talks. If you want to come even earlier you may
do so whenever you please.
Preserve your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
