247. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
January 9, 1937
CHI. MUNNALAL,
This time you will have to get a certificate from Mirabehn.
Supervise the scavenging work. Take Mirabehn with you. Wash
her clothes, etc., when necessary.
I am not writing a separate letter to Balwantsinha.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8590. Also C.W. 7007. Court-
esy: Munnalal G. Shah
