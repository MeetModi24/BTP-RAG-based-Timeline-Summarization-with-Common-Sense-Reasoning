241. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
BARDOLI,
July 26, 1931
CHI. VASUMATI,
I think you know one Krishnadevi who used to live in the
Ashram. She now lives in Solan, which is on the way to Simla.
I am thinking of sending you either there or to the place to which
Padma is going. I think she will go to some place near Almora.
Would you like to go there? As Sarojinidevi will be with
Padma, at either place you will have the company of a mature
woman.
I shall reach Borsad on Tuesday. If you like you may come
to see me there and stay on as long as I am there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9328. Also C.W. 574. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
