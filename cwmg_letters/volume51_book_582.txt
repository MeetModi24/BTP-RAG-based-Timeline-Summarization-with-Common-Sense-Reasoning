582. LETTER TO GULAM RASOOL AND AMINA QURESHI
November 6, 1932
CHI. QURESHI,
I got your beautiful description. I wish both of you to have
equally beautiful health.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. AMINA,
I will not tolerate your silence. I wrote to you and asked you
to improve your health. I should be very happy if you could
pick up courage and try.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6662
