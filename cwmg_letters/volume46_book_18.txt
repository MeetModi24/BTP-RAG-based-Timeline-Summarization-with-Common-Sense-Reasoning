18. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
April 22, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your letters. Why is it that you fell ill as soon as you
got back there? Cannot you again run away from there and go
to the Ashram? If there is nothing particular you have to do
there, you should come away. I am writing this from Surat on
my way to Bardoli.
I think I shall have to stay in Bardoli for
at least eight days. I am more or less all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3399
