101. LETTER TO PAPMA RUKMINI
CAMP KHURJA,
November 3, 1929
MY DEAR PAPMA,
I was pleased beyond measure to receive your well-written
letter. Why did you not come to me? If I had the time I
would have come over to see you. You must continue to write to
me and tell me how you are getting on.
Yours sincerely,
MISS PAPMA RUKMINI
1 NORTH END ROAD, DELHI
From a microfilm: S.N. 15714
