790. LETTER TO S. K. GUPTA
October 29, 1945
BHAI SHRIKANT,
I have your two postcards. I shall start touring after
finishing the work here. I shall probably reach the Ashram by
February. Write to me then. In the mean time carry on the
constructive work as I have suggested.
Do not trust the newspaper reports. I am not going to leave
Sevagram.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI S. K. GUPTA
EXCISE INSPECTOR
6 RAILWAY ROAD
FARUKHABAD, U. P.
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 9696. Also Pyarelal Papers. Cour-
tesy : Pyarelal
