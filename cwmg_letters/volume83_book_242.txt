242. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
POONA,
March 4, 1946
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I am returning the accompanying [letters]. Decide after most
careful thinking.
Meet Dr. Mehta. Decide something about the workers.
Think about other things also which you feel should be taken
into consideration.
If you do not do that, I see nothing
but obstacles in the way. Take the help of all those who are
there.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
How is Pillay? What do you do about Pirone, the leprosy
patient?
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8418. Also C. W. 5610. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
