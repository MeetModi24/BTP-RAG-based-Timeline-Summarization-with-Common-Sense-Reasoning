441. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 26, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your three letters. I gave all letters to Mahadev Desai. I
can't account for the understamping and bad sticking. I am
ashamed. Shall inquire. The rest of your letters I understand.
We must leave some things in God's hands. I had no difficulty in
Allahabad. Jawaharlal was quite nice. I had no particular talks.
He was too busy for them.
There was a good reply from Shummy.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3948. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7257
