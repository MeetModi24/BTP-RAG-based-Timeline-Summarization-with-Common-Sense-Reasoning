50. LETTER TO TARADEVI
MAGANWADI, WARDHA,
July 3, 1935
CHI. TARA,
I have your letter as also those of your mother and Laxmi-
devi. We must do our best and then accept whatever happens.
Where can we find a better Seva Sadan than the one where
your mother is? The path of service is difficult. Happiness and
unhappiness are all matters of the mind. We must regard both
alike. If Laxmidevi has reached there, I shall not write to
her separately.
Blessings from
BAPU
TARADEVI
BARUA KOTHI
SANDILA
DIST. HARDOI
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
