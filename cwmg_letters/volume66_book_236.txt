236. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
October 3, 1937
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
We want to do so many things but God alone knows what
is best for us. Now that you have stayed over, it is all right.
Come over tomorrow with Lilavati and take the evening train
with Jamnalalji or by yourself. You need not leave tonight.
Blessings from
BAPU 2
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 392
