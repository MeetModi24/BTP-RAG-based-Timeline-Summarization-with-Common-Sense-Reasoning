166. LETTER TO NAVIN GANDHI
November 24, 1945
CHI. NAVIN,
I have your letter. It is not very clear but it will do for
my purpose. I am sending it to Dr. Krishnavarma. I wish you
would learn to describe things clearly as they are.
Blessings from
BAPU
NAVIN GANDHI
45 NEHRU ROAD
VILE PARLE
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
