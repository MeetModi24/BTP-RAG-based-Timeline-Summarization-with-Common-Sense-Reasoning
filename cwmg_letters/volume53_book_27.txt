27. LETTER TO K. KELAPPAN
January 12, 1933
MY DEAR KELAPPAN,
I have your letter. On receipt thereof I telegraphed to
Gopalan to confer with Rajagopalachariar.
I hope that you have got over the effect of the accident and
do please see me on your way back.
Yours sincerely,
