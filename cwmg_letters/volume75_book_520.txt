520. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
March 25, 1942
CHI. MUNNALAL,
If you must leave you may. I leave the decision to you. You
may take up the Charkha Sangh work if you can. You yourself
will have to decide that. Peace you will find only in the cave of
your heart, neither in the nether world nor in heaven, nor in the
the forest. But that is only my personal opinion. Do not write
anything during the night even if you wake up and are unable
to sleep again.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8475. Also C.W. 7168. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
