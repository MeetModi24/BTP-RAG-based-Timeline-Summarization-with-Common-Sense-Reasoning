451. LETTER TO SIR KISHUN PARSHAD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 7, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
It gave me joy to hear from the lips of Babu Rup Narayan
of the great interest you have been taking in the khadi movement
and of the fact that you had already commenced spinning your-
self. I commend to your attention the manner in which the
movement is being handled by the Mysore State. I have no
doubt that if it is properly taken up it will be a blessing to the
poor agriculturists in the Nizam's Dominion.
Babu Rup Narayan tells me that I am to expect a sample
of your yarn and your eldest son's. I am looking forward to the
receipt of the samples. And if you will permit me, they will go
to our Museum where samples of yarn spun by distinguished
persons are collected.
Yours sincerely,
H. E. MAHARAJAH SIR KISHUN PARSHAD
YAMINUS-SULTANATH
CITY PALACE
HYDERABAD (DECCAN)
