438. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA P. KAMDAR
August 24, 1932
CHI. HARI -ICHCHHA 1,
Won't you mind if I did not write to you for five months?
I did not expect that you would be so lazy, and you do not
even write your full address, so that I have to send my letter via
the Ashram. Now give up your laziness. What was wrong with
your health? Write to me in detail about Rasik after meeting
him. Which school do Tara and Vasant attend?
Sardar Vallabhbhai and Mahadevbhai are with me.
How do you spend your day? Do you read anything or spin?
Have you got acquainted with Raihanabehn? How are your
eyes?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7470. Also C.W. 4916. Courtesy:
Hari-ichchha P. Kamdar
