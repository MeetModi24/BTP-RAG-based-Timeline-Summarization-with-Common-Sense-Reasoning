274. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
PATNA,
Monday, August 15, 1921
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have sent an article for the special Pateti issue of Sanj
Vartman. I have had it copied. I have not read the copy. If there
are any mistakes left, please see to them. Meet Kapadia and see if
he has any difficulty in reading the handwriting. I wish that there
should be no mistakes and no omissions in the article.
Your health will be all right now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
