349. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
August 8, 1941
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter. The news about Rajendra Babu was pain-
ful. Come over only after completing your work. If you think it
necessary to stay on for the sake of Rajendra Babu, you may do
so. Here it rained today and everybody felt relieved. Kanchan-
behn has fallen ill. The rest are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3562
