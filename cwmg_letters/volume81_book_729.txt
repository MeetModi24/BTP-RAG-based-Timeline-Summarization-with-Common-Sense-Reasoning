729. LETTER TO DURGA M. DESAI
October 23, 1945
CHI. DURGA,
From the letters that I get I find that you are both far from
well. Why is it so? Bablo must be fine.
After the operation Sushi must have got rid of her ailment.
Sushilabehn is at work here as I am dictating this letter. She
informs me that Sushi has completely recovered.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
