471. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
August 28, 1932
DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I prefer to write to you in Urdu unless you forbid it. It is
helpful to take full rest during the menstrual period. On other
days do only as much work as Narandas assigns to you. One
whose heart is pure and free of passions has nothing to hide
from the world. I do not know of any book that deals with
your pam. I shall look for it.
You can consult Mrs. Lazarus, she ought to know. Dr.
Sharma must have received my letter. Your Hindi script is good.
Keep writing a little in this way. How long is Kudsia to stay
there?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 255
