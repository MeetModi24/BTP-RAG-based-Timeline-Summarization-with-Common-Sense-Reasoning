111. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
[January 7, 1940]
CHI. KANAIYO,
If the elders are likely to be pained by your coming away so
soon and if you feel like it you may stay on for some time. But
then what about the water scarcity? Won't you have to stop both
eating and drinking water? Water is required even for cooking.
If you don't eat at all, an additional advantage will be that Naran-
das will be able to contribute to famine relief!
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
