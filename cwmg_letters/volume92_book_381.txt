381. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
Y. M.,
November 15, 1930
CHI. KANTA,
I have both your letter and your report. The report is very
good. As far as possible do not use pencil for letters to be sent
by post. Let me know especially about your mental state there.
Serve Gangabehn well.
Where is Brother? A letter from him was expected but I
see none.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
