2. LETTER TO SHANTA PATEL
September 16, 1934
CHI. SHANTA3,
I am returning your letter with this. I can't understand why
you want it back. I have explained all my reasons to Maganbhai.
I will not, therefore, spend time in repeating them here. You
will get my blessings. Isn't that enough? Have you informed
Lakshmidasbhai ?
What are you studying at present? I hope you know that Ba
is there just now, and also that Ramdas is ill.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. SHANTABEHN SHANKARBHAI PATEL
JAMES VILLA
ELLIS BRIDGE
AHMEDABAD
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4071. Also C.W. 22. Courtesy:
Shanta Patel
