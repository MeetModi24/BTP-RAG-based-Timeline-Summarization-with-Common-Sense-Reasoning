487. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
March 29,1935
A determined refusal solves all problems. One of the maxims
of practical wisdom offered by the English weekly Punch is this:
Whenever in doubt, "Don't is Punch's advice. . . ."
We can see that the prevailing atmosphere is thoroughly immoral.
In such circumstances silent service is the only golden path.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 157
