158. LETTER TO TOWN CLERK, DURBAN
14 MERCURY LANE,
[DURBAN,]
September 17, 1901
TO
WILLIAM COOLEY, ESQ.
TOWN CLERK
DURBAN
DEAR SIR,
I have to thank you for your letter of the 12th instant con-
veying thanks to the Indian Vigilance Committee for what it was
able to do in connection with the measures adopted for the
prevention of the plague.
I am to say that the Committee did what was merely its duty
and, should an occasion arise again, the co-operation of the
Indian community, in any measure that may be undertaken by
the Town Council in the interests of the health of the Borough,
will be as readily forthcoming as before.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
