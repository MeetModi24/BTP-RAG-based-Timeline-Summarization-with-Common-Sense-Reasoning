491. LETTER TO DHIRENDRA N. CHATTERJEE
ON WAY TO POONA,
September 24, 1945
CHI. DHIREN,
Yesterday I got your letter. I only wish that everything is
all right with you. Give up your forgetfulness. Sodepur is a
big educational centre for you. I hope to reach there around
November 2. Abba, Kanu and others will be with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
