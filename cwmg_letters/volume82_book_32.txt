32. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
POONA,
November 4, 1945
CHI. JANAKI MAIYA,
Your joy must have added a cubit to your stature. You will
have had my telegram. Now you must give up all attachments
and look after Jamnalal's work of cow-protection. You must
become an expert in that.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 5852
