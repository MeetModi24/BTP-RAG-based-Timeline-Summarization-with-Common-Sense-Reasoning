681. LETTER TO PYARELAL
SEVAGRAM,
March 5, 1941
CHI. PYARELAL,
This time I am sending only Valunjkar to receive you. I
cannot send Mahadev because he is in Delhi. Otherwise I would
have sent him. I could send Sankaran. But Valunjkar has
come today. So I am sending him. You will of course come by
the first train. The rest when you arrive here.
Blessings from
BAPU
