254. LETTER TO N. C. BARDALOI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 28, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I referred your letter to the A.I.S.A. Office and here is the
statement prepared by the Office. I do not wish to judge. But I
want you to see the correct position for yourself. There should be
no laxity in the handling of public funds. And resentment of
discipline, I hold to be a grave fault with us. Without discipline,
Bardoli would have been a perfect fiasco. There were over 100
workers under Vallabhbhai and they all acted as one man. I am
not aware of a misunderstanding having ever arisen. There were
over 1,000 workers at the time of flood relief last year. It was
the same thing then as in Bardoli.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. C. BARDALOI
SANTI BHAVAN, GAUHATI
