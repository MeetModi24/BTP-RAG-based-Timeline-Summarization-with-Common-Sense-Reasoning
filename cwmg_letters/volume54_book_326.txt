326. LETTER TO AMARNATH TANDON
April 2, 1933
BHAI AMARNATH,
The books should be lent to the untouchables on the same
conditions as are applicable to others. But they should not be
charged any subscription.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[PS.]
Congratulations on the additions to the library.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 229
