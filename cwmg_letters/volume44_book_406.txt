406. LETTER TO GOVIND PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 9, 1930
CHI. GOVIND,
I have your letter. If your eyes are weak, it is right that you
should not read much. How many chapters of the Gita have you
learnt by heart? If you learn the whole of the Gita by heart, you
will have plenty of material for reflection when your mind is not
occupied otherwise. Then it will not be necessary for you to read
anything else. You should, of course, fully understand what you
learn by heart.
Is the yarn which you spin sufficiently strong? Is it even?
Who makes the slivers?
Blessings from
BAPU
