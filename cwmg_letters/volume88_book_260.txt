260. LETTER TO JAYACHANDRA VIDYALANKAR
NEW DELHI,
July 5, 1947
BHAI JAYACHANDRAJI,
I have your letter. My energy is depleted now. I cannot
take up any new venture. There is also no time to look after
everything. I am also no use in respect of the Oza Memorial.
Moreover my stay here is uncertain.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
