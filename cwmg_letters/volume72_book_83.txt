83. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
May 17, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
Your corrections are quite good. They should have occur-
red to everyone of us, but there you are.
Here are some letters for you.
I shall examine your translations and return to you. Have
commenced t[he] [e] xam [i]5nation already.
Lalita Kumari is down with headache. Nothing much. She
has these attacks often.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
J.L. came in yesterday. He left this morning.
From the original: C.W. 3968. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7277
