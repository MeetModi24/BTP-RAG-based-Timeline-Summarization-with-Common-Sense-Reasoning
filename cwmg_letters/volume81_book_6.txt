6. LETTER TO MADHAVDAS G. KAPADIA
July 19, 1945
CHI. MADHAVDAS1,
It is not good that you still don't feel comfortable there.
You should resolve that you are to get well and not to budge from
the place. Follow Dr. Krishnavarma's instructions scrupulously
and cheerfully. I am writing this before the morning prayer,
soon after rising. It is now time for prayer. I had got up at
4 o'clock. I arrived here yesterday at 4.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati
G.N. 2724
