471. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
September 25, 1946
CHI. BRAJKRISHNA,
Your letter is full of ignorance and sentimentality.
What I am doing I am not doing in anger. The error
you people committed became merely the means. I was awaken-
ed. I sensed luxury in my food. The changes I have made
are natural to me. It is not at all an atonement. You should
rather pray that it may become natural to me. If it does not,
I shall revert to my old diet. I have not given up leafy vege-
tables. I take their juice. There is more of jaggery than fruit.
If it becomes necessary to take wheat I shall take it. Your
atonement does not lie in feeling distressed, but in being awake
and alert. Nor does it lie in fasting or imposing hardships on
yourself. It lies rather in overcoming ignorance and sentimentality
and giving yourself up to work with carefulness and detachment.
Do not worry about me at all.
Blessings from
BAPU
