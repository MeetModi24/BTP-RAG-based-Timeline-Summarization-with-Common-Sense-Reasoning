576. LETTER TO PREMA KANTAK
October 6, 1945
CHI. PREMA,
I read your letter and tore it up. I am returning with Su-
shila the cutting sent by you.
I asked Sushila to read out your article, so that I might not
make any mistake in understanding it. It will be no good getting
it published in English. Its publication in Marathi is more than
enough. Your language is not faulty. But everything is not to
be said on every occasion. We shall discuss the matter when
we meet some time. If you wish to come specially to discuss it,
take an appointment and come. Bapa has sent the statement
about your camp to the Trustees. We have fixed a meeting of
the Committee here on the 16th. I will see then.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10439. Also C.W. 6878. Cour-
tesy : Prema Kantak
