440. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday, Bhadra Sud 8 [September 15, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I did get your letter. I have received Lady Pattani's reply,
which please find enclosed. You may now take possession of the
bungalow whenever you wish.
MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
HOMI VILLA
PANCHGANI
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 12279
