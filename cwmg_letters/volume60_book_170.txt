170. LETTER TO PURNACHANDRA SARMAH
January 30, 1935
MY DEAR SARMAH,
I have your letter. You have to fix up everything with
Annadababu and get him to approach the A . I . S . A . if he has a
workable scheme.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI PURNACHANDRA SARMAH
PRESIDENT
DISTRICT CONGRESS COMMITTEE, NOWGONG (ASSAM)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
