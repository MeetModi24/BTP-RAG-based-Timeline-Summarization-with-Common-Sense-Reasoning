70. LETTER TO M. A. JINNAH
August 29, 1944
BHAI JINNAH,
I have your letter. I got neither a telegram nor a letter by
the return of post. That made me impatient and I sent the tele-
gram. Afterwards I got your reply. Thanks. I am glad to learn
that you are keeping well.
I have fixed my engagements up to the 7th September. I
have kept myself free after that date. Hence I can present myself
on or after the 9th, whenever you call me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
