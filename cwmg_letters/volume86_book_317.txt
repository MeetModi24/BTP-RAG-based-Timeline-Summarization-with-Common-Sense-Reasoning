317. LETTER TO DINESH SINGH
SRIRAMPUR,
December 20, 1946
CHI. DINESH,
I received the cheque for Rs. 1,000/- sent by Bhai Feroze
Gandhi on your behalf for relief work in Noakhali. Please
form Bhai Feroze.
I may not remember to write to you. When you find me
free from here, write to me and meet me. I hope everything is
going on well.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAJASAHEB KALAKANKAR
KALAKANKAR HOUSE
PRATAPGARH
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 8677
