238. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
October 17, 1934
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
Dinkar Pandya must have reached there. I have had a talk
with Jayaprakash. He has incurred some debt already. Prabhavati's
expenses will be met here. I have fixed Rs. 325 a month for Jaya-
prakash.
This time you may send a cheque for Rs. 150 out of
which I shall send Rs. 100 to him keeping back Rs. 50 for Prabha-
vati as it has never been the practice so far to meet her expenses
here. This sum of Rs. 50 goes towards the expenditure incurred
during her recent visit to Patna.
Blessings from
BAPU
191
[PS.]
How did you like my second statement1?
From Hindi: C.W. 7999. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
