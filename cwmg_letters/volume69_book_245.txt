245. LETTER TO LAKSHMI NARAYAN GADODIA
May 7, 1939
BHAI LAKSHMI NARAYANJI,
Balvantsinhaji is a member of the Segaon Ashram. He
is devoted to the cow. He has served the cow since his child-
hood. Please depute someone to guide him to the dairy and
also arrange for him to visit the Government farm.
Has the dispute regarding the mosque been settled?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2627
