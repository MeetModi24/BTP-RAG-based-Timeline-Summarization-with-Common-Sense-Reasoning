83. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 28, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I have your letter. I have gone through some more sheets of
your rendering. It is likely that as I proceed it will run smooth.
I have the earth bandage on and am lying on my back as I write
this. I do so in the midst of the visitors. Therefore no more.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3680. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6489
