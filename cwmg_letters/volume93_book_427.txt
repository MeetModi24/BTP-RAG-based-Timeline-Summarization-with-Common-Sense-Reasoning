427. LETTER TO N. S. VENKATAKRISHNAN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 19, 1939
DEAR FRIEND 2 ,
The portion you object to is inoffensive in terms of non-
violence of the type the Congress has reached. Moreover Congress
Governments have to represent both Congressmen and non-
Congressmen.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C. W. 10973. Courtesy: N. S. Venkatakrishnan
