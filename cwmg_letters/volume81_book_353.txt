353. LETTER TO MRS. GEORGE JOSEPH
September 1, 1945
DEAR SISTER1,
I saw Chi. Babu's wedding card. May Babu and her
husband live long and serve the country.
Why is the invitation card in English? Why not in Malaya-
lam or Hindustani? Why so much infatuation for English?
Blessings from
BAPU
MRS. GEORGE JOSEPH
KALLOZHAM
CHENGANNUR
(TRAVANCORE)
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
