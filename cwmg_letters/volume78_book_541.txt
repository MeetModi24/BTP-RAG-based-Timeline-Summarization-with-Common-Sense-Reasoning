541. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
SEVAGRAM,
December 3, 1944
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letter. May God cure you all. Give me all the
news. You would have noticed that from tomorrow I am going
to stop writing letters, etc. I shall resume in January.
Blessings to you all
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy : National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
