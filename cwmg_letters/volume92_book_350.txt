350. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 7, 1930
CHI. SUMANGAL,
I have necessarily to think of you every day, because I have
with me your translations of the Hindi bhajans of the Bhajanavali;
Kakasaheb had asked for them. I am translating them into
English with his help. We both find your translation in-
accurate at places. It is certainly hasty. Still, as a whole it can
be considered satisfactory. We are both well. Write to me in
detail about the work going on there. Kakasaheb sends his
blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy:
