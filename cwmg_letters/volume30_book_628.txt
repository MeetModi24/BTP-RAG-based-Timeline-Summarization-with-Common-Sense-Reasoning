628. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Thursday, June 3, 1926
BHAI BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your letter today. I am sorry and surprised. There
was a letter and a telegram from you regarding Devdas. The
telegram was replied to by wire, and I asked Mahadev to in-
form [you] about Devdas's condition. I do not remember any
other correspondence nor am I displeased with anything you did.
Surely there ought to be some reason for displeasure and you have
given me none. I have told you and I repeat that you may come
to the Ashram whenever you please.
Devdas will be discharged from the hospital by tomorrow, and
will straightway proceed to Mussoorie. If you are still there you
can see him on his way to Mussoorie, as he will certainly inform
you. You need not delay coming here just in order to see Dev-
das. My visit to Finland is 99 per cent cancelled.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2352
