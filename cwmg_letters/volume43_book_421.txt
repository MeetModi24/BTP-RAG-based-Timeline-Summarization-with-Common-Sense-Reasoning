421. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
May 20, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
What shall I say to comfort you? God will assuredly pro-
tect you. You can write to me. I should indeed like you to do so.
Tell Kamalabehn to take care of her health. Convey my respect-
ful greetings to Swarup Rani . My blessings to Sarup3, Krishna .
If Jayaprakash is outside convey my blessings to him. Write to
me at the Ashram address. I keep fine health. I eat the same food
here that I did outside.
Do not worry about me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3388
