46. LETTER TO REVASHANKER JHAVERI
JAIL,
Silence Day, March 13, 1922
I enjoy perfect peace. I was arrested only after I had eradi-
cated my anger, had undergone atonement and purified myself.
What better lot can there be for India or for me? Please do
not worry about me at all . . . .
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 48
