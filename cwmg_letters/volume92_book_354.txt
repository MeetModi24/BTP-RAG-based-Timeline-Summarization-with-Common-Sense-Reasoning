354. LETTER TO RALIATBEHN VRINDAVANDAS
September 18, 1930
DEAR SISTER1,
Mani writes that you think of me. That is but natural. I
on my part think of you very often. When I recall your face I
am reminded of Narottamdas because of your likeness to him.
May God grant peace to you and to Mother.
Jai Shrikrishna from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 11532
