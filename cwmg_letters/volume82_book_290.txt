290. LETTER TO THE AGA KHAN
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR,
December 14, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your kind telegram. I do not return from Madras
via Poona. The probability is that I shall return via Bezwada to
Wardha in February, when a date mutually convenient can be
agreed upon either in Bombay or Poona, wherever it is convenient
for you at the time.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. H. THE AGA KHAN
BOMBAY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
