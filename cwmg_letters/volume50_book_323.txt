323. LETTER TO BABALBHAI MEHTA
August 4, 1932
BABALBHAI,
I got your letter. My experience tells me that, instead of
bothering about how the whole world may live in the right
manner, we should think how we ourselves may do so. We do
not even know whether the world lives in the right manner or in
a wrong manner. If, however, we live in the right manner, we
shall feel that others also do the same, or shall discover a way of
persuading them to do so.
To know the atman means to forget the body, or, in other
words, to become a cipher. Anybody who becomes a cipher
will have realized the atman.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9449
