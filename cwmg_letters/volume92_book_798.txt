798. LETTER TO N. V. MATHANY
WARDHA,
[September 24, 1934]
BHAI MATHANY,
I have your letter, I do not remember to have received
your letter of August 31.
As for Bhavnagar, I wrote what I thought. I know Bhai
Balwantrai fairly well. Occasionally I even have personal contact
with him. I know his ideas.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI N. V. MATHANY
C/O MRS. T. P. ALLADEEN
LALBAZAR
CHHAPRA
From the Gujarati original : M. K. Gandhi Papers. Courtesy:
National Archives of India
