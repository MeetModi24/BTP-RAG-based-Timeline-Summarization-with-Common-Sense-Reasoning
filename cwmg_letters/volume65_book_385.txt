385. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
June 25, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
All right. I will not go there to see Amtul Salaam. Your
presence will be enough. I am not scared. I wanted to come
merely for my satisfaction. Let me know the result immediately.
Bablo has written a beautiful letter. I will write to him
afterwards. Will you please detain Bachu for Amtul Salaam?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
If you need more help, shall I send someone from here?
Prepare the envelopes for the letters to Shantikumar and Rame-
shwar there.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11523
