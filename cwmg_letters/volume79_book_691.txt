691. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
April 20, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Here is Grimm's Law. It is a very good guide. You can
frame a law for the languages of India with its help. Ask me if
you cannot. It is a very interesting study. You will see from it
that learning scripts is child's play. I have received a chart which
makes it very easy. There is hardly any original script. At the
moment I am very busy. I can't write more than this. In a
few hours I have to go to the station.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KRISHNACHANDRA
ASHRAM
SEVAGRAM
