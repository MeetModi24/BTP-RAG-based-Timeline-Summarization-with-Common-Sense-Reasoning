612. LETTER TO PYARELAL
February 9, 1947
CHI. PYARELAL,
The lessons you give to Manu tend to be very long. I have read
them today. Today's lesson is also long. She cannot digest such long
lessons. She must be given short lessons and the handwriting should
be big and beautiful like beads. Even I am not able to read these.
There should not be so much scratching. She has made this complaint
twice or thrice before. I have asked her to write herself. What I have
written is only on my behalf. But Manu has seen it and she approves
of it. Her grasp is slow. Moreover, right now she does not even wish
to see all the letters that are received concerning her. Even so they
affect her.
Now Shailen has come to read the newspaper. So, I should stop.
Blessings from
BAPU
