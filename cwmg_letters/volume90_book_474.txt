474. LETTER TO SANKARAN
NEW DELHI,
January 29, 1948
BHAI SANKARAN 2 ,
Chi. Kishorelal gave me news of the death of your daugh-
ter Sulochana. I had no idea of it at all. What can I write to
you? What comfort could I give? Death is a true friend. It is
only our ignorance that causes us grief. Sulochana's spirit was
yesterday, is today and will remain tomorrow. The body, of
course, must die. Sulochana has gone taking her failings with
her, leaving the good in her behind. Let us not forget that or
her. Be even more true in the discharge of your duty.
Blessings from
BAPU
