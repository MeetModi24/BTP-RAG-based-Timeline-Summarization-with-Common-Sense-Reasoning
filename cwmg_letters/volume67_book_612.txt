612. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
UTMANZAI,
October 9, 1938
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your three letters. We have Lindlower's book; but if
you still want to order a copy you may certainly do so. It
does not matter whether or not you get the amount from home.
I do not want to force you to do anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4306
