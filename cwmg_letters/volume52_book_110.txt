110. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
November 27, 1932
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
I got your letter. I like your idea of going to Wardha.
Countless people have benefited by fasting. I know, however,
that you did not. I attribute that to my insufficient experience.
But you can continue the asana exercises and other remedies.
Do go to Lonavala for some time. The air and water of a place
certainly have an effect on health. However, I don't wish to
bother you with my suggestions just now. Do what you like, but
improve your health.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 905. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi.
