667. LETTER TO TARABEHN MASHRUWALA
[February 13, 1941]
CHI. TARI,
I have your postcard. Your weight can be said to have
considerably improved. I am anxious to know the X-ray
report. Manu is doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
