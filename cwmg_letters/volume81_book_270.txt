270. LETTER TO LAKSHMI
August 24, 1945
CHI. LAKSHMI1,
Balasundarambehn writes to say that you have given birth
to a son. May you both live long and serve the country. Only
yesterday you were a little girl who used to cheat me. Now
you have become a mother. What a miracle!
Blessings from
BAPU
MRS. SATYAMURTHI
CAMP HARIPAD MATOM
KOTTAYAM (S.I.)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
