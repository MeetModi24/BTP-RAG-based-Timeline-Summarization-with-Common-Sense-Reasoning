424. LETTER TO LAKSHMINARAYAN AGRAWAL
NEW DELHI,
July 22,1947
BHAI LAKSHMIBABU 3 ,
This friend has come from Bihar. What do you say about
what he has to relate? I do not want to form an opinion on
the basis of a one-sided account. Can anything be done in this
matter?
How is the work there going on?
Blessings from
BAPU
