394. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 22, 1936
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I have your letter. You must not go on grumbling. You
should work within your capacity. Not much may be expected
from Tyagi. He is a good man but he cannot keep his mind steady.
You have to take the injections; start on them soon. Kanti is
with me all along. I am keeping well. It is naturally hot. I
shall leave for Bangalore about May 8, taking Sardar with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 332
