291. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
July 3, 1933
CHI. LILAVATI,
I got your letter. Your resolution is good. Cling to it now
with complete faith. Do not attempt anything beyond your
capacity. One should not obstinately persist in doing a thing
which is beyond the capacity of one's body. If you remain hum-
ble, everything will be all right. Write to me regularly. God will
give you peace of mind.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9326; also C.W. 6601
