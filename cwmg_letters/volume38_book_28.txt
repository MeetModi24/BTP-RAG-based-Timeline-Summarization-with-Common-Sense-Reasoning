28. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJI
Silence Day, November 5, 1928
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I got your letter. I have not been able to go through all the
appendices.
Mahadev does not seem to have conveyed the message. We
are experimenting here with bread-making. We also wish to start
bee-keeping. Please find some useful books on 'bread-making'
and 'bee-keeping'. I wrote to Mahadev about a book on bread-
making.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4707. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarji
