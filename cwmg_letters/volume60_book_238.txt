238. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
February 7, 1935
DEAR THAKKAR BAPA,
My point was not that Thar-Parkar had not enough money.
But the point was to satisfy the desire of Pratap Dialdas that this
money should be spent on Thar-Parkar. All, therefore, that you
need do is to make the necessary entry in the books transferring
this small amount to Thar-Parkar and restoring the equivalent
to the General Fund.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
