142. LETTER TO DHIRU
July 2, 1932
DEAR DHIRU,
I am writing to Gajanan about drawing. It requires some
help from others. There must be more of practice.
I am writing slowly the Urdu copy-book. Do you want the
copy-book or do you want to see the way I write the copy-book?
If you want to see that, I may tear off the pages I have written
and send them to you. If you want a copy-book it can be had
even there.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 61
