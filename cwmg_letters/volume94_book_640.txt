640. LETTER TO BUTT KRISTO PAUL & CO., LTD.
KAZIRKHIL,
RAMGANJ P.S.,
NOAKHALI DrsTT.,
February 28, 1947
GENTLEMEN,
I thank you for your prompt attention to my suggestions with regard
to the first-aid box. I have examined the contents of the few box[es]
sent by you. Whilst I was about to send you my considered opinion
Shri Satis Chandra Das Gupta was with me and so I handed the box
to him to examine it and give his expert opinion on the contents and
the make-up of the box. Naturally I cannot hurry him. He is such a
busy man. I will therefore advise you not to multiply the new pattern
and await my considered reply. I have some what I think valuable
suggestions. But having taken advantage of Satis Babu's presence I
want to fortify myself with his expert assistance.
Yours sincerely,
BUTT KRISTO PAUL & CO., LTD.
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
