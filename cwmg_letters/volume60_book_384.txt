384. LETTER TO HATIM ALVI
March 9, 1935
MY DEAR HATIM,
I have your letter. The pamphlet you refer to has not yet
been received. When I get it I shall certainly go through it for
your sake if it does not require too much time. But tell me
of what use can my opinion be in an essentially domestic quarrel
of a family in which I would be considered an utter outsider?
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
