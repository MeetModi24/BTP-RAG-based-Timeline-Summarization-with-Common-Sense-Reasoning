5. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Saturday [February 16, 1929]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letters. I have written to Father and expect to
receive his reply at Delhi. Whatever happens you are not to worry.
The rest when we meet, since I hope to reach the Ashram on
Wednesday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3314
