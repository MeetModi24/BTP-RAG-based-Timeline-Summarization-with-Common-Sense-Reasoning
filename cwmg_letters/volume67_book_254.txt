254. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
9 p.m., July 19, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Give the two friends from Travancore the accompanying two
letters, one is addressed to Vallabhbhai and the other to Sir
Purushottamdas.
The friend from Denmark seemed to me to be an excellent
man. I have already written you this, but his face is still before
my eyes and so you find me mentioning it again.
Some other letters also are enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Urgent
If two commodes can be spared from Maganwadi, send
them here and also the two thin mattresses belonging to Nimu.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11609
