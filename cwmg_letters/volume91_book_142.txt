142. LETTER TO F. G. PRATT
NADIAD,
April 6, 1918
DEAR MR. PRATT,
Your letter of the 29th ultimo has been redirected to me from
Indore. I thank you for it. Evidently it was His Excellency's
desire that notwithstanding the final letter I should have sought
an interview with you. Do you think that we may usefully meet
and discuss the situation? I had a full chat with Mr. Ghosal
and Mr. Hood yesterday. We were able to reach a reasonable
solution regarding Limbasi. But I am anxious that an equally
reasonable solution may be reached on the general question.
Your charge about my taking on too many responsibilities is
only too true. I can but plead helplessness. I know that I should
be there and handle the mill-hands instead of leaving them to
Miss Anasuya Sarabhai. And yet I dare not leave the Kaira
matter. I could not avoid it in the first instance.
I shall look forward to your visit to the school. I am
anxious to interest you in my experiment. Do please apologize
on my behalf to Miss Green for my having run away from
Ahmedabad.
I have your second letter also for which many thanks.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 10668. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
