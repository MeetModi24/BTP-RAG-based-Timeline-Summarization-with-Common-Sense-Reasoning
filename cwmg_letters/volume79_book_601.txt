601. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
BOMBAY,
April 6, 1945
CHI. MALKANI,
I had your telegram. Today I have received the letter. I
have sent a telegram to Jairamdas. You must have seen it. I
am here till the 20th. Come over. We shall talk over the whole
thing. So I shall not write more. You have done good work
in jail.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 944
