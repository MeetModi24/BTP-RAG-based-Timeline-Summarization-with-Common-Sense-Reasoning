580. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
POONA,
October 7, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I got your letter . You will never find any suggestion for self-
suppression in my letters. I would approve of only such non-
attachment as involves no self-suppression.
I am putting off the other questions for my next letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
