397. LETTER TO ISWAR SARAN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 12, 1939
DEAR MUNSHIJI,
I should like to see your creation. But I fear there will be
no time left. I should be reserved for such special work which
my health still permits me to do.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MUNSHI ISWAR SARAN
6 EDMONSTON ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From the original: C.W. 10204. Courtesy: Municipal Museum, Allahabad
