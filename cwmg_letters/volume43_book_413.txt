413. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day [On or after May 12, 1930]
CHI. RAMA,
I used to be happy to see you. I had not thought that you
would show such courage and fortitude. How is Mahalakshmi?
How are the two Dahibehns?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5321
