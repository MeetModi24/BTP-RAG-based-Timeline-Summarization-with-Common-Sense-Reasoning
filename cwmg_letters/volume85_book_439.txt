439. LETTER TO VICHITRA NARAYAN SHARMA
NEW DELHI,
September 20, 1946
BHAI VICHITRA,
I think the substance of Jajuji's letter to you is that the
exhibition which I have allowed to be put up in the name of
the Gandhi Ashram and on their own responsibility should be a
training camp and not a money-making business. It would
be better to leave out woollens also. Whoever needs these can
get them from the Gandhi Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VICHITRA NARAYAN SHARMA
GANDHI ASHRAM
MEERUT
