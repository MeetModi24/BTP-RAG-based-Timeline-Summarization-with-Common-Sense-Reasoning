681. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
POONA,
June 6, 1933
CHI. NIMU,
When Ramdas is out of jail you would not be expecting
letters from me, would you? I am writing this for a specific
purpose. I have seen what Ramdas wrote to you. I have also
had a long discussion with Jamnalalji. You should boldly let
me know what you yourself would like to do. Personally, I
like the idea of your going to Wardha. May you both live
there in good health and devote yourselves to God, that is,
to service.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Nirmala Gandhi Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
