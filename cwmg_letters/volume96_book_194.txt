194. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
BOMBAY,
February 6, 1915
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letters. I am writing against time. My travels are not
yet finished. I leave here for Poona very shortly and then go to Bolpur.
In a fortnight's time I may have some peace at Santiniketan. My ribs
are not yet right. I have asked Jamnadas to write to you at length.
More next week I hope.
With love,
Yours,
OLD FRIEND
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy: National
