9. LETTER TO DR. ATMARAM K. BHAGAWAT
October 21, 1946
BHAI BHAGAWAT 1 ,
I am able to write to you only now. Go for the medical
conference.
I have got Appa Saheb's letter. I am writing to him at
his address. He will have recovered by now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C. W. 2740. Courtesy: Manilal B. Desai
