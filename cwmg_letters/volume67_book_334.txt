334. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
August 10, 1938
CHI. AMRITLAL,
There is no change in Pyarelal's condition. At the moment
there seems to be no danger. Kaka will regain strength slowly.
Do not be impatient.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Kaur and Krishna, Jamnalal and Ramana Maharshi, Ba.
