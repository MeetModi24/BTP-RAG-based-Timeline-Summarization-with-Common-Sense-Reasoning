60. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
June 14, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
We arrived here at 8 a.m. The train was late. I have
your letter. My mind is there. But my body must be here
till Monday for Harijan's sake. If the weather is good, I hope
to be with you on Tuesday morning about 7.30 a.m. I shall
have taken milk on the way. I understand about Lilavati. I
do not know who will be with me if any at all. We shall see.
Do not worry. I quite see that till the things have shaped,
you will have to be in Segaon. 'Be careful for nothing.' If
someone is at the gate to show me the exact way to take, it
would be good. It may be Govind or Dasrath—is that the
other comrade's name? But it does not matter if no one
can be spared.
Love.
BAPU
