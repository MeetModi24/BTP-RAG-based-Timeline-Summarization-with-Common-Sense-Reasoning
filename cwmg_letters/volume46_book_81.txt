81. LETTER TO TAN YUN-SHAN
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 4, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
You must come again whenever you like.
My message to the Chinese students is: "Know that the
deliverance of China is through ahimsa pure and unadulterated."
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 9673; also The Sino-Indian Journal-Gandhi Memorial
Number, Vol. I, Part 11, p. 37
