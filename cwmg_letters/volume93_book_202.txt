202. LETTER TO PYARELAL
September 4, 1937
CHI. PYARELAL,
You fasted yesterday and you are fasting today also. For
what reason? Is it because of Yoga's engagement? If that be
so it is not right. I do not interfere because I think you do
everything after proper consideration. I wanted to talk to you
after I had the news about Yoga. But where was the time? My
health too did not permit it. There is no strength at all to talk.
I am dieting because of my illness.
Blessings from
BAPU
