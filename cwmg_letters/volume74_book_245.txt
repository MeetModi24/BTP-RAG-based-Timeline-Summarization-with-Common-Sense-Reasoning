245. LETTER TO DEPUTY COMMISSIONER, WARDHA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 12, 1941
THE DEPUTY COMMISSIONER
WARDHA
DEAR FRIEND,
This is to confirm the phone message of Sjt. Jamnalalji to
you that Sjt. Vinoba Bhave who was released today is to re-offer
civil disobedience at Nalwadi on Monday the 14th instant at
6 p.m.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
