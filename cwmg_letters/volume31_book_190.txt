190. LETTER TO NAJUKLAL N. CHOKSI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday, Asadha Sud 11 [July 21, 1926]
BHAI NAJUKLAL,
I have your letter, and Moti's too. Moti seems to think that
she has taken proper care of her health by not neglecting her
medicine, whereas medicine is the least part of the business. Illness
is caused by violation of rules of diet, exercise, rest, etc. Being care-
ful about health means cheerfully observing these rules, and break-
ing them is to be guilty of extreme lethargy. Explain this to her.
I look upon Hindu-Muslim riots as a kind of surgical opera-
tion. It would have been excellent if we could have avoided it,
but evidently we have been suffering from inflammation of this
particular limb and the condition was no longer curable by banda-
ging the part. When this rioting is over, one day we shall certain-
ly become united. And if this society is much too decayed to last,
let it perish. In any case the present condition is not worse than
the profound darkness in which it was slumbering.
Blessings from
BAPU
