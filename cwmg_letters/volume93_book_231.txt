231. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEGAON,
Silence day, April 18, 1938
CHI. SUSHILA,
Pyarelal told me that you had cried and my heart wept. A
wicked person sees everyone as wicked. Do I also belong to
the same category? How very perverted I must be to imagine
even for a moment perversity in a pure-hearted girl like you?
Why should you serve me? Where do I have the right to take
service from you? Your duty is clear. Stay with Mother and
do whatever work of service you can from there. It is too true
that I have ruined the life of a saintly man like Pyarelal. Is
that not enough? Am I a godfather to you or the opposite of
one?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
