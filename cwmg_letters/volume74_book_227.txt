227. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
July 4, 1941
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your postcard. You must now be getting ready to go
to Deoli. I am sure you will come here on your way back. I
had a letter from Jayaprakash. He seems to be somewhat dis-
satisfied with what I am doing. But then was he ever satisfied?
I am going to reply to him. Tell him that, though he re-
mains dissatisfied [with me], I will be fully satisfied [with him]
if he improves his health. Rajkumari has gone to Simla for a
change of air. Rajen Babu must needs fall ill the moment he
arrives there. Ba is fine. I am quite well. Sushila is still here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3561
