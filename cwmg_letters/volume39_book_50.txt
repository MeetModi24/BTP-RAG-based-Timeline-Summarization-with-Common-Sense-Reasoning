50. LETTER TO H. T. HOLLAND
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 11, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
It is only now I am able to acknowledge your note. I had
not one minute to spare in Shikarpur. I had to disappoint many
who had more or less expected I would visit their institutions. In
the circumstances I was unable to make time for your institution.
You will please therefore forgive me.
Yours sincerely,
REV. H. T. HOLLAND
SETH HIRANAND CHARITABLE HOSPITAL
SHIKARPUR
From a photostat: S.N. 15006
39-29
