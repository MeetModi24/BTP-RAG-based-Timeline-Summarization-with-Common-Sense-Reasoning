316. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
[Before April 28, 1930]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have written to you about Kevalram. He is very shy. Hence
I have sent him this note. He can look after the Congress office.
It will be good if both of them join the Congress and work
there. I do not know what the situation in the Congress at
present is and so I am sending you this note. Kevalram has
lived in the Ashram. I have known him for many years. His
father is the Dewan of Wadhwan. Nirmala is the daughter of the
late renowned Kevalram Dave of Kathiawar. At one time there
was a plan for me to undertake the responsibility of bringing
her up in S.A. But just then Bhai passed away and I lost his
son. Similarly Nirmala and others were also lost to me, and
Kevalrambhai's aspiration remained unfulfilled. Bhai's sons could
not be recovered but the lady, it would seem, can now be
reached. This is the reason I have been cultivating her and
Kevalrambhai for some years now. I have written this so that
you know with whom you have to deal.
Blessings from
BAPU
