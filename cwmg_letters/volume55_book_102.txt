102. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[On or after May 2, 1933]
DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I have your letter. You should not be perturbed on hearing
of my fast. Now I will be unable to write letters but my heart
will be with you. Keep writing to me. May God make you
well. Great service is to be taken from you. But God knows
better about everything. Whatever He does will be for our good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 283
1 Amina G. Qureshi
2 Beginning "Uth jag musafir bhor bhai"; "arise, awake, O traveller, it
is morning now."
3 From the similarity in contents of this and the other letters of May
2, it appears that this was written some time between May 2 and May 8.
