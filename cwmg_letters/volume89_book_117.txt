117. LETTER TO DHIRU
CALCUTTA,
August 27, 1947
CHI. DHIRU,
I have your letter. I see nothing wrong about your going
to Bombay if Balkoba wants to take you along.
I understand what you say about eczema.
This should be shown to Balkoba.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
