254. LETTER TO A. SUBBIAH
ASHRAM,
January 27, 1930
MY DEAR SUBBIAH,
I grieve for you and your wife, for you must naturally have
felt it. Death causes no sorrow in me now. It has become to
me a veritable friend. Comparing death with birth, death may
almost be an event for rejoicing and birth for sorrow. Death
may be a deliverance, birth is imprisonment. Even a living
baby dies after and never before . . . If there are still more debts
to pay, there is another body to inhabit.
Yours,
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32579/63
