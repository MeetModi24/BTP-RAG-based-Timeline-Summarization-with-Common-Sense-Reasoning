185. LETTER TO MANILAL B. DESAI
SEVAGRAM,
August 16, 1946
CHI. MANILAL,
Chi. Paramanand has given me all the news from there. He
has brought a cheque for Rs. 5,000. This can be credited only
there. I have written to Maganlal regarding the problems that
will have to be tackled in connection with his appointment as a
trustee. Paramanand will tell you. I also learn that Gangabehn
has arrived. That is a good thing. I hope Purushottam is
better there. I expect you have had enough rainfall. Have you
started implementing the suggestions I had left? How is Gokhale
getting along? Balkrishna should write to me. Blessings to all
the friends there.
SJT. MANILAL
URULI KANCHAN
From a copy of the Gujarati Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
