618. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
YERAVDA,
December 19, 1932
CHI. TARAMATI,
I had received your last letter all right. There was nothing
in it that called for a reply. So I saved the time. I did hope
that Mathuradas would stop losing weight. We are all fine.
Dilip's education will be progressing smoothly.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
21 MINT ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
