131. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
February 12, 1932
CHI. MANGALA (DARLING),
I got your letter. You must learn to avoid even the few
mistakes which you make in reciting the Gita verses. Otherwise
you will cease to be ladki and will remain a lakdi for ever.
Where does Kamu live? How is her health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4075. Also C.W. 39. Courtesy:
Mangalabehn B. Desai
