690. LETTER TO DR. B. S. MOONJE
SIMLA WEST,
July 9, 1945
DEAR DOCTOR SAHEB,
I had your telegram. And now I have your letter. I have
seen in my experience of sixty years that those who have listened
to me have never committed suicide.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. B. S. MOONJE
BHONSLE MILITARY SCHOOL
NASIK
