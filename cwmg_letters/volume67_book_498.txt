498. LETTER TO ATULANAND CHAKRABARTY
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 15, 1938
DEAR ATULANAND,
I see no inconsistency between my letters to you. Your note
read like giving stone when bread was wanted. I have in mind
the growing distrust and bitterness which no writing can possibly
remove. I have told you you have to be your own missionary.
I have written as I have felt. Please be patient with me and go
your way.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 1480. Courtesy: A. K. Sen
