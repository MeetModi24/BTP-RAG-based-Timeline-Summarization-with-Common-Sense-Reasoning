191. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
SEVAGRAM,
February 7, 1945
CHI. SHRIMAN,
I have gone through what you wrote about the Mahila
Ashram. It is good.
You can explain the objective in two or three lines. Do so.
In doing it keep in mind the promise made by Jamnalalji.
So far as possible we should consider and implement it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 304
