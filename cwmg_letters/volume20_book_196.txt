196. LETTER TO K. P. JAGASIA BROTHERS
DEAR SIRS,
BOMBAY,
[After July 19, 1921]
With reference to your letter of the 19th July last, I am
unable to advise you except to say that you should do what every
merchant is doing. The idea of raffle does not commend itself
to me.
Yours truly,
From a photostat
S.N. 7580
1 To questions from the audience
2 Jagasia Brothers, General Import and Export merchants of Karachi,
had written to Gandhiji, complaining of heavy losses in business and stock of
foreign cloth lying idle due to the swadeshi movement. They had sought
Gandhiji's advice as to how to meet their liabilities and asked whether he
would approve of their disposing of unsold stocks through a lottery.
