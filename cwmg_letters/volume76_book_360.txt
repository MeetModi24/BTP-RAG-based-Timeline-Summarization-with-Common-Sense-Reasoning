360. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
July 20, 1942
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
I have your letter. I see that your work is going on well.
It is good. Who can say at the moment what is going to
happen? We should all be ready and do whatever comes to
our lot.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1092
