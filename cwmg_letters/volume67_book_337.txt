337. LETTER TO UMABAI L. ATHAVALE
SEGAON,
August 11, 1938
DEAR SISTER,
I hope you are not brooding over Nana's death. I am im-
pressed by your courage. After seeing you, I am able to under-
stand Nana's spirit of sacrifice and self-control better. Nana's
body has perished, but his spirit will remain in the Mahila
Ashram and will be a source of inspiration to the sisters.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
[From Gujarati]
Bapuki Virat Vatsalata, p. 75
