332. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
[SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA, (C.P.)]
September 14, 1941
CHI. RAMDAS,
I got your postcard. I understand what you say. I hope you will get this.
You have not given your address. What you have written to Nimu is right. I
hope your food arrangement is satisfactory.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
