6. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 31, 1929
CHI. MAHADEV,
Herewith Motilalji's telegram expressing his joy. It shows
that the decision not to go [to Europe] was correct. Motilalji's
permission to me to go was only to keep my way open. But
what is our duty now? There is of course the task of prepar-
ing the Ashram. We are daily making progress in our ideas about
this, and I see that in order to attain our objective through
non-violence we have got to put the Ashram forward as a means.
But my purpose in writing this is to suggest to you to consider
with Vallabhbhai what Gujarat, and Bardoli, can do.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/
