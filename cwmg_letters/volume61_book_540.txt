540. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
August 28, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
What is the use of your being in that clear air and having
good honey and fresh fruit, if you have laryngitis? I hope it is
now a thing of the past.
I am passing on your letter to Kumarappa. I know that he
won't feel lonely there.
How do you like this note-paper and the cover, all made
here? Is the border too rough? What of the colour?
I must remember to send you rice and the spindle.
I had better news about Devdas.
Love to you both.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3543. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6352
