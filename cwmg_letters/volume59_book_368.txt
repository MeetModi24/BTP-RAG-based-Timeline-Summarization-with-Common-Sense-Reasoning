368. LETTER TO BHAU P. PATIL
November 13, 1934
MY DEAR BHAU,
I have your letter. What you say is all good. You should
write to Thakkar Bapa. After all he is the keeper of the purse.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI BHAU P. PATIL
SHRI CHHATRAPATI SHAHU BOARDING HOUSE
SATARA CITY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
