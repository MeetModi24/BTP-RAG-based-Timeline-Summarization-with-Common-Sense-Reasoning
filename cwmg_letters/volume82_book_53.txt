53. LETTER TO SIR EVAN M. JENKINS
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD, POONA,
November 7, 1945
DEAR SIR EVAN,
I have to thank you for your letter of 1st instant regarding
the mercy petition of Shri Haridas Mitra and three others. Will
you please convey my thanks to His Excellency for the commu-
tation of the death sentences?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 51
