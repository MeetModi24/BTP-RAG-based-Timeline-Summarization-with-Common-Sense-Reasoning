291. LETTER TO MADHAVJI V. THAKKAR
April 29, 1929
BHAISHRI MADHAVJI,
I have not heard from you for some time. I interpret this
to mean that you are in tolerably good health. If you write to
me at Tuni, the letter will take the least time to reach me.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6778
