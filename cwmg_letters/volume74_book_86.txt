86. LETTER TO SARANGADHAR DAS
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
[Before May 15, 1941]
DEAR SARANGADHAR DAS,
I was delighted to read your very interesting letter to Raj-
kumari.
Yes, I remember having received your statement. I can remem-
ber nothing that jarred on my ears. Rajkumari was then in the
Punjab.
I am glad you came in such close contact with Harijans. 'C'
class is undoubtedly best. "A" and "B" are [an] eternal sore. It is
of our making. I hope you are none the worse for your experience.
I know there is trouble outside. Rajen Babu cannot leave Bihar
for the present. As I have already told Padhiary we have all to
learn the art of solving our own difficulties. . . .5
BAPU
From a copy: C.W. 10516. Courtesy: Government of Orissa
