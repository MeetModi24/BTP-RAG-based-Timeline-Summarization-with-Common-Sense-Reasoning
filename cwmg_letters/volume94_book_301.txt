301. LETTER TO RAMLAL VARMA
SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA,
October 29, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter of the 25th July has been lying in Gandhiji's file all this time. He
is very sorry he could not reply to it earlier. He wanted to send you a helpful reply.
I am now to say that he is very sorry he does not know what help he can render
in the matter.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI RAMLAL VARMA
DAILY "TEJ"
SHRADDHANAND BAZAR
DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
