186. LETTER TO C. NARAYANA MENON
[Before December 7, 1932]
DEAR FRIEND,
It seems to me that if the letter addressed to the Zamorin and
signed by all students who believe in the removal of untouch-
ability be sent, it will be a striking thing showing the Hindu
world that the rising generation is bent on removing the black
mark and it would be a great thing if the example of the Hindu
University was followed by the Hindu students throughout
India.
Yours sincerely,
The Hindustan Times, 9-12-1932
