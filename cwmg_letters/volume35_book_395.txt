395. LETTER TO D. N. BANERJI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
January 31, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. If you are absolutely clear in your mind
about non violence, it is your duty to make that statement before
the judge and decline to give evidence and cheerfully suffer the
consequence. You should believe that it would be the duty of
a judge administering a penal judgment to punish those who do
not obey laws of the country in which they live. And, in this
instance, there can be no question of civil disobedience either, be-
cause the law of punishing witnesses who do not answer questions
will be enforced even after swaraj.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. D. N. BANERJI
94, BARADEO
BENARES CITY
From a photostat: S.N. 13058
