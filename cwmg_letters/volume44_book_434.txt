434. LETTER TO PADMA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 14, 1930
CHI. PADMA,
What a careless girl you are! Why do you write what looks
like the marks left by a fly crawling across the sheet, and spoil your
handwriting right from this age? Can you not get ink there?
And you don't write anything about your health. You are quite
right when you say that our sacrifices are nothing as compared
to the sacrifices made by the women on that side. Well, then, learn
to make similar sacrifices while you live there and devote your-
self wholly to service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6115
