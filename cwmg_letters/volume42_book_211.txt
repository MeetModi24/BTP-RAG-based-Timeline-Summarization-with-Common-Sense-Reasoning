211. LETTER TO TOTARAM SANADHYA
PRAYAG,
Silence Day [November 18, 1929]
BHAI TOTARAMJI,
The doctor's opinion about Gangadevi is not to be entirely
relied upon. For the present she needs nothing besides milk, curds
and fruits. Anyway I am returning soon. After observing
Gangadevi's health and her wishes I shall make the changes that
may be necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2335
