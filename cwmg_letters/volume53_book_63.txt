63. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
January 15, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
You seem to have lost confidence in me. It doesn't matter.
Go and live somewhere for a year and then tell me. Will it give
you confidence if I suggest that Narandas may pay a short visit
during the last month or even earlier? It will not matter if you
are not able to return to the Ashram at all. Create the atmos-
phere of the Ashram wherever you live. You need not then go
to the Ashram. Will this satisfy you?
I feel much worried that Purushottam's health does not
improve. I have been thinking what to do.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 870. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
