464. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
Saturday [October 27, 1928]
CHI. RAMDAS,
I have your letter.
Let me know what work you are now busy on. It is good
that you joined the Mandal of Mithubehn.
Ba is getting impatient to send Nimu there. Nimu has not
asked for anything. But today Ba was insistent and hence I write
this much to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I read the accompanying letter after writing this one. But
now I do not have to give you any guidance. Under the tender
care of Mithubehn you will have no discomfort.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6858
