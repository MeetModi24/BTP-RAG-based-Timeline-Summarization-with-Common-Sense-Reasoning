456. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
September 7, 1938
I have written to Vijaya. Manjula may examine her. She
may go to Maroli if it is necessary. Let her not try quack
remedies.
How is it that you too get fever? What is it due to?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10771
