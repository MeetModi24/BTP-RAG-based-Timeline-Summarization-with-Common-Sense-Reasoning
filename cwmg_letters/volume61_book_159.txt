159. LETTER TO VAIKUNTHLAL L. MEHTA
BORSAD,
May 27, 1935
BHAI VAI[KUNTH]1,
Bhai [Ma]thu[ra]das Vasanji informs me that as you are
unable to sign the Harijan Sevak Sangh pledge, you wish to
resign. Is this true? I didn't believe it. The pledge is only a
symbol of our mental attitude.
How can we dispense with it?
How can we ask anybody else to take a pledge which you cannot
take? I believe that Bhai Mathuradas [has not understood you].
Write [to me] and explain the correct position. The Bombay
branch of the Sangh must be placed on a sound footing.
BAPU
[PS.]
I will leave Borsad on the 31st.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1362
