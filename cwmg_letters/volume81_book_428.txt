428. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL M. TRIVEDI
September 10, 1945
BHAI CHIMANLAL,
Your letter. I will not have any time till the 22nd. After
that God alone knows.
I know nothing about insurance. You can see Acharya
Kumarappa at Maganwadi, Wardha [about this].
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI CHIMANLAL MANEKLAL TRIVEDI
ANAND BHUVAN
AMRAVATI ROAD
NAGPUR, C. P.
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
