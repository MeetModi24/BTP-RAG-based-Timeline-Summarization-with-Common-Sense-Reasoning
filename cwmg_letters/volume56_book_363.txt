363. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
December 15, 1933
CHI. AMALA,
I was disappointed not to see you. I wrote in answer to your
fear that I would not drive you out. You must compose your-
self and live and think and speak naturally. If you love me, you
cannot fear me.
I have no blood-pressure. Do write.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
