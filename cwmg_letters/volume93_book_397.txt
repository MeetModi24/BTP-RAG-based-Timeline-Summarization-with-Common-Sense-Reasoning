397. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 17, 1939
CHI. SUMANGAL,
I have your letter. Do write what you feel you must. It is
not as if you had to follow my advice. You must follow the
dictates of your inner voice.
It is good you decided to get married. I have no girl in
view. Only your own endeavour can bear fruit. You must be
patient.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
