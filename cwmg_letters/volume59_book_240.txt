240. LETTER TO BIRENDRA NATH GUHA
October 18, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I understand all you say. I have had
a chat with Sitaramji about the workers whom you mention, and
I am writing to Charu Babu.
SHRI BIRENDRA NATH GUHA
VIDYASHRAM
B 76 COLLEGE ST., MARKET, CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
