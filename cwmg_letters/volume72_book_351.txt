351. LETTER TO CHARU PROBHA SENGUPTA
July 20, 1940
DEAR CHARU PROBHA,
I have an idea that I wrote to you in reply to your indict-
ment of man. If not, this is to say you have to be patient.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI CHARU PROBHA SENGUPTA
123/1/ UPPER CIRCULAR ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 8708
