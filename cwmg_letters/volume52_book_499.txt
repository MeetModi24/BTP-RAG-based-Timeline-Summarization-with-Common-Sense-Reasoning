499. LETTER TO TOTARAM SANADHYA
January 5, 1933
BHAI TOTARAMJI,
I have seen the views expressed about the Giri family. It is
a pity that we could not convert them to goodness. Do send
me some time an account of your farming. I hope you are
keeping well.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2539
