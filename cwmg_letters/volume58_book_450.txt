450. LETTER TO S. N. BOSE
September 5, 1934
DEAR SATYANANDA BABU,
I have your letter. You have put me several posers which,
of course, I shall bear in mind at the forthcoming meeting.
Of course the secession of Malaviyaji and Bapuji Aney compli-
cates the situation. But these are the fortunes of political life.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SATYANANDA BOSE
4 NUNDY STREET
BALLYGUNGE
CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
