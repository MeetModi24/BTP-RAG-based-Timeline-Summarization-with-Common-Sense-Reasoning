312. LETTER TO N. P. COWIE
BOMBAY,
June 5 [1919]
DEAR MR. COWIE,
I beg to remind you of my letter of . . .2 regarding orders
passed by the Sind Authorities against certain subjects of Kathia-
war States. I shall be obliged if you will kindly favour me with an
early reply.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat : S.N. 6636
