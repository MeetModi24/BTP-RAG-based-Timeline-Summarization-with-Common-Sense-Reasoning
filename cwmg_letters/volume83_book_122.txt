122. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
February 11, 1946
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have your letter. Now we shall see about it only after I
come. You may not fix up a woman cook for the time being.
The matter about Dr. Dinshaw will also be settled after my
arrival.
I propose to reach there on the 18th. From there I proceed
to Bardoli on the 28th or on the 1st of March. I shall bring
with me as small a party as I can. You need not obtain anything
for their sake. You should lodge me where Doctor says. I am
even prepared to put up in a tent.
I shall be having a mos-
quito-net.
I shall see what I can do about Harijan.
Blessings from
BAPU
