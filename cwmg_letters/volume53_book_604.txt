604. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
March 3, 1933
DEAR MR. JAYAKAR,
I thank you for your two letters with your opinion. It will
assist me greatly. I would like you to bear the Bills in mind
and do whatever is possible.
I hope you have recovered all your lost strength.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 20445
