199. LETTER TO MOHAMMED HUSAIN KHAN
NEW DELHI,
July 12, 1947
I have your letter. It is not good. Let us go by convention. The
reply I sent is from minister Ansari Saheb of the Bihar Government.
If there is any error in it, it should be pointed out. In any case how
can I compel him?
I do not think it would be appropriate to write to Pandit
Jawaharlal.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
