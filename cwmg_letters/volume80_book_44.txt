44. LETTER TO DESAIBHAI PATEL
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 28, 1945
BHAI DESAIBHAI,
May the marriage of Chi. Sumanbehn and Chi. Satyakam
be fruitful. May both of them become true servants.
Blessings from
BAPU
ACHARYA DESAIBHAI
VITHAL KANYA VIDYALAYA
NADIAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
