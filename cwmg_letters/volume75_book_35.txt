35. LETTER TO RATILAL DESAI
October 16, 1941
BHAI RATILAL,
It seems I have not replied to your long letter. If so, I write
now. Your letter has helped me a lot in understanding the prob-
lems. I am getting on with the work. Let us see what happens.
Can we not cultivate or humour the Burmese?
I believe that a person like you can do a great deal of work
in this direction. I wish you do your best. I hope all are well.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
RATILAL DESAI
94 MOGHUL STREET
RANGOON
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
