74. LETTER TO ARTHUR HARRISON
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
September 29, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I could give you a few minutes
if you could come to 88 Knightsbridge, on . . .3
Yours sincerely,
ARTHUR HARRISON, ESQ.
BROTHERHOOD OF THE WAY HOUSE
61 HUGH STREET, S.W.
