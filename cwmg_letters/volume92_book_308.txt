308. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
April 16, 1930
MY DEAR MIRABAI,
I have your letter. What advance shall I make? Of course
I should greet the friends just as before. But not having any
sense of sin, how shall I give them satisfaction by talking? Their
grievance is evidently most against me. The only way I can
give them satisfaction is by correct conduct. Can you tell me
where I have erred?
I often sing to myself, 'We shall know each other better
when the mists have rolled away.'
I am likely to come to Bombay next week.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original : Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
