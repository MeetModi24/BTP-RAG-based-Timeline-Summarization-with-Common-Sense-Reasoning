50. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
November 14, 1936
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
Ghee should be eschewed unless you are sure of its quality.
Oil may be used by those who can digest it.
But the quantity
of milk should certainly be increased to make up for the lack of
ghee. With some effort pure ghee ought to be available. In
the matter of spices the attitude should be liberal. Students
should go only as far as they can. Give them black pepper if
it seems necessary, even green chillies if they are habituated
to them. But the value of simple pure food should be explained
to them and they should go as far as they can in the matter.
1 A word or two are illegible here.
It would be best for the students to grind the flour them-
selves; it will be cheaper, too, in the end.
Blessings from
BAPU
