195. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
DANDI,
Tuesday, April 8, 1930
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter and postscript. I hope Mother is not suffer-
ing from cancer. But the life is so artificial that one wonders
that we do not suffer more than we do.
Yes, the things are hastening to an end. You were right in
not going when you did not feel like it. You will hear all about
the exclusive field I have suggested for women.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5390. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9624
