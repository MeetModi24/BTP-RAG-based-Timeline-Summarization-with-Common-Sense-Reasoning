544. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
September 22, 1938
CHI. MARY,
I am glad you were none the worse for the experience. Find
out exactly what you are expected to do as Commissioner and
then certainly accept if it appears worth while. Your letter to
Amtul Salaam is excellent. I have handed it to her.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Here up to 29th.
From a photostat: G.N. 6071. Also C.W. 3401. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
