214. LETTER TO A. H. WEST
AHMEDABAD,
September 14, 1916
MY DEAR WEST,
I have your letter and copy of letter to Mr. Rustomjee. You
know that my heart is with you. I will say nothing about your
letter except to say that it hurts me to think that you felt so hurt.
For once you have taken Mr. Rustomjee seriously. I know that you
must soon after writing that letter have regained your usual
gaiety and philosophic calm. In any event this is merely to say that
you have the power to vote to yourself as much as you need per
month. Please use it without hesitation. Funds you have there. I
shall answer for you and your use of them. I see nothing wrong
in your using the funds. They were sent for the struggle. You and
a few others now are in the struggle which must be maintained.
I hope therefore that you will not argue with me again about the
rightness of the act. Imam Saheb now sees the thing properly.
Mr. Petit has asked me more than once for the balance of the
fund. What is the balance there now. Do please also send me the
items of expenditure after the date up to which the accounts
were rendered. We return only the balance which is left after pro-
viding for your losses there.
It is sad to think that Polak leaves South Africa. He is a big
asset.
We are mad after handloom work. So more later. With love,
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W.4423. Courtesy:
A. H. West
