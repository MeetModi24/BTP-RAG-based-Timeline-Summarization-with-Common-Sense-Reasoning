447. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
August 30, 1933
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have sent a reply to your wire. I should like very much to
go there immediately, but I can't do that.
I think it will be
better to pay a visit to Bombay first and then to Wardha. I wish
to acquaint myself with the atmosphere in Bombay. The Harijan
work there also seems to have become slack and, if possible, I
wish to infuse some life into it.
My health is steadily improving. I can eat with appetite.
Take care of your health. Letters for N. and Amala are enclosed.
The former seems to have become a little unsettled.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2923
