319. LETTER TO GOPINATH BARDOLOI
October 11, 1947
MY DEAR BARDOLOI1,
All the three grounds are sound, assuming of course that
there is no mistake about facts. You should certainly take the
much-needed rest. I am saying all this without knowing the
situation. I feel like the blind leading the blind. Therefore I am
perhaps a worthless guide. May not Kakasaheb be your guide
today? The best thing for you is to pray and do the best you
can, health or no health.
This is a poor letter. But I can give you nothing better.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G. N. 5
