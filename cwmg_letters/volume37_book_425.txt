425. LETTER TO T. R. PHOOKAN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 18, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. What appeal can I issue for you to take?
What I have suggested is that you should personally go to moneyed
Congressmen and ask them to help you out of the difficulty. I
feel that that is the only proper course for you to adopt, and I
feel also that those Congressmen who have money should bear the
burden. I suggest your taking an audited account to them.
You are at liberty to make whatever use you like of this letter
for approaching Congressmen.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. T. R. PHOOKAN
GAUHATI (ASSAM)
