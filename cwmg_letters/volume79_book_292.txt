292. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
February 27, 1945
CHI. MANI,
Chi. Dahyabhai writes to say that you will be released
tomorrow and that your health is none too good. Do come
and see me if you can. If you cannot come, write a detailed
letter. I am looking forward to seeing you. It is long time
since we met last.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 132
1 A Polish Engineer interested in village reconstruction movement.
Gandhiji gave him this name.
