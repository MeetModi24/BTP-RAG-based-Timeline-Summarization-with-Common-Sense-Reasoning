339. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
September 9, 1946
CHI. A. S.,
I have your letter. God has granted your wish, for I can-
not leave here before the 24th.
If you work only as much as your health permits, both
your body and mind will become as strong as steel. You will
then be able to do as much work as I want you to do.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 528
