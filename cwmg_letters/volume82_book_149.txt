149. LETTER TO N. G. RANGA
ON THE WAY TO SEVAGRAM,
November 19, 1945
BHAI RANGA,
I have your letter. Follow the dictates of your heart. No
matter what I say, if you have doubts you must pause. I know
that your first duty is to strengthen the peasant organization in
Andhra so that it becomes a model for the whole world. It is
difficult for me to go there.
Blessings from
BAPU
PROF. RANGA
NIDUBROLU
ANDHRA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
