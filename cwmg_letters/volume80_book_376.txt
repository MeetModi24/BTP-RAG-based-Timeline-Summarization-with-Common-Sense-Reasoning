376. LETTER TO OM PRAKASH GUPTA
May 30, 1945
CHI. OM PRAKASH,
Your beautiful letter is before me. I am disposing of all the
pending letters today. You must be getting on well. Let me
know what I have to do after I return.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
