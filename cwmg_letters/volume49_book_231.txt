231. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
March 13, 1932
CHI. PUSHPA,
I have written at length about speaking in some other letter
and you may read it. God has given us the various abilities to
control them, and some to suppress them altogether. He has
given us a mouth not to go on speaking in season and out of
season, but to speak for doing God's work. God has given us
abilities for doing both good and evil but we have to get rid of
the evil impulses completely. Do you understand now?
Blessings from
BAPU
