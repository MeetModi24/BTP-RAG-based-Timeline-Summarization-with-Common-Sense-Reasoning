187. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SIMLA,
May 13, 1946
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
Badshah Khan's letter is enclosed herewith. What does he
write about Hanif3? Sohanlal had met Badshah Khan.
I hope the Begum is all right. I have forgotten her name.
Will she continue to live there? Convey my blessings to her.
I hope your health is all right. I may go to Delhi tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 516
