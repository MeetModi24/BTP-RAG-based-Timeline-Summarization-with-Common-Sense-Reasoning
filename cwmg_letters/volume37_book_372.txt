372. LETTER TO D. B. KRISHNAMMA
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 4, 1928
MY DEAR KRISHNAMMA,
I was delighted to receive the album and the frame. Some of
the reproductions are very striking. You will show me the ori-
ginals when, if ever, I come to Rajahmundry.
I hope you are keeping quite well.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRIMATI D. B. KRISHNAMMA
RAJAHMUNDRY
From a microfilm: S.N. 14787
