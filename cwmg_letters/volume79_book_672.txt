672. LETTER TO R. K. SIDHWA
BOMBAY,
April 18, 1945
BHAI SIDHWA,
I have your letter.
I shall let you know beforehand if I write something of the
kind that you fear.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI R. K. SIDHWA, M.L.A.
VICTORIA ROAD
KARACHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
