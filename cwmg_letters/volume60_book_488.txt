488. LETTER TO TAGADUR RAMACHANDRA RAO
March 30, 1935
Your letter confirms the opinion I have already given. Your
fast was a species of violence. Apathy can be turned into sympathy
only by sustained, silent and selfless service.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
