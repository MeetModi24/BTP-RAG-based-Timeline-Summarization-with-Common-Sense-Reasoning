453. LETTER TO HEMANT KUMAR NILKANTH
NEW DELHI,
September 23, 1946
CHI. HEMANT KUMAR,
I got your letter about Nanalal Kavi. I had written some-
thing to his son about the matter. I got the news late; it is
not that I omitted to write for want of courtesy. I shall now
see what I can write. Have you now recovered fully?
Blessings from
BAPU
SF. HEMANT KUMAR
HARIJAN ASHRAM, SABARMATI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
