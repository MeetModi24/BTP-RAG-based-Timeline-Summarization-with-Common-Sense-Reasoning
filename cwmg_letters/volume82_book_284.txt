284. LETTER TO R. G. CASEY
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR,
December 12, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your kind letter of 10th instant regarding the Gope
Palace. This is a matter of such a delicate nature that I do
not think I can usefully interfere. And why should there be any
hurry if a popular government is shortly to replace the present
regime?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 112
