304. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
July 28, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
The tone of Balvantsinha's letter did not suggest [that the
refuse] was burnt out of enmity. His letter expresses nothing but
grief. Almost all the refuse is manure except broken glass. I feel
that what Balvantsinha says is correct; it would have been better
to have asked him before getting the refuse burnt.
When he
had asked for help, it should have been given. It is true that
a farmer would be deeply hurt if his manure is burnt. Try to
pacify Balvantsinha.
Blessings from
BAPU
