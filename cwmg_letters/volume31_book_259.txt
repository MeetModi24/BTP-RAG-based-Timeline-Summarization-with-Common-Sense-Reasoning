259. LETTER TO GORDHANBHAI M. PATEL
THE ASHRAM,
July 31, 1926
BHAISHRI GORDHANBHAI,
I have your letter. I do write occasionally about Patidar.
I do not look upon the Ramayana and the Mahabharata as historical
narratives. I have not attained self-realization. If it is true
that my influence over the people has declined, I do not know
the reason. I would attain self-realization this very day if I were
totally free from attachment to the ego. I cannot go into
samadhi. I do not know what will happen even tomorrow, let alone
in 1930.
Vandemataram from
GORDHANBHAI MOTIBHAI
JOGIDAS VITHAL'S POLE
BARODA
