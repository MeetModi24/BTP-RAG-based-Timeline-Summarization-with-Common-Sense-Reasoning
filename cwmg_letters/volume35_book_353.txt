353. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
January 15, 1928
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I remember Akhil Babu well. I am writing to him about
the accident to his wife. I remember the incident you refer to.
I have always liked him. Whilst I accepted Monmohan Babu's
repudiation, I thought none the worse of Akhil Babu. What
you now say about Monmohan Babu certainly makes me sad.
Dr. Ray has sent me two letters. In the first he refers to
khadi admiringly and in the second he declares his firm faith in
it and looks forward to Jamnalalji's visit. If I can, I shall send
you copies of the letters.
I am glad Hemaprabha Devi is cheerful now. It is strange
Tarini should still be unwell. Can he not conduct his researches
in a better climate?
With love,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1582
