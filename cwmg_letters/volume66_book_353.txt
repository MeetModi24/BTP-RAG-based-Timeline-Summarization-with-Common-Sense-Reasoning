353. LETTER TO RABINDRANATH TAGORE
CALCUTTA,
November 8, 1937
DEAR GURUDEV,
Your
messenger
has
brought your precious note with
receipts. I have done nothing. It is God's prompting; your
labours and prayers have borne fruit.
May you have com-
plete rest from worry and toil over the financial difficulties.
I am well, thanks.
Love.
M .
K . GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 4648
