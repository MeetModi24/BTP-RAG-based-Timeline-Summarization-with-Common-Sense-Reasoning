154. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
May 7, 1945
BHAI ICE,
I have your letter. It is good. Everyone says, one must
take leafy vegetables and fruit. You say, no. Please let me have
your reasons. Don't you take those things? Is milk alone suffi-
cient diet?
You can certainly cook separately. I on my part want to
give you two rooms for your patients. As I am away you may
take those from the Secretary. If you can cure any patients I
refer to you, I shall be very happy and can then fix your mon-
thly salary.
Make use of whatever utensil is necessary for distilled water.
Such water will be of use to us.
Do treat Balkrishna if he himself consults you. His condi-
tion has remained unchanged by Dr. Sushila's treatment.
I am constantly worrying about Hari-ichchha. My faith in
you will greatly increase if you can cure her.
I have answered all the questions, haven't I?
Write to me
if anything has been left out.
BAPU
