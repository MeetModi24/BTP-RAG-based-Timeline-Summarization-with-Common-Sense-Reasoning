88. LETTER TO MANGALDAS PAKVASA
September 1, 1944
BHAI MANGALDAS 1 ,
I got your letter and your note. Both are useful. I hope to
reach there on the 9th. I shall return at the earliest on the
10th. If you can get time while I am there, please do come and
see me. Otherwise write to me at Sevagram and ask for power
of attorney of any kind you need. It is a matter of satisfaction
that your health is improving, though slowly.
Blessings from
BAPU
MANGALDAS PAKVASA
29 DUNGARSINH ROAD
MALABAR HILL
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 4687. Courtesy: Mangaldas
Pakvasa
