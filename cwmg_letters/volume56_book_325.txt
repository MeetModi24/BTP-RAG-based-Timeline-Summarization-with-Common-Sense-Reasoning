325. LETTER TO GODAVARI
December 4, 1933
CHI. GODAVARI 1 ,
I am longing to see you ever SInce I arrived. Rajendra
Singh inquired in the morning and I sent a message [for you]
to come at 8 o'clock, but you have every right to come whenever
you wish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 9670
