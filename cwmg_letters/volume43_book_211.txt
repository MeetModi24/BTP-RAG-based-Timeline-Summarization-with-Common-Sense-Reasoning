211. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
April 10, 1930
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter. Glad mother out of danger. It may be possible
for you to do something in the drink campaign if it ever comes
into being. If it comes, it will come naturally. No time for more.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5391. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G.N. 9625
