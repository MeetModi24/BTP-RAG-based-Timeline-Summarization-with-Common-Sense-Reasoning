583. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
Thursday [May 27, 1926]
CHI. DEVDAS,
Once again I have been acting as a tyrant to you as if I could
not tolerate anybody being healthy. I cannot write long letters
to you because I do not get a single minute free.
I have been thinking whom I can send over to you. We may
certainly like that that person should accompany you to the Hima-
layas, but have we any right to spend money in that manner?
You should certainly go. I shall send someone to help you there.
Leave the choice to me.
I see that Surendra cannot leave now. You may take Brij-
kishan with you if he agrees. You will certainly get some company
during the journey when you start from there.
Your long letters seem too short to me.
I have not yet been able to decide about the trip to Europe.
At present I am waiting for a letter or telegram from Rolland.
Raja is of the view that if I go, I must take you with me. Would you
like to go?
Blessings from
BAPU
