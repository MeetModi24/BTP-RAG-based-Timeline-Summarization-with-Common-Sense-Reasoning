141. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
September 19, 1932
CHI. NANIBEHN JHAVERI,
I ought not to forgive you for keeping me without a letter
for such a long time. But a yajna bears fruit only if one forgives
one's bitterest enemy before commencing it. If, therefore, I did
not forgive my daughters like you, would I not perish utterly?
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I I , p. 51
