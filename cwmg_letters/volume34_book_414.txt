414. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJI
[After September 5, 1927]
There are of course many types of valour in the world. There
should be an Indian type for an Indian Victoria Cross. If a Gango
Teli moves with his bullock in endless circles and crushes oil for
society and does it selflessly, is it not great valour? The devout
Gango became famous obviously because he had courage. Why
did not Ghelo the oilman attain fame?
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
