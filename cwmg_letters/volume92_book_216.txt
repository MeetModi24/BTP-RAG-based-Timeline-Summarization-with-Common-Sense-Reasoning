216. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA R. VYAS
LAHORE,
Silence Day [December 30, 1929]
CHI. KANTA,
I was expecting your letter. I hope you, your mother and
father are well. Now we shall be meeting very soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
