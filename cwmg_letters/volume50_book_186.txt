186. LETTER TO SHANTI
July 8, 1932
DEAR SHANTI,
Countless insects are born during the rains. You should not
collect the snails. Such insects are the creation of God. We do not
know the use of all of them.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 193
