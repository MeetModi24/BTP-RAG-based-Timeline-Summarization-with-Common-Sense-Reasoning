523. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
March 25, 1942
CHI. VANUDI,
One hour you are pleased and the next displeased. This won't
do. If there is somebody to teach the asanas1, etc., they do benefit.
Since nobody has studied the technique scientifically, they have
not been systematically popularized. Learn them patiently.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5788. Also C.W. 3011. Cour-
tesy: Vanamala M. Desai
