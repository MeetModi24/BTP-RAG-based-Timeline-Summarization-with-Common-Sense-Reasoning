162. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
October 26, 1933
CHI. MARY,
A 'tragedy of errors'. The man who brought the first note
said no answer was required. Now Madan Mohan sends a re-
minder. Do please both of you come at 4 p.m.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6014. Also C.W. 3340. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
