353. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
May 7, 1928
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter. I hope you are properly fixed up now.
Anyway you will insist on finding for yourself the comfort your
health may demand.
I have carefully gone through the revised translation. It is
very good.
You should occasionally go to Vallabhbhai's meetings if he
desires.
Love.
BAPU
PS.
Surendra took charge of the tannery yesterday.
SHRI MIRABEHN
SWARAJ ASHRAM
BARDOLI
Via SURAT
From the original: C.W. 5301. Courtesy: Mirabehn
