293. LETTER TO L. ROBERTSON
BOMBAY,
June 9, 1918
L. ROBERTSON, E S Q .
CHIEF SECRETARY
POLITICAL DEPARTMENT
GOVERNMENT OF BOMBAY
DEAR MR. ROBERTSON,
Your telegram No. 4630 was received by me at Nadiad only
yesterday evening on my return from Kaira. Before I undertake
to support the resolution given in your telegram I should like to
know more fully about it. I should like also to see the scheme.
My address is care of Mr. Rewashankar Jagjiwan, Laburnum
Road, Chowpati.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
India Office Judicial and Public Records: 3412/18; also Bombay Govern-
ment Home Department Special File No. 1788 of 1918
