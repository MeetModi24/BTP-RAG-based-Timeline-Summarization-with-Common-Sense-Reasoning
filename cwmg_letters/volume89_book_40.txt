40. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
SODEPUR,
August 13, 1947
CHI. RAJEN BABU,
Kakasaheb is here. He says that the meeting of the Hindus-
tani Prachar Sabha must be held in Delhi irrespective of my
presence or absence there. I think I shall not be there. Still it
would be good to hold the meeting.
Just now I am held up in Calcutta. You will learn from
the newspapers what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
