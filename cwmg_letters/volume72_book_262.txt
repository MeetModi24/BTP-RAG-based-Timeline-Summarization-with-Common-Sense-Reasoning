262. LETTER TO JETHALAL G. SAMPAT
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 25, 1940
CHI. JETHALAL,
I have already talked the matter over with Jajuji, for the
arrival of your postcard coincided with his coming. I will go
through Sarvodaya.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9870. Courtesy: Narayan J. Sampat
