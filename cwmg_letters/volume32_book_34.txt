34. LETTER TO FEROZE
November 15, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter. There is no royal road to the control of passions.
Constant endeavour and constant waiting upon God are the two
things needed.
Yours sincerely,
FEROZE
V. CHAKWANO
P.O. PHALIA
(GUJRAT DISTRICT)
PUNJAB
