497. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
December 5, 1934
I am getting ready for jail. I see that it is difficult to avoid
it any longer. "Make the best of today, for who has seen
tomorrow?"
If you have copies of the Samkshipta Balakanda which I had
brought out in Natal and Gatulalji's Subhashit Lahari, send them
with Andrews. The latter is needed for Gatulalji's verse transla-
tion of the Gita which it contains.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 154
