422. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
December 26, 1933
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Here is a cutting from the M. Mail. Though the whole
conversation practically referred to you and your views, natu-
rally the interviewer could not reproduce it all. I was shown
the proof. It is a fair presentation of the substance of what I
said. Please read it carefully and correct me where you find me
to have erred about yourself. There is a great deal of misunder-
standing about you in our circles too. But it does not worry me.
You will find also the programme so far as it goes.
I hope Mother is progressing.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1933. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
