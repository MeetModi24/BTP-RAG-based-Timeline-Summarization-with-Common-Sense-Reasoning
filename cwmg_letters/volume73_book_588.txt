588. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 4, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your letter.
I have not intentionally avoided answer to any of your ques-
tions. If I have, you will repeat the question or questions. The
fact is I never have your letters before me at the time of replying.
I have to rely upon memory.
I was afraid the letter was overweight. You have done well in
telling me of the neglect.
Yes, the library has undergone the necessary change. I think
you will like it. It has now become a proper room, the coolest in
the Ashram. Madalasa rests there.
Mira can't bear the heat. She is off to Nasik today. Sending
you a copy of Pyarelal's pamphlet.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4013. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7322
