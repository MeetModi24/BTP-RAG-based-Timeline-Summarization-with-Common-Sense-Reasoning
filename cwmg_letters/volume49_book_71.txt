71. LETTER TO NILKANTH B. MASHRUWALA
January 29, 1932
CHI. NILKANTH,
I got your letter. What is Kishorelal's diet? The copy of
Young India has come with me here. Most probably Gandhi
Vichar Dohan also is with me. I cannot revise it from here and
return it. Let the thing, therefore, be printed as it is. Are all
the prisoners kept together? Request Nathji to visit the Ashram
off and on. How is his eczema? How often can you see
Kishorelal? Where is Tara ? How is she?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Who is Lakhu? I don't remember him.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9419. Courtesy: Nilkanth B.
Mashruwala
