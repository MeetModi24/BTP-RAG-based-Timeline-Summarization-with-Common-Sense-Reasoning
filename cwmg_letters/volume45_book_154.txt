154. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 22, 1931
CHI. MANI,
I have your beautiful long letter. Do I have to send back an
equally long reply? My travels are from one end to the other
of the compound. There is no guard or anybody else here to
quarrel with. The sky is the ceiling of my train. If I were to at-
tempt a description of its innumerable stars, I would fail. And the
stars I see, you too see them. So I have nothing to write about. I
too understand that you will remain outside prison for only a
few days. For at present jail is our real home.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- 4: Manibehn Patelne, pp. 78-
