104. LETTER TO NAN MENON
January 17, 1935
MY DEAR NAN,
You have written a very good letter in good steady hand.
You were quite right in wishing me a Happy [New] Year be-
cause you wrote your letter on the 27th December. But there
is no use now in my returning the wish, for the New Year is
well on its way.
You must tell me how you felt when you began your spin-
ning on the new wheel. Do you know how to spin on the takli?
Love and kisses.
BAPU
My Dear Child, p. 121
