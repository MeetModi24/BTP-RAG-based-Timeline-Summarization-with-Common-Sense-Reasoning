100. LETTER TO WANDA DYNOWSKA
POONA,
August 1, 1946
MY DEAR UMADEVI,
The enclosed is a reply to Gandhiji's letter to the Private Secretary to
H. E. the Viceroy.
In Gandhiji's opinion the reply is good and should set your mind at
rest. No exception can be taken to what they are doing.
I hope you are well. I was away for six days and returned day before
yesterday.
Yours with love,
AMRIT KAUR
From a photostat : G. N. 1208
