252. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
October 20, 1934
BA,
Know that it is good that I have not called you to Bom-
bay. There is no point in your going where Perinbehn has been
insulted. This is one of the main reasons. Another is that it is
proper for you to be there as long as Ramdas is [in the hos-
pital]. The third reason is that my heart is no more with the
Congress. I have stopped Jamnalalji too. What shall I call you
here for when it is certain that I am leaving the Congress? Nimu
is keeping fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3081
