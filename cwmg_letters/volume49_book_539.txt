539. LETTER TO KHUSHALCHAND GANDHI
May 16, 1932
RESPECTED BROTHER,
You seem to have demonstrated a miracle. From the letters
which I got, I had given up hope altogether. But the letters which
I get now have raised in me the hope that we shall meet in the
present life.
If you can, kindly write a letter to me, otherwise dictate one.
Mahadev is with us.
Respects to you both.
MOHANDAS
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
