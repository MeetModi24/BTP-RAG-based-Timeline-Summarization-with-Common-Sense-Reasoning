560. LETTER TO PRANLAL D. NANJI
1944
If you have to go to Deolali, you can have exemption from
having to come to Wardha. Coming here is chiefly a matter of
sentiment, whereas going to Deolali may be your duty.
From a facsimile of the Gujarati in Pranlal Devakaran Nanji Abhinandan
Granth, between pp. 14 and 15
