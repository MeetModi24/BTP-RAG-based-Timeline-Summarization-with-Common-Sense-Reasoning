186. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 30, 1928
MY DEAR SHANTIKUMAR,
You must continue to send me all new additional facts and
figures. I enclose herewith the combined balance-sheet sent by
you the other day.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 13125; also C.W. 4786. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarji
