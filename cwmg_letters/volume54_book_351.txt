351. LETTER TO V. R. DIGHE
April 5, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter giving an account of buffalo sac-
rifices in Janjira State. I shall see if it is possible for me to do
anything in the matter.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. V. R. DIGHE
KHADI BHANDAR
DADAR
From a microfilm: S.N. 20836
