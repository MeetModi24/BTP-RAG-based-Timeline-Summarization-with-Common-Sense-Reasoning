335. LETTER TO G. RAMACHANDRAN
June 14, 1937
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRAN,
Nothing from you for a long time. Saraswati said the wea-
ther was hot there. Is it cool enough for Amtul Salaam to come?
She is anxious to go there as soon as she can.
How are you? What about . . .1 How is Saraswati far-
ing?
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6703
