568. LETTER TO FELIX VALYI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 24, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Much as I would like to write for your
monthly review, I am so overworked just now that I have re-
solved not to write for any magazine for some time to come.
If I ever get moments of leisure and I am duly reminded, I
would write something later for you. But it may be all hoping
against hope.
Yours sincerely,
FELIX VALYI, ESQ.
HOTEL RICHMOND
GENEVA (SWITZERLAND)
From a photostat: S.N. 10833
