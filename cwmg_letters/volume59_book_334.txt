334. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
November 8, 1934
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I got your letter. My New Year blessings to both of you and
the children.
You should not be impatient. Everything will be all right.
Do village [reconstruction] work staying where you are. The work
is such that it can be done by everybody anywhere.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5526
