185. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
February 22, 1946
I got your letter. There was nothing improper in what
you said. From a philosophical point of view nobody has any
right. But we were not discussing philosophy. I see nothing
improper in somebody saying to his elder, 'I have a right over
you'. I can understand your having such thoughts at present
because of your illness. Banish them. I hope your fever has
quite gone.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 209
