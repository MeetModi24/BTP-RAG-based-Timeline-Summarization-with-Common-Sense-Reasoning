348. LETTER TO RANCHHODDAS
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 29, 1945
BHAI RANCHHODDAS,
I have gone through your English letter. I do not want to
get involved with the questions raised therein. Had you met
me I would have spent some time in talking to you. How can
I prevent you from sending to the Press the questions you have
raised? What is the significance of those questions without my
replies? Hope you are well.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
