426. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
DELHI,
February 26, 1931
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Vallabhbhai told me that this time you could not accompany
him because of Dilip's illness. Except for satisfying of political
curiosity and facing Delhi's biting cold, I do not think you have
missed much. And if by remaining there, you have been able
to comfort Taramati and solve her difficulty, at least something
will have been accomplished.
Here the negotiations are still in mid-air. The talks have been
warmer and more friendly than I had expected, but it is not
possible to come to any conclusion from them.
It has already been explained to some extent which are the
mills whose products are to be boycotted. But which of the
goods of the mills thus listed should be boycotted is difficult to
say. But if a resolution can be drafted in consultation with
Shankerlal, I shall get it passed.
Blessings from
BAPU
