126. LETTER TO JAYANTI N. PAREKH
November 12, 1935
CHI. JAYANTI,
I got your letter.
Swaraj is definitely connected with politics. But many an
activity can have its roots in swaraj, and not in politics. For
example, I would definitely regard it your contribution, how-
ever small, to the winning of swaraj if you all three brothers
engage yourselves in business—at present not for your own benefit
but to help your father and to payoff his debts. Please remember
that our means of winning swaraj are non-violent and doing one's
dharma is the most important of non-violent means.
May this year see your noble aspirations bear good fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
