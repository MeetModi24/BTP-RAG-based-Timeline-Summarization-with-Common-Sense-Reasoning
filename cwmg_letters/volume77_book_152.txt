152. LETTER TO SAROLA
PALM BAN, JUHU,
June 2, 1944
MY DEAR SAROLA,
I did have your wire. Pyarelal acknowledged it, too. You
must have got it by this time. I do not know what is going to
happen to me on recovery. I cannot guide you from a sick-bed.
As yet I am allowed only to tackle light correspondence. All I
can say is that you should do such national service as comes your
way. How is Dipak?
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
