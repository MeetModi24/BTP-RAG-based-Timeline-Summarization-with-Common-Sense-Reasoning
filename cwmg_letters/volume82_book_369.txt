369. LETTER TO ANNASAHEB SAHASRABUDDHE
SODEPUR,
December 23, 1945
BHAI SAHASRABUDDHE,
I have your letter. I have gone through everything. I feel
that at the moment nothing needs to be done in this connection.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SAHASRABUDDHE
KHADI KARYALAYA
MAHAL, NAGPUR
