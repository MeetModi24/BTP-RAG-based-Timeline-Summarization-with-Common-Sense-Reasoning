352. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
October 8, 1932
CHI. RAMA (JOSHI)1,
Have you requested me not to take the trouble of writing to
you, so that you also may not have to write to me? But how
can I, when I am well enough and eat and work as usual, and
get time to write, refrain from writing? I don't mind Vimu's
having had a trip to Yeravda, but has she started working as a
scavenger-girl? The brave Dhiru seems to have become a little
wiser.
Has his health improved?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5336
