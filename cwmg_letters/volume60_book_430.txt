430. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
March 20, 1935
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
Why should doctors get ill? And how are you going to dis-
charge the tremendous burden you have undertaken if you will
develop a weak body? I hope you are now completely restored.
We cannot be party to prosecution against Harijans who
indulge in marriages in breach of the Sarda Act. It is a reform
which Harijans must tackle for themselves. We have no right to
undertake prosecution until savarna Hindus have proved them-
selves above reproach in this matter and until there is no distinc-
tion between savarnas and avarnas.
What is this complaint about a separate silk store being con-
ducted under your patronage and supervision and by a man dis-
charged by the A.I . S. A.? There is a long complaint made by
three writers from Sialkot.
Yours sincerely,
DR. GOPICHAND
LAHORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
