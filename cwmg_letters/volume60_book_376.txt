376. LETTER TO O. V. R. SESHAN
WARDHA,
March 8, 1935
MY DEAR SESHAN,
Better late than never. Your letter of 11th ultimo has lain
with me all these many days. It is quite true that I do not
appreciate the idea of raising funds by means of dramas. Those
who stage plays should do so because it is a good thing in itself.
SJT. O. V. R. SESHAN
24 BUNGALOW
TATAPURAM (COCHIN)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
