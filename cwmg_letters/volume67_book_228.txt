228. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
SEGAON,
July 14, 1938
CHI. PREMA,
You have not asked for a reply to your letter, but I felt
like writing at least a postcard. Though I do not write to you, I
naturally think of you on innumerable occasions. May you go on
making steady progress. The rest from Harijan, and from Mahadev.
Blessings from
BAPU
