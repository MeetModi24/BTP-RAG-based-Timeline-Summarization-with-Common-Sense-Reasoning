213. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
July 16, 1932
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I got your letter. I had asked you to write to me anything
about yourself if you wished to do so. If I can share from here
your joys and sorrows, I should like to do so. I cannot write
anything regarding the Conference. Respectful greetings from
us all to Grandmother. Does anyone stay with Gokibehn? I
wrote to her some days ago. My blessings to Sumati.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4797. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
