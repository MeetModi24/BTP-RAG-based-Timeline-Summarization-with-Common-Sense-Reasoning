244. LETTER TO BAL D. KALELKAR
August 1, 1936
CHI. BAL,
From my experiments in cooking I have come to the fol-
lowing conclusions: By cooking for oneself one saves money, im-
proves health, becomes more enthusiastic and improves general
knowledge. One also automatically
gains knowledge of the
science of nutrition and becomes more efficient in serving food
to others.
