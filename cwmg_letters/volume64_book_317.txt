317. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 25, 1937
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
You did not write to me probably because I was touring.
We returned yesterday. Write and tell me how you are. Come
if you wish. Don't feel hesitant. I got a letter from Madam
Wadia. She has acknowledged the letter. Prabha is with me.
Saraswati is fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 373
