377. LETTER TO V. G. CHERTKOV
SABARMATI,
April 26, 1925
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your greetings of 28th February, 1924. I was
then unable to write to you. I hope you will keep me acquainted
with Tolstoyan activities in Russia and condition of things in
general.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 11077. Courtesy: Tolstoy State Museum,
Moscow
