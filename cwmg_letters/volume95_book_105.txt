105. LETTER TO JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
YERAVDA,
October 30, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your warm letter of 9th Sept last. I may not say anything
beyond thanking you for the letter.
Yours sincerely,
M.K. GANDHI
REV. J.H. HOLMES
From a photostat of the original : C.W. 113520. Courtesy : Robert D.
Berman, New York
