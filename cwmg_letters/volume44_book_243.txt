243. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
September 26, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (DESAI),
I got your letter. I wrote to you about Pyarelal in my last
letter. I have not yet been able to see him, but I can get news
about him now. I shall of course be able to see him. Whether he
will be kept with me rests on God's will. In any case, we shall meet
when I am released and he will live with me then. But who knows
what the future holds? Kakasaheb will be released at the end
of November. I suppose Pyarelal's term will also have expired
by then. At last, he now finds support in the Gita and the Rama-
yana and I feel greatly relieved. I could not understand why they
did not help him till now. You yourself admit that you can be
safe only if you write to me. Well, then, give me full details when-
ever you write.
I did not ask for my old pair of sandals. There was a new
pair about which you seem to have forgotten. But at present I
carry on with the one I have.
Blessings from
BAPU
