429. LETTER TO VALLATHOL NARAYANA MENON
SEVAGRAM,
November 18, 1944
DEAR VALLATHOL 1 ,
May Mallika and her husband live long in harmony and
serve the country to the best of their ability.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI VALLATHOL
CHERUTHURUTHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
