284. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
August 21, 1936
CHI. SHARMA,
. . .1 G i v e me the full title of Babbitt's book. You may not
mind the loss of the book but I cannot help feeling upset till
it is found. How can a book disappear like this? And the same
goes for the khadi. However, I do not feel as bad about it as
about the loss of Babbitt. I still hope I shall find it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 260
