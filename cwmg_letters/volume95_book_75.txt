75. LETTER TO E. STANLEY JONES
NIPANI,
March 30, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
My answer to your letter is 'yes'. It means the discovery of
God's laws.
God has laid me low for the moment. But He is infinitely
merciful.
My apologies to Mrs. Jones. I have not forgotten her letter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C. W. 11343. Courtesy: Mrs. Eunice Jones Mathews
