384. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Tuesday
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I am a little worried because you were to come the next day.
And it is now four days. I hope you are not ill. If you are, do let me
know. It is needless to write if you are all right. Of course, I know
you will come as soon as you are free.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S. N. 32775
