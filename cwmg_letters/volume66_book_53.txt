53. LETTER TO E. K. PALIA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 13, 1937
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Not knowing anything of transactions
relating to land, I cannot be interested in your scheme.
Yours sincerely,
M. K . GANDHI
SHRI E . K . PALIA
6/ CUBBON ROAD
BANGALORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
