51. LETTER TO GOPABANDHU DAS
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 24, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I had correspondence with Lalaji about
this. He too told that at the present moment he had sufficient
funds at his disposal. I think that the balance of the last famine
collection can be safely used for charkha work.
I am thinking of sending you an expert adviser of the type
you want. It may take a little time to find out the proper man.
I hope you are yourself keeping good health. I am getting
stronger day by day. Govindji has not written to me since the
Cawnpore Congress. I do not know therefore at the present
moment even where he is.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. GOPABANDHU DAS
PURI
