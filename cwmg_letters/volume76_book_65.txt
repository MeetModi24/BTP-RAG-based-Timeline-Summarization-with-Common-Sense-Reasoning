65. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
April 18, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Even now I am clear about rice. But if the women insist
on it, they should be given it. What about the men? They
should be consulted. In such things there is much room for
judiciousness and generosity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4422
