106. LETTER TO G. J. ADAM
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 5, 1906
DEAR MR. ADAM,
I enclose herewith for your information the circular letter addressed
by some of the Members of the House of Commons.
Perhaps you will be so kind as to furnish the Press with information.
I remain,
[Enclosure]
Yours faithfully,
G. J. ADAM, ESQ.
24, OLD JEWRY
LONDON, E.C.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4490
