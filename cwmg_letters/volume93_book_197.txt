197. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL VIDYARTHI
SEVAGRAM,
August 9, 1937
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
I have your letter. Authoritative opinion of course can
always be had from the Congress Office at Allahabad. My
personal opinion is that Committees should not be formed till
there is provision in the Constitution for use of khadi. But
my personal opinion has no weight.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 11684
