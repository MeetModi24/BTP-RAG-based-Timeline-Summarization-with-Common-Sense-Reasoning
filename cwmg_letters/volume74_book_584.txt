584. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 28, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your little note. I have answered all your three questions.
You should join the Women's Tagore Memorial Committee and
Education Board. For Hancock let it be 4 p.m. on 11 th October.
I did not give the exact date. I am glad you are definitely on
the mend.
The apples have come. Have not tasted them.
Let it be
once a month.
You will be pleased with this performance.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4086. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7395
