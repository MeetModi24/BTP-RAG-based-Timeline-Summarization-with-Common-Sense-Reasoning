323. LETTER TO TEVANI AND DEBORAH
NEW DELHI,
October 12, 1947
DEAR TEVANI AND DEBORAH,
I have your letter. At the end of your letter you tell me
that the Deputy Commissioner has promised to help you. May
your wish be fulfilled. I think I told you when you were
Delhi that the whole thing would depend upon the manager
there.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
