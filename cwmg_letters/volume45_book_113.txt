113. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 12, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I am writing this letter though I have nothing special to write
about. Probably you will get this letter at the Ashram. You must
have received my previous postcards. I keep very good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3410
