432. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
Silence Day [May 26, 1930]
DEAR NIMU,
I hear from Ba that both you and your mother have returned
to the Ashram. It was good. But what about your complaint about
constipation? Are you not brave? How is Savitri ? Is she called
by this name or any other?
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 576
