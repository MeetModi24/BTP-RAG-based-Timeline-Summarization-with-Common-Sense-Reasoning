546. LETTER TO PYARELAL
December 3, 1946
CHI. . . .
I have written about this matter to Suhrawardy. Read it when you
come here. Write to Sir Datar Singh that I have written to the Premier.
I shall inform him of the developments.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
