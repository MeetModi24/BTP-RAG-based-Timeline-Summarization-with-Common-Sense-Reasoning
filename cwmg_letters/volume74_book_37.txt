37. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 3, 1941
MY DEAR AMRITLAL,
I was distressed to see your pompous statement to the Associa-
ted Press. I told you to work silently and under Satis Babu's
guidance.
You have taken no mission or authority from me.
You know that you have been sent for a domestic reason. You
have therefore told a deliberate lie. Unless you somehow correct
yourself, I shall have to repudiate you entirely. I am sorry. I had
expected better things from you.
Here is a letter from Sailen.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10301. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
