756. LETTER TO GORDHANBHAI L. BHAKTA
PATNA,
March 1[4]1, 1934
BHAI GORDHANBHAI,
I have your letter. You have given much information. Let
me know if you come to know more about Durbari. You should
stick to the Ashram for the time being. God will clear the way
for you when the moment comes. It is enough that you keep
yourself ready.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 10993. Courtesy: Gordhanbhai
L. Bhakta
