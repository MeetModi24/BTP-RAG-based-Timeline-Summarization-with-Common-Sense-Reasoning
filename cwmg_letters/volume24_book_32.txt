32. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
Thursday [May 15, 1924]
BHAISHRI DEVCHANDBHAI,
I have your letter.
I have done my duty. Now let things take their own course.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
DEVCHANDBHAI PAREKH
TAKHTESHWAR PLOT
BHAVNAGAR
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 6006. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
