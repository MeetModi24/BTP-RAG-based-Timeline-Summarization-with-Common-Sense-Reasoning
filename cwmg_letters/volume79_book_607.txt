607. LETTER TO HARIBHAU JOSHI
BOMBAY,
April 7, 1945
BHAI JOSHI,
I have your letter. What can my blessings do, especially
when I am somewhat against it. Even so I wish you success.
Write to me and to others in Hindustani.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
HARIBHAU JOSHI
LOKSHAKTI
POONA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
