544. LETTER TO VIMAL KISHORE MEHROTRA
January 8, 1933
CHI. VIMAL 1 ,
Here is some coloured paper for you. I got your letter, it is
good. Children like you do not need any rice. But you are used
to it and if you cannot give it up you may certainly eat it. But
eat only a little quantity.
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 3423. Courtesy: Parashuram Mehrotra. Also G.N. 6101
