277. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
BARDOLI,
January 5, 1942
CHI. MARY,
You can come at any time. You can never be a burden.
Your friends too may come if they can be cooped up in odd cor-
ners. Do just what you like. There is no depression in me.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6083. Also C.W. 3413. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
