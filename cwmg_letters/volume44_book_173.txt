173. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 6, 1930
CHI. SHARDA (BABU),
I do receive all your letters. It is true that I do not write
to you every week. I realize that what I have been writing
about non-stealing and the other vows is rather complicated and
it does not interest you. It is not the language that is difficult,
but the ideas are difficult to grasp and they are stated tersely.
That is the way of explaining a lot in a few words. Now, if you
understood those things from Chimanlal or Premabehn and read
them again, you would get interested. You can definitely under-
stand it if you have the desire. I hope you are not having the
asthma trouble now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9889. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
