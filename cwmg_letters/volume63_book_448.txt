448. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
October 5, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
You should not expect any letter from me for some time
now. Mira and Nanavati have been seriously ill. I have to spend
the whole day in nursing them. Jamna too should not expect
a letter from me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8508. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
