150. LETTER TO G. SITARAMA SASTRY
January 29, 1935
MY DEAR SITARAMA SASTRY,
I had your three letters, two of which do not call for any answer.
Have you sent Kumarappa your form duly filled in? If the
experiment that is being made in the use of groundnut oil for
lighting purposes is successful, it will have far-reaching conse-
quences. I hope that whoever is carrying on the experiment will
persevere in spite of failures. I am sorry that you could not collect
much funds. But the absence of response ought not to cause any
worry to you. You should simply cut your coat according to the cloth.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI G. SITARAMA SASTRY
VINAYA ASHRAM
CHANDOLE P. O., GUNTUR DT.
