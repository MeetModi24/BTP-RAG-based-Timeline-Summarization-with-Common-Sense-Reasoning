326. LETTER TO RAMNIKLAL MODI
DELHI,
Silence Day, December 23, 1929
CHI. RAMNIKLAL,
I have your letter.
Mahadev is here. Vallabhbhai, too, is here.
You were right in sending Henry's wire by post. In such
matters, you may decide for yourself and do what you think
best. No one rule can be laid down which can be followed in
all cases. Our aim should always be to save money.
Your reply to Manilal Kothari was correct.
Budhabhai is our neighbour. I intervened in his affair. If,
now, we do not accept the deposit, our service will remain in-
complete. The burden on us will not be much. If Budhabhai
and Nanibehn are innocent, we should keep the money. If they
are guilty, we cannot but know that by this means. In this
complex world, who can say which man or woman is pure and
which impure? To me it seems our clear duty to accept the
deposit and to maintain relations with both. It is not we who
have to provide a room to Nanibehn. She is to be given a room
in Budhabhai's house. If she comes and lives in the Ashram,
she will of course live as the other women do. More about this
when we meet.
If the Thana land can be disposed of for Rs. 9,000, nothing
will be better than that.
Pyarali separated from us from
Wardha. Write to him at his Bombay address.
We have to meet the Viceroy at 4.30.
Blessings from
BAPU
