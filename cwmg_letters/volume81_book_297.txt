297. LETTER TO GORDHANDAS CHOKHAWALA
August 28/29, 1945
CHI. GORDHANDAS,
I have your letter. It made me very happy. Now Babudi
has to be very careful about her diet. She will gain strength
speedily if she does not make a mistake. Since the poison has
passed out of the body all the other diseases should go. Shakari-
behn's joy will be limitless.
Blessings from
BAPU
GORDHANDAS CHOKHAWALA
SURAT
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
