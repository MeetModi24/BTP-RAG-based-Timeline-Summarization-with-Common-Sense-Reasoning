293. LETTER TO GOVINDDAS
BENARES,
[August] 1 2, 1934
BHAI GOVINDDASJI,
I have gone through your letter. My article or statement did
not at all imply that Congress Committees should not or cannot
be formed in the Indian States. The Committees already existing
such as the Baghelkhand District Congress Committee about which
you wrote to me should certainly continue to exist. It will, of
course, be a matter of extreme regret if such committees are dis-
allowed in any State.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[From Hindi]
Madhya Pradesh aur Gandhiji, p. 103
