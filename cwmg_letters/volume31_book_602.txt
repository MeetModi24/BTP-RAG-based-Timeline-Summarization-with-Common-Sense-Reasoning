602. LETTER TO NATHUBHAI NEMICHAND PAREKH
SABARMATI,
[November 4, 1926]
BHAI NATHUBHAI,
I have been receiving your loving letters. If I do not reply,
do not feel that I am indifferent to your letters. I have been
patiently discussing the topic in Navajivan. I welcome opposition
from simple-hearted men like you. Certainly it is not my aim
that the dogs should be killed at once. My article seemingly
expresses a new idea and therefore readers take time to under-
stand it, but ultimately people like you will surely follow it or
I will come to see my mistake.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI NATHUBHAI NEMICHAND PAREKH
CALICUT, MALABAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6248
