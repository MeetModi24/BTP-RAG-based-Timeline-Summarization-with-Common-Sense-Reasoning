242. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
July 20, 1934
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your two letters.
Have no anxiety about my fast.
God will see it safely through.
This body can come to no harm as long as God wants it to
render even the least service.
You should tell your parents if it is utterly impossible for you
to live with your husband. You must listen calmly to whatever they
say and bear it.
If you can, you may certainly go to Kumba-
konam and live with your husband. If the heart does not accept
something, there is no gain but only harm in forcing it, the
heart being the means to the deliverance of the soul from bondage.
Gita declares thus and this is also the experience of all. Do write
to me about your activities.
May God give you peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
