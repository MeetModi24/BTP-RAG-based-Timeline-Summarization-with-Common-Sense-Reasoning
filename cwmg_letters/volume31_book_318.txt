318. LETTER TO NAJUKLAL N. CHOKSI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Thursday, Sravana Shuklapaksha 4, August 12, 1926
BHAI NAJUKLAL,
You must have now returned to Bombay. Velanbehn is
yearning to see Moti. If possible, please send her for a few
days. If, however, that is not possible, please do not at all hesi-
tate to say so.
I hope you are in good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12134
