76. LETTER TO SYED ROSS MASOOD
CAMP ASAURA,
October 29, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. It will certainly give me pleasure
to renew my acquaintance with the students of the college and
the professors. I shall gladly address them at the time mentioned
by you. As you have conferred with the Reception Committee I
take it that there will be no conflict with the other appointments
the Committee might have made.
Yours sincerely,
SYED Ross MASOOD, ESQ.
VICE-CHANCELLOR
MUSLIM UNIVERSITY
ALIGARH
