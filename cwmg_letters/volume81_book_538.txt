538. LETTER TO ANAND G. CHOKHAWALA
October 2, 1945
CHI. ANAND1,
It is good that you wrote.
You are doing well in joining the Prabhatpheri . Don't be
too mischievous. You don't harass Sharda, do you? Now you
should help her.
Blessings from
SARDAR AND BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C.W.
10088. Courtesy: Sharda G.
Chokhawala
