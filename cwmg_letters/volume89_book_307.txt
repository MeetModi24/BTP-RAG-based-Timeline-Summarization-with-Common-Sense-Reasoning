307. LETTER TO M. A. HUNAR
DELHI,
October 7, 1947
CHI. HUNAR,
Why should your salary be paid from here? It should be
drawn from Patna because isn't the work done there meant for
Patna? Probably there will never be an occasion for us to stay
together.
I don't know what to do.
Blessings from
BAPU
