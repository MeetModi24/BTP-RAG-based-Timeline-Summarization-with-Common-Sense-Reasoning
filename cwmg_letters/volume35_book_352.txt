352. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
Sunday [On or before January 15, 1928]
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I have your letter. I shall come there on the 24th or 25th.
But I understand that they will take me directly to Vartej.
Aren't you coming to Porbandar? Isn't there any remedy to put
your health right. Mine is all right. One cannot rely upon
newspapers even about this.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5905
