378. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
July 30, 1933
MY DEAR ANAND,
Just a line to acknowledge your letter. I have come very
near Vidya. She knows her course of action but has not enough
courage to take it cheerfully. But she is struggling most bravely
and I think successfully. She sits by me all the time I am at the
Ashram. But she is not likely to see me tomorrow. And she will
probably leave for Wardha tomorrow night. You need not worry
about her.
I do not know what can be sent you next week. If it is at
all possible, I shall certainly send something.
Love.
BAPU
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
