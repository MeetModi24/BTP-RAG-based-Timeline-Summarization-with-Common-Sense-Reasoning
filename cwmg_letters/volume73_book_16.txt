16. LETTER TO A SIND WORKER
[Before September 21, 1940]
Do not think I am unconcerned about Sind. But I feel help-
less. The people have not the capacity to adopt my remedy, and
I have no other. They must, therefore, go to another physician.
The Bombay Chronicle, 23-9-1940
1 Vide pp. 18-.
2 The letter was reported under the date-line "Sukkur, September 21".
