114. LETTER TO RANCHHODDAS
URULI KANCHAN, DIST. POONA,
August 2, 1946
BHAI RANCHHODDAS,
I have your letter and cheque for Rs. 100-8-. I have
passed on the cheque to the driver through the General Manager.
Indeed, who can touch him whom God protects?
BAPU
COCHIN (MALABAR)
