762. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
April 2, 1934
CHI. MRIDU,
I had told you I would send the definition of swadeshi. Here
it is. You may offer your comments on it if you want. I hope
you are keeping fit. How was Mummy's health? She must have
gone. Write to me occasionally. There is a heart-searching
going on within me at the moment. You will see the result.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Show the definition to Shankerlal if he is there. Thinking
he may not be there, I am not sending it to him.
From the Gujarati original; C. W. 11188.
Courtesy; Sarabhai
Foundation
