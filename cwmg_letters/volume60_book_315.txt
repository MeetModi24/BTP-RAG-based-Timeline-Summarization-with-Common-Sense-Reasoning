315. LETTER TO SURESH SINGH
February 16, 1935
BHAI SURESH SINGH,
I have your letter. Whatever is possible should be done.
Who is the custodian appointed by the Court of Wards? You
should adopt all possible simplicity in your life. Keep writing
to me. Serve villagers to the extent you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8689
