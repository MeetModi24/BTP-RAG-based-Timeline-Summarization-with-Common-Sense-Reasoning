386. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 1, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. You should hope to remain alive for
service and also be prepared to die. Herewith a letter to
Harilal. I do wish to send Manu [to you] but [cannot do so]
because of ignorant attachment. I am sending home-made anti-
phlogistine.
I have already told Harilal. He will leave on the 3rd.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 571; also Bapuke Patra-8: Bibi
Amtussalaamke Nam, p. 266
