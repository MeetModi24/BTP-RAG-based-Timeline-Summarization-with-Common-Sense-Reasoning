654. LETTER TO TARABEHN MASHRUWALA
[February 1, 1941]
CHI. TARI,
I was very happy to see your letter. Continue to write like
that. Get well soon. Manu has not completely recovered but
she is improving. Right now at 4.30 p. m. she has no fever
at all.
Blessings from
BAPU
