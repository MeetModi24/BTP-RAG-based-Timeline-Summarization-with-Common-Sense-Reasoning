280. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
October 4, 1930
BHAI RAMESHWARDAS (DHULIA),
Your letter. Do not take it so much to heart. Recite
Ramanama and be cheerful. Bear whatever pain there is after
taking the necessary treatment and do whatever service you can.
No reason to worry if you cannot get up [early] in the morning.
Blessings from
BAPU
