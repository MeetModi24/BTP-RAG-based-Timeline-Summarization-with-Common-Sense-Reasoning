423. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
July 29, 1935
BHAI MUNSHI,
Your readiness to accept my suggestion regarding advertise-
ments embarrasses me because it increases my burden and
makes me afraid lest I should exploit your habit of accepting my
views and in consequence lose a co-worker like you. I have no
doubt, however, that such self-restraint will definitely enhance the
prestige of the paper.
I do admit that Bhulabhai's opinion is too technical.
I understand about the advisory board.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 9580. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
