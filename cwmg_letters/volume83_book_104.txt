104. LETTER TO SANKARAN
SEVAGRAM,
February 8, 1946
CHI. SANKARAN,
I got your letter. I reached the Ashram on the 6th morn-
ing. The issue of "Jivansakha" has not yet been found. Why
are your envelopes marked 'Confidential'? Such letters are handed
over to me unopened, and I can see them only when I find time.
For instance, though your letter had reached us in Madras, I
opened it only yesterday. There was no scope for it to be opened
during the journey. You do not write to Sushilabehn at all.
Why is it so?
Blessings from
BAPU
