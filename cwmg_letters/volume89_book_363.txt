363. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
October 20, 1947
CHI. SHYAMLAL,
I read your letter a second time today; it is good that you
wrote it. I am sending this to Sushila Pai; she will deliver it to
you wherever you may be.
I do not so far know what arrangements have been made
for her lodging. It will be enough if she can have some peace
at last.
Pakvasa is a man of very simple habits.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
