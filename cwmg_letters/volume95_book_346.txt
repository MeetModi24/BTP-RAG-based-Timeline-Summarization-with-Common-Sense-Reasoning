346. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Tuesday
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have received your letter. God will take care of Ramdas.
You may come whenever you feel like. We shall look after
Shanta here. It is good that Kusum would also accompany her.
Do you write to Devdas?
My health is fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9379.
