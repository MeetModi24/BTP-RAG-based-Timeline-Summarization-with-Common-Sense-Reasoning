145. LETTER TO VERRIER ELWIN
May 7, 1933
MY DEAR VERRIER,
I cannot enter upon the ordeal without talking to you. It is
a matter of great joy to me that I have the prayers of many
many true friends to speed me on-Truth is God and He will
give me all the food I shall need during the fast. I wish I had
time to talk more to you.
I must not write separately to Mary.
I hope you are all well.
Love to you all.
BAPU
