266. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
BARDOLI,
December 31, 1941
CHI. ANAND,
Jairamdas informs me that Vidya had fallen ill. God is test-
ing you. You have to pass the test. Do not worry about the deaf-
ness, either. Everything depends on God. Do not labour beyond
your capacity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
