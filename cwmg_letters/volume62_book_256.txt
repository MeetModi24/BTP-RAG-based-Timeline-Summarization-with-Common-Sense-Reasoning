256. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
February 28, 1936
CHI. AMTUSSALAAM,
I did not write to you as you had told me not to. I have not
still started dictating my letters. We are going to Savli today,
and I hope to reach there [Delhi] on the 8th. I expect to learn
more about this at Savli. I hope to see you much improved in
health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 331
