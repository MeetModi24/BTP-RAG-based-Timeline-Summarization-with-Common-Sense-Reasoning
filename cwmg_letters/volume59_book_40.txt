40. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
September 20, 1934
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
Please read the accompanying postcard. Do you think
there is any truth in it?
I hope the Kumar Saheb has recovered.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5937. Also C.W. 3255. Courtesy:
Mahesh P. Pattani
