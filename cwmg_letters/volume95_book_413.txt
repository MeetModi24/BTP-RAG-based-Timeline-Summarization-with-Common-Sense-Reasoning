413. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI H. SHAH
CHI. PUNJABHAI,
I have your letter. You will have regained your health. I was
relieved to know that you would come over as soon as you recovered.
You must listen carefully to what I say and then do as seems right to
you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : G.N. 4018
