26. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
October 15, 1941
DEAR AMBUJAM 1 ,
I have just heard from Jamnalalji that he has been able to sell
the jewellery at over Rs. 9,000. He considers this a good price.
The letter is misplaced. If I can trace it, I shall let you know
the exact amount. You have said something in your letter. Please
let me have your concrete suggestion, if you have any.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
