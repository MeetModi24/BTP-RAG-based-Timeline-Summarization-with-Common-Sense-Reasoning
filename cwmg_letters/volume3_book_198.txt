198. LETTER TO ABDULKADAR
RAJKOT,
May 7, 1902
DEAR MR. ABDULKADAR 1 ,
I enclose herewith a Gujarati letter addressed to you as well
as Messrs Rustomji and Miankhan. I do hope you will have
the letter properly read and understand it. I need not add any
further to it. You have not acknowledged any of my letters. I
shall thank you to let me have draft for the balance of my bill
which I need badly.
Yours truly,
From a photostat of the office copy : S.N. 3964
