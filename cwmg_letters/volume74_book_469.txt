469. LETTER TO JAINARAYAN VYAS
September 2, 1941
BHAI JAINARAYAN,
I have your letter. We should not make it a rule that a policy
adopted in respect of British India should be [applied] also in the
States. My personal opinion remains that those who wish to follow
the policy of non-violence should keep clear of the various current
entanglements.
Blessings from
BAPU
JAINARAYAN VYAS
BRAHMAPURI
JODHPUR, MEWAR
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
