235. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM,
May 10, 1920
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I shall start from here tomorrow night by the Gujarat Mail
and reach there on Wednesday the 12th in the morning. I in-
tend to leave for Ahmedabad the same day. Reserve a seat in
the Mail. Inform Ahmed Haji Khatri about my coming.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
93 BAZAR GATE
BOMBAY (FORT)
