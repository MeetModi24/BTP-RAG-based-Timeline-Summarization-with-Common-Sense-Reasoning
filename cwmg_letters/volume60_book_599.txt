599. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
WARDHA,
April 18, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
Please convey my condolences to Sardar Devraj's people. Yes,
I had the privilege of meeting him. He was himself an institution.
I return not on 27th but on 25th at the latest.
I do hope Shummy will get over his flu in good time. The
slivers went to you yesterday.
Love to you both.
BAPU
SHRI RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
JULLUNDUR CITY
PUNJAB
From the original: C.W. 3713. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6869
