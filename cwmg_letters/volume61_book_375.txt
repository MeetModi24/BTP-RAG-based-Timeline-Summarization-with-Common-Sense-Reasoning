375. LETTER TO KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
July 16, 1935
CHI. KAMALNAYAN,
I heard from your father that . . .2 now does not want to marry
you. She was, therefore, released from the engagement. That is
the only proper course for us. I hope you are undisturbed. Rest
assured that your stars are fortunate, and that you will get a wife
best suited for you. At present, you should devote yourself exclu-
sively to your studies and to building your character. You have
still not written to me. Improve your English. Take interest in
your studies, and train your body to bear strain. Do not get
tired of physical work, much less feel ashamed of it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 3050
