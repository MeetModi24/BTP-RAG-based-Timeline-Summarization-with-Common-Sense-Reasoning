199. LETTER TO DHIRU
July 9, 1932
DEAR DHIRU,
I do not consider your letter to be clear. You say that there
is no difficulty in spinning yarn of 15 counts, and when you reach
30 counts, it is done up. But there is a great difference between
yarn of 15 and 30 counts. Do you say that there is no difficulty in
spinning yarn up to 30 counts? If you say that, what is the speed
for spinning yarn of 30 counts? What is the waste in spinning
a tola of yarn ? What is the speed for spinning yarn of 40 counts?
What is the waste after it? What is the reason that there is
difficulty in spinning yarn of finer counts? Prabhudas has much
faith in you. If you want to fulfil his hopes, you should be en-
grossed in the spinning-wheel and find out its faults if there be any,
and should find out its measure of strength. By spinning on one
spindle only, perhaps, yarn of finer quality might be obtained.
Also find out whether you can obtain as much yarn upon a
spindle by the Magan spinning-wheel, as you would get on a
spindle by an ordinary spinning-wheel.
Many Muslims believe in rebirth. There is a great diffe-
rence between one who tells a falsehood once and one who has
formed a habit of telling lies.
BAPU
