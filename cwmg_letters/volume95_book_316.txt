316. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
[CHI.] KAKA,
Although the pressure of time is perhaps equally the same to
us, I want to pass over from me to you. My opinion is that the matter
of Talimi Sangh should be printed in Harijanbandhu. I am neutral
regarding the changing [of the script] to Nagari. But to me it is not
that that should not be done. Therefore, Mahadev only remains. Both
of you discuss it. Since you are there, go there today itself for half
an hour, have it discussed. If you have any hesitation write to me so
that I shall have the talk.
5th July will do. Send a wire. Cannot we get a rosary of 108
beads in Wardha? I want four. Please inquire.
Blessings from
BAPU
