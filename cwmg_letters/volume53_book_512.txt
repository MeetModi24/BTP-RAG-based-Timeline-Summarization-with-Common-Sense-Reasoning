512. LETTER TO ESTHER MENON
February 23, 1933
MY DEAR CHILD,
Having dictated this letter1, it has got to go to Tangai,
though it is going to cost 2½ annas to the Harijan cause. Let
us hope that both Nan and Tangai will make up for the loss a
thousandfold and more.
Love.
BAPU
My Dear Child, p. 119
