165. LETTER TO UMADEVI BAJAJ
November 27, 1935
CHI. OM,
I got your letter. I find it intolerable that teachers in
your school cannot speak with their pupils in any language other
than English. You should report this matter to the management
politely. Why do they behave in this manner? Your letter is fairly
good. You do not take much time to get used to such things.
Learn whatever good you find there, but try to keep away from
whatever is undesirable.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 342
