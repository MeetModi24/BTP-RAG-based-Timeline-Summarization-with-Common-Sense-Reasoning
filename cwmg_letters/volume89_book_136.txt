136. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
CALCUTTA,
August 29, 1947
CRI. PREMI,
Your letter. God is my guide. But the same can be said
for all.
I don't know where I shall be tomorrow. The work here
is tough. Who can say what will happen ultimately?
Tell Father that I have replied to his telegram.
Do you regularly help him?
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
