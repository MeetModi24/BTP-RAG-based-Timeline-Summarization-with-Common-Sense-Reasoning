324. LETTER TO T. SUNDARAM
February 5, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Please do not ask me to read and correct
what you have written. It is really beyond me, and from what
little I have read, my advice to you is to abstain from writing any-
thing at all about temple-entry or anything else to the papers.
You have no restraint over your pen.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. T. SUNDARAM
TYPIST
TEPPAKULAM P. O.
TRICHINOPOLY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19304
