111. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
March 17, 1933
MY DEAR AGATHA,
I have your letter. I thought that I had sent you a parcel
containing 10 copies of the Harijan. Anyway, copies are being
sent to all those friends who were helping during my visit, but if
you want more copies, you should tell me.
Love.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
MISS AGATHA HARRISON
119 COWER STREET, W.C. 1
From a photostat: G.N. 1463
