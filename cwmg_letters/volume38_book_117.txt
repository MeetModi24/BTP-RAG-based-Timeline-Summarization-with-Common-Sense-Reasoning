117. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Silence Day [November 26, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
Do you expect a letter from me by every post? What a girl!
Well, I shall try to write.
All the news about me is in the letter to Ashram women
and to the [Udyoga] Mandir. So, I will not write more today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3324
