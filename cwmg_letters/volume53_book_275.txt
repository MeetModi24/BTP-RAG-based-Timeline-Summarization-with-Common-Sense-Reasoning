275. LETTER TO S. D. SATAVLEKAR
January 30, 1933
BHAI SATAVLEKAR,
I am sending for your perusal a book by the late Rajendralal
Mitra. Return it to me after reading. I shall then read it along
with your comments. One thing needs consideration. There
could hardly be any doubt that the meanings he has inferred are
the same as the meanings inferred by Hindus who studied Vedas
and performed cow-sacrifices on their authority. If this is a fact,
is there a historical or some other reason for it?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI PANDIT SATAVLEKAR
SWADHYAYA MANDAL
AUNDH
DIST. SATARA
From Hindi: C.W. 4770. Courtesy: S. D. Satavlekar
