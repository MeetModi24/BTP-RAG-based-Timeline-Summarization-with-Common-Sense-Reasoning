223. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
[July 23, 1924]
BHAI FULCHAND,
I have your letter. There is only one way for you. The
management are pledged to take the untouchables into that school.
That pledge cannot be broken.
You must welcome the
untouchables, and if, in consequence, all the students leave the
school you should put up with it. If the management hand over
the building to you and want to establish another school, they may
do so. How can the principles proclaimed when the foundation
was laid be now set aside? I shall certainly write a note on this
in Navajivan.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Do not give up your peace, patience and humility.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2821. Courtesy: Shardabehn Shah
