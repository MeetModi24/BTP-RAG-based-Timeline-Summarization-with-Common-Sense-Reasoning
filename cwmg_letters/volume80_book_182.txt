182. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
May 11, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
Give Chandrani whatever amount she needs for the journey
to Delhi. See that she reaches safely. Tell her, I have already
written to Viyogi Hari and that she should go straight by tonga
to Kingsway [Camp]. Let her stay at the Harijan Colony. Tell
her to write to me. Tell her to take care of her health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10629
