155. LETTER TO KAPILRAI MEHTA
February 19, 1932
CHI. KAPIL,
I was glad you wrote to me. I was wondering where you
were. I would advise you not to become the least impatient but
to take complete rest and build up good health. Don't be in a
hurry to start eating cereals, but go on living on milk, curds and
fruit for some more time. You should also eat lettuce and some
raw tomatoes. By raw I mean not unripe; I mean fully ripe but
uncooked tomatoes. And write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Tell Nanabhai that I often think of him and the Dakshina-
murti people.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3977. Also C.W. 1598. Courtesy:
Kapilrai Mehta
