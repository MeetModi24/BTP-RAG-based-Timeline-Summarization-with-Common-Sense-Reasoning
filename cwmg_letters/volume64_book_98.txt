98. LETTER TO P. G. VENKATADESIKAN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 27, 1936
DEAR VENKATADESIKAN,
I was glad to hear from you.
I hope you will stick to your spinning, propagating khadi
and assiduously learning Hindi.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI P. G. VENKATADESIKAN
3 CHAKRAPANI NAIDU GARDEN
PAPER MILLS ROAD
PERAMBUR
MADRAS
From a photostat: G.N. 10845
