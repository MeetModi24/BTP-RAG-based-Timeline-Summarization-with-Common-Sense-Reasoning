580. LETTER TO ESTHER MENON
[SABARMATI,
October 27, 1926]
MY DEAR CHILD,
My blessings on the addition to the family. Hope you and the
baby are steadily progressing. Any of the names suggested by
you is good. The shorter the better.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat of the original in N.A.I.; also My Dear Child, p. 79
