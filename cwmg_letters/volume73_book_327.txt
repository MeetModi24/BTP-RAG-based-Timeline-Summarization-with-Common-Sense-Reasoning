327. LETTER TO LAKSHMI SATYAMURTI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
December 30, 1940
MY DEAR LAXMI,
I must forgive you for writing in English. But you must hurry
up with your Hindi. Tell Father1, it will be a shame if he does not
fully recover during the rest he has imposed on himself.
Love.
BAPU
KUMARI LAKSHMI
C/O SHRI S. SATYAMURTI
TYAGARAYANAGAR
MADRAS
From the original: S. Satyamurti Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Mu-
seum and Library. Also C.W. 10276. Courtesy: Government of Tamil Nadu
