531. LETTER TO METROPOLITAN OF CALCUTTA
SEVAGRAM,
December 3, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your kind letter of 25th November and for
responding to Sir Tej Bahadur's invitation .
You must have seen from the papers that from tomorrow,
I am imposing on myself abstention from day-to-day routine—
work-fast. I see clearly that I must take rest, if I am to avoid
a breakdown. The fast is to end with the month.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
THE METROPOLITAN
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
