246. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
June 10, 1942
CHI. KAKA,
It is good that you are leaving for Bombay. Remember
that you are going to Varma not only to gain something from
him, if possible, but also to give him the benefit of your com-
pany. Restrain his bad habits. He talks too much. His lan-
guage also is not always pure. Pull him up. He listens to
criticism lovingly made and reforms himself too. Understand his
remedies. If you do not like any of them, leave them alone.
Blessings from
BAPU
