142. LETTER TO ANTOINETTE MIRBEL
[As AT] SABARMATI,
June 15, 1925
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to receive your letter. But I must not en-
courage you to undertake what is a long and expensive journey
for the mere purpose of seeing me. Nor is it necessary for you
to see me for your spiritual progress.
It comes through service
rendered in the name of God.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MADAME ANTOINETTE MIRBEL
100 RUE BRULE MAISON
LILLE—NORD, FRANCE
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
