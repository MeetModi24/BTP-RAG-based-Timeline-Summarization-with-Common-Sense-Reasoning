354. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
May 7, 1928
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
Your letter. Hip-baths should not be given up. Yes, do go
to Almora. Stick to the same diet as in Delhi, but there is no
harm in eating more if you have the appetite.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2357
