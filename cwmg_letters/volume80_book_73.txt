73. LETTER TO G. V. NARAYANA MURTI
April 30, 1945
Your information is incomplete. How did you get the piles?
Do you eat hot foods such as chillies and do the piles bleed?
Meanwhile drink plenty of boiled water, take orange-juice and
juice of raw vegetable such as carrots, palak and snake-gourd.
You can drink not more than three pounds of milk and take
three ounces of fresh coconut well chewed. Drink the water of the
coconut. If the piles bleed, take rest, omit one meal and apply oil
to the affected part.
From the original : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
