294. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 31, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
I had got word, before you brought it, that David would be
coming at 3.
I am replying to Dr. Khare. Send the letter today. What
a pitiable condition!
Two other letters also are enclosed.
What The Hindu says about the Governor is strange. I wish
to write even more strongly than that.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11616
