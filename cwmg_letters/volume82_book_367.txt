367. LETTER TO HARIGANESH PHATAK
SODEPUR,
December 23, 1945
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I have your letter written in English. Why did you not write
to me in Marathi if you could not write in Hindustani?
There was nothing wrong in sending Savitribai's name. It
was your duty to do so. It is good that you wrote to Thakkar
Bapa. I have been corresponding with Premabehn. I shall keep
Savitribai's name in mind. What is her husband doing?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 6103
