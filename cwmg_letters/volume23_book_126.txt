126. LETTER TO SIKH FRIENDS
[POONA,]
March 4, 1924
DEAR FRIENDS,
I learnt through Punditji Motilal after you had gone that
the S.G.P.C. was actually defending the accused in the matter of
the prosecution of Akalis. I learnt, too, that a Hindu temple
within the precincts of the Golden Temple had been destroyed
by the Akalis and that the latter took their stand upon religion.
In your letter, which you have promised, I would like you please
to deal with all these questions.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 3767
