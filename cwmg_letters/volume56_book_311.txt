311. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
November 28, 1933
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
I got your letter; I was waiting for it. How is your health?
I see that other Ashram girls also are there. Who are they?
Look after all of them.
Do you read or write anything?
The Wardha address is given on the back.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3188. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
