223. LETTER TO SIR MUNCHERJI BHOWNAGGREE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 20, 1906
DEAR SIR MUNCHERJI,
Mr. Ally, Mr. Ritch and I will wait on you tomorrow at 11.30
unless I hear to the contrary.
I am,
Yours truly,
SIR M. M. BHOWNAGGREE
196, CROMWELL ROAD, S.W.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4611
