127. LETTER TO S. DIGEY
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 8, 1906
DEAR SIR,
Sir Muncherji gave me your name as the friend of the cause of
British Indians in the Transvaal.
I venture to enclose herewith copies of several Representations
forwarded to Lord Elgin, whom, as you know, the Deputation meets
to-day at 3 p.m.
I remain,
[Enclosure]
Yours faithfully,
SAM DIGBY, ESQ.
NATIONAL LIBERAL CLUB
LONDON
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4525
