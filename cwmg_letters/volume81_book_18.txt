18. LETTER TO ROMEN CHATTERJEE
July 20, 1945
CHI. ROMEN,
I am sure you acted after careful thought. College education
has no value for me. Almost all the boys that come out take
up jobs-and jobs that bring no benefit to the country, only
cause it harm. What can you do? You are a child. All the
elders want you to go to college. How can you disobey them?
Be good.
Blessings from
BAPU
