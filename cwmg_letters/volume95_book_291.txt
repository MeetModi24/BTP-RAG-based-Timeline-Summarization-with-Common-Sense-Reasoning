291. LETTER TO SHARDABEHN G. CHOKHAWALA
CHI. SHARDA,
Anandi writes to me that you say you are never going to get
well. It is not that at all. All you have to do is to learn to exercise
restraint and have confidence in yourself. I have discovered after
examining you at Wardha that there is nothing else wrong with you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9964. Courtesy: Sharadabehn G. Chokhawala
