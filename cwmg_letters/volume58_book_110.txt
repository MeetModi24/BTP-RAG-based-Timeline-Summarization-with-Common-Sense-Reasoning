110. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
June 21, 1934
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I could read your letter only this morning. You may cer-
tainly live with Krishnan Nair at the Ashram. One learns by
doing. There is no need at present to join Vinoba. No time to
write more. Are you keeping well?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2414
