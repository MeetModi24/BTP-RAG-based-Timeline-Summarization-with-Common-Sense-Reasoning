132. LETTER TO V. R. KOTHARI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 10, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I appreciate what you say. I enclose here-
with a cheque upon Jamnalalji for Rs. 2,500/-. You will please
let me have the promised letter in due course.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1 Hundi
SJT. V. R. KOTHARI
SHUKRAWAR PETH
POONA CITY
From a microfilm: S.N. 11129
