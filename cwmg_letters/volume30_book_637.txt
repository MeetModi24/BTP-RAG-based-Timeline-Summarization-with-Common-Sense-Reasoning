637. LETTER TO PERIN CAPTAIN
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 5, 1926
I have neglected your letter for a few days. Please give me
some more particulars about the Hin. . .2 School, Matunga. What
is the attendance? How did it come under the protection of the
Stree Sabha? Who is on the committee that is looking after the
School? How many teachers are there? Who is the head master?
Are there any girls in the school? And are the children doing
any spinning? If so, what is the monthly output? Do they
have the takli or the wheel? And please add such other infor-
mation as you may think necessary.
You may treat the Finland visit as off. Mr. Andrews will
be leaving probably today for Delhi.
Yours,
MRS. PERIN CAPTAIN
ISLAM CLUB BUILDING
CHAUPATI
[BOMBAY]
