342. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
Silence Day, February 2, 1942
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You should see the knife they give me. It is full of dirt. In
jail the knives are kept shining clean. This can also be as clean;
in fact all our implements should be as clean.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4412
