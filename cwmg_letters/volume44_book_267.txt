267. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI
September 29, 1930
CHI. PUNJABHAI (JUNIOR, BARODA),
I got your letter. Your illness has lasted too long. That is
always the way of the body. It is more brittle even than a glass
bangle. We should, therefore, take care of it only in order that
we may be able to serve others. Get well soon. Write to me
from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4016
