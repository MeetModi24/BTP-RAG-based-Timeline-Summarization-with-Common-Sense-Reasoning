305. LETTER TO ABDUL GHAFFAR KHAN
SEVAGRAM,
January 17, 1942
DEAR KHAN SAHEB,
Here are the two copies. You will show them to the Mau-
lana Saheb and to Jawaharlal. The letter to the Government
you will send when and if you come to the conclusion that you
will better serve the cause by being in the jail than by being
outside and working among the people. For you are not likely
to get the permission asked for.
The resignation you will send if your co-workers and Dr.
Khan Saheb agree.
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
