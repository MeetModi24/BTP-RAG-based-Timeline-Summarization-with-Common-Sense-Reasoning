242. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA RAO
August 21, 1945
BHAI RAMACHANDRA RAO,
I have your letter. It is not proper to order a charkha from
outside. You must get one made there. In the mean time use
a takli. Understand the full meaning of spinning.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAMACHANDRA RAO
MADURA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
