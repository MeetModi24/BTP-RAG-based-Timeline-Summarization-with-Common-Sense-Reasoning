218. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
May 19, 1937
MY DEAR REBEL,
This is just to send you a sample of the lace now being
manufactured in Andhra out of hand-spun yarn. The price is
Re. 0-3- per yard. Do you, i.e., your customers, need any?
Is it up to the mark? There is some for sale in the Bombay
Depot.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3783. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6939
