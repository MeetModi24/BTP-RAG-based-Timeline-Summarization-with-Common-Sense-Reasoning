343. LETTER TO H. A. POPLEY
August 18, 1934
DEAR REV. POPLEY,
I thank you for your letter. I wish it was possible for me
to go to Bombay if only to see Dr. Eddy. But medical friends
forbid any such movement, and I do not feel the call to over-
ride their advice. By as much conservation as possible of the
energy being daily gathered I am trying to regain lost strength
as quickly as possible.
My fast was a rich experience for me. You have perhaps
come across my reflections on it in the current issue of Harijan.
REV. H. A. POPLEY
ERODE (S. INDIA)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
