174. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
[BOMBAY,]
April 5, 1919
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
For 24 hours I was sad beyond measure over the Delhi tra-
gedy. I am now happy beyond measure over it. The blood spilt
at Delhi was innocent. It is possible that the satyagrahis in Delhi
made mistakes. But on the whole, they have covered themselves
with glory. There can be no redemption without sacrifice. And
it fills me with a glow to find that full measure was given even on
the first day and that too at the very seat of the power of Satan.
I want to share this happiness with you if you can take it.
I hope you received my letter answering your doubts. I have
filed an appeal against you and here is a copy thereof. You can
do what you like [with] it. But I must have Gurudev's opinion.
With deep love,
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
