55. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEGAON,
July 31, 1939
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have received your letters. It is time for the post. I
shall not write much today. I am thinking about you. There
have been no rains here. People are anxious.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4323
