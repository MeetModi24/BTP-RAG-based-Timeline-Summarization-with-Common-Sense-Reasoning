367. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
July 15, 1935
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got both your letters. Obtain Babuji's permission and stay
there as long as you wish. Since we shall be meeting soon, I don't
write more. I can't think of anything which I should like you to
bring with you from there. I hope you will see Vallabhbhai.
Ramdas also is in Bombay. His address is: 1, Ash Lane, Fort.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Ba is in Delhi.
