128. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
June 25, 1934
CHI. RAIHANA,
I am in a hurry today, and so I am writing in Gujarati.
I have taken an extract from your letter for Harijan. I hope
Harivadan and Hamida have now regained their balance of
mind. Jail is not meant for those who find jail life a pleasure.
One must be able to live the same kind of life outside also and
to devote oneself to constructive work. I hope you are very
well. My regards to Father and Mother. Saroj sees me every
day.
Blessings from
BAPU
