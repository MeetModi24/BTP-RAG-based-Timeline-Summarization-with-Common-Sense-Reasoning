363. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
May 7, 1945
CHI. NARAHARI,
Consult me before writing anything in this matter.
BAPU
[PS.]
Is this Mastram's son ? I do not know him at all. Mastram is
Nanabhai's friend. He has betrayed Nanabhai. Nanabhai told me that.
Now Mastram also must be involved in this. In that case it seems
to me that nothing should be written to him.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
