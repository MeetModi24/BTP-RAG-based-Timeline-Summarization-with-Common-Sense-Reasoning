361. LETTER TO SISTER AMATA
August 21, 1934
MY DEAR AMATA,
I had your precious letter just after the completion of the
fast. God bless you all the larks. I must not try to write too
many letters as yet though you will be glad to know that I am
slowly but surely regaining the lost strength. Only I have need
yet to be careful.
Love to you all.
BAPU
SISTER AMATA
EREMO, FRANCESCANO
TREVI (UMBRIA)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
