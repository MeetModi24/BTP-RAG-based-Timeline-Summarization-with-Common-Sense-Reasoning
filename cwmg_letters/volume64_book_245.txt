245. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
POONA,
January 9, 1937
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Yesterday I received another wire from R. saying you and
K. should not accompany me. But I do still expect to hear
directly from C. P. saying "yes". However, we must be ready
for either event.
The weather here is good—but not so bracing as at Segaon.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3763. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6919
