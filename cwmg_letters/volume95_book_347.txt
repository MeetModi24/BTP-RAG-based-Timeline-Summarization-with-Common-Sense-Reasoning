347. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Tuesday
CHI. VASUMATI,
Whether I write or not, you have got to write regular
letters to me. How did you get fever? It is time you became absolutely
healthy now. Exert your body also only to the extent it can take the
strain.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S. N. 9349
