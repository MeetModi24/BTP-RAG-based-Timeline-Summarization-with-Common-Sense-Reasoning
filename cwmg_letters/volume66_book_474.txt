474. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
HARIPURA,
February 18, 1938
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
How shall I reproach you for not writing?
What is the
reason? I do hope to leave this place probably on the 20th
or the 21st. I shall see the gain in your weight. Rama-
chandran did not turn up.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 394
