436. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
Unrevised
SEGAON, WARDHA,
March 8, 1937
MY DEAR REBEL,
I have an excuse for writing to you. You have left a small
blue bottle. It contains a white powder resembling epsom salt.
What is it? I do hope you had no trouble in the train. The
moral is no onion, no sweet potatoes, a fair quantity of garlic.
Bowels ought not to be loose. Hip and friction sitz baths and
garlic ought to set the bowels right and eradicate eczema. You
must take at least 3 lb. of milk per day. Try unboiled fresh milk
twice a day. It must be taken whilst it is yet warm from the
udder.
Your place is vacant and looks dismal. Fancy doing without
one's Secretary ever at your side! But such is life, everything fleet-
ing, only God is, nothing else is.
