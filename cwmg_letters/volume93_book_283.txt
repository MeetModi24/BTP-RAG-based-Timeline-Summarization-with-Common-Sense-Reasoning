283. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
June 5, 1938
STUPID SUSHILA,
How stupid you are ! Sitting there, you cry and make me
cry ! !! You are unnecessarily taking the blame upon your self.
I have not given up taking service from Ba. Other women
feel a little unhappy, but they are cheerful. They have
also understood the point. A. S. does feel miserable. But I am
also making her laugh quite a bit. She is more stupid than
you. She too will calm down in a few days. I hope that abiding
by my order, both of you will have arrived here. It is impossible
to start today. I am writing this letter so that the day does
not pass without communication in case you are unable to
leave. Stop crying now. I am eager to see you laugh. You
have committed no sin.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
