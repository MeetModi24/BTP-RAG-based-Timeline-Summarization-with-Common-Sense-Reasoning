303. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
December 18, 1932
CHI. BABU ALIAS SHARDA,
Is it that you have boycotted me? You used to write beauti-
ful letters every week and now you are silent. Why? If you
are taking pity on me, I have no use for it. What I want is
beautiful letters, even if I do not write.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9959. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
wala
