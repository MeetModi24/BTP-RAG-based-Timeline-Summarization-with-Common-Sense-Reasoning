429. LETTER TO TRAMBAKLAL
August 22, 1932
CHI. TRAMBAKLAL,
Chhaganbhai has full trust in you. Doctor also trusted you.
I am sure that you are doing your best to keep Doctor's family
united. Please write to me and let me know about any difficul-
ties which hinder your efforts. And let me also have any suggestions
which you would like to make.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9436
