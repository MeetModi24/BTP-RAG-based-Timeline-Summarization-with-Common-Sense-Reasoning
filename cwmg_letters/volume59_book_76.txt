76. LETTER TO MADHAVDAS AND KRISHNA KAPADIA
September 24, 1934
CHI. MADHAVDAS AND KRISHNA 2 ,
You will be glad to learn that I had a letter from Medh to-
day in which he has promised to contribute something at any rate.
I will let you know as soon as I receive the amount. How much
do you owe in all? I hope both of you are well. Do you earn
anything?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
