416. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[After August 27, 1938]
CHI. MAHADEV,
Herewith both your articles.
You must have received one bundle of letters sent with Raja-
ram.
Herewith also I am sending a few letters which are ready.
Rajkumari has reached safely. The 'satyagrahis' are quite well.
They do not give any trouble.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11617
