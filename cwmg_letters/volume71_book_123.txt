123. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
January 12, 1940
CHI. MANILAL, SUSHILA,
I got your letters. I do make every possible effort to write, but
there is so much work every day that something is always left over.
I have already written to you about your fighting there. I will
write more when I get time. Medh's letter is lying with me. I
have nothing special to write. The final responsibility rests on you.
Kishorelalbhai is staying here at present. There are hopes of
his improving. Jairamdas also is here. He is very much better.
Kunvarji is gaining weight rapidly. Thus Segaon has turned
into a hospital just now. I am not likely to start a struggle here
immediately.
Ba is in Delhi. Lakshmi is very weak. She is improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4907
