58. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
May 8, 1941
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
I forgot to send you blessings for your birthday, didn't
I? What can I do? But you know that you have them. All the
same have them in writing too. Since you are getting them
late, have them with interest. Anyway you know how things
are. I have Narandas's letter. If it needs a reply, I will send
it by and by.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
