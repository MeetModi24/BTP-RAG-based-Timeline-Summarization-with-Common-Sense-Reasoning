423. LETTER TO P. C. GHOSH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 30, 1938
MY DEAR PROFULLA,
I am glad you are having an exhibition for khadi and
allied village industries and hope that it will be a glorious
success as well from the point of view of numbers who attend
as of sales.
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
I am out of stock for palm-gur and need it badly for Pyare-
lal who is just convalescent. Please send the maximum quantity
carried at the minimum cost per railway parcel or postal,
whichever is cheaper.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
