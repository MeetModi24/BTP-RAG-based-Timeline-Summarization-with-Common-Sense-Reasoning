653. LETTER TO E. W. ARYANAYAKUM
April 15, 1945
CHI. ARYANAYAKUM,
What is the harm if I arrange to get another room for Rama-
chandran in Rustom Bhavan itself? After all he is here for six
months. Isn't he? Pyarelal needs all the rooms. He has a lot
of papers and books.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
