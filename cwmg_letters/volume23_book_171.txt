171. LETTER TO SHAUKAT ALI
March 18, 1924
DEAR FRIEND AND BIG BROTHER,
It ill becomes you to have typhoid fever or any fever. Let
illness be only my lot in our circle. But I do not want to inflict
a long letter on you. May God soon restore you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 8520
