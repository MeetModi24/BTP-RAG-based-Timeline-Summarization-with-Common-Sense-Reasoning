239. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 11, 1938
CHI. SUSHILA,
How are things with you? When will you calm down?
The 9th of May was auspicious for us as the 14th [of April].
The 9th had a special tranquility. On the 14th I was very much
upset. On the 9th I was miserable but calm. How nice it would
be if you too could understand this in your heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
