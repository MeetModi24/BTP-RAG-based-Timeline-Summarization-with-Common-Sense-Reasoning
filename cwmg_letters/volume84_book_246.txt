246. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
NEW DELHI,
May 23, 1946
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I forgot to write about Keshu. I do not know to whom
I may write. What is wrong in Keshu going home? He may
exert good influence there. It will not be wrong to write to him
to that effect.
At present Aryanayakum is not there. Who has been making
a demand for money? This question arises from Chimanlal's letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 4556
