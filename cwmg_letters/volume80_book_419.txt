419. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
PANCHGANI,
June 3, 1945
Take courage in both hands and do this. You know Dr.
Gilder well. Go to him and, if he permits you, go to Nagpur .
Here you can come whenever you want. I would not advise you
to go to Wardha.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./24
