397. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
August 4, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
Although you say I may post your article to you I think you
are in error if you leave on 6th. Hence it is being sent to Lucknow.
No corrections. Don't much appreciate this article. The thing
requires a lot of work behind it. Discuss it with me when you
come.
I am handing your note to Babla.
Rajen Babu is ill. Not very.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3992. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7301
