246. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
POST ANDHERI,
March 29, 1924
MY DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
Here is a copy of a letter received by me, which speaks for
itself. Please let me know what truth there is in the charges,
and if you do not know anything, please inquire and advise me
as to what should be done.
Yours sincerely,
Encl.
SJT. JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
HYDERABAD (SINDH)
From a photostat: S.N. 8627
