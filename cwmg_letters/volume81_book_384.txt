384. LETTER TO GANESHSHASTRI JOSHI
To be delivered by hand
September 3, 1945
BHAI GANESH SHASTRI,
There is too much of cholera around Sevagram. What does
Ayurveda have to say? People are succumbing to the disease
every day; how can we then promptly cremate the bodies?
Where to find all the firewood? How quickly and in what
manner should the body be removed? If they are to be buried,
how should it be done and by whom? Please think over the
problem. Send a reply or meet Dr. Sushila and discuss the
matter with her. Be prompt.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W.933. Courtesy: Ganeshshastri Joshi
