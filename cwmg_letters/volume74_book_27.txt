27. LETTER TO NANABHAI I. MASHRUWALA
April 28, 1941
BHAI NANABHAI1,
You have completed three score. I do hope that you will
regain your health and will complete two score more in order that
you may go on serving.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6694. Also C.W. 4339. Courtesy:
Nanabhai I. Mashruwala
