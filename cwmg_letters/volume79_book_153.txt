153. LETTER TO BHULABHAI J. DESAI
SEVAGRAM,
February 2, 1945
BHAI BHULABHAI,
What you say is correct. Come whenever you wish. I have
no fear. It is the newspaper reports that impelled me to cau-
tion you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Bhulabhai Desai Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
