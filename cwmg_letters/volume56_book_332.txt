332. LETTER TO VIDYA A. HINGORANI
December 7, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
I got your letter. When I advised you to give up inhibition I
meant that you should not hesitate to take what help you need
from other brothers and sisters. One can get such help through
heart-felt humility. If you can't get it, then do engage a maid-
servant. The attempt should be gradually to do away with the
practice of employing servants and do all work by ourselves; but
this is possible only when your health improves. Do not think that
I am asking you to do everything all at once. Do it as your
strength permits.
Have you understood me now? Do write to me all details.
Blessings from
BAPU
