229. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI
February 6, 1935
CHI. GANGABEHN (JHAVERI),
I was waiting for your letter. It is good that you are living
the life of a student. Navin and Mahesh are bound to benefit
immensely from your diligence.
The two brothers will grow wiser with the passage of time.
Do not budge from there for some years to come. Study as
much as you want to.
Improve your health.
Never forsake
simplicity.
I hope you are keeping up the spinning. Use village-
made things as much as you can. Build up your health by keep-
ing strictly to the rules regarding diet and rest. Keep writing to
me now and then. And tell Mahesh to write.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3120
