461. LETTER TO SHANKARRAO DEO
July 15, 1937
MY DEAR DEO,
I have your letter enclosing a newspaper cutting . I have
forwarded the cutting to Mr. Kelkar for confirmation. As soon
as I receive his reply, you will hear further from me.
I hope your progress towards complete recovery is continuing.
Yours sincerely,
The Bombay Chronicle, 27-7-1937
