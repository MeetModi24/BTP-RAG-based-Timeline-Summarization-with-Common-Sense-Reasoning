371. LETTER TO SHAMAL R. RAVAL
August 14, 1932
CHI. SHAMAL,
When you are afraid of anything, you should repeat Rama-
nama. The fear will then disappear . You should go to sleep repeat-
ing it. Does anybody sleep near you? We should have faith that
God is near us. If a person has such faith, what need he fear?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3145. Also C.W. 2880. Courtesy:
Shamal R. Raval
