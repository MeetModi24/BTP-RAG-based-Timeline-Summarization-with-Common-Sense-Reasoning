330. LETTER TO PRANJIVAN
October 23, 1937
BHAI PRANJIVAN,
If every school which has functioned for many years looked
at its own history, it would find someone or the other [from
among its students] who has ultimately risen to fame. So, as
far as I am concerned, I would consider such a thing a mere
accident. For an old school like yours the real occasion to feel
elated would be when it takes measures leading to improvement
and makes progress in such a way that it has no parallel in the
field. What is there to be proud about accidental achievement?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[NATIONAL] SCHOOL
RAJKOT
