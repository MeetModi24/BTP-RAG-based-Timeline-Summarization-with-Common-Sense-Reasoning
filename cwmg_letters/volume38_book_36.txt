36. LETTER TO JEHANGIR B. PETIT
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 8, 1928
DEAR MR. PETIT,
Here is a copy of the letter I have given to the blind boy. I
am posting it to your Fort address as I have asked the boy to
take the original to the School. But as you might not have
gone there and might not have yet given instructions at the School,
I am writing this to you so as to enable you to do the needful.
Yours sincerely,
JEHANGIR B. PETIT, ESQ.
PETIT BUILDING, 359 HORNBY ROAD, FORT, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 12990
