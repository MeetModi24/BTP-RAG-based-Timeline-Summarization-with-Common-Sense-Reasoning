305. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
RAJKOT,
May 25, 1939
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Balkrishna is not going away for years and I shall be back
only by the middle of July. Therefore there would have been
no harm even if you had accompanied Balkrishna. Anyway your
decision was good enough. Balkrishna's going is taking so much
time that very soon it may get cold there and then it will be no
use going. Anyway whatever is deemed best should be done. I
must know soon about it.
About the books for you I said what I did after full thought.
Sushila is laid up with fever.
Blessings from
BAPU
[Ps.]
We reach Bombay on the 2nd and thence to the Frontier
Province around the 5th.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4316
