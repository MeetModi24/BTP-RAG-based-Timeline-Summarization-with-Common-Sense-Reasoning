27. LETTER TO HARILAL GANDHI
MOTIHARI,
November 14, 1917
CHI. HARILAL,
Today is Diwali day. May the New Year bring you pros-
perity. I wish that all your aspirations are fulfilled and that all
of you increase in your wealth of character, and pray that you
realize more and more that this is the only real Lakshmi and our
highest good lies in the worship of this alone.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. IV
