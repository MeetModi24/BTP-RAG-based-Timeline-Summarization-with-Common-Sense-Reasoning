244. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
February 7, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter. Read the accompanying letter to Harilal.
If you approve of his coming to me, give it to him and, if he is
really willing, send him here. He may now perhaps improve more
if he stays with me.
I hope Gokibehn is all right.
I am enclosing a letter received from Prema.
How is Lila-
vati? How is Kusum's health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8429. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
