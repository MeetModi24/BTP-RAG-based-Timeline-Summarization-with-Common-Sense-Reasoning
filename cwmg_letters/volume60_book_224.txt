224. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
February 5, 1935
BHAI MUNSHI,
I have your letter. It is indeed worth considering to what
extent we can participate in swadeshi exhibitions. It may be an
act of kindness to give a place to the bullock-cart in an exhibi-
tion of aeroplanes but what would be the purpose of it? What
would a giant intend to convey by lifting numerous dwarfs on
the nail of his little finger? Considering this you may do whatever
you think is in the interest of the villagers.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7567. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
