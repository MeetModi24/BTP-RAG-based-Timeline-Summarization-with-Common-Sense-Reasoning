467. LETTER TO SAMPURNANAND
September 8, 1938
BHAI SAMPURNANAND,
I agree with everything that you have said. The Congress
has merely given a new name to the language. It has not made
any other difference. No honest person would reject a word be-
cause of its Hindu or Muslim origin. What can we say of
others? And it has become fashionable these days to oppose
anything done by the Congress or Congressmen. Do you want
to know my views on the subject or something else? Because I
have spoken and written at length on this subject.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From Hindi: C.W. 10133. Courtesy: Kashi Vidyapeeth, Varanasi
