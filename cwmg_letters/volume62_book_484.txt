484. LETTER TO GOVIND V. GURJALE
May 18, 1936
DEAR GURJALE,
I have your letter. Much as I should like to visit you, I
cannot do it. I have come here for Sardar's health. I have
other patients, too, with me. And I must keep time.
My advice to you is for you simply and solely to live like a
villager. Therefore your own expenses can only be under Rs.
10 per month. If there is any productive work to be done,
get it done by paid labour. Labour combined with intelli-
gence must always be self-supporting.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1399
