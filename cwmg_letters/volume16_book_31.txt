31. LETTER TO ISHARDAS KHANNA
LABURNUM ROAD,
BOMBAY,
August 20, 1919
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter with the judgment and the evidence in the
case of your son Karamchand. Please let me have copy of the
petition submitted to His Honour the Lieutenant-Governor. Why
did you not give evidence in the case to support the statement that
your son was not in Hafizabad on the 14th April? Please send
me also copy of the full text of evidence. Please let me know
also what happened to the three others who were sentenced to be
hanged.
Yours sincerely,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 6814
