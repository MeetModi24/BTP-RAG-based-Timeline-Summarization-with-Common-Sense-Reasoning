592. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
BIRLA HOUSE,
April 4, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
You see I am in Bombay and am fixed here up to 14th.
God knows the next, if He allows freedom up to 14th. I am well.
What about Lahore sister-in-law? Is she getting better? Shummy
must get well quickly.
Sushila hurt herself on trying to enter the prayer compound.
Her arm is in a sling. There is no fracture. Krishna is also well.
Badshah Khan is here as quiet as ever. He is sitting by me as
I write this.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4153. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7788
