439. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
April 24, 1932
CHI. JANAKIBEHN,
Please write to me. Why do you remain ill? What is your
diet? You should eat fruit in sufficient quantities. You ought
to improve your health. You need not worry about Jamnalal
or Kamalnayan or anybody else. Do you read anything? Who
are your companions in jail?
All three of us are quite well. We often think about you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2898
