34. LETTER TO JANKIE AMMAL NAIDOO
[As AT] WARDHA,
June 8, 1936
MY DEAR JANKIE,
I was delighted to have your letter. It is good Father is
coming to India.
I wish you too had come. Of course after
the rich living of Natal, you may not appreciate the simple life
here. And you will be horrified to see the appalling poverty
of the villages. That experience would chasten you. However,
Father will tell you on his return what India is like.
Yes, all the Phoenixites are well.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Address: M. K. Gandhi
Wardha
India
SHRI JANKIE AMMAL NAIDOO
37 MALLINSON RD., SYDENHAM
DURBAN, NATAL
From a photostat: G.N. 10463
