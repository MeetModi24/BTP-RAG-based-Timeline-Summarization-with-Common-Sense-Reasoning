15. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
July 20, 1945
CHI. VANU,
If you become Vanu-di1, wouldn't that be your undoing?
Are you going to remain silly all your life? Then remain Vanudi.
If you have any silliness in you, leave it there. As for the extra
fat, you have gone there to shed it, and also to improve the ear.
Isn't that so? You have planned the prayers, etc., fairly well there.
I have built high hopes on the result of your stay there. My
two notes are for both you sisters.
The rest from the other letters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 5794. Also C.W. 3017. Cour-
tesy : Vanamala M. Desai
