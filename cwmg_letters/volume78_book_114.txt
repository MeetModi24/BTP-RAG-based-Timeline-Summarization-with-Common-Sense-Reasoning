114. LETTER TO BISHOP OF RANGOON
ON THE TRAIN FOR BOMBAY,
September 9, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your kind letter bringing me your good wishes. I am
in God's good hands. I fancy I understand what you mean.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
RT. REVD. BISHOP OF RANGOON
HOTEL CECIL
DELHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
