629. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
January 6, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Krishnadas has not been given any quinine. He is being
kept on oranges. There is no fever today. Shastriji's hands
looked more emaciated today. The Vaidya will be leaving for
Bombay in a day or two. This place did not suit him. I shall
do the needful about Sankaran. Tari will have settled down there.
I hope you are well. Ba is all right. There is a big crowd of
people here. The place is almost suffocating. Send your articles
regularly. Mother must not worry about Pyarelal.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. W. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
