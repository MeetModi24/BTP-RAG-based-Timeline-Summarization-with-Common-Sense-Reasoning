44. LETTER TO V. L. PHADKE
Friday, November 9, 1928
BHAISHRI MAMA,
I got your letter. I was a little ill and that has delayed this
reply. I think this is not a case of Ghanchis against Hindus, but
one involving four well-to-do business men. I see no need for us
to do anything more about it. It is plain that the Bhangi student
was not a votary of non-violence. He adopted a course which
he thought best, and I do not think we need do anything more
about it. Even if you think it is a case between Ghanchis and
Hindus, your ultimate aim is to win over the former by and by.
I would not mind even if a few inmates of the Ashram laid down
their lives. Anyone who professes non-violence will not deserve
a certificate until he passes the supreme test.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3821
1 A community in Gujarat traditionally engaged in oil pressing
