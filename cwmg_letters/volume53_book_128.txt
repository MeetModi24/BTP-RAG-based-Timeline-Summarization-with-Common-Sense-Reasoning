128. LETTER TO V. M. NAWLE
January 20, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 17th instant. I never had your letter
regarding Sjt. Vaidya's letter nor Mr. Seymer Stone's. If you
have kept a copy, please let me have it, as also your explanation
about Sjt. Vaidya.
It is a most difficult thing for me to spare you one hour just
now. You should first reduce to writing exactly what your for-
mula is. It does not matter whether your English is good or bad.
You may even write to me in Marathi. I shall have no difficulty
in following it, if the writing is legible.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18983a
