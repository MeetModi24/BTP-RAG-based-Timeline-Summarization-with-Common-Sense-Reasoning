343. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
AHMEDABAD,
August 18, 1931
MY DEAR BHRR,
Your letter. Yes, you have to be on the shelf well dusted
and ready when the emergency arises. You are still the succe-
ssor. Love to you all, specially to the new arrival. Smother
him or her with kisses. Mind that the flowing beard does not
prick the tender skin.
Yours,
BHRR
From a photostat: S.N. 9577
