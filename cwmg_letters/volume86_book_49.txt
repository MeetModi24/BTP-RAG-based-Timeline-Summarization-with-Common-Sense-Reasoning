49. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
October 26, 1946
CHI. AMRIT,
This is just a line under great pressure of work. I do hope
you will not be in a hurry to see the cut healed. Let it take
its course. It will be the shortest.
I leave tomorrow morning.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 4184. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7820
