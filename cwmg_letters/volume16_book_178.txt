178. LETTER TO A FRIEND
[DELHI,]
Kartik Sud 7 [October 31, 1919]
DEAR FRIEND,
Chi. Chhaganlal writes to me to say that you have been wait-
ing to receive 50 spinning-wheels from me. I don't recollect any-
thing about this. All the same, if you want any in addition to
the ten I sent, I shall certainly be in a position to supply them.
You should, however, get them made there. I am at present in
the Punjab.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 5714
