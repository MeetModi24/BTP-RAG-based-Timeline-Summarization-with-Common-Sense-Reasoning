220. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
ASHRAM,
[October 28, 1920]
CHI. DEVDAS,
We arrived here at eleven. You must have got my letter of
yesterday's. Mathuradas has given me news about your health. I
am sure he will continue to do so. I am trying not to worry over it.
Balibehn is here. When she returns, she will take the children
along with her. Deepak, too, is going to Lahore for the holidays.
Mr. Andrews is here. He will leave tomorrow. Jinavijayji has also
come along with me.
Ba must be in good cheer. I shall leave for Mehmedabad on
Monday morning and then go on to Nadiad in the afternoon. I
shall leave Nadiad on Tuesday and be there on Wednesday morning.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 7172
