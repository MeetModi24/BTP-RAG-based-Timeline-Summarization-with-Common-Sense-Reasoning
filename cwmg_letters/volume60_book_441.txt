441. LETTER TO R. S. HUKERIKAR
March 22, 1935
DEAR HUKERIKAR,
I think that when Congress village workers are invited, they
should go and put their viewpoints at the meeting. If there is
agreement on any items, we should work in co-operation. For a
true village worker the question of prestige does not come in at
all; he does not work in order to acquire influence or prestige.
He works because he must. He cannot help serving the villagers.
What under certain definite circumstances one should do
depends solely upon one's own judgment based on those circums-
tances.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. S. HUKERIKAR
PROVINCIAL CONGRESS COMMITTEE
DHARWAR, KARNATAK
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
