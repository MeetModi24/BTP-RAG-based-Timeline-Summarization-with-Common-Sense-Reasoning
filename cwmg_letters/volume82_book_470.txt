470. LETTER TO K. F. NARIMAN
SODEPUR,
January 4, 1946
BHAI NARIMAN,
I have your wire. I am very happy. But do not lay down
any conditions. Remain a true soldier of the Congress.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI KAIKHUSHRU NARIMAN
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
