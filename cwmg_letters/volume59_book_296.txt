296. LETTER TO HAR DAYAL NAG
November 2, 1934
DEAR HAR DAYALBABU,
I had your letter of 18th October last. You know what I
have done. Let us hope that the Congress will be cleared of
the hypocrisies that you are afraid of.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI HAR DAYAL NAG
CHANDPUR (BENGAL)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
