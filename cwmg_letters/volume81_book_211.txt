211. LETTER TO MOHAMMED SALIM
August 15/16, 1945
MOHAMMED SALIM SAHEB,
I have your letter. I had seen what was written to you
before. You must know that I have no private money of my
own. Therefore please excuse me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
MOHAMMED SALIM, SHOPKEEPER
BELBAGH, JABALPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
