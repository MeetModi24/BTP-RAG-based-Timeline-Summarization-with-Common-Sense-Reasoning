677. LETTER TO S. K. GUPTA
October 18, 1945
BHAI GUPTA,
I am glad you have a high opinion of the Sevagram [Ashram].
Holding that opinion, make as much headway as you can.
Otherwise there is nothing in Sevagram and you may give up
the idea of going there.
S. K. GUPTA
EXCISE INSPECTOR
6 RLY. ROAD
FARUKHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
