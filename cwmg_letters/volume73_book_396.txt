396. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
February 2, 1941
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
Ramjibhai is full of praises of you. I am giving this letter
for his satisfaction. What need I say to you on behalf of the Hari-
jans? No service we render them can be enough.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2713. Courtesy: Dahyabhai M. Patel
