799. LETTER TO AMBUJAMMAL
WARDHA,
October 12, 1934
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have both your letters. Certainly, you may write in
English. Your fast does distress me. The fast should not have
been undertaken. Your parents will be pained and they will
consider it coercion. I hope you will experience perfect peace.
Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
