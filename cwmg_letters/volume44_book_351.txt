351. LETTER TO DURGA GIRI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 24, 1930
CHI. DURGA,
Why do you say: "What more can I write?" So many things
happen within the span of one week! You must be capable of
describing them. A multitude of thoughts arise in the mind of a
girl of your age. The thoughts can be written down, but on one
condition—one must have the zeal and the concentration of mind
for it. If you maintain diary and note down everything, you can
find from it the topics to write upon.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Virat Vatsalata, p. 32
