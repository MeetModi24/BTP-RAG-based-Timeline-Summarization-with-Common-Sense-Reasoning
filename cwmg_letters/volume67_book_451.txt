451. LETTER TO HARSHADABEHN DIWANJI
September 6, 1938
DEAR SISTER,
I got your loving gift. I have heard that women are in
the habit of concealing their age. Have you justified this belief
by concealing yours? Be that as it may, live a full hundred
years.
If you want my compliments for spinning a hundred rounds
more, you have them. But where is the room for compliments
when one is doing one's duty?
You may come whenever you wish to.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HARSHADABEHN DIWANJI
KHAR
BOMBAY
From Gujarati: C.W. 9795
