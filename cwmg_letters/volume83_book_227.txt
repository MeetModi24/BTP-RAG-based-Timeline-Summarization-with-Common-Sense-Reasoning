227. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
POONA,
March 1, 1946
BHAI RAJA,
I have your letters from time to time. Rajkumari replies to
them. From my point of view what you did s is quite all right.
The sphere of service has no bounds. I on my part wish that
you should take rest. Come over, if you can. If nothing else,
at least we can have a good laugh together.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 2116
