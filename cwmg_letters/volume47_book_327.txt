327. LETTER TO ROMAIN ROLLAND
AHMEDABAD,
August 15, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your most affectionate letter. How I would have
loved to see you if I had gone to England, but it was not to
be. I feel that it was God's will that I should not go. But I am
not yet without hope that some day, somehow we shall meet in
the flesh.
Yours sincerely,
M. ROMAIN ROLLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 17475
