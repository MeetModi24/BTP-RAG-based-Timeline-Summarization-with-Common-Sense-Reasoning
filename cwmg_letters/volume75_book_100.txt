100. LETTER TO DUNCAN GREENLEES
November 1, 1941
I have your letter. It makes strange reading. I have no narrow
patriotism about me. I would do anything to promote goodwill
among men. Nothing can be done without men of the same opin-
ion joining and co-operating.
You may call them a party and
condemn them. But they are not a party if they are against no one.
Did Jesus and his twelve chosen disciples form a party or not?
And is there any difference between Imperialism and Nazism?
I see none. The latter is the logical outcome of the first. I cannot
take up arms for Imperialism in the vain hope of destroying Nazism.
I would rather be ground down between the two in resisting them
unavoidably. Surely your logical brain should see this.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
