208. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
POONA,
June 23, 1944
CHI. MANUDI,
Your falling ill as soon as you arrived there has shaken me.
If you faithfully carry out all that I have told you, you would
never fall ill. The decision to study is a good one. But you
must not study just to get through the examination. Study what-
ever you can without straining your eyes. You are impatient as
all young people are. But I expect patience from you. The
virtues that I have seen in you are not found in all girls. Having
regard to them, when I see the smallest drawback in you it seems
to me a mountain and something unbearable.
Blessings from
BAPU
