135. LETTER TO DR. T. S. S. RAJAN
March 18, 1933
DEAR DR. RAJAN,
Sjt. Ganesan was talking to me the other day about the
impending closing of a Theosophical Harijan school in Madras.
It would be a pity if a school of such a long standing should be
closed or pass out of the public hands. If therefore the facts about
the school are as they have been stated to me, I think that
every effort should be made to save that school. Sjt. Ganesan tells
me you have already all the figures.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20619
