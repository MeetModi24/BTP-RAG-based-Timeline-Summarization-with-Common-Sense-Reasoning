379. LETTER TO POPATLAL
Shravan Vad 5 [August 7, 1917]
BHAISHRI POPATLAL,
I was extremely happy to read of your going to the Ashram.
Take whatever subject suits you after consulting everyone there.
Be careful that you do not spoil your eyes.
I hope to be back there next week.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: S. N. 6384
