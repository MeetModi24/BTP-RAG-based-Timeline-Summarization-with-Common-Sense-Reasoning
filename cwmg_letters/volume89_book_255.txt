255. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
NEW DELHI,
September 25, 1947
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
There are many Harijans in Sind. They are in great dis-
tress and are faced with starvation. They should be transferred
to Kathiawar. If, therefore, your Company sends over a ship or
two to bring them over free of charge to some port in Kathia-
war, their safety can be ensured. It is doubtful just now whether
they will be able to preserve their religion or save the honour
of their womenfolk. Bapa will see you with this letter. Please
do this immediately, if possible. You may show this letter to
Walchandbhai or any others in your Company with whom you
feel like sharing it. I am addressing a similar letter to Shoorjibhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 4810. Courtesy: Shantikumar
N. Morarjee
