273. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 11, 1937
CHI. SITA 1 ,
Your handwriting is very beautiful indeed.
Write Gujarati also in an equally beautiful hand. Write
in both the languages. I hope you will show us your face
some day. If you were here Kanam would get company and
I would teach you according to the new method. Do you know
what this method is? Ask Sushila.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4868
