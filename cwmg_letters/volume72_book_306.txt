306. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 9, 1940
CHI. AMALA,
I will keep in mind what you say and do the needful.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. MARGARETE SPIEGEL
IVANHOE
OPP. BACK BAY BATHS
BOMBAY, FORT
From the Gujarati original: Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
