92. LETTER TO M. K. ACHARYA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 6, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have now been able to go through your pamphlet. I must
confess that it does not carry conviction. The statements you
make about untouchability are wholly unsupported. The solution
you suggest is no solution. Your meaning of prarabdha is such
that if it were true there would be no scope left for mutual help,
and every atrocity on earth would be justifiable. And therefore
all the declamations against Europeans of South Africa for their
treatment of our countrymen would be found to be wholly
wrong. Nor in my opinion have you presented the present move-
ment against untouchability in a just manner.
Yours sincerely,
SF. M. K. ACHARYA
10 D, QUEENSWAY
RAISINA, DELHI
