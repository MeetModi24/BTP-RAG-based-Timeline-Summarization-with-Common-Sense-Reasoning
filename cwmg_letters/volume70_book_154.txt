154. LETTER TO INDU N. PAREKH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 27, 1939
CHI. INDU,
Is there no limit to your foolishness? If you would listen
to me, get out of all this entanglement and plunge yourself in
whatever little service you can do. This is the only way to come
up. Your brothers will look after themselves. Tell Bapasaheb
about the shortage. Of course you will certainly speak to the
Sardar. Personally I should like you to free yourself at the earliest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6257
