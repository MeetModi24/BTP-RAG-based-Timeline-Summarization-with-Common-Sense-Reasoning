168. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
December 18, 1936
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I have received your telegram. Why do you insist on com-
ing to Faizpur? Come to see me at Segaon and talk to me
there. You may stay on if you like the place. Why do you have
misgivings when I have already told you that I shall keep you
142
in Segaon? In spite of all this if you want to come to Faizpur
do come. I expect to be back in Segaon on the 29th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 366
