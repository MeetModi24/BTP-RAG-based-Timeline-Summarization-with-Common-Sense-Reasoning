93. LETTER TO INDULAL YAGNIK
November 12, 1923
BHAISHRI INDULAL,
Read this carefully and show it to Abdul Gani. Make any
suggestions you would like regarding language, etc. I see no
alternative but to give up oranges and raisins. I am not at all
convinced that it is necessary to take them. Even if I were to
lose a few pounds in weight, that would be nothing compared to the
satisfaction I would derive. I see that if I am to be true to my
nature, I can do nothing else. I have waited long for J.
MOHANDAS
From a handwritten draft of the Gujarati : S.N. 8038
