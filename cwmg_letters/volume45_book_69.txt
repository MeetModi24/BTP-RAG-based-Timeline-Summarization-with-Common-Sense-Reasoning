69. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 3, 1931
CHI. MANI,
I have your letter.
If you meet Father, tell him that I envy his lot, for he is
both in and out of the house of rest and is able to visit the doctor
every day. He never received such delicate attention so long as
he was a free man. And I hope that in this way he will get rid
for good of the trouble in the teeth and in the nose.
Will he be my neighbour this time too?
If you meet Rajendrababu, ask him to write to me. Did he
get my reply to his letter?
Keep on giving me all the news as long as you are not ar-
rested.
Dahyabhai seems to have taken a vow never to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, pp. 47-
