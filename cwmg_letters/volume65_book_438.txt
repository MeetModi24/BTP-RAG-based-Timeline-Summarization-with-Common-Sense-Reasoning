438. LETTER TO INDIRA NEHRU
July 10, 1937
CHI. INDU,
Whatever you may be to others, I find you a lazy-bone.
Kamala was never forgetful! Jawaharlal gave me news of you.
Why are you still so delicate in health? You must build up
your physique. I was hoping that you would come over here.
Write to me in detail.
How is Mummy1? Where is Sarup2?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Gandhiji-Indira Gandhi Correspondence. Court-
