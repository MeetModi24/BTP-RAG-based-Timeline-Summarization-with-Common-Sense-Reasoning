116. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
June 26, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
After I had half torn up Prema's letter, which I am en-
closing, I remembered that I should be sending it to you. Hence
I joined the pieces.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W.8497. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
