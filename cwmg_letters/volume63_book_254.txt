254. LETTER TO SAHEBJI MAHARAJ
August 5, 1936
DEAR SAHEBJI MAHARAJ,
Many thanks for your letter. We shall await the mistry on the
12th. He will be paid his travelling expenses to and fro and
the pay will begin as from the date of his departure from
Agra.
I am expecting much out of this experiment.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 2165
