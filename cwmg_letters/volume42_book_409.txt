409. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 24, 1930
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. You may come whenever you wish to.
I shall be here up to the 2nd of March, if I am not arrested
before then. On the 2nd is Rukhi's wedding.
How did Kamala develop constipation? Never forget that
it should not be tolerated even for a day.
Gangabehn must be giving you all the news about the ebb
and flow here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9275
