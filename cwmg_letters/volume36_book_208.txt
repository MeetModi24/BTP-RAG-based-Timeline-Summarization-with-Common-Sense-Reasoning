208. LETTER TO RAMI GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 3, 1928
CHI. RAMI,
I have your letter. The handwriting this time cannot be
considered good. The lines are not straight. You should improve
your health. Look forward to doing a good bit of khadi work
during the National Week.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9707
