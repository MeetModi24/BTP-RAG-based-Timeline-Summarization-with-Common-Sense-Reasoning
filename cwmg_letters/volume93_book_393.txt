393. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
RAJKOT,
May 13, 1939
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
Please fix up with T. Bapa about the donation referred to by
you.
Yours,
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA, M. L. A.
LAHORE
From the original : Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy
