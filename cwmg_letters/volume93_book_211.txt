211. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 30, 1937
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have already replied to your letter about the question of
franchise. Remit Rs. 3,600 to the Gandhi Seva Sangh. Kishore-
lal will then consider the matter in consultation with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
