296. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
DELHI,
March 13, 1936
MY DEAR ANAND,
Of course you come when you can. Only here in the Harijan
colony you may be crowded out. There will be an influx of
visitors presently. And of me you will have precious little in the
way of talking. Hope you are both getting well.
Love.
BAPU
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
