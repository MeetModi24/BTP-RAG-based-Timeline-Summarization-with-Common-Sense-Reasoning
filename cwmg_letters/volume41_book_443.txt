443. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
[End of September 1929]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL (JOSHI),
I have had no letter from you for two days. You should not
understand from this that you must write every day even when
there is nothing to write about.
I return the bill of the Khalishpur Ashram. The khadi which
we have received from there was not ordered by us. If, however
you think that its cost is as shown in the bill, remit the amount.
Write to me and let me know what the quality of the khadi and of
the yarn is.
Has the elder Gangabehn calmed down? What happened
about Yashodadevi?
See how things are with Krishnamaiyadevi. If you think it
advisable to ask her to leave, let her go. If you can take Mahavir
into confidence and find out why those people are frequently ill,
try to know it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 146
