151. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI N. PATEL
January 22, 1933
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I got your letter of the 20th. You may fix the day with me
in advance and come, with or without Lalita. You can write to
me anything you wish to in this matter. I did not get your
previous letters. If I had received them, I would not have left
them unanswered.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8998
