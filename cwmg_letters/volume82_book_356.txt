356. LETTER TO TARA N. MASHRUWALA
SODEPUR,
December 22, 1945
CHI. TARA,
I have your letter.
You will succeed in your work with the K. G. N. M. Trust.
Help Sushila rid herself quickly of the gall-stone. She will be all
right. Sometimes diagnosis of stone, etc., turns out to be wrong.
It has been observed that doctors also commit errors.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TARABEHN MASHRUWALA
AKOLA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
