535. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
March 31, 1938
CHI. LILA,
I am sorry that I will not be able to speak today.
But it
is as well that it is so. I have already written to Ba and made
my suggestion regarding Kanam. If she permits you to take
charge of him, do so. If you do, you should give up the kit-
chen work. You may give some help if necessary. Mix well
with Nanavati. Talk less. Don't speak thoughtlessly. Never
shout at anybody. Our servants are not mere servants. Treat
them as your own little brothers and sisters. Cultivate cordial
relations with Mirabehn. She is doing very good work at present.
Continue to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9370. Also C.W. 6645. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
