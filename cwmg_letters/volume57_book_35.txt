35. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
January 22, 1934
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have your letter.
Though difficult it is our dharma to
bear the unbearable.
I am myself having tangible experience of
the difficulty just now. Only by passing through the ordeal can
our study of the Gita prove its worth.
Let us see what happens and where God leads us.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am writing this from Kumarika.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1707
