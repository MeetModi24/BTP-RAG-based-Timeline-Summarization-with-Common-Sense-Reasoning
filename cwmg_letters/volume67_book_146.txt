146. LETTER TO E. RAGHAVENDRA RAO
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 2, 1938
DEAR RAGHAVENDRA RAO,
I must thank you for your frank letter. As you must have
seen, such of your objections as have force have been antici-
pated by the Working Committee. But it seems to me that you
are judging a big institution by the shortcomings of individual
members. The Congress has to be judged by its capacity of
resistance to the foreign exploitation of the country. In that test
I hope that even the present ministry won't be found wanting.
I invite you to return to the Congress fold and fight impurity
wherever you see it with all your might.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
