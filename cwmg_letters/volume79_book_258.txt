258. LETTER TO SHRIRAM PODDAR
SEVAGRAM,
February 20, 1945
CHI. SHRIRAM1,
Your letter is clear enough.
Should I ask a person who is fond of drinking to drink?
I shall not suggest what you should do. Do what your heart
prompts you to do. Do not listen to your father, or to me or
to anyone else. Follow only the promptings of your heart. Per-
haps in that alone lies your good.
It is not at all necessary for you to come here. I shall how-
ever say that when even the educated are resorting to artificial
therapy who is going to find fault with you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : C.W. 812
