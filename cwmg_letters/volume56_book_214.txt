214. LETTER TO MAHENDRA AND SUDARSHAN DESAI
WARDHA,
November 7, 1933
CHI. MANU AND MAVO,
I hope you got my letter of New Year blessings. Write to
me occasionally. Study with great care. My tour begins from
tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3161. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
