255. LETTER TO SIR EVANS GORDON
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 26, 1906
DEAR SIR,
Mr. Ally and I are greatly obliged to you for your letter of the
26th instant. Under separate cover we have sent you a breakfast invita-
tion card as also a draft constitution of the Committee. We trust
that you will be able to spare the time for attending the breakfast.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
MAJOR SIR W. EVANS GORDON 2
4, CHELSEA EMBANKMENT, S.W.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4643
