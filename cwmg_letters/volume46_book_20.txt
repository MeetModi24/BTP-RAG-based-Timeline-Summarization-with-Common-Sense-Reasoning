20. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
BARDOLI,
April 22, 1931
It grieves me to have to bother you again. But the Settle-
ment, so far as Gujarat is concerned, is in imminent danger of a
breakdown. I enclose copies of the correspondence between the
Bombay Government and myself. I am straining every nerve to
prevent a breakdown. I am trying all the powers of persuasion
I can command. But the strain is proving unbearable.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Home Department, Political, File No. 33/XI & KWs/1931. Courtesy:
National Archives of India
