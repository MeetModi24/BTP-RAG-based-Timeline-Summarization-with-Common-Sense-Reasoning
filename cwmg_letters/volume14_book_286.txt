286. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
AHMEDABAD,
May, 1918
CHI. MAGANLAL,
The Government is fighting a war. It is in difficulties. We,
that is to say, the people, want swaraj through its co-operation.
We cannot have swaraj until we have made ourselves fit for it.
One aspect of this fitness is surely that we should share its bur-
dens. Our object in running the Ashram is to strengthen the
character of the inmates. Is it not, then, the duty of the Ashram
to offer its help? The end of the war will see us better qualified
[for swaraj]. I think we young people should go. One of us will
stay behind with the children. Let us know, soon, what you think
of this.
Blessings from
BAPU
ps. If we go, we take others as well.
