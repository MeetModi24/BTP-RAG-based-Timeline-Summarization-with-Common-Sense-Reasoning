82. LETTER TO G. B. VEERASWAMY
AHMEDABAD,
May 20, 1915
DEAR VEERASWAMY,
I have your letter. I shall know in due course what you
propose doing.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
G. B. VEERASWAMI
1/ NALLATHAMBHI MUDALI STREET
TRIPLICANE
MADRAS
From a photostat: C.W. 11014. Courtesy: Balachandran
