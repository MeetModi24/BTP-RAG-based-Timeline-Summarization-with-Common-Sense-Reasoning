253. LETTER TO EMMA HARKER
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 28, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your letter. You can come and see the Ashram for
yourself. But I know that the Ashram life will not suit you in
any shape or form. It is really too simple and too hard for one
brought up like you. It is hard even for those who have been
for a long time here. And it is a life of continuous body labour.
Yours sincerely,
EMMA HARKER
2 BELGRADE TERRACE, KARACHI
From a microfilm: S.N. 13502
