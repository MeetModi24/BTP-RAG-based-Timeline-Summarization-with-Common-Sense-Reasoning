416. LETTER TO NAVINCHANDRA N. DESAI
March 2, 1937
CHI. NAVIN,
I got your letter. You seem to be doing good work. May
you succeed in your effort. The boys and girls always have my
blessings. Guard their character. May they ever grow in body,
mind and spirit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2171
