308. LETTER TO SUDHIR GHOSH
DELHI,
October 7, 1947
MY DEAR SUDHIR,
Your note.
I hope you have my message as also my wire.
I am quite clear. You should leave that place. More from
Horace.
Reuter's summary is quite fair. You can't help people who
would see red in everything. I hope however the storm has
blown over.
I am glad Shanti has passed one test.
Love.
BAPU
Sudhir Ghosh Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
