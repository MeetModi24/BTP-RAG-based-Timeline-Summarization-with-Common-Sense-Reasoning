200. LETTER TO NATVARLAL J. VEPARI
June 29, 1941
BHAI NATVARLAL,
I learn from the office that this is the third or fourth letter
from you [though] containing fewer criticisms. Please warn your
men.
I am sending your letters to Mahadevbhai. Since he has
had a talk with you, you should write direct to him at Harijan
Ashram, Sabarmati. He will reply to you. The point is that
it will be enough if you give a report on the basis of the ma-
terial you have received. For finally I myself will have to look
into everything.
I will, therefore, take your [help] if I par-
ticularly need it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 10121
