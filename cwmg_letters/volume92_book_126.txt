126. LETTER TO RAMPRASAD VYAS
SABARMATI,
September 1, 1929
BHAI RAMPRASAD,
I have your letter. Herewith the acknowledgment. Both the
items of work are good. That relating to Daridranarayana is wider
in scope.
Whatever others may do, you cannot offer a bribe. I would
see nothing wrong in entering in the accounts such expenditure
as is incurred purely in connection with the business.
The problem of those with large families is not one to be
discussed in Navajivan. It raises many other questions. It is of
no importance compared to the great misfortune of India's slavery.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/145
