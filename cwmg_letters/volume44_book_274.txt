274. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI M. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 2, 1930
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
As advised by the doctor, do take a full month's rest. The
work of service will never end. If you become stronger, you will
be able to work better. Moreover, our programme is such that
wherever we are, we can do some work. Write to me regu-
larly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8989
44-13
