40. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
[Before September 30, 1933]
CHI. KAKA,
. . .2
I have been thinking of handing over the Ashram permanently
to the All-India Harijan Sevak Sangh for use as a Harijan settle-
ment, a Harijan hostel, office of the Harijan Sevak Sangh and
a tannery. What do you think about it? Jamnalalji liked the
idea very much. Chhaganlal, Chimanlal and Maganbhai also
have liked it.
Let me know your plans.
I am fixed here up to October 15 for reasons of health.
I am well on the whole. I have brought a large army with
me. Anandi, Bachu and Babu have accompanied me, as also
Prabhudas. Mohan and Vanamala have gone to Kathlal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9478. Courtesy: D. B. Kalelkar
