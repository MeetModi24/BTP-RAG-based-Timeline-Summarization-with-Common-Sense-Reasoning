98. LETTER TO GOVIND MISRA
CAMP KHURJA,
November 3, 1929
DEAR GOVIND BABU,
Mr. Banker has sent me copy of your letter to him regard-
ing Niranjan Babu. Your letter is vague and general. If you
have really anything against Niranjan Babu that you can sub-
stantiate, you must be absolutely precise and give full particulars
which you can establish. When did you first entertain doubts
about his honesty?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. GOVIND MISRA
GANDHI SEVASHRAM
CHAMPAPURHAT, DT. CUTTACK
From a microfilm: S.N. 15675
