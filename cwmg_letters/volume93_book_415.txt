415. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
ON THE TRAIN,
AFTER ITARSI,
July 28, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
At Mathura yesterday I posted you a letter. You will have
received it. I asked you to meet Devdas. You should not worry
about anything. Keep writing to me. There have been many
visitors every night.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
