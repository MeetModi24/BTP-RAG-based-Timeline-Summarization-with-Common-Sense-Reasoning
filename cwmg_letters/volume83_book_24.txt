24. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
MADRAS,
January 24, 1946
CHI. MRIDU,
I have your letter of January 11, 1946. I have been very
busy and it is nearly six in the morning. What is being done
for women seems to be good. Jawaharlal has done a very
good job.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MRIDULA SARABHAI
SHAHIBAG
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
