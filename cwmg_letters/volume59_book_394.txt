394. LETTER TO DUNCAN GREENLEES
November 14, 1934
DEAR DUNCAN,
I was glad to receive your letter. I shall follow the develop-
ment of your idea of rural school with great interest.
Of course,
I know your love for India.
You may depend upon my ex-
ploiting it as much as I can. At the present moment Frontier is
on the horizon. When a corporate fast comes, you will certainly
find your name in the list of the Brotherhood. Both the Marys
are doing well.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
