497. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
BORSAD,
June 17, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
I thank you for your telegram about Khan Saheb Abdul
Ghaffar Khan. I have shown it to him.
I expect to hear from you in due course with reference to
my desire to visit the Frontier Province.
Yours sincerely,
H. W. EMERSON, ESQ.
SIMLA
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
