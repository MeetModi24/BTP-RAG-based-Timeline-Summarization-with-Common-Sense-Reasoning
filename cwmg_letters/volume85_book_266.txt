266. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
NEW DELHI,
August 30, 1946
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have not been able to write to you two. However I
often think of you. I shall therefore only express the hope in
this letter that both of you are calm. Whatever you do, do
as a part of your duty. I hope that you will make each other
happy, spending the least time in idle talk, and enjoy dreamless
and sound sleep at night. Your daily activities must be going
on as regularly as clock-work. You will also be improving
your Urdu. I am not in a position yet to say when I shall be
free from here. I am trying to make it as early as possible.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8392. Also C. W. 7216. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
