302. LETTER TO TARINI P. SINHA
NANDI HILLS,
May 6, 1927
MY DEAR TARINI,
I was delighted to have your letter. Yours has been a protrac-
ted illness. I hope, however, that you will soon regain your original
vigour. You will not overtax yourself with your resumed studies.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1568
