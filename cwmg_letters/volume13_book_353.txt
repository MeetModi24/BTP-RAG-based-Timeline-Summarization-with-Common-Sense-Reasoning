353. LETTER TO FULCHAND SHAH
MOTIHARI,
Ashadh Sud 11 [June 30, 1917]
BHAI FULCHAND,
I gave you one letter of Mrs. Besant to keep. I am sending
you another. I hope you are preserving cuttings from the papers
there.
Miss Faering's letter this time is worth reading. Her love for
the Ashram adds to our responsibility. We would do well to culti-
vate and maintain the purity which comes naturally to a heart
of transparent honesty like hers. Such striving is the only justi-
fication for our existence.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
The very day I wrote to you, Surendra fell ill. The mis-
take was mine. I gave him full freedom all at once. His stomach
had not improved enough to digest everything. He fasted yester-
day. Today he is better. Everyone here has observed ekadashi to-
day.
From a photostat of the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 6364
