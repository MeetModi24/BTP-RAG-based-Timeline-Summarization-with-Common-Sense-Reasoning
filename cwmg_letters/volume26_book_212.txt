212. LETTER TO ASSOCIATED PRESS OF INDIA
March 24, 1925
In handing the above correspondence for publication, it is
only necessary for me to point out that the agreement embodied
therein marks a step forward in the movement now going on at
Vykom. The satyagrahis respecting the boundary line, in spite
of the removal of the barrier and removal also of the order of pro-
hibition, would, on the one hand, show the completely non-
violent nature of the struggle and, on the other, prove the bona
fides of the Government declaration that they favour the reform
for which the satyagrahis are fighting. I am hoping that the
opponents of the reform will reciprocate the gesture made by the
satyagrahis.
The Hindu, 24-3-1925
