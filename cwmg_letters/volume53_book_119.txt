119. LETTER TO KIKABHAI WAGHELA
January 19, 1933
BHAI KIKABHAI,
I got your letter. Please do not be impatient. I will do the
best I can. I did not wish to deceive people by using the word
'Harijan'. Since the word Dhed and others like that in common
use are not proper, I accepted the word 'Harijan' at the request
of one or two Antyaja friends. You may rest assured that I am ready
to lay down my life in order to wipe out this blot. Though the
Harijans are despised today, they will soon become respected.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 87
