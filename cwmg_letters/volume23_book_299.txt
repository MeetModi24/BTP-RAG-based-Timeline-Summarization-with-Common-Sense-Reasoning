299. LETTER TO JOSEPH BAPTISTA
POST ANDHERI,
April 6, 1924
DEAR MR. BAPTISTA1,
I thank you for your letter of the 5th inst.
I wrote to you immediately on receiving your letter. Wednes-
day, like Monday, is my day of silence. You are quite correct in
saying that my views are practically what they were before. At the
same time, if you could find time after next Sunday, I should be
delighted to give you any day between 5 and 6 p.m., except Mon-
days and Wednesdays.
Yours sincerely,
JOSEPH BAPTISTA, ESQ.
MATHARPACADY
MAZAGAON, BOMBAY
From a copy: S.N. 8685
