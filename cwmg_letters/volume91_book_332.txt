332. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Bhadrapad Sud 8 [September 6, 1924]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Your prediction about the person on fast came true. But he
stuck to his decision. His fast was a result purely of ignorance.
Read the [accompanying] letter. Go to receive Polak. He
will be staying only with Petit. Inform him about my programme.
How can I be sure right from now? You will know it on the
18th. My visit to Delhi is not yet certain.
Blessings from
BAPU
