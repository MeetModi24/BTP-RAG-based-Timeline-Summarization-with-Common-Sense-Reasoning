293. LETTER TO REHMAN JIVABHAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 14, 1939
BHAI REHMANJI,
I have your letter.
I am pained but not surprised by
what you write.
Nowadays there is so much mutual distrust
that a Hindu is afraid to employ a Muslim who is a stranger.
That fear is bound to disappear. Please do not lose heart.
Meet Mridulabehn. Show her this letter.
Regards from
M. K. GANDHI
DRIVER REHMAN JIVABHAI
TOY MARKET, PANKORE NAKA
AHMEDABAD
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9654. Courtesy: Mulubhai
Nautamlal
