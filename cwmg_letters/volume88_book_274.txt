274. LETTER TO MADALASA
NEW DELHI,
July 7, 1947
CHI. MADU,
I have your letter. You must have got my telegram. I hope
Bharat has regained health. Do not let him wander around.
I wish you to get engrossed in the Mahila Ashram work.
That is a major activity among the many activities of Jamna-
lal. One reason for keeping you near by is that you may get
immersed in that work. Now stop having more children if both
of you can exercise self-control so that you can look after the
two you have and manage the Mahila Ashram work. The Ashram
does need a worker like you. If you start doing that work,
Shriman will take more interest in it.
Sushila has come from Wardha today.
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, pp. 333-
