491. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
March 15, 1942
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. Jayaprakash must take milk if he cannot take
curds. It may even be better. He may drink as much milk as he
wants. It would be better to avoid butter for the present. He can
grow some vegetables in the jail garden to meet his needs of
uncooked vegetables. Throughout the year Khan Saheb ate ve-
getables, grown by himself. It is difficult to preserve good health
without greens.
You need not feel unhappy for not being able to stay with
Father. You are wedded to service. You should not worry. I can
see no other way.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3573
