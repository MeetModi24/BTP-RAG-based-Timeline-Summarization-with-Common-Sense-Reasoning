66. LETTER TO CHAND TYAGI
January 3, 1931
BHAI TYAGIJI,
I have your letter. Why does not Balbir write to me? He
should let me know his wishes. He could certainly go to the
Ashram if he really wants to. Who is now the Principal of Prem
Mahavidyalaya? Why has Devasharmaji been transferred to
Kanpur [jail]? Write out your ideas on fasting and other subjects.
We shall see about their publication later on.
Give in detail your daily routine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3267
