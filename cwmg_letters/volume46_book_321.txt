321. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 31, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
I enclose herewith copies of the correspondence that has taken
place between the Bombay Government and myself.
Yours sincerely,
Encls.
H. W. EMERSON, ESQ.
SIMLA
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
