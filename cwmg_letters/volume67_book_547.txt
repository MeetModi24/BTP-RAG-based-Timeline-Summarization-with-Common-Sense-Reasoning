547. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
September 23, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
I got your letter. Do certainly take tub-baths. It is good
indeed that you have left off medicines. That you are not able
to sleep is too much. Give up everything except water for a day,
and see what happens. The cause of insomnia can be nothing
but indigestion.
CHI. VIJAYABEHN
C/O RAMBHAI HIRABHAI PATEL
SAURASHTRA SOCIETY No. 25
ELLIS BRIDGE
AHMEDABAD, B. B. & C. I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7099. Also C.W. 4591. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
