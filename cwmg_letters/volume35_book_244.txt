244. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
BERHAMPUR,
Silence Day [December 5, 1927]
SISTERS,
I got your letter written by Manibehn. I have very little
time to write today. I am convinced that we should not permit
jewels in the Ashram. As long as there is terrible starvation in
our land it is a sin for us to keep or to put on a ring weighing
even a grain. Our clothes must be just sufficient to cover our
nakedness and to protect us against heat and cold. All of you
should try to reach this ideal.
I shall not write today about how the desire for ornaments
arises. It looks as though you have also not understood my
question properly.
How is it that Lakshmibehn is ill? She never used to fall ill.
Blessings from
BAPU
