39. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
June 9, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
You have, I believe, my letter about Kanu. They have
all been very busy sight-seeing. We hear from them from time to
time. The other day they paid us a visit also. Here is another
letter from Prema. You got the earlier too, I hope.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8493. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
