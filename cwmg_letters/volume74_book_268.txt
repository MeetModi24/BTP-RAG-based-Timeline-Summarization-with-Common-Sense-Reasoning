268. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
July 20, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your first letter after Jamnalal's arrival there. Let us hope
the climate of Simla won't worry him.
All well here. Kanchan and Kusum arrived here today.
More from Prabha.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4247. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7879
