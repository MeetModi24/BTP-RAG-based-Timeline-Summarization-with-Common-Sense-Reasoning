238. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
FROM M. K. GANDHI
AHMEDABAD,
May 27 [1916]
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have two letters this week from you. You want all the news
from here. I think I have supplied you with it. Today Harilal's wife,
who has been here for a few days, leaves for Calcutta to join her
husband. Manilal goes as escort. We shall be today 20 in the Ashram
when Chanchi has gone. Maganlal is doing well with his handloom work.
Ramdas and Devdas are going forward with their studies, which consist
of Sanskrit, Gujarati, Tamil and Hindi. I am their sole teacher. Prabhudas
is with his father just now. I do not know what Chhaganlal will do
in the end. Maganbhai has left the Ashram for good. He could not
as he said bear the responsibility. He could not comply with all the
rules. So now Maganlal is the only responsible man left with me barring
Manilal. There are other new men. They are good but still untried.
What I have said may yet come true-everyone may find it necessary
to leave me. I think I have strength to face that situation. You say
you are morose there. Can you not see much in the above to give
you strength and comfort? To be confined ought not to make the

difference it seems to make in your life. Oh, if you could but see there
are millions much worse off than you are. What of the combatants

as it faces you.
With love,
OLD FRIEND
