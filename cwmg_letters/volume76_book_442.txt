442. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
DETENTION CAMP,
November 5, 1942
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
I have just read about the sad but heroic death of Hon'ble
Peter Wood in action. Will you please convey to Lord Halifax
my congratulations as well as condolences on the sad bereave-
ment?
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Transfer of Power, Vol. III, p. 209
