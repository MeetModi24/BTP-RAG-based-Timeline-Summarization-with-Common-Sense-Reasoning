465. LETTER TO UMADEVI AGRAWAL
September 2, 1941
CHI. OM,
At last you have taken the trouble of writing a letter. Kakaji
would soon return now. And with what great and vivid experi-
ence! The description of your place is such that I wish I could
send all my patients to you, not only Janakidevi and Madalasa.
What do you say?
Blessings to both from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, pp. 344-
