543. LETTER TO KRISHNARAJU
SEVAGRAM,
December 3, 1944
BHAI KRISHNARAJU,
My letter-writing activity ceases from tomorrow until the end
of this month. Hence I am sending this letter to convey my good
wishes for your success in your treatment of the three friends.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 10357
