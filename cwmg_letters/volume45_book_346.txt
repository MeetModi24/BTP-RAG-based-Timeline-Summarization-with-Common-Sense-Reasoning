346. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
1 DARYAGANJ, DELHI,
March 8, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
Here is a telegram from Nellore (copy telegram). I suppose
the lathi-charge was due to the fact of the settlement not having
been received there. Anyway I feel that I should bring all such
matters to your notice unless you want me to do otherwise.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
