539. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
June 14, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
Your letter. You must not write when you have run down
to the extent you have. I can never misunderstand absence of
letters from you.
The restrictions are bad. But nothing to be done till you
are with me.
Hope all things will go well regarding family affairs.
I hope to go to Sevagram in the beginning of July. The
weather there may be too trying for you then.
I am well.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
You must not allow Gope or his wife to be a load on you.
From the original: C.W. 4159. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7794
