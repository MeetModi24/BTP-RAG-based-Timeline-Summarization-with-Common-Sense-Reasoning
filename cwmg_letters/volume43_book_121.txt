121. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
SAMNI,
Monday [March 24, 1930]
CHI. MAHADEV,
I send seven articles in this packet. It is about 10 p.m. now.
I have revised the articles, but you should revise them further if
necessary. I do not think I have left out anything. I am sending
with this some letters for Ashram inmates. See that you do not for-
get to pass them on to the persons concerned and do not lose them.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I wished to write to the women there, but all the available
time was taken up by Young India and rest. There are three loose
letters and two envelopes.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11484
