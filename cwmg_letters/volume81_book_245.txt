245. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
MEHTA AROGYA BHAVAN, POONA,
August 22, 1945
CHI. AMRITLAL,
Chi. Abha is going to appear for the examination. If she
has to go to Bombay she can appear for it there. However, if
you appoint Dr. Sushila Nayyar as the examiner she can appear
from here and expense can be avoided. You will have to send
the question paper, as also the necessary instructions. I hope
you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI AMRITLAL NANAVATI
GUJARAT VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
