409. LETTER TO GOVERNING DIRECTOR,
PRINCE PEOPLE & CO.
November 16, 1934
GENTLEMEN,
I thank you for your letter of 11th instant. I would like
to know what work you have already done and what you think
you can do in respect of introducing the use of chakki-ground
flour, hand-husked rice and village gur in your own lives and in
the villages under your care. I would like also to see the con-
stitution of your company.
Yours sincerely,
GOVERNING DIRECTOR
PRINCE PEOPLE & CO.
DARBHANGA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
