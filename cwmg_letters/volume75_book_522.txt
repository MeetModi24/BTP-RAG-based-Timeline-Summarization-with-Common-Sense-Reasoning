522. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
March 25, 1942
BHAI JIVANJI,
It seems we will have to bring out the Urdu Harijan, in
litho. Can this be done there? I think you did point out some
429
difficulty. I am trying to make the arrangements at this end
about the editor and so on. The matter thus has not been for-
gotten. We also wish to run an Urdu press.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JIVANJI
NAVAJIVAN KARYALAYA
P.O.B. 105
AHMEDABAD
B. B. & C.I.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9950. Also C.W. 6925. Cour-
tesy: Jivanji D. Desai
