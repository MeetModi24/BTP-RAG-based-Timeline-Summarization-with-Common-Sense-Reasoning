66. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
SEVAGRAM,
July 27, 1945
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have your letters. It will be good if your presence can
help to settle the dispute there.
Hoshiari is brave. She will succeed. It is good that you are
also there.
I am keeping well. Mirabehn is anxiously awaiting you.
It will be good if you have a look at Dr. Sharma's handi-
work. See what he is doing.
Things are going on well here. By the route which you have
taken you cannot go to Balkrishna.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1965
