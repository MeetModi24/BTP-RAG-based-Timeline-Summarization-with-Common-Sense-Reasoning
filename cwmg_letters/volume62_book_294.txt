294. LETTER TO SHANKERBHAI B. PATEL
DELHI,
March 12, 1936
CHI. SHANKERBHAI,
I indeed made inquiries of you all, but none of you turned
up. It was certainly good that narrow caste bonds were broken.
SHRI SHANKERBHAI
JAMSI VILLA, ELLIS BRIDGE
AHMEDABAD, B. B. & C. I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4088. Also C.W. 51. Courtesy:
Mangalabehn B. Desai
