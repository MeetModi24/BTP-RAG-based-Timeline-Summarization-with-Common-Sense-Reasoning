125. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 27, 1940
CHI. SHARMA,
I got your beautiful letter. Since I wish to demonstrate
on this occasion total ahimsa as I have visualized it, I do not
intend to send more than two or three persons. It is likely that
I may not send any.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah
Varsh, between pp. 292 and 293
