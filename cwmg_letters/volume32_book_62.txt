62. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
Kartika Vad 1 [November 20, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have your letter. May you live long and may your purity
grow. No one in this world is free from imperfection. We can only
strive to get rid of it. You are making an effort in that direction.
A person who makes an honest effort knows no fall. This is the
divine pledge.
We shall now meet on the 4th. I intend to go via Tapti Valley
[Railway]. Shastriar arrives tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2878
