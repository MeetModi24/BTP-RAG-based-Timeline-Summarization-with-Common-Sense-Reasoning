525. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
March 28, 1938
CHI. BHANSALI,
I hope you are doing well. You will be glad to learn that
these days while I do my sacrificial spinning the yarn rarely
snaps. Of course, it does surprise me. You also should try to
spin even, strong and unbroken yarn, though the quantity may
be less, instead of spinning much and for many hours.
It is
good that you are taking the injections. Let us hope that will
put you right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8354
