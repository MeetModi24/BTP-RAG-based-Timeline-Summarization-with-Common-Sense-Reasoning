483. LETTER TO NANALAL KAVI
THE ASHRAM,
September 24, 1926
BHAISHRI NANALAL KAVI,
I have your letter. Thanks. I did not write to you to
invite you to act as a panch, but only requested your help as a
friend. But I see that I have not been able to explain my point
of view. I will, therefore, trouble you no further.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRINIVAS, SIR JAGMOHANDAS'S BUNGALOW
NAPEAN SEA ROAD
BOMBAY
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19951
