423. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
December 1, 1925
DEAR MOTI BABU,
I have your letter of 27th ultimo. The previous letter I have
not received. If I had, I would have sent you may congratula-
tions at once. But it is not too late even now.
Pray accept
them now. I hope that you will have to face no extraordinary
difficulties in the transition stage.
How are you getting on otherwise?
I am going to Wardha today and hope to rest there for ten
days. Please write to me there if necessary care of Sjt. Jamna-
lalji Bajaj.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 11022
