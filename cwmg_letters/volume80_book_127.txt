127. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 5, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
The meeting is on the 17th. I have gone through the
agenda. I want that at least the four of us should be of one
opinion and finish the work soon. So, if possible, let me have the
opinions of the three of you on all the topics or of those available.
Why is the agenda in English? When shall we arrive at Hindi?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHYAMLAL
KASTURBA NIDHI OFFICE
SCINDIA HOUSE
BALLARD ESTATE
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
