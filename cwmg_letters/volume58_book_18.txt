18. LETTER TO P. NIRUPAMA
May 23, 1934
CHI. NIRUPAMA,
I have your letter. The language is good. Ornaments are
unnecessary; they tend to create in the girls fascination for exter-
nal beauty. There should be much less fondness for ornaments
in a poor country. All these reasons go against ornaments.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUMARI P . NIRUPAMA
C/O SHRI P. MANJUNATH NAYAK
SUDAMA KUTEER, UDIPI
