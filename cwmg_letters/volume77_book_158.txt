158. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
JUHU,
June 5, 1944
DEAR DR. JAYAKAR,
I am glad you have returned. I hope to send you in a day
or two the papers I had intended to. I propose that we should
meet after you have studied the papers.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhi-Jayakar Papers. File No. 826, p. 8. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
