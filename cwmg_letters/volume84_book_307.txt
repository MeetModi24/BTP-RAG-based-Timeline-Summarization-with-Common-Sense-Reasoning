307. LETTER TO HOSA RASHID KHAN
MUSSOORIE,
May 31, 1946
CHI. HOSA,
I have your letter. I like it. I am not happy that Amtul
Salaam has left. I know that she can be a great comfort to
you. May God grant you peace.
Why did you write to me in English? Do you not know
245
Hindustani? You could have written to me either in the Deva-
nagari or in the Urdu script.
Blessings from
BAPU
BEGUM RASHID KHAN
RASHID NIVAS
INDORE, C. I.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
