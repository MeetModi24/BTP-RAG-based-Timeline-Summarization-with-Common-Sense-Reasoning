104. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
November 27, 1932
CHI. DUDHIBEHN 1 ,
I got your letter. Just now, both of you should look after
each other and the children. When Valji is completely all right,
I shall be happy if your suggestion is acted upon. With whom
do you live there? I think it would be very good if you could
stay there for a long time.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. V. G. DESAI
SANJAULI, SIMLA HILLS, N.W. RLY.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7444. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
