361. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
Saturday
CHI. NIMU,
Even if all the people there oppose your new diet, you should politely
disregard the opposition and stick to it provided, that is, you have faith in it
and feel that it benefits you.
How did you come to think of Wardha? You do know, do you not, that
there are not many women there.
You should learn to open out your heart when writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
