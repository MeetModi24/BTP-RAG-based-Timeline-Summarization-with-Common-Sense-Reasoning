17. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
August 7, 1944
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
This is a painful story. There is only one remedy for this, viz.,
to act as Chimanlal advises. You should yourself talk it over with
him. Regard me as non-existent. If a difference still remains in
your viewpoints, then please accept his. In no case, however,
should there be ideological differences. If there is any let me
know.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4443
