149. LETTER TO POONAMCHAND RANKA
[February 15, 1946]
BHAI POONAMCHAND 2 ,
I hear that you have deliberately formed a sort of group,
excluding from it those who can be regarded as fully eligible,
whereas you have sent in the names of those who don't deserve
to be returned to the Assembly. I am pained to hear all this.
Why such fascination for the Assembly, why the lure, and that
too for a man like you? Adopt the path of humility, justice and
truthfulness.
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
