168. LETTER TO HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
SEVAGRAM,
August 14, 1946
BHAI MAHTAB,
I got your letter in English. Why in English? In the letter I
had sent to you, one of the complaints was that you yourself
were going to start a mill. If it is so, do you need to ask
for my opinion? In any case I would hardly have appreciated
your reasoning that Orissa, because it is poor, needs a mill.
For, that would mean that every poor region or village should
have mills to remove its poverty. And if such is your view, I
can hardly say anything.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI HAREKRUSHNA MAHTAB
CHIEF MINISTER
ORISSA
