245. LETTER TO JANARDHAN SHARMA
January 29, 1933
MY DEAR JANARDHAN,
I was pleased to receive your letter. God will forgive you
for having thought low of Harijans, if you will abide by your
pledge and continue to treat them as if they were blood brothers
and sisters.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. JANARDHAN SHARMA
C/O B. PANNALALJI
CLERK, CHANDNI CHOWK POST OFFICE
DELHI
From a microfilm: S.N. 19259
