347. LETTER TO RAMA DEVI CHOWDHRY
April 4, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. The correction has been made. Send
me the names and other particulars of the ladies working with
you. Send also your own particulars. I hope the work is done
daily and regularly. I shall have a better idea of it if you give
me your daily programme.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI RAMA DEVI
ANTI-UNTOUCHABILITY LEAGUE
CUTTACK, ORISSA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2787
