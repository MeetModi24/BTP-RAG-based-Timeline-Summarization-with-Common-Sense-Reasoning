500. LETTER TO SHANKARRAO GHATGE
October 27, 1932
The atman, as everyone will agree, is not destroyed with the
body. Similarly the atman exists prior to the birth of the body. If we
accept this, we have no reason to believe that the atman will not
be reborn in a body or that it did not occupy a body in the past.
But to hold that the atman will necessarily inhabit a form because
it is inhabiting one at present would be merely falling in with
the popular belief.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 182
