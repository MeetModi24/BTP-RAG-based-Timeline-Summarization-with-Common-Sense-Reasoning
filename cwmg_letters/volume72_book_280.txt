280. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
BIRLA HOUSE, ALBUQUERQUE ROAD,
NEW DELHI,
July 2, 1940
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
I have to thank you for your very prompt reply to my letter
of 20th ultimo. I thank you too for sending me the full mean-
ing of what you had actually said. In trying to interpret you,
my object was to put your proposals in as favourable a light as I
could. I am thankful for the corrections. I would read your
letter to the Working Committee.
I am glad also that you have no objection to my making a
public appeal to the British people on behalf of non-violence.
Needless to say that I can in no way associate your name with
my appeal.
Our talks and correspondence shall remain confidential as
desired by you.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a printed copy: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National Archives
