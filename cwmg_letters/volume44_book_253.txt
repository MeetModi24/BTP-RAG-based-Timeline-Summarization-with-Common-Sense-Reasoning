253. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 27, 1930
CHI. GANGABEHN (SENIOR),
The responsibility on you is great. God will give you the
strength to shoulder the burden.
Ambalal wrote to Kakasaheb. The latter is in excellent health.
He spins and cards. He walks at regular hours and eats normal food.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Ramibai has got what she wanted. It becomes her well
indeed.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- 6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 42; also C.W. 8759. Courtesy: Ganga-
behn Vaidya
