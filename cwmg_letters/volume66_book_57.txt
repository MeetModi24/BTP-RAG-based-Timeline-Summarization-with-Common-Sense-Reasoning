57. LETTER TO GOKULDAS
WARDHA,
August 13, 1937
BHAI GOKULDAS,
It is the right and duty of the subjects to complain to the
State against the menace of leopards and wild pigs. One should
be polite and reasonable in whatever one does.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
