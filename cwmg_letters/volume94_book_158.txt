158. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
December 6, 1941
CHI. TARAMATI
I have your letter as also Harkisan's. You must send me news
every day. Bhaskar Patel has a sanatorium there, hasn't he ? Has he
examined him (Mathuradas) ? You should give up all worry and nurse
him cheerfully.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TARAMATI MATHURADAS
WINDI HALL
DEOLALI
G. I. P. RAILWAY
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
