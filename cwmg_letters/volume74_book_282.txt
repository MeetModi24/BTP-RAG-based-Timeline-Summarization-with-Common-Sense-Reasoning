282. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 23, 1941
CHI. AMRITLAL,
You are fast leaving the years behind you and I still believe
that you are the same today that you were when I knew you first.
How can I shed this illusion? Remain the same that you were
and put time to shame.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10801
