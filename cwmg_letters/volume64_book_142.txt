142. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
December 11, 1936
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your two letters in front of me. I understand about
Parameshwari and I have sent him a copy of your letter advising
him to give up the Delhi farm. I too understand that the finan-
cial aspect is not important. The important question for me is
one of honour and right judgment. I must not abuse your con-
fidence and generosity nor permit anyone else to do so. Let us
see what happens. I understand your point about Travancore.
Nevertheless it was unnecessary for me to do more than I did.
I express my impressions as they occur.
As regards the
current developments you will read what I have written in the
Harijan.
Thakkar Bapa writes that you are somewhat indisposed.
What is it? And why? Dates are coming regularly and the rug
has been received. It is quite warm.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8026. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
