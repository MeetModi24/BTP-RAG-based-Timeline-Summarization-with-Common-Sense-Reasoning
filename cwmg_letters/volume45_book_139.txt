139. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
January 18, 1931
CHI. GANGABEHN,
When children grow up, they become our friends. One can
only advise a friend. He may then do what he thinks best, and one
should not mind if he does so. Moreover, we hold all human
beings in equal regard; how, then, can we be pleased with some
and offended with some others? You should not, therefore, feel
hurt about Kaku in any way.
Take care of your feet. You should keep them in warm water
for a long time and then apply kokum oil. That will be enough.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 8770. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
