404. LETTER TO NARASINHRAO B. DIVETIA
[Before October 14, 1932]
All of us were pained to read about your daughter's death.
Mahadev told me that she was the only one left to you. I need
not offer my condolences to you. Both of you possess true
knowledge. May God grant you peace.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 146
