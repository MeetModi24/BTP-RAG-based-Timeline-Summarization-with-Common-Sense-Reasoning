118. LETTER TO RABINDRANATH TAGORE
NEW DELHI,
April 2, 1939
DEAR GURUDEV,
I have your letter full of tenderness. The problem you set
before me is difficult. I have made certain suggestions to
Subhas. I see no other way out of the impasse.
I do hope you are keeping your strength.
Charlie is still in the hospital.
With love.
Yours,
M. K . GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 4650
