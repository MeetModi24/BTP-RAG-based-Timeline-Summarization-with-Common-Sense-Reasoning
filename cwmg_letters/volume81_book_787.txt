787. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
October 29, 1945
BHAI MUNSHI,
I got your letter and a copy of the speech just now. I had
read somewhere about your having written Gujarati-Hindi. I was
very pleased. It is good, moreover, that everything went off
well. I hope you are in good health. I have always written
samun sutharun without knowing the origin of the phrase. You
have now taught me that it is same sutare . Why need this
origin displease me?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7689. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
