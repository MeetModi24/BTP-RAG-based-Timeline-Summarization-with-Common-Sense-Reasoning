218. LETTER TO HARIBHAU JOSHI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 13, 1945
BHAI JOSHI,
I have your letter and also the book. I shall read it and
do what is necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HARIBHAU JOSHI
"LOKSHAKTI',
291 SHANIVAR PETH
POONA 2
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
