345. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
March 12, 1945
CHI. MRIDU,
You are perfectly entitled to come to me and understand my action.
You need not come immediately. Come whenever you can and take
my time. It is true that I am fed up. It is not any more necessary
that things should be explained to the public at large in the way it
can understand. But I am not applying this to you. Yashodhara is very
sensible. She could have asked me. But it is amazing how hesitant
women are. I shall not issue my statement right now. I have a telegram
from Mathuradas. I made my decision only on that basis. Now I have
your letter. So the matter is ended. The sin or merit of the postponement
will be divided between you two. If it is merit, would I also not have
my share? From my point of view, I have done nothing wrong. When
I come to know, I shall proclaim it from the house-tops. Do not be
upset. I am what I have always been.
Blessings from
BAPU
