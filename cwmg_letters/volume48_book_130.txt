130. LETTER TO ALBERT EINSTEIN
LONDON,
October 18, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to have your beautiful letter sent through
Sundaram. It is a great consolation to me that the work I am
doing finds favour in your sight. I do indeed wish that we
could meet face to face and that too in India at my Ashram.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PROF. EINSTEIN
From a photostat: C.W. 9500
