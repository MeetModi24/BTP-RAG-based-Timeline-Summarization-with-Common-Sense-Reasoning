161. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
Silence Day [September 1, 1930]
CHI. MOTIBEHN,
Ba told me that you feel depressed. Why should you? One
who reads the Gita and reflects over its teaching should never
feel depressed. How can that man or woman who daily medi-
tates on God and believes that He always dwells in our hearts
ever feel depressed? Try and get rid of your depression.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3736
