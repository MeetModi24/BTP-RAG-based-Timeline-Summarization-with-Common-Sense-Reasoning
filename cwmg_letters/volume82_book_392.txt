392. LETTER TO HARIPRASAD SHASTRI
MAHISHADAL,
December 26, 1945
BHAI HARIPRASAD SHASTRI,
I have your letter. Why did you write in English? What
I am doing is also for the sake of Truth. Who knows what
consummation will serve Truth. It is better to keep on doing
what we consider our duty irrespective of the result.
SHRI HARIPRASAD SHASTRI
SHANTI SADAN
30 LANSDOWNE CRESCENT
LONDON—W II
