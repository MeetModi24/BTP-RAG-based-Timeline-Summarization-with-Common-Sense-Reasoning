63. LETTER TO JAGANNATH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 9, 1940
MY DEAR JAGANNATH,
Surely Dr. Gopichand needs no introduction or certificate
from me. That he is the agent for everything I stand for is more
than enough. Don't worry for him. In the Punjab, he is his own
introduction and advertisement. I am glad he is free from the
Parliamentary burden. I shall expect great things from him for
constructive work.
Please hand the enclosed to Shakuntala Devi.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 985. Courtesy: Lala Jagannath
