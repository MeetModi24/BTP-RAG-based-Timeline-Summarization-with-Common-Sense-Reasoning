181. LETTER TO U. GOPALA MENON
December 6, 1932
MY DEAR GOPALA MENON,
I have your letter. I do not want you to be writing to me
when you are so fagged out that you want immediately to go off to
sleep. Your sleep is more precious in these days of strain and stress
than mere writing to me. When writing is an absolute necessity
of course you have to sacrifice sleep, but in that case you must use
your words like a miser, and if you can manage with one sentence,
do not give me two, and send only a postcard, and in emergent
cases you may also use the wire. In other words, please conserve
your energy for the work before you. One person alone giving
me the necessary report of work from day to day will be quite
satisfactory.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 18663
