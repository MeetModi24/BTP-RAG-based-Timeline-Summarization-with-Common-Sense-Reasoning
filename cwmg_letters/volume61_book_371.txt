371. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
July 15, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
Herewith an extract from Devsharmaji's letter which concerns
you. I would advise you to engage this gentleman. That will part-
ly satisfy your urgent need for a Hindi teacher. Write to him
directly [at]: Gandhi Sevashram, Post. Gurukul Kangri, Saharanpur
District.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9085
