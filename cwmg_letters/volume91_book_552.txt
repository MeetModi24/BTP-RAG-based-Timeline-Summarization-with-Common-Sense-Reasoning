552. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
KASHI,
Monday [January 10, 1927]
DEAR HARIBHAU,
I have received your letter. There is no reason to be un-
happy about the resolution passed by the school committee with
regard to Martand. I personally feel that your decision to keep
him there is best. But if you wish to send him to Wardha there
should be no difficulty. It would be as well to know what
Martand himself wants.
My health is fine. I am going to Bihar today. Devdas is
with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Haribhau Upadhyaya Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
