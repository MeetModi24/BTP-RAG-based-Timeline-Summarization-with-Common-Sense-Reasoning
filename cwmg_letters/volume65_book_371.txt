371. LETTER TO BABURAO D. MHATRE
June 22, 1937
DEAR MR. MHATRE,
I am glad of your report which will be very helpful in
regulating traffic and in repairing, if it is possible, the bad
roads. I am passing on your report to the Sardar.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: C.W. 9827. Courtesy: Baburao D. Mhatre
