62. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
SEVAGRAM,
January 16, 1945
CHI. BABUDI,
I read your postcard addressed to Father and Mother . You
have lost your common sense. It is fair neither to Vallabhraibhai
nor to you that he should send you medicines by post. You have
tried homoeopathy. We have an allopath here, as also a vaid.
A homoeopath also may join. I am also here. If, therefore,
you can summon up courage, come over. Resolve that you will
return only when fully recovered or when I admit defeat. But
don't come merely because I ask you to. Come only if you are
fully convinced that my advice is correct, for only then will you
benefit.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10050. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokha-
wala
