497. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C.P.,
September 7, 1940
CHI. JAMNALAL,
What does the enclosed letter say? You may do whatever
you consider proper.
I hope Rajendra Babu is well.
How is your health?
Haribhau has written to me. I shall write about it in Harijan
Sevak.
Blessings from
BAPU
