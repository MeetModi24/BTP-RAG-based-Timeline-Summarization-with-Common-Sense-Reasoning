229. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
April 7, 1927
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I have your sweet letter. You will see that the tour is cancel-
led for the time being. I assure you that I shall take no rash
steps and try my best to give the system rest. Pray do not worry
on my account. Do you, Hemaprabha Devi, the boy and Tarini
get well?
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
Amboli till 18th instant and then Mysore
SJT. SATIS CHANDRA DAs GUPTA
HOME VILLA
GIRIDIH
From a photostat: G.N. 1566
