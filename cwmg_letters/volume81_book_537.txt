537. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
October 2, 1945
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter. I am well. I intend to reach Calcutta
on November 2. I shall be staying in Sodepur with Satis Babu.
Reach there a little earlier or on the same day. We shall think
about what you should read.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati G.N. 3583
