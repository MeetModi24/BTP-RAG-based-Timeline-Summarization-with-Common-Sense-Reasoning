403. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 4, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letters. I am not worried. I can't feel at ease
as long as your cough persists. There is fever too. Ramanama
should cure both. I will not compel you to break the fast.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi G. N. 552
