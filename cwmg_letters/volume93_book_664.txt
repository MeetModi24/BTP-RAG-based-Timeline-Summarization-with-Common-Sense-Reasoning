664. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
February 11, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you even today! I had sent you
word in a letter to Tari yesterday. Shastriji is indeed in a bad
shape. All the rest are doing well. My weight has increased
by one lb. this week.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
