489. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
February 20, 1933
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
Your desire is noble and your efforts are wonderful. You
have put your discoveries before Narandas and me. Now listen
to what Narandas says and go on doing your work with single-
minded devotion. Others also will follow your example and all
shortcomings will disappear.
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 352. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
