556. LETTER TO E. E. DOYLE
Immediate
January 10, 1933
DEAR COL. DOYLE,
I had hoped to receive your answer by this time (11 a.m.)
in reply to my inquiry about Appasaheb Patwardhan. I would
like you please to answer my query today and allay, if you will,
the growing anxiety.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
