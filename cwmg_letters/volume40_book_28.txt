28. LETTER TO KANTHIMATHI
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 24, 1929
MY DEAR KANTI,
I have your letter. If your mother-in-law is so unreasonable,
I suppose it is better that you let her buy two Bangalore saries.
You will wear them only when you find it absolutely necessary for
the sake of your mother-in-law. I am sure that if you persist,
you will infect your surroundings with your faith in khadi.
Yours sincerely
SMT. KANTHIMATHI
C/O DR. C. B. RAMA RAO
KANTHI NIVAS
BASAVANGUDI
BANGALORE CITY
From a microfilm: S.N. 15012
