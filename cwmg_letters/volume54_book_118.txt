118. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA
March 17, 1933
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRA,
I have your postcard. Here is a letter for N.
Though as a vegetarian I do not like the idea of poultry-
farming and chicken egg-selling, I am unable to say that no
Harijan Seva Organization should do so, and if you feel like intro-
ducing that industry, you should unhesitatingly do so. Is this
ordinary hatching of eggs or is it the new innocent method of
the laying of sterile eggs?
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20598
