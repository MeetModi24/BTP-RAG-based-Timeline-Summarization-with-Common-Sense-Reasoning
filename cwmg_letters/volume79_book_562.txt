562. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
March 29, 1945
BHAI SATISBABU,
What I told you is correct and what I told Kaviraj is also
correct. It would be best if the villagers could manage with the
herbs available locally. Your work is stabilized and your question
is good. The rest when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1641
