410. LETTER TO MANUBEHN S. MASHRUWALA
August 6, 1940
CHI. MANUDI 1 ,
It is but natural that you should expect my letters. But
where is the time? Yes, I do assume that even if I don't write to
you, you will not chide me in your heart. I do get news of you
from time to time. Are you not coming here some time? Do
not wait for my invitation. Ba's should be sufficient. Do not
come now even if Ba calls you. Wait till the weather improves.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANUBEHN MASHRUWALA
"BALKIRAN"
SANTA CRUZ, B.B. & C.I. RLY.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2677. Courtesy: Kanubhai Mashruwala
