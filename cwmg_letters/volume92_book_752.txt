752. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
February 26, 1934
CHI. NANIBEHN,
I have been expecting your letter, but it has not yet arrived.
Gangabehn too has lapsed into silence, no doubt because she
wants to be kind to me. But what if I did not care for such
kindness? What is the result of your operation? Who did it?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari-Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 28
