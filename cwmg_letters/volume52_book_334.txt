334. LETTER TO PRAGNANESWAR YATI
December 21, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter and the three enclosures. I have gone
through the dedicatory letter, as also Swami Kevalanand's, but
you do not expect me just now to go through the synopsis of your
treatise on transmigration. For me it is a fascinating subject
but there are so many fascinating things that I am obliged to put
aside.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. PARAMAHAMSA PRAGNANESWAR YATI
329 SADASHIV PETH, POONA CITY
