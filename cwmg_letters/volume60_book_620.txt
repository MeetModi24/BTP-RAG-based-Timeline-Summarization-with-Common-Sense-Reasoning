620. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. BAVISHI
April 22, 1935
BHAI PURUSHOTTAM BAVISHI,
I saw your letter yesterday. You may come with your friend
today at two. I shall of course be observing silence, but that
will not matter. For most of the time you will be doing the
explaining.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 122. Also C.W. 4747. Courtesy:
Purushottam K. Bavishi
