629. LETTER TO GULAM RASUL QURESHI
January 1, 1933
CHI. QURESHI,
I would not consider it good health if you were always
constipated. You must get rid of the constipation. Try Ganga-
behn's tablets. Change of diet may help.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10815. Courtesy : Gulam
Rasul Qureshi
