295. LETTER TO FRANCESCA STANDENATH
NEW DELHI,
July 9, 1947
MY DEAR SAVITRI,
Your second letter just received. I was glad.
You may send your answer to Sevagram, Wardha, C. P., India.
We are born to endure hardships. I therefore do not send you
a word of sympathy for all you have gone through. It is enough
that you are cheerful in spite [of it all.] Keep the gold watch.
An English firm has sent me one. Others have offered.
How do you pass your time? Do you earn something?
What is your age?
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
