248. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
NANDI HILLS, MYSORE,
Chaitra Sud 13 [April 14, 1927]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I do think of you very often. How are you getting along
now? When will you be going to the Ashram? How is Vidya-
vati? Whether or not I write to you, you must write regularly to
me. I think I am slightly better now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3329
