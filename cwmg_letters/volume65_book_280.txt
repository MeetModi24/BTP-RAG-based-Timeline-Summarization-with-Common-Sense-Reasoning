280. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
June 2, 1937
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
May you live long and do great service. The sun of hope
dwells not outside but within oneself. Search for it there, and
you will surely find it. Tell Mother and Gokibehn that I think
of them often. Blessings to Sumati and Mani.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4801. Courtesy: Shantikumar
N. Morarjee
