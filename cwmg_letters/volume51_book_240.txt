240. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
September 30, 1932
MY DEAR AGATHA,
I could read your agony through your cable and your letter.
But it was all well worth it. I was glad you had Charlie
Andrews by your side. I had fullest faith in your doing all you
could. Nothing more could be expected of anyone. As you would
perhaps be reading one another's letters, I am not saying more
in this brief love note.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1458
