212. LETTER TO DHIRUBHAI DIKSHIT
SEVAGRAM,
August 20, 1946
CHI. DHIRU,
I cannot quite understand why you had to undertake the
fast. Who made the complaint? And have I not said that no
one may undertake a fast without asking me? This is the best
course.
Blessings from
BAPU
SF. DHIRUBHAI
URULI KANCHAN
