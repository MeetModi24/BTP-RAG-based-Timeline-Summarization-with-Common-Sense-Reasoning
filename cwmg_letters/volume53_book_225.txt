225. LETTER TO G. V. MAVALANKAR
January 27, 1933
BHAI MAVALANKAR,
I got your letter. Blessings from us all to both the new
entrants to the ranks of the twice-born. May they live long and
do honour to the sacred thread.
The weakness will decrease day by day.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1236
