648. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
April 15, 1945
CHI. CHI[MANLAL],
I have your letter. Pass on the accompanying letters. You
give fairly detailed news. Let us see if we can use a part of
Durga's house during her absence. We have, of course, K.'s house
in the last resort.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10621
