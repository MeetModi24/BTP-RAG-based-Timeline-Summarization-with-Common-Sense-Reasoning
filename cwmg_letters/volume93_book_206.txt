206. LETTER TO TARADEVI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 18, 1937
CHI. TARA,
You can write a letter in a beautiful hand while lying in
bed. Let us see how you are going to work after completing
your studies.
Blessings from
BAPU
TARA KUMARI
MAHILA VIDYALAYA
LUCKNOW, U . P .
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
