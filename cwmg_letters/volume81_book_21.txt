21. LETTER TO SARALA MEHTA
July 21, 1945
CHI. SARALA1,
I have your long letter. What can I do in the family affairs ?
Time will do its own work. But bear in mind that the one
who has truth on his side will not be harmed. Bhai Nanalal is
there - all of you should see him. I am very busy these days.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHANDRAKUNJ
JAGNATH PLOT, RAJKOT
