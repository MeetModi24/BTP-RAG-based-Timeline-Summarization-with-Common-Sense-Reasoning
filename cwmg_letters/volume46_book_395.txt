395. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
BARDOLI,
June 4, 1931
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I got your letter. The Ashram is the right place for you. You
cannot leave the girls. You should even take in others who may
wish to join. You can, therefore, serve best in the Ashram.
Vasumati may live in Nadiad if she wishes to do so.
Santa Cruz khadi is very good.
Let me know when you are leaving. Write to me always and
tell me how you think or feel about any matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 54. Also C.W. 8776. Courtesy: Ganga-
behn Vaidya
