60. LETTER TO V. K. KRISHNASARMA
PANCHGANI,
July 26, 1946
BHAI KRISHNASARMA,
I got your letter. Though in your view it contains deep
knowledge, I can find none in it. I have read the book by
Thomas a Kempis. It is beautiful.
Blessings from
BAPU
V. K. KRISHNASARMA
7009 KALASEGUDA
SECUNDERABAD
