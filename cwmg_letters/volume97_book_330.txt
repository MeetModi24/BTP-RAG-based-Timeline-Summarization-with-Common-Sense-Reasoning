330. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
April 22, 1941
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I got your postcard. It is all right that Ba and Lakshmi have arrived
there. Kashi will pass one month easily enough. But then, she has to return
to it. I hope you and Amba are quite well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PRABHUDAS GANDHI
GANDHI ASHRAM
ASAFPUR
DISTT. BADAUN
U.P.
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33036
