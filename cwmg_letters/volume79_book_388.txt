388. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
SEVAGRAM,
March 10, 1945
BAPA,
I have your note. You should get the eye operated upon.
Gurupadam will do it skilfully. Get well and come. Leave be-
hind whatever suggestions you want to make. Write to me if you
wish to. We will do whatever we can in your absence.
BAPU
THAKKAR BAPA
SCINDHIA HOUSE
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
