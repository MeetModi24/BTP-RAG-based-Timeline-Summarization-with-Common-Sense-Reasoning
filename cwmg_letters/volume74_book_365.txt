365. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
August 11, 1941
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
I have your reply regarding the expenditure. I quite under-
stand. I make no distinction between you and Viththaldas. My
letter to you includes him just as one to him includes you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10851. Courtesy: Purushottam K.
Jerajani
