360. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
SEGAON,
February 12, 1937
BHAI KHAMBHATTA,
You should of course not leave Pandit half way. Go to
Gaurishankar only if Pandit's treatment does not suit you. It is
certainly our dharma to try, within limits, to cure physical suffer-
ing. But it is also our dharma to bear it cheerfully, knowing
that all suffering comes only to test us.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
DE BELVEDERE COURT
CHURCH GATE RECLAMATION
BOMBAY
