402. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
August 21, 1941
CHI. KANCHAN,
You should feel satisfied with this letter. I hope you reached
there safely. Take care about your food and get well. Arrange
about your studies. Write to me regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have written regarding the Autobiography.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8273. Also C.W. 7154. Cour-
tesy: Munnalal G. Shah
