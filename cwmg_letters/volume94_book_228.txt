228. LETTER TO B. S. MOONJE
"DILKHUSHA",
PANCHGANI,
July 8, 1944
DEAR DR. MOONJE,
In reply to your letter of the 2nd inst. to Gandhiji, I am to say that you are
at liberty to publish your letter of the 27th June to him.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
