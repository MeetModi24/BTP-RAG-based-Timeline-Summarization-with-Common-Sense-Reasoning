118. LETTER TO S. HOLLICK
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 6, 1906
DEAR MR. ROLLICK,
I now enclose a copy of the Memorial about which we had a
chat this morning. You will find it at page 8 of the copy I am sending.
I shall thank you to let me have the copy back after perusal.
Yours truly,
Enclosure
S. HOLLICK, E s Q .
62, LONDON WALL, E.C.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4503
