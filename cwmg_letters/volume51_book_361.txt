361. LETTER TO DR. SYED MAHMUD
October 9, 1932
DEAR DR. MAHMUD,
I have your two letters-the only ones to be received by me.
I hope you had my note sent through Kamala. Tell the 4-year
old dame that she was forgiven for not completing her fast. Prayers
of little ones are better than their fasts. Who knows but that it was
such prayers that were answered. Well, the whole time the fast
was on, I was thinking too of the Hindu-Muslim question and ask-
ing God to give me light. How I wish your, Maulana Abul Kalam's
and Malaviyaji's efforts fructified. It would be a grand day if we
could achieve not a paper but a heart unity.
I am glad your wife and sisters are better . You too must be
well now or you must have a compulsory fast for regaining health.
My salaams to both the Maulanas.
Love from us all.
BAPU
