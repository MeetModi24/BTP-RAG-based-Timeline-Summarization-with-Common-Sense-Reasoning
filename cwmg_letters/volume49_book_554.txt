554. LETTER TO BABALBHAI MEHTA
May 20, 1932
CHI. BABALBHAI,
We should do a thing to please elders if it is not immoral. It
cannot be a matter of dharma to sing group-prayers aloud. Hence
you cannot offer satyagraha to get freedom to do so. There is no
loss of self-respect at all in giving finger-prints in a jail. The prac-
tice of obtaining prisoners' finger-prints has no bearing on the
fear of their running away.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9452. Also C.W. 9115. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
