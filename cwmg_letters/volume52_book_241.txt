241. LETTER TO BEGUM MOHAMMAD ALAM
[December 12, 1932]
DEAR SISTER,
This letter of yours is quite perfect. Hindu ladies usually
write an atrocious hand, but the letters from Muslim ladies are
all good. That is why I do mind when any letter is not up to
the mark. Good that my teasing has accomplished all this! Let
us hope that Doctor Saheb makes a full recovery this time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 23
