415. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
NEW DELHI,
January 19, 1948
MY DEAR DESHABHAKTA,
Your letter. What do we care what others say?
I forgot to mention your fracture. Are you permanently cured?
As you know the fast was broken yesterday.
Yours,
BAPU
KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
GUNTUR
