369. LETTER TO CHAKRAYYA
SEVAGRAM,
March 8, 1945
CHI. CHAKRAYYA,
It is good you wrote. I shall write to Sharma on hearing
from you. But it is your duty first to have a full talk with him
yourself. If he does not come, write to him and then write to
me also. In the meantime do not spend anything on your
house. Spend the very minimum on the arrangements. Meet the
Harijans also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
