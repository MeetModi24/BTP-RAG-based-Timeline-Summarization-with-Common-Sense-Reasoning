169. LETTER TO N. G. RANGA
November 20, 1946
MY DEAR RANGA 1 ,
This is one of the many letters about you. If there is any
truth in it, you will tell me. Why such complaints? My move-
ments are fleeting. Hence the address is Khadi Pratishthan,
Sodepur, Bengal.
Blessings from
BAPU
PROF. N. G. RANGA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
