412. LETTER TO REHANA TYABJI
January 5, 1925
MY DEAR REHANA,
I am glad you are coming. Do you know that a friend of my
father got cured of his malady by constant attendance on God?
May you not do likewise? You can be well, if you will be well.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 9599
