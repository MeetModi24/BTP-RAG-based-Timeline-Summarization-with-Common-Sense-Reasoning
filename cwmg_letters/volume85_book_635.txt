635. LETTER TO JANAKIRAM
NEW DELHI,
October 18, 1946
BHAI JANAKIRAM,
Your case has appeared in the newspapers; there is no need
to say anything more.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI JANAKIRAM
KADAVUR
VIZAGAPATAM
SOUTH INDIA
