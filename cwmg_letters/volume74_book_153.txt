153. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
June 3, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
These days the well-water remains almost clean, so there is
no problem. When the rains begin you will have to be careful.
Keep an account as far as you can of the fruit sent by
Janakibehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4393
