155. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
March 22, 1929
CHI. VASUMATI,
I write this letter on board the ship. I got your letter. I
only suggested the ideal. From that you may do what Nature lets
you do. You already have my permission to move to a separate
room. I am surprised about Yashodadevi. I will certainly dis-
cuss the matter with her when I return there. It is no doubt
intolerable that they should talk among themselves at night.
Discover a remedy. I am writing about it. Take Chhaganlal
Joshi's help. More when we meet. I hope Sulochanabehn is
doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 506. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
