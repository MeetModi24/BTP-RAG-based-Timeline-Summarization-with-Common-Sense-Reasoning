502. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
December 6, 1934
I have now come across the letter from the German friend.
I have written to her. Here is a copy of it so that you too can
send a reply if you like. The reply is belated, but better late
than never.
Encl. 1
SHRI JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
MARKET STREET
HYDERABAD (SIND)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
