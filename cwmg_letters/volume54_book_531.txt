531. LETTER TO RAMESHWARLAL BAZAJ
April 20, 1933
BHAI RAMESHWARLAL,
I got a letter from you after so many months. You do not
write how your business is going on. Describe how you live. I
hope your mind is at peace. What happened about your esta-
blishment in London? One can solve all problems if one is patient.
Rukmini is in Deolali at present. She is all right.
We are all well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9064
