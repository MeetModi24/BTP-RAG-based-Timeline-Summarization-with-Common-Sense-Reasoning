105. LETTER TO MAITRI GIRI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 8, 1930
CHI. MAITRI,
I have your letter. Don't mind if you make mistakes. You
should go on writing to me, with proper care. I was very happy
to learn that your health had improved.
You are perfectly right when you say that, as moderation in
eating is good, so is moderation in providing food to the mind.
Prabhubhai is very well qualified to teach you; learn from him.
Blessings from
BAPU
