48. LETTER TO DUDABHAI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Tuesday, June 22, 1926
BHAI DUDABHAI,
I have your letter. Write to Bhai Balwantrai saying that
you have not received your salary yet. It is necessary that you
keep on writing to him about all the difficulties you have. He
too has suggested it. I was very happy at your decision not to
leave the school at any cost. I had a talk with Bhai Balwantrai
about your salary. Most probably you will have no difficulty
now. Write to me promptly when Lakshmi's clothes are torn and,
if the new ones are to be stitched here, send me her measure-
ments also.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19629
