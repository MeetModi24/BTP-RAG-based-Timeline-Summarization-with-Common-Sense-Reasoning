184. LETTER TO SHUAIB QURESHI
June 26, 1925
All you say about Hindu-Mussalman quarrels is too true!
I am following the course adopted by the Prophet's companions
in Osman's time. They withdrew to the caves when Islam was
split up into rival factions. We may figuratively retire into our-
selves whilst the two may be fighting like cats and dogs.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
