320. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
March 20, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
I am indeed glad that you have taken up the propagation of
village industries and sanitation. You should succeed in this work.
These activities are, according to me, an integral part of
swaraj.
Bhanushanker does not remember the dates.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8486.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
