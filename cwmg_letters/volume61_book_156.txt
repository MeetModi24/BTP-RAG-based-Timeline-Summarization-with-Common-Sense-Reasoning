156. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 26, 1935
DEAR DAUGHTER,
Your letter. You did nothing wrong in coming to the sta-
tion. Why do you unnecessarily distress yourself? You did well in
giving me all the news. Gangabehn I hope, is cheerful. Raj-
kishori must have got my letter. Do only as much as your health
permits. I shall reach on the 2nd.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 327
