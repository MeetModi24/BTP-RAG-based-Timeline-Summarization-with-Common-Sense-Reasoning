96. LETTER TO S. M. MATE
September 17, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your wire and letter both received at the
same time. I may not as a prisoner answer individual questions.
But I have sent to the authorities a general statement for publica-
tion. If they allow its publication, you will see your point covered
by it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. S. M. MATE, M.A.
TILAK ROAD
POONA 2
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. III, p. 181
