220. LETTER TO DILKHUSH B. DIWANJI
July 3, 1941
BHAI DILKHUSH,
I got your booklet. I will try to read it, and if I feel that I
should write something, I will do so. Since you are all coming,
I would naturally like to have a special word with some of you
but I have to restrain myself.
Blessings from
BAPU
DILKHUSH DIWANJI
"GANDHI KUTIR"
KARADI, via NAVASARI
B.B.&C.I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2648
