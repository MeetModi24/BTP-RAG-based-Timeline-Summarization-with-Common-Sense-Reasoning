105. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Friday, November 23, 1928
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I can understand your misery. Physical separation will how-
ever always be there. Shake off anxiety and be firm and devoted
to your duty.
Keep writing to me. Go to Dwarka only if you want to,
otherwise don't.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3343
