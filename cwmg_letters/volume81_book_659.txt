659. LETTER TO GAJANAN NAIK
October 17, 1945
CHI. GAJANAN,
I have your letter. I regret that I cannot make you under-
stand such a simple thing. If the person who writes is himself
not frank, what can be done about what he writes? I tell you
it is your dharma to be frank; only then can an inquiry be
held. If you wish to write to me secretly I don't want to listen
to anything and don't want to be influenced.
AKHIL BHARATIYA UDYOG SANGH
WARDHA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
