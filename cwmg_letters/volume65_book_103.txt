103. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
April 17, 1937
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
I have not written to you at all recently.
I wrote to
the Hindu Mission. We should investigate. If the Christians
are holding out inducements to win over Harijans, nothing more
need be done beyond publishing the facts. It is of course our duty
to remove the hardships the Harijans may be suffering.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3958. Also C.W. 143. Courtesy:
Parikshitlal L. Majmudar
