524. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS NEVATIA
December 10, 1934
CHI. RAMESHWAR,
Let me have a full report about Ghani. Pay him at least
Rs.30. I will write more tomorrow.
What arrangements have
been made for his meals? Can you not find a Muslim with
clean habits? If you can find even a Christian cook, he will
do. If there is a big railway station nearby, Ghani can go there
daily for one meal. What is the climate of the place like? What
is the size of the population?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3040
