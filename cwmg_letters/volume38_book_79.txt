79. LETTER TO JAL KHAMBHATTA
November 17, 1928
BHAISHRI KHAMBHATTA,
I have your letter. It is good news indeed that you have re-
turned and are all right. I am very happy to know that your
health is completely restored. Please let me know if you have
had any new experiences about water treatment. Be careful
and preserve the health you have recovered. My blessings to
you both.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 5018. Courtesy: Tehmina Khambhatta
