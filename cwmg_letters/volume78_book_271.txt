271. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
October 21, 1944
By all means study Sanskrit. The effort made for learning
correct pronunciation will not be wasted. Chaste pronunciation is
a requisite for [learning] any language. But perhaps for Sanskrit
accuracy of pronunciation is imperative. It is not at all neces-
sary for you to study English. Assimilate the knowledge you
already have and add to it.
My blessings of course are with you.
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen, p. 358
