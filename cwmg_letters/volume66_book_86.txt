86. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 22, 1937
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I send with this a letter from Jayanti addressed to me.
I
replied to him saying that it would be better if he wrote directly
to you instead of writing to me. I think it is best that you
yourself should reply to the accompanying letter.
I am getting papers from Nariman. I am not sending copies
to you. I will of course send copies of papers which you ought
to get.
Don't worry about my health. I am taking proper rest and
will now increase the period.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
POONA
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 210
