396. LETTER TO NARASINHDAS GORDHANDAS
NEW DELHI,
October 24, 1947
BHAI NARASINHDAS,
I have your letter. I have given a brief reply to it through
Harijan. I don't propose to go further than that. My relatives
are those who follow me. I have passed on your letter to Sjt.
Narandas, adding that he should also show it to Nanabhai
Bhatt.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
