218. LETTER TO RAMKRISHNA DALMIA
SEVAGRAM,
October 11, 1944
BHAI DALMIA,
I have your letter. I think what I did was right. I know
your letter was prompted by love. Do keep writing like this.
Blessings from
BAPU
DALMIA-JAIN NIVAS
NEW DELHI
