413. LETTER TO SECRETARY, MYSORE ASSEMBLY
CONGRESS PARTY
[Before November 16, 1944]
Mr. Pyarelal . . . has sent a letter to the Secretary to the Mysore
Assembly Congress Party, conveying Gandhiji's thanks for the invitation to
the Party's conference (held yesterday), adding that anything that helped con-
structive activities and advanced the cause of popular freedom had his bles-
sings. He also congratulated the Party on its good work in the Legislature
during the first term of its existence under the new constitution.
The Hindu, 19-11-1944
