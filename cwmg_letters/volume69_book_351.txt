351. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, W A R D H A ,
June 12, 1939
MY DEAR AMRIT,
I have your letter.
Of course everything is forgiven you. The fact is I have not
felt offended against you. My love burns as bright as ever. It is
little worth if it cannot stand the heart pourings of loved ones. I
simply gave you my reactions to your letters. You are as
much wanted here as ever. You will find me not in the least
altered or if altered, it will be for the better. I shall know how
much more tender you are than I had believed you to be.
Navin is in Bombay tending his sister. His address is care
Dr. Shah, Polyclinic, Queen's Road, Bombay.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3919. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7228
