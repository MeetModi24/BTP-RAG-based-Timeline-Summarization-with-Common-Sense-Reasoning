219. LETTER TO V. T. KRISHNAMACHARI
SEVAGRAM,
August 22, 1946
BHAI KRISHNAMACHARI,
The Rajasthan branch of the Charkha Sangh informs me
that the jaipur State does not give khadi full exemption from
tax or licence fee, etc. Can't poor khadi be exempted?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR KRISHNAMACHARI, DIWAN
JAIPUR
