600. LETTER TO GOKULBHAI BHATT
October 9, 1945
BHAI GOKULBHAI,
I have your letter. I have preserved your letter written in
pencil. I shall read it at leisure in order to satisfy my curiosity
whether I interpreted it that way because I read it in a
hurry or whether it does warrant the interpretation.
Even a co-worker like you, I could meet only for a few
minutes. Of course I did not like it, but one has to swallow
such [bitter] pills.
I think the draft you have sent is quite right. Obtain the
approval of others and begin the work. I think the names
1 Vide p. 313.
mentioned by you will be useful. They will both be quite helpful
to you in your work.
GOKULBHAI BHATT
BHAGINI SEVA MANDIR TRUST
VILE PARLE
