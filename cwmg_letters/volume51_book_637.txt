637. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO
GOVERNOR OF BOMBAY
November 13, 1932
TO
THE PRIVATE SECRETARY TO
H. E. THE GOVERNOR
BOMBAY
DEAR SIR,
I have seen Lord Sankey's public appeal to me. I feel that
it calls for an immediate reply from me. I therefore submit the
enclosed for His Excellency's approval and despatch by cable.
I do not know whether this falls within His Excellency's jurisdic-
tion or His Excellency the Viceroy's. If the latter, I request
that the text of my reply to Lord Sankey be wired to H. E. the
Viceroy for being cabled to the Lord Chancellor.
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 3868. Also Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home De-
partment, Special Branch, File No. 800(40) (4), Pt. II, p. 5
