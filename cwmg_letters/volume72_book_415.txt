415. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 7, 1940
MY DEAR AMBUJAM 1 ,
I was glad to hear from you after a long silence, if only
to remind me that I had not sent you the promised foreword
My memory does betray me nowadays. What I intend to do
I often take for granted I have done. Since you have not re-
ceived the previous letter I am inclined to regard it as another
betrayal of memory. I shall not be happy till I receive your
letter that you have the foreword.
I am sorry for the delay
wholly unintended.
It is a good thing you are attending to the parents. I
hope both are well.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9614. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
