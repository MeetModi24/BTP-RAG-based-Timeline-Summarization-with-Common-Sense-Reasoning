492. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 19, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I am glad to get even two lines from you.
Amtul's address is not legible. Give it to me tomorrow.
I wanted to reach there tonight. But it has not been possible.
I had to go to two places and there were other difficulties also.
By the grace of God we shall meet tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 567
