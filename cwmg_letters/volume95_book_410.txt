410. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Although the [blood] pressure has gone up, I must write to you.
Your letter is very long but the thing is . . .1 work and Kanchan . . .2
I have already given to you. When I have not entrusted you with
anything, you should observe silence and spin. Either you should
completely forget Kanchan or set up a home with her. If you read the
Gita put it into practice. The soul alone is the refuge of the soul.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8237
