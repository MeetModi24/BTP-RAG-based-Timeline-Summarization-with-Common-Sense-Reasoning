376. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
WARDHA,
July 18, 1935
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
I am glad the burden of the book is off your mind. Of
course Simla was the right place for it.
I understand what you say of Khambhatta.
As to the young Bengali, apart from the desire to learn,
the proposed book will be of little value unless it has outstanding
merit. I may also tell you that Dr. Pattabhi is writing the Con-
gress history.
Yes, Jawaharlal's discharge will be an important event. The
Socialist creed would be most attractive, if the sting of compul-
sion was out.
Love.
MOHAN
From a photostat: G.N. 990
