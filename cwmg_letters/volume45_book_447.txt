447. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
April 14, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your letter and your wire. I shall be in Bombay on the
16th, the 17th and the 18th. I will be staying at 7 Laburnum Road.
On the 20th and the 21 st, I shall be in the Ashram, when it is pro-
posed to perform the betrothal of Senior Lakshmi. My health is
fairly good. Perhaps I get a little temperature in the afternoon.
Mirabehn arrived here yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3396
