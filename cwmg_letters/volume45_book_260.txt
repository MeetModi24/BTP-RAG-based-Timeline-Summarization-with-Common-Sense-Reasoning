260. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
PRAYAG,
February 16, 1931
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I am leaving for Delhi today. My headquarters there will be
Dr. Ansari's place, in Daryaganj. I do not know how long I shall
stay there but it will be for two or three days at least. How is
Arun and what is the news about Charu ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1685
