373. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
August 19, 1938
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Relieve Salekar so that he can go to fetch the medicine.
Entrust his work to Rajbhushan. If there is any difficulty about
this, let me know.
BAPU
[PS.]
Gunvant should not take it. Let him discuss with me. I
did not read one of the letters. The telegram in reply to this is
given just now so that it can be sent early tomorrow. You need
not put yourself to inconvenience to come here in the morning.
If you can come conveniently, you may. Perhaps that may be
enough. This was one . . .1
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8569. Also C.W. 7034. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
