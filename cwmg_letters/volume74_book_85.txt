85. LETTER TO PRESIDENT, BENGAL PROVINCIAL
CONGRESS COMMITTEE
[Before May 15, 1941]
The only advice I can give you at present is, that those who
are allowed to be outside should devote themselves to constructive
work, specially communal.
Really speaking, it is not unfortunate; for, we expected such
arrests They test our strength and solidarity.
The Hindu, 16-5-1941
