532. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
July 30, 1937
CHI. KAKA,
I have gone through what you sent with Vora. Nothing
else occurs to me. Clause 11 should come at the end, or Clause
21 should replace Clause 11 and the latter should become Clause
12. You will understand this change as soon as you read the
draft.
I am sending herewith an essay by Jawaharlal on
Hindi. If you can go through it today, please do so and send
your suggestions. Jawaharlal has asked for my comments. I
should send them immediately. If you are very busy and can-
not send any suggestions, don't worry. It will suffice if I get
them before you leave tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7702
