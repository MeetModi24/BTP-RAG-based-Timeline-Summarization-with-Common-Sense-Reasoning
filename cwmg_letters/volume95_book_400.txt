400. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
Y.M.
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Received your frank letter. The best medicine for you is that

you ... This universe is like a big machine. If its various components
do not observe the rules the machine will break down. One who does
not observe the rules will certainly not become a part of the machine
and therefore "he will perish like a scattered cloud". My advice to
you is that you should live in the Vidyapith the way your inner self
guides you. Find peace of mind anyhow.
There is truth in what you write about prayers. We are all
seekers. No one is perfect. Therefore there is no limit to our
shortcomings. But our dharma is clear.
"God's whole creation, living and non-living, contains both
good and evil. The wise accept the good and leave the evil (like the
swan) which drinks the milk and leaves the water behind."
Keep writing to me.
Blessing from
BAPU
