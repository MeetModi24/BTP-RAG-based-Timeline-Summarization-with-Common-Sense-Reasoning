810. LETTER TO E. W. ARYANAYAKUM
October 31, 1945
CHI. ARYANAYAKUM,
If you have read about the confusion prevailing in Travancore
regarding education, let me have your opinion. Many people
are writing to me that I must say something about it. I had
not read anything till now, nor had I any [other] material.
Now Shri Cherian Kopen has sent a letter and also a lot of
literature with it. I have read it and if you have not and wish
to read it, I can send it. I have sent a similar letter to
Kumarappa.
Blessings from
BAPU
ASHRAM
SEVAGRAM
