400. LETTER TO BENARSILAL BAZAJ
January 22, 1930
CHI. BENARSILAL,
I have a letter from Jamnalalji and another from Rukmini's
paternal grandfather. Phalguna Shukla 2 is an auspicious day.
It falls on Sunday, March 2. Regard this date as fixed for the
wedding which will be solemnized in the Ashram. I hope it
suits you. If you wish to write anything please do.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRIYUT BENARSILAL
NEW SWADESHI MILLS
RAILWAYPURA
AHMEDABAD
From Hindi: C.W. 9301. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
