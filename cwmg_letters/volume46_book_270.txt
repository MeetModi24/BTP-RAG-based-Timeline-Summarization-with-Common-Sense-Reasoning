270. LETTER TO SECRETARY, INDIAN ASSOCIATION, IRAQ
As AT SABARMATI,
May 26, 1931
THE SECRETARY
INDIAN ASSOCIATION IN IRAQ
P. Box No. 71, RESIDENCY ROAD
BAGHDAD
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your printed letter of 28th April last. I do not know
anything of the previous ones. They were probably received
whilst I was in jail. I wish your Association every success. Will
you give me some idea of the number and the profession of Indians
resident in Iraq and will you also tell me whether the relations
between you and the Arabs are perfectly cordial?
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 17152
