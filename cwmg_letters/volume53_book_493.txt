493. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
February 20, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
You wrote to me after a long time. Write regularly from
now on. It is not good for Premabehn to get angry with you.
But a boy or girl who is eager to learn will not mind the teacher
getting angry and will learn attentively.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati:
C.W. 9637 Courtesy: Ravindra
R. Patel
