221. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
N[EW] D[ELHI] ,
May 20, 1946
BETI A. S.,
I have your letter. I asked Abha to read it to me while I
was having my meal. Pyarelal read it. Now I am destroying
it. What is there in it to hide? What harm is there if you take
your sister-in-law with you to her village and train her? I would
like it. Do what you want to do.
I understand what you say about Hanif.
I do not know how long I shall be here.
To you all,
Blessings from
BAPU
