185. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
September 21, 1932
You should not be perturbed about the fast. God has sent
me unsought an opportunity for which I was pining. We
ought not to feel unhappy when our desire is granted. All three
of us are happy and, as we watch God's sport, dance with as much
delight as we can. But we have not yet learnt how to dance well.
I have obtained permission for people to write to me. You may,
therefore, write to me.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 66
