444. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
SEGAON,
September 18, 1939
MY DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
Please see that I get a car for Gosibehn at the latest at 1.30
p. m. She has to transact business at Maganwadi and then en-
train for Bombay. I hope Devi and Premi have arrived safe and
sound.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 11046. Courtesy: Arjun Jairamdas
