640. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
November 13, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
I read the names of the new office-bearers. Let me see now
what work you do during this year. Do not distrust one another.
Do not be rude to anybody. You should be polite even to-
wards one another. Make the best use of every minute. Playing
at the proper time also is good use of time. Reading at the time
fixed for playing and playing at the time meant for study, this
is bad use of time.
BAPU
