300. LETTER TO RATHINDRANATH TAGORE
July 28, 1941
MY DEAR RATHIN2,
Your letter of 24th instant. I have not the appeal by me
just now.
I think the proposed project is included in the ob-
jects. If it is, I have no objection. But please ask the others
and secure their consent.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RATHINDRANATH TAGORE
"UTTARAYAN"
SANTINIKETAN, BENGAL
C.W. 10319. Courtesy: Visvabharati, Santiniketan
