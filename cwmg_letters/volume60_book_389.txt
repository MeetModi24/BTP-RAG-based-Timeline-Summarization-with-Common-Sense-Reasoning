389. LETTER TO ACHYUT PATWARDHAN
March 9, 1935
MY DEAR PATWARDHAN,
I have your letter. I am sorry that Sastri or Ganesan took
all these many months to discharge a small amount. Of course
I know that you would gladly take up the Harijan printing
again for my sake. I am in correspondence with Sastri and
Ganesan about it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. A. PATWARDHAN
ARYABHUSHAN PRESS
POONA 4
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
