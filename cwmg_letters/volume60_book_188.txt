188. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRAN
February 1, 1935
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRAN,
Herewith is a letter from the office of the Municipal Com-
missioner regarding the complaint of Kuppachari. Please see the
Chairman of the Reception Committee. The poor man ought
to receive some compensation from whoever may be considered
primarily liable.
Encl. 1
SHRI RAMACHANDRAN
BANGALORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
