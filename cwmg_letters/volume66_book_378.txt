378. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON,
November 29, 1937
You must not get ill. How I wish you had full nature
cure. Hope the niece is better. I had a personal letter today
from the Viceroy of good wishes. You must write daily. Please
thank the Maharaja for the fruit. But tell him why foreign
fruits when this land of ours has plenty of both fresh and dried
fruit.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3834. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6990
