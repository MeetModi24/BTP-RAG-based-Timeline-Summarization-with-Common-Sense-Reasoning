368. LETTER TO N. G. APTE
March 7, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. There is an unfortunate history about
the juices you sent with Sjt. Marathe. I could not tempt any
person to try those juices as there were plenty of fruits and I
could not try them myself for the simple reason that I limit myself
to five articles per day and there can be found no room for any
of the two juices.
I thought that I had returned the papers to Sjt. Marathe
but now that you remind me I shall make a search.
What sample are you referring to?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. G. APTE
754 SHUKRAWAR
POONA 2
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
