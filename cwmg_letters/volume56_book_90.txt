90. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL PAREKH
[Before October 11, 1933]
I am grieved to learn that you have not succeeded in bring-
ing about conciliation. I am more grieved that the conciliation
was obstructed by preliminary difficulties. I cannot decide, without
hearing both the sides, what information could be asked and what
information the mill-owners could not supply.
It is not necessary to refer such a question to arbitration.
Mr. Gulzarilal Nanda, Secretary of the Labour Association, saw
me and [I] told him that if I am asked to give my decision, I
must have the arguments of both the sides before me. This can
be done by written statements.
The Bombay Chronicle, 11-10-1933
