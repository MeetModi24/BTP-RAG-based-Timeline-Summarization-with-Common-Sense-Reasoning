143. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
[After March 11, 1924]
Keep your body fit. Then only can I take sufficient work
from you and give you some more still.
Take milk at least for fifteen days, if you think you need it.
Eat fruit, roti will do you harm. Take curds without fail.
Your pronunciation is no doubt bad, but do not worry much
over that. English is not our language. The way the French
pronounce [it] is very bad, but no Englishman ever complains
about it.
From the Hindi original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5999. Courtesy: G. D.
Birla
