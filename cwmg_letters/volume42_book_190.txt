190. LETTER TO KALAVATI TRIVEDI
KALAKANKAR,
November 14, 1929
CHI. KALAVATI,
I have your letter. It is good that you have now got over the
loss of your jewellery. If we think deeply over it we shall find
that nothing in this world belongs to anyone individual. When
a certain thing that we had come to regard as ours is lost or des-
troyed we feel grieved. But if we look upon a thing as belonging
not to us but to God, there would be no grief. The question then
arises: If nothing belongs to anyone, who should take care of
anything and why? The answer is that though we are not the
owners of a thing, we hold it, when it has come to us through
our labour or through some other rightful means, in trust or
guardianship for God. It then becomes our duty to guard it.
And, if, in spite of our guarding it with diligence and care, it
comes to be destroyed or lost we should not feel grieved.
Blessings from
BAPU
