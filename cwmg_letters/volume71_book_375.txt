375. LETTER TO ABUL KALAM AZAD
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 4, 1940
DEAR MAULANA SAHEB,
You shall address me as you like. I do not know that you
addressed me differently.
I have written to Lord Linlithgow as suggested by you.
I quite agree with you about the Pattabhi episode.
I am inclined to think that there should be a considered reply
from you to the Lahore resolution.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 7842 a. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
