302. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Sunday [January 22, 1922]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
If the Bombay Committee has the money earmarked then
certainly the amount can and should be given from it. The
money so set apart is not meant to be kept. It is only desirable
that the Provincial Committee should pay it. Even now I would
take it to mean that the money is to be given not for the temple
but for the uplift of Harijans. Even then, I see no harm in
your arguing the case with the Provincial Committee. I have
consulted Bhai Shankerlal.
Blessings from
BAPU
