471. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I cannot do anything from here. I do not have that much
power and authority. I have sent your letter to Purushottamdas
Tandon. He will do whatever he can. See him.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI JANAKIDEVI
MIRA GOVINDPUR
RAE BARELI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
