252. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM,
October 17, 1944
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
Received your letter. It is good. Your health is good.
Well done. The more you improve your health the better will
be the work you do. I can send all the money that is needed.
If you send a certificate from Bhagirathji I can even send it from
Ba's fund. But in that case a certain procedure will have to be
followed. Budget sanction will be required. Satis Babu may
himself write about the cow, he can also advise regarding khadi.
He is a trustee as well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Akbar has gone to a village in Gujarat. Zohra will join
him after having had training in nursing at the Ashram.
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 485
