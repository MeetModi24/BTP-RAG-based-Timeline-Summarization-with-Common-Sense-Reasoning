279. LETTER TO MATHURADAS PURUSHOTTAM
October 4, 1930
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. What you write about weavers is true.
There is a special class of workers in Ahmedabad who starch yarn.
We see such workers elsewhere too. Cannot we supply starched
warp to weavers? If you ask Ramjibhai and the others, they also
will come and help. They will have to do the work entrusted to
them by Chhaganbhai or Surendra. If, however, you feel that
I should write to them, write to me again. I have had no letter
yet from Motibehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3745
