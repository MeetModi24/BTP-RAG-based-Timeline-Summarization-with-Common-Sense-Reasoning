149. LETTER TO VIPIN D. PATEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 3, 1938
CHI. BABA,
I got your letter only today. How old will you be on
your next birthday? Why did you forget to write that? And
shouldn't one who asks for blessings give something in return?
What will you give? What new thing will you do in the new
year?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 162
