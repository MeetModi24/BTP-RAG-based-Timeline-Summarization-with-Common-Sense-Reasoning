25. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
SEVAGRAM,
August 6, 1941
CHI. MRIDU,
I have your letter. What you say is not correct. It seems you
have not understood at all the nature of the struggle this time. It is
no good people going to jail simply because others have done so. If
there are poor people who want to do constructive work but cannot
you will be needed to organize them. You will be needed even if there
is a riot again. It will do even if no one from Gujarat goes to jail
for the present. If you and others do not handle the crisis of floods,
who else will ? Why can't you see that ?
Your letter bespeaks cowardice and impatience. It does not behove
you. I shall explain further if you come here.
Blessings from
BAPU
[P.S.]
If you did not leave Gujarat now to go anywhere, I would be happy.
