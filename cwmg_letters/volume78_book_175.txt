175. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
[After] September [27,] 1944
CHI. SHRIMAN,
I got your manuscript . I will try to read it. Maganbhai
says that what you have written about Hindi-Urdu has caused
much confusion. You must have received my letter. I will
be leaving on the 30th; or may be even earlier.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 301
