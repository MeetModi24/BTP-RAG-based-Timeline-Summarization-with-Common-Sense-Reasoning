487. LETTER TO PETER J. MASCREEN
January 5, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You will pardon me for telling you that
my reading of Hinduism is wholly different from yours. The
doctrine of transmigration does not entitle one man to claim supe-
riority over another. Hinduism is not wholly different from
Roman Catholicism, as you imagine. The fundamentals of all
religions are the same.
Yours sincerely,
PETER J. MASCREEN, ESQ.
C/O J. MASCREEN, ESQ., VELLIYIL PARAMPIL, TANGACHERRY
QUILON
