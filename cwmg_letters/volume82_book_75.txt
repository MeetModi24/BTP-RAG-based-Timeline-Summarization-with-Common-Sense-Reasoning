75. LETTER TO K. N. KATJU
November 8, 1945
BHAI KATJU,
I have your letter. I liked it very much. But I had written
to the Viceroy as soon as I got your telegram. In your letter I
have all that I wanted. I will use it and also your name if
necessary. But I hope the thing will be done without this being
necessary. These days you are under great pressure of work. I
hope you are well. How long do you have to stay there?
Blessings from
BAPU
