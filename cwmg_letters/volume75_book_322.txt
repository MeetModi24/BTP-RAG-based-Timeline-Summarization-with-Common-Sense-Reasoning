322. LETTER TO NRISIMHAPRASAD K. BHATT
SEVAGRAM,
January 25, 1942
BHAI NANABHAI,
Received your letter only today. It was good that you were
able to keep Narandas. Your encouragement will drive away his
fear. I have him with me because we have only a small number of
honest and able workers like him. The money should be deposited
with Bachharaj & Co. only. But do what all of you think proper.
If Jerajani continues to insist, take him on. He too is an old
hand. He will be useful to you. You will have to look carefully
into this. Don't give too much of your time and certainly not
your physical strength. But if you are aware of this responsibility,
you can without stirring from Ambla rectify everything and remove
the irregularities. You are the helmsman.
Preserve your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Please give the enclosed to Narandas. Don't starve him of
finances for khadi work.
