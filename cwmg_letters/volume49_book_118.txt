118. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM D. SARAIYA
February 10, 1932
CHI. KAKU,
I got your letter written in a beautiful hand. You have plenty
of things to write about. Even if you give an account of what
you did during the last year, you will have enough material. You
should regard spinning as yajna and do it regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 2807. Courtesy: Purushottam D. Saraiya
