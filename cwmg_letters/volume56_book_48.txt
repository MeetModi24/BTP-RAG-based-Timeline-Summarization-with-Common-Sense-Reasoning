48. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
September 30, 1933
The report about me which has appeared in papers is correct,
and still not true. That is how a doctor would put it. But
those who know about my health should not be alarmed by the
report, for my blood-pressure used to remain higher there than
it does here. No significance should be attached to increase or
loss in weight in regard to my health. But now even the weight
is not less than 101. I take sufficient rest.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 136
