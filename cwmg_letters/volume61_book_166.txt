166. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 28, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
Read the accompanying letter and give it to Harilal. Do you
think there is anything in it?
Keshu has gone to Bombay. He wants something by way of
scholarship. I have told Panditji about Gajanan. He will have
a talk with Gajanan. Go on giving for the present.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8446. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
