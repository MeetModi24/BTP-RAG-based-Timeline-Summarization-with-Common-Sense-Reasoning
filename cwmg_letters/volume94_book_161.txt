161. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
BARDOLI,
December 21, 1941
CHI. TARAMATI,
I have your letter. You have given good news. If the improvement
continues as now, he will soon be rid of the trouble. Dr. Gilder said
that there was certainly a chance for improvement. Everything depends
on patience, courage and observance of rules on the part of Mathuradas.
He must observe silence properly.
It is always crowded here.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI TARAMATIBEHN MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
WINDI HALL
DEOLALI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
