462. LETTER TO SIR ROBERT MCCARRISON
November 23, 1934
DEAR MAJOR-GENERAL,
I thank you for your letter of 20th instant. As soon as the
scheme of the Association is complete a copy shall certainly be
sent to you.
I thank you for allowing your name to appear among the
advisers of the Association.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
