3. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
DEHRADUN,
October 16, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
There is no letter from you. I got, however, Jamnalalji's
letter written from the Ashram and that means the mail from the
Ashram has been received here. I read in it about Umiya's
betrothal. May both be happy. I send with this Chi. Shanker-
lal's letter, which you should hand over to Umiya. Read it and
pass it on to her.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have not revised this.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5460
