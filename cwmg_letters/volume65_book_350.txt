350. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
WARDHA,
June 18, 1937
MY DEAR REBEL,
I have not kept you without letter two days running.
Sometimes I have written two days running. As you must have
seen I have declined to make any statement on the office issue.
The Times of India interview was exhaustive and the last word
so far as I am concerned. Now the Working Committee has to
have its say.
Your Hindi writing is getting scarce. But I don't grumble.
You have your hands more than full. I therefore satisfy my-
self with what I get.
Khan Saheb, Mehr[taj] and Lali came in yesterday.
ROBBER
[PS.]
Still steaming.
Love.
From the original: C.W. 3789. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6945
