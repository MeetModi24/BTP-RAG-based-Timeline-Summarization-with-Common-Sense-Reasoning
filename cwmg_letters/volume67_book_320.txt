320. LETTER TO NANA DHARMADHIKARI
August 7, 1938
BHAI NANA,
I thought that you had left without permission and that
is why I was angry. I see now that you had been called away.
No doubt I thus did you an injustice, though unintentionally. I
make amends for it now by acknowledging my mistake through
this letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 4754
