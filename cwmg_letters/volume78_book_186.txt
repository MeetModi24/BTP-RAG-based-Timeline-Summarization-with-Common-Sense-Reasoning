186. LETTER TO HUNDRAJ M. PARWANI
October 1, 1944
DEAR PARWANI,
I had your letter. I am powerless. I have no authority.
Moreover I do not understand Sind politics.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI HUNDRAJ MULCHAND PARWANI
BUNDER ROAD
KARACHI, SIND
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
