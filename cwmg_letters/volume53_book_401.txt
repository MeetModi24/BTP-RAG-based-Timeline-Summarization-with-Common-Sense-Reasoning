401. LETTER TO T. K. S. RAJAN
February 14, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am glad you have already taken the
work among Harijans. You would report to me a fortnight hence
the results of your activity, and I will say what more can be done.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. T. K. S. RAJAN
SALEM
