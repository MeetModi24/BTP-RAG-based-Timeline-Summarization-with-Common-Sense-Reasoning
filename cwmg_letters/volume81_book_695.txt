695. LETTER TO DEVPRAKASH NAYYAR
October 19, 1945
CHI. DEV,
I have your frank letter. I understand what you say. If
you don't need a change of air, I have nothing to say. But I
consider it necessary for a person who is either bodily or mentally
sick. It is necessary to have a fine sense of perception to under-
stand everything.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi : Documents relating to Gandhiji.
Courtesy: National Archives of India
