37. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
PANCHGANI,
July 23, 1946
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have your letter. Just now give Ramprasad what Jajuji
has suggested. I hope to arrive there by the 7th. If necessary,
I will think further about the matter. It is bad that Gordhandas
is having low fever. He should take rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 10650
