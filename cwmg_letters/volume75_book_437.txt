437. LETTER TO A. V. THAKKAR
February 27, 1942
BAPA,
Spend up to Rs. 300, from the amount marked, for putting
up four lavatories in the fair at Okha. Why did you not think of
it earlier?
* * *
Have gone through the papers about Dharma Prakash. It was
good that they were sent to Viyogi Hari. The payment should
be made only when the account is submitted and I pass it.
* * *
I have sent Balkrishna there. I hope I have not encumbered
you thoughtlessly. In my view, it is beneficial to us to accommodate
people like him. But if you hold a different view, I will not do such
a thing again. I do not believe that everybody should relish what
I like.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
