270. LETTER TO DEVPRAKASH NAYYAR
December 10, 1946
CHI. DEV,
I have your letter. It is good. I agree with what you
say but at the same time I think that you yourself have to find
your own way. We shall do what we can. Come to me when
you get leave for this year. Perhaps I may not engage you
in the work here. But we shall have a talk and if I can throw
some light on the issue I will do so. There is no hitch from
my side but do as is convenient to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy : National Archives of India
