454. LETTER TO RAMCHANDRA N. KHARE
August 26, 1932
CHI. RAMBHAU,
Was the Hindi of your letter your own or did you write it with
Parasramji's help? The handwriting was good. If the Hindi was
your own, it was good. I hope to get a detailed description of the
Janmashtami celebration.
A few sentences in English will give me some idea of your
progress in that language.
BAPU
[PS.]
The "Om" which you have carved is good enough, but I
don't think it is clear enough to be recognizable. How much
time did it take you to carve it?
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 296. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
