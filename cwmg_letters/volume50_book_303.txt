303. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
July 31, 1932
CHI. RAMA (JOSHI)1,
If there is no letter from you in the next post, I will kick up
a row. I want a detailed letter, giving me information of personal
interest and about the activities of the group as a whole. How are
Vimu and Dhiru? Do you find the Ashram the same place that
it was, or better or worse than it was?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5335
