529. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
January 8, 1933
MY DEAR C. R.,
You are silent, but I know that means overwork. Here is a
copy of Rao Bahadur Seshu Aiyar's letter. There is much in his
suggestions which I like. I have simply sent him an acknow-
ledgment saying that temple-entry propaganda should go side
by side with the working of his suggestions wherever such work is
feasible.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19197
