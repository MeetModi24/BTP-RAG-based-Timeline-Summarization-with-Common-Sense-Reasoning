494. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
ON THE TRAIN TO WARDHA,
March 3, 1940
CHI. PREMI, 3
You have given me news regularly. I wonder if you are
still there. I hope the progress continues.
Love to you all.
BAPU
SHRI PREMIBEHN
C/O. DR. C. S. SAHANI
KHANDWA
From the original: C. W. 11049. Courtesy: Arjun Jairamdas
