361. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 27, 1930
CHI. MANGALA,
Are you a walking-stick or a spoilt girl or both? You
have tried to improve your handwriting. You must try harder.
Learn the second chapter by heart and pull Pushpa's nose for
me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11089. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
