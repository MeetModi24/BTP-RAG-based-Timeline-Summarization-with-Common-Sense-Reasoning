571. LETTER TO LALCHAND J. VORA
WARDHA,
September 8, 1935
BHAI LALCHAND,
You are conducting a good many useful activities. If such
work is carried on with the co-operation of all and purely in a
spirit of service, it will certainly bear fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10497. Also C.W. 9127. Cour-
tesy: Lalchand J. Vora
