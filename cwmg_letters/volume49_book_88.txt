88. LETTER TO MARCHIONESS CARLA VITELLESCHI
February 2, 1932
DEAR CARLA,
I got your letter. Yes, you are right. Had I not felt for
you as for my own daughter, I would not have spoken to you
as strongly as I did that morning. I am now glad of the step
you have taken. I am quite sure that your place is by your
husband's side. You must shake off your uncertainty and waver-
ing nature.
You may write whenever you feel like it.
May God give you light and peace.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Not remembering your full name I am addressing the en-
velope by your maiden name.
MADAM CARLA
HOTEL DES INGENIEURS
52 RUE BLANCHE
PARIS-MONTMARTRE, FRANCE
From a photostat: G.N. 2511
