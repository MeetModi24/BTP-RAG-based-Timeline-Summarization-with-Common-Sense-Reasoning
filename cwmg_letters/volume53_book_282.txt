282. LETTER TO DR. RAGHUVIR SINGH AGRAWAL
January 31, 1933
BHAI RAGHUVIRJI,
I have your letter. I am not permitted to send for doctors
from outside. They can come only with the special permission
granted by the Government. So it cannot be helped. I have gone
through your book and shall carry out the experiment prescribed
therein when I have some leisure.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
DR. R. S. AGRAWAL
RAM EYE CHARITABLE HOSPITAL
BULANDSHAHR
From Hindi: C.W. 9665. Courtesy: M. S. Agrawal
