160. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
SEVAGRAM,
June 2, 1940
MY DEAR MALKANI,
By mistake a note I wrote for the bearer to take to you was
posted. Hence this one is being given to Tejuramji. He is a good
and industrious worker. He was dreaming of founding an ashram.
I have advised him to join an institution. If you can make use of
him, he will be a valuable worker.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 934
