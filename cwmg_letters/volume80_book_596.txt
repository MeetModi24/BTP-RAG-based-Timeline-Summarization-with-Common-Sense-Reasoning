596. LETTER TO KULKARNI AND SUDHA KULKARNI
June 21, 1945
CHI. LAMBU AND KULKARNI,
I got your second letter. I have given it to Khersaheb to
read.
Blessings from
BAPU
[C/O] ADIVASI SEVA MANDAL
KANYA CHHATRALAYA
MOKHADA, THANA DISTRICT
