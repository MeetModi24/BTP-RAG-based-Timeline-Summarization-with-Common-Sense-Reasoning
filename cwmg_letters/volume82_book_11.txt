11. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
POONA,
November 2, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
Of course you can't forget me. Your post has been coming
regularly. And it gives me all the information I want.
Good Shummy gives me news about you and the family,
forbidding me to write in reply. I have taken him at his word.
Pyarelal is quite well and has also commenced some work.
I entertain the hope that he will settle down to work.
Sardar returns tomorrow from Bombay.
My programme, D.V., is : Leave here 19, Bombay 20, Sevagram
21 to 29, entrain for Calcutta 30 morning, reaching Calcutta
1st December.
Gomati has been suffering from fever of long duration, now
Chhaganlal Gandhi is laid up. Poor Kashi !
I am well. No worry on my account.
You must keep well. Love to your party and the friends
there.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4171. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7807
