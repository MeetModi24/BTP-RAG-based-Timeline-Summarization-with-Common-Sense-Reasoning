167. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Saturday, July 17, 1926
CHI. MOTI,
I have your postcard. Recovering lost health is also work.
But a person who is lethargic by nature will be lethargic in
improving his or her health as well. I hope you do not behave
in that manner. You should take every care and recoup your
health soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12131
