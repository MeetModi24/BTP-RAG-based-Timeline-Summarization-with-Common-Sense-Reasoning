283. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
WARDHA,
December 13, 1929
DEAR SISTER,
How are you now? I want you to be perfectly healthy. My
own health can be described as pretty good. I am taking milk,
curds and fruit only, no cereals at all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1665
