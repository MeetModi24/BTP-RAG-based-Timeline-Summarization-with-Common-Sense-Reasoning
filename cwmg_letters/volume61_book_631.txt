631. LETTER TO BHUJANGILAL CHHAYA
MAGANWADI, WARDHA,
September 25, 1935
CHI. BHUJANGILAL,
I am guilty of delay in replying to your letter. There is no
need at all to try to see Harilal. You yourself must decide about
your future.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 2605
