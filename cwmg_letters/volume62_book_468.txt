468. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
May 14, 1936
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
Your letter reached me only last evening. Your birthday
falls today. Where shall I send the telegram? It may not reach
you today even if I should send it now. It is 2.45 and we are
in Nandi Durg. You have my blessings of course. May you live
long and may your noble inclinations ever increase!
Pranams to Maji. Blessings to Gokibehn and Sumati .
Blessings form
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4725. Courtesy: Shantikumar
N. Morarjee
