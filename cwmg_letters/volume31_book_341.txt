341. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI
THE ASHRAM,
Thursday, August 19, 1926
BHAISHRI PUNJABHAI,
I have gone through the translation of Manache Shloka fairly
carefully. I felt that it contained quite a few errors. It was
beyond my capacity to examine it very closely. Besides, my
knowledge of Marathi is not much. I would, therefore, advise you
to get it thoroughly revised by a scholar who knows both Marathi
and Gujarati.
SJT. PUNJABHAI
ASHRAM
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 12253
