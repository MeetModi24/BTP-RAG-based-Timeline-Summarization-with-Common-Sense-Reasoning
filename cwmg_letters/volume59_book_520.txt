520. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
December 10, 1934
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I got your letters. You should not expect any article from me
just now. I can't even think what to write.
I do not know the
situation there. I do believe, however, that the Agent should
stay. Whether he is a good or a bad one will depend on our
luck. But if there were to be no Agent, nothing could be done.
What is to be done if none of you knows the art of using the
Agent well? Or probably experience will teach you that art. It
will be enough if you yourself keep away from intrigues. Should I
take it as certain now that you won't send a permit for Ramdas?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4828
