48. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL AND DURGA DESAI
[May 20, 1924]
CHI. MANI,
I got your letter and postcard. I was very pleased to read what
you say about "Tyagamurti". Purity and restraint are virtues to
be treasured. We shall discuss them when we meet. You should
now by God's grace get rid of the vestiges of the fever still troubling
you. Vasumatibehn is going to Deolali and not to Hajira. You
must give up all idea of leaving Hajira soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. DURGA,
After all you have not written to me. How do you do there?
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne
