298. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEVAGRAM,
"VIA"
WARDHA,
October 24, 1944
DEAR RAJAJI,
Herewith is a letter from Shri Joseph Victoria, a leper. Can you
do anything for this unfortunate ?
Yours sincerely,
SHRI C. RAJAGOPALACHARIAR
48 BAZLULLAH ROAD
MADRAS
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
