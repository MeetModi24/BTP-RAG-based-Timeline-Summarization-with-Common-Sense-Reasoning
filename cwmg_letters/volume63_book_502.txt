502. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
KASHI,
October 26, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
I have not been able to write to you before now. I have
just got good news from Munnalal about you both. I hope
you are well both in mind and body.
It is a matter of joy
that our weaknesses exhibit themselves as a warning to us.
Therefore the memory of the last day's incident should bring
you joy only.
Prabha is here.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6366. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9832
