295. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
June 26, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
I am enclosing a letter for Velchand. If you think it all
right, send it to him wherever he is. I had a talk with
Lakshmidas. I think he is right in being keen that Velchand
should join.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9082
