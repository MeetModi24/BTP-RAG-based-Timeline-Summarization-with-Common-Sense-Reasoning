291. LETTER TO MAURICE FRYDMAN
SEVAGRAM,
February 27, 1945
DEAR BHARATANAND 1 ,
Two lines in haste. Unless you are determined, try liver
injections and hope to live to serve. But if you regard them as
sinful I have nothing to say.
Love.
BAPU
BHARATANAND
BOMBAY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
