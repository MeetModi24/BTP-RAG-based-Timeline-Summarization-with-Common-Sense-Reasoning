475. LETTER TO A. A. SHAIKH
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 25, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. We have so many demands for free copies
of Young India that it is difficult to cope with all the applications.
You as an Association should surely be able to subscribe to the
paper if you appreciate it and you should know that Young India
is not run for profits. And if there are and when profits are
made, they are all used not for anybody's personal gain but for
corporate public work.
Yours sincerely,
A. A. SHAIKH1, ESQ.
BERLIN CHARLO
MOMMSENSTRASSE 41, GERMANY
From a photostat: S.N. 15124
