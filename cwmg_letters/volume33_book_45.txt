45. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
[February 6, 1927]
. . . has come. But can one force another to accept something as
his dharma? It binds him who voluntarily accepts it. We can
only explain things.
Ramachandra has written to Revashankarbhai about the
[water] lift. You can ask for Rs. 5,000. You should draw the rest
of the amount on the Ashram [account]. If the lift proves its uti-
lity we would recover our initial investment. What is the posi-
tion regarding the patent? Have you sent them a reply? . . .
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7774-A. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
