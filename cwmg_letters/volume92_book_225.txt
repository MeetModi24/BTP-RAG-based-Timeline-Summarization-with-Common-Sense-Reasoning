225. LETTER TO JAGAT NARAIN LAL
[1929]
Krishna was the servant of His servants. He was always
immersed in work of service without the least slackness. No task
was too small for Him. One can truly worship Him only by
following Him.
PT. JAGAT NARAIN LAL
'MAHAVI', PATNA
From a copy of the Hindi: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/119
