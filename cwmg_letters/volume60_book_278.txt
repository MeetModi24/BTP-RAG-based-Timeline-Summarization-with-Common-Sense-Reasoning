278. LETTER TO P. S. S. RAMA IYER
February 11, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is difficult for me to try to remove
your doubts.
They can only be solved by prayerful waiting.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI P. S. S. RAMA IYER
SANKESWARAM VILLAGE
CHITTUR (COCHIN)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
210
