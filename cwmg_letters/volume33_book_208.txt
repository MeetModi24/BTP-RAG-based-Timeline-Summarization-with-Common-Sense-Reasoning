208. LETTER TO VELANBEHN
March 28, 1927
Here is one sick person asking another why the latter has
fallen ill.
Now never mind if you have fallen ill. I hope you
are cheerful as ever; or are you not? In our ignorance we might
say we cannot prevent or control diseases. But can it not be
said that being cheerful in spite of ill health rests entirely with
us? So never lose spirit, though ill, and always remember Rama.
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
