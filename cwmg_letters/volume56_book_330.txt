330. LETTER TO BHAILAL M. PATEL
December 7, 1933
BHAI BHAILAL,
I got your letter. I am happy to learn that you have been
doing Harijan work with single-minded devotion.
May your
devotion remain the same for ever and your work bear fruit.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
