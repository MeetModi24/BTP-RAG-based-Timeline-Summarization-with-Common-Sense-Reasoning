600. LETTER TO P. J. ANDREASEN
March 2, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and a copy of The Secret of the
Universe.
Though you have asked me to acknowledge your letter only
after I have read the book, I do not want you to be in suspense,
because I am so preoccupied with the work on hand that I have
no notion when I shall be able to reach the book.
Yours sincerely,
P. J. ANDREASEN, ESQ.
30 EVANS WAY, BOSTON, MASS.
From a photostat: S.N. 20437
