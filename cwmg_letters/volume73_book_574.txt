574. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 1, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
You must not upset your system. The contract is not to eat
rather than eat hastily. You know, too, that you can live on fruit
juices. Take a little longer rather than endanger your health.
No sign of Shah.
Don't worry if you can't collect for Andrews Memorial.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4011. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7320
