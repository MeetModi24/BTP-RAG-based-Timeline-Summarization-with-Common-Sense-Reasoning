14. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
Silence Day [February 25, 1929]
CHI. KUSUM,
You have become somewhat slack these days. I was glad to
know that you have made it up with Gangabehn. If the three
of you become one at heart, then others may join you and the
women's wing which seems to be cracking up may become
whole again.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1779
