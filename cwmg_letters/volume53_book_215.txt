215. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
January 27, 1933
DEAR MAJOR BHANDARI,
With reference to the late Dr. Mehta's estate I need to see
the following parties in addition to the thirteen for whom permis-
sion has already been given:
(1)
Padmabehn, Dr. Mehta's grand-daughter.
(2)
Sjt. Kantilal A. Doshi, the son of the well-known Morbi
shroff and engaged to be married to (1).
(3)
Shrimati Vrajkunwar Ratilal Sheth, wife of Sjt. Ratilal
Sheth for whom permission has already been granted.
I hope that early permission will be received as I have to
advise about the marriage of (1) with (2), and it is proposed to be
celebrated in February.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
