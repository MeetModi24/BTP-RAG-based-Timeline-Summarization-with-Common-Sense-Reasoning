354. LETTER TO MARY CHESLEY
[March 1, 1935)
MY DEAR MARY C.,
I have your very wonderful letter. You must shed this fear
complex of your inability to earn your bread by the sweat of your
brow. I am not going to criticize anything just now. You must
continue to give me a vivid description of your labours and I will
tell you whenever I think you are going the wrong way. Only
both of you, all of you, must keep well.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6039. Also C.W. 3369. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
