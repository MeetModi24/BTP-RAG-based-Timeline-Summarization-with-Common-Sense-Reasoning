120. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
[June 12, 1924]
. . . It was kind of you to have written to me about Ramdas.
I agree with you that he has a modulated voice and can make
good progress in spite of his age. But the poor boy has not still
found his peace. Had he remained in Bombay in the natural
course, he would have continued his lessons, but he will not go to
Bombay particularly for music. Will you please accept his and
my thanks for yourself.
The Story of My Life, Vol. II, p. 265
