344. LETTER TO B. G. KHER
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 26, 1939
BHAI BALASAHEB,
As you are going to leave your post, take double interest
in the work on hand and have such faith that whatever seeds
you have sown will never be wasted.
This great sacrifice will
yield tenfold reward to the Congress.
Blessings from
BAPU
