410. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
June 2, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
What Kanu says about you is true. I have told him that
he should improve his handwriting. I do hope that in the mat-
ter of handwriting he will reach the ideal of Ramdas Swami.
It has started raining here today. It is raining right now.
Blessings from
BAPU
