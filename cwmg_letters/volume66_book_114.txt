114. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 29, 1937
CHI. NARANDAS,
If you have been able to draw up Rameshwari's program-
me, send it to me.
When should she reach there, by which
train and for how many days will you want her there?
I read the few lines written by Jayantilal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./ll. Also C.W. 8537: Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
