218. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 29, 1937
CHI. KANTI,
Have you boycotted me? I wait for your letter every day,
but get none. I have carried out all your wishes. What more
do you want now? I don't approve of your silence.
I had meant to write to Nanjappa at the same time that I
wrote to you. But I couldn't do so owing to pressure of work.
I don't write with my right hand on any day except Mondays,
and on Mondays the work of Harijan leaves no time for any
other work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7331. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
