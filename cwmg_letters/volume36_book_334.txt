334. LETTER TO KUNVERJI KHETSHI PAREKH
Sunday [April 29, 1928]
CHI. KUNVERJI,
I have your letter. I have never felt that you have taken up
khadi work for the sake of money, and my question arose only
from yours. Will you always be able to live contented doing khadi
work? You certainly know there is no financial gain in this.
It provides plain bread. I assume from your letter that you will
not be able to live in the Ashram if a rule is adopted that only
brahmacharis can do so. Even in that case I believe there will be
no problem in employing you in khadi work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9711
