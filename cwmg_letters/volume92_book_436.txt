436. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
Silence Day, May 4, 1931
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. You are too sensitive. You should not
be angry over what Gangabehn wrote. It is better she expresses
herself instead of keeping her feelings bottled up. Understand
her love and her service. You should continue to work patiently
and remain quiet. Do not get into argument with anyone. Do
not brood too much. Reflect over what you read and remain
cheerful. Do keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar
and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 Gangabehn Vaidya
254
