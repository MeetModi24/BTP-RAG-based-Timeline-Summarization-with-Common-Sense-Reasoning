648. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA N. KHARE
November 13, 1932
CHI. RAMACHANDRA,
I got your letter. It was good that you went and stayed with
your maternal uncle. If you had stayed for some time longer,
perhaps you would have benefited still more. I hope you will
now rapidly regain your strength.
Did you ask Premabehn's forgiveness? Has she forgiven
you?
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 300. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
