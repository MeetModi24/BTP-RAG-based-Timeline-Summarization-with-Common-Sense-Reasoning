131. LETTER TO JEKOR
July 1, 1932
DEAR JEKOR,
Those who are released go back. This is a useless question.
When there is nothing specially to ask, nothing should be asked.
But write an account of your work every week. (Your) hand-
writing is good but there is much scope for improvement yet.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 53
