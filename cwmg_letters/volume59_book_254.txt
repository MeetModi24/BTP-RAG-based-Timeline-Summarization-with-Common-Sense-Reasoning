254. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
CONGRESS CAMP,
October 22, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
I have your letter. You did not offend me by not saying
good-bye. I do not need these formalities. I hope you are doing
your work well and getting ample tuition work. I hope to be
back even before 1st November.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
