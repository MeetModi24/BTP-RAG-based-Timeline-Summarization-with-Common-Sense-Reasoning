28. LETTER TO MADALASA
POONA,
November 4, 1945
CHI. MADALASA,
You are now the mother of two sons. Janakibehn is so
delighted that she sent me two telegrams. Were it not for her
wire, I would not have known. I sent a wire in reply, which she
must have received.
I got your letter and was pleased to read it. When I return
from the tour, you may take me to your place.
It is very good indeed that your mother-in-law is with you.
I hope both of you are well.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 326
