274. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
July 24, 1938
MY DEAR AMRIT,
I don't know whether it is your misfortune or mine. I wrote
yesterday a note to you and by my stupidity it was left out. I
have been waiting for you all the morning. If you come after 2
p.m. I shall have taken the weekly silence. I see Jivraj is
coming with you. I suppose he does not want to hear my voice.
He wants to see my face and take the b.p. I hope you are quite
fit. How nice if you could walk.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3634. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6443
