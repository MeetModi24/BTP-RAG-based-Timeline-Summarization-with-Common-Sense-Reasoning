307. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
SEGAON,
August 5, 1938
CHI. AMRITLAL,
Chimanlal has been sent to hospital for an operation for
piles. Please send someone to find out if he requires anything.
If you yourself can visit him, do so.
Kaka is all right I hope. Tell him that I have sent a tele-
gram to Bal three days back.
Look after your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10752
