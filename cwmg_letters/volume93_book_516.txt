516. LETTER TO GOPAL NARAYAN SHIROMANY
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 6, 1940
DEAR SHIROMANY,
I have read the judgment. You may convey my opinion to
anyone about what cannot come under the purview of Defence of
India Rules. But what is the good of giving it to the Press? If
there is any advantage in it, do explain it to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHIROMANY
SAINIK OFFICE
AGRA, U. P.
From the Hindi original: C. W. 10943. Courtesy: Gopal Narayan
Shiromany
