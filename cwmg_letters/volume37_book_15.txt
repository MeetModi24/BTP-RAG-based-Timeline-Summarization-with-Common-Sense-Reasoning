15. LETTER TO BECHAR PARMAR
July 2, 1928
BHAISHRI BECHAR PARMAR,
The drawbacks which you think belong to the barber's profes-
sion are probably to be found in all professions. If everyone,
however, clings to his own profession for a living, there would be
minimum friction.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5567
