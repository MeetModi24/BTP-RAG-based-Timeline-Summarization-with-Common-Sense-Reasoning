398. LETTER TO T. H. BAIRD
MAHISHADAL,
December 28, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 18th November. I am forwarding your
letter and pamphlet to Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru. Your preten-
sion appears to me to be too great.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
T. H. BAIRD, ESQ.
33 PARK AVENUE
PORTOBELLO, EDINBURGH
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
