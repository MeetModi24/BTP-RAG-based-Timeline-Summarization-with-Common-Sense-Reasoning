349. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
DELHI,
December 12, 1933
BA,
This is the second letter to you in jail. I hope you are
getting Jam-e-Jamshed and Harijanbandhu now. Write to me if you
want anything else. Mathuradas is still with me. I arrived in
Delhi on the 10th. I shall be leaving for Andhradesh on the 14th
and Mathuradas will go to Bombay. Do not worry about me in
the least. I will write to you regularly and tell you exactly how
I am keeping. I take milk, mavo, etc., in sufficient quantity. The
cold which I had has disappeared. Dr. Ansari is here. I am
staying this time with Ghanshyamdasji.
a
Jmnalalji also is likely
to arrive here today. Prabhudas and his wife also are here. He
will settle and take up some work somewhere on this side. Do not
worry about Manilal. I have written to him a long letter. I don't
wish to suppress him. Santok's mother has passed away. I have
written a letter to her today. Brijkrishna is seriously ill. He is con-
fined to bed. He is being treated by Dr. Ansari. They are hopeful
that he will get all right. Radha seems to have fallen ill again.
If you or any other women want anything, write to me about it.
I hope your jaws are functioning all right. How is Mani's
knee? Have you with you anything to read?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Bane Patro, p. 2
1 A Gujarati daily published from Bombay
