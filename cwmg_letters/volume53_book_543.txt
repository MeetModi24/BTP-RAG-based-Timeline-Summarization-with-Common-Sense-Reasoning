543. LETTER TO SESHGIRI BALKRISHNA RAO SONDE
February 25, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of the 11th instant. There was a similar
letter from another friend, whose name I forget at the moment,
to whom I gave my views.
I am quite convinced that sacrifice of dumb animals to ap-
pease a deity cannot have any religious or other merit about it. It
is a sinful act. These sacrifices are a relic of barbarism and it
would be a great sin if, in the face of the anti-untouchability
movement with temple-entry as a part of it, animal sacrifices are
not stopped altogether.
You can make whatever use you like of this letter.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SESHGIRI BALKRISHNA RAO SONDE
LANDOWNER AND MERCHANT
SIRSI (N. KANARA)
