322. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
March 8, 1939
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
This is just to send you wagon-loads of love. You must be well
and strong. If [the] heat [is] unbearable and if you will remain in India,
you should move to a cooler place. You will join me in Delhi on 15th
if you are in fair condition.
Love.
UPPER HOUSE
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy : National
Archives of India
