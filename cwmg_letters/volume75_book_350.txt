350. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
February 7, 1942
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. I am writing to Mathura Babu regarding
the honey. There is no stock here just now.
There is no harm in adding salt to vegetables, but it would be
better not to. Or you may add just a little. There is no need to
add salt to uncooked vegetables.
Ripe tomatoes should never be cooked.
When you get honey, you need not eat palm gur.
Sushilabehn's address is: C/O Dr. Gopichand2, Lajpat Rai
Bhavan, Lahore.
Ba's health is not quite good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3570
