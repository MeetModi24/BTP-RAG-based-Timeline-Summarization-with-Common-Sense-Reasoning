67. LETTER TO NAJUKLAL N. CHOKSI
BANGALORE,
June 28, 1927
BHAI NAJUKLAL,
I have your letter as also Chi. Moti's. Neither her elders nor
you have been able to cure Moti of her lethargy. Let us now see
whether her offspring succeeds in doing that. I hope you have
recovered your health completely. It is some satisfaction to know
that you have not been infected by Moti. I say this because the
experience of the world is that when two persons live together
one of them cannot but be influenced by the other. Hence either
Moti will be infected by you-it is clear that she has not been—
or you will be infected by her. I am all the time afraid that you
will be infected by her. Write to me when the delivery is expected,
and even otherwise write from time to time. For some time yet
I shall remain hereabouts.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAISHRI NAJUKLAL CHOKSI
SEVASHRAM
BROACH
