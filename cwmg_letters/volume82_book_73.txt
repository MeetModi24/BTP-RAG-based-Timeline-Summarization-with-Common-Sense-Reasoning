73. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN CHAUDHARY
November 8, 1945
CHI. RAMNARAYAN,
I have your postcard. I liked it. Keep on sending your
views even if unsolicited. In the course of my tours I found
confirmation of what you wrote.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapu—Maine Kya Dekha, Kya Samjha?, p. 164
