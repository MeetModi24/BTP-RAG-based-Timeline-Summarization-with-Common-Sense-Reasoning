51. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
August 20, 1944
You have entered the forest. May you cross it and live
many more years. After all your faith in me is not blind. It is
unshakable and will remain so. Mustn't I become and continue
to be worthy of such faith?
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 202
