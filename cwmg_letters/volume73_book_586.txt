586. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
April 3, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Your writing to me was all right. Time will solve many of the
problems. It is for you to find out a way of living that will satisfy
you, for who else can understand what will satisfy you? There
is always scope for independent work. And as for being released
from me, that will be possible only when you ask for it. The game,
therefore, is entirely in your hands. If you still feel troubled,
what is to be done? But God is always there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8508. Also C.W. 7128. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
