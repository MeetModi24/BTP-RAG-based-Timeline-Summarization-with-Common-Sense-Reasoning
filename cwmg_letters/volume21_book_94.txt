94. LETTER TO BANARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
ON THE TRAIN,
September 25, 1921
BHAISHRI,
I read your postcard only now.
I could not send you the money. I shall now give it to you in
Bombay. I shall arrive in Bombay on the 2nd October and will
stay there till the 4th. I want you to be in Bombay while I am
there.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
BANARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
SHANTINIKETAN
BOLPUR
E. I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2578
