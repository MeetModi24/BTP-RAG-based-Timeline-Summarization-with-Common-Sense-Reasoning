380. LETTER TO HUSSAIN
MUSSOORIE,
June 7, 1946
BHAI HUSSAINBHAI,
I have your letter of May 22, 1946.
You are needlessly worried. Why should such a large and
educated community as the Shias feel helpless?
Your son had been to see me in Delhi. I appeased him by
exchanging a few pleasantries. More when we meet some time.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
PRESIDENT
ALL-PARTIES SHIA CONFERENCE
BOMBAY
