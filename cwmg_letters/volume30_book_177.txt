177. LETTER TO LALJI
ASHRAM,
March 23, 1926
BHAI LALJI,
Your letter. What you have heard about my vow is correct.
Hence I shall have to rest satisfied, with no more than wishing
your conference all success. I want every Antyaja to abstain from
drinking and give up meat-eating; and also to resolve to wear
khadi exclusively.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19882
