413. LETTER TO MADALASA
NEW DELHI,
September 16, 1946
CHI. SILLY MADU,
I got your silly letter. Even so I find it sweet. You are as
silly as ever. With Shriman looking after all your affairs, how
are you ever going to be wise? Kamalnayan has immersed
himself in business running into lakhs. The sisters are busy
with their families. What is so strange about his going his
own way? Don't mind Savitri leaving. Enjoy yourself and be
happy. Leave all to Rama—even Kamalnayan. Nothing is
going to happen to him as long as God protects him. Don't
worry about anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 329
