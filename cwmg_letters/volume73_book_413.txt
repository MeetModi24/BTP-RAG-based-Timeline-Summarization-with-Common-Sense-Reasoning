413. LETTER TO T. B. SAPRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
February 9, 1941
DEAR SIR TEJ BAHADUR,
Your letter has just reached me. It disconcerts me somewhat.
I think I have told you that I am not going to write to him unless
I know that he wants to come to some settlement. What I do
know is all to the contrary. I wish I had a copy of your letter
to Qaid-e-Azam Jinnah. Of course I do not want you to do any-
thing further so as to remove misunderstanding, if there is any.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 7582. Also C.W. 10281. Courtesy: National
