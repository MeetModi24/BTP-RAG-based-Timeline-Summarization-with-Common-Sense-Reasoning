141. LETTER TO PUNDALIK KATAGDE
September 17, 1944
BHAI PUNDALIK,
I have your letter. I do believe that those who have com-
mitted mistakes should admit them only if they are convinced
that it is their duty to do so, and not because I want them
to. The call for observance of duty must come from within.
Hence I will not issue a public statement. It would be misunder-
stood.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4226
