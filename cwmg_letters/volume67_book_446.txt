446. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
September 5, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Herewith a telegram for Devdas. We will not take any-
thing in Harijan in that way. Your labour, however, has not
been wasted.
I will get your note typed here. My article is almost ready.
If it is ready earlier, or even otherwise, I will send it complete
from here. Somebody will come.
Your letter was welcome. It is brave of you to write direct-
ly to me. I have torn up the letter after reading it. It is a
strange story! I will write when you come. There is nothing to
worry about. The thing is so ridiculous. Do we have the time
just now for such things? Do your duty without thinking about
my blood-pressure. It would have been altogether wrong if
you had not written the letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11640
