655. LETTER TO P. B. CHANDWANI
BOMBAY,
April 15, 1945
CHI. CHANDWANI,
I was very happy to have your Hindi letter. Learn Hindi
thoroughly. I will try to go to Mahabaleshwar on the 20th.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI P. B. CHANDWANI
OLD SUKKUR
SUKKUR
SIND
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
