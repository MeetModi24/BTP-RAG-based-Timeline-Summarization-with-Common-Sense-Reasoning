186. LETTER TO R. D. RANADE
NEW DELHI,
June 27, 1947
BHAI RANADE,
Why should you atone for any error that I may commit?
It is totally against Hinduism.
"Raghupati Raghava" is not a bhajan, it is a Ramadhun. This
refrain is nobody's monopoly.
