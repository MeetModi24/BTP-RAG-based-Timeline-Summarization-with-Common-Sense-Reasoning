515. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
May 10, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
We should not follow anybody. If you wish to get your hair
cropped, you should go ahead and do so. You will not then feel
the weight of hair on your head. Your time will be saved, and
there will be no more dirt in the hair. However, if you wish
you may let your hair remain. Don't mind if people call you
a bear. That is only a good-humoured jest.
From Gujarati: C.W. 9443. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
