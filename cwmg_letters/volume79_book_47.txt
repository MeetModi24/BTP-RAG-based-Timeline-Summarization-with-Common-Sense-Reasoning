47. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
January 12, 1945
CHI. LILI,
I have your letter. I am daily getting stronger. I was
glad that this time you felt satisfied. You served me with great
devotion and have gone there well in time. It does not matter if
you have lost a day; you have not missed your lessons. Even
where you are, it is me you are serving. Do your work in that
faith. You are then bound to pass. You are violating dharma in
desiring to leave before I do. What would happen if all the
workers did that?
I never said that the students should observe a strike on the
9th. On the contrary they should devote themselves all the
more to service on that day. That would please the teachers, too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 9599. Also C.W. 6571. Courtesy:
