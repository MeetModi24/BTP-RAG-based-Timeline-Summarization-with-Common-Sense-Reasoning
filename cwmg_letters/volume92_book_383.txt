383. LETTER TO KRISHNA NEHRU
Y.M.,
November 15, 1930
CHI. KRISHNA,
What a clever girl you are! You came out so easily. Let
us see what you do now. Did you have any information about
that crazy man who paid the fine? The same thing happened
to Shyam Kumari. All of you will be having enough chances. You
need not feel sad.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C. W. 10868. Courtesy: Selected Works of
Jawaharlal Nehru
