96. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 3, 1940
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
You may build a house in the Harijan Colony if Bapa and
others approve. I have understood the other matters. Do what-
ever you think right. I understand what you say about spinning.
Has the spinning programme undertaken by the Congress helped
khadi work?
I am all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2482
