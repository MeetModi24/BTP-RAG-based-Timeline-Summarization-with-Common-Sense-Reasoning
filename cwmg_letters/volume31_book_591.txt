591. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
Asvina Vad 9, 1982, October 30, 1926
CHI. MOTI,
I have your letter. My mind feels [now] at ease. On read-
ing the postcard, I felt that I should now talk to Lakshmidas,
and I have done so. May God give you the strength to stick
to your resolution. Do not give up the practice of writing to me
from time to time. There was much illness here, but things are
improving now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12139
