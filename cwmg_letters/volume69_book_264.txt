264. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
BOMBAY,
May 11, 1939
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your letter. Yes, it would be good if you could rid
yourself of your fondness for books. You can certainly purchase
on my behalf any religious book you may need, but for the
rest you should seek your brother's help. What I mean is: have
just one book. Draw all your wisdom from that one book. What
better do you need than the Ramayana, the Gita or Ishopanishad?
Balkrishna's fever causes concern. Sushila will write about
it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Mahadev will remain with me.
