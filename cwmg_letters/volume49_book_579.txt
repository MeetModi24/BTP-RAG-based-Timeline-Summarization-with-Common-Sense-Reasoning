579. LETTER TO SATYACHARAN LAW
May 24, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your circular letter about Acharya Ray's seventieth
birthday celebration. I send you the accompanying humble tri-
bute in the hope that it would be allowed to reach your hands
by the authorities.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. SATYACHARAN LAW
SECRETARY
ACHARYA RAY'S BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION COMMITTEE
CALCUTTA
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. I
