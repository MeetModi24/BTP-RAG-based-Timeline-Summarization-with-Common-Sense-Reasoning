10. LETTER TO K. R. KRISHNA MURTHI
January 11, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of 6th instant, and I should be
sorry if what you said was found to be correct.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. R. KRISHNA MURTHI
JOINT SECRETARY
THE DHARMA VEERA ASSOCIATION
GURUVAYUR
From a microfilm: S.N. 19219
