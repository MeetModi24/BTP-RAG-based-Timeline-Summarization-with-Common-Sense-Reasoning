464. LETTER TO JETHALAL G. SAMPAT
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 23, 1944
CHI. JETHALAL,
I am not at all in a condition to write to anybody, but how
can I remain without sending you an acknowledgement? I got
your detailed letter. I hope you will be able to make your way
through the difficulties. You must have written to Jajuji.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9872. Courtesy: Narayan J. Sampat
