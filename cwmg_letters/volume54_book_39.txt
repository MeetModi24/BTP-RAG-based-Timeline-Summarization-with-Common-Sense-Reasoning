39. LETTER TO THE MANAGER, "MILAP"
March 10, 1933
THE MANAGER
THE "MILAP"
LAHORE
DEAR FRIEND,
It was not possible to send you any message in reply to your
telegram. I am therefore returning the voucher received by me so
as to enable you to collect the rupee.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm : S.N. 20527
