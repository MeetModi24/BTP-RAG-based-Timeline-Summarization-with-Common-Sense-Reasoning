502. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
January 31, 1932
CHI. TARAMATI,
I hope you are not upset because of the absence of
Mathuradas. How is Dilip? Do write to me. Did you visit
Mathuradas? Where is he? What does he get to eat? How is
his health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
