207. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
April 8, 1947
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I have your telegram . I want to make public use of all the
telegrams.
I would not be sorry even if everyone there gets killed al-
though innocent.
No letters from Pyarelal, Amtussalaam and others. I shall
enclose with this as many [letters] as possible.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 8975
