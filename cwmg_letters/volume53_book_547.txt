547. LETTER TO M. C. RAJAH
February 25, 1933
DEAR RAO BAHADUR,
I thank you for your letter of 22nd instant enclosing copy of
your Bill to amend the Code of Criminal Procedure. I shall
surely study it and publish the text in the next issue of Harijan
and comment upon it. If there is anything special about the
Bill which I cannot incorporate in my note in the Harijan I shall
certainly write to you about it separately.
I hope you are get-
ting your copy of the Harijan. You will please let me have any
comment you may have on it.
I am glad you are doing your best to expedite the passage of
the two Untouchability Bills.
Why should you thank me for any humble effort that I can
make for the common cause? Am I not myself a member of
the community? I may not be yet a fit member, but I shall
leave no stone unturned to become fit.
Yours sincerely,
RAO BAHADUR M. C. RAJAH, M.L.A.
ROYAL HOTEL, DELHI
