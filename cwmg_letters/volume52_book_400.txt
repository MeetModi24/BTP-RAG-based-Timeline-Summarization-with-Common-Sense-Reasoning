400. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
December 26, 1932
BHAI MUNSHI,
Your letter did not require an immediate reply. Meanwhile,
Anandshankarbhai gave me news about your health. He painted
a more serious picture of it than I had thought. And that has
prompted me to suggest my quack-remedy. Madan gives
nature-cure treatment. If, with Dr. Gilder's permission and after
careful inquiry, you take it, it will probably help you.
It seems Jijima is not going. My congratulations to her. I do
hope she will complete a hundred.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7523. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
