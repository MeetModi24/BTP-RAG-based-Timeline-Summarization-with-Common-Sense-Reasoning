393. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Sunday night, Vaisakha Sud 1 [May 4, 1924]
CHI. VASUMATI,
You need not stop writing to me. On the contrary, if there is
no letter from you, I would feel perplexed. It will be enough if I
have the freedom to reply at my convenience. I am getting better.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the original Gujarati: C.W. 614. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
