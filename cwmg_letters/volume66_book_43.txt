43. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[August 11, 1937]
CHI. MAHADEV,
Janba hasn't come yet. But I am sending whatever is ready.
Mohanlal's cheque also is enclosed. Credit the money to the
account for Harijan work.
Bhansali must have arrived there. I have sent some material
with him, too. Send [the enclosed to] Amtul Salaam.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11549
