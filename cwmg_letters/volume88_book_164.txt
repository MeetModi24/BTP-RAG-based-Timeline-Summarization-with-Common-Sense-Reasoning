164. LETTER TO DILKHUSH DIWANJI
NEW DELHI,
June 24, 1947
CHI. DILKHUSH DIWANJI,
Parents' blessings do not cease when they die. Though the
body perishes, the atman inhabiting it certainly does not. You
must free yourself from the delusion that it does.
I remember your mother. She was very particular about
punctuality. I have passed on the cheque for Rs. 74 for Harijan
work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10432. Courtesy: Dilkhush Diwanji
