587. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 14, 1935
DEAR DAUGHTER,
Why can we not address the daughter as Bibi? Why should
you worry about repaying Devdas? You have no need now to
purchase the ticket for the Sammelan. We shall see on my arrival
there. More on meeting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 324
