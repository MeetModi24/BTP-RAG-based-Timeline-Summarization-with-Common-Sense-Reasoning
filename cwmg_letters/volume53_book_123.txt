123. LETTER TO S. SALIVATI
January 20, 1933
MY DEAR SALIVATI,
I had your telegram and your letter1, for which I thank
you.
I think that your letter seems completely exonerative. But
I would have preferred to have seen the copy of your original
despatch. In any case, I never questioned the goodness of your
motive.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18978
