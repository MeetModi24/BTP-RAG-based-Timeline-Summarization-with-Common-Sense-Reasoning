564. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
Asvina Krishnapaksha 2, 1982 [October 23, 1926]
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I have your letters. I am happy every time to read that you
keep good health. Quite a few people are ill here these days. How
is the weather there? In all, how many charkhas are plying?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6527
