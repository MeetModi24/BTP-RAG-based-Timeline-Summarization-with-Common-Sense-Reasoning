119. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
JUHU,
May 22, 1944
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I got your wire and letter. Pyarelal has replied to them. I
have been given permission to write to a few and am, therefore, writ-
ing this letter. If your health is good enough, give there whatever
service you can, otherwise go to Sevagram. I am regaining
strength fairly well. My health is certainly better than yours.
But who will let me work, and am I a person who will work?
Kanti arrived here yesterday. He is all right. He will return
to Mysore in a couple of days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 477
