39. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
September 29, 1933
CHI. MANI,
I got your postcard. Stay there as long as necessary and
get well, and then come. I also had a letter from Father, from
which I have come to know that now Chandubhai is with him.
I am very glad. Continue to write to me. Inform Dahyabhai
that I have written to Karamchand and replied to the questions.
I am well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANIBEHN PATEL
PAREKH STREET
SANDHURST ROAD
BOMBAY 4
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 108
