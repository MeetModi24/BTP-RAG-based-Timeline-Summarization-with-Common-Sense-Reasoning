151. LETTER TO KHANDUBHAI K. DESAI
November 19, 1945
BHAI KHANDUBHAI,
What message can a labourer give to a labour union? I
have not heard of anyone sending a message to himself.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KHANDUBHAI DESAI
MAJOOR MAHAJAN SANGH
LAL DARWAJA
