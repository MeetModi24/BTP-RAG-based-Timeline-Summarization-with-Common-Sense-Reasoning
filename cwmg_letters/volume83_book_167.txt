167. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD, POONA,
February 20, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
I refer to the letter I wrote on November 25, 1945, when
Sir Evan Jenkins was P. S. V. He asked me to send Shri Prabhu
Dayal Vidyarthi to New Delhi which I did. Since then my son
has been in correspondence with me as he was seen by Sir John
Thorne in this connection. But I have had no official intimation
of the result of the examination or what is proposed to be done.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 64
