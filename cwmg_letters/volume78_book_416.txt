416. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 16, 1944
CHI. LILI,
I have your two letters. People die all over the world and
they will go on dying. For your part you have to excel in
your studies. Everything else will follow. Try to build up the
atmosphere of Sevagram as far as possible.
Blessings from
BAPU
LILAVATI UDESHI
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
