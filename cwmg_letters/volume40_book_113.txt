113. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
RANGOON,
March 11, 1929
BHAISHRI KHAMBHATTA,
As there is a case pending against me in a Calcutta court, a
definite date can be given only after the hearing of the case. You
should not, therefore, expect from me much advance notice. Wire
to me at Calcutta on the 25th. If you are keen that this ceremony
should be performed only by me, I request you not to be in a
hurry for the present. Wait till I have more time.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
275 HORNBY ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6592
