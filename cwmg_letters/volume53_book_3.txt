3. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
January 11, 1933
MY DEAR GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your letter of the 6th instant. I am surprised that
Kanhaiyalal should have written to you. I do know him well
by correspondence. He has been in the Ashram sent by Soniramji.
He frequently sends me questions for solution. He should not
have written to you without reference to me or at least to
Narandas. You need not think of him any more.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a copy: C.W. 7918. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
