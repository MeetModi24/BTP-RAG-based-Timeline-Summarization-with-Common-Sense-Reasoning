422. LETTER TO NILKANTA DAS
As AT SABARMATI,
June 6, 1931
DEAR NILKANTA BABU,
I have your letter. I can well understand the position but in
cases like the one you mentioned very little can be done just now.
I can only say that if in spite of these provocations, peaceful picket-
ing cannot be continued it should be suspended for a while. But
propaganda of an intensive type should be taken by going to the
people in their own homes.
CH. NILKANTA DAS
BHUBANESHWAR
