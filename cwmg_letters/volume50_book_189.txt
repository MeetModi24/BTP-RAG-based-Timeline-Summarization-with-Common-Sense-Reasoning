189. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
July 8, 1932
DEAR MAHAVIR,
Now you must have received the money. All should keep
the diary complete. You all write to me the full programme
for a day or two. Durga must be better now.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(3), Pt. II, p. 199
