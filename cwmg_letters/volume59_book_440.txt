440. LETTER TO SATISH MITTER
November 21, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your warm letter and a copy of your book
which, I know, I shall read with interest and profit.
If you
have anything new in the supplement of your book, you will
please pass it on to me. I see you have missed the most impor-
tant conchshell bangles of Bengal.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI SATISH MITTER
2 LOUDON STREET
CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
