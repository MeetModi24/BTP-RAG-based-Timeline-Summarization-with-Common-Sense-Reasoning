31. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
June 7, 1936
CHI. MARY,
I return the letter from Hardwar and retain the other
papers sent by you.
Sumitra had left for Nagpur when your letter and mine
were received at the Gurukul. I now send them all to you.
If Gopal is there, he might know her address.
As to Tara's other papers and things, they should be left
untouched till we know what can be done with the will. We
have first to trace her relatives who may be interested in her
property. The bicycle can certainly be used by Gopal. Simi-
larly you may use the sita[r].
I fear Dr. Nur Jahan has to be asked to keep her English
things with her for the time being.
We reach Wardha [on
14th].
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6062. Also C.W. 3392. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
