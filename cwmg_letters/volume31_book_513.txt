513. LETTER TO KALYANJI V. MEHTA
Bhadra Vad 14, 1982 [October 5, 1926]
BHAISHRI KALYANJI,
A letter was written to you, at my instance, concerning Bhai
Manibhai. I shall be happy to have your immediate reply.
Manibhai, too, is worrying himself about the matter.
BAPU
BHAISHRI KALYANJI VITHALBHAI MEHTA
SWARAJ ASHRAM
BARDOLI
Via SURAT
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2679
