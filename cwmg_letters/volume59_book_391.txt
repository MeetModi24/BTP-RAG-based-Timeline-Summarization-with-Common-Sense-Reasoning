391. LETTER TO TEJ RAM
November 14, 1934
DEAR RAI BAHADUR,
I have gone through your scheme of improvement of villa-
ges. You will pardon me for saying that it does not appeal to
me as practical. What are the improved tools that you have
made? I would like you to follow the progress of the Association
that is being formed and then say whether you can make any
contribution to it. I am passing your letter on to Sjt. Kumarappa.
Yours sincerely,
RAI BAHADUR TEJ RAM
RETIRED EXECUTIVE ENGINEER
SANDA ROAD, LAHORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
