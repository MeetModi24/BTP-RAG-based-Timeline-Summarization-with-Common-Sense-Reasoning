100. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
NEW DELHI,
April 29, 1946
CHI. MRIDULA 3 ,
I got your letters. I am overburdened with work.
I showed your letter to Sucheta. She will go to Jharia and
work there. I see that at present your presence in Ahmedabad
is necessary.
I hope you are taking care of your health. Do continue the
hip-bath and the friction-bath. Have the mud-packs at night. I
hope Saraladevi is maintaining good health. What is the news
about Nimubehn?
Regarding me nothing is definite. I shall know in a day or
two.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
