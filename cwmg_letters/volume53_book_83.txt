83. LETTER TO BINDU DASTANE
January 16, 1933
Bangles and kumkum are the marks of a married woman or
a maiden eligible for marriage. Therefore one who desires mar-
riage may certainly adorn herself with both. You may, by all
means, wear the bangles and the kumkum if you are fond of
them. Anyway, you may wear them if Mother wants you to;
you must not hurt her feelings.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 57
