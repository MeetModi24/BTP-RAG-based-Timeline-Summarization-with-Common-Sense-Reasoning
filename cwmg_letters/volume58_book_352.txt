352. LETTER TO N. R. NADIG
August 19, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is beyond my power to give you any
help; nor can I make any suggestion as to the way in which
you should go about your work.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. N. R . NADIG
W. T . SANITORIUM
MIRAJ
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
