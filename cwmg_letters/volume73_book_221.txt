221. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
November 25, 1940
DEAR ANAND,
Your letters are helpful. Continue. How is Jairamdas? How
about your ears? How about Vidya?
BAPU
SHRI ANAND T. HINGORANI
UPPER SIND COLONY
KARACHI SADAR
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India, and Anand T.
Hingorani
