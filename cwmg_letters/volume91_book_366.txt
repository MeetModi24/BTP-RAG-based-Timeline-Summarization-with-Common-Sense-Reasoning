366. LETTER TO INDRA VIDYAVACHASPATI
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 28 [1925]
MY DEAR INDRAJI 2 ,
I am sending you herewith something received from a correspondent.
What have you to say regarding the statements made therein? Are there really
any editions of the Satyartha Prakash which do not contain Chap. XIV about
Islam? Was the chapter written or inserted after the death of the Maharshi 3 ?
And if so, by whom? Gandhiji wants your detailed reply and he thinks
only you could enlighten him in the matter.
What is this rioting in Delhi? Newspaper accounts are meagre. We read
something whilst we were in Travancore, and have heard nothing more.
Yours sincerely,
MAHADEV DESAI
[PS.]
Please return enclosure with your reply.
From a photostat: G.N. 7207. Also C. W. 4867. Courtesy: Chandra-
gupta Vidyalankar
1 From the reference to Gandhiji's visit to Travancore, which took
place in March, 1925
2 Son of Swami Shraddhanand
3 Swami Dayanand
