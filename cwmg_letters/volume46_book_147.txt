147. LETTER TO LAKSHMINARAYAN GADODIA
May 10, 1931
BHAI LAKSHMINARAYANJI,
I have your letter.
I saw the letter from Alexander Drew. Your reply is perti-
nent. I regard this loss as gain. The condition of the Con-
gress committees is everywhere poor.
There is much risk in picketing the ships bringing foreign cloth.
We shall see what is possible and proper if the struggle is renewed.
The best work is to work among the masses.
I am proceeding to Simla tomorrow (Monday). The halt at
Delhi will be for about three hours. Jamnalalji will be accompany-
ing me.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
