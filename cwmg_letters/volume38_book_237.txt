237. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
WARDHA,
Silence Day, December 10, 1928
CHI. KUSUM,
I got your letter. It is good that you have started taking
quinine every day. What about hip-baths? It is very necessary
to take them. They will definitely counteract the toxic effects of
quinine.
You can accept service from Kanti One who is always ready
to serve may certainly accept service from others. This will do
for today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1766
