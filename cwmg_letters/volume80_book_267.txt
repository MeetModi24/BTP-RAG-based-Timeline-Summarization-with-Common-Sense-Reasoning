267. LETTER TO SARAYU DHOTRE
May 21, 1945
I got your letter yesterday after 5 o'clock. I could not have
got the reply posted then. I have written to Rameshwardasji
today. Let us see what happens. Never forget that we are
poor. We have no right to behave like the rich. Our right
comes from our duty. Do you understand that we must "re-
nounce and enjoy" the things we easily get?
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. SARAYU (DHOTRE)
KHADI VIDYALAYA
From Hindi : C.W. 839. Courtesy: Sarayu Dhotre
