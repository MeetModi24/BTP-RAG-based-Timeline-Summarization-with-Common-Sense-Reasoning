474. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
ABBOTTABAD,
July 15, 1939
CHI. LILA,
I got all the news about you. Without getting nervous try
and overcome your difficulties. You should study only as much as
you can digest. You should take proper rest. Take part in the
girls' games. See that you don't get brain-fag. It is good that
you go to school walking.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9592. Also C.W. 6564. Court-
esy: Lilavati Asar
