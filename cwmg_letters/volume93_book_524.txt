524. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
June 24, 1940
CHI. LILA,
I have received your letters. You have settled well. Have
you met Shankar? He will be of maximum help in the matter
of books. Such books are available even second-hand. We
shall try from here of course. Sushila does not have any.
Continue to study patiently. Meet Radha. She must be having
some books. Do not hesitate to go to her. Today Sushi's
tonsils were removed. Mahadev, etc., are at the hospital.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI LILAVATIBEHN ASAR
KANJIKHETSI KANYA CHHATRALAYA
65 MINT ROAD
BOMBAY FORT
From the Gujarati original: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
