343. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
SEVAGRAM,
March 5, 1945
BAPA,
Herewith the statement on Orissa. Whatever changes it was
possible to make have been made. Ramabehn intends to take
the responsibility. We have therefore to place it before the com-
mittee. Chi. Mridula most probably knows about it. The medi-
cal part of it will have to be shown to the doctors.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
