112. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 15, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Today nothing more than a postcard. There is no letter from you.
Asaf Ali will now be coming. Prabhakar is all right. The homoeopath
can do massage also. I have been having my stomach and back
massaged by him for the last two days. There is definite improvement.
More about it later.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
