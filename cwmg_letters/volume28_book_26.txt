26. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Shravana Vad 7 [August 10, 1925]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. Your handwriting is very clear, I want it
just like this every time. Both of you will be happy to know that
my weight is nearly eight stone, i.e., 112 lb. I had come down
to 106 or 108 in Juhu. I was weighed in the Jamshedpur Hospital.
The Dadabhai Centenary falls on 4th September. I will
definitely go to Bombay then. So I shall have only a few days
at the Ashram. I must reach Bihar on the 12th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: C.W. 591 also S.N. 9346.
Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
