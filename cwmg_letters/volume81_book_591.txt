591. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS TANDON
October 8, 1945
BHAI TANDONJI,
I have your letter dated October 1, 1945. How can I ride
two horses? Who will understand me when I say rashtrabhasha=
Hindi and rashtrabhasha=Hindi+Urdu=Hindustani? I shall certainly
serve the cause of Hindi, but by remaining out. The Standing
Committee should excuse me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
PURUSHOTTAMDAS TANDON
HINDI SAHITYA SAMMELAN
ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
