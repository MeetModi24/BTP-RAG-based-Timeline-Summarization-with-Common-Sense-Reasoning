491. LETTER TO KULWANT SINGH
SEVAGRAM,
March 22, 1945
SARDARJI,
Why should you write to me in English? The simple answer
to the question you have put to me is that those who are in the
Congress remain in it as its servants. I am not even a member
of it. However I am its servant.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SARDAR KULWANT SINGH
P. O. MOGHAPURA
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
