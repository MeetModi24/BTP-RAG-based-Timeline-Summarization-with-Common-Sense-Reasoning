475. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 19, 1945
CHI. SUSHILA,
It is 9 p.m. just now. But let me write a few lines. I am
busy the whole day, except for massage and so on. I am go-
ing to Bombay tomorrow night. Shall be back here after stay-
ing there for three days.
Your letter is good. The description is vivid. Never mind
if we get no help from the Government. Hire a car if it is
necessary. We shall see later about buying a car or a lorry.
Pyarelal is the same. What can I add to what you already
know?
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
WARDHA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
