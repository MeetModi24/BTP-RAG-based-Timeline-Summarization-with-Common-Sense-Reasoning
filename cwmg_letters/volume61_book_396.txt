396. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
July 21, 1935
BHAI MOOLCHAND,
The wife has an equal right to the husband's earnings. But it
does not mean that she can utilize the money just as she pleases.
Your decision to maintain an account is quite correct.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 838
