122. LETTER TO SUKHDEV PRASAD SINHA
SABARMATI,
March 13, 1926
BHAI SHUKDEV PRASAD SINHA,
Your letter. A vow applies to only good deeds. One does
not resolve to do something wrong. If through ignorance one
should make any such vow it is one's duty to break it. For ins-
tance if one had vowed to commit adultery, one should withdraw
from it promptly, on coming to one's senses. If one does not, one
sins.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
