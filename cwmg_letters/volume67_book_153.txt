153. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
[June 6, 1938]
CHI. MANILAL,
I forgot yesterday to enclose a letter for Kanam. Here it is.
I am sending the message also. I had almost forgotten about it.
Don't worry about me. You are bound to feel the pain
of separation, but you should bear it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4874
