505. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
August 20, 1935
BHAI VALLABHBHAI
I got your letter. The Committee is well constituted. It
is essential that it should finish its work as soon as possible. . . .1
Morarji and Chandubhai are arriving here on the 25th morning.
How are your piles?
Kumarappa still shows signs of fever. I intend to get him
examined by the Civil Surgeon today.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 180
