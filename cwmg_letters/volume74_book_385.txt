385. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
August 15, 1941
DAUGHTER,
Today I will not write in Urdu. Your being with Kakasaheb
is by itself half the medicine. If he gets congenial company and
the kind of music he likes, what else can he want? You two
sisters should now leave only after fully restoring Kakasaheb to
health. After that, I will give Saroj her fill of khakharas and you a
slap.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9683
