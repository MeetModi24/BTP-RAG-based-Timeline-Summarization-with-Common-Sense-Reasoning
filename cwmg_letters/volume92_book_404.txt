404. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
January 9, 1931
CHI. VIDYA (HYDERABAD),
I have your letter. You have to improve your handwriting.
You should practise with a printed book before you or you
should learn from someone who can write Devanagari well. Do
not be lazy in taking hip-baths.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
