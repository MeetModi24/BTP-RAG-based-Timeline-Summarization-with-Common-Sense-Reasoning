79. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Bhadarva Sud 10 [September 8, 1924]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your two letters. I did not reply to the first one as
it was not clear where you were staying. You should keep very
well there. Recover your health completely. Nothing has been
decided yet about my journey. I shall know about it during the
course of this week.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRIMATI VASUMATI PANDIT
C/O MR. AMBALAL MATHURADAS
MESSRS STRAUSS & CO.
BATALA
PUNJAB
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 455. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
