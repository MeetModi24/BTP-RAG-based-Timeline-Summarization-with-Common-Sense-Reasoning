185. LETTER TO HARJIVAN KOTAK
August 13, 1945
CHI. HARJIVAN,
I have your letter. You have the zeal, so you will certainly
get the work. My mind is working in a different way these days;
so I shall not be able to suggest anything. It is very difficult to
accommodate you in Poona. So meet me only when I return to
Sevagram.
Blessings from
BAPU
HARJIVAN KOTAK
GRAMODYOG GANDHI HAT
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
