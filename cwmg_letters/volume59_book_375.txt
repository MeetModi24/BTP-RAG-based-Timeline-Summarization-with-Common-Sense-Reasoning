375. LETTER TO G. N. KANITKAR
November 14, 1934
DEAR BALUKAKA,
The list you have sent me is interesting. I hope you will
take up sustained and silent work with a fixed determination
not to give it up.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI G. N. (alias BALUKAKA) KANITKAR
SVAVALAMBAN RASHTRIYA PATHSHALA
CHINCHAWAD, POONA DT.
From a photostat: C.W. 965. Courtesy: G. N. Kanitkar. Also Pyarelal
Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
