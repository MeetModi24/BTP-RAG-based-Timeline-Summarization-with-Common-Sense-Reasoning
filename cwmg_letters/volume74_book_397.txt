397. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
August 20, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
What you say in your letter is perfectly correct. When I
get up at the sound of the bell, I look at the watch and when
twenty minutes pass I start the prayers.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8486. Also C.W.7152. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
