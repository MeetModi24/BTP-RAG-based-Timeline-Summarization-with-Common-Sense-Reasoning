107. LETTER TO IMMIGRATION OFFICER
[JOHANNESBURG,
August 10, 1913]
THE IMMIGRATION OFFICER,
PRETORIA
SIR,
I understand that an Indian named Purshotam Mavji was,
whilst on his way to India, deprived of his registration certificate
last week. This certificate was obtained by him in terms of Act
36 of 1908. Will you kindly let me know whether the information
given to me is correct, and if it is, in terms of which section of the
new Act Mr. Purshotam Mavji was deprived of his certificate?
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
From a photostat of the draft in Gandhiji's hand: S. N. 5854
