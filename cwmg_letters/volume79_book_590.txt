590. LETTER TO PARNAM JEWANAM
BOMBAY,
April 4, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter.
I have no doctor in view for you. You should seek Rajaji's
assistance or Shri Jagadisan's who is deeply interested in the leper
problem.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI PARNAM JEWANAM
MADRAS HINDU SEVAK SANGH
MADRAS, S. INDIA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
