243. LETTER TO KEDARNATH SANDILYA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 15, 1945
BHAI KEDARNATH,
You alone should decide the matter about which you write.
I have explained the general rules. You should follow them as
much as you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KEDARNATH SANDILYA
C/O SUB-POST MASTER
JEHANABAD
GAYA (BIHAR)
