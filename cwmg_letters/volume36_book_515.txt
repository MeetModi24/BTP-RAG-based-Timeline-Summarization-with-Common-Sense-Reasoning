515. LETTER TO DEVI WEST
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 22, 1928
I have your letter. I had the charming photograph also.
I call it 'charming' because it is lifelike.
What you say about Maganlal is too true.
I wish I had the time to describe the many changes we have
made in the Ashram. Prabhudas has just now descended from the
hills where he was convalescing. Krishnadas is here and so are
Chhaganlal and his wife. His parents are also here for the time
being. Devdas is in the hills, I am keeping fairly well. It is
possible that we may meet next year. If all goes well, I might
visit Europe next year.
From a photostat: S.N. 14339
