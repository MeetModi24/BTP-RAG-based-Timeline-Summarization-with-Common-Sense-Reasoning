264. LETTER TO VIJAYARAGHAVACHARI
July 18, 1941
DEAR FRIEND,
Of course, I want to know all about your health. For I
want you to live the full century in good order and condition.
But please don't expect my co-operation in anything to per-
petuate the memory of anything done by me. If the things I do
have no vitality in them, let them perish. If they have any,
then what need of any prop?
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
