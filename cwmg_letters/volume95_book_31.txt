31. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL G. BANKER
Friday [1920]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I have your letter. I had also received your earlier letter. How
can you divert your anger with Saraladevi to me? Replying to your
letters is going to be very difficult for me. You should, therefore,
come here to get from me the answers to your questions. You may
start writing letters when I forbid you to come here. And if you are
afraid of Saraladevi, she is not here today and will not be coming here
till Tuesday.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
