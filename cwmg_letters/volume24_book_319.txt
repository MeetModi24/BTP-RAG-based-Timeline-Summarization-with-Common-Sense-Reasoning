319. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
August 13, 1924
I do believe that it is a mistake, a dangerous thing, to get
orders from me in every matter. My answer is bound to be
only according to the way a question is put. I may make a
mistake in giving my answer.
Everyone should draw his own
corollaries from general principles.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 74
