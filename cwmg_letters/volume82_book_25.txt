25. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
November 3, 1945
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
You too have fallen ill. What is to be done when even a
warrior falls ill? Get well soon. Ask without the slightest hesi-
tation for whatever services you need.
I hope Kashi is well.
If you are not able to write, let Kashi write.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 9224. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
