181. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
BOMBAY,
Friday, July 15, 1921
CHI. MANI,
I should like to reply at length to your letter but I do not
have the time for it. It will soon be 11 p.m. I shall answer your
question though. For those who stock [foreign] cloth for selling it,
the question of consigning it to fire or giving it away to others
does not arise at all.
I have not yet been able even to read the leaflet. Our cause
will prosper in the measure in which we put up with assaults by
wine-dealers.
MANIBEHN
C/O SHRI VALLABHBHAI JHAVERBHAI PATEL
BHADRA, AHMEDABAD
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-Manibehn Patelne
Blessings from
BAPU
