338. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 11, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Post the accompanying to Jairamdas at Hyderabad. Send
the following telegram today, if possible:
"Jairamdas
Prem Bhavan
H'bad. (Deccan)
Girdharijidas had reported your health. You must leave
Sindh. G. D. will suggest Nainital. Love.
Bapu."
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11623
