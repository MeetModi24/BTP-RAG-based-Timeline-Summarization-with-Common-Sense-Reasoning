326. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI PATEL
December 20, 1932
CHI. DAHYABHAI,
I had intended to write a long letter but now there is no time
for it. Get well soon. Ba, Velanbehn and Bal are sitting near
me as I write this letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro‒4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 153
