150. LETTER TO KIKABHAI L. WAGHELA
[Before November 21, 1935]
Pandit Malaviya has not arrived at Wardha. There is little
possibility of his coming here. It will not be necessary for me to
visit Dr. Ambedkar, but if it is necessary I will not hesitate to
do so. I will try to visit the Harijan locality when I visit Ahme-
dabad, but my programme after arrival at Ahmedabad will be
fixed by Mr. Vallabhbhai Patel.
The Hindu, 22-11-1935
