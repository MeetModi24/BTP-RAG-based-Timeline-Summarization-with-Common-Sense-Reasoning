46. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Sunday, February 21, 1926
CHI. MOTI,
I have been getting your letters regularly. An occasional
irregularity suggests the inconveniences of the household, some
of which are inevitable and some you can get over with firmness;
this you must do. I still find your handwriting unsatisfactory. Of
course I can see some effort in it, but unless you draw your letters
like pictures with the help of a copy-book it is not going to
improve. I want you to learn this from my experience. You have
got to improve your hand. You must not fall ill, and I am hop-
ing too for news of constant improvement in Najuklal's health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12117
