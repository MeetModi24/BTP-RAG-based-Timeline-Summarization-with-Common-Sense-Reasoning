261. LETTER TO DADUBHAI DESAI
SEVAGRAM,
October 19, 1944
BHAI DADUBHAI,
I have your letter. We should have only women workers
for women's institutions. Let them make mistakes, if they will,
and learn in the process. Does not the Government tell us that
we shall have swaraj when we have gained experience? Let us
learn from them that women who have all along been oppressed
by us must have a right to make mistakes if we want to help
them become independent.
Blessings from
BAPU
DADUBHAI DESAI
NADIAD
