316. LETTER TO RAMAGOPALA SHASTRI
December 20, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. It is wholly unnecessary for you
to trouble to come to Poona, and in any case the interpretation
of an Arya Samajist will be discounted by the sanatanist Shastris.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. RAMAGOPALA SHASTRI
SECRETARY, PUNJAB DALIT SEWA MISSION, LAHORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 18755
