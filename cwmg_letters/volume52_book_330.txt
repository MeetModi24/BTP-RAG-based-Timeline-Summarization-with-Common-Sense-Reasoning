330. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
December 21, 1932
MY DEAR VENKATAPPAYYA,
It is just like you to steal into Calicut and not say anything
to me. How are you keeping, how is your wife, and how is your
daughter?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. VENKATAPPAYYA
C/O SJT. GOPALA MENON, CALICUT
From a microfilm: S.N. 18768
