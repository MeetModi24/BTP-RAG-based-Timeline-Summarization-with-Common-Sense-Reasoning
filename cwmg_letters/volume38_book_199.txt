199. LETTER TO NIRANJAN PATNAIK
WARDHA,
December 6, 1928
DEAR NIRANJAN BABU,
I have sent you a telegram today about Sambalpur. I leave
here on the 20th by passenger and reach Sambalpur on the 21st
evening, and leave Sambalpur on the 22nd evening. No great
preparations about my comforts need be made. Only goat's
milk might be made available. No fruits are to be brought from
Calcutta.
I send you now translation of a letter from Sjt. Jethalal Govind-
ji. You have known him through the columns of Young India.
He is the organizer of the Bijolia centre on the self-sufficiency plan.
I would like you to read his letter side by side with the figures
you gave me in one of your letters which I reproduced in Young
India and tell me where he has erred.
Yours sincerely,
