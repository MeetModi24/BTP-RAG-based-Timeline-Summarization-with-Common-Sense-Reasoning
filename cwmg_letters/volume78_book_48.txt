48. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 20, 1944
DEAR DR. JAYAKAR,
Many thanks for your letter of 17th instant [and] good
wishes. Do you know for the moment our meeting has been
delayed owing to Quaid-e-Azam's illness?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Your letter which was marked for express delivery was re-
ceived only today.
Gandhi-Jayakar Papers: File No. 826. Courtesy: National Archives of
India
