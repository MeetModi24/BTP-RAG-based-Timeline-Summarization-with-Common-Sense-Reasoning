437. LETTER TO RAMADEVI CHOWDHARY
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
February 14, 1941
CHI. RAMA1,
I was very happy to read your letter. Congratulations to
the teachers who have made up their mind. I hope that other
teachers too will make a similar sacrifice and will not leave their
schools. Congratulations to the villagers also. If this work succeeds,
it will be a great service.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAMADEVI
ASHRAM
BARICUT P.O.
ORISSA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9298
