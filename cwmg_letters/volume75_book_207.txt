207. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
December 4, 1941
CHI. MAHAVIR,
I have your letter. Munshiji says you should send your appli-
cation in March. I learn that there will be no point in sending
it before that. You should, therefore, wait till March. Write to me
in March. There is no need at all to send Satyadevi just now. I
am likely to leave this place for Bardoli on the 9th.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MAHAVIR
C/O SHETH BHIMJI KARA
CHANDAVARKAR ROAD
BORIVLI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6244
