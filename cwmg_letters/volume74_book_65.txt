65. LETTER TO LAKSHMI GANDHI
May 9, 1941
CHI. LAKSHMI 3 ,
Because you need help or Ba needs a companion I am send-
ing Lilavatibehn. Even earlier I had thought of Lilavatibehn,
thinking that Ba would need an escort if she were required to
come before Sushila does.
I hope the children are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2137
