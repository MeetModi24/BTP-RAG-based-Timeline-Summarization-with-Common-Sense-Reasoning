508. LETTER TO B. K. DUTTA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 16, 1938
DEAR DUTTA,
I was delighted to hear from you. I hope you will quickly
build up your shattered body.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI B. K. DUTTA
C/O SHRI RAJKUMAR SINHA
THE MALL, CAWNPORE
From a photostat: G.N. 9281
