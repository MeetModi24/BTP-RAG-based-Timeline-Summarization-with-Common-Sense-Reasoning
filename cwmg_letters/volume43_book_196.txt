196. LETTER TO AMINA TYABJI
DANDI,
April 8, 1930
MY DEAR SISTER,
Abbas Saheb is going tonight to Nadiad as even Fulchand
has been arrested. Now there is hope of his being arrested. He
thinks that here I am blocking his way. He may be right.
Khuda Hafiz.
Love.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 9687
