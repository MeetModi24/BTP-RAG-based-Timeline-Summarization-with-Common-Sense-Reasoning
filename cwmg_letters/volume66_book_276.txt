276. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
October 11, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have not been able to read again the reply to the criti-
cism. Mira also has not been able to read it. Please go
through it carefully. I don't wish to detain Janba here.
I had enclosed Sir Jogendra's letter with the letter to Raj-
kumari. She did not receive it.
Did Mira forget it or was it
left out there? Please inquire.
Do take me to Shriman.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11576
