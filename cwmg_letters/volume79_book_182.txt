182. LETTER TO ZABAKBEHN
SEVAGRAM,
February 6, 1945
CHI. ZABAKBEHN,
There is a letter from Chi. Rasik. Trambaklal has passed
away. I had not been in much contact with him. But Popatbhai
moved a lot with me and so I remember him well. What conso-
lation shall I offer you? God looks after everybody and protects
everyone. We are all subject to our karma. We should patiently
submit to whatever happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am not writing separately to Chi. Rasik. I got his letter
only today.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10218
