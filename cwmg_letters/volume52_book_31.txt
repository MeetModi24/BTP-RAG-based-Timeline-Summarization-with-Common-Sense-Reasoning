31. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
November 20, 1932
CHI. JAMNA 1 ,
Did I not sign somewhere in a corner the letter in which I
wrote about the primus stove? If I forgot to sign it and if you
want my signature on it, return the last page.
If you need a stove very much, you may use in place of the
primus stove the kerosene stove which is available [in the market].
Mahadev tells me that these stoves are very good, and are also
cheap at present. Why not try this stove? It is also very easy
to light.
I hope Purushottam and Kanu are well.
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 863. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
