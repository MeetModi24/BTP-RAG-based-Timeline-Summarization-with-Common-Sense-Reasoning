11. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
WARDHA,
February 24, 1935
CHI. SUMANGAL,
I have your distressing letter. Please come over. Ask
Narendra Dev why he has not yet recovered.
Blessings from
BAPU
SUMANGAL PRAKASH
KASHI VIDYAPITH
BANARAS CANTT.
From a photostat of the Hindi: Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy:
