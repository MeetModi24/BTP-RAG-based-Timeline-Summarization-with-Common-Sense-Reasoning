153. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
October 24, 1933
DEAR THAKKAR BAPA,
Herewith George Joseph's letter and a copy of my reply
to him.
Also herewith a letter of complaint against poor Bapineedu
and a copy of my reply.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
[PS.]
I got the letters regarding N. [The National] Call has done
great injustice to her and S3. I have written to Sahani; I had
expected his reply today.
From a photostat: G.N. 1129
