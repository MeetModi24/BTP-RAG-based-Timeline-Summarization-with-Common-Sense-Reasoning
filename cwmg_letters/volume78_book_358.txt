358. LETTER TO TOTARAM SANADHYA
SEVAGRAM,
November 6, 1944
BHAI TOTARAMJI,
Parikshitlal has given me all the information. Your body
has of course grown old, it will go when it has to. You have
been dedicated to service all your life, so it is your dharma to
accept it from those who wish to offer it sincerely. Of course
Ramanama is always there to sustain us.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2530
