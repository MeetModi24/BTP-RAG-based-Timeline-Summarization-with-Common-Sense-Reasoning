17. LETTER TO DUNICHAND
ABBOTTABAD,
July 20, 1939
DEAR LALA DUNICHAND2,
It is no want of time that prevents me from going to the Pun-
jab. But I feel utterly powerless to do any good. In other words,
it is lack of confidence that keeps me.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 5585
