196. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
POONA,
June 19, 1944
CHI. MANUDI,
I have your letter. If you behave as you promise, I shall
be very happy. I am glad that you did not go to the cinema.
Even if I do not write to you, you should write to me regularly.
It would be very good if the climate suits Yukti. The weather
there is supposed to be good.
I am improving day by day.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./XXIV
