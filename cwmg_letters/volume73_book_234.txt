234. LETTER TO ABDUL WADUD SARHADI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 28, 1940
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Opinions differ in your province itself.
Everyone is left to his own free choice whether to offer civil dis-
obedience or not. But if he does, he has to conform to conditions
laid down, one of which is belief in Hindu-Muslim unity. In the
circumstances no one should have anything to say against civil
disobedience.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
