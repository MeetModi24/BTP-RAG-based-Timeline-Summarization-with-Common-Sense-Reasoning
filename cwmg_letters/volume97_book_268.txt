268. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
WARDHA,
April 16, 1935
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
You should make it a rule always to write your address at the top of the
letter. The paper on which you have written your letter is of the finest quality.
It cannot be hand-made paper. Go and see for yourself and assure yourself
whether it is so or not. Let me know its price, size, etc. Who makes that
paper? What daily wage does he get for the work? And similar details. I
have still not received the ink-and-pen-stand. What do you do for your milk?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PRABHUDAS GANDHI
GULERIA
BADAUN
U.P.
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33049
