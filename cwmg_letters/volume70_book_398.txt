398. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 12, 1939
Those who are members of committees need not resign just
now. It does not matter if a Government official becomes the
chairman. It will [in fact] give us an opportunity to test him
thoroughly. If necessary we can even resist him. Perhaps these
committees will themselves fade out.
A municipality may keep up all relations. The non-co-opera-
tion this time is not like that of 1920. These are my personal
views and you may discuss them with Sardar.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 174
