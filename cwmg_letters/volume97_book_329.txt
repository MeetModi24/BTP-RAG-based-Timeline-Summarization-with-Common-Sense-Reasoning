329. LETTER TO RAMDAS AND NIRMALA GANDHI
[SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA, (C.P.)]
March 27, 1941
CHI. RAMDAS AND NIMU,
I received the letters of you both. I will not tempt you to come here in
May. The best thing will be for you to go to a cool place and rest. You may
go to Maroli. Perhaps Chorwad. Next in the list is Ghogha. It will suffice if
the place is on a sea-coast. If, however, you wish to have a taste of the hot
air here, by all means come over.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAMDAS GANDHI
SHANTINAGAR SOCIETY
SABARMATI
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
