36. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
Jeth Vad 7 [June 21, 1927]
BHAISHRI VALJI,
I have just now received a telegram from Swami to the effect
that your mother fell dead while she was walking with you. I
can offer only felicitations on such a holy death. We should all pray
for such a death. Her bodily presence with us was a source of
support and comfort, and if we feel grieved for the loss of that
feeling, our grief would be nothing but selfishness.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From Gujarati: C.W. 7392. Courtesy: Valjibhai Desai
