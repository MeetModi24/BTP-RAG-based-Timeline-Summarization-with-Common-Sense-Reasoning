347. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Friday, Sravana Shukla 12, August 20, 1926
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I enclose a telegram from Motilalji. The reply which I have
sent to him is on the back of the same sheet. I have also sent
you a wire as under:
Motilalji wires I approve your candidature Assembly Gorakhpur. Have
replied know nothing about your candidature. There must be some mis-
take. Do you know anything about alleged representation.
What can all this mean? I am awaiting your reply.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
I hope you are keeping good health.
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6133. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
