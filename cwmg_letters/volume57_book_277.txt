277. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
PATNA,
March 12, 1934
CHI. MANUDI,
You wrote after keeping me waiting for quite a long time.
I understand the reason now. Bali writes and tells me that you
did not listen to her advice. You should avoid pickles and oil.
You should live only on milk, unspiced vegetables, fruit and
rotli. If you do so, you will not fall ill. One should learn to
relish food that is good for one's health.
I will convey to Ba the contents of the letters from all of you.
She will feel very happy.
I hope you have all completely re-
covered now.
Write to me regularly. Obey Bali's instructions.
Write to
your friends at Wardha.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1530. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
