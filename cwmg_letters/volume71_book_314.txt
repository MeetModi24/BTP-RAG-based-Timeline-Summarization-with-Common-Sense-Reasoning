314. LETTER TO RASIKLAL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 8, 1940
BHAI RASIKLAL,
I am reaching your letter of February 7, 1940, only today.
The celebration is over now; I am watching your work. I am
firm in my opinion. Those who will remain steadfast will not
only keep their own self-respect but will also uphold the prestige
of the people of Limbdi. If. . .2 workers keep themselves out, the
number of workers counted by you is good. But strength lies in
quality, not in numbers.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
