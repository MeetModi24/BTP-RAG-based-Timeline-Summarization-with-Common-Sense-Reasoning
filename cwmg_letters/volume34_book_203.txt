203. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Silence Day [July 25, 1927]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have received your two letters. Study as much as you like,
but take good care of your health.
I think you intend to call on Haribhai's widow, Chi. Kusum.
If you do so, find out what her condition is. I have written a
letter to her. What is her wish? Are her parents alive? How
old is she, and what is her financial condition? Is she self-controlled
or overcome with grief? If you know the background to her
marriage, let me know it. If not, try to learn it. I had intended
to know about it from Haribhai himself, but he is gone.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 616. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
