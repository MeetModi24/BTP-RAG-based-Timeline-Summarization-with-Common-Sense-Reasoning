78. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
Silence Day [February 14, 1927]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
You must have got the news about Manilal. If Manilal has
not shown the letters written to him, you will know from
letter to Chhaganlal. Please inform everyone. I do not know if
Nanabhai will have Panditji or someone else to perform the rites;
I have left it to him. Please ask Panditji to be ready in case he
has to go. The wedding will be at Akola.
Herewith two more letters from Mirabehn. I have already
asked Mahadev to write to you about Parasram.
Please let me have the news from Vijapur. Ask John to
write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7764. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
