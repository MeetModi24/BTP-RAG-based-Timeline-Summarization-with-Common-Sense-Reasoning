350. LETTER TO N. R. JOSHI
SEVAGRAM,
March 26, 1945
MY DEAR JOSHI,
Have I not helped ? I have the thing still in mind. I may fail to
do more. If I do, it won't be for want of effort. Please give my love
to them.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI N. R. JOSHI
ALLAHABAD AGRI. INSTITUTE
ALLAHABAD
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
