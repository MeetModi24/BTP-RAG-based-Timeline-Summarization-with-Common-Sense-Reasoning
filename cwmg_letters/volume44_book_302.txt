302. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI MADHAVJI THAKKAR
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 12, 1930
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
I have your letter. We can easily get rid of any habit
when we begin to dislike it. And a woman with your strong
resolve should experience no difficulty at all in doing so. You are
doing well in clinging to the diet which you have adopted.
As
we observe others becoming more indifferent towards or losing
faith in the programme of picketing liquor-booths, etc., we should
become more vigilant ourselves and let our faith shine more brightly.
That is bound to produce an effect.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6800
