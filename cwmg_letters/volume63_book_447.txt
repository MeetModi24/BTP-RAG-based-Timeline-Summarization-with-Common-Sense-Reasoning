447. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
October 5, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
I am sending whatever could be got ready. My article is
still unfinished, and I must detain Kanu for an hour more if
I wish to complete it. I shall, therefore, send it with some-
body who may happen to come or with a special messenger
tomorrow morning or this evening. Amidst patients I am
carrying on as best as I can for the day. Workers from Kathia-
war may be invited if Sardar gives 12th/13th to Narandas. Inti-
mate to him whatever Sardar wishes.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Somebody will be going there as I shall of course have
some post to send.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11494
