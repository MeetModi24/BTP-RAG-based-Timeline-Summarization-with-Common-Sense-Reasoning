215. LETTER TO RANGANATH PRADHAN
[After September 26, 1947]
ASSISTANT DEWAN SAHEB,
Your letter of 26th September arrived here yesterday from the
Navajivan Office. I am not in a position to give my views about lathi
training. You may consult Sardar Patel. It is he who is in charge of
the affairs dealing with the rulers and the subjects.
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1
This is a reply to a letter dated September 26, 1947 from Ranganath
Pradhan, Narsingpur Durbar.
