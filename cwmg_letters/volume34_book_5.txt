5. LETTER TO SONJA SCHLESIN
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 16, 1927
DEAR MISS SCHLESIN 1 ,
Here is a letter from Dr. Jones which perhaps you would like.
I hope you had my long letter and I do hope also that you
will make it a point to see Mr. Sastri. When are you coming
here? I wish you could also go to Phoenix and see Manilal and
his wife.
Yours sincerely,
MISS SCHLESIN
POST Box No. 2284
JOHANNESBURG
From a photostat: S.N. 12360
