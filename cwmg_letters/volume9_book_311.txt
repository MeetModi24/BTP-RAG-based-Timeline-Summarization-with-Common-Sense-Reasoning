311. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
[LONDON,]
October 18, 1909
DEAR MR. MAGANLAL,
I have your post card of the 15th ultimo. With regard to Mr. Budrea,
I shall be obliged if you will kindly immediately forward the documents
to him at his Dannhauser address. The documents should be sent by
registered letter post. I am writing to Mr. Budrea also.
Yours sincerely,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5132
