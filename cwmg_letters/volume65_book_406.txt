406. LETTER TO BHARATAN KUMARAPPA
June 29, 1937
MY DEAR BHARATAN,
I had a chat with Fischer about Rao the dairyman. Others
have also talked to me. Parnerkar went to him to give him
instructions and examine the ghee. Rao seems to be no worker.
He talks too much and nonsense. If he is what Fischer and others
describe him to be, he should be sent away.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat : G.N. 10120
