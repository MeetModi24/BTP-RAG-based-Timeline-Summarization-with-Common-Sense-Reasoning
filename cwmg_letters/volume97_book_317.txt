317. LETTER TO RAMDAS AND SUMITRA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
April 26, 1940
CHI. RAMDAS,
I liked your having gone to Ahmedabad for you will have more experience
there. You will get there the company of our own people and Nimu will
have better facilities for her studies. But from the point of view of the children,
I am not so happy at your having left Bhavnagar. They had got used to the
place. But, then, does it always happen as one wishes? Hence, one should
believe that what happens on its own is for the best and be content.
I will certainly urge Ba to go to you but there is little likelihood of her
going. If she finds the heat unbearable, she will prefer to go to Mithubehn at
Maroli. I need not write separately to Nimu.
CHI. SUMI,
You have done very well indeed. Go on writing in this way.
Blessings from
BAPU
