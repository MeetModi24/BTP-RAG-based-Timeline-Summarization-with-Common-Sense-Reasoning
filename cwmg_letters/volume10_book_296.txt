296. LETTER TO REGISTRAR OF ASIATICS
DURBAN,
October 26, 1910
SIR,
I have the honour to acknowledge your letter of even date regarding
the Indians on Salisbury Island.
I am unable to say what the men will do at Volksrust in the event
of their reaching there. They have been advised that they have a legal
right in terms of Act 36 of 1908 to apply for duplicates of Certificates
of Registration and to receive duplicates if their applications are in order.
I shall be obliged if you will be good enough to reply whether you are
prepared to afford them facility to make their applications in terms of
Act 36 of 1908 and the Regulations.
I have the honour, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
Indian Opinion, 29-10-1910
