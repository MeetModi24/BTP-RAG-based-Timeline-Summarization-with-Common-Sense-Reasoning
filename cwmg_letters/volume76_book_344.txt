344. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 16, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
There is comparative rest today. All the meetings and Press
interviews are over. All my fatigue and physical weakness are
gone. There is no cause for worry. This change is due I think
to the increase in the milk intake. I have dropped bread and
butter.
If Shummy wants you there till 15th August you can stay
there. Nothing extraordinary is likely to happen meanwhile.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3690. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6499
