682. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
BOMBAY,
April 19, 1945
CHI. CHAND,
I have received your nice letter. You have to complete your
course there. In the meantime I will get some more news of
Satyavati. Keep on writing to me at Mahabaleshwar. I will
decide. Convey my blessings to all the girls there.
Manu has again got fever with blood [in the sputum].
She feels weak but has not lost much weight.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI CHANDRANI
KASTURBA NIDHI SHIBIR
SURESH KUTIR
MANDA PESHAWAR ROAD
BORIVLI
From a photostat of the Hindi : Chandrani Papers. Courtesy: National
Gandhi Museum and Library
