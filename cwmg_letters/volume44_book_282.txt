282. LETTER TO MANSHANKAR J. TRIVEDI
October 5, 1930
CHI. MANU,
How did you get fever? Ordinarily you would deserve a
prize for the way you take care of your health. You have suc-
ceeded, by your own efforts, in building up a strong body. It is
for you now to preserve your improved health. When Kaka-
saheb is released, let him find you a trained worker in a fit
condition.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7765
