426. LETTER TO KHANDUBHAI DESAI
November 17, 1944
BHAI KHANDUBHAI,
May the Labour Day function bear fruit. Labour has made
great strides in Ahmedabad. But much remains yet to be done.
I cannot be satisfied till the respect for labour rises higher than
that for capital, nor should labour be satisfied. We will not
attain that position till it has attained unity, 100 per cent
honesty and education with knowledge. There is no place for
caste, creed or communalism in the ranks of labour. There
should be equality of rights between men and women.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Gujarati : Gujarat Samachar, 3-12-1944
