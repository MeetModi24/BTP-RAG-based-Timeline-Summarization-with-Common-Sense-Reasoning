500. LETTER TO BENARSILAL AND RUKMINI BAZAJ
May 8, 1932
CHI. BENARSI,
I read your remarks. You must have read Father's letter
addressed to me. It was first received there and then redirected
to me. I did not understand it clearly.
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your letter. As soon as you came down to the plains,
both of you lost health again. Does that mean that you should
permanently stay at a hill station? I hope that you have comp-
letely recovered now. Do you take out Madhav for a walk
every day? See that you do not let the child's health suffer for
any reason. Write to me regularly. If you follow Mirabehn's
practice and fix a day for writing to me, you will not have to
try to remember.
PS.
You must have heard about Gangadevi's death.
From Gujarati: C.W. 9442. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
