478. LETTER TO SALVATION ARMY, BOMBAY
BORSAD,
June 14, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have a letter from Mr. John Collett which I enclose herewith.
I send you also a copy of my letter to Mr. Collett. Will you
please tell me whether you will care to receive the cards and
the medals for the use mentioned in the letter?
Yours sincerely,
Ends. 2
THE OFFICER COMMANDING
THE BOMBAY BRANCH
SALVATION ARMY, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 17285
