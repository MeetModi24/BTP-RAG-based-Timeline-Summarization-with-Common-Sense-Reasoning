241. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO LORD CREWE
[LONDON,]
September 2, 1909
SIR,
I beg to draw the attention of the Earl of Crewe to the following
cablegram received from the Chinese Association :
Reuters report interview Smuts indicates settlement Asiatic question. If so, why
continued arrest Chinese? Twenty-seven within week.
I have not received a copy of Reuter's report referred to in the
above cable, and I do not know what truth there is in the settlement
