286. LETTER TO B. G. KHER
PANCHGANI,
July 17, 1944
BHAI KHER,
Why did you run away without calling on me? Do come
when you are free and I will forgive you this lapse. Send me
yours and other people's opinion about what I am doing.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI BALA SAHEB KHER
Ex-MINISTER
KHAR, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 2769
