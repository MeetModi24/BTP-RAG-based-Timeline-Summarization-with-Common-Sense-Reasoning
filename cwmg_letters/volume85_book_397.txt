397. LETTER TO KHURSHED NAOROJI
NEW DELHI,
September 15, 1946
DEAR SISTER,
Your letter. You have done well to join the C. S. P.
Who suggested that you showed off or did anything to please
anybody? If you did, you [would] not be a dandi that you are.
Cheer up!
Love.
KHURSHED NAOROJI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
