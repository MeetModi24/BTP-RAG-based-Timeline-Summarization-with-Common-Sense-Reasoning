418. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
ON THE TRAIN,
July 6, 1939
CHI. KANCHAN,
Though I do not write to you I do think of you. I hope
you remember what I told you. I hope you are calm. Study
well. Take full advantage of the quiet you have got just now
and improve your knowledge. Who conducts the prayers at pre-
sent? What prayers are sung in the morning and in the evening?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8292. Also C.W. 7051. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
