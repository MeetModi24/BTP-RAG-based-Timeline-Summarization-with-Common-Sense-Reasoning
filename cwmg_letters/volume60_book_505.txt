505. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
April 1, 1935
BHAI THAKKAR BAPA,
I got the papers sent by you. I have written to both the Iyen-
gars and sent the papers over to Malkani.
I will wait for you after the 12th. I am holding myself ready.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 22753
