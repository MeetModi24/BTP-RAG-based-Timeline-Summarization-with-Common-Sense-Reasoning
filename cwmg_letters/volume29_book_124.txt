124. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
SABARMATI,
Paush Krishna 12 [January 11, 1926]
BHAI RAMESHWARJI,
I have both your letters. You should repeat Ramanama
in a detached spirit. You should not brood over your past sins
but regard it as God's grace that you are free from them now and
implore Him to keep you away from them in future.
You should engage yourself in some altruistic activity.
I have no doubt that the work of skinning and tanning is
religious. This work cannot be carried on at your place. If
you desire you can give some monetary help for cow-protection.
Finally you should study the Ramayana and such other works.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 162
