365. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 22, 1941
BHAI SATIS BABU,
The son of Amrita Babu, Ramendra Nath, is studying there.
He has joined the ninth class and needs some books. Please send
for him and ask what books he wants and what they would cost.
He is a 'free' student. His address is: 4/ Sham Charande Street.
They say it is a minute's walk from Pratishthan office. Every
week I read Rashtravani attentively.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SATIS BABU
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
15 COLLEGE SQUARE
CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2734
