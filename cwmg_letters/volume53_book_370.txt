370. LETTER TO N. S. VARADACHARI
February 11, 1933
MY DEAR VARADACHARI,
I had your letter of the 6th instant. I am looking forward
with interest to the proceedings of tomorrow. If you can get me
a brief statement such as the opinion published in this week's
Harijan, do get it for publication in the next issue. Even an
endorsement of the opinion from a well-known pandit or pandits
I would like.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. S. VARADACHARI
A. I. S. A. KHADI VASTRALAYA
MADRAS
