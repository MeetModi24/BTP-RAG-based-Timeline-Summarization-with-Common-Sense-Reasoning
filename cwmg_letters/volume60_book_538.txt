538. LETTER TO GOVIND RAO
April 6, 1935
MY DEAR GOVIND RAO,
Though as you say there was no excuse for the negligence,
you have now done full reparation to Shri Thomson.
As for the ladies, wait till the next cold season. In any case,
will they pay their own board expenses? Do they know Hindi?
Of course you will write to the manager when the time for send-
ing them comes.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1383
