133. LETTER TO P. G. MALKANI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 16, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I have made careful enquiry and I can
find no trace of the draft mentioned by you. It is evident that the
draft has been miscarried somewhere. It is however fortunate
that it has not been cashed by anybody. If you still desire to send
the amount, please do so to the above address.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. G. MALKANI
HD. TOWN, MANGHA ASTAN, KARACHI
From a microfilm: S.N. 19362
