313. LETTER TO VICEROY
DELHI,
February 28, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I understood that you would send your note on the points
you expounded yesterday and that, thereafter, I was to send you
my note on picketing. But, as it is getting late in the day, without
further waiting I enclose my note on picketing.
I would like to know early, if I may, whether I shall be
wanted tomorrow. I am anxious to know early, as I have an impor-
tant public engagement tomorrow which I must postpone, if I
am to be at the Viceregal House tomorrow.
I am, etc.,
