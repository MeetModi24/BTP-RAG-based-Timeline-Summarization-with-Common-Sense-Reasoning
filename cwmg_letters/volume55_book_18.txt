18. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
April 24, 1933
DEAR THAKKAR BAPA,
I am going to deal with Dr. Ambedkar's suggestion for the
revision of the Yeravda Pact in the ensuing number of Harijan.
You must have seen it and given your thought to it. Please let
me know what you think about it. Please share this letter with
Ghanshyamdas and let me have his opinion also, or, you may
give me your joint opinion.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 1120; also S.N. 21052
