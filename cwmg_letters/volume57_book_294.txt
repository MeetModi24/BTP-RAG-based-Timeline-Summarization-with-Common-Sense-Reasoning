294. LETTER TO PARVATI P. DESAI
March 15, 1934
CHI. PARVATI,
You have been to see Pragji and still given me no news of
him. Why? How is Pragji? What does he read and what
physical work does he do? What is his food?
Is he happy?
Who are his companions? Why is he silent?
How are you and the children?
. . .1 write . . .2 to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5037
