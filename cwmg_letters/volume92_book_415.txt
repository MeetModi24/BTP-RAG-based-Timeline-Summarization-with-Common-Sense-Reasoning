415. LETTER TO N. P. RAGHAVAN
February 2, 1931
MY DEAR RAGHAVAN,
I have your letter. Since you do not get the time, the
prayer hours you mention are quite good. I hope you are
keeping well.
Yours,
BAPU
SJT. N.P. RAGHAVAN
KHADI DEPOT
PAYYANUR
MALABAR
From a photostat: C. W. 10864. Courtesy: N. P. Raghavan
