5. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 16, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am moving in the matter. Only my
capacity for collecting monies has suffered a check which, on
occasions such as this loss to the Society, I somewhat deplore.
I am glad you have written to Seth Ambalal. I am also
writing to him. Jamnalalji is due here very shortly. I therefore
do not propose to write to him.
Yours sincerely,
RT. HON. V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
DECCAN GYMKHANA P.O.
POONA CITY
From a photostat: S.N. 10936
