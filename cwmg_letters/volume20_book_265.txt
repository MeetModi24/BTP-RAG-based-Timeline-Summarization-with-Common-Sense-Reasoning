265. LETTER TO KHWAJA
[After August 17, 1921]
DEAR KHWAJA SAHEB 2 ,
You will please forgive me for not acknowledging your wire
earlier regarding cow-sacrifice. I know that in many parts of India,
the work done was marvellously good indeed. You wanted me to
wire regarding scarcity of grain. It is no use my wiring anything
now. I shall hope to meet you on the 4th when you will men-
tion the matter if the situation still requires consideration.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 7599
