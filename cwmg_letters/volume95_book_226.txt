226. LETTER TO ULI RITTER
BIRLA HOUSE,
NEW DELHI,
November 21, 1947
DEAR RITTER,
Herewith enclosed is my answer to your father's letter as per
his advice that I should send it to you.
I hope you are flourishing in Bombay.
Yours sincerely,
M.K.GANDHI
ENCL : ONE
ULI RITTER, ESQ.
ENGINEER
C/O MESSRS. VOLKART BROS.
BOMBAY
From a copy : Pyarelal papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
