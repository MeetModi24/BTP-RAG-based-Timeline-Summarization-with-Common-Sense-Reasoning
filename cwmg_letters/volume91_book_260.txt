260. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
NAGPUR,
March 19 [1921]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
Ever since I saw Mother, her face has been before my eyes.
I wish you would respect her wish that you should wear her
tulsi beads. When you feel that it is sinful to wear them, you may
discard them. The sacred beads given by one's mother are a
symbol of immortality. If you have not yet started wearing them,
do so and send me a telegram.
My travel schedule will be as follows :
20-21 Jabalpur
22
Gondia up to 12 o'clock
22-23 Nagpur mail
24
Cuttack from morning, care Utkal Society
Till 29th I shall be touring in Orissa.
31st expect you at Bezwada.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32740
