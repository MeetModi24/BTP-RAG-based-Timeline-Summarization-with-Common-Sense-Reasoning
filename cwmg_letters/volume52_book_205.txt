205. LETTER TO U. GOPALA MENON
December 9, 1932
MY DEAR GOPALA MENON,
I have your letter. I think that you are keeping the opinion
of avarnas also. I quite recognize that the opinion based upon
a knowledge of the local situation should prevail. I am glad that
things are shaping themselves quite well there.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18675
