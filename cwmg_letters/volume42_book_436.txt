436. LETTER TO LILAVATI KODIDAS
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 6, 1930
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. It will be a good thing if you form the
habit of writing with ink and in a good hand. See that you keep
hysteria at arm's length now. You should reason with Uncle and
explain to him that, since the jewellery is lying unused, it would
be better to [sell it and] get interest on the money. Do not be
in a hurry to return. You are, however, free to return as soon as
you can do so with complete peace of mind. Live on milk and
fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. LILAVATIBEHN KODIDAS
C/O MR. DWARKADAS GOKULDAS
KALBADEVI, BOMBAY N o . 2
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9271
