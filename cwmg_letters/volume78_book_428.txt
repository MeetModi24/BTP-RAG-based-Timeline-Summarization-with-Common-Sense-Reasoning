428. LETTER TO DR. JIVRAJ MEHTA
SEVAGRAM,
November 17, 1944
BHAI JIVRAJ,
Chi. Sushila is arriving there to see Mathuradas 2 and his
ailing friend. I am not very keen just now to start a maternity
hospital here. Since Sushila will not devote all her time to this
work I am thinking what her position in this project should be.
Again I fail to see how the Committee can work efficiently if
she were not on it. Sushila believes that with the time she will
give, she can do justice both to the local maternity home and the
Committee. She should have a doctor under her to attend to the
needs of the maternity home. If you wish you may discuss the
matter with her. I have told Abid Ali that he should obtain an
estimate of expenditure but should not incur any expenditure
just now.
I hope you are fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. JIVRAJ MEHTA
BOMBAY
