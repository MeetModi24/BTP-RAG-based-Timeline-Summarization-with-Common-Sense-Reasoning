409. LETTER TO AMINA G. QURESHI
April 9, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER AMINA,
I am awaiting a letter from you after your visit to Qureshi.
I also wish to know what you feel concerning tobacco and chi-
llies. Write frankly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6666. Also C.W.4311. Courtesy:
Hamid G. Qureshi
