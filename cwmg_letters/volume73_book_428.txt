428. LETTER TO GANDHI ANNAMALAI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
February 12, 1941
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of the 7th. He would ask you not to leave
your job just now.
The fight here will be a long-drawn-out affair, and you
need not take the plunge just yet.
Yours sincerely,
MAHADEV DESAI
From a photostat: C.W. 10346. Courtesy: Jairamdas Doulatram
