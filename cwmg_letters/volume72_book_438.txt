438. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 15, 1940
Yes, your appointment stands.
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
It is good you are bringing Taralika along. Charu should be
sent somewhere early. If you wish to bring him here, do so. You
may bring five copies of Satis Babu's book with you.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HEMPRABHA DEVI
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
15 COLLEGE SQUARE
CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1638
