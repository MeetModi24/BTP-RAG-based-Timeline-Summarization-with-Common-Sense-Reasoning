169. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
BARDOLI,
Sunday, August 5, 1928
CHI. KUSUM,
I got your letter. It is strange that you should have a
headache. Take care.
If you have any explanation why . . . bhai does not admit his
error, think over it and let me know. Is it possible that you
misunderstood at any time what you were told? Personally,
I have again written to say that . . . bhai should be relieved.
Write and let me know what arrangements have been made
for the Bal Mandir
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1757
