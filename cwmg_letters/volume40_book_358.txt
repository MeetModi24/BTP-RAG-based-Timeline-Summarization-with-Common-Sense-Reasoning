358. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
NELLORE,
Silence Day, May 13, 1929
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I have your letter. You should not hesitate to write to me
even if you misspell words. You have no reason to be ashamed of
your spelling mistakes. If one were to devote plenty of time just
for this task it also can be mastered. But it would not be right
to devote so much time to it. Do therefore what you can. We
value a letter for its ideas, not its grammar.
Do settle the matter relating to Kaku's father. At the same
time improve your health too. Maitreyi too will benefit.
I do believe that girls too should have education. We shall
talk about this. Our separation will end soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G.S. Gangabehnne, p. 18
