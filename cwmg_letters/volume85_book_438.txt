438. LETTER TO SANKARAN
NEW DELHI,
September 20, 1946
CHI. SANKARAN,
I have your letter. Now I can reach there any time after
the 25th. I shall explain then. Meanwhile, do whatever is
possible.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SANKARAN
ASHRAM
SEVAGRAM
