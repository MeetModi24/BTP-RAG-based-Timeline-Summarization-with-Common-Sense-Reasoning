121. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKER PATTANI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Thursday, July 8, 1926
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter. I wanted you to shake off your burden of
administration for a few days. If that happens, I believe your
health will improve speedily. Now we can say there has been some
rain here. The sky continues overcast. Hence as you have stirred
out already, I see no difficulty in your coming over here. More-
over, there can be no great difference between the climate of
Dhrangadhra and that of Ahmedabad. Therefore, do come if you
can. I shall then be able to know something at any rate about
your health. And if you permit me to do some simple experiments,
we may try such changes of diet as would suit you. And I hope you
will not bring with you any petitioner here. But I will lay down
no conditions for your coming here. Come here then on your
own terms. But please do come.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
