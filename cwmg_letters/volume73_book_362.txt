362. LETTER TO NILA NAGINI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 21, 1941
CHI. NILA1,
I do hope you received my letter in reply to yours some days
ago. Now I have another from you through Mr. Whittamore.
He is a most lovable man. We all had a very profitable time with
him.
You are doing good work there. Do ask Sirius to write to me.
Where do you propose to put him?
You will be welcome whenever you can come.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1218
