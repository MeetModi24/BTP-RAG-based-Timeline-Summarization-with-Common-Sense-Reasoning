200. LETTER TO LAKSHMIDAS P. ASAR
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Friday, Asadha Sud 13 [July 23, 1926]
CHI. LAKSHMIDAS,
You must have got my letter in which I wrote to you about
Ramji. I send with this the reply I have received in the matter
from Khushalbhai. After reading it, I have written to Ramji
and advised him to be patient and, if necessary, to have an addi-
tional lavatory constructed. I get your letters regularly. I liked
the argument you advanced to Ghanshyamdas. I cannot see how
it can be improved upon. I will collect and send to you the infor-
mation you have asked for respecting cotton. Your first article will
appear in this week's issue of Navajivan. As you will see, I have
omitted from it two short paragraphs and also made a minor cor-
rection.
JAYAJIRAO COTTON MILL
GWALIOR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12210
