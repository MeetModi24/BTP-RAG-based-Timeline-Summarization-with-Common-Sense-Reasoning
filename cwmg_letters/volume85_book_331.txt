331. LETTER TO GIRIRAJ KISHORE
NEW DELHI,
September 7, 1946
CHI. GIRIRAJ,
I was distressed to see your letter. Yesterday I heard the
purport of your letter. This morning I went through Krishna-
murti's article. I could not follow it fully so I asked to see
your letter. What a messy handwriting! It is unbecoming for you
to write such a hand. You are a teacher, your handwriting
should be a model, as should be all your work.
Krishnamurti's article is good, but it is unnecessary to pub-
lish it. The English handwriting is also not good, and the
letters are too small.
275
Do everything neatly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 8777
