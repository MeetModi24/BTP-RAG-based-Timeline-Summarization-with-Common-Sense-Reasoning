323. LETTER TO GOVERNOR OF BASRAH PROVINCE, IRAQ
November 6, 1934
GOVERNOR OF THE BASRAH PROVINCE
IN THE KINGDOM OF IRAQ AND
PRESIDENT OF THE BASRAH DATE ADVISORY BOARD
BASRAH
DEAR GOVERNOR,
I thank you for your kind letter intimating despatch of a
consignment of dates. I know by experience the renown of Basrah
dates. I am looking forward to the receipt of the consignment.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
298
