89. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
BORSAD,
June 29, 1931
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter. It is now nearing 10.30 p.m. But I must give
you a line. I am glad you are settling down. You have gone
there just in time. Do come nearer Gangabehn. She is troubled
at heart. You will be also helpful to Father Elwin.
You will be pleased to know that Surajbehn has adopted the 1/
sari. At first I did not notice the change. Write to her a brief note.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 9666; also C.W. 5432. Courtesy: Mirabehn
