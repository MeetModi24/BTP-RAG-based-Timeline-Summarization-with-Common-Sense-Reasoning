66. LETTER TO RATILAL M. VORA
SEVAGRAM,
January 17, 1945
BHAI RATILAL,
Your letter dated 5-10-1944 has been lying around and came
into my hands only today. Such things happened while I have
been ill and moving about. I hope your son-in-law is there and
has recovered too. I would be happy if you could give me his
news. I have sent the photograph along with my blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
RATILAL MAGANLAL VORA
2ND FLOOR, ROOM 62, 47 GOSWAMI BUILDING
PINJARAPOLE LANE
BOMBAY 4
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
