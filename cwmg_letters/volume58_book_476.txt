476. LETTER TO B. J. MARATHEY
September 8, 1934
MY DEAR MARATHEY,
I am glad you have sent me an exhaustive reply, and that too
promptly, to my inquiry. I shall now make use of it.
yours sincerely,
SHRI B. J. MARATHEY
SECRETARY, H.S.S.
MAHARASHTRA BOARD
POONA
