154. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
February 18, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
You have raised a correct question. You are sure that beat-
ing necessarily involves violence but it is not so. Supposing
somebody has been bitten by a snake and is about to faint. In that
case he will have to be fed even if it means beating him. This
is no violence because this beating is inspired by love and is for
102
the good of the patient. But supposing I have an ill feeling for
you in my mind but do not beat you for want of an opportu-
nity. Such an ill feeling is violence. The origin of violence lies
in our own minds.
You can have cod-liver oil massage even if you have never
taken it orally.
Blessings from
BAPU
