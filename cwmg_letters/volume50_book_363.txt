363. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
August 12, 1932
BHAI KHAMBHATTA,
I deliberately postponed writing to you. I wished to write
only after I had used the oil sent by you. I have now started
using it. It is ten days now, but there is no change. Mahadev
massages the arm with it daily. There is nothing wrong with the
arm in other respects, and so there is no cause for worry.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6601. Also C.W. 4387. Courtesy:
Tehmina Khambhatta
