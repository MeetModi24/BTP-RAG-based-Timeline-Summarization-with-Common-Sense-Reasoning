468. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI MADHAVJI THAKKAR
November 24, 1930
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
Madhavji writes and tells me that you keep worrying about
the children. Why? Reflect over the verses which we recite
daily and burn up all worries. God will look after the children. I
have written a letter to Calcutta. We may request the elders as
earnestly as with propriety we can and then, trusting them, rely
on God.
Blessings from
BAPU
