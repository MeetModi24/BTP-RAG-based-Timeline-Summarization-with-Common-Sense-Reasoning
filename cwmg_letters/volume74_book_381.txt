381. LETTER TO MADALASA
August 14, 1941
You are both poets. There is this difference, however, that
though he is a poet he clings to the earth and takes pleasure in
his work. You soar in the sky and so you remain absorbed in
your thoughts. That is why you are always dissatisfied with your
servants. As long as you remain so, how can you bring credit to your
house as a housewife? Here are heaps of blessings from me!
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 320
