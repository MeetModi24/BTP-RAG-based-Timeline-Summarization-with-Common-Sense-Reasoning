127. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 26, 1941
DEAR SISTER,
I have your nice letter. Thakkar Bapa had written everything.
Let us hope what has been done would become permanent. See
that your health does not suffer.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7996. Also C.W. 3093. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
89
