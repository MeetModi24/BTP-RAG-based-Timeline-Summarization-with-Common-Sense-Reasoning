348. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have received both your letters. I shall not use them for a
public purpose. Can all letters be so used? I could not contain
whatever I came to know. Right now everything is messed up. But
everything will turn out well if all women are truthful. I am keeping
fairly good health. Where has Manibehn gone? I am satisfied that
your health is good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S. N. 9357
