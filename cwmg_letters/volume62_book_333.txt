333. LETTER TO RABINDRANATH TAGORE
DELHI,
March 27, 1936
DEAR GURUDEV,
God has blessed my poor effort. And here is the money.
Now you will relieve the public mind by announcing cancel-
lation of the rest of the programme. May God keep you for
many a year to come.
Yours with love,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 2288
