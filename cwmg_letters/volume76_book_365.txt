365. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA BHAVE
July 22, 1942
CHI. BALKRISHNA,
It is surprising that your appetite was not fully restored
there. Stay there as long as you can preserve your strength.
There is no need at all for you to worry about me. Of course,
my weight had gone down. It is now going up. It has reached
103.. This time even persons like you will be able to sacrifice
themselves. Wait and see. If such a time is to come, it will
come of itself.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI BALKRISHNA
TUPAROO, DALHOUSIE
PUNJAB
From Gujarati: C.W. 807. Courtesy: Balkrishna Bhave
