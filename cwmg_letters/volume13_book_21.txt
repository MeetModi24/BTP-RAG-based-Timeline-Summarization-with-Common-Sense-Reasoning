21. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
BOMBAY,
February 7, 1915
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
I have hardly any time left to me to attend to correspondence.
I have a moment left whilst I am waiting for Mr. Setalvad to
come. I shan't be long before I am with you. We go to Poona
tonight. I may leave for Bolpur on the 18th or even earlier.
Hope to drop you a wire as soon as the date is fixed. With love,
Yours ever
MOHAN
From the original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5663. Courtesy: Radhabehn
Choudhri
