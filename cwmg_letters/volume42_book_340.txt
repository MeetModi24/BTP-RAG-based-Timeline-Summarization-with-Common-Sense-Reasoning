340. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
LAHORE,
Silence Day, December 30, 1929
SISTERS,
I am writing this simply to let you know that I remember
you on this Silence Day. I hope to arrive there on the 5th. It
is very cold here. There is noise all around me. I am sitting
in a meeting and shall not, therefore, try to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3714
1 What follows is from The Tribune.
