571. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
October 5, 1945
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letter. My blessings are always with you. You
will do much work after you recover. Think only of God. You
will then have found Vidya and served her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
