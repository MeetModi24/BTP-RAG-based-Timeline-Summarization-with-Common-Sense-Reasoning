10. LETTER TO A. RANGASWAMI IYENGAR
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 16, 1927
MY DEAR RANGASWAMI,
I have your letter enclosing extract from the Rev. Mr.
Holmes' letter for which I thank you.
I have also your letter about the forthcoming special meeting
of the All-India Congress Committee. I do not suppose you ex-
pect me to take any notice of this letter. I presume it is a multi-
plex copy sent pro forma to all the members of the Working Com-
mittee.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. A. RANGASWAMI IYENGAR
DESHBANDHU BUILDINGS
MOUNT ROAD
MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 14160
