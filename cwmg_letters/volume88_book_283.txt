283. LETTER TO RAVI SHANKAR SHUKLA
NEW DELHI,
July 7, 1947
BHAI SHUKLAJI,
I have a long letter from Dr. Hasan, a copy of which I am
sending to you. Is this episode closed now? Has everything been
cleared up? Is there no more complaint against the Doctor?
Blessings from
BAPU
