497. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
July 28, 1947
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. I have gone through Kishorelal's let-
ter. I understand Manilal's view. I prefer mine. If Sita were
to marry today, she would not have my blessings. The world
endures through vows. If I had not taken a vow before my
mother, today I would be a drink-addict, a meat-eater and a
lecher. If I was saved from these three vices, the credit goes
to the vow. And I had not taken the vow of my own free
will, but at my mother's instance and through my eagerness to
go to England.
But in this case you are the mother and Manilal is the
father, and I admit that the father and the mother know better
than the grandfather. Therefore, what you two advise will be
best. If you leave the decision to me, I shall not be able to
take any other view. I send herewith the letter which I re-
ceived from Sita today. I have replied to her. I think she will
send the letter to you.
I should be happy if you could come to some definite deci-
sion. I shall most probably leave Delhi tomorrow.
