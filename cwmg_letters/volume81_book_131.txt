131. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
August 5, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
It is the same to an inmate of the Ashram whether he works
as a manager or as a sweeper. Being a sweeper probably entails
a greater responsibility. In being a manager there is no author-
ity, only service. One should be prepared for both. If one finds
being a sweeper pleasant and being a manager unpleasant, it is
a sign of ignorance. And, if being a manager smacks of author-
ity it means one is conceited. Keep yourself ready for manager-
ship. Do not bother about whether or not you get it. I myself
do not know when you may get it.
Blessings from
BAPU
