382. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
1 DARYAGANJ, DELHI,
March 20, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
I thank you for your letter just received. I knew about the
meeting you refer to. I have already taken every precaution pos-
sible and hope that nothing untoward will happen. I suggest that
there should be no display of police force and no interference at
the meeting. Irritation is undoubtedly there. It would be better
to allow it to find vent through meetings, etc.
Yours sincerely,
H. W. EMERSON, ESQ.
CHIEF SECRETARY TO THE GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
NEW DELHI
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
