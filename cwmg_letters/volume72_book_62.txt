62. LETTER TO AKBAR HYDARI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 9, 1940
DEAR SIR AKBAR,
Your letter makes strange reading. Communal unity would
never come by generalizations or glossing over serious disturbances.
I do not know what Bihar incident you refer to. But if there was
a denial of justice in Bihar, it is no precedent worthy to be copied.
And why should public criticism of a big disturbance prejudice
judicial proceedings against individuals?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 8018
