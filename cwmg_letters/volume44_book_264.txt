264. LETTER TO VENILAL GANDHI
September 28, 1930
CHI. VENILAL GANDHI,
I have your letter. For your two-year-old illness the proper
remedy is not a vaid's medicine but change of air and diet
or, if necessary, a long fast. Hundreds recover their health in this
manner.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 916. Courtesy: Venilal Gandhi
