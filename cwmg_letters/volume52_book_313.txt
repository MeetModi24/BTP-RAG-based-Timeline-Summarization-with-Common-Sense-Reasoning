313. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
December 19, 1932
BHAI KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your postcard. I had the registered letter too. I
did not reply to it as it contained nothing new and I have no
time these days. If you read carefully all my letters and your
questions alongside, you will find the answers there.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4266
