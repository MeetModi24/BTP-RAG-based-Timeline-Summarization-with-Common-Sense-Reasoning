729. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
December 25, 1933
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have received your letters. But where do I have the leisure
to write replies? Today being a silence day, I am scribbling as
many letters as possible.
You should take money from the Harijan Fund account with
Jamnalalji to meet the cost of the tin sheets sent to Nasik and
to make up the deficit Gosibehn has in the Harijan budget,
then let me know how much money is still left. I think it is
only proper that Gosibehn is paid every month. I shall continue
my efforts to raise money from Bombay.
In what form a reply should be given about Vithalbhai is
a real problem. I shall think over it.
I have not seen Munshi's statement. He has talked to you.
He has also told you many other things about himself. I have
told him to do what he thinks proper. I also said that I had
no intention at the moment of guiding anybody.
The newspapers say that Jivaraj has taken charge. If the
reports are correct, congratulate him. I was a little worried.
Surely he is taking some rest? What does he have to do?
I have not been able to write to Gokhale, Bapa and others.
I hope to write to them tomorrow.
I had known about the Orissa Committee at Ahmedabad.
It will be good if some money is collected.
Do take rest and get rid of your pain.
Taramati must be doing well.
Brijkrishna seems to have fully recovered. There was a telegram.
Blessings from
BAPU
