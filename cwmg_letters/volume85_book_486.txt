486. LETTER TO S. P. PATWARDHAN
NEW DELHI,
September 27, 1946
CHI. APPA,
I have your postcard. In my opinion milk and fruit-juice is
the diet for you, and of course rest. Maybe even a single day's
fast without water will prove efficacious. If the bowels don't
move having an enema might be necessary. But since Dr. Bhagawat
has arrived there is no need for me to say anything more.
Ramanama is of course there. I hope you have received the
postcard I wrote yesterday. It would be easier to guide you if
the cause of the pain could be detected.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. APPA PATWARDHAN
P. O. LANJE
