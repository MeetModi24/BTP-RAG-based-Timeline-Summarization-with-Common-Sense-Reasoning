252. LETTER TO SAMPURNANAND
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 19, 1938
BHAI SAMPURNANAND,
What is this news item about a Congress platoon of 10,000 to
be formed in Farrukhabad? If an armed platoon is envisaged, is
it compatible with the Congress creed of non-violence?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From Hindi: C.W. 10132. Courtesy: Kashi Vidyapith, Varanasi
