252. LETTER TO J. L. MAFFEY
ST. STEPHEN'S COLLEGE,
DELHI,
April 27, 1918
DEAR MR. MAFFEY,
I duly received your wire as also your note of the 19th instant
for both of which I thank you.

M. K. GANDHI
