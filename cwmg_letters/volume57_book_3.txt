3. LETTER TO MANIBEHN N. PARIKH
January 16, 1934
CHI. MANI1,
Give me detailed news about yourself.
How did you spend
your time, what did you read and what did you think about?
Write about the children, too, and tell me whatever you wish to.
Everybody may do as he or she wishes.
There is no time
to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5975. Also C.W. 3292. Courtesy:
