49. LETTER TO PROFESSOR PARMANAND
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 31, 1906
DEAR PROFESSOR PARMANAND,
I am sorry you cannot be here today. Pillay's case is very sad. I
do not know what should be done, but when we meet we shall have
to think it out. He seems to have difficulty in finding even his food.
Will you go into his case thoroughly and if necessary find him lodgings
at India House?
Yours sincerely,
PROFESSOR PARMANAND
65, CROMWELL AVENUE
HIGHGATE
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4430
