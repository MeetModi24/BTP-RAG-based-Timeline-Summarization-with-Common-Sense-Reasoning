325. LETTER TO VIRENDRA PRAKASH
October 5, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I cannot imagine a more effective method of honouring the
memory of the great Swami Dayanand than by every Arya
Samajist devoting his best energies to the cause of the Harijans
during this wave of reform.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 145. Also Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 109
