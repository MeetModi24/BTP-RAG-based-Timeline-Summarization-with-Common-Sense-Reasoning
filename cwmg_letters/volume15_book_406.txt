406. LETTER TO D. N. NAGARKATTI
[July 14, 1919]
DEAR SIR,
Could you please let me know whether in the Nizam's
Dominions, the industry of cotton-spinning by hand is still going
and if it is what counts are spun and what quantity of yarn is so
turned out annually? Please also let me know how many hand-
looms are working in His Exalted Highness' Dominions and what
quantity and value of cloth is being manufactured annually.
Yours sincerely,
D. N. NAGARKATTI
ASSISTANT DIRECTOR OF INDUSTRY
HYDERABAD, DECCAN
From a photostat: S.N. 6748
