49. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
[SEGAON,]
April 3, 1937
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I hope you are following my prescription about your toe.
Local earth bandage should also be applied.
Of course you won't sign the big book1, if it degrades you
in your own estimation. The A. I. V. I. A. meeting accepted Jajuji's
resignation and did not appoint anyone else. Kumarappa did
not shine at the meeting. But it is all right. The things will
shape themselves.
Here it is like monsoon.
Love.
TYRANT
[PS.]
You have not replied to Kishorelal's letter.
From the original: C.W. 3773. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6929
