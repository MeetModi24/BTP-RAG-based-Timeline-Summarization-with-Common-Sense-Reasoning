185. LETTER TO PIERRE MARTIN
PATNA,
May 16, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
I have got your letter of 17th April addressed to Miss Owen .
I would advise you not to come to India in the expectation of seeing
me and learning from me the philosophy of non-violence. I am not
a reader of books. I am a doer. Probably by being in contact with
my daily life you will learn nothing unless you are a man of non-
violence yourself. And if you are, what need can there be in
watching me doing something hour to hour? Another word for non-
