149. LETTER TO RASIK DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 24, 1930
CHI. RASIK (DESAI),
Did you uphold my honour? Did you keep the vows which
you had taken? Give me all the details. How did you spend the
time? Were you lazy? Were you talkative? Did you keep good
health? Reply to these and many other similar questions. What
friends did you make?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6617
