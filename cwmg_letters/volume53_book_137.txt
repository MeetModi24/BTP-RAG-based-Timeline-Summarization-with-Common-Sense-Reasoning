137. LETTER TO K. V. SESHA AIYANGAR
January 21, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have been glancing through the numbers of the Bharat
Dharma and the pamphlets you have kindly sent. The prece-
dents of prayopavesan you have collected have proved useful to me.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. V. SESHA AIYANGAR
23 NADU STREET
MYLAPORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 18988
