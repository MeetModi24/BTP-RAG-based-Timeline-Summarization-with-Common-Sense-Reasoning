311. LETTER TO K. S. SUBRAMANIAM
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
September 14, 1928
MY DEAR SUBRAMANIAM,
I have gone through the circular letter . You will find in
the enclosed the additions I have made.
With reference to the figures as to spinners, I think that it is
necessary to have the census taken from year to year and as accu-
rately as possible.
Yours sincerely,
Enclosure: 1
SJT. K. S. SUBRAMANIAM
A.I.S.A., AHMEDABAD
[ENCLOSURE]
ADDITIONS TO THE CIRCULAR LETTER DATED JUNE 22, 1928
No. 28. Add after "Progress made":
also information as to whether spinning is done on wheels or
taklis, and what is done with the yarn spun.
To have the following as Item No. 32:
The average condition of spinners, weavers, carders, giving
how many days in the year they work at the rate of how many
hours, their other occupation if any, and what is the average earn-
ing from their other occupation, when they are not spinning, or
weaving or carding.
