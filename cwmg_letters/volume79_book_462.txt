462. LETTER TO DR. JIVRAJ MEHTA
SEVAGRAM,
March 19, 1945
BHAI JIVRAJ,
As is your custom, you have given me a perfect reply and
have satisfied Chi. Indu too. Listen to Hansabehn. Do not
take upon yourself any extra work and render greater service to
the country.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. JIVRAJ MEHTA
22 CURZON ROAD
NEW DELHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
