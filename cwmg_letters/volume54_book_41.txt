41. LETTER TO THE PRESIDENT, YOUNG MEN'S
BUDDHIST ASSOCIATION
March 10, 1933
THE PRESIDENT
YOUNG MEN'S BUDDHIST ASSOCIATION
MAHA BODHI ASHRAM
PERAMBUR, MADRAS
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I wish that it was in my power to help
you. Whatever was possible for me to do I did some years ago.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20537
