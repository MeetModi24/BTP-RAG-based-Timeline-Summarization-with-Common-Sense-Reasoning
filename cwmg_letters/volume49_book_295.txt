295. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
March 26, 1932
CHI. MANI,
Today I received permission to write to fellow-prisoners.
Hence this letter. As I have got this permission, I take it that
the recipients of my letters will be allowed to reply to me. Reply
to this letter at once. I do not write today to other women
prisoners, namely, Lilavati4, Nandubehn and Mridula . So let me
have news of them too. Who else are with you?
1 Sumant Mehta
2 Gangabehn Jhaveri
3 Pannalal Jhaveri, Gangabehn's step-son
4 Wife of Harilal Desai
5 Nandubehn Kanuga
6 Mridula Sarabhai
240
Mahadev has been brought here. We three are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MANIBEHN PATEL
PRISONER
PRISON, BELGAUM
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 80
