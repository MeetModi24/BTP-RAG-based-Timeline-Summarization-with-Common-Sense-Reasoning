283. LETTER TO JEHANGIR C. PATEL
[May 28, 1946]
BHAI JEHANGIRJI,
I have your letter. I welcome your suggestion. I am glad
that you like the work at Uruli. We can induce others only after
the tenements at Uruli Kanchan are ready.
I had to leave Bhai Dinshaw at Delhi. I came here today.
All is well. I may stay here for about ten days.
SHETH JEHANGIRJI PATEL
PATEL COTTON COMPANY
10 CHURCHGATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
