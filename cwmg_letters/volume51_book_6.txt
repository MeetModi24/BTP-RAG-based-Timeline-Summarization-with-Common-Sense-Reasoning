6. LETTER TO NARMADABEHN RANA
September 1, 1932
CHI. NARMADA,
I have your letter. This time your handwriting is better. If
you keep up the effort it will gradually improve. You say that you
have learnt everything. This cannot be true. There are many
things to be learnt. Your Gujarati is still very weak. You have
yet to learn Sanskrit. Ginning, carding, weaving, sewing, em-
broidery, spinning yarn of fine count-all this remains.
You should know arithmetic and also learn nursing. You
will learn all this in time.
Speak less, think more. I understand what you say about
your dress. At the moment, I cannot think of anything against it.
I wish you to become a very good girl.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2762. Courtesy: Ramnarayan N.
