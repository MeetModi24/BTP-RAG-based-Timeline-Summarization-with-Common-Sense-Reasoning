212. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
January 4, 1936
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I am distressed by my inability to come there but I could
not accept the strict conditions laid down by the doctors. The
better course indeed is not to go at all if my health is as
bad as they think. Now, in any case, you should come here
after finishing the work of raising contributions for the Harijan
cause in a few days. Bring Rajen Babu also with you. Probably
you will be able to raise the needed money at Ahmedabad itself.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
GUJARAT VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
[From Gujarati]
