394. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
SEVAGRAM,
April 10, 1940
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I have your letter. If you go to Panchgani, I think it would be
better for all of you to go. I will make inquiries at Panchgani. If
proper arrangements cannot be made in Panchgani, then, I think,
it would be better to pass the summer where you are. There will be
no harm if the temperature does not go above 102°. But if your
health deteriorates, we will have to find out some other way. I am
not writing separately to Balkrishna and Kanchanbehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
