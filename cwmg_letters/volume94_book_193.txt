193. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
CONGRESS HOUSE,
VITTHALBHAI PATEL ROAD, BOMBAY,
August 7, 1942
MY DEAR LOTUS-BORN,
I have your dear letter. I miss you at this meeting which you would
have liked. But your contribution to the struggle is to restore your broken
body to full health and then report yourself to me for the next order.
Therefore, concentrate on the full restoration of your health. First thing
first. Cheer up.
Love.
PLAYMATE
SHRI PADMAJA NAIDU
ZAHEER MANZIL
RED HILLS
HYDERABAD
DECCAN
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
