15. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
KARACHI,
Wednesday, February 6, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I have really no time to write. I know how much you are
working. After the work in Sind is over, I may have to go to
Delhi. I know that some things go on more smoothly in my
absence, for the excitement and agitation caused by my presence
subside then.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5384
