253. LETTER TO ARUNCHANDRA GUPTA
October 18, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter of October 2nd as also your letter of 27-7-1944 have been
duly received by Gandhiji. Thanks for your good wishes.
What you say about Bengal is true. The matter is deeply exercising
Gandhiji's mind. But at present the scope for effective action seems to be
very limited indeed.
He feels the handicap and the limitation galls him.
He is therefore waiting upon God to show him the way.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI ARUNCHANDRA GUPTA
SECURITY PRISONER, SPECIAL RESERVE JAIL
P.O. BUNA DUAR
JALPAIGURI DIST. (BENGAL)
From a photostat : G.N. 8670
1 & 2 Akbarbhai Chavda and his wife
