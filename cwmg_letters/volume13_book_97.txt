97. LETTER TO MAHATMA MUNSHIRAM
AHMEDABAD,
Jeth Shukla 2 [June 14, 1915]
MAHATMAJI,
As I was preoccupied with making all sorts of arrangements
after the boys' arrival from Gurukul, I could not write to you
earlier. The boys can never forget the love you showered on
them. I am highly obliged to you for giving shelter to my boys
and colleagues.
I have started an Ashram at Ahmedabad at any rate for the
time being. A Hindi version of its rules and regulations is being
prepared. When ready, it will be sent to you for your opinion.
I have not at all forgotten my promise to go to Hardwar again and
spend a few days with you. I will certainly come when I get the
time.
Looking forward to your kindness,
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 2208
