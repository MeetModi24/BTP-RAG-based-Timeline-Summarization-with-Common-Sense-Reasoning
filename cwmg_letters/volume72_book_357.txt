357. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEGAON,
July 21, 1940
CHI. SHARMA,
Why did you neglect the thing till it has developed into a
sinus? What has happened to your medical training? Why
are you retaining Draupadi and the children there? I do not
understand your ways. As for police harassment, I shall do
whatever I can.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 287
