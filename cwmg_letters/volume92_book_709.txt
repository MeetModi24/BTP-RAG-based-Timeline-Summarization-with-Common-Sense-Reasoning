709. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 30, 1933
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Herewith is Tersey's letter. Vithalbhai has to be cremated.
Do you have anything to do with the arrangements? You must
have seen my note. If anyone hopes for my presence, disabuse
him of the idea if you can. Of course if pressure is brought to
bear on me I shall be able to manage, but I would rather that
was not done. I do not feel at ease outside. My mind is always
in jail. I shall tour for the Harijan cause. I hardly think of
anything else.
Do make it a point to meet Hasanbhai regarding the market.
If everywhere you find only dirt keep yourself aloof. Personal
selfishness appears to be playing a considerable part in
this matter.
The money for Utkal does not seem to be coming. Then,
Harakhchand's report has come. I shall send it later. The purpose
of writing this is not to say that you should work beyond your
capacity. As for me, I see the sad state of affairs in Bombay
and feel distressed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal
