436. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
July 23, 1947
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have your letter. What you say about Pushpabehn is correct.
I have written to her. Read the letter.
The incident about the Gonds is painful. We are very far
from ahimsa. We should go on striving.
Hoshiari should not fall ill.
I have no time to write more. Go on doing whatever you can
there.
There will certainly be mistakes. Our task is to rectify them
and go forward.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have forgotten the difference between Gonds and Harijans.
I could not distinguish between a whip and a cane.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 1985
