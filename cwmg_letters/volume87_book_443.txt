443. LETTER TO A PHYSICIAN
May 16, 1947
I had read your letter as soon I received it. But I saw the
accompanying article only today. I was happy to read it. It is not
enough to say that you have cured all your patients through yoga
exercises. You ought to have indicated the particular exercises
and mentioned whether diet, etc., played an important part.
In all books whether worthwhile or no, details are men-
tioned. You should do the same.
I thought of you when out of sheer despair I had to seek
the help of doctors for my own little girl.
I told myself how
nice it would have been if I could have cured this girl with the
help of yoga exercises, etc. She had appendicitis and inflamma-
tion. She could not be cured with the earth-water-and-diet
[treatment]. I consulted two naturopaths. They also could not
help. A hakim even suggested treating her with myrobalan and
wood-apple. This also did not help. Now she is lying in the
hospital. I who believed in nature cure and yoga exercises had
to confess defeat. I did not like it. But I could do nothing
about it. If the girl took fruit she used to get seven or eight
motions. She was kept on just a little buttermilk. I could stop
neither her motions nor the inflammation of her appendix. Now,
tell me, what should I have done in such a case?
[From Hindi]
Biharni Komi Agman, p. 372
