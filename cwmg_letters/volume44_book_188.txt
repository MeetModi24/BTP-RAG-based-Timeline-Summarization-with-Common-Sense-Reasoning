188. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 8, 1930
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
Your regards have been conveyed to me. You are in my
thoughts every day. As usual, I have the watch before me as a
constant reminder. You can write to me. Are you out of the
wood? My regards to Grandmother. May God give you peace
of mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4719. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
