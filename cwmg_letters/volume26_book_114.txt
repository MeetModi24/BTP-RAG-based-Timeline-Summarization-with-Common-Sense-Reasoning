114. LETTER TO DR. MANCREVOUR
February 28, 1925
I thank you for your letter. My belief in satyagraha and non-
violence is as green as ever. I am myself still practising non-co-
operation and so are thousands of men and women of India. The
agreement with those who differ from me is to suspend non-co-opera-
tion as a national programme. This suspension leaves those who
wish free to enter the legislative councils of this country.
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
