424. LETTER TO T. B. SAPRU
SEVAGRAM,
November 17, 1944
DEAR SIR TEJ,
Gandhiji has your letter of 13th instant. He is of opinion that it will
be better not to use his name in public in connection with the forthcoming
conference, but if you feel you must he would not mind.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
RT. HON'BLE SIR TEJ BAHADUR SAPRU
19 ALBERT ROAD
ALLAHABAD
Gandhi-Sapru Papers. Courtesy: National Library, Calcutta
