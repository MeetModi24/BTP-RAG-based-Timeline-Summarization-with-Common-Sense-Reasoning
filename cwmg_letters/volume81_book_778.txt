778. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
October 28, 1945
CHI. CHAND,
I have your postcard. Rest assured about your welfare.
Your spirit of service will ever grow. Make your body as strong
as steel and complete your studies. I hope to reach Sevagram
on 21st November.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUMARI CHANDBEHN
DAGA MEMORIAL HOSPITAL
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
