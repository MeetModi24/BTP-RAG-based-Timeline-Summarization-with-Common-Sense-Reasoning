168. LETTER TO MAITHILISHARAN GUPTA
February 20/22, 1932
BHAI MAITHILISHARANJI,
I have received the gift you sent. I will read it with interest.
I shall enjoy reading it as the subject too is after my heart.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
February 22, 1932
[PS.]
I have finished Panchavati and liked it. Have started on
Saket.
From Hindi: C.W. 9454. Courtesy: Bharat Kala Bhavan
