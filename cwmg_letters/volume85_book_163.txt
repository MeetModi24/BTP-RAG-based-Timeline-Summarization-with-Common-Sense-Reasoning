163. LETTER TO MOHANLAL M. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
August 13, 1946
BHAI MOHANLAL (GADHADAWALA),
I think we should all be content with whatever Shantilal
can do. But if Sir Chunnilal wishes to offer something, I would
say let us have the estate known as Preston and the bungalow
so that we can also open a sanatorium for the poor. Shantilal's
plot cannot accommodate two projects. Therefore, please consider
this matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
SF. MOHANLAL MOTICHAND SHAH
GADHADA, via BHAVNAGAR
