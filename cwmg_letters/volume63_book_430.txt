430. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
SEGAON,
October 2, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
You may bring in a doctor, if you wish, to put your mind
at rest. It would be good if Amtul's letter goes today. The
rest when you come. At present Rajendra Babu and others have
gathered here. Nanavati is still not free from fever but he is
cheerful.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11489
