477. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
November 27, 1930
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I have your letter and report. The report is excellent. You
say that you saw no cow in the places which you visited; the posi-
tion is the same in almost the whole of Kheda district. People are
selfish and are simply not interested in keeping cows. That is
why we have taken up cow-protection work.
Who cooks your meals?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G.S. Gangabehnne, p. 48; also C.W. 8766. Courtesy: Ganga-
behn Vaidya
