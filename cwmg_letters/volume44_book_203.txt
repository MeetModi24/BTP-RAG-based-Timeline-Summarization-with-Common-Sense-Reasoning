203. LETTER TO BENARSILAL BAZAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 11, 1930
CHI. BENARSI,
I have your letter. You were arrested and released too!
I am confident that in all that you do you will be calm, truthful
and non-violent. I have, therefore, no worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 9304. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
