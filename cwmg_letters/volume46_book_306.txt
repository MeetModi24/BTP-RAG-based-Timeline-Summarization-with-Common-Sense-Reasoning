306. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
BARDOLI,
May 30, 1931
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I saw your letter to Mahadev. You have sent the correct
reply to Polak. I had sent an almost similar reply to the cable
from those gentlemen. Even now I hold that my going would be
pointless until the Hindu-Muslim problem is resolved. But, to go to
meet the members of the Cabinet and other people is another
thing.
Subhasbabu met me again and we had a long talk. But it is
difficult to say anything; I saw Sen Gupta's letter in the news-
papers yesterday. Let us see what happens on the 9th. I have
asked Subhasbabu too to come over.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a copy of Hindi: C.W. 7886. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
