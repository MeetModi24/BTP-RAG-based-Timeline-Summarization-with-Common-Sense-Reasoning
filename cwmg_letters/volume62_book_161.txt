161. LETTER TO G. V. MAVALANKAR
November 25, 1935
BHAI MAVALANKAR,
You must of course be a member of the Reception Commit-
tee. Take me for the opening ceremony as you wish, after con-
sulting the Committee. Convey my condolences to the family
of the deceased.
Blessings from
BAPU
