172. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
GAMDEVI, BOMBAY,
July 11, 1921
BHAISHRI DEVCHANDBHAI,
I got your letters. To be sure, Kathiawad has more than
fulfilled our expectations. I should like you now to devote your-
selves to the production of khadi.
I shall certainly not start civil disobedience all at once. Your
arithmetic is not correct. We shall have six crore spinning-wheels
plying without any effort on our part. We have not had to spend
much in introducing 20 lakhs. It could not have been other-
wise for, after we have spent a little, people spontaneously take up
anything which is useful to them. The work of enrolling more
members is also going on.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Gujarati: G.N. 5717
