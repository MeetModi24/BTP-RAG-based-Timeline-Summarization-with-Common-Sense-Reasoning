306. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
NEW DELHI,
July 10, 1947
CHI. RADHAKRISHNA,
When everything is so clear cut, it would be quite right to
follow Kamalnayan's suggestion.
You must understand that I really have no time.
Blessings from
BAPU
