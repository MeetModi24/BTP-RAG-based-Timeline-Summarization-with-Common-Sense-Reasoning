340. LETTER TO NAGADI
[March 19, 1946]
BHAI NAGADI,
I have your letter of February 26, 1946. I understand what
you say. I take it that when the time comes you as well as the
others will rise to the occasion. Your son is going to come and
see me.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
