546. LETTER TO RAGHUVANSH GAUR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
March 24, 1941
DEAR RAGHUVANSH,
Rajkumaribehn tried and failed. In the circumstances you
should for the time being employ yourself in some paying work
whereby your knowledge may not be lost, and cultivate non-violence.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 140
