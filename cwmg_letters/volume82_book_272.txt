272. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SODEPUR,
December 9, 1945
CHI. SHARMA,
I had sent a copy of your letter to Gadodiaji. I have a
long reply from him, saying that whatever you have said is all
fabrication and where it has any truth it has never been
hidden nor is it a matter of shame. I only hope that you
have not done anything improper and if you have you must
frankly confess it. I enclose a copy of Gadodiaji's letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI H. L. SHARMA
KHURJA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
