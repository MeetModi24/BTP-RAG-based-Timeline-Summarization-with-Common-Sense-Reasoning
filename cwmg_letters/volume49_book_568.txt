568. LETTER TO ARUN DAS GUPTA
May 22, 1932
Mother tells me you are ailing and that you insist on reading
and working. Will you not give yourself rest and the body a
chance of recovery? Though death and life are the faces of the
same coin and though we should die as cheerfully as we live, it
is necessary until life is there to give the body its due. It is a
charge given to us by God. And we have to take all reasonable
care about it. Do write to me if you can. God bless you.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 168
