169. LETTER TO CHANDAN PAREKH
RAJKOT,
April 14, 1939
CHI. CHANDAN,
I got your letter. I wrote one letter to you at Dehra
Dun. You don't seem to have received it. Write to me now.
Ba has completely recovered now. You needn't, there-
fore, come for her sake.
If you feel inclined to go to Bihar, do go. There is of
course plenty of work to be done there. But it may be desir-
able now to wait for Shankar.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 950. Courtesy: Satish D. Kalelkar
