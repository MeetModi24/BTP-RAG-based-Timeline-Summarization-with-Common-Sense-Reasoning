362. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
August 11, 1941
DEAR IDIOT,
Your note. You do not say whether you lost your headache
and why you had it. I do not mind your imprisoning Jamnalal
for a year, so long as you do not have to be with him all that
time. I have heard that Simla is at its very best in winter and
it is the cheapest-rent is nothing and vegetables and fruit you get
for a song. Valji was there during a winter and profited by his
stay.
I have another commission for [you]. Find out from some-
body what to do to get rid of white ants. We have a positive
infliction.
They have now attacked my bathroom and may
spread throughout the place if their mischief is not stopped in
[time].
228
The agricultural department must know the remedy.
Have I not told you that malish is done by Dhiren and office
work by Lakshmidas?
Amtul Salaam is quite well. She has added seven pounds to
her weight and is active the whole day long.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Are the apples from your garden?
