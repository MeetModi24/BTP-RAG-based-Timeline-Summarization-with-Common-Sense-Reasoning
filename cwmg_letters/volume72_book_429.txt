429. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 11, 1940
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
With reference to your kind letter of 31 st July, I am now able
to send you some papers. I have picked up a letter from one
whom I know well and who has never deceived me. That is
marked (A). Another is from Hinganghat (Dist. Wardha, C. P.).
I do not know the writer. But the information he gives can be
easily checked. It is marked (B). (C) is copy of a minatory no-
tice. These are but samples. The complaints are universal.
About the increase in the salaries given to persons appointed
to the new posts created I send you a sample list herewith. I
cannot have free access to this kind of information. The figures
I am sending you are supplied by those who claim to know.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
I was glad to have the good news about Lord Hopetoun.
M. K. G.
From a printed copy: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
