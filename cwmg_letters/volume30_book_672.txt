672. LETTER TO CHUNILAL D. GANDHI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 11, 1926
BHAISHRI CHUNILAL,
I have your letter as well as the hundi from your friend.
I intend to use the money for propagating khadi.
SJT. C. D. GANDHI
C/O TATA MILLS (LTD.)
BOMBAY HOUSE, BRUCE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19611
