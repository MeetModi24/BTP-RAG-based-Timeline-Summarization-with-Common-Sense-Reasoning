193. LETTER TO UTTAMCHAND SHAH
November 29, 1945
CHI. UTTAMCHAND,
I have such deep faith in you that I am sure that you will
succeed in your new undertaking, too, and that the work of the
Charkha Sangh under its new policy will progress in Gujarat at
least. Remember that it is your duty to take care of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9443
