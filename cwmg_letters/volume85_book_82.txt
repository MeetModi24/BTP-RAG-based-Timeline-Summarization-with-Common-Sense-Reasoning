82. LETTER TO GHARPURE
POONA,
July 29, 1946
I arrived here only yesterday. Today early in the morning
after the prayer, I devoted one hour to reading the book you
have sent. Although I did not have much time, I could not
put down the book after starting it. It is a very fascinating and
instructive work. I thank you. I am returning the book. Your
effort is commendable, no doubt about it. What help can I
give you? Your work alone will take you to your goal.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI GHARPURE, CURATOR
INDUSTRIAL MUSEUM
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
