432. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
WARDHA,
December 18, 1925
DEAR MOTI BABU,
I have your letter. I shall certainly help you to the best of
my ability in your khaddar work. But I need not say more as
you will be sending a representative to Kanpur.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. MOTILAL ROY
PRABARTAK SANGH
CHANDRANAGAR
From a photostat: G.N. 11023
