393. LETTER TO G. LAKSHMI AMMA
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
The condition you mention is truly deplorable. You and I
have to do the best we can. Nothing like patience and perseverance.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
G. LAKSHMI AMMA
DEPRESSED CLASSES MISSION
MANGALORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
