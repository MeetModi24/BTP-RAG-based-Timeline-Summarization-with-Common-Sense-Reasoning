79. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 22, 1936
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
Two of your letters are lying with me unanswered. To
me Diwali and Holi are the same. When there is a fire raging
before our eyes, it is a sin to celebrate Diwali. You indeed
deserve congratulations for settling the brothers' quarrel. I
believe that that is enough for the success of your Bombay trip.
It was good that there you could consult the doctors also. What
is wrong in serving your mother?
Now you will be able to
66
serve her with knowledge. Service to your mother can be a part
of the larger service to the country.
Do go to Trivandrum if you can improve your health there.
You might get well if the climate there suits you. However,
go only if Ramachandran permits you.
As far as I am con-
cerned I prefer Indore. But do whatever you think is best.
Hope Bari is improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
