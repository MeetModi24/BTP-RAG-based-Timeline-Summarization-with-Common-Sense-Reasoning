383. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
Silence Day [December 6, 1937)
CHI. MAHADEV,
I believe that we have somewhere the resolution regarding
the Indian States. What you have written about Ratne is correct.
Please ask Jamnalalji. Perhaps he will remember. Girdhari must
have given you the two items which I sent last night.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11585
