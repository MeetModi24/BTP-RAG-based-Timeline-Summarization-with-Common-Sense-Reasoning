313. LETTER TO B. G. HORNIMAN
[BOMBAY,]
June 6, 1919
DEAR MR. HORNIMAN,
I was much relieved to hear of your safe arrival. I was deeply
hurt to read Mr. Montagu's reference to you. I dare say you have
vindicated yourself. You will see my reference to the matter in
Young India.
Mrs. Naidu will tell you all about the situation here. There
will be no peace in India until the Rowlatt legislation is with-
drawn. Mahomedan sentiment must be appeased and the Punjab
sentences revised. Will you write for Young India?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
