66. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
WARDHA,
Thursday [December 17, 1925]
BHAISHRI VALJI,
I hope you have written to Chaunde Baba. Let me also
know when the day is fixed. When you come, bring along the
account books, the members' names, addresses, etc. I have looked
through the constitution. We should call the annual meeting.
We shall also think about appointing an efficient Working
Committee.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7742. Courtesy: Valjibhai Desai
