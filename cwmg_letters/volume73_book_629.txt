629. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
April 13, 1941
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I got your letter. Somehow obtain the certificate. Jajuji is
not here. I will ask him when he comes.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HEMPRABHADEVI
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
SODEPUR, via CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2736
