103. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
Sunday [January 3, 1926]
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I had your letter. You need not be sorry about Martand's
failing. Very few boys have escaped it. We should remain watch-
ful, and cleanse our own self, for our own sins are reflected in our
children. I hope you are quite well.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
70, SARAFA, INDORE
From the Hindi original: C. W. 6056. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
