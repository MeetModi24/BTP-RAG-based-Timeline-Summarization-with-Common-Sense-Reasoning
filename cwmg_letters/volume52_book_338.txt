338. LETTER TO RATILAL SHETH
December 21, 1932
BHAI RATILAL,
Read the long letter I have written to Bhai Nanalal. I have
talked frankly to Chhaganlal about his vices. I also told him
about the letter which Lilavati wrote to me. I thought that it was
absolutely necessary to do so. He, too, confessed that he had
those vices in the past. But I could see from his face that he
had them still. I also tried to convince him that, instead of be-
ing angry with Lilavati, he should believe that she had done him
a great service. He has definitely promised to reform himself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7170. Also C.W. 4664. Courtesy:
