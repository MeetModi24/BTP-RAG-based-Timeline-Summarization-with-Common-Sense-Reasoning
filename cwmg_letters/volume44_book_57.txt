57. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 21, 1930
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
Your note. When your brother returns he must first come to
the Vidyapith and see things for himself. Then, but not till then, he
is free to make his choice. You had my previous letter sent from
Karadi about him, had you not? Give my love to him when
he comes. And remember me to your sister when you write to
her.
'Thodsow' is a Tamil corruption of 'Dodhso' G;1~12l).
Love from us both.
Yours,
BAPU
[PS.]
Yes, your book was duly received, thanks.
