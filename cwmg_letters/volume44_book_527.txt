527. LETTER TO PADMA
December 14, 1930
CHI. PADMA,
I have your letter. This time the handwriting was better.
Improve it still further. I was happy to learn that there was
nothing serious about the tumour. All the same, take care of
your health. I am glad that you write every detail in the diary.
To what term has Sarojinidevi been sentenced?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6117
