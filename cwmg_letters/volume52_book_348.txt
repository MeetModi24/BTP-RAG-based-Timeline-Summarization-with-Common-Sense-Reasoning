348. LETTER TO DIWANCHAND
December 22, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. You are wrong in thinking that
Guruvayur is the only temple left to be opened, but whether it
is the only one or one out of many yet to be opened, the fast is
not my act, therefore if it comes I shall be unable to avoid it.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. DIWANCHAND
GORJRA (DISTRICT LYALLPUR)
From a microfilm: S.N. 18781
