101. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
Friday [November 23, 1928]
BHAI CHHAGANLAL,
I forgot to write about one thing. Call in Dr. Talwalkar
or Dr. Kanuga to examine Kashi . I am afraid she has got
dysentery. If that is so, she will have to be given injections of
ipecacuanha. Place this suggestion of mine before the doctor
whom you call in.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 10
