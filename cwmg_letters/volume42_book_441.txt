441. LETTER TO PURSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 9, 1930
MY DEAR SIR PURSHOTTAMDAS,
I thank you for your letter.
I must not enter into argument with you, for argument is
useless when conviction on either side is deep-seated. I can
only give you my assurance that I shall take no hasty step. A
risky step need not necessarily be a hasty step.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR PURSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
BIRLA HOUSE, ALBUQUERQUE ROAD
NEW DELHI
Purshottamdas Thakurdas Papers, File No. 96, 1930. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
