233. LETTER TO MANIBEHN NANAVATI
March 2, 1946
CHI. MANIBEHN 2 ,
Kishorelalbhai writes to tell me that I have not replied to
your letter. If so, it is a matter of shame for me. It is, however,
clear that I tend to forget. How can a forgetful person take up
any responsibility? What should I do? Do become an Agent
and show your mettle.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MANIBEHN NANAVATI
