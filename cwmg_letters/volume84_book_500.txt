500. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
POONA,
July 4, 1946
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
There is no point in the questions which you have asked.
A man who is wicked by nature will show his wickedness on
any occasion. If such a rule is to be framed, it can have no
room for exceptions.
I shall answer the rest of your questions when I go there
in August.
Blessings from
BAPU
