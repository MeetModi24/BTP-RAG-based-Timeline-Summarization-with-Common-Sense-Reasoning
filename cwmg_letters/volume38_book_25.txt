25. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[November 5, 1928)
CHI. MIRA,
I hope you got my wire . You must [not] wear yourself out.
There is no occasion for rushing. And do not deny yourself what
you may definitely need for your health. If you see any report
about my ill-health do not be alarmed. I had a slight attack of
malaria. There is nothing today.
Love.
BAPU
SHRIMATI MIRABAI
KHADI DEPOT, MUZAFFARPUR, BIHAR
From the original: G.N. 8212; also C.W. 5222. Courtesy: Mirabehn
