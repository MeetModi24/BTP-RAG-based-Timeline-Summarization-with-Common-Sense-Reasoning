49. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
BORSAD,
June 23, 1931
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
I forgot to get Sardar to wire though we talked for one
minute. You did well to send a reminder. I have now written.
You may expect a wire from Bardoli tomorrow or the day after.
BAPU
ADHYAPAK J. C. KUMARAPPA
GUJARAT VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
From a photostat: G.N. 10097
