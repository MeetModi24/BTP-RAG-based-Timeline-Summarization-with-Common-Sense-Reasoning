569. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
September 24, 1941
CHI. NARAHARI,
Chimanlal told me about your blood-pressure. It is a warning
to you.
You need rest.
If you go and hide yourself some-
where, you will be on your feet once again. Don't ignore the
warning.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am not writing anything for Mahadev, for he will have
left the place.
