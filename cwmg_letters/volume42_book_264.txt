264. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
December 7, 1929
I am very pleased with your decision. . . . There too your
time will not be wasted. Dilip and Tara are getting education of
the body and mind. Almora is not different from the Himalayas
and innumerable sadhus have lived there. Many seekers have
attained self-realization in the Himalayas; and if Tara remembers
this her soul too will be ennobled.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 97
