481. LETTER TO VIMALCHANDRA V. DESAI
February 19, 1933
CHI. NANU,
I got your letter. Your handwriting still leaves much to be
desired. If you understand that one should write each letter as
carefully as one draws a figure, your handwriting will be beautiful.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 5757. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
