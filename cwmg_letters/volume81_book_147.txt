147. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
August 8, 1945
CHI. RAJKUMARI,
Your letter. Your illness does disturb me. But what is the
use of my disturbing myself over the inevitable, whatever the
reason may be?
There was nothing wrong in your sending the wires and
mentioning the proposed hanging .
I am glad Shummy is better.
The cutting is no doubt vicious and untrue. But you are
right. It should be ignored. It cannot be answered even by
producing facts. It can only be answered by right action.
I wrote to Raja Daljit Singh yesterday. A copy will go
with this.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4274. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7906
1 A well-known Egyptian leader (1860-1927). This was sent on his death
anniversary.
2 Of Mahendra Chowdhary, who was hanged on August 7. Vide pp. 105-.
