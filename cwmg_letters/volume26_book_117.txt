117. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Phagan Sud 6, 1981 [February 28, 1925]
BHAISHRI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
The charkha that I got specially made for you has arrived. It
is certainly very fine to look at. Bhai Mahadev and I have both
tried it; it works very well. I wonder if anyone at our place
can set it up properly. Please let me know how it works. I had
asked Chi. Maganlal to send one more charkha; I do not know if
it has reached you. You must have received my previous letter.
I am proceeding to Vykom.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 1606. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
