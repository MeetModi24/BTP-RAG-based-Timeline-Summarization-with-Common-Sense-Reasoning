410. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
RANCHI,
[Adhik] Ashvin Shukla 9 [September 25, 1917]
DEAR BHAISHRI,
I got a letter from you in Bombay as I was going to catch the
train. I had asked my nephew to go to you in that connection.
Now I have Ramnarayanji's letter. He appears worthy of being
taken up. I have asked for some more information from him.
Two teachers from Maner have offered to come. I have already
engaged one of them. I am having talks about the other. They
will be able to join after two months. Ramnarayanji will be the
third. We should be able to carryon with these.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[From Hindi]
Panchaven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad
