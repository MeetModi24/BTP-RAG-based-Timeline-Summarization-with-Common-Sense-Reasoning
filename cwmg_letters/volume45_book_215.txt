215. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
February 6, 1931
CHI. KUSUM,
You know well enough what time I can spare when out of
jail. I shall not, therefore, be able to write as often is I used to
do from jail. Panditji passed away today. And so it has be-
come uncertain where I shall be going from here and where I
shall stay. If you wish to write, you may do so at Allahabad.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1819
