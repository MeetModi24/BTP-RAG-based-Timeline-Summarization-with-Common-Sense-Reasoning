67. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
August 28, 1944
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have your letter. It is worthy of a sinha . Ghanshyamdasji
worked according to his capacity and so did Swami. The thing is
that we should rest content with whatever anyone offers us or
else there is only disappointment in store for us. Is it a small
thing that Satis Babu appreciated your merits much more than
others? There will never be a cause for disappointment if you work
with detachment. We should not sit in judgment on others.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1946
