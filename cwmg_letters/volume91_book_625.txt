625. LETTER TO ALICE VARLET
January 30, 1928
DEAR FRIEND1,
I was pleased to receive your letter. It was a pleasure to
see you all. Do please come when you wish.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MISS VARLEY
BENTINCK HIGH SCHOOL
VEPERY
MADRAS
