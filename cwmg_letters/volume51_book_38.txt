38. LETTER TO MOHAN N. PARIKH
September 7, 1932
CHI. MOHAN,
May you live long. Be good and serve the country. Keep
the vow which you have taken, namely, that during the year
in which you are entering you will learn by heart four chapters.
And also make an effort to understand their meaning.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9183
