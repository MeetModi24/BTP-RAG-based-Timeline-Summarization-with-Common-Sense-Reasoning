161. LETTER TO TARADEVI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 28, 1937
CHI. TARA,
You are a lazy girl and you advance the excuse that your
aunt has forbidden you to write. Are you ashamed of asking
forgiveness for not writing till today?
And when you do write you have nothing to say except
that you are preparing for class X. Let us see what you
are going to write in your next letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TARABEHN
MAHILA VIDYALAYA
GANGA PRASAD ROAD
LUCKNOW
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
