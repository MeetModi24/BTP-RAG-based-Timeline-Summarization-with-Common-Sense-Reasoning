201. LETTER TO H. M. AHMAD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 1, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am passing it on to a friend who is
better able to reply to your questions than I am, and I have
asked him to write to you directly.
Yours sincerely,
H. M. AHMAD, E S Q .
SCHUHUMANNSTR.17
BERLIN N.W. 6
