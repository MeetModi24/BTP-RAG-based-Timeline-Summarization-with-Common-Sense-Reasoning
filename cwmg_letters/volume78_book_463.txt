463. LETTER TO T. R. NARSIMHACHAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 23, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your cautions. Everyone is useful. I shall not
enter upon the fast unless I have the clearest possible call from
the inner voice which is God.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI T. R. NARSIMHACHARJI OF TIRUPATI
SRIRANGAM
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
