332. LETTER TO VISHWANATH PRASAD MISHRA
February 7, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Whilst I should be delighted to see elec-
tion of Harijans to all elective institutions, I must not intervene by
way of anticipating results. It would be hardly proper for a pri-
soner to do so.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. VISHWANATH PD. MISHRA
PLEADER
CHAPRA
From a microfilm: S. N. 19306
1 The source is illegible here.
2 The addressee had requested Gandhiji to send his blessings for the suc-
cess of a Harijan candidate in a bye-election for the membership of the Bihar
and Orissa Legislative Council.
