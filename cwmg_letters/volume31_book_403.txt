403. LETTER TO CHHOTALAL TEJPAL
Bhadra Sud 1, 1982, September 8, 1926
BHAISHRI CHHOTALAL TEJPAL,
I do not see any connection between the evil of untouchability
and the question whether dead bodies should be carried on
shoulders or in a cart. I do not wish to make a will to oblige
people, after my death, to have my body carried in a cart, for I
see a sort of egotism in the idea. Moreover, if they cremate my
body on the Ashram premises, I would rather that they carried it
on a few bamboo poles on shoulders or with hands. I don't feel
that it is a matter of dharma to insist on dead bodies being carried
in carts. I fully accept, however, the necessity and propriety of
carrying them in that manner in certain circumstances.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
