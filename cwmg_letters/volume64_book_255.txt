255. LETTER TO MUTHULAKSHMI REDDY
January 11, 1937
DEAR SISTER,
I am writing this in a moving train. I am taking no part
or interest in the elections. But I agree with you that
women should be treated on a par with men. I go further,
where they are equal in ability they should be preferred. I do
not know what has happened in your case but you are in safe
hands. Both Pandit Jawaharlal and Sardar Patel would not be
party to favouritism. Unconscious injustice is sometimes inevitable
in human institutions.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
