30. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
WARDHA,
April 25, 1935
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I had your letter. I spoke to Munshi and wrote to Sardar.
You will be keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
74 WALKESHWAR ROAD
BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
