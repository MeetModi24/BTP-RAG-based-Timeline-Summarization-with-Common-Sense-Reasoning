257. LETTER TO A. CARTWRIGHT
[JOHANNESBURG,]
August 5, 1908
DEAR MR. CARTWRIGHT,
I enclose herewith copy of my letter to Mr. [Hosken.] Comment
is superfluous. I have used therein strong [expres]sions because I was
unable to use stronger ones. I have written exactly as I feel. While I
take pride in the fa[ct] of so many of my countrymen going to gaol,
and suffering even unnecessary hardships, I cannot help feeling the
situation most keenly, especially when I, who am chiefly responsible
for all these things, have to remain unhurt. I do think that, subject
to correction by you, it is time for you now to move editorially and
give the Transvaal Press a lead.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4855
