377. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI M. THAKKAR
June 3, 1931
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
Have you taken a vow not to write to me? Madhavji remains
somewhat worried about the children, particularly about Chandra.
There is a proposal to send Chandra to the Ashram. Let me
know your view in the matter. Tell me about your daily progra-
mme of work there. I should like you and your sister to come if
possible and see me here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6816
