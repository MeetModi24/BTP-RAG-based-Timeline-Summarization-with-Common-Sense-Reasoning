91. LETTER TO DHARMDEV SHASTRI
July 30, 1945
BHAI DHARMDEV SHASTRI,
I have your letter. I have told Shyamlalji that Rs. 200 may
be sent to you. That is the amount he had asked for. Your letter
mentions a larger amount. The rest will depend on Bapa's letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DHARMDEV SHASTRI
ASHOK ASHRAM
KALSI, DEHRADUN
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
