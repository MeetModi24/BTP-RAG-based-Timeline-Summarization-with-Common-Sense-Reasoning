193. LETTER TO J. SIVASHANMUGAM PILLAI
October 4, 1944
DEAR PILLAI,
Many thanks for your letter. Am sorry for the delay in
replying.
My own opinion is that you should do what you think will
best serve the 'Depressed Classes'.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 9257. Also C.W. 5065. Courtesy: J. S. Pillai
