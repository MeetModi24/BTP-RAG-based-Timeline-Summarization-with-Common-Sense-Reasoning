677. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA GARU
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 12, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
What a sad letter you have sent me. I appreciate the confi-
dence you have given me. After all it is these domestic troubles
which enrich one's life, for, they enable us to realize the vanity of
worldly pomp, worldly riches and worldly happiness and enable
us also to realize the true beauty of ahimsa, otherwise, love of the
purest type.
I am glad both your wife and daughter are improving. I
hope that the improvement will be continuous and permanent.
Yes, Devdas had an operation. He was discharged last week
and is now taking his convalescence with Jamnalalji at Mussoorie.
I am not going to Finland. There was a talk about my going.
But I decided not to go.
Yours sincerely,
