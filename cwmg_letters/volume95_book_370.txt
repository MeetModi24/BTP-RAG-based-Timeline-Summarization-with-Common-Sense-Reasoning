370. LETTER TO SHANTA S. PATEL
Tuesday
CHI. SHANTA,
It will not do if you do not study. Can one ever feel lazy about
studying? You must always study regularly even if it is little. May
be chillies are causing constipation. You must take some fruit with
milk and drink plenty of water.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9420
