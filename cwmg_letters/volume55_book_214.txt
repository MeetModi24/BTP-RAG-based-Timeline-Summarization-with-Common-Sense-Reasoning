214. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
PARNAKUTI, POONA 6,
June 6, 1933
MY DEAR AMALA,
I have your letter. You are with me when you are at the
Ashram doing the Ashram work. I am sure you understand this
very simple truth. There were many prisoners occupying the same
yard, sharing the same verandah. Do you suppose that they were
living with me?
Love.
BAPU
