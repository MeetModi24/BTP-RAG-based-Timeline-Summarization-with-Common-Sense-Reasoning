40. LETTER TO ARDESHIR E. KATELI
October 2, 1943
SHRI KHAN BAHADUR SAHEB,
You have sent me a copy of the reply given by the Gov-
ernment of Bombay to my letter. Pyarelalji has translated it
to me. I understood the first letter of the Bombay Government
according to my lights. But now I understand that, once I con-
vey my intention to stay here, I cannot subsequently change
my mind. This does not fully conform to the complete idea of
'one's free will'. But I have come here and am staying here for the
sake of service. That is why I accept the condition laid in the
reply to my letter, and it suits me well. Why should a sevika
even entertain any wishes of her own? So long as revered
Kasturba is here, I shall also be here.
