52. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
October 26, 1946
CHI. KRISHNADAS 1 ,
I have your letter. I agree that we should not demand yarn
against [the sale of] such items as cotton-mats, carpets, webbing
tape, etc.
Similarly, it seems right not to ask for yarn against khadi
required for hospitals and such other institutions.
Tell Jajuji that I have his letter. Perhaps it would be only
proper for Rajaji not to say anything because he is in the
Government. I am writing to Pattabhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. KRISHNADAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM
