274. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
FOOLISH DAUGHTER,
You read the opposite meaning of what I write. It is no
atonement to do something which gives you pleasure. Your massage
gave me pleasure. . . . 2 A man does not display love when he is
angry. I showered so much love on you because my anger had cooled
down. Do you get me now?

Kanu does not undertake a fast. Apply ointment to the piles.
The question of accompanying me remains. There should be no
promise to keep you always with me. This insistence has to be
abandoned.
BAPU
