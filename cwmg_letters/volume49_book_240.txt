240. LETTER TO NEWLY MARRIED COUPLE
March 14, 1932
CHI.,
You two have shown a new path. Both of you have my bless-
ings. Sardar voluntarily adds his. We wish that you two should
devote yourselves to selfless service. That your request for bless-
ings comes on a printed card makes the request purely formal
and to that extent it loses its value. If blessings are worth soli-
citing, they are worth soliciting in a self-written letter, which
should also state some good resolutions made by the couple to
mark the auspicious event.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 15
