710. LETTER TO DR. KRISHNABAI NIMBKAR
October 20, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter of the 10th instant. I have read all your
papers. Jajuji also must have gone through them.
I have already formed my opinion that everyone should
spin as a part of yajna. Its real impact will be known only
when people are convinced that yajna is much more than money.
Why should we worry whether the work is difficult or easy?
Now you also are coming to Poona leaving Madras. Let us see
what happens now.
DR. (MRS.) KRISHNABAI NIMBKAR
192 POONAMALLE HIGH ROAD
VEPERY P. O.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
