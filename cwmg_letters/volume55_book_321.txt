321. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
July 17, 1933
CHI. AMALA,
I shall try to send another chain. Do not worry about any-
thing, not even for me. After all God keeps us all and He is an
all-sufficing Protector.
Your Hindi letter was quite nice for you. The alphabet is not
yet correctly traced. You should compare it with the original.
You are not going to be unnerved on hearing that I am
taken to Yeravda or some such prison.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
