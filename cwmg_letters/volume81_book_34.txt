34. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA C. MEHTA
July 23, 1945
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
I got your loving gift of the loin-cloth. But if all the girls
should send things like this, wouldn't that spoil me? I would
get into the habit of wearing new and nice loin-cloths. The
really good and proper thing to do would be to give the best
thing you make or get made to your best student. That would
be truly as good as giving the thing to me.
Now you should learn hand-weaving also.
I hope your health is good and your work is going on well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9438
