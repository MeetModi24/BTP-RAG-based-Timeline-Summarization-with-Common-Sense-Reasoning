495. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
March 30, 1935
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your letter. It is good you are having treatment there.
Your leaving this place was also to the good.
You may certainly ask me any questions arising out of my
reply.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2439
