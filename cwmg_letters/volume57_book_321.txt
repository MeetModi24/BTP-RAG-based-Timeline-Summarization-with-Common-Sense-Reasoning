321. LETTER TO NARASINHARAO B. DIVATIA
PATNA,
March 23, 1934
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
It is my misfortune that I have to write only letters of con-
dolence to you.
I have just received a letter from Gokulbhai from which
I learn that your daughter's son, Premal, has passed away and
left you to a lonely existence. Poor boy, how was he to know
that you could not be lonely? Anybody who has a living faith
in God, who can call him lonely?
Gokulbhai writes that both
of you comfort those who come to comfort you. I was filled
with joy and pride to read it. May your faith in God increase.
Premal himself has passed to a blessed state. Perhaps you remem-
ber that I met him once in the blue bungalow.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Narasinharaoni Rojnishi, p. 58
