613. LETTER TO RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
November 10, 1932
DEAR RAMANAND BABU,
I thank you for copies of the annual reports. . . .1 for the
improvement of the Backward Classes of Bengal and Assam.
Glancing through the pages of the reports I note the steady pro-
gress made by the . . .2 in the work of amelioration. May its
efforts bear increasing fruit from day to day.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: C.W. 9581. Courtesy: Santa Devi
