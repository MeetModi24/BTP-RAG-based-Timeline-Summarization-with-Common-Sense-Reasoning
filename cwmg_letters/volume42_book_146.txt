146. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
MY DEAR MALKANI,
I am surprised that you had not heard from me up to the
28th ultimo, the date of your letter. I hope however that you
did receive the letter after the despatch of yours. In any case
now you do not need my letter as you have made up with
Jamshedji. I hope the work will go on without a hitch now.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
SJT. MALKANI
TILAK CONGRESS BHAWAN
HYDERABAD, SIND
From a photostat: G.N. 897
