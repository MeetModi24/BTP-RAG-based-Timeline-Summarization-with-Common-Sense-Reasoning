277. LETTER TO HARSHADA DIWANJI
February 24, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I received yesterday the packet containing the skein of yarn
and the letter. Today I have another letter and a cheque for
Rs. 54. I can never remember birthdays. I have no idea how
old Diwanji is. Now I send him my blessings. And he has many
more years still to live. He has to serve with this very body.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HARSHADABEHN DIWANJI
15TH STREET, KHAR
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10223
