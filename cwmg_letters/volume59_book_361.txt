361. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
November 12, 1934
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I am not happy at the delay in the healing of your ear.
I have sent letters to the doctors.
Jayaprakash seems to be ill.
He wants Prabhavati to go
to Ahmednagar. If she goes, it is best from the point of view
of the Ashram that she should go just now. If, therefore,
Jayaprakash wants her to go and if you permit, I will secure
Vinoba's permission and send her away. She is extremely busy
even today. . . .3
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 141
