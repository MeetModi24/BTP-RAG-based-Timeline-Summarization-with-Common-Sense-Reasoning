371. LETTER TO DR. M. A. ANSARI
November 13, 1934
DEAR DR. ANSARI,
It was a great effort for me to resist the temptation to re-
spond to your telegram as you would have liked me to do. But
I felt that I must not respond. I resisted the temptation else-
where also. I hope, therefore, that you will forgive me.
Could you send me replies to the enclosed questions?
Encl. 1
DR. M. A. ANSARI
DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
