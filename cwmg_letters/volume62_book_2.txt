2. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
October 1, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
I got your letter. I understand about Ramji. His nature is
exactly as you describe it. What can we expect from one whom
even a man like Lakshmidas found difficult to deal with?
It is
our penance to suffer him. The rest when we meet.
Tell me about the machine, too, when you come. If the
engine-pump proves to be an enemy of the bullock, then we had
better be careful about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
