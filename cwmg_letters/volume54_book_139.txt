139. LETTER TO G. SUBRAMANIAN
March 18, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I wholly disagree with you that there
are no workers in the Harijan cause in the State of Mysore ex-
cept yourself. My information is quite to the contrary.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. G. SUBRAMANIAN
PROPAGANDIST
SRINGERI POST
From a microfilm: S.N. 20617
