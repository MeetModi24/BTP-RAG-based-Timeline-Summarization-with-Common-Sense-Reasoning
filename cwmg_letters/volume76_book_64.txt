64. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS SHAH
April 17, 1942
BHAI KRISHNADAS,
I have your letter.
I do have faith in Ayurveda but what
can I do if the vaidyas lack competence? All the same I keep
in touch with them and take what I can out of them. It is pre-
cious little, however.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. KRISHNADAS SHAH
BHARATIYA UDYOG PRACHAR
14 BHASKAR LANE, 3RD FLOOR
BHULESHWAR, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 11536
