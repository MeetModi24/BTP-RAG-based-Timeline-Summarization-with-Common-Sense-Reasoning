30. LETTER TO NANABHAI I. MASHRUWALA
WARDHA,
September 28, 1933
BHAI NANABHAI 1 ,
I got your letter. You ought not to come here despite your
weak health. I am quite well. When I have regained complete
strength and can travel, your love will certainly draw me to
Akola. Manilal seems quite busy. He has undertaken a big
responsibility.
Jamnalalji gave me a report about Tara .
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. NANABHAI MASHRUWALA
AKOLA, BERAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6689. Also C.W. 4334. Courtesy:
Kanubhai Mashruwala
