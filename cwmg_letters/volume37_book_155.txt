155. LETTER TO MATHURA PRASAD
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I am sorry not to have been able to reply to your letter
earlier.
I am unable to guide you in the matter mentioned by you.
It is likely that, if you try, you may get some help from the
Government.
MATHURA PRASAD
BHABUA (BIHAR)
From a microfilm: S.N. 13900
