469. LETTER TO HORACE ALEXANDER
NEW DELHI,
July 26, 1947
MY DEAR HORACE,
I have yours of 22nd.
It is high time we met. I am not here now long I hope.
I should be leaving in two or three days. Patna for four or
five days and then Noakhali just before August 15.
You will know my programme and see where we should
meet.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G. N. 1446
