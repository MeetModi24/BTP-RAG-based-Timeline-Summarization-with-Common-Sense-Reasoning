133. LETTER TO VANAMALA PARIKH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Saturday [January 17, 1931]
CHI. VANAMALA,
What an intelligent girl you are! A sum which would seem
difficult to other children, you found quite easy. Here is another,
a little more difficult, sum. If a girl spins 252 rounds in an
hour, how many would she spin in twenty minutes?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5760
