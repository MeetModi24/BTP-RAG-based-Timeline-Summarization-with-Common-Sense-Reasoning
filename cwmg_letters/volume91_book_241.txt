241. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
AMBUR,
Saturday [August 14, 1920]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I think a lot about you. I shall await your letter.
I would particularly suggest that you should consider how
much we harm ourselves and the country by anger. I am not
writing this to find fault with you. I agree that you are under
a nervous strain. But it is a mental trouble and so you can
acquire control over your mind.
Would you make it a point to read the last stanzas in
chapter 11 of the Gita which have been spoken by Krishna in
reply to a question by Arjuna! These stanzas contain the remedy
for all ailments.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 32714
