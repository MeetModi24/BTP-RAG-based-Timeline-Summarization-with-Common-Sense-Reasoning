195. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
February 2, 1931
CHI. LILAVATI,
I got your letter. Make full use of the opportunities there. But
do not be impatient while doing that. Follow Khurshedbehn's
advice. If the food does not agree with you, tell them so. Write to
me whenever permitted to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9319
