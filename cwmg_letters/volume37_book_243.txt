243. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 25, 1928
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letters. You should now recover complete health.
If that is at all possible, you ought to make it so. You write
kef, but the real word is kaph. It is an English word and means what
we call galafo. The word kaph, however, has become part of
our language and we can freely use it. Kef means intoxication, and
the word is not taken from English.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. VASUMATIBEHN
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 497. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
