429. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
September 10, 1945
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
There is nothing strange in what you have said. I would
like to take you somewhere if I have to travel any time. It
will be only a question of convenience. Write to me when the
time comes.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
CHI. CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH
RAJKOT
KATHIAWAR
From a copy of the Gujarati ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
