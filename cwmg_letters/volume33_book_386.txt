386. LETTER TO SRI PRAKASA
NANDI HILLS,
May 26, 1927
MY DEAR SRI PRAKASA,
I was delighted to receive your letter redirected to me
from the Ashram. Your yarn is passable, and you should enlist
yourself as a member of the Spinners' Association without any
misgivings.
In the hot days, I can understand the takli giving you trouble.
It would then be best to do it at dawn when you can see the
thread clearly without artificial light at all. At that time there
1 Permanent address
is even in the driest climate a certain humidity which makes the
slivers more workable. And, at the time of doing the takli, if
you are doing it in quiet, you can recite all your Bhagavad Gita
or any other favourite shlokas.
Herewith receipt for Rs. 265-3- which they have sent me
from the Ashram.
I am glad you have decided to make khaddar wear and spin-
ning compulsory in the Vidyapith.
I hope you have not abandoned the idea of passing some
time at the Ashram.
Yours sincerely,
