114. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 10, 1930
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA,
I was very happy to read your letter. Why was the hand-
writing so poor? There are some ovis of Ramdas Swami on how
to acquire a good handwriting. I have sent a translation to the
Ashram. Ask Haribhai to send you a copy of it.
See that in the spinning competition one of the women wins.
How did you become ill? I thought that you were quite strong.
Get rid of your fever. How is your eye? I should be happy if
all of you immediately went to the Ashram. Chandan, Tara and
Vasant should also write to me. Kakasaheb sends his blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
