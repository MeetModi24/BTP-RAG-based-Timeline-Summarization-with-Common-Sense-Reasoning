254. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
April 14, 1930
CHI. KUSUM,
I have written about the anti-drink campaign and boycott of
foreign cloth. Do you think you can do anything in the matter?
Do you have the courage to take a leading part in that work?
I got your letters.
What are you doing there?
They told me yesterday that there was definite information
that my arrest was imminent and kept me awake the whole night.
I am still waiting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1797
