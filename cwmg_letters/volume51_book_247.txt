247. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
September 30, 1932
MY DEAR BHRRR,
Your faith was indeed great and events have justified it. It
was so vital that you needed no effort to restrain yourselves from
running to me as so many friends did. Yes, the prophecy or pre-
sentiment of Mrs. Abbas has come true. My warm congratulations
to her.
I am flourishing on milk and fruit.
Love from us all.
Yours,
BHRRR
From a photostat: S.N. 9580
