200. LETTER TO MOHAMMED HUSAIN KHAN
HARIJAN COLONY,
July 17, 1947
BHAI SAHEB,
I have your letter. What need for me to argue? Enclosed is a
copy of the Prime Minister's letter. In view of the letter I can only
say that you may file a suit against him. I cannot pass a judgment.
I have done whatever I could.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
