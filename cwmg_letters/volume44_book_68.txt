68. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 26, 1930
CHI. MANUDI,
I have your letter. Even if, as you say, you went away of
your own accord, I don't mind. I only wish that you should
become a real worker and that your body should become strong.
In future write in ink.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1503. Courtesy: Manubehn
Mashruwala
