503. LETTER TO PRINCESS EFY ARISTARCHI
October 28, 1932
I have almost regained my normal health and am pursuing
my normal activities without much strain being felt. Please there-
fore entertain no anxiety about me. What the future has in store
one does not know, and one is not permitted to pry into it. God
will take care of the future if we would mind the present.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 184
