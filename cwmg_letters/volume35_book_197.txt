197. LETTER TO ASHRAM CHILDREN
November 21, 1927
The natural beauty of Kandy is so great that one would simply
gaze for ever. There are hills, trees and greenery all round and
nowhere does one see a dry spot. I very much enjoyed walking in
solitude in such a place. Kakasaheb was talking to me on some
matter. I was listening to him with my ears, but the eyes were
engrossed in looking on God's play. I wonder why, with such
temples in existence, men spend lakhs and crores of rupees in erect-
ing big temples so that people may meditate in them on God.
How far has the existence of temples helped religion? Think on
this question and let me know your conclusion.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
