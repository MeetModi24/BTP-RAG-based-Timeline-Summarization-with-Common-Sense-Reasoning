10. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
June 2, 1936
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
The enclosed letter is for your information. I have written
to him that he should place his suggestion before you and before
Bapa if the latter happens to go to Bharuch.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
We shall be here till the 13th.
This letter certainly does not mean that you are to accom-
modate him. I do not even know him. This responsibility is
yours entirely. The reply to Jagjivandas amounts only to this.
