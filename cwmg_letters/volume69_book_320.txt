320. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
RAJKOT,
May 29, 1939
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter arrived today after unexpected absence. But I
was not worrying. There is enough here to occupy me. Heat is
your enemy. Stenches and crowds add to the difficulty. From
your telegram I infer that you are safely in Hazara. I hope it
is cooler or rather less hot there. I expect to get away on 6th
from Bombay for the Frontier Province. But God disposes.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6442. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10037
