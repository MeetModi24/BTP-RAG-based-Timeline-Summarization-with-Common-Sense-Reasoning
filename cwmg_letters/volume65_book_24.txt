24. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
March 22, 1937
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
Parameshwari Prasad says that he is prepared to hand
over the possession of the farm, etc., even today. He will sign
the necessary documents and he can leave the farm four or five
days later.
M. K. GANDHI
From Hindi: C.W. 8029. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
