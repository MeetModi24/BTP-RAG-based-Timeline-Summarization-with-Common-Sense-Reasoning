241. LETTER TO SECRETARY, HOME DEPARTMENT,
GOVERNMENT OF BOMBAY
'DILKHUSHA', PANCHGANI,
July 9, 1944
SECRETARY TO THE GOVERNMENT OF BOMBAY (H.D.)
POONA
SIR,
I have received your letter of 7th instant in connection with
the ground in H.H. the Aga Khan's Palace where Shri Mahadev
Desai's and Smt. Kasturba Gandhi's bodies were cremated. My
purpose is served by the present arrangement for which I thank
the Government.
Yours, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 3939
