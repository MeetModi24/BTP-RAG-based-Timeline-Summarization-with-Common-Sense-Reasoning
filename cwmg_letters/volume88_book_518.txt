518. LETTER TO SYED MINHAJUL HASAN
NEW DELHI,
July 30, 1947
BHAI HASAN 1 ,
I have had a talk with Shuklaji. I find that you have lost
the confidence of the others. Under the circumstances you had
better resign from the Ministry without rancour and devote your-
self to whatever other public service you can take up. This should
not cause resentment. A ministry can work only with a homo-
geneous team. This is only my advice. Do as your inner voice
prompts.
Blessings from
BAPU
