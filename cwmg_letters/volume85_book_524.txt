524. LETTER TO POTTI SRIRAMULU
NEW DELHI,
October 2, 1946
CHI. RAMULU,
Now all your letters to me are in English. It is not good.
Do not go about with a placard hung round your neck. The
thoughts confined to the placard are valueless.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 107
