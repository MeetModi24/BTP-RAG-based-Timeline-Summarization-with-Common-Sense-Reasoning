494. LETTER TO MRS. SHUKLA
September 25, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
Chi. Nirmala had come to see me. She told me that you
had had a fall and were bed-ridden. I was sorry to learn this.
You must be older than 1. Get someone to write to me about
your health.
Pranams from
MOHANDAS
MRS. SHUKLA
BARRISTER SHUKLA'S BUNGALOW
RAJKOT
KATHIAWAR
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
