490. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
ON WAY TO POONA,
September 24, 1945
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I hope you are all right. I have had talks with Prafulla.
I may reach there on 2nd November. I shall be having a talk
with Sarat Babu. He is coming to Poona. I hope all of you
are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
