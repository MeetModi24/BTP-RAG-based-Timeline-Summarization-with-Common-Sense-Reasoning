333. LETTER TO N. G. RANGA
SODEPUR,
December 18, 1945
BHAI RANGA,
I have gone through your letter. I will read your pamphlet.
You are doing a lot of work.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
PROF. RANGA
NIDUBROLU
