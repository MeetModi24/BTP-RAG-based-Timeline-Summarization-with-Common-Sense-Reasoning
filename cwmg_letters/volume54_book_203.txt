203. LETTER TO N.
March 24, 1933
It is not enough that you will strive. It is necessary that
you have the strength. Striving is enough for God. But the
promise is that real striving generates always enough strength.
Therefore I shall judge you in accordance with the actual result
you show. Have I made myself clear? You have a fearful struggle
in front of you to overcome the wretched past. But if truth
really possesses you, there is nothing to fear. Light dispels the
deepest darkness. Truth conquers the blackest sin which may be
otherwise rendered as untruth. I want you therefore to be on
the watch-tower.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy:
Narayan
Desai
