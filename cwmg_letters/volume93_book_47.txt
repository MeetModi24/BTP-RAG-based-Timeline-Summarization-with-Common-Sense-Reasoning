47. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
WARDHA,
June 12, 1935
CHI. SUMANGAL,
I have your letter. We shall never own defeat, even if we
forfeit life itself.
I have gone through the diary. I have pointed out the
remedy. The diagnosis is accurate. If possible refrain from think-
ing too much and take up some work of service. It could be
the service of your mother or of the children of Rajgarh. I am
surprised at whatever Prabhavati wrote to you about Kanta.
You did well to stop her and to have restrained your own
curiosity.
You must cling to Ramanama.
Blessings from
BAPU
