202. LETTER TO RASIKLAL U. MEHTA
SEVAGRAM,
February 9, 1945
CHI. RASIKLAL,
Go to S. A. and do as much public service as you can. You
should not hanker after money.
Blessings from
BAPU
RASIKLAL UMIYASHANKAR MEHTA
G/20 SIKKANAGAR
VITHALBHAI PATEL ROAD
BOMBAY 4
