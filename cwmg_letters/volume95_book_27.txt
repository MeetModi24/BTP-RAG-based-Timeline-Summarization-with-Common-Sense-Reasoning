27. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
LAHORE,
Friday [On or before January 30, 1920]
RESPECTED ANASUYABEHN,
For the moment I am not too crowded here. I feel like writing
to you about swadeshi. Are you paying attention to it at all? It is a
stupendous work. It is fraught with difficulties but unless it is taken
in hand there is no chance for the country to overcome starvation.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 11572
