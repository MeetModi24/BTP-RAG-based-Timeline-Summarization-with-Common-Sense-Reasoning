560. LETTER TO BAL KALELKAR
April 9, 1935
Like you, I, too, am preparing for an examination. You will
appear for your examination without leaving that place, and will
have only three or four examiners to satisfy. But I shall have to
go as far as Indore for my examination, and as for the number
of examiners, the very thought of it frightens me. . . . Let us
see what happens.
Has not Kaka also to appear for an examination? That means
that all three of us are busy.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
