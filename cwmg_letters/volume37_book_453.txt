453. LETTER TO HORACE HOLBY
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 26, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter sending me the magazine World Unity.
I have instructed the Manager to put you on the exchange list.
You are certainly at liberty to copy whatever you like from
Young India, and if I find something in your magazine I might
consider to be of value for the readers of Young India, I shall take
the copy with due acknowledgment.
Yours sincerely,
HORACE HOLBY, ESQ.
EDITOR, "WORLD UNITY"
4 EAST 12TH STREET, NEW YORK
Copy to the Manager, Young India, for necessary action.
From a microfilm: S.N. 14397
