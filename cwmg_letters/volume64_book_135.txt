135. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
SEGAON,
December 7, 1936
CHI. RADHAKISAN2,
I do not at all like this fever. Neither you nor Anasuya
should fall ill. You may continue with the baths even when you
have fever and the water should be cold, not hot.
I have also
recommended a mud-pack.
Come and let me explain if you have
no fever tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9122
