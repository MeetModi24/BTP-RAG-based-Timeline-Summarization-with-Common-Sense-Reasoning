327. LETTER TO AYLMER MAUDE
[LONDON,]
October 29, 1909
DEAR SIR,
I wrote to you last week in reply to your kind note, and as I am
anxiously awaiting an appointment from you, I take the liberty of
reminding you again. I wonder if my letter has miscarried.
Among the things appertaining to passive resistance that I wish
to discuss with you, I would appreciate your advice as to whom I should
approach for the publication of Tolstoy's "Letter to a Hindoo" which,
I presume, you read at Tolstoy's when you were with him in Russia last
month. I offered it to the Daily News for publication, but Mr. Gardiner
has sent a message that it is too long for his columns.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
AYLMER MAUDE ESQ.
GREAT BADDOW
CHELMSFORD
