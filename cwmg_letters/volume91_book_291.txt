291. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
SABARMATI,
December 11, 1921
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. If you are referring to the
amendments suggested by Mr. Jayakar they are in my opinion
destructive of the vital parts of our programme. To return to
Government schools and law-courts is to rob non-violence of half
its charm. But your letter leads one to hope that you are coming.
In that event you will press your views. I would gladly agree to
any amendment which may not destroy or mutilate the programme.
I wrote to you yesterday.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C. Vijayaraghavachariar Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
