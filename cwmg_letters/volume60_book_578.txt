578. LETTER TO AVADHESH DUTT AVASTHI
April 12, 1935
CHI. AVADHESH,
Your letter is good. So be it. You will not be on a salary
but I shall give you the fare when you happen to leave. Rid
yourself of anger and acquire humility.
No one is high or low
here, all are alike. There is no master and no servant. We are
all servants. We are not ashamed to do any kind of work.
Blessings from
BAPU
