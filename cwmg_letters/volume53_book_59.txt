59. LETTER TO RATILAL K. SHAH
January 15, 1933
BHAI RATILAL KUNVARJI,
I got your letter. You have given very important information.
But we find the same thing in regard to many temples. Hoping
that what happens occasionally will by and by happen every day,
we should go on trying.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 92
