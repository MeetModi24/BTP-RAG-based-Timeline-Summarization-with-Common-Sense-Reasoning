226. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
June 13, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
Your letter brought tears to my eyes. God will certainly bless
service so conscientiously and selflessly rendered.
I would have joyously taken you both to Quetta if I could
have gone. But it was not to be.
Yes, you will convince me of the necessity of removing the
Khadi Bhandar to the Mall.
I can't write much as I am writing with the left hand. The
right needs rest.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3538. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6347
