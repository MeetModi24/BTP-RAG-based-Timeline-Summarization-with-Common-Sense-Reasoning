106. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
JUHU,
May 14, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
I had hoped to see you in Bombay.
Now that is over.
You must build up your health.
You should live on butter-
milk, milk, rice if you can digest it, and juicy fruits like mosambis.
You need no medicine.
Mirabehn will write about my health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7080. Also C.W. 4572. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
