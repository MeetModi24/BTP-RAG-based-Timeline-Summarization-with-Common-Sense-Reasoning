449. LETTER TO J. S. HOYLAND
October 20, 1932
MY DEAR HOYLAND,
Your letter is among the treasures I have received on the
fast. It is a matter of great joy to me that if some have misunder-
stood the fast, friends like you have had no difficulty in appreciat-
ing it. And if one may judge an act by its results, it has proved
itself to have been from God.
Love to you all from us all.
BAPU
From a photostat of the original: C.W. 4507. Courtesy: Woodbrooke
College, Birmingham and Mrs. Jessie Hoyland
