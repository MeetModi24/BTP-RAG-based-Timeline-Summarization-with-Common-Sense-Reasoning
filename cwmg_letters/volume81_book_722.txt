722. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
October 21, 1945
CHI. MANILAL,
I got your joint letter.
Everyone is concentrating on Arun. Let me see what happens.
I am writing this at night. I have asked him also to write.
We have had rains here recently.
I have told you that my work would always go on. Sushila
is a very good worker. She has taken over all Rajkumari's
work. Kanaiyo is still here. He quickly disposes of all the
lengthy Gujarati letters and the other special work which I entrust
to him. No work here, therefore, has stopped or presents any
difficulty. I take proper sleep and rest. Do not, therefore, worry
about me.
Pyarelal's fever has come down today. Perhaps it will touch
normal now. Sushila looks after him, but he is being given
nature-cure treatment.
Arun is giving no trouble to anyone. He remains happy.
Sumi has reached Nagpur.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4961
