415. LETTER TO CHAMPABEHN R. MEHTA
August 21, 1932
CHI. CHAMPA,
I hope you received my postcard. Do not worry about any-
thing at all. God will solve all problems. Prabhashankar has sent
me a copy of the letter which you wrote to him. But as I ex-
plained to you in a previous letter, unfortunately for you, he and
I cannot agree. I will go on acting and advising you as seems best
to me. May God give you patience.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8753. Also C.W. 1041. Courtesy:
Champabehn R. Mehta
