97. LETTER TO MANSHANKAR TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 4, 1930
CHI. MANU,
Both of us liked your letter. You do everything with determi-
nation and you will, therefore, do well whatever you attempt. Don't
be eager to do anything beyond your strength. When Shankar
is released, tell him that I expect a detailed letter from him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7759
