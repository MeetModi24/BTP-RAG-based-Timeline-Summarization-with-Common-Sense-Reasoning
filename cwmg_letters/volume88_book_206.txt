206. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
June 30, 1947
CHI. AMRIT,
Better a p. c. than no letter. Sushila is just off to Wardha
to attend to her hospital work. Nothing certain about me.
Hope you are better. It is not so oppressive here as when you
left. Love to you all.
BAPU
[PS.]
When this was going to be posted, your letter was received.
How funny. My movement still uncertain.
From the original: C. W. 4194. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7830
