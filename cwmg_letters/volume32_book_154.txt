154. LETTER TO HARDAYAL NAG
[December 17, 1926]
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter which I was delighted to receive. I do not
know what I shall be doing at Gauhati. I shall act as the Spirit
leads me.
Charkha is surely not dying in Bengal. It may be in Chand-
pur. The output of khaddar in Bengal is steadily increasing and
so are the sales.
If you are not keeping very good health, I certainly do not
think it is worth while troubling [you] to come to Gauhati. What-
ever I do there will undoubtedly be known to the public. But I
have advised all non-co-operators not to trouble about coming
to Gauhati with the expectation of any fireworks display or even
the expectation of any non-co-operation programme being discus-
sed, apart from khaddar.
Forward, 24-12-1926
