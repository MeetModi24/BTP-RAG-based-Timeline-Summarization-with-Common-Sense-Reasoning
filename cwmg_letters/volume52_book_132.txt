132. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
November 30, 1932
CHI. MANIBEHN,
I enclose Dr. Kanuga's letter received today. You will see
from it that there is no cause for anxiety about Dahyabhai. The
fever may persist for a few days more but that does not matter.
We three are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro‒4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 95
