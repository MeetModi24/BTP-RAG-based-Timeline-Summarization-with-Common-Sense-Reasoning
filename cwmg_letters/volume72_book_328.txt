328. LETTER TO VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
July 15, 1940
BHAI VALLABHRAM,
I understand. If your needs were within my means to meet,
I would give you a place at Sevagram. I should like you to settle
in a village and teach Ayurveda. But I know it will be difficult.
It will be easy in Bombay. If you feel that my plan is worth
considering, come over. You can come any day except Monday,
Tuesday and Wednesday. I understand about Shankerlal. Also
about Valjibhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
VAIDYARAJ VALLABHRAM
DHANVANTARI AYURVEDA HOSPITAL
151 PRINCESS STREET, BOMBAY 2
From Gujarati: C.W. 2911. Courtesy: Vallabhram Vaidya
