102. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
October 14, 1933
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
I have your telegram to which I have sent you a reply as
follows:
Your wire. Hope Gurudev will be soon restored. My love. Mohan.
Hales wrote to me suggesting that I should at once proceed
to Midnapore and I understand that he has published the letter
even without waiting for my reply. Here is a copy of my reply
to him for your information.
You know that I am taking all the rest that the doctors have
advised and not moving out till the 7th of November. My weight
has steadily gone forward and blood-pressure has steadily de-
creased.
I have told the C.U. Press I have no objection to their
publishing the passage on ahimsa.
Love.
MOHAN
Encl. 2
REV. C. F. ANDREWS
SANTINIKETAN
(BENGAL)
From a photostat: G.N. 3797
