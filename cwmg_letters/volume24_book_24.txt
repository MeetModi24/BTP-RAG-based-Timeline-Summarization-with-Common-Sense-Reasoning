24. LETTER TO SIR PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
ANDHERI POST,
Vaisakha Sud 10 [May 14, 1924]
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I have your letter. I cannot act in regard to the Akalis in
the way you think. The rule that even a mother does not
serve food to her child unless it asks for it applies to all my acti-
vities. If it is the will of God, He will use me as an instrument.
He holds the threads in His hands. I am but one of the threads.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 3178. Courtesy: Mahesh Pattani
