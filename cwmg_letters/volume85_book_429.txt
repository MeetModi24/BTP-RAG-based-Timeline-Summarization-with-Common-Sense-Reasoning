429. LETTER TO ABDUL HAQ
NEW DELHI,
September 19, 1946
BHAI SAHEB,
I got your letter. I could go through it only today. I
have no knowledge of what you write about. If you kindly
send me the minutes of the proceedings, I shall be able to
understand and also to suggest what ought to be done.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
MAULVI ABDUL HAQ
DELHI
