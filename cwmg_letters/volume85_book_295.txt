295. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
NEW DELHI,
September 3, 1946
CHI. BHANSALI,
Guide Pushpa as you think fit. Let us hope that she be-
comes a sincere and dedicated worker. Sushilabehn has written
suggesting that she should become a nurse. Discuss the matter
with her also, and then guide Pushpa as you think best. I shall
approve of either idea. Pushpa alone can say what would in-
terest her more. It is difficult to say whether she knows what
is best for her. That she is a simple girl we have observed ever
since she first arrived.
Please eat regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 9274
