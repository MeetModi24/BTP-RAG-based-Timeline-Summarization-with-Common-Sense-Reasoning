124. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 30, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is nothing from you today. Vasumatibehn is having a little
fever today. Chakrayya is down with fever at Madras. Lakshmipati's
letter is odd. He says that he is improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
