412. LETTER TO BULAKHIDAS
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 10, 1930
BHAI BULAKRIDAS,
I have your letter. It is only through sacrifices such as yours
and your wife's that the drink evil will end. I hope that your wife
has now fully recovered. Give my compliments and blessings to
her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3138
