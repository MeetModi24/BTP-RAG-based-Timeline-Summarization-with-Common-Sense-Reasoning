335. LETTER TO LILAVATI SAVARDEKAR
AHMEDABAD,
August 16, 1931
DEAR SISTER,
This is my message:
Whilst it is true that man must shed his vice for the sake of his
fallen sisters I am quite certain that the evil will be eradicated
only when some sister from amongst them rises in revolt against
the evil and with the fire of her own purity burns the evil in the
others.
Yours sincerely,
SM. LILAVATI SAVARDEKAR
From a photostat: S.N. 17482; also Bombay Chronicle, 29-8-1931
