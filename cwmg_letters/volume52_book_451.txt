451. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
January 1, 1933
CHI. SHARDA,
Nobody need take any food which does not suit him.
It does not matter if you have taken up study of English.
If you have in your hand the key of grammar, you can learn it
well by yourself.
Don't be lazy about writing to me.
BAPU
