608. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
October 16, 1946
CHI. BABUDI (SHARDA),
I have your letter. Your weight will increase if you eat
properly. Shakaribehn should try hip-bath and friction-bath.
She should put a cold mud pack on the part of the head
affected by migraine. I am held up here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C. W. 100671. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokhawala
