471. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJI
Silence Day [On or before October 29, 1928]
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I have your letter. I was disappointed with the draft for an
organization to promote swadeshi. I smelt in it merely a com-
mercial spirit. From one point of view, however, what is happen-
ing is good. The draft shows the mill-owners as they are, and what
can be better than that we should seem what we are?
I should certainly like you to keep yourselves away from this
move, if you can. I am surprised to know that Shri Jaisukhlal
is associating himself with it. You may, if you wish, show this
letter to him and to anyone else who is likely to appreciate my
views.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4706. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarji
