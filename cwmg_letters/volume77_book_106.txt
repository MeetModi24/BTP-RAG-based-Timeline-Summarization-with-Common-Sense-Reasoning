106. LETTER TO R. R. DIWAKAR
May 18, 1944
Your letter is very good. You are right and so am I, each in
his own way. Therefore you should do only what appeals to
your heart and head. I have learnt now not to blame anybody
for his action so long as that action has come from the heart as
has yours and many other co-workers'. You know my view.
Weigh it and act according to your light. This ought to satisfy
you. Don't expect orders from me especially while I am on a
sick-bed.
Love.
BAPU
From a facsimile: Mahatma-Life of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, Vol. VI,
between pp. 328 and 329
