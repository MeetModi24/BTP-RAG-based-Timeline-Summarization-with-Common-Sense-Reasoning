376. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS BIRLA
SEVAGRAM,
November 10, 1944
BHAI RAMESHWARDAS,
I am sending Chi. Sankaran for six months to learn the
science of village sanitation and Chi. Sharda for some homoeopa-
thic treatment. Can I put up these two in the dharmshala in
your charge? If Sharda is accommodated her husband will also
stay with her. Write to me without hesitation what the position
is. Ghanshyamdas left today. It was good he came. A lot of
work could be done.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Sankaran is leaving today. He will stay with Raihanabehn .
But he can do so only for a few days as there is not enough
room.
R. BIRLA
BOMBAY
