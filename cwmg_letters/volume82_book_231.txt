231. LETTER TO MADALASA
SODEPUR,
December 6, 1945
CHI. MADALASA,
You want no reply, but I do wish to reply. You have had
fever again. I do not like it at all. Make a practice of lying in
the sun. You may, if you like, increase the duration gradually.
Cover up the body to start with and, as you feel warmer,
remove the covering gradually till finally you are stripped of all
clothing. This will not only cure the chest complaint but I am
sure make the whole body perfectly healthy.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 327
