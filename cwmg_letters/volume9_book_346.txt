346. LETTER TO H. JUST
LONDON,
November 10, 1909
DEAR MR. JUST1,
With reference to the official letter No. 34924/1909, may I trouble
you to send me a copy of my letter of 24th August last referred to in the
above mentioned letter. My clerk seems to have mislaid the carbon
copy.
I remain, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
H. JUST, ESQ.
COLONIAL OFFICE
DOWNING STREET
[LONDON]
Colonial Office Records: 291/142
