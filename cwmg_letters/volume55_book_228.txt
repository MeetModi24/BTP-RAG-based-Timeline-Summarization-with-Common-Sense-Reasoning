228. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
June 9, 1933
CHI. MANUDI,
I have heard that the operation for the removal of tonsils
was very painful. But I am sure that by the time you get this
letter you will be completely all right.
You must have got my
previous letter.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 1523. Courtesy: Manubehn Mashruwala
