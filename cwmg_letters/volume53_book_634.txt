634. LETTER TO DR. B. C. ROY
March 5, 1933
DEAR DR. BIDHAN,
I enclose herewith a letter from Sjt. Charu Chandra Mitra.
Will you please make what enquiry is possible and perhaps write
direct to Sjt. Charu Chandra Mitra, sending me a copy of your
reply, or, if you prefer it, send your reply directly to me? If
the reformers were in error, we should publicly apologize.
I hope that the Board is doing constructive work.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1 Letter
