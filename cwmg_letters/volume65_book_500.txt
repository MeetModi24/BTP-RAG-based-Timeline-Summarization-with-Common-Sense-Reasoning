500. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
July 20, 1937
CHI. KAKA,
I got your note only yesterday. It was undated. It must
have been written before you got my letter.
I am enclosing my opinion for Tandonji's benefit.
I have not yet been able to read today's article.
I have forgotten what it was about Sholapur. You may
reply at any rate. Let the thing take its own course. I can't
recollect anything.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7703
