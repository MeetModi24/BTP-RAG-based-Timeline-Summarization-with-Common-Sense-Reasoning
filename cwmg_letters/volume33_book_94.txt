94. LETTER TO HEMAPRABHA DEVI DAS GUPTA
Saturday [February 19, 1927]
DEAR SISTER,
What can I write to you? May your wisdom, your faith and
your devotion all come to your succour. Can Anil die? Anil is
with us for ever, only his body has been shed. Pray, do not lose
heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1661
