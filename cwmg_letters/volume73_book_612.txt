612. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
April 8, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Kanchan arrived this morning. She is quite happy. There is
no cause at all for worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MUNNALAL SHAH
KHADI KARYALAYA
MOOL P.O.
CHANDA DISTRICT
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8501. Also C.W. 7133. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
