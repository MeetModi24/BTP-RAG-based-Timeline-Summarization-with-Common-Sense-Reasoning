237. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[January 6, 1937]
CHI. MAHADEV,
Amtul Salaam is going there. She is leaving with us and
going to Bombay. These days I am inundated with news about
people's impurity. Send a telegram to Madgavkar as follows:
"Thanks. Reaching Tomorrow." Mention the time also. Send
a similar one to Premlilabehn. Then one to Dewan, Morvi:
"Hear Maharaja seriously ill. Hope he is recovering—Gan-
dhi."
More when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11561
