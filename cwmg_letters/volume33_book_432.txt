432. LETTER TO DR. M. S. KELKAR
NANDI HILLS,
June 3, 1927
DEAR DOCTOR,
Your letters are always interesting and instructive. Do I
gather that you would prefer my giving up wheat-meal or oat-
meal, salt, soda and vegetables altogether and confine myself to
milk, water and fruit? Am I right in thinking that you consider
raw milk better than boiled milk?
Yes, I well remember your talk about sterile eggs and their
culture. I shall enquire here myself; but if you have any literature
on the subject, I would like to study it. Without further con-
sideration I am prepared to say that I would put sterile eggs, as
you have described them to be, under the same category as milk.
I am therefore open to receive further instruction about them and
their culture.
You must some day try to convince me about the value of
astrology; for, I do not deny the possibility of its being a true
science. But I do not regard the investigation and use of every
science as beneficial to mankind.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 14145
