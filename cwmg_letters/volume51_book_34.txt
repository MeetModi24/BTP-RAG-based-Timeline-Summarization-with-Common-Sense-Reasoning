34. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
September 5, 1932
Your mention of that magnificent story of the Buddha brings
to my mind many sacred things. Yes, I do dream many dreams.
All may not be airy nothings. Or I would be crushed under the
weight of the love I appropriate from people of all sorts and
conditions-men, women, boys and girls.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 6
