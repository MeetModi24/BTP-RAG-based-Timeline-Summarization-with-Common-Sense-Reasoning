90. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
May 16, 1941
DEAR AMRITLAL,
I have your letter. You do not even read my letters fully.
I have told you that you are free to do what you like so long
as you do not act as my agent or in my name or under my instruc-
tions. I have no confidence in your judgment. But if you have and
if people want you, you would be wrong not to go. I have also
told you that I shall be no longer responsible for your expenses.
It is no use your returning to Sevagram. If no public body will
have you or pay for your keep, you should quietly settle down and
earn your livelihood and reconstruct your broken home. Please
read this carefully and act according to your own judgment.
Sailen and Dhiren I am trying to mould. It is a difficult
task. Please tell Vina and Abha I have their letters. They should
employ their time usefully. Why did Vina give two bangles to
Kanchan?
Yours,
BAPU
