439. LETTER TO BENODE PAUL
November 21, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
The duty of village workers will be to study the indus-
tries of the villages they cover and help the villagers to revive
or introduce such industries that would utilize their idle hours
and put a few coppers in their pockets.
Of course, part-time
workers will be quite welcome, and those who cannot contri-
bute by way of work will contribute cash wherever they can.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI BENODE PAUL
15 BRAJANATH MITTER LANE
CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
