305. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 23, 1940
BHAI SATIS BABU,
Why did you give money to Harilal and how much did you
give? It has done him no good. It was all spent on drinks.
What you write about Annada is right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2733
