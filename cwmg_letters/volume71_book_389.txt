389. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
April 8, 1940
MY DEAR KU[MARAPPA],
Dr. Mehta gave a bad report of your gross misbehaviour.
You must bring down your b.p.
It is suggested that you
should come and stay here for a few days and be under obser-
vation. Then during the hot season you should go up some-
where and rest. The brain requires more rest than the body.
You can come today, if you will.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10151
