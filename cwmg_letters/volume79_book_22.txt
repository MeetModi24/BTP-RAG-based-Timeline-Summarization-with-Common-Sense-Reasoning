22. LETTER TO PARAMANAND K. KAPADIA
[January 7, 1945]
This is just to give you my blessings. I shall not condole
with you. Father has cast off the body and become one with the
elements. What is there in it to grieve over?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PARAMANAND KUNVARJI KAPADIA
T. BHAVNAGAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 11589
