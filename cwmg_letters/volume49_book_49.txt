49. LETTER TO HARIPRASAD
January 24, 1932
CHI. HARIPRASAD,
God will reward you well for your devoted services to Ganga-
devi. But the Gita teaches us to perform our job or our service
without consideration of the result. Therefore, on your part ren-
dering of service is an end in itself and that is how it should
be. I hope you are keeping well. Give me your daily routine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2550
