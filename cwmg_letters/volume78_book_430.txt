430. LETTER TO OMKARNATH THAKUR
SEVAGRAM,
November 18, 1944
BHAI OMKARNATH 2 ,
Your letter reached me today. May your pious wishes bear
fruit. Your statement is very long. I shall go through it when
I have the leisure and write to you if I have anything to say.
Blessings from
BAPU
PANDIT OMKARNATH
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
