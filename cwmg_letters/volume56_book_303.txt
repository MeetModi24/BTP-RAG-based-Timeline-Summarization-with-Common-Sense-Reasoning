303. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
November 26, 1933
CHI. PREMA,
I got news about you from time to time from Sushila, and
also from some others. I am glad that you got my letter. Whether
you gained or lost, you will be able to judge only after you
are released. There is no doubt that the experience is invaluable.
I understood your time-table. I am very happy that you
have been able to preserve your health. The key was in your
own hands and I see that you have used it well.
What can I write about Harijan work? It is going on. I
have been experiencing people's boundless love. My body also
has been giving excellent service. The weight had risen to 110
lb. This is no ordinary improvement. Chandrashankar is trying
hard to fill Mahadev's place. Mirabehn also is here. And there
is Ramnath, whom you do not know. Besides, there is Janaki-
behn's Om. She is a brave girl and also has a fine intelligence.
God has kept her in excellent health, too.
I don't have time to write more now. I have to write many
other letters. It is only during the silence period that I can write
most of my letters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10352. Also C.W. 6791. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
