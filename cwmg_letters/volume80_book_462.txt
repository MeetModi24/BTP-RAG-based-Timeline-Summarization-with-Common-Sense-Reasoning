462. LETTER TO MOHAN KUMARAMANGALAM
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
MY DEAR MOHAN,
I have your cuttings. They are interesting and ably written.
I am doing what I can in the matter.
Please make no public use of this correspondence.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MOHAN KUMARAMANGALAM
RAJ BHAWAN
SANDHURST ROAD
BOMBAY-
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
