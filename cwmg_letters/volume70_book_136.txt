136. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
August 22, 1939
CHI. KISHORELAL,
I am writing to Lakshmidas. . . .2 Do not enter [any-
thing] concerning Jamnalal in the account books. Leave it for
the present.
As I have not been able to digest what you say about for-
giveness, try to convince me. If not now, whenever you have
the time. I shall understand even if you write a few words.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Gandhi Nidhi File.
