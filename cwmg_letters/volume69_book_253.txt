253. LETTER TO MIRZA ISMAIL
As AT RAJKOT,
May 8, 1939
DEAR FRIEND,
How I wish I could accept your very kind invitation. But
Rajkot still holds me. And as soon as I finish Rajkot, I am
bound to go to the Frontier Province. You will therefore please
excuse me and plead for me before His Highness too.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 2181
