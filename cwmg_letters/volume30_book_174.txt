174. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
ASHRAM,
Monday, March 22, 1926
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I had the telegram from Ghanshyamdas and you, and I have
sent a reply to Ghanshyamdas. Shankarlal is the cause of it all.
He is not very discreet. But let us say no more of it. You will
find me ready when you are; but after the 31 st.
Bhai Pyar Ali and Noorbano desire to spend the summer
wherever I do. It will do if they can have a separate cottage or
even a couple of rooms. They propose to live on their own. So
you may now do whatever you think proper.
I am in quite good health. Here it is not exactly summer
as yet; only today it may be said to be somewhat warm.
Blessings from
BAPU
