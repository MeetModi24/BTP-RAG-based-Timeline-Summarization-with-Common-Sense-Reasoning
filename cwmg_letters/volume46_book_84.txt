84. LETTER TO KISANSINH CHAVDA
BORSAD,
May 4, 1931
BHAI KISANSINH,
I got your letter. I read no new argument in it. All the
objections which you urge have been answered several times in
Young India and Navajivan. All that I can do now is to discuss
the question briefly with you if you come and see me some time.
I cannot spare the time for this before the month of June. I
would advise you meanwhile to think again over what I have
been writing.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9295. Courtesy: Kisansinh Chavda
