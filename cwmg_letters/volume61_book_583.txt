583. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
WARDHA,
September 12, 1935
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
How well you have joined Kamala! It is the best tonic for
her. I shall keep a note for her herewith. Your messages are
being duly received here. And Sarup repeats what she receives.
Let us hope all will end well. Please thank Dr. Atal for his
messages and letters which have been most helpful. I expect a
regular mail from you whilst the crisis lasts. Typed sheets
are with me. I shall go through them as soon as possible.
Mahadev had to go to Bombay to help Vallabhbhai about an
inquiry. And he is still there. Rajagopalachari has just dropped
in with Laxmi and her baby boy. Devdas was badly ill. Ansari
has packed him off to Simla. I have Mira on my hands prostrate
with bad fever.
I would like you to allow yourself to be elected President for
the next year. Your acceptance will solve many difficulties. If
you think fit, send me a wire.
Has Indu been fixed up?
Khurshed is here. She will be writing to you by the ordinary
mail.
Love from us all.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1935. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
