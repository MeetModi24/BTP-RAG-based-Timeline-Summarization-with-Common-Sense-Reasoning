202. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Ashadh Sud 15 [July 16, 1924]
Do not worry about my health. I have increased the quan-
tity of my food again. Who can understand my mind today? I
myself do not know where it will land me. The struggle is of course
going on. I do not insist on anything. I only try to make and keep
myself as pure as I can. I consider that to be my only duty. God
may then pour into it anything He likes. Young India and Nava-
jivan mirror it for the most part.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 73
