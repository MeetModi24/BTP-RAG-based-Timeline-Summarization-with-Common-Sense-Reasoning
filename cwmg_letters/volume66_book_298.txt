298. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 16, 1937
CHI. AMLA,
Khurshedbehn tells me you were complaining of absence of
letters from me. You have never been out of my mind. I
have not written because I thought you did not need to hear
from me. I knew that you were getting on quite well. But
do tell me all about you. Here things are much the same as
you left them. Do drop in some time, if you can. Remember
me to your mother.
Love.
Blessings from
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
