24. LETTER TO BENARSIDAS CHATURVEDI
April 28, 1935
BHAI BENARSIDAS.
I had your letter. Even if we do form a committee, who will
do the actual work? We shall deliberate in the standing committee.
Will you attend it?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI BENARSIDAS CHATURVEDI
120/ UPPER CIRCULAR ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2568
