207. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI PATEL
November 18, 1929
BHAI SHIVABHAI,
I have your letter. It is the assurance of the Gita, and is
proved in experience, that anyone who strives sincerely for self-
control is bound to succeed in his effort. Of course such striving
requires patience. More when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
