163. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
November 17, 1941
CHI. LILI,
I have your letter. I like my own marks. But if you study
regularly you are sure to pass. Do not worry, but go on studying
regularly and with confidence. The result is in God's hands.
Take care of your health. Everything is all right here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9596. Also C.W. 6568. Cour-
tesy: Lilavati Asar
