472. LETTER TO D. NARAYANA RAJU
January 3, 1933
MY DEAR NARAYANA RAJU,
I have your letter. I am quite sure that Lakshmamma should
not fast in connection with the opening of the temple you men-
tion. I have already wired to her.
1 For Gandhiji's message, vide p. 232.
2 Vide p. 249.
3 This sentence is in Gandhiji's hand. For the telegram, vide p. 347.

Whilst Guruvayur is pending, only propaganda should be
carried on. Fasting is a method applicable only in rare cases.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. D. NARAYANA RAJU
