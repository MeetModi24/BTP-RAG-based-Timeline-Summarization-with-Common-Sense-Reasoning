508. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM BAVISHI
April 2, 1935
BHAI PURUSHOTTAM,
I wrote to you on 28th March asking you to come to In-
dore. But I see from your letter of 31st March that you had not
received my letter up to the time of your writing. Maybe I wrote
Shampur instead of Shajipur in the address.
Please do both of you come to Indore. I cannot fix the time
from here. But we shall certainly talk things over.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 123. Also C.W. 4746. Courtesy:
Purushottam Bavishi
