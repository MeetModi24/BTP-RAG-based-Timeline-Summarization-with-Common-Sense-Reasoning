336. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
SEVAGRAM,
July 16, 1940
BHAI MUNSHI,
I had your letter. I solved your problem yesterday itself. I
have nothing to say now. Two courses are open, and either will
be right if it is within your strength. "One's own dharma though
devoid of merit is better."
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7654. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
