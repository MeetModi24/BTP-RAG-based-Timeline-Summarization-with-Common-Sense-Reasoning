231. LETTER TO SECRETARY, NATIONAL LIBERAL CLUB
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 21, 1906
THE SECRETARY
NATIONAL LIBERAL CLUB
WHITEHALL, S. W.
DEAR SIR,
I shall thank you to redirect to the above address the letter lying
for me at the Club.
Yours faithfully,
