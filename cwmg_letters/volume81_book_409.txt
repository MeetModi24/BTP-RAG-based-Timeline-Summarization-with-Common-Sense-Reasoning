409. LETTER TO KAILAS D. MASTER
September 8, 1945
CHI. KAILAS,
I have not been able to write to you. Since you have settled
down I am no longer worried. As I have a lot of work, I write
only to those to whom I must. Why are you still so restless?
Do good service and earn merit. Teach others such as Hoshiari-
behn. Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
