507. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday [July 16, 1927]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. You have done well in writing to
Dr. Chandulal. Your stomachache has got to go.
I am of course well. I suffered from migraine for a few
days. It was cured by application of mud-packs. If Taramati
has not yet written me a letter, tell her that I am waiting.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
HOMI VILLA
PANCHGANI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
