477. LETTER TO M. E. C. MATHEW
CAMP, SODEPUR,
January 5, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I do not know where I shall
be at the time you reach India. However, I reciprocate your
wish that we should meet. You will have no difficulty in find-
ing my whereabouts at the time you come to India.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
M. E. C. MATHEW, ESQ.
182 SEA BOURNE ROAD
SOUTH BOURNE WEST
BOURNEMOUTH, HANTS, ENGLAND
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
