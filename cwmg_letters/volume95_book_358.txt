358. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Silence day
CHI. VASUMATI,
I could not find time even to talk a little this time. Could there
be anything to talk? I saw that you have not yet gone to the new room.
After realizing that I have permitted you unhesitatingly, you may
continue to stay where you are. You must make full use of the Jodani
Kosh while writing Gujarati.
I hope now there are no noises at night.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9373
