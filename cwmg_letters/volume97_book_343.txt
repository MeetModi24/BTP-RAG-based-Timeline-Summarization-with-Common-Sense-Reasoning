343. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
January 23, 1945
CHI. RAMDAS,
I got your letter. I am opening the Superintendent's letter. We will now
see what Sumi wishes. A letter from her is due. I have no trace of cough at
all. I cannot say, either, how weak I am. I observe silence the whole day just
to save time. And it agrees with me. I speak at night.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
