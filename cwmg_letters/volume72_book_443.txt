443. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
August 17, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You are right. You should yourself do whatever you can. But
if some difficulty crops up that must be got over, do consult me.
Ba's problem is perennial. It has got to be put up with. Keep
on reporting to me. I shall do whatever I can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4358
