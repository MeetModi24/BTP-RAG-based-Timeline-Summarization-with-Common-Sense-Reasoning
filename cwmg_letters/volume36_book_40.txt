40. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
Tuesday [February 21, 1928]
BHAI RAMESHWARDASJI,
Your letter. Certainly, do leave the boys at the Wardha
school. They will be well looked after in the Ashram. Be at peace
by imprinting Ramanama in your heart.
I am keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 194
