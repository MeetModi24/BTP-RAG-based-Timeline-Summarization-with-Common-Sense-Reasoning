312. LETTER TO BUDREA
[LONDON,]
October 18, 1909
DEAR SIR,
With reference to your letter of the 8th inst., the deposit receipt was
on the 12th inst. forwarded by me to Mr. Maganlal Gandhi with a
request to obtain your signature thereto, as I thought that you were in
Durban. I have now written to him asking him to forward it to you.
Immediately upon receipt thereof duly completed by you, I shall re-depo-
sit it as requested.
Yours faithfully,
MR. BUDREA
C/O
GURDEEN AHEER
DANNHAUSER
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5133
