143. LETTER TO G. S. NARASIMHACHARI
October 4, 1934
DEAR NARASIMHACHARI,
Here is my message:
"Students can easily spin in the name of and for the sake
of Daridranarayana for half an hour daily without any interference
with their studies and can add something, be it ever so little,
to the wealth of the country and establish a living connection
with millions of their countrymen who are strangers to education
and who never know from year's end to year's end what a full
meal is."
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI G. S. NARASIMHACHARI
C/O VICE-CHANCELLOR, ANDHRA UNIVERSITY
WALTAIR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
