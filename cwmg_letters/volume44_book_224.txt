224. LETTER TO AMINA QURESHI
September 21, 1930
CHI. AMINA,
I got your letter. Many kisses to Mian Abdul Majid. As
for Sultana, she would not even let me touch her. How is your
health now? Take proper care about food.
Have you kept up your study of Urdu?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6658
