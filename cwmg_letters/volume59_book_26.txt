26. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
September 19, 1934
BHAI THAKKAR BAPA,
I got your letter regarding Surajbehn. This whole episode
has been a painful one.
But I hope the pain will be forgotten
by and by. I am writing to Bhai Chitalia that Surajbehn may
remove the maternity home to his building and start a Seva
Mandir. After she has done so she should hand over the key to
Gokulbhai.
I understand about the money believed to be lost.
Give the accompanying letter to Lakshmi. Devdas has left
a fairly heavy burden on you.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 22757
