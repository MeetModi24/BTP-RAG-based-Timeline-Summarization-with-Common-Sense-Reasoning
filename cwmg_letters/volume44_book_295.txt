295. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 11, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. The postcard addressed to you at Patna
does not seem to have reached you, for I had asked you in it
to send me a wire but I did not get any. I now learn from the
letter that your mother-in-law has passed away and that both
Jayaprakash and you have been a good deal shaken. Why should
we ever grieve over death, which no one can escape? More-
44-14
over, the lady was quite ill. She has been released from her
suffering. Hence, those whom she has left behind suffer because
of their selfishness. Your duty now is to see that you get strong
quickly. I don't think your disease has yet left you. For some
time, you may also write directly to me. This in addition to the
letters which you should continue to send through the Ashram.
May God grant patience in suffering to you all. I keep good
health. The weight is 104. I think that is quite good.
Blessings from
BAPU
