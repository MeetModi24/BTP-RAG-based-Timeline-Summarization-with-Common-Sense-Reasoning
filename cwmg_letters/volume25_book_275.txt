275. LETTER TO COL. MEL
AS AT SABARMATI,
November 13, 1924
DEAR COL. MEL,
Mr. Pragji K. Desai whom I know well and who was with
me in South Africa is at present a prisoner in the Hyderabad Cen-
tral Jail. He was convicted some time ago at Surat under
section 124 A of the Indian Penal Code.
I am told that
1. Mr. Desai has lost in weight,
2. he is isolated from the rest of the prisoners and has
therefore no human contact save that of his keepers,
3. the greens he gets are said to be as a rule grassy and un-
eatable,
4. he has applied for permission to spin cotton but he is per-
mitted only to twist prepared yarn. If the authorities are not
prepared to supply Mr. Desai with slivers, I can arrange for
such supply if permitted.
Rather than publish the above information in the Press, I
am passing it on to you for kind inquiry. I may state that
Mr. Desai is a vegetarian and did not possess a robust constitu-
tion by any means when he was outside. In my opinion there-
fore he needs a light but nourishing diet such as milk and
baker's bread.
I am,
Yours truly,
From a photostat: S.N. 11721
