44. LETTER TO URMILA DEVI
SABARMATI JAIL,
March 13, 1922
MY DEAR SISTER,
You have neglected me entirely. But I know that you have
done so to save my time.
I want you to devote the whole of your time to nothing but
charkha and khaddar. It is the only visible symbol of peace, all-
India unity and our oneness with the masses including the so-
called untouchables.
Please show this to Basanti Devi and Deshbandhu. I hope
he is well and strong. Prisoners cannot afford to be ill.
You know, of course, that Shankerlal Banker is with me.
With love to you all.
SRIMATI URMILA DEVI
NARI KARMA MANDIR
CALCUTTA
Speeches and Writings of M. K. Gandhi, p. 742
