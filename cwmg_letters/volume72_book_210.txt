210. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
June 8, 1940
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have been forgetting to write about Abha. You should write
to Mahila Ashram and inform them that her expense will not
be borne from here. She is to be admitted as a freeship holder.
If the practice of exempting students from fees has been discon-
tinued, then it is a different matter. With whom does the de-
cision rest at present?
BAPU
[PS.]
I hope she will be allowed to wear the same clothes that she
does here.
