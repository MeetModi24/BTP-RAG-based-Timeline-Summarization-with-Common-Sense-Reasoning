190. LETTER TO VAKAYIL ACHUTHAN NAIR
January 25, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I must thank you for your having taken such trouble to fur-
nish me with the papers in connection with the Guruvayur case.
I have had them looked up. I now return them to you by registered
post. You need not now trouble to discover the missing papers.
I see that the information contained in the papers you were good
enough to send me is all that I wanted to get.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. VAKAYIL ACHUTHAN NAIR
GURUVAYUR
From a microfilm: S.N. 19005
