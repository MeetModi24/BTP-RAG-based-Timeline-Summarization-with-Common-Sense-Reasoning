40. LETTER TO MAHESH CHARAN
July 23, 1945
BHAI MAHESH CHARAN,
Sjt. Jajuji has talked to me about the matter you mention
in your letter. I am satisfied with what is being done.
Blessings from
BAPU
GANDHI ASHRAM
KHADI BHANDAR
32 LATOUCHE ROAD
LUCKNOW
