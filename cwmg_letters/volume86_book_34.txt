34. LETTER TO MAHESH DATT MISHRA
October 24, 1946
CHI. MAHESH,
I have gone through your whole letter. Give up your in-
sistence on accompanying me to Calcutta at present. If I feel,
after reaching there, that your coming may be good, I shall
send for you. Now go home and get ready for Harijan. Improve
your health.
I admit my mistake about Harijan; forget it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 6716. Also C. W. 4460. Courtesy:
Mahesh Datt Mishra
