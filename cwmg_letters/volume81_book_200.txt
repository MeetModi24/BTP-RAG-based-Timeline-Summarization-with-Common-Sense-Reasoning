200. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
August 14, 1945
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. I shall be leaving on the 19th. After
that my programme will be uncertain. As Sardar desires I could
spend three months with him and then go to Bengal, then to the
Frontier Province and then to Madras. That means I shall
have to be away till December. Under these circumstances you
can stay with me if you come at the end of December. But that
is the time when you are required to stay there.
My blessings to Akbar. I am all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
