628. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
Via WARDHA,
January 4, 1941
CHI. LILA,
I wrote you a postcard but it has no value in your eyes.
Have you read the poem "Try Again"? Even a hard rock is
worn by the friction of a rope. Are you not somewhat softer
than a rock? You have got to pass. It does not matter if it
takes a little time. Is not even that for my sake? This much
is written with your pen. The rest is in M. 's letter.
A birthday means one year less from the years assigned to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI LILAVATI UDESHI
KANJI KHETSI CHHATRALAYA
65 MINT ROAD
BOMBAY FORT
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
