81. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
November 23, 1936
MY DEAR C. R.,
Don't be angry. I have taken a liberty with your note on
the Travancore Act—a liberty I do not remember having ever
taken with your writings. The reasons for the amendments and
the addition you will easily understand and I would fain hope
as easily accept as valid. Anyway, I have felt the duty of
making the corrections for the sake of the cause.
Love.
BAPU
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
