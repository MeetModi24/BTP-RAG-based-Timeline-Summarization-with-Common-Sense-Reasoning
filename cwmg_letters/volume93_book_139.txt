139. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
October 3, 1936
CHI. LILA,
It seems you expect a letter from me every day since I
have got you into the habit. I never said that I would write
to you daily. It just happened that I did so because I wanted
to comfort you. Do you still need that support?
Blessings from
BAPU
