164. LETTER TO SHARAYU DHOTRE
November 24, 1945
CHI. SARAYU,
I am glad to have your letter. But I continue to have
doubts about the propriety of what you are doing. Anyway it will
be enough if you work for the well-being of the person whom
you consider your husband and do not have the slightest lustful
desire for anyone else. I shall be happy if you write to me
sometimes.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
