200. LETTER TO R. PIGGOTT AND A. M. WARD
POST ANDHERI,
March 22, 1924
DEAR MISSES PIGGOTT AND WARD,
I have your letter of the 16th instant.
I am ashamed to say that I have entirely forgotten the inter-
view mentioned by you. However, I am sending your letter to a
friend in Sindh who, if he can see his way, I am sure, will assist
you. You will please excuse me if I have not addressed you correct-
ly. I showed your letter to Mr. Andrews. He distinctly remembers
having met you, but cannot give me any information about you.
Yours sincerely,
MISS R. PIGGOTT AND MISS A. M. WARD
HYDERABAD (SINDH)
From a photostat: S.N. 8562; also C.W. 5140
