315. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
April 27, 1930
CHI. TARAMATI,
Mathuradas has informed me of your brother's death. The
Gita teaches us not to grieve over death. Birth and death are
our two friends and both are equal. Let us try each day to be
pure and alert in the knowledge that death is certain.
Blessings from
BAPU
