316. LETTER TO DIRECTOR, THE TIMES OF INDIA,
BOMBAY
SEGAON,
August 7, 1938
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for your letter of 28th ultimo and a copy of
The Story of the World in Pictures.
I do not consider myself in any way qualified to pronounce
an opinion on ventures like yours. I would therefore ask you to
excuse me for my inability to comply with your request.
Yours faithfully,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
