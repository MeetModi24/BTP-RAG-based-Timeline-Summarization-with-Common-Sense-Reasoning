444. LETTER TO A. KALESWARA RAO
July 12, 1937
MY DEAR KALESWARA RAO,
It is good of you to send me fruits without sending me bills.
I appreciate the thought underlying your gift. But you do make
it difficult for me to send regular calls. Anyway, please stop the
chikus. The majority contain maggots.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SRI A. KALESWARA RAO
BEZWADA
