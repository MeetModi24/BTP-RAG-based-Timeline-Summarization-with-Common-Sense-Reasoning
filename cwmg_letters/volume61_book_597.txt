597. LETTER TO G. V. MAVALANKAR
September 14, 1935
BHAI MAVALANKAR,
Our only consolation is the fact, isn't it, that others too have
like you to pass through the trials of a householder's life? But I
hope everything is all right now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1245
