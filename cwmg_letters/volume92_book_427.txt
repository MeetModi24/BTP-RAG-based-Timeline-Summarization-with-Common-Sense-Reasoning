427. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
DELHI,
February 28, 1931
CHI. KANTA,
I have not been able to write to you at all. It has troubled
me but I was helpless. Your health must now fully improve. I
can understand your being impatient to meet me. But I am
afraid we shall not be able to meet. Let us see what happens.
I have no time just now to dictate more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy:
Gandhi National Museum and Library
