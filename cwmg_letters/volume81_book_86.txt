86. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 30, 1945
BROTHER RAJEN BABU,
I had given a wire about Mahendra : "Your wire. Better see
Viceregal Secretary about Mahendra."
The new Government
cannot interfere with the Viceroy's decision all of a sudden but
there is some hope from Jenkins. Did our people not send any
appeal to London? Is the person not yet hanged? If he is alive
whatever you have to do in that respect do it here. To send
deputation to London is futile.
You must be feeling better.
From a copy: C.W. 10591
