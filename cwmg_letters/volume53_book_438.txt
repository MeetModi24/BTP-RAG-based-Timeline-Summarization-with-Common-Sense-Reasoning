438. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
February 17, 1933
CHI HEMPRABHA,
I have your letter. Do whatever khadi work you can but
with a carefree attitude even though the work prospers but a little.
If our faith is sustained to the end, khadi and Daridranarayana are
bound to emerge victorious.
When it becomes possible you can come here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1698
