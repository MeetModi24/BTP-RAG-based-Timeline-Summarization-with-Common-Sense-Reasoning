409. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
ABBOTTABAD,
July 14, 1939
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
I have your letter. I should say let well alone. Do the
best you can.
Yours,
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND, M. L. A.
PUNJAB
LAHORE
From the original: Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
