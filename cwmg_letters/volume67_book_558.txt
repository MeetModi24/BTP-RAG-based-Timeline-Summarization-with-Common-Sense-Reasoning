558. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
DELHI,
September 27, 1938
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter. It is good that at last [Jayaprakash] has
gone there. Please keep me informed about the treatment. Give
me details about the diet there. I hope the handwriting will not
378
get smudgy. Write to me about the estimated expense there, so
that I may arrange for the amount to be sent. I should like
you to bring Saraswati along with you when you return from
there. I will write to her.
I am leaving this place for the
Frontier Province on the 30th. Address your letters: c/o Dr.
Khan Saheb, Peshawar.
I am all right.
Amtul Salaam,
Mahadev, Pyarelal, Sushila are with me. Mahadev is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3522
