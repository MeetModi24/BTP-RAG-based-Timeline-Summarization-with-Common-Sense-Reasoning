177. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
February 5, 1945
CHI. CHAND,
This is foreign-certainly mill-made. Why should you have
it? You ought to have a notebook of hand-made paper. You can
keep this. You could use it for some other purpose or give it
away. Anyway, you should do only what you wish, not what I
suggest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: Chandrani Papers. Courtesy : Gandhi
National Museum and Library
