352. LETTER TO NARAYAN DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 25, 1930
SHRI NARAYANRAO,
Or should I address you as Bablo2? You have drawn beautiful
lines and curves indeed. But, instead of scribbling such marks,
learn to make every letter in a word as beautiful as a picture. I
believe you must be full of fun now because Mahadev has return-
ed. Don't send him back immediately.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9474
