248. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
CAMP: KHADI PRATISHTHAN,
SODEPUR (NEAR CALCUTTA),
December 8, 1945
DEAR MR. ABELL,
I thank you for yours of 1st instant. I shall send you in
due course fuller details about Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia and
have written to Shri Prabhu Dayal Vidyarthi to proceed to New
Delhi and report himself.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, pp. 63-
