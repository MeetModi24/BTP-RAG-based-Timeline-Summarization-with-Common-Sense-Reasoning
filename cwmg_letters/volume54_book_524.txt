524. LETTER TO G. V. MODAK
April 20, 1933
DEAR CAPT. MODAK,
I have your letter. Your book has been kept by the Superin-
tendent, for, in his opinion, it is not contemplated that such
books should be given to me. Unless therefore you get the permis-
sion from the Government, the book cannot be delivered to
me.
Yours sincerely,
CAPT. G. V. MODAK
C/O S. I. SOCIETY
POONA
From a microfilm: S.N. 21007
