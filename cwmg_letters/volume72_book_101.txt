101. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
SEVAGRAM,
May 21, 1940
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I have your letter. It is not yet time for you to go to Rajkot.
If you build up your physical strength there, you will have taken
one step forward.
Blessings from
BAPU
