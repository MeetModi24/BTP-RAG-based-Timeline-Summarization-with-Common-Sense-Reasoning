138. LETTER TO PEN HASSELROT
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter as also the sum mentioned in it by you.
I enclose herewith the form sent by you duly receipted. The
second volume of [The Story if] My Experiments with Truth may
be translated by you whenever you wish to upon the same terms
as the first.
Yours faithfully,
Encl. 1
PEN HASSELROT, ESQ.
POSTGIRO 675, STOCKHOLM 7
From a microfilm: S.N. 15652
