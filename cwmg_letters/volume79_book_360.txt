360. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
March 7, 1945
CHI. MAGAN 1 ,
Jekibehn says that the sisters must get a share of their mo-
ther's jewellery. Personally I should like that. I had a letter
from Mayashankar. I should like you to meet him and satisfy
him. He has served your father very faithfully. There is no news
about Ratilal . We may get some news if we announce a reward.
Most probably I shall go to Bombay at the beginning of April.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have just heard that Ratilal is in Ahmedabad, safe and
sound.
SHRI MAGANLAL PRANJIVANDAS
BARRISTOR
ANDHERI, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 1032. Courtesy: Manjula M.
Mehta
