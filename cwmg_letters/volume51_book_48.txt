48. LETTER TO MATHURI N. KHARE
September 10, 1932
CHI. MATHURI 1 ,
This time your handwriting was bad. There were quite a
few mistakes in the language too. You should try and avoid
such mistakes as far as you can. And one can always improve
one's handwriting if one takes sufficient care.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 268. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
