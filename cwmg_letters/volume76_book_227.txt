227. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 6, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
It seems that there is no more now of the other pads.
One rupee was paid to Nanavati before your letter came.
There is nothing wrong with Mahadev's spectacles. The
terrific heat is responsible for all the mischief. It will go some
day and the cool air will be all the more appreciated.
You will have to prove your proposition about the balances.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
I think I have told you about Sushila having passed.
From the original: C.W. 4129. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7438
