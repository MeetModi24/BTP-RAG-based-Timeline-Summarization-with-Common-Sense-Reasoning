303. LETTER TO NATINDRA NATH DAS
SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA,
November 1, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of the 25th ult. for which he desires me to
thank you.
You should seek guidance from Congress leaders. He is unable to
advise.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI NATINDRA NATH DAS
CONTAI P.O.
MIDNAPORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
