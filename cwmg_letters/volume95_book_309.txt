309. LETTER TO MANU H. GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday
CHI. MANU,
I have your letter. Improve your handwriting still further.
Your letter pleases Ba more than me. Take care of your health. Pay
attention to your studies. Do not make noise during prayers and in the
kitchen.
Ba is all right now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1501 Courtesy: Manu S. Mashruwala
