254. LETTER TO JUGAL KISHORE BIRLA
SRIRAMPUR,
December 7, 1946
BHAI JUGAL KISHORE,
Baba Raghavdas gave me a full account of the passing
away of Malaviyaji. He also mentioned your pledge. You
should therefore certainly set apart Rs. 25 lacs for the temple.
However, I am afraid, it will not be conducive to the progress
of Hinduism if Malaviyaji's concept of the temple complex is
translated literally. If the spirit of his concept is followed, it
would raise Hinduism to greater heights. Today Hinduism is
being compared with other religions. Under the circumstances,
if we followed [his words] literally Hinduism would perish, while
the spirit behind them will put new life into it. Baba Raghav-
das will tell you the rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
