212. LETTER TO ZAHID HUSSAIN
September 16, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. How wicked of you to fall ill when
every ounce of your energy is wanted at your post of duty.
May God restore you quickly.
As promised I made the appeal the very evening. But no-
thing had happened in response.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
ZAHID HUSSAIN SAHEB
CAMP: KARACHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
