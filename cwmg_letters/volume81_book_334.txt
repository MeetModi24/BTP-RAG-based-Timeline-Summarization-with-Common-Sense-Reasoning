334. LETTER TO PANNALAL
August 31, 1945
BHAI PANNALALJI,
I have gone through your letter carefully. I will see the
pamphlets when they arrive. Keep up your interest in the pro-
pagation of Hindustani.
The pamphlets have come.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. PANNALAL
19 THORNHILL ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
