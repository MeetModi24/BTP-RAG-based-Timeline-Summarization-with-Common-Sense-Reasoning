186. LETTER TO RAMKRISHNA BAJAJ
SEVAGRAM,
August 16, 1946
CHI. RAMKRISHNA,
You are going West. I do not quite see its benefits. But
when a strong wind is blowing who can remain unaffected?
Think what you will be taking from here and what you will be
bringing from there. The student days are for the development
of thought.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 3067
