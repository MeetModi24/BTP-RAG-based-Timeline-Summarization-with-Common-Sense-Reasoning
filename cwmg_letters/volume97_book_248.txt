248. LETTER TO KASTURBHAI LALBHAI
May 20, 1934
BHAI KASTURBHAI,
I have written to Chamanbhai. I hope that you will prevent implemen-
tation of the Mill-Owners' Association's resolution. I will not let the tradition
which has been followed for so many years be broken without sufficient
reason.
The insistence on paying Mr. Patankar his fee also seems to me to be
derogatory to our good name.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 33150
