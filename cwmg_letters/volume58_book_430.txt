430. LETTER TO H. N. KUNZRU
September 2, 1934
DEAR HARIJI,
I do remember our conversation at Chheoki and I remem-
ber also that I entirely approved of your suggestion. But the
matter was forgotten entirely, and had it not been for your
reminder, I would not have written to Thakkar Bapa. I am now
writing to him.
Yours sincerely,
PT. HRIDAY NATH KUNZRU
ALLAHABAD
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
