123. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
May 23, 1938
CHI. JAMNALAL,
There is a wire from Gosibehn. She has lost her mother. I
have sent a wire. You may send a wire or write a letter.
I hope Rajendra Babu is cheerful. Please let me know the
temperature if anybody is coming this way.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2991
1 This is in the form of a postscript to Mirabehn's letter to the addressee.
