379. LETTER TO ZOHRA ANSARI
September 21, 1936
DEAR DAUGHTER ZOHRA,
I cannot attend your wedding but I shall certainly be
with you in spirit. You will both have my blessings on that day.
May God grant you long life and happiness.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Urdu: Mahatma, Vol. IV, between pp. 112-
