127. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
Tuesday [November 27, 1928]
BHAISHRI CHHAGANLAL,
I don't have time today to write at length. I was very much
pained to read about the affair concerning . . .3 My pain is all
superficial, so it has not disturbed me in my work. All the same,
the thing fills me with anguish. I have written a letter to him.
Probably he will show it to you.
1 & 3 The names are omitted in the source.
2 Vide the preceding item.
More in the next letter.
You should not lose courage. Acquit yourself well in the post
you have accepted. May God help you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, pp. 64-
