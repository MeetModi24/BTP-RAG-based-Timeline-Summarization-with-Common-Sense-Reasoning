523. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
February 24, 1933
I had your beautiful letter. I prize your love. Of course
I was not angry with you and therefore there is nothing to for-
give. On the contrary, it is a matter of joy to me that I have
friends to whom I can write without reserve and without any fear
of being misunderstood.
However, I am glad that you have resolved upon not spend-
ing money on love cables. Love need not express itself in words,
but if it must, love-letters ought to be quite enough. Immedi-
ately we realize that we have nothing of our own, that all we
have is held in trust for those in greater need, we have to spend
it like misers.
I hope you are getting your copy of Harijan every week.
