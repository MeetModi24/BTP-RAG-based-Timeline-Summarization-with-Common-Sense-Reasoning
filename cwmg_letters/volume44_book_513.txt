513. LETTER TO TOTARAM SANADHYA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 7, 1930
BHAI TOTARAMJI,
Clay may be applied on the head. A mud-cap can be placed
on the head while working in the sun. I used it on many occa-
sions during the march. A towel spread over with clay and tied
on the head forms a cap and provides protection. How is Ganga-
devi's health? Does Dhiru behave well? Is he very boisterous?
Blessings from
BAPU
PANDIT TOTARAMJI
HARIJAN ASHRAM
SABARMATI JN.
B. B. & C. I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2540
