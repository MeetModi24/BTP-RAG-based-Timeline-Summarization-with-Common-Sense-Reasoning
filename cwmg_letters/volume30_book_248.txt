248. LETTER TO MIRZA KAZIM ALI
[On or after April 1, 1926]
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am getting quite well now. It is difficult
for me to send Young India free. You should really go to some
reading-room and read it there. There are thousands of poor
students and it is beyond my capacity to supply them all free
copies.
MIRZA KAZIM ALI
STUDENT
HYDERABAD
From a microfilm: S.N. 19889
