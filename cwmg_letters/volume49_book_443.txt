443. LETTER TO ASHRAM GIRLS
April 24, 1932
CHI.,
If you get your hair cropped, you would save the time you
have to give in taking proper care of it, save expense over oil,
comb, etc., and get rid of the false notion that there is beauty in
hair. Without hair, the head remains clean. For a woman,
absence of hair is a sign that she is observing brahmacharya. If
girls and grown-up women get their hair cropped, the practice
would cease to be regarded as sign of widowhood. If we think
about the matter we may discover some other benefits also. But
are not those I have mentioned enough just now?
From Gujarati: C.W. 9058. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi. Also Mahadevbhaini
Diary, Vol. I, p. 120
