418. LETTER TO DR. B. B. YODH
SEVAGRAM,
March 14, 1945
BHAI YODH,
What you wrote about Sir Purushottam made me happy. I
am sure under your treatment he will be fully restored.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. YODH
RAVAL BUILDING
WELLINGTON ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
