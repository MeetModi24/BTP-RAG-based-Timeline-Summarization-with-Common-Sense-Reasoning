377. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
March 9, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
You seem to have written the above in haste. Try and see
if you can steady your mind. Read The Hound of Heaven1, think
over it and understand its meaning. You will not be happy any-
where if you turn your back upon the "Hound".
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 5845. Courtesy: Munnalal G.
Shah
