453. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
September 16, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I am sending your letter to Sushilabehn. You have rightly
understood the problem of cremating the dead but yours is an
old method.
Somebody has to devise a new method. This
should not be beyond the sphere of nature cure. How to dis-
pose of dead bodies at a place where water comes up after a
little digging and wood is not available? We have to go deep
into the matter. Come after the A. I. C. C. meeting.
KRISHNAVARMA
NATURE CURE HOSPITAL
MALAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
