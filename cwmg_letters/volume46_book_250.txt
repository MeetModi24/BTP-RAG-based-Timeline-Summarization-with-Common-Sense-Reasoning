250. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
As AT SABARMATI,
May 24, 1931
Here is another letter for you to read and deal with. I would
like you to answer it directly. I have not acknowledged it. Please
let me know whether there is any truth in the statement made.
Encl. 1
SYT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARIAR
GANDHI ASHRAM
TIRUCHENGODU (S. INDIA)
From a microfilm: S.N. 17131
