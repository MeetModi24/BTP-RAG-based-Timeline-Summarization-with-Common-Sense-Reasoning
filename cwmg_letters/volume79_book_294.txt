294. LETTER TO KESHAV DEO MALAVIYA
SEVAGRAM,
February 27, 1945
BHAI KESHAV DEO,
If in the past the A. I. C. C. looked after the repairs of the
Swaraj Bhavan it should continue to do so even now. Perhaps
Chi. Kamalnayan understands this thing better. If so, consult
him. I have an idea that you have with you some A. I. C. C.
funds. The building is in a very bad shape and it is getting
worse.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
