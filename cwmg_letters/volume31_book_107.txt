107. LETTER TO TEHMINA KHAMBHATTA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Tuesday [July 6, 1926]
DEAR SISTER,
Your letter to hand. I was certainly expecting it. Every
time I take up Mrs. Eddy's book, I do remember Bhai Kham-
bhatta. I am very glad to learn he is recovering. How can we get
tired of a diet or a medicine which proves beneficial to us? I have
certainly not forgotten about sending my opinion of Mrs. Eddy'S
book. But since there is no urgency about it, I give it only
such time as I can spare from my other occupations.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. TEHMINA BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
275, HORNBY ROAD
FORT
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 4362. Courtesy: Tehmina Khambhatta
