431. LETTER TO BIRENDRA NATH GUHA
September 2, 1934
DEAR BIREN,
I am glad to know that Kshitishbabu has become softened a
bit. I wonder if he got my letter.
I was sorry to learn that Dr. Indra Narayan Sen has been
lost to us. Let us hope that he has been lost to us only for some
time to come.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
