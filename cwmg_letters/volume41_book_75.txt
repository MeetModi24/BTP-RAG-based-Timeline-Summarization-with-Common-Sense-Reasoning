75. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Silence Day [June 24, 1929]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. It is good that you observe the rules.
Discuss everything with Father at length. We are in a secluded
spot and we are going to spend eight days here. I will not write
more as I want to finish [the work] on the Gita.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3350
