447. LETTER TO V. A. HODKE
April 13, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to have your card telling me that the buffalo-
sacrifice was stopped and that the sacrifice of other animals was
reduced and that it was likely to be stopped altogether next year.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. V. A. HODKE
SECRETARY
PRANI DAYA SANGH
SIRSI (N. CANARA)
From a microfilm: S.N. 20919
