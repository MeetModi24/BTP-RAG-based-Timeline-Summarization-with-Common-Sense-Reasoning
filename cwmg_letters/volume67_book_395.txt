395. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
[August 23, 1938]
CHI. AMRITLAL,
There is no cause for worry about Kaka. We shall have
to proceed carefully.
Ask Dr. Mahodaya if it would not be
better to give two grains of calomel every half an hour eight
times. Go on giving him glucose, as much as he can digest. Give
him small quantities of mosambi juice. There are toxins in the
body and they will remain for some time. If he commits no
mistake in eating, all will be well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10767
