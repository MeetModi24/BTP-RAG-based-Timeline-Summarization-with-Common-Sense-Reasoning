340. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
December 9, 1933
CHI. MANU,
It seems you have completely forgotten me. You don't even
reply to me. Shouldn't you write at least about Kusum's health?
How is she? I had a letter from Ba from jail. She is worrying
about you and Kusum. Write to her. How are you? How is
aunt Bali? I am writing this in a running train.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Write to me at the Wardha address.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2663. Courtesy: Manubehn
S. Mashruwala
