68. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
Monday [November 22, 1926]
DEAR SISTER,
I am very happy to have your letter. You must always
write to me.
I do not know the reason why Prafulla Babu left. Such
things will keep on happening in the world. You are right in
saying that in suffering the soul blossoms. This is the teaching of
all religions.
I am relieved to know that all of you are now keeping good
health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1615
