127. LETTER TO MOHANLAL NAYYAR
[On or after August 28, 1947]
I have your letter. . . . What does it matter if everything
you possess in Gujrat is lost? In this general conflagration no
one may expect to escape. Sushila is in her proper place. She
is under God's care. Pyarelal will meet me today or tomorrow.
He is expected.
Mahatma Gandhi-The Last Phase, Vol. H, p. 380
