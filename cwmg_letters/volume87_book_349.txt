349. LETTER TO SHIV BALAK BISEN
April 28, 1947
CHI. BISEN,
I read your letter again. I do not wish to lose any of you.
Whoever goes, will go of his own accord. You do have the capa-
city to work. I like that, but the lack of harmony among you
all pains me. You ought to be keen to have it removed.
I see no need to do anything [in this matter] till I return
from Delhi. Nor do I wish to show your letter. This is a mat-
ter which you should discuss with them. You should listen to
what they have to say. If you do not have the courage nor the
desire to do so you should forget the thing. If you wish to come
to a decision before I leave for Delhi, you should yourself discuss
the matter with them immediately today, or take whatever other
action you wish to.
Blessings from
BAPU
