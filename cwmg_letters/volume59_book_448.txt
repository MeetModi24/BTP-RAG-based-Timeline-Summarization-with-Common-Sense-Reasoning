448. LETTER TO SADULLAH KHAN
November 22, 1934
MY DEAR SADULLAH KHAN,
I have your letter. I am looking forward to receiving and
testing the gur sent by you.
You have all worked wonders in connection with Dr. Khan
Saheb's election.
SHRI SADULLAH KHAN
DR. KHAN SAHEB'S ELECTION BOARD OFFICE
QISSAKHANI BAZAR, PESHAWAR CITY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
