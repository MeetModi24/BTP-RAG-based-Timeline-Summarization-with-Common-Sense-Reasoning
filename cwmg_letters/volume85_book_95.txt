95. LETTER TO T. PRAKASAM
POONA,
July 31, 1946
BHAI PRAKASAM,
I have found your letter (of July) among my papers. I am
sorry that we could not meet. It is good that the Harijans
were let off.
I am aware of the constructive work you are doing in the
Madras Presidency. I shall be very happy if it shows some results.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI T. PRAKASAM
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
