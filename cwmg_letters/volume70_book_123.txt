123. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
August 19, 1939
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I have your letter. I think Vijaya is still in Varad. Again
there has been no letter. I am forwarding the vaid's letter to Varad.
She had not gone to Bombay at all. Naranbhai had gone
to Bombay before she left.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10793
