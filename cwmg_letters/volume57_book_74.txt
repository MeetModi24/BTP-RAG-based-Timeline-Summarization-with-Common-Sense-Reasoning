74. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
January 30, 1934
CHI. JANAKIBEHN,
Is it right to complain if Jamnalal gets excited owing to
weakness of the brain?
Should we mind the bad temper of a
person who is sick? It should be always ignored. Or did you
write the letter for my amusement? Tell Madalasa that she seems
to have forgotten me completely. That will not do. Om is fine.
How is Ramakrishna? How are you? Take care of Vali.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2930
