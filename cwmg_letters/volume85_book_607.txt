607. LETTER TO DURGA M. DESAI
NEW DELHI,
October 16, 1946
CHI. DURGA,
Rest assured that I will not go against your wishes and
encourage Bablo in any way. I also read the letter from Nara-
hari. I shall not enter into any argument because now there is
no question of sending Bablo to Afghanistan.
I think it is right for you to stay wherever Bablo may be
living. I also like the idea of [his] working for Nayee Talim.
Let us all wish that he may keep good health and grow strong
in body, mind and spirit.
My return to Sevagram is again delayed. Everything is in
the hands of God.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. DURGABEHN DESAI
ASHRAM, SEVAGRAM
