516. LETTER TO ELEANOR M. HOUGH
CAMP HARDOI,
October 12, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You will please excuse me for my inabi-
lity to send you the introduction you want.
Yours sincerely,
MISS ELEANOR M. HOUGH
2115 F STREET, N.W.
WASHINGTON, D.C., U.S.A.
