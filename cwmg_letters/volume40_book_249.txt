249. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
April 18, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I have had so much wandering to do these days that I don't
get my post regularly. And I would be putting people to diffi-
culty if I asked for the post to be brought to me in villages. They
can bring it only in a special car. I, therefore, do not know
where I shall get yesterday's and today's post.
I discovered an old letter from Rupnarayanbabu, which I
enclose with this. If you know what happened afterwards, or can
inquire from him, and if there is anything I can do, write to me
about it or ask him to write.
I wrote to you a detailed letter yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I also enclose with this a note about the expenditure in
Wardha which I had got prepared. It need not be returned.
Send me the details about the money received from Ran-
goon for the Kathiawar Political Conference. The money was col-
lected by Maneklal.
