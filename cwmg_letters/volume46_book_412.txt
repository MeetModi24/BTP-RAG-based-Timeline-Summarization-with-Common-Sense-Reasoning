412. LETTER TO NAJUKLAL N. CHOKSI
June 5, 1931
CHI. NAJUKLAL,
I have your letter. If an operation offers the only cure for
Moti have it done quickly. You have done well to take up the job
at a library. What is your salary there?
I have looked into the article about Champaran. That
sugar can certainly be regarded as indigenous. How pitiable is our
condition that if anybody wishes to give up eating such sugar, he
must stop eating sugar altogether.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12148
