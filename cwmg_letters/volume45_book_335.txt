335. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
DARYAGANJ, DELHI,
March 6, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your letter. I can understand your eagerness to meet
me. I hope to leave this place on Sunday and arrive there on
Monday. We shall thus be meeting on Monday evening.
MAHADEV
FOR BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3408
