591. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Silence Day [July 18, 1927]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I wrote you a letter about Pyarelal. Could it be that you
have not received it? I have detained Shankaran here. There
has not been any letter from you for a long time. All of us are
going to Mysore today. We shall be there for three days. By
the 23rd we shall be back here (Bangalore).
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAs TRIKUMJI
CASTLE HILL
PANCHGANI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
