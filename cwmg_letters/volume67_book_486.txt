486. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
September 13, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
This is the result of overworking. You must now stop work-
ing altogether and take complete rest. You will perhaps get more
peace of mind if you come over here. If you do not come, keep
Sushila there.
I am not at all worried.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11650
