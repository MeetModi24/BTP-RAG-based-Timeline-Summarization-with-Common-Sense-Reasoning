169. LETTER TO SIR EVAN M. JENKINS
SEVAGRAM,
November 25, 1945
DEAR SIR EVAN,
Under pressure of work and preparations for shifting from
Poona to Sevagram I had overlooked acknowledging your letter
of 9th instant regarding Shri Basuda Singh. Meanwhile, I saw
in the papers and from private telegrams received from Bihar that
His Excellency had commuted the death sentence. Will you
please therefore convey my thanks to His Excellency for the
commutation?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, pp. 56-
