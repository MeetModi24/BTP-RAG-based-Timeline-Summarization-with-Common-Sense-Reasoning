28. LETTER TO UTTIMCHAND GANGARAM
NEW DELHI,
April 19, 1946
MY DEAR UTTIMCHAND,
This I must write to you in English. You are wrong.
When you say you are right and I do not understand you, I go
to my betters and seek confirmation of your prop[osition]. If
they do not confirm you, I pass their opinion on to you.
What you will have to do is to teach me your conundrums
when I am free to take a few lessons from you. Heaven knows
when that time will come.
Receipt of the money sent by you was acknowledged on
11-3-46. It is to be found in my register book.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Thanks for the interesting puzzle.
SHRI UTTIMCHAND GANGARAM
BOMBAY BAKERY, HYDERABAD (SIND)
