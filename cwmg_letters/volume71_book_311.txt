311. LETTER TO VINAYAKPRASAD G. PANDYA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 8, 1940
BHAI VINAYAKPRASAD,
I could reach your letter only today.
If you bear with the
opposition and go on doing your work with single-minded devo-
tion, a day will come when the spinning-wheel will have become
popular.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VINAYAKPRASAD G. PANDYA
BAJVADA, KHATRI POLE
BARODA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1368
