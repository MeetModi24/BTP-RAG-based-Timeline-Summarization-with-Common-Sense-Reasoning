315. LETTER TO ISABEL BAMLET
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 10, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you.
For me the problem of life is not quite so simple as it appears
to you. I am sure you do not want me to enter into the reason
for my conclusions. You believe in God's guidance and so do I.
Let me walk as He may seem to me to guide me.
I may without using your name make the main contents of
your letter as a text for an article in the pages of Young India
edited by me. I suppose you will have no objection.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
I. BAMLET, ESQ.
C/O IMPERIAL BANK OF INDIA
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: C.W. 4443. Courtesy: Mrs. Carlisle Bamlet
