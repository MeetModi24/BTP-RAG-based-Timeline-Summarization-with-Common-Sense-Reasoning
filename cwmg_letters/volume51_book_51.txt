51. LETTER TO NARMADABEHN RANA
September 10, 1932
CHI. NARMADA,
You may go on hoping to receive a long letter from me even
at the risk of being called greedy. One day I may think of
writing such a letter to you.
Your Gujarati is not good. You should get your letters
corrected by someone. But do not bother if that cannot be done.
In any case, you should continue to write to me whatever you
wish to.
I should like you to learn in time everything that is worth
learning in the Ashram.
Who teaches you Gujarati? What other languages are you
learning?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2763. Courtesy: Ramnarayan
N. Pathak
