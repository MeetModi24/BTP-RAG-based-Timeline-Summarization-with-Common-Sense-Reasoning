138. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
May 7, 1933
CHI. MARY,
You do not expect or want a long letter. This will be my
last for four or at least three weeks. Let not the fast disturb your
well-earned rest. I expect you to return to the Ashram refreshed
for much concentrated work.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3329. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr. Also G.N. 6003
