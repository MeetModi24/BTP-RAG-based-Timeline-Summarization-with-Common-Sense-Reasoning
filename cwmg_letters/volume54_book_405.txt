405. LETTER TO MADHAVJI AND KRISHNA KAPADIA
April 9, 1933
CHI. MADHAVJI AND KRISHNA,
I have received letters of both of you. I too had written to
Medh. He wrote to me. I did not inform you as the promise
was to materialize only after some time. I am very happy to
know that you have a little peace of mind. There are always ups
and downs. The great thing is that you have not lost your peace
of mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
MADHAVJI GOKULDAS
SHAMJI SHIVAJINO MALO
MANORDAS STREET
BOMBAY
