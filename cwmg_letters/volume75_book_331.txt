331. LETTER TO MANJULA M. MEHTA
January 30, 1942
CHI. MANJULA,
I have your letters. I acted upon them immediately. I took
Champa to task. She has realized her error somewhat and feels
ashamed. But my words are likely to have no permanent effect on
her. After my rebuke, she gave me the accompanying letter. I
have read it. It means nothing.
When are you coming here? If you wish, I would shift Champa
from that room. It is reserved for you exclusively. It will take
some time before your building is ready. Till then you may
treat this room as your own. I have put Champa there as your
representative.
Ratilal has run away to Wankaner on somebody's mare. It
is not proper for Magan not to go and see him. An attendant can
be found there.
I hope all of you are doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1025. Courtesy: Manjula M.
Mehta
