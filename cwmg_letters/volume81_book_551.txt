551. LETTER TO YUSUF MEHERALLY
October 3, 1945
BHAI MEHERALLY1,
Acknowledgement of your good wishes is just an excuse
for writing to you. I hope you are all right. I have found an
occasion for expressing my wish.
YUSUF MEHERALLY
WADIA LODGE
IGATPURI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
