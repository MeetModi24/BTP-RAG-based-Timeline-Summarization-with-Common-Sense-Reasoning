318. LETTER TO ABDUL GHAFFAR KHAN
BHAI BADSHAH KHAN,
I want a trustee for the Charkha Sangh who is a true Khudai
Khidmatgar and also a Muslim. Who else can I find but you? Shall I
make you a trustee?
BADSHAH KHAN
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr.
Sushila Nayyar
