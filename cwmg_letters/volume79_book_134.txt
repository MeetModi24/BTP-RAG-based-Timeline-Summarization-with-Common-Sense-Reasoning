134. LETTER TO KAMALA LELE
SEVAGRAM,
January 30, 1945
CHI. KAMALA,
I have your letter. Since the patient here, who was under
Chandi Prasad's treatment, had also become very ill I wired him.
He was due yesterday. Let us see if he comes today. His address
is: Lakshminarayan Mandir, New Delhi. Get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
KAMALA LELE
MAHILA ASHRAM
WARDHA
