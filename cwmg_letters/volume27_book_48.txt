48. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
Vaishakha Vad 6 [May 14, 1925]
CHI. MANI 2 ,
I was delighted to have your long letter. It is indeed very
difficult to work among women. Nevertheless you should patiently
do what you can. Dahyabhai must have gone to Mount Abu or
to Navibandar. I have not forgotten about the conch-shell bangles
you wanted. They are available in Dacca, which I shall be
reaching in about three days. Has Father had any opportunity to
go for a change?
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MANIBEHN
C/O VALLABHBHAI PATEL, BARRISTER
AHMEDABAD
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne, p. 22
