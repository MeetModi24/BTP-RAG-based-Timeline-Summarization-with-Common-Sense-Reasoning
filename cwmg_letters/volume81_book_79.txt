79. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
July 28, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have your letter concerning Dharmadev Shastri. It is all
right. For the eight committees of C. P. and Berar you have
asked for the sanction of Rs. 200 at the rate of Rs. 25 for each
committee. Earlier you had asked for Rs. 50 for each commit-
tee. For the present you may send Rs. 200. Later we shall see
what to do.
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
