526. LETTER TO DR. M. A. NARAYANA
April 20, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I must not be expected to decide upon
the private character of people whom I do not know. The
method of working out the programme of untouchability is before
the public. It is generally known now that only those with pure
character and a religious spirit should enter upon this service,
and no others.
Yours sincerely,
DR. M. A. NARAYANA
C/O THE NEW PHARMACY
23 44TH STREET
RANGOON
From a microfilm: S.N. 21005
