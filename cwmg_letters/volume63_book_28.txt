28. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[After June 6, 1936]
CHI. MIRA,
Yours of 3rd just to hand. Yes, Sejila should have a shed
by himself, so as to leave your verandah free. On second thoughts
I think it will be wise to bring the commode and the pot from
Maganwadi for European visitors. Therefore this is in addition
to the plan suggested by me.
If you get hold of peacocks, I do not mind. But I know
nothing of their habits.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6346. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9812
