304. LETTER TO D. D. SATHAYE
January 16, 1942
DEAR SHRI SATHAYE,
Gandhiji had your letter in Bardoli and now he has your p.c. He has been
so busy that his personal correspondence is in arrears. He wishes me to tell
you that you are of course on his list of satyagrahis but there is no satyagraha
just now.
The constructive programme is there for everyone to follow.
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
SHRI D. D. SATHAYE
127 GIRGAON ROAD, BOMBAY 4
From the original: D. D. Sathaye Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
