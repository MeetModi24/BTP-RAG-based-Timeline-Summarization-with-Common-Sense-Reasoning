186. LETTER TO SWAMI SHRADDHANAND
Ashvina Krishna 2 [October 14, 1924]
BHAI SAHIB,
I have your note. I am trying to make the necessary ar-
rangements for Vykom; I hope help will reach the satyagrahis.
I have carefully gone through your statement. I shall keep it
with me.
Yours sincerely,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SANNYASI SWAMI SHRI SHRADDHANAND
BURN BASTION ROAD
DELHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2206
