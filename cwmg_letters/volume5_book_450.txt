450. LETTER TO DR. E. NUNDY
21-24 COURT CHAMBERS,
JOHANNESBURG,
September 20, 1906
DEAR DR. NUNDY,
My answer to both your questions is yes, if the Court is composed
of men of known standing and ability.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
[DR. EDWARD NUNDY
JACOB CHAMBERS
COURT ROAD
JOHANNESBURG]
Pretoria Archives : L. G. File No. 93 : Asiatics
