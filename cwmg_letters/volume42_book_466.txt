466. LETTER TO MURIEL LESTER
SABARMATI,
February 23, 1930
MY DEAR MURIEL,
Your letter is lying in front of me demanding an answer. On
the eve of what promises to be a life and death struggle I think
of all my English friends known and unknown. You are not the
least among them.
My love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6561
