118. LETTER TO M. M. MALAVIYA
May 21, 1944
PUJYA BHAI SAHEB,
The doctor has permitted me to write such letters. I don't
deserve your love. I know that I am unable to fulfil your wishes.
The doctors don't permit my travelling long distances. The
fact is I don't feel as if I am out of jail. Is being released on
grounds of health any release at all? Let us see what way God
shows me after I am all right.
YOUR YOUNGER BROTHER
From a microfilm of the Hindi : M.M.U./XXIII
