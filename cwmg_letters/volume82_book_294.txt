294. LETTER TO ATUL CHANDRA GHOSH
KHADI PRATISHTHAN, SODEPUR,
December 14, 1945
BHAI ATUL BABU,
What can I do? I cannot be young for ever. Therefore
be content with whatever service I can render from one place.
Please tell the people of Manbhum that we can achieve everything
through ahimsa and the symbol of ahimsa is the charkha.
Blessings from
BAPU
BABU ATULCHAND GHOSH
NIVARAN ASHRAM
PURULIA (MANBHUM)
