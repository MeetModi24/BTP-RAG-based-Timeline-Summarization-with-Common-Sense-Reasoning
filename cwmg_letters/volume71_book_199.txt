199. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 30, 1940
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Padma Pant of Cawnpore has sent me copy of his letter to
you. I hope you will probe the truth.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Have you seen the latest pamphlet of the Jamiat-Ulema-i-
Hind? They are dangerous friends. I wonder if the W[orking]
C[ommittee] had a full talk with Moulvi Kifayatulla Saheb!
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1940. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
