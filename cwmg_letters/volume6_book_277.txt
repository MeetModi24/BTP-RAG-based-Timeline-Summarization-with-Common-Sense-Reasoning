277. LETTER TO D. G. PANSE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 29, 1906
DEAR SIR,
Some time during the month, on my return to the Hotel, I found
a card left by you. I kept it in the hope of being able to call on you
during my stay here. I however find that it is not possible for me to do
so. I therefore now write to apologize.
I am,
Yours truly,
D. G. PANSE, ESQ.
INNS OF COURT HOTEL
HIGH HOLBORN
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4660
