473. LETTER TO K. RENGARAJAN
November 25, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
You should have mer[cy] on me. I am one and senders of
papers are many. How can I cope with the work except by ex-
cluding much unsolicited matter?
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat : C.W. 10232. Courtesy : Gandhi Seva Sangh, Sevagram
