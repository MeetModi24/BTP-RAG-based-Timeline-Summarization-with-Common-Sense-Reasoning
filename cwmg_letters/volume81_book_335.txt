335. LETTER TO RAMBHAI MAMTANI
August 31, 1945
BHAI RAMBHAI MAMTANI,
I have your letter. Now you can see me only when I return
to Sevagram. You should ask for the answer to your first question
only there.
For the rest, have patience and see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
TALIMI SANGH
