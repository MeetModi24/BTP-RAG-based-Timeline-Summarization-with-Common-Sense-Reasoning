130. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
October 21, 1933
CHI. MARY,
You may take barley not because you have some with you
but because you need it. But if you are constipated, it is not
barley you need but some green, such as gourd or the like.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6011. Also C.W. 3337. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
