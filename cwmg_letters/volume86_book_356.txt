356. LETTER TO BELA MITRA
SRIRAMPUR, NOAKHALI,
December 27, 1946
DEAREST BELA,
Haridas has come back. He is full of hope but at the same
time what a burden of anxiety he carries! Will you still remain
bed-ridden?
I send you my hearty blessings. Recover soon and
help your husband in his work, giving him courage and strength.
I stop here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Bengali: C. W. 1471. Courtesy: A. K. Sen
