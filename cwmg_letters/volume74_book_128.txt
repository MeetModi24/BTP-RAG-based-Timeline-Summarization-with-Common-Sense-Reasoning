128. LETTER TO SAVITRI BAJAJ
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 26, 1941
CHI. SAVITRI 1 ,
Since you have passed in the first division, many must have
congratulated you. If you want my congratulations you can have
them. I am not at all surprised that you obtained the first division,
because the subjects that you had to study were not difficult for
you. The difficult and useful examination for our country is that
of the Charkha Sangh. There all-round development is needed,
and the examination to which I am referring is the Prathama.
It is no doubt interesting. I hope you are keeping your word.
Here it is terribly hot.
Blessings from
BAPU
