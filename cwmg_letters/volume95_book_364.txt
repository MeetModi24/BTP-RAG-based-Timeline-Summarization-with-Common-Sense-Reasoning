364. LETTER TO MANGALABEHN PATEL
Silence day, BORSAD
CHI. MANGALA,
I have your letter. The news is correct. You are not scared on
hearing about Kamubehn, are you? We have learnt not to be scared
of death and to treat birth and death alike. He who is born, dies, and
he who dies is born. Where then is the reason to feel sad?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : C. W. 11104. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
