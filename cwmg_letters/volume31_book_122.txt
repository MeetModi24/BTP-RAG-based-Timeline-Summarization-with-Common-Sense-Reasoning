122. LETTER TO LALCHAND JAYCHAND VORA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
[July 8, 1926]
BHAI LALCHAND,
Your letter. At this crucial stage in khadi [work] I cannot
advise unattached persons to run a risk and maintain themselves
on khadi bhandars alone. They should join the stores run by
the Charkha Sangh or some other public organization. Many
khadi lovers are doing this today.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
LALCHAND JAYCHAND VORA
SAURASHTRA KHADI BHANDAR
49, EZRA STREET
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 7752. Courtesy: L. J. Vora
