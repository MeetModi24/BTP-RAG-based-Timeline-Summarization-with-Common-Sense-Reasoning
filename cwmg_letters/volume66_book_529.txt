529. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[After March 28, 1938]
I can say you are now doing my work. It was a great relief
to me that you were looking after Amtul Salaam and that you
saw her off.
You won't worry about me.
I shall try to be there as early
as I can.
Be patient with Lilavati.
Love.
From the original: C.W. 6423. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10018
