524. LETTER TO BHUPENDRA NARAYAN SEN
March 26, 1945
MY DEAR BHUPEN,
I shall be presently off to Bombay. By all means come and
pass a few days with me on my return to Sevagram.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI BHUPENDRA NARAYAN SEN
99/2, BALLYGUNJ PLACE
P. O. BALLYGUNJ
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 10067
