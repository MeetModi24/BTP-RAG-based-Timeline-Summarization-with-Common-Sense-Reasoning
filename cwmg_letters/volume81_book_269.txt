269. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
August 24, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your letter. Give Mama full liberty. Tell him: "Bapu
will worry about you but will not restrain you. You may leave
at your own risk. Do not expect to come back. May God help
you." You did much for Mama. Now I do not want you to carry
that burden.
I have not seen the prohibitory order concerning Kamrej
but if it is what you say, I would certainly not like it.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. KRISHNAVARMA
NATURE CURE HOSPITAL
MALAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
