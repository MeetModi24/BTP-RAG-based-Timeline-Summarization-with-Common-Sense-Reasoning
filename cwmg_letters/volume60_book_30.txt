30. LETTER TO MIRZA ISMAIL
December 23, 1934
With regard to the visit to the Frontier Province I hope
you saw the statement I issued to the Press. I shall strain every
nerve to avoid a clash. With me it is not a matter of sacrificing
anything for anything else. Is not the vast meaning of sacrifice
self-purification? So long as my submission to the Viceregal
will does not harm the cause it shall continue. You may de-
pend upon it that I shall take no hasty step.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
