423. LETTER TO TOWN CLERK
[JOHANNESBURG,]
June 22, 1905
TO
THE TOWN CLERK
P.O. BOX 1049
JOHANNESBURG
SIR,
Re: THE RIGHT OF INDIANS TO TRAVEL IN THE MUNICIPAL TRAM-CARS
If the Tramway Committee has considered this matter, I shall thank
you for a reply to my communications herein.
1 Vide "Letter to E. A. Walters", May 25, 1905.
My client, who applied for the monthly pass, is very anxious to
bring the matter to a head, and test his right, should the Committee
decline to entertain his application favourably.
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
M.K. GANDHI
Letter Book (1905) : No. 397.
