799. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
POONA,
October 31, 1945
CHI. KANCHAN,
I do not like your recent letters. Ask me about Madras
when I arrive there. I hope to reach there on November 21. I
have written a short letter to Munnalal and am daily awaiting
his reply. Ask him why he has not sent it till now.
When will you improve your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8261. Also C.W. 6986. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
