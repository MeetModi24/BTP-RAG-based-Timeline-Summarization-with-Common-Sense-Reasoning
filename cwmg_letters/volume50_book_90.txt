90. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
June 23, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA,
Yours is the first letter I see. The figure of four is written
differently in Hindi and in Gujarati-'t [in Gujarati] and l( [in
Hindi]. I am glad that you are learning verses from the Gita by
heart. Pronounce the words correctly. And also try to understand
the meanings of the verses.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5775. Also C.W. 2998. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
