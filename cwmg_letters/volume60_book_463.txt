463. LETTER TO SUDHIR KUMAR RUDRA
March 27, 1935
DEAR SUDHIR 1 ,
C. F. Andrews has just written to me about your serious illness
and your recovery from it. God be thanked for it. You have to
serve for many a year yet to come. I hope you will soon regain
all your lost strength.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SUDHIR KUMAR
20 ALBERT ROAD
ALLAHABAD (U. P.)
From the original: C.W. 9740. Courtesy: Rajmohini Rudra
