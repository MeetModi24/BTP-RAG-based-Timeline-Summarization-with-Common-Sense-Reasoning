113. LETTER TO RAM NARESH TRIPATHI
SEVAGRAM,
January 25, 1945
BHAI RAMNARESH1,
Why should I bless you on your daughter's wedding? For
you and me birth, death and marriage are just different forms of
the same thing, are they not? Still, if you want my blessings you
have them.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAM NARESH TRIPATHI
SULTANPUR
