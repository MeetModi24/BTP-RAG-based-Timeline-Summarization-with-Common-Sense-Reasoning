1. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Tuesday [June 15, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
There has been no letter from any of you today. I certainly
hoped for one from Devdas. There is no harm if you cannot
come on the 26th. But that should be only on health grounds.
Bhai Amritlal Sheth has sent me a list today. You may take it
that when you come here you will have to spend three or four
days in Kathiawar.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2867
