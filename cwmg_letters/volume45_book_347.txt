347. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
DELHI,
March 8, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
I thank you for your prompt reply. I quite agree with you
that the instructions must take some time to reach all the diffe-
rent places. I thought it my duty to pass on the wire for such
action as you might think necessary. And I feel grateful that you
have taken action on the wire.
Yours sincerely,
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
