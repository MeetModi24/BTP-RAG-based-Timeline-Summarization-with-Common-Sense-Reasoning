350. LETTER TO ABDUL MAJID CHAUDHRI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 23, 1940
DEAR CHAUDHRI SAHIB,
I have very carefully read your letter. In the Congress
itself there is no Hindu raj. A Muslim divine rules it. There
is no hypocrisy about the Congress. Englishmen, Christians, Par-
sis and Muslims have dominated the Congress. In any case the
Congress will never coerce any community to remain within its fold.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
CHAUDHRI SAHIB ABDUL MAJID, B.A., LL.B.
GUJRAT, PUNJAB
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
