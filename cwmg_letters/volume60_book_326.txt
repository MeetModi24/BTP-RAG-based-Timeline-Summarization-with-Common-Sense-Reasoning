326. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
February 21, 1935
BHAI RAJENDRA PRASAD,
Jayaprakash had been to see me. I do feel that the Congress
should fix one day for the Bengal troubles. I have even drafted
a resolution and given it to Jayaprakash. He will send it on if
he likes it.
This letter should be shown to the Sardar as I have no time
to write separately to him. I am leaving for Nagpur just now
and shall be back tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: A.I.C.C. File 1935. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
