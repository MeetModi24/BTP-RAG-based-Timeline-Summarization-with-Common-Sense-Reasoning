315. LETTER TO GULBAI TATA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 26, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I was very sorry to learn that you had a fall. Get moving
about soon. I too do not go out for walks. I take a walk in
the plain stretch in the courtyard. I may go out today. I thank
you for your gift. I cannot read either English or any other
musical notation. You have not sent the words.
Dr. Sushilabehn will give you this letter.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SISTER GULBAI TATA
FOUNTAIN HOTEL
MAHABALESHWAR
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
