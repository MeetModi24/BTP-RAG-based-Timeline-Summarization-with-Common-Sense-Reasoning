215. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
September 18, 1939
CHI. AMRITLAL,
You must have got Vijaya's and Uttamchand's letters which
I sent with Shriman yesterday.
It is time for Sushila to arrive but one cannot say when
she will come. There is no news from her.
Bisen should take hip-baths as also sun-baths. He is sure to
benefit from them.
I would certainly try and see how far
they are helpful to him if he comes here some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10795
