143. LETTER TO "KAISER-I-HIND"
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your circular letter dated 26th October. The only thing
I can say with reference to the Viceroy's declaration is that unless
there is a full response to what must be frankly considered to be
the conditions enumerated in the leaders' manifesto there can be
no peace.
Yours sincerely,
THE EDITOR, "KAISER-I-HIND"
FORT, BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 15757
