116. LETTER TO RADHA GANDHI
November 14, 1945
CHI. RADHA,
I have your postcard. You are quite modest. Can we say
that now you have settled down in Rajkot?
Manilal and Sushila are at Akola. Their son Arun is with
me. I shall send your postcard to Manilal.
I hope all of you are all right. You must do some work of
service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
74
