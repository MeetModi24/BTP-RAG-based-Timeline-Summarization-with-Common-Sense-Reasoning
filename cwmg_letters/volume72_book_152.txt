152. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 31, 1940
CHI. BRAJKRISHNA,
Yes, do start a camp at Narela.
It is not good to detain
Nayar there under some pretext or the other. But you must
do whatever you consider advisable. You have to do something
about the widespread nervousness among the people.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2480
