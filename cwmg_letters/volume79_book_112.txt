112. LETTER TO RAJDEV
SEVAGRAM,
January 25, 1945
BHAI RAJDEV,
You have my blessings of course. Ours is to make the effort.
Whether finally you are sent to the gallows or are released is
something over which we have no control.
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
