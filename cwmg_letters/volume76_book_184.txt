184. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 26, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
You have given me a graphic account of the interview.
It was a good thing you wrote and you got an appointment.
Of course, he was sweet and friendly and will always remain so.
But that makes no difference to the plan. It will mature slowly.
You therefore need not be in a hurry to come away. The
weather is much too hot for you.
Love.
BAPU
RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
MANORVILLE
SIMLA W .
From the original: C.W. 4123. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7432
