580. LETTER TO EKANATH
February 6, 1947
BHAI EKANATH,
I have your letter. I am glad to learn that you try to
provide treatment to both Hindu and Muslim patients and they
accept your services. I have already spoken about cleanliness in
my speeches. But I shall keep your suggestion in mind and will
say something about it.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
