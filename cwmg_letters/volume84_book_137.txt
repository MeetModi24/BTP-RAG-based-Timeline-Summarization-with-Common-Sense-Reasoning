137. LETTER TO U. N. DHEBAR
SIMLA,
May 6, 1946
BHAI DHEBARBHAI2,
I have your letter. I see no way of improvement for the
small principalities of Kathiawar so long as they are concerned
only with themselves. Their subjects have no strength. I shall
congratulate you and Kathiawar if you can find a way.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. UCHHARANGARAI DHEBAR
RAJKOT
