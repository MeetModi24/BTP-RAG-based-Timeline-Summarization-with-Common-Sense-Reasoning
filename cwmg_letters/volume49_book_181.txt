181. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
February 26, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I got your letter after I had waited for one for a long time.
Tell Chhotubhai that both of us often think about him. Do
you get any news about Pyarelal? How is Chandubhai's health?
Where is Dr. Sumant? How does he keep? I am more or less
all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1828
