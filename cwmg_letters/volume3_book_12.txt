12. LETTER TO SECRETARY OF STATE FOR INDIA
P. O. Box 1302,
JOHANNESBURG,
August 25, 1898
THE RIGHT HONOURABLE
LORD GEORGE HAMILTON
PRIVY COUNCILLOR, ETC.
SECRETARY OF STATE FOR INDIA
LONDON, ENGLAND
RIGHT HONOURABLE SIR,
On behalf of ourselves and other Indian British subjects re-
siding at Johannesburg in the South African Republic we beg
herewith to hand you the enclosed petition
We have the honour to be,
Right Honourable Sir,
Your most obedient servants,
A. CHETTY
A. APPASAMY
Colonial Office Records: Memorials and Petitions, 1898
