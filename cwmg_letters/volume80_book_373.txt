373. LETTER TO KUNDAR DIWAN
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 30, 1945
BHAI KUNDAR,
Herewith my Foreword. I am no longer fit for such work.
If I wish to live, it is in order that I can take part in the yajna
of freedom by doing something original. Writing such prefaces
is not that work. You will be writing so many things. Please do
not ask for anything from me.
I find the discourses on the Gita interesting. But I cannot
say what you can. Where do I have that knowledge? You may
reject my Foreword if you find it of no use.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUNDAR DIWAN
M. S. MANDAL, DATTAPUR
NALWADI, WARDHA
