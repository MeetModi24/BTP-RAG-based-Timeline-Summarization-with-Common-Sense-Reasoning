474. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
May 1, 1932
CHI. PUSHPA,
Gandhiraj means that a person, that is Gandhi, will rule the
country. What is the good of that? In swaraj, everybody will
rule, and everybody will include you, me and all others. If you
do not understand how that can be, ask Premabehn to explain
it to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3987. Also C.W. 33. Courtesy:
Pushpabehn N. Naik
