237. LETTER TO BRITISH COMMITTEE OF
INDIAN NATIONAL CONGRESS
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 21, 1906
THE SECRETARY
THE BRITISH COMMITTEE OF THE INDIAN NATIONAL CONGRESS
84 & 85, PALACE CHAMBERS
WESTMINSTER
DEAR MR. HALL,
With reference to the £3.10. paid by Mr. Naoroji on a cablegram
sent by the British Indian Association of Johannesburg, will you kindly
deduct the amount from the draft forwarded to Sir William Wedderburn.
Will you also kindly send to the Secretary of the Hamidia Society,
Box 6031 , Johannesburg, India regularly. I will bring the annual
subscription when I am over there.
I am,
Yours truly,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4626
