398. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
Y. M.,
December 21, 1930
CHI. KANTA,
There is again no letter from you. How is that? You should
not put off writing till the last day. You should write early.
From Narandas's letter I thought that your letter would be en-
closed but it was not. This is your third lapse. Do not commit
it a fourth time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
