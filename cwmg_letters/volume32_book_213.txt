213. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
ON THE TRAIN,
January 4, 1927
BHAISHRI,
You ask me not to write merely to acknowledge your letters,
and want to know when I am going to Kathiawar. I must
reply, then. I am hoping that I shall be there in the beginning
of March.
Look after your health.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
If the Hindus forgive Abdul Rashid what can be more
welcome than that?
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3210. Courtesy: Mahesh
Pattani
