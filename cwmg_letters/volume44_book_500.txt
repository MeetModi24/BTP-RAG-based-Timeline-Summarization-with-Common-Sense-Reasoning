500. LETTER TO CHAND TYAGI
December 5, 1930
BHAI TYAGIJI,
I was very pleased to have your letter. Take milk if the
weakness does not go despite a regular diet. Try cooked cereals
before you do that. Don't be obstinate. I was sorry to learn of the
conditions in Gurukul. Is Abhayji aware of them? What was
Ramdevji's reply? Baldev may well learn carpentry. Write to him,
he should learn to construct charkha, loom, takli, etc. Who is the
principal in Vri[ndavan] Gu[rukul]? Tell Premrajji to write to
me how all things are. What is going on there?
BHARATIYA PATHSHALA
Blessings from
FARRUKHABAD
BAPU
[PS.]
I never knew that your Urdu letters were like print, they are
very good.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3266
