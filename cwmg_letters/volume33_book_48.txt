48. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
AKOLA,
February 7, 1927
MY DEAR BHRRR1,
I was delighted to receive your note. Of course you had great
success in Kathiawar. Ramdas told all about it.
Sohaila certainly will have my blessings, if she and hers will
behave themselves and wear khadi.
Here is the letter you want for Dr. Mehta. But must you go
to Rangoon for the amount? Why can you not collect what you
can locally and the rest may be collected without your going to
Rangoon, seeing that you will allow Hindus to participate in
the contributions? Of course if you go to Rangoon you will
collect the small amount you mention in a few days.
With love,
Yours,
BHRRR
From a photostat: S.N. 9557
