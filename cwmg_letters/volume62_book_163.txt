163. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
WARDHA,
November 26, 1935
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
It was settled yesterday with Thakkar Bapa himself that Mal-
kani would do his work independently while remaining the Joint
Secretary. But he came to me early this morning expressing his
inability to hold the office. I am enclosing a copy of the letter
I have written to Thakkar Bapa regarding this development. Hence
it would be superfluous to go into further details here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8014. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
