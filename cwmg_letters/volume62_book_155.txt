155. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
WARDHA,
November 23, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your two letters and ragi, also samples of soya beans,
and now cow-gram. We have already cooked ragi, but not
exactly in the fashion mentioned by you. Cow-gram does not
contain the quantity of fats that soya beans do. I am now trying
to find the chemical constituents of ragi. If you can find them
through any doctor please send them.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: Ambujammal Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
