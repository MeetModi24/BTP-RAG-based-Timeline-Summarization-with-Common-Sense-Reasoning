588. LETTER TO K. VISVA EASAN
October 29, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have read the cutting sent by you. I do think that the
cruelty to the cat was abominable and in a State regulated by
a system of punishments, the punishment would be justified.
SJT. K. VISVA EASAN
TRICHINOPOLY
S. INDIA
From the office copy: S.N. 19959
