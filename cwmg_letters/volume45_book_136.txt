136. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI PATEL
January 18, 1931
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
I have your letter. I understand the situation in which you
are placed. If you only remain firm in your vow about the struggle,
many of your problems will be solved. I don't remember having
written anything special to you in reply to your letter about jail life.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9504
