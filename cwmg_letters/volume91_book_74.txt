74. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI H. PATEL
Wednesday [Before February 10, 1915]
BHAISHRI MAGANBHAI,
I have your letter. I will never leave you so long as you do
not want to leave me. I am aware of your trouble. I want to
take work from you keeping that in mind. Take the vows
only when you think it right to do so. I shall arrange to have
the meeting with your father in good time. The rest from my
letter to Maganlal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 11009. Courtesy: Suryakant C.
Patel
