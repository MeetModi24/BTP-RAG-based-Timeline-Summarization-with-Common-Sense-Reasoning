441. LETTER TO JAMNA GANDHI
[February 28, 1942]
CHI. JAMNA,
Why are you getting fever? Give up all worries. Come over
here whenever you wish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati:
M.M.U./II.
Also C.W. 8600.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
1 The letter is written below the one to Narandas Gandhi of this date.
