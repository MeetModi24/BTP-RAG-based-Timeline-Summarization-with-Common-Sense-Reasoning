152. LETTER TO SARALADEVI CHOWDHARANI
DELHI,
September 22, 1924
I have your pathetic appeal. I do want to live. I was
imposing on myself 40 days. But on deeper scrutiny I saw I
must impose the minimum consistent with the situation. If God
wants more service through this body He will surely spare it.
The Tribune, 27-9-1924
