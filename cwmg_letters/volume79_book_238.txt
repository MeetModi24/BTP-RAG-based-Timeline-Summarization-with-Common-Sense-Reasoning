238. LETTER TO BABA MOGHE
SEVAGRAM,
February 18, 1945
BHAI BABA,
I have just read your letter. In my view your decision is
faulty. In my view it would be wrong for you to go to Khan-
desh now. Your duty is to improve your body. If the body
is the temple of God it is sacred. Spinning should not be taken
literally; its implications should be understood. This is my view.
If this does not appeal to you, you are entitled to your view.
What I said on your return was only a joke.
Blessings from
BAPU
C/O PRABHAKAR
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9020
