49. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
[Before June 22, 1911]
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
Well, I was the first to have sent a letter. The Rabbi was all the
better for having neglected the forage bag. Mrs. Gandhi was very much
upset to think that Manilal should not get the biscuits, etc.
I hope that Big eyes is better as also Devdas.
Have suggested a Coronation picnic at the Farm for the 22nd.
Please see my letter to Ritch. Something ought to be done at the time
of the Coronation.
So long as work is allowed to act upon us by way of intoxication,
so long must depression continue. The best deeds if used as intoxicants
are practically worthless for the satisfaction of the Inner Man. If I
feed a hungry man for show or for satisfying my pride or giving me
a false happiness I gain nothing. The hungry man is certainly fed as
he might be from a mechanical contrivance, say, by pressing a button.
Manilal is splendid.
With love,
UPPER HOUSE
