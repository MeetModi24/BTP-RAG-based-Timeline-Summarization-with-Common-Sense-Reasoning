386. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 9, 1934
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I had your letters but where was the time to reply? Now
that you have reached the Doctor you should no longer be in
much need of my letters. You must have seen my statement .
There is no question of your going to jail now. For the time
being your staying in the Ashram seems best. Later we shall see.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 299
