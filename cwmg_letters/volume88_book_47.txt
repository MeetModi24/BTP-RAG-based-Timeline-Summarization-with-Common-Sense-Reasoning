47. LETTER TO JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
NEW DELHI,
June 4, 1947
DEAR DR. HOLMES1,
This will be presented to you by Dr. B. C. Roy, one of
our foremost physicians. Any country would be proud of him.
I am sure that he needs no introduction from me. But he
insisted. Hence this note. I know you will do all you can to
bring him before America.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C. W. 10968. Courtesy: Prof. Roger W. Holmes and
Frances L. Brown
