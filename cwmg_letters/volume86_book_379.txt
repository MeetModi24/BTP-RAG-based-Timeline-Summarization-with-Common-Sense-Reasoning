379. LETTER TO SYED MAHMUD
SRIRAMPUR,
December 31, 1946
BHAI SYED MAHMUD,
I have your letter. From what the Bihar Muslim League
has reported and what is being conveyed to me, I can't make
out the truth. Write to me how many things are false in the
Muslim League report. I have written to Srikrishna Sinha.
I have already received a telegram that he will send the report. But
you send me yours. Also write what the condition is at present.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 5101
