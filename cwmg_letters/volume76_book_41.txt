41. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
SEGAON,
April 13, 1942
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I read your letter addressed to Kishorelal.
You must both stop grieving over the dead. Keep yourselves
immersed in your work there.
Keep away from quarrels.
Let
those who indulge in backbiting do so. Settle the disputes, if
you can. Otherwise keep away from them.
I can well realize even at this distance that your task is
difficult. Carve your way through it. God is our Protector in all
misfortunes. He cannot be seen, but is always with us. If you
cultivate this faith, you will never feel helpless.
Ba is much better. The others also are quite well.
Blessings from
BAPU
