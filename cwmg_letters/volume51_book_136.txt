136. LETTER TO LAKSHMIDAS P. ASAR
September 19, 1932
You may be too old to be my son, but I have always re-
garded you as a wise son ever since you first met me in Amrit-
sar. I, therefore, assume that you have fully understood the
meaning of my fast, and also that, if I pass away, you will
prove yourself a worthy heir. It is because I have this faith in
you that I do not write to you except when necessary.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 49
