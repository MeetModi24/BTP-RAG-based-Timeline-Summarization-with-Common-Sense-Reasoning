269. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
PANCHGANI,
July 15, 1944
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
You were not present but Sumati and Jehangirji [Patel] wit-
nessed the beast in me. They forgot that beast and understood
my love. I am tied to you all by the bonds of love. My un-
worthiness prevents me from sending for you. But how long can
I do without you? I am sure you will wash off Sushila's pain
with love.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 844
