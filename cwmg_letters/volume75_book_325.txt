325. LETTER TO ABDUL GHAFFAR KHAN
January 26, 1942
DEAR KHAN SAHEB,
Puri tells me you would like me to appeal for funds for your
work. If he has understood you correctly I would gladly make the
appeal. Will you please confirm the information and tell me how
much is the need?
I hope you had my letter sent to you at the Maulana's in-
stance.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
PS.
Are the teeth giving satisfaction?
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
