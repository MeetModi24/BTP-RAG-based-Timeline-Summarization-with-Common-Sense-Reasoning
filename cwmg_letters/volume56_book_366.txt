366. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI M. THAKKAR
WARDHA,
December 15, 1933
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
Do not worry about the children. Nor about Madhavji.
I have had a discussion about the children. If Madhavji
wants to do what seems to him his dharma, don't stand in his
way. If you wish to come and see me you may come wherever
I am.
Blessings to all sisters from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6824
