245. LETTER TO S. SRINIVASA IYENGAR
SABARMATI,
January 17, 1930
MY DEAR FRIEND,
The prayer bell is just ringing 4 o'clock early morn. Preoccu-
pations compel me to burn after-midnight oil. I can no longer
delay acknowledging your angry letter. Though I have an answer
to everyone of the statements you have made I must restrain
myself. I can only give you my assurance that my affection for
you is no more diminished because of political differences than
for Malaviyaji for the same cause. But this I cannot prove by
words. Future conduct alone can prove the truth of my assurance.
I did not write my letter to hurt your feelings. I wrote in order
to be true to you, a friend and associate, and to myself.
We shall know each other better when the mists have rolled
away.
Meanwhile I anticipate your forgiveness for offence given
utterly unconsciously.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
