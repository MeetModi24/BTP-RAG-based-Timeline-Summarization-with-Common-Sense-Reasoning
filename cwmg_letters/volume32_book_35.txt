35. LETTER TO FELIX VALYE
November 15, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
Mr. Pickthall asks me for an article or a message for your
magazine. I have not a moment to spare for writing an article.
What message shall I send you save to say that my nationalism
is intense internationalism? I am sick of the strife between nations
or religions.
Yours sincerely,
DR. FELIX VALYE
HOTEL RICHMOND
GENEVA
From a photostat: S.N. 19739
