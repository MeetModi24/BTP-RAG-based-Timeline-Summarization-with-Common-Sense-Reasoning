336. LETTER TO KUSUMBEHN DESAI
November 8, 1934
CHI. KUSUM,
I have your letter. You have now accepted some responsibi-
lity at Rajkot. If your health permits and if you can do some
work I shall certainly send for you and give you some teaching
work. But I am worried about your health.
RAJKOT
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
