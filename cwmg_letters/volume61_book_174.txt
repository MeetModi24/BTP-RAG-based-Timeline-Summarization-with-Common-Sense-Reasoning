174. LETTER TO VIPIN D. PATEL
BORSAD,
May 31, 1935
CHI. BABA1,
Manibehn says today is your birthday. What will you do
on this day? Won't you do some act of service? If you wish to
do so, then ask Manibehn what you can do. You will certainly be
growing up. You must also become good.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 163
