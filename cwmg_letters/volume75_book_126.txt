126. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH
November 8, 1941
BHAI PRITHVI SINGH,
I have read the account that you sent me about the opening
ceremony. It was very good. I understand you would not be
able to come before the 20th. When Bhai Devraj arrives I shall
explain a few things.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5654. Also C.W. 2965. Courtesy:
Prithvi Singh
