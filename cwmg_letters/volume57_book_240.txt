240. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
March 1, 1934
CHI. RAMA,
I have your letter. Joshi will soon be released now. You
two, therefore, may discuss and decide the matter.
I will
approve of whatever decision you arrive at. I have written to
Joshi in detail. I thought both of you, mother and daughter, were
quite happy there. I also believe that it can do no good to
continue to stay in a place where one feels ill at ease.
Write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5364. Also Bapuna Patro-7: Shri
Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 299
