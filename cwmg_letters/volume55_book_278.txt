278. LETTER TO M. S. WAJID HUSAIN
June 30, 1933
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for the shoes and the chappals you have sent and
I am glad that although your descent is high you have taken up
the useful calling of a shoe-maker.
Yours sincerely,
M. S. WAJID HUSAIN
SHOES CURIO CHAMBER, VICTORIA STREET, LUCKNOW
