67. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
BORSAD,
June 24, 1931
BHAISHRI FULCHAND,
I have your letter. Your explanation does not absolve you
from blame. My point is that it is wrong to criticize one State
in the territory of another. Such criticism involves violence. You
should think over all that I have said to you from the point of
view of non-violence. If you think that the principle of non-
violence itself is an error, my argument loses all force.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2845. Courtesy: Shardabehn Shah
