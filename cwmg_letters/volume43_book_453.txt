453. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day [On or before June 16, 1930]
CHI. PREMA,
It seems it is your letter the authorities have held back. I
am sure it was entirely innocent. But what can we do? If
every letter were to be received a jail would have no meaning.
Write again.
Blessings from
BAPU
