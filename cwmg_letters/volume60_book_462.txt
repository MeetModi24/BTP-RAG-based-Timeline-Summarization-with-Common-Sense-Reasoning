462. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
March 27, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
You are as good as your word. You have given me a wire,
a postcard and a good letter. Your overlooking Kumarappa's notice
is therefore excused. It was not meant for you. You were no
guest of the Association. You came as a member of the family.
Among the commissions for Shummy please add the medi-
cinal and food value of tamarind if I did not ask you to note it.
As I am making extensive use of it and as there is a supersti-
tion against its use in these parts, I am anxious to have medical
opinion as early as possible.
Heat is daily growing here. It does not worry me. The wet
cloth on the head cools the whole system.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
JULLUNDUR CITY, PUNJAB
From the original: C.W. 3524. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6333
