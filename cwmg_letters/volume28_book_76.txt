76. LETTER TO PROTAP CHANDRA GUHA ROY
[Before September 1, 1925]
I thought you had surrendered already. I was thinking of
you early this morning and thought you had gone without my see-
ing you. I am glad you have come. Do keep well in the jail,
go in for introspection. There is much work to be done if we are
to get swaraj before long. We must achieve power, but that will
not be without enlightened consecration. I know that consecration
through the charkha is the best and the noblest because it is so
selfless. You cannot spin the wheel and not think of the dumb
millions. I know nothing better.
Forward, 1-9-1925
