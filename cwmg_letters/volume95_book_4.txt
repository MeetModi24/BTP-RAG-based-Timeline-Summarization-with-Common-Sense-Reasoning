4. LETTER TO J. X. MERRIMAN
4 VICTORIA STREET,
LONDON, S.W.,
July 16, 1909
DEAR MR. MERRIMAN,
I am much obliged to you for your courteous note.
Of course I have no claim upon your attention, save what your
humanity may prompt you to allow me.
I take it in any case that you will see Lord Ampthill if he writes
to you, and do what you reasonably can.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
THE RIGHT HON. J. X. MERRIMAN
REFORM CLUB
PALL MALL, S.W.
From a photostat: Gandhiji's Letters to Merriman. Courtesy: South African
Library, Cape Town
