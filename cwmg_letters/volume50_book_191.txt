191. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
July 8, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
It is my belief that nobody really knows the origin of the name
Bharatakhanda. But it is said the name Bharatakhanda came from
Bharat, the son of Shakuntala. You need not give any impor-
tance to things like the shaking of legs, etc. In olden times, if
there was knowledge in one direction there was also ignorance
in another.
We may describe the sister as a sahadharmacharini if she is
staying with her brother. But it is customary to use that term
only for a wife. I always tear up the letters.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9953. Courtesy: Sharadabehn G.
Chokhawala
