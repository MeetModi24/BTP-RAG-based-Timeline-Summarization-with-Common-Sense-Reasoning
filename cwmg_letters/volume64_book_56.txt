56. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
November 16, 1936
CHI. PRABHA,
I have no time at all. I have received your letter . You
must obtain permission to come here for improving your health.
If you can, give the accompanying to Jayaprakash. Ba and Lila-
vati have arrived. Kanu has come from Bombay. Manu is
in Delhi. She has not yet come. Vasumati has left today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3486
