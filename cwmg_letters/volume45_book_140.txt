140. LETTER TO JANAKIBEHN BAJAJ
January 18, 1931
CHI. JANAKIBEHN,
Shouldn't I say that you have done me a favour by writing
to me after many days? The task in Calcutta is a difficult one,
but not so for you. Ghanshyamdasji is making a good contribution
through his work.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchve Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 73
