242. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 9, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
I am having long and pleasant chats with J. L. I have a
journalist with me recommended by J. L. He leaves tomorrow.
L. Fischer is his name.
Khurshedbehn is still here. She has been looking after the
guests. Maulana comes tonight.
Here is a letter from Benares. Perhaps you remember the
thing.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4131. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7440
