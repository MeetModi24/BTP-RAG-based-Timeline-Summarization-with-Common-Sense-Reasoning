214. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
October 8, 1932
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
You will get all news about me. I had a letter from Badridutt even before
I got yours. I have wired and even written a letter. The news about
Devakinandan is also painful.
I hope all of you are getting along quite well. These days, I use the
Gandiva spinning-wheel. It is less strenuous to work with. It permits great speed.
BAPU
[PS.]
Ask Hemi to write to me.
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 32985
