27. LETTER TO MANMOHANDAS GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 24, 1930
BHAISHRI MANMOHANDAS,
I have your letter. I have not however received the proofs
of the book. I am making inquiries. I will go through them
when I get them.
Your being my namesake has at least this advantage for me,
that I am saved from a good deal of correspondence.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7
