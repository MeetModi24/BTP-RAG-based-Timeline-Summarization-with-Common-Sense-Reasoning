96. LETTER TO LALITA BOSE
POONA,
November 11, 1945
CHI. LALITA,
If you can't read Hindi, someone will read this out to you.
Why should I write in English?
Did you type the letter? What are you doing?
I am glad you sent the Netaji photographs. They are
beautiful.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI LALITA BOSE
38- ELGIN ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
