240. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
SABARMATI,
January 8, 1930
BA,
I am sending herewith a letter from Ramdas. If I sat down
to write myself, I would have to stop the spinning for that time.
That would be so much loss to the country. Is it not better that
I dictate this letter?
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32579/43
