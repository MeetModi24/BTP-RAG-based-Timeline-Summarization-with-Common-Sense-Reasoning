389. LETTER TO A PUBLISHER
NEW DELHI,
January 13, 1948
Who am I to write a foreword for the autobiography of a
celebrity like sage Romain Rolland who, alas, is no more among
us? I consider myself unfit for the task. What is more, I have
not even had a moment to read the volume.
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C. W. 10590. Courtesy: Madeleine Rolland. Also Romain
Rolland and Gandhi Correspondence, p. 352
