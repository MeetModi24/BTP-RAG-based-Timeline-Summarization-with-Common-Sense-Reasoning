94. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 2, 1945
DEAR ICE,
I love your Hindustani. You should go on writing like this.
Leave space at the top and enough margin.
Choose the leprosy patients [for your treatment] and keep them
somewhere in Sevagram. I shall manage the expense for both
milk and ice needed for that. A big thing would be accomplished
if Hari-ichchha gets well after some time. Try to learn the art
of making ice in Sevagram. When will your [equipment] for
making distilled water come? Balkrishna's . . .1 to the Vaidya . . .2
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 5875
