217. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN CHOUDHRI
MIRZAPUR,
November 19, 1929
BHAI RAMNARAYAN,
I got your letter. I had forgotten about ghee. There was
no need to abstain from ghee in my presence. Give up the restric-
tion and take the required quantity of ghee. You must build up
your body. My train will reach Ajmer on the 25th morning. It
will be my day of silence.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapu: Maine Kya Dekha, Kya Samjha?
