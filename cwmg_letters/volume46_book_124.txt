124. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
BORSAD,
May 7, 1931
MY DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
This letter is for your information. If you have anything
to say you will please guide me.
Yours sincerely,
GANDHI
Encl. 1
SJT. JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI
A.I.C.C. File No. 273, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
