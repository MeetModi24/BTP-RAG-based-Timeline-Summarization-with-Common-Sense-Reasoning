144. LETTER TO NIRMALA H. DESAI
Silence Day [On or after February 15, 1932]
CHI. NIRMALA (AUNT),
What sort of a girl you are that you complain of absence of
letters from me while you yourself do not write. Did Durga con-
vey my message to Mahadev? How is she? Ask her to write
to me. How is Bablo ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9461
