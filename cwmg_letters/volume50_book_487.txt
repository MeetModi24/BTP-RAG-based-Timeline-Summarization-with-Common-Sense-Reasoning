487. LETTER TO MATHURI N. KHARE
August 31, 1932
CHI. MATHURI,
I got your letter. Do you remember that I had advised you
to do only as much work as you could? Your speed in carding
is certainly good. But is the quality of slivers as good as the
speed? Whatever you do should be neat and well done, no
matter if the speed is slow. Speed will increase slowly.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 267. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn
N. Khare
