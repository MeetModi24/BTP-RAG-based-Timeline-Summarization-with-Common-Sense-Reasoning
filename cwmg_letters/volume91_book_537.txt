537. LETTER TO V. L. PHADKE
Ashwin Vad 14 [November 4, 1926]
BHAISHRI MAMA,
I have your letter. I can have nothing to say as long as
you stick on there.
I shall start writing on what must be done to remove un-
touchability. Your view does not tally with our idea of asking
for contributions from Antyaja friends. Others may well serve in
that way. Friends expect different from me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 3815
