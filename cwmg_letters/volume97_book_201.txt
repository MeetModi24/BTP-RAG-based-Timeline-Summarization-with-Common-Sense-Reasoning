201. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
April 18, 1932
CHI. NIMU,
I received your letter. Ramdas has sent on to me the letter you wrote to
him. I have written to Narandas and asked him to call you over to the
Ashram. Probably the children will get all right there. Moreover, in the
Ashram, there are also such experienced women as Lakshmibehn, etc. As a
general rule, it is best to give no medicine to children. If you are careful
about what they eat, they will not fall ill. If they do not pass stools with
ease, you may give them a little quantity of castor oil. It will be good if you
write to me regularly every week. There is no reason for you to feel lost.
Blessings from
BAPU
