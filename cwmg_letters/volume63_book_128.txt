128. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM BAVISHI
WARDHA,
June 29, 1936
BHAI BAVISHI,
Unless I write this postcard, writing to you may be de-
layed. God will surely lead you to where your mind is fixed.
You will find no one to advance you money until your experi-
ments succeed. The Bhangi quarter incident is surprising.
To you both,
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 126. Also C.W. 4751. Courtesy:
Purushottam Bavishi
