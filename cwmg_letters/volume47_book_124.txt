124. LETTER TO PADMA
BORSAD,
Silence Day [July 3, 1931]
CHI. PADMA,
I got both your letters. In one you ask for coloured paper
and in the other a little piece from your letter paper. Why do
you fall ill repeatedly? Can you not discover the cause?
Are there any irregularities in your diet? Let me know
when you get a reply from the U.P.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6126
