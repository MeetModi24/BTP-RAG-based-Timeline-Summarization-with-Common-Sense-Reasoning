32. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
August 10, 1941
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Your letter has just arrived. Radha's letter too arrived at the same
time. I am sending it to you. I think it would not be good to make
her give up the job she has got. You may disregard the money. You
may disregard experience. But you may not disregard the loss of
reputation. Is it Radha's medical knowledge you need or just her
company? If you want only the company, why not have Bindu ?
Or should I find somebody else ? It is embarrassing that just when
you expressed a desire to call her, Radha finds herself in an awkward
situation.
Blessings from
BAPU
