360. LETTER TO SUDHIR GHOSH
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 30, 1945
MY DEAR SUDHIR,
I have your interesting letter.
I am in Panchgani till the
end of June. Dr. John Everton will certainly be able to see me
in Panchgani during June, any afternoon. Of course I am silent
but that should not matter. Of course he will give me previous
information.
Love to you both.
BAPU
SHRI SUDHIR GHOSH
1 UPPER WOOD STREET
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
