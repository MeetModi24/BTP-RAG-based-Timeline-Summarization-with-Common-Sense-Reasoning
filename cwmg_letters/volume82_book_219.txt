219. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
December 3, 1945
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
You have fallen ill. It should not have happened. What
you say is correct. Do what you think is proper. It will be all
right if you go to different villages.
Do not worry about me. God will keep me fit as long as
He will. You may come whenever you get an opportunity. You
had better not come if there is no particular business.
There is nothing more to write. I am writing this at night.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 540
