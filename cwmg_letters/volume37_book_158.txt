158. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
August 4, 1928
MY DEAR BHRRR, l
It is not for me to like or dislike. When all are locked up,
things will take their own course. But more when we meet.
Please tell Raihana I began Gopi's diary and had to leave it to
come to Bardoli.
Yours,
BHRRR
From a photostat: S.N. 9564
