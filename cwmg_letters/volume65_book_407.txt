407. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
June 29, 1937
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I have your letter. I do not think it is necessary for you
to leave the work there as long as you are mentally satisfied
and also something is accomplished. I have suggested your
leaving Nepal only when you are no longer interested in the work
there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6552
