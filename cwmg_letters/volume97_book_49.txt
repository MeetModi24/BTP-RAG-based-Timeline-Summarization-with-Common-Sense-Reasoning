49. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
DELHI,
[September 18, 1924]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
Yesterday I wrote to you all in a letter addressed to Ba. Today I am
writing to all in a letter addressed to you. I want that we should think about
what steps to take to save our dharma. This is the time of our trial. What
does it matter if I am alive or dead. You should remain engrossed in your
work. Do not worry but keep thinking about my work. I am learning as well
as teaching the lesson of ahimsa. Love abounds around me. I can therefore
put up with . . .2 by resorting to a fast. I am very happy to know that
Balkrishna has arrived there. My only woe is that I am not there.
I do not know by heart Draupadi's prayer "Krishna Govind" but it is
very much in my heart. I have now earned enough merit to be able to say
that prayer. At the time of trouble, devotion and worship is prayer. Without
devotion, there is no worship and without worship, there is no devotion.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32920
