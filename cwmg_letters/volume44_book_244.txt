244. LETTER TO PANNALAL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 27, 1930
CHI. PANNALAL,
I have your letter. I hope you know that some persons in
Wardha can spin on the takli 200 rounds an hour. I was amazed
to read this. Write to someone in Wardha and find out how one
may acquire such speed. Chhotelal did give me the details.
Don't let despair come near you. It means lack of faith in
God. Faith in God means hope. How is your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3105
