56. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 21, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your letter. You can't undermine your body because someone
else's is much worse than yours. You can try to bring your weak-
bodied neighbour to your strength. The same thing applies to
one's house. No doubt you have to give up superfluities as you
would superfluous fat or even muscle.
The day is full.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4104. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7413
