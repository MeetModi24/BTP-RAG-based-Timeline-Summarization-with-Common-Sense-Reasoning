11. LETTER TO SIR MAHOMED HABIBULLAH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 3, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I knew Sjt. Sastri's suggestion. I do
think that the South African journalists' coming to India is likely
to result in a better understanding.
Yours sincerely,
SIR MAHOMED HABIBULLAH SAHEB BAHADUR, K.C.I.E.
MEMBER, VICEROY'S COUNCIL, NEW DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 11997
