233. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
BOMBAY,
August 20, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
You were in my thoughts on the journey. Do not force
yourself to do anything. Work done willingly gives satisfaction
and peace. It is enough if we perform our dharma whether
Sharda stays or leaves. Remain cheerful and look after your
health.
Blessings from
BAPU
