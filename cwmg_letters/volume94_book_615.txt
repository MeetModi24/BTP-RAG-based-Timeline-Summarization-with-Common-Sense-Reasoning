615. LETTER TO PYARELAL
February 13, 1947
CHI. PYARELAL,
Do whatever you can about Shahpur. The task is very difficult.
But it has to be so. Manu's lesson is as short as it should be. The
handwriting is not big and beautiful. There is also shortage of space.
It should not be so. Today this much is enough.
Blessings from
BAPU
