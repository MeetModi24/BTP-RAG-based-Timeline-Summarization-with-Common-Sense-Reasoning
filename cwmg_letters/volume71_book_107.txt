107. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
January 6, 1940
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. It is enough for me that you reached there
safe. We shall meet again when God wills. Go on doing your work
without worrying. How is Rajendra Babu's health? Sushila has left.
Rajkumari has arrived but will go to Madras on the 9th and return
after the 15th. Ba is well enough. I am of course all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3540
