270. LETTER TO JAMNA GANDHI
Silence Day [Before April 28, 1935]
CHI. JAMNA,
I have your letter. How did your health take a bad turn once again? Did
the Punjabi vaidya's treatment fail? I hope you have gone to Rajkot and that
your health has improved.
Purshottam's letter is such that instead of being unhappy by it, we should
be happy. He should get the blessings of us all in his happy decision.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33764
1 Vide fn 1 to the preceding item.
2 In his letter to addressee's son Purshottam, dated April 18, 1935, Gandhiji says
"May you live long and may your aspirations be fulfilled" and approves of his plan of
going to Chorwad in Saurashtra; vide Vol. LX, p. 431. Then on April 28 of the same
year, he writes to Purshottam's wife Vijaya at her Chorwad address. Evidently by that
date Purshottam and Vijaya had got married. The reference to his "happy decision",
presumably the decision to marry, suggests that this was written before his marriage to
Vijaya.
