183. LETTER TO ACHYUTANAND PUROHIT
WARDHA,
December 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter as also your telegram. I have not said
anything to you because I am unable to fix the date. It will be
somewhere between the 20th and 23rd instant.
There will be besides my wife three or four with me about whom
you need not worry to make any special arrangements. They will
stay where you put me up. No special arrangements are necessary
for me either. I would like you to save every pice you can of
the funds that you may collect. You need not send for any fruit
for me. Ordinary simple food will do. All that you may ar-
range to have ready is two pounds of goat's milk. One thing
I do need is a commode kept in a clean place. You will please
not send for any fruit from Calcutta.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 13009
