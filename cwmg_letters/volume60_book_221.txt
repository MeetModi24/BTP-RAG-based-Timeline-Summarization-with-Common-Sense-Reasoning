221. LETTER TO P. G. MATHEW
February 5, 1935
MY DEAR MATHEW,
I had already told you that you should not do anything
at Thana. You are not built for any physical work. I wish you
would stick to the family and serve it by getting an employment,
however humble it may be.
SHRI P. G. MATHEW
CHEPPAD
HARIPAD (TRAVANCORE)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
