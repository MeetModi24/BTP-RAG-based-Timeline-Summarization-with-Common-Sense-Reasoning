484. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI A. MEHTA
September 9, 1933
BHAISHRI BHAGWANJI,
You have done well in going to Rangoon. I hope your trip
will bear fruit and there will be complete understanding among
the brothers.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5818. Also C.W. 3041. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
