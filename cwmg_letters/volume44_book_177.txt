177. LETTER TO KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 6, 1930
CHI. KAMALNAYAN,
I have your letter. It is well written. If you have enough
work there I do not see any need of your going to Ajmer. But
someone must go if the need in Ajmer is urgent. It is difficult
to give a decisive opinion from here. What has Mother to say?
Only the leader of the batch can take a policy decision. Ask
Surendraji who is there these days.
As yet it is well-nigh impossible for me to write letters in
Marathi. I get very little time even to read. Tell Janakibehn
to write to us.
Blessings from Kakasaheb.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, pp. 276-
