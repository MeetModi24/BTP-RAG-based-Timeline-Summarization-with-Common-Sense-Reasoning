414. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
POONA,
September 9, 1945
CHI. KISHORELAL,
I have your letter and also the telegram. Sushila left in the
morning and I think she will leave for [Sevagram] today itself.
I can't stop her from going even if I want to. Moreover, I want
her to go and look you up. Your letter suggests that quarrels
are still going on there. Your blood-pressure is lower than it
should be. I should not worry but I am not happy about it.
That is why it is good that Sushila is going there. I have faith
in her competence.
Blessings from
BAPU
