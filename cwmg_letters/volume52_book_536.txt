536. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
January 8, 1933
CHI. PREMA,
Don't be altogether childish.
Do not forget that you have two duties. One duty is to pour
out your heart to me. But one cannot do that duty mechanically.
If the spring in your heart has dried up, what can you do? The
other duty is to give a report of your work. That certainly
can be done mechanically. Do that duty at any rate.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10321
