14. LETTER TO SARASVANI
January 11, 1933
MY DEAR SARASVANI,
I like your letter. You should accept my assurance that there
is no intention whatsoever of wounding the feelings of orthodox
Hindus. No temple will be touched where the majority of orthodox
Hindus are opposed to the entry of Harijans into the temple which
they are entitled to visit and which is in their neighbourhood.
Yours sincerely,
MISS SARASVANI
C/O SJT. K. V. RAMASWAMY AIYAR
CHOKKIKULAM, MADURA
