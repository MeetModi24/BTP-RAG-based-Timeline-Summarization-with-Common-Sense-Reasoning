336. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 4, 1941
CHI. AMBUJAM1,
I got your letter and the book. The book is fine. It is
good you are continuing Hindi and spinning. You have to give
up grief. Leave all joys and sorrows to God. Ranganayaki is
all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Ambujammal Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memo-
rial Museum and Library. Also C.W. 9616. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
