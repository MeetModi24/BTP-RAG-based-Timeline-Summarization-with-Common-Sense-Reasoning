61. LETTER TO BISHVA NATH GUPTA
September 22, 1934
MY DEAR BISHVA NATH,
I received only today your letter addressed to Deenabandhu
Andrews. He had forgotten all about it. I do not know whe-
ther your wife has met the expected fate, or whether she is still
lingering.
I shall be in Wardha till the middle of October. You are
at liberty, therefore, to come to Wardha between now and
then. I do hope that you are now in comparatively greater peace
than when you wrote your letter to the Deenabandhu.
SHRI BISHVA NATH GUPTA
KUCHA MAIDAN
DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
