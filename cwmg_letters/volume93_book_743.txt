743. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C. P.,
May 13, 1941
CHI. LILA,
You were supposed to write to me daily. Would it be
futile to hope for this from you?
Among the fruit you have sent, the apples are no good.
Half of them are rotten. Who bought them? Surely Janakibehn
would not have had them sent if they were not good?
Termites had invaded your clock. A. S. has removed the
termites and put the clock in your suitcase after cleaning. Did
you leave your suitcase open?
What about tea?
Blessings from
BAPU
