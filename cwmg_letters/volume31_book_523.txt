523. LETTER TO R. GANGADHARAN
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 7, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I would like you to look at the problem
in another way. The constitution of the sexes is the . . .1 tempta-
tion that nature has put in our way. If we succumb to it, we
remain rooted to the earth. If we surmount it, we rise higher.
The tongue is given to us to taste and to speak. But the more
we restrain it the better we are, and so with most things in nature.
It would be wrong, therefore, to say that it is a fetish of nature's
law to restrain our carnal aptitude.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. GANGADHARAN
THOPPIKANILAKAM
VAIKOM
From a photostat: S.N. 19717
