116. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
July 27, 1928
DEAR SISTER,
What shall I say to you? We were all prepared for Nikhil's
passing away. He is relieved of his pain. He was such a wise and
sober lad that he must have attained divine life. Satis Babu has
amazed us all by his absolute calm. I expect the same calm from
you all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1659
