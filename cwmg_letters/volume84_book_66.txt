66. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA BHAVE
April 24, 1946
CHI. BALKRISHNA1,
Take whatever steps may be necessary regarding your need
for milk. If you can keep a cow for that purpose, that of course
will be the best way.
How is your health? It seems you are experiencing difficulty
in getting land there. Discuss the matter with Bhai Bhansali and
others.
Other things seem to be going on well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati ; C. W. 816. Courtesy; Balkrishna Bhave
