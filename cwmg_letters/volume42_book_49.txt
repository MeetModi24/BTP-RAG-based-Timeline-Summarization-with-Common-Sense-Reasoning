49. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
October 23, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I have your letter. Read the letters to Ishwarlal and Dwarka-
nath and hand them over. Now I need write nothing to you
about them.
My consent for your visit to Lahore was conveyed in a
previous letter. As, however, you have requested a reply by wire,
I am sending one.
I understand what you say about signatures. When you can
create confidence in Narandas, he will certainly work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5465
