434. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS
October 31, 1947
CHI. RAMESHWARDAS,
Your letter must have been received. Sushilabehn probably
forgot about it. Now she is at Wardha. She will come after
some time. Vinoba's father passed away. It is as it should be.
Have not all of us to go? The person who gave away his sons
for the service of others was indeed a great man. Let us learn
from him the lesson of sacrifice.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 170
