94. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 20, 1928
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
Your two letters.
It does not matter if you have sent nothing for Bardoli.
Enough contributions are pouring in.
If I am hard up I shall
certainly bother you. There is now little possibility of an agree-
ment.
It is all right if it comes about and none the worse if it
does not. The reins of satyagraha rest in the hands of God alone.
Vallabhbhai is here today.
I shall write again in Navajivan on the boycott.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From Hindi: C.W. 6162. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
