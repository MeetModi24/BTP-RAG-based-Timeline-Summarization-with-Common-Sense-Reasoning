73. LETTER TO J. W. PETAVAL
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
November 24, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. In order to know something about your
activity as distinguished from your writings, I asked a kind friend
in Calcutta to see your place. The enclosed is an extract from his
letter. You, will, therefore, see that I am in my own way trying
to study your movement. Let me feel my way to it in the manner
I know.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19748
