92. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
[1928]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
Improve your health. If there is anything more to be written
about Sharada, do write. Discuss things with her.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro - 6 : Gangabehnne, p. 17; also G.N. 11264
