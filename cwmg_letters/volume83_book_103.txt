103. LETTER TO VEENA
SEVAGRAM,
February 8, 1946
CHI. VEENA 1 ,
I got your letter as also the previous one. How can I find
time to write while I am constantly on the move? It is good
news that you are keeping well.
Your parents arrived here yesterday. Everyone is fine. Why
are your envelopes marked 'Private' ?
It would be good, if you could come to Bardoli. I shall
try to leave here on the 17th and reach Poona on the 19th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
