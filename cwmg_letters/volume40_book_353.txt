353. LETTER TO GANGADEVI SANADHYA
May 11, 1929
CHI. GANGADEVI,
I have your letter and that of Totaramji. It is good that
Totaramji's eyes are all right. He should see that they are not
spoiled again.
Do not leave off sun-baths. I am confident that your pain
can be cured—at any rate controlled—by nature-cure, never with
drugs. Your diet should be simple and not too much. Keep
physical exertion to the minimum.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2546
