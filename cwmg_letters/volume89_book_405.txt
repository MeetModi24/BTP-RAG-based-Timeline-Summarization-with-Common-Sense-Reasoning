405. LETTER TO EDITH HUNTER
NEW DELHI,
October 27, 1947
DEAR MRS. HUNTER,
Dr. Tamhankar told me yesterday that your husband had
gone to his rest. Death I regard as a friend. Hence when a dear
one leaves us, there is no cause for regret. May this knowledge
of the function of Death sustain you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MRS. EDITH HUNTER
C/O KERNE HILL ROAD
LONDON S. E. 21
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
