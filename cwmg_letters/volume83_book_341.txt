341. LETTER TO SARDUL SINGH CAVEESHAR
POONA,
March 19, 1946
BHAI CAVEESHAR,
I have your letter of March 3. It pains me that you start
writing without thinking. I feel, it is not worth pointing out the
mistakes in your letter as well as in the statement.
SARDUL SINGH CAVEESHAR
LAHoRE
