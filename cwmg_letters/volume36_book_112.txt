112. LETTER TO RAMI GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 10, 1928
CHI. RAMI,
So this time I did get a letter from you. I hope all of you
are keeping well. Chi. Kumi is arriving tomorrow. Tulsidas is
going to Bombay. Chi. Devdas has gone to Delhi. I am keeping
well. Give up the habit of beating children and getting angry with
them. You can take a lot of work from them by amusing them.
Food habits should be regular.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9708
