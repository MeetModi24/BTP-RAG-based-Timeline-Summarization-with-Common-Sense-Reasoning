618. LETTER TO GANGA P. BHATT
NEW DELHI,
October 17, 1946
CHI. GANGA,
I had your earlier letter. If you have to follow Purushottam
it is no more than your dharma. You ought now to give up
your hankering after social service. Purushottam has some kink
somewhere but I cannot place it. He ought to withdraw from
some field of work or other. He alone can know best whether
his body will stand up to it, whether he can do as much as this.
If he cannot, you must give up your attachment [for social
service], but this too appears difficult. You may therefore go
on living as seem best to you. I am held up here.
To you both,
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. GANGABEHN PURUSHOTTAM
29 BHAWANI PETH
POONA 2
