565. LETTER TO PREMLILA THACKERSEY
January 10, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
There is no need for pomegranates for the time being. Sardar
has stopped eating them for some time. I will not fail to write
to you and ask you to send them when they are needed.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: C.W. 4827. Courtesy: Prem-
lila Thakersey
