420. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
December 25, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
I got your letter. I am glad that you have improved,
even if it was with the help of medicine. What we want is that
you should improve, no matter how. Write to Manu from time
to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9636. Courtesy: Ravindra R.
Patel
