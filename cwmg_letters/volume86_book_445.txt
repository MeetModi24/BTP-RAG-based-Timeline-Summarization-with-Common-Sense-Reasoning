445. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM AND ABHA GANDHI
January 11, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
Day by day try to get better and when the time comes to
die, die with a healthy body. If you don't get rid of the cough
and fever it will not be a sacrifice of the purest type. But what
can you do in this? Let it be as God wills.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. ABHA,
Continue making progress. I am sending the tooth-powder.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 559
