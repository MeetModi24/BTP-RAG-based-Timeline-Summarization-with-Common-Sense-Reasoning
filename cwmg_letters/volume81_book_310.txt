310. LETTER TO KISHANDAS BEGRAJ
August 29, 1945
BHAI KISHANDAS,
I have your letter. You do not understand a joke. I have
often said that the journalists deserve to be shot. The beauty
of it is that no one was shot nor did anyone imagine he would
be shot. They took my remarks in good humour. The camera-
man handed [ the camera] to an old man like me and then took
it back. I took it from him deliberately. I did well in doing
so. Both gestures were non-violent.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KISHANDAS BEGRAJ
C/O NEW ASIATIC INSURANCE
18 HENAM MANSION
MAHATMA GANDHI ROAD
KARACHI
