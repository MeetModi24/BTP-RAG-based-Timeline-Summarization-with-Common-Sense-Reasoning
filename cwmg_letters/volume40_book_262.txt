262. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
April 24, 1929
BHAISHRI FULCHAND,
I got your letter. You yourself should tell Nanabhai what
you wish to. Do not draw away Venilal and thus obstruct
Nanabhai's work.
I shall have nothing to say if Nanabhai can
spare him.
Have you returned in good health? Did you go to Abyssinia?
If you had any experiences that I should know about, write
to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2860. Courtesy: Shardabehn Shah
