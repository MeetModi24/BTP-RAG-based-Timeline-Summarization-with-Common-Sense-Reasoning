9. LETTER TO MANIBEHN N. PARIKH
June 3, 1932
CHI. MANI 3 ,
Patience is learnt through long effort. The secret of regard-
ing sorrow as joy is learnt by leaving everything to God. It is
for this very reason that those three verses have been included in
the prayer for women. I hope you do remember these verses.
Through effort, one can learn to bear suffering.
BAPU
