14. LETTER TO THOMAS COOK & SON
January 5, 1945
Ref. Your letter No. TT/CB2708 (Foreign Exchange Dept.),
dated 11-12-1944
DEAR SIRS,
With reference to your above-mentioned letter enclosing a re-
ceipt in duplicate of even date for Rs. 3,000 in my favour, I have
to inform you that I have authorized Messrs Bachhraj & Co.,
Ltd., Bombay, to collect the amount from you.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
MESSRS THOS. COOK & SON, LTD.
P. O. Box No. 46
BOMBAY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
