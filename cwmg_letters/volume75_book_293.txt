293. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
January 11, 1942
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Herewith a letter for Kanta. I like your observing silence. I
will not ask you to give it up. You may, however, give it up, if
you like, for the sake of serving others or even without any reason.
I would like it if Lakshmidas or someone like him works with
you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1942
