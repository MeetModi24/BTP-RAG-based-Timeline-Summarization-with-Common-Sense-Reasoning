292. LETTER TO MAHATMA MUNSHIRAM
BETTIAH,
Vaishakh Shukla 5 [April 26, 1917]
MAHATMAJI,
I was very glad to receive your letter. The new name you
have adopted is really most appropriate.
The work here is enormous. Tyranny, by God's grace, will
end. But I shall certainly have to stay over here for four to six
months. Babu Brajkishore Prasad and others who are helping
me are all worthy people.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 2209
