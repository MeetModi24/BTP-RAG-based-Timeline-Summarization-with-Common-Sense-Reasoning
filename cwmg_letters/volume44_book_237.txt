237. LETTER TO ABBAS
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 22, 1930
CHI. ABBAS,
I got your letter. What you have said about the mal is clear
enough, but I think I shall understand it fully only when I read
the letter a second time. I have preserved the letter. Describe
to me your experiences in jail. What did you read, how much did
you spin and card and in what spirit did you observe the prison
rules? How was your health? If you have any views to express
about the method of carding which Mathuradasbhai is persuading
people to adopt, please do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6303
