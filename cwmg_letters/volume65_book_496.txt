496. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
July 20, 1937
CHI. PREMA,
How useless? I got your letter of the 16th today, the
20th, at 11. It is ekadashi today. How can I send my blessings
so that they should reach you on the dashami ?1 You must have
got my previous letter. What shall I say to you? You have
my blessings, of course. Continue to grow and be victorious.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10392. Also C.W. 6831. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
