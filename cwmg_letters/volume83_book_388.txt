388. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI
March 28, 1946
CHI. MAGANBHAI,
You eat more uncooked vegetables than you should. A
couple of tolas is more than enough. Increase the quantity of
milk, curds, ghee. Vallabhram Vaidya is there to help you to get
rid of the impurities in your blood; take lohabhasma in consulta-
tion with him. Don't think there is harm in it. If the body is
deficient in iron, you should take it from other sources as may
be proper and regain your strength. Do get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
