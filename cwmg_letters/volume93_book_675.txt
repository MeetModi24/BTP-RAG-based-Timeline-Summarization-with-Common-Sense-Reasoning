675. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
February 23, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have received your letter. If you cannot keep Tari there,
I am afraid that it will not be possible for her to maintain her
health here. What happened about Tari being put up in the
Harijan Ashram? But I do not wish to burden you. Do what
you think fit. I shall see what can be done. Satya has my
blessings. May her new journey be successful. I am of course
looking after my health. I do very little work at night. I try
to go to bed as soon as the prayers are over.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
