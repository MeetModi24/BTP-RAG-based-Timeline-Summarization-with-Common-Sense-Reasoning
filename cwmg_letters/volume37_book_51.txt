51. LETTER TO B. M. TWEEDLE
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 11, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter and the packet of pencils. You have
correctly guessed when you say that I might not approve of your
method of raising funds for clearing your debt . It would seem
to me to be easier to make a direct appeal for a donation than
to sell a particular thing at double or treble its cost price. In
either case an appeal to one's charitable instincts has got to be
made. Why then mix it up with the selfish instinct? But I may
not argue.
As required by you I am reposting the box.
Yours sincerely,
MISS B. M. TWEEDLE
WESLEYAN MISSION VILLAGE INDUSTRY
TRIVELLORE, CHINGLEPUT DISTRICT, SOUTH INDIA
From a microfilm: S.N. 13472
