56. LETTER TO RAJARAM R. BHOLE
WARDHA,
August 18, 1935
DEAR RAJARAM,
I have your p. c. If you feel that your health will permit,
I have no objection to your finishing your course in college. But
you will come to a decision after seeing Dr. Ambedkar.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
SHRI RAJARAM BHOLE
C/O K. D. BANKER, ESQ.
EMPIRE OF INDIA LIFE HOUSE
WALKER ROAD, NAGPUR
From the original: R. R. Bhole Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
