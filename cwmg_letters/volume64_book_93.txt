93. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
November 26, 1936
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
You wrote to me after a long time.
I have followed what
you say about your activity. Now stick to what you have
taken up. Improve your health. For that you should be free
from mental tension.
A letter for Balram is enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 391. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
