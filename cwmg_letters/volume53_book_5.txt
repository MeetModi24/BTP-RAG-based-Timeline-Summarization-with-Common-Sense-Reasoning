5. LETTER TO C. Y. CHINTAMANI
January 11, 1933
DEAR MR. CHINTAMANI 2 ,
I have your letter. No excuse necessary for dictating your
letter. I am not so vain as to think that I am the only hard-
worked and hard-working man in all India.
The pamphlet about 'Fasts' is being sent herewith. You can
keep it as long as you like, and keep it altogether, if it is of use to
you.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. C. Y. CHINTAMANI
17 HAMILTON ROAD, ALLAHABAD
From a photostat: S.N. 19227
