663. LETTER TO AMINA QURESHI
April 2, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER AMINA,
Keep up the courage you have displayed. You are bound to
get over the craving for chillies, etc. If you wish to have your
body shining like gold and to devote yourself to service, you
398
have to overcome that craving. There is no need at all to rub
the teeth with tobacco. In place of it, use charcoal dust strained
through fine cloth and mixed with salt. Use a neem or babul
twig as brush. Since you have progressed so far, get rid of all
weak habits. But do only as much as your strength permits. If
one attempts such things out of regard for anybody one may be
led into untruth and false show. Let them never come near you.
Keep writing to me.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 10796. Courtesy: Gulam
Rasul Qureshi
