62. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 3, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I have not had a moment for writing to you. I gave you a
wire about Dhami. Jawaharlal is already on the war path.
Of course, you will bring the cooker and everything else you
may need. But why cooker? You are going to take your meals
with me as before. The delay is unfortunate. It is not only the
work for which you are coming. However don't delay any longer.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3936. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7245
