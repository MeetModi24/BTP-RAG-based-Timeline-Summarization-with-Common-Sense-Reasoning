183. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL V. SHAH
June 21, 1941
BHAI CHIMANLAL,
Received your letter. I find your article entirely innocent.
I do not like that again and again you have to submit to the
censors.
But the newspapers have learnt the art of survival,
of which I am completely ignorant. Therefore I cannot be of
much help. Munshiji is there. Therefore I would ask you to
act only according to his advice.
If he has to ask anything
about you he will ask me. I do not want to give my indepen-
dent view when he is there. Show him this letter too and be
guided by his advice.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
