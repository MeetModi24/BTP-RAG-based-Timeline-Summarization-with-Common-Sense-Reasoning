492. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI PATEL
October 26, 1932
CHI. DAHYABHAI1,
Probably you will now get regular letters from Manibehn
also. That means you will get more literature to read or have
read out to you. And as you are allowed to do more reading, the
time is drawing near when you will also be permitted to leave the
bed. But don't be impatient for that. I am sure you know that it
is possible for one to serve even from the sick-bed.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DAHYABHAI PATEL
RAM NIVAS
PAREKH STREET, BOMBAY-
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 149
1 Son of Vallabhbhai Patel
