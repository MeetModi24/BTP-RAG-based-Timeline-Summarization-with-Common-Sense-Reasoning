183. LETTER TO DR. A. U. KAZI
May 25, 1942
BHAI KAZI,
I have your letter. I remember you quite well. I can cer-
tainly take work from you but, I suppose, your requirements
would be considerable.
Please let me know what they are.
Can you stay in Ahmedabad or would you like to be with me
at Sevagram?
Blessings from
BAPU
