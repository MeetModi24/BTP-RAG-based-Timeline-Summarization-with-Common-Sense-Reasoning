72. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
December 20, 1925
This is my last letter to you before we meet at Cawnpore
where a mere woman displaces a mere man. May your words
come out of purity, may you adorn Indian womanhood and
Hinduism. May your words be as balm to the Hindu-Muslim
wound. You are too great to notice the childish display of unchi-
valrous incivility.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
