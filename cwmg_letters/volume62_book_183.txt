183. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
December 3, 1935
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
Your letter. I have forgotten the letters connected with the
swadeshi industry. You can do what seems fit to you. It
would not be proper to send suggestions from here.
I had given an appointment to the sadhu but refused to
keep it as he desired me to go to his place. We could certain-
ly meet on my coming to Delhi if he wants it.
You are keeping well, I hope.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2430
