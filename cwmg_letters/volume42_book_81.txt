81. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
ASAURA,
October 30, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I received your letter from Chalala also only yesterday. That
is to say, letters dated the 21st and the 27th were also received
yesterday. Sometimes it happens like that. I see from your
letter that you were unable to write all the things you had in
mind and so please make a note of all the points you wanted to tell
68
me about before you come to see me. It is really very good that
your health has improved because of your walks. It is indeed a
very good practice to have a change of air while doing one's work.
It was really good that Lilabehn left. She badly needed a
change of atmosphere mentally.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 15828
