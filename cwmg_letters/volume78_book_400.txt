400. LETTER TO SARASWATI K. GANDHI
November 13, 1944
CHI. SURU,
Received your letter. Kanti has written me a nice and
long letter. May you both study well, continue making progress
and rendering service. Of course I do hope to see Shanti . I will
some day. How do I know that you are not as silly as you used
to be?
Blessings to the three of you from
BAPU
SHRI KANTI GANDHI
2994/ VANI VILIA MOHOLLA
MYSORE
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 3456. Courtesy: Kanti Gandhi.
Also G.N. 6182
