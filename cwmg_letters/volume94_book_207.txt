207. LETTER TO BRIJMOHAN BIRLA
PALM BUN, JUHU,
June 1, 1944
DEAR BRIJMOHANJI,
I have your letter of 29th ult. Bapuji would like you to wait till you
hear from him. He proposes to write to Shri Khabardar 1 himself and will then
let you know.
Yours sincerely,
SETH
BRIJMOHAN BIRLA
8 ROYAL EXCHANGE PLACE
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library. Courtesy:
Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
