421. LETTER TO ANANTRAI P. PATTANI
NEW DELHI,
October 28, 1947
BHAI ANANTRAI,
The Sardar has done what was necessary in connection with
your wire.
Please read the reverse side of the enclosed letter from Jaisukh-
lal and do what needs to be done.
I hope you are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
ANANTRAI PATTANI
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
