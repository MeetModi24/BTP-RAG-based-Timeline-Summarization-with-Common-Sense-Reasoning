427. LETTER TO CHAKRAYYA
SEVAGRAM,
March 15, 1945
CHI. CHAKRAYYA,
I understood your letter. The misunderstanding was on your
part. It is good that it has been cleared up. There is no need
for any other atonement.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHAKRAYYA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
