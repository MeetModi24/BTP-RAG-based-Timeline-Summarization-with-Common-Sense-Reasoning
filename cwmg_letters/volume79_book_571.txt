571. LETTER TO NANDLAL PATEL
ON THE TRAIN,
March 31, 1945
CHI. NANDLAL,
Take care of the house as you would of jewellery. Never
have others do the work which you can do yourself. I hope
Chi. Hari-ichchha is well and happy. You must not lose heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 9252. Also C.W. 2717. Cour-
tesy : Nandlal Patel
