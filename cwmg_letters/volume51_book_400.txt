400. LETTER TO RASIK S. DESAI
October 13, 1932
CHI. RASIK 2 ,
I was surprised to see your note. I hope you have gained peace
of mind as much as you have improved in health-my health
also has improved. Blessings to you all. Sardar and Mahadev are
quite well.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6619. Also C.W. 4351. Courtesy:
Valji G. Desai
