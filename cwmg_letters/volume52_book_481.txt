481. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
January 4, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
I got your letter. In what I wrote I had in mind only the
mutual dharma of husband and wife. I certainly did not mean
that you should stay somewhere away from the Ashram for a
long time and that Narandas should never visit you. But don't
you agree that he should go to see you only when the work in the
Ashram permits him? And, if you yourself go for a short stay
in the Ashram every six months, would it be necessary then for
him to visit you? I would certainly not think of being harsh on
you in a matter like this. On the contrary, I would wish to meet
your wishes as far as possible. Have no doubt at all that it has
always been my effort to create conditions in the Ashram so that
no woman in it should feel herself dependent.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 869. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
