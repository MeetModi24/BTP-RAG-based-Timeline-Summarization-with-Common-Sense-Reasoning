574. LETTER TO MANZAR ALI SOKHTA
April 12, 1935
MY DEAR MANZAR ALI,
Did you not teach me that 'Sokhta' means burnt? Not
until you have burnt your 'I' into 'O' will you achieve success.
You say you want funds. What for? Not for cleaning your neigh-
bours' streets and everything else, nor for pounding his and your
paddy and wheat. You do not need funds; you need to sweat
and produce wealth out of waste.
Yours,
BAPU
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
