640. LETTER TO P. R. LELE
March 5, 1933
DEAR MR. LELE,
I thank you for your postcard.
Yes, I have already got Sjt. Jayakar's opinion. I under-
stand what you say about yourself.
I hope it would be possible
for you to continue. Can you tell me how Sjt. Jaisukhlal is get-
ting on?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. R. LELE
31 MURZBAN ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
