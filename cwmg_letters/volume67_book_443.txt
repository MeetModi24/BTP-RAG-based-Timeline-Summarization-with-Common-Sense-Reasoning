443. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 4, 1938
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
How long will you live on medicines? Which kingdom do
you aspire to win? Go slow. Do only as much as you can.
Take care of your health, otherwise you will be guilty of violence.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 224
