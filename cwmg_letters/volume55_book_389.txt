389. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
July 31, 1933
DEAR BH-R-R-R 1 ,
What nonsense! You had a perfect right to explode as you
did. The prolonged discussions were enough to try anybody's
nerves. But I never doubted your capacity and willingness for
suffering when the occasion arose. Do take full rest and plunge
when the time comes. This time it is going to be a long-drawn-
out affair. That does not matter. But it gives one breathing
time. Once in, however, there should be no rest after. That is
the ideal.
Love to you all.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
Good-bye. I expect to find myself H.M.'s guest before to-
morrow dawns on me.
From a photostat: S.N. 9585
