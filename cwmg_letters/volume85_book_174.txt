174. LETTER TO BEGUM LATIF SYED
SEVAGRAM,
August 15, 1946
DEAR BEGUM SAHIBA,
I learnt about Dr. Latif only through Sarojini Devi. I was
grieved. May God grant you courage and may you always serve
the country as Doctor Saheb did.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
BEGUM LATIF SYED
HYDERABAD (DECCAN)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
