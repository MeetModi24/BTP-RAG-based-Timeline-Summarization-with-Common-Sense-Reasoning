219. LETTER TO ANASUYA JAJU
AHMEDABAD,
January 25, 1936
CHI. ANASUYA,
It pains me to think that I shall not be attending your
wedding, but I am helpless. Be an ideal wife. Remain the ideal
sevika that you are. Bring honour to dharma and to the coun-
try. Rest assured that you could not have a more suitable
husband than Radhakisan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9119
