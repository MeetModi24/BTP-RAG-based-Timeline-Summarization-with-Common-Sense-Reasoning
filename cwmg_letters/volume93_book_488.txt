488. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
February 15, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
What can I write to you? Whatever I have done has been
out of a sense of duty. You must calm down. Accept my
advice. Forget about leaving. Have trust in God. Ba is
unhappy about the talk of your going. The matter came up
while we were in the car. It is not at all proper for you to be
obstinate.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
