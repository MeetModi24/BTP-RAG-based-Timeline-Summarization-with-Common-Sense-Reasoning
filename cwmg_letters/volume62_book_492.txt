492. LETTER TO RAMIBEHN K. PAREKH
May 19, 1936
CHI. RAMI,
I have your letter. You did well in writing to me. I shall
send for Manu after I have reached Wardha, although, if Manu
so wishes, she might even now go to Wardha since Nimu has
taken up her stay at Maganwadi. I trust you and the children
are keeping well. I hope Kunvarji is well enough.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9726
