272. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI PATEL
November 19, 1933
CHI. DAHYABHAI,
You must have received my earlier letter. Enclosed is a
letter to Gordhanbhai. Give it to him after reading it. Do not
forget that it is your duty to strive with me if you are not satis-
fied [with my explanation]. Pass on their letters to Ba and Mani.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 156
