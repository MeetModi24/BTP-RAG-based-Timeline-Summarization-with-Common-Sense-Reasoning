380. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
January 27, 1941
CHI. KAKA,
I got the draft ready for you. Then at night I started think-
ing. First of all I did not like the name. Find some other name.
Then consider whether such a Sangh is very necessary just now.
Come tomorrow at four. Today the Ashram is full. Enclosed are
Bal's letters. They are very fine indeed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10939
