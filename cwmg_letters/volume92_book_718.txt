718. LETTER TO N. S. HARDIKAR
As AT WARDHA,
November 23, 1933
DEAR DR. HARDIKAR,
You promised "more again" in your letter, but the promise
has not materialized. Nevertheless, friends have been keeping me
informed of your progress, though for the last eight days I have
had no news of you. I wonder if you are still in hospital.
Wherever you are, I hope you have made steady progress.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. N. S. HARDIKAR
K.E.M. HOSPITAL
PAREL, BOMBAY
From the original: N. S. Hardikar Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
