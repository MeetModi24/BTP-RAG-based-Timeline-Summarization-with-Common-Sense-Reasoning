88. LETTER TO S. NAGASUNDARAM
March 13, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of 10th instant enclosing copy of a
printed appeal to the Government and the Legislatures.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. NAGASUNDARAM
FIRST FLOOR, LUXMI NIVAS
NEAR KING'S CIRCLE
MATUNGA, BOMBAY 19
From a microfilm: S.N. 20565
