226. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 13, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
I send you herewith a letter from Agatha. Reply to it. Send
Agatha's letter to Ghanshyamdas. It would be better to help her
come here. Write about the fare, etc.
Agatha may also write
whatever she wishes.
Do not forget to bring jamun or, if they are not available,
their stones.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11605
