214. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
NEW DELHI,
May 19, 1946
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Sushila told me all about you. She said that it was highly
desirable that you should go to some hill station for the sake of
your health. I should of course be glad. It would be good if
you could do some such thing. Go to Panchgani or Almora or
Mussoorie or Nainital or Abu. I think you can easily get ac-
commodation in Abu. The air and water there are said to be
good. I have had no experience of the place myself. I tell you
what I have heard.
I am very busy. I am dictating this. There you have to
take up a new task.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
1 Surendra Medh
