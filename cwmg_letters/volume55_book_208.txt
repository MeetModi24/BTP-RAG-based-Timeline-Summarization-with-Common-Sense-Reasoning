208. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
June 4, 1933
CHI. NARANDAS,
From today I have started writing a little. The burden on
you seems to have proved unbearably heavy, but I hope that
it will become somewhat lighter now. I myself shall not be able
to write much, but I will dictate. You may, therefore, write to
me now about important matters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8381. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
