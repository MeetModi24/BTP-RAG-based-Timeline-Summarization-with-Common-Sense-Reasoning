132. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
May 27, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
What reply did you give Vinoba? Where did you catch
the illness? But what has happened has happened. Now get
well soon and return here. We shall forget the past.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7082. Also C.W. 4574. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
