266. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
WARDHA,
March 23, 1935
CHI. RAMDAS,
I got your letter. I have only now read Patilji's letter to J. He has been
very much hurt. I have consoled him. It would of course have been better if
you had told him in advance. But do not worry about it now. Do not enter
into partnership with anybody until you have become quite proficient. I would
suggest that you should try to get a job in a big press. Personally I do not
like press work. If, however, you do wish to take up such work, first take up
a job in a press and learn the work. My own preference still is for a tailor's
or a cobbler's profession. Both are first-class professions. They have great
room for artistic skill. Nimu can give you all possible help in that work. You
may execute 'a deed of partnership' with her. No loss is ever possible in
these professions. But this is mere pretentious advice from me. Do only what
pleases you. Go and visit Radha when you get an opportunity to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
