59. LETTER TO BABUBHAI JHAVERI
NEW DELHI,
October 27, 1946
BHAI BABUBHAI,
You should let your wife do what she thinks right. I see
no point in dragging her to court. I have torn up the letter.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. BABUBHAI JHAVERI
248 DOSHIWADA'S POLE
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
