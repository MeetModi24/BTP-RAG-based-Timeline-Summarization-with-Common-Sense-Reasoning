37. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 11, 1939
MY DEAR STUPIDITY,
What made you think I was annoyed? I wrote in all inno-
cence, all love. Does a parent feel annoyed when he asks his child
to be patient over something he needs? You don't [see] the care
with which I am searching for a good cupboard. But you are so
touchy, so stupid that you can easily misunderstand things.
However, you must bring the books and the things that you need.
You will offend me, if you do not. You will wire the date of your
arrival.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3958. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7267
