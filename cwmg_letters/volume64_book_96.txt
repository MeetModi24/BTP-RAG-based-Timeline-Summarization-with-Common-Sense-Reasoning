96. LETTER TO MIRZA ISMAIL
November 27, 1936
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of 9th instant.
I await your
further letter as also information about the images.
You were good enough to tell me that I was free to make
use of your workshops, etc., to make researches and get such
assistance as was easily available. I know Mr. M. Frydman of
your electrical department. He is a lover of villages and has
the village mind.
Though he has been helping me with his
ideas for some years and has recently made me some village
tools, I would like you, if it is proper, to instruct him and the
Director of Industries to give me or the Village Industries Associa-
tion such assistance as they can appropriately render. Being
in a village myself I do need such assistance fairly often.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR M. ISMAIL
DEWAN, MYSORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
