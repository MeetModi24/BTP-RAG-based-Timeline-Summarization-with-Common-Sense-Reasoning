538. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
WARDHA,
August 28, 1935
BHAI MUNSHI,
Please be satisfied with the message I have sent. There is such
a crowd of people living in the Ashram just now that I do not
get a minute free. Last night I could go to bed only after 11.25
p.m. and got up at 1.50. It is 2.40 as I write this. From 6 in the
morning the daily round starts.
Blessings from
BAPU
ADVOCATE MUNSHI
RIDGE ROAD, BOMBAY
From Gujarati: C.W. 7585. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
