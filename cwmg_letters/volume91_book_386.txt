386. LETTER TO KHWAJA ABDUL MAJID
ON TOUR,
May 30, 1925
DEAR KHWAJA SAHEB,
I have your letter at last. I hope you will be able to trans-
fer to Delhi without any difficulty. I suppose it is better for you
to issue your appeal after the transfer. I am afraid you may
not enter the Councils whilst you are doing the University
work.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
KHWAJA SAHEB ABDUL MAJID
NATIONAL MUSLIM UNIVERSITY
ALIGARH
From the original: A. M. Khwaja Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
