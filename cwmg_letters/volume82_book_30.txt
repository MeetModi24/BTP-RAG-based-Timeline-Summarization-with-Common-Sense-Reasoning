30. LETTER TO ANASUYA SARABHAI
POONA,
November 4, 1945
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I have your letter. Is there any Divali? Who will celebrate
it? Are you all right? How is Shankerlal2? I am well. There
is so far no visible improvement in Sardar's health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : G.N. 11564
