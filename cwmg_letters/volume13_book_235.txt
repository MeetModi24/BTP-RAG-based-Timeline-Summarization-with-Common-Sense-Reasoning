235. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
AHMEDABAD,
January 11, 1917
DEAR ESTHER 3 ,
I was delighted to have your note from Bombay. I assure
you that we miss you both very much. You were hardly guests to
us; you had become members of the family. Hope Miss Peterson
is quite herself again. I redirected a batch of letters yesterday
and am doing likewise today.
With regards to both of you,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
My Dear Child, p. 3
