355. LETTER TO SHANMUKHAM CHETTY
February 8, 1937
MY DEAR SHANMUKHAM,
Your addressing me familiarly revives old memories and old
flavours. I reciprocate.
I like your frank letter. What I expect you to do is to
pull your weight in the right direction. The reform must not
be confined to the borders of Travancore. You can do a great
deal to avoid the subtle persecution that must be going on there
of the Nambudiris who have dared to accept the Travancore
Proclamation.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
