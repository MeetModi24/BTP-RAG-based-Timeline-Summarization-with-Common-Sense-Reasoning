375. LETTER TO COWASJI JEHANGIR
SEVAGRAM,
November 10, 1944
DEAR SIR COWASJI,
Somebody had conveyed to me the news of your dear son's
passing away. I had thought I would write to you a letter of
condolence but being burdened with work it slipped from my
memory. Last night I remembered it when I happened to read
the news again in Vallabhbhai's letter to Dahyabhai. And now
I write this. Please know that I share your grief. There must
be hundreds like me sharing your grief. May they all lighten
your burden and may God grant you peace.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
