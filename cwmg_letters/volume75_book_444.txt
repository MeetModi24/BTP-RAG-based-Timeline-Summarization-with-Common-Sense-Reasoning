444. LETTER TO KANTI GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
February 28, 1942
CHI. KANTI,
We got a letter about Saraswati's illness when we were all
expecting her to come. Even if she had come here instead of
going there she would have had treatment. But we ought to
do what you think right, shouldn't we? Everything here is all
right. Mahadevbhai has however grown a little weak.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Chhaganlal Gandhi Papers. Courtesy:
Gandhi Smarak Sangrahalaya, Ahmedabad
