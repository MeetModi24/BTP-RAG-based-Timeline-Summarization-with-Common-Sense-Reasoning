104. LETTER TO BHULABHAI J. DESAI
SEVAGRAM,
January 24, 1945
BHAI BHULABHAI,
Newspaper reports startle me. Jinnah says one thing and Li-
aquat Ali another. About me also it is reported that bypassing
the Working Committee I want a coalition government to be
formed. What do these reports mean? I have complete trust in
you. Please see that nothing is done without the approval of the
Working Committee. I go by what I wrote to you in Gujarati.
It contains nothing which would support the newspaper reports.
I can understand everything happening together. But please
make it clear that we cannot go a single step without the consent
of the Working Committee.
Blessings from
BAPU
