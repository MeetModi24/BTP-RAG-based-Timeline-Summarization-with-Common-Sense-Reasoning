187. LETTER TO T. MORISON
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 16, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I beg to enclose herewith the papers promised by me.
I shall thank you to return the Minutes of the meeting with Lord
Elgin after you have done with them.
I am,
Yours truly,
Enclosure
THEODORE MORISON, ESQ.
C/O THE EAST INDIA ASSOCIATION
9, VICTORIA STREET, W.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4578

2 Prominent European member of the Transvaal Legislative Assembly
