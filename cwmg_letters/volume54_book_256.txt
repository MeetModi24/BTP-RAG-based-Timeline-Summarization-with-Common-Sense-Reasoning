256. LETTER TO BHAU PANSE
March 28, 1933
CHI. BHAU,
I have your letter. For the present, you should continue to
stay there. You should learn how to mix with people and yet
remain uninvolved. You should cheerfully clean latrines and do
other physical work which other people ordinarily do not do.
You should keep up the desire to return to the Ashram, so that
you may be able to observe all its rules. For the present, how-
ever, you should keep the wish in check, stay there, and build up
good health. We only wish to serve our fellow men wherever we
may be, and there, too, you have enough opportunities for service.
It is very good indeed that you come in contact with labourers.
Write to me regularly. Fix a particular day of the week for
that purpose and write on that day without fail.
Blessings from
BAPU
