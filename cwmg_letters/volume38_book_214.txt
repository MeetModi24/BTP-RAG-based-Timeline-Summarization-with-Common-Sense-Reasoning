214. LETTER TO FRANCISCA STANDENATH
December 7, 1928
I have your letter subscribed also by Satyavan. Of course
you will wear exactly what you like instead of my musts and
must-nots. Do keep on smiling.
I am just now in Wardha. Mirabehn has come here to see
me. She will presently be on the move again. Ba is with me.
We are all well.
I hope both of you are happy there. You must be happy.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. FRANCISCA STANDENATH
GRAZ
From a photostat: S.N. 15091
