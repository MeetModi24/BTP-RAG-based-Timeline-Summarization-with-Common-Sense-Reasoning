383. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 5, 1940
CHI. ANAND,
Why English? I thought you wanted a few lines for some
book. What can I say about the whole series? Shall I praise
a collection of my own writings? My blessings are always with
you. Why are you not satisfied with this?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India and
Anand T. Hingorani
