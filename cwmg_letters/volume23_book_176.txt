176. LETTER TO HOWARD S. ROSS
POST ANDHERI,
March 18, 1924
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of the 15th February.
In the present state of my health, I am able only to give my
attention to things I know and which I dare not avoid.
Yours sincerely,
HOWARD S. Ross, ESQ.
MESSRS. MONTY, DURANLEAU, Ross AND ANGERS
BARRISTERS AND SOLICITORS
VERSAILLES BUILDING
90, ST. JAMES STREET
MONTREAL (CANADA)
From a photostat: S.N. 8523; also C.W. 5120
