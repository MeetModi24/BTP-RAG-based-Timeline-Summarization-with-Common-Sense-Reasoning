376. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
August 13, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I can easily plead guilty. Had I been always right in every-
thing I said and did with Amtul Salaam, she would not have
developed as she has. But as I told you in another connection, I
am not past redemption. I can improve, am improving.
I now understand about you. I agree with you that you
should not return till you have regained your strength and
weight. But it may also be that you will do so ultimately only
here. For the winter months are always good here. But I don't
worry about the future.
Kanchan who was ill is now out of the wood.
I have just received a wire from Bul that she has been re-
leased but says she cannot come here. I shall know more fully
tomorrow.
Here is a letter for you. You will tell him I had as yet no time.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4051. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7360
