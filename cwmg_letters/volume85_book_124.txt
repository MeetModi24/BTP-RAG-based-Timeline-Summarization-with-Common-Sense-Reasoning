124. LETTER TO QURESHI
URULI KANCHAN, DIST. POONA,
August 3, 1946
BHAI QURESHI,
Bapuji is here these days. He will leave Poona for Sevagram on 5-8-46
by the morning train at 10.40. At Kalyan Station, his bogey will be atta-
ched to the Nagpur Mail.
I have to request you to send, if possible, a tin of cow's ghee with
someone to the Nagpur Mail leaving Bombay at about 4.30 p. m. on 5-8-46.
The gentleman can return after delivering the tin at Kalyan Station to one
of us in Bapu's compartment.
121
Please excuse us for the trouble. I am writing this at Bapu's
instance.
Pranams from yours respectfully,
MUNNALAL
