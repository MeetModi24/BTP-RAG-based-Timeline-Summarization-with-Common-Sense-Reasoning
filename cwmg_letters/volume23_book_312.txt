312. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
ANDHERI,
Chaitra Sud 5 [April 9, 1924]
BHAISHRI FULCHAND 5 ,
Bhai Chunilal has written to me a long letter about the
school there. Therein he has made several allegations against
you. I trust I can ask you to see him personally, know from him
