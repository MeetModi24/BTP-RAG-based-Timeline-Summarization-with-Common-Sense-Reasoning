450. LETTER TO ANNAYYA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 23, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. The letter you mention cannot be managed
through the Protector of Immigrants. If you can spend some
money on lawyers' fees, I can write to someone in Durban who
might be able to do something. But if you have [any] friend
or if you know any lawyer in Durban you should write to him
directly.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. ANNAYYA
FREE INDIA NO. 39869
OVERSEAS LINE, KIRKEE
From a microfilm: S.N. 14877
