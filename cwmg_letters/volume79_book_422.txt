422. LETTER TO J.R.D. TATA
SEVAGRAM,
March 15, 1945
DEAR JEHANGIRJI,
I have just heard from Bapa that you are recouping your
health in Ooty. I should like a line to know how you are. Of
course I shall not expect you at the Trustees' meeting in Bombay
if you are at all unwell. I write this purposely in English as
you might not have all your staff there.
Love.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI J. R. D. TATA
OOTY, SOUTH INDIA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
