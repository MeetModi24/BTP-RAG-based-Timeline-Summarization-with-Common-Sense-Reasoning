20. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C. P.,
April 8, 1942
BHAI GHANSHY AMDAS,
I have replied to your telegram. Your essay is good but it
has become very controversial, and it is full of politics. I expected
from your pen something of a more permanent value. Jamnalal's
politics were not his strong point. You might have seen that at the
meeting of Mill-owners' Association I did not even touch upon
his political work. Even his politics could have been presented
in moral terms.
The criticism of the British should find no place in your essay.
I am surprised that Kaka did not notice it. We shall talk in greater
detail when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I hope you are well and you have found out what quantity of
butter you need.
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8056. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
