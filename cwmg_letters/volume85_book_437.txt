437. LETTER TO ANJANA CHOWDHARY
NEW DELHI,
September 20, 1946
DEAR ANJANA,
I have your letter. I must thank you for the account you
have sent me. How long does it take you to spin four hanks
(of 640 rounds each) every day? How much time does Subha-
dra spend on spinning?
What does Sita do?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapu: Maine Kya Dekha, Kya Samjha, p. 186
