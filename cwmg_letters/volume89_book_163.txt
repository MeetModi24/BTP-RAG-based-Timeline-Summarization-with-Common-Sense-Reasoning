163. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
September 2, 1947
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
I replied to your message of yesterday.
I would have started for Lahore today but for the flare-up
in Calcutta. If the fury did not abate, my going to the
Punjab would be of no avail. I would have no self-confidence.
If the Calcutta friendship was wrong, how could I hope to affect
the situation in the Punjab? Therefore my departure from Cal-
cutta depends solely upon the result of the Calcutta fast. Don't
be distressed or angry over the fast.
Blessings from
BAPU
