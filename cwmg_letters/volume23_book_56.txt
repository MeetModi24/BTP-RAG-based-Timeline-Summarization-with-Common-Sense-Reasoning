56. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
SABARMATI JAIL,
March 18, 1922
DEAR JAMNALAL,
From the purely economic point of view, I can say that un-
less merchants dealing in foreign yarn and foreign cloth give up
their trade and unless the public give up their fondness for foreign
cloth, starvation—the chief malady of our country—will not be
ended. I hope all businessmen will participate fully in the pro-
pagation of khaddar and the spinning-wheel.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
