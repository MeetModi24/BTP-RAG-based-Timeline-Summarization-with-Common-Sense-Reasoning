194. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
POONA,
June 19, 1944
BHAI MUNSHI,
We shall meet on the 24th at five o'clock. I am maintaining
my progress, although slowly.
Blessings to all from
BAPU
SHRI KANU MUNSHI
ADVOCATE
26 RIDGE ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : C.W. 7674. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
