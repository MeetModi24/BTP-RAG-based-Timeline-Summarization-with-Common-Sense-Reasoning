331. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
I follow, follow sure to meet the sun
and confident that what the future yields,
will be the right, unless myself be wrong.
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
Let the above comfort and cheer you. Here is your letter.
Yours sincerely,
UPPER HOUSE
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy : National
Archives of India
