378. LETTER TO T. PALANIVELU
August 13, 1941
DEAR PALANIVELU,
Truth can only be reached through purity of life and a reverent
study of the scriptures of the world.
No need to come to me.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI T. PALANIVELU
SECRETARY, ARYA SAMAJ
46 PANDI ROAD
VILLUPURAM
From a photostat: G.N. 6091
