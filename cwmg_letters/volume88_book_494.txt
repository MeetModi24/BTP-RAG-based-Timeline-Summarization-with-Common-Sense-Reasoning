494. LETTER TO JUGATRAM DAVE
NEW DELHI,
July 28, 1947
CHI. JUGATRAM,
Jivanji has passed on to me the postcard that you had
written to him.
The distinction that you make seems to be too fine. There
is no substance in it. Show me if you see any. I hope you
are getting on well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
