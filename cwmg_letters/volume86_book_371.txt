371. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
December 30, 1946
CHI. BABUDI,
I got your letter today. I had made all arrangements for
Calcutta. If Anand gets constant fever, Gordhandas should
give up his attachment, or Anand himself should be trained to
live with him. But I think all this advice is useless. What is
in store for you will happen.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 101075. Courtesy: Sharda G.
Chokhawala
