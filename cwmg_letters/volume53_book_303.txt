303. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
February 3, 1933
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
I got a letter from you after many days. Do we tolerate in
other people what the Harijans do, i.e., refuse to work and yet
ask for more money? What error do you see in what Narandas
says? Shouldn't we expect them to give eight hours' work? Mis-
placed pity is violence. I make no distinction between them and
other people, or only that we may tolerate faults in them which
we would not in other people. Discuss this also with Totaramji,
Panditji and Chimanlal and try to understand the point. I hope
you will see the error in your reasoning or show me mine.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 351. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
