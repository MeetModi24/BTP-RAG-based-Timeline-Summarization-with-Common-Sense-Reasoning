262. LETTER TO C. P. RAMASWAMI AIYAR
August 24, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. The "Selection" has not yet
been received. I suppose, it will be in due course.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR C. P. RAMASWAMI AIYAR
DIWAN SAHEB
SHAKHIVILAS
TRIVANDRUM
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
