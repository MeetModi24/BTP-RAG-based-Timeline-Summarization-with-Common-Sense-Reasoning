165. LETTER TO L. M. JAMES
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 13, 1906
DEAR MR. JAMES,
I have your note of the 12th instant. You may have seen a short
report of the interview with Lord Elgin in The Times of the 9th instant.
We are to see Mr. Morley on the 22nd instant. There is some
hope of a commission being appointed. I think you should on your
side send a reminder to the Foreign Office.
Yours truly,
L. M. JAMES, ESQ.
CHINESE LEGATION
PORTLAND PLACE, W.
