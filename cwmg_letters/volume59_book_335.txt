335. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
November 8, 1934
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter. You should get the money for the dairy
from Ambalalbhai. I will remit the amount. Narahari is here.
You must send for the special cot, or buy one, on which Jamna
can take sun-bath.
I understand what you say about Harilal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8422. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
