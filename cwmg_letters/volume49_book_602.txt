602. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
May 29, 1932
Received the letter from Venice. Let me know how you
passed your time on the steamer, what things you observed during
the voyage and how you spent your money, so that I may have
an idea of your powers of description and of what you regard as
simplicity. . . .2 Take walks for exercise and make yourself physically
fit. Do not get others to do what you can do for yourself. Do
not use a conveyance in order to reach a place if you can nego-
tiate the distance on foot. Fight against cold by exercise, not
by sitting near the fire place. . . .3
Write to your father regularly. Send him a statement of
accounts from time to time. Remember that parents can never
have enough of letters from their children. They are interested in
the minutest details about them. Fulfil your father's expectations
about you.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, pp. 186-
