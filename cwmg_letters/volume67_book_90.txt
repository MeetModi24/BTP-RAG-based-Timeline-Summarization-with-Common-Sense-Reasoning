90. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
MARDAN,
May 7, 1938
CHI. AMRITLAL,
You must have got my reply.
I think it would be best to
use Gokuldas's money for purchasing books.
I hope Chakraiyya will not lose the weight which he has
gained.
It is sad that Vijaya is not improving. If she has not come
there, write to her to come and see me in Bombay. I am also
writing to her. Please inquire about the letter which got lost.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10746
