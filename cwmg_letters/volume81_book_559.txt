559. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
October 4, 1945
CHI. MRIDULA,
This time you are not coming for nothing. I shall be as
I am at present when you come over. Strength goes away at a
horse's pace and comes back at an ant's. Come on Tuesday the
9th. I am keeping 3 p. m. free. I hope you are all right.
MRIDULABEHN SARABHAI
KASHMIR HOUSE
NAPEAN SEA ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
