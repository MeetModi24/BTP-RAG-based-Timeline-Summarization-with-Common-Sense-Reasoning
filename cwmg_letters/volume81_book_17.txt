17. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
July 20, 1945
BHAI AMRITLAL,
I have your letter in Hindi. It is good that you wrote it
in Hindi. It will not be proper for you to come here right now.
The Ashram is very unsteady. If you have blood-pressure go to
the Khadi Pratishthan. There is work to be done there. May-
be, Ranu and Shanti can also stay there. Consult Dhiren.
Sailen has recovered.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 10405. Courtesy: Amrita Lal
Chatterjee
