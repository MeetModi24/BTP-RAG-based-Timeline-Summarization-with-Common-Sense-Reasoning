221. LETTER TO DUNICHAND
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 13, 1945
BHAI DUNICHAND,
I learnt from what you did about the Harijans of Dukhaidi
that you are still alive. How wonderful! I even wrote a
letter of condolence to your wife. The poor woman must have
laughed. What could she have written in reply? Is it my fault
or yours if Dunichand continues to live? I think the fault is
mine. As a punishment I must die before you. I want to live
up to 125 years. Since you are older than I you must live even
after 125 years. My apologies to Deviji.
Look after the Harijans.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 5587
