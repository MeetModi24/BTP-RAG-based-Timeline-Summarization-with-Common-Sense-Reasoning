403. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
WARDHA,
April 12, 1940
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
I hasten to acknowledge with thanks your very prompt and
clear reply to my letter of 4th instant. It frees me from anxiety,
and ought to set at rest the domestic difference.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a microfilm: No. 109: Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
