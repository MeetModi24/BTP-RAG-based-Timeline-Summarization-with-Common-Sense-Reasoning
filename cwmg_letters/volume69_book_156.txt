156. LETTER TO E. C. GIBSON
ANANDKUNJ, RAJKOT,
April 9, 1939
DEAR MR. GIBSON,
I arrived here this morning in connection with the fulfilment
of the terms of the Thakore Saheb's Notification. Sardar Patel
too flew here this morning on my invitation.
I believe that His Excellency has sent you certain instructions
in connection with the matter. If you think that we should meet
I am at your disposal whenever you like.
I have sent a letter to the Thakore Saheb of which I send
you copy of translation herewith.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
From a copy: C.W. 10171. Courtesy: D. B. Kalelkar
