238. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[SABARMATI,
December 8, 1921]
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have your telegram as also your letter. I hope Mrs. Nehru
is calm and unruffled. Carry on your work without worrying.
Devdas is going there; you will have all the help that he can
give. Ask for more help if you need it. Improve the standard of
the Independent. Keep a good control over [correspondents']
reports. It does not matter if you get fewer [reports] but see that
they are good. Take Satisbabu's help if you can get it. I have
not written to Andrews, but you can write to him. I have not
written because it may imply some kind of pressure.
I should be glad to have a letter from Mrs. Nehru.
There is always a danger in taking vows prescribed by others.
Take those that you can and which you think worth taking
from among the ones suggested to you and then stick to them
tenaciously. You need not take them if you do not have the
strength to take them just now. There is no harm in not taking
them, but there certainly is in not keeping them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat: S.N. 11428
