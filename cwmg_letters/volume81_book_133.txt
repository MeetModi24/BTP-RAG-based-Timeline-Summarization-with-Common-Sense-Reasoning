133. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS BIRLA
SEVAGRAM,
August 5, 1945
BHAI RAMESHWARDAS,
I have your letter. Chi. Vasant has suffered much. I hope
his fever is now completely gone.
In the case of typhoid one
has to be even more careful during convalescence because the
disease weakens the stomach. The diet therefore has to be
watched. If the doctors permit hip-bath can be of much help.
I hope Chi. Asha is perfectly well and so also is Jugal
Kishore . . . . Will Rajendra Babu be able to put up with the
dust of Pilani? . . . I understand about Ghanshyamdas.
Blessings from
BAPU
