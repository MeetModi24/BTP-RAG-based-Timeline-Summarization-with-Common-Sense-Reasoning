657. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
March 20, 1933
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
Read this letter. I have written to Shantilal that I like the
idea. Let the bungalow go. The rest of the land may be
kept and huts built on it in which occasional visitors may stay.
To what extent this will be practicable, what arrangement can
be made for water, whether occasional visitors can stay in this
way and from where they will buy provisions, all this is for
Shantilal and you to consider. May be the idea is impractical.
They must have received the letter I wrote to Vijapur as you
had suggested.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Gandhi Nidhi File (August 1976).
