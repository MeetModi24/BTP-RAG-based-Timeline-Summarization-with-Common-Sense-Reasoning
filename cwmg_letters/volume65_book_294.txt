294. LETTER TO P. K. CHENGAMMAL
June 4, 1937
DEAR CHENGAMMAL,
I have your letter as also your son's. If and when Gopalan
comes to India, I shall see what can be done for him. If he
has not left already let him know that life in India is difficult
and the climate also may not be agreeable to those who have
been born and bred in South Africa. If, however, Gopalan can
lead a simple life and can accommodate himself to Indian man-
ners and customs, he will have no difficulty.
Yours sincerely,
SH. P. K. CHENGAMMAL
19 DAWIS STREET
DOORN JOUTEIN
JOHANNESBURG
SOUTH AFRICA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
