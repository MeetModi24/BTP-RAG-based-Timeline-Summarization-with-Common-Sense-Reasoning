204. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
[Before August 13, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
Does Vidyavati take a steam [bath]? She should be made to
lie wrapped in a sheet soaked in cold water. Soak the sheet in
clean, cold water, wring it well and spread it on the bed. Let
Vidyavati lie on it, then wrap the rest of the sheet around her
and cover her with a woollen blanket. The face would be left
uncovered. She should lie on the sheet naked to the waist. If
the body does not warm up in this position and she feels the cold
she must leave the bed. I have an idea that Gangabehn knows
about this bath because I had instructed her about it. Leave it
if you have not understood it. Bathing in water in which neem
leaves have been boiled may do some good. I suspect Vidyavati
does not stick to her diet.
I am glad to learn that Ma is better.
Tell Kamlabehn Gandhi that I am not replying to her letter
as I shall reach the Ashram on Monday.
Blessings from
BAPU
