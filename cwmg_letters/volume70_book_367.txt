367. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 30, 1939
MY DEAR C. R.,
Yours to Mahadev of 28th was duly read by me. I agree
with every word of what you say. I had anticipated you as you
will see from Harijan. Keep well. We—Rajendra Prasad and
I—are off to Delhi, I hope, only for a few hours.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
4 8 BAZULULLAH ROAD
THYAGARAYANAGAR
MADRAS
From a photostat: G.N. 2076
