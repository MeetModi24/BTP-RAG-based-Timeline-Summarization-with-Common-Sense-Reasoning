218. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
June 10, 1935
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I got your letter, as also Manu's. I very much liked the lat-
ter's decision to come here. Kanti or Kanu will go to the station
on the 15th morning to receive her. I understand the other things
that you write about.
Blessings from
BAPU
