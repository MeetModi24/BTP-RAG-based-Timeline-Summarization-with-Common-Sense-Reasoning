57. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
October 2, 1933
CHI. MARY,
Duncan brings me your and his love as I am walking on the
terrace. I shall treasure that love. But I want you to be free
from your weakness and be up and doing as early as possible.
May God restore you soon. Of course I keep myself informed of
your progress.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6006. Also C.W. 3332. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
