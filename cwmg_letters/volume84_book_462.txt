462. LETTER TO C. W. B. RANKIN
BHANGI COLONY, READING ROAD,
NEW DELHI,
June 26, 1946
DEAR MAJOR RANKIN,
I got your letter last evening and am sending you a copy of the me-
morial in the case of Haridas Mitra.
Mr. Carden Noad in his letter to Gandhiji of the 7th instant says:
"I enclose a signed copy of the new memorial. A signed copy has
been sent to the Viceroy."
It must have been lost in the post. He has been wired today to send
a copy direct to H. E. also. In the meantime please put the one I send you
before the Viceroy.
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 54
