554. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
September 21, 1941
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have your letter. Do not leave the place till you are
completely restored.
338
It is perfectly all right that you interpreted my letter as
referring to you. I however know from my own experience as
well as from others' that one stands to gain a lot by accepting
one's mistakes. This is pure practical wisdom. In account books
we proceed after correcting a mistake, if any; the same applies
to our moral accounts.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8048. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
