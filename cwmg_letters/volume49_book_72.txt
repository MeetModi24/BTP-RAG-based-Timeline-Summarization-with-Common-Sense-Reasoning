72. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
January 29, 1932
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got the letter you wrote from Siwan. I also received sub-
sequently your earlier letter. I hope you get my letters. Tell
Swarupraniji that she should not feel worried at all because
Swarup and Krishna have left. God protects all his creatures.
How is her health? My diet is practically the same as it was
when I was out of jail. The weight is 106 pounds. Sardar
Vallabhbhai is with me. Both of us are quite well. Do you
read any newspapers?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3423
