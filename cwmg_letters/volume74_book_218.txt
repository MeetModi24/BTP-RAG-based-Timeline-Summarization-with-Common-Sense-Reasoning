218. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
July 3, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
Your note. I missed you last night and yet was I wishing that
you would not come. When I learnt that Undhru was sent to you,
I thought you were being attended [to]. On inquiry just now, I find
that only Undhru was sent. I hear just now that Undhru was
sent with full instructions. Nevertheless I am sending Puri to see
how you are faring. You will break your silence to speak to him.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6484. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9879
