142. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 15, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you even today. Since Mahadevbhai
has obtained permission to see Pyarelal, Mother can gladly meet
Pyarelal. Since I have vacated the hospital room, Mother's
accommodation will be no problem at all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
