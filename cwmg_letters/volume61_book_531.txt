531. LETTER TO C. R. SRINIVASAN
August 26, 1935
I am really and literally drained dry. I have no gift for weav-
ing messages to order. This village work is so taxing and so baffling
that if I could help it I would stop all writing and simply bury
myself in a village and there work away for all I am worth and
that I should love to do [in] perfect silence. In the circumstances
you will please excuse for not sending you a message.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
