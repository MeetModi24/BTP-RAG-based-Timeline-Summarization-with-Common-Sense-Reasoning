380. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
SEVAGRAM,
April 5, 1940
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I have your letter. The heat must not be unbearable. Su-
shilabehn tells me that you overexercise a little. You should feel
no pain in any part of your body. In a disease like yours, one
benefits by doing as little exercise as possible. You must not ex-
ceed the limit.
1 Seksaria College of Commerce
2 Vide "Sindh Tragedy", pp. 71-.
Rami writes to Ba. She must be writing to you, too.
Blessings from
BAPU
