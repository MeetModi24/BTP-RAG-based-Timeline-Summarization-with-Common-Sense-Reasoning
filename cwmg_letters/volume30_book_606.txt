606. LETTER TO MAHADEO V. PANDLORKAR
May 30, 1926
So far as I know Jiwatma is to Paramatma what a drop is to the
ocean. And even as the properties of a drop are identical with
those of the ocean, the properties of Jiwatma are identical with
those of Paramatma.
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 19584
