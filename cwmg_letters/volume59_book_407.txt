407. LETTER TO DR. B. SUBRAHMANYAM
November 16, 1934
DEAR DOCTOR,
I had expected your letter and it has come. Of course, I
expect you to do a great deal in the direction of village work.
Have you already begun the work of re-introducing the chakki,
the hand-pounding of rice, the village kolu for pressing oil, and
such other obviously simple things? You should take a census
of the industries of villages served by you and make them busy
hives of small industries instead of being full of drones as they
have become.
Yours sincerely,
DR. B. SUBRAHMANYAM
HARIJANA SEVASRAMAM
SITHANAGARAM, via KOVVUR
WEST GODAVARY DT.
