443. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
SEVAGRAM,
March 17, 1945
BHAI GOPICHAND,
I have gone through all your articles. They are good. I
do not propose to make any changes in them. Do you intend to
get them published? If you do, then go through them carefully.
Will it be in the three Punjab scripts and not in Hindustani?
I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
