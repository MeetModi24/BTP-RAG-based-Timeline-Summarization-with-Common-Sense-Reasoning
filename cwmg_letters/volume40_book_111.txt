111. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
RANGOON,
Silence Day [March 11, 1929]
SISTERS,
Today I have just enough time to drop a line to you to show
that I remember you.
I may get your letter, if at all, by the next mail. Letters take
a full seven days to reach me here.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro - 1: Ashramni Behnone, p. 50
