231. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
July 30, 1936
CHI. KANU,
I am sending six letters with this. Keep copies of the letters
to Jugalkishore and Gangabehn. The rest may be posted
straight away. Make a copy of the letter to Satyanand Bose also.
In this way you are getting closer to Mahadev's place.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II.
