111. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI MEHTA
ASHRAM,
Posh Vad [February 11, 1918]
BHAISHRI BHAGWANJI,
I have your letter. It shows that what may have been intended
as help has the opposite effect sometimes. That is how I have
felt about the article in Gujarati. I can do the work in Kathiawad
in my own way.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C. W. 3027.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
