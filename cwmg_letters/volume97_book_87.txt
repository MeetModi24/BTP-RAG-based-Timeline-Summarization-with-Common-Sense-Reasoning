87. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
[After January 27, 1928]
CHI. NIMU,
You have taken a vow not to write to me, haven't you? This is not correct.
You have not kept your pledged word and now Ramdas writes and tells me
that you would come only if I send for you. Is this not a very strange way of
behaving? Do you wish to look upon me as a father-in-law? Why should you
think it necessary to be sent for, in order that you may come to me? I will
expect you to come over in reply to this letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
