325. LETTER TO KALIDAS NAG
January 29, 1937
DEAR KALIDAS NAG,
I have your letter written on the boat taking you to Honolulu.
I have no inspiring message to give to anybody if non-violence is
not its own message. But I can state my own experience of nearly
fifty years of practice that there is no force known to mankind
which is equal to non-violence. It cannot, however, be learnt
through books. It has got to be lived.
You ask me to mention books. For the reason just stated it is
difficult to single out books purely dedicated to an exposition of
non-violence.
Richard Gregg's Power of Non-violence may be
studied with advantage. Tolstoy's later writings are also aids to a
contemplation of non-violence.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. KALIDAS NAG
C/O THE UNIVERSITY OF HAWAII
HONOLULU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
