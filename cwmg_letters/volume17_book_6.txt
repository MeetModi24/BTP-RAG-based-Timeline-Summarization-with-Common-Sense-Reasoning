6. LETTER TO L. FRENCH
LAHORE,
February 3, 1920
Inquiries are daily being made with reference to those Martial
Law and other political or semi-political prisoners not yet discharg-
ed. I have not worried you with even an inquiry, as I know it
takes time to prepare lists of all the possible prisoners fit for dis-
charge.
But if you could now tell me whether the other prisoners in-
cluding Bhai Parmanand and those who were sentenced during
the Lahore Conspiracy trials are to be discharged [sic].
Exception has also been taken to the undertaking taken from
the recent discharges. Is it possible to give a reason for making
the distinction?
From a copy: S.N. 7102
