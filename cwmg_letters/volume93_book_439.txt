439. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEGAON ASHRAM,
WARDHA,
September 1, 1939
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Give my condolences to the relatives of the late Lakshmidas
Tairsi. I only have sweet memories of his company. I had once
written to him. I had also received a reply.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHETH MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
74 WALKESHWAR ROAD
BOMBAY
