397. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
February 2, 1941
DAUGHTER,
I got your letter. I sent you a telegram yesterday and also
wrote a postcard. I find I understand nothing.
I have faith in
prayer. Get all right first.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 471. Also Bapuke Patra–8: Bibi Amtus-
salaamke Nam, p. 209
