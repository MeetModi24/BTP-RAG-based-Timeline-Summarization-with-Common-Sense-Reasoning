750. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
February 25, 1934
MY DEAR PLAYMATE,
Can't give you a love letter. Whether I am to plant your
stolen tree or do some other thing depends upon you and not
me, for you will be pulling the strings. Only don't you tax me
overmuch-and then you can't brave the sun. More when we
meet. You must be fit.
Love.
SLAVE-DRIVER
SHRI PADMAJA NAIDU
GOLDEN THRESHOLD
HYDERABAD
DECCAN
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
