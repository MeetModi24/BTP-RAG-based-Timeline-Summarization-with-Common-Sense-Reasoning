330. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
Saturday
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
I was reading Carlyle yesterday. I copied the accompanying for
you. Is it not splendid ? May you and I make it part of our lives !
With love,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy : National
Archives of India
