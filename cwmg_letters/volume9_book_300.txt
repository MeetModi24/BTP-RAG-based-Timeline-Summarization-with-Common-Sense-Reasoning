300. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
October 8, 1909
MY LORD,
I am obliged to Your Lordship for your letter. As you have thrown
the sole responsibility on my shoulders, I have adopted the middle course
and combined the two letters into one. I enclose herewith copy of the
letter as it has gone forward. I trust it will meet with Your Lordship's
approval. In the meantime, the order is being sent to the printers to
strike off 2,000 copies of the statement .
I remain, etc.,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5120
