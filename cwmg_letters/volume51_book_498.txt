498. LETTER TO TARA R. MODI
October 27, 1932
CHI. TARA,
If I got your letter, it is unlikely that I did not reply to it. If
you wrote to me directly, it is possible that your letter was lost
on the way. If you sent it with the Ashram post, I would certainly
remember receiving it. If you had asked me any special questions,
please send them again. However, you should be able to get much
from my numerous letters and articles.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4174. Also C.W. 1673. Courtesy:
Ramniklal Modi
