172. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
April 17, 1914
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
What about the house at Mountain View which the Polaks want
to buy from you? What are the terms you have suggested?
I have a letter from Douglas Hall, written for Gokhale, asking me
if I would go to London before returning to India.
Mrs. Gandhi is still feeling well. If the improvement continues, it
may be all well.
Yours.
UPPER HOUSE
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy : National
Archives or India
