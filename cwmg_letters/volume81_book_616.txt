616. LETTER TO DINESH SINGH
October 11, 1945
CHI. DINESH,
I was happy to receive your letter. It is good that all are
together. Complete your studies and render public service to
your heart's content.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DINESH KUMAR
KALAKANKAR KOTHI
LUCKNOW (U. P.)
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 8676
