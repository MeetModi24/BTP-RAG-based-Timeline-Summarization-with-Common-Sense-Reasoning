389. LETTER TO GOVERNMENT OFFICER, CHINDE
[JOHANNESBURG,]
May 23, 1905
THE GOVERNMENT OFFICER
REPRESENTING THE COLONIAL SECRETARY,
CHINDE
BRITISH CENTRAL AFRICA
SIR,
At present in the Transvaal there are a few hundred Indians who
are desirous of proceeding to Chinde if they can receive employment
on the Railways that, I understand, are being constructed under the Govern-
ment. Some of them have already worked in Chinde or in other parts
of British Central Africa.
I shall be obliged if you will kindly let me know whether there is
any scope for them and, if there is, where they should apply.
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
M. K. GANDHI
Letter Book (1905) : No. 120.
