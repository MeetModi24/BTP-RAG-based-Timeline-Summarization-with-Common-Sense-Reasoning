221. LETTER TO LAKSHMIBEHN KHARE
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 20, 1930
CHI. LAKSHMIBEHN (KHARE),
Your joining the picketing will prove a shield for the other
women. As these are all novel experiences some are likely to get
hurt. Still we should not retrace our steps. You have inexhaust-
ible strength. Put it to good use and bring credit to yourself as
well as to the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati: C.W. 276. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn Khare
1 On the occasion of Gandhiji's birthday the addressee had offered to
dedicate her life to him.
