396. LETTER TO BHULABHAI J. DESAI
August 24, 1934
If you wish to get elected to the All-India Congress Committee,
I would advise you to control the desire. You may get elected
to it when they all want you there with one voice. You will then
be joining it with honour. Even if you are not a member just
now, you are as good as one. That is enough for the present and
in the prevailing atmosphere.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
