387. LETTER TO G. MUKERJI
November 14, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter and copies of your prospectus. The prospectus has
made no appeal to me. I shall be glad to know what work you
can do. I am passing on your letter to Sjt. Kumarappa.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI G. MUKERJI, M.SC. (CALIFORNIA, U. S. A.)
BANSBERIA P. O.
DT. HUGHLY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
