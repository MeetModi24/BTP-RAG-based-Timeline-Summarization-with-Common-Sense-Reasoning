184. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
[Before June 25, 1938] 1
CHI. KAKA,
I want that book about marriage rites. Send two or three
copies. I hope to marry Vijaya to Manubhai shortly. Nanabhai
will most probably attend and he himself will perform the rites.
But he says he would like to perform the rites according to your
booklet. I therefore think it necessary to send a copy to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7686
