333. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 2, 1941
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I was very happy to read your letter. Be careful about your
diet and safeguard your health. I am certain you will pay the
money. How is Kanti?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S. N. 9744. Also C. W. 724. Courtesy:
