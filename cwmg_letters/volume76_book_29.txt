29. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEGAON, via WARDHA, (C. P.),
April 11, 1942
MY DEAR C. R.,
On the off chance that you will be on today's G. T., Khurshed-
behn is going to fetch you. You must come and finish the
national week here and give us a report of your doings.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10910. Courtesy: C. R. Narasimhan
