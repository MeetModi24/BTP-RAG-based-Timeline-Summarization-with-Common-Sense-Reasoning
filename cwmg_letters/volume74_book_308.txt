308. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
July 30, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your two letters came in together.
So you see it works the same way at both ends. We must
suffer these little pricks of life.
If Jamnalal must leave earlier, he must not be prevented.
I am glad you are pursuing the Agra case.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4038. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7347
