200. LETTER TO MADELEINE ROLLAND
SEVAGRAM,
February 9, 1945
DEAR MADELEINE,
My whole heart goes out to you now that the one for whom
you seemed to be living has gone. Nevertheless the true position
is that you should work ever so much more than before assuming
of course that your physical condition makes work at all possible.
Love.
BAPU
MLLE. ROLLAND
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
