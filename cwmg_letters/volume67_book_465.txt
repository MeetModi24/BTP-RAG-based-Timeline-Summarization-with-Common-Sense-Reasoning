465. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
September 8, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
There is falsehood, that is madness, in every line of Sherale-
kar's letter. Why reply to him?
You may come whenever you wish. Three o'clock will be the
best time. How did Amritlal get indigestion?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10909
