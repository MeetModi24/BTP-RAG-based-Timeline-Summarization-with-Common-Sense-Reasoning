362. LETTER TO KALYANJI V. MEHTA
June 20, 1937
BHAI KALYANJI,
I got your letter. If possible take Ba to Manilal once.
If possible try and make Kanam spin on the takli regularly.
He had started that here. But the practice was given up later.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2714
