224. LETTER TO LAKSHMIDAS
March 30, 1926
CHI. LAKSHMIDAS,
I am very glad that you are improving. Do not think of
running away from the place before you have completely re-
covered.
Anandi has another attack. Whenever she has temperature
it is very high. Today she has been given a castor-oil purgative;
also three grains of quinine; and I have instructed Vallabhbhai
to get a mixture from a doctor. When we get it, it will be conti-
nued according to your wish.
Mani mixes well with us. But I have not yet been able to
remove that horrible nose-ring. The girl is very loving, she is
also vivacious and she does talk a lot.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 19399
