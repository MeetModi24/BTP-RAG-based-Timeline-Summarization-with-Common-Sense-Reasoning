478. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM AND ABHA GANDHI
January 16, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. I am writing this on Baqui's telegram.
I have sent him a telegram saying that, if he wants to come,
there is no objection. Remain calm. I have given a short state-
ment. I will send you a cutting when it is published.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. ABHA,
I shall start the silence day on Sunday at 12 noon. I shall
end it there on Monday at 12.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 564
