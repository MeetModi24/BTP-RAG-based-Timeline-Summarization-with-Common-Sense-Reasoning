517. LETTER TO BALA, JAYA AND OTHERS
May 14, 1934
CHI. BALA, JAYA, BAPU, UMIYA, DAMAYANTI, BALI AND MANEK,
A letter which you get somebody else to write for you cannot
be interesting. One written by you yourselves, no matter what
its contents, would have given me pleasure. God will protect
me as long as He wants me to serve.
This is true of everybody
else.
Blessings from
BAPU
[Ps.]
Just now I am touring in small villages, like Maroli.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2708
