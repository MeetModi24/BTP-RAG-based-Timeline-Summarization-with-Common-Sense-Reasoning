353. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
Y.M.,
September 14, 1930
MY DEAR ANAND,
I am glad you are feeling well in both body and mind. The
greater your moral progress the greater will be Father's reconci-
liation and happiness. The only way to serve elders and for
that matter anybody else is to do the right thing without osten-
tation and never to feel irritated. I know this is more easily
said than done. But it has got to be done.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
I am quite well.
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
