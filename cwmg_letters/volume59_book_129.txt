129. LETTER TO SHAM LAL
October 3, 1934
DEAR LALA SHAMLAL,
Herewith letter from Shri Ramrichhpal Singh. I detained
the letter asking the writer for permission to send it to you, as
it contained statements that appeared to me to be startling and
which I was not prepared to believe until I had referred the
letter to you. You would please now let me have your criti-
cism upon the letter and return the letter with it. I have not
kept a copy of that letter.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
