338. LETTER TO MANGALDAS PAKVASA
[CAMP] SODEPUR,
ON THE TRAIN [TO SANTINIKETAN],
December 18, 1945
BHAI MANGALDAS PAKVASA,
I have your letter and the opinions.
It was good that you
sent copies of the opinions to Jajuji. I shall now see what can
be done.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 4784. Courtesy: Mangaldas
Pakvasa
