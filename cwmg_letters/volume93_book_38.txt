38. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
WARDHA,
May 9, 1935
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. It is good you went to Rajkot with your
brother. Now come whenever you find the time. You certainly
have my permission to come.
Blessings from
BAPU
