80. LETTER TO SARALA DEVI
July 29, 1945
DEAR SARALA1,
I was delighted to have your letter. Of course you will
write whenever you like and come to me as soon as you are able
to. Meanwhile, love.
BAPU
(M. K. GANDHI)
SHRI SARALA DEVI
POLITICAL PRISONER
DISTRICT JAIL
ALMORA
From a photostat: G.N. 9089
