461. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
SEGAON,
September 8, 1938
MY DEAR AGATHA,
My capacity for work is very limited and work is daily
mounting. Hence the delay in writing to you. But there has
been not a moment's delay in action on this side. The whole
sum is guaranteed. I gave Shuaib your address and he definite-
ly promised to give the whole thing proper form and write to
you. He was to send you or Harold £100 on a/c. I am now
taking action.
The rest from Mahadev.
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
