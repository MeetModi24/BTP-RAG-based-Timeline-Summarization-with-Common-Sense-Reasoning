52. LETTER TO ANTYAJA BROTHERS AND SISTERS
[October, 1924]
ANTYAJA BROTHERS AND SISTERS,
I constantly think of the antyaja brothers and sisters who live in the
Ashram. I wish you all to live in complete harmony with one another, also
always wish your. . . . 2 I wish you to be votaries of truth. . .3 to eschew
greed so that additional antyaja families can be provided accommodation. The
uplift of antyajas depends upon your goodness. Ultimately. . .4, uplift depends
upon. . . . 5 All of you brothers and sisters should spin as a rule and while
spinning, keep repeating Ramanama. May it always be well with you.
Blessings from
BAPU
ANTYAJA FAMILIES
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 34026
