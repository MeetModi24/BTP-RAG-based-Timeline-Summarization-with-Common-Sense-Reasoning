149. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 23, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you even today. Mahadevbhai is there
today. He might have met you. Everything is going on nicely. Only
I am under great pressure of work.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 Widow of S. Srinivasa Iyengar
