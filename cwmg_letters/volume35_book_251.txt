251. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
[BOLGARH,
December 10, 1927]
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
I am now going through the whole programme. Sambalpore
will be dropped. I am thankful you are not sending me to
Jamshedpur. I am here till Monday. I reach Sakhigopal Monday
night and Balasore Wednesday. The rest is uncertain. Yes,
indeed, the Kharagpur victory was God's gift.
With love,
MOHAN
[PS.]
I am better.
C. F. ANDREWS
BALASORE
From a photostat: G.N. 2625
