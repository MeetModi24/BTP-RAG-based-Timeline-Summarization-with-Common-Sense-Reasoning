698. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
March 23, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your telegram. It arrived today. You must have
sent it late.
I write about Madalasa today. Madalasa keeps on having
ulcers in her mouth. I kept her on orange juice for a few days.
Now she takes vegetable soup, fruit, etc. But the ulcers do not
go. Can you suggest some remedy ?
Sharada's Anand is always ill with something or other. I
feel like sending him to you. Now it may be too late but if
you wish that he should come, I am willing to send him.
Where should he stay if he comes there? Can he be put up with
Devdas? Would there be any place in your house? Please consider
and let me know. Do not hesitate to say no. Since I am
always in need of help, I go on burdening you. You know my
nature, don't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial
