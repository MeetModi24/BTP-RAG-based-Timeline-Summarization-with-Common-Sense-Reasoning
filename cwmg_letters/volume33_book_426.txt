426. LETTER TO M. M. GIDWANI
NANDI HILLS,
June 2, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
It was good of you to have sent your article for Young India
entitled "The Present Situation". But I am afraid that it will not
be of much use for the readers of Young India. That it will lead
popular education is an admitted fact. But the only question is
how to do it and who shall do it. And Young India is published
either to suggest new lines of thought or to show practical methods
of working out plans and policies already accepted. I return your
article lest you may want it.
Yours sincerely,
PROF. M. M. GIDWANI, M. A.
EDITOR,
"SINDHUDESH"
KARACHI
