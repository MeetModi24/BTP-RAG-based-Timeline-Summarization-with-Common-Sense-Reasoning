96. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL
ON TOUR,
February 19, 1927
BHAI PRABHUDAYALJI,
Your letter has been moving along with me. I could lay
my hand on it only today.
However much the world may change, if you are convinced
that khadi brings material as well as spiritual benefit and that
other stuff merely pleases one's fancy, you should be content
with khaddar clothes alone.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: G.N. 10063
