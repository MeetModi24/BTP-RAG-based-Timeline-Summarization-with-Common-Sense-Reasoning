59. LETTER TO G. V. SUBBA RAO
May 24, 1924
DEAR MR. SUBBA RAO,
[I have known] Sjt. Aurobindo Ghosh's views throu[gh] my
son, who specially saw him, and then throu[gh] Mr. Das. I agree
that our basis should be spiritual. And I am trying in my own
humble way to conduct every activity from a spiritual point of
view.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original: G.N. 3623
