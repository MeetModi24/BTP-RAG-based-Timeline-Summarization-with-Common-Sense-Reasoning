187. LETTER TO N. S. PHADKE
POST ANDHERI,
March 20, 1924
MY DEAR MR. PHADKE,
I have your letter.
The article on Continence referred to by you was written by
me not because I believed that India was over-populated, but
because I believed that self-restraint was good in every case and
much more so at a time when we were ourselves in a state of
slavery. I am totally opposed to artificial means of controlling the
birth-rate, and it is not possible for me to congratulate you or your
co-workers on having brought into being a League whose activities,
if successful, can only do great moral injury to the people. I wish
I could convince you and your co-workers to disband the League
and devote your energy to a better purpose. You will please pardon
me for giving my opinion in such a decisive manner. I have not
hesitated to do so because I know something of such activities in
England and France.
Yours sincerely,
N. S. PHADKE, Esq.
RON. SECRETARY
BOMBAY BIRTH-CONTROL LEAGUE
GIRGAUM
BOMBAY
From a photostat: S.N. 8538; also C.W. 5130
1 Presumably, the reference is to the article "In Confidence" published
