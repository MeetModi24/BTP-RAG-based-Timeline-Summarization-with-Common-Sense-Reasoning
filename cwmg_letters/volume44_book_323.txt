323. LETTER TO RATILAL SHETH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 17, 1930
BHAI RATILAL,
I have your letter. You may send me the valkal. In the
past also someone had sent a garment like that from Africa. I
think it was given away to some person at his request.
I hope all your difficulties in your business are over. Vande-
mataram to Narbheram and other friends.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7165
