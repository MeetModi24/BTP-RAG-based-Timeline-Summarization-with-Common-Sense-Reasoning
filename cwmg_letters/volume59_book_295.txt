295. LETTER TO DR. K. A. HAMID
November 2, 1934
DEAR DR. HAMID,
I had your letter of22nd October. Khan Saheb Abdul Ghaffar
Khan does not like travelling for the purpose of giving lectures.
I, therefore, do not want to act against his inclinations. You must,
therefore, do the best you can. Moreover, as I have retired
from the Congress, such commissions ought not to be entrusted to
me.
Yours sincerely,
DR. K. A. HAMID
JALGAON
E. KHANDESH
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
