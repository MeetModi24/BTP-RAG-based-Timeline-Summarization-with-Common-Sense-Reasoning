535. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
November 1, 1932
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I specially remember you today. I have had no letter
from you since long.
I didn't get any even in the last week of September.
How are all of you? How is Grandmother?
How is
Gokibehn ? Are your worries over now?
I wish you a happy New Year.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4798.
Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
