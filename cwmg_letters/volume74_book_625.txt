625. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
October 4, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Yours.
Again the pain. I suppose it will come and go till at last
it is itself exhausted in the effort, if we do not feed it. That is
nature's way. And you are following it, I am sure.
No fried
chips or anything of the sort.
You did not tell me about this exposition of the Bible. But
having met ma[n]y Seventh-day Adventists, I can understand his
zeal.
We shall discuss the propriety of your joining the Goseva
Sangh.
Of course you can resign from the Ed[ucation] Board if it
became necessary.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4090. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7399
