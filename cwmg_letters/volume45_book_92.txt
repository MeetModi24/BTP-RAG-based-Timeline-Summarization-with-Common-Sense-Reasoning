92. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
January 10, 1931
CHI. SHARDA,
If you get my letters late, is that my fault alone? Are you
so busy that you have to write in the night and spoil your hand-
writing? Why should you not stay there if it suits you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9897. Courtesy: Shardabehn Chokha-
wala
