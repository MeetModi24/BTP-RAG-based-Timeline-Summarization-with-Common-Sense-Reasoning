115. LETTER TO AGA KHAN
February 10, 1946
BHAI SAHEB,
I got your letter today. You certainly know my plight, and
why I am unable to be present. I expect the Diamond Jubilee
to be celebrated in a fitting manner, and may you live for many
more years.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
H. H. THE AGA KHAN
LAND'S END
MALABAR HILL
BOMBAY
