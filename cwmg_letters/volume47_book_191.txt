191. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
SIMLA,
July 20, 1931
CHI. PREMA,
Kisan met me. She must have written to you about that.
I felt that she ought to devote herself more to service.
I got your letter.
Do you still beat children? Ramabehn was complaining that
you did. Did you satisfy Panditji? Have you cultivated close
relations with Gangabehn? She seems to be unhappy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10259. Also C.W. 6707. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
