301. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
August 3, 1934
CHI. LILAVATI,
I got your letter. What you say is correct. Follow Naran-
dasbhai's advice. Sushilabehn also is there now. You may consult
her too. Now think over my argument. Do you want a degree
or knowledge? Do you want education which brings you knowledge
or to work for some sort of degree? Do you know that a degree
does not serve the purpose of knowledge ? . . .
From Gujarati: C.W. 9873
