294. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
August 24, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
Here is Sitaram Sastri's letter for you to deal with. You
forgot the sandals. I wonder if you got your own pair. If not,
you should procure a pair there or write to Valunjkar at once.
I hope you are well and well settled. I am looking forward
to your first letter after settling down to the new life. Do not
make any time-table.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6362. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9828
