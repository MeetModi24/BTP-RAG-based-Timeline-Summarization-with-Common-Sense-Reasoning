93. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
POONA,
July 31, 1946
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
You are not looking after your health-it is not good.
Abidali writes to say that he has broken his fast and has left
the Congress House. He has written a sweet letter.
I have to go and see the Governor today. I understand
it is to be purely a courtesy call.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, pp. 318-
