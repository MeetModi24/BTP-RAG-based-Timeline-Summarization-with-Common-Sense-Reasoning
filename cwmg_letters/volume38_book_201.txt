201. LETTER TO R. VENKATRAM
WARDHA,
December 6, 1928
MY DEAR VENKATRAM,
I have your letter and the issues of your journal. You fairly
guessed my general view about journalistic ventures. If you watch-
ed me working for full 24 hours, you would have pity on me and
not ask me to read any journal quite apart from my attitude. Much
as I should like to, I am much obliged to deny myself the pleasure
of reading the literature that is pouring upon me from all sides.
You will please therefore excuse me.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. VENKATRAM
EDITOR, "INDIAN STATES JOURNAL"
EMPIRE BUILDING, FORT, BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 13763
