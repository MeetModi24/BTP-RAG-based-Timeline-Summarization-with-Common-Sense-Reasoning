716. LETTER TO BARBARA
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 23, 1945
CHI. BARBARA, ALIAS VASANTI,
Everyone is afraid for you. The reason is that having been
born in a cool place, you may not be able to bear the heat.
You will perhaps be able to put up with the heat if you lead
a life in keeping with the climate. You are getting on well with
your work.
Can you read and understand Hindustani?
Blessings from
BAPU
