197. LETTER TO T. L. KANTA RAO
February 1, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 26th ultimo. I would refer you to
Shri G. Sitarama Sastry, of Vinay Ashram, Chandole, who will
be the Agent in charge of the Guntur District. I have no doubt
that he will take all the assistance that medical men can give.
Sanitation and hygiene are an integral part of the village uplift
programme.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI T. L. KANTA RAO
SECRETARY
THE GUNTUR DT. L. I. M. ASSOCIATION,GUNTUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
