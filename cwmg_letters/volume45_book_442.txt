442. LETTER TO J. H. GARRETT
[April 12, 1931]
DEAR MR. GARRETT,
Perhaps you have heard from the Government of India that
I would seek an interview with you regarding the Gujarat affairs
arising out of the settlement. If you are agreeable and can spare
the time, I would like to wait on you today at any time conve-
nient to you.
Yours sincerely,
A.I.C.C File No. 16-C, 1931. Courtesy; Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
