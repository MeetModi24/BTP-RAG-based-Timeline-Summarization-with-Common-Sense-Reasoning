478. LETTER TO POTTI SRIRAMULU
BHANGI NIVAS,
NEW DELHI,
September 26, 1946
BHAI SHRIRAMULU,
Bapuji got your letter. He says that for a Hindu to fast when Muslims
are killed and thus to give up his life is quite wrong. One should not do
this. About the temple of Nellore, Bapuji was under the impression that it
had been thrown open to the Harijans. Do not be hasty. The Madras Gov-
ernment say that they are going to have the temple opened to Harijans.
Yours,
AMRIT KAUR
