564. LETTER TO KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
October 5, 1945
VAHALAN BEHN,
Your letter requires no argument. It needs prayer. You must
go your own way. You sisters are all chips of the old block.
It is therefore well with you.
Just as there is acceptance
so is there rejection of a thing. Similarly there can be neither
rejection nor acceptance. Truth-seeker must have that position
also. I neither accept nor reject the statement that there is life
on the planet Mars.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
ORIENT CLUB, BOMBAY 7
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
