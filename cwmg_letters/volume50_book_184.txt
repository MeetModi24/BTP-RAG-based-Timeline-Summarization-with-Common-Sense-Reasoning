184. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
July 8, 1932
DEAR PUSHPA,
You have improved your handwriting very much.
To write a biography means to write whatever we know
about the life of a person which is fit to be published. If one
feels sleepy at the prayer time, one must stand up.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 197
