528. LETTER TO VANAMALA PARIKH
December 14, 1930
CHI. VANAMALA,
As you used quite a big slip of paper for me, I have pre-
served it. If Dhiru uses bad language, you should lovingly per-
suade him not to do so. You should also inform Premabehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5756
