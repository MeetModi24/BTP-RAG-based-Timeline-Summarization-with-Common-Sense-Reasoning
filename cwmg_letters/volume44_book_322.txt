322. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 17, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (DESAI),
I have your letter. I shall expect letters from you from time
to time. For the present, you should write regularly. Do not get
disheartened. I saw Pyarelal again. I shall see him once more.
He has no complaint now. I read in the papers about the
Government having taken possession of the Sevashram hospitals too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1807
