478. LETTER TO LILAVATI
September 8, 1934
CHI. LILAVATI,
I got your letter. If you hear from Siddhimati, give me the
news about her.
Do all of you eat your meals at different times or together?
How many take your meals together? Do you get any fruit there?
Do you get good milk ?
What books do you read? I should like you to Improve your
handwriting still further.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9329. Also C.W. 6604. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
