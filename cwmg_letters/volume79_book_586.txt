586. LETTER TO N. C. VAKIL
BOMBAY,
April 3, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I have not a moment to spare.
Please, therefore, send me your concrete scheme in a nutshell
and if necessary I shall make time for you during my stay in
B[om]bay.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. N. C. VAKIL
CHAIRMAN, B.H.A.
311 TARDEO ROAD
BOMBAY 7
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
