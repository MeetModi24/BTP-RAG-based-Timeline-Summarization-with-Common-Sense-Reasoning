388. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 30, 1941
DAUGHTER,
You have sent for Zohra to Bombay. This I consider a great
mistake. Akbar showed me your letter. If Zohra does not go to
Aligarh, she may come here. If she wants to stay in Indore, she
may. She is sure to lose in Bombay. I am all right.
Rajkumari
is going to Benares today for Vidyarthi Sammelan. Manu is still
having fever.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 468
