359. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
September 22, 1930
CHI. SHANKERLAL,
On the 18th I had a letter ready for Behn. On the 19th I
got a wire signed by you three. These days I am taken up by
the idea of a book on spinning. Many problems come up which
a book should help us to solve. I shall write more about this if
you feel that my suggestion can be carried out.
Do keep writing to me. I do not know how long the present
arrangement of writing and receiving letters will last. How is your
health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: G. N. 11554
