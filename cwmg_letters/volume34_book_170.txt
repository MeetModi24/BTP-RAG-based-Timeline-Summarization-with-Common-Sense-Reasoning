170. LETTER TO JETHALAL GANDHI
BANGALORE,
Ashadh Vad 5 [July 19, 1927]
BHAI JETHALAL,
I got your letter and the booklet about snatakas which you
have published. I could read the booklet only today. I can
see that you have put great labour into it. The booklet is useful.
I will find use for it in Young India and Navajivan too.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Sabarmati Snataka Vibhag, Book 6, No. 4, Shishir, 1984, p. 34
