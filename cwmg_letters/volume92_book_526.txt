526. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Y. M.,
May 5, 1932
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Dahyabhai first gave me the news about Indu's illness. I
have now obtained more information from Shankerlal. How did
Indu fall ill? How is she now? Is it because of her illness that
you are there? How long will you remain there? How are you?
Tell Saralabehn I am deliberately not writing any letter to
her. Of course I think of everybody.
We are well.
You must have come to know of Yashoda's death. Give
our regards to everybody there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: G. N. 11557
