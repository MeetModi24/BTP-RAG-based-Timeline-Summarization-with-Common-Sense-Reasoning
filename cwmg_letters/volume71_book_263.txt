263. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
SANTINIKETAN,
February 18, 1940
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I trust you are doing well. Since Sushilabehn is there, I
do not worry about anything. I hope you are well looked after.
A[mtul] S[alaam]behn was worrying about you.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9734. Also C.W. 714. Courtesy:
Navajivan Trust
