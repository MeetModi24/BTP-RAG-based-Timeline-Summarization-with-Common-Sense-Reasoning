111. LETTER TO JAMNA N. GANDHI
January 12, 1931
CHI. JAMNA,
You are welcome to the Ashram, but see that you do not lose
there what you have gained in Bombay. If you think that your
health suffers in the Ashram, you should see no harm in staying
in Bombay for education. Really speaking, we should know the art
of adjusting our mode of living to any climate. If we do not know
it, however, and if we can easily go and live where we can keep
good health, it is best that we do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 846. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
