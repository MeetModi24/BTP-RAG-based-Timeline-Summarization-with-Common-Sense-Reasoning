154. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 28, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Today also only a postcard. The blood-pressure was 134/84 this
afternoon, and 158/96 in the morning. Ba, Mahadev and Ramdas are
going to visit Pyarelal tomorrow. The permission was received only
today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
84
