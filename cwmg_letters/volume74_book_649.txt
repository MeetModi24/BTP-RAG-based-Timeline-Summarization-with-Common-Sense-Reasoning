649. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 10, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
This is according to the new rule. Your note is cheery.
May the improvement be steady and continuous.
Everybody
is wondering whether you are at all coming. I continue to say
you are though an uncertainty has crept into my heart.
Kamaladevi is leaving just today. She will meet her mother
and come over for a few days' stay. Dr. Das will leave for Cal-
cutta in two or three days; he is going for a few days only.
Satyamurti has come today and is putting up at the Bungalow.
The weather here is generally good, though it warms up a
little in the afternoon. The rest is O. K.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4253. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7885
