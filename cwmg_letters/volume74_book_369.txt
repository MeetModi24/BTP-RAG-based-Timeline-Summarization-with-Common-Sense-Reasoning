369. LETTER TO MIRZA ISMAIL
August 12, 1941
DEAR SIR MIRZA,
I have your very interesting proposal for which many thanks.
It does not attract me for the simple reason that I do not trust
the British Government to do the right thing. Moreover any
participation in conferences invited by the Government presupposes
aid in the war effort by the participants. I cannot, I must not.
My love to the whole family.
Yours sincerely,
M. K.
