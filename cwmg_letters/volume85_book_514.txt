514. LETTER TO JAMSHED MEHTA
NEW DELHI,
October 1, 1946
BHAI JAMSHEDJI,
Tulsiram is an admirer of yours. That is why I was drawn
towards him. He pleaded with me to write something to you.
I told him that I knew you better than he did. You are
dedicated to service. May your work of service grow.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. JAMSHED MEHTA
KARACHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
