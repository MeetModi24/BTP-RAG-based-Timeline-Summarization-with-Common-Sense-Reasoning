118. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 18, 1939
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter. I will write more if I get time. Tell
Chhaganlal that I have not been able to write to him. I shall
arrange to send somebody if the drought continues. Whom should
I send on the Rentia Baras Day? How about Mirabehn?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8560. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
