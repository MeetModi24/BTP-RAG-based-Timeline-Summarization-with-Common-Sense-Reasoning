342. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 23, 1930
CHI. SHARDA,
There is no question of jealousy when it comes to learning
from somebody. Who says it is jealousy? Don't again let asthma
come anywhere near you. Your handwriting is not bad at all.
It will no doubt improve gradually. Where is Shakaribehn?
Tell her or write to her that she should write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9893. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
wala
