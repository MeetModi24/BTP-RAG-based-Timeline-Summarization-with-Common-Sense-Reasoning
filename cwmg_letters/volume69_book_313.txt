313. LETTER TO MANU SUBEDAR
May 27, 1939
BHAI SUBEDAR1,
I got your letter. You have suggested a very difficult way.
It seems almost impossible to harmonize mutually contradictory
points of view and run the Government. It is one thing to do
justice to all points of view and be generous to those holding
the minority view, but to include all parties in the Ministry
seems another matter altogether. I have made no attempt at
all to study the working of the different Ministries and do not
know their problems. All the same I intend to put your sug-
gestion before the Bombay Ministry. But before doing that I
intend to put your letter before Sardar. You do not object to
that, do you? I will not proceed further before I hear from you.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From Gujarati: C.W. 5803
