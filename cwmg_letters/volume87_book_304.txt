304. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
PATNA,
April 22, 1947
CHI. BRAJKISAN,
I have your two letters. My going there is not certain. Do
not believe the newspapers.
If I invite the Socialists there will be an outcry in the coun-
try. It would be better that Jayaprakash sees me whenever
necessary. The whole situation is getting out of hand. No one
knows how it can be remedied. You should not worry. It will
be as God wishes.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I got the list.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 2502
