201. LETTER TO ZOHRA ANSARI
June 29, 1941
DAUGHTER ZOHRA,
I was delighted to have your letter. Faridbhai writes and
says that you looked after his mother very well. God will look
after your well-being. Have some patience. One day I shall
certainly let you go to jail. I hope Shaukat is all right. Love
to the children.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Urdu: Mahatma, Vol. VI, between pp. 48 and 49
