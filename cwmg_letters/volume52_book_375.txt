375. LETTER TO T. S. KALYANA RAMA IYER
December 24, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 16th instant. I have since heard from
co-workers and I find that every precaution that should be legiti-
mately taken was taken in order to ensure voting on the part of
those who are bona-jide temple-goers. Thus, the question was how
to distinguish the bona-jide temple-goers from the non-believers
in temple going.
I accept Sjt. Rajagopalachari's statement
that it was not possible to have "an inquisition or a confession of
faith". When the referendum is completed and the whole report
is before me, I should be better able to estimate the value of the
referendum. After all, the referendum by itself is not going to
decide the Guruvayur temple-entry question, but it would be a
material factor in helping me to decide whether the contem-
plated fast is to be taken or postponed.
Yours sincerely,
