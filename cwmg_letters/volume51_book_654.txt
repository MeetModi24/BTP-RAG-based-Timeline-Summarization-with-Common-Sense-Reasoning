654. LETTER TO KESHORAM TANDON
November 13, 1932
BHAI KESHORAM TANDON,
I have your letter. The eradication of untouchability does
not necessarily imply inter-dining and intermarriage.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI KESHORAM TANDON
JAMUNA COAL TRADING CO.
FARUKHABAD, U.P.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5057
