385. LETTER TO DR. S. C. ANAND
November 14, 1934
DEAR DR. ANAND,
I appreciate your offer to assist the All-India Village Indus-
tries Association that is being formed. I would like you also to
tell me what work you and the two friends whom you mention
will do. Can Mr. Goyal give me the comparative food values of
gur and sugar? I am passing on your letter to Sjt. Kumarappa.
Yours sincerely,
DR. S. C. ANAND, M.B.B.S., LIEUT. I.M.S. (RETD.)
DASSAN STREET
DELHI
