240. LETTER TO JATINDERNATH
PANCHGANI,
July 9, 1944
DEAR JATINDERNATH,
I have gone through your letter. It makes me sad. Ill as I
am, I am powerless to help much. But taking the recital as
true, the question is why did not the two men who were so
brutally assaulted resist unto death. Non-violent resistance is
possible without inflicting injury on the injurer. I know this is
more easily said than done. But if we are to learn the art of
non-violence, we must refuse to be living witnesses of our own
degradation. You are at liberty to show this to any friend you
like. Mind, I cast no reflection on the living ones. My object
is to point the moral.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
