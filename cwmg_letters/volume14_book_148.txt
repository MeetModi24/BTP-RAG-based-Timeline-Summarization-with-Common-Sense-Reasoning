148. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
AHMEDABAD,
Maha Vad 5 [March 3, 1918]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
Santok and Ramdas arrived here yesterday. They will leave
for Rajkot tomorrow.
Shri Khushalbhai and Narandas are against letting Krishna
and Purushottam go, and so the idea has been given up. I too
thought they were right. If Purushottam goes to Rajkot, we
must also let him go to Morabi. If Krishna goes to Rajkot this
time, we should have to let others, too, go to their own places.
I thought, therefore, that, though you would have liked them to
go, [it would be better] not to let them, especially as the elders,
too, were of the same mind . . . .
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5733
