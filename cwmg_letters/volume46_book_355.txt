355. LETTER TO G. V. KETKAR
As AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
Yes, Syt. Rajawade's case caused me great deal of trouble
and worry.
I have just got the complete file of Syt. Rajah's case. I am
studying it.
Nothing can be done in the Chimer case just at present.
Deo did write to me recently.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SYT. G. V. KETKAR
THE KESARI AND THE MAHARATTA OFFICE
568 NARAYAN PETH, POONA CITY
From a photostat: G.N. 7965. Also C.W. 980. Courtesy: G. V. Ketkar
