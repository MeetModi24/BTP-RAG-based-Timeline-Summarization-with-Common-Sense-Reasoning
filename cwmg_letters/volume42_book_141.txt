141. LETTER TO LADY MONCRIEFF-SMITH
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. The monies that
I receive are earmarked for certain definite charitable purposes.
I am therefore unable to use them for any other purpose how-
ever laudable it may be.
Yours sincerely,
LADY MONCRIEFF-SMITH
CHAIRMAN, DELHI HEALTH AND BABY WEEK 1930
19 AKBAR ROAD, NEW DELHI
From a photostat: S.N. 15739
