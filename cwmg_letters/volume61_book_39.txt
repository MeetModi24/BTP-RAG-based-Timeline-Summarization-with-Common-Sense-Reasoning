39. LETTER TO AYODHYA PRASAD
May 1, 1935
BHAI AYODHYA PRASAD,
Your letter to Avadhesh was read out to me. If you wish
that he should remain with me you have to forgo the temptation
of calling him for a short visit.
It would cause interruption in
work and waste of money.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3216
