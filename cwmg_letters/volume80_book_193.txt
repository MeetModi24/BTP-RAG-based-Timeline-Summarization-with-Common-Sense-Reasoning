193. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
May 12, 1945
SHRI CHHAGANLAL,
I have gone through your long letter to Bapa. He suggests
that I should see you. I have written to you that I am always
ready to see you. So come and see me whenever and wherever
you want. Remember that whatever difficulties we imagine are
created by us.
Convey my blessings to everyone there.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
ANAND BHAVAN
RAJKOT
KATHIAWAR
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
