518. LETTER TO LALCHAND
September 29, 1945
BHAI LALCHAND,
I had your letter and I had made inquiries. I am sorry to
learn about Pandit Ramarakhamal. Console his family members
on my behalf. I feel that the respect that was shown to the late
Atma Singh was misplaced.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI LALCHAND, CLERK
Loco WHEELSHOP, N. W. R., MOGHULPURA
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
