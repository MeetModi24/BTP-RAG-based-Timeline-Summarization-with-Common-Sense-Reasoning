281. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
Kartik Vad 3 [November 14, 1924]
BHAISHRI KAKA 2 ,
After writing an article for the Kelavani issue I have
started thinking more about children's education. Can't we try
it on the Ashram children? That is, if the ideas stated there
appeal to you. A child calls a pot a pot, but he does not draw
a picture of it. Similarly he may read the alphabet but may not
draw pictures of the letters. A child hears a new word before
he reads it and he pronounces it or rather repeats it as he
hears it. Why shouldn't we stop Lakshmi, Rasik and the other
children from writing and teach them first to draw? Could we
not give them a good many things orally? Just now they should
use the hand for drawing only. For this the teacher should know
the rudiments of drawing. I am now going deeper into the subject
and hence stop here. For the present just think over it. More
when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
