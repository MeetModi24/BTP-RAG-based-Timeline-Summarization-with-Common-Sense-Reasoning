585. LETTER TO L. N. GUBIL SUNDARESAN
WARDHA,
April 14, 1935
I do not know anyone who can be spared from India. I do
not know if any of the ardent Congressmen can suggest a solu-
tion to this difficulty. But I sincerely feel that unless we are able
to find out an Indian fit enough to discharge the duties of a represen-
tative, we and the Americans cannot understand each other
so well as we ought to.
The Hindu, 17-4-1935
