373. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
August 12, 1941
DEAR SISTER,
Mahadev is collecting funds for relief. I have not seen
Ramachandran's letter of July 18. Send it to me and I shall
reply.
I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7999. Also C.W. 3090. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
