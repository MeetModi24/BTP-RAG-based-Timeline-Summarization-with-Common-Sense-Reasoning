154. LETTER TO K. RAJAGOPALACHARIAR
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
July 5, 1921
DEAR SIR,
Received yours of the 28th last. I am shortly dealing with the
matter referred to in your letter in Young India. Thank you for your
suggestion.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI K. RAJAGOPALACHARIAR
TIRUPATI
MADRAS STATE
From a photostat: G.N. 5668
