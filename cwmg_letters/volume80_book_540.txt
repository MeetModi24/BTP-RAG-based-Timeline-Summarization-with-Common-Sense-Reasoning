540. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
June 14, 1945
CHI. A. SALAAM,
I got your letter. There was a letter from Kanchan from
Sevagram. You may come only when you can do so in a re-
laxed frame of mind. Why should you ask for a message from
me? You yourself are there. What more do you need? You
should, on the contrary, teach others not to ask for messages
from me. I am writing this reply at once. Hamid (Amina's)
has come here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 498
