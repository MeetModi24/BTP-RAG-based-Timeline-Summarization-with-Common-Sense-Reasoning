150. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
May 4, 1937
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Where do you intend to take me? Wherever you take
me, you will have to look after a big crowd. I won't be able to
dissuade anybody from joining. I personally don't mind, but
we must consider the convenience of the owner of the bungalow
in which we are lodged. Mirabehn has already served notice.
She will accompany me wherever I go this time. I myself
don't think that I need sea air. You may certainly keep me
in Bardoli for as long as you wish, or in Surat, if you like. If,
you don't mind a big crowd accompanying me, please don't
think that I am objecting to it, though of course I do feel very
hesitant about it. Here is the list as at this moment:
Ba, Kano, Mira, Pyarelal, Mahadev, Radhakrishna, Kanu,
Manoharlal, Sharda.
I hope you are taking rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
DR. KANUGA'S BUNGALOW
ELLIS BRIDGE, AHMEDABAD
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 200
