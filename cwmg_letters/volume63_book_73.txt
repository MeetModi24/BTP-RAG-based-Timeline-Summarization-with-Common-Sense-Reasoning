73. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
June 17, 1936
CHI. SHARMA,
I have two letters from you. I am sorry to hear the news
from your family. 1 Do treat the patients if you have now gained
confidence in your treatment or leave them to themselves; let
them carry on with the routine treatment.
Come over here to discuss your own future and Draupadi's
too. That I am living away in Segaon should not matter. It
is possible to make daily trips from Maganwadi. The air is
pretty cool here, the rains have been copious and have not stop-
ped even now. What was the total expense of your travels?
Did you achieve what you wanted? Is there still anything else
that you might have learnt there? Did you acquire sufficient
knowledge of anatomy-physiology?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 253
