427. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[Before October 2, 1936] 1
CHI. MAHADEV,
I didn't at all like your returning after having arrived so
late in the day. However, I did not have the courage to insist
on your staying over, with the result that both of you were quite
exhausted and reached at 11 in the night. I would not wish
to ask anyone to stop over and sleep for the night in this wea-
ther. Nanavati's fever persists. It is not malaria; it may be
typhoid. There is no need to send a doctor from there; I
hope to cope with it.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11497
