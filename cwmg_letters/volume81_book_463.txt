463. LETTER TO VINA CHATTERJEE
September 17, 1945
CHI. VINA,
I have your letter. You will have to wait for your mother's
sake. I remember to have already written to you. If not, it
may be due to oversight. I feel your going to Calcutta is a
waste but your heart is there and, if Sailen bears the expense, you
can go. Maybe, it would be better if you went after your
marriage. I won't be able to help you in this. I hope you
are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
