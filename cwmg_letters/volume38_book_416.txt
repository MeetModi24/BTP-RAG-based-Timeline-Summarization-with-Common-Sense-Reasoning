416. LETTER TO ZAFAR-UL-MULK
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 17, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have kept the letter you left in Calcutta all this time. I was
sorry indeed that I was silent when you came. I had not thought
of the silence when I asked you to come again. I was none the
less sorry than you were that we could not talk. Do please write
to me whatever you want to say and if you get the time do
come and pass a few days when I am at the Ashram. At the pre-
sent moment I am preparing to go to Sind.
Yours sincerely,
ZAFAR-UL-MULK, ESQ.
LUCKNOW
From a microfilm: S.N. 14986
