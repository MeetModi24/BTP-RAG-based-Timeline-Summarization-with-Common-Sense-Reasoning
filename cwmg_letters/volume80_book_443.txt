443. LETTER TO GOPE GURBUXANI
PANCHGANI,
June 6, 1945
CHI. GURBUXANI,
I have your postcard. You have yourself said that you do
not want a reply to every letter. Even so, I have written quite
a few letters. You should not have published what I have written
to you and you should have had the English version passed by
me. I have noted that you are working for an Insurance com-
pany.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1315
