107. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
JUHU,
May 20, 1944
MY DEAR BROTHER,
Bapa just informs me of your loss. Why should I be the
only loser through death of a dear one. My sympathy in abun-
dance, if your philosophy stands in such need.
I feel like being in a wilderness. The so-called freedom jars.
But God is our help. And so I take heart.
With love.
Yours,
LITTLE BROTHER
From a photostat: G.N. 8821; also Letters of the Right Honourable V. S.
Srinivasa Sastri, pp. 358-
