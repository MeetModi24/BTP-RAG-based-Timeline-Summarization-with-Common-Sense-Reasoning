350. LETTER TO ABANINDRANATH TAGORE
SODEPUR,
December 21, 1945
DEAR ABANI BABU1,
I hope you received my telegram sent to you yesterday
from Santiniketan. How I wish it were possible for me, being
in Calcutta, to come to you and meet you face to face! But I
know that I must deny myself that pleasure!
I went yesterday morning to see Nand Babu's museum and
in it in a glass case he showed me some rare specimens of your
producing beautiful art out of almost nothing, even straws!
You must live to give India and the world more of such
things.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI ABANINDRANATH TAGORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
