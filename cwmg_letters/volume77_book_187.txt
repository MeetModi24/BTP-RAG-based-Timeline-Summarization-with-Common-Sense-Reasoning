187. LETTER TO SURENDRA
[After June 17, 1944]
CHI. SURENDRA,
I have your letter. Are you troubled by the menace of dogs?
Of jackals? Rabid foxes? Snakes? How is the dwelling? Is the
foundation high enough? Does it have a verandah? How many
rooms does it have? How is the roofing? What are you reading
these days? Do you take any exercise? What work are you doing
among the people? I have nothing to say about your not parti-
cipating in any public activities. It is good that you refrain
from coming to me. But don't hesitate to pay me a visit, if you
wish to, when I reach Sevagram. Nathji had met me. He will
come here. I am here till the 30th. I am keeping fairly well.
The two [kinds of] worms won't leave me yet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
