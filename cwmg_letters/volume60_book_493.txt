493. LETTER TO KASIM ALI
March 30, 1935
SAIYED SAHEB,
I have your letter. I shall inquire regarding the matter at
Indore. I am of course also looking for the book.
We have not started having any books written for the Village
Industries Association. When did you send your application? I
have been hunting for it in the office here. Send me a copy. How
and where would you work for the Sangh?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SAIYED KASIM ALI
VISHARAD
BETUL, C. P.
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9749
