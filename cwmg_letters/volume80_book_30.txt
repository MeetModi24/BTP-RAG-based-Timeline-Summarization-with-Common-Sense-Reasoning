30. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 26, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I was sorry to read about Ram Narayan's illness. Keep me
informed. Let us hope he will recover.
Balkrishna has lost a lot of weight. Are not dates available
in the Ashram? Look in my room. If there are any, give them
to him; if not, let Chimanlal write to Rameshwar Das Birla. You
will have the dates.
I understand about Om Prakash. Put up with him as long
as you can.
Speak again to Ashadevi. You should do what you can
easily do.
The discharge will stop. It is not anybody's duty to sleep
on hot sand. You can use my wooden divan. If you sprinkle
some water over the sand, the heat will disappear.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4511. Also C.W. 5897. Courtesy:
Krishnachandra
