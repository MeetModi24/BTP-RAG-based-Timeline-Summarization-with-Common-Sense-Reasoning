547. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
February 1, 1947
CHI. MANILAL,
I got your fine letter. You seem to have made good
changes in your diet. I like them. My tapascharya is like the hills
which seem beautiful from a distance. I undergo no suffering.
People come and see me daily. My ahimsa is being severely
tested. You need not worry, however. Remain absorbed in your
duty. Do not let the fact of Manu sleeping with me perturb
you. I believe that it is God who has prompted me to take
that step. If, however, you cannot understand, do not get upset
and bear with me. I write this because Kishorelal and others
have got upset. I see no reason for that at all. I think Sushila
will go there at the earliest opportunity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati : C. W. 1445. Courtesy: Sushila Gandhi
