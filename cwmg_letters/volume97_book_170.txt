170. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
October 23, 1930
CHI. RAMDAS,
I have had no letter from you. I have asked you to write to me at least
once a week so long as you are free. Reply to my questions.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
