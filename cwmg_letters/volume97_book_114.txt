114. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
Tuesday Evening [February 26, 1929]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I see from Nirmala's letter to Ba which I read today that Ba should not
accompany me . Deep down in her heart, Nirmala is restless. Most probably
Ba will arrive by tomorrow night's train and reach Ahmedabad the next
morning.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 33097
