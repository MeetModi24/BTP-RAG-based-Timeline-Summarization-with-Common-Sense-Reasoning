668. LETTER TO MOHAMMAD YUNUS
April 17, 1945
BHAI YUNUS,
I have your letter. May your marriage prove good for
both of you and for the country. May both live in harmony
and render service to the country. You may come whenever you
feel like it.
Blessings from
BAPU
BHAI YUNUS
KHADI GATE
PESHAWAR
From a copy of the Urdu : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
