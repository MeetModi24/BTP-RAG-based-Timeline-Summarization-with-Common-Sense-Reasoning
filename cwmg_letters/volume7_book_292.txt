292. LETTER TO G. K. GOKHALE
JOHANNESBURG,
November 22, 1907
DEAR PROFESSOR GOKHALE,
I have sent a letter addressed to you through Mr. Ameeroodeen
Fajandar, one of the delegates from the Transvaal who will attend the
Congress at Surat.
May I draw your attention to the fact that the
struggle we are undergoing here has resulted in making us feel that we
are Indians first and Hindus, Mahomedans, Tamils, Parsees, etc. after-
wards. You will notice, too, that all our delegates are Mahomedans.
I am personally glad of the fact. And it may also happen that there will
be many Mahomedans, having South African connections, attending the
Congress. May I ask you to interest yourself in them and make them
feel perfectly at home? A Hindu-Mahomedan compact may even
become a special feature of this Congress. The rest of the struggle you
know from the papers.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
