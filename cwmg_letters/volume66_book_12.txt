12. LETTER TO ATULANANDA CHAKRABARTY
August 3, 1937
DEAR ATULANANDA,
I hope your daughter is well and wholly out of danger.
I have gone through your articles carefully. I still do not see light.
It seems to me that no culture league will answer the purpose you
and I have in view. It has got to be done by individuals who
have a living faith and who would work with missionary zeal. Try
again, if I have not seen what you see in your proposal. I shall be
patient and attentive. I want to help if I can see my way clear.
Yours sincerely,
M . K . GANDHI
The Lift of Mahatma Gandhi, p. 366
