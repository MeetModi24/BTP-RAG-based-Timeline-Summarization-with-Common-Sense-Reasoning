639. LETTER TO MARGARET JONES
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 6, 1941
CHI. KAMALA,
You have done well in giving me your long letter which
I am sending to Mary.
I had no doubt about your passing
with distinction. May you finish your course equally well.
Of course you will write when you like.
BAPU
Bapu—Conversations and Correspondence with Mahatma Gandhi, p. 197
