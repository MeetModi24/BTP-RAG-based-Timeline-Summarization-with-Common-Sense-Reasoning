524. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA TRIVEDI
December 13, 1930
CHI. RAMACHANDRA,
I have your letter. Write in ink and shape the letters well.
Good that all have arrived at the Ashram. Tell Jiji to give up
the practice of untouchability and live in peace. There is no
dharma in untouchability.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5265
