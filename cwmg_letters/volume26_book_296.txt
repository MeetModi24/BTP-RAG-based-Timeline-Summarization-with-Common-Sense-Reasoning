296. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
Chaitra Krishna 12 [April 20, 1925]
BHAI BRIJKRISHNA 2 ,
I have your letter. I liked it very much. I shall write
something about Delhi on the strength of the letter. I hope your
mind is quite calm.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2349
