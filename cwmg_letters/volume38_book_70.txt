70. LETTER TO MURIEL LESTER
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 16, 1928
I have your two letters. I was delighted to have your
description about the opening ceremony. I hope you will flou-
rish more and more. Do keep me in touch with your movements.
I should certainly be delighted to see Mrs. Winifred Dicken-
son when she comes.
I don't need to give you any information about the Ashram
because you must glean everything about the Ashram from the
pages of Young India.
Yours sincerely,
MISS MURIEL LESTER
KINGSLEY HALL, POWIS ROAD, BOW, E. 3
ENGLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 14414
