165. LETTER TO FEROZABEHN TALEYARKHAN
July 6, 1932
DEAR SISTER,
Received your letter. You must be having the benefit of cold
weather.
Information has been received about Devdas being
completely cured and hence there is no cause for anxiety. You
are keeping yourself well-informed. Ba is happy.
My visits are
still disallowed. Vandemataram from the Sardar and Mahadev.
Blessings from
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No. 800
(40)(3), Pt. II, p. 173
