271. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
RAJKOT,
May 13, 1939
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. You did a very good thing in visiting
the patient. Do keep me informed from time to time. Write
to him that I often inquire after his health. Take proper care
of your health also. Sushila will write to you in detail about
it. Her health has gone down considerably.
Ba is in Bombay and will come here after four or five days.
I am well. The nights are cool here, and so we don't feel the
heat very much.
The struggle will drag on. There is no cause for worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3532
