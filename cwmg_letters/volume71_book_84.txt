84. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 28, 1939
CHI. KANAIYO,
You are certainly lazy in the matter of writing letters. You
know that at present I would expect letters from you. There has
been nothing from Radha, either, after two letters from her.
Don't you think you have been hasty in resuming the use of
your throat? If we consult a doctor and if his instructions are not
contrary to dharma, we should follow them. Now write to me in
detail. Can you use your throat for all purposes? Do you find any
difficulty in talking or singing? Remember that if there is scarcity
of water, etc., it is the duty of all those who are not needed
there to leave the place. I did not remember this, otherwise
perhaps I would have dissuaded you from going to Rajkot. Now
keep this in mind while you are there.
Inquire about Manjula and find out how she is.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
