284. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL G. BANKER
Monday
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I have your note. I am pained. If you have calmed down, come
now or any time. I want you to have peace. If you do not come now,
I shall definitely wait for you in the morning. But I want you to come
right away.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 32721
