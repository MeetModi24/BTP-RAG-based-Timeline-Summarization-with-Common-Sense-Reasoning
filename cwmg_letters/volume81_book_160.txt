160. LETTER TO PREMLILA THACKERSEY
August 9, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter.
I should certainly like to stay with you. But you know
I am not going there for a change. I want to take Sardar to
Dinshaw's clinic for treatment. You should, however, assume
that I am staying with you. I will assume so myself. I hope
to start from here on the 19th. I am waiting for Sardar's letter.
You got a fine samadhi constructed. That was good. Arrange-
ments have also been made for people to visit it.
I hope all of you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4836. Courtesy: Premlila
Thackersey
