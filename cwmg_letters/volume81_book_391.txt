391. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
POONA,
September 4, 1945
CHI. DEVDAS,
I have your postcard. Do come over. Rajkumari had a
talk with me. She wrote to you only yesterday. I hope you
are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DEVDAS GANDHI
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
