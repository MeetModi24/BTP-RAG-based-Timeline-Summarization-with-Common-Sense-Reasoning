452. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 10, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
All the way through I kept thinking of you. Why am I not
able to make you understand my point? Maybe my love is
imperfect or, which is the same thing, impure. It is acceptable
to me that you have come to me for my sake and you are going
to stay also for the same reason. But those who come only for
my sake also do my work and at times there is separation. But
that is only for the sake of my work, not otherwise. This much
in brief. Your interpretation of it has no place in it. You will
be well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
