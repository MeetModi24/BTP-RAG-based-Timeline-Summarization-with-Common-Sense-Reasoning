520. LETTER TO PYARELAL
June 15, 1940
CHI. PYARELAL,
What you have written is certainly useful. That means
that the blame I put on A. S. was not justified.
Then she said that the papers fell out from her Koran.
They were saved from falling into somebody's hands. What did
those papers contain? Into whose hands could they have
fallen? Or have I understood it all wrong?
On the whole in your opinion she is a saintly woman and
I am being unfair to her.
BAPU
From the
Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial
