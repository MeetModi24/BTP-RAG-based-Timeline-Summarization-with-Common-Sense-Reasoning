464. LETTER TO RAGHUNATH S. DHOTRE
ON THE STEAMER TO SODEPUR,
January 3, 1946
CHI. DHOTRE,
I think I told you to resume the remittances to Ramachan-
dran. If not, please understand from this letter that the money
is to be paid from October 1945. That makes three months to
date. Send a sum to cover this period and later send a hundred
rupees every month. The address is: Shri G. Ramachandran,
c/o Gandhi Ashram, Tiruchengodu.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
