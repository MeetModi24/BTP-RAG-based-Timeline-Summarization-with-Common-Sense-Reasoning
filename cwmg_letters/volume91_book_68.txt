68. LETTER TO V. G. CHERTKOV
TOLSTOY FARM,
LAWLEY STATION,
TRANSVAAL,
November 26, 1910
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter of the 29th September last for which
I thank you. I have also received your translation of Tolstoy's
letter to me on Passive Resistance. It is a most pathetic thing
that your letter should have been received after his death.
You will see from a few copies of Indian Opinion I am sending
you, that I had the letter already translated by a friend here.
Mr. Kallenbach got the translation made.
I thank you for introducing Mrs. Mayo's name to me. The
lady has already written to Mr. Kallenbach. I am now sending
her all the particulars needed by her.
I shall appreciate a few copies of the magazine in which
Tolstoy's letter may be published.
I am,
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
V. G. CHERTKOV
From a photostat: C.W. 11073. Courtesy: L. N. Tolstoy
