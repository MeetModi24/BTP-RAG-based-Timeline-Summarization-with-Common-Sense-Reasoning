534. LETTER TO SULTANA QURESHI
SEVAGRAM,
December 3, 1944
DAUGHTER SULTANA1,
Now you are married. It is good that you found a partner
within the family itself. May you both be happy and bring credit
to Imam Saheb by your selfless service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10764. Courtesy: Ghulam
Rasool Qureshi
