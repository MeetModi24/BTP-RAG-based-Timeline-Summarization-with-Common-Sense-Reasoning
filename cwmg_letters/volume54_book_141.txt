141. LETTER TO D. VALISINHA
March 18, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and a copy of the Maha Bodhi
Journal.
My attempt is not to replace Hinduism by any other ism, but
to reform Hinduism. My own belief is that Gautam Buddha
himself did not desire to found a separate sect or religion but to
rid Hinduism of what appeared to him to be evil. In any case, it
is my conviction that there is no occasion whatsoever for Hindus
to change their faith.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. D. VALISINHA
GENERAL SECRETARY
MAHA BODHI SOCIETY
4-A COLLEGE SQUARE
CALCUTTA
