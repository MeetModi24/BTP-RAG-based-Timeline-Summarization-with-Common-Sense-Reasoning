59. LETTER TO MAN/BERN PATEL
SEGAON,
August 13, 1937
CHI. MANI,
The car should wait for me tomorrow morning 7.30 near the
railway crossing. I expect to reach there about that time.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Please send the accompanying note to Chhotelal just now
or in the morning. Inform Mahadev so that he may put up to
me whatever work he wants to.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11550
