318. LETTER TO S. K. PATIL
July 12, 1947
You are enunciating the doctrine of an eye for an eye and
a tooth for a tooth. Only you will wait till the 15th August.
Both these statements ill conform with the Congress policy. Has
the Congress policy changed? Congressmen have changed I know,
but I am not aware of any change in the Congress constitution.
Secondly, if Congress policy or practice changed who com-
pels you to wait till 15th August? Who will be responsible for
the incalculable harm that will have overtaken the people of
India as well as Pakistan in the meantime?
Who can control
the people if they go mad and launch on a course of retaliation?
Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase, Vol. II, p. 308
