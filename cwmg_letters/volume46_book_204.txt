204. LETTER TO BOYD TUCKER
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 19, 1931
MY DEAR BOYD,
I have your letter. I understand more fully the reason for
your wanting to accompany me to London should I go there. At
the present moment however there is neither any prospect of
going to London nor to America, to America much less. I shall
certainly publish your letter regarding missionary enterprise.
BOYD TUCKER, ESQ.
C/O POSTMASTER
SRINAGAR (KASHMIR)
From a photostat: S.N. 17100
