8. LETTER TO SYED ZAHIRUL HAQ
SABARMATI,
November 10, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. It may flatter my pride, but I
hope I have none. I am conscious of my unfitness and limitations.
If there was the slightest chance of successful intervention, my
seclusion would not deter me. But I see none. I therefore
remain still and pray.
The spinning-wheel is not dearer to me than precious lives.
I take to it as a child to its mother's breasts, because I believe it
to possess the capacity of saving millions of lives from penury
and degradation. I commend it to you.
Yours sincerely,
The Hindu, 19-11-1926
