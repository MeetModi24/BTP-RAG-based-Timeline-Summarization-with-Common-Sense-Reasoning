322. LETTER TO PREMA KANTAK
SEVAGRAM,
July 5, 1942
CHI. PREMA,
I got your letter. You have sought my permission to
come here. So far as I am concerned, you have it. Deo's per-
mission also is essential. Get your doubts removed when you
come. If you use your intelligence, you yourself can solve all
your doubts. I assure you that there is no substance in them.
There is no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10428. Also C.W. 6867. Courtesy:
Prema Kantak
