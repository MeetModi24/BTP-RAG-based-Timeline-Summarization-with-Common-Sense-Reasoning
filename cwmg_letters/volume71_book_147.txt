147. LETTER TO KESHAVRAM R. TRIVEDI
January 19, 1940
BHAI KESHAVRAM,
I am very late in replying to your letter. You should consult
Lakshmidasbhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
KESHAVRAM RAMSHANKAR TRIVEDI SISODRAWALA
SHUDDHA KHADI BHANDAR
NAVASARI
B. B. & C. I. RLY.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
