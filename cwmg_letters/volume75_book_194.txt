194. LETTER TO SAMPURNANAND
November 29, 1941
BHAI SAMPURNANAND,
I got your letter and the book. I like your line of thinking,
so I shall certainly try and read the book. Prabhavati will read it
first. She asked for the book as soon as she saw it. At the moment
she is on her way to Jayaprakash from where she will proceed to
Bihar. I shall get the book only at Benares.
I hope you were all right in the jail.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SAMPURNANANDJI
JALIPADEVI
BENARES
From the Hindi original: Sampurnanand Collection. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
