320. LETTER TO S. RADHAKRISHNAN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 28, 1940
DEAR SIR RADHAKRISHNAN,
I have just finished your convocation address which you so
kindly sent me. I liked it very much. Your language is all your
own. You will, however, let me say that I miss the strength which
I would expect from your pen or speech. The message of non-
violence demands the utmost strength without sting behind it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a facsimile: Mahatma, Life of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, Vol. 6,
between pp. 8 and 9
