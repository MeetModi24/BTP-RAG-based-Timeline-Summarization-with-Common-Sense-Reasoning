286. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 28, 1938
CHI. KANTI,
I got your letter. I am returning Saraswati's letters.
She
writes to me also in the same strain. I feel that you two should
get married and after marriage, as you suggest, stay separately in
Bangalore and observe self-control till your studies are over. But
I see positive harm in keeping Saraswati in Bangalore without
marriage. After you get married, Saraswati perhaps may not
insist on staying with you and may come to me or go to Trivan-
drum. I am prepared to write to Ramachandran on hearing
from you. I have discussed the matter with Navin. If Rama-
chandran put those questions, it is a painful thing.
If you have given up milk and ghee, you have certainly not
done right. It would be duragraha. Resume normal food immed-
iately. If necessary send me a wire.
Saraswati regularly writes to me, and so do I to her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7343. Courtesy: Kantilal Gandhi
