330. LETTER TO A. K. CHANDA
August 31, 1945
BHAI CHANDA,
For the time being please do not take any decision about my
visit to Silchar. Let us see what God bids me do, and where He
takes me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI A. K. CHANDA
SILCHAR (ASSAM)
