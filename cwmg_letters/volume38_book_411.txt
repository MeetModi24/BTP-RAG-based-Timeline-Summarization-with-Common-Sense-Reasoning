411. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Wednesday [On or before January 16, 1929]
CHI. NARANDAS,
You seem to have been hurt. I would have been happy to
respect your wishes in regard to Chi. Santok3, but I did not ap-
prove of your plan. For the manner of living of the mother and
the daughters is so expensive that they would always have been
a cause of discontent in the Ashram. Santok never liked the
common kitchen, nor the inmates of the Ashram.
About the khadi training section, I have already stated my
view that neither suggestions can be supported. I see nothing
wrong in the view that the Ashram and not an individual should
have the agency. I shall decide finally on the 18th at the
latest. I want you to take interest in all activities and participate
in them. Trust Chhaganlal. He is a sincere and hard-working
man. Do not mind his errors, but consider his motive.
About Sannabhai, I have simply said that Chhaganlal Joshi's
decision should be final. For no one of whom the secretary
does not approve should remain.
You may write to me anything you wish to. If you can come
over for the 18th, do so. If you think that both of you should
come, do that. If you do not, I shall reach the decision which
seems best to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro- 9: Shri Narandas Gandhine— Part I, p. 47
