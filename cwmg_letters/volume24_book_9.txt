9. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
ANDHERI,
Thursday [May 8, 1924]
BHAISHRI,
I have your letter. I emphatically told you not to attach any
weight to my opinion. Only a resolution which suits all should be
passed. I have sent an article to Navajivan, which will perhaps
throw more light on the matter. I can think further about this
only after I have met all the workers and understood their points
of view. It is not at all proper to be guided solely by what I
think in the matter. Why don't you yourself think over every
aspect and advise the people to do what you consider proper?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
DEVCHANDBHAI PAREKH
VARTEJ
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: S.N. 5690
