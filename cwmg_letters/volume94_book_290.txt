290. LETTER TO A. KALESWARA RAO
October 14, 1944
DEAR KALESWARA RAO,
Bapu was very glad to have your letter of the 8th inst.
He is sorry about Shri Narayanaraju's demise. We shall have to make such
sacrifices before the battle of freedom is won.
Thanks for your suggestions which have been duly taken note of by Gandhiji.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI A. KALESWARA RAO
SANTIKUTIR
NANDI GRAM
KRISHNA DISTT.
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
