294. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
SABARMATI,
December 18, 1921
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I had been to Karamsad yesterday. Hence, I have not been
able to write about the late Dhondi. I have read his papers
today. In such cases it seems proper that the Provincial Com-
mittee pays the money after investigation.
Jamnadas and Kunzru have arrived today. Bhai Kanji will
give you news of them.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32720
