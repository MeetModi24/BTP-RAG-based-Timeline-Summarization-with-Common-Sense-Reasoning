356. LETTER TO G. K. DEVADHAR
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 16, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your two letters. I explained the contents to Mano-
rama. She says she will wait for a reply from Mrs. Devadhar to
whom at your instance she has written. She seems to be somewhat
unwilling to move out. But if you or Mrs. Devadhar write to her
probably she would go to the Seva Sadan.
I know that she will never be able by remaining here to earn
as much as she can by finishing the course there and I have told
her so too. But probably being just now comfortable here she does
not want to move out unless she has something definite from you
for as soon as I mentioned your letters to her, she said she had
not heard anything from you directly and that she was waiting
for a reply from Mrs. Devadhar, who I hope is quite all right
and who, you should tell her, is free to come to the Ashram as
her own home whenever she feels inclined.
Yours sincerely,
