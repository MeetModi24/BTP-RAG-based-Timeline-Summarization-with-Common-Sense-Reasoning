184. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Ashadh Sud 2 [July 4, 1924]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your letter and postcard at the same time.
It was good that you stayed there for a month more. The
more our trust in God grows and we realize our littleness, the more
we become free from worry. Can worry lighten our grief?
Blessings from
BAPU
SISTER VASUMATI
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 448. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
