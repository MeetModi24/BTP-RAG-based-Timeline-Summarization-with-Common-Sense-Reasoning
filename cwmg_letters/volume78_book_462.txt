462. LETTER TO TOTARAM HINGORANI
SEVAGRAM,
November 23, 1944
MY DEAR TOTARAMJI 3 ,
Anand is somewhat reconciled to Vidya's death. He sees that
a soul does not die with the dissolution of the body. His one and
real unhappiness is that you are not reconciled to your wife. As
a dutiful son the grief is natural to him. Is there no possibi-
lity of reconciliation? Pardon me for this intrusion. I feel for
Anand.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand T.
Hingorani
