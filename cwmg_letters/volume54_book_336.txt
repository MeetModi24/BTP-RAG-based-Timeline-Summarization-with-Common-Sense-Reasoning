336. LETTER TO GIRDHARILAL
April 4, 1933
MY DEAR LALA GIRDHARILAL,
I have your letter. I am glad you are again moving for-
ward.
I do not think that Dr. Agrawal will get the permission.
If he does, I have no doubt that the Sardar may give him the
time and so would I. But even if he asks for permission he sh-
ould not use our names. If I can summon up sufficient courage,
I shall ask for the permission myself.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20823
