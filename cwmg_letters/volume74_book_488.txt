488. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
[September 7, 1941]
CHI. KANCHAN,
I do not know what truth there is in the accompanying.
Mu[nnalal] claims that it is the plain unvarnished truth. Even
if it is not so, there is an agreement between us that you should
take no offence.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Will you let yourself be medically examined by Manjula-
behn?
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8271. Also C.W. 7156. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
