298. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
August 31, 1938
CHI. SUMI,
Improve your handwriting still further. Write to me in Hindi sometimes
so that I may know how well you write it. Kanam and Arun are quite happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
