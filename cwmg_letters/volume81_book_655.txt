655. LETTER TO KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
[October 17, 1945]
VAHALAN BEHN,
Your letter. I note what you say. We serve according to
our lights.
The Bengal tour will be postponed by a few days. The date
is not yet fixed.
What you say about Satyavati is disquieting. But nothing better
was to be expected. Let us hope she will witness the wedding.
Rajkumari may go to London for a month. . . .4 I shall do my
best about Nirmal Kumar whom I know and whose work I value.
My love to Satyavati.
KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
THE I. N. A. DEFENCE COMMITTEE
82 DARYAGANJ, DELHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
