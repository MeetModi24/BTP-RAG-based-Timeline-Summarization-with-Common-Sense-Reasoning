94. LETTER TO DR. B. PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
June 22, 1936
DEAR DR. PATTABHI,
You have a brilliant boy, quite like his father. But he
does not seem to have been able to carry the whole joke to
you.
When I saw Mrs. P. decked literally from top to toe,
I said: "Now I know why Father was afraid to come to Banga-
lore. For the sin of this barbarity is more on Father's shoul-
ders than Mother's." Now you may take the joke as seriously as
you like. I quite agree with your philosophy. Wives and grown-up
children must have vacation from their husbands and parents.
Love.
M. K. GANDHI
Incidents of Gandhiji's Life, p. 226
