174. LETTER TO BHARATAN KUMARAPPA
SEVAGRAM,
November 26, 1945
CHI. BHARATAN,
Read at least this much Hindi. I am not taking you for
Harijan. This is just one of the possibilities. You are not bound
in any way.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
