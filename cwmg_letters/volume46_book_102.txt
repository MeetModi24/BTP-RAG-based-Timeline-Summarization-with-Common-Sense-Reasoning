102. LETTER TO H. HARCOURT
[AS AT] SABARMATI,
May 6, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. You will forgive me for sending
you a belated reply but I was helpless. I am not able to say
that the Round Table Conference has established lasting goodwill
between the two nations. But it can do a great deal of good if
the representatives of both the nations are determined to come
to an agreement. I am quite aware and I am thankful that I
94
have a very large circle of unknown friends in the British
Isles.
Yours sincerely,
H. HARCOURT, ESQ.
LONDON S.E. 19
From a photostat: S.N. 17036
