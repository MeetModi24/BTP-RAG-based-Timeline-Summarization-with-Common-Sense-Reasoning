242. LETTER TO SECRETARY FOR INTERIOR
July 22, 1912
THE SECRETARY FOR THE INTERIOR
PRETORIA
SIR,
Mr. R. M. Sodha is one of the British Indians admitted last
year in virtue of the settlement. As was mentioned by me last
year, Mr. Sodha intended to do business in the Transvaal for a living,
but, as it was thought legislation would be passed during the last
session, Mr. Sodha was publicly supported. But he naturally does
not wish to remain idle, and is anxious to take out a licence to
trade. I take it that, on the strength of the permit issued to him, he
will be unable to take out a licence. Will the Government, therefore,
be pleased to authorize the Receiver of Revenue to issue a licence
to Mr. Sodha without production of a registration certificate.
I have, etc.,
[M. K. GANDHI]
