34. LETTER TO GANGADEVI SANADHYA
Silence Day [Before February 25, 1929]
DEAR SISTER,
Bhai Chhaganlal writes that you have again had a relapse.
As you are resolved not to go out of the Ashram for treatment all
I can say is may God give you peace. Sickness and death are
ever with the body. Since we revere the Gita why should we grieve
over illness or death? Of course, we should not deliberately fall
ill.
Should you feel like going out of the Ashram for a change
of air there would be no shame or sin in doing so. "May Tulsidas
by whatever means find refuge at the feet of the Lord."
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2547
