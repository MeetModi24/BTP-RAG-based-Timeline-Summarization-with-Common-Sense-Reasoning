340. LETTER TO H. G. PERRY
BOMBAY,
[On or after April 14, 1924]
DEAR MR. PERRY,
If you could come on Sunday next at 2 p.m., I shall be glad
to see you. I do not know that I shall have much to say, as my
talks with the Swarajist leaders are not yet finished.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 8728
