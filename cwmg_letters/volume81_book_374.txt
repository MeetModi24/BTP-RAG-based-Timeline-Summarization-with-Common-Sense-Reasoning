374. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
September 3, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I got your letter. I am not sending Sushilabehn just now.
She has a meeting on the 9th. I will see after that. I hope the
kitchen there is running smoothly. Durgabehn must be helping
and Pushpa must be giving her full co-operation. I think Mohan-
singh can make bread, etc., with great speed. Show this letter
to Krishnachandra and the others, too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8436. Also C.W. 5595. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
