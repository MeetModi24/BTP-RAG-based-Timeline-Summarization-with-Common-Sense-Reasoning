196. LETTER TO NANDALAL BOSE
May 15, 1937
DEAR NANDA BABU,
You cannot afford to have indifferent health. I hope,
therefore, that you will be soon restored. I would not think
of risking any damage to your health although your presence
may be urgently needed. But I understand what you mean. As
soon as the site is finally chosen, I hope to send you topo-
graphical details and the draft plan that may be prepared.
Sardar Patel is inviting Mhatre and Ramdas, both of whom
you know, to select the site and prepare the sketch plan.
I am in Tithal till the 30th instant.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 9826
