174. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
October 6, 1934
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter.
A letter for Harilal is enclosed. Read it and then pass it on
to him. He wants to read some literature about the science of
khadi. I am sure you have some there. Give it to him. There are
books both in English and Gujarati.
Read the article on the takli in Harijan . I hope you get
those journals regularly now.
Kanaiyo has settled down to
his work very well. He takes little time to mix among strangers.
Radha seems to have become very thin. She will arrive there
in a few days. I hope she will study there as much as she can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8420. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
