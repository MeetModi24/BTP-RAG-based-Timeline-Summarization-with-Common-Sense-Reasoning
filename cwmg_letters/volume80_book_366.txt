366. LETTER TO BURJORJI F. BHARUCHA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 30, 1945
BHAI BHARUCHA,
I have your postcard. Please understand that it is Improper
to drag me into such activities.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
BURJORJI FARAMJI BHARUCHA
