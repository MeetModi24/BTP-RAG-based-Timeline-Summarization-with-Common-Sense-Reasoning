160. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
SWARAJ ASHRAM,
Saturday [August 4, 1928]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I am alarmed by your letter. It seems you are seriously ill.
Please ask the doctor to write to me and tell me what he thinks
about the illness and what treatment he is giving. Is it necessary
to send anyone from here?
I am at Bardoli just now. Write to me at this address.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. VASUMATIBEHN
KANYA GURUKULA
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 489. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
