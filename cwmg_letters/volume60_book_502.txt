502. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
WARDHA,
April 1, 1935
MY DEAR AMRIT,
I did not want you to send me the enormous quantity of
sugar and gur you have sent me. At the rate you are going, you
will presently be my universal provider.
Your warning me against overwork induces the retort:
"Physician! Heal thyself."
Do you remember Rhys Jones? He asks me to take all the
work I can from Shummy. Have I not anticipated Rhys?
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3526. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6335
