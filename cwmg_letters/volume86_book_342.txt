342. LETTER TO MEMBERS OF FENI SUB-DIVISION
MUSLIM LEAGUE
December 25, 1946
GENTLEMEN,
I have just received your postcard scribbled out in ink
and thank you for your advice. I am unable to follow your
advice which is definitely based on ignorance of facts.
In the
first place, I know that the situation is not normal here and
that in so far as I can contribute to the Bihar problem, I have
to inform you that such influence as I have on Bihar can be
and is being efficiently exercised from Srirampur.
Yours sincerely,
M . K . GANDHI
My Days with Gandhi, p. 122
