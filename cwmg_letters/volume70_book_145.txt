145. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 25, 1939
CHI. VIJAYA,
You must have received the letter I wrote to you before I
got yours. I am here for the present. If, therefore, you can
free yourself from there, come over immediately.
I hope you
are keeping good health. Naranbhai must be improving.
Here owing to the rains everything is fine. The fear of
drought has disappeared. I hope everything is all right there
too.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VIJAYABEHN
C/O NARANBHAI VALLABHBHAI PATEL
VARAD
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7113. Also C.W. 4605. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
