224. LETTER TO RAMAKRISHNA BAJAJ
ON NOAKHALI TOUR,
December 1, 1946
CHI. RAM,
You have come back after gaining a lot of experience. Now
give its benefit to the country and also to your own business.
We shall meet if I can free myself from here. There is no use
coming here. I do not want to send for even Mataji. I may
send for her only when I emerge from darkness into light. I trust
she is all right, as also Savitri.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 3068
