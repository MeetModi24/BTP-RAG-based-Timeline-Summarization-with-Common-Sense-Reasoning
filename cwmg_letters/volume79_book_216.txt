216. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
SEVAGRAM,
February 12, 1945
MY DEAR KU.,
S[ushila] has gone to Nagpur for a patient. She is likely
to return tonight. If you want to catch her at the station, you
can do so.
I know the remedy. But let the doctors speak. Then if
necessary, I shall put in my word.
I had your note about your book. I hope to produce some-
thing worth while.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10170
