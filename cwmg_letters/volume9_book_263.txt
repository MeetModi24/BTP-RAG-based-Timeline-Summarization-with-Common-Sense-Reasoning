263. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
September 16, 1909
MY LORD,
I have to thank you for your kind letter of the 15th instant. I have
now written to Lord Morley as per copy. I have made only a slight
verbal alteration in the opening words of the substituted paragraph.
Instead of "we" I have inserted "my colleague and I". The rest is
exactly as drafted by Your Lordship.
Lord Crewe we see today. I shall bear in mind the valuable advice
given by you and will give you the result of the interview.
I remain, etc.,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5079
