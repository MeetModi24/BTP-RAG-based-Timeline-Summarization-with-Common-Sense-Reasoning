368. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
July 15, 1935
BHAI KHAMBHATTA,
Please send me a copy of the letter you wrote about the
hospital. I hope your health is better everyday.
Blessings to both of you from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6610. Also C.W. 4400. Courtesy:
Tehmina Khambhatta
