230. LETTER TO MURIEL LESTER
AS AT SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA (C.P.),
CAMP PANCHGANI,
[July] 12, 1944
MY DEAR MURIEL,
I have your two dear letters. You are going through wonderful if
also frightful experiences. Let us hope it is the darkness before dawn.
But we must so work that the hope may be realized. We too are having
our own experiences though of a different type from yours. I am
working with might and maintain the direction through prayer and fasting.
My energy is still very limited. Ba and Mahadev live as inspirations.
More from Agatha and Horace to whom I have written at length. 2
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
