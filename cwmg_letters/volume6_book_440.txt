440. LETTER TO KALYANDAS MEHTA
[JOHANNESBURG,]
April 23, 1907
MY DEAR KALIANDAS,
I have not heard from you at all for some time. Do wake up. I
enclose herewith the Deed of Transfer No. 1287 of 1906 in favour of
Foolmania. There was a messenger from her yesterday saying that
she was ill, and required the title deed. I am, therefore, sending it to
you there, and, if there is a call for it, hand it over after taking a receipt.
Inquire also why it is wanted.
Yours faithfully,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4736
