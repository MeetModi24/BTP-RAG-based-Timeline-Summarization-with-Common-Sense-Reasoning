24. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
SEGAON,
August 7, 1937
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
For Sita's sake I would say let us not leave her as Editor just
yet. She should be better known than she is among our own circle.
Mere possession of literary merit is not enough for our purpose.
Our readers should know her as a co-worker. Do you not agree?
The Education Minister1, C. P., comes, sees me on Tuesday
at 2.30 p.m. and the Excise Minister at 5 p.m. I think you,
Bharatan, Sita, if she is well, Jajuji3, Nayakam and Kaka should
be present. Will you inform them all?
Love to you—the trio.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10127
