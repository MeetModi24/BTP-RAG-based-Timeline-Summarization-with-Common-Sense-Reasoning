245. LETTER TO RAMTARAN MUKHERJI
December 13, 1932
Thank you for your letter. All cannot have the gift of
Shankaracharya. I am using in my own humble manner such
gifts as God has given me. Just as religious preachers like Shanka-
racharyaji have time after time dedicated their great learning for
the sake of religion, so have others promoted religion on prayer
and fasting.
