396. LETTER TO SIR M. V. JOSHI
October 12, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter which was received only yesterday. As I
have already intimated in reply to a previous enquiry through
Shrimati Saraladevi Ambalal Sarabhai, I very much regret that I
shall be unable to give evidence before your Committee. I hope
you will therefore please excuse me.
Yours sincerely,
SIR M. V. JOSHI
AGE OF CONSENT COMMITTEE, CAMP AHMEDABAD
From a photostat: S.N. 13550
