183. LETTER TO CHAKRAYYA
SODEPUR,
May 12, 1947
CHI. CHAKRAYYA 3 ,
Lilavatibehn writes that you have been admitted to hospital.
How nice that Lilavatibehn is looking after you! Have patience.
What God wills will come to pass. Do not have any worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1
Popularly known as Sane Guruji, the addressee went on a fast unto death
on May 1 to secure entry of Harijans into the Vithoba Temple at Pandharpur (vide
also Vol. LXXXVII, pp. 391 and 401). He gave up the fast on May 10, after an
assurance that the temple would be opened to Harijans.
2 Illegible in the source
3 A Harijan from Andhra who had joined the Sevagram Ashram in 1935 and
become an expert craftsman. He was sent to Bombay for treatment of what was
later diagnosed as brain tumour.
