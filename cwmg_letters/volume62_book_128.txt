128. LETTER TO INDU N. PAREKH
November 12, 1935
CHI. INDU,
I at any rate didn't know that you had gone to Calicut. I
have dictated letters for your two brothers and so there is nothing
new to tell you.
Harbhai and Moolshankar have come to see me today.
They will stay here for some time. If you write to this address,
they will get your letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6272
