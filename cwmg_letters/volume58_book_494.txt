494. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHARMA
September 12, 1934
CHI. SHARMA,
Your letter. I also saw your letter to Prabhavati regarding
the keys.
I was already apprehensive about catching fever. Anyway,
it is now a fact and I hope it will be only to the good. It was
quite right to get him treated by the doctors. You have to be
patient. The doctor's instructions must be adhered to, you have
only to turn yourself into a nurse and follow them. This augurs
well in every way. God alone controls the results. I must get a
letter from you daily.
For the time being, how can I even mention South Africa?
But I shall keep up the effort of clearing the way and we shall
wait and watch.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
1932-48, facing p. 93
