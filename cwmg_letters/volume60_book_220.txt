220. LETTER TO S. TIRUVENGADASAMI
February 5, 1935
MY DEAR TIRUVENGADASAMI,
I have your report of the working of your school. I hope that
the school will more and more go in for industrial education.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI S. TIRUVENGADASAMI
TEACHER, H. S. S. SCHOOL
BAZAR STREET, MANNARGUDI (TANJORE DT.)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
