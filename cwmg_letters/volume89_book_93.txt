93. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
CALCUTTA,
August 22, 1947
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
I have your wire. The work here is proceeding well. Yes,
I must go to Noakhali. Some days are to be given to Bihar.
Under the circumstances, when can I go to the Punjab? Never-
theless, you must tell me when I have to go. No time to write
more. I wanted to write in English but the pen went off into
Hindustani. See the enclosed; is there any truth in it?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
