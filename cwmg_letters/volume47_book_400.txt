400. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS
AHMEDABAD,
August 22, 1931
MY DEAR KRISHNADAS,
I was wondering why you had not written before now. I was
getting somewhat impatient and angry with you. I was thinking
of you intently only yesterday and there is a letter from you. If
you are again ill why wait till there is a collapse? Why not go to
Comilla or perhaps better still to the Ashram? It is your old
home.
There is nothing certain about me though I feel that I shall
be able to be here till the 8th September when the Working Com-
mittee meets.
I take it you are getting Young India regularly.
SYT. KRISHNADAS
P.O. SINHERGAON, BENGAL
