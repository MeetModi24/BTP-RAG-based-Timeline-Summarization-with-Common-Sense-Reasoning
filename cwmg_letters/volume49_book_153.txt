153. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
February 18, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
I got your letter. If you cannot think what to write in your
letter, take Premabehn's help. In a few days you will learn to
write a very good letter. I am very glad that you feel happy
there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9421. Courtesy Ravindra R. Patel
