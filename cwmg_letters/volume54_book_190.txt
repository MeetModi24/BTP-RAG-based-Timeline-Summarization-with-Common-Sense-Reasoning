190. LETTER TO N. Y. NADKARNI
March 23, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the information contained
therein. When reformers persevere in their effort I have no
doubt that they will leave an impression upon the sanatanists.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. Y. NADKARNI
4TH CONTRACTOR'S CHAWL
CHARNI ROAD
BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 20678
