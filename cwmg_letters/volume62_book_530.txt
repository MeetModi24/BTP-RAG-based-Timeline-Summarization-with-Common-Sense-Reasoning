530. LETTER TO TIRUPUR KHADI-PRODUCING
ASSOCIATION
[Before May 28, 1936]
All I can say is that it is wrong for you to sell khaddar in
the circumstances in which you are doing. It hurts the poor peo-
ple in whose interest the affairs of the All-India Spinners' Asso-
ciation are conducted.
The Hindu, 30-5-1936
