425. LETTER TO NARMADA BHUSKUTE
October 16, 1932
Service, not literature, is our mission. Literature is a means
of service, therefore so long as we have some work of service on
hand we must quietly devote ourselves to it. The Gita says that
God will grant the means to those who have devotion. Do add
to your literary education whenever you have some time. In that
matter too take it that reflection is more important than read-
ing. However little you may read, you should digest all that you
read.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, pp. 141-
