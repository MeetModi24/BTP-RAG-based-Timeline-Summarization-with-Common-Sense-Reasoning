206. LETTER TO SHAMALDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
February 10, 1945
CHI. SHAMALDAS,
Read the enclosed letter If you could bring justice to the
case, it will gladden the soul of Kevalrambhai, wherever it is.
In his last days he had been in touch with me over spiritual
matters, as had been your father. They were both prepared to
go to [South] Africa but they passed away. I had even prepared
the ground for them to reach S. A. But isn't fate always a
couple of steps ahead of us?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHAMALDAS GANDHI
C/O "VANDEMATARAM"
BOMBAY
