54. LETTER TO JAMNADAS GANDHI
January 25, 1932
CHI. JAMNADAS,
You came and left without saying much. Now write to me
and make the letter as long as you wish. How far are you succee-
ding in your work? How many pupils does the school have? And
how many teachers? Where is Jagannath?
Where are his
brothers? How is your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9361
