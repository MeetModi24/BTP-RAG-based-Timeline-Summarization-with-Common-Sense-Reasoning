680. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
October 19, 1945
MY DEAR KU.,
I have hurriedly gone through the cutting returned herewith.
There is nothing new in it. The reference to Hitler is out of
place. It does not seem to misrepresent my views.
I note what you say about Kishorelal. So your hawk-like
eye has detected an error ! ! !
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10181
