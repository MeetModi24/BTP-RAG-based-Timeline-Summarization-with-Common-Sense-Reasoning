508. LETTER TO K. R. CHHAPKHANE
February 23, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
Perhaps you know the correspondence I have been having
with Sjt. Athavale. As he refers me to you and perhaps rightly,
I approach you and would ask you kindly to let me know under
what conditions and terms I handed over to you the collections I
had made in Sangli during my visit.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. CHHAPKHANE
SANGLI
