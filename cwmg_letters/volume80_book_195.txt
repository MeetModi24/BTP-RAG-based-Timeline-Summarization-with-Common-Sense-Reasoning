195. LETTER TO RAMALAKSHMI AND PRAVINBALA
May 12, 1945
CHI. RAMALAKSHMI AND PRAVINBALA,
I have the letter signed by both you sisters and a money
order for Rs. 49. One can say that all the girls have done very
well. I am depositing the money in the fund for Harijans.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAMALAKSHMI ASAR
C/O SHRI VALLABHDAS RAMDAS
VIYOG SADAN, HABIB ROAD
KANDIVALI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
