398. LETTER TO MAHOMED HABIBULLAH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
May 24, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter enclosing copy of Sjt. Sastri's
cable for my confidential information.
Yours sincerely,
SIR MAHOMED HABIBULLAH
VICEROY'S COUNCIL MEMBER
SIMLA
