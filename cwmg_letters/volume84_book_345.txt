345. LETTER TO BRIJMOHAN
MUSSOORIE,
June 3, 1946
BHAI BRIJMOHAN,
I could reach your letter only today. The letter and the
rejoinder will appear in Harijan Sevak without your name.
Why this fear?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. BRIJMOHAN, PH.D.
P. O. BANARAS HINDU UNIVERSITY
