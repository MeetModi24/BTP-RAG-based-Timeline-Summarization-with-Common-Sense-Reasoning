302. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
Silence Day [December 17, 1928]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I got your letters. It was impossible to write yesterday.
You have displayed much firmness and patience. That is how
we should always act. You are on the potter's wheel, and I am sure
God will mould you well. Do not lose heart in regard to brahma-
charya. Surely it is a difficult task. If we believe that we can
succeed in it merely by our own effort, [we should remember
that] in the story of the Yaksha, as we saw, the god of wind
found it impossible to blow away even a straw by his own strength.
But even the most difficult task becomes easy when human effort
is supported by divine grace. Both of you should strive, but
leave the result to God and you will surely get it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 31
