519. LETTER TO KULKARNI
October 30, 1932
Vidya means knowledge and avidya means activity. The mean-
ings of sambhooti and asambhooti are related to the meanings vidya
and avidya. Hence asambhooti means the body and sambhooti means
the self. I interpret the expressions thus for my own satisfaction.
I feel that this interpretation explains the meaning of the Isho-
panishad easily and satisfactorily.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 188
