561. LETTER TO P. N. RAJAMANICKAM CHETTIYAR
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 23, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter and the parcel of khadi for which I
thank you. If you persist long enough you will find that you
have all the assistance you need. Your yarn is not as even as
it might be nor as strong. You must attempt to spin better.
Your weaving is also not close enough but all these things
are merely a matter of practice and practice will make you
perfect.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. N. RAJAMANICKAM CHETTIYAR
BHARATHA KHADDAR PRACHAR SALA
TIYAGADURGAN (S.A. DIST.)
From a microfilm: S.N. 19562
