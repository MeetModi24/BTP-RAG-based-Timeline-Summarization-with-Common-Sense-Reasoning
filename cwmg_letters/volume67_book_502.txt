502. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
September 15, 1938
BHAI PURUSHOTTAMDAS,
Do come to Segaon towards the end of October. Most pro-
bably I shall have returned from the Frontier Province by that
time.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Gujarati original: Purushottamdas Thakurdas Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
