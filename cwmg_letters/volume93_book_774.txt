774. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
July 15, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Your two letters came together. Today also I am so busy
that I cannot write to you. I do not think I am going any-
where from here. You do come here after completing your two
weeks. You will certainly learn a lot there. I for one wish
that you should gain experience in different hospitals.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
490 MULJI MANSION
EDENWALA ROAD
MATUNGA (G. I. P.)
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
