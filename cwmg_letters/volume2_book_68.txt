68. LETTER TO "THE NATAL MERCURY"
DURBAN,
November 19, 1897
THE EDITOR
The Natal Mercury
SIR,
I beg to enclose herewith for publication copies of the correspon-
dence between the Government and myself with reference to the
reports which have appeared in the papers regarding alleged
attempts of Indians to come into the Colony by way of Delagoa
Bay.
I am, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
The Natal Mercury, 20-11-1897
