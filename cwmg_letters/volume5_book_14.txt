14. LETTER TO J. DE VILLIERS
[JOHANNESBURG,]
July 12, 1905
TO
J. DE VILLIERS, ESQ.
18 AEGIS BUILDINGS
JOHANNESBURG
DEAR SIR,
Re: Ismail & Lucas
I have purposely delayed sending you cheque for this bill in the hope
of being able some time to wait upon you with a view to getting a reduc-
tion. Owing to pressure of work, however, I have not been able to get
out of the office. All that Said Ismail had was this claim. To him, there-
fore, the loss of £1,300 and payment of costs are a very great loss. May
I, therefore, ask you to make a substantial reduction in your account?
I approached Mr. Leonard and he was good enough to grant a reduction.
I enclose your bill herewith.
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
