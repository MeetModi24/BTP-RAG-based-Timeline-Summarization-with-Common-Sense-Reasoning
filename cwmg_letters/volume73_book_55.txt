55. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
October 3, 1940
CHI. KUNVARJI1,
I do not recollect getting Variava's report. It is surprising
that you cannot keep up your weight there.
Do go to Mysore
[for treatment]. I see nothing wrong in that. I have received
Rs. 300 from Bombay. Will any more be coming?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9742. Also C.W. 722. Courtesy:
Navajivan Trust
