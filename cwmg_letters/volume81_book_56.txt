56. LETTER TO JAMSHEDJI N. MEHTA
July 25, 1945
BHAI JAMSHED,
I got your letter along with the typed matter. It is surpris-
ing that the Gita is full of those things and yet you could find no
consolation from it. Because you were used to English that
appealed to you immediately. Be that as it may, it is good that
the depression has left you. For the rest take it that whatever
happened at Simla was for the best.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHETH NUSSERVANJI
KARACHI
From a copy of the Gujarati Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
