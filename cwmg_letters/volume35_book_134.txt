134. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
Sunday [October 30, 1927]
CHI. MIRA,
Your letters have been a great comfort to me, as they have
enabled me to know all about the patients. I am glad you are
clearing the kitchen thoroughly. I did write last Monday. You
must have got that letter by now. More tomorrow.
Love,
BAPU
[PS.]
I hope to be for a day at the Ashram during the return journey.
From the original: C.W. 5296. Courtesy: Mirabehn
