491. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
September 13, 1938
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Explain fully to Kashinath the contents of the enclosed. If
he does not have a copy of the Gram Panchayat Act, ask him to
get one from the Government. After he has got it, we shall advise
whether a court should be constituted.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8567. Also C.W. 7039. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
