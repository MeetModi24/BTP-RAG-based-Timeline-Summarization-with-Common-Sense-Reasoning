404. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
June 5, 1931
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I hope you did not lose in S. India what you gained in
Ceylon. If you are fresh and do not mind the journey do come
to Bardoli for Sunday so that we might have a quiet chat here
before beginning operations on Tuesday. I hope Kamala and
Indu have profited by the rest.
Yours,
BAPU
PANDIT JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
C/O SJT. JALBHAI NAOROJI
NAPEAN SEA RD.
BOMBAY
From Gandhi-Nehru papers, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
