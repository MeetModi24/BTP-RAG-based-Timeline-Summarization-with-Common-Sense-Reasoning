75. LETTER TO YESHWANTRAO HOLKAR
DELHI,
March 17, 1939
MAHARAJA SAHEB1,
I have not forgotten the sweet memories of Hindi Sahitya
Sammelan. I hope that the work of setting up a Hindi Univer-
sity in Indore is making progress.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
