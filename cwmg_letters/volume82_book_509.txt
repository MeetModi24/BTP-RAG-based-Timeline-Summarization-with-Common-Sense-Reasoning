509. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
KHADI PRATISHTHAN,
SODEPUR (NEAR CALCUTTA),
January 8, 1946
DEAR MR. ABELL,
I enclose herewith a cutting for you to read. Can the
information therein be true? Probably you could let me know
without troubling His Excellency.
Enclosure : 1
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 44
