643. LETTER TO JANAKIBEHN SOMAN
BOMBAY,
April 13, 1945
DEAR JANAKIBEHN,
I was happy to have your letter. I have sent it on to Braj-
kishorebhai. Your letter reminds me of the old times. May
you all keep well. Please tell Totaramji to write to me a few
words occasionally.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JANAKIBEHN SOMAN
GUJARAT VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
