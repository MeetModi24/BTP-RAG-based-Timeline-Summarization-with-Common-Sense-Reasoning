249. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
June 25, 1934
CHI. RAMDAS,
Sharma must have arrived there. He will therefore tell you everything. If
you wish to leave that place, there is no room for choice. Chorwad is the
only choice left. Teethal is out of the question.
By all means call the girl baby 'Usha'. You surely know that 'Okha' is the
same as 'Usha'. If she brings you luck, we will say a thousand times that it is good
she has come. Has Vinoba regained strength? How is Manoharsinh? How is Devaki
who was bitten not by a snake but a scorpion? How is Nimu? How is Sarita?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAMDAS GANDHI
CHARKHA SANGHA OFFICE
WARDHA
C.P.
