392. LETTER TO H. J. KHANDEKAR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA, C. P.,
November 13, 1944
DEAR KHANDEKAR,
I have your letter. I have become afraid of you after your
unauthorized misleading report of our conversation I express no
opinion on your contemplated meeting or its programme.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI H. J. KHANDEKAR, M.L.A.
ITWARI, NAGPUR
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
