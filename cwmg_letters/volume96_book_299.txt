299. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
February 3, 1934
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
You have complained that I have not acknowledged your cables
and letters. It is not so. But if my letter has miscarried I cannot help.
Anyway you are always before my mind's eye and I do expect some
day to greet you here. When are you coming ?
Love,
UPPER HOUSE
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy : National
Archives of India
