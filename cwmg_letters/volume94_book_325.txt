325. LETTER TO T. S. S. RAJAN
January 10, 1945
DEAR DR. RAJAN,
On my return from Bombay on the 1st inst. I read out your letter of
15-12-'44 to Bapu.
With regard to the action you have taken about the Bharadwaja Ashram, I am
to say that you have Bapu's approval.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
DR. T. S. S. RAJAN
TRICHINOPOLY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1 This reply by Pyarelal was in response to the addressee's letter of
January 9, 1945, wherein recalling his acquaintance with Gandhiji, he gave him
his assurance regarding the eye-treatment of Parnerkar's mother.
2 The note is written on a sheet bearing a letter dated January 9, 1945
from John Sargent to the addressee.
