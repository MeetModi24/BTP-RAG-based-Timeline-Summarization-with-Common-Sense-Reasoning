440. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
ASHRAM,
Monday, April 26, 1926
BHAISHRI KAKA,
I have your letters. I shall certainly answer your question
about cow's milk, but not today.
When asked about the new almanac Swami said that he did
not understand the problem. I at any rate do not. Explain it to
us after you get well and revise the almanac. Can we, however,
introduce any changes without first discussing them at length in
Navajivan? I have not yet been able to understand the signi-
ficance of the proposed changes. Shri Harihar is expected here
in a few days; I shall try to understand it from him. . . .1
My Mussoorie trip is off for ever. The meeting with the
Governor will also take place either in Poona or Bombay, in
June. At present the air is thick with talk about a trip to Fin-
land. I have not yet received the final reply. I still think that
they will not agree to my condition regarding my dress. If the
journey is finally decided upon, I shall be away for not less than
three months.
More in my next.
Blessings from
BAPU
