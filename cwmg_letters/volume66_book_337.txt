337. LETTER TO TANDON
[October] 29, 1937
BHAI TANDONJI,
I have received your telegram concerning me too. I had
not seen anything else apart from that. It is good that you
sent a report about it. I deliberately refrained from replying
to your telegram. This year there were so many telegrams from
all over the world that I was staggered. And I took it to be
God's grace. I had published a brief message of thanks in the
newspapers.
I decided not to reply individually to anyone.
Anyway what reply can I give through words? So much love
can be reciprocated only through action. Let us see for what
purpose God uses me as His instrument.
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
