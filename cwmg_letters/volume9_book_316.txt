316. LETTER TO N. M. COOPER
[LONDON,]
October 21, 1909
DEAR MR. COOPER,
Will you kindly send Mr. Doke's book as follows: 24 copies to
Dr. Mehta, 14 Mogul Street, Rangoon, India; 250 copies to Messrs
Natesan & Co., Booksellers, Madras, India.
250 copies to the Manager, [Inter]national Printing Press, Durban,
Natal, South Africa. (Postal address, Box 182, Durban, Natal)
Yours faithfully,
N. M. COOPER, ESQ.
154, HIGH ROAD
ILFORD
ESSEX
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5140
