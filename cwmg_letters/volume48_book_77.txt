77. LETTER TO PASTOR FORELL
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
September 30, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your telegram advising me of Sundaram's
address on the Indian movement, and for your sympathies and
prayers for the success of my mission.
Yours sincerely,
HERR PASTOR FORELL
SWEDISH CHURCH
BERLIN
From a photostat: S.N. 17898
