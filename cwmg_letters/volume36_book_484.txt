484. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Friday [June 15, 1928]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. I have written to Pandit Abhayji about
the state of affairs at the Gurukul. I feel that reform can be
brought about only if some man is there. There was a theft here.
Surendra was beaten up, even Shankerbhai got a slight beating.
A sum of Rs. 200 was stolen from the store. A number of
changes are taking place in the Ashram. There is no time to
write more. Surendra is in good health. Mahadev fell off the
top of the well and got badly hurt.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VASUMATIBEHN
KANYA GURUKUL
DEHRA DUN
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 478. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
