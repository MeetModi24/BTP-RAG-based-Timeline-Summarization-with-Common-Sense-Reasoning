164. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
Aso Vad 5 [October 7, 1925]
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
I have not been able yet to read your long letter. I had
your second one yesterday. It is certain that I will not be able
to go there on the 31 st October but I shall fix the date when I
reach the Ashram in the beginning of November.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 2692. Courtesy: Dahyabhai M. Patel
