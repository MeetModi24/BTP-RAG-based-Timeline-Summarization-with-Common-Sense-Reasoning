425. LETTER TO NATVARLAL J. VEPARI
August 25, 1941
BHAI NATVARLAL,
Now you should not send me any further material. The
report you send should be the final one. If either party desire to
tell me more, they may do so in writing and send it to me. After
I receive your report, I shall consider you free from responsibility.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10125
