127. LETTER TO LORD IRWIN
KINGSLEY HALL,
Bow,
October 17, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter came as if in response to my prayer. I was about
to write to you to send me an appointment when your welcome
letter came. I shall be with you at 10 a.m. on Wednesday if the
hour is not too early. So far as I can see, my work at the
R.T.C. is nearly finished. But I cannot take any serious step with-
out conferring with you. Of all this when we meet.
Pray excuse the left hand writing.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 9439
