49. LETTER TO SURAJRAM PUROHIT
SEVAGRAM,
May 6, 1941
BHAI,
I have read your letter. I could not decipher your signature
and Mahadevbhai is not here.
You will be stronger if you leave your native place. Whatever
the amount you may earn outside, you can give a good fight from
there whether you are one or five. The vital thing is that you
should go out and earn in a spirit of yajna. Then you will spend
your wealth for the welfare of the people.
The other way is what you have suggested. If you land in
jail there, then that is indeed welcome. If you are guided by the
considerations of safeguarding your body or wealth, it is a vain
endeavour.
This covers replies to all your questions.
Blessings from
BAPU
SURAJRAM PUROHIT
SANSTHAN RAJPIPLA LOKSABHA
136-140 MEADOWS STREET, 2ND FLOOR
BOMBAY
