82. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
[After February 15, 1927]
BHAISHRI RAMESHWARJI,
I have both your letters. The garland that you got was given
[to me] for khadi work; so the price you paid for it would be
utilized for the same cause. For cow-protection too we should do
only what is right, and now I shall need quite a lot for it since
the tanning school has started functioning. But we must be patient.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 206
