346. LETTER TO NARAYAN MORESHWAR KHARE
June 2, 1931
CHI. PANDITJI,
Thakkar Bapa says in his letter that, though they had invited
you to the gathering of Bhangis at Dakor, you could not attend it.
Is this true?
How is Rambhau progressing?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 216. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn Khare
