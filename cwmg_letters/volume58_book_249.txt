249. LETTER TO SANATANISTS
[July 22, 1934]
In reply to a letter of an orthodox sanatanist of Benares, Mahatma
Gandhi says that he is always prepared to hear the discussion of pundits
with a view to obtaining correct interpretation of the Shastras on the subject
of untouchability, but if the conclusion arrived at by the President was not
convincing to him he will not be bound to accept it.
The Hindustan Times, 24-7-1934
