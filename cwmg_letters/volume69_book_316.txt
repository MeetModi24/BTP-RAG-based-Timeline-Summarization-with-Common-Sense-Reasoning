316. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
RAJKOT,
May 28, 1939
CHI. MANILAL,
This note will be brought by an old client of mine, Shaikh
Farid. He had a store in Pietermaritzburg. He has to go there
because of the death of his brother. But he doesn't know anybody
now in Pietermaritzburg.
Ascertain his need and introduce
him to somebody if you can or do whatever else may be
necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANILAL GANDHI
"INDIAN OPINION"
PHOENIX
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4896
