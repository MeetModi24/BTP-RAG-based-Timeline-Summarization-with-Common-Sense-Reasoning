273. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
VEJALPUR,
April 17, 1930
CHI. MIRA,
I have your two letters.
I can understand how the mishap occurred as to the yarn.
I understand too your natural desire to come. But I am [sure]
restraint was better. It is a fine saying "there is no remission of
sin except by being born again."
You know however that you are at liberty to come if you
cannot help it. But then you must come plainly to satisfy the
natural desire. If you can suppress the desire, so much the better.
Calcutta and Karachi have broken the spell. Somehow I
am unmoved. But there is no suspending even if moving violence
takes place.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5392. Courtesy: Mirabehn
