101. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
Silence Day, September 4, 1944
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your letter. Why are you wasting so much time
over such a trivial matter? You did what seemed to be proper
at the time. This should be enough. Your letter is a symptom
of an unhealthy mind. Get rid of it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4446
