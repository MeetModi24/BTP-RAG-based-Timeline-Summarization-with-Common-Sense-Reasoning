354. LETTER TO SANKARAN
NEW DELHI,
September 9, 1946
CHI. SANKARAN,
Both your letters came into my hands only yesterday. I
shall now be here till the 24th, but I think it is pointless for
you to come here to see me. Stick on there and do whatever
work Sushilabehn gives you.
Your chief task is to bring about a change in your diet. You
should set about this. If you maintain good health under all
circumstances, I shall feel that at least in your own case nature
cure has been of benefit. Chimanlalbhai does not take medi-
cines. It would be a great thing if he, too, showed some
improvement. The rest when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SANKARAN
SEVAGRAM
