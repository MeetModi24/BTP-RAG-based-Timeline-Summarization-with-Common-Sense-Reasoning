290. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
March 16, 1930
CHI. VIDYA,
I have heard much about you from Anand, and I was glad
to hear about your devotion to God. You should not be anxious
on account of Anand. God will protect him.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of
India and Anand T. Hingorani
