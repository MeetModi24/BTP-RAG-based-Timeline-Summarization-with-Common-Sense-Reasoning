524. LETTER TO IQBAL KRISHAN KAPOOR
SEVAGRAM,
September 15, 1941
DEAR KAPOOR,
I think your case IS clear. The order is humiliating. You
will disregard it not as a satyagrahi under the struggle but as
an individual who values his self-respect above so-called freedom.
Therefore, no general instruction is necessary.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The History of the Indian National Congress, Vol. II, p. 275
