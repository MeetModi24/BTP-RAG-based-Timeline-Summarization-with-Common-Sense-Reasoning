621. LETTER TO SUDHIR GHOSH
June 26, 1945
BHAI SUDHIR,
Only today I am able to reply your letter of June 11. You
know what I am doing.
I am very keen to go to Bengal. But I wish to go to all
the [nearby] places.
Blessings to Shanti 2 and to you from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Sudhir Ghosh Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
