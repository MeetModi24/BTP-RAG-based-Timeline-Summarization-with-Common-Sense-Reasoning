31. LETTER TO S. PONNAMMAL
April 26, 1933
MY DEAR PONNAMMAL,
I have your letter. You do not need a formal note of
introduction to Sjt. Kelappan. You can simply present this note
to him and tell him the whole of your story. I know that he will
make the fullest use of your services and do whatever is possible
to help you in your work.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19050
