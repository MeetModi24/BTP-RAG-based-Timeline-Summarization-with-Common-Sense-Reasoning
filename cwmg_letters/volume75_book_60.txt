60. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
October 22, 1941
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
How did Babudi fall ill again there? Does she take hip-baths?
Does she over-exert herself? What is her diet? Does she use a mos-
quito-net? The money was to be paid as usual. I was under the
impression that it was being paid to Jajuji.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.G. 128
