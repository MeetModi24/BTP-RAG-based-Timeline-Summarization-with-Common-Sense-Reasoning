334. LETTER TO H. C. DASAPPA
SODEPUR,
December 18, 1945
BHAI DASAPPA,
I have your letter. Meet me in Madras when I go there.
I doubt whether I shall be able to go to Mysore. Be in Madras,
both of you. Blessings to Yashodhara.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI H. C. DASAPPA
SHRI KASTURBA SHIBIR
PADURAVALLY
V. V. MOHALLA POST
MYSORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
