379. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
Y. M.,
November 8, 1930
CHI. KANTA,
I have your letter. It is well that you have gone with
Gangabehn. Serve her with care and be brave. You have the
capacity. You have the desire. You now have an opportunity.
May God give you good health. Promise me that you will never
do anything shameful. Gangabehn also has great faith in you.
May God bless you. Do write to me in detail from time
to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
