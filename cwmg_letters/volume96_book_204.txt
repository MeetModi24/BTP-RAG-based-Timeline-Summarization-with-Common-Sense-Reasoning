204. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
April 1 [1915]
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letters. Sometimes I have no time and I am travelling.
Naturally I must then only send love. Today is such a time. I have
just returned from Calcutta and can only send you love.
Your diaries are still interesting for the omissions.
Yours,
OLD FRIEND
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
