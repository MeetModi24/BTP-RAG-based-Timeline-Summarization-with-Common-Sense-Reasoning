168. LETTER TO M. MADIAH
January 30, 1935
MY DEAR MADIAH,
I have your letter. I think you have got to exercise patience.
We have made great headway, and we shall do more if there is
self-restraint. I do not believe in propaganda through pamphlets
or in costly buildings. A Bhajan Mandiram is good. But you
should be satisfied with an unpretentious humble building.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI M. MADIAH
ADI-KARNATAKA STREET No.
MALVILLI TOWN, MYSORE DISTRICT
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
