654. LETTER TO RUTH S. ALEXANDER
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 8, 1926
DEAR SISTER,
Mr. Andrews has brought me your precious letter. It revives
old and sacred memories. Kallenbach has not yet come to me
though he is threatening always. I dare say one fine morning
I shall find a cable from him saying he has started.
Do you ever see Young India? I call it my weekly letter to
friends. Please remember me to Mr. Alexander. Mr. Andrews
has told me how helpful he was in his mission.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. RUTH S. ALEXANDER
HEILBRON
LEIGHTON ROAD
ST. JAMES, C.P.
From a photostat: S.N. 10766
