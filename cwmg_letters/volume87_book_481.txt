481. LETTER TO ARBAB SHER AKBAR KHAN
May 21, 1947
In view of the draft of the agreement you have sent me,
I hardly think it worthwhile our meeting.
The agreement
seeks, in my opinion, to displace the Government for a parti-
cular purpose. This, I think, no government can or should do.
My views being as strong as they are, the best way for
you is to settle directly with the Government.
Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase, Vol. I, Bk. II, p. 328
