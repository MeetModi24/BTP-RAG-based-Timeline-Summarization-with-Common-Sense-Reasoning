234. LETTER TO V. V. DASTANE
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 30, 1927
MY DEAR DASTANE,
I have your letter. I am sorry that I did not see you. If
I was what I was before the collapse I would certainly not have
turned away a single aspirant for khadi service merely on the
ground of want of accommodation. But Jamnalalji and Shankerlal
must manage according to their abilities and their self-confi-
dence. You must discuss the pros and cons with them. I shall
do likewise but my doing will be in a leisurely fashion. Of course
you must not suffer any loss of confidence if it is at all possible.
But if we are really helpless, we must not pretend to be brave.
Above all we must be true.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. V. V. DASTANE
A.I.S.A. (MAHARASHTRA BRANCH)
PIMPARALA P.O.
