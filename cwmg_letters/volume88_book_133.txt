133. LETTER TO MOHAN SINGH THAKUR
June 18, 1947
CHI. MOHAN SINGH,
So your brother is gone, as you too will, and I and all
of us. Our grief is due to selfishness. So do not grieve. Do
what you think is your duty. If you can come back, do.
Blessings from
BAPU
