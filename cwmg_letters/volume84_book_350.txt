350. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
MUSSOORIE,
June 4, 1946
MY DEAR C. R.,
Your letter with enclosure. I drink in all you say or send.
There are obvious difficulties. But I am hoping that they can
be got over.
I have sent a short note to Sir Alladi.
How is your son? What is or was the matter with him?
Love.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
