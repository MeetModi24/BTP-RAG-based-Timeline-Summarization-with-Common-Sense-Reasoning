267. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA MUKHERJEE
SRIRAMPUR, NOAKHALI,
December 9, 1946
DEAR SATIS BABU 1 ,
Did Krishnadas tell you about the very silly mistake I made,
viz., that you had gone to your rest? How I came to have
that impression I cannot make out except for the fact that I
had not heard from you for a long time. You can therefore
imagine my joy when he told me that you were very much
alive, were able to have regular walks and were able to give
instruction to true seekers as usual. I would love to think
that you will finish your full span of 125 years which I may
not do for want of the equanimity prescribed in the conclud-
ing verses of the Gita, our kamadhenu. I am trying hard to reach
that state. Do please write to me when you have the time.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
