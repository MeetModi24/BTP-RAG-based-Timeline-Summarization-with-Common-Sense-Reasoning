328. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
August 31, 1945
CHI. BHANSALI,
I have your letter. Accept the invitation and go there for
three to four days if it is necessary.
Hope you remember about your diet.
Blessings from
BAPU
SEVAGRAM ASHRAM
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
