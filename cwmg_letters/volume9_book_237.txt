237. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
[LONDON,
End of August,] 1909
CHI. MANILAL,
Received your letter.
I shall consider myself fortunate if your mind is perfectly quiet,
if you are thoroughly absorbed in your work and if you are doing your
studies without any distractions. I do not think it necessary for you
to come to this country in a hurry. People here appear to be very
degenerate. We shall talk more about this when we meet.
That you are doing the work of teaching children is a very noble
thing.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C. W. 86
Courtesy: Sushilabehn Gandhi
