37. LETTER TO KANTI GANDHI
May 1, 1935
CHI. KANTI,
I wished every day to write to Ramachandran, but could not
decide what to say. I have not [even] asked you what you have
been doing. Now read both the accompanying letters. If you
like them, send them on to Ramachandran. Show me the letter
which you write forwarding them. Show it to Mahadev and Hari-
lal too. Send a copy to Devdas. It is my wish that this alliance
may prove an ideal one.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have gone through the cash book. I assume that you
daily complete the ledger. Let me know how far you have
come.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7295. Courtesy: Kantilal
Gandhi
