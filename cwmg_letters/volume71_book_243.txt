243. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
February 10, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have your letter. I have no objection to your coming over.
If by spending Rs. 100 you can get peace of mind, the money will
be well spent—whether it will be so is for you to consider.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8552. Also C.W. 7073. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
