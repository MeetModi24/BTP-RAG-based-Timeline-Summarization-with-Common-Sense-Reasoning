77. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
HARIJAN MANDIR, READING ROAD,
NEW DELHI,
April 25, 1946
MY DEAR MR. ABELL,
I am writing on Gandhiji's behalf.
He was under the impression that all I. N. A. prisoners were going to
be released in batches. Meanwhile he gets from Shri Raghunandan Saran
a paper with reference to three trials. A copy thereof is enclosed.
Before Gandhiji offers any further remarks he would like to know, if
you do not mind, why these impending trials and consequent irritation,
seeing that independence is imminent?
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 45
