179. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 6, 1928
BHAI MOOLCHANDJI,
Please excuse the delay in replying.
If one working in a subordinate position really possesses higher
merit than his senior, the latter will recognize his talent. But then
there is one condition; the subordinate must have perfect humility
and patience.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 762
