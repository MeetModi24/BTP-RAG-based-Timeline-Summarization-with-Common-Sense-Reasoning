454. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
March 24, 1935
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
The article "Sacrifice" is good. I shall go through the whole
series.
What struck you in Satisbabu's work? Tell me other things
too that I ought to know.
Is it possible now for Prabhavati to commence her studies?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 1097
