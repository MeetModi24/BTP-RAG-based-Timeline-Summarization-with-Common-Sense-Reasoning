291. LETTER TO PYARELAL
July 23, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
You were to write something on education. You have not
done it yet. It will be good if you write. Why not give your
own experience? You should not think that you cannot express
yourself if you hold a view different from mine.
Blessings from
BAPU
