567. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL RAINA
March 31, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I have sent "New Kashmir". But I can
give no guidance. I have not even seen Kashmir. You must be
your own judge.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SHYAMLAL RAINA
P. O. RANBIRGANJ
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
