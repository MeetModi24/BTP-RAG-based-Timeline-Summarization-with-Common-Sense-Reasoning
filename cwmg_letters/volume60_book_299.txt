299. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
February 14, 1935
CHI. NARANDAS,
I am enclosing a letter for Harilal. You are not to remain
neutral in this matter. You have to guide me, and Harilal too.
You are near him whereas I am far away.
Kanti is here just now and will remain here for the present.
See if anybody there has Gatulalji's verse translation of the Gita.
I need it, but can't get a copy anywhere.
I hope you know that Kale's machine is with me just
now and I am testing it. We have started husking paddy here.
I did it in an ordinary quern set up for light grinding.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8430. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
