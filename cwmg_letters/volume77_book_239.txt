239. LETTER TO BHARATI SARABHAI
[After July 8, 1944]
CHI. BHARATI,
I have forgotten the talk I had with Pyarelal. But I like
your book . It certainly has a beautiful theme. I like best the
portrayal of your heart, as presented in the book. I cannot be a
judge of the language. I have hardly read a few poems and
can appreciate little. I found the language affected. Moreover
I certainly wish that your poetic faculty blossoms through the
medium of Gujarati. Is there any dearth of lovers of English?
How many lovers can Gujarati boast of?
Blessings from
BAPU
B. SARABHAI
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
