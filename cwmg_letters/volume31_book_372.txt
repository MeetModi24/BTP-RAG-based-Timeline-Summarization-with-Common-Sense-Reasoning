372. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
September 1, 1926
BHAISHRI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I have your letter. I have also read the reply sent by your
secretary. You need do nothing more. Have you improved in
health? Jamnalalji is here at present.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SJT. GHANSHYAMDASJI BIRLA
PILANI
RAJPUTANA
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6134. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
