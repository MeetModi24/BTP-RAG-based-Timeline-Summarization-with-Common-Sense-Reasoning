468. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Sunday [March 7, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. The money lying in the bank is meant
only for South India. In my opinion, the money should be sent
to Rajagopalachari for helping the poor of the area.
My visit to Sinhgadh has now been postponed because there
is no facility of a bungalow. Jamnalalji suggests Abu. And I
am trying to convince him that my health will be fine even here.
Now let us see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
WINDY HALL
NASIK ROAD
DEOLALI (G.I.P.)
