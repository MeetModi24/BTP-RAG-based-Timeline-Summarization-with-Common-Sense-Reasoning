174. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM,
Kartik Sud 2 [November 5, 1918]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I hope you are well. I feel I am regaining strength. Do you
know Jamnalalji? Once or twice he came to see me at Reva-
shankarbhai's place, and it is my impression that you were pre-
sent. He will co-operate fully in the Hindi Sahitya Sammelan
Work. Please see him. We must now start the work immediately.
Swami Satyadevji 3 is also there at present and will be there
for another two days. He will proceed to Madras on Thursday
night. Keep me informed of whatever you may be doing.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
