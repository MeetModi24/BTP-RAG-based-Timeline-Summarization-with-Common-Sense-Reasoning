75. LETTER TO ELIZABETH F. HOWARD
November 25, 1932
DEAR SISTER,
I thank you for your letter and your sympathy and also your
own poem. Let me confess to you that I am so overwhelmed with
untouchability work that I have not had the time to go through
the poem, but I did not want to delay writing to you till I was
able to read the poem. I must also tell you that I am a slow
reader of poetry.
It was very good of you to have informed me of John W.
Graham's death. I should not have known it otherwise. It was
as you very properly say a very splendid way to die.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 864
