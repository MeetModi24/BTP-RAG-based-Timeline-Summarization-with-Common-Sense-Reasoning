335. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 28, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have your letter. What you have sent to Lala Ramswarup
by way of donation is all right. Send it to the Trustees.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHYAMLALJI
KASTURBA SMARAK
SCINDIA HOUSE
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
