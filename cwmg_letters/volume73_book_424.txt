424. LETTER TO MANUBEHN S. MASHRUWALA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
February 11, 1941
CHI. MANUDI,
Is what I have written above all right? So long as you were
unmarried, it did not matter if expenditure on your account was
met from public funds. But don't you think it should no longer
be so? If this hurts you, I do not want the money.
Get well soon.
Vinabehn has arrived safely.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1577. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
