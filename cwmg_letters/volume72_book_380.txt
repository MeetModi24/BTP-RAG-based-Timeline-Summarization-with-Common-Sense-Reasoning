380. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
July 29, 1940
CHI. KAKA,
Here is Brelvi's letter. The rest tomorrow at 4 o'clock. Never
mind if what you have written is too long. How can we help
if luck is against us? Tomorrow Rajaji and party are coming,
but I shall give you whatever little time I can spare. We will
finish the work bit by bit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10934
