373. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
October 21, 1947
CHI. NARAHARI,
An old letter of D. G. Tendulkar written in August last has
been lying with me. I could read it fully only yesterday.
He is writing a big volume, or probably has finished one.
He has impressed me as a very industrious man. He wants to
see Mahadev's diary. He writes and tells me that Bablo and
Paramanand are agreeable. If you have no objection, I see no
harm in letting him see it. Think it over and let me know.
I hope everything has been properly attended to.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S. N. 9146
