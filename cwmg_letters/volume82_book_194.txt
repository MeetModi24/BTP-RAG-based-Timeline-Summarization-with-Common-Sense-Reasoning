194. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
[Before November 30, 1945]
Arun is a good boy but he is not up to the mark. He does
not even know additions, though he talks of additions, subtrac-
tions, multiplications and divisions. It was only when I tested
him yesterday that I could understand the report given to me
by Valji. But it is not his fault. It is the parents' fault and
now that of us all who are here. I am the first among them.
Am I not?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4960
