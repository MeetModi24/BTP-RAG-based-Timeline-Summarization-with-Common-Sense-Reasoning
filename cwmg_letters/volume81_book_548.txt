548. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
POONA,
October 3, 1945
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter. The yarn figures this time must be inter-
esting. Let me also know how much the yarn would fetch if
sold.
You must have finished the work there. Come as soon as you
have done so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8632. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
