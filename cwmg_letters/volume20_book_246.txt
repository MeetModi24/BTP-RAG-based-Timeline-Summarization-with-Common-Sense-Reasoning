246. LETTER TO MANILAL KOTHARI AND
FULCHAND SHAH
BHAISHRI MANILAL AND FULCHAND,
[KANPUR,
August 9, 1921]
With reference to the proposal for holding a [special] session
of the Kathiawad Political Conference at Wadhwan and the sug-
gestion regarding its presidentship, I wish to say that it is not
possible for me to express an opinion now. If you ask me when
my tour is over in September, I shall be able to give a definite
reply. If I decide to accept the presidentship, I will not have things
half done. I cannot rest content with merely expressing an opi-
nion on civil disobedience. I am, therefore, unable to decide at this
stage whether I should accept the presidentship.
[From Gujarati]
Gujarati, 14-8-1921
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
