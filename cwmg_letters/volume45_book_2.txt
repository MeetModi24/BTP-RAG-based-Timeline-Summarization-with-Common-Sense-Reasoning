2. LETTER TO SANKERJI
YERAVDA,
December 16, 1930
MY DEAR SANKERJI,
I was glad to hear from you of your progress. Can you give me
the figures of khadi production in Malabar, or at least in Cochin?
Do you know where Ramachandran is? Do please write to me
from time to time.
Yours,
BAPU
The Hindu, 29-12-1930
