307. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
CHI. MANILAL,
I had your letter. There was nothing worth replying to; so I
saved time [by not writing]. What can I say if the family wants to
travel and even if the children have to put up with the heat?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 1438. Courtesy: Sushilabehn Gandhi; also
G.N. 5023
