393. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
January 20, 1930
DEAR SATIS BABU,
Either a p.c. or none. I have yours. It is well you are now
going thoroughly into the Utkal khadi business. When I was in
Utkal, I had spoken to the workers about their laziness and
ignorance in the presence of Niranjan Babu. I hope both of you
are well. Have you not sent Rs. 2,500 to Abhoy Ashram? Have
you got the amount? I have a reminder from the AA.
Yours,
BAPU
SJT. SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
SODEPUR
From a photostat: G.N. 1614
