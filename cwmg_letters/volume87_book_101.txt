101. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
PATNA,
March 17, 1947
CHI. VALLABHBHAI,
Please read the enclosed papers about Paul and do what is
necessary. I am sorry to have to put you to all this trouble but I
cannot help it. However, if you can find no time at all to read
them, forget about the matter and return the papers. If you do
anything about it, you need not send them back.
Nathji and Swami had been here. We talked at great length.
They themselves will write to you.
Sushila writes that you are not keeping good health; please
be careful.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 351
