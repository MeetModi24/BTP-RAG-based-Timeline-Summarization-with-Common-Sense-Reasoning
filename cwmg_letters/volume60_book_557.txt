557. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 8, 1935
DEAR BIBI,
You should get yourself examined by the doctor for your
brother's satisfaction if for nothing else. It is rather difficult
to say how long I shall remain at Wardha. If you follow my
instructions to the letter, I shall rid you of pleurisy and the weak-
ness of the heart. What is the alternative if the committee does
not permit a structure of sun-dried bricks?
Blessings from
BAPU
