201. LETTER TO VASUKAKA JOSHI
November 18, 1940
DEAR VASUKAKA,
Your letter is interesting. How long have you been on this
diet? What is the quantity of lemon-juice, the variety of vege-
tables and their weight and the weight of baked sweet potato?
Sweet potatoes vary in size.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 7916
