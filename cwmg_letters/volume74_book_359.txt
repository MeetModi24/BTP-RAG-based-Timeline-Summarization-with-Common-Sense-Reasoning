359. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
August 10, 1941
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
You have been sending me useful material. The friends from
Burma are also coming. I feel the proper course for me would
be to issue a statement only after I have had talks with them
all. I need a copy of the Burma Pact.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4735. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
