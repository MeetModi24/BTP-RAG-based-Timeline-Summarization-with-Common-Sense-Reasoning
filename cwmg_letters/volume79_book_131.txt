131. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
January 29, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have gone through everything. I have also made an inquiry.
I understand your letters but others may not. Therefore it is
as well that ordinarily they should be only for me. Remem-
ber that improvement in the behaviour of persons whom we
have engaged as friends, though on wages, depends entirely on
ourselves, i.e., principally on you and Chi[manlal]. This deserves
to be given thought. I am not writing anything more. Nor have
I the time. If you ask me anything, I shall reply.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 5820. Courtesy: Munnalal G.
Shah
