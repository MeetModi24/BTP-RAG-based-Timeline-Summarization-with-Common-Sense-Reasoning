492. LETTER TO NIRANJAN SWAMI
August 17, 1935
BHAI NIRANJAN SWAMI,
I found nothing in your articles to justify their publication.
Even so, your labour in thinking on those subjects and writing
the articles will not be altogether wasted.
What shall I say to Chi. Vallabh ? Vinoba will see what
should be done about him. I cannot interfere with the running
of that Ashram. I know, however, that Vallabh has to carry
heavy enough burden in managing the Nalwadi Ashram.
BAPU
SHRI NIRANJAN SWAMI
AULPAD
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10466
