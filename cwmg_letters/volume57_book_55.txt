55. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
January 27, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
I have your postcard. My letter must have crossed it. You
will have seen from it that I was actually moving to have you
sent to Sabarmati if only by way of trial.
I hope you are keeping well. Did I tell you that that German
friend was with us for some days? He has made himself quite
useful.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
