281. LETTER TO M. S. ANEY
[May 28, 1946]
BAPUJI,
I have your letter. Since nothing has yet happened there is
no need to hurry. Vallabhbhai will write. He said he would.
Vandemataram
BAPU
BAPUJI ANEY
11 STANMORE CRESELEY
HAVELOCK TOWN
COLOMBO, CEYLON
