379. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 5, 1939
CHI. MAHADEV,
There is no letter from you today. Acting on your telegram,
I have sent a telegram to J. that he should follow your advice.
But he does not seem to like the advice. Let him enter the
field then. I consider the Viceroy's statement pretty bad. You
will have seen my reply. Things will take their own course.
Do not worry. You must regain your health.
Both Vijaya and Sharada will be married here at 3 o'clock
(by my watch) on the 7th. Nanabhai will conduct the cere-
mony. Nanabhai will be coming here tomorrow with Manubhai.
Gordhandas will arrive on the 7th morning. No guests have
been invited. We do not want to invite any. The poor people
of Segaon will witness the wedding. The altar will be built
under a mango tree.
Blessings from
BAPU
