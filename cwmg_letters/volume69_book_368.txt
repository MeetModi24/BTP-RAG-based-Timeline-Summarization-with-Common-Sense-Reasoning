368. LETTER TO VANAMALA PARIKH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 17, 1939
CHI. VANAMALA,
Your friend is sitting near me and I have heard from her
plenty of stories about your sense of humour. I hope you are
adding to it there. I have your letter. Guess who this friend of
yours is. Perfect your Urdu.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5787
