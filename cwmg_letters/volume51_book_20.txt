20. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
September 3, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. [You must] improve your handwriting
a good deal. Ask Premabehn to teach you how to do it. If you
do not at once go to sleep as soon as you lie down, you should
close your eyes and begin repeating Ramanama. You will soon fall
asleep.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9432. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
