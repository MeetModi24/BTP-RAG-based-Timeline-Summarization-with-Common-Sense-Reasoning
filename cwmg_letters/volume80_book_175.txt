175. LETTER TO S. K. PATIL
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 10, 1945
BHAI PATIL 1 ,
I am not satisfied with the report you have sent to Pyarelal.
I wanted the published public programme of the Party. Still
it is something. I hope all the efforts being made will bear fruit
and the constructive activity will progress.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI S. K. PATIL, M.L.A. (BOMBAY)
HIRA HOUSE
381 SANDHURST ROAD
BOMBAY 4
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
