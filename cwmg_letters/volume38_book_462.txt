462. LETTER TO THE SECRETARY, ALL-INDIA
SPINNERS' ASSOCIATION
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 24, 1929
THE SECRETARY
A.I.S.A., AHMEDABAD
DEAR SIR,
With reference to your enquiry, it is difficult to say now
whether I should be able to give a few days for Utkal tour when
I go to Andhra. Where would they want me to go and how much
time will they want?
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13294
