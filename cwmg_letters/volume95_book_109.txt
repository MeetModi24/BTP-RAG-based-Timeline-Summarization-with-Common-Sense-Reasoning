109. LETTER TO SHARADA G. CHOKHAWALA
SIMLA,
[May-August 1931]
CHI. SHARADA,
Have you stopped writing letters to me because I have stopped
writing? I have stopped writing because I am not in prison. I must
be in prison to write letters regularly. Should I go to prison, or will
you write even otherwise?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C. W. 9960. Courtesy : Sharadabehn G.
Chokhawala
