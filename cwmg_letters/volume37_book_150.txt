150. LETTER TO CHOWDHARY MUKHTAR SINGH
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August, 4 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
Whist there is an undercurrent of truth in your letter there is,
I am afraid, much confusion of thought. If a people will not
become industrious and self-supporting by following a useful occu-
pation they will never be able to throw off [the] foreign yoke.
Prosperity is totally different from ability to support oneself by
dint of labour.
Yours sincerely,
CHOWDHARY MUKHTAR SINGH 2
