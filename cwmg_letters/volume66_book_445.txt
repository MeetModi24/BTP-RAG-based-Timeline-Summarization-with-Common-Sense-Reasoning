445. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
February 5, 1938
CHI. KAKA,
Please don't come today at any rate. Come at 3.00 the day
after tomorrow. Do I have to draft the syllabus ? Or will it
be the one you have brought from Aligarh? If it is the latter,
I have already seen it. I find it difficult to write the introduc-
tion. I wish to leave on the 8th. I shall see if I can give it
before that date. I shall sign and send over the Kamath [papers]
after I have gone through them. Be sure that you at any rate
come over. Kishorelal also may come if his health permits.
Blessings from
BAPU
