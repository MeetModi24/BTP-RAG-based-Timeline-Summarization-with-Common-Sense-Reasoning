367. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
May 16, 1929
CHI. MIRA,
I have one letter of 11 th. Of course you write as often as you
like and make use of the distance-destroying conveniences. I
simply pointed out that we might not disturb our peace when and
if they were withdrawn.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
I am going away from the Calcutta route now. So I may not
write at all now. For we meet at Bombay on 22nd, God willing.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5377. Courtesy: Mirabehn
