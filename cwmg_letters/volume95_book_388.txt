388. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Sunday
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Pratap Pandit should be returning to Bombay this evening by
the Gujarat Mail. Please send the car for him at 7.30 p.m. Sir
Prabhashanker Pattani is here these days. Come over in a day or two
and meet him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 32771
