372. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
Silence Day [November 22, 1937]
CHI. MAHADEV,
I want to see the resolution about Mysore passed at
Calcutta. This means a little more work for you.
I should also
like to see a copy of the letter you have written to Jawaharlal.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11580
