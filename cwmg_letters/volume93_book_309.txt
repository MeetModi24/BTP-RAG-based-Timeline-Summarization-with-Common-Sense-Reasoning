309. LETTER TO HANNA LAZAR
August 28, 1938
MY DEAR HANNA,
Just one line to tell you I have your letter from Mombassa
with your carefully prepared notes with considerately made mul-
tiple copies.
I hope you had an enjoyable voyage and that you have
regained lost health. You must make proper preparations for
your next visit here.
Except from you no further letter from Johannesburg.
Love to you all.
BAPU
[PS.]
Pyarelal is normal today. How nice you were not here
during the typhoid he was passing through.
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence.
Courtesy :
National Archives of India
