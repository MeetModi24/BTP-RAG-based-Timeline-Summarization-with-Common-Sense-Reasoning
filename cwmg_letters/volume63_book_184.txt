184. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
July 16, 1936
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I got your note well in time. It is easy to say, 'May you
live a hundred years.' However, it is for you to make the effort.
It is enough if we regard our body as a trust held in behalf
of God and use it accordingly. Take care of your body. Take
up no more burden than it can carry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10718
