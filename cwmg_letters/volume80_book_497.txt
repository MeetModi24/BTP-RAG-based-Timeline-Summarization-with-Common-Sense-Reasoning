497. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
June 10, 1945
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letter. Now Jairamdas is with me. I have asked
him to write to you. It is only to console you. Do get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
