252. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
August 4, 1936
BHAI MUNSHI,
Since he insists on starting another magazine, why should
we retain Hans? Why not write to him:
"Since you contemplate another magazine you must keep Hans.
Parishad can easily manage with other title."
You may carry out your own suggestion if you do not think
this right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7604. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
