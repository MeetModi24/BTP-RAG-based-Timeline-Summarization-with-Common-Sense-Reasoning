479. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
September 11, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
It is just now striking 9.
Sushila has not returned and so
I feel a little worried about your health. For the present let
the post come unopened. You need not carry any load. I am
thinking about future arrangements for Harijan.
I am not at all
worried. The Great Editor will do whatever is necessary.
I am having hearty talks with Mother.
She is a flawless
jewel.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11645
