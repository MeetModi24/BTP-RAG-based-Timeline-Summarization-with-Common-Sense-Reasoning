149. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
JUHU,
May 31, 1944
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I have your letter. Let me write a few words to you too,
since I am writing a little to all those dear to me. You seem to
be well settled there. Satis Babu gets, I hope, the help which
you should give him. Take care of yourself. Restrain your desire
to visit me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1944
