279. LETTER TO LORD WAVELL
'DILKHUSHA', PANCHGANI,
July 17, 1944
H . E . THE VICEROY
VICEROY'S CAMP
DEAR FRIEND,
I ventured to write to you yesterday repeating my previous
request. I feel that my effort would be incomplete unless I
sent the accompanying to the Prime Minister. May I seek your
help, if you agree, to send the enclosed in the quickest manner
possible?
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 10502. Courtesy: India Office Library. Also
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, p. 11
