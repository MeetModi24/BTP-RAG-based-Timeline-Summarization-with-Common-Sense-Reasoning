132. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA AND
MUNNALAL G. SHAH
June 30, 1936
CHI. BALWANTSINHA AND MUNNALAL,
I shall find some other remedy for the worms. All my
thoughts are with you.
I am again sending the milk today. It is no trouble.
Yesterday too I sent it of my own accord. I have not met
Kanchan so far; she might come today.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
You may use Jamnalalji's portion, and Nanavati may use
Ba's. Hurry up with the weaving shed as soon as the skies
are clear. What more need I say in the matter? Fill up the
vacancy that might occur. Here too there is sure to be some
vacancy. The person who has to do the work should find out
what must be done.
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 6995. Courtesy: Munnalal G. Shah.
