146. LETTER TO BHOLANATH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 3, 1940
BHAI BHOLANATH,
I got your letter. I am aware of the difficulties there. I
don't know what may happen.
Although I am making some
arrangements, the results may be meagre. If the people have
the strength to resist, they must do so. No one should think
that I can counsel compromise with such injustice. Men may
break, but they should not bend before brute force.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1311. Also Gandhi Shatabdi Smarak Granth
—Yug Purush, p. 184
