373. LETTER TO K. V. RATNAM
July 16, 1935
DEAR RATNAM,
I must thank you for the fountain pen you have sent me
through Shri Kumarappa. I have received it and it seems to be
a good substitute for the foreign pens one sees in the bazaars.
Yours sincerely,
M. K . GANDHI
K. V. RATNAM AND BROTHERS
RATNAM FOUNTAIN PEN WORKS
RAJAHMUNDRY
From a photostat: G.N. 816
