406. LETTER TO PYARELAL
October 12, 1945
CHI. PYARELAL,
I must have a letter from Manu. When I see the situation you
are in, I hate myself. And yet, who am I to interfere ? Do as your
heart prompts you. Take a decision.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
