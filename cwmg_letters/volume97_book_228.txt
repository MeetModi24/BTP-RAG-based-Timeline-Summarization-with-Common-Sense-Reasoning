228. LETTER TO BUDHABHAI
March 5, 1933
CHI. BUDHABHAI,
Parvati has made a serious complaint against you. Have we not made an
agreement that you will not abuse her nor beat her? To abuse or beat a weak
woman ought to be regarded as a terrible form of violence. Wake up, reform
your ways, calm down. If a person like you cannot exercise such self-control,
whom else can we expect to observe ahimsa?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33121
