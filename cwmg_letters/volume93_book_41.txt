41. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
May 20, 1935
CHI. SUMANGAL,
I have your letter. I know what value to place on the
sentiments it contains. I know how permanent they are. Remain
quiet for a couple of months. Improve your health. Eat spar-
ingly. Find pleasure in what is good. If what pleases is not
good, give it up. Do not worry about the future. Take good
care of the present.
This covers everything. I shall be at Borsad from the
23rd to the 31st. Then Wardha.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy:
