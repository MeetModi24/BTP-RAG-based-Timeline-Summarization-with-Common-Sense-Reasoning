587. LETTER TO T. J. KEDAR
SODEPUR,
January 16, 1946
BHAI KEDAR,
I got your letter of January 12. I was very happy. Let us
see what happens.
I hope to reach Sevagram on the 8th. I shall stay there for
ten days and then proceed to Poona.
SHRI T. J. KEDAR
ADVOCATE
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
