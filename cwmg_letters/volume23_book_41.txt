41. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS
[SABARMATI JAIL,]
Sunday, March 12, 1922
MY DEAR KRISTODAS 2 ,
The correspondence, reports, etc., should come to you for dis-
posal.
Unless it is too much for you, all articles must finally pass
through your hands.
I have several names as Editor (Satis Babu Rajagopalachari4,
you, Shuaib 5 , Kaka 6 , Devdas 7 .
It would be better now if Satis Babu gave you the permission
to sign articles.
The room should be entirely at your disposal. You should lock
the verandah door from inside. Fix up the whole office there.
Hardikar and the Bulletin staff should be there for work but under
your permission.
Of course you have my blessings. God will give you all the
strength and wisdom you need.
BAPU
Seven Months with Mahatma Gandhi, p. 260
