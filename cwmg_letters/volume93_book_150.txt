150. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL VIDYARTHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 23, 1936
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
Your letter is good. The Bhajan Mandali's object is the
same as yours. But of course, you must arrange things as
you want. Both ways are good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 11679
