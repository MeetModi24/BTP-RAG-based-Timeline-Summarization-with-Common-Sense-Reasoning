176. LETTER TO N. D. KOWALI
SIMLA,
July 18, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
At one time I thought that I would publish your letter in
Young India and deal with it. But on a second reading of your
letter I have come to the conclusion that the cases, such as you
mention, not being of common occurrence, I should give you a
private reply.
The two families excommunicated should boldly face ex-
communication and suffer the consequences. As reformers they
will form new ties and having broken the caste barrier, they will
have ample compensation in kindred reformers helping them on
occasions of marriage or mourning. And if side by side with this
indifference, they would remain courteous towards the persecutors
it would be found that the edge of persecution will lose its sharp-
ness.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 3274
