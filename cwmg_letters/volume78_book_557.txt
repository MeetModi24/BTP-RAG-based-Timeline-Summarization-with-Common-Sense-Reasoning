557. LETTER TO BENARSIDAS CHATURVEDI
SEVAGRAM,
December 27, 1944
BHAI BENARSIDAS,
It is natural to feel sad at Father's passing away but if we
reflect for a moment we shall realize that it is futile to grieve
over the inevitable. Why then should one grieve? And who is it
that dies? Surely not the jiva which was always associated with
us, which is with us now and will remain with us hereafter.
Father's last words are very precious to me. I shall regard
them as a blessing.
Blessings from
BAPU
PANDIT BENARSIDAS CHATURVEDI
FEROZABAD
DISTRICT AGRA (U. P.)
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2575
