118. LETTER TO G. SITARAMA SASTRY
SEVAGRAM,
Independence Day, January 26, 1945
MY DEAR S. SASTRY,
Deshabhakta Venkatappayya is older than I am. But he
shows the same intellect and energy as before. May he live long
and die in harness. He deserves it.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
SITARAMA SASTRY
VINAYA ASHRAM
DIST. GUNTUR
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
