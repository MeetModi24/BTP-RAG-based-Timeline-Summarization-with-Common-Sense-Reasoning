29. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
SEVAGRAM,
August 14, 1944
CHI. VALJI,
I feel relieved that I am able to payoff today this measure
of my debt to you. I feel that something still remains to be paid.
You are not bound to accept the changes I have suggested.
You may accept them if you feel that they are improvements.
This will be sent by registered post.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7499. Courtesy : Valji G. Desai
