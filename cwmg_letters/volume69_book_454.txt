454. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
ABBOTTABAD,
July 12, 1939
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
I got your letter. You never take care of your health. This
is no good. If you wish to be treated by Vijaya's vaid do so.
You have to get well for the sake of your work.
Ba and others will arrive day after tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 424
