611. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
Monday [October 24, 1927]
CHI. DEVDAS,
I have Dr. Rajan's letter about you today. There is nothing
of note in it. There is a telegram from the Viceroy today
and so I have to be in Delhi on November 2. Hence, from
Mangalore I shall either go back to Madras or, if I can get the
booking on the steamer, I shall go to Bombay and from Bom-
bay to Delhi. I shall have to get back to go to Colombo. I
wish you now to be calm.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. DEVDAS GANDHI
C/O DR. RAJAN
TRICHY, S. INDIA
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10899. Courtesy: Awadhanandan
