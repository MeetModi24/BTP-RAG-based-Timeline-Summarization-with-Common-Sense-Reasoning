99. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI H. PATEL
GURUKUL,
Friday [March 17, 1916]
DEAR MAGANBHAI,
I cannot forget Fakiri. We have a lot to learn from his
death. I shall write about it when I have the leisure. I shall
definitely be here till the 20th, and maybe till the 21st. The
subsequent programme has not yet been fixed.
Manilal will have gone to Ajmer. If not, he should go at
once.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I wish you would make up your mind and come to a deci-
sion.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 11002. Courtesy: Suryakant C.
Patel
