57. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
June 4, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
You did well in writing the letters. I want to know you as
you are and not as you would be.
I shall love you in spite
of your faults. You have no cause to fear me. You must dismiss
from your mind the thought that I hate you.
I hate nobody
in the world. You never gave me any cause for hating you.
On the contrary you have given me many reasons for loving
you. You must not therefore let your imagination run away
with you.
I have read your letter to Mira.
I think you should go to
a cool place. I could easily send you somewhere. Why should
you swear by German medicine? There are competent doctors
enough in Ahmedabad. Tell Parikshitlal and he will take you
to one. You should be sane in these matters.
You will be a
cause of great anxiety to me, if you become ill and your body is
undermined.
Why do you want to be like Mira? She is not perfect.
No one is perfect. You should strive to be good according to the
gifts God has given you. No two persons are alike in the world.
But all can be good if they choose to.
We shall soon meet. I would like you to be fit when we
meet. And this you will be, if you won't brood over your ill-
ness and take the necessary measures for getting well.
I enclose herewith some letters to be handed to those
concerned.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
