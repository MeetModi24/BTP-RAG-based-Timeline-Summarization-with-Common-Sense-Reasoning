27. LETTER TO THE VOGLS
JOHANNESBURG,
February 1, 1908
DEAR MR. AND MRS. VOGL 1 ,
Kindly accept my thanks for your congratulations . I do indeed
believe that your good wishes are an expression of the heart and not
merely a formal one.
Mahomed Khan told me yesterday that Mrs. Vogl was keeping
indifferent health. I was sorry to hear it. I wish I could come over
to see her as also to thank you both personally, but just at present I
must know no rest. The work of destruction is finished, that of construction
has commenced-a far more difficult one, but, seeing that I have not
relied upon my own strength but on the strength of Truth, otherwise
spelt God, I am quite at ease.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
