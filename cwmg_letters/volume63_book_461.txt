461. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
October 8, 1936
CHI. LILA,
I have your letter. You would be stable if you stayed with
Mahadev; going to Nagpur is lack of stability. Therefore you
had better stay with him; or at the Mahila Ashram if you can
bear with the life there, though I am afraid you will not be able
to. Be sure I have not given you up for lost.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9350. Also C.W. 6625. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
