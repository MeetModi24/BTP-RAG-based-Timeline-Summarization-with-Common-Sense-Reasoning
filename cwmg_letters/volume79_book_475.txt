475. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
SEVAGRAM,
March 20, 1945
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
Again the same thing has happened. Although I sent you a
telegram, Vimaladevi will not be going to Delhi now. She will
instead go to Simla with her husband. So forget about her now.
Blessings from
BAPU
HARIJAN ASHRAM
DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
