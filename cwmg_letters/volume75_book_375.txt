375. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
February 13, 1942
MY DEAR AMRITLAL,
Your letter.
You can send Vina when you like. She will be well kept
in Rajkot.
Sailen will send you at least Rs. 25 per month.
As for yourself I am not sure of your being happy here,
less sure of your wife. Then the Ashram is crowded. Where
to put you is also a question. If you come, how many will you
be? Will you be where I put you and do the work that may be
entrusted to you? You will see that I am anxious to help you
to the best of my ability.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10336. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
