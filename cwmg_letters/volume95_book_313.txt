313. LETTER TO VIMALA JOSHI
Tuesday
CHI. VIMU,
Your letter can be considered good for you. I thought you had
completely forgotten me. Now keep writing. Grow strong quickly.
You should go to the Ashram if you feel inclined. Write to me if you
want.
Blessings from
BAPU
