30. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
RAJKOT,
March 5, 1939
CHI. MANI,
Why are you uneasy? Are such experiences new to you?
You have exceeded my expectations in this matter. I have come
on my own. I thought it my duty. God has inspired me to
come. Do not be in the least unhappy. I am not writing any
letters now, excepting one to Ba and this to you.
BAPU
SHRI MANIBEHN PATEL
PRISONER
C/O FIRST MEMBER
RAJKOT
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 125
