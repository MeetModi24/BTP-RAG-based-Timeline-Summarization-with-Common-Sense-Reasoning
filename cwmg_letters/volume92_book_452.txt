452. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
Silence Day, June 8, 1931
CHI. MANGALA,
You must always write as neatly as you have written this
time. If all of you improve your handwriting, mine will automati-
cally improve. And how nice it would be if my handwriting
improved!
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11095. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
