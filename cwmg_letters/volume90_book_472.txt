472. LETTER TO VIJAYA WALJI SODAWALA
NEW DELHI,
4.30 a. m., January 29, 1948
CHI. VIJAYA,
You did well in writing to me. It is a matter of shame that
you should be put to such straits. It is being delayed probably
because the arrangements are new.
I hope everything will be
all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: K. G. Mashruwala Records. Courtesy:
Navajivan Trust, Ahmedabad
