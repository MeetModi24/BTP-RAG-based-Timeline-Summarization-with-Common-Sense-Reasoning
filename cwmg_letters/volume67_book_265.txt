265. LETTER TO R.
July 22, 1938
BHAI R.1,
Balkrishna conveyed to me your message. I find your case
similar to that of a man living in the midst of fire yet pleading
to be protected from fire. I think you should satisfy your sexual
desire through G. This is the way of the world. Those who
want to be free of passion cannot do so by merely wishing it.
The wish must be accompanied by incessant effort. This is beyond
you. This is what I feel. If your heart indicates another way
and you can master your sexual impulses I for one shall be ex-
tremely happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 212
