242. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
July 18, 1938
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Your question is relevant when considering brahmacharya.
Whether procreation by itself is right or not is a separate issue.
We can even question whether it is right for a brahmachari to
engage in any activity at all. But the problem before us is to
determine when brahmacharya is violated. I hold that no viola-
tion of brahmacharya occurs when a man does anything while
remaining free of passion and attachment. The vital fluid is
power and its proper use does not impair brahmacharya; this is
the assumption underlying my interpretation.
Blessings from
BAPU
