251. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
August 28, 1946
CHI. SUSHILA,
I got your letter here. I am glad that Ila has recovered.
All that I could write to you is covered by my letter to Mani-
lal, a copy of which is attached. For the present I am camp-
ing here. I cannot say when I shall move out.
SMT. SUSHILA GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
