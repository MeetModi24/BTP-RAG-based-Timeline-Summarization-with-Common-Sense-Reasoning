552. LETTER TO SECRETARY, HINDI
UNIVERSITY COMMITTEE
April 7, 1935
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter. The funds I have asked for are for the
propagation of Hindi in the South. Thus I can spare you nothing
out of that one lakh. If there are any further donations they can
be put down. Of course the work undertaken by your committee
is commendable.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SECRETARY
HINDI UNIVERSITY COMMITTEE
INDORE
CENTRAL INDIA
[From Hindi]
Veena, Tributes Issue, April-May 1969
