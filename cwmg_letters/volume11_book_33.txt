33. LETTER TO L. W. RITCH
[CAPE TOWN,]
April 17, 1911
MY DEAR RITCH,
I have your letter. I did not write to you yesterday. There
was nothing to write about. I have written a leaderette in the
Guj. columns of I. O. showing how the public can, if they wish to,
appreciate your work. It should have been printed last Saturday.
If Lane is right, we should have the definite result known
this week. Though Smuts would like to do it, I cannot imagine
the thing being carried over to the Coronation time. However,
it is no use speculating. If there is disappointment again on
Wednesday, we should still know the worst before many more
days have gone by.
I do hope that Gregorowski will be able to go to Bloem-
fontein. It will be very sad if he cannot. In that event it would
be better to ask Lapin to send whom he likes. If there is any-
thing definite on Wednesday, I propose to ask for relief for Mrs.
Sodha This will be in your hands on Thursday. I may there-
fore wire tomorrow regarding this matter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 5468
