399. LETTER TO KHUSHALCHAND GANDHI
March 12, 1935
REVERED BHAI,
Narandas wrote to me about your illness. If you are at all
inclined to take treatment, I have suggested an extremely simple
one.
I do not expect a letter from you.
Respects from
MOHANDAS
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
