252. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
July 23, 1934
MY DEAR MALKANI,
Your letter. Have read your suggestions which will be dis-
cussed in Benares. You have not sent me your franchise proposal
though you mention it in your letter.
I heard from Jamnalalji some days ago but forgot to write
to you to say that he is revising the whole constitution of the
Sangh and thinks that you could not be taken. More in Benares.
Yours,
BAPU
PROF. MALKANI
BIRLA MILLS
DELHI
From a photostat: G.N. 908
