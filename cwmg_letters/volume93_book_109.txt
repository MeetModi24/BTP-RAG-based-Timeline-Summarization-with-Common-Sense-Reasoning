109. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
May 30, 1936
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. It is an excuse that hand-made paper is
not available there. Do not people find similar pretexts for not
wearing khadi? All these are signs of laziness and lack of
earnestness.
I shall reach Wardha latest on the 17th or 18th. It is pro-
bable that I shall leave by the 14th or the 15th.
Prema's address is: Sasvad, near Poona.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
