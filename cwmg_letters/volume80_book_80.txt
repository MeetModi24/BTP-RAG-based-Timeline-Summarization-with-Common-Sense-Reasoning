80. LETTER TO SAROJINI
April 30, 1945
CHI. SAROJINIBEHN,
I keep receiving many complaints against you. You do not
even pay for your expenses to the Secretary. Pay for your expenses
and leave the Ashram. You are not likely to benefit from staying
in the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 5870
