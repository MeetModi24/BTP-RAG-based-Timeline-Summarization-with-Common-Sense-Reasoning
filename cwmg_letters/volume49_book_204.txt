204. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
March 5, 1932
CHI. KUSUM,
What a woman you are! You wrote to me a postcard and a
letter, and yet could tell me nothing. What did you read all
these months, what thoughts came to you, how much did you
spin, what health did you maintain, what places did you visit?
If you wish, you can tell me all this and much else.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1830
