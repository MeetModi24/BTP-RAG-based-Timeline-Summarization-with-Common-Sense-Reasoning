40. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
November 20, 1932
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
You must not expect long letters from me these days. Give
up worrying for Kudsia. Whatever is destined for her will happen.
Don't talk of your own death. Why do you get so disheartened?
God will exact abundant service from you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 264
