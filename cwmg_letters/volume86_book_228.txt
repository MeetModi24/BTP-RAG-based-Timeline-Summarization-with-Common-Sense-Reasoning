228. LETTER TO J. B. KRIPALANI
[December 2, 1946]
DEAR PROFESSOR,
Do you know anything about the Madras Charkha Scheme?
How are you getting along?
Whose wire is this? I cannot persuade myself to invite anyone
here. It is all darkness for me; I could think of something if I
saw a ray of light. I am watching developments.
When is Sucheta coming?
Krishnadas met me and gave me all the news.
Blessings from
BAPU
Enclosure : A telegram
ACHARYA KRIPALANI
NEW DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
