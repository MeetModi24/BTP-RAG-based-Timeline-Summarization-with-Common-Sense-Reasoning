403. LETTER TO H. J. HOFMEYR
Delivered Personal
[JOHANNESBURG,]
June 2, 1905
TO
H. J. HOFMEYR, ESQ.
ZIMAN'S BLDGS.
JOHANNESBURG
DEAR MR. HOFMEYR,
I must confess that your letter enclosing cheque staggers me because
I venture to think that there is a principle at stake. The cheque was given
to me ear-marked. You know the money belonged to me. It did not come
out of anything I had for Said Ismail, and seeing that the property to
buy which it was given was not purchased, I think I was entitled to a
return of the full cheque. I know that I won't come to grief with reference
to the charges deducted by you, but, to my mind, it is hardly conducive
to the growth of confidential relations which ought to exist between pro-
fessional men. I hope you do not mind my writing to you so frankly,
but I thought that I ought to pass on the thoughts that occurred to me
when I saw your cheque, letter and the bill. Of course, I accept your
cheque, so that anything I have said herein does not disturb the deduction
made by you.
I am,
Yours truly
M. K. GANDHI
Letter Book (1905) : No. 220.
