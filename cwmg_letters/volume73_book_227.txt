227. LETTER TO CHIANG KAI-SHEK
November 26, 1940
DEAR GENERALISSIMO,
I am deeply grateful to you for your affectionate letter received
through the goodwill mission you have so thoughtfully sent. Shri
Tai Chi-Tao has given me, too, your verbal message. My only
regret is that Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru will not be able to receive
the members of the mission when they reach Allahabad.
I have no doubt that the mission has drawn our countries
close together.
May your deliverance take place sooner than expected.
With revered good wishes for you, your good wife and your
nation.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 10410. Courtesy: Pyarelal
