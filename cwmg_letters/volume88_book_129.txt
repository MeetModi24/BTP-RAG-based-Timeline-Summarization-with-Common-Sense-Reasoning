129. LETTER TO SURENDRARAI B. MEDH
NEW DELHI,
June 17, 1947
CHI. MEDH2,
Tell the satyagrahis that they should adhere to truth and
non-violence and not violate their pledge even in the face of
death.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 5005
1 Omission as in the source
2 A co-worker of Gandhiji in South Africa
