18. LETTER TO KISHENSINGH CHAVDA
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
Jeth Sud 8, 1982 [June 18, 1926]
BHAISHRI KISHENSINGH1,
It is good that you wrote to me. The letter was left in the
diary and, as soon as you left, I got busy with other matters. I
could never get away from them and remember it. Please pardon
me.
I enclose a few scribbled sheets .
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19400
