80. LETTER TO M. A. JINNAH
August 31, 1944
BHAI JINNAH,
I have your telegram. Thanks.
I had written a letter to you on the 29th. You must have
got it. As mentioned in it I have already fixed up appoint-
ments with various people up to the 7th. I can, therefore, start from
here on 8th September at the earliest and reach there on the
9th. I hope the day will suit you. If it does, kindly let me know.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
