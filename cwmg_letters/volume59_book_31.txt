31. LETTER TO LAKSHMI GANDHI
September 19, 1934
CHI. LAKSHMI,
Devdas has gone away but as you have a person like Thakkar
Bapa to look after you, I think you do not feel the absence of
Devdas over much.
LAKSHMI
DELHI
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
