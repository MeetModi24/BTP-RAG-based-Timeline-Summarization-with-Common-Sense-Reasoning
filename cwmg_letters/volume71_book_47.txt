47. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 14, 1939
CHI. LILA,
I have your letter. What shall I write? There isn't even a
minute to spare. Mahadev was here to see me only recently.
Now he is in Mysore. He left on Sunday. Probably he will
return on Sunday. Bablo has gone with him. Ba is still in
Delhi. Lakshmi is better. All the patients are improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Study attentively during the holidays and pass your examina-
tion.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9378
