360. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
[April 30, 1930]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I was in fact expecting a letter from you. It is 10 p.m. now.
I will not, therefore, write more. Kamalabehn also should write.
It will be at least a week before we start on the march. Come
before that.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. VASUMATIBEHN
SATYAGRAHA CAMP, BHIMRAD
Via SURAT
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 544. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit; also
S.N.9280
