244. LETTER TO MISS E. J. BECK
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 22, 1906
DEAR MADAM,
I shall thank you to let me have the book of instructions for Indian
youths coming to England for education as per advertisement in the
magazine.
I am,
Yours faithfully,
MISS E.J. BECK
233, ALBION ROAD
STOKE NEWINGTON, N.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4631
