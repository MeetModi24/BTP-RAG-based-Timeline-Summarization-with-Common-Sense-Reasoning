291. LETTER TO ABDUL QAIYUM
October 14, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of the 6th instant.
In regard to your question Gandhiji desires me to say that if the party members
are in favour of attending the session of the Assembly he is not going to interfere.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
JANAB ABDUL QAIYUM
M. L. A. (CENTRAL)
ABIGUZAR
SRINAGAR
