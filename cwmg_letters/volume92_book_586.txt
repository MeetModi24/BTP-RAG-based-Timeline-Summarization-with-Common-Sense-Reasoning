586. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
October 2, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
I shall not write a long letter now. I hope you are well.
I am regaining strength. It is God's mercy. Convey my blessings
to Anand.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
