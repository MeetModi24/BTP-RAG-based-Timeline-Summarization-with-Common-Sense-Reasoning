356. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Maha Vad 4 [February 12, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Give the enclosed papers to Maulana Shaukat Ali and con-
vey his reply to me. Or he himself can send me the reply.
You must have received the message I sent for Shuaib. I
am leaving for Rajkot on the 14th night. It would be nice if
he could be there at that time.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Yes, that Englishman is none other than Polak.
