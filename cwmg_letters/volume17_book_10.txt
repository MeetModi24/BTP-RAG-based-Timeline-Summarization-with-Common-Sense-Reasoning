10. LETTER TO BRIJSUNDER DASS
2, MOZANG ROAD,
LAHORE,
February 5, 1920
DEAR B. BRIJSUNDER DASS,
I have received your printed circular on the Oriya Movement.
I have not got the papers; probably they will follow. I would,
however, like you to keep me informed of the progress that the
Movement may make from time to time. I am giving a para-
graph to it in Young India which, I hope, you receive.
Yours sincerely,
PRESIDENT
ORIYA PEOPLE'S ASSOCIATION
CUTTACK
From a photostat: S. N. 7105
