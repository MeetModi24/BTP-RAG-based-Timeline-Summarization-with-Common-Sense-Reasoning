408. LETTER TO HILDA CASHMORE
November 16, 1934
DEAR SISTER,
I have your undated letter. I shall be delighted to see you
and Miss Dorothy Hersey on 4th December next at 3.30 p.m. I
hope the time will suit you.
Yours sincerely,
MISS HILDA CASHMORE
FRIENDS' CENTRE
ITARSI (C. P.)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
