312. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
May 4, 1929
CHI. MIRA,
I am without any news from you for four days now. In the
little village where I am writing this there is no chance of my
getting anything today. Such is the most interesting tour this
time. Orissa villages and Tamil Nad were nothing. Here is all
touring in the interior. I should learn much more, if I had lei-
sure. But I must take what comes my way and be satisfied. Health
still excellent.
Love.
BAPU
SHRIMATI MIRABAI
C/O BABU RAJENDRA PRASAD
P.O. ZERADAI
(SARAN) BIHAR
From the original: C.W. 5370. Courtesy: Mirabehn; also G. N. 9426
