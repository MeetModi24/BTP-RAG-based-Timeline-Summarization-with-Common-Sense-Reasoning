141. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
DATTAPARA,
November 12, 1946
BHAI RAJENDRA BABU,
You have again fallen ill! How are you now? What is the
condition in Bihar? Are those who committed the atrocities
relenting? Do they need me there? They should not. If the
Bihar fury does not abate, I do not wish to remain alive because
my life would then be meaningless. Write to me what precisely
the condition is.
The work here is very delicate. Let us see what comes about.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. RAJENDRA PRASAD
PATNA
