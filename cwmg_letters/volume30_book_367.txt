367. LETTER TO N. C. KELKAR
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 17, 1926
DEAR MR. KELKAR,
I have your wire in reply to which I have sent the following:
"Do come. Man proposes God disposes."
I had no idea of this informal conference.
Panditji tele-
graphed to me saying I should fix dates convenient for me before
leaving for Mussoorie and inform him and Mr. Jayakar of the
dates. I wired accordingly to both of them and Mr. Jayakar wired
to me saying he would come but that I should send my own wires
to you, Dr. Moonje, and Mr. Aney which I did. Whether the
conference will be fruitful or not will depend on the mode that
guides us all when we meet. I have not heard from Panditji as to
what he proposes to do or what he expects, or on what basis he
has decided this informal conference.
I have just now received wires from Dr. Moonje and Mr. Aney
that they will attend. Mr. Aney's says that friends in Bengal and
elsewhere should also be invited. But I presume Panditji has issued
invitations himself.
Hoping to have the pleasure of meeting you on Tuesday.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. C. KELKAR
"KESARI" OFFICE
POONA
