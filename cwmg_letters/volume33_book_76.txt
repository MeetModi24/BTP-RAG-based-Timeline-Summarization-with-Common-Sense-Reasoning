76. LETTER TO HEMAPRABHA DEVI DAS GUPTA
DHULIA,
Monday [February 14, 1927]
DEAR SISTER,
God is testing you severely. Never give up. Happiness and un-
happiness, birth and death, old age and disease are ever with
us. I could not bear to see you crying when I was at Sodepur.
It is good if God would let our dear ones be with US, nor is it
bad if He takes them away. They belong to Him; and what is it
to us if He takes away his own? Surely, He is going to take
away everyone. Being the greatest of all actors we call Him
Natavar, the Supreme Actor. He tosses us as He pleases. We are
after all puppets. Is it not better to dance in tune with Him
than with the world?
May God grant peace to you all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1663
