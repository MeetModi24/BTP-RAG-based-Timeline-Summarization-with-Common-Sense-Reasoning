126. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEVAGRAM,
May 25, 1940
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
What you desire is of course good. It is, however, advisable to
keep patience as long as my letters do not reach you in the natural
course. I shall remember your wish. It will be fulfilled in its own
good time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4343
