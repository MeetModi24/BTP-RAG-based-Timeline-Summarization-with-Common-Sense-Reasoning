83. LETTER TO DR. GOPIGHAND BHARGAVA
WARDHA,
November 23, 1935
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
I have copies of your letters to Shankerlal. So far as I can
see, I have nothing to say on the arrangements about the
Lahore Bhandar. But in this matter Shankerlal's word has to
be final for he knows ever so much more than I do.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
13 MALL ROAD
LAHORE
From the original: Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
