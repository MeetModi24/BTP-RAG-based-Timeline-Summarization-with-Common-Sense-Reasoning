264. LETTER TO V. K. VILASINI
SABARMATI,
July 31, 1924
DEAR FRIEND,
This is the answer to your question.
Love Truth at all cost. This can be done only by loving
all that lives, and feeling for them.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRIMATI V. K. VILASINI
HILL PALACE
TRIPPOONITARA, COCHIN STATE
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
