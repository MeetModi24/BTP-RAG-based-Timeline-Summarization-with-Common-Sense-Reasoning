139. LETTER TO MURIEL LESTER
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
I have your letter. As there was nothing particular to write
to you about I have been silent because there is not one moment
to spare. But I am following your work from the letters you
send me. I am asking the Young India Manager to put you on
the complimentary list so that you may continue to get your
Young India whether you send the subscription or not and hence-
forth if you really cannot afford it do not pinch yourself and send
the subscription. You have a right to receive a free copy. You
will see what I have done in connection with the Viceregal letter.
I do not know what will come out of it. Write to Mohanlal your-
self asking him to send a complimentary copy to you.
MURIEL LESTER
KINGSLEY HALL, Bow. E/
From a photostat: S.N. 15677
