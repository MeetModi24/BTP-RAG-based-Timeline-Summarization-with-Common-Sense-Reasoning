146. LETTER TO VISHVANATH SINGH
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
I have no hesitation in saying that, if you propose to remarry
you should marry the girl-widow although she lived with her
husband for some time. I agree with you that it is inadvisable for
a widow who has children to remarry.
Yours sincerely,
VISHVANATH SINGH, ESQ.
12 HORI SARKAR LANE, BARABAZAR, CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 13908
