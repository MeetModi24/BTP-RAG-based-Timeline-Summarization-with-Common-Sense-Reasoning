74. LETTER TO G. D. CHATTERJEE
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 27, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,

article. I am inclined to deal with the portion quoted by you in
the pages of Young India.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI

LAHORE
