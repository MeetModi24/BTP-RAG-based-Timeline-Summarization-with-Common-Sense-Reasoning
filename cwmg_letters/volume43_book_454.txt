454. LETTER TO RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
YERAVDA CENTRAL PRISON,
June 18, 1930
DEAR RAMANANDA BABU,
Your letter of 25th May was given to me three days ago. I
thank you for all you say in it. I was glad to learn that my
brief account of Kavi Rajchandra was safely in your hands. I
was wondering whether it had reached you or not.
I have permission to receive The Modern Review among other
magazines. Will you please send me copies from the May
number? I asked the Young India office some time ago to send
me the magazine but somehow I have not received the copies. I
did not write directly.
I hope to see your contribution in Young India before long.
Please remember me to Pandit Benarsidas Chaturvedi.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
120- UPPER CIRCULAR ROAD
CALCUTTA
From the original: C.W. 9314. Courtesy: Sita Devi
