365. LETTER TO J. S. HOYLAND
December 15, 1933
MY DEAR HOYLAND,
You asked me some time ago, whether you could publish those
hymns. Of course you can, provided that you give me no credit
for the composition. You may say in the introduction that I had
prepared a rough translation for the use of English friends but
principally for Mira and that you had worked upon the translation.
I receive your postcards lovingly signed by so many friends. I
know that my work finds a place in your silent prayer meetings.
Love to you all.
BAPU

ham, and Jessie Hoyland
