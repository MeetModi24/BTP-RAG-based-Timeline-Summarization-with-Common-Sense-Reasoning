2. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
April 1, 1942
CHI. VANUDI1,
I have your letter. You must either get well there or come
over. Do not lose patience and courage. Make up your mind that
the ear trouble has to be cured.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5789. Also C.W. 3012. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
