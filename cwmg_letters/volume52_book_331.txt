331. LETTER TO SADASHIV RAO KARNAD
December 21, 1932
MY DEAR SADASHIV RAO,
I am glad you are keeping me well-posted with progress of
work. It was good to ignore the assault. We must be prepared
for such things and bear them without retaliation.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SADASHIV RAO KARNAD
C/O SJT. GOPALA MENON, CALICUT
From a microfilm: S.N. 18769
