172. LETTER TO HARILAL GANDHI
October 2, 1918
CHI. HARILAL,
I have a feeling that I am now going. I have very little time
left. The body is becoming weaker and weaker. I am not able
to eat anything. But my heart is at peace and so I do not find
the going at all difficult. I think whatever inheritance I am
leaving to you brothers is appropriate. What would you have
gained if I had left money? But the inheritance of character
which I am leaving to you is invaluable in my view. I wish
you to cherish it. Follow the path of religion. I have written
a letter to Devdas. Ask for a copy of it. If you think you can
apply that letter to yourself, do so.
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 11272. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
