142. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
ASHRAM SABARMATI,
January 8, 1930
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
How did you manage to fall ill ? You must have of course known that I
was opposed to your going to Lahore. You should for the present avoid going
after new adventures and even resist the temptation of seeing new places.
Your duty is to be content with what you can think and do without moving
about and to live according to a fixed schedule. When Devdas arrives there
and relieves you, you may certainly go to Vijapur.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PRABHUDAS GANDHI
JAMIA MILIA
KAROL BAGH
DELHI
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33006
