465. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
BORSAD,
Silence Day [July 27, 1931]
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. If you cannot see me for the present
give up the desire. You may freely write to me. If I get a
chance I will myself come that way. I shall consider myself a
little free when the matter of Bardoli and Borsad is settled. You
should not feel upset. Do not think too much.
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
