274. LETTER TO SATYAWATI
BARDOLI,
January 3, 194[2]
CHI. SATYAWATI,
I received your letter. I hope you are well. There is no need
to refuse B class. We should accept such amenities as the rules
permit. We may well refuse others.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
