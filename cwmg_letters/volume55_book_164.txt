164. LETTER TO P. N. RAJBHOJ
May 7, 1933
BHAI RAJBHOJ,
Ramji suggested that you two should go round among the
Harijans in the countryside. I do not like it. In my opinion
you should stay at the Ashram, learn whatever is worth learning
and immerse yourself completely in the Ashram life. Ramji is in
a very unhealthy state, he has no confidence in anyone, and does
not want to observe discipline.
What good will his touring
bring?
Talk him out of it and persuade him to do peacefully what
he can in the Ashram. You may do whatever is possible in this
matter.
If, in spite of all this, you are set on touring you may do so
but on your own, not as representing the Ashram. Speech-making
does not fall within Ashram activities at the moment.
Blessings from
BAPU
