409. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
June 30, 1937
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
I send herewith a postcard from Bhai Soni Balji Talsi. I
have not followed what he wants to say in it. I have replied to
him that he can get no money from here. He should not give
up his profession. If he gets any spare time after doing his work,
1 Vide pp. 362-.
2 Hindi Sahitya Sammelan; vide also p. 337.
he may give some of it to service and do what you ask him to
do.
Blessings from
BAPU
