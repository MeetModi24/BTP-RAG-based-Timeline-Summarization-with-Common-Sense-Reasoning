455. LETTER TO G. S. B. SARASWATI
January 2, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. As I have no personal knowledge of your
work, my recommendation to the Servants of Untouchables Society
can carry no greater weight than your own. I would, therefore,
advise you to approach the Society directly.
Its headquarters
are Delhi.
Yours sincerely,
SREE SWAMY G. S. B. SARASWATI
INDUKURPET, NELLORE
