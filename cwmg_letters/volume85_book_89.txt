89. LETTER TO GOVIND REDDY
POONA,
July 30, 1946
CHI. GOVIND REDDY,
I have your letter. I have not known you enough. Consult
Krishnachandraji and others and do whatever is considered
proper. I hope to reach Sevagram on the 6th or 7th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 10666
90
