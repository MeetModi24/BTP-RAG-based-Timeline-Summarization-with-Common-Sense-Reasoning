7. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 2, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
I devote all my time these days to writing letters. I am sen-
ding one article with this. Another which I have begun may go
tomorrow. If somebody goes there in the evening, I will send it
with him. Otherwise I shall myself bring it tomorrow morning.
If somebody brings the evening post and returns the same day he
may take the article with him. Doctor is there and will return in
the evening. He can bring the mail. He will be coming by car.
Perhaps I will send the article with the car.
Blessings from
BAPU
PS.
I am sending with this [copies of] my letters to Nariman.
Send a copy to Jawaharlal.
Send the following wire:
"Begum Ross Masood, Bhopal. My deepest sympathy in
your irreparable loss. Gandhi."
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11544
