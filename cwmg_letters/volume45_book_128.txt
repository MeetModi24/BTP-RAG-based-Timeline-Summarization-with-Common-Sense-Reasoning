128. LETTER TO KALAVATI TRIVEDI
January 17, 1931
CHI. KALAVATI,
Your letter. Pay no heed to anyone's criticism. Pay attention
to what the chief says. Shamalbhai and Gangabehn do not wish
to save you. Resolve this problem by talking it over with them.
No father has the right to forcibly send away his daughter to her
husband's house.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5275
