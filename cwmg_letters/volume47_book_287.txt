287. LETTER TO A YOUNG WOMAN
August 6, 1931
MY DEAR . . . ,
I have your letter. I must not write at length. You will
see my letter to . . . and perhaps my letter to . . . . You must not
be ruffled over this incident. Neither you nor . . . will feel upset
if you will appreciate a parent's affection and anxiety. I have
held . . . [your] family a model family in which the children
enjoy the fullest liberty tempered only by the peremptory demands
of parental affection. The present is an incident of that type.
But you will tell me frankly and fully how you two have felt.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Laburnum Rd. Bombay, till 8th at least.
From a photostat: S.N. 9632
