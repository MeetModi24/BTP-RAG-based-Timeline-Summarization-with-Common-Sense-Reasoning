308. LETTER TO PYARELAL
August 27, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
Do not be in a hurry to regain strength. If you can suck
the pomegranate seeds without swallowing the stones do so.
Stop all work until you are free from fever for 24 hours.
Blessings from
BAPU
