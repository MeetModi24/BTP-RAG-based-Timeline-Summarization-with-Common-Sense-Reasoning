591. LETTER TO DR. SUBODH MITRA
BOMBAY,
April 4, 1945
DEAR DR. MITRA,
Your letter. You should make a concrete proposal and
present it to the Trust Board through the Bengal Committee.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. SUBODH MITRA, M. D. & C.
3 CHOWRINGHEE TERRACE
112 GOKHALE ROAD
ELGIN ROAD P. O., CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
