417. LETTER TO H. S. SUHRAWARDY
[May 12/13, 1947]
I recognize the seriousness of the position in Bengal in the
matter of the partition. If you are absolutely sincere in your
professions and would disabuse me of all the suspicion against
you and if you would retain Bengal for the Bengalis—Hindus
or Mussalmans—intact by non-violent means I am quite wil-
ling to act as your honorary private secretary and live under
your roof till Hindus and Muslims begin to live as [ the] brothers
that they are.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C. W. 10562. Also My Days with Gandhi, pp. 232-
