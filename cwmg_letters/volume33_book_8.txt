8. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
BETTIAH,
Silence Day [January 24, 1927]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
Herewith letters from Balkrishna as also my reply to him.
Please post them to his address.
I have written to doctor about Rukhi.
It appears we shall need a waterworks at the Ashram. The
water will have to be lifted after digging deep into the river. Get
some expert to prepare an estimate. Contact Vallabhbhai.
Is not the water to blame for so much illness there? It is
no good if we can avoid illness only with the greatest care.
Please think it over. I am sure you looked very well after Mr.
and Mrs. Lawrence who visited the Ashram especially at my
instance. It appears Saklatwala too paid a visit. I hope you
have the latest letter from Mirabehn which I had asked Sub-
baiya to send over to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Tulsi Maher will now reach there soon; at the moment he is
travelling with me.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8693. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
