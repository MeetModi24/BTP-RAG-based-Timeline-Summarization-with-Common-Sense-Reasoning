411. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
February 15, 1933
MY DEAR THAKKAR BAPA,
I have your letter of the 9th instant, regarding Travancore
Christian Pulayas. If they are unapproachable I don't know
that we can exclude them from our service. After all, what is the
service? The admission to schools we may not refuse to Christian
unapproachables, or to wells. Again we may not refuse to share
the wells with them. The line has to be drawn at the temples of
course. Therefore you can say that Christian unapproachables
would be entitled to the same civic services as the other
unapproachables. When it comes to the question of giving indi-
vidual scholarships, there may be a difficulty. But I am not in-
clined to anticipate and provide against it now. When it comes,
we shall deal with it. All this, however, is my individual view
and you should take it for what it is worth.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
