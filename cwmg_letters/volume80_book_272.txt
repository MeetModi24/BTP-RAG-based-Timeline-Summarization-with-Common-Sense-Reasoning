272. LETTER TO RAMDAS DASAPPA
May 21, 1945
CHI. RAMDAS,
It is good that you wrote. Though there are slips, do not
worry about it. Improve your Hindustani as well as your
health. Do not hide anything. If you have the slightest pain,
let me know immediately. Come back whenever you want.
You have written about your birthday. May you live long and
render plenty of service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
