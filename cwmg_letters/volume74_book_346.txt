346. LETTER TO KOTWAL
August 7, 1941
BHAI KOTWAL,
I got your letter. If you have become impatient, you may
certainly go [to jail]. I certainly feel at present that those who
understand the secret of ahimsa should remain engrossed in con-
structive work. Ultimately everybody will get an opportunity of
going. Not "Ajmer"; it is certainly a slip. If you must go, go
to Ajmer and obtain the permission of the local committee.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3601
