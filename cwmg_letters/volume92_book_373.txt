373. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 24, 1930
CHI. SHANKERLAL,
It was a very happy coincidence that you went to Mussoorie
and were able to meet everyone. I have found Nandlal's book-
let about spinning quite satisfactory. There are of course mistakes
in it. I have pointed out some of them. I am still corresponding
with him. He may be able to write if he makes more effort. I
do not think Prabhudas can write. Krishna would be able to
write better than him. Prabhudas has not worked much with his
hands. Kaka (Kalelkar) would be arriving there within a month.
It is being arranged that he should make a major contribution
to it. The book must be made perfect.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32733
