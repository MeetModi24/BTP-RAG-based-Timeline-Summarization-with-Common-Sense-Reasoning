433. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
April 11, 1933
DAUGHTER RAIHANA,
I have your letter written on the train. Sarojini also has
sent one. She says that she misses you now that you have left.
What could not be accomplished in Poona must be achieved in
Mussoorie. Only after that can anything be considered. Greet-
ings from all of us to you all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: S.N. 9658
