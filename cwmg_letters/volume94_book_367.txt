367. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 14, 1945
CHI. SUSHILA,
Your expected letter has come. I abide by what I wrote about
Manu in the morning. She and the sisters should do what they think
fit.
It hurts me that Shyamlal's affairs are disorganized. You would
not have told him about it and I do not want to say that he should
use your name. I am watching the developments. Enough if you take
care of your first division. That would be the best education you could
give to others.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
