631. LETTER TO KUNVERJI K. PAREKH
[February 21, 1928]
CHI. KUNVERJI,
I have your letter. I am better. Chi. Rami and others will
leave along with Ramdas on Thursday morning. They will stop
at Rajkot and be with you in two or three days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9706
