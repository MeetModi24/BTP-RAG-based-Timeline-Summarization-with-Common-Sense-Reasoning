536. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
May 29, 1936
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have your letter. Both of you are passing through a severe
test. We ought to learn to live in a village, come wind, come
rain. The city-dwellers have their own risks to run and yet live
there, and so do the villagers. At present we have ceased to
belong to the town nor have we come to belong to the village and,
hanging midway between the two, we are in a miserable state.
Blessings to both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8603. Also C.W. 6993. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
