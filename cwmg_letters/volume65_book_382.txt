382. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
June 25, 1937
CHI. PRABHA,
I got your letter just now. Why do you worry? You will
have done your duty if you go on nursing to the best of your
ability. Our life is not in our hands. Tell Harshubabu on my
behalf that he should not worry about the body but meditate on
Rama. He will do as He wills. I will be satisfied if I have a
postcard from time to time.
338
Ba will come on the 30th. Shantabehn who was at Khedi
has arrived. She will stay here. After some time she will leave
for England.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3503
