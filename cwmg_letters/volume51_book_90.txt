90. LETTER TO SARALA DEVI CHOWDHARANI
September 16, 1932
I have your letter overflowing with love. That the children
also have joined you in expressing their love made the letter as
dear to me as a sacred gift. I took this step only when I was
convinced that it was my duty to do so. I have taken it in God's
name and for His work. I have faith in Him that He will pro-
tect my honour and, therefore, do not worry. All of you should
rejoice that one of your own family has got such a holy opportunity.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 28
