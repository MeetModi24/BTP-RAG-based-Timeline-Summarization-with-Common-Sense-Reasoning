461. LETTER TO G. V. GURJALE
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 24, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I would ask you to correspond with Sjt.
C. Rajagopalachariar, (Gandhi Ashram, Tiruchengodu) to whom
I have forwarded your letter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 1380
