484. LETTER TO SARASWATI
July 17, 1937
CHI. SARASWATI,
I have your letter. Look after Amtul Salaam well and learn
Hindi and Urdu from her. Will Paparamma never write to me?
Ba is improving. Do you ever write to Lakshmi? As soon as
you become a good student you will be permitted to come to
me.
Consult Amtul Salaam if you find it difficult to read and
follow this letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6161. Also C.W. 3434. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
