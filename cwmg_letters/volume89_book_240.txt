240. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
NEW DELHI,
September 22, 1947
CHI. VALJI,
I saw your three articles yesterday and revised them the
same day. Today I have sent them to Harijan. I liked all three
of them. Who will render them into Gujarati? If you send
[the translation] soon it can reach by this week. It would be
better if hereafter you send versions in all the three languages.
I shall be able to do but little. I hope you are keeping well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
