461. LETTER TO D. N. SARMAH
November 23, 1934
MY DEAR SARMAH,
I have your letter. It would be a great thing if you can
remove the indebtedness of sweeper families.
The judgment you have sent me in the matter of a sweeper
defendant is very interesting.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI D. N. SARMAH
SECRETARY, H. S. S.
JORHAT, ASSAM
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
