357. LETTER TO R. K. GOLIKERE
April 6, 1933
MY DEAR GOLIKERE,
I was certainly surprised to get your letter, and still more
so to read the contents. I thank you for thinking of me and
sending me your book. I shall certainly make time to read it.
I shall understand more fully than now the significance of your
new definition of astronomy.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. K. GOLIKERE
SARASWAT BUILDINGS
GAMDEVI, GRANT ROAD
[BOMBAY]
