338. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 24, 1939
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
Mr. Thompson has been here. He told me that you had
thought I was guilty of discourtesy in making the statement I
did on your declaration . I have not understood Mr. Thompson
but I told him, I would write to you and make amends if I was
guilty. Will you please enlighten me in the matter? I have no
doubt that the friendship that has sprung between us will bear
the strain of differences of opinion that may arise among us.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a microfilm: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
