230. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 15, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Herewith some letters. I do not know if you brought liquid
paraffin for Mirabehn yesterday. If you did not bring it, then
please remember to bring it. If you do not come, send it with
the person who comes. I hope you have asked Vyas about jamun
stones and written to Bombay to discontinue sending fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11606
