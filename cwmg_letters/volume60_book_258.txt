258. LETTER TO JUGATRAM DAVE
February 9, 1935
BHAI JUGATRAM,
Where is the need for me to send a message when Sardar
is the President. In spite of his being there, you ask for a message
and hope to get it! Then tell the Raniparaj that they are not depen-
dent on cities but cities are dependent on them.
If at all they
want to preserve their self-respect, they should themselves meet
all their requirements and be self-reliant. We rarely need the
things which we are not able to produce in the villages.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Harijanbandhu, 3-3-1935
