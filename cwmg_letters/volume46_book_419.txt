419. LETTER TO K. V. VEL
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 6, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of the 30th April last. My mess-
age is:
Let Japan study the non-violent movement of India and
help her.
Yours sincerely,
K. V. VEL, ESQ.
POST Box 9
AKASAKA, TOKYO (JAPAN)
From a photostat: S.N. 17238
