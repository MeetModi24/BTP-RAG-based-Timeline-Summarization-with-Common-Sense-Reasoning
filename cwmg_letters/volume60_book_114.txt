114. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
January 21, 1935
I see no harm in going. I think you should discuss every-
thing but making it clear that you have no mandate. You could
only convey the purport of the conversations to the Commit-
tee and its decision.
It should be strictly understood that
though you would meet as representatives, the talks must be
regarded as informal till they reach the stage of some concrete
proposal to be discussed by the respective committees.
At the
end of the conversations the agreed purport should be reduced
to writing and no report should be given to the Press of the
conversation.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
