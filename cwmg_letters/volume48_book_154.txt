154. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
October 26, 1931
CHI. MANI,
I continue to get your letters. Do not stop writing because I
cannot send any reply. Nowadays I have simply not the time to
write letters. Today I am utilizing a few minutes snatched out
of the Conference sessions.
I was delighted to learn that Dahyabhai had recovered. Con-
vey my good wishes to him and Yashoda.
Ask Lakshmidas and Manjubehn to write to me. I think
we shall be in England till at least one more mail steamer arrives.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, pp. 79-80
