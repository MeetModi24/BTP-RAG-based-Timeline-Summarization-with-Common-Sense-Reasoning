456. LETTER TO DECCAN EDUCATION SOCIETY
March 25, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
Who can fail to be enthused over the noble record of the
service rendered by the D. E. Society and the Fergusson College
to the cause of education? I wish the forthcoming function every
success.
This is all I have the time to send you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: C.W. 9735. Courtesy: Deccan Education Society, Poona
