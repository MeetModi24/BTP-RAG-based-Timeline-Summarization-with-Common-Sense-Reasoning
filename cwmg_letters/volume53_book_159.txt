159. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
January 22, 1933
CHI. SHARDA,
When a person wears khadi with an enlightened mind, he
does it for the sake of its simplicity, for the help it brings to the
starving people, for the purity it embodies. This knowledge calms
down our passions. Hence, I would say that khadi, when worn
with such awareness, helps brahmacharya. Do you get the point?
BAPU
