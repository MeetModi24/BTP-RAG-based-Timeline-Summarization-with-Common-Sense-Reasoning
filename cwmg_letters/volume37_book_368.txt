368. LETTER TO FULSINH
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 3, 1928
BHAI FULSINHJI,
I have your letter. I thank you for writing to me imme-
diately what you know about the monkeys. Have you made
sure that in both cases the nuisance can be stopped? Kindly
make still more detailed inquiry and let me know.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 227
