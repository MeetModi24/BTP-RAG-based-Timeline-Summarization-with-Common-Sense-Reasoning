42. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
[May 4, 1940]
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. I see no flaw in it. I shall not enter
into an argument. May your noble wish be fulfilled.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: Rameshwari Nehru Papers. Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
