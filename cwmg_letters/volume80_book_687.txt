687. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
'MANOR VILLE', SIMLA WEST,
July 9, 1945
CHI. NARAHARI,
It is not impossible for you to be able to manage the Ashram.
Only, you should feel interested in the work and have confidence
in yourself. There are plenty of other things I can write about,
but where is the time? Today is a silence day and the Commit-
tee is not meeting where I am staying.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9134
