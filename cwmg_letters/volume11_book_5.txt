5. LETTER TO L. W. RITCH
[CAPE TOWN,]
Tuesday [April 4, 1911]
MY DEAR RITCH,
I have now got Evir's cert[ificate]. Saw Lane.
He thinks
it will be well but the Bill may not come up for two weeks.
He suggests my not waiting. I am now trying to see all the
members I can. Have wired today and await some instructions.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the original in Gandhiji's hand: S.N. 5408
