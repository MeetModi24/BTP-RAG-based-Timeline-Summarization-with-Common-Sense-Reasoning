6. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
DELHI,
March 15, 1937
CHI. AMRITLAL,
We have to send to Radhakrishna one pukka maund of
jaggery before 25th April. It should be securely packed and should
be of good quality, i.e., such as will not become too soft. Jaggery
which is likely to become so, we ourselves should use. Send the
quantity to him in instalments as and when it is ready.
Take care of your health in whatever you do.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10728
