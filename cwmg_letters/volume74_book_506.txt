506. LETTER TO G. RAMACHANDRA RAO
September 11, 1941
DEAR FRIEND,
Atheism is a denial of self. No one has succeeded in its
propagation. Such success as you have attained is due to your
sincere work among the people round you. I am sorry I cannot
invite you to come here. I have no time to spare for talks.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
An Atheist with Gandhi, p. 26
