282. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEVAGRAM,
October 23, 1944
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Swami has a letter from Dilip from which I learn that
your health has again had a set-back. How is it that you have
contracted influenza, etc.? I must have the details. If you cannot
write Dilip should. I am tied up with work. I hope Dilip is
calm.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
