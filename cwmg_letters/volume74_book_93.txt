93. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
May 17, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Who reprimanded you? I do not even remember if anybody
told me anything against you. If it had been so I would have
at least told you about it. Remember the three monkeys. Do not
lend your ears to such idle talk. Tell yourself 'If there is any
complaint Bapu would tell me' and ignore the complainants. Why
do you feel hurt?
If you have work with me come any number of times, other-
wise don't come at all.
Is it all right now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4389
