191. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
February 2, 1931
CHI. VASUMATI,
How fortunate you are. You got beaten and were also award-
ed a jail sentence. All of you have upheld the honour of the Ashram
and brought glory to your sex. All people do not yet see the full
significance of this incident. Its real value will be appreciated
after some time. I hope you did not even feel angry with the person
who beat you. All of them are like our brothers. If we do not feel
angry, they are bound to change some day.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9320
