200. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
SEVAGRAM,
October 5, 1944
DEAR SISTER,
I am quite sure that I sent from Panchgani a reply to your
letter. I do not keep copies of such letters. However I do
remember one thing. Do not rush to break free of your bonds.
It is your duty to look after Father.
Hope you are in good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 8006. Also C.W. 3103. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
1 The source has 'as'.
2 Gandhiji had written to the addressee from Poona on June 16; vide
Vol. LXXVII, p. 316.
