435. LETTER TO GLADYS OWEN
PATNA,
May 16, 1947
DEAR GLADYS,
I am glad that you went to Lahore and did what was
possible. I am sending you a copy of my reply. I think it is
wrong for friends to come to India to learn non-violence, whe-
ther from me or anybody else. Don't you?
I am plodding. The work is difficult but faith to be faith
has to overcome mountains. We have to try to cultivate such
faith.
Love.
BAPU
MISS GLADYS OWEN
NUR MANZIL
LAL BAGH
LUCKNOW
From a photostat: G. N. 6200
