204. LETTER TO RANI VIDYAVATI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 9, 1937
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. I have instructed the Charkha Sangh
office to send the charkha, the spindle, etc., by railway freight.
You will get the things in a few days.
I hope you are in good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
RANI VIDYAVATI
BARUA KOTHI
SANDILA
DISTRICT HARDOI (U. P.)
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
