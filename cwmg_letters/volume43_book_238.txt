238. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
DANDI,
April 12, 1930
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
Your wire. I was out visiting the villages in the morning
and had instructed Pyarelal to send you a reply telegram but he
forgot. I will not send one now at double rates. See my letter
of yesterday and decide on the right course. Come here if you
wish to or proceed to Delhi if that seems proper.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2380
1 Wife of Dr. Jivraj Mehta, physician and Congress leader of Bombay
