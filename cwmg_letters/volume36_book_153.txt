153. LETTER TO MRS. JOSEPH A. BRAUN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 21, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
It was thoughtful of you and the members of your club to
send me through Mrs. Sharman a cheque for Rs. 70. I value the
gift for the heart that prompted it. I am utilizing the same for
supplying the needs of one who had devoted himself to propa-
gate the message of the spinning-wheel.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. JOSEPH A. BRAUN
RFD 3
BIRMINGHAM
MICHIGAN, U.S.A.
From a photostat: S.N. 14268
