90. LETTER TO DR. M. A. ANSARI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 5, 1926
DEAR DR. ANSARI,
I have your telegram about your immediately sailing for
England. It came upon me as a surprise as I knew nothing of
your impending visit nor am I any better off now. However,
on your return there will be sufficient still left of me as your
patient for you to examine and tamper with.
But a telegram today says you are to be one of the Hindu-
Moslem Committee now to be appointed. Does it mean that
your departure is postponed or that the Committee is to carry
on its work after your return? I have responded to Pandit
Motilalji's imperative call but I am diffident about our ability
to do anything.
Whenever you go my good wishes attend you. I hope Begum
Ansari is much better now. I wonder how Hakimji is faring.
Yours sincerely,
DR. M. A. ANSARI
DELHI
