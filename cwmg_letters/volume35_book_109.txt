109. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
October 21, 1927
CHI. MIRA,
I get your letters regularly. I do not prohibit you from
writing as many letters as you like. I simply said that I should be
satisfied so long as you gave me one per week. I should be
anxious if I did not get even one. I should welcome one every
day if you felt like sending one.
I wonder if you do not find moving about in the sun rather
trying. Do you wear a sunshade? You must not hesitate to use
a hat if you need one.
Though I take the place of mother or rather because I take
that privileged place the natural mother should be more to you
than ever before. My connection with you to be pure must
strengthen all natural affections. Only, they should become purer
and lose all selfish taint.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5288. Courtesy: Mirabehn
