453. LETTER TO NARGIS CAPTAIN
April 24, 1934
Your letter.
I must not exchange my rupees for one-anna
pieces. Satyagraha is not at a discount.
It commands for me a
premium. Therefore the true ones are reserved. On due occasion
they will give a good account of themselves. Therefore I want
you to rejoice over my decision.
Love.
SHRI NARGISBEHN CAPTAIN
COMARA HALL
PANCHGANI
Via POONA
From a copy: C.W. 7953. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
