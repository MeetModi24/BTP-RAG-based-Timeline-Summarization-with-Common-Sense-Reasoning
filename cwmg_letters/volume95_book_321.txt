321. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
BHAI KRISHNACHANDRA,
Among all his activities, Tyagiji showed special preference for
brahmacharya and Harijans. How nice it would be if in this [rainy]
season all the students and others of the Gurukul could be more firm
on these two issues.
Blessings from
BAPU
