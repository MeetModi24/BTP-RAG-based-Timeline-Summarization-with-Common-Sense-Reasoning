543. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
January 8, 1933
BOYS AND GIRLS,
I got your letter. One can say that there is a rush for ad-
mission. Try to acquire the art of learning even if you have
no teacher. Increase your knowledge whenever you can learn
it yourself. After thinking write to me what you can all study
by yourselves and what each boy or girl wants to study.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
