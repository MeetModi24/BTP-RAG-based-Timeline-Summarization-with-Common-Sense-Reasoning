68. LETTER TO RAJKISHORI MEHROTRA
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 28, 1927
CHI. RAJKISHORI,
I wrote two letters to you but got no reply. Now that I have
your letter, I write this. Let me know how you are observing
your vow. And how are the boys? How is your health? What
have you been reading these days? Letters can be received at this
address up to 10th July, after which they should be addressed to
the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6659. Courtesy: Parashuram Mehrotra
