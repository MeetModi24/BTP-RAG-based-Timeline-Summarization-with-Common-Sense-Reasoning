724. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
October 22, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
How is it that Babudi has again fallen ill there? Does she
take hip-baths? Does she exert herself? What does she eat?
Does she use a mosquito-net? The money was to be given
according to the usual rule. My impression is that it has been
given to Jajuji.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.C. 128
