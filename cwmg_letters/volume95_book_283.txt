283. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL G. BANKER
ASHRAM,
Bhadrapad Vad 7
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
Sister told me about the censorious remarks. Censure is
nothing new to you. It would be cowardice on your part to leave
Ahmedabad or sister's house from fear of criticism. There is not the
slightest reason to do such a thing. Who would ever take note of such
criticism? What is so surprising about people talking? Our duty is to
