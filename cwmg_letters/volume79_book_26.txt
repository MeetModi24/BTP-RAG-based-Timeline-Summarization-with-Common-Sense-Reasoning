26. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
January 9, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
I should be no cause of worry. I am in God's hands and
that should be more than enough. I am daily gaining ground.
So Beryl is fixed up. She has sent me a handwritten book
"Khalil Gibran" and handspun.
I have not written hitherto. If you think I now can, you
may hand her the enclosed.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Don't worry about yourself either, come what will. I envy
you your snow.
This is supposed to be the best part in Simla. So said Valji
Desai.
RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
SIMLA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
