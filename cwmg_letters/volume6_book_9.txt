9. LETTER TO S. M. MANGA
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 25, 1906
DEAR MR. MANGA,
Will you please give me a call on Monday between nine and half
past in the morning, for all the other days I think I shall be engaged.
Yours truly,
S. M. MANGA, ESQ.
106, BARON'S COURT ROAD
WEST KENSINGTON
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4392
