534. LETTER TO AVADHESH DUTT AVASTHI
August 26, 1935
CHI. AVADHESH,
To follow the immediate dharma as it presents itself can have
nothing but good consequences.
People have no right to remam in an institution when they
knowingly violate its rules.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI AVADHESH DUTT AVASTHI
VILLAGE RAMPURVA, POST BADWAPUR
DIST. BAHRAICH, U.P.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3218
