462. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
June 27, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
You must now be getting my letters regularly. You should
assume that as long as I am permitted to write from here I will
regularly write to you. My weight is satisfactory. It is a little above
103, and so there is no cause for worry. It is indeed welcome news
that Father's health is improving. You have lost much weight.
How is that?
443
Take special care and improve your health. You should take
milk or curds and fruit, and also have exercise. A letter for Jaya-
prakash is enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3360
