413. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
BIRLA HOUSE,
MOUNT PLEASANT ROAD, BOMBAY,
July 4, 1939
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter. I am writing to Mary Barr.
The note you gave me about khadi is still lying with me. I
shall dispose of it at leisure. Is anything special going on there?
A letter from Manju is enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
We are leaving for the Frontier Province tomorrow. Ba will
probably accompany me.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8557. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
