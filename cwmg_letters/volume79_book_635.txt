635. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
April 12, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Twenty-five rupees for the distilled water, and that to be
paid by the hospital! We certainly can't take that from Hari-
ichchha. There appears to be some misunderstanding. Please ex-
plain. I hope you are well. Yes, Sushilabehn was injured, but
no bones were broken. There is no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
MUNNALAL
SEVAGRAM ASHRAM
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
