179. LETTER TO MAHESH DATT MISHRA
NEW DELHI,
June 26/27, 1947
CHI. MAHESH,
I have your letter. Do what service you can from there.
Take care of your health.
My programme has become quite uncertain. I cannot say
where I shall be. Now you have to look after things there.
It is good if you can write something for Harijan.
Whenever you want to send anything you have written,
do please send it. Nothing is certain about Kashmir.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 6722. Also C. W. 4464. Courtesy:
Mahesh Datt Mishra
