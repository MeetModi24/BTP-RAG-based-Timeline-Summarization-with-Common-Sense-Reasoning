158. LETTER TO JANAKDHARI PRASAD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
October 25, 1933
DEAR JANAKDHARI BABU,
I have your sorrowful letter. You must not give way to grief
as you have done. I hope you received my letter written to you
on 15th inst. There need be no apology for writing to me. Do
please write as often as you wish. You have every right to have
my opinion and advice. If you can accept the advice I have given
in my previous letter, it will solve all your difficulties.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 55
