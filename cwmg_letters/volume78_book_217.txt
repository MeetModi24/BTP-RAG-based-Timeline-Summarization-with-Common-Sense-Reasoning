217. LETTER TO KUNDAR DIWAN
October 11, 1944
BHAI KUNDAR,
The spinning-wheel is the symbol of non-violence for those
who project non-violence in the spinning-wheel. What I said
was only to explain this. There should be no difficulty in under-
standing this.
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
