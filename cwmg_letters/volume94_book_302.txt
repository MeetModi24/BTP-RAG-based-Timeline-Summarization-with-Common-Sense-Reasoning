302. LETTER TO HARIBHAU PHATAK
SEVAGRAM, "VIA" WARDHA,
November 1, 1944
DEAR SHRI PHATAK,
Shri A. V. Thakkar in a letter dated 16.10.1944 writes to me as follows:
"Bapu had transferred Rs. 2,000/- to Shri Haribhau Phatak for Harijan work.
But no work seems to have been done so far. A lot of correspondence has been
passing between Shri Phatak and Shri Barve of Dhulia, the President of the
Maharashtra Sangh. The main point of contention seems to be that Shri Phatak would
not agree to realize Rs. 3/- or Re. 1/- from the Members of the Committee as required
by the Constitution of the Sangh."
Gandhiji has desired me to draw your attention to it. A line in reply will oblige.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI HARIBHAU PHATAK
POONA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
