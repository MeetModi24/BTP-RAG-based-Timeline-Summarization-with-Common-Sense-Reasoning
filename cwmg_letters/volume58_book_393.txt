393. LETTER TO BIRENDRA NATH GUHA
August 24, 1934
MY DEAR BIRENDRA,
I was glad to have your letter with a copy of the manifesto.
You should continue to send me such information as you may
think necessary for me to possess.
I doubt not that Kshitish-
babu will, in the end, see what to me is the obvious truth.
SHRI BIRENDRA NATH GUHA
VIDYASHRAM
B-76 COLLEGE STREET MARKET
CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
