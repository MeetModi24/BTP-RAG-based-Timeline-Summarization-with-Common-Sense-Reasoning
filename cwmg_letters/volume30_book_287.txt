287. LETTER TO RAKHAL CHANDRA MAITY
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 6, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. What you say about the classification as
to dining at the Sadakat Ashram surprises me. I am sending your
letter to Sjt. Rajendra Babu asking him to reply to it.
I agree with you that the prayer should be short, intelligible
and that it should proceed from the heart. It should be addressed
to the Supreme God and in a college or any such institution, it
should be a prayer common to all.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. RAKHAL CHANDRA MAITY
SADAKAT ASHRAM
DIGHAGHAT P.O.
PATNA
