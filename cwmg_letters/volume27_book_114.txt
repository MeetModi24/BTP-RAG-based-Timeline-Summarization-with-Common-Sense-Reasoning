114. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
Jeth Sud 9 [June 6, 1925]
CHI. NARANDAS1,
No news from you. I want you to send me every month your
report on khadi work. It will be better still, if you send it every
fortnight.
It seems you have not been able to make an inquiry about
Shri. . . . Now do so quickly. There are allegations of mis-
appropriation and adultery against him. How can you possibly
make an inquiry in the matter of the allegation of adultery?
With regard to this I would only ask you to go to . . . with your
eyes open. Examine the accounts minutely, so that if they are
found to be kept honestly I can unhesitatingly declare them to
be so.
You know that I have already written in Navajivan about the
allegations made against him.
I hope you all are keeping well.
My regards to Khushalbhai and Devbhabhi .
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 6292. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
