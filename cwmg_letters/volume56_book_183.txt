183. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 30, 1933
Have you any share in the move to get me to light Vithal-
bhai's funeral pyre? You must have read my note. If anybody
hopes that I should attend, please discourage him if you can.
If they put pressure on me, I shall be able to cope with it, but
it will be better if they don't do that. I can take no interest in
the outside activities; mentally I am in jail. I will somehow go
through the Harijan tour. I hardly even think about other things.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 139
1 Addressee's cousin
56-11
