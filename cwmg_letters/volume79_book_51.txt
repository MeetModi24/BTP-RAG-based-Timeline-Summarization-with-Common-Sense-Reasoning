51. LETTER TO BARBARA
SEVAGRAM,
January 14, 1945
DEAR BARBARA,
Since you undertake to remain here, at least meanwhile,
there should be no difficulty in the Nayakums going for a change.
I am with you.
Love.
BAPU
BARBARA
C/O NAYAKUMJI
SEVAGRAM
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
