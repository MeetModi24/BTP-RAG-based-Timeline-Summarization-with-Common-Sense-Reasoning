272. LETTER TO ABDUL GHAFFAR KHAN
[July 7, 1947]
DEAR BADSHAH,
No news from you. I hope you had my long letter and
that you have acted up to it. Your and my honour is involved
in strict adherence to non-violence on our part in thought, word
and deed. No news up to now (9.30) in the papers.
Love.
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase, Vol. II, pp. 279-80
