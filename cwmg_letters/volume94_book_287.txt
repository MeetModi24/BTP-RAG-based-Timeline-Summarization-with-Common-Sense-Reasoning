287. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
[October 9, 1944]
CHI. MRIDU,
After reading Munshi's letter put it in an envelope and return it.
Read Jehangirji's letter to Bapa. Accept any date that is ultimately
decided if you approve of the letter. Speak to Ambalalbhai 2 about this
letter. In that case, I would not be required to write to anyone else.
If Mahmud 3 is definitely coming, inform me telegraphically and let
me know in detail about his requirements. We shall put him up only
at Bajajwadi. We shall not trouble him to come to Sevagram. I shall
go to Bajajwadi on that day. If you have understood my argument,
discuss it with Bapa and Mangaldas or if you would rather keep quiet,
then do so. In my view the question is important. Today there is no
one who will hear me in the matter.
Don't spoil your health by running about too much.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11119. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
