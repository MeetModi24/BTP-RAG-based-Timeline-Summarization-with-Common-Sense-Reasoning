1. LETTER TO RAJARAM R. BHOLE
DELHI,
January 16, 1935
MY DEAR RAJARAM,
I have your letter. Seeing that you have put on weight I
would advise you not to leave the Sanatorium if the authori-
ties still want to continue the treatment and do not mind your
prolonged stay. It is no use your going to Poona and becoming
worse. He who passes time in rendering himself fitter for the
service, which he cannot do otherwise, is also doing service.
Therefore, staying in Mysore need not be regarded as waste of
time.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RAJARAM BHOLE
P. K. T. SANATORIUM
VONTIKOPPAL
MYSORE
From the original: R R. Bhole Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
