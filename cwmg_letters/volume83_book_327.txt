327. LETTER TO KASHIBEHN GANDHI
POONA,
March 18, 1946
CHI. KASHI1,
How are you now? You should do whatever you can from
where you are. Both of you can give lessons to Gajaraj. It
must be pretty hot there. It is boiling hot here. The nights and
mornings are pleasant.
Blessings to both from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 9232. Courtesy : Chhaganlal
Gandhi
