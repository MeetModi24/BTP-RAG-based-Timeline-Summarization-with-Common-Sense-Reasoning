319. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
SEGAON,
October 19, 1939
CHI. KANAIYO,
Your birthday note is lying in front of me. May you rise
higher every day and grow stronger in body and mind. May your
angularities get smoothed out in the course of time.
Blessings from
BAPU
