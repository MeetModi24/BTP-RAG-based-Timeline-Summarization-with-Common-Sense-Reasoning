499. LETTER TO M. G. BHAVE
SEVAGRAM,
September 9, 1941
DEAR BHAVEJI1,
Your letter. I retain the same opinion as before. I am ut-
terly opposed to communalism in everything but much more so
in sport.
You may make what use you like of this opinion. Please
do not ask me to do anything more. I have no time.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
