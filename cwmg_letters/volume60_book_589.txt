589. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
April 15, 1935
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
The right hand is aching, but there is no time now to write
with the left. I got the letters of you both. Don't expect Ramdas
now. Do the best you can and go ahead. Kishorelal has gone on
a tour of Poona and other places. Tara has got whooping cough,
which is likely to be quite prolonged.
Harilal is still with me. He wants to marry again. Let us see
what happens. Ramdas is trying hard for something in Bombay,
but has found nothing suitable as yet. Kanti is with me of course.
I am in fine health, and so is Ba.
Blessings from
BAPU
