366. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
August 29, 1926
DEAR SATIS BABU,
Here is another letter of the type I sent you before.
Mr. Bharucha has told me all about you. He wanted me to
send you some money. I wish I could do so. But it is not
possible.
Mr. Birla has offered to loan Rs. 70,000 for one year certainly
without interest and without security to the Association. But, I
feel that unless we have a reserve in the Association coffers or at
least that amount, we must not make use of the loan. We con-
sidered the matter yesterday in the Council and others also agreed.
Have you any opinion about this?
I hope both you and Hemaprabha Devi are doing quite all
right.
Yours,
SJT. SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
170, BOW BAZAAR STREET
CALCUTTA
