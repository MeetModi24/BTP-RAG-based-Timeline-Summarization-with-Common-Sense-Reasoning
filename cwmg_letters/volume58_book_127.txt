127. LETTER TO NANABHAI I. MASHRUWALA
June 25, 1934
BHAI NANABHAI,
I had received your letter. I selected 'Govind' from the
list of names which you had sent. But I cannot press for its ac-
ceptance. I have written to Manilal and Sushila and sent the
suggestion to them.
I would be happy t o hear that you are improving.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6691. Also C.W. 4336. Courtesy:
Kanubhai N. Mashruwala
