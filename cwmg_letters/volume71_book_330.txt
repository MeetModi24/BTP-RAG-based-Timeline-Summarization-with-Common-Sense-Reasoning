330. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
ON THE TRAIN,
March 13, 1940
BA,
I did not at all like leaving you. Follow Sushila's instruc-
tions as regards your medicine and food. I slept a great deal on
the way. There wasn't much disturbance at the stations. There
are about twelve girls with me from the Mahila Ashram. Do not
worry about me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXIV
