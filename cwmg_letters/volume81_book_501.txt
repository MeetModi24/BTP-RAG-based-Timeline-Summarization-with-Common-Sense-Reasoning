501. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
September 27, 1945
CHI. SITA,
You write in bold and distinct characters and therefore they
look beautiful. Never mind if writing thus takes more time.
Ultimately that won't take much time.
You must not fail. Do come to me after your examination
is over. You must not let the examination weigh on your mind.
Why should it when you have worked hard?
Arun and Ila are quite cheerful. Arun is still quiet but
Ila makes up for it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4956
