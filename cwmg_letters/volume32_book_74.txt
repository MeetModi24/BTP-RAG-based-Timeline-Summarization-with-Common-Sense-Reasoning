74. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
Wednesday [November 24, 1926]
BHAISHRI DEVCHANDBHAI,
I have your letter. There will be accommodation for as
many as will come. We shall consider the cases of Hakim Saheb,
Dr. Ansari and others. If you arrive on Monday evening, it will
be all right.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5710
