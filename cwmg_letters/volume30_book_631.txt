631. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 4, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It was certainly very good and generous
on the part of Mr. Kelkar to offer the use of the Kesari and the
Mahratta presses. You will please ask someone to pass on to me
any appeal that you may make for funds and I shall try to do
what I can as a matter of simple duty.
Yours sincerely,
RT. HON. V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
POONA CITY
From a photostat: S.N. 12052
