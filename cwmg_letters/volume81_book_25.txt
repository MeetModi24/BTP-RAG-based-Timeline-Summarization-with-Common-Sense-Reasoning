25. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
July 22, 1945
CHI. MANUDI,
Do you believe that Jaisukhlal refused to send you money
because he distrusts you? If you think so, you are doing a great
injustice to your father. I have told Jaisukhlal that he should
not send money directly to you, and that is why he mentioned
my name. If you want money, write to me. But where is the
need? I can write to Dr. Mehta. If you do not stay there in
peace and keep on saying that you wish to run away, it will
pain me. How do you know that Rs. 10,000 are to be set
[apart] for you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M . M . U . / I I I
