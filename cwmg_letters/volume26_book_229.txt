229. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Chaitra Sud 2 [March 26, 1925]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
Here is Hakim Saheb's telegram. Can you send me Rs. 25,000
now? If you can, will you send it to Hakim Saheb's address
or to Jamnalalji's firm in Bombay? If it could be credited at
Delhi some commission might perhaps be saved. I shall be at
the Ashram till the 1st of April; after that I shall be proceeding
to Kathiawar again. I have got to reach Faridpur on May 2.
I hope your wife is quite well.
I intend to take up the work of cow-protection on my own
lines, or rather I will have to take it up. I expect to receive
help in this work from all of you brothers. I have agreed to
take up this work after much hesitation.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6108. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
