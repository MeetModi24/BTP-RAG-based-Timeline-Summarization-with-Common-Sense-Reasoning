18. LETTER TO JETHALAL G. SAMPAT
March 6, 1929
BHAISHRI JETHALAL,
Your letter about the khadi scheme is lying before me. I
am thinking about it on board the steamer. I see that one who
draws up a good scheme should himself be ready to implement
it. Where have we the workers? Why don't you yourself write
the pamphlet, and yourself implement the scheme? I will of
course think over it. But meanwhile you also should consider it.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9844. Courtesy: Narayan Jethalal Sampat
