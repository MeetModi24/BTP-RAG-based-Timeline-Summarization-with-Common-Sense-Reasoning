260. LETTER TO SIR WILLIAM VINCENT
April 30, 1918
DEAR SIR WILLIAM VINCENT,
I ruffled you on Sunday. But I really came to further the
object for which you have overworked yourself. I merely came to
tell you that the release of the Brothers Ali was calculated to en-
courage recruiting. If I did not believe this, it would have been
sinful for me to expect you to give me a single minute of your
time.
You asked me whether I had brought the authorities a single
recruit. I suggest to you that it was not a fair question and one
might truly serve the Empire and yet not bring a single recruit.
I hope you will not resent this letter, but accept it as an
honest explanation of a visit which you so hastily misunderstood.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
