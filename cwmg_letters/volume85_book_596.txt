596. LETTER TO KANTILAL N. KAMDAR
NEW DELHI,
October 15, 1946
BHAI KANTILAL,
I referred your letter to a specialist in medicine. He says
what you say is right but that there are so many scientific
hitches involved that it is better not to go through the process.
I therefore do not want to involve myself in the matter.
M. K. GANDHI
KANTILAL NAGARDAS KAMDAR
G. S. MEDICAL COLLEGE
PAREL, BOMBAY 12
