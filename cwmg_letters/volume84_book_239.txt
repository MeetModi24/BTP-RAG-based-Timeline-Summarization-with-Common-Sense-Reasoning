239. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
NEW DELHI,
May 23, 1946
CHI. KANCHAN,
Munnalal is now prepared to have a separate establishment
and live with you. What is your wish now? Would you like
to start as soon as possible or are you content that you have the
promise? If you keep good health there and the work is going on
well you should not mind waiting a little longer. Don't think of
what I would like but state your own independent view.
I am not writing separately to Zohra. I hope she is all
right. Look after the women there carefully.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8257. Also C. W. 6981. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
