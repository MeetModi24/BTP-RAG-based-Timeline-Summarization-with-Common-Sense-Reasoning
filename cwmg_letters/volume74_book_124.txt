124. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
[May 26, 1941]
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I got your postcard after a long time. Do not worry about the
loss of a few pounds if otherwise you keep good health. I received
money sent by Jugatrambhai. I expect Rami is with you. The
children must be fine. Ba arrived the day before yesterday. She is
all right. Devdas, Ramdas and Lakshmi also have come. Lakshmi
and the children are going to Madras. Devdas will leave for Simla
tomorrow. Ramdas will go to Ahmedabad.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KUNVARJI KHETSI PAREKH
ZANDU PHARMACY
DADAR, BOMBAY 14
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9748. Also C.W. 728. Courtesy:
Navajivan Trust
