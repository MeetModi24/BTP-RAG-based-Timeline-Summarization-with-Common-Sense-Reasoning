265. LETTER TO MILLIE GRAHAM POLAK
May 26 [1917]
MY DEAR MILLIE,
This illness of Henry's must have been the greatest trial of his
life. He need not allow the illness to upset his belief in a guiding
providence. Nala is said to have been bitten by a cobra. The cobra
when asked by Nala, told him that he was bitten so as to transform
his appearance and so enable him successfully to hide his identity during
his banishment. I do believe that our illnesses often come to us as
disciplinary measures.
After a long silence I received a letter from Natesan today in
which he says that he has been away in Ooty, etc. This may account
for his silence. But he is, I know, very careless about answering letters.
I am not excusing him. I am describing his limitations.
You will be glad to read the enclosed letter. I do hope that Kothagiri
is near enough for Ceilia to meet Miss Frering. She is a most kind-
hearted girl. Do write to her if you feel inclined. Miss Esther Frering,
Spring Cottage, Kothagiri. Please send the letter to Ahmedabad after
you have done with it.
With love,
Yours,
BHAI
