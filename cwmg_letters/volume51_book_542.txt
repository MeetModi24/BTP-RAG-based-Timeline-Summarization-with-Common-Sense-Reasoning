542. LETTER TO BHIKHIBEHN
November 2, 1932
CHI. BHIKHIBEHN,
I am glad you wrote to me. If you learnt reading and
writing in the Ashram itself, then you have made very good pro-
gress. And that is as it should be. Still if you are a little more
careful, your handwriting, as also your Gujarati, will improve
further.
Tell me something more about yourself when you write
again. What village do you belong to? Are your parents alive?
What is your husband?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1743
