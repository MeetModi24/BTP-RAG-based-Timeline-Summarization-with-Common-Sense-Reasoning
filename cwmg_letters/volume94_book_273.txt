273. LETTER TO TARADEVI NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM,
August 25, 1944
DEAR SISTER,
I know you want to have Sushila with you for a good many days.
Sushila was going there direct. You may as well say that I have made
her get down on the way. Her luggage too is lying at Bajajwadi. I
need Sushila here. Hence I wish you would let her remain here. What
I would like most is all the three of you coming here. Then I shall
be able to see you, Pyarelal will be able to see you, and it will be
convenient in every way. If you really cannot come, then I shall send
Sushila when I conveniently can. Remember that my days are
numbered. Hence I would again urge you to come.
Blessings to the three of you,
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
