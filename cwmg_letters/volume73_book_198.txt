198. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 16, 1940
CHI. ANAND,
Do work, taking care of your health. Amtussalaam gets full
help from both of you. Really, God is the sole protector for her
and all of us.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India, and
Anand T. Hingorani
