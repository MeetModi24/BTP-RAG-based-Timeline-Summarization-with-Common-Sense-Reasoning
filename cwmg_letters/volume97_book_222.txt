222. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
December 25, 1932
CHI. NIMU,
At long last, I got a letter from you. Kanu's name can certainly be
changed. Let me have the name you would like me to give him, and I will
do accordingly. I do not insist on any particular name. Nor does Ramdas.
Ba is here. She can see me from time to time.
BAPU
