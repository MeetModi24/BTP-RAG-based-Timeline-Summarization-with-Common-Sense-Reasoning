128. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 9, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your letter.
I am sorry to have to report to you that the apples were 19
short. They were weighed at the station. The weight showed 2½ seers
less. The Station Master won't issue the certificate; though the
weighing was in his presence he refused to give the certificate. You
should lodge a complaint on your side. I am doing likewise here.
I am well. I wish you could say the same of yourself.
No more possible today.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4115. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7424
