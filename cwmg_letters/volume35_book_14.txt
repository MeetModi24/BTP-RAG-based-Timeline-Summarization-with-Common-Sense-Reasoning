14. LETTER TO PRAGJI DESAI
[Before September 22, 1927]
BHAISHRI PRAGJI,
I got your letter. You have been giving good help to
Sastriji. Whatever the likely decision in your case, you need not
feel worried. I am sure Sastriji must be doing something on his
own to help you. Now that you have joined Indian Opinion, you
must have given up the idea of earning money there. Am I right?
How is Medh faring? How is your health? Whatever happens,
do not be tempted by the luxuries there, and keep away from
untruth, secrecy and so on.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5042
