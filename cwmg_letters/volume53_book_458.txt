458. LETTER TO VIMALA JOSHI
February 18, 1933
CHI. VIMU,
I got your letter, and also the peahen sent by you. But
what is the use of a peahen who does not speak at all? And
your handwriting is no better than your peahen, and, moreover,
the letter is written in pencil. This will not do.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5309
