85. LETTER TO MESSRS LIENGIER & CO.
MOTIHARI (BIHAR),
January 21,1918
MESSRS LIENGIER & CO.
MADURA
The method that I have adopted for reinstating those who
have left off weaving is to supply them with yarn, at the lowest
market rates, to buy out all the cloth they may manufacture, for
cash, at the highest market rates, the yarn to be paid for in instal-
ments, without interest, convenient to the weaver. This has en-
abled them to earn at the rate of about Rs. 17 per month. These
weavers do not give their whole time to weaving and their manu-
facture is confined to the coarsest cloth. They do not want to as-
pire higher and what they earn is enough for their wants. But I
know that a clever weaver manufacturing finer counts, with per-
haps a little pattern-work, can make twenty-five rupees per month.
Every weaver lost to the country is, in my opinion, so much national
waste, and every weaver reinstated is so much national gain. What-
ever the plan you may adopt, I would like you to keep me inform-
ed of your activity from time to time.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
