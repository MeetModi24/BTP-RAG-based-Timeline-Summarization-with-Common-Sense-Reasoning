410. LETTER TO E. SOMAN APPASAMY
NATURE CURE CLINIC,
6 TODIWALA ROAD,
POONA,
October 26, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has received your letter of 24th inst. He is surprised to hear from you
that Shri Kamaraj and Shri Bhaktavatsalam used his name to persuade you to stand
for election. He had said nothing of the kind to them. In the company of others
they came to see him and enquire about his health as he was under the same roof
as Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. Dr. Pattabhi who led them pointedly remarked that they
had only come on a friendly visit as they knew he took no interest in elections,
and as a matter of fact during the few minutes that they were with him there was
no talk about the elections.
Yours sincerely,
S.N.
SHRI E. SOMAN APPASAMY
"PRITHI SADAN"
25 TIRUMALAI PILLAI RD.
THYAGARAJANAGAR, MADRAS
