221. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
ANAND BHAWAN, ALLAHABAD,
February 7, 1931
DEAR BROTHER,
Jawaharlal was much touched by your wire. Thank you for
it. I would love to see you and hear all you have to say to me,
for I am not at all sanguine of fruitful result. The atmosphere
in India does not seem to me to support your and other friends'
jubilation. I would like however to feel that there was no founda-
tion for my fears. You may come whenever you are free. The
Working Committee meets here on the 13th instant. If it is at
all possible, we should meet on or before 11 th instant.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Letters of Srinivasa Sastri, p. 207; also the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's
Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
