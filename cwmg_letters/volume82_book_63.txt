63. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
POONA,
November 8, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have your letter. It is difficult to reply to it. But one
thing is clear, namely, that you should tell Kanchan in plain
words that you do not look upon her as your wife and that she
also should not look upon you as her husband. You should
accept no service from her. Indeed, you two should not live in
the same place. I am prepared to arrange for your stay in
different places. This would be a painful duty. But your letter
makes it incumbent upon me to take such a step.
As for serving people, you can only tend those of the sick
who require tending.
Since I hope that we shall now be meeting on the 21st, I
do not write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8427. Also C.W. 5602. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
