71. LETTER TO SHANKARLAL G. BANKER
NEW DELHI,
October 28, 1946
CHI. SHANKARLAL,
I have sat down to write letters after the morning prayer.
I have your letter before me, as also Anasuyabehn's.
I am getting ready for Bengal. I will be doing there what
God commands. I wish to be there as long as He wills. I
hope you are calm. You should be. How do you spend
your time?
My address will be: Khadi Pratishthan, College Square,
Calcutta. But I want to go on to Noakhali.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHANKARLAL BANKER
AHMEDABAD
