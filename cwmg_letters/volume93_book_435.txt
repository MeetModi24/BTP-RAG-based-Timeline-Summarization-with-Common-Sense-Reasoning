435. LETTER TO PYARELAL
[After August 28, 1939]
CHI. PYARELAL,
What you have written about Sushila is not correct. If it is
not her duty to remain in Delhi on account of Mother, she has
to pass her test here. In that alone lies her good. Devdas says
that there is no need to think about him. Ba is pining for her.
That is sufficient for Sushila. She is asking me every now and
then when Sushila will be coming. My saying that you find
pleasure in making a person cry is to be understood in terms
of popular expression. When we make someone cry through our
action, it is said, and rightly, that we find pleasure in
making them cry. Should it not be said that at Abbottabad I
derived pleasure from making Sushila cry? No matter how much
I myself may have cried at heart.
Blessings from
BAPU
