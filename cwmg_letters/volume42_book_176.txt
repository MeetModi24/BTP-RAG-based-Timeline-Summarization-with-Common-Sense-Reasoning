176. LETTER TO ALI MOHAMAD A. ALLADIN
CAMP RAI BARELI,
November 14, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I know nothing of the incident referred
to by you but assuming that some Sikhs and some Hindus mis-
behaved themselves that is no reason why it should be a bar
against Hindu-Muslim friendship or that the sins of a few should
be imputed to the whole people.
Yours sincerely,
ALI MOHAMAD A. ALLADIN, ESQ., M.A.
ALLADIN BUILDINGS
SECUNDERABAD, DECCAN
From a microfilm: S.N. 15744
