248. LETTER TO REGISTRAR, VISHVABHARATI
ON WAY TO PATNA,
April 13, 1947
SIR,
Received your letter dated 24th March. I humbly accept
the honour which the Senate has decided to confer upon me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
REGISTRAR, VISHVABHARATI
P.O. SHANTINIKETAN
DT. BIRBHUM (BENGAL)
From a copy of the Bengali: C. W. 10621. Courtesy: Vishvabharati,
Shantiniketan
