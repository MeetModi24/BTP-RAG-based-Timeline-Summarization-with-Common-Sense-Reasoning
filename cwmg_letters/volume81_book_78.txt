78. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
July 28, 1945
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. Vichitra had written to me. I cannot
open a clinic for you. I have explained everything to you. I
know your ability. I know your shortcomings too. Do what-
ever you can on your own strength. I did what I could. I
shall be happy if your work gets going, that is to say, if the
poor are served. I do not feel like writing more.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. HIRALAL SHARMA
NAGLA NAWABAD
KHURJA, U. P.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
