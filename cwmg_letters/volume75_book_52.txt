52. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA C. MEHTA
Diwali [October 20, 1941]
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
You are improving fast now. What good news! I hope you
write regularly to people at home. You must keep perfect health
during the coming year and render service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9433
