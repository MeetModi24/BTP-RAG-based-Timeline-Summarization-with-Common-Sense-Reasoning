21. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
December 8, 1939
CHI. DEVDAS,
Where is the need to guide you ? You are not likely to neglect
anything in Ba's service. Who can charge you with neglect if any-
thing happens to Ba? She will pass away wherever she is destined
to. No one protects another. God alone protects all. Ba will
give you the details about the death of Ashalata's child. She was
present there at the time. Gopalrao's son died in a matter of
a few minutes. He was stung by a black scorpion. He screamed
and fell down dead. All of us live in the jaws of death. As long
as it does not swallow us we may go about dancing.
J. has written to Jinnah. Now one may say this too is over.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2136
