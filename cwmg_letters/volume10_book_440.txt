440. LETTER TO H. S. L. POLAK
March 17, 1911
MY DEAR MR. POLAK,
I hope you have succeeded in rousing our friends there to a sense of
their duty. Though I still hope that there will be a favourable reply to
our telegram to Smuts, we, at this end, are making every preparation for
a renewal of the struggle. Cartwright is simply disgusted, with his own
party as well as with Smuts. I understand that he will be leaving the
Leader within a week or so, unless something he expects turns up.
Sincerely yours,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 5313
