436. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
Silence Day, October 221, 1928
CHI. RAMDAS,
I have your letter. Ba is better. There is no cause for worry.
Nimu arrived yesterday. She is all right. The moment she ar-
rived, she joined in nursing Ba. Others who were ill have almost
recovered. You must have got the news about a daughter having
been born to Sushila.
Nanabhai has sent a cable that she should be named "Dhairya-
bala".
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6854
