421. LETTER TO T. R. BHATT
April 11, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
If you will do your own bit for the
removal of untouchability in your part of the world, you will
find that your example will be copied by others if they find in
you a determined and selfless worker.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. T. R. BHATT
3 LAWRENCE ROAD
NAMNAIR, AGRA CANTT.
From a microfilm: S.N. 20887
