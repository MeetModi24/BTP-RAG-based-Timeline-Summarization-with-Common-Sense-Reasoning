114. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
June 22, 1934
BHAI RAJENDRA PRASAD,
I had your letter from Jiradei. Not a day passes when we
do not think of you. I hope you are composed now. How
much time do you need to resolve your domestic affairs? 1 What
about the expenses? Where will Janardan live now? Write
to me about all this. I hope the relief work is going on.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 9716. Courtesy: Dr. Rajendra Prasad
