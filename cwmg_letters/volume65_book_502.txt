502. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 20, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
Herewith the mail. From your note I can't decide what
should be done at the moment. We shall see later. As I have
already sent [most of] the material today, it was not possible to
send this early. I could have sent two articles by 1 o'clock.
But Kanu told me that the papers for Harijan would be sent later
in the evening. I, therefore, went on writing till 3 p.m.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11535
