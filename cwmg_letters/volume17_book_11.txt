11. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
LAHORE,
Thursday [February 5, 1920]
MY DEAR CHILD,
I see I have hurt you; forgive me. I wrote as I did because
I love you so. I am afraid it is no use your coming here, be-
cause I shall be touring from the 11th. So I cannot be with you
even if you come. I hope to be with you on the 23rd at the
latest. And we shall have some time at least together. Mean-
while please take the hip and the sitz baths. And why will you
not be examined by the doctor who is there? If I were you I
should have no such compunction. But I don't wish to press you.
With prayerful love,
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat of the original in N.A.I.; also My Dear Child, p. 57
