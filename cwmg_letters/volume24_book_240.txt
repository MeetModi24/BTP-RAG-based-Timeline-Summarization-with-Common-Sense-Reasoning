240. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Ashadh Vad 10 [July 26, 1924]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. Cast aside all your mental worries, take
proper treatment and improve your health thoroughly while you
are there. I am making arrangements for [your stay at] Hajira.
Gangabehn writes to say that she will come here on Monday.
Radha is not able to take enough food.
Blessings from
BAPU
SISTER VASUMATI
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 451. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
