305. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 27, 1936
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
Let me see what great feat you perform, now that Kanti has
gone there. Do not pick quarrels. Persuade him sweetly if
you wish to. As far as I can tell, it is now impossible to stop him.
Carry out Dr. Bharadwaj's instructions. While you have been
advising others, you yourself need to look after your own health.
Do not expect any long letter from me for some time now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 343
