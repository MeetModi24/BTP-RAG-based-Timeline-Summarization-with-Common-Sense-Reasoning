130. LETTER TO KESHAV GANDHI
September 19, 1932
CHI. KESHU,
Ink blots cannot be erased. You should let them remain or
should take another sheet.
We should act with humility even towards a person who is
rude to us, but should not submit to him.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3281
