148. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 22, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I could write yesterday with great difficulty. Today that is not
exactly so. There are still five minutes for the prayer bell to go. Sardar
has been started on 2oz. of separata today. Rajkumari has arrived
today. Rajaji and Bhulabhai have gone.
Ranganayaki has come for a few days. I am all right. I can say
that the backache has gone. I cannot say whether there is still a trace
left. It has started getting quite cold.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
