103. LETTER TO AKSHAYA KUMAR ROY
September 29, 1934
DEAR AKSHAYABABU,
I was distressed to hear through Deenabandhu Andrews that
you were suffering from leprosy. I was, however, glad to learn
that there was every chance of your getting rid of it, as the treat-
ment was begun in the early stage of the disease. May God give
you peace.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI AKSHAYA KUMAR Roy
18 GOBRA HOSPITAL
GOBRA ROAD, CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
