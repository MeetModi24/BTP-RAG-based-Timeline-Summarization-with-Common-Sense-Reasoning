458. LETTER TO JAMNADAS GANDHI
BORSAD,
June 12, 1931
CHI. JAMNADAS,
You are a strange man. After having spent days with me here,
you go back and write from there a letter about an important
matter. It raises several questions. Should I answer them by
post, or call you back here? When I wanted to know why you
thought of coming as far as Bombay, you could give no answer
and hurriedly went back, and now you write to me a letter from
there asking for Rs. 5,000. This is now my advice to you. Write
to Kakasaheb and convince him about the propriety of your
request. If you do this, my task will be easier. Have Manu and
Babi reached there?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 9324. Courtesy: Jamnadas Gandhi
