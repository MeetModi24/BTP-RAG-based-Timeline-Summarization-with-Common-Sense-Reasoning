412. LETTER TO S. A. WAIZ
September 16, 1946
BHAI WAIZ,
I have your letter. Indians abroad—Pardesvasi Hindi. Pravasi
is not correct. It means travelling or traveller. "Abroad" is
not given in the Harijan Dict., because perhaps it is common.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI S. A. WAIZ
I. I. C. A.
SOHRAB HOUSE
235 HORNBY R[OA]D
BOMBAY FORT
From a photostat: G. N. 7940
