253. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 13, 1940
CHI. LILA,
I very much want to write to you, but what can I do? Please
think that Mahadev writing to you is as good as my writing.
If you cannot restrain yourself, by all means do come. But I
won't be here. We leave on the 15th night.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9935. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
