305. LETTER TO PYARELAL
August 17, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
There seems to be something the matter with my health
today. I could not even sleep at night. Could yesterday's
conversation be the reason for it? I did not wish to express my
opinion. But since you insisted I wrote it to you. Right now
I have only one wish : not to entertain any thought, utter any
word, write any sentence, that may hurt you. I am only
counting days for you to recover fully. I hope for your part
you will make full efforts. If you resolve to get well, you will
certainly succeed.
Blessings from
BAPU
