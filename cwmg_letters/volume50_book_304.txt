304. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
July 31, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA,
How was Surendraji's tree uprooted?
Plant another in
its place. Ask Viramati and Chanchal to write to me. Why can
you not spin as fine yarn as Keshu?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5778. Also C.W. 3001. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
