146. LETTER TO M. K. GOVINDA PILLAI
& WIJNANA CHANDRA SEN
AS AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 29, 1928
MY DEAR FRIENDS,
I have your letter. Please give me your respective ages, whether
you are married, whether you have parents, whether you know
any other language beyond Malayalam, what training you have
received in English and where you were trained. What certificate
does the head of your institution give you? And you should send
all the other particulars which I should know.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. M. K. GOVINDA PILLAI
SJT. WIJNANA CHANDRA SEN
ARYA SAMAJ, KOTTAYAM (TRAVANCORE)
From a photostat: S.N. 13008
