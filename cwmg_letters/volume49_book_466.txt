466. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
April 30, 1932
CHI. MANGALA (DARLING),
To follow one's atman does not mean acting as one pleases. It
means obeying the voice of God who dwells in our heart. We pray
daily, keep vows and practise self-control in order that we may
hear Him. When we are convinced that we have heard the voice
of the Lord, we should not worry no matter who is displeased
with us.
Blessings from
BAPU
