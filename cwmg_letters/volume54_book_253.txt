253. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRAN
March 28, 1933
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRAN,
It is refreshing to see your signature after so many months.
I have been following you nevertheless.
Narayana Iyer I know well from his Permanent History of Bharat
Varsha, and I think I met him also in Trivandrum. He is a learn-
ed man but not a very clear thinker. That was the impression
that his Permanent History left on my mind. But he is an enthu-
siast. Anyway, I shall try to read his pamphlets.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20734
