530. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 26, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Disappointment cannot come anywhere near a worshipper
of non-violence. Your indecision comes from your fear of com-
mitting mistakes. We must quickly take decisions even if they
should turn out to be mistaken. Mistakes can be rectified.
Blessings from
BAPU
