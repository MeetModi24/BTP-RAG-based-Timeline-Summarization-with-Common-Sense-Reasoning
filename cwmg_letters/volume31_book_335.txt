335. LETTER TO NARAYANDAS BAJORIA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday, Sravana Shukla 10, August 18, 1926
BHAISHRI NARAYANDASJI,
I thank you for the draft for Rs. 5,000 which you sent at
the request of Jamnalalji. The money will be spent in meeting
the expenses on a building for the Ashram.
SHRI NARAYANDASJI BAJORIA
117, HARRISON ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 12251
