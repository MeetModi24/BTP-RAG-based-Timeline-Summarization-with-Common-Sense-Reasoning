524. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
March 28, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
You might think I had completely forgotten you. But it
is only today that I have some time to write a few brief notes.
You seem to be progressing well. Get completely all right by
the time I return. Don't give up hip-baths and friction-baths. Go
over to a diet of fruit juice as soon as you feel the slightest trouble.
I am keeping well enough. It is very hot here. But it does
not trouble me. I hope you have not decided not to write to
me if I do not write to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7078. Also C.W. 4570. Courtesy:
