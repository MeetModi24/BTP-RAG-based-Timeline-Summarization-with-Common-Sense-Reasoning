293. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
SHARDA,
I have your letter. Tell Shakaribehn that everybody would
know what to write to me. I would accept any mad thing one may
write to me.
How is it that you youngsters consider yourselves alone?
Those who want to do service and find joy in doing it find enough
company in their work. Of course, those who wish to indulge in small
talk would feel lonely. But do we want to indulge in small talk?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9883. Courtesy: Shardabehn Chokhawala
