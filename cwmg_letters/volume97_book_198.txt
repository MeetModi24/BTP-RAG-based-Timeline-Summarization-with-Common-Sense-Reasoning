198. LETTER TO KASHI GANDHI
February 18. 1932
CHI. KASHI,
I have your letter. You need not at all worry about Prabhudas. God has
looked after him and will do so still. After meeting him, let me know all the
facts. Probably you know that I had sent to Prabhudas my replies to some
questions I had received concerning Krishna.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33088
