333. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI AGRAWAL
July 5, 1935
DEAR SISTER,
I, on my part, have instructed Moolchandji not to exercise
any compulsion on you. And you too should treat him in the
same way. You should have all liberty in respect of food. But as
you cannot bring yourself to accept the mode of life followed by
Moolchandji you may live separately and do as you please. This,
however, does not absolve him of the duty of providing for your
maintenance.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 836
