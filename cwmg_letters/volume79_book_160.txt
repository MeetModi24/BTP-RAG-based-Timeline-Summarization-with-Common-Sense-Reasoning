160. LETTER TO BAJRANG SINGH
SEVAGRAM,
February 3, 1945
BHAI BAJRANG SINGH1,
I have your letter. May God save you. If you have to go
to the gallows, face it cheerfully.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
BAJRANG SINGH
CONDEMNED CELL, CENTRAL JAIL
NAINI, U. P.
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
