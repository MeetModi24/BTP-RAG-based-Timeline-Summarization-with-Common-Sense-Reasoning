320. LETTER TO EDITOR, "TEJ"
Sravana Sud 13 [August 13, 1924]
BHAI GUPTA,
I daily bow, times without number, to that Krishna alone
who is the author of the Gita, who is the master of 16,000 sen-
ses, who is an akhand brahmachari1, who is free from passion and
who is the Lord of our hearts, and to none else.
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
