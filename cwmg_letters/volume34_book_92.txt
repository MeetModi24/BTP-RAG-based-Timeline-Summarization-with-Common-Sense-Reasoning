92. LETTER TO WILLIAM SMITH
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 3, 1927
DEAR MR. SMITH,
I thank you for your two letters and very carefully prepared
notes and the Italian pamphlet illustrating the principle and use
of castrating forceps.
I have now gone through your notes, and if you are free on
Tuesday next at 4 p.m. I shall be delighted to see you.
Could I publish your valuable notes in the pages of Young
India or make other public use of them?
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 12919
