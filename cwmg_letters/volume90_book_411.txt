411. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
January 18, 1948
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
Give up your fast.
I am sending herewith a copy of the telegram received from
the Speaker of West Punjab. Zaheed Hussain had said exactly
what I had told you.
May you live long and continue to be the jewel of India.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2210
