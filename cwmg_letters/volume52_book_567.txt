567. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
January 10, 1933
CHI. HARIBHAU,
Why have you written or dictated no letter to me? You seem
to be taking a long time to recover after the operation. How are
you now? What was the trouble? How is the nursing? What is
the news about Rohit Mehta?
I often see Jamnalalji. He keeps good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 6074. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
