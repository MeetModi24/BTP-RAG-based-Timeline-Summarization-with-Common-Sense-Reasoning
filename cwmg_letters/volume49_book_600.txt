600. LETTER TO VENILAL GANDHI
May 29, 1932
CHI. VENILAL,
I got your letter. Your illness was such that you are bound
to take time to regain strength. Try to regain it patiently.
You have practically got a new life. Make the best use of it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 917. Courtesy: Venilal Gandhi
