118. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 6, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Has there not been idiocy somewhere about your collapse?
Why did you take that pill? But there you are. "There's many
a slip between the cup and the lip." So we shall both believe when
you are actually installed here.
Take care of yourself. You should not be anxious about your
health whilst you may not be indifferent about it.
Talimi Sangh meetings going on. About sixteen of them dined
at the Ashram kitchen.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4114. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7423
