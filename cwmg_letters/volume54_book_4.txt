4. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
March 6, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
The fact that you have been putting on weight in the
Ashram shows that the Ashram has suited you. As one's health
improves, one's heart and mind too gain strength.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9638. Courtesy: Ravindra R.
Patel
