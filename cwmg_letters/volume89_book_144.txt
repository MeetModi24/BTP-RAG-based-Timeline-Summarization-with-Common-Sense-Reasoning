144. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
BELIAGHATA,
August 30, 1947
CHI. VALLABHBHAI,
I have your letter. I have received from Jawahar also a
telegram similar to what you have sent. My reply is contained
in the accompanying letter. I therefore do not write more here.
May God give all of you the strength and the wisdom the
situation demands. Did you ever think that you would have to
face such a difficult situation so soon? His will be done.
