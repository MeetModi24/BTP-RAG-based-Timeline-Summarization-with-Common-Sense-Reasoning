295. LETTER TO INDIRA NEHRU
SEGAON,
October 15, 1939
MY DEAR INDU,
I had thought that you had forgotten me altogether. Your
letter has belied the fear, thank God. I hope you are keeping
well. In a way, I envy you for the experiences you are having
there
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9802. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
