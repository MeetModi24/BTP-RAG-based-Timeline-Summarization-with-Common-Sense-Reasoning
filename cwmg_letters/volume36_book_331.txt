331. LETTER TO KALYANJI MEHTA
April 28, 1928
BHAISHRI KALYANJI,
You know I cannot attend the Conference. I am sorry I
cannot, but just now I am helpless. The contribution of the Rani-
paraj men and women to the present movement is as much a
matter of satisfaction to us as it is worthy of them. I regard this
movement as one for developing fearlessness and for self-purification.
How can they who call themselves Raniparaj live in fear? In
the sacrificial effort of self-purification, how could they afford
to have vices like drinking, gambling and wearing foreign cloth?
I trust, therefore, that the Raniparaj men and women will give
increasing importance to the spinning-wheel, use more and more
khadi and abstain from drinking and give up other vices.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
