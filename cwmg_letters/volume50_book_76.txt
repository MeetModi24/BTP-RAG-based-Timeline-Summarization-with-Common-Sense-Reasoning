76. LETTER TO A CHILD
June 19, 1932
Generally people do not like to clean utensils or lavatories.
We, therefore, get such work done by members of certain com-
munities. This is wrong. Anybody, therefore, who does such work
for the good of the world does service through it.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 233
