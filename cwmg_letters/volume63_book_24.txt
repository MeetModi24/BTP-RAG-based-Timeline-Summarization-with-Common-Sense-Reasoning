24. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
June [6]1, 1936
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. Serve Mirabehn as much as you can.
It would have been better if you had been to see Premabehn.
Do not undertake anything beyond your capacity. Do not talk
too much. Do nothing but bury yourself in work. Do not talk
unless it is necessary. I expect you have brought to Segaon only
the necessary things.
I hope you are in good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9343. Also C.W. 6618. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
