535. LETTER TO TOTARAM SANADHYA
May 22, 1932
BHAI TOTARAMJI,
Your reminiscences of Gangadevi sent here are sacred.
Though I had inferred a great deal about her qualities your
reminiscences of her are truly astounding. The Ashram is blessed
where such a chaste woman lived.
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Banarsidas Papers. Courtesy: National Archives
of India
