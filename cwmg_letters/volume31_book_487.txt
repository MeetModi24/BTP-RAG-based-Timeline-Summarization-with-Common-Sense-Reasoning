487. LETTER TO MOTI CHOKSI
THE ASHRAM,
Saturday, Bhadra Vad 4 [September 25, 1926]
CHI. MOTI,
I got your five and a half lines. They are not uniformly straight,
and if they were re-written properly they would become five.
The letters which compose the lines are of all shapes and sizes,
some big, some small, some thin and some thick. Even so, it is
a letter from you, and that is a favour, no doubt. Let me
know what opinion the doctor gave after examining your blood.
It is good that you have started reading again. If we fix a time
for everything and adhere to it, we improve both in physical
and in mental health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12135
