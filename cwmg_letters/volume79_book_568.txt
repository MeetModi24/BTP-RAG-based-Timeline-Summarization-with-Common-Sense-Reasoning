568. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
ON THE WAY,
March 31, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I do not like to leave the Ashram any time, and this time I
like it the least. But why should one cling to anything?
I read your papers. I had a talk with Jajuji. I think I
shall be able to cope with the matter. Let us see what happens.
You should be alert. Understand your responsibility. Give
up what your body does not accept. Shakaribehn can do much,
but that depends on you. If Hoshi ari behn opens up she can go
far. Anasuya cannot be kept with Prabhakar. But if nobody
else takes charge of her, there will be no alternative. Do what
you think best. Since I shall not be there, some accommodation
must be available. Have a frank talk with Ashadevi. Remember
the dictum: "Speak the truth, speak gently", and understand
its meaning.
Blessings from
BAPU
