91. LETTER TO V. S. BHASKARAN
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 5, 1928
MY DEAR BHASKARAN,
I was glad to receive your letter. Of course I forgive you for
all the mischief you have done. But your letter only confirms
what I heard about you, and it was undoubtedly wrong.
You now ask me with my influence to restore the money that
you withdrew and say that you will accept penance in the way of
fasting and what not. That would not be correct penance. You
must now submit to what Ramanathan or Rajaji may say with-
out in any way being influenced by me. That is the correct posi-
tion for you to take. And, if you can't have the money restored,
you should submit to their judgment cheerfully and expect to earn
it by honest means in future.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. V . S. BHASKARAN
C/O POSTMASTER, RANIPET
