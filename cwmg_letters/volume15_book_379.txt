379. LETTER TO E. W. FRITCHLEY
June, 1919
DEAR MR. FRITCHLEY,
I was delighted to receive your long letter and in accor-
dance with it I had hoped to have the pleasure of meeting you at
Laburnum Road. I returned to Bombay on Tuesday. I hope that
you will drop in during the week.
Yours sincerely,
E. W. FRITCHLEY, ESQ.
ARCHITECT
FORT [BOMBAY]
From a photostat: S.N. 6702
