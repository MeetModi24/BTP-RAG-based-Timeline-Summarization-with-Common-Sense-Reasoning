147. LETTER TO M. M. MALAVIYA
NEW DELHI,
April 7, 1939
BHAI SAHEB,
I got your letter through Sundaram. I got the wire too. It will
be a pilgrimage for me to come for your darshan. I shall try my best.
I am trying to do something about Bannu. It is a difficult
matter.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From Hindi: C.W. 10229. Courtesy: Bharat Kala Bhavan, Varanasi
