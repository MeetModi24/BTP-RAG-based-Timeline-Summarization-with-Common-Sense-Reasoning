21. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
MUSSOORIE,
October 18, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
Today I have not yet received the mail sent by you. I see
that Panditji, too, worries about your health. Bring it round soon.
I cannot write more today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5462
