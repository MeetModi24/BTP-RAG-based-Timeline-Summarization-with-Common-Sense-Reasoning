108. LETTER TO MATHURI N. KHARE
February 6, 1932
CHI. MATHURI,
I certainly like your getting up early in the morning, but a
girl of weak health like you must be free to sleep whenever she
wants to. You should do only as much as you can without
getting tired or bored.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 261. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
