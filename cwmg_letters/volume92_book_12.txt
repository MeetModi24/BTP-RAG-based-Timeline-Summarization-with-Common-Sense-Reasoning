12. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
[After February 16, 1929]
CHI. PRABHA,
I still hope to reach there on Wednesday. But in case the
work is not finished in time for that, on Thursday morning at
the latest. Your patience is bound to be rewarded.
Blessings to Gomati and Rajkishori.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3306
