313. LETTER TO RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
PATNA,
March 22, 1934
DEAR SISTER,
I thank you for your cheque for 38-7- and your letter. I
am writing to Dr. Royden. Of course the contribution is hand-
some from her poor congregation.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
JULLUNDUR CITY
PUNJAB
From a photostat: G.N. 6318. Also C.W. 3509. Courtesy: Rajkumari Amrit
Kaur
