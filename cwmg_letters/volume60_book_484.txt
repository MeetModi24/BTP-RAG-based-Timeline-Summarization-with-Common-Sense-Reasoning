484. LETTER TO REGINALD REYNOLDS
March 29, 1935
MY DEAR ANGADA 1 ,
This is just to tell you that I wrote to David Pyke. He must
be a brave boy.
Why don't you tell me something about yourself?
I am enjoying my silence.
Love from us all.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 4545. Courtesy: Swarthmore College, Philadelphia
