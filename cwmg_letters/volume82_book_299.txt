299. LETTER TO J. C. GUPTA
SODEPUR,
December 15, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Many thanks for your letter.
I am doing all I can about the prisoners. I do not think
I need trouble you to see me about them.
It was a pleasure to see your blind son and his wife.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI J. C. GUPTA
23 CIRCUS AVENUE, CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
