220. LETTER TO S. SUBBA RAO
October 14, 1934
MY DEAR SUBBA RAO,
I have your letter. I very much fear that you will have to
find out something for yourself there.
Surely this should not be
difficult in the surroundings where you are comparatively better
known.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI S. SUBBA RAo
MARUTERU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
