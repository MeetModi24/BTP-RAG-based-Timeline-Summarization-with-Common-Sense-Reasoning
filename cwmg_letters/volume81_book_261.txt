261. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
POONA,
August 24, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
Your two letters at the same time.
What a tragedy you have related !!!
Subhas Bose has died well. He was undoubtedly a patriot
though misguided.
Your gum has caused much trouble. I blame the dentist.
Do let me know the train you will come by.
P[yarelal] is in Bombay. S[ushila] will tell you all about
him.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3698. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6507
