384. LETTER TO BRIJLAL NEHRU
MUSSOORIE,
June 7, 1946
BHAI BRIJLAL NEHRU,
I have your letter to Pyarelal. What I want to know is
mentioned in the third paragraph, viz., what treatment, tried
by you, do you suggest for the ailment? I shall be glad to have
something on this so that I can understand things better.
Blessings from
BAPU
1 WARRIS ROAD
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
