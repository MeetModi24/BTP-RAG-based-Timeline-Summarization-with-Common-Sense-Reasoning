559. LETTER TO LADY KAUL
SEVAGRAM,
March 29, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have received your cheque. I shall of course deposit it.
The acknowledgment is enclosed. But I have not been able to
decide so far whether I should issue the appeal about the Fund.
Will you approve if I use this money in the way the late Raja-
saheb had wished even if I do not issue an appeal?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
LADY KAUL
SANGSAR
JIND STATE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
