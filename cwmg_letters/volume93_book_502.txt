502. LETTER TO GLADYS OWEN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 11, 1940
MY DEAR GLADYS,
Though your question was urgent, I could not overtake it
before now. I think you should hold on to the Ashram and do
what you can. It is clear that you are wanted there.
You must have read all about C. F. A. He was a man of
God.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a photostat: C. W. 11281. Courtesy: Dr. Priyamvada Mathur
