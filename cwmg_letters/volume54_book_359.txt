359. LETTER TO V. S. KATHAVTE
April 6, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have our letter. I think that you have to give up all
idea of marrying the girl. You do not seem to realize that she
is only eleven years old. She cannot have any idea of marriage
at her age, and if she has, it is an unhealthy and premature know-
ledge, which is not to be encouraged. Apart therefore from the
close relationship which undoubtedly exists I think that the age
of the girl is an effective bar, and you ought not to read into
her eyes any message for you.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. V. S. KATHAVTE
SAVANUR
From a microfilm: S.N. 20849
