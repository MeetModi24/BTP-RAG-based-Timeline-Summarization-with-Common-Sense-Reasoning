689. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
'MANOR VILLE', SIMLA WEST,
July 9, 19451
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your letter. You should never give up weaving. Master
all the activities relating to cotton. I note what you say about
the Khadi Vidyalaya. You have to be prepared to accept what-
ever services you can easily get.
Balkrishna must get well. Is he now anxious to go to Poona
not because I say so, but of his own free will?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4517
