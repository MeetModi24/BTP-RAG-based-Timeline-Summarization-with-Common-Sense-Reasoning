45. LETTER TO SATYANANDA BOSE
January 13, 1933
DEAR SATYANANDA BABOO,
You are always so good and always think of me whenever
there is anything to say to me.
I think that this is hardly the time to consider improvements
in Dr. Subbaroyan's Bill. The first thing is to get the Viceregal
sanction. When that is had, many improvements may be possible.
I am not therefore just now applying my mind to your suggestion.
After the principle of the Bill is once accepted, the Government
themselves may take it up or may advise amendments so far as that
may be necessary to free the Bill from any possible political mis-
chief. So far as I am concerned, my one thought in connection
with the temple-entry is the purification of caste Hindus. But of
course I shall bear your suggestions in mind whenever the occasion
arises for their use.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SATYANANDA BOSE
4 NUNDY ST.
BALLY GUNGE
CALCUTTA
