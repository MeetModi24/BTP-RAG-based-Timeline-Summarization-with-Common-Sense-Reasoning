306. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
Kartik Krishna 13 [November 24, 1924]
MY DEAR BRIJKRISHNA,
I heard last night of your bereavement. I have sent a telegram
today. May God give you courage. If we realize that birth and
death are both the same, we should have no cause for grief.
A true friend never dies. One's wife is such a friend. If you con-
stantly cherish the memory of her virtues, death would make no
difference. May God give you the strength of character to keep
the vow of ekapatnivrata.
Blessings from
BAPU
