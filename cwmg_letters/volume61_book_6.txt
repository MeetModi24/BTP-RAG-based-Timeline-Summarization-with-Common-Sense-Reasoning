6. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
[After April 26, 1935)
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I enquired and found your assertion to be true. The unasked-
for report gave me pain. Now Mahadev on being questioned
tells me that Kanti had tried persuasion. Did you inform before
you left? This much seems true even now that they were willing
enough to dine.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1875
