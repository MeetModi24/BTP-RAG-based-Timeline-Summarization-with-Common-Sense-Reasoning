428. LETTER TO G. SITARAMA SASTRY
July 30, 1935
MY DEAR SASTRY,
About the barter essay I have not been idle. Shri Vaikunth
Mehta's name has been suggested by Prof. Shah. K. suggests Prof.
Ranga. Who is Prof. Rao of Lucknow? Is he an Andhra man?
May the prize be given if the best and the most convincing essay
is against the barter system?
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9173. Courtesy: G. Sitarama Sastry
