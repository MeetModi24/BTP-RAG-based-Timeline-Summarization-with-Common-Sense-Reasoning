231. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM,
June 6, 1942
BHAI SATIS BABU,
How can I go to Bengal? I have clearly expressed my
view. Those who are asked to move in this manner should
flatly refuse and should not budge from their places even if
191
they have to be shot unless and until they have been provided
with alternative accommodation and all the amenities. There is
no other course open. If in offering resistance thus some of us
have to die, it can't be helped. They ought to give prior notice.
Blessings from
BAPU
