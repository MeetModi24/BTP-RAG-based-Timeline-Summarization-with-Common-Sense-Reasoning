293. LETTER TO K. B. JOSHI
'DILKHUSHA', PANCHGANI,
July 19, 1944
MY DEAR JOSHI,
I was wondering why you had not written all these days. I
was inquiring about you only the other day. Vaikunthbhai gave
me all the information in his possession. I quite agree with you
that something should be done and that quickly. Fortunately
Vaikunthbhai is wide awake, and I am hoping that something
will be done. You will please keep in touch with me but you
know our ultimate goal. We have to reach a process whereby
everyone can manufacture paper in his own house just as everyone
can do spinning in his own house. I know that the problem
is difficult; but if we are to serve 40 crores of people, we must be
able to devise some such method as I have adumbrated. If I have
relaxed the original restriction, it is in order to reach that goal.
Yours,
BAPU
SJT. K. B. JOSHI
C/O TULPULE'S BUNGALOW
1195/ SHIVAJI NAGAR
POONA 4
