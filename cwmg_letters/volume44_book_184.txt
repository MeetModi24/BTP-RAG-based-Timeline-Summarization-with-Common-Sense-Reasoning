184. LETTER TO KALAVATI TRIVEDI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 7, 1930
CHI. KALAVATI,
Your letter reached me belatedly. Go if you must to serve
your mother-in-law. Most probably this letter will reach you
there. Observe the rules well. Be very strict regarding your diet
also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5250
