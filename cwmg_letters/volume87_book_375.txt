375. LETTER TO P. S. SANE
May 3, 1947
BHAI SANE GURUJI,
Your wire. I am sorry you don't understand my simple argu-
ment. Initially you wanted to undertake the fast because of the
criticism from some four or five people, and now you are demand-
ing opening of the temple for the Harijans. I do not understand
where dharma comes in. In any case I would appeal to you to
give up your fast.
Shankerrao and Balasaheb are your friends, not enemies.
You should also listen to the advice of friends such as these. May
God grant you better counsel.
Yours,
M. K.
[From Hindi]
Biharni Komi Agman, p. 288
