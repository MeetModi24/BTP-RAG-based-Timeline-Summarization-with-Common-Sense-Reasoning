422. LETTER TO L. W. RITCH
[ JOHANNESBURG,]
March 11, 1911
MY DEAR RITCH,
I have your telegram, as also your letter. You will have received
copy of the telegram sent to certain Members. I could not quite
understand your meaning, but I interpreted your wire to mean that
wires should be sent on behalf of the Association, giving the views on
the Bill to the Transvaal Members who have at all interested themselves
in the matter . You will see among the names three which are not
Transvaal. I thought that these three Members were specially entitled
to the wire. The views are also represented in the Petition which
I hope you will circulate to the Press also. There is a wire from
Cape Town in the Dairy Mail to the effect that General Smuts has
placed on the Table printed correspondence between the Imperial
Government and the Union Government regarding the Bill. I hope
you will be able to get a copy of it and send it.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 5280
