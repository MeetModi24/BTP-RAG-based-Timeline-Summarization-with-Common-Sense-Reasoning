304. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
December 23, 1940
CHI. SHARMA,
Your name is already with me. But why should I send you
[to jail] as long as I wish to take constructive work from you? And
how can I send you unless you become a social animal? Tell
me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh,
p.294
