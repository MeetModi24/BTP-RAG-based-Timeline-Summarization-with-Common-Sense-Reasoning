318. LETTER TO NAGINDAS AMULAKHRAI
SABARMATI,
August 13, 1924
I have gone through your first book and was impressed by
the hard work you have put into it. The arrangement is on the
whole quite good. I have noticed nothing in it which could be
considered inconsistent with the author's object. I see no im-
propriety in its being translated.
[From Gujarati]
Gujarati, 2-11-1924
