127. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
Monday [January 11, 1926]
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have been eagerly waiting for you. I think you will certain-
ly be here on the 16th. Bring your family along. For the present
you will put up with Ba, or Kashi, or Maganlal. After your arrival
here I shall make suitable arrangements about your board. I am
prepared even to put up a new structure for you. I would not
inconvenience you in any way. I want to be here for one year,
during which I desire your presence.
Of course, I have written to Jamnadas. But it seems he is
out of his mind; he has lost the faculty of discretion. Well, he
too has been asked to come here. Bring him along if you can
persuade him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7707. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
