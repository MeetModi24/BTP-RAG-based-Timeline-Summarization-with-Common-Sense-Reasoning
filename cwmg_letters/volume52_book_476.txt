476. LETTER TO M. S. PARKHE
January 3, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the sample envelopes.
I can say nothing about the embossed bust, because I am
no judge of such things.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. M. S. PARKHE
THE BHARAT ENVELOPES CO., POONA 2
