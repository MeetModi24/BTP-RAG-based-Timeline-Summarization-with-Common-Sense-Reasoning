171. LETTER TO SATINDRANATH SEN
November 20, 1946
DEAR SATIN1,
Amrit Babu has given me your letter.
A most complex position faces Bengal. I am not sure what
is the exact thing to do. I am hoping that light will pierce
through this darkness. I can therefore give you no guidance
at present. Wait, watch and pray.
BAPU
SHRI SATINDRANATH SEN
BARISAL 2
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
