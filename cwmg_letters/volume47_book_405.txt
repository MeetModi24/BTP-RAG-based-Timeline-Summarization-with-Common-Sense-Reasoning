405. LETTER TO K. D. UMRIGAR
AHMEDABAD,
August 22, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of 17th August enclosing Nine-
teenth Century Review article. I shall see what can be done with it
after I have read it.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. K. D. UMRIGAR
BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 17571
