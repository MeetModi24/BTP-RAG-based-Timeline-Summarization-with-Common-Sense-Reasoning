715. LETTER TO ANANTRAM
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 23, 1945
CHI. ANANTRAM,
I hear that you have recovered and render considerable service
to others. I am very happy to know this. May your life remain
devoted to the service of others. Learn Urdu also and make
progress in it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
