426. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
March 19, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
I have your letter. I have already heard something about
Neelmani. I shall know something more from Bhagwanji. I am
afraid such things will go on happening and we have to over-
come them as best we can. It is no doubt good to remove all
young men without exception. Our safety lies only in young girls
and responsible men remaining.
Bhagwanji will complete his thirty-five days there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9073
