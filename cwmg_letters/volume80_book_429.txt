429. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
PANCHGANI,
June 4, 1945
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I got your letter yesterday. I signed the photograph. May
you live long and continue to serve. Why do you say that you
have "lived for many years and have few more left"? Surely,
125 years are not for me alone. That should be everybody's
life-span and it should be the ideal for all to attain it through a
pure life.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : C.W. 4740. Courtesy: Shantikumar N.
Morarjee
