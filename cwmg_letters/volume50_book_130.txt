130. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
July 1, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
Find out the meaning of "like a cipher" from the letter to
Mangala. There has been no rain here. I believe I am not being
partial. If Bawa Muldas really said what he is supposed to have
said, it was wrong. Thereby, even the widow has been wronged.
One should not tell a lie even to relieve someone from misery.
That surely is no way of ending misery.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9952. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
wala
