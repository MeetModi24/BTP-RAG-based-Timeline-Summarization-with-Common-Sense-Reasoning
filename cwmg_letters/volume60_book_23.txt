23. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA
December 21, 1934
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
I had your letter. Find out the count, evenness and strength
of the yarn spun by the villagers. Get the weaving done soon.
Do not neglect their other occupations. What kind of rice
[,polished or unpolished,] do they eat? Sow some seeds of cotton
and leafy vegetables in your courtyard. It is better for one to
do one's own manual work, as far as possible.
All are well, I hope.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2784
