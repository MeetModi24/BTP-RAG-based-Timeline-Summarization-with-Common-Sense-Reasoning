506. LETTER TO CHUNILAL
May 22, 1936
BHAI CHUNILAL,
I have your letter. Now that Thakkar Bapa is arriving there
everything must settle down. From now on it is not necessary
to send the papers to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
1 The addressee "had gone down with a severe attack of malaria."
