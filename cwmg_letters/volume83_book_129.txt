129. LETTER TO LABHSHANKER VYAS
[On or before February 13, 1946]
BHAI LABHSHANKER,
I got and read your letter today. May all the girls become
good and bring credit to the institution .
Blessings from
BAPU
LABHSHANKER VYAS, PRINCIPAL
H. K. VIDYALAYA
SABARMATI
From a microfilm of the Gujarati
M.M.U./XXII
