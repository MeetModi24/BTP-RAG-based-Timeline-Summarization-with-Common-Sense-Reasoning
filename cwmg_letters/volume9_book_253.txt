253. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
September 9, 1909
MY LORD,
As suggested by Your Lordship, I wrote to the Secretary to Lord
Morley. Enclosed is a copy of his reply.
I wrote to Lord Crewe on Saturday; no reply has arrived as yet.
I beg to enclose herewith copy of my letter to Lord Crewe.
I remain, etc.,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5058
