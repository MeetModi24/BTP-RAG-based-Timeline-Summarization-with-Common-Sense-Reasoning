629. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
June 3, 1926
I have decided against, not in favour of, going to Finland.
There is just the possibility that if Mr. . . who sent the invitation
is in any way likely to be put in an embarrassing position by
my not going, then I must go. But I see no reason for embar-
rassment. So you may take it that I am not going.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 87
