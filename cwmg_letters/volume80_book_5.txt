5. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 25, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I felt sad after reading your letter. I am returning it to you.
Remind me about it when I return there. I cannot do anything
by writing. You are weak, but if you have gained sufficient
strength, write to Shri Ramprasad. See what reply he gives.
Improve your health. I am writing to Munnalal. If he cannot
have peace of mind there, let him come here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10626
