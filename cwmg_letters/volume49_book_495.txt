495. LETTER TO E. E. DOYLE
May 7, 1932
DEAR COL. DOYLE,
I observe from Kaka Kalelkar's letter to me that though
he and Sjts. Narahari Parikh and Prabhudas Gandhi were put
together at Visapur, they were separated on being admitted to
the Belgaum Central Prison. They are all inmates of the
Ashram. Prabhudas Gandhi was Kaka Kalelkar's pupil and Nara-
hari Parikh his assistant and colleague. I do not know that there
was any reason for separating them from one another. If there
was none, you will perhaps agree that in the present state of
the health of both Kaka and Prabhudas Gandhi, if all the three
are together they can be mutually helpful.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Government of Bombay, Home Department, I.G.P. File No. 9
