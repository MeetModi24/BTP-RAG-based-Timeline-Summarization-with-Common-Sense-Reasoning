463. LETTER TO SHRIKRISHNA SINHA
NEW DELHI,
November 5, 1947
BHAI SHRIBABU,
I have not written anything about your Diamond Jubilee
celebration. What was there to write? May you serve more
and more.
The [enclosed] letter is only one amongst many of this
kind that I have received. I have not sent any reply to the
writer nor do I wish to.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
