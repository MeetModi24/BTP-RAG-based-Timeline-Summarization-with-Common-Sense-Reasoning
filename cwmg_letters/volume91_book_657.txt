657. LETTER TO AMBALAL SARABHAI
July 30, 1928
RESPECTED BROTHER,
You have there an amount of Rs. 1,21,000 in the name of
either Vallabhbhai or Punjabhai. I would like you to transfer the
money to Shri Jamnalalji's account. Hit is not inconvenient will
you please do this on receipt of this letter? The purpose of writing
this letter is to save the interest on the amount. There is no
intention of keeping any money in Vallabhbhai's name at the
moment.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11122. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
