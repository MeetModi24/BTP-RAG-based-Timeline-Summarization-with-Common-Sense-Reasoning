131. LETTER TO JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
Air Mail
CALCUTTA,
August 29, 1947
DEAR DR. HOLMES 2 ,
Many thanks for yours of 11th instant.
I am forwarding your letter with enclosures to Punditji. It
is a sad story.
We are all looking forward to your arrival.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
KENNEBUNK BEACH
MAINE
NEW YORK, U. S. A.
From a photostat: C. W. 10969. Courtesy: Roger W. Holmes and Mrs.
Frances L. Brown
