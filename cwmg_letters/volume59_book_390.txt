390. LETTER TO V. BHASHYAM IYENGAR
November 14, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. Of course, I shall make full use
of your generous offer. I am passing on your letter to Sjt. Kuma-
rappa.
Yours sincerely,
DEWAN BAHADUR V. BHASHYAM IYENGAR
"VARDHANI"
KILPAUK, MADRAS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
