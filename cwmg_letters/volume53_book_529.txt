529. LETTER TO MUNI SHANTIVIJAYJI
February 24, 1933
MUNISHRI SHANTIVIJAYJI,
I have known N. through correspondence
since she
communicated to me her intention to fast by way of protest
against me. It was from her own letter that I learnt how she
regarded you as her all in all. As I heard certain allegations
against her while our correspondence was going on, I wrote to
her about them immediately and suggested that she should come
and see me. She did so immediately. It appears from our con-
versation that the allegations against her may be baseless. I have
been guiding her to an extent, but I think it to be my duty
to ascertain your wish before I give her any positive advice.
Hence this letter. I have written it with her knowledge. She will
remain here till your reply is received. I request you to have no
hesitation in making any suggestion you wish to do.
