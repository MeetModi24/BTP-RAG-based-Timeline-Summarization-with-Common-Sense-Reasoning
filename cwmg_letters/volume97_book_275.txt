275. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
WARDHA,
July 21, 1935
CHI. RAMDAS,
I wish you well. But the course you have adopted seems to me fraught
with danger. You have become impatient to make money and therefore are
not content with mere commission. Have patience. Get some experience. Calm
down. Be satisfied with a little. But then, one remembers one's master's counsel
only as far as the gate. 6 In the end you will go where your fate leads you.
This is but a caution by an old father.
1 Harilal Gandhi's daughter
2,3, & 5 A few words at these places are illegible in the source.
6 A Gujarati saying, meaning that it is futile to give advice to one who is not
willing to listen to it
