14. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
February 8, 1928
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
Your letter. Some digestible preparations can be made with
oil. But this experiment cannot be conducted from a distance. At
present fasting is the most essential and the best remedy for you.
I have no doubt about it.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6154. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
