276. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
April 27, 1929
CHI. GANGABEHN SENIOR,
You seem to have stopped writing now. I must have letters
from you, no matter whether they are foolish, whimsical, long or
short, so long as you pour out your heart in them.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro -6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 24
