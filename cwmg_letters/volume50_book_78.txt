78. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
June 20, 1932
MY DEAR BHRRR1,
You can't disappoint me even if you try. You may not
therefore allow such a thought to depress you. You have simply
to dance when Raihana sings. We are all glad you seem to be
nearing the end of earthly troubles.
Please send my salaams to the Hamid Alis. I am sure they
do not think you have spoilt their holiday by giving yourself well-
deserved rest on that beautiful hill. You will be surprised to know
that I have never been there.
Our love to you.
Yours,
BHRRR
From a photostat: S.N. 9578
