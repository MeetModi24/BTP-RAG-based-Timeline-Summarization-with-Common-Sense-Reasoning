345. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Silence day
CHI. VASUMATI,
Whatever changes have been made are appropriate. I am more
confident because Gangabehn has started staying with you. The fact
that Premabehn has mixed with everyone is a good thing according
to me.
The situation about the prayers would continue to remain the
same so long as it does not acquire life.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9315.
