333. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 27, 1930
CHI. MATHURADAS (TREASURER),
Have you understood me regarding the interviews?
The
Government has found my list too long. Now I shall not get
into further argument. Let us be content with exchange of letters.
You must take Tara Mashruwala who was injured while
picketing liquor shops, to Dr. Jivraj and have her examined for
head injury. I hope it has not left any permanent damage.
Tell Hansabehn 'Congratulations. May you live long.'
Blessings from
BAPU
