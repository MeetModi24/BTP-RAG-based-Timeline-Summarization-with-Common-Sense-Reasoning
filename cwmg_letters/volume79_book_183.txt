183. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI M. PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
February 6, 1945
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
Bhai Gokuldas is gone. Condole with the family on their
loss. Truly speaking, what is there to condole about such a
death? He has done a lot of work. I knew him well. He will
certainly be missed. It is the duty of those whom he leaves
behind to fill the void.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAOJIBHAI MANIBHAI PATEL
VITTHAL KANYA VIDYALAYA
NADIAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
