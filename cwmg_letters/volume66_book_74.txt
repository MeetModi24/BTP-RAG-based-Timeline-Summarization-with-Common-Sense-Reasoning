74. LETTER TO BHARATAN KUMARAPPA
August 19, 1937
DEAR BHARATAN,
I have your note about Rao. I got it during my walk at 7
a. m. This reply will go when Chhotelal leaves. You can come
either at I o'clock or at 4 p.m. today. Between 2 and 4 I
am fixed up. I hope you have taken all the papers and cash
from Rao. I should like to see him if he could be induced to
come.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3593
