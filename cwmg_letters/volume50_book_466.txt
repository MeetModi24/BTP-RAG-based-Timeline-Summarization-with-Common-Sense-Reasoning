466. LETTER TO SUNDERBEHN BHAGVAT
August 28, 1932
CHI. SUNDERBEHN 2 ,
I got your letter. All three of us send our blessings to you
and Deshpande . We hope that your union will be happy, that
both of you will live long and devote yourselves completely to
service. Only then will your union be said to have justified itself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2169
