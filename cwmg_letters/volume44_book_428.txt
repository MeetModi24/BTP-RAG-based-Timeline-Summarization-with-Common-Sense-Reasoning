428. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI PATEL
November 13, 1930
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
I came to know only from your postcard that you had been
released. Pyarelal had completely forgotten to tell me about it.
How did you get scabies? It is a disease which is in fact quite
easy to cure. I hope you observe proper restrictions in your diet.
Write to me and tell me how long you were in the temple, and
describe your experience in it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9501
