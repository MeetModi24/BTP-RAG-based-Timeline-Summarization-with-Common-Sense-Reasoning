149. LETTER TO D. V. GOKHALE
July 2, 1934
DEAR MR. GOKHALE,
I thank you for your letter. I do hope that Mr. Bhopat-
kar has no trace left of the scratches and other injuries received
by him.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MR. D. V. GOKHALE
568 NARAYAN PETH
POONA 2
From a photostat: G.N. 6105; also C.W. 9713
