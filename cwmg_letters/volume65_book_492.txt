492. LETTER TO WANDA DYNOWSKA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 20, 1937
DEAR UMA,
I was glad to have your descriptive letter. I hope you are
keeping sound health during your wandering. You will come
when you like and stay in Segaon as long as you feel happy.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI UMADEVI
C/O SHRI M. FRYDMAN
MYSORE ROAD, BANGALORE CITY
From a photostat: G.N. 1200.
Also C.W. 5094. Courtesy: Wanda
Dynowska
