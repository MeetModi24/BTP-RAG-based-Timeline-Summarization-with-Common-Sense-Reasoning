361. LETTER TO DR. B. R. CHUGH 3
May 2. 1945
G[andhiji] says he started the course only yesterday. It will last at least for
10 days. After that it will be better to let the climate do its work. It seems to agree
with him so far. The present course deals only with amoebic-hookworm. The tremor
of the hands and general weakness will still require attention unless the climate does
all these things. The best time to test your treatment will be after G.'s descent to
the plains.
From the original: Pyarelal Papers, Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
