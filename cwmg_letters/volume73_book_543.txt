543. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 24, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
Your little note.
You never doubted the welcome you were to receive there.
I did not. I hope Tofa remembers us all. I know your love of
flowers. But I cannot give you satisfaction here. The place is much
too disorganized for sporting flower-beds.
Harijan will be out on 5th April unless some mishap pre-
vents it. Probably it will be Ahmedabad. Chandrashanker seems
to have neglected his duty to an unforgivable extent.
I am hoping that he has not been wilfully dishonest. But
neglect such as he seems to have shown produces almost the same
result.
I am keeping well in spite of the heat.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Amtul Salaam is doing well. No asthma.
From the original: C.W. 4004. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7313
