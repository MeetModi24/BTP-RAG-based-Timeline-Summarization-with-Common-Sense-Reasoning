360. LETTER TO MAHARAJA OF DARBHANGA
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your telegram redirected from Borsad. I have
not seen the landholders' manifesto referred to by you. If you
will kindly send it to me I shall see what can be done.
Yours sincerely,
MAHARAJA OF DARBHANGA
From a photostat: S.N. 17194
