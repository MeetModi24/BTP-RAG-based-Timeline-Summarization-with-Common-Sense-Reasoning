427. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
July 5, 1937
CHI. MANILAL-SUSHILA,
I got your letter. This will be carried by Ramdas. You
two brothers may discuss the problem and do what you think
best. I want to see Ramdas's physique as strong as it was be-
fore. Today I have no time to write more. And where is the
need, when two persons are going there just after meeting me?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4865
