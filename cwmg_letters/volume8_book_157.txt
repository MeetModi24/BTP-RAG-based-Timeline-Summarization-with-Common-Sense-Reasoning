157. LETTER TO E. F. C. LANE
JOHANNESBURG,
June 3, 1908
DEAR MR. LANE,
I wrote to you on Saturday, asking for General Smuts' permission
to publish correspondence that has passed between us and was marked
"confidential" in connection with the Asiatic Act. May I now have a
reply?
I am,
Yours truly,
ERNEST F. C. LANE, ESQ.
PRETORIA
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4819
