264. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
ON THE TRAIN TO WARDHA,
October 6, 1939
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I have Sushila's letter. No matter what happens and how
it happens, we must patiently and cheerfully bear it and do our
duty. The letter for Medh is enclosed. Knowing that my Diwali
greetings may not reach you with this, I intend to send a few
words by cable. Have the two organizations there come together?
Manilal and the children must be well now.
We shall reach
Wardha this evening.
The accompanying is for Medh.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4906
