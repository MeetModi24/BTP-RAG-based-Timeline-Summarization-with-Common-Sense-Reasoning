400. LETTER TO JAGANNATH
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 13, 1929
MY DEAR JAGANNATH,
I have your letter. I expect that Manilal will be going to
Sind. I do not know where he is at the present moment. I leave
on the 31st instant for Karachi. It would be better to send some-
one on behalf of the Society in advance.
As to my visit to the Punjab for the opening ceremony, etc.,
in April, please ask me later. I am awaiting a communication from
Pandit Motilalji before I can decide.
Yours sincerely,
LALA JAGANNATH
CARE PEOPLE
COURT STREET, CALCUTTA
