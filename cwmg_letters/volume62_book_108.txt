108. LETTER TO NIRMAL KUMAR BOSE
WARDHA,
November 6, 1935
DEAR NIRMAL BABU,
Ghani tells me that ten rupees out of his account are missing
and that you propose to reimburse the loss from your pocket.
That is not to be thought of for a single minute. Such things
will always happen especially where Ghani is involved. He has
been brought up in the lap of luxury. He is a spoilt child of his
family. You must not, therefore, worry over the loss. Surely it is
enough that you have given him your precious time and bestowed
on him such extraordinary care.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. NIRMAL KUMAR BOSE
6/IA BRITISH INDIAN STREET, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: G.N. 10521-a
