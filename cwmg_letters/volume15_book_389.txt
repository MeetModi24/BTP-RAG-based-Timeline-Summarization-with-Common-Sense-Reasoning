389. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
Thursday, Ashadh 1975 [July 3, 1919]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I forgot to tell you that the man who has arrived from Bagasra
should be asked not only to teach but also to look after weaving.
Look after Shankarlal properly. Tell him all about the work.
I have told Ba that he should have greens and other things with
his meals.
Tell Kishorelal that I have sent him a loaf today for Surendra.
Thinking that Balubhai may not send it in time, I send one today.
If needed in future, I shall be able to send more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 6716
