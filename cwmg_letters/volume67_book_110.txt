110. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
May 15, 1938
CHI. SUSHILA,
If you wish, I would take you and the children, and Manu
also, to Delhi. You may stay there as long as you like.
BAPU
[PS.]
God has given enough to Surendra. And you have an inde-
pendent hoard of your own. Moreover, boarding will be free.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1570. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
