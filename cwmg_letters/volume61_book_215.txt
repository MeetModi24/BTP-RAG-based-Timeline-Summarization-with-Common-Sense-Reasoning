215. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
June 9, 1935
CHI. VIJAYA,
It is but right that you should pursue your studies instead
of coming here to labour.
Even if either husband or wife desires their relations to be
like those of a brother and sister but cannot live like that in the
same house, it will be their dharma to separate.
Blessings from
BAPU
VIJAYABEHN NARANBHAI PATEL
VARAD, via BARDOLI, TAPTI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7058. Also C.W. 4550. Courtesy:
