428. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA
April 11, 1933
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRA,
I thank you for your postcard. I see that you are rendering
whatever service it is possible to render to N. Let's hope that she
will pass through the fire all right and come out all the purer
for the ordeal.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20897
