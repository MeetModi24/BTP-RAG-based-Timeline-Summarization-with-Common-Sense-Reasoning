282. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
July 3, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
You may not cable Amery. Let things shape themselves.
Shivarao need not worry. Read my "Appeal to Every Briton"
and do lobbying among the Britons. Don't expect letters from
me now. You should write. I shall see that someone writes to
you. I must reserve writing for the papers.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3979. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7288
