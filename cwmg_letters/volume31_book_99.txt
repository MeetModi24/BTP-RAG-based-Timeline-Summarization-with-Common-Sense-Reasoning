99. LETTER TO SHALIGRAM SHASTRI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 3, 1926
BHAI SHALIGRAM SHASTRI,
Your letter to hand. Also received your telegram. You must
have got my telegram informing you that Harihar Sharma was
going back and that you might fix any date after August 16. I
can well understand the reason for your not coming at this time.
I send you a copy of the scheme which Pandit Harihar
Sharma has drawn up in consultation with the local workers. Please
study it and place it before the committee if necessary, and obtain
their opinion also on this.
From a microfilm of the Hindi: S.N. 19650
