24. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
April 25, 1941
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
What is this? Why should exertion lay you low? You know
the trick. When there is undue exertion fast partially or wholly.
Partial fast means living on juicy fruit only. When there is fever
fast is obligatory. I wish you would be soon in Coonoor.
I am glad you were able to go to Kodambakkam.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10152
