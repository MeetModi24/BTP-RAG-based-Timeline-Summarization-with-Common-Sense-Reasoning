438. LETTER TO P. C. GHOSH
SEVAGRAM,
March 16, 1945
CHI. PROFULLA,
Dr. Mitra's scheme is not for the villages. Probably he does
not even know the villages. This shows that merely being clever
is no use to us. I will reach Bombay on the 31st. It will be
good if you come there. It has become very hot here now.
Blessings from
BAPU
P. C. GHOSH
1418 GARIAHUT ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
