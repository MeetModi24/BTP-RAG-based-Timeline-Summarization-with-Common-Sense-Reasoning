381. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
DELHI,
March 20, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
As promised, I send you translations of a circular and a
notice issued by the Mamlatdar of Borsad. They speak for them-
selves. They are, in my opinion, clearly contrary to the terms of
the settlement.
Yours sincerely,
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
