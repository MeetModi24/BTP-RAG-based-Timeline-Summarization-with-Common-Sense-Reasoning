334. LETTER TO F. MART BARR
August 16, 1934
CHI. MARY,
I had your note. Thank God, the fast went off well though
not without causing acute discomfort the last day. I am picking
up well. Jamnalalji is in Bombay. He underwent the ear opera-
tion today quite successfully. I am here for some days. Mira is
working away in England. Do write regularly.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MARY BARR
KHEDI, NEAR BETUL, C. P.
From a photostat: G.N. 6025. Also C.W. 3354. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
