108. LETTER TO REGINALD REYNOLDS
ALIGARH,
November 4, 1929
MY DEAR REYNOLDS,
Another good letter from you.
You shall certainly learn the other things you wish to. If you
have strength of body and freshness of mind, I would like you
to learn as much of all the activities as you possibly can.
Mr. Silcock who knows you and Mr. Wigham motored with
me yesterday from Delhi to Aligarh.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 4527. Courtesy: Swarthmore College, Philadelphia
