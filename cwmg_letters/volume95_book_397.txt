397. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
Tuesday
DEAR SISTER,
The courier brought your letter as I was about to set out for a
walk with our guest. If you can send the man tomorrow, that is, on
Wednesday, I shall let you know my opinion after seeing the letter.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W.11157. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
