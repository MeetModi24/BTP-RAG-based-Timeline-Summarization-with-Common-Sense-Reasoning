617. LETTER TO KUSUM NAIR
BOMBAY,
April 8, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I hope you will be able to read my hand. I have shown
you the way to true success. Work hard, be unpretentious and
self-reliant. That is the secret of success in work.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KUSUM NAIR
N. G. P. P. BUREAU
73 LAKSHMI BUILDING
SIR P. MEHTA ROAD
BOMBAY 1
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
