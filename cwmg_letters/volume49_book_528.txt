528. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
May 15, 1932
CHI. MAHAVIR,
I got your letter. I cannot understand why you did not get
mine. In future I will send through the Ashram my letters to
you. All of you also should send your letters to me through
the same channel. I hope the money has been sent to you. Please
drop the idea of going to Kalimpong. If all of you keep good
health, you may remain where you are. And everyone in the
family should take up some work. What is everybody's pro-
gramme during the day? Do all of you keep a correct diary?
How is it that I have had no letter from
Maitri? Has
Krishnamaiyadevi recovered now? With whom do you stay?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6235
