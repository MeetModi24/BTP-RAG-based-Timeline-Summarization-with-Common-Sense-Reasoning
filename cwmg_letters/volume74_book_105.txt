105. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
May 21, 1941
CHI. DEVDAS,
I have sent Mahadev today to Ahmedabad. There is much
fear there. Most probably Mahadev will reach Simla. If you
can stay here till the 27th, he will meet you here itself. Other-
wise you will meet direct in Simla.
Most probably I will
have all of you here in Sevagram on the 24th. But I shall see.
Ba must be fine. Tell Lilavatibehn that I have her postcard. I got
Ramdas's letter also. All will meet here on the 24th. Durgabehn
has gone to Bombay to undergo clinical examination.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2138
