400. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
GAUHATI,
April 14, 1934
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I got up this morning at 12.15 in order to overtake arrears.
You have been ever in my mind. I hope you will be per-
mitted to receive this letter. I would like a line from you to
tell me how you are keeping and what you are doing.
You must have seen my two decisions.
That they are almost
simultaneous is a mere coincidence. The revival of the Swarajya
Party is a right step. There is no doubt that we have in the
Congress a body of men who believe in Council-entry and who
will do nothing else if they cannot have that programme.
Their
ambition must be satisfied. The other decision about confining
C. R. to myself so far as the goal is concerned is much the most
important. It was inevitable. Having arrived at it, I can see
the rightness of the decision for a thousand reasons.
I have
given the precipitating cause. But the decision was slowly coming
to me. I do hope that you have not been upset by it. You were
before my mind's eye the whole time the decision was taking shape.
I concluded that, though it might produce a momentary shock, you
would ultimately see the truth of it and be glad. I wonder!
We all often talk about you. We are a large party. I was
with Mother and the members of the family for nearly two hours
when I passed through Allahabad.
Love.
BAPU
