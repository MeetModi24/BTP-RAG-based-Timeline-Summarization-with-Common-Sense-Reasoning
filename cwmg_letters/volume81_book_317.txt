317. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
POONA,
August 30, 1945
CHI. PRABHA,
I do not at all remember whether I have written to you.
Your letter of the 1st is lying in front of me. I am writing this
after the morning prayer. I hope you keep good health. You
may come when you can. Just now I shall have to stay with
Sardar in Poona. I may have to be here for three months.
After that I shall be touring. You should stay in the Ashram.
If there is suitable work for you there and you enjoy peace of
mind and keep good health, settle down there. Do as you please.
How is Father?
Blessings from
BAPU
