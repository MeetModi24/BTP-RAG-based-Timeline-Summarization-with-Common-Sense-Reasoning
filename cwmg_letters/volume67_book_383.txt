383. LETTER TO PARVATIDEVI GHORPADE
August 20, 1938
DEAR SISTER,
I learnt about you from Bhai Kaujalgi's letter to Gandhi Seva
Sangh and also read the account of the concessions that you
have given to the people of your State. I compliment you on
your generosity. It is worthy of you. May God make you more
generous still and grant you long life.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7454
