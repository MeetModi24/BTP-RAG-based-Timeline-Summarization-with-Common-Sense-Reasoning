119. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
MATHURA,
November 6, 1929
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I have your letter. If the lady wants to have some expe-
rience of life at the Ashram she can certainly do so. If she
arrives will she come single or will it be the couple?
I have
already sent the Introduction that Vaijnath had asked for. Till
now my health has been quite all right. I hope to leave Agra for
Sabarmati on the 24th evening via metre gauge. The 25th falls
on a Monday. I hope you are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 6068. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
