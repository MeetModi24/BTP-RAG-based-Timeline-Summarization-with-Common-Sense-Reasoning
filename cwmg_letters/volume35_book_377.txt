377. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD MISHRA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
January 26, 1928
BHAI RAJENDRA PRASADJI MISHRA,
Your son has come to me and says that, although he and his
wife wish that she should give up purdah, you oppose this step.
He asks me what his duty is. I have told him that, for the pres-
ent, he should obey you, and engage a tutoress for his wife. One
can be sent from here. I would advise you to allow the couple
to act in accordance with their own wishes. In this age purdah
is not practicable, nor is it necessary. In ancient times this evil
custom did not exist.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
