216. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 3, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
Your two letters today.
I do take note of things that go on. Of course many
things will happen. A big system will not die without much
effort.
It is still terribly hot here. There is no sign of the wind
changing. I am not likely to call you before the end of this
month. There is no need for you to worry about anything at
this end.
Abha is going to Rajkot, Laxmi to her husband and Vasu-
mati to Ahmedabad.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4127. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7436
