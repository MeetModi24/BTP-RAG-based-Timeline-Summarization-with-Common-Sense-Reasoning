504. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 22, 1937
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Maulana Saheb stopped for a day in Wardha and we had
a long chat. He showed me the draft agreement between Mus-
lim League members of the Assembly and the Congress members.
I thought it was a good document. But he told me that whilst you
liked it, Tandonji did not. I have written to the latter about it
as the Maulana suggested I should. What is the objection?
The Rs. 500 salary with big house and car allowances is
being severely criticized. The more I think of it, the more I
dislike this extravagant beginning. I talked about this, too, to the
Maulana.
How is Indu?
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1937. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Also A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 236
