70. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
WARDHA,
October 14, 1935
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Here is a telegram from New York and the reply to it.
Please copy it out on a telegraph form and send it. We do not
have to pay any money as you will see. It is possible that as
the cable went to Sabarmati the money also went there. But
there should be some authority at Bombay Central. Of course,
they may not accept it because the 14th is already over. Even
then there is no harm. In that case we would be spared the
trouble. We do not want to spend any money. If it cannot be
done, return the telegram and the reply.
You will be well. Taramati and the children too will be
well. Do not ask how burdened I am.
Blessings from
BAPU
