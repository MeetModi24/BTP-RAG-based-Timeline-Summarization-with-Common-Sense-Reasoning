150. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
December 12, 1936
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I am very happy that Tulsidas's Ramayana is being translated
into Tamil by you. Your noble effort will enable the Tamil-
knowing people to have the benefit of the unrivalled prasadi
of Tulsidas.
Blessings from
BAPU
