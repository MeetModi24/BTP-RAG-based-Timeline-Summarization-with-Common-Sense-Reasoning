57. LETTER TO R. K. SIDHWA
[Before October 22, 1941]
The responsibility for the stoppage of satyagraha in Sind is
Maulana Azad's and ultimately mine. I shall not grant per-
mission to any Sindhi friend to offer satyagraha without the per-
mission of the Maulana.
I can safely state that neither you nor any other Congressman
from Sind has any hand in the stoppage of satyagraha in Sind.
The Hindu, 23-10-1941
