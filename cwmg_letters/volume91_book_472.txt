472. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday [April 2, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your telegram. Surely it does not mean that your
health has taken a turn for the worse?
Blessings from
BAPU
SF. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
WINDY HALL
NASIK ROAD
DEOLALI (G.I.P.)
