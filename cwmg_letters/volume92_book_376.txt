376. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 27, 1930
CHI. SHANKERLAL,
I have received your letter; also Anasuyabehn's. We do
not know when our real New Year starts. Or shall we say that
it has started on the 12th March? For the time being, I am
spinning on a takli. But I do not like such a slow speed. In
Wardha they spin 132 rounds in half an hour. 80 rounds are quite
common. If you have people who are experts in spinning on
the takli, let them write to me about that art of spinning. I have
of course asked for the instructions from Wardha. Instructions
from two or three places would be more than sufficient. I have
acquired good speed in spinning on the charkha. I think I can
still improve.
This is the second urgent letter to you. Harjivandas writes
from Amreli that he is not getting certificate for [his] khadi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32729
