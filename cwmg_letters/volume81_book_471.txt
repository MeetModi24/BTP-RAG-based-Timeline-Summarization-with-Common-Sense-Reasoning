471. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
September 19, 1945
CHI. PRABHA,
You are silly. What was so private in your letter? My plans
are a little uncertain. For the present stay on there and read
what you can. Let me know what you read. I intend to go to
Bengal in October. Meet me then. In the mean time I am
sure you will continue to write to me. See that you keep in good
health. You must serve on the Kasturba Committee.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 3582
