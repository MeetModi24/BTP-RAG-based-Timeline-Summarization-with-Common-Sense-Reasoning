120. LETTER TO JETHALAL JOSHI
July 29, 1928
BHAISHRI JETHALAL,
It is certainly not a fast if you eat fruits. The aim behind a
fast may be purification of the body or of the mind or of both.
A fast contributes very little to purification of the mind. You will
find the replies to the other questions in Niyamavali.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1352
