189. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
August 13, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
What is there so confidential about your letter? You have
not understood my point. It is as well. I suggest that you show
your letter to Munnalal. If that is the case he should withdraw
himself. Anything done just for my sake is to be regarded as
futile. Anything that is done has to be right. Only then can it
do good.
That was my view about the management. Since I am not
aware of everything, the final decision should be taken by those
who know all the facts.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4522
