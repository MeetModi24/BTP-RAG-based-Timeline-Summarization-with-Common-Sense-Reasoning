35. LETTER TO DR. DALAL
THE ASHRAM,
Monday, June 21, 1926
BHAISHRI,
Girdhari writes today that he has been discharged [from
the hospital]. May I then write a word of thanks? I know
writing minimizes the value. You and I are both busy. Your
time should not be wasted.
These youths and I can perhaps
repay your services to some extent by devoting ourselves to the
service of the country. Devdas has said a lot about your simpli-
city and I am very happy to hear it all.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
DR. DALAL
CHOWPATTY
BOMBAY
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19919
