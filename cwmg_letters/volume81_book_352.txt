352. LETTER TO UPENDRA CHOWDHARY
September 1, 1945
BHAI UPENDRA 2 ,
I have your letter. What I have suggested should be imple-
mented. Of course your sister-in-law has my blessings. Let her
render much service. That alone is real mourning and shraddha.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI UPENDRA CHOWDHARY
PIPRA
P. O. KESHAVNAGAR
MONGHYR (BIHAR)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
