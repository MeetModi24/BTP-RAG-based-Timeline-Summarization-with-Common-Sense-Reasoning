611. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
October 9, 1938
CHI. BABUDI,
You have learnt well how to write business-like letters. Not
even a word more than necessary. What a dull letter!
We have reached Utmanzai today. I am continuing my
silence.
We are all fine here. Ba is suffering a lot in Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 10002. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokhawala
