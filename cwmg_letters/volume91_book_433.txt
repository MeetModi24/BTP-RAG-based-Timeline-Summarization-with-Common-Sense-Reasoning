433. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Paush Sud 3, Friday [December 18, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Your two letters arrived together today. If Dr. Dalal goes
there again, you must see him. Tell his wife about it in advance.
Who is the doctor there? It would be proper not to sit up even
for writing so long as the pain persists. The pain will subside on
its own. Be out in the sun morning and evening as much as
possible. You must definitely take enema or a purgative if your
bowels do not move. I think enema would be better. But consult
the doctor and do as he says. Girdhari will of course stay with
you till I send somebody else. I intend returning to the Ashram
early. But I can know only after going to Kanpur. You must
always dictate to Girdhari your letters to me.
If Devdas is surprised by anything I do, so are you bound
to be.
Let me know if I should write to Taramati at any particular
address.
Blessings from
BAPU
