190. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
NAINITAL,
May 18, 1931
BHAI FULCHAND,
Pattani Saheb writes to tell me that at the Bhavnagar
session you had resolutions passed on the policy of the Jamnagar
State, and also indulged in criticism of that State. This is against
our policy. If you agree with this view, you should write in the
matter to Pattaniji. Bhavnagar gives us many facilities and I
do not wish that they should be misused.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2843. Courtesy: Shardabehn Shah
