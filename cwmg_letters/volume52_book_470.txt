470. LETTER TO DHARMA VIR VEDALANKAR
January 3, 1933
MY DEAR DHARMA VIR,
I was glad to receive your letter and cuttings giving a des-
cription of the various activities that took place on the Shrad-
dhanand Day.
Yours sincerely,
PT. DHARMA VIR VEDALANKAR
SHRADDHANAND BAZAR, DELHI
From a microfilm: S.N. 19148
