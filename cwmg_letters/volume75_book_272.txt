272. LETTER TO JAFAR HASAN
BARDOLI,
January 3, 1942
MY DEAR JAFAR HASAN,
Your letter.
My address was written and distributed. My remarks in the
course of the discussion could only be in the same strain as my
address. My address was published in the papers too. There is
nothing in that address to warrant the Maulana's criticism.
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
