204. LETTER TO KAMALA AND VASANTI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 12, 1945
CHI. KAMALA AND VASANTI 2 ,
I have letters from both of you. They are good. You are
doing good work. There is no need to feel unhappy. Donald
Greene will get well.
Keep fit and render lots of service.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KAMALABEHN
FRIENDS' SETTLEMENT, RASANIA
HOSHANGABAD DISTRICT
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
