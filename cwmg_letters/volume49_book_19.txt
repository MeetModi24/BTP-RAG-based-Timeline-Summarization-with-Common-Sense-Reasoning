19. LETTER TO PADMA
January 15, 1932
CHI. PADMA,
Vasumatibehn told me that you had not fully recovered.
Write to me and give me detailed news about your health. Where
is Sarojinidevi at present? And how is she? Do you get any
news about Father? Do you write to him? Tell me what work
you do every day.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6128. Also C.W. 3480. Courtesy:
Prabhudas Gandhi
