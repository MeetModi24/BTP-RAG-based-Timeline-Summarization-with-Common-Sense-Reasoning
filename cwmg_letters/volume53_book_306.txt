306. LETTER TO MOHANLAL M. BHATT
February 3, 1933
The world is not a carefully drawn geometrical figure but is
a supreme work of art painted by the brush of a mysterious artist,
whose significance is known only to the artist and will ever remain
unknown to us. That leaves only desireless effort to us.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, p. 113
