404. LETTER TO GANGABEHN MEGHJI
ANDHERI,
Wednesday [May 7, 1924]
PU. 2 GANGABEHN,
I have your letter. I was glad to know that you would be
going to the Ashram shortly.
Kaka should certainly visit your place when he goes to
Bombay next time. Tell him this when you go to the Ashram.
I hope you have reduced your self-imposed burden of dispens-
ing medicines.
I am writing to Ba about your intending to go to the Ashram.
I, too, will go there at the end of this month.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Gangaswarup Gangabehnne, p. 4
