266. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 10, 1936 1
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Khan Saheb has received the usual notice to attend the
meetings in Bombay. He has no desire to attend them and I do
not want to press him. And in Bombay he will be asked to attend
meetings and functions at which he would be called upon to speak.
I do not want him to do so just now. I want him rather to pass the
year with me. And he is none too strong nor invulnerable to attacks
of illness. Will you, therefore, please excuse him from attendance?
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1934. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
