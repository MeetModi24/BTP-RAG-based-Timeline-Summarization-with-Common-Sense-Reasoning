714. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 23, 1945
CHI. TULSI,
How is it that you again got fever? You had my blessings,
you had khakhra from me. Will all this go in vain? You will
never fall ill if you follow the rules of health. The most im-
portant rule is to utter Ramanama from the heart. The other
rules are comparatively easier.
Blessings from
BAPU
