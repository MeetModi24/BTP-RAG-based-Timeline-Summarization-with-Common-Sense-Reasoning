503. LETTER TO UPTON SINCLAIR
December 6, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I was thankful to hear from you after a long time. You have
mailed your books to Ahmedabad.
My headquarters just now
are Ashram, Wardha. Therefore probably the books are still
somewhere in transit. I am looking forward to receiving both
The Cry for Justice and Epic Campaign. If you don't mind, I would
like you to send a fresh parcel so that if the parcel already sent
miscarries I may not be without the books.
Yours sincerely,
UPTON SINCLAIR, ESQ.
STATION A. PASADENA
CALIFORNIA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
