443. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
CUDDAPAH,
4 a.m., Before Prayers, January 2, 1934
CHI. MANI,
I do not know whether I shall be able to hear directly from
you now. I do get news of you from Father, but that is not
enough to satisfy me. I, therefore, write to Dahyabhai for more.
Write to me if you can. Keep your body and mind fit. I am
doing well enough. I write a long letter to Ba every week as
a regular thing. Nothing more today.
Address your letters to Wardha.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MANIBEHN PATEL, PRISONER
HINDALGA CENTRAL PRISON
BELGAUM
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 116
