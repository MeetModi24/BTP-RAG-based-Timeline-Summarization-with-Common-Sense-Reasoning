501. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
September 10, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your letter in perfect Hindi.
Sarojini went yesterday—as chirpy as ever. She was in-
teresting without being instructive. What new things could she
tell?
Sarup is still here. She leaves tomorrow for Allahabad. She
tells me J2 & R3 are both keeping well. She herself is pulled
down but is otherwise fit.
Annapurna is better. Rajen Babu came in today with his
wife.
I have not met him yet. He is resting in Wardha. He
is well though weak.
Mahadev won't be here till he has finished Ahmedabad.
They want me to send you to Alwar on 1st October. I
have sent a flat refusal.
If you were well I would certainly
have sent you, but there is no knowing when you will be quite
fit. When you return it might be well to bring someone with you.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4069. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7378
