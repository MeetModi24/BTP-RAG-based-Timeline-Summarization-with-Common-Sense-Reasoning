485. LETTER TO CHAMANLAL
SEVAGRAM,
March 7, 1941
DEAR CHAMANLAL,
I must look at your book and send you something.
Yes, I would like you for the present to concentrate on Hari-
jan work.
Offer your services to Mohanlal of Lalaji's Society.
He is in charge of that work on behalf of the Harijan Sevak Sangh.
No one works separately in my name. Put yourself also in touch
with Shri Rameshwari Nehru whom you must know. Later on, if all
is well you will offer civil disobedience, if required. You should
spin regularly, make your own slivers and learn the science of the
charkha.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
