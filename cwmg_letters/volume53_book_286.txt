286. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
February 1, 1933
DEAR MR. JAYAKAR,
Herewith copy of my letter to the Private Secretary to the
Viceroy. I hope I have correctly set forth your advice. If you
could directly write to the Viceroy, I would ask you please to do so.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Home Department, Political, File No. 50/II/33, p. 6. Courtesy: National
Archives of India. Also S.N. 19279
