93. LETTER TO JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
ANAND,
March 17, 1930
CHI. JAYAPRAKASH,
Nowhere else have I observed such zeal for sacrifice as has
been displayed by the Ashram women. At present the women
are to a great extent managing the internal affairs of the Ashram.
The chance of acquiring such experience will never be repeated.
I would therefore advise you to send Prabhavati there. After my
arrest the Ashram women too will court imprisonment. I think
Prabhavati should join them. She is worthy in every respect. I
hope your work is proceeding well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3362
