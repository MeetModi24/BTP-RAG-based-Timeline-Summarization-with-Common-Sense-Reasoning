622. LETTER TO RAMA RAJU
March 4, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. Of course I would gladly dis-
cuss the problem of untouchability with Pandit Krishnamacharya
if he could possibly visit Yeravda.
The difference between Malaviyaji and myself is as you pro-
perly say unfortunate, but since the difference was there it is as
well that it has come out. It promotes healthy public opinion.
Yours sincerely,
RAMA RAJU
MASULIPATAM
From a microfilm: S.N. 20453
