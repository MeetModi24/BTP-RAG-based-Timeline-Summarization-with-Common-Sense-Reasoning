501. LETTER TO NORA MORELL
December 6, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. It was received
some time ago, but pressure of work has prevented my replying
earlier.
The best way you, living in Germany, can serve India is to
befriend those Indians who are living in Germany, and secondly
to live up to the doctrines that you approve.
Yours sincerely,
MADAME NORA MORELL
BERLIN-WILMERSDORF
RUDESHEIMER PLATZ 6
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
