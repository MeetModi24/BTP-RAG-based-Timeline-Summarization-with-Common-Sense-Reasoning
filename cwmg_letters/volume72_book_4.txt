4. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
SEVAGRAM,
April 18, 1940
CHI. PREMA,
I have your letter, as also the pad. It would have been
better if you had written to me about the fast before under-
taking it. I might not then have tried to dissuade you, but
would have, instead, shown you how to use it in a better way.
I hope you are returning to normal food gradually. Your letter
is incomplete. You have not said all that you should have said.
That is not right. Write to me now if you can. If you want to
come and talk things over personally, you may come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10406. Also C.W. 6845. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
