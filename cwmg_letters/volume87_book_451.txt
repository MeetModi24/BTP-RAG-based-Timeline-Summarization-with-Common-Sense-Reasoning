451. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
PATNA,
May 17, 1947
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
Your note . I must deny myself the pleasure of going to
Mussoorie. I am quite fixed up here and can easily delay
coming to Delhi till 31st May or even a day later.
Give my love to all at Mussoorie and take full rest whilst
you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the original: Jawaharlal Nehru Papers (M. O. Mathai). Courtesy:
Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
