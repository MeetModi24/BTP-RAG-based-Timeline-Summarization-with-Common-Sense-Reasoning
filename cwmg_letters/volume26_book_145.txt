145. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
ON THE WAY,
Phagan Sud 11 [March 6, 1925]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL 2 ,
If the man from Ankleshwar comes, give him his ring and ask
him to write to me if he wants to say anything. Do not allow
him to stay at all in the Ashram. The fellow is obviously mad.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
You must have sent the Rs. 5,000 of Wadhwan. Do the needful
about the cheques for Rs. 4,000.
CHI. CHHAGANLAL
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 10245
