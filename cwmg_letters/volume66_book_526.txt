526. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
March 28, 1938
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I have already sent the answers to your questions. Try to
discover why you keep good health in Bombay without any effort
while you barely manage to do so in Segaon. Is the cause
psychological or external? If it is external, is it the food and
water or the climate, or both? If it is food, you can eat the
same food in Segaon as you do in Bombay. If the reason is psy-
chological, [the cure] is in your own hands. If it is the climate
and water, we are helpless. But I don't think that they count
so much.
The date of my return will be decided in Calcutta.
Blessings from
BAPU
