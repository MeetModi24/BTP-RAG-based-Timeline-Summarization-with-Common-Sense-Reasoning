583. LETTER TO NARMADABEHN RANA
November 6, 1932
CHI. NARMADA,
I got your letter. You should put a point at the end of every
sentence. It is called full stop.
You wrote utasva. The correct word is utsav. You wrote
pootri. It should be putri. You wrote svikarsho. It should be
sveekarsho.
Please do not feel unhappy that Panditji has stopped your
training in music. You should tell yourself that he must have some
reason for doing that and accept his decision.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 2766. Courtesy: Ramanarayan N. Pathak
