251. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 19, 1938
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
These two friends belong to the Quilon Bank. They wish
to tell you their story and also want your advice. Give them
some time. It is a sad story.
They want to see Sir Purushottamdas also.
I have given
them a note.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 222
