139. LETTER TO AMRITLAL KHETSI
BOMBAY,
Phagan Sud 10 [March 5, 1925]
BHAISHRI AMRITLAL,
I am sorry to learn that Chi. Rami is ill. While I am at
Vykom keep me informed about her. Tell her to write to me as
soon as she gets well.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
BHAISHRI
AMRITLAL KHETSI
NEAR KANYASHALA
DHRANGADHRA
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 677. Courtesy: Navajivan Trust
