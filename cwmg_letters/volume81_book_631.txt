631. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
October 14, 1945
CHI. KISHORELAL,
Are you determined to die? Convince me if there is any logic
in your thinking so that I too may follow you. Now it is high
time [you listened to me]. Whoever writes, writes only about your
getting weaker and weaker. How can I make you understand?
What can I do if my going there is getting postponed?
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
