278. LETTER TO R. E. HAWKINS
SEVAGRAM,
September 4, 1944
DEAR MR. HAWKINS,
Do please see me in Bombay. I hope to reach on 9th. You will
arrange the time when I reach there. You won't feel disappointed if
I cannot find the time. I should be obliged if we can discover a way
out.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
THE GENERAL MANAGER
THE OXFORD UNIVERSITY PRESS
B. I. BUILDING
NICOL ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
