437. LETTER TO EDMOND AND YVONNE PRIVAT
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 15, 1940
MY DEAR ANAND AND BHAKTI,
It was a delight to hear from you after such a long lapse of
time. Yes, you are passing through most anxious times. There must
come light out of this darkness.
Love.
BAPU
MONS. EDMOND PRIVAT
SAN BIAGIO
LOCARNO (TESSIN)
SWITZERLAND
From a photostat: G.N. 8801
72-25
