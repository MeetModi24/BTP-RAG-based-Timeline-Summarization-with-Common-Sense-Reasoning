465. LETTER TO M. W. H. DE SILVA
BHANGI COLONY,
READING ROAD, NEW DELHI,
September 24, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. Please do not think of a gar-
den party for anyone, much less for the charkha class people.
They do not come here now. Moreover, I am averse to en-
couraging entertainment being provided to anyone at this time
of scarcity. Every morsel of food saved is so much food gained.
Nevertheless, your good wishes I shall always treasure.
M. W. H. DE SILVA
CEYLON GOVERNMENT REPRESENTATIVE IN INDIA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
