310. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 11, 1940
DEAR MALKANI,
Nothing can be spared just now from A.I.S.A. I am trying
to get some funds. You must do what you can there. Yours, I
know, is a difficult task. If the Hindi Prachar work is too much
for you, you should reject it.
Love.
BAPU
PROF. N. MALKANI
TILAK CONGRESS BHAVAN
HYDERABAD, SIND
From a photostat: G.N. 939
