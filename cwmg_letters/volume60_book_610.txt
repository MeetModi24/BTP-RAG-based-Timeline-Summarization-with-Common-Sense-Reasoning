610. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
April 19, 1935
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
I got your letter. I have asked for your opinion about Hari-
bhai. What is your wish? I didn't approve of his having spent so
much. All the same, your opinion will help me to come to a
decision.
Now about the Udyoga Mandir money. I don't remember
anything about the matter. Write to Narandas and ask if he knows.
He will be there at the end of the month.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4030
