20. LETTER TO H. B. TEJUMAL
[November 12, 1926]
DEAR FRIEND,
To quote from scriptures will not help you. Draupadi's prayer
is a celebrated instance. If one has faith in one's prayer, I have
not a shadow of a doubt in my mind that it can move mountains.
Faith and proof are contradictory acts. Hence illustrations are of
little avail. The only thing is to pray whether one gets an
answer to one's prayer or not. Prayer should never be directed
to a selfish object.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. H. B.
TEJUMAL
MEDICAL PRACTITIONER
NEW SUKKUR
SIND
From a photostat: S.N. 19731
