301. LETTER TO FRAMROZE B. GARDA
BARDOLI,
May 30, 1931
DEAR SARDAR GARDA,
I have your letter. If you will not accept arbitration in the
matter of your complaints, I do not know how to give you satis-
faction. The matter must now pass into lawyers' hands and if they
advise that the letter written by you undertaking to restore the
lands in question to the original holders against the considerati-
on named by you gives sufficient cause of action, law must take
its course.
Yours sincerely,
SARDAR FRAMROZE GARDA
NAVSARI
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-C,1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
