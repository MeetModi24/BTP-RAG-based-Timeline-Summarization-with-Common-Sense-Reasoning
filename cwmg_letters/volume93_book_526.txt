526. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
July 8, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
One Gujarati article at least is going with this. I hope to
send the rest from Wardha. The thumb-nail keeps reminding
me of my foolishness. It troubles me occasionally.
Blessings from
BAPU
From
the Gujarati original:

Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
