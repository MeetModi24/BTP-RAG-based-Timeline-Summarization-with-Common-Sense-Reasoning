473. LETTER TO NARAYAN DESAI
May 1, 1932
CHI. NARAYANRAO ALIAS NARAYAN DESAI ALIAS BABLO,
If we read the Gita and understand it, we shall know how to
do our duty. By spinning during the National Week or at other
times, we increase the wealth of the country and serve the poor.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9479
