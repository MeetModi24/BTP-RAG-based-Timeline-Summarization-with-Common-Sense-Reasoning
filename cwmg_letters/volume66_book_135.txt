135. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 4, 1937
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. Amtul Salaam has arrived here. It was
almost impossible for you to accompany Ramdas as he himself
left with Kallenbach. Let us see what happens now. . . .1 Tell
me what kind of patients you admit. Is fruit available there?
And vegetables? Can you get cow's milk?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 264
