217. LETTER TO SAKARLAL
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI,
BOMBAY,
Friday [June 18, 1920]
BHAI SHRI SAKARLAL,
I have just received a telegram about Vrajlal. It is not that
you alone have lost a jewel-like brother; we, too, have lost a co-
worker no less precious than a brother. His hallowed soul is now
resting in an exalted place. Bhai Vrajlal gave an excellent de-
monstration of what the higher life can be. I know you cannot
but grieve; but lighten the burden of your grief with the thought
that his life was sublime and that all of us share your grief.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : C.W. 843
