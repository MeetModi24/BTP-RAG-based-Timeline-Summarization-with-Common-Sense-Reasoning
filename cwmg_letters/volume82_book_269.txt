269. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
SODEPUR,
December 9, 1945
CHI. SHRIMAN,
I got your letter today. I made very few changes. I am
sending it back.
I am happy to hear that Madalasa is all right. Tell her
that I think of her every day.
Do not give any importance to my cold. I did have a
touch of it but am I not a "mahatma" after all?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, pp. 307-
