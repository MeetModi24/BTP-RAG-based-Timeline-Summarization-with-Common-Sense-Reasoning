417. LETTER TO S. K. BOLE
ON THE TRAIN,
July 6, 1939
DEAR RAO BAHADUR,
I have read your letter to the Prime Minister with great care.
I think that if you will help the Ministry you will find that no
tapper need give up his tapping and there need be no unemploy-
ment. The whole of the nira can be turned into gur. Those
who are used to alcoholics won't be satisfied with non-alcoholic
nira.
I am sorry for the threat issued in your last paragraph.
Request for help and threat go ill together.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 4879
