251. LETTER TO VAMAN G. JOSHI
October 20, 1934
Congress should not on any account vote for non-Congress
candidates, much less against Bapuji Aney in spite of his unfor-
tunate anti-Congress attitude on the Communal Award. I call it
anti-Congress in the sense that it is against the declared policy
of the Congress on the question. I therefore suggest that Con-
gressmen should unreservedly vote for Bapuji Aney as opposed to
the non-Congress candidates. But Congressmen ought to make
it clear that they do not in any way by their vote identify themselves
with Sjt. Aney's attitude on the Communal Award.
Reminiscences of Gandhiji, p. 212
