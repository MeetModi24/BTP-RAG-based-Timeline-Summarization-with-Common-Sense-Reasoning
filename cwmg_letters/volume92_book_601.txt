601. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 1, 1932
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
How are you? How is Shankerlal? Has Indu recovered
fully?
362
Write down your reminiscences of Punjabhai and send a
copy to me.
May the New Year help us all to achieve greater purity.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANASUYABEHN
SEVASHRAM
MIRZAPUR, AHMEDABAD
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32813
