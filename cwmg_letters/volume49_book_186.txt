186. LETTER TO NANI MENON
February 28, 1932
MY DEAR NAN,
Your attempt to write a letter to me was very good. And
what a reindeer you have sent. What lovely horns! I am sorry
I am not playing with the goats, though they are brought before
me everyday twice to be milked. I don't play because when they
come, I am always doing something which I must not give up.
Yes, there are a few flowers but nothing much to speak of. The
ground is stony and they can't afford in prisons to layout flower-
beds. You must write again.
Kisses to both of you.
BAPU
My Dear Child, p. 117
