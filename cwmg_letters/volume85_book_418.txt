418. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
NEW DELHI,
September 17, 1946
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I am in the Harijan Colony today.
It is 3 a. m. As I
have woken up, I have started writing letters.
I am writing
to Sharda. She has fallen ill again. Never mind if Shakaribehn
has gone away. There was no alternative. It seems now her
time will be divided between Surat and Sevagram. Do only
that which can be done well. It will be good if you do not
over-burden yourself. I hope Pushpa is doing well.
A good many persons seem to be ill there. They should
all get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 10652
