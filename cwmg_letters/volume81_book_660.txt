660. LETTER TO MAYASHANKER
October 17, 1945
BHAI MAYASHANKER,
I have your letter. I am helpless. Do write whatever you
wish to say.
MAYASHANKER
C/O MAHENDRA BHOGILAL & Co.
DIVANCHAND BUILDING
75 JHAVERI BAZAAR, BOMBAY
