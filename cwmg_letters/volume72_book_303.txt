303. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
SEVAGRAM,
July 9, 1940
DEAR BROTHER,
Have just read your letter. Your detachment is simply won-
derful. Bapa is an authority on many things-road-making,
Harijans, Bhils, Society's affairs, forlorn causes, etc. But I never
knew that he was an authority on interpreting Tulsidas. I should
still cite Sita's example for our women. My incitement has never
gone beyond. But I must not weary you. Keep me always on the
straight and narrow path.
Love.
M.K.G.
From a facsimile: Mahatma: Life of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, Vol. V,
between pp. 352 and 353; also Letters of Srinivasa Sastri, p. 320
