227. LETTER TO DIPAK DUTY CHOWDHARY
August 19, 1945
CHI. DIPAK,
I have your letter. It is good you let me know. You will
have arranged whatever treatment was possible. What else can we
do? The result is in God's hands, is it not? What are you doing?
Blessings from
BAPU
