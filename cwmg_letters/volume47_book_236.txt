236. LETTER TO TARABEHN MODI
BARDOLI,
July 26, 1931
CHI. TARA,
I got your letter. I approve your idea of doing some service
there whenever occasion offers itself, and improving your health
as much as you can. Anyhow learn the art of living with all
types of women. Remember that one who seeks to serve does
not always get the place or work of one's own liking. Our duty
is to embrace with joy whatever opportunity for service may
arise at any place. This is not meant as criticism, but is an
expression of hope for the future.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4173. Also C.W. 1672. Courtesy:
