306. LETTER TO DEV PRAKASH NAYYAR
DELHI,
October 7, 1947
CHI. DEV,
You must pay Hunar's salary from there only. Why does
he write to us?
Sushila has a slight fever. Just now at 5 o'clock in the morn-
ing she is sleeping.
A copy of the letter I have written to General Shah Nawaz
and his own letter are enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
