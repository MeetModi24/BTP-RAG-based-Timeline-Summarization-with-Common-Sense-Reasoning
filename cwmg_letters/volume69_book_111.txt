111. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
March 31, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your wire was duly received. I needed it. The headache
must go. Kallenbach went today. He was fretting. He will
take the steamer to Aden and thence to Durban. The house is
empty. Here is a copy of letter from Subhas.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3905. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7214
