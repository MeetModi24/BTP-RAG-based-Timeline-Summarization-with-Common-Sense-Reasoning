282. LETTER TO DR. B. C. ROY
October 12, 1939
DEAR DR. BIDHAN,
I know your partiality for me. But I feel utterly unable
to shoulder the burden. Jawaharlal is the only man with drive
to take my place. His difference of outlook will be softened.
But what does it matter, if he carries your minds with him in
his new ideas? We shall not get a more open and sincere man
than Jawaharlal with his driving power. Make therefore what
use you can of me through him. I have hitherto influenced the
country through the Committee. Now I must do so to the extent
that I influence Jawaharlal. Don't you agree?
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
