752. LETTER TO ICHCHHANAND
October 25, 1945
SWAMIJI,
I have your letter in English.
If you did not know the
national language Hindustani, you could have written to me in the
language of your province. Why this infatuation for English?
I don't know when I shall be able to reach your book and
make time to read it.
ICHCHHANAND
SOUTH GOVINDPUR
P. O. KATRASGARH
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
