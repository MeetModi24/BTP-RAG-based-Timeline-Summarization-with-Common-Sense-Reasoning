593. LETTER TO JON ROMMEL
March 2, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and your book called Regenera-
tion. I glanced through the pages, but I must confess to you that
I found in it nothing to appeal to me.
Yours sincerely,
JON ROMMEL, ESQ.
NIAGARA FALLS
N. Y.
4111 SAUNDERS ROAD
LEWISTON
From a microfilm: S.N. 20425
