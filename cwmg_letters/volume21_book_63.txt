63. LETTER TO SIDNEY BURN
[After September 18, 1921]
SIR,
I have the honour to acknowledge the receipt of your letter
of the 18th instant. I had intended to pass through the Puduk-
kottai State on my way to Chettinad. But in view of your letter
I shall alter my route.
I have, etc.,
From a photostat: S.N. 7618
