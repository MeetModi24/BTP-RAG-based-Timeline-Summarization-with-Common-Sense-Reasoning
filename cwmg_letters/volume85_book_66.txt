66. LETTER TO B. G. KHER
PANCHGANI,
July 27, 1946
BHAI BALASAHEB,
I am of the view that the Governor's invitation should be
accepted. At present we are not offering non-co-operation. I
expect to reach Poona by tomorrow evening.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI BALASAHEB KHER
POONA
