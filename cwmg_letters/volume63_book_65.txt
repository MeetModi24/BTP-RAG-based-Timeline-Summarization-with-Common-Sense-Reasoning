65. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
June 15, 1936
CHI. PRABHA,
Kanti is not here today. I saw your letter and opened it.
I am preserving it as you have permitted Kanti to read it. I
shall not destroy it till Kanti and Saraswati arrive on Friday.
However, no one else will read it.
I do not myself fancy Kashi. I gave the advice I did be-
cause it is your duty to please Jayaprakash in such a matter.
Now there is no question of your going there since Harsubabu
himself says no and J. P. does not wish to displease him. Sitab
Diyara is your Kashi for the present, and all that you do there
with understanding is of course your education. Do acquaint
yourself with the villagers. Do you pray alone or do other women
join you? It is surprising that there is none in the village who
can sing the Ramayana.
I shall go to Segaon tomorrow. Probably I shall go alone
since my hut is not yet ready. Perhaps Lilavati may accom-
pany me.
My weight at Bangalore was 114 lb. and my blood pres-
sure 156/110, which may be taken as satisfactory. Sardar is at
Bombay.
Blessings from
BAPU
