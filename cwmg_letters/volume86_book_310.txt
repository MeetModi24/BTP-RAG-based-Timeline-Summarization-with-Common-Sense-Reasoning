310. LETTER TO ARUNA ASAF ALI
December 18, 1946
CHI. ARUNA,
I should like you to accompany Asaf to America. You
will be able to give him much help there, and it would also
be a service to the country. I would also say that you will be
able to do much independent work in America.
I think it is your duty to accompany Asaf.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi G. N. 2201
