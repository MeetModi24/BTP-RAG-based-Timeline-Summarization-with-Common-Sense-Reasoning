96. LETTER TO VANAMALA PARIKH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 10, 1931
CHI. VANAMALA,
If you do not know how to do oral sums in arithmetic, you
should learn it. Can you tell me orally how much 2& lb. of ghee
would cost if one maund costs Rs. 30?
Your handwriting is good, but you can make it better still.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5759
