261. LETTER TO MRS. S. R. DAS
SODEPUR,
December 8, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I was happy to see you, but it also made me unhappy. I
was not prepared to see you in such broken health. Urmiladevi
has given me a cheque for Rs. 500. I will spend that money on
the Adivasis.
You know who the Adivasis are, don't you?
Thakkar Bapa and Khersaheb are working among the Adivasis.
Blessings from
BAPU
MRS. S. R. DAS
1 AUCKLAND PLACE
CALCUTTA
