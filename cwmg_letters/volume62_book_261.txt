261. LETTER TO NIRMALABEHN J. SHROFF
SAVLI,
February 29, 1936
CHI. NIRMALA,
This is no letter of condolence. Of course we do feel pained
at the death of anyone and, from that standpoint, there is certainly
pain at the death of your husband. You have not become a widow,
and if you think you have, you were as much a widow in your
married state. I expect you do not think that there has been any
change in this state. Carry out your duty firmly. If you get the
custody of your child, rear him. Do not ever get your head shaved.
I see no need for any change in your way of life. In my view, it
is your duty to continue your work of service as before. May God
grant you the good sense and the strength to do your duty.
1 Vide letters to Premabehn Kantak, 6-5-1936 and 21-5-1936.
In fact, there is no need to write even this much as Nathji
is already there by your side.
Blessings from
BAPU
