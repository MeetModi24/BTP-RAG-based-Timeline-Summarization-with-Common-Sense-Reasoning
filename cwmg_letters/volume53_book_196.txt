196. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
January 25, 1933
MY DEAR THAKKAR BAPA,
I have your three letters, one only requires an answer. If it
could be decently done I would certainly like the name of the
Society to be more in keeping with the object we have in view.
I should incur the risk of the ridicule of the unthinking if all those
who compose the Society agree that Rajaji's suggestion be adop-
ted and if it is not against the constitution to do so. But if the
change is adopted, let it not be adopted in a hurry.
I have the articles for the English weekly.
I had a telegram from Shastri accepting the offer.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20102
