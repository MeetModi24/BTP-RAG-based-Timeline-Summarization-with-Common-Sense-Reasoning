634. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
January 15, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter today after many days. You must not
do this. I have allowed only one day to pass without writing
to you. What you have written about Janakibehn will be very
useful. Vinoba has just arrived after his release. Hence only
this much for the present. Brother is doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
