375. LETTER TO DR. M. A. ANSARI
March 8, 1935
DEAR DR. ANSARI,
I understand your letter. I shall not strive with you but shall
plead with Rajenbabu to release you. I can see quite clearly that
if we are to have your services for many a year, you should not
have any regular responsibility to shoulder. I see too that you
must go to Europe from time to time.
My love to you all.
Yours,
Ansari Papers. Courtesy: Jamia Millia Islamia Library
