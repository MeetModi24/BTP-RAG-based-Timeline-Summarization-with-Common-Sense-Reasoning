49. LETTER TO MANAGER, INDIAN PRESS LTD.
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 11, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter and the book called The Coming Renaissance .
I am very sorry to have to tell you that I have not a minute
to spare for reading and reviewing the book. Nor is [it] usual for
me generally to review books in the pages of Young India. Do you
wish me to return the book?
Yours sincerely,
THE MANAGER
THE INDIAN PRESS LTD., ALLAHABAD
From a microfilm: S.N. 13471
