553. LETTER TO MANGALDAS HARKISANDAS
NEW DELHI,
October 7, 1946
BHAI MANGALDAS,
I was no doubt informed about your earlier cheque. Your
cheque for Rs. 100 dated October 4, 1946, has reached me
safely.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MANGALDAS HARKISANDAS
BHAGA TALAO
SURAT
