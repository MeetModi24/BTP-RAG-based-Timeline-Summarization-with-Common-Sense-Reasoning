138. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
October 21, 1931
CHI. PREMA,
I have started getting letters from you now. I am eager to
reply to them at length, but I have no time for that. Please,
therefore, be satisfied with this acknowledgment.
Why do you fear that I would yield on any essential matter?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10265
