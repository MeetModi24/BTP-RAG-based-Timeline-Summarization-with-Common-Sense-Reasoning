628. LETTER TO NANABHAI MASHRUWALA
ASHRAM,
Maha Krishna Paksha 2 [February 7, 1928]
BHAI NANABHAI,
I have your letter as also the letters of Sushila and Amina.
Sushila has spread a sweet atmosphere around her and is win-
ning everyone's love. I hope you are keeping well.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 6675
