185. LETTER TO VITHALDAS V. JERAJANI
May 26, 1942
BHAI VITHALDAS,
I am thinking over your letter of the 12th. Who can predict
what will happen if there is a conflagration? But, without worry-
ing about it, we have to think of what we can do. We will
discuss that at the forthcoming meeting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of Gujarati: S.N. 9803
