411. LETTER TO ADA RASENGREN
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 23, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I must not publish the article you have
sent me. Almost every Indian believes that England was in the
wrong and responsible for the calamitous War. I do not now wish
to rake up an old controversy without any occasion for it.
As desired I have sent a copy of your essay to Mr. Natesan,
Madras. I have given your card to the Manager of Young India.
Yours sincerely,
MISS ADA RASENGREN
Ro, LIDINGO VILLASTAD
From a photostat: S.N. 12466
