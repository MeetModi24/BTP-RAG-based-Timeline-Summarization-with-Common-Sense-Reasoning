159. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Tuesday [October 1, 1929]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letters. I feel that you should stay on in Bijapur
as long as you feel physically and mentally fit, so that some
propaganda for khadi is done and Chhaganlal gets assistance.
Help as much as you can in carding and making of slivers.
Introduce, if possible, spinning in the children's school at Bijapur.
Make friends, if you can, with the leading families there. But in
the last resort you must do what appeals to you. You cannot
teach carding now to those who do not spin. Once they have
mastered the art of spinning there is no harm in teaching them
what they like. The carding-bow cannot be given free to
anyone. There is no harm in giving it at concessional rates.
We are today in a village. We have arrived here after
leaving Mirabehn and others in Lucknow. We will all assemble
tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
