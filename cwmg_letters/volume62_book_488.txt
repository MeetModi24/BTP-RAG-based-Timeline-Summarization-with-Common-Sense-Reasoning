488. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 18, 1936
DEAR DAUGHTER,
Why are you quarrelling with Bapa? Was not whatever he
wrote meant for your own good, to protect you from Rukmini?
Is he to blame? Bapa asks me to explain it to you. Tell me
what there is to explain.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 336
