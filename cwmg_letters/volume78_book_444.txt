444. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
November 20, 1944
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
You have rightly cautioned me. I shall do what I can.
The result will be what we all deserve.
Who knows what will happen tomorrow? Ramji hadn't known
what was to happen the next morning. You may return after
straightening out things there and setting your mind at rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1950
