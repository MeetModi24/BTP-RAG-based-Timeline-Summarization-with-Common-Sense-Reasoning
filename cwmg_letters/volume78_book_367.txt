367. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
November 8, 1944
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I heard from Dr. Mehta about the set-back in your health.
There is nothing particularly wrong with your health. You have
spoiled your health by over-exertion and worry. You ought to
utilize your knowledge for the benefit of your own health. English-
men are not philosophers, but they keep themselves free from worry
so as to preserve their health and for the sake of their health
they never let any external trouble overcome them. You have such
capacity. Stop worrying about me and about other matters. You
are giving your services even from the sick-bed. Nothing at all is
definite about the fast.
Blessings from
BAPU
MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 204. Also from a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy:
