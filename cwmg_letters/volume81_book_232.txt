232. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
BIRLA HOUSE,
BOMBAY,
August 20, 1945
MY DEAR KU.,
Here is your manuscript, my foreword and my article.
If possible all will go by registered post. The article and the
foreword have been carefully written. You may go without any-
thing from me if you disapprove of the article.
You have to
master at least one craft and acquire a workable knowledge of
Hindustani. You can easily make time for both; have your
letter-head in Nagari and Urdu scripts adding the Roman if
you like.
On the existing ones have rubber stamps for Urdu
and Nagari.
Love.
In haste,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10178
