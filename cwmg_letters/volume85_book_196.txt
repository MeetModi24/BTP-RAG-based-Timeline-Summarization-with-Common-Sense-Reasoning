196. LETTER TO MANGHARAM BHAVANDAS
SEVAGRAM,
August 17, 1946
BHAI MANGHARAM,
I got your letter. Why should you grieve over your mother's
death? We are all destined to go the same way-some now,
some later. Our dharma is to go on doing our duty.
The money will be used for Harijan work.
Blessings from
BAPU
MANGHARAM BHAVANDAS
HYDERABAD (SIND)
