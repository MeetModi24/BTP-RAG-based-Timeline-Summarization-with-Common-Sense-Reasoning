578. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
DELHI,
October 4, 1938
CHI. MIRA,
It is good you are interesting yourself in Balkoba's health.
It is well you are taking quinine. The letters to be posted to
Utmanzai till further instructions. Though Mahadev is staying
here, his letters too must be sent to my address.
I am keeping extra well. It is a wonder to me. Nothing
seems to worry me, though there are enough things to worry
about. The weather has become extra hot. Quite unusual for
Delhi at this time of the year.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6406. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10001
