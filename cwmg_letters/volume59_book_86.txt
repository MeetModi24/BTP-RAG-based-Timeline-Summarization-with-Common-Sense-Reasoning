86. LETTER TO V. V. DASTANE
September 25, 1934
The strongest reason for wearing khadi and sacrificial spinning
is that we would thus identify ourselves with the poorest.
Khadi
which at present appears costly to us can in course of time be-
come the cheapest of all cloth and that too with just a little effort
on everybody's part.
DASTANE
BHUSAVAL
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
