362. LETTER TO G. V. NARASIMHACHAR
December 24, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I appreciate your zeal but cannot endorse
your action. I am quite sure in my mind that till the Guruvayur
question is settled, no one should fast in respect of temple-entry.
Try still more to convince the opponents of reform that it is neces-
sary if Hinduism is to live. For the abandonment of your project-
ed fast, you may make use of this letter, and seeing that the fasting
method has been applied by me in the first instance, I would ask
you to listen to my advice.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. G. V. NARASIMHACHAR
"MADHAVA VILAS", BRAHMIN STREET, BELLARY
