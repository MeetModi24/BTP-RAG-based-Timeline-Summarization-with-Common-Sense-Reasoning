9. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 21, 1940
CHI. VIJAYA,
I have your letter. I hope Father is calm. It is best that
you stay there for the present. Write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7127. Also: C.W. 4619. Cour-
tesy: Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
