335. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
DELHI,
After starting fast [September 17, 1924]
CHI. RAMDAS,
You will have heard about my atonement before this rea-
ches you. Do not be scared; concentrate on your studies. If
you cannot have patience, do run up here. But it would be
best to be patient. I am sure of it. Why should we feel un-
happy on account of physical pain? But while there is life in
the body, we must take the maximum work out of it. The fast
is at least for 21 days. Afterwards we shall see. Read this to
Gijubhai and others.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. RAMDAS
DAKSHINAMURTI BHAVAN
BHAVNAGAR
KATHIAWAR
