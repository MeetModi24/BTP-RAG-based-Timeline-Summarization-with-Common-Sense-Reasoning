156. LETTER TO G. N. KANITKAR
ON WAY TO LANKA,
November 8, 1927
MY DEAR KANITKAR,
I have gone through the report you left with me. It makes
interesting reading. I hope that you will realize all the expecta-
tions raised in the report and that you will succeed in your at-
tempt at turning out the best and the cheapest wheel.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: C.W. 961. Courtesy: Gajanan Kanitkar
