344. LETTER TO MANISHANKAR GANPATRAM
October 7, 1932
Inter-dining and intermarrying with Antyajas are not a neces-
sary part of the movement for the eradication of untouchability.
There can be no coercion in these matters. Nor should any-
body be prevented from having such relations with untouchables
if he wishes, just as nobody is prevented from having them with
members of other communities. Eradication of untouchability has
nothing to do with inter-dining and intermarrying.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 114
