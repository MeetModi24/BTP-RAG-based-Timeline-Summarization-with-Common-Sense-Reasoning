193. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
June 29, 1925
MY DEAR CHARLIE,
I have your letter and the article on "Father Forgive Them"
passage. I must have the original letter before I can make use of
your article. Please search and send. I am fixed up in Bengal
till the end of July. I hope Barasaheb is suffering less pam.
Pray give him my love and tell him I constantly think of him.
Yours,
MOHAN
C. F. ANDREWS, ESQ.
C/O PRINCIPAL S. RUDRA
SOLON
SIMLA HILLS
From the original: C.W. 6049
