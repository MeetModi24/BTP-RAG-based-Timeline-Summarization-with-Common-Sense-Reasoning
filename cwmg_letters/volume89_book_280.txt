280. LETTER TO DR. JOHN MOTT
NEW DELHI,
October 1, 1947
DEAR DR. MOTT,
I got the sixth volume of your speeches and your letter
yesterday for which many thanks.
I often wonder if we all don't speak and write much and
do little.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
