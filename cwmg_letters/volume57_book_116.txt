116. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
February 4, 1934
Why have you not given me the full details of the earth-
quake? Did you get the letter I sent through Rajendrababu?
You should now give me all the news. You will learn more from
the letter to Jayaprakash.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3440
