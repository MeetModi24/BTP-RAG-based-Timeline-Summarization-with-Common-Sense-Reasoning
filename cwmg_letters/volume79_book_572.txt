572. LETTER TO RAMPRASAD
ON THE TRAIN,
March 31, 1945
CHI. RAMPRASAD,
On the train I am only thinking of the Ashram. Stay on if
you can bear the heat without discomfort. Be friendly with all
and do whatever Ashram work you can do. Finish all the pend-
ing work. Write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
