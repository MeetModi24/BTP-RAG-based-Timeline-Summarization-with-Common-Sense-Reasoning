635. LETTER TO MOHAMMED YASIN
'MANOR VILLE', SIMLA,
June 27, 1945
BHAI MOHAMMED YASIN,
I have gone through your letter. If Pyarelal cannot give you
satisfaction, then I can't do anything more because I do not have
the time. I have heard everything you have to say, so please
give up the desire for a personal discussion with me. The real thing
is work, and work alone, and the most important work is plying
the charkha oneself and persuading others to ply it. This means
that one has to learn and use in practice all the processes from
growing cotton to spinning the yarn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
