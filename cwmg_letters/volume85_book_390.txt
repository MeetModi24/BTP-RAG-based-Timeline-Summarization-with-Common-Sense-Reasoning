390. LETTER TO K. S. DESHPANDE
NEW DELHI,
September 13, 1946
BHAI DESHPANDE,
Sushilabehn told me about your father's death. I will not
express grief. He was a very virtuous and devout man. He
has left behind a great legacy for you. You must add to it
and bring glory to your work of service.
A wire has been sent regarding the Harijan temple.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI TATYA DESHPANDE
74 NARAYAN PETH
POONA
