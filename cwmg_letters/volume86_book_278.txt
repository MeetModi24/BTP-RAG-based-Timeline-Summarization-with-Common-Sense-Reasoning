278. LETTER TO SHANTA
December 11, 1946
CHI. SHANTA,
I have your letter. Do as much work as you can.
You
are bound to succeed. Teach the people to be clean. They
should keep themselves, and the water and the lavatories clean.
This kind of work does not require money. This can be done
only by patience and by example. You have to keep healthy.
The body is the abode of God, it is the real temple. Temples
made of stone are temples only in name.
I am here on a difficult mission. Though there is dark-
ness, I am not worried.
Blessings from
BAPU
