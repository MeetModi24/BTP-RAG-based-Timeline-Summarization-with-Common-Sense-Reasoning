126. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
MOULMEIN,
March 13, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
Today also the mail is about to leave, and so I write only
this.
Everything is going on all right. I think of all of you.
Everyone must have recovered. It would be good if all of you
sat in the sun every morning with your bodies exposed. If anyone
falls ill, he should immediately stop eating, take only water
and have motions with the help of an enema. There seems to be
no need for any other treatment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5393
