527. LETTER TO MAULVI M. ASHRAF
As AT WARDHA,
May 27, 1936
DEAR ASHRAF,
What can you expect from me? No doubt you have written
to Kumarappa and Shankerlal Banker. They can send you what
little literature the two Associations have produced. I am sure
you do not want Harijan, which deals with untouchability mainly
and some moral problems.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MAULVI M. ASHRAF
POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC INFORMATION DEPARTMENT
SWARAJ BHAWAN, ALLAHABAD
A. I. C. C. File, 1936. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
