452. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL VIDYARTHI
BIRLA HOUSE, NEW DELHI,
January 25, 1948
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
I have your letter. You are doing good work. Keep work-
ing in the same way. We have never done any work in the
villages and therefore the difficulties arise. You have to pro-
duce there milk, fruit and vegetables. Leafy vegetables take
very little time to grow. Do you not have any cow at all in
your village? Meet the zamindars and try to soften their hearts
with love. So far we have not seen the true strength of ahimsa.
Now is the time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 11666
