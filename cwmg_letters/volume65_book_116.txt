116. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
HUDLI,
April 21, 1937
BHAI MUNSHI,
Do come on the 25th. If the meeting of the Working
Committee is to be held in Prayag and I have to go there, you will
know. Then do not come. As yet I have received no information.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7617. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
