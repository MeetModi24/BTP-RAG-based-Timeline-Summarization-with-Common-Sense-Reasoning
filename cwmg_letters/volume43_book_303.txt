303. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
April 22, 1930
CHI. NARANDAS,
What you write about Sharja is correct.
The accompanying letter is for Gangabehn. Read it and
give it to her. I have no time to write more. It will be 11.30
p.m. in a few seconds.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 8103. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
