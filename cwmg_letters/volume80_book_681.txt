681. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
SIMLA,
July 7, 1945
BHAI SHYAMLAL,
I have your letter of the 2nd. Dharmadev Shastri is here.
If Bapa approves of his scheme, I too shall.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SHYAMLALJI
KASTURBA SMARAK NIDHI
CENTRAL OFFICE, BAJAJWADI, WARDHA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
