43. LETTER TO C. V. RENGAM CHETTI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 9, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I wish you could soften down a bit. In
any event at the present moment I am not in active charge of
the affairs of the Association and I am too much preoccupied
in the commitments I have undertaken to attend to anything else.
But another non-Brahmin, that is, Seth Jamnalalji is in charge, and
I assure you that he is a shrewd and capable business man. If you
convince him of your case, he will not hesitate to intervene.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. C. V. RENGAM CHETTI
NARAYANAVARAM
From a microfilm: S.N. 13720
