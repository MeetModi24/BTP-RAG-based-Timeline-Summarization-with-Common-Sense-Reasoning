42. LETTER TO RAMBHAU BHOGE
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 27, 1945
BHAI RAMBHAU,
I have your letter. Never mind whatever punishment has
been given to you. I trust that your wife and child will be looked
after. Keep well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. RAMBHAU BHOGE
DISTRICT PRISON
DHULIA, KHANDESH
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
