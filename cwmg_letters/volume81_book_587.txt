587. LETTER TO G. V. MAVALANKAR
October 8, 1945
BHAI MAVALANKAR,
I approve of your draft. On page 5 (e), I feel you should
add "or its equivalent" after "Vernacular IV Standard". But do
what seems best to you. The Committee is not meeting here, but
in Sevagram on the 25th, 26th and 27th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1254
