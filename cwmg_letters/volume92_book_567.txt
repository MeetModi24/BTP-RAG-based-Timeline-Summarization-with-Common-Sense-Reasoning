567. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
August 31, 1932
CHI. NIMU,
I learn from a postcard from Narandas that Baba is very
ill. It will be as God has willed. Do not panic. If you do, it
will harm Baba. You should know that the mother's feelings
always affect the child. We must do our best. There should
then be no cause for panic. I am keeping Ramdas informed.
It will be good if you keep me informed every day. Follow the
doctor's instructions carefully.
BAPU
[PS.]
Give this to Nimu after reading.
CHI. NARANDAS GANDHI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From the Gujarati original: Nirmala Gandhi Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
