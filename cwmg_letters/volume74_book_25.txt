25. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
April 27, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I got your very long letters. They were welcome. But you
should get rid of the letter-writing mania. I do not believe that
you have overcome your attachment to Sevagram. In this matter I
am only giving you my own views. You must keep Kanchan with
you wherever you stay. You may separate afterwards. It was a
condition that if you got fever you would leave Mool. Please,
therefore, come back.
[PS.]
Wrote this much despite the weakness. Today is the third day
of fasting. I will break the fast in the evening.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8502. Also C.W. 7134. Cour-
tesy: Munnalal G. Shah
