420. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
August 21, 1932
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I got your letter. It would be very good indeed if Ramibehn
recovers before leaving that place. I hope you got all my letters.
Regain the weight which you have lost. Feel no hesitation in
eating any food which may be necessary for your health.
The change which has taken place in Kaku's brother and
sister is very good.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 64. Also C.W. 8794. Courtesy:
Gangabehn Vaidya
