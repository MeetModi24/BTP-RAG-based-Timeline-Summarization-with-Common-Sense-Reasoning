99. LETTER TO P. N. RAJBHOJ
May 2, 1933
BHAI RAJBHOJ,
You must not get agitated over my fast. Remain there
and be prepared for intelligent service. If I survive I shall at-
tempt to use this body for further service but will consider it a
blessing of God even if I succumb. My mission will not end with
death. The yajna of Harijan service that has been commenced
can never end. Even the death from fasting of thousands of
savarna Hindus will not be too much in my opinion. There is
little possibility of my writing but you may continue to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 780
