9. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 21, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter full of beautiful description. I am glad you are in
such peace and you have the benevolent company of Harakhchand-
bhai. Durgabehn is well now.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6478. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9873
