142. LETTER TO RUKMINIDEVI BAZAJ
February 9, 1934
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your postcard. I am writing this letter before the morn-
ing prayer. I rise daily at three.
The line "Who can harm him whom Rama protects?" is
constantly in my mind. But sometimes I also feel that after all
nobody is immortal. Doesn't everybody die sooner or later?
The only question is when. Why, then, grieve over or rejoice
at it?
In the final reckoning, are not night and day the
same?
Blessings from
BAPU
