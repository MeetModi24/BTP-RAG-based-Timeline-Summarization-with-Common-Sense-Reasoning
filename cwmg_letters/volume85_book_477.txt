477. LETTER TO S. P. PATWARDHAN
NEW DELHI,
September 26, 1946
BHAI APPA,
I am ashamed to read about your illness. If we workers
keep falling ill, the service that we render will also be sickly;
and how can any illness come to a nature cure worker? Now,
get well soon, and write to me in detail. For the sake of people
like you Dr. Bhagawat's retirement from the profession would
be interrupted, wouldn't it?
Blessings from
BAPU
APPASAHEB PATWARDHAN
P. O. LANJE
DT. RATNAGIRI
