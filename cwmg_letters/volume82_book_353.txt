353. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
SODEPUR,
December 22, 1945
CHI. KANTI,
I got your letter. It is quite amusing. You are working hard
to pass three examinations at a time. I wish you success in all
the three. Be a good doctor. Set a fine example of success in
inter-caste marriage and be an expert at spinning and constructive
work. Truly speaking, all the three examinations are a test of
ahimsa.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7382.
Courtesy: Kantilal
Gandhi
