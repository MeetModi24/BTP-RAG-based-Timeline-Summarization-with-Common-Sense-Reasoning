347. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
MOTIHARI,
June 17, 1917
MY DEAR ESTHER,
I am going to Ahmedabad for 4 days. I return on the 28th
instant at the latest. There is no secret about what I have and you
have every right to ask. My faith in Truth and Love is as vivid
as in the fact that I am writing this to you. To me they are
convertible terms. Truth and Love conquer all.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
My Dear Child, p. 14
