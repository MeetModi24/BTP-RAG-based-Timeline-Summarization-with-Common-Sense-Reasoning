163. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
May 8, 1945
BAPA,
I have your letter. My work is going on according to my
ways. The Government's reply has come today from Hyderabad.
I shall see what can be done. Go through it when you come
here. Take rest.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
