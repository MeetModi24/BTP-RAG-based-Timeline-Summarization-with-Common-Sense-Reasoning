727. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
October 22, 1945
CHI. CHAND,
Satyavati has departed. She has been released from suffering.
She is of course immortal. Our duty is not to grieve over it
but to do our duty to the utmost, dedicating ourselves to the
cause of India's freedom.
Blessings from
BAPU
DAGA MEMORIAL HOSPITAL
NAGPUR
From a photostat of the Hindi : Chandrani Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
