293. LETTER TO KAPILRAI MEHTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 10, 1930
CHI. KAPIL,
They got your letter at Vile Parle. Although one's home may
be near, one should not go there for the sake of the comforts one
may get there or for the love of one's relations. But there can be
no objection to a worker going to his home to get proper nursing
during illness, with the willing consent of his co-workers and in
order to lessen the burden on them. It depends on one's attitude.
You should take care of your health and improve it. How can
you let it become delicate at this young age? Sun-bath, pranayam
shavasan and eating less help considerably in asthma.
Kakasaheb is quite well. He sends his blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
