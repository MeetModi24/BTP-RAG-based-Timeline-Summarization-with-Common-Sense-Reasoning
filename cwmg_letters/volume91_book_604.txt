604. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Sunday [September 25, 1927]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. If you find Panchgani more congenial,
then do try for a bungalow there. It is a pity that we cannot
ask for Pattani Saheb's bungalow. But I have no doubt that it
is our duty not to ask for it.
I had received your telegram concerning Manilal's brother.
I am quite well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
PANCHGANI CASTLE
PANCHGANI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
