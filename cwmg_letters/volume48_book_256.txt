256. LETTER TO SIR SAMUEL HOARE
VILLENEUVE,
December 7, 1931
DEAR SIR SAMUEL,
I am reducing in writing the gist of our last conversation.
You were good enough to say that neither the Prime Minister's
declaration nor your speech in the House of Commons were the
last word on Safeguards or Reservations, and that it would be open
to any member of the proposed Working Committee to suggest
amendments or the removal of any of them as also to press for-
ward the important investigation of the financial transaction to be
taken over by the National Government. You also said that
whatever you would be sending to the Working Committee for
consideration would not be merely formal, but that the Working
Committee's recommendations would receive the greatest consi-
deration from His Majesty's Government. If this is the correct
impression, I would like you, if you don't mind, to confirm it by
Air Mail. My address in India would be Ahmedabad.
Yours sincerely,
The Hindu, 1-2- 1932
