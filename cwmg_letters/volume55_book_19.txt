19. LETTER TO C. Y. CHINTAMANI
April 24, 1933
DEAR MR. CHINTAMANI,
You know by now Dr. Ambedkar's suggestion for revising
the Yeravda Pact in so far as the panel system is concerned. I
would like you to give me your opinion about the alternative he
suggests. If you have discussed it publicly in The Leader, do
not please trouble to answer this letter. If you have not, I
would esteem your opinion. I am discussing the alternative pub-
licly in the succeeding issues of Harijan.
Yours sincerely,
[PS.]
This is the exploit of my new typist. Please have mercy on
him and me. I hope you are well.
