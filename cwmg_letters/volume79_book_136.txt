136. LETTER TO SHRIPAD JOSHI
WARDHA,
[After January 30, 1945]
I have read what Kakasaheb wrote. I like it. I have read
this letter just now. I thought that the typed matter was a
copy of it. Ask me whatever doubts come to your mind. You
will write to me. Won't you? I will also reply only in writing.
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Gandhiji : Ek Jhalak, p. 49
