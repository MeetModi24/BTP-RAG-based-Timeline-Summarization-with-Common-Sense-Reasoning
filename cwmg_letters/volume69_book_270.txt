270. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
RAJKOT,
May 13, 1939
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I heard about your having been taken to Jaipur. Improve
your health fully. The weight should not go down any further.
You must eat fruit in sufficient quantity. Don't eat anything
between meals. You may take some Ayurvedic medicines if you
wish. Write to me at Rajkot. For some time now I shall have
to stay here. You needn't worry about things here. Mahadev
is with me. He is keeping well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3003
