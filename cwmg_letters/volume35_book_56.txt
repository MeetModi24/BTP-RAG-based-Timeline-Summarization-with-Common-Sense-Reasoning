56. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
Silence Day, Aso Sud 8 [October 4, 1927]
SISTERS,
Your reply to my last letter was what I anticipated even
when I wrote it. The first step in self-purification is the admis-
sion and eradication of whatever hatred there is in one's heart.
As long as we harbour ill will or suspicion against our neighbour
and do not strive to get rid of it, we cannot learn our first
lessons in love. In the Ashram, we must develop the strength to
do at least this much.
Think well over the matter of prayers. I also believe that the
seven o'clock meeting should not be given up. You accepted
it as your special duty to make your class spiritually effective.
For the present I can only suggest that those of you who have the
will and the energy to attend the 4 a.m. prayers may do so, with-
out entering into any discussion about it with others, and there-
after to keep up the habit, in spite of every hardship, as long
as health permits.
Blessings from
BAPU
