303. LETTER TO K. KELAPPAN
AS AT THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
December 23, 1927
MY DEAR KELAPPAN,
I have not heard from you as it were for ages. Here is a
letter1; please read it and tell me if you know anything about
this colony and, if you do not, you must visit it, and report.
What have you done about the committee that I proposed
and what have you done with the fund left with you?
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. KELAPPAN NAIR
PAYOLI
N. MALABAR
From a microfilm: S.N. 14624
