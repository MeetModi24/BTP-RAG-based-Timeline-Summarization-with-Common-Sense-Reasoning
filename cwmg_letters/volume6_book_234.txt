234. LETTER TO REMINGTON TYPEWRITER CO.
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 21, 1906
THE MANAGER
THE REMINGTON TYPEWRITER CO.
100, GRACECHURCH STREET, E.C.
DEAR SIR,
Will you kindly have your machine now removed and let me have
your bill.
Yours faithfully,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4623
