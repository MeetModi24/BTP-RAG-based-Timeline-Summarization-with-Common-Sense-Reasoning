505. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 15, 1926
DEAR MOTI BABU,
I had your letter. I am distressed to find that there was
fire in your khadi depots. I hope that it has not caused much
disruption of work.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
BABU MOTILAL ROY
PRABARTAK SANGH
CHANDRANAGAR
BENGAL
From a photostat: G.N. 11027
