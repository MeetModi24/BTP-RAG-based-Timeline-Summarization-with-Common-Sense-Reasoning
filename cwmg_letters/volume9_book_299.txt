299. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO LORD MORLEY
[LONDON,
October 8, 1909]
SIR,
I beg to enclose herewith a copy of the last letter from the Earl
of Crewe and the reply thereto, in connection with the Transvaal
British Indian question, for Lord Morley's information.
I remain, etc.,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5118
