375. LETTER TO SURENDRA
February 16, 1937
CHI. SURENDRA,
When I said 'yatra' I meant a pilgrimage. Otherwise
I
would have used the word 'musafari'. We are trying to include
in the morning prayer, not the whole Upanishad, but cert-
ainly the first verse. Tena tyaktena is more pregnant with mean-
ing. "Left over from yajna" can be derived from it. Tena tya-
ktena can bear two meanings: one, that which is given by God,
and two, that one must renounce everything. But when one has
renounced everything, what is left over? However, life must go
on and so we have "bhunjitha". That is to say that one must
partake, like a medicine, of what God has given. And this
applies not only to the food that we must eat but to all the needs
of body, mind and soul that we must satisfy.
