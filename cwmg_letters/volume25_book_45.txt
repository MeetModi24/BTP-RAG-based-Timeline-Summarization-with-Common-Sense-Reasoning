45. LETTER TO A FRIEND
[September 2, 1924]
DEAREST FRIEND,
Though we rarely write to each other and rarely meet, you
are what I have described you.
I am sick of the domestic fight that is raging in the country.
I have therefore entirely capitulated. I do not want to be in the
Congress if I must be without all old friends. I have talked to
Mrs. Besant. I am in correspondence with Pandit Motilalji and
now I am putting myself in touch with you. Shall the spinning-
wheel be the only bar? Will you not wear khaddar and take the
spinning-wheel yourself for the sake of the poor and the down-
trodden of India? Unless you are opposed to it as a matter of
principle, I want you to consider my proposition seriously enough!
I hope you are keeping well. Please reply at Sabarmati.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 10146 A
