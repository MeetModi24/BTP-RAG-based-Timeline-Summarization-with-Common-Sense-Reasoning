594. LETTER TO S. RADHAKRISHNAN
BOMBAY,
June 21, 1945
BHAI RADHAKRISHNAN,
I have your letter. Though I have no time, I am writing
you these few lines. I like your letter. Have faith that I will
do whatever I can. I intend to show this letter to Jawaharlal.
Hope you are well.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR RADHAKRISHNAN
30 EDWARD ELIOT ROAD
MYLAPUR, MADRAS
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
