315. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[After March 24, 1924]
DEAR MAHADEV,
This much is worth taking. It certainly should be used at
least in the Gujarati Navajivan. If we can leave out something
and have this in its place we may do so. Or, you may publish
a supplement. Do whatever is proper.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 8571
