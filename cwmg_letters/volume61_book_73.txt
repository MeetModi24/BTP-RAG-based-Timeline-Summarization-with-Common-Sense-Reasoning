73. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
May 11, 1935
MY DEAR JERAMDAS,
What is this? What about Anand ? How are you getting
on? Do you want me to resume payment to Anand? Do keep me
informed of your doings.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Malkani is here. Nothing serious about snake-bite.
From the original: C.W. 9714. Courtesy: Jairamdas Doulatram
