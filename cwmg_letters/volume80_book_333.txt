333. LETTER TO M. S. KELKAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 28, 1945
BHAI ICE,
I have your letters. Balkrishna is not satisfied. Dev has
borrowed a little from you. For the rest he is carrying on
on his own. Hoshiaribehn too is not wholly satisfied. I merely
caution you. A doctor who finds fault with his patient is not
a doctor at all. . . .1 I shall be very happy if Hari-ichchha gets
well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
