260. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
July 21, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
You must have got my postcard.
Amritlal is sending the
name of the medicine. Try to regain the lost weight. It is rain-
ing hard here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7089. Also C.W. 4581. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
