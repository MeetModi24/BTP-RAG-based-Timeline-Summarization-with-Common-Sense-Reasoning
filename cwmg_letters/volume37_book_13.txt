13. LETTER TO SECRETARY, JODHPUR STATE
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 1, 1928
DEAR SIR,
The enclosed letter has been received by me for publication.
But before I do so, I should like to have a reply to the letter on
behalf of the State. I shall be obliged if you will kindly let me
have a brief reply.
Please return the letter at the time of acknowledgment.
Yours sincerely,
Enclosure: 10 sheets
From a microfilm: S.N. 12396
