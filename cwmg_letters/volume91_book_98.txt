98. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI H. PATEL
[February 21, 1916]
I had expected to leave here today and reach there to-
morrow morning. But how can I meet Mr. Robinson today, it
being Sunday? So now I shall only leave on Monday and
reach there on Tuesday. Most probably I shall leave by the
Gujarat Mail. If Santok accompanies me, I shall leave by the
Kathiawar Express. Most probably she will come with me. We
shall talk when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10997. Courtesy : Suryakant C.
Patel
