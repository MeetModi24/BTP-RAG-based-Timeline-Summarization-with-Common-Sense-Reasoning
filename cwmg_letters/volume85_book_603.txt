603. LETTER TO JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
NEW DELHI,
October 16, 1946
DEAR DR. HOLMES1,
Your kind letter of 27-9-1946 and the little book came into
my hands only yesterday. His goods had to follow Devdas.
He could not carry them in his 'plane'.
I am glad Devdas made it a point to see you, no matter
how busy he was.
The book I am keeping on my desk, in the fond hope that
I shall snatch a few minutes to drink in the wisdom it con-
tains.
The rest you get from the papers.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C. W. 10967. Courtesy: S. P. K. Gupta. Also
Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
