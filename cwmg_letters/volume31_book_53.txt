53. LETTER TO JAGJIVAN
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
Jeth Sud 13, 1982, June 23, 1926
BHAI JAGJIVAN,
Your letter. You should not leave your present school in a
hurry. Moreover, I will have to ask Amritlal Sheth before
taking you elsewhere. My advice to you is, tell him about all
your difficulties and stay where you are.
C/O ANTYAJA SHALA
RANPUR
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19633
