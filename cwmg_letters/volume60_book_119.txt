119. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
January 21, 1935
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. You can certainly stay with Gangabehn.
I should like it if you settled there. Yes, your visit to Madras is
still to come off. Ambujam's Ashram is ready. She expects you
there. Go to Bochasan when you return from there. You may
like to go to Madras via Wardha. I expect to reach Wardha on
the 29th.
How did Gangabehn's finger come to have abscess?
I hope Ramniklal has recovered his strength. Let him write
to me when he feels impelled. How is Tara ?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I just got word about the arrival of the parcel of ink. I
shall write about it after examining it.
SMT. VASUMATIBEHN
HARIJAN ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9391. Also C.W. 636. Courtesy:
Vasumati Pandit
