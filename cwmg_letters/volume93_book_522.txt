522. LETTER TO PYARELAL
June 16, 1940
CHI. PYARELAL,
A. S. categorically says that she never told you that she
did not tell me. Anyway, she told me and I wrote to you
only after she had told me. She said it with such pain that I
asked her why she herself did not feed you. I did not want
to force her. I would never do it. I would only wish that you
eat your food regularly and regain your failing health.
As for my words, no doubt should have arisen in your
mind at all. Sushila had already told me. But since I know
that you do not approve of Sushila telling me anything about
you, I usually do not act on what she says. If A. S. had not
told me, I would not have told you anything even though I
would have felt upset.
There was no need for me to comment on that letter of
yours. I was not surprised by what you wrote. Where is the
need for me to show 'delicacy' in my dealings with you? If
I come to know something, I would give my reactions. What
was your last sentence? 'Is it about her whom you consider a
saintly woman?' That is my question. Even though I am aware
of her many qualities, I have my doubts about her being a
saintly woman. She eats herself away. I have already told
her that.
I would feel happy if you see her as a saintly
woman. In that case I will try to see her as such. I think
now all the questions have been dealt with.
BAPU
