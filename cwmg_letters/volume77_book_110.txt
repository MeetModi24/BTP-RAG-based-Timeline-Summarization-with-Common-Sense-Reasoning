110. LETTER TO DURGA M. DESAI
JUHU,
May 20, 1944
CHI. DURGA3,
I may be regarded as crippled for the present. God does
not allow even a Mahatma's pride to last. These lines are for
all. Once I start writing letters everyone expects one from me,
but before I can satisfy their wish, maybe I will be there. Write
to me in detail. Let all those who wish to write do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./XXIII
