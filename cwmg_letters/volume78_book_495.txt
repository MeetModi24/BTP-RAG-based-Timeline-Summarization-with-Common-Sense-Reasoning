495. LETTER TO TEJRAM BHATT
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1944
BHAI TEJRAMJI,
You spend money needlessly. I can no longer attend to
personal problems. I cannot look into your case. I have no
time at all. Please have mercy on me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
TEJRAM BRATT
DEHRA DUN
