57. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
TELEPHONE No. 1635,
TELEGRAMS "GANDHI",
JOHANNESBURG,
May 14, 1907
MY DEAR CHHAGANLAL,
I have your letter. You can send £ 15 to your parents. I
am not yet in a position to write to you at length. The headache
has evidently come to stay for the time being.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
From a copy: S.N. 4744
