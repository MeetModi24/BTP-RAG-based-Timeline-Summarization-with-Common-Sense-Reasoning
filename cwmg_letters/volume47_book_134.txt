134. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
BORSAD,
July 6, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your letter. I will have a talk with Jayaprakash when
he comes here and do what is necessary. I should like you to
come early. I am not at all happy that your health is deteriorat-
ing.
Rajendrababu is in Ahmedabad today. We shall meet at
Anand station in the train for Bombay. He will stay in Bombay
for about four days and then leave for that side. You should stop
worrying unnecessarily. God does the worrying for us; why then
should we worry? Even if we worry what can we do? We
realize every moment in our lives that we cannot move a single
leaf unless He wills. Why, then, should we feel proud, or
worry?
Blessings from
BAPU
