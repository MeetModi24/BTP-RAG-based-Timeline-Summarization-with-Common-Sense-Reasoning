183. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
BOMBAY,
September 30, 1944
DEAR DR. JAYAKAR,
Many thanks for your kind letter.
I did what I thought was the best for the country. Of
course, you should now express yourself freely on the question.
That way only we shall arrive at the truth.
I am preparing for my departure and writing this against
time. Hence this brief note.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhi-Jayakar Papers: File No. 826. Courtesy: National Archives of India
