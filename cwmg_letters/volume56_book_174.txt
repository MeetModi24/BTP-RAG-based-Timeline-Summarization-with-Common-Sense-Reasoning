174. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
October 28, 1933
BHAI MUNNALAL,
I got your letter. If you wished to live with me, why didn't
you say so all these days? It is not proper for you to feel un-
happy as you do. You cannot accompany me on my tour. If
you sincerely wish to stay in one place and work, you may work
under Vinoba or Jamnalalji. But I am afraid you will not be
able to stick to any place. If you can disprove my fear, I should
like you to do so. I know you very well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8646
