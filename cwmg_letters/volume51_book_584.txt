584. LETTER TO RATILAL SHETH
November 6, 1932
BHAI RATILAL,
I wrote a letter to you at your Bombay address, to which I
expected a reply but did not get any. However, I had a wire from
Lilavati today. She informs me that Chhaganlal has gone to
Thitan, but he accepts Nanalal and you as arbitrators. This light-
ens my burden. Now I am waiting for your letter. I should like
you to go to Rangoon as early as possible. It would be very good
if Nanalal and you went together. This problem must be solved.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7169. Also C.W. 4663. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
