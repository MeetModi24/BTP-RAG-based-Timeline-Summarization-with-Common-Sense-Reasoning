468. LETTER TO JUGALKISHORE BIRLA
SEVAGRAM,
September 2, 1941
BHAI JUGALKISHOREJI,
I have your sweet letter, although I think something still
remains to be done. There is no particular need for you to come.
Come when you have the leisure. My blessings are to be taken for
granted for all noble work.
Blessings from
BAPU
SETH JUGALKISHORE BIRLA
8 ROYAL EXCHANGE PLACE
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
