331. LETTER TO ANANTRAM PURI
POONA,
March 18, 1946
CHI. ANANTRAM,
I am glad that you have concentrated your attention on farm-
ing. Do you try any farming without the use of bullocks? I
can tell you, in that too, there is something special. Keep well.
Blessings from
BAPU
