485. LETTER TO ZAKIR HUSAIN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 27, 1938
MY DEAR ZAKIR,
I opened at Haripura the conversation about Hindu-Muslim
unity. You were to have talked to me about it the next day
if we could meet again. Unfortunately, I was unable to spare
the time. I would like you, if you will, to put down your
thoughts in writing and let me hear from you.
M . K . GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
