510. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
June 12, 1945
CHI. MIRA,
Your welcome letter. Tell me if it is the same thing whether
I put the address in English or Hindustani, that is, for receiving
the post.
I note that Ramprasad was to leave you on 11th. He must
have gone.
The papers are making a lot of fuss over nothing. Let us see.
You seem to have done well in Delhi. It is a hard and even bad
thing that a cow should have such preferences as you describe.
Is that a general rule?
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABEHN
KISAN ASHRAM
MULDASPUR
BAHADARABAD P. O . , via JWALAPUR
NEAR HARDWAR (U. P.)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
