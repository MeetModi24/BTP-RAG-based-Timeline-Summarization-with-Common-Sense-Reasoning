403. LETTER TO DIWAN CHAMANLAL
NEW DELHI,
July 20, 1947
BHAI CHAMANLAL,
I have your letter. I had a talk with the Raja Saheb of
Sirmur.
For me to take the reins of the Congress in my hands is
certainly difficult, even if it is not impossible. Who will listen
to me today and why should they? Yes, the disturbances make
me unhappy but what can I do?
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From the Hindi original: Diwan Chamanlal Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
