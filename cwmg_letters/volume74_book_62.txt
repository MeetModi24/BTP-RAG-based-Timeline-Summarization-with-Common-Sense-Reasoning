62. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
May 9, 1941
CHI. LILA,
I hope you reached there safe. From today's letter I
can see that it was a good thing that I sent you there. Now,
while you are there, let not your health suffer and try to overcome
whatever shortcomings you have. Speak little and that too after
due thought. Here everything is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9595. Also C.W. 6567. Cour-
tesy: Lilavati Asar
