252. LETTER TO S. C. MUKHERJEE
December 8, 1940
With regard to the renewal of the deposit in the Bank of Nag-
pur Ltd. on behalf of the Jallianwala Bagh Trust Fund I am of
opinion that the deposit should be renewed for a period of three
years from the date of renewal, bearing interest at the rate of 4 per
cent per annum provided that in the event of the Trust requiring the
funds or part thereof at an earlier period, the withdrawal should be
subject to reduction of interest at the rate for the shorter period and
provided further that in the event of the Bank rate suffering Increase
the Trust should have the benefit of such increase.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
M. K. GANDHI
