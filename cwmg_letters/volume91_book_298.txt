298. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
December 26, [1921]
MY DEAR PADMAJA4,
I am surprised not to see you in Ahmedabad. You have to
be bright, strong and active if you want to do your bit of
service in the battle for freedom.
With love,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
KUMARI PADMAJA NAIDU
GOLDEN THRESHOLD
HYDERABAD
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
