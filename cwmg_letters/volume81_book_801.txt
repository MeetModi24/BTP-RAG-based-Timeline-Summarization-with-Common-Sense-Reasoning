801. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
October 31, 1945
BAPA,
Yesterday I got Mridula's letter. What you said was right.
Send three months' money to Lila Jog without any comment.
If you wish you can send it to me. I shall handle it in my
language and in my own way. We shall thus close the chapter.
Fix any days between November 22 and 28.
BAPU
KASTURBA GANDHI SMARAK NIDHI
WARDHA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
