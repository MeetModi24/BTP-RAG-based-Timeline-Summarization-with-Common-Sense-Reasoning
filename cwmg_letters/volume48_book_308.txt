308. LETTER TO RABINDRANATH TAGORE
LABURNUM ROAD,
BOMBAY,
January 3, 1932
DEAR GURUDEV,
I am just stretching my tired limbs on the mattress and as
I try to steal a wink of sleep I think of you. I want you to give
your best to the sacrificial fire that is being lighted.
With love,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 4632
