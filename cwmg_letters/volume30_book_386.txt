386. LETTER TO A TRUSTEE FOR PARSI RUSTOMJEE
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 19, 1926
You rarely write to me but as I also do likewise I suppose I
must not complain. I was happy to hear personally from Sorabji
all the good news about you and your business.
This is written to tell you that I would like to help Sorabji
in the manner proposed by him if it is at all possible. You will
see from a copy of my letter to Mr. Doull what is proposed. Please
see if the thing can be at all done.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19477
