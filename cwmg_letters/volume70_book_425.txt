425. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
ITARSI,
November 18, 1939
CHI. BHANSALI,
Nayan is pining for a letter from you. It is your duty, since
it is ahimsa, to satisfy her. We may not keep up personal rela-
tions with our relatives but we should not hurt them either. As
the circle of our kith and kin widens, we must not get involved
and maintain relations with them only for the sake of service.
Take care of your health in your present exertions.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 433
