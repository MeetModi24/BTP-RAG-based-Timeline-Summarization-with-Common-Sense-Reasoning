351. LETTER TO UTTAMCHAND GANGARAM
SEVAGRAM,
March 27, 1945
MY DEAR UTTAMCHAND,
Here is the answer to your puzzle. It is from one who is familiar
with a billiards table.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI UTTAMCHAND GANGARAM
HYDERABAD
