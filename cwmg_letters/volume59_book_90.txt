90. LETTER TO C. ABDUL HAKIM
September 27, 1934
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for your letter forwarding resolution of the Dis-
trict Board, North Arcot. I am grateful to the Board for the
resolution.
Yours sincerely,
JANAB C. ABDUL HAKIM SAHEB BAHADUR
PRESIDENT, DISTRICT BOARD, NORTH ARCOT
ARKONAM
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
