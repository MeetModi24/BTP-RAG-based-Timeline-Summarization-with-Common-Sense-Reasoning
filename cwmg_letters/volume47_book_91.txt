91. LETTER TO GANGABEHN AND NANIBEHN JHAVERI
BORSAD,
June 29, 1931
CHI. GANGABEHN AND NANIBEHN,
As it is 10.30 p.m., I will not write much. In the circum-
stances supposed in Nanibehn's question, our duty is to be quiet.
The responsibility of what happens after we have advised others,
does not rest on us. The lesson that the Gita teaches is that
we should leave the result to God. We are only His servants
carrying out His orders. Sing the song: "Let me be your servant."
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3116
