16. LETTER TO ANNE MARIE PETERSEN
June 4, 1932
"Be careful for nothing" is one of the verses that has ever re-
mained with me and taken possession of me. If God is, why need
I care? He is the Infallible Caretaker. He is a foolish man who
fusses although he is well protected.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, p. 201
