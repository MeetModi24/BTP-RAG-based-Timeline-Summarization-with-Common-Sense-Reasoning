122. LETTER TO VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 19, 1939
BHAI VALLABHRAM,
I have your letter. It will make me very happy indeed if
Chandanbehn is completely cured. If you have the courage to
treat Vijaya's father3, go and visit him even without being sent for
and examine him. Vijaya should be in Varad. There has been
no letter from her for some time.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VALLABHRAM VAIDYA
MANDVINI POLE,
DEVNI SHERI
AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 2907. Courtesy: Vallabhram Vaidya
