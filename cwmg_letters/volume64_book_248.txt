248. LETTER TO JAI SINGH
[Before January 10, 1937]
If Sikhism is a part of Hindu culture, as I believe it is,
what is the meaning of change of faith and name? If Sikhism is
not part and product of Hinduism you are simply creating a
conflict among the Harijans without achieving any noble purpose.
The Bombay Chronicle, 12-1-1937
