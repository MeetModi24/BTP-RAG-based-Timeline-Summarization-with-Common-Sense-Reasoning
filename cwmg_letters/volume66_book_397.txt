397. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
JUHU,
December 17, 1937
CHI. LILA,
Do stay with Durga till I come. This is your duty for the
present. Even if, therefore, you must go somewhere, you should
postpone doing so. But in future also do only what is within
your capacity. We may wish to do a good many things, but if
we don't have that much strength we should give in.
I am doing fairly well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9368. Also C.W. 6643. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
