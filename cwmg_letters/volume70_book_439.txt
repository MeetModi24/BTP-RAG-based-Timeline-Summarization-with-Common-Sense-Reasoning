439. LETTER TO SARASWATI GANDHI
November 25, 1939
CHI. SARU,
I got your letter. I hope you are well. I am glad that
Kanti has arrived. Keep writing to me. I am writing this on the
train. We shall reach Wardha today.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6176.
Also C.W. 3450. Court-
esy: Kantilal Gandhi
