51. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
July 25, 1945
CHI. SITA,
I read your letter. Your curriculum is good. You are
working hard. Do not worry about whether you will pass or
not. Don't do anything at the cost of your health. Do not
write in a small hand. Examine carefully what is written in this
postcard.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUMARI SITA GANDHI
MASHRUWALA BUNGALOW
AKOLA, BERAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4954
