174. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
November 11, 1940
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I have your letter.
May every New Year, as it comes
round, increase your spirit of service.
Shankerlal should re-
cover completely. I am getting ready for a severe test. God
knows what will be the outcome.
Sarladevi must be getting better day by day.
Blessings to all from
BAPU
SHRI ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
C/O VEDANT COLLEGE
XI MAIN ROAD
MALLESWARAM, BANGALORE
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 11563
