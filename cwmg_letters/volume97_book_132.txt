132. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
RAI BAREILY,
November 13, 1929
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I have your letter, I am prepared to send you to Vijapur if you can keep
well there. And Kashi and Chhaganlal will have less worries for you. They
will get some help also. Moreover, if you find one or two students in Vijapur,
they will learn from you and you will get some help. I may take the final
decision but let me know your own views. A person who takes the decision
should have all the information. It is possible that you know something which
I do not and therefore arrive at a wrong decision.
I understand about Prem Vidyalaya. I will take care of it. I have written
to Bhagirathji. Your going there at this time would have been improper. Keep
on writing to me regularly. Your health should not deteriorate there. If there
is the slightest chance of it, you are not going to stay there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32948
1 & 2 The letters are in a different hand but the subscription in both is by Gandhiji.
