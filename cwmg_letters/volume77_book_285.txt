285. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
PANCHGANI,
July 17, 1944
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
Read the accompanying letter. If such a man can be accom-
modated in any capacity, please make use of him. I think he
is trustworthy. You need not create a special job for him. The
letter may be used only if you think that such a man can be of
real use somewhere.
Do you have in mind any shorthand writer? The Hindi
speeches may be published and sold by the Maharashtra Committee.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 4802. Courtesy: Shantikumar
N. Morarjee
