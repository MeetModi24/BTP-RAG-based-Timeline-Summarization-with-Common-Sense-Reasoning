339. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
June 15, 1937
CHI. MIRA,
I imagine your having just reached or just reaching Dal-
housie.
Subhas Babu has given ample instructions regarding
the route, expenses and timings. It is a perfect letter. Raizada
Hansraj wired offering to take you in. But I have wired, you
would be with Subhas Babu. And with the latter you would
get good medical aid. I shall expect a wire today from you.
The heat is still oppressive here. I have dropped bread al-
together.
Here is Rajkumari's letter.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6381. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9847
