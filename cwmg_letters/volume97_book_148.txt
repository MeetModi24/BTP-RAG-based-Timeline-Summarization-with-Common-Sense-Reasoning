148. LETTER TO JYOTI PRAKASH
ASHRAM SABARMATI,
February 9, 1930
BHAISHRI 5 JYOTI PRAKASH,
I have received your letter. Do include the Ramayana in the curriculum.
Keep sending me your diary every week. Give each hour's account of your
activities in it.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JYOTIPRAKASH
LALA GYAN PRAKASHJI MASTER
RAMJAS SCHOOL, No. 2
ANAND PARBAT
DELHI
From the Hindi original: S.N. 34066
