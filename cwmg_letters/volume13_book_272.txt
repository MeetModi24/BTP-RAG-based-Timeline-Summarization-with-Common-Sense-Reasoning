272. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
MOTIHARI,
Sunday [April 15, 1917]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
I hope you have received my letter from Muzaffarpur. So
far nothing has happened. I have arrived at Motihari. Share
the news in my letter only with those with whom you deem it pro-
per to do so. If you want to refer anything to me, please do so. I
shall reply if I am free. It appears I shall have to stay long here.
Let all of you be calm. Share this with all the inmates of the
Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
Narhari is translating Gokhale's speeches. So also is Govadia.
Make inquiries of them and ask them to do it quickly. Chha-
ganlal may keep in touch [with their progress].
