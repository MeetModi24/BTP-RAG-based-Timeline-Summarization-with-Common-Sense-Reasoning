274. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
October 2, 1932
CHI. CHHAGANLAL (JOSHI),
Do not expect a long letter for some time yet. I have start-
ed writing letters to the Ashram and intend to write to as many
persons as I can. You exercised good self-control in not coming
to see me. If you had come, I would have certainly welcomed
you and not found fault with you. But I admire you the better
for not coming. The get-up of Ashramvasi Pratye is beautiful in-
deed. As for the contents, I have yet to look into them.
I know that anti-untouchability work in villages is very
difficult. Only workers like you can judge how far the awakening
witnessed during the week of the fast has spread among the vil-
lages. I think the fast should have been longer for that to have
happened. But this is merely how the matter appears to human
reason. I fasted as long as it was God's will that I should. Who
knows how many more fasts He will make me undertake in
future? We should thus be content to live as He wills, and be
ready to dance with joy even if He throws us into a pan of boiling
oil. Has He not given His word that He will give us the strength
to do so?
BAPU
