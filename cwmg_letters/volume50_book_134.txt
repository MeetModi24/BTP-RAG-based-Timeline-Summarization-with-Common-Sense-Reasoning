134. LETTER TO INDU N. PAREKH
July 1, 1932
DEAR INDU,
Your weight must be said to have been much reduced. For
the present leave off prayers. In the morning, sleep as long as you
can. Increase the proportion of milk and reduce that of any other
thing you like. Then see if the weight increases or not. It is
enough that what you can do, you should do with attention and
interest. Never mind if less (work) is done for the present.
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No. 800
(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 55
