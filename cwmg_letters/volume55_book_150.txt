150. LETTER TO GANGABEHN B. JHAVERI
May 7, 1933
CHI. GANGABEHN JHAVERI,
I got your letter.
If you observe proper rules about eating and remain
calm, your health must improve.
It is very necessary that you should be consistent in any
treatment which you try.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3954
