275. LETTER TO M. M. ANANTA RAU
March 30, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Forgive me. I evidently missed your
question. You may show the correspondence between us to any-
body you like.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. M. M. ANANTA RAU
SANATANA DHARMA KARYALAYA
40 ISWARDAS LALA ST.
TRIPLICANE, MADRAS
From the original: C.W. 9579
