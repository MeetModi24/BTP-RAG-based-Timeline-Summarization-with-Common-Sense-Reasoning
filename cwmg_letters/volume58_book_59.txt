59. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
June 4, 1934
Though the limited freedom granted to me was conveyed
to me orally by the Superintendent, it was actually a tele-
phone message that he read out. At that time I accepted it,
but within half an hour after he had left I wrote out and dis-
patched a letter to the Government.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 132
