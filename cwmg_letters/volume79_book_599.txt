599. LETTER TO LILAVATI MUNSHI
April 6, 1945
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have gone through your letter. Why should I feel hurt?
But I am not prepared to go to Chembur. Taking me there
will be disastrous. I would like to go there for Munshi's sake
and for your sake. But I would not knowingly transgress the
limit I have laid down for myself. Do not compel me to do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. LILAVATI MUNSHI
26 RIDGE ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
