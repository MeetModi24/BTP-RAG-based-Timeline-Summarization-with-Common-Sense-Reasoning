150. LETTER TO MATHURI N. KHARE
February 18, 1932
CHI. MATHURI,
I get your letters regularly. But I hope you will satisfy my
wish. Learn the alphabet carefully with Gajanan's help and then
write to me in a beautiful hand. Won't you do this?
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 262. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
