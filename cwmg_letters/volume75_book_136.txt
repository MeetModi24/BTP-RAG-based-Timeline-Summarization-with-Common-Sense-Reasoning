136. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 10, 1941
MY DEAR A.,
Your letter. It is well Rajasaheb is gone. He was a living
death. I felt I should send you a wire. And so one is going.
Bul has been here for a few days. Gosibehn came in last night
from Mussorie having seen Indu and J[awahar] L[al].
I reported to you about the apples.
This record from Vaidya is very good. You should write to
him.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3683. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6492
