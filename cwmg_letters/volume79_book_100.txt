100. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
SEVAGRAM,
January 23, 1945
CHI. BABUDI,
What you say is correct. Nevertheless I would certainly pay
some attention. And Shakaribehn is bound to look after you. I
know, however, that the right place for you is where Chokhawala
lives. And I can have nothing to say if you keep good health.
After knowing my mind, therefore, you may do as you wish. But
please bear in mind that whenever you wish to come here you
can do so without hesitation. You need not feel hesitant about
coming to me.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10051.
Courtesy: Sharda G.
Chokhawala
