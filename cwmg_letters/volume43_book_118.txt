118. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
BUVA,
Sunday, March 23, 1930
CHI. PREMA,
You seem to have taken a vow not to write to me. I know
that you are submerged in work. That is precisely why I must
have a letter from you. You should not work so much that you
may fall ill. Speak less loudly and spare your throat.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-5: Ku. Premabehn Kantakne, p. 9
