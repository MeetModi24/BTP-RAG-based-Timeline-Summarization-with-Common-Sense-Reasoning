185. LETTER TO MANJULABEHN M. MEHTA
[November 13, 1940]
Urmila is enjoying herself. Today she brought her autograph
book and got everyone to write something in it. I asked her if
she was getting everyone to write in it, to which she replied that
she approached only those whom she thought to be good people.
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 1612.
Courtesy: Manjulabehn M.
Mehta
