95. LETTER TO GOVIND PATEL
January 10, 1931
CHI. GOVIND,
I got your beautiful letter. The word is not dandvrat but
dandavat and the phrase means respectful greetings offered by
prostrating oneself straight like a stick on the ground. In the
original phrase, dandavat is an adjective and the noun, pranam1, is
understood. But we have been using the word dandavat as a noun too.
Blessings from
BAPU
