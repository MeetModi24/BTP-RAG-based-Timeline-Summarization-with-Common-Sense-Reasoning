13. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
May 22, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
My condolences on the loss of your companion, the squirrel.
You are right in thinking that those round you do not act up to
the principle they profess. They do not realize that mere re-
fraining from killing is not enough. It is necessary to show active
sympathy for sufferers.
You should not be anxious about me. The march will do
me good. I am certainly keeping well.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Enclosed for Lalji living there.
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
