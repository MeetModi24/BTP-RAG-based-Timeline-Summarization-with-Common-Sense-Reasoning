4. LETTER TO SHAMALDAS GANDHI
November 1, 1945
CHI. SHAMALDAS,
The character sketch you have given of Sardar is good but
how is it that I don't find Mahadev in it? If you can think of
Sardar without Manibehn, only then can you think of him with-
out Mahadev. Is it merely due to oversight? Or is there art in
leaving out Mahadev?
Blessings from
BAPU
