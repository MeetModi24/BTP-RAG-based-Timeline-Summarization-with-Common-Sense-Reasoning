725. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
[October 22, 1945]
CHI. BABUDI,
Why do you fall ill? If your food habits and ventilation
are taken care of nothing should happen to you. Do you drink
boiled water? Do you know how to repeat Ramanama?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.G. 128
