61. LETTER TO LILAVATI MUNSHI
NEW DELHI,
October 27, 1946
CHI. LILAVATI (MUNSHI),
I have your letter. I like your idea. But for the time
being you or people like you will not be going with me.
I have no idea what God wants me to do. I shall see my way
after I reach there. And if I feel the need, I may write to
you or send a wire.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
