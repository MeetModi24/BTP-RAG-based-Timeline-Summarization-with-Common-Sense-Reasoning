74. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM,
April 20, 1942
CHI. MIRA,
I have your letter. I must let you come here and see what
can be done. You are undertaking a job which leaves you no
other consideration. I have sent you a wire. I do not know
whether it will reach you. Everything has become so uncertain
nowadays.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6496. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9891
