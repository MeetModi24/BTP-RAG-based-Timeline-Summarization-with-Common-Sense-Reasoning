269. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
January 29, 1933
BHAI MOOLCHAND,
(i) The varna system proper is no longer in practice and
there are no varnas like the Bhangi or the Chamar. If we have
to make a proper division we have to take into consideration the
qualities of each individual.
(ii) It is a matter of one's inclination.
(iii) Inter-dining should be left entirely to individual
choice.
(iv) Who bothers about anyone's wearing the yajnopavit these
days? Having no preference in this matter I have no opinion to
offer.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 769
