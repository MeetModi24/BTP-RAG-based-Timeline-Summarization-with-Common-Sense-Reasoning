602. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
April 18, 1935
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
I got your letter today. May you live long and may your
aspirations be fulfilled. I had got your previous letter, too. I
shall be satisfied if you improve your health somehow . Your plan
of going to Chorwad is a good one. Can't one serve wherever
one happens to be?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
