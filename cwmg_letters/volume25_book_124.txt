124. LETTER TO ANNIE BESANT
DELHI,
September 18, 1924
DEAR DR. BESANT,
I thank you for your letter. You know of my fast. I shall not
therefore have the privilege of presiding at the celebration of 2nd
October. Of course, I shall hope to send a message.
Regarding the Congress passing the constitution I see many
difficulties. But I am keeping an open mind. We shall discuss
it when we can meet. I do not expect any untoward result from
the fast.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
