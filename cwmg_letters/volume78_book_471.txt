471. LETTER TO AKBARALI I. LOKHANDWALA
SEVAGRAM,
November 24, 1944
BHAI AKBARALI,
I have your letter. What advice, much less help, can I offer
you? I don't even fully understand this kind of work. I am
sorry that you are in trouble and that things are getting worse.
May God soon relieve you from your troubles.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
AKBARALI ISMAILJI LOKHANDWALA
GODHRA
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
