359. LETTER TO PALTU JHA
SODEPUR,
December 22, 1945
DEAR PANDITJI,
I have your letter.
I have already given you my reply with regard to Sanskrit.
I have written at length about Varnashrama. A collection of my
articles' has also been published. I have expressed my present
views in the foreword . The gist of it is that it is the duty of
every Hindu to regard himself as a Harijan, that is, the lowest
among the Shudras. Thus alone can Hinduism be purified and
saved. This includes my reply to your questions.
I cannot say when I shall be able to read your two books.
I should like to read them.
You have the name and address on your card printed in
English! Why and for whom?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
