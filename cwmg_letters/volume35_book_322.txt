322. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Monday [January 2, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got both your letters. On my part, I have tried my best
to bring you here and am still trying. Let's see what God wills.
Keep in mind the verse from the second chapter of the Gita:
"Whose mind is untroubled in sorrows and longeth not for joys".
My health is fair. I shall certainly be here for the next three
months at least. Mrityunjaya and Vidyavati are both doing
well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3303
