92. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 3, 1930
CHI. SHARDA (BABU),
I receive your letters fairly regularly. You must make it a
habit to revise whatever you may write. No letter should be re-
garded as completed unless you have revised it. Ask Chimanlal
to write to me. I hope you are having no more asthma.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9886. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
wala
