496. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
September 8, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
If evil thoughts come to mind it means that the mind is
empty and Nature abhors a vacuum. So when the mind is not
reciting Ramanama or doing His work, then the Devil takes
possession of it. So when evil thoughts invade the mind, the only
way to check their attack is to recite Ramanama and to do
Rama's work. It can never fail. Go on increasing the recitation
of Ramanama till you succeed. There is no other remedy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4403
