343. LETTER TO AYLMER MAUDE
LONDON,
November 10, 1909
DEAR MR. MAUDE,
I have not succeeded in getting the Manchester Guardian to take up
Tolstoy's "Letter to a Hindoo". I have not been able to go to the British
Museum myself, but I asked a friend to look up Ballows' books. His
books are there.
Could you please now tell me whether you would act as co-judge
with Dr. Clifford with reference to the Passive Resistance Essay.
I remain, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the handwritten original signed by Gandhiji: C. W. 4439
