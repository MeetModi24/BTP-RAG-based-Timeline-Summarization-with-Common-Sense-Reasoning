23. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
WARDHAGANJ,
October 9, 1935
CHI. GANGABEHN,
It is a difficult time for you. Tell Bachubhai that he should
not lose his peace of mind in spite of his painful condition. Our
faith is tested only in adversity. Do drop me a few lines from time
to time. Nath's presence there must be a great support for you.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 85. Also C.W. 8820. Courtesy:
Gangabehn Vaidya
