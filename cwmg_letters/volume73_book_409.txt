409. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
SEVAGRAM,
February 7, 1941
DEAR AMRITLAL,
You have done well in writing as you have. The ferment will
do good. You must wait and watch and you will say that no step
will be taken that can possibly harm the village life conception.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 1455. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
