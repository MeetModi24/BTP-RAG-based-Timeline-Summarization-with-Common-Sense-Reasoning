476. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
NEW DELHI,
January 29, 1948
SHRI BALVANTSINHAJI,
What Bapuji said I am writing to you in my own words. Hoshiari-
behn had gone to Khurja, but she is now back here. She returned only
yesterday. She goes back to Khurja today, as some physician there has
promised to restore her to health in a month's time. She has decided to
take his treatment, and Bapuji has approved of her decision. Bapuji says:
"Hoshiari will be able to devote herself fully to serving others only after
she is physically all right herself, and so I have agreed to her taking the
treatment of this physician."
Please show this letter to Chimanlalbhai also. The rest you will learn
from Chimanlalbhai's letter.
With regards,
BISEN
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 1990
