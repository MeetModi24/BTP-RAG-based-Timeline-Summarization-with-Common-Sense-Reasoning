25. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS
DELHI,
January 29, 1925
MY DEAR KRISHNADAS 1 ,
The enclosed is for Pyarelal. I hope you are not worrying
about me. I am receiving all the attention I need. Mahadev is
not overworked. Deen Dayal has found himself with me again.
He has taken over personal attendance and freed Mahadev for
the most part for personal correspondance . Private negotiations are
now going on for a settlement of the Hindu-Muslim problem. It
is difficult to forecast the result. We are here till at least 31 st. I
hope you are getting stronger daily. It is very cold here and
I am glad you are not here. Mr. Andrews is here and will be
for two days longer. Tell Kikibehn I was glad to hear that she
was taking a regular tailoring class at the Ashram. It distracts
her attention and will do good, if she does not overwork herself.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 5598
