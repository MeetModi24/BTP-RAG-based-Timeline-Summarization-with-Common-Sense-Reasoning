506. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 21, 1940
STUPID GIRL,
What can I write to you? I know that it is very mean to
go off leaving me in this condition. But if what I consider
uncivilized attitude you consider duty, you must do your duty;
and in that case it becomes my duty to encourage you in that.
If you would listen to me, I would say that (1) you should not
talk of going till you have not convinced me of your position.
In other words, you must not go. And if that is not possible
(2) you should not utter a word about going till the mists have
rolled away. Even Mother, if she understood this, would not
keep you in Delhi for a moment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
