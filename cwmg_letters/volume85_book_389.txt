389. LETTER TO ANANTRAM
NEW DELHI,
September 13, 1946
CHI. ANANTRAM,
I have your letter. Have a good time in the hospital. Do
some service as well. Go to the Ashram only when you are
fully calm. Ramanama can work wonders. You have to prove
it. Who will believe one's just saying it?
What if I did not come to the jail? How can I find time
for such things?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of Hindi : S. G. 134
