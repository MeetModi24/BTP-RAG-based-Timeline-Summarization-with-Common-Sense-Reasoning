161. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
AZAMGARH,
October 3, 1929
CHI. BHANSALI,
Nobody in this world remembers everything. Some have sharp
memories, some weak. Your memory now has become weak.
Why should you feel unhappy about it? You very well know the
verse we recite in the morning. Does it not say that happiness
is not happiness and misery is not misery, that the only misery
is forgetting Vishnu, and the only happiness thinking of
Narayana? You have that happiness because you have certainly
not forgotten God. So one may say that you have all happiness.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32578/49
