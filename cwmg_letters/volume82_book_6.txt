6. LETTER TO DESHPANDE
November 1, 1945
BHAI DESHPANDE,
I have been told that those who take part in prabhatpheri
speak ill of Shri Bhopatkar. Find out about this. If they speak
ill of any leader try to stop them. What is the good of doing
this? I see the harm clearly. So long as they are in the Cong-
ress no one should speak ill of others.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
