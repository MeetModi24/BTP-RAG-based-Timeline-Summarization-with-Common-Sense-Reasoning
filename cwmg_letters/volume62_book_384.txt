384. LETTER TO BHUJANGILAL CHHAYA
April 20, 1936
CHI. BHUJANGILAL,
I have your letter. At present nothing is certain as to where
I may settle. Even after I have settled down you must obtain
your father's permission. It would not be proper to let you stay
with me against your father's wish. You have to have patience.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2638
