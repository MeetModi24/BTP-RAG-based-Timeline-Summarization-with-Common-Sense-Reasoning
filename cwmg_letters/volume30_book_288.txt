288. LETTER TO V. L. PHADKE
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Tuesday, April 6, 1926
BHAI MURABBI MAMA,
I have your letter. You can come over if you can do so with-
out any difficulty. By the 10th Nanabhai too will return from
Singhagarh. Bring with you the letter I wrote on what was talked
over with Nanabhai. Swami is here. I shall ask him about
Chhagan when I see him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3813
