375. LETTER TO A PUBLISHER
PANCHGANI,
June 3, 1945
DEAR SIR,
Gandhiji has read your letter of 28th ult. received only yesterday. He would
like to know before deciding, what profits you make out of the publication mentioned
by you and what you pay Prof. A. D. Yates.
Yours faithfully,
NARAHARI PARIKH
THE MANAGING DIRECTOR
KAPOOR BROS. LTD.
PUBLISHERS & BOOKSELLERS
52 ANARKALI, LAHORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
