199. LETTER TO DHIRUBHAI B. DESAI
June 29, 1941
CHI. DHIRUBHAI 2 ,
I had replied promptly to Bhai Motichand Kapadia's let-
ter. I have mentioned it in my note, too. Unfortunately I didn't
keep a copy. But I remember the reply, which I enclose with
this. Read it and give it to Bhai Motichand.
Your previous letter did not call for a reply. For after that
Bhai Munshi resigned and Mahadev also met him.
I hope you and Madhuri are keeping fine.
How is the atmosphere there?
Give the accompanying to Bhulabhai if you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
