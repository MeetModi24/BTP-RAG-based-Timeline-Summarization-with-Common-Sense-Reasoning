146. LETTER TO GANGASHARAN SINGH
BORSAD,
May 10, 1931
BHAI GANGASHARAN SINGHJI,
I have your letter. Success must come where Rajendrababu
is the leader. Boycott of foreign cloth, propagation of khadi and
the boycott of liquor and other intoxicants are of the utmost Im-
portance for us today.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI GANGASHARAN SINGH
CONGRESS COMMITTEE, AMAHRA
P.O. NIHATTA, DIST. PATNA, BIHAR
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 11008
