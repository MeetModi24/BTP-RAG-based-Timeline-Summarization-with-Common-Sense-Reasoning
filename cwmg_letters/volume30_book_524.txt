524. LETTER TO SEWA RAM
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 12, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
Fearlessness comes out of a living faith in the existence of the
soul apart from the body and its persistence after the dissolution
of the body. And this is attained by a continuous realization of
the futility of all worldly ambition.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SEWA RAM
22, JAIL ROAD
LAHORE
From a photostat: S.N. 19551
