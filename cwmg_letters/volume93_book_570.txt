570. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
ON THE TRAIN,
September 26, 1940
CHI. LILI,
I have your letter. You must not think about anything
else at all. Your duty is to become a
doctor. With
this
thought in mind, concentrate on your studies. Lakshmidas is
in the Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
LILAVATIBEHN UDESHI
KANJI KHETSI CHHATRALAYA
65 MINT ROAD
BOMBAY FORT
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
