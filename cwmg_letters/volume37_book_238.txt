238. LETTER TO DR. JOSIAH OLDFIELD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 24, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to have your letter. Though I could not visit
Europe this year, if all goes well, I expect to do so next year,
when I have no doubt we shall meet somewhere. Of course if you
anticipate your proposed visit to India, we shall meet here no
doubt.
Yours sincerely,
DR. JOSIAH OLDFIELD
LADY MARGARET HOSPITAL, DODDINGTON, KENT
From a photostat: S.N. 14366
