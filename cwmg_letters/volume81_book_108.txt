108. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
SEVAGRAM,
August 2, 1945
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letters. Since Jairamdas is out I see no need to
write to you. Abha has kept one skein of yarn for you.
Vidya was good but cannot take the place of God. I am
an iconoclast. So I wanted you to forget Vidya. But we see
God in numerous images. You have chosen to see God in the
image of Vidya. So long as the delusion persists who can make
you understand? If you can forget her easily do so. Then
Vidya will rise and also you.
Nowadays my movements are very uncertain. So please do
not come. Stay there and recover and do what service you can.
Jairamdas has gone to Lucknow taking Premi with him.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Gokhaleji died at Bhimavaram.
