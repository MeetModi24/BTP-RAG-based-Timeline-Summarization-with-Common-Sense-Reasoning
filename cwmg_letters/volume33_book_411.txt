411. LETTER TO TARUN CHANDRA SINHA
NANDI HILLS (MYSORE STATE),
May 31, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. At the present moment I can only ask
you to read the reprint of the articles I wrote in Young India.
The reprint is called Self-restraint V. Self-indulgence. It can be had
at the Young India office, Ahmedabad. And if you propose to get
the book and read it, you may correspond with me after you have
read it and tell me whether you have found any help. Mean-
while I can only pray that God may give you the right guidance
and strength of mind to do the right thing.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. TARUN CHANDRA SINHA
SHUSUNG P.O.
(MYMENSING DISTRICT)
BIHAR
From a photostat: S.N. 14136
