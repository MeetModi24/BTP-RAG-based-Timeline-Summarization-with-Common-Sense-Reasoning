351. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
CHI. VASUMATI,
Received your letter. It is all right. Stay there only for the
present and improve your health. You are already having good
company. Hence, the only complaint can be about the absence of my
letter, is it not? I shall not leave any cause for that complaint.
Moreover, the climate would soon be cold there.
Let us see what happens when I go to Ahmedabad in July. Come
there if you cannot help.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9366
