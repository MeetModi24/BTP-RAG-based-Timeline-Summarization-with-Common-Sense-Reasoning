484. LETTER TO NAGINBHAI T. MASTER
SODEPUR,
January 5, 1946
BHAI NAGINBHAI,
I have your letter. May your efforts for the sake of Vijaya-
behn and Valji bear fruit. I can see that you will be able to
cope with the task, and I am pleased.
SJT. NAGINBHAI MASTER
BOMBAY CONGRESS COMMITTEE
CONGRESS HOUSE
VITHALBHAI PATEL ROAD, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
