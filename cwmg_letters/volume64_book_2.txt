2. LETTER TO KASTURBHAI LALBHAI
ON THE TRAIN,
November 3, 1936
BHAI KASTURBHAI,
I have gone through the papers concerning Gordhanbhai's
demands. He has no right to ask for the money. Assistance can
be given for the labourers' school in Nadiad but its [accounts]
should be scrutinized and the school should function under the
supervision of the Majoor Mahajan and your association. Such
is my firm opinion. The correspondence suggests that, accord-
ing to the resolution passed, a trust-deed should immediately be
made.
I am returning the papers.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
