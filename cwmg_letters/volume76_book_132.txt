132. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 11, 1942
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
Bari Khan often rings me up to say I should let you come.
He says you have been asking him to obtain my permission for
you to come. This does not seem right. You have been staying
there because it is your duty to do so. How then do you say that
you want my permission to come? Mother is going to Patiala.
If you want to see her you can do so at a wayside station. I
have suggested that she should go via Wardha and stay there
for a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 473
