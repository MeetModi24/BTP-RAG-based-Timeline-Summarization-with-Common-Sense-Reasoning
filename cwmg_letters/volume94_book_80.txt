80. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 23, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you today. Pyarelal did not come.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
