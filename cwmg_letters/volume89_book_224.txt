224. LETTER TO J. P. BHANSALI
NEW DELHI,
September 19, 1947
CHI. BHANSALI,
I got both your letters. You are sure to render service wher-
ever you are. But I see that your mind continues to be unsteady.
You are not satisfied with the Ashram. I think you are certainly
being useful to the Ashram. Your boarding at the Ashram there-
fore is no less than charity. Even in the village you will certainly
be fed by the people. Thus as in the immortal words of Akha
Bhagat: "Live as you may, but know God anyhow."
Blessings from
BAPU
