156. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[On or after February 11, 1934]
DEAR DAUGHTER,
You are stupid. Who told you I was displeased? Do you
not see I have put myself in front of you? I came up to you
twice and you were asleep. I do not come to you often, for I
am busy. But I direct everything from here. But you are stupid,
obstinate and sensitive. No silence today or tomorrow. You
must learn to obey cheerfully. Your peace lies in obedience not
in wilful silence or anything else. That is the meaning of dis-
cipline and faith.
Did you understand?
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 292; also Bapuke Patra-8: Bibi Amtussalaam Ke
Nam, p. 44
