143. LETTER TO AVADHESH DUTT AVASTHI
May 24, 1935
CHI. AVADHESH,
Service to all living beings may be said to be the object of
human life.
2. The observance of the eleven vows and avoidance of their
opposites is essential.
3. Garuda, indeed, spoke in anger; but it does not mean
that we, too, may get angry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3215
