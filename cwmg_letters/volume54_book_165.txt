165. LETTER TO M. MEYAPPA
March 21, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of 17th instant and I am glad that
you distributed khadi to poor Harijans and that you presided at an
anti-untouchability meeting. There are many more things that
you can do, and these you can study from the pages of the Harijan.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. M. MEYAPPA
VETRIYUR, via SIVAGANGA (S.I.R.)
