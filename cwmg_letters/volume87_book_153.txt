153. LETTER TO NAGEN BABU
PATNA,
March 25, 1947
BHAI NAGEN BABU,
I got your letter. Bhai Kalipada will give you all the news.
I can only say that my heart is in Noakhali, and whatever I
do here is bound to have some effect there. No one need be
scared and no one should be a coward. What God wills will
be done.
Yours,
M. K.
