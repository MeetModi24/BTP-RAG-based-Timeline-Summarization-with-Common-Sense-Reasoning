403. LETTER TO TARA MASHRUWALA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 8, 1930
CHI. TARA (OF AKOLA),
I have your letter. (After how many months did I get one ?)
You say nothing about the headache from which you suffered
after the injury to your head. Give me one day's sample from
your diary. You can teach Sita a little Sanskrit even as she plays
with you. She should of course sing Jhanda Uncha Rahe Hamara1,
but likewise she should also keep murmuring some easy verse
from the Gita.
Give my blessings to Mother.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4775
