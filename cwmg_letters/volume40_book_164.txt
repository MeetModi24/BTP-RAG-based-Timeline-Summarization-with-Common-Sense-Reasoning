164. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Silence Day [March 25, 1929]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. Vasumatibehn writes that you are suffer-
ing from headaches. Father's letter has arrived. I am enclosing
it. The rest on meeting.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Rajendrababu is here.
[PPS.]
Tell Vasumatibehn and Kusumbehn that I am not writing
to them today.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3322
