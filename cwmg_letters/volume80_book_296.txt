296. LETTER TO KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 24, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have been looking forward to your coming.
I have now
your note deferring coming for I come there on 31st. So I am
sending you letters I kept for you.
On getting the news you have given me I said to myself:
"Beware of the Greeks especially when they bring gifts. This
may be all unjustified; the relief may be genuine." Let us
hope so.
Love to you all.
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
