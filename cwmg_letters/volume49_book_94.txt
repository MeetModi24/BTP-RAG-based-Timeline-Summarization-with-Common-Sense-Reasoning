94. LETTER TO NIRMALA H. DESAI
February 4, 1932
CHI. NIRMALA2,
There are indeed some persons who shed tears when they
are happy and smile when they suffer. Our effort should be not
to shed tears or smile in either condition.
On what subjects have you written compositions? Which
poems do you like best in the text for the seventh form?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9463
