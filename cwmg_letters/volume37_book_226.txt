226. LETTER TO C. A. ALEXANDER
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 18, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your prompt reply. I have now heard from
Mr. Saklatwala too.
Many thanks for your enquiry about my health which I am
glad to say is quite good.
I have now returned to Sabarmati.
Yours sincerely,
C. A. ALEXANDER, ESQ.
GENERAL MANAGER, THE TATA IRON AND STEEL CO. LTD.
JAMSHEDPUR (via TATANAGAR)
From a microfilm: S.N. 12778
