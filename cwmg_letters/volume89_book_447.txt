447. LETTER TO VALLABHRAM
NEW DELHI,
November 3, 1947
BHAI VALLABHRAM,
I got your letter.
I cannot excuse your falling or remaining ill. How can a
vaid who remains ill himself cure other people? This applies to
you vaids as to hakims and doctors.
You can stay in the bungalow at Panchgani. It is meant to
be given rent-free only to Harijans. But it is given on rent to
other respectable people also when it is not occupied.
You may use the accompanying letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : C. W. 2924. Courtesy: Vallabhram Vaidya
