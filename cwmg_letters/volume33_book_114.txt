114. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
GULBARGA,
Maha Vad 6 [February 22, 1927]
PRIEST OF DHEDS AND BHILS,
Please do come, and do bring Dheds and Bhangis along with
you. Please inform Jugatram or whoever is to be informed so that
he may make the necessary arrangements. If they carry their
food and bed with them as also a lota and a rope and are prepared
to sleep on the ground, they need not give any prior intimation.
We have just reached Gulbarga by the train we took at
Sholapur. And I have got some time to write letters; I am steal-
ing a few moments to indulge in a little fun with you, and also
finish off some work by the way.
Hail, Thakkar Bapa, high priest of the lowly ones!
Whatever you would have from
BAPU
[PS.]
'Bapu' is certainly better than 'Mahatma'.
