339. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
March 17, 1940
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I read your letter as well as your note. I share your unhap-
piness. I am confident that nothing less can satisfy us on this occasion.
I see no defect in my scheme. It is for their good also. Their unwil-
lingness proves that they do not like independence for India. The
report about the Princes is intolerable. Who told you that I do not
wish to see them? I shall meet them at the slightest hint. The fact
is they do not want to see me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am prepared to go to Calcutta for the Seva Sadan, if you so
desire.
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8035. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
