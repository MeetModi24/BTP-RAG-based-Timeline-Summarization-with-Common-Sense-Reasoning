133. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 20, 1933
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
You must have got the wire I sent about you. I sent a letter
too. Your mind must not waver. Call it my order or my wish,
doing or not doing what I have said is totally in your hands.
You will give up all worry if you follow what I say. May God
grant you recovery and peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 273
