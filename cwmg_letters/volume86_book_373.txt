373. LETTER TO SAMPURNANAND
December 30, 1946
BHAI SAMPURNANANDJI 1 ,
How can I ignore your letter? I trust the silver jubilee
will be celebrated in fine style. May Kashi Vidyapeeth bring
forth true workers.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi: C. W. 10428. Courtesy: Kashi Vidyapeeth
