370. LETTER TO MILLIE GRAHAM POLAK
[NADIAD,]
July 29, 1918
MY DEAR MILLIE,
Sorabji is no more. A cable has just arrived from Johannesburg
giving the sad news. There is nothing striking about this death.
Many like Sorabji have died before now. But Sorabji has
played such an important part in our lives that his sudden death
comes like a rude shock. It is only one's faith in the indestructi-
bility of energy and continuity of effort that reconciles one to acti-
vity in life. An event is a shock when we do not understand its
purpose and its seasonableness. But in God's plan, I suppose,
there is nothing unseasonable and nothing purposeless.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary, Courtesy: Narayan Desai
