485. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
NEW DELHI,
July 27, 1947
CHI. VALJI,
You are quite right. I can do nothing in a matter such as
this. To the innocent, punishment is like a reward, and the
guilty repays a part of his debt through it. Both, therefore,
should shed fear and appear in the court without any lawyers. I
will go through the draft. If God requires my services, He will
let me live not a hundred and twenty-five but a hundred and
fifty years, and if He doesn't require them any longer He may
take me away even today. One must live as Rama bids.
Blessings from
BAPU
PROF. VALJI G. DESAI
14 GANESHWADI
POONA-
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 7503. Courtesy: V. G. Desai
