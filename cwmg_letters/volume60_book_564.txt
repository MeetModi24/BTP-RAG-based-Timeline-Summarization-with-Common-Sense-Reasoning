564. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
April 10, 1935
BHAI JIVANJI,
I finished Yeravda Mandir ten days ago. I hope to finish
Gandhi Vichar Dohan tomorrow. The remaining things I think I
shall be able to finish when I take a vow of silence next time.
But I shall see. I have not enjoyed a minute's leisure during the
present period of silence.
Read the accompanying letter. Wherever Mohanlal is, you
may send him if you wish to. Do what is necessary and inform
me. Do you want Yeravda Mandir immediately?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9939. Also C.W. 6914. Courtesy:
Jivanji D. Desai
