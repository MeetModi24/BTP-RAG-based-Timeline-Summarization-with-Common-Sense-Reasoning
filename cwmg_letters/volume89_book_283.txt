283. LETTER TO ANANTRAI P. PATTANI
[October 1, 1947]
CHI. ANANTRAI,
I have your wire. You sent a similar one to the Sardar,
which was quite right. Now that the whole administration has
come into India's hands, let us caution the person in charge of
the department concerned. That should suffice. Act as the Sardar
advises.
Blessings from
BAPU
ANANTRAI PATTANI
BHAVNAGAR
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
