444. LETTER TO KUNVARJI PAREKH
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 12, 1930
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I have your letter. Chi. Rami will leave for Rajkot by the
6th or the 7th. Will that be all right and serve the purpose? I
hope you are keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9715
1 According to the Gujarati calendar
