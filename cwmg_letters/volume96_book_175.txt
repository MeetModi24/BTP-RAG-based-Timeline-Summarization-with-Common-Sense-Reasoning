175. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
May 6, 1914
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
Jeki has been found lying more than once. I therefore told her that
she could not stay away from her husband unless she was absolutely
superior to him. Indeed she is inferior to her husband. The latter is
no hypocrite. But Jeki is a finished hypocrite. You may understand
what all these discoveries must have cost me. However she has now
consented to go to Fiji. I have Dr. Mehta's authority by cable. So she
goes next Sunday escorted by Miss West. She leaves by a Castle boat
for Cape Town, and will there book for Fiji.
You did not ask for the English inscription. You had it there. You
