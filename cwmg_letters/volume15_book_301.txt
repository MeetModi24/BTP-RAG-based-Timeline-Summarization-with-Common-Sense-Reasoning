301. LETTER TO VALJI DESAI
BOMBAY,
Wednesday [May 28, 1919]
BHAISHRI VALJI 2 ,
I have been wanting to write to you. I find it possible to do
so today. May your married life be happy and prove beneficial
to the country. Let me know to whom you got married. I trust
you are quite well.
I should like you to write something for Young India.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: C.W. 3164. Courtesy: V. G. Desai
