109. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 3, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
You have fallen ill again. This is sin. You overworked and
did not care about food and sleep. Why? The body is meant for
service. How can we render service, if we do not keep it fit?
You did not exert yourself too much in giving yourself the
massage, did you? Take rest and recover. Take quinine if it is
necessary. Eat well. Take fruit. Sleep well. Use a mosquito-net. I
have written to Govind. Read the letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 9027. Also C.W. 9151. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
