487. LETTER TO BISWANATH DAS
SEVAGRAM,
March 22, 1945
DEAR BISWANATH DAS,
Let the young man you mention write after the hot season,
i. e., after June. I am likely to be away about 10th April. Hope
you are well.
Love.
BAPU
BISWANATH DAS
BELLAGAM, P. O. PULSORA
DIST. GANJAM, ORISSA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
