94. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Saturday [March 23, 1929]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have all your letters. I would learn about Andhra Desh on
reaching Calcutta. We will reach there tomorrow.
Kusumbehn must have arrived. I am not writing to her sepa-
rately.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : S.N. 32847
1 From the contents; vide Vol.XL, pp. 129, 173, and 177. Gandhiji
reached Calcutta on March 24, 1929, a Sunday.
