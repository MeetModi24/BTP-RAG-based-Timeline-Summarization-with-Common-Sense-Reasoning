220. LETTER TO S. A. BRELVI
NEW DELHI,
September 18, 1947
BHAI BRELVI,
I have your telegram. This is the complete English report.
There is no time to write more. It is difficult to say what will
ultimately happen in Delhi. If the Pakistan Government can
bring about some improvement, the people here can return to
sanity. For my part I am pledged to do or die.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
