544. LETTER TO DHIRENDRA CHATTERJEE
SEVAGRAM,
December 3, 1944
CHI. DHIREN,
I have your letter. From tomorrow my silence starts. I shall
not be writing letters till the 31st. I am happy to learn that
you are doing well. By any means get rid of your constipation.
Sailen has improved a lot.
Blessings from
BAPU
DHIREN CHATTERJEE
KHADI PRATISHTHAN
