29. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA (C. P.),
December 10, 1939
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
What is this cutting about? I should like to see your speech
and like you to reply to the article.
What an extraordinary fatwa of Jinnah about Congress
Ministries!
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1939. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
