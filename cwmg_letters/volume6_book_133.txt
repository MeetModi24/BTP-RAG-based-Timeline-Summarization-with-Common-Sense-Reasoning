133. LETTER TO MRS. FREETH
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 8, 1906
DEAR MRS. FREETH,
I am sending this letter on the off chance of its reaching you. If
you receive it you will be surprised to know that I am in London. As
my departure from Johannesburg was very hurried I forgot to take
your address with me. I asked my clerk to send it, but have not yet
received it. I shall be exceedingly sorry if I have to leave London without
seeing you. If you receive this letter, I hope you will let me know
immediately your proper address.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. FREETH
LATE MRS. PILCHER
ST. JOHN'S WOOD ROAD
LONDON
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4517
