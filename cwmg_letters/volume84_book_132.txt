132. LETTER TO MOHAN AND ANASUYA PARIKH
SIMLA,
May 6, 1946
CHI. MOHAN AND CHI. ANASUYA,
I knew the date of your wedding but I wished to say nothing
on that occasion. May you both lead shining lives, dedicate your-
selves to service and live up to the age of 125, serving till the end.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9191
1 Wife of Munnalal G. Shah
2 Dr. Dinshaw Mehta
3 May 4, 1946
