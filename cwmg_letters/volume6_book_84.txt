84. LETTER TO SIR HENRY COTTON
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 2, 1906
DEAR SIR HENRY,
I am obliged to you for your letter of the 1st instant. I have now
placed myself in communication with Mr. Harold Cox. I went over
to the House twice to see him but I could not catch him.
Yours sincerely,
SIR HENRY COTTON, M.P.
45, ST. JOHN'S WOOD PARK, N.W.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4465
