271. LETTER TO THE NIZAM OF HYDERABAD
POST ANDHERI,
April 5, 1924
TO
HIS EXALTED HIGHNESS THE NIZAM OF HYDERABAD
HYDERABAD
(DECCAN)
YOUR EXALTED HIGHNESS,
I beg to acknowledge Your Exalted Highness's letter of the
1st April. I received also the letter of the 1st ultimo to which I
replied on the 5th ultimo. I am surprised that the reply did not
reach Your Exalted Highness. I now enclose a copy thereof.
I remain,
Your Exalted Highness's faithful friend,
Encl.
From a photostat: S.N. 8428
