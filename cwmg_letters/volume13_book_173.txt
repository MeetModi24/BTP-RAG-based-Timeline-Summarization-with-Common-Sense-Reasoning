173. LETTER TO "NEW INDIA" ON BENARES
'INCIDENT'
[MADRAS,]
February 17, 1916
In your editorial note of even date, you suggested that I have
reverted to the Benares incident under missionary influence. May
I say that the missionaries had absolutely nothing to do with my
statement and that there has not been any conversation about
it between any missionary and myself?
New India, 18-2-1916
