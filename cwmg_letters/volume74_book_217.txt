217. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
July 3, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Here is Jhind's reply. It is bad, it is evasive. But what can
you do? I am glad they were all agreeably surprised to see you
looking so well. I am glad, however, I got nervous and packed you
off. It was necessary for you to go to Simla, well or ill.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4028. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7337
