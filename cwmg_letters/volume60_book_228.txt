228. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
February 6, 1935
CHI. NARAHARI,
I received your letter. If unpolished rice is soaked in cold
water for three hours as is done in the case of pulses, it will cook in
very little time. We make rice poorer in food value by getting
it pounded and spending money on it. Unpolished rice should
become one mass after being cooked. As long as the grains
remain separate, you must know that it is not cooked.
Do try snuff. You will have to inhale it only as a medicine,
so take small pinches of it. The snuff for medicinal purposes
should be perfectly pure. You may be knowing that the snuff
available in the market is very much adulterated.
There is no harm in sending with Surendra one of the two
men from Orissa.
Mahadev has gone to Calcutta.
Blessings from
BAPU
