262. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[After April 3, 1924]
BHAI MAHADEV,
This much should of course be taken. It will certainly do for
Gujarati Navajivan. If, therefore, you can drop some other items
and include these, please do so or take out a supplement. Do
what you think is proper.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 8571
1 Vide "Appeal to the Public", 24-3-1924.
2 The letter seems to have been written after Gandhiji resumed the editor-
ship of his weeklies, which he did on April 3.
