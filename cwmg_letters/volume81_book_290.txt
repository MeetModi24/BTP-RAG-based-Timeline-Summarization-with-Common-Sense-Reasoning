290. LETTER TO P. C. GHOSH
August 27, 1945
BHAI PROFULLA,
(1) Read this. Is it all right? What has to be done?
(2) Should I visit Calcutta only in November and not
before?
175
(3) How is your health?
(4) What about Amtul Salaam?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have your letter. I must go to Madras on December 11.
So if I am to go to Calcutta only after November 15, then it
can be only in January. Let us see what happens. I will not
go in October.
BAPU
DR. PROFULLA CHANDRA GHOSH
14/ GARIAHAT ROAD
BALLYGUNJE, CALCUTTA
