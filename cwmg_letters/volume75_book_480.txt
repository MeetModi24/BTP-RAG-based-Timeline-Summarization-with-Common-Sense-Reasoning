480. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
March 10, 1942
BHAI BEHRAMJI,
I hear that you have again fallen ill. If you have survived
so many illnesses in the past, why may you not survive this one,
too? But after all we are in God's hands. He will do what He
wills. Let not, therefore, your serenity be affected.
Blessings from
BAPU
