176. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
August 12, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
Plan a programme of study for Gajraj and let me know.
His studies should be properly attended to. If you wish to ask
me anything about this matter, you may do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8439. Also C.W. 5590. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
