650. LETTER TO AMBALAL SARABHAI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 8, 1928
DEAR BROTHER,
A district conference is to be held here regarding Bardoli.
It has been unanimously decided to invite Shri Kelkar to preside
over it. I feel that it would add to the effectiveness of the con-
ference if you could agree to be the chairman of the reception
committee. If you have no objection to it in principle, I would
urge you to accept the position. I feel there is need at the
present juncture for us to express our unanimity of views.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
