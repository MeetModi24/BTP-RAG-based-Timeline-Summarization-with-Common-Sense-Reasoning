116. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 21, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Today it is partly Diwali and partly the New Year. Tomorrow
it will fully be the New Year day. Right now there are too many people.
Sardar is here from today. I want to treat him myself with earth and
water. The place is very crowded with people.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
