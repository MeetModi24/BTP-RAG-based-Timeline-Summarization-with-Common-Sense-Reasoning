211. LETTER TO SHRIKRISHNADAS JAJU
SEVAGRAM,
August 19, 1946
BHAI JAJUJI,
Read the enclosed letter and send me your comments.
Can we today produce khadi for the market? Can we render
any other service? The other questions are also there. Think
over them too.
Blessings from
BAPU
