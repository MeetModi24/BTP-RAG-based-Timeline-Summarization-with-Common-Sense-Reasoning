347. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
[November 1938]
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have heard that Govind is taking up a job in a Christian
mission. The mission intends to plant its flag in Segaon or has
already done so. Find out the facts without making too much
fuss.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8378
