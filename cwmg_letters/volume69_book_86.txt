86. LETTER TO SHARDABEHN G. CHOKHAWALA
DELHI,
March 20, 1939
CHI. BABUDI,
How are you? Are you quiet now? I do not find time to
write.
But what about you?
What did you observe in the
Congress? Are you keeping good health ? We shall be in Delhi
for a couple of days more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 10020. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokhawala
