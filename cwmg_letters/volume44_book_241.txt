241. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Tuesday [September 23, 1930]
CHI. MOTIBEHN,
Here's a short reply to your long letter. Instead of brooding
over the past, we should think of what lies ahead of us. Look
upon all the inmates of the Ashram as Meghji and shower sweet
love on them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3741
