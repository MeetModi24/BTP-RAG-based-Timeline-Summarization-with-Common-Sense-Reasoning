282. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
IN THE TRAIN,
November 22, 1933
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I got your loving letter. I read the verse. Knowing that
God is behind all, good and evil, I remain unaffected. If He was
behind good only, I would certainly have felt uneasy. He alone
is good. If I could persuade you to join me in this tour, your
health would automatically improve. But I know that is impos-
sible. I hope you glance through the issues of Harijan.
Why doesn't Ramabehn remember me?
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5931. Also C.W. 3247. Courtesy:
Mahesh P. Pattani
