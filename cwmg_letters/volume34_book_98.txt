98. LETTER TO JOSHI
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 5, 1927
MY DEAR JOSHI,
I believe you are on the Committee of the Imperial Citizen-
ship Association. The enclosed speaks for itself. If you agree with
the view I have set forth in the letter, you will please expedite
business.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 12365
