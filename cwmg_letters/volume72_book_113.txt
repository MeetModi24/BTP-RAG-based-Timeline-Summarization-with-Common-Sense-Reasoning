113. LETTER TO M. MUJEEB
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 24, 1940
MY DEAR MUJEEB,
Raju has been and gone to his place. He wants to work there
if he can. I gave him all the time he wanted and put him in touch
with Kaka's office. At his wish I have given him a note to Dr.
Pattabhi.
I am sure you will strike the balance between the two seeming-
ly conflicting duties. Please tell Dr. Zakir that the Urdu school
thing is being fixed up satisfactorily.
Love.
BAPU
PROF. MUJEEB
JAMIA MILLIA
NEW DELHI
From the original: C.W. 1466. Courtesy: M. Mujeeb
