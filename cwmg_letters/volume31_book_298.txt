298. LETTER TO MULCHAND U. PAREKH
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Saturday, Asadha Vad 14, August 7, 1926
BHAI MULCHANDBHAI,
Shri Fulchand tells me in his letter that you have Rs. 200
with you. You may spend it.
I send with this a draft for
Rs. 300.
SHRI MULCHAND U. PAREKH
VARTEJ
KATHIAWAR
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 12242
