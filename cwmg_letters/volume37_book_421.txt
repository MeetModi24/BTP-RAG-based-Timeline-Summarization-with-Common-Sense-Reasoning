421. LETTER TO L. V. PATTANAYAKA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 18, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Karmabhumi means to me a land not of
enjoyment of pleasure but enjoyment of work by way of sacrifice.
My authority for saying that Sita was spinning is based
upon a fact that, in her times, as history tells us every household
had a spinning-wheel. There is no reason to believe that Sita's
household was an exception.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. L. V. PATTANAYAKA
P.O. DIGAPAHANDI (GANJAM)
From a microfilm: S.N. 13560
