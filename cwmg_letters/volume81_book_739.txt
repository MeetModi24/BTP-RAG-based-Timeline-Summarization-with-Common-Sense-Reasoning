739. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
October 23, 1945
BAPA,
I have no objection to the Gujarat training scheme being
conducted in Sabarmati provided the sisters who come for train-
ing are qualified, whether they come from villages or not. In
any case they should be working in villages or at least should be
interested in village work.
BAPU
