442. LETTER TO ASAF ALI
[Before November 22, 1934]
MY DEAR ASAF ALI,
God is great. I can trace nothing but the hand of God in
all these successes. May we deserve them!
How I wish your
success will lead to heart unity between the two brothers—Hindu
and Mussalman.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Hindustan Times, 23-11-1934
