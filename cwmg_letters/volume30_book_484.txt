484. LETTER TO D. VENKATA RAO
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 3, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter as also Krishnabai's. I understand the posi-
tion and appreciate it. I quite agree with you and Krishnabai
that her talent for painting should be developed. I therefore quite
understand her desire to stay with you and go on with her painting
work. I would like to have some photographic specimens of her
work.
I am not writing to her separately. Do please keep me inform-
ed of her progress from time to time.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. D. VENKATA RAO
DAMERLA HOUSE
RAJAHMUNDRY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19526
