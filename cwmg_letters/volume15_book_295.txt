295. LETTER TO COL. TUKE
THE ASHRAM,
May 25, 1919
DEAR COL. TUKE,
I thank you for your letter of the 24th instant. Regarding
those who were wounded at the time of the military operations
to quell the recent disturbances, I would like you, if you could,
to give me an idea of the expenses likely to be incurred. I do
not anticipate any difficulty in meeting them, but perhaps it is
as well to know the requirements. Will you kindly send your
reply to Mr. Vallabhbhai Patel, Bhadra, who will deal with the
matter? I am leaving tonight for Bombay.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 6625
