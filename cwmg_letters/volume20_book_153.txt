153. LETTER TO N. C. KELKAR
BOMBAY,
July 4 [1921]
DEAR MR. KELKAR,
Of all congratulations, I appreciate yours most because I am
in need of your whole-hearted co-operation, if you can give it out
of conviction. I have your letter about the Working Committee.
In future, you will do as the spirit moves you. I only feel that
we should make the Working Committee a swift, powerful and
homogeneous body. There is room in it for differences of opinion,
not conviction. I do believe we can achieve all we want by effi-
ciently working the Congress constitution. But I can't force my
faith on India. It will come if we are destined to achieve our
freedom this year.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 3122. Courtesy: Kashi Nath Kelkar
