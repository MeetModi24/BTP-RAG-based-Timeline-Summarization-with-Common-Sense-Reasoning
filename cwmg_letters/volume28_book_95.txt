95. LETTER TO JETHALAL MANSUR
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
Tuesday, [After September 10, 1925]
BHAI JETHALAL,
I have your letter. Where is the trust deed ? Where is the
deed of transfer by the State in respect of the land? Who will be
the priest? On receiving the answers to these three questions I shall
immediately send the money. Our understanding is that the State
will contribute as much as the amount you collect and you will
receive the same amount through me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 11135(2)
