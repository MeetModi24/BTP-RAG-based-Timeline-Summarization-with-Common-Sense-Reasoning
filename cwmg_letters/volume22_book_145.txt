145. LETTER TO PARASRAM MEHROTRA
[February 6, 1922]
CHI. PARASRAM 2 ,
I have your letter. Is any work in connection with the spin-
ning-wheel being done in Allahabad?
Blessings from
BAPU
MASTER PARASRAM
ANAND BHAVAN
ALLAHABAD
