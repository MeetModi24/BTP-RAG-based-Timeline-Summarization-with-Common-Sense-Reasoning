275. LETTER TO MOTASINGH PATARA
February 11, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
Your telegram to the All-India Village Industries Associa-
tion Board has been transferred to me. I am sorry that I cannot
participate in your Conference.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI MOTASINGH PATARA
JULLUNDUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
