269. LETTER TO SOONABAI SERWAI
[Before February 19, 1931]
I do not see how strikes and hartals, brought about by force,
are in any way useful to national interests.
Mr. Gandhi has desired his correspondent to draw his attention in
future to cases in which violence or force was used by Congress workers.
He has again implored Mrs. Serwai to take to swadeshi.
The Pioneer, 21-2-1931
