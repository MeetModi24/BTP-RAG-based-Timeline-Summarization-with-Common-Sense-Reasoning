692. LETTER TO NAGINDAS DALSUKHBHAI SHAH
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 17, 1928
BHAISHRI NAGINDAS,
I have your letter. If you come on Tuesday at 4.30, we shall
talk.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
SHRI NAGINBHAI DALSUKHBHAI SHAH
VAKIL DALSUKHBHAI SHAH'S HOUSE
GODHRA
