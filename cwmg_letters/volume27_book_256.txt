256. LETTER TO NISHITHNATH KUNDU
148 RUSSA ROAD,
CALCUTTA,
July 22, 1925
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I did discuss the conditions of the commit-
tee at Dinajpur with Deshbandhu. He told me that at the present
moment it was difficult for the provincial committee to help a
district like yours. There are no funds with the committee. What
it is now possible for me to suggest is that workers who have full
faith in khadi should put themselves in correspondence with Khadi
Pratishthan and work under it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 8021
