509. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
September 16, 1938
MY DEAR C. R.,
You must have got my wire. Why should anything tor-
ture you? So long as you can easily wear the thorny crown,
you may wear it. But if those who have thrust it on you are
jealous of your wearing it, it would be good riddance for you.
Anyway your philosophy must not desert you. Was your letter
a sign only of momentary weariness?
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2071
