535. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
May 29, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
So you are in Segaon already. I do not mind. If you
get there the comforts you need, it will be where your heart is.
If you get the rains, of course there can be no building. There-
fore let all labour that can be used be given to the walls and the
roof. If these are ready and the walls dry, the rest can be finish-
ed even after the rains. But I am not going to worry, if in spite
of all effort the building is not ready for occupation by the time
we return. No strain should be put upon your weak body.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
The accompanying for Munnalal. I hope you got a basket
of fruit sent by Janammal.
From the original: C.W. 6342. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9808
