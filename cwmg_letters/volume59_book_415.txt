415. LETTER TO TARA SINGH
November 17, 1934
DEAR MASTER TARA SINGH,
I was delighted to have your letter. I am here till 15th Decem-
ber I hope. The earlier you come the better. Khan Saheb, his
brother and his son would be leaving here on the 24th for a brief
tour in U. P. They may return about the 4th December. Khan
Saheb after staying with me for about four days is likely to go to
Bengal.
Yours sincerely,
MASTER TARA SINGH
22 ELPHINSTONE CIRCLE
BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
