59. LETTER TO KUSUMBEHN DESAI
June 18, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR)3,
I got both your letters. You cannot come to see me till the
restriction on Mirabehn is lifted. Isn't that what self-denial means?
We shall not do justice to Harilal if we do not publish his
letters to you. Surely it was no fault of his that you could not
live up to his ideals. You should do so now. You ought not
to withhold the letters in order to hide your weakness. But why
should you despair and feel weak? Or is it, rather, that you
think you are too old? How can you give up hope at the age
of twenty-four or twenty-five? In fact this is the time of life when
you can strive and rise. Take care!!!
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1840
