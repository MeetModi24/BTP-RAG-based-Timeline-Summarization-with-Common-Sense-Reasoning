251. LETTER TO C. P. SRINIVASA IYER
January 29, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I cannot recall a single occasion when
I called the Mahabharata a heap of rubbish. I have not had many
occasions to speak or write about the Mahabharata, and when I did
write about it I compared it to a diamond mine, whereas I com-
pared the Gita to a diamond chest. Perhaps, the Shankaracharya
had that simile in mind, but at the present moment, for sanata-
nists, whether they are big men or small men, any stone is good
enough to fling at me.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. C. P. SRINIVASA IYER
NORTH VILLAGE
CHITTUR (COCHIN STATE)
