49. LETTER TO ADA WEST
May 20, 1924
MY DEAR DEVI 3 ,
I have just got your welcome letter. I am slowly gaining
strength. I am at a sea-side place. Hope to go to the Ashram next
week. How long are you staying there? How are you keeping?
I must not write more. But I am asking Ramdas to give you all
the news.
My love to you all.
Your brother,
M. K. GANDHI
MISS ADA WEST
23, GEORGE STREET
SOUTH LINCOLNSHIRE
From a photostat: G.N. 7618; also C.W. 4433. Courtesy: A. H. West
