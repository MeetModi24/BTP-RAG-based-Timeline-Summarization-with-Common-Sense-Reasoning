39. LETTER TO J. ROYEPPEN
HOTEL CECIL,
[LONDON,]
October 30, 1906
MY DEAR JOSEPH,
I must cancel the appointment for the evening I have given you,
because I shall now be exceedingly busy, and it is only in the evenings
that I am in a position to dictate anything. I shall write to you if I have
a free evening.
Yours sincerely,
JOSEPH ROYEPPEN, ESQ.
36, STAPLETON HALL ROAD
STROUD GREEN, N.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4419
