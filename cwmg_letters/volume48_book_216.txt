216. LETTER TO W. TUDOR OWEN
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, S.W. 1,
November 20, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am likely to be free about 5 p.m. on
Wednesday next. I would like you please to telegraph to me if
that hour will suit you.
I would be glad to renew old memories, if we can possibly
meet.
Yours sincerely,
W. TUDOR OWEN, ESQ.
BROADHURST
LITTLE COMMON
BEXHILL-ON-SEA
From a photostat: S.N. 18328
