182. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Tuesday, Maha Sud 11 [February 11, 1919]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I did not write to you yesterday because there was nothing
definite to write. We were to meet today at 7 p.m. and we
met. Ambalalbhai, Vallabhbhai, Bachubhai, etc., were present.
The discussion was quite good. Ultimately, signatures were
given for the following Pledge. Ambalalbhai, Bachubhai, etc.,
asked for 48 hours to consider. Bhai Subedar was also present.
But he left a little early because it was time for his train. It
appears that there will be at least as many more signatures com-
ing. I think I shall be sending the letter to the Viceroy to-
day. I have kept the draft of the letter ready. But my mind
is still undecided about sending it.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
I have just read that the law will be valid for three years.
Now the struggle seems to me necessary.
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 32736
