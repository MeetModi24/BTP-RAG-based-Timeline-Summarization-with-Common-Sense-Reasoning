142. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 22, 1930
CHI. PREMA,
I have your letter. Kaka tied the sacred string on my wrist
on the Balev day and also bowed to me on your behalf.
Panditji's patience and capacity for self-sacrifice are as great
as you describe them to be. He has also displayed forbearance
of a high order.
Henceforth, you should not delay your going to bed or pre-
vent someone from doing so till ten. The moment it strikes nine,
we should be in bed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10231; also C.W. 6679. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
