526. LETTER TO AMBIKAPRASAD
Asvina Sud 2 [October 8, 1926)
BHAI AMBIKAPRASADJI,
I have your letter. I am not qualified now to say anything
by way of guidance on the problem of Hindu-Muslim unity. It
seems to me that I am serving the cause by my very silence.
Please, therefore, excuse me.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7483
