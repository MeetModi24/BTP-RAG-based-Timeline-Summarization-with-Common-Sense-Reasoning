288. LETTER TO JOHN HAYNES HOLMES
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 20, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I cannot resist you, but I take you at
your word. I send you a single sentence as follows:
Tolstoy's greatest contribution to life lies, in my opinion, in
his ever attempting to reduce to practice his professions without
counting the cost.
Thanks for your inquiry about my health. I appear to be
keeping well at the present moment.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 14287
