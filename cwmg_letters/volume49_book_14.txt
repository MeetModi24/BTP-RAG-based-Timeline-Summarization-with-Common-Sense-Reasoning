14. LETTER TO ABBAS
January 13, 1932
CHI. ABBAS,
Write to me and tell me what improvements you have adopt-
ed in your method of carding, etc., whether you have accepted
the improvements suggested by Mathuradas and whether anybody
has taken up the latter's work. I hope you keep good health.
How many attend the class at present?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6305
