283. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
March 9, 1936
CHI. NARAHARI,
I hope Sumitra is doing well. See that she writes in ink.
I am sure Ramji does not cause any trouble. The goshala
must be working well.
I wish to spend a fortnight in Delhi. But let us see what
happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
