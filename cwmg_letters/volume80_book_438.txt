438. LETTER TO MIR MUSHTAQ AHMED
PANCHGANI,
June 5, 1945
BHAI MUSHTAQ AHMED,
It is good that as far as possible you do not write or speak
in English.
The answer to your question is covered by the opinion I have
given. I feel that it is not proper to enrol new members as long
as the Congress is an illegal organization. The Congress Assem-
bly is a different thing. In this matter each province can act
as it likes.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
JANAB MIR MUSHTAQ AHMED
34 PREM HOUSE
CONNAUGHT PLACE
NEW DELHI
