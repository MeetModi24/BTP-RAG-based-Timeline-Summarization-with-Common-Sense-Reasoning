347. LETTER TO ADAMSALEH A. PATEL
ASHRAM,
April 14, 1926
BHAI ADAMSALEH ALIBHAI,
You have left me far behind. I have undertaken to reform a
single person, and that is my own self. And I realize how diffi-
cult it is to reform him. Now, need I answer your questions?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SJT. A. A. PATEL
PANOLI
DISTRICT BROACH
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19901
