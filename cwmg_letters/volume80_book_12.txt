12. LETTER TO VAMANRAO JOSHI
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 25, 1945
BHAI VAMANRAO JOSHI,
I have your very neat and cheerful letter. I am very happy
that you are keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
I Balvantsinha's niece
[PS.]
For the time being I am here.
VIR VAMANRAO JOSHI
AMRAOTI, BERAI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
