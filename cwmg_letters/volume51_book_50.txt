50. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
September 10, 1932
CHI. VASUMATI,
Indeed! You could barely find time to write to me! What
indolence! And the letter also is as good as no letter. What
about your experiences during all these months?
I hope you will go and see Qureshi. Did you see Pyare Ali's
Ashram?
Did you visit Amina's children?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 577. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
