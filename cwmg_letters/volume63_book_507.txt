507. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
ON THE TRAIN,
October 28, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
This is my 2nd letter during travelling and only to tell you
that you are never out of my mind. Hope the progress of both
continues.
I had a very busy day in Delhi doing quiet work. Ba is
with us. Manu remaining with Devdas. It is delightfully cool
in Delhi just now. Devdas is much better.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Tomorrow Rajkot. 30th Ahmedabad.
From the original: C.W. 6367. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9833
