80. LETTER TO KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 27, 1941
DEAR SISTER,
I have your two letters, received simultaneously.
Here is a draft for your reply. It gives you my reaction to the
Government letter. Of course you will reject it, if it does not
correctly represent your view.
I understand and appreciate all you say in your second letter.
My advice is that you should come here first and stay a few days
so that we may have a leisurely chat. Probably Satyawati will come.
You will then see her too. I would advise you to see Sarup and
the Hospital and then see J. and Indu. When you have finished
this programme, you may break the order. The rest when we meet.
Love.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
