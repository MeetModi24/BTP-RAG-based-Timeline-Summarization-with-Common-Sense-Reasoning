357. LETTER TO E. E. DOYLE
August 10, 1932
DEAR COL. DOYLE,
Referring to my letter of the 22nd and 24th ultimo and your
kind reply, I have not since heard from you and Kaka Kalelkar
who you said was to be permitted to write to me about his
health. I would appreciate early attention to this matter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Government of Bombay, Home Department, I.G.P. File No. 9
