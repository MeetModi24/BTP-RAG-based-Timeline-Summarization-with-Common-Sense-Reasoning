754. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 23, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. I am sending the letter to Tari. It
could have gone today. But I just could not attend to it.
That shows how overworked I am! For this reason I am late
by a day.
The riots in Bombay and Ahmedabad are causing anxiety.
Let us see what God wills. I am keen at the moment that you
should finish your examination soon.
There is no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal
Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
