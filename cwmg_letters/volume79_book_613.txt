613. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
April 8, 1945
CHI. SUMI,
It is not good that you often get headaches and dysentery.
With such health you should not exert yourself too much. You
should study less. Everything will be ruined if your health is
ruined. I am here at least till the 14th.
Blessings from
BAPU
SUMITRA GANDHI
C/O LADY SUPERINTENDENT
BIRLA HIGH SCHOOL
PILANI
