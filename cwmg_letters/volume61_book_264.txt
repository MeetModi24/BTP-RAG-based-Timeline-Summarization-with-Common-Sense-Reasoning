264. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
WARDHA,
June 21, 1935
CHI. MARY,
You have given me good news. I expect better tomorrow.
Where are you staying? Are you comfortable?
Strange, Margarete had no letter from me. I have searched
my file. I cannot find her letter which shows that it is destroyed
and no such letter is destroyed without answer. Please ask her
with my love to write again and I will reply at once. All well
here.
Love.
BAPU
182
[PS.]
Mira has shown me your letter. K. may come any day in
July and will stay perhaps a month. Mira need not write now.
From a photostat: G.N. 6046. Also C.W. 3376. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
