71. LETTER TO UMADEVI BAJAJ
[Before January 7, 1935]
CHI. OM,
I write this letter with a pencil since I am writing while
having my meal.
It is a bad habit to write while eating and it is equally bad
to write with a pencil. Please don't copy my example in these
respects.
It seems that your ear is still giving you trouble. You must
go to Bombay. I am thinking of sending you a wire.
Write to me about Madalasa also.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 341
1 Vide the following item.
2 Addressee's elder sister
