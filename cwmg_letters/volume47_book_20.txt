20. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
BORSAD,
June 19, 1931
CHI. NARANDAS,
I don't have time to write much today. The information
about Manjula causes me anxiety. She has got over so many
illnesses before now that she will probably get over this one, too.
We should do our best for her treatment. If Mahavir wants to
join the Vidyapith, let him. Read my letter to him. Sitla
Sahay asked me to send my letter to him through you, and I am
doing so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I
