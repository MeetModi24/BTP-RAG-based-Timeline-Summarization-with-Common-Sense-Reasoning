464. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
April 27, 1934
CHI. MANILAL,
Read the accompanying letter from Miss Schlesin. I have
replied to her that I could not guide you from here, and that
she and Kallenbach should do that. I do hold that, if you have
been making racial attacks, you should desist. But I don't feel in-
clined to criticize you from this distance.
Two letters from Ba are enclosed.
I feel completely tired as I write this letter at 3.45 a.m. Hence
I will not add anything more. Ramdas and Devdas have got
daughters. All the four are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4819
