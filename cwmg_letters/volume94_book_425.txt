425. LETTER TO ARUNA ASAF ALI
SODEPUR,
January 7, 1946
CHI. ARUNA 2 ,
I read your little note immediately it was received. But your joint
letter to Maulana Sahib I read early morning today (silence-day)
immediately after the 4.30 prayer. You will let me say that it is eloquent
but not cogent. It does injustice to yourselves, the Maulana and the
cause. But I must not argue whilst you are under detention of your
own making and yet-falsely believing yourselves to be free.
Of course you must do as your comprehension of truth bids and
not as anybody else however high says. And I honour you for such
action.
It is for your sake that I resort to a foreign tongue and a thing
I will not do even for you, if you were free.
Blessings to both from 3
BAPU
From a copy : C. W. 11339. Courtesy: Aruna Asaf Ali
