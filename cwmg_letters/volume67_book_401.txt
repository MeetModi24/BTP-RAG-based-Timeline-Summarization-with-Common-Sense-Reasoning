401. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 24, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Fresh ginger is needed for Pyarelal. I have of course made
inquiries in Varoda. Rajaram will see about it. But if he is not
able to procure it, please see to it yourself.
Ice and vegetable soup have been discontinued for the
present. He will take whey.
If you have not written for grape-fruit along with pome-
granates, do so now. Through God's grace my silence is conti-
nuing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11632
