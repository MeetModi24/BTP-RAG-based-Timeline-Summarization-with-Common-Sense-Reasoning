346. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
CAMP LUCKNOW,
April 1, 193[6]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Kindly have Rs. 15,000 (fifteen thousand only) sent to
the Secretary, Dakshina Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha, Madras,
out of whatever funds have been collected for the 'purse'
in connection with the Indore session of the Hindi Sahitya
Sammelan.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2960
