461. LETTER TO MADALASA
ON THE STEAMER TO SODEPUR,
January 3, 1946
CHI. MADALASA,
I have your letter. My previous letter seems to have gone
astray.
You have no reason at all to feel depressed. I have no time
today to write more. I have to deal with the rest of the mail.
Get well soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 5856
82-22
