109. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 23, 1940
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Read the enclosed and guide me. I have told the writer that
his suggestion attracts me and if I see my way clear I shall act up
to it in part or wholly.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1940. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
