414. LETTER TO SHANKARBHAI
DEAR SHANKARBHAI,
I may not be able to convince you as regard temple entry, but
how can you, on account of that activity, abandon me like others?
Temple entry is not a new activity for me; still on occasion I have been
able to get help from you. Shankerlal says that now he is not able to
have any help from you for Harijan work. I hope that except for
temple entry, you will certainly provide funds for other activities.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a copy of the Gujarati: S.N. 32754
