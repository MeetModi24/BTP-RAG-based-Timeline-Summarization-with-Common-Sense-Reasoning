472. LETTER TO HORACE G. ALEXANDER
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 25, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter which I have kept by me for a long
long time.
You certainly did not write your first letter in any wrong
spirit.
I have now got a parcel of Quaker books which I suppose
I owe to you.
I have not had the proposal referred to by you about the
Young India literature.
Yours sincerely,
HORACE G. ALEXANDER
BIRMINGHAM, ENGLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 15044
