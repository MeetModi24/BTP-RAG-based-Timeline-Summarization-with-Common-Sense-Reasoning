276. LETTER TO VASUDEV
SODEPUR,
December 9, 1945
BHAISHRI,
Your letter is written in a very bad hand. And that too
in English. Can't you express your views in Hindustani?
I have gone through the printed statement. I did not like it.
Its is pompous and lacking in humility. There is also exaggeration
in it. Read what I have written about students. Writing will
not help. Only work will. Constructive work is easy as well as
difficult. Meet Rajendra Babu. Do what he says.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
VASUDEV
YOUTH LEAGUE
PATNA
