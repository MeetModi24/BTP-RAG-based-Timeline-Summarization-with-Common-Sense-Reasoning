47. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
October 1 [1927]
CHI. MIRA,
I had expected something from you yesterday but nothing
came. You are not going to be moody at all nor nervous in trying
to avoid nervousness. And do not always think what I would like
and not like but do what you think is right even though it may
turn out to be not as I would have liked. I want you to be strong
in body, mind and soul!
You will not make your time-table too rigid without intervals
of breathing time.
I should like to know your weight.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5299. Courtesy: Mirabehn
