652. LETTER TO RAGHUBIR SAHAI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA (C.P.),
October 10, 1941
DEAR RAGHUBIR SAHAI,
I thank [you for] your very full and instructive letter. What
I knew from various correspondents, you confirm in your full
letter.
I am watching. Hope you are well.
Yours,
BAPU
MR. RAGHUBIR SAHAI
BADAUN, U.P.
From a photostat: G.N. 10206
