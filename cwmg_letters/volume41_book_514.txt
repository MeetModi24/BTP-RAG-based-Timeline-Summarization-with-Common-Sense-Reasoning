514. LETTER TO HARI G. GOVIL
CAMP HARDOI,
October 12, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the cutting . You should ask
Mr. Holmes as to what is happening to the American Edition of
the Autobiography.
Yours sincerely,
HARI G. GOVIL, ESQ.
INDIA SOCIETY OF AMERICA
1107 TIMES BUILDING
NEW YORK CITY
From a photostat: G.N. 1025
