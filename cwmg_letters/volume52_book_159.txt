159. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
December 5, 1932
CHI. MIRA,
You are not to be affected by the little fast which began on
Saturday and ended on Sunday at 1 p.m. It was on account of
Appasaheb, that noble worker. Can you recall him? I took
only fruit yesterday and milk this morning.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6253. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9719
