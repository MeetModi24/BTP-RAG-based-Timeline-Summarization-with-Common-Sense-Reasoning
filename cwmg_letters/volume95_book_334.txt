334. LETTER TO MUNNALAL
LUCKNOW,
Silence day
BHAI MUNNALAL,
I have your letters with me. I do not have a moment to spare.
The letters are difficult to answer. It is better that you adopt a course
which your inner self prompts you to follow.
Blessings from
BAPU
