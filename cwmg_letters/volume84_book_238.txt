238. LETTER TO MANIBHAI DESAI
NEW DELHI,
May 23, 1946
CHI. MANILAL,
I have your letter. Don't mind Joshi having left. What
led to his leaving was, of course, bad. One is bound to have
such bitter as well as sweet experiences. The work there seems
to be progressing very well. I hardly get any time to write
or dictate letters. The work here and the work for Harijan is
more than enough for me. But "Nobody can harm him whom
Rama protects." A bhajan has been printed for distribution.
I shall send you a copy of it.
The path of brahmacharya is as grand as it is difficult. The
deeper one reflects over it the more clearly one realizes its grandeur,
holiness and purity. I realize that the more important thing is
to know how to attain it. After deep thinking I have come to
the conclusion that Ramanama is the best thing. But Rama-
nama must come from the heart and not be pronounced merely
with the tongue. It must, of course, go hand in hand with incessant
service. One must eat only to keep the body going. And the
quantity of food must be moderate. Ramanama is not a substitute
for these and other essential conditions. They are, in fact, included
in Ramanama. They are even a sign of Ramanama having
penetrated one's heart. It is plain, moreover, that unless all
attachment vanishes brahmacharya is impossible. Deduce the rest
from this.
Blessings from
BAPU
