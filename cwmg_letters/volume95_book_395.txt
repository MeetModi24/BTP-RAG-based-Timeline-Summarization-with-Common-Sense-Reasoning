395. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
Tuesday, SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI
DEAR SISTER,
Mahadev is somewhere in the Ashram, but I do not know where.
I have read your note. Please go to the station if you can. There is
no need to escort Dr. Ansari to the Ashram. It will be enough if you
can arrange to have him brought here. Jamnalalji and Zakir Husain
will be accompanying him.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11154. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
