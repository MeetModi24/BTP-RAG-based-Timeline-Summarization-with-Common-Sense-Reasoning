513. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 3, 1935
DEAR BIBI,
I have your letter. From Indore I return to Wardha. You
should stay at Wardha till your cottage is ready. You can even
prolong the stay.
Regarding your cottage, I shall write to
Malkani that it should be constructed of baked bricks and consist
of two rooms even at the cost of a slight increase in the outlay.
You will have fully recovered by the time I return, won't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 322
