21. LETTER TO PRIME MINISTER'S SECRETARY
JOHANNESBURG,
June 12, 1907
THE ACTING SECRETARY TO
THE PRIME MINISTER
[PRETORIA]
SIR,
With reference to your letter of the 4th instant No. 14/1, I regret
that the Prime Minister considers it unnecessary to receive a deputation
on behalf of my Association in connection with the Asiatic Registration
Act.
In view, however, of the fact that the date of enforcing the Act
has not yet been gazetted, my Association once more approaches the
Government and respectfully suggests that the offer of voluntary re-
registration may be accepted and that the Act may afterwards, by a
short Bill, be made applicable to those who do not abide by the
voluntary offer.
I have, etc.,
Essop ISMAIL MIA
ACTING CHAIRMAN,
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
Indian Opinion, 22-6-1907
