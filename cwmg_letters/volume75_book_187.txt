187. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
November 27, 1941
CHI. MAGAN,
It is good that Urmi has started going to school. Since you will
be spending most of your time in Bombay, why should you construct
a big house here? For brief visits you may come whenever you wish
and you will be accommodated with the others. You have as yet
spent on nothing else besides timber. The timber can be used for
other purposes.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MAGANBHAI MEHTA
4 B TEJPAL ROAD, Top FLOOR
GAMDEVI
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10117. Courtesy: Maganlal P.
Mehta
