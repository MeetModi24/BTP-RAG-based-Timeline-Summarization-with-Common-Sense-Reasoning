378. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
July 18, 1935
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
You ought not to fall ill. If you have been laid up because
of my letters it reflects badly on your obedience. You are not to
feel hurt over anything I may say, only to get well promptly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 1713
