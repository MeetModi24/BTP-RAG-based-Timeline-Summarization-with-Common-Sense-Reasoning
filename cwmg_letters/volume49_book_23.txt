23. LETTER TO JAISHANKAR P. TRIVEDI
January 18, 1932
BHAI TRIVEDI2,
I got the two lots of fruit and vegetables which you sent.
Please don't send any more now. Both of us get these things here
if we want them. It would be better from every point of view
that you should use your time and money for more important
1 Vide also p. 5.
2 Prof. J. P. Trivedi of Poona Agriculture College
13
work. You may rest assured that, if I want anything, I will
not hesitate to ask you for it. Surely you know this! I had met
Manu in Villeneuve. Send him my blessings.
Blessings from
BAPU
