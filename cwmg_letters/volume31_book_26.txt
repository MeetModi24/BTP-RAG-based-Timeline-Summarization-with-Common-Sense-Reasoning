26. LETTER TO D. N. BAHADURJI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 19, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
Nurgisben sent me your yarn for examination. It was not
bad at all. The strength was nearly 50 and evenness over 40.
This is an extremely good record for a beginner and for one who
has not sat at the wheel for hours at a time. What I would like
you to aim at is 70 marks for strength and over 45 for evenness.
The highest yet obtained is 79 and 49, respectively.
I hope Mrs. Bahadurji is keeping all right. She owes me a
letter.
Yours sincerely,
D. N. BAHADURJI, ESQ.
RIDGE ROAD
MALABAR HILL P.O.
