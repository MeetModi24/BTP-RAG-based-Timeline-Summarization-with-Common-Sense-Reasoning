158. LETTER TO SAILENDRA NATH CHATTERJEE
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 7, 1945
CHI. SAILEN,
Father asked me if he might take you to Calcutta for treat-
ment. What could I do except to say, 'Yes'? If you could be
treated here I would send for you. But it is not possible. I
would advise you to go to the Malad Nature Cure Hospital. If
you are agreeable I can send you to Dr. Krishnavarma. But
you will have to pay the charges there. It would be good if
Rishabhdas could give his consent. The climate of Malad is good.
It is a suburb of Bombay.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Learn to write in Hindustani.
