340. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
December 21, 1932
CHI. PREMI1,
Your Hindi letter is extremely good. The characters too
are very well formed. I did not write as I thought there was
no letter from you. Inform Father when you write to him that
we are all well. Jamnalalji too is quite well. I saw him only
today.
This is in Mahadevbhai's hand.
Blessings from
BAPU
PREMI JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
MARKET ROAD, HYDERABAD, SIND
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9249. Courtesy: Jairamdas Doulatram
