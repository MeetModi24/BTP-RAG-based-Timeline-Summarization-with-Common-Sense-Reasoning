74. LETTER TO JAYANTIPRASAD
SEVAGRAM,
October 25, 1941
BHAI JAYANTIPRASAD,
I have your letter. I can certainly say that 'the Ashram is full
of unclean things and that Pratibha may continue her study where
she is'. My statement would suggest that you had written to me
about Pratibha. Still, if you can find the letter, I should like to
see it. I cannot keep Pratibha in the Ashram. I also do not approve
of the craze for passing examinations at such great inconvenience.
I have no right to give money for such a purpose.
I have received the yarn.
Blessings from
BAPU
