327. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
January 5, 1928
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
You must have received my letter sent with Jamnalalji. I
had also sent a telegram asking you not to attend the Assembly
till your health returned to normal.
I wanted to mention this
to the pujya Malaviyaji but we were so busy with other things
that I did not remember you. I see no need of writing to him
now. You must have sent the money to Jamnalalji? I have not yet
heard about it.
The pujya Malaviyaji's speech had a magic effect and he
proposes to make a mighty effort in this matter. Let us see what
happens. Up to the end of March, I shall be at the Ashram itself.
On the 17th I shall have to leave for Kathiawar on a five days' tour.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6151. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
