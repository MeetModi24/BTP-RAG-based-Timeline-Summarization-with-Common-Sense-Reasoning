41. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
September 30, 1927
CHI. MIRA,
The post is just due but the time for posting is also due. I
fully expect something from you today. You are not going to
think that you may not write more than once a week.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
For Ramdas
From the original: C.W. 5280. Courtesy: Mirabehn
