46. LETTER TO SARALA
August 13, 1947
CHI. SARALA,
I have your letter. You are a sensible girl. You have under-
stood why I don't send for you here. Have patience. Complete
the Nayee Talim course and attain the highest proficiency in it.
You must be reading in the newspapers about the developments
here. I have been able to write this much under great pressure.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 8769
