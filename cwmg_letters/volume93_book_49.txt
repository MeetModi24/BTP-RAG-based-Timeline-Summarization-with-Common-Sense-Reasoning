49. LETTER TO SUMANGAL PRAKASH
WARDHA,
July 3, 1935
CHI. SUMANGAL,
I have your letter. If for the sake of your parents you must
earn money and go out for the purpose or take to writing you
should do so. Maybe in so doing you will find greater peace.
Do not feel yourself bound by any past commitments. That
alone is binding which brings us peace and promotes dharma
Dharma means dharma for the moment.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : Sumangal Prakash Papers. Courtesy:
