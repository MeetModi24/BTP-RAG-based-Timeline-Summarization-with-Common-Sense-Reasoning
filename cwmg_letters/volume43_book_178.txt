178. LETTER TO LALA DUNICHAND
DANDI,
April 6, 1930
DEAR LALA DUNICHAND,
The news that Lala Shamlal of Rohtak has been arrested has
prompted Lala Surajbhan to rush to the Punjab. I have approv-
ed of the suggestion. Please make what use you like of him. I
do hope that you and Mrs. Dunichand will not fail to sacrifice
your all in this final battle for freedom.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 5588
