457. LETTER TO TARACHAND
PANCHGANI,
June 7, 1945
BHAI TARACHAND,
I have your letter. It is very clear. It will help me. I will
not be slack. I am sending the letter to Sriman Narayan.
I shall take a decision after consulting him and shall write to
you again.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. TARACHAND
11 CHATHAM LINES
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
