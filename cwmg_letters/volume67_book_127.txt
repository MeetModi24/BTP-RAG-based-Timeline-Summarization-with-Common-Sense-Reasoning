127. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
May 26, 1938
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
How very business-like and prompt you are! I am so glad
you were able to go into the Gurgaon D. C. C.'s affairs. I hope
your advice will be accepted by both parties, as it should be.
Today I have your letter about my notes of my talks with
Jinnah. I think my second talk with him was inevitable.
I
hope it won't do any harm. If you have time I would like you
to give me a line about Jal after you have seen him. How I
wish you will give yourself rest during your European tour and
not rush as you have been doing here all the time.
Love.
BAPU
A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 279
