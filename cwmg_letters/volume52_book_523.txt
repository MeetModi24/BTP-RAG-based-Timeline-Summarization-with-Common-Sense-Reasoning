523. LETTER TO DR. HIRALAL SHARMA
January 8, 1933
DEAR DR. SHARMA,
Amtussalaam tells me you are likely to pass through Sabar-
mati in the near future. I would like you to stay for a few months
in the Ashram and make your experiments and at the same time
observe if the Ashram-life suits you and you, the Ashram. If you
cannot do this, stay, if you can, for a few days for Amtul
Salaam's treatment. She has great faith in you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 15
