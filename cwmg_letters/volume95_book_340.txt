340. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
CHI. VASUMATI,
I am not getting your letters regularly. I must have them. I
want your diary. How is your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9359
1
This and the preceding letters appear to be of 1927, in which year
the addressee had begun the practice of maintaining a diary; vide Vol. XXXIII,
pp.346, 388 and 465.
