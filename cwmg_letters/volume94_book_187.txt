187. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
July 1, 1942
CHI. SUSHILA,
Do not stop writing to me altogether. Pyarelal and Mahadev have
gone to Bombay today. M. has gone there for the recommended check-
up and Pyarelal for his eyes. Mahadev will return within two days.
Pyarelal will be staying on for eight days if necessary. My health one
may say is good. The times are hard. Mostly I observe silence.
Janakibehn has come today.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR, M.D.
L.H.M.C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
