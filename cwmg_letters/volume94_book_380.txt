380. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
June 7, 1945
CHI. LILI,
Should I pity your brother and sister-in-law or be angry with
them ? What else can be the result when they cannot control their
lust ? It is up to you to consider your duty in the matter. Any religion
taught by others is mere tautology. As for me, I am not a learned
person in any sense. May God help you, your brother and sister-in-
law. May it be well with the boy.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. LILAVATI UDESHI
G. S. MEDICAL COLLEGE
LADIES HOSTEL
PAREL
BOMBAY 12
