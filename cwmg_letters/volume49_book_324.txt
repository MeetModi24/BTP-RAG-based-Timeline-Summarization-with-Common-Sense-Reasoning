324. LETTER TO PUSHPA S. PATEL
April 3, 1932
CHI. PUSHPA,
This time your handwriting was a little better. Keep on
improving it.
About spinning, read the letter which I have written to Kanta.
If a Sardar does not order about others, how could he be
called Sardar?
Having taken the part of Mother India in the dramatic
piece, you should now cultivate the virtues befitting her.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3985. Also C.W. 31. Courtesy:
Pushpabehn N. Naik
