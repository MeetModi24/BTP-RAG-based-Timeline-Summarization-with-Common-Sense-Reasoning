633. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
BOMBAY,
April 12, 1945
CHI. KISHORELAL,
I am always thinking about you all. Today I had a letter
from Dr. Ice. He is keen on treating you. Do not run away
from there if you can put up with the heat and undergo Dr.
Ice's treatment. I hope Gomati is well. She should also consult
Dr. Ice about her treatment. He is a nice man.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
