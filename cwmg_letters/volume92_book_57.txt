57. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
July 10, 1929
DEAR MOTILALJI,
Here is a free translation of Vithalbhai's letter.
"If you do not accept or rather ask for the crown this year,
you would be committing another Himalayan blunder. I am coming
on the 17th to persuade you to see my way." I can guess what he
has to say. But I shall await his arrival. I thought you should
know this latest development. Your wire has brought me relief.
I wish Jawahar will come to a decision and end the uncertainty.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: Motilal Nehru Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
