314. LETTER TO GOVINDDAS
NEW DELHI,
July 11, 1947
CHI. GOVINDDAS,
I have your letter and your long statement. Where is the
time to read the statement? And what is the need of it? Of
what importance is one such temple being opened? You should
understand the situation there and do independently what you
think proper.
Blessings from
BAPU
