245. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
[July 10, 1944]
CHI. AMRIT,
I better conserve my time and energy for the business before
me. I have no news to give you. Play I dare not when there
is work which calls me. And I have hardly enough energy to
cope with the daily post.
Love to all.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4201. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7837
