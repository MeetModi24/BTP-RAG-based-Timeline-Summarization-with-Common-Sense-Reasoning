440. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEGAON,
September 7, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
What you said yesterday was not right. You have to under-
stand your duty. You have to understand what Mother says.
You must come only after pacifying her. I hope you are in good
health. It pains me that you are unnecessarily making yourself
unhappy. May God grant you peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
