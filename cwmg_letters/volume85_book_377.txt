377. LETTER TO K. M. PANIKKAR
NEW DELHI,
September 12, 1946
BHAI PANIKKAR,
I have your letter. I understand your point. That your
boys and girls will learn to read, write and correctly under-
stand Hindi and Urdu—even this will be good.
But I cannot understand why, even in Bikaner, you cannot
get all the work done in Hindustani. We shall talk about it
if we have occasion to meet. I appreciate English in its own
place.
BAPU
DEWAN, BIKANER
