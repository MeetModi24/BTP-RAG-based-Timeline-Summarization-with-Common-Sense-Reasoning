505. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
Y. M.,
February 8, 1932
MY DEAR ANAND,
I was so glad to see Vidya. She was looking very well
indeed. I am now sending for your help, not for publication, a
rendering of my speech on God. It is only a fragment
of a fuller thing in Y. I. which you should see, if you have
not already.
Love to you both.
BAPU
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
