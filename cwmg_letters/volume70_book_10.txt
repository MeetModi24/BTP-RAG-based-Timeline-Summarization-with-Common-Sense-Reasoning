10. LETTER TO SHARDABEHN G. CHOKHAWALA
ABBOTTABAD,
July 17, 1939
CHI. BABUDI,
What shall I write to you? Are you not going to take care of
your health so long as you are away from me? Does it mean I
should keep both of you with me or that you should take a
divorce? It will not do for you to fall ill so often. Has there
been any change in your diet?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10014. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
