458. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
September 2, 1933
CHI. AMALA,
I have your letters. You should not expect frequent replies
from me. Whenever I can I will certainly write to you.
I had said nothing to you about my health because I had
referred to it in some other letters. I am getting on as well as
possible. I am regaining strength steadily. Therefore, there is
no cause for anxiety. I am taking milk, fruit and vegetables.
I hope that you are steadily qualifying yourself.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
