456. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 22, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I have your letter. If you will only realize that the verses
are a part of our prayers and that, therefore, we should learn
them by heart, you will be able with some effort to get absorbed
in them. You should not lose heart if you do not succeed in
the effort. All those who recite them do not become absorbed
in them. If, however, you continue to have faith and recite
them, one day you will find that you can become absorbed in
them. Moreover, the verses have a deep meaning. If you meditate
over it, that, too, will help you to concentrate on them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1810
