238. LETTER TO PANDIT GOKARAN NATH
THE SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
May 22 [1920]
DEAR PANDIT GOKARAN NATH 2 ,
As I hope to meet you soon at Benares, I do not propose
to deal with your letter regarding Shaukat Ali's speech by
post.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
5 NEILL ROAD
LUCKNOW
From the original: A.I.C.C. File. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
