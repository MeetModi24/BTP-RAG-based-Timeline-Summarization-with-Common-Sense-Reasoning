45. LETTER TO LIEUTENANT-GOVERNOR'S SECRETARY
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
P. O. Box 6522,
JOHANNESBURG,
November 25, 1903
TO
THE PRIVATE SECRETARY
TO HIS EXCELLENCY THE LIEUTENANT-GOVERNOR
PRETORIA
SIR,
I have the honour to acknowledge your letter of the 24th instant,
numbered 97/2, in reply to my letter of the 14th instant regarding trading
licenses held by British Indians.
Since the date that the British Indian deputation waited on His
Excellency at Johannesburg, no communication has been received from
the Colonial Secretary on the question.
His Excellency was pleased to say to the deputation that the matter
would be considered at a meeting of the Executive Council at an early
date and that a reply would then be sent to the Association.
May I know whether the Association will receive such reply?
I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant
ABDOOL GANI
CHAIRMAN,
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
Pretoria Archives: L. G. 97/2, Asiatics 1902-1906.
