572. LETTER TO IFTIKHAR-UD-DIN AND ISMAT
October 5, 1945
BHAI IFTIKHAR AND ISMAT,
I have your wire. May you both fare well and serve the
community. It is all right that you have joined the League.
There too work as a friend. Never have enmity against anyone.
21 ROOK MAIN ROAD
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
