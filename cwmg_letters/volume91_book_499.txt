499. LETTER TO ANIL KUMAR MITRA
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 30, 1926
MY DEAR ANIL,
I have your letter. I am sorry that you are unable to go
back to Santiniketan. You will let me know how you finally
fix yourself up. I would like you not to publish the autobio-
graphy just now. Because I am revising the first part with a
view to publishing it in book form in English. As soon as it is
revised and published you shall have a copy when you can make
the necessary corrections and publish the translation. But before
you do so, I would like you to send the manuscript for Maha-
dev to see. As you know, Mahadev knows Bengali.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: S.N. 32253
