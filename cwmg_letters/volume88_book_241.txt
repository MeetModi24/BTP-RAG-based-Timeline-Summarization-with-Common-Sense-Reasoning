241. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
NEW DELHI,
July 3, 1947
CHI. LILI,
I got your postcard and also the telegram. You are born
for service. Don't worry. Do to the best of your ability whatever
work comes to you unsought. I don't like your relations calling
on you for help. But you cannot escape such requests. If your
brother recovers soon and you can be free, you will be able to
devote yourself to study. Even now I suppose you are not kept
busy the whole day looking after him. What is necessary is that
the mind should form the habit of study. Sushila is at Wardha.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 10436. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
