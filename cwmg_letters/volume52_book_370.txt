370. LETTER TO MUKANDI LAL
December 24, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your interesting letter of the 16th instant. I
fully realize that it will be only by persistent and gentle effort that
the deep-rooted evil can be removed.
As to the proposed scholarships, I would advise you to discuss
your project with Pandit Kunzru who is the Chairman of the U.P.
Branch of the newly formed Society.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. MUKANDI LAL
BARRISTER-AT-LAW
LANSDOWNE (U.P.)
From a microfilm: S.N. 18800
