621. LETTER TO KANTIPRASAD C. ANTANI
SABARMATI,
[January 10, 1928]
BHAISHRI KANTIPRASAD 2 ,
I have received your book. It will be difficult for me just
now to find time to read it carefully. The remedies for the evils
of the Indian States are not different. I therefore find it hard
to spare time for a study of their special problems.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Purusharthi Kantiprasad Antani, pp. 79-80
