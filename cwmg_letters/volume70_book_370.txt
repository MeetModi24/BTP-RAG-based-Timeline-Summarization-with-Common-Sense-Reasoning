370. LETTER TO SHARDABEHN G. CHOKHAWALA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 30, 1939
CHI. BABUDI,
I have your letter. You must make it a point to go out for
regular walks. Is it that you don't get any woman friend to accom-
pany you? Here everything is all right. Premabehn arrived today.
I shall have to leave for Delhi tomorrow. I hope to return on
Thursday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10017. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokha-
wala
