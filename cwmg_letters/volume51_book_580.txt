580. LETTER TO BHAU PANSE
November 6, 1932
CRI. BHAU,
I do not have time to write much today. I hope you got
the letter which I wrote after the last weekly post. I am eager
to know the effect of the fast. You should know how to resume
food gradually after the fast has ended. Please remember that in
connecting one's food with dharma one is likely to go to an ex-
treme.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6741. Also C.W. 4484. Courtesy:
Bhau Panse
