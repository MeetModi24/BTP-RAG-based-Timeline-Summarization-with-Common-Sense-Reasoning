159. LETTER TO AMRITA LAL CHATTERJEE
May 7, 1945
CHI. AMRITA LAL,
Let them write what they want. I do not wish to send for
them here. I have not the time either.
I have advised Sailen to go to the Malad Sanatorium.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 10403. Courtesy: Amrita Lal
Chatterjee
