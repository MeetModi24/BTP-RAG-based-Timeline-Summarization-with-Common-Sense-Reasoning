453. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
October 20, 1932
BHAI VALJI,
I got your letter which arrived with the Ashram mail. You
seem to have lost considerable weight. Regain it now by eating
properly and giving rest to the mind. You must have got the regis-
tered letter which I posted yesterday in regard to your book.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7442. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
