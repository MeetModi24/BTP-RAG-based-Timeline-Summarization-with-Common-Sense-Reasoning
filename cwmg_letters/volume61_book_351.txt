351. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
WARDHA,
July 11, 1935
CHI. PREMA,
I got your letter just now. Since it was written on the occa-
sion of your birthday, have my blessings first.
What a woman you are! You don't say which birthday you
celebrated.
Your noble aspirations will assuredly be fulfilled.
One who strives for good aims never strives in vain, and one
who strives for evil aims never succeeds. The latter only seems
to succeed sometimes.
About other things, when I have more time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10375. Also C.W. 6814. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
