589. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM L. BAVISHI
September 13, 1935
BHAI PURUSHOTTAM,
I got your postcard. Send me the name of the village you
have in mind. The decision regarding Vinoba is not yet final.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 127. Also C.W. 4750. Courtesy:
Purushottam L. Bavishi
