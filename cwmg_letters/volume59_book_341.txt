341. LETTER TO MARIA
November 9, 1934
DEAR LARK,
I have your very carefully written postcard both in Italian
and English. I do prize your prayer of which at this moment I
am much in need. I cannot forget the Larks and they have al-
ways my blessings in so far as I may be fit to give them.
SISTER MARIA
LARK OF ST. FRANCIS
EREMO FRANCISCANO, TREVI (UMBRIA)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
