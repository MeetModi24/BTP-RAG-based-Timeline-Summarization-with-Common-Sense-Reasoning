368. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
SEVAGRAM,
July 25, 1940
CHI. KAKA,
Please write to Bhai Saxena and tell him that I have care-
fully gone through his articles from beginning to end. I did
not find in them a clear answer to the question what the mind
can do or cannot do. He has not correctly understood my ideas
on Hindi and Urdu.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10933
