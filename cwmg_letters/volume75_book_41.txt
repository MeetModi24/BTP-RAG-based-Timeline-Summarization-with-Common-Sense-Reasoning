41. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 17, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
This I am writing after curfew. It will be posted by Prithvi-
chand who is leaving tomorrow.
I am glad you report marked improvement. May it continue.
I have told Satyamurti plainly that so far as I am concerned
no change can be made. If he thinks otherwise he is free to culti-
vate public opinion in his favour.
You are right about Harijans.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4100. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7409
