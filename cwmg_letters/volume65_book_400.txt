400. LETTER TO MANUBEHN S. MASHRUWALA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 27, 1937
CHI. MANUDI,
I had your letter. You should find time for reading and
practising on the sitar. Ba and Kano are coming tomorrow.
Khan Saheb and Mehrtaj were occupying Ba's room. Kusum
Desai also is coming. Now the house will be full. You had rain
there but here we have had none.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
SHRI MANUBEHN
C/O VORA HARIDAS VAKHATCHAND
BEHIND HIGH SCHOOL
RAJKOT C. S., KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2669. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
