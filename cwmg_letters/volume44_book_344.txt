344. LETTER TO PADMA
October 23, 1930
CHI. PADMA1,
So you blame me! You yourself never write, but expect letters
from me. What is the nature of the tumour in the throat? You
should get that ascertained immediately. Did you show it to
Gangabehn? Do you spin daily on Prabhubhai's spinning-wheel?
I should be happy if you do. How fine it would be if you spin
that number of rounds every day! How is Sharda's health? What
do you read? What food do you take? Do you go to bed daily
exactly at nine? What work did you do in the U.P.? Whom did
you meet while there?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6113
