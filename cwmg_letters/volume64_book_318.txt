318. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
January 25, 1937
CHI. LILI,
I have had no letters from you recently. We returned from
the tour yesterday. I had a letter from Nanavati from which
I learnt that your brother could not be said to have improved
yet. You will have got my last letter. I hope you are following
the suggestions I made in it.
Tell Nanavati if he is there that I didn't reply to him since
he told me he would be reaching there about the 25th. Have
you taken your little pair of scissors with you or left it here?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9354. Also C.W. 6629. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
