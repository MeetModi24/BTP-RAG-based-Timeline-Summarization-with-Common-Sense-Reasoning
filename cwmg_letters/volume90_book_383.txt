383. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
NEW DELHI,
January 12, 1948
DEAR DESHABHAKTA,
It is refreshing to get a fairly long letter from you. I referred
to it yesterday in my prayer speech.
What you say is too
shocking for words. I cannot move out of Delhi.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3233
