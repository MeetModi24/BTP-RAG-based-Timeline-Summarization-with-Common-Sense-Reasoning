242. LETTER TO THE TEACHERS AND PUPILS
OF GURUKUL
POONA,
Friday [November 5, 1920]
TEACHERS AND PUPILS OF GURUKUL 2 ,
I have your letter. How can I forget that the Gurukul has
bound my children with bonds of love? What message can I
send you? If I must, I will tell you: Do you keep off the sin of
modernity? Do you think of the downtrodden of India by spin-
ning every day? Have you realized that plying the little spinning-
wheel today is a great yajna?
No step along this path is ever wasted.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi : S.N. 7429
