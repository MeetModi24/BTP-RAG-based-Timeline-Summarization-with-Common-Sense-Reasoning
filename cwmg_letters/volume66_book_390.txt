390. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
December 8, 1937
CHI. SHARDA,
You dare not accept defeat. Remain calm.
Keep on
writing to me in detail. I could not [write] regularly. Only
today I have taken up some letters. It would not be so al-
ways. Do all your work with scrupulous care.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9977. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Cho-
khawala
