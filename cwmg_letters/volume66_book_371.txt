371. LETTER TO JEHANGIR VAKIL
November 22, 1937
MY DEAR VAKIL,
I was able to read your letter only this morning.
I cannot
overtake my correspondence in time.
Your love I treasure, of your hate, I know nothing.
But
even if I had known it, I would not have minded it. How
could one help acting according to one's nature? 'In Christ'
and 'in God' have for years been convertible terms for me.
I
may not, probably will not, take the word in the same sense as
most Christians do. But that matters little.
Yours,
M . K . GANDHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
