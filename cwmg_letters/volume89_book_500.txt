500. LETTER TO MAHESH DUTT MISHRA
November 9, 1947
CHI. MAHESH,
I have your letter. If you can be spared from there,
come over here. I am so busy that I do not know how much
time I can give you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 6724. Also C. W. 4468. Courtesy:
Mahesh Dutt Mishra
