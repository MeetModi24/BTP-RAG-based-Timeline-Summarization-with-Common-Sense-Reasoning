75. LETTER TO ABDUL MAJID KHAN
January 19, 1945
MY DEAR PROF. ABDUL MAJID KHAN,
You should boldly stand for the coming Panjab University
Fellowship elections. They will choose you for your worth as
a nationalist. My hearty blessings to you.
Since you have the support of all the eminent Congressmen,
if you are defeated it will be because the majority of graduate
voters are not Congress-minded. It will also show that in your
province at least the Congress hold on the educated men is not as
great as it should be.
I hope you will win.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
PROF. ABDUL MAJID KHAN
6.B KAPURTHALA HOUSE
LAKE ROAD, LAHORE
