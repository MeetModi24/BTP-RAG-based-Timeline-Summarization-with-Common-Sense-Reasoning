689. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Saturday [October 27, 1928]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I receive your letters after long intervals. I would be happy
if without straining yourself you could come here for a day, but
only if it does not cause you any physical strain at all. There is
an epidemic of fever around here these days. It seems in this
season the peasant families do become afflicted with fever. You
must follow Dr. Jivraj's advice and spend one more year in the
peaceful atmosphere of a hill resort.
Mahadev is at Bardoli this year. He will be in Bombay on
Sunday. I should be very happy if your programme of going to
Almora materializes. Prabhudas was immensely benefited.
Blessings from
BAPU
SF. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
VICTORIA LODGE
MATHERAN (G.I.P. RLY.)
