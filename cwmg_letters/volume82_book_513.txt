513. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
SODEPUR,
January 8, 1946
CHI. DEVDAS,
I got your letter about Prabhu Dayal on 3-1-1946. I am
very busy myself. Prabhu Dayal seems to be getting along fairly
well. I expect you have detained him, let me know the develop-
ments.
Kanchan has fallen ill. I have to go to Assam today.
I hope you are all well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SF. DEVDAS GANDHI
"HINDUSTAN TIMES"
NEW DELHI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
