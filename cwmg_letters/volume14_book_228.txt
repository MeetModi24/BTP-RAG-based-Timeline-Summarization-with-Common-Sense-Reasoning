228. LETTER TO SHIVDAS AND POPATLAL
NADIAD,
Chaitra Sud 1 [April 12, 1918]
DEAR SHRI SHIVDAS 1 ,
I got both your letter and postcard. I have written to them
to send you Rs. 10/-. Let me know if you find that this is not
enough. Maintain a diary. Is the plague still raging there?
BHAI POPATLAL 2 ,
I read your lines. I have not lost my faith in you. Let me
know what you have been doing and the state of your eyes; I shall
then entrust you with some work you can do there.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[PS.]
The earlier Motilal's family comes over here, the better.
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 2857
