148. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
June 3, 1938
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. You will understand everything by and
by. Have patience for the present.
Don't run away from there in haste. Stay on till Durga
has completely recovered. Here Kanchan is looking after every-
thing quite well. The bread she makes is also quite good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9372. Also C.W. 6647. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
