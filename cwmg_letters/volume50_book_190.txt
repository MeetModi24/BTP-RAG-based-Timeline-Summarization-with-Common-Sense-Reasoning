190. LETTER TO INDU N. PAREKH
July 8, 1932
DEAR INDU,
It is very good that you have confidence in Narandas. As
he says it is possible that you will benefit by being patient in
taking food and sleeping for a long time. Do you get motions
properly?
BAPU
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 201
