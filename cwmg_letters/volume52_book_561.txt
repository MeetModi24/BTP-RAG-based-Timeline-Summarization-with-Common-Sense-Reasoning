561. LETTER TO S. PONNAMMAL
January 10, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
I was very much touched by your letter. I am still unable
to know why you should be getting only Rs. 8 per month. Is it
possible for you to see Sjt. K. Madhavan Nair? I am writing to
him and sending him a copy of your letter. He may even find you
out instead of putting you to the trouble of going to him. If
it is not a matter of much distance between Calicut and your place,
the matter is simple. If it is a long distance to be covered, it may
be difficult for you. I quite see that full use is not being made of
your talents. Is your letter your own composition?
Yours sincerely,
S. PONNAMMAL
PATTERI HOUSE
CALICUT
