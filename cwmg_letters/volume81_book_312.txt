312. LETTER TO S. NIJALINGAPPA
August 29, 1945
BHAI NIJALINGAPPA,
I have your letter. Now the Working Committee and the
States People's Conference are functioning.
Do you think I
should say something?
This question deserves consideration.
Write to both.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
