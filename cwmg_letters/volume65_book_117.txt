117. LETTER TO DR. JAWAHARLAL
NEAR HUDLI (BELGAUM),
April 21, 1937
MY DEAR DR. JAWAHARLAL,
Thank you very much for your detailed letter of the 14th.
You are right in thinking that the recent attack of influenza had
something to do with the extinction of life in the child. I
suppose she will have to stay there for about a fortnight before
she is in a condition to travel. How is it that there is no kitchen
attached to the maternity hospital? Please remember me to
Mrs. Jawaharlal who I hope is steadily improving.
Yours sincerely,
DR. JAWAHARLAL, M.L.A.
SWAROOP SADAN
CAWNPORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
