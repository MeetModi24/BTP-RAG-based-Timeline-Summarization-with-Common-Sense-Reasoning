225. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 25, 1940
DAUGHTER,
I got your letter. You are in too great a hurry. I shall send
both Akbar and Hanif1, if they are of any use to you. You have
not gone there for doing khadi work. It is good if it appeals to the
poor Muslims. But first you should look with your eyes open and
find out who the poor are and how they live. You have only one
mission: to stop the bloodshed. If you get involved in the political
controversies between Hindus and Muslims, rest assured that your
mission will fail. You have seen Anand's letters, also those of Jam-
shedji. Have differences cropped up between you and these people?
I shall get nervous if you develop disagreements with such people.
What does Hatim say? In any case, try and understand Maulana
Saheb's view. He will come here. We are not going to do anything
against his wishes.
Pyarelal will now come on Thursday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 453
