382. LETTER TO RAMESHWARI NEHRU
MUSSOORIE,
June 7, 1946
CHI. RAMESHWARI,
I know Ratnamayidevi very well. I have no objection to
your taking her in. I do not know myself who is a Gandhian.
Gandhism is a meaningless word for me. An ism follows the
propounder of a system. I am not one, hence I cannot be
the cause for any ism. If an ism is built up it will not endure,
and if it does it will not be Gandhism. This deserves to be
properly understood.
I like your work, it is neat and clean. It is you who
have built up the Balika Ashram, you conduct it and I would
be pleased if Ratnamayidevi gave you full satisfaction.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 8010. Also C. W. 3110. Courtesy:
Rameshwari Nehru
