330. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
POONA,
March 18, 1946
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your letter. I have left it to you to decide about
teaching. The general rule is that a manager only manages.
But he reserves to himself the right to make exceptions. It is
true that teaching also is a part of the management.
There is no need of more land for farming, is there?
It is good that you go into the village.
I am well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 4485
