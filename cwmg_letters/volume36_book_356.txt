356. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[May 9, 1928]
CHI. MIRA,
Your letter. Never mind the cold bath if you took all the
precautions after.
The bath in the circumstances you men-
tioned was almost inevitable. In future it is better to remember
that a sponge in such circumstances is better. Tell Pyarelal to
write to me. Chhotelal has taken up his work.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5302. Courtesy: Mirabehn
