223. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
February 15, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I tasted the dalia. I had asked for a sample only from what
was cooked in the kitchen. What was sent had been left in the
cooker for two hours. It had puffed up but even then it tasted
half-cooked. There were hard grains, and bran stuck in the
mouth. I think we cannot cook dalia from Jowar as we can from
wheat. After the flour has been removed, no nutrient is left.
The Jowar, therefore, should be turned into fine flour and soaked
in water, and then ghensh or raab should be prepared from it. Your
reading of the Gita is too slow. This also is an art. You should
read it faster.
Blessings from
BAPU
