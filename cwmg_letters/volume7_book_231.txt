231. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
[JOHANNESBURG,]
October 14, 1907
MY DEAR MAGANLAL,
I have your letter. Please tell Mr. Budrea that I have noted all fees
with the utmost care. As he was absent, many letters I wrote have not
been charged for at all. Still, tell him that he can strike off any fees
charged by me and I shall accept his decision. With reference to his
papers, I am looking into the matter. You can be very plain to him
with reference to my bill. I should feel sorry for him to think that I
could ever betray the trust reposed by him in me and charge fees in an
unwarranted manner. I would like him to go through every item and
put his cross against everything he considers objectionable.
I have received Mrs. Doman's account of distribution.
Yours sincerely
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4769
