312. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
June 30, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your two letters. The Consul is being contacted for
the visa. I hope you will not have to go to Calcutta for that. Dr.
Kellogg has not sent a reply. It should have come. I shall
make other arrangements.
Shri P.'s letter contains nothing. I
have written to him that I cannot pay any heed to his letter. I
have not attached any weight to his letter. That is why I did
not write to you about it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha, pp. 172-
