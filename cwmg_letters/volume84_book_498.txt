498. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
POONA,
July 4, 1946
MY DEAR MR. ABELL,
I am enclosing a copy of a cable 2 received this morning by Gandhiji
from H. H. the Aga Khan.
Gandhiji would like to know if H. E. can do anything to help In the
matter of the air passage for Ashwin Chawdhuri which the Aga Khan
wants. 3
I hope you are enjoying your respite from the heat.
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 98
