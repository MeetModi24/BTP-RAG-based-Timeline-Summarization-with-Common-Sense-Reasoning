271. LETTER TO THAKORELAL
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday, Chaitra Vad [5] [April 3, 1926]
BHAI THAKORELAL,
I have your letter. I find it impossible to guide or advise
you through letters. I certainly do not want you to stay here,
giving up your studies. If you come here during your vacation,
we can talk about this and, may be, you will get some consolation.
Owing to certain difficulties we had to withdraw all stocks of silk
from the Khadi Bhandars.
