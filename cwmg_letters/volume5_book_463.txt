463. LETTER TO DR. E. NUNDY
21-24 COURT CHAMBERS,
JOHANNESBURG,
September 27, 1906
[DR. EDWARD NUNDY
JACOB CHAMBERS
COURT ROAD
JOHANNESBURG]
DEAR DR. NUNDY,
By class distinction I mean that there should be no legislation apply-
ing to Asiatics, Coloured people or Indians as such.
All legislation ought to be, [as] laid down by Chamberlain, of a
general character.
Yours truly,
(Signed) H. M. GANDHI
for M. K. GANDHI
Pretoria Archives : L. G. File No. 93 : Asiatics
