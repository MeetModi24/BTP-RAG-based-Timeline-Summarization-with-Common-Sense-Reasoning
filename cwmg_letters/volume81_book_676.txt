676. LETTER TO AGRAWAL
October 18, 1945
BHAI AGRAWAL,
If your intention is to run the clinic just for philanthropy and
to use only such medicines as anyone can prepare with some effort,
I believe it is bound to succeed.
ASSISTANT SECRETARY
THE HINDUSTAN MERCANTILE ASSOCIATION
641 CHANDNI CHOWK
DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
