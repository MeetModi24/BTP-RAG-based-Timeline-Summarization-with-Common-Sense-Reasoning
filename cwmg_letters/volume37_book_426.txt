426. LETTER TO MAHARAJA OF NABHA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 18, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I had a visit too from Sjt. Ganesan. I
regret I am unable to help you. I have given you my reasons
and I gave them to those friends who came to see me on your
behalf.
Yours sincerely,
H. H. THE MAHARAJAH OF NABHA
THE OBSERVATORY, KODAIKANAL, SOUTH INDIA
From a microfilm: S.N. 13565
