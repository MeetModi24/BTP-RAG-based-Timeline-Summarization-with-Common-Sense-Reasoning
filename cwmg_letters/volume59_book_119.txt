119. LETTER TO BEHCHARDAS
September 30, 1934
BHAI BEHCHARDAS,
I got your letter. I showed it to Kakasaheb. He is not satis-
fied with your work. He also says you have not returned to
him Rs. 25 which was paid to you in connection with your work
and which you have to return. There are other complaints too.
In these circumstances he has no wish or courage to entrust
you with any work.
BEHCHARDAS
DELHI
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
