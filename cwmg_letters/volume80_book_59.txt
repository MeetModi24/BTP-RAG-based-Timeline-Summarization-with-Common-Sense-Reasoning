59. LETTER TO GAJANAN NAIK
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 29, 1945
My views on palm gur are firm. People should have per-
mission to make palm gur or toddy wherever there are palm
trees. Our requirement can be met with this gur and sugar-
cane gur. I am of the opinion that palm gur is better than
sugar-cane gur.
CHI. GAJANAN 2 ,
This is for you. I did forget about palm gur. I had to write
in a hurry. That was the last day. You could have added that
and wired me. I had written "etc.". That includes gur. Gur has
lost nothing. When was it bitter?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GAJANAN
MAGANVADI
WARDHA
