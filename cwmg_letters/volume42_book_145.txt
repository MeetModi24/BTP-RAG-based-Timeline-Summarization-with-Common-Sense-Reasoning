145. LETTER TO K. SANTANAM
CAMP HATHRAS,
November 8, 1929
MY DEAR SANTANAM,
I enclose herewith copy of reply from Satis Babu of Khadi
Pratishthan in reply to my enquiry. Chhaganlal Joshi and Subra-
maniam are now with you. You will do whatever you think
proper.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
PT. K. SANTANAM
From a microfilm: S.N. 15759
