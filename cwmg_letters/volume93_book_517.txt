517. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 7, 1940
DEAR FRIEND,
I was glad to have your letter. Who says you are old?
And of course I am young enough to be your son. I am sorry
I cannot move out. What is poor B. P. . . .1 doing now? C. D.
is suspended.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
THE ARAM
SALEM
From a photostat: C. Vijayaraghavachariar Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
