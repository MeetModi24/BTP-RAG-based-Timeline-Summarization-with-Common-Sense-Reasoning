441. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHARMA
August 27, 1933
BHAI SHARMA,
Do come after ten days.
I have no idea where I shall be.
You will learn from the newspapers. Make independent arrange-
ments for your stay. I hope your problems have been resolved.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh
(1932-48), between pp. 52 and 53
