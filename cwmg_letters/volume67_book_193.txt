193. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
SEGAON,
June 29, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
It is not your fault, either. Nor is it Amtul Salaam's. I had
no other intention except that of changing Amtul Salaam if it was
necessary for you to remain there for more than six days. You
ought not to indulge in conjectures about my intentions. You
may come only if the doctor gives full permission. Lilavati can
be sent without any inconvenience.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 4579. Courtesy: Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
