339. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
February 3, 1937
CHI. NARANDAS,
Your description is excellent. That day of death was indeed
a day of celebration. Here also Krishna and Kanaiyo behaved
very well.
I hope the school is going on all right.
How is Purushottam ? How is Vijaya ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8514. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
