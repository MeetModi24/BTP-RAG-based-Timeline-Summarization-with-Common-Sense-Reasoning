109. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
June 24, 1936
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
I do not remember to have told Sardar that the Godhra
Ashram was running very well. I told him that the Ashram
was working and had not been closed. However, you certainly did
well in informing me of the situation. Do what you think right.
You do not say whether the decision about Bharuch is correct.
I have not been able to understand the effect of the decision.
Is that the whole resolution, or is there anything more to it?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4038
1 Feeling of devotion
2 Firm faith
3 Harijan Ashram, managed by Vithal L. Phadke
