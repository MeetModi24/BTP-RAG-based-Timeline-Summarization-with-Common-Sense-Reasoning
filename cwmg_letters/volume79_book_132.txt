132. LETTER TO JAYA
SEVAGRAM,
January 29, 1945
CHI. JAYA,
There is no cause at all for fear of Vasant succumbing.
Typhoid is not a very serious matter. With proper care the
patient recovers. Have courage. Cheer up Vasant.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./XXIII
