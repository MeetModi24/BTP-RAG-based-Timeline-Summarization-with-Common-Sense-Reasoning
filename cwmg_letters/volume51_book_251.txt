251. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
September 30, 1932
I feel love flowing from every line in your letter. Is it not
my good fortune that I should get a daughter like you? I have
readily accepted the position of father which you and others have
bestowed on me. And I have done so with God as witness. May
He make me worthy of it and protect my honour. I am rapidly
regaining strength.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 94
