395. LETTER TO MILDRED
February 17, 1942
MY DEAR MILDRED3,
Just a p.c. to send you and yours our love. Your contribu-
tion is welcome. Every copper tells when it becomes part of a
heap. Yes, we are going through a terrible ordeal.
Love to you both.
BAPU
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
