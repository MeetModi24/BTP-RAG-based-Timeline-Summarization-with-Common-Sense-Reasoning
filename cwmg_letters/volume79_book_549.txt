549. LETTER TO DADUBHAI
SEVAGRAM,
March 28, 1945
BHAI DADUBHAI,
Why should I appoint the Durbar or anyone else as arbitrator ?
It was my duty to send your son's letter to you. I have indeed
no reason to disbelieve your letter.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
