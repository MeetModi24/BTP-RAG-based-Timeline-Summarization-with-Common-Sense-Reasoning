221. LETTER TO SECRETARY, CONGRESS COMMITTEE,
CHITTAGONG
As AT SABARMATI,
May 21, 1931
THE SECRETARY
CONGRESS COMMITTEE
CHITTAGONG
DEAR SIR,
In reply to your telegram I have sent you the following:
Impossible give opinion without knowing full facts.
Subject to any change that might have to be made after
knowing further facts I can say that punitive tax imposed for
anything done since Settlement would be no violation thereof.
Nor refusal to pay such tax will necessarily be violation of Settle-
ment. But those who will embark upon no-tax campaign will do
so after fullest consideration and on their own sole responsibility.
Yours sincerely,
