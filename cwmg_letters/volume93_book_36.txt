36. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
May 4, 1935
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND 1 ,
God will bless you in your new dedication as He has done
hitherto . . . 2
Shri Sharma of Khurja will probably send you a list
of medical books to be bought. Will you please get them
for him? Perhaps the booksellers will quote cheap rates
to you.
BAPU
DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
LAHORE, PUNJAB
From the original : Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
