311. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
July 10, 1933
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your letter. Purushottam has resumed work, but his
mind is still not at peace.
Keshu is still here. He is calm. I feel that our duty towards
Jnana requires us to do something more for her.
Tell Panditji that I understand what he says regarding
Gajanan and Dhiru. Ramdas and Nimu are here. Kano has
taken ill and so they have had to stay on. Mahadev is comp-
letely free. Babla has an attack of fever, but today it is very
slight. There is no cause for worry at all.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8390. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
