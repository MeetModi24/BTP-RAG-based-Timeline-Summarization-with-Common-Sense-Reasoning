246. LETTER TO LADY RAZA ALI
[As at] SEGAON,
September 27, 1939
MY DEAR POORVI,
I am writing this on a moving train. Sir Raza told me that
you were ailing. I promised to write to you and in order that
I may not forget I am writing this now. I hope this will find
you fully restored.
Love to you all.
BAPU
LADY RAZA ALI
78 JONES STREET
KIMBERLEY, S. AFRICA
From a photostat: G.N. 7749
