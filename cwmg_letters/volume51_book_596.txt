596. LETTER TO BALIBEHN M. ADALAJA
November 7, 1932
CHI. BALI,
You wrote to me after a long time. But I don't blame you.
I believe you are carrying a heavy burden. I would not find
fault with you if, in the midst of all that work, you cannot write
purely formal letters. It seems you live only to look after your
sisters' children. That is also one way, and a good one, of follow-
ing the path of dharma. Who could have prevented you if you
had chosen to be selfish and confine yourself to your own affair?
Instead, you have chosen a path of service, however modest.
May God bless you.
Blessings from
BAPU
