406. LETTER TO HASTMAL PATWA
MAHISHADAL,
December 28, 1945
BHAI HASTMAL PATWA,
I received your telegram at Mahishadal. I do not know what
can be done about it. Write or telegraph to Jawaharlalji.
SHRI HASTMAL PATWA
13 NARMAL LOHIA LANE
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
