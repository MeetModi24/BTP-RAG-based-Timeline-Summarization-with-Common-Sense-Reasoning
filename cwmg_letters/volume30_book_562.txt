562. LETTER TO G. K. DEVADHAR
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 23, 1926
MY DEAR DEVADHAR,
I have your letter. It will be a pleasure to meet you when-
ever you can come. And when you do come of course you will
stay at the Ashram. I know that you are not likely to come here
before the rains have probably set in. At the present moment we
are boiling here.
I don't want you to find yourself in the oven
with us.
I hope Mrs. Devadhar is better now. She must come next
winter to the Ashram and pass a few days here.
When you return you will send me a report about Mano-
rama.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. G. K. DEVADHAR
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19563
