745. LETTER TO JEHANGIR PATEL
October 24, 1945
BHAI JEHANGIR,
I have your letter. Gradually form the habit of speaking
and writing in Gujarati. Read and write a little of Gujarati
every day.
Your letter is clear and I have understood it. I feel that
just now we should go to the sanatorium and shift to Tryambak
Road after we get electricity and water. But I don't want to do
this if Dinshaw does not like it.
I understand about Verrier. More when you come over.
I hope Mother is all right and you did not get fever again.
JEHANGIR PATEL
PATEL BROTHERS
10 CHURCHGATE STREET
BOMBAY 1
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
