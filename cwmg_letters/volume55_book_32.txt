32. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
April 26, 1933
DEAR SIR PURUSHOTTAMDAS,
As you were actively connected with the Yeravda Pact, I
would like you to let me have your opinion on Dr. Ambedkar's
suggestion for the amendment of the Yeravda Pact.
I hope you are keeping well.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 21062
