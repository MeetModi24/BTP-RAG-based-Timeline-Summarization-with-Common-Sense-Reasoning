417. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[September 7, 1927]
CHI. MIRA,
I anxiously wait for your wires and they come but not to
present me with a clean bill. But we must not grumble. Even
illness must be turned to advantage and must be taken cheerfully.
Your last wire has come just now to tell me that perhaps fever
is under control. Let us hope it is. I often think of wiring to you
but say to myself I have no right. But my prayers and my
blessings are with you always.
"The same in happiness and misery!" is the teaching of the
Gita.
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5271. Courtesy: Mirabehn
