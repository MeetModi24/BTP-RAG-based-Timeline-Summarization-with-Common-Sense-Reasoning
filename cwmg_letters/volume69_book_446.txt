446. LETTER TO VIDYAVATI
ABBOTTABAD,
July 11, 1939
CHI. VIDYA,
I had your letter of June 13. I could not reply earlier.
My health is now all right. Do come over when I reach
Segaon.
Tell me all about the conditions there. If you write to
Rajendra Babu and others, they will all listen to you.
Where is Lakshmi these days?
What does she do?
Have
you fully recovered? I shall be here till the end of this month,
if not longer.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati File. Courtesy: Gandhi National
Museum and Library
