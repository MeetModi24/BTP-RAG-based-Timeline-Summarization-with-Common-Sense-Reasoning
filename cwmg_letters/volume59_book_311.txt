311. LETTER TO MURJOR M. MANA
November 5, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. There is no doubt that any testimonial
that can be devised for Jamshed Mehta will not be too much for
his unique services. But how is it that you are the only per-
son moving in the matter? I would strongly dissuade you from
taking the lead. There should be a universal appeal from the
citizens of Karachi or nothing at all.
And if the citizens of
Karachi do not move of their own accord, I will be the last per-
son to prompt them.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI MURJOR M. MANA, B.E. (CIVIL), ETC.
BUNDER ROAD
KARACHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
