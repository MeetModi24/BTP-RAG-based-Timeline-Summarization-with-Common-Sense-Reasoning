257. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 20, 1945
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I have your letter. In the circumstances, Kanchan must go.
You may accompany her or send somebody or let Munnalal
come or send somebody. Is this not the only way?
How can I decide about Akbar and Zohra? Akbar is
doing good work in Samau. He would go if I were to order
him. But I have left it to him. Zohra is still studying. You
should consider this also if possible. It is a very difficult situa-
tion.
You cannot go anywhere leaving the things in the middle
there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 495
