358. LETTER TO SIKANDAR
August 10, 1941
MY DEAR S.,
Your impatient letter to hand. No hurried action need be
taken. Haste is waste. Time is always in favour of Right. I see
no harm in your practising. But your practice should also be calm,
not tempestuous.
Rajkumari does not return before the end of the month, if
then. It will depend upon her health. Her address is . . . .1
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
