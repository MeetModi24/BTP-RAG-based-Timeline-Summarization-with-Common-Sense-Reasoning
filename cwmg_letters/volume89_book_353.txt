353. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
October 19, 1947
CHI. VALJI,
I have sent as many of your articles as I could read. It
would be better if you could translate the English articles into
Gujarati, too.
Blessings to all of you from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C. W. 3173. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
