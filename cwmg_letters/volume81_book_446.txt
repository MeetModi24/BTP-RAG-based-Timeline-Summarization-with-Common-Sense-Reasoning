446. LETTER TO DR. B. S. MOONJE
POONA,
September 14, 1945
BHAI MOONJE,
I have your long letter. What is the point of my replying to
it? Your views and mine are oceans apart. It is well that we
can continue to be friends in spite of the differences. Why is
your letter in English? Why not in Marathi or Hindustani?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. MOONJE
BHONSLE MILITARY SCHOOL
NASIK
G. I. P. RAILWAY
From the Hindi original: C.W. 9763. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
