691. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 6, 1928
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. You must have already received my
letter and my blessings. If the climate of Matheran suits you
better than that of Deolali, nothing like it. Then I would not be
keen about Almora. I shall leave here on the 23rd and proceed
to Wardha via Bardoli. Hence I shall not be passing through
Bombay. Mahadev is still at Bardoli. He will reach here to-
morrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
VICTORIA LODGE
MATHERAN
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
