85. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
BORSAD,
June 28, 1931
CHI. MAHAVIR,
Probably the cause of your not keeping well at the Ashram
lies in yourself. I believe that we can train the body to adjust
itself to any place. The main factor is food. Through experi-
ments one can find out what food suits one in a particular season.
There is no doubt, moreover, that much depends on one's mind.
Hence, if you are very eager to go and live in the Vidyapith,
you may go there after obtaining the permission of Narandasbhai
and Chhaganbhai. You may stay wherever you think you will
keep good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
