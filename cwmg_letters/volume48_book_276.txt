276. LETTER TO CARL HEATH
S.S. "PILSNA" ,
December 20, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of friendly farewell. I treasure
the thought that among the many friends of India's cause I can
count those whose names you have kindly sent me.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 1026
