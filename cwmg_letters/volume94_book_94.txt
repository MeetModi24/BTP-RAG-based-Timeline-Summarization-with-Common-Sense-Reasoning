94. LETTER TO KAMALADEVI CHATTOPADHYAYA
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA, C.P.,
October 3, 1941
DEAR KAMALADEVI,
I got your letter only yesterday. It was received earlier but I don't
succeed in reaching the post the day it is received. I am glad you
have returned home after a long absence. You can come on Sunday
or Tuesday next. You will be more comfortable at the Bungalow but
you could come directly to the Ashram if you will prefer. We shall
talk at 4.00 p.m. on either date.
I hope you are quite well.
Love.
BAPU
From the original : Kamaladevi Chattopadhyaya Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
