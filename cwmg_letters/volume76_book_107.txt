107. LETTER TO SOHANLAL DUGAR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 2, 1942
BHAI SOHANLAL,
My blessings to the couple on the occasion of your son's wed-
ding. I hope they will both render true service.
Blessings from
BAPU
