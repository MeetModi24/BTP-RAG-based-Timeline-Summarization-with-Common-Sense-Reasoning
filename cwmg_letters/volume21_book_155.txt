155. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 27, 1921
If I have really conquered the six enemies of man, the people
would readily accept whatever I do or speak as born of expe-
rience. But I myself am not aware of having conquered the pas-
sions. Have I succeeded even now in overcoming the fear of
serpent-bite? That I have not rid myself of it signifies my spiri-
tual darkness.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 37
