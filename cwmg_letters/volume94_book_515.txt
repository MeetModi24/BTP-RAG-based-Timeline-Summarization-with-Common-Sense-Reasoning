515. LETTER TO PYARELAL
URULI,
August 1, 1946
CHI. PYARELAL,
I feel happy that here we have the Gitai and discourses in Marathi.
It is absolutely peaceful here. No one disturbs me. Faces of Rajkumari,
Sushila and Abha were around me. What does that mean? We shall
have to give thought to this place also. Anybody who wishes can come
tomorrow after 3 o'clock.
Blessings from
BAPU
