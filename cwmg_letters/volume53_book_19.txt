19. LETTER TO INDRA VIDYALANKAR
January 11, 1933
CHI. INDRA,
I have a letter from some Harijans of your area, I am en-
closing it. Read it and after an inquiry do whatever is possible.
I have written to them to see you.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
Enclosure: Letter from the Harijans
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7204. Also C.W. 4862. Courtesy:
Chandragupta Vidyalankar
