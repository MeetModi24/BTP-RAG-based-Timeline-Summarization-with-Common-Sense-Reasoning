45. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
HYDARI MANSION,
August 13, 1947
CHI. KANAIYO,
If you read the newspapers you would have known that I
have stayed on in this place, and am now in a Muslim's house
in a Muslim locality. It is a palatial building. We are all under
Rama's care and we have to live as He wills. I shall not know
till the last moment when I shall leave this place. I write all
this just because I am pleased with your letter. I have sent the
relevant portion from it for publication in Harijan. Abha is fine.
However, I will not say that she is completely all right. Amritlal
is here.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
