640. LETTER TO VAJUBHAI SHUKLA
October 15, 1945
BHAI VAJUBHAI,
Just now I heard about your wife's demise. What can I
say by way of consolation? Everyone born has to die. Some die
sooner, some later.
Blessings from
BAPU
VAJUBHAI SHUKLA
RAJKOT
