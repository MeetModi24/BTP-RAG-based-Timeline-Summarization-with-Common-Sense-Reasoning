327. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKER PATTANI
Magsar Sud 6 [December 2, 1924]
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I write this letter on the train going to the Punjab. I got
your letter. I shall clarify the Congress policy regarding the Native
States. I hope to have some peace under your shelter after the
Kathiawar Conference.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 3182. Courtesy: Mahesh Pattani
