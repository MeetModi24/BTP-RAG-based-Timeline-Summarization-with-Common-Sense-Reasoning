421. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
January 10, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
The accompanying article won't do. Your reply is no reply.
Is not the article in Hindi ? Your reply also should be in Hindi.
The manner of replying should be different, too. There is no
hurry about it. We will think it over when we meet. We should
also know who has sent you the article. Show it to Kaka. I
think the writer is a friend of his.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
An article is enclosed. Send it on if you like it. Otherwise
bring it back and discuss it with me.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11586
