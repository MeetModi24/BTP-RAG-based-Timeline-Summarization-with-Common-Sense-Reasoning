79. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
April 21, 1942
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have your letter. It is heart-rending. I am publishing
Satis Babu's letter in Harijan. Send your scheme about goseva.
The meeting will be on the 29th. Come if you want to.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1715
