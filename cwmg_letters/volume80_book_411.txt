411. LETTER TO R. K. NANDKEOLYAR
PANCHGANI,
June 2, 1945
BHAI NANDKEOLYAR,
I have your letter. In my opinion, you should clearly write to
the United Provinces Government that you cannot comply with
their order. You have not committed any crime. You have faith
only in ahimsa. You do not want to enter the State unneces-
sarily but if it becomes necessary, you will enter it without giving
any notice. Write to this effect and be quiet. Go to U. P.
when you find it necessary. When you reply to them, send a
copy to the Press also.
Blessings to you both from
BAPU
SHRI RAMKISHORE NANDKEOLYAR
NANDA VILAS, GAYA (BIHAR)
