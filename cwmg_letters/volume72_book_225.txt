225. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 12, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
After many days there is a post for you. Here it is. Lila-
vati has passed her examination. She is delirious with joy. Pro-
bably she goes to Bombay today to arrange for her college course.
Valjibhai's son Manu comes first and has gained prizes. He is a
wonderful boy. The heat is melting.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3973. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7282
