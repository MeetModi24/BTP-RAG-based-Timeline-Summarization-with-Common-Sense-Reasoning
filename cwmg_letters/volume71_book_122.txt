122. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
January 10, 1940
BA,
I have your letter. Prabha has given in her letter all the news.
Tell Devdas that I don't at all like his falling ill. He doesn't take
care to put in enough exercise. If he did that and kept regular
hours for meals he would not fall ill. You may stay there as long as
necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXIV
