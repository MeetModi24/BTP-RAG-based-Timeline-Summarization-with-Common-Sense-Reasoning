399. LETTER TO GOPINATH
September 25, 1936
BHAI GOPINATHJI1,
. . .2
Now tell me if there is in the Ayurvedic system
any medicine for malaria as efficacious as quinine. Which medi-
cine is definitely more effective than the allopathic drugs? In
the villages, in spite of my strong inclination to the contrary, I
have to take recourse to quinine, soda-bicarb, potassium per-
manganate and tincture iodine. . . .3
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Gujarati, 3-1-1937
