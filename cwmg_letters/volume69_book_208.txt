208. LETTER TO VIJAYA GANDHI
ANANDKUNJ, RAJKOT,
April 24, 1939
CHI. VIJAYA1,
Which birthday of yours will it be? English girls conceal
their age. Are you also doing something of that kind? Be that
as it may. May you live long and still remain young for ever!
But you must not neglect spinning. You must do more spinning
this year than you did last year.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
