302. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
March 14, 1936
CHI. SHARMA,
I had your letter which prompted me to send for Draupadi
and here is her reply. But I have repeated my invitation.
You are gaining good experience. It will be nice if you have
nothing further to learn from any institution after you come here.
You may remain there as long as your knowledge remains incom-
