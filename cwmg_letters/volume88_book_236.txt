236. LETTER TO VASANTLAL
NEW DELHI,
July 2, 1947
BHAI VASANTLAL,
I have your letter. I am rather surprised that you have to
recommend Sheth Husainbhai to me. And who am I to write to
Sardar Vallabhbhai? Perhaps he knows Husainbhai better than I.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
