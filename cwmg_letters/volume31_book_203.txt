203. LETTER TO MAGANLAL SUNDERJI
THE ASHRAM,
July 24, 1926
BHAISHRI MAGANLAL SUNDERJI,
I got your letter. I think that in any temple which we may
visit, we should perform worship which is in harmony with the
atmosphere of the place. It seems to me improper, therefore, to
see Mahadev in the image of Krishna in a temple dedicated to
His worship.
MOHANDAS GANDHI
C/O SHRI DHIRAJLAL LAKSHMICHAND CHOKSI
MANDAVI CHOWK
RAJKOT
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19936
