416. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
NEW DELHI,
January 19, 1948
CHI. MIRA,
Your note. All anxiety is over. I hope you got my letter
dictated during the fast. I was working top speed. Your note
for Harijan will receive attention.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABEHN
P. O. RISHIKESH
DIST. DEHRA DUN
From the original: C. W. 6533. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9928
