471. LETTER TO MUNNALAL
YERAVDA MANDIR
June 30, 1930
BHAI MUNNALAL,
I am both surprised and pained to know that you have fled.
As long as we live in the body, how can we run away from work?
Even if it is right for some persons to refrain from physical acti-
vity altogether, that cannot be so for you or me. One who has
attained perfection can perhaps become free from the duty of
working. I have already drawn your attention to your unsettled
state of mind. Wake up and start working.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 6648. Courtesy: Munnalal; also
G.N. 8648
