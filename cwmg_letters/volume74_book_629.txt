629. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 5, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Yours. Whatever happens, you belong to Sevagram. And
you will serve whether you are bodily in Sevagram or elsewhere.
Therefore no worry please. You must shed the nervousness too
about being ill in Sevagram. I have decided not to worry or
to worry as little as I do, say, about Ba or, say, Ramsarandas.
All well here.
The weather began to be chilly from yesterday.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4091. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7400
