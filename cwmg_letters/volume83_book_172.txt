172. LETTER TO MAHARAJA OF REWA
DINSHAW MEHTA CLINIC, POONA,
February 20, 1946
MAHARAJA SAHEB3,
Yesterday you presented me a cheque for Rs. 1,001. I con-
sidered whether or not I should accept it. My heart says that I
should not; I am, therefore, returning it. Please excuse me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
MAHARAJA
REWA
