413. LETTER TO N. S. VARADACHARI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 23, 1926
MY DEAR VARADACHARI,
I had your letter. I enclose herewith a copy of a letter written
to Ganesan. You may apply your mind separately to the proposal.
Of course the proposal is useless if you cannot be in Madras. I have
made the proposal so as to enable you to be nearest to your
centre of activity. I do not want to drag you all the way here
if it is at all possible.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
SJT. N. S. VARADACHARI
ERODE
From a microfilm: S.N. 19489
