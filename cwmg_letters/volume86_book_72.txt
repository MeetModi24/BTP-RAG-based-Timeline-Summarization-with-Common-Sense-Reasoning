72. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
October 28, 1946
CHI. BRAJKRISHNA,
I think it useless for you to come to Bengal at present.
Your health is not such that it can bear the strain. Otherwise
also, there is work to be done here. I do not know what the
conditions there will be like.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 2495
