213. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
June 9, 1935
BHAI MUNSHI,
In Jamiatram Kaka you have lost a source of great strength.
I had a letter from Amritlal regarding Sikar. I can't decide even
about the propriety or otherwise of a committee without under-
standing the position better. I am afraid we shall not be able to
get any good out of it. If my fear is unwarranted, I can decide that
only after I understand the position more fully.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati: C. W. 7575. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
1 In the former Jaipur State, where a satyagraha had been launched
against excesses of the State Government
