106. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 11, 1926
MY DEAR C. R.,
No news from you since your last telegram. For fear you may
not come, I send you the enclosed letter from Kelappan. What
do you advise? If you think that this help should be given,
please give out of the Travancore funds left with you.
When are you sending your next promised instalment? Do
not wait till the readers have forgotten all about the first.
Yours,
Encl. 2
SJT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARIAR
GANDHI ASHRAM
THIRUCHENGODU
From a microfilm: S.N. 19351
