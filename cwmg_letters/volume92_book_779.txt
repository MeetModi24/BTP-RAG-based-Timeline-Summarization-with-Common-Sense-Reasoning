779. LETTER TO AMBUJAMMAL
June 23, 1934
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your two letters. I get very little time for writing
letters. Hence I am not able to answer your letters promptly.
I hope all after-effects of the fever have disappeared now. I
think I have already acknowledged receipt of the Ramayana. You
should stop worrying. Become an expert in Hindi.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
What is the difference between Amma and Ammal? In
Hindi we say Parvatibai, or devi or behn. What is the usage
in Tamil?
