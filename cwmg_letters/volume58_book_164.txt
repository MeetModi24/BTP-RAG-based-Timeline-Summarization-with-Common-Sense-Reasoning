164. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
AJMER,
July 6, 1934
CHI. MAHADEV,
I rose at 2.30 today. After writing the diary, I am writing
this. I am fairly exhausted now. I don't like the discussions
and the meetings. I feel the lack of sleep all the time. The corre-
spondence has piled up.
I attend to as many letters as I can.
Durga and the other women came and saw me at Bhav-
nagar. She and Velanbehn are waiting for you. It will be better,
therefore, to go and see them before joining me. Bablo is at
Dihan. I can't think what should be done about all of them.
But come to a decision only after meeting me.
As we shall be meeting in a very few days now, I don't write
more. What Chandrashankar has written to you is enough.
Take care of your health after you leave.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
