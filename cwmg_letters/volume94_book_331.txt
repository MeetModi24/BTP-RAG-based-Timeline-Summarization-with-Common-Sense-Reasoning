331. LETTER TO JAGJIVAN RAM
SEVAGRAM,
January 28, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of 22nd inst.
Gandhiji says you can come and see him at your convenience. But you should
know that he maintains silence during daytime these days. You will please send
intimation of your arrival in advance.
Regarding Gandhiji's correspondence with Shri Sivashanmugam Pillai of Madras,
it will be necessary to obtain his permission first as his letters were marked personal.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI JAGJIVAN RAM
KADAM KUAN
PATNA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
