387. LETTER TO RAMDEV
PANCHGANI,
May 31, 1945
BHAI RAMDEV,
I have received Pandit Vinayak Rao's reply and other papers.
I am going through them carefully. I am doing what I can.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RAMDEV
C/O PANDIT VINAYAK RAO, BARRISTER
JAMBAG
HYDERABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
