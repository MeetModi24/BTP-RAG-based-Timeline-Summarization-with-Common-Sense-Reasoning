43. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
[October 18, 1941]
MY DEAR IDIOT,
One letter was entrusted to P[rithvi]chand for posting.
Your note to hand. You should pass a day in Jullundur when
you descend and do what you can. The nights are getting colder
night after night, not so the days. The cottage is nearing com-
pletion. No more now. Munshi and others have come.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4101. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7510
