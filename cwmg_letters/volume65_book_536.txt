536. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
July 31, 1937
CHI. NARAHARI,
I got your letter. I would like you to examine Venilal
Buch's demand on Navajivan Karyalaya and settle it. I think
for the sake of propriety you should get the consent of the
trustees.
The best thing, therefore, would perhaps be that you
yourself should call on Jivanji and get his consent, so that I
might be relieved of that burden.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9107
