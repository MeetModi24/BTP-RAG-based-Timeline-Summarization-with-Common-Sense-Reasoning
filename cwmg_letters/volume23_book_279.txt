279. LETTER TO GANGADHARRAO DESHPANDE
POST ANDHERI,
April 5, 1924
MY DEAR GANGADHARRAO,
I have your letter of the 31st March. I have seen it only today.
As I do not know the latest development, I am not writing anything
in Young India, but I am sending a letter to Sadashivrao Herewith
a copy of that letter.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Encl.
SJT. GANGADHARRAO B. DESHPANDE
BELGAUM
From a photostat: S.N. 8670
