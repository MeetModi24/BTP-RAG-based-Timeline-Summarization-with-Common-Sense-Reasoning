365. LETTER TO R. S. IYER
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 17, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter as also the yarn packet. The manager of the
Weaving Department tells me that it will take about one month
before the yarn can be woven. This department always remains
crowded and orders received from outside have to take their turn.
The charge of weaving cloth of 50" width is annas 6½ per yard.
After receipt of your reply the order will be put in turn.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. S. IYER
clo "THE TIMES OF INDIA"
BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19471
