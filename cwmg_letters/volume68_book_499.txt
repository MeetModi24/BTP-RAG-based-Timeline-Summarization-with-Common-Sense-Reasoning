499. LETTER TO BALKRISHNA SHARMA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 20, 1939
BHAI BALKRISHNA SHARMA,
What have they been doing in Kanpur? Why all this riot-
ing? I Did not another Ganesh Shankar Vidyarthi offer himself
as a sacrifice?
Blessings from
BAPU
