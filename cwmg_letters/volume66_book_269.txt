269. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
October 10, 1937
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
Your letter. All are well. Stay on there till the matter is
resolved. Today Vijaya is leaving for home. What can be
done? Lilavati has arrived and badly needs rest. Dahyalal
has been given charge of the kitchen. Start taking the medicine.
Thousands of blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 393
