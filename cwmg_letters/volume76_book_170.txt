170. LETTER TO PRATAPRAI M. MODI
May 24, 1942
BHAI PRATAPRAI,
I have received your book. I would like to read it, but I
cannot spare a single moment to read such books. You will
please, therefore, excuse me.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
PROF. PRATAPRAI MODI
'PARIMAL'
TAKHTESHWAR PLOT
BHAVNAGAR (KATHIAWAR)
From Gujarati: C.W. 1637. Courtesy: Prataprai M. Modi
