341. LETTER TO GOPALASWAMI AYYANGAR
SEVAGRAM,
March 5, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to hear from you. I entirely agree with you regarding
the States. As to foreign rule, I could not open the subject with Q.A.
Jinnah. He has excluded the States from his calculation. You have not
expected me to give any thought to the rest of your scheme. For my
opinion is that when the whole of foreign rule goes from the whole
of India, then only we shall be free to think with a true sense of
responsibility.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. GOPALASWAMI AYYANGAR
WINDSOR PLACE
NEW DELHI

Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
