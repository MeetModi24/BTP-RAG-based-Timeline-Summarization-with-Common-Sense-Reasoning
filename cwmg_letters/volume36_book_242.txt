242. LETTER TO ALICE SCHALEK
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 7, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter with your notes of the interview. The notes
required considerable revision. I therefore send you a clean copy.
Yours sincerely,
MISS ALICE SCHALEK
AUSTRIAN JOURNALIST
NERON'S HOTEL
LAHORE
From a photostat: S.N. 14284
