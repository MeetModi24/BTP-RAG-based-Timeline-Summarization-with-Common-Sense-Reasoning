77. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
CALCUTTA,
August 19, 1947
CHI. SUSHILA,
I got your letter and the wire. When can a message be sent
to Manilal? After the 15th? That is over.
What do you mean by saying that Sita has been betrothed?
According to me, the betrothal took place the day they stole each
other's hearts. Does betrothal mean that they have now taken a
step further and can take more liberties with each other? But you
are now leaving. What is bound to happen will happen.
You must have received my long letter.
I am writing this letter under very great pressure for time.
I understand about Sita's college. Some inconvenience must
be endured, or boys and girls become secretive and think that
they are progressing.
Show this letter to Manilal also.
Blessings from
BAPU
