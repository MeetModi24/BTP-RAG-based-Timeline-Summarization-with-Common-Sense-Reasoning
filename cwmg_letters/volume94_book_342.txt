342. LETTER TO GOSIBEHN CAPTAIN
SEVAGRAM,
March 7, 1945
DEAR SISTER1,
Khurshedbehn is there. She has got disgusted with my contacts
with women. And I hear so is N.behn. Is not the same true of you
other two ? If so, why not discuss the whole tiling with me ? There
is quite a storm here. I am contemplating a statement. I do not know
whether it will see the light of day.
Do write me fearlessly. I have no repentance of what I have done.
Hope you are well.
Love.
BAPU
GOSIBEHN CAPTAIN
ANDHERI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
