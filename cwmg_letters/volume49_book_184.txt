184. LETTER TO MAHAVIR GIRI
February 27, 1932
CHI. MAHAVIR,
I got your letter. I was glad to know that you kept good
health there. Since you left the Ashram to go to Darjeeling, it
would have been better if you had proceeded directly there.
The place is certainly cold, but this is considered the best season.
Even persons who are ill go there in this season. Do you meet the
Gujaratis who live in that place?
Be very careful in spending money and keep accounts. Write
to me regularly. See that you and your sisters do not forget your
studies.
Blessings from
BAPU
