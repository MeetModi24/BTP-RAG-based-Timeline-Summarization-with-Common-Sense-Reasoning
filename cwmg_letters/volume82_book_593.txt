593. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SODEPUR,
[On or before] January [19] 1946
CHI. GHANSHYAMDAS,
The enclosed letter has been received from Kanya Gurukul.
I think I had better send it to you. I do not know what exactly
is to be done.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original; C.W. 8076. Courtesy; G. D. Birla
