421. LETTER TO GULABCHAND JAIN
July 4, 1937
BHAI GULABCHAND,
I have your letter as well as a copy of the correspondence.
I have no idea what I can do in this matter. We should be
content with whatever can be accomplished by a pure agitation
conducted locally.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7743
