144. LETTER TO MADHAVDAS AND KRISHNA KAPADIA
WARDHA,
October 22, 1933
CHI. MADHAVDAS AND KRISHNA,
I got your letter. My blessings to you both. May you be
happy and have peace of mind during this year.
The tour will commence on the 8th.
Ba knows your address, of course.
Ramdas is fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXII
