78. LETTER TO MAITRI GIRI
March 12, 1933
CHI. MAITRI 1 ,
I have your letter. Have you tried a purgative for constipa-
tion? If constipation persists even after taking a purgative, then
try to live on milk and green vegetables cooked without oil. Milk
should be taken unboiled.
Why has Durga stopped writing to me? Mahavir also has
not written recently.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6238
