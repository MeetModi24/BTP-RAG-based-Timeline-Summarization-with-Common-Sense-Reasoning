386. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
January 28, 1941
DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. I wrote three letters to you. You might
have got my letter of yesterday. What will you do by coming
here when you are ill? And now you are meeting Qaid-e-Azam
and others. Let us see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 467
