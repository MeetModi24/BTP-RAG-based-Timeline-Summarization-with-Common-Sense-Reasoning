58. LETTER TO MISS A. PYWELL
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
October 31, 1906
DEAR MISS PYWELL,
I have your note of the 26th instant. I should be very sorry to have
to leave England without having made your acquaintance. Will you
kindly tell me whether I am likely to find you any day it may be
possible for me to take a run to Leicester. It is just possible that I
may be able to put in a day without disturbing my work.
Yours truly,
MISS ADA PYWELL
35, MELBOURNE STREET
LEICESTER
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4439
