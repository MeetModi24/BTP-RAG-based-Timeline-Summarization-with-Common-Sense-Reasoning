200. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
December 1, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Yes, there has been some misunderstanding. I had told Arya-
nayakum clearly; some books are to be kept and for them a
couple of almirahs, too. I have. . .5 to Aryanayakum. His
language is such; pay no heed to it.
BAPU
