364. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
Magsar Vad 6 [December 17, 1924]
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
I have both your letters. I feel like writing, but what can I
write without all the necessary information? I do not like to
write without the support of solid facts. Now you may come to
me after I return from Belgaum and explain the thing to me.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
BHAI DAHYABHAI MANORDAS PATEL
TALUK SAMITI
DHOLKA
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2691. Courtesy: D. M. Patel
