74. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
September 15, 1932
DEAR MAJOR BHANDARI,
Thanks for promptly sending the two important telegrams.
If you have authority to pass replies I would like you to send ac-
companying replies by express message. You will see in my reply
to Rajagopalachari that I have said that he could come when-
ever he chooses. This I have done in the belief that the fact of
your sending me a telegram asking for interview presupposes
authority for you to grant it. In case you want to see the ori-
ginal telegrams I enclose them herewith and you will please
return them to me tomorrow morning.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 5127
