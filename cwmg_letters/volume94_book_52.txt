52. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
August 30, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. Pyarelal has arrived. I gave him your letter to
read. He seems to be in a cheerful mood. We have not talked. Most
probably he will come there and also go to Delhi.
Annapuma's fever is not going. Manu is giving her the medicine
that was given to Rajkumari. Prabhakar is well. I had thought your
suggestion about the diet was to be continued only up to the 13th, when
you would be arriving. I shall carry on with what I am taking now
and after your arrival on the 13th, shall make whatever changes you
suggest. So far, 2 oz. of butter is having good effect. I am gradually
increasing the milk quota. The bowel movement has considerably
improved. The enclosed cartoons have been brought by Pyarelal. You
will like them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
