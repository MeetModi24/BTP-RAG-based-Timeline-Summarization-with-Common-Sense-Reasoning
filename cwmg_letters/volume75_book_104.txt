104. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
November 2, 1941
CHI. KANTI 2 ,
I was surprised to read your letter to the grandmother. How
small-minded you are! Not a trace of generosity. And no limit to
suspiciousness? Who writes to Saraswati ? I inquire and learn
1 Secretary, Baquerganj District Storm and Flood Relief Committee
2 Son of Harilal Gandhi
3 Addressee's wife
whatever I can regarding you. Nor is it that I do not reply to
your letters. And, apart from pointing out your errors to you, what
else need I do? What Ba wrote was based on experience. But let
it be. I have written even this merely to explain your error to
you. Kishorelalbhai also wrote his letter at my instance. I myself
wished to write, but afterwards, to save my time, I asked him to
do so. Even behind my not writing to you there is no other motive
but to save my time, though I do not remember any occasion
when I did not reply to a letter from you.
Blessings from
BAPU

Courtesy: Kantilal
Gandhi
