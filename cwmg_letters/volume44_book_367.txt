367. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 27, [1930]
I got your letter. I felt very happy to read it. Dilip must
be looking big now. I have never seen him after I met him in
Deolali. If you go and mix with friends from time to time, your
mind will remain peaceful. Pyarelal is in this prison, but he is
not kept with me. I do see him occasionally, however. If you
have cultivated no love for reading, I would advise you to do so.
There are many books published by the Navajivan Karyalaya
which are worth reading and which you can easily understand.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 102
