242. LETTER TO KUMBHAKONAM LAWYERS
[NADIAD,]
April 19, 1918
I was delighted to receive your letter signed by so many of you.
I shall send you a teacher as fast as I can. I am trying to secure
the services of a volunteer who would teach Hindi for the love of it.
The success of this great national effort depends almost entirely
upon the action of the Presidency of Madras. But I have great
faith in the Tamil brethren rising to the occasion. There will be
no limit to our power for serving the land as soon as we make
