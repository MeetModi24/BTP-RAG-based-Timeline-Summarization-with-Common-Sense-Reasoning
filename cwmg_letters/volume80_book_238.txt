238. LETTER TO K. NATARAJAN
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 15, 1945
DEAR MR. NATARAJAN,
It was good of you to have written to me on my remarks
about the industrialists and to have sent me a copy of the Indian
Social Reformer containing your note on them.
How I should love to write to you in Hindustani of which
your long residence in Bombay should have made you a scholar.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI K. NATARAJAN
KAMAKSHI HOUSE
BANDRA, BOMBAY 20
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
