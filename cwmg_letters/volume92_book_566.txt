566. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 28, 1932
CHI. NANIBEHN,
I have been awaiting your letter every week. But it seems
you have come out with a vow not to write. Write to me in
detail and soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari-Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 26
