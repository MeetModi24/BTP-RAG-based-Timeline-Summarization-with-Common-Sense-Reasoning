522. LETTER TO JETHALAL G. SAMPAT
September 17, 1938
CHI. JETHALAL,
I got your postcard, etc. I got Lakshmidas's letter today,
which I am sending. I have been collecting literature which can
be helpful to you and sending it. You may leave out of it what-
ever is not useful. It is not necessary to reply to me. Where is
the need for replying to what I send to you for you to think
over?
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9867. Courtesy: Narayan J. Sampat
