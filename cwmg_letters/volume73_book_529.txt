529. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAZAJ
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 21, 1941
CHI. RUKMINI,
Your letter of February 24, 1941, is before me. Only today I
could get time to write a couple of lines. I hope you have re-
gained plenty of strength. The marriage must have passed off with-
out any hitch. Today Ba has gone all by herself [to Delhi] to be
treated by Dr. Sushila. She has piles.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RUKMINI DEVI
C/O SHRI BENARSIDAS BAZAJ
THATHERI BAZAAR, BENARES CITY
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 10128. Courtesy: Benarsilal Bazaj
