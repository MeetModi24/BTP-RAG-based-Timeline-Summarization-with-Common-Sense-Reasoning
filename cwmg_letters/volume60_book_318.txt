318. LETTER TO BUDHABHAI AND JUTHABHAI
WARDHA,
February 19, 1935
BHAI BUDHABHAI AND JUTHABHAI,
You have given me good news. Send a sample of the salt you
have obtained to Satisbabu at 15, College Square, Calcutta. Give
him my name and also the details of the process by which you
got the salt. Send him, and me as well, the samples of the soap
you have made. We shall proceed further on receiving Satis-
babu's report.
Many inventions like yours remained unutilized because of
our foolishness. No one will have to starve if we know how to
put to proper use the wealth lavished by God on this country.
Go on doing whatever you are able to do.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4589
