70. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
SABARMATI ASHRAM,
Saturday, Jeth Vad 1, 1982, June 26, 1926
CHI. MOTI,
At last, after many days and that too on reminding you, I do
have a letter from you. Will this laziness ever go? Anandi had
fever and it has now subsided. Velabehn also had fever for the last
few days. She is better today. Radha and Kusum are still in bed.
Have you maintained the habit of reading or have you now
given it up owing to laziness? Can I ask about the spinning?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12133
