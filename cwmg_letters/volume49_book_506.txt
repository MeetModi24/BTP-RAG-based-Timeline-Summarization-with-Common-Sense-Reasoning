506. LETTER TO MAHENDRA V. DESAI
May 8, 1932
CHI. MANU,
I have already described my experiences in England which
were worth describing. After all, can I describe things as well as
you do? If, however, you improve your handwriting, I will think
about some other experiences in England and write about them.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7429. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
