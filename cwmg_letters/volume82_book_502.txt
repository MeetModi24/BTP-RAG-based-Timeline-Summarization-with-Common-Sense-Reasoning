502. LETTER TO SAROJ BASINI GOHO
SODEPUR,
January 7, 194[6]
DEAR SISTER,
I have gone through the papers you gave me and have
started work in that connection. Please do not worry.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRIMATI SAROJ BASINI GOHO
6/ KANTAPUKUR LANE
BAGH BAZAR P. O.
CALCUTTA
