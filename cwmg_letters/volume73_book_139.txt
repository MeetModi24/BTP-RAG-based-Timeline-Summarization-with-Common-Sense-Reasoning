139. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 2, 1940
CHI. MIRA,
I have your good letter. I am preserving it, for some time
at any rate. You must keep your body in good order.
The
things you want are being got ready and will be sent soon, in-
cluding your khadi.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABEHN
PALAMPUR
KANGRA DISTRICT, PUNJAB
From the original: C.W. 6463. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10058
