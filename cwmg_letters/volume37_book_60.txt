60. LETTER TO DR. JOSIAH OLDFIELD
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 13, 1928
MY DEAR FRIEND,
What a joy to receive your letter after so many years.
You must have seen in the Press that I could not make up
my mind to go to Europe this year. I have a mind to do so
next year. If I do I have no doubt that we shall meet somewhere.
But whether I shall be able to come to your Hospital as a friendly
visitor or as a patient to receive medical and nursing care I do
not know.
Yours sincerely,
DR. JOSIAH OLDFIELD
LADY MARGARET HOSPITAL, DODDINGTON, KENT
From a photostat: S.N. 14352
