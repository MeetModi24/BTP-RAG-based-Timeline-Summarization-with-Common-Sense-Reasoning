262. LETTER TO SURESH SINGH
July 17, 1941
BHAI SURESH 2 ,
You have asked a proper question. I do not feel like say-
ing anything.
In such matters I have always given the first
place to Jawaharlal's views and he is not here. His policy has
been the policy of the Congress. Since he is in prison my mind
does not work at all.
Russia is not a country like Spain or
China. The English are even now giving [them] help. I find
a great difference between Lenin and Stalin. Lenin's Russia
is no more. But this is going into the merits and shortcomings.
I may be wrong in my observation. As long as I am not able
to do something with all my heart, it is my nature to keep
silent. I hope your ulcer is cured by now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8692
