418. LETTER TO MANU MASHRUWALA
NEW DELHI,
January 19, 1948
CHI. MANU1,
I had your letter. Now there is no more reason for worry.
Ramdas was so worried that he came running here yesterday,
but by that time the fast was over. All of you will be well.
Do not worry about me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C. W. 2683. Courtesy: Kanubhai Nanalal Mashruwala
