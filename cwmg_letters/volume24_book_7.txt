7. LETTER TO G. A. NATESAN
POST ANDHERI,
May 8, 1924
DEAR MR. NATESAN 3 ,
Refreshing to see your writing again. Do please come either
on your way or return. Somebody has evidently played a hoax.
I am not coming to Madras for months to come. If and when I
come, I would love to stay with you, if it is at all possible. I am
sorry I did not read your speech nor did I know of your resolu-
tion.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
G. A. NATESAN, ESQ.
"INDIAN REVIEW"
MADRAS
From a photostat: G.N. 2234
