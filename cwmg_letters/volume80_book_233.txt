233. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
May 14, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
What I wrote to you has happened. Sailen Chatterjee is
an employee of an insurance company. He lives on whatever he
earns. I have advised him to go to your clinic. The doctors
say he keeps getting well and falling ill again. He has agreed
to go to you. The matter of expenses has to be considered.
Madhavdas's case is entirely different. In any case, Sailen must
give something. If you run a free hospital, it will get filled up
and no one will be benefited. That can be discussed when we
have the time. I have written to Sailen to go to you and show
my postcard. He lives in Wardha.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. KRISHNAVARMA
NATURE CURE CLINIC
MALAD, via BOMBAY
