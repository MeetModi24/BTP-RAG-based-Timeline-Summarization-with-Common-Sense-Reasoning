126. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
WARDHA,
August 20, 1939
CHI. VIJAYA,
It is very unkind of you not to write to me these days. I
hope you received the letter sent yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7112. Also C.W. 4604. Court-
esy: Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
