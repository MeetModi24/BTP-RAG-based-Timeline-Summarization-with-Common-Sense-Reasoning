540. LETTER TO THE MANAGING COMMITTEE,
SUNFIELD SCHOOL
November 2, 1932
I can well agree with much of what you say about the mate-
rial and the spiritual. Matter without spirit is dead and spirit
without matter cannot move. So long as we think of them and
not it, each needs the help of the other. But I must not wander
into that very beautiful realm.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 197
