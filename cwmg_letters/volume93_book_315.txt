315. LETTER TO PYARELAL
September 5, 1938
CHI. PYARELAL,
Let no one hurt Mother's sentiments. You will not hurt
Sushila of course.
Mahadev's letter about Rajkumari contained her views about
all of you. My reply to your letter is still pending.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
