246. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
September 30, 1932
DEAREST BROTHER AND FRIEND,
Your wire and your letter are my treasure and my food. I
shall not misunderstand you. Under better auspices I anticipate
no difficulty in rendering a full and intelligible account of my
doings in London. But that is a matter of small account. I want
our love to stand the severest ordeal.
I am flourishing.
With deep love,
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 94
