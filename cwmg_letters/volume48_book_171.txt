171. LETTER TO REGINALD REYNOLDS
[October/November, 1931]
MY DEAR REGINALD,
Just a line in reply to your question whilst I am sitting at
the Conference. I favour preference to Lancashire to help a
partner nation in its distress, assuming of course that partnership
was possible. Why should Japan complain that I prefer a part-
ner in distress? If India becomes partner instead of remaining
subject, there is no Empire. You must relate all my acts to
ahimsa. In ahimsa there is no room for immoral expedience.
Of course must meet before I leave.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 4541. Courtesy: Swarthmore College, Philadelphia
