306. LETTER TO INDIRA GANDHI
February 28, 1945
CHI. INDU,
Shall I write to Dr. Katju1? I shall write about the Commit-
tee too. If you can go to Kashmir, it will be good for you and
Rajiv . I am well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 9804. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
