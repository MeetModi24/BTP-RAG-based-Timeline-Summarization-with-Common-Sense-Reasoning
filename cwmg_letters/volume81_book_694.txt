694. LETTER TO RAM MANOHAR LOHIA
October 19, 1945
BHAI RAMMANOHAR1,
It is good that you got the wire regarding Mauritius. I am
glad that I could get your letter under that excuse. Let me
know about your health if you can, else let the jailor do so.
RAM MANOHAR LOHIA
CENTRAL JAIL, AGRA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
