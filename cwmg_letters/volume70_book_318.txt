318. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 19, 1939
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
I read your letter to Mahadev. Will the work you have
done ever be undone? There is no need for newspaper publicity.
What do you intend to do now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M. M. U./III
