304. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
May 7, 1927
MY DEAR BROTHER,
Thank God the tension is over. It is a pleasure to write to
you. You are going under the best of auspices. May your way
be uniformly smooth and may God give you all the strength and
wisdom you will need.
Here is Andrews's latest letter. If you care, I would like
you to cable you will soon join him. Or on receiving a line from
you, I would cable if you like.
The Viceroy's letter is very good indeed.
If you at all find the time, please ascend the hill once more.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
V. S. Srinivasa Sastri Papers (Correspondence No. 477). Courtesy:
National Archives of India
