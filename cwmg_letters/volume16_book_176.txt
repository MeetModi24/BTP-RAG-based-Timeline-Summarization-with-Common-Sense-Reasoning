176. LETTER TO SIR GEORGE BARNES
DELHI,
October 31, 1919
Your kind letter of the 21st instant has been redirected to me
at Delhi.
Though I can't yet take the view you take of the changed
position, I am prepared to consider it the second best and I shall
do what I can to prevent agitation for realizing Mr. Montagu's
original declaration. Could you announce the name of the
Indian member? I have heard it is to be Mr. Shastriar. I ven-
ture to say that no other better choice could be made. Could you
also say when Sir Benjamin Robertson is likely to sail for South
Africa?
India Office: Judicial & Public Records: 6140/19
