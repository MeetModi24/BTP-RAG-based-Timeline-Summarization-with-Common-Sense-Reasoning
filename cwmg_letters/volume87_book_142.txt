142. LETTER TO PUSHPA K. DESAI
PATNA,
March 24, 1947
CHI. PUSHPA,
You feel tempted to go out with Bhansalibhai, but that is
not proper. Your dharma lies in completing the khadi work and
learning to see God in that work alone. You have a long
way to go yet. You must learn to be steady in your mind.
Do not think of going out anywhere.
Blessings from
BAPU
