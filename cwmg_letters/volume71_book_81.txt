81. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 28, 1939
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I have your letter. I shall preserve the Chinese letter.
The deliverance day has received a full-page advertisement in
The T[imes] of I[ndia]. But in truth, it seems to have fallen flat
everywhere.
Have you read Fazlul Haq's indictment ? Should nothing be
said or done about it?
You have not sent me Kumarappa's letters to which you had
raised strong objection. He is here. I asked him and he says he
sent nothing recently. Do please send me what you may have.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1939. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
