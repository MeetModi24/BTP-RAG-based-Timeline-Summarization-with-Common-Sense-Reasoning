186. LETTER TO RAMPRASAD
SEVAGRAM,
August 13, 1945
CHI. RAMPRASAD,
I have not given thought to what you should do in my
absence. You are not to come to Poona. I wish the child to get
well. I understand about Pushpa.
Blessings from
BAPU
C/O SULOCHANA BHATT
145 A, VIGAS SHERI
KALBA DEVI ROAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
