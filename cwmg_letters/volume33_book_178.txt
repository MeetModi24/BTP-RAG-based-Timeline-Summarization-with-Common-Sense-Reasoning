178. LETTER TO G. K. TILAK
IN MOVING TRAIN,
March 21, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter has been following me all this time. As you had
taken considerable pains over your questions, but as I must not
take them in Young India, I send you replies herewith. You may
publish them if you like.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
The Hindustan Times, 30-4-1927
