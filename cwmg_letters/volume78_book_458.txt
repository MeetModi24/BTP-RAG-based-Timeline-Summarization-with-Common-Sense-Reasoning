458. LETTER TO KUSUM M. KOTHARI
SEVAGRAM,
November 22, 1944
CHI. KUSUM,
I got you letter. How do you meet your expenses? Where
are you? You and your sisters should not give up your studies.
Blessings from
BAPU
KUSUM MANILAL KOTHARI
TAKHTESHWAR PLOT
BHAVNAGAR
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
