70. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
January 29, 1932
CHI. JANAKIBEHN,
I could only look at you and didn't get time to speak even
one word to you. Well, I have time enough now to write. You
also should now write to me as long as you are free. In which
prison is Kamalnayan lodged? How is he? Ask Madalasa and
Om to write to me. How do you keep?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
As you know, Sardar is with me. We pass our time quite
happily. We eat and sleep and pace up and down.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2894
