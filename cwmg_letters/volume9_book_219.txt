219. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
August 14, 1909
MY LORD,
I thank you for your letter of the 12th instant. It encourages me to
send you a copy of letter written by Mr. Ritch to Lord Crewe and of
the cablegram attached to it. The cablegram, I am sure, will be painful
reading to Your Lordship, as it is to me.
I am, etc.,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5010
