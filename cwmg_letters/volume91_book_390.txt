390. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Jeth Sud 14, Saturday [June 6, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Efforts have been made to get money from Badrul. But
there has been no success. Something will certainly come from
Naik in the end. A sum of Rs. 4,000 has so far been received
from him. Instructions have been sent that this should be paid
to the Committee there. Shuaib has been entrusted with the
task of collecting money from these two sources. Keep in touch
with him.
You will have regained your health. What is the condition
of your nephew?
We are at Darjeeling. We shall leave Darjeeling on Tuesday.
Blessings from
BAPU
MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
93 BAZAR GATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
