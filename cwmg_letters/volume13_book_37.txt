37. LETTER TO D. B. SHUKLA
Phagan Vad 1 [March 2, 1915]
BHAISHRI,
I have your letter. May I thank you for sending Rs. 1500?
My health is fairly good. There is still pain in my ribs but
Dr. Dev said that there was no pleurisy now.
I am left without shelter through revered Gokhale's death. I
am thinking of leaving here on Thursday and returning to Bolpur.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
REVERED BHAISHRI DALPATRAM BHAVANJI SHUKLA
BARRISTER
CIVIL STATION
RAJKOT
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 2326
