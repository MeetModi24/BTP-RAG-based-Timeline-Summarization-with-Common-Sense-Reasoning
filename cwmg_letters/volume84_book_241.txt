241. LETTER TO SURENDRA P. BHAGWAT
NEW DELHI,
May 23, 1946
BHAI SURENDRA,
I had your letter. I am unable to guide you. Please there-
fore excuse me.
SURENDRA PURUSHOTTAM BHAGWAT

373 SANDHURST ROAD
