147. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 24, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I didn't see any letter from you in the mail this week. You
must have received my letters to Jayaprakash and to you. My
weight, which was going down, has again increased by one pound.
What is your daily programme of work these days? Vallabhbhai
told me that Jayaprakash was not in good health. How is that?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3367
