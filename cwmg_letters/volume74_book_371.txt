371. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
August 12, 1941
MY DEAR KU[MARAPPA],
Your article on industrialization I consider weak. You have
flogged a dead horse. What we have to combat is socialization of
industrialism. They instance the Soviet exploits in proof of their
proposition. You have to show, if you can, by working out figures
that handicrafts are better than power-driven machinery products.
You have almost allowed in the concluding paragraphs the validity
of that claim.
Do not trouble to answer this but you may deal with it in the
next issue.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10155
