408. LETTER TO KANU GANDHI
September 8, 1945
CHI. KANU,
I got a letter from you after many days. I will merely say
that Ramdas must get well soon. You cannot afford to fall ill.
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
SHRI KANU GANDHI
C/O RAMDAS GANDHI
KHALASI LINE
NAGPUR
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 9520. Courtesy: Kanu Gandhi
