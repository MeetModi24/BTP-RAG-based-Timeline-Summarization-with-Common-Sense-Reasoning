135. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
WARDHA (C. P.),
January 15, 1940
DEAR CHARLIE,
If you think it proper tell Gurudev that I have never ceased to
think of his wire and anxiety about Bengal. I feel that Subhas is
behaving like a spoilt child of the family. The only way to make up
with him is to open his eyes. And then his politics show sharp
differences. They seem to be unbridgeable. I am quite clear the
