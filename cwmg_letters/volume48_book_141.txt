141. LETTER TO MIRZA ISMAIL
October 22, 1931
DEAR SIR MIRZA,
It will be a great triumph of yours if you convert Dr. A.
Having suffered like him in S.A.2, Dr. A. always commands my
sympathy in all he says. He needs the gentlest treatment.
Yours sincerely,
M.K.G.
