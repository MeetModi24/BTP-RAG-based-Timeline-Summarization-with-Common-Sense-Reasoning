421. LETTER TO VIDYA A. HINGORANI
December 25, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
I got your letter. It is good you have gone to Father's. I
shall send you out when you are physically fit. I prefer Wardha
but if you would rather go to Bhavnagar, I shall send you there.
Now at least you should improve your health, do some physical
labour, give up worrying and crying. Learn English and Hindi.
Go on writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi: Courtesy: National Archives of India and
Anand T. Hingorani
