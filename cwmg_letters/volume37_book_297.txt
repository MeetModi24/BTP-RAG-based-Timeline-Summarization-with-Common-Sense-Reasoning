297. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DAULATRAM
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
September 9, 1928
MY DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
I have your letter. I have glanced at the speech of which you
have sent me a copy. Personally, I do not mind Devanagari
and Urdu being optionally used. For, ultimately that script will
survive whose users have greater vitality. The fact however is that
I do not trouble myself about these things. I believe in subscribing
to any solution that the leaders may evolve, so long as it does
not commit me to a breach of any fundamental conviction.
Of course I admired your yielding on the Sind question. But
I was curious to know what made you yield, because I knew
that you had very strong conviction about it.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 13519
