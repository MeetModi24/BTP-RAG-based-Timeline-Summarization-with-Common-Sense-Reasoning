328. LETTER TO BALIBEHN M. ADALAJA
AND KUMIBEHN T. MANIAR
March 23, 1936
CHI. BALI AND KUMI,
There is nothing particular to write to you. However, I
am writing to acknowledge the letter you sisters have sent me.
I was indeed very glad that you both saw me at Sabarmati. I
expect the children are quite happy and hope that Kumi is now
quiet and peaceful.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1555. Courtesy: Manubehn
S. Mashruwala
