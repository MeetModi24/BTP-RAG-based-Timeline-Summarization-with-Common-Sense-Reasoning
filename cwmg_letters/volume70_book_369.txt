369. LETTER TO KANCHANBEHN M. SHAH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 30, 1939
CHI. KANCHAN,
I have your letter. You should write your address in every
letter. Munnalal has gone to Ramana Ashram and I have received
a nice letter from him from there. He says that he has sent for you
there. Go if you feel like it. He will return in about a month's
time. Sharda has left and Premabehn has arrived. Lilavati is
here. Vasumati will be coming in a few days. Thus the house
remains full. Take proper care and improve your health. I am
leaving for Delhi tomorrow. I hope to return on Thursday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8289. Also C.W. 7063. Court-
esy: Munnalal G. Shah
