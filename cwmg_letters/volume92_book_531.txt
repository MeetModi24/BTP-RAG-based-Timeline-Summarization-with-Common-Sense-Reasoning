531. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
May 15, 1932
CHI. MANGALA,
Why did your hand shake? Why did your eyes burn? When
the eyes burn, sprinkle hot and cold water. What was it you
had asked me about the rules of the kitchen? I do not remem-
ber. Write again.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11097. Courtesy: Pushpa Naik
