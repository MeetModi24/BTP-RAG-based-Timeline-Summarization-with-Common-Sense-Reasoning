78. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 4, 1931
CHI. RADHAKRISHNA,
Keep on sending me letters. They contain the news I look
forward to. When Janakibehn arrives, tell her to write to me.
They may certainly arrest Vinoba if they wish. Is there any
news of Chhotelal? How is his health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3036
