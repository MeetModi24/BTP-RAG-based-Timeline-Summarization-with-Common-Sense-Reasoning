17. LETTER TO RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
April 24, 1933
DEAR RAMANANDBABU,
I must trouble you once more. You must have seen Dr.
Ambedkar's alternative to the panel system in the Yeravda Pact.
I should esteem your opinion on his suggestion, if not for pub-
lic at least for my private use.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. RAMANAND CHATTERJEE
"[THE] MODERN REVIEW", CHITPUR ROAD
CALCUTTA
From the original: C.W. 9503. Courtesy: Santa Devi. Also S.N. 21046
