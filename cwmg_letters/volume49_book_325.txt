325. LETTER TO KISAN
April 3, 1932
CHI. KISAN,
Do you wish me to address you as Krishnakumari? Your
letter was very much to my liking. It is good that you have
taught exercises to Jamnabehn and others.
I believe you must have recovered by now.
Anyway do write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
