271. LETTER TO YASHODHARA DASAPPA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 21, 1945
CHI. YASHODHARA,
Tell Dasappa that the climate here is in no way inferior
to that of Nandi. We are certainly being looked after.
I hope you are getting on well.
Is Dasappa calm now? If not, he should write to me or
you should. The work of Kasturba Smarak Nidhi is difficult.
Do it and have it done very carefully.
You will of course work for Hindustani [Prachar Sabha].
Let Ramdas stay as long as he likes.
Blessings from
BAPU
