185. LETTER TO DR. B. S. MOONJE
WARDHA,
December 4, 1928
DEAR DR. MOONJE,
I have just received your letter. If you want an early date
and if it is convenient I suggest Thursday next at 4 p.m., i.e., 6th
instant; if that day is not convenient, 1 1 t h Tuesday at 4 p.m.
Yours sincerely,
DR. B. S. MOONJE
NAGPUR
From a photostat: S.N. 13014
