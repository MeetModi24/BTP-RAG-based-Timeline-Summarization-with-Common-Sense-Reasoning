185. LETTER TO DHIRENDRANATH DAS GUPTA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 10, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. The meeting of the A.I.S.A. comes off on
the 26th instant when I presume your application will be before it.
My cancellation of the touring programme has brought the
finances of the Association to a standstill and they are much too
low for the applications in hand. There may be therefore un-
avoidable difficulty in helping you.
I am slowly regaining lost strength.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. DHIRENDRANATH DAs GUPTA
VIDYASHRAM
KULANRA POST (SYLHET)
From a microfilm: S.N. 14089
