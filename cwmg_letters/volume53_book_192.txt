192. LETTER TO N. H. PURANDARE
January 25, 1933
MY DEAR PURANDARE,
I have your postcard. I do not know how to console you and
how to deal with you. I am sorry I find it so difficult to please
you and I must not betray the trust imposed upon me even
in order to please you. Therefore please regard what I have
offered as final.
Yours sincerely,
PROF. N. H. PURANDARE
604 SADASHIV PETH
POONA 2
From a microfilm: S.N. 20098
