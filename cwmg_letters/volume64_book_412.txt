412. LETTER TO SAMPURNANAND
[February 1937]
How is it that the majority of you Socialists keep such bad
health? Narendra Dev is a chronic sufferer from asthma, Meher-
ally is down with heart trouble, Jayaprakash is ill and now you,
who seemed to be the healthiest of the lot, are also confined to
bed. Evidently none of you can look after himself. Come to Wa-
rdha for some time and stay with me. I promise to send you back
fully cured.
Memories and Reflections, p. 97
