17. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
December 20, 1934
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I have your letter. I see from the newspapers that there is
again some trouble. I shall look into the scheme of Hindi Vidya-
pith when I get it.
Correspondence courses have been introduced in the Mahila
Ashram. I wish, if I may, to include your name among the
teachers to be appointed for it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: C.W. 6081. Courtesy: Haribhau Upadhyaya
