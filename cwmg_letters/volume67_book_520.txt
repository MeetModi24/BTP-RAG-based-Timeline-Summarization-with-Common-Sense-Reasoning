520. LETTER TO MANUBEHN S. MASHRUWALA
September 17, 1938
CHI. MANUDI,
Consider yourself fortunate that you have arrived when I am
observing silence. That silence is for the whole country. You
have the benefit of being a witness to it. The silence is not an
expression of diminished love. Love does not care for words
uttered by the tongue.
I will certainly write to you when you go to Bombay. You
should write, too.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 1572. Courtesy: Surendra B. Mashruwala
