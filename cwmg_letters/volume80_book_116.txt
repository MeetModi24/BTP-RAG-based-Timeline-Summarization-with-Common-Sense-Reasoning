116. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 5, 1945
CHI. LILI,
Your letter is both good and bad. It is good, you do not
wish as a point of honour to go anywhere without work. But
it is bad that you are not engrossed in your studies and still
feel hurt that I have not taken you with me. You should know
that I know you better than you do yourself. I also know better
in what lies your good. Therefore, after you have argued the matter
with me, you should quietly and gladly agree to do what I say
and act accordingly. You have to get through at the first attempt.
Blessings from
BAPU
LILAVATI UDESHI
S. G. S. MEDICAL COLLEGE
LADY STUDENTS' HOSTEL
PAREL, BOMBAY
