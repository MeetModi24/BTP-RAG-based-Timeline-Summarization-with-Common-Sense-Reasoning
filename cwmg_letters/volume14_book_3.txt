3. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
MOTIHARI,
CHAMPARAN,
October 10, 1917
CHI. CHHAGANLAL 1 ,
I have your letter. I take it that you will come to Broach.
West keeps on shouting for books. It will be good if you send him
a dictionary and other suitable books from time to time. Also, send
Doctor's Gujarati book for sale there. Let him keep the proceeds.
I have received copies here.
How is it that Prabhudas does not recover?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the original postcard in Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5644.
Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
