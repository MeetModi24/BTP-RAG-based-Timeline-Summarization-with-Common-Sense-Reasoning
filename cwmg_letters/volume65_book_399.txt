399. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
June 27, 1937
CHI. NARANDAS,
You have mentioned some letter. I don't remember it. If
you had asked any question in it, please repeat it. It can be said
that now you have sufficient experience of Kanaiyo. If you have
been able to form any opinion, please let me know. What is his
state of mind? Please write whatever you would like to say about
him so that it may be of help to me.
Write to me in detail about your experience of Kamala, too.
Your letter about khadi was very good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8529. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
