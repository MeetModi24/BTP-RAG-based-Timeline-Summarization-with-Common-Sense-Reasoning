103. LETTER TO ERNEST ESDAILE
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
October 9, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. If Monday, the 19th inst., at
8 o'clock is convenient to you, I would gladly address the members
of the Club.
Yours sincerely,
ERNEST ESDAILE, ESQ.
CONSTITUTIONAL CLUB
NORTHUMBERLAND AVENUE, W.C. 2
From a photostat: S.N. 18045
