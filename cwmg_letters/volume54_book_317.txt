317. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
April 2, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
There is no need to write to you separately since I write to
Purushottam regularly. Cling to Purushottam's treatment till he
admits failure. I am sure, simple remedies will cure your disease.
Patience and faith are absolutely necessary. I do not know yet
what is the matter with Kanu. I have been thinking about
Kusum. You have wisdom and can also write well. Use your
ability and pour out your heart in long letters to me.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 874. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
