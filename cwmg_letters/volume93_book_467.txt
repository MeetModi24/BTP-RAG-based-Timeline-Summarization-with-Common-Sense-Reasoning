467. LETTER TO C. VIJAYARAGHAVACHARIAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 12, 1939
DEAR FRIEND,
Mahadev has shown me your letter to him. Why do you
say I am cold towards you? I would ask you please not to
mistake our differences in viewpoints for coldness. I hope
you are keeping well.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
