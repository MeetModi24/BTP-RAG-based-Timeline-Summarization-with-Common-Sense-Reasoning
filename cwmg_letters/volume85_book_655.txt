655. LETTER TO ISHWARDAS NATWAR
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
BHAI ISHWARDAS,
I have your two letters.
Not a pie should be spent on
your son's wedding.
The [desire to] come to me is ignorant attachment. Be-
sides, there is nothing certain about me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ISHWARDAS NATWAR
MOHAN ASHRAM
HARDWAR, U. P.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
