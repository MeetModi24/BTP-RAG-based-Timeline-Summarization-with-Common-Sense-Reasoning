32. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
NEW DELHI,
April 19, 1946
CHI. A. S.,
I have your letter. My order is that you will do as you
like. I am not Almighty. So you can go wherever you want.
Zohra is [like] your daughter. You can take her wherever you
wish. Badshah Khan is here. For the present he will stay here.
Till now I have not been able to talk to him. I shall be glad
if you keep good health. I find myself very busy. I shall be
here up to the 25th.
Blessings to Zohra.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 511
