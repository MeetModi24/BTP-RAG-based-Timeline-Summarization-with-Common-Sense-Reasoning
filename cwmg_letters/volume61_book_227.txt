227. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
June 13, 1935
MY DEAR MALKANI,
I have already written to you about Bapa.
Now I have your letter about the Pani Fund. I shall write
again. I have seen also Jugalkishore Birla's objection. Was the
first use of his name without sanction? If it was with his sanc-
tion, then the second use is inoffensive. But if the first use was
without sanction, I do not know what answer you would give.
I had written to him. He said he might pay later on.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 917
