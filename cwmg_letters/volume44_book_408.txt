408. LETTER TO RAMCHANDRA KHARE
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 9, 1930
CHI. RAMBHAU,
I have your nice letter. Your handwriting too is good. It is
all right that you draw lines. I do not remember having not
replied to any of your letters.
It is good that with the advance of winter you are also gaining
weight. If you take enough exercise you will beat the cold instead
of the cold beating you.
Do you write to Lakshmibehn from time to time? It is good
that you are learning bhajans by heart. You have to preserve and
enhance the prestige of Panditji's position. That will happen
only when you outshine him. Panditji never had the opportunity
that you have at your age.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 286. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn Khare
