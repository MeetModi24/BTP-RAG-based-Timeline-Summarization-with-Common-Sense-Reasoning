257. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
July 24, 1932
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I hope all of you reached the Ashram in comfort. Qureshi
should take care and see that his health, which has been improving,
should not decline again. I hope you also have paid a visit to
Imam Saheb's grave. Ask Qureshi to write to me about it. If it is
considered desirable to put up a wall round the grave, that may
be done. Where is Kevalram ? How is his health? Ask him to
write to me. Nirmala it seems, has fallen into a long sleep. I
have had no letter from her at all recently.
Give me news about Dhiru and Vimala . If you have seen
them recently, let me know how their health is.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5503
