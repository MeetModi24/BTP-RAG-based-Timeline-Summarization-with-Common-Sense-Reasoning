76. LETTER TO HORACE G. ALEXANDER
ON TOUR,
October 10, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have been keeping your letter in front of me all these days.
As your time for going to the Ashram, Sabarmati, is now nearing
according to your letter, I write this to say how welcome you
would be at the Ashram. But I regret to inform you that I shall
not be there to receive you personally. I am just now touring
in the south in connection with the message of the spinning-wheel
and shall be so doing till the middle of November after which I
shall be going to Orissa. I do not expect to be in the Ashram before
the beginning of January.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
HORACE G. ALEXANDER, ESQ.
C/O J. S. HOYLAND, ESQ.
HOLYROAD
NAGPUR
From a photostat: G.N. 1404
