94. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
December 1925
I wish to do something that will bring you peace. So write
to me unreservedly, as unhesitatingly as a son to his father, a
friend to his friend and a patient to his doctor. Ask me for any-
thing. After all I am bapu, I am a friend and I certainly am
a physician.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 83
