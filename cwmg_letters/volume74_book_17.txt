17. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
April 23, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
If one thinks deeply over it, the examination point of view,
the knowledge point of view and the proficiency point of view,
all are one and the same. A person takes an examination in order
to become an expert, i.e., to acquire perfect knowledge. Kanchan
does become restless at times, but there is no cause for worry.
She is under my constant observation. Do you write harsh
letters to her? Ask her to show me all your letters, so that you may
keep your pen under restraint and it will be easy for me to guide
you.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MUNNALALJI
CHARKHA SANGH, KHADI VASTRALAYA
MOOL, DISTT. CHANDA
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8503. Also C.W. 7135. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
