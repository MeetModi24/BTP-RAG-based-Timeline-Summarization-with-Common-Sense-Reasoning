363. LETTER TO RAMNAVAMI PRASAD
RANCHI,
[About July 8,1917]
DEAR SHRI RAMNAVAMI PRASAD,
My heart cries out at what Babu Brij Kishore tells me about
your physical condition. You ought not to be ill. There is much
work to be done for the country, and what will happen if the
country's soldiers become ill? Keeping this in mind, I hope,
you will try to rid yourself of the ailment. Physicians will not
be able to remove the ailment. The cause of the illness is
self-indulgence, its cure restraint. We are not always able to
spot out self-indulgence and do not always know the kind of
self-control we should exercise. But on a little reflection we
can see both.
Yours
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi original in Gandhiji's hand: G. N. 734
