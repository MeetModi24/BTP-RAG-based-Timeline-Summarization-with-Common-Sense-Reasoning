437. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
ABBOTTABAD,
July 9, 1939
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
I hope you reached the dairy farm today on time. About
your expenses there, I have written to Sardar Saheb that I shall
be responsible for whatever expenses are incurred.
It is hot here too; but no hot breeze. I sit bare-bodied.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1922
