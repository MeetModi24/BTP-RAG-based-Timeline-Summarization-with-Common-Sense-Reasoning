39. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
Personal
DETENTION CAMP,
September 27, 1943
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
On the eve of your departure from India, I would like to
send you a word.
Of all the high functionaries I have had the honour of
knowing, none has been the cause of such deep sorrow to me
as you have been. It has cut me to the quick to have to
think of you as having countenanced untruth, and that regard-
ing one whom, at one time, you considered as your friend. I
hope and pray that God will some day put it into your heart
to realize that you, a representative of a great nation, had been
led into a grievous error.
With good wishes,
I still remain,
Your friend,
M. K. GANDHI
