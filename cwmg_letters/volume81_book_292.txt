292. LETTER TO GHANSHYAM SINGH GUPTA
August 27, 1945
BHAI GHANSHYAM SINGH,
Read this. If it is to be removed in six months, then it
would be better to postpone the satyagraha.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GHANSHYAM SINGH GUPTA
SPEAKER
DRUG
