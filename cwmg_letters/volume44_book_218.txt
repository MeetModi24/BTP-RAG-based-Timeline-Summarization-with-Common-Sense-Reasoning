218. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
September 18, 1930
BHAI RAMESHWARDAS (DHULIA),
Your letter. As you have survived the snake-bite, God will
certainly exact more service from you. What treatment did you
have? Was the snake poisonous? God will give you peace. Be
certain that Ramanama is the kalpadruma for us.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 175
