476. LETTER TO R. KRISHNIER
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
October 31, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have seen your letter to Subbiah. I do not think I can go
through Bombay to Wardha. Bardoli is the most direct and less
expensive route. You may therefore send your collection to me
at the Ashram as I do not know when I am likely to be in
Bombay.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. R. KRISHNIER
82/24 MIRANDA BUILDING, DADAR
From a microfilm: S.N. 13577
