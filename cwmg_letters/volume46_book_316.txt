316. LETTER TO SHRIRAM SHARMA
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 31, 1931
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter. You do not tell me why the Congress
workers were arrested. In any case there is nothing in the Settle-
ment to prevent Government from undertaking prosecutions for
offences, fancied or real. If the offence alleged was not com-
mitted, it is open to the arrested workers to be defended by
pleaders.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. SHRIRAM SHARMA
GENERAL SECRETARY
DISTRICT CONGRESS COMMITTEE
ROHTAK (PUNJAB)
