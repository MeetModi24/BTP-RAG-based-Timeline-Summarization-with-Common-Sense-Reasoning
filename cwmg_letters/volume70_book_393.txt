393. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON,
November 11, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your wire is disturbing. May God keep you. I hope you
will not have to go through a strenuous programme. I shall
expect a wire tomorrow though it is Sunday.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3940. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7249
