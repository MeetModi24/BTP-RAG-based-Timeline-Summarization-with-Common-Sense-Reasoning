102. LETTER TO KRISHNA GOPAL
BORSAD,
June 30, 1931
DEAR LALA KRISHNA GOPAL,
I have your telegram. I have moved in the matter. I do
not know what success will attend my effort. Please say nothing
in public about this. I may deal with the case in Young India
next week unless Pandit Jagat Ram is sooner discharged.
Yours sincerely,
LALA KRISHNA GOPAL
SIALKOT (PUNJAB)
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy; Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
