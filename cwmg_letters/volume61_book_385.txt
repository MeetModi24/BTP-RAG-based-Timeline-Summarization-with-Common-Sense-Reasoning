385. LETTER TO KEVALCHAND K. MEHTA
July 20, 1935
BHAI KEVALCHAND,
I have your letter. I am afraid I understand nothing about
electricity and the immediate solution is not in my hands. So many
such things are being done in our country today against which
there seems to be no help.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 147
