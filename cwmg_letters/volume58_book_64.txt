64. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
June 4, 1934
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
Your letter and the doctor's telegram arrived together.
It
is excellent that the operation was performed. I think you will
have been discharged by the time I reach Bombay. Have no worry
about anything. I am writing to Chandrakanta and Raje. It
is good that both of them are there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 308
