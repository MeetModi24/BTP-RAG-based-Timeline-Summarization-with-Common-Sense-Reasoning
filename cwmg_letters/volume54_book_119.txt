119. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
March 17, 1933
MY DEAR MARGARETE,
Why do you want to come here for three days or at the most
a fortnight? If you have at all imbibed the central truth of the
Gita, it will tell you that this kind of wish has got to be subjugated
and sublimated into pure action, which for you consists in doing
your duty there. You should hold on to your savings, and if you
cannot restrain yourself from spending them somehow, you should
send them here for the Harijan cause.
I do hope you got all my previous letters, as also the Harijan
which is being posted to you every week.
Love from us both.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
