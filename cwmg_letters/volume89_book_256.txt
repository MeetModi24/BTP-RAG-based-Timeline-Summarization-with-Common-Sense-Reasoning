256. LETTER TO SHOORJI VALLABHDAS
NEW DELHI,
September 25, 1947
BHAI SHOORJIBHAI,
There are many Harijans in Sind. They are completely ruined
and have nothing to eat. They should be sent to Kathiawar. So
if your Company sends over one or two steamers to bring them
over free of charge to some port in Kathiawar they will reach
here safely. At the moment it is doubtful whether they will be
able to preserve their religion or the womenfolk their honour.
Bapa will bring this letter to you. If possible, do this im-
mediately. I have written a similar letter to Shantikumar also.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 4098
