379. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Vaishakh Sud 11 [May 4, 1925]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. I think you need a change of air if
only for a short time. There is the problem of Harikishen. But if
you can manage, you must steel your heart and go at least for
a week, even if it is only to Matheran. I am being looked after
very well here. Do not worry at all. Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
93 BAZAR GATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
