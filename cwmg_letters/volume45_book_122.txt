122. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 15, 1931
CHI. RADHAKRISHNA,
Tell Chhotelalji to write to me if he is permitted to write
letters. Has he also been released under [Section] 160? Essential
silk means that which is required for the borders in khadi or with-
out which khadi itself cannot be sold. If the question of principle
is settled, the rest can be done as suits the occasion.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3037
