73. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
September 24, 1934
MISS MIRA,
Your letter is before me. This will probably be my last letter
to you while you are in Europe.
Khan Saheb A. Ghaffar Khan is with me. His daughter is
with his brother's wife there. He wants the girl to come back
and have her training at the Ashram. He would like her to accom-
pany you. Mahadev will send you the address. If you see her,
i.e., if this reaches you in time to see her, you will bring the girl
with you.
The Brothers' friendship seems to me to be a gift from God.
Love.
BAPU
Government of Bombay, Home Department, Political, File No. 129/34.
