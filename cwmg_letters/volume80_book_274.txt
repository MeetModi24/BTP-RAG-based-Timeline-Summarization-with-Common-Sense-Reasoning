274. LETTER TO ABDUL GHANI DAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 21, 1945
BHAI ABDUL GHANI,
If you sent me a wire about the Lahore riots, it is possible
I did not reply to it. I did not want to interfere. My silence did
not mean I was displeased. On the other hand, it was good
that precious lives were laid down for the sake of the country.
Blessings from
BAPU
ABDUL GHANI DAR
LUDHIANA (PUNJAB)
