551. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
April 7, 1935
BHAI MOOLCHAND,
I have your letter.
Concerning the Khudi incident justice
should be demanded from where it is due. Can truthful testimonies
be got? Can any impartial man go there? Many such questions
arise.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 829
