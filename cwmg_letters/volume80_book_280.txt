280. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 22, 1945
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I saw your note at the bottom of the letter from Kailas.
Mridulabehn is still here. A few days back I received your
letter to Bapa and also that of Chimanlal. Both the letters are
good. There is a different version also. I have already received
it. If I have the time, I shall find out more. I have noted
your programme. You have made the right decision. Amba
will like it there and only there will she be able to look after
her health. You will shine wherever you go and you will be
in demand. So your place is wherever there is work. However,
you should think about your family also.
Read the letter I have written to Kailas.
Blessings from
BAPU
