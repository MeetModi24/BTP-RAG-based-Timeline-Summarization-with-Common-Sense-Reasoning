506. LETTER TO WAI GORAKSHA MANDAL
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 2, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Please let me know the number of mem-
bers requiring cotton, the quantity required and the count they
spin. Are they skilled spinners? Can they pass the ordinary tests
about strength and evenness? Are they too poor to buy cot-
ton? Do they know carding? If not, who makes slivers for them?
On receiving your replies to these, I shall be able to decide.
Yours sincerely,
WAI GORAKSHA MANDAL
