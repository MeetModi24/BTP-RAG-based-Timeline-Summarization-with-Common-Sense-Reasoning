506. LETTER TO K. SANTANAM
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 1, 1929
MY DEAR SANTANAM,
I have your letter. You have to be firm and strong if you
want the Punjab to come out well during the Congress. Unless the
Congress is put on a proper footing, you should refuse to give
any help and even make a public statement. As it is I am
sending your letter to Motilalji and asking him to do whatever
is possible.
If I were you, I should not bring in Lalaji Memorial in dis-
cussing the subject. Let the Memorial stand on its own footing.
You should go on with collections irrespective of whether the
Congress is to be held in Lahore or not. Those who want to
revere Lalaji's memory will pay in any case. What I am an-
xious about is that the Congress in the Punjab should be a
genuine register of representatives.
You will see the letter I have published in Young India.
Of
course, you will easily guess that it is from the Punjab. What a
heart-breaking revelation. It is this root evil which I am likely
to deal with.
I am leaving for Sind tomorrow and shall return on the
15th instant. The enclosed is my programme.
How is Krishna doing?
Yours sincerely,
PANDIT K. SANTANAM
LAHORE
