45. LETTER TO REV. CORNELIUS GREENWAY
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
(INDIA)
February 21, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. What I have given you at the top is my
permanent address and any remittance you may send to that
address will be duly received.
I appreciate your prayers and good wishes of all of which
this country stands badly in need.
I do not keep any photograph of mine nor I have given a
sitting for years. All the photographs therefore that you see are
snapshots. I wish therefore you will not want one.
Yours sincerely,
REV. CORNELIUS GREENWAY
409, COHAMVET ST.
TAURTON MASS
From a microfilm: S.N. 14114
