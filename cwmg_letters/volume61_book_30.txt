30. LETTER TO JOHARILAL MITTAL
April 30, 1935
BHAISHRI MITTAL,
Daily I have been waiting for a letter and some drafts from
you. Apart from Rs. 500 from the Gujaratis and Rs. 101 from
Bhadwas I have so far received nothing here.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
GENERAL SECRETARY
RECEPTION COMMITTEE
HINDI SAHITYA SAMMELAN, INDORE
[From Hindi]
Veena, Tributes Issue, April-May 1969
