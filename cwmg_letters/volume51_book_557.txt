557. LETTER TO PARAMANAND DESAI
November [3, 1932]
CHI. PARAMANAND 2 ,
I have your letter. I am glad that Mother and you have gone
there. I very much wish that both of you should now stay perma-
nently in the Ashram. In which form are you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9483
