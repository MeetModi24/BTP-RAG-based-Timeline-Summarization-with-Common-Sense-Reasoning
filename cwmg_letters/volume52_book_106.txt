106. LETTER TO UMADEVI BAJAJ
November 27, 1932
CHI. OM,
You seem to be a very cunning girl. Because you don't
spin fine yarn, you gift away the coarse yarn which you spin
and take credit for voluntary service. Is it Vinoba or Janaki-
maiya who teaches you this?
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 335
