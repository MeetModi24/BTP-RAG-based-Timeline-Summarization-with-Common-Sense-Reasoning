101. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 8, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
There is no letter from you today. Pyarelal's statement has appeared
in the newspapers in these parts. What about there ? Right now the
meeting of the Charkha Sangh is in progress.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
