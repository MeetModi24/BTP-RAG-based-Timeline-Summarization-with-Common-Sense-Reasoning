100. LETTER TO R. L. HANDA
SEGAON,
August 10, 1939
DEAR FRIEND,
I have seen your letter and the accompanying articles on
Kathiawar States. Both are welcome. While your letter to me is
appearing in the next issue of the Harijan, the articles will be publish-
ed at a later date as soon as space is available. Your approach
to the problem of States I consider, on the whole, as rational and
workable.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Leaves from a Diary, p. 77
