675. LETTER TO CHUNILAL V. MEHTA
SIMLA,
July 4, 1945
BHAI CHUNILAL,
You have lost a brother who was a true brother. I had no
idea that Sir Mangaldas was your brother. He was a great help
to me as a member of the vaidyas' advisory committee. What
shall I do now? Whose loss is greater? Yours or mine? Or of
neither?
Blessings from
BAPU
SIR CHUNILAL V. MEHTA
RIDGE ROAD
MALABAR HILL
BOMBAY
