482. LETTER TO KUSUMBEHN DESAI
August 31, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I got your letter. You must have got my letter in which I
have explained which letters I meant. I can see from here your
vacillation. But it is bound to give place one day to steadiness of
mind. I will not give up my faith.
Do you write any time to Khurshedbehn ? All three of us are
doing well. You probably know that Sardar is going ahead fast
with his study of Sanskrit.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1846
