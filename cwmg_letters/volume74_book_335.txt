335. LETTER TO S. N. CHATTERJEE
August 5, 1941
DEAR SAILEN,
I have read the enclosed. Rajkumari's criticism is quite cor-
rect. The mistakes she has collected are unpardonable. If you do
not make a serious endeavour to improve, you will never make any
progress. Your mistakes are purely due to carelessness.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10343. Courtesy: Amrita Lal Chatterjee
