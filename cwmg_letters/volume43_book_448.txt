448. LETTER TO PATRICK QUINN
May 27, 1930
DEAR MR. QUINN 1 ,
Will you please order raisins 2 lb., kishmish 1 lb. and send me
some common writing paper or a scribbling pad?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 240
