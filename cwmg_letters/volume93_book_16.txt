16. LETTER TO TARADEVI
WARDHA,
March 21, 1935
CHI. TARA1,
I have your letter. I do not know the rules of the Seva
Sadan. But if you must go, I do not think there will be any
difficulty. Your going will certainly please Mother. Do go if
you feel so disposed. But your work should not suffer. I am
writing to Mother. How far have you progressed in your
studies?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI TARAMATIBEHN
MAHILA VIDYALAYA
LUCKNOW
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy : Gandhi
National Museum and Library
