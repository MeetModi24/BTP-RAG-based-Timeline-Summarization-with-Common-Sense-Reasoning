55. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
January 2, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. Certainly I am responsible but I had
understood that you had nothing to hide. Do not read the
letters that you get or don't let them affect you. Amtussalaam
has not come here so far, she is probably at Indore. It is a great
thing that Krishna has recovered. What is your diet these days?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a facsimile of the Hindi: Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsha,
between pp. 140 and 141
