36. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
SURAT,
May 5, 1921
BHAISHRI DEVCHANDBHAI 1 ,
I had your telegram. It had become impossible for me to go
there. If you will, you can see that no home in Kathiawad is left
without a spinning-wheel. But can a person ever rise to heaven
except by giving up his life? Do you yourself spin? Do you use
khadi exclusively, at home and outside?
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: G.N. 5728
