133. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
[PHOENIX]
[September 22, 1913]
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
I have your letters. I still expect a wire from you. As we are
issuing on Wednesday, I must not wait to write at length. I only hope
that your expectation will be realized. You do not seem to have received
my letter on Sunday. Anyway I expect a wire from you at noon. More,
therefore, then. No time to write separately to the others just now.
I wrote to Miss Schlesin saying she need not worry about replying
to the papers. As it so happens the Leader's is an inspired article and
reproduced in the Mercury.
With love,
UPPER HOUSE
