377. LETTER TO MOHANLAL M. SHAH
NOAKHALI,
December 31, 1946
BHAI MOHANLAL,
Bulsaria informs me that Vachharaj is no more. The passing
away of a good, silent worker is a painful thing. Who will now
look after his work? What will they do about his Panchgani
estate? Please think over it and do what is necessary. For
my part I am stuck here and I see no possibility of my
getting out.
I hope you are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHETH MOHANLAL
GADHADA
