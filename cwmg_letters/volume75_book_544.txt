544. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
March 30, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
I have not a minute to spare. I shall try to start tomorrow. I
hope Ba, Narendra Dev, Sharda and Abha are well. Tell Krishna-
verma to take proper care of Janakibehn. What happened to the
friend from Morvi who was on a fast?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4261. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N.
