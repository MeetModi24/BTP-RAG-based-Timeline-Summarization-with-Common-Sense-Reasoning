476. LETTER TO KENNETH SAUNDERS
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 25, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is a difficult job for me to promise to
read any book. But if you care to send me your book I shall
only hope to get a few moments to go through it.
I am sending by registered book post two volumes of Young
India and a copy of my autobiography.
Yours sincerely,
KENNETH SAUNDERS, ESQ.
HIGH ASCRIS
BERKELEY, CALIFORNIA
From a photostat: S.N. 15126
