653. LETTER TO JETHALAL G. SAMPAT
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
July 18, 1928
BHAISHREE JETHALAL,
I got your postcard. Largely it is only the English-educated
people who are politically conscious. If they believe in khadi, a
khadi atmosphere will be created in the country and no one will
find any other cloth in India.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9842. Courtesy: Narayan Jethalal
Sampat
