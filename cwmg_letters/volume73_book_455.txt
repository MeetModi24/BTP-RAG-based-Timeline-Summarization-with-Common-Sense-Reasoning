455. LETTER TO T. B. SAPRU
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
February 22, 1941
DEAR SIR TEJ BAHADUR,
I was sorry to learn about your illness. Do please write only
when you are quite well and strong.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR TEJ BAHADUR SAPRU
19 ALBERT ROAD
ALLAHABAD, U.P.
From a photostat: G.N. 7580. Also C.W. 10287. Courtesy: National
Library
