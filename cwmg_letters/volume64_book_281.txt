281. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
January 17, 1937
CHI. MANUDI,
I got your letter. I understand what you tell me. I remem-
ber you often. I very much wanted to take you with me,
but I felt that it would not be right to do so. You must be
getting letters from Nimu. I want to reach there on the 24th.
Let us see what happens. There it is cold; here it is terribly
hot.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 1563. Courtesy: Manubehn S. Mashruwala
