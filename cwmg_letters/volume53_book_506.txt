506. LETTER TO ELLEN HORUP
February 23, 1933
MY DEAR ELLEN,
I was delighted to hear from you. I appreciate all your
affection. I have gone up hills but I have never walked along
them all day long but I do know however what you mean by the
mountains reaching the horizon.
The knowledge that I am a guiding varde to some friends
humbles me and makes me more and more conscious of the
tremendous burden that I carry on my shoulders. I have to
watch myself and incessantly pray to the God of Truth that I
may not prove a false guide to anybody.
Yours sincerely,
MISS ELLEN HORUP
GENEVA
From a photostat: S.N. 20335
