58. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
March 11, 1939
CHI. MIRA,
I am daily getting stronger. Therefore there should be no
anxiety. I reach Delhi on 15th and know not how long I shall
have to stay there. There is just a possibility that I would have
to come back to Rajkot. I must think of nothing else before Raj-
kot is set on its feet.
Ba goes with me to Delhi without Kano .
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6435. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10030
