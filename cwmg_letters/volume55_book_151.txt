151. LETTER TO NANABHAI. MASHRUWALA
May 7, 1933
BHAI NANABHAI,
I got your letter. No one should be upset by my fast. Try
to do as much more service as you can.
Should my friends wish me to refuse a God-given gift or
to welcome it?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6687
