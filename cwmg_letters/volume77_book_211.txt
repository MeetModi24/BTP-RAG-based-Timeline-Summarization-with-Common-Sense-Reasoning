211. LETTER TO VIJAYA ANAND
POONA,
June 25, 1944
MY DEAR VIJAYA ANAND 1 ,
I have your dear letter. You did well in writing to me so
frankly as you have done.
I have signed 10 albums for you.
I hope the Maharaniji is well. Love to you all.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
