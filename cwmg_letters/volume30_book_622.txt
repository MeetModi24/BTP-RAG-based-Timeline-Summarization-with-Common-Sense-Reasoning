622. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 3, 1926
DEAR MOTILALJI,
Here is a copy of Vithalbhai's reply to a copy of your letter
which I sent him.
I hope you are gaining by your stay in Mussoorie.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
PANDIT MOTILALJI NEHRU
MUSSOORIE
From a photostat: S.N. 11318
