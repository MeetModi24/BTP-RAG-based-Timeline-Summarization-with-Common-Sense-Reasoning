565. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
September 22, 1940
CHI. SUSHILA,
Today only this much. We are leaving on Wednesday for
Delhi. We shall leave for Simla on the same day. We have
to reach there on Friday. Come to the station if you are free,
otherwise whenever you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
LADY HARDINGE COLLEGE
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original:
Pyarelal Papers.
Nehru
Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
