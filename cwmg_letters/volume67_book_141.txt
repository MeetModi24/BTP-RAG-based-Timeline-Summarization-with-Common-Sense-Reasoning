141. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
May 30, 1938
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I shall make no change in your time-table now. Talk less.
I shall release you from Sharda's tuition. I shall protect
you. You have given me good warning.
Your food charges may well be met here. What you spend
on books is too much. It would be good if you received books
from home. Are you not satisfied with whatever books we have?
Mahadev also has many books. You can look at them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4292
