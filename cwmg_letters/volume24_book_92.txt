92. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SABARMATI,
Jyeshtha Sud 1 [June 3, 1924]
BHAISHRI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I have your letter. I have written to the leader of the Antyaja
Mandal that you made no promise to give them Rs. 30,000.
It is no doubt bad that the community is divided into two
factions. If, however, your group behaves courteously with the other,
it will stop the venom from spreading. It is of course true that
peace and strife cannot go on together. If you choose one or the
other and stick to it, then only can you get its fruit. The result
of strife we see in the condition of Europe. True love is altogether
absent there. The way of peace has so far never been practised by
communities.
Yours sincerely,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6047. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
