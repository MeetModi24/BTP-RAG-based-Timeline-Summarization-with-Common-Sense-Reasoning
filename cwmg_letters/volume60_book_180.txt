180. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
January 31, 1935
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
Herewith a small item for Harijansevak. If possible gather some
more information about this matter there. I fail to understand
this boycott.
What have you done about the Hindi lessons for Prabhavati?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1098
