118. LETTER TO LAKSHMI NARAYAN
ON THE TRAIN,
April 26, 1937
BHAI LAKSHMI NARAYAN,
Moral degradation is absolutely inexcusable.
There could be different legal opinions in the matter of zone
ticket, etc.
Propagation of Hindi has definitely suffered on account of
Pandit Sharma's downfall.
Inscrutable are the ways of God.
[Adoption of] Hindi-Hindustani should never lead to spoiling
of the language. It is indeed true that deliberate omission of non-
Sanskrit words, such as Persian, etc., makes the language chaste.
Yours,
M.K. GANDHI
SHRI LAKSHMI NARAYAN
BHARATIYA VISHARAD
HINDI VIDYAPITH
DEOGHAR, BIHAR
