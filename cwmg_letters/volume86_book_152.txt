152. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
November 15, 1946
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I have your letter. There is no doubt at all about my
going to Dashgharia. Discussion is going on with the Muslim
League. There is no need for you to come here. You should
get absorbed in your own work. Do not worry too much. Do
what your strength permits you to do and be content.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 539
