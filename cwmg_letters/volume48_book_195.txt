195. LETTER TO H. H. MONTGOMERY
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
November 11, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have kept your long letter by me all these days to be able
to send you just a line of thanks for it and for the sentiments
expressed therein. Charlie Andrews also shared it with me and
he found great joy to read the sentences about him.
Yours sincerely,
THE RIGHT REVEREND BISHOP MONTGOMERY
NEW PARK
MOVILLE
CO. DONEGAL, IRELAND
From a photostat: S.N. 18131
