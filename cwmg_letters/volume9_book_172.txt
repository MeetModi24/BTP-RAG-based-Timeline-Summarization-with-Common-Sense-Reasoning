172. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
[R.M.S. Kenilworth Castle,]
July 7, 1909
CHI. RAMDAS,
I am on this steamer.
Blessings from
BAPU
MASTER RAMDAS GANDHI
INDIAN OPINION,
PHOENIX, NATAL
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand, written on a picture-postcard
of the steamer: C. W. 84
Courtesy: Sushilabehn Gandhi
