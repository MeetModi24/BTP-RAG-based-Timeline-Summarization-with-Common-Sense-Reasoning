288. LETTER TO M. VISVESVARAYYA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 15, 1937
DEAR SIR M. VISVESVARAYYA,
I thank you for your book. I am keeping it in front of
me and I shall read it at the first opportunity. Nothing would
give me greater pleasure than to be able to agree with you.
I thank you for tackling the Orissa flood problem.
Of
course, you will take your time.
Yours sincerely,
M . K . GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 9837. Courtesy: Government of Mysore
