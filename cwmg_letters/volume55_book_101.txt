101. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
May 2, 1933
MY GOOD DAUGHTER,
Though you have sent a new bhajan for the second fast, it
cannot be sung. You know that such bhajans, unless they are sung
well, fail to make any impression on my heart. I shall sing the
same old one. Well, you can now send me the good wishes
from Father, Mother, and you girls and boys.
BAPU
From the Urdu original. Courtesy: National Archives of India. Also from
