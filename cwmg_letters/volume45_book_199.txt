199. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI M. THAKKAR
February 2, 1931
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
The kindness which Gangabehn and others have tasted, all of
you will taste by and by. You should be patient and keep yourselves
ready for it. It is not enough that we should suffer beating. When
beaten, we should not get angry with the person who beat us but,
on the contrary, should pray for his welfare. I think we shall
now be able to meet somewhere.
Blessings from
BAPU
