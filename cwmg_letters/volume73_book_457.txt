457. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
February 22, 1941
CHI. ANAND,
I have your letter. Meet me at Allahabad itself. I was sorry
to note the contents of the letter. We should accept the will of
God.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India, and
Anand T. Hingorani
