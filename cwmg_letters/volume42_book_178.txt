178. LETTER TO ALAVI
CAMP RAI BARELI,
November 14, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I went through a few pages of your book. A book like yours
is not in my line. I am no judge of English poetry and I have
no time to give myself for pleasures of that character. But I saw
sufficient of your pages to discover that it contained many errors
typographical and other. And as I was going through the few
pages I could not help asking myself why you had devoted your
precious time to writing on a thing on which Englishmen would
write with greater confidence and authority and why you had
not given your time to writing something in Urdu and for the
Urdu-knowing countrymen of ours.
Yours sincerely,
PROF. ALAVI
MUSLIM UNIVERSITY
ALIGARH
From a photostat: S.N. 15765
