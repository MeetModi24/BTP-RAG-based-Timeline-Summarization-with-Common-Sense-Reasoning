148. LETTER TO DINESH SINGH
NEW DELHI,
April 7, 1939
CHI. DINESH,
I had your letter and your mother herself arrived. She
stayed with me for a couple of days. My efforts are continuing.
You should not worry but concentrate on studies.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Mother's health was improving. She has also taken some
medicines from here.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 8673
