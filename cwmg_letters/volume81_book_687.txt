687. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
October 19, 1945
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
I have your letter. My going there has been put off by a
few days. It will now be in the middle of November or towards
the end. It depends on Sardar's health.
What shall I say regarding you? It is true that you have
learnt nothing from me, and also true that nobody has learnt more
than you have. But that is neither here nor there. Talk things
over with me when I arrive there. Pyarelal is ill. He will recover.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
It has not been decided who will accompany me. It was for-
tunate that you survived. This is how your life will pass. You
will have fully recovered by now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu and Gujarati : G.N. 490
