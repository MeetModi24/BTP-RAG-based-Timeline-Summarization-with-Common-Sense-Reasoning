395. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
MIDNAPUR,
Ashadh Vad 1 [July 7, 1925]
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I have overworked my right hand, so I am using the left
hand. You will get an idea of my preoccupations from Nava-
jivan and Young India. I have now resumed the unfinished tour
of Bengal. It will be completed in eight days.
A woman gave me a present. It will be fitting to send it
to you. Hence I do so.
My entire programme has become disarranged for the pre-
sent. So I cannot write anything about my movements with
certainty. I shall be here the whole of this month. The pro-
gramme afterwards will be finalized after the 16th.
I hope you and Shankerlal are keeping well. This time
neither Mahadev nor Krishnadas is with me. I have left Mahadev
behind for the collection of funds and have sent Krishnadas for
improving his health.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Satis Babu is with me.
