203. LETTER TO VINA CHATTERJEE
May 12, 1945
CHI. VINA,
I have your letter. It is good you wrote to me. Keep your
body and mind healthy. Write to me in Hindi, whatever its
quality. Tell me all your experiences of Borivli. How much
progress have you made in Urdu?
Zohra has not written. Tell her also to write: what she has
learnt, how she is, and so forth. Each one should set down her
experiences separately.
Manu has gone to Bombay. Her health has not improved.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
