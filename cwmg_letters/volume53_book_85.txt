85. LETTER TO VATSALA V. DASTANE
January 16, 1933
By doing our best to remove suffering and by faithfully ob-
serving the rules of truth and so on we serve living beings. Those
who indulge in untruth, violence, hoarding, thieving and abrahma-
charya hurt life itself. By observing truth and so on they alle-
viate suffering, which amounts to serving.
[From Hindi]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. III, pp. 57-
