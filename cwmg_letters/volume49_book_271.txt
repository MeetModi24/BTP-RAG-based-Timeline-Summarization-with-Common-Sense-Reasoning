271. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
March 22, 1932
MY DEAR AGATHA,
You will recognize this hand . Mahadev came in just when
my right hand had begun to refuse to work for me, not that
there is any continuing pain but writing with it is difficult. I
do a fair amount of writing with the left hand and am using
Mahadev's hand whenever I can.
You [talk] of Dr. Ramchandra Rao. I can't identify him.
He is not likely to be permitted to see me. We are now three
and we are all keeping quite well, and having a fair stock of
books, newspapers and spinning-wheels, we have enough to oc-
cupy our time.
With love,
Yours sincerely,
BAPU 6
From a photostat: G.N. 1452
