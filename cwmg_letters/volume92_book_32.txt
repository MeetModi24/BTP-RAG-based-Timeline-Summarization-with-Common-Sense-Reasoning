32. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
Silence Day [April 15, 1929]
CHI. KISHORELAL,
I have your letter. Though I have sent an answer to the
letter Harjivan wrote to me, I accept your advice and shall put
up with his having taken away Sharada.
I have read the comments of the Times. The writer was
within his rights to write what he did. Even a hypocrite can
write thus.
I still think that it was my moral duty to write what I did
in Navajivan. I hope you are keeping well. I stop here as it is
now time for the mail to go.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10713. Courtesy: Gomatibehn Mashru-
