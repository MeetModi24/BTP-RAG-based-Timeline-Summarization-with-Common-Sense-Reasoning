223. LETTER TO C. P. RAMASWAMI AIYAR
WARDHA,
September 20, 1939
DEAR FRIEND,
A friend unconnected with politics writes to me to say that
the labouring population living near the sea and the back-
waters are suffering great privations due to want of employment.
The writer thinks that if I drew your attention to the matter,
relief was likely to be given to these people.
Yours sincerely,
SIR C. P.
TRIVANDRUM
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
