53. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 16, 1939
CHI. BABUDI,
What a shame! Why did you let asthma attack you? And even
if it did, why did you get frightened? Don't you see you upset Sha-
karibehn's programme? You should keep in the house a kettle
with a spout, and also secure a rubber tube of sufficient length. That
will serve your purpose. Get a brass or copper kettle.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10019. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Cho-
khawala
