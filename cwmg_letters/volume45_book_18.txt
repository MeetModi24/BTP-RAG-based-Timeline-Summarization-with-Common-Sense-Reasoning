18. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 21, 1930
CHI. MANI (PATEL),
Is it possible that I should go on writing to you even if you
never drop me so much as a line? Have you forgotten your
promise that you would be writing to me? But better late than
never. Realize the importance of keeping promises. Give me an
account of your jail experiences. Did you keep good health?
What food did you take?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 75
