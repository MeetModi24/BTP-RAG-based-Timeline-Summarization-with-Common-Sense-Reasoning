66. LETTER TO M. VISVESVARAYYA
[After August 15, 1937]
DEAR SIR VISVESVARAYYA,
You know the havoc wrought by the floods in Orissa. I have
advised the Chief Minister Shri Vishwanath Das to approach you
for advice and guidance. I have no doubt that you would give
him such assistance as it may be in your power to give.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M . K . GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 9836. Courtesy: Government of Mysore
