308. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Sravana Sud 10 [August 10, 1924]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. My weight has no doubt gone down,
but in other ways my health is definitely good. Do whatever
you can after reading about Malabar in Navajivan.
I hope you go out for walks regularly. You need not worry
if any letters or words in your writing are scratched.
Today Haribhai has come here.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Make it a habit to take black French plums soaked in water.
Your second postcard has come just. Most probably I shall be
here. Do come soon.
BAPU
SISTER VASUMATI
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 453. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
