479. LETTER TO MURIEL LESTER
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 25, 1929
MY DEAR MURIEL,
I have your letters. I see your work is daily prospering.
May it be a complete blessing to all who come under its influence.
Nothing is yet decided about my European visit this year.
You shall know about the decision in due course.
Yours sincerely,
MISS MURIEL LESTER
Bow, E. 3, LONDON
From a photostat: S.N. 15147
