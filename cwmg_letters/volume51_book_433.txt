433. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
Monday [October 18, 1932]
CHI. MANILAL,
I got your letter. You may come whenever you wish. It
would be good if Sushila can accompany you, but she need not
come if she is weak. Bring Sita if she comes. You should take
care and see that your bowels move regularly. Take only light
food. It will help you if you can take more milk. Take care and
safeguard your health. Tell Ba that I have asked for a pres-
cription from Major Advani. She should consult a doctor there
too. I hope that Pyarelal has received my letter. Convey my
blessings to Manibhai and Gulab. I had expected Deva today.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANILAL GANDHI
MANI BHUVAN
LABURNAM ROAD
GAMDEVI, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4797
