292. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Thursday [December 15, 1921]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I got both your letters together. I do not share your mis-
giving. I do not wish to give up the use of footwear, but I shall
think over it. We should do what we can and leave the rest to
Him. Today I am sending a copy of the resolution to Shanker-
lal. Please see it. You must take all the rest that you need to
recover your health. If you can you may drop in on the 22nd.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
93 BAZAR GATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
