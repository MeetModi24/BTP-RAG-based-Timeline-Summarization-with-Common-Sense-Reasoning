532. LETTER TO RAMNATH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 27, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. It is unfortunately true. But we have
not yet been able to arrive at a stage when we can manufacture
khadi thread. It will take some time.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. RAMNATH
OVERSEER
P.O. SHEIKHAWAHAN
BAHAWALPUR STATE
From a microfilm: S.N. 13432
