649. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
[After July 6, 1928]
DEAR BHAGWANJI,
Come when all your expectations have died out and only a
sense of duty is left behind and there is perfect agreement bet-
ween you two.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original; C.W. 9789. Courtesy; Bhagwanji P. Pandya
1 & 2 This was in reply to the addressee's letter enumerating his difficulties
in joining the Ashram and from the contents appears to have been written
after the preceding one.
