241. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN PATHAK
October 16, 1944
BHAI RAMNARAYAN 1 ,
I have the letter signed by you and Bhai Umashankar. I
was surprised to find that you two made a common cause. The
inquiry is now on under Jajuji's supervision. Hence I will not
express any clear opinion at the moment. I will write again
within three or four days.
Blessings from
BAPU
ACHARYA RAMNARAYAN PATHAK
BHARATI NIWAS
ELLISBRIDGE, AHMEDABAD
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
