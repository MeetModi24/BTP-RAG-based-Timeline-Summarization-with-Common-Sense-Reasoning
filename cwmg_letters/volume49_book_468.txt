468. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
April 30, 1932
CHI. VIDYA,
It is not at all necessary to wear a pin in your hair. Dreams
come to everybody. Nobody knows why they come. You will be
able to Improve your handwriting if you try hard enough. Do
not get disheartened.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9426. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
