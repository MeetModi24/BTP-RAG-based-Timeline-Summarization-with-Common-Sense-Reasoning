150. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 24, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
The prayer bell has just gone. Sardar is fine. But Ba continues
to have the cough. What can one do ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
