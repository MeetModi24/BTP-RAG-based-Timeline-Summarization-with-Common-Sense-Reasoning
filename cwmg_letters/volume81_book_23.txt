23. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
SEVAGRAM,
July 22, 1945
DEAR DR. JAYAKAR,
I thank you for your letter which I am forwarding to the
Maulana. I know he will appreciate it.
You may depend upon my doing whatever is possible for me
to do.
I hope you are keeping well and fit.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. M. R. JAYAKAR
WINTER ROAD
MALABAR HILL
BOMBAY
Gandhi-Jayakar Papers, File No. 826. Courtesy: National Archives of India
