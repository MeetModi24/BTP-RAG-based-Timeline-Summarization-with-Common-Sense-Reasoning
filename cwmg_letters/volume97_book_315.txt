315. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 7, 1939
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
It would not be improper for you to write one letter every year addressed
to Bitor. It is good news that Amba's eye is now all right.
Your description of Mathura is fine. Let us now see what it all comes
to.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PRABHUDAS GANDHI
ASAFPUR
DISTT. BADAUN
U.P.
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33047
