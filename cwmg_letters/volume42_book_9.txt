9. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
MUSSOORIE,
October 17, 1929
CHI. VASUMATI 1 ,
I have your letter. I would not advise you to go to Almora
just now. For some time the conditions there have been unsettled.
It is also a question whether Prabhudas should be sent there. We
shall consider later whether you can go. If you wish to go to
some hill station, it may of course be possible to make arrange-
ments elsewhere. Or, it may be possible to put you up with
Mathuradas in Almora. But, if you keep well at Vijapur, what is
the advantage of going to a hill station? At a hill station it will
be very cold now. Here in Mussoorie I am just now sitting
wrapped in blankets.
Blessings from
BAPU
