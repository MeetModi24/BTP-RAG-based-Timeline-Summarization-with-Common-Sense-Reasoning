182. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
February 19, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
Your two letters came the same time.
I am glad you like
the place and the people. You should arrange your hours as
you think proper in consultation with the manager. For the
girls, you should do as the manager advises.
When you have
acquired sufficient control you may make drastic changes. For the
lice, rub spirit into the roots of the hair and wash them with
carbolic soap and the lice will disappear.
The hair should be
combed daily with a clean comb. Let the girls take for the
time being the food that is cooked for them.
There can be no harm whatsoever in your being interested in
natural scenery and Islamic architecture.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
