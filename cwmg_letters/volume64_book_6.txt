6. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON,
November 5, 1936
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Well, I returned yesterday in good order and condition and
found both the patients in a fit condition.
I hope you got my letter written on the train and posted
at Bhusaval.
No more time to say more.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3751. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6907
