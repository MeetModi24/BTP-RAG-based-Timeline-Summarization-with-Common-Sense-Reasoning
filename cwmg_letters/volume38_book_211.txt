211. LETTER TO AKSEL G. KNUDSEN
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
December 7, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I wish indeed that I could at once comply with your request.
But it is really almost a physical impossibility. The word 'almost'
is a polite superfluity. Whilst therefore you have my good wishes,
you will please excuse me. But you may select anything you like
from my writings for your journal.
Yours sincerely,
AKSEL G. KNUDSEN
BREDGADE
90 SKERN, DENMARK
From a photostat: S.N. 13776
