3. LETTER TO KISHORELAL G. MASHRUWALA
Silence Day [January 28, 1929]
CHI. KISHORELAL,
You have fallen ill properly after you went from here. Does
this illness not teach you that you must not be rushing about?
Karsandas has written a very long letter which he has ask-
ed me to pass on to you to read. Read it and think carefully
about it and then discuss it with him.
How is Gomati ?
The work here is going on well. I have withdrawn Naran-
das from the khadi work I think he will stay on here. Maha-
dev and Narahari have gone to Bardoli. I am going to Sind
on the 31st. I am giving my dates overleaf.?
Blessings from
BAPU
