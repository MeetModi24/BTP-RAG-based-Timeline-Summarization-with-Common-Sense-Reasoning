57. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
April 28, 1933
DEAR THAKKAR BAPA,
I have several applications for financial assistance from Hari-
jan students but I feel perplexed without an Education Board
whose formation I have suggested. Will you please set up one as
quickly as possible and announce the appointment, constitution
and rules?
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19059
