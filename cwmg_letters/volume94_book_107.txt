107. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
October 12, 1941
CHI. NIMU,
Why has Usha fallen ill ? If you avoid giving milk or soup while
the fever is rising, it comes down quicker. Here, this is how the
treatment of all fever starts. It applies to everybody, young and old.
Fever causes no fear. Giving enema does become necessary. The
patient must be given as much water as possible. Hot water would
be better. Salt and soda can be added to it. Lime juice can also be
added. If Kanu really wants to come, send him. Or, bring him at Diwali
time if he can make it. I think Ramdas too will be having holidays !
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Sansmarano, pp. 202-
