265. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Monday [April 6, 1925]
BHAISHRI, GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I have your letter. I certainly was not joking when I said
that you surpassed me. I see nothing wrong in it. Surely, one
should endeavour to surpass one's elders and friends in moral
strength. It is my duty to add to the moral heritage bequeathed
to me by my elders. I always pray to God that He may grant
my friends more strength than I possess, which only means that He
may save them from my shortcomings. I do wish you surpass
me in moral strength. Therein lies the fulfilment of my associa-
tion with you. Similarly you should wish greater strength to me.
This is one thing in which competition will not breed jealousy.
I hinted at re-marriage only to safeguard you against any
eventuality.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From the Hindi original: C. W. 6110. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
