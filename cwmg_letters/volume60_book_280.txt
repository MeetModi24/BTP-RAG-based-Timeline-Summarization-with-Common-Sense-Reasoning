280. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
February 11, 1935
BHAI THAKKAR BAPA,
Enclosed is a reply to Dewan Bahadur Bhashyam and
others like him. Pass it on to Viyogi Hari for Harijansevak after
you have read it. I am also writing to the Dewan Bahadur
and sending him a copy. I have not so far received a single copy
of the constitution. Send me a few.
Go at the problem of Ganesan patiently.
I am in correspondence with Sastri regarding Harijan.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1151
