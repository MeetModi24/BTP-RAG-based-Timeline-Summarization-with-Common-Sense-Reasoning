303. LETTER TO SHANTA S. PATEL
October 3, 1932
CHI. SHANTA1,
You are becoming careless about your handwriting. The
letter also is as bad as the handwriting. I hope now you are all right.
Don't mind Sardar's jokes. You may write what you wish to.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4066. Also C.W. 17. Courtesy:
Shantabehn Patel
