295. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
October 3, 1932
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
You asked me about rotlo. But my rotlo has dried up. God
has snatched it away from me. I am now regaining strength with
milk and fruit.
I hope all of you are all right. Ask Valji to write to me.
How was his health [in jail] ?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7438. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
