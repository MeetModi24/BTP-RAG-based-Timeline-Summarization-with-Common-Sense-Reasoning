451. LETTER TO ACHYUT PATWARDHAN
NEW DELHI,
April 8, 1946
BHAI ACHYUT,
Yesterday I wasted time wondering whether to write or send
a wire to you and, owing to the burden of work, I could do
neither. Today I received your letter. The release is hardly a
cause for congratulations, but I am glad that you are well. Every-
thing is uncertain about me. I would, of course, like to meet you
soon, but let us see when God makes it possible. I am very glad
that you are going to Vinoba. You might possibly meet Kishore-
lalji and Kakasaheb too in Wardha Sevagram.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ACHYUT PATWARDHAN
VINCENT SQUARE
TRAM TERMINUS
DADAR
BOMBAY
