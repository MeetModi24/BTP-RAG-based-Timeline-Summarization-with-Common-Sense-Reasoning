16. LETTER TO ELIZABETH MARI MOLTENO
July 20, 1914
DEAR MISS MOLTENO,
I had your two letters. I am sorry we were not able to meet to
say goodbye to one another. Mrs. Gandhi and I cannot forget the
affection you and Miss Greene showed us during our stay in Cape
Town. May God reward you for it.
Do please write to me occasionally. My address will be Rajkot
via Bombay.
With our united regards to you both,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Molteno Murray Family Papers. Courtesy: University of
Cape Town Libraries
