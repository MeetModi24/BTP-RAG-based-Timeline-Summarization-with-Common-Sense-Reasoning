108. LETTER TO VIMALCHANDRA V. DESAI
September 29, 1934
CHI. NANU 1 ,
I got your letter and article. Your handwriting leaves much
to be desired. The letters in the same word should not be written
apart. It would be good if you showed your letters to some-
body after writing them.
How long do you intend to stay
there?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 5739. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
