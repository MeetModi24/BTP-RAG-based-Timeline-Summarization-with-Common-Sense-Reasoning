391. LETTER TO C. A. TULPULE
SEGAON (WARDHA),
June 26, 1937
MY DEAR TULPULE,
You are quite correct in writing to me as you have done.
Other friends also have been writing to me and letters such as
yours will help me to gauge public opinion. For, it is one thing for
me to hold a theoretical opinion, another thing to base it on prac-
tical experience.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI C. A. TULPULE, M. L. A.
TILAK ROAD
POONA
From the original: C.W. 2897. Courtesy: C. A. Tulpule
