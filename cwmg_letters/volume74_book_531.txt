531. LETTER TO KANHAIYALAL VAIDYA
September 16, 1941
BHAI KANHAIYALAL,
I have your letter. It is a regrettable thing. Please do not
expect any money from me. I do what I can within my limits
and in my own way. At present I do whatever I can while
remaining in the background. Those who pursue satyagraha
must put up with suffering. Therein too lies the way to alleviate
suffering.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
