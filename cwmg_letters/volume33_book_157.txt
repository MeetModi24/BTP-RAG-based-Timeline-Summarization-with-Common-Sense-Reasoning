157. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
[On or after March 14, 1927]
DEAR SATIS BABU,
What a joy Kshitish Babu's decision! I have telegraphed my
approval and warned him against being ill in Sodepur.
I am glad Tarini is getting better.
How about Hemaprabha Devi and the boy? I shall feel re-
lieved when you can really tell me that you are all yourselves
again.
I shall inquire about the commission.
With love to you all,
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
[PS.]
19-21 Gurukul Kangri,
Dist. Bijnor
23 Bombay
25-26 Kolhapur
27-4th April Belgaum
5-12 Madras
From a photostat: G.N. 1632
