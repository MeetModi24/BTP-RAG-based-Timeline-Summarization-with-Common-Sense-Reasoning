140. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
January 6, 1930
CHI. RAMDAS,
We arrived here on Saturday night without any mishap during the
journey. Write and tell me how you are keeping. Is Lakshmidasbhai's dietetic
experiment still going on? How is Jivandas? Has he returned? If not, where
is he? Ba will leave tomorrow for Vijapur. Nimu is there, and so is Manu,
as also Kashi. She is therefore keen to go there. Manilal has gone to Akola.
He will return with Sushila in a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
