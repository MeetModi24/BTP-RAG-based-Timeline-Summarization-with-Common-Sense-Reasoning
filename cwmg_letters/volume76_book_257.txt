257. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
June 13, 1942
MY DEAR C. R.,
Your letters. The big people will be going in a day or
two. You must come and have a little rest before you embark
on another tour.
I want you to come for a little rest, a little
fun. But you should try to let me see my error which you see
so clearly.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2090
