71. LETTER TO SIR MAHOMED HABIBULLAH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 16, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your kind letter. I am sorry that it is not possible for
you to accept my first suggestion. I appreciate your objection.
From your letter I gather that the decision about Sir K. V.
Reddi had already been taken. I am afraid I shall not be able
to support the appointment, and may feel called upon to oppose
it. You might have seen in the papers that the matter has already
leaked out. I have myself received a letter asking me to protest
against the proposal. But I do not yet see my way clear to take
any step. Have you ascertained from Sastriji his opinion about
the appointment of Sir K. V. Reddi?
Yours sincerely,
SIR MAHOMED HABIBULLAH SAHEB BAHADUR, K.C.I.E.
NEW DELHI
