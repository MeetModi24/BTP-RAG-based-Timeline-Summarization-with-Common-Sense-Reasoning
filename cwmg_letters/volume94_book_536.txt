536. LETTER TO MAULANA HIFZURREHMAN
NEW DELHI,
October 15, 1946
DEAR MAULANA SAHEB,
I have read all your papers. The attack is on me, not on Kripalaniji.
If what I have always believed is a crime, then Kripalaniji is guilty.
Yours sincerely.
M. K. GANDHI
