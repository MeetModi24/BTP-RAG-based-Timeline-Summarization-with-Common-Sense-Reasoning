197. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
CAMP ALLAHABAD,
November 17, 1929
MY DEAR SATIS BABU,
I have your letter. I have not yet got the soya bean packets.
I note what you have written to Govind Babu. I do not attach
any importance to his opinion. You are having your share of sor-
rows and worries with a vengeance, Tarini incapable, Charu fol-
lowing suit and Hemprabhadevi again bed-ridden. It is appalling.
May you have the strength to bear the burden.
I have got a letter from Ram Binod of which I enclose a
copy herewith. I also enclose a copy of my reply. Have I cor-
rectly represented your position?
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
