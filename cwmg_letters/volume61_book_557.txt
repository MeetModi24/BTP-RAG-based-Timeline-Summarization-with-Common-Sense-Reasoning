557. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
WARDHA,
September 4, 1935
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
You can imagine the relief I had on receiving your Wire.
As usual Mahadev is taking this with him. I wish I had come
myself but I must not. You will frankly give me your opinion
on all the things of common interest. Unless there is an insupera-
ble bar you should take charge of the Congress ship next year.
You will wire to me Kamala's condition on reaching there. The
news of your release must itself have brought her considerable
relief.
I hope you were keeping well.
Love.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1935. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
