631. LETTER TO RANDHEER NAIDU
BOMBAY,
April 12, 1945
MY DEAR MAINA,
I see Mother was able to give you my message. I do
wish you would recover but if you must leave before us all,
I know you will be brave and be full of faith in God.
Love.
BAPU
C/O SAROJINI NAIDU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
