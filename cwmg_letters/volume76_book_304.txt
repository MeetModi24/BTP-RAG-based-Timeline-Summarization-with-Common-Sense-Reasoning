304. LETTER TO GAJANAN T. MADKHOLKAR
June 29, 1942
BHAI MADKHOLKAR1,
Your letter is lying before me. The thing is I am in a di-
lemma. I fully endorse the idea of bringing together all the areas
of Maharashtra into one province. But I am doubtful about the
wisdom of starting a movement for the purpose at the present
juncture. I don't know how far it is proper to attach special
importance to this one problem when everything is threatened.
If in the end India attains independence all such problems will
be solved automatically. That being my view, what help can
I give you?
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
