49. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
November 14, 1936
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I got your letter. I am immediately writing to Chowdhari
at the address that I have been given.
I have not yet received the book sent by the Parsi sister.
You certainly did well in buying a cow. I am glad that you
feel happy there.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 145
