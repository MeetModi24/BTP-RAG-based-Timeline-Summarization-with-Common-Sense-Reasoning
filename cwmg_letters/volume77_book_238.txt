238. LETTER TO GUNOTTAM HUTHEESING
July 8, 1944
CHI. RAJA,
I am simply waiting for the 20th. One need not fear the
climate of this place because one can always return to one's own
place, if the climate does not suit one. Death strikes a man but
once; cowardice hundreds of times. Moreover you had better
pay a visit, if only to fulfil a promise. If you are still hesitant
in spite of all this persuasion, you should stay put. It is sur-
prising that Krishna cannot take care of herself. She must stay
on for the sake of Indira1, else I would have dragged along both
of you. Let me have your final decision.
Blessings from
BAPU
HUTHEESING
BOMBAY
