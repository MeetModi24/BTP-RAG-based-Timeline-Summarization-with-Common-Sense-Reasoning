337. LETTER TO VAMAN KRISHNA PARANJPE
August 31, 1945
BHAI VAMAN KRISHNA PARANJPE,
I have your letter. I very well remember your grandfather .
He was a great orator and a man of courage.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI VAMAN KRISHNA PARANJPE
VAKIL, SHUKRAVAR PETH
POONA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
