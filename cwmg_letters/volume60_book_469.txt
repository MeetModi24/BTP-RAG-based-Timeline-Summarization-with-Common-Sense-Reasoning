469. LETTER TO HUSSAIN
March 28, 1935
In my opinion your plan must fail. When a house is on
fire, the finest essay showing how to quench it would not abate
the fury of the fire.
A bucketful of water, however clumsily
handled, will somewhat affect the fire.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
