430. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
NEW DELHI,
January 21, 1948
MY DEAR C. R.,
Of course you were right in speaking to me through your
silence. You might not have broken it as you did through yours
of 16th instant. From calm I have entered storm. Thank God I
have fairly learnt to face either with some amount of indiffer-
ence. I hope you are faring well in every respect. I observe
that you have lighted upon the fittest job for you.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G. N. 2194
