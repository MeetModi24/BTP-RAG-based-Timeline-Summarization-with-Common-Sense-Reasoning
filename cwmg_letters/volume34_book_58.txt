58. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
BANGALORE,
June 26, 1927
MY DEAR MALKANI,
Your letter cuts me to the quick. I do not mind what hap-
pens to the Mahavidyalaya but I do mind what happens to a
man. I had considered you to be good, strong and immovable
under the greatest stress. You have been weighed and found want-
ing. This desertion in indecent haste is to me inexplicable. I am
sorry also for Thadani. He forgot the ordinary gentlemanly beha-
viour in his hurry to do good.
You may show this to him. May God help you and me.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 875 & S.N. 12599
