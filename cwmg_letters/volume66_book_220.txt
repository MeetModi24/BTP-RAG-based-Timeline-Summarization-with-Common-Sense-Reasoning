220. LETTER TO SYED BASHIR AHMED
[Before September 30, 1937]
It amazes me that you fail to see the great merit of the
Congress having given dictatorial powers to a single Mussal-
man in the important matter of choosing Muslim Ministers. Of
course, the Maulana can err, but it is irrelevant. But what is
great and relevant is the fact that a Mussalman divine has been
given powers, which the Congress has not given to any single
person.
The Hindu, 2-10-1937
