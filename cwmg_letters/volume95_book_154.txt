154. LETTER TO VENKATASWAMI NAIDU
SEVAGRAM,
WARDHA [C.P.],
May 14, 1941
DEAR VENKATASWAMI,
I note what you say about the nomination of candidates for the
M. U. Senate.
Yours Sincerely,
M.K. GANDHI
SHRI K. VENKATASWAMI NAIDU
B.A. B.L.
KILPAUK
MADRAS
From a photostat: Venkataswami Naidu Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library
