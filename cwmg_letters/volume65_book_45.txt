45. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
April 2, 1937
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
What is this! I do not in the least insist on keeping Mal-
kani. If his leaving brings greater benefit to the Harijan
Niwas it is our duty promptly to remove him from there. Hence
whatever is good may be done.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 8030. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
