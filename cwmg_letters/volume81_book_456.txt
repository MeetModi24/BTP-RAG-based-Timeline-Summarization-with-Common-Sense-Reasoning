456. LETTER TO BENARSIDAS CHATURVEDI
September 16, 1945
BHAI BENARSIDAS1,
Your letter has pained me. But it is not proper to resign
on that account. Merely giving up sugar, etc., will not do.
Acquiring control over the mind is quite another matter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2517
