573. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
November 6, 1932
CHI. JAMNA,
Your letters please me. Keep up the fearlessness which you
show in them. I thought you kept better health in Rajkot. If you
keep well in the Ashram both mentally and physically, I would
not like you to leave it even for a moment.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 861. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
