377. LETTER TO NARAYANRAO KULKARNI
December 24, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Where the villagers are so cruel as to
threaten complete boycott of untouchables who would make use
of public wells or tanks, the ideal course for Harijans would be
to vacate such a village. Boycott of workers is a simpler matter.
They should have the courage to brave all the risks attendant upon
working for the reform. If they continue their service without
retaliation, the opposition will die out. The other way of course is
to await the conversion of the villagers.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. NARAYANRAO KULKARNI
POST ASHTA (SATARA)
