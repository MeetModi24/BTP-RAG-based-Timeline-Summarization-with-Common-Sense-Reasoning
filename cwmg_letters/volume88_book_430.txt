430. LETTER TO PUSHPA K. DESAI
NEW DELHI,
July 23, 1947
CHI. PUSHPA,
I am writing this at 4.25 in the morning. There should be
no complaint against you. You ought not to leave the Khadi
Vidyalaya without permission. Those who cannot maintain disci-
pline prove useless in the end. So wake up. Write to me what
happened. How are you? How is your work going on? How
is your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
