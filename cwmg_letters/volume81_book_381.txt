381. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
September 3, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA
You have taken charge. Let us see what happens. Every-
thing will be clear if you have patience. Read what I have
written in my letter to Munnalal.
I hope Hoshiari is well and also others.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4524
