104. LETTER TO SIR WILLIAM VINCENT
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
February 12, 1919
I observe that the Committee appointed to investigate the
cases of Messrs Mahomed Ali and Shaukat Ali has completed
its deliberations. I wonder whether the Government have now
arrived at any decision over this case.
N. A. I.: Home: Political—A: July 1919: No. I & K. W.
