340. LETTER TO GANGABEHN
"MANI BHUVAN",
LABURNUM ROAD,
GAMDEVI, BOMBAY,
Sunday, Jeth Vad 2 [June 15, 1919]
DEAR SISTER,
A school for teaching spinning has been started here today. It
will work every day from 12 to 3. I should like you to attend.
Behn Ramibai showed me hand-woven cloth. It was excellent.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
GANGA SWARUP BEHN GANGABAI MEGHAJI 2
KANJI KARSANDAS BUILDING
HOLI CHAKLA
FORT, BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 5736. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
