163. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 23, 1942
MY DEAR C. R.,
So Laxmi has passed first class! This is mere introduction.
Though we differ as poles asunder, my heart goes out to
you in your stand against hooliganism at your meetings.
I found in Bombay it was no use my making any attempt
to see Q. A. Moreover he was not there.
You know you are to come here almost monthly to rest here
and be free from the care of attending meetings.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Kamalnayan asks me whether the house you are in may be
transferred to you or any of the family.
From a photostat: G.N. 2086
