132. LETTER TO LAKSHMI
[September 19, 1924]
CHI. LAKSHMI,
This letter too is for all. But should I call Lakshmi a bad girl
or a good girl because the boys say so? Will she not keep her
promise and write a letter? I am looking forward to a beautifully
written letter. I remember all the children during my fast, and I
even ask myself, "Do all the children spin regularly? Do they
study? Do they speak the truth? Do they obey the rules?" Who
will tell me?
I get immense peace from my fast. No one need worry about
me.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. LAKSHMI
SATYAGRAHA ASH RAM
SABARMATI
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 5645. Courtesy: Chhaganlal Gandhi
