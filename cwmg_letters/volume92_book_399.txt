399. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
December 21, 1930
CHI. MRIDULA,
SO you came away before time, did you? How was your
health? Write to me your experiences. Tell Khurshedbehn to write.
What was your diet while you were in the nursing home?
How did you spend your time?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 11110. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
