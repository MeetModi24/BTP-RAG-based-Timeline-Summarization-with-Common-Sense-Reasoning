316. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
April 2, 1933
BOYS AND GIRLS,
I would like you to collect the bones you come across. I will
find a use for them. Manure is certainly made by grinding them.
But how to grind them or whether they can be put to still better
use-I will have to find out.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
