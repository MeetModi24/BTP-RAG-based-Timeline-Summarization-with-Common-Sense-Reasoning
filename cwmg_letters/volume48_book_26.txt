26. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
KINGSLEY HALL, Bow,
LONDON, E.,
September 22, 1931
CHI. SUSHILA1,
I have your letter. I am writing this while attending the
Conference. Mahadev, Pyarelal, Devdas and Mirabehn are with
me. The cold here is still bearable. But the work has turned out
to be heavier than I had thought.
I cannot say what the
outcome will be. I shall be here for at least a month longer. I
got Manilal's cable a little late. It is natural that you two should
wish to come here, but it is best that you should restrain the
wish. Staying with me, you would not be able to tour and see
this country well, and the expense, I think, would be much too
heavy and beyond our means.
Jayashankar's death was a release to him from his pain. It
would be very good if the two brothers settled down there. As
long as you are there, see that you do your work well and sincerely.
Blessings from
BAPU
