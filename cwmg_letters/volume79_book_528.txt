528. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM,
March 26, 1945
CHI. AMTUSSALAAM,
I have regularly written to you and also to Kanchan.
What can I do if you do not get the letters? It is a pity
your health has deteriorated so much. You have got to get well
somehow. And both of you should stay there till you get leave.
Keep writing to me. Lavanyachanda is brave. I hope she
has recovered.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 494
