480. LETTER TO SAROJINI NAIDU
PANCHGANI,
June 9, 1945
MY DEAR SINGER,
I have kept yours of 13th ultimo just to give you a few
lines of love for your great motherly affection. Your wire was
good as from a philosopher, who could put her philosophy to
practice at the right moment. Your letter brings out a mother's
affection at its best. I do not know whether to love you best as a
poetess, philosopher or mother? Tell me.
Love.
SHRIMATI SAROJINI NAIDU
SPINNER
HYDERABAD, DECCAN
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal. Also Mahatma Gandhi-
The Last Phase, Vol. I, Book One, p. 111
