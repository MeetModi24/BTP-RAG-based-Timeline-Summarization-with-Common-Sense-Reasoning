451. LETTER TO LORD LINLITHGOW
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 20, 1940
DEAR LORD LINLITHGOW,
As I have not had any acknowledgment—a most unusual thing
for you—of my letter of the 9th instant, lest it might have miscar-
ried I send herewith a copy of it in order to make assurance
double sure.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a printed copy: Lord Linlithgow Papers. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
