405. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
August 5, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I agree with you. Would you consider Kanchan touching me
undesirable? Should she avoid touching a man even for the sake of
serving him? Tell me unhesitatingly what you wish.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8530. Also C.W. 7099. Cour-
tesy: Munnalal G. Shah
