460. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
September 2, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
I have your letter. I feel that for the time being you should
stay there. Later on we shall see. Anand will stay here for the pre-
sent. I will try and go over to Wardha by the end of this month.
Keep mind and body fit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
