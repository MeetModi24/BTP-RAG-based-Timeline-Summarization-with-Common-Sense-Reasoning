273. LETTER TO PREMA KANTAK
April 18, 1947
. . .1 Even to avoid causing pain to dear ones, one cannot
stop what one considers a yajna. But where one is both the doer
and the deed one must realize the difficulty of a neutral attitude
and accept any step that may be taken against one. My ideas
are what they have always been. In fact I am becoming more
and more convinced about them. I see nothing wrong about
them. . . .2
From a copy of the Gujarati: G. N. 10460. Also C. W. 6898. Courtesy:
Prema Kantak
