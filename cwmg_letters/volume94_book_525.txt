525. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
NEW DELHI,
September 17, 1946
CHI. SUSHILA,
You must be well. It will not do to fall ill. There is a letter from
Manudi. I have kept it for you. She will be coming. Jaisukhlal will
be coming too. Do not fall to the temptation of abandoning your work
there and coming here. I would certainly be happy if you could come.
Jivraj has examined me. I felt fine. The blood-pressure is 160/98.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SUSHILABEHN
KASTURBA HOSPITAL
SEVAGRAM
WARDHA
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
