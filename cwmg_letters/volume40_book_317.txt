317. LETTER TO RUKMINI
May 4, 1929
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your letter only today. By all means you too may go
with Radha. Now you will have but a few days at Matheran.
Let us now see what Jamnalalji does. I am sending him a wire.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9045
