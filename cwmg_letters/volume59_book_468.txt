468. LETTER TO LILAVATI MUNSHI
November 23, 1934
CHI. LILAVATI,
Remember, no weakness. Labour is its own reward. Victory
or defeat is a matter of chance, and very often he who loses wins
and he who wins loses. I completely forgot to write to Gosibehn.
I am writing now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7562. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
