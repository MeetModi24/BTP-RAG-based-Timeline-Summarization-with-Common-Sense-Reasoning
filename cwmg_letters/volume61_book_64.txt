64. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
May 9, 1935
I got your letter regarding Titus. If the dairy at the Ashram
cannot be retained, he may go.
BAPU
[PS.]
I shall have to write to Mathew again.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-9: Shri Narandas Gandhine, Part II, p. 190. Also C.W. 8442.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
