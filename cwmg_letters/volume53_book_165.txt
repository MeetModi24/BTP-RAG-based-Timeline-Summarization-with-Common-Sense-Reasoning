165. LETTER TO BALIBEHN M. ADALAJA
January 23, 1933
CHI. BALI,
Kamu writes and tells me that you had no time and that is
why you have not written to me. I don't believe this. How can I
believe that you do not get time just to write one letter?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1520. Courtesy: Manubehn
Mashruwala
