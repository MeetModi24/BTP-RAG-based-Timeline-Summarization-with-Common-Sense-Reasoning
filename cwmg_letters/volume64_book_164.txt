164. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
December 16, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have gone through your preface. It is no doubt good. No
changes are called for. I have therefore made very few. Whether
the reader will be able to digest it is a question. Let us however
leave it at that.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have sent three articles, not including one by Andrews.
I sit down now to write the Gujarati.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11510
