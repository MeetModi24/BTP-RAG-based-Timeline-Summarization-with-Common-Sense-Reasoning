169. LETTER TO SWARUPRANI NEHRU
November 28, 1935
DEAR SISTER 2 ,
I have a letter from you after months and that too in res-
ponse to one of mine. Still, something is better than nothing.
I too got a letter about the rise in Kamala's temperature.
Let us hope that it has come down by now.
You must be getting letters from Krishna3; there is hardly any
chance of her writing to me. Jawaharlal will never have any respite
from work. Did he have rest even in jail? He wrote a book there
and now that he is out he writes letters which read like books.
Ramdas is keeping indifferent health and passing his days in
Bombay. He is expected here this week. Ba is all right and so is
Mahadev. I hope you are keeping well and cheerful.
MOHANDAS
From the Hindi original: Gandhiji-Indira Gandhi Correspondence. Cour-
tesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
