620. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
SIMLA,
June 26, 1945
BAPA,
Your letter to Narahari is before me. Send the invitations
for the Executive Committee meeting of the Kasturba Nidhi for
any day in August convenient to you. If it becomes necessary to
change it on my account, we shall do so.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
