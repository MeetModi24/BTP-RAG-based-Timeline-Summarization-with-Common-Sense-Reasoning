414. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
August 4, 1942
MY DEAR C. R.,
How can you expect me to approach Q. A. after his perform-
ance? Will he not be right in showing me the door if I
dare to go to him? I should certainly refuse to see a person
whom I thoroughly distrust and discredit.
Supposing he is
great and good enough to see me, what am I to say to him?
Begin by giving him an explanation of all the charges? I had
thought that you would wire to me not to go and that you
were disowning him after his performance. The Muslims who
had expected me to see him no longer think so. I do not
think I shall wire you to come here.
This has nothing to do
with your wire. There is not the atmosphere.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Of course you must pay your monthly visit.
