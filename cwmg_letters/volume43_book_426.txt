426. LETTER TO SARALADEVI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
May 25, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
I have received the fruit sent by you. I am asked to write
to you not to send them hereafter. Any fruit I require is given to
me here. Mangoes and oranges are given to the Jailor to eat.
When there has been conflagration on all sides how can I eat
mangoes? I took oranges for two or four days and [then] gave
them up. I do not see any necessity for them. [If] I require I
shall indeed take them. I shall ever cherish the love behind the
idea of sending fruit. I hope the children are all right.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 574
