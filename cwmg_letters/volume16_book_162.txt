162. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
[October 23, 1919]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
After reading the accompanying letter1, make what arrange-
ments you think best for Miss Faering. I think Narahari's last sen-
tence is perfectly correct. I had thought of sending him a tele-
gram about her while on my way here, but gave up the idea. If
you take her out for a walk every morning, as I used to take you
out, this flower will bloom the better and will give sweet fragrance.
You are looking after Mahadev, I am sure.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C.W. 5778. Courtesy:
Radhabehn Choudhri
