117. LETTER TO T. N. KALIDASS
As AT THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 12, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I shall not be able
to go through the papers you have sent me for the time being at
least. You will have to be satisfied with whatever I am able to
write in Young India out of my experience. I might endeavour to
draft model instructions and publish them in Young India.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. T. N. KALIDASS
HIGH COURT VAKIL
THE RAILWAY PASSENGERS ASSOCIATION
TANJORE
S. INDIA
From a microfilm: S.N. 13375
