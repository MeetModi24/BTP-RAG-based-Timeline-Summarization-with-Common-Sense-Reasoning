274. LETTER TO MOTILAL ROY
February 11, 1935
DEAR MOTIBABU,
I have your letter. I understand what you say. You will
please let me know what finally happens in connection with khadi.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI MOTILAL ROY
PRABARTAK BHAVAN
61 BOWBAZAR STREET, CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
