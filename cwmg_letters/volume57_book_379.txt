379. LETTER TO CHARU PROBHA SEN GUPTA
As AT PATNA,
April 9, 1934
MY DEAR CHARU PROBHA,
Nonsense, you must not give way to despair like this.
Sadhus will only put one interpretation on the Gita. It is an
interpretation born of laziness of mind.
You should read the
3rd chapter of the Gita again and again. There Krishna says,
"I do not stop working for one single moment. If I did, the
whole world would perish."
He says again, "No one stops work
for a moment, only everyone but the knowing one works helpless-
ly."
Let us work knowingly and voluntarily, not for self but for
the whole of humanity and we shall surely see God face to face.
Our humanity is India.
We may not serve her so as to injure
others and [then] it becomes service of God. We must learn to see
God in such service. This is the yoga of the Gita and no other.
Don't you write doleful letters to me. Just begin the service
that easily comes to you and you will soon expand and find joy.
Love.
BAPU
