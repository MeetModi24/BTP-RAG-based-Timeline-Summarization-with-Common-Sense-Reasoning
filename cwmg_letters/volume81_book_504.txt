504. LETTER TO MAGANBHAI P. DESAI
September 27, 1945
CHI. MAGANBHAI,
Your letter. As I feared it might rain, I had told Chi.
Amritlal what to do. So I am doing nothing more. Go even
now if you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MAGANBHAI DESAI
GUJARAT VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
B. B. & C. I. RLY.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
