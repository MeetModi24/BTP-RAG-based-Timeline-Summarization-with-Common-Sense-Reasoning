76. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 12, 1940
BHAI SATIS BABU 2 ,
Annada's letter is enclosed. When he writes that whatever
happened that day was due to panic, we should reopen the case
so far as necessary. What shall I do about the auditors?
First send reply to all the issues raised by Annada. How are
the prices of khadi brought down? How and how much capital
is invested in khadi work?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2732
