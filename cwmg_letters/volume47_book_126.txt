126. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
BORSAD,
July 4, 1931
BHAISHRI MUNSHI,
It is good you wrote the letter. I am writing to Sir Cowasji.
I believe in the saying, "Take care of the pence and the
pounds will take care of themselves." However, what preparations
need I make? As a servant, I have only to deliver the Master's
message. If those to whom the message will be addressed listen
to it, all will be well in the end. But shall we ever see such a
fortunate day? How can there be rains without thunder and
lightning?
There is yet neither thunder nor lightning, nor
even a cloud. Tell me, now, what I should study. Just now I
cannot give my mind to problems of the constitution. Tell Lila-
vati that it is fixed on the lace of the Madura sari. Rajagopala-
chari has now become her advocate. After writing a long essay
to him on the subject of the lace, I am writing this to you.
Lilavati should show me the lace when I come there. Rajaji has
raised a nice question and asked for my verdict in the matter.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 7516. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
