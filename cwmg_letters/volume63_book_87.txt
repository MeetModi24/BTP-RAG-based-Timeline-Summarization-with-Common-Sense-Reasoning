87. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 20, 1936
MY DEAR MALKANI,
I have not heard from you for a long time as it seems to me.
How is the school going? Is Rukmini causing further trouble?
Please pass the enclosed on to Thadani. The article gives
another method [of] interpreting the Vedas. Is his theory making
any headway?
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 918
