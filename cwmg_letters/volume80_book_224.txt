224. LETTER TO JIVAJI RAO SCINDIA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 13, 1945
MAHARAJA SAHEB,
I congratulate you on what you have done for the Harijans.
I am confident that you will be taking the necessary steps to
see that your decision is implemented. It is in your hands to
educate the people to get rid of their superstitions. Otherwise
your decision will remain confined to the Gazette only. As you
may know I have said that Hinduism will perish if any kind of
untouchability persists.
May God grant a long life to the son on whose birth you
have done such a good deed, and keep him healthy physically
and mentally.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
MAHARAJA OF GWALIOR
GWALIOR STATE
