454. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
August 31, 1941
CHI. MANI,
I deliberately refrained from writing to you. I do not wish to
send you to jail just now. I will certainly send you when the time
arrives. Even being outside you are doing useful work. The time to
send you will certainly come. For the present go on doing service
without any other thoughts and improve your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MANIBEHN PATEL
68 MARINE DRIVE
BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 131
