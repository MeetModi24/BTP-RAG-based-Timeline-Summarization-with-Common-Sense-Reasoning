274. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
[December 10, 1929]
BHAISHRI DAHYABHAI,
I was very unhappy to hear first from Ramjibhai and then
from Thakkar Bapa that you scolded and sent back the Antyaja
workers whom, as you knew, Thakkar Bapa had accepted. I think
you have made a big mistake. Thakkar Bapa told me that you
asked for his forgiveness. You should have asked forgiveness of the
Antyaja men and women. If I am unfair to you in this, please
correct me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[Ps.]
Your report is not to be published.
From Gujarati: C.W. 2700. Courtesy: Dahyabhai M. Patel
