486. LETTER TO LAKSHMI SATYAMURTI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 7, 1941
DEAR LAKSHMI,
Tell father he had no business to fall ill as he has done. He
must learn to avoid anxiety and he will sleep well.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: S. Satyamurti Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
