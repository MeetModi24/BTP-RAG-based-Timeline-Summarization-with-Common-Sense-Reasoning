192. LETTER TO MAHALAKSHMI MADHAVJI THAKKAR
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 10, 1930
CHI. MAHALAKSHMI,
I got your letter. As your work increases, your handwriting
improves. You should improve it still further. You should not try
to give a flourish to the characters. For instance, the sign for the
sound "u" should be written thus—'<l and not thus—(\). If you
form the habit of writing each letter of the alphabet as it is printed,
your handwriting will appear very beautiful. I wish to see you
and all other women perfect in every respect. I do not exaggerate
when I say that I have put all my hopes in you women. I strongly
feel that the ultimate victory of non-violence depends wholly on
women.
Blessings from
BAPU
