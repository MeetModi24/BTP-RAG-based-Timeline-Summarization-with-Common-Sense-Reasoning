132. LETTER TO N. VYASATIRTH
November 16, 1945
BHAI VYASATIRTH,
I have your letter. You must be aware that ordinary
marriages no longer have any interest for me. I am interested,
if at all, in a caste Hindu marrying a Harijan. For, if we wish
to observe Varnashrama dharma, we should all belong to one caste,
i. e., of Harijans. And how else can we prove that we have
really become Harijans? But what if marriage between a Bhangi
and a Brahmin is not for observing dharma but for indulgence?
All the same you have my blessings if your marriage is for the
service of the people and if both of you live a life of self-restraint.
Blessings from
BAPU
