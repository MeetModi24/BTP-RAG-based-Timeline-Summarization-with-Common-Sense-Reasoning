540. LETTER TO TARABEHN JASWANI
June 30, 1928
CHI. TARABEHN,
After you reached Rangoon, I have had no letter at all from
you. Divali has written that she will send you a box spinning-
wheel and I have told them to send it to you. There must be at
least one spinning-wheel. Do you keep good health? One must
take good care. Recently a lot of changes have taken place here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8782
