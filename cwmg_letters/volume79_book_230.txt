230. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
SEVAGRAM,
February 17, 1945
CHI. BABUDI,
I read Gordhandas's letter. Since Anand is very ill Shakari-
behn is going there. She has been crying. Let her come. I do
hope that Anand will get well. Neither you nor Gordhandas
should worry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10053. Courtesy: Sharda G. Chokha-
wala
