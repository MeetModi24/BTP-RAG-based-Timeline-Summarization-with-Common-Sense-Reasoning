512. LETTER TO BENARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
SABARMATI,
Bhadra Krishnapaksha 12 [October 3, 1926]
BHAI BENARASIDASJI,
I have your second article. Kindly let me know when I ad-
mitted that it was an error on my part to have stated that 500
workers should be permitted to emigrate to British Guiana as an
experimental measure. I do not remember having said that.
If you can, please send the article in which I have said so. I will
publish your article along with that one.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
SHRI BENARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
FEROZABAD
E. I. R.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2566
