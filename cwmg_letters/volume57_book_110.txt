110. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
February 3, 1934
CHI. VALJI,
Having sent a wire to you, I didn't write. After reading
your letter I see that your coming may even be delayed. But I
assume that it will not be and, therefore, do not send the issues of
Harijan. My programme is enclosed. The earlier you come, the
sooner can your illness be tackled. You will find the work ready
for you. I had arranged to send you to Bihar, but now I will
not do that. With your present health it will be difficult for
you to work there.
The rest when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7462. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
