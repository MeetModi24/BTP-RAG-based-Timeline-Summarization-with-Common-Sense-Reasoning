455. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
WARDHA,
August 7, 1935
CHI. AMALA,
Your wonderful letter. Nobody believed that you were going to
shave your head and if you had, the hairs would have begun to
grow again. I hope the grandchildren are doing well.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
