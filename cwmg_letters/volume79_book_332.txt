332. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 3, 1945
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have also forwarded to Balasaheb, with my recommenda-
tion, your letter addressed to me. He may not come for a
month, in which case I have requested him to suggest another
name. Perhaps Kanaiyo can be of help there. He is of course
writing to him. Let us see what happens.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8621. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
