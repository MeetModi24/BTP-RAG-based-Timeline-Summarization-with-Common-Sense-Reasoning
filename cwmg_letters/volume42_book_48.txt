48. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
MUSSOORIE,
October 23, 1929
CHI. MAHADEV,
At long last I got the Gita proofs yesterday. Redirected from
place to place, they arrived very late. Now I shall be here today
and tomorrow only. I don't think it will be possible even to
have a look at them during that time. Both of you seem to have
taken a good deal of pains over the thing. How is Durga's health
nowadays? Has she resumed her kitchen duties? I hope she
does not exert herself beyond her strength.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11462
