386. LETTER TO APAJI AMIN
March 10, 1945
BHAI APAJI AMIN,
I have the postcard bearing your signature. I wish you
success. I knew Bhai Karunashanker well. He was a gem of
Gujarat. His services were matchless.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
[From Gujarati]
Vipravar Karunashankarne Shraddhanjali
