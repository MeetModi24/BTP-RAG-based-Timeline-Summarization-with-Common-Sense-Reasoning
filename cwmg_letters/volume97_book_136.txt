136. LETTER TO NIRMALA GANDHI
December 9, 1929
CHI. NIMU,
I got no opportunity at all to talk to you to my fill. I called you to massage
my legs so that I might talk to you but I was so tired that I soon fell asleep.
However, I had nothing more to say about Bachu than I did. You should go
1 The addressee's first child, a daughter, born in October 1929 and later named
