431. LETTER TO VAIKUNTHLAL L. MEHTA
CONTAI,
December 31, 1945
BHAI VAIKUNTHBHAI,
I got your postcard. You may gladly inform Khushal Shah
of your disinclination to join the sub-committee, adding at the
same time that, since Satis Babu has not tendered his resignation,
the question of your joining does not arise.
VAIKUNTHBHAI
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
