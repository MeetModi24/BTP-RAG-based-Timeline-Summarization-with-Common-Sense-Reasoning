163. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
February 5, 1942
CHI. LILI,
I have your letter. I do not like it at all that you are accepting
defeat. You will become fully qualified if you give one more year. Why
should you become a coward after having spent two years ? Hence,
you must complete the term and gain whatever knowledge you can
even if you are not appearing for the examination. Knowledge thus
gained will never go waste. You must become proficient in physics,
which is an interesting subject. And chemistry is a very useful subject.
If you acquire a good knowledge of it, it will be very useful in the
Ashram. Hence my advice is : Never give up even unto death.
It rained here one day. It is biting cold. The Goseva meeting took
my time.
Blessings from
BAPU
