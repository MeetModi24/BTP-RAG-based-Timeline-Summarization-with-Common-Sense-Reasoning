141. LETTER TO SIR MUNCHERJI BHOWNAGGREE
HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,
November 9, 1906
DEAR SIR MUNCHERJI,
I do not overstate when I say that probably you will be responsible
if some measure of success is gained by the Deputation. Immediately
Mr. Ally and I went to Sir Lepel Griffin, he told us he had received
your note and that he entirely agreed with you that the Deputation
should wait on Mr. Morley. He was most sympathetic and enthusiastic
and this is no doubt due to you.
I am now sending [a letter to ask an appointment with Mr. Morley.
Mr. Ally and I had half an hour with Lord George Hamilton who
was sympathetic but there was a ring of non possumus about all he said.
However, he has told us that he will carefully go through the Ordinance.
I am,
Yours truly,
SIR MUNCHERJI BHOWNAGGREE, K.C.S.I.
198, CROMWELL ROAD, S.W.
