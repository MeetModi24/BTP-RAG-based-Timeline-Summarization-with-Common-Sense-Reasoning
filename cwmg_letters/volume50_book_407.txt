407. LETTER TO MATHURADAS
August 20, 1932
The plan of voluntary dress-making for yajna is not intended
to provide the work of stitching khadi clothes for the poor. The
idea behind it is to promote quicker sale without incurring loss,
of khadi woven by the poor and to reduce its price which is felt to
be high. . . .2
Do not pay attention to carnal thoughts either. Once we
have made up our mind in regard to a thing, we should take it
as finally settled. A vow means that the mind stops thinking
about the thing concerning which we have taken the vow. When
a business man has concluded a deal, he does not think further
about it and directs his attention to other things. We should act
in the same manner with regard to vows.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I, pp. 372-
