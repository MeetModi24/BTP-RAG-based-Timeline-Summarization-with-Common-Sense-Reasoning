651. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
CHI. SUSHILA,
I am sending you this (Manilalbhai's letter). Nowadays I
mostly observe silence. There is a great load of work. God
carries it and hence I don't feel it so much.
The less one's
faith in Him the more one feels the burden. And I am certain
there can be no burden where there is perfect faith.
I take it
that all of you are well. My return to Sevagram has been post-
poned for the present.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. SUSHILA GANDHI
AKOLA
