341. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
May 29, 1945
MY DEAR C. R.,
Yours written on the train.
I am overwhelmed with work. Hence the delay, if delay
it may be called.
I agree with you about the existence of the evil. My diffi-
culty is as to the way to deal with it. I want you to depend
upon me to do so as soon as I see the way. Nothing will then
stop me. Nor am I unvigilant.
I am glad you liked your stay in Mahabaleshwar.
I hope Papa and Narasimhan came to you at once and
that you are doing well.
I am on your latest pamphlet.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2106
