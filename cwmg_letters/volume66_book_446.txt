446. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
SEGAON,
February 5, 1938
CHI. SHARDA,
Received your letter. The blood-pressure is somewhat
high, but there is no cause for anxiety. There is pressure of work
too. We shall leave for Haripura on the 8th. It is well that
you have had a waist-coat made for yourself. The body must
be kept warm. You must walk a lot. When you write to me
at Haripura address the letter to Congress Camp, Haripura.
Do you remain cheerful?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 9983. Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
