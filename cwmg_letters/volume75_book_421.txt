421. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
February 23, 1942
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got all your letters.
I sent Vinabehn yesterday under
Narahari's escort. He will bring her to you. I am hopeful that
she will prove a worthy worker.
If she works well, we will fix
some pay for her. Money must be remitted to Pyarelal's mother
regularly. Or is it necessary for her to ask for it? I do not
know the position.
I note that you have paid the money for
khadi. Do you still continue to do khadi work, or does it mean
that you have given it up? I hope Jamna is quite well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W.8599. Cour-
