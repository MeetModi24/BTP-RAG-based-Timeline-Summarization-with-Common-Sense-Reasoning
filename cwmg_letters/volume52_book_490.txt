490. LETTER TO KURUR NILKANTAN NAMBUDIRIPAD
January 5, 1933
MY DEAR NILKANTAN NAMBUDIRIPAD,
I thank you for your letter enclosing the considered opinion
of the Government Advocate on "Usage".
Yours sincerely,
SJT. KURUR NILKANTAN NAMBUDIRIPAD
T. C. HOUSE, TRICHUR
