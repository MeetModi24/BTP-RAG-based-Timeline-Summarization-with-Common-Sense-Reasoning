432. LETTER TO VICEROY
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 21, 1938
DEAR FRIEND,
I was much touched by your affectionate letter of good
wishes about my health. I delayed acknowledging it till I was
a little better. Though I have not crossed the danger zone, I
am much better than when your kind note was received.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M . K . GANDHI
H. E. THE VICEROY
NEW DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
