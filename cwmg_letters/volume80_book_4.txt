4. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 25, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I got your postcard of the 14th only today. I think Ram-
prasad must have left by now. You need not, therefore, go.
Take charge of the kitchen yourself. Put up cheerfully with
everybody's temperament. In that lies your victory. Servants
are not servants but our brothers and sisters. It is a virtue,
not a sin, to run the kitchen with their help. But one should
know how to do that. The servants should be treated as one's
brothers and sisters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8457. Also C.W. 5574. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
