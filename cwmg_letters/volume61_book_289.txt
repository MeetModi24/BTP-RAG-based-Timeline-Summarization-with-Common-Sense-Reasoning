289. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
June 26, 1935
CHI. MARY,
Chhotelal has just arrived. I read the paragraph about the
cooker to Mira. She remembered nothing about it. She has been
searching it, but till now it has not been found. Don't you
disturb yourself about it. If you can give me more particulars
my search will be facilitated, unless I have found the thing before
the arrival of your reply.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6049. Also C.W. 3379. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
