550. LETTER TO SARALABEHN
SODEPUR,
January 12, 1946
CHI. SARALA3,
I have your letter. It gives all the news. Do whatever your
health permits.
It is good that you informed me about the Nainital friend.
When it is turned into a hospital the house, I am sure, will
prove a great help to the poor.
I hope you have not forgotten your Urdu lessons. Ten
minutes devoted to a thing daily produce far better results than
hours spent on it irregularly; this is an unwritten law.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRIMATI SARALABEHN
LAKSHMI ASHRAM
[KAUSANI]
