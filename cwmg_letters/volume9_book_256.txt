256. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO LORD MORLEY
[LONDON,]
September 10, 1909
SIR,
I have your note of the 8th instant. I beg to enclose herewith, for
Lord Morley's information, copy of letter addressed by me to the
Private Secretary to the Earl of Crewe.
Yours faithfully,
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 5059
