183. LETTER TO KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
July 16, 1936
When did the pilgrimage become due? If you are merely
referring to Khan Saheb's manifesto, it contains no such message
for me. The incident is too trivial for me to notice. My undertaking
with the Brothers is that I am not to rush without a call from
them, unless I have an irresistible urge. I have none of the latter.
Saints are both real and false. The real ones never want
any noise round them but it does become their hard lot. The
false ones cannot live without noise.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
