68. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
November 20, 1936
CHI. MANUDI,
I had your letter. Now you are expected here any day.
Come and start singing bhajans for us immediately. There are
certainly numerous things to learn here if one wants to.
Kishorelal and Gomati are touring in Gujarat these days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 1561. Courtesy: Manubehn S. Mashruwala
