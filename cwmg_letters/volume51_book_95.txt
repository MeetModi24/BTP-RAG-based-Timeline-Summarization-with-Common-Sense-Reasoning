95. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS
September 17, 1932
Continue to write as if nothing is to happen. But it may be
that this will be my last letter to you. In that case bear in mind
that I expect you some day to gravitate to the Ashram and real-
ize all my hopes of you. As for the coming fast, I hope you
have understood that it is a rare privilege God has provided for
me. It is therefore a matter for rejoicing rather than sorrow.
At the same time it is not meant to be copied by anyone else. It
can be taken up when there is the clearest possible call from within.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, pp. 34-
