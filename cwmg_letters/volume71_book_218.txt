218. LETTER TO VIDYAVATI
ON THE WAY TO DELHI,
February 3, 1940
CHI. VIDYA1,
I hope Chi. Virendra is all right. Address your reply to Birla
House, New Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
RANI VIDYAVATI
5 SUDIRAM BUILDINGS
GUNGE NAWABKA PARK
AMINABAD, LUCKNOW
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers.
Courtesy: Gandhi
