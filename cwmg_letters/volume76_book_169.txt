169. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
May 24, 1942
MY DEAR KU,
Strange I was counting the days to see when you were com-
ing. Since you promise to labour you will be deemed worthy of
your hire.
I am glad B. has returned. It is good Sita has not returned.
June is the month when the hot winds stop. She is too frail
to take kindly to this heat.
It will be time before I can deal with your speech. I need
much peace for developing my theme.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10165
