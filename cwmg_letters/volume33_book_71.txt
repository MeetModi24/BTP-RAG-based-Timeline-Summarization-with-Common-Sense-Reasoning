71. LETTER TO SUSHILABEHN MASHRUWALA
Sunday [February 13, 1927]
CHI. SUSHILA,
Herewith the telegram I have from Manilal, and also a copy
of the letter I had written to him; so you will now understand
what I expect from both of you. God alone would know how
fortunate you are but Manilal, I think, has certainly been lucky
in getting you.
Get used to writing in ink. Try to improve your Gujarati
as much as possible. Write to me if you get the time. Remem-
ber you can always write to me frankly.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
My tour programme can be had from Nanabhai.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 1122. Courtesy: Sushilabehn Gandhi
