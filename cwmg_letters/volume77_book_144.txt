144. LETTER TO KANTILAL GANDHI
May 27, 1944
CHI. KANTI,
I have done what you wanted. Write a short letter of
thanks on behalf of both. Write, in Hindi, saying that you will
both try to be worthy of his generosity. Write to this effect, if
you don't mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KANTI GANDHI
1882- WESLEY ROAD
NEAR Bus STAND
MYSORE
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 7368. Courtesy : Kantilal
Gandhi
