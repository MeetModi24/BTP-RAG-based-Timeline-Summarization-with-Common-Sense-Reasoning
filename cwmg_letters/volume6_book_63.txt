63. LETTER TO H. COX
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 1, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I thrice attempted to see you at the House, and sent my card, but
I have not been fortunate enough to meet you. I enclose herewith a
letter given to me by Sir William Wedderburn. Sir Henry Cotton has
sent me a message that you have consented to join the deputation, for
which both Mr. Ally and I are extremely obliged to you. If you could
kindly give me an appointment, I shall wait upon you at the time given
by you and place the position before you. Lord Elgin has fixed Thurs-
day the 8th instant at 3 o'clock to receive the Deputation at the Colonial
Office. Sir Lepel Griffin has been asked, and has consented, to head
the deputation.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
Enclosure
HAROLD Cox, ESQ., M.P.
HOUSE OF COMMONS
LONDON
