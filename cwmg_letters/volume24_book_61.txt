61. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
Vaisakha Vad 6 [May 24, 1924]
BHAISHRI GHANSHYAMDAS,
The Mahars living in this place tell me that you have pro-
mised to pay them Rs. 30,000/- for constructing a temple and a
hostel, provided I approve of the proposal. Have you really told
them any such thing? The name of their leader is Bhosle.
Yours sincerely,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[PS.]
Please send your reply to Sabarmati. I shall reach there on
Thursday.
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6046. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
