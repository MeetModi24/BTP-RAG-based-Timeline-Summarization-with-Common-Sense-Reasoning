31. LETTER TO N. C. KELKAR
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 10, 1922
DEAR MR. KELKAR,
I have your letter.
As you know, the rumours are thick about my arrest, but if
I am free, I shall certainly come over to Bombay as soon as you
are ready. If I get my well-deserved rest, I know that you will
do all you can to further the movement. I have really very
little to say in addition to what I have said in my article "If I
am arrested" in Young India. I was in Ajmer yesterday, and I
have given some advice about the Khilafat which I may note
down, otherwise you will hear it from Mr. Chhotani and others.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. N. C. KELKAR
POONA
