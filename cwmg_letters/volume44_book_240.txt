240. LETTER TO V. G. DESAI
September 23, 1930
BHAISHRI VALJI,
You must be aware that formerly I used to write dates accord-
ing to our calendar. Later I realized that this insistence was wrong.
Everywhere outside India they use dates [according to the Grego-
rian calendar]. We must recognize this fact. Besides there is no
single calendar accepted throughout India. Different regions fol-
low different calendars. Surely we are not going to reject every-
thing foreign. There are other arguments too. But this would do
for the present. Let me know what you think is the right thing to
do. What are your activities just now?
Blessings from
BAPU
