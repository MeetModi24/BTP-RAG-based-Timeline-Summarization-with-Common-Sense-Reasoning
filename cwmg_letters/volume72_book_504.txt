504. LETTER TO SHAKRIBEHN C. SHAH
September 10, 1940
CHI. SHAKRIBEHN,
Why do you feel worried? Happiness and misery, health and
disease, youth and old age are always with the body. Who knows
which is the fruit of sin and which of good deeds? There is nothing
wrong in your staying on there. Come here whenever you wish.
You may start as soon as you get this letter so that you may
be free from worry. You have to bring Sharda with you. Every-
thing will be all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.G. 31
