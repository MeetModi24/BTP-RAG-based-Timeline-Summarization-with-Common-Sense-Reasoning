556. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
April 8, 1935
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
What else could Ansari have done? He can't refuse anybody,
rich or poor. He was once visited by a woman out to rob him,
and he emptied his purse into the fold of her sari. It will, there-
fore, be a mercy to him to free him.
Personally I am all in favour of the policy of doing silently
what we can and saying nothing to or begging nothing from the
Government.
Let them plunder Ras. We will get back every inch of the
land. When to have me there is entirely in your hands. But
please do not take me out in a procession. You may, if you wish,
have me at Borsad.
I hope you will not have a relapse.
Mani tells me in her letter that the nose has been giving
trouble. Is that true?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro‒2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 162
