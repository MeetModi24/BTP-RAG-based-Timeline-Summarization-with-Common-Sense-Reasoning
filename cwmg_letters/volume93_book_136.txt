136. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
September 16, 1936
CHI. LILAVATI,
I have your letter. If you have not understood even after
so many letters from me, how am I going to make you under-
stand now? Read all the letters again and act accordingly —
joyfully. I do not know how much and what kind of persua-
sion you want from me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have written this in great haste.
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
