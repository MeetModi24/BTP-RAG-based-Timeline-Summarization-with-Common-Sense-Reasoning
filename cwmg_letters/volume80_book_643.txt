643. LETTER TO JUGAL KISHORE BIRLA
June 28, 1945
BHAI JUGAL KISHORE,
I hope you have received the telegram I sent last night in
reply to yours. Just now I received Chi. Basant's letter and also
the note. I only hope and I pray to God that He may grant
you long life and good health to shoulder the cares of the world.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHETH JUGAL KISHORE BIRLA
NEW DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
