241. LETTER TO KHALIQUZZAMAN
SABARMATI,
January 9, 1930
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
Even before the Independence resolution Congress chairmen
of local boards have refused to give addresses. I am quite clear
that you should not present the address and therefore should
resign the chairmanship.
KHALIQUZZAMAN
LUCKNOW
From a copy : Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32579/45
