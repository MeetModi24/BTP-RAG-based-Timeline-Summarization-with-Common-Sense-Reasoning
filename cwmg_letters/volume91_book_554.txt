554. LETTER TO RAMKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
ON THE TRAIN,
Paush Krishna 5 [January 23, 1927]
DEAR RAMKRISHNADAS,
I have received your invitation. Convey my blessings to the
bride and the bridegroom.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
BHAI RAMKRISHNADASJI
KRISHNA NIVAS
KATRA KHUSHALRAI
DELHI
From the Hindi original : Brijkrishna Chandiwala Papers. Courtesy:
Gandhi National Museum and Library
