203. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
January 30, 1940
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Your letter states the truth. All of you should put your heads
together and find out a solution. The milk you are having you will
continue to get. It was not intended to harm anyone's health by
hasty reduction.
I notice no agitation in your letter, nor any exaggeration. I
had already experienced what you describe.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1928
