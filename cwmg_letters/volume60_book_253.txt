253. LETTER TO ADOLPH MYERS
WARDHA,
February 9, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I shall keep the copy of your magazine
by me. But it is hopeless for me to attempt to write anything for
you. I have not a moment to spare.
Yours sincerely,
ADOLPH MYERS, ESQ.
EDITOR, "SWORDS AND PLOWSHARES"
C/O TIMES OF INDIA PRESS, BOMBAY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
