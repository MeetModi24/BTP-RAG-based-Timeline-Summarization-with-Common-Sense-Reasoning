213. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C. P.,
June 2, 1942
MY DEAR C. R.,
Yours. Nothing will delight me more than to come near to you
[rather] than you to me. It seems to me that the time has come for
you to come here and convert me before you go on further with
your propaganda. It is ugly to find ourselves talking at each
other. I suggest that we talk to each other. It would be a
great tragedy if you cannot convert your best friend whose love
you do not doubt.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 10915. Courtesy: C. R. Narasimhan
