39. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
June 15, 1932
DEAR AGATRA 2 ,
I have your letter. You are quite right in scrupulously
honouring the restrictions. Indeed by a long course of habit I
have lost all curiosity. For a prisoner's curiosity can only be
idle, as he can do nothing even if he came to know certain things
through indirect or illegitimate means. And what is perhaps
more, I have abundant faith in friends doing their very best so
long as they believe in the justice of the cause.
Yes, Mahadev was able to see both his wife and son only last
week. He is entitled to a visit per month.
Love from us both.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1454
