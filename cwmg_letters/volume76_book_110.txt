110. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
May 3, 1942
CHI. KAKA,
You did not seem sufficiently interested yesterday. I do forget
small details.
I suggested your name but kept quiet when you
declined. You yourself should have suggested someone for Vice-
President. All that, however, can be rectified. All that we
wanted was to put the ship to sea.
We shall do something for
the Sammelan Committee. But only when Shriman comes. Won't
he? Carry on the correspondence. Ask Amritlal to send yester-
day's report.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10957
