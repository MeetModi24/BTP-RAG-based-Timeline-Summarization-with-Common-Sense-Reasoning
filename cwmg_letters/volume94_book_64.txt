64. LETTER TO PYARELAL
SEVAGRAM,
September 8, 1941
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have received your letter. I do not think it advisable for you
to have the operation in Wardha. If facilities are easily available in
Bombay or Delhi, I see no need to have it done in Wardha. I would
go by Sushila's decision in this matter. If she decides in favour of
Bombay, it does not matter if she has to waste two or three days.
But she would probably prefer Delhi. She can give more personal
attention there. In my opinion Sushila should reach Delhi by the 15th.
I would consider unavoidable the time spent in undergoing the operation.
One may take into consideration the effect it may have on others. But
I would treat it as of very little importance. The main thing is the effect
it may have on me and on you. I do not think it will make any difference
in this regard. I would not like to detain you with me. I would also
not like it if you stayed on there or in Delhi without any valid reason.
But so far, not a single day has passed like that. Hence, I am not
anxious.
Blessings from
BAPU
