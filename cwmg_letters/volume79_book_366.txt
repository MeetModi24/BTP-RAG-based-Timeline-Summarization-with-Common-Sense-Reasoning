366. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 7, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
There is no discourtesy in your question. Your question
shows how imperfect a medium language is.
If I stop sleeping together for all time it will mean that I
have been mistaken. Otherwise why should I stop it? There is a
221
limit to abstaining from it for the sake of friends. Sleeping to-
gether came with my taking up of brahmacharya or even before
that. Can you understand it now?
The dangers you have pointed out are certainly there. But
for that reason, a good act should not be avoided. Only we
should be vigilant.
The experiment is not meant to be imitated. But if I can
become a perfect brahmachari thereby, would I not be able to
contribute more to the welfare of the world? Even if there is a
single person who can train himself that way, he should do it. I
was not out for an experiment, but when the situation presented
itself before me I did what I felt was necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
