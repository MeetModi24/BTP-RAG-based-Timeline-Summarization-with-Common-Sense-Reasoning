248. LETTER TO GIRDHARILAL
March 28, 1933
DEAR LALA GIRDHARILAL,
I do not know Dr. Tripathi, nor does any of the other three.
But you do not want anybody to write to him, do you?
Prickly heat could be easily got over if the doctor would allow
you to take Kuhne-baths, otherwise called hip-baths, in which
only the hips remain under water and the legs are kept outside.
Perhaps you know that.
Yours sincerely,
LALA GIRDHARILAL
109-A MAHENDRA MANSIONS
ESPLANADE ROAD, BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 20729
