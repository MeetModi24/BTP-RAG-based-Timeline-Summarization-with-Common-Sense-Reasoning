507. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN CHOKSI
Bhadra Vad 11 [October 2, 1926]
CHI. MOTI,
I got your letter. Doing service there is your main duty and
it should make you completely happy. Take care of your
health.
You should improve your handwriting. Today was Mani's
birthday. She came to me on her own and took three vows in
my presence, not to tell an untruth, not to do mischief and to
get up at four in the morning. I will watch how long the child
can keep her vows.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12137
