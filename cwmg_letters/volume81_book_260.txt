260. LETTER TO SUSHILA PURI
August 23, 1945
CHI. SUSHILA PURI,
I have your letter. Study well, serve the people and do
whatever Mother tells you to do.
Blessings from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SUSHILA PURI
DEEP NIVAS
40 NISBAT ROAD
LAHORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
