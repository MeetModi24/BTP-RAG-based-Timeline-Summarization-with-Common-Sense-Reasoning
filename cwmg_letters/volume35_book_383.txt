383. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
[After January 27, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
Your letter. Babuji must have recovered by now. What can
I say to you? I am almost helpless.
I had been to Kathiawar for a few days. Mrityunjaya and
Vidyavati both accompanied me then. Their health is quite
good at present. The wedding of Ramdas and Nimubehn took
place on Vasant Panchami . It was all done in a very simple manner.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3340
