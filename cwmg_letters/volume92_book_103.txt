103. LETTER TO CHANDULAL
SABARMATI,
August 16, 1929
BHAISHRI CHANDULAL,
I have your letter. Real generosity lies in releasing a per-
son who does not apologize. We praise Tilak for not having
apologized to the last. Why may not this gentleman's case be
like his? Why should he apologize so long as he is not convinc-
ed of his guilt? You may continue your efforts. And be patient.
I want to co-operate with all the Native States, particularly
with Gondal, because I was attached to Gondal. I would be
very much pleased if most of what I have heard should turn
out to be false. And even if it is true, I shall be very happy if
I know that the Thakore Saheb has undergone a change of heart.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/117
