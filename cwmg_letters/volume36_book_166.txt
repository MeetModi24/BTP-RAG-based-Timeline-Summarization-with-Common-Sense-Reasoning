166. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 26, 1928
I have your letter. Before I decide anything I await Romain
Rolland's letter. The argument given in your letter to Mahadev I
had anticipated. But this is not a love letter. This is written to send
you the enclosed letter from Dr. M. E. Naidoo. Please deal with it
yourself and at once. I have told him that you would reply to it.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
SJT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
GANDHI ASHRAM
TIRUCHENGODU
From a photostat: S.N. 13131
