333. LETTER TO MAHARAJA OF MORVI
[After October 14, 1947]
I have both your telegrams. There was a time when I
could send replies to individual messages. I am no more in
that position, hence I have acknowledged them all through the
newspapers without mentioning names and places. The same
fate awaited those that came from abroad.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
