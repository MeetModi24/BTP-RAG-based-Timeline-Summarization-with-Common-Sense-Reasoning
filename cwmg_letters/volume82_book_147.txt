147. LETTER TO KHURSHED NAOROJI
ON THE WAY TO SEVAGRAM,
November 19, 1945
DEAR SISTER1,
Those who make mistakes with individuals cannot make
much success with causes; for the latter are never apart from in-
dividuals. What you want to say is that individuals are not to
be thought of apart from the causes they may represent or may
be induced to represent. But this is all nonsense. Let the God
of Truth alone guide us.
BAPU
[PS.]
I have no merchant princes. You are at the Conference;
send details to Kasturba Fund Secretary.
SHRI KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
82 DARYAGANJ, DELHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
