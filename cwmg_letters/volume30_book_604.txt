604. LETTER TO A. A. PAUL
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 30, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter enclosing copy of a letter from China. I
think I have already told you that if no local circumstances pre-
vent me, I would be prepared to visit China next year in accor-
dance with the desire of the Chinese friends.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 11372
