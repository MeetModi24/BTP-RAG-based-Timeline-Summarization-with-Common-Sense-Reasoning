312. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
July 2, 1942
CHI. KRI[SHNA]CH[ANDRA],
Let Sinhraj be there. I will see about Chandrashekhar.
I understand about Mohan Singh. I think it should suffice to
put some potassium permanganate in the water but it would be
better for those who have any doubt to boil it. Ask them. We
shall manage with some other cereal in place of wheat. We shall
live on potatoes and other roots if we do not get even bajra, jawar,
rice, etc., or, if the situation worsens still further, on whatever
God gives. However, everything should be given by weight.
If you can give facilities to Rajarao without much difficulty,
then do so.
Let the Ferozepur people stay.
Whatever is necessary should be done for Ramdas's friends.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4436; also S.N. 24485
