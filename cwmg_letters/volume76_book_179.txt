179. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
May 25, 1942
CHI. NARAHARI,
There is a report in the newspapers that burglars broke
into the Ashram and made away with some things. How far is
it true? 1 would not be surprised if something like this had really
happened. Do you remember the remedy 1 had suggested? 1
hope you do. We have not been able to employ it fully. No
time would be inopportune for making a start.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9127
