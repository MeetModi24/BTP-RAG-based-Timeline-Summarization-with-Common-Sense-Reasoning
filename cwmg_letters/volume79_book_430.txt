430. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
March 15, 1945
MY DEAR C. R.,
I have just learnt that G2. is unwell. I have written to
him. This is just to tell you that all is well. Mridula and
Bapa came here. More probably from them or later. Love to
you all. Hope L. 3 is well.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2104
1 For Bombay, to attend the Kasturba National Memorial Trust meeting
2 G. D. Birla; vide the preceding item.
3 Lakshmi Gandhi
