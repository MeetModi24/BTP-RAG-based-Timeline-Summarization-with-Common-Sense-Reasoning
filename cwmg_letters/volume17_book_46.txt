46. LETTER TO ESTHER FAERING
BOMBAY,
Wednesday [March 3] 1920
MY DEAR CHILD,
The case is going on, and as I have a few minutes' interval,
I want to give you a few lines.
You are constantly in my mind. Sometimes I even feel un-
easy when I think of our chats and when I think that in some
things I might have appeared harsh when I had wanted to be
gentle. One's speech cannot be judged by one's intentions but
only [by] the effect it produced on the hearer. Are you happy
and joyful? How are you in body?
I would like you to return Mr. Banker's trunk. If you need
another, you may take one in Madras. You will let me have your
programme, of course.
The case is finished and the judgment has been reserved. I
have sent you a telegram .
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
