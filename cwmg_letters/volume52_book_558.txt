558. LETTER TO SARAT CHANDRA GUHA
January 10, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of the 2nd instant. Upon hurried
reading I can find nothing to suggest and you will excuse me for
not giving more concentration to your letter. I have no spare time.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SARAT CHANDRA GUHA
KALIGHAT, CALCUTTA
From a microfilm: S.N. 19212
