371. LETTER TO SUSHILA GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
September 11, 1946
CHI. SUSHILA,
I had your letter and the newspaper cuttings. You will
see that I have made use of them. I shall be here till the
24th. Then off to Sevagram. Arun, Ila will be well. I also
have your letter of 3-9-1946. You are not to worry about what
will happen to Ila.
To you all,
Blessings from
BAPU
SUSHILABEHN GANDHI
AKOLA
