74. LETTER TO MOHAMMED SHAFEE
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
March 3, 1926
DEAR SHAFEE SAHEB,
I received your letter whilst Pandit Motilalji was here. I
showed it to him and discussed it with him. I showed your letter
also to Rajendra Babu. I have no doubt that the Frontier
Province should have the same treatment as any other province
but as Congressmen we may not ask for the extension of reforms
which we condemn as wholly unsatisfactory and inadequate.
I have written to Motilalji a letter which he must have
shown to you and other friends. It conveys fully my opinion
about this unfortunate trouble.
Yours sincerely,
MAULANA MOHAMMED SHAFEE
DELHI
