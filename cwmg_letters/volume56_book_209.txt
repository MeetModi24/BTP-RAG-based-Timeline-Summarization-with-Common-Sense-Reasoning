209. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
November 5, 1933
CHI. PANDITJI,
I manage to get news about you. I see that you have been
spending your time well. Here Lakshmibehn has been doing very
useful work. She is looking after most of the things. Though a
musician has joined, I have requested her to lead the bhajans, etc.,
during the prayers and she does so. On Thursdays and Fridays,
Mathuri sings the bhajans in her beautiful voice. I didn't know
that she could sing with such a full voice. Lakshmibehn is bound
to write about the other things, and so I don't mention them here
in order to save time. Kaka's seven-day fast ended on Thursday.
It was necessary. I assume you have heard everything. Chandra-
shankar, Mirabehn, Nayar and Jamnalalji's Om will accompany
me in my tour. Ramnath 'Suman', a friend of Haribhau's, also
will be one of the party. And of course Thakkar Bapa. The
tour will last for nine months without a break.
I don't get full reports about what Rambhau does in
Ahmedabad. He himself never writes. Probably you get some
news. Gajanan and Dhiru are doing fairly well. The former is
discontented. He says that he doesn't receive proper education.
Yoga seemed to be a fine girl. She is intelligent, too. I didn't
know she knew Gujarati so well.
Blessings to all from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 247. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
