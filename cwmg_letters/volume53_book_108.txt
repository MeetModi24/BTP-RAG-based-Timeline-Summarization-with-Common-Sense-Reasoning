108. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 18, 1933
MY DEAR AMTUSSALAAM,
I must hurry on to write to you. Therefore this English
letter. I would like you to make up your mind quickly as to
the treatment to be followed. My advice is, if you get a doctor
there who would take real interest in you, you should give him
the fullest chance by obeying all his instructions and doing as he
bids. That is to say, you should do as Veenabehn advises you.
There should be no time wasted in simply making a decision. You
should be in the hospital and take what medicine or Injection is
given to you and undergo the operation that may be needed.
Do not think of the removal of the womb unless the doctor says
so. Never mind the letter written to Dr. Sharma. You yourself
say that he cannot cure piles. After you are well you shall come
and see me.
Love. God may keep you well.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 272
