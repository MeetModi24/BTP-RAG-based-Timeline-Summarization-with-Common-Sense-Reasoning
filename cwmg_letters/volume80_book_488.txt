488. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
June 9, 1945
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
I have your letter. Bring Babaji down to simple diet. He
must get well somehow.
Money has been sent to Bhai Ramaswamy. That was meant
for his parents. I do not know if he has asked for anything else.
Should I take it that your health is perfect?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 9030. Also C.W. 9154. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
