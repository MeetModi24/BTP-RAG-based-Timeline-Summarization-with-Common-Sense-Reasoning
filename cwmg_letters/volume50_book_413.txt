413. LETTER TO GULAB
August 21, 1932
CHI. GULAB,
A general statement that your speed in spinning and making
slivers has increased tells the other person nothing. If you say
that now you spin 160 rounds instead of 150 or make seven in-
stead of five tolas of slivers per hour, that will tell him something.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1722
