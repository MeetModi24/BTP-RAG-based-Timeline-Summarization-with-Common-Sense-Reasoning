181. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
CAMP RAI BARELI,
November 14, 1929
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
I have nearly finished your monograph on Public Finance.
So far as I have gone I like it and I would like to publish it in
Young India and then perhaps separately in pamphlet form. If
you are agreeable please telegraph your assent, Anand Bhawan,
Allahabad.
This letter should be in your hands on Saturday.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
SJT. KUMARAPPA
GUJARAT VIDYAPITH
AHMEDABAD
From a photostat: G.N. 10084
