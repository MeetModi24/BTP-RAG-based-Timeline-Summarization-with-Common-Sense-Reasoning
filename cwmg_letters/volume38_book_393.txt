393. LETTER TO THE SECRETARY, BEE-KEEPERS'
ASSOCIATION
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 12, 1929
THE SECRETARY
BEE-KEEPERS' ASSOCIATION
SANAWAR (PUNJAB)
DEAR SIR,
We in the Satyagraha Ashram are interesting ourselves in the
question of bee-keeping. We have learnt about the Bee-keepers'
Association from the publication of the Punjab Agricultural De-
partment entitled A Guide to Bee-keeping. We will be obliged
if you would kindly let me know whether the Bee-keepers' Asso-
ciation at Simla mentioned in the book is still in existence and
whether someone from the Ashram can go there for receiving
practical instructions in bee-keeping.
Thanking you in anticipation,
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 14984
