363. LETTER TO T. B. KESHAVA RAO
December 24, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am quite clear that there should be no
fast by anybody in connection with temple-entry in any place till
the Guruvayur question is finally settled. In the meantime a per-
sistent but gentle propaganda should be carried on for convincing
the orthodox people. I have written to Sjt. G. V. Narasimhachar
asking him to give up the idea of the fast.
SJT. T. B. KESHAVA RAO
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SECRETARY, DISTRICT ANTI-UNTOUCHABILITY LEAGUE
BRAHMINS STREET, BELLARY
From a photostat: G.N. 160
