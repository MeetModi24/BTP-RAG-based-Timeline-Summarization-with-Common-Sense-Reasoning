522. LETTER TO CHAMPA R. MEHTA
SARANIA ASHRAM,
January 10, 1946
CHI. CHAMPA,
I got your letter here last evening. You seem to have
succeeded very well. Kanta of course is a very obliging lady.
She knows the art of looking after children. I am, therefore,
not surprised by what you say. I am only pleased. How is
Sarala? How is Kanta's own health? Ask her to write to me.
You have said nothing about Shanti. I trust the children
are all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8759. Also C.W. 1046. Cour-
tesy : Champa R. Mehta
