516. LETTER TO J. N. SAHNI
December 10, 1934
MY DEAR SAHNI,
I wonder how far you have answered the "National Call" and
respected private friendship by publishing unfinished and
unauthorized garbled version about the correspondence I am
carrying on with the authorities. If you think you have
advanced the national cause thereby, I have nothing to say. It
would have been wise, if you had referred the matter to me
as sometimes even strangers do.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI J. N. SAHNI
"NATIONAL CALL"
DELHI
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
