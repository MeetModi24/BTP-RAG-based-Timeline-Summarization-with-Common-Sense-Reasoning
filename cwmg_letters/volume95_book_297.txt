297. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
Silence day
CHI. MAHADEV,
This is not right. There is no question of your finding the time.
Does a carpenter have to find time for his son? The son works with
him and becomes like him. It is true, of course, that you have to be
an ideal father. This thought should help you to have sound sleep.
