379. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
WARDHA,
July 19, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
Your letters can't tire me, for I am interested in your true
happiness.
You may send the little bigger size cooker through some-
one coming. There is no immediate hurry.
I am glad you have a good daughter-in-law.
I shall be relieved when I hear that Kichi's finger is com-
pletely healed.
I hope Father is keeping good health.
Love.
Blessings from 2
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9603. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
