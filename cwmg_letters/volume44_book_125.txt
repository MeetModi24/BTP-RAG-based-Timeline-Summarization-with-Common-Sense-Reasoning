125. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 18, 1930
CHI. RAIHANA,
Khuda Hafiz1,
Your Gujarati letters are wonderful. Do continue to write to
me. It is fine indeed that you taught prayer songs to Mrs. Lukmani's
monkey-army. Take care of your health in everything you do. I
have not seen your card and public letter.
Which Sirat is Father translating? Convey my Vandemataram
to Mother, as also to other members of the family who may be
there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9619
