342. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Sunday morning
CHI. VASUMATI,
I learnt about your release. About [your] meeting [me] I learnt
from your letter to Gangabehn. If I write more I would miss the
posting time or the sleep which is weighing my eyes down.
Blessing from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : S.N. 9380.
