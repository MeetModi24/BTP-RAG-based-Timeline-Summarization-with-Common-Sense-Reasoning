276. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 24, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
You must have got the two letters sent with the doctor.
Write to Narahari and tell him that Parnerkar cannot be
spared. Apart from the moral consideration, he is busy at pre-
sent in the farming and the co-operative dairy experiments. If he
wants, he may have the man sent by Trivedi. You may send
the thing to Jugatram over your signature.
Write to Krishnan
and ask him to keep me informed. The Trivandrum affair was
well settled. I did have fears about Khare. The cart must have
reached by now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11613
