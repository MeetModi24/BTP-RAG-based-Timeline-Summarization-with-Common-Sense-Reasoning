290. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
March 15, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
It is just now 12.40 a.m. The alarm that should have gone
off at 2.30 a.m. went off at 12 midnight.
Having got up, I am
attending to arrears at an affected place in Bihar.
That many Hindus are callous to the sufferings of animals
is but too true.
It is a mark of degradation and lifelessness of
the religious spirit. You do not need to be a Hindu but a true
Jewess. If Judaism does not satisfy you, no other faith will give
you satisfaction for any length of time. I would advise you to
remain a Jewess and appropriate the good of the other faiths.
My weight is 107; the b.p. has not been taken for days now.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
