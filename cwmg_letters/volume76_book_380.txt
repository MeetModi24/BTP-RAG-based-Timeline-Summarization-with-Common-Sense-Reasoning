380. LETTER TO ASAF ALI
July 28, 1942
MY DEAR ASAF ALI,
Many thanks for your letter. The propaganda you refer
to is vicious. I am going to notice it in Harijan.
As to the charges against me, it has been my lot for the
past 50 years to be misunderstood. This last attack is not sur-
prising. I shall, however, see what is to be done. Both the Cong-
ress and I will survive the venom.
My love to you both.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 36
