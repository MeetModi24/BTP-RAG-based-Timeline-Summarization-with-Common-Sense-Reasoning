227. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
Chaitra Sud 5 [April 6, 1927]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I have been getting your letters. There is absolutely no
breach of vow in your going to Chi. Mama. A visit on such an
occasion was naturally expected. Now you should return to your
place in the Ashram only after Chi. Mama is completely at
rest. You should make Chi. Mama learn this formula: It is
none of our concern whether the body falls or lasts, the soul
cannot depart. Why should we bother about the body? We
should be satisfied with thought of the immortal sel£
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am fine.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 8825. Courtesy: Gangabehn Vaidya
