195. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
Monday [June 29, 1925]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have your letter. I have written something quite different-
ly about Alwar this time. I am afraid it will take a little time
to decide about my going there. I think it will be possible to go
only in the beginning of August. I intend to pass the last week of
July in the Ashram and then start the tour. I am sure you will
come on the 16th. I hope you received the wires I sent there
to you and to Sabarmati.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati original: G.N. 2854
