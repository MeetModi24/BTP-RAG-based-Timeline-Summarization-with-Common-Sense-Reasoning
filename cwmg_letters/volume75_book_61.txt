61. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
[October 22, 1941]
CHI. BABUDI,
Why do you fall ill? If your diet is correct and you have
enough fresh air nothing should happen. Do you drink water
after boiling it? Do you know how to recite Ramanama?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.G. 128
