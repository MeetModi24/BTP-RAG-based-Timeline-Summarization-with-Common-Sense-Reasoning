327. LETTER TO ADA WEST
October 5, 1932
MY DEAR DEVI,
So you fasted! Just like you. I am regaining my lost strength
rapidly. It was all a rich experience. I have gone through many
and more prolonged fasts. But none was so joy-giving.
It seems that the work there is too trying for you. Of course
you must not work beyond your strength.
Ba and Devdas I see frequently nowadays. Manilal must be
reaching Bombay in a day or two. Poor boy! He is coming
to see me dying. He will have the disappointment of his life.
Ba has aged.
Love.
BHAI
From a photostat: C.W. 4435. Courtesy: A. H. West
1 The source does not mention the addressee, but in "Diary, 1932", under
the date October 5 Standenath is listed among those to whom Gandhiji
wrote letters. Satyavan, mentioned in the letter was addressee's husband,
Frederic Standenath.
