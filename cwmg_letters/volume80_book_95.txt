95. LETTER TO RAGHUVIR SAHAY
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 2, 1945
BHAI RAGHUVIR SAHAY,
Why do you write to me in English? Your daughter is get-
ting married in your own community, isn't she? Why do you seek
my blessings for such a marriage? You know-don't you?-that
I am a votary of inter-caste marriages. I like this marriage but
please spare me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAGHUVIR SAHAY
ADVOCATE
BADAUN
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
