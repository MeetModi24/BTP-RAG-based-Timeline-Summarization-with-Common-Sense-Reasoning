322. LETTER TO GIRIRAJ KISHORE BHATNAGAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 27, 1945
CHI. GIRIRAJ,
I had given you a prompt reply. This is the second letter.
What you are doing is good. I was happy to hear about the
children. I had made a few more inquiries.
I hope your
health is perfectly good. I shall go to Panchgani on the 31st.
Nanavati has reached Mysore.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GIRIRAJ
HINDUSTANI PRACHAR CLASS
ENGLISH SCHOOL
NAPAD, via ANAND
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
