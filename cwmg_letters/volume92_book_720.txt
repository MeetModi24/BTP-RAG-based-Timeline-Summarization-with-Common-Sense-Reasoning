720. LETTER TO CHANDRAKANTA
November 26, 1933
CHI. KANTA,
Are you getting well? Are you satisfied? What news about
Sumangal? Ask Prabha to write to me. Tell my walking sticks,
Yashodhara, Lambus, Jekore Meera, etc., that they should
not expect separate letters from me, but that they will get
replies from me if they write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: Chandrakanta Papers. Courtesy:
Gandhi National Museum and Library
