439. LETTER TO V. G. SAHASRABUDDHE
SEVAGRAM,
March 16, 1945
BHAI SAHASRABUDDHE,
Bring the girl before the 24th. What will Balasaheb be able
to do? Explain to me when you come. Remember my time is 3
o'clock.
Blessings from
BAPU
V. G. SAHASRABUDDHE
MAHAL, NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
