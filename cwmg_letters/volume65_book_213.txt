213. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
May 17, 1937
CHI. AMRITLAL,
I hear from Anna and Munnalal that you are not keeping
well. That ought not to be. Make whatever changes in your
diet you think might help. Reduce your work. If this does
not help, then go to some cool place. If you think it desira-
ble to go to Malad, you may come back when the rains start.
If your health is good, I will get plenty of work out of you.
If you keep weak, I will hesitate to entrust any work to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10731
