442. LETTER TO RANJITSINH HARBHAMJI
POONA,
September 14, 1945
BHAI RANJITSINHJI,
I have your letter. It is rather strange that often one can-
not distinguish between a friend and a foe. Another strange thing
is that you make yourself a foreigner by not writing in your mother
tongue. Why are you deliberately ruining yourself?
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RANJITSINHJI HARBHAMJI
RAVA VILAS
RAJKOT
KATHIAWAR
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
