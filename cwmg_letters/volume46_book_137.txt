137. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
BORSAD,
May 8, 1931
CHI. JAMNALAL,
Tell the people of Karnatak that they have already contri-
buted much to the struggle; they must also now take part in the
constructive work. Much still remains to be done for khadi; the
boycott of foreign cloth is for the sake of khadi. Had the boycott
not had the purpose of serving the poor, at least I would not have
been as absorbed in it as I am now.
Some friends from Karnatak are possessed with the idea that
Karnatak should form a separate province. Why do they worry?
The Congress has already formed a province of Kannada-speak-
ing people, it will come into being when we have swaraj.
I am happy that Lingayats and others have come together.
It is as it should be.
MOHANDAS
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, pp. 73-
