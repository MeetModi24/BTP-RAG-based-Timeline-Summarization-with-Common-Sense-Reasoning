158. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI G. PATEL
August 9, 1945
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
Do you also hanker after blessings? Of what avail are they, if
you do your work well? If you do not, they will never make up
for the deficiency. You should know that.
Blessings to all from
BAPU
SHIVABHAI PATEL
VALLABH VIDYALAYA
BOCHASAN, KHEDA
From a photostat of the Gujarati : S.N. 9520. Also C.W. 439. Courtesy :
Shivabhai G. Patel
