293. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
March 26, 1932
CHI. DEVDAS,
I received today permission to write to fellow-prisoners, and
hence this letter. I think of you every day. Mahadev has been
brought here. All three of us are well. More after I hear from
you in reply to this. Give me news about your health, your
reading and your companions. I write to Lakshmi regularly and
receive her letters written in beautiful Hindi.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2022
