368. LETTER TO MULCHAND PAREKH
October 9, 1932
Send a report and accounts to Thakkar Bapa and request him
for help. While the movement for self-purification is in full
swing, take a pledge that you will sell yourself and your be-
longings but will not let any school or Ashram be closed for want
of funds. Do not reconcile yourself to the inability of Kathiawar
to collect such a small amount. You have dedicated yourself to
this work. You ought not to give up the task in despair so soon.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 118
