341. LETTER TO UTTIMCHAND GANGARAM
September 1, 1945
DEAR UTTIMCHAND,
Yours with cheque with thanks. It will be used as you have
directed, i.e., to pay for defect in interest and for a purpose in
khadi that would have pleased Mahadev if he was in our midst
in his body. If I do not remember rightly please correct me.
I have your magic square and puzzle. I must tax myself
and friends.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
It is yet too early to say anything definite about Sardar.
SHRI UTTIMCHAND GANGARAM
BOMBAY BAKERY
HYDERABAD (SINDH)
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
