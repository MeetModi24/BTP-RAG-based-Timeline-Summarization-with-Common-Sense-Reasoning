223. LETTER TO A GIRL
March 12, 1932
CHI.,
Learning the Gita by heart only requires a good memory, and
the task is easy. But understanding the meaning calls for the use
of one's intellect. This is a difficult thing, and, therefore, uninte-
resting. When, however, you feel interest in exercising your in-
tellect, the desire to understand the meaning will come to you.
You should, therefore, learn to take interest in subjects which
require you to use your intellect.
Blessings from
BAPU
