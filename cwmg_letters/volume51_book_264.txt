264. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
[October 1]2, 1932
BHAISHRI MUNSHI,
I had got your letter overflowing with love. I am using the
strength which I am regaining to reply to such letters. The outcome
of the fast was not man's achievement. I do not even feel that
'I' have done something. The Gita says: "Do everything as My
instrument, and offer it to Me as sacrifice." I feel thus every
moment and experience the intensest joy. When you write to Lila-
vati, tell her that I often think of both of you.
Sardar and Mahadev are very well. Convey our vande-
mataram to all friends there.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 7517. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
