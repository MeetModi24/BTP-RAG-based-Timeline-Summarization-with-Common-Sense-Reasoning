322. LETTER TO SHYAMLAL
SEVAGRAM,
December 11, 1944
DEAR SHYAMLALJI,
I have your letter of 2nd inst. and the enclosures.
As desired by Bapu I have written to Dr. Dharam Prakash to send his accounts
as he had offered to. His reply is being awaited. Bapu would get the accounts
scrutinized when they arrive.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI SHYAMLALJI
HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH
[CAMP] KINGSWAY
DELHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
