469. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
September 19, 1945
CHI. NARANDAS,
I like the above statement. I feel that swaraj lies in its
complete observance.
This is in reply to your postcard, for publication with the
statement or below it.
I will of course send something with Kakasaheb.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Kanaiyo is in Bombay. I hope you have recovered.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8630. Cour-
tesy : Narandas Gandhi
