611. LETTER TO FULCHAND B. SHAH
November 9, 1932
BHAI FULCHAND,
This time I got your letter later than I had expected, but
I am relieved now that I have got it. I had heard about the
illness there, and hence I was all the more eager to hear from
you. I do get reports from Ahmednagar. All the prisoners are
now out of danger. They are being looked after well enough.
Gangabehn and others even visited them. All the three of us are
well here. I have almost regained my strength.
Devdas is in Prayag and Pyarelal is in Bombay. Jamnalal-
ji keeps moderately good health. He is suffering from some ear
disease, but there is no cause for anxiety. Ba is at Sabarmati.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9466. Courtesy: Chandrakant F. Shah
