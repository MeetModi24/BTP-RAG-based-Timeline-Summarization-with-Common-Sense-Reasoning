161. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
December 15, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
Ramachandran has become impatient. It is necessary to con-
sult you before sending him a wire. I am in need of an annual
diary for noting down appointments. Haven't you received any?
I also want a wall calendar. This was a digression. I would
certainly like to solve the Trivandrum problem quickly if no date
has been given to anyone. Guide me. We shall reply tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11513
