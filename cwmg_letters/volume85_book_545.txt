545. LETTER TO GENERAL MANAGER, G.I. P. RAILWAY
"VALMIKI MANDIR", READING ROAD,
NEW DELHI,
October 7, 1946
THE GENERAL MANAGER
GREAT INDIAN PENINSULA RAILWAY
BOMBAY
DEAR SIR,
I thank you for your letter No. 20435-V 395 of 28th ultimo.
My suggestion is that the driver himself should be asked as
to what article or articles of use he would want and you or the
1 The addressee had said that he had received a further sum of Rs.
1200 to be given as a reward to the driver whose vigilance had averted a

Railway Board should decide what should be done. The donors
will have no opportunity of coming to a correct decision and

Yours faithfully,
