111. LETTER TO RAMJI G. BADHIA
February 6, 1932
BHAISHRI RAMJI,
Your letter made me very happy. Your decision to follow
Shankerlal's advice is certainly very good. I expect much from
you. I felt relieved to know that Jivanlal's health was all
right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./III
