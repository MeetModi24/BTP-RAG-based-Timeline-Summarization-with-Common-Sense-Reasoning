55. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
October 4, 1927
CHI. MIRA,
I have not omitted to write to you a single day after you
left me but this may not reach you the day after the letter of
yesterday (Monday) for now that I am going further South the
distance between you and me is growing. There is however just
a chance of this reaching you the day after yesterday's letter.
It is too warm just now to let me write more and I must prepare
for the meeting that is presently coming off. Heat notwithstand-
ing, I am keeping quite well. Are you?
With love,
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 5283. Courtesy: Mirabehn
