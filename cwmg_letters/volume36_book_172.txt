172. LETTER TO M. DEWANDAS NARAINDAS
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 28, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your two letters. I shall need much more information
than you have given in your letter before I can place your name
before the Managing Board. You must state your age, whether
your parents are alive, what is your future aim. In no case can
you be admitted until you have tested yourself in Karachi for at
least 6 months
(a)
by first spinning for at least one hour daily from slivers
carded by yourself;
(b) by learning, if you do not know it, Hindi so as to be
able to speak and write correctly;
(c)
by wearing khaddar to the exclusion of all other cloth;
(d) by securing the free permission of your parents.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. M. DEWANDAS NARAINDAS
STU. STD. VII
NEW HIGH SCHOOL
KARACHI
