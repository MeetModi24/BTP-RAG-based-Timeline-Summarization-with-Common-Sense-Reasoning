464. LETTER TO K. NAGESWAR RAO
January 3, 1933
MY DEAR NAGESWAR RAO,
I enclose a telegram received by me. If you know Sjt. Kasi
Krishnamacharya, I would not adopt the suggestion of the sender
of the telegram and drag him all the way to Poona, but if you will
give his opinion on the same question put by me I should value
it, and if you get the opinion please tell me also briefly who Kasi
Krishnamacharya is.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19146
1 From The Hindustan Times, 4-1-1933
