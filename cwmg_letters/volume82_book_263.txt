263. LETTER TO V. V. SAXENA
SODEPUR,
December 8, 1945
BHAI SAXENA,
Kishorelalji has forwarded here your letter addressed to
him. I shall not be able to reach Sevagram before February
in any case. It will be best if you write to me what you want
to discuss with me. In this way both of us will save time—
and money of course.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
PROF. V. V. SAXENA
VANASTHALI VIDYAPITH
VANASTHALI (JAIPUR)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
