427. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
January 8, 1947
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I was glad to have your letter. Nothing but dharma can
help you. If it is God's will that we should meet in person while
doing our duty, we shall certainly meet. Not otherwise. How
does it matter even if we don't? We have never been separate
spiritually nor shall we ever be. Whatever happens, remain
happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 584
