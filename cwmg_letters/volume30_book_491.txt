491. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Thursday, May 6, 1926
CHI. RADHAKRISHNA,
I read your letter to me and the one to Shankarrao. I am
sure your rule about eating salt-free food is not for all time. Its
significance lies in that it reduces the keenness of the palate. This
is the main point to bear in mind, and, therefore, as a guest at
someone's place you may accept whatever simple food is offered.
You may leave aside any item on the menu, with or without salt,
which is not acceptable to you. But the three items-milk, boiled
rice and chapatties-can be had anywhere. You may leave aside
curry or vegetables which contain chillies and spices. Where hand-
ground flour is not available, you may use mill-ground flour with-
out any hesitation. When the road surface is very hot and in a
place strewn with thorns, you should certainly use shoes, which in
fact are called kantarakhan 1 or pagarakhan 2 . You should not hesi-
tate to write to me whenever you are faced with such conflicts of
duties. Take very good care of your health.
