272. LETTER TO NAWAB SAHEB
POONA,
August 25, 1945
DEAR NAWAB SAHEB,
I am sorry we could not meet early.
How nice it would
have been if we could have discussed things of common interest!
Let me hope it would be still possible.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
(By HAND) SHUAIB QURESHI
POONA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
