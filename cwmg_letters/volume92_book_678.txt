678. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
May 7, 1933
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I can understand your suffering. Keeping my company is
disastrous. I myself am wholly in other hands, not knowing
what will happen the next moment. I feel my freedom as slav-
ery and slavery to the God of Truth as freedom. I did not
have the slightest desire to undertake a fast, and yet I have
had to do so. But now that I have undertaken one, the load that
weighed on my mind is lifted in a way that defies description.
You should therefore rejoice at the fast. Nothing untoward will
happen to my body, but what even if my faith proves misplaced?
In that case you may know that there was no further service to
be taken from this body. Do not therefore feel troubled. Keep
your mind fixed on the thought that the body is more fragile than
a glass bangle and that the atman which inhabits it is the only rea-
lity, and go on doing whatever service you can. Just now Shankerlal
and you should rest. Come during the last days of the fast.
Blessings from
BAPU
