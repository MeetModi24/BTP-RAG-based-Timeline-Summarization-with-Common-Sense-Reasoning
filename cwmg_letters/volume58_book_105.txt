105. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
June 18, 1934
CHI. JAMNA,
I got your letter. I didn't get the previous one. But
Prithuraj tells me that it was given to me. You have not said
in your letter how you are faring. I cannot write more just
now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 885. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
