437. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
February 17, 1933
I got your letter. You will see from the Harijan that it is
not very necessary now to go to Panditji. We should not mind
that the difference has come out in the open. . . .
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 120
1 Between Gandhiji and Malaviyaji over the untouchability Bills
2 Omission as in the source
