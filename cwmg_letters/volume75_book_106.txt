106. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 3, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
It will be nice to make the help to Harijans dependent upon
their conforming to your conditions. You should raise a whole-time
worker there.
Your dream was strange. Sometimes these do come. I attach
no importance to them.
When you come you will miss Raihana. She has become a
member of the family and sings every evening. She has taught
some a few verses from the Koran. She sings the shlokas correctly.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4112. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7421
