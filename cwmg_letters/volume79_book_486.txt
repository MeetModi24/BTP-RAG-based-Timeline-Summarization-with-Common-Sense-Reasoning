486. LETTER TO KHURSHED NAOROJI
SEVAGRAM,
March 22, 1945
VAHALAN BEHN,
I have your letter per friend. I answered all your previous
letters. I do hope you had my replies. I can assure you that
not a moment is wasted and taken away from the constructive
programme. I am dealing with the post as it is brought to me
without leaving any arrear.
Love to you all.
BAPU
SMT. KHURSHEDBEHN NAOROJI
DUNLAVIN LODGE
POONA 5
From a copy ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
