646. LETTER TO ABDUL GHAFFAR KHAN
October 15, 1945
BHAI BADSHAH KHAN,
There must be plenty of bustle because of the elections.
Will it be proper for me to go there at that time? Or shall I
come later? I don't know when I will be able to make it.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
BADSHAH KHAN
CHARSADDA
FRONTIER PROVINCE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
