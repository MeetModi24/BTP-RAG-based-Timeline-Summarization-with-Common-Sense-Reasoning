600. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 1, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I sent you a long letter posted this morning with late fee. It was
written last night.
Your brief note today. So you got three letters at a time. They
answer all your questions. I have sent an appointment for 11th for
Hancock. He has now sent a long wire for a wire to be sent to
his paper. How can I cope with such work? I am trying.
Herewith a letter.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4088. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7397
