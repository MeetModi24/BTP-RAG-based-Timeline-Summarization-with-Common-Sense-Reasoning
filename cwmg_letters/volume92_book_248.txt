248. LETTER TO MOTILAL NEHRU
SABARMATI,
January 20, 1930
DEAR MOTILALJI,
I have your letter. I did not realize you were so bad. In the
circumstances there is no occasion for touring. After all we have
said our say. Let those who wish seek election.
I do not think they will begin arrests so soon. But if they do,
all the better. They are not likely to take all of us at the same
time. If they do and if they put us all together, we shall have
a rare time of it.
I am giving careful hints in Young India and Navajivan.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[PS.]
I am not writing to Jawaharlal today. The messenger is being
detained for the time being.
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32579/57
