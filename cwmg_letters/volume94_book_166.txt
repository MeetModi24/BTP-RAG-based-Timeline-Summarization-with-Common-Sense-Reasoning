166. LETTER TO KAMALADEVI CHATTOPADHYAYA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C. P.,
February 26, 1942
DEAR KAMALADEVI,
I reached your letter of 12th only today. You had no business to
have the accident. I hope, however, you are out of the wood now.
Of course, you will come when you like.
Yes, Jamnalalji's death is a personal loss to many of us.
Love.
Yours,
BAPU
From the original : Kamaladevi Chattopadhyaya Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
