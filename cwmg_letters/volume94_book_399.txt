399. LETTER TO EVAN JENKINS
SEVAGRAM,
August 4, 1945
DEAR SIR EVAN,
I wonder if it is possible to send this in the Viceregal bag ?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR EVAN JENKINS
P. S. To H. E.
VICEROY'S HOUSE
NEW DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
1
Presumably the letter to Lord Pethick-Lawrence of even date
congratulating him on his appointment as Secretary of State for India; vide
Vol. LXXXI. p. 69.
