634. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
UTMANZAI,
October 13, 1938
CHI. BABUDI,
Lately I have not had any letters from you. I would surely
expect them even though I am extremely busy. How is your
health? Is your mind at peace? It has started getting quite
cold here at night. It has not had any adverse effect on me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 10000. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokhawala
