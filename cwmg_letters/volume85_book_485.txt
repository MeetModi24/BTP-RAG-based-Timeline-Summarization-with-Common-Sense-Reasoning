485. LETTER TO INDUMATI G. TENDULKAR
NEW DELHI,
September 27, 1946
CHI. INDU,
I have your letter. May you be happy in marriage and do
great service. Come to Sevagram when you can. For the pre-
sent I shall be here.
Blessings from
BAPU
