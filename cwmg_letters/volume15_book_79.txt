79. LETTER TO INDIAN NATIONAL CONGRESS
December 26, 1918
I regret being unable to attend the Congress this time. My
health is too poor. I hope delegates from both sides will be there
at the Congress. I pray to God that the Congress may succeed in
its labours.
MOHANDAS KARAMCHAND GANDHI
[From Hindi]
Pratap, 30-12-1918
