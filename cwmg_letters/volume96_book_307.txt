307. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
WARDHA,
August 16, 1937
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
What you have done is all right. I had a long talk with Andrews.
I do not know what he will be able to do. The more I observe the
events happening, the more convinced I feel of the correctness of my
advice. But it is likely to be a voice in the wilderness. Nevertheless,
if you feel as strongly as I do, you will take up the firm and only
stand that is likely to do good in the end. Without that there will be
no happy home for the Jews in Palestine. No more today. See the
enclosed.
Love.
UPPER HOUSE
