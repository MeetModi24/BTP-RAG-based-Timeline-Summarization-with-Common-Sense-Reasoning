401. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
June 23, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have received your letter. I slept a lot last night. Yes,
Ba is unhappy. She has a cough. For her sake your absence
irks me. Be here by Sunday if you can do so.
Have I told you that we are co-existing with snakes and
scorpions? God protects us. Have you got the antidote for
snake-bite?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
