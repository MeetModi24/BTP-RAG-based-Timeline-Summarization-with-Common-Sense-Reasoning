113. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
HUDLI,
April 20, 1937
DEAR C. R.,
Personally I think it is better to ignore Rajah's letter. But
you may take notice of it, if you think it necessary in the public
interest. No time for more as the bell for attending morning
prayer has gone.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
49 FAZALULLAH ROAD
TYAGARAYA NAGAR
MADRAS
From a photostat: G.N. 2061
