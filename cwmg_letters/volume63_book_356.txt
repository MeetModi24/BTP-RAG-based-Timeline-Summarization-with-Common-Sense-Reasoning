356. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 15, 1936
MY DEAR IDIOT,
I am glad your thumb seems to be quite restored. But I
shall wait. The oozing must be stopped, not merely suppressed.
Please do not trifle with it. I do not mind your feeling lazy
and not taking up routine work. But you must not deceive your-
self and imagine yourself to be well when you are not.
How I wish you could keep Tai there for some time. My
love to her.
I have not yet read the Statesman article. It is with me. I
shall see what I can do with it.
I have sent a longish letter to S [hummy]. Do not worry
over letters to me. Write daily, if it pleases and relaxes you.
No strain, please. I shall not imagine all sorts of evil when I
do not hear from you, now that I know you are free to miss
a day or two.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3742. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6898
