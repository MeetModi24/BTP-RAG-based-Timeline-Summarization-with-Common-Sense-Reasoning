181. LETTER TO RATNAMAYI DEVI
August 12, 1945
CHI. RATNAMAYI,
When I received your letter, a doubt arose in my mind. I had
believed that you were self-sacrificing and brave and were not
scared of living in villages. You should not have waited for my
orders. That does not mean that I have ceased to be your well-
wisher. But now that you have settled down there, of what
help can I be?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RATNAMAYI DEVI
MAHILA ASHRAM
