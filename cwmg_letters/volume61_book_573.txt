573. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
September 9, 1935
MY DEAR MALKANI,
Your batch of papers to hand. You will find in Harijan your
wishes fully given effect to. I have also written to Hariji. I
wrote to Gulistan last week to be excused. I am drained dry. And
as you know I have enough hay on my fork. You should there-
fore plead for me to all the 'beggars'.
Love.
BAPU
PROF. MALKANI
HARIJAN SEVAK SANGH, BIRLA MILLS
DELHI
From a photostat: G.N. 924
