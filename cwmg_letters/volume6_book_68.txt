68. LETTER TO H. KALLENBACH
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 2, 1906
DEAR MR. KALLENBACH,
Just one line to thank you very much for your letter. I dare not
say more. I am working under greater pressure than in J'burg.
Except for one night, I have not gone to bed before one o'clock. At times
I have sat up till 3.30 in the morning—and I do not know when I shall
retire tonight. It is now 10.15. I shall look forward to your letters
every week. If I don't write again from here, you will know why.
Yours sincerely,
H. KALLENBACH, ESQ.
P. O. Box 2493
JOHANNESBURG
SOUTH AFRICA
