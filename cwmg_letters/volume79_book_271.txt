271. LETTER TO JAGANNATH
WARDHA,
February 23, 1945
BHAI JAGANNATH,
I have your letter. You are sure to do well. What do you
get now?
Something might be possible if I see the girls. I shall consult
Sushilabehn. The girls should write to me. What does the eldest
boy do? He should write to me. Write to me in the Devanagari
or the Persian script.
Blessings from
BAPU
JAGANNATH
CLOTH
MILLS
DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
