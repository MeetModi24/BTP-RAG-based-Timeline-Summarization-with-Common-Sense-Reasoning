248. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 27, 1930
CHI. LILAVATI (ASAR),
I have your letter. I think it best for you that till you have
sufficiently recovered you should remain in the Ashram and serve
there. If Khurshedbehn calls you, Narandas will not stop you
from going. A volunteer should be happy to work anywhere,
doing any work she is asked to do. Surely, you have not remain-
ed behind in the Ashram because you wanted it or because you were
lazy? Moreover, what is the Ashram if not a place of service?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9567
