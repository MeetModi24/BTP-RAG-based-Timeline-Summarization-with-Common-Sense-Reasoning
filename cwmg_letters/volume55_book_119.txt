119. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
May 5, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
Your impatience for my letters pleases me very much. God
willing, I will satisfy your desire fully after the fast. Stick to
the doctor's treatment and cure your disease. Let my fast
redouble your efforts to cure it. Whether I live or die, you will
have to continue to serve. I hope Purushottam writes to you.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 880. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
