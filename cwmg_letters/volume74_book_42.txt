42. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
May 5, 1941
BHAI MUNSHI,
Follow the example of Asquith. When he fell ill he went on a
cruise in the Mediterranean even while the War was going on.
Why worry about Ahmedabad? Your job is to return completely
restored. You may worry about all other things after that.
Blessings to both of you or all from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7659. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
