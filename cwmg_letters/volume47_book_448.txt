448. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
ON BOARD THE S.S. "RAJPUTANA",
Silence Day [August 31, 1931]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
I have your letter. About Lakshmi I have sent a message with
Joshi. If Maruti is ready, they may marry at any time. We
should put no pressure on him.
Krishnakumari's eczema must have been cured by now.
Why don't you try and cultivate a perfect understanding of
heart with Narandas? Formerly you used to like him so much.
Has he changed now? I have not felt that he has.
The Ashram is yours, whatever it is, and it is there that you
must seek your happiness and peace of mind, if you can get any.
If you resolve thus, you will get everything there.
You should, however, continue to pour out your grievances to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 61. Also C.W. 8787. Courtesy: Ganga-
behn Vaidya
