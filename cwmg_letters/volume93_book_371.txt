371. LETTER TO SRI PRAKASA
BARDOLI,
January 12, 1939
BHAI SRI PRAKASA,
Why an invitation to tea on the occasion of your father's
completing 70 years? I was very pained to receive your letter.
What is the relation between tea and snacks and your father's
religious life? I find the two very contradictory.
For me this auspicious day is meant for religious activity and
thanksgiving. And whatever is to be done, why should it be
from your side? Is there no public life in Kashi? Would not
the public do anything?
In spirit I shall be there.
Blessings from
BAPU
