239. LETTER TO P. KODANDA RAO
ON THE TRAIN,
September 25, 1939
MY DEAR KODANDA RAO,
I have your and your partner's letters of good wishes. I
know you too well to underrate your affection. I treasure it.
About non-violence, I work in faith and I hope with the de-
tachment prescribed in Gita.
My love to you both.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI KODANDA RAO
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
POONA 4
From a photostat: G.N. 6285
