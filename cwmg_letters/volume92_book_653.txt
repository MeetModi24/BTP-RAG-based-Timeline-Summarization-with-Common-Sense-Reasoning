653. LETTER TO SARALADEVI SARABHAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
February 18, 1933
DEAR SISTER,
I have your telegram. I desisted from writing directly to
you lest what I desired or suggested became a burden to you.
I made the suggestion to Ramjibhai thinking that you would
decline without hesitation if you had not still recovered fully.
In my foolishness I assumed that he would not use my name in
writing to you. You were quite right to decline. Send Vidya-
behn or Sharadabehn if you think it fit. I shall not insist. I
must say you have taken very long to recover.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
SUPERINTENDENT
YERAVDA CENTRAL PRISON
POONA
