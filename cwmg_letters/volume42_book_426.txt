426. LETTER TO REGINALD REYNOLDS
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 4, 1930
MY DEAR REYNOLDS,
Here are two letters for you.
I hope you received my previous letter. I want you to tell
me all your experiences after you separated at Lahore. I have
called Young India my weekly letter to friends. I hope, therefore,
that you are carefully taking in that mouthful.
No time to dictate more.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
