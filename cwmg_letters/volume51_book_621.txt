621. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
November 10, 1932
BELOVED DAUGHTER RAIHANA,
I hope you received my postcard. My fear has come true.
Since you write to me regularly, whenever I do not get a letter from
you I feel uneasy. When you are not well you can ask Father or
Mother to write a postcard. Whatever may happen, I certainly
do not wish that you yourself should write to me when you are ill.
How did you fall ill? Did you overwork? You ought not to fall ill.
I hope you are better now. You need not reply to this letter your-
self. It will do if Father drops me a postcard. I have not received
Hamida's letter so far. Khuda Hafiz.
Blessings from
BAPU
