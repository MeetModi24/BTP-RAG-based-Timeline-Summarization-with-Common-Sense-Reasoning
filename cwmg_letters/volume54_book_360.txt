360. LETTER TO KESHAV
April 6, 1933
MY DEAR KESHAV,
I like your frank letter. I thoroughly understand your posi-
tion, and though I may not quite agree with it, I can appreciate
it. You know my own fundamental position, don't you? I
never feel that what appeals to me as the whole truth must neces-
sarily appeal to the others as such. Truth that we see is al-
ways relative knowledge and therefore it need not have universal
application. My prayer therefore for my neighbours is: "May
they see the Truth as Thou the All-knowing One, would have
them to see it."
Yours sincerely,
