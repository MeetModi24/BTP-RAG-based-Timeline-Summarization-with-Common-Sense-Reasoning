220. LETTER TO MOHAN SINGH
BIRLA HOUSE,
NEW DELHI,
November 1, 1947
BHAI MOHAN SINGHJI,
I have your letter with two enclosures. I have read the
pamphlets hurriedly but I do not like them in English. It is not your
or my mother tongue. I write in English with difficulty and never
know authoritatively that it is idiomatically correct. I hope you are
aware that the masses have no knowledge of English. As a language,
it is understood only by an insignificant minority. I therefore suggest
your writing in Hindustani either in Nagari character or Urdu,
preferably in both.
As to blessings, I told you I think quite clearly that every good
