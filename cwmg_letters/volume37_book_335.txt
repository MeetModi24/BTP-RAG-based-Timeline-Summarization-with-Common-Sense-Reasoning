335. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS
September 21, 1928
MY DEAR KRISHNADAS,
I have your postcard . . . . 1 is a scamp. He is not truthful
when he says that he has walked all the way from here to Cal-
cutta to meet you. He has been going to several places; among
them was Brindaban from where he wrote saying that he was practi-
cally fixed up. He is good-hearted but he is thoroughly untrust-
worthy.
I showed your letter about Ram Binod to Rajendra Babu
who was here on his return from Europe. He is of opinion that
some step will be necessary.
SJT. KRISHNADAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 13689
