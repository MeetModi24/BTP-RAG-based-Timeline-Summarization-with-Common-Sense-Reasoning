101. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
August 24, 1947
Punjabis in Calcutta have been pressing me to go to the Pun-
jab at once. They tell me a terrible story. Thousands have been
killed. A few thousand girls have been kidnapped! Hindus cannot
live in the Pakistan area, nor Muslims in the other portion. Add
to this the information that the two wings of the army took sides
and worked havoc! Can any of this be true?
When do you think I should go to the Punjab if at all? I
have still work in Calcutta, then in Noakhali and Bihar. But
everything can be laid aside to go to the Punjab if it is proved
to be necessary.
Mahatma Gandhi—The Last Phase, Vol. II, p. 385
