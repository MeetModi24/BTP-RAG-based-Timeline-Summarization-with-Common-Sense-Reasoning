511. LETTER TO SARASWATI
July 23, 1937
CHI. SARASWATI,
I have your letter.
Yes, you may certainly come over
here if Ramachandran, Paparamma, Father and Kanti approve
of it. On my part, I shall love to have you here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6162.
Also C.W. 3435. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
