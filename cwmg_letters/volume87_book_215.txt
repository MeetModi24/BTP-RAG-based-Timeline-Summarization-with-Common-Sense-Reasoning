215. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 9, 1947
CHI. AMTULSALAAM,
Have you boycotted me? There is not a single letter from
you. Think over what happened on the last day. Forget that
I was angry; try to understand the reason behind it. Write to
me about the conditions there. How is your health?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 581
