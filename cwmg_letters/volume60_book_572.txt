572. LETTER TO BENARSIDAS CHATURVEDI
April 11, 1935
BHAI BENARSIDAS,
I have your letter. It has not hurt me. A man should do only
what he can. I would advise you first to get married and then
to devote yourself to the task of popularizing Hindi. You will
be able to accomplish three purposes in this way: Vishal Bharat,
propagation of Hindi and writing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2555
