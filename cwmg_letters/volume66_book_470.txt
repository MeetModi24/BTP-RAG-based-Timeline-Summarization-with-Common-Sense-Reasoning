470. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
February 16, 1938
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I hope it is all right the way I am writing to you, isn't it?
You will have news of me from my letter to Lila. It is not good
that I have no letter from either of you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 397
