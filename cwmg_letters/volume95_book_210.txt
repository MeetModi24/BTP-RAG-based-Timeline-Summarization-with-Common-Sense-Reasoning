210. LETTER TO J. WOLFF
CALCUTTA,
August 21, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your cablegram preceding your letter. To your cable-
gram I sent the following reply: "WOLFF POST RESTANTE JERUSALEM.
AM HELPLESS. GANDHI".
You are impatient. Non-violence is never impatient. Impa-
tience betrays lack of faith and understanding. Go on doing your duty
to the full and leave the rest to God.
J. WOLFF, ESQ.
POST RESTANTE
JERUSALEM
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
