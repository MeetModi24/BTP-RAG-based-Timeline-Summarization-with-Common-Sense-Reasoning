395. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
October 24, 1947
CHI. NARANDAS,
Read this letter. Send it on to Nanabhai. Let me know
what you think after reading it. Ask Nanabhai also to let me
know what he thinks. I have written something about this in
Harijan, which you will read in due course.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 8646. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
