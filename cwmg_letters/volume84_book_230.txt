230. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR N. MORARJEE
NEW DELHI,
May 21, 1946
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I have your letter. You did well in not coming here.
Where is the need to meet a person when there is no work?
Aspire to live up to 125 years. Everybody who serves should
have this aspiration. Its fulfilment rests with God.
Succeed in your undertaking there and return soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 4808. Courtesy: Shantikumar
N. Morarjee
