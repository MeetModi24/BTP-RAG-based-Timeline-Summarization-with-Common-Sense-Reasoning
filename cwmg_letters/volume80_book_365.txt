365. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
May 30, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have not been able to reply to one of your letters. You
did well in sending the money to Ramaswami for his parents for
the months of April and May. I think nothing is left now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10635
