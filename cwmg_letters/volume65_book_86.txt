86. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 13, 1937
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I got both your letters today, just now. Kanu got the letter
of the 5th the day before yesterday. Yesterday he didn't come.
He got the letter of the 9th yesterday.
If you want to come to Belgaum, you may. Most probably I
will stay there till the 21st. I will return here latest by the 24th
Kanti will come from Rajkot. Probably he will go back
to Rajkot. Then he will go to Mysore and from there to Tri-
vandrum. There is thus no question of Saraswati's coming to
Belgaum.
You are out and out a fool. I didn't write to Kanti. He
wrote on his own. You are needlessly suspicious that I don't
trust you.
We will discuss your future when we meet. About the
land also, we will decide then. The wedding is fixed for the
18th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 379
