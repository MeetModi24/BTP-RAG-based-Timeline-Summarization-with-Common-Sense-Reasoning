62. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
April 17, 1942
MY DEAR C. R.,
So you could not give me a day ! ! ! Supposing you had to
give one more day to Delhi.
But you know best what to do
at a given moment.
I hope you will find at least a day for
Sevagram on your way to Allahabad.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2085
