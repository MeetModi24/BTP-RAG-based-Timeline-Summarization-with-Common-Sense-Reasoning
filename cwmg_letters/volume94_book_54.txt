54. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
SEVAGRAM,
September 1, 1941
CHI. MRIDU,
I have your postcard and letter. Is it Mahadev who is blessed in
having you as secretary or is it you who are blessed in being Mahadev's
secretary ? Who can answer ? Since you have not come I keep
postponing revising your writing and attend to other things. You should
not fall ill now. Why should I worry about Mahadevbhai ? Good doctors
and you, too, are there. Now do not let him work so much.
Blessings from
BAPU
