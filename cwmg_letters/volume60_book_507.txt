507. LETTER TO H. L. SHARMA
April 1, 1935
CHI. SHARMA,
I have your letter. In the absence of a wire from you I take
it that Devi is doing well. So far I have never had anyone given
injections. We made use only of mud poultices and similar remedies.
Yes, you may certainly go to Simla if it is possible. I shall send
you the books too if I can lay hands on them. Your fear as to
what will happen after I break the silence is groundless.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3153
