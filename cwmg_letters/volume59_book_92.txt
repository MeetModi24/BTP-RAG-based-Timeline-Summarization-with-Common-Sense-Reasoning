92. LETTER TO MATILDA B. KALLON
September 27, 1934
MY DEAR MATILDA,
I have your letter. I have written to K. Rama Menon
to the effect that your being a Christian should not be in the
way of the hostel being entrusted to your care. Your letter is
completely satisfactory.
SHRIMATI MATILDA B. KALLON
NETTUR
TELLICHERRY, N. MALABAR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
