41. LETTER TO MRS. PERIN CAPTAIN
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
June 22, 1926
You do remember Miss Haussding. Don't you? You will
meet her on Friday. I had expected a letter last week. But
none came. Her main letters have, however, begun to arrive.
She is therefore likely to come by the boat (Razmak) mentioned
by her. And if she has arrived, please telegraph to me so that I
can send someone to meet her at Ahmedabad station.
Yours,
MRS. PERIN CAPTAIN
ISLAM CLUB BUILDINGS
CHAUPATI
BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 19631
