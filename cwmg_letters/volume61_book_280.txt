280. LETTER TO SHRINATH SINGH
June 23, 1935
BHAI SHRINATH SINGH,
I have your letter. The committee's decision may be
conveyed to Shri Suryanarayanji by the Sammelan office. That
was my idea in sending his letter to the office.
I am exchanging views with the Nagpur friends about the
next session of the Sammelan. I have also received your letter
in that connection.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SHRINATH SINGH
SECRETARY, HINDI SAHITYA SAMMELAN
P.O. Box 11
ALLAHABAD (PRAYAG)
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5662. Also C.W. 2974. Courtesy:
Parashuram Mehrotra
