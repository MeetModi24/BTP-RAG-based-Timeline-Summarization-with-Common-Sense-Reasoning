7. LETTER TO LAKSHMIBEHN KHARE
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 5, 1930
CHI. LAKSHMIBAI,
It was in July that I was given your letter of May. There is
little we can do about this. It would be enough if I could write
Marathi as well as you write Gujarati. I am of course learning it
from Kakasaheb.
Always look upon all children of Vasant's age as if they were
Vasant himself.
Write to me regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 285. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn Khare
