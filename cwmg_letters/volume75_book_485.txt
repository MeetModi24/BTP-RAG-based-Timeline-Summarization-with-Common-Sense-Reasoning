485. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHASTRI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 11, 1942
BHAI HIRALAL SHASTRI,
You did well to remind me about the Jaipur worker. I had
forgotten. Jamnalalji should be the model for all constructive workers
generally and for Jaipur workers particularly. To sing praises of
Jamnalalji's virtues or to commission statues of him would be an
insult to him. You may do it, but if along with it you do not
emulate Jamnalalji all the praising of Jamnalalji will be in vain.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Hiralal Shastri Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
