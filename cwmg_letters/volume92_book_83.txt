83. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SABARMATI,
August 5, 1929
MY DEAR C. R.,
I was deeply pained to learn that in spite of repeated re-
quests by letter and telegraph, you had failed to send your yarn sub-
scription for the A.I.S.A. If the salt loses its savour, etc., etc. We
might as well shut up shop if the tallest partner in the business
is proved guilty of gross negligence. Do please send your yarn.
My experiment goes on merrily.
How are you and your prohibition work ?
Yours,
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/93
