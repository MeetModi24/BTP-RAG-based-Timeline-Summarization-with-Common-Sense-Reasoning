32. LETTER TO HARKISHEN LAL
November 28, 1920
MY DEAR HARKISHEN LAL1,
Your letter has followed me in my travels. If you prove a
true prophet the blame will be partly yours also. You may not
sit still and let the roots of violence sprout and say: 'Behold what I
said has proved true.' But whether you prove a true prophet or
false, non-co-operation has to go on, till it stops by the weight
of its own violence. Therefore you are expected to strain every
nerve to falsify your prophecy.
The demand regarding Khilafat is: Restore to Turkey what
was hers at the time of the War with completest safeguards for the
self-determination of Arabs and the Armenians. Regarding the
Punjab, full reparation in accordance with the demands of the
Punjab. This should be followed by complete swaraj in accordance
with the wishes of only chosen leaders of the people. You will
see this suggestion in my letter to every Englishman.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
