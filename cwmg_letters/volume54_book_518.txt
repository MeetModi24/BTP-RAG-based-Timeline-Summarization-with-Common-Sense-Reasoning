518. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[After April 19, 1933]
DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I had sent a telegram regarding your coming but it was good
that the doctor did not allow you to leave. Travelling in a feeble
condition is not advisable. Do what the doctor says. God will
make you well. Do not worry about serving. There shall be occa-
sion enough for service when you are all right again.
Blessings from
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 282
