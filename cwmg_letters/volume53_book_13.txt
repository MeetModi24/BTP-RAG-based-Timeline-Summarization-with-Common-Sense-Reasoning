13. LETTER TO G. DORASWAMY
January 11, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter of 8th inst., enclosing a copy of
the resolution of your college. I hope that you are also doing
constructive work. Without that the resolution cannot have much
meaning.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. G. DORASWAMY
No. 45 PACHAIYYAPPA'S HOSTEL
KILPAUK, MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 19221
