628. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
October 4, 1941
CHI. NARANDAS,
I have your letter.
I had the previous one, too. The
constitution drafted by you seems all right. It is enclosed. I
have signed my approval on the draft itself.
The programme there succeeded very well indeed. Dhiru
proved to be of great help. By the 2nd, you will perhaps have
reached the figure of one lakh. The Lord's will prevails. Kan-
aiyo left yesterday for Calcutta to complete his training in pho-
tography. Abha might come with him. Everything is left to
her father.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8593.
Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
