340. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
October 3, 1924
MY DEAR PADMAJA,
I hope you received my wire. Are poetesses' daughters all
silly? You seem to delight in your illness. Do be good and get
well.
With much love.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRIMATI PADMAJA NAIDU
HYDERABAD
DECCAN
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
