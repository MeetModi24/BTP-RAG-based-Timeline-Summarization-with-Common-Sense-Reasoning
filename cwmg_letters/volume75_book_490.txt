490. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
SEVAGRAM,
March 15, 1942
MY DEAR KU[MARAPPA]
Jhaverbhai tells me you have approved of his plan and budget
for oil-presses in selected areas throughout India. Please let
me have your formal approval so as to enable me to examine the
plan.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10163
