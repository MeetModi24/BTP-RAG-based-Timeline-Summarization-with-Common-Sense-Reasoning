8. LETTER TO CHANDRAGUPTA VARSHNEYA
October 12, 1941
BHAI CHANDRAGUPTA,
It would do to say that there is no truth in the statement made
by the person who has not given his address and whom you do not
know. Why inquire into it? I sent the letter because I thought
he was one of your acquaintances.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI CHANDRAGUPTA VARSHNEYA, B.Sc.
AJMER
