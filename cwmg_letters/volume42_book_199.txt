199. LETTER TO NAWAB OF BHOPAL
CAMP ALLAHABAD,
November 17, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I am much obliged to you for your letter of 11th inst. I can
in return give you my assurance that I shall not make any in-
discreet or illegitimate use of the information you have so gene-
rously placed in my possession.
Yours sincerely,
HIS HIGHNESS THE NAWAB SAHEB OF BHOPAL
BHOPAL (C.I.)
From a photostat: S.N. 15770
