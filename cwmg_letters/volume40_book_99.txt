99. LETTER TO EDMUND PRIVAT
CAMP, RANGOON,
March 9, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I was indeed looking forward to
my tour in Europe this year but owing to the exigencies of the
situation in India, I am obliged to cancel the tour. It is difficult
now for me to say when, if at all, I shall be able to visit Europe.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
M. EDMUND PRIVAT
SWITZERLAND
From a photostat: G.N. 8790
