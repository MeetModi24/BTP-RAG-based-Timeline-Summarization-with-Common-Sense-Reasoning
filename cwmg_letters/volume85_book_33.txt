33. LETTER TO C. W. B. RANKIN
CAMP: PANCHGANI,
July 22, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter to Rajkumari of 13-7-'46 was received on the
18th instant. Having slept over it for four nights, I feel it to
be my duty to say that it seems His Excellency is wrong to delay
the release of Shri Haridas Mitra. It is inconsistent with the
declared policy of the Government.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MAJOR C. W. B. RANKIN
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government 1944-47, p. 55
