255. LETTER TO JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 27, 1930
CHI. JAYAPRAKASH,
Prabhavati writes that you are not keeping well. After acquir-
ing so much learning, why can't you keep your body perfectly
healthy? It is essential to make an effort in this direction. What
are you doing now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3374
