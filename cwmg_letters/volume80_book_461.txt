461. LETTER TO MAURICE FRYDMAN
PANCHGANI,
June 8, 1945
MY DEAR BHARATANAND,
You are under promise to behave yourself and be well.
No thoughtless experiments allowed.
As to the 16 Poles, send me the draft you would like me
to sign. Is my signature of any value in such a case? But I
do not wish to anticipate my judgment. I shall know when the
draft is before me.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI BHARATANANDJI
JASSAWALA NATURE CURE CLINIC
CUMBALLA HILL
BOMBAY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
