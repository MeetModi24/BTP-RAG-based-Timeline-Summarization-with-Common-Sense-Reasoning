259. LETTER TO GOBIND DAS CONSUL
ON THE TRAIN,
October 1, 1939
DEAR FRIEND,
I have just glanced through your production for five minutes.
I have nothing whatsoever to say against the front page or the
contents. You have every right to express your views in the
manner that may seem best to you.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a facsimile: Mahatma Gandhi-The Great Rogue of India, between pp.
4 and 5
