755. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
March 8, 1934
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Herewith the second letter from Agatha Harrison. As she
says in the letter, let her carry out her programme in Bombay
at her ease and then go to Poona. I suppose she will be putting
up at the Servants [of India Society] at Poona.
I sent yesterday a copy of my letter to Hoare. I am sending
another copy now. Yesterday's copy is meant for Agatha and
today's copy is for you.
It seems Faiba is coming to Hyderabad. It is good. A lot of
work has cropped up for me in Hyderabad.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
The monthly expenses of Agatha are Birla's responsibility.
She estimated £200 for this visit. Of this, £100 was arranged
in England and £100 will have to be found here. See if
Bhulabhai can take that burden.
