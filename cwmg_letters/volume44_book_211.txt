211. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 14, 1930
CHI. MANI (PATEL),
I write this as you wanted me to write. But goodness knows if
it will reach its destination. They allowed me to send your letter
to me for Father to read. Write if you get the necessary permission.
Make full use of the enforced rest you have got. That too I consider
a part of our service. Take good care of your health. Draw up a
regular programme of work. Let me know what food you get.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 74
