271. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
July 26, 1932
DEAR MAGANLAL,
I expect a letter from you every week, but I am disappointed.
Mr. Polak has informed me that you are getting on all right and that
you are engrossed in your studies. I receive letters from Manjula .
Write to me in detail. Let me know what subjects you are
studying.
Develop your body well.
Hope you are writing to the Doctor regularly.
Yours,
BAPU
