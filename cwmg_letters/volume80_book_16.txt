16. LETTER TO V. I. MUNISWAMY PILLAY
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 26, 1945
DEAR MUNISWAMY,
Your letter reached me too late to overtake the wedding
date. May the couple have a long and happy life of service to
the motherland.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI V. I. MUNISWAMY PILLAY
SATH VILAS
OOTY
SOUTH INDIA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
