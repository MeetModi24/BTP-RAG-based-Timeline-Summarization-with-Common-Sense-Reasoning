124. LETTER TO DR. B. C. ROY
AS AT THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
December 9, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have purposely kept your telegram pending correspondence
I am carrying on with Motilalji and other friends. I am not
yet certain about my going to Gauhati. Motilalji just sends me
a peremptory wire, but I am awaiting for his considered reply to
my letter. In any event, if I do go to Gauhati, on my way going
there I shall be in Calcutta only for a few hours pending the
departure of the Assam Mail. I am therefore unable to give you
any definite reply just now.
I hope the Hospital is flourishing.
Yours sincerely,
DR. BIDHAN Roy
CHITTARANJAN HOSPITAL
RUSSA ROAD
CALCUTTA
