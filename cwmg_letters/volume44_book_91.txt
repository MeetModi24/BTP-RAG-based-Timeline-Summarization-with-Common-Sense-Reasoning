91. LETTER TO KAMALNAYAN BAJAJ
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 2, 1930
CHI. KAMALNAYAN,
I got your letter.
Can you read my handwriting in
Gujarati? If you cannot read it, I will write in Hindi. Write to
me often in the same way that you have done this time. Anyone who
goes to visit Father should tell him that he should come out with
added weight.
You should write a neat and clear handwriting. Improve
your health as much as you can.
Kakasaheb sends you his blessings.
61
Where is Om? Tell Madalasa that she should write to me.
Ask Kamala and Rameshwar also to write.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Where is Radhakisan? How is he?
