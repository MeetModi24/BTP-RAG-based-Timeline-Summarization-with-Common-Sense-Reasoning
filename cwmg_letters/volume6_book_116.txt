116. LETTER TO J. ROYEPPEN
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 6, 1906
MY DEAR JOSEPH,
Please call here if you can tomorrow at 5 o'clock p.m. I would
like you to attend the House of Commons meeting and to distribute
the Representation by the Delegates, as also the personal representation
by you and others. I shall endeavour to have your representation printed.
If you can come do not fail.
Yours sincerely,
JOSEPH ROYEPPEN, ESQ.
36, STAPLETON HALL ROAD
STROUD GREEN, N.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4501
