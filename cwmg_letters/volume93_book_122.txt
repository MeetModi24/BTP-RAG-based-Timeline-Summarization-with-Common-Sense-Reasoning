122. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 20, 1936
CHI. NANIBEHN,
Your letter came in all right. I would certainly like to settle
down in Gujarat in the midst of all of you. But my sadhana
has to be at Segaon for the present. My coming in October
will be as good as not coming. We shall of course have the
satisfaction of seeing each other.
I hope you and Gangabehn are keeping yourselves fit. I
shall not ask about your minds.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Ba, Manu and Kanu, Prabhavati, Balvantsinha, are doing
well.
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari-Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 28
