348. LETTER TO CHANDRASHANKER P. SHUKLA
POONA,
March 21, 1946
CHI. CHANDRASHANKER,
The above demand is reasonable. Send Jivanji a copy of
this letter and of my reply. But that does not mean that I or
any person authorized by me cannot use these letters as may seem
proper to us.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 2332
