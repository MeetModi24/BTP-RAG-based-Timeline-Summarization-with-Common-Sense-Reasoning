261. LETTER TO REGISTRAR OF ASIATICS
[LAWLEY,]
August 19, 1912
THE REGISTRAR OF ASIATICS
PRETORIA
SIR,
I have the honour to acknowledge receipt of your letter of the
18th instant. I hope to be able to forward the list of six British
Indians at an early date. Consultations are now being held as to
the names, the fittest to be submitted out of the many received.
I have, etc.,
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 5699
