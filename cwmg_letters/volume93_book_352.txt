352. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 6, 1938
MY DEAR SLAVE,
I am sorry to hear about Father's and your illness. I sent
you a wire which I hope you received. I am yearning for good
news from you. Having undertaken the great mission, you must
keep yourself fit.
What a brave letter you have written. It is quite worthy of
you. May your mission be crowned with success!
I knew it is a difficult task. But nothing is difficult for God.
If He wills it, you will pull through with credit.
Love.
SLAVE-DRIVER
SHRI PADMAJA NAIDU
ZAHEER MANZIL
RED HILL
HYDERABAD DECCAN
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
