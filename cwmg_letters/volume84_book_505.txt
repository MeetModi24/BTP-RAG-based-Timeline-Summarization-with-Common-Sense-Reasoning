505. LETTER TO J. J. SINGH
BOMBAY,
July 6, 1946
DEAR SINGH,
Our mutual friend, Mr. Louis Fischer tells me that your
persistent labours were solely responsible for the passage of the
Indian Immigration Act. I congratulate you on your successful
labours. You will, perhaps, oblige me by sending me a copy of
the Act.
M. K. GANDHI
The Hindustan Times Weekly, 27-4-1969
