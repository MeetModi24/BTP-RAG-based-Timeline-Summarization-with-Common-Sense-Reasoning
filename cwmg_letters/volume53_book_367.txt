367. LETTER TO P. NARAYANAN NAIR
February 11, 1933
DEAR NARAYANAN,
I have seen your letter to Mahadev Desai.
I have asked
the correspondent to send me cuttings from Mathrubhumi to which
he refers. As soon as I get them I shall certainly let you have
them. Meanwhile, I am glad of the assurance you have given me
that Mathrubhumi never descends to the abuse of any community.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20215
