568. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 30, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
It is difficult to answer your question regarding the diary.
Do as you think proper. Experience alone shows what is best. You
had better write on a separate sheet anything which specially needs
to be told to me. You should not have the burden of reading the
diary.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4383
