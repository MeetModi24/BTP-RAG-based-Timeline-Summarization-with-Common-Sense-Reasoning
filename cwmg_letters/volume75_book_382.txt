382. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
February 15, 1942
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. You should write to the sisters and to
Janakibehn. A meeting will of course be held here on the
20th. I myself have called it. I have asked Rajen Babu to
attend. I do not have the courage to ask you. I had a letter
from Jayaprakash. It is good. They have struck out three lines
from it. A full account of Jamnalalji's passing away has appear-
ed [in the newspapers] and, therefore, I am not writing about it
here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3567
