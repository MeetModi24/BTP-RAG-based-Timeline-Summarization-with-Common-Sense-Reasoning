18. LETTER TO ATULANAND CHAKRAVARTY
POONA,
November 2, 1945
BHAI ATULANAND,
I have your letter and also the book. I am reading it. I
shall soon write to you again. Have you finished Call it Politics1?
You do follow Hindi, don't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : C.W. 1485. Courtesy: A. K. Sen
1 The title is given in English in the source.
