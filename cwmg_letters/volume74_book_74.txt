74. LETTER TO RAMMANOHAR LOHIA
May 11, 1941
BHAI RAMMANOHAR,
I was very glad to have your letter. I have written to
Haridattaji. It is good that almost everyone spins. Your father's
journeys continue. He keeps sending me his diary. My blessings
to everyone.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
