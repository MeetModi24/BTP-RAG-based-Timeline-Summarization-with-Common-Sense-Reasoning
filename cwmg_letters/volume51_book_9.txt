9. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
September 1, 1932
CHI. GANGABEHN,
That there is no one living in the house in Kamatipura and
that some property from it was stolen, you should look upon as a
test of your faith. Keep repeating to yourself: "It is a blessing
that the chains have broken, it will now be easier for me to find
Shri Gopal."
Kaka wrote to me, too, about Chapter XVI. At present,
however, I have no time for it. Ask Ramibehn to write to me.
I hope your health is now all right. Besides, the hakim is with
you. What more do you want? Give my due regards to him.
Has Nathji's eczema been cured?
Write to me regularly.
Lakshmi Jerajani impressed me as a wise and smart girl.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-6: G. S. Gangabehnne, p. 65. Also C.W. 8794. Courtesy: Ganga-
behn Vaidya
