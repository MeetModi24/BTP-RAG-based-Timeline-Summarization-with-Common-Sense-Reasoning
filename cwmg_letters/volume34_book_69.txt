69. LETTER TO BANARASIDAS CHATURVEDI
BANGALORE,
June 28, 1927
BHAI BANARASIDASJI,
Your letter. I cannot understand why there was no reply at all
from the Ashram. I am looking into it. It appears that I shall not
be able to go to the Ashram for nearly two months. There would
be nothing wrong if I happened to get your letter during my ill-
ness.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2572
