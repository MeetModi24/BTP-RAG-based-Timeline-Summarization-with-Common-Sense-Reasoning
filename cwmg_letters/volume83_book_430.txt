430. LETTER TO LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
CAMP, NEW DELHI,
April 5, 1946
DEAR LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE,
I have your kind and considerate letter. I note what you
say. As it has turned out, I am here till 16th instant at least.
Maulana Saheb wants me till then.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 180
