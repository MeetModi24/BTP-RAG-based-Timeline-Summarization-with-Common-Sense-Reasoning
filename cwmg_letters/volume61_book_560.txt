560. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
WARDHA,
September 5, 1935
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Mahadev left yesterday for Prayag to meet Jawaharlal. But
I see from the latter's wire received today that Mahadev will
not be able to meet him, since he is leaving this evening.
The Bombay Government has made its reply as venomous
as it could. Its meaning is clear. Every effort will be made
to suppress the facts. I now feel that we should not publish the
correspondence for the present. It may be published together
with the report of the Committee, when it is received, as our
introduction to the report explaining the genesis of the Committee.
Do you see anything against this? It is desirable that the Commit-
tee should finish its work as soon as possible.
I hope Baba is all right now. At present we have some
meeting or other everyday. . . .2
Mahadev will return day after tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 184
