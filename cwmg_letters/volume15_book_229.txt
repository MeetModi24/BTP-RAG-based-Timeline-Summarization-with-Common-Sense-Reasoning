229. LETTER TO G. E. CHATFIELD
THE ASHRAM,
April 24, 1919
DEAR MR. CHATFIELD,
I have just discovered my failure to pass on definite instruc-
tions as to sending the leaflets published after my speech of the
14th. I send you copies herewith for your perusal.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
From a photostat: S. N. 6563
