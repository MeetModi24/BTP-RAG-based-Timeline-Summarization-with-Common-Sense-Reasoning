323. LETTER TO S. H. PANDIT
[After January 9, 1945]
Gandhiji is grateful for your assurances and your reminding him of old
acquaintances. He is glad to hear of your work and he will read the report when
it comes, and let you know if anything worthwhile strikes him to add.
From the original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
