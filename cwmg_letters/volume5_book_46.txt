46. LETTER TO MISS BISSICKS
[JOHANNESBURG,]
August 5, 1905
DEAR MISS BISSICKS,
I am very sorry for your troubles. I am afraid it would not be now
possible to recall the things mentioned by you, as they are included in
the sale, as I understand from the Trustee. The sale has realized only
£210-0- as a going concern. I understand Brown Bros. have bought
the business.
I am sorry I shall not be able to cycle to you on Monday as I told
Sister Heliel I might do.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
Miss Bissicks
C/O Box 4207
Letter Book (1905): No. 872
