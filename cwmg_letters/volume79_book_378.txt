378. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
March 9, 1945
I suggest only this in this connection. In the interest of
work you may relieve anybody and accept whoever may be avail-
able as substitute. I am asking them to change the arrange-
ment regarding milk. Please do not lose patience if I proceed
slowly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8462. Also C.W. 5572. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
