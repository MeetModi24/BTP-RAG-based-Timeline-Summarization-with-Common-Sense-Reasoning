62. LETTER TO REGINALD REYNOLDS
March 13, 1930
MY DEAR REYNOLDS,
Take care of yourself. Besides looking after Young India, do
please identify yourself with the activities of the Ashram. I am
anxious for it to become an abode of peace, purity and strength.
You I hold to be a gift from God for the advancement of that
work.
Yours,
BAPU

delphia
