334. LETTER TO JAMNADAS GANDHI
Sravana Vad 1 [August 15, 1924]
CHI. JAMNADAS2,
Take Revashankerbhai's advice. I see no harm in utilizing
Doctor Saheb's money for the building.
I am leaving for Delhi tomorrow. The agitation will subside
by itself.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. JAMNADAS K. GANDHI
OPP. MIDDLE SCHOOL
NAVA PARA, RAJKOT
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 6027. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
