421. LETTER TO P. KODANDA RAO
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
February 11, 1941
MY DEAR KODANDA RAO1,
You have no business to be ill.
When people are in need of blessings, they do not seek them
by post. So Mary will have to come here and ask them with due
supplication.
As for your suit, it has failed in spite of my pleading. My
whole soul rebels against any speechifying. And about Gokhale
I simply cannot do it. You will, therefore, have to do without me.
Lastly it is cruelty to animals to drag me from Sevagram
except when duty makes a peremptory call.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 6282
