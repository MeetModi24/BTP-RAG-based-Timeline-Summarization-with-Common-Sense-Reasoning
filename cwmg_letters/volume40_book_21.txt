21. LETTER TO MADHAVJI V. THAKKAR
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 23, 1929
BHAISHRI MADHAVJI,
I got your letter. Do not eat bananas. There is no harm
in eating a few apples after boiling them without peeling and then
removing the skin, seeds, etc. Pineapple may also be eaten, if
It is sweet. The number of fasts should be limited by your capa-
city to go without food. I do not understand why you cannot
have a sun-bath in Calcutta. Even when a sun-bath is not possi-
ble you can, and should, take Kuhne bath in the tub. I do be-
lieve that it would be better if you can continue to wear a
kachchha.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
