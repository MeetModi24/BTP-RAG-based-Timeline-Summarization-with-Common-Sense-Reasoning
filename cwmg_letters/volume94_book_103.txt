103. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 10, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
I have your letter. Congratulations about Annapurna. There is a
letter from Sankaran. He continues to take enema. He will therefore
send everything except the syringe. Shall I send another syringe ?
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
