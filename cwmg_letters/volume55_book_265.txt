265. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
June 26, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
How is it that you fell ill again?
I will discuss with Dinshawji about Purushottam after the
period of silence ends, and then send a wire to you. I remember
about him.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 883. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
