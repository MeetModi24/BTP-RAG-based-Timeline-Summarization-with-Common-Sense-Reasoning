175. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
SEVAGRAM,
February 5, 1945
CHI. GHANSHYAMDAS,
Dinshaw has sent the draft to De[vdas]. He wants Bhiwandi-
wala, who had helped him and who has faith in nature cure, as
also Fikriyar Jung, who was Finance Minister to the Nizam and
believes in these remedies, to be included among the trustees. I
think there is no harm in it. About the rest I have written to
Dinshaw. If you will show this to Devdas I shall make do with
this one letter.
If you take me to Delhi I shall have to visit Pilani, Mira's
place and also Dharmadev's. I shall stay at the Harijan Nivas.
Blessings from
BAPU
BIRLAJI
N.D.
