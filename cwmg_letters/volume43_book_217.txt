217. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
DANDI,
April 10, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have already replied to your letter. You two must have dis-
cussed the matter by now. It is being rumoured that I shall be
arrested today, and so I don't write more. May God bless you
both and give you sufficient firmness of mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3364
