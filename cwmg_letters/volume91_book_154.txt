154. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
MOTIHARI,
[On or before May 24, 1918]
BHAISHRI VALJI,
I have talked to Panditji about you. He asked me about
the salary you would expect. I suggested Rs. 300/-. I got the
impression that he thought the amount a little too high. So I
want to ask you what is the minimum you will accept.
Bandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 11270. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
