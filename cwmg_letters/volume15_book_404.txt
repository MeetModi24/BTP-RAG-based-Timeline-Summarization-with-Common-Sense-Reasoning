404. LETTER TO F. C. GRIFFITH
BOMBAY,
July 14, 1919
DEAR MR. GRIFFITH,
I have your note . I have made no statement to the Press
and shall certainly make none until I hear further. I am not
responsible even for the A. P. message that appears in the Press
today.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. G.
From a photostat: S.N. 6746
