221. LETTER TO RAGHUNATH SAHAI
ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 30, 1920
DEAR SIR,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I agree with you
that the scene described by you was most disgraceful. I am inquir-
ing into the matter. Please let me hear from you again if you
receive further particulars about the outrage. I hope to corres-
pond with you further on hearing from my co-workers.
These regrettable incidents will now and then undoubtedly
happen. It will be for us to check all such violent tendencies. But
I think it hardly proper to stop a big movement because of the
over-zeal shown by some students.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 7313
