240. LETTER TO P. C. JOSHI
BIRLA HOUSE,
MOUNT PLEASANT ROAD,
BOMBAY,
August 21, 1945
DEAR JOSHI,
Advocate Bhulabhai Desai handed me the enclosed opinion
yesterday. It is, as you will see, based on the papers I gave him
at your instance some time ago.
Having had the opinion, you have the right to publish it
for what it may be worth. The news, almost daily poured into
my ears by Congressmen to the effect that the Party is unscru-
pulous in its methods and that it even resorts to violence, makes
it difficult for me to accept the opinion which is after all based
on the papers that were in my possession on the date they
were handed to the Advocate. But I cannot judge the Party on
one-sided evidence. Nor have I the leisure to shoulder the burden
of studying the evidence in a manner that would enable me to
form an opinion.
The bitterness between the Party and Congressmen, so far as
I am aware, is growing. The best course perhaps is for you to
consider the position in the light of Congressmen's opinion about
the Party and then do what may appear best to you.
If you propose to publish the enclosed you will please publish
this too together with it.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Encl. 1
SHRI P. C. JOSHI
RAJ BHUWAN
SANDHURST ROAD, BOMBAY 4
