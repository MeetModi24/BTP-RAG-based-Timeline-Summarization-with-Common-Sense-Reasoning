319. LETTER TO NARAYAN MORESHWAR KHARE
CHI. PANDITJI,
I received your letter yesterday. Of course, you have my
blessings for your new enterprise. We are bound to achieve success
to the extent we make efforts. May your pupils imbibe music in their
lives and learn that music is meant for service.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
