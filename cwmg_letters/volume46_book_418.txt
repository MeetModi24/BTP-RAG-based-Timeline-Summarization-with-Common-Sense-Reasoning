418. LETTER TO DUNICHAND
As AT SABARMATI,
June 6, 1931
DEAR LALA DUNICHAND,
I thank you for your letter. I shall await your analysis of
all the cases.
Why was the meeting dispersed by the Magistrate? What
was the ostensible cause and how was it dispersed?
Please let me know as soon as the Government publish their
opinion about the Ludhiana outrage.
Yours sincerely,
LALA DUNICHAND
ADVOCATE
AMBALA
A.I.C.C. File No. 16-B, 1931. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
