429. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
March 15, 1945
CHI. GHANSHYAMDAS,
I heard from Bapa today that you had fever. Your fever
worries me. Why should you get fever? If you need Rameshwar-
das there, keep him. Even so, I will stay only at the Birla House.
I will leave here on the 30th. The programme after the meeting
is indefinite. If you cannot come because of weakness I shall
manage.
Blessings from
BAPU
SETH GHANSHYAMDAS BIRLA
BIRLA HOUSE
ALBUQUERQUE ROAD
NEW DELHI
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8066. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
