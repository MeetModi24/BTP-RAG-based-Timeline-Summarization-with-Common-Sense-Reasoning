678. LETTER TO A. S. SAHAJANANDA
[On or before October 19, 1945]
DEAR SWAMIJI,
I have your letter. Of course during my visit to Madras, I
would like to visit many places. But I am afraid I shall have
to deny myself that pleasure. At the present time, the idea is to
confine the visit to Madras, stay there for some time, and do the
work that I can. Therefore, for the sake of the cause itself, all
friends must spare me as far as possible. The forthcoming Bengal
visit will tell me what my body is now capable of standing.
A. S. SAHAJANANDA
NANDANAR MUTT
CHIDAMBARAM
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
