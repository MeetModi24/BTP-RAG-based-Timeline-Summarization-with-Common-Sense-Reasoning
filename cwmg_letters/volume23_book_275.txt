275. LETTER TO H. M. PEREIRA
POST ANDHERI,
April 5, 1924
MY DEAR PEREIRA,
I thank you for your letter of the 25th February last.
I have received the interesting cutting you have sent me.
Please continue to send cuttings that you may come across in
connection with the national movement.
I remember your father well. What are you doing there?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. M. PEREIRA, ESQ.
MERRICK
LONG ISLAND
NEW YORK, U.S.A.
From a photostat: S.N. 8666
