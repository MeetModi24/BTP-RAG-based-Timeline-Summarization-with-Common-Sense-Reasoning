37. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
Sunday [On or after May 10, 1925]
DEAR BRIJKRISHNA,
Your letter. I trust you; but to seek financial assistance on
someone's behalf is outside my province. If I were to act thus
with my rich friends our relations would become defiled. In this
matter you must solve your difficulties by your own efforts.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 2370
