25. LETTER TO DR. B. S. MOONJE
ABBOTTABAD,
July 23, 1939
DEAR DR. MOONJE1,
I like your frank letter. But I cannot make the response you
desire, for our ways are so hopelessly different.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. B. S. MOONJE
19 ORTHODOX QUARTER
CARD ROAD, SIMLA
B. S. Moonje Papers. File No. 24/1939. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
