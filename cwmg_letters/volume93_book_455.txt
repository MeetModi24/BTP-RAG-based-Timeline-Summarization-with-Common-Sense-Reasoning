455. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 13, 1939
CHI. SUSHILA,
If Prithvi Singh can do the massage, you must tell him.
Kr[ishna] Ch[andra] must be told about bathing. Both of them
have to be seen on the first day. If I have to do all that myself,
it would be a great burden on my silence. It would be difficult
to write down the instructions for them every time. Hence, in
these modifications I need your complete co-operation.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
