436. LETTER TO G. L. KHANOLKAR
SEVAGRAM,
August 28, 1941
DEAR KHANOLKAR,
I had your wire as also your letter. I am in correspondence
with Vijayalakshmibehn as also others and am doing all that is
possible for me. How did you come to know of the treatment?
Or are you relying upon the newspaper reports? Keep me posted
with all the information you can get. What are you doing in Cawn-
pore?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
