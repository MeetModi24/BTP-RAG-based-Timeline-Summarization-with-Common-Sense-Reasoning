282. LETTER TO VASUDEV V. DASTANE
[May 28, 1946]
BHAI DASTANE,
Bapa writes that you have started Harijan work in your
area in right earnest and are likely to make a success of it. I do
hope you will.
I expect everything else is all right there.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VASUDEV DASTANE
BHUSAVAL
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
