85. LETTER TO THAKUR PRASAD SHARMA
May 13, 1935
BHAI THAKUR PRASAD SHARMA,
Good relations cannot be brought about by concealing our
faults. If both the parties jointly appoint an arbitrator, they need
not go to the law-court. But if this is not possible I see nothing
wrong in making an attempt to obtain justice through the courts.
One cannot lay down a fixed principle in such matters. Every
case is to be judged on its own merits.
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI THAKUR PRASAD
JALPA DEVI
BENARES CITY
