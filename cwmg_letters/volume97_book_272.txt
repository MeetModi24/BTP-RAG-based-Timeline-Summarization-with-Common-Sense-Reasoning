272. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
WARDHA,
April 29, 1935
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
I did get the figures about jaggery and sugar sent by you. I have found
no exaggeration in them. They lie buried in other papers. I will look for
them today.
If no paper is available there, it might perhaps be unnecessary to order
new paper to be made. Where such paper cannot be procured at all, I would
not consider it right for a poor man to get it from outside and use it. That
you are able to get milk is welcome news. If despite your effort, you cannot
obtain cow's milk, use goat's.
Blessings from
BAPU
