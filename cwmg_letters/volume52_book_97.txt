97. LETTER TO RUKMINIDEVI BAZAJ
November 26, 1932
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your postcard. Keep the resolution which you have
made. A person who learns to keep his small resolutions auto-
matically acquires the strength to keep big ones. You must do
something to improve your health. I hope there is no cause
now to make you inwardly unhappy. If you come here and live
with me, I would daily give you fresh goat's milk to drink. Whose
handwriting do you see above? Try and recognize it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9146
