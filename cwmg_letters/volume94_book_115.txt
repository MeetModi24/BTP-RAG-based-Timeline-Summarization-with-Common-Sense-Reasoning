115. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
October 20, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Your cover was so heavy that it got tom and was sent in a
Government cover.
I have broken your rule quite a bit. Hence, I also observe silence
in the evening while keeping the eyes closed. I stop at exactly 8.30
at night. My health is of course good. If I do more work, the blood-
pressure rises. My weight went down by one lb. yesterday. I think
it was due to the rush of work. My diet has been all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
