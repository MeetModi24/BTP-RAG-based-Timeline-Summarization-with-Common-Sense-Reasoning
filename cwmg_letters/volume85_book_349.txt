349. LETTER TO PRABHU
NEW DELHI,
September 9, 1946
BHAI PRABHU,
Sushilabehn has given me your two cheques. The beginning
has been good. The receipt, etc., will be sent by Kanu Gandhi.
It will go along with this letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Both the cheques will be utilized only for the work that
was dear to Mahadev.
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 9229
