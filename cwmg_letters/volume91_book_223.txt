223. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
LAHORE,
Magsar Sud 14 [December 6, 1919]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
Bhai Ratanlal of Hindustan has passed away. Please go and
look up his family. Let me have an idea of their situation.
Also let me know who now looks after Hindustan. You should
also meet Bhai Mavji Govindji and inform me of the latest posi-
tion regarding the Padhiar memorial.
CHI. MATHURADAS TRIKUMDAS
247 BAZAR GATE STREET
FORT, BOMBAY
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
