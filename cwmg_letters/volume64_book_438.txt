438. LETTER TO VIYOGI HARI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
March 8, 1937
BHAI VIYOGI HARI,
Anasaktiyoga in Hindi should be available at the Sasta Sahitya
Mandal. If it is available there or anywhere else, do arrange to
send a copy of it to Rajkumari Amrit Kaur, care of Mr. Fielden,
4, Bhagwandas Road, New Delhi. We reach Delhi on Monday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1096
