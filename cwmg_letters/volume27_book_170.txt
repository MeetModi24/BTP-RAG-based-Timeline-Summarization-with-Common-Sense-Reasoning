170. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
Ashad Sud 1 [June 22, 1925]
BHAISHRI DEVCHANDBHAI,
I can understand monetary help from the Princes. I
hesitate however to entrust all the work to their officials. But I
shall be able to come to a definite decision after all of you meet
and think about it. We have to think how we can reduce our ex-
penses every year and put the people on their own feet.
Blessings from
BAPU
