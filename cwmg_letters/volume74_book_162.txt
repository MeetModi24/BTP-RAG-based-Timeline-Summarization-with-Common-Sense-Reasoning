162. LETTER TO NATVARLAL J. VEPARI
June 9, 1941
BHAI NATVARLAL,
I got your letter. I am sending to Chandrashankar the papers
sent by you, and asking him to send his reply immediately.
I see that Chandrashankar has no more trust in your im-
partiality. He often writes to Mahadev, and sometimes to me
also, complaining against you, but I didn't think it necessary to
take up your time by sending his letters to you. I have written
to him that the final decision will be mine and that your re-
port will cover only the figures.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10120
