3. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 3, 1930
CHI. GANGABEHN JHAVERI,
The letters received in May were handed over to me only a
short while ago. I have faith in your strength and devotion to God.
He will certainly give you wisdom. Take care of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Give my Vandemataram to Chhotubhai.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3101
