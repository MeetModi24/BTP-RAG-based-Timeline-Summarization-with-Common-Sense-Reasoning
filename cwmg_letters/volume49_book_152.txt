152. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
February 18, 1932
CHI. VANAMALA,
Take this a letter for you in your own strip of paper. When you
have no ruled paper, you should rule a sheet with your own hands.
When you see Narahari, tell him that I was glad he asked
for and got class 'C'. I lay down one condition, however. He
should take care of his health and not fall ill again. See that you
learn now to write a letter properly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5765. Also C.W. 2988. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
