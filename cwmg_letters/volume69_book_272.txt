272. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
RAJKOT,
May 13, 1939
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. It was for your own good that I did
not bring you up to Viramgam. For your sake I drank the
mango juice you had prepared even though I had no desire
for it. What can I do if you go on fidgeting unnecessarily?
You have no faith either in God or in me. Your heart is full of
misgivings which consume you. You are always in a harassed
state. I tell you, you must do the work you have been doing.
You will gain everything through it.
I am quite well. There is no cause to worry about the
Hindu-Muslim problem.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 416
