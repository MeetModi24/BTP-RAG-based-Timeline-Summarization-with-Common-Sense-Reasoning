59. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
[As AT] SEGAON,
June 13, 1936
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have written to Mangaldas during the free time I now
have in Madras. If there is time Mahadev will enclose a copy.
I hope you did not find the journey difficult. Finish your work
soon and come back. Do keep up the walks.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
89 WARDEN ROAD, BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 195
