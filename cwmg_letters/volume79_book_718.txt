718. LETTER TO GOPINATH BARDOLOI
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 23, 1945
BHAI BARDOLOI,
I find your Hindustani very sweet. I do not find any difficulty
in reading your handwriting. You must improve your Hindustani.
You handwriting should also be better than Mahavir's. Make
an effort.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GOPINATH BARDOLOI
GAUHATI, ASSAM
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
