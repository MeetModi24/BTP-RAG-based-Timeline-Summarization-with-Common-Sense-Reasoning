27. LETTER TO BERYL
SEVAGRAM,
January 9, 1945
MY DEAR BERYL,
So you are at last fixed up. I hope the choice is good. Do
write everything to me.
I prize your khadi and more so your handwritten "Khalil
Gibran". I have commenced reading it.
Do write to me all about Mother. She has suffered. R. K.
has told me all about her.
Love and kisses.
BAPU
BERYL
C/O RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
SIMLA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
