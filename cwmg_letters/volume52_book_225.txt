225. LETTER TO ASHRAM BOYS AND GIRLS
December 11, 1932
DEAR BOYS AND GIRLS,
You should know that I have suggested the changes in food in
order to improve the health of the people and increase their self-
control and peace of mind. Those who do not like them may
not try them. Those who have tried them and found that they
do not suit them may give them up. Nobody should accept
them unwillingly or out of false shame. I hope you observe
silence during meals and when spinning for yajna.
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II
