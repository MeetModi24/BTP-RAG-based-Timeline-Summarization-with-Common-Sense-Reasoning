5. LETTER TO MOHAN N. PARIKH
September 1, 1932
CHI. MOHAN1,
If your handwriting is not good there is no harm in dictating
your letter. This does not mean that you may neglect to improve
your handwriting. You are now big enough to be able to write
a good hand.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9182
