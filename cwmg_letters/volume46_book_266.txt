266. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
As AT SABARMATI,
May 26, 1931
Here is a letter from L. R. Guruswamy Naidu of Koilpatti
Taluq Congress Committee. I enclose also a copy of my letter
to him. You should vigorously move in the matter if the facts
are correctly set forth in Guruswamy's letter.
I expect to see you in Bombay on the 9th.
SYT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARIAR
GANDHI ASHRAM
TIRUCHENGODU (S. INDIA)
From a microfilm: S.N. 17148
