15. LETTER TO KRISHNADAS
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 13, 1926
MY DEAR KRISHNADAS,
I have your letter. I want you to continue to write to me
regularly.
I agree that it is better for Guruji at the present moment
not to be in Calcutta. After all the climate in Calcutta is by no
means ideal. His health is a paramount consideration, the com-
pletion of your book a subsidiary one and I certainly like the
idea of your cooking also for him. A delicate and loving touch
makes all the difference in the world when the cooking has to be
done for a patient so responsive to environment as Guruji.
I am slowly gathering strength and giving myself complete
rest. Subbiah attends to all the English correspondence and
Chandra Shankar now takes down Gujarati dictation. Presently
I shall be dictating articles for Young India and Navajivan. Devdas
is still at Deolali. Nobody has heard of Tulsi Maher for over 10
days now. Do you know where he is?
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 14101
