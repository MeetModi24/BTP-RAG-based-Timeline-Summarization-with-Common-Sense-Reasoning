185. LETTER TO SOHANLAL
NEW DELHI,
June 27, 1947
BHAI SOHANLAL,
I have your letter. I have no doubt that we should continue
our work. The result is in God's hands. Even so ask Shri Jajuji.
If you want to send him this postcard do so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 6865
