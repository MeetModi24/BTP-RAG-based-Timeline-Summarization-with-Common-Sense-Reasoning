349. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
SEGAON,
June 17, 1937
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I have your letter. What a life! But I have nothing to
say as long as you derive satisfaction by living there. Write to
me whenever you find an opportunity. Give me an account
of your activity.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6551
