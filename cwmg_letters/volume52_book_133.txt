133. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
November 1932
CHI. PANDITJI,
The Bavas have proved quite a challenge for us. You did
right in going. That temple also will be opened to Harijans
one day. However much disheartened you may feel by the atmos-
phere in the surrounding villages, you should continue to visit
them. Love will melt all knots.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 238. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
