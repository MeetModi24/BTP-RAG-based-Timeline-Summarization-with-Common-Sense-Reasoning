582. LETTER TO P. R. LELE
February 28, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I knew about
Jaisukhlal's illness and I wrote to him on Sunday. Will you be
in full charge then? I received today Sjt. Tricumdas's letter with
opinion. I have sent him a letter of acknowledgement.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. R. LELE
THE RECLUSE
31 MURZBAN ROAD
FORT, BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 20405
