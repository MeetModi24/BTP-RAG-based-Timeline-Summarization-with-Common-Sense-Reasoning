310. LETTER TO DR. S. C. BANERJEE
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
September 14, 1928
DEAR SURESH BABU,
I have your letter. This is what you said in the letter dated
7th August in answer to which I sent the telegram:
Herewith I am sending a copy of our Ashram report for 1927. We
shall be much obliged if you can kindly give publicity to it in some issue
of Young India with suitable comments.
It is this report that I sent for so as to enable me to deal
with it in the pages of Young India as you desired. I am still without
that report.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13684
