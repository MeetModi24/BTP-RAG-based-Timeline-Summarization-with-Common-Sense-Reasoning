350. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
POONA,
March 21, 1946
CHI. LILAVATI,
One should never rush in haste to a conclusion. An institu-
tion cannot transgress its framework of rules whether good or
bad. What you write about the medical [technique] is not some-
thing which can be summarily dismissed. Vijaya should firmly
stick to her studies. No one forbids her to study. In the mean-
while she will have an opportunity to take the examination. We
can easily absorb her in the Kasturba Gandhi National Memorial
Trust work when she is fully qualified even before her examination.
Don't let her lose heart. Nor should you despair in her case.
Let me know, if there is any hitch in her attending the classes.
I shall be prepared to have her examined privately.
All of you have to learn the lesson from Vijaya's case that
a degree [by itself] does not qualify one, although it will often
be a sign of being qualified. One does come across such cases
nowadays. If a person has the same name as a degree-holder's,
or adopts it, then steals the certificate and puts up a sign-board,
would people go to him for long? Have there been any such
cases? I have very little knowledge of all this. Hence it would
be more than having a degree, if Vijaya acquires real proficiency.
I can actually point out a number of such cases.
I understand what you say about your examination.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. LILAVATI ASAR
