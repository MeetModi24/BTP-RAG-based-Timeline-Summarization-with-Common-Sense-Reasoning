441. LETTER TO S. SATYAMURTI
As AT SABARMATI,
May 15, 1931
MY DEAR SATYAMURTI,
I have your letter. So far as I know there is no chance of
my going to London. Several things have to happen before I
could consent to go.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: S. Satyamurti Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
