219. LETTER TO R. K. PRABHU
POONA,
June 16, 1944
DEAR PRABHU,
I have read your letter to Pyarelal. I want to see you when I have
finished the medical treatment and am free for work. I wanted to write
to you even before your letter reached me. That was in connection
with the book you have written. I am now discussing with the Navajivan
Trust the whole thing. I shall do nothing in haste and certainly not
without meeting you. You are no stranger to me.
Yours,
BAPU
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
