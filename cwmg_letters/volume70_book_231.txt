231. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 24, 1939
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
My blessings are always there for as many khadi bhandars
as are opened and for as much khadi as is sold. Moreover
Sardar is opening your bhandar. So it is bound to be a success.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10842. Courtesy: Purushottam
K. Jerajani
