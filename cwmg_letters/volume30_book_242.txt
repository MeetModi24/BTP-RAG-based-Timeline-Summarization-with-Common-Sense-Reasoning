242. LETTER TO REV. JOHN M. DARLINGTON
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 1, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I remember our meeting.
The incident referred to by you has never happened. I do
not know where the report occurs. Whilst I have the highest
regard for the teachings of Jesus, I have never held the belief at-
tributed to me.
Yours sincerely,
REV. JOHN M. DARLINGTON
14-2, SUDDER STREET
CALCUTTA
From a photostat: S.N. 19406
