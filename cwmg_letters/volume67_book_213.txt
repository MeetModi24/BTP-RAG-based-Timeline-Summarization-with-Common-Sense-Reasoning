213. LETTER TO RAMNIK
July 8, 1938
CHI. RAMNIK,
I get from time to time news of your illness from your
father. I understand that you are not recovering. To get well, to
fall ill or to remain ill, all this is not in our hands. But it is in
our power to preserve equanimity in all circumstances, not to
forget God and not to lose the joy within. I hope you are
preserving all this.
Blessings from
BAPU
