514. LETTER TO P. N. RAJBHOJ
April 19, 1933
BHAI RAJBHOJ,
Your Hindi letter is extremely good.
I trust you are comfortable in the Ashram.
I shall make an effort to answer your questions in the Harijan
Sevak. Nevertheless I may state this.
Here is one way of eliminating caste distinctions among Hari-
jans-the caste considered higher should establish all social rela-
tions with the castes considered lower.
Wherever underground drainage is installed the Harijans
should master the craft of weaving. There is no harm in seeking
the assistance of the police wherever Harijans are prevented from
using the palki. There are other ways also which I shall mention
in the Harijan Sevak.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 784
