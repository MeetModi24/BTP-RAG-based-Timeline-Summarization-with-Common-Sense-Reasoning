213. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 12, 1937
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
I have your letter. I have not forgotten the Andaman Prison-
ers. Where are they being treated as 'C' class prisoners? So
far as I know these class distinctions are gone. These friends
should help the Congress Ministers for they have their difficulties.
You may give them the assurance that I shall strain every nerve
not merely to see that they are well treated but that they are
discharged. But I must be able myself to give the assurance in
every individual case. Their noble response to me has paved the
way, but it ought not to be held as sufficient for full fruition
of the common hope. The main reason for my going to Calcutta
for the A. I. C. C. is to examine the whole position, and to see
what I can do in the matter.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
