461. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
DELHI,
April 15, 1946
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have instructed Rajkumari to reply to your letters. I cannot even
write letters, because there is no time.
I have been thinking about Dilip. I have talked over the matter.
I had a long talk with Usha's father as well. I feel the girl is nice.
I even had a talk with her. The father says that the boy and the girl
may meet and marry if they feel so inclined. As I am writing this,
Sushila gives me your letter. Seeing that your letter is in English, it
occurs to me that the matter should be kept from Delhi. I find the
talk of dark and fair irksome. And I would not be happy if the decision
to marry was guided by consideration of external beauty. Now you
may do whatever you think fit. We should not do anything by which
the father and daughter may be left in the lurch. Hence write to me
a prompt reply.
Are you still having allergy rashes or have they subsided ?
Blessings from
BAPU
