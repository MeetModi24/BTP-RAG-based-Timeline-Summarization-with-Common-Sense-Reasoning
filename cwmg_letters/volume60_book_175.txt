175. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
January 30, 1935
BHAI KHAMBHATTA,
I hope you are now improving. You must give up your attach-
ment to Bombay. Be content with what God has given you.
Are you likely to find any difficulty in living in Poona? Do let
me know.
Blessings from
BAPU
