233. LETTER TO BALBHADRA
SODEPUR,
December 6, 1945
BHAI BALBHADRA,
I have your letter. Since Satyawati has passed away, I should
certainly like to be better acquainted with you.
SHRI BALBHADRAJI
6 PYARELAL BUILDING
KASHMERE GATE, DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
