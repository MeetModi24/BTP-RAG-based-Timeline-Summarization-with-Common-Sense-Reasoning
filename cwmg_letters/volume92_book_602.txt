602. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
YERAVDA MANDIH,
November 2, 1932
CHI. NANIBEHN,
I had received Gangabehn's own letter. You have been
detained in order to nurse the patients. If we look at it object-
ively this is wrong. But we cannot always do so. So it is all
right that you are detained. Try to free yourself at the first
opportunity. But give service without hesitation so long as it is
necessary. Do not think that you are committing any wrong.
Hence, get rid of your sadness of heart.
Both of you always have my blessings. May God protect you
and may your desire to serve grow from day to day.
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari - Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 27
