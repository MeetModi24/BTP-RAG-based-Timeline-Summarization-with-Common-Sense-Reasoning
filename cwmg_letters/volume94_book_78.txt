78. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM,
September 21, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Pyarelal writes that you have a slight temperature because of a
throat infection. How can you have a bad throat ? Surely you remember
the English saying : Physician, heal thyself! So, both you and Annapurna
should get well quick.
Is there any doubt you will be with me for the coming [rentia]
baras ?
Your letter of the 15th was received after a delay of two days.
It must have just remained in the letter-box of the post office.
I am getting on quite well. Today my weight was a little over 103
lbs. It means an increase of at least 1½ lbs. In other ways too
I am quite well. I am more or less sticking to the time of going to
bed. I am not able wholly to avoid talking while walking.
All patients are well. Prabhakar is having something like eczema
on his hands and legs. Hence, I have kept him on leafy vegetables
and fruit. That has done him a lot of good.
What has been decided about Mother ?
Blessings from
BAPU
