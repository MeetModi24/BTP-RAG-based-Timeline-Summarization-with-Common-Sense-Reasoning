145. LETTER TO KAMALA NEHRU
October 4, 1934
CHI. KAMALA,
You must have received the letter I wrote to you. I do wish
to come to you but also realize that it would not be right. From
somewhere or other I manage to get news of you. And Feroz
sends a daily postcard since Prabhavati came away. Keep up
your spirits. You should not worry on account of Jawaharlal's
absence from your side. It is good that Indu has arrived. Tell
her to write to me. Recite Ramanama.
Blessings from
BAPU
KAMALA NEHRU
ALLAHABAD
[From Hindi]
From the manuscript ofMahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
