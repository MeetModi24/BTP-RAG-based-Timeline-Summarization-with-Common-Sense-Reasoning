30. LETTER TO PARASURAM MEHROTRA
BORSAD,
June 20, 1931
CHI. PARASRAM,
I had your letter. Why do you ask whether or no I have
confidence in you? If I had none, could I have exhorted you to
make big sacrifices?
Yet, I may tell you that I cannot pro-
nounce you free from restlessness. Can you spin 250 yds. in an
hour on the takli? The pictures you have sent are highly ob-
jectionable. All of them deserve to be burnt up.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I will look into members' spinning.
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7489; also C.W. 4966. Courtesy:
Parasuram Mehrotra
