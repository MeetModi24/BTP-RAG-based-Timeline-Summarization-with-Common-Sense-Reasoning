323. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 12, 1940
CHI. MIRA,
I have your second letter. I have given you all the details
I knew of P1. You are not to defy any orders. There is no C.D.
for anybody as yet. When it is declared those engaged in con-
structive activities will be unaffected. You are engaged in such.
Why do you anticipate my orders? Last time you were engaged
in political activities. This time you are not. A satyagrahi is
always prepared for, but does not anticipate, repression. He Im-
putes no evil to his opponent. Here is an interruption.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6451. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10046
