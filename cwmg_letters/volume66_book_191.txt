191. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
September 21, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
Please have a talk with R. But [before that] ask Appa. If
R. is impotent, G. cannot but know about it. Then she should
not live with him, nor should she be harassed. R. must have
taken treatment. However, if medicines always worked, why do
we have so many advertisements about them? But ascertain all
the facts. Go and find out what G. has done and what the
atmosphere there is like.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11571
