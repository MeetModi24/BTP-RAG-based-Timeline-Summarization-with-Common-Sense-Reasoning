347. LETTER TO ANNIE MASCARENE
SEVAGRAM,
March 18, 1945
DEAR MASCARENE,
I remember you showed me Sir M. Ismail's letter in which he
asserted that you had never spoken ill of anyone behind his back.
As to the 2nd question I can give no opinion. I do not profess
to understand Travancore politics. You should do what you think is
best.
Yours.
BAPU
MISS A. MASCARENE
STATE CONGRESS OFFICE
