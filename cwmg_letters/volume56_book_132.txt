132. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
October 21, 1933
CHI. VANAMALA,
I hope you have now completely got over your illness. Now
go and join Anandi, Bachu and Babu as soon as possible.
If you
had been well and had come here, both you and I would have
been happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5782. Also C.W. 3005. Courtesy:
Vanamalabehn M. Desai
