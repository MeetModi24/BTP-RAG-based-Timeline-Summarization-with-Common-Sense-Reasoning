291. LETTER TO BHAU PANSE
February 1, 1933
CHI. BHAU,
How did you get fever? I hope you are all right now. If
your health does not improve in Rajkot, you will come here. We
shall then think about a fast. In Rajkot you may eat the food which
you get in the normal course, that is, rotli made from wheat flour,
ghee, milk, green vegetables and fruits. Jamnadas knows some-
thing about this subject. Follow his advice. There is also a good
vaid there. If necessary, you may consult him. Instead of my
advising you from here, I think it will be better if you let Jamnadas
guide you there. Show this letter to him. Write to me regularly.
BAPU
