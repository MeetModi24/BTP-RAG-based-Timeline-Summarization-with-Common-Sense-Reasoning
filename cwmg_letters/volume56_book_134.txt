134. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
October 21, 1933
CHI. JAISUKHLAL 1 ,
I got your letter.
I had heard about Umiya's illness. I had talked about her
with Jamnalalji. You should solve the problem about the charkhas
there. One of you should try to gin with the help of one of your
charkhas and make the necessary changes in it. Keshu's letter
is enclosed.
Vinod and Kusum wrote to me also. I hope you keep good
health.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
You must have read everything about Prabhudas's marriage.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./III
