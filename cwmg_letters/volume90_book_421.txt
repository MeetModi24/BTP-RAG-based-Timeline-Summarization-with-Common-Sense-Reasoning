421. LETTER TO PIROJ WADIA
NEW DELHI,
January 19, 1948
DEAR SISTER,
Did you do your Gujarati? Can you read this? I read
your letter to Sushila. God has seen us through this penance.
It remains to be seen how the people behave now. You should
both do there what you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
PIROJ WADIA
BOMBAY
