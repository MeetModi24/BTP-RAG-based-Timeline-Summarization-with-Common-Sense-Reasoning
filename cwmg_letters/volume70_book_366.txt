366. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 30, 1939
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Strange! No letter, no telegram! Rajen Babu and I are off
to Delhi tomorrow to see the Viceroy. Hope to return on Thurs-
day.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
POONA HOTEL
POONA
From the original: C.W. 3662. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6471
