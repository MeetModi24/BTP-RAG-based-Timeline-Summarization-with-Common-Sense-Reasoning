135. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
BORSAD,
May 8, 1931
CHI. NARANDAS,
I got your two letters.
I felt a weight off my mind to know that you approved of
my suggestion regarding Lakshmi. Kaka and Vallabhbhai are also
of the same opinion as yours. I have yet to hear from Vinoba
and Kishorelal. I was not at all pleased by Dwarkanath's bill.
I have kept it back and written to him strongly about it. I will
let you know after I hear from him. You need, therefore, do
nothing about it for the present. The general suggestion you have
made concerning such bills does not require to be acted upon
immediately.
123
I understand what you say about Shankarbhai. I wholly
agree with your decision. Don't mind what Bhagwanji thinks
about it. You will have to pay Rs. 35 to that Punjabi vaid. All
of them charge their fees in this way. If all the patients im-
prove, the fee charged by him will not seem excessive. All the
same, we should know on what basis he charges his fee. You,
therefore, did right in writing to Chandrashankar about it. Don't
put too many persons under his treatment at a time. Watch how
he treats each case and then proceed further. I shall be leaving
for Simla on Monday. No address is necessary for writing to
me there. I have replied to Pragji. Rukhi and Benarsi have
arrived. The latter is returning today. Rukhi will stay on today
at any rate. I may send her by some train tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
