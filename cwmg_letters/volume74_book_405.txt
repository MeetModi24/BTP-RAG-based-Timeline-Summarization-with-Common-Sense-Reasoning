405. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
August 21, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
There is no letter from you today. I hope this is not due
to illness.
Jamnalalji liked Anandamayi Devi's Ashram. He could get
peace there. He sent me a telegram to permit him to stay there
for some more time. I have said yes. He had a long talk with
Jawaharlal and had fruit with him. He also met Indu.
Here everything is all right. Sardar was released, and I
had a talk with him on the phone. I hope you did not find it
difficult to decipher this letter. Do you mind its being in
Hindi?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 7883. Also C.W. 4251. Courtesy:
Amrit Kaur
