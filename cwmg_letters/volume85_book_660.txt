660. LETTER TO SANKARAN
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
CHI. SANKARAN,
If you remember the particular issue of Jeevan-Sakha, I shall
obtain it from here. Perhaps it cannot be said that you possess
scientific knowledge.
That you have denied being a doctor is
quite proper.
You will gain nothing by going around with me.
By all
means work in the Talimi Sangh if Aryanayakumji will have you.
What is the need of taking Jajuji into the Trust? He
carries on his own activity. I do not find any harm in your
working there. He will have assistance and you will add to your
experience. My experience is little. You may sleep if you must.
It will not be right to retire to a village.
502
It will be good to have a talk with Su[shila]behn.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. SANKARAN
