474. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Saturday [May 1, 1926]
BHAI RAMESHWARJI,
I have your letter. I will get the Rs. 50. It is difficult to
cure your physical ailment till your mental illness is cured. You
might possibly benefit by living in a quiet place for some time.
And of course there is Ramanama.
Yours,
MOHANDAs
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 163
