747. LETTER TO DILIP KUMAR ROY
WARDHA,
February 21, 1934
DEAR DILIP,
It was a great grief to me that though I was in Pondicherry,
I was not able to see any of you.
Ambalal Sarabhai gave me your letter of October only
yesterday. It had gone with Bharati to Oxford. I wrote to you
about your book when I received it. I hope you got that letter.
Do write to me whenever you feel like writing. I am glad
H. is there. Has he given up drink altogether? Tell him he owes
me a letter. I would like to hear from him.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Golden Book of Dilip Kumar Roy, p. 122
1 Daughter of Ambalal Sarabhai
2 Harindranath Chattopadhyaya, husband of Kamaladevi
