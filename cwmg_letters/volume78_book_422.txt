422. LETTER TO H. J. KHANDEKAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 16, 1944
BHAI KHANDEKAR,
I got your letter. I was grieved to learn about the death
of your child. May God give you peace and patience. I have the
notes of the talks I had with you but I do not wish to send
them. Not words but your conduct alone can inspire confidence.
May God bless you.
M. K. GANDHI
HEMCHANDRARAO JAGOBA KHANDEKAR
ITWARI
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
