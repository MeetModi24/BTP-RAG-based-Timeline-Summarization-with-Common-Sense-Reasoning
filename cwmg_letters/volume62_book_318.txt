318. LETTER TO LILI H. SHAH
March 20, 1936
CHI. LILI,
You are now about to fly away. But you could not get
time to write in ink the letter seeking my blessings! Are you
so busy with work, or are you mad with joy? Whatever the
case, you both certainly have my blessings. May you both live
a pure life, continue to be pure and dedicate yourselves to the
country.
Blessings from
BAPU
[C/O] SHRI HIRALAL AMRITLAL SHAH
CHAPSI BUILDING
PRINCESS STREET, BOMBAY 2
From Gujarati: C.W. 9720. Courtesy: Lilibehn A. Pandya
