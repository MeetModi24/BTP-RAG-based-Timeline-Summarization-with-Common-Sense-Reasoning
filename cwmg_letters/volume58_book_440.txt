440. LETTER TO MAHENDRA V. DESAI
September 3, 1934
CHI. MANU,
You have indeed taken good pains to improve your hand-
writing, but there is room for plenty of improvement still. I have
accepted Kaka's article. He should, therefore, send the second
instalment. How did you get conjunctivitis? Is the eye all right
now?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7467. Courtesy: Mahendra V. Desai
