274. LETTER TO VITHAL S. PANDIT
March 30, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your interesting letter of 26th instant describing your
work in the matter of untouchability. You should continue to
inform me of the progress from time to time. I don't need to give
you any special guidance, but I take it that you are diligently fol-
lowing the columns of the Harijan.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. VITHAL S. PANDIT
HON. SECRETARY
R. D. HARIJAN SEVA SANGH
KUDAL (DIST. RATNAGIRI)
From a microfilm: S.N. 20755
