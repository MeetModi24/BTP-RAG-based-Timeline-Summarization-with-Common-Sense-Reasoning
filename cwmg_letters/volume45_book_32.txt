32. LETTER TO MATHURI KHARE
December 27, 1930
CHI. MATHURI,
Is not this coloured paper lovely? It is a very good thing that
you no more doze off during prayers. With effort handwriting
can become as beautiful as pearls.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 258-a. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn Khare
