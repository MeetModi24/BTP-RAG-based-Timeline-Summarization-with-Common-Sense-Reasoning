140. LETTER TO KUNVAR SIR JAGDISH PRASAD
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 21, 1939
DEAR SIR JAGDISH,
I know you have been putting up a good fight on behalf of the
Indians in S. A. I have been staying their hand. But they are getting
restive. They won't be satisfied with any patch -work settlement. I
know, too, that nothing brave is to be expected from here. It is
therefore doubly necessary not to prolong the agony. If you can, you
should tell Rama Rau to keep himself in touch with the Passive
Resisters. As you know there are two parties there. He should take
the P.R.s into his confidence. They complain that he does not even
