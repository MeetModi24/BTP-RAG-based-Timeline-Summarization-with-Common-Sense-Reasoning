518. LETTER TO M. N. ROY
SEGAON, WARDHA,
July 27, 1937
DEAR FRIEND,
I entirely agree with you that every Congressman should fear-
lessly express the opinion he holds after due deliberation. You
ask me how you can best serve the Congress. Since you are new
to the organization, I should say you would serve it best by
mute service.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI M. N. ROY
"INDEPENDENT INDIA"
BOMBAY 4
Police Commissioner's Office File No. 3001/H, p. 23. Courtesy: Govern-
ment of Maharashtra
