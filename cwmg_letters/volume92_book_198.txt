198. LETTER TO JAYAPRAKASH NARAYAN
SABARMATI,
December 4, 1929
CHI. JAYAPRAKASH,
I was delighted to have your letter. I cherish the wish that
your marital life may be an ideal one. About Prabhavati's
attitude of indifference we shall talk when we meet.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From the Hindi original: Jayaprakash Narayan Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
