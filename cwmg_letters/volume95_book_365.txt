365. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI N. PATEL
IN MOVING TRAIN
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I have received all your letters. I shall reply when I am free. I
am helpless till I find some time.
Certainly there should be picketing of the main godown at
Anand. Tongawallas and others cannot be boycotted right now.
They can be persuaded. Immediate boycott would mean violence. A
minute dose of somal given with love can be life-saving. But a gram
of somal given with envy can take life.
From the photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 9012
