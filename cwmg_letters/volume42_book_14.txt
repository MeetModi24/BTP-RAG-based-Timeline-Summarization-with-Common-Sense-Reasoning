14. LETTER TO P. RANGANADAN
CAMP MUSSOORIE,
October 18, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I had hoped to deal with your matter much earlier but the
papers you gave me were left buried in an unassorted heap. I
have now come across those papers and you will find reference in
the ensuing number of Young India. The reason for writing this
letter is only to know what you are doing now and whether the
Board took any further action.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. RANGANADAN
SRI RAMANA BHAVANAM
ARNI (N. ARCOT DT.)
From a microfilm: S.N. 15678
