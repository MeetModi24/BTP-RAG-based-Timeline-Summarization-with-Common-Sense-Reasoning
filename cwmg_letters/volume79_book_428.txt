428. LETTER TO TARACHAND
SEVAGRAM,
March 15, 1945
BHAI TARACHANDJI,
I may write in Hindustani, may I not? What I say is clear.
If all the leaders are agreeable, I will not insist on a plebiscite.
They include independent Muslims, Sikhs and others. Everything
can be done if all of them will be happy.
If there is a trace of suspicion, it is better to give a reason-
able interpretation.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. TARACHAND
C/O SHRI RAJENDRANARAYAN
CHANDNI CHOWK
DELHI
