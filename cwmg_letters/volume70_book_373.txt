373. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 31, 1939
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
Why do you fall ill so often? You must take care of your
health. Send me wire at Delhi.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
11 CHOWPATTY SEAFACE
BOMBAY
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 237
