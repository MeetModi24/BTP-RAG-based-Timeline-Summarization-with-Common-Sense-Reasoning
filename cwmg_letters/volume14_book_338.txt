338. LETTER TO DATTATREYA DABHOLKAR
[NADIAD,
July 9, 1918]
CHI. DATTATREYA,
I congratulate you on your standing first in the examination of
standard five. I want that you should rank high in character just
as you maintain the first place in your studies.
I was happy that you donated the first month's amount of the
scholarship of the Ashram. When I go there, I shall explain to
those in the Ashram the meaning of your gift. These generous
inclinations which your father inculcates in you from this early
age are a noble inheritance. See that you nourish them.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. IV
