717. LETTER TO DEVENDRANATH DEVDHAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 23, 1945
CHI. DEVENDRA,
I am very happy to know that you have escaped death. The
truth is that God has saved you. He wanted to save you and
therefore the conditions became favourable. From your letters I
find that yours is a life of service. And now since God has given
you a new life be more service-minded and learn more for the
sake of service and lead a simple life.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
