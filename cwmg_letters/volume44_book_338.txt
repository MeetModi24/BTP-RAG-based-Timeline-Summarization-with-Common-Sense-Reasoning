338. LETTER TO BEHRAMJI KHAMBHATTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 20, 1930
BHAISHRI KHAMBHATTA,
I felt happy on reading your letter. I keep good health. How
is your health? I have no doubt at all that you two will always
use your gifts in the service of noble causes. Do you intend to
stay in Poona for the present?
To you both,
Blessings from
BAPU
