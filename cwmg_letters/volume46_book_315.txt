315. LETTER TO ANAND KISHORE MEHTA
AS AT SABARMATI,
May 31, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of the 27th instant which you have marked
"Reminder" . I am not aware of the receipt of a previous letter. I am
sorry to have to tell you that I cannot sympathize with the object
of your memorial, much less give my name to the Committee. If I
had any influence over the Committee I should advise its disband-
ment.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. ANAND KISHORE MEHTA
GENERAL SECRETARY, THE ALL-INDIA
BHAGAT SINGH, RAJGURU, SUKHDEV
MEMORIAL COMMITTEE
ANARKALI, LAHORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 17170
