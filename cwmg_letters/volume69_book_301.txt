301. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
RAJKOT,
May 23, 1939
CHI. PREMA,
I got your letter only today.
Immediately after reading it
I passed it on to Narandas. I had read about Deo in the
papers. The remedy is patient endurance and time. You need
not even reply to the charges, nor attend their meetings. If Deo
had not attended the meeting, Dr. Khare would not have got so
excited. If the opponent is not present, the critic finds no in-
terest in abusing him.
I see no need for you to give up associating with Deo. So
long as the hearts of both of you are pure and your association
is exclusively for service, I see no necessity at all for you to give
up Deo or change your work. Maybe it is necessary for you to
change your outward behaviour. That is for you to judge. I
might be able to judge if you were to see me and I could have
a heart-to-heart talk with you.
I am hoping to arrive in Bombay on the 2nd.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10399. Also C.W. 6838. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
