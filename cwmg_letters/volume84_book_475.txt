475. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
June 29, 1946
CHI. A. S.,
Can I point out a mistake when I see one? I had ordered
you to do what you wished. You acted on the advice and I
observed what you did. I showed my dislike of your engaging
in gossip.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G. N. 730
