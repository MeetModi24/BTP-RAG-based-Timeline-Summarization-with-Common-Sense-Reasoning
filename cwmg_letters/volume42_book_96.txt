96. LETTER TO S. SHANKAR
CAMP KHURJA,
November 3, 1929
MY DEAR SHANKAR,
I was glad to hear from you after a long time. Your letter
is very scrappy. I would like you to give me an accurate ac-
count of your khaddar work. Are you spinning regularly? Are
you making up Hindustani? I observed that you have more time
at your disposal than you need and I should have expected you
to utilize it for mastering the details of khadi technique and in
mastering Hindustani.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. S. SHANKAR
REDDY GARDEN
PERAMBUR (MADRAS)
From a microfilm: S.N. 15206
