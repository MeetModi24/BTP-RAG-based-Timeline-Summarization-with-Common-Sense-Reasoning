178. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Wednesday [March 24, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I have your letter. I have also got a letter from Hakim-
saheb. The following telegram has been sent to him today:
"Thanks letter. Any arrangement you friends may make
will suit."
Now whatever you decide will be final. You may, if you so
desire, put me at any other place before I proceed to Mussoorie.
As for me, I am prepared to go to Mussoorie direct. It does not
matter if it is too cold there; I can stand it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2860
