366. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
June 21, 1937
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I have read your letter and Jawahar's reply. It seems Nari-
man will fall into the pit he is digging himself. Let us wait and
see what steps he takes next. We needn't do anything in a hurry.
The matter is bound to come up at the Working Committee. It
is certainly meeting after a long interval, but that can't be helped.
Let events take their own course. I had a long letter from
Lothian, but I have not yet been able to read it. I hope you
are well enough now to move about.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 202
