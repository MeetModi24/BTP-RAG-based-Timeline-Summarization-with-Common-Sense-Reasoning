154. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
Thursday [January 21, 1926]
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I read your letter only on Tuesday; so I could not send for
Rameshwarprasad, but yesterday he called on me along with
Keshavdasji. I took them for a walk. I invited Rameshwar-
prasad to join the students in their prayer, and this he has started
doing from today. I give them readings from Bhaktarajani Yatra .
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2856
