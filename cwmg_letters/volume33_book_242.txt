242. LETTER TO ASHRAM WOMEN
[April 11, 1927]
SISTERS,
You have absolved me [from writing to you]. But how can I
take advantage of it unless I have to? My health is not so bad
now that I cannot write to you. Yesterday I even walked a
good distance. To write to you is therefore not much of a strain.
Have any of you decided to supervise the common kitchen
by turns? Lakshmibehn had definitely expressed willingness to
do so. If no one else has done it so far at least she should do
it. If there is any defect in the common kitchen, the fault will
be that of all. Will it not? You may free yourselves from kitchen
work when the men have acquired as much skill as you have at
present, but certainly not till then.
Please find enclosed Mirabai's letter.
Give it to Chi.
Maganlal. I send it as it is worth reading.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3644
