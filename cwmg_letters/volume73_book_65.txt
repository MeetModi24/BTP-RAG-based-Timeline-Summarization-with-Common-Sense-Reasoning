65. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
October 6, 1940
CHI. PREMA,
I have your letter and your book. I read the dedication.
I wore the dhoti and I still wear it along with the others. I
have kept the book with me. I do wish to go through it.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PREMABEHN KANTAK
ASHRAM, SASWAD
POONA DISTRICT
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10411. Also C.W. 6850. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
