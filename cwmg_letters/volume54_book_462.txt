462. LETTER TO M. N. CHOWDAPPA
April 14, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. You have not sent me the cutting con-
taining the address referred to by you. I remember that a portion
of the address was read to me but I did not then think that there
was anything for me to comment upon.
I do not understand the meaning of the 4th paragraph of
your letter.
Yours sincerely,
THE ASSTT. EDITOR
"PRAJA [MATA]1"
143- MINT ST.
MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 20937
