62. LETTER TO RANI VIDYAVATI
July 15, 1929
DEAR SISTER,
I have your sorrowful letter. What should I write to you?
God alone can give you strength. May it be well with you.
You should not start on uncooked food all of a sudden.
Leafy vegetables can be certainly taken raw. But if you want to
experiment, you should start with leafy vegetables, fruit and milk.
There is no harm in taking a little salt.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi Na-
tional Museum and Library
