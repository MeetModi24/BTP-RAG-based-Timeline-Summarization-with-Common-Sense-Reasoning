429. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
NEW DELHI,
4.15 a.m., July 23, 1947
CHI. SITA1,
I have your postcard. Why do you lose hope so soon? No
one has hurt your self-respect. Such things do happen when one
is a student; there is nothing unusual about it. You must calm
down and concentrate on your studies and build up a strong
body. Observe all the rules carefully. Keep writing to me.
Sumi will be well. Her Bengali handwriting seems good. Have
you tried to write something in Urdu below hers? Blessings to
Vinodini . Write to Manilal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers.
Courtesy: Pyarelal
