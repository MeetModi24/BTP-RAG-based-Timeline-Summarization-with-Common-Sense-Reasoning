185. LETTER TO SECRETARY, ALL-INDIA SPINNERS'
ASSOCIATION
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 30, 1928
TO
THE SECRETARY
A. I. S. A.
AHMEDABAD
DEAR SIR,
With reference to your letter No. 2169 dated 28th instant
regarding private agencies, it is difficult to give an opinion
straightway. I do consider it necessary to acquire greater control
over private agencies. Before I can advise, it will be well to obtain
concrete suggestions from the Tamilnad Agency.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13139
