140. LETTER TO SARDUL SINGH CAVEESHAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
January 18, 1940
DEAR CAVEESHAR,
I am surprised at Gianiji so hopelessly misunderstanding me.
What I said was that you never claimed to represent Sikhs in the
sense that you were above sectarianism. What was said was said
by way of a compliment to you. But you do not cease to be
a Sikh even as I don't cease to be a Hindu because I refuse to
represent those Hindus who believe untouchability to be part
of Hinduism.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
