497. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
WARDHA,
March 31, 1935
MY DEAR MALKANI,
Your letter. I have an impression that I wrote to you say-
ing that you might build with the second-hand material inclu-
ding burnt bricks. I note what you say about Amtul Salaam. She
is also a burnt brick—very much so. She deserves all the love
you can give her. I note also what you say about the Indore
boarding.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 914
