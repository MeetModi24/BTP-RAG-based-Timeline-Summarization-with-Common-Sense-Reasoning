592. LETTER TO S. D. SATAVLEKAR
ON WAY TO PESHAWAR,
October 5, 1938
BHAI SATAVLEKAR,
What a nice letter you have written to me! I am writing
this in a moving train. The Prince may certainly come in
November. For some days now I shall be in the Frontier Province.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 4779. Courtesy: S. D. Satavlekar
