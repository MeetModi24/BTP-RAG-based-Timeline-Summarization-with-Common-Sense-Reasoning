427. LETTER TO F. MART BARR
August 24, 1933
CHI. MARY,
I was sorry to part with you without having much of a chat
and then not to be able to write to you all these days was a
matter of grief to me. Now that I am able to write again I am
dictating as many letters as I can. I hope you are feeling quite
at home there. I want you to tell me everything about your
experience at Wardha and the people there. Of me, you will
have gleaned from so many sources. So I need not say anything.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6005. Also C.W. 3331. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
