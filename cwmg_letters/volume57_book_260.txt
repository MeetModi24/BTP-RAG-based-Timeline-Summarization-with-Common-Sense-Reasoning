260. LETTER TO HIRALAL SHARMA
BELGAUM,
March 6, 1934
BHAI SHARMA,
Your letter is good.
My advice to you is to use the house Jamnalalji suggests. You
should take the children too. Your duty is to look after them.
You should use the tent if one is provided. Your day must be
spent wholly in the Ashram.
I find your temperament is such
that you can adjust anywhere. I want you to reach the Ashram
soon. I have to take plenty of service from you. I want to know
your wife too. I pass through Wardha on the 10th instant. You
can try to arrive there by that date. I wish to send Amtus
salaam when you are at Ashram.
Blessings from
BAPU
