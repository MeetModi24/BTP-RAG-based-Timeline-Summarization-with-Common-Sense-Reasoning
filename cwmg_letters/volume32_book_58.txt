58. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
[November 19, 1926]
BHAISHRI DEVCHAND,
I have your letter. We should not put Dr. Pranjivan to
inconvenience But we can have Revashankerbhai Would you
like Nanabhai Narsinhprasad? Do you want Jamnalalji? We
have the big problem of English, otherwise we could have
Rajagopalachari. Would you like to have Kripalani? But I
should suggest that you choose a real lover of khadi. I think
we should look at the work of a man rather than his name. I
have received your wire. The 30th will suit me.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5705
