94. LETTER TO GIRDHARILAL
March 14, 1933
MY DEAR GIRDHARILAL,
I was glad to receive your weekly report and to notice that
the progress was maintained, though still slow. May it keep
steady and result in permanent cure is the wish and prayer of
us all.
Yours sincerely,
LALA GIRDHARILAL
109-A MAHENDRA MANSIONS
ESPLANADE ROAD, FORT
BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 20580
