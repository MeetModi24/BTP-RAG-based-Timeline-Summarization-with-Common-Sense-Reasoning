452. LETTER TO DR. HIRALAL SHARMA
April 13, 1933
DEAR DR. SHARMA,
I have your letter. I am glad that you would be in the
Ashram soon, if you are not already there by the time this reaches
the Ashram. I hope you have brought with you your four-year-
old daughter also and I hope that the little boy stuck up for his
sister. I would call him very unchivalrous if he did not insist
upon her being with him. You will please make yourself at
home in the Ashram and express to Narandas freely all your
requirements.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
DR. H. L. SHARMA
C/O THE ASHRAM
SABARMATI
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 29
