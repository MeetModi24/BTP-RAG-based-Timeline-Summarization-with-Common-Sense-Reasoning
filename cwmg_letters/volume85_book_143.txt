143. LETTER TO DR. B. S. MOONJE
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA C. P.,
August 8, 1946
BROTHER MUNJE,
I have both your letters. My views on bigamy are opposite
to yours. What can I do?
About Ahmedabad I have said what I wanted to.
Your
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Hindi : C. W. 9764. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
