155. LETTER TO SAMPURNANAND
SEVAGRAM,
May 31, 1940
CHI. SAMPURNANANDJI,
I have your letter. I have a fear that if there is disorder
I may prove ineffective. Because no one will listen to me. I
tell those who ask me that everyone should be at his post-using
the lathi if they must but with non-violence if they have the faith.
I do not worry, whatever may happen. We can but make
the effort and say: "God's will be done."
You cannot take office like that. If there is disorder, of what
good will the office be? Still, if I can find a way I shall.
There is no sign from Simla.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From the Hindi original: Sampurnanand Papers. Courtesy: National
