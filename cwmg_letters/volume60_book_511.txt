511. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
April 21, 1935
DEAR BIBI,
You must have got my letters. How did you fare in Khurja?
Now at least the idea of living at Harijanvas has been put off,
has it not? Your health must have improved. Are you coming
to Indore? Everything is all right here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 321
