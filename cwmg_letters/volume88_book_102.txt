102. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
DELHI,
June 11, 1947
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have your letter.
I get no free time at all, and so you
should not worry if I do not reply or am late in replying.
Kosambiji has passed away. We shall be honouring his
memory by sending out scholars of Pali.
We may take it that physical division of the country is now
certain. Since the Congress has accepted the plan, we must
now look for another way. That way I have been showing.
Just as land or other property can be partitioned, so also can
men's hearts. If, therefore, our hearts are true we can behave
as if they had not been partitioned.
I hope Kanchan and the baby girl are doing well. Kan-
chan must be delighted beyond measure.
Find out where I am in July. You will also have to con-
sider whether you will be able to free yourself from your work
at the time. I shall be here up to the 15th at any rate. After
that, Rama alone knows.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I got your long letter just now. I understand your unhappi-
ness. We shall talk over the matter when we meet.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8410. Also C. W. 5622. Cour-
tesy: Munnalal G. Shah
