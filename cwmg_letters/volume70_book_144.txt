144. LETTER TO SHIVJI DEVSHANKAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 25, 1939
BHAI SHIVJI,
I got your letter. Since people attach excessive importance
to my words, I write only what is absolutely necessary. Despite
that if once in a while a mistake remains, should not people
overlook it?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./III
