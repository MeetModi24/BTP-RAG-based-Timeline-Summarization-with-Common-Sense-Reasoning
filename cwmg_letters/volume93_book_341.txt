341. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 16, 1938
MY DEAR LOTUS-BORN,
So 17th Nov. is your birthday! Many happy returns. But
for us these returns can never be happy unless they provide us
with fresh scope for service. And you are going to Hyderabad
specially to render service. You will write to me of your doings.
You are under promise not to overstrain yourself.
Love.
Yours,
PLAYMATE
SHRI PADMAJA NAIDU
ZAHEER MANZIL
RED HILL
HYDERABAD DECCAN
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
