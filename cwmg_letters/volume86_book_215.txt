215. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA RANO
November 29, 1946
BHAI RANO,
I had your wire. As it is, I do not interfere in such matters.
Moreover I am not at all acquainted with this case.
I am returning the proforma for reply.
Vande Mataram from
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI RAMACHANDRA RANO
HARIJAN BADRI
HYDERABAD (SIND)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
