92. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
ON WAY TO DELHI,
September 13, 1924
MY DEAR R.,
I am on my way to Delhi for quarrel. I read your letter to
S. I am sad because you are sad. I wish you were by my side.
How can you continue when we [are] so hopelessly divided?
Read my article in current Young India fifty times if necessary and
you will find out my meaning perhaps. It is written most for
you. Your letter to S. suggested it. We are not giving up boycotts
because they are removed from the national programme for a
time. Surely, if we have courage of our convictions, we can revive
them any moment. Why should suspension mean abandonment
if we have trust in ourselves?
Yours,
M. K. G.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
