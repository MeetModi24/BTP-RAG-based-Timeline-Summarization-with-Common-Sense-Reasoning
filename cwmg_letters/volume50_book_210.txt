210. LETTER TO M. A. KHAN
July 14, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your admonition. You do not expect me to
argue with you. I fear that as a prisoner, I would not be permit-
ted to enter into argument over political matters. But I can
tell you that deep thinking in the solitude of a jail has not in-
duced a change in my outlook.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
MOULVI SAHEB M. A. KHAN
KOTWAL STREET BAZAAR 3
FEROZEPUR CANTT.
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. II, p. 185
