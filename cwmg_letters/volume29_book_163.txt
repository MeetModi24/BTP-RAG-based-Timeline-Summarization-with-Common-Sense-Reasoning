163. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Maha Vad 2 [January 30, 1926]
BHAI RAMESHWARJI,
I have your letters. A receipt has been sent to you for the
amount which will be spent according to your instructions. Next
time you want to send any money, please send it either by regis-
tered or insured packet. For your health, you should consult a
specialist doctor or a vaid and the disease can most likely be cured
if you follow his advice. Sprue is not an incurable disease. It is
necessary to control your diet. Ramanama itself is an invaluable
remedy for mental illness. You may find some difficulty in repeat-
ing that auspicious name but with constant and sincere efforts
you will come to like it. We must not hanker after preyas but only
mind our shreyas. And we should stick to it even though it may
not be pleasant. There is no doubt that its ultimate outcome will
certainly be very good.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 218
