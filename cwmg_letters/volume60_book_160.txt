160. LETTER TO RAYUDU RANGAIAH
January 29, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I do not understand the burden of your
complaint. You have made general statements. I would ask
you to discuss whatever you have with Sjt. Bapineedu, Secretary of
the local Sangh.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI RAYUDU RANGAIAH GARU
PRESIDENT
WEST GODAVARY DISTRICT
ADI-ANDHRA ASSOCIATION, ELLORE (W.GODAVARY DT.)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
