207. LETTER TO NILKANTH MASHRUWALA
Monday [August 13, 1928]
CHI. NILKANTH,
I had no wire from you, but I received one from Akola.
Balubhai's soul rests in peace. We may shed tears through sel-
fishness, but you at any rate have learnt the truth about the
highest good of human life. Use your wisdom at this juncture
and, displaying patience yourself, comfort others. Ask for any
service we can render from here.
Write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 9172. Courtesy: Nilkanth Mashruwala
