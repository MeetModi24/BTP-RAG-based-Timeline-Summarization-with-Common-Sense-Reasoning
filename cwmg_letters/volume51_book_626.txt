626. LETTER TO S. M. MICHAEL
November 11, 1932
I should be sorry, indeed, if my prospective fast should de-
prive anyone of his or her intellectual freedom. Anyway, I have
declared in emphatic terms that it is intended only to affect the
mass mind. Naturally friends and co-workers who believe in the
removal of untouchability will be stirred to action. That is no
matter for regret. Nevertheless, if you have a clear call of con-
science you will, of course, fast. But you will hardly expect it to
move me if I continue to believe that I am also obeying God's call.
The Hindu, 21-11-1932. Also Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 236
