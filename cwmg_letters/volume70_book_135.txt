135. LETTER TO POPATLAL CHUDGAR
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 22, 1939
CHI. CHUDGAR1,
Sardar is in sole charge of everything. I will approve of
whatever he decides, if at all my approval is necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI POPATLAL CHUDGAR
BARRISTER
RAJKOT CITY
KATHIAWAR
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9831.
Courtesy: Popatlal
Chudgar
