172. LETTER TO BHARATAN KUMARAPPA
January 26, 1940
MY DEAR BHARATAN,
I have sent a note of acknowledgement to R. B. Jesudasan.
You will be glad to know that the first parcel was found in the
dispensary safely put away. Sushila was not in Segaon when it
was received and so I must have given it to the compounder. I
could not have read the advertisement note, etc. However, all's
well that ends well.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3587
