279. LETTER TO RAMIBEHN K. PAREKH
PATNA,
March 12, 1934
CHI. RAMI,
I saw your handwriting after many months.
Please write
to me from time to time. Ba is always inquiring about you all.
She would feel happy if I could give her some news about you.
Take care of yourself. Ask Kunverji to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9720
