29. LETTER TO VIDYAVATI
October 15, 1941
CHI. VIDYA,
There is no end to your misery. But what is it to you? Some
may die today, some tomorrow, all are destined to die. Why
grieve over it? Moreover, you have dedicated yourself to the
country, then why be perturbed? Be alert, give courage to every-
one as before and become even more absorbed in service. May
God grant you peace.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Rani Vidyavati Papers.
