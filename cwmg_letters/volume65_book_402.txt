402. LETTER TO MIRZA ISMAIL
WARDHAGANJ,
June 28, 1937
DEAR SIR MIRZA,
I appreciate your letter. I must not anticipate the Working
Committee's decision. I know it will tax all its patriotic spirit.
I am in letter and in spirit praying for God's guidance during
those days.
My kindest regards to you, Lady Mirza, Humayun and the
rest of the family.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 2180. Also from a copy: G. A. Natesan Papers.
