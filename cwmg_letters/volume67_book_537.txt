537. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
September 20, 1938
CHI. BABUDI,
Write to me daily. Be careful about food. If there is any
trouble let me know. You will have started getting the men-
ses. If not, sitting in warm water will certainly bring them on.
Pyarelal's room is sufficiently bright. You should strip your-
self there, plaster your body with mud and walk about for a
364
while. Then when the mud is dry take a bath in the opposite room.
This will regularize the menses.
I have detained Dadasaheb only for your sake. Recite the
Gita properly. Learn music from Nanavati and English from
Bhansalibhai.
Keep the kitchen, etc., very clean.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 9996. Courtesy: Shardabehn G. Chokhawala
