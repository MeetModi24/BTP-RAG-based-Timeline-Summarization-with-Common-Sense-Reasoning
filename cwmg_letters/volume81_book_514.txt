514. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
September 29, 1945
CHI. RAMDAS,
I am sending this-Sumitra's letter1-because you must see it.
I would say that you should let Sumi gain experience by staying
at Pilani and Delhi. I am all right.
RAMDAS GANDHI
KHALASI LINES
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
