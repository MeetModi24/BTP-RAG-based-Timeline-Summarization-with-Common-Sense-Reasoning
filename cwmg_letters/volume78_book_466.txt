466. LETTER TO MARUTI SHARMA
SEVAGRAM,
November 23, 1944
CHI. MARUTI 1 ,
I was happy to have your letter. It was wise of you to have
cancelled your plans of coming here. Who but Bapa would
accommodate you? I have not seen many people whose gener-
osity can match Dada's [and] Bapa's. Do as he bids. It is
certainly a fine idea to build yourself a house. It is right that
you and Lakshmi practise abstention. In the present circum-
stances it is better not to go about meeting people.
It is
our duty to save every single pie.
I hope the children are
fine.
Blessings from
BAPU
