59. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 14, 1928
Here is a letter and copy of my reply. You will either
write to the complainant yourself or enable me to send him a further
reply.
I have your telegram which surprises me. I could not possibly
go to the length you suggest. Enclosed is a copy of the letter I
sent to Sir Mahomed Habibullah. Please discuss the subject no
more with anybody and destroy the enclosed.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
KHADI VASTRALAYA, ESPLANADE, MADRAS
From a photostat: S.N. 12788
