447. LETTER TO CHINNARAM THAPAR
CONTAI,
January 1, 1946
BHAI CHINNARAM,
I have your letter. I neither take any interest in the As-
sembly elections nor keep track of them.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI CHINNARAM THAPAR
VICE PRESIDENT DISTRICT CONGRESS COMMITTEE
LYALLPUR (PUNJAB)
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
