123. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 25, 1940
CHI. MIRA,
I have your chatty letter. I envy you your scenery. But I
have to be in the midst of the storm. I have taken indefinite si-
lence since yesterday for the sake of peace and work. It will
break for the W[orking] C[ommittee] or accidents.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6453.
Courtesy: Mirabehn.
Also G.N. 10048
