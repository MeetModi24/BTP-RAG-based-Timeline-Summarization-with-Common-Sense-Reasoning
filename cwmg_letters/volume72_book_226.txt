226. LETTER TO SIR SAMUEL HOARE
June 12, 1940
I was delighted to have your unexpected letter. I thank you
for it. It revived the memories of the frank and cordial talks we
used to have. You are passing through trying times. My inces-
sant prayer is that peace may take the place of strife.
SIR SAMUEL HOARE
2 CHESTER PLACE
REGENTS' PARK
N.W.I. LONDON
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
