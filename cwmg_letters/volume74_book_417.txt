417. LETTER TO ADVAITAKUMAR GOSWAMI
August 24, 1941
BHAI ADVAITAKUMAR,
I have your letter. It is difficult to advise you. Not being
acquainted with you, what can I say? Still this much is clear,
that if you are to gain self-confidence you must leave everything
for a time and find some occupation which will enable you to
meet all your expenses. Even the man who shoulders his own
burden and does not worry others does in a way serve the
country.
Blessings from
BAPU
