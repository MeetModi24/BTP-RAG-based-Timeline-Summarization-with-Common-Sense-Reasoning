802. LETTER TO VASANJI HANSJI
October 31, 1945
BHAI VASANJI HANSJI,
I have the hundi for Rs. 4,205 sent by you as well as the list
of donors and the report of the agreement sent by Pranshanker
Joshi. I shall use the money as desired. Convey my thanks to
all the donors.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
VASANJI HANSJI
GANDHI YUVAK BHAJAN MANDALI
67/B STREET, JOHANNESBURG
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
