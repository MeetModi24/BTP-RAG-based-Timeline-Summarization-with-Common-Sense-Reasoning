212. LETTER TO S. G. VAZE
SEVAGRAM,
October 10, 1944
MY DEAR VAZE,
It was good you wrote. Your letter reached me too late
even for a wire. Do please congratulate the Rajasaheb on the
liberal measure he has adopted for the people of his State.
Aundh has no reason to regret the reforms adopted there nor
will Phaltan.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI S. G. VAZE
SERVANTS OF INDIA SOCIETY
POONA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
