82. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
Diwali [October 25, 1927]
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I have received your presents. I started them from the very day
I received them. I have lost one handkerchief out of them. We are a
big crowd here. It is surprising that some of the small things at least
are saved.
I understand about Ceylon : I think your decision is right. You
must understand that the days of sitting around with me are over. It
seems criminal to have even a moment of external peace. Hence I must
learn to find peace in turbulence. And what is true for me will also be
true for my colleagues, is it not so?
Blessings from
BAPU
