201. LETTER TO PUNJABHAI SHAH
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 23, 1926
BHAI PUNJABHAI,
I wrote to Revashankerbhai on receipt of your message. I
enclose with this his reply. When you are strong enough and
can have someone to accompany you, you may go to Bombay
for a day or two. I hope you are all right now.
SHRI PUNJABHAI HIRACHAND SHAH
OPPOSITE VIRCHAND DEEPCHAND LIBRARY
MANEKCHOWK
AHMEDABAD
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12211
