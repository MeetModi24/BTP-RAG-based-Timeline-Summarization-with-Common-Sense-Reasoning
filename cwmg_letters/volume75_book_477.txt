477. LETTER TO SHASHI R. MEHTA
March 9, 1942
CHI. SHASHI 1 ,
I tried but could not reply to your letter immediately. Come
soon after you have passed the examination. Tell Prabhashankar-
bhai that I got his letter. I have asked Maganbhai to write to him
and he does, so that I need do nothing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 1051. Courtesy: Champabehn Mehta
1 Daughter of Ratilal P. Mehta
2 Addressee's maternal grandfather
