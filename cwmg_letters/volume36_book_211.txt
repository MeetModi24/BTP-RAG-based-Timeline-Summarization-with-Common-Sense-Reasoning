211. LETTER TO B. SHIVA RAO
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
April 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. The enclosed is the best I can do for
you. You want an article. You might as well get blood out of
stone as get an article from me.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. B. SHIVA RAO
THE THEOSOPHICAL SOCIETY
ADYAR
MADRAS 3
From a photostat: S.N. 13158
