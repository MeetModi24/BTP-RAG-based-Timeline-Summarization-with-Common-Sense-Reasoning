266. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
July 19, 1941
CHI. AMRIT,
Your brief note.
You have your mouthful. May you have full success.
I
hope your tooth trouble will be completely dealt with. Maha-
dev is preparing to go to Bombay. No more possible today.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4246. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7878
