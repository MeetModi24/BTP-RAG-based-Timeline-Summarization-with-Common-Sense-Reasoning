633. LETTER TO MAHENDRA PRATAP
KAZIRKHIL,
RAMGANJ P. S.,
NOAKHALI DISTT.,
February 25, 1947
DEAR RAJA SAHEB,
I have your wire. You are probably almost right not for the argument
you have used but for deeper reasons. I have advisedly used the adverbs
'probably' and 'almost'.
Yours sincerely,
RAJA MAHENDRA PRATAP
BRINDABAN
