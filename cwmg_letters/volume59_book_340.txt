340. LETTER TO KEDARNATH SAHA
November 9, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I have seen your letter to Sjt. Kumarappa who has not yet been
able to take over charge. If you will send me a sample of your
knives and chappals1, I will endeavour to find a market for them if
they are truly serviceable. Are the chappals made of slaughtered
hide or dead-cattle hide? You may also send me samples of raw
mulberry. I will have experiments made on them, and then it
will be possible either to send someone there as an instructor or
have someone from there for instruction.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI KEDARNATH SAHA
PLEADER
HAZARIBAGH
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
