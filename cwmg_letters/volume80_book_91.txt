91. LETTER TO TARACHAND
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 2, 1945
BHAI TARACHANDJI 3 ,
My three colleagues here strongly object to your estimate of
expenditure. The Navajivan Press is a large establishment. We
can get them to do all the printing work. I think even the
committee of the Hindustani Prachar Sabha will be startled. We
shall place this issue before the committee after I reach Wardha.
It will be good if in the mean time you can send manuscripts of
a few text books. That Dr. Abdul Haq will not be able to come
is also a matter of concern.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. TARACHAND
HINDUSTANI ACADEMY
ALLAHABAD
