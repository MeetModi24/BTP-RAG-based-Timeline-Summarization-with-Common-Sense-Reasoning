48. LETTER TO S. R. SCOTT
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 23, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have hitherto refrained from replying to your letter of the
17th instant in the hope that I would hear from you in reply to
my letter which I sent you to acknowledge yours of the 12th
instant. I thank you for copies of your paper on Gujarati poetry.
I like it very much.
In view of my last letter you will please tell me whether you
want me to publish your letter of the 12th instant. If you do,
it will naturally have a foot-note somewhat after the style of my
last letter to you.
Yours sincerely,
REV. S. R. SCOTT
MISSION PRESS
SURAT
From a photostat: S.N. 14115
