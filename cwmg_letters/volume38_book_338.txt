338. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
Silence Day [December 24, 1928]
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have received your letters. Father is here, his health is fair.
I have not been able to see him so far. I am glad to know that
you are getting well acquainted with Kusum.
You need not keep anything from her. Being firm herself she
can advise you correctly. However much other people may dis-
suade you, always inform me if anyone falls ill.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 3319
