81. LETTER TO M. G. BHANDARI
Urgent Please
September 16,1932
DEAR MAJOR BHANDARI,
The enclosed is a message which I should like very much
now to go if it is at all possible, and there might be no difficulty
or delay seeing that it is addressed to the Government of India.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 3858
