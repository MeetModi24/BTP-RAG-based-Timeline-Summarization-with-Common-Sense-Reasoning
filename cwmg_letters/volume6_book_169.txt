169. LETTER TO T. MORISON
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 13, 1906
DEAR SIR,
Mr. Ally and I, as you know, have come here as a Deputation
from the British Indians of the Transvaal. We shall be obliged if you
will kindly give us an appointment to wait on you in connection with
our mission.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
THEODORE MORISON, ESQ.
C/O THE EAST INDIA ASSOCIATION
3, VICTORIA STREET
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4560
1 At one time Principal of the Mohammedan College at Aligarh; appointed
to the Supreme Legislative Council by Lord Mayo and, towards the end of 1906, to
the India Council by Morley
