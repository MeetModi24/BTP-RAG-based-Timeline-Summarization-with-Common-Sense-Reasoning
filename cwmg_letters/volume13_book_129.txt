129. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
AHMEDABAD,
Kartak Sud 3 [November 10, 1915]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I had forgotten your address.
Your post card indicates that you have not forgotten me. I
have high hopes of you. That they may be fulfilled, may God give
you greater moral strength.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi
