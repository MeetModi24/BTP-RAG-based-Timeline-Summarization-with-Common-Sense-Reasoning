442. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
February 28, 1942
CHI. DEVDAS,
I have your letter. Did I seem to have written to you to
criticize you? I had no such intention. I did try to warn you,
of course. But I entirely agree that you can act only within the
limits of your capacity and according to your lights.
I concede that the existence of your Association has enabled
the newspapers to do something, but that is very little. The
newspapers have no real freedom. But I do admit that whatever
little they have is not to be sacrificed.
You did well in sending a cable to Manilal.
I have not sent
any. I did not intend to send one, either.
Ba is keeping very well these days.
Mahadev is better. He
will of course have to take prolonged rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Keep in touch with Balkrishna
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2149
