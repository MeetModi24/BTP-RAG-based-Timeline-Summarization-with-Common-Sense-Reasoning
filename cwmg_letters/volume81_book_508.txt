508. LETTER TO KANJI J. DESAI
September 28, 1945
BHAI KANJI,
I have your letter. I am doing what I can from here. I
can very well understand your feelings as a father. But God
alone is the true Protector of you, me and Pushpa, so let us take
refuge in Him. There is no doubt that you will be able to find
a suitable husband for your younger daughter.
KANJI JETHABHAI DESAI
OLD HANUMAN LANE, SECOND CROSS LANE
ROOM 4, SECOND FLOOR
RAJDAS CHAWL
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
