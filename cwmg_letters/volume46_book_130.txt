130. LETTER TO LIVINGSTON & DOULL
[As AT] SABARMATI,
May 8, 1931
DEAR SIR,
With reference to your letter of the 3rd March last, I en-
close herewith power of attorney form duly signed by me in favour
of Mr. Doull.
Yours faithfully,
Encl. 1
MESSRS LIVINGSTON & DOULL
SOLICITORS & NOTARIES
