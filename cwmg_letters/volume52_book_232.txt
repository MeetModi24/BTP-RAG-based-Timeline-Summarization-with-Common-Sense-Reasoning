232. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
December 11, 1932
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I got two letters from you. I want an account from you of
the yearly increase in the number of charkhas, the expenditure,
the number of carding bows, the quantity of cotton carded, or
spun or woven, and other activities-an account like the one
maintained in all offices. I was glad to learn that you are keep-
ing good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
