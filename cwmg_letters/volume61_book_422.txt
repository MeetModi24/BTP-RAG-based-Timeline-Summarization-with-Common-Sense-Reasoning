422. LETTER TO PADMA
July 29, 1935
CHI. PADMA,
If I used a coloured slip of paper, it would cost one pice more
and, besides, it won't be hand-made paper. One must not fall ill.
Do you have a Magan spinning-wheel? How much yarn do you
spin everyday? It is really good that Sheela is studying in a
Harijan school. Do all the others spin? Here it has been rain-
ing more than we need.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6152. Also C.W. 3508. Courtesy:
Prabhudas Gandhi
