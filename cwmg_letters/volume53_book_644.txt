644. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
March 5, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
I heard that you have had another attack of asthma. How
is it? You did well in writing to Purushottam not to be in a
hurry to leave Santa Cruz. He had promised to write to me,
but till today I have had no letter from him.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 873. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
