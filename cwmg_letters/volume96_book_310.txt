310. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
SEGAON,
October 11, 1937
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
Today being silence day I use the right hand. The Palestine question
does now engage my attention. It is becoming more and more intricate
for want of a firm declaration such as I have suggested. But that will
never come unless there is an urge from within. We must explore
possibilities when you come. I hope there will be no hitch about your
coming.
Mira's letter will give you titbits.
Love.
UPPER HOUSE
