472. LETTER TO MANIBEHN N. PARIKH
May 1, 1932
CHI. MANI (PARIKH),
I was expecting that you would come this week. When you
come, most probably you will be able to see Mahadev too. I
now get news from Belgaum.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5972. Also C.W. 3289. Courtesy:
Vanamala M. Desai
