543. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
[After August 3, 1940]
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
Please have this immediately investigated and justice done
to the poor Chamars, if the facts are true.
You will write to the correspondent. I have not.
Yours,
BAPU
From the original: Dr. Gopichand Bhargava Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
