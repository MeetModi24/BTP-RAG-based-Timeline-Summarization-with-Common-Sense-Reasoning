314. LETTER TO R. SANKARANARAYANA IYER
[Before December 20, 1932]
I have your letter. I forwarded your telegrams to Calicut.
Beyond that I would do no more. The vote of the avarnas is
wholly unnecessary. The call is not in answer to what the
avarnas want, but it is a call to self-purification, penance, and dis-
charge of an overdue debt and when a man pays his debt, he is
unaffected by the fact whether the creditor demands payment or
not.
The Hindu, 21-12-1932
