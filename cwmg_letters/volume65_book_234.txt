234. LETTER TO M. A. JINNAH
TITHAL,
May 22, 1937
DEAR SHRI JINNAH,
Mr. Kher has given me your message. I wish I could do
something, but I am utterly helpless. My faith in unity is as
bright as ever; only I see no daylight out of the impenetrable
darkness and, in such distress, I cry out to God for light.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
File No. 3001/H/IV-8/38. Police Commissioner's Office, Bombay. Court-
esy: Government of Maharashtra. Also Leaders' Correspondence with Jinnah,
p. 37
