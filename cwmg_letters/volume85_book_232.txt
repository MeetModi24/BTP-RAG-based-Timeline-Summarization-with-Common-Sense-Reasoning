232. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
SEVAGRAM,
August 24, 1946
CHI. KAKA,
I have your letter.
Next week the dictionary must be published. We can
only try, however. Please send me what you can. That will
satisfy me. How can we pass imperfect material in order to
finish the work? After all it is only a beginning. It was not
possible to send for you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati
G.N. 10973
