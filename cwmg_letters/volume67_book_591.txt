591. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
October 5, 1938
CHI. ANAND,
Got your letter only today. I am writing this on a moving
train. Now [I think] there is no need of a preface. And why
so much burden on me? How can I bear it?
I am glad to know that both are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India and
Anand T. Hingorani
