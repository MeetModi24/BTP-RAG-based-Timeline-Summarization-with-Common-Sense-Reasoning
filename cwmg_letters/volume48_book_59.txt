59. LETTER TO H. HARCOURT
88 KNIGHTSBRIDGE,
LONDON, W.,
September 29, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. My time-table is packed; I cannot
therefore send you an early appointment. If you could call at
88 Knightsbridge on 13th October at 9.30, I should be delight-
ed to meet you.
Yours sincerely,
H. HARCOURT, ESQ.
119 GIPSY HILL
UPPER NORWOOD, S.E. 19
From a photostat: S.N. 17824
