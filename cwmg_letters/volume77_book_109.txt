109. LETTER TO VIJAYA M. PANCHOLI
JUHU,
May 20, 1944
CHI. VIJAYA,
From today I am exercising the liberty of writing a few
letters. I am in Juhu till the 29th at any rate. Then, maybe
three weeks, in Poona. The silence will end on the 29th. But
do come over here. Perhaps it may be difficult to accommodate
you here. I am staying in a hut. You may, therefore, stay some-
where else. I am helpless. In Sevagram all of you could have
been accommodated. I am glad that Nanabhai has been re-
leased. About me it is as you say.
Blessings to all of you from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 7148. Also C.W. 4640. Courtesy :
Vijaya M. Pancholi
