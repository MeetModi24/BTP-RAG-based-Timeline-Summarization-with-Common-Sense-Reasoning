597. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1940
CHI. LILI,
L.'s health is fine. You will get the pen along with this.
Blessings from
BAPU
LILAVATI UDESHI
KANJI KHETSI CHHATRALAYA
65 MINT ROAD
BOMBAY
