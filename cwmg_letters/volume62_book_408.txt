408. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
SEGAON,
April 30, 1936
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
I got your letter. You have done well in giving me an
account. What a good thing it would be if by this time we
had cultivated the strength to raise contributions!
I expect the Bharuch affair has been settled.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4035
