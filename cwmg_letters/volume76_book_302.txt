302. LETTER TO H.E.B. CATLEY
SEVAGRAM,
June 29, 1942
DEAR MR. CATLEY,
I wish I could send you an encouraging reply to your effort.
Time alone will show which way the right lay.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. E. B. CATLEY ESQ.
EDITOR, "PIONEER"
LUCKNOW
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
