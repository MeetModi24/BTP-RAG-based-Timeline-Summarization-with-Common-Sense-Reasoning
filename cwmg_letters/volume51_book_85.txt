85. LETTER TO NARGIS CAPTAIN
September 16, 1932
No cause for sorrow; every cause for rejoicing that a member
of the family has been given by God the opportunity of offering
the supreme sacrifice for the purest cause that could be thought
of. There should be no imitation of the fast even partially. For
you all it is a time for greater devotion to work and self-puri-
fication. You are going to brace up everyone who is at all
disposed to give way to grief.
Love to you all including the caged birds.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, pp. 27-
