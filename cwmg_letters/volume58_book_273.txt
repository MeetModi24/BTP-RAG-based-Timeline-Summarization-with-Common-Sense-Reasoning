273. LETTER TO SARITA
July 26, 1934
CHI. SARITA,
I have your letter. I was waiting for it. It is not that you
have not seen happiness as well as misery. Mix well with the
sisters there. I have no other place in Bombay. My relations
with that family are very old.
Isn't Nirmala's place a home?
It is always crammed. But whether it is crammed or not there is
room in that family for everyone. Still if you can manage to stay
there, do so. I have written to both the sisters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: G. N. 11529
