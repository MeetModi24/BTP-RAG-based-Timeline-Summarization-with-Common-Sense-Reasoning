449. LETTER TO MADALASA
NEW DELHI,
September 22, 1946
CHI. MADU,
I have your letter. I liked this one. If it is true that so
long you have only been receiving, then you have to pay twice
the amount in debt. You should therefore go on paying it
and be happy. Will you be able to come to Wardha about
the time that I arrive there?
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchaven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, pp. 329-30
