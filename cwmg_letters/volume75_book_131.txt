131. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
November 9, 1941
CHI. MAGAN,
I got your letter. Champa now writes and says that now she
will come only after Nirmala's wedding.
I advise you to go to
Ahmedabad and fetch him. If you wish, all of you may go and
live in the Red Bungalow or bring him here. I see no other way.
You must have got my letter addressed to you at Mahabalesh-
var. In it I have replied to your question regarding yourself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1022. Courtesy: Manjulabehn
M. Mehta
