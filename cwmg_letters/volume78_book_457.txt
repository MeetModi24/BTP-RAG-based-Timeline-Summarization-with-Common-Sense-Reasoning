457. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
November 22, 1944
CHI. MAGAN,
I had your letter. I have written to Champa's adviser. I
will forward the reply to you. I am not in a condition just now
to take up a long inquiry. I am buried under public work.
Hope you are all happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MAGANLAL PRANJIVAN MEHTA, BARRISTER
82 GHODBUNDAR ROAD
ANDHERI, B.B. & C.I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 1031. Courtesy: Manjula M. Mehta
