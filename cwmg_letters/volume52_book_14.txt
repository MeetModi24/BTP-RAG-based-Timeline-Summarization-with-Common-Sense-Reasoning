14. LETTER TO DR. RAGHUVIR SINGH AGRAWAL
November 17, 1932
BHAI RAGHUVIRJI,
I have your letter and the book . I have no time at present
being totally immersed in the service of Harijans. But I shall try
to follow the treatment you prescribe. Moreover, all three of us
here are condemned to spectacles. It is hoped that we all will
