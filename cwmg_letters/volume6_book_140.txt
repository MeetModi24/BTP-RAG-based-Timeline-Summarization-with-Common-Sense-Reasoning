140. LETTER TO SIR CHARLES DILKE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 9, 1906
SIR,
We beg to thank you for your presence at the deputation to Lord
Elgin, in spite of the inclemency of the weather, and for the great support
you have given to our cause by your presence. We venture to hope
that you will be good enough to continue to take an active interest in
the matter until a full measure of justice has been secured for the British
Indians in the Transvaal.
We beg to remain,
Your humble servants,
[M. K. GANDHI
H. O. ALLY]
SIR CHARLES DILKE
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4519
