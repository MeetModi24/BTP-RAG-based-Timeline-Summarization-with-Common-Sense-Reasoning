615. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 2, 1926
DEAR MR. JAYAKAR,
I have your letter. I am disinclined even to write as much
as I have about the Pact . You won't find me writing or doing
anything further. I have no desire whatsoever to intervene
except to bring about peace. The whole of this quarrel is so painful
to me.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 11317
