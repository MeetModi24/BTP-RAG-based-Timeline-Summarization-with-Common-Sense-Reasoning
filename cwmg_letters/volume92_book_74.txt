74. LETTER TO AKBAR HYDARI
SABARMATI,
August 2, 1929
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your prompt reply. Evidently I confounded
customs with octroi duty but I did mean the latter. All over
India khadi is being exempted from octroi duty, for it means
tax on the labour of the poorest and the most deserving. I wish
you could exert yourself to have this tax removed. The best way
to promote this village industry is to have a department as
Mysore has for the supply of cheap and efficient wheels with
accessories. Under capable management there is ample scope for
improvement.
Yours,
A. HYDARI
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S.N. 32577/86
