438. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
BORSAD,
May 8, 1931
CHI. MRIDULA,
I do hope that you will find time to go to Simla for a few
days. Both Papa and Mummy were of course anxious and
hoping that you would go and be with them at least for a
week.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C. W. 11180. Courtesy:
