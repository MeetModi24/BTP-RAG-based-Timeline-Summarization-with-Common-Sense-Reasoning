654. LETTER TO HENDERSON
BHANGI COLONY,
NEW DELHI,
April 10, 1947
DEAR DR. HENDERSON,
I am glad to have your letter and to learn that we have corresponded
with each other before.
I am, I confess, surprised at your question. He who believes in
a living God never feels the slightest doubt about his mission, especially
in a country which has regained its lost independence.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
20 A GOLBURN ST.
SYDNEY
AUSTRALIA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
