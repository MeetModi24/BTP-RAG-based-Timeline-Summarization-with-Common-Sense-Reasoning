67. LETTER TO MOTIBEHN MATHURADAS
YERAVDA MANDIR,
January 3, 1931
CHI. MOTI,
If you wish to continue to be Moti of Vijayanagar and not
Motibehn in Vijayanagar, you must learn to make your hand-
writing as beautiful as pearls. The handwriting in your letter was
quite good, but you should improve it a little every day. It is one
of the virtues of a person devoted to dharma that he or she is
exact in every matter, small or big. Will no one in Cochin agree
to keep Shantu exclusively on fruit? Write to them and see.
One should not be lazy in such matters. If pus continues to issue
from a child's gums, we ought not to remain indifferent. If we
do, the child will not live long. It is easy to cure such diseases
in childhood. The alternative is for you to keep Shantu with
you and bring him up yourself. I am glad that you have taken up
the task of keeping children clean.
Blessings from
BAPU
