147. LETTER TO ABDUL GHAFFAR KHAN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 13, 1941
DEAR KHAN SAHEB,
In accordance with your letter I am sending you Girdharilal
Puri and Krishnadas Gandhi. They will be able to help in or-
ganizing spinning, etc., and discussing plans for the Ashram. I do not
anticipate their stay there beyond seven days. But if you want them
longer Puri can give more time. Krishnadas has very great respon-
sibility here.
As to Alighul Khan I am quite clear that he should resign.
He cannot help the war effort.
Love to all.
BAPU
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
