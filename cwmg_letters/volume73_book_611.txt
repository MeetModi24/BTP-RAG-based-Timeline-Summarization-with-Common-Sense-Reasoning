611. LETTER TO PRITHVI SINGH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 8, 1941
BHAI PRITHVI SINGH,
I am happy that you write to me regularly. You are working
so hard that I am sure your efforts will bear fruit.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5646. Also C.W. 2957. Courtesy:
Prithvi Singh
