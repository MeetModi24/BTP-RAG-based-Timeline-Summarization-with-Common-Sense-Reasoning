439. LETTER TO RUKMINIDEVI BAZAJ
August 24, 1932
CHI. RUKMINI,
I got your letter, as also Radha's. Try and get back the weight
which you have lost. It was good that you went to see Devdas.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. BENARSIDAS
K 23/96 PANCHGANGA
BENARES CITY
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9140
