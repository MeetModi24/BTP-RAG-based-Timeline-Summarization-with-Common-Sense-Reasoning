339. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
October 24, 1939
DAUGHTER,
What am I to do? I did my best. Certainly Ba is ex-
tremely displeased with you. I don't know about others. Your
duty is to leave this house. You may return when the cloud
blows over. I had no inkling of Ba's displeasure. I was sur-
prised when I noticed it today. How can I send for Hanif here
under these circumstances?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 432
