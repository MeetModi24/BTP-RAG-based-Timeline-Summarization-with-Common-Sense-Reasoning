455. LETTER TO RAOJIBHAI M. PATEL
April 26, 1932
CHI. RAOJIBHAI,
I got all your letters, and I have also replied to them. I
am very glad that you go and see Abbas Saheb. I would be
happy if you come and see me here. But it would be more
honourable for us if you do not come. When we are trusted, we
should interpret the conditions on our side to our disadvantage.
This is why I do not ask you to come.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8997
