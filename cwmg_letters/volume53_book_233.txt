233. LETTER TO N. H. PURANDARE
January 28, 1933
MY DEAR PURANDARE,
I had written to you before receiving your postcard of the
25th. I have nothing more to add to what I have said. If you
are prepared to take over the book, I shall gladly go through the
estimates you have received and you will pay no more than the
market rate.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20134
