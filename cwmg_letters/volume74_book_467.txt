467. LETTER TO POKHRAJ
September 2, 1941
BHAI POKHRAJJI,
I have read the letter Dr. Hasan sent you. In my view there
is now no reason why the document should not be got from the
Commissioner. If the Council's permission is considered necessary
it should be obtained.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers.
Courtesy: Pyarelal
