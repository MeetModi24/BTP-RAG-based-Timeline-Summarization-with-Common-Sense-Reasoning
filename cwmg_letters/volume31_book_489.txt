489. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Saturday, Bhadra Krishna 4 [September 25, 1926]
BHAI RAMESHWARJI,
I have your letter. I shall select the books. It is only
through patience and contentment that man can be cured of
his restlessness.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 186
