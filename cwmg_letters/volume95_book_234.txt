234. LETTER TO T. S. AVINASILINGAM CHETTIAR
BIRLA HOUSE,
NEW DELHI,
December 13,1947
MY DEAR AVINASILINGAM,
Please read the enclosed and tell me whether what is attrib-
uted to you is true. I do not feel like publicly dealing with it unless I
know the exact fact.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI T.S. AVINASILINGAM CHETTIAR
MINISTER FOR EDUCATION
From the original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
