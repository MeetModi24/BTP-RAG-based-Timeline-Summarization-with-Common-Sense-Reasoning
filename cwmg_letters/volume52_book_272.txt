272. LETTER TO HOME SECRETARY, GOVERNMENT
OF BOMBAY
December 16, 1932
THE SECRETARY TO THE GOVERNMENT OF BOMBAY
HOME DEPARTMENT
DEAR SIR,
With reference to my letter dated the 29th ultimo regarding
permission to see Sheth Jamnalal Bajaj from time to time, both in
respect of his health and untouchability, I shall be grateful for
a reply.
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40)(2), Pt. I, p. 417
