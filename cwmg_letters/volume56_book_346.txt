346. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
DELHI,
December 11, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
I got your letter. It is good that you are under the special
care of Ramabehn. Obey her. I have advised Dwarakanath
that for some time you should be kept on milk, fruit and vege-
tables. That will improve your blood.
You also require steam-
baths and hip-baths. The eyes should be sprinkled with warm
water four or five times daily. The water must be clean.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9639. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
