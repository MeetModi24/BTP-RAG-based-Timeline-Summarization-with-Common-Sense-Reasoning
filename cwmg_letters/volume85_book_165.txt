165. LETTER TO DR. S. R. U. SAVUR
SEVAGRAM,
August 13, 1946
BHAI DR. SAVUR,
I got your letter. I am sorry I cannot accept the use of
the Roman script for learning or teaching Hindustani. I have
explained the reasons in Harijan. If we have occasion to meet
I shall be able to explain to you how the Roman script would
be disastrous for us.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. S. R. U. SAVUR
DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC INSTRUCTION
MADRAS
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
