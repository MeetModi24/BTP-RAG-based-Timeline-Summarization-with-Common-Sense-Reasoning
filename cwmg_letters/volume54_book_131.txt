131. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
March 18, 1933
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I hope you are
strong and well. I appreciate all you say about the organization
of weaving in Hissar and I know that you will do the best that is
possible.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 20610
