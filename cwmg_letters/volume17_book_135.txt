135. LETTER TO LAZARUS GABRIEL
[SINHGADH,]
April 30, 1920
I have given my two sons to South Africa and they can stay
there as long as they choose. More it is not in my power to give.
Every available man is wanted here and so every available pie.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
