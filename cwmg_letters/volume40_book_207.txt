207. LETTER TO RADHABEHN GANDHI
April 10, 1929
CHI. RADHIKA,
I have been worrying about your health. You should observe
how your body works and put it right. In no case should you
work beyond your strength. I hope your mind is at peace.
Does Keshu write to you regularly? He is known to be rather
lazy in writing letters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 8675. Courtesy: Radhabehn Choudhari
