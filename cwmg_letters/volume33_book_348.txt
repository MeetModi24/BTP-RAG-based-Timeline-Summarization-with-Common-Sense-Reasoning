348. LETTER TO MULCHAND AGRAWAL
NANDI DURG,
Vaisakha Krishna 2 [May 18, 1927]
BHAI MOOLCHANDJI,
I have your letter. You have started khadi work, for which
I thank you. Nowadays owing to my illness, Jamnalalji is handl-
ing the work of the Charkha Sangh. I shall pass your letter to
him. He is due here in a day or two.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 761
