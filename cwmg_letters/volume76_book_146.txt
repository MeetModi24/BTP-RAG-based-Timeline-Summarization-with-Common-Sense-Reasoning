146. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
May 18, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
It is the Harijan day today. Where is the time for writ-
ing letters? It is warm no doubt but I do not feel it much.
Everyone is well. Mathew is ill. I brought along Jaisukhlal's
little daughter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 4264. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7806
