423. LETTER TO P. KODANDA RAO
ON THE TRAIN,
November [18]3, 1939
MY DEAR KODANDA RAO,
Your argument is quite good. But tell me who is today the
League of Nations ? The situation is baffling but deeply interest-
ing.
I am hoping that our country will play an important part
in this moving drama.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: G.N. 6283
