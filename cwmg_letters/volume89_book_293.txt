293. LETTER TO AVANTIKABAI GOKHALE
October 4, 1947
CHI. AVANTIKABEHN,
How is it that you have cancer? I learnt about it only
from your letter. Is there any hope of its being cured? Please
write to me in detail.
Why do you remember the dhotis even now? You have ob-
served the rule long enough. Overcome that attachment now.
I hope Baban Gokhale is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Adarsh Hind Mahila, p. 1
