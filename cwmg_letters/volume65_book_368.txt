368. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
June 21, 1937
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
The blanket that I have sent you is a hand-woven one
from Scotland. It is said that even the wool is hand-spun. The
beauty of it lies in the composition of different colours. The pur-
pose in sending it to you is that you may try and make use of
the design in Kashmir or some other place. Otherwise preserve it
as a sample. I had forgotten to write about this. I am glad
you reminded me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10832. Courtesy: Purushottam
K. Jerajani
