537. LETTER TO E. W. ARYANAYAKUM
SEVAGRAM,
March 27, 1945
CHI. ARYANAYAKUM,
This letter is for both of you. Dev's studies will be com-
pleted in April. He asks me, since I shall be away then, what
he is to do. If you let me have your opinion, I can give him
definite advice.
When is the meeting of the representatives of all the institu-
tions? There is going to be one I suppose.
I see from today's telegram that if you do not go to Bombay
the quorum will not be complete. Even so if you have work
here, I shall not insist that you should leave it and go.
Blessings from
BAPU
TALIMI SANGH
SEVAGRAM
