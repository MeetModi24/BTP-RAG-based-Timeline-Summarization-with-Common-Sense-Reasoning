32. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
December 23, 1934
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
You must have seen the mill-owners' resolutions. See that
they don't quarrel among themselves. If any of them listen to
you, advise them. I have written to Kasturbhai and Chaman-
bhai.
I do hope that wherever you go you will tell the people
about the Village Industries Association. It has great potentia-
lities.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro‒2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 150
