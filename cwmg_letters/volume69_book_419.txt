419. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
ON THE TRAIN,
July 6, 1939
DEAR DAUGHTER,
You have only to place the spinning work at Ahmedabad
on a sound footing. Do whatever Lakshmidasbhai suggests in
this connection. Do not spend more than Rs. 2,000 this time.
Help Mridulabehn in every possible way. Do not bribe anyone
into doing work. You have to do the work while preserving your
health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 422
