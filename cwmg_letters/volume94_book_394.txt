394. LETTER TO A. R. TIJARAYE
SEVAGRAM,
July 7, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Your letter of 22nd inst. to Gandhiji.
He notes what you say with regard to the Forward Bloc political prisoners in
C. P. The question of the release of all the political prisoners is fully in his mind
and he is tackling it in the way he knows.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI A. R. TIJARAYE
TILAK VIDYALAYA
VILLAGE UPLIFT CENTRE
KOLAPARDI, POST MADAMENDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
