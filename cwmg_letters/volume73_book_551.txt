551. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SEVAGRAM,
March 24, 1941
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have read the set of rules and regulations prepared by Bhai
Kundar. It is good. If there is any need for making certain
changes, please make them. Otherwise get copies made on the
office cyclostyle. We can always make some changes later.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4380
