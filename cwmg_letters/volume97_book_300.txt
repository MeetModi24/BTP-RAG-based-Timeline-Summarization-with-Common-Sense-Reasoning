300. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 14, 1938
CHI. RAMAD,
Today I just feel like addressing you as I did when you were a little

child . . .1 is still not ready with me. You are welcome to have returned 2 . I will
leave from here for Delhi on the 19th. I shall be there for at least eight days.
You may therefore go to Delhi directly from there or accompany me from here.
I wish you to come here so that we may go to Delhi together. A conch-shell or
a flute instead of [a clock] will do for Kanam for his [bag]. You will then be
safe. Bring from there a small conch-shell or flute. Let him blow the conch-
shell or play on the flute. Bhansalibhai teaches him with all love. More when
we meet. I am quite well. I keep unbroken silence these days.
Blessings from
BAPU
