32. LETTER TO B. L. RALLIA RAM
October 16, 1941
DEAR R.,
Many thanks for your letter. You and your friends will be
welcome on 8th November at 4 p.m.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
B. L. RALLIA RAM
GENERAL SECY.
A. I. COUNCIL OF INDIAN CHRISTIANS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
