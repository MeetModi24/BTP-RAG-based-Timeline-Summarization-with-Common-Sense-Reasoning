32. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
August 15, 1944
MY DEAR VENKATAPPAYYA,
I have your two letters and your three notes. You are truly
amazing. I suppose you are much older than I. But your energy
never wanes. May it be always thus. How about Savitri?
With love,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 3226
