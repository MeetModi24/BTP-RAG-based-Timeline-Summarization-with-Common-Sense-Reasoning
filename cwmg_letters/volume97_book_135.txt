135. LETTER TO PRABHUDAS GANDHI
ASHRAM,
November 26, 1929
CHI. PRABHUDAS,
Advise Chhotelal after reading the enclosed letter. Write to me. I have
written to him that if it was done in the past and if Hari Vallabhji advises
that a holiday be declared, he should do so.
Where should I write if I want to write to Bhagirathji at his residence?
I should get letters from you regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 32971
