404. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
ON THE TRAIN,
July 6, 1939
CHI. LILA,
What weakness you are displaying! It is now that
you are being tested in your life. Matriculation is only an
excuse God has provided. You have been sent to Vanita
Vishram on that pretext. If you fail there, your ashram life
will have gone in vain and my hopes will have been destroyed.
Why should you be defeated by minor inconveniences? Even
if you help the lady who is looking after things there, you will
be doing admirable work and satisfying your needs also in the
right way. But you must have that much love within you; you
must have patience and courage. Bear it in mind that your
[matriculation] is in Vanita Vishram, not in New Era
Blessings from
BAPU
