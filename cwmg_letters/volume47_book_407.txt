407. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
AHMEDABAD,
August 22, 1931
CHI. JANAKIBEHN,
Is it out of pity for me that you have not written to me
for some time and have not permitted Madu and Om to write
either? I don't want pity; I want a letter. Has your health
improved? What do you eat?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2893
