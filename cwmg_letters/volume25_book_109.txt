109. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
Bhadarva Vad 3 [September 16, 1924]
BHAISHRI VALLABHBHAI,
My decision will be known to you even before this reaches
you. You are a lion, and will not therefore be perturbed. Please
go on doing with greater energy all the work we have resolved to
do. Let not anyone be nervous. I propose to complete my fast
in this very place. I am afraid Manibehn will be very much
upset. Please reassure her. I am not writing a separate letter
to her.
BAPU
BHAISHRI VALLABHBHAI PATEL
BARRISTER
AHMEDABAD
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro 2—Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 10
