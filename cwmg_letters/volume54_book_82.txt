82. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
March 12, 1933
I wrote one letter this morning and posted it. It must be
on the way. I need not, therefore, write more in this. I hope you
have been to see Ramabehn.
Devdas will have arrived there.
BAPU
In all, there are 16 letters, stitched together.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./I. Also C.W. 8332. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
