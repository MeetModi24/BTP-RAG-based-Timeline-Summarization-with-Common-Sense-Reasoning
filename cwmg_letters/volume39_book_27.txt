27. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
SHIKARPUR,
February 8, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I got your letter. I understand the matter about Shivabhai.
I am dictating this letter while eating. It is time for a meeting.
I had certainly intended to return there on the 16th but this
time everything goes wrong. There is an urgent message from
Motilalji and so I shall have to go to Delhi. I must reach there
on the 17th. The 18th is Monday. I shall, therefore, return
there earliest on the 19th. I did not go to Delhi for the sake of
Rasik. I shall be going now for the Working Committee, and
shall see Rasik if he is alive then. There is so much disturbance
during the journey that I can barely manage to finish the work
for Young India, and that too after quarrelling with people. I
have to disappoint many. You are one of them, for it was my
intention to write to you every day but cannot manage to do that.
