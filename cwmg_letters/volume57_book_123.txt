123. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
February 5, 1934
CHI. MARY,
I hope you got my letter at your Betul address.
The Hyderabad visit has been put off by nearly 20 days.
I do not go there before 10th I think. Even that is not quite
certain. And then I am not supposed to stay there for more
than 4 hours.
We are all doing well. No time to say more.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6019. Also C.W. 3348. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
