449. LETTER TO K. M. MUNSHI
June 7, 1945
BHAI MUNSHI,
I got your letter. You are suffering from fever and tonsillitis.
Do you think you will get well there? In your condition I would
advise Bombay. You will not get elsewhere the help you can in
Bombay, and in June the climate is not bad. However, treat this
as no more than advice.
I have noted what you say regarding Kasturba Memorial
Office. It was Bapa's suggestion. It is a good one.
What shall one say about the climate in England!
Blessings to you all from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am hoping to reach Sevagram in the beginning of July.
From a copy of the Gujarati : C.W. 7687. Courtesy: K. M. Munshi
