79. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 15, 1940
CHI. DEVDAS,
I have your letter. What you write is correct. Our people
are easily influenced. I got your telephonic message. I do not
intend to write anything immediately. I will write when the
time comes. We had heard the news about Ramu. I did not
feel unhappy but Ba did that you had not informed us. Ba still
has such attachments though they have weakened considerably.
Her forbearance, equimindedness, generosity, fortitude and firm-
ness astonish me. Her health is good and she remains cheerful.
I see no need for anyone to go to America. Only what we
can do here will have some effect. Nevertheless if an occasion
arises, we shall see.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2123
