19. LETTER TO ABDUL HUQ
July 20, 1945
BHAI SAHEB,
Zohra has come to me about Shaukatullah Ansari's house
which is in your possession and which you refuse to give up. I
should like to hear what you have to say in the matter. You
were a friend of the late Dr. Ansari . How can there be any
litigation with you? I shall hope to hear from you.
Yours,
M. K.
DR. ABDUL HUQ
DELHI
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
