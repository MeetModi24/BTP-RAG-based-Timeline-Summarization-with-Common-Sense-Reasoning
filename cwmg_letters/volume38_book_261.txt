261. LETTER TO REVA DATTA
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, WARDHA,
December 13, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your long letter containing interesting infor-
mation.
It was a severe disappointment to me that I could not join
the Convention at Mysore, where I expected to see many friends.
But I could not make time for it.
It is just likely that I should be in Calcutta on the 2nd
January and I should be delighted to meet you in Calcutta. My
own dates after then would be uncertain, though according to
present arrangements, I should expect to be the whole of
January at Sabarmati.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. R. DATTA
C/O
MRS. HENSMAN
LOCOCK'S GARDENS, MADRAS
From a photostat: S.N. 13793
