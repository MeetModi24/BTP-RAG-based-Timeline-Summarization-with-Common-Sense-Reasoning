72. LETTER TO E. L. L. HAMMOND
MOTIHARI,
January 14, 1918
TO
E. L. L. HAMMOND, ESQ.
SECRETARY
PROVINCIAL RECRUITING BOARD
BIHAR AND ORISSA
DEAR MR. HAMMOND,
You will forgive me for not replying earlier to your letter of
blank date in December. The fact is that I have been travelling
out of Champaran. I returned only on the 12th instant. My
difficulty just now is that whilst the agrarian position remains
uncertain, I would make no headway. The Agrarian Bill is now
before the Council. My way will be clearer after it is passed. I shall
then try to follow out your suggestion and see what can be done.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
