268. LETTER TO T. J. BENNETT
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 28, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I hope you have received the invitation card which the Delegates
have ventured to send to you for the breakfast tomorrow morning at
10.30 at the Hotel Cecil. I venture to trust that you will be good
enough to honour the Delegates by your presence.
I am,
Yours truly,
T. J. BENNETT, ESQ.
HARWARTON HOUSE
SPELDHURST
TUNBRIDGE WELLS
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4655
1 A similar letter was sent to J. M. Robertson, M.P.
