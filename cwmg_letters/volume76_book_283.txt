283. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 22, 1942
CHI. NARANDAS,
I hope what I have written in Harijanbandhu is all right.
Abha must now be a familiar figure there. I hope all of you like
her. Is it necessary to give some more help to Gokibehn Send
arrears of the money that goes to Pyarelal's home from the day
it became due to the present day, to Mohanlal Nayyar, Arya
Samaj Mandir Building, Hanuman Road, New Delhi.
Here the monsoon has started very well indeed. Kanaiyo
is fine. Kusum must be well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8605. Cour-
tesy: Narandas Gandhi
