68. LETTER TO DR. HIRALAL SHARMA
June 18, 1932
DEAR FRIEND,
Shrimati Behn Amtussalaam sent me some time ago your
pamphlets and the two volumes of your book, Light and Colour in
the Medical World. I am grateful for your having treated Amtul-
behn with extreme care and attention which she tells me you gave
her. As for the literature on Light and Colour I can express no
opinion, as I have no experience, worth the name, of this treatment.
As I have not much faith in drugs and as I believe in the great
healing power of the sun, naturally I would love to find that the
methods you are advocating are sound and can bear investigation.
If under the restrictions in which I am living I can make any
experiments, I shall do so.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Chhayamen Mere Jivanke Solah Varsh, p. 8
