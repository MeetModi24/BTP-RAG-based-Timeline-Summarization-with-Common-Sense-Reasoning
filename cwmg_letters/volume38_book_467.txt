467. LETTER TO K. S. KARANTH
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 24, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. The first case is not a question of shuddhi
but of reclamation and, if the so-called converts have no sense
of Christianity in them, they should be treated as Hindus.
As to your second question: If the girls look grown up, are
intelligent enough to understand what they are doing and they
cannot be kept long enough without danger of their being given
to a life of shame, they should be married.
About the child widow: If a husband can be found who
would marry her but who would postpone co-habitation till the
girl reaches the proper age, I should see no harm.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. S. KARANTH
VASANTA OFFICE, MANGALORE
From a photostat: S.N. 14896
