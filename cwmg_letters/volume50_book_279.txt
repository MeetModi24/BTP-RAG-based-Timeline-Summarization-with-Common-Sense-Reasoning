279. LETTER TO TARAMATI M. TRIKUMJI
July 26, 1932
MY DEAR TARAMATI,
I have received a letter from Mathuradas from Belgaum in
which he writes that, unless he sends for you, you should not go
to Belgaum. Mathuradas's health is good till now. I have received
the book on astronomy. Hope you and Dilip are well. There is
no letter from you now.
Yours,
BAPU
SMT. TARAMATI MATHURADAS
21 MINT ROAD, FORT
BOMBAY
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. 11, p. 447
