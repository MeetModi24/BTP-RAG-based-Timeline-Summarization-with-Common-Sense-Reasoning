164. LETTER TO V. VENKATASUBBAIAH
February 4, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
I got your letter only today. Please send me your detailed
budget and if it commends itself to me, I shall submit it to the Trust.
As to the scheme for an institute, will naturopaths agree? If you
are young enough I would like you to come here by appointment
and treat patients according to naturopathy. Naturopathy here
works but I am sorry to confess that only allopathy flourishes.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI V. VENKATASUBBAIAH, M.L.A.
KASTURIDEVINAGAR
NELLORE (ANDHRA)
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
