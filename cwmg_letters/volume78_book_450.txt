450. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
November 21, 1944
CHI. AMRIT,
Your letter came in today.
You have to submit to the relief in instalments for the sake
of your brothers. Amid conflict of duties, the choice is often a
very delicate affair. In every case you will be cheerful and keep
your body in a fit condition. The climate there must be most
bracing. I hope you had a good time with Kamalnayan .
I am well. Sushila has gone to Bombay to see Mathuradas.
She is due any day now.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4149. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7784
