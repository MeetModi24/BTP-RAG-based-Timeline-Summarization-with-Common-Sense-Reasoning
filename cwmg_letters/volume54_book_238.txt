238. LETTER TO LAKSHMI JERAJANI
March 27, 1933
CHI. LAKSHMI1,
At last I had a letter from you. I did write to you. I hope
you keep good health. What is your daily programme of work
now?
Blessings from
BAPU
[Ps.]
Can I take it that Uncle has completely recovered now?
CHI. LAKSHMI JERAJANI
KHADI BHANDAR
KALBADEVI 2
BOMBAY
From Gujarati: C.W. 2814. Courtesy: Purushottam D. Saraiya, Bombay
