467. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
February 24, 1930
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
I have your letter. I had not read the speech. I hardly
get time to read.
You will see the forthcoming issue. It will contain much.
The substance has already appeared in Gujarati Navajivan. Perhaps
we shall get a few moments to discuss things more fully when we
494
meet on 1st March. My letter to the Viceroy will also clarify
the issue.
I am glad there is nothing serious with Kamala. But why
should she not now go to a hospital and have the necessary treat-
ment?
Yours,
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru papers, 1930. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
