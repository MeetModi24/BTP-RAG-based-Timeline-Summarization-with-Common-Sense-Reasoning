394. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 23, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Why need we reply to the Sentinel's canard2? What a canard!
I feel that falsehoods which no one is likely to believe should
be ignored. If you feel otherwise, however, we may reply.
I will not, I cannot, send the report about the tourists
with Prabhu Dayal. It will require time. I will, therefore, get
it typed here.
Pyarelal passed the night fairly well.
Please send the ice.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Chimanlal is being discharged today. So if you are coming
by car, bring him with you, or if anybody is coming from the
bungalow he may come with him. In the last resort, he may come
by tonga. If a tonga suits you better, you may hire one. But
in any case the carriage will have to be arranged by you.
Telegram for Rajkumari:
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11629
