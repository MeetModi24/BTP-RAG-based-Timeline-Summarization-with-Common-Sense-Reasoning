776. LETTER TO GIRIRAJKISHORE
October 28, 1945
CHI. GIRIRAJ,
I have your letter. Both the sisters were happy . Your work
is progressing well. Take care of your health. I have received
part of the dictionary. I just glanced at it for two minutes. I
hope to go through it a little more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 8775
