469. LETTER TO DR. B. S. MOONJE
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 13, 1931
DEAR DR. MOONJE,
I thank you for your letter of 6th June. Even if I succeed
in going to London, at present I have no intention of taking ex-
perts with me. The scope of my work will be so limited as not
to require much expert advice.
Yours sincerely,
DR. B. S. MOONJE
FIRGROVE
SIMLA
From a photostat: S.N. 17278
