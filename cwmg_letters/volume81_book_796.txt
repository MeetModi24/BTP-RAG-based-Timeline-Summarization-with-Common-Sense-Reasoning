796. LETTER TO DR. KRISHNABAI NIMBKAR
October 30, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. Now that the members of the Working
Committee are released write to them in detail. Meet me, if
I am here, when you reach Poona.
DR. KRISHNABAI NIMBKAR
192 POONAMALLE HIGH ROAD
P. O. VEPERY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
