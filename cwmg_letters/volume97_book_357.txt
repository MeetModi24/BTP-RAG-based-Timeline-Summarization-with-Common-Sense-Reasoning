357. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
Thursday, 10 p.m.
CHI. NARANDAS,
Now what? However, since you have the firmness to keep your pledge, let
all be arrested.
You must have got the bag of letters sent yesterday. About the other
things, in other letters.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33254
