59. LETTER TO J. J. DOKE
[VOLKSRUST,]
Wednesday [October 14, 1908]
DEAR MR. DOKE,
I am writing this from the Court House. I had hoped to be able
to send you something before I was fixed up. But I have been too
busy otherwise. I thank you very much for your good wishes. My sole
trust is in God. I am therefore quite cheerful.
I am,
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original in Gandhiji's hand: G. N. 4092
Courtesy: C. M. Doke
