379. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 25, 1937
CHI. MIRA,
I am glad you are better. Here is Dr. Dharmavir's letter.
If he thinks that sleeping in the open there is injurious, you
should not insist on sleeping outside. It is well to do as he says.
The liver, the spleen and the glands should be dealt with and set
right. What is your food now? Do you get good fruit and vege-
tables? Have you got rid of the cold?
Ba returns tomorrow. We are a growing family.
Govind has gone to Kakasaheb. He wanted some such
thing.
This is being written just before the morning prayer.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6377. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9853
