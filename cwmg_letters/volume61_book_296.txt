296. LETTER TO VANAMALA N. PARIKH
June 26, 1935
CHI. VANAMALA,
Now that you have recovered your health, try to preserve
it. If you are sure that the giving up of salt and pulses has
definitely benefited you, keep up the practice. You may gratify
your palate as well as you can with the things that help you to
maintain good health.
I do not understand what you will gain from a hand-written
magazine. What is the value of my blessings, then? All the same
it may be a source of innocent pleasure and, therefore, if you are
keen on getting my blessings you have them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5784. Also C.W. 3007. Courtesy:
