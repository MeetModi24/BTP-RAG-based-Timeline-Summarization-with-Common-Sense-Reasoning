180. LETTER TO PRABHUDAYAL VIDYARTHI
TITHAL, BULSAR,
May 17, 1937
CHI. PRABHUDAYAL,
Yes, if you cannot give full time to paper-making because of
the heat, then give five hours. Your health should not suffer on
any account.
Blessings from
BAPU
