228. LETTER TO MOHANLAL K. MEHTA
KULAPAHAR,
November 21, 1929
BHAISHRI MOHANLAL,
I have your letter. A vow once taken ought not to be given
up even though not merely three but three thousand friends urge
you to do so and hold out threats against you if you do not
comply.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 235
