557. LETTER TO AMRITLAL T. NANAVATI
September 27, 1938
CHI. AMRITLAL,
You do not seem to have started the music lessons for Sharda.
Do not wait for her to request you. It is intolerable that Cha-
kraiya should go on losing weight. Think over this. I have kept
the question of his pay pending. Please have a talk with him and
decide.
Vijaya's health does not seem to have improved. You must
be keeping up your health.
Let me know the arrangements for work there. And about
sanitary work.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10774
