73. LETTER TO TAN YUN SHAN
[April 9, 1937]
DEAR FRIEND,
Many thanks for your letter. I have written to Gurudev
expressing my sorrow over my utter inability to attend the func-
tion. Yes, indeed, we want cultural contact between the two
nations. Yours is a worthy effort. May it bring fruit.
Yours sincerely,
Twenty Years of the Visva-Bharati Cheena Bhavana, 1937-1957, p. 16
