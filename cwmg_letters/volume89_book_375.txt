375. LETTER TO OM PRAKASH
October 21, 1947
BHAI OM PRAKASH,
The law of ahimsa is that one does not hit back at the
assailant, faces the attack in a spirit of love and dies in the
process. When one dies facing the assailant's attack in a spirit
of love, a change of heart takes place in him. It is not always
easy to say if the love was true or when the change of heart
on the part of the assailant comes about. But while practising
ahimsa, it is enough to keep in mind the law of ahimsa and
the way it works.
