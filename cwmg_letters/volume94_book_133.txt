133. LETTER TO KAMALADEVI CHATTOPADHYAYA
SEVAGRAM,
Via WARDHA (C. P.),
November 8, 1941
DEAR KAMALADEVI,
I have your letter. You will come when you wish . . . . The
visit to J. L. can be fixed up after your arrival.
Love.
BAPU
From the original : Kamaladevi Chattopadhyaya Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
