129. LETTER TO NARGIS CAPTAIN
March 18, 1933
MRS. NARGIS CAPTAIN,
I have your letter. What a frail body God has given you!
We have to be thankful to Him that your mind remains as fresh
as ever, though the body so often fails you.
I hope you will see me on your way back to Bombay.
I take it that you will pass the hot months in Panchgani. Like
the poor, Harijans are with you wherever you go, therefore, even
in Panchgani you will have some Harijans to move amongst and
care for. That's what Khambhatta is going to do during the
two months that he wants to pass in Mahabaleshwar.
I enclose herewith a returned letter which I had sent to you
at Kumbhakonam.
