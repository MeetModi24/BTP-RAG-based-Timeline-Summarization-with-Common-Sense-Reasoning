259. LETTER TO D. B. KALELKAR
June 24, 1940
CHI. KAKA,
Your letter will not do. The cutting does not support your
case. Just now I am busy with Harijan work. It was with some
reluctance that I spared time even to read what you have sent.
I will draft a letter and send it after I am free from Harijan
work. You will of course see my letter. Let Anna go. Release
him completely. You will not be able to shoulder the responsi-
bility for his nephew. It will be better if you frankly tell him so.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10932
