154. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL GANDHI
April 10, 1930
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I have your letter. I send herewith a draft of your acceptance letter. I
understand about khadi. Krishna would have gained some strength by now.
About me, there is a scare daily about the proverbial tiger coming. It kept
me awake the whole of last night but the tiger did not come. Let me see
when it does.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 32853
