501. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
NEW DELHI,
July 28, 1947
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have your letter. I shall let you know when something
is settled about the date. We shall see what we have to do
after we reach Noakhali. Let us not incur any expenditure now.
Babua should be well. Is not typhoid caused by water? Every-
one should drink boiled water.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9283
