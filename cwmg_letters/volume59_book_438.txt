438. LETTER TO G. MUKERJI
November 21, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
You should follow what appears in the Press from time
to time and guide yourself accordingly. I would not trouble
you to come here, at least for the time being.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI G. MUKERJI, M.SC.
BANSBERIA P. O.
HOOGHLY DT.
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
