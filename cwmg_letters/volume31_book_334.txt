334. LETTER TO BHAGIRATH KANODIA
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
Wednesday, Sravana Shukla 10, August 18, 1926
BHAI BHAGIRATHJI,
I enclose a receipt for the amount of Rs. 5,000 which you sent
at the request of Jamnalalji. I thank you for the sum.
SHRI BHAGIRATHJI KANODIA
C/o BIRLA BROTHERS, LTD.
137, CANNING STREET
CALCUTTA
From a microfilm of the Hindi: S.N. 12249
