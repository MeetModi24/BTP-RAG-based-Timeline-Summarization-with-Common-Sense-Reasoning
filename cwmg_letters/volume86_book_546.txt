546. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
February 1, 1947
CHI. VALLABHBHAI,
These two friends have told me the tale of their woes. What
can I tell them, or do for them? If what they say is correct, it
is a painful matter. They asked for an introduction to you; so
I am giving this to them.
I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SARDAR VALLABHBHAI PATEL
1 AURANGZEB ROAD
NEW DELHI
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 347
