178. LETTER TO MOOLCHAND AGRAWAL
November 24, 1941
BHAI MOOLCHANDJI,
Blessings to Chi. Savitri and her husband. It is good that both
are khadi-wearers, and that there will be no purdah during the
wedding. I hope both of them will continue to render service and
live happily.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 842
