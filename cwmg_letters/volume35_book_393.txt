393. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
January 30, 1928
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I have now got and read your statement. It is fairly accu-
rate. I have doubts as to one or two things. You have signed it.
Is it the original? Or is it copy of a statement sent to some
paper? I may not print it in Young India.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1585
