102. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
April 29, 1942
BHAI HARIBHAU,
Please convey my blessings to Chi. Brihaspati and Chi. Ram-
kunwar. I do hope they will both render service to the country.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
P.O. RAJKOT, via VADNAGAR
MARWAR
From the Hindi original: Haribhau Upadhyaya Papers. Courtesy: Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
