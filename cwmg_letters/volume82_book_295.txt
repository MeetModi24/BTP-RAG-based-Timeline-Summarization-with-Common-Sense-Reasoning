295. LETTER TO V. G. GAVANDE
SODEPUR,
December 14, 1945
BHAI GAVANDE,
Ramkrishna has given me your statement. When you send
something to someone, whether it is long or short, it should
always be legible. What you have sent is illegible. Unless one
gives it concentrated attention one can't read it. Where do I
have so much time? I am sorry to say I have not been able to
read it. In writing this my intention is to tell you that anything,
any statement you send, should be in a clear and legible hand.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI V. G. GAVANDE
MAHAL, NAGPUR
