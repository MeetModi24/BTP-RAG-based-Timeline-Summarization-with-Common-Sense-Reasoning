63. LETTER TO WANDA DYNOWSKA
PANCHGANI,
July 27, 1946
DEAR UMA,
I have the History of the Poles and your two letters. R.
Kumari is in Calcutta. She will be back at the end of the month.
I did write. There is an acknowledgment and a promise of
inquiry and of writing again. As soon as I have a definite
answer I shall let you know. Keep cool and patient.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G. N. 1211. Also C. W. 5102. Courtesy: Wanda Dynowska
