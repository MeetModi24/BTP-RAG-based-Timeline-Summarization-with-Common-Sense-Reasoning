325. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
October 22, 1937
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. I have torn it up.
There is no pos-
sibility at all of my going to the Frontier before the 7th. I hope
to return to Wardha on the 1st or the 2nd.
You may, therefore, certainly come over as planned.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3504
