237. LETTER TO D. K. GOSAVI
July 7, 1941
MY DEAR GOSAVI,
Rajen Babu's return date is uncertain. He won't come be-
fore the middle of July. Deokinandan's argument is plausible
but not convincing. You may anticipate Rajen Babu's return
and come and bring D. with you.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI D. K. GOSAVI
CONGRESS HOUSE
POONA 5
