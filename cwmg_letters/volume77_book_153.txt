153. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
JUHU,
June 3, 1944
MY DEAR AMRIT,
Your note in reply to mine . Your first letter was never
received. Sushila will write to you fully. I am progressing steadily
though slowly. You must not worry. If God wants more service
from me, He will surely keep me and give me sufficient strength
to do His work. How about you? How about Shummy and
Beryl? Give me all the details you are permitted to give. Are
all the old attendants still with you?
Love.
BAPU
From the original : C.W. 4144. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7779
