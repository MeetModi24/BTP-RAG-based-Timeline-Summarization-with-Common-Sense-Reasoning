174. LETTER TO VITHAL R. SHINDE
September 20, 1932
I have your touching letter. I have absolutely no notion
as to how I am to be disposed of. Therefore it is too early to
say anything. I certainly begin the fast here at 12. God alone
knows where, when and how it will end. All the same, pray
accept my thanks for your sympathy and offer.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 58
