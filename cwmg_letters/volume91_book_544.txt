544. LETTER TO VRAJLAL JAGANNATH SHARMA
Kartik Krishna 6, 1983 [November 25, 1926]
SHRI VRAJLALJI,
I have your letter. I beg to be excused for the delay in
replying. Your mother can certainly come here. She can also
have a room to herself. I hope she will not be hindered by the
problem of untouchability.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI VRAJLAL JAGANNATH SHARMAJI
C/O B. N. MOTIWALA, ESQ.
MALABAR HILL
BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 10577
