498. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
ON THE TRAIN,
October 24, 1936
CHI. AMALA,
Mira and Nanavati had been ill, and so the delay in
replying. They are now better. I am on my way to Bena-
res.
I return to Segaon about 5th November.
I reach
Ahmedabad on 30th and [shall] be there for four days at least.
I hope you understand my corrections. Of course you need
milk and butter and so do you need fruit. Take it as medicine
to save more expenses.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
