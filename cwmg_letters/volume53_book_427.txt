427. LETTER TO AGATHA HARRISON
February 17, 1933
MY DEAR AGATHA,
I have your letter with the two enclosures which you dug
out of Andrews's pocket.
Now that I give you a long letter in the shape of Harijan,
you must not expect personal letters except rarely.
I know that you are working full speed and that is all we
can do. The results are controlled by the Infinite Power, and
hence we may not worry.
I have enclosed a number of important cuttings with
Andrews's letter. Do get them all and read them.
Love from us both.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1461
