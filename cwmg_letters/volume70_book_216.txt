216. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 18, 1939
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your letter. I hope to be here for the present. I
cannot say when and where I may be required to go.
I understand what you say about Brother.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2473
