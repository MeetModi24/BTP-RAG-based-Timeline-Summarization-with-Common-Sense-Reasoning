427. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
April 23, 1932
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
If you have faith in yourself, my hopes in you will be realized.
You must have written to the Ashram for Gangabehn
[Vaidya]'s powder and pills.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1835
