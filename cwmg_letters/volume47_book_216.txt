216. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
SURAT,
July 24, 1931
CHI. KUSUM,
I got all your letters. Since you said in all of them that
you would soon come and see me, I did not write even to acknow-
ledge them. I am writing this because your last letter shows that
you are not sure in your mind. I shall be going to Borsad in a
day or two. From there, I intend to go to Ahmedabad. I don't
know, though, whether I shall be able to do so.
My going to England is completely uncertain. Come and see
me when you can. Ask Dahiben to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1824
