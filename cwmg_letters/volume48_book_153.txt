153. LETTER TO MIRZA ISMAIL
[After October 24, 1931]
DEAR SIR MIRZA,
Many thanks for the cutting which I have read. I hope
you[r] effort with Dr. Ambedkar will prevail. It was a joy to
me to meet Humayun at Oxford. I wish I could have seen more
of him.
Yours,
M.K.G.
From a photostat: G.N. 2188-
