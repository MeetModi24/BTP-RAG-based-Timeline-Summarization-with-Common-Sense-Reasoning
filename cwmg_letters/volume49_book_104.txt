104. LETTER TO RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
February 6, 1932
DEAR RAMANANDA BABU,
Many thanks for The Modern Review. Do please send me
The Golden Book of Tagore. It will be allowed. My love to Gurudev
when you meet him.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
"[THE] MODERN REVIEW"
UPPER CIRCULAR ROAD, CALCUTTA
From the original: C.W. 9501. Courtesy: Santa Devi
