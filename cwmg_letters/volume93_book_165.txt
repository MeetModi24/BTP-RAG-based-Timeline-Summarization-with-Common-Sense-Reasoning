165. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
WARDHA,
February 24, 1937
DEAR PLAYMATE,
When are you going to be fit for play? It is a shame for
you to be bedridden so often. Not enough for me that you can
laugh at your illness. Love.
BAPU
