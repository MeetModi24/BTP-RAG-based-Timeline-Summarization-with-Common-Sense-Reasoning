565. LETTER TO KISHORELAL MASHRUWALA
March 15, 1927
CHI. KISHORELAL,
I have your letter. Bhai Karsandas's scheme is very good
but he does not have competent women workers. Bombay is
not the field for such work.
Is it not a wonder that you are well? How are you now?
Manilal and Sushila are well. I left the Ashram yesterday.
15th-17th Bardoli, 19th-21st Gurukul Kangri, and on Friday Bijnor.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C.W. 10722. Courtesy : Gomatibehn
Mashruwala
