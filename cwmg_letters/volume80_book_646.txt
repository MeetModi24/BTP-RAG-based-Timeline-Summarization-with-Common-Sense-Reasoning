646. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
[PANCHGANI/] SIMLA WEST,
[On or before June 19/] June 30, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your letter of the 3rd. It is good that you gave
me the detailed information about uncle. I on my part have
put uncle in your hands in the hope that he will get well. For
the sake of his treatment certainly be firm with him. I wrote
this letter long back in Panchgani. I did not post it as I left
the place. Now I wish to inquire about uncle and Sailen.
Give whatever treatment you think fit to Sailen. Both of them
should get well. I will have to think about your expenses. Sailen
should write to me. Please show this to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
