292. LETTER TO HANNAH LAZAR
[August 1, 1938]
If he has money to spare, he has to send you back when
the climate is bearable and you have trained yourself in South
Africa for the Indian life as you now know it-no risks can be
taken here. And what is the use of your going away from me
for three months? And if you have to go away, then off to the
far south, that is to say, since you can't stay here in Segaon
for three months, you have to go away. Therefore, the safest
thing is to go to the far south, i. e., South Africa, and then, if
all is well, you may return when the cold climate sets in.
From the original : Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy:
National Archives of India
