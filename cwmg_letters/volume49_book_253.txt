253. LETTER TO MANGALA S. PATEL
March 20, 1932
CHI. MANGALA (DARLING),
When we get swaraj, we will certainly do a great many things.
You will see caves and many other things besides. But there are
many interesting things to see all around us, and we can see
them even today. If you know how to see these, then alone will
you see other things correctly. Have you learnt to look at the
sky? I am learning to do it in my old age.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4079. Also C.W. 43. Courtesy:
Mangalabehn B. Desai
