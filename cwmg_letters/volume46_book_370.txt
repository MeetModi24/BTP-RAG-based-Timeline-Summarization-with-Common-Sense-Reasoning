370. LETTER TO CHARLES F. WELLER
AS AT SABARMATI,
June 3, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your warm letter of 17th April. 1933 is a
far cry for me and I could not even make tentative arrangements
for that year. You will therefore have to write to me, if all goes
well, towards the middle of 1932 when I might be able to give a
decisive answer. I am sure you will appreciate my difficulty.
Yours sincerely,
CHARLES F. WELLER, ESQ.
CHICAGO'S FELLOWSHIP OF FAITHS
CHICAGO (U.S.A.)
From a photostat: S.N. 17205
