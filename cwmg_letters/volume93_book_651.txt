651. LETTER TO DR. ABRAHAM PAUL
SEVAGRAM,
Via WARDHA,
January 31, 1941
DEAR DR. ABRAHAM,
Your two letters. The quarrel you describe is heart-rending.
I am doing what I can. As to medical men doing war work,
there is room for difference of opinion. I am firmly of opinion
that those who so help, promote war as an institution. There
are many things we do under the name of virtue.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. ABRAHAM PAUL, M. B. B. S.
SERANGO P. O.
GANJAM DIST.
Via PARLAKIMEDI
ORISSA
From a photostat: C. W. 10977. Courtesy: Dr. Abraham Paul
