235. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
[February] 7, 1940
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I am sending the accompanying letter to you because you
know more about the matter referred to by the correspondent. You
know about my doings in Delhi. Now cables are being exchanged
with London. I am leaving for Santiniketan on the night of the
15th. From there to Calcutta on the 19th.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 239
