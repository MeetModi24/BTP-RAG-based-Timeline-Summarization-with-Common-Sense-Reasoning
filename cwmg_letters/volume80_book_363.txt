363. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
May 30, 1945
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
I saw what you wrote at the bottom of Vinodini's letter.
How did you get dysentery? The ideal diet is that which im-
proves one's health day by day. Do you think there is some
defect in your diet somewhere? You yourself should discover it.
I am glad indeed that Manu has gone there. She will continue
to cause me worry till she gets completely well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
SIND MARKET No. 35
MAHATMA GANDHI ROAD
KARACHI
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./24
