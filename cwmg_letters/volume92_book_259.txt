259. LETTER TO WILFRED WELLOCK
February 2, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
You will forgive me for not being able to write to you
earlier. It is impossible for me to deal with every friendly letter
separately. I only hope that you are following carefully the
pages of Young India in which I pour out my heart weekly. It
seems to me the problem is beyond Mr. Benn's capacity even
if he has the will. Justice will not be done to India till Britain
realizes that India has been terribly wronged and that, therefore,
she has to part with millions that are being yearly drained
from India.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
WILFRED WELLOCK, ESQ.
From a copy: Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S. N. 32579/70
