446. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
[May 26, 1930]
DEAR GANGABEHN (ELDER),
I remembered you when I was reading Ch. XII and XIII.
I have written at the top of the translation of Ch. XII, which
is short, that it is worthy of being committed to memory by all, if
not in Sanskrit, at least in Gujarati. It is easy to understand
it in Gujarati. When we know the line of devotion shown there-
in, we have finished all. You must have undoubtedly written a
letter, but it has not yet come to my hands.
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
