217. LETTER TO KANHAIYALAL VAIDYA
SEVAGRAM,
December 7, 1941
BHAI KANHAIYALAL,
I have sent a telegram. You will have seen Munshiji. Being
too busy I could not answer your letter [earlier]. Also, there was
nothing to say.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
