405. LETTER TO LALITA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 8, 1930
CHI. LALITA,
I have your letter. You have done all right in going with
Gangabehn. Work sincerely now and be brave. For your age,
the handwriting is bad and the language also is not quite correct.
Make an effort and improve both. You can do that even while you
stay there. What you will require for the effort is not time but
perseverance. Progressing slowly, you can improve quite a lot.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9218
