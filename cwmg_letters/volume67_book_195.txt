195. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON,
June 30, 1938
MY DEAR IDIOT,
You won't mind the missing of a day now and again.
My statement is still hanging fire. It is still to be mended.
I am having extraordinary opinions on it.
Did I tell you Kallenbach's niece had come here? She is very
weak in body. She is most anxious to help. I wish you would see
her. If she does not remain well, I shall have to pack her off.
Shanta has gone back.
Balkrishna is not bad.
Love.
ROBBER
From the original: C.W. 3868. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7024
