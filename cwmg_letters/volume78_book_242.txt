242. LETTER TO MANGALDAS PAKVASA
SEVAGRAM,
October 16, 1944
BHAI MANGALDAS,
It was of course my fault that I misunderstood what you
said. I beg your forgiveness for that. However, though my mis-
understanding you two may be pardonable, whose forgiveness am
I to ask for having taken interest in your story of a theft and the
remarks I made on hearing the report? It has relieved me some-
what to know that Jinnah Saheb has punished me fairly well for
that. But for me this punishment is not enough.
The alternating improvement and worsening in your health
is not a good sign. Please do take care.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 4689. Courtesy: Mangaldas
Pakvasa
