665. LETTER TO RAMPRASAD
April 17, 1945
CHI. RAMPRASAD,
I have your letter. Let Mama also write. Please go to
Mirabehn. All the information is given in the enclosed letter.
Say to Mirabehn : "Give my wages to Bapu. He will pay me. I
have come to assist you for two months."
You can easily do this. Write to me when you arrive there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
