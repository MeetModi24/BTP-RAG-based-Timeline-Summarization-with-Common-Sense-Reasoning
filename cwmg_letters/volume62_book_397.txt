397. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
WARDHA,
April 23, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
I am going to Nandi Durg, taking Sardar with me. I am
prepared to take Kusum with me if you can send her and if
she wants to come. We shall be leaving here on the 8th. She
can accompany Sardar who is coming here from Ahmedabad.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8487. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
