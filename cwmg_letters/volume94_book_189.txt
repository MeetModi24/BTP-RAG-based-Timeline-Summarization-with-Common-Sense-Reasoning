189. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
July 9, 1942
CHI. SUSHTLA,
You will have seen the doctors' bulletin in the Press. There is no
cause for worry at all. Sardar unnecessarily called them. I feel nothing
but exhaustion. He only suggests rest. I have given up roti I am taking
more of mosambis. I am of course taking vegetables.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
