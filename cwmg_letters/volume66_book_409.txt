409. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
JUHU,
December 30, 1937
CHI. VIJAYA,
I have your letter. It is surprising that spinning on the takli
tires you out. Build up your strength so much and so well that you
will not get fatigued no matter how long you spin. Find out the
reason why you get fatigued. See that you don't fall ill again.
Kanu and others will write about me. Ask Vinoba about your-
self. If he and Vallabh leave perhaps you need not stay on at
Nalwadi. Write to me from time to time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7077. Also C.W. 4569. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
