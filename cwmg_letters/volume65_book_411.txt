411. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
June 1937
CHI. JAMNALAL,
If you have agreed to pay Rs. 1,000 towards Harjivan's
expenses, then please send it by wire. The following is the
answer on behalf of the Sangh:
"Wiring thousand cover travelling. Regret inability advance
loan."
I understand that you have not given him any assurance
about the loan. So I do not see any need for your giving it.
I am sending back Shankar's letter addressed to you. It is
good to send him books.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am sending Harjivan's also.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2985
