529. LETTER TO KANTILAL AND SARASWATI GANDHI
March 26, 1945
CHI. SURU AND KANTI,
I have letters from both of you. It is good that Harilal
has arrived there. You have to serve him in any case. What-
ever he may be he is your father. It will be good if he
recovers. [Suru,] you will pass the examination.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Kanti can stay in the Ashram, [if not] with friends. I
cannot say what will happen if I go somewhere.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 6184. Also C.W. 3458. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
