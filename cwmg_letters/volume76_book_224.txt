224. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 5, 1942
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
I received your letter.
There is nothing from Maulana. In one letter he had
said that he would be accompanying you here.
Fischer has arrived. I do manage to give him an hour every
day. He is staying at the Ashram.
Hot winds are blowing here.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
