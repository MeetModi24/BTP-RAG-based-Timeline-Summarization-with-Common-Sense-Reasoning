572. LETTER TO HEMA PRABHA DAS GUPTA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Sunday, May 23, 1926
DEAR SISTER,
Your letter this time made me very happy. You do not say
anything about your pleasant or unpleasant experiences at the
Ashram. I know that there is hard work at Sodepur, and I have
written to Satis Babu about that. On no account, however, should
you stay there at the cost of your health. You should not draw a
single pice from the amount kept in the bank. I do know that
khadi work cannot be done without hardship voluntarily suffered,
but such hardship should be within one's capacity. May God
grant both of you peace and strength.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1647
