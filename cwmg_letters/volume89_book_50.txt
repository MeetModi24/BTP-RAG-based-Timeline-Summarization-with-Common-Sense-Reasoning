50. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
August 13, 1947
DAUGHTER AMTUL SALAAM,
Today I have come to stay in a Muslim house. Shaheed
Saheb is with me. God knows what will happen. I shall be
tested. I do not know when I shall see you now.
Yesterday I met Baqui. He had with him Nawab Saheb's
son and daughter. I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 585
