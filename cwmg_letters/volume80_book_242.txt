242. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL TRIVEDI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 15, 1945
BHAI CHIMANLAL,
I have your letter. Sir Radhakrishnan's letter is clear. You
should arrange a suitable programme. You have already received
my blessings, haven't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI CHIMANLAL TRIVEDI
MEHERVILLA
OPP. SUPER TALKIES
GRANT ROAD, BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
