283. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA KAMDAR
January 7, 1942
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA,
Valjibhai tells me that your husband has passed away. You are
a brave woman and have wisdom. Bear the separation patiently.
Look after the children and engage yourself in some public service.
Do not cry. Write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7473. Also C.W. 4919. Courtesy:
Hari-ichchha Kamdar
