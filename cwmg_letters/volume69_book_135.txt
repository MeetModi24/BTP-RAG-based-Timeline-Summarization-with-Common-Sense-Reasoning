135. LETTER TO R. K. JHA
NEW DELHI,
April 5, 1939
BHAI JHA,
Have received your letter. The telegram could not be replied
to. I wish satyagraha at both the places, Rajnandgaon and
Chhuikhadan, to be suspended for the time being. I am writing
in Harijan. It should be carefully read and people should be
educated.
M. K. GANDHI
The Hitavada, 12-4-1939
