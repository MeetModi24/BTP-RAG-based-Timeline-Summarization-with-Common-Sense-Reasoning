330. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 18, 1930
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I got your wire, but very late. Did you get my letter after-
wards? Both of you must have recovered your peace of mind now.
I see from your wire that now your health at any rate is all right.
The weakness must disappear. Do you go out for a walk daily?
What does Jayaprakash intend to do?
My health is good. I have again started eating dates and raisins.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3373
