303. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
November 4, 1934
MY DEAR ANAND,
I was glad to have your little note and to observe that you
were making steady, though slow, progress. I am looking forward
to seeing you with Jairamdas as soon as you are discharged. But
don't worry the doctors over your discharge. Let there be a com-
plete recovery. I must not attempt to write to Vidya just now as I
want every minute that I can spare.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI ANAND T. HINGORANI
K. E. M. HOSPITAL
PAREL, BOMBAY
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand T.
Hingorani
