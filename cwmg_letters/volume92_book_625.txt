625. LETTER TO SHANTA S. PATEL
[1932]
CRI. SHANTA,
I have your letter. It is good. You must now make your
handwriting as good as the letter. When we meet, remain young
enough to serve me as a staff for support. Some people however
old they grow remain children at heart. What does child mean?
Write your answer.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 4052
