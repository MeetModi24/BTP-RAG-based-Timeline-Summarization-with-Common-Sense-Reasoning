135. LETTER TO VISHWAMITRA N. VAIDYA
SIMLA,
May 6, 1946
BHAI VISHWAMITRA,
You rightly employ the term "culprit". If Ayurveda is in a
poor state the fault lies with the vaidyas, and they alone can re-
medy it. Other help will follow as a matter of course.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
VAIDYASHRI VISHWAMITRA NANALAL
GHEEVATO
KHODANO PADO
PATAN
