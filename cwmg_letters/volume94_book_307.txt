307. LETTER TO K. VENKATARAMANI
SEVAGRAM,
November 9, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji had your letter of 31st October.
Gandhiji says you can come here whenever you like for your week's stay here.
You will, of course, send previous intimation of your arrival.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
SHRI K. VENKATARAMANI
34 ALAMELUMANGAPURAM
MYLAPORE
MADRAS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
