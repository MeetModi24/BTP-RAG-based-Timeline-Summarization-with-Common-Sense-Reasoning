274. LETTER TO SIR RAYMOND WEST
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 29, 1906
DEAR SIR RAYMOND,
May I, on behalf of Mr. Ally and myself, take this opportunity
of repeating our thanks for your noble and inspiring utterances at the
breakfast today? I know that we will have the benefit of your support
and advice in our struggle for existence. The thought that so many
distinguished men are heart and soul with us buoys us up and enables
us to hope for better things though the cloud seems to be the blackest.
I remain,
Yours truly,
SIR RAYMOND WEST, K.C.I.E.
"CHESTERFIELD"
COLLEGE ROAD
NORWOOD, S.E.
