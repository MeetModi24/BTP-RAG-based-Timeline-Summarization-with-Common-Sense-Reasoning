460. LETTER TO GANGABEHN B. JHAVERI
February 18, 1933
CHI. GANGABEHN,
Your letter was welcome. I had been waiting for it. Come
and see me next Tuesday. I am not writing more here as we
shall meet soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3952
1, & 3 The names have been omitted.
