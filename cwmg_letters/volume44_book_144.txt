144. LETTER TO SATYADEVI GIRI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 22, 1930
CHI. SATYADEVI,
It is better you correct the mistakes in your letter then and
there than that I should read it making the corrections. The
benefit is twofold. You will notice your own errors and I will have
no corrections to make. Isn't it good?
Tell Mother to write to me describing her present acti-
vities.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Bapuki Viral Vatsalata, p. 34
