534. LETTER TO ANNE MARIE PETERSEN
October 2, 1945
MY DEAR MARIA,
Your letter.
You are foolish, so are we all, some more, some less. There-
fore why worry? You must be well and live long enough to see
your work flourish.
Your application is under way. The Board meets some time
this month when I hope the application will be finally consi-
dered. The ideal will be to find your expenses from the sur-
roundings. We must strive to reach it.
Of course you will see me in Madras when I come there.
Have you fixed your date for the departure overseas?
Send my love to Esther when you write to her and accept
it for yourself.
BAPU
MISS MARIE PETERSEN
SEVA MANDIR
PORTO Novo (S. INDIA)
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
