119. LETTER TO VALJI G. DESAI
February 10, 1932
BHAISHRI VALJI,
I got the packet sent by you. I will look into the transla-
tion at least, as and when I get time and let you know when I
finish it. I have already replied to your previous letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 7419. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
