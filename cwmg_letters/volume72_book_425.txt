425. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM K. JERAJANI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 9, 1940
BHAI KAKUBHAI,
Please thank Dr. Vaidya on my behalf for his joining you.
You will certainly have warmth from him. You have done well
in taking over the responsibility of the exhibition.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10847. Courtesy: Purushottam
K. Jerajani
