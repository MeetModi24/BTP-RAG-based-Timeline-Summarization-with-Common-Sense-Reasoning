147. LETTER TO SARASVATI GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
November 3, 1940
CHI. SURU1,
What a girl you are! Having joined Kanti you write neither
to me nor to Ba. This is not proper. I hope all of you are
well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6180. Also C.W. 3454. Courtesy:
Kantilal Gandhi
