606. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 2, 1941
According to me you have escaped death. I was afraid that
you might not survive. Don't you think, therefore, that you have
really escaped. Don't worry about anything. Let Bombay sink into
the Back Bay if it will. If Janaka did not feel the least concerned
while Janakapuri was burning, why should we prove ourselves
weaker than Janaka? Shouldn't we do better than he? How
we can do that, only God knows. But Janaka himself has shown us
how we can be like him.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 181
