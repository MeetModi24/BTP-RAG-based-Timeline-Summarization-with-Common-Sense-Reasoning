657. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEVAGRAM,
February 4, 1941
CHI. MATHURADAS,
In my view, a woman should be made the President. But
she should not be the fourth candidate. When it is the Hindus'
turn a Hindu woman should be selected. When it is the Christians'
turn there should be a Christian woman. Similarly, with the
Parsis and the Muslims. If the prevailing system has to be
broken, it should be done by a non-Congress person. This is
my firm view.
You will be keeping good health. You must go out for
walks. You must follow restrictions in your diet.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I feel Dr. Gilder should not come here and Mahadev
should not go there to meet him. All others are busy or in
jail. You can give this to him to read.
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal
Papers. Nehru
