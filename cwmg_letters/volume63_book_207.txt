207. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN GANDHI
July 22, 1936
CHI. VIJAYA 2 ,
I have your little note. You do not write with an open
heart still. I am glad that Jamna has asked for a copy of the
Gita with the Sanskrit text and printed in large type for your
use. I have asked for one and shall send it to you both.
The third chapter of the Gita which you have learnt is
very useful to us. Its effect is true to its heading . It inter-
prets (in the highest sense) yajna as body labour. However, I
have described fetching water, grinding corn, sweeping and the
processes connected with spinning as the panchamahayajnas. How
many of them do you perform?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II.
