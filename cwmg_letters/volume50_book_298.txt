298. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS PODDAR
July 31, 1932
CHI. RAMESHWARDAS,
I got your two letters. It is good that you left Ram to take
care of Vinoba. This is not separation but union. Do not remove
him from there. If Gangadevi wants to learn English, she
should certainly be permitted to do so. A husband has no right
to hinder his wife's progress.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 183
