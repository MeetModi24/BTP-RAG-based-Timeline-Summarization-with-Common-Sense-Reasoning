515. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Shravan Vad 1, Tuesday [August 24, 1926]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. You must have received my letter of
yesterday about Miss Haussding. Pattani Saheb's bungalow can
certainly be rented. And if no other place is available, I should
not hesitate to ask him for the bungalow.
Blessings from
BAPU
SJT. MATHURADAs TRIKUMJI
HOMI VILLA
PANCHGANI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
