63. LETTER TO GANGABEHN ZAVERI
March 13, 1930
CHI. GANGABEHN ZAVERI,
I believe you have joined the common kitchen now. Will
you also stay in the Hostel? Nanibehn should live only on milk
and fruit. If she does that, she will be all right.
This is a rare opportunity for women. I think it will be a
great achievement on their part if they assume the entire burden
of the internal management of the Ashram.
I have no time to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3099
