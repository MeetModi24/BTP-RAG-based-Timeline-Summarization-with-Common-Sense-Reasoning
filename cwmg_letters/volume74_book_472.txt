472. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
SEVAGRAM,
September 3, 1941
CHI. MANI,
I have your letter. You have done well in giving me all the
details. I sent yesterday Jassawala's letter. I strongly urge your
starting treatment as advised by him. Once the health badly
deteriorates, all remedies are likely to fail. I do think you must
have a discussion with Dr. Nathubhai.
Keep me fully informed.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 131
