388. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
April 21, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
Jamnalalji has given full authority to commence building
operations . If you come tomorrow evening or 23rd morning, I
could explain everything. I leave for Nagpur 23rd evening, re-
turning 26th evening or 27th morning at the latest.
More when we meet.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6328. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9794
