329. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 18, 1930
CHI. PREMA,
I have your letter. Describe to me your experience in
Bombay. You are not well advised in refusing to get your throat
examined by a doctor. A disease must be nipped in the bud. A
stitch in time saves nine. This saying is completely true.
I interpret image-worship in two senses. In one form of
image-worship, the person who contemplates the image becomes
absorbed in the contemplation of the qualities for which it stands.
This is image-worship in its wholesome form. In the other form
of it, the person who contemplates the image does not think about
the qualities but looks upon the image itself as the primary thing.
This is idolatry and is an evil.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10239; also C.W. 6687. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
