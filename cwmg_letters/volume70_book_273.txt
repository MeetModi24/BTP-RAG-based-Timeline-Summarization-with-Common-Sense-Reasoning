273. LETTER TO KUNVARJI K. PAREKH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 8, 1939
CHI. KUNVARJI,
I got your postcard. I had made arrangements for you at
Nagpur. But since you went to Miraj, I wired to you not to
come away in haste. The poor have but to put up with common
hardships. I, therefore, encouraged you to stay on. Now patiently
overcome the other difficulties that may arise and see if you derive
any benefit. If you do not improve write to me immediately.
Keep me informed about your progress. Do not send for the
children. The best plan would be for Bali to take them to Bombay.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Surendra will write more from Bombay.
