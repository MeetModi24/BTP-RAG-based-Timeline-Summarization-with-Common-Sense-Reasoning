179. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
TITHAL,
May 16, 1937
CHI. MRIDULA,
I have your letter. Come as soon as you can. I cannot
off-hand think of any woman's name. But can you not postpone
the matter till you come here?
One name has occurred to me while writing this. But I shall
not let you have it now. If you cannot hold on and if you
cannot check your curiosity then write to me and I shall let you
have it. Otherwise, I shall tell you when you come. You may
guess if you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
MRIDULABEHN SARABHAI
SHAHI BAUG
AHMEDABAD (B. B. & C. I. RLY.)
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11204. Courtesy: Sarabhai
