385. LETTER TO ABDUL HUK AND KAIKHUSHROO
[JOHANNESBURG,]
May 20, 1905
MESSRS JALBHAI SORABJI BROS
84, FIELD STREET
DURBAN
MY DEAR SHRI ABDUL HUK AND KAIKHUSHROO,
Your letter to hand. The power of attorney passed by Sheth Azam
Gulam Hussain has been found.
Hussain Issop seems to be an employee in the shop. He has asked
for an advance of £15 against his salary. He says that you have asked
him to get my sanction. I think there should be no objection to giving
him such an advance, if his work is quite satisfactory, if he is trustworthy
and is really in need of money. But I leave that to your discretion.
Salaams from
M. K. GANDHI
From the Gujarati in Gandhiji's hand: Letter Book (1905) : No. 103.
