471. LETTER TO ZAHID ALI
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
January 25, 1929
MY DEAR ZAHID ALI,
I have your letter. Please tell me how many charkhas you
have, whether they are in perfect working order, whether they
have spindles. And give me also the size of the wheels and the
kind of spindle-holders and a detailed specification. Tell me also
the price required and I shall try to do what I can.
Yours sincerely,
ZAHID ALI, ESQ.
THE CHHOTANI SAW MILLS
SEWRI BUNDER, BOMBAY
From a microfilm: S.N. 14995
