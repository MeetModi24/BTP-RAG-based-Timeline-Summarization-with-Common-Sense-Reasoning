55. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
September 11, 1932
CHI. CHHAGANLAL (JOSHI),
Though I am not sure you will get this letter, I am writing
it. I would have been glad if you had written to me giving me
your impressions about the Ashram. If you get this letter, you
may write to me even now. I must know everything, though of
course there is nothing I can do.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5508
