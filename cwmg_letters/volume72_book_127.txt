127. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 25, 1940
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have read your letter to Rajku[mari].
None of you is to
be selected. Render such service as you can. If the battle is
joined, truth and falsehood will stand revealed. You must do
something in the villages if you can. I am keeping good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2481
