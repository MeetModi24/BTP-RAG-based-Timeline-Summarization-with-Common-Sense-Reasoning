223. LETTER TO BALIBEHN M. ADALJA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
June 11, 1940
CHI. BALI 2 ,
I got your letter. You have been born to wear yourself out in
the service of the family. And so you take upon yourself even
unnecessary burdens, but God fulfils everybody's aspirations. I do
not, therefore, pity you. I shower praises on you from here.
I know that you will train Saraswati perfectly well. You
must have got her tonsils removed. It will be better if it rains
there now. You have taken a great risk by collecting such a large
crowd there at such a time. Kunvarji will most certainly spend
the monsoon months in Panchgani. He is doing very well. Kumi
must be well.
Blessings from
BAPU
