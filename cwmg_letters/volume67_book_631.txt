631. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
UTMANZAI,
October 13, 1938
CHI. MIRA,
Your draft letter to Dr. B. is perfect.
I can't alter a word.
I have filled in the blank.
Ba is still ailing in Delhi though D. says she is on the
mend.
Mahadev is bound to benefit by his stay in Simla. More so
he will be for want of work and being cut off from me.
We may be on the move in a day or two.
I hope Balwantsinha is happy among his cows. What about
the ailing calf ?
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6408. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10003
