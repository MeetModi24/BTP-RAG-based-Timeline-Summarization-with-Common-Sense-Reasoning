335. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
August 10, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
I got your letter and rakhi. I am writing this letter with
the rakhi tied round the wrist. Congratulations on the improve-
ment in your health. Kaka and Nana are fairly well. The
danger seems to have passed. I am doing well. The diet is prac-
tically the same. Girdhari and his wife (recently married) have
come here for today.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Rajkumari arrived on Tuesday.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7094. Also C.W. 4586. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
