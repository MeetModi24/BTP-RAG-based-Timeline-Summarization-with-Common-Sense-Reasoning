309. LETTER TO NAGJIBHAI
ASHRAM,
April 8, 1926
BHAI NAGJIBHAI,
I have your letter. If you regard Vishwamitra, Vasishtha and
others as historical figures, it will be difficult to answer your
questions. If you can look upon the Ramayana as a religious work
and the legends of Vishwamitra, Parashurama and others as alle-
gorical, you will be able yourself to understand their significance.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19899
