436. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
[January 20, 1929]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I got your letter. It is good that you left Wardha. You have
not disobeyed. Come here whenever you feel like doing so.
Keep yourself in good health and keep your mind free from
worry . My programme of leaving for Sind on the 31st stands.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 504. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
