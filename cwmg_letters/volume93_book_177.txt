177. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
TITHAL, BULSAR,
May 13, 1937
CHI. MRIDULA,
We arrived here yesterday afternoon. Now you come as
soon as you can. Both of us are waiting for you.
Mummy I hope is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C. W. 11203. Courtesy: Sarabhai
