472. LETTER TO MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
August 31, 1940
CHI. MAGAN,
See that you pass. Keep your pledge of fulfilling Manjula's
wish. She is as gentle as a cow.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MAGANLAL P. MEHTA
C/O Y.M.C.A.
NEW DELHI
From Gujarati: C.W. 1607. Courtesy: Manjulabehn M. Mehta
