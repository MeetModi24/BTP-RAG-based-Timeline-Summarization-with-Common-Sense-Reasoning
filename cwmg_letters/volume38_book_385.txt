385. LETTER TO GANGADHARRAO B. DESHPANDE
January 9, 1929
BHAI GANGADHARRAO,
This telegram was received today. What is it about?
I think you will send me an account of Pundalik's satya-
graha. What was the outcome of the satyagraha?
I hope your health is all right.
Kaka is, of course, quite busy.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 5225
