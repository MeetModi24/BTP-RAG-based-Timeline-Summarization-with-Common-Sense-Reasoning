280. LETTER TO K. SADASHIVA RAO
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
September 7, 1928
MY DEAR SADASHIVA RAO,
I have your letter. If we will serve the country and not think
of ourselves, we will rejoice even when the world pelts us or
ill-treats us. And I am sure that you will feel all the better and
stronger if you will take the shock in good grace and really feel
that a load is off your back.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. SADASHIVA RAO KARNAD
KODAIBAIL, MANGALORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 13511
