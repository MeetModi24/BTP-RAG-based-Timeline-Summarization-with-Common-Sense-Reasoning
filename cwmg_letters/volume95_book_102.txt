102. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL G. BANKER
Monday [On or before April 14, 1930]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I can understand your requirement. But I would value even a
single minute I may get in Gujarat. I have sent you a telegram only
yesterday morning. You would surely have received it. I have sent
a telegram to Bhai Khatri also saying that I should be spared.
I am sending herewith a booklet which you should print and
