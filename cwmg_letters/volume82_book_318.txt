318. LETTER TO JANAKIDEVI BAJAJ
SODEPUR,
December 17, 1945
JANAKIMAIYA,
It was good that I got your telegram.
I was a bit worried. You must have got Sushila's wire
and letter. I hope you remember about the cow. You are
coming to Madras, aren't you?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 5851. Also Pyarelal Papers.
Courtesy : Pyarelal
