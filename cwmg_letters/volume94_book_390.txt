390. LETTER TO CARL HEATH
AS AT SEVAGRAM,
CAMP: "MANOR VILLE",
SIMLA WEST,
July 5, 1945
DEAR FRIEND,
Gandhiji has your letter of 21st June.
He is too busy with the Conference work to be able to write to you himself.
He desires me to thank you for your kind message. He appreciates and fully
reciprocates the spirit behind it. He is bending all his energy to end the deadlock
and you may rest assured that if the offer is cent per cent sincere it will be all well
in the end.
Thanking you once again.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
CARL HEATH, ESQ.
57 MANOR WAY
GUILDFORD, SURREY
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
