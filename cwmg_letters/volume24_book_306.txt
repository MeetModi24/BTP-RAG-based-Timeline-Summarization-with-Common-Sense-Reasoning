306. LETTER TO C. R. DAS
SABARMATI,
August 10, 1924
DEAR FRIEND,
A friend of Mr. Khopkar has handed the enclosed to me.
He says Mr. Khopkar thinks you could not have signed the paper.
But if you have, Mr. Khopkar is prepared, he says, to prove his
innocence to your satisfaction. Will you please tell me what I
should say to Mr. Khopkar's friend?
I hope you and Mrs. Das are keeping well.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MR. C. R. DAS
CALCUTTA
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
