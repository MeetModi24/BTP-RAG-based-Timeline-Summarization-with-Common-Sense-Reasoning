79. LETTER TO CHANDAN PAREKH
October 27, 1935
CHI. CHANDU,
I got your letter. Before you go to Vartej, please do pay
a visit here. You will be returning there before December. I
will be here all the while. Meanwhile, I am collecting as much
information as I can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 937. Courtesy: Satish D. Kalelkar
