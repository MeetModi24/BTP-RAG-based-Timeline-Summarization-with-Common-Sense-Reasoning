513. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
December 8, 1934
CHI. NARAHARI,
I have your letter.
The money given by Bhai Mavalankar and Ranchhodbhai
for the dairy is welcome. I had a letter from Ambalalbhai say-
ing that he did intend to pay the loss up to December 31. You
may, therefore, spend just now from the money received from
Mavalankar and Ranchhodbhai, but credit the sum to next
year's account. Similarly, when you get the money from
Ambalalbhai, you will have to return to me the Rs. 1,000 which
I sent. The account will balance then. Send the figure of the
loss up to December end to me first and then to Ambalal-
bhai. If I am in jail by that time, follow the instructions given
in this letter. When drawing up the account to determine the
amount of the loss, let me know the sums, if any, that you may
have received from other sources.
We should not include any
of them in the amount to be paid by Ambalalbhai. One such
sum, for instance, is that received for the Goseva Sangh and
[utilized for the dairy] as suggested by yourself. I have written
briefly about this to Narandas also. He doesn't know about your
last letter.
Ratilal indeed has had a narrow escape. I hope the thief
will be caught. Advise Prabhashankar to strengthen a little the
guard at the bungalow.
He may ask for legitimate help from
the police. If Ratilal feels afraid, spare a room for him in the
Ashram. It should be given on rent. If any cash or valuables are
being kept in the bungalow, I have already suggested that they
should be removed.
Blessings from
BAPU
