487. LETTER TO MAHENDRA V. DESAI
September 9, 1933
CHI. MANU1,
I got your letter. It is better to use pen for writing. I think
I owed you a letter. And now you will owe me one. You seem to
be making good progress in your study.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3159. Courtesy: Valji G. Desai
