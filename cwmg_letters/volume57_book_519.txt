519. LETTER TO MADHAVLAL PATEL
BALIANTA,
May 14, 1934
BHAI MADHAVLAL,
I got your letter. Enclosed are one letter for the girls and
one for Mithubehn.
I think it will be wise to pay the fine and save the land.
It is not worth while accepting the balance of Rs. 4-10-.
If the lender, however, wants to accept it, he certainly may.
All these are bitter draughts. But they seem the right things
to do and it is only by doing the right that we can follow truth.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6109. Also C.W. 3419. Courtesy:
Madhavlal Patel
