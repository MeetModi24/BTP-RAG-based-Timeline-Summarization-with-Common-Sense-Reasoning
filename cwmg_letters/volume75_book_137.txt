137. LETTER TO DR. S. K. VAIDYA
November 10, 1941
BHAI VAIDYA,
Your yarn is very fine indeed. There is room for improvement
in the strength. I suppose you will get a piece woven specially
from this yarn? Why do you not yourself get a loom installed? I
certainly believe that for you your own method is the best one.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5753
