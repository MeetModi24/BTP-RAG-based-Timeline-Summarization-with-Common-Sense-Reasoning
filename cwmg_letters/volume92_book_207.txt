207. LETTER TO V. THARAI SWAMY PILLAI
WARDHA,
December 16, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I am sorry I have not been able to overtake your letter
earlier. My message is :
In all you do always think of the old country and her
starving millions.
Yours,
V. THARAI SWAMY PILLAI
'ALCOVE', TURRET ROAD
COLOMBO
From a copy : Kusumbehn Desai's Diary. S. N. 32579/25
