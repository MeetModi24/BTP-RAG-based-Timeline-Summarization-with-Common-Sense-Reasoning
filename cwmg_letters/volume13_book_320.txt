320. LETTER TO W. B. HEYCOCK
MOTIHARI,
May 24, 1917
DEAR MR. HEYCOCK,
I arrived here last night and hope to remain in Motihari up
to Sunday afternoon. I should be at your disposal during the time.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original in Gandhiji's hand in the National Archives of India;
also Select Documents on Mahatma Gandhi's Movement in Champaran, No. 96, p. 165
