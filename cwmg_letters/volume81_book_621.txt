621. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
October 12, 1945
CHI. RAMDAS,
Sumi's letter is at the back of this. So now there is no
question whether she should go or not. Yesterday Manilal and
Sushila left for Akola. Arun has stayed back. Valjibhai teaches him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
