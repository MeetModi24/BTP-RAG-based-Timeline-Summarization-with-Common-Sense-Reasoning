47. LETTER TO E. W. ARYANAYAKUM
CALCUTTA,
August 13, 1947
CHI. ARYANAYAKUM,
You will learn of the conditions here from the newspapers.
Today I want to write about the goshala.
There is a great uproar and the trouble is twofold; they
cannot run the goshala themselves nor will they let others do
so. And you have such a fiery temper that you cannot get
along with anyone.
Now tell me what the fact is.
You are all keeping well, I hope. I have no idea when if
ever I shall be able to return.
Blessings from
BAPU
