418. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
February 15, 1933
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
Your letter has reached me today after a long time. I
hope you received the special letter I wrote four days back. I
wrote it on learning of your fits. But your letter contains no men-
tion of the fits. I gave the details of my food, etc., in that letter.
Blessings to Kanta. Keep writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
