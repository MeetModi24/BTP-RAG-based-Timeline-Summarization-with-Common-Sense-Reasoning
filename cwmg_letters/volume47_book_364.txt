364. LETTER TO V. S. NARAYANA RAO
AHMEDABAD,
August 19, 1931
DEAR NARAYANA RAO,
I have your letter of 15th instant.
I had long correspondence with the Dewan Saheb but there
was nothing conclusive to report to you. I cannot therefore give
you any definite advice either. You must really therefore recall
all the arguments that you had with me, that is to say, so much
of them as you could assimilate, and then examine the circum-
stances there existing at the present moment and do as the inner
voice bids you. I cannot take the responsibility of giving a final
decision from this distance.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. V. S. NARAYANA RAO
SECRETARY, BANGALORE DT. CONGRESS COMMITTEE
BANGALORE CITY
