47. LETTER TO ISHWARLAL JOSHI
October 23, 1929
CHI. ISHWARLAL,
I have your letter. It will be enough if you give six months
to Delhi. I have told them to let you go, if you willingly agree,
only on the condition that Devdas teaches you English and so
arranges your work that you can spare time for study. I will send
the letter through Devdas himself. He is out of Delhi at present.
During the six months we will think out where to send you
after the period. We need not think about that just now. I hope
you understand that the question of consulting me or obtaining
my consent in addition arises only after the Secretary's permission
has been obtained.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9279. Courtesy: Ishwarlal Joshi
