107. LETTER TO VITHAL V. DASTANE
SEVAGRAM,
January 24, 1945
BHAI DASTANE,
I have gone through your programme. I like it and it has
my blessings. You must have received another letter of mine.
Blessings from
BAPU
DASTANE VAKIL
BHUSAVAL
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
