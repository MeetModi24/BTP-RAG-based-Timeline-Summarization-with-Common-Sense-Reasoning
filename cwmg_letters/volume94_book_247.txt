247. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
PANCHGANI,
July 25, 1944
CHI. MRIDU,
I have your letter. I have explained everything to Kher Saheb 1 .
Hence I am not writing a separate letter to you. Are you coming or
not? We are waiting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 11230. Courtesy: Sarabhai Foundation
