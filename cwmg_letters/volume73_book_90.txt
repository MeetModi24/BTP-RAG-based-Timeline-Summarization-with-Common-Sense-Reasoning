90. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
October 17, 1940
CHI. KANCHAN,
I have your letter. You will be fully restored to health
in a few days. Write to me regularly. Tell Sushilabehn every-
thing about yourself. Tell her also about all your worries.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8280. Also C.W. 7104. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
