166. LETTER TO M. K. PANDURANGA
January 30, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your exhaustive letter. I have handed your
book to a friend who is interested in food reform. As soon as
it comes back to me, I shall go through the chapters mentioned
by you.
I understand all that you and your wife have gone
through.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI M. K. PANDURANGA
BLISS CULT COLONY, AMBATTUR R. S.
VILLIVAKKAM P.O., MADRAS
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
