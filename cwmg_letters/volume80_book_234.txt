234. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS BIRLA
May 14, 1945
CHI. RAMESHWARDAS,
Read this and then reply if there is anything you want to
say. I have replied saying that I shall pursue the matter if he
sends me something to support such allegations.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RAMESHWARDAS BIRLA
BIRLA HOUSE
MOUNT PLEASANT ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
