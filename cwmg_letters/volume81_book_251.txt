251. LETTER TO VINA CHATTERJEE
August 22, 1945
CHI. VINA,
I often think of you. I hope you are well there. Now it is
obligatory for you to get well and remain well. I hope you are
cheerful. Sailen will be well. Abha is with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
