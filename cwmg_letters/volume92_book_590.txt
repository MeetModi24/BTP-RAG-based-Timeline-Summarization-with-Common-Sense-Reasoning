590. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
October 9, 1932
CHI. TARAMATI,
I have your letter. I have now regained sufficient strength.
I am writing and going for walks properly. I am also spinning
as before. Hence there is no reason to worry at all. I had
received a long letter from Mathuradas. He is keeping good
health. The letter was about the fast.
Blessings to all of you from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
