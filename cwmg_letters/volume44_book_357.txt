357. LETTER TO KUNVERJI MEHTA
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 26, 1930
BHAI KUNVERJI,
I have your letter. If you have not yet become a beloved
guest of the Government, you will get this letter. Lakshmidas
having gone, let me know what new arrangement is made. When
you meet the inmates of the temple1, give my blessings to them.
After all these years of work, people should be able to devise means
on their own.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2687
