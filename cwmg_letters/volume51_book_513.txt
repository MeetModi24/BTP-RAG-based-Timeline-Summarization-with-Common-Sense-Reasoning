513. LETTER TO PREMLILA THACKERSEY
Padavo, 1989 [October 30, 1932]
DEAR SISTER,
I am sure you got the letter which I wrote to you. And still
it seems you could not restrain yourself on the Diwali day. I got
your loving gift. The containers were returned. I hope you will
not take any trouble now. I will not fail to write to you when
I need anything.
All of us wish and pray that the New Year may bring you
prosperity and happiness, that your aspirations may be fulfilled
and your devotion to service may grow stronger.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4826. Courtesy: Premlila
Thackersey
