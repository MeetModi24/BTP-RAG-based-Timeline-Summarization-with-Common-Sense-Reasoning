598. LETTER TO KANTA
BOMBAY,
April 5, 1945
CHI. KANTA,
Just now I got the news that you have given birth to a son
and both of you are well. Both should keep well and you
should bring up the child in a way that will make him a true
servant.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
