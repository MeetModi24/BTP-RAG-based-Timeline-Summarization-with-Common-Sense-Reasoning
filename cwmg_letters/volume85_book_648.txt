648. LETTER TO BLIND STUDENTS
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
BLIND STUDENTS,
Thanks for the gift you have sent through Jayantilal on the
occasion of Charkha Jayanti. It will be used in the service of
Harijans.
Those who blindly drift along although they can see are
truly blind. Those who go along the straight path are not blind.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
