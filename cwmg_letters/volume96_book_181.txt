181. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
Tuesday [May 19, 1914]
MY DEAR LOWER HOUSE,
Please read the enclosed and return. I am much better today and
for the first time working at the Press. More later.
With love,
UPPER HOUSE
From the original: Gandhi-Kallenbach Correspondence. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
1 The enclosed letter from Emily Hobhouse is not available.
