61. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
October 6, 1940
CHI. MIRA,
I have your two letters.
I have made arrangements with
the Harijan Colony.
They will send you slivers when you need
them. If you will give notice in time I shall see to their being
sent. I hope what you have are quite good. Yes, I have been
to Simla. It has been a good experience. I hope you will get
the solitude you want.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI MIRABAI
PALAMPUR
KANGRA DISTRICT
PUNJAB
From the original: C.W. 6460. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 10055
