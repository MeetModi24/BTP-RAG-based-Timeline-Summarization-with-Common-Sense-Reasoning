565. LETTER TO LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
NEW DELHI,
October 10, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
It was good of you to send me your wishes for my birth-
day which is synonymous with the rebirth of the spinning-
wheel in 1918.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
LORD PETHICK-LAWRENCE
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government 1944-47, p. 217
