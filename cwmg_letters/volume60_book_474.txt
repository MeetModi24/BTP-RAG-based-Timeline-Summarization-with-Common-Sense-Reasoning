474. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM BAVISHI
March 28, 1935
BHAI PURUSROTTAM,
I have your letter. I have read the pamphlet you have sent
me. I liked it. Send me the book about your scheme. I shall reach
Indore on the 20th and stay there for four days. Will it be possi-
ble for you to see me during those days?
Keep on sending me anything which you feel would be
useful to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI P. L. BAVISHI
SHAMPUR (MALWA)
GWALIOR STATE
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 124. Also C.W. 4745. Courtesy:
Purushottam Bavishi
