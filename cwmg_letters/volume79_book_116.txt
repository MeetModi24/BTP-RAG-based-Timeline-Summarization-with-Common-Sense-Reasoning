116. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
SEVAGRAM,
January 25, 1945
CHI. SHRIMAN,
The love of you two is indescribable. I feel like coming to
you if only for the sake of that love. However I cannot leave this
place while the Camp is on. Silence is welcome for I can spare
myself. You can take it that I have resumed work. Nevertheless
I would like to come.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 303
