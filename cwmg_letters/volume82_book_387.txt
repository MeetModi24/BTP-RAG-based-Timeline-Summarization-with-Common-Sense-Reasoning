387. LETTER TO HOMI
MAHISHADAL,
December 26, 1945
BHAI HOMI,
I would advise you not to get involved in the Assembly
business. How does it matter if the people ask you to? More-
over, of what use can my testimonial be? For a noble person
his own noble acts or humanity are the testimonials.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
