461. LETTER TO DR. E. NUNDY
21-24 COURT CHAMBERS,
JOHANNESBURG,
September 26, 1906
[DR. EDWARD NUNDY
JACOB CHAMBERS
COURT ROAD
JOHANNESBURG ]
DEAR DR. NUNDY,
I hold that a Colony has a perfect right to regulate immigration even
as to British subjects, but not so as to make class distinctions.
You may make what use you like of this communication.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
Pretoria Archives : L. G. File No. 93 : Asiatics
