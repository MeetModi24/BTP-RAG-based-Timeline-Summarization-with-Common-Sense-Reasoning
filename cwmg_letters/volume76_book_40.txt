40. LETTER TO ANNAPURNA
April 13, 1942
CHI. ANNAPURNA,
You were punished as you deserved. Do not make such a
mistake again. You can do a lot of service even while resting.
What is necessary is the urge to serve. That you certainly have.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANNAPURNA
SWARAJYA ASHRAM
BARDOLI
TAPTI V. RLY.
B. B. & C. I. RLY.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9436
