140. LETTER TO DALJIT SINGH
August 7, 1945
BHAI DALJIT SINGHJI,
I have read both your books. I thank you for sending them.
Rajkumari passed them on to me.
In both you have talked of miracles. I give no importance
to miracles. Our religious literature is full of miracles. I think
it is necessary that you should give the Hindi or Gurmukhi
original which you have translated into English. How can one
study them without their originals?
M. K. GANDHI
STRAWBERRY HILLS
SIMLA 1
From a copy of the Hindi; G.N. 7905. Also C.W. 4273. Courtesy;
Amrit Kaur
