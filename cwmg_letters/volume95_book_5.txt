5. LETTER TO W P. SCHREINER
July 24, 1909
DEAR SIR1,
Mr. Hajee Habib, President of the Pretoria Anjuman-e-Islam
and I, as you may be aware, are in London as a deputation on behalf
of the Transvaal British Indians in connection with the struggle that
has now gone on in that Colony for the last two years and a half.
We have heard a great deal about your noble and self-sacrific-
ing work in connection with the welfare of the coloured races of South
Africa under the Draft South Africa Act.
If you would kindly appoint a time, my colleague and I would
like to make your acquaintance and pay our respects to you.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
M. K. GANDHI
THE HON. W. P. SCHREINER
TRAFALGAR SQUARE
MORLEY'S HOTEL, W.C.
From a photostat : BC. 112 file 12 (3.1). Courtesy: University Library,
Cape Town, South Africa
