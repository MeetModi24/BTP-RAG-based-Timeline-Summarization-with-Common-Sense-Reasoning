74. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
May 11, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
You seem to be unnecessarily worrying about your boy's
marriage. This is essentially a matter which you should let Father
and Mother manage in the best manner they can. It is a difficult
thing. But they seem to have got over every difficulty. You should
avoid all anxiety.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: Ambujammal Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
