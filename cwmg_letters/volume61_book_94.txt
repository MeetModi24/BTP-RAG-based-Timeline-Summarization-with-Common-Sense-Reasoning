94. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
May 15, 1935
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I didn't like the joke about crows in Delhi. Such jokes don't
benefit the people in any way. There is also no fight going on
just now.
I had a letter from Amritlal. I have replied and said that
it would be proper if he expressed regret in his newspaper.
The rest you will know from Mahadev's letter. I am extreme-
ly busy these days. Andrews is here. Ceresole and Wilkinson
arrived yesterday. Some others also are arriving. I shall barely
get ready by the 21st to leave. I don't think it will be possible to
bring Mahadev along with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, p. 167
