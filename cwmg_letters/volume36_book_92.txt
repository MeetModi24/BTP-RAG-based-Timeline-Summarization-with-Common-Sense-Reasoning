92. LETTER TO R. NORA BROCKWAY
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 5, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I was delighted to receive your letter and to find that Mr.
Brockway was doing so well. I hope the recovery has been pro-
gressively rapid.
Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru is here at the present moment and
I have shared your letter with him.
Yours sincerely,
MISS R. NORA BROCKWAY
ST. CHRISTOPHER'S TRAINING COLLEGE
KILPAUK
MADRAS
From a photostat: S.N. 13099
