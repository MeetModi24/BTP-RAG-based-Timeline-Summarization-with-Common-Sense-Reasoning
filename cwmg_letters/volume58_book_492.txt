492. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
September 12, 1934
God will restore you. Keep repeating the name of Him
whose das you are considered to be, that is, to whom your name
signifies that you have been dedicated. Know that Ramanama
is the only remedy that never fails. There is no remedy like peace
of mind. Who has ever escaped birth, death, old age and disease?
They are inseparable from the body. If, however, we preserve
equality of mind towards them, they will seem the same though
in fact different. . . .3
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy; Narayan Desai
