487. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
May 8, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
It is just now 2.25 a.m. The ink in the pen has run out.
Hence the pencil writing.
I hope you are at peace with yourself
now. You have evidently little faith in the ultimate rightness of
things or the victory of truth. If you have, you ought not to worry
as you do over my safety. If you will obey, you will go on with
your work without brooding over me or my doings. I am quite
well and happy. And so should you be. I hope the heat there
is not too trying for you.
Love.
BAPU
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
