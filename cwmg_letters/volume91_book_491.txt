491. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
THE ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
June 3, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I could not trace your previous letter.
If you will kindly write to me again putting the questions that
you did in your previous letters, I shall try to answer your
questions.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. ANAND T. HINGORANI
C/O SJT. GOBINDSING A. ISRANI
NEAR GHAR CANAL
LARKANA
SIND
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India and Anand T.
Hingorani
