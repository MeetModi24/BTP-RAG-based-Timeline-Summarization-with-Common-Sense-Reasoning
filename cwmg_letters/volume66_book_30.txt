30. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
August 8, 1937
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
All illness should be regarded as misconduct punishable under
the I. P. C.! I do hope you will recover soon. If you cannot come
I shall reconcile myself to the absence of your bulky contribution
to the debate.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10128
