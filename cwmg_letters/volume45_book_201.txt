201. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
February 2, 1931
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have no idea when I shall reach Calcutta. If at all I go there,
I cannot ignore Khadi Pratishthan. But it may not be possible to
stay there all the time. Tell me about Arun's health. I shall leave
for Bombay tomorrow or the day after.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1683
