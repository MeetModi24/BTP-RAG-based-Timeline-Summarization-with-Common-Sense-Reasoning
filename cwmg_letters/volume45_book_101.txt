101. LETTER TO HEMPRABHA DAS GUPTA
January 10, 1931
CHI. HEMPRABHA,
I have your two letters together. So far I have received no
letter from Birla about khadi. What is the harm in his taking
over charge of all the khadi stock at the Pratishthan? Let him
sell at any price. I want to understand all this. Do everything
with patience. It is for us to perform our duty, the result lies
in God's hands. I have no anxiety as you are already blessed
with patience.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1681
