216. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI
February 6, 1931
CHI. GANGABEHN JHAVERI,
I got your letter . You should remain firm. You should tell
the people very clearly that we are not fighting at present for
redress of individual hardships. This matter must be fully clari-
fied. We shall then know the weak from the strong. And that
is necessary.
Blessings from
BAPU
