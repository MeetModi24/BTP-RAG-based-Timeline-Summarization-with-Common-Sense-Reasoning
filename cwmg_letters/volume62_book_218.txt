218. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
[After January 22, 1936]
CHI. MIRA,
So you are laid low. I hope you will soon be well and
return to your post. You may certainly seek the solitude of the
hills. I am making steady progress in spite of the blood-pres-
sure. I think I have discovered the cause of the present increase.
But I shall know more on Wednesday. There is no cause for
anxiety. I am allowed plenty of exercise and solid food.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6308. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9774
