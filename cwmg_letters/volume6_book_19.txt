19. LETTER TO A. H. WEST
HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,
October 26, 1906
DEAR MR. WEST,
Ever since Saturday last I have not had breathing time, and except
for one night I have not been able to go to bed before one o'clock in
the morning. I have written a very long letter to Polak, and have
asked him to send it on to you for perusal. Please read it yourself and
show it to Chhaganlal. It will give you in full detail everything about
my movements. As this is being typed at 8.30 p.m. you will excuse me
for not giving you a long letter. I see that I shall be busy up to the
end of my stay here. Under the circumstances it is hardly possible to
cut myself away from London for full one day. I have, therefore,
asked Miss Pywell to see me in London by appointment, and have
offered, if you will let me, to defray her expenses. I am only hoping
that she will come. I have had a chat with Mr. Mukerji about his
contribution.
Yours sincerely,
A. H. WEST, ESQ.
Indian Opinion
PHOENIX
NATAL
