681. LETTER TO V. L. PHADKE
Monday [October 15, 1928]
BHAISHREE MAMA,
I was distressed to hear the news about Dalsukhbhai. Please
console his relatives on my behalf. I very well remember Dal-
sukhbhai's affectionate face. The void left by his passing can
never be filled. But we must all shoulder the responsibility. Tell
his relatives that those who knew Dalsukhbhai share their grief.
You have not given any address, so I send this letter through you.
Blessings from
BAPU
MAMA SAHEB
ANTYAJ ASHRAM
GODHRA
B.B. & C.I. RY.
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 32842
