300. LETTER TO SHRILAL BHATT
NEW DELHI,
September 3, 1946
BHAI SHRILAL,
I got your letter of the 29th on the 31st. The names of
Vithaldas and others are not to be found on the Exhibition
Committee. Have you consulted Jajuji and Kumarappa? I am
glad that you have stopped the sale of khadi. I am of the
250
opinion that products of other village industries ought to be
sold as usual. This covers the answer for you.
SHRILAL BHATT
GANESHBAG
DADISHETH AGYARI LINE
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
