88. LETTER TO SHRIMAN NARAYAN
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 1, 1945
CHI. SHRIMANNARAYAN,
The March issue of Humayun Kabir's India carries a review
of your book by Sikander Chaudhary. Read it.
I hope Madalasa is well.
The pen moved in Gujarati and I let it.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 305
