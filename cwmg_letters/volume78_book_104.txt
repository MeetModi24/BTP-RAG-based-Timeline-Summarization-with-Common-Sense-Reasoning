104. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR H. PAREKH
September 5, 1944
BHAI PRABHASHANKAR,
I have your postcard. Please bear in mind that it is for
you to win over Champa. You alone know how to do it.
Vandemataram from
M. K.
SHRI PRABHASHANKARBHAI
DERA SHERI
RAJKOT 2
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./XX
