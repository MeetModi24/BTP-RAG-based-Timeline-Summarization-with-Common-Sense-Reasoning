94. LETTER TO JOYADEVI
SEVAGRAM,
January 21, 1945
CHI. JOYADEVI,
I had promised to send you my blessings on the 1st but it
slipped my mind. The girl whom I had asked to remind me
did so only today. I hope you are all well.
Blessings from
BAPU
JOYADEVI
8 EXCHANGE PLACE
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
