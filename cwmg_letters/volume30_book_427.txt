427. LETTER TO SOMNATH
ASHRAM,
April 24, 1926
BHAI SOMNATH,
I have your letter. You have asked enough questions. May
I now request you to restrain yourself? Questions relating to the
Ramayana, the Mahabharata, idolatry, etc., are occasionally dis-
cussed in Navajivan. If you read those articles carefully, you will
find your questions answered.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19903
