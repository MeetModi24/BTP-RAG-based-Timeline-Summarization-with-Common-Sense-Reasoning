125. LETTER TO RISHABHDAS RANKA
SEVAGRAM,
May 26, 1941
BHAI RISHABHDAS,
I have your letter. I am pained. I had just finished your letter
when Girishwar came in. He came to give me some information
on behalf of the Nagpur Bank. From what he had to say and
from the auditor's report it seems you have made some mistakes.
If that is so you must humbly admit it. In the circumstances
I shall not send Kanubhai just now. When things have cleared
up there, you can come and complete the accounts.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
