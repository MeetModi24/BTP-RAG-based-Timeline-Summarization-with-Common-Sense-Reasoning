51. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
April 3, 1937
MY DEAR KU.,
I have seen both Bhagwandas and Shankerrao. I have
not yet reached the bottom. Shankerrao is not of the School.
He is Choudhury's find. Bhagwandas won't go just yet. He
assured me he won't go without my permission.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10116
