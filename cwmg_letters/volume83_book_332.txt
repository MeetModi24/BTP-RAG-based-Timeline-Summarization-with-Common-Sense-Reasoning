332. LETTER TO KEDAR
POONA,
March 18, 1946
BHAI KEDAR,
Shrimati Janakidevi has sent me your letter dated February
22, 1946, to her and also your article. I listened to everything
[being read out to me]. I do not agree with your view-point.
True, we can accomplish more after achieving independence, but
for neglect of cattle, we alone are responsible; the Government
has no hand in it. I am afraid, you have not thought over
it fully.
Thanks for sending the cheque for Rs. 500.
I am sending
it to Mirabehn as indicated by you.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI KEDAR
(INDIAN DAIRY CORPORATION, ROORKEE)
DEHRA DUN
