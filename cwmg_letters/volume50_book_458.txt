458. LETTER TO VIMALA JOSHI
August 27, 1932
CHI. VIMU,
Your handwriting is indeed very bad and yet I like your
letter. Now see that your eyes are cured soon. What is wrong
with them? It would be very good if you will now settle down
in the Ashram.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5311
