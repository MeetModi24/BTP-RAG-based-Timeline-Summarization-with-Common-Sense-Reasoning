499. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
May 8, 1932
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
Your letter smacks of a complaint that Narandas does not
listen to you. Please do not think so. Your duty ends when you
have informed [him]. You told him without expecting a res-
ponse, didn't you?
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 347. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
