22. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
March 7, 1930
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
After a talk with Sitla Sahai, I have decided to send him
there. Let him see what he can do there and you will watch
developments. If he and you decide that he should come back,
he may do so. His wife and children will remain here and he can
draw upon the Ashram for his bare upkeep. The rest you will hear
from him.
Yours,
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1930. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
