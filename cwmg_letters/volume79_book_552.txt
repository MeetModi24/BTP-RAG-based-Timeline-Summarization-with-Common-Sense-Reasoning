552. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 28, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Stay in the Ashram and do whatever work there is to do. I
am happy with the new man. You should not be upset by my
insistence. My insistence is really no insistence because I have
placed the entire responsibility on your shoulders.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4507
