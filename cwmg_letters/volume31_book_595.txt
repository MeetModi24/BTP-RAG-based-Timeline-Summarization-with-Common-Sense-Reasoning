595. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
Asvina Vad 13, 1982 [November 3, 1926]
BHAISHRI DAHYABHAI,
I have your letter. Do come and see me. I should have
replied earlier but I could not manage to reply yesterday while
on the train. Please forgive me.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
BHAISHRI DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
TALUKA SAMITI
DHOLKA
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2697. Courtesy: Dahyabhai M. Patel
1 A Congress worker and journalist of Saurashtra
