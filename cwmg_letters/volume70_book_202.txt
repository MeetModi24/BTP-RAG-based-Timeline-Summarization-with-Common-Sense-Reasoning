202. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 11, 1939
CHI. NARANDAS,
I note what you say regarding your birthday. Give the en-
closed letters to the persons concerned.
Soon you will have come to the end of your vanavasa . Time
is flying. Since you keep an account of every moment of your
time and are spending it well, you have no problem how to
pass it. You still have a lot of work to do. God will grant
you a long life for that.
It is enough that you have followed my point about the
Praveshika. I am in no great hurry about it.
Kanaiya is really keeping good health. He is always en-
grossed in some work or other. Pyarelal also entrusts a good
deal of work to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8561. Courtesy:
