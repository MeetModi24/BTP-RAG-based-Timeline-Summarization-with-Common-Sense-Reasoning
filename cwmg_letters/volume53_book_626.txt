626. LETTER TO P. N. SANKARANARAYANA AIYAR
March 4, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your postcard. I had your letter also with copy of
your report and pictures. These are lying in my file awaiting
disposal. When I reach them and if I have anything to say I
will certainly write to you.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. P. N. SANKARANARAYANA AIYAR
No. 1 3RD STREET
GOPALAPURAM
CATHEDRAL P. O., MADRAS
From a microfilm: S.N. 20457
