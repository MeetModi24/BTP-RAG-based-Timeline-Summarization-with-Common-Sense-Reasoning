755. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
May 24, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Ba has now arrived. She is fine. Devdas, Lakshmi and
Ramdas have come too. Today only this much. You will have
got over your first day of the examination by the time you receive
this.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
SOHANLAL COLLEGE
16 LAKE ROAD
LAHORE (PUNJAB)
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
