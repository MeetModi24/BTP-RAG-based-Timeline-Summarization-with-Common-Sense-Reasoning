63. LETTER TO MRS. BLAIR
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 28, 1927
DEAR MRS. BLAIR,
You are so good. Whenever there is
1, you never
fail to send it to me. I hope that those who have given their
names will persist to the end. Of course it is quite the proper
thing to spin wool in Darjeeling. The chief thing is to do hand-
spinning. I expect you some day at the Ashram. Of course I am
still convalescing, and have to do what little I can in the South.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. BLAIR
MALL VILLA 3
DARJEELING
From a microfilm: S.N. 14175
