313. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
December 26, 1940
CHI. AMRIT,
I have your promised wire. I was glad. Everything going
well.
You will know the contents of your parcel. Evidently it
was a conspiracy. Why should there be prunes? Do you know
that the bottles cost a lot in transit? But you will retort, 'Love
counts not the cost'.
Hitler letter not yet published.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3955. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7304
