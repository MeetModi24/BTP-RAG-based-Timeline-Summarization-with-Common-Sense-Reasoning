10. LETTER TO V. N. SOMASUNDARAM
March 7, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. The only thing I
can advise is that you should on the one hand persuade the tem-
ple trustees and on the other cultivate public opinion among the
present temple-goers.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. V. N. SOMASUNDARAM
43 SALGADO ST.
MUTWAL
COLOMBO
From a microfilm: S.N. 20485
