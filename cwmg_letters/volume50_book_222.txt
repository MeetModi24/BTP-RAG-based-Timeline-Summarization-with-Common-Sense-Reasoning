222. LETTER TO MADALASA BAJAJ
July 17, 1932
CHI. MADALASA1,
The word abhiman connotes an undesirable quality, where-
as swabhiman connotes a good one. If you think yourself an im-
portant person being a big man's daughter, you would be
abhimani . If, however, somebody tries to insult you but you are
not intimidated by him, you will be said to have preserved
your swabhiman or swaman. Why doesn't Om write?
Kamala of course, I must not expect to write.
Babu must be growing very fast now. Does he still love to
eat plenty of sweets?
Don't be lazy in writing to me. Ask Balkrishna to write
too.
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 313
