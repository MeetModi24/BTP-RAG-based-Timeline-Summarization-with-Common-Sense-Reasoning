332. LETTER TO BHATE
MUSSOORIE,
June 2, 1946
BHAI BHATE,
I have your letter. I had drawn but a vague likeness of
Bhai Datar; I cannot delineate it clearly. I have approved of
the amendments suggested and left the final decision to the
Doctor, because from this distance I do not want to interfere.
Besides, my knowledge is limited.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
