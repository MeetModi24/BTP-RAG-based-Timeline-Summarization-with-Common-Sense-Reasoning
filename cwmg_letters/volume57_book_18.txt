18. LETTER TO SHANTIKUMAR MORARJEE
January 20, 1934
CHI. SHANTIKUMAR,
I got your letter. You must have left the hospital now and
are probably able to walk about. I have come to such a distant
place that letters take four days to reach me.
I have heard about Dr. Mulgaonkar, but I don't know him
much personally. Please give him my compliments for wearing
khadi. Ask Gokibehn to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4722. Courtesy: Shantikumar
Morarjee
