704. LETTER TO CHHOTUBHAI SUTHAR
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 22, 1945
BHAI CHHOTUBHAI,
I have gone through what you had sent. It was Kakasaheb
who made me interested in Tarak Mandal. My interest continues
and so I have welcomed your activity. Abide by the pledge you
have published in the journal. May the activities of your Mandal
go on increasing. Keep on sending me your literature. I would
have sent the subscription if I were not a beggar.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI CHHOTUBHAI SUTHAR
TARAK MANDAL
THAKKAR BUILDING
ANAND
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
