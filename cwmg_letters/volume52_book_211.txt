211. LETTER TO DR. M. S. KELKAR
December 9, 1932
DEAR DR. ICE,
I have your letter. We must agree to differ about temples.
People are allowed freely to see me only when they have real
untouchability matters to discuss. To meet you in connection
with my health special permission has to be obtained. This is
quite unnecessary. I am being well looked after and am rapidly
regaining strength.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. M. S. KELKAR
172 AUNDH ROAD
KIRKEE, POONA
From a photostat: G.N. 6643
