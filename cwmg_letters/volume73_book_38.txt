38. LETTER TO JAMNALAL BAJAJ
On THE TRAIN,
September 25, 1940
CHI. JAMNALAL,
I read your account of the Jaipur incident only today. I sat
down to write about the matter in Harijan, but then decided
not to write just now. I felt that if I wrote, you would attract
all the more attention, and so gave up the idea. But if you
think that my writing will do any good, I am prepared to
write. How is your health and that of Rajendra Babu? I am
going to Simla. I will return to Sevagram on Sunday or Monday.
I hope the work there is progressing to your satisfaction.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3015
