481. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
June 14, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your letter. I understand your difficulties. God will
protect you. Don't worry, and stay on there. You may come
when you can. I have had to come to Borsad for some days.
But you should write to me at the Bardoli address, since I shall
probably have returned there by the time I get your reply.
It is not at all certain whether I shall be going to England.
Even if it is decided that I should go, I shall not have to leave
before August 15.
What is your present diet?
How is Father's health now?
Rajendra Babu was ill for some time. He is better now.
He will be here in a few days.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3414
