122. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
[Before November 12, 1935]
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I have your letter. I learnt only today from Narandas's letter
that you were running fever. Your letter came later.
It seems you have not properly understood the point of what
I have written to Jethalal. I did disapprove of Bharatan getting
Rs. 50. However, he had given up a post of Rs. 300 a month
and even now he can get a job which would bring him the same
income or even more. From the Rs. 50 which he takes, he gives
as much as half to a poor student whom he supports. Bhai Je-
thalal is looking for some other job or occupation and will leave
the work of the Seva Sangh as soon as he gets any. I had clearly
seen this. I hope you will be able to see the difference between
the two cases. Those of you who take more than Jethalal does
have dedicated yourselves to public work and your market value
is greater than what you are getting at present. That is the
difference. Even so, I do expect increasing self-sacrifice, within
your capacity, from you all, though I wouldn't feel disappo-
inted, either, if that expectation did not materialize.
Get well soon. We shall meet when it is so willed by God.
Blessings from
BAPU
