186. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
Monday, June 23, 1941
CHI. MIRA,
I have read the enclosed. It is a good collection. All these
verses have more meanings assigned to them than what you
have found. But for us the literal meaning is enough.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6481. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9876
