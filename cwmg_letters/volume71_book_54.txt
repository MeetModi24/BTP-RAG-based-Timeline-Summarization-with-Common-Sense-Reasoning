54. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
SEGAON, WARDHA,
December 16, 1939
CHI. KANCHAN,
I am replying to you by return of post. You will be able to go
wherever you want. But you can devote your time exclusively to
study only in Vithal Kanya Vidyalaya. Stay there as long as it is
necessary. Come over when you feel inclined. See that your
health does not suffer. My visit to Bardoli seems to be off for the
present.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8286.
Also C.W. 7066. Cour-
tesy: Munnalal G. Shah
