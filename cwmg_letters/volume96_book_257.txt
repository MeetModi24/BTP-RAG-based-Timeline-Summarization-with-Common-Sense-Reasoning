257. LETTER TO HERMANN KALLENBACH
FROM M. K. GANDHI
MOTIHARI,
April 17, 1917
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I am on one of the loveliest spots of the earth—very near the
Himalayas — and yet I cannot get a glimpse of them. Just now it
is 3 a.m. I have been writing the whole night. I have come here to
settle some labour troubles. The authorities do not want me. They have
asked me to leave. I do not go. I am therefore to be tried for contempt.
You will not hear from me now for some months, perhaps. I have
asked Chhaganlal to write to you. There is nothing from you.
With love,
Yours,
OLD FRIEND
