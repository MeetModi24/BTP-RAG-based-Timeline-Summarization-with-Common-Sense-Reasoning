314. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
[Before April 27, 1930]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. You are right that we should not have
any secret dealings. Let them get at the money any time they
choose.
I shall write to Taramati about her brother. Let us see what
happens about the mills.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum
and Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
