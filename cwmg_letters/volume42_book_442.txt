442. LETTER TO R. B. MOTWANI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 9, 1930
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
The problem you present should not cause any difficulty to
a real lover of his country. He will neither marry nor fall other-
wise. Love of the country should be sufficient to keep him un-
touched by base passions.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
R. B. MOTWANI
C/O SIND CENTRAL CO-OPERATIVE BANK, LTD.
KARACHI (SIND)
From a photostat: G.N. 2777
