249. LETTER TO NAJUKLAL AND MOTIBEHN CHOKSHI
September 30, 1932
CHI. NAJUKLAL AND MOTI,
I had got your postcard. God has given me a new life. He
will now lead me where He wills.
I hope all three of you are well. I am now regaining
strength.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12151
