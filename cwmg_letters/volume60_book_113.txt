113. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
HARIJAN HOME, DELHI,
January 21, 1935
DEAR SISTER,
I have sent in your application to Kumarappa. You shall
be your own judge as to the enforcement of rules 9 and 101
in your own person. What I expect you to do is not a visit
to every village in your district, but acquisition of a right
knowledge of the villagers' wants and organization of the effort
to meet them.
For this you are fully fitted in spite of your
dilapidated body of which we must talk when we meet.
I leave on 28th which is my silence day. Come if you
easily can between 26 and 27.
The cold spell has meant more than the loss of a crore of
rupees for the starving millions.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3520. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur
