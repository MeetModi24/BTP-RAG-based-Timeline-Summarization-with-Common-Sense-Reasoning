251. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
December 8, 1940
MY DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
I had your long letter. You will not overstrain yourself. Do
conserve your energy.
Amtul Salaam has told me your love towards her and how
you all rained torrents on her.
Your letter on the situation I understand. She is now under
treatment. I won't send her back till she is quite fit. But neither
she nor I will be happy till Sind is free from senseless murders.
Her heart is in Sind.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 9266. Courtesy: Jairamdas Doulatram
