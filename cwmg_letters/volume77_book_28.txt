28. LETTER TO ARDESHIR E. KATELI
May 26, 1943
BHAI KHAN BAHADUR,
I gave you on the 4th a letter addressed to the Central
Government and along with it was a letter to Qaid-e-Azam
Jinnah. Another I gave you on the 15th. With that there
was a letter to Lord Samuel. Please be good enough to inquire
and let me know whether these two letters have reached the
proper quarters, and whether the letters to Qaid-e-Azam and
Lord Samuel have been forwarded to them.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 6302
