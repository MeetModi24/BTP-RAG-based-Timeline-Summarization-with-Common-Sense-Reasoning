382. LETTER TO DHARMANANDA KOSAMBI
May 5, 1947
Your postcard. Death is our true and unfailing friend. He
takes charge of one when one's time is over. So, if you must
depart, first enshrine Rama in your heart and then go to meet
Him cheerfully. Where is . . .3
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Biharni Komi Agman, p. 297
