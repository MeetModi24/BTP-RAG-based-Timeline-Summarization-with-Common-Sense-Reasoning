323. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
August 2, 1941
CHI. NARANDAS,
Kanaiyo is terribly upset. Do console him, please. I need
him badly here but if he wishes to stay on there or if you
feel the need to keep him there, you need not worry about me.
I have always regarded it as my dharma to take whatever work
I can from those persons who feel the need to be near me. Any
other course would not be compatible with scrupulous adherence
to truth. Realization of God also is impossible in any other way.
The renunciation of grihasthashrama is inspired by the same ideal.
It follows, therefore, that it is my duty to think of Kanaiyo's
welfare and take into consideration what will suit you. The rest
Kanaiyo will tell you.
Blessings from
BAPU
