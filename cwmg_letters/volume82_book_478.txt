478. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
SODEPUR,
January 5, 1946
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I sent Rs. 10,000 to Dinshawji yesterday. You will be able
to meet from it all expenditure from this month up to the time
I return.
Kanchan had an attack of cold and fever. But there is nothing
to worry about. She is being treated by Sushilabehn.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8612. Also C.W. 7201. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
