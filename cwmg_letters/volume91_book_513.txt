513. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SABARMATI, ASHRAM,
Shravan Sud 7, 1982, August 15, 1926
CHI. TARAMATI,
Though at last after many days I have your letter there is
nothing in it. You have not even said what Rohit is doing and
how he is getting on. Do you go out for walks there? You must
walk a lot and do physical exercise and build up your health.
You must also read a little.
Blessings from
BAPU
