182. LETTER TO MAHESH DUTT MISHRA
November 27, 1945
CHI. MAHESH,
I have your postcard. I never thought that you were good
for nothing. Do write to me when you are on leave. I can
send for you if it is convenient.
My address: Khadi Pratishthan, Sodepur, 24 Parganas.
Blessings from
BAPU
MAHESH DUTT
9 HAMILTON ROAD
ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
