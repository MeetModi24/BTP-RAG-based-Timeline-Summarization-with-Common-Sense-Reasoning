483. LETTER TO HASMUKH
SODEPUR,
January 5, 1946
BHAI HASMUKHBHAI,
I have your long letter. What is there for me to bless in
what you write? I have not even understood the dispute fully.
Besides, if the work is noble it does not need blessings from any
man, however great. The work is its own blessing.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
1 & 2 The letters are in Devanagari script.
