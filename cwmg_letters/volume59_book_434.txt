434. LETTER TO S. SATYAMURTI
[Before November 21, 1934]
Your suggestion appeals to me, but am I now any good as
an adviser in these matters? My mind is shunted off these things.
You have Sjt. Rajagopalachariar nearby. Then you have the Pre-
sident, and Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel who is as good. Then, of
course, Dr. Ansari too is there. Don't misunderstand. I attach
great importance to these. But I am not a man just now. Do
save me from having to tax my mind about such political affairs.
The Bombay Chronicle, 22-11-1934
