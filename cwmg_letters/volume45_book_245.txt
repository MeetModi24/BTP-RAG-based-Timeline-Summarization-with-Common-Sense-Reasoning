245. LETTER TO TARA MODI
February 13, 1931
CHI. TARA,
I got your letter. I do not understand the complaint against
Mithubehn. Whatever the truth in it, our only aim is to serve.
We neither desire nor ask for any reward. The reward of service
is greater zeal for service. You need not get frightened by any
false charge. I take it you got the clothes, etc. You should conti-
nue to write to me. Take care of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4171
