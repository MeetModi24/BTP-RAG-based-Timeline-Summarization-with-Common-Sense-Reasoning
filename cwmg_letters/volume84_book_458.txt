458. LETTER TO DINANATH
NEW DELHI,
June 25, 1946
BHAI DINANATH,
I shall never forget the services you all rendered me and my
companions with such single-minded devotion regardless of whether
it was day or night. God will reward you all for it.
Blessings from
BAPU
