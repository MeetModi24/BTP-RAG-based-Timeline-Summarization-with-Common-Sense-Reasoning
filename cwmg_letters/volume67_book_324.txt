324. LETTER TO RADHAKRISHNA BAJAJ
August 7, 1938
CHI. RADHAKRISHNA,
I shall want the motor-car tomorrow, Monday, at 12 o'clock,
if it is not raining. Balkrishna is to be taken to Nagpur.
It was my fault that the car was kept here the whole day
yesterday. I was sorry Kishorelal did not turn up yesterday.
My heart cried and memory failed. What am I to do?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 9123
