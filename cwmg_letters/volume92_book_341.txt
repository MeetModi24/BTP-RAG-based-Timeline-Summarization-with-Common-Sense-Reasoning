341. LETTER TO ANAND T. HINGORANI
Y.M.,
August 23, 1930
MY DEAR ANAND,
I have your delightful letter. You do not tell me how you
fared physically. I have already written to you. I am glad
Vidya is with you and has seen a little of the Ashram life. Tell
me how you are in mind and body.
Love.
BAPU
From a microfilm. Courtesy : National Archives of India and Anand
T. Hingorani
