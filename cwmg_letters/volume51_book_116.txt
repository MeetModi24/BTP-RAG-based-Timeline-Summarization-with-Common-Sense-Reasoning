116. LETTER TO HANSA MEHTA
September 18, 1932
You or the Doctor need not be agitated by my decision to
fast. On the contrary, you should rejoice that God has granted
to a co-worker a holy opportunity like this. Such an opportu-
nity comes rarely to anybody. Moreover, God will keep me
alive if He wants this body to do more service still. If, on the
contrary, my time is up, I shall not live even though I may
be eating as usual.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. I I , pp. 47-
