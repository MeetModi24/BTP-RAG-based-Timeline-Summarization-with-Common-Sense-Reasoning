210. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
July 4, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
What a heavy mail today! You must have received the mater-
ial sent earlier with Kaka.
I hope you have sent the wire to
Kanti. My stars seem to be unfavourable. But if one knows
how to offer up misfortunes also to the Lord, even unfavourable
stars become favourable. I have yet to learn that. Two art-
icles are enclosed. Giri's letter is there. Kanaiyo is going there
for it. About the rest in the next letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11602
