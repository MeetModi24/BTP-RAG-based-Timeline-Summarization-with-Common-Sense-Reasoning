586. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
[After September 12, 1935]
DEAR DAUGHTER,
All your efforts are in vain until you have been able to win
over Kanam.
I don't think it will affect me, if anyone touches my feet.
But I want to put down this idolatry. I do not regard myself
so pure.
I see no benefit in cutting off the hair but you can certainly
have it trimmed by someone if it comes in the way of your
doing your work.
I do not accuse you of harbouring any impurity. There can
be no fear on my part if you find nothing wrong with yourself.
It is a matter of your own choice whether or no to go to Kan-
pur or any other place.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 601
