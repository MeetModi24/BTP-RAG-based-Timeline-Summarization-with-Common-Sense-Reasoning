84. LETTER TO KAMALA BENKEL
SEVAGRAM,
January 21, 1945
MY DEAR KAMALA,
I see you are taking a further course. I am satisfied so
long as you fit yourself for full service and settle down.
I did see Donald and he knew all about me.
Hope you will keep well.
Love.
BAPU
KAMALABAI BENKEL
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
