518. LETTER TO SECRETARY, HINDI
SAHITYA SAMMELAN
April 3, 1935
SECRETARY
ALL- INDIA HINDI SAHITYA SAMMELAN
INDORE
DEAR SIR,
I was under the impression that those arranging the Samme-
lan and the exhibition were largely the same people. The peo-
ple who had approached me in connection with the Sammelan
had talked about the exhibition. I, therefore, gave my consent for
another speech for the exhibition and that in the same letter
which dealt with the Sammelan matters.
Inform me of the train I should board on the 19th and
how I should reach there.
M. K. GANDHI
[From Hindi]
Veena, Tributes Issue, April-May 1969
