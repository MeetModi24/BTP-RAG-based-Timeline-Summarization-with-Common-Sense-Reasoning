289. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
Silence Day, December 16, 1940
DAUGHTER,
If you wish to act on principles, where is the room for
service to me? In that case, all your time should be devoted
to the service of Shastriji. I do everything in the capacity of
a father, not of a mother, although I aspire to be a mother. The
status of a father sets its own restrictions on a man.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 462
