273. LETTER TO LEON POLAK
July 26, 1932
MY DEAR LEON,
I am glad you wrote to me about your engagement to Marie
Lindrum. May you both have many many years of happiness and
service.
My love to you both.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Bombay Secret Abstracts, Home Department, Special Branch, File No.
800(40) (3), Pt. 11, p. 453
