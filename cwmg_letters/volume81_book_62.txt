62. LETTER TO PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
July 26, 1945
DEAR PATTABHI 2 ,
Pyarelal has asked you about the accuracy of your reported
speech . Now comes the enclosed from the angry worker who
takes me to task about palmyra trees. Did I ever say about pal-
myra trees [anything] of the kind reported to have been said by me?
I have much to say about the other parts. But I forbear pending
your reply. Hope you are well.
Yours,
BAPU
MACHHALIPATTAM
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
