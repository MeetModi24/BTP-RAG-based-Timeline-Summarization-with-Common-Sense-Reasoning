134. LETTER TO MRS. BARNES
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 8, 1906
DEAR MRS. BARNES,
If this letter finds you, I know it will surprise you. If you are staying
in Westbourne Park Road kindly drop me a line and I shall make it a
point to see you before I leave for Johannesburg, where I have been
living now for some years.
Yours sincerely,
MRS. BARNES
36, WESTBOURNE PARK ROAD, W.
From the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4518
