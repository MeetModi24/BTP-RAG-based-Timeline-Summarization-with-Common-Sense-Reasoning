416. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
September 28, 1936
CHI. NARANDAS,
Herewith the message. Tell Mother that I am longing as
a child to rest my head in her lap and Father's. It is unthink-
able that I should go to Ahmedabad and not see them. So take
it that, God willing, I shall be there. The date will be around
27th or thereabouts.
1& Devkunwar and Khushalchand Gandhi
Your purpose could be met if someone reaches there between
the 2nd and the 12th. I am considering whom I can send. You
may send me a wire if you have some other names in your mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8506. Court-
esy: Narandas Gandhi
