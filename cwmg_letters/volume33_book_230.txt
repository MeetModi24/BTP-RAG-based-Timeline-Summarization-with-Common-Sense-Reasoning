230. LETTER TO NANALAL KAVI
April 7, 1927
The poet says that Damayanti though innocent, was charged
with stealing. It was her bad luck. Something like it has hap-
pened to me. I took pains and fondly wrote out a harmless little
letter praising you, which you did not like. Every morning I
ask forgiveness of God even for any unconscious faults. Won't
you please pardon me this unintentional slip, for which I apolo-
gize to you? Unless you forgive me I will not stop begging
pardon.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan
Desai
