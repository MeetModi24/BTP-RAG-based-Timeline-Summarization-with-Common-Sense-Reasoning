43. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
October 15, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your well-written Hindi letter. You must get rid of
your fever altogether. How I wish you were here! Just now the
weather is very good. But I suppose that must not be.
The month of January I shall have to give to Gujarat.
February will probably be devoted to Delhi. It is not so cold
there at that time.
Vasumati is due here this week. Amtul Salaam and Prabha-
vati are here and so is Ba. Devdas has now returned to Delhi.
He still remains depressed.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Did I ask to send me your recipe for rasam ? If not, I do so
now. You may send me medical opinion on the quality of rasam.
