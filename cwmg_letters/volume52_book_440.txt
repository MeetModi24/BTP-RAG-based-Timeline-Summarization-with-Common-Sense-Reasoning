440. LETTER TO KESHAV GANDHI
January 1, 1933
CHI. KESHAV (JUNIOR),
I got your letter . You should regard all children as your
brothers and help them as much as you can. There is only one
friend, God. He never forsakes us and He fulfils all our aspirations.
Why, then, do we need any other friend?
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3285
