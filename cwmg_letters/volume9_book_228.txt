228. LETTER TO LORD AMPTHILL
[LONDON,]
August 24, 1909
MY LORD,
I beg to enclose copy of letter I have sent to Lord Crewe regard-
ing a cablegram just received from Bombay.
The letter will speak for itself, but I should like to add that these
deportations are becoming more and more serious and unreasonable.
Mr. Polak, who is the sender of the cablegram, reports in his letter receiv-
ed to-day that he is being guided by the acting editor of The Times of
India, Sir Pherozeshah Mehta, and other leading men.
I remain, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy signed by Gandhiji: S. N. 5026
