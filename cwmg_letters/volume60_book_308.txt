308. LETTER TO SUSHIL KUMAR SEN
February 15, 1935
DEAR FRIEND,
I had your respected father's letter. I was distressed to learn
of his illness. I hope he will soon recover. Then you send your
considered opinion on my questionnaire I would like you to quote
the Ayurvedic text in support of your statement. I am aware of
the modern scientists' opinion on the comparative qualities of dif-
ferent milks.
KAVIRAJ SUSHIL KUMAR SEN 2
KALPATARU PALACE
CHITTARANJAN AVENUE NORTH, CALCUTTA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
