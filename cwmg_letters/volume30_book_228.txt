228. LETTER TO ABDUL HUSAIN
ASHRAM,
March 31, 1926
BHAISHRI ABDUL HUSAIN,
I have your letter.
You can yourself resolve the fix which
you have got into. If you feel it your dharma to give up meat-
eating you should firmly resist your mother's pressure. If the
giving up of meat-eating is only an experiment, it would be
wrong to hurt your mother for this.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19885
