3. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
January 1, 1945
CHI. RAJKUMARI,
This is merely in answer to yours received at 4 p.m. after my
3 postcards had gone. This therefore though written today will
be posted probably tomorrow.
Yes, a happy New Year to you, such happiness as you can
derive from within. Happiness without there is none so far as I
can see.
I have your two dhotis. I am wearing them, too. They are
fine. But the real fineness comes from the knowledge that the
handiwork is yours so far as yarn is concerned.
Yours with love
BAPU
From the original; C.W. 3694. Courtesy; Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6503
