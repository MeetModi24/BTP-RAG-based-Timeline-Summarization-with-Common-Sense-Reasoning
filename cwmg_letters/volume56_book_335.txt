335. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
December 8, 1933
CHI. MARY,
We pass through Betul on 15th. I am quite sure that if I
carried on a propaganda for selling puff powder you will find me
deserted and pieless.
You are right about sword practice.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat : G.N. 6016. Also C.W. 3342. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
