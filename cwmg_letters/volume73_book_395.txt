395. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM, via WARDHA,
February 1, 1941
DAUGHTER,
A telegram has been sent to you. What do you expect
from me? You may not make use of my name for anything. You
have not understood at all. So long as you are not quite healthy,
you will not be able to do anything thoroughly. Just leave
everything for the present. Get well first. Attend to other things
later.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 470
