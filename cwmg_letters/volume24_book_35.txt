35. LETTER TO NARASINHRAO BHOLANATH DIVETIA
Vaisakha Sud 13 [May 17, 1924]
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I have your letter. I am relieved to hear that you are not
offended. But I have already offered an apology in Navajivan; it
must have been printed by now.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[From Gujarati]
Narasinhraoni Rojnishi, p. 43
