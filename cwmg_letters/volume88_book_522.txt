522. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
ON THE TRAIN,
4.30 a. m., July 31, 1947
CHI. MANI,
Read the enclosed' and do what is necessary. Your stead-
fast devotion to Father has given you a magnificent opportunity
to serve. Utilize it as you wish.
Is there any truth in the letter I wrote about the Khaksars?
They have now written to me in detail. Pass on the enclosed
to Rajkumari.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANIBEHN
C/O SARDAR PATEL
1 AURANGZEB ROAD
NEW DELHI
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 139
