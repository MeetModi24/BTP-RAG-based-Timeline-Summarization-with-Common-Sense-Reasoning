297. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
SEVAGRAM,
October 23, 1944
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I gather from Dilip's letter to Swami that your health has
deteriorated once again. It says you have had influenza and so
forth. What is this ? Why has it happened ? I want detailed
information. If you cannot write, let Dilip do so. I am deeply immersed
in work.
Dilip must be quiet.
Blessings from
BAPU
