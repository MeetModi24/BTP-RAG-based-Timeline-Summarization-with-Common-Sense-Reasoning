105. LETTER TO DHARMAPRAKASH
November 2, 1941
BHAI DHARMAPRAKASH,
I have your letter. The depressed classes question is very com-
plicated. It transcends politics. If enough money cannot be raised
for our conference it should be postponed. How much can you get
with my help? It will be more befitting if you make do with what-
ever you can collect on your own. You must also have a clear
aim in view. Remember that Dr. Ambedkar has placed a definite
aim before himself. Study it. Digest it. And also understand fully
what your own is.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
