27. LETTER TO RAMACHANDRA
March 9, 1933
MY DEAR RAMACHANDRA,
Here is a letter which I would like you to read and hand it
to N.
I would like you also to give me the full name and address of
R. of the Engineering College. I understand that he has been her
chief companion in connection with the scavenging work.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm : S.N. 20503
