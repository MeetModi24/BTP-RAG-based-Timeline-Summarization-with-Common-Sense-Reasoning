483. LETTER TO KANTI GANDHI
[After March 10, 1942]
CHI. KANTI,
I am just not able to write to you. You do understand the
pressure on my time. I am trying to detain Saraswati. At one
time she had agreed to work in a hospital. But now she says she
is uncertain, and that I should not depend on her. She is as
simple as she was.
Shanti mixes with all well. He is as full of
pranks as you were. His face too resembles yours. Like an old
friend he has taken possession of my bed. He likes jaggery as much
as you like it. On the whole he is all right.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Chhaganlal Gandhi Papers. Courtesy:
Gandhi Smarak Sangrahalaya, Ahmedabad
