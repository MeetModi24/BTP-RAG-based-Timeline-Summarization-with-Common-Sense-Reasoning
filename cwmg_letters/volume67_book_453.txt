453. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
September 7, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
You may begin in the morning with juice of fruits-either
mosambi or grapes. Increase the quantity of milk by 8 oz. Take
less rice. Eat more wheat instead. You do drink enough water.
Stop walking too much, but do continue pacing about for two
hours slowly and at uniform speed. The hour for retiring at
night must be strictly observed. Do not worry if any work is left
unfinished. A few things can be got done here. More in the
next letter. Do not put yourself to any inconvenience for coming
today.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11641
