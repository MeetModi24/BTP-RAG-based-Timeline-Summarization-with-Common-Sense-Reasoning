772. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
[July 13, 1941]
CHI. AMALA,
Mahadev is quite well. He has gone to Gwalior. Today
he is in Delhi. He may be back tomorrow. The day after
for certain.
You must get rid of your illness. Of course, you will drop
in here when you can and wish.
Love.
BAPU
