29. LETTER TO COLONIAL SECRETARY
[JOHANNESBURG,]
June 18, 1907
THE HON'BLE THE COLONIAL SECRETARY
PRETORIA
SIR,
The Acting Secretary to the Right Hon'ble the Prime Minister
informs me that my letter of the 12th instant on the question of the Asiatic
Registration Act has been transferred to your Department.
My Association ventures to hope that the matter therein referred
to will receive your favourable consideration.
I have, etc.,
Essop ISMAIL MIA
ACTING CHAIRMAN,
BRITISH INDIAN ASSOCIATION
Indian Opinion, 22-6-1907
