544. LETTER TO CHANDRANI
October 2, 1945
CHI. CHAND,
I have your letter after a long interval. I don't like your
not being well. You don't have to study at the cost of your
health. Satyavati is just the same.
DAGA MEMORIAL HOSPITAL
NAGPUR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
