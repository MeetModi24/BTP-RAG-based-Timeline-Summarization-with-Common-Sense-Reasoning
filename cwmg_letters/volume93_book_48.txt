48. LETTER TO RAJARAM R. BHOLE
WARDHA,
June 26, 1935
MY DEAR RAJARAM,
Though it is fairly cool now it is nothing like Poona yet. If
you come after the first week of July, it would be pleasant. But
you are at liberty to come earlier if you wish to.
Yours,
BAPU
SJT. RAJARAM BHOLE
C/O MINERVA TALKIES
POONA CITY
From the original: R. R. Bhole Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
