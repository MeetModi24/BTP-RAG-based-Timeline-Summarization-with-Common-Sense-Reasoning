198. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
Monday [1926]
CHI. MANI,
I got your letter. You did well to go to Ahmedabad city
at the time of uncle's (Vithalbhai's) visit.
Manu and Manilal can be brought round only with
patience.
Ba again said she would start on Sunday. She must reach
[Sabarmati] on Wednesday at the latest.
I am writing this just before retiring to bed. So I will not
write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne, p. 41
