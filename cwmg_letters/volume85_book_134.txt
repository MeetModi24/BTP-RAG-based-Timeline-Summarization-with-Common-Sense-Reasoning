134. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
August 7, 1946
CHI. MANILAL,
I arrived here yesterday. Sushila, Ila and Su's two friends
joined me at Akola. I read your letter. We had a long talk.
I approved of your idea and therefore I stated categorically that
it was Sushila's duty to stay here and look after the children
till you sent for her. Her own desire is to help you and join in
the struggle, but her duty is to do as you wish. I assume that
whenever you feel that Sushila should return there, you will
unhesitatingly write or cable. Sushila is returning to Akola today.
She will stay for a day at Mahila Ashram. The rest Sushila will
write, or Kishorelal at any rate. He keeps indifferent health.
Blessings from
BAPU
