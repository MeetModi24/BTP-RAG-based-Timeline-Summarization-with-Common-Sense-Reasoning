431. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
May 6, 1936
DEAR DAUGHTER AMTUSSALAAM,
I have your letter. What shall I write to you? I have a
telegram from Dr. Ansari; you must follow his instructions. I
wanted to take you to Bangalore with me, but let us now see
how things take shape. You shall follow the treatment unques-
tioningly. The first thing is to get well. After that we shall see
what we can do.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Urdu: G.N. 334
