164. LETTER TO ABBAS TYABJI
October 26, 1933
MY DEAR OLD BEARD,
Your letter about Kamala does credit to you. As I have to
advise her, I was bound to know the reasons for your opinion.
You have given me the straightest opinion. Raihana holds the
contrary opinion. Yours is based largely on instinct and so is
Raihana's. My instinct too has been against her. But I have
refused to act on my instinct in the face of her emphatic contra-
dictions. Of course I brought my own suspicions to her notice.
I hope you had a successful time in Amreli. Did it do much
good? Did you sell much khadi? Were you able to do some
substantial Harijan work?
Love to you all.
Yours,
BHRRR
From a photostat: S.N. 9588
