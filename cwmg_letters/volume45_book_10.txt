10. LETTER TO RADHABAI OAK
December 19, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
You must have got my message. I was happy to learn that
you are working hard in the field of public service. But it seems
you fight shy of khadi saris. How can you serve the poor if you
feel averse to the sacred products of their labour? Think over this.
Vandemaiaram from
MOHANDAS
SMT. RADHABAI OAK
C/O SUSHILA
