277. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
RAJKOT,
May 15, 1939
Nanabhai had given me news of you. I am glad that you
have gone there and that Manubhai is with you. Do come here
on your way back. But don't be impatient. Ba is not here at
present.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7111. Also C.W. 4603. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
