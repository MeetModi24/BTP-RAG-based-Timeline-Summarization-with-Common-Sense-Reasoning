485. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
March 21, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
Why should you have even a piece of wood under the
head? Sleep in shavasana as I do. If you must have something,
have a stone or a brick.
Do not give up carding and spinning. This one thing has
become part of our life. Devote one hour regularly to this. You
will acquire speed.
Why should you feel disappointed if you have to give up
Nayee Talim? But some work has to be taken up.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi ; G.N. 4487
