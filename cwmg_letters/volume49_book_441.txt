441. LETTER TO TARAMATI M. TRIKUMJI
April 24, 1932
. . .1 How is your own health? Do you meet anybody?
You should take up some work of public service which is within
your capacity. Occupying oneself with such work helps very
much in keeping the mind calm. Serving one's neighbours also
does that. There is none in this world who would never need
the help of another.
[PS.]
If you wish to come and see me, you may.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 109
