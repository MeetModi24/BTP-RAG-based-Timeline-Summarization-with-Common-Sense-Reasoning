256. LETTER TO L. GIBARTI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
April 3, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter for which I thank you. I appreciate the
sentiment underlying your proposal.
But I consider myself
wholly unfit to shoulder the burden. My methods too of serving
the oppressed people are so different from those generally accepted
by Socialists and they are yet in the nature of an experiment. I
therefore fight shy of belonging to any organization that I do not
know thoroughly and that I cannot serve usefully.
Yours sincerely,
L. GIBARTI, ESQ.
SECRETARY
INDIAN DEPT.
LIGAGEGEN KOLONIALGREUEL UND
UNTERDRUCKUNG
BAMBERGER STR. 60
BERLIN, W, 50
