63. LETTER TO DR. B. PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
October 27, 1946
BHAI PATTABHI,
It would be well if you issued a statement to the effect
that the attack on [the policy of] not having any new mills
in Madras was not proper.
I am going to Bengal.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
