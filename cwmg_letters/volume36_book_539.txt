539. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ASHRAM,
[June 30, 1928]
I feel that the Government must come to terms as regards
Bardoli. . . .2 met me. He had certainly promised that he would
take no step that would weaken our demand. I do not know
what is going on now. But does not satyagraha serve mainly as
a test of people's courage, firmness and peaceableness?
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 93
