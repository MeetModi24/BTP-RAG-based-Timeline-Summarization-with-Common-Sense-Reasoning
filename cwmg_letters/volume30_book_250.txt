250. LETTER TO A. JOSEPH
April 2, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter enclosing copies of your letters to The
Hindu and to Mr. Satyamurti. I have also copies of The Hindu
containing the advertisements referred to by you. I am entire-
ly of opinion that advertisements about things injurious to the
nation should not be taken by public journals at all but it is very
difficult for me to interfere in such matters. I can only express my
own opinion through the columns of Young India which I do from
time to time. As you might have noticed, I had occasion to refer
to this matter of immoral advertisements only recently.
Yours sincerely,
A. JOSEPH, ESQ.
519, SILVER STREET
ST. THOMAS MOUNT
MADRAS
