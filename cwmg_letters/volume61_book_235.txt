235. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
WARDHA,
June 15, 1935
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I have your letter.
I hope that Kichi is out of fever. What is the matter with
him? When you are not there, with whom does he stay? How
does he pass his time? Does he interest himself in any thing or
person?
Kamala has written to me. How nice it would be if you
could come with her. But I know you must not leave Father or
Kichi just now.
Yes the Quetta disaster is awful. You should ponder over
what I have written thereon.
Prabha is with her father. She returns in July. Amtussalaam
is here.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: C.W. 9601. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
