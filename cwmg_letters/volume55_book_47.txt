47. LETTER TO J. D. JENKINS
April 27, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for an inscribed copy of Jeans's The Stars in Their
Courses. I had a glance at it before, but of course I shall prize
the book you have sent me. I see it is interspersed with marginal
pencil lines. The passage that you have marked at page 152 had,
when I first read the book, made a good impression on me.
As I was finishing your letter and leaving aside the book for
the moment, I saw that the copy comes out of your own collection.
I shall prize the book all the more for that knowledge.
Yours sincerely,
J. D. JENKINS, ESQ.
HAMMERTON HOUSE
23 KAHUN ROAD
POONA
From a photostat: S.N. 19057
