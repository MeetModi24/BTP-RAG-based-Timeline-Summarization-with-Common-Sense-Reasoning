75. LETTER TO L. F. MORSHEAD
MOTIHARI,
January 15, 1918
TO
L. F. MORSHEAD
COMMISSIONER
TIRHUT DIVISION
BIHAR
DEAR MR. MORSHEAD,
I have your letter of the 14th instant. I have now carefully
gone through the Bill. I see that I must revise the view that I
took of Mr. Kennedy's amendment in my conversation with you.
I fear that his amendment will not meet the case if it is to cover
the whole of section 3. I can accept Amendment marked A in
place of clause 2, section 3. Mr. Kennedy's proviso marked B by
you is wholly unacceptable. Clause 1 of section 3 is necessary
for the repeal of contractual tinkathia 3 . Section 5 subject to the
amendment suggested by me in my letter to the Government, dated
19th December, is necessary to give effect to the other recommen-
dation of the Committee beyond recognition of khuski contracts.
My position is clear. I would consider pledging of a tenant's land
for the growing of particular crops as a revival of tinkathia.
Mr. Kennedy's effort, if I have understood him correctly, is devoted
to securing such pledging. Between these two extremes there is
no meeting ground.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
