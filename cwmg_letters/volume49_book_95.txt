95. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
February 5, 1932
CHI. MIRA,
After all I have an excuse for writing to you again.
Do you know what happened to the toys that Doris's child-
ren had sent me and which I had asked you to keep for our
children? If you know where they are and if they can be easily
traced, let the children have them.
Another thing is about the case containing books. Do you
know if Pyarelal or Mahadev sent the case to the Ashram or the
Vidyapith? Perhaps Manilal knows. If you have no time to think
of this, much less to attend to it, do not bother.
I hope you got my letter directly that I wrote to you yesterday.
This though being written today (Friday) will be posted only on
Tuesday.
Remember, please, that you will share with the Ashram the
letters. I may write to you directly.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6215. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9681
