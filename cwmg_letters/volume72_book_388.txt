388. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
August 1, 1940
CHI. VIJAYA,
You are crazy. They have triumphed that are dead. Why
then grieve over death1? Those who do their duty have nothing to
grieve over. I understand why you did not come. Your place is
there. Come here for a rest when you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7131. Also C.W. 4623. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
