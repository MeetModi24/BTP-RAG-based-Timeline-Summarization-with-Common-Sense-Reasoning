649. LETTER TO RATILAL P. MEHTA
November 13, 1932
CHI. RATILAL,
I was glad to see your letter. I have often told you that
God protects you. Don't mind the theft. Do not replace the arti-
cles which have been stolen. If we have too many things with us,
we may even lose some through theft.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8764. Also C.W. 1048. Courtesy:
Champabehn Mehta
