333. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
PATNA,
March 27, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
Your letter is before me. Do not try to do too many things
at a time. If you will serve Harijans, then do not think of the
meningitis patients. They will be nursed otherwise. Whatever
is needed to qualify you for your task, you will certainly do.
You must not fear me. I am not going to drive you out unless
you drive yourself out. You have need to fear yourself rather
than any other person. I am well.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
Why do you not write in Gujarati any more?
