33. LETTER TO M. R. JAYAKAR
August 15, 1944
DEAR DR. JAYAKAR,
I am grateful to you for your two letters. The long one is
illuminating. I am aware of the dangers. I am not afraid of
coming away with empty hands. I am doing what I have done
all my life. I know you believe in the all-pervading Deity. I ask
you to pray that I may be blessed with the strength to do the
right thing.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Gandhi-Jayakar Papers: File No. 826. Courtesy : National Archives of
India
