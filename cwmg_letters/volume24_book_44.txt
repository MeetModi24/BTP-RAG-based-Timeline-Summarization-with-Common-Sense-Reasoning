44. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[May 19, 1924]
You have not written the letter as a Mr. Hyde, but as Shekh-
challi. Dr. Jekyll too has the right to build castles in the air;
particularly when he happens to live in the Ashram castle. What
is wrong is the desire to live away from me. Whatever that may
be, I am not a stupid bania to sell my precious wares cheap by get-
ting you a lucrative post and taking money from you for the Ashram.
That much money you can get for the Ashram even by begging. I
want to conduct the Ashram either by begging or by doing manual
labour. There are many more things I would like to say, but you
may spell them out from what I have said already. The body of
a self-controlled man must be healthy. There is a contradiction
between physical education and spiritual education. There is a
direct relation between health and spiritual strength.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MAHADEV DESAI
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI 1
