726. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
SEVAGRAM,
April 26, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
My faith is well founded. It rests on direct experience. All
learning is limitless. Fortunately, even the examiners cannot go
beyond the limitlessness of learning. So, why should we be
scared by it? You are of course not the type to be scared. So
far, the fast has gone on all right. I have been able to carry
on the daily routine. I am writing this to you at 6 a. m. I
have been taking plenty of water.
It would be very nice if you could come here in June.
Durga is well. Prof. Trivedi had blood-pressure. Now he is
having difficulty in urination. He has diabetes. But his con-
dition is fairly good. Durga is being given rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
