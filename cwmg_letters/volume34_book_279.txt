279. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
August 9, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. Mahadev has not been able to place
before me the papers you refer to as yet; for, he has found me
too busy.
The inks I shall test and let you know my impression.
How is your daughter getting on both in health and studies?
She must some day come to the Ashram.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. KONDA VENKATAPPAYYAGARU
GUNTUR
From a microfilm: S.N. 14212
