287. LETTER TO PRIVATE SECRETARY TO LORD MORLEY
[LONDON,]
September 30, 1909
SIR,
I beg to enclose herewith for Lord Morley's information, copy of
letter addressed by me to the Private Secretary to the Earl of Crewe.
I remain, etc.,
M. K. GANDHI
India Office Records: 3815/09; also, from a photostat of the typewritten office
copy: S. N. 5103
