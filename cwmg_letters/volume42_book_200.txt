200. LETTER TO B. RAM VARMA
CAMP ALLAHABAD,
November 17, 1929
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. The Raja Saheb of Avagarh has promised
a fat contribution but I have not received anything yet. He
however said that he would send the contribution at an early
date. I have no doubt that he will do so. I have nothing but
sweet recollections of my visit to Etah. You were all very kind and
I think that the Etah contribution was not bad.
Yours sincerely,
SF. B. RAM VARMA
PRESIDENT, DT. CONGRESS COMMITTEE
ETAH
From a photostat: S.N. 15774
