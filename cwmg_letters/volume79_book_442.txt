442. LETTER TO SUPERINTENDENT, BHAGALPUR JAIL
SEVAGRAM,
March 17, 1945
THE SUPERINTENDENT
BHAGALPUR JAIL
BHAGALPUR
DEAR SIR,
Here is a cutting from the Aj of 14th instant. It contains
disquieting news about Shrimati Prabhavati Devi, your prisoner.
I shall thank you to tell me what truth there is in the news.
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
