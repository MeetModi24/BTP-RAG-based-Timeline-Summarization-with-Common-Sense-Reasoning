19. LETTER TO ISHVARLAL JOSHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
July 8, 1930
CHI. ISHVARLAL,
How are things with you? What progress have you made?
Have you kept up your study of English? Is Lakshmidasbhai
satisfied with your progress?
Is Indu there? How is she? Ask her to write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the original in Gujarati: C.W. 9311. Courtesy: Ishvarlal Joshi
