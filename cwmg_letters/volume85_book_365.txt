365. LETTER TO DR. R. G. COCHRANE
NEW DELHI,
September 11, 1946
DEAR DR. COCHRANE,
I have just read your letter which I am publishing in
Harijan and a copy of which I am sending to the Bihar
Minister concerned. I suggest that you should send me a well-
considered medical opinion, signed by as many medical men as
possible. I have been carrying on correspondence with the Sind
Ministers also with whom I have not been able to make much
headway. Hence my suggestion.
Yours sincerely,
MISSIONARY MEDICAL COLLEGE
VELLORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
