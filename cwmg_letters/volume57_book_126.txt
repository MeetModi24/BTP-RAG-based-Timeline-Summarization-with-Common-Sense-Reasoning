126. LETTER TO MATHURI N. KHARE
February 5, 1934
CHI. MATHURI,
I was very happy to see your handwriting after a long time.
You have improved your handwriting, too, a good deal. I am
also very happy that you find everything there congenial. I hope
you do sing bhajans. Write to me from time to time. I am glad
that you write to Rambhau.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 270. Courtesy: Lakshmibehn N. Khare
1 Devotion to God
2 Devotional hymns for early morning
