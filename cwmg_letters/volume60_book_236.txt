236. LETTER TO DR. N. B. KHARE
February 7, 1935
DEAR DR. KHARE,
I have your letter. I knew you were coming to Delhi and
I saw that we would be crossing each other without meeting.
I did send a message to Shri Kanetkar. He complained
that it was too brief. Little does he know how hard pressed I am
to take any burden beyond the daily routine work. If I go to
Nagpur, I shall certainly refer to the proposed memorial.
Yours sincerely,
DR. N. B. KHARE, M.L.A.
3 ELECTRIC LANE
NEW DELHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
