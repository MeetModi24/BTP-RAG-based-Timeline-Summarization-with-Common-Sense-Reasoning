359. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
NAVAGAM,
Purnima [July 23, 1918]
DEAR MAHADEV,
The result of your losing your temper was that you could not
come, nor could Shivabhai. We two enjoyed walking down to
to this place. We arrived at 10.15. The people were surprised.
The poison which the Circle Inspector has sown here must
be dug out. We shall, therefore, stay on here for three or four
days. You or Shivabhai may bring over the mail.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Inform Anasuyabehn that we are here up to Thursday at any
rate.
Bring, or send, postal covers, postcards and plain envelopes.
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: C. W. 5790. Courtesy:
C. K. Bhatt
