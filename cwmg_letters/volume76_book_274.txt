274. LETTER TO K. SUBBA RAO
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA, C. P.,
June 19, 1942
DEAR SUBBA RAO,
Do let me know if you had further development over that
Press correspondence.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI K. SUBBA RAO
FREE PRESS
P.O.B. 400
MADRAS
From a photostat: G.N. 5665. Also C.W. 2977. Courtesy: K. Subba Rao
