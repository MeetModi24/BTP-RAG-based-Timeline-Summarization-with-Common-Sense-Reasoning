513. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
KARACHI,
Sunday, February 3, 1929
CHI. KUSUM,
The women's section should be cleaner. Let all the women
divide the work among themselves. A lot of water is thrown in the
kitchenyard; this should stop. Now that you have two bathrooms out-
side, everyone had better go there. The room where Yashodabehn
lives, that too should be cleaner. Do something about the water.
I am feeling weak these days. I cannot understand why.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1775
