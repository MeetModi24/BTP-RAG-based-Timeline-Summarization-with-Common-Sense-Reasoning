594. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
April 5, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
What you say is correct. By all means go to Mool. There is
only one condition: if you get ill, you must return immediately. Do
not be eager to call Kanchan there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8505. Also C.W. 7132. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
