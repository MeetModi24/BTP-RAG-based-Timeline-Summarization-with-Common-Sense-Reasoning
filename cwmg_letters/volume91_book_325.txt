325. LETTER TO JAIRAMDAS DOULATRAM
July 10, 1924
MY DEAR JAIRAMDAS,
I have neglected you altogether. I am ashamed of myself.
But my utter helplessness is my only excuse. I shall be free
only tonight after prayer. We therefore meet then.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From the original: C.W. 11036. Courtesy: Arjun Jairamdas
