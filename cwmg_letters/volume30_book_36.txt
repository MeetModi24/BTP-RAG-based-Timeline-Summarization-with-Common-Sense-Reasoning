36. LETTER TO SUPERINTENDENT,
LEPER ASYLUM, PURULIA
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
February 19, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your very prompt and exhaustive reply. I
am forwarding a copy of it to the friend who is ailing. He is a
professor in a college and I know he will feel thankful for the
pains you have taken.
Please remember me to Dr. Santra.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 14110
