181. LETTER TO W. CHURCHILL
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 15, 1906
WINSTON CHURCHILL, ESQ.
HIS MAJESTY'S UNDER-SECRETARY OF STATE FOR THE COLONIES
WHITEHALL
SIR,
Mr. Ally and I, who have come as a Deputation from the Trans-
vaal on behalf of the British Indians, venture to request an appoint-
ment with you in order to enable us to place the British Indian position
in the Transvaal before you. We shall be extremely obliged if you could
spare a short time to enable us to wait on you.
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Yours obediently,
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S. N. 4572
