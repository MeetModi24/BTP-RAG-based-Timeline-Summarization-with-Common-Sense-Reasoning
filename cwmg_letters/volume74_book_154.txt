154. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
June 4, 1941
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
The letter has had no effect on me because I did not find
anything new in it. If I had written anything about that, I
would have given them another chance to tell lies, and it would
have been of no use. The principle is that except for doing our
103
own duty we should not bother about anything else. But my
unwillingness had nothing to do with the principle.
I am glad to know that there is no atmosphere of fear in
Calcutta. It would be very satisfying if at the back of this fear-
lessness is a restraint in taking revenge. Perhaps the riots, etc.,
will increase but it will be good if neither party goes beyond limits;
any other course would do no good to the country. From today
there is a change in the weather. A cool breeze has started.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 8044. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
