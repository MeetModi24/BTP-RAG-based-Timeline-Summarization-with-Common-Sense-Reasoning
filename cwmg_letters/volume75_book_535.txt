535. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
[After March 27, 1942]
CHI. JAWAHARLAL,
Khurshedbehn is very unhappy. She feels that your heart
has dried up towards her. Send for her, give her some affection.
You know she adores you.
Today I woke up at two. I was thinking of you and Rajaji
only. I am clearly of the view that we cannot accept this 'offer'.
If you also agree, then talk to Rajaji and reach a final decision.
If you are inclined to agree with Rajaji then the matter deserves
further consideration.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: Gandhi-Nehru Papers.
