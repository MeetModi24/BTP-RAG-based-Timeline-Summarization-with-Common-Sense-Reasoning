482. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 9, 1940
CHI. PREMI,
I have your letter. I want a letter daily. There is no harm
in staying on there as long as you like. We shall be leaving
here for Calcutta on the 15th evening. We shall return on the
27th or the 28th.
Are you able to read my handwriting? If you cannot, I
shall write in English.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI PREMIBEN
C/O DR. C. S. SAHNI
KHANDWA, C. P.
From the Hindi original: C. W. 11048. Courtesy: Arjun Jairamdas
