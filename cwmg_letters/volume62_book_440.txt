440. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
May 8, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
Your Sweet letter. I leave by the Grand Trunk after 6
p. m. I had a long talk with J. about the road through the vil-
lage. His mind is working on it. Do keep yourself perfectly
well and cheerful. Do not overwork yourself even though my
cottage itself remains unfinished. No fretting.
I hope you got the rammers.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6333. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9799
