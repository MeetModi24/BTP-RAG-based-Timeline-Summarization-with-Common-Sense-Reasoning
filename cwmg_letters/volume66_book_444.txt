444. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI AND FAMILY
February 3, 1938
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA, SITA, ARUN,
Welcome to you all. When are you coming here? Akola is
just on the way. Nanabhai is not keeping well. Go to him first;
it is like a pilgrimage. We leave for Haripura about the 7th or 8th
instant. I take it you will accompany us. I am keeping well
enough.
Blessings from
BAPU 2
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4870
