287. LETTER TO LAJPAT RAI
NANDI HILLS,
April 30, 1927
DEAR LALAJI,
I got your letter and the Trust papers. Let me congratulate
you on your decision. This amount, I am afraid, will not be
sufficient; we will require yet more funds, for which we must
wait.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
[From Hindi]
Lala Lajpat Rai-Ek Jeevani, p. 468
