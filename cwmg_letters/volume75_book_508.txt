508. LETTER TO PREMA KANTAK
March 22, 1942
CHI. PREMA,
I got your letter. I wear Deo's gift every day. The dhoti
is very light. It is beautiful.
Write to Sucheta thus: 4 'I have been asked to take up this
work.
Please let me know what things I will have to do. My
hands are always full. I am already doing women's welfare work.
What more should I do that we are not doing?'
Write as above and let me know what her reply is.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1042. Also C.W. 6863. Courtesy:
Prema Kantak
