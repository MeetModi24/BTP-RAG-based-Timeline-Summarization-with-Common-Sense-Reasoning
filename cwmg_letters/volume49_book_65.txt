65. LETTER TO AMINA G. QURESHI
January 28, 1932
CHI. AMINA,
I got your letter. Noorbanu also gave me news about you
yesterday. I am sure that you two will do full honour to the
position which Father held. May God grant long life to you both.
I do know your courage.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6660. Also C.W. 4305. Courtesy:
Hamid Qureshi
