309. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
January 19, 1942
CHI. LILI,
I have your letter. I have not a single minute to spare. It
is not proper for you to lose heart. You must make up your
mind to pass. We are going to Kashi today, and shall be back
on the 24th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 10089. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
