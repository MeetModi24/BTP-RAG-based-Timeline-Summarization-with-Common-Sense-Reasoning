99. LETTER TO TARA JASANI
MAGANWADI, WARDHA,
August 9, 1939
CHI. TARA,
I don't feel like going for walks without all my [walking-] sticks.
Kanu Bablo are busy, and so they do not come out with me.
Instead of my giving you a description of the mountains, you
may see them with your own eyes some day.
What work will you do if there is a drought? How much
will you contribute to spinning yajna?
It is good that you attend Kanji Muni's discourses. Your
revered parents always used to listen to him with rapt attention.
Blessings from
BAPU
