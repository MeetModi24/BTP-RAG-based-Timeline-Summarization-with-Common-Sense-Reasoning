455. LETTER TO F. MART BARR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 2, 1942
CHI. MARY,
I have your letter for which I was waiting. I am glad you
are flourishing. Continue to report progress. Yes, Jamnalalji's
death is a great blow. We must resign ourselves to God's will.
Love.
BAPU
Bapu—Conversations and Correspondence with Mahatma Gandhi, p. 206
