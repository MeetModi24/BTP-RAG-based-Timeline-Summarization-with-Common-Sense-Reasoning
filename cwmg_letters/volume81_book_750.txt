750. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
October 25, 1945
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
I have your letter. You have given a very moving account.
Satyavati was exactly as you have described. I do not recall the
name of anyone, but convey to all the members of the family
the message I had sent you in my telegram. How nice that
Satyavati's desire in regard to marriage was fulfilled!
If I think of any rites in connection with the death I shall
write to you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 2489
