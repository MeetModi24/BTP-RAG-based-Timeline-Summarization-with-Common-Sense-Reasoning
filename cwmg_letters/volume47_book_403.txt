403. LETTER TO RACHEL M. RUTTER
AHMEDABAD,
August 22, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter and the pains you have been
taking. I can't make use of the letter you have sent me. But
why should you worry ? Your personal contacts would be your
substantial work.
I am sorry you were suffering from phlebitis at the time
you wrote. I hope this will find you in good health and condition.
Yours sincerely,
MISS RACHEL M. RUTTER
WINCANTON
SOMERSET (ENGLAND)
From a photostat: S.N. 17569
