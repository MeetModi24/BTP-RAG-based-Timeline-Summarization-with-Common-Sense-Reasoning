203. LETTER TO SECRETARY, POONA SARVAJANIK
SABHA
October 11, 1934
THE SECRETARY
THE POONA SARVAJANIK SABHA
SHUKRAWAR PETH
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter enclosing resolution of the Poona
Sarvajanik Sabha.
I am treasuring all the criticism that comes
under my notice, and I give you my assurance that I shall take
no step without giving the utmost consideration to all this criti-
cism.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
