265. LETTER TO MANSINGH JASRAJ
ASHRAM,
April 3, 1926
BHAISHRI MANSINGH,
Your letter. Your guess is correct. I have no daughter. And
that woman has been playing the hoax everywhere. This has
appeared once in Navajivan; but I shall write again.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI MANSINGH JASRAJ
C/O SJT. SHAMALBHAI BABARBHAI
ADEN CAMP
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19890
