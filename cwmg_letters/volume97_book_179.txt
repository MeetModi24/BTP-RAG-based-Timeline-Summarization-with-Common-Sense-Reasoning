179. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
January 4, 1931
CHI. RAMDAS,
While your case is pending, should not I write to you? What is Surendra's
diet? I have heard that he has gone down in health. Give me particulars of
your daily routine. What is your diet there? Do they let you spin?
Blessings from
BAPU
