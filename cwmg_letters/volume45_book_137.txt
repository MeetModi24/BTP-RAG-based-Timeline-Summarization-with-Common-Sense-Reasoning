137. LETTER TO JAMNA N. GANDHI
January 18, 1931
CHI. JAMNA,
I don't at all remember having received a letter from you
about Dharmadas and Kalyandas. If I had received any, I would
have replied to it.
If you don't keep good health in the Ashram, I should certainly
like you to go to Bombay and adopt the diet I have suggested.
Tell Kusum that she should not be impatient to start eating
solid food. One can live on fruits and milk for the whole of one's
life. She must fully regain her health. There is nothing wrong with
my health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 847. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
