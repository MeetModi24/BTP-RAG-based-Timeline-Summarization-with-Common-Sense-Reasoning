4. LETTER TO RAFI AHMED KIDWAI
July 18, 1945
BHAI RAFI1,
I learnt of your release at Kalka. I arrived in Sevagram this
evening. It is good you were freed. Do you get fever? Do
you have weakness? Write to me fully.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAFI AHMED KIDWAI
ANAND BHAWAN
ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
