577. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
BOMBAY,
March 31, 1945
CHI. MUNNALAL,
You have now an opportunity to set everything right. Use
it. Take some work from Mohan Singh and Ramprasad if you
can. See how he makes biscuits and bread with tomato juice in
them.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8458. Also C.W. 5573.
Courtesy; Munnalal G. Shah
