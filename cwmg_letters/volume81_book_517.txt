517. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
September 29, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
Krishnachandra writes and says that you have become some-
what weak. I hope you are better now and that you and Gajraj
are carrying on well. I am all right. There is a little weakness
which will go.
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
