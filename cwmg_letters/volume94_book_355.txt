355. LETTER TO MIAN IFTIKHAR-UD-DIN
April 20, 1945
DEAR MTAN SAHEB,
I had your letter of 14th inst. I showed it to Gandhiji.
He is of opinion that under the circumstances you describe you should behave
and act as if no restriction notice had ever been served on you.
With kind regards,
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
MIAN IFTIKHAR-UD-DIN
21 AIKMAN ROAD
LAHORE
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and Library.
Courtesy : Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
