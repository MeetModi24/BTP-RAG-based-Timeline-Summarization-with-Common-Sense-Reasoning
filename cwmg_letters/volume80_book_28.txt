28. LETTER TO OM PRAKASH GUPTA
MAHABALESHWAR,
April 26, 1945
CHI. OM PRAKASH,
Unless you scrupulously follow all the Ashram rules, you will
gain nothing by merely staying in the Ashram. You will have
to get a certificate from the inmates of the Ashram. If you are
hot-tempered, cure yourself of it. The Ashram is the place to over-
come temperamental shortcomings. Not everybody can do so.
But you must. You have to provide an ideal. If you do not like
the Ashram, it would be futile for you to stay there.
Blessings from
BAPU
