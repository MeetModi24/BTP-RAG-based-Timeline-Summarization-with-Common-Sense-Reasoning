391. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
August 2, 1940
MY DEAR IDIOT,
So you will be delayed again. I have said don't hurry. I
shall postpone remaking your mattress till after your return.
The mistakes you point out are there. When you come they
will be avoided. I do revise the English translations, can't revise
the Hindustani.
Khanderia's letter I shall see. Maulana is just coming.
Ku. is here under my direct treatment and so is A. S. She is
on her back. I have put her on a few ounces of milk.
Love.
TYRANT
From the original: C.W. 3991. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7300
