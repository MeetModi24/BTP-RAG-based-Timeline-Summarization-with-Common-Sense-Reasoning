239. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
Friday [December 9, 1921]
CHI. MAHADEV,
Received your telegram. I am not happy that you have had
to pay security for publishing the Independent. But it is our duty to
do whatever Panditji wishes. I would certainly be pleased if you
are arrested. Do tell Panditji, however, that it would be better not
to pay any security if it is again demanded and to issue a hand-
written paper instead. It is the easiest thing to do. They will
certainly arrest you, but that does not matter. Sarup and Ranjit
are going there, they also can become proprietors. I shall certain-
ly try to send someone from here.
Pyarelal can certainly come.
Blessings from
BAPU
