293. LETTER TO T. PRAKASAM
August 27, 1945
BHAI PRAKASAM,
I have your letter. The students performed a good play.
Let us hope that they will display in real life what they have
depicted in the play. Let us see how much Sardar benefits.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
19 RAJABAHADUR MUDALIAR STREET
T. NAGAR, MADRAS
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
