77. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEGAON,
August 6, 1939
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM 2 ,
I got your two letters together. I have already written to
you to do what you please. If you wish to come over here, do
so. Discuss the matter with Shankerlalbhai.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 430
