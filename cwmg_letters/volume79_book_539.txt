539. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM,
March 27, 1945
CHI. AMRIT,
This letter will be given to you by Dr. Gurbuxani and his
wife Vimalabehn. Both are educated. Both come from affluent fami-
lies. They have given up Government service and also luxurious
living in order to be with me. The wife cannot stand this heat.
Perhaps she is pregnant too. They have lived in Simla. They
are coming to Simla. Put them on to some public work if you
can. They will have to be given some salary. Be frank if they
cannot be useful. There is no question of favour in service. Here
they had started with the cleaning of latrines.
Blessings from
BAPU
