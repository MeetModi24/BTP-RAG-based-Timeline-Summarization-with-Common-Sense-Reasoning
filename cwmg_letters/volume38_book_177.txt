177. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
WARDHA,
December 3, 1928
MY DEAR JAWAHAR,
My love to you. It was all done bravely. You have braver
things to do. May God spare you for many a long year to come
and make you His chosen instrument for freeing India from yoke.
Yours,
BAPU
A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 68
