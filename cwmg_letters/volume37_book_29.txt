29. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
July 5, 1928
Why do you take the injustices being done in the world to
be injustice? Doesn't the world itself mean selfishness? How
can it go on without selfishness? It is in such a world that the
Gita teaches us to be detached. Why do you expect selfishness
to be considerate to victims of tuberculosis or similar diseases?
But need I talk wisdom to you? What surprises me is that you
felt hurt by the attitude adopted by the owner of the bungalow
towards you.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 94
