351. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
PATNA,
April 28, 1947
CHI. SATIS BABU,
I have your letter. Now I can go there only after my re-
turn from Delhi. We shall know more after Nirmal Babu's ar-
rival.
There is a report in the Hindustan Standard about some inci-
dents in Comilla. Do you know anything about it? I have
passed it on to Sailen. Dr. Mahmud has not yet returned.
He is coming on Wednesday. Perhaps he will travel with me.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9078
