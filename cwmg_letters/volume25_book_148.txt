148. LETTER TO TULSI MEHAR
DELHI,
[September 21, 1924]
CHI. TULSI MEHAR,
It was only yesterday I had decided to address the Ashram
letter to you and I have your letter today. To you and all the
other inmates of the Ashram my only appeal is: Follow truth and
non-violence; hate no creature in the world; card, spin, and
weave for the sake of the starving millions of India and propa-
gate the same. Learn to read and write by all means. Cultivate
soul-force. Give the first place to the charkha among village
industries.
I am quite happy. Let no one have the slightest worry on
my account.
Blessings from
BAPU
TULSI MEHAR
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM
SABARMATI
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6520
