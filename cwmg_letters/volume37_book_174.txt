174. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKER PATTANI
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 6, 1928
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
I got your letter. Need I thank you?
No one need worry about me at all. God will keep me all
right for His own purpose so long as He wants me to work,
and when He gets displeased even a thousand physicians will not be
able to help me. But now a settlement seems certain.
Nevertheless, Vallabhbhai desires that I should stay here for
a few days more.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
Don't worry about coming to the Ashram. If you can find
the time to come, you will undoubtedly be welcome.
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 3224. Courtesy: Mahesh Pattani
