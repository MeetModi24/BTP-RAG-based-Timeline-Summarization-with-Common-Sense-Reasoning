543. LETTER TO DR. PARASHURAM
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 14, 1926
DEAR DR. PARASHURAM,
I have your letter. The only advice that I can give you is:
'Go back to your place, return to your practice and there do
what service you can. And make no fuss about it.'
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 19719
