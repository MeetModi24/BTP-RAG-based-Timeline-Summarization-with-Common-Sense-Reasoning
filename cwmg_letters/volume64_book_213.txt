213. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
TILAKNAGAR,
December 27, 1936
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I got your letter. I have to go to Travancore on the 10th.
Kanti arrived the day before yesterday. I had a three minutes'
talk with him last night. You must reach Segaon on the 3rd.
I am in a great hurry.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 370
