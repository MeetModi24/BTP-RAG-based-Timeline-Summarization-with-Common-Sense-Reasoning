484. LETTER TO T. B. SAPRU
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
March 7, 1941
DEAR SIR TEJ BAHADUR,
I hope you have kept free of fever. If you propose to invite
Qaid-e-Azam and me, I shall of course present myself. But I
would urge you to think fifty times before issuing these invitations.
Failure would be bad. And what I fear is that the time for com-
munal settlement has not come. But you need not mind my
caution, if you feel otherwise.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
