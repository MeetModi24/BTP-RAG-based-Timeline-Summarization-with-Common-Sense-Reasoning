206. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 18, 1940
DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. Everything will be sent afterwards to
Anand. I have heard some fulminations from the Press. Whatever
we do, will be done in the name of God alone. We are not doing
anything to please anyone else. If the Hindus go berserk, we shall
think again. Whatever you do, you should do only after consulting
Maulana Saheb. When he leaves the place, then it will be a
different thing.
It was good that you went to Hatim's place.
You shall, of course, keep silent. You should not be afraid of
abuses. I shall certainly go to the telephone, but I do not know
whether I shall be able to hear. Qureshi's letter is enclosed.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 447
