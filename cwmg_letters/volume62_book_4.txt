4. LETTER TO FULCHAND K. SHAH
October 1, 1935
BHAI FULCHAND,
I was very happy to have your letter; I had thought you
had completely forgotten me.
All that I have to say to the friends of Harijans is that they
should strengthen their faith in the purity of their task.
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
