84. LETTER TO TOTARAM HINGORANI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 7, 1939
MY DEAR HINGORANI,
It gives me great joy to find that you are reconciled to Anand
and Vidya and that they are near you to render you such filial
service as you may need.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI TOTARAM HINGORANI
KARACHI
From a microfilm. Courtesy: National Archives of India, and Anand
T. Hingorani
