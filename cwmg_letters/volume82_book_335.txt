335. LETTER TO SANKARAN
SODEPUR,
December 18, 1945
CHI. SHANKARAN,
I have your letter. I understand. I have already written
for the expenses of [your] son and sister being paid.
Come here after improving your health. Write to me about
everything there. Let me know how many patients there are and
what treatment they are being given and so on.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SANKARAN
BALESHAWAR SINGHJI
JIVAN SAKHA, LUKERGANJ
ALLAHABAD
