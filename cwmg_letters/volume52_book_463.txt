463. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
January 3, 1933
MY DEAR MARY,
I must dictate this letter. You will be entitled to treat us as
hosts or hostesses when we agree to look upon you as a guest.
But I must flatly decline to give you that treatment.
The experience you have related is not uncommon; hence
we have the proverb that it takes two to make a quarrel. I think
that your interference was quite timely and proper.
Love from us.
BAPU
From a photostat: G. N. 5987. Also C.W. 3344. Courtesy: Miss F. Mary Barr
