574. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
ON THE TRAIN,
March 31, 1945
CHI. HOSHIARI,
You may open your mouth but only when necessary. Be
true to your name. Take part in all the activities of the Ashram.
Keep your body fit and let Balvantsinha go for a few days.
Write to me. Practise writing every day.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
