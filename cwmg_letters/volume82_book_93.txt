93. LETTER TO RATILAL TANNA
November 11, 1945
BHAI RATILAL,
I have your letter. My messages have dried up. And even
if they have not, you should not ask for a message regarding
Dhebarbhai . If you cannot understand what this means, ask
Dhebarbhai himself.
Blessings from
BAPU
RATILAL TANNA
JANI BUILDING
RAJKOT
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
