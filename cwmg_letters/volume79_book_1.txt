1. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
SEVAGRAM,
January 1, 1945
MY DEAR C. R.,
This is the first letter to you on 1st January in the place of
speaking to you. I shall speak only at the time of opening the
prayer at 7.30. My sin in playing with Ayurveda has laid me low.
Now I am weak because according to the law of natural science
I am throwing off the poison. Don't you be anxious for me.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
BAZULLAH ROAD, THYAGARAYA NAGAR
MADRAS
From a photostat : G.N. 2100
