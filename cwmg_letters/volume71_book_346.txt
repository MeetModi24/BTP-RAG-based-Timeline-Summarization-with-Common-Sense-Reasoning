346. LETTER TO C. RAJAGOPALACHARI
ON THE TRAIN,
March 21, 1940
MY DEAR C. R.,
I saw so little of you in Ramgarh. It is bad. Did not see
Aiyamuthu. I understand you have gone to Delhi. Therefore on
your return you should break the journey and stay a few days in
Sevagram or Wardha whichever suits you better. There are
things to discuss.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 2079
