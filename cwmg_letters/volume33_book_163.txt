163. LETTER TO MAGANLAL GANDHI
[Before March 16, 1927]
CHI. MAGANLAL,
Kaka Saheb had suggested that the Managing Committee should
see all relevant letters so as to know my views on various matters.
This is right and so henceforth I shall write separately on business
matters so that everyone can go through the correspondence.
Again I find you as uncharitable as before. I agree it is un-
avoidable since you are not aware of it. Shake off this attitude.
You will be able to do so if you try. You must participate in the
work of the School Committee. If you wish to be nominated
to the Education Committee, it can be done. Do not stand on
prestige. More when I get your letter. Try to pacify Ramachandra.
You should have a better appreciation of the difficulties of the
lift.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 7761. Courtesy: Radhabehn Chaudhri
