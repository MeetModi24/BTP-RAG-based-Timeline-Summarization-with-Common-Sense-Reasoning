238. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
PARNAKUTI,
POONA,
January 8, 1937
MY DEAR IDIOT,
You are straining every nerve to deserve the title. What a
scene on Thursday over a most trifling thing! And that too
after assuring me that you were indifferent to such things. How-
ever, let the past be buried.
This is merely to inform you that Ramachandran wires
you and Khan Saheb could go with me. I do not want to
bank on that wire. I expect confirmation from C. P.
I hope you had a good journey and that you have no
trouble. Do please observe the dietetic rules.
Love.
TYRANT
[PS.]
Please remember me to Mrs. Swaminathan.
SHRI RAJKUMARI AMRIT KAUR
C/O SHRI AMMU SWAMINATHAN
GILCHRIST GARDENS
CHETPUT
MADRAS
From the original: C.W. 3762. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6918
1 &2 The drafts of the telegrams are in English.
