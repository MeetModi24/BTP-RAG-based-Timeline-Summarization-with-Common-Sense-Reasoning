342. LETTER TO PADMAJA NAIDU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
November 20, 1938
MY DEAR LOTUS-BORN,
No letter from you. I hope no news is good news both
about your health and your mission. You are not to wear your-
self out on the mission work. I shall never excuse myself if you
damage your health by overwork.
Love.
PLAYMATE
SHRI PADMAJA NAIDU
ZAHEER MANZIL
RED HILL
HYDERABAD
DECCAN
From the original: Padmaja Naidu Papers. Courtesy : Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
