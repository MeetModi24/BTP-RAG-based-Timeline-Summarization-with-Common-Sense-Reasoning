466. LETTER TO URMILA DEVI
October 22, 1932
By a constant study of the Gita, you should learn to shed all
care. When God is the caretaker for us all, why need we carry the
burden? Ours is but to do the task that falls to our lot.
Hence I would urge you not to think of retirement. True
retirement is not a physical state, it comes from within. We have
to find retirement in the midst of ceaseless work. And, are not the
minds of those who are living in caves, oftener than not cease-
lessly at work?
We should not brood on our hard lot. The lot of those who
would serve is generally, if not always, hard.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 168
