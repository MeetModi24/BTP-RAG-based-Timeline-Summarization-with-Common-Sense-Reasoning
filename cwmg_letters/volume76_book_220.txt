220. LETTER TO N. R. MALKANI
SEVAGRAM,
June 3, 1942
BHAI MALKANI,
Jajuji has sent me your letter about money. He says:
In my opinion this expenditure was not necessary. I had advised
against it. Even so it has been incurred.
His advising against it has hurt me a little. What is all
this and how did it happen?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 942
