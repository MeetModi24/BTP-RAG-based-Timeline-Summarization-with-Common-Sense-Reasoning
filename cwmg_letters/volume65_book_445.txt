445. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
July 12, 1937
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
Mahadev tells me, Sita has come. Hope she is well. He
also tells me, you may not come till the weather clears. If so
what about Rao? All the accounts I receive go to show that he
is no credit to us. He seems to be incurring losses. Please
investigate.
Read the enclosed essay and tell me if it is worth publishing
in Harijan.
The summary of your address at Bezawada 3 will appear in
due course.
You should think how we may guide our . . . 4 Ministers in
the matter of village improvement.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10121
