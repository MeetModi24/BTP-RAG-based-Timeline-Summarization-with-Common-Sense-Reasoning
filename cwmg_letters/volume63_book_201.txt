201. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
July 21, 1936
CHI. AMTUL SALAAM,
I hope you have received my letter. I am enclosing letters
to Tyagi and Raj and also one to the students.
Return the letter to Rammehar noting on it all the facts.
Ask Malkaniji why he had to be relieved.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 341
