285. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS BIRLA
SEVAGRAM,
October 23, 1944
BHAI RAMESHWARDAS,
Is the land belonging to the Godrej Farm available? How
does Jivanlalji help? Dinshaw's search is going on. These days it
is difficult to obtain a vehicle for travelling. Can you do any-
thing about it?
It has been proposed that the Birla House at Sevagram be
used for the Maternity Home. Is there any difficulty about it?
Blessings from
BAPU
