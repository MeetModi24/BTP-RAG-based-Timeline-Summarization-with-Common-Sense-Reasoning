637. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
October 6, 1941
MY DEAR IDIOT,
Your note has reached me in time. Why you should get
three letters at a time I do not understand. But let us not
forget that this is but a small village.
You have to work out your karma and that cheerfully. It
is likely that winter will set in early this year. So many addi-
tions are being made to the cottage that you will hardly recog-
nize it when you return.
Love.
BAPU
[PS.]
M[ahadev] does not reach here before 19th. He is at pre-
sent in Mussoorie.
[PPS.]
I am glad Raja Saheb is better.
B.
From the original: C.W. 4092. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7401
