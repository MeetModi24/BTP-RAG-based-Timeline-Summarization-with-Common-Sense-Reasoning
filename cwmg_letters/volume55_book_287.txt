287. LETTER TO ABHAYDEV SHARMA
July 2, 1933
BHAI ABHAY,
I read your letter addressed to Rajendra Babu. It seemed to
me good and appropriate. The sentiment is beautiful. The occa-
sion does not appear to have arisen so far for a fast unto death
which may yet have to be undertaken one day.
There is no doubt that only harm has resulted from under-
ground activity.
Let us see what is possible.
It all rests with
Rama.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9662
