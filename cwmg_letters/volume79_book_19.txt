19. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
SEVAGRAM,
January 5, 1945
BHAI B. S.,
I have your letter. I am quite well. Your work was sure
to proceed well. I have never found you wanting in diligence.
Don't leave Satis Babu's work incomplete.
By all means go to Khurja for a few days from there and
then come here. There is work here too and there is not. Get
well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
