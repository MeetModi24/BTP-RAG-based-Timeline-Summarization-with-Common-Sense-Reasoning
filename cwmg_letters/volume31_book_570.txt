570. LETTER TO WADHOOMAL MANGHIRMAL
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
October 24, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am sorry for your misfortune. I can
only advise you to bear it bravely and think no more of the
one who used to be your wife at one time. What does a woman
do when her husband lives with other women? She simply sub-
mits and is still content to live with her so-called husband. You
do not need to do that. You should devote your energy to bring-
ing up the children she has left behind.
Yours sincerely,
WADHOOMAL MANGHIRMAL, ESQ.
AERATED WATER SHOPKEEPER
SEHWAN
(BIHAR)
