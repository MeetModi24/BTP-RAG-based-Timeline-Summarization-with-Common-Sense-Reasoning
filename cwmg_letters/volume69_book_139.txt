139. LETTER TO RAIHANA TYABJI
NEW DELHI,
April 5, 1939
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your letter. I never even dreamt that I had anything
more to do or say after all that had happened. I only quote
your own words here. Yes, Raihana, I do admit that Mother
and you people are not deceitful and this I admit sincerely. I
never believed that you people could ever be deceitful. Is Saroj
with you?
I am happy to hear that Mother is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: S.N. 9675
