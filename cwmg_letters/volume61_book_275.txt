275. LETTER TO F. MARY BARR
WARDHA,
June 23, 1935
CHI. MARY,
If the place agrees with you, you should stay as long as they
will let you. I want you to return strong and healthy. Ask them
what it is that causes fruit to disagree with you.
I am not likely to be out of Wardha for another five months.
Yes. Chhotelal is as you describe him.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 6047. Also C.W. 3377. Courtesy: F. Mary Barr
