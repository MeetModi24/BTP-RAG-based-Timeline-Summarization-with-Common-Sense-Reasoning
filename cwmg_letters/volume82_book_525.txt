525. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
SARANIA ASHRAM,
January 10, 1946
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your two letters before me, which I am enclosing.
I am sorry to learn that Parnerkar's sister's son has passed
away. I understand what you say about Anantramji. It will
be good if he grows leafy vegetables. See how much land
Aryanayakumji wants and where he would like to have it. In
my opinion, we should give him whatever he wants. Maybe
I have left out something but today only this much.
Blessings from
BAPU
