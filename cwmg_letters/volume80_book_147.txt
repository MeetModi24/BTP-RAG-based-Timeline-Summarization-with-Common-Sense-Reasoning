147. LETTER TO KRISHNAVARMA
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 7, 1945
BHAI KRISHNAVARMA,
I have your letter and I am answering it at once. You are
perfectly right in sending a man to be with Madhavdas, to keep
watch over him and make him eat regularly. Do not worry
at all that he may say something or disobey you. He has lost
grip over himself. That is why I have entrusted him to your
care. Make him read this letter so that he knows what I think
about him. Let him feel that this letter is addressed to him also.
You must accept money for expenses.
Let me know about it.
Please keep on writing to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. KRISHNAVARMA
NATURE CURE HOSPITAL
MALAD
