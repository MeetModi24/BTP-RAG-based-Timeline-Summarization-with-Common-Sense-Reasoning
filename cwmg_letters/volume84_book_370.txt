370. LETTER TO G. E. B. ABELL
BIRLA HOUSE, MUSSOORIE,
June 6, 1946
MY DEAR MR. ABELL,
The enclosed has been sent to Gandhiji by the father of the interned
writer.
Gandhiji wishes me to ask you if from your exact knowledge of the
working of the administration you can say whether anything can be done to
enable the young man to fulfil what appears to be his laudable wishes?
With kind regards,
Yours sincerely,
AMRIT KAUR
Gandhiji's Correspondence with the Government, 1944-47, p. 85
