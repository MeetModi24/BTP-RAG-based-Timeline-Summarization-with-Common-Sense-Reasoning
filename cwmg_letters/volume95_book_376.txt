376. LETTER TO PYARELAL
CHI. PYARELAL,
I am writing this much in a very perturbed state of mind. I am
in no position to argue. I have come to realize that I should not argue.
It is enough that my instructions are carried out. In taking you to
Simla, I may take less work from you, but there is plenty to be done
on the way. And even at Simla, my physical needs are there. Now I
shall do as you say.
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy: Beladevi Nayyar and Dr. Sushila Nayyar
