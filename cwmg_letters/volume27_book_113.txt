113. LETTER TO NISHITHNATH KUNDU
[DARJEELING,]
June 6, 1925
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter . I am now trying to discuss the thing with
Deshbandhu.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
NISHITHNATH KUNDU
DIST. CONGRESS COMMITTEE
DINAJPUR
