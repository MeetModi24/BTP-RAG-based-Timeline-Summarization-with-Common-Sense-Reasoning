578. LETTER TO DEVCHAND PAREKH
Asvina Vad 6, 1982 [October 26, 1926]
BHAI DEVCHANDBHAI,
It is necessary to think and decide whether cotton should
be stocked next year.
Ch. Jaisukhlal writes to say that, if we wish to hold a khadi
exhibition at the time of the Conference we should start preparing
for it from now on. I too think so.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5704
