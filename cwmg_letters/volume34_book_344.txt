344. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
Shravan Vad 8 [August 20, 1927]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letter. How old is Haribhai's widowed sister
and how well educated? How about her constitution? Please
let me have these and other facts about her. There was a letter
from Kusum from Broach. She appears to be calm. Why did
you have to stop studying with the help of Shri Mrityunjaya?
Did you not begin studying Gujarati some time ago? How
much Gujarati does Uttamchand's wife know? We should not
undertake a task beyond our capacity.
I should like to have the daily diary completed for the re-
maining period. What I get from it, I do not get from the
letters.
I am all right. Our Mysore tour will end on the 29th.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 592. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
