398. LETTER TO NARAYAN M. KHARE
JUHU,
December 17, 1937
CHI. PANDITJI,
My blessings to Yoga and Soman. May they be happy, live
long and by their life add lustre to the Ashram.
Tell Yoga
that she has dried up after writing to me only one letter. Ask
her to shake off her lethargy.
Lakshmibehn must be quite all right now.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4047
