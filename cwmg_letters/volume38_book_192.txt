192. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
WARDHA,
Wednesday, December 5, 1928
CHI. KUSUM,
I got your letter. I expect to get from you detailed news
about the Ashram. Does the kitchen run punctually? Is the noise
less? Does everybody help Gangabehn? Is anyone ill? How
does Balbir behave? How does Padma fare?
You want to hear about me. If I get some time, I would
certainly write. As it happens, however, I get no time here even
to talk with anyone. I keep Pyarelal also very busy with work,
so he too cannot spare time. Be patient for a while.
Prabhavati must have left now, and so I do not write to her.
If I knew that Vidyavati was there, I would have written to her.
If she is there tell her that she ought not to fall ill.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1762
