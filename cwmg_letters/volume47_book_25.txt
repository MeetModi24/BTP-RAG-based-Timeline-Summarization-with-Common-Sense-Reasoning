25. LETTER TO H. W. EMERSON
BORSAD,
June 20, 1931
DEAR MR. EMERSON,
With reference to your letter enclosing extract from a report
dated 19th May from the District Magistrate, Surat, I now send
you a translation of the statement that Naran Dulabh the party
named in the extract, has made.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Encl. 1
Home Department, Political, File No. 33/9/1931. Courtesy: National
Archives of India
