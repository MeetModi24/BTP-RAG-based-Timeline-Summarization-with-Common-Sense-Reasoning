645. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
NEW DELHI,
October 20, 1946
CHI. AMRIT,
Your wire came in last night. So you were still suffering.
That did signify subsiding.
Let me hope it is better as I am
writing this 5.35 p.m.
Here is a letter from V. G. D. You will see amusing re-
marks about the vocabulary. I must fully share the guilt with
you about "architect". It should [have] been ~if:(lT. We must
give a list of amendments. You may return the letter and I
shall make notes.
Renuka and her sister were with me last night.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C. W. 4180. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G. N. 7816
