174. LETTER TO P. C. JOSHI
JUHU,
June 11, 1944
MY DEAR JOSHI1,
I had expected a prompt reply to the questions I had raised
at our meeting . Meanwhile some additional questions have arisen
which please, answer when you answer my first questions.
1. What is the meaning of "people" in "people's war"? Does
it mean war on behalf of India's millions, or the Negroes in East,
South or West Africa, or the Negroes of America, or all of them?
Are the Allies engaged in such a war?
2. Are the finances of the Communist Party, represented by
you, subject to public audit? If they are, may I see them?
3. It is stated that the Communist Party has actively helped
the authorities to arrest leaders and organizers of labour strikes
during the last two years.
4. The Communist Party is said to have adopted the policy
of infiltrating the Congress organization with a hostile intent.
5. Is not the policy of the Communist Party dictated from
outside ?3
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
Correspondence between Mahatma Gandhi and P. C. Joshi, p. 2
