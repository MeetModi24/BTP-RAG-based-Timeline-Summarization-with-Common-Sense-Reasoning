242. LETTER TO SURENDRANATH VISHWAS
SODEPUR,
December 7, 1945
BHAI SUREN BABU,
I have your informative letter. It will be useful to me.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI SURENDRANATH VISHWAS
1/23 PRINCE GULAM MOHAMMED ROAD
KALIGHAT
CALCUTTA
