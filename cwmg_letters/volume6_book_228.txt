228. LETTER TO LORD STANLEY
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 20, 1906
MY LORD,
May I remind you that Mr. Morley receives the deputation regarding
the Transvaal British Indians on Thursday at 12.20 and that the
members meet at 12 o'clock at the India Office?
I remain,
Your Lordship's obedient servant,
THE RT. HON. LORD STANLEY OF ALDERLEY
18, MANSFIELD STREET, W.
From a photostat of the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4616
1 A note in the office copy initialled by]. W. Godfrey says that, although this
