267. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
August 24, 1945
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
Sharda is in the hands of God, and He will do what He
pleases. It seems she has both typhoid and malaria. If she is
destined to live, she will survive.
Kelkar's letter is enclosed. Provide for the expenses on
account of the two patients, as he says, in excess of the amount
already sanctioned for him. Can pay. . . is for three months.
The doctor may go to Akola.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 10640
