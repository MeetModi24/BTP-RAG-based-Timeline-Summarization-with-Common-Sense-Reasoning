437. LETTER TO PREMLILA THACKERSEY
YERAVDA MANDIR,
November 14, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
You must have got my letter acknowledging receipt of the
cotton, and also the one I wrote in reply to yours. Please send
again three pounds of cotton. I hope all you sisters are well.
Blessings from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 4816. Courtesy: Premlila Tha-
ckersey
