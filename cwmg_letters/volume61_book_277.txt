277. LETTER TO PADMA
June 23, 1935
CHI. PADMA,
By replying to your letter with a postcard, I am saving a
pice for the Daridranarayana. You are all getting along very well.
Study well but train your body well, too, and do not give up
physical labour. Here it is all labour, as you must be observing
from Harijan Sevak.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6151. Also C.W. 3507. Courtesy:
Prabhudas Gandhi
