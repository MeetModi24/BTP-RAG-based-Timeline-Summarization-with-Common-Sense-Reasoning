76. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
September 21, 1941
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Miss Moore will deliver this letter to you. She had once stayed
in the Ashram. She had so adjusted herself that she had become a
member of the family. She wants to meet as many persons as possible
in her quest for truth. Put her up and let her see all the activities.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
SEVASHRAM
MIRZAPUR
AHMEDABAD
From the Gujarati original : S. N. 32780
