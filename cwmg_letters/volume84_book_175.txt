175. LETTER TO BABULAL SHANKARLAL
SIMLA,
May 12, 1946
BHAI BABULAL,
You will find in Harijan the reply to your letter. You
must not accept defeat. When you go to collect slivers you can
do spinning, carding and sliver-making and bring enough slivers
to last a week. Find the rest in my article.
Blessings from
BAPU
BABULAL SHANKARLAL
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
