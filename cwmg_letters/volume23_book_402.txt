402. LETTER TO SWAMI ANANDANAND
Tuesday [May 6, 1924]
BHAISHRI ANANDANAND,
Here is Valji's salute. He wants to see the proofs. He insists
on his spelling being retained. According to the saying, "even a
kick from a milch cow is welcome", we have to accept all his con-
ditions. His pamphlet can be published only next week. I am
1 The postmark carries the date "7 May '24".
2 For the letter to the Navajivan Press, vide the following item.
3 This letter was evidently written the same day as the preceding one.
sending it to you immediately on receiving it, so that you may be
able to send the proofs to him.
What is the name of Valji's brother? And his address?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 7754
