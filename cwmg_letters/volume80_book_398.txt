398. LETTER TO MANGALDAS PAKVASA
PANCHGANI,
June 1, 1945
BHAI MANGALDAS,
I hope your health is perfectly all right. I am enclosing
herewith the Trust-deed of the Nature Cure Clinic. The ne-
cessary changes are already included in it. But I am having
them typed separately and enclosed herewith. Incorporate them
in the Trust-deed and get it signed by the Trustees who are
present. Ghanshyamdas will sign it when he comes here. The
changes in the aims were not covered by the talk I had with
Ghanshyamdas. So I have taken his permission. If you want to
see it, I shall send it.
I would like to get this Trust-deed translated into Hindi
and feel that it would be better if it is registered in Hindi. If
you cannot get it translated there, then send it to me after
finalizing it. I shall get it translated. I would appreciate it if
you did not take so long over it as I did.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANGALDAS PAKVASA
DUNGARSINH ROAD
MALABAR HILL, BOMBAY
