235. LETTER TO SAROJ MOHAN SEN
January 28, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I cannot imagine Sjt. Sasmal being
guilty of any such thing as you attribute to him. But if he or
anybody has been guilty of action such as you describe you should
institute proceedings against them. I am sending your letter to
Sjt. Sasmal asking him to enquire into the matter.
Yours sincerely,
KAVIRAJ SAROJ MOHAN SEN
VAIDYASASTRI
CONTAI, P.O. MIDNAPORE
