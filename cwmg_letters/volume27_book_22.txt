22. LETTER TO RABINDRANATH TAGORE
CALCUTTA,
May 7, 1925
DEAR GURUDEV1,
Suneeti Devi tells me she is going to Bolpur to take part in
the celebration of your 64th birthday. May I add my wish and
prayer to the many that will be sent up tomorrow for your health
and long life?
Andrews told me in one of his letters that you were not keep-
ing well. I hope that you are now feeling stronger.
I am,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SIR RABINDRANATH TAGORE
SANTINIKETAN
From a photostat: G.N. 4628
