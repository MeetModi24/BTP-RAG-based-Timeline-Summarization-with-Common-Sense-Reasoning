389. LETTER TO GOKULCHAND NARANG
September 3, 1945
BHAI GOKULCHAND NARANG1,
I have read your letter. I am helpless. I reply only when I
am asked. Hardly ever about elections. I have very little interest
in them.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. GOKULCHAND NARANG
SAVOY HOTEL
MUSSOORIE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
