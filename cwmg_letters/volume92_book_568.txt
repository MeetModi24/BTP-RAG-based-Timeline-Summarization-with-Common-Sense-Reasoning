568. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
August 31, 1932
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
Your letter with the rakhi arrived, though rather late. All
the time I have been wanting to reply but I can manage it
only now. You did not write about Indu's health. Do so now.
How is Mridula? According to Dahyabhai, she has written
to me but I have not so far received the letter. How is
Saraladevi?
I keep thinking of you all.
All the three of us are well. How is your health? And what
news of Gulzarilal?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S. N. 32821
