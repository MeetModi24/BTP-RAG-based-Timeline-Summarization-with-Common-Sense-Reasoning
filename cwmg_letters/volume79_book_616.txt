616. LETTER TO G. B. PANT
BIRLA HOUSE, BOMBAY,
April 8, 1945
BHAI GOVIND PANT,
How surprising-yesterday I sent you a telegram and today I
receive your letter. Undergo the operation and then come here
when doctors permit you. I may be here till the 20th. Then
Mahabaleshwar. Let us see where God takes me. Get well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GOVIND BALLABH PANT
DR. JOSHI'S HOSPITAL
KAROL BAGH
NEW DELHI
