321. LETTER TO PREMI JAIRAMDAS
October 5, 1932
MY DEAR PREMI,
Yes, I had all you sent me. You will now tell Father I had
his love letter and that I am getting on well.
You must be quick and begin to write in Hindi.
Love to you all from us all.
BAPU
KUMARI SHRI PREMI JAIRAMDAS
PREM BHAVAN
HYDERABAD, SINDH
From a photostat: C.W. 9242. Courtesy: Jairamdas Doulatram
