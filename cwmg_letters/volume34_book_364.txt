364. LETTER TO A GUJARATI STUDENT
[After August 23, 1927]
It is quite proper that fasting has been extolled among us.
But what kind offasting is it? There is a religious dictum to the
effect that the man who starves his body but leaves his mind un-
controlled is a hypocrite. We have ultimately to control the mind
even in fasting. Fasting or not fasting is the same thing for a
man who has conquered his mind. But conquest of the mind is
so difficult. We can control the mind only by repeatedly con-
trolling the body. My salutation to those who adopt this discipline
of fasting and do so for self-purification.
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
