47. LETTER TO OMAR HAJI AMOD
[JOHANNESBURG,]
August 5, 1905
DEAR SHETH OMAR HAJI AMOD,
Your letter to hand. I am glad to know that you have secured
advertisements in Maritzburg.
I am sure you have visited Phoenix. Please do so regularly. You
should see that your sleep is not disturbed.
Salaams from
M. K. GANDHI
OMAR HAJI AMOD, ESQ.
Box [441]
DURBAN
From the Gujarati m Gandhiji's hand: Letter Book (1905): No. 874
