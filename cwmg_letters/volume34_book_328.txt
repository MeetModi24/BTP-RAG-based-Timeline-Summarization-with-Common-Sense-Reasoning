328. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
Silence Day, 1927
CHI. MANI,
Your letter. Keep a record of your experiences of life in
villages for future use. Never be impatient, despondent or uneasy.
I have many questions to ask you but not now; I shall ask you
when we meet or when your present task is finished. Write to
me regularly. Do preserve your health.
I hope you met uncle. He has gone there with high hopes
of being useful. May these be realized.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro—Manibehn Patelne, p. 64
