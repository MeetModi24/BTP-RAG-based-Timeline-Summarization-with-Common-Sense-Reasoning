84. LETTER TO E. W. ARYANAYAKUM
August 20, 1947
CHI. ARYANAYAKUM,
I have your letter.
I have written to Chimanlal to give Rs. 75,000 to Chi.
Radhakisan for the goshala.
My letter about the goshala must have reached you.
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
