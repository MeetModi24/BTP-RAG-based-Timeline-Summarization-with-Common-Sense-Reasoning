201. LETTER TO DALAI LAMA
BHANGI COLONY,
READING ROAD,
NEW DELHI,
April 7, 1947
DEAR FRIEND,
I have three letters from different functionaries saying the
same thing. I thank you for them.
I advised the friends who saw me on your behalf to give
up secretiveness and superstition if Buddhism (which is but an
attempt to reform Hinduism) is to live.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
H. H. THE DALAI LAMA
TIBET
