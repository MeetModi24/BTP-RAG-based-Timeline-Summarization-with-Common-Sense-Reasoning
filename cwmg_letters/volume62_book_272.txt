272. LETTER TO RAMNARESH TRIPATHI
WARDHA,
March 5, 1936
BHAI RAMNARESHJI,
I have your letter and the annotated version of Ramacharit-
manas, too. Daily for half an hour during these days of rest, I
listen to the recitation from the Ramayana. For the last three days
I have been reading your book. Apart from reading the passages

on to the biography. I have great faith in your rendering.
