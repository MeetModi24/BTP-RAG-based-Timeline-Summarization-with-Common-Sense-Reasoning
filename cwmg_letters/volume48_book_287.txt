287. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
December 28, 1931
MY DEAR JAWAHAR,
Indu gave me your letter. Somehow or other your arrest
did not come upon me as a surprise. I have not yet been able to
go to Kamala. I may tonight or tomorrow for certain. You
will be glad to know that I have read your second series of letters
to Indu. I had some suggestions to make, but of that, when per-
haps we have come to our own.
Meanwhile love to you and Sherwani.
A Bunch of Old Letters, p. 104
