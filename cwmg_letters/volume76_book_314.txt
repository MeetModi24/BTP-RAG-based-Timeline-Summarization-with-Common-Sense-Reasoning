314. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 3, 1942
CHI. AMRIT,
You are too much disturbed about me. There is nothing but
fatigue. I shall try to give me as much rest as possible.
J. L. and Mridula are coming tomorrow. Satyavati comes
tonight. Profulla tomorrow. Ku. and Kamla are here. So you
see we are a large family. Kher was here about Goseva and
several others. They all went yesterday or the day before.
Jodhpur's letter is bad but what more can you expect from these
gentlemen. Let us hope that Sri Prakasa's visit will be fruitful.
Ba has a little fever today after a long spell of freedom. I
think she will be free tomorrow.
Narendra Dev is splendid.
He has 4 lb. of milk and still
feels hungry!!!
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 4136. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 7445
