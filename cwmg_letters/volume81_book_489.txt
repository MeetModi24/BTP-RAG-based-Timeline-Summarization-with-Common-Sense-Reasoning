489. LETTER TO AMTUSSALAAM
ON WAY TO POONA,
September 24, 1945
CHI. A. SALAAM,
I got your letter, as also the khadi. What will it matter if
you are not with me on the 2nd October. All those who do my
work are with me even though they are physically away. Aren't
you doing my work there? You will, moreover, be awaiting my
return. Isn't that enough? Get well soon.
I am fully recovered now. Do not worry about me. Raj-
kumari has fallen ill. Just now she is better. She is with me
in the train. Zohra is in Poona.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 501
