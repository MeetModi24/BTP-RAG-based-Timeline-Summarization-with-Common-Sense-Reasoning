386. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
December 8, 1937
CHI. LILAVATI,
Are you still your old self—agitated, restless, overstrung,
emotional, sentimental, unsteady, unmethodical? What will
you do after I die? I would have brought you with me had
it been in my hands. I couldn't easily leave any of you behind.
But my sense of duty made me feel helpless. Write to me. I
will get your letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 9588. Also C.W. 6560. Courtesy:
Lilavati Asar
