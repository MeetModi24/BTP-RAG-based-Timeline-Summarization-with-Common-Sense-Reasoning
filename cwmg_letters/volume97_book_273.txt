273. LETTER TO RAMDAS GANDHI
[May 12, 1935]
CHI. RAMDAS,
What keeps you happy will keep me happy, too. You will develop strength
of character and understand your duty only by acting as you wish to. I do
not worry. Sumitra has once again run back here. She will not get here the
company of. . . 3 she had in the Kanya Ashram. But she is getting along
quite well.
Andrews has already arrived here. . . . 4 Harilal has started looking for
another wife. . . . 5 He will find some work to do. He is staying with . . .
just now. He will peep away from drink and . . . .
I will be going there most probably on the 22nd 6 The same evening, I
will leave for Borsad with Sardar. Sardar also will arrive in Bombay on the
22nd [to see off] a Kamala Nehru. 7
So, after all, I have got letters from Manilal and Sushila. I will send . . .
only with . . . .
Andrews will be here upto the 26th at least . . . .
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : Mrs. Sumitra Kulkarni Papers. Courtesy : Nehru
Memorial Museum and Library
1 Prabhudas's wife, Amba. Gandhiji has humorously compared her to the goddess
Ambabhavani.
2 As suggested in the source
3 The source is illegible here and at many other places.
4 He had returned from England on May 9.
5 Harilal's first wife, Gulab alias Chanchal, had died towards the end of 1918.
6 Gandhiji arrived at Bombay from Wardha on May 22.
7 She was to leave for Europe on the 23rd for medical treatment.
