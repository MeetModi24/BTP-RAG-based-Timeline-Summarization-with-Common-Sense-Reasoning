104. LETTER TO VINOBA BHAVE
November 28, 1936
CHI. VINOBA,
Balkrishna is fine.
I hope you have received the dates.
Let me know whether they are of a better quality. Where is the
need for my coming there early? I promised Dev and Dastane
that I should be there towards the first week of December, but
the stipulation was that you felt the need of my presence there.
My coming causes unnecessary excitement. Do you think I
should come? I do not think so. When I come, possibly a
few good workers will get tied up on my account. Khan
Saheb will be with me. Pyarelal will be there. Sahasrabuddhe
and Vaikunthlal are of the opinion that I should not make the
visit before the 21st. Think over all this and write to me and
persuade Dev and Dastane.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
