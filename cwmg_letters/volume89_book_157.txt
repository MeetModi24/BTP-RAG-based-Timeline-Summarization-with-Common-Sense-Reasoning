157. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
September 1, 1947
CHI. MANI,
I am glad that you do not feel overburdened. There should
be someone with the Sardar who is one hundred per cent fit.
Give him my letter when he is free.
Send to Sushila the material meant for her.
Last evening's developments here were altogether unex-
pected. The man who was alleged to have been stabbed was not
stabbed at all. There was a scuffle between two persons, in
which that man fell to the ground. Now I hope to get more news.
I have just come out after a bath and am writing this.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANIBEHN PATEL
NEW DELHI
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 142
