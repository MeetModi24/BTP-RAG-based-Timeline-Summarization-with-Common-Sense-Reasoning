553. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
October 3, 1945
CHI. JAMNA,
Why have you gone so weak? If you try a change of air
here, it may help.
Kanaiyo has come back after finishing his work in Bombay.
At present Santok, Radha, Keshu and his wife have come. I am
not writing a separate letter to Purushottam.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
