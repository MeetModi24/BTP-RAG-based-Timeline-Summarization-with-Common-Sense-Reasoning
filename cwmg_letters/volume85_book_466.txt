466. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL GANDHI
DELHI,
September 24, 1946
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
I have both your letters. I got the cheque with the second
one. I hope to be in Wardha on the 2nd October. I am glad
that you and Manu will be going there. It is good news that
you have got well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati : M.M.U./III
