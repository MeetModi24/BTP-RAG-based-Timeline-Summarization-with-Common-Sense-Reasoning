299. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
SEVAGRAM,
February 27, 1945
CHI. SITA,
We are poor. We want to live with the poor, hence we
should learn to put up with sun and rain. But put up with
only as much as you can. Don't ruin your health. I have just
returned from a meeting of the Hindustani [Prachar] Sabha;
hence I happened to start the letter in the Devanagari script.
Blessings to all.
BAPU
SITA GANDHI
C/O NANABHAI MASHRUWALA
AKOLA
