507. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
March 22, 1942
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
The accompanying is for your information. I have given no
reply.
You must have got your teeth treated. I am also eager to
know about the yogi.
Acharya is improving very rapidly. He even went out for a
walk today. His digestion is improving.
It is getting hotter.
Mahadev and Vanu must get well.
As for news, it is for
you to give me some.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, pp. 271-
