488. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
BOMBAY,
September 24, 1945
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
I have your letter . You should act in the case of Shastriji
along the lines you have indicated regarding Vinoba.
It is sad about Kanchanbehn. Also about Dr. Mahodaya.
I am getting ready to catch the train.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 4530
