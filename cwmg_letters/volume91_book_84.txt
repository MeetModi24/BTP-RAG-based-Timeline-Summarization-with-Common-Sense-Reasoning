84. LETTER TO G. B. VEERASWAMY
AHMEDABAD,
May 30, 1915
DEAR MR. VEERASWAMI,
You may come here at any time you like and you will stay
if we mutually agree. . . .1 now nothing is obligatory. English
books are stocked here in a large quantity and newspapers are
also received. Those who are in the Institution do write letters
and stamps or post-cards are provided for them. The rest can
be discussed personally if you decide to come. Servants of India
Society may not admit you as you are only a matriculate.
Yours truly,
GANDHI
From a photostat: C.W. 11015. Courtesy: Balachandran
