269. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
SEGAON,
October 7, 1939
MY DEAR AMRIT,
I missed you last night. But I was glad to learn that you had
gone to fulfil your mission. You will keep your health in proper
order and condition. I found the papers in extraordinarily good
condition. You will leave there on 11th instant and report your-
self in Segaon on 13th. Of course a car will await you at the
station.
More from Mahadev.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3660. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6469
