284. LETTER TO SHARDA G. CHOKHAWALA
BARDOLI,
January 8, 1942
CHI. BABUDI,
I have your letter. Yes, since Bombay agrees with you it will
be good if you go there. Weakness and cough must not persist. It
would never do if they become chronic. Come over to Sevagram
when I return from Kashi. You may come even earlier. But it is
true that without Shakaribehn you will take no rest.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 10042.
Courtesy: Shardabehn G.
Chokhawala
