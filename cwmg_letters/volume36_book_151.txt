151. LETTER TO FRANZ RONO
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
March 21, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I can only say to the youth that
they should turn their tremendous energy of youth through
spending it in sacred service but not dissipate it through speeches
and writings and the like which are becoming so much the fashion
nowadays.
Yours sincerely,
From a photostat: S.N. 14265
