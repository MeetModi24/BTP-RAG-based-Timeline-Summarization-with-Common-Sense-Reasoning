172. LETTER TO TARABEHN N. MASHRUWALA
December 18, 1936
CHI. TARI,
I feel like writing: 'How shall I make you understand?'
Will you wake up only when your condition becomes utterly
hopeless? To improve your health you should make up your
mind to get well at least to the extent of two annas in a rupee.
Why do you lose hope? If you are giving much help there, then
do stay on. I will not say anything.
Tell Nanabhai that Kishorelal has taken the responsibility of
dealing with his long letter, so I have not written anything to him.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI TARABEHN MASHRUWALA
AKOLA
BERAR
