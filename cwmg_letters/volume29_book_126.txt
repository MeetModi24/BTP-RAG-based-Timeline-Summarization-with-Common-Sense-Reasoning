126. LETTER TO VITHTHALDAS JERAJANI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday1, January 11, 1926
BHAI VITHTHALDAS,
I have received a complaint that the khadi from Tirupur is
deteriorating day by day and malpractices there are on the in-
crease. You know everything about this khadi. So please let me
know what your experience is.
I read your letter to Narandas and had a good laugh. I do
think there is no truth whatever in the matter you talk about. And
if there is, it can be considered a very good thing from a certain
point of view.
Vandemataram from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9761
