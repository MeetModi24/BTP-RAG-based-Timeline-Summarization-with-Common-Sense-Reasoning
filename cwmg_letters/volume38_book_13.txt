13. LETTER TO V. K. U. MENON
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
November 3, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I am glad you are helping Krishnaswami.
I know Kurur Nilakantan Nambudripad. He is a good man.
But I have no knowledge of his business ability, nor am I able to
judge the selection of a place. Generally speaking, work like that
done in Bardoli can be best done in British India proper. But I
would ask you to correspond with Sjt. Rajagopalachariar, Tiru-
chengodu, South India, and be guided by him.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. V. K. U. MENON
10 SATTAR BUILDINGS, MAHIM, BOMBAY
