62. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
April 5, 1937
BAPA,
Here is a telegram from Ghanshyamdas. What is this?
Whatever it is, get it clarified. Probably Ghanshyamdas has
already done so.
You are transferring the responsibility regarding Bharuch
to me, but I do not mind. I am not the person who will feel
ashamed of anything. I do not know, though, what I shall be
able to do. I will have to consider how far my intervention
would be proper.
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1176
