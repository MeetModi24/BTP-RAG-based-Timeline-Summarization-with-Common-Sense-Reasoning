367. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
SEGAON,
August 18, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
You are quite lazy in writing letters. That will not do. Have
you not promised to write every third day? One must keep
one's promise even unto death.
Pyarelal's typhoid still persists. In Segaon the cholera is
raging. We can do nothing but wait and see.
I am observing
complete silence these days. That is the only way in which I
can preserve my peace of mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
