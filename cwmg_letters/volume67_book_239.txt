239. LETTER TO VIJAYA N. PATEL
SEGAON,
July 18, 1938
CHI. VIJAYA,
You must have reached there safely. I hope the journey was
comfortable. Ba's verandah looks deserted without you. Sushila
seems to have lost her occupation. Remember that crying is
prohibited. I am awaiting your letter.
Sushila and Arun
arrived yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI VIJAYABEHN
C/O NARANBHAI VALLABHBHAI
VAROD, via BARDOLI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7088.
Also C.W. 4580.
Courtesy: Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
