438. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
April 20, 1934
BHAI GHANSHYAMDAS,
I have fully read your letter to Amrita Bazar Patrika. I
liked it immensely. It should be sent privately to the editor.
If he so wishes he may publish only the relevant portion withholding
your name and the personal element in it. If he does not want
to, he may withhold it altogether.
I hope you are keeping well and taking proper exercise.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Hindi: C.W. 7952. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
