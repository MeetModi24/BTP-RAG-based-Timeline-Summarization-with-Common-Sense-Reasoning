91. LETTER TO DR. PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
POONA,
July 30, 1946
BHAI PATTABHI,
Your Hindi letter is very good. If you go on like this,
you will write Hindi better than English. Your pleading is un-
necessary for your sincerity is beyond doubt. Now forget all about
it. Immerse yourself in khadi work and become of steadfast
mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. PATTABHI SITARAMAYYA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
