180. LETTER TO NIRMALANANDA
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
November 12, 1940
DEAR NIRMALANANDA 1 ,
There is no fasting just now. If it comes, no one will be allowed
to join me. But what you suggest may be adopted after I am
gone. If I survive, I shall issue instructions about the future.
You should, therefore, qualify yourself by further discipline and
hold yourself in patience.
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1393
