194. LETTER TO MIRABEHN
SEGAON,
July 19, 1936
CHI. MIRA,
You need not attempt to come here for the statement.
There is no hurry. I cannot even give time, today being Hari-
jan editing day. And of course you were right in postponing the
statement to the plants which in village life take the prior
place.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 6356. Courtesy: Mirabehn. Also G.N. 9822
