293. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
July 26, 1941
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have given you full freedom. Only thus will you become steady
one day. The notion that there is no freedom here is but a
product of your own imagination. The mind is the sole cause
both of our bondage and our freedom. You will be calm only
when you stop thinking altogether and devote yourself exclusively
and ceaselessly to work, be it in Sevagram or on the top of
the Himalayas-only, he who climbs the top of a mountain is
in danger of falling. There is no such danger in a place on the
plains, like Sevagram.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8495. Also C.W. 7150. Cour-
tesy: Munnalal G. Shah
