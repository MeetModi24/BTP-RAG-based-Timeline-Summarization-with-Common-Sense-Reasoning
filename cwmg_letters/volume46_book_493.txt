493. LETTER TO A. Y. C. WULFSE
As AT SABARMATI,
June 17, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter.
I return the ring herewith.
I hope that you will succeed in your desire to create a work
of art that will benefit mankind.
I hope also that you have regained your health.
I got the two portraits for which too I thank you.
Yours sincerely,
A. Y. C. WULFSE, ESQ.
AMSTERDAM, HOLLAND
From a photostat: S.N. 17301
