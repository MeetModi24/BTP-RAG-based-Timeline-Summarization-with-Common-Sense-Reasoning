149. LETTER TO MULKRAJ
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 26, 1939
DEAR LALA MULKRAJ,
I had a wire from Shri Mookerjee about Punjab National.
I have not replied as I have sent you the papers about the Nagpur
Bank. There is now no question of banking the money elsewhere.
I hope therefore that you have given effect to my instructions.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
