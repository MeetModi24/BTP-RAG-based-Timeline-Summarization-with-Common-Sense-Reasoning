87. LETTER TO DEV PRAKASH NAYYAR
CALCUTTA,
August 21, 1947
CHI. DEV,
I don't see the difficulty Jivanji has pointed out in respect
of the article "My Neighbours". I sent for the book and read it.
As far as I understand the English language, the improvement
you suggested has no place in it. If Jivanji could suggest some
other improvement I might understand his point.
I have sent replies to the other questions with Shah Nawaz.
Do write to me whatever you wish. Never mind if I am not
able to reply promptly although I shall try to do so.
I got up at 2.30 and am now writing this. It is 3.30 by
the Calcutta standard time.
It is undecided where I shall go. But the talk is going on
about my leaving soon for the Punjab.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
