305. LETTER TO VIDYA R. PATEL
November 26, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
How are you now? Are your monthly periods normal? Do
you pass normal stools? Do you get any discharges in urine?
Do you hear from Raojibhai? What is your diet?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 9635. Courtesy: Ravindra R. Patel
