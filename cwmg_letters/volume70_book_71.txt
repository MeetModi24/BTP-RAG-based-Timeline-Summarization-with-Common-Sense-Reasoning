71. LETTER TO RAKHAL
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 5, 1939
BHAI RAKHAL,
I was happy that you came to Segaon and stayed here for
some days. I wish that the faith in truth and ahimsa, which has
grown in you, may also grow in the hearts of all revolutionary
brothers and sisters. Those whom I met gave me this assurance.
I know that we will never be able to achieve independence without
truth, ahimsa and so on.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 4667
