349. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS THAKURDAS
September 13, 1936
DEAR SIR PURUSHOTTAMDAS,
You will please explain to the South African delegation my
sincere regret over my inability to welcome them in person.
Apart from convalescence it is difficult for me to tear myself
away from my new home Segaon. I pray to God that this
visit may result in removing cobwebs of misunderstanding and
bringing the two countries closer together.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
