197. LETTER TO MAITRI GIRI
February 2, 1931
CHI. MAITRI,
You have acted as befits the brave daughter of a brave father
and brought honour to the Ashram. Do not nurse the slightest ill
will against the person who beat you. I hope you don't feel any
pain now. Write to me. Most probably I shall soon arrive there.
Blessings from
BAPU
