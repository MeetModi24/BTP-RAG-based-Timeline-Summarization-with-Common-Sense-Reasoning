97. LETTER TO VASANTLAL MORARKA
SEVAGRAM,
January 22, 1945
BHAI VASANTLAL,
Do you want my blessing for February 111? Anything done
for Jamnalalji has my blessing. Do whatever you can yourself
or get it done by others.
Blessings from
BAPU
VASANTLAL MORARKA
NAVAJIVAN
SANGH
1321 HARRISON ROAD
CALCUTTA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
