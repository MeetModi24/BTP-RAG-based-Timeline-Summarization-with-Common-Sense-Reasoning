95. LETTER TO RAMNARAYAN CHOWDHARY
July 30, 1945
BHAI RAMNARAYAN,
Doing only writing work and neglecting the cow does not
appeal to me. But whether it appeals to me or not is irrelevant.
Your staying here is a difficult problem. There are people
living here who cook their food separately and so on. I do not
want to increase their number. If you cannot lead an ashram
life then what is the use of living in the Ashram? You may stay
in the Khadi Vidyalaya if Jajuji will have you. If you want
to do the work of propagating Hindustani you should speak to
Kaka, or to Shyamlal if you want to work in the Kasturba
Memorial Trust. You have a great deal of ability. You can
earn Rs. 150 from anywhere. I can think of engaging you for
only one work. Nor will Jamnalalji's spirit allow me to think
of any other. Think of what you want to do. Talk to me or
write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
