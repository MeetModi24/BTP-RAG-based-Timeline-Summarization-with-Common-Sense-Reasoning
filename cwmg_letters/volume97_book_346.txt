346. LETTER TO SUMITRA GANDHI
[SODEPUR,
January 14, 1946]
CHI. SUMI,
I will not reply separately. How can I spare the time?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SUMITRA GANDHI
BIRLA GIRLS' SCHOOL
PILANI, JAIPUR STATE
