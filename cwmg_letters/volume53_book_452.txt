452. LETTER TO BHAGWANDAS
February 18, 1933
DEAR BABOOJI,
I have your letter of 13th instant. The reversion to the
longer style of addressing was purely mechanical without thinking
that I had taken to the more homely style. You will please keep me
to it if in the hurry of dictating correspondence I repeat the mistake.
I have already written to Ghanshyamdas about his promised
contribution. I have no doubt that it will come if it has not
already. He might have forgotten it. I will no longer belabour
the point about the necessity of newspapers being self-supporting.
I observe that you have sterilized my argument through your
great soul theory; only this soul had no greatness thrust upon
him when he developed and reduced to practice the proposition.
I am deeply grieved to have the disturbing news about Shiva-
prasad's health and the death of his grandson through accident.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: S.N. 20274
