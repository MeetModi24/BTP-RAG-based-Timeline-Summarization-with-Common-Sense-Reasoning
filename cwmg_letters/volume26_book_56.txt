56. LETTER TO RAMESHWARDAS BIRLA
SABARMATI,
Magh Krishna 4 [February 11, 1925]
BHAI RAMESHWARDASJI,
Your letter. Jamnalalji is here at present. He tells me
that Rs. 10,000 have been received at his firm. I shall use the
sum for the service of the Antyajas.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[PS.]
Glad to know that you are quite well.
From the Hindi original: C.W. 6104. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
