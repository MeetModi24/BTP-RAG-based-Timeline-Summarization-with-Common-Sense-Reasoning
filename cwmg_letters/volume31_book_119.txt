119. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
July 8, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
Enclosed is from Mr. Ambalal sent to me in reply to my
letter. Jamnalalji told me that he sent you all it was possible for
him to send. I have heard nothing yet from Mr. Birla. I notice
subscriptions are coming in slowly.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. One letter and one cheque for Rs. 200/-
From a microfilm: S.N. 19658
