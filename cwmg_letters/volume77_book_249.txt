249. LETTER TO MIR MUSHTAQ AHMED
July 11, 1944
DEAR MIR SAHIB,
I am clear that the conditions are derogatory and unaccept-
able.
Detenus who decline to avail of facilities with humiliating
restrictions do well.
But my opinion carries no authority.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MIR MUSHTAQ AHMED SAHEB
34 PREM HOUSE
CONNAUGHT PLACE
NEW DELHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
