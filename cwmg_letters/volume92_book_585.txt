585. LETTER TO NANIBEHN JHAVERI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
October 2, 1932
CHI. NANIBEHN JHAVERI,
I am resuming writing letters to the inmates of the Ashram
and your name comes first. Do not misinterpret my forgiveness.
Has your brother fully recovered? Make your health perfect.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Manavtana Prahari-Pannalal Jhaveri, p. 27
