531. LETTER TO BALVANTSINHA
June 13, 1945
CHI. BALVANTSINHA,
Your express telegram was unnecessary. I was not late in
replying. Anyway, what has happened has happened.
I am sorry that the trees are dead.
Serve Kishorelalbhai and Gomatibehn. They must recover.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi : G.N. 1963
