32. LETTER TO RAMNAVAMI PRASAD
[MOTIHARI,
November 21, 1917]
BHAISHRI,
I shall leave this place at 10 a.m. on the 23rd. Meet me on
the train at Muzaffarpur. I shall then tell you about the petition.
I see no harm in accepting the fees, if offered. Second school was
opened yesterday.
Bandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi original in Gandhiji's hand: G.N. 735
