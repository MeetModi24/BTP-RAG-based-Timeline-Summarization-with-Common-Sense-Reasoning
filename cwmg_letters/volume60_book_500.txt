500. LETTER TO AMRITLAL V. THAKKAR
March 31, 1935
BHAI THAKKAR BAPA,
I am not observing silence so as to avoid reproaches but to
listen silently to whatever anyone has to say.
Please see whether Sastri alone will be able to manage the
column and also find out whether he and Ganesan will get on.
lf you have doubt about Ganesan's honesty, then by all means
relieve him. Keep him only if you have no doubts about him.
lf the debt is incurred on account of the Congress, we have
got to pay it. lf the drafts are wholly worthless, then take it that
the institution is worthless and you do not have to pay a sin-
gle pie.
I shall be in Indore from the 20th to 24th and after that here.
BAPU
SHRI TH[AKKAR BAPA]
HARIJAN S[EVAK] SANGH
TRICHINOPOLY S. I.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1154
