387. LETTER TO S. R. DESHPANDE
September 6, 1926
DEAR FRIEND,
My heart goes out to you. In a case like yours God alone
can help.
Whether we believe in Him or not does not much
matter even as ignorance of law does not save us from its sanc-
tions. God is the supremest law.
I suggest to you that the purpose of our being is to know
ourselves. The way to know oneself lies through service of all
that lives. And we cannot serve humanity without sacrificing
ourselves. Self-sacrifice is therefore the highest law for us.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. S. R. DESHPANDE
DONGRE MANSIONS
CHIKHALWADI
