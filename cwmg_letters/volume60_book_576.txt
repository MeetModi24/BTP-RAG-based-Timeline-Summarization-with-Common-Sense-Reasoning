576. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
CHI. NARANDAS,
WARDHA,
April 12, 1935
I send herewith a copy of Ramanlal's report. If you can go
to Sabarmati for two or three days and inspect the accounts,
I would feel easy in my mind and also know what I should do.
Is Titus inefficient or dishonest, or is Ramanlal's letter without
basis?
Attend to this immediately if you can.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8438. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
