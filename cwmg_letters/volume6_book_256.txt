256. LETTER TO SIR ROPER LETHBRIDGE
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 26, 1906
DEAR SIR,
I am extremely obliged to you for your letter of the 23rd instant.
I venture to send you herewith a breakfast invitation card as also
the draft constitution of the South Africa British Indian Committee.
If you can join the Committee, your patronage will be valued.
It was an agreeable surprise to me that you were connected with
The Englishman of Calcutta. I may state that the late Mr. Saunders
rendered the most valuable assistance to me when I was in Calcutta
in 1896 and 1901 in connection with British Indians in South Africa;
indeed, he even gave me notes of introduction to Sir Charles Turner
and others and was largely instrumental in evoking from Lord Curzon
a most spirited letter of sympathy regarding the status of British Indians
in South Africa.
I remain,
Yours faithfully,
[Enclosure]
SIR ROPER LETHBRIDGE
199, TEMPLE CHAMBERS
TEMPLE AVENUE, E.C.
