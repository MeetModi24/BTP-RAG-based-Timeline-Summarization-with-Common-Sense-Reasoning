178. LETTER TO GIRDHARLAL T. MODI
BOMBAY,
September 28, 1944
BHAI GIRDHARLAL,
Sushilabehn told me about your illness. I wish very much to
call on you, but you know how helpless I am.
Pyarelalji will tell you more. Get well soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 10213. Courtesy: Jayabehn Modi
