413. LETTER TO AN ENGLISH WOMAN
December 28, 1932
MY DEAR SISTER,
I must dictate if I must write at all. You are pouring your
love on me, not merely showering it. I have your well-marked
book, your telegram, your letters laden with sacred cards, each
bearing some verse or proverb written by you, and your gift of
£6 for Harijans, for all of which God bless you.
You will not expect me to give you long letters. I can only send
you a line to tell you that you are never out of my mind.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 18834
