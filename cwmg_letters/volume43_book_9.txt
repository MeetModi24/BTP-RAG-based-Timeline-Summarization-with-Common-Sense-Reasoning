9. LETTER TO VITHALDAS JERAJANI
March 5/61, 1930
BHAISHRI JERAJANI,
I have your letter. You may go to any place which Shanker-
lal may ask you to visit. Don't think it necessary to obtain my
permission in such matters. When I need you, I will call you
from wherever you are.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9769
