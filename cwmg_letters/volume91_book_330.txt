330. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
Shravan Vad 13 [August 28, 1924]
CHI. MATHURADAS,
I have your letter. It seems our letters have crossed. You
will have received my letter today. This time I shall not run. I
think it will be best to get down at Grant Road only. How
long can one keep running?
Blessings from
BAPU
