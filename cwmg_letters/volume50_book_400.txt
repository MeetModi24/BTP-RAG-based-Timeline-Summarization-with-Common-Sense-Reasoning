400. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
August 19, 1932
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
I got a letter from you after a long interval. Do not worry
if you cannot keep to the prayer hours in the Ashram. Both
Jamna and you have weak health, and if a person cannot get up
early in the morning he should not force himself to do so. The
secret of being able to get up early is to go to bed early.
I think that hip-bath, sun-bath, fruit diet and milk, only as
much as one can digest, would be a sure remedy for anaemia.
Open air also is essential.
The book on health 1 has discussed only the diseases of which
I had some experience.
BAPU
From Gujarati: C.W. 904. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
