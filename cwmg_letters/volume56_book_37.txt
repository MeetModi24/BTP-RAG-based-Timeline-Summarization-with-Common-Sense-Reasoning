37. LETTER TO PARIKSHITLAL L. MAJMUDAR
September 29, 1933
BHAI PARIKSHITLAL,
I got your letter. Jivanlalbhai and Nanabhai will try about
the Ashram in Kathiawar after Diwali.
We will give them
whatever help we can in their efforts. If they can do nothing,
I think it will not be possible for us to maintain the Ashram.
But I am sure that they will not let it die. Whether the ashrams
at Bhavnagar and Vartej should be combined, requires to be con-
sidered.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3996
