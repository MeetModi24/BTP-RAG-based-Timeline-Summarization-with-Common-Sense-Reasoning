435. LETTER TO GANGABEHN JHAVERI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
Silence Day [May 26, 1930]
DEAR GANGABEHN JHAVERI,
[I hope] you and Nanibehn are all right. Now can you stay
alone? I do not write more as it is dark.
Blessings from
BAPU
Mahatma Gandhi: Source Material for a History of the Freedom Movement in India,
Vol. III, Part III, p. 576
