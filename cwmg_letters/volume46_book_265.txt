265. LETTER TO L. R. GURUSWAMY NAIDU
As AT SABARMATI,
May 26, 1931
MY DEAR GURUSWAMY,
I have your letter describing the difficulties of picketing. If
you have correctly described your picketing there seems to me to
be nothing wrong about it and that the action of the autho-
rities was not only contrary to the terms of the Settlement but
in my opinion also illegal. Please continue to give me information
about any further development. I would like you also to keep in
touch with Syt. Rajagopalachari to whom I have sent your letter.
Yours sincerely,
SYT. L. R. GURUSWAMY NAIDU
PRESIDENT, TALUQ CONGRESS COMMITTEE
KOILPATTI (MADRAS PRESIDENCY)
