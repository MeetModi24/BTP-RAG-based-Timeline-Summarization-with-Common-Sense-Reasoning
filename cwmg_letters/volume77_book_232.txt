232. LETTER TO MANU S. MASHRUWALA
PANCHGANI,
[July 6, 1944]
CHI. MANUDI,
Are you offended with me? How long will you remain so?
And is it right for children to feel offended with their parents?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C.W. 5062. Courtesy: Surendra
Mashruwala
