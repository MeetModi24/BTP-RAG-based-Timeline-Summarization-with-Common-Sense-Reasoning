339. LETTER TO V. G. JANARDAN RAO
CALCUTTA,
December 26, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I have transferred it to Sjt. Ghanshyamdas
Birla who will correspond with you directly.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. V. G JANARDAN RAO
GURU RAJA VILAS
SRIRAMPET, MYSORE
