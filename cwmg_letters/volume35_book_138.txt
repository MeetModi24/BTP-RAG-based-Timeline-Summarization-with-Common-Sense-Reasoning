138. LETTER TO PARASHURAM MEHROTRA
Tuesday, November 1, 1927
CHI. PARASRAM,
I have your letter just now. Rajkishori's soul is certainly
at peace. You must have fortitude. May God grant you peace
and faith.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original: C.W. 2972. Courtesy: Parashuram Mehrotra
