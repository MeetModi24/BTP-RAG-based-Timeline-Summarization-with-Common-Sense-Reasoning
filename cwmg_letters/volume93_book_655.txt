655. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
February 2, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Even when you receive a letter from Mahadev, take it as
from me, because when I myself do not find the time, I tell
him to write you a postcard. Anyway, I take care to see that
someone writes to you every day. On two or three days I
could not do even that. I did well not to have replied to that
Swami. We shall laugh over it when we meet. I am absolu-
tely all right. Manu is now having very slight fever.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original: Pyarelal
Papers. Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library. Courtesy: Dr. Sushila Nayyar
