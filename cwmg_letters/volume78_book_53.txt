53. LETTER TO NAGINDAS T. MASTER
August 22, 1944
BHAI NAGINBHAI,
Whatever best you think you should do. I have not thought
over it seriously. If you want to do satyagraha or act against law
you must give previous notice. If you do not act [up] to this,
it shall not be called a non-violent satyagraha. I am not in
favour of [a] mass civil disobedience movement.
From a copy: File No. 3001/H, Commissioner of Police, Bombay
