146. LETTER TO PRABHASHANKAR PATTANI
WARDHA,
Monday [December 13, 1926]
SUJNA BHAISHRI,
You know that Chi. Mathuradas stays in your bungalow at
Panchgani. He has not so far paid any rent. He and I earnestly
desire that something should be paid. I have also told him that
he should vacate the bungalow during the season; they are there-
fore, getting ready to vacate it by the end of February. I wish
you to let me know the amount of the rent. Write to me about
your health.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 3208. Courtesy: Mahesh
Pattani
