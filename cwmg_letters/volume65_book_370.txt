370. LETTER TO J.C. KUMARAPPA
June 22, 1937
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
I have read your essay on democracy and your speech on con-
version. Both are good but you must work out your individualistic
328
cultural democracy. The charge against Hinduism is that it is
too individualistic. I have thought it to be a baseless charge. But
you seem to have some other idea. My own opinion is that
Hinduism has made the greatest research in that line but simply
failed to carry out its discoveries in practice and therefore seemed
to be individualistic, i. e., selfish.
Love.
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 10119
