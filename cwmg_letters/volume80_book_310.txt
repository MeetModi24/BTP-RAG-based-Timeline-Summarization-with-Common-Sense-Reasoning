310. LETTER TO SHEIKH FARID
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 25, 1945
BHAI FARID,
I have gone through your letters. How can I write? Dr.
Patel whom you mention is dead. Now there is one Dr. Jesu-
dasan at the hospital. He is a good man. There is no need of
a letter of introduction to him. You may go there. My son
Manilal is in India these days. May you get well.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy of the Urdu : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
