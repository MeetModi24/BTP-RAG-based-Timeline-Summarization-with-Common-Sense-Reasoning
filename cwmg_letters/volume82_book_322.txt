322. LETTER TO RAM MANOHAR LOHIA
SODEPUR,
KHADI PRATISHTHAN,
December 17, 1945
BHAI RAM MANOHAR,
I am sorry that your father passed away suddenly yesterday.
We used to meet often. I had sent Pyarelalji and Prabhavatibehn
to see his charkha activity. In my view he died in the manner
he wanted. He had been engrossed in his work.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
DR. RAM MANOHAR LOHIA
AGRA
