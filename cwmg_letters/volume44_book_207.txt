207. LETTER TO MADHAVDAS
YERAVDA MANDIR,
September 12, 1930
CHI. MADHAVDAS,
I read in the letter from the Ashram that you went and visit-
ed Ramdas and others. I was glad. How are you? How is
Krishna? Has she recovered her health now? Can you two do
any work in the present movement? Where do you live?
Blessings from
BAPU
