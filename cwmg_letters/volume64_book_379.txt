379. LETTER TO SOHANLAL OBEROI
February 17, 1937
BHAI SOHANLAL,
Telling lies is not less of a sin than lechery. But one who
is
a libertine in secret is guilty of three sins: he falls himself,
he brings about another's fall, and he also lies.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6864
1 A khadi worker of the Punjab
