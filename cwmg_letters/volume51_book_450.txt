450. LETTER TO SHANKAR
October 20, 1932
I am sorry about the objection to the name of Harijan. You
may use any name that your friends like. But do please explain to
them that I had not the remotest idea of thinking of Vishnu or
Shiva. For me the name means 'people of God'. I make no distin-
ction between Vishnu, Shiva or Brahma. They are all names of
God. But let their decision be taken and prevail.
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II, p. 163
