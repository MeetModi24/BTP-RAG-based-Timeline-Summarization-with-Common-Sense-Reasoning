205. LETTER TO RAMABEHN JOSHI
July 14, 1934
CHI. RAMA,
I have not yet been able to persuade any woman [worker] to
go there . I am still trying. Please regard it as your dharma to
stay on till somebody relieves you. Mahadev will arrive here
today. I will know more after he comes.
Write to me sometimes.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5365
