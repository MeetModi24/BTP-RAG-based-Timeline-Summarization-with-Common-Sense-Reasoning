248. LETTER TO N. C. KELKAR
SATYAGRAHA ASHRAM,
September 21 [1920]
DEAR MR. KELKAR 2 ,
May I thank you for the splendid response the nationalists of
Maharashtra are making to the Congress reso[lution]. I am
sure that your great resolve brings swarajya nearer and if Maha-
rashtra will enthusiastically take up all the items outlined in the
n.c.o. the rest of India will follow. And if she does, swaraj
within a year is not unlikely to be a reality. I have sent you a
further amendment of the 1st article of the Congress constitution
which I hope you have received. I would very much like to see
our draft in print if it is possible even before the All-India Com-
mittee meets. If you think that any minor details are left out
they can be incorporated when the Committee consider the draft.
If you generally approve of the draft and the report will you
please telegraph?
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat: S.N. 7264
