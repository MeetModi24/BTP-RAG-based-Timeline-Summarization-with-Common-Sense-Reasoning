398. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 11, 1940
CHI. PRABHA,
I have your letter. I am glad that you have come to like the
place. Take care of your health. Rajendra Babu has recovered. I
am quite well.
I am writing to Jayaprakash.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3539
