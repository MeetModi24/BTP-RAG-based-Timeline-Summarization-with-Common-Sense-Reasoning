430. LETTER TO VIDYA HINGORANI
August 24, 1933
CHI. VIDYA,
I got news of you from Anand. I hope you are all right
in body and mind. I hope Mahadev is keeping well. How are
the others? Write to me in detail.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Hindi. Courtesy: National Archives of India
and Anand T. Hingorani
