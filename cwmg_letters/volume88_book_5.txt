5. LETTER TO JIVANJI D. DESAI
DELHI,
May 25, 1947
CHI. JIVANJI,
It is nearing 10 now. I finished the Hindi in Patna, the
English on the train, and have just finished the Gujarati. They
will go by air-mail tomorrow and you should, therefore, get
them on Monday. That is what you wanted.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I have not revised the Gujarati. I have revised one English
item. Please revise the rest there.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G. N. 9977. Also C. W. 6951.
Courtesy: Jivanji D. Desai
