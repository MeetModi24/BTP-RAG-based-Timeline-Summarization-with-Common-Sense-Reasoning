64. LETTER TO SARASWATI GANDHI
October 27, 1946
CHI. SARU (SARASWATI),
I am able to write only this much while preparing to go
to Calcutta. I got your letter. Kanti is doing well. You too
are growing wise. May you both make progress, enjoy long
lives and always render service.
Blessings to all three of you from
BAPU
SHRI KANTI GANDHI
BANGALORE
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
