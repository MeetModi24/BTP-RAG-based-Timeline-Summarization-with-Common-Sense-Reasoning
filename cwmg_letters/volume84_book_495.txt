495. LETTER TO THANMAL SOUCHETI
POONA,
July 3, 1946
CHI. THANMAL,
I got the sad news of Sheth Pratapmal's death. May every-
one get a father like yours. He made such sacrifices and yet
how silently he rendered service! May you grow as selfless as
he was and continue his tradition.
Blessings from
BAPU
THANMAL SOUCHETI
C/O SJT. CHANGNIRAM PRATAPMAL
SOJAT ROAD, MARWAR
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
