470. LETTER TO S. D. SATAVLEKAR
August 28, 1932
BHAI SATAVLEKAR,
I feel somewhat anxious as I have sent you three letters
but have received no reply.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
[PS.]
In one of the letters I have made a request for another set
of the Sanskrit Readers.
From Hindi: C.W. 4765. Courtesy: S. D. Satavlekar
