492. LETTER TO AN ENGLISH GIRL
January 5, 1933
MY DEAR DAUGHTER,
I was very pleased to get your letter. You are quite right
in addressing me as you have done. I enjoy the happiness of hav-
ing thousands of daughters. You are a welcome addition to the
ever-growing family; and since I, a puny mortal, cannot cope
with such a large family, I entrust you all to the safe keeping
of the All-powerful and Eternal Father, and so I never feel the
burden of having the large family; on the contrary, only the
joy of possessing the trust remains.
I see that though you are an invalid, you have a strong
faith and a great heart. May your faith increase with the pro-
gress of years and may you ever find peace in the midst of pain
and sorrow.
Yes, I knew when I was fasting I had the prayers of many
pure souls like yourself, and that knowledge sustained me greatly.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat No. 13. Courtesy: National Archives of India. Also My
Dear Child, p. 123
