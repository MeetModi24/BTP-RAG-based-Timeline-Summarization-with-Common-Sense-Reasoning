44. LETTER TO MRIDULA SARABHAI
SEVAGRAM,
August 24, 1941
CHI. MRIDU,
I had your letter. The figs arrived today. They seem to be good.
I shall give you the corrected draft when you come. It is a matter
of saving time. Mummy I hope is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : C. W. 11215. Courtesy : Sarabhai Foundation
