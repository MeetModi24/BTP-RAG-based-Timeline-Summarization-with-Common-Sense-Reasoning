259. LETTER TO ATMARAM
SODEPUR,
December 8, 1945
BHAI ATMARAM,
It is certainly a bad thing that during prayer undesirable
thoughts come. But you should not worry. Take any sloka of
the prayer and concentrate on it. If even this is not possible
repeat Ramanama from the depths of your heart with such
concentration that there is no room for any other thought. It
is no doubt difficult but it can be done. Such has been my
experience and the experience of other people as well. That is
why Ramanama is said to be the panacea for all ills.
