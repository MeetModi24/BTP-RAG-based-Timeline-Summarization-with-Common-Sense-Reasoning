621. LETTER TO HOMI TALEYARKHAN
"BIRLA HOUSE",
MALABAR HILL, BOMBAY,
April 10, 1945
MY DEAR HOMI,
I am washed out and there is plenty of 'planned' work in
front of me. Hence is it not the duty of friends like you to
save every minute of mine? Really I should not have to write
even this note. Karanjia has written to me too. Both of you
should have patience and it may be rewarded.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI HOMI TALEYARKHAN
AHMED MANZIL
WARDEN ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
