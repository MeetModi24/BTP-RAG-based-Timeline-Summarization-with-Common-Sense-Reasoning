117. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
January 14, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I have your long letter. Since you have stayed on there of
your own free will, what can I say? Personally, I don't like your
decision at all. There will always be something or the other in a
home requiring one's presence to attend to. I fear the longer you
stay on there the greater will be the delay in your recovery. From
what source did you arrange to get the money to pay your rent?
Whenever you happen to be in such difficulties about money, you
may write to the Ashram. My daughter is a daughter of the
Ashram. You have, therefore, every right to request and get money
from there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3395
