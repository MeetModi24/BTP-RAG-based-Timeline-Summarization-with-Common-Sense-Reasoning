521. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
NANDI DURG,
May 25, 1936
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I was delighted to get your letter. Krishnaswami must be
happy now. Your daughter-in-law too is happy, I hope. You
must be keeping well, physically as well as mentally. Your
parents too must be doing fine.
The climate here is friendly. Sardar takes plenty of walks
daily. The others too are all right and so am I.
Yes, we shall be in Bangalore for a fortnight, going there on
the 31st instant. It will be good if [you] too can come. We
have to go to Wardha via Madras only.
Kamala wants to spend two or three days with me. I have
sent my consent.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Tell me if you can read the above with ease. There is Mrs.
Esther Menon living there in Shamrock Cottage. See her if you
can on my behalf and befriend her.
From a photostat of the Hindi: C.W. 9607. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
