152. LETTER TO GIRWARDHAR
SWARAJ ASHRAM, BARDOLI,
August 4, 1928
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your booklet . I do not know whether I shall
ever get the time to read it.
1. There are no courtyards specially reserved for women in
these parts as in Bihar.
2. Though there is no special courtyard or room reserved,
males do not have free access to the females.
3. Women at public meetings have special enclosures reserved
for them, as a rule.
4. Many educated women do dislike manual labour.
Yours sincerely,
From a microfilm: S.N. 13902
