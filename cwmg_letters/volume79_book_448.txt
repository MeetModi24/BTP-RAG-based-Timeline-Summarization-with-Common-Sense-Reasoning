448. LETTER TO FRANY TALYARKHAN
SEVAGRAM,
March 18, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I got your yearly bulletin. I hope you do not expect me to
read it. I just turned the pages for a few minutes.
Blessings from
BAPU
FRANY TALYARKHAN
SHAHARUKH SABAWALA
SIR P. M. ROAD
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
