580. LETTER TO ANASUYABEHN SARABHAI
NANDI,
Tuesday [On or after May 17, 1927]
CHI. ANASUYABEHN,
I had desisted from replying to your last letter hoping that
you would soon be arriving here. Meanwhile however I have
had letters from Saklatwala. Hence this letter to you. I also
enclose his letters as well as a copy of my letter to him.
My health is certainly good. The doctors say my blood-
pressure is a little low. It has certainly not gone up.
Rajaji arrived here today. I take it you will now be arriv-
ing soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: S.N. 32786
