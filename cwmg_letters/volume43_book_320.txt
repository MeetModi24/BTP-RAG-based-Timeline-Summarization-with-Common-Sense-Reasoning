320. LETTER TO DOROTHY D'SEUA
CAMP KARADI,
April 25, 1930
DEAR LITTLE FRIEND,
Don't you be afraid of the doings of the processionists. They
mean no harm to you whatsoever and if you believe in God why
should you fear anything or anybody. However I shall do what-
ever may be necessary.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MISS DOROTHY D'SEUA
No. 47 GOUGH ROAD
AGRA (U. P.)
From a photostat: G.N. 1369
