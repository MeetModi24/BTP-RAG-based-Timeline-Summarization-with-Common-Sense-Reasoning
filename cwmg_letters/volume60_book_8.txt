8. LETTER TO VALLABHBHAI PATEL
December 17, 1934
BHAI VALLABHBHAI,
I got your letter. I am not surprised that the lawyers did
not like Khan Saheb's statement. We should be satisfied if our
lawyers approved of it. It was the only one which could serve
our purpose. The Government is not likely to understand any-
thing at present.
Deenabandhu is arriving today and we shall know from
him what happened.
I think Jamnalalji will leave here on Thursday. Please do
stay on there till then at any rate.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro‒2: Sardar Vallabhbhaine, pp. 145-
