200. LETTER TO HARI SINGH GAUR
October 11, 1934
DEAR SIR HARI SINGH GOUR,
I have your letter. Whatever my personal opinion may be,
so long as I remain a member of the Congress I may not go against
the definite policy and programme of the Congress. Therefore,
you must excuse me for my utter inability to give you my personal
support in your candidature.
Thank you for your inquiry about my health. It is quite
good.
Yours sincerely,
SIR HARI SINGH GAUR
NAGPUR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
