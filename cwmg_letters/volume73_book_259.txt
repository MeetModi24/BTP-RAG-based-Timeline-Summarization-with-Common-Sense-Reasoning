259. LETTER TO VIJAYABEHN M. PANCHOLI
December 9, 1940
CHI. VIJAYA,
I got your letter. When I don't write anything, you may take
it that my health is all right. Do not believe newspaper reports.
Yes, I do feel exhausted and so I am taking a little rest. Amtul
Salaam is here. She will go to Sind again. Manu has arrived.
There are many others. The Ashram is full. Pyarelal will court
imprisonment tomorrow.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7135. Also C.W. 4627. Courtesy:
Vijayabehn M. Pancholi
