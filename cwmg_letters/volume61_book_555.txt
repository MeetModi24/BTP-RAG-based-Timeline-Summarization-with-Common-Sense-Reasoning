555. LETTER TO KRISHNACHANDRA
WARDHA,
3 a.m., September 3, 1935
CHI. KRISHNACHANDRA,
You will experience supreme bliss and of course realize the
presence of God when, faithfully believing that He is witness
to every act, you dedicate all your deeds, thoughts and speech to
Him. Remember, "Whatever you do, whatever you eat", etc.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 4276
