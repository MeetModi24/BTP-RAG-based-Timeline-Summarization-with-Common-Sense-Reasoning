431. LETTER TO NARANDAS GANDHI
SEGAON,
August 31, 1938
CHI. NARANDAS,
Please find enclosed a cheque from Gokaldas. Send him a
receipt from there. I have not written to him from here. I
hope Jamna is calm.
What is the news about Harakhchand?
Manju has not replied to my letter. Can't Purushottam
satisfy her love of music?
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
You have been covering years well. Cover many more and
continue to serve.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./II. Also C.W. 8547. Courtesy:
Narandas Gandhi
