498. LETTER TO MANU GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 5, 1930
CHI. MANUDI,
I got your letter. Since you are learning lathi exercises, I
shall have to learn them from you. A letter in a word should
not be split from the rest of the word. If there is not sufficient
space at the end of a line to complete a word, you should leave
the available space blank. The correct word is not nabadai but
nabalai. When the 'u' sound is short, it should be indicated by
the sign ~ and not by c.... For the present, pay attention to
correctness in handwriting and not to flourishes. If it is correct,
flourishes will follow without any effort on your part.
Blessings from
BAPU
