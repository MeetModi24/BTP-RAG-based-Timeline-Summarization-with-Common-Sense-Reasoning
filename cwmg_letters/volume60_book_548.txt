548. LETTER TO BHAGWANJI P. PANDYA
April 7, 1935
CHI. BHAGWANJI,
Show the accompanying to Balvantsinha.
Carry out my
suggestion and end the bickerings. Look into Harilal's complaint
regarding milk, etc. He seems to have a great deal to say.
Letters for the family are enclosed.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 385. Courtesy: Bhagwanji P. Pandya
