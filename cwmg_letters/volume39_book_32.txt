32. LETTER TO BALI VORA
Saturday [February 9, 1929]
CHI. BALI,
It is good you went there. To you, Rasik was as dear as
life. I can understand that, among us all, you will suffer the
most. But I know that you are wise. Keep up courage. Console
yourself with the thought that nothing was wanting in the care
of Rasik. I do not write separately to Kumi. We shall meet
at Delhi on the 17th, if you have not already left.
Blessings from
BAPU
