226. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
April 11, 1947
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
I have gone through your papers. You should not have
undertaken the fast without my consent. It is good that you
have broken it. You had better do only such work as Shanta-
behn lets you do. More if we meet any time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9035. Also C. W. 9159. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
