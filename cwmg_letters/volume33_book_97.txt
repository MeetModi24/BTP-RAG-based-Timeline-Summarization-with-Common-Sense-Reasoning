97. LETTER TO SATIS CHANDRA DAS GUPTA
[After February 19, 1927]
DEAR SATIS BABU,
I have your pathetic letter which I see you wrote to lighten
your burden. Gods, it is said, take away those they love most. The
human mind has devised many methods of assuaging grief. But
the real fact is not to feel the grief. But that is almost superhuman
especially in Hindu society. Please tell Hemaprabha Devi not to
think of what Anil would have done, if he had lived. His work in
that body was finished. A rare soul such as Anil's had much higher
work to do and he left the body. Let us not grieve over our loss
which may be the world's gain. It is as well that we do not
know the mysteries of life and death. I want you both now to
get rid of your weakness. Pray do not think of stirring till you
have fully recuperated yourself.
With love,
Yours,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 1634
