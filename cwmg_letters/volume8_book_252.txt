252. LETTER TO H. L. PAUL
JOHANNESBURG,
August 4, 1908
DEAR MR. PAUL,
I have your note of the 30th ultimo. I have sent Joseph £20.
It is not possible for me to collect any more or to send more. Every
penny is now wanted for the struggle.
With kind regards all round,
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a typewritten copy of the original : C.W. 4549. Courtesy: E. J. Paul
