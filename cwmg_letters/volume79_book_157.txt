157. LETTER TO KAMALESH
SEVAGRAM,
February 2, 1945
BHAI KAMALESH,
I have your letter. I can't help it if my article has caused a
commotion. I am not satisfied with your work. I do not wish
to go into the reasons. The Rashtrabhasha Sammelan is narrow
in its scope since it does not teach Urdu. Had Urdu continued
to enjoy the place which it deserves and which I tried to
give it, the propagation of Hindustani would have become
redundant. To me this is self-evident.
But why should I
argue? If everyone does what he likes where is the room for
contention?
Blessings from
BAPU
KAMALESH
GU[JARAT] PRA[DESH] RA[SHTRABHASHA] PRACHAR SAMITI
KHADIA, AHMEDABAD
