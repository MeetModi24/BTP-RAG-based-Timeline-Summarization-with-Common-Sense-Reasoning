38. LETTER TO RAMJIBHAI
SEVAGRAM,
January 11, 1945
BHAI RAMJI,
Owing to my illness I could not answer your letter sooner.
Your resentment is not justified. You are an indefatigable wor-
ker; therefore, you will certainly go on working and that is good.
Still I am sending you the frank letter from Chi. Krishnachandra.
You may come if you feel like it after you have read the letter.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAMJIBHAI
