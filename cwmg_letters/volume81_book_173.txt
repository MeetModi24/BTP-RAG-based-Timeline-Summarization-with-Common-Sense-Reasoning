173. LETTER TO ARUNA ASAF ALI
SEVAGRAM,
August 12, 1945
DEAR ARUNA 2 ,
The distress is of your own imagination. I fear that the
Maulana's letter was of my doing. There was no question of
isolating you from the rest or of expecting you to suppress any-
thing. You were represented as being very ill and so there was
a mention of your illness and special urgency of your release.
Asaf had no hand in it. So far as I know he came to know of
the letter after it was dispatched. Will you not give him and to
everyone else the liberty of free thought that you claim for
yourself? I hope you are well.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: Aruna Asaf Ali Papers. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
