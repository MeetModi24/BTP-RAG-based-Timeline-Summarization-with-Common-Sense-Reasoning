369. LETTER TO DR. MARTIN
March 7, 1935
DEAR DR. MARTIN,
I am thankful to you for your letter giving all the informa-
tion about the Pendra Road Sanatorium. You may have come
to know that I was recently in Nagpur. I am sorry I had not a
moment left to visit your Hospital.
Yours sincerely,
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
