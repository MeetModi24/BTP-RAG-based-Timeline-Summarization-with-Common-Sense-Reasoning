177. LETTER TO MRS. FREETH
[HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON,]
November 14, 1906
DEAR MRS. FREETH,
I am exceedingly sorry that I shall not be able to be with you on
Sunday evening. If you are free some other evening next week I should
like to accept it provisionally.
I send you the photograph I promised. To Mrs. Gandhi's right
is the only son of my widowed sister.
Yours sincerely,
[Enclosure]
MRS. FREETH
48, FINCHLEY ROAD, N.
From the typewritten office copy: S.N. 4568
