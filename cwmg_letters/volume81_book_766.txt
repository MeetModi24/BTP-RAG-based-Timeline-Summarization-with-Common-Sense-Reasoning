766. LETTER TO JATINDAS M. AMIN
October 27, 1945
CHI. AMIN,
You don't know how much anxiety you are causing to all
of us. Your dharma is to admit that you yourself are suffering
from illusion instead of saying that others have turned against
you. You should not go to any shop or ask for money and
spend it. If you lose your temper all the time, you had better
leave the Ashram. Come here if you feel like it. You are not
in a position to go to the Himalayas. Your place is either near
your father or near me. Do what you think fit after thinking
over all this. At any rate, leave the Ashram.
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
