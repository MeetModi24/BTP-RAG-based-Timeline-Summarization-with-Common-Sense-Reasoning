169. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL V. SHAH
SEVAGRAM,
June 14, 1941
BHAI CHIMANLAL,
Received your letter. Do defend yourself by all legal
means. Don't submit. Let them fine you if they choose. Under-
go imprisonment if they offer it. Let the press be closed down
if there is no one to run it after you go. Or let the magazine
run only as a newspaper. If that too is not possible, then let
things take their own course.
I will only insist that you
should not do anything under pressure. But since Munshiji is
there, there is no need to consult me. Be guided by his advice.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
