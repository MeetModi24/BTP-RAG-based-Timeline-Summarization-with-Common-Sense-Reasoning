32. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
August 8, 1937
CHI. MAHADEV,
I think I will have to send you to Dhulia for a day. Get
ready soon. I am returning your article with this. I have de-
leted the paragraph about khadi. Try to understand the reason.
If you can't, ask me when I am free.
I don't know when I shall be able to reply to the letters.
Send the correspondence regarding Nariman to Jawahar. Or
make a short summary of it.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11545
