391. LETTER TO DHAN GOPAL MUKERJI
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
May 28, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your kind letter with your subscription for Young India.
If you do not get it regularly, please report it to the manager
and let me have a line also.
Please do not have any anxiety about my health. I am tak-
ing all the necessary care and am giving myself as much rest as
it is possible to give.
Yours sincerely,
DHAN GOPAL MUKERJI, ESQ.
THE TOWN HALL CLUB
123, WEST 43RD STREET
NEW YORK
From a photostat: S.N. 12507
