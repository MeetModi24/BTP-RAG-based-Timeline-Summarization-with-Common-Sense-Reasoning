203. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
July 14, 1934
CHI. AMALA,
I am sorry you are not at peace now at Sabarmati. Will you
tell me what you would like to do? It is a terrible thing for
anyone to live under a sense of humiliation. I would like you
therefore to make your choice.
I had hoped that you would be
happy in Sabarmati. One thing you would make up your mind
about. You cannot stay with me, unless I can settle down any-
where for any length of time.
And you won't feel happy in
Wardha in any case. You should be sure also that I do not want
to drive you away from me. On the contrary I want to make
you happy in so far as it is within my power.
Please do not worry about my fast. It would be all right.
You simply cannot understand it and its value. You will, by and
by.
Love.
BAPU
