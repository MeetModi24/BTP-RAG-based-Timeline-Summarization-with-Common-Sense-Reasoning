481. LETTER TO KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
SEVAGRAM,
November 26, 1944
DEAR KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA GARU,
Bapu was delighted to see your letter of 4th instant. He was sorry to learn
that you are not keeping well. You have got to keep well.
He is not anxious to undertake a fast. He is praying for light.
Yours sincerely,
PYARELAL
DESHABHAKTA KONDA VENKATAPPAYYA
GUNTUR
From a photostat : G.N. 3227
