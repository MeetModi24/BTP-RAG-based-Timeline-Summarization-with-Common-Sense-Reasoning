502. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI M. PATEL
September 27, 1945
BHAI DAHYABHAI,
I have your letter. My advice to you is that you should
save the money you would spend on coming here and use it for
service.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI DAHYABHAI PATEL
C/O SHETH JAMNADAS ADKIA
211-13 KALBADEVI
BOMBAY-
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 2714. Courtesy: Dahyabhai H. Patel
