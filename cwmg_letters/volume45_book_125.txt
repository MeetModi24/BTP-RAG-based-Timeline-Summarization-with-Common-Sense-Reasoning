125. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
January 16, 1931
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I have already written to you about the books. Don't worry
about my health on any account. It is certainly good. How old is
Tara? I hope you write the diary regularly. It is my experience
and of many others that doing so helps us to keep a watch
over our truthfulness.
What is the position about Chandubhai's hospital now? For
what purpose is the rest of the building being used? Pyarelal is in
fine health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1817
