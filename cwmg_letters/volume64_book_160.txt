160. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
December 15, 1936
CHI. MAHADEV,
I am sending herewith quite a big lot—Gujarati articles, one
article about Govind's letter, another about Mysore, a telegram
for Kasturbhai and the mail.
I understand about butter. If Dharmadhikari is there he
should come and see me, otherwise anyone who is there.
You may send with the bearer whatever little you wish to.
Ganapat will of course come at noon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11509
