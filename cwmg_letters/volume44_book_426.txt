426. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
November 13, 1930
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your insipid letter. How is it that, though participat-
ing in such a great revolution, you could not think of anything
to write about? It is possible, of course, that the mind may be
stunned by the great changes taking place and reason may stop
working; if so, one cannot think of anything to write about. At
such a time, one's mind may be wholly absorbed in work and can
think about nothing else. If you are in such a sublime state of
mind, I have nothing to say. I would then be satisfied even with
a letter to this effect: "Bapu, everything is going well. Vasumati."
Blessings from
BAPU
