221. LETTER TO GANGABEHN VAIDYA
[1929]
CHI. GANGABEHN,
Herewith the common letter1, and your letter after corrections.
The sh at the end of a verb indicating tense is always palatal.
You will improve your handwriting quickly if you write slowly
with your pen and write big letters.
Do not grudge the expense on things that may be necessary
to improve your health. Do not be impatient either.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 11271
