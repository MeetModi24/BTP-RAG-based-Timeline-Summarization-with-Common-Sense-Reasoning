310. LETTER TO MARGARETE SPIEGEL
SEVAGRAM,
March 1, 1945
DEAR DAUGHTER,
I have your parting letter. Of course you are brave, you are
simple. There you will pave your way, wherever you are. Keep
good health and write from your new place. Kh[urshedbehn] is
here.
Love from all.
BAPU
(M. K. GANDHI)
DR. M. SPIEGEL
Spiegel Papers. Courtesy : Nehru Memorial Museum and Library
