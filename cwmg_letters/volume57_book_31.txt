31. LETTER TO MADALASA BAJAJ
January 22, 1934
CHI. MADALASA,
Have you resolved not to write to me till I write to you?
Are you testing me in this way or sparing me out of pity for me?
Tell me how you are in mind and body. Ask Vatsala to write
to me. What are you studying? Do you take your meals at re-
gular hours?
Om is quite happy. She is getting fatter.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Panchven Putrako Bapuke Ashirvad, p. 315
1 Uma, the youngest daughter of the addressee
2 Kisan Ghumatkar
3 The source has these two sentences in the form of questions. Om, how-
ever, was with Gandhiji at this time.
