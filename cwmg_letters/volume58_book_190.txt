190. LETTER TO DAHYABHAI PATEL
KARACHI,
July 11, 1934
CHI. DAHYABHAI,
Let me have detailed news about Vallabhbhai's health by
return of post.
Ask Manibehn to write to me in detail about her health.
Mahadev of course will bring all news.
I hope you are doing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
DAHYABHAI VALLABHBHAI PATEL
RAMNIVAS, PAREKH STREET, BOMBAY 4
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 160
