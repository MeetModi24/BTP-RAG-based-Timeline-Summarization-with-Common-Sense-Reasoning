200. LETTER TO D. E. WACHA
RAJKOT,
Sunday, May 18, 1902
DEAR MR. WACHA,
I have your letter. Although I think the sentence referred
to by you may stand as it is, now that it has struck you as
perhaps unwarranted in order to avoid the slightest semblance of
exaggerated language, I propose the following in its place. "It
is now evidently sought as much as possible to reach the same
figure by taxing the children of indentured men after they have
attained the artificial majority." I suppose you are printing the
memorial. If so, I hope you will let me have a few copies.
I am,
Yours truly,
From a photostat of the office copy : S.N. 3967
