280. LETTER TO JAMNABEHN GANDHI
June 30, 1933
CHI. JAMNA,
I hope you got my letter. Since I wanted to call Puru-
shottam here, what I wrote to him was different from what I
did to you. I have now sent a wire and am waiting for him.
To whom else did I say I would write? I have forgotten.
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 884. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
