486. LETTER TO HARIBHAU UPADHYAYA
POONA,
July 1, 1946
BHAI HARIBHAU,
I do not like to preside over any meeting. I am now tired
of these things. I have already explained this to you. Invite
Bapa or somebody else. Please excuse me.
Blessings from
BAPU
