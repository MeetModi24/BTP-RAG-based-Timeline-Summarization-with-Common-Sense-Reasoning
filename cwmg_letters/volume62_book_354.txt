354. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
ALLAHABAD,
April 5, 1936
MY DEAR REBEL,
Your letter duly came today. You shall have the shawl if
it is unsold when I reach Lucknow. But you will have to deve-
lop a kind of village art, cheap yet real beautiful. You must
have seen something about art in my latest article on birth-
control. The wooden spoon made by Navin out of waste bam-
boo is, you have admitted, a thing of beauty and yet quite cheap.
The value of women like you taking up village work lies
in your capacity for making it attractive in spite of the necessity
of conforming to cheapness. Perhaps art to be real must be
cheap in terms of coin.
No more today.
Love.
TYRANT
