426. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
SABARMATI,
Wednesday [May 30, 1928]
CHI. VASUMATI,
I have your letters. Talk with the Principal gently from
time to time about the uncleanliness and take measures to re-
move it. Here things are going on fairly well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 475. Courtesy: Vasumati Pandit
