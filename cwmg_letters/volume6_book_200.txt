200. LETTER TO DADABHAI NAOROJI
HOTEL CECIL,
LONDON, W.C.,
November 17, 1906
DEAR MR. NAOROJI,
I have your notes. I had hoped that I would be able to wait on you
personally and explain the letters from Mr. Polak. However, I have been
so very busy in connection with the Asiatic Law Amendment Ordinance
that I have not been able to do so.
Now that Tatham's Bill has been rejected by the Natal Assembly,
nothing remains to be done for the present.
The petition from Mr. Abdul Ganie you have dealt with already.
I return Mr. Polak's letters addressed to you for your file.
I remain,
Yours truly,
M. K. GANDHI
DADABHAI NAOROJI, ESQ.
22, KENNINGTON ROAD
LAMBETH
From a photostat of the typewritten original: G.N. 2278
