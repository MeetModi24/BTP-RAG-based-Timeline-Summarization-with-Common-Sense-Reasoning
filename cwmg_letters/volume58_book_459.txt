459. LETTER TO B. J. MARATHEY
September 6, 1934
MY DEAR MARATHEY1,
I have sent you one thing in connection with Rajbhoj's com-
plaint. I am expecting an answer from you.
I now send you
a letter from Dr. Mulay which please return.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI B. J. MARATHEY
POONA
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
