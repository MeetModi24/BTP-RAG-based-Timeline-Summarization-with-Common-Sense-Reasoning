440. LETTER TO JETHALAL JOSHI
Bhadrapad Krishna 1 [September 12, 1927]
BHAI JETHALALJI,
Your letter. I would advise you to see the Secretary of the
Ashram and there take up some work if you find any. There is
little possibility of my coming to the Ashram during this year.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1354
