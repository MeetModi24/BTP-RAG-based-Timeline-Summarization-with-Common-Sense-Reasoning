538. LETTER TO BALWANTSINHA
September 20, 1938
CHI. BALWANTSINHA,
You must have heard the story about the true mother and the
false claimant. The false claimant agreed to having the baby
cut up in two with one portion each being given to the two
claimants. But the true mother told the judge that if that was
the only way out she would withdraw her claim and let the
other woman have the baby. It would at least be alive then.
Now, let us see who proves to be the true go-sevak. Both of you
may be such or both may turn out to be worthless or one true
and one false. The problem thus poses three questions for me.
Never say die.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 1910
