501. LETTER TO M. W. H. DE SILVA
NEW DELHI,
September 29, 1946
DEAR FRIEND,
It is very good of you to want to do something for the
charkha class. So far as I am concerned, the delicacy of your
gesture is equal to the best you can do. However, in order to
please yourself, I suggest a small donation to the cause of the
removal of untouchability.
Yours sincerely,
M. W. H. DE SILVA
(CEYLON REPRESENTATIVE IN INDIA)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
