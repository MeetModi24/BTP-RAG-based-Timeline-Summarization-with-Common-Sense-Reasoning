498. LETTER TO SITA GANDHI
July 20, 1937
CHI. SITA,
Your English letter was very good indeed. Won't you write
an equally good one in Gujarati ? Or is it that you don't know
at all how to write Gujarati? Whatever that be, now that you
have started, keep writing to me from time to time. You can
also describe the things you are doing there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 4867
