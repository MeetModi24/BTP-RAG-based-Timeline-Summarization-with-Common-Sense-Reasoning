367. LETTER TO AMIN
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 30, 1945
CHI. AMIN,
That you make efforts is itself proof of the presence of both
the virtues in you. Man's duty is but to endeavour. Success
depends on the grace of God. We should seek solitude among
the multitudes-inaction in activity-but such activity should be
without desire for fruits. That is what you are doing. Remember
214
Ramakrishna Paramahansa was not inactive. After all you do not
indulge in worldly behaviour.
Blessings from
BAPU
