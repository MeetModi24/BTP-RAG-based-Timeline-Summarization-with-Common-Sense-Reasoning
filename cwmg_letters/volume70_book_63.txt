63. LETTER TO POTTI SRIRAMULU CHETTY
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 3, 1939
MY DEAR SHRIRAMULU
I have your letter.
I must not write to Rajaji. You should go to the Kodambak-
kam Ashram and offer your services as a volunteer. They will
accept you if you are a steady worker.
Yours sincerely,
BAPU
From a photostat: G.N. 112
1 Vide p. 24.
2 A Congressman of Nellore who fasted to death in 1953 for the formation
of a separate Andhra State
3 Harijan Ashram
