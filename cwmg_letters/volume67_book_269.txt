269. LETTER TO AMRIT KAUR
July 23, 1938
MY DEAR AMRIT,
Pray don't come tonight. The weather is awful. Wet, wet,
wet everywhere. Tomorrow you will come anyhow, unless of
course you are ill and it is raining hard as it is now. Your
corner is of course reserved.
Love.
BAPU
From the original: C.W. 3633. Courtesy: Amrit Kaur. Also G.N. 6442
