99. LETTER TO RAJENDRA PRASAD
November 11 2, 1945
BHAI RAJEN BABU,
I have your telegram. But how long it took! Mahendra
Gope was hanged yesterday and your telegram has come today.
How did this happen? Did I not give my opinion in the case?
It will be good if in this case also you find out the facts as I
had asked you to do in the case of Mahendra Chowdhary.
Was the case really political?
I want proof.
I hope you are well.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
