258. LETTER TO MANILAL GANDHI
POONA,
March 6, 1946
CHI. MANILAL,
I have your letter. I will have to go there on the 11th. I, there-
fore, think it useless for you to come here just for two days. I shall be
busy with Harijan. My health is good. I hope you got my message.
Blessings from
BAPU
CHI. MANILAL GANDHI
C/O NILKANTH MASHRUWALA
SOUTH AVENUE, BOMBAY-25
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 4974. Also C.W. 1387. Cour-
tesy : Sushilabehn Gandhi
