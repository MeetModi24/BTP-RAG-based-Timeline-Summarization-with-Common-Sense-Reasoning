140. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA
WARDHA,
Magsar Sud 7, 1983 [December 12, 1926]
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA,
I had certainly preserved this song of yours. Send me an
acknowledgment. I am sure you are continuing your Hindi studies
and attending prayers regularly. Chandan and others must have
improved their health. Do all the women spin?
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original: C.W. 4909. Courtesy: Hari-ichchha Kamdar
