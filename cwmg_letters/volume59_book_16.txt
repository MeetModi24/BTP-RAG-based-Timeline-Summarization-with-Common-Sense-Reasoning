16. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
September 17, 1934
CHI. BRAJKISHAN,
Your letter. What happens after meeting Damodardas is
yet to be seen. Till then I shall remain doubt-ridden. I cannot
be just either to Damodardas or to Santok and others. And now
you too are involved. Damodardas alleges that you have mis-
understood him but I have no doubts about you. My fear in
respect of you is of a different kind. You have not realized even
now the value of money. In spite of your renunciation, your
mind is still that of a moneyed man and not averse to possession.
An action performed without the prompting of the heart loses
all its value.
I trust you maintain good health there. Prabha has gone
to Patna; she might return on Friday.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 2424
