252. LETTER TO K. P. RAMAN PILLAI
January 29, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for the letter giving me the information that
Koteshwaram Mahadev temple near Kuzithurai in South Tra-
vancore was opened to the Harijans. I hope to see that other
temples follow suit.
Yours sincerely,
SJT. K. P. RAMAN PILLAI
OLACODE
COLACHEL, P.O.
S. TRAVANCORE
From a microfilm: S.N. 20142
