406. LETTER TO VITHTHALBHAI M. PATEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
April 12, 1940
CHI. VITHTHALBHAI,
I have your letter. I could read it only today. Pay no attention
to others' criticism but go on doing whatever Harijan work you can.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 83
