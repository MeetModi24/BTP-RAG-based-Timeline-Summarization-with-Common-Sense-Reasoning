485. LETTER TO DR. M. D. D. GILDER
PATNA,
May 22, 1947
BHAI GILDER,
Chi. Samyukta is my grand-daughter. She is Chi. Manu's
sister and daughter of my nephew, Jaisukhlal Gandhi. She has
been suffering for a long time. Her father, therefore, has wired
to me to write a letter of introduction to you, requesting Dr.
Jivraj Mehta and you to examine her and suggest some treat-
ment for her.
I think you have once examined her. I do not know if
Dr. Mehta is there. I am, therefore, writing to you. Please
do your best. Chi. Samyukta's husband can afford to pay your
fees. You may, therefore, charge whatever fees you wish. They
want a letter of introduction only because they are not sure
whether you will now be able to spare time from your public
work. If I write a letter of introduction to you, they hope to
be able to see you.
Hope you all are happy.
Blessings from
BAPU
