54. LETTER TO MANIBEHN PATEL
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
May 7, 1941
CHI. MANI,
Nandubehn [Kanuga] was complaining against you very
much, saying that you were obstinate and ruining your health.
You are not eating properly, she said. I regard this as a sign
of despair. A satyagrahi will always keep his health in the
best of conditions. I, therefore, strongly advise you to improve
your health.
My blessings to all the women. I do get reports about the
work there.
I keep excellent health. Ba is in Delhi. She has become
very weak.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MANIBEHN PATEL
PRISONER
YERAVDA CENTRAL PRISON
YERAVDA
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-4: Manibehn Patelne, p. 127
