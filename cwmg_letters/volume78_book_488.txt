488. LETTER TO BALUBHAI P. MEHTA
SEVAGRAM,
November 27, 1944
BHAI BALUBHAI,
I have your letter. I am no more useful for personal work.
My strength is limited. I can hardly cope with public work
either. There are hundreds who are afflicted like you. Would
I have the time if I set out to meet all of them? One should
find out one's own way out of one's troubles.
Blessings from
BAPU
B. P. MEHTA
GANJIKHET
NAGPUR
