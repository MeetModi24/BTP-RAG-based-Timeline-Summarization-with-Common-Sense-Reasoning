190. LETTER TO RAMASWAMI
SEVAGRAM,
February 7, 1945
CHI.
RAMASWAMI,
Why should I now write to you in English? You ought to
know Hindi by this time.
So you have resolved to go. If the Government don't send
you, you will adopt other means. That too is a way. By all
means go. Keep whatever links you can. Be a true servant.
Blessings from
BAPU
RAMASWAMI
TALIMI SANGH
SEVAGRAM
