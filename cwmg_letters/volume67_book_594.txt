594. LETTER TO C. F. ANDREWS
October 5, 1938
If absence of letters means neglect, then I have neglected you.
The fact is these days you are ever before me. You see I
have come out of my shell and begun to speak to Europe. It
is a peace without honour.
SF. C. F. ANDREWS
BANGALORE
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
