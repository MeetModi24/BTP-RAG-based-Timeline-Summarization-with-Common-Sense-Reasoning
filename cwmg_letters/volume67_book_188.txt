188. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
June 25, 1938
CHI. MAHADEV,
Please see Kanti's letter. Send the accompanying wire to
Ramachandran. Hope the wire to Kanti was despatched yester-
day.
Last night I took serpina. At 3.30 a. m. the blood-pressure
was 190/112. Just now it must be less. Nothing need be done
because of this. I shall have to reduce the work. Send the
accompanying wire to Hanna.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Did you give Kaka the letter regarding marriage rites? Now
at 6.30 a. m. it is 180/102.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11599
