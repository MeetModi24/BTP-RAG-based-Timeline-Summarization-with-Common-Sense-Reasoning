431. LETTER TO ALUBEHN MISTRI
SEVAGRAM,
March 16, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
You are taking good care of Bhai Dinshaw. God certainly
gives him the help he needs. If there is any such provision in
the Kasturba Memorial Fund, he will certainly get it.
Blessings from
BAPU
ALUBEHN MISTRI
JAL CHAMBERS
OPPOSITE RAILWAY HOSPITAL
CHURNEY ROAD JUNCTION
BOMBAY
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
