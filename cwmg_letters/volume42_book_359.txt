359. LETTER TO G. D. BIRLA
[Before January 9, 1930]
BHAI GHANSHYAMDASJI,
I did not answer your last telegram. What instructions can
I give from this distance? There can be nothing lacking in your
attention. I am aware that Keshu needs some comforting. To
that end I am sending Devdas. I have little faith in medicines.
But I do not interfere in the treatment of those away from me.
Hence there was no need for any instructions in response to your
telegram. My treatment is quite well known-fasting or fruit
juice, sun-baths, sleeping at night in a well-ventilated room and
enema in case of constipation. Many patients like Keshu have
been cured by this treatment. But I do not wish to practise my
theories from a distance. You can do whatever you like. Medi-
cines should not be suggested to Keshav unless he asks for them.
I hope Keshav will be out of all danger by the time this reaches
you.
Yours,
MOHANDAS
From Hindi: C.W. 6179. Courtesy: G. D. Birla
