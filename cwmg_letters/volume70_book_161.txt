161. LETTER TO A. GUPTA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
August 29, 1939
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I am entirely at one with you
that imputation of motives is a frailty common to all. If you
will re-read my statement1, you will find that it is of a general
character. I have just read in today's paper that there was a
black-flag demonstration against Subhas Babu at Patna. I was
sorry to learn of it.
Yours sincerely,
SHRI A. GUPTA
BENGALI ASSOCIATION
DINAPORE, BIHAR
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
