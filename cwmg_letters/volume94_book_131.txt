131. LETTER TO SUSHILA NAYYAR
November 6, 1941
CHI. SUSHILA,
Vasumatibehn's fever persists, but is on the decline. She is
cheerful. I am doing very well. I am very busy.
Blessings from
BAPU
DR. SUSHILA NAYYAR
L. H. M. C.
NEW DELHI
From the Gujarati original : Pyarelal Papers. Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library. Courtesy : Dr. Sushila Nayyar
