203. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
February 25, 1946
CHI. MUNNALAL,
I have read your letter. I am returning it, in case you wish
to preserve it. I will of course not keep you here against your
wish. I have already told Dinshaw. Let us see now what
happens. I remember all the facts given by you.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G. N. 8424
1 Vide p. 179.
