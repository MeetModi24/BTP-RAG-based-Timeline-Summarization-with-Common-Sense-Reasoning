664. LETTER TO PANNALAL JAIN
SABARMATI,
Bhadrapad Shukla 11 [August 26, 1928]
BHAI PANNALALJI,
I have your letter. Your complaint is just. But there are
difficulties in increasing the size of Hindi Navajivan. With great
difficulty it has only just started paying its way. It attempts to
give a summary of all useful articles. This attempt will be re-
doubled hereafter.
Yours,
MOHANDAS GANDHI
SHRI PANNALAL JAIN
KALYANMAL MILLS, INDORE
[From Hindi]
Madhya Pradesh aur Gandhiji, p. 150
