433. LETTER TO MANAHAR DIWAN
September 10, 1945
BHAI MANAHAR,
I have your second letter. All has ended well with Shastriji.
I sent a telegram and also a letter. God alone can reward you
for your services. But you don't want any, do you?
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI MANAHAR DIWAN
DUTTAPUR
