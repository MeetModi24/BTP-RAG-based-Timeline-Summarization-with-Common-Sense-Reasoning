380. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
February 7, 1939
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have your letter as also the wire. If you are not allowed
to have the needed rest there, rush down here. You will defini-
tely get rest here. There is at least some peace of mind. The
changes I introduced have not been received well. But the student
of the Gita must find peace of mind wherever he goes.
I am getting along. I am trying to reduce as much as
possible the burden on Vallabhbhai. But he has a worrying
temperament. Let us see what happens. The Statesman cannot
write anything else.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Now it is almost time to go to the marriage mandap.
