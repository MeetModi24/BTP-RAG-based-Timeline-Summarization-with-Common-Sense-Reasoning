256. LETTER TO SOHANLAL OBEROI
June 18, 1935
BHAI SOHANLAL,
What you have written is correct. Many girls suffer the
fate that befell your sister. I shall try to write about it. I hope
your efforts will succeed.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Don't you have good slivers? Or good cotton?
SHRI SOHANLAL OBEROI
A. I. S. A. DEPOT
ADAMPUR, JULLUNDUR DISTRICT
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6863
