216. LETTER TO BRIJKRISHNA CHANDIWALA
June 9, 1935
CHI. BRIJKRISHNA,
This time you have punished me severely. I waited and wait-
ed for your letter and was always disappointed. A man does not
die by desiring to die. Come here after you have done with Kash-
mir. I shall assign you work. Let us wait and see. No one is al-
lowed to go to Quetta.
Prabhavati is at Savali these days and Ba at Bochasan.
Blessings from
BAPU
