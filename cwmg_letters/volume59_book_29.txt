29. LETTER TO BALVANTRAI P. THAKORE
September 19, 1934
BHAI BALUBHAI,
If it is found that the law does not permit it, we shall bear
the criticism that may be made.
I was of the view that the
Trustees had a right to make such a gift . The right to make a
gift which would do the Vidyapith no harm but, having regard
to the circumstances then prevailing, could only be considered
beneficial to it, is implied in the Trust Deed itself. This is, however,
a layman's view. Only a competent lawyer's opinion can be regar-
ded as authoritative.
Nor have I expressed pain to anybody with regard to giving
away the books.
The only question can be, and is, not to do
anything which might be improper. I had no other meaning
in what I wrote except that if we were making any mistake we
should rectify it. Please, therefore, banish that idea from your
mind.
If there is delay in preparing a catalogue of the books or
in receiving a list from Jivanji, let me know. Can we not make
some arrangement, pending the construction of a building, to see
that the books are being used?
BALUBHAI
KHADIA
AHMEDABAD
[From Gujarati]
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy; Narayan Desai
