160. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
SEVAGRAM,
August 12, 1946
CHI. LILY,
I have your letter. If you cannot get [money] from Jayanti-
lal, tell Sumatibehn about it. She will either give you the money
or get you the book. If there is any difficulty, do let me know.
If necessary, you may show her this letter.
I hope your studies are progressing well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI LILAVATIBEHN
G. S. MEDICAL COLLEGE
LADIES' HOSTEL
PAREL, BOMBAY
From a photostat of the Gujarati : C. W. 10241. Courtesy: Lilavati Asar
