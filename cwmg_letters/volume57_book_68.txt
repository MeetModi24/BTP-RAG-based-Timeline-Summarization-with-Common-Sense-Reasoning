68. LETTER TO MATHURADAS TRIKUMJI
ON WAY TO COONOOR,
January 29, 1934
Bihar and Midnapur are wringing my heart. I have written
to Swami about this. If you have not read it, please do.
Rajendrababu wants the Ashram inmates who have been re-
leased to help. I have sent a wire to Ahmedabad and also to
Swami. If your health were good I would have suggested your
going there too. If it seems necessary to go even at obvious
risk to one's health, one should go. But I see no such need just
now. I have written to Rajendrababu and asked him to let me
know if he requires my presence there.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 142
