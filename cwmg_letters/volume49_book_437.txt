437. LETTER TO DUDHIBEHN V. DESAI
April 24, 1932
CHI. DUDHIBEHN,
It is less trouble to me to reply to a letter immediately I get
it, if I have the time to do so. Writing will stop automatically
when I can no longer use my hand. A wife can go where her
husband does, but sometimes dharma indicates a different course.
What is essential is to have patience. We should cheerfully do—
learn to do—the duty which has come to us unsought. Why
should a woman believe herself weak? Husband and wife are
friends and equals of each other. A weak wife makes the husband
also weak. Hence she should be strong even for his sake.
Blessings from
BAPU
