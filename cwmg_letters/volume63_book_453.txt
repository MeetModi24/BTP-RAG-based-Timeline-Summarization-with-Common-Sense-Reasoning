453. LETTER TO NANDALAL BOSE
October 6, 1936
DEAR NANDLAL BABU,
Just a line to thank you for your letter and to tell you I
shall be at your disposal on 13th instant. You will please wire
to Mahadev when you are coming.
Yours sincerely,
M.K.G.
SHRI NANDLAL BOSE
C/O RABINDRANATH TAGORE
6 DWARKANATH TAGORE LANE
JORASANKO, CALCUTTA
From a photostat: C.W. 9778
