111. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
MOTIHARI,
Ashadh Sud 9 [June 29, 1917]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I am impressed by your patriotism. May God grant you good
sense, strength, spirit of sacrifice and health.
I have written to Bhai Jehangirji Patel. I enclose a copy of
the letter. You must, in all humility but with firmness, have
the resolution passed. When the time comes, those who are
