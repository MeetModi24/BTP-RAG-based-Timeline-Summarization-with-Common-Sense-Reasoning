181. LETTER TO BUDHABHAI
January 11. 1931
CHI. BUDHABHAI,
I received your letter. Your diet is all right. Be very careful how you
behave towards Parvati. Never be alone with her and never allow impure
feelings to come anywhere near you. Such feelings towards one whom you
have accepted as a sister should be unthinkable. Be content with whatever
she accepts of what you tell her with a detached mind.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Gujarati original : S.N. 33126
