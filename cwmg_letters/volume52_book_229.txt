229. LETTER TO PADMA
December 11, 1932
CHI. PADMA,
I hope you have become calm now. Do you understand now
what true freedom means? If you have understood it, tell me
what it is. You may come again if you wish to see me. I see
that your discontent also is a cause of your ill-health. Understand
this properly and become calm. If you feel the slightest desire
to marry, let me know. Take complete rest there.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 6142. Also C.W. 3494. Courtesy:
Prabhudas Gandhi
