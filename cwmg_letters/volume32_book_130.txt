130. LETTER TO ROLLO RUSSELL
AS AT THE ASHRAM,
SABARMATI,
December 9, 1926
I was delighted to hear from you after so many years. I have
got the book you have sent me. Much as I would like to read it, I
do not know when I shall be able to read it.
Mine is a very difficult life-more so than when you saw it
in Johannesburg. I have also after some experience come to see
that it is not what you read but what you think that affects your
life. I do hear about you from the Polaks now and then and it gives
me delight to know that you were the same innocent soul that I
knew in Johannesburg.
I am sending a line to Mrs. Russell.
Yours sincerely,
KENT HOUSE
1, NOTTINGHAM PLACE
W.I.
From a photostat: S.N. 19767
