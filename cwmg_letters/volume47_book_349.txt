349. LETTER TO MAZHAR ALI ALAVI
AHMEDABAD,
August 18, 1931
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter.
I have no objection to your dedicating your book to me.
Please remember me to your father. I hope he is now well.
Yours sincerely,
PROF. MAZHAR ALI ALAVI
KOTHI No. 131
AMINABAD, LUCKNOW
From a photostat: S.N. 17504
