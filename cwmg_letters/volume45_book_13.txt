13. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAM GANDHI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 19, 1930
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
Since you have been doing your duty with sincere devotion,
you should feel no other desires. What path should I suggest to
you? We daily recite the verse Prajahati yada kaman. You should
burn up every personal desire. You will then yourself discover the
path. Reflect over the verses describing the qualities of the sthita-
prajna and know that they are for persons of your character. Think
which of those verses you are not able to follow in conduct, and
why. If you do this, all your mental problems will disappear. Write
to me from time to time. My respectful greetings to the elders, and
blessings to Jamnadas.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati: C.W. 901. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
