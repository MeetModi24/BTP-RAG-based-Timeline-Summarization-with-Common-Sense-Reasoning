44. LETTER TO JOSH
November 6, 1945
BHAI JOSH,
I have your letter. I knew that your Josh would come down.
Now I see that you wish to have a heart-to-heart talk with me.
Please come on the 13th. I will keep myself free at 3 in the
afternoon.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
JOSH SAHEB
TAHIR PLACE
SHANKER SETH ROAD, POONA
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
