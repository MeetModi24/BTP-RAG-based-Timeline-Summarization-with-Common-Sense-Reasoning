377. LETTER TO RAJARAM BHOLE
June 4, 1945
DEAR BHOLE,
Your P. C. Do come on 6th inst. at 4.00 p.m. I am silent during
the day always. But it does not matter. You will speak and I shall
answer in writing.
Yours,
BAPU
SHRI R. R. BHOLE
PALLAZZO HOTEL
PANCHGANI
From the original : R. R. Bhole Papers. Courtesy : Nehru Memorial
Museum and Library
