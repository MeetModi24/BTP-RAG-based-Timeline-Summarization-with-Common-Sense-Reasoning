422. LETTER TO J. L. GOHEEN
November 18, 1934
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your letter. I have now received the parcel of
eggs for which I thank you. Though you have sent me no bill
for the eggs, I shall thank you to tell me at what price they are
sold. I shall send the container back as soon as possible.
I have your second letter. Please give my regards to your
pupils who, I hope, will be discharged as competent workers. If
you will render me a regular bill, I should like you to send me
eighteen eggs per week till further instructions.
You can make
the commencement on the receipt of this.
Yours sincerely,
J. L. GOHEEN, ESQ.
SANGLI INDUSTRIAL AND AGRICULTURAL SCHOOL
AMERICAN PRESBYTERIAN MISSION
SANGLI (M. S. M. RLY.)
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
