414. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
SODEPUR,
May 12, 1947
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
I finished my post. After that I read some more letters
from another file in which I found your letter. You have done
the right thing about Chakrayya. I came to know about him
from Lilavati.
I am happy to learn the good news about the hospital
there. You are right when you say that where there is con-
sciousness of dharma even thorns seem like flowers. There is
some complaint from Sankaran; I am enclosing the letter.
What is it about? I have written to him to talk to you and
I say the same to you. We are but a few. If there were to be
reservations amongst us where would we find straightforwardness?
Look into it.
I hope you are in good health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9036. Also C. W. 9160. Courtesy:
Prabhakar
