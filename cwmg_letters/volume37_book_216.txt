216. LETTER TO TULSI MAHER
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
August 15, 1928
CHI. TULSI MAHER,
I have your letter. The beauty of the Himalayan region
needs no description. Many changes are coming about in the
Ashram. Kishorelal's elder brother Balubhai has passed away.
The sample of khaddar is good. How much does it cost?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G.N. 6535
