279. LETTER TO MANUBEHN S. MASHRUWALA
BARDOLI,
January 6, 1942
CHI. MANUDI 1 ,
I have your letter. You did well in going there. Write to me
regularly from Bombay. The cold seems to have ended here.
Blessings from
BAPU
SMT. MANUBEHN MASHRUWALA
BAL KIRAN
P.O. JUHU, BOMBAY 24
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 2680.
Courtesy: Kanubhai
Mashruwala
