348. LETTER TO QUAYAM ALI M. SALEMWALA
ASHRAM,
April 14, 1926
BHAISHRI QUAYAM ALI,
I have your letter. The site of the Jallianwala Bagh was pur-
chased with the help of the funds raised for the purpose. The
site was cleared to make room for the park. No memorial has been
raised because the circumstances in the country are not favourable.
How can we raise the edifice of freedom while we undermine
its foundations? The trustees, I believe, fight shy of a memorial
because they have this fear.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI QUAYAM ALI MOHAMAD ALI SALEMWALA
C/O MOHAMAD ALI & SONS
SOMERSET STREET, CAMP
KARACHI
