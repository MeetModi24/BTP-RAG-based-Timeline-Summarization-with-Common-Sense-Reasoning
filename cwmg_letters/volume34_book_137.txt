137. LETTER TO SHAPURJI SAKLATVALA
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
July 13, 1927
MY DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter of the 16th June last through Bharucha. I
understand and appreciate the viewpoint elaborated in your
letter, and it would give me much pleasure if I can do anything
whatsoever to assist you in coming to a settlement with the Tatas.
But I know my limitations and I would like you to recognize them.
All I can do is to work through Panditji which I am doing.
Yours sincerely,
SHAPURJI SAKLATVALA, ESQ., M.P.
HOUSE OF COMMONS
LONDON S.W. 1
From a photostat: S.N. 12532
