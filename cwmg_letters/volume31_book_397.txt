397. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL KRISHNALAL MEHTA
[On or after September 7, 1926]
BHAISHRI JAISUKHLAL 2 ,
I have your letter. Now at last I have some peace. I
have made no attempt at all to understand the currency problem.
I live from moment to moment, submit to the pressure of the
moment and become engrossed in whatever it forces on me. For
the time being I have escaped from the pressure of the currency
problem.
I have written to Lalji Sheth about the deputation from South
Africa and am awaiting his reply.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12240
