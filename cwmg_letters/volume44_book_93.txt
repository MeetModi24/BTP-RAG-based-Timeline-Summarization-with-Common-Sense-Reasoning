93. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
August 3, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (SENIOR),
I got your letter. No one inspired by a noble idea has ever
laboured in vain. You are the first to give me precise informa-
tion about Indulal. I am glad.
I see that you are keeping up a fairly good correspondence
with all friends. Do you write to Sushila (the Punjabi)? If you
know her address, ask her to write to me. What does she do?
My regards to all.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1801
1 Indulal Yajnik
