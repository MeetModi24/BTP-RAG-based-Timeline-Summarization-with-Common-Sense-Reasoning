182. LETTER TO POLICE COMMISSIONER, BOMBAY
RAJKOT, KATHIAWAR,
March 12, 1902
TO
THE POLICE COMMISSIONER
BOMBAY
SIR,
Will you be good enough to let me know on what condi-
tions permits are issued to people desiring to go to South Africa?
I have the honour to remain,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the office copy: S.N. 3941
