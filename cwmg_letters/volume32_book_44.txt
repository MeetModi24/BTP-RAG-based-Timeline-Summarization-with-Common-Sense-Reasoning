44. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
Kartika Sud 11 [November 16, 1926]
BHAISHRI MUNNALAL,
I have your letter. One who tries to observe brahmacharya,
truth, non-violence, non-thieving and non-possession, who believes
that untouchability is sinful, who labours without feeling any
fatigue, who thinks it is his dharma to clean latrines, who controls
the palate and spins and cards daily and wears pure khadi, only
such a person may stay in the Ashram.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
SHRI MUNNALAL GANGADAS SHAH
BURHANPUR
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: S.N. 19964
