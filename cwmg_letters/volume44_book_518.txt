518. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
December 11, 1930
MY DEAR KUMARAPPA,
I note what you say about Kamalabehn. May she prosper
in the right sense!! 'This correspondence' need not cease. I would
gladly continue it if you will further cross-question me. Many
things I have noticed become clear through sheer lapse of time
and consequent, unconscious observation.
Love.
BAPU
