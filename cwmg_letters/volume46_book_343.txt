343. LETTER TO MANILAL AND SUSHILA GANDHI
BARDOLI,
June 2, 1931
CHI. MANILAL AND SUSHILA,
I had the letter you both wrote on board the ship. I also
got news of your having reached there. I will now expect your
regular letter by the next mail. Ba is with me at Bardoli. She
was with me at Simla too. Nothing is certain as yet about my
going to England. The solution of the Hindu-Muslim problem
seems to be far away yet.
As for the rest, everything is all right.
I have no time to write more.
Sita-I have forgotten her other name-must have now put on
weight again. The climate there is such that one simply cannot
fall ill. I should be equally happy if Sushila too puts on weight.
Blessings from
BAPU
