289. LETTER TO PRABHAKAR
NEW DELHI,
July 7/8, 1947
CHI. PRABHAKAR,
I have your letter. Sushilabehn told me everything. No
blemish can attach to the innocent. Those who accuse them
become themselves soiled. You do not, therefore, have to
undergo penance. Still we should be careful in everything.
Take care of your health.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Hindi: G. N. 9039. Also C. W. 9163. Cour-
tesy: Prabhakar
