501. LETTER TO KUSUM DESAI
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 6, 1930
CHI. KUSUM (DESAI),
Your letter consisted of three sheets, but they seemed to have
lost the middle sheet. I did not get it. If you remember its con-
tents, write to me again.
Pyarelal's health has become very
good. His weight is 122 pounds. He gets three pounds of milk,
one pound of bread and vegetables.
At present, both of us have become crazy about spinning.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 1812
