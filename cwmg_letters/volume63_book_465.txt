465. LETTER TO DEVDAS GANDHI
October 10, 1936
CHI. DEVDAS,
I am glad to learn that the whole line of thought has been
Ba's own. She certainly has that power, and the letter is in-
deed a good one. Dr. Mahmud writes a long letter which I am
enclosing. Read it and tear it up. Am I to understand from
the rather long letter you wrote that you can now write fairly
well? The two patients take a good deal of my time, which I
certainly like to give.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2035
