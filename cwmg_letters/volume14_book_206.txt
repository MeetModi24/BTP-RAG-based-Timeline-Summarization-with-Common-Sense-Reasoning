206. LETTER TO V. S. SRINIVASA SASTRI
April 5, 1918
I thank you for your note. However anxious I may be to win
your approbation for any conduct of mine, I share your anxiety
that your conscience may not in any way be coerced. I know that
you will keep in touch with the Kaira affairs as they develop from
day to day.
From the manuscript of Mahadev Desai's Diary. Courtesy: Narayan Desai
