155. LETTER TO P. C. JOSHI
MAHABALESHWAR,
May 7, 1945
BHAI JOSHI,
It will be all right if Mohan comes on the 24th. If he
comes any time between 2 and 4, I will see him.
Yours,
M. K. GANDHI
SHRI P. C. JOSHI
COMMUNIST PARTY
RAJ BHAVAN, SANDHURST ROAD
BOMBAY 4
From a copy of the Hindi ; Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy; Pyarelal
