83. LETTER TO TARAMATI MATHURADAS
Bhadarva Sud 12 [September 10, 1924]
It is only today I got a letter telling me of your illness. Please
do not worry at all. Resolve to get well and keep on repeating
Ramanama. It will lessen the grief on account of your illness and
may cure the malady as well. A lot of work has still to be taken
from you. Get well and do write to me. May God give you
long life.
[From Gujarati]
Bapuni Prasadi, p. 75
