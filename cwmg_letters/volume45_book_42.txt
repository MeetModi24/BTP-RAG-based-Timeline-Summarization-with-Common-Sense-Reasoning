42. LETTER TO JUGATRAM DAVE
YERAVDA MANDIR,
December 29, 1930
BHAI JUGATRAM,
I have been expecting a letter from you every day. You
have piled up a fairly big heap of facts before me. It requires a
special art to deal with Mithubehn. Use all the art you have.
I have received complaints against her from the women too.
In any case, Time is the friend of all. He sets everything right.
Probably that is why in English they call Him Father Time. We
believe Him to be God Himself. If He destroys the worlds, He
does that too for their good.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 2690
