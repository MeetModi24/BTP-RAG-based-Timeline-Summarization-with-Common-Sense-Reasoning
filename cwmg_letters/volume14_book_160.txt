160. LETTER TO MANSUKHLAL MEHTA
March 7, 1918
DEAR SHRI MANSUKHLAL,
I am not pained by your criticism. I do not make light of
the Kathiawad problem. It seems so big to me that, for the pre-
sent, it is beyond my capacity. It is not either that I have not
thought about it. I have decided to leave it alone after full
deliberation. Possibly, it is weakness on my part to have done so.
In that case, I need strength first. That I cannot have as a gift from
you. There should be a fire inside, and this is lacking.
[From Gujarati]
Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. IV
