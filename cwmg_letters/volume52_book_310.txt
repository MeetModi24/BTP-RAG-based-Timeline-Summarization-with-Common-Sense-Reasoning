310. LETTER TO GOPIKRISHNA VIJAYAVARGIYA
December 19, 1932
BHAI GOPIKRISHNA,
Received your letter. If we exist, God exists, for God is the
sum-total of all life just as the sun is the aggregate of rays. In
order to have faith in God we have to have faith in ourselves,
which is acquired through service without consideration of results.
Another way is to have faith simply because the rest of the world
has it.
I do not even think what the goal of independent India will
be. This goal will be clear with the coming of independence.
The rest you can see from my writings.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS GANDHI
[From a facsimile of the Hindi]
Madhya Pradesh aur Gandhi, p. 137; also Mahadevbhaini Diary, Vol. II,
p. 355
