415. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
July 27, 1935
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
I got your letter. I don't want the happy relations between
Narandas and you to be endangered. I, therefore, will keep you
out of this trouble about the school. But I see from your letter
that acute temperamental differences still persist between you. You
have only cultivated a kind of mutual forbearance. It is enough
for you that your work goes on smoothly. Do write to me,
