320. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
SEVAGRAM, WARDHA,
July 12, 1940
CHI. NARAHARI,
Bhai Maganlal, Dr. Mehta's son, is there.
I am sending a
letter for him at the Ashram address. Send it to him wherever
he is staying. Give him whatever help he asks for. Introduce
him to the leading citizens.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9118
