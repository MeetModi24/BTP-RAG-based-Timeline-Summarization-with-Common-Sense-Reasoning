771. LETTER TO J. C. KUMARAPPA
October 28, 1945
MY DEAR KU.,
You have a good certificate about the sculpture. As soon as
it is on view in Maganwadi I shall set about collecting. I am
glad Mrs. Hopman is herself coming to pay your commission.
The b. p. is good.
You must not gloat over your brother's exploits. Wait and
watch.
Love.
BAPU
DR. KUMARAPPA
A. I. V. I. A.
MAGANWADI, WARDHA
From a photostat: G.N. 10182
