152. LETTER TO MUNNALAL G. SHAH
January 22, 1940
CHI. MUNNALAL,
What shall I write to you? Everybody complained that you
talk too much. You should sit alone somewhere and spin. You may
sit in that room only for doing library work. I have taken to pro-
longing my silence. It gives me joy and happiness. It is the only
remedy for you. Wherever you go, you will find human nature
the same. Whether we are happy or otherwise depends entirely on
ourselves. Valjibhai is to be sent tomorrow to get his teeth ex-
tracted. Will you accompany him? It may take about a week.
Do see today's Ashram Note.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 8554. Also C.W. 7071. Courtesy:
Munnalal G. Shah
