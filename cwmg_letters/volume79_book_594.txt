594. LETTER TO PURUSHOTTAMDAS GANDHI
BIRLA HOUSE,
April 4, 1945
CHI. PURUSHOTTAM,
(1 ) Your first view is correct.
(2) My view about propagation of music is that that alone
is real music which uplifts the soul. This contains everything
I should like to suggest.
(3) I have already explained that music is a constructive
activity, but that it is not included in the Congress programme.
Swaraj can be conceived without it. It cannot, therefore, be one
of the objects for which Narandas is collecting a fund. That is
why I have said that, if we do not demand a salary for you, you
should take the money from me. It would be all right if you
took it from the Satyagraha Ashram money lying with Naran-
das. If there is any difficulty in doing even that, I would find
the money in some other way.
Blessings from
BAPU
From Gujarati : C.W. 913. Courtesy: Narandas Gandhi
