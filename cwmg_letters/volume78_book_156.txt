156. LETTER TO CHIMANLAL N. SHAH
September 21, 1944
CHI. CHIMANLAL,
I have your letter. Even if our talks remain inconclusive,
I have decided to reach there on the 1st. I shall return after
attending to the 2nd.
I hope Vallabh Swami undertook the fast after due thought.
It has become difficult to fix the proper conditions for fasting.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati
G.N. 10613
