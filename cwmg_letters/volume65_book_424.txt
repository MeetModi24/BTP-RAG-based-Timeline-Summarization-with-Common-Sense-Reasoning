424. LETTER TO PREMABEHN KANTAK
July 5, 1937
CHI. PREMA,
This is just to tell you that a copy of Gitai has been sent
by the earlier post. You must have received it. The rest when I
get time.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 10391. Also C.W. 6830. Courtesy:
Premabehn Kantak
