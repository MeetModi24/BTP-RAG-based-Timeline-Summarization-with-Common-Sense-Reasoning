265. LETTER TO SHANKERLAL BANKER
Monday [May 2, 1921]
DEAR SHANKERLAL,
I have your letter. Your sister said you had some trouble
in understanding my letter. I shall explain when we meet. My
main point is that you should stop worrying. Mother is bound to
depart, leaving her son behind. How long can you grieve over
her death?
I shall have to go to Allahabad on the 6th. I shall reach
Bombay from Bassein by any available train. Reserve two seats for
me for the night train to Allahabad. I would you like to come
by the same train. I shall definitely be there on the 10th.
I am also writing directly to Bassein.
Vandemataram from
MOHANDAS
4th Kapadvanj
5th Nandarbar
From the Gujarati original; S.N. 32685
