69. LETTER TO HARI-ICHCHHA P. KAMDAR
January 29, 1932
CHI. HARI-ICHCHHA,
I hear from Rasik that you gave birth to a child but the
baby died after four months. That is what our life is like. All
children born do not live, and even those that live will die some
day. Need I, therefore, console you? Moreover, the children
by the first wife are yours, aren't they? If the baby had lived,
you would certainly have had my blessings for it. I would have
been happy if you had come to see me. We shall meet now
when God wills. Meanwhile, write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
If you wish, you may write to me directly.
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 7468. Also C.W. 4914. Courtesy:
Hari-ichchha P. Kamdar
