369. LETTER TO JAWAHARLAL NEHRU
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 22, 1937
MY DEAR JAWAHARLAL,
Your letter has just reached me. Though three days might
prove too few, they would be better than nothing. It is a pity
Indu can't come with you. I had thought that the operation
that she had for tonsils many years ago was the last. I take it
this would be as simple as the previous one.
Love to you all.
BAPU
Gandhi-Nehru Papers, 1937. Courtesy: Nehru Memorial Museum and
Library
