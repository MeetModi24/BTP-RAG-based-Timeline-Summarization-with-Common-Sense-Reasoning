401. LETTER TO ANIL K. MISHRA
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
BHAI ANIL,
If you want to draw up your programme after consulting
me you will never be able to do anything. Chart your own
course from what I have written for the general public.
Blessings from
BAPU
From the Hindi original : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
