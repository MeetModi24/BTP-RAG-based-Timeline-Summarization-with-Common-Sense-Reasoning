175. LETTER TO VIDYAVATI
June 16, 1941
CHI. VIDYA,
A great calamity has indeed befallen you. These are your
moments of trial. You have to bear it with fortitude.
There
is a tuberculosis hospital in Delhi but it is for the benefit of Delhi
people only and is always full. But the one at Bhowali is the
best, where people belonging to the U.P. are given priority, and
there is another at Dharampur. There is no cause for worry.
If Rajendra observes restraint in diet he will recover. If the tu-
berculosis is in its early stages it is not incurable.
Blessings from
BAPU
