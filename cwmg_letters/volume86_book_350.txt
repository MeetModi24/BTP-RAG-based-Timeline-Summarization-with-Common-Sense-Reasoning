350. LETTER TO HOSHIARI
SRIRAMPUR,
December 26, 1946
CHI. HOSHIARI,
Your letter. I like it. The reason for my not writing to
you is that I am confident that if you are fully engaged in
your work, you will not worry about a letter from me.
Blessings from
BAPU
