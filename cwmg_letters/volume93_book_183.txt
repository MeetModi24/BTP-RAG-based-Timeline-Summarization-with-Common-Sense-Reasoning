183. LETTER TO LILAVATI ASAR
TITHAL,
May 25, 1937
CHI. LILAVATI,
I shall be coming there on the 12th instead of on the 1st.
It will of course be nice if you can maintain good health and
remain cheerful there. But if you cannot do so, the correct
thing would be to go to your brother in Bombay. Munnalal
writes that you have lost weight. It is not good that the
stomach-ache persists. You did well in going to Nagpur.
Blessings from
BAPU
