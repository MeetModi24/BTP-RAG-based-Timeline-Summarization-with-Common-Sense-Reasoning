291. LETTER TO RANI VIDYAVATI
March 17, 1930
DEAR SISTER,
I have your letter. When the women at the Ashram are
ready to go to jail, you ladies may also go. In the meantime
you should do organizational work in villages.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI RANI VIDYAVATI
BARUA
Via HARDOI
U. P.
From the Hindi original : Rani Vidyavati Papers. Courtesy: Gandhi
National Museum and Library
