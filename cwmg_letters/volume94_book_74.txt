74. LETTER TO PYARELAL
September 19, 1941
CHI. PYARELAL,
I have your letter. I had my doubts about Dr. Joshi. But even such
experiences are useful to us. Your reply was of course proper.
Why did the car have to go to Lucknow ? Whatever happens, do
not leave anything unfinished there. It does not matter if a couple of
days more have to be spent.
Think over what I wrote about Mother yesterday.
Blessings from
BAPU
