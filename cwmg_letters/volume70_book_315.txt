315. LETTER TO NAJUKLAL N. CHOKSI
SEGAON, WARDHA,
October 18, 1939
CHI. NAJUKLAL,
I was glad to have your letter after a long time. Moti should
get well.
There is no question of violence or non-violence on the part of
God. Prahlada's non-violence, however, remained inviolate. If
the whole world were non-violent there would be no Hiranya-
kashipu or his like. But if there should be any, then there would
be many more like him. God will always use them. However, they
do not cease to be violent. How do we know for whose de-
struction Hitler was born? But will Hitler's violence ever count
as non-violence?
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 12153
