499. LETTER TO SHIVABHAI G. PATEL
SEGAON,
March 13, 1938
CHI. SHIVABHAI,
I hope you do not expect the customary words of condolence
from me. Hira has departed after repaying her debt and
receiving her due. What is there for grief in this? Your duty
is clear. You should bring up the child and not harbour even
399
the thought of marrying again.
If you find this beyond your
strength, do what is possible for you. Do not deceive yourself.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9518. Also C.W. 434. Courtesy:
Shivabhai G. Patel
