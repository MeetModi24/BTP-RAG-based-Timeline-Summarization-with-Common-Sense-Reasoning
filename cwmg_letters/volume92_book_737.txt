737. LETTER TO GORDHANBHAI L. BHAKTA
January 23, 1934
BHAI GORDHANBHAI,
I had got your letter. Let me know if you have received
any further information about Madhavji. I had only the infor-
mation you gave me. Where is Bhai Durbari? How is he? Where
was his case taken up? Write to me all that you know.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI GORDHANBHAI L. BHAKTA
BHAKTA PATIDAR VIDYARTHI ASHRAM
NAVSARI
B. B. & C. I. RLY.
From the Gujarati original: C. W. 10992. Courtesy: Gordhanbhai L.
Bhakta
