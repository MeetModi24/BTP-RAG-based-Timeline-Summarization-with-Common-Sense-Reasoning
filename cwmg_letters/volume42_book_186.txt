186. LETTER TO SOUTH AFRICAN INDIAN CONGRESS
CAMP KALAKANKAR,
November 14, 1929
THE SECRETARIES
SOUTH AFRICAN INDIAN CONGRESS
[DURBAN]
DEAR SIR,
I enclose herewith letter received from the Accounts Officer,
Telegraph Check Office, Calcutta, with reference to my applica-
tion for refund to be made of the amount sent by you for a
message which amount was not used.
Yours sincerely,
Encl. 1
From a microfilm: S.N. 15767
