585. LETTER TO GANGA DEVI
NANDI DURG,
Vaishakh Vad 14 [May 29, 1927]
DEAR SISTER GANGA DEVI,
I have received your letter. It makes me anxious that you
are still so weak. If you feel hungry, you should increase the
quantity of milk. We get good milk there. Hence it will be all
right to take it even unboiled. Along with milk, I also take the
juice of ten neem leaves. It does not spoil the taste of milk. Try
it for a couple of days.
Blessings from
BAPU
