380. LETTER TO SUCHETA KRIPALANI
ON THE STEAMER,
December 25, 1945
CHI. SUCHETA,
I have your report. It is very good. You have given all
the information. I got your report the day before yesterday after
the women of the camp met me.
I have written to Purnima.
Hope P[rofessor] is well.
Blessings from
BAPU
SHRI SUCHETA DEVI
SWARAJ BHAVAN
ALLAHABAD
From a copy of the Hindi : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
