547. LETTER TO NARAHARI D. PARIKH
September 19, 1941
CHI. NARAHARI,
I have your letter. It seems perfectly all right to me. Tell
Bhai Bhagwanji that I got his letter too. He should get relieved
from there and come here. I will see how to fix him up. He
will certainly be of use, if he stays here. Show this to Bhai
Bhagwanji and send him.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9125
