19. LETTER TO PRINCIPAL, SANGLI INDUSTRIAL
AND AGRICULTURAL SCHOOL
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 18, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
I thank you for your very full letter. Only one further inquiry
I would esteem it a favour if you would answer. Is there any
difference in the ratio of nutrition between fertilized and sterile
eggs? In your school, do you also teach cattle-breeding and
dairying?
Yours sincerely,
THE PRINCIPAL
SANGLI INDUSTRIAL AND AGRICULTURAL SCHOOL
SANGLI
From a photostat: S.N. 14164
