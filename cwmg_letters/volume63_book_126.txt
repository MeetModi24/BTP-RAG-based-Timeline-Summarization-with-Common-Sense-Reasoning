126. LETTER TO GITA BAJAJ
SEGAON, WARDHA,
June 28, 1936
CHI. GITA 3 ,
You should live up to your name. Widowhood and the
married state are not of our choosing. No one has control over
life and death. Hence remain calm and dedicate yourself to
service. I just got the news from Jamnalalji. Write to me.
Blessings from
BAPU
[From Hindi]
Gandhiji aur Rajasthan, p. 204
