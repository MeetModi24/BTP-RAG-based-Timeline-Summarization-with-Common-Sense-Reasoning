278. LETTER TO DR. GOPICHAND BHARGAVA
July 28, 1934
DEAR DR. GOPICHAND,
Can you not collect from the public what you have spent
for the Congress? But if you must pay, it is better to do so as
early as possible and avoid interest. Therefore you must give more
attention to practice than you are doing.
I am quite clear that you should keep away from the Congress
and the elections.
I do not at all appreciate Dr. Kitchlew's fast. I hope it will
have ended when this reaches you.
I shall await your letter on khadi.
I am ashamed to have put you to all that taxing trouble
during my stay.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
From a copy: Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
