139. LETTER TO RUKMINI BAJAJ
SEVAGRAM,
January 31, 1945
CHI. RUKMINI 2 ,
I got your letter only today. At last you have come. You
write about Dr. Jussawala. I am glad to learn that. You have
enough money for it. By all means put yourself under Jussawala's
treatment in Bombay and get well. I follow your argument.
We want your health to improve, no matter whether in Bombay
or Poona.
I shall be happy if you write to me regularly.
I am well.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
I am also glad to learn that Radhika's health is improving.
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 9065
