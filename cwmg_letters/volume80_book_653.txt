653. LETTER TO SHAMALDAS GANDHI
SIMLA,
June 30, 1945
CHI. SHAMALDAS,
I have your letter. My congratulations to Bhogilal are con-
tained in the telegram I sent you. Now it is your responsi-
bility to see that Mahendra does not squander money. I had
the same relations with Kevalrambhai as I had with your father.
Then both of them left. Now bring credit to what I have
done. Convey my thanks to Bhogilal for bringing credit to the
late Kevalram. May he bring him more. I do not have time
to write more.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a copy of the Gujarati : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
