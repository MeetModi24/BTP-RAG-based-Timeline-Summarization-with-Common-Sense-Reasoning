277. LETTER TO PRABHAVATI
BARDOLI,
May 26, 1931
CHI. PRABHAVATI,
I could not write to you from Nainital, but I did get your
letter. I understand your difficulty. In what manner do you
serve father-in-law? It would be very good indeed if he lets you
serve all the time. That will keep your mind engaged and if,
in consequence, the fainting fits stop, your health will improve.
I completely forgot to write to Narandas about sending money
to you, though of course he knows about it. Has there been
any difficulty about the matter? Write to Narandas and request
him to send you any sum you require. For some time I shall be
staying in Bardoli.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 3413
