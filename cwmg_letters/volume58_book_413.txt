413. LETTER TO BALIBEHN M. ADALAJA
August 29, 1934
CHI. BALI,
I got your letter. I was pleased. I also believe what you
say about Harilal. I had a letter from him written from Por-
bandar. I heard from him nearly after a year. I have of course
cautioned him. I am glad that Manu has shed her fear. How
can we cure her irritability?
Your health seems to have gone down badly. Kusum's also
is none too good. But since both of you sisters know the sacredness
of service, you will not fail in it.
All are well here.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: C.W. 1531. Courtesy: Manubehn S.
Mashruwala
