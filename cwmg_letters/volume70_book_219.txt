219. LETTER TO RAMASINHA
SEGAON, WARDHA,
September 19, 1939
BHAI RAMASINHAJI,
Only today I could reach your letter of August 28. The
letter is excellent. If the Garasia brethren really take up the
work which you have described, both they and Kathiawar would
forge ahead.
Vandemataram from
M. K. GANDHI
From a photostat of the Gujarati: G.N. 5228
