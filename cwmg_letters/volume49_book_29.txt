29. LETTER TO RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
January 22, 1932
DEAR RAMANAND BABU,
May I ask you for the same courtesy you extended to me
during my last incarceration ? I did see the current issue of
The Modern Review.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
SJT. RAMANANDA CHATTERJEE
SHANTINIKETAN
From the original: C.W. 9526. Courtesy: Santa Devi
