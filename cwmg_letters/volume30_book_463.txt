463. LETTER TO JAISUKHLAL
ASHRAM, SABARMATI,
Friday, April 30, 1926
CHI. JAISUKHLAL,
I have your letter. I am sending Chi. Maganlal's criticism
for your information. I see that Abbas Saheb is doing fine work.
What shall I write to Ramjibhai about the car? I understand
from what you say in your letter that for the present you want him
to keep the car as it is.
