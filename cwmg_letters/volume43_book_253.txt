253. LETTER TO MAHADEV DESAI
[April 14, 1930]
CHI. MAHADEV,
I have your letter.
I have arranged to meet all of you on Wednesday at Vejal-
pur. I shall go there from wherever I happen to be. I shall
try to reach the place early in the morning. The headquarters will
be mostly at Karadi now.
If Swami' s request stands, I shall leave for Bombay on Thurs-
day evening and return from there on Sunday morning. I don't
feel inclined to go to Bombay just now.
Your description of the happenings in Ahmedabad. . . .1
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 11481
