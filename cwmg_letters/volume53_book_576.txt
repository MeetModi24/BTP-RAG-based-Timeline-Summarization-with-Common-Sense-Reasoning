576. LETTER TO R. KAIMAL
February 28, 1933
DEAR FRIEND,
I have your letter. I believe I have a previous letter of yours
unanswered. I must however deal with it when I come to it
in the regular course.
With reference to your question in your letter of 23rd ins-
tant, my emphatic answer is, like the rest, the present priesthood
is decadent. If it was not, we should find Hinduism in a different
state altogether.
Yours sincerely,
SF. R. KAIMAL
TRIVANDRUM
From a microfilm: S.N. 20400
