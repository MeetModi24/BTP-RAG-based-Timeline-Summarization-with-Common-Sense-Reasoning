78. LETTER TO CHHAGANLAL JOSHI
October 29, 1929
CHI. CHHAGANLAL,
Just now [I saw] the other mail in which [I found] your letter.
The resolution . . .3 about the kitchen has not pained me
but, on the contrary, has satisfied me, for I do not wish anything
to be done under pressure from me. Everything done under
pressure is likely to end in failure. I, therefore, welcome the
Managing Committee's resolution. Whatever changes need to be
introduced in conformity with it, let them be introduced by all
means.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
Twice or thrice have I conveyed my permission to you to
go to Lahore. One of these letters has come back to me, which I
am returning with this.
BAPU
[From Gujarati]
Bapuna Patro-7: Shri Chhaganlal Joshine, p. 173
