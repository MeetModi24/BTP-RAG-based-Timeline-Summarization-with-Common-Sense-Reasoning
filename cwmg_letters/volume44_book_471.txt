471. LETTER TO MATHURADAS PURUSHOTTAM
November 24, 1930
CHI. MATHURADAS,
The question is this: Vithal says that it is not necessary to
rub [the string] with leaves if it has been rubbed with candle-wax.
You say it is necessary. If it is, why is it? Vithal should correct
his error. We find that rubbing with candle-wax is enough. The
cotton does not stick [to the string].
Blessings from
BAPU
