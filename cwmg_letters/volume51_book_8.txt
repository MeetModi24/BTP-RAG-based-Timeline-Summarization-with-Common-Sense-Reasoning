8. LETTER TO SHARDA C. SHAH
September 1, 1932
CHI. SHARDA,
I am absolutely certain that you would do credit to the post
of a secretary. Do not work beyond your capacity. And, do not
be deterred by anything that may happen. You should never
worry. How is it that your yarn snaps so often on the takli?
Luck and perseverance contribute in equal measure in every act
we do. We can control our efforts, so, let us work to our fullest
capacity and leave the result to luck. The Gita teaches us this
very lesson.
BAPU
