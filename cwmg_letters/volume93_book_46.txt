46. LETTER TO RANI VIDYAVATI
WARDHA,
June 12, 1935
CHI. VIDYA,
If you are learning Bengali there and if everyone is envious
of your cheerfulness, I shall congratulate you. What right does
one who is both mother and father have to worry? Also, is
there a rule for one who is only a mother that she must remain
free from anxiety?
There is no chance of my going to Calcutta now.
Blessings from
BAPU
