464. LETTER TO VASUMATI PANDIT
November 24, 1930
CHI. VASUMATI,
You had forgotten to sign your letter. I understand what
you say about Sarbhon. You did right in leaving the place only
after consulting Nath, Narandas and Mahadev. It was not then
necessary to explain things to me in detail.
I am afraid the constipation may persist. Don't neglect to
write to me regularly.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati: S.N. 9278
