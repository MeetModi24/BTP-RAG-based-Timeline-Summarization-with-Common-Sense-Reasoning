386. LETTER TO K. T. GHANASHAM
SEVAGRAM,
November 13, 1944
DEAR FRIEND,
I assure you I am giving due weight to every argument
advanced against the contemplated fast. I wonder if this will
reach you, as you have given me no address.
Yours sincerely,
K. T. GHANASHAM
KARACHI
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy : Pyarelal
