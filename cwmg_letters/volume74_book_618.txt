618. LETTER TO S. AMBUJAMMAL
October 3, 1941
CHI. AMBUJAM,
I am using your cheque for Deenabandhu Memorial. I am
glad to have good news about Kichi and his wife. I never expected
anything but great courage from Mother. The weather in Seva-
gram has been good. I am inquiring about the box you left with
me. I sent it to Bombay there and then, and forgot all about it.
I am glad you reminded me.
Love.
BAPU
SHRI AMBUJAM AMMAL
96 MOWBRAY'S ROAD
ALWARPET 3
MADRAS
From a photostat: C.W. 9617. Courtesy: S. Ambujammal
