407. LETTER TO BIBHAWATI BOSE
September 8, 1945
DEAR SISTER,
I have your warm invitation. But my work this time would
take me to Sodepur. Of course I shall hope to come to your house
some time during my stay.
Hope you are well.
Yours sincerely,
M. K. GANDHI
MRS. BIBHAWATI BOSE
1 WOODBURN PARK
CALCUTTA
From a copy : Pyarelal Papers. Courtesy: Pyarelal
