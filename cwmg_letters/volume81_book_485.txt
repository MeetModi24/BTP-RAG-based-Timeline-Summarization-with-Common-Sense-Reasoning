485. LETTER TO KANCHAN M. SHAH
September 24, 1945
CHI. KANCHAN,
You seem to have again fallen seriously ill. Get well fully.
It is good that Dr. Lilavati is there.
If you feel better, write to me. Get well soon.
Blessings from
BAPU
From a photostat of the Gujarati : G.N. 8263. Also C.W. 6987. Cour-
tesy : Munnalal G. Shah
