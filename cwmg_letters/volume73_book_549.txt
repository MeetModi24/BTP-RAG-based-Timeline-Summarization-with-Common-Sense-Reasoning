549. LETTER TO KASTURBA GANDHI
March 24, 1941
BA,
Either write or dictate a letter to me. A letter from Kanti is
enclosed. On one side he has written to you and on the other to
me. I get regular news about Manu. It looks as if she will have to
be operated upon. I have written and suggested that another
doctor should be consulted and, if he also is of the same opinion, she
should be operated upon. There is no risk at all in the operation.
Do not worry. Now that you are there, go through the full treat-
ment and get well.
Here it is quite hot. Do not worry about me. Things are all
right. Lilavati looks after my food, etc.
Blessings to Lakshmi and the children.
Blessings from
BAPU
[PS.]
A letter from Mani is enclosed. Reply to her at length.
Lakshmi or Sushila will write it for you.
From a microfilm of the Gujarati: M.M.U./XXIV
