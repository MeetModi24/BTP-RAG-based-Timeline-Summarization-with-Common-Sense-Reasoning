25. LETTER TO HARINDRANATH CHATTOPADHYAYA
KUMARA PARK, BANGALORE,
June 19, 1927
DEAR FRIEND,
Have you not unconsciously made a mistake and written to
me when you should have written to the Poet?
Or, have you
really thought that I know the literary men and artists of Europe?
If you have, it is then a case of distance lending enchantment to
the scene. I have hardly any correspondence even with M. Romain
Rolland. I do not think that I have written more than two
letters to him. The European correspondents are all common
readers of Young India. I do not even remember their names, and
they will not be the persons whom you have in view. Now, please
tell me what you will have me to do.
Yours sincerely,
H. CHATTOPADHYAYA, ESQ.
KODIALBAIL P.O.
MANGALORE
From a photostat: S.N. 12773
